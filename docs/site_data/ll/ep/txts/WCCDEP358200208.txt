Westmr.  Alice Demson Aged about Sixty Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a hired Yearly Servant with General Mosson in St. James Palace in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds hath quitted said Service about five Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 4th of January 1765 Before} Thos: Balack  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Alice Demson
