Middlesex  John Medhurst maketh oath That at Michaelmas one thousand seven hundred and Sixty nine he enters upon and Rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Twenty Six Pounds in Little George Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret in Westminster in the County of Middlesex and Continued in the occupation of such house for the Space of half a year and Paid Six Guineas for and upon Account of Rent for the Said house & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlemt. there And this Depont. Saith that he hath not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards nor Served any Parish office nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement
