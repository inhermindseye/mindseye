Westmr.  Ann Carey aged upwards of 30 Years upon Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes, was an Apprentice, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year; says she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true, that she was born in Snows fields in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalene at Bermondsey in the County of Surry, and cannot give any Account whatsoever of the Settlement of her Father or Mother

Sworn the 24th, day of November 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Ann Carey
