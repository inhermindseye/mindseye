Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Morfat aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Dr. Toplis No. 2 Queen row Pimlies in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Fourteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement And this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she is now Chargeable to the said Parish

Sworn this 12th. day of Febry. 1798 before us N Bond Jas:

her Sarah Morfat Mark
