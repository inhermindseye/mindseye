Middx  Ann Cunningham aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath says she never was married And that she was bound Apprentice to & served four Years Apprenticeship with Mrs. Ashbourn a Mantua maker in White horse Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 27th. day of April 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

Ann Cunningham
