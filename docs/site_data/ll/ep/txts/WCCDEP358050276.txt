Middx 

Ann Hart aged upwds. of 22 Years upon Oath saith she Served Seven Years Aprentiship  with Mr Nowland a Peice Broker in White horse Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middlesex and has been out of her time about one Year and has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 21st 1750 before}

The mark of Ann Hart
