Middlesex  Sarah Williams Aged about fifty One Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband at the Parish of St. Peter Le Poor Bread St . London in the year 1761 and by whom she hath Two Children now provided for, says before she Intermarried to her said late Husband and whilst he was a Single man he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Messrs. Coleman and Lucas Merchants in Mining Lane in the Parish of St. Dunstans in the East London for three Years at the yearly Wages of Seven pounds hath quited the said Service about 24 years ago and after quiting the same he or ever Rented Rates Tenth:on Lodging of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or ever gained any other Settlement.

Sworn this 1st. day of Septr. 1784 before Rt. Taylor Jno Barnfather

Sarah Williams
