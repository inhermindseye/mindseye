Middx  John Knight upon Oath saith he rented house in Clare Court in the parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £20 and lived therein abt. 4 Years and hath quitted the same abt. 19 Years & has not since rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 10th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

Jno. Knight
