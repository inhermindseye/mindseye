Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Adams the widow of James Adams  deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with on Dove a Pernkemaker in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief he did not afterwards in any Act to gain another Settlement Her hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that she was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 30 day of April 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Adams
