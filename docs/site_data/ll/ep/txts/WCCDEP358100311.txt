Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to Marybone C to wit John Rofe Aged about Fifty two years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his now Wife at Saint John Chappell in the precinct of the Savoy in the year 1755 and by whom he hath three Children now living Namely John aged 29 years or thereabouts Pellajah Aged about 18 years now provided for and Wm. George aged about 11 years Says about Seven years ago he went and Resided in the parish of Saint Marry Le Bone otherwise Mary Bone and Rented a Part of a Sawyers Yard of One Isaac Newton a Carpenter at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds upon a Lease for Five Months or thereabouts payable Quarterly And also Rented Apartments in the House of Mr. Gwadkins in Castle Street in the said Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone for three Months at the yearly Rent of Eight Pounds and Quitted the same about Seventeen years ago since which time he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th- day of Decr. 1784  Wm. Gascoigne & J Hubbald

John Rofe
