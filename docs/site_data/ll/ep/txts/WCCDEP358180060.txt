Middx  Thomas Haswell  removed from the Parish of Wimbledon in the County of Surry by Order Dated the 3d. Decr. last upon his Oath saith that by Indenture Dated the 14th. Day of October 1728 he was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Thos. Hammerton in Warder Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Plaisterer that since his said Apprentisp he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne the 1st. Febry 1742 before} Wm: Jones

Thos. Haswell
