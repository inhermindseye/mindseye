Westmr. to wit Eleanor Moore Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with Robert Moore her Husband who has been gone for her about four Years and does not know whr. his is living or Dead And by whom she hath Two Children now living namely Jane Aged about Eleven Years and John Aged about Eight Years Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Daniel Peirs a Silver Smith in Spur Street Leicester Fields in the Parish of Saint Anns Soho in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Paid Taxes or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief Says that she this Examinant Rented a House in Princes Street Saint James's but without any knowledge of her said Husband or did he ever know who the Landlord of the Premises was or ever engaged for the paymt. of the Rent or orwise

Sworn this 22d. day of Novr. 1774 before- R. Butler W Addington

Elenor Moore
