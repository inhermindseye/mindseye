Middx  Mary Macdonald upon Oath saith she was married to Daniel Macdonald at the Fleet London on the 1st Jany. 1754 & that the sd. Macdonald is a native of Ireland and that at the time of her sd. intermarriage she was the wido. of Fras. Farrell an Irishman who never gained a Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or belief and by whom she has a Child named Thos. aged 8 yrs born in Parkers Lane in the Parish of St Giles in the fields in the County of Middx and this Examint. further Saith that she is a Native of Ireland & never gained a settlemt. in England

Sworn Janry 22d 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Macdonald
