Middlesex ss}  Mary Notsby The widow of John Notsby deced Maketh oath that She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her said said late Husband or of his Parents Save that her Said husband has informed her that he was born at Boston in New England That this Depont. Mother has informed her That her Fathr. Joseph Quarnes about twenty Years Ago rented two houses of the yearly Rent or Value of Eleven Pounds in Ill and lins Rents in the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middlesex where this Depont. lived with him as part of his Family & where he died this Depont. being then about Eight Years old That She this Depont. was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save What She might have Acquired by Marriage with her said late husband

Sworn this 23 day of January 1772 before  Saml. Spagg

Mary Notsby
