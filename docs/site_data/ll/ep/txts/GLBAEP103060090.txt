Aldgate Middx

John Cooper  Cordwainer Maketh Oath that he Servd Seven year's Apprenticeship by Indenture to Peter Wakeman of Cradle Court in Aldersgate Street in the Parish of Saint Botolphs without Aldersgate in the City of London  Cordwainer hath not been a hired Servant, nor rented a House of ten pound a year, nor never paid any Parish taxes, nor Servd any parish taxes, Since his Servitude as abovesaid hath a wife Alice and one Child named Elizabeth Alice Cooper aged Eleven years

Sworn the 19th day of October 1747 before Robert Dennett  Richd: Ricards

John Cooper
