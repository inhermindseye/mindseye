Middlesex to wit Sarah Fletcher Aged about Thirty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was Married or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says she Rented and lived in a House in Woodstock Street near High Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for Seven Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about four Years ago since which time she never gained any other Settlement other by Renting House Tenement or Lodgings of Ten Pounds a Year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1782 before R Butler W Gregson

Sarah Fletcher
