Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Coppock aged about 63 years upon her Oath saith that about 31 years ago she Intermarried with Samuel Coppock her late Husband who has been dead about seventeen years at the Parish Church of Saint Brides London That her said late Husband Rented Appartmts. unfurnished of a Mr. Hawkins in Houghton Street for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Nine Pounds during which time her Rented a Cellar of a Mr. Knight in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the Yearly rent of Six Pounds that after he quitted said Apartments and Cellar he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he night have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Janry. 1799 before me Menter

Catrin Coppock
