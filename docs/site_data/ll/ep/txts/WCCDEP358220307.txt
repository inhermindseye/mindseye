Westmr. to wit Elizth. Lawes Aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of George Lawes who has been dead about Eight Months ago and with when she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Margaretts Westminster above Seventeen Years ago Says her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Jackson a Grocer in College Street in the said Parish of St. Margarets Westminster & Served five Years the first part of his Apprenticeship and then Married to this Examinant and his Master gave him up his Indentures and after his sd. late Husband left his said Master he never Rented House or paid Taxes neither hath this Examinant done Any Act since her said late Husbands Death whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 12th. day of Decr.1771 before me R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Elizth. Lawes mark
