Westmr.  John Harris Aged about Thirty seven Years Upon Oath says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says his Father Robert Harris Rented a House in Bennetts Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Seven Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds, and after he quitted same never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or Belief.

Sworn the 17th day of December 1767 before} Barth: Hammond

John Harris
