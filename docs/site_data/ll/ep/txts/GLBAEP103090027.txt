Middlesex ss The Examination of Catharine Gahagan of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County single woman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Twellth day of February 1766 Who Saith That about three Months ago at the house of one William Hills in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County she the said Catherine Gahagan was delivered of a Male Bastard Child and that the said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that one Riley Towers of the Precinct of Saint Catherine in the said County Silver Smith did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child And that the said Bastard Child hath been since Baptised by the name of Riley at the Church belonging to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate & registred by the name of Riley Towers .

Sworn this 12th day of February 1766 before  Chris: Scott

Cathrine Gahagan Her Mark
