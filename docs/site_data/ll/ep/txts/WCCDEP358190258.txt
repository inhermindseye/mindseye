Middx  Alice Smith aged upwards of 45 Years Lodging in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex , Upon Oath Saith she was never Married that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Hailston a Brick layer in Ship Yard in the Said Parish of St Clemt Danes at the Yearly Wages of five pounds for Two Years & hath Quitted her said Service upwds. of Twenty Years & hath not since rented a house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn July 31st: 1762 before } Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Alice Smith
