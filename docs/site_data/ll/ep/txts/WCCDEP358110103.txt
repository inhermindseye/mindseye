Middx  Priscilla Wordan removed by Order of two Justices dated the 20th. of May last from the Parish of St. Bennet Pauls wharf London upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Edwd. Wordan at the Fleet London abt. 19 Years ago and has one Son by him Charles aged upwards of fifteen years who never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for One year That her sd. husband has informed her he lived a Servant with Mr Sloper an Apothecary in Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for three years & upwds. one year of which time was before his Intermarriage wth. this Examinant & the remainder afterwards and that his Wages was five or Six Shillings P Week

Sworn 20th. Octr 1752 before}

The Mark of Priscilla Wordan
