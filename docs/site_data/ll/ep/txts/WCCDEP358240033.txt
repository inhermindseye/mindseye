Middlesex and Westminster }  St Geo:  Blooms- bury Middx  (to wit) Henry Jones  Singleman Aged 38 years and upwards upon his Oath saith that he never was Married, that he lived as an hired yearly Servant with John Butcher  Esquire No 20 in Hart Street  Bloomsbury in the Parish of St. George  Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for twelve months and about 3 or 4 days over at the Yearly Wages of £15 and that he was paid 10 Shillings and Six pence for the few days Service over the year that he quitted that Service about Eight Years ago and that he hath not since rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year lived paid any parish Taxes or lived as an hired Yearly Servant for twelve months under one hiring or done any other Act or thing whereby he might acquire any legal settlement

Sworn before me the 6th. day of June 1791 } Wm Kitchiner

Henry Jones
