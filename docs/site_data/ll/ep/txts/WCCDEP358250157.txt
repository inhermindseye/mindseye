Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Thompson aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes. That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Garvie a Jeweller in Thanet Place in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Sixteen Months at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of March 1795 . } N Bond

her Ann Thompson Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
