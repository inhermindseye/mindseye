Saint Clement Danes 26th. January 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) John Dickens aged 57 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his late Wife Mary (then Mary Kingdom Widow) at the parish Church of St. Nicholas Deptford sometime in or about the Month of February in or abt. the year of one Lord 1763 that previous thereto that he served the last five years of his Apprenticeship under Indre with Mr. Sidley a Taylor in Great Shire Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster and Coutny of Middlesex that since the Expiration of his Apprenticeship he never lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. for a twelvemonth together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the 26th January 1793 before Me } D Walker

John Dickens
