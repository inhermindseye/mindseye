Middlesex ss}  Sarah Cottrell the wife of Charles Cottrell maketh oath that her Said husband about four years & half ago deserted her & went to Places to her unknown & she does not know where now is That about five years ago her sd husband lived in & rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in St Catherines Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & resided thereon for about the Space of Six Months & has not Since to this Deponts knowledge or belief rented a house of ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors rates or Kings taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband in White Chapel Church in the Sd County of Middlesex

Sworn this. 16. day of March 1784 before R Davies

Sarah Cottrell
