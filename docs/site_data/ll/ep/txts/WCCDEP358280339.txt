St Clement Danes Septr. 10th 1791

Middx & Westmr. GG}  G (to wit) Ann Mc. Kenzey Aged 19 years & upwds Upon Voluntary Oath saith that she never was Marrd. that she lived nor acquired any legal settlement in her own Right That her Father Willm. Mc Kenzey Rented & was Married as has as this Deponent has been Informed & verily believes) to Ann his present Wife at the parish Church of St Clement Danes in the said County & Liberty 20 years ago & upwards that her said Father since his said Marriage Rented & with her Mother Resided in New Inn passage at the Yearly Rent of £18, for about Six Months and since hath not acquired any Subsequent legal Settlement as she hath also been Informed & believes And this Deponent further on her Voluntary Oath saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children That is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes when born That Willm. Morgan who now lives in a Court in Bishopsgate Street nearly facing New Street who is by Trade a Shoemaker , & who frequently goes to Sea is the only real & true Father thereof He having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of February last & at several times since at or some or one of which times he begot the same the same Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that thereon as aforesd

Sworn before me the 10th Septr 1791 } R Butler

Ann Mc Kenzey
