Westmr.  Mary Kelly aged about 21 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant that her father and mother were bothe Nahoes of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England and that she (this Examint.) was born at Mr. Thos. Plimptons a Cheesemonger in  Clements Lane in the Parish St Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that upwards of five Years ago she was delivered of

a Male bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Crayden in Newtoners Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the field in the County of Middlesex which has been baptized Daniel Shirley .

Sworn Octr. 26th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Kelley .
