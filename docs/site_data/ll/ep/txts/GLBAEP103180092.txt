Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bastings the widow of John Wellman Bastings  deced maketh oath That her Said husband as she has been informed & believes was the Son of John Wellman late of Great Yarmouth in the County of Norfolk  Fisherman by Mary his wife both deced whose Maiden Surname was Bastings That She this Deponent was lawfully married to her Said late husband by the name of John Wellman Bastings He (as she believes) taking upon him & adding his Mothers Maiden Surname of Bastings By Which name he was Called When he married this Deponent but what was the real Motive Which induced him to add his Mothers Maiden Surname to his Own Surname Wellman She Can't Set forth or discover That her said husband has informed her & Which She believes to be true that he was born in Great Yarmouth aforesaid & as this Deponent has also been informed & believes received Some Education in the Charity School there And Whether her Said husband ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself She Can't in any Wise Set forth or discover nor can She in any Wise Set forth or discover where her Said husbands Parents or either of them was or Were legally Settled at the time of their respcive deaths That She this Depont. Since her Sd husband death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself And She hath three Children namely Ann Aged about Eight Years & three months William 

aged about Six years and Seven Months and Edward Aged about two years and one Month the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband

Sworn this - 18. day of November 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Elizabeth Bastings
