Middlesex ss}  Peter Wright maketh oath that he was born in Stress pay near Castle Grant in that part of Great Britain called Scotland where his Parents always resided That he this Deponent was never bound Apprentice in England nor ever lived as a hired servant there for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or ever did any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for himself in England That he hath a Wife named Mary & three Children namely Dorthy  aged about eight years Peter aged about four years & Eleanor Aged about eight months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 21 day of February 1777 before}

The Mark of Peter Wright
