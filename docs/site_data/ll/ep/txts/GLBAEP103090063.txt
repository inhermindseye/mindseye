Middlesex ss  William Isaac a singleman Maketh oath That divers years ago he was bound Apprentice by Indre to John Carr of the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex  Smith for the Term of Seven Years and Served the Whole time of his apprenticeship in the said Parish and by Means thereof as he is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that he has not since; lived as a hired servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor served any Parish office nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act whereby to gain a settlement subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship.

Sworn this 23d day of April 1767 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Wm. Isaac
