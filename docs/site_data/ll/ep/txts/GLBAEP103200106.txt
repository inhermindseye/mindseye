Middlesex ss}  Mary Thompson  widow of Thomas Thompson deced maketh oath that Since her sd husband death she this Depont (having no Child or Children) was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Elizabeth Webb in Camomile Street in the Parish of Allhallows on London Wall in the City of London & Continued there in Such Service under such hiring for the Space of one year & three quarters That she has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or been Married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 20. day of Febry 1784 before J. Hawley Robt. Smith

The Mark of Mary Thompson
