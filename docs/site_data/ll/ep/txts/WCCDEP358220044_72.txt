Westmr ss.  Anne Gee aged about twenty eight years on her voluntary Oath saith she never was married, and that on Whitsunday last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in her then house in Bennetts Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the name of Hester : and says that one

Reginald Tucker a Brasier in the Strand is the Father of the sd. Child, he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in her house, and his house, about the Month of June last was twelve Month, and several times since, but the particular days or times this Examinant cannot as certain: And this Examinant further saith that the said Reginald Tucker is the true and real Father of the said Child, of which she was so delivered as aforesd, and no other person whatsoever

Sworn this 12th. day of July 1769 before 

Ann Gee
