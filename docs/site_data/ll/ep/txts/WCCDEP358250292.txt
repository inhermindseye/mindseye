Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Maria Goft aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she was bound by the Trustees of the Greenwich Christy School, Apprentice to one Frances Dormer of Mile End in the Hamlet of Mile End Old Town in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex for the Term of Three years that she Served her said Mistress duly and Verily during the said Term and that after she was out of her said Apprenticeship she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1797 before us }
