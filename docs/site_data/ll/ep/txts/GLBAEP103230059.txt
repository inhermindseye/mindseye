Middlesex ss  Alexr Miller maketh oath that he was heretofore  duly bound Apprentice by Indre to James Adamson of Mundlins Rents in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Mr. Waterman & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship there That he has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or a Lodging or other Tenement of the Same Yearly rent or Value or Served any Parish office or Paid any Boors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Apprenticeship That he hath a wife named Christian Miller and two Children namely Christian aged about four years & Nine Months & Ann Aged about nine months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 28. day of June 1787 before  Rob: Smith

Alexr Miller
