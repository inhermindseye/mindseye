Westmr.  Rachel Williams Aged about Fifty seven Years, upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Williams who has been dead about Twenty three Years ago, says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Coombe's a Taylor in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and Upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds quitted same about Seventeen Years ago, and never after Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 5th. day of August 1768 , before} 

Rachel Williams
