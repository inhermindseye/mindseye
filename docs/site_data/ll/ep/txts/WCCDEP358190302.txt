Middx  Thomas Jones aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath says he Rented a House in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Yearly Rent Fourteen Pounds lived therein upwards of Twelve Years and Quitted same upwards of Nine Years and hath not since Rented a House or paid any Taxes

Sworn the 16th. day of Novr. 1762 before} Paul Vaillant

The mark of Thos. Jones
