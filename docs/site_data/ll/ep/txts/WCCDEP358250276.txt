Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Thomas Tiffin aged about 64 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a house in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Twelve years at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and upwards that he hath quitted said House about Twelve years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of Janry. 1797 before me } J Reeves

Thos. Tiffin
