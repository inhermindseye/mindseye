Westmr. 

Saml. Robinson upon Oath saith he intermarried with his wife Jane upwards of 16 Years ago in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn and that he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twenty five pounds in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Six Weeks & upwards and quitted the same upwards of 12 Years ago since which he has not rented a house of ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn June 23d. 1748 before} Tho Lane .

Samll Robinson
