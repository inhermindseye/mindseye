Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Mary Burgess Aged 29 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her late Husband at the parish Edward Burges at Christ Church in the Co. of Surry on the 27th. day of August 1787 by Banns That previous there to and while he her said Husband Edward Burges was single and unmarried not having any Child or Children he lived as an hired yearly Servt. or Clerk with Messrs. Sanderman & Graham in  America Square John Street Minories for two years together at the Wages of Fifty pounds for the first and Sixty pounds for the second year that during part of his said service he lived lodged & slept with Mr Burdett No.1 Old Change in the parish of St. Michael Queen in the City of London Glazier at the lu upwards of two Months and that he quitted the same sometime in October 1785 as she verily believes & in the November succeeding he left the Service of Messrs. Sanderman & Co. And hath not since in his life time after quitting the sd Mr. Burdett done any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief that she hath one Child Named Edward Aged two years who was begotten by her said Husband and is the her only Surviving Child - And that is

unable to Maintain herself & Child without some Relief And their said Husband is now Dead as she is Informed & Newly belives

Sworn the 16th day of Septr. 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Mary Burges
