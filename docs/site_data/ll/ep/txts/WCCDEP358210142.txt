Westmr.  Alice Thompson lodging at the Ship Alehouse at Fleet Ditch London on behalf of John Thompson an Infant aged about Six Weeks Son of Willm. Thompson by Martha his Wife who died on Friday the 27th, day of February 1767, Upon Oath says the said William Thompson is the Son of this Deponent and that he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Sutton a Stationer in Barbican in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate London and served four Years or thereabouts the first part of his said Apprenticeship & then went to Sea and never was Assigned over to any Person nor ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to this Deponents knowledge or belief.

Sworn the 4th. day of March 1767 , before} Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Alice Thompson
