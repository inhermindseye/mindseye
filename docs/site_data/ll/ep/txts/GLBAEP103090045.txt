Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Horrocks Singlewoman of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County taken before us two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the said County this 11 day of October 1766 .

Who Saith that about a Month age in Ball Court in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the said County of Middlesex she the said Elizabeth Horrocks was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath been since Baptised & registred by the name of Mary Horrocks And that one Anthony Fisher of the said Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch Cheesemonger did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child which is since become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 11th; day of October 1766 before  Chris Scott R Pell

Mary Horrocks Her Mark
