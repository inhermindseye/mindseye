Middx & Westmr}  Ann Roffee Aged about Sixty four Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Henry Roffee who has been Dead about four Years and a half, Says that before his Intermarrd with this Examinant her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in little Saint James Street in the Parish of Saint James in the liberty of Westmr for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds or upwards and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Twenty four Years ago and after he Quitted the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or ever gained a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 14th day of Decr 1774. Before  Sampn Wright W Addington

Ann Roffee
