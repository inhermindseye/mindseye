Middx  Rd Jones upon Oath Saith he was married at the Fleet London upwds. of 40 Years ago of his Wife Jane by whom he has a Daughter Sarah aged abt. 20 Years who never was married nor ever was an apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year nor ever rented ten pounds a year or pd. Parish taxes to this Examints. knowledge or belief that he was bound Apprentice from Westmr. Blue Coat School to his father Rd Jones  Senr . a Taylor and lived with him as his Apprentice in Tothil Street in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westmr. for two years or thereabouts and then his father absconded & this Examinant never was assigned over nor ever rented ten pounds by the year or pd. parochial Taxes

Sworn July 13th 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Rd. Jones
