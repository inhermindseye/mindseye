Middx & Westmr:}  Sarah Langstead Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Saith She never was Married that she lived as a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr: Long a Scowerer in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath Quitted her said Service upwards of Seventeen Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 7th: day of July 1770 before me.} R. Butler

Sarah Langstead
