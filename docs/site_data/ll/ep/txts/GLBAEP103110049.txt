Middlesex ss}  John Welles a Singleman Maketh oath That divers Years Ago he was hired and lived as a Yearly hired Servant with one John Harris who then Kept the Flying Horse Ale house in the Parish of St. John Hackney in the County of Middlesex and Continued in Such Service for the space of two Years and upwards there at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the said Parish That this Depont. hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor did he ever rent a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his Knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Servitude abovementioned

Sworn this 30 day of March 1772 before  Rob Pell  Cha: Digby

John Welles
