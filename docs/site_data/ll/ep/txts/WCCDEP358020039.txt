Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Grace Mitchell aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that about in July 1770 she Intermarried with Lawrence Mitchell her present Husband (who has deserted her) at the Parish Church of St. Mary Lambeth that she hath 2 Children living by her said Husband vizt. John aged about 16 years unprovided for and Mary Elizth. aged about 10 years provided for that her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Ext. And whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Bullon who kept the Hampshire Hog on Bethnell Green in the Parish of St. Matthew Bethnell Green in the County of Middlesex for 12 Months and upwds. that he hath quitted said Service upwards of 18 years ago since which time he has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 or done any Act whereby she might have gained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of March 1787  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordon

Grace Mitchell
