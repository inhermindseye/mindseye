Middlesex ss}  Mary Jefferys the Wife of John Jefferys maketh oath that her Said husband Who is now Absent from her has informed her & Which She believes to be true That he was born in the Parish of Saint Gregory in the City of Canterbury And this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting the Settlement of her Said husband That she was lawfully married to her Said husband whom she believes to be still living and that She hath a Child named Thomas Jefferys Aged about nine Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband

Sworn this 27 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Jefferys
