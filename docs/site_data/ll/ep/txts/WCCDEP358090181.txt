Middx  Elizabeth Lashbrook Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or lived was an Apprentice Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Durhans a Taylor in Vinegar Yard in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service About Three Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 14th. day of Decr. 1780 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Elizth. Lashbrook Mark
