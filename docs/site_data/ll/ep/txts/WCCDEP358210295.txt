Westmr.  Ann Schellinger Aged about Twenty seven Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Edward Schellinger (gone from her) with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Enfield in the County of Middx Says she Rented a House the back of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Five Pounds, lived therein upwards of Two Years, Quitted same about Two Years and an half ago; and never after Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 14th day of July 1768 , before}
