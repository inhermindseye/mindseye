Middx  Silvester Smith upon his Oath saith that on the 25th- Day of May 1727 he Intermarried with Anne his now Wife at the Fleet  London & that before his Intermarriage with his sd. Wife & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr- Wm- Berry in Nicholas Lane  Lombard Street in the Parish of St. Nicolas Acons in the City of London for the Space of Two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & further saith that soon after his sd. Intermarriage his sd. Master Wm- Berry told this Examinant if he wd. continue to live with him he would make it up to him & give him one Guinea more, pursuant to which Agreement he continued to live with his sd. Master after his sd. Intermarriage for the Space of Ten Months or thereabts- & during the last Three Months of such Service he lived with his sd. Master in Abchurch Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Abchurch in the City of London aforesd. that he hath not since he left his sd. Masters Service rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space

of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 28th- Day of July 1741 before W Hayton Thos . Deveil

Silvester Smith
