Westmr.  Deborah Robins aged about 75 Yrs. upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Robins who hath been dead about 11 Years Says her sd. late husband lived in & rented a house in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £14 for one Year or thereabouts & left the same about 2 Months before his Decease Says neither her sd. late husband after he quitted said house nor this Examint. since his Death have rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 10th. Septr. 1747 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Deborah Robins
