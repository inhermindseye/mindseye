Middlesex ss}  Joyce Hole the widow of William Hole deced maketh oath that Sometime ago her Said late husband rented a house in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County of the yearly Rent of Twenty two Pounds & resided therein for many years & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there & did not afterw do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

Joyce Hole
