Middlesex ss}  John Keller maketh oath that he heretofore rented a Dwelling house in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and during Such his residence therein he was charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the same That he has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act whatsoever to gain a Settlement in any other Parish or Place

Sworn this 11. day of January 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of John Keller
