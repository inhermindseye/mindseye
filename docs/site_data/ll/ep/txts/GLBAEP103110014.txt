Middlesex ss}  Robert Weatherspoon Maketh oath That he Cohabited for Several Years with one Margaret Weatherspoon Since deced as if they had been Man & Wife although in truth they never were Married to each other nor was the Said Margaret Weatherspoon during Such Cohabitation to this Depont. Knowledge or belief the Wife of any other Person And this Depont. Saith that during Such Cohabitation of aforesaid She the said Margaret Weatherspoon was delivered of four Children to wit Ann Weatherspoon Aged about thirteen Years born in White horse Court in the Parish of St Mary Whitechappel in the County of Middx & Baptized and registred in the Same Parish Robert Weatherspoon Aged about ten years born in Queen Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & there Baptized and registred in Aldgate Church  James Weatherspoon Aged about Seven years born in the Same Parish & there Baptized and registred in the Sd Church and Guy Weatherspoon Aged about Six Years born in the Same Parish and Baptized and registred in the Said Church all which Said Children are Bastards and illegitimate as this Deponent is advised and apprehends and have not nor hath eithr of them done any Act to gain a Settlement in their or any or eithr of their own right.

Sworn this 16th day of March 1771 before Wilmot B Camper

Robert Weatherspoon
