Middlesex ss}  Mary Dyer  Singlewoman maketh oath that about Eleven years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with John Sanders in Bytlers Buildings in the Parish of St Botolph witht. Aldgate in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the space of one year & half That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26. day of June 1784 before}

Mary Dyer
