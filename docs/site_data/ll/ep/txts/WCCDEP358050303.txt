Middx  Eleanr Weatherby upon Oath saith she never was married and that abt. 30 years ago she lived a yearly Servant with Mr John Ward at Stratford in Essex for two years and upwards at the yearly wages of four pounds and has not since she quitted her said Service rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 28th. 1750 before H Fielding

The mark of Eleanr Weatherby
