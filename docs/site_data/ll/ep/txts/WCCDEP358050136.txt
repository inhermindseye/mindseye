Middx  Cathr. Woodward  wido . of Richard Woodward upon her Oath saith she knows nothing of the Settlemt. of her sd. late husband nor can give any Information of his Parents or Place of Birth and that she was born in Watergate Lane at Banin in the County of Cork in Ireland where her father lived in and rented a house and that she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant whils as singlewoman rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes.

Sworn Octr. 28th. 1749 before} Tho. Lane

The Mark of Cathrn. Woodward .
