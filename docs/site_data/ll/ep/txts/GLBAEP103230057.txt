Middlesex ss}  Mary Cotsford  Wife of William Cotsford maketh oath that her Said husband is lately gone away from her to Places to her unknown & left her wholy destitute of Support That her Said husband lately rented a house in the Dark Entry in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at the Yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds & resided there on for the Space of Ten Months and has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 25. day of June 1787 before  R Davies

Mary Cotsford
