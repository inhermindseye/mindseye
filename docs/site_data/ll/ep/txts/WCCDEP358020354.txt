St Clement Danes Novr. 15th 1791

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Catherine Forrest Aged 35 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Fossett Taylor in Maiden Lane in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of £5 - that she left such Service abt. the End of the year 1782 or beginning of the year 1783 and since has not lived as an hired yearly Servant, Rented any House or Apartment of £10 Pr. Annums paid any parish Taxes or otherwise acquired any other legal Settlement And she is now distress and unable to maintain maintain herself

Sworn the 15th Novr. 1791 before us D Walker Philip Dyot

Catherine Forrest
