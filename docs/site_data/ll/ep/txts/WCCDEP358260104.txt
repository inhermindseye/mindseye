Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Beck of Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westmr. on behalf of John Fossey an Infant aged about Seven years Maketh Oath and Saith that she has been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that the said John Fossey was born a Bastard at a Pawnbroker in King Street  Seven Dials in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this Ninth of February 1796 N Bond E Read

her Elizth. Beck mark
