Middlesex & Westminster}  passd. to St James to wit Ann Bibby Aged 69 Years or thereabouts upon her oath Saith that she Intermarried with her late Husband  John Bibby at the Parish Church of Saint Margarets at Lyme Regis in the County of Norfolk about 42 Years ago and that she does not know of any Settlement her said Husband ever gained And this Examinant further upon her oath Saith That since her said Husbands Death she lived as a hired Yearly servant with a Mrs. Walker in Bentinck Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds & since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may has gained a Settlement Elswhere

Sworn this 22d. day of April 1785 before Sampn Wright Thos. Gilbert

her Ann Bibby Mark
