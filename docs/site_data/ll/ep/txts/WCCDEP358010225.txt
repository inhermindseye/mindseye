Saint Clement Danes.

Sophia Burney aged 21 years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she lived with Mr. Mills of Blue Cross Street Leicester Fields in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex as a Hired Yearly Servant at £4:0:0 Year, that she a for upwards of the Year that she quitted that Service about 5 years ago since which Time she has not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained the a Settlement And Saith that she never was Married

Sworn this 15 July 1788 before  Sampn Wright  Addington

her Sophia Burney  Mark
