Middx  Eliz: Glen aged abt. 42 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. David Glen a Taylor in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. for Seven Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & after his decease Continued to live a Yearly Servt. in the same house with his Nephew James Websters for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds that she quitted the said Mr. Websters Service abt. five Years ago and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 3d. day of Octr. 1757 . before me} Barth: Hammond Elizabeth Glen
