Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Brassett the widow of Thos Brassett deced maketh oath that since the death of her said husband she this Deponent has not been hired or lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year with her Son John Brasset or with any other Person or Persons whomsoever

Sworn this 27 day of September 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Eliz Brassett
