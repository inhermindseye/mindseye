Middx & Westmr to wit}  Elizth. Ransford Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with John Cooper a Cabinet maker near the quieting place in the Parish of Great Marlow in the Country of Bucks for five Years upwards at the Yearly Wages Thirty Shillings for the first Year forty Shillings for the 2d. fifty Shillings for the 3d. Sixty Shillings for the 4th. & Three pounds Ten Shillings for the last Year hath quitted her said Service about five Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 19th. day of February 1772 before W Kelyngs Sam Wright

her Elizth. Bansford Mark
