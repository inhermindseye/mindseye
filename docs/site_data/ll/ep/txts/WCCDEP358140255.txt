Middx.  Eliz  Margt.  Smith aged abt. fifty five years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Smith who has been dead about Seven Years says her said husband  often times in his life time inform'd her that he lived a hired yearly Servt. whilst he was a Single Man with Mr. Lamborn Button seller in St. Clements Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds for one year and upward's and after he quitted said Service never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year; says she has one Child by her sd. husbd. Mary -aged abt. Seven Years & that she has not since her sd. husbands death Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn the 9th June 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Smith
