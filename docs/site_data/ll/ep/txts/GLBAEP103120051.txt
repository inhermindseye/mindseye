Middlesex ss}  Grace Gilbert  singlewoman Aged about twenty Seven Years maketh oath That sometime ago she was hired and lived as a hired servant by the year by and With one Mrs. Padget in the Parish of Saint James in the City of Bristol and Continued in such service for the space of one year and upwards and by means thereof gained a legal settlement there and that she this Deponent has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 10th day of July 1773 before B Camper

Grace Gilbert
