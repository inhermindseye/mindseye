Middx  Able Minion Aged about Fifty Years upon Oath Says she is a Singlewoman & without Child or Children Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mascall a Bricklayer in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middx One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & hath quitted her said Service upwards of two Years & hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert. for One Year.

Sworn the 15th of Decr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Able Minion
