Westmr.  Sarah Mayland aged about 38 Years upon Oath says she intermarried with Abraham Mayland in the Parish Church of St. Ann Westmr . at the Fleet London says she Rented a house the Bottom of Chancery Lane in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West in the City of London of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds, lived therein about two Years and quitted same about five Years ago And hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes

Sworn the 23d. Sepr. 1765 . before R Kilsha Fran: Cary

Sarah Mayland
