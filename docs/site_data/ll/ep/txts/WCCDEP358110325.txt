Middx.  Lawrance Carney upon Oath saith he is a Native of Ireland and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant nor rented ten Pounds by the Year or paid Parochial Taxes & that his father Bartley Carney rented A house of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds at Killebening in the County of Rose oman in Ireland & lived therein Several Years.

Sworn Octr. 8th 1753 before}
