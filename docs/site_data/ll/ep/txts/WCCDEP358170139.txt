Middx  Alice Lake  Wife of John Lake upon her Oath saith that upwds. of Twenty Years ago she intermaryed with her sd. Husband Jno. Lake at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holbourn on the 18th- Day of Octr- & that her sd. Husband was bound an Apprentice to and served his Apprentiship with his Father in the Parish of Wiggen in the County of Lancaster that since his sd. Apprentispp he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her Husband absconds from her & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herself

Sworne the 29th. of Septr. 1741 before}

The mark of Alice Lake
