Middx  Mary White aged abt. 61 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Giles White with whom she intermarried abt. thirty years ago at the Parish Church of Deptford in the County of Kent says her said Husband Rented a House of of Ten Pounds a year in Crown Court in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes lived therein abt. two years. & quitted same abt. ten years ago & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 16th. day of March 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary White
