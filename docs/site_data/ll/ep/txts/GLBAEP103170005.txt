Middlesex ss}  Carolina Floyd  Singlewoman maketh oath that She never was married and that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages by & with Hans Tennis of the Precinct of Saint Catherine in the Said County Victualler & Continued in Such Service for the Space of three years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lives as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a Child named Peter James Hedgley aged about fourteen Months Which was born in the Parish of St. Saviour in Southwarke in the County of Surry & is illegitimate & Bastard and that one Peter Hedgely late of Newington in the Same County Painter is the Father of the Said Child

Sworn this 9th day of September 1776 before  Jno Sherwood W Blackmore

The Mark of Carolina Floyd
