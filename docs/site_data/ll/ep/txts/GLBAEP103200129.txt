Middlesex ss}  Margaret Ross  widow of John Ross deced maketh oath that many years ago her sd late husband lived in & rented a house of the yearly rent of Twelve Pounds near the Butcher Row in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & paid Poors rates for the Same That her sd late husband who died about two Months ago in the Parish of St George Southwark in Surrey has not Since renting the sd house as above rented a house of the yearly rent of ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere that She hath three Children namely Sarah aged near thirteen years Ann aged ten years & Margaret aged Seven years who have not gained any Settlement in their own right the lawful issue of this Depont by her sd husband to whom she was lawfully Married

Sworn this 17th day of April 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Margaret Ross
