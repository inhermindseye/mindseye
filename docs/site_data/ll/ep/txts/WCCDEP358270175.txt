(Militia Man's Wife ) not Sworn

Saint Clement Danes March 17th- 1794

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit,- Elizabeth Roberts aged thirty eight Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was married by Banns at Liverpool about twenty three Years ago, that to Howel Roberts her Present Husband who is now in actual Service in the Westminster Regiment of Middlesex Militia, that she has two Children by her said Husband, now living Vizt- Howell Roberts aged between Six and Seven Years, and Marianne Roberts aged between three and four Years-and that she is poor and unable to Maintain herself and her said Children during the Absence of her said Husband-and that her said Husband is now so serving in the Militia as a Substitute for Mr. James Jackson of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 17th. Day of March 1794 Before me}
