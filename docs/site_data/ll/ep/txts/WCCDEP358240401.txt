Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields no Settlemt. there, the Premes where she lodged being is St James's parish  Frances Brown  Widow aged about 68 years Upon Oath saith that she was Married le Paul Brown at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Fields in or about the year 1762 that he left this Examinant about seven years and went to Bombay after their Marriage and went to Bombay in the East Indies that she hath head her said Husband is since dead that she And this Examinant faith or saith that she hath nor never had any Child by that Husband that she hath a Child by a former Husband who has been settled his own right for about  22 years ago that subsequent thereto she lived as an hired yearly Servant (at the Annual Wages of £5 that with Mr. William Massa formerly of Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for near three years at and Resided during the last ten Months of said her Service and at his Workshop in Wardwick Street in the Parish St. James Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminister in the Said County that she left that Service about 20 years ago and hath not acquired any legal settlement to since to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 28th. April 1794 before us } M Addington Edw Read
