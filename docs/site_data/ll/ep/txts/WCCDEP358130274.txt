Middx  Grace Darby aged 48 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married, says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Morgan Dunavain a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for three Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abt. Sixteen Years; since which time she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn February the 9th: 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

Grace Darby
