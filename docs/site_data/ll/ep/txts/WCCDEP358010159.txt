Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Fulton Thomas aged about 52 years upon his Oath saith that about 23 years ago herented & lived in a House in Lambs Conduit Passage in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £21 and paid Taxes due for the same that since he quitted said House he has not Rented any Place of the yearly Rent of £10 paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Novr. 1787  Sampr. Wright N Bond

his Fulton Thomas Mark
