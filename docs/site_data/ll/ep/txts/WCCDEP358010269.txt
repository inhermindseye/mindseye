Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) James Baxter aged forty four years thereabout upon his Oath Saith that he rented and lived in a House Clarye Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of three year, at the yearly rent of fifteen Pounds, that he quitted Such his House about five years and since then has not rented any other House or Tenement of the yearly Value of ten Pounds paid any Taxes or served any Parish office or done any other Act to gain any Subsequent Settlement and this Examinant further saith that he in not married but is poor and unable to maintain himself that he hath four Children by Sarah Pichersgill who was lived with Laminantly all living the eldest named James , the second Thomas , the third Sarah , the fourth Richard -all born in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch illegitimate Children and the eldest aged five years a thereabouts the second about from the third about two-the fourth and youngest only one Month-thereabouts

Sworn this 26th. Febry 1789 before me Wm Kitchiner }

James Baxter
