Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Susannah Norton aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or ever Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That her Father & Mother Rented and lived in a house in Church Yard Alley in the Parish of Saint Clement Dunstan in the West in the City of London for two Years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds and that after they quitted said house they never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 16th. day of March last past she this Examinant upon was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Nurse in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been half baptized by the Name of Joseph That one John Brown a Journeyman Taylor who did lodge at Mr. Rotherys Arundel Street and did Work in Golden Square is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of June last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Brown is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 22d. day of April 1790 } R Butler

Susahannah Norton

Susannah Norton lodges at No:43 in Vere Street at a Bakers.

Settled Pays. £10.. at 2d pd. Juo.
