Middx  Thos. Parry upon his Oath saith that abt- Fifteen Years ago he being at that Time a Singleman & not having any Child or Children lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Chapman in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of Two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that he hath not since he left his sd. Service rented Ten Pounds pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 13th- Day of August 1741 before}
