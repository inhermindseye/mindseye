Middx  Anthony Carlton on the behalf of John Cramp a Lunatick upon his Oath saith that he has been informed & believes that the said John Cramp about 33 years ago was bound an Apprentice for four years to one Mr. Long at the two Brewers in Water Lane in the Parish of St. Ann in Blackfryers London Victualler & Waterman That he served him as an Apprentice for the said Term of four Years & hath not since his said Apprenticeship rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as this Examinant has been informed & verily believes

Sworn the 18th. Day of Septr. 1755 before us} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Anthony Carlton
