Middx to wit Charles Brighting Aged about Thirty Two years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented House paid Taxes Or ever lived a hired yearly servant for One year says he was bound and apprentice for seven years to One Mr. Tash a Watch Movement Maker in Normans Buildings in the Parish of Saint Luke in the County of Middlesex and served his said master for the Space of Five years under such Indenture when his said Agreed to give up his Indentures and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly servant for One year

Sworn this 31st. day March 1781 before R Butler

Charles Brighting
