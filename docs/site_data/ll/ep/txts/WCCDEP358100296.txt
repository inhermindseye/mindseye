Middx & Westmr}  Swear to St. Clemt. A  Bridgett Kelly aged thirty one Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That She never was married Rented house or Lodging or any Tenement of 10s. a Year Value - Says that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Danworth in Denzell Street Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of a Year at the Wages of four pounds ten Shillings a Year that the quitted her said place about twelve Months ago, and that since she to quitted said place she has not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere than in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 26th. Novr- 1784 before  Wm Kitchiner

Bridgett her Mark Kelly
