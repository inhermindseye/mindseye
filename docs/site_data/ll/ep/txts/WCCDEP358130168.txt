Middx  Mary Slater Wife of Peter Slater upon Oath Says She Intermarried with the sd. Peter Slater at the Fleet London on the 25th. Day of February abt. Sixteen Years ago, Says her husband was bound an Apprentice for five Years to Robert Fair brother in Indian Court in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx. and serv'd him there one Year & a half the first part of his sd. Apprentiship & then his said Master and this Ex- aminants Husband parted by Con- -sent and never was assigned over to any other Person as her said Husband hath inform'd her and as She verily believes. Says her said husband never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes

or was a Yearly Servant for one Year Says She hath Two Children, John aged about Eleven Years and Eleanor aged abt. Eight Years, & her said Husband being very ill and Poor is not able to maintain himself and them.

Sworn the 26th. Day of May 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham  Barth. Hammond

The mark of Mary Slater
