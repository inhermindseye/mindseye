Middx  Sarah Pace aged abt. 41 Yrs. upon Oath says She Intermarried with Benjn. Pace at the Fleet London abt. 26 Yrs. since says the sd. Benjn. Pace hath absconded from her for abt these Twenty - Years neither hath she seen him or heard from him since says her sd. Husbd. hath often informed her & which information she believes to be true that he served his Apprentiship of Seven Years with Mr. Vastin a Grocer in Bishopsgate Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Bishopsgate in the City of Londn . & that he never Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn Janry 18 1757 before} Barth:Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Sarah Pace
