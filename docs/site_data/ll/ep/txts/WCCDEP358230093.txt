Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Collings Aged about 43 years upon her Oath Saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr Bawn hatter Bell yard Histr street Hill in the Parish of St Margaret in the City of London for 18 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of ten Guineas that she hath quitted said service about seven years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 18th day of October 1799 before us G S Goodenough Saml. Edwards

Her Elizh Colling mark
