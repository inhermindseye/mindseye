Westmr. 

Ann Alcock aged 27 Years Wife of Wm. Alcock aged 23 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Wm. Alcock in the Parish Church of Saint Andrew in Holborn on or abt. the 9th. Day of Febry 1745 Says her sd. husband before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Burghall Cheesemonger near the New Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £5 & £6 for two Years and upwards & left his sd. Service about Michas 1745 Says her said husbd. never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 28th. 1747 before me  Wm: Jones

Anne Alcock
