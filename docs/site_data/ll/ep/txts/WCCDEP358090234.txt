Middx to wit Ann Weaver Aged about Thirty five years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she Lived a hired yearly Servant in the Capacity of a Cook with One Mr. Richardson a Cow keeper in Gray's Inn Lane Road in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of Ten Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of One year ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or Living a hired yearly servant for One year And further says she hath One Child Named Harriott Aged about Two years and an half a Bastard born on the Body of this Examinant by One Thomas Wheat deceased and which said Child was born in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and which said Child hath gained no other settlement then by Birth as this Examinant is Informed & Believes

Sworn this 18th. day of June 1781 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Ann Wever
