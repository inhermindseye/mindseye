Before Birth

St Clement Danes 7th June 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnant  Reputed Father (to wit) Ann Britain  singlewoman aged thirty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Price in Blackmoon Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for about two years, at the annual Wages of Seven Guineas, that she quitted that service about two years ago and hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever since, whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge or belief And this Examinant Upon her voluntary Oath further saith that She is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes, that Thomas Wankling otherwise Wantling who now does or lately did Work as Journeyman with Mr Jackson the Smith in Great Shire Lane in the  liberty and County aforesaid with only real and true father of the Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant as aforesaid he having first had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body sometime in or about the Month of March last and at several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child a Children thereon And that he and no one else is

the  real true father thereof

Sworn the 7th day of June 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Ann Britain 's Mark

Witness G Jarrett

Re Sworn the 9th June 1793 before me } D Walker

7th June 1793} Mr Britain lodges at No 5 King Street Drury Lane Wankling taken & agreed to Mary the Girl was they were Married on the 8th instt  at the parish Church of St George Bloomsbury the Banns has been 3 times published there
