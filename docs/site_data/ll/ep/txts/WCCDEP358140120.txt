Middlesex  Sarah Fennell upon Oath says she intermarried with her husband Richd. Fennell in the Parish Church of St. James Westmr. in the Month of September 1749. says her said husbd. is a Soldier in the 2d. Regiment of Foot Guards and is drafted off to the Isle of Wight says he bound himself by Indenture for 4 Years to Thos. Pickle the corner of Maggots Court in Piccadilly in the Parish of St. James Westmr. Brazier and lived with him during all that time and had meat drink washing and Lodging & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year says she has two Childn. by him Elizth : aged Seven Years and Richd . aged abt. Eight Months

Sworn this 20th of May 1758 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Fennell
