Middx and Westmr. Elizabeth Corfield aged abt. 28 years Upon Oath says She never was Married and that She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Longville a Shoemaker in Wych Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for two years at the Yearly Wages of £4 and quitted her said Service in Janry last And further Upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born and that One John Morgan a Shoe Maker had carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of July 1759 and at several other times since at one of which times he get her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said John Morgan and no other person did beget the same.

Sworn this 15th. day of March 1760 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz. Corfield

N: B: he is to be found at his Club in Hewets Court by St. Martins Ch : the first Tuesday after L: D:{ Upon the Complt. of Mr Wilkinson Ovr. J: P:
