St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Upon Enquiry not Settled here story falce (to wit) Hannah Walker aged 23 years and upwards Singlewoman Upon Oath saith That she never was Married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Woothead at the White Horse Clare Market in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said Liberty and County for about two years at the Annual Wages of Seven pounds that she quitted that service about ten Months ago and hath not since rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year paid any parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent further on her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant & with Child of with a Child or Children w Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the paid parish of St Clement Danes and that Joseph Beards a Journeyman Carpenter who works with Mr Smith some where in or near Chancery Lane in the said County or in the City of London is the only real and true Father of such Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as afore and he having had Carnal knowledge of this De Deponent's Body sometime in or about the Month of December last and at several time since at some or one of which times he did 

beget the same thereon and their D Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant, thereon on the Body of her this Deponent and lastly this Deponent saith that no other person but the said Joseph Bard Beards hath ever been criminally connected with her this Deponent and that she is unable to defray the expence of her lying in or to maintain herself till the time of her delivery with out assistance

Sworn the 11th. day of August 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Hannah Walker

Mr. Collett Oversr Mr Talboys Beadle

Augst 14th Mr Walker lodger at the White Horse Clare Markt the Man taken & discharged
