Westmr.  Eliz Rogers aged about 24 Years removed from the Parish of St. Brides London by Order dated the 24th. Day of November 1746 upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Miller at the Temple Eating house in Sheir Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for the Space of three Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds ten Shillings and four pounds and hath left her sd. Service about one Year Says she hath not since she left the said Mr. Millers Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1746 before}
