Middx  Martha Lake upon Oath saith she never was married and that she is the Daughter of John Lake by Eliz . his wife both deceased that  she was born in King Street in the Parish of St. Margt. in the City of Westmr. where her sd. late father rented and lived in a house for sevl. Years & there dyed & that she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn Janry 22d. 1753 Before Fran: Bedwell
