Middlesex ss}  Samuel Robinson maketh oath That divers years ago he rented a house in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds & Continued in the occupation of the same for the Space of Two years & half & by means thereof he gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & hath a Child Ann Robinson aged Twelve years by his late wife deced

Sworn this. 22. day of September 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes  Samuel Robinson
