Middx & Westmr.}  Robert Gibbeson aged about 30 years on his Oath says he never was Married or Ever rented House or Paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Richard Gammon  Esqr . in Lawrence Poultney Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin Orgars in the City of London upwards of one Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Fourteen Pounds hath Quitted his said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th day of November 1769 before Us R Butler W Kelyngs

Robt. Gibbeson
