Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Honor aged about 67 years upon her Oath saith that about 36 years ago she Intermarried with William Honor her late Husband who has been dead about Twelve years at the Parish Church of Saint Bartholomew by the Exchange That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Gunpowder Alley in the Parish of Saint Brides otherwise Bridget in the City of London for about Seven years at the yearly Rent of Nine Pounds and Paid all Taxes due for the same (save and except the Poor Tax) and that after her said late Husband Quitted said House he never did any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of November 1795 before us N Bond W Kinnard

her Margaret Honor mark
