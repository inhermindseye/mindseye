Middx  Margt. Trogar  Wido . of Angelo Trogar who upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the sd. Angelo Trogar at the Greek Chapel in Change Court upwds. of 30 years ago, and that her said late husband informed this Examinant he lived as a Yearly Servant with his Uncle George Constantine at the Gresian Coffee house in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £12 for one Year & upwards whilst a Singleman which Information this Examinant believes to be true and that neither her Sd. late husband nor this Examinant Since his Decease ever Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 10th. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Margt Trogar
