Middx.  Mary Smith aged abt. 55 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Wm. Smith who dyed the 18th. of Novr. 1757. says her said late husband informed her & which information she verily believes to be true that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Wm. Frostnail a Butcher in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 23d. of Janry. 1758 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Smith
