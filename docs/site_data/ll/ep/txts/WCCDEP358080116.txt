Middlesex & Westminster}  Elizth. Lewis Aged about Seventy Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Geo: Lewis who has been dead Sixteen Years ago and with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London upwards of Thirty Six Years ago and by whom she Two Children now living both of which are provided for Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Croson Court Butcher Row for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Eighteen Years ago since which time her said Husband never Rented House or Tenement or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlemt. neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death.

Sworn this fifth day of Febry. 1777 before R Butler

her Elizth. Lewis Mark
