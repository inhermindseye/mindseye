Middx.  Sarah Williams aged abt. 56 Years lodging at Mr Powells in Hollis Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx, Upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Williams to whom she was Married at May fair Chapel abt. Ten Years ago, says her said husband Rented a house known by the Name of the Ship Alehouse by King Davids Lane Ratcliffe highway in the Parish of St. Paul Shadwell in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £20 for several Years; Quitted said house abt. Seven Years and has not since Rented Ten Pounds by the Year nor paid Parochial Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 13th. day of Decr. 1760 , before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Sarah Williams

Upon Mr. Kinley Complt. J: P:
