Middlesex ss.  Daniel Fishpoole maketh Oath that about Fifty years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to Robert Hall of Burr Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Hatter & served the whole of his said Apprenticeship with his said Master there that he has not since his said Apprenticeship rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn the Day of 1795 before}
