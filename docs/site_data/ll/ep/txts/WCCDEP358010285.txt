Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Veartey aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that on the 29th. day of June 1784 She Intermarried with William Veartey who is gone to the East Indies at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone and by whom she hath one Child living namely Ann aged about fourteen Months that her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Raiksted in Berners Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months and upwards and that after he quitted said Service he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of April 1789 R Butler D Walker

Elizabeth Veartey
