Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr} to wit, Eleanor Bushnell aged thirty eight Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was Married, and further this Examinant on her Oath saith, that she is the Mother of two illegitimate Children Vizt Charlotte aged Nine Years or thereabouts of whom she this Examinant was delivered at the House of a Mr- Griffin a Carpenter situate in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Mary aged Six Years or thereabouts, of whom She this Examinant was delivered at the House of Mr. Millington Situate in Little Shire Lane in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and this Examinant further saith that John Hindson who was formerly a Sheriff Officer & lived in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls but who now keeps a Public House in Propect Row Saint George Fields calld the Gibralter Coffee House is the Real and true Father of such Children named Charlotte and Mary whereof she this Examinant was so delivered as aforesaid the said John Hindson having many times had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinant's Body

Body at some or one of which times he did beget on this Deponents Body such Children as above Stated and further this Deponent saith that She is unable to maintain both her said Children and that they must therefore become both of them Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 11th. Day of January 1791 before Wm Kitchiner }

Eleanor Bushnell

Hindson taken and Bond of Hindson Shangways and Appleyard to Indenre if, the Parish Exted. The Woman & Hindson agreed & she kept the Children.
