Middx  Ann Chunn aged abt. 21 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she never was as Apprentice or Yearly Servant and was born in the parish of St. Clement Danes (as she hath been informed & believes) and that her father Wm. Chunn deceased rented & lived in a house in the said Parish and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the afsd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to Maintain the same when born and that Mr. Richd. Veale  Gent . did beget the sd. Bastard Child or Childn. on her Body the said Rd. Veale having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant sevl. times is the Months of  July & August last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Richd. Veal and no other Person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn April 6th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Chunn
