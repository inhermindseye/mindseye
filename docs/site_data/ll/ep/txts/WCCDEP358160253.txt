Middx The Examination of Elizth- Butler  Singlewoman lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 13th- Day of February 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. upwards of 4 Years ago She lived as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Goodwin in the Parish of St. Helens in the City of Worcester for the Space of 3 Years and upwards at the yearly Wages of two Pounds & ten Shillings & hath not since She left her sd. Services rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife, And this Examinant further on her Voluntary Oath saith that She is Pregnant with Child or Children which when Born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when Born And further saith yt. one Mr. Richard Tisdell in the Temple did begett the said Child or Children on her body She this Examinant at such time liveing as a Servant with one Mrs. Giles Milliner in Fles

and he the said Mr. Tisdell lodging in the same House & comeing home late one Night in the Month of June last locked her in his Room & with Force had Carnall knowledge of her Body at which time he got her with the Bastard Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant And that he the said Richard Tisdell & no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworne the Day and Year above menconed before} W Hayton

elizabeth butler
