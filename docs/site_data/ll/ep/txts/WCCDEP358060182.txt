Middx  Eliz Bennet aged abt. 74 Years upon Oath Saith she is the Wido . of Tho Bennet who dyed abt. 20 years Since and that about 3 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Young at the Yearly Wages of £5 for five Years or thereabouts & lay the last two Years of her sd. Service in her sd. Mistresses house in Fountain Court in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Says she has not Since She Quitted her said Service rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 7th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Bennet
