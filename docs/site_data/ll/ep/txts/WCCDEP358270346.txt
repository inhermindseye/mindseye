Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Sarah Vaughan aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Briggs in Clement Inn for 18 Months at the yearly Wages of 5 Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Wm. Wright a Blacksmith who worked in Liquor Pond Street but has been at Sea ever since May last she not having seen him ever since is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Wright is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Janry. 1798 before me  Gs. Goodenough

her Sarah Vaughan Mark

AW Burbidge Beadle

Father never Apprehended Child born in the house the 6th. day of January 1798 named Sarah Child died in the house sometime in the Month of January 1798
