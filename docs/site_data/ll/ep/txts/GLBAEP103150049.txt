Middlesex ss}  Joseph Darke maketh oath That divers Years ago he rented a house of Mr Thomas Linton (Since deced) of the Yearly Rent or Value of Eight pounds near Great Bandy Legg Walk in the Parish of St Saviour in Southwarke in the County of Surry & lived therein for about the Space of Sixteen Years & that during Such his Residence therein he was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since elsewhere rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or King Taxes or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of August 1775 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

Joseph Darke
