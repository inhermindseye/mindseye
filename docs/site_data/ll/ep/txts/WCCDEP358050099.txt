Middx  Catherine Thompson Wife of Joseph Thompson upon her Oath saith she intermarried with he sd. Joseph Thompson in St. Mary Gate Church near the City of York about Years ago and that her said husband was born in the Parish of Great Osborn in the County of York where his father lived in and rented a house several Years and paid towards the poor rate of the said Parish and that her husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 7th. 1749 before}
