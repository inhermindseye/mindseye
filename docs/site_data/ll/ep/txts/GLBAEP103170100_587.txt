Middlesex ss  Catherine Russell the widow of Joseph Russell deced maketh oath that her said late husband was duly bound & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Robert Bundick in King Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the said County Hair Curler & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That this Deponts husband to her knowledge or belief never rented a house of Ten pounds of a year nor ever Servd any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain anothr Settlement That She was Married to her sd. husband at the Fleet & has not Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 11. of Octobr. 1777 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Catherine Russell
