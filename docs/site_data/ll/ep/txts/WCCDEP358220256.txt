Westminster to wit Mary Williams Aged about fifty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Williams who has been dead about Eight Years Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Blue Stile in Greenwich in the County of Kent upwards of five Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Ten Years ago and hath not since quitting the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 17th. day of April 1771 before}  W Kelynge

Mary Williams
