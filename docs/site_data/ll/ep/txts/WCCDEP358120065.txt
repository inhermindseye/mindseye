Middx  Mary Collins aged 28 yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that near three years ago she was delivered of a female bastard Child at Mr Harr is a Buckle maker in Middle Row in the parish of St. Andw. Holbn. above Barrs Middx. which was baptized Esther and that upwds. of one Year since she was delivered of another female Bastard Child at Mr Lanes in Purple Lane in the parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the same County which was baptized Mary

Sworn 25th. Febry. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Collins
