St Clement Danes 6th July 1786

Middx & Westminster  to wit William Lambert Aged about Thirty four years upon his Oath saith That his Father Richard Lambert  Rented in his Life time rented and Lived Tin a House in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for several Years where this Examinant was Born that his said Father has been Dead Several Years that his said Father never to the best of this Deponent Knowledge gained any other Settlement nor has he this Examint to the best of Knowledge or  belief gained any Settlement in his own Right that he is Sick Poor and unable to Maintain himself

Sworn before me this 6th July 1786 R Butler

William Lambert
