Middx 

Hannah Withey  Wido . of John Withey upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. John Withey at the Parish Church of St. Martin Ludgate upwards of 20 Years ago Says her sd. late husband has been dead about 10 Yrs. Says he Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Chelenor a Taylor in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex as she has been informed & beleives Says neither her sd husband in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examints. Knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 30th. 1747 before  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Hanh. Withey
