Middlesex to wit Richard Collins Maketh Oath and Saith that about Twenty six Years ago he hired a Messuage or Dwelling House in  Tooley Street in the Parish of Saint Olaves in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds that he lived in the said Dwelling House for the Space of Five Years and upwards that he hath not since rented any other House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards served any Parochial Office or done any other act to gain a Subsequent Settlement in Any Parish Precinct or Place whatsoever That he was lawfully married to his late Wife  Margaret at Plymouth in Devonshire by whom he has Issue living one Daughter named Ann aged Twenty One Years or there abouts who was born in the Parish of Saint Olaves aforesaid and is now in the Service of Mrs. Macdonald at the Sign of the two Brewers in Ratcliff Highway .

Sworn this 5 Day of June 1792 before  Rob Smith R Davies Richd. Collings
