Middlesex ss}  Jane Mc Colley the widow of Mathew Mc Colley deced maketh oath That although She hath been married to three different Husbands yet she Cannot at this distance of time recollect or setforth Where they or any or either of them were or was legally Settled Nor Can she this Deponent give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late Father or Mother deced That She this Deponent as she has been Credibly informed & believes was born in Swan Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County that she this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement for herself Save What she may have Acquired by her respective marriages

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Jane Mc Colley
