Middx  Rachel Blay aged about 63 years upon oath says She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Linnell a Butcher  in Claremarket upwards of a year & quitted the same about Eight Years ago  And further says that during the whole time of her said Services She was a Widow without Child or Children And that She hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 26th. Day of April 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Rachel Blay
