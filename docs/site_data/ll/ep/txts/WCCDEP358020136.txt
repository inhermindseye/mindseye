Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Dautree aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Hendy in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Four years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds that she has quitted said Service about Twenty years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of April 1789 R Butler

her Mary Dautree mark
