Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} the vide the other side to wit Margarett Ashburn aged 15 years or thereabout upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Lodging paid Taxes or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant Saith that she was bound Apprentice to one Alexr. Biggs of Newington Green in the Parish of Saint Mary at Newington in the County of Middx  Baker and Chandler by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clement Danes untill she should Attain her Age of 21 years or day of Marriage according to Act of Parliament Says that she lived under such Indre for the space of Six Months or thereabouts and left her said Masters Service on Sunday last Says she never gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 5th day of May 1785  Jno Barnfather  James Croft

her Margarett Ashburn Mark
