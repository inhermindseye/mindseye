Westmr.  Catherine Bamford Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Harding a Painter in Charles Street the Parish of St. Pauls Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of forty Shillings hath quitted her said Service about Ten Years ago and hath not since quitting the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 2d day of February 1771 before us R. Butler W Kelynge

her Catherine Bamford Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham

Error in this Examination being Passed to St. Pauls Covent Garden  instead of St. Mary le Strand and Consent to be delivered up and Mr. Yewd Consented likewise to Accept of the Pauper on being passed 18th. Febry 1771
