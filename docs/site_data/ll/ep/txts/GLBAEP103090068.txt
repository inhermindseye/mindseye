Middlesex ss  Ann Vaun  Widow of Thomas Vaun deced maketh oath That her said husband has informed that he was duly bound apprentice by Indre to one James Dew the parish of of St. John in Hertford in the County of Hertford  Baker and that he Served his said Master for the Sapce of 3 year and upwards there; under the said Indre Which Information this Depont. verily believes to be true And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said late husband never Rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards of served any Parish offices or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement Subsequent to his said apprenticeship and this Depont. Saith that she hath a Child named William aged about Six Years the Lawful Issue of this Deponent by her sd late husband.

Sworn this first Day of July-1767 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

The Mark of Ann Vaun
