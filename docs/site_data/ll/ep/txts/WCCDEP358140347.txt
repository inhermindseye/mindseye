Middx.  Mary Whitaker aged abt. 40 Yrs. Upon Oath says she never was Married and that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Spiring a Taylor in Yeates Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr in the County of Middx. for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5..10..0 and hath Quitted said Service abt. Twelve Years and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 12th. day of Feby. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Whitaker
