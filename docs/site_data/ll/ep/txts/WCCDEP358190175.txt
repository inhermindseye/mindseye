Middx  Mary Dove aged about 60 Years Lodging in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upon Oath saith she is the Widow of James Dove who has been Dead upwards of Four years to whom she was Married at the Tabernacle belonging to the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields about 36 Years ago That her said Husband lived in and Rented a House in King Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for three Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds or thereabouts Quitted the Same about Twenty Years and never after Rented any House or paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate Says she hath not since her Husbands Death Rented any House save one at Hammersmith of Four Pounds a Year for which she paid neither Land Tax or Poor Rate

Sworn the 15th. day of Sepr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Dove
