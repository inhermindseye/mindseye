Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Mallett aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that on the 13th. day of November 1786 she Intermarried with Lewis Mallett her present Husband at the parish Church of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey by whom she hath One Child in living vizt John aged about Fifteen Months That her said Husband Served his time as an Apprentice to Mr. Johnstone in Blackmoor Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of May 1795 . } N Bond

her Ann Mallett Mark

Witness Jas Talboys .
