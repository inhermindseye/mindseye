Middx  Eliz: Holland aged abt. 21 yrs. upon Oath says she intermarried with her husbd. Thos. Holland at the fleet London abt. five years ago says her sd. husbd. served his Apprenticeship to his father Thos. Holland a Plaisterer in Crutched fryans in the Parish of St. Olave Hart Street in the City of London as she hath been informed & believes says her sd. husband never Rented Ten Pounds a yr. paid Parish Taxes or was a hired yearly Servt

Sworn the 16th. day of May 1757 before} Benjamin Cox  Theodr Sydenham

The Mark of Elizth. Holland
