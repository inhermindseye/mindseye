Middx to wit Catherine Riley Aged about Sixty Eight Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes says She lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Riley an Auctioneer in Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and an half at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said service Upwards of Ten Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 16th. Day of Novr. 1780 before  David Walker R Butler

Catrn Riley
