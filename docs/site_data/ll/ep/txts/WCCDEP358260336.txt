Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Cockburn aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she was bound an apprentice to Mrs. Kellam in Bennets Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years that she duly served her said Mistress such Term and that after she quitted her said Mistress she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Septr. 1798 before me  W Goodenough

her Mary Cockburn Mark

Witness H Day Beadle
