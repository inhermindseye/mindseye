Elizth. Wells Aged about Twenty Six years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired yearly servant for One year says her Father James Wells served seven years Apprenticeship with One Nicholas Crope  The Corner of Thames Street in the Parish of Allhallows the great in the City of London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship He never gained any other Settlement then as aforesaid neither hath this Examinant since her said Fathers Death

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1781 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth Wells  Mark
