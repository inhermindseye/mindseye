Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Sprignell the widow of Richd Sprignell deced & who was formerly the widow of James Barnes deced maketh oath That she Can give no Account respecting the Settlements of her said late husbands or of the Settlements of their Several Parents or any or either of them Nor Can this Deponent give any Account touchg the Settlement of her late Parents or either of them That she this Deponent was born in the Parish of Southell in the County of Bedford That she never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself Save What she might have Acquired by marriage with her said late husbands whose respective Settlements are unknown to her

Sworn this 2d day of February 1776 before  Wm.. Blackmore

The Mark of Elizabeth Sprignell
