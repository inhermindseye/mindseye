Middx  Mary Johnson upon Oath saith she is the wife of Peter Johnson (at present in Westmr. Infirmary) to whom she was married at the Fleet London upwds. of 8 Years ago & by whom she has a Child now with her (viz) Frances aged upwds. of 7 years and that she has been Informed & verily believes her sd. Husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr Veredit a Gold & Silver Lace Wenore in Belton Street Long Acres in the Parish of St Giles in the Field in the County of Middx and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant

Sworn Jany. 31st 1753 Before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Johnson
