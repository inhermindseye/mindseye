Middx.  John Darcy aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says; he Intermarried with Mary his wife abt. 28 Years ago; says he Rented a house in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx. for 15 Years at the Yearly Rent of £13 for the first two Years & £15 for the last thirteen Years and hath quitted sd. house abt. fifteen or Sixteen Years,  since which Time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 3d. Feby. 1759 before me} Barth: Hammond

John Dacy
