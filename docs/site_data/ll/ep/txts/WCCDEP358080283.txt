Middlesex  Elizth. Appletree Aged about Sixty four years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Nathl. Appletree who died about Twenty years ago says her said Husband when living Rented and lived in a House in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for Three Years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £16 & paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Thirty years ago and after her said Husband quitted the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 12th. day of Septr. 1778 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Eliz Appletree Mark
