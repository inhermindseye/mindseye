Middx The Examination of Bridgett Thorp  Singlewoman Passed from the Parish of St. Bridgett vulgarly St. Brides  Londn . by Order bearing date the 15th. Day of Decr. Inst. Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith yt. she is Pregnant with Child or Childn. wch when Born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx she being poor & not able to maintaine the same wch Born And further upon her Oath saith yt. one Thos. Lavender  Staymaker did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body she at such time living with her Father Wm. Thorp in the Parish of St. Bridgett aforesd. Staymaker & he the sd. Thos. Lavender at the same time liveing Journeyman with her sd. Father Wm. Thorp there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the lodgings of her sd. Father sevll. times at one of wch times he got her with the Bastard Child or Childn. with wch she is now Pregnt. of And yt. he the sd. Thos. Lavender & no other Person is the time & reall Father thereof And this Examint. further saith that abt. two Years ago her Father lived in & Rented a House of £12 a Year in Angel Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for Nine Months that he hath not since yt. time Rented any other House Paid Parish lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beliefe And yt. she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. to any Person but Continued to live with her sd. Father

Sworne the 18th. Day of Decr. 1739 } Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Bridgt. Thorp
