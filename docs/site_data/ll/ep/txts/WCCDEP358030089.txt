Middx  Mary Milner  Wido . of Henry Milner removed from the Parish of St. Geo: Hanover Square by Order bearing Date the 22d. Day of Janry Instant upon her Oath saith she inter- -married with the sd. Henry Milner at the Fleet London on the 2d. Day of Janry 1731/2 or 1732/3 Says the sd. Henry Milner dyed the 2d. Day of Novr. 1741 by whom she hath one Child (to wit) Francis aged upwards of 9 Years Says the sd. Hen Milner before his Intermarraige with this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Callow a Wharfinger near Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for 14 or 15 Yrs. at the Yearly Wages of £10 as her sd. late Husbd. Informed her Says her sd. late Husband never after he left the sd. Mr Callows Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she hath not since the Death of the sd. Henry Milner

Intermarried with any other Person rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 24th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Mary Milner
