St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Irish Casual Resident in Vere Street (to wit) Margaret Mc Gin aged 74 years or thereabouts Widow Upon Oath & saith that she was Married to Luke Mc Gin abt 30 years ago at the parish Church of St Giles in the fields by Banns that he died on or about the 11th. day of July 1789 that she hath not any Children living by her said Husband that he never acquired any settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief in this Kingdom and that she hath never lived as an hired yearly Servant or been Married since her said Husband's Decease or before she intermarried to him or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever to the best of her knowledge or belief whereby to obtain any Settlement Saith that she now lodges in Vere Street and has lodged in this parish for several years last part and hath not acquired any legal settlement in England to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 13th day of March 1793 . before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Margaret Mc Gin's Mark
