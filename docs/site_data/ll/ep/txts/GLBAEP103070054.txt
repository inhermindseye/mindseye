Middx ss.  Eleanor Bird  Wife of Thomas Bird (who is lately gone away from this Depont. to Parts beyond the Seas) Maketh oath That her sd Husband has informed her that about twenty Seven Years Ago he was bound apprentice to Mrs. SarahWells a Cook who then lived in the Parish of st. Catherine Cree Church in the City of London & duly Served his apprenticeship With her in the sd. Parish And Saith that her sd. Husband Never rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year nor never served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement to the knowledge of belief of this Deponent.

Sworn this 7th day of Decr. 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

Eleanor Bird
