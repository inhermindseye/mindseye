Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Pearson aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Sixth day of January 1790 she Intermarried with John Pearson her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint giles in the fields by whom she hath Four Children living Vizt. Mary aged about Eight years Anna aged about Six years Ann aged about Three years and Eleanor aged about Sixteen Months That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Fullwoods Rents Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Five Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that he hath quitted said house about Eight years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of March 1799 before us } Menter John Collick

Mary Pearson
