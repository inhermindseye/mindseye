Middx  Rebecca Fleming aged about 20 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Christian a french Platemaker in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx for three Years at the Yearly Wages of £3..10..0, and hath quitted said Service about One Month And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to Maintain the same when born, says that John Williams a Journeyman Taylor (who is run away) had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body sometimes in the Month of April last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Williams and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 3d. Augst. 1765 . Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Rebecca Fleming
