Westmr. 

Mary Shirmon  Wido . of Wm. Shirmon who dyed upwards of three Years ago upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Wm. Shirmon in Trinity Church in the City of Coventry about 18 or 19 Years ago Says her sd. late husband (whilst a Singleman) hired a Yearly Servt. with the Dutchess of Monmouth four Years & upwds. at the Yearly wages of Five Pounds and during the time of his sd. Service lay in Brewers Street or Jar man Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. Says that in then her sd. late husband after he Quitted his sd. Service nor has this Examinant since his Death rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 21st. 1748 before} J Coulson Dan Gaels

Mary Shirman
