Middlesex ss}  Margaret Perry maketh oath that sometime ago she was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with one Nicholas Foy a Victualler in the Hamlet of Mile End new Town in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such Service under such hiring for the Space of two years & half & has not lived as a hired Servant for the Space of year elsewhere nor was ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere That She hath two Children namely Elizabeth Hawkins aged about Six years & Sarah Hawkins aged about three years both of whom were born in the Parish of St Mary White Chapel in the sd County & there baptised & registred & which sd Children are Bastards by reason that this Deponent was never married to their Father Edward Hawkins

Sworn this 25 day of August 1783 before R Davies J Hawley

The Mark of Margaret Perry
