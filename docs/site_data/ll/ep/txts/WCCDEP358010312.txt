Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex ss  Robert Coney aged 30 Yrs. or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That about 10 Yrs. ago he intermarried with Ann his present wife at the parish Church of St. Mildred in the City of London by License that they were married between the Hours of 8 & 9 that there were present at the time, the clerk, the Pew opener & a Lad who lived fellow servant with his sd. wife that he lived as a hath four Children by his sd. viz Robert aged 9 yrs. ann 6 yrs Sarah 4 Yrs & Charles aged 15 Months that he never served any apprenticeship that he lived as a hired yearly servant at the Hope in Mitre Court Fleet street for one Yrs. & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four pounds & That afterwards he kept a House No. 2 in Great shire Land for 6 Months and upwards at and under the yearly Rent of £12 that he paid Taxes that since he quitted that House he has never done any act matter or thing whereby he way have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 4th. Novr. 1789 before  R. Butler

Robt Coney
