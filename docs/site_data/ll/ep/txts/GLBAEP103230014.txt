Middlesex ss}  Jane Cooper the widow of Benjamin Cooper deced maketh oath that She has been informed by her sd late husband & which she believes to be true that her sd late husband was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to & Served the whole thereof with Joseph Hemingin the Parish of St Bololph witht Aldgate in the said County Bucklemaker and did not afterwards to this Deponts Knowledge or belief rent a house of Ten Pound a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Apprenticesp nor hath She this Depont Since his death lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year or been married or done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself and that She was lawfully married to her sd late husband

Sworn this 5. day of February 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Jane Cooper
