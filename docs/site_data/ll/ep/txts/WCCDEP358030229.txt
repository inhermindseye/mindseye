Middx  Mary Green upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman & was never married Says she was bound an Apprentice to & Served 4 Years Apprentiship with Mrs. Moore a Lace Joyner in Holborn in the parish of St. Andrew in Holborn London & hath been out of her time abt. 7 Yrs. Says she never was a Apprentice Yearly Servt. for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 16 1746 before  Wm. Jones

Mary Green
