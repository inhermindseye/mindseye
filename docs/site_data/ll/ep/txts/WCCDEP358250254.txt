Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Bolton aged about 58 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Pinkney in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Five years at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Twenty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained or subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of Octr. 1796 } John Hatchers

her Mary Bolton Mark
