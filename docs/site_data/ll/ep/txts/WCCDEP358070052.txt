Westmr. to wit John Wright Aged about Twenty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West London about Four Years ago and by whom he hath one Child now living which this Examinant now Provides for Says he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. George Mynitt a Taylor in St. James Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn This 21st. day December 1772 before  John Goodchild R Butler

John Wright
