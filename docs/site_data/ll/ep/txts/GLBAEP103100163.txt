Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Preston  widow of Robert Preston  mariner deced maketh oath That She has been informed by her Said late husband (& which information this Deponent believes to be true) That her said husband was born in the in the County of Kent and that he also Served an apprenticeship in the Sea Service but with whom he never informed her. That about Seventy four Years ago She was lawfully married to her said late husband in t he Parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate London That to her Knowledge information or belief her said husband never rented a house of the Yearly Rent or value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish offices nor Paid any Scavengers Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to her knowledge information or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right (Save What he might Acquire by his Birth and Apprenticeship as aforesaid. That this Depont. Cannot Set forth or discover the Settlement of her Said late husbands Parents or either of them & That She this Deponent Since her Said husbands death hath not rented a house of the Yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to her knowledge or belief hath She done any Act Since her Said husbands Death to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this day of 1769 before}
