Saint Clement Danes

Westminster } to wit Margaret Hewer aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that about Three years ago she Intermarried with Israel Hewer her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath no Child living. That she hath been informed by her said husband which information she verily believes to be true That he was bound an Apprentice to one Samuel Loftus in Charlotte Street Portland  Place in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for Seven years during which time he boarded and lodged in his said Masters house and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement & saith that he is now chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn this 28th. day of July 1798 before us } R. Ford Edwd: Naish

her Margt. Hewer Mark

Witness AW Burbidge
