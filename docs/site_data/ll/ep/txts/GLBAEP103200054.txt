Middlesex ss}  Mary Tyler  Singlewoman Aged about Thirty Years maketh oath that She was born in the Parish of Allhallows the Great in the City of London and never lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year in any one Place nor was ever bound an Apprentice nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right & this Depont hath been informed by her Father Harvey Tyler & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with a Person whose name She Can't now recollect at the Corner of Dowgate Hill in the said Parish Farrier .

Sworn this 3d- day of October 1783 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Mary Tyler
