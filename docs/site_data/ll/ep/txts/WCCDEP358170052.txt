Middx  The Examination of Sarah Thacker  Wido . of Jno. Thacker in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes taken the 9th. Day of May, 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that on the 4th- Day of May 1736 She Intermarried with her sd. late husband Jno. Thacker & that her sd. Husband was by Indenture dated the 31st- Day of Decr. 1729 bound an Apprentice to & served 5 Yrs. Apprentiship with one Mr. Geo: Cleter in ye. Parish of St. Andw- Holbourn above Barrs in the County of Middx & further upon her Oath saith that neither her late husband in his Life Time nor she since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & hath two Children (namely) Ann aged abt. 3 Yrs. & Thos . aged abt- 2 Weeks & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & them

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane W Hayton

The Mark of Sarah Thacker
