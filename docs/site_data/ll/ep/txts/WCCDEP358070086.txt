Westmr. to wit Samuel Collins Aged about Twenty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Catherine his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes upwards of One Year and an half ago Says he Served Two Years and an half the latter Part of his Apprenticeship with Henry Lewes a Copper Plate printer in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster when this Examinant was Discharged from his said Apprend. by Consent before Sir  John Fielding  Knight about Six Years ago and Since which time this Examinant has never Rented House paid Taxes or dene any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 3d. day of April 1773 before me R. Butler

Saml Collins
