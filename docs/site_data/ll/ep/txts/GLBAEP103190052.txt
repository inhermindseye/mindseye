Middlesex ss}  Dorothy Barker  Wife of James Barker maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her at Sea That She hath been informed by her sd husbands Father & which She believes to be true that her said husband was duly bound to & Served three Years & upwards of his Apprenticeship with his Uncle William Barker in the Parish of Bilson in the County of Stafford  Buckle Chaffe Filer & to her knowledge or belief her Said husband has not Since done any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She hath three children namely Sarah Barker Aged about Six Years Mary Barker aged about Nineteen Months both of whom were born in Cock Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & are both Bastards by reason this Deponent was not then Married to her Sd husband and Jane Barker Aged about eight Months who was born in the Same Place the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom She was then lawfully married

Sworn this 12 day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Dorothy Barker
