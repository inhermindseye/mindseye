Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Lee Read of No. 11 New Square Lincolns on behalf of Wm. Read an Infant of the age of 10 years or thereabouts (this Exts. Nephew) upon his Oath saith that Jas. Read this Exts. Brother & Father to the said Wm. Read Rented and lived in a House in Tothill Street Westmr. in the Pish of St. Margarett in the Liberty of Westmr . for 12 Months at the yearly Rent of £10 that he di in the said House whereby he did no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th- day of July 1787  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordons

Lee Read
