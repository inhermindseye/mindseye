Middx  Ann Chambers aged abt. 27 years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Jacob Rempell a Ginger bread baker in Blackmoor Strt. in the Parish of St. Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex at the yearly wages of £3. for three years & upwards quitted her sd. Service abt. 18 months and has not since rented ten Pounds a year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant  for one year And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith She is Pregnant wth. Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Rt. Pool a Taylor did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. Rt. Pool having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinant sevl. time in the Months of February & March last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. bastard Child

or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the sd. Robt. Poole & no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn June 19th 1755 before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Ann Chambers
