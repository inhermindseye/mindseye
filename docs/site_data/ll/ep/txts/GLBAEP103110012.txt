Middlesex ss}  Sarah  Webster the now wife of John Webster mariner Maketh oath That She heretofore was lawfully Married to one George Ord lately deceases by whom She had two Children who are Still living Namely Thomas Michael Ord Aged about and Sarah Margaret Ord Aged about neither of whom have as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement in his or her own right That Since the death of her Said late husband Ord She hath been lawfully Married to her Present husband Webster who is a Scotchman by birth as he has informed her & who as She verily believes is now at Sea on board his Majestiesship Salisbury by whom She hath also a Child now living named John Webster aged about That She this Depont. Cannot in any wife Set forth or discover the place of Settlement of her said late former husband Ord nor whether her Said Present husband Webster ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt. in England in his own right or ever derived any Settlement there But this Depont. Saith That her late Father Richard Hoare deced divers Years ago rented a house of the yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds in the Parish of St. Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry & was also Charged to & paid the Poors Rates for the Same at which time She this Depont. lived with him as part of his Family & by means thereof She (as advised) derived a Settlement there from him & She hath not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She might Acquire by her Marriages as aforsaid and that her said late Father did not do any Act to gain a Settlement for himself and Family Subsequent to his living in the Parish of Saint Mary Rotherhith as aforesd. He upon leaving that Parish went & refered in parts beyond the Seal.
