Westmr.  Mary Carroll Wife of Wm. Carroll upon Oath saith she was Inter married with to the sd. Wm. Carroll on the 25th. Day of July 1739 in the house of Jno. Carven in Bullen Court Maiden Lane by Mr. Doner a Priest of the Church of Rome Says her sd. husband whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Paul Garsey at the Horseshoe and Magpye in Drury Lane for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds Dyet washing and Lodging and left his sd. Service upwds. of 12 Yrs. ago since which time he has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained

a Settlement in any other Places to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 5th. 1749 before 

Mary Carroll
