Middx & Westmr.}  John Evans Aged about Sixty Nine Years on his Oath Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Henry Frame a Taylor in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of forty Years ago and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 2d day of July 1770 before me } Tho Lane

John Evans
