Middx.  Martha Griffiths aged abt. 34 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Griffiths who has been dead abt. Nine Months by whom she has Two Children Elizabeth aged three Years and Wm : aged abt. three Months, says she has been informed & which Information she verily believes to be true that her said husband served four Years & a half the first part of his Apprenticeship with his father Wm: Griffiths a Plaisterer in Rose Street by Covent Garden in the parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and then his father died says her said husband Never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes to her knowledge or belief

Sworn May 24th. 1760 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Martha Griffiths

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Ponten J: P:
