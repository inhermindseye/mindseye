Middx & Westmr.}  James Wilson Aged about 28 Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says his Father Wm. Wilson Rented and lived in a House in Hare Court Aldersgate Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldersgate London for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of £10 & upwards & paid parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House upwards of 10 Years ago and never rented House afterwards or over gained any other subseqt. Settlemt. neither hath this Examinant since his said Fathers Death.

Sworn this Day of January 1777.}
