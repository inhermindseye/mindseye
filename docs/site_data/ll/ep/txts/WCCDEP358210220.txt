Westmr.  Margt. Mayberry aged about 19 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married nor ever rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says she was born in the Parish of Bewdley in Worcestershire and can give as Account of the Settlement of her Father or Mother, And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 21st, day of November last she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Poole in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath been baptized Ann and is now living and chargeable to the said Parish, says that Robert Duncomb who is now an Apprentice to Mr. Dry a Carpenter & Undertaker at Greenwich in Kent did beget the said female Bastard Child on her Body, of which she was delivered as aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the Month of January 1767, and at several other times afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of her said female Bastard Child, and that the said Robert Duncomb and no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn 24th. December 1767 . before} 

Margaret Maybury
