St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St. Alphage London  (to wit) James Edwards Aged about thirty Nine years Upon Oath saith that he Intermarried with his present Wife Elizabeth at the Old Church on Snow Hill Birmingham about twelve years ago by whom he hath now living two Children Namely Sarah Aged Seven years or thereabouts and William Aged One year five Months and upwards. That previous to his Marriage and while he was single not having any Child or Children he lived as an hired yearly Servant in the Capacity of Peter Warehouseman with Mesn. Lloyd of Territt at the Corner of Hart Street & Wood Street in the parish of St. Alphage in the City of London for Six years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £20 and since hath not by any means wtsoever acquired any other legal Settlement And Lastly that about Eight years ago being removed from Windsor to the said parish of St. Alphage as his last legal settlement was relieved by the said parish of St. Alphage

Sworn the  8th. Augst. 1791 before R Butler D Walker

James Edwards
