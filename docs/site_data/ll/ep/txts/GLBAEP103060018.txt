Middx  Robert Hare Maketh Oath that About Twenty Sixteen Two years ago he was a hired a Servt . by the Year to Mrs. Harston in Clergy Street  then in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middx Since by Act of parliamt. called the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in Middx and Served her as such there One whole Year and upwards And has gained no Subsequent Settlemt to his knowledge and that he hath A Wife Named Ann

Sworn this 9th Janry 1743 before. Robert Dennett

Robert. Hare
