Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} to wit Elizabeth Simons aged 49 years and upwards upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Abington in Beaufort Bints in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster at the yearly wages of £8.8. - that she quitted his service about 13 years ago and has not since acquired a gained any subsequent legal Settlement And that she is now Po and unable to maintain herself.

Sworn the 5th. day of Novr. 1791 Before me  Wm Hitchiner

Eliz Simons
