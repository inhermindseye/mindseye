Middx & Westmr.}  George Edwards aged about forty seven Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented house or paid Taxes Says he was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Saml. Willard an Oil man and Colour Man in Maiden Lane Covent Garden in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . and served his said Master upwards of six Years under the same Indres when his Master gave him up the same since which time this Examinant never gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. Day of Jany 1777 R Butler John Croft

George Edwards
