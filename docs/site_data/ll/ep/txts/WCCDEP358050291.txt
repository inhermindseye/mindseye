Westmr. 

Martha Leary  Wido . of Danl. Leary passed from the parish of St. Martin in the Fields with her two Children John aged upwds. of 7 Yrs. & Margt . aged abt. 16 months by Order dated the 2d. of this Inst. August upon Oath says she was married to the sd. Danl. Leary  at a House opposite the Cole Yard in Drury Lane by a Priest of the Church of Rome on the 29th. of March 1741 that her sd. late husband was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England and further Saith that her own mother Ann Peacock deced after the Death of this Examints. father & whilst she Continued a Wido. lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £24 in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for the Space of two Years and that she this Examinant at the age of 12 Yrs. or there abt. was put by her sd. late Mother to one Mrs. Mayners a School mistress in Bedford bury to learn French and Plainwork for which her mother gave the sd. Mrs. Mayners Six Guineas & this Examinant was to live with the sd. Mayners three years

& her sd. mistress was to provide her Dyat Lodging and Washing during that time but she cannot remember whether any Indentures were Signed between them that she lived under this Agreement with the sd. Mrs. Mayners near one Year & then her mother took her away and that she never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant or did any Act (except as aforesaid) where by to gain a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 4th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

Martha Learey
