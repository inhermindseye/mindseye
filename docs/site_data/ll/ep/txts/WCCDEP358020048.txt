Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr. to Wit Elizabeth Thorp aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that about 7 years age she Intermarried with Jas. Thorp her late Husband (who has been dead about 9 years Months) at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London that she hath no Child or Children living that her said late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examint. and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Sayer in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 18 Months or thereabouts at the yearly wages of £17 that he had quitted said Service about 7 years since which time he never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Septr. 1787 R Butler

Elizabeth Thorp
