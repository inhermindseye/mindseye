Westmr.  Ann Bolan aged about 56 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Lady Sanderson in Beaufort Buildings in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx , for Nine Years of thereabts. at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds, quitted said Service about five and twenty Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Augst. 27th. 1765 . before} Fran Cary

Ann Bolan
