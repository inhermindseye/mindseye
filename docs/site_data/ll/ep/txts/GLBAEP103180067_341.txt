Middlesex ss}  Mary Day  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago her Father John Day rented a house in Goodmans Yard in the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the Sd County & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the same as She this Depont. has Been informed by her Mother & Which She believes to be true & at which time this Deponent lived with him there as part of his Family That to this Deponts knowledge or belief her Said Father has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Depont never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant by the year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself

Sworn this 28th day of Septemr 1778 before W Blackmore Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Mary Day
