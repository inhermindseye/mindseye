Middlesex ss}  Thomas Mc Nelly maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre to John Pantree of Bear binder Lane in the Parish of St Mary Woolchurch in the City of London Butcher & after having served his sd Master there for the Space of four Years he was in pressed into his Majesties Service on board a Man of War & never afterwards returned to his sd Mar That since such his Apprenticeship he has not Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 11. day of March 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Thomas Mc Nelly
