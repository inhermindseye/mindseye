Middx ss  Rebecca Whalley Aged about Thirty One years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Rowland Jones an Attorney in Hamil Street Hyde Park Corner in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for fifteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds being in the Capacity of a Wett Nurse hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two years and an half ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained any or Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Decr. 1775 before  Wm Kitchiner

Rebecca Whaley
