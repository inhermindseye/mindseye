Middx  Edy Featum  wife of John Featum (who is now at Spithead head  on Board his Majestys ship Prince George ) Maketh Oath and saith that about thirteen years ago her said husband was bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven years to one Mr  Simon Card who then lived in Hagiston near Hackney Middx and lived with his said Master there some time and then his said Master removed to Black fryers where Her said husband lived some time with his said Master after which his said Master removed to White Cross Alley in Middle Moore fields in the parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middx and served his said Master his Apprentice in the said last mentioned parish about one year and then his said Master died And that after the death of his Said Master he continued the Remainder of his Apprentiship wth. his sd. Masters Son Painter Card in the said last mentioned parish And that she hath two children by her sd husband Elizth . three years & Jno . one year

Sworn 15th Janry 1745 before Robert Dennett

her Edy Featum  mark
