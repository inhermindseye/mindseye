Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Pearson aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 6th. day of January 1790 she Intermarrd. with John Pearson her present husband who is Now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields by whom she hath Five Childn. living Vizt. Mary aged about Eight years Anna aged about Six Years Ann aged about Three years and Eleanor aged about Sixteen Months That her said Husband lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Abbott in Lincolns Inn fields but at what Wages she this Deponent doth not know that her said Husband during such Service Slept over the Stables in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that her said Husband deserted her about Three Months since and where he is now she this Deponent doth not know

Sworn this 15th. day of March 1799 before us G S Goodenough

Mary Pearson
