Middlesex ss}  Susannah Fellows the widow of Thomas Fellows deced maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house in Church Yard Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement nor hath She this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Susannah Fellows
