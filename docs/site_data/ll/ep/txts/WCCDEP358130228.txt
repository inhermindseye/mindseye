Middx.  Sarah Fielding aged abt. Thirty Seven Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Alexr. Fielding deced by whom She hath two Children (to wit) Frances aged abt. five Years & Letitia aged abt. three Years Says her said late Husband was born in the Strand opposite Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. & that her said late Husband in his life time nor She this Examinant since his Death never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 27 day of October 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Fielding
