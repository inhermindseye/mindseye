Middx  The Examination of Richd. Evett on the behalf of Ann Baggs Widw . of Wm. Baggs Deced Taken the 31st. Day of March 1739 Who upon his Oath saith yt. ye. sd. Wm. Baggs before his Death Rented a House in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £17 or thereabts. & lived in the same for the Term of 17 Years or thereabts & there Dyed And this Examint. further saith that the said Ann Baggs hath not since the Death of her sd. Husband Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinant's Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane

Richd. Evatt
