Middlesex ss}  Sarah Bruce the wife of John Bruce maketh oath that her said husband is by birth a Prussian as he has informed her and never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in England That previous to her marriage with her said husband She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain wages by & With Mr Bradley a Victualler in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & Continued in such Service under such hiring there for the space of two years and has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Save what She may have Acquired by her marriage as aforesd and that her said husband is now absent from her

Sworn this 24. day of June 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Bruce
