Westmr.  George Knight aged about 62 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in St. Clement's Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds lived therein upwards of two Years and Quitted same about Twenty Eight Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 14th day of Decr. 1765 , before} Paul Vaillant

George Knight
