Middx  Eliz. Hale aged 29 Years Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that abt. 1 Year ago She lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Tanner at the Anoydyne Necklace in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £4. & that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented £10 a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 23rd. of July 1742 before}
