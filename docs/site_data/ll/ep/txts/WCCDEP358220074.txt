Middlesex & Westmr }  Mary Field aged about forty Years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Merigot late of Skinner Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph London Clockmaker deceased upwards of Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about five Years ago and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. day of October 1769 before Us R. Butler W Kelyngs

Mary Feild
