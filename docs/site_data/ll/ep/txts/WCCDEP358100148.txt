Middx to wit Susannah Lewis Aged about Sixty one years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Danl. Lewis her late Husband deceased at May fair Chapple about 35 years ago And by whom she hath four Children being all of whom are now Provided for Says her said Husband rented and hired in a House in Hart Street  Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . for sevl. years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards hath quitted said House upwards of Thirty years ago and after quitting the same He never gained any other Settlemt. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year Paying Taxes or or.wise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 8th. day of Octobe Novr. 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Susannah Lewis mark
