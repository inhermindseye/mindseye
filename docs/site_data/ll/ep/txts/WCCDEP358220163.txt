Westmr  Thomas Brees Aged about Sixteen Years on his Oath says he never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says he was bound Apprentice to and served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Thomas Tattenham a Chimney Sweeper in One Tun Alley in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 14th. day of July 1770 before Us  W Kelyngs Petr Planck

his Thos. Brees Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
