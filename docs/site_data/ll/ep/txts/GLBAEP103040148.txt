Middlesex ss. The Voluntary examination of Sarah Butterfield of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Singlewoman taken on Oath before one Rice Davies  Esqr . one of His Majesty's Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Twenty fourth Day of October in the Year of our Lord One Thousand Seven Hundred & Ninety eight

Who saith that she is now Pregnant with one or more with Child or Children and that the said Child or Children is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate and that George Longlands Apprentice to Mr. Pope of King Street Tower Hill in the said Parish Button Maker is the Father of the Said Bastard Child or Children

Taken and signed the Day and Year above written before R Davies

Sarah Butterfield
