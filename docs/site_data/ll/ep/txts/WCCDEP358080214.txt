Westmr- to wit Eleanor Coleman aged about sixty Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Cornelius Coleman who has died about two Years ago with whome she Intermarried in the County of Corke in the Kingdom of Ireland about forty Years ago and by whom she has two Children now living as she believes both of which are now provided for Says her said husband Rented and lived in a house in Wych Street in the parish of St- Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . for twelve months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of £31 hath quitted the said house about four Years ago since which time she never gained any other subseqt- Settlement either by renting house paying Taxes or orwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said husbands Death

Sworn this 13th- Day of Febry 1778 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Eleanor Coleman Mark
