Middx & Westmr.}  Susannah Kay aged about 43 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father John Kay before his Intermarr. with her said late Mother and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Esqr- in Cork Street Burlington Garden in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for 2 Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £10 as she believes married her said late Mother from the abovementd. place and after quitting the said last mentioned place he never gained any other subsequent Settlement to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 29th- Day of March 1777 before me.}

her Susanh- Kay Mark.
