Middx The Examination of Sarah the Wife of Jas. Hart Passed from the Parish of St. Peter in the East in the City & County of Oxford by Order bearing Date the 9th. Day of Novr. Inst. Who upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband Jas. Hart was bound an Apprentice to one Mrs. Coster Perukemaker in the Parish of St. Peter in the East in ye. City & County of Oxford aforesd. Wido. for the Term of Eight Years & Served her as an Apprentice for the space of Seven Years & upwds. & never was assigned over to any Person for the remainder of his sd. Apprentiship as her sd. Husband informed her & as she verily beleives And further saith yt. her sd. Husband Jas. Hart upwds. of one Year ago lived in & Rented a Ground Floor in ye. House of one Mr. Bruce in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £10 & lived in ye. same for the space of one Year & upwds. yt. the sd. House was let out into Eight Apartmts. that there was but one Street Door to ye. sd. House wch all the Tents. were obliged to go in & out at that the Water Yard & Privy were for the use of ye. Sevll. Tents. in Comon that they never were Rated or Assessed but yt. ye. sd. Mr. Bruce ye. Landlord Paid all Taxes in Generll. wch was Rated or Assessed on the sd. House And this Examint. further upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband hath not since he left his sd. Mistress Rented £10 a year (same as aforesd.) Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife That her sd. Husband now absconds from her & haveing one Child (to wit) Sarah aged one Year & upwds. and being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Child

Sworne ye. 17th. Day of Novr. 1739 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Sarah Hart
