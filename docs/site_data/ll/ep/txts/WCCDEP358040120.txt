Westmr.  Ruth Cheals  Widow of John Cheals upon Oath saith her said late husband John Cheals lived in and rented a house in the Back of St Clemts. in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £18 for one Year & upwds. & last the same abt. 15 Years ago Says her sd. husband never after be quitted the sd. house nor has this Examinant since his Death rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has a Son named Thos. aged upwds. of fourteen Yrs.

Sworn Decr. 11th. 1747 before} Tho Lane .

Ruth Cheals
