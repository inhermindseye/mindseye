Middlesex ss  Edward Mason maketh oath that about Forty years ago he was hired and lived as a hired Servant  at Certain Wages by & with Thomas Smith a Butcher  in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year and upwards That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish Office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 4. day of July 1788 before  R Davies

Edwd. Mason
