Saint Clement Dane

Middlesex to wit Sarah Jaquet aged about 55 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Peter Jaquet her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Luke Old Street about 30 years ago that her said Husband a has been dead about about 18 years that her said late Husband about 7 years before his Death rented he took part of a House in Crown Court Batcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for which he paid £10 a year that he kept same till the day of his Death that this Ext. has done no act to gain a Settlement since her said Husbands Death and further this Ext. upon her Oath Saith that Abraham Cahais this Exts. Brother about 6 years ago went to Sea and has not since been heard of that he served his time as an Apprentice to Abraham Cahais this Exts. Father in Crown Court afsd. that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he left his Child behind him vizt. Sarah aged about 12 years which

was born in Crown Court Afsd. that said Child has never been placed out Apprentice

Sworn this 19th. day of Febry. 1788 R Butler

her Sarah Jagues Mark
