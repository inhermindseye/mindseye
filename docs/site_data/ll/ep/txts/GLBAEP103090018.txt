Middlesex ss  Esther Williams the sister of Ann Turnay who is a single woman Maketh oath That her said Sister Turnay lately lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages with one Mr William Henson in the Parish of Saint Michael in Crooked Lane in the City of London  Dry Salter and Continued in such service for above the Space of a Year And by means thereof as this Deponent is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that her said sister to this Deponts. knowledge information or belief hath not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And that her said sister is disordered in her Mind and is likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate Middlesex

Sworn this 14th. day of December 1765 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Hester Williams
