Middx  John Vicary of the parish of St Clemt Danes on behalf of Mary Stanley an Infant aged abt. 7 Yrs. upon Oath saith he has made Enquiry concerng. the Settlemt. of the sd. Infant & has been informed that she is the Daughter of Benj. Stanley by Mary his Wife (who both abscond from her) and that the sd. Benj. Stanley lived a Yearly Servant whilst a Singleman at the White horse Alehouse in the Haymarket in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year & Six months & has not since he quitted that Service rented Six pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn May 7th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell  Tho: Lane J: Vicary
