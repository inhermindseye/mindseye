Middx  Richard Heath aged about Sixty Years on his Oath says he is a Widower and hath no Child or Children living says he Rented and lived in a House in Blackmore Street Clare Markett at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said Ho about Six Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise Howsoever

Sworn this 28th day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

his Richd: Heath Marks
