Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Gould aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Lee in Arrundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service about 4 years Ago since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Richard Oakfoot a Waiter at No. 5 in Oxendon Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of March last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Richard Oakfoot is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 11th. day of Novr. 1788 before } M Addington

her Mary Gould Mark

Mary Gould lives at Mrs. Dunn a Pattern Maker in New Inn Passage

Richard Oakfoot is a Tall Thin Man fair Complexion pitted with the Small Pox a Water at Shaws No. 5 Oxendn. St. Man taken & Committed afterwards Disd. upon giving Secry. to Pay 2d: P week

Mr. Shaw Oxendon St. who keeps a Gaming He gained in the Note

Child Christened by the Name of Mary Gould born 12th: Novr. 1788

This Woman has a Child at Enfield named Chas. Gould illegitimate if she comes again No favor

Child dead
