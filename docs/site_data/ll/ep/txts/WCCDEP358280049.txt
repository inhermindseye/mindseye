Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Margarett Bell aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Richard Bell her Uncle in Stanhope Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 16 Months at the yearly Wages of £4..10..0 hath quitted said service about Three years ago since which time she hath done no Act to gain any Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And that one Thomas Vobe a Waiter who lately lived at the Devil Tavern in Fleet Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of April last and at Several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that the said Thomas Vobe is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 30th. Day of Novr. 1786 before} Wm Kitchiner

her Margarett Bell mark
