Middx  Margt Cowen Wife of Wm Cowen  gone from her Aged abt 44 Years Upon Oath Says that she Intermarried with her said husband in the Parish Church of Warborough in the Kingdom of Ireland Says her husband is also a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England that he Rented a house in the Parish of St Mary in the City of Dublin and lived therein upwards of Six Years and Paid all Taxes and hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes to the best of her knowledge and belief

Sworn the 9th Day of June 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Margt. Cowen
