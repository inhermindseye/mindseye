Middx 

Cath. Holmes  wife of Robert Holmes (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Robert Holmes on or abt. the 8th. Day of January 1738 in the Parish Church of St. Giles in Newcastle in the County of Stafford Says her sd. husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Joseph Whiston a Silk dyer near Exeter Exchange in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath 3 Childn. (Viz) Robt. aged upwds. of Six Years Mary aged upwds. of four Years & Richd. aged upwds. of one Year & is poor and not able to maintain them

Sworn 2d. March 1746 before  Tho:Burdus

Catherin Holmes
