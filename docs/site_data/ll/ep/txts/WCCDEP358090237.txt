Middx to wit Jane Redman Aged about Forty years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Samuel Redman who died about Nine years ago and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn about Seventeen years ago and by whom she hath One Child living Namely Ann Elizabeth Aged about Twelve years (now provided for) says her said Husband was an Irishman and never gained a settlement in England to her knowledge or belief neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the Twenty Seventh day of April last she was delivered of Two Female Bastard Children in the House of Mr. Stables a Taylor in Devereux Court near Temple Barr in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which have since been Baptized by the Names of Rachel Mary and Susannah Catherine and are now become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same And further says that One John Foster late an Ironmonger and who now Lodges at One Mr. Smarts in Scotland yard is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of Her Body at Different times for near four years past at One of which times He got her with Child of the Two Bastard Children of which she was delivered as aforesaid And that the said John Foster and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of July 1781 before }
