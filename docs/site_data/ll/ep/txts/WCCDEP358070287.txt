Westr to wit Mary Dawney  Singlewoman Aged Sixty Years and upwards upon her Oath Says she never was Married was an Apprentice or Rented a House of Ten pounds a Year or lived a Yearly hired Servant for a Year Says her Father Jas Dawney Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Black - A Shoemaker in the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate in the City of London where Name she Says her said Father about Two Years after he was out of his said Apprenticeship went for a Soldier and was in Colonol Honeywoods Company for many Years and died in Chelsea College or Hospital and never after after his said Apprenticeship Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, as her said father hath often informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true.

Sworn this 17th day of August 1775 before  Sampn Wright T: Heslove

her Mary Dawney Mark Paid 2s Oaths 2s Witness James Viger
