Westmr.  Rowland Richardson upon Oath saith he intermarried with his wife Ann Richardson about fifteen Years ago in the parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields by whom he hath two Children Sarah aged upwds. of 5 Years & John Rowland aged near 2 Years and that he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Shoepine or Fan Stick maker & Lived the last Year of his time with the sd. Mr. Shoepine at the Corner of Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one yr.

Sworn Novr. 25th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Rowland Richardson
