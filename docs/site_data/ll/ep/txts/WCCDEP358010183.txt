Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Stone aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with William Stone her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about 4 years ago that this Examinant has been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that her said Husband was born in Drury Lane opposite Brownlow Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex that he has done on act to the best of this Examinants knowledge to gain any other Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Janry. 1788  Sampr Wright Maddington

Mary Stone
