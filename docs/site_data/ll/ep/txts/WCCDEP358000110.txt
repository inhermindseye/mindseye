Saint Clement Danes September 20th. 1785

Middlesex 

passd. to St Giles's in the Fds. (to wit) Mary Beardmore  Widow upon her Oath saith that she was married to Robert Beardmore her late Husband by a Protestant Minister according to the Rites of the Church of England at in the Kingdom of Ireland that since their intermar- -riage her said Husband and she rented and lived in a House Shop and Parlour part of a House situate in Great Earle Street Seven Dials in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields at the Yearly Rent of twelve pounds per Annum for about three years that the said Tenement was unfurnished, that since quitting the said House her said Husband in him life time nor this Examinant since his Death have not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to gain a Settlement subsequent to Renting the said Hence in Earl Street Seven Dials aforesaid

Sworn this 26th Septr 1785 before  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordon

Mary Beardmore
