Middx  Edward Edwards aged abt. 56 Years upon his Oath saith he lived in & rented a house in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds for the Space of four Years or thereabts. & hath left the same about 29 Yrs. & hath not since that time rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 14th. 1745 before Wm. Jones Edwad Edwards
