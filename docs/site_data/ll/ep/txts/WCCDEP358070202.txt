Middlesex & Westmr.}  Alice Allan Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Nelson an Oilman in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds since which time she has never done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Captain  Wm. Richards of the 66 Regiment of Foot and who is now at Edinburgh had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House on the Sixteenth or Seventeenth day of December last at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said William Richards and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 27th. day of July 1774 before  William Addington .

her Alice Allan Mark
