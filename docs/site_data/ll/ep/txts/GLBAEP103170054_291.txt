Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Orranger the widow of Edward orranger deced maketh oath that divers years ago & after her said late husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain wages by & with Thomas Jurynam of nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said county Distiller & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year nor been since married or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain another Settlement.

Sworn this. 12. day of Febry. 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Elizabeth Orranger
