Middx  Sarah Nixon  Wido . of James Nixon upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her sd. late Husband James Nixon on or abt. the 8th Decr. 1723 & that about Sixteen Years ago her sd. late Husband lived in and rented a House in the Fore Street by the Strand  in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and upwards for the Space of one Year or thereabts. that her sd. late Husband never after in his Life Time rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes nor hath this Examinant since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 29th. Day of October 1741 before}

Sarah Nixon
