Middlesex  Elizth. Steele aged about fifty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Thomas Steele who died about Eleven Months ago with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields about Nineteen Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said late Husband rented and lived in a house in Compton Street in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same and lived therein to the time of his Death and after her said Husbands Death she never gained any other subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 19th day of May 1779 before M Addington

Eliz Steel
