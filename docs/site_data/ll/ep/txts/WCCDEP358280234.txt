Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  Mary Eleanor Burridge  aged about 21 Yrs. Upon her Oath Saith that she never was married Rented House Messuage or Tenement or paid Taxes That she lived with Mr. Cogburn in Catherine Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields for five Years at the Yearly Wages of £3 That she quitted said Service about Two Years since which times she has done no Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement And further that Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith That about Two Years and a half ago she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mrs. Collicks in New Inn a Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes who has since been Christened by the Name of Thomas That one Thomas Stevenson a Compositor is the true and real father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body same and begotten the same thereon and that the said Thomas Stevenson is only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 20th. July 1789  before me} R Butler

her Eleanor Burridge Mark

Thomas Stevenson who is a Compositer at the Morning Harr Alice in Newcastle Street a tall Stout Man Dark Hair wears it loose abt. his Neck Dark Complexion very black Beard Roman Nose.

Want. made out.
