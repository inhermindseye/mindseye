Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Colquhoun aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that about Twelve years ago she Intermarried with Humphrey Colquhoun her late Husband at the parish Church of Saint Annes Westmr . That her said Husband has been dead about Eight years That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Little Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . and County of Middlesex for about Six years and paid Taxes for the same but what Rent he paid for the same she this Examinant doth not know and that after he quitted said house he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1795  M Addington E Read

her Ann Colquhoun Mark

Witness B Newman
