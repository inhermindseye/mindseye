Middlesex ss}  Sarah Grontt  Single woman maketh oath that Sometime ago she was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with James Arnold in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Victualler & Continued in such service for the Space of a year and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she has not Since lived as a hired Servt for a year nor hath she been ever married or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15 day of July 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Sarah Grontt
