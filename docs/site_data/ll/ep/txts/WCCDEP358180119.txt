Middx  Thomas Spencer upon his Oath saith that he Intermarryed with his present Wife  Margarett about 3 Years since at the Fleet  London that about Ten Months ago he lived in & Rented a house in Vine Street in the Liberty of Saffron Hill in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £11. for the Space of Three Months & upwards that since he left the sd. House he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif that he hath 2 Childn. Viz. Johannah aged abt. 2 Years & Margt. aged abt. 3 Months & being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne 15th. Octr. 1743 before Thos. DeVeil

Thos. Spencer
