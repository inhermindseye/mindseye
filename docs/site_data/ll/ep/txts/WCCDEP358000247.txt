Ann Spencer lives at Mr. Laws Cumberland Court}

Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Spencer aged about 23 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that on the 21st. day of Novr. 1785 last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at one Mr. Laws in Cumberland Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Sarah And further saith that One Joseph Stackpole a Counsellor who lives at No. 4 Elm Court Temple is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Depts. Body sometime in the Month of January 1784 and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Joseph Stackpoole and no or. Person did beget the said Child  of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 13th. day of March 1786 before me  Wm Hitchiner

Ann Spencer
