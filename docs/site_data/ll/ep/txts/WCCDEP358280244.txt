Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Whiteing aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the 26th. day of June 1787 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Dunks in Howard Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Richard That one Richard Barry a Journeyman Taylor who lodges and works with a Mrs. Stone in Essex Street is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Richard Barry and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of Octr. 1789 } Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Whiting

Elizabeth Whiteing lodges at Mrs. Davis the Corner of Yeates's Court Clements Lane

Richard Barry is a Tall Thin Man fair Complexion wears his Hair Tied behind.

Warrt. Issued Man taken.

Settled £10 pd
