Middlesex  T passd. to St. Botolph Billinsgates  upd. ago (to wit) Jane Jackson aged about fifty Eight years on her Oath says she never was married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Wilson a Dry Cooper in Botolph Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph Billinsgate in the City of London for two years, at the Wages of Six pounds a Year, That she quitted her said service about two years ago, & that since she quitted same, she has not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or Belief she may have gained a settlement elsewhere than in the Parish of Saint  Botolph Billinsgate aforesaid

Sworn this 24th. Janry 1785 before  Jinkin Jones N. Conants

her Jane Jackson Mark
