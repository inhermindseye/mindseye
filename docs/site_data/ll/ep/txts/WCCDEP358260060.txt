Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Reeves aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith that about Twelve years ago she Intermarried with John Reeves her present husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint James Westmr . That her said Husband Rents and lives in a house in Warren Street in the parish of Saint Clement Pancras in the County of Middlesex and pays the yearly rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and pays Taxes for the same

Sworn this 24th- day of June 1795 M Addington N Bond

her Elizth. Reeves mark

Witness Rickd Dobbins
