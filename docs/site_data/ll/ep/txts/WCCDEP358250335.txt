Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Isabella Church aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she was bound an Apprentice by the Officers of the Parish of Saint Anthony in the City of London to one Mr. Newman a Baker who then lived in Charterhouse Lane where he lived for Four years that then her said Master removed to Stepney in the Hamlet of Mile End Old Town in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex where she lived for the Space of Six Months That then her Mistress dying her said Master told this Deponent that she was at Liberty to go about her Business that she accordingly went to Service but never lived Twelve Months at any one time or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she hath an illegitimate Child living Vizt. Thomas born on the 21st. day of March now last past in the Workhouse belonging to Saint Sepulchre without

Sworn this 19th. day of Janry. 1798 before us N Bond  Matthew

her Isabella Church Mark
