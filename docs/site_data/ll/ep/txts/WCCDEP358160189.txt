Middx The Examination of Jno. Stringfellow living in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the third Day of Novr. 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Wm. Whitfeild at the Dukes Head in Devereux Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of 4 Pounds he at that Time being a Singleman & not having any Wife or Child & hath left his sd. Service abt. Nine Year to his now best Remembrance & further saith yt. since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif. That his Wife now lyes Inn & he being Poor is not able to maintaine her at this Time

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

John Stringfellow
