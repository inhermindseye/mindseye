Middx to wit Hannah Moore aged about Sixteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Saml. Coppock deceased in that Part of Shire Lane which lies in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Eleven Months ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement by living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 16th. day of Decr. 1779 before  Sampn. Wright  Davis Walker

her Hannah Moore Mark
