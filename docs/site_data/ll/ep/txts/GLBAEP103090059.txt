Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Dorothy Scott of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Widow taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this Seventeenth Day of February in the Year of our Lord 1767 .

Who on her oath Saith That on the Twenty Third day of April last Past in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate She the said Dorothy Scott was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which has been since baptised & registred by the name of Dorothy Scott and is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish And this Examinant farther Saith That one Andrew Scott now or late of Artichoack Lane in the Parish of St. George in the County of Middlesex  Lighterman did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Sworn this 17th. day of Febry. 1767 before  Chris: Scott

Dorothy Scott
