Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster to wit Susannah Fletcher aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that about 6 years ago she Intermarried with Moses Joseph Fletcher her late Husband (who has been dead about 10 Months) at the Parish Church of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr. and by whom she hath one Child living vizt. John Archibald aged about Two years That her said late Husband was an Irishman & never gained any Settlement in England that before her Intermarriage she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Sillitox who kept Bakers Coffee House Change Alley Lombard Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Woolnorth in the City of London for 2 years & upwards at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas hath quitted said Service upwards of Six years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Decr. 1787  Sampr Wright MAddington

Susannah Fletcher
