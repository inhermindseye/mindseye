St Clement Danes August 11th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. in St. Mary le bone  (to wit) Hannah Fatham aged Nineteen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Harrison Hair Dresser in Edgware Road in the parish of St. Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for a year or upwards at the annual Wages of £3:0:0 that she quitted that service about five years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 11th day of August 1794 before us} J Hind Edwd: Naish

Hannah Fatham
