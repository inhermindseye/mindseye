St. Clement Danes 12th Novr 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Andrew Middx (to wit) Elizabeth Clarke  singlewoman aged about thirty four years Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she Rented and resided in an unfurnished Apartment in Doddington Street in the parish of St Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for a year or thereabouts at the Annual Rent of Ten Guineas that she quitted the same Apartment about Midsummer last and she hath done no Act Matter or thing whatsoever since whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 12th. day of November 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Elizabeth Clarke
