Middx  Mary Lock aged upwds. of 50 years upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Rt Lock who was an Irishman & never gained a Settlemt. in England to her knowledge and that before her Marriage to the sd. Rt Lock & whilst a Singlewoman she lived a yearly Servt. with Mr. Jas Stevens a Writing master in St Clemts. Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes at the yearly Wages of £5 for two years & upwards & quitted her sd. Service upwds. of 20 years ago & never rented ten pounds by the year nor paid parish taxes

Sworn 26th. July 1755 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Lock
