Middx  Lucy Morrison upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Jas Morrison to whom she was married in the parish Church of St. Martin in the fields on the 25th. Septr. 1738 and that her sd. husband Rented a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds lived therein about two Years and quitted the same between Six and Seven years ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the year nor paid parish Taxes to this Examint. knowledge or belief and that her sd. husband now absconds from her & she know not where he is

Sworn Octr 23d. 1754 : before Fran: Bedwell

Lucy Morson
