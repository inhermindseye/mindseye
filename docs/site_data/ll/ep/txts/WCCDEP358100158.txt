Middlesex to wit Wm. Slater aged about Fifty five years on his Oath says he has an Illegitimate Child living by one Martha Lawrence lately deceased named Catherine Aged upwards of five years born a Bastard in Long Lane in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great London and that she has gained no or. Settlement than by Birth

Sworn this 20th. day of Decr. 1783 ,  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

William Slater
