Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Martha Daniels aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That her Father Robert Daniels Rented and lived in a House in Camomile Street in the Parish of Allhallows  London Wall in the City of London for several years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that she this Deponent Resided with her father in the said House as part of his family

Sworn this 9th. day of Novr. 1798 before us  Wm Goodenough W Carter

her Martha Daniels Mark

Witness Day
