Middx  Eliza. Phillips on behalf of her Son

Rd. Phillips upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Hannah Phillips at the Fleet London upwds. of Seven Years ago that he was bound apprentice by Indenture dated the 5th. Octr. 1738 to Joseph Staymaker a Blacksmith & lived with him as his apprentice in Barnaby Street in the parish of St John  Southwark in the County of Surry about two Years & then the sd. Rd. Phillips this Examint & his sd. Master parted by Consent & he never was assigned over to any other person nor ever rented ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn May 8th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Rd. Phillips
