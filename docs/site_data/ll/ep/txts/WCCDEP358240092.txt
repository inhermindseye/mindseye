St Clement Danes April 19th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD  Children  (to wit) Mary Brown Aged 38 years and upwards Widow of Joseph Brown deceased Maketh Oath and Saith that she was Married to her late Husband Joseph Brown at the parish Church of St Sepulchre London on or about the 20th. day of February 1773, that she hath been Informed & believes that her said Husband while he was Single under Indentures duly executed served in Apprenticeship of Seven Years with Mr. Hooper in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes  Bookbinder & Stationer and lodged & boarded in his said Master's House during such Service & that since the Expiration of his Apprenticeship he never obtained any subsequent Settlement to his knowledge or belief And this Deponent saith that her said Husband Died the 26th. March 1791 . And that since she hath never been Married nor otherwise acquired any Settlement in her own Right And she further saith that she hath three four Children and having by her said Husband now living Namely Joseph Aged Six Years on the Sixth day of July last Elizabeth , Born on the Eighth day of May 1789 and Sarah Born on the Eigth day of July 1791 And that She this Examinant is Ill & unable to maintain herself and Children without Assistance

Sworn the 19th. day of April 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Mr Brown
