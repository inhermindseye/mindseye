St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) Elizabeth Tynmore  Singlewoman Aged 30 Years and upwards Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servt. with Robt. Pemberton  Esqr . Cock's Court Serle Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty with whom she has been in that capacity, & regularly served from the 28th June 1789 , at the Annual Wages of Nine Guineas, And this Examt. further (on her Voluntary Oath) saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children who is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St.. Clement Danes And that Thomas Masters who lately lived as Footman to Mr. Lee in Tooks Court Cursitor Street in the County of Middlesex and this Morning absconded from his said Master's Service, but whither this Deponent knows not is the only Real and true father thereof of such Child or Children where with she this Deponent is so Pregnant as aforesaid he & no one else having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body several times, at some or one of which times he did beget the same thereon

Sworn the 22d June 1791 before me } R Butler

Elizabeth Tynmore
