Middx  Mary Allerston upon Oath says She never was Married, that She lived a hired Yearly Servant upwards of one Year with Mr. Andrews a Pastry Cook in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about Sixteen Years ago, Says She hath not since Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 1st- Day of Janry. 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Mary Allerston
