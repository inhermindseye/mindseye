Westmr.  Andrew Wills of Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London Perukemaker on the behalf of Elizabeth the wife of George Wills Upon Oath says that the said George Wills is this Deponents Brother and is now abroad in the East India Company's Service; Says the Said George Wills Rented a house in Charles Street in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards, lived therein about Two Years and quitted same in January 1756 and hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn the 29th, day of October 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

Andr Wills
