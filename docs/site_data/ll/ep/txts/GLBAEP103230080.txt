Middlesex ss}  William Moore maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house at the yearly rent of Eighteen Pound in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house Loding or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pound for Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of October 1787 before  R Davies

Wm Moore
