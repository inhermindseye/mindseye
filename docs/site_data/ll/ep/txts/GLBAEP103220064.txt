Middlesex ss  William Thomas maketh oath that divers Years ago he rented a Publick house the Sign of the Bricklayers Arms in Mount Street in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in Westmr in the said County of about the Yearly rent of Twenty Six Pounds & was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement and that he hath a Wife named Dorothy to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 8 day of August 1786 before R Davies Robt. Smith

Wm Thomas
