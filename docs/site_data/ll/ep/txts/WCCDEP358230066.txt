Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Hugh Mc. Dougall who is insane Maketh Oath and Saith That he this Deponent hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Hugh Mr. Dougall Served upwards of Two years being the remainder of his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Aslimore a Brushmaker air Snow Hill in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre within in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of May 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

B Newman
