Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Nugent aged about 64 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1750 she Intermarried with Thomas Nugent her late Husband who has been dead about Eight Months in Dublin according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Fleet Street Dublin about Five years at the yearly Rent of Thirty pounds That after he quitted said House which was about Thirty years ago he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing Whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th day of Septr. 1795 } M Addington

M Nugent.
