Middx  Ann Hewett upon Oath saith She is the Wife of John Hewett That She Intermarryed with the said John Hewett at the Fleet London on the 7th. Day of January 1753 Says her said Husband rented a House upon Snow Hill in the Parish of St. Sepulchres London of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & lived in the same severall Years Says her said Husband hath not since he quitted the said House on Snow Hill rented Ten pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 7th. Day of Jany. 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

Ann Hewett
