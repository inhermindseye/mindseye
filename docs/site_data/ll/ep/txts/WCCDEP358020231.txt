Saint Clement Danes Novr- 18th. 1790.

Middlesex (ss)  Margaret Renchman  Singlewoman aged 70 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Atkins a Watch maker in Palsgrave head Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds for upwards of one Year and that since quitting his Service which was about 28 Years ago she has never lived as an hired Yearly Servant any where for twelve Months, paid herself any Tenement of this Yearly Value of ten Pounds or done any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge or belief she may have acquired a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. Nov- 1790 before me Wm Kitchiner }

her Margt. Renchman Mark
