Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Beale of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate London Singlewoman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Sd County of Middlesex this 18th Day of September 1770

Who on her Oath Saith That Sometime in the Month of February now last Past in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd. County of Middlesex She the sd Elizabeth Beale was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which said Child hath been Since Baptized and Registred by the name of William Ashby And that one William Ashby of Aldgate High Street in the sd. Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate London  Butcher did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child.

Sworn this 18th day of September 1770 before  Chris Scott

The Mark of B een Elizabeth Beale
