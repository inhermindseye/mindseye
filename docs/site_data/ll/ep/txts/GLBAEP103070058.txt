Middx ss.  William Bristow Maketh oath that abt. Twenty five Years ago he rented a house in ye Parish of St. Paul Deptford in ye County of Kent & paid ten Pounds a Year rent for ye Same & likewise all parochial Taxes And this Dept. Saith that he hath not since Rented a house of Ten pounds by ye Nor & upwards elsewhere nor Never been a hired Servt. nor Served any Parish offices elsewhere Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 17 day of Febry 1752 before us  Richd. Ricards  John Lawton

William Bristow
