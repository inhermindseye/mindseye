Middx  Robt. Warner Aged about 40 Years Upon Oath says he Intermarried with Elizth his Wife in May Fair Chapel about Fourteen Years ago says he has two Children by her John Robert Aged about three Years and a half and Mary Aged about Two Months Says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and Whilst he was Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Anthony Weldon  Esqr in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx One Year at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds and hath not since Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 11th Day of August 1763 before} Paul Vaillant

Robert Warner
