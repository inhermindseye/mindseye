Middlesex ss}  Thomas Webb maketh oath That divers Years ago he took & rented a house of Six Pounds a Year in Crown Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same & at the time When this Deponent first entred upon the said house & for Sometime after the Kings Taxes assessed upon the Same was not Lumped or Farmed by the assessors of the Said Tax That this Deponent has ever Since & Still doth inhabit in & rent the said house and hath not done any Act to gain a Settlemt elsewhere

Sworn this 27th day of November 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Thomas Webb
