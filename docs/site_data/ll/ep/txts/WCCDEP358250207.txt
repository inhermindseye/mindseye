Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr. to wit Mary Morris aged about 24 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married bound an Apprentice lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or a Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes That her father Edward Morris Rented and lived in a house at Fulham in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex for several years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and paid Taxes and that after her father quitted Said house he never did any Act whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th day of Novr. 1795  N Bond W Kinnard

her Mary Morris Mark

Witness Richd. Dobbins
