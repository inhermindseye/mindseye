St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster (to wit) Mary Fegen  Singlewoman Aged twenty four Years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married That she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Dr. Brocklesby No.11 in Norfolk Street in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County for about fourteen Months at the Annual Wages of £8 that she quitted her said Service in or about the Month of December 1787 and since hath not done lived as an hired Yearly Servant for a twelve month s under one hiring, Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby she might acquire any other legal settlement

Sworn the 4th June 1791 Before me  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Fegen 's Mark
