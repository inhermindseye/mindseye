Middx.  Cathne. Ball on the behalf of her husband Richard Ball aged upwards of 57 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds lived therein abt. four Years & hath quitted same abt. ten Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn the 10th. Janry 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Cathne. Ball
