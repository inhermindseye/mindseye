Middlesex ss}  Mary Robson the widow of William Robson deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to and Served his apprenticeship with one William Robinson in the Parish of St Martin in the City of Lincoln  Farrier & Blacksmith & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont farthr Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said late husband never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself Since his Death & that She hath three Children namely William Aged about nine years & half Ann aged about three years & Elizabeth Aged about four months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband

Sworn this 24d day of December 1773 before B Camper

The Mark of Mary Robson
