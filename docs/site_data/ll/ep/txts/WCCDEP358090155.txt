Middx  Ann Dutton Aged about Thirty one Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband James Dutton at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the County of Middlesex about Ten Months ago says her said Husband is an Irishman and never gained any Settlement in London to her knowledge belief says before her Intermarriage to her said Husband And whilst She was a Singlewoman She lived as a hired Yearly Servant with George Birch  Esqr. in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Nine Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes on living a hired Yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 8th. day of Septr. 1780 before M Addington

Anna Dutton
