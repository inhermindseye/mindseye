Middx.  Sarah Thorp aged abt. 22 Years upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mrs. Hart for 14 Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £5 and hath quitted said Service abt. three Years; Says she lived the four first Months of her sd. Service with the said Mrs. Hart in Lemon Street in the Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex and then Mrs. Hart Removed into Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx aforsd. where this Examinant lived with her abt. Ten Months the last part of her said Service and then was discharged and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or lived a Yearly hired Servt. for One Year and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. she being poor & Unable to Maintain the same when Born, says that one Michl. Solomon in Angel Court by Hounsditch

Hounsditch in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate London did beget the said Bastard Child or Children on her Body he the said Michael Solomon having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of May 1758. and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that he the said Michael Solomon and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 13th. Feby. 1759 before me} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Thorp

Upon the Application of Robert Wimberley  Over . J: P:
