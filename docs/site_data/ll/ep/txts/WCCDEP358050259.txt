Westmr. 

Eliz: Wilson upon Oath saith about 12 Years ago she intermarried at the fleet London with Thomas Wilson who went from her about nine Years since & she knows not where he now is & that she can give no Information whatever concerning the Settlement of her said husband Says she lived a Yearly Servant whilst a Singlewoman with Mr Vaughan a Fan painter next Door to the Chapel in Russell Court in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of

four Years at the Yearly Wages of £3..10..0 and hath quitted her sd. Servi  about 16 Years; since which she has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn May 21st. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz Wilson
