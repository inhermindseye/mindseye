Middlesex ss  John Stamps maketh oath That he has been Credibly informed and verily believes such information to be true That he this Deponent and also this Deponts. late Father John Stamps deced were both born in the Parish of Halcaswal in the County of Worcester And this Deponent doth not know nor was ever informed nor Can in any manner discover or setforth Whether his said Father ever did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his birth and in his own right nor the settlement of his said Fathers Parents And this Deponent Saith that he never was bound apprentice or lived as a hired servant for the year or rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor ever served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes Nor to his knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a settlement in his own right And that he hath a wife named Phebe and three Children namely Thomas Aged about five years Ann aged about three years & Rebecca aged about one year and half the lawful issue of this Depont. by his said wife.

Sworn this day of December 1767 before}
