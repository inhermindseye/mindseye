Middx 

Mary Warden aged upwards of 29 Years upon Oath saith she never was married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Thomas Etheriedge  Colourman in the Strand in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of four pounds for one Year & upwards & was discharged from her sd. Service about a fortnight since And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a female Bastard Child on the 30th. Day of May last at a house in Warder Strt. in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes (as yet unbaptized) which is likely to become chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same and that Edward Wyat Colourman is the reputed father the sd. Edward Wyat having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinant in the month of August last & at sevll other times since at one of which times

he got her with Child of the said female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. and that the sd Edward Wyat and no other Person is the true and reall father thereof

Sworn June 11th. 1747 before Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Mary Warden
