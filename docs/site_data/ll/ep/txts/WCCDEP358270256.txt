Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Sarah Love aged about 33 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with St. George Thomas at Deal Park in Sussex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Thirty Guineas that she quitted said Service about Two years since which time she has done no Act to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that on the 9th: day of December 1795 she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the Grange Inn in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath not as yet been Baptized That one Richard Webster who lives as a Footman to one Mr Lewis in Devonshire Place is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that he and no other person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof and that this Deponent is poor and unable to maintain such Child without Assistance from the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 9th. day of Janry. 1796 } Edw. Read

Her Sarah Love Mark
