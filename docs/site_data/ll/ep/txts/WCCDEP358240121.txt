St. Clement Danes July 2nd 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Brides London  Joseph Messeldine Aged 50 Years or thereabouts Widower Upon Oath Saith That he never Obtained any legal Settlement in his own Right that he intermarried with his late Wife Ann at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about 13 13 Years ago who died about 9 or 10 Months ago and by whom and he had no that he has no legitimate Issue that he was informed and verily believes his Father William Messeldine intermarried with Sarah this Deponent Mother at Bath in the County of Somerset in or about the Year 1730 That he this Deponent was born some time in or about August 1744 That his said Father rented and Resided in a House in Goldsmith Street in the Parish of Saint Bridge otherwise Bridget in the City of London on about 8 Years but what he paid for the same this Deponent doth not know by Information or otherwise That his said Father And this Deponent further saith That his said Father & Mother both Died in the Work or Poor House of the said parish of St Bride That his said Wife was removed from St Luke Old Street to the said parish of St Bride otherwise St Bridget and Died in the Workhouse belonging to that parish And That he knows not any of any other legal Settlement that he in entitled unto save as afsd. in the parish of St Bride otherwise St Bridget and that he is poor Ill & unable to support himself without Assistance

Sworn the 2d. day of 1792. before us }

Sworn the 4th day of Before Us.  Sampr Wright M Addington

Joseph Messeldine
