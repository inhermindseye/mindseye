Middlesex ss}  William Lewis maketh oath that he Cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of his late parents deced or either of them That he this Depont. was born in East cheas in Swan Alley in the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County & never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year nor ever Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own Eight

Sworn this 24 day of January 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of William Lewis
