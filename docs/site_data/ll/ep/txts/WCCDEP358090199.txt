Middx & Westmr. to wit Sarah Palmer Aged about Twenty One years On her Oath says She never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father William Palmer Rented and lived in a House in Change Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of four Years at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds hath quitted the said House about four years ago since which time he hath never Rented House or Tenement or gained any other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says She is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish She being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born And further Says that One Thomas Fowler otherwise Elson an Apprentice to One Mr. Paul a Glazier in Water Lane Fleet Street London did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in One Mr. Jenkinsons House in New Exchange Court in the Strand sometime the latter End of June or the beginning of July last but the particular Day She does not now remember at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which She is now Pregnant & that the said Thomas Fowler & no other person did beget the same & that he is the true Father thereof

Sworn before me this 22nd Feby. 1781

Resworn the 12th. day of  Abrm Baley

her Sarah Palmer Mark
