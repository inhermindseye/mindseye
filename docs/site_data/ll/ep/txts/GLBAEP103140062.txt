Middlesex ss}  Ann Goodluck  Singlewoman maketh oath That on the Twenty fifth day of October one thousand Seven hundred & Sixty Seven She this Deponent was deliverd of a Male Bastard Child at the house of George Langley in Lambeth Marsh in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry  Shipwright which Said Bastard Child hath been Since baptised and registred by the name of Thomas Goodluck in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westminster in the said County of Middlesex & hath for Sometime past been & Still is Chargeable to the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the same County .

Sworn this 14th- day of November 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

Ann Goodluck
