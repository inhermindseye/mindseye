Middx.  Wm. Gardner aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edwards an Auctioneer in Threadneedle Street in the Parish of St. Bartholomew by the Exchange in the City of London for 2 Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds he being at that time a Single Man & hath quitted that Service abt. Twenty Years says he hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 21st. day of Janry 1758 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane

W Gardner
