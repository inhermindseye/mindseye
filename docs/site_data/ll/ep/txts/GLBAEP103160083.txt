Middlesex ss}  Prudence Frear maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Mr Gaskin in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Victualler at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for many years & by means thereof gained a legal settlemt there & that she did not afterwards live as a hired Servant for a year many other Parish nor was she ever married nor did she do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Prudence Frear
