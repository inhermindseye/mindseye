Middx ss.  Frances Lindsay maketh oath that she was born in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx And that she this Deponent hath not gained any legal Settlement otherwise than in right of birth to the Knowledge of this Depont & as this Deponents verily believes And this Depont. Saith that about Twenty nine Years ago She was Married to one John Bignall a Carpenter who then lived in the Said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate & with whom this Depont. lived as his wife for Three years & upwards & by whom this Deponent had 2 Children one named Elizabeth & the other Mary And this Deponent Saith yt Subsequent to her Marriage with the said John Bignall she was Credibly informed & verily believes that the said John Bignall was married to anr. Woman before he Married this Depont. with whom he went away with when he left this Deponent by which this Deponts. Marriage was void.

Sworn the 26 Day of August 1749 before  Richd: Ricards

The Mark of Frances Lindsay
