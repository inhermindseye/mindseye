Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Barry aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that in the Month of May 1787 she Intermarried with James Barry her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields that he has deserted this Examinant about Two Months That her said Husband informed this Examinant that his Settlement was in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London by living Twelve Months and upwards as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cox who kept the Mitre Tavern in the said Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London that after he was quitted said Service about he never did any Act to the best of this Examinants knowledge to gain a Subsequent Settlement that she hath Two Children living viz dilicet Diana and Mary both Twins born in the Month of January last And both by this Examinants Husband

Sworn this 17th day of June 1788 Mercer J Barnfather

her Mary Barry Mark
