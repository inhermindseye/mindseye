Middx  Eleanor Wheeler on the Behalfe of Catherine an Infant Child aged about Eighteen Months upon her Oath saith that the said Catherine was born in the House of one Mr. Emes in Eagle Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex and this Examinant cannott give any Account of the Father & Mother of the said Child

Sworne the 14th- March 1742 before.} Chas: Egerton

Eleanor Wheeler
