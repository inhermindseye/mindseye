Westmr.  Martha Abbit aged 60 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Abbit with whom she intermarried at Lambs Chappel in King Street Westmr . about 53 Years since says her sd. late husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £25 in D uke Street Lincolns Inn fields in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and lived therein upwds. of four years & hath quitted the same about 23 Yrs. since which time neither her sd. late husband whilst living nor hath this Examinant since his Decease rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 5th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Martha Abbit
