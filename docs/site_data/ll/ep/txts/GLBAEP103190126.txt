Middlesex ss}  Sarah Smart the widow of James Smart deced maketh oath that her Sd late husband Sometime Ago lived in & rented a house in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & paid Kings Tax Poors Rates for the Same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself

Sworn this. 10. day of July 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Sarah Smart
