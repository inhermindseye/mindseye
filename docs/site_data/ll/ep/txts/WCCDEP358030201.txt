Middx  Ann Clark upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Ferdinand John Paris in Surry Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds for the Space of Seven Years and hath left her said Service about two Years Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Paris's Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a male Bastard Child on the 13th. Day of July last at the house of Mr. Ridgemont in Witch Street in the sd. Parish of St Clement which has been baptized Thomas & is chargeable to the said Parish and that one Richard Gardiner is the reputed

Father the sd. Richard Gardiner being acquainted with this Examinant had earn all knowledge of her body in the month of November last & at several other times since in the house of Mr. Trolope a Merchant in Friday Street London at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. that he the sd. Richard Gardiner & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof.

Sworn Augt. 12. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Ann Clark
