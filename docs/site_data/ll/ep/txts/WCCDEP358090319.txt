Middlesex & Westmr}  Charlott Ayrey Aged about Nine Nineteen Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mills at the Feathers Tav in the Strand for several Years before his Intermarried with her Mother and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Par (she being poor and unbale to maintain the same when born) And further that one John Bamford a Book keeper at the Angel Inn is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at he Lodgings in Angel Court sometime in the Month of November last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her wth. Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Bamford and no other person did beget the same that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 3d day of July 1782 before R Butler

her Charlott Ayrey Mark
