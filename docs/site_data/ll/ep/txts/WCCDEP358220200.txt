Westmr. to wit Celo Sewrey Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. James Tew  Attorney at Law Cliffords Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West London upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Seven Years ago and after quitting her said Service she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30th. day of Novr. 1770 before Us} R. Butler 

Celo Sewrey
