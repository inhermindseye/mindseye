Mary Larchin aged about Twenty Eight Years lodging with one Mrs. Wells in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she never was an Apprentice lived a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place that her late Father Wm. Larchin hath been dead abt. 12 Yrs. & was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Bullock at the Crown Tavern in Pallace Yard in the Parish of St. Margt . in the Liberty of Westmr . Vintner & after out of his sd. Apprentiship lived with the sd. Mr. Bullock as a Drawer for the Space of Two Years or thereabts. & then Intermarryed with this Examinants Mother also Deced. & left the sd. Mr. Bullocks Service abt. Thirty Three Yrs. since that this Exaiants Father in his Life Time nor her Mother since his Decease never rented Ten Pound's a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Exaiant hath been Informed & beleives

Sworne the 11th. of Decr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Mary Larchin
