Middx & Westmr}  Nicholas Logan Aged about Thirty five Years on his Oath Says he never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Richard Edwards a Gardiner at the Neat Houses in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Guineas hath quitted his said Service about fourteen Months ago since which time he never Rented House or paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th. day of Octr. 1770 before Us W Kelyngs Petr Planck

his Nichs Logan Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
