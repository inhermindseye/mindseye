Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Edward Burbidge one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and saith that he has been credibly informed which Information he verily believes to be true that Richard Nicols an Infant aged about Four years was born a Bastard on the Body of Jane Jones at the House of a Mr. Diamond in Angel Court Westminster in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn this 25th. day of Septr. 1788 M Addington N Bond

Edwd. Burbidge
