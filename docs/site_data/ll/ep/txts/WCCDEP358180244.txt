Middx  Jane Ellis aged about 26 Years upon her Oath saith that she was never married Says that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Jackson in Tavistock Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £5: for the Space of 1 Year & upwards & hath left her sd. Services abt. Seven Months since which time she hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 1st. Novr. 1744 before}

The Mark of Jane Ellis
