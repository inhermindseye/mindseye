Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Nash Aged about 49 years upon her Oath Saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr Bowen at the Sign of the Board head in Exeter Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent garden in the Liberty of Westminster for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Forty Shillings that she hath quitted said house about Thirty five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this first day of October 1799 before us} John Collick Benja Gth

her Mary Nash mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
