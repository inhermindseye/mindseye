Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Margaret Downing aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with Mr. Foster a Jeweller in Richmond Buildings in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about five years-since which time she has done no act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th, day of Janry. 1790  ampr Wright M Addington

Margaret Downing Her Mark
