Middx  Chas. Pamplin aged upwds. of 14 Yrs. upon Oath Saith he is the Son of Chas. Pamplin by Susa . his Wife both deceased and that he hath been informed & believes his sd. late Father Served Seven Years Apprentiship with a Baker in Penny farthing Street in the parish of St. Joles in the City of Oxford and that he this Examint. never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 13th. Augt 1751 before  Tho Lane

Charles Pamplin
