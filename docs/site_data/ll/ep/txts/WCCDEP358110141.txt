Westmr.  Margt. Sparing  Wido . of Robert Sparing upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd late husband Rt Sparing in the Parish Church of St. Andw. in Holborn about Twenty years ago and that about nine Years ago her sd. late husband Rented a house in Witch Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £26 and lived therein near three months & there dyed & this Examinant continued after her sd husbands Decease to live in & Rent the sd. house for two Months or thereabouts & has not Since She quitted the sd house Rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt.

Sworn Jany. 6th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Margt. Sparing
