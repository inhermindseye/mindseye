Middx and Westmr.}  St. Clemt. Danes to wit Dennis Daniel Bowers aged 32 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he never was Married rented a House or paid Taxes served any apprenticeship or ever lived as an Hired yearly Servant for one year And this Examinant further saith that that he was born in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex where this Examinant's father rented a House of above £10 a year that his father died in the said House upwards of 20 years ago that since his said Fathers Death the this Examinant hath been to sea and was in the Workhouse of Saint Clement Danes in September last and that he never did any act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his this Deponents knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere than in the said Parish of St Clemt Danes

Sworn this 10th day of Decr 1784 R Butler

Dennis Daniel Bowers
