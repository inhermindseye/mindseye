St Clement Danes June 12th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary le Strand  (to wit) Ann Smith  singlewoman aged fifty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Dyas at a House then called the Kings Arms Tavern in Catherine Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex for about three years at the Annual Wages of Five pounds that She quitted that service about eighteen or twenty years ago and hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 12th day of June 1793 before Us J Pownall W Addington

Ann Smith 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
