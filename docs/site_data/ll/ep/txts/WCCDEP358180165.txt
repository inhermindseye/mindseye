Middx  Ann Chapells  Singlewoman aged abt. 25 Yrs. Removed from the Parish of Barkhamsted St. Peters in the County Hertford by Order Dated the 10th: Day of Febry 1743 upon her Oath saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Tharp in Claremarkett in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Butterman at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of Five Years & upwds. & hath left sd. Service abt. 5 Yrs. since which time she hath not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when borne & that John Rose of the sd. Parish of Barkhamsted St. Peters did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body the sd. Jno. Rose being acquainted with this Examinant used to visit her at 

the House of one Mrs. Brockwells where this Examinant Lodged where he had Carnall knowledge of her Body once in the latter End of June or beginning of July last at which time he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. John Rose & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 14: Feb: 1743 before}

The mark of Ann Chapell
