Middx ss  Margaret Forrest  Widow of Jonathan Forrest deced Maketh oath that her father Mother Sarah Lancas deced hath often informed her that about forty two Years ago he lived as hired Servant (being then a Single man) with Mr. Robert Walker  Hallkeeper at Guild hall who then lived in Hall Court in the Parish of Saint Michael Bassishaw in the City of London & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. has also been informed that her sd. father Never rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards Nor Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement which information this Depont believes to be true And this Deponent Says that her Mother is now living whose, present Name is Sarah Lancaster & who first intermarried with the sd. Robert Hutson this Deponts. said father and who after his Decease intermarried with one John Lancaster who is also deceased but from What Parish or place the sd. John Lancaster Came from or the Parish or place where he was last legally Settled this Depont. Can't Set. forth having no knowledge or information thereof

Sworn this 29 day of Nov 1753 before us  Richd: Ricards  Walter Berry

Margaret Forrest
