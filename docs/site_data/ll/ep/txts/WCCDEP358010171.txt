Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizth. Dowse aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice That she lived with her Mother she Died which was in the Month of Novr. 1716 that her said late Mother Rented of Mrs. Andrews in the Strand in the Precinct of the Savery a Shop Parlour & Garrett for which she paid Twelve Pounds a year for Eighteen years or thereabouts that after she quitted said apartmts. She never Rented any Place of £10 a year

Sworn this 14th. day of Decr. 1781  Sampr Wright  John Croft

E Dowse.
