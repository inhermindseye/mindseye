Middx.  Ann Green aged abt. Eighteen Years upon Oath Says She was Bound Apprentice to Ignatius Plant by the Parish of St. Clement Danes as appears by Indenture bearing date the 15th. of May 1754. Says She Served abt. three Months of her Apprentiship when the said Ignatius Plant dyed & further Says She never was assigned over to any Person Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the th Octr. 1756. before}
