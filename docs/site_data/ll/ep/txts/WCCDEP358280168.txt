Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Eales aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1777 she Intermarried with Thomas Eales her late Husband who has been dead about 3 years at the Parish Church of Romford and by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Elizabeth aged about Eight years that her said late Husband lived as a hired yearly Servant with John Heaton Esqr. in the Parish of Romford in the County of Essex for Four years or thereabouts that after he quitted said Service he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 29th. day of January last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Bolton in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary Ann That one William Wilkins Servant to Mr. Dawson a Brewer at Kensington Gravel Pits is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of April 1787 and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was a delivered as aforesaid And that the said William Wilkins is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 22nd. July 1788 } R Butler

her Elizabeth Eales Mark

Elizabeth Eales lives at Mr. Miller No. 121 Drury Lane

William Wilkins is a Middle sized Man Thick Set has a large Dimple in his Chin

Man taken and Committed to Clerkenwell Bridewell

Man paid £15 and disd.
