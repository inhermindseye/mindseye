Westmr. Middx 

Eliz Holborn  Wido aged abt. 63 Years removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the fields by Order dated the 13th. Instant upon Oath saith she intermarried wth. her late husband Cornelius Holborn at the Fleet London upwds. of 40 Years ago and that her Sd. late husband dyed about 26 Years since Says she has been informed and believes her sd. late husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes and that her sd. late husbands father lived in and rented a house in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx but Knows not the Rent and that he never Kept any other house afterwards Says she this Examint. was born either in Whitechapel or at Milend and that her Father Kept house Sevll. Years in Hounsditch

Sworn the 24th. Days of October 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Eliz. Holborn
