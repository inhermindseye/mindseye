Middx 

Anthony Forster aged about 44 Years upon Oath saith he never was married and that about Seven Years ago he lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Weston in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Nine pounds for four Years and upwards and has not Since he quitted his sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 3d. 1749 before}

The mark of Anthy Forster
