Middx  Grace Ford aged 23 Years upon her Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Lord Castlemain at Epping Forest in Essex at the yearly wages of Five Pounds for the Space of one Year that she quitted her sd. Service about 18 Months ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year and this Examinant further upon her voluntary Oath Saith that she is the pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St Clement Danes where she now Lodges she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Richard Bates a Drawer at the Union Coffee house in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body

the said Richard Bates having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinant sevl. times in the Months of Decr. and January last at One of which times he got her with Child of the bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Richd. Bates and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn the 10th. Septr. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell Gries Ford
