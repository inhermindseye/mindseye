Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ruth Vinon aged about 57 years now chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upon her Oath saith That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Smith in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds That she hath quitted said Service about a fortnight only

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry. 1798 before me} J Hunter

her Ruth Vinon Mark

H Day Beadle
