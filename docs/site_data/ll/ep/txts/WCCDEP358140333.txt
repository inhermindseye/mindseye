Middx.  Ann Temple aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath says she never was Married, says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Cornelius at the Rose Tavern near Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for three Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £6 and hath quitted said Service abt. Twenty Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Decr. 19th. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Ann Temple
