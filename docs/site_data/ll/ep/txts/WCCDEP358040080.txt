Westmr.  Margt. Bramston aged 67 Yrs. & upwds. upon Oath says she is the Wido . of Thomas Bramston who has been dead upwds. of 30 Years Says she lived in and rented a house in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £16 for one Year & upwds. & left the same about four Years ago Says she has not since she quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Setlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 12th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Margt Bramston Bramston
