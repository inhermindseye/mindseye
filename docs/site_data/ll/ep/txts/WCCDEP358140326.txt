Middx.  Sarah Rogers aged abt. 29 Years upon Oath says; she is the wife of Saml. Rogers who is now at Sea by whom she has one Child Saml . aged abt. four Years; says her said husband is an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England says her said Son Saml. was born in Princes's Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx.

Sworn this 28th. day of Novr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Rogers

Upon the Complaint of Mr Wilkinson Ovrs.
