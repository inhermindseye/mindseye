Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Sutherland aged about 47 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that about 26 years ago she Intermarried with John Sutherland her late Husband (who has been dead about 5 weeks) at   May Fair Chaple that she hath 5 Children living by her said Husband 4 of which are provided for the one unprovided for is named James aged about 7 years that her said late Husband Rented a House in Essex Court White Fryars in the Precinct of White Fryars in the City of London for 3 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £12 that he hath quitted said House about 12 years since which time her said late Husband has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Janry. 1787  Wm. Gascoigne  Jno Machin Mary Sutherland
