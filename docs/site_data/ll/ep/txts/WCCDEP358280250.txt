Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Martha Edger aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Lowerback in Jermyn Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Nine Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Fifteen Months since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 14th. day of August last she was delivered of a male Bastard Child at the house of a Mrs. James in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of William That one Nicholas Broads a Porter who lived at the Somerset Coffee house in the Strand is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Nicholas Broads is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 7th. day of Novr. 1789 } Wm. Kitchiner

Martha Edger

Martha Edger lives at Mrs. James in Milford Lane.

Nicholas Broads is a Slim Man about 5 foot 6 Inches high fair Complexion Rather pitted with the Small Tox.

Not taken.
