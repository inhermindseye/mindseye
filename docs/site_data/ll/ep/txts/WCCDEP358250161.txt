Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Histon of No. 4 Clements Lane or behalf of Elizabeth Holmes an Infant of the Age of Two years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that the said Elizth. Holmes was born in Gun Alley Saint John Street in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 11th. day of May 1795 } M Addington Edwd: Read

her Sarah Histon Mark

Witness B Newman
