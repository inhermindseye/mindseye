Westmr.  Samuel Seavern aged about 46 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Anne his Wife at the Fleet London upwards of Twenty three Years ago by whom he hath four Children Anne aged abt. Seventeen Years Catharine aged about Eleven Years Samuel aged about Nine Years and Keziah aged about Six Years says he this Examinant was born in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and never Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 13th. Decr. 1765 , before} Paul Vaillant

Saml. Seavern
