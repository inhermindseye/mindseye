Middlesex and Westminster} 

he was taken up & Committd to Bridewell aftwds Marrd the Woman before the Birth of ye. Child Nil his Exn. 7. Decr. 1785. to wit Mary Graves aged 24 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenemts. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Lewe Jenkin who keeps the Crooked Billett in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for fifteen Months after the Rate of five Pounds Ten Shillings for the first half year and after the Rate of Six Pounds for the Remainder of the time hath quitted said service about 2 years since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And further say that one Samuel Haines  Journey or an Hair Dresser who lodges in a Court in Grays Tan Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at sevl. difft. times at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Samuel Haines is the Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 3d. day of Novr. 1785 before me  Edward Hodsoll

her Mary Graves mark
