Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Craig aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Harman in Castle Street Oxford Market in the Parish of Saint Margaret Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th- day of May 1796 before us N Bond W Kinnard

her Ann Craig Mark
