Middx ss  Mary Jolley  Widow of George Jolley Deced Maketh oath that her sd late husband in his life time often informed her this Depont. that he was bound to duly Served his apprenticeship With William Gardy of Blue Anchor Alley Yard in ye Parish of st Mary Whitechappell in ye County of Middx  Carman & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in that Parish Which informed this Depont. believes to be true And this Depont. Saith that her sd late husband did not after his apprenticeship gain any Subsequent Settlement to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent And this Deponent Saith that she hath a daur Now living Named Mary the Aged about Twenty eight Years the lawfull issue of this Deponent by her sd late husband And Saith that her sd. Daur hath not gained any legal Settlement than in right of her sd. father as aforesd.

Sworn this 27 day of April 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  Saml Gower

his Mary Jolley Mark
