Westmr. to wit Saml. Massey Aged About Twenty four Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship  with one Mr. Whitehead a Shoemaker in Little Turn Still Holbourn who afterwards removed into Duke Street Lincolns Inn Fields in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex & says that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship He never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise Gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of Decr. 1773 before R. Butler Sampn Wright

Samuel Massey
