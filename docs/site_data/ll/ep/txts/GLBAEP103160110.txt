Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Sabieri  wife of Lewis Sabieri  mariner maketh oath that her Said husband has been gone away from her for sometime past That her Said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born in Spain & to her knowledge or belief he never did any Act to gain a Settlement in Great Britain That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Father or Mothers Parents That her late Mother Grace Budge deced has informed her that She the said Grace Budge was born in Carlisle City but in What Parish there She never told her & this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting her late Mothers Settlemt previous to her Marriage with her late Father Wm Budge deced That her late Father has informed her & which she believes to be true that many years ago he lived as a yearly hired servant with a Certain Person in Rood Lane in the Parish of Saint Margaret Pattens in the City of London but whose name she does not now remember That her said Father died about fourteen years ago That sometime before his death he informed this Depont & which She believes to be true that he had often received relief from the Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish of St Margaret Pattens as being the place of his last legal settlement & this Deponent is the better induced to believe such information to be true for that upon Application lately made by her to Mr Eaton of the Sd Parish Schoolmaster respect that matter he informed her that about fourteen years ago he being Churchwarden & Overseer of the Poor of the Said Parish of St Margaret Pattens had divers times relieved her Said Father as being one of their Poor then legally Settled with them as appeared by Certain Entry's made in his Books respecting the then Poor of the Sd Parish That her Said Father to this Deponents knowledge or belief did not afterwards do any Act to gain another settlement That she this Deponent never did any Act to Acquire Settlement for herself Save her Marriage as aforesaid That she hath two Children namely Lewis William aged about Fourteen years & Ann Mary aged about Five years & Seven Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband.

Sworn this. 27. day of July 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarke

Eliz Sabieri
