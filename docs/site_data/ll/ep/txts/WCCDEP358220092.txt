Middx & Westmr}  Mary Gard Aged about 27 years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Samuel Gard who has been Dead about 23 Weeks and by whom she hath one Child Named Jane aged about Four years Says her said Husband before his Inter- marriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Sutton Esqr. in Broad Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph Bishopgate London upwards of Twelve Months at the Yearly Wages of Fourteen Pounds and Quitted his said Service about Sixteen Years ago and after he Quitted the same never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th day of November 1769 before Us R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Mary Gard mark

Witness Thomas Jones
