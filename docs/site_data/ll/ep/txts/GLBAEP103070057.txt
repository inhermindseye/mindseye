Middx ss.  Letitia Taylor  Singlewoman maketh oath that about eight Years ago she lived as a hired Servant by the year with Mr. Elrich Houster a Dutch Merchant in Size Lane in ye Parish of st. Bennett Shere Hogg in the City of London & continued in Such Service for ye Space of three Years at ye rate of Six Pounds by the year Wages And this Depont Saith that abt. three Years ago She this Dept. Married one William Eadsall Deced who lately lived in Deans Court in ye Parish of st. Ann within Aldersgate Londo n But upon Inquiry lately Made by this Depont. this Depont. finds that the sd. William Eadsall at ye time he Married this Dept. was for a Considerable time before Married to another Woman which former Wife is still living By Means whereof this Dept. Marriage at this Dept. is informed & believes became bird in Law And this Dept. Saith that she hath not Since her Servitude gained any legal Settlemt. to her knowledge of belief

Sworn this 29 Jany. day of January 1752 before us  Richd: Ricards  Thos: Quarrill

The Mk of Letitia Taylor
