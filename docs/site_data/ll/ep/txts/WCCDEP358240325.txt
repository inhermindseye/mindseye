St Clement Danes September 19th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. Augustin Bristol  (to wit) Susannah Wilburn  Widow aged Twenty Seven years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She was Married to John Wilburn her late Husband at that the parish Church of St Giles in the Fields Middx on or about the 15th day of August in the year 1785 (by Banns) that previous thereto her said Husband Served his Apprenticeship (under Indentures) with Mr Knight Hair Dresser on St Austin's Bank in the parish of St Augustine in the City of Bristol and slept in his Masters House in there during such Service that his Apprenticeship expired about six Months before his Marriage with this Deponent and he never after during his Life obtained Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that he died about four years ago And She this Examinant hath not Since been Married or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain or become entitled any unto any legal Settlement since her said Husband's decease

Sworn the 19th day of September 1793 before us} Pitchell Edwd : Naish

Susannah Wilburn 's Mark

Witness Garrett

Removed by Mr Allaway Overseer & Burbidge Beadle
