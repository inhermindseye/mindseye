Middx  Ann Bellasyse upon Oath saith she is the wido. of Wm. Henry Bellasyse who dyed about 4 weeks since to whom she was married in the parish Church of St. Martin at Birmingham on the 9th. of May 1746 & by whom she has four Children (viz.) Margt . aged upwds. of Seven Years Richard aged upwds. of five years Ann aged upwds. of three Years & Wm. Henry aged nearly Six Months And this Examinant further maketh Oath her sd. late husband informed her he was bound Apprentice by Indrs dated 7th. Janry 1740 for Seven Years to Jas. Hardey a Silver Turner & lived wth. the sd. Jas. Hardey as his apprentice in Maiden Lane near Haberdashershale in the parish of St. John Zachary London near three years at the End of which time he was discharge from his sd. Master & that he never was assigned over to any other person nor ever rented ten Pounds by the Year or Paid Parish taxes. All which Information this Examt. believes to be true

Sworn April 2d. 1754 before} Tho: Lane .

The Mark of Ann Bellasyse
