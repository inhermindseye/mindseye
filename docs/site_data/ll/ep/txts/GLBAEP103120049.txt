Middlesex ss}  John Pepper maketh oath that he Well knows a Child named Mary Skinner aged about twelve years and now living in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex The which Child as he this Deponent has been Credibly informed and Verily believes was born a Bastard in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry and that such Child hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right and this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting the settlement of such Child

Sworn this 30th. day of June 1773 B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of John Pepper

Ann Pritchard the mor Mary Pepper her sisr}
