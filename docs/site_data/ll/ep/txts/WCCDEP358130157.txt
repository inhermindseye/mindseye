Middx  Sarah Skillem Aged about 38 Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Matthias Skillem and that her said late Husband rented a House in Peter Street Clare market upwards of Ten pounds a Year for several Years and that he lived therein till the time of his Death Says She hath not since his Decease Rented a House paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year Says that on the 15th. day of Febry 1755 She was married at the Savoy Chappel to James Allen a Taylor but this Examinant hath since been informed the said Marriage is a void Marriage And further upon her voluntary Oath Says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that the said James Allen who now lives at the Horse and Groom in the Parish of Hackney did beget the said Bastard Child or Children of which She is now pregnant he having had carnal Knowledge of his Examinants Body on the sd. 15th. day of Febry 1755 & at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Children of which She is now pregnant and that the said James Allen & no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 10th. Day of May 1756 before} Tho Lane .

Sarah Skillem
