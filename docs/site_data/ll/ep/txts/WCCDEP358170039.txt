Middx The Examination of Ruth Chamberlain  Wido . of Batton Chamberlain Deced Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Who upon her Oath saith yt. before her Intermarriage with the sd- Batton Chamberlain (which was on the 28th- Day of July 1716) his Father Sons Chamberlain lived in & rented a House in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of Years or or thereabts- of the Yearly Rent of £30 & upwards And this Examinant further on her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband in his Life Time neither hath She since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant or was an Apprentice or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place as this Examinants sd. late Husband Informed her & which She beleives

Sworne this 11th- Day of April 1741 before}
