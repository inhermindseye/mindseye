Westmr to wit Thos. Adams Aged about Twenty Nine Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields about Eight Years ago by whom he hath Two Children now living Vizt. Elizth . Aged about Seven Years and Ann Aged about Three Years Says before his sd Intermarriage and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a Covenanted Servant with Mr. Charles Pearce a Taylor in Castle Street in the said Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds for the first Year and Eight Pounds for the last Year hath quitted his sd Service about fourteen Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year

Sworn this 19th. day of Decr. 1770 before Us.} Thos: Kynaston

Thomas Adams
