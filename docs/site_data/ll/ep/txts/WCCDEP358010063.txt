Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Brown aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Wilmot a Butcher in Fletcher Street Clerkenwell in the Parish Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx for 13 Months at the yearly Wages of £3 hath quitted said Service about 13 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. saith that she has one illegitimate Child living vizt. Elizth. Margt. Steers aged about 5 years & upwards born at the House of a Mr. Daniel the bott of Clerkenwell Green in the said Parish of Saint James in the Clerkenwell

Sworn this 31st. day of Janry. 1787  Jno. Machin  Jno Barnfather

her Mary Brown Mark
