Middx to wit John Kelly Aged about Six five Sixty six Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at Saint Marys in Dublin about Twenty Years ago and by whom he hath one Child living namely Catherine Aged about fifteen Years Says before his Intermarriage to his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Charles Wilson at the Sign of the George in Piccadilly in the Parish of Saint George Hanover  in the County of Middlesex in the Capacity of a Groom and lived with his said Master upwards of Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds besides Diet ant Ledging hath Quitted his said Service about Thirty Years Ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 8th. day of March 1780 before R Butler Abrm Baley

his John Kelly Mark
