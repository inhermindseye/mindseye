Middx  The Exaicon of Jno Hess lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 29th Day of Novr. 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. upwards of fourty Years Since he was bound an Apprentice to & Served seven Years Apprentiship with one Mrs. Dixon at the Sun Alehouse by Clemt. Inn Gate in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes aforesd. & further saith yt. he never Since his sd. Apprentiship rented any House pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or belief & that he is aflicted with ye. Rheumatism & being Poor is not able to maintaine himself & Wife

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

John Hess
