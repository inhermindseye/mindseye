Middlesex ss  Mary Chaloner the Wife of Samuel Chaloner maketh oath that her Said-husband is lately gone away from her to Places to her unknown and left her wholy destitute of Support That her Sd husband was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to William Richards of the Parish of St George the Martyr in the County of Surry  Gun maker but Served the last ten Months of Such Apprenticeship with his Said Master in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County of Middlesex Where his Said Apprenticeship ended That he has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a Child named Ann Aged five Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 19. day of June 1787 before  R Davies

Mary Chaloner
