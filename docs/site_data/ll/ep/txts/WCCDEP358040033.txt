Westmr.  Ann Smith upon Oath saith she never was married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Susa. Stonhill opposite St. James Church in Pickadilly in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for two Years & upwds. & hath quitted her sd. Service about 9 Years Says she has not since she left the sd. Mrs. Stonhills Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. with any Person one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 15th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Ann Smith
