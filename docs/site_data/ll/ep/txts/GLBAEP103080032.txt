Middx  Mary Oates the Widow of William Oates deced Maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her that he duly served his Apprenticeship with one William Lasting a Chair Carver in the Parish of St Nicholas Coleabby in the City of London & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And that he did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards Nor serve any Parish offices nor live as a hired servant by the year nor gain any subsequent settlemt which information this Deponent believes to be true And this Deponent saith that to since her said husbands Death she hath not done any Act to Acquire a settlemt in her own right

Sworn this 22nd- day of February 1758 before us Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Mary Oates
