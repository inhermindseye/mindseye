Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit.  John Watkins  Perukemaker Maketh Oath and saith that in the last Week in the Month of January in the year 1776 he agreed with one Joseph Norton the then Tenant and Occupier of a Publick House in Vinegar Yard in the parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex known by the Sign of the Halfmoon  and in pursuance to become the Tenant of the said House and in pursuance of the said Agreement he immediately paid Ten pounds to the said Joseph Norton for the Fixtures therein and entered directly upon the said House as Tenant agreeing to pay Ten pounds a year Rent for the same which was the same Rent the said Joseph Norton his predecessor pd And saith that under the said Agreement he did actually inhabit and occupy the said House and there carry on the Business of a Victualler for upwards of seven Weeks but after he became in the Occupation thereof in manner aforesd. the Landford of the said House having refused to accept him this Examinant as Tenant a few Days before the Lady Day then next he quitted the said House and took part of the Fixtures he had purchased of the said Joseph Norton but he paid no Rent nor any Taxes for the said House And since his quitting the said House he hath never rented or agreed to Rent a Tenement or Lodging at so great a Rent as Ten pounds a year paid any kind of Taxes or done any other Act which to  knowledge or Belief hath acquired him a subsequent Settlement

Sworn the 26th Day of November 1790 . Chas. Sheppard J Barnfather

Jno Watkins
