Middlesex ss}  Janet-Brutton maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with one Mrs Lucy Butler in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand in the sd County near the church there and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of Twenty Months That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30 day of May-1783 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Janet Brutton
