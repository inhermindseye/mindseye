Westmr.  Ann Peace Aged about Forty Five Years upon her Oath says she is the Wife of Wm. Peace (who now lies ill of a fever) with whom She Intermarried at the Parish Church of St: Dunstants in the West London about Thirteen Years ago by whom She hath Three Children namely Evan aged about twelve Years Alexr. Aged about Seven Years and Henry Aged upwards of Two Years Says her said Husband hath often times Informed her and which Information She Verily believes to be true that he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Weston a Plaisterer in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr and never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yrly. Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th Day of Decr. 1768 Before} Tho: Lane

Anne Peace
