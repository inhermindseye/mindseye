Middlesex ss}  Peter Wright maketh oath That divers years ago he rented a house at the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas in Snows Fields in the parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey  in the County of Surrey & Continued in the possion thereof for half year & upwards & paid rent for the Same & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife name. Mary & three children namely Dorothy aged about eight years Peter aged about four years & Eleanor aged about eight months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 21 day of Febry 1777 before}

The Mark of Peter wright
