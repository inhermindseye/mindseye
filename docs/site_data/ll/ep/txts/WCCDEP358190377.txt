Middx  Elizth. Clarke Aged about 46 Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Francis Clarke with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Martins in the Fields about Thirty Years ago Says her said Husband Never Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Sert. Says her said Husbands Father Captain  Francis Clarke Rented a House in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Upwards of Forty Years at the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds Says she this Examinant hath not Since the Death of her said Husband Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year.

Sworn the 14th Day of September 1763 before} Paul Vaillant

Eliz Clarke
