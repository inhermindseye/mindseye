Middx  Elizth: Skinner Aged about 27 Years upon Oath Says She Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Clark a Salesman in Great White Lyon Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx and hath been out of her said Apprentiship about five Years and hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the Day of June 1763 before}
