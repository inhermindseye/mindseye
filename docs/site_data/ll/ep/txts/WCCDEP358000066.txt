Saint Clement June 21. 1785

Swears to St. C. D.  Sarah Aris upon her Oath Saith that she is aged twenty seven Years or thereabouts That she was married to Christopher Aris her now Husband about eleven Years ago at the Parish Church of Saint John Westminster and this Examinant further Saith that her said Husband is now to the best of this Examinants Knowledge and belief abroad in the East Indies that this Examinant has no Child or Children now living by her said Husband that she does not know that her said Husband has run since their Intermarriage gained any settlement, That her said Husband has informed her that he Served his Apprenticeship to his Father in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and had never gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. June 1785 before me  Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Aris .
