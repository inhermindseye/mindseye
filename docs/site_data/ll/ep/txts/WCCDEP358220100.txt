Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Gibson Aged about Twenty Seven Years with her Child named Mary on her Oath says she never was Married Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Mears a Tallow Chandler in Hounsditch in the Parish of Saint Botolp Aldgate London upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said about Seven Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yrly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th. day of December 1769 before us R. Butler Petr Planck

her Elizabeth Gibson mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
