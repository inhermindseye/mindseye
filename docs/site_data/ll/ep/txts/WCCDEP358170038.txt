Middx  The Examination of Elizth- Barber  Wido - of John Barber living in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 8th. Day of April 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that Thirty Three Years ago to her best Remembrance she intermarried with her sd- late Husband John Barber & further on her Oath saith that her sd. Husband served Six Seven Years Apprentishipp with one Mr- Richd. Billington in Drum Alley in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes  Button Maker & further saith that her sd. Late Husband in his Life Time nor hath she this Examinant since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor & Lame is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Eliz: Barber
