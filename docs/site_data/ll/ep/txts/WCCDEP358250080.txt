St Clement Danes 9th Janry 1793

Middx & Westminster }  Streatham Surry this Man Marrd Brooker Vide her Examt or page 23 the otherside of this Leaf (to wit) Edward Still aged 21 years upwards singleman Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he lived as an hired yearly servant with Mr Heath in the parish of Streatham in the County of Surry for one year at the Annual Wages of £10:10s that he quitted that Services about four years ago and hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve mon  together rented any House or Apartment of £10. a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 9th day of January 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Edward Stills Mark

Witness B Newman
