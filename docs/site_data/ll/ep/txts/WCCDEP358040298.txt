Middx 

Mary Hargiss aged abt. 47 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about Seven Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Rock a Hosier near Norfolk Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & at the Yearly Wages of £4..10..0 for four Yrs. or thereabts. & has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 17th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Mary Hargess
