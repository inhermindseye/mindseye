Middx  Ann Gall lodging in Mr Johnsons Tenements in Vear Street in the parish of St Clement Danes upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Roger Cannon a Fellmonger at And over in Hampshire for one year at the yearly Wages of thirty five Shillings quitted her sd Service in last March and has not since rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes And further upon her voluntary Oath Saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the aforesd. Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor and unable to maintain the Same when born and that the sd. Roger Cannon her late Master did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the Said Roger Cannon having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the month of February last twice

at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which She is now pregnant and that the said Roger Cannon & no other person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn Novr. 20th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Anne Gall
