Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit, Michael Child Aged 57 Years or thereabouts, upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a House in Yates's Court, Carey Street of the Annual Rent of upwards of ten pounds a year, that he quitted same about October 1786 And that he this Deponent never since then hath Rented any other House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year value, paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Augst. 1797 } 

Michl, Child
