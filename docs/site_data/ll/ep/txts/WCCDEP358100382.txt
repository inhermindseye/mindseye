St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  passd. to Winchester to wit John Higgins aged about 17 years upon his Oath saith That  he never was bound Apprentice  or ever lived as an Hired yearly Servant for one year  did any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a settlemt in his  own Right, Saith that he has been informed his Father Charles Higgins and his Mother Elizth. Higgins were Married at Winchester , that after their Intermarriage they Rented a House Called by the Names the Bell and Crown in High Street in the Parish of St. Maurice in the City of Winchester for some years and that this Examinant was born at Resaid House That his Father died when this Examinant was very young, and his Mother died in St Clements Workhouse abt. 2 years ago Months ago

Sworn this 12th. Apl. 1785 before G Mercer Jno Barnfather

John Higgins
