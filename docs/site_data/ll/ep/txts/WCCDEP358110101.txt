Westmr.  Mary Sweeting upon Oath saith she intermarried with her Husband Nath Sweeting abt. 7 Yrs. ago at the Fleet London by whom she hath one Child Chas. aged abt. 6 weeks and that her sd husband hath informed this Examint. & she verily believes he Served his Apprentiship with Mr White Shoemaker in Carey Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never Since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Octr. 14th. 1752 before} Fran. Bedwell

Mary Sweeting
