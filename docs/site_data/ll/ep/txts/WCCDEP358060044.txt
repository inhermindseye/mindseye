Middx  Mary Jackson  Wido . of Jno. Jackson who dyed upwds. of 20 Years ago upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her sd. late husband abt. 38 Yrs. ago in the Parish Church of St. Brides London and that her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Delauder a Watchmaker in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and that neither her sd. late husband nor this Examint. Since his Decease have rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 2d 1750 before Fran: Bedwell

Mary Jackson
