Middx  Cathn. Batty aged 23 Years upon Oath Saith She never was married & never rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes nor was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant and that her father Joshua Batty rented a house in Hollywell Street in the parish of St Clemt Danes of the Yearly Rent of twelve Pounds & upwards lived therein Several Years & quitted the sd house upwds. of twelve Years ago, Since which he has not rented ten Pounds a Year or Paid Parish taxes

Sworn July 16th 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cathn. Batty
