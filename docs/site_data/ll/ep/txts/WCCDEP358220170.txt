Middlesex & Westminster }  Mary Walton Aged about thirty three Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Thomas Walton (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at Portsmouth in the County of Southampton about Eleven Years ago and by whom she hath three Children now living namely Ann Aged about Seven Years John Aged about Six Years and Thomas Aged about Six Months Says that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Whites Alley in the Liberty of the Rolls for Six Months or thereabouts at the the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted the same about Twelve Months ago and after he quitted the same he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th: day of August 1770 before us} R Butler Thos. Kynaston

The mark of Mary Walton
