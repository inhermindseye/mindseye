Middlesex ss}  Nicholas Osborn maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Dunderidge a Sugar Refiner in the Parish of St James at Garlickhith in the City of London & Continued in Such Service there under Such hiring for the Space of one year & three Quarters That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Sarah and a Child named Nicholas aged Nine Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 7. day of September 1786 before  Robt. Smith R Davies

The Mark of Nicholas Osborn
