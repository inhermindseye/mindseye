Middx  Hannah Herring aged about 45 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Richard Herring who has been Dead about Nine Years to whom she was Married at the Fleet London about Twenty Years ago That he lived in and Rented a House known by the Sign of the White Horse in Drum Alley in the Parish of St.. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for several Years at the yearly Rent of £14 Quitted the Same about Thirteen Years and never after Rented any House paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate Also says that since the Death of her Husband she hath not kept House paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes nor been a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months together in any one Place

Sworn the day of Septemr. 1761 Before me}
