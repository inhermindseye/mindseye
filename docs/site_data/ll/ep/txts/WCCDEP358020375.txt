St. Clement Danes 16 Janry.1792

Middlesex & Westminster}  (to wit) Benjamin Newman on behalf Mary Powell a Single person Aged thirteen years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she hath informed this Deponent which he verily believes to be true that she is the legitimate Daughter of of John Powell by service his Wife late Joyce Colerick who were Married at the parish Church of Martley near the City of Worcester the City of Worcester but to but that she doth not know how long ago, that her said Father & Mother with 4 of their Children are in the Workhouse belonging to St. George Hanover Square about 4 or five Months but and could not tell how they were Settled there
