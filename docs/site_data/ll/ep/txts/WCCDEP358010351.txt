Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to Wit) Elizabeth Harris  singlewoman aged fifty Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she Never was Married that she never was bound as an Apprentice or ever served as such that she never lived as an hired yearly Servants for one year Rented any House or Tenement of the yearly Value of ten pound paid taken ever did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to gain or acquire any Settlement in her own right And this Examinant saith that she was born in Little stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint clement Danes in the County of Middlesex where her father was a Housekeeper and served the Office Churchwarden Overseer and Beadle of the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and never in his Life time gained any other Settlement and that she this Examinant hath been since her Father Dece relievebly and in the Workhouse of that Parish of Saint Clement Danes as an Inhabitant duly Selled in that Parish

Sworn this 5th. July 1790 before me R Butler

Elizth Harris Ye her Mark
