Westmr. to wit Ann Crockett Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Cullum a Butter Man in Holles Street Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for One Year and Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that One Samuel Royle  Servant to the said Mr. Cullum had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time the begining of March last but the particular day she does not now remember and at several different times both before and afterwards at his the said Mr. Cullums House at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. 1774 before R. Butler

her Ann Crockett mark
