St Clement Danes 11th April 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the field  (to wit) Elizabeth Robinson  Singlewoman Aged forty seven years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bulkley Hatter and Hosier in the Hay market in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for about three years at the Annual Wages of Nine pounds that She quitted that Service about twenty years ago and never obtained any Subsequent legal Settllement by any way or means whatsoever as She best knows or believes And saith that She is Poor and unable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn the 11th day of April 1792 before Us  Sampn. Wright M Addington

Elizth. Robinson
