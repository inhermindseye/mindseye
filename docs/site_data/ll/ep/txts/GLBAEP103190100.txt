Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Jane Hopperton of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County this Sixteenth day of June 1780 -Who Saith that She is now with Child of a Child or Children And that the said Child or Children is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate and that John Christian of Barnaby Street Southwark e in the County of Surry  Inn holder is the Father of the sd Child or Children Wherewith She now goeth and is Pregnant

Sworn this. 16. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Jane Hopperton
