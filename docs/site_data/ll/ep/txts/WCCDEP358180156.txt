Middx  Mary Reece aged about 30 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she was born in Golden Lane in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex and cant give any Account of her fathers Settlement other than that he was born in Wostchester but in wt. parish this Exaiant knows not that her Mother  Margt. Reece served her Apprentiship with one Mrs. Rowell in Golden Lane aforesaid Lace Joiner That this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 16th. Janry 1743 before} Wm Jones J Poulson

The Mark of Mary Reece
