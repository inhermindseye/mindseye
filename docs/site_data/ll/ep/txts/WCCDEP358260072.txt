Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Thorpe of Bear Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Ann Patterson who is insane Maketh Oath and saith That the said Ann Patterson lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mrs. Ford in  South Audley Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for about Fourteen years at the yearly wages of Seven Pounds that she said Ann Patterson hath quitted said Service about Fourteen Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Septr. 1795 } J Hind Russl Clarke

Jane Tho
