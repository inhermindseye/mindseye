Middlesex ss  Rebecca Dunbar the Widow of Richard Dunbar deced maketh oath that She has been informed by her said late husband & which She believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre to one Stevenson a Hair Dresser & Perukemaker in Greek Street in the Parish of Saint Ann in Westmr in the Said County & Served the whole of his Apprenticesp there That her Said husband did not afterwards rent a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement to this Deponents Knowledge information or belief And that she was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn this. 5. day of September 1786 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Rebecca Dunbar

Mrs Stevenson the Mistress is still livg the Corner of Greek Street
