Middx  Mary Foster  Wido . of Edwd. Foster who dyed about a fortnight since upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Edwd. Forster about 12 Years ago at the Fleet London and has by him three Children (viz) Henry aged about 11 Years Edward aged abt. 9 Years and George aged about 3 Years Says the sd. Edwd. Forster Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Mathews a Butcher in the Play house Passage Clare market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlx and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examints. knowledge or beleif.

Sworn April 5th. 1751 before  Tho Lane .

The mark of Mary Foster
