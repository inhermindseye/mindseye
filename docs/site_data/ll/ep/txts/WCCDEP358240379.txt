St Clement Danes 23d Janry. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Husband born in Whitchurch Hants ye wher. his Right Settlemt  (to wit) Sarah Wedge aged 34 Years or thereabouts Wife of William Wedge now in his Majesty's Service on the Continent Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said William Wedge at the parish Church of St Margaret Westminster on on or about the fifth day of Septr. 1788 that she hath one Child by her said Husband born in Wedlock namely John Charles aged five years & upwards that which is now living with her that She hath been informed and verily believes her said Husband was born in the parish of Whitchurch in the County of Hants in lawful Wedlock And this Examinant saith that her said Husband never did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement on his own Right to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief and she knows not of any settlement entitled to best by her said Husband's Birth as aforesaid

Sworn the 23d day of Janry. 1794 before us}

Sarah Wedge 's Mark

Not Removed the Woman declaring her Husband had obtained a Settlemt. by Service in the 3d Region out of Guards
