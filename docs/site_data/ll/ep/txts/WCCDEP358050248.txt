Mason aged about 61 Yrs. upon Oath saith he intermarried with his prest. wife Catherine (who is now about 50 Years of age) in the Parish Church of St. Patrick in the Subburbs of Dublin in Ireland Says he rented the Crown Taphouse in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Rent of £30 and lived therein upwards, of one Year and hath quitted the same abt. 18 Yrs. and has not since rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif except a house of the Yearly Rent of £20 in Tybarn Road in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields  Middx which he rented of one Mr. Cottaral and lived therein abt. Eight works a little after he left St Clements

Sworn May 12th 1750 before} Ja Croft

Richd Mason
