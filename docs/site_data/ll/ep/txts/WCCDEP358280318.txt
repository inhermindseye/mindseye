St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster } (to wit) Mary Sloper Aged abt. 31 Years upon her Oath saith that she lived as an Hired Yearly Servant with Mr Dyer in Newcastle Court Co in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Coal Merchant for 3 Years & an half at the Yearly Wages of £7 that she quitted that Service in January 1790 and since has not to the best of her knowledge Information or belief Gained any other legal Settlement And this Examinant further on her Oath saith that she is now pregnant with a Child Child or Children likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes and that Thomas Thornton at Mrs Taplens No. 2 Maid Lane in the Bow of Southwark is the only real true father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the Same thereon And that she is Distressed and unable to provide for the said Child or Children whereof she is so pregnant which must therefore become Chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn before Me the  4th. March 1791 } R Butler

Mary Sloper

Mary Sloper lives at Mr Nichols's New Palace Yard Westmr . Gent.
