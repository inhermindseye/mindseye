Middlesex ss}  Catherine Ellister the wife of John Ellister maketh oath That She had a Daughter named Catherine by the Deponts. first Husband John Richards deced That this Deponts. said Daughter Catherine who did some time before christmas last did in her life time inform this Depont. & saith information this Depont. verily believes to be true that She was lawfully married to one John Watson who is also deced That her said Daughter left behind her at her decease three Children namely Michael Watson Aged about eighteen years now apprentice in Bishope gate Street London  Wm. Watson Aged about Sixteen years also an apprentices  the Said City and Elizabeth Watson aged about Nine years now residue in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate in the Said County all of Which are the Lawful Issue (as this Depont. verily believes) of her Said Daughter by her said late husband That this Depont.Cannot set forth Where the Said John Watson was legally settled & his Death But saith that her Said Daughter did after her husbands Death being a house of the yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds in the Sant Parish St. Botolph Aldgate & lived therein for a Considerable time & by means thereof gain a legal settlement there That to this Deponts. knowledge or belief her said Daughter Catherine Watson did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement and that this Deponent Said Grand Daughter Elisabeth Watson hath not as get done Act to gain a settlement in her own right to this Depont. knowledge belief

Sworn this 18th. day of June 1768 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Catherine Ellister
