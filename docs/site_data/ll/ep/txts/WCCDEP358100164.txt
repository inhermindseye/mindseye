Middx  Martha Sherkin Aged about Fifty fiv Years on her Oath says she never was married or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Rented & lived in a House at Little Chelsea in the Parish of St. Luke  Chelsea in the County of Middx upwds. of Twenty years at the yearly Rent of £10. and lived therein to the time of his Death & this Examinant further says she Rented and lived in the same House upwards of a year since which time she hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement or Lodging of £10. a year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 9th. day of Janry 1784  Sampn Wright  Thos: Gilbert .

her Martha Sherkin Mark
