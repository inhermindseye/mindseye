Middx 

Mary Martin aged abt. 30 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that on the Nineteenth Day of January last she was delivered of a male bastard child at Mr. Brastables in Milford Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the sd County of Middlesex which has been baptized William and is become a Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes and that William Forbes of the same Parish Taylor did beget the said Male bastard Child on her body the said William Forbes  having had carnal knowledge of the Examint. in the month of April 1749 and at

several other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the said male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said  William Forbes  and no other person is the true and real father there of

Sworn June 9th. 1750 before} Tho Lane H Fielding

Mary Martin
