Middx  Hannh. Howard  wife of Mathew Howard (gone from her) upon Oath saith the said Mathw. Howard about Ten Years since rented a house in the Litah Street in the Parish of St. Michael in Worcester of the Yearly Rent of Six pounds and lived therein for Six Years or thereabouts and pd. his Share to the poors Rate of the sd. parish and that her sd. husband has not since he quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 4th 1749 before H Fielding

The mark of Hanh. Howard
