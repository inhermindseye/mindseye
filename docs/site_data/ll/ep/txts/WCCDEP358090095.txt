Middx to wit Thomas Evans aged about fifty Years on her Oath says he never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says he lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Fancourt an Oilman near Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Capacity of a Porter for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Guineas a Year besides Bed & board hath Quitted his said service upwards of Three Years ago since which time he hath gained no other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 16th. day of Novr. 1779 before R Butler

Thomas Evans
