Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Forshall aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that on the 23rd. day of Octr. 1785 -last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Akerman in Chair Court Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Country of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the Name of Thos. Savage and that one Edward Savage who lived at Mr. Riley's the Fishmonger in Bloomsbury Markett is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Edward Savage is the only real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 18th. day of April 1788 } R Butler

Jane Forshall

Jane Forshall at Mr Jarmans a Smith No 23 in Kings Gate Street Holborn Gave 2

Edward Savage is a Short Thin Man Pitted with the Small Pox his Hair hanging in his Neck
