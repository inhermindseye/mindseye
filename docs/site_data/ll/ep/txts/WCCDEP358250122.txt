St Clement Danes 16th April 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Pancras Middx (to wit) Elizabeth Tims aged about eighty years Upon Oath saith that She was Married to William Tims at the parish Church of St Andrew's Holborn in the County of New about between about sixty years ago by that previous thereto while her said Husband was single and unmarried her lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr: Walker Cowkeeper near the Foundling Hospital in the parish of St. Pancras in the County of Middlesex for ab upwards of a year act at what Wages this Deponent's knows not that he Married from the Said Mr. Walker's and never after quitting his Service did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in his own right to the best of her knowledge information or belief neither hath

Sworn the 10th day of April 1794 before us M Addington B. Kennett

Elizabeth Tims 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
