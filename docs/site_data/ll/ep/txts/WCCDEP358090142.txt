Middx to wit Ann Gill Aged about Fifty four Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Horman a Victualler in Conduit Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for three Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Nine Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 16th. day June 1780 before- M Addington John Machin

Ann Gill
