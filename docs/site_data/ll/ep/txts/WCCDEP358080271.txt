Middx  Jane Earnest aged about Thirty Six years on her Oath says she is the Wife of John Earnest gone to Sea with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holbourn about five years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living says her said Husband has two Children by a former Wife namely Sarah aged about Fourteen years & Ann Maria aged about 13 years, Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Leapers Court Baldwins Gardens in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars for five Years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of 20 and Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Thirteen years ago since which time her said Husband never Rented House or Tenement of £10 a year or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 1st. Day of August before me  Wm Barnfather W Mainwaring

Jane Earnest
