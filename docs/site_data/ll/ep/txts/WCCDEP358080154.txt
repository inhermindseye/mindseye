Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Lloyd Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says Her father Rees Lloyd Rented and lived in a House in Shire Lane in the Liberty of Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of fourteen pounds and upwards and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Seventeen Years ago since which time her said Father never Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain any other Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 9th. day of July 1777 before  Jno Machin G Mercer

her Ann Lloyd Mark
