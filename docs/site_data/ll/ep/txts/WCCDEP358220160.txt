Middlesex & Westmr:}  Elizabeth Taylor Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Saith She never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr: Jordan at the Red Lyon in Dirty Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for three Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th: day of July 1770 before me}

her Elizabeth Taylor Mark

Witness Will: Hughes
