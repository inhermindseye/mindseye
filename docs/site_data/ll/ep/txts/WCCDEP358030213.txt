Middx  Robert Ainslo upon Oath Says he intermarried with his Wife Mary Ainslo in the Parish Church of Acton in the County of Middx Says he lived in & rented a house in St Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £12 for Seven Years or thereabouts & paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes & hath quitted sd. house about Seven Years says he hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworne Sepr. 6th 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The mark of Robert Ainslo
