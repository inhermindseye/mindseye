Middx  Margt. Frogley aged abt. 66 Years lodging at Mrs. Baxters in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Robert Frogley who has been dead abt. Four Years. Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant for two Years at the Yearly Wages of £4 with Henry Jenkins  Esqr . and lived the last Six Months of her said Service with the said Mr Jenkins in Boswell Court  in the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes and quitted said Service abt. Michaelmas last and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds by the Year or paid Parochial Taxes

Sworn Novr. 22d. 1760 before me} Tho Lane

The Mark of Margt. Frogley
