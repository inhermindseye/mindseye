Westmr.  William Forrester Aged about Fourteen Years Upon Oath says that on or about the Twenty Seventh day of March 1766, he was bound Appren- -tice by the Chapel Wardens of Bridewell Precinct London , to Stephen Frankland of Skinner Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph Bishops -gate London Weaver and served about Twelve Months of his said Apprenticeship , and then his Said Master failed and went away; says he never was Assigned over to any Person nor ever lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year

Sworn the 28th. day of January 1768 , before  Geo. Reid W Kelynge

The Mark of William Forrester
