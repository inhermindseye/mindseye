Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Smith aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith That she Intermarried with John Smith her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint James in the City of Dublin by whom she this Deponent hath One Child living Vizt. William born on or about the 28th- day of June now last past at the House of Mrs. Webb in Cross Street  Gosport That her said Husband was born in the Parish of Saint James in the City of Dublin that he never Served any Apprenticeship lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Rented any House Tenement or Lodging for Forty days in England Paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any Settlement in England That she this Deponent was born in the Kings County in Ireland and this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that her said late Husband upon left this Deponent last Tuesday nor hath she seen been since nor does she know and where he is gone she this Deponent doth not know

Sworn this 24th- day of Febry. 1797 before me G J Goodenough

her Ann Smith Mark Witness B Newman
