Middlesex ss.  Ann Hanson of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Singlewoman maketh Oath and Saith that she is well acquainted with Isaac Lardent late of the Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the said County Weaver , and that she has heard the said Isaac Lardent Say his Two Sons namely Isaac aged about Twelve Years and James aged about Ten Years were both born in the Parish of St. Matthew Bethnal Green in the said County, And this Deponent further saith that the said Isaac Lardent the Younger and James Lardent have not nor hath either of them to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent done any Act to gain a Settlement in their or his own right that the said Isaac Lardent the Father hath deserted his said Children, and that the said Children being Poor & unable to provide for themselves have applied to the Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate for relief & have been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 28 Day of Decr. 1798 before R Davies H: Reynell

Ann Hanson
