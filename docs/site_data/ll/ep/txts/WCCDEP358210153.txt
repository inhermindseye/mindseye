Westmr.  Susannah Johnson aged about 37 Years Upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Pritty a Pawnbroker in Vere Street Clare market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one whole year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds quitted said Service about seven Years ago, and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year. And further upon her Oath says that on or about the Ninth day of July 1765, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Bray in Kings head Court Shoe Lane in the Parish of St. Brides London which hath since been baptized by the Name of Daniel Long ; says that one Richard Long Late a Porter at the Bolt and Tun Inn in Fleet Street is the Putative Father of the said Male Bastard Child and that he and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 15th, day of April 1767 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Susannah Johnson
