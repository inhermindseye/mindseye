Middx  Jane Christopher Aged about Forty Years On her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Doctor Egerland who lodged at Mr. Cornwalls a Taylor in Stanley Street Clare Market Upwards of One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about fifteen Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement Either by Renting House paying Taxes or living hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 15th. day Janry. 1780 before R Butler

her Jane Christopher Mark
