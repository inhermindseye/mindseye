Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Graves aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she was bound an Apprentice to one Archibald Roxburgh a Shoemaker in Clare Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Seven years. that she Served her said Master under such Indentures Ten Months or thereabouts that then she was discharged by the Magistrates in consequence of the ill Treatmt. she Received that after she quitted her said Master she never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or bound an Apprentice to any other Person And this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Walter Greaves a Journeyman Taylor who lodges at No. 3 in the Little Sanctuary  Westminster is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant there and that the said Walter Greaves is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 3rd. day of Febry. 1797 before me Gs. Goodenough

Margaret Graves
