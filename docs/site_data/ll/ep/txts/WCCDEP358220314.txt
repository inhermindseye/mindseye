Westmr. to wit Mary Harreman Aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Charles Cartwright at the Sign of the Bibbs in Great Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for three Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Services upwards of Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 4th. day of Janry. 1772 before R Butler W Kelyngs

Mary Harreman
