Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Jenkins Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Parish of Saint Johns Southwark in the County of Surry to one Mr. Whitchurch a Carpenter  near Queen hithe in the Parish of Saint Michael Queen hithe in the City of London and after she was out of her said Apprenticeship she hath never done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1776 . before R Butler Wm Kitchiner
