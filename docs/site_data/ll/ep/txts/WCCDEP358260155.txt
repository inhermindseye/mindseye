Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Evans of No. 16 Great Shire Lane on behalf of John Shepstone an Infant aged about Three years Maketh Oath and Saith that she was informed by Hannah Shepstone the Mother who is now dead that the said John Shepstone was a Bastard and was born at the house of one Mr. Sawyer a Glazier in Monmouth Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1796 N Bond Rast: Clarke

her Mary Evans Mark
