Middlesex ss  Amelia Frances Smith the widow of John Smith deced maketh oath that her said late husband heretofore rented a house in Star Court in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County and during such his residence therein he was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same She this Deponent paying the same to Mr John Freeman the then Collector of such Tax That after payment of such Tax this Deponts said husband never rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That Since her said husbands death She hath not lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been Married nor rented a house or Lodging at the yearly rent of Ten pounds or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 3. day of May 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Amelia Frances Smith
