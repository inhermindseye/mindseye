Middlesex ss.  Margaret King  Singlewoman maketh Oath that about Sixteen Years ago and when she was about Thirteen Years of age she was bound Apprentice by the Overseers of the Poor of the Precinct of St. Katherine to Thos. Haines of Queen Street in the Parish, of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx  Gunsmith until she shod. attain the Age of Twenty One Years that she lived with her said Master in Queen Street & served him there under the said Indenture about Four Years, when with the knowledge & Consent of her said Master she went to live as a hired Servant by the year with Mrs. Cleeves a Corn chandler in Church Row in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate London at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds & continued in her Service near Three Months, that Notwithstand her said Master gave her Liberty to go & live with Mrs. Cleeves, he did not deliver her up her Indentures or have them Cancelled so that she could not but be considered as his Apprentice, that after she left Mrs. Cleeve she did not live with any other Person with the knowledge & Consent of her said Master nor has she done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement Since her Said Apprenticeship

Sworn this 11th Day of August 1798 before  Wm: Horseteen

her Margaret King Mark
