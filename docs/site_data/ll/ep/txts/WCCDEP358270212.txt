Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Bedwith aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that on the 20th. day of May last she Intermarried with Thos. Bedwith her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Ann's Soho  That on by whom she hath no Child or Children living That on or about the 29th. day of October last previous to her Intermarriage with her said Husband and whilst she was a Singlewoman she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Margaret Rainer and in now become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Clovely Shoemaker who lives at the sign of the Red Lyon in Windmill Street in the Haymarket is the only real and true father of the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon

Sworn this 24th day of Novr. 1794 } M Addington

her Mary Bedwith Mark

Witness Jas Talboys  Beadle

Child born in the Workhouse  29th. Octr. 1794 and named Margt. Rainer

Sent to Enfield  10th. Jany. 1795

Man never taken
