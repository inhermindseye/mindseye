Middx  Ruth Hambleton upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of John Hambleton who hath been dead abt. 4 Months that the sd. John Hambleton was bound an Aprentice to & served his Apprentiship with Mr. Hippoll Ginger Bread Baker in New Street in the parish of St. Martin in the feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. that since his sd. App: her sd. late Husbd. in his Life nor this Exaiant since his Death never rented ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Pson as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 24th. Novr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ruth Hambleton
