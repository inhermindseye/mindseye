Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  Frederick Edward Rickets aged about 25 years upon his Oath saith that about 5 years Ago he Intermarried with Sarah Rebecca his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields that he hath Two Children living by his said Wife vizt. William aged about Four years and Sarah aged about Three Months That this Examinants father Rented and lived in a House in Kenrietta Street Portman Square in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for Fourteen years on thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Thirty Six Pounds that he lived with his said Father in the said House till his Intermarriage and that he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paying Taxes or Serving any Apprenticeship

Sworn this 16th. day of Decr. 1788 J Barnfather N Conant.

Frederick Edward Rickets
