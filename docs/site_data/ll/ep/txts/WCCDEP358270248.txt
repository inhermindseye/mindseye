St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to Wit Sarah Giles aged about 26 years Upon Her Oath Saith that she never was Married, That she lived as a Hired Yearly Servant with the Revd: Dr Thomas Powell at High gate in the Parish of Hornsey in the County of Middlesex , three years at the Yearly Wages of Nine Guineas, that she hath Quitted said Service about three years Back, since which time she hath done no Act or Deed to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon Her Oath saith that on the 16th. day of August now last Past she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child at No. 1. Chair Court, Ship yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes . That one Joseph Draper a Shoe Maker who lived at No. 45 or 46 Chandes Street, but where he lives now this Deponent does not know, is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and Begotten the Same thereon and that the said Joseph Draper and no other Person did Beget the Same and that he is the only Real & True Father thereof

Sworn this 23d. day of Septr: 1795 } J. Hind

her Sarah Giles Mark

Sarah Giles lives at No. 8. Ship yard

Man taken & Bailed

23d. Octr. 95 Order of Adjon made for him to Pay 2s. P week

The Woman Nurses the Child herself and Receives an Allowance from the father
