Middx  Thomas Patrick upon Oath saith her rented a house in Nokceboy Court in the Strand & Parish of St. Clement Danes of the yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds lived therein about four Years and quitted the said house about the Year 1724 and has not Since rented ten pounds by the year or pd. Parish taxes And that in the 28th. May 1716 he was married in the Parish Church of St Margt. Westmr . to Jane his Wife

Sworn 7th. May 1754 before Fran: Bedwell

Tho Patrick
