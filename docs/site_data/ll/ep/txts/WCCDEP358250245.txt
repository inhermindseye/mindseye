Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Mumford aged about 51 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Miss Heard at Highgate in the parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service only about Three Weeks

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1796  N Bond Abraham

her Elizabeth Mumford Mark
