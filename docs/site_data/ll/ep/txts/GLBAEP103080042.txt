Middx ss  Alice Rotherham  Singlewoman Maketh oath that about Twenty Six years ago she lived as a hired servant by the year with one Mr Edwards a Corn factor who then and still lives in Savage Gardens in the Parish of St. Olave in Hart Street in the City of London and Continued in such service for the Space of a year at the yearly wages of Six Pounds and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont saith that she hath not since lived as a hired servant by the year nor done any Act to her knowledge or belief to Acquire a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th day of January 1759 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Her Alice Rotherham Mark
