Middx & Westmr}  Elizth. Donaldson aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband James Donaldson (gone from her) at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields about Nine Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children living Namely Margt . aged about Nine years and Elizth . aged about five Years Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Hungerford Markett in the sd. Parish of St. Martin in the fields upwards of Two years at the yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds hath quitted sd: House upwards of Three Yrs. ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlemt.

Sworn this 22d. day of May 1783 before  Thos: Gilbert  Wm Kitchiner

Elizth. Donaldson
