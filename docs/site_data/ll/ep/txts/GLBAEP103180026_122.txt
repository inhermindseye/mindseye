Middlesex ss}  Anne Wenman  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago her late Father Henry Wenman deced rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards in that part of Fetter Lane which is in the Liberty of London in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn London & resided therein for many years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement & in Which house this Deponent lived with her Said Father as part of his Family That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year in any one place nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 20 day of March 1778 before P Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

Anne Wenman
