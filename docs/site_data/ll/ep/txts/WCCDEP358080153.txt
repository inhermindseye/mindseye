Middx & Westmr.}  John Lees aged about seventy two Years on her Oath says he Intermarried to Catherine his now wife at Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about forty five Years ago and by whom he has one Child now provided for Says he Rented and lived in a House in Pump Court White Hart Yard in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the said County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of £9-10s-0d and paid Land Tax due for the same hath quitted the said House about eighteen Years ago since which time he hath never Rented house or Tenement of £10 a Year paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. Day of July 1777 R Butler Abrm. Baley

John Lees
