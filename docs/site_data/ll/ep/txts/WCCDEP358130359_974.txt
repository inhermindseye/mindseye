Middx  Mary Spencer aged abt. 45 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Wm. Spencer says her said husband served his Appren- ticeship of seven years to Mr. Hutchins a Pipemaker in the Coal yard in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx

Sworn the 6th of June 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Mary Spencer
