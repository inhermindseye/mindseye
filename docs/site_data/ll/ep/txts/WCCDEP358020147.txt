Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Benjamin Wingrove  Master of Saint Clements Workhouse on behalf of Eleanor Mc. Intosh aged about Ten years and John Thos. Mc. Intosh aged about Nine years Two Infants Maketh Oath and saith That he has been Informed by Mr. Intosh the father which Information he believes to be true that the Father of the said Two Children was a Scotchman and never gained any Settlement in England and that the Mother was an Irishwoman and never gained any Settlement in England That Eleanor was born in the Parish of Saint Mary at Nottingham in the County of Nottingham and John was born in the Parish of Wellwyn South Wield near Brentwood in the County of Essex and that the said Childn. never was bound an Apprentice or did any Act to gain any other Settlement

Sworn this 25 day of May 1789 R Butler D Walker

Benjn Wingrove
