Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Harriet Augusta Gardiner aged about 35 - years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wilford in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about serv years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Month Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might ha gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Augt. 1798 . before me  Saml. Edward W Carter

her Harriet Augusta Gardiner Mark

Witness B Newman
