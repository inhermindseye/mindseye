Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Anthony Wright on behalf of William Wright who is ill Maketh Oath and saith that the said William Wright never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Settlement in his own Right That John Wright the father Rented and lived in a house in Saint Alley Sherborne Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary AbChurch in the City of London for several years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and that after the said John Wright quitted the said house he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of May 1798 before us } W Addington Rupt. Clarkes

Anthy Wright
