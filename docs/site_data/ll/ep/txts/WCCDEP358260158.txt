Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Hinton aged about 54 years upon her Oath saith that she Rented and lived in a house in Denzel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about Nine Months at the yearly Rent of Twenty eight Pounds that she quitted said House about Seven Months ago since which time she hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this Ninth day of Novr. 1796 G Reeves H Hinton
