Westmr. ss  Mary Bakewell Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes, Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Doctor Twells the Corner of Queens Court  Great Queen Street  Lincolns Inn Fields in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for Ten Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years & hath not since she quitted her said last Service ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 20th. day of July 1770 before Us} Jno: Wright

Mary Bakewell
