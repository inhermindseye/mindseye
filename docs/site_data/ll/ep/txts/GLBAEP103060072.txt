Middx  Thomas Williams maketh Oath and Saith that about Twenty Eight Years ago his Father Thomas Williams Rented a House in the parish of St. James Westminster in the County of Middlesex and lived therein about Eight Years and paid Twenty four pounds a Year for the same And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And that he has gained no Subsequent Settlement but in Right of his said Father and that he hath a Wife named Elizabeth

Sworn this 11th: day of September 1746 Before Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

Thomas Williams
