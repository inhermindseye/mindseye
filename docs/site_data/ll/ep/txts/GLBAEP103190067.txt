Middlesex ss}  William Savage maketh oath that he can give no Account respecting his late Father John Savage deced has divers times informed him & which this Deponent believes to be true That his sd Father was born in Chipping Norton in the County of Oxford & was the owner of a Small freehold Tenement there & this Deponent Can give no farthr Account respecting his sd Fathers Settlement That this Depont was born in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County of Middlesex & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement in his own right That he hath a wife named Sarah to Whom he was Married to at the Fleet divers Years ago & before the late Marriage act took place

Sworn this 29 day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Wm Savage
