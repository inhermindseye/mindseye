To her Settlemt. by Service before Marriage, her Husband having left her & she not knowing his Settlement

Bastard Child

Middlesex ss}  Lucy Harrison the wife of William Harrisson maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her in Places unknown & hath left her destitute of support & Maintenance And she can give no Account respecting her Said husbants Place of Settlement That she this Deponent some years ago & While a singlewoman was hired for the Space of a year & lived as such hired servant at Certain Wages by & with one William Brooks a Watchmaker in Thames Street in the Parish of St James at Garlick hith in the City of Londn & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of Eleven Months & upwards when She was paid her Wages then due & went away & the Same day after her departure this Deponts sd Mistress Came to her to Come to live with her again & this Depont. thereupon Came back the same night to her sd Masters house & Continued on in such her service for about the Space of two months more so that this Depont remained & Continued in such service for the Space of fourteen months & upwards That she this Depont hath two Children namely Daniel aged about four years & Lewis Aged about two years the Issue of this Deponent by her sd husband to whom she was lawfully Married That she this Depont hath a Child named Elizabeth Boston aged Seven years & upwards which was born in St John Street in the Parish of St Sepulchre London & there baptized & registred the which Child is illegitimate & a Bastard & that one Thomas Boston deced did get her with Child of the sd Bastard Child

Sworn this 25 day of July 1783 before}

The Mark of Lucy Harrison
