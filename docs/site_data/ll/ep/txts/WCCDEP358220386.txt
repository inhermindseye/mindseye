Westmr. to wit Martha Harrison Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of John Harrison (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at Croydon in Surry about five Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says that the last House that her said Husband Rented was in Mermaid Court in the Parish of Saint George in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry who rented the Same hear half a Year at the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds hath quitted the same upwards of Two Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn this 21st. day of Septr. 1772 before} R. Butler 

Martha Harison
