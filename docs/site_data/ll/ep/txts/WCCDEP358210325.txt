Middx & Westmr} to wit The Examination of Elizth Eve of the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  SInglewoman Aged 36 Years or thereabouts taken the 5th Day of Novr 1768

This Examinant Saith on her Oath that She was born as She hath been Informed by her Parents as Mile End Old Town in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the said County of Middlesex and lived as an Yearly Servant at the Wages of Twenty Shillings for the first Year and was to have Forty Shillings the next year for one Year and an half with one Captain Marsh in the said Parish of St Dunstan Stepney and received Wages for that time Accordingly And this Examinant Saith that She hath not lived as an Yearly hired Servant with any Person Since quitting that Service and that She hath not Rent any House or done any thing to gain any Settlemt. elsewhere And this Examt. Saith that upwards of Eight Years ago She was deld. of a femal Bastard Child which was begotten on her Body by John Alldie late of Naimten Butts in the County of Surry Bricklayer lately deced which was born & baptized by the Name of Murry Alldie in the sd parish of Newington

Sworn the Day & Year above sd Before Thos Lane W Kelynge}

Eliz Eve
