Middx  Ann Cuthbert aged upwds. of 40 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Scattergood in Princes Street in the parish of St George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . at the yearly Wages of Four pounds for one Year and upwards and quitted her sd. Service abt. Eight years ago Since which she has not rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn April 9th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Cuthbert
