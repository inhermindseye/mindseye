Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Elizabeth Sharp aged about 81 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1734 she Intermarried with William Sharp her late Husband who has been dead about upwards of 50 years at the Parish Church of Saint Brides That after her said Husbands Decease she this Deponent rented a Shop in Clare Market in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for 9 years at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds during which time she this Deponent lodged at Mr. Blakes in Holles Street That after she quitted said Shop she this Deponent Rented another Shop in the upper Market house for about 16 years and paid the Weekly Rent of Six Shillings for same and paid all Taxes due for the same That after she quitted said Shop which is about Sixteen years ago she this Deponent hath lived ever since with her son James Sharp in the parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 5th day of Janry. 1795 } W Addington

Elizabeth Sharp
