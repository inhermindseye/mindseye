Middx 

Jane Carter  Single woman upon Oath saith that upwards of 20 years ago she lived a yearly Servant with one Mr Clark a Tobacconist next door to the Talbot Alehouse in the Strand in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for One year and upwards at the yearly Wages of Four pounds Says about Six Months after she quitted Mr. Clarks Service she Intermarryed with Thomas Lilley at the Fleet London Says the said Thomas Lilley was a Leather Breeches maker and rented a house near Clements Inn Gate in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes of the yearly Rent of £14 and Lived in the same several Years Says the sd. Thomas Lilley had a Wife liveing at the times of his Intermarriage with this Deponent as she hath been informed beleives

Sworn 31st. May 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Jane Caster
