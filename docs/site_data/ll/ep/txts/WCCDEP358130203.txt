Middx  Mary Kinaston aged abt. 39 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with John Kinaston her late Husband Deced at the Fleet London abt. Twenty Years since, Says the said John Kinaston before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst a Singleman lived a hired Yearly Servant upwards of two Years with Mr. Wright a Shoemaker in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of Thirty Pounds Says She hath not since the Death of her said late Husband Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 4th. Septr. 1756 . before} Barth:Hammond

The Mark of Mary Kinaston
