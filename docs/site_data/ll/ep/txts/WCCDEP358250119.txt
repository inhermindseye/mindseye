Saint Clement Danes , Febry 19th 1794.

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Clement Danes to wit Ann Townsend Widow of Edward Townsend deceased upon her Oath saith that she is forty seven Years of Age or thereabouts, that she was married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Bridget otherwise Saint Brides in the City of London by Banns on the 25th Day of October 1768 That after her Intermarriage her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Greyhound Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes or the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds for about three Years that they quitted the same about twenty one Years ago and that after quitting the same her said Husband in his Life time did not Rent neither hath she this Examinant since his Decease Rented any Messuage or Tenement of the Yearly Valued of ten Pounds paid any Taxes, lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year or done any other Act Matter or Thing Whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 19th. Day of Febry. 1794 before  Wm Kitchiner

Ann Townsend
