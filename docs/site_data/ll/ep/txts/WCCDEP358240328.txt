St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Greenwich Kent  (to wit) Alice Cox  Widow aged about thirty years Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Robert Cox at the parish Church of St Clement Danes in or about the Month of August 1780 that she is informed & believes that previous thereto her said Husband was bound by Indre an Apprentice to Mr Sly Shoemaker in Church Street or Church Lane in the parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent with whom he served abt. three years part of his time and was transferred afterwards turn'd over to another Person of the same Trade in the said parish of Greenwich with whom he served the Residue of his said Apprenticeship (except about two years), that he left his said last Master without his consent and never after lived lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief that he Died about April last and that She hath not since been Married or acquired any legal settlement in her own right

Sworn the 3d day of October 1793 before Us} Rich Ford Bond

Alice Cox 's Mark

Witness Garrett
