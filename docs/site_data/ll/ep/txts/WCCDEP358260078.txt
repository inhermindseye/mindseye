Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of John Webb an Infant aged about Eight years Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that his father John Webb Rented a House at Saint Albans in the Abbey Parish in the County of Herts for several Months and that  after he quitted said house he & his family went into the Workhouse belonging to the Abbey Parish

Sworn this 5th. day of October 1795 before us W Kinnard J Bond

B Newman
