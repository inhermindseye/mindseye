Westmr.  Mary Hobbins aged about 30 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Rainsford a Cutter-near Durham Yard in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Six Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds quitted said Service about Six Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 19th, day of May 1767 . before.} Fran Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Hobbins
