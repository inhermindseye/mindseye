Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr .} to wit Catherine Patterson aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs.. Cole No. 2 George Street Aldgate in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate in the City of London for 17 Months or thereabouts hath quitted said Service about Six Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlemt. And further saith that she hath an Illegitimate Child living vizt. Eleanor Cliffen aged upwards of 3 Months born in Storey Street Hospital

Sworn this 23rd. June 1786  Sampn Wright N Bond

Catherine Patterson
