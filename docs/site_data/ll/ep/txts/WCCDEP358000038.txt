Saint Clement Danes

A Passd. to St. Pancras  William Buckingham aged 43 Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he was Married to Ann his late wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Seventeen years ago. That he hath five Children living by said wife Namely Ann aged 36 years or thereabouts William aged 15 Years or thereabouts who are now both Provided for and Sarah aged 12 years or thereabouts Elizabeth aged 11 years or thereabouts and Benjamin aged 9 years or thereabouts. who are now in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Pancras That his said Wife departed this Life about 3 Years and a half ago. That since their Intermarriage They rented and lived in a House in George Street Battle Bridge in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for about 3 Years at the Yearly rent of Eleven Pounds four Shillings that They quitted then said House about above 10 Years ago since which time he this Examinant hath not any done any Act Matter or Thing whereby the best of her his knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 30th. day of April 1785 before us  Sampr Wright W Addington

Wm. Buckingham
