2d. Examin. Desertion

St Clement Danes August 27th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  3 Children  Desertion (to wit) Mary Carrad aged thirty one years or thereabouts Upon Oath Saith that she was Married to her present Husband William Carrad at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields in the liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex on or about the twenty third day of July 1786 that her said Husband has Rented and Resided in a House and premises in Star Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for four years and upwards at the Annual Rent of Twenty two pounds that he still occupies and Resides in the said House and premises at that Rent that she hath four three Children by her said Husband now with her this Examinant namely Richard aged six years on the Ninth day of May last William aged four years on the 15th day of February last  Mary aged three years on the 30th. day of August 1792 and Thomas born on the 2d day of July in the year 1792 that her said Husband hath  deserted & her and neglects and Refuses to maintain herself and Children & that she is pregnant destitute and unable to provide for herself and family without assistance and they are become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 27th day of Augst 1793 before me} Wm. Kitchiner

Mary Carrad 's Mark

Witness Garrett
