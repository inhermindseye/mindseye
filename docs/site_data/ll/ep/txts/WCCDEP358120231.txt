Edwd. Orfeur aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath saith abt. 17 Yrs. ago he lived wth. Mr Saunders Edwards in St. Clements Inn in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for three Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £40 and during all that time lodged in the sd. Mr Edwards Chambers in Clements Inn aforesaid and quitted the sd Mr. Edwards Service near 14 Yrs. and has not since rented ten pounds by the year pd parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Novr 26th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Edward Orfeur
