St Clement Danes August 6th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Andrew above Bars Middx  (to wit) James Stone  Senr . aged 77 Years or thereabouts and James Stone his Son aged 37 Years or thereabouts severally Make Oath and say And first this Deponent James Stone Senr. for himself saith that he intermarried with Elizabeth his late Wife at the parish Church of St Andrew Holbourn about a Years ago by whom he had only one Child Namely the said James Stone Junr. that this Deponents said Wife Died about 24 Years ago that during his said Marriage he this Deponent with her said Wife Rented and Resided in an Apartment at the House Groom in Red Lyon Street in the parish of St Andrew Holborn above the Bars to the said County of Middlesex for upwards three Months or upward, at the Weekly Rent of six shillings or thereabouts that he quitted the said Lodging several Years ago and hath not since Resided in Rented any House or Lodging of £10 a Year paid any parish Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing Whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement And this Deponent James Stone the Son for himself saith that he hath never obtained or acquired any legal Settlement in his own right and that the Settlement he is informed and verily believes that the last legal Settlement of this Deponent's father the said James Stone is in the said parish of St Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the said County of Middx.

Sworn the 6th August 1792 before us } Thos. Gordon  Sampr Wright

Stone his Jas Stone Mark
