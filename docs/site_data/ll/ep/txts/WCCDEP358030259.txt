Middx  Eliz: White aged about 21 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was never married Says she was delivered of a Stibborn male Bastard Child on the first Day of July last in the house of one Mrs. Ridgemont a parish nurse in Witch Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex Says the Overseers of the poor of the said Parish paid the Nurse for this Exainats Lyeing in she being poor & not able to pay for the same Says one Michael Macdonald a Taylor did beget the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body she this Examinant living a Yearly Servant at the Sign of Porto Bello an Ale house in St. Martins Lane where the said Michael Macdonald became acquainted with her and had Carnal knowledge of her Body the latter End of the Months of Septr. or the beggining of Octr. last was Twelve months and at sevll. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male

Bastard Child she was Delivered of thus aforesaid and that he the said Michael Macdonald & no other Person was the true and reall Father thereof

Sworne Novr. 18th. 1746 before}
