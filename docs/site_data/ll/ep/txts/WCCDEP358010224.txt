Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Benjamin Newman One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and saith That he has been informed which Information he believes to be true John Wilson an Infant aged upwards of Three Months was born a Bastard in Saint Anns Workhouse in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westmr.

Sworn this 10th. day of July 1788 } Addington Edw : Read

Benj: Newman
