Westmr.  Martha Tomkinson aged about 22 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Younger a Shoemaker for thirteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Three Two Pounds and Ten Shillings, says Mr. Younger lived Twelve Month's or thereabts. the first part of this Examinants Service in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and then removed into Russell Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the said Liberty of Westminster where this Examinant lived with the said Mr. Younger One Month or thereabouts and then quitted her said Service and hath not since Rented the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes where she now lodges, she being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born, Says that Mr. James Keal of New Inn in the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body sometime in the Month of May 1765 and at several other times since, at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Keal and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn 9th. Jany. 1766 before} Paul Vaillant

Martha Tomkinson
