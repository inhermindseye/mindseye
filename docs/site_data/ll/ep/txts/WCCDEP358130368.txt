Middx  Eliz: Muirhead aged abt. 37 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Thos. Muirhead (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the fleet London abt. six years says she cannon give any Account of his Settlement & that before her Intermarriage she lived a hired yrly. Servt . with Mr. Bird a Shipwright at the Wet Dock in the Parish of Rotherhithe in the County of Surry for one yr. & Upwds. at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds

Sworn the 13th of June 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

Eliza Muirhead
