St. Clement Danes 15th July 16th.. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary Newington  (to wit) Rebecca Clark  singlewoman aged thirty three Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Smith at No. 4 Charlotte Row Walworth in the parish of Saint Mary Newington in the County of Surrey for a a Year and upwards at the annual Wages of six pounds that She quitted that Service about five or six years ago and hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together under one hiring or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 15th. day of July 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Rebecca Clarks Mark

Witness Garrett
