Westmr. to wit Edward Harris Aged about forty Eight Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Thomas Foley  Esqr . in the Capacity of a Groom in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for five years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Ten Guineas besides Cloaths Diet and Lodging hath Quitted his said Service ever since the Year 1751 and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 3d. day of February 1772 before} Tho Lane R Butler

his Edward Harris mark
