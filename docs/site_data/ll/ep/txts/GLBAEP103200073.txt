Middlesex ss}  Ann Davis  Singlewoman maketh oath that heretofore She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with David Mackey  Victualler in Ratcliffe highway in the Parish of St George in the Sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of Three Years That she was never Married nor hath She Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this. 29. day of November 1783 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Ann Davis
