Middx  Elizth. Smith aged abt. 70 Yrs. upon Oath says her late Husband Henry Smith Rented a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds lived therein abt. Six Years & hath quitted the same abt. 30 Yrs. ago Says her said late Husband never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes, Says She hath not since the Death of her sd. late Husband Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant or Intermarried with any other Person

Sworn the 7th of Janry 1757 before}
