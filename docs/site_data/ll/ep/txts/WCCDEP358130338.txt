Middx  Ann Hannah Treadaway aged abt. Twenty three years Upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her husband Wm. Treadaway in the Parish Church of Chippen Burnett in the County of Hertford on the Twenty Ninth day of Febry 1756 Says her sd. Husband Served Seven years apprentishipp with Mr. Jno. Grayn  Breethes maker at Eaton in the County of Bucks as her sd. Husband informed her & as She verily beleives Says he never rented Ten Pounds a year pd. Parish Taxes or was a hired yearly Servt. has one Child by him Wm . aged abt. four Months

Sworn the 16th. day of May 1757 before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Hannah Treadaway
