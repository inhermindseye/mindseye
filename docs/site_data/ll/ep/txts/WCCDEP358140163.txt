Middx.  Mary Harwood aged abt. 67 Years upon Oath says She is the Wido. of Saml. Harwood who has been dead abt. Ten Years says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yrly. Servant with Mr. Woodhouse an Attorney at Law in Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for four Years at the Yearly Wages of £5 and hath quitted sd. Service abt. five Years. since which time she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of Octr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Harwood
