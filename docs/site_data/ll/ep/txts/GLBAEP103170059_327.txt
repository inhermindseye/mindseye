Middlesex ss}  Ann Litchfield  widow maketh Oath That She Well knows one Elizabeth McBean the Widow of Hugh McBean deced & also knew the Sd Hugh McBean That divers Years ago the sd Hugh McBean Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Eight pounds in Ganglane in the parish of Greenwich in the County of Rent & as She this Deponent has been informed & Verily beliefs was Charged to & paid Poors Rate & Kings Taxes for the Same & By means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that he did not afterward to this Deponents knowledge or beliefs do any Act to gain anothr. Settlement nor hath the sd. Eliz: McBean his Widow Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself to this Deponts knowledge or belief

Sworn this. 28. day of Febr. 1 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Saml Wood

Ann Litchfield
