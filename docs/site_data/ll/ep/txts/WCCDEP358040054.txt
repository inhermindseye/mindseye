Westmr  Martha Rich upon Oath saith she never was married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Johnson a Tallow chandler in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of £5. for one Year and upwards & hath left her sd. Service about ten Months Says she has not since she quitted the sd. Mr. Johnsons Service rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 29th. 1747 before}
