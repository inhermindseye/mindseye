Middlesex ss}  Mary Watley the wife of William Watley maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesahip the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with John Allport a Shoemaker in the Parish of Church Hill in the County of Oxford to her Knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this 12. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Watley
