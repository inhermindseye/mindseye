Middx  Mary Millward aged abt. 28 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. Says her father Peter Millward Rented a house in Mouldmakers Row in the Parish of Chorist Church St. Leonard Foster Lane in the City of London at the Yrly. Rent of Twenty Pounds. & upwards; lived therein abt. Twenty four Years & there dyed

Sworn the 9th of June 1757 . before} Barth. Hammond

Resworn the 10th of June 1757 before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Mary Millward
