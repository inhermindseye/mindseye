Middlesex to wit Mary Charlton Aged about fifty years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to John Charlton (gone from her) at Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland and by whom she hath one Child named Hannah by a former Husband now provided for Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Bennetts Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One year or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds as she believes and paid Land Tax due for the same and quitted said House in the year 1768 and went soon afterwards abroad since which time he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husband left her

Sworn this 14th. day of Febry 1776 before}

her Mary Charlton Mark
