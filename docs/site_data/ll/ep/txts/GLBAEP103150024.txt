Middlesex ss}  Hannah Routledge the Widow of Archibald Routledge deced maketh oath That her said late husband has informed her & which she believes to be true That Some Years ago he was bound to & Served his Apprenticeship with Messieurs Routledge & Lamb in the parish of Saint Mary in Reading  in the County of Berks  Linnen Drapers That this Deponts said husbands Sister has also informed this Deponent and which She believes to be true That her Said late husband also rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds in the same parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponts Knowledge information or belief her said husband did not afterwards rent a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath five Children namely John aged about four Years & four Months & Ann Aged about three Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this 17th day of March 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of Hannah Routledge .
