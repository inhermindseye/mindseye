Middx  Elizth. Graham aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with on Mr. Nixon a Hatter in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year and four Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath Quitted her said Service about Nine Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 15th. day of May 1770 before me } Thos. Kynaston

Eliz Graham
