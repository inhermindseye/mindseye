Westmr. to wit Mary Hallier Aged about Twenty Seven Years On her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or Served any Apprenticeship whatever says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one James Burns a Taylor in King Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Guineas hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this Twenty fourth day of March 1772 before} Tho. Kynaston  John Cox

Mary Hallier
