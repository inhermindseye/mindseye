Middx.  Alice Hopkins aged 48 Yrs. upon Oath says She never was Married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Henry Woodfall a Printer in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. fifteen Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Janry. 25th 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Alice Hopkins
