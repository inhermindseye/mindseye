Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah  Elizabeth Greives aged about 67 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House paid Taxes Lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born in the House of a Mr. Henley a Coalmerchant in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx that she this Ext never Rented any Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 or done any Act whereby she may have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 23rd. day of June 1787 R Butler

Elizth. Greives
