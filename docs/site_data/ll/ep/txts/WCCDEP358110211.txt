Mary  Ann Patty Wife of Tho Edward Patty upon Oath Saith she was married to the sd. Edwd. Patty on the 16th. Day of Novr. 1746 in the Parish Church of St. Ann Blackfryers and has by him three Children Eliz aged between two & three Years and Eliz & Sarah Twins aged five Weeks and further that she has been informed, her sd husband never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Parish Taxes and that his father Edward Patty Served his Apprentiship with John Dry a Sawyer in Horton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. which Information this Examint. believes to be true

Sworn 15th March 1753 before}

The Mark of Ann Patty

Ann Patty  wife of Thos. Patty Says her his & as father Ser his true wth. Mr Dry a Sarvig  & that she has Mary & Child aged upwds. 4
