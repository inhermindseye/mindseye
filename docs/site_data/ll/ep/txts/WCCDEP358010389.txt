St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) John Worthington Aged abts. thirty years Upon his Oath saith that he served his Clerkship  with Str. Peart & with whom he was Articled but Boarded Lodged with his Father in Holborn in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex That after the Term for which he was Arcticled expired he lived with them him & Messrs Est County, Shilling as an hired yearly Clerk  for five years or thereabouts & and that during the lather part of the time he belived with them for three years afterwards he lody in Thorney Street of Bloomsbury and  saith that since he so served his Clerkship he has not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby he Might gain any  other Settlement to the best of his Knowledge or belief

Sworn the 24th day of February 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

J Worthington
