Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William Frier aged about 37 years upon his Oath saith that about Nine years ago he Intermarried with Margaret his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields by whom he hath Three Four Chidren living namely Elizth. aged about 8 years Frances aged about 6 years Mary Ann aged about 4 years and William aged about 18 Months That he this Examinant was born at Rumsey in the Parish of Extra in Hampshire that his Father kept house there several years and that he this Examinant lived with his said Father till he was 24 years of Age and that he has never Rented any house Tent. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st- day of March 1789 R Butler D Walker

Wm: Fryer
