Middx  The Examination of Anne Walton on the Behalfe of Ann Walton an Infant aged abt. Six Years in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 11th. Day of May 1741 Who upon her Oath saith yt. Jno. Walton the Father and Ann Walton the Mother of the sd. Infant are both dead & that the sd. Jno. Walton lived in & rented a House in White Hart Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for the Space of 3 Years & upwds- & that after the Death of Anne Walton the Mother this Examint. Intermarried with the sd. Infants Father Jno. Walton who hath been dead abt. one Year & Three Quarters & further upon her Oath saith that the sd. Jno. Walton never after rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one

Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knoweldge or beleife & this Examinant being Poor is not able to maintaine the sd. Infant

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed, before J Poulson W Hayton

Ann Wallton
