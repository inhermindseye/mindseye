Middx  Eleanor Maddan Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one M Mackean at the Temple Hall in Shire Lane in the Pa of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for th Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted said Service about Eighteen Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Ch which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that James Clarke a Journey Baker (who lately worked at a Baker on the Right Hand side of Salsbury Court Fleet Streets who works in the Daytime as a Tallow ship Porter betwixt Billing  & Tower Wharf ) is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her this Examinants Lodging at one Mr Murphys in Drury Lane the begining of April last and a one other time afterwards at one of which times he got her Child of the Child or Children of which she is new pregnant that the said James Clarke and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 4th. day of Novr. 1779 before}
