Middlesex ss}  Jane Hall  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre by the then Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St Giles Cripplegate London to Mrs Clements a Weaver of Ironmonger Row in the Parish of St Luke in the Sd County of Middlesex & after having Served about five years of her said Apprenticeship there her said Mistress absented herself from this Deponent & this Deponent never afterwards lived or resided with her said mistress elsewhere That she this Deponent since hath not lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year elsewhere nor hath she been ever married nor hath she to her Knowledge or belief done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29 day of October 1785 before R Davies R Smith

The Mark of Jane Hall
