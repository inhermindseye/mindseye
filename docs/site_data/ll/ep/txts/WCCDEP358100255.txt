Middx to wit John Allmett one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of five Children which the Father & Mother has deserted (vizt.) Ann Aged about Thirteen years Elizth . Aged about Seven years Edwd . Andrew aged four years James aged upwds. of two years & Thomas aged about fourteen Months on his Oath says that he has been credibly Informed that John Woolley the Father of the said Five Children served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Scuffom a Coach Harness maker in the Markett. Place at Kingston upon Thames in the Parish, of Kingston in the County of Sunny & that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or or wise gained any or. Settlemt.

Sworn this 13th. day of Septr. 1784 R. Taylor Jno Barnfather

John Allmett

Sworn this 15th. day of Septr. 1784 R. Taylor Jno. Machin

John Allmett
