Middx & Westmr-}  Eleanor Kidd of the Parish of St- Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Singlewoman on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lives a hired Yearly servant with Mr- Jno- Bowers a Grocer in Drury Lane in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and has so done for upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of £6 and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one William Bowers a Shopman to Mr- John Bowers of Drury Lane aforesaid is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body about the beginning of October last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl- difft- times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant & that the said Wm- Bowers and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 22nd- Day of April 1777 . before me R. Butler

Enala Kidd
