Middx  Henry Shone Aged about Sixty nine years on his Oath says he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Harris a Butcher in Red Lion Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his Apprenticeship he went and lived in divers places as a Weekly Servant only untill about Twenty seven years ago as he believes when he went to live as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Hudson whose Wife Kept a Butcher Shop in Bath Street  Cold Bath fields in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex upwards of Six years at the yearly Wages of Ten Pounds a year besides Diet and Lodging hath quitted his said service upwds. of Twenty years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. by living a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 8th. day of October 1783  Sampn Wright  Wm Kitchiner

his Henry Shone mark
