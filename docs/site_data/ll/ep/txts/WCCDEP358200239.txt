Middx.  Mary Keeling aged about 50 Years lodging at Mrs. Raynes in Houghton Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex upon Oath says, she is the Widow of William Keeling who has been dead about seven Years with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London about four and twenty Years ago, says her said late husband Rented a house in Sawyers Yard Clements Lane in the aforesaid parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds, lived therein about one Year and a half and quitted same near Eight Years, says her said husband never after in his life time, neither hath she since his Death Rented house paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn 1st. March 1765 , before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Keeling
