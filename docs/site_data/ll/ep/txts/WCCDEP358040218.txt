Middx 

Eliz. Barlow aged upwds. of 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Robt. Hadwin at the Elephant & Castle near Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds for one Year & upwards and quitted her sd. Service about 16 Months ago since which time she has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that John Long  Servant to Mr. Staples a Whale bone merchant at E in the reported did legal the sd. bastard Child Children on her body the sd. John Long having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the latter Exd of the month of January or the beginning of February last and at

several other times since of which in the house of the sd. Mr. Staples where this Examinant lived fellow Servant with him and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of she sd. bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that he the sd. John Long & no man also is the true & real father thereof

Sworn July 18th. 1748 before} J Coulson

The Mark of Eliz Barlow
