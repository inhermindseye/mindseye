Middx  Robt Cobham aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his Prest. Wife  Sarah at May Fair Chapel  on the 11th. Augt. 1745 & hath by her one Child Jas . aged abt. 8 months Says her sd. late Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Crang Organ maker at the Elephant & Castle in the Strand in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middlx & has been out of his time abt. 6 yrs. & never rented ten pounds a year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 26th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Robert Cobham
