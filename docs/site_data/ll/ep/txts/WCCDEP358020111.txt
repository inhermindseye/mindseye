Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Tremlett aged about 30 yeares upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Pearson in Cooks Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Nine Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Four Months years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she hath Two illegitimate Children living vizt. Henry aged about 3 years born at the House of a Mr. Marley in Ogle Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex And Thomas aged about seven Months born in Rodneys Row Shadwell in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell

Sworn this 29th. day of Novr. 1788 R Butler

Elezabth Tremlett
