Middx  Geo: Menelaus aged abt. 60 Yrs. upon Oath saith he lived a yearly Servant with Mr Jas. French  Taylor in Norfolk Strt. in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for three Years at the Yearly Wages of £4 for the first Year £6 for the Second Year and £9 for the third Year that he was a Singleman all the time of his sd. Service which he quitted abt. 40 Yrs. ago and has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Feby. 24th 1753 . before} Fran: Bedwell

George Menelaus
