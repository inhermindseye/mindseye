Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Lydia Lafortune aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1758 she Intermarried to Anthony Lafortune her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields that her said Husband has been dead about 11 years that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Feather bed Lane in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for about 11 years at the yearly Rent of £20 hath quitted said House about 12 years since which him she hath done no net Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 30th- day of Septr. 1786 D Walker Russ: Clark

her Lydia Mark
