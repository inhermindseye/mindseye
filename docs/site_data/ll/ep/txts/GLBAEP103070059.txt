Middx.  Mary Wicks  Singlewoman Maketh oath that abt. Sixteen Years ago She this Depont. lived as a hired Servant with Mr. Nathaniel Collyer of the Parish of St. Mildred in the City of London  Linen Draper at the Yearly Wages of fifty Shillings & Continued in Such Service for the space of Seven Years And this Depont. hath not Since gained any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 7th day of March 1752 before us  Richd. Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

Mary Wickes
