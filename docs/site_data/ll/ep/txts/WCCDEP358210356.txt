Middx & Westmr}  William Upsdale Aged about thirty three Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his late Wife at the Parish Church of St Giles in the Fields about Ten Years ago by whom he has Two Children now living Namely William Aged about Seven Years and John Aged upwards of Three Years Says before his said Intermarriage with his said late Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Thos. Perry  Carpenter in Drury Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr and Served Two Years the first part of his Apprenticeship and then his Master & this Examinant parted by Consent Says he never was Assigned over to any other Master nor ever Rented a House of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th Day of Janry 1769 Before me} Tho: Lane

William Upsdale
