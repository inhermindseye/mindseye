St Clement Danes September 7th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary Whitechapel (No Appeal.)  to wit ' Mary Hughes aged 49 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her George Hughes at the parish Church of Saint Martin Ludgate London about twelve years ago that She Subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented a House in Swan Yard in the parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex And Resided therein for ab three Months and upwards at that she hath been informed and believes that the Rent thereof was £10 a year that he quitted that House about 5 years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief that he left her about Nine Months ago and hath not ob heard from him since and knows not whether he is living or Dead

Sworn the 7th day of September 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Mary Hughes 's Mark

Witness Richd. Dobbins
