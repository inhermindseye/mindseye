Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to Sarah Pointon Aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one John Shredd in Stacey Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx for 18 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about 4 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant on her oath Saith that on the 12th. day of June 1785 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of a Mr. Smallwood a Taylor in Yeales's Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of John Pointon That one Richard Oxley a Coal merchant who lived in Compton Street and has been dead about 12 Months ago was the Father of the said Child he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body and begotten the same thereon And that the said late Richard Oxley and no one else did beget the said Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and

that he was the only Real and true father and that she is Poor and unable to maintain the same

Sworn the 27th. Day of Octr. 1786 before} R Butler

her Sarah Pointon mark
