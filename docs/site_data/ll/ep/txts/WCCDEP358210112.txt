Westmr.  Mary Lowndes aged about 44 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Samuel Lowndes who has been dead about One Month, says her said Husband Rented a house in Charles Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds, for Eight Years or thereabouts quitted said House about Eighteen Years ago & never after Rented house or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 3d, of Decr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant W Kelynge

Mary Lowndes
