Westmr.  Sarah Bawler aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Gotobed in Peter Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly wages of Four pounds for two Years or thereabouts & has not Since she quitted her sd. Service (which is about ten Years ago) rented Ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Septr. 28 1752 before}

The mark of Sarh. Bawler
