St Clement Danes May 21st 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Clement Danes  (to wit) Michael Jones aged about 54 years Upon Oath saith that he was Married to Mary his Wife at the Chapel in the Savoy on or about the twentieth day of January 1783 (by Ba that subsequent thereto he Rented and resided in an unfurnished Apartment at the House No 26 Wych Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for five years at the annual Rent of £1 for about the first two years and of Fourteen Guineas for the Remainder of the said Term that he quitted the same in the Month of December 1789 and hath not obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the beat of his Knowledge or belief

Sworn the 21st. May 1794 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Witness  Michl. Jones
