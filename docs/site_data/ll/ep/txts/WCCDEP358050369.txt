Middx  Mary Evans aged upwds. of 23 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that upwards of two Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Wheeler Cheesmonger in Clare Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly wages of three Pounds for two Years and upwards and has not since she quitted her said Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of St Clemt. Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that John Bridgen a Fortman to Sr Henry Penrice at Offley in Hertfordshire did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body this Examinant

at such time living with her father and mother at Offley aforesaid there the said John Bridgen became acquainted with her and had carnal knowledge of her body several times in the month of April last at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant and that the said John Bridges and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn 21st. Novr. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Evans
