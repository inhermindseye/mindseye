Middx.  Eliz: Wright aged abt. 46 years upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Wright (gone from her) says her said husband Rented a house of Ten Pounds a year in Aldersgate Street in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldersgate in the City of London for six yrs. or thereabout & hath quitted same abt. twenty years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a yr. or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 23d. of May 1757 . before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Eliz: Wright
