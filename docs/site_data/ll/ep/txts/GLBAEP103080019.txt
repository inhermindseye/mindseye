Middx ss  Sarah Darling Maketh oath That about forty years ago Joseph Walton duly was  apprentice to Mr Palmer a Waterman and Lighterman in Thames Street in the Parish of Allhallows Berking in the City of London and duly served his apprenticeship & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent saith that the sd. Joseph Walton to her knowledge or belief do was never a hired servant by the Year Nor Rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards nor served any Parish offices Nor gain any subsequent settlement And this

Sworn this 23 day of September 1757 before us  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Sarah Darling
