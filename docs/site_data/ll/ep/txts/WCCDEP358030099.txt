Middx  Eliza. Hope removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx & by Order dated the 18th. of Febry Instt. upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her Husbd. James Hope about 3 Yrs. since at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband was born in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex Says he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year Served a Parochiall Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says her Husbands Father Edward Hope rented a house in Witch Street in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes of £20 a Yr. & upwds & lived therein 20 Yrs. Says after her sd. Husbands Father left sd. House he rented another house in Witch Street in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes of £10 a Yr. & upwards & lived therein one Year & hath left same upwards of Seven Years Says her sd. husbands Father

Edward Hope never after rented a house in Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 19th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz. Hope
