Middx  Richd. Forshall upon Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizth . his present Wife at abt. Years since by whom he hath Three Children (to wit) Elizth. aged abt. five Years Ann aged abt. Three Years & Jane aged upwds. of one Year Says he Rented a House in Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds lived therein abt. & hath quitted the same abt. Twelve Months Says he never after Rented a House paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office.

Sworn Octr. 19th. 1756 before}
