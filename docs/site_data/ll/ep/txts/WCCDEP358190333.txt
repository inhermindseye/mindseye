Middx  Elizth Carr Aged upwards of Sixty Years Upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Robt Carr who was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England Says before her Intermarriage with the said Robt. Carr and whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr Gamlin in Bedford Court in the Parish of St Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster two Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and never Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 23 Day of March 1763 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Elizth Carr
