Middx  Ann Bray upon her Oath saith she inter- -married with her Husband Leonard Bray on the 27th. Day of March abt. 17 Years since in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland Says her sd. Husband was born in Abbe Lace in Queens County in the sd. Kingdom of Ireland & lived in & rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £20 in Coles Lane in Henry Street in the parish of St. Mary aforesaid in the City of Dublin for the Space of five Years or thereabouts Says her sd. Husband hath not since he left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says the sd. Leonard Bray has been abroad & beyond the Sens upwards of twelve months last past and further upon her Voluntary

Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to be chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born & that one Lawrance Williams a Shoemaker is the reputed Father the sd. Lawrance Williams having had carnall knowledge of this Examinants Body in the month of April last in the house of one Mrs. Hall in Newmers Lane in the parish of St. Giles in the Feilds and at several other times as well before the sd. Month of April as since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. Lawrance Williams & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworn July 30th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Ann Brey
