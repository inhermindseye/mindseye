Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Ousman aged about 38 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 5th. day of February 1787 he Intermarried with Elizabeth his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Philip in Birmingham by whom he hath Three Children living Vizt. William aged about Nine years Ruth aged about Three years and Thomas aged about Twelve Months That previous to his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant for with one John Blest of Eccleshall in the Parish of Eccleshall in the County of Stafford for Three years and upwards at the yearly wages of Fifty Shillings That he hath quitted said Service upwards of Twenty years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of June 1797 before us  N Bond Jr. Solsey.

William Ousman
