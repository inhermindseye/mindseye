Middx to wit Elizabeth Forbes aged about Forty Eight Years on her Oath says she Intermarried with her late Husband Francis Forbes at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster about Seventeen Years ago and who died about Two Years ago and by whom she hath four Children now living Namely Mary Aged about Fifteen Years now Provided for William aged about Thirteen Years who is also Provided for Elizth . aged about Eleven Years and Francis aged about Nine Years says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Round Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds hath quitted the same about Six Years ago and after quitting the same her said Husband never Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pound a Year Paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 12th. day of Augt. 1779 before us R Butler Abrm Baley

Eliz Forbes
