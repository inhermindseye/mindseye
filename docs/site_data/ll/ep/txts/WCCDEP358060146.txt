Middx  Eliz: Pearce  Wife of Robt. Pearce (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Rt. Pearce at Lincolns Inn Chapel about 3 Yrs. ago & has by him one Child viz. Robt . aged about 5 Weeks and that at the time of her marriage with the sd. Robt. Pearce she was the Wido . of Thos. Farrand with whom she intermarried abt. 15 Yrs. ago at the Church of St. Bennet Pauls wharf & who dyed abt. 4 Yrs. since leaving her with 3 Childn. who are Stile living (viz) Thos . aged about 9 Yrs. Eliz aged upwards of 7 Yrs. & Joseph aged Six Years and further saith that her sd. late husband Thos. Farrand rented a house in Ship Yard in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £26. & lived therein a considerable time & there dyed & her prest. husband Robt. Pearse (after his intermarriage with this Examinant) continued to rent the sd. house & lived therein abt. 18 months & afterwards rented a house in Witch Street in the same parish of St Clemt Danes of the Yearly Rent of £16 and lived therein tile the time of his absconding from this Examinant

Which was abt. one week ago and she this Examinant continues stile in the last mentioned house

Sworn Augt. 2d. 1751 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz Pearse
