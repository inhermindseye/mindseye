St. Clement Danes September 27th 1793

Middlesex And Westminster }  St Pancras  (to wit) Esther Griffiths  singlewoman aged about 64 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Richard Jones for eight years and upwards at the Annual Wages of eight Guineas that during the last five Months of such Service she she same lived lived with her said Master at the Welch Charity School in the Grays Inn Lane Road in the parish of St Pancras in the County of Middlesex until the time of his decease on or about the 3d day of September 1792 and that She hath not since that time done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 28th day of September 1793 before us W: Blamire J Hole

Esther Griffiths
