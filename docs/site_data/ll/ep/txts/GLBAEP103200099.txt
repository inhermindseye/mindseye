Middlesex ss}  Christian Wigmore the widow of John Wigmore deced Maketh oath that her Said late husband heretofore in his life time rented a house of the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds near Globe yard in the Parish of St George in the Said County & resided thereon for Several years as she this Depont has been Credibly informed & believes & which She believes to be true That her Sd husband to her knowledge or belief has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain anothr Settlement

Sworn this 12 day of Febry 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Christian Wigmore
