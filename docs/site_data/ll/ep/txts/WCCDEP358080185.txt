Middx & Westmr-}  John Davis aged about 26 Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Plumber at the Talbot Inn in Shrewsbury upwards of three Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds besides Bed and Board hath quitted the said Mr- Plumbers Service about eight Years ago and after quitting the above mentioned Service he came to London and has lived in several different Places the last of Which was with Messrs. Redform & Hollingsworths Livery Stables in Little Moor Fields in the Parish of St- Giles's Cripplegate London but says he never was hired into such Place but that this Examinant was to have 5 Shillings a Week besides Bed and Board, and hath continued in such last mentioned Service for upwards of two Years and hath not gained any or- subseqt- Settlemt- than as before mentioned

Sworn this 24th- Day of Novr- 1777 before R Butler

his John Davis Mark
