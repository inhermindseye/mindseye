Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Gladman aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that on the Eighth day of Janry. last past she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Novice in Crown Court Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the Name of Hannah That one Andrew Watson who worked with a Mr- Milcolm near Kennington Green is the Real and true father of such Child he having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that he is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 26th. day of february 1789 } Wm Kitchiner

Mary Gladman

Andrew Watson is a a Middle sized Man rather Stout has a Mole on his Right Cheek is about 22 years fair Complexion and light Hair.
