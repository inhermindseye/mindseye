Middx  Jeremiah Surman upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with his present Wife about Seven Years since That about Seven Years ago he lived in & rented a House in York Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of Sixty Pounds & lived in the same near Twelve Months That since he left the said House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife That he hath one Child (to wit) James aged Eleven Years & being Poor is not able to maintaine himself & Family

Sworn the 17th- Novr. 1742 before} Tho Lane

Jere Surman
