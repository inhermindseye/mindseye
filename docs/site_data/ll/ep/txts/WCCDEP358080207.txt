Westmr. to wit Simon Furrier Aged about Forty One Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with his late Wife  Ann at the Parish of Saint Sepulchre London in the Year 1765 and who has been dead upwards of five Years ago and by whom he has no Child or Children living And this Examinant saith that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Thos. Sones a Watchcase maker in Lilly pott Lane Noble Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Staining London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds Per Annum paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Janry 1778 before me  Wm Kitchiner

Simon Furrier
