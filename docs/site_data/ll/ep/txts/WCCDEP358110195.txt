Middx  Fras. York upon Oath saith about fifteen Years ago being then a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Abel Kettleby  Esqr . in Essex Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and has not since he quitted Mr. Kettlebys Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Febry 27th. 1753 before} Tho Lane

Francis York
