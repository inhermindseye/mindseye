Middx ss  Catherine Smith Maketh oath that about Nineteen Years ago her Brother George Jenant lately deced (as he has often informed this Depont. as this Dept. verily believes) was duly bound apprentice to Richard Franks of ye Parish of St. Anne Limehouse in ye County of Middx with whom he Served Seven Years & by Means there of gained a legal Settlemt in ye sd. Parish And this Dept believes that her sd. Brother did not afterwards gain any Subseqt. Settlemt. And this Depo that her sd. Bror. left behind Child Named Elizabet Jennant aged about Eight years ye lawfull issue her sd Bror . & his Wife Deced

Sworn ye 15th. of Decr 1750 before us  Rd. Ricards  Saml Gower

his Catherine Smith Mark
