Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Lee Aged about 18 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married never bound apprentice or lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or rented any House tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement in her own right That she hath been informed which information she verily believes to be true That her Father served a regular Apprenticeship to a Baker in Silver Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex that since he hath been out of his time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 20th. Day of Decr 1799 before us} Ws Woodnough

Har Susannah Lee Mark
