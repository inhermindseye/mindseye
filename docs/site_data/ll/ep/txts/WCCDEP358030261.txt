Middx  Mary Brickley aged abt. 27 Yrs. living in St. Clemts, Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes upon Oath Says she intermarryed with her husband John Brickley at the Fleet London on the 26th. Day of Decr. 1741 Says her sd. husband Inlisted himself for a Soldier in General Armstrongs Regt. & went to Port Makon upwards of two Years & a half since and this Examinant has been informed that her sd. husband dyed there abt. Six Months ago and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. parish she being poor & not able to maintain the same when born Says one Richard Oak at the Dukes head in Bow Street in Bloomsbury did be get the said Child or Children on her Body she this Examinant having Servt. with the said Mr. Oak he the said Mr. Oake

had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of March last in White Lyon Court in Dutchy Lane in the Precinct of the Savoy and at sevll. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastd. Child or Children she is now pregnant with and that he the sd. Richd. Oak & no other person is the true & wall father thereof

Sworn 18th. Novr. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Brickley
