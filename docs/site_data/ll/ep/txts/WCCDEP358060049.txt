Middx  Eliz. Parker aged abt. 70 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 30 Years ago she Lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Askey a Midwife opposite Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlx for, Ten Years at the Yearly Wages of £4 and that she has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 12th. 1750 before}

The mark of Eliz Parker
