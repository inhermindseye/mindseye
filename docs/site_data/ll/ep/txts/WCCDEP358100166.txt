Middx & Westmr.} to wit George Berkenhead aged about Thirty Seven years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Sarah his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Mary at Cambridge in the year 1774 by whom he hath two Children namely Paul aged Six years & Samuel aged four years Says before his Intermarr. to his said Wife he served seven years Apprenticeship to Wm. Long a Carver & Gilder in Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . & after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10. a year Paying Taxes or or.wise howsoever

Sworn this 17th. day of Jany. 1784  Tho. Collins  James Croft

Geo. Berkenhead
