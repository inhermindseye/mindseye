Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Thomas Jones aged about 30 years upon his Oath saith that about Nine years ago he Intermarried with Charlotte Jones his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom he hath One Child living Vizt. Solomon aged about Twenty Months That he this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Burnett in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about fifteen Months at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Sixteen years since which time he hath done no Act Matter on thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th- day of Septr. 1796 G J Goodenough

her Thos. Jones Mark
