Middx  Biddy Jackson Aged about Twelve Years Upon Oath Says she was bound an Apprentice by and with the Consent of the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. to Mrs Tinsley in Paradise Row in the Parish of St Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry and lived with her in the said Parish of St Mary at Lambeth upwards of Four Months

Sworn the 24 Day of March 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Biddy Jackson
