Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.}  Thomas Buck aged 38 years or there abouts on his Oath saith that he was married to Ann his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square about 10 years ago and hath One Child by her now living vizt. Thomas aged 8 years or thereabouts and that he was married to Sarah his present Wife at the said Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square about 4 years and an half ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children living and that the last time he heard of her she was in a Salivation in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint George Hanover Square and that when he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Colonel Fitzroy in Stretton Street in the sd. Parish of Saint George Hanover Square for 4 years and upwards and was all the time in the same Street and there discharged and have not since Quitting that Service when Single done any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Exts. knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Janry 1786 before M Addington N Bonds

Thos. Buck
