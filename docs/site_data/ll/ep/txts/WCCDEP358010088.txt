Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Elizth. Staples aged 39 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that She never was married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Richardson a Disenting Minister in Little Moor fields in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate in the City of London for upwards of 12 Months at the yearly wages of £5 that the hath quitted his said Service about 14 or 15 years ago and since then have never lived as an hired yearly Servant for one year with any other Person Rented any House or Tenement of the yearly Rentor Value of £10 paid Taxes or done any or Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief She may have gained or acquired any Subsequent Settlement and that she is poor & Sick & unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 10th. day of April 1787  Rt. Taylor G Mercer

Eliz Staples
