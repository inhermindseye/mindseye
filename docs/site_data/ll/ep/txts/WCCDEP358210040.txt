Westmr.  Mina Baker aged about 21 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Giles Baker with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Outwell in the County of Cambridge on or about the 17th. day of March 1765, by whom she hath one Child (to wit) Thomas aged about Six Weeks; says her said husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that before his intermarriage with her this Examinant, being at that time a Single Man and without Child or Children he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Young a Surgeon in Great Russell Street  Bloomsbury in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx, for one Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds and hath quitted said Service near two Years And never Rented house or paid Taxes; Says her said husband is gone from her and she being poor is not able to Maintain herself & Child

Sworn April 12th. 1766 before} Paul Vaillant  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Mina Baker
