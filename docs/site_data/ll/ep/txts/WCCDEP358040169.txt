Westmr.  Richd Bennett aged about 60 Years (one of the Rople called Quakers) upon his Soloman Affirmation Saith he intermarried with his Wife Margaret in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about 34 Years since and that about 4 Years ago he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £12 for Seven Years or thereabts. in Maypole Alley in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

This affirmation was made the 9th. April 1748 before me} Wm: Jones

Richard Bennett
