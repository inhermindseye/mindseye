Middx.  Ann Wright aged abt. fifty Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Wright who has been dead about fourteen Years, says her said husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Norris a Peruke maker in St. Clements Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never Rented house or paid Taxes as her said husband often times informed her and which information she verily believes to be true, says she has not since her said husbands Death Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for One Year

Sworn the 19th. day June 1765 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Ann Wright
