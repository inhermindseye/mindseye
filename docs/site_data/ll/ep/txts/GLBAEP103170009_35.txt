Middlesex ss  Rebecca Mesteyear the wife of Henry Mesteyear maketh oath that her Said husband is gone away from her to places unknown & left her destitute of Support & Maintenance That She this Deponent has been lately informed by Mr Shervin one of the present Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields Westmr . in the said County That divers years ago her said husband lived in & rented a house in Martlock Court in the said parish of Saint Martin in the Fields Which house this Deponent has Seen & apprehands & Verily believes the Same to have been let to her Said husband at more than the Rent of Ten pounds by the year That this Deponents husband to her knowledge information or belief has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that She was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn the 21st day of September 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Marks

Rebecca Mesteyear
