Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Merciea Chambers aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or Served any Appceship that she was born at in Trinity Parish in the City of Ely in the Isle of Ely that she has done no Act to gain a Settlement alias than by Birth And further the Ext. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are like to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes That one William Smith  Clerk to Mr. Drummond the Barker who lodges at Dr. Reynolds at Charing Cross is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Ext. Body sometime in the Month of Septr. last and at One other time since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Smith is only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of April 1787 } R Butler

Merciea Chambers

Warrt. made out.
