Middx  Ann Gorman  Singlewoman aged abt. Nineteen Years upon her Oath saith that her Father & Mother were both Natives of Ireland & never rented Ten Pounds a Year or lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. whereby to gain a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or beleife that her Mother Rose Gorman hath been dead upwds. of Six Years & her Father went to France in abt. Six Months after the Death of her Sd. Mother that this Examinant hath not heard from him since & where he now is knows not that this Exaiant was born in Little St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . says she never was an Appren- -tice or Yearly hired Servt. for One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 9th. of Augt. 1741 before us} J Bromfeild

The Mark of Ann German
