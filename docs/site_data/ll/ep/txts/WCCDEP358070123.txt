Westmr. to wit Peter Taylor Aged about Twenty Two Years on his Oath Says he never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says he was bound an Apprentice to and served Years Apprenticeship with one Mrs. Edward a Chimney- Sweeper in Lukeners Lane otherwise Charles Street in the Parish of Saint Giles's in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 26th. day of Augt. 1773 before me R. Butler

his Peter Taylor Mark Witness Petr Sidebotham
