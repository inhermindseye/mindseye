Westmr.  Susanna Sampson aged about 40 Years upon Oath says she never was married, That She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. White in that part of Cecil Street in the Strand which is in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of a Year, at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds, and quitted same about Eight Years ago and hath not since lived in any other Service for one Year nor Rented Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 1st. day of October 1767 . before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The mark of Susanna Sampson
