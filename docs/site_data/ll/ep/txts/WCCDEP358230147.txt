Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Gregory Aged about 28 years upon her Oath Saith That about Eleven years ago she intermarried with Thomas Gregory her present Husband (who has deserted her) at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square by whom she hath living One Child Vizt. Sarah aged about Six years That her said Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr Worthy a Taylor in Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex That since he hath been out of his time he hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for twelve Months rented any House tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the Best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 9th day of August 1800 before us} John Collick  Benjm Gee

The Mark of Ann Gregory
