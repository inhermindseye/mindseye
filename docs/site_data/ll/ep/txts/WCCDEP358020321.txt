Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Eleanor De Fereand  Wife of William De Fereand Aged 39 years or thereabout Upon her Oath saith that her Mother Elizabeth Watson is now living with this Deponent of the Age of Sixty years or thereabouts, now Infirm as well Mentally as Bodily, that she in the wife of Richard Watson new in the Workhouse belonging to the parish of St. Giles Chipplegate in the Liberty and Freedom of the City of London that this Depont. hath been informed and believed that her said Mother & her said Husband were Married at the parish Church of St Luke in the County of Middlesex on or about the 19th day of November 1760 that the said Richd Watson  While was bound by Indenture of Appren ticeship to a John Priest  Citizen & Brass Founder of Greek Street in the parish of St Giles's Cripplegate ale London Resident in the parish of St Giles Cripplegate in the Liberty of the said City and regularly served his Apprenticeship And that since he hath not Gained any subsequent legal Settlement as this Deponent hath been informed and believed and that her said Mother is now unable support herself

Sworn the 25th July 1791 before us  Sampr Wright Richd 

Eleanor De Fereand
