Middlesex ss}  Ann Morgan  Singlewoman maketh oath That many years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year by & with John Gardiner in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County Victualler at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant elsewhere for the Space of a year nor hath She been ever married or done any Act to gain anothr Settlemt

Sworn this 27. day of January 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Ann Morgan
