Middlesex ss}  Mary Evans the wife of John Evans maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one John Smith a Shoemaker in the Town of Plymouth in the County of Devon & did not afterwards to her knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She was lawfully married to her said husband by whom She hath a Child & lawful Issue now living named  John aged about Eleven Months.

Sworn this 31. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Evans
