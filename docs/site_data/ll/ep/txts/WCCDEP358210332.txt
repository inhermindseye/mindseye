Middx & Westmr} to wit Mary Dawney  Singlewoman Aged Sixty Yrs and upwards upon her Oath Says She never was Married was an Apprentice or Rented a House of Ten pounds a Year or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year Says her Father Jas. Dawney Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one a Shoemaker in the parish of St. Giles Cripplegate in the City of London whose Name She cannot now recollected Says her said Father about Two Years after he was out of his said Apprenticeship went for a Soldier and was in Colonel Honey Woods Company for many Years and died in Chelsea College or Hospital and never after his said Apprenticeship Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as her said father hath often Informed this Examinant and which Information She Verily believes to be true

Sworn this 21st. Day of Novr. 1768 Before us} Tho: Lane  Thos: Kynaston

her Mary Dawney Mark
