Middx  John Leonard Blackmoore removed from the Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel by Order dated the 6th. Novr. Instant upon Oath says he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Philip Martin Taylor in Water Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and hath been out of his time abt. 26 Years Says he has not since the Expiration of his sd. Apprentiship rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 7th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Jno. Leond. Blackmoor
