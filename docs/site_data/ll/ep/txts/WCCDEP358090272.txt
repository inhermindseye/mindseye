Middx to wit Francis Lissner Aged about Thirty Eight Years on behalf of Samuel Winspear (who now lies ill of a Fever) on his Oath Says that the said Samuel Winspear this Examinants Journeyman served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. French a Glass Cutter in Shoe Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Settlement either by renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 13th. day of Decr. 1781 before R Butler Jno. Barnfather

Francis Lissner
