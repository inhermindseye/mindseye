Middx to Wit Jane James Aged about 38 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of St. Margaretts  Westmr . about 12 years ago and by whom she hath one Child living namely Jane aged about 10 years now in the Workhouse belonging to St. Pauls Covent Garden Says her said late Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Butley a fruiterer in Covent Garden  in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for two years & upwards at the yearly Wages of £16. a year or thereabouts and after quitting his said Servant he never gained any or. Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death.

Sworn this 6th. day of Augt. 1783  Sampn Wright W Addington

her Jane James mark
