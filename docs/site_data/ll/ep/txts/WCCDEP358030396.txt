Westmr: 

Mary Pennell aged about 60 Years upon Oath saith she never was married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Roger Brownsword in Grange Court and Carey Street above Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds and left her sd. Service about 4 Years Says she has not since she left the sd. Mr Brownswords Service rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn 23d. June 1747 before.
