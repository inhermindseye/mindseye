Middx  Mary Betts aged about 23 Years Lodging in Searle Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to provide for the same when Born And that John Fletchyer , Gentleman who now lodges at Mr. Hall in Brownlow Street Holborn did beget such Child or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of January One thousand seven hundred and Sixty one and at several times since at which or one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant And that the said John Fletchyer is the true and only Father thereof

Sworn the 22d: day of April 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Betts
