Westmr ss  John Rofe Aged about forty two Years on his Oath Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with One Wm Jabes a Sawyer in Alphabet Court Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the liberty of Westminster and soon after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he Intermarried with Elizabeth his now wife at St John Chappel in the Precinct of the Savoy in the Year 1755 and by whom he hath three Children now Living Namely John Aged about Eighteen Years (now Provided for) Pellajah aged about Seven Years & Wm George Aged about Nine Ten Months says about Seven Years ago he went and resided in the Parish of St Mary Le Bone otherwise Mary bone and rented a Part of a Sawyers Yard thereof One Isaac Newton a Carpenter at the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds for five Months or thereabouts Payable Quarterly And also Rented Appartments in the house of Mr Gwadkins in Castle Street in the said Parish of St Mary Le Bone for Three Months at the Yearly Rent of Eight Pounds And Quitted the same about Seven Years ago since which time he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise Gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of Febry 1775 before} W Addington

John Rofe
