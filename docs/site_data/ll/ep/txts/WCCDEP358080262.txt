Middlesex to wit Elizth. Yon Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to Peter Yon her late Husband at the Parish Church of Shoreditch in March 1777 And by whom she hath one Child living named Peter Aged about four Months Says her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Anns Black Fryars London to one Thomas Butler a Fishing Rod maker then of the Parish of St. Clement Danes but now of the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden untill her said late Husband shod. Attain his Age of Twenty One years as by the said Indenture Appears And this Examinant further says that her said late Husband served the said Mr. Butler under such Indenture for the Space of five Years last past in Log Alley in the aforesaid Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden and that he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement than as aforesaid.
