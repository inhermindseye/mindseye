Westmr. to wit  Mary Row aged about 27 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year Says her father John Row rented and lived in a House in Three Tun ally White Chapell in the parish of Saint Mary White Chapll in the County of Middlesex where father sd. Deponent was born for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Nine Pounds or thereabouts and paid Land tax due for the same as her Grandmother as often informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and lived therein to the time of his Death which is upwards of Twenty Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent settlement than aforesaid

Sworn this 18th- day of March 1778 before Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Row
