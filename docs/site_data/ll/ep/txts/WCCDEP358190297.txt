Middx  Catherine Brown aged about 32 Years Widow of William Brown who has been dead near six Months Says She Intermarried with the said William in the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex about five Years since Says her said late Husband lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Pain in Bond Stables in the Liberty of the Roll in the County of Middx (whilst a Singleman ) for several Years Says She hath not since the Death of her said Husband Rented a House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 26th. day of Octor. 1762 before}
