Westmr.  Eliz Gray  Wife of Saml. Gray (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Saml. Gray in the Parish Church of Tillicorbut in Ireland abt. 6 Yrs. ago Says she knows nothing of her said husbands Settlement Says she was born in Monoghan in the sd. Kingdom of Ireland & never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Child viz Wm. aged 2 Yrs. & upwds.

Sworn Decr. 24th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz Gray
