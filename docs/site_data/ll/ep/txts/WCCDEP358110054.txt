Middx  Sarah Knight aged 24 Years upon Oath saith She never was married and that about Six Years ago she was hired to live with Mr. Mills a Tapman in Vine Street in the Liberty of Hatton Garden & Ely Rents within the parish of St. Andrew in Holborn in the County of Middx and lived with him as a Yearly Servt. in Vine Street aforesd. about five Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds and has not since she quitted her sd. Service Rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. County of Middx (where she now Lives) she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born; and that Richd. Wright Gent did beget the sd. bastd. Child or Children on her body the sd. Richd. Wright having had carnal

Knowledge of this Examinant in the month of Decr. last and at sevl. other times since in the house of Mr Remnant in Arundel Strt. in the afsd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes (where this Examinant Lived a Servant & the sd. Rd Wright Lodged) at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. bastard Child or Children with which She is now pregnant and that the said Richd. Wright and no other person is the true & Real father thereof

Sworn May 25th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Knight
