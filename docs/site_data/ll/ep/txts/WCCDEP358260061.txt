Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys and of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizabeth Glascow who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Elizabeth Glascow is the legitimate Daur of James and Mary Glascow that she never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That her father James Glascow has Rented House in Kings Head Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields for many years and pays all Taxes for the same That he lets the said house in Tenements and and this Examinant further Saith that he has been Informed which Information he also believes to be true that the said James Glascow the father of the said Elizabeth Glascow lodges in Stewarts Rents in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and has no done for Six Months past

Sworn this 27th- day of June 1795 M Addington N Bond

Jas Talboys
