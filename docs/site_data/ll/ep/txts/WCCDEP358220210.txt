Westmr.  Mary Mould Aged about fifty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Medhurst formerly a Slop Seller in East Smithfield for Ten Years or upwards and her said Master quitting Business he went to live in Great George Street in the Parish of Saint Margarets Westmr . where this Examinant lived with him upwards of Eleven Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and after quitting the same she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th: day of Decr. 1770 before Us} R Butler Tho Kynaston

her Mary Mould Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
