Middlesex ss}  Jane Headley  Singlewoman maketh oath That her late Father Richardson Headley deced lived in a Publick house of his own in the Parish of Lewisham in the County of Kent in Which house he died & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwds do any Act to gain anothr Settlement That She this Dept. was born in the Same Parish & never did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 3d day of June 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Jane Headley
