Middx  Ann Davis aged 21 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice by the Trustees of St Clemts. Charity School to Goodwin Wido. for the term of Seven Years & lived with the sd. Mrs. Goodwin abt. Nine months in St. Martins Court in the parish of St Martin in the fields Westmr at the End of which she dyed and this Examint. never was a yearly Servant for one Year nor ever rented ten pounds by the Year nor pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn Novr. 16th. 1754 before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Davis
