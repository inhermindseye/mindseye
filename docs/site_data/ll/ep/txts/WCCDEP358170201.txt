Middx  Mary Tristram  Wife of James Tristram upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her Sd. Husband James Tristram on or abt. the 19th. Day of Septr. 1735 at the Fleet  London & that her Husband was bound an Apprentice to & served Five Years the First Part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. Edwards in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Shoemaker & then the Sd. Mr. Edwards Dyed that he never was an apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. rented Ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief that he now absconds from her that she hath Two Childn. viz Elizabeth aged Two Yrs. & upwds & Sarah aged abt. 3 Months & being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworn the 13th- of Jan: 1741 before} Tho. Lane .

The Mark of Mary Tristram
