Westmr to wit Patty Aldridge Aged about Eighteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Daniel Sutton a Surgeon at Kensington Gore in the Parish of Saint Margarets in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 5th. day of Novr. 1771 before Us} Tho: Kynaston  John Goodfield

Pattey Alldridge
