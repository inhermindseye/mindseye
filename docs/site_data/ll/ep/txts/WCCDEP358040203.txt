Westmr. 

Dorothy Carr aged about 24 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Coombes a Taylor in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds for one Year and upwards and left her sd. Service in April last and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Children Children which is likely to be born and bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that John Luxmoor of the same Parish Gents is the reputed father the sd. John Luxmoor Lodging at Mr. Coombe's Whilst she lived there a Servant became acquainted with her and had

carnal knowledge of her body in the beginning of November last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the bastard Child or Children she is now pregnant with and that the sd. John Luxmoor and no other Person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn 14th. June 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Dorothy Carr
