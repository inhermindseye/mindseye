Middlesex ss}  Jane Brown  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years Ago she was duly bound Apprentice to one Mrs Glanvill of Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Milliner With whom she lived for near the Space of Three Years & then She went & lived with anothr Woman whose name she has forgot in the Same Place & Parish who Carried on the Business of a China woman with whom she Continued during the residue of the Terms for which she was bound Apprentice That she this Depont never lived any Where as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year nor ever was married nor ever did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement Subsequent to such her Apprenticeship in manner as aforesd

Sworn this. 28. day of October 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Jane Brown
