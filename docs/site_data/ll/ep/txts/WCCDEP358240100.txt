Rebecca Hughes

St Clement Danes May 9th. 1792.

Middx & Westminster }  St Martin in the fields  (to wit) Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the said parish of St Clement Danes on behalf of her two illegitimate Childrens Rebecca Hughes now Weak & Ill Upon Oath Saith That the said Rebecca Hughes hath Informed him this Deponent and what he verily believes to be true that She is aged about 27 years, was never Married That she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Haley Lace Maker in Long Acre in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for about two year at the Annual Wages of £8..8..0 that She quitted that Service the Month of November last and hath not since then Rented any House or Apartments of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any other legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge belief And that She is Ill & Poor

Sworn the 9th May 1792 before us} R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Benj. Newman
