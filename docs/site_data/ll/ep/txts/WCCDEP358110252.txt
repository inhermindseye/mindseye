Middx  Sarah Count on Behalf of Lucy an Inft. aged abt. 4 Yrs. upon Oath Saith She was well acquainted with Bridget Coyle the late Mother of the sd. Infant (who dyed abt. 2 yrs. ago) that the sd. Bridget Coyle was a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England nor ever was married, to this Examinants knowledge or belief and that the sd. Bridget Coyle was deliver'd of the abovementioned female Child at a house known by the Sign of the Goat in Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx

Sworn May 29th 1753 before} Tho Lane

Sarah Count
