Middlesex ss}  Jane Holman the wife of John Holman maketh oath That her Said husband who is now at Portsmouth was by Birth a Foreigner & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in Great Britain That Several years ago her late Father George Sheppard deced rented a house in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates & other Taxes for the same & where this Deponent lives with him as part of his Family That this Deponts Father to her knowledge or belief did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Depont. been ever bound Apprentice or ever lived as a hired servant for a year or done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She has Acquired by her marriage as aforesd

Sworn this. 4. day of December 1778 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Jane Holman
