Middx  Wm Mason  Removed from St Bridget London Upon Oath Says he served two Years and upwards the last part of his Apprenticeship with one Mr Browne a Silver Smith in Clements Inn passage in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr: in the County of Middx and hath been out of his said Apprenticeship upward of Thirty Years Says he Intermarried with Juliana his Wife in the Parish Church of St Clement Danes Soon after he was out of his said Apprenticeship and never Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn Januy. 28th 1763  Before}

Wm Mason
