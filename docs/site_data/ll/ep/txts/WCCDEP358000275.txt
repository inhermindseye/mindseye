Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizth. Findlass aged about 16 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes that she never laied as a hired yearly Servant or ever was bound an Apprentice that her Father Sebastian Findlass Rented and lived in a House in Angel Court Westmr. in the parsih of Saint Margaretts on the Liberty Westminster for Ten years at the yearly rent of  upwards hath quitted said House about 5 Months ago since which time She hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1786  James Croft  Jno Barnfather

Elizabeth Finlass
