Middx.  George Morris aged about 67 years upon Oath says he lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. James Tuttle an Ironmonger & Smith opposite the Talbot Inn in the Strand upwards of Seven Years Says he quitted the said Mr. Tuttle's Service about the year 1725 and soon afterwards hired himself to one Mr. Fielder in the same House with whom he lived a Covenant Servant for two years at the yearly Wages of Twelve pounds ten Shillgs. & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 10th. Day of April 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of George Morris
