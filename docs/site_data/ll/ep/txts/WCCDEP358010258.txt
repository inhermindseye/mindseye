Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Wright aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that on the 24th. day of August 1767 She Intermarried with John her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone that she has Seven Children living by her said late Husband vizt. Joseph aged about 18 years provided for Tuke Kilgore aged about 16 years John aged about 14 years James aged about 13 years Elizabeth about 12 years William aged about 9 years and Amelia Kilgour aged about Two years unprovided for That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Chair Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Five years at the yearly Rent of Six Pounds and paid all Taxes due for the same that he kept said House till he died which is about a Fortnight since

Sworn this 2nd. day of January 1789 R Butler

Elizabeth Wright her mark
