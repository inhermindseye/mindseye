Westmr  Eliz: Waite upon Oath Says she intermarried with Thomas Waite at the Fleet London on the 23d. of Janry 1741 Says her sd. Husband Thos. Waite is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or beleif Says her sd. Husband hath Inlisted himself in Genl. Cornwalls Regimt. of Mariner but whether he is now living this Examint. cannot tell Says she has one Child by the sd. Thos. Waite (to wit) Eliz aged upwds. of 4 Months & being Poor is not able to maintain herself & Child Says before she intermarried with the sd. Thomas Waite & whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Saml. Butler a Sadle Tree Rivets in Hartshorn Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £4 & married from the sd. Mr. Butlers Service

Sworn Augt. 1st. 1747 before} Wm: Jones  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Eliz Waite
