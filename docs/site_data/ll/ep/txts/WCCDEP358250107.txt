St Clement Danes October 2d. 1793

Middlesex And Westminster  St Mary Avery Southwark to wit Sarah Dill aged Nineteen year or thereabouts the wife of Joseph Dill a Waiter upon her Saith Oath saith That she was married to her present Husband the said Joseph Dill on the Thirty first day of August last at the Parish Church of Saint Olave  Southwark , That she has been informed by the said Joseph Dill that he is the son his Father rented a House in the Parish Borough Market in the Parish of Saint Mary Overy  Southwark And that her said Husband also informed her that he had never rented a House paid Taxes or done any Act whatever whereby to gain any subsequent legal Settlement And this Examinant upon her Oath further Saith That her Husband never informed her of his ever having served under any Indr of Apprenticeship an which tiation this Examinant believes in t And also that she this Examinant is now pregnant with one or more Child or Children and is unable to maintain herself her and Husband having left and deserted her and gone where he is gone she knows not

Sworn the 2d. day Novr. 1793 Before us} M Addington Wm Kitchiner

The Mark Sarah Dill

Witness Jas Talboys
