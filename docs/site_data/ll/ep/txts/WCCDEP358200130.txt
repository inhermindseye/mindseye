Middx  Sarah Lee Aged about Twenty Eight Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Wm- Lee with whom she Intermarried in the parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about Nine Years ago Says her said Husband Rented a House in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of four Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Eight Pounds and hath not Since Rented Ten Pounds by the Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn the Day of 1764 Before}
