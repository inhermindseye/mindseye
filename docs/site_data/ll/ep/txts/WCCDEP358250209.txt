Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr. to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Ann Simcock and George Simcock Two Infants Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Mary Ann Simcock and George Simcock are the illegitimate Children of Mary Simcock who has deserted them That the said Mary Ann was born in the house of a Mr. Munday in Bennetts Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster on or about the 25th. day of January 1789 and that George was born at No. 5 in Bennetts Court in the Parish and Liberty aforesaid on or about the Third day of January 1791.

Sworn this 25th. day of Novr. 1795  N Bond W Kinnard

Jas Talboys
