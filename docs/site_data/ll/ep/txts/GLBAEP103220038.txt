Middlesex ss  Ann Wood  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore hired by and lived as a hired Servant with Mr Adams a Grocer in the Parish of St Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & upwards That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subseqt Settlement

Sworn this. 11. day of May 1786 before R Davies Robt. Smith

Ann Wood
