St Clement Danes 5th Octr 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Botolph without Aldersgate  One Child  Martha Workman aged forty two years and upwards Upon Oath Saith that she was Married to her present Husband Samuel Workman at the parish Church of St Botolph without Aldgate London on or about the seventeenth day of May 1771. (by Banns) that She is informed and verily believes her said Husband was bound (by Indre) an Apprentice to Mr Fell Farrer of Jerusalem Passage in the parish of St James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex and served the whole of his Apprenticeship with his said Master but this Deponent saith that her said Husband (having Married with her this Examinant previous to the expiration thereof) lodged during the last two three Months together with her this Examinant at the House of Mr Mackintosh in Long Lane in the parish of St Botolph without Aldersgate London that his Apprenticeship ab expired about 21 years ago and he hath not since done any Act Matter or thing wtsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief that she hath one Child by her said Husband Named Johanna aged about eleven Years & Nine Months or thereabts that her said Husband was Impressed about four Months ago and she is become destitute

and unable to provide for herself and Child without assistance

Sworn the 5th day of October 1793 before us} Rich. Ford  Wm Kitchiner

Martha Workman
