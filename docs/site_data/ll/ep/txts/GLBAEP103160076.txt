Middelsex ss}  Ann Johnson the Widow of Robert Johnson deced maketh oath That divers years ago her said late husband rented a house in Swan Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same That her Said husband did not afterwards rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And that she this Deponent since his death hath not rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this. 15. day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Ann Johnson
