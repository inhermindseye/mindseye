Westmr.  Mary Jenkins aged about 42 Years upon Oath says She is the Wife of Henry Jenkins gone from her with whom She intermarried in the Chappel at Kentish Town in the Parish of St. Pancras in Middlesex about Seventeen Years ago; Says her said Husband Rented a House in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds and Eleven Shillings lived therein about one Year and hath quitted same fourteen Years or thereabouts and hath not since rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 6th. day of October 1767 before} Paul Vaillant 

Mary Jenkins
