Middx  The Examination of Phillipp Williams on the Behalfe of Thos. Williams lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 11th. Day of March 1740 who upon his Oath saith that about two Years ago the sd. Thos. Williams (being at that Time a Widower & haveing no Child or Children) lived with one Mr. Hosier in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . Syderman for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of eight Pounds or thereabts. And During all that Time he lodged at one Mr. Clunnsin the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesd. And this Examinant further saith yt. he hath not since yt. Time rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place as this Examinant is in Informed and beleives That the sd. Thos. Williams is now Sick & Poor & not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Philip Williams
