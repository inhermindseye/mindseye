Middlesex ss}  John Plumb maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house at the yearly Rent of Twelve pounds in upper East Smithfield in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd county & lived there in for the Space of three years & upwards. & by means there of gained a legal settlement there that he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain anothr Settlement And that he has a wife named Mary to whom he was lawfully Married

Sworn this 13. day of February 1777 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of John Plumb
