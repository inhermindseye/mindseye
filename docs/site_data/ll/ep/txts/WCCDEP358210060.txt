Westmr.  Ann Hill  wife of George Hill on the behalf of Ann Betson an Infant aged about Seven Years Upon Oath says the said Ann Betson is the Daughter of this Examinant by Robert Betson her late Husband deceased; says the said Robert Betson Rented and lived in a house in John Street Golden Square in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of of Fifteen Pounds for five Years or thereabouts and after he quitted said house never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 9th, day of July 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos: Balack

The Mark of Ann Hill
