Middx.  Mattw. Chamberlain  aged on the behalf of Martha Humphrys a Lunatic Widow of John Humphrys deceased Upon Oath says that she said Martha Humphrys Being a Widow and without Child or Childn. lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Unwin in Drapers hall in Broad Street in the Parish of St. Peter Poors London for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four pounds or thereabouts and hath quitted said Service abt. three Years since which time she has not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year as the said Martha Humphry 's often times informed this Examinant when she was in her Senses; and which Information this Examinant verily believes to be true

Sworn this 1st. day of Octr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Matt. Chamberlain

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Burnthwaite Ovr . J: P:
