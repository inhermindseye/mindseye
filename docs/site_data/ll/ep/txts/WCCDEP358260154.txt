Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Lewis Augustine Peacock aged about 37 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Foche in Pall Mall in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Twenty Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 7th day of Octr. 1796 } Rusl Clarke W Kinnard Lewis Augustine Peacock
