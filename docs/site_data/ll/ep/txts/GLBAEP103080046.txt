Middx ss  Nicholas Mackean maketh oath that about forty seven years ago he was duly bound an apprentice by Indre for the Term of Seven years to John Bishop of St Martin le Grand in the parish of St Leonard in Foster Lane in the City of London  Cordwainer with whom this Deponent served all his time save about fourteen Months and then left his sd Master And this Depont saith that he hath not since lived as a hired servant by the Year Nor been an Apprentice Nor rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards Nor Done any Act to the knowledge or belief of this Depont. to gain any subsequent settlemt.

Sworn this 9th. day of april in the year of our Lord 1759 before us  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

his Nicholas Mackean Mark
