Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Brooks the Widow of Thomas Brooks deced maketh oath That her Said husband has informed her That When a Singleman he was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages with one Captain Tunner (Who then kept a Grocers shop at Queen Stairs) in the Parish of St. Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry and that Continued there in such service for two years and which information this Depont. believes to be true and this Depont. saith that to her Knowledge information or belief her Said husband subsequent to such his servitude never Rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards. nor served any Parish offices or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or lived as a hired servant for a year or did any Act to gain a settlement after his Said servitude and this Depont. farther. saith that since her Said husbands Death She hath not rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or lived as a hired servant for a year or done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 22 d day of June 1768 before  Chris Scott  Geo: Garrett

Elizabeth Brooks  Her Mark
