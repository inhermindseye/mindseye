Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Perry aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months ever never was bound an Apprentice or ever Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes That her father James Perry has Rented a house for these Seven years last past in Little Wild Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and pays Taxes due for the same and which house to the best of this Deponents knowledge is of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards

Sworn this 4th. day of Augt. 1797 W Olkey. W Kinnard.

her Mary Perry Mark
