Middlesex  Ann Gravadeer the widow of Francis Gavadeer deced maketh oath That her late husband was by birth a Foreigner & never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in England or Wales That She this Deponent many years ago being then of about the Age of nine years was duly bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseersof the Poor  of the Parish of St Matthew Bethnal Green in the Sd County to Wm Cotter of Blue Anchor Yard in Hair Brain Court in the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel Middlesex  Carpenter & lived with him there for about four years & half & from thence She removed with her Said Master to Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate Midd & Where She lived with him for about Six Months & then by reason of ill Usage which She received from her Said Master She of her own Accord & witht her Said Masters Consent left her Said Masters Service & never afterwards lived with him nor has She Since Seen him That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself to her Knowledge or belief Save what She has Acquired by her Marriage as aforesaid

Sworn this 8 day of June 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Ann Gravadeer
