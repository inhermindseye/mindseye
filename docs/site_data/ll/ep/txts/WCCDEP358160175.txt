Middx  The Examination of Thomason Kitts  Singlewoman , Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 2d. Octr. 1740 Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith yt. she is Pregnant with, Child or Childn. wch when Born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor and not able to maintaine the same when Born And further saith yt. one John Parker  Peruke maker did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body she at such time living with one Mrs. Eaton in Chelsea where he the sd. Jno. Parker came frequently to see her & had carnal knowledge of her Body Sevll. times in the Month of April last & at Sevll. other times since at one of wch times he got her with the Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now Pregnt. & yt. he & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof, And further upon her Oath saith yt. abt. 4 Years ago she lived a yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Henley in Grange Courts in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the space of one Year & upwards at the yearly Wages of £6 And hath not since yt. time Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworn the Day & year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Thoma. Kitts
