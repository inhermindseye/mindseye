Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Allen aged about 23 years upon her Oath Saith that about 8 years ago she intermarried with her present Husband who has deserted her at the Parish Church of Saint Georges Southwark by whom she hath living two Children Vizt. James Aged about 5 years and John aged about two years That her said Husband was born in Ireland and never did any Act Matter or Thing whereby he May have gained a Settlement in England That she this Deponent while single lived with her Father who rented a House in Lee's row in the parish of Saint George Southwark in the County of Surry for nine Months at the yearly Rent of thirteen pounds that he hath quitted the said House about nine years since which time he hath not rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge or Belief

Sworn this first day of March 1800 before us} Jam Collick  Saml. Edwards

Mary Allen
