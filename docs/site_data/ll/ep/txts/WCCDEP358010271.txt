Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Matthew Jackson aged about 31 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Martha his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Sepulchres and by whom he hath One Child living vizt. William aged about four years that he this Examinant served Five years Apprenticeship to his Brother Edward Clarke in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre without in the County of Middlesex that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Febry. 1789 M Addington N Forster

Mattw Jackson
