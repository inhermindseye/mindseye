Middx  Ann Moore aged 28 Years upon her Voluntary Oath Saith She never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Randolph in Surry Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex near two Years at the yearly Wages of £5. that she quitted her sd service abt. four years ago and has not since rented Ten Pounds by the year Paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year and this Dept. further when tarily maketh Oath that she is Pregnt. with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Rd Maile  a mason did beget the sd. bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. Rd Maile having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant thrice in the months of April and May last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant and that the said Rd Maile and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Febry 5th 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Moore
