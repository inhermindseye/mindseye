Middlesex & Westmr} to Wit Rachael Jones aged about Forty Two Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Omell a Musician in King Street Soho in the Parish of St. Anns in the Liberty of Westmr . for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Sixteen Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 30th. day of June 1783  Sampn Wright John Hagar

her Rachael Jones mark
