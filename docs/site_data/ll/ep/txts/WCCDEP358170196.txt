Middx  Susannah Gatehouse (being one of the People called Quakers) on the Behalfe of Hugh Alesworth aged abt. Seven Years Penelope Alesworth aged abt. Two Years & Catherine Alesworth aged abt. Six Months the Children of Thomas Alesworth by Anne his Wife lately Deceased upon her Salema Affir Macdy Oath saith that the sd. Thomas Alesworth is her Brother & is ran away from the sd. Infant Children & left them in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & that the sd. Thomas Alesworth was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentisp with his Father Chas. Alesworth a Taylor in the Markett Place in the Parish of St. Lawrance in Reading in the County of Berks that since his sd. Apprentiship he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Affirmed Sworn the 6th. of Janry 1741 before} Tho Lane . Jas: Gronous

Susann Gateous
