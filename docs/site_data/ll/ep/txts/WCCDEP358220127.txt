Middx & Westmr}  John Flax Aged about 23 Years on his Oath Says he never was married nor ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes Says that he was bound Apprentice to and Served five Years the first part of his Apprenticeship with one James Searls a Baize Weaver at Colchester in the County of Essex and after he left his Said Master he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 22d day of Febry 1770 before me} R. Butler

his John Flax Mark

Witness Petr. Sidebotham
