Middlesex & Westminster}  Swear to St Clemt . A. to wit Mary Joysey aged about 48 years on her Oath says that she Intermarried with John Joysey deceased between 11& 12 years ago at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx and by whom she hath no Children living That after their Intermarriage her said Husband Rented and Lived in a House Called three Jolly Butchers  Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid for 12 Months or thereabouts at a yearly Rent of upwards of £10. hath quitted said House about Ten years ago That her said Husband died soon after quitting the said House and that after quitting said House neither did he in his Life time did nor hath she since his decease done any Acted

Sworn this 16th. day of Novr . before me  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Joysey
