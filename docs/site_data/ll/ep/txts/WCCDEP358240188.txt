St. Clement Danes 23d Janry. 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St. C D  (to wit ) Ann Johnson aged 44 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs Silvester in Holles Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for five year or thereabouts at the Annual Wages of five pounds that She quitted that service about four years ago and hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelvemonth together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 23d day of January 1793 before Me } D Walker

Ann Johnson 's Mark

Witness Garrett
