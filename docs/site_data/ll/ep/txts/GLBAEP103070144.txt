Middx ss  Anne Healey  Singlewoman Maketh oath that about seven years ago she lived as a hired Servant by the year with Sampson Tasker of Crowchers Ropewalk in the parish of Saint George in the County of Middx at the yearly. Wages of Four Pounds ten Shillings and continued in such service for the Space of Three Years and upwards And of the ans thereof gained a Legal Settlement there And this Deponent Saith that she has not since gained any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 30th day of September 1755 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Anne Healey
