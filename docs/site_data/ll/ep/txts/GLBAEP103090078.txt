Middlesex ss  Joanna Holbeche the Wife of Richard Holbeche mariner maketh oath That her said husband is now on Board his Majesties ship Gibralter as She believes And this Depont Saith that her said husband has informed her (and which information She believes to be true) That her said husband was bound to; & duly served the whole of his apprenticeship with, one Mr White in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx  Tyre Smith ; And this Depont. farther Saith that to her knowledge or informacon her Said husband never rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards or ever Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his said apprenticeship And that she hath a Child named Ann Aged about Twenty Months the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her said husband.

Sworn this 29th. day of Septembr. 1767 before  Chris: Scott

Joanna Holbeche
