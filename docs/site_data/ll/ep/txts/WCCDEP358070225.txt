Westmr. ss  Hannah Malin  Wife of John Malin (gone from her) On her Oath Says she Intermarried with her said Husband at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about four Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living namely Mary Aged about Two Years and Maria Aged about three Months Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Purvis a Perukemaker & Hair Dresser in George Yard Lombard Street in the Parish of St. Edmund the King London and after he was out of his said Apprenceship he never Rented House paid Taxes on did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 16th. day of Novr. 1774 before-  Sampn Wright W Addington

Hannah Malin
