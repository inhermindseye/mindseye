Middlesex to wit Mary Teasdell Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant in one Mrs. Barton in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of Sa Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One Year Thr Months at the Yearly Wages of Nine Guineas a Year hath quitted her said Service about Eighteen Months ago since time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement An further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Parish of Sa Clement Danes (she being poor and unable to maintain th same when born) And further Says that Richd. Godsoc Shopman to Mr. Lunn an Umbrella maker in the Str is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of Body in his the sd. Mr. Lunn House some times in the Mon of June or July last but the Particular days or times shed not remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at On of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Richard Godsoc & no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 23d Day of Janry 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Tisdell
