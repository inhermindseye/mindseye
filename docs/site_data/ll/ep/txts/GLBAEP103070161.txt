Middx ss  Mary Reave  Singlewoman Maketh oath That about Nine Years ago she was bound apprentice to her Grandmother by Indenture who then lived in Horsely Down Back street in the parish of St John in the County of Surry and served Six Years of her apprenticeship and then her Grandmother died And saith that she was never Turned over & any other Person And that she lived as a hired servant by year nor hath been Married And hath not gained Any Settlement subsequent to her sd Apprenticeship to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 11th day of May 1756 before us  Richd. Ricards  Chris: Scott

his Mary Reave Mark
