Westmr  Eliza; Chace aged upwds: of 21 Years upon Oath saith she lodges in the Appartment of Mr. James Pryor in the Angell Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and further saith she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Jno. Miller at the Temple Eating house in Sheir Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £5 Pounds for 4 Years & upwards and left her said Service about three Weeks ago And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she never was married & that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that Joseph Kearle a Shoemaker in Great Sheir Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes did beget the sd. Child or Children on her Body he the said Joseph Kearle being acquainted

with this Examinant had Carnall knowledge of her body in the month of December last in her sd. Master's house & at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the said Joseph Kearle & no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn & July 20th. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

Elizabeth Chace
