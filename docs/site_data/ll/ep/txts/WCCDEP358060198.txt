Westmr.  Ann Oates  Wido . of Joseph Oates who dyed the 2d Day of May last upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Joseph Oates at Stepney Church upwds. of 8 Yrs. ago by whom she hath two Children Joseph aged upwds. of 5 Years and Sarah aged abt. 17 months Says her sd. late husband Served 5 Years of his Apprentiship with Mr Bridgens Dyer in Fore Street in the Parish of St Giles Cripplegate London & then his sd Master dyed & he never was assigned over to any other person rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 1st. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Oates
