St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Elizabeth Barrett  Wife of Richard Barrett Aged 44 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith That abt. Eighteen Years ago she was Married to her present Husband Richd. Barrett at the parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex That since ther their Marriage her said Husband Rented and with her lived in an Apartment in an House in Portsmouth Street next the Black Jack in the parish of St Giles aforesaid in the Fields , aforesaid in the Said County of Middlesex for Ten Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of £18 that they quitted the said same House about Ten Years ago And that since her said Husband quitted the said Apartment he hath not to the best of her knowledge or belief done any Act or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain a Subsequent Settlement And that her said Husband as this Examinant has been informed and believes) now lives in the Service of Thos Grace the the present Lord Lientenant of the Kingdom of Ireland And lastly that Examinant is incapable of providing for herself

Sworn the 28th day of July 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Barrett
