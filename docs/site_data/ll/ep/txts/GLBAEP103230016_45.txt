Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Helen Gibbs  wife of John Gibbs of the Parish of st Botolph gate in the sd county Mariner and late the widow of William Knarston deced taken upon oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this ninth day of February one thousand Seven hundred and eighty Seven -Who upon oath Saith that on eighteenth day of July 1783 at Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County She the said Helen Gibbs was delivered of a Male Bastard Child Since baptized & registered by the name of Henry Sheppard And that the said Bastard child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that Henry Sheppard of the same Parish Mariner & Master of a Ship did gether with child of the said Bastard Child and this Examinant farther Saith that her said late husband Kuarston died on the fifteenth day of April 1782 being about the Space of Six Months before the sd Henry Sheppard had Carnal Knowledge of her Body and that long Since the birth of the said Child She has been Married to her Present husband John Gibbs who is now absent from her at Sea & his Settlemt unknown

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me  Robt Smith

Helen Gibbs
