Middx  Sarah Turner upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Turner & that Since the Decease of her sd. husband She rented two houses at the botto of Essex Stairs in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly rent of £ 24 as constantly lived in one of the sd. houses that she quitted the sd houses abt. 12 years ago & has not since rented ten pounds a year pd. parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn 14th Decr. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Turner
