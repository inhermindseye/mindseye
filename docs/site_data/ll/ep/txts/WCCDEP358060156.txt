Middx  Hannah Evans aged upwds. of 20 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she was born in the Parish of Widborn in the County of Hereford where her father rented a house and Served the Office of Constable and that she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt Danes Middx (where she now lodges) she being poor & unable to maintain the same & that John Hetherley did beget the sd. bastd. Child or Childn. on her body the sd. John Hetherley having had carnal knowledge of this Examint. Sevl. times in January last and twice in Febry last in the house of Mr Pele a Surgeon in Wellelop Square (at which place this Examint. lived as a Servt . and the sd. Jno. Hetherley was Apprentice ) at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Childn. with which She is now pregnant & that the sd. John Hetherley & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Augt. 21st. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Hannah Evans
