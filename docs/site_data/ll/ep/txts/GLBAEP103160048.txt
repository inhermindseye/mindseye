Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Redmond of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me George Colson Smith  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this 23. day of April-1776

Who Saith that She is now with Child of a Child or Children And that the Said Child or Children is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that one Patrick Bryant of the Same Parish Bricklayer is the Father of the said Child or Children.

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me  Geo C Smith

The Mark of Mary Redmond
