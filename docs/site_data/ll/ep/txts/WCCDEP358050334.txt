Middx  Lucy Pepper aged abt. 53 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married that she was bound Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mrs. Baxter a Fan painter and lay during the whole term of her Apprentiship at her fathers in Chair Court Ship Yard and in Crown Court Butcher Row in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 8th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

Lucy Pepper
