Middlesex ss}  Thomas King maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to one Emanuel Robinson of Church yard alley in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship there Save the space of nine months or there abouts & then this Deponent was presed on board a man of War & his Apprenticeship expired at Sea That he this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or forced any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship.

Sworn this 20. day of March 1777 before}

The Mark of Thomas King
