Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Christopher Corrall aged about 25 Years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 22d. day of July 1794 he Intermarried with Mary his prevent wife at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a first floor at Mr. Bartleys in Bell yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Three Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds that he hath quitted said Apartments about Three years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of January 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

C. Corrall.
