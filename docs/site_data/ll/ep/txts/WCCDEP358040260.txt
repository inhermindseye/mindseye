Westmr. 

Mary Goss upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 8 or 9 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. West a Houses at the Corner of Lime Street in the Parish of St. Dennis Back  church London for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and has not since She quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for on Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 21st 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Goss
