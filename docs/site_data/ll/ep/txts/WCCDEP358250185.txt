Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Humphrey Miller upon his Oath saith that about Twelve years ago he Intermarried with Hannah his late wife at Newbury Church in Berkshire by whom he hath one Child living namely Richard aged about Seven years That his said Wife has been dead about Four years That he this Deponent has Rented a Shop of a Mr. Robinson in Clare Market in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the weekly Rent of Four Shillings ever since November last

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1795 before } W. Mathew

Humphrey Millier
