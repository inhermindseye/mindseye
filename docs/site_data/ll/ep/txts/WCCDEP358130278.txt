Middx  Mary Simmonds aged abt. 21 Yrs. upon Oath says she was bound an Apprentice by the Church Wardens & Overseers of the Parish of Lambeth in the County of Surry to Sarah Burmaster in Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn & lived with her abt. three Years & then with the Consent of her said Mistress lived with one Mr. Collyer a Jeweller in Bull & Mouth Street in the Parish of Christ Church in the City of London for one Year & a half

Sworn Feb: 15th. 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Simmonds
