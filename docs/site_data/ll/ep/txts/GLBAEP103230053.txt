Middlesex ss}  Ann Scott the widow of Thomas Scott deced maketh oath that She has been informed by her Said late husband & which She belives to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticesp with his father Henry Scott in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph with Aldgate in the Sd County Salesman & did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement having never rented a house or lodging of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes That She this Depont Since her sd husbands death hath, not lived as hired Servant for a year or been married or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that She was lawfully married to her Said late husband

Sworn this. 18. day of June 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Ann Scott
