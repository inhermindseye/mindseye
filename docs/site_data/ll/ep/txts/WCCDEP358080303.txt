Middx to wit Mary Bird Aged about Thirty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she served Six Years and an half the first part of her Apprenticeship with one James Lee of Hair Curler in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . when her said Master died and after her said Masters Death she never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement by living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year. And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to Maintain the same when born And that one John Collins a Day Labourer who works for one John Blackman a Farmer at Clapham in Surry is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of June last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. times afterwards and before for that the said John Collins is the Father of anr. Bastard Child of which she was delivered at Camberwell in the Parish of Peckham in the County of Surry & which Child is named Sarah and is now of the Age of Eight Years And further says that the said John Collins & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father of both the said Bastard Children.

Sworn this 24th. day of Octr. 1778 before}
