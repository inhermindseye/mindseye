Middlesex ss }  Mary Day  Single woman Maketh oath that her late Father Thomas Day deced has informed her & Which She believes to be true that he was born in Vine Court in Red Cross Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & She Can give no other Account respectg his settlement That She this Deponent was born in Greenwood Court in the Same Parish & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 5th day of August 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Mary Day
