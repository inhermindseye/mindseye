Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Tighe aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that about Eight Nine years ago she Intermarried with Edward her present husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bone by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Dorothy aged about Eight years That her said Husband is an Irishman and never gained any Settlement in England That she this Examinant before her Intermarriage with her said Husband and whilst she was a Singlewoman She lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Jackson in Nassan Street in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Ten years since which time She hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of April 1798 before us J Robinson R. Ford

her Mary Tighe mark
