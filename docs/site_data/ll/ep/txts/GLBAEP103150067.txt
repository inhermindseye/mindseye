Middlesex ss  Thomas Ballard maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to his late Father Thomas Ballard of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Waterman deced with whom he Served near Six Years of Such Apprenticeship in the said Parish & by means there of gained a legal Settlement the That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of November 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

Tho. Ballard
