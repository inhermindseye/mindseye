Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Mary Dewel, the Daughter of Josiah Dewell aged ab. 27 years Single Woman . She never lived as a hired Servt . in any place more than two Months at a time her Father lived in a House No. 9 New Inn several years at upwards of £10 P Ann, since which time She has not done any Act Matter a thing whereby She might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18 Day of Novr. 1796 } Saml. Edward

Cath Mary Dewell
