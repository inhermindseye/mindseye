Middx  Catherine Williamson aged abt. 37 Yrs. Wido . of Isaac Williamson upon Oath Says she Intermarryed with her late Husband Isaac Williamson abt. 20 Years since in the Parish Church of Wigton in the County of Cumberland Says her sd. late husband hath been dead abt. 3 Months Years Says he was born in the said Parish of Wigton and Served Seven Years Apprentiship to a Soap maker and Tallow Chandler in Ireland as she hath been Informed & beleives Says her sd. late husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochiall Office lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 7th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Cathn. Williamson
