Westmr. to wit Theodocia Hopkins Aged about forty seven on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly servant with one Mr. Chandler a Carver and Guilder in New Street near Broad Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Years or thereabouts hath quitted her said service about Six Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1773 before W Kelyngs James Pulding

Theodocia Hopkins her marks
