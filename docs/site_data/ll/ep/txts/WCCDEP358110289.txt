Middx  Fras. Bowiss on behalf of Rd. Clark a Lunatic upon Oath saith the sd. Lunatic  Rd. Clark lived a Yearly Servant with the late Sd Carpenter in Grosvenor Square in the Parish of St Geo Hanr. Square in the Liberty of Westmr. for two Years & upwds. & has not Since he left the sd. Service rented ten Pounds Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Augt. 11th. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Fran: Bowis
