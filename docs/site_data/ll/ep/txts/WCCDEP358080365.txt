Middx to wit Henry Croasdale one of the Beadles belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Eleanor Pemberton an Infant aged about Thirteen Years on his Oath says that he hath looked at an Indenture of Apprenticeship whereby it appears that the said Eleanor Pemberton was by Indenture bearing Date the 16th. day of November 1778 bound out an Apprentice by the then Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish to one Henry Eagleton then of Bagnio Court Newgate Street in the Parish of Christ Church London that her said Master whent to live at Hogsdon in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex where the said Mr. Eagleton has resided for upwards of Two Months do the said Mr. Eagleton has lately informed this Examinant and which Information he verily believes to be true

Sworn this 8th- day of June 1779 before R Butler Abrm Baley

Henry Croasdell
