Aldgate Middx

Barbary Worthy  Singlewoman Maketh Oath that She was bound an Apprentice by Indenture for Six years to Ann Harrison a Childs Coat: maker And the three last years of the Said Six she Servd her Said Mrs. in Newport Street in the Parish of Saint Ann in the City and Liberty of Westminster And hath gaind no Settlement Since

Sworn 26th day of November 1748 before Richd. Ricards N. Dukinfield

Balray Worthy
