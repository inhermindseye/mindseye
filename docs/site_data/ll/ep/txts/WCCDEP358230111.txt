Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Moses Price Aged about 64 years upon his Oath Saith That he was bound an Apprentice to one Thomas Hill a Shoemaker in the Parish of Saint John Hampstead in the County of Middlesex for Seven years That since he hath been out of his time he hath not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d day of Janry 1800 before us Wd Woodenough Winter

Moses Price
