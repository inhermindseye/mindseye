Middx.  Mary Evans aged abt. 29 years upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Streater a pastry Cook the Corner of Clare Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. for One Year certain at the Yearly wages of Four Pounds and hath quitted sd. Service abt. three years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt. for one year. And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes where she now lodges she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born; says that One John Freeman a Shoemaker who lodges at No. 8. in Stuarts Rents in Drury Lane had Carnal knowledge of her Body in some publick the house of in Piccadilly sometime in the Month of October 1758. and at One other time since at one of which times he

he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which She is now pregnant and that the said John Freeman and no other person did beget the same

Sworn this 30th. June 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Evans

Upon the Complaint of Mr Burnthwaite Ovr . J: P:
