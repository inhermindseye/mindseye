Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Jefferys aged about 46 years upon his Oath saith that about 16 years ago he Intermarried with Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom he hath Three Children living Vizt. Thomas aged about Sixteen years Richard aged about Ten years and Sarah aged about Seven years That he this Deponent Rented unfurnished Apartments of Mr. Candy in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds that he hath quitted said Apartments about Two years since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of March 1798 W Carter

John Jefferis
