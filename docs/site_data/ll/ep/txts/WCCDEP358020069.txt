Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Wilson aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Foot in Gloucester Street in the Parish of St. George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex for 15 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £6 that she hath quitted said Service about 4 Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of April 1788 D Walker Rupt: Clarke

her Sarah Wilson Mark
