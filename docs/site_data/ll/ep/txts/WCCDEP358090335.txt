Middx & Westmr. to wit Elizth. Bearley Aged about 47 Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with Robt. Bearley her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about 14 Years ago and by whom she hath O Child named Letitia aged about Ten Years Says her sd. late Husband Rented & Lived in a House in Little Catherine Street late Called Eagle Court Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for 9 Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of £10. hath quitted said House about 3 Years ago and after quitting same he never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10. a year paid Taxes or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 19th. day of October 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth. Bearley Mark
