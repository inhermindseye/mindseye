Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Whitcroft aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that she never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever found an Apprentice that she hath been informed that she was born in Great White Lyon Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and that her father William Whitcroft kept house and paid Taxes in the said Parish of Saint Giles in the fields for many years

Sworn this 7th. day of April 1797 before us  N. Bond P. Henderson

Elizabeth Wheatcroft
