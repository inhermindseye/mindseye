Before Birth 

St Clement Danes August 20th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnancy  Reputed Father} (to wit) Hannah Bear  singlewoman aged eighteen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Jones in Carey Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for a year and upwards at the Annual Wages of six pounds that she quitted that service about five years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes when born And that Thomas Appleyard who has been lately pressed and is as She believes now in his Majesty's Service is the only real true father of the said Child or Children where with she is so pregnant as aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body first in or about the three years ago and several times between that and the Month of June May last at some or one of which  times he did beget the said Child or Children thereon and that he and no one else is the real true father

that thereof & that she is distressed and unable to support herself during the time of her lying in without assistance

Sworn the 20th day of August 1793 before us}

Hannah Bear 's Mark

Witness Garrett

Man not taken
