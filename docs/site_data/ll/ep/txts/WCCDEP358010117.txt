Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Edward Groombridge aged about 21 Years upon his Oath saith that he never was married Rented House paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that he was Born in Halmond Richmond in the Parish of Richmond in the Co of Surry where his Father & Mother has Rented a House for many years that he has done Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of July 1787 R Butler

Edward Groombridge
