Middlesex ss}  George Foster maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at certain Wages by & with Edmund Cowell in the parish of Waltham abbey in the county of Essex Farmer & Continued in such service for the space of two years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement That he hath a Wife named Sarah and a child named Sarah aged about Six years the lawful Issue of this Depont by his said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 29 day of March 1777 before Jasper Clarkes  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of George Foster
