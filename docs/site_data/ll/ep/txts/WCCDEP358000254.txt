Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Lucy Wilson Aged 63 years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that She Intermarried with David Wilson her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn London about  Nine years ago, that her said Husband hath been Dead upwards of 5 years - that her said late Husband Rented and Lived in a House Known by the Sign of the Wheat Sheaf in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew

Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middx for 1 year at the yearly Rent of £30 as she believes & paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about 11 year ago since which time he hath not gained any other Settlement to the best of this Deponents Information Knowledge or belief And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that She hath  not since the Death of her said late Husband done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge she may have gained any Settlement in her own Right and that she is poor and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn this 20th day of March 1786 before us M Addington Sampr Wright

her Lucy Wilson Mark
