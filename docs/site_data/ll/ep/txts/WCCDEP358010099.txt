Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Thos. Spencer an Infant Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been credibly informed which Information he verily believe to be true that William Spencer the Father of the said Thos. Spencer served Seven years Apprenticeship with one Thos. Brignall a Butcher in Aldersgate Sto in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldersgate in the City of London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of May 1787 . G Mercer Jno. Barnfather James Talboys
