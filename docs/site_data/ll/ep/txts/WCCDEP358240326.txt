St Clement Danes Septr 27th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster}  Parish of Hale in Co. Norfolk  (to wit) Susan Wright aged 28 years or thereabouts singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with a Farmer in the parish of Hale in the County of Norfolk for a year from Old Michaelmas to Old Michaelmas at the Annual Wages of £4 that She quitted that Service about five or six years ago And hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement & that She is pregnant and unable to do pay the expence of her lying in without assistance

Sworn the 29th. day of Septr. 1793 before us W: Blamire J Hole

Susanah Wright

Removed by Mr Talboys Beadle the Woman was brot to Bed at Hale & afterwards cameup with the Child to make the father provide for it she was Admitted in the House & there continued til abt.
