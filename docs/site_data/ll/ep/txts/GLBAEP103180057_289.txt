Middlesex ss}  Patience Barrett maketh oath that She is the widow of Thomas Barrett deced That her Said late husband divers Years ago was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Francis Seale in Seething Lane in the Parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for divers Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn this. 15. day of August 1778 before Jasper Clarkey  Jno. Sherwood

The Mark of Patience Barrett
