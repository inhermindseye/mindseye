Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Chapling aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 23rd. day of July 1777 she Intermarried with John Chaplin her late Husband who has been dead about Fifteen Months at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bone by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Mary Ann aged about Six years That her said late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cave in Mount Street Grosvenor Square in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months and upwards but what wages he had she this Deponts. doth not know and after he quitted said Service he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of May 1796 } Robinson N Bond

Sarah Chaplin
