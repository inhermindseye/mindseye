Middx  Catherine Peirson aged about 27 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is a Singlewoman & is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born Says one Richard Eberell now a Journeyman to Mr. Wright Brushmaker in the Strand is the Reputed Father of the said Child or Children he the sd. Richard Eberell having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sevll. times in the Month of the July last at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Richard Eberell & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworn 6th. April 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Cath: Peirson
