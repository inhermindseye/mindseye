Westmr.  Ann Millard aged about 19 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes that her father John Millard lived in and rented a Farm at Wrap at in the County of Somerset of the Yearly Rent of Eighty pounds for nine months or thereabouts and quitted it upwards of Eight Years ago since which he has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn July 20th. 1749 before H Tho Lane

Ann Millard
