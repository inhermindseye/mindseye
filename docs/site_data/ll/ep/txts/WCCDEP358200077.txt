Westmr  Mary Mills Aged about Forty four Years Upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Wm. Mills at the Fleet London upwards of Nineteen Years ago Says her said Husband Served five Years Clerkship with Mr. Edmond Pead  Attorney at Law Says her said Husband Served the Last Year of his said Clerkship in Baldwins Court in Cloak Lane in the Parish of St Mary Aldermary in the City of London hath Left said Apprentiship about Twenty Years and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year This Examinantions False Paid Land Tax or Poor Rate as this Examinant hath been Informed & which Information she Verily Believes to be true Says she has One Child by her said Husband (Viz) Mary Aged about Ten years

This Examinations False

Sworn the 3d of March 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Mills

Upon Mr Robt Williams Complt Ovr. J. W
