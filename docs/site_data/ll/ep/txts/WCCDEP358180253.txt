Middx  Hannah Ford  Widow  removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields by Order dated the 10th Day of Novr. Instant upon her Oath saith she Rented a House in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds & lived in the same Nine Years or there abouts Says after she left sd. house in Stanhope Street she rented a house no & Door to the Coopers Arms in Cow Cross in the Parish of St. Sepulchers in the sd. County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & lived in the same two Years & upwards Says she has not since she left the House in Cow Cross Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 17th. Novr. 1744 before me} Wm. Jones

Hannah Forde
