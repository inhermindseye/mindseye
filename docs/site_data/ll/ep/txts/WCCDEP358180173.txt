Middx  Lucy Brown aged about 30 Years Removed from the Parish of St Giles in the Feilds by Order Dated the 27th. Day of Janry last upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never Married that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Maxfeild in Bennetts Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of 51s. & £3. for the Space of 3 Years & hath left her sd. Service abt. 11 Yrs. since which time she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne the 20th. March 1743 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Lucy Brown .
