Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Mc.Cullum Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Malcolm Mc.Cullum who is now in America and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about Seventeen Months ago and by whom she hath one Child now living named John aged about fifteen Months says her said Husband was a Native of Scotland and never gained any Settlement in England either by renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says before her sd. Intermarriage with her said Husband and whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Collick an Attorney in Dyers Buildings Holbourn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn London upwards of fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds and soon after her sd Marriage left her place since which time her said Husband or this Examinant never gained any other Subseqt. Settlement.

Sworn this 9th. day of Septr. 1776 before  John Croft  Jno. Machin

her Ann Mc.Cullum Mark
