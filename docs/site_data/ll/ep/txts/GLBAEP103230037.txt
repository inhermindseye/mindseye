Middlesex ss}  Hannah Small the wife of John Small maketh oath that She well knows a child named Joseph Halling  Karver aged about thirteen years who was born in high street in the Parish of st Giles in the Fields in the said County and was there baptized & registered this Depont being present at the Baptism of such child and which child has not yet done any Act to gain a Settlement for himself and such child is a Bastard and illegitimate by reason his Mother who is lately Dead was never married to the Father of the said child to the knowledge or belief of this Depont

Sworn this 19 day of April 1787 before  R Davies  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Hannah Small
