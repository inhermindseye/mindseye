Middx  Marmaduke Brown upon Oath saith he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £20 in Bedford Bury in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middx lived therein abt. 5 years and has quitted it abt. 13 years Since which he has not rented ten Pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor hired a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn March 26th. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Marmaduke Browne
