Ann Glanvill aged 68 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was Married to Thomas Glanvil her late Husband at the Parish Church of Eton in the County of Buckingham That after their Intermarriage her said Husband rented and lived in a House at Eton for near Thirty Years and paid Poors Rate for the same during all that time That her said Husband died in the said House and that after his Death this Examinant rented and lived in the said House for about two Years and paid Poors Rate for the same as aforesaid that she quitted her said House about four Years ago and since that time has not rented a House or paid Taxes lived in Lodgings of £10 a year, lived as an Hired yearly Servant or done any other Act Matter or Things whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or Belief she may have gained a Settlemt. elsewhere and that Being poor and in Extreme Distress she this Examinant hath  for three nights wander'd about and lodged in the open air in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 4th. day of Decr. 1784 R. Butler

Ann Glanvill
