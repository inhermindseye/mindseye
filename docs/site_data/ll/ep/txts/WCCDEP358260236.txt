Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Peter Sorleat aged about 68 years upon his Oath saith that he was never Married, Rented a Shop in Clear Market for four years at five Shilling P Week and has quitted the same about Nine years since which time he hath done no Act Matter a thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22nd. day of Decr. 1797 before me } Edwd: Naish

Peter Soralet
