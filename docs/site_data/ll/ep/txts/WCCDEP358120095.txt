Middx  Mary Shirley upon Oath Saith that she was married to Wm. Shirley in the Parish Church of St Brides London on the 4th. Septr. 1753 and never was married before her intermarriage wth. the sd. Shirley as afsd. and that about two years ago she was delivered of a male bastd. Child (baptized Thos .) in the house of Mr Dailey a Breeches maker in Swallow Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr.

Sworn May 3d 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Shirley
