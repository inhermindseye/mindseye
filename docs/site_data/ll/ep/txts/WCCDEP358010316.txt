Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Esther White of great shire Lane on behalf of John Sidley an Infant aged about Three years Maketh Oath and saith that she has been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that the said John Sidley was born a Bastard on Garlick Hill in the Parish of saint James Garlick at Hith Church in the City of London

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1789 Addington Beading

her Esther White Mark
