Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster .} to wit Mary Ann Distiff Smith aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the 22d. day of May 1796 she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of a Mrs. Maning in Yeates Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the name of Mary Ann Isabella That one Richard Murphy a Clerk at the Excise Office and who lives at No. 17 Bishops Court is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Richard Murphy is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 18th. day of Septr. 1798 before me } J Hunter

Mary Ann Smith
