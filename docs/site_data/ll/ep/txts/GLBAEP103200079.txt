Middlesex ss}  Jane Dennis the widow of John Dennis deced maketh oath that she has been informed by her sd late husband & which she believes to be true That many Years ago her sd husband was duly Bound to and Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Thomas Walker a Perukemaker in Peters Alley in the Parish of St Peter in Cornhill in the City of London & to this Deponts Knowledge or belief her Sd husband Since Such his Apprenticeship as aforesaid has not rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards or served any Parish office or Paid Any Poore Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement & that She this Depont Since his Death has not done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her sd husband at the Fleet about thirty eight Years ago & this Depont was once relieved by her sd husbands Master who was then Churchwn. of the sd Parish of St Peter in Consequence of her sd husbands being legally Settled there.

Sworn this 13. day of Decemr 1783 before Vagrant Order}

The Mark of Jane Dennis
