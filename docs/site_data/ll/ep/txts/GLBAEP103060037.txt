Middx  David Cossartt maketh Oath and Saith that about Twenty Six Years he was Bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to David Allen in Grafton Street in the parish of St. Ann Westminster in the County of Middlesex  Hatter and Served all his Apprentiship there and this Deponent further Saith that he Rented a House in King Street in the parish aforesaid and lived therein about four Years and paid thirteen pounds a Year for the same and has gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge

Sworn this 2 day of March 1744 Before Richd Ricards

Daivd Cossartt
