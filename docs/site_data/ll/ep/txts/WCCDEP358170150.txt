Middx  Joseph Wymen on the Behalfe of Joseph Maddox upon his Oath saith that the sd. Jas: Maddox was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Saml. Saltis  Button Mold maker in Tewin Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldersgate London that since his sd. Apprentiship he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 14th. Day of October 1741 before}

The mark of Jose: Wymen
