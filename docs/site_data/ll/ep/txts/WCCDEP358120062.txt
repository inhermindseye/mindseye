Middx  Mary White upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr King a Mason opposite the printing house in the parish of St. Maudlin in Oxford for 5 Years at the Yearly Wages of £2 quitted her sd. Service abt. 3 Yrs. ago and has not since rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Feby. 25th. 1754 before}

The Mark of Mary White
