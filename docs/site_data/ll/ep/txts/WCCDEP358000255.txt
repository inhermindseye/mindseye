Saint Clement Danes

City & Liberty of Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Mary Smith aged 23 years or thereabts upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Hinnard of Brompton Row Kensington in the parish of Kensington in the County of Middx for 14 Months at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas hath quitted said service about twelve Months ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st day of March 1786  Sampr Wright N Bond

her Mary Smith Mark
