Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Susannah Jefferys aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year that She was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Dunstan at Stepney to one Mr. Kitchin in Brook Street Stepney for 7 years that after she had Served about four years of her said Apprenticeship She was Assigned over to one Mrs. Chapman in Ratcliff Square in the Parish of Saint Dunstan at Stepney in the County of Middlesex for the Remainder of her said time that her said Mistress did died about Five Months abouts before her said Apprenticeship expired and that she this Examinant never did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 15th, day of Decr- 1789 R Butler D Walker

her Susannah Jefferys Mark
