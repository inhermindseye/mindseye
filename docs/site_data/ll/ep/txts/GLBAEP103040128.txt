Middlesex ss.  Eleanor Swinney  Singlewoman maketh Oath that she has heard her Mother say that her late Father Patrick Swinney was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to Mr. Cox of the Pageants in the Parish of St. Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surrey Ship Breaker and served the whole of his said Apprenticeship with his said Master there that her said late Father did not since his Apprenticeship Rent any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or pay any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement that she this Deponent was born at Globe Stairs in the said Parish of St. Mary Rotherhithe as she has been informed by her Mother, a further for herself saith that she has never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the year in any Parish or Place for the Space of One year together or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own Right & that she never was Married And further saith that she is now with Child & being with Child is deemed & taken to be a Person chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex where she now resides

Sworn this 30 Day of May 1798 . before H: Reynell R Davies

her Eleanor Swenney Mark
