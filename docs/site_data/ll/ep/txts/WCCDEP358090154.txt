Middx to wit Alice Cook Aged Forty Nine Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes or served any Apprenticeship says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one John Hudd a Clock Forger in Duke Street Artillery Lane in the Liberty of the Tower of London at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twenty five Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 4th. day of Septr. 1780 before  Sampn Wright M Addington

her Alice Cook mark
