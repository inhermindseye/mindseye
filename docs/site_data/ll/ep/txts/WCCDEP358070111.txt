Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Kezar aged about Thirty Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Peter Kezar a German gone from her and who never gained a Settlemt. in England either by Renting house or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year before Marriage Says before her Intermarriage with her said Husband she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Father in Law Thos. Storey a Scourer in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Nine Years ago & hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1773 before R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Elizth. Kezar Mark
