Middx  Rebecca Gear Aged abt. Forty Nine Years Upon Oath Saith She is the Wido. of Jno. Gear that the sd. Jno. Gear lived in & Rented a House in Pichadilly in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. for Seven Years or thereabts. at the Yearly Rent of Thirty three pounds that the sd. Jno. Gear after he quitted sd. House never Rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes neither hath this Examinant Since his death rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn the 10th. day of June 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Racca Gear
