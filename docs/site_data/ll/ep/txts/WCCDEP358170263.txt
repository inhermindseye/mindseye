Middx  Mary Wright  Singlewoman aged about Twenty Years upon her Oath saith that she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for the Space of one Year rented House or paid Parish Taxes that her Father Francis Wright never rented House or paid Parish Taxes but Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Draper a Peruke Maker in Horton Street  Clare markett in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex & hath been Dead abt. Five Years and this Examinant being Poor and Lame is not at present capable of providing for herself

Sworne the 26th.. Augt. 1742 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Wright
