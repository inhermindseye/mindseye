Westmr. to wit Mary Elley Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her said Husband John Elley at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of Seven Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Namely Mary Aged about Three Years and John Aged about Seven Weeks Says her said Husband (who now lies under Sentence of Transportation) is a Native of France and never gained any Settlement in England  either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly or Covenanted Servant for one Year Says that before her Intermarriage and whilst she was a Singlwoman she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Precious West. a Cabinet Maker in High Holbourn in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for fourteen Months at the Yearly of five Pounds hath quitted her said service about 9 Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever gained any or Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 16th. day of July 1774 before  William Addington  Sampn Wright

Mary Elley
