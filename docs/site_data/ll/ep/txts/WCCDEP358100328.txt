Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to St. C. D. A to wit Jane Scott aged about Sixty years upon her oath saith She never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hawkins a printer in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 12 years & upwards At the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted said Service about Ten years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Setttlement elsewhere

Sworn this 4th. day of Janry. 1785 M Addington

her Jane Scott mark
