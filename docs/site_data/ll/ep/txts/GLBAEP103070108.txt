Middx ss  Alice Snook the widow of Robert Snook deced Maketh oath that she this Deponent here to fore Married with one Emanuel Hubbard who as he has informed this Deponent served part of his Apprenticeship with one John Hubbard a Cordwainer in Church Yard Alley in the parish of St. Olave in Southwark in the County of Surry And then afterwards went to Sea And Never afterwards gained any Subsequent Settlemt. to the knowledge or belief of this Depont. And this Deponent farther saith that about fifteen years ago she and hers sd. late husband Hubbard was passed from the parish of St. Mary White Chappell Middx to the sd. parish of St. Olave as the place of their last legal settlement which order or pass to the knowledge or belief of this Depont was never vacated or set aside And this Depont farther saith that since the decease of her sd late husband Hubbard She intermarried with one Robert Snook deced who was an Irishman but him what parts of Ireland he Came from or where he was legally settled this Depont. cannot set forth

Sworn this 13 day of May 1754 before  Rd. Ricards  Chris: Scott

Alice Hubbard hir Mark
