Middx  Mary Ross aged about 45 Years Lodging in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . Upon Oath says she was never married that she lived a hired yearly Servt . with Mr Pennington at the Crooked Billett in Wych Street in the sd Parish of St Clement Danes for one Year at the yearly Wages of four pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service upwards of Eight years & hath not Since rented a House or lived a hired Yearly Servt.

Sworn August 14th 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Ross
