Westminster .  Hannah Richardson Aged about Thirty four Years on her Oath Saith she never was Married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says that she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr: Goodwin a Plumer in Bride's Lane in the Parish of Saint Bride's otherwise Bridget in the City of London for Fourteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Six Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further Says that on the thirteenth day of June 1766 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of one Mrs: Winter in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Frederick William And further Says that one William Massey a Watch Spring Maker (and who now lives as this Examinant believes in or about London but at what Place this Examinant cannot Set forth) is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of September 1765 and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid And that the said William Massey and no other person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn this 18th: day of July 1770 before us } Petr Planck

her Hannah Richardson Mark.
