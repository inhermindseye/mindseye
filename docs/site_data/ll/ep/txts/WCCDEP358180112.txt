Thomas Wood aged 72 Years upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served his Apprentiship with his Father  William Wood in Essex Street & Boswell Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that since his sd. Apprentiship he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Septr. 7th. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

Tho: Wood
