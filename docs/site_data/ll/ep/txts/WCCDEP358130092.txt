Middx  James Hawkins Aged about 50 Years upon Oath Saith that about Thirty years ago being at that time a Singleman he Lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Thos. Winsmoore a Baker in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One year and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds Ten Shillings Says he hath not since he quitted the said Mr Winsmoores Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a yearly hired Servant

Sworn the 6th. Day of Decr. 1755 before} Saunder Welch

James Hawkins
