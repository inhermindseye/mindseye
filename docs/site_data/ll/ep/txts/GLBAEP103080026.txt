Middlesex  Judith Ward  widow of John Ward deced Maketh oath that her son in Law Joseph Morgan was bound an Apprentice to Mr Archer a Boxmaker in Wallbrook in the parish of Saint Stephen in Walbrook in the City of London with whom he duly served his apprenticeship & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there as this Depont. has been Credibly informed and verily believes And this Depont saith that she doth not know where the said Joseph Morgan now is nor whether he be living or Dead But saith that there is now living issue of the Body of the said Joseph Morgan to wit a Daughter named Mary aged about seventeen years born of ye Body of this Deponents late Daughter who intermarried with the sd. Joseph Morgan & is lately Dead And this Deponent farther saith that the said Joseph Morgan did not a after servy his apprenticeship as abovesaid Rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor serve any Parish offices nor live as a hired servant by the year nor gain any subsequent settlement to her this Deponts knowledge or belief

Sworn this 11 day of January 1758 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Judith Ward
