Middx ss The Examination of Elizabeth Stevenson Singlewoman taken on oath before Me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx residing in or Near the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx this twelfth day of October 1753

This Examinant on her oath Saith That about Seven Months ago one Thomas Arnold late of Gravesend in the County of Kent  Shoemaker did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at the dwelling house of Mrs. Vernon known by the Sign of the Faulcon Situate in Old Fish Street in the City of London Where this Deponent then lived And the said Thomas Arnold had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and so had Several times Whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she Now goeth and is Pregnant And this Examinant Saith that the sd. Child or Children of Which She now goeth with and is Pregnant as aforesd.

is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the sd. Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate She not being able to provide for the Same And that the sd. Thomas Arnold is the true and only Father of the sd. Child or Children so likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesd. And lastly Saith that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whatsoeover

Sworn this 12th day of October 1753 before me  Richd Ricards

The Mark of Elizabeth Stevenson

Warrt. made out 13th Octr.
