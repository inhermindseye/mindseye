Middlesex and Westminster }  St. Martin in the Fields  (to Wit) James Lewis Aged Sixty Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he intermarried with his late Wife Ann at the parish Church of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex about 31 or 32 years ago that then he Rented and lived in an House in New Bedford Court White Hart Yard in the said parish of St Martin in the Fields which he kept about Sixteen years at the Annl. Rent of £16. that he quitted the same about Fifteen years ago and since hath not done any Act or thing whatsoever whereby He might obtain any Subsequent legal settlement that his said Wife has been Dead near 18 Years And that he is now poor & unable to support himself without Assistance

Sworn the Ninth day of Decr. 1791 before us.} Sampn. Wright N Bond
