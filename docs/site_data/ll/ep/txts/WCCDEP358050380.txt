Middx  Mary Norman aged upwds. of 19 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt . for one Yr. rented Ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes that her late Father Rented a house in Church Street in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx & lived therein & pd. Scot & Lot  sevl. Years & there dyed

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Norman
