Middx & Westmr}  Elizabeth Burry Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Rowland Thomas a Hairdresser  the Corner of Exeter Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for one Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Seven Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Oath Says that on the 25th. day of August last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Mr. Macdonalds Hospital near Tyburn Turnpike which hath since been baptized by the name of Ann and is now become chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further Says that one William Denvis  a Journeyman Hairdresser who now lodges at No.12 in Buller Court near Southampton Street in the Strand is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in Decr. 1781 but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said William Denvis & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 4th. day of Febry. 1783 before R Butler

Elizabth Burry
