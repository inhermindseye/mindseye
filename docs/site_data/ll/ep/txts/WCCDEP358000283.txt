Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Brett aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Perkins a Coach Carver in Little Queen Street in the parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of 3 pounds hath quitted said Service about 17 years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Deponents knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of May 1786 before us  Sampr Wright M Addington

her Mary Brett Mark
