Middx  The Examination of Rebecca White  Widw . of Thos. White Deced Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 15th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her late Husband Thos. White about 9 Months ago Rented & lived in a House in Butcher row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £21 for the space of Six Months & there Dyed And this Examint. further saith yt. she hath not since the Death of her said Husband Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath 4 Children (to wit) Michael aged 10 years. Margt . aged 8 Years Jno. Bishop aged 4 years & upwards. & Grace aged 3 years & upwards. & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herself & Children

Sworn the Day & year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Rebeckah White
