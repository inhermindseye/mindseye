Middlesex, to wit, Anthony Mussard maketh Oath and Saith, that some time in the Month of April One Thousand Seven Hundred and Eighty Eight he hired a House in Manor Row , in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate , in the County of Middlesex , at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds that he lived in the said House for the Space of Twelve Months or thereabouts, and was rated to and paid Poor's Rate for the same, that he has not since he quitted the said House lived in any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards for the space of Forty Days, or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement in any Parish Precinct on Place whatsoever, That about Sixteen Years since he was lawfully Married to Hannah Greaves (long since deceased) at the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone , by whom he has Issue living (as he believes) Two Children, namely John aged about Ten Years, and Elizabeth aged about Eight Years who are now in Scotland , and supported by their Grandmother That on the Fourteenth Day of June One Thousand Seven Hundred and Eighty Eight he was lawfully Married to his present Wife  Elizabeth , at the Parish Church of Saint Botolph Aldgate

Sworn this Day of August 1790, before}
