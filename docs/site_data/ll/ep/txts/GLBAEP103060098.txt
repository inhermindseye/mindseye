Aldgate Middx

Jeremiah Warren Maketh Oath that upwards of Thirty years ago (he being then a Single man) was a Hired Servant by the year and liv'd two years and upwards as Such with Edmund Gutteridge who kept the Sign of the Black Horse Inn in Coleman Street in the Parish of Saint Stephen in Coleman Street in the City of London And that he hath gaind no other Settlement Since

Sworn 14 day of March 1747 before Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

Jeremiah Warren
