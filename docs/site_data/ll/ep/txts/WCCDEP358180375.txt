Middx  Sarah Connell aged about 50 Years upon her Oath saith she is the Widow of Charles Connell  deceased and that the sd. Charles Connell lived in & rented a house in Duke Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £36 for the Space of one Year & Six Months or thereabouts and never after (in his Life time) nor hath she since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or other -wise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 9th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones Sarah Connell
