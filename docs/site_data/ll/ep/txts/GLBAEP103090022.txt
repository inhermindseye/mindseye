Middlesex ss  Hannah Biggs  single woman Maketh oath That about sixteen Years ago She was bound Apprentice by the then Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex to one John Mac Donald who then kept a Green Shop in whites Yard in the Parish of Saint Mary White chappel in the sd. County with whom She lived under such Indre for the space of two Months and then her sd. Master left her And thereupon her said Master Mac Donald as she was informed & verily believes Turned her over (by delivering up her Indre & Paying the sum of Fifteen shillings with her) to one John Smith of Whites Yard aforesaid Taylor with whom she lived for the Space of two Months in the said Parish of St Mary White chappel And then her sd. late Master John Smith removed from Whites Yard aforesaid to t the Corner of Black Jack Alley in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the sd. County of Middlesex
