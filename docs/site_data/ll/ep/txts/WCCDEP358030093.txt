Middx  Esther Edgecomb  Wido . of Christopher Edgecomb removed from the Parish of St: Antho. vulgarly Antholin London by Order dated the 1st Day of Febry Instant upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late Husband on the 9th. Day of Septr. about 39 Yrs. since at St. Jas: Chappell Says the sd. Christr. Edgecomb lived in & rented a house in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £17 for the Space of two Years & upwards & hath left the same upwards of 20 Yrs. Says her sd. late Husband never after Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 3d. Febry 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Esther Edgcomb
