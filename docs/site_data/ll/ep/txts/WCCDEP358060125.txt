Westmr.  James Seddon upon Oath saith he was by a Pass dated the 14th. Instant Sent as a Vagrant from the parish of St. Mary le bow Londo. to the parish of St Clement Danes Middx and further upon his Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Thomas Gwillam a Distiller  opposite great Wild Street at the Yearly Wages of £6.10.0 for one Year & upwards & was all the time of his sd. Service a Singleman & quitted the sd. Mr Gwillam upwds. of 40 Yrs ago and that in the Examination annex'd to the sd. pass the sd. Mr. Gwillams house is said to be in the aforesd. parish of St. Clemt. Danes but this Examinant having seen the sd. house hath informed himself that it is in the Parish of St Giles in the Field in the County of Middx

Sworn June 15th. 1751 before H Fielding James Seddon
