Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Greenhough Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Peter Greenhough a Taylor in Arundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and has so done upwards of Two years at the yearly wages of four Pounds And further up her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Domes she being Poor and unable to main the same when born Says that one Robert Fenton a Coachman to Lord Belasyse in Grovenor Street had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of February last past and at several different times Afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Robert Fenton and no other person d beget the some and that he is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1772 before me}

her Jane Greenough Mark
