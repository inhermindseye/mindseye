Middx  Ann Coulson aged abt. 23 Years Upon Oath Says She was never Married and that She lived a hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Bury a Cabinet Maker in Bishopgate Street in the Parish of St. Ethelburga's in the City of London one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds and hath left her said Service abt. 7 Weeks. Says She hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or Paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn the 1st. Day of May 1756 before}
