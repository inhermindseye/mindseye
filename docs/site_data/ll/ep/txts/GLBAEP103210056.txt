Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Margaret Sarah Page of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Single woman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the sd County this twenty eighth day of January 1785 who Saith that on the twenty ninth day of December 1784 in the sd Parish She this Examinant was delivered of a female Bastard Child since baptized & registred by the name of Martha Page and that she sd Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate and that one Bernard Shevener late of Ostend in Flanders Mariner who lately died there did get her with Child of the Sd Bastard Child.

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Sarah Page
