St. Clement Danes 10th febry.1792

Middlesex and Westminster}  Removed before very Recently vide Page 318)} (to wit) Elizabeth Campion Aged about 38 years Wife of Jas. Campion Maketh Oath and Saith that she was Married to her present Husband James Campion at the parish Church of St James Westmr. on or about the 14th day of September 1783 that she hath two Children now living by her said Husband Namely Ann Aged One year & Eleven Months and upwards and John Aged Ten Months and upwards that her said Husband served the last year of his Apprenticeship (under Indentures duly executed) with his father Thomas Campion in Coventry Court in the Haymarket in the parish of St. Martin in in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County and Lodged and Boarded in his said Father's House and he hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent Knowledge or belief

Sworn the 18th day of febry. 1792 before us Addington Thos. Gordon

Elizth Campion 's Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
