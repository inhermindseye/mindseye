St Clement Danes May 4th: 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Lacton orse Latton Essex but no Settlemt went in Service before Lady day 1791 & quitted after Lady day 1792 Reced more than a years Wages  Order Appealed against and quashed Pauper only resided there 10 Months  (to wit) Elizabeth Cook  Singlewoman aged 12 21 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Montague Burgoyne  Esqr . at their Country Seat called Markhall in the parish of Lacton otherw otherwise Latton near Harlow in the County of Essex for a year or upwards at the annual Wages of Seven Guineas that she quitted that Service in or about the latter end Month of April 1792 and hath not Since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve month together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief And that She is pregnant and unable to do pay the Expences of her lying in without assistance

Sworn the 4th. day of May 1793 before us N Bond Mitchell

Elizabeth Cook 's Mark

Witness Garrett

Order & Pauper was afterwards Removed by Mr Allaway to Shepton Montague Somersetshire Vide Examn page this Book page
