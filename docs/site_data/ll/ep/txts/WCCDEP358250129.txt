Saint Clement Danes

Settlemt. in St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars Johanna Pynor aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that in the Month of April 1788 she this Deponent Intermarried with John Pynor her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields by whom she hath one Child living vizt. Maria aged about Six Years That her said Husband Rented Apartments at No.18 in Dean Street Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Two years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas that he hath quitted said Apartments about five years since which time he has not Rented any house Tent. or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 2d. day of October 1794 before} Edw Read  Edwd: Naish

Johnna Pynor
