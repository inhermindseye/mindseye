Middlesex to Wit.}  Sarah Walker Widow of William Walker Servant deaced maketh Oath and saith that she is near 23 Years of age and that she was married to her said late Husband at the parish Church & Saint George Hanover Square upwards of three Years ago And saith that about five Weeks before her Intermarriage with her said late Husband she was delivered of a female Bastard Child (since baptized Elizabeth Mary Ann and now living with her this Examinant) at the House of Mr Stewart Tobacconist in Sharrard Street in the Parish of Saint James within the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex And saith that the said William Walker her said late Husband was the true and Real Father of her said Child so born in Sharrard Street aforesaid and no other person whatsoever And this Examinant further saith that her said late Husband William Walker whilst a singleman lived an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Johnson who kept an A la mode Beef Shop in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the space of two Years or thereabouts and continued in his said Service in Blackmoor Street after he was married to her this Examinant and up to the time of his Death And this Examinant also lived a Servant with the said Mr Johnson for above several Years and until she was was married

Sworn the 17 Day of December 1790 R Butler

Sarah Walker
