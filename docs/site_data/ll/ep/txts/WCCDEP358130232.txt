Middx  Mary Goram aged abt. fifteen Years upon Oath says She was never Married was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servt. Says her Father & Mother are both dead & that She this Examinant was Born in the Parish of St. Mary at Stanford in the County of Lincoln as her later Mother has informed her & wch. Information She verily believes to be true

Sworn the Novr. 15th. 1756 before} Barth:Hammond

The Mark of Mary Goram
