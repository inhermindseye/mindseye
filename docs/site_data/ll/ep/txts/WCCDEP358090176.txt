Middx to wit Elizabeth Ward Aged about forty years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired yearly Servant with one Captain Cooper in Catherines Court Great Tower Hill in the Parish of All Hallows Barking in the City of London Upwards of Three years at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Thirteen Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of Decr. 1780 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Elizth. Ward Mark
