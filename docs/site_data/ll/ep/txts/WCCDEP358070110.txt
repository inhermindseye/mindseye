Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Dickinson Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Dicknison with whom she intermarried at Liverpool in Lancashire about four Years ago and who has been gone to Sea upwards of Two Years and has never been in England since to her knowledge or beleif nor has she ever heard from him for two Years last past And further upon her Oath says she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child on the Nineteenth of June last past in the House of and Fras. Guly at the Crown in Butcher Row Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which has since been Baptized by the Name of Jane says that one John Gray one of the Proprietors of the Pinner Stage is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body on the fourteenth of September 1772 in a field adjoining to Pinner and at no other time afterwards and further says that the said John Gray did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 7th. day of July 1771 before} 

Elizth Dickinson
