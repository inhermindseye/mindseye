Middx.  Mary Carney aged abt. 48 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Lawrence Carney who has been dead abt. half a Year says her said husbd. Rented and lived in a house in Hollis Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for Nine Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds and quitted same abt. Two Months before his death. says her Said husband in his lifetime (after he quitted said house) nor she since his death never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Octr. 20th 1759 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Carney
