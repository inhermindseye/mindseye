Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Hall aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that on the 28th. day of March last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of John That one Wm. Brooks a Tunner to Justice Hydes & who lives in Princes Street Westminster is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sevl. times within these Two years last past at one of which times he did beget the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Wm. Brooks is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 11th. Day of May 1787 } Wm. Kitchiner

her Mary Hall mark

Adjudication 2/6 a Week the Men 6d. a Week the Woman he paid the Lying Inn-& Expr. up to the 9th. June 1787
