Middx.  Ann Higgins  Single Woman aged abt. 21 Years on the behalf of her brother John Higgins an Infant aged upwards of Nine years upon Oath says that John Higgins her father was born in the Parish of Chippenham in the the County of Wilts and learned the Trade of a Shoemaker with his father there as her father informed her and as she verily believes says her father also informed her that his father lived in and Occupied a house of his own in the said Parish of Chippenham for Sevl. Years.

Sworn this 22d. day of Sepr. 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

Ann Higgins

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Burnthwaite & Mr. Wilkinson Oversrs. J: P:
