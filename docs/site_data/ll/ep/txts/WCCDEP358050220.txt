Westmr. 

Nathl. Exton upon Oath saith he an Intermarried with his wife Ann about 14 Yrs. ago at the Fleet London by whom he hath one Child Mary aged about 9 Years Says he was by Indenture dated the 5th. of June 1720 bound Apprentice to Edward Armon Taylor & Served 4 Years & upwds. of his sd. Apprentiship with the said Edwd. Armon in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and then his sd. Master broke and this Examint. never was assigned over to any other person rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 10th. 1749 before}

Nathanel Exton
