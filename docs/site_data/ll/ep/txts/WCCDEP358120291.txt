Middx  Sarah Henderson aged 39 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servt. with Mr Brazier a Green near Temple bar in the parish of St. Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex at the yearly wages of Six pounds for one year and upwards that she quitted her said Service abt. ten years ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn March 4th. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell ,

The Mark of Sarah Henderson
