Middx  Susanah Marks  Widow aged 34 Yrs. & upwards upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husbd John Marks abt. 14 Yrs. since in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes who hath been Dead 2 Yrs & upwds that the sd. John Marks was bound an Aprentice to & served his Aprentiship with Mr. Hutchinson Taylor in Carey Street in the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes that the sd. John Marks in his Life or this Exaiant since his Death never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath two Children vizt. Elizabeth aged 4 Yrs. & upwards & John aged 6 Yrs. & upwards & being Poor is not able to provide for them

Sworne 4th. April 1744 before}
