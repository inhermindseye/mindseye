Middx.  Elizth. Earle aged abt. 44 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Earle (gone from her) with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London abt. 25 Years ago says her said husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servt. with-Bray Esqr . in Arrundell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas & then intermarried with this Examinant says her said husband never Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 3d. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Elizth. Earle
