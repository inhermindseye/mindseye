Middx.  Elizth. Peaton aged 31 Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Edwd. Peaton who hath been dead abt. Ten Years Says the said Edwd. Peaton before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with this Examinants Father James Lyddall a Taylor at the White horse in White horse Yard Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Fifteen Pounds Diet, Washing, & Lodging & that after he quitted his said Service he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes. Says She hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Jany Febry 1st -1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizabeth Peaton
