Westmr.  Thomas Tillard aged about 41 Years upon Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Robert Callis a Butcher in Water Lane in Fleetstreet in the Parish of St. Bridget enlgarly Bridges London and hath been out of his said Apprentiship about Eighteen Years Says he never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 8th. 1749 before H Fielding
