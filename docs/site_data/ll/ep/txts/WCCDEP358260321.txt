Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Clancy aged about 20 years upon his Oath saith That he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice that he was born in Bennetts Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  where his father kept house for Three years on thereabouts

Sworn this 25th. day of Augt. 1798 before me } Wm Hyde

his James Clancy Mark

Witness H Day
