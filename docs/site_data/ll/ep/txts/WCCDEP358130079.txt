Middx  Ann Weston Wife of John Weston upon Oath saith She intermarryed with the said John Weston at the Fleet London sometime in the Month of Octr. 1753 by whom She has a Child named Elizabeth aged about Ten Months Says her said Husband informed her he was bound an Apprentice for seven Years to his Father Edward Weston in the Parish of Chipping norton in the County of Oxford Carpenter and served him about five Years the first part of his said Apprenticeship in the said Parish of Chipping norton and then came to London & never was assigned over to any other Person

Sworn the 11th. Day of Novr. 1755 before } Barth: Hammond

The mark of Ann Weston
