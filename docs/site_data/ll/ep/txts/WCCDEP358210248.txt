Westmr.  Mary Farquhar Aged about Thirty nine Years, Upon Oath says she intermarried with Alexander Farquhar (who is now gone from her) in the Parish Church of Stoke in the County of Southampton  about Nine Years ago, says her said Husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief, says before her intermarriage with the said Alexander Farquhar , (and whilst she was a Singlewoman ) she lived a hired Yearly Servant with John Popham  Esquire in Kensington Square Square in the parish of Kensington in the County of Middlesex for Fifteen Months or thereabouts, at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds; Quitted said Service about Eight or Nine Years before her Marriage, and never after Rented Ten pounds a Year, paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 29th. day of February 1768 before}  Thos: Balack

Mary Farquhar
