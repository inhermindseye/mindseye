Middx  Frances Thompson aged about 41 Years upon her Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Lawrance an Attorney at Law in Blackman Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and quitted her said Service in last July since which she has not rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn April 1st. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of France Thompson
