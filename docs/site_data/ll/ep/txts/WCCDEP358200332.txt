Westmr.  Thomas Perry aged about 42 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Anne his wife in the Parish Church of St. Mary in the Town of Stafford about Eleven Years ago, by whom he hath 5 Children (to wit) Ann aged about 9 Years Mary aged about 7 Years James aged about 5 Years George ttaged about 4 Yrs. and Anthony aged about 2 Years, says he Rented a house in Vere Street Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Eight Pounds lived therein about three Months and quitted same in Decr. 1764, and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 11th. Decr. 1765 before} Paul Vaillant Thoms. Perry
