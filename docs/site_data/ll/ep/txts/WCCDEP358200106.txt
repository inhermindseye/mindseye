Middx  Robt. Currant Aged upwards of Eighty Years Upon Oath Says he rented a House in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of thirty Pounds lived in same several years & hath quitted same upwards of ten Years after that Rented a House in White Horse Yard in the same Parish Yearly Rent Eighteen pounds lived in same almost One Year & hath not since Rented House paid taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 12th Day of May 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Robt. Currant

Upon Mr Browns Complt J. W
