Middx  Martha Burvell upon Oath Saith she is the wido . of Jas. Burvell decd. and that she has been often told by her sd. late husband he was born in the Parish of St James at Dover in the County of Kent which Information she verily believes to be true and that she can give no further Accot. of his Settlemt.

Sworn 29th. July 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Martha Burvell
