Middlesex ss}  Ambrose Lees maketh oath that he knows a child named Jane Lockhart as he has been informed aged about four years & now resident in the Workhouse belonging to & Situate in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County the which Child as this Deponent has been in the Sd County The Which Child as this Deponent has been Credibly informed & verily believes was born at a house known by the name of No 1 in Bishops Court in the Parish of St James Clerkenwell in the sd County of Middlesex & that Such is a Bastard.

Sworn this. 7. day of Janry. 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Saml Wood

Ambrose Lees
