Middx  to wit Elizth. Nevitt Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Yomiga Taylor in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of four Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Eight Months Ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement and further on her Oath Says that on the Tenth day of Febry last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish and is now Chargable thereto
