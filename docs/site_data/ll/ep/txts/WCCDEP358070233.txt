Westmr to wit Frances Mathews On her Oath saith she is about Sixty Years of Age and yt she Intermarried with Edward Mathew at the Fleet London about Thirty Years ago, says her said Husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained any settlement in England to the best of her knowledge & beleif and says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr Jackson a Master Taylor in Paradise Row in the Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Guineas a Year has Quitted her said service about Twelve Years ago, and since that time has never rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st day of Decr. before W Addington Sampn Wright

The Mark of Frances Mathews
