Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Esther Cole aged about years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 20th. day of February now last past she this Deponent Intermarried with James Cole her Present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said Husband Served his Apprenticeship to a Mr. Mc. Donald a Taylor near Clements Inn gate in the said Parish and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted her and refuses to Allow her any Support and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself without Relief from the said parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 30th. day of April 1799 before me} Saml. Edwards

her Esther Cole mark
