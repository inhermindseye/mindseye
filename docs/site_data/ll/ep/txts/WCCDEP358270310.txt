Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Naish aged about Twenty four Three years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That on or about the Eighth day of April now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of John That one John Broad who did lodge at Mrs. Willis's opposite the Dover Castle on the Narrow Wall  Lambeth and now works near the same place is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Broad is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 7th. day of July 1797 before me  Gs Goodenough

her Catherine Naish Mark
