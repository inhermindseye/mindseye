Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Ann Ingram  Wife of Daniel Ingram  Whites Alley in the Liberty of the Rolls  Chandler on behalf of her Mother Ann Bedford who now lies Ill at Mr Lightfoots Holler Street in the said parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and saith that her Father who Barnaby Bedford (who is now supported by the parish of St. Pancras in their poor House) Rented & lived in an House in Brookes's Garden Tottenham Court Road in the parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for abt. Fourteen Years Years at the yearly Rent of £14 which House they quitted abt. 5 Years ago And that her said Mother has informed this Deponent which she verily believes to be true that her said father paid Ten pounds a Year Rent for the same House And this Examinant further saith That her said Father never since Rented any House or Lodging of £10 a year or did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge or belief he might have gained any other Settlement And that her said Mother is Ill, Poor and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn the 4th. March before Thus  Sampn Wright N Bond

Ann Ingram
