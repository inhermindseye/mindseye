Middx  Phabe Holden  Wife of Harding Holden upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Harding Holden at the Fleet London  on the 21st. Augt 1749 and hath by him one Child Thomas aged about 13 Months Says her sd. husband is gone from her and she knows not where he is and that she hath been informed and beleives he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Peter Harding Leatherseller in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 28th 1750 before  Tho Lane .

The mark of Phabe Holden
