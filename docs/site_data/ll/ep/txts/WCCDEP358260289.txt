Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Nash aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bowers at the Sign of the Boars head in Exeter Street in the Parish of  Saint PaulCovent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Forty Shillings that she hath quitted said house about Thirty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th.. day of June 1798 before us J. Reeves Saml. Edward

her Mary Nash . mark

Witness B Newman
