Westmr.  Sarah Rucks aged upwards of 10 yrs upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year Says her late Father Andrew Ruck was a Notice of Jamaica and never gained a Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or beleif and that her late mother Abigail Ruck Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Mr Slade Cane Chair maker opposite the Cock Brewhouse in Houndsditch in the parish of St. Botolph  witht. Aldgate London and never was a Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes as this Examint. has been informard & verily beleives

Sworn Janry 11th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Sarah. Ruck
