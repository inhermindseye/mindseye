Middx  Martin Bourne on behalf of Peter English a Lunatic upon Oath Saith the sd. Michl. English (before his Lunacy) has oft informed this Examinant he was born at Munster in the Kingdom of Ireland & this this Examint. can give no further Accot. of his Settlemt.

Sworn Augt. 1754: before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Martin Bourne
