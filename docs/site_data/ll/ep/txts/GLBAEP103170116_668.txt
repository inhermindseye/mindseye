Middlesex ss}  Christian Brady the widow of Charles Brady deced maketh oath That after the death of her sd late Husband She lived in & rented a house of Fourteen pounds a year in the  parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & resided thereon for the Space of Nine Months & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That she this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten pounds or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor been Married or done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this 10 day of December 1777 before P. Rowlend

The Mark of Christian Brady
