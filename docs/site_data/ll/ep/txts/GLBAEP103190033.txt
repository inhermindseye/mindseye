Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Roe the wife of Thomas Roe maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her in his Majesties first Regiment of Foot Guards That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that her sd husband was duly bound to & served the whole of his apprenticeship with Richard Varnish Worsted Weaver in the City of Norwich & to her knowledge or belief has not since done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere & that she was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn the. 9. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Roe
