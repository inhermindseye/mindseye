Middx.  James Harris Aged about 42 Years upon his Oath Saith he Intermarried with his Present Wife  Mary about 21 Years since at the Parish Church of St. George Southwark by whom he hath 2 Children (to wit) James Aged 12 Yrs. & Wm. aged about 10 Yrs. Says he Served 3 Yrs. & upwards the latter Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Babb in Cow Lane in the Parish of St Sepulchers London Blacksmith and hath not since his said Apprentiship Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworne 10th. Novr. 1744 before} Chas: Fraser

The mark of James Harris
