Middx  Ann Brown upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Champ in High Street in the Parish of Alhallows in Oxford at the yearly Wages of £2. for two years or thereabouts. Quitted her said Service about 11 years ago and has not since Rented Ten pounds by the year Paid Parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn July 18th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Ann Brown
