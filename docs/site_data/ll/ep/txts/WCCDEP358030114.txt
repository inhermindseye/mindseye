Middx  Eliz Milkin  Wife of Paul Milkin upon Oath Says before she intermarried with the said Phillip Milkin she intermarried with one Robt. Wilkinson since deceased Says the sd. Rt. Wilkinson Served abt. 8 Months the latter Part of his Apprenticeship with one Mr. Smith in Bond Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. Gunsmith Says her sd. late Husband Robt. Wilkinson never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Child living by the sd. Robt. Wilkinson (to wit) Robt. aged upwds. of 8 Yrs. & that by Order under the hands & Seales of two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace bearing Date the 8th. Day of July 1740 She this Examinant with the abovesaid Robert Wilkinson then aged abt. two Yrs. (& Edward Wilkinson then aged abt. 4 Months since deced) were removed from the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes to the

sd. Parish of St. George Hanover Square agt. which Order there was no Appeale

Sworn March 15th 1745 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Eliz: Milkin
