Westmr.  Margt Budels aged 30 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Stuart opposite Oxford Chapel in the Parish of St. Geo However Squares in the Liberty of Westmr. St. Mary le bone in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £6 for one Year & upwds. quitted her sd. Service abt. 7 Years ago & has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 5th 1752 before  Saml Peckell W Feilding

The Mark of Margt. Budels
