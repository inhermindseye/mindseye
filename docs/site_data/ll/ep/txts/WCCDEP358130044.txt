Middx  Ruth Turner  Widow aged about 69 years upon Oath says that during her Widowhood and being without Child or Children She lived a hired yearly Servant for 10 years or thereabouts with with Mr. Gibson at a Cellar in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the yearly wages of Three Pounds and quitted the said Service upwards of three years and has not since lived in Service elsewhere nor Rented Ten pounds by the year, nor paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the day of Augst. 1755 before}
