Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Malcomb aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the year 1772 she Intermarried with Saml. Linter her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch that he has been dead about Nine years that she has one Child living by her said late Husband Vizt. Sarah aged about Fifteen years That her said late Husband informed this Examinant that he was born at the corner of Lumbard Street in the Precinct of White Fryars in the City of London and that he never served any Apprenticeship lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th day of Augt. 1789  Tho. Vaughan N Bond

her Sarah Malcomb Mark
