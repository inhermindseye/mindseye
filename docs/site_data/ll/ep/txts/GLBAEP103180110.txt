Middlesex ss}  Ann Leggatt  Singlewoman maketh oath That by a Pass order under the hands & Seals of Parker Rowlands Clerk & Jasper Clarke  Esqr two Justices of the peace for the Sd County of Middx bearing date the Sixteenth day of November one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy Six She this Depont was removed from the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County to the Parish of St Mary Newington in the County of Surry as being the Place of her last legal Settlement against Which order of Removal no Appeal was ever made or taken by the Parish officers of St Mary Newington to the knowledge information or belief of this Deponent That She this Deponent Since Such her removal as aforesd hath not lived as a hired servant for a year or been Married or done any Act to gain another Settlement.

Sworn this 15th day of December 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Ann Leggatt
