Middlesex ss  John Robertson Maketh oath That he was born in North Britain and th his Parents to his knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a settlement in England; That divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at the year wages of Five Pounds with one Mr Kelvington in the Parish of Thesk in the County of york and continued in such Service for two years he being during such his Servitude a single & unmarried man and by means them as he is advised gamed a legal Settlement there; That at the he hath since lived as a hired servant by the year in the Part of Great Britain Called England yet he was during such his last mentioned servitude lawfully married to present wife and could not as he is advised there by gai a subsequent settlement; That he never rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Tax nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any act to gain a Settlement subsequent to his Servitude first Above mentioned That he hath a wife named Amelia , and three children namely Christian , aged about three years & two months Jane , aged about two years & James , aged about seven Months the Lawful Issue of this Depont. by his Said Wife.

Sworn this day of September 1770 before}
