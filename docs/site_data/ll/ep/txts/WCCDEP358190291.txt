Middx  Ann Woodcock Aged About Forty Seven Years upon Oath says she never Married that she lived A Hired Yearly Servant with Mrs Webb who kept an Earthen Ware Shop over against Mr Shelmerdines in Near Street by Clare Market in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & hath quitted her said service about Fourteen Years and hath not since Rented House pd. Taxes or lived A Hired Yearly Servt. for One Year.

Sworn ye 20th Day of Octr 1762 Before} Paul Vaillant

Ann Woodcock her Mark
