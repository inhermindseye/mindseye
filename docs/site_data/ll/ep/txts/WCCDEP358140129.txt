Middx.  Thos. Giblett aged abt. 72 Years upon Oath says he served four Years and upwards the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Thos. Raban  Perukemaker in Warwick Lane Newgate Street in the Parish of Christ Church in the City of London and Never Rented A house of Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 10th. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Thos. Giblett
