Middx.  Eliz: Miller aged abt. 56 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of George Miller who has been dead abt. Twenty one Years says that She hath One daughter named Mary aged abt. 25 Yrs. who lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Tilliard in Tavistock Street in the parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. for three Years at the Yearly Wages of £3 and hath quitted said Service abt. three Years; says that since her said Daughter quitted her said Service in Tavistock Street she this Examinant lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Whitehall a Butcher who lodged at Mr. Dobeys a Cheesemonger in Clare market in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for One Year & a half at the Yearly Wages of £5 and hath quitted sd. Service abt. One Year and a half & hath since Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn this 11th. day August 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Miller
