Middlesex ss}  Hannah Percivall the widow of William Percivall deced maketh oath that Since her Sd husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with one Mr Hall a Victualler at the Sign of the Black Horse in Kings land Road in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the Sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such Service under such hiring for about the Space of three years She this Deponent being then an unmarried Woman & having no Child or Children living That She has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29 day of Novemr 1784 before J. Hawley Robt: Smith

The Mark of Hannah Percivall
