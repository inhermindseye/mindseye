Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Finch aged about 22 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Clarke in Princes Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster for about Five years at the yearly Wages of Twelve Guineas that he hath guitted said Service about Eight Weeks since which time he has done no Act to gain subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of March 1789  Sampn Wright  Wm Kitchiner

his John Finch Mark
