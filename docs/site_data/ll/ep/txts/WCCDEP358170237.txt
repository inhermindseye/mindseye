Middx  Mary Lloyd aged abt. 44 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Newhouse in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of Four Years & 6 Months & hath left the same about Eight Years that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 29th. May 1742 before}
