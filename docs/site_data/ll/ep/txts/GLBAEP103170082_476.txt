Middlesex ss}  Mary Burgess  Singlewoman maketh oath that about Six years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at certain Wages by & with morris Edwards at the Black Horse Alehouse upper East smithfield in the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the said county & continued in Such Service for the Space of two years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any act to gain a Settlement Since Such her Service as above Stated.

Sworn this 10 day of June 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Burgess
