Middlesex to wit John Daney Aged about Twenty Six Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year otherwise than as herein after mentioned Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Thomas Harris a Chairmaker in Lambeth Marsh in the Parish of St. Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry and served his said Master under that Indenture for upwards of Two years when his said Master went abroad and this Examinant not going with him he went and lived with one John Gifford a Baker in the aforesd. Parish and lived with him for upwards of Twelve Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds and upwards payable Weekly besides Diet and Lodging and after quitting the sd last mentioned place he never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly or Covenanted Servant for one year.

Sworn this 10th. day of June 1778 before  Abrm. Baley

his John Daney Mark
