Westmr.  Mary Jones upon Oath saith she is the Widow of John Jones by whom she hath 3 Children Eliz . aged about 3 Years Ann aged about two Years and Sarah aged one Year Says she hath been informed and verily believes her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with a Wheelwright upon the point in the parish of in Dover in the County of Kent and further Says neither her sd. late husband nor this Examinant ever Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 10th. 1751 before
