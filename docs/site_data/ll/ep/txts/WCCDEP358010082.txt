Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westminsr. ss)  Isabella Welbank aged 48 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived with Antony James in Bell Yard as a Yearly hired Servant for 2 years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of 6 Guineas She has quitted said service about 7 years ago since which time she has not lived as a Yearly hired Servant for one year or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21th day of March 1787  Thos: Gordons Maddington

her Isabella Welbank Mark
