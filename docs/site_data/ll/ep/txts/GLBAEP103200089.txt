Middlesex ss}  Sarah Plantin maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Edward Ratcliffe of Bathwell in the County of Essex  Farmer & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of about three years & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 12. day of Janry 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Plantin
