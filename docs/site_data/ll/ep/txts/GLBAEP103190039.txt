Middlesex ss}  Mary Keld the widow of Stephen Keld deced maketh oath That divers Years ago her sd husband rented a house of Five pounds a year in Great Gardens in the Precinct of Saint Catherine in the said County & was charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same and that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That she this Deponent since his death hath not done any Act to gain a settlement for herself & that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 13. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke W Blackmore

The Mark of Mary Keld
