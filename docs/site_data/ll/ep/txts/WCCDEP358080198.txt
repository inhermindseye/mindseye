Westmr- to wit Elizth- Dighton aged about 56 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of David Dighton who has been Dead upwds- of eight Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says she Intermarrd- with her said late husband at the parish Church of St- Andrew Holborn about thirty Years ago Says her said husband before his Intermarrd. and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with James Hemmings at the George Inn in Little Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmrs - for eight Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds a Years hath quitted his said Service about thirty Years ago since which time he never gained any other subsequent Settlement either by Renting house or paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said husbands Death

Sworn this 29th- Day of Decr- 1777 before  Jno. Machin  John Cox .

Elizabeth Dighton
