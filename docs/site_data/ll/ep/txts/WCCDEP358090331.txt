Middx  Elizabeth Farquhar aged about Thirty eight Years on her oath says, she is the Wife of Robert Farquhar with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Sepulchre London in the year one thousand seven Hundred and sixty two, and by whom she hath six Children now living namely Robert Aged about Seventeen Years now Provided for, Elizabeth aged upwards of Fourteen Years, Mary aged about Seven Years George aged upwards of Five Years. James aged about four Years and Charles aged upwards of Two Years the last five of white are unprovided for, And further says that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Newport Market , in the Parish of Saint Ann in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Eighteen Pounds, hath quitted the said House about Three Months ago, and hath not since he Quitted the same ever Rented House Tenement or Lodgings of Ten pound a Year, paid Taxes or gained any other Settlement.

Sworn this 1st- day of October 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Farquhar
