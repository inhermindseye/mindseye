St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) Ann Nash aged eighteen years and upwards upon Oath Saith that she never was married, that she nev lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hershaw in Clements Inn passage for thirteen Months at the yearly Wages of Forty shillings, that she was paid one years Wages only, and the last quarter's payment she reced about a Month previous to her departure, that She quitted that Service about four y or five Six or Seven Months ago and hath never obtained any subsequent legal settlement as she best knows and believes And this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes when Born, And that Smith a Hair Dresser who lives in Houghton Street in the said parish, and keeps a Shop there, is the only real and true father of such Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid he having first had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body in or about the Month of October last and at several once since few times since at some or when or at one of which times he did beget the Said Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant, as aforesaid on this Deponent's Body And

this Deponent also upon her Voluntary Oath saith that the said Smith & no other person is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn the 17th day of April 1792 . before Me R Butler

Ann Nash 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys

The Girl lives at the Ship by Essex Stairs
