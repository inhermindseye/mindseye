Martha Ellis & illegitimate Child

St Clement Danes 6th March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  Child St Brides  (to wit) Martha Ellis  singlewoman aged 29 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Ross an Attorney in Old New Boswell Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for One year Nine Months & upwards at the Annual Wages of Seven pounds that she quitted Service about five years ago and hath not obtained Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief belief And that this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that on the 25th day of January last she this Examinant Examinant was delivered of a four Male Bastard Child at the House of Messrs Ellis & Twycross at No. 4 Middle New Street Fetter Lane in the parish of St Brides ot St Bridget otherwise Saint Bride London which is now living and with her this Examinant for Nurture & chargeable to the parish of St Clement Danes aforesaid

Sworn the 6th day of March 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Martha Ellis

Woman & Child Dead
