Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Elizth. Burley aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1773 she Intermarried with Chas. Burley her present Husband (who is at present ill in St. Bartholomews Hospital ) at the Parish Church of St. Mary Le Bone and by whom she hath 3 Children living vizt. Wm. aged about 8 years Jas . aged about 9 years and Elizth. aged about 3 years. That her said Husband Rented & lived in a House in Ogle Court in the said Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middx for 3 years or Thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £30 & paid all Taxes due for the same that he hath quitted said House about 11 years ago since which time he has not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. Augt. 1787 before G Mercer Wm. Gascoignes

Elizth. Burley
