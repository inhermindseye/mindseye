Middx  Ann Watts aged abt. 24 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she rented a house of the yearly Rent of Twenty two pounds in Drury Lane in the parish of St Clemt. Danes lived therein upwds. of Three months & has left the sd. house abt. 6 months Since which she has not rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become chargeable to the aforesd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the Same when born and that William Stringer  Gent did beget the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her body. the sd Wm Stringer having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the latter End of June or in the beginning of July last and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the sd. Wm. Stringer

and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn March 1st. 1754 before} Tho Lane

Ann Watts
