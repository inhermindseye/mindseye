Middlesex ss}  Mary Greenow the wife of John Greenow who is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore maketh oath that her said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true That he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his apprenticeship with a Taylor (whose name She Can't at present recollect) in the parish of Chipping Norton in the County of Oxford & to her knowledge or belief her said husband has not Since done any Act to gain Another Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 22. day of April 1780 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Mary Greenow
