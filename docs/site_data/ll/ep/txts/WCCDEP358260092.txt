Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Eleanor Henly aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that about Ten years ago she Intermarried with Wm- Henly her late Husband who has been dead about Eighteen Months in Dublin that her said late Husband was an Irishman a never gained any settlement in England and also that she this Deponent is an Irishwoman and never gained any Settled in England and this Deponent further saith that she hath Two Children living by her said late Husband Vizt. Elizabeth aged about Seven years born at the house of a Mr. H a Grocer in Whitecross Street in the Parish of Saint Giles without within Cripplegate in the City of London on or about the 20th. day of October 1788 and Mary born at the house of a Mr. Smallman in Castle Alley Whitechapel in the Parish of Saint Mary Matfellon alias Whitechapel on or about the 18th. day of June 1792

Sworn this Seventh day of Decr. 1795 before us W Kinnard N Bond

Eleanor Henly
