Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Oddy aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith That she Rented and lived in a house in Nags head Court Fish Street Hill in the Parish of Saint Benedict in the City of London for Nine Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty Guineas that she hath quitted said House about Twelve years since which time she hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of March 1797 before us} N Bond Rusl: Clarke

her Sarah Oddy Mark

Witness B Newman
