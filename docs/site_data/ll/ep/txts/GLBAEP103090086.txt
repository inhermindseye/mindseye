Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Eleanor Bowler of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Widow of John Bowler deced taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Thirteenth-day of November in the year of our Lord 1767

Who on her oath Saith That She is now with Child And that the said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that one Thomas Hill of the Parish of Trinity Minories in the said County Gunsmith is the Father of the Said Child.

Sworn This 13th day of November 1767 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Eleanor Bowler
