Middlesex ss.  John Ingleton Maketh Oath that about Fourteen Years ago he Rented a House of Ten Pounds a Year in Dean Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & Lived in the same about Ten Years & was charged to and paid the Poors Rate for the same that he has not since he quitted the said House Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his Knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of July 1795 before}
