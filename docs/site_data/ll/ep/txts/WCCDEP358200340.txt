Westmr.  Sarah Allmond aged upwards of 20 Years upon Oath says she never was married Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year says her father James Allmond Rented a house and Gardens in the Parish of St. John in the City of Chester of the Yearly Rent of Sixty Pounds for several Years, quitted the same about four Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes; And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . where she now lodges she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born, says that Benjamin Fidler who is a Waiter at the Dolphin Tavern at Temple bar in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body sometime in the Month of June last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Benjamin Fidler and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 30th. of Decr. 1765 before} Paul Vaillant

Sarah Allmond
