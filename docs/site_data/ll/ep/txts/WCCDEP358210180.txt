Westmr.  Joseph Dunlop aged about Thirty five years Upon Oath says, that about thirteen Years ago he lived a hired Yearly Servant with George Fox Lane  Esqr , (now Lord Bingley) in Cavendish Square in the Parish of St. Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for near the space of two years at the Yearly Wages of Thirty five Pounds and then intermarried with Ann his first Wife and continued in said Service under the same hiring for one Year or thereabouts after his said Marriage and was discharged his Service in Cavendish Square aforesaid where he resided in the house of the said George Fox Lane Esqr .  during the last Six Months of this Examinants Service; says his first Wife Ann hath been dead about Six Years, and that he intermarried with Margaret his present Wife in the Parish Church of St. Margaret Westminster abt. four Years ago; says from the time of his first Wife's Death to the time of his second Marriage he never lived a hired Yearly Servant for One year, and further upon his Oath says he never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 22nd, day of Augst,, 1767 , before}  W Kelynge

Joseph Dunlop
