Middx  Ann Skinner aged abt. Eighteen Years Upon Oath Says She was never Married and cannot give any Accot. of the Settlemt. of her father or Mother Says her Grand father Jno. Skinner Rented a House in the Parish of Rigate in the County of Surry & Served the Office of Overseers of the poor there as She hath been informed & verily believes Says She was brt. up in the Workhouse belonging to the sd. parish & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt.

Sworn the 28th. Day of May 1756 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Ann Skinner
