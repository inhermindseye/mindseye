Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Turner aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Waite in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Thirty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Novr. 1796   Rd Thompson N Bond

The Mark of Hannah Turner
