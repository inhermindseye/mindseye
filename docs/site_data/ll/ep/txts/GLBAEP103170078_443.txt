Middlesex ss  Margaret Jolly the widow of Richard Jolly deced maketh oath That She can give no account respecting her sd late husband Jolly's Settlement That many years ago & long before her marriage with the sd Richd Jolly She was married at the Fleet to one Willm Austin who as she has been informed by him & believes was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Thomas Cartwright in Kingsland Road in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the said county Stocking Frame Knitter & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that he did not afterwards to this deponts knowledge or belief rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or serve any parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent Since there Spewe deaths of her sd late husbands hath not lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or paid any poors Rates or kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself save what She has acquired by her Second marriage

Sworn this 19 day of May 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Margaret Jolly
