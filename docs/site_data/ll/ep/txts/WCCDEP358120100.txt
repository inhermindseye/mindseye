Middx  Mary Woolerton aged 64 Years upon Oath saith she is the wido. of Wm. Woolerton who dyed abt. 15 years Since that her sd. late husband lived in & rented a house next the Lamb Inn in the Back of St Clements in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes of the yearly Rent of £10 for Seven or 8 Years and afterwards rented a house of £20 by the Year in St Clements Church Yard in the same Parish and lived therein abt. one Year & that her sd. late husband after he quitted the sd. last mentioned house never rented ten pounds by the Year or pd. parish taxes nor has this Examint. since his decease rented ten pounds by the year pd. parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year (except) with Mrs. Santilow at Sunbury for one year & Six months at which time she had a Daughter named Mary who never had been an apprentice or lived a yearly Servant for one Year at the time she was wth. Mrs. Santilow

Sworn May 10th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Woolerton
