Middx  Hannah Stewart upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Alexr. Stewart an Irishman who never gained a Settlement in England and that she was bound Apprentice for three Years to Mary Smith a Millenar at Newcastle upon Tyne in the County of Northumberland & Served her as an Apprentice for 3 months & lodged at that time in the Side in the Parish of St. Nichs . in Newcastle afsd. & then they parted by consent & this Examinant never was assigned over to any other person and never rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt.

Sworn Feby. 5th. 1753 Before}
