Middx  Danl. Jones aged abt. Twenty Nine Years Upon Oath says he was Bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Benjn. Hopkins a Butcher in Tower Street in the Parish of Alhall Barkin London that he this Examinant served Six Years & upwards of his sd. Apprentishipp & then his sd. Master left Business & this Examinant went to Sea, Says he never was Assigned over to any person, Rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 4th. day Febry March 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Daniel Jones
