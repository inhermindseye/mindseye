St Clement Danes 13th February 1792

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Henrietta Bernard  Widow of Joseph Bernard Deced Aged about twenty Nine Years Upon Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband Joseph Bernard at the parish Church of St. Luke Chelsea Middlesex on or abt. the 22nd. day of December 1778 that her said Husband hath Informed her which else verily believes that previous to then Marriage and while he was single & unmarried not having any Child or Children lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Strannaugh at the Artichoke Peckham in the parish of Peckham in the County of Surry for three years at the Yearly Wages of Nine Guas. that he quitted that service about fourteen years ago and since hath and obtained any no other legal Settlement and to the best of this Deponents knowledge or belief and this Deponents further saith that he Died on the 9th of August last that she hath one one Child now living by her said Husband namely named Joseph aged Six Months & upwards that she this Deponent hath not gained any Settlement since her said Husbands Decease and is now unable to provide for herself without Assistance

Sworn the 13th febry 1792 before us  Sampr Wright Addington

Henrietta Bernard
