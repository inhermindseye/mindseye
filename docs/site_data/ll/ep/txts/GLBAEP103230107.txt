Middlesex ss}  Matthew Claverly maketh oath that he lately rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Guineas in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & was Charged to And Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house or Lodging of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18. day of January 1788 before  Robt. Smith

Matthew Claverley
