Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Ann Simpson an Infant aged about Eleven Months Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Mary Ann Simpson was born a Bastard in the Westminster Lying In Hospital on or about the 11th. day of June 1795 That Mary Simpson the Mother of the said Child when Single lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Jan Nixon an Attorney in Thornaugh Street in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for about Fifteen Months at the yearly wages of Six pounds and that after she quitted her said Service she never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement otherwise than by Married which this Deponent is informed does not Relate to the Child in Question

Sworn this 17th. day of May 1796 before us N Bond W Kinnard

Jas Talboy
