Westmr.  Patrick Plinikin upon Oath Saith he Intermarryed with his Wife Ann at the Fleet London abt. 13 yrs. ago and has by her one Child Mason Plinkin aged about Seven Years that he was born in the Parish of Thilbegnet in the County of Gallowage in Ireland and never gained a Settlemt. in any other Places to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 21st. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Part Plinikin
