Middx  Eleanr Nichols  Wido . of John Nichols upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. John Nichols at Gils field Church in Montgomeryshire about Sixteen Years ago and that her said late husband about 12 Yrs. since rented a house of the yearly Rent of 50s. in the said parish of Gils field and paid his Share of the poor rate of the said parish and lived in the said house abt. 4 Yrs. and this Examint. further saith that her said late husband after he quitted the said house or this Examinant since his Decease have not rented Ten pounds a year sd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 28th 1750 before}

The mark of Eleanr Nichols
