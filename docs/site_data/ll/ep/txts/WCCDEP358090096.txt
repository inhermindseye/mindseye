Middx  Matthew Cook aged about Forty Six Years on his Oath say's be Intermarried to his present Wife Hannah at the Parish Church of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Year 1757 and by whom She hath no Children now living  says before his Intermarriage to his first Wife Mary he served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Bissett a Bookseller in Saint Clements Church Yard and with whom he lived as a hired Yearly Servant for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds besides diet and Lodging and after quitting his said Masters service he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 23nd. day of November 1779 before  Abrm Baley

Matthew Cook
