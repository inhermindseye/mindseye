Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Clarke aged about 41 years upon her Oath saith that about 24 years ago she Intermarried with John Clarke her present husband at the parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields That her said Husband was bound Apprentice to Wm. Fell a Taylor who lived in Newcastle Cowit for seven years that after he had served Six years of his said Apprenticeship his Master gave him up his Indentures and that the last Six Months of his said Service with his said Master was in Bread Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster where he was discharged and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of Augt. 1790 M Addington Sampn Wright

Her Mary Clarke Mark
