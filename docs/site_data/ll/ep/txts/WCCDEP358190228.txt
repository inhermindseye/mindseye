Middx   John Palmer aged about 46 years removed from the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster upon Oath saith he intermarried with his present Wife  Esther in the Year 1742 Says that before his Intermarriage and while he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant (in the Capacity of a Porter ) with Mr. Creamer at the Five Bells in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for One year & a half at the yearly Wages of £5 and that he Quitted the Same a day or two before his said Marriage Says he never rented a House or paid the Land Tax of Poor Rate

Sworn the 4th: day of March 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of John Palmer
