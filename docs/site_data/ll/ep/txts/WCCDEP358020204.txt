St Clemt. Danes May 1st. 1790

Middlesex (ss)  Sarah Godfrey aged 21 Years upon her Oath saith That she Never was Married That she was bound Apprentice to Mr Murphy a Milk Man in Crown Court Russell Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex for the Term of seven Years that she served 3 Years of her time and was then discharged from her said Master by Order of the Magistrates about 5 Years ago since which time she hath done No Act Matter or thing to the best of her knowledge to gain Any other settlement

Sworn this 1st day of May 1790 before N. Bond W Addington

her Sarah Godfrey Mark
