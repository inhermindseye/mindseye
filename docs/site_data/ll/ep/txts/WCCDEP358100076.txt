Middx to Wit Elizth. Catlin Aged about Thirty years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one year Says she served Seven Years Apprenticeship with her Mother Cath: Catlin a Milliner and Clear Starcher in Meads Court  Old Bond Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex and after she was out of her sd. Apprenticeship she never gained any or. Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 11th. day of June last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the name of Elizth . and is now Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same And further says that one Mr. Loyd David Allen by Mistake called an Attorney who lately lived at No.1 New Inn is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his the sd. Mr. Loyd David Allen Chambers the 10th. day of Septr. 1782 at which time he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. And that the said Mr. Loyd David Allen & no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 3d. day of July 1783  Wm Kitchiner

Resworn this 15th day of July 1783 }

her Elizth. Catlin mark
