Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Eleanor Allsop of the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County this Twenty third day of october 1776 Who Saith that She is now with child & that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard & to be Chargeable to the Said parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that one George Booge late of the Sd parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate  Mariner is the Father of the Said Child

Sworn this 23 day of October 1776 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Eleanor Ellsop
