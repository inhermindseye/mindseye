Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Alice Kelly aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Leigh at Buttle Bridge in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Pancras in the County of Middx for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about 5 years ago since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of Janry 1788 .  Sampr Wright M Addington

her Alice Kelly Mark
