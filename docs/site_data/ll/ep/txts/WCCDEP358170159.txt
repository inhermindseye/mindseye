Middx  Eliz: Coker  Wido . of Richd. Coker upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with the sd. Richd. Coker abt. Years since & that her sd. late Husband in his Life Time lived in and rented a House in Angell Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds for the Space of Six Years and upwds. that since her sd. late Husband left the sd. House he did not in his Life rent Ten Pounds a Year pay Parish Taxes nor hath she since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or Beleife

Sworne the 22d. of October 1741 before}
