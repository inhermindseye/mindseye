Middx  Eleanr Smith aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servt. with Mr. Short in Surry Street in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & upwds. at the yearly Wages of Four pounds quitted her sd. Service abt. 8 Yrs. ago & has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn May 25th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

E Smith
