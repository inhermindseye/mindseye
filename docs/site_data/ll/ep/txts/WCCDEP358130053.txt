Middx  Ann Lloyd  Widow aged about 60 Years upon Oath says that She lived a Yearly Servant with one Mr. Lloyd in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for Seven Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings and quitted the same about three Years since And further says that during the whole time of her said Service She was a Widow without Child or Children and that She hath not since Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 23d. Day of Septr. 1755 before } Barth: Hammond

Ann Lloyd
