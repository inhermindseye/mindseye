Middx to wit Ann Lovin aged about Forty Years (on behalf of William and Grace Morrell Children of Robt. Morrell who is now a Prisoner in Tothill Fields Bridewell ) On her Oath says that her Brother Robt. Morrell before his Intermarriage with Sarah his now Wife he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Willows at Great Chelsea in the Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea in the County of Middlesex in the Capacity of a Coachman upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds besides Dyet Lodging and Cloaths as she believes and after quitting the said Mrs. Willows's Service he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever as her said Brother has this Day Informed her and which Information she Verily believes to be true

Sworn this 24th. day of March 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Ann Lovin Mark
