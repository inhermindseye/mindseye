Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to Allhallows Barking to wit Elizth Reynolds aged about 64 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Joseph Reynolds her late Husband deceased at Cannon Street Church  Ratcliff Highway about 30 years ago and by whom she has no Children living Says that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Bear Street Lane  upper Thames Street in the Parish Allhallows Barking for 30 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £12 and upwards and paid Taxes for the same hath quitted said House about Ten years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 17th- day of Febry 1785  Sampn Wright  Thos: Gilbert

her Elizth Reynolds mark
