Middx 

Eliz Hamblin upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Nichl Hamblin who dyed abt. Six months since with whom she intermarryed and that her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with his father Hamblin in Ayre Street in the Parish of in the City of Exeter in the County of Devon and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examints knowledge or beleif that she hath two Children by her said late husband (viz) Benj. aged upwds. of two Years and Joseph aged about nine months

Sworn Janry 20th. 1749 before}
