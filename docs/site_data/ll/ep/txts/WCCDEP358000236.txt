Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Ruth Langford aged 72 years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That She Intermarried with Wm. Langford her late Husband at the Parish Church  Fleet about 36 years ago That her said Husband Informed then Examinant that he Resided with his Father who rented & Lived in a House Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the said of St. Clement Danes for about 5 years That her said Husband Departed this Life upwards of 4 years ago That her said Husband in his Life time on this Examinant since his Decease have not nor hath either of them done any Act Matter or things thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief they a either of them might have gained any Settlement in their own Right and that She that Examinant in Poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 24th Febry 1786 before me R Butler

her Ruth Langford Mark
