Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Henry Day one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Dunn who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Mary Dunn lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Clark at the Kings head in Gerard Street in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for Six years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she quitted said Service about Fifteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or they whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of July 1799 before us} Menter G S Goodenough

Henry Day
