Westmr.  John Warne of White's Grounds Southwark  Currier on behalf of his Brother Joseph Warne aged about Twenty seven Years Upon Oath says the said Joseph Warne was never married and that he lived a Covenant Servant by the Year for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds or thereabouts (with dyet and lodging) with Mr. John Homan a Currier in Trotter Alley, Barnaby Street in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry, quitted said Service about seven Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 9th, of Janry. 1767 , before } Paul Vaillant  Thos Kynaston

John Warne
