Middx.  Eliz: Peeke aged abt. 38 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Willm. Peeke (now at sea) says her said Husbd. served his Apprenticeship of seven years to Mr. Chas. Bourwood a Shoe maker in Palsgrave head Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. & that he never Rented ten Pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes says she hath three Childn. by her said Husbd. (Viz:) Mary aged abt. 8 yrs. Cath : aged abt. 5 years & Susa . aged abt. one year & a half

Sworn the 30th day of April 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Peeke
