Saint Clement Danes

Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  on the behalf of Catharine Lenoir aged 25 or thereabouts Upon this Oath Saith That he has has been informd which information he believes to be true that her father kept House on Snow Hill that after his death her Mother went to live in Walworth in the County of Surry that she lived there and Rented House for upwards of One Year that after since she quitted her said Mother she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge and belief

Sworn at this 2d day of July 1788 before Philip Dyot D Walker}

Benj: Newman
