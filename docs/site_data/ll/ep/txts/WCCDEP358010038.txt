Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Mary Healy aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that about 10 years ago she Intermarried with Joseph Healy her late Husband (who has been dead about 2 years) at the parish Church of Saint Mary Lambeth that she hath no Child or Children living that her said late Husband and this Ext. Rented and lived in a House near Bagnigge Wells in the parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middx for 6 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £10 And paid all Taxes due for the same that she hath quitted said about 5 years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th day of Novr. 1786 }

Mary Healey

Never Sworn
