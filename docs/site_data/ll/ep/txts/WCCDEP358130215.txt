Middx.  Sarah Hoy aged abt. Sixty Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Charles Hoy who hath been dead abt. Twenty Years says the said Chas. Hoy informed this Examinant & which Informacon she believes to be true that he was born & served his Apprenticeship in Willington in the County of Somerset Says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to her knowledge Says She hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes Says abt. Sixteen Years ago She this Examinant lived a hired Yearly Servant with Dr. Jennings in Castle Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings but had at that time a Daughter living (to wit) Mary aged abt. Twelve Years

Sworn the 22d Sepr. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Hoy
