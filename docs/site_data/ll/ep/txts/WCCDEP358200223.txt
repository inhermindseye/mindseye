Middx  James Cobham Aged about fourteen Years upon Oath says he was Bound Apprentice by the Church Wardens & Overseers of St. Clement Danes to Joshua Roper of Turnmill Street in the Parish of St. John Clerkenwell in the County of Middx Cabinet founder with whom he lived about Nine Months and then went away & never after was Assigned over to any Person

Sworn the 24th. of Jany 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of J C James Cobham
