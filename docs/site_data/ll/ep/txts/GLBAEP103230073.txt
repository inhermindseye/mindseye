Middlesex ss}  Sarah Vince the wife of Richard Vince maketh oath that She wall knows a Child named Mary White half which was born of the body of this Deponents late Sister Ann Lambort deced in the Queens Lying in Hospital Tottenham Court Read in the said County the which Child is Aged about twelve years & two Months and is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason that this Deponents Said Sister at the birth of Such Child had never been married and her Settlement at the birth of the Sd Child was in the Parish of St. Olave in Silver Street in the City of London And this Deponent Saith that the Said Child has not as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 28. day of September 1787 before  R Davies  Robt Smith

The Mark of Sarah Vince
