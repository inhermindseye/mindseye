Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Shirley Aged about 29 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was married That on the 4th day of November 1792 she was Delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of a Mrs Rayner in Brew house yard in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the City of London which Child hath since been Christend by the name of Mary

Sworn this 16th day of Octr. 1800 before us} Saml. Edwards  John Collick

her Mary Shirley Mark

Witness B Newman
