Middx  Martha Skidmore Aged about Thirty Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Buddle a Stone Mason opposite Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County Middx upwards of Fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about two Years & hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the Day of 176 Before}
