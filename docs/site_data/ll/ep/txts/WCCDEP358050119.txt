Westmr.  Sarah Horseman on behalf of Mary Smallshaw aged upwds. of Sixteen Years upon Oath saith the sd. Mary Smallshaw is her Daughter by her late husband Robt Smallshaw deceased and that she sd. Mary Smallshaw never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year & that her sd. late husband Robt Smallshaw Served his Apprenticeship with Mr Stevens Leather frame maker in Black and white Court in the Great Old Bailey in the parish of St. Sepulchers London and never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Octr. 23d. 1749 before} Tho. Lane .

The Mark of of Sarh. Horseman .
