St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Shields aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Simpkins at the Crown and Anchor in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 18 Months at the yearly Wages of £9..10..0 hath quitted said Service about 12 Months since which time she has not done no Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. Day of May 1787 R Butler

Ann Shields
