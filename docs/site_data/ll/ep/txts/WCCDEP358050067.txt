Westmr.  Hannah Millington upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Wm. Millington who dyed the 18th. Day of Febry. last Says she Intermarried with the sd. Wm. Millington on the 28th. Day of Septr. 1724 at the Fleet London Says her sd. late husband rented a house in Hollis Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £28 and lived therein upwards of two Years and quitted said house at Xtmas last Says her sd. late husband after he quitted said house never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief Says she hath 3 Children viz. Wm. aged 15 Yrs. Thomas aged 12 Yrs. & Sarah aged abt 9 Yrs.

Sworn 8th. July 1749 before} Samuel Clarke

The mark of Hanh. Millington
