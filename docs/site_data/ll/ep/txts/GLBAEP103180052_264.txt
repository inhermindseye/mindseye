Middlesex ss}  John Ellis maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house in Cable Street in the Parish of St George in the Sd County of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 15. day of July 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey  Wm: Blackmore

John Ellis
