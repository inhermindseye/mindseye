Middx  Mary Canton on Behalfe of Elizabeth Marlcombe an Infant aged about Nine Years upon her Oath saith that Patrick Marlcombe the Father of the said Inft. is absconded & this Examinant dont know where he is that he was born on the Seas & never gained any Settlement in England as hath been Informed & beleives Says Mary-the Mother of sd. Inft. is dead & cant give any Account of her Settlemt. that said Elizabeth Marlcombe was born in the House of one Mrs. Hall next door to the Falcon & Maypye in the Parish of St. Bridgett otherwise Brides  London

Sworne the 25th. July 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Canton
