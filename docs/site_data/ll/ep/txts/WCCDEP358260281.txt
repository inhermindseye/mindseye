Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Douglass aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she hath been informed by her Parents which Information She verily believes to be true that she was born in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 14th. day of May 1798 before me } Saml. Edward .

her Margaret Douglass mark

H Day Beadle
