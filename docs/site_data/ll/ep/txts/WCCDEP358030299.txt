Middx  Sarah Bowen  Wife of George Bowen upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. George Bowen at Stepney Church on or abt. the 6th. Day of Janry upwds. of 13 Years ago Says her said husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship wth. Mr. Joshua Gamble a Taylor in the back of St. Clements in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx. Says the sd. Geo. Bowen never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Child named Chas. aged about Eleven Years

Sworn Decr. 31st. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Sarah Bowen
