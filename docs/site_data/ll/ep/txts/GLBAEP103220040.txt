Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Glass the wife of John Glass maketh oath that her husband John Glass heretofore rented a house of the Yearly rent of Twenty Pounds & upwards in the Parish of St Giles Without Cripplegate in the City of London & resided thereon for about the Space of a year That her Said husband has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served Any Parish Office or Paid Any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That she hath two Children namely John Glass aged about Nine Years & Elizabeth aged about two Years & Six Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom She lawfully married & that her Said husband is now absent from her in the Workhouse belonging to the Said Parish of St Giles without Cripplegate

Sworn this. 26. day of May 1786 before  Robt: Smith  Tho. T: Lotton

Eliz Glass
