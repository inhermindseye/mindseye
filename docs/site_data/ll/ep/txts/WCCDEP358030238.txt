Middx  Mary Lodge aged upwards of 24 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she was born in the Backside of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Says her Father Joseph Lodge deced Served his Apprentiship with his Father Joseph Lodge a House Joyner in the Backside of St. Clements aforesaid and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place Says she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 27 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Lodge
