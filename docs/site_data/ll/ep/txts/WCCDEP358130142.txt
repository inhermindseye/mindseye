Middx  Ann Watts aged about 54 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Watts deceased And that her said late husband Rented a Farm in the Parish of Tottenham High cross in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Fifty Pounds & upwards & lived in the same several Years and there dyed Says her said late Husband hath been dead about 20 years and that She hath not since his decease Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 14th. Day of April 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Watts
