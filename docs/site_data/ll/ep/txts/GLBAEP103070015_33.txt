Middx ss  Ralph Lomax Maketh oath that about Thirty Years ago he was bound apprentice to his Father James Lomax of the Parish of St. Margaret Westminster in the County of Middx with whom he duly Served his apprenticeship and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlemt. in the sd Parish And this Deponent Saith that he hath Not Since gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn the 3. day of Novr 1750 before us  Richd: Ricards  Saml Gower

Ralph Lomax
