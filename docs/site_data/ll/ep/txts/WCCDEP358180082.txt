Middx  Edward Barrett aged 81 Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he was born in the Parish of Alhallows Moore in the County of Hereford that upwards of Fifty four Years since & whilst he was a Singleman he was hired to live -with Sr. William Langhorne at Charlton in the County of Kent for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds that during the time he lived with the sd. Sr. William Langhorn he Intermarried with his present Wife  Frances but notwithstanding his sd. Intermarriage continued in his sd. Service upwards of one Year & a halfe that since he left his sd. Service he never was an Apprentice Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlement in anyother Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne 4th- June 1743 before} Wm. Jones .

The Mark of Edwd: Barrett
