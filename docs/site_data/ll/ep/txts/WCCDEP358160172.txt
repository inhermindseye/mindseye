Middx  The Exaicon of Abell Bird Aged abt. 70 years Lodging in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the third day of Septr. 1740 Who upon his Oath Saith that he Rented a House in the Parish of Richmond in the County of Surry of the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds and lived in the same for the Space of Eleven years and hath been come away abt. Seventeen years And further Saith that he hath not since he left the sd. House rented Ten pounds a year pd. parish Taxes lived a yearly hired Servt. with any person for the Space of one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. many other place to his knowledged or or beleife

Sworn the day & year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Geo: Tarry

The mark of Abell Bird
