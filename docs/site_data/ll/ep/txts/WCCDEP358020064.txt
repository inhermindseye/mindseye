Saint Clement Danes

Westminster to wit James Talboys of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  Peruke maker on behalf of Eleanor Banes who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed by the said Eleanor which Information he believes to be true that she was born in Crown Court Little Russell Street in the Parish of Saint Paul & Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex that her Father Rented House in the same Court for 29 years or thereabouts that she never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or ever married

Sworn this 18th. day of March 1788 D Walker Rupr: Clarke

James Talboys
