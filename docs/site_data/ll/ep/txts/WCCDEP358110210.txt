Middx  Cathn Brown upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of John Brown (gone from her) by whom she has one Child (viz) Catherine aged about Six Years, that her sd. husband & herself are both Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England and that her sd husband informed her he was born in the Parish of St Mary in Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland and never gained a Subsequent Settlement elsewhere

Sworn the 15. March 1753 } Fran: Bedwell

Cathrine brown
