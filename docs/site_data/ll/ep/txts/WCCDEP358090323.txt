Middx to wit Jane Perry Aged about 50 Years on her Oath say's she is the Widow of John Perry who died in the Year 1762 and by whom she hath no Child or Children living say's that being a Widow & without Child or Children living she lived as a hired yearly servant with Mr. George Scrivener a Butcher in Portsmouth Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx for 8 Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of 4 Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly servant for one year

Sworn this 9th. day of July 1782 before R Butler M Addington

Jane Perry
