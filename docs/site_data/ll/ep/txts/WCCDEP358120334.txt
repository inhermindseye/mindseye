Middx  Eliz. Hall upon Oath saith she is the widow of Thomas Hall who (as she hath been informed & verily believes) Served his Apprentiship with Peter Ostler a Blacksmith at Long Melford in the County of Suffolk and never rented Ten Pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn 5th. May 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz Hall
