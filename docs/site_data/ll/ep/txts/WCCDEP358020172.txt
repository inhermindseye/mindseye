Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Talboys  Beadle of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Susannah Hunt  Singlewoman Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been credibly Informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Susannah Hunt was born in Old Gravel Lane in the Parish of Saint George in the East in the County of Middlesex and that she never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever did any Act in her own Right to gain a Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 12th. day of Novr. 1789  Tho Vaughan N Bond

James Talboys
