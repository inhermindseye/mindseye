Middx  Robt. Howard upon Oath Says he inter- -married with his Wife  Eliz: Howard at the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes abt. 25 Yrs. since by whom he hath two Children Ann aged about twelve Years & Sarah aged about 5 Months Says he Served 7 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mr. Marshall a Taylor in the Parish of Airdmagh in the County of Airdmagh in the Kingdom of Ireland & never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 14th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Rt. Howard
