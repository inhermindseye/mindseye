Middx.  Mary Briggs  Wido . upon her Oath saith that her late Husband Henry Briggs hath been Dead upwards of four Years and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and not able to maintain the same when born and that one Wm. Mussell Dealer in Coales did begett the said Child or Children on her Body she this Examinant at such Time being the said Wm Mussells  Servant the sd. Willm. Mussell had Carnall knowledge of her Body sevell. Times in the Month of March last and at several other Times since at one of which Time he gott her with the sd. Child or Childn. with which she is now Pregnant and that he the sd. Wm. Mussell and no other Person is the true & real Father thereof

Sworne the 5th. Octr. 1742 before} W. Hayton

Mary Briggs
