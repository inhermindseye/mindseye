Westmr. to wit Jane Morris Aged about forty five Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Henry Bullock in Thrift Street in the Parish of Saint Anns Soho in the Liberty of Westminster for three Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Guineas hath quitted the last mentioned Service upwards of Ten Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 8th. day of April 1772 before W Kelyngs 

her Jane Morris mark
