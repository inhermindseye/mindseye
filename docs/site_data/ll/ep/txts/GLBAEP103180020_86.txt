Middlesex ss}  David Allen maketh oath that he is by birth a Scotchman & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England or Wales That he hath a Child named William Allen aged about Ten years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his late wife  Rebecca which Child was born in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the Said County who hath done no Act to gain a Settlemt for himself & this Deponent Can give no Account respecting the Setlement of his sd wife or of her Parents or any or eithr of them.

Sworn this 5. day of Febry 1778 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of David Allen
