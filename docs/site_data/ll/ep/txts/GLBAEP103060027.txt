Middx  Ann Willcox  wife of Joseph Willcox (who is in parts beyond the Seas) Maketh Oath and Saith that her said husband several years ago was bound Apprentice by Indenture to Mr. Steel in Fleming Church Yard in the precinct of Saint Katherines in the County of Middx and served his said Master there as such about three or four years and then her said husband went to Sea and gained no Subsequent Settlemt to her knowledge.

Sworn this 9th. Novr 1744 before Robert Dennett

Ann Willcox .
