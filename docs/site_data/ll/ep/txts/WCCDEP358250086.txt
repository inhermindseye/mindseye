St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Martin in the fields (to wit) Charlotte Keyser  Singlewoman aged 19 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never lived as an yearly for a twelve month together or otherwise obtained any legal settlement in her own right that she hath been informed which she verily believes to be true that her father and Mother were not legally Married that she this Examinant was born in Duke's Court Broad Court in the parish of St. Martin's in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex sometime in or about the year eform Lord 1793

Sworn the 16th day of January 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Charlotte Keyser 's Mark

Witness Garrett

Removed by James Talboys  Beadle 16th Janry. 1793
