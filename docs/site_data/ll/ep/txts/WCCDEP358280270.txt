Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  Jane Williams aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she was born in Wales and that she never did any Act to gain any other Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Richard Howell a Clerk to Mr. Maddocks a Linnen Draper in Cheapside is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of May last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so Pregnant and that the said Richard Howell and no other person did beget the said same and there he is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 25th. day of febry. 1790 } R Butler

her Jane Williams Mark
