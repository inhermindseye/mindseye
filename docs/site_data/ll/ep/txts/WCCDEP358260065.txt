Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Israel Hewer of Kings Head Yard Wych Street upon his Oath saith that about Six years ago he Intermarried with Elizth . his late wife at the parish Church of Saint Mary at Newington by whom he hath one Child living Vizt. Mary aged about Eight Weeks That he was bound an Apprentice to one Samuel Loftus in Charlotte Street  Portland place  for Seven years that the last Three Months of his same Apprenticeship his he this Deponent lodged at a Public House in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for Seven years during which time he lodged and boarded in his Masters house in the Parish of Charlotte Street  St. Mary le bone aforesaid and that after he was not of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of July 1795 } M Addington

Israel Hewer
