Middx.  Frederick Turbefield aged abt. 36 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Catharine his Wife at the Fleet London abt. Seventeen Years ago says he served three Years and upwards the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Stratton a brazier in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and never Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Frederick Turbefield
