Middx  to wit Elizabeth Eadson Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House as paid Taxes or hired yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Christ. Eadson Rented and lived in a House in Church Street in the Parish of Newington in the County of Middlesex Upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds on thereabouts And afterwards kept another House on the other side of the Way at the Yearly Rent of Seven Pounds
