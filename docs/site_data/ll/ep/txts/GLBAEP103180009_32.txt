Middlesex ss}  Robert Corbett maketh oath That his late Parents deced have informed him & which he believes to be true that he was born in Sun Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant in any place for the Space of a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right and this Deponent Saith that he Cannot With Certainly Set forth where his Parents deced were legally Settled at the time of their respaived deaths but is induced to believe that they were then Settled in the sd parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn the 10 day of January 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Robert Corbett
