Westmr.  Ann Bunston  Wido . of Lactantius Bunston upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Lactantius Bunston at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about 6 Years since Says the sd. Lactantius Bunston lived in & rented a house in Hollis Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £18 for One Year & upwards & there dyed Says she has not since the Death of her sd. late husband rented a house of ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 24th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

Ann Burnston
