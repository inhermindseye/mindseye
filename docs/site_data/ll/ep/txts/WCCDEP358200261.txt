Middx.  Thomas Thompson aged about 29 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his Wife in the parish Church of St. Margaret Westminster about Nine Years ago by whom he hath one Child named Thomas aged upwards of four Years, says he Rented and lived in a House (known by the Sign of the Marquiss of Granby) in Villiers Street . York Buildings in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for five Months and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twenty two pounds hath Quitted same about four Years and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 25th. day of April 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant  Thos Kynaston

Thos: Thompson .
