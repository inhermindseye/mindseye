Wages £10 Mary Landon Wife of Silvanus Landon (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Silvanus Landon at the Fleet London about Ten Years ago by whom she hath one Child (viz) Mary aged upwds. of 9 Years Says her sd. husband lived a Yearly Servant whilst a Singleman with Mr. Thornhill an Apothecary in Leadenhall Street in the Parish of St. Katherine Creechurch London for one Year & upwards & quitted his sd. Service about 3 Months before his intermarriage with this Examinant Says her sd. husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 19th. March 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

Mary Landon
