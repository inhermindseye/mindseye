Middx  Ann Brown upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Barnaby Brown by whom she has a Child Rowland aged abt. 11 Years, who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. and that her sd. late husband frequently informed this Examinant he was born at Glenduse in the Shire of Galloway in Scotland and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant Rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes which information she believes to be true

Sworn April 15th. 1754 Barth: Hammond

The mark of Ann Brown
