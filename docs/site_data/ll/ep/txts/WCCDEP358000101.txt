August 25th. 1785

Middlesex 

taken and paid for (to wit) Elizabeth Hyde  singlewoman , aged Nineteen years or there abouts upon her Oath Saith That she never was married that about a fortnight ago to wit in the 10th Day of August Instant she was delivered of a female Bastard Child since Christened by the Name of Frances  and further this Examinant on her Oath Saith that James Sharpe of Clare Market Buteland and was one else is the father of the said Child having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body and legs their the same thereon and that she this Examinant being poor and unable to maintain herself in her lying in, has been best to and in the Workhouse of Saint Clement Danes and is now maintain there at the Expence of the Parish

Sworn this 25th. Augt. 1785 before R Butler

Elizbeth Hyde
