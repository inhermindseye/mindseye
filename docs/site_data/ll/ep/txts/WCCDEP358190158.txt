Middx  Judith Lawrence aged about 55 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Thos. Lawrence who has been Dead about 15 Years as she has been informed to whom she was Married at the Fleet about the Year 1722 That he was before his Intermarriage and while Single a hired Yearly Servant in the Capacity of Coachman to Mr Ward in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for about three Years that she cannot tell what Wages he had and that he intermarried with this Examinant while he lived in that Service That he never rented a House or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 18th. day of July 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Judith Lawrence
