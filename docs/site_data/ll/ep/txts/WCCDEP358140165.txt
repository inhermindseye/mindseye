Middx.  Thos. Waite aged abt. 59 Years upon Oath says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with his father Thos. Waite a Staymaker in Exeter Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and never was a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 14th. day of October 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Thos. Waite
