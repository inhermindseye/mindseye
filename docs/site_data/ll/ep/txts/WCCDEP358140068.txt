Middx.  Frances Roberts aged abt. 42 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Stephen Roberts who hath been dead abt. four Years says her said late husbd. Rented a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Twenty six Pounds lived therein abt. two Years & quitted same abt. Eight Years before his Death & never after Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says she hath not since his death Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 3d. day of Janry. 1758 before} Barth: Hammond

Frances Roberts
