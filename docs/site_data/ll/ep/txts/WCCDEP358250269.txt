Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Nicholas Sizes aged about 27 years upon his Oath saith that about Nine years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Anns Soho by whom he hath Two Children living Vizt. Henry Frederick aged about Five years and William aged about Seven weeks that he Rented and lived in a house in Dean Street Fetter Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Bars in the City of London for about Two years at the yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds that he hath quitted said house about Three years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3rd. day of Decr. 1796 . Robinson Rupt Clarke

His Nicklas Nicholas Sizes Mark
