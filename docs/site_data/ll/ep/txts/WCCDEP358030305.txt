Westmr. 

David Barber upon Oath saith that abt. 14 Years since he lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Thos. Milbourn a Tallow Chandler in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds for the Space of One Year and upwds. and hath not since he left his sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or belief.

Sworn Janry 6th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark  of David Barber .
