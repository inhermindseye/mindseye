Middx.  Letitia Dickson aged about 36 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of David Dickson who has been dead about seven Months by whom she hath one Child named David aged about three Weeks says she can give no Account of the Settlement of her said late husband other than he was born in Scotland and never gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or belief says before her marriage with the said David Dickson and whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Shepherd a Broker in Maypole Alley in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx for three Years at the Yearly Wages of four pounds Quitted that service about twelve Years ago and never after Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 5th. July 1765 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Letitia Dickson
