Middlesex to wit Phebe Major Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or ever served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. William Pleasants at the sign of the Sun in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Three Years or thereabout at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of July 1776 before}
