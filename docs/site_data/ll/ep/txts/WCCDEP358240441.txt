Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. Pish of Wimbledon  to wit John Stringer aged about Sixty Six years upon his Oath saith That he lived as a hired yearly Servant with George Bond of Wimbledon in the Parish Church of Wimbledon in the County of Surrey for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Sixteen Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Twelve Month Years ago since which time he this Deponent has not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes lived as an hired yearly Servant for One year or done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn the 17th. day of Septr. 1794 before J Hind Robt: Burd Gabriel

John Stringer
