Westmr.  Thos. Stevens aged about 34 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Fleet London about fourteen Years ago by whom he has two Children Elizabeth Charlotte aged upwards of four Years and Mary aged about three Weeks, says before his intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bartholomew Elliot at the Peacock Inn in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, ten Shillings, and after he quitted said Service never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn Jany. 21st. 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant

Thomas Stevens
