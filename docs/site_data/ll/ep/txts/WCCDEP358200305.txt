Westmr.  Richard Archdale Rooke of Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Mercer on the behalf of Mary Keen a Lunatic Upon Oath says the said Mary Keen lived a hired Yearly Servant with this Examinants Mother Esther Rooke in Church Court in the parish of Kensington in the County of Middlesex for three Years or thereabouts and hath quitted her said Service about Seven Months.

Sworn the 8th. day of October 1765 , Before  Fran Cary R Kilsha

Archdale Rooke
