Middx  Chas. Prityard aged about 90 Years upon his Oath saith his Father  John Prityard lived in a House of his own in the Parish of Cherlstock in the County of Salop and had an Estate of £60 a Year or thereabouts and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes and further Says that he this Examinant was born in Ireland but in what Parish knows not Says he never was an Apprentice Rented ten pounds a Year or was a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Febry 13th. 1744 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Chas Prityard
