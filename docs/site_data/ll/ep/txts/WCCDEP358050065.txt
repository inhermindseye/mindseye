Middx  Sarah Morgan  Widow of Thomas Morgan who dyed about twenty Years since upon Oath saith that about 18 Months since being then a Widow without Child or Children she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Steele in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds ten Shillings for two Years or thereabouts and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes as otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 30th. 1749 before}
