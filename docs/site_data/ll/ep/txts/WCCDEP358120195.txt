Middx  Hannah Shoaker aged upwards. of 60 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Phillips in Ship Yard in the parish of St Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds ten Shillings for fifteen Months quitted her sd Service abt. one year ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one Year

Sworn Septr. 18th. 1754 before}
