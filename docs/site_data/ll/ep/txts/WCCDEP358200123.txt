Westmr.  Sarah Cook Aged about Twenty Nine Years Upon Oath Says That on the Fourteenth of February 1758 she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Swallow Street in the parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Eleanora is now Living and this Examinant being poor is unable to maintain the said Child

Sworn the 7th of July 1764 Before R Kilsha Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Sarah Cook
