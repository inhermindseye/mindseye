Middlesex & Westminster to wit Mary Dax Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Davison a Taylor in Fountain Court Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath Says that on the first day of July last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Work house belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which has since been baptized Thomas and is become Chargable to the said Parish And that one James Madan late a Porter to a China Man in Thames Street is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime about the Middle of Septr. last in her said Masters House and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. And that the said James Madan and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 3d day of Augt 1778 before  Sampn. Wright

her Mary Dax Mark
