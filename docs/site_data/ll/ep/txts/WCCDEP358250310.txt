Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Brown aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Peter Brown her late Husband who has been dead about Two Years at the Parish Church of Saint Brides That her said late Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship to Mr Richardson an Upholsterer in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and that after her said late Husband was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Septr. 1797 before me} J Reeves

Elizabeth Brown
