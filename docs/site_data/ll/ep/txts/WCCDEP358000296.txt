Saint Clement Danes May 23rd 1786

Middlesex (to wit) John Cannon aged fifty four Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that about 17 years ago he Intermarried with his Present Wife  Jane at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Lambeth and by whom he hath two Children living Vizt. William aged Eleven years and Jane aged Seven years both unprovided for that he Rented and lived in a House in New Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about 10 years at the yearly Rent of £18 hath quitted said House about 7 years since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 23d. day of May 1786 R Butler

John Cannon
