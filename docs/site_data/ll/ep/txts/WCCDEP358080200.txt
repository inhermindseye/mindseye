Middx & Westmr-}  Sarah Fielder aged about twenty eight Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr- Peter Sidebotham  Attorney in Coney Street Lincolns Inn in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - upwards of fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about two Months Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain any subseqt- Settlemt- And further upon Her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Randall a Clerk to Mr- Wood in the Temple is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowl of her Body the latter and of Septr- last but the particular Day she does not now rember at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Randall and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 30th- Day of Decr- 1777 before }

her Sarah Fielder Mark
