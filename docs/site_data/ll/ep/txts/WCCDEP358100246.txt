Middx to wit John Devere Aged about Forty Five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Botolph without Bishopgate  London about 16 Years ago and by whom he has four Children living namely John Aged about Twelve Years Mary Aged about Nine Years Thomas aged about five Years and Elizabeth Aged about Six Weeks, Says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife & whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Demeire a Merchant at Amen Corner Pater Noster Row in the Parish of Christ Church Newgate London upwards of four Years at the Yearly Wages of £12 besides Diet & Lodgings hath quitted his said Service upwards of Thirteen Years ago since which time he never gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of Augt. 1784 before M Addington Edwd: Naish

Jno Devere
