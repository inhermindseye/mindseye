Westmr  Eleanor Fox  Widow of Richard Fox Deceased on the Behalf of Mary and Ann Green two Infants the Daughters of Wm. and Letitia Green both Deceased Upon Oath Says that the said Wm- Green Served Seven Years Apprentiship with a Baker in the Parish of Watlington in the County of Oxford as this Examinant hath been Informed and which Information she Verily believes to be true

Sworn the 16th of April 1764 Before}

The Mark of Eleanor Fox

Upon Mr Ralph Wms . Complt J W
