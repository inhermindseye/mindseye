Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Jackman of the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the peace in & for the sd County this Fifteenth day of March 1777 who Saith That about two months ago at the dwelling house of one John usher Situate in Angel Court in the San parish of Saint Botolph without Aligate aforesd She the sd Elizabeth Jackman was delivered of a Male Bastard child since baptired by the name of Allen Lambert and that the said child is likely to be come chargeable to the said parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that one Allen Lambert of the parish of Case horton near Croydon in the County of Surry  Millwright did get her with child of the said Bastard child

Taken and Signed the day & year above written before me  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Jackman
