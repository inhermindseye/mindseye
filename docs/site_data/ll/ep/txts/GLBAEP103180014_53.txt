Middlesex ss}  John Andrews of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Beadle maketh oath That he Knows a child now in the Workhouse of the said parish named Charles Kelley aged about Six years & five Weeks who as this Deponent has been Credibly informed & believes was born in the Parish of Saint George in the said County & this Depont. is the better induced to believe the Same to be true for that upon his inspecting the Register of Baptisms of the said parish of St George & by a Certificate of the Reverend Mr Colby Curate thereof it manifestly appears to this Deponent That one Charles Son of Charles Kelley  Mariner by Mary his wife was baptized there January the Twelfth one thousand Seven hundred & Saventy two being than Thirty two days old and this Deponent Cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of the said Childs Parents or eithr of them

Sworn this 21 day of January 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey P. Rowlands

John Andrews
