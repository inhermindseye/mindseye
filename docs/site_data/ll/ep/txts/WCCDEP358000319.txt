Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Mackenzie aged  50 years & upwards a Widow upon her Oath Saith that since the Decease of her Husband and having no Child or Children living she went to live as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Hawkins of Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that she Continued in her said Service for  One year at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about two years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of July 1786 before  Wm Kitchiner

Jane Mekensey
