Middx to wit Elizabeth Howell Aged about Forty Years on her Oath says She is the Wife of Richard Howell gone from her with whom She Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Brides in the City of London about Seven years ago and by whom She hath no Children having says her Husband Richard Howell Rented and lived in a House in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for four Years at the yearly Rent of Thirty Six Pounds and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Nine Months ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Janry. 1781 before  Sampn Wright

her Elizth. Howell Mark
