James Whitby about 4 years 8 Months old

St Clement Danes June 30th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  James a Whitby  born Novr/89 in Wedlock}  Child of Risborn & Mary Whitby  Settlement in Columpton Devonshire in right of his fathr}  Sworn  (to wit) Elizabeth Poolman  singlewoman on behalf of James Whitby a Poor Child (which was left with her this Examinant by the Mother, who hath desorted is) Upon Oath that she hath been informed which she Verily believes to be true that the said Child named James Whitby was born sometime in or about the Month of November 1789, in lawful Wedlock, at the lying in Hospital Brownlow Street  Long Acre that he is the legitimate Child of Risbon and Mary Whiltey that they were  Married at the Church of the Parish of Shipton Mallet in the County of Somerset on or about the 29th day of November 1788 1788 that the said Risbon Whitby obtained his settlement in the Parish of Columpton in the County of Devon by serving his Apprenticeship with his Father Thomas Whitby a Sargemaker in that parish And that she known not by information or otherwise of any other Settlement the is in said Risbon Whitby is entitled into unto

Sworn the 30th day of June 1794 before us M Addington J: Hind

Elizth P Poolman's Mark

Witness

Removed Talboy July1794
