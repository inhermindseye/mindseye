Middlesex ss}  Mary Reeve the Wife of Samuel Reeve maketh oath That She well knows a Child named Ann Boyd aged about five years & half & is now resident in the Workhouse belonging to the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Chargeable to the Sd parish The which Child was born as this Depont has been informed & verily believes in the house of one Mr Crockatt in the Parish of St John Wapping in the Sd County & this Depont Can give no other Account respecting the Said Chiles Settlement nor Can She give any Account respecting the Settlement of the Sd Chiles Parents Wm & Jane Boyd or either of them

Sworn this 23. day of January 1777 before Jasper Clarkes Parkr Rowlands

Mary Reeve
