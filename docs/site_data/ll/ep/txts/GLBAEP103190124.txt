Middlesex ss}  Mary Powell the Wife of Thomas Powell maketh oath That her Said husband is now absent from her in his Majesties Service on board the Royal Oak That he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly Served his Apprenticeship with one William George a Carpenter & Joiner in Reading in the County of Berks & has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief That She was lawfully married to her Sd husband by whom She hath a Child & lawful Issue now living named Mary Aged Ten Years

Sworn this. 5. day of July 1780 before}

The Mark of Mary Powell
