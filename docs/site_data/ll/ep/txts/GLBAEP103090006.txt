Middlesex ss  Clementinah Wheeler the Wife of Robert Wheeler maketh oath that she hath been informed by her Said husband That divers Years ago and when a Singleman he lived as a hired servant by the Year at Certain Yearly Wages with Thomas Lane of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Esquire and one of the Masters of his Majesties high Court of Chancery and that he Continued in such service for above the space of a year & by means thereof gained a legal settlement in the Said Parish the which Information this Deponent believes to be true And this Deponent Saith that her said husband since such his servitude as aforesaid hath not lived as a hired servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor served any Parish offices nor Paid any Poors rates or kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement to the knowledge information or belief of this Deponent And this Deponent farther Saith that She hath a Child named William Aged about Eleven Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband And that her Said husband is lately gone away from her to Places Unknown to her

Sworn this 23d day of July 1765 before us  Chris: Scott R Pell

Clementina Wheeler
