Middx  Mary Clark  Wido . of John Clark upon Oath saith she can give no Accot. of her sd. late husbands Settlemt. & that she was born at Edgmint near Sheowsbury in the County of Salop where her father had a house & Ground of his own & that she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge

Sworn Augt. 8th 1751 before Fran Redwell

The Mark of Mary Clark
