Middx & Westmr}  Ann Ware aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was Married And that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Faulkner a Milliner in Piccadilly in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Months and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived as a hired Yearly Servant with any other Person

Sworn this 11th. day of May 1769 before Us R Butler W Kelyngs

her Ann Ware Mark Wittness Fras Bishop
