Sarah Downes lives at No. 9 R Bell Court Grays Tom Lane }

Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  taken and Security given to wit Sarah Downes aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith she never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Anderson who kept the five Bells in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 14 Months and upwd. at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted said Service about 3 years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same where born And further saith that one Richard Long who lives as a Servant with one Mr. Hill an Altorney at No.15 Coney Court Grays Inn is the Father of the said such Child or Children he having had Carnal knowledge of the Examinants Body sometime in the Month of March last and at sevl difft. times afterwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Richard Long and no other Person did be yet the same and that he is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 17th day of Augt. 1785  Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Downs
