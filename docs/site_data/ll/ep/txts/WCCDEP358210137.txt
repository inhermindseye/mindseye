Westmr.  Margaret Deveil aged about 48 Years

James Betts aged about 48 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with his Wife Sarah in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on the 17th. day of February 1767. says he served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. William Boughton a Blacksmith in the Parish of Morden in the County of Bedford and never Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year and further upon his Oath says the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish of Morden granted a Certificate to the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Pullocks hill in the said County of Bedford acknowledging him to be an Inhabitant legally settled in the said Parish of Morden .

Sworn the 19th. day of February 1767 before} Paul Vaillant

James Betts
