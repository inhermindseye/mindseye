Westmr.  Mary Dodd Aged upwards of 19 Years on her Oath says she never was married rented house paid taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship says she lived a hirely Yearly Servant with one Mr. Calvert a Cheesemonger in Fleet Market in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn London upwards of 14 Months at the Yearly Wages of St hath quitted her said Service above 9 Months ago since which time she never rented house paid taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. Day of April 1776 before  R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Dodd Mark
