St. Clement Danes 29th Febry. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Thomas Cole Aged Thirty Six Years & upwards Upon Oath saith That he intermarried with his present Wife Sarah formerly Sarah Coombs Spuister at Walcot Church Bath about Eleven Years ago, that since their said Marriage he this Deponent Rented & Resided in a House in Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for about Six Months at the Yearly Rent of £15 that he quitted that House about three Years ago, and since hath not obtained any other legal Settlement That he hath two Children now living by his said Wife Namely Frances Aged Nine Years and Nine Months & upwards and Susanna who was Aged Six Year on the 7th. September last And that he is at present incapable to provide for his said Wife & Children without Assistance

Sworn the 29th. day of Febry 1792 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Thos. Cole
