Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Elizabeth  Glenn  Singlewoman Aged 27 years or thereabouts on behalf of John Jenkins Aged 37 years & upwards now very Ill upon Oath saith That she hath been Informed by him the said John Jenkins that he is of the Age of 37 years and upwards that while he was single and unmarried he  was Articled to Mr. Boyden Haw Daeper King Street in the parish of St Margaret Westminster for 7 years and secured his said Master the whole Term that the last forty days of his said Service he lodged in the Broad Sanctuary in the said parish of St Margaret and hath since done  no Act whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlemt. and that he is sick Poor and unable to maintain himself

Sworn the 1st. Novr 1791 before us }

Elizabeth Glenn
