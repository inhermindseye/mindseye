Middlesex ss}  Mary Skinner  Singlewoman maketh oath that She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents or eithr of them That She this Depont was born in Swan Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County and never was bound Apprentice nor ever lives as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right That She hath a child named John Moses aged about four years & three months which was born a Bastari in Swan Alley a foresd & that one John Moses of the same place Cabinet maker is the Father of the said child.

Sworn this 25 day of February 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Skinner
