Middx.  Mary Bland aged abt. 30 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Walter Bland (new at Sea) With where she intermarried at the fleet London abt. Nine yrs. ago & by whom she hath one Child (to wit) Saml. aged abt. three Yrs. says her sd. husband Rented a house of fourteen Pounds a Yrs. in Ship yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Libty of Westmr . & County of Middx. lived therein abt. six Weeks & paid half a Quarters Rent to one Mr. Harrison of Fetter Lane Bricklayer & hath quitted sd house abt. Eight yrs. since which time he hath not Rented ten Pounds a yr. or paid Parish Taxes to his Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 20th. of July 1757 before} Mary Bland
