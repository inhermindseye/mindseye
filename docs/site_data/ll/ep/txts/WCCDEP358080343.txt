Middx to wit Hannah Mansfield now Hannah Nichols (on behalf of Ann Mansfield her daughter) aged about fifty five years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late husband Nicholas Mansfield at the Parish Church of Saint George Bloomsbury about Twenty five Years ago and by whom she had the above named Ann Mansfield aged about Nineteen Years says her said late Husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England other than by Renting a House in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for three Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Nine Pounds but paid no Parish Taxes due for the same and this Examinant further says that her said Daughter was born in the aforesaid House and never gained any other Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or Belief and further says that before her Intermarriage with her said late Husband Nicholas Mansfield she lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Mrs. Toderick a Tea Shop in Great Russell Street in the aforesaid Parish of Bloomsbury upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and that she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement until she Married to her last Husband John Nichols deceased

Sworn this 19th. day of March 1779 before  Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

her Hannah Nichols Mark
