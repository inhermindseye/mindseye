Middx  Edwd. Andrews upon Oath saith he Served 7 yrs. Apprentiship with Mr Ganderton a Glazier in little Essex Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex has been out of his time near 7 years and never rented ten pounds by the year paid Parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn July 23d 1755 before}

The mark of Edwd. Andrews
