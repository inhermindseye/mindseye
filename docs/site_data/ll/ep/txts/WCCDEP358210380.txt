Middx.  Elizth. Thomas Aged about 26 Years on her Voluntary Oath says she never was married And that on the 18 Day of Febry. last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Richd. Hollingworth the Cor- ner of white Horse Yard Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized by the Name of John says that one Robt. Green a Hatter in Holywell Street is the father of the sd. Male Bastard Child he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at a House in Cold Bath fields where an acquaintance of this Examinant Lodged some time in the Month of June 1768 but the particular Day this Examinant cannot Ascertain And says that the Male Bastard Child of Which she was deliver- ed as afsd. the said Robt. Green and no other Person in the true and real father thereof

Sworn this 2d. day of May 1769 before me} Tho Kynaston

her Elizth. Thomas Mark Witness Petr Sidebotham
