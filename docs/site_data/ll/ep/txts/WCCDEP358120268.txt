Randall Carter upon Oath saith he served the two last years his Apprentiship with Mr. Geo Sunderland a Taylor in the Parish of Allin in the County of Chester  and that he never rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Janry 22d. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Randal Carter
