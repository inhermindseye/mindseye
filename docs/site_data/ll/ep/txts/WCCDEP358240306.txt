St Clement Danes Augst. 22d 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St C D  One Child  (to wit) Ann Cheevers Wife of George Cheevers & aged twenty three years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband George Cheevers at the parish Church of St Clement Danes  Middlesex on the 14th February in the year 1790 (by Banns) that previous thereto and while he was single & unmarried he served his Apprenticeship with Mess Broughton & Twiss Fishmonger Gilberts passage Clare Market and Boarded & Lodged at with Mr Broughton's in Gilberts passage aforesaid that sin since the Expiration of his Apprenticeship (which expired about 4 or 5 years ago) she he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge Information or belief that she hath one Child now living with her by her said Husband Namely George aged the one year And on the 29th day of July last that her said Husband left her on Monday last & entered to in his Majesty's

Navy Service & that she is distressed and unable to maintain herself & Child without assistance

Sworn the 22d day of August 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Ann Cheevers 's Mark

Witness Gyanett

(lodges at No19 Mr. Hills Holles Street
