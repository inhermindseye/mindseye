Westmr.  Eliza. Tomlinson aged about 19 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was never marryed Says she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the 3d. Day of Novr. last in the house of Mrs. Richard in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Wetstmr. which is as yet unbaptized Says one Edward Collins an Apprentice to Mr. Nelson an Apothecary in Drake Street by Red Lyon Square did beget the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body this Examinant being a Servant to the said Mr. Nelson the said Edward Collins by that means became Acquainted with her & had Carnal knowledge of her Body the latter End of the Month of February last or the beginning of March last and at sevll. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child which she was Delivered of as aforesaid and that he the said Edward Collins & no other Person is the true & real father thereof Says before she lived with the sd. Mr. Nelson she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. she was born in the Parish of Newark upon Treat in the County of Nottingham where her father now lives in & rents a house & pays his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says she never rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 14th. 1747 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Eliza. Tomlinson
