Westmr. 

Christr. Russell upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife Ann in the Parish Church of St Margaret Westmr. upwards of 30 Years ago by whom he hath two Children now at home with him (viz) Ann aged abt. 18 Yrs. and Jane aged about 11 Years neither of whom ever were Apprentices or Yearly Servants for one Year Says he lived in and rented a house in Theobald Row near Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St George in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Fifteen pounds for Six months or thereabouts and quitted the same about Seven Years ago since which time he has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 7th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

Chrisr: Russell
