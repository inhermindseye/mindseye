Westmr.  Sarah Lambeth Aged about Thirty one Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Lambeth who has been dead about Four Years; Says her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Wm: Robinson of the Inner Temple , Attorney , for Five Years; and after he served Four Years or thereabouts of his said Appren- -ticeship he intermarried with this Examinant, says her said Husband during the time he served said Apprenticeship Boarded and lay in the House of Mr. Peter Ross opposite the Dolphin in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St. George the Martyr by Queen Square in the County of Middlesex ; Says her said Husband never Rented House or paid Taxes neither hath she since her said Husband's Death Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 14th, day of July 1768 , before W Kelynge

Sarah Lambeth
