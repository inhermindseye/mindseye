Middx The Examination of Rebecca Brown  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 20th. Day of Decr. 1739 Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnt. with Child or Childn. wch when Born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when Born And further upon her Oath saith yt. one Thos. Shewbrick  Comander of the Mary Ann bound from South Carolina for England did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body she this Examint. at such time being a Passanger in the said Ship & there the sd. Thos. Shewbrick had Carnall Knowledge of her Body Sevll. times at one of wch times he got her with the Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now Pregnant and that he & no other Person is the true and reall Father thereof

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The RC Mark of Rebecca Brown
