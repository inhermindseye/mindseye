Middx   Catherine Messenger aged about 40 years removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx by Order dated the 17th. day of February instant upon Oath says she is the Wife of Robert Messenger a Soldier now in Germany That he rented a house of £16 a year next the Crooked Billet in little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr: and lived therein upwards of Three Months Says he hath not rented any House or paid Land Tax or Poor Rate since he quitted the Same which is about Ten Years ago

Sworn the 20th. day of Febry 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond

Cetren Messenger
