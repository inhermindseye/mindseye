Middx.  Mary Clarke aged abt. 69 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Clarke who hath been dead abt. ten Years says her said late husband was a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or belief says before her Intermarriage with the said John Clarke & whilst she was a single Woman she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with one David Wilkinson a Butcher in Hollis Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for 2 Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn the 5th. of May 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Clarke
