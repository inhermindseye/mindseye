Jones  SingleWoman aged 60 years or thereabouts upon Oath saith that this Examinant never was Married & that this Examinant lived as an Hired yearly Servant with Mr Goodchild, no. 2 Jooley Street in the Parish of St. Olaves in the County of Surrey for one year & upwards at the Annual Wages of £7. Per Ann. that this Examinant Quitted the Said Service several years back and this Examinant hath not since lived as an Hired yearly yearly Servant for one year Together under one Hiring, or done any thing other Act, matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legals Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge, information or belief

Sworn the 21 Day April 1795 }
