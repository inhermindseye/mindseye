Middx ss  Sarah Leggatt  Singlewoman maketh oath that about one year and ten Months ago she lived as a hired servant by the year at the Wages of five pounds a year with one Mr. David Thomas who lived at the sign of the Cock the Corner of Sherborn Lane in the parish of St. Mary Woolnoth in the City of London with whom she lived one year and five Months And saith that she was never Married Nor hath gained any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 29th day of August 1754 before  Richd Ricards  Chris. Scott

his Sarah Leggatt  Mark
