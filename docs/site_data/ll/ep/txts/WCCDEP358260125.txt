Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Lock aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that about 14 years ago she Intermarried with Richard Lock her late Husband who has been dead about Twelve Months at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster That her said late Husband previous to his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Miss Whitmore at Barnes in the Parish of Barnes in the County of Surry for Twelve months but what wages he had she this Deponent doth not know and that after he quitted said Service he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or did any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of April 1796 J lend Rusl: Clarke

Sarah Lock
