Middlesex ss}  Isabella Simpson the Wife of John Simpson Maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majesties Ship the Ramalies in his Majesties Service that he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he Served his apprenticeship with one Taylor who was a Taylor in the Parish of Saint James in the City of Bristol & has not Since done any Act to gain anr Settlement to her knowledge or belief & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband by whom She hath a Child & lawful Issue now living named Mary aged about Sixteen Weeks

Sworn this 28 day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Isabella Simpson
