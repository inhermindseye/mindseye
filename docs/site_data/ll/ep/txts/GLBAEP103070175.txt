Middx ss  John Hardy Maketh oath that about Twenty years ago he Rented a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middx and paid fifteen pounds a Year Rent for the same & parish Taxes And by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt there And saith that he has not since gained any subsequent settlement having never since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards Nor served any Parish offices Nor done any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlement And that he hath a Wife Named Sarah

Sworn this 12th day of January 1757 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

John Hardy
