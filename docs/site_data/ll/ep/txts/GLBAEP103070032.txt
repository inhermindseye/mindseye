Middx ss  Mary Smith the Widow of John Smith deced Maketh oath that hersd. late husband has often informed this Depont that he was bound apprentice by Indre to James Charlton of Blosom Street in the Liberty of Norton Falgate in the county of Middx Worsted Weaver And that he lived with his sd Master two Years in thesd. Liberty of Norton Falgate & then his sd. Master removed into half Moon Alley in Bishopsgate Street in the parish of St. Botolph witht Bishopsgate London and there Served three Years Moore with his sd. Master And that during the last eleven Months of his apprenticeship this Deponents sd. husband (as he informed this Deponent) lived with one Mrs. Walker in Ball Alley in the parish of st Leonard Shoreditch and paid his Master Six pence by the Week for ye Space of Six Months which information this Deponent believes to be true By Means whereof this Deponent sd. Husband (as she is informed) gained a legal Settlemt. in ye sd. parish of st. Leonard Shoreditch And this Depont. farther Saith that to her knowledge information or belief hersd. husband Never rented a house of Ten pounds by ye Year upwards or ever paid any Parochial Taxes or was ever a hired Servant by the Year And this Deponent Saith that she hath two Children

to Wit Elizabeth aged about ten Years and Mary Aged about five Years the lawfull issue of this Dept. by hersd. late husband.

Sworn this 10th day of June 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  Saml Gower

his Mary Smith Mark
