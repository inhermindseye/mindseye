Westmr. to wit John Gilbert Aged about Sixty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Sarah his Now Wife at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes on the 23d. day of Decr. 1770 Says he Rented and lived in a House at Hatton Wall in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Years to the best of his Remembrance at the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about 27 Years ago and after quitting the same he never Rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 24th. day of Decr- 1770 before me}
