Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex Westminster  to Wit to wit Sarah Mountain aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or ever bound an apprentice that her Father served his time as an apprentice to one Mr. Phillips a Brazier in Maid Lane in the Parish of Saint Saviour Southwark that he also Rented a house in Maid Lane in the said Parish and Paid the Kings Tax for the same and that he never did any act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of July 1790  Sampr Wright  Edwd: Naish

her Sarah Mountain  Mark
