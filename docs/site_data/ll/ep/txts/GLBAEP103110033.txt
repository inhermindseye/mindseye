Middlesex ss}  Ann Amery the Widow of Richard Amery deced Maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the Year for the Space of two Years & upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds with one William Jesser  Slop Seller in the Parish of Saint Mary Hill in the City of London & was Widow during Such her Servitude & had no Children then living and this Deponts. Saith that She hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29 day of November 1771 before Wilmot B Camper

Ann Amery
