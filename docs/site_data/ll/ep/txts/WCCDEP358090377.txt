Middx  Mary Lockhart Aged about 46 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband John Lockhart at the Parish Church of Christ Church in the City of Edinburgh  Eighteen Years ago Come the 21st. of May next And by whom she hath three Children living namely Margt . Aged about Fifteen Years Ann Aged Eleven Years & James Aged upwards of Two Years Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Jonathan Pettit at the Barley Mow in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County for 4 Years & upwards at the yearly Wages of Five pounds that her sd. Husband went down to Scotland where he married to this Examinant and lived with him about five years that her Husband afterwards Came to London and enlisted into the 3d. Regimt. of Guards & was in the Regimt. about five years & was then discharged & had Pension from Chelsea Hospital That afterwds. her sd. Husband enlisted again into the same Regimt. about Two Years & was again discharged from thence And after being a 2d. time Discharged was sent down to Shireness as an Invalid and where he now at present is And further says she can give no further or otherwise Acct. of her sd. Husbands Settlement than as aforesaid.

Sworn this 4th. day of March 1783 before me  Wm Kitchiner

her Margt Lockhart  Mark
