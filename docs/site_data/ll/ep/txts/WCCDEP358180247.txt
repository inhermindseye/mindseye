Middx  Sarah Scatliffe aged about 57 Years upon her Oath saith that she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Kilby in Fountain Court in the Parish of St. Clemt: Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Six Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & hath left her said Service about Seven Years since which time she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 3d. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Sarah Scatliffe
