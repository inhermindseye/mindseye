St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminister}  Margaret Dowlin aged 27 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married - That she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Foster of Richmond Buildings in the Parish of Saint Ann John in the Liberty of Westminster for chwards of Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of 6 Guineas that she quitted her said Service abt. 18 Months ago since which time she has not Done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 17th. day of May 1786 before us  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordon

her Margt. Dowlin Mark
