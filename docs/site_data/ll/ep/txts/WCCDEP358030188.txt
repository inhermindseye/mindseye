Middx  Jennett Hattesley on Behalf of Mary Forsythe aged upwards of 19 Years upon her Oath saith the sd. Mary Forsythe was born in the parish of Steven in Badenoch in the Shire of Inverness in Scotland as she hath been informed & beleives Says the sd. Mary Forsythe never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 19th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Forsythe
