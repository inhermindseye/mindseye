Westmr.  Jane West on Behalf of Ann Appleby aged about 16 Years Daughter of William Appleby by Frances his Wife both deceased upon Oath saith she has been informed & beleives that the said Wm. & Frances Appleby intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Ann Westmr. and that the sd. Wm. Appleby Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Mims a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to their Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry. 1st. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Jane West
