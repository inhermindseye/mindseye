Middlesex  Robert Noble upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to & served Six Months the first part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Jno. Exley near the new Church in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes Cordwainer & then his sd. Master broke that he never was Assigned over to any other person Rented Ten pounds Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne 20th. Janry 1743 before} Wm: Jones

Robt Noble
