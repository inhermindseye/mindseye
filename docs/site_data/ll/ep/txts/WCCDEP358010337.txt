Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jesse Richardson aged about 46 years upon his Oath saith that he served seven years Apprenticeship to one Thomas shirley a Lapidary who lived in St. Altmans Church Yard Derby in the Parish of Saint Altman in the County of Derby and that after he was out of his said apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes ever lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or done any act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of May 1790  Sampr Wright N Bonds

The of Jesse Richardson Mark
