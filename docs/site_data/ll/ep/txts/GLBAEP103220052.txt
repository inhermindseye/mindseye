Middlesex ss  Richard Wilkinson maketh oath that he lately rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas in New Gravel Lane in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell in the Sd County & resided thereon for the Space of one Year & three quarters That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 5. day of July 1786 before J. Hawley R Davies

The Mark of Richard Wilkinson
