Middx.  Elishe Oakley on the Behalf of William Tortorshell an Infant aged abt. three Years upon Oath says that her Son William Tortorshell the father of the said Infant has absconded for Debt and where he is this Examinant cannot tell; Says the said Wm. Tortorshell the father served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Baverick a Watch maker in Clements Lane in Lombard Street in the Parish of St. Edmund the King in the City of London and hath been out of his said Apprenticeship about Ten Years and never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one year.

Sworn 20th. Augst. 1759 . before} Tho Lane  Barth: Hammond

Elishe Oakley

Upon the Complaint of Mr Joseph Burnthwaite Overseer J: P:
