Middx & Westmr.}  Hannah Cake aged about Thirty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was married And that she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. West Attorney in that part of Shire Lane which is in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Seven Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 29th. day of June 1769 before Us W Kelyngs Jno: Wright

The mark of Hannah Cake

Witness Petr Sidebotham
