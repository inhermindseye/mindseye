Middx.  Sarah Archer aged abt. Fifty four Years lodging in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath says she is the Wife of Richd. Archer with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West London upwards of thirty Years since says her said husband Rented a house in the Strand in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for several Years at the Yearly rent of Twenty Pounds & hath quitted said house abt. twelve Months says he hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn this 30th of Augst. 1757 before} Barth: Hammond Sarah Archer
