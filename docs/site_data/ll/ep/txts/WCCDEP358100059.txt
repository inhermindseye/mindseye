Middlesex & Westmr .} to wit Elizth. Dunn aged Sixty Nine Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband deceased at May fair Chaple in the year 1751 and by whom she had sevl. Children who are all since dead Says her sd. late Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Hancock a Bricklayer in Petty France in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr. and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Tenement or Lodgging of £10. a Year Paid Taxes or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husbands Death

Sworn this 8th. day of April 1783 R Butler W Addington
