Westmr. 

Mary King aged about Years upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. John Hill a Watchmaker in Little East Street near the Seven Dials in the Parish of St. Giles in the feilds in the  County of Middx at the Yearly wages of £4 for the Space of one Year & upwards & hath quitted her sd. Service about upwds. of 2 months and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a female Bastd. Child on the 4th. Day of January Instant at Mr. Lyes in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized Elizabeth & is likely to become Chargeable to the sd.
