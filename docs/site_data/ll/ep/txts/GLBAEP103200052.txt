Middlesex ss}  Jane Poor  Singlewoman otherwise Jane Hall maketh oath that she hath a son named William Poor who was born of this Deponts Body at the house known by the Sign of the Twelve Bells in St Saviours Church yard in the Parish of St Saviour in Southwarke in the County of Surrey which son is aged about Nineteen Years is an illegitimate Child & a Bastard by reason that this Depont was never Married to his Father & which Child to this Deponts Knowledge or belief never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived a hired servant for the Space of a Year nor ever did any Act to gain a settlemt for himself

Sworn this 24th day of Septemr 1783 R Davies J Hawley

The Mark of Jane Poor
