Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Morgan aged about 42 years upon her Oath saith That about Twenty years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Morgan her late Husband who has been dead about Ten years at the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry That on or about the 26th. day of May 1795 she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Wynn in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the name of William That one William Wynn a Taylor who lives at No. 2 Staples Inn Buildings the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Williams Wynn is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of April 1798 before me } J Hunter

Susanah Morgan
