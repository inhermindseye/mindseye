St Clement Danes 4th Febry. 1794

Middx and Westminster }  St Clemt. Danes Maiden Settlemt  (to wit) Sarah Yates aged about Fifty nine years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to George Yates at the parish Church of St John Westminster about 41 years ago (by Banns) that her said Husband died about 14 years ago that she this Examinant hath been informed and verily believes that her said Husband was a native of Ireland and saith that he never obtained any legal settlement in this Kingdom to the best of her knowledge information or belief that she this Examinant hath not since his death been Married or obtained any legal settlement in her own Right that previous to her said Marriage with the said George and while she was single and unmarried she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs Rixon in Howard Street in the parish of St Clement Danes for about two years and an half at the Annual  Wages of £4 for the first year and £5 Yearly afterwds. that she quitted that place about a Quarter of a Year before her Marr Marriage with the said George and she is now distressed and unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn the 4th day of Febry. 1794 before me Wm Kitchiner }

Sarah Yeates 's Mark

Witness B. Newman
