Middx  Joseph Muskett aged about Twenty Eight Years passed from the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx by Order bearing Date the 4th. Day of January last upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with his now Wife  Eleanor on the 27th. Day of May 1736 at a Church in little Britain & that before his Intermarriage with the Sd. Eleanor & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Jno. Chockley at the White Hart in St. John Street for One Year & a half at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and further saith that after he left the said Mr. Chockley Service he never lived with any other Person whilst a Singleman as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year but after his said Intermarriage with Eleanor his non Wife he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Gladhill at the Crooked Billett in Witch Strt . in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the

Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & had at that Time One Child by his said Wife. Viz: William aged about Six Months since Deced that this Examinant never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 23d. Day of February 1741 before me} Tho. Lane .

Joseph Muskett
