Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Thornton aged about 66 Years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 8th. day of January 1769 she Intermarried with Thomas Thornton her late Husband who has been dead upwards of Twenty years at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch That her said late Husband Served his Apprenticeship to a Mr. Scott a Taylor the corner of Katherine Convit Tower hill in the Parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of April 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

her Mary Thornton mark
