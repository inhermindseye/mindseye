Middx.  Cathne: Lucas aged abt. 58 Yrs. upon, Oath says She is the Widow of Abraham Lucas who hath been dead abt. Twelve Yrs. says her said late Husband Rented a House opposite St. Clemts. Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx . of the Yearly Rent of Thirty five Pounds lived therein upwds. of three Years & quitted same abt. Six Months before his Death says her said late Husband never after Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. or paid Parish Taxes neither hath she since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or Intermarried with any other Person

Sworn Febry. 12th. 1757 before}
