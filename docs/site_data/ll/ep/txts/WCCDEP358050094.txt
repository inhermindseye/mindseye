Westmr. Eliz Barkwith wido. of Edwd. Barkwith who dyed on the 26th. Day of October last upon Oath saith she intermarried with her said late husband Edwd Barkwith in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn London on the 23d. Day of June 1728 by whom she hath one Child now at home with her Ann aged upwds. of Six Years Says the said Edward Barkwith was bound Apprentice by Indenture dated the 24th. Day of June 1709 to Mr. James Steward of the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr Taylor and never after his sd. Apprentiship rented a house of Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 31st. before H Fielding

The mark of Eliz. Barkwith
