Chays. a Child of wch she pregnant to one Her said. of Fulham of she dd. or if man ever taken. taken & 20th pd, to wit Mary Benbough aged 19 years or thereabts upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lives at this present time with Mrs. Davisin Cumberland Cour t for her Victuals and Lodging and has for 5 Weeks past that about 3 years ago she went to live with the said Mrs. Davis as a hired yearly Servant at the yearly wages of four Pounds and continued to live with her at that time for 14 Months and upwards hath quitted said Service about a year and an half since which time she has been in Lodgings in Browns Buildings and Nags Head Court and no where else And further on her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And further saith that one John Howard a Taylor who lived with her in Nagshead Court and who now works for one Mr. Griffiths a Taylor Fulham is the father of the said Child

or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinant Body several different times at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Howard and no other Person did be get the same and that he is the Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th- day of Octr. 1785 R Butler

Mary Benbow
