Middx.  Mary Grantham aged abt. 47 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Thos. Grantham (now at Sea) with whom she Intermarried at the fleet London abt. 27 Years ago says her said husband served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years to one Mr. Pearce a Shoemaker in St. Martin's Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. & never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 9th day of March 1758 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Grantham
