Middx.  Eleanor Elizabeth Edwards on the behalf of Wm. Edwards her husband upon Oath says that the sd. Wm. Edwards is now a Lunatic that she intermarried with the said Willm. Edwards upwards of twelve years since & hath three Children by him (to wit) Mary aged abt. twelve yrs. Margt. aged abt. seven years & Alice aged upwards of two Yrs. says her said husband hath often times when he was in his Senses informed her & which information she verily believes to be true that he was born in the Parish of Pwllheli in the County of Carnarvon & served his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Morgan a Shoemaker in the same Parish

Sworn this 18th day of July 1757 before me} Barth Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Edwards
