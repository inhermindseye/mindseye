Middlesex ss  James Donaldson  a Singleman maketh oath That he never was informed or Can he in any manner set forth the Place or Places of settlement of his late Parents or either of them But this Depont saith that he has been informed by his late Mother deced and also by his sister that he this Deponent was born in College Yard in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex about thirty Years ago The which information this Deponent verily believes to be true And this Depont farther Saith that he was never bound an Apprentice or ever lived as a hired servt. for a year or ever served any Parish offices or Paid any Poors rates or kings Taxes or rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards or ever did any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in his own right.

Sworn this 10th. day of October 1766 before  Chris: Scott

James Donaldson his Mark
