Middx  Eleanor Fox Upon Oath she is the Widow of Richd. Fox who was a Native of Ireland that she this Examinant is a Native of Ireland says she has two Children by the said Richd. Fox Richd Aged upwards of Twelve Years and Eleanor Aged upwards of Ten Years both born in little Russell Street in the Parish of St Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middx

Sworn Janry: 18th. 1763 before me} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eleanor Fox
