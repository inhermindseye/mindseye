St. Clemt. Danes .

Margaret Halfpenny aged 21 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she Never was Married, that she is a Native of Ireland and never gain any settlement in England and this Examinant further upon her Oath saith That she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child on the 1st. day of July 1788 at the a House of in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Margaret That one Edward Aldridge a Cook who did live at St Clemt. Coffee House in St. Clemts. Church Yard is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body & begotten the same thereon and that the St. Edward Aldridge & one other Person did beget the same and that her she only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of Novr. 1789 before} R Butler

her Margt. Halfpenny Mark

Edwd. Aldridge . is a tall thin Man large Nose. thick Lips Pale Complexion wears his own Hair in a Club light Colour'd Hair power'd abt. 23 Yrs. generally wears black & a Green Suitour Coat
