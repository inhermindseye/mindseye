Middx  John Albone Aged about 34 Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Ruth in the Parish Church of Kanesum in the County of Somerset about Ten Years ago Says he Lived a Hired Yearly Sert . whilst a Singleman with Mr. Cavanagh at the Ship & Star in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for 18 Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about Twelve Years & hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 9th of May 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of John Albone

Upon Mr Oliver Complt J. W
