St Clement Danes 5th September 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  William Coates  singleman aged 40 years or therebouts Upon Oath saith that she or he never was Married that he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Gittor in the St Clement Danes  Strand for near two years or thereabouts at the Annual Wages of £10 for the first year and twelve pounds for the second year that he quitted that Service about fourteen or fifteen years ago & hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 5th day of September 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Wm Coats
