Middx  Hannah Shepherd upon Oath saith she was married to her present husband Zechariah Shephard at Mayfair Chapel on the 18th. Novr. 1751 and that the said Zechariah Shephard Lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Hudson a Peruke maker in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for Six Years & upwards and afterwards Rented a house in Milford Lane in the same Parish of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwds. and lived therein upwds. of Eight or Nine Months as he informed this Examinant & which she verily believes And further that at the time of her intermarriage with the above mentioned Zech. Shephard she was the Wido. of Richd. Haynes who dyed abt. two Yrs. ago and by whom she hath two Childn. now with her (viz) Thos . aged upwds. of nine Years and Edward aged about Six Years and that her sd. late husbd.

Richd Haynes rented 2 houses in Horn Court Hollywell Strt. in the afsd. Parish of St. Clement Danes  and lived therein sevl. Years & there dyed and this Examint. continued to live in & Rent the sd. house near one year after his Decease

Sworn March 13th. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Hanah Shepherd
