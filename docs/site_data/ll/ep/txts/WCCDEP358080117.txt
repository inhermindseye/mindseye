Middlesex & Westmr}  Charles Nicholson Mandell Aged about forty Nine Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife (who is now in Saint Georges Workhouse ) at the Fleet London about Thirty Years Ago and by whom he hath one Child living now provided for Says He Rented and lived in a House near Chelsea Bridge in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex for four years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds and paid parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Sixteen Years ago since. which time He never Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 7th. day of Feb. 1777 before  John Cox  Wm. Kitchiner

Charles Nicholson Mandell
