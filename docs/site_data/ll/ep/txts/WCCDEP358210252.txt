Westmr.  Catharine Morris Aged about Thirty four Years, Upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Morris (who is gone from her) with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn London Upwards of Fifteen Years ago, says her said Husband Rented a House known by the sign of the Taylor's Arms in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for half a Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty one Pounds, quitted same about Two Years ago, and hath not since Rented House or Paid Taxes to her know- -ledge or belief

Sworn the 8th. day of March 1768, Before} 

Cattern Morris
