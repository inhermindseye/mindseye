Geo: Ptolemy upon Oath saith that upwds. of 25 years ago being then a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Henry Howarth Esqr . at Clowis in Radnorshire for four Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £3 and has not since he quitted his said Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 9th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of  Geo: Ptolemy
