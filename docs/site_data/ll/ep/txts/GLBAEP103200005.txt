Middlesex ss}  Sarah Staple  Singlewoman maketh oath that sometime ago she was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Mr John Pyecroft a Mast factor in the Parish of St Magdalen in Old Fish Street in the City of London at Certain Wages & Continued in such Service under such hiring for the Space of one year & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That she hath a Child named Will Burr aged Sixteen Weeks born of her Body in Cartwright Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County which Child is illegitimate & a Bastard by Reason this Depont was never Married And that one William Burr of Cartwright Street aforesd Labourer at No 33 there is the Father of the Said Child.

Sworn this 31st-day of May 1783 before R Davies R Smith

Sarah Staple

Settlemt. By Servitude in St. Magdalen Old Fish Street

Bastard Child born in Cartwright Street Aldgate Middx
