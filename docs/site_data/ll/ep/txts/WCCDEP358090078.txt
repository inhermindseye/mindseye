Middlesex to wit}  John Williams of St. Clement Dane Oilman & Grocer maketh Oath & saith that He did keep a House & shop at the Rent of Thirty Two Pounds a Year & paid Taxes for the same in Portugal Street & quitted the same at Lady Day 1779 & signs that time He hath not Rented a House in any other Place or done any Act whereby to gain a Legal Settlement in any other place & doth Believe that He is settled in the Parish of St. Clement Dane in the City of Westminster & in the County of Middlesex

Sworn Before me this Day of September} J Girdler

John Williams
