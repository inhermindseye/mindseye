Middlesex & Westmr.}  Thomas Davis Aged about Seventy One Years on his Oath Says he rented and lived in a house known by the Sign of the Goat in Bennett Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster now called the Marquis of Grandby for Seven Years or thereabts. at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds hath quitted the same about Eighteen Years and hath not since he quitted the same ever rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 27th. day of February 1770 before me} Tho: Fellows

Thomas Davis
