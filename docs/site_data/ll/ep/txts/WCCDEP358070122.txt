Westmr. to wit John Mercer Aged about Thirty Eight Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Frances his Wife at St. Leonards Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex about Sixteen Years ago and by whom he hath four Children Namely John Aged about fourteen Years Mary Aged about five Years & Samuel & Rebecca Twins Aged about fifteen Months Says that since the time of the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Mary Le Cheap London acknowledging this Examinant to be a Parishoner legally Settled in the sd Parish of St. Mary Le Cheap he never Rented Rented House or lived a hired Yearly Servant before Marriage neither has he done any other Act since Marriage whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of August 1773 before  Tho Kynaston

John Mercer
