Middx  The Examination of Sarah Swan  Widw . of Jno- Swan taken the 9th. Day of July 1741 Who upon her Oath saith yt. about 22 Years ago her late Husband Jno. Swan Rented a House in Butcher row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Pounds or thereabouts & lived in the same for the space of Eight Years & upwards And further upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband in his life time nor hath she since his Death ever Rented any other House paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she is Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before}
