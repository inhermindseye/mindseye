Middx.  Eliz: Willson aged abt. 44 Years upon Oath says; she is the Widow of Major Willson who has been dead about 7 Years; says her said husband informed her and which information She Verily believes to be true that he was bound apprentice for Seven Years to John Stone Junr. a Baker and after having Lived with him abt. two Years was assigned Over to John Stone  Senr . with whom he served the Remaining five Years of his said Apprenticeship at Edmonton in the County of Middlesex and that Neither her said late husband in his life time as he further Informed her nor has she this Examinant since his decease rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 15th. day of Decr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Eliz. Willson

Upon the Oath Complt. of Mr. R: Wimberley Ovr . J: P:
