Middx  Joseph Bayley on Behalfe of Sarah Bayley his Sister upon his Oath saith that the Sd. Sarah Bayley is a Singlewoman & was never married & that upwards of One Year ago she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mrs. Phillips in Southampton Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for the Space of One Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds that since she left her said Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that the Sd. Sarah Bayley is now Melancholy & not able to maintain herself

Sworne the 11th. Day of May 1742 before} Tho. Lane  Jas: Gronous

Joseph Bayley
