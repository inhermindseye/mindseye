Westmr.  Hannh. Priest upon Oath saith she is the wido. of Fras. Priest with whom she intermarried in Whitechapel Church upwds. of 30 Years ago and that her sd. late husband Rented a house of £10 by the Year & upwds. in Shoreditch in the Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middx & lived therein sevl Years and never afterwards nor has this Examinant Since his Decease rented ten pounds pd. parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn 19th. Feby. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Hannh Priest
