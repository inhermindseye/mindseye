Middx  Frances Champion aged about 55 Years upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband Robt. Champion in the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex about 26 Yrs. since Says her sd. late Husband Rt. Champion hath been Dead about 16 Years that he in his Life time nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice of Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. to her knowledge or beleif Says her sd. late Husbands Father Wm. Champion lived in & rented a House in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the sd. County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £30: & lived in the same for the Space of 16 Years or thereabouts

Sworne July 9th. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Francis Champion
