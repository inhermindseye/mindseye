St Clement Danes April 10th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Margaret Westmr-  (to wit) Ann Lawton aged 20 years and upwards Wife of John Lawton (who has enlisted in the Marines and is now from her) Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said John Lawton at the parish Church of Kensington in the County of Middlesex (by Banns) on or about the 14th day of November 1791 that previous thereto her said Husband was Served his Apprenticeship under Indenture in an Apprentice with Mr Ging a Carpenter in Cooper's Street for 5 years and upwards and during that time lodged with his said Master in Cooper Street in the parish of St Margaret Westminster during such his service that he quitt left his said Master about four Years ago without consent and And this Examinant saith that her said Husband hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge information or belf belief And that She hath one Child by her said Husband now living named William ag born on or about the 20th. day of February last

Sworn the 10th day of April 1794 before us} J Pownall Rupt Clarke

Ann Lawton 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
