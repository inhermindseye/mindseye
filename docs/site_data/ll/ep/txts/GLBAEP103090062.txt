Middlesex ss}  Mary Mc. Koy  Single woman maketh oath That She knows a Child named William Cooper Aged about three Years who is become Chargeable or likely to be so to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex And this Deponent Saith That one Ann Tillroy deced did in her life time inform this Deponent (& which information this Deponent believes to be true) That she the said Ann Tillroy was the Mother of Such Child And that the same was born of her Body as an illegitimate or Bastard Child in the house of one Mr Beatley in Hodgsdon Square in the Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the said County of Middlesex And this Deponent farther Saith that she hath also been informed & believes the same to be true That one William Cooper who is lately gone abroad as an East India Soldier (as is alleged) is the Father of the said Child And that he & the said Childs Mother deced were Scotch People.

Sworn this 14th day of April 1767 before  Chris. Scott R Pell

Mary Mc Koy Her Mark
