Middlesex, to wit, Jane Munt , Singlewoman , maketh Oath and Saith, that on or about the Thirty First Day of March One Thousand Seven Hundred and Eighty Nine , she was delivered a Male Bastard Child in Wiltshire Lane in the Parish of Saint John of Wapping , since Baptised at Wapping Church by the namely John , and that John Anns , then of Wiltshire Lane aforesaid was the Father of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this 30th Day of August 1792 , before}
