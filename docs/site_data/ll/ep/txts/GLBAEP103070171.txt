Middx  Thomas Cox Maketh oath that about fifty four Years ago he was bound apprentice to Anthony Harrison  Watch maker who then lived in Fore street in ye parish of St Giles Witht Cripplegate in ye City of London with whom he served five years of his sd apprenticeship & then his Master removed in to Birchin Lane in ye Parish of St. Michael Cornhill in ye City of London and there this Deponent served the residue of his sd apprenticeship & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And saith that he has not since gained any subsequent settlemt. having never rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards Nor been a hired servt by ye year nor served Any Parish offices

Sworn this 16. day of october 1756 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Thomas Cox
