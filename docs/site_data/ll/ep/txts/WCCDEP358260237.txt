Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jenkin Davis aged about 41 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Mary Davis his present Wife at the Parish Church of Nankunlley in Cardigan about Twenty years ago by whom she hath Six Children living Vizt Mary aged about Eighteen years provided for Elizth . aged about 14 years David aged about Ten years Ann aged about Eight years Catherine aged about 4 years John aged about Twelve Months unprovided for That he this Deponent Rented a first floor unfurnished of a Mr Poole in Plough Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Four Months or thereabouts at the Weekly Rent of Four Shillings and Three pence

Sworn this 23d. day of Decr. 1797 before me } W Carter

his Jenkin Davis mark
