Middlesex ss}  Ann Taaff the wife of Michael Taaff maketh oath that her said husband is at present absent from her That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he is by birth an Irishman & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in any part of Great Britain That She this Deponent previous to her marriage with her said husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with Thomas Lisse by in Little Moor fields in the Parish of St Giles without Cripplegate London packer at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for Seven years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she did not afterwards live elsewhere as a hired Servt for a year or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Save What she has Acquired by her marriage as afore said

Sworn this 22. of August 1777 before Parkr Rowlands  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Taaff
