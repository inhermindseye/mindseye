Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Carey  widow of James Carey deced maketh oath that Since the death of her said late husband she has been hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages by & with Joseph Smith of Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & Continued in such Service for about the Space of two years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived elsewhere As a hired Servant for a year or been Married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 17. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Carey
