Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Eversidge aged about 52 years upon his Oath saith that he is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That he never lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or ever bound an Apprentice That he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that he was born at Aylesbury in the Parish of Aylesbury in the County of Bucks where his father kept house for several years

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry. 1798 before us } M Addington Rupt Clarke

his John Eversidge mark
