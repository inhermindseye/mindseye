Westmr.  Mary Bishop upon Oath saith she is the wife of Joshua Bishop (who is gone from her) and that she intermarried with the sd. Joshua Bishop about one Year ago at the Fleet London that she has been Informed and believes her sd. husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes and that she has been further Informed and verily believes that Thomas Bishop her Husbands father Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Madeap a Ropemaker in Old Street in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of middlesex and never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly

Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 9th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Bishop
