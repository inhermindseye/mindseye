Middx  Ann Whable aged about 40 Years upon Oath says She was never Married rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father  James Whable Rented a House upon Cuckolds point in the Parish of Rotherhith in the County of Kent of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid parish Taxes as She hath been informed and which information She verily believes to be true

Sworn the 2d. day of Novr. 1762 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The mark of Ann Whable
