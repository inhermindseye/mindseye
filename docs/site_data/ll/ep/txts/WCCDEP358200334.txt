Westmr.  Daniel Obrien aged about 57 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house next the Play house Passage in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds lived therein upwards of three Years & hath quitted same about Ten Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 11th. Decr. 1765 , before} Paul Vaillant  Frans Cary

The Mark of Danl. Obrien
