Middx ss  Silence Grover Aged upwards of Sixty five years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Grover who has been dead upwards of Twenty years and with whom she intermarried a the Parish Church of Saint Bennetts Pauls wharf in the City of London about Forty six years ago and by whom she hath only a Daughter now alive (who is married and provided for) Says her said late husband Rented and lived in a House in Clare Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster about Six years at the yearly Rent of about £20 and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Thirty four years ago & never after Rented House paid Taxes lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or any otherwise gained a subsequent Settlement, neither has this Examinant since her said Husbands Death.

Sworn this 12th day of Octr 1775 before  David Walker

Silence Grover
