Middlesex ss}  Ann Followard the widow of Lawrence Followard deced maketh oath that Since her Sd husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year with Mr. Lynam a Victualler in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service for many Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13 day of March 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Ann Followard
