Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Kay aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Owen in Lyons Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx and Liberty of Westminster for Two years at the yearly wages of Four Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Six Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish That one Henry Grist who lives at the Temple of the Flora is the father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Henry Grist and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 22d. day of June 1795 } Edw: Read

her Elizth. Kay Mark

Witness Jas. Talboys
