Middx  James Hunt aged about 25 Years upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Frances on the 1st. Day of March last at the Fleet London Says he was born in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx where his Father Leonard Hunt lived in & rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £20 & upwds. & paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 6th. June 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of James Hunt
