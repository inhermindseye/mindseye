Middx  Ann Shipwash aged about 55 Years upon her Oath saith that she was never married Says she lived in & Rented a house in Sheir Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £33 & lived in the same for the Space of Six Years or thereabouts & hath left the same about 13 Years, since which time she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne Novr. 6th. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

Ann Shipwash
