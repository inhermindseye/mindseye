Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Cobden aged about 60 years upon his Oath saith that in the year 1762 he Intermard. with Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey that he Rented and lived in a House in Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months or thereabouts & the yearly Rent of £14 that he hath quitted said House about 6 years ago since which time he has not Rented any House Tenement on Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of March 1788  John Croft  Wm Gascoigne

John Cobden
