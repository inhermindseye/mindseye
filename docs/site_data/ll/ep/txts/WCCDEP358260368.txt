Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Beale aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hitchen in Holywell and Newcastle Streets in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Six years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Four years since which time She hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Novr. 1798 before me } Saml. Edward

her Mary Beale Mark
