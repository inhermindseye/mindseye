Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit John Baker aged about 62 years upon his Oath saith that about Eight years ago he Intermarried with Jane his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary at Shrewsbury by whom he hath Two Children living namely Tacchaw aged about 5 years and William aged about Eight Months That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Martin Clement Danes for about Two years at the yearly Rent of Eight Guineas and paid All Taxes due for the same That he hath quitted said house about Eighteen years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter on thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of July 1795 } Rupt: Clarke

his John Baker Mark
