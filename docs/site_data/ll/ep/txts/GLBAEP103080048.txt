Middlesex ss The Examination of Sarah Primer of the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in ye sd. County Singlewoman taken on Oath before Me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx residing in or Near the sd Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate this Fifteenth Day of August 1759

This Examinant on her oath saith that about Nine Months ago one John Marshall of the Parish of St George in the County of Middx Labourer did Prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at the house of one Mr. Grissell in Artichoake Lane in the sd Parish of St. George And the said John Marshall had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she now goeth with and is Pregnant And this Examinant saith that the said Child or Children of which she now goeth with and is Pregnant as aforesaid is or are likely to be born a bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to ye sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate she being Poor and not able to provide for ye same And that the said John Marshall is the true and only father of the said Child or Children so likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesd. And Castly saith that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whatsoever

Sworn this 15th- Day of August 1759 before Me  Walter Berry

The Marke of Sarah Primer
