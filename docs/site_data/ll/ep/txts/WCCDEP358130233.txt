Middx  Elizth. Partridge aged abt. forty Years upon Oath says She never was Married Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant upwards of Six Years with Mrs. Bishop a Linnen Draper in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. Six Years since which time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the day of Novr. 1756 before}
