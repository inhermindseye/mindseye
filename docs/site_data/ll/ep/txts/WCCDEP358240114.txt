St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  (to wit) Elizth. Mortons John Jones Aged about 39 Years Upon Oath saith That he was Married at the parish Church of St John Wapping to Ann his late Wife at tha parish & about Years ago That during their intermarriage That he Rented & with her Resided in an House in Ship Yard at the in the parish of St Clement Danes the Liberty of Westmr. for 6 Months at the Annual Rent of £10 & paid the Rates for the same That he quitted it at or about Midsummer 1788 and since quitting hath not obtained any Subsequent legal settlement to his knowledge Information or belief And that he has been Ill is now Weak and unable to support himself Without Assistance That his said Wife Died about 3 Years ago he hath not since been Married nor hath any Child Children now living

Sworn this 8th day of June 1792 before Me}
