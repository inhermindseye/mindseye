Middlesex ss}  Robert Hilsey maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice to and Served the Whole thereof with Robert Scriven in that part of  Love Lane Which lies in the Parish of Saint Mary Hill near Billingsgate in the City of London That Since Such his Apprenticeship he has not lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house or other Tenament of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement And that he hath a wife named Hannah to Whome he was lawfully married about Twenty Years ago That about Ten Years ago his Said wife left him & Went to places unknown to him & this Deponent did not See her nor did he know Where She was nor did he ever receive any information Whether She was dead or living for the Space of about Seven years afterwards & therefor Apprehends her to be dead he this Deponent was Married to  Mary Haywood (Since deced) in the Church belonging to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishopsgate London by Whom he hath a Child now living named Elizabeth aged about two Years & Which Child was born in Lamb Alley in the Same Parish & this Deponent had been Married to the Said Childs Mother for about nine Months before that he Knew his Said wife  Hannah was living

Sworn this. 28 of November 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Robert Hilsey
