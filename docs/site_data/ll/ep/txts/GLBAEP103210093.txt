Middlesex ss}  Bartholomew Gaffney maketh oath that in the month of August in the year one thousand seven hundred and seventy six he this Deponent was lawfully married to his now wife by the name of Sarah Barlow she being then a widow in the parish Church of Saint Mary Newington Butts in the County of Surrey accordg to the usual forms and Ceremonies of the Church of England.

Sworn this 5. day of August 1785 before}
