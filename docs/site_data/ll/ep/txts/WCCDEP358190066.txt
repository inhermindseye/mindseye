Middx.  Mary Bunting aged abt. 52 Years (lodging at a common lodging house in Kings head Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx) Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Wm: Bunting who has been dead abt. 13 Years says her said husband Rented a house in New Inn Passage in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for One Year & a half at the Yearly Rent of £14 and never after (he Quitted said house nor she this Examinant since his Death) Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn Novr. 5th. 1760 before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Bunting
