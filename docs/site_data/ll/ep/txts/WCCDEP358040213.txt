Eliz. Botts  Wido . of Richard Botts upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Richard Botts about Sixteen Years ago at the Fleet London and that the sd. Rd Botts was born at the Abbey Forehead in Shrewsbury in the County of Salop & was bound Apprentice to Mr. Spendlove a Shoemaker near the same Place with whom he Served Part of his Apprentiship & never was Assigned over to any other Person and that neither her sd. late husband in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 9th. 1748 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz Botts
