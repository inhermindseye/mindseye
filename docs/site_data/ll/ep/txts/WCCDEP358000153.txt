St Clement Danes Novr 17th- 1785

Middx ss} 

Swears to St. Clements known & sent Into the Ho.-  Robert Bryerley aged Eighty Six Years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith that he rented and lived in a House in Hemlock Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of £25 for several Years that he quitted the same about twenty Years ago, and since that time has not done any Act whereby to the best of his Knowledge he may have acquired any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. Novr 1785 }

his Robert Bryerley Mark
