Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to Alderman bury C to wit Alexr. Kennedy Aged Sixty Seven years or thereabouts upon his Oath says he was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Nathl. Cross of Aldermanbury in the Parish of Saint Mary Aldermanbury in the City of London  Musical Instrument maker under an Indenture of Apprenticeship for Seven years says he served five years and upwards and then had his Indentures given up to him by the Order of  the Lord Mayor That he quitted his said Master about Forty Seven years ago since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing Whereby to the best of his knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 18th. day of Decr. 1784 M Addington Thos: Gordon

Alexander Kennedy
