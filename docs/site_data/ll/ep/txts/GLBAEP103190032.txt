Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Crawley the wife of John Crawley maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Dunkirk That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served his Apprenticeship with one Bunyan a Bricklayer in the Parish of Saint Ann in Newbury in the County of Berks & to her knowledge or belief has has not Since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement & that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 9. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Crawley
