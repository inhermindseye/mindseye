St Clement Danes Feb 17th. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) Mary Talbot aged 48 Years or thereabouts Singlewoman, Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she rented and readed in a House No. 18 In Red Lion Court in the parish of Saint the fields Dunstan in the West in the County City of London Middlesex for four Years or thereabouts at the Annual Rent or Sum of Twenty two pounds that she quitted the said House about the year 1782, and that she has done no Act Matter or Thing whatsoever whereby to gain any subsequent legal Settlement the best of this deponent knowledge Information a belief.
