Middx  The Examination of Eleanor Monk Lodging at Mr. Huson's in yt. Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 7th. day of March 1738 Who upon her Oath saith yt. she is a Singlewoman & was never Marryed & that on the 5th. day of Febry. last she this Examint. was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of the said Mr. Huson in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid wch. hath since been Baptized John And this Examinant further saith that John Grayham in Princes Street in Drury Lane  Vintner did begett the said Child on her Body she this Examint. at such time living as a Yearly hired Servt . with the sd. Jno. Graham in Princess Street aforesaid at which Place the sd. Jno. Graham had Carnall Knowledge of this Examint's. Body Sevll. times in the Months of April May & June last in the sd. House at one of wch times he the said Jno. Grayham got her with the said Male Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesaid and further saith yt. he the sd. Jno. Grayham & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof.

Sworne the day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane  James Gronous

The Mark of Elenr. Monk
