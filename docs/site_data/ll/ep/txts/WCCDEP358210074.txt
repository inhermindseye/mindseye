Middx.  Joseph Lewis aged abt. 36 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his Wife at the Fleet London about fifteen Years ago by whom he has three Children Joseph aged about thirteen Years George aged about four Years and penelope aged upwards of one Year say's he served one Year and upwards the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Joseph Hickman  Air Pumpmaker in Stonecutter Street in the Parish of St. Brides London & hath been out of his said Apprentice ship about fifteen Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn the 11th. Septr. 1766 , Before} Barth: Hammond  Philip Dyot

Joseph Lewis
