Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Henry Snow aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that he was placed out by Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square to a Mr. Harrison of the Edgware Road in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex Hair Dresser for 5 years & 12 that he served One year and an half of the his said Apprenticeship that then his Master took him before Two Justices and had him Regularly discharged that since he left his said Master he has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th day of April 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

his Henry Snow Mark
