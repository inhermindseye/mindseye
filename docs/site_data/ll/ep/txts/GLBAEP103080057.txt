Middlesex ss The Examination of Jane Gater of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in said County single woman taken on oath before Me one of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the County of Middx residing in or Near the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate this seventeenth day of May 1760

This Examinant on her oath saith that about eight Months ago one Thomas Smith the Younger of Watling Street near Cheapside London  Upholsterer did Prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at the house of his father Thomas Smith in Moor Fields in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishopsgate London Broker And the said Thomas Smith the Younger had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body several times whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she now goeth with and is Pregnant And this Examinant saith that the said Child or Children of Which she now goeth with and is Pregnant as aforesaid is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate she being Poor and not able to Provide for the same And that the said Thomas Smith the Younger is the true and only father of the said Child or Children so likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesaid And lastly saith that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whatsoever

Sworn this 17th. day of May 1760 before Me  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Jane Gater
