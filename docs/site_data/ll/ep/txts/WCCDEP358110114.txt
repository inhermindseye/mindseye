Middx  Ann White aged about 25 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she hath been informed & verily believes she was born in the house of Mr Kidd a Butcher in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex and cannot give any Account whatever of the Settlement of her father or Mother And further that She (this Examint.) never rented Ten pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes nor ever was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Novr.17th 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann White
