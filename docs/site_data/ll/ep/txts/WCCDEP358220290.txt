Westminster  Mary Davey Aged about Thirty One Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of John Davey with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Dunstans in the West about Three Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Namely Mary Aged about Two Years and Elizabeth Aged about five Months Says that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Chichesters Rents Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Nine Months at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds hath quitted the same about Ten Months Ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn this 25th. day of September 1771 before me R. Butler R Amy

her Mary Davey Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
