Westmr. 

Richd. Crossing upon Oath saith he Lived a Yearly Servant with St. Thos. Jones in Boswell Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £10 for the Space of two Years or thereabouts whilst a Singleman and continued with the sd St Thos. Jones abt. 3 Years after his Intermarriage with his late Wife Grace that he has not Since he quitted his sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 17th 1749 before} Tho Lane

Richard Crossing
