Middx  Jane Gladman aged about 50 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant wth. Mrs. Mary Godfrey in Southampton Street in the Parish of St Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. for Seven Years & upwards at the yearly Wages of £6 quitted her sd Service abt. one Year ago and has not since rented ten Pounds by the year on pd. Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn 30th. Augt. 1754 before} Tho Lane  Benjamin Cox Jane Gladman
