Middle Sex ss  John Thatcher Maketh oath that abt. forty Seven Years ago he was bound apprentice by Indre of apprenticeship to one John Allen a Comb Maker on London Bridge in the Parish of St. Magnus by London Bridges in the City of London With whom he duly Served his apprenticeship in ye sd. Parish And Saith that he Never was a hired Servant by the Year Nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards Nor Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Settlement Subsequent to his apprenticeship as aforesd. to his knowledge or belief And that he hath a wife Named Mary

Sworn this 4th day of October 1752 before me us  Richd : Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

John Thatcher
