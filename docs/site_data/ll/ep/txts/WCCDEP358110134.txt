Middx  Sarah Raney aged upwds. of 18 years upon Oath Saith She never was married nor ever rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or was an Apprentice or yearly Servant and that her late father John Raney as she has been Informed and believes was born in Claremarket and she can give no further Accot. of his Settlement And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith, she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes She being poor and unable to Maintain the Same when born and that John Culbert otherwise Cuthbert a Staymaker did beget the said

bastard Child or Children on her body the said John Culbert otherwise Cuthbert having had carnal Knowledge of this Examint. in the Month of April last and at Sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said John Culbert otherwise Cuthbert and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1752 before W Fielding

The Mark of Sarh. Raney
