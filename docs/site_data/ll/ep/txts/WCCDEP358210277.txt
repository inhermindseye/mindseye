Middx & Westmr.  Ann Guppy Aged about Twenty two Years, Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married, and that on the Twenty eighth day of April 1768, who was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Gray (Parish Nurse) in Hollis Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized by the Name of Elizabeth , says that one Joseph Weekly a Journeyman Taylor who works for one Mr. Gray in Stanhope Street in the Parish aforesaid is the reputed Father of the said Female Bastard Child he having had carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of August 1767, and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid, And that he the said Joseph Weekly and as other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 26th, day of May 1768, Before} 

The Mark of Ann Guppy
