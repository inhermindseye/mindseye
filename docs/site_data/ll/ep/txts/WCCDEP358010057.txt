Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit  Mary Price aged about 63 years upon her Oath saith that about 40 years ago she Intermarried with John Price her late Husband (who has been Dead about 18 years) at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes that her said Husband lived as a hired yearly Servant with Dr. Altree in Norfolk Street for 3 years at the yearly Wages of £6 that he had quitted sa Service about 22 years before his death during which time he never did Rented any House Tenement or Lodgi of £10 a years paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath this Ext. since her said Husbands Death lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 25th. day of Janry. 1787 R Butler Mary Price
