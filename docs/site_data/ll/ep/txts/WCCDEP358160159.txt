Middx  The Examination of Thos. Marshall Passed from the Parish of St. Giles in ye. Fields in the County of Middx by Order. bearing date 6th. June last Taken ye. 17th. Day of July 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. abt. 14 Years ago he was bound & Served 7 years Apprentiship to one Mr. Jno. Brooks in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Water Gilder , And this Examint. further upon his Oath saith yt. he hath not since he left his sd. Master Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife, that he hath a Wife & two Childn. (to wit) Jno. aged abt. 7 years & Wm. aged upwds. of 3 years & being Poor & lame is not able to maintaine himselfe & Family

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Thos. Marshall
