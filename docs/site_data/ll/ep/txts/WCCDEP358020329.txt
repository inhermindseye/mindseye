St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Sarah Downs Aged about 30 years Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Anderson at the five Bells in Surrey Street about the strand upwards of fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of £7 and quitted the same about Ten Nine Years ago & since hath not obtained any other legal settlement (by any way or means whatsoever to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 10th day of August 1791 before me R. Butler

Sarah Downs
