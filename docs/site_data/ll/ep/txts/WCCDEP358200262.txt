Middx.  James Cobham aged about 14 Years Upon Oath says he was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the poor of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx to Joshua Roper of Turnmill Street in the parish of St. John Clerkenwell in the same County Cabinet founder with whom he lived as an Apprentice upwards of One Year and then went away from his said Master and never was Assigned over to any person whatsoever.

Sworn the 26th. of April 1765 , Before} Paul Vaillant  Thos. Kynaston

James Cobham his Mark
