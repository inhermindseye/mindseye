Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Edward Burbidge of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  Peruke Maker Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed that Ann Leach a Female Child of Mary Leach  Singlewoman was born at Mr. Flaxtons a Muffin Baker in Little Saint Andrews Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex And further saith that the said Child is Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and is as above stated and this Examinant is informed is illegitimate

Sworn this 12th. day of March 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

Edwd. Burbidge
