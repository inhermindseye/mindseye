Middx.  Elizth. Murphy aged about 25 Years upon Oath says she intermarried with her present husband Dennis Murphy in the parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex about Six Years ago by whom she hath One Child, John aged about five Years, Says her said husband Rented a house in Butcher Row in the said parish of St. Clement Danes for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and Upwards hath Quitted said house about four Years and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn 6th. May 1765 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Elizabeth Murphy
