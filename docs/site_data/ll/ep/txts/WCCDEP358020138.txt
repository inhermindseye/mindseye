Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex ss:  James Talboys one of the Beadles of the above Parish of on behalf Jonathan Woods who is Ill maketh Oath and saith that he has been informed by the said Jonathan Woods which information he believes to be true that the sd. Jonathan Woods is about 16 Years of age and that about 2 Years ago he was bound an Apprentice by Indentures duly executed to one John Nott of Haydon Court in the Parish of Saint James Westminster in the County of Middlesex  Hairdresser that when he had served 2 Months with his said Master his sd. Master he quitted his House in Haydon Court aforesaid and that he the said Jonathan Woods hath not since had any Information where his said Master is and that since he hath quitted his sd. Master he has done no Act matter of or Thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of February April 1789 R Butler D Walker

James Talboys
