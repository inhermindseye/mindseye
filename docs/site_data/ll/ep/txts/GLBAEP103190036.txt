Middlesex ss}  Ann Munks the wife of John Munks maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Resolution That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served his Apprenticeship with one John Newman a Ship Carpenter in the Parish of St Mary in the City of Bristol & to her knowledge or belief has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement That she has a Child named Mary aged Thirteen Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this. 11. day of March 1780 before W Blackmore

The Mark of Ann Munks
