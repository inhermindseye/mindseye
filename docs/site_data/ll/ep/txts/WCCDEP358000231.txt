Saint Clement Danes 21st. February 1786

Middlesex & Westminster to wit) Catharine Peet  singlewoman aged eighteen Years or thereabouts on behalf of Jane Peet her Sister an Infant of the Age of fourteen Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that the said Jane Peet , was placed out Apprentice by the Officers of the Parish of Saint Paul Deptford to one Catharine Peet this Examinants Aunt, who at the time lived in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell that she served as an Apprentice to her said Aunt for about two Years, and then her said Aunt placed her with one Mrs. Selby to whom she paid for her board and it was agreed between her said Aunt and said Mrs. Selby that the said Mrs. Selby should procure a Service for the said Apprentice Jane Peet ,-and this Examinant further saith that the said Mrs. Selby did accordingly procure a Place for the said Jane Peet to live with one Mr-Pair a Watch care maker in Islington Road facing Sadlers Wells in the Parish of Saint Mary Islington James Gler manuall in the County of Middlesex at the wages of one shilling a Week besides Board - that Mrs the said Jane Peet lived with the said Mr- Pair under such Agreement for about eighteen Weeks - and quitted her said place about a fortnight three years ago - That said Mrs Selby reced the said Wages the greater part of which she has laid out for Cloaths for the said Jane Peet

Sworn this 21st. Febry 1786 before  Sampr Wright M Addington

her Cath: Peet Mark

To Sworn the Parish being obtned this 22nd. July 1786 before Mercer John Croft
