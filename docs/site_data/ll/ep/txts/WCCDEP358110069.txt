Middx.  Richd. King aged abt. 57 Years upon Oath saith he Rented a house in Houghton Street near Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlx of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds and lived therein upwards of one Year quitted the sd house about Seven Years ago and has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn July 17th. 1752 before}
