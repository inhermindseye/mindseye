Westmr. to wit Ann Austin Aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father John Austin Rented and lived in a House in King Street Golden Square in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of fifteen Pounds and upwards and when this Examinant was born And further says that she was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Bowen a Childs Coc maker in Swallow Street in the sd. Parish of Saint James and lived within upwards of One Year & then parted by Consent since which time she has done no Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Septr. 1773 before R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Ann Auston  Mark
