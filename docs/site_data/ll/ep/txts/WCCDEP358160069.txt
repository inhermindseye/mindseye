Middx  The Exaicon of Mathew Turner aged 70 Years & upwds. Lodging in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx who upon his Oath Saith that he Served two Years or thereabts. the Latter part of his Apprentispp wth Henry Darker in the Parish of St. John in Southwark  Hatmaker and further Saith after he was out of his Apprentishipp he Rented a House in Nookes Hole in Tooley Street in the parish of St. Olive in Southwarke of the Yearly rent of Five pounds and lived in the same for the space of Twenty Years or thereabts. & paid the Scavengers Rate due for sd. House for Sevll. Years and hath not since he left the sd. House Rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleife.

Sworn the 22d. day of Augst. 1739 before} Jas. Gronous  Thos. Diveil

The mark of Mathew Turner
