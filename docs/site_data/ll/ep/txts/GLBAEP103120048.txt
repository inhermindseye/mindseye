Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Berry the widow of George Berry deced maketh oath that he hath informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound apprentice to one James Birch a Perukemaker of Watling Street in the Parish of Saint Augustine in the City of London & served six years of such his Apprenticeship there & then left his Said Master & enlisted himself for a Soldier & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement to his Said Apprenticeship That she this Depont. Since her Said husbands death hath not done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right That she hath two Children namely John Aged Fourteen years who hath not done any Act to gain a settlement for himself and George Aged one year the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her Said late husband

Sworn this 2d day of June 1773 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

Elizabeth Berry Her Mark
