Westmr.  Ann Lee removed from the Parish of Saint Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx by Order dated the fifth Day of June last upon Oath Says she is a Singlewoman Says she was hired to one Mrs. Holland in Calenders Yard in Long Alley near Moor- -fields at the Yearly Wages of Thirty Shillings and a months Warning or a Months Wages Says she lived with the said Mrs. Holland under the said Agreement for one Year all but four Days Says the said Mrs. Holland turned this Examinant away at a minutes Warning & would not pay her any Wages Says she hath not since she left Mrs. Hollands Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 11th 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Lee
