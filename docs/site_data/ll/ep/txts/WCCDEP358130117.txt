Middx  George Gregory aged about 53 years upon Oath says he rented a House of Ten pounds a year (to the best of his knowledge and belief) in Maypole  Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and lived therein upwards of a year & Quitted the said House abt. Twenty years ago and hath not since rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 11th. Day of Febry. 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

George Gregory
