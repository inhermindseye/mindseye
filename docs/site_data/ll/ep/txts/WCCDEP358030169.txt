Middx  Cathn. Roberts on Behalf of Mary Jones an Infant aged upwards of 3 Years upon Oath saith the sd. Infant was born at the Paviers Arms in Water Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. Says she knows nothing of the Settlemt. of the Father or Mother of the sd. Infant other than that they were born in Wales

Sworn 25th. June 1746 before}
