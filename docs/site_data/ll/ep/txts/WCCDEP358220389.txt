Westmr. to wit Eliz: Meadows Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Wermell at the Sign of the Sun near Clements Inn Gate in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Six Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived an hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further Says that on the 23d. day of December 1768 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Andrew Thomas in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized by the name of Ann Meadows Says that one Michael Charles a Dealer in Coals in Little Drury Lane is the Father of the said Female Bastard Child he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the House of him the said Michael Charles sometime in the Month of March 1768 but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. Different times after at one or which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered and that the said Michael Charles and no other person as the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 2d. day of October 1772 before me} 

her Eliz: Meadows Mark
