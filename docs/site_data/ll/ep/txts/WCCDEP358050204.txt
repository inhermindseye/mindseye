Westmr. 

Bridget Mc. Kertny aged about 60 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 20 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Moses Hart in the Parish of Isleworth in the County of Middlesex  for two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £6 and has not since She quitted her Sd Service rented Ten pounds a Years paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 16th 1749 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Bridget Mc. Kertny
