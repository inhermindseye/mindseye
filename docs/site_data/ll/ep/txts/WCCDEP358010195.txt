Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Hard aged about 30 years upon his Oath saith that she Intermarried with Patrick Hart her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about 11 years ago that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Fleet Lane in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre in the City of London for about 3 Years at the yearly Rent of £14 that he hath quitted said House about 8 years ago since which time she hath done no Act gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Febry. 1788  Sampr Wright  Thos. Gordon

her Ann Hart Mark
