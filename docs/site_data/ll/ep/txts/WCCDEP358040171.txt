Middx & Westmr.}  Abigal Munday aged upwds. of 25 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that about 1 Year ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Abington at the Kings Arms Tavern in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds and Six Guineas for one Year and upwards and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a male bastard Child on the fifth Day of April Instant at a house in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx which is since baptized Thos. and is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same and that Edward Bates Cook to the Right Honble Lord Townsend is the reputed

father the sd. Edward Bates having lived fellow Servant with this Examinant at the Kings Arms Tavern aforesd. there became acquainted with her and had carnal knowledge of her Body several times in the months of June and July last at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesaid and that he the sd. Edward Bates & no man else is the true & real father thereof

Sworn 11th. April 1748 before} Wm: Jones  Tho. Lane

Abigail Munday
