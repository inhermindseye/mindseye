Middx  Susanna a Fosset  Wido . of Vincent Fosset who dyed upwds. of 3 Yrs. ago upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Vincent Fosset at Stepney Church about 14 Years ago and that she has been informed her sd. late husband upwds. of 30 Yrs. ago lived a Covenant Servant with Mr. Smith an Orice Weaver in Kings head Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and that about Ten Yrs. ago she was removed together with her sd. late husband from the parish of St Botolph Aldgate London and that since the sd. removal neither or sd. late husband nor this Examinant since his Decease have rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examinants Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt 31st. 1750 before}
