Middlesex ss}  Mary Buckingham the wife of John Buckingham maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on Board his Majestiesship the swallow sloop of war that her said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Mr Bailey a Ribbon Weaver in the City of Coventry in the County of Warwick but the Particular Parish this Deponent has forgot & has not since done any Act to gain another settlement to her knowledge or belief & that she was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 25 day of February 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Buckingham
