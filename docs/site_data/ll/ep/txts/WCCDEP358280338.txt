St Clement Danes Septr. 7th. 1791 .

Middlesex & Westminster }  taken & Secy Bond given Thomas's Father & Mr Raine Secys Settled & liv'd  (to wit) Catherine Kisbey Aged 28 years & upwards Upon Oath saith That she was Married That she to one Phillip Kisby at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes aforesd. by Banns about 6 years ago that her said Husband Died about 3 years ago that previous to said his his Intermarriage with this Deponent & not having Child or Children & being single he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Philip Rabhel facing Southampton Street Strand about 2 years at the yearly Wages of £9 or £10 or thereabouts that he quitted that Service about Six years since and did not by any means whatsoever gain any Subsequent legal Settlement in his Life time neither hath she this Deponent since his Death done any Act whereby to acquire any other legal settlement And this Examinant further saith that on the 28th day of October 1790 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the lying Hospital Store Street Middlesex which hath since been Christened at St Andrew's Holborn by the Name of John Thomas Kisby ) And that John Thomas Kisby who is a Clerk to Mr Rain Mr Rain No. 19 Thaives Jo Holborn is the only real and true Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body And that he and no one else hath begotten the same thereon

Sworn before me the 7th Septr 1791 } R. Butler

C Kisdey
