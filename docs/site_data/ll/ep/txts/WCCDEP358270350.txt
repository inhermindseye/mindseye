Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Page aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she hired as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Knight near the Exeter Change in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about a Fortnight only And this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Ship who did live Shopman with the said Mr. Knight is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which She is now pregnant and that the said John Ship is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of Feby. 1798 before me  Gs. Goodenough

her Elizabeth Page Mark

Man never Apprehended

Child born in the House the 14th. day of March 1798 named John

Sent to Enfield  14th. July 1798
