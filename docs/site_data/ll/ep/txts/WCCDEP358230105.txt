Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Elizabeth Hind Aged about 40 years upon her Oath Saith that about Twenty years ago she Intermarried with her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said late Husband rented Apartment corner of Air Street  Piccadilly in the Parish of S aint James in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for Ten Years at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds that he hath left the said Apartments about Eighteen Months since which time he hath not rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th day of Jany 1800 before us} Saml Edwards W Parsons Hday Beadle

Her Elizth. Hind Mark
