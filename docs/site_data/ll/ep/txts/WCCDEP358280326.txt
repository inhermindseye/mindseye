St Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster } (to wit) Mary Fegen  singlewoman aged 24 Years or thereabouts Upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she hath for a twelve month last past Cohabited and lived with Henry Jones of Bow in the Kings H Yard Bow in the County of Middx  Labourer and that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become to be chargeable to the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty at Westminster and County of Middlesex aforesaid And saith that the said Henry Jones is the only Real and true father of such Child or Children whereof she is so pregnant as aforesaid he & no one else having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same thereon

Sworn the 4th. June 1791 . before me} Edward Hodsoll

Mary Fegen's Mark

Vide Examn. as to Settlemt. entd. of this day in the Genl Examn. Book

Henry Jones Middle Size Man Grey Hair Works at the Lime Kilns Bow or Stratford 4th June taken given 2 or 3 days by Mr. Justice Walker (Marrd.. the Woman 7th June 1791 )
