Middx  Ann Savage Aged abt. Twenty two Years Upon Oath Says She lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Harris at the Bull Head in Vear Street near Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. fifteen days and further Upon her Voluntary Oath says She is pregnant with Child or Children wch. is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish She being poor & unable to maintain the same when Born Says one Robt. Pacey a Butcher who Lodged at her sd. Masters had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of June last and at sevll. other times since at one of wch. times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with wch. She is now Pregnant

Sworn the 23th. Decr. 1762 before Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Savage
