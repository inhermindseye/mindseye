Middx  Eliz Cox  Wife of Thomas Cox upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Thomas Cox at the Fleet London upwds. of 9 Yrs. since by whom she hath one Child Susa . aged about 6 Yrs. Says she hath been Informed & believes her sd. husband Served his Apprenticeship with Mr. Miller a Shoemaker  opposite Arundel Street in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt 31st 1751 before}
