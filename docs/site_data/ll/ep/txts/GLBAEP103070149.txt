Middx ss  James Burrough Maketh oath that about Nine Years ago he was bound apprentice to Benjamin Cook  Cardwainer who then lived in Greys Thurrock in the County of Essex where this Depont. served the greatest part of his time And this Deponent saith that his sd Master afterwards removed into the Parish of Allhallows on London Wall in the City of London where this Depont. served the residue of his said apprenticeship and by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt. in the same parish And this Deponent has not since gained any subsequent Settlement never having rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any parochial offices nor done any act to acquire a subseqt. Settlemt. And this Depont. says he has a wife Names Eleanor

Sworn this 19th day of Decr. 1755 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris Scott

James Burroughs
