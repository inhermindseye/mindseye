Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Gale aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she was bound Apprentice by the Church wardens and Overseers of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes to one Mr. Walker a Taylor who lived in Wych Street in the said Parish for Seven years that her said Master died when she had served Five years and Three Quarters of her said Apprenticeship and that she has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Jeremiah Sullivan a Journeyman Dyer who lodges at No:2 Church Yard Court Harp Alley is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of February last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she is so pregnant and that the said Jeremiah Sullivan is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 1st. day of July 1790 } R Butler

her Sarah Gale Mark

Sarah Gale lodges at No:1 Knight Court

Jeremiah Sullivan is a Short Man fair Complexion Light Hair hanging in his Neck

Man taken and Leey. Bond exted he gave Bail to Appear at the sessions on the 13th. Septr ;
