Middx and Westmr.}   Ann Bunker aged about 28 years Lodging in White Horse yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Clarke at the Cheshire Cheese in Surry Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for One whole year at the Yearly Wages of £5 Quitted the same last May And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when Born And that one John Moss a Journeyman Perukemaker did beget such Child or Children he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in or about the Month of August One thousand seven

hundred and Sixty one at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 20th: day of Febry 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Bunker
