Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Smith aged about 53 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Beckwith in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Eight years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds That she hath quitted said Service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Febry. 1797 before} John Reeves Mary Smith
