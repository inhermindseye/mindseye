Westmr  Alice Birch aged about Upwards of Sixteen Years on her Oath says that she never was married, Rented House, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says she was bound an Apprentice by the Chapell Wardens and Overseers of the Town of Uxbridge in the County of Middlesex to One James Colman as Poulterer in Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster by Indenture Dated the 8th day of August 1771 but her Master using this Examinant Ill was obliged to part with him, and an Application to the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes was by them Informed that the Indentures were good for nothing and would never gain a Settlement thereby and further says that since parting with her said Master she has never done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th day of March 1775 before R Butler Sampn Wright

The Mark of Alice Birch
