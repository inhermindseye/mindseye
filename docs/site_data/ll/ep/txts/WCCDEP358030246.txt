Middx  Margt. Morris  Wido . of Thos. Morris upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late husband Tho. Morris in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in holborn abt. 27 Yrs. since Says the sd. Tho. Morris hath been dead near one Year Says he Served 7 Yrs. Apprentiship with his Father in Little Kirby Street in the Liberty of Saffron hill Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the parish of St: Andrew Holborn in the County of Middx as she hath been informed & beleives Says her sd. late Husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servts. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or belief Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief Says she hath one Son (to wit) Charles aged abt. 26 Yrs. who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant or otherwise gained a

Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 7th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Margt. Morris
