Middlesex ss}  Catherine Lewis  Single woman maketh oath that she was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr Andrew Gu ant a Victualler in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such Service under such hiring for the Space of three years & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18 day of December 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Catherine Lewis
