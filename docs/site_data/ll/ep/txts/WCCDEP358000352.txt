Saint Clement Danes

Dennis Kinslow aged 37 or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he became acquainted with Elizabeth Benman about 21 Years ago. That She then lived in James Sheet . Westminster , That after that she went to live in the Service of Lady Egremont in Piccadilly in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square and lived there above 12 Months as he now best remembers and believes That since she Quitted same she has had Two Children Vizt. Daniel and Catherine , That Daniel aged about 17 Years was born in little Russell Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury and positively Saith That he never was Married to her and that the 2 Children above mentd. are illigitimate

Sworn before me 10 Septr. 1786 before D Walker

Dennis Kinslow
