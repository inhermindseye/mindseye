Middlesex ss}  Edward Dalton maketh oath that about Eight years ago he lived in and rented a house at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds in Lamb Alley in Abchurch Lane in the Parish of St Mary Abchurch in the City of London and resided thereon for the Space of Three Months That he has not Since rented a house Lodging other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That He' hath a Daughter named Alice aged about twenty four years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his late wife  Margaret deced to whom he was lawfully Married The which Alice the Daughter never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to this Deponents knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 9th day of April 1788 before  R Davies  Robt Smith

Edw Dalton
