Thos. Bead Aged about Twenty Seven Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Sarah his now Wife at the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the Year 1767 And by whom he hath one Child now living Named Isaac Aged about One Year & an half Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with his Father John Bead a Cordwainer in the Town of Pembroke in the County of Pembroke And further Says that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting 10 Pounds a Year Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 12th. day of August 1771 before R A

Thomas Bead
