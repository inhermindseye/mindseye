Middlesex to wit Mary Corbett aged about 76 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs Blackworth a Surgeon in Vere Street for two Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds and afterwards with one Mr- Johnson a Music Master in the Strand in the parish of St- Clement Danes for one Year at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about three Years ago since which time she has gained no other subsequent Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or orwise howsoever

Sworn this 25th Day of Febry 1778 before  Abrm Baley

her Mary Corbett Mark
