Middlesex ss}  Sarah Dowdall the widow of Henry Dowdall deced maketh oath that her said husband was by birth an Irishman and never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in England That She previous to her Marriage with her said husband was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages by & with Mr Mooney at the Sign of the Beehive in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County and Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of four Years & was there upon married to her said husband and She has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself save what She may have Acquired by her Marriage with her Said husband

Sworn this. 25. day of January 1788 before  R Davies

Sarah Dowdall
