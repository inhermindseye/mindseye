Middx and Westmr.} 

Mary Saunders aged about 29 Years a Vagrant taken out of the Street the 20th. Day of August last in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westmr. upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is a Singlewoman and was on the sd. 20th. Day of August delivered of a female bastard Child in near Burlergh Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes and is become Chargeable to the same Parish Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Mercer at the Crown Alehouse in Vinegar Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the sd. Liberty of Westmr. for two Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds and hath left her sd. Service upwds. of one Year Says she has not since she left the sd. Mr. Marcars Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 9th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Saunders
