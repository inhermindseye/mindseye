Middlesex ss}  Ann Nicholas the widow of Anthony Nicholas  deced maketh oath that her said late husband was a Foreigner by birth as he has informed her & as She believes & that to her knowledge or belief he never did any act to gain a Settlement in Great Britain That in or about the year one thousand Seven hundred & forty She this Deponent married one Isaac Kirk the Son of one Stephen Kirk a Staymaker both of whom have been long Since dead That the said Stephen Kirk at the time of this Deponents marriage with his said Son & for a long time before lived in & rented a house in Greyhound Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the City & Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex of the yearly Rent or Value of Thirty pounds & was Charged to & paid parish taxes for the same as this Depont. has been informed by her said late husband & verily believes & at which time this Deponts said late husband Isaac Kirk & for many years before lived with his said Father there as part of his Family & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents Knowledge or belief her said late husband Kirk never did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself & that she this Deponent Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right save what She might have Acquired by Marriage with her Said Second husband who as before Stated never did any act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in any place in the Kingdom of Great Britain.

Sworn this 29th day of October 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Nicholas
