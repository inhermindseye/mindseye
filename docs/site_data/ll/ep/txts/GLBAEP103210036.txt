Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Wormlayton  Single woman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Rouse a Victualler in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for four years & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10. day of Decemr 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Eleanor Wormlayton
