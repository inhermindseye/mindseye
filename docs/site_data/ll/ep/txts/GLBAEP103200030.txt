Middlesex ss}  Mary Johnson the widow of Robert Johnson deced maketh oath that her sd late husband to her knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in the kingdom of Great Britain That this Deponts late Parents were both born in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & never did any Act to this Deponts Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement elsewhere That she this Deponent was born in the sd Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate & never did any Act to gain a Settlement of sew here to her knowledge or belief save what she acquired by her Marriage as aforesd.

Sworn this 18th day of July 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Mary Johnson

To her Husbands Fathers Settlemt. by Birth
