Middlesex ss  Ann Hutchins  Single woman maketh oath That some years ago she was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year with James Fortune of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Taylor and Continued in such service for the Space of two years at the yearly wages of Five Pounds and by means thereof as She is advised gained a legal Settlemt. there and this Deponent Saith that she hath not since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st day of February 1770 before  Chris Scott

The Mark of Ann Hutchins
