Middx.  Sarah Abberly aged abt. 22 Years upon Oath says she never was an Apprentice Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year that her father Chas. Abberly served his Apprentiship with a Bricklayer in Charter house Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchre's in the County of Middx & never Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Feb: 15th 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Abberly
