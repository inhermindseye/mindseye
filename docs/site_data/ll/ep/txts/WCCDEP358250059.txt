Martha Bower

St. Clement Danes 28th July 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. Pancras Middx to wit Martha Turtel  Singlewoman aged six year and upwards Upon her Oath (on behalf of her illegitimate Child Martha Bower ) saith the she this Deponent  never was Married that her said Child Martha Bower was born of the Body of her this Deponent out of lawful Wedlock on or about the 25th. day of January 1780 in Cumberland Street in the parish of St Pancras in the County of Middlesex at the a House of then occupied by a Mr Armstrong a Journeyman Carpenter That her said Child hath been Christened by the Names of Martha Bowar in the parish Church of St Mary le bone in the said County That her said Child hath never been bound an Apprentice or otherwise obtained or become intilled rents any subsequent legal settlement and that she this Deponent is unable to provide for the said Child without Assistance

Sworn the 28th July 1792 before us} M Addington Thos. Gordone

Lydea Turtle
