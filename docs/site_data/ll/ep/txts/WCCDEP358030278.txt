Westmr.  John Oakley removed with Elizabeth his Wife from the Parish of Brington in the County of Northton by Order dated the 29th. Day of Octr. last upon Oath saith he intermarried with his sd. Wife Elizabeth in the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Town of Northampton on or about the 31st. Day of March last Says his late Father Wm. Oakley Whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Chapman at No bottle in the Parish of Brington in the County of Northampton aforesd. for one Year and upwards as this Examinant has been informed and verily beleives Says his said late Father never afterwards rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif Says he was by Indenture bearing Date the 21st. Day of Decr. 1714 bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to John Cross a Taylor in the Parish of Harlston in the said County of Northton and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with the sd. John Cross in the sd. Parish of Harlston Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes

lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones .

The Mark of John Oakley
