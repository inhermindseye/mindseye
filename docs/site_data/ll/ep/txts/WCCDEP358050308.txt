Middx  Martha Atwaters  wido . of John Atwaters who dyed abt. 19 Yrs. ago upon Oath saith that about Seven Yrs. ago being then a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Davis a Sheriffs Officer for one Year and upwds. at the yearly Wages of £3..10..0 and that she lived the last Six months of the said Term with the sd. Mr Davis in Tennis Court near Middle Row in the parish of St Andrew Holborn above the barrs in the County of Middx and has not since she left her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in an other place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn 7th. Sepr. 1750 before} Geo: Errington

The Mark of Martha Atwaters
