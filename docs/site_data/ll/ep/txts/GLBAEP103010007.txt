Middlesex, to wit, Mary Jenkinson maketh Oath and Saith, that some time in the Month of November in the Year of our Lord One Thousand Seven Hundred and Eighty Four, at the House of Mr. Morgan the Corner of Peter's Court , Rosemary Lane , in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child, since Baptised by the Name of Elizabeth Lampard , that the said Bastard Child is likely to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate that Thomas Lampard late of the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County, Shoemaker , did beget the said Child, and that the said Thomas Lampard died on or about the Nineteenth Day of March last

Sworn this Day of June 1790 before me }
