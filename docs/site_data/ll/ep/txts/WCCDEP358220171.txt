Middlesex & Westminster }  Martha Hillton Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of William Hillton gone from her with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes upwards of four Years Ago and by whom she hath one Child now living namely John William Aged about two Years Says that her said Husband Served Six Years the latter part of his Apprenticeship with one Mr: Harding a Hatter in little East Cheap in the Parish of Saint Andrew Hubbard in the City of London (as her said Husband has often informed her and which information she this Examinant Verily believes to be true) and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th: day of August 1770 before us} R. Butler

The Mark of Martha Hillton

Witness Will: Hughes
