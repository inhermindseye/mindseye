Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Jane Young  Widow of Richard Young upon her Oath Saith that in her Husbands Lifetime she and her Husband rented and lived in a House in Gardiners Lane in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westminster at the Yearly Rent of twelve Pounds for upward of forty Days that since quitting such House she has not lived in any other House out of the said Parish of Saint Margaret or rented any House or Tenemt of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds nor done any Act whereby as she best knows and believes she may have acquired a Subsequent Settlement and further this Examinant Saith that she is Sixty four Years of Age or thereabouts and that she is poor and is great Distress and is unable to maintain herself, and has not any Child or Children now living who are any way maintained by

by or live with her this Examinant

Sworn this 5th Day of Jany 1791 before us N Bond Sampn Wright

her Jane Young Mark
