Middx (ss)  Swear to St. Clemt.  Richard Coombe aged about 48 years upon his Oath Saith That he lived as an Hired yearly servt . with Mr. Cawley of Norfolk Street at in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex apothecary for about 3 years that he quitted his said Service abt. 27 years ago that since he quitted his said Service he has not lived as an Hired yearly Servt. for 12 months paid Taxes or rented Lodgings of £10 a year nor done any other Act matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinant Knowledge of Belief he may have gained a settlement elsewhere orse than in the sd. Parish of st. Clemt: Danes

Sworn this 4th day of Novr: 1784 before me  Edward Hodsoll

Richd Coombe
