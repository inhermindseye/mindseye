Middlesex ss}  Ann Amery  Widow of Richard Amery deced Maketh oath That her Said late Husband has informed her (& which She believes to be true) That her Said husband was hired and lived as a hired Servant for the Space of two Years & upwards at the Yearly wages of Six Pounds with one William Jesser a Slop Seller in the Parish of St Mary Hill in the City of London he being at that time an Unmarried Man & had no Children then living
