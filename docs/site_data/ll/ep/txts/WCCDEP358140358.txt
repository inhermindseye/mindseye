Middx.  Dorothy Stichton aged abt. 29 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Walter Stichton who has been dead abt. Nine Years says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Pennington at the Crooked Billet she backside of St. Clements Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for 4 years at the Yearly Wages of £5 and hath Quitted said Service  upwds. of One year & hath not Since Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Yr.

Sworn this 8th. of March 1760 . Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Dorothy Stichton
