Middlesex ss.  Elizabeth Pettifer the Wife of John Pettifer (who has left her) maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West in the City of London on or about the Sixth Day of May 1787 that her said Husband about Two Years ago Rented a House of Sixteen Pounds a Year in Queen Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & lived in the same upwards of Three Months and was charged to & paid Poors Rate for the same that he has not since he quitted the said House Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement, that she has Four Children Namely John aged about Ten Years. George aged about Five Years Richard aged about Three years and Mary aged about Nine Weeks the lawful issue by her said Husband-That her said Husband has deserted her and does not allow her any thing towards the support of herself & her said Children and that she has been, and Two of her said Children Namely George & Richard now are chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 25th Day of April 1798 before H: Reynell

her Elizh. Pettifer Mark
