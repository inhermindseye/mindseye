Westmr.  Thomas Peach Aged about Forty two Years, Upon Oath says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Jacob Vale in Major Forbes Passage in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster  Brushmaker , and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year, says he hath a Child by Sarah his late Wife deceased (to wit) Alice Aged about Ten Years

Sworn the 25th. day of May 1768 , Before W Kelynge 

Thomas Peach
