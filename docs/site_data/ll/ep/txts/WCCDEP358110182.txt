Westmr.  Patrick Egan upon Oath Saith he Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds in Bennets Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . Lived therein near two Years in and paid to the poor Rate of the sd. house and that he has not since he Rented the sd. house Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 20th. Febry 1753 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Pattk Egan
