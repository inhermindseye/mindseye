Westmr.  Sarah Lodge Aged about Sixty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Philip Lodge with who has been dead about Nine Years and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish of Saint Brides London above Forty Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Except Two who are provided for Says her said late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Joseph Brewer at the Black Rasen in fetter Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn London for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds as she belives hath quitted his said Service upwards of Forty Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain Subsequent Settlement neither as this Examinant since her said Husband Death.

Sworn this 23d. day of March 1773 before Us R Butler Tho. Kynaston

her Sarah Lodge Mark

Witness E: Field
