Middlesex ss.  Jane Harper the Widow of James Harper decd. & formerly the Widow of John Waugh her first Husband decd. maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said first Husband John Waugh at the Parish Church of St. Botolph Aldgate London about Eleven years ago that her said Husband at the time of his Marriage with her, lived in & Rented a House of Thirty Pounds a year in Lower Eastsmd . in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & was charged to and paid the Poors Rate for the same, that he died in the said House on or about the Tenth day of Feby. 1789 . and that she has Two Children Namely John aged about Ten Years & James aged about Nine Years the lawful issue by her said Husband who have not done any Act to gain a settlement in their own right, And this Deponent further saith that she was lawfully Married to the said Jas. Harper her Second Husband at the Parish Church of St. Botolph Aldgate aforesd. on or about the Fifth Day of Novr. 1789 until which time she lived in & Rented the House her former Husband Rented in Eastsmd . aforesd. that her said last mentd. Husbd. James Harper lived in & rented the said House about Eight Months after his Marriage with her as aforesd. and did not after he quitted the Same Rent any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or pay any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlemt. nor has she since her said Husbands Death done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right, that she has a Child named Jane aged about Four years the lawful issue by her said last mentd. Husband

Sworn this Day of March 1798 before}
