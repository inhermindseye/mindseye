Middx.  Elizabeth Johnson aged about 38 Years Upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Summerfield a peruke Maker in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for three Years or there abouts at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds, hath quitted said Service about twelve Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 27th. day of March 1765 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth. Johnson
