Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Naish aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Miss Pearce in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service only about One Month And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Macdonald an Apprentice to one Mr. Pearce a Taylor in Carey Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of June last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Macdonald is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Decr. 1794 } J Hind

Catherine Naish

John Macdonald about 20 years of Age Rather Tall & Thin dark Brown Hair down his Back fair Complexion

Man never taken Qy. what become of the Child

Child born the 7th. March 1795 named Sarah Died in the House  23d. March 1795
