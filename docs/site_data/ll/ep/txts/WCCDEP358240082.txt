Charlotte Stafford  Widow & two Children

St. Clement Danes April 11th. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  Stafford Charlotte Wid of Jno } St CD  (to wit) Carolina, Barrett aged 37 years or thereabouts Wife of Barnabas Barrett Maketh Oath on behalf Charlotte Stafford Widow and saith that, her late Husband John Stafford this Deponent's Beather lived as a Covenant or hired yearly Servant with Mr William Richardson late of Clements Lane but then of New castle Court in the paid Parish of St Clement Danes for between her said three years and Lodged and Boarded in his said Masters House in Newcastle Court aforesaid, but what Wages he received for such Service this Deponent doth not know by Information or otherwise and never obtained our Subsequent legal Settlement, as she best known & believes And the said Charlotte Stafford for herself Maketh Oath and saith that she was Married to John Stafford this Deponents late Husband at the Parish Church of St Clement Danes on or abt. the 28th. April 1779. that her said Husband was unfortunately drowned or she was Informed & believes on the 1st. day of April last as he was going down the River, that she hath two Children now living by her said Husband Namely John born on the 12th. day of January 1782. and William born on the 12th day of November 1784, that the said Children are now deponent on her the said Charlotte Stafford the last mentioned Deponent

who further saith her said Husband hath never obtained any Settlement since her knowledge of him And that She in Poor and unable to maintain herself and said Children without Assistance

Sworn the 11th day of April 1792 before me} R Butler

her Carrolina Barrett Mark
