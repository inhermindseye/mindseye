Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Mundin aged about Thirty five years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or Tenemt. of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes says she has three Illegitimate Children by one Thos. Smith lately deceased namely George aged upwards of Eight years (now Provided for) Susannah aged about five years and Elizth . aged upwards of three years Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Backhouse a Harpsichord Maker in German Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of one year at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about 13 years ago since which time she hath never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry. 1784  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

her Jane Mundin Mark
