Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Daniel Simmons aged about 75 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a house in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Sixty Pounds the he hath quitted said house about Seventeen years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of June 1798 before us } Robinson R. Ford

Daniel Simmons
