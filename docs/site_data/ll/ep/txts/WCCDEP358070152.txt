Westminster to wit Mary Worrall Aged about forty Six Years on behalf of Daniel Henry and Rebecca Infants by Daniel Henry Wood their Father lately deceased on her Oath says that the Sd. Daniel Henry Wood her Son aged about Thirteen Years and Rebecca Wood her Daughter Aged about Ten Years were both born in George Court Rupert Street in the Parish of Saint James in Liberty of Westminster and Says that her said Two Children Never did any Act whereby to gain any Settlement.

Sworn this 11th. day of Decr. 1773 before  Sampn Wright  Wm. Kelynge

Mary Worral
