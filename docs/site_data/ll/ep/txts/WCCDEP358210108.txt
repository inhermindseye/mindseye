Westmr.  Elizabeth Clark aged about 22 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Thorpe a Baker in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds, hath quitted said Service upwards of two Years, and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 22d, day of November 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant .

The Mark of Elizabeth Clark
