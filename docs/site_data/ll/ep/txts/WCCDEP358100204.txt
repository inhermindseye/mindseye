Middx to wit Esther Pulley Aged about fifty Six Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of George Pulley with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Great Ealing in the County of Middlesex upwards of Twenty Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Namely John Aged about 17 Years now provided for and Esther Aged about 19 Years unprovided for or did she ever gain any other Settlement than hereafter mentioned and further Says that after her said Husbands Death she Rented and lived in a House in Little Russell Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and upwards that she this Examinant hath quitted said House upwards of four Years Ago and hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of April 1784 before Thos: Parker James Croft

Hester Pulley
