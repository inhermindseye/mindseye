Middx & Westmr}  Martha Stephenson Aged Six four Years on thereabouts on her Oath Says She is the Widow of Edmund Stephenson who has been dead upwards of Twenty five Years Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children She lived a hired Servant with Thos. Maples a Taylor in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for fifteen Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five pounds and after She quitted said Service never Rent Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th Day of Decr 1768 Before me} Tho Lane

Martha Stephenson
