Middx.  Mary Jaques aged abt. 60 Years Lodging at Mrs. Rattinbury's in Lyons Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . Upon Oath says abt. 14 Years ago she intermarried with her present husband Joseph Jaques in Lincolns Inn Chapel that her said husband hath absconded from her abt. Ten Years and before he left this Examinant he Rented a house in Broad Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £20 hath Quitted said house about Ten Years and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 1st. Sepr. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Jaques
