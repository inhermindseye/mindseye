Middx  Joseph Berry aged about Sixty one Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the Year 1749 and by whom he hath No Children says he was bound an Apprentice to and served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Willim- Seed a Lace Weaver in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and after he was on of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23rd- day of Octr. 1778 before  Abrm Baley  John Goodchild

Joseph Berry
