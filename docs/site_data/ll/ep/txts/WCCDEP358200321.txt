Westmr.  Ann Maddison aged about 29 Years upon Oath says she was never married and that she was bound Apprentice by the Overseers of the poor of Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx to Robt. Routledge of Carey Street in the said Liberty of the Rolls Taylor and served about Six Years of her said Apprenticeship and then her said Master removed into Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. says she served about Six Months of her said apprenticeship in Portugal Street aforesaid and then the said Robt. Routledge died; says she never was Assigned over to any Person Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr. 16th. 1765 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Ann Maddison
