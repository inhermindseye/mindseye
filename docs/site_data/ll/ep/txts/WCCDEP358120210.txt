Middx  Eliz. Deacon aged abt. 40 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant wth St Joseph Aylesse  Bart in Carey Street in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx for three years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five pounds for the first year and Six pounds by the year afterwards that she quitted her sd. Service upwards of 15 years ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Octr 9th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark  of Eliz Deacon
