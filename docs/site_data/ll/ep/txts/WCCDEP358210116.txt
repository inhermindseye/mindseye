Westmr.  Sarah Terrill aged about 46 Years Upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Merrick at the Sign of the Castle the backside of St. Clements in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings, hath quitted said Service about Ten Years, and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 16th, day of December 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

Sarha Terrill
