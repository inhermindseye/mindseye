Middx to wit Thomas Steele Earle Aged about Thirty years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about Six years ago and by whom he hath three Children now living namely Thomas Aged upwards of five years who was born a Bastard in Grub Street in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate before this Examinant married to his said wife. Richard aged about three years and Mary aged about nine Months says he Served Seven years A pprenticeship with one Mr Hart a Mast Maker at Shad Thomas in the Parish of Saint John Horsly Down fair Street in the Borough of Southwarke in the County of Surry and after he was out of his said Apprentice- ship he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th day of October 1775 before R Butler

Resworn this 1 th day of October 1775 R. Butler

Thos Steel Earle
