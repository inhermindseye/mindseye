Middlesex ss}  John Newton maketh oath that he lately rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds a year in Back Street in the Hamlet of Mile End Old Town in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the sd County & was charged to And Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That he hath a Wife named Anna & two Children namely John aged five years & Margaret aged Thirteen Months the lawful Issue of this deponent by his sd Wife to whom he was lawfully Married.

Sworn this. 17. day of February 1784 before J. Hawley R Davies

John Newton
