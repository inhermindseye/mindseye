Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Barton who is ill Maketh Oath and saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that she Intermarried with John Barton her late husband at the Parish Church of Heston Middlesex about Eighteen years ago That the said John Barton served an apprenticeship of seven years under an Indenture with Humphry Barton his Father in Portsmouth street in the Parish of saint Clement D Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and hath not obtained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of Decr. 1789  Wm. Kitchiner R Butler

Benj: Newman
