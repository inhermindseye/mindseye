Middx  Mary Martin upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Neale at the Sun Alehouse near Clements Inn Gate in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & hath left her sd. Service upwards of two Years since which time she hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 22d Octr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Martin
