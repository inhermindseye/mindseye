Nil a parishioner of St Martin in the fields

St. Clement Danes May 14th 1794

Margaret Cunneh Misnamed Margt Connor

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. Clemt Danes but on Husband's being taken he sworn to a subsequent Settlemt in St Martin in the fieds Vide page 323 in the Examn- Book No. 24  Margaret Connor Wife of Thomas Connor now from her Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said Thomas Connor at the parish Church of St. Oswald in the City of Chester about 31 years ago (by Banns) that her Husband Rented and Resided in a House in Denzell Street in the parish of St Clement Danes for about seven years at the Annual Rent of £14 a year or thereabouts as this Deponent is informed and believes and quitted the same about four years ago and hath never since obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Deponent further saith that her Husband for several years past has neglected to  maintain her and she has to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 14th day of May 1794 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Margt Connor 's Mark

Witness B Newman

The Man was taken is name Thomas Cunneh he was discharged at Bow Street having gained a Settlemt. subsequent to the one here and the Woman was accordingly passed there 

Mr Allaway the Overseer admitted her without a regular Order or Examination
