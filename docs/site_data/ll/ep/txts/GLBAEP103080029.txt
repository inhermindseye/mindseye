Middx ss  Mary Thomas  Widow of Thomas Thomas deced Maketh oath that about Thirty Years ago her said late husband when a single man lived as a hired servant or Clerk by the year with Mr Agar a Merchant in the Parish of Saint Margaret Pattens in the City of London and continued in such service for about the Space of eight years & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent saith that to her knowledge informacon or belief her said late husband did not afterwards live as a hired servant by the year nor Rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor serve any Parish offices nor do any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlement nor hath this Deponent gained any settlement in her own right subsequent to her said husbands settlemt. above set forth

Sworn this day of January 1758 before us}
