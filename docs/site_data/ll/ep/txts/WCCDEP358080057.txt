Middx to wit}  John Nosier aged about thirty six Years on his Oath says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Hambridge a Hogg Butcher in Woods Close in the parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for four Years and upwards at the yearly Wages of twelve pounds and as over since lived with the said Mr. Hambridge as a Weekly Servant and further says that he never lived as a hired yearly Servant with any other person excepting Mr. Hambridge neither hath this Examinant gained any other Settlement than as above mentioned.

Sworn this 20th. day of May before} M Addington

his John Nosier Mark
