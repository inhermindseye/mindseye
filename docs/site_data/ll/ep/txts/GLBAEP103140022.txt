Middlesex ss}  Margaret Fox  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was born in the Parish of St Margaret in the Town of Ipswich in the County of Suffolk where this Deponents Father & Mother are also legally Settled that She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived any where as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 14th day of January 1774 before  Jno Sherwood Wilmot

The Mark of Margaret Fox
