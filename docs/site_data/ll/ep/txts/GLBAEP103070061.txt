Middx ss.  Margaret Compton  Wife of Walter Compton Maketh oath that her sd. husband has often informed her ye abt. twenty Years ago (he being then a Single and Unmarried Man) he lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one Mr. Emerson a Sugar Baker who then lived in the Parish of St. John the Evangelist in the City & Liberty of Westminster in ye County of Middx & that he continued in Such Service for the Space of two Years & gained a legal Settlement in ye sd. Parish and reced ten Pounds a Year Wages And this Deponent farther Saith that her sd. husband hath not Since rented a house of ten Pounds a Year or upwards Nor Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief And that she hath three Children Thomas aged abt. Nine Years & eleven Months John Aged abt. two Years & Walter aged abt. Nine Months the lawfull issue of this Depont. & her sd. husband & Saith that her sd. husband is now obtained a prisoner in his Majesties Prison of New gate -

Sworn this 15 day of April 1752 before us  Rd: Ricards  Thos: Quarrill

his Margaret Compton Mark
