Middx ss  James Dunstall Son of James Dunstall deced Maketh oath that his said Father has informed this Depont. that he this Deponts sd Father duly Served. his apprenticeship with Stephen Peirce a Cooper of the Parish of Allfriston in the County of Sussex And that by Means thereof this Deponts said Father gained a legal settlement in the same Parish And this Dept saith that his said Father did not afterwards gain any subsequent settlemt. to this Deponts knowledge information or belief And this Depont. saith that besides this Depont. his said father left behind him two other Children this Depont Brother and Sister to wit Martha aged about Six years and upwards and Edward aged about three years and upwards the lawfull issue of this Deponents said Father and Mother

Sworn this 4th day of Augt: 1755 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

his James Dunstall Mark
