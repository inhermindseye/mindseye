Middx  John Boore aged about 60 Years upon Oath saith that he rented a Shop in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for the Space of about Six Years at the Rent of Six shillings a Week Also says he was charged and paid to the Poor for the Same and that during all the time he kept such Shop he lodged in or about Claremarket in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes Quitted the Same about thirty Years and hath not since kept House Rented a Seperate Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate nor been a hired Yearly Servant for One year.

Sworn the 5th. day of Sepr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

John Boore
