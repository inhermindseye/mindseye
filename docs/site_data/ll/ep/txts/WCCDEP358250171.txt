Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Susannah Demaine aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Atkinson who kept a Cook Shop in Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Fourteen Months at the yearly wages of Five pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th day of May 1795 } N Bond

her Sush. Demaine Mark

Witness B Newman
