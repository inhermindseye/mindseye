Middx  The Examination of John Johnson passed from the Parish of Newport Pagnell in the County of Bucks by Order bearing Date the 20th- Day of January last Taken the 2d. Day of February Who upon his Oath saith that he served four Years the latter part of his Apprentiship to with one Mr. Piper in Blackamoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Coop & further on his Oath saith that he hath not since he left his said Master Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

John Johnson
