Westmr.  Jno. Simpson upon Oath Saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Coll Mading for the Space of three Years at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for the first Year and Six pounds by the year afterwards and that he lodged the last two Months of his sd Service at his Masters Stables in Ham Yard in the Parish of St. George  Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr ., that he left the sd. Coll Mading about Six Years ago and has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. (in any part of Great Britain) for one Year

Sworn March 30th. 1753 before} Sam Pechell  Tho Lane

John Simpson
