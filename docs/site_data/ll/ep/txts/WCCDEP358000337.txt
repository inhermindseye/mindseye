Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Hannah Weaver aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that about 21 years ago she Intermarried with Wm. Weaver  at the her Present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch and by whom she hath 4 Children living Vizt. Wm. aged about 17 years Rebecca aged about 12 years Sophia aged about 10 years & Richard aged about 7 years-that her said Husband Rents a Lodging of Mr. Morgan a Hatter in the Strand in the Precinct Parish of St. of the Savoy at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds that her said Husband has lived there since Midsummer day last that her said Husband Refuses to maintain this Ext. & his Son Wm

Sworn this 8th- day of August 1786 G Mercer Jno Martin

Hannah Neaver

Nesworn this 9th. Augt- 1786  Jno Barnfather G Mercer

Hannah Weaver
