Westmr.  Mary Carey Aged upwards of Sixty four Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Henry Carey who has been dead about Twelve Years; says her said Husband Rented a House (known by the sign of the White Horse) in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for several Years, at the Yearly Rent of Thirty six Pounds and Upwards and after he quitted same never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, neither hath she since her said Husbands Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 1st. day of April 1768 , Before} 

The Mark of Mary Carey
