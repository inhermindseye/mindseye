Middx.  Ann Cowhone aged abt. 52 years upon Oath says that She is the Widow of Robt. Cowhone & being without Child or Children lived a hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Shaw at the White horse by Clemts. Inn Gate in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for one year at the yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. Eight yrs. & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a yr. paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 23d. of July 1757 before}

Anna Cowhone
