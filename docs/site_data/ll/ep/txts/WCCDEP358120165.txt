Middx  Sarah Newman upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of Edwd. Newman (gone from her) to whom she was married at the Fleet abt. two Years since & by whom she has one Child Sarah aged nine months Says she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband was born in Hide Street in the Parish of St. Barthw . in the City of Winchester & never was an apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever rented ten pounds by the Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn July 27th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Newman
