Aldgate Middx

Jonathan Waller a Fellowship Porter Maketh Oath that he Servd Seven Years Apprenticeship to Thomas Godard of Little Distaf Lane in the Parish of Saint Margaret Moses in the City of London  Baker , And that he never rented a House of ten pound a year, nor never paid any parish taxes, nor Servd any Parish Office, nor never gaind any other Settlement to his knowledge Since his Servitude of a Apprenticeship as abovesaid hath a wife  Elizabeth

Sworn 17th day of December Ano Dom 1746 before Robert Dennett Richd: Ricards

Jonathan Waller
