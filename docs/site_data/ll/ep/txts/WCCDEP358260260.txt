Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Arabella James aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that that on or about the 23rd. day of April 1788 she Intermarried with Luke James her present husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Epsom by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. John aged about Nine years That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Cherry Garden Street in the parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry for Seven Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Seventeen pounds that he hath quitted said house about Twenty Months since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she is poor and unable to maintain herself and her said Child and is now Chargeable to the parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 23rd. day of March 1798 before us} N Bond Rupt. Clarkes

her Arabella James mark

Witness B Newman
