Westmr.  Eliza. Leach upon Oath saith she in the Widow of John Leach deceased by whom she hath a Daughter named Ann aged about 15 Years Says her said late husband lived in and Rented a house in Craven Yard  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds for Six Years or thereabouts & quitted the same about two Yrs. ago Says her sd. late husband never after he left the said house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since the Death of her said late husband rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 18th. 1747 before} Wm. Jones

Eliz Leach
