St. Clement Danes April 3d 1792

Middlesex and Westminster } Passed to Lewisham Kent Rended there last 40 days (to wit) Sarah Harbert Aged 29 Years and upwards Upon Oath Saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Barson in Water Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for very near two years at the Annual Wages of Ten Guineas that she quitted that
