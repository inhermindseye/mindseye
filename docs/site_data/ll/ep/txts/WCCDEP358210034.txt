Westmr.  Elizabeth Bevan aged about 33 Years Upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Parker a Linnen Draper in the Strand in the Parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. for three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds; hath quitted said Service about five Years, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn March 11th, 1766 before} Fran. Cary  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Eliz: Bevan
