Middx  Eliz: Wilmott aged abt. 25 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Towell at the Anchor in Feathers Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx for the Space of Two Years & Four Months at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds & hath left the same abt. 9 Yrs. that since she left her said Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 26th. of June 1742 before J Bromfeild W. Hayton

The Mark of Eliz: Wilmott
