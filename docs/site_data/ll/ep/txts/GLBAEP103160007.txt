Middlesex ss}  Rebeccah Baker the Widow of John Baker deced Maketh oath That her said late husband divers Years ago rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds Situate in Hatcliff Highway opposite to Farmer Street in the Parish of St Paul Shadwell in the sd County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her Sd husband did not afterwards rent a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That her Said husband resided in the Said house for the Space of Ten Years And that she this Deponent since his death hath never been Married nor done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 3d day of February 1776 before  Geo: C. Smith  Wm.. Blackmore

The Mark of Rebeccah Baker
