Saint Clement Danes 4th. Septr. 1787

Middlesex & Westminster to wit, James Tallant aged Sixty Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a House in Bedford Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster at the yearly Rent of ten Guineas for about four years that he has quitted the same about Sixteen Years ago and since that he has not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge he may have gained any subsequent Settlement and that he is poor and Sick and unable to maintain himself

Sworn this 4th. Septr. 1787 before D Walker Rup: Clarke

James Tallant
