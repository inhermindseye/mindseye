Westmr. to wit Jane Stevenson Aged about Twenty one Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Benjamin Stevenson kept the Sign of the Swan at Ulsbiup in the Parish of Ulsbiup in the County of Leicester for sevl. Years but being her Fathers own Estate cannot say what was the Yearly Value but bef: it was worth to be let Thirty Pounds P Ann and where her said Father died And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one Geo: Abingdon a Musician Brother to Wm. Abingdon a Dancing Master in Beaufort Buildings who she believes Secrets himself in Town had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at different times for upwards of fifteen Months last Past and has Cohabited with the said George Abingdon ever since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as afsd. And that the said Geo: Abington and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1770 before me W Kelynge

Jan Stavenson
