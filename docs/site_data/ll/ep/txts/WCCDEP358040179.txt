Westmr.  Susanna Harding aged abt. 23 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she never was married and that upwds. of one Year ago she lived a Yearly Servt. with the Rd. Mr. Bellinger in Plow Yard in Seething Lane in the Parish of Alhallows Barking  London at the Yearly Wages of £4 for one Year and upwards Says she has not since she quitted the sd. Mr. Bellinger Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 30th. 1740 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Susa. Harding
