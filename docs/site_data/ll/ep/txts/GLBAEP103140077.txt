Middlesex ss  Ann Fleming the Widow of George Fleming deced maketh oath That She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her said late husband nor Can She give any Account as to the Settlement of her late Father Jeffery Powers deced That her late Mother Ann Powers has informed her that She lived as a hired Servant for a year while a Singlewoman with one Captain James in the Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Depont. was born in the said parish & was never bound Apprentice or ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself Save What She might have acquired by marriage with her said late husband whose Settlement is unknown to her.

Sworn this 3d day of January 1775 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Fleming
