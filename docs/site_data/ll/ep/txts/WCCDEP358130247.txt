Middx.  Margt. Harrison Wife of Joseph Harrison upon Oath says She Intermarried with the sd. Joseph Harrison at the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn abt. Eight Years ago says She hath One Child by her said Husband (to wit) Humphry aged abt. Seven Years that her said Husband is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England Says before her Intermarriage with the said Joseph Harrison & whilst a Single Woman She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Parrey at the Taylor's Arms in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx. for two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds

Sworn the day of 175 before}
