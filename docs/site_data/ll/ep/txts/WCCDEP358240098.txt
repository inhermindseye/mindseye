St Clement Dane May 5th 1792

Middx & Westminster }  St Paul Covent Garden  (to wit) Ann Matthews  Singlewoman aged 24 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith That She never was Married, That she was hired as a yearly Servant at the Annual Wages of £7 by Mr. Carter at No: 6 in Tavistock Street in the parish of St Paul Covent Garden , in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx Middlesex on the 20th. day of July in the year of our Lord 1790, and That he lived with him the said Mr Carter at No: 6 Tavistock Street aforesaid under such hiring from the said 20th July 1790 untill the 20th day of July in the year 1791 & then quitted his Service, That She hath not Since obtained any legal Settlement by any Act or means whatever (to her knowledge or belief) And that She is now Pregnant unable to Support herself without Assistance

Sworn 5th May 1792 before before Us} Edw. Read  Wm. Hyde

Ann Matthews
