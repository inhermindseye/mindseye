Westmr ss  Sarah Starling Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of William Starling (gone from her) with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the field in the Liberty of Westminster about Ten Years ago, and by whom she hath Three Children now living viz: Isabella aged about nine Years, Archibald aged about four Years and James Aged about Eighteen Months, says her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Christian Lahn a Taylor in the Parish of Clapham in the County of Surry and served the said Christian Lahn for five Years and and Upwards who Assigned him over to one Mr. Henry Desterberg a Taylor in Cecil Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and served the said Mr. Henry Desterberg two Years the latter part of his said Apprenticeship , and after her said husband was out of his said Apprenticeship he has never Rented house paid Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 12th day of June 1775 before  M Addington Sampn. Wright

her Sarah  Starling Mark Witness James Viger
