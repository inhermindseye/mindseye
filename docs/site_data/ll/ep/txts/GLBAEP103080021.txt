Middx ss  Elizabeth Drewett  wife of Edward Drewett (who is gone away from her to places Unknown) maketh oath that her said husband Rented a Farm about four years ago at Kensington Growel Petts in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middx and paid One hundred and forty pounds by the year Rent for the same & paid all Parish Taxes & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent saith that her sd husband has nor since Rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year or upwards Nor Served Any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement to this Deponents knowledge or belief And that She hath two Children to wit Stephen Edward Aged about two Years and three quarters and Thomas Aged about Nine Weeks the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her sd husband And that her said husband is lately gone away from her to places Unknown to her

Sworn this 14 day of October 1757 before us Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Elizth Drewet
