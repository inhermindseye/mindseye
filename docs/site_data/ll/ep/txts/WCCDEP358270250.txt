Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Martha Rogers aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Walton at the Bull in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Two years at the yearly wages of £4:10 and that since she quitted said Service she has done no Act to gain a subsequent settlement And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that on the 23d. day of December 1791 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr Hunt in Milford Lane in the said Parish which hath since been Christened by the name of Richard That one Richard Portsmouth a Coal heaver who did work on the Wharfs the Cotton of Milford Lane is the father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Richard Portsmouth is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Octr. 1795 } Rupt: Clarke

her Martha Rogers Mark

Childs name Richd. Rogers Admitted in the House with the Mother 24th. Octr.1795 Disd. 26th. Decr. 1795 with the Mother Qy. any Allowance to the Mother Man never taken
