Middx.  Joseph Wilson aged abt. 73 Years ( removed from the Parish of St. Bride in the City of London to the Parish of St. Clement Danes ) Upon Oath says he intermarried with Isabella his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Ann Soho abt. Thirty Six Years ago says before his said Marriage and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr John Shudall a Robemaker next Lyons Inn Gate in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middlesex for Ten Years at the Yearly Wages of £8 Diet Washing and Lodging and Quitted said Service Upwards of Forty Years ago after which he Rented a Shop in the Kings Bench Walks in the Inner Temple London for Fourteen Years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds a Upwards and after he Quitted said Shop never Rented Ten Pounds by the year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn Octr. 28th. 1760 . before me} Barth: Hammond

Jos. Wilson
