Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Green aged about Thirty years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Joseph Green who died about Five years ago and by whom she hath no Children Living says Her said Late Husband before His Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst He was a Singleman He lived a Hired yearly Servant with One Mr. Beoch at Eastbrough in the Parish of Richmansworth in the County of Herts upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas and After quitting his said Masters Service He never gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since Her said Husbands Death and further On her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born and further says that One Archibald Roxburgh a Shoemaker in Clare Court did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of Her Body at Her Lodgings No. 3 Browns Buildings Drury Lane for upwards of One year and an half past at One which times she got Her with Child of the Child or Children of which She is now Pregnant and that the said Archibald Roxburgh and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 31st. day of Augt 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner

Ann Green
