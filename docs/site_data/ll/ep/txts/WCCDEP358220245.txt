Westmr. ss  Martha Smith Aged about Sixty Two Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Smith who has been dead about Seven Years Says that being a Widow she rented and lived in a House at Knights Bridge in the Parish of Saint Margarets in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds hath quitted same about Three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1771 before R Butler W Kelynge

her Martha Smith Mark

Witness. George Langley
