Middx  Henry Roberts maketh Oath and Saith that about Thirty three Years ago he was Bound Apprentice by Indre for Seven Years to Mr . Phelix Oneal by St. Katherines Dock in the precinct of St. Katherines in the County of Middx Cork Cutter and served all his Apprentiship there and gained no Subsequent Settlement since to his knowledge

Sworn this 3d. day of January 1744 Before Robert Dennett

Henry Roberts
