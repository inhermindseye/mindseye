Middx  Robert Heather upon Oath saith he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes and that he hath been Informed and verily believes his father Robert Heather decd was born in the Parish of Webston in the County of Surry and that he can give no further Account of his Settlement

Sworn Decr 28th. 1750 before  Tho Lane

Robt Heather
