Westmr. to wid Elizth. Carrier the Younger Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her late Father Robert Carrier before his Intermarriage with the Mother lived a hired Yearly Servant with with one Mr. Knowles at the rising Sun Vere Street Clare Markett and after quitting his said Service never gained any Subsequent Settlement as her sd. Mother has often Informed her and wch. Information she verily believes to be true And further says that on the Second day of August 1772 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. Standage a Broker in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which has since been baptized by the Name of George Says that one George Warren a Brushmaker who now works at One Nor. Richards in Cannon Street London is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge some time in the Month of November 1771 but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at Sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said George Warren and no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 2d. day of June 1773 before  Sampn. Wright

her Elizth. Carrier mark
