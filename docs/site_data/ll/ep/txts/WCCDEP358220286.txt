Westmr. to wit Thos. Walter aged about Thirty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Jane his Now Wife at the Parish of Iver in the County of Bucks about Six Months ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says he served five Years the first part of his Apprenticeship one John Henson a Baker in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and afterwards went to Sea & has been in England about Six Years and after serving the said Mr. Henson five Years he never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 1st. day of August 1771 before me W Kelynge

his Thos. Walter Mark
