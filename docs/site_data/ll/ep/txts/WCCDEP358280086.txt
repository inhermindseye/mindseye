Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Catherine Haswell aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that on the 21st. March last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one William Hearne a Barge Master lives at the Strand on the Green in the County of Middx is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of June last and at several times since a one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she was so delivered as aforesaid that the said William Hearne is the only real true father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of April 1787 .} Wm. Kitchiner

her Catherine Haswell mark

Settled & £15.15.0 Paid in full Child Christend. Josh . sent to Enfield  13 May 1787
