Westmr.  Charlotte Hartman Aged about Sixty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Jacob Hardman who has been dead about Eight Years and with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about Twenty Nine Years ago Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Davis a Shagereen Casemaker in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily bels. to be true And further says that her said Husband after he was out of his said Apprenticeship He never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death done Any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemts.

Sworn this 16th. day of Febry. 1771 before Us} Tho Lane .

Charlotte Hartman
