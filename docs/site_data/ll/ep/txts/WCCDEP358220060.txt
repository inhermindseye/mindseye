Middx & Westmr.}  Fowke Hussey aged about forty Eight Years On his Oath Says he never was married and that he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Benion a Surgeon and Appothecary in Bow Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 22d. day of August 1769 before Us} R. Butler Petr Planck

Fowke Hussey
