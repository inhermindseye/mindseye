Middlesex ss  Mary Hawker  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years ago She was bound Apprentice to one Mr Watson of Church Yard Alley in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County to be taught & instructed by his Wife the Trade & Business of a Mantus maker & Served the whole of Such her Apprenticeship in the Said Parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent was never Married nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to her Apprenticeship.

Sworn this 29th. day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Mary Hawker
