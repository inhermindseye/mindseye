Saint Clement Danes 10th Octr 1792

St C D to wit Ann Reak  Widow upon her Oath saith that she verily believes she is near Seventy Years of Ago, That in her Husband life time the together with her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Butcher Row in the Parish Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Value of upwards of ten Pounds for upwards of one Year and that after quitting Rent House her said Husband in his Life time nor this Deponent since his Dece have not nor hath either of then rented & lived in any Messauge or Tenement in this Kingdom of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds, nor done any Act Matter or thing whereby to gain or acquire any other Settlement than in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes of and that she this Deponent is poor & in peat Distress and unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 10th Day of Octr 1792 before me } R. Butler

Ann Reak
