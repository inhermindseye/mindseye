Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} 

passd. to St Andw.- Holborn to wit Richard Blake aged about 51 years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year lived as a hired yearly Servant or Served any Apprenticeship Saith that his Mother Rented and Lived in a House in Bangor Court Shoe Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Barrs in the City of London at the yearly which was Rent of Eight Pounds and duly late  for and paid Parish Taxes due for the same that his Mother hath quitted said House about 20 years since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 18th. day of Octr. 1785  Sampr Wright J Addington

R Blake
