Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Montague Aged about 45 years upon his Oath Saith that on or about the 16th day of January 1780 he intermarried with his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch That he rented and lived in a house N 17 Porter Street in the Parish of Saint Anns in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for Nine Months at the yearly rent of £26 that he hath left the said Premises about Eleven years since which time he hath not rented any House tenement or Lodging of ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of March 1800 before us} John Collick  Benjm Gee

John Montagu
