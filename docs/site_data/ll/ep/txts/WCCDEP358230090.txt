Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Arnold aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 25th. day of September 1797 She this Deponent Intermarried with Richard Edward Arnold her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Margaret Westmr . by whom she hath one Child living vizt. Edward aged about 13 Months That her said Husband Served Apprenticeship to Mr. Brodie a Smith in Carey Street in the Liberty of the Rolls Four years under Articles of Agreement Indicted and properly executed for Three years and that after he quitted said Mr. Brodie he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Septr. 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

her Sarah Arnold mark
