Westmr. to wit Mary Janes aged about twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Lovlace Bigge  Esqr . in Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Services about three Years and an half ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 16th. day of Novr.1771 before W Kelyngs

mary Jones
