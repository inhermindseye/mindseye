Middx  Ann Smith  Wido . of Major Smith upon her Oath saith that she Intermarryed with the sd. Major Smith abt. Years ago & that her sd. late Husband in his Life time lived in and rented a House in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds for the Space of one Year & upwds- & further saith that since her sd. late Husband left the sd. House he did not in his Life rent Ten Pounds a Year pay Parish Taxes nor hath this Examinant since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Spaces of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 22th. October 1741 before}
