Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Hickman aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that on the Twentieth day of December 1794 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Griffiths in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary That one James Leister a Copper Plate Printer who lodges at No. 6 Clements Lane is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same thereon and that he and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 7th- day of July 1795 before} Rupt: Clarke

her Sarah Hickman Mark

Witness B Newman
