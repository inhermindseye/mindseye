Middx. & Westmr.}  John Breeden Aged about Sixty five years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Twelve years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living says he Rented and lived in a House known by the Name or Sign of Ben Johnsons Head in Bath Street in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for five years at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Eighteen years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 11th. day of Jany. 1781 before  Sampn Wright R Butler

his John Breeden Mark
