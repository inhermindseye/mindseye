Middx  Margt Forster upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband John Forster about 31 Years ago that her sd. husband has been gone from this Deponent about 16 Yrs. and She knows not where he now is and that she hath been informed by her sd. husband that he was born at Congerton in the County of Chester and has never heard nor can she give any further Account of his Settlement

Sworn Novr. Decr. 5th 1751 before
