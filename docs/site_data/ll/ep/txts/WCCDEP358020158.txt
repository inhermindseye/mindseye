Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Maria Matilda Swain aged about 17 years upon her Oath saith that She never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year. that she is the illegitimate Daughter of Hannah Swain and Stephen Robinson and that She was born in Love Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary at Hill in the City of London

Sworn this 17th. day of Augt. 1789 N Bond Edwd: Naish

her Maria Matilda Swain Mark
