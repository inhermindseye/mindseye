Westmr.  Joshua Ray aged about 50 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in Castle Court Chanders Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds lived therein about One Year and hath quitted same about Seven 5 Years And hath not since Rented house of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn Septr. 23d. 1765 . before R Kilsha Fran Cary

Joshua Ray
