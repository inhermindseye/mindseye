Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Ellis aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that about Twelve years ago she Intermarried with Thomas James Ellis her present husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Fulham That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Three Months at the yearly Rent of Twenty three Pounds and paid Taxes due for the same that he hath quitted said house about Eleven years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of Ap May 1798 before me} Saml Edward

her Mary Ellis mark

Witness B Newman
