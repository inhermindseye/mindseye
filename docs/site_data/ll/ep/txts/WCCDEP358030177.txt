Middx  Alice Austin  Wife of William Austin (gone from her) upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. William Austin abt. 5 Years since at the Fleet London Says the sd. William Austin before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Thos. Chatt  Distiller at the Corner of Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of one Year & upwards as this Examinant has been informed & verily beleives And hath left sd. Service upwards of 6 Yrs. Says the sd. Wm. Austin never after he left the sd. Mr Chatt Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly

hired Servant whilst a Singleman or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 8th. 1746 before}

June 12th. 1754

Says she was married to Chatt at a Church abt. 5 miles out of Croyden her husband been dead 12 Yrs. & she was his Wife 4 years & half
