St Clement Danes Novr. 16th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary le Strand  (to wit) William Hasgood aged near 49 years year and upwards Upon Oath (on behalf of Joel Sparks  singleman aged fifty Nine years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that that the said Joel Sparks he hath been informed and says verily believes that the said Joel Sparks was Married to Ann his late Wife at the parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn about 22 years ago that his said Wife died about Nine years ago that he hath a Son aged abt. 20 years who maintains himself that the said Joel Sparks he this Deponent Rented and Resided in an House in Swan yard about two years at the Annual Rent of Fourteen pounds that he quitted that place about five years ago And this Deponent saith that he informed & Verily believes the said Joel Sparks hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes Served any parochial Office or done any Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 16th. day of November 1792 before Us } N Bond Thos. Gordon

Joel Sparks
