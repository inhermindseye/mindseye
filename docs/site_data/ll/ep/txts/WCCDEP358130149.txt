Middx  Hannah Bray aged abt. 32 years upon Oath says She lived a hired yearly Servant upwards of four Years with Mrs. Carter in Norfolk Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and quitted her sd. Service about five years ago & hath not lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year since Says She hath one Child named Elizabeth aged upwards of four years and And further upon her voluntary Oath says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes and that Joseph Ralph a Taylor who Lodges at Mr. Mercers in new Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes did beget the said Child or Children of which She is now Pregnant he the said Joseph Ralph having had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of July or August 1755 in the House of Mr. Mercer in now Court aforesaid
