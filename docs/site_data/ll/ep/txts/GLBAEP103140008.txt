Middlesex ss}  James Crichton  mariner maketh oath That his late Father James Crichton deced was by birth a Scotchman and never did any Act to this Deponents knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in England That this Deponts. Grandmother has informed him that this Deponents Mother was born in Portsmouth in the County of Southampton where he this Deponent was also born as he has been informed and believes That this Deponent never did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right in England and that he hath a wife named Margery and two Children namely Thomas aged about nine Years & James aged about three years the Lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said wife.

Sworn this 20th. day of November 1773 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

James Crichton
