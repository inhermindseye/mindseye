Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Hargill the wife of John Hargill maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Terrible That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served his Apprenticeship with one Charles Stewart a Carpenter in the Town of Henley upon Thames in the County of Oxford & to her knowledge or belief has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And that she was lawfully married to her said husband.

Sworn this. 11. day of March 1780 before W Blackmore

Elenor Hargill
