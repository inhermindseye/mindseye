Middlesex and Westminster}  A Swear to St. Clements  John Herbert aged 43 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he was Married to Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex upwards of 9 years ago by whom he hath 2 Children living Vizt. Jane aged about 8 years and Ann aged about 10 Months that before his Intermarriage he this Examinant lived as an Hired yearly servant with Mr. Thomas Tomkins of Norfolk Street in the strand in the Parish of Saint. Clemt Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwds. of 4 years at the wages of 10 Pounds for the first Year 12 Pounds for the 2d. £14 for the 3d. & 16 years Pounds for the 4th. year that he quitted his said service about 10 years ago that since he quitted his said service he has not done any Act M other act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a settlemt. elsewhere

Sworn this 22d day of Decr. 1784 R Butler

his John Herbert Mark
