Middlesex ss}  Mary Coins maketh oath that She was heretofore duy bound Apprentice by the Parish officers of the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the said County to William Scales of Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County untill She Shod. Attain her Age of twenty one years or be married to learn the business of a Hat & Bonnet maker which his Wife Carried on That her said Master dying about Four years ago as She now best recollects She was there upon discharged from her said Apprenticeship That although since such her discharge from her Sd Apprenticeship She hath been hired & lived as a hired Servant in different Places yet She never Continued in any one of such Services for the space of a year & She hath not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She Continued divers Years as an Apprentice under the said Indre and at the time She was discharged there from her said Master & Mistress were Inhabitants in the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 19. day of June 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Mary Coins
