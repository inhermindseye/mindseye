Middlesex Robert Dunbar maketh oath That

Middlesex ss}  Philip Hadling maketh oath that he was heretofore bound Apprentice by Indre to Harry Digance of Arundell in Sussex  Mariner for the term of three years & Served the whole of Such his Apprenticeship That his Master with his Ship making Voyages to & from America this Depont lay on board Such Ship when he returned to England and once at the begining of Such Apprenticeship the Sd Ship being moored & lying off the Dog and Duck in the Parish of St Mary Rotherhith in the County of Surry he this Deponent lay on board Such Ship there for the Space of three Months but never before or afterwards ever lay on Board such Ship for the Space of forty days in anyone Port or Place in England That he this Deponent since the expiration of his Sd Apprenticeship has not done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere That he hath a wife named Susannah Ruth to whom he was lawfully married & a Child named Sarah Aged Nine Months the lawful issue of this Deponent by his Said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 29. day of December 1784 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Philip Hadling
