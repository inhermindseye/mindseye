Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Stains Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Treasurer a Butcher in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Months ago since which time she hath gained one other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish (she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) Says that one Charles Mason a Journeyman Butcher to her said late Master is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House some time in the Months of April or May last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Charles Mason and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 18th. day of Feby. 1778 before R Butler

her Mary Stains Mark
