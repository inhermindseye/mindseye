Middlesex ss}  Edward Green the elder maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with his Father Edward Green in Doncaster in the County of york Surgeon & Apothocary & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and has hot Since done any Act to gain another Settlement and that he hath a wife named Mary to Whom he was lawfully Married.

Sworn this. 10. day of April 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

Edwd: Green
