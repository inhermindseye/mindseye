Middx  The Examination of Susanah Lashly  Widw . Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 23d. Day of April 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that upwds. of Eight Years ago and after the Death of her Husband George Lashly she was hired to & lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Newness Merchant in Berry Street in the Parish of St. Katherine Creechurch London for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds And this Examint. further saith that she hath not since that time Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & that she is now Poor and Infirm & not able to maintaine herself

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Samuel Clarke

The Mark of Susanah Lashly
