Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Phillis Somerville of No.11 Clements Lane on behalf of James Somerville  aged about Eight years and John aged about Six years Two Infants maketh Oath one Saith that they are the Two legitimate Children of James and Ann Somerville who were married on or about the 3d. day of February 1788 at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields That the said James and Ann Somerville are both now dead And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that the Said James Somerville was bound an Apprentice to his father John Somerville for Seven years that he served the last Three years and a half of his said Apprenticeship with his said father was in Mr. Burtons house in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that after the said James Somerville was out of his said Apprenticeship he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter subsequent settlement

Sworn this 4th- day of Augt. 1796 } Saml. Edward .

her Phillis Somerville Mark
