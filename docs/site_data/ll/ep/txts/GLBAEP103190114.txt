Middlesex ss}  Ann Dawn the Widow of William Dawn deced maketh oath That her Said husband who lately died at chatham has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly Served his Apprenticeship with one Mr Johnson a Bricklayer in the Town of Doncaster in the County of York & has not Since done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband.

Sworn this. 27. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Dawn
