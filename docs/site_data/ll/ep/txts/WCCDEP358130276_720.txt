Middx.  Thos. Thackthwaite aged abt. 57 Yrs. upon Oath says he Rented a house in the Parish of Eaton in the County of Bucks of the Yearly Rent of Seven Pounds Seventeen Shillings & Six pence for Nineteen Years & paid to the Poor there

Sworn Feb: 15th. 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

Thomas Thackthwaite
