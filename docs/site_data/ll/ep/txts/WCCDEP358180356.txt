Middx  Eliz. Watkyns removed from the Parish of St. Giles Martin in the Feilds by Order dated the 16th. Day of July Instant upon her Oath saith she was never married Says her Father  Thomas Watkyns deced lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Hugh Powell at the Corner of Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Taylor for the Space of 1 Year at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds Meat Drink Washing & Lodging and never after he left his Sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant Says she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 18th. July 1745 before}

The Mark of Eliz Watkins
