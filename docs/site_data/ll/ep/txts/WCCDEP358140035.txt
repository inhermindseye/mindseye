Middx.  Simon Charles aged abt. 38 Yrs. lodging in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. upon Oath says he was married to Elizabeth his Wife at the fleet London about fourteen Years ago says he served his Apprenticeship of Seven Yrs. to Robt. Howard a Taylor the Corner of Dutchy Lane in the Strand in the Precinct of the Savoy in the said County of Middx & never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant

Sworn the 7th. of Sepr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham Simon Charles
