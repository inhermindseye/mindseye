Westmr.  Eliza. Child aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Geo. Langdale  Surgeon in Old. Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Wetsmr. for one Year and upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & left her sd. Service on the 12th. of Decr. Int. And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born and that Mr. Thos. Eld (who lodges and Boards with the sd. Mr. Langdale) did Beget the sd. Child or Children on her Body the sd. Thos. Eld having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the latter End of April last & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Childrn. with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Thomas Eld & no other Person is the true and reall father there of

Sworn 14th. Decr. 1747 before} Tho: Lane .

Elizabeth Child .
