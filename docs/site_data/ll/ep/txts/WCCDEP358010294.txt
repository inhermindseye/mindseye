Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Richard Tuder aged about 80 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented a House in Southampton Buildings Chancery Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for about Seven Years at the yearly Rent of £20 that he hath quitted said House about Twelve Years since which time he has not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of June 1789 D Walker Rupl: Clarke

Richd. Tuder
