Middx  Elizth. Gibbs Aged about Twenty Eight Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed was an Apprentice Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father Wm. Gibbs Rented a House in White Chaple in the Parish of St. Mary White Chaple in the County of Middx One Year & upwards at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards & never after Rented a House or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx she being Poor & unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Thomas Evans a Journeyman Shoemaker who Lodges in Butcher Row in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of April Last and

Once afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant & that he the said Thomas Evans & no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 19th of Jany. 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Elizth. Gibbs
