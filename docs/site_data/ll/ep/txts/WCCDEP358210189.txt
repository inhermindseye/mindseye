Westmr.  Mary Parker aged about 53 Years upon Oath says She never was married, and That She lived a hired Yearly Servant upwards of a Year with one Mr. Reynolds a Butcher in Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds, and hath not since lived in any other Service nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 2d. day of October 1767 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Parker
