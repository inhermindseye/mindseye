Desertion

St Clement Danes July 4th 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  two Children (to wit) Elizabeth King aged 25 years or thereabouts Wife of Willm King Maketh Oath and saith That She was married to her present Husband Wm King on the 24th day of Septem September 1783 at the parish Church of St Bride otherwise Bridget in the City of London That since their said Marriage her said Husband rented & with her rented in a House in Bear Yard in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty for about three years at the Annual rent of £22 That they quitted it about twelve Months ago and that since  then her said Husband hath not (by any way or means whatever) obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement and This Deponent also saith That she hath two Children by her said Husband now living namely Mary  on born on the 27th day of June in the year 1784 and Elizabeth born on in about the 11th. day of Septem September in the year 1786 or 1787. both and This Deponent farther saith That her said Husband has neglected  to maintain and provide for her self this Deponent and their said Children for two Months last had and still continues so to so to do And that she is unable to support herself & Children without Assistance

Sworn the 4th day of July 1792 before me} R Butler

Elizabeth King
