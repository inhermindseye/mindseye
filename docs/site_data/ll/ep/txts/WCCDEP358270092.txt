Before Birth

St Clement Danes 9th January 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Pregnancy  Vide Settlemt. page 23 in Examn. Book No. 25 reputed Father Settlemt. Entered page 24 same Book reputed father (to wit) Elizth. Brooker aged 20 years upwards Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married & Voluntarily saith upon her Oath saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes when Born and that Edward Still of the parish of Streatham  Labourer  did beget the said Child or Children in the Body of her this Deponent he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body on or about the 15th. day of April in the year of our Lord 1790 and at several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children wherewith she is now pregnant and that he and no one else is the real true father thereof

Sworn the 9th day of January 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizth. Brooker 's Mark

Witness B Newman
