Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swers to St. C. D. to wit Mary Parker aged about 21 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Anthony Hornby in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 13 Months & upwds-at the yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted said Service about 12 Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlemt than as aforesaid

Sworn this 27th day of May 1785  Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Parker Mark
