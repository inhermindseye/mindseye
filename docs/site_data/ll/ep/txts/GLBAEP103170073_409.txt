Middlesex ss}  Ann Mayhew the widow of Wm Mayhew  deced maketh oath that her said late husband divers years ago rented a house of Ten pounds a year & upwards in Rosemary Lane in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & in not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement elsewhere Her hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1777 before  Jno Sherwodd

The Mark of Ann Mayhew
