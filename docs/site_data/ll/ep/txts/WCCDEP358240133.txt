St Clement Danes 14th Augt 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St Clemt Danes  (to wit) John Darby aged 43 Years or and upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married (by Banns) to his late Wife Sarah formerly Sarah Colwell  Spinster at the parish Church of St. Sepulchre London on or about the Eleventh day of February 1770 that since her said Marriage he this Deponent Rented and Resided in an House in Golden's Buildings in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County for half a Year and upwards at this Annual Rent of Thirteen pounds that he quitted that House about Christmas 1780 and hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent farther saith his said Wife died about Six Years ago that he hath two Children by his said Wife now living and un provided for Namely Thomas aged 15 Years on the 23d. day of March last & Elizabeth Catherine aged Eleven Years on the 31st day of said March last.

Sworn the 14th Augst 1792 before Me } R Butler

John Darby Mark

Witness Richd Dobbin
