Middlesex ss}  Bridgett Quidley the widow of John Quidley deced maketh oath that She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband That she this Depont was born near Dublin in Ireland & never lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever bound Apprentice nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year nor ever paid any Poors Rates of Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement for herself Save what she may have acquired by her Marriage That she hath a Child now living named Elizabeth Quidley Aged about twelve years the lawful Issue of this Depont by her Said husband The which Child was born in Church Yard Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & hath not yet done any Act to gain a settlement for herself & that by reason of this Deponts poverty She is unable to provide for such Child

Sworn this 26 day of July 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Bridgett Quidley
