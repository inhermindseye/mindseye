Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Mary Ann Douglas aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Harden in St. Martins Lane in Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the county of middlesex for two year and  half at the yearly Wages of five bounds that she Rath quitted said service about two years and a half since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for twelve months or done any act to gain a subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinant knowledge information or belief

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1790 before Sampr Wright N Bond

Mary Ann Douglas
