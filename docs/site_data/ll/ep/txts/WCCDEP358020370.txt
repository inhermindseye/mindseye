St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Hannah Wells aged 26 years and upwards singlewomam Maketh Oath and saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Smith No. 10 Lyons Inn for about Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of £6 that she left that Service about twelve years since and hath not obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief - that she has been Maintained in the Workhouse of this parish since such Serviced and is now Poor and unable to maintain herself herself

Sworn the 17th day of December for 1791 before me  Wm Hitchiner

Hannah Wells
