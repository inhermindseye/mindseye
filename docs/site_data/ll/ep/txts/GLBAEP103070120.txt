Middx The Examination of Sarah Smithsan Smisan singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace lorge county of middx residing in or Near the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in ye County of Middx aforesd. this 17 day of October 1754

This Examinant on her oath says that about the beginning of the month of February last past one Thomas Deller of the parish of Thunderidge in ye County of Hertford  Farmer did Prevail with this Examinant to suffer him to lye with her at this Examinants Mothers house Mrs. Elizabeth Smisan And the said Thomas Deller had Carnal knowledge of this Examints. body & so had several times whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she now goeth with and is Pregnant And this Examinant Saith that the sd. Child or Children of which she now goeth with & is pregnant as aforesd. is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards to be Chargeable to the sd parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate she not being able to provide for the same And

that the sd. Thomas Deller is the true and only Father of the sd. Child or Children so likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesd. And lastly saith that this Examinacon is Voluntarily And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whomsoever

Sworn this 17th day of October 1754 before  Richd: Ricards

The Mark of Sarah Smisan
