Middx  Martha Ingham aged about 60 Years Lodging at Mr. Trotters in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of John Ingham who has been Dead about Eighteen Years That since his Death she this Examinant lived in and rented a House in Round Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for the Space of Four Years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds Quitted the Same about Six Years That she hath not since kept House paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate

Sworn the 23d. day of July 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

Martha Ingham

Upon Mr. Figg's Complaint
