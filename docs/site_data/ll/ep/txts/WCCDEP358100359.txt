Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Geo: Blooms bury to wit Wm Gowers aged about 53 years on his Oath says he Rented and lived in a House in Little Russell Street Bloomsbury in the parish of Saint Georges Bloomsbury for upwards of Six years at the yearly Rent of £22 and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Ten years ago since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 17th. day of Febry 1785  Sampn Wright  Thos: Gilbert :

William Gowers
