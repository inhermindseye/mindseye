Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Loyd Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired yearly Servant with Mr. Webb a Comedian in Bedford Street  Covent Garden in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of twenty Months at the yearly Wages of five Guineas hath quitted her said Service in March last since which time she hath gained no other Settlement by living a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise housoever

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1783 W Addington James Croft

Ann Loyd
