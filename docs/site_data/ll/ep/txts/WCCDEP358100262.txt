Middx & Westmr}  pd. to Geo Hanr Sq.  Hannah Slaughter  Singlewoman , aged twenty four Years or thereabouts, upon her Oath Saith That she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with the Bishop of Peterborough in Conduct Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas, for upwards of twelve Months That she quitted her said place between three and four Years ago, That since she so quitted her said Place, she has not lived any where else as an hired Yearly Servant for the Spare four Year together at any one time that she has not Rented any House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year or paid any Taxes for done any other Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have gained any other Settlement and this Examinant further saith That she never was married

Sworn this 13th. O. tr. 1784 before me G Mercer Jno Barnfather

Hannah Slaughter
