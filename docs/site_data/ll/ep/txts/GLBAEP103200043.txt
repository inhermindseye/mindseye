Middlesex ss}  Mary Warren the widow of John Thomas Warren deced maketh oath that she has been informed by one Sarah Smith the Daughter in Law of this Deponts Said late husband & who now resides at Islington & which Information this Deponts believes to be true that the Said John Thomas Warren heretofore rented a house of the yearly Rentor Value of Ten Pounds at Mount Hill in the Parish of St Luke in the sd County & resided therein for many years & did not afterwards to this Deponts knowledge information or belief rent a house of the yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or do any act to gain a settlement elsewhere That she was lawfully married to her Said husband & Since his death has not done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 12th day of August 1783 before R Davies J Hawley

Mary Warren

To her Husbands Settlemt. by Renting a House & paying Taxes
