Middx  Elizth. Keet Aged about Twenty five Years Lodging at Mr. Merediths Turner in Prince Street Opposite St. Anns Church in the Liberty of Westmr . Upon Oath Says she was never Marryed that on the Twenty Eighth Day of November 1761 She was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Wm- Oxlard in the Backside of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been Baptized by the Name of William is now Living and Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes Says One Wm- Milward who lives at Mr. Stanhopes a Stocking Trimmer in Brewhouse Yard in the Town of Nottingham did beget the said Child on her Body & that he the said Wm- Milward & no other Person is the true & real Father thereof Says she Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Harnett the upper End of North Lane in the Parish of Westgate in the Liberty of the City of Canterbury upwards of four Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service about Eight Years & hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Sert for one Year

Sworn the 10th of Septr 1764 Before Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Elizabeth Keet
