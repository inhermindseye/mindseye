Westmr.  Mary Wright aged about 72 Years Widow of Thomas Wright deceased with whom she intermarried in the Parish of St. Margaret Westminster near fifty years since, says that her said late Husband hath been dead about Twenty six Years and that she can give no account whatever of his Settlement other than he was born some here in Lancashire , says She lived a hired yearly Servant before her intermarriage with Mr. Arnold a Brewer near the Broadway Chapel in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westminster for One Year and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five pounds and hath not since lived in other Service for one year Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 29th. day of August 1767 before} Thos Kynaston  Fran. Caryl

Mary Wright
