Middlesex ss.  Isabella Turvey  Singlewoman maketh Oath that about Three years ago she was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year with Mr. Nathan of Mansel Street in the Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds and continued in his Service under such hiring upwards of One year, that she has not since She quitted his Service lived as a hired Servant by the year with any other Person in any Parish or Place for the space of One year together or done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further Saith that on or about the Eighth Day of October last in the Dwelling House of Robert Bruce Nr 2 . Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex She was delivered of a Male Child since Baptized at Aldgate Church by the Name of John David Davis And that David Davis late of Upper Eastsmd . aforesaid Dealer & Chapman (but where he now is she cannot tell) is the Father of the Said Child, And further Saith that the said Child is Illegetimate & a Bastard by reason that she never was Married to the said David Davis And that She has become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 14th Day of Novr. 1797 before R Davies H: Reynell Isabella Turvey
