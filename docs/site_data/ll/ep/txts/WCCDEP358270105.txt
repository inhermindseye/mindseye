After Birth Amey Higgs and Child

St Clement Danes April 1st. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Child's birth}  Reputed Father}  Amey Higgs aged 29 years or thereabts. Widow Upon Oath saith that  she was delivered on the 26th Day of January 1793 of a Male Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Clarke in Wych Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster  was delivered of And saith that one John Thomas who is now  in the Workhouse of this parish and is by trade a Shoemaker is the only real true father of the said Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid he and no one else having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body first in or about the Month of April 1792 and at Several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child thereon And that the said Child is become chargeable to the paid parish of  Clement Danes

Sworn the 1st April 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Amey Higgs Mark

Witness Jas. Talboys

quar to the Woman's Settlement
