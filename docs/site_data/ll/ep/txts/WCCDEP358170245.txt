Westmr.  Eliz: Harrison upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman That she lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Petree in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & hath left her sd. Service upwards of Two Months That she is Pregnant with Child or Childn. which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes this Examinant being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one Mr. Wm. Bridgens Gent did begett the said Child or Children on her Body the Sd. Mr. Bridgens living with the sd. Mr. Petree where this Examinant was a Servt. as aforesaid there became acquainted with her & had Carnal Knowledge of her Body on the 27th Day of Febry last & at Sevll other Times since at one of which Times he Got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Childn with which she is now pregnant & that he & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 30th. of July 1742 before} Tho Lane

Elizabeth Harrison
