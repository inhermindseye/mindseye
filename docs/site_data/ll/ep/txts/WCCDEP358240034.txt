Saint Clement Danes June 17th. 1791

Middlesex and Westminster }  This Order Appealed agst. & quashed}  St. Dunston West London }  Mary Ward aged Sixty two Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she is a Widow and that since she has to been a Widow and whilst show as a Single Woman not having any Child or Children she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Richard Woodham at Cliffords Inn Coffee House in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the west in the City of London for upwards of one Year at the Wages of Seven Pounds a Year and when Mr. Woodhouse quitted the said Coffee House she continued at the same Wages with his face for Mr. Eales for about a Year and an half that she quitted the said Mr. Gale is Service about two Years ago and hath not since done any hit whereby to gain any other Settlement or the best knows and believes

Sworn this 17th. June 1791 before us D Walker R Butler}

her Mary Ward Mark
