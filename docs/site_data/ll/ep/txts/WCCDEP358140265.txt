Middlesex .  Richd. Austin aged abt. 30 years upon Oath says he never was Married nor never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or yearly hired Servt. for one year. says his father Timothy Austin Rented and lived in a house in Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. known by the sign of the White horse for fourteen Months at Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds and quitted it abt. fourteen years ago. and after he quitted said house never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn this 9th. July 1759 . before}

Richard Austin
