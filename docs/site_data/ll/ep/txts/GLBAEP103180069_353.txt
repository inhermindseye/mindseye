Middlesex ss}  Thomasin Swan maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting her Parents Settlement or eithr of them That She was born in Farthing Hatch in the Parish of St Giles Without Cripplegate London & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 7 day of october 1778 before P Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Thomasin Swan .
