Middx  Sarah Holmes upon her Oath saith that on the 24th. Day of June 1734 she Intermarried with her present Husband Robert Holmes at the Fleet London that her sd. Husbd. lived in & Rented a house in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £30. or thereabouts for the Space of one Year & upwards & left the sd. House some time in Janry last & then went abroad that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented ten pounds a Year pd. pish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 12th. Novr. 1743 before}
