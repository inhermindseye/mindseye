Middx  Cathn. Owen aged abt. 74 Years upon Oath saith she is the widow of John Owen & that upwards of 20 years ago being then a Widow she rented a house of the yearly Rent of £16. in Angel Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex & lived there about one year and has not since she quitted the sd House Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Febry. 2d. 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cathn. Owen
