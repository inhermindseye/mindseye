St Clement Danes 4th April 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Mary le bone  (to wit) Sophia Wills  Singlewoman Aged Nineteen years & upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married, that She served very near two years (under Indentures of Apprenticeship duly executed) with Mrs. Lucy Stephens in Great Portland Street loading Pern John Street  Oxford Road within the parish of St Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex that she left her Apprentice said Mistresses service upwards of two years ago, then went abroad and hath not since obtained any other legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief And that she is Ill Poor and unable to provide for herself without assistance

Sworn the 4th day of April 1792 before us N Bond Sampn. Wright

Sophia Wills
