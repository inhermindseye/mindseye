Middlesex ss.  Peter Dall maketh Oath that on or about the Twenty fourth Day of July One Thousand Seven Hundred & Sixty Seven he was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture at Watermans Hall to Thomas Biffen of Customs House Court Beer Lane in the Parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London  Waterman & Lighterman , for Seven Years, that he duly served his Master there under the said Indenture about Four Years, when his said Master delivered him up his Indentures & accepted the Sum of Nine Guineas in Satisfaction for the remainder of the Term then to come & unexpired, that he was never turned over by his said Master to any other Person, nor did he serve any Person under the Indenture with the knowledge & Consent of his said Master, that he has not Since his said Apprenticeship Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement that he has a Wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully Married at Aldgate Church about Twenty three, Years ago

Sworn this 19th, Day of Novr. 1798 before R Davies H: Reynell

Peter Dall
