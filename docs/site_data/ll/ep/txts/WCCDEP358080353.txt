Middlesex to wit Sarah Collett Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Dalton in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that one Mr. Richards Clerk to on Mr. Pardon an Attorney in Wells Street in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry (as she believes) is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at the Salutation Tavern near Temple Bar the 22d. day of Sepr. last and at several different times afterwards at her Lodging in Gutter Lane London at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Richards and no other person did beget the same & that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of May 1779 before}

Sarah Collet
