Middx & Westmr.}  Issac Trueman Aged about 23 Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented house or paid Taxes says he served seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. John Pepper a pockett Bookmaker in George Street Foster Lane in the Parish of St. Leonard Foster Lane London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented house paid Taxes or done any or Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. of Janry 1777 . before Us  John Croft  Tho Barnfather

Isaac Trueman
