Westmr.  Mary Richardson  Wido . of Christr. Richardson (removed with her two Childn. Martha Richardson aged abt. 3 Yrs. & Jas. Richardson aged abt. 10 Months from the Parish of St. Nicholas Deptford by order dated the 14th. Instt.) upon Oath saith She inter- married with the sd. Christr. Richardson at the Fleet London on the 30th. Day of June 1745 and that her sd. late husband rented a house at the Corner of Clare Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £20 or thereabouts and lived there in abt. two Yrs. & has left the same abt. Seven Yrs. since which time neither her sd. late husband nor this Examinant since his Death have rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 16th. 1748 before Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Mary Richardson
