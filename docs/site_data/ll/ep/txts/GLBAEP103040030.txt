Middlesex ss.  Phebe Allen the Wife of William Allen (now in St. Thomas's Hospital ) maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her Said Husband at Christ Church Spittlefields on or about the Twenty Sixth Day of April 1788 . that her said Husband about Eighteen Months ago Rented of Robert Everson a Shop & Back room on the Ground Floor, a Back Room on the First Floor & the Cellar of his Dwelling House Nr 2 in Queen Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex a the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds and lived there about One year that her said Husband has not since he quitted the Said House Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement, that she has Three Children Namely Ann aged about Eight Years. James aged about Seven Years & Phebe aged about Four Years the lawful issue by her said Husband

Sworn this Day of May 1797. before.}
