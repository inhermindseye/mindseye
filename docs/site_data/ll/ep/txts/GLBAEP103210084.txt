Middlesex ss}  Charles Earls maketh oath that divers Years ago he rented a house of the yearly rent of Sixteen Pounds a year being a Publick house in St Catherines Court in the Precinct of St Catherines in the said County & resided thereon for Six Months That he this Depont has not since rented a house of Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or served any parish office or paid any poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this. 6th  20th. day of July 1785 before J. Hawley Robt. Smith

Charles Earl
