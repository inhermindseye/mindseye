St Clement Dane

Middlesex & Westminster}  John Ashton Aged 38 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he was bound an Apprentice by with the Consent of his Father John Ashton to John Grace  Cross Court Russell Court in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the said Liberty and County for Seven years and duly Served the whole of his said time lodging & Sleeping at his said Masters House And this Examinant saith that since his said Apprenticeship expired he hath not (by any means whatever) acquired any other legal settlement And that he is Poor and unable at present to maintain himself

Sworn the 11th. Augst. 1191 . before us L Walker Philip Dyot

John Ashton
