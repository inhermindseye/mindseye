Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Mary Partridge Aged about Twenty six Years Upon her Oath Saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired Yearly Servant at Mr. Calverts in New Street Covent Garden in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for about 7 Years at the Yearly Wages of 8 & 12 Quas. that she hath quitted said Service about four Years; since which time she has not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinant Knowledge information And belief

Sworn this 6th May 1790  Edward Gray  Tho Collin

Mary Partridge
