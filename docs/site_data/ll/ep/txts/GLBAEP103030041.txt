Middlesex ss.  Eleanor White the Widow of James White decd. Maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband a the Parish Church of St. Sepulchre about Twenty Years ago that about Nine Years ago her late Husband Rented a House of Twelve Pounds a Year the Corner of Cock Alley Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & lived in the same about Three Quarters of a Year that her said Husband did not after he quitted the Said House Rent any other House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or pay any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or do any Act to her Knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement Nor has she since his Death done any Act to her Knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this Day of 1795 before}
