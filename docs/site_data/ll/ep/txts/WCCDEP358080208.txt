Middx & Westmr-}  Elizth- Payne aged about thirty seven Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Wm- Payne (gone from her) with whom she Intermarrd- at St Ann Black Fryers London about 17 Years ago and by whom she hath three Children now living namely Ann aged about five Years Elizth - aged about three Years and Thomas aged about Eighteen Months Says her said husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a Covenanted Servant with one Mr- Dane a Taylor in Bell Court St- Martins le Grand in the Psh of St- Ann Aldersgate London upwards of three Years and after quitting his said Masters Service he never Rented house pd. Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn 29th- Day of Janry 1778 before  Jno. Barnfather  Cha. Matthews .

her Elizth- Payne Mark
