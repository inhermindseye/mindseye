Middx  Sarah Hill upon Oath Saith She is the Widow of John Hill who dyed about 9 Years Since and that her sd late husband informed her, he lived as a Yearly Servant (whilst a Singleman) with Mr. Fuller a Cowkeeper in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Pancras in the County of Middlx at the yearly Wages of Six pounds, for two Years and never after he quitted his sd Service nor has this Examint. Since his Decease Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Jany. 8th. 1753 before} Tho Lane

Sarah Hill
