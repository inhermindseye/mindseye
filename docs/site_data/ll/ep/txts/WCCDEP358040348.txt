Middx  Robt. Pope upon Oath saith he Served Years Apprentiship with Mr. Hassard a Felt Monger at Bruton in Somersetshire and never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Pla  to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 4th. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Rot. Pope
