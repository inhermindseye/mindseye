Middlesex ss}  Mary Ashley the widow of Jacob Ashley a Soldier deced maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the place of Settlement of her said late husband's Parents or eithr of them That her said husband has informed her & which information she believes to be true that he was born in the Town of Yarmouth in the County of Norfolk That to this Deponents knowledge information or belief her said husband was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right and that She this Depont Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this day of January 1774 before}

The Mark of Mary Ashley
