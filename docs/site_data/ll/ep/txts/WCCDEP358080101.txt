Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Buzvargus aged about 51 Years on her Oath says she never was married Says she Rented and lived in a House in Aff. Alley York Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of two Years at the Yearly Rent of £28 and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted her said House upwards of seven Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subseqt. Settlemt. either by renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 16th. Day of Decr. 1776 . R Butler J Hubbard

Mary Buzvargus
