Middx  Mary Tye upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she is now a Yearly hired Servant to Mr. Harper a Butterman in Clare markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middlesex & hath so been for the Space of Two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she is pregnant with Child or Children which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that Joseph Walker now of St. Giles in the Feilds in the Sd. County of Middx  Butcher did begett the Sd. Child or Childn. on her Body this Examint. on the 11th. of Octr. last being with the Sd. Joseph Walker at a Shop he then had in Clare markett where the Sd. Joseph Walker had Carnall Knowledge of her Body at which Time he gott her with the Sd. Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant & yr. he & no other Person is the true & real Father thereof

Sworne the 12th. of May 1742 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Tye
