St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster}  Swears to St. Clements to wit Sarah Wilson aged about 47 years on her Oath says that while she was a Singlewomen she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Simmonds of Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 12 Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas hath quitted said Service about five years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 20th day of June 1785 R Butler

Sarah Wilson
