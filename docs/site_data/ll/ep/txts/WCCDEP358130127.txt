Middx  Susanna Duke aged about 49 Years upon Oath says She never was married Says She lived a hired yearly Servant upwards of year with one Mr. Horn a Perukemaker in King Street in the Parish of St. George  Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex at the yearly wages of Three Pounds Meat Drink & Lodging and quitted her said Service about Eight years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year Paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 13th. Day of March 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Susa. Duke
