Middx  Ann Clarke aged about 25 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Martha Pugh who kept an Earthen Ware and Chandlers Shop in Brooks Street in the parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for two Years or there- abouts at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds hath quitted said Service about five Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 30th. day of April 1765 before M Ading Paul Vaillant

Ann Clarke
