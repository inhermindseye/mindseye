Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (ss)  Ann Toms  Widow of James Toms deceased upon her Oath saith that She was married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Peter in Norwich about twenty eight Years ago that she does not knows that her said Husband ever acquired a Parochial Settlement in his own Right And this examinant further saith that her said Husband hath informed her that he was born at the Sign of the Blue Posts in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that his father then kept the said House as a Public House And that his said Father was duly setled in and a Parishoner of the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that he had never gained any other Settlement And this Examinant further saith that she this Examinant and her said Husband were about Some Years ago papd from the City of Norwich to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that her said Husband is now dead and she this Examinant is upwards of Sixty eight Years and incapable of getting her bread.

Sworn this 7th Octr. 1790 before me Wm Kitchiner }

her Ann Toms Mark
