Middx  Judith Murphey upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her husband James Murphey at the fleet London about Ten years ago that at present the sd. James Murphey absconds from her & she knows not where he is and that about Seven Years since her sd husband rented a house in Kingshead Court Drury Lane  at the in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Eleven Guineas and lived therein about four months and that her Husband has not since he quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 20th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

Judith Murphey
