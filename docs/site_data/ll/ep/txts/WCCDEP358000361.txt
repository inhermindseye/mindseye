Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Benjn. Newman One of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement danes aforesaid Matter Oath & Saith That Ann Pearson (the Wife of William Pearson as this Deponent is informed) accd her Family are now in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and are Chargeable to the said Parish

Sworn this 26th. Septr. 1786 before  Sampn Wright

Benj: Newman
