Middlesex ss}  Susannah Nicholson  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father Peter Nicholson heretofore rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & died legally Settled in the Said Parish That She this Depont is also legally Settled in the Sd Parish in right of her sd late Fathr. She never having been bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Paid Any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever was Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 7.day of Novr 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Susannah Nicholson
