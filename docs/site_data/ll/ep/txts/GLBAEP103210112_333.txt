Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Eleanor Cooper of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County a Widow Woman taken on oath before me Robert Smith  Esquire  one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the said County this Second day of November 1785 who saith that she is now with Child and that the said Child is likely to be borne Bastard and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate and that William Jones a Servant to messieurs Ellis's of old Compton Street in the Parish of St Ann in Westminster in the said County Oil and Colourmen is the Father of the Said Child

Taken and Signed the day & year above written before me  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Eleanor Cooper
