Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Lyne Aged about Twenty three Years, upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married, and that on the Thirtieth day of May last, she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. Foster in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath been baptized by the Name of Elizabeth , Says that one John Spencer who is Clark of the Parish Church of Clewer in the County of Berks is the reputed Father of the said Female Bastard Child he having had carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of August 1767, and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid, and that the said John Spencer and no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 29th. day of July 1768 before} 

The Mark of Elizth. Lyne

Re-Sworn Novr. 26th. 1768 before me W Kelynge
