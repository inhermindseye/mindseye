Middx  Margt. Sherrat aged 50 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that about twelve Years ago she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Wm Delahunt at the Corner of Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes at the yearly Wages of £5 for one Year & upwd. and has not since she quitted her sd Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn 10th Jany. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Margt. Sherrat
