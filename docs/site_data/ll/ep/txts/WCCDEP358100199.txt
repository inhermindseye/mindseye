Middlesex & Westminster}  Mary Bissell aged about Twenty five Years now living at No.15 Vere Street on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Nelson Oilman in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 14 Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further Says that one James Batty a Butcher in Clare Markett is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings in Holles Street sometime in the Month of September last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Batty and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 24th. day of March 1784 before R Butler

her Mary Bissell mark
