Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Peachey aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Ponten in Stanhope Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Fifteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of May 1797 before me} J Hunter

Mary Peachey
