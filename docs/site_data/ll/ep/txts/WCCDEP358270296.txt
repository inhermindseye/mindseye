Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Alice Evans aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That She has lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Williams at the Sun in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of Twelve Months last past at the yearly wages of Six Guineas And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one William Harby who lives Shopman at No. 171 in Oxford Road is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant thereon and that the said William Harby is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 9th- day of March 1797 before me} Saml. Edward .

Alice Evans

Man taken and Paid £20 on the 10th: day of March 1797 Release Exted

Child born in the House on the 3d. day of June 1797 named Ruth Ann Evans

Sent to Enfield  14th. Augt. 1797 Died the 16th .. Do.
