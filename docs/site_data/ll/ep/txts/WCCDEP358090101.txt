Middlesex to wit John Shireson Aged about Thirty five on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Martha his lately at the Parish Church of Shoreditch about Twelve Years of and by whom he hath one Child now living namely Sophia Aged about Eleven Years Says his first Wife died about Eight Years ago without any or Child living by her And further Says that he Intermarried to his present Wife Sarah at Shoreditch Parish aforesaid about Six Years and by whom he hath Two Children now living namely John Aged about five Years and Ann Aged about Two Years And this Examinant further says that his Father John Shireson rented and lived in a House in Baldwins Garden Grays in Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Twenty Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or thereabouts and paid Parish Taxes due for the same Quitted same about fifteen Years ago and after this Examinant having his said Fathers Service he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by living as a hired Yearly or Covenanted Service whilst a single Man Renting House or orwise how soever

Sworn this 7th. day of Decr. 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

John Shireson
