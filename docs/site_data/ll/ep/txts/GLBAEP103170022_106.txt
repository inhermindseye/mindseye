Middlesex ss}  Mary Stevens  Singlewoman maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Father deced nor of the Settlement of her late Mother deced Save that She has been informed & believes that her Mother was born in the parish of Eltham in the County of Kent in which parish She this Deponent was also born That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived in any one Service or Place as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 5: of November 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Saml Wood

The Mark of Mary Stevens
