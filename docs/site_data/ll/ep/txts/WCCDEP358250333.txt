Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Brown aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Potter at the Sign of the Green Dragon in Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Two Years since which time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Janry. 1798 before us R. Ford 

her Elizabeth Brown Mark

Witness H Day
