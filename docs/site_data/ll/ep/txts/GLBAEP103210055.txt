Middlesex ss  Sarah Page  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by & with John Nehrling in the Victualling office Square in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of fourteen Months That She has not since lived as a hired servant in any one place for the Space of a year nor was she ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28. day of Janry. 1785 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Sarah Page
