Westmr. 

Cathn. Lefevre upon Oath saith she never was married Says she was born in Durham Yard in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. where her father Daniel Lefevre deceased lived in & rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds or thereabouts for several Years & paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says her sd. late father never after he quitted sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 17th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Cath. Lefevre
