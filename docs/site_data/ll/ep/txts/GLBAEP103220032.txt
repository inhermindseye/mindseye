Middlesex ss}  Ann Armmur  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages by & with Captain Christie of Burr Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two Years & upwards That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year in any one Place nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 25 day of April 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Armmur
