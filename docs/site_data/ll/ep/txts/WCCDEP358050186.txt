Westmr  Benj Ansell upon Oath saith he Rented house of £20 a Year in Peter Street in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and lived therein three years & upwards and quitted the same abt. 14 years ago since which he has not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 6th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

Benjamin Ansell
