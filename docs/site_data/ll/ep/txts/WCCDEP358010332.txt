Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Kelly aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that on the 28th. day of augt 1786 she Intermarried with Lockland Kelly her present Husband who has been dead about deserted her at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. John aged about Two years That her said husband Rented and lived in a house in new Street Saint Giles's in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of middx for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds that he hath quitted said house about Three years since which time he hath not never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pound a year said Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of April 1790   Tho Colling  John Fendals

Mary Kelley
