Middx  Mary Humphries aged abt. 21 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servt. wth. Mr. Raikes apothecary  in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middx at they yearly Wages of £5 for one year and 8 months quitted her sd. Service in Octr. last and has not Since rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parish taxes

Sworn June 18th. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Humphress
