Middlesex ss}  Martha Ellard the widow of George Ellard deced maketh oath that Since her Said husbands death She this Deponent hath lived in and rented a house at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds and ten Shillings in White horse Street in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the Said County & Continued in the occupation thereof for the Space of half a year That she has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That she this Deponent hath three Children namely Patty aged about Eleven Years George aged about Seven Years and half and Nathaniel aged about five Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband to whom she was lawfully married none of which have as yet done any Act to gain a settlement for themselves

Sworn this. 24. day of June 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

Martha Ellard
