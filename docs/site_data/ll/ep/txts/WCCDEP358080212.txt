Westmr- to wit Job Thomas aged about 56 Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to with Elizth - his now Wife at the Parish Church of St- James Westmr - about 20 Years ago by whom he has three Children now living all of whom is now provided for Says this Examinant Rented and lived in a house known by the Sign of the Two Brewers in Longditch in the Psh of St- Margt- Westmr - for twelve months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of £40 and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted that said house about seven Years ago since which time he never Rented house paid Taxes or orwise. gained any other subseqt- Settlemt.

Sworn this 5th- Day of Febry 1778 before me  John Cox . G Howard

his Job Thomas Mark
