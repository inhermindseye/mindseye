Middx  The Examination of Christopher Hillibrand Taken the 5th. Day of June 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. about 12 Years ago he lived in & Rented a House of Eighty Pounds a Year for the space of Ten Years or thereabouts in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx And this Examinant further upon his Oath saith that he hath not since that time Rented any other House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife That he is now Poor & Old & not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Samuel Clarke

Christ  Hillibrand
