Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Frances Sandiford of the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the said County this twenty ninth day of July one thousand seven hundred & eight five who Saith that she is now with Child and that the said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be chargeable to the said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that Francis Dorrington  of Burr street in the said Parish Footmen is the Father of the said Child.

Taken & Signed the day and year above written before me.  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Frances Sandiford
