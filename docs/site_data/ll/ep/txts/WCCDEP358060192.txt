Middx  Amelia Williams upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Robt. Williams who dyed on the 3d. of May last and that she has two Childn. by her sd. late husband (now with her) Wm . aged abt. 12 Yrs. & mary upwds. of one Year Says her sd. late husbd. rented a house of £13 by the Year in St Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and lived therein about Nine Months and that neither her sd. late husbd. nor this Examt. Since they Quitted the sd. house have rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 21st. Octr 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Amelia Williams
