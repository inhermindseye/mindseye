Middx  Ann Heritage upon Oath saith she is the wife of Joseph Heritage ; and that at the time of her marriage to Heritage she was the Widow of Wm. Taylor who dyed upwds three Years since and by whom she has a Child now living (viz) Eliz , aged about three Years and that the sd. Wm. Taylor rented a house of the yearly Rent of £9 in Joy Lane in the Parish of St. Paul at Canterbury lived therein near two Years & Pd. to the Poor rate of the sd. Parish & there dyed & this Examinant continued to live in and rent the sd. house near a Year in her Widowhood

Sworn Augt. 11th 1753 before Fran: Bedwell

Ann heritage
