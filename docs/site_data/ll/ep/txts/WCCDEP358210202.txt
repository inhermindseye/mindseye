Westmr.  Mary Row aged about 57 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Row with whom she intermarried at Warwick Street Chapel  Soho , about 37 Years, ago, says her said husband Rented a house in Wych Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for seven Years at the Yearly Rent of Seven Pounds & was rated to & paid the Land Tax for same & after he Quitted it, Rented another house in Kings Arms Court Hollywell Street in the same Parish of the Yearly Rent of Nine Pounds for two Years or thereabouts and was also Rated to & paid the Land Tax for same; quitted the last mentioned House about Seventeen Years ago and hath not since Rented House or pd. Taxes

Sworn Octr. 28th. 1767 . before W Kelynge

Mary Row
