Middx  Susa. Danks aged abt. twenty three Years upon Oath says she lives a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Bearcroft in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & hath so done for three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds ten Shillings five Pounds & five Pounds ten Shillings & further upon he Voluntary Oath says she was never married & that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born & further says that Thos. Williams  Coachman to Lady Fane in Swan Walk Chelsea had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body in the Month of March last & at several other times since at one of which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn this 12th day of Octr. 1757 . before me} Fran: Bedwell Susanna Danks
