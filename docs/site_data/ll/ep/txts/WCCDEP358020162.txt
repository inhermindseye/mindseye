Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Groves aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she was born at Mile End in the Parish of Saint Dunstan at Stepney in the County of Middlesex and that she never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes ever lived and a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an apprentice

Sworn this 2d. day of Septr. 1789 J Addington Sampn Wright

her Mary Groves Mark
