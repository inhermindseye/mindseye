Middx.  Mary Dawson aged about 26 Years Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that sometime about the Middle of the Month of April 1765 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Appartment of Susannah Milwood in the House of Mrs. Morgan a Chandler's Shop the End of Silver Street in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized by the Name of George 

Sworn the 18th. day of June 1765 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos: Balack

The Mark of Mary Dawson
