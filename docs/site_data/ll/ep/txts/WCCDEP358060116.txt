Middx  Mary Dowling  Wido . of Hugh Dowling who dyed abt. 9 Weeks since upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Hugh Dowling at the Fleet London abt. 27 Yrs. ago and has by him one Child living namely Will Dowling aged upwards of 9 Yrs. Says her sd. late husband rented kept a Shop in Claremarket in the parish of St Clemt Danes for the space of one Year & lived all that time at his Fathers in Bear Yard in the same parish Says her sd. late husbands Father was many Years a housekeeper. & pd. Parish Taxes in Bear Yd . aforesd. & that the sd Hugh Dowling never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn June 7th. 1751 before}

The Mark of Mary Dowling
