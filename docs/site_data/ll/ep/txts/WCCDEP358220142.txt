Middlesex & Westminster }  Joseph Warne Aged about Thirty Years on his Oath Says he never was married nor ever Rented House paid Taxes or was an Apprentice to any person whatever Says that after he came to London he Entered into Articles and lived a Covenanted Servant with one Mr. John Homan a Currier in Trotters Alley in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry for One Year and five Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds and his said Master & this Examinant disagreeing he quitted his said Service about Eleven Years ago and since he quitted that Service he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1770 before Us  R. Butler W Kelyngs

his Joseph Warne Mark

Witness Petr. Sidebotham
