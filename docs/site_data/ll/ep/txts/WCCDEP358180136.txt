Middx  David Lockley aged abt. 56 Years upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to & served his Apprentiship with Mr Henry Hillsborough  Engraver in Exeter Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship abt. 32 Years since which time he hath not rented ten Pounds a Yr. Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne 2d. Decr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

David Lockley
