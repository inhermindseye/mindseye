Middx  Grace West aged about 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about one Year since she lived Servant with Mr. Green (formerly Shoemaker to the Queen) in New Green in the Parish of Richmond in the County of Surry for the Space of five Years or these a Cmts & (by Agreemt.) had in Consideration of her sd. Service Dyet washing Lodging and Cloathsinlien of Wages & has not since she left the sd. Mr Green rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Octr. 28th. 1749 before} Tho. Lane Mrs. Hex Brentford

The Mark of Grace West.
