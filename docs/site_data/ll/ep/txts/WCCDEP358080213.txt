Westmr-  Elizth- Proctor aged about twenty eight Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of John Proctor gone from her with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St- Mary Lambeth about eight Months ago by whom she hath one Child now living named John aged about five Months as also another Child by a former husband named Ann Leonard aged two Years and nine Months and whose Father was a Watchman and never gained a Settlement in England to her Knowledge or belief And this Examinant further saith that her present husband named Jno- Proctor Rented and lived in a house in Little Elbow Lane in Thames Str - in the parish of Saint Michael Royal London for six Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of thirteen pounds hath quitted the same about six Months ago since which time this Examinant her husband hath never gained any other subseqt- Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or orwise howsoever

Sworn this 6th- Febry 1778 Before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Elizth- Proctor Mark
