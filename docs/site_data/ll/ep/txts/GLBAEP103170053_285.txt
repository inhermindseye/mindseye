Middlesex ss  John Smith maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house of Twelve Guineas a year in Rosemary Lane in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & resided thereon for the Space of two years & upwards & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that he hath a wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 5 day of February 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of John Smith
