Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Harris aged about 63 years upon her Oath saith That on or about the 20th. day of January 1762 she Intermarried with Wm. Harris her late Husband who has been dead about Two years at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes That her said late Husband to the best of this Deponents knowledge Information or belief never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes nor where he was born she this Examinant doth not know That she this Deponent whilst single lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Long a Watchmaker in Holborn in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Thirty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Augt. 1795 .} N Bond Rupt Clarke

her Ann Harris Mark
