Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Alexr. Marshall of Brownlow Street Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex Carver and Gilder on the behalf of Sarah Marshall Singlewoman his Sister now in Lodgingsin Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex and so ill that she cannot Attend to be examined upon his Oath saith that ceted for rly ig Settlement in her own Right Saith that John Marshall their Father is dead that in his Life time he rented and lived in a House in Knightsbridge in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex at the yearly rent of £10 and upwards and paid Taxes for the same for upwards of years and after he quitted such House till the time of his Death did not nor did Sarah Marshall this Examinants Mother who Survived the said John Marshall her Husband but is since dead nor either of them Rent any other Tenement of Ten Pounds Value Per Annum or pay any Taxes
