Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Kelly  Singlewoman Aged about Seventeen years on her Oath Says she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says that this Examinant and her late Brother were in the Workhouse belongg. to the Parish of St. Brides London for several years and to which Parish her Grandfather as also her late Father and Mother belongd as She has often been Informed and which Information she verily believes to be true And this Examinant further saith that about Three years and half ago her Father in Law Mr. Hodgson out of Compassion to this Examinant took her out of the aforesaid Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Brides and bound this Examinant an Apprentice to one Mrs. Willington a Mantua Maker in Litchfield Street in the Parish of Saint Anns in the Liberty of Westminster for Six Years, The first Three years to work below Stairs and the last Three years above Stairs the first Three years of which said time this Examinant served accordingly That this Examinant soon after the first three years of her Apprenticeship left her said Mistress and afterwards falling a Casualty in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes was passed on the aforesaid Parish of Saint Anns Soho , Saint Anns soon afterwards appealed to the Quarter Sessions And the Court were of Opinion that as the Indenture was for Six years only this Examinant Cod. gained no Settlement thereby And further says she can give no further or other Account of her Settlement than as abovementioned

Sworn this 8th. day of Augt. 1778 before  Jno. Barnfather  James Croft

her Elizth. Kelly Mark
