Middx & Westmr.}  William Nurse one of the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St- Clement Danes maketh Oath that on Searching the Register of Marriages in the Parish Church of Saint Faith's London about three Weeks ago found therein that the then Elizth. Cope was married to John Cornwall her husband in the Year 1775 and by whom she hath one Child now living named John aged about four Years as he has been Informed and verily believes That her said Husband Rents and lives in a House in the Parish of St. Romwald als Runwald in the Borough of Colchester in the County of Essex upwards of four Months at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and has gained no other subsequent Settlement since as he verily believes.

Sworn this 21st. Day of April 1777  John Croft  John Cox .

Willm. Nurse
