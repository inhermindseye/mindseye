Middx ss The Examination of Sarah Smithson  Singlewoman Taken on oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the County of Middx (whereof one is of the Quorum) and both residing in or near to the Limits of the parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd. County this tenth day of February 1755

This Examinant on her oath saith that about the beginning of the Month of February one thousand seven hundred and fifty four one Thomas Deller of the parish of Thunderidge in the County of Hertford Farmer did Prevail with this Examinant to suffer him to lye with her at the house of Elizabeth Smithson this Examinants Mother And the sd Thomas Deller had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body and so had several times whereby she conceived with Child of a female Bastard Child of which she was Delivered at the workhouse belonging to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate on the twenty fourth day of November last past which said female Bastard Child hath been baptised by the Name of Elizabeth Deller And hath been now is and still is likely to be Chargeable to the sd Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate this Examinant being Poor and

not able to provide for the same And this Examinant farther saith that the sd. Thomas Deller is the true and only father of the sd. female Bastard Child And that this Examination is voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whomsoever

Sworn this 10th day of February 1755 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

The mark of Sarah Smithson
