Westmr. to wit}  Mary Reed Aged about Twenty three Years upon her Oath Says She never was Married and that She lived a hired Servant with one Mr Mackay: a Fruiterer in Henrietta Street in the parish of St Paul Covent Garden in the liberty of Westmr. for one Year and Two Months as the Yearly Wages of Six pounds Six Shillings hath quitted said Service about four Months and hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30 Day of Decr. 1768 Before me} Tho Lane J Fielding

her Mary Reed Mark Witness Petr. Sidebotham
