Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Sarah Dibble aged 56 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that about 27 years ago she intermarried with Thos. Dibble her late Husband who has been dead about 16 Months at the Parish Church of St. Mary Overys that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Lads Court Mai Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Mary Overys for upwards of 12 years at the yearly Rent of £5..10..0 and Paid the Kings and Watch Rate due for the same hath- quitted said House about 14 years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of September 1786  James Croft  Jno. Barnfather

her Sarah Dibble Mark
