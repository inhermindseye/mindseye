Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Lyman aged about Nineteen years upon her Oath Saith that she never was married that she was bound an Apprentice to one Mrs. Winchet in Sweed land Court Tower hill for Seven years that she Served her said Mistress for Three years or thereabouts that she and her Mistress not agreeing her Mistress told this Examinant that she might go to Service but would not give up her Indentures that she accordingly hired herself to one Mr. Dawes a Broker fronting Tower hill in the parish of Saint Botolph within Aldgate in the City of London which whom she lived near Three Months at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds with the Consent and knowledge of her said Mistress, that after she left Mr. Dawes Service she this Deponent never did Acquaint her said Mistress where she went to live nor to the best of her knowledge her said Mistress never knew

Sworn this 7th day of Janry. 1797 . R. Ford Robinson

her Hannah Lyman Mark
