Middlesex ss}  Mary Edwards maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages by & With Mr Powers a Salesman in Rose mary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued there in Such h- Service under Such hiring for the Space of two years That She has not Since lived as a lived Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 7th day of November 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Mary Edwards
