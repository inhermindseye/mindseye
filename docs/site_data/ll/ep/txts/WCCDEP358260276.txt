Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Brown aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Mc. Culloch in Berwick Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of May 1798 before us J Reeves Saml Edward

her Elizth. Brown mark
