Middx & Westmr.}  Stephen Bagnall Aged about Sixty Years on her Oath says he Rented and lived in a house in Oxford Road in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for five Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds hath quitted the same about Nine Years and hath not since he quitted his said House he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 26th. day of February 1770 before } Tho Fellows

Stephen Bagnall
