Middx  Mary Dixon aged about Forty Seven Years on her says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Whiter in Craven Buildings in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of Six Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1779 before R Butler

her Mary Dixon Mark
