Middx.  Jane Pety aged abt. 28 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Pety who now absconds from her and hath so done for abt. a Month past and that she intermarried with the said John Pety on the Parish Church of St. Bennet Pauls Wharf London upwards of one Year since says her said husband never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year as her said husband hath informed her and as she verily believes says her said husbd. hath also informed her that Edwd. Pety his father served his Apprenticeship to a Sawyer by Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. now Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 29th. day of July- 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Jane Pety
