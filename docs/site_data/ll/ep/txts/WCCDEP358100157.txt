Middlesex  Elizth. Oram Aged about Thirty three years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of Hendon in the County of Middx about Eight years ago and who died about Six Months ago by she hath two Children living namely John by a former Husband and Amelia by her late husband Says her said Husband served seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Evans an Apothecary at Knights bridge in the Parish of St. Margts- Westmr. and after he was out of his sd. Apprenticeship he never gained any or Subsequent Settlemt. And further says that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House at Milbank in the afsd. Parish of St. Margarets Westmr. for sevl. years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and Rented same to the time of his Death further says that this Examinant never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement since her said Husbands Death orwise than as aforesaid

Sworn this 18th. day of Decr. 1783  Robt. Taylor  Jno. Barnfather

Eliz Oram
