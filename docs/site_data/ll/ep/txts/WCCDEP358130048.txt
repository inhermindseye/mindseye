Middx  Edward Morris aged about 43 Years upon Oath says that about Eight years ago he rented a House then the Sign of the Golden Lyon near Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St: Clement Danes & lived in the same above 4 Years at the Yearly Rent of £26 & paid all Parochial Taxes for the same And further says that he has not since Rented a House elsewhere nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 10th. Day of Septr. 1755 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Edwd. Morris
