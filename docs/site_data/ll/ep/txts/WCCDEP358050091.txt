Westmr.  Eliz Wood Wife of Joseph Wood (gone from her) upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the said Joseph Wood about Sixteen Years since at the Fleet London and that the said Joseph Wood Served his Apprentiship with Mr John Leggit a Shoemaker in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 18th. 1749 before} Gabriel Fouace

Eliz Wood
