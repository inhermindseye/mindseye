Middlesex, ss,  Richard Bennett maketh Oath, that in or about the Year 1733, he was bound Apprentice by Indenture for the Term of Seven Years to Joseph Carpenter of Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , Brasier and Broker , that he duly served the whole of his said Apprenticeship to his said Master, that his said Master resided in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate during the whole of this Deponent's said Apprenticeship, and that he this Deponent has not since his said Apprenticeship rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards, paid any Poor's Rate or King's Tax lived as a Yearly hired Servant for a Year with any Person or Persons whomsoever, or done any Act to his Knowledge or Belief to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of April 1790. before}
