Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Sells the wife of William Sells (who is now absent from her by reason of Sickness) maketh oath that She has been informed by her Said husband & Which She believes to be true that he was heretofore hired. & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages by & with one Richardson a Victualler & who kept the Sign of the White horse in Brick Street in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in Westmr in the Said County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two Years That her Said husband to this Deponts Knowledge or belief has not Since Such his Servitude rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish Office or Paid Any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That she hath a Child named Elizabeth Aged about eight Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 13. day of June 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Elizabeth Sells
