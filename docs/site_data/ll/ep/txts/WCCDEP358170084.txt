Middx  Elizth- Hind  Wido . of Jno. Hind upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband by Indenture dated the 5th- Day of November 1728 was bound Apprentice to one Mr. Mathw- Brown of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Glazier & served him as an Apprentice for the Space of Seven Years that her sd. late Husband never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or Beleife

Sworne the 7th. Day of July 1741 before} Jas Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. Hind
