Saint Clement Danes 26th. 1785

Middlesex  Swears to St. Clemts. (to wit) Thomas Sangster aged 29 Years or there- -abouts upon his Oath saith he was married to Elizabeth his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about 5 years ago that he has no Children now living by his said Wife that this Examinants Mother after the Death of his Father rented and lived in a House in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the yearly Rent of 14 pounds and this Examinant lived with her there as Part of her Family for several years and has never done any Act to Gain a Settlement in his own Right that he is act of work and unable to maintain himself and his said Wife

Sworn this 20th- day of Octr 1785  Wm Kitchiner

Thos Sangster
