Middlesex ss}  Ann Evans  Singlewoman maketh oath that about ten years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Stanford at the Sign of the Swan with two Necks in Lad St Lane in the Parish of St Lawrence Jewry in the City of London & continued in Such Service under such hiring for the Space of twenty Months & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st- day of May 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Ann Evans
