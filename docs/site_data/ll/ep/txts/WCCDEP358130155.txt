Middx  Benjamin Hollis aged about 52 Years upon Oath says he served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with Mr. James Singer a Taylor in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes Says he has a Wife named Judith aged about 45 Years and that he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 5th. Day of May 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

Benjamen Hollis
