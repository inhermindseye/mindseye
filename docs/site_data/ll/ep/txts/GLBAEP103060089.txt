Aldgate Middx

Ann Wobshire Maketh Oath that she hath often heard her Father John Wobshire declare that he was born and Servd his Apprenticeship to John Pile a Carpenter in the Parish of Saint Martins in the City of Salisbury in Wiltshire that he is now a Soldier in Flanders (if living) and that her mother Mary Wobshire is lately Dead and hath left three Children beside this Deponent (to wit) Sarah aged ten years and Six Months Mary aged Seven years and upwards and John Wobshire aged 3 years & Six Months

Sworn 12th day of October 1747 before Robert Dennett Rd. Ricards

the mark of Ann Wobshire
