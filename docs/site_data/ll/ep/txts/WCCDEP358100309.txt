Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to St. Clemt . to wit Sarah Wilson aged about 28 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House Or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says she was born in Sadlers Court   Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex & Liberty of Westmr. and Can give no further or other Account of her Settlement than as before mentioned

Sworn this 16th. day of Decr. 1784 }

her Sarah Wilson Mark
