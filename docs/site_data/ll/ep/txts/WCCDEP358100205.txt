Middlesex to wit Ann Patridge Aged about about 68 Years On her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year says her Father Jno Patridge a Watch Maker Rented and lived in a house in Swan Yard in the Parish of Christchurch Newgate Street London for Several Years at the Yearly Rent of £10 and upwards and after his Death her Mother Kept the same House for some time afterwards and can give no further or other Account of her Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 12th day of April 1784 before us  Thos: Parker James Croft

Ann Patrige
