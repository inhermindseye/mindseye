Middx to Wit John Tomes Aged about 59 Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his late Wife  Elizth. Tomes at the Parish of Yeasar in the County of Hereford upwards of 30 Years ago & by whom he never had any Children Says that being a Widower & without Child or Children he lived as a hired Yearly Servant in the Capacity of a Coachman with one Mr. Fisher a Stock Broker near Vaux Turnpike in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry Upwards of 18 Months at the Yearly Wages of 11 Guineas besides a Livery Diett & Lodging hath quitted his sd. Service about 13 Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. Either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10 a Year paying Taxes or Living a hired Yearly Servant for One year

Sworn this 20th. day of Decr. 1782 before R Butler John Hagar

John Tomes
