Middx ss  Margaret Hopkins the widow of Lockling Hopkins deced Maketh oath that her sd. late husband was an Irishman born but from part of Ireland he came she cannot Setforth having no knowledge or information of ye same And this Deponent Says that her sd. husband has informed her that he was never a hired Servant by the Year in England nor did any Act Whereby to gain any legal Settlement in England And this Depont farther Saith that about Six years ago being then a Singlewoman She lived as a hired Servant with Mr. Charles Porter in Ram Alley in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West in the City of London at ye Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & Continued in such Service Upwards of one Year And Saith that she has not Since lived us a hired Servant for ye Space of a Year or upwards nor gained any Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 31 day of October 1753 before us  Richd: Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

The Mark of Margaret Hopkins
