Middlesex ss}  Lawrence Shaw maketh oath That his Fathr Thomas Shaw has informed him & Which he believes to be true That his Said Father was duly bound Apprentice to a person who Carried on the Trade of a Cordwainer (but whose Name he Cant recollect in the Parish of Andley in the County of Stafford & Continued with him there for the Space of a Year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That this Deponts Father to his knowledge or belief hath not Since Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he this Depont. never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever Rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 5th day of December 1775 before}
