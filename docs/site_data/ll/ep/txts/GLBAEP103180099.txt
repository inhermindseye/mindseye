Middlesex ss}  Mary Chandler  Singlewoman maketh oath That her late Brother William Chandler deced has informed her & Which She believes to be true That many years ago her late Father William Chandler also deced rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex & resided therein for many Years & Carried on the Business of Chandler there & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates & other Taxes for the Same & that he died legally Settled in the Same Parish & was there buried & this Deponent is the rather induced to believe Such information to be true for that about Twenty Years ago as this Deponent now Cash recollects & remembers her late Sister Sarah Chandler deced was received into the Workhouse of the Said Parish & there relieved & was afterwards bound out Apprentice by the Parish officers of the Said Parish as being legally Settled there in right of her Said late Father William Chandler That She this Deponent never was bound apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in her own right.

Sworn this 30. day of November 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Mary Chandler
