Middx  Eliz; Simms aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Jones at the Oxford Arms in Houghton Street Claremarket in the parish of St Clemt Danes Middx for five Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds quitted her sd Service about fourteen Years ago and has not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn July 17th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Simms
