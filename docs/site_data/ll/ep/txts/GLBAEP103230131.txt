Middlesex ss}  Catherine Milestone the wife of James Milestone maketh oath that her said husband has informed her and which she believes to be true that he was born at Edinburgh in Scotland and never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England that She was heretofore lawfully married to one Henry Bridges her first husband and about Thirty years ago as She now best a members he rented a house at the yearly rent of Six Pounds in White Rose Alley in White Cross Street in the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate in the City of London and during such his residence therein he was charged to and Paid Kings Taxes for the same and died there legally settled That She afterwards married one John Scannell her Second Husband since deced but the place of his lawful Settlement She Can't Set forth That She this Deponent hath pone no Act Since such her Several Marriages as aforesaid to gain a Settlement save what She may have Acquired by such her said respective Marriages

Sworn this 9th day of April 1788 before}

The Mark of Catherine Milestone
