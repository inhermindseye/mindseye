Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit William Croxon aged about 22 years upon his Oath saith that he never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant ever bound an apprentice that this Exts. Father & Mother informed him which information he verily believes to be true that he was born in Jewin Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldersgate in the City of London that he has done no Act whatever to gain any other Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of July 1787  Sampr Wright  Thos. Gordon

his William Croxon mark
