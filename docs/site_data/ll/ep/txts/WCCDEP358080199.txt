Westmrs- ss  Isabella Heyman aged about 27 Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of David Starlece Heyman who has deserted her and with whom she Intermarrd. at the parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn in the Year 1773 and by whom she hath no Children living Says her said husband Rented and lived in a house in Gunpowder Alley in the parish of Saint Bridget otherwise Saint Brides London of the Yearly Rent of Eleven pounds lived therein about six Months and paid Rent due for the same but whole any Taxes this Examinant cannot setforth hath quitted the said house about four Years ago since which time her said husband never rented house or Tenemt- of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or orwise howsoever wise gained any other subseqt Settlement

Sworn this 30th- Day of Decr- 1777 . before} M Addington

her Isabella Haymon Mark
