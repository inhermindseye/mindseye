Middx  The Examination of John Bayley liveing in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 10th- Day of March 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that about twenty five Years ago he lived with one Mr. William Alexander in Clare Markett in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes Butcher for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of fifteen Pounds with Meat Drink Washing & Lodging & further on his Oath saith that he hath not since he left the sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beliefe & being Poor & Old is not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

John Bayley
