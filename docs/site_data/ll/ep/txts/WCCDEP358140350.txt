Middx.  Mary Jenkins aged upwards of 30 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Henry Jenkins (at Sea) with whom she Intermarried in Kentish Town Chapel abt. Nine Years ago by whom she has Two Childn. Henry aged abt. five Years and Thos. aged abt. Two Years. Says her said husband Rented a house in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry  of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Guineas lived therein abt. Seven Months and hath quitted same abt. Seven Years and has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid parish Taxes to this Examinant's knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 19th. Feby. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Jenkins

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Wilkinson Ovr. J: P:
