Westmr.  Wm. Jackson upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Hannah at the Parish Church of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. abt. 10 Years ago by whom he hath 3 Children (viz) Thomas aged upwards of 9 Yrs. Ann aged upwards of Six Years & Hannah aged upwards of one Year Says he lived a Yearly Servant (whilst a Singleman) with Mr. Underhill a Peruke maker in Pallmall in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £13 for one Year & upwards Says he has not since he Quitted the sd. Mr. Underhills Service rented a house ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 12th 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

Wm: Jackson
