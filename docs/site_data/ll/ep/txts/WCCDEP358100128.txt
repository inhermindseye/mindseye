Middlesex  Sarah Richards Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Richard Richards who is now in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of St. Margarett Westmr together with her Three Children Says she married to her said Husband at the Parish Church of Shoreditch about Thirteen Years ago Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Lawson a Shoemaker in Tothill Street in the Parish of St. Margaret Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 25th. day of Septr. 1783 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Sarah Richards Mark
