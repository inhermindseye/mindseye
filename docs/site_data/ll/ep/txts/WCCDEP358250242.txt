Saint Clement Danes June 1st. 1796.

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit. James Talboy one of the Beadles Sarah Tutton Aged thirty three years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that She never was Married That She lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hawks Ryder Street in the Parish of St. James Westminster in the County of Middx Fallow Chandler for near two years at the yearly Wages of two Gunieas Per Annum that She quitted the said Service about Six Years ago, that She hath not since she quitted the same as a hired yearly Servant for the space of twelve Months, Rented ten Pounds a Year or done any Act matr. or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement to the best of her knowd. or belief
