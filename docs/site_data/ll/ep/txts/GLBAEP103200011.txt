Middlesex ss} The Examination of Sarah Staple of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this 13 day of June 1783 Who Saith that on the eighth day of February last past at the house of one Mr Johnson in Cartwright Street in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate she the said Sarah Staple was delivered of a Male Bastard Child Since baptized & registered by the name of Will Burr And that the sd Bastard Child is Since become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that one William Burr late of Cartwright Street aforesaid Labourer did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child.

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me R Davies

Sarah Staple

Touching the Father of a Bastard Child after Birth
