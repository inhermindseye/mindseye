Middx  Jane Bunn  Wife of Barnaby Bunn upon her Oath saith that she was married to her sd. Husband on the 28th. Day of May in or abt. the Year 1735 at the Temple Church & that her sd- Husband lived in & rented a House in St. Clemts. Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds for the Space of 4 Years & hath been come away from the same abt. 3 Yrs. & that he hath not since he left the sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that he now absconds from her & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 26th- of Augt. 1741 before

Jane Bunn
