Middx  Margarett Beckworth upon her Oath saith that she is the Widow of Richard Beckworth who hath been dead about Ten Years That she cant give any Acct. of the Settlemt. of her sd. Husband other than that he was born at Norwich but in wt. Parish knows not & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that on the 15th. Day of August Instt. she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. Darcey in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Baptized Mary & that one Francis Collins a Peruke maker did begett the sd. female Bastard Child on her Body she at such time Lodging at one Mr. Phillpotts in Ship Yard aforesaid where the said Francis Collins became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body & in the Month of Decr. last & at sevll. other times since at one

of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child Which she was delivered of as aforesaid & that he the sd. Francis Collin & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 30th Day of Augt. 1743 before} J Poulson Thos. DeVeil

Margaret Beckworth
