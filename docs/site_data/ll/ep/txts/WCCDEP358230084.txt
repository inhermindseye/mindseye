Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Needham aged about 24 years upon her Oath Saith that on or about the 23d day of Augt 1796 she intermarried with John Needham her present Husband by banns at the Parish Church of St Giles Cripplegate by whom she hath two Children living viz Martha aged 2 years and one not yet named aged about 10 months that her said Husband rented a House in Frances St in the Parish of St Mary Newington in the County of Surry for near 12 Months at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas that he hath left the said House about Six Weeks since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any other Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief and this Examinant further saith that on Tuesday last her said Husband left her since which time she hath not seen or heard any thing of him

Sworn this 16th day of August 1799 before us G S Goodenough Saml. Edwards

Sarah Needham
