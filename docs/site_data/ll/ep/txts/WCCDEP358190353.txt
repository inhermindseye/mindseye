Middx  Rebecca Jenkins Aged about Sixty Years Upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Thos. Jenkins with whom she Intermarried in the Year 1730 in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr and County of Middx Says her said husband was born next Door to Doyleys Warehouse in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middx aforesaid Says her said Late husband Served his Clerkship with Mr Sands of Clemt. Inn in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes Says her said husband never Rented house or paid Taxes Says she hath not Since her husbands Death Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn ye 24 Day of May 1768 before} Barth: Hammond

Rebeck Jenkins
