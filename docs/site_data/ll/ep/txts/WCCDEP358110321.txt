Westmr.  Mary Williams aged 21 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servt. wth. John Dalzell a Tobacconist in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the yearly Wages of Forty Shillings for one year & upwds. Quitted her sd. Service upwds. of 4 Yrs. ago & has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Septr. 3d. 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Williams
