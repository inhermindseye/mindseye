Middlesex  A  Swears to St. Clements  Ann Evans  Widow aged forty six years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that She was married to Robert Evans her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about twenty Three Years ago That her said Husband served as an Apprentice with Mr. Osborne, in Star Court Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , a Livery Lane Weaver , under Indentures of Apprenticeship for Seven Years That after her said Husband quitted his said Master till the time of his Death, or this Examinant since his Death have not nor hath either of them Rented any House or Tenement of ten Pounds a Year Values paid Taxes or done any Act matter or Thing Whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information and belief this Examinants said Husband in his life time or this Examinant since his Death may have gained any other Settlement than in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes as above stated

Sworn this 18th Decr. 1784 before me  Wm Kitchiner

Ann Evans
