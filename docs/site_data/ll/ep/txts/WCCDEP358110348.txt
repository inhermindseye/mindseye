Middx.  Edwd. Wilson upon Oath saith he never was married and that he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Ralph Barnes the Filazer of London & Middx.  at the yearly Wages of £30 for one Year & upwds. during which time he lodged in a house at the Corner of Featherstone Buildings in the parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex & has not since he quitted his said service rented ten Pounds a Year Paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Novr. 15th 1753 before} Barth: Hammond

Edwd. Wilson
