Middx  Elizabeth Walker aged about 27 Years upon Oath says She was never Married Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Fry at the Castle in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes two Years and upwards Yearly Wages Seven Pounds ten Shillings and quitted her said Service about Seven Years and hath not since lived a hired Yearly Servant a Year Rented a House or paid any Taxes

Sworn the 20th. day of November 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizth: Walker her Mark
