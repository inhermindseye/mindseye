Middx  Laetitia Morecroft aged about 38 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr Macintosh a Taylor at the Yearly Wages of £4 for one Year and upwds. and during her sd. Service lay in one of her sd. masters Appartments at Mr Merrideth's a opposite Southampton Strt. in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx And that she has not since she quitted her sd. Service (which is abt. 7 Yrs ago) Rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt for one Year

Sworn March 5th. 1753 before}
