Middx to wit Elizth. Walker aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year says her Father John Walker before his Intermarriage with her late Mother and whilst he was a singleman he lived a hired Yearly servant to Lord Carpenter and afterwards to Lord Ward who Gotheresided in the Parish of Saint George Hanover square but whr. he lived with Lord Carpenter or Lord Ward last she Cannot possitively say but that he lived upwards of One year with each at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds and upwards besides Diet Lodgings and Cloaths hath quitted his said service upwards of forty Years ago as her said Father and Mother has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and after quitting his last servitude he never gained any other Subsequent Settlemt. neither hath this Examinant since her said Father Death

Sworn this 3d Day of Decr. 1779 before  Jno Barnfather

her Elizth. Walker Mark
