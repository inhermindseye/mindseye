St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster (to wit) Charles Westricher Aged about 20 Years of No. 958 Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes  Hair Dresser (on behalf of Thomas Honour his Nephew Aged about Seven Years) Maketh Oath and Saith That Thomas Honour the Late of the Father of his said Nephew Intermarried with Mary Honour the late Mother of the said Thomas Honour the Child about 14 Years ago That the said Thomas Honour the father was by Indentures bound to and duly Served his Apprenticeship with Mr. Sullivan late of No. 2 Beaufort Buildings Strand Hair Dresser with whom he Board & Lodged the whole of the time And this Examinant further saith the said Thomas Honour the father never to the best of this Examinants Knowledge or belief gained any Subsequent Settlement And that the said Thomas Honour the Son is the Legitimate Son of the Said late Thomas & Mary Honour & has not gained any Settlement in his own Right

Sworn the 7th. March 1791 before Me  Wm Kitchiner

Charles Westricher
