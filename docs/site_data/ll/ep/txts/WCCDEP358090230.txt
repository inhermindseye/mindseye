Maria Wilkinson Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath says she Intermarried with Robert Nesbett in the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn London about Nine years ago but the said Robert Nesbett being at that time a Married Man this Examinants Marriage with the said Robert Nesbett was of no Effect nor Cod. this Examinant gain any Settlement thereby as she has been Informed and or believes And further says that she has Two Children now living by the said Robert Nesbitt Vizt. Ann Aged seven years the 25th. Augt. 1781 born in the Pallant in the City of Cirencester and Sarah aged 5 years the 16th day of December 1781 born at Petersfield in the County of South -ampton And further says that her said Two Childrens hair gained no other settlement that by Birth as she has been Informed and verily believes
