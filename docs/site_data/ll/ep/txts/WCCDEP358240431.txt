St Clement Danes -Augst 11th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Daur of Josh. & Mary Woodcock }  Settlement  in St Martin in the fields in right of father}  (to wit) Maria Woodcock aged eighteen years and upwards singlewoman Upon Oath Saith that she never was Married that she never lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief that she is informed and verily believes that She is the legitimate Daughter of Joseph Woodcock by Mary his late Wife deceased that her father has Rented and resided in the House (wherein he now desells) situate in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for several year at the Annual Rent of about £33 (as she believes) and paid the Poor Rates for the same And this Deponent saith that she is pregnant and unable to pay the Expences of her lying in

Sworn the 11th day of August 1794 before us } N Bond Edwd: Naish

Ma

Maria Woodcock
