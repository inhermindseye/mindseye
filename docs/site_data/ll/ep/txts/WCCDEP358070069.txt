Westmr. to wit Mary Grace of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  Widow and Jane Cole of the same Parish Widow make Oath and first this Examinant Mary Grace for he saith that she is the Widow of Thomas Grace who has been about three Months ago and with whom she Intermarried the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Four Years ago and by whom she hath Four Children now living Vizt. Francis Aged about Twelve Years William Aged about Five Years Thomas Aged about Three Years and Sarah upwards of One Year Says her said Husband before her Intermarriage and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Shaw at the White in Clements Inn Gate in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes for upwards of One Year and three Months at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily to be true and after he quitted the said last mentioned he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any of Act wh to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or Cole And this Examinant Jane Cole for herself saith that know the said Thomas Grace before his Intermarriage that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with the Sd. Mr in Clement Inn Gate upwards of One Year but at what Yearly Wages his Examinant cannot set forth but verily belives what the other Examinant has sworn to be is true

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry 1773 before me R. Butler

Mary Grace Jane Cole
