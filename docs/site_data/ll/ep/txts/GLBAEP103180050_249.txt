Middlesex ss}  Sarah Bartley  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servt by the Year by & with Commissioner Jenkins in the Victualling office in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at Ceratin Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done or acquired by any Act what so ever or derived from her parents a Subsequent Settlemt & that She was never married.

Sworn this- 11. day of July 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

Sarah Bartley
