sther Wyatt aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of John Wyatt with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields upwards of three Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says before her Intermar riage with her said Husband and whilst she was a Singlewoman she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Medley in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on the 7th. day of November 1773 which as upon afterwards baptized Seming and is become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same and that one Samuel Bean who now lives in the City Chamber in Bishop Gate Street is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of March preceeding her being so brought to bed as aforesaid and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Childs of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Samuel Bean be no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 30th. of September 1779 before R Butler

Hester Wyatt
