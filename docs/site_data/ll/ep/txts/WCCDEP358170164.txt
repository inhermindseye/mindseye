Middx  Johannah Griffin  Wife of a John Griffin upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her sd. Husband John Griffin at a Chappell in Masham Street Westmr . abt. Fourteen Years ago & that her sd. Husband served Three Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Jarvis a Taylor in Naked Boy Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & that he hath not since his sd. Apprentisp rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that he now absconds from her & she having Two Childrn. vizt. Thomas aged Seven Years Sabastian aged Sixteen Months & being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworne the 24th. Day of October 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Johanna Griffin
