St Clement Danes 31st. Decr. 1791.

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Ann Westminster }  (to wit) Ann Smith Aged 40 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband John Smith now Deceased at the parish Church of St Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex on the 21st. day of September in the year 1761. that he hath Informed her wch she believes that previous thereto & while her said late Husband was single & unmarried & A having any Child or Children he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Darby in for about Newport Market in the parish if St. Ann Westminster or for about few years but at what Wages this Deponent doth not know, that he hath Informed her he quitted the same Service about Six years previous to their Marriage & never after acquired any other legal Settlement And this Deponent further saith that she was removed by Orders of Removal with their Daughter Mary Ann Parn the parish of St Mayared to the said Parish of St Ann in the Liberty of Westmstr . and maintained by the said parish against which Order there never was any Appeal to this Deponents belief And she saith that about three years ago she was in the Workhouse belonging to the same parish laid mentioned And that she hath never obtained or become intilled to any Settlemt. in her own Right since her said Husbands Dece & is now unable to support herself with and Assistance

Sworn the 31st. day of Decr. 1792 . before us  Sampn. Wright M Addington
