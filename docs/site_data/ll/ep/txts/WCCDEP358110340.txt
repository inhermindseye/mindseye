Middx.  Eliz. Smith aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that about 6 Years age she was hired to live with Mr Belchier a Banker & lived with the sd. Mr Belchier as a Yearly Servant in Lombard Street in the parish of St. Edmd. the King in Londons for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year pd. parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr. 3d. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz Sarah
