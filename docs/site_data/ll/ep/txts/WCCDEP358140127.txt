Middx.  Sarah Langford aged abt. 32 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Jonathan Langford with whom she intermarried at in the Parish Church of Christ Church in Spittal fields on or about the 3d. of April abt. fourteen Years ago by whom she hath two Children John aged  twelve Years & Chas . aged seven Years says her said husband was by Indenture dated the 5th. day of Novr. 1734. bound Apprentice to & served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Jos: Remnant in Little Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. & never Rented ten pounds a Yr. or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 9th. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Langford
