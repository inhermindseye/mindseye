Middx  Eleanr Hull aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Taylor a Clear Starcher in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes for two Years & upwards Quitted her sd. Service abt. 10 Yrs. ago and has not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the aforesaid Parish of St Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Thomas Johnson (a Salesman who now Keeps a house Known by the Sign of the Naked boy and Woolpack in Broad St Giles's ) is the Reputed father; the sd. Thomas Johnson having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant once in the month of June last at a house (then kept by one Mrs. Flowers) in Crown Court  Broad St Giles's ) at which time he got her with Child of the said Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the sd. Thomas Johnson and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Febry 13th. 1753 before W Fielding

Eleanor Hull
