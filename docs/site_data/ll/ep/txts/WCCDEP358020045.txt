Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Norgrove of No. 46 Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes on behalf of Two illegitimate Children vizt. Michael born at the House of one Mr. Shell in Wych Street in the said Parish and John born at the said House both on the 21st. day of Augt. 1787 Maketh Oath and Saith That one Martha Rana the Mother of the said Two Children died on Monday the 3d. Inst. in the said House and was Buried by the said Parish That one John Shelley who is at this present time ill in St. Georges Hospital lived and Cohabited with the said late Martha Rana for these 5 years last past but never was married to her and that the said John Shelley is the father of the said Two illegitimate Children

Sworn this 10th day of Septr. 1787 Wm. Achiner

Sarah Norgrove
