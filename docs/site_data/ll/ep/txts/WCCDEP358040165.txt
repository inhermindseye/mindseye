Westmr.  Mary Stickler aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Francis Stickler on or abt. the 17th. of Octr. last at the Fleet London Says her sd. husband Served Seven Years Apprentisp. with Mr Cummins a Sawyer in St Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif Says her said husband absconds from her & being pregnant with Child & near the time of her Delivery she is not able to maintain herself

Sworn March 22d. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Stickler
