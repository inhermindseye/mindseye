Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Baker aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that She never was Intermarried with Thomas Baker her present Husband at the Christ Church London by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Wm. aged about Six years That her said Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Wilkinson a Printer in Clare Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th day of May 1798 before me G Goodenough

her Sarah Baker mark
