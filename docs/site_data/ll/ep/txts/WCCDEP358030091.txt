Middx  Ann Heydon aged about 20 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married & that about 4 Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Moye & Mrs. Cobbin Ship Yard in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £2..10..0 for one Years upwards Says she hath not since she left her said Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born & that Wm. Rogers late of Harrow on the hill  Husbandman did begett the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her Body the sd. Wm. Rogers living a Servt . with

this Examinants Father at Watford in the County of Hertford there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of May last & at several other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Wm. Rogers & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne Janry 25 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Heydon
