Westmr.  Mary Hughes aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Andrew Parnall Haberdasher of Small Wares in the Strand in the Parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . and County of Middlesex for 4 Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds and quitted her sd. Service abt. 19 Years ago since which time she has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 26th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Margt. Hughes
