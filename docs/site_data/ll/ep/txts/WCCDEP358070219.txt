Westmr ss  Mary Aycock Aged about 64 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Wm Aycock who has been dead about 4 Years says her late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Ashen Tree Court in the Precinct of White Fryers for 4 Years or thereabout at the Yearly Rent of 5 Pounds & was Rated to and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same has quitted the same about four Years

Sworn this 3d day of Novr 1774 Before R Butler W Addington

her Mary Aycock Mark
