Mary Hand Aged about Twenty five Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of George Hand with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn in the Liberty of the City of London about Seven Years ago Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprentice with a Taylor in the Parish of Woolwich in the County of Stafford and never after Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year as this Examinant hath been Informed by her said Husband and which Information she Verily Beleives to be true Says she has One Child by her said Husband (Viz) Mary Aged about Two Years.

Sworn the Day of Novr 1764 Before}
