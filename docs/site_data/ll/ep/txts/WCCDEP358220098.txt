Middlesex & Westminster ss  John Jenkins aged about Thirty Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife in the Parish Church of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Three Years ago Says that before his said Intermarriage and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Berry who keeps the Queens head Tavern in Holbourn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted his said Service about five years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 12th. day of December 1769 before us} R Butler Thos Kynaston

his John Jenkins mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
