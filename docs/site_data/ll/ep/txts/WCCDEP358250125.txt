Saint Clement Danes May 5th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. Ann Black Piars London to wit Elizabeth Tynmore  Singlewoman aged thirty six Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married, that she lives as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Charles Moody Coal Merchant in Glass House Yard in the Parish of Saint Ann Blackfryars in the City of London for twelve Months wanting two Weeks, that the said Mr. Moody discharged this Examinant from his said Service two Weeks previous to the End of the said twelve Months with Intent that this Examinant should not become a Parishioner or gain a Settlement in the said Parish of Saint Ann Blackfryars -And this Examinant further saith that since quitting the said Mr. Moodys Service she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge she may have acquired any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th Day of May 1794 before} Rich. Ford  Edwd: Naish .

Elizabeth Tynmore
