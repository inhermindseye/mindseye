Saint Clement Danes 22nd- August 19th 1785

Middlesex(to wit)  Passd. to St. Geo: Bloomsbury  Benjamin Wingrove  Master of the Work house belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed and believes that John Thompson  Shoemaker the father of Ann Thompson an Infant aged about twelve Years and now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid rented a Shop in Bloomsbury Market in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of ten Pounds, for upwards of one year, and during that time lodged either in the said Shop or in a Garrett being another Part of the same House which during great Part of that time he also rented and that since that time he has not done any Act Matter or thing whereby as this Examinant is informd and believes he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement and further this Examinant saith that the

the said Ann Thompson the Infant has not, as upon her Information to this Deponents Examining her ever been Put out Apprentice or done any Act whereby to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 22nd Day of Augt. 1785 before} Addington J. Reading

B Wingrove
