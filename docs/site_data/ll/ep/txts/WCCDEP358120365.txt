Middx  Jane Williams upon Oath Saith she was married to Thomas Williams at the Fleet London upwds. of 6 years ago by whom she has 2 Childn. Eliz aged abt. five years and William aged abt. 18 months that her sd. husband rented a house of the yearly Rent of £10 in Red Lyon Court in Whitehart Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. lived therein near one year & quitted the sd. house at Lady day last & has not since rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parish taxes

Sworn 20th. June 1755 before} Barth. Hammond  Tho Lane .

The mark of Jane Williams
