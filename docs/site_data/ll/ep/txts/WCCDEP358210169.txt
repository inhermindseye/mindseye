Westmr.  Mary Robinson aged about 28 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Robinson with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn on the 7th, day of June 1760, by whom she hath One Child, William aged about Seven Years; says her said Husband has absconded her, and that he hath of ten times informed her, which information she verily believes to be true that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Granger a Staymaker in Wardour Street in the Parish of St. Jame in the Liberty of Westminster , and never Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year;

Sworn the 2nd, day of July 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Frans Cary

The Mark of Mary Robinson
