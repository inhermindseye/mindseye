Middlesex ss}  Hannah Swales  Single woman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired yearly Servant by & with William Weatherhead in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Baker at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under such hiring for the Space of one Year & three Quarters That she has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this. 5. day of August 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Hannah Swales
