Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Alice Curtis aged about 62 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Poynter in Devereux Court in the Parish of Said Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the yearly wages of Four pounds that She hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing Whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of July 1799 before us  Saml. Edwards Menter

her Alice Curtis mark

Witness B Newman
