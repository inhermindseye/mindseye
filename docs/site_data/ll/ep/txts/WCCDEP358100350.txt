Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Johns Southwark to wit Maria Chester aged about 44 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Robt. Chester at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn about 8 years ago Who has been gone from her about 4 years and can give no Account at all of her said Husbands Settlement Says that about 20 years ago she Intermarried to Jas. Hopper her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of Saint Michaels  Foster Lane and who has been dead about 12 years Says that he Rented House in Free School Street Horsely Down in the Parish of Saint Johns Southwark for 12 Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of five Pounds and paid the Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Ten years ago since which time she has done no Act matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 14th day of Febry. 1785  Sampn Wright M Addington

Maria Chester
