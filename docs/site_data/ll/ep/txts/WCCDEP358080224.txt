Middx & Westmr.}  James Butler Aged about Nineteen Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary Munday his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes the Nineth day of March Instant and by whom the said Mary Monday is now pregnant with Child as he believes and which is one reason that this Examinant hath Consented to Marry Says this Examinant was bound and Apprentice by Indenture to one Mr. Cam a Coach maker in the Parish of all Saints in the Town of Northampton for Seven Years and Served his said Master for about Two Months under the said Indenture for Six Weeks and upwards and then this Examinant ran away and Came to London and further says that the Parish of all Saint in the said Town of Northampton is the Parish where he this Examinant was born and further says that has gained no other Subsequent Settlemen than as aforesaid

Sworn this 9th. day of March 1778 before  John Croft

James Butlers
