Middlesex ss}  William Hudson maketh oath that he was born in Kingston in Jamaica and never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year nor ever Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever in any Act to gain a Settlement in any part of great Britain or Ireland.

Sworn this 31 day of Janry 1777 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of William Hudson
