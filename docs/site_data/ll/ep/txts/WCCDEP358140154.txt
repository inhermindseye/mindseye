Middlesex .  Elizth: Chubb aged abt. 80 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Saml. Chubb who has been dead upwards of forty Years says she cannot give any Account of his Settlement other then he was a West Country Man says before her Intermarriage with the said Saml. Chubb she served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. John Sells a Staymaker in Howard Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and hath not since her said husbands death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 31st. of August 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Chubb
