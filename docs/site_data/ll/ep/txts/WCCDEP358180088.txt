Middx  Sarah Freeman aged about Forty Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Miles Victualler in Essex Street in the said Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of one Year & upwards that since she left her said Service she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge beleife

Sworne the 8th. July 1748 before} Tho Lane

Sarah Freman
