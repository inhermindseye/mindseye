April 2d. 1753

Middx  Eliz Collins aged 18 Years upon Oath Saith She never was Married and that she was bound Apprentice to Mr Rd Robins of Philpot Lane in the Parish of St. Alphage London & lived with her sd Master in Philpot Lane afsd. three Years and upwds. & then ran away from him & never was assigned over nor lived 40 Days Servant with any person by her sd Masters Consent or knowledge

Sworn the Day & Year above Mentioned before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz Collins
