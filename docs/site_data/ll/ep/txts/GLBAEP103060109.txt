Aldgate Middx

William Crafts Maketh Oath that he Servd Seven Years Apprenticeship by Indenture to Thomas Powell of Horsely down then in the Parish of Saint Olave now the Parish of Saint John in Southwark in the County of Surry  Fisherman and that he hath gaind no other Settlement since hath a wife named Bridgett

Sworn 17th day of December 1748 before me Richd Ricards N. Dukinfield

William Crafs
