Middx  Honor Gorman aged abt. 54 years upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Jas. Gorman deced and that since the decease of her sd. late husband She this Examinant (not having any Child or Children at that time) lived a yearly Servant with Mr. John Cuthbert a Staymaker  in little Russell Street in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of westmr. for 3 Years & upwds. at the yearly wages of £3. quitted her sd Service abt. 5 Yrs. ago & has not Since rented ten pounds by the year pd. parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn 14th July 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Honor Gorman
