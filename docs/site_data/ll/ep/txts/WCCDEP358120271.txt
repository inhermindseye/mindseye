Middx  John Davis upon Oath saith he rented a house of the yearly Rent of £12 in Cucumber Court on the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx lived therein about 18 months and has quitted the same abt. 3 years since which he has not rented ten pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Janry 23d. 1755 before}
