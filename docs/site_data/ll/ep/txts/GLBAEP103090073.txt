Middlesex ss  Esther Heritage aged fifty two years or thereabouts the Widow of Edward Heritage deced. Maketh oath that divers ago her said late husband lived as a hired Servant by the Year When a Singleman (as he has informes this Depont. & as She believes) With one Thos. Brewer of the Parish of S aint John in the Devizes in the County of Wiltshire  Horse Dealer and Continued in such service for many Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds and by Means thereof as she is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said late husband never did any Act Whereby to gain Settlement subsequent to such his servitude Nor hath she this Deponent since his Death done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right And that She hath a Child named Thomas aged about ten Years the Lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband

Sworn this 5th: day of August 1767 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Esther Heritage
