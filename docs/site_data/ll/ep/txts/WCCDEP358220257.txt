Westmr to wit Ann Watkins Aged about fifty Eight Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Watkins who has been dead about fifteen Years Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children (except Two who are now provided for) she went to live as a hired Yearly Servant at the Westminster Infermary in the Parish of St. Margarets in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years and Three Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 22d day of April 1771 before Us} R. Butler 

Anne Watkins
