Middx to wit Carolina Ferguson Aged about Twenty Six years On her Oath says she is the Widow of James Ferguson who has been dead upwards of Three years and with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields about Eleven years Ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living namely Daniel Walter aged about Nine years and Carolina aged about five years, Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Stanhope Street Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for three years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Thirty Two Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said house upwards of Three years ago & hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented house paid Taxes or otherwise gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn the 3d day of Novr 1775 before  David Walker

Carolina Ferguson
