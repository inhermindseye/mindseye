Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

Chayes Edwd. Cooper with Child named Jas Cooper born in Essex St Qr if taken to wit Mary Godfrey aged 33 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or Tene of 10 Pounds a year Paid Taxes Saith that she lived 5 years as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Darby in East Street Red Lyon Square in the Parish of Saint Geo: Queen Square at the yearly Wages of Nine Guineas hath quitted said Service only a week ago that on or about the 4th Sept. 1778 she was delivered of a male bastard Child at the House of one Edwd. Wheeler a Green Grocer in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes afterwards Christened at St. Clements Church by the name of James Cooper that Edwd. Cooper a Gentlemans Servant who lately lived with a Mr. Edward No. 22 Percy Street Rathbone Place and no one else in the Real and true father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body and be gotten the same that the said

Edward Cooper and this Examinant have always maintained the said Child till about Seven Months ago the said Edwd Cooper Refused any longer to Contribute to the Support of the said Child and sent the same back to this Examinant since when this Examinant hath maintained it herself and that she is now Sick and poor and unable to maintain the said Child whereby the same must be Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 21st- day of Octr. 1785  Jno. Machin

her Mary Gofrey mark
