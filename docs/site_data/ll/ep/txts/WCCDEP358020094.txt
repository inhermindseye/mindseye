Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Margaret Sharp aged about 67 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Nash in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £5. that she hath quitted said Service about 14 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 7th day of Augt. 1788 R Butler

her Margaret Sharp Mark.

15th Augt. 1788

(to be indexd) Ann Maylin  Wife of John Maylin passd to Hodgdon Herts as a Vagrant Examination annexd the duplicate of pass
