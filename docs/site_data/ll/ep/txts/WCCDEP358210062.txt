Westmr.  Elizabeth Lowen aged about 66 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Lowen who has been dead about twenty four Years; says she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true, the said William Lowen Rented a house of Ten Pounds by the Year in Bennets Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and lived in the same upwards of one Year and that her said late husband after he quitted that house nor she since his Death never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge and belief.

Sworn Augst. 21st, 1766 . before} Fran. Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizabeth Lowen
