Middx  Cathn. Coultman aged abt. 25 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Wm. Bird Hardwareman in Butcher Row in the Parish of St Clement Danes at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings for one Year and quitted her sd. Service upwds. of two Years ago and that she has not since she quitted her sd. Service Rented ten Pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Septr. 27th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell Catherine Coltman
