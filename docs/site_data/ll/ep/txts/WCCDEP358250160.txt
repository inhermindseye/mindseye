Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Rachael Edwards aged about 59 years upon her Oath saith That on or about the 27th. Septr. 1763 she Intermarried with Thomas Edwards her late Husband who has been dead about Fourteen years at the Parish Church of Saint Martin Ludgate That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house known by the Sign of the George in Holles Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Three years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and paid Taxes That he quitted said House about 28 years ago after which time he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement nor hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 27th. day of April 1795 } N Bond

Rachel Edwards
