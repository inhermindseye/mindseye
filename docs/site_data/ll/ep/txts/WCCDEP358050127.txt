Westmr.  Eliz. Bettison aged about 60 Years upon Oath saith she lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Nicholson in Hollywell Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds and hath left her said Service upwds. of Eight Years she being at that time a Wido . without Child or Children and that she hath not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Octr. 27th. 1749 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Eliz Bettison
