Middlesex ss}  Thomas Parten maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr Wyatt Attorney at Law in the Parish of Saint Swithin at London Stone in the City of London & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of fifteen Months That he has not Since lived as a hired Servt. for the Space of a year elsewhere or rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish Office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Charlotte & two Children namely William Aged about Seven years & Elizabeth aged about three Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Said Wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 16 day of Decemr 1784 before R Davies Thomas Parton
