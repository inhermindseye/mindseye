Middlesex ss  Penelope Pollard the widow of John Pollard  deced maketh oath That her Said late husband as She has been informd & believes was born in Nightingale Lane in the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & to this Deponts knowledge or belief he never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired few for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwds nor ever Served any parish office or paid any poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right & this Depont Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of his parents or eithr of them That She this Depont was Married to her Said husband at the Fleet about thirty Seven years ago & Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 4 day of January 1777 before}  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Penelope Pollard
