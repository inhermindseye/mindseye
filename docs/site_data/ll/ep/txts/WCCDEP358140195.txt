Middx.  Jonathan Floyd aged abt. 69 Years upon Oath says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at May fair Chapel abt. 20 Years ago says he served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Hendley a Plaisterer next door but one to the Bear and Ragged Staff in West Smithfield in the Parish of St. Sepulchre London and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th. day of Decr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

Jona Floyd

Upon the Complaint of Mr Wm. Hale  Ovr . J: P:
