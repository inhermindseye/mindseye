St. Clement Danes October 22d 1793

Middx and Westminster }  Marriage wrongly stated  Whitchurch Hants Vide Subseqt. Examination Entd 297  (to wit) Sarah Wedge aged 34 years or thereabouts Wife of William Wedge Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her said Husband William Wedge at the parish Church of St Margaret Westminster on or about the fifth day of October 1784 (by Banns) that she is informed and believes her said Husband was born at Whitchurch in Hampshire but knows not of any other settlement he has or is entitled to that She hath two Children by her said Husband namely Georgiana Mary aged eight seven years on the first day of November 1792 and John Charles aged five years on the 7th. day of October last instant that her said Husband is a private in Lord Charles Hilgrey 's Company of the third Regiment of Guards and is now abroad that she this Deponent now dwells at the House of Mr Bignall No 16 Little Shire Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex and is unable to maintain herself & family without assista assistance

Sworn the 22d. day of Octr. 1793 before Me}

Sarah Wedge 's Mark
