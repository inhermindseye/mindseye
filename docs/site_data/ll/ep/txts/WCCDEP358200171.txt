Westmr  John Wade Aged about Twenty Six Years Upon Oath Says he Rented a House in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . Three Quarters of a Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds hath quitted said House about One Year & a half & hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the Day of 1764 Before}
