Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Jaimeson of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me One of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Twenty eighth-day of April 1780 -Who Saith that on the Second day of April instant at the Work house belonging to and Situate in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County She the Sd Mary Jaimeson was delivered of a male Bastard Child Since Baptized by the name of Samuel George Jaimeson And that the said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the said Parish and likely so to Continue And that William Richardson of Horsely Down Street in the Parish of St Olave in Southwarke in the County of Surry  Gentleman did get her with Child of the sd Bastard Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me  Jasper Clarke

Mary Jamison
