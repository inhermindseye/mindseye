Middlesex ss.  Margaret Minihan the Wife of James Minihan maketh Oath that she was Married to her said Husband at the Parish Church of Thirleigh in the County of Kerry & Province of Munster on or about the Twenty eight Day of April One Thousand Seven Hundred & Seventy eight , that her said Husband about Five Years ago Rented a House of Fifteen Guineas a Year in Charlotte Street in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middlesex of Mr . Jas. Nowlan & lived in the same upwards of Three Years that her said Husband has not since he quitted the said House, Rented any other House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement that she has Four Children Namely Elizabeth aged about Ten Years, Catharine aged about Eight Years, Thomas aged about Six Years & Ann aged about Four Years the lawful issue by her said Husband, And this Deponent further saith that her said Husband by reason of his ill state of Health, is unable to provide for himself & Family, and therefore she has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate for Relief, and has been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 27th, Day of Octr. 1798 before R Davies H: Reynell

her Margaret Minihan Mark
