Westmr.  Sarah Alsop Aged about Twenty Years Upon Oath says she never was Married, nor ever Rented House, paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, Says her Father Thomas Alsop lived in and Rented a House in the Parish of Ashford in the County of Middlesex for several Years but what Yearly Rent he paid for the same this Examinant cannot tell, says that she was Born in the said Parish

Sworn the 6th, day of June 1768 , before} J. Miller John Goodchild

Sarah Alsop
