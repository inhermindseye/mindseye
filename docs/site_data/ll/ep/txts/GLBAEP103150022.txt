Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Brown of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County widow taken on oath before me - John Sherwood - Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this - Eleventh day of March 1775 .

Who Saith That on the Fifth day of September now last past in Crown Court in the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath been Since Baptised & registered by the name of Sarah Spree and that the said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that John Spree now or late of the Parish of Woolwich in the County of Kent  GunSmith did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child.

Taken & Signed the day & Year above written before me  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Mary Brown
