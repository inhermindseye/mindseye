Westmr.  Charles Hutton aged near 15 Years upon Oath saith his Father Jonathan Hutton deceased lived in and rented a house in Hollis Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £20 for sevll. Years Says his sd. late Father never after he quitted the sd. house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 9th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

Charles Hutton
