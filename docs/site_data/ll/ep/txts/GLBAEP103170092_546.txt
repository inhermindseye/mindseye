Middlesex ss}  Nathan Haswell maketh oath That divers years ago he rented a house in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County of the yearly rent of Twenty pounds & continued in the occupation thereof for about two years That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Alice to whom he was lawfully married in the parish church of Sunderland near the Sea in the Bishoprick of Durham

Sworn this 27 day of August 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

Nathan Haswell
