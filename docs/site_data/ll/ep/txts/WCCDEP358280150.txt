Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Porter aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Parry a Bricklayer in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about 2 years at the yearly Wages of £6 that she hath quitted said Service about 3 weeks And further this Examt. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish And that Joseph Parry the Son of the said Mr. Parry is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Months of May or June last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Joseph Parry is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 11th. day of Febry. 1788 } R Butler

her Elizabeth Porter Mark

Elizabeth Porter lodges at Mr. Taaffes Clements Lane
