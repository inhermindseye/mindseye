Middx  Ann Thomas aged about 22 Years Removed by an Order dated the 27th. day of February last from the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Newell at the back side of St. Clements and the Corner of Exeter Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds Quitted the same about two Years and hath not since rented any House or been a hired Servant for one year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is great with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to provide for the same when Born And that John Allen who keeps a Cheesemongers Shop in Bell Yard near Temple Bar did beget such Child or Children he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of July One thousand seven hundred and Sixty and at several times since at which or one of which times he did beget on her Body the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant And that he the said John Allen is the true and only Father thereof

Sworn the 2d. day of March 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Thomas
