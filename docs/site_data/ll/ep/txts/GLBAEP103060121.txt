Middx ss  Hugh Gibson maketh oath that the last place about Thirty Year ago he lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one John Clerchy an Inn keeper who keept the Sign of the Queens Head in the parish of Bromley in the County of Kent with whom he lived about 6 Years and reced Six pounds by the Year Wages & hath not since lived in any other place as a hired Servant by the Year nor gained any Subsequent settlemt. And that he hath Wife named Sarah

Sworn this 13th. Day of Octobr. 1749 before me  Richd Ricards  Saml Gower

The Mark of Hugh Gibson
