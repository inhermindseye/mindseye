Middx,  Susannah Horton aged upwards of 60 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Hugh Horton with whom she intermarried in the parish Church of St. Clement Danes about Thirty six Years ago says her said husband served seven Years Apprentice ship with Mr. Shaw Perukemaker in Essex Street in the said parish of St. Clement Danes and never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year and that on the 9th. day of October 1744 The Church Wardens and Overseers of the poor of the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid granted a Certificate to the Parish of Norwood in the Precinct of Hayes in the County of Middlesex allowing this Examinant and her said husband to be Parishioners legally Settled in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn the 17th. May 1765 , Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Susanah Horton
