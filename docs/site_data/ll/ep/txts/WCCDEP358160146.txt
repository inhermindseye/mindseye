Middx  The Examinacon of Elizth. the Wife of Robt. Wilkinson Lodging in ye. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. County of Middx taken the 8th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. in ye. Month of Febry. 1736 she Intermarryed with ye. above Named Rt. Wilkinson & yt. he ye. sd. Rt. Wilkinson was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Harris in Bond Street in ye. Parish of St. George Hanover Square in ye. Liberty of Westmr . Gunsmith & lived with him there about 5 Years & then his sd. Master failed And further saith yt. the sd. Mr. Harris being in Mean Circumstances & not able to provide for her sd. Husband let him out for abt. 8 Months the last past of his Apprentiship to one Mr. Smith in the sd. Parish of St. George Hanover Square at 4s P Week Meat Drink Washing & Lodging and recd. the Wages yt. became due for his sd. Service as this Examint. hath been informed & verily beleives And she further saith yt. her sd. Husband never Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes or othewise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That she hath two Childn. by her sd. Husband (to wit) Robt. aged 2 Years & upwds. & Edwd. aged abt. 4 Months & he haveing Absconded from her is poor & not able to maintaine them

Sworn ye. Day & Year above menconed before Houlson Wm. Hayton

The Mark of Elizth. Wilkinson
