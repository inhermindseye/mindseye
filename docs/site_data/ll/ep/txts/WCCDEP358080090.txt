Middx & Westmr}  Ann Monday about 21 Years on her Oath says she never was married rented house or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Dickinson who lived at the house of one Mrs. Smith in Frith Street in the Parish of St. Anns Soho in the Liberty of Westmr . upwds. of one Year at the Yearly Wages of £4 hath quitted her said Service upward of four Years ago since which time she hath never lived in one continued Service for one Year or orwise gained any other subseqt. Settlemt.

Sworn before us the 23d. day of October 1776 Jno. Machin Mercer

Ann Monday
