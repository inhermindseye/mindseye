Middx to Wit Elizth. Gunn Aged about fifty Seven Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Benjamin Gunn with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Botolph Bishopgate about 39 Years ago & by whom she hath no Children now living Says that being a Widow & without Child or Children she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Hayward in Burleigh Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of two years & an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Three Quarters of a year ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 30th. day of Jany. 1783 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth. Gunn Mark
