Middx   Robert Heather aged about 64 years Lodging in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx upon Oath saith he is a Painter by Trade and lived in and rented a House in Earls Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for upwards of Two years at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds Quitted the same in the year 1735 and hath not since rented any House or paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes

Sworn the 26th.. day of November 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Robt. Heather
