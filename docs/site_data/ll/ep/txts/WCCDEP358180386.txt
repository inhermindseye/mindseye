Middx  Mary Burnett upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her Husband  Edwd. Burnett on or abt. the 25th. Day of May 1718 at Lambs Chapell in the Parish of Cripplegate London Says the sd. Edward Burnett Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Chandler a Peruke maker in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Says the sd. Edward Burnett never Rented ten pounds lived a Yearly hired Servt or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Octr. 2d. 1745 before}
