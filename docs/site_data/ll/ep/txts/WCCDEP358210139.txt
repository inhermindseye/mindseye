Westmr.  John Robins aged about 47 Years Upon Oath says, he intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at the fleet London about twenty Years ago; says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Wm: Altham a Nail founder in Winchester Court Cripplegate with whom he served three Years or thereabouts the first part of his said Apprenticeship and then was Assigned over to one Mr. Bignall a Nail founder in Parker's Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and continued to serve the said Mr. Bignall in Parker's Lane aforesaid until the Expiration of the said Indenture of Apprenticeship-says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 19th, day of February 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

John Robins
