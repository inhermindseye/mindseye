Saint Clement Danes Decr- 24th 1785

Middlesex ss  Thomas Pritchard aged seventy two Years a thereabouts upon his Oath saith, that he never rented any House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds, paid Taxes, lived for one Year as an hired Servant, served as an Apprentice, or did any Act, Matter or thing whereby to gain any Settlement in his own Right That his father Robert Pritchard in his Lifetime rented and lived in a House and Long Lane in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great in the City of London and lived there for about five Years, that his Father bought the Lease of the said House, and it up himself, that after quitting the same House his said Father in his life time never did any Act whereby to this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief she gained any subsequent settlement that this Examinant lived with his father as part of his family in Long Lane , aforesaid has received a Weekly allowance from the said Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great in the City of London aforesaid for sometime then he was out of Work and that he is now poor and cannot get Employment to maintain himself and

is under the Necessity of becoming Chargeable to the Parish

Sworn this 24th Decr- 1785 before  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordone

Thomas Pritchard
