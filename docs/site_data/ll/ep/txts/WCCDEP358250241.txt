Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizabeth Smith who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that Thomas Smith then father of the said Elizabeth Smith Rented and lived in a house in Rose Lane in the parish of Christ Church Spitalfields in the County of Middlesex for several years at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards and that after he quitted said house he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of May 1796 W Kinnard Rupt: Clarke

B Newman.
