Middx ss  July Campbell  Single woman Maketh oath that about Twenty one years ago she lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one Mr Madden of Prujanes Court in the parish of Saint Sepulchre in the City of London with whom she lived for the Space of a Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds a Year And by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there and saith that she has not since lived as a hired servant by the year nor done any act to her knowledge or belief whereby to gain any subsequent settlement

Sworn this 12th: day of Janry. 1758 before us  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

her July Campbell Mark
