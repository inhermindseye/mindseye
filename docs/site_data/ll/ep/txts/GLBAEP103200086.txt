Middlesex ss}  Margaret Weatherspoon the Wife of John Weatherspoon maketh oath that her sd husband is now absent from her in Places unknown that he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Thomas Hayes a Victualler at the Sign of the Blue Boar in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Boltolph without Aldgate London & Continued in such Service under Such hiring for the Space of three years & has not Since done any act to gain a settlement elsewhere to this Deponts knowledge or belief & that She was lawfully Married to her sd husband

Sworn this 31t- day of December 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Margaret Weatherspoon
