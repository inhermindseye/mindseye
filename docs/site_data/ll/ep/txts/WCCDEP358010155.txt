St. Clement Danes.

Benjamin Newman one of the Parish of St. Clement Danes Yeoman maketh Oath & Saith That he has been informed that a Female Child named Mary Butter , the Child of Lucy Emblan a Singlewoman who never was married was born at the House of Mr. John Smith , in Swan Yard in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand and further Saith that the said Child is now Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes and is as this Deponent is informed Illegitimate.

Sworn before us this 20th. Octobr. 1787 . D Walker Rapt: Clarke

Benj: Newman
