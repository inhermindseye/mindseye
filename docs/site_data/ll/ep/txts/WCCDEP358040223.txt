Westmr. 

Ann Gough aged about 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Robinson a Tobacco merchant in Bread Street in the Parish of Allhallows  Bread Street London at the yearly wages of £5 for one Year & upwds. and Quitted has said Service in Octr. last and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. where she now lodges she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that John Bates a Drawer at the horn Tavern in Fleet Street did be get the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. this Examinant at that time living a fellor Servt. with the sd. John Bates at the Horn Tavern aforesaid there he became acquainted with her and had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the month of Febry last and

at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnt. and that he the sd. John Bates and no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn July 27th. 1748 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Ann Gough
