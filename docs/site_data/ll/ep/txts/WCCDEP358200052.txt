Middx  Thos. Hanes Aged about 21 Years upon Oath Says he never was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servant Says his Father Richard Hanes lived in and Rented two Houses in Horn Court in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of twelve Pounds and upwards lived in the same several Years and there Dyed Says his Mother Hannah Hanes after the Decease of his Father never Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn ye 14th Day of January 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Thos. Hanes

Upon Mr Robt Wms Complt J. W
