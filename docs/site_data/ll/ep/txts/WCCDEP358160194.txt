Middx  The Examination of Nichs. Corbet passed from the Parish of St. George Hanover Square by Order dated the 27th. of Octr. last Who upon his Oath saith that he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Lord Craves in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx for the Space of 7 Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £7 he this Exaiant at that Time being a Singleman And hath left the sd. Service Upwards of 30 Years to his now best Remembrance And further saith he hath not Since that Time rented any House paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for one or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
