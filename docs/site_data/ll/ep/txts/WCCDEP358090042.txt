Middx to wit Sarah Thornton Aged about Thirty Seven Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of John Thornton (who is now on Board the Security now lying at Jeningham  near Chatham in the County of Kent ) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Shoreditch on Friday in Easter Week last by whom she hath One Child Named John Born a Bastard at the House of Mr. Marrowback in the Green Walk near Christ Church Surry in May 1775 and further say's that her said Husband was bound an apprentice by Indenture bearing Date the Seventh day of April 1760 to his Brother William Thornton a Taylor in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  and afterwards her said Husband Master removed into Serle Street Lincoln Inn fields where her said Husband served the Latter part of his Apprenticeship Vizt. Six Years in all & at the End of Six Years his Master William Thornton delivered up his said Indentures to her said Husband and this Examinant Positively Say's that the said Indres were delivered up some Years before the said William Thornton the Master removed out of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 20th day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

her Sarah Thornton Mark
