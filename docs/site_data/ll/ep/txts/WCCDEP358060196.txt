Middx  Mary Godfrey aged abt. 65 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of George Godfrey who dyed about 4 Years Since and that her sd. late husband rented a house of and Mill in the Parish of Luton in the County of Bedford of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards and lived therein Sevl. Years and that neither her sd. late husbd. nor this Examinant since they Quitted the sd. house have rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Sevt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 26th 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Godfrey
