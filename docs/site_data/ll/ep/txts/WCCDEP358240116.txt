St Clement Danes 18th June 1792.

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Mary le bone  (to wit) Elizabeth Wood  singlewoman aged twenty three Years & upwards Upon Oath and saith That She never was Married That she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Jno. Mountresore Esqr. at No. 53 Lower It for three Years & upwards that the last place in which she resided during such Service was at his House Nod. 53 Lower Harley Street Cavendish Square in the parish of St. Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex where she lodged from the latter latter end of January 1791 till the begin first or second of June then next following when she quitted such Service since which time she hath not lived as an hired Yearly Servant for twelve months together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year or done any other Act Matter or thing Whatsoever Whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And Saith that she is pregnant and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn the 18th day of June 1792 before Us } Joseph Faikney  Chas. Sheppard

Elizabeth Wood
