Westmr.  Frances Beaman  Wido . of Frances Beaman upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Francis Beaman in the Parish Church of Old Hallis in the City of York abt. 17 Yrs. since by whom she hath two Children (viz:) Mary aged abt. 14 Years & James aged about 12 Yrs. Says her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Watkins in Old Street Square in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex  Glover and never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has not since the Death of her sd. Late husband rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 12th. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Frances Beaman
