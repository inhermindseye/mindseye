Middx  Hannah Bradley aged abt. 29 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that about Nine Months ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Briton at the Fountain Tavern in Catherine Street and Exeter Street in the Strand at the Parish of Yearly Wages of £5..10..0 for one Year and upwards and lay during her sd. Service in a Garret in the sd. house in Exeter Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn Janry 29th 1750 before  Tho Lane .

The mark of Hanh. Bradley
