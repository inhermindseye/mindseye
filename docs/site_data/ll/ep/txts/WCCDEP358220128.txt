Middlesex & Westmr.}  Mary Perry Aged about 29 Years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Ebden in Cross Street  Hatton Garden in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex for One Year and Eight Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Nine Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn this 24th. day of February 1770 before us R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Mary Perry Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
