Middx  The Examination of Alexr. Morris - on ye. Behalfes of Ann Deering an Inft. aged one Year & upwds. now in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 17th. Day of July 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. Jno Deering Deced Father of the sd. Inft. Served 7 years apprentiship with one Mr. Witsworth in the Parish of St. Benedict Vulg: Bennet Fink  London  Blacksmith And this Examint. further saith yt. he hath not since he left his sd. Master Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place  as this Examint. hath been informed & verily beleives, that the Father of the sd. Inft. hath been Dead about 11- Months And yt. the Mother is run away & left the sd. Inft. Ann Deering unprovided for.

Sworn the Day & year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Chas: Egerton

Alexer Morris
