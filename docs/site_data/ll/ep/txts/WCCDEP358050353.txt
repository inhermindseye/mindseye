Middx  Sarah Gilbert upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Martin Gilbert who dyed about 8 Weeks Since Says her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr Jona. Blanks in St. Andrews Parish in the Town of Cambridge Woolcomber and never rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 3d. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Sarh Gilbert
