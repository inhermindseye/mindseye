Middx.  Sarah Green aged abt. 22 Years upon Oath says she never was married; says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Fisher at the Red Lion Alehouse in Butcherhall Lane in the Parish of Christ Church in the City of London for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds and hath Quitted said Service abt. One Year; and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 23d. of March 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Sarah Green

upon the Complaint of Mr. Wimberley Ovr J: P:
