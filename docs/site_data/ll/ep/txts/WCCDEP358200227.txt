Middx  Thomas Kearey Aged about forty Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Catherine in the Parish Church of St. Orgin in the City of Dublin about Twenty three Years ago Says he is a Native of Ireland & never Gained a Settlement in England Says he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with One John Hoy a Shoemaker in the said Parish of St. Orgin in the City of Dublin & never after Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says he has three Children by his said Wife (to witt) Mary Aged Twelve Years Larnes Aged Seven Years & Martha Aged about three Years & a half

Sworn the 30th. of January 1765 Before R. Hodgson

Thomas Keary .
