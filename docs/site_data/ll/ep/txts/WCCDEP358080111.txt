Middx & Westmr.}  Elizth. Cowen Aged about Seventy Two Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Patrick Cowen who has been dead upwards of Twenty Years ago and by whom she hath one Son now provided for Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house old Tothill Street in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House near forty Years ago since which time her said Husband nor this Examinant Never gained any other subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 24th. day of January 1777 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Cowen
