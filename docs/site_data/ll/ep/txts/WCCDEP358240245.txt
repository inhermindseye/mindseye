St Clement Danes April 6th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  (to wit) Elizabeth Jenkins Wife of Samuel Jenkins a Substitute in the Middlesex Militia Upon Oath saith that on the 8th day of Septr 1789 she this Examinant being then single and unmarried was Married (by Banns) at the parish Church of Hinlingham near Downham in the County of Norfolk to the Serves for a person in the New Church parish
