Middlesex ss  Mary Hall the wid of John Hall deced maketh oath that her sd late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to & Served the Whole of Such his Apprenticeship by & with Thomas Cartwright a Victualler in the Parish of Allhallows the Less in the City of London & did not afterwards do any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement & that She this Depont Since his death has not done Any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 21. day of May 1784 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Mary Hall
