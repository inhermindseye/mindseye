Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Fowler aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 21st. day of April 1764 she Intermarried with John Fowler her late Husband at the Parish Church of Honit That her said Husband has been dead about Six years That he Rented and lived in a house in Chandos Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for about Two years at yearly Rent of Seventeen Guineas That after quitted said House which was in the year 17 he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or did any Act whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement nor hath this Examinant since her said Husbands D done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a Settlement in her own Right

Sworn this 24th. day of Octr. 1795 } Russ: Clarke J Mathew

Sarah Fowler
