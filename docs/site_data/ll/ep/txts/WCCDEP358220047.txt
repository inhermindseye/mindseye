Westmr.  James Haymers Aged about forty Years on his Oath Says he never was married and that he rented and lived in a House in Alehouse Yard in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted same about four Years ago and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 20th. day of July 1769 before Us R Butler W Kelyngs

James Haymers
