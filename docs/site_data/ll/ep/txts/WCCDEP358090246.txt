Middlesex to wit William Buckingham Aged about Thirty Seven years On his Oath says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Fourteen years ago and by whom He hath Six Children now Living Namely Ann Aged about Twelve years now provided for William Aged about Eleven years, Sarah Aged about Nine years, Elizabeth Aged about Seven years, Benjamin Aged about four years, and Mary Aged about Ten Months, and this Examinant further says that He Rented and Lived in a House at Battle Bridge in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds but paid no Parish Taxes due for the same as He remembers Hath quitted the said House about five years ago since which time He hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement Either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes or otherwise Howsoever

Sworn this 20th. day of Augt. 1781 before  Thos: Parker  Wm Kitchiner

Wm Buckingham
