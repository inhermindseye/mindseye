Westmr to wit   Margt. Waters otherwise Dowdall Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Says that she was Married to one Thomas Dowdall by a Roman Catholick Priest at a House in Rosemary Lane near Tower Hill about Ten Years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living named Elizabeth Aged about Six Years and which was born in the Westminster Hospital and who is now with her reputed Father at Greenwich in the County of Kent Says she was born in Queens County in the Kingdom of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in London either by rating Ten Pounds a Year paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that an Monday the 2d. Instant show delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Houghton Street Clare Market at present cherished is now living and is become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same And further Says that one Menzie Fishmonger in Mount Street Grosvener Square is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of his Body in a House the Corner of St. Martins Court Three sevl. times and one other time in her Mistresses house in Piccadilly some time in the Month of August or September last but the particular Days she does not now remember above belief times he got her with Child of the Child & Children man Bastard Child of which she was so delivered and that the said Menzie and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and bone Father thereof

Sworn this 4th. day of May 1774 before R. Butler

her Margaret Waters mark
