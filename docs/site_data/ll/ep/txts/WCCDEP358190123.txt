Middx  Catherine Warren aged about 26 Years Lodging in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hodgson Perukemaker in Carey Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for Four Years at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds quitted the Same about Ten Weeks And further upon her voluntary Oath says she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to provide for the Same when Born And that James Carr a Journeyman Perukemaker who works with the said Mr. Hodgson did beget such Child or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of October One thousand seven hundred and Sixty at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant.

Sworn the 1st. day of May 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Catherine Warren

Upon Mr. Figg's Complaint
