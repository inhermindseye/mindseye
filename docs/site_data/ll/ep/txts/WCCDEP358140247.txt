Middx.  Susannah Hemblen aged abt. 27 Yrs. on the behalf of her Mother Margt. Hemblen a Lunatic upon Oath says she is the Widow of Saml. Hemblen who has been dead about Eleven Years; says her said Mothers being a Widow lived a hired Yearly Servt. for three Years (at the Yearly Wages of £6 or thereabouts) with Genl. Greys in George Street  Hanrs. Square in the Parish of St Geo: Hanr. Square in the Liberty of Westmr. and hath quitted said Service abt. four Years and has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Yr.

Sworn this 2d. May 1759 before me} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Susanna Hemblen

Upon. the Complaint of Mr. Matravers Ovr. J: P:
