Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Sarah Murry aged about 42 years upon her Oath saith that about 25 years ago she Intermarried with James Murry at the Island of Guernsey and that this Ext. has not heard of him for this 5 years that her said Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship with a Mr. Gine the Banker in Lombard Street in the Parish of Alhallows Lombard Street in the City of London that her sd after her said Husband was out of his Apprenticeship he never did any Act Matter or thing Whereby to the best of this Depts knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Augt. 1786  Sampn Wright M Addington

Sarah Murray
