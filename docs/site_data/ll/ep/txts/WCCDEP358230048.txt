Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Samuel Gent aged about 60 years upon his Oath saith that he never was married That She this Deponent lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Heaton in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Six years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Sixteen pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of March 1799 before me Menter

Saml. Gent
