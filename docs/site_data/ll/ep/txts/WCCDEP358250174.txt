Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Duncan Warren aged about 65 years upon his Oath saith That he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Weston a Shoemaker in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Ten Pounds that he hath quitted said Service upwards of Forty years ago since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of June 1795 before  J. Hlund

Duncan Warran
