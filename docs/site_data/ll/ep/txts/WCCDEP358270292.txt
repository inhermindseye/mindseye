Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Gibbs aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bird in Thanet Place in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Sixteen Months at the yearly wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Nineteen Months since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And this Deponent further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Hugh Mole who is Cornet in the 21st. Regiment of Light Dragons which is now in the West Indies is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Hugh Mole did beget the same thereon and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 22d. day of Febry. 1797 } John Collick

her Elizth. Gibbs Mark

Witness B Newman

Father never taken

Child born at Mr. Birds No. 4 Howard St. named Mary Ann Gibbs

Sent to Enfield  14th: April 1797 Died the 25th. Novr. 1797
