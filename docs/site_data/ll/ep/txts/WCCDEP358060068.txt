Middx  Eliz: Blakey upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Andw. Blakey (who absconds form her and that the said Andrew Blakey Served his Apprentiship with Mr Wood a Baker at Epsom in Surry and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Febry 7th. 1750 before}

Elizbeth Blakey
