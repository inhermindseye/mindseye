Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Carr aged about Nineteen years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Richard Trist a Taylor in Arundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Twelve Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Robert Rogers who did live at the sign of the Eye and Helmet in Wapping but is now on board the William Pitt Indian man now Sailed for Bengal is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th- day of Octr. 1796 } J Reeves

Elzbeth Carr

Man never taken
