Middx to wit Isabella Smith Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Bridd in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement And further On her Voluntary Oath says She is Pregnant with Chid or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish She being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born And that One Richard Parr who lives at Mr. Patricks a Tin Plate worker in Newgate Street did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body in her said Master House some time in the Month of August but the Particular day She does not now remember and at several Different times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which She is now  Pregnant and that the said Richard Parr and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of Jany. 1781 before R Butler

her Isabella Smith Mark
