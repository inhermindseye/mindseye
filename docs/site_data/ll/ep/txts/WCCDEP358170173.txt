Middx  Mary Drury aged abt. Forty Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that abt. Eight Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Scholey at the Kings Arms Tavern in Fleetstreet in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West  London at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the Space of One Year and upwards & further saith that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 7th. of Novr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Mary Drury
