Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westmr.}  to wit Lucy Wilson aged about 63 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried to David Wilson her late Husband at the parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn London about Nine years ago who has been Dead upwards of 5 years Saith her said Husband hath 2 Children now Living by a former Wife Namely Mary Aged about 14 years and David Aged about 10 years And further saith that her said Husband Rented and Lived in a House know by the Sign of Wheat Sheaf in Red- Lyon Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middx for one year at the Yearly Rent of £20 as she believes & paid Taxes due for the Same hath quitted the said House about 10 years ago in since which time he hath not to the best of this DExaminants Knowledge Information or belief gained any other Settlement And  further faith that She this Examinants hath & done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gaine any Settler
