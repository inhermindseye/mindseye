Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Kelly aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Barter at the Sun in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented a House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Janry. 1796 } M Addington

her Ann Kelly mark
