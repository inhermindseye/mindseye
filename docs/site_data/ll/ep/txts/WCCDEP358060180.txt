Westmr.  Ann Tappin  Wido . of John Tappin upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said John Tappin in the Parish Church of St Clement Danes abt. 15 Yrs. ago Says her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Geo Whiteman in little Rider Street in the parish of St James in the Liberty of Westmr. Tallow Chandler and never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. nor has this Examt. Since his Decease done any Act whereby to gain a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 5th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Tappin
