Middx The Exaicon of Anne Bright  Singlewoman taken the Ninth day of Octr. 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that she was bound an Apprentice to & Served Six Months the first part of her Apprentispp with one Mr. Bent at the Black Boy in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx  Victualler & then her sd. Mrs. Broke and further Saith that she never was Assigned over to any Person neither hath this Exaiant rented any House or since the Expiracon of her Indentures of Apprentiship lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yeare or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworn the day & Yeare above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Anne Bright
