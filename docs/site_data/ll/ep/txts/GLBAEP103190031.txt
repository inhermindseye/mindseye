Middlesex ss}  Jane Nathy the wife of Alexander Nathy maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Dunkirk That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served his Apprenticeship with one Alexander a Baker in the Parish of St John in the City of Execeter in the County of Devon & to her knowlge or belief has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement & that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 9. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Jane Nathy
