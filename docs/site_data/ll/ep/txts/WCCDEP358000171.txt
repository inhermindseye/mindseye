St. Clement Danes

passd. to St. Martins  Mary Cookson aged 23 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married Rented a House or paid Taxes or over lived as an hired Yearly Servant Saith That she was  bound Apprentice by Indentures duly Executed by of saint to one Alexander Dow of Great May's Buildings Saint Martin's Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex for the Term of seven Years that she served the whole of her Apprenticeship since which time she this Examinant hath not done any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Decr. 1785 before us  Sampr Wright J Addington

Mary Cookson
