Middlesex  Jane Ward aged about 33 yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married. That she is the Daughter of George Ward by Doreas his wife both deceased That she hath been Informed and verily Believes the sd. George Ward Served his Apprentiship with a Taylor at Carbelonsdel in Westmorland and never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or Lived as a Yearly Servant. And that she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year pd Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 15th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

Jane Ward
