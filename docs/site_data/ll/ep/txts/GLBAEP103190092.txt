Middlesex  Ann Craydon  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago she was hired & lived as a hired Servt . by & with one Mr Cupelow a Victualler who kept the Sign of the Three Tons at Billingsgate in the Parish of St Mary Hill near Billingsgate in the City of London and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages there for the Space of a year & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain another Settlement That She hath two Children namely Sarah Crow aged Six Years and ten Months born in Johnsons Change in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the Sd County and John Crow born in the Same Place and Parish aged about Six Years & one Month both of Whom are illegitimate and Bastards by reason this Deponent was never married & which said children were both baptized in Whitechapel Church

Sworn this. 5. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Craydon
