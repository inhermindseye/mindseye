Westmr.  Jane Turner  wido . of Joseph Turner who dyed about 25 Years ago upon Oath Saith about 20 Years since being at that time a widow and without Child or Children she lived a Yearly Servant with James Pechey  Esqr . in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages or £5 for one Year and upwards and has not since she left the said Mr. Pecheys Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 9th 1752 before Tho: Lane
