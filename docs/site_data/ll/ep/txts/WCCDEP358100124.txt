Middlesex to wit Henry Croasdell One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  (on behalf of Mary Harwood Singlewoman a Lunatic Aged about Thirty Years) on his Oath Says that on strict Inquiry into the Settlement of the said Mary Harwood was Informed by Mrs. Richardson her late Misstrss that the said Mary Harwood lived a hired Yearly servant with her upwards of fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages Seven Pounds Vizt. the first Twelve Months in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West London and the last Three Months in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex where she quitted her said Service about Six Months ago and that she hath gained no other Settlement since quitting her said Service all which Information this Examinant verily believes to be true

Sworn this 18th. day of Septr. 1783 before  James Croft  Tho. Barnfather

Henry Croasdell
