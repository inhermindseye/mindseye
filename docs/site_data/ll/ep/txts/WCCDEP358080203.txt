Westmr- to wit Mary Gosling aged about 23 years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Groom at t he Red Lyon & Still in Drury Lane in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the Co. of Middx upwards of three Years at the Yearly Wages of £4 hath quitted her said Service about five Years ago since which time she has gained no or. subsequent Settlemt. either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th- day of Janry 1777 before  Wm Kitchiner  Tho Bullard

her Mary Gosling Mark
