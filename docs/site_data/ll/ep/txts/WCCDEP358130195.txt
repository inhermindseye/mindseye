Middx  Sarah Rushfield aged about Thirty five Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of James Rushfield Deced. Says he Served Seven Years Appren. with Mr. Davis a Shoemaker opposite St. Stephens Church in Walbrook in the Parish of St. Mary Wool Church London as this Examinants said late Husbd. informed her & as She verily believes Says her said late Husband in his life time nor She since his Death never Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 13th. of Augst. 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Sarah Rushfield
