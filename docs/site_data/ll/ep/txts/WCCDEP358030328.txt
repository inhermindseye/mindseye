Middx  Nath. Goldsmith (removed with Sarah his Wife from the Parish of St. Ann Westmr. by Order dated the 22d. Day of January Instant ) upon Oath saith he intermarried with his sd. Wife Sarah on or about the 17th. Day of March 1741 at the fleet London Says before his sd. Intermarriage & whilst a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Geo: Cornelins  Peraks maker in Deverence & Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £5..10..0 Me at Drink & Lodging for three Years & upwards Says he quitted his sd. Masters Service for the Space of two Months or thereabouts & then came & lived with him again under the same Agreemt. as before in Deverence Court aforesd. for the Space of one Year Says he hath not since he left the said Mr. Cornelins's Service rented ten pounds a Yrs. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 23d. 1746 before  Tho Lane .

Nathaniel Goldsmith
