Middx.  Mary Heap  Wife of Thos. Heap Lodging in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx Upon Oath Says that about Twelve years ago she Intermarried with her sd. Husband Thos. Heap in the Parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate London Says she hath three Children by him Vizt. Jno. aged about five years Andw. aged about three Years & Abraham aged about Eleven Months Says her said Husband rented a House in Houghton Street in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the yearly rent of Seventeen Pounds & lived in the same almost Seven years & hath quitted the same about two years & hath not since rented Ten pounds a year Paid Parish Taxes or Served a Parochiall Office

Sworn Jany 26 1761 before Paul Vaillant

The mark of Mary Heap
