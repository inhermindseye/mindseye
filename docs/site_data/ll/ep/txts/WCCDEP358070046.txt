Westmr. to wit Robert Shread Aged about Eighty Four years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields about Thirty two Years ago and by whom he has no Children now living Says he Rented and lived in a House in Crown Court Russell Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Eight Years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds hath quitted the same upwards of Sixteen Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived as hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 4th. day of November 1772 before Us W Kelynge John Cox

Robert Shread 2s.
