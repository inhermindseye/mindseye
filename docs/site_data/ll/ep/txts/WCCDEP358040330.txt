Middx  Mary Excell upon her Oath saith she is the Widow of Nichs. Excell (who dyed abt. 6 Years since) by whom she hath one Child Mary aged abt. 8 Yrs. Says she knows nothing more of her sd. late husbands Settlement than that he was born at Tidbury in Gloucester shire and that she has not since his decease rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 2d. 1748 before } Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Excell
