Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Hubbard aged about 18 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she is the illegitimate Child (as she is informed) of Mary Hubbard who lived as Servant for fourteen Months with Mr Roberts at the Union Coffee house in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas And this Depont. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Walt Diddle who lives in Pye Street  Westminster is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of June last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Watt Diddle and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only Real & true father thereof

Sworn this 14th. day of Novr. 1794 } N Bond

her Hannah Hubbard Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
