Middx. 

Rose May aged upwards of 15 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she hath been informed & verily believes she is the Daughter of John Farrel by Martha May  Singlewoman & that she was born in the house of next Door to Mrs. Sykes in Angel Court  Drury Lane in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and further saith She never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Octr. 31st. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane

The mark of Rose May
