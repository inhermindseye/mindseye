Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Margarett Edwards aged 23 years or thereabts. upon her Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further on her Oath saith the George Scrivenor a Waiter who lives at the Seagal Coffee House in Holborn is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of May last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she is now pregnant and that the said George Scrivenor and no other Person did beget the said Child and that he is only  Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 3d. day of March 1786 R Butler

Margaret Edwards

the taken a Bond from Geo Such of the Market.
