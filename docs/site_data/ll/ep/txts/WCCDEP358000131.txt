St. Clement Danes

Swears to St. Clemts.  Richard Smith aged 57 Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he was Married to Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the field in the County of Middlesex upwards of 12 Years so that since their Intermarriage they Rented and lived in a House Called the Wheatsheaf in Clement Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes on the county of Middlesex for upwards of 4 Years and at the Yearly Rent of £30 he quitted his sd.  House about 8 Years ago Since which time he this Examinant hath not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge information or belief he may have gained a Settlemt. elsewhere

Sworn this 20th. day of Octr. 1785 before one J Addington

Richard Smith
