Middx  The Exaicon of Sarah the wife of Jno. Breech Lodging in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes taken the 16th. day Augt. 1740 Who upon her Oath Saith that her sd. Husband Jno. Breech rented a House in Little Drury Lane in the parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . of the yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds and lived in the same for the Space of one Year & halfe or thereabts. and hath been come away from sd. House abt. two years And further Saith that her sd. Husband hath not since rented any House pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband is now on Board his Majestys Ship the Tyger & haveing two Children (to wit) Susannah Aged abt. Seven years and Elizth . abt. Six Months is not able to maintain them

Sworn the day & year above named before} Jas: Gronous J Poulson

The mark of Sarah Breech
