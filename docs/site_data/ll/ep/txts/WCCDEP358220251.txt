Westmr. ss  Elizabeth Gates Aged about forty Seven Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Wm. Gates who has been Dead about fifteen Years ago Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Thos. Brake  Esqr . in Mansel Street Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex for five Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds hath quitted her said Service about Ten Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 27th. day of March 1771 before  Petr Planck J Girdler

Eliz Gates
