Middlesex to wit. The examination of Elizabeth Davies of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Singlewoman taken on Oath before us Two of His Majesty's Justices of the Peace in and for the said County of Middlesex this First Day of Novr. in the year of our Lord One Thousand Seven Hundred and Ninety Seven .

Who Saith that on or about the Twenty Eighth Day of August now last past in the Dwelling House of Judith Coleman situate in Greenwood Court Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate aforesaid she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child since Baptized in the Parish Church of St. Paul Shadwell by the Name of Judith And that Francis Emanuel now or late of the Parish of St. George in the said County Mariner is the Father of the said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the Day & Year first above written before R Davies H: Reynell

her Elizh. Davies Mark

Resworn 23d Novr 1797 before same Justices
