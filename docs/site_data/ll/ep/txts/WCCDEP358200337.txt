Westmr.  Prudence Dean aged about 40 Years upon Oath says she was never married and that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Hannah Bates in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds, quitted said Service about Ten Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 23d. day of Decr. 1765 , Before} Frans Cary

The Mark of Prudence Dean
