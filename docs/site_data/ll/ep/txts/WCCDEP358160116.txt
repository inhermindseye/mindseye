Middx The Examination of Mary Nash  Widw . aged abt. 76 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 23d. Day of Febry. 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. 26 Years ago she lived in and Rented a House in Clare Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £20 for the space of one Year & upwds. And further upon her Oath saith that she hath not since that time Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife And being Poor & Old is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Mary Nash
