Westmr.  Ann Pool Aged about Fifty Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Pool who has been dead about Twenty Years, and that he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, was an Apprentice, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says her said husband hath informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that he was born in King Street in the Parish of St. Margarett in the City of Westminster

Sworn the 26th, day of Janry. 1768 before} Willm. Timbrell  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Ann Pool
