Middx & Westmr}  Sarah Bambrough Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Jno. Bambrough Rented and lived in a House called or known by the Sign of the Lyon and Cat in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for many Years at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds or thereabouts and where this Examinant was born And further Upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says one Charles Witherington late a Servant to Captain Clements in Clarges Street  Westminster had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time the latter end of September last and at several other times since at one of Which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that the said Charles Witherington and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 23d. day of May 1769 before me} Thos Kynaston

Sarah Ann Bambrough

Mr. Jon. Wickham  Attorney in Frome
