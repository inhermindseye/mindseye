Middx & Westmr.}  William Wicks Aged about Twenty Eight Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of Reading in Berks about Eight Years ago and who has been dead about Three Years Says he never Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice nor lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says he was born in the Parish of Cookham in the County of Berks when this Examinants settlement now is and that he has not done Any Act whereby to gain any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of August 1769 before me} R. Butler

Wm Wicks
