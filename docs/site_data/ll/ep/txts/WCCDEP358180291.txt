Middx  Ann Warner  Widow ( Removed with Ann her Daughter aged about 14 Yrs. from the Parish of Isleworth in the County of Middx by Order dated the 12th. Febry Instant upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with her late Husbd. Wm. Warner on the 26th. Day of Decr. about 28 Years at the Parish Church of St Clement Danes in the sd. County of Middx Says the sd. Wm. Warner was by Indre Dated the 4th. Day of March 1707 bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mr. Thos: Honley Butcher in Claremarkett in the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes & after the Expiration of his said Apprentiship rented a Shop in the same Markett of the Yearly Rent of £14 for two Years & upwds. & never after gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 16th. Febry 1744 before} Wm. Jones .

Ann Warner
