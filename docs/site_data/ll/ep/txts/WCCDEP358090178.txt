Middlesex to wit Mary Fry Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Holloway at the Crown and Anchor Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds since which time She hath gained no other Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says She is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes She being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born and that one John Young an Apprentice to her Master did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House about the Middle of May last but the Particular Day She does not now remember and at several Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which She is now Pregnant And that the said John Young and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 3rd. day Decr. 1780 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Mary Fry
