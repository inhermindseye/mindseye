Middx  George Gillinder aged upwards of Sixty Years upon Oath says he Rented a House in Bennett's Court  Drury Lane at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds & paid all Parish Taxes lived therein about five Years and quitted the same about Eight Years since and afterwards Rented two other Houses in the same Court, the one at £11 & the other at £9 a Year the last of which he quitted about two Years & a half, since which he hath not Rented any other House Says he Intermarried with Sarah his present Wife about 40 Years since in the Kingdom of Ireland

Sworn the 18th. day of Octor. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

George Gillinder
