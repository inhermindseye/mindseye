Middx  Joseph Thomas aged abt. 18 Years upon Oath saith he has been Informed & verily believes that his father Joseph Thomas Deceased Served that first three years & upwards of his Apprentiship with this Examints. Grandfather a Taylor in the Parish of St John the Baptist at Cardiff in the County of Glamorgan & never was assigned over to any other person and that he this Examint. never  was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn April 10th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Joseph Thomas
