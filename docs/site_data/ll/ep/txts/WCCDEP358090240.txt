Middx to wit Ann Doughty Aged about Twenty One years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she has lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Owen a Taylor in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of Six pounds & where she at present lives And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to to become Chargeable to the said Parish (she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born) And further that one John Millington a Servant to one Mr. Palmer a West India merchant who now lives at No. 2 Park Street in the City of Bristol did begot such Bastard Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at having of women the Strand sometime in the month of November last but the particular day she does not now remember and at one other time afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Millington and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 21st. day of July 1781 before  Sampn Wright

Ann Doughty
