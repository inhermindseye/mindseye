Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Isabella Swale aged about 57 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Stevens who lived in the Country in the Summer time and in London in the Winter that at the yearly wages of Five pounds that she lived with the said Mrs. Stevens for Twelve years or thereabouts And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that the last Forty days of her said Service with the said Mrs. Stevens was at the house of a Mr. Lewis at Hammers in the Parish of Hammersmith in the County of Middlesex that she hath quitted said Service about Twelve years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of August 1796 J Robinson N Bond

Isabell Swale
