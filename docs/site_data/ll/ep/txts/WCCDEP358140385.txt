Middx.  Mary Head aged abt. Sixty Years lodging at Mr. Greenough's in Arrundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Geo: Head who has been dead abt. fifteen Years Says that the said Geo: Head Rented a Tenement consisting of a Shop and two rooms in Black horse Yard in Monmouth Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the said County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and lived therein Sevl. Years and after he Quitted said Tenemt never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes Says she has not Since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 28th. of June 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary h. The Mark of Mary Head

On Mr. Pollards Complt. W. C
