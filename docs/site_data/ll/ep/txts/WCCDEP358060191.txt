Middx  Rebecca Hill upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Robt Hill on the 29th. of October about 32 Years since in the Parish Church of St Botolph Aldgate London & that the said Robt. Hill abt. 25 Yrs. since (being at that time a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Thompson at the Plough Inn in Cary Street for Sevl Years at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds and has not Since he Quitted that Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 19th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Rebecca Hill
