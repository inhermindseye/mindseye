Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Howard aged about 69 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired yearly servant for one year ever bound an apprentice or Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes that she was born at Bedford  in the Parish of Saint Paul in the county of Bedford where her Father was bred and born and fivethere this Examinant on behalf of sarah Howard her Sister further maketh Oath and saith that her said sister was born in the said Parish of saint Paul in the County of Bedford and that she never gained any settlement either by Marriage Renting house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes ever living as a hired yearly servant for one year on ever being bound an apprentice

Sworn this 15th. day of Febry. 1790  Philip Dyot D Walker

her Mary Howard  Mark
