Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Peisley aged about 42 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented a house the strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly Rent of One Hundred Guineas that he hath quitted Said House about Eighty years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Septr. 1789 R Butler

Jas. Peisley
