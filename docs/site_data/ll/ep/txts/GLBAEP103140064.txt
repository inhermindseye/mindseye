Middlesex ss}  William Lindsay maketh oath That he was duly bound apprentice by Indre to one George Crew of St Martins Lane in the parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the sd County  Taylor & Served about two years of Such his Apprenticeship & then this Depont with his said master removed into Litchfield Street in the Parish of Saint Ann Soho in the City & Liberty of Westmr. in the said County where this Depont Served the remainder of his said Apprenticeship & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Depont. has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten pounds or Serve any parish office or paid any poors Rates or Kings Tax or to his knowledge or belief hath he done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement that he hath a wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully Married.

Sworn this 14th. day of November 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

William Lindsay
