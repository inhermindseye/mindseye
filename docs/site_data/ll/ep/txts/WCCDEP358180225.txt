Middx  Mary Hiscocks upon her Oath saith that on the Eighteenth Day of March  about 14 Years since she intermarried with her present husband  Richard Hiscocks at the Fleet London that the sd. Richard Hiscocks Served One Year & upwds. the last part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Wm Hiscocks  Shoemaker in  Glassenbury Court in the parish of St. Paul Covt Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. that since the Expiration of his said Apprentiship the sd. Richd: Hiscocks hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 24d. Augt. 1744 before} Ja: Fraser

The Mark of Mary Hiscocks
