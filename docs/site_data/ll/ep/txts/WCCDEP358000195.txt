Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) John Ashton aged 32 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he never was married That he was bound an Apprentice by Indentures duly rented to Mr. John Grace of Russell Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex that he served seven years and upwards, being than his whole time under this Indres that since quitting his said Masters Service he has never done any Act Matter or this whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 3rd Janry 1785 before  Tho Collen G Mercer

John Ashton
