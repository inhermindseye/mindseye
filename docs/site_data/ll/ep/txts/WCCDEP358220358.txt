Westmr. to wit David Evans aged about Fifty Three Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with his late Wife  Elizth. Evans at m Fair Chapell about Twenty Eight Years ago and who has been Dead upwards of 10 Years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living (except one Illegitimate Child before Marriages) which is now Provided for in Philladelphia Says that he being a Widower and without Child or Children he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one George Green a Distiller in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 15 Months and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £8. hath quitted his said Service upwards of 9 Months ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 12th. day of June 1772 before me.} Tho Lane

The Mark of David Evans
