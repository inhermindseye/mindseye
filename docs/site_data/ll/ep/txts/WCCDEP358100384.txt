Middlesex (ss)  Mary Dax Aged about 30 years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she Lived as a Hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Flynn of Norfolk Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes for Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted said Service upwards of Eighteen Months, Since which time she has gained no other settlement than as aforesaid and further on her Voluntary oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and is become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when Ban and further saith that and James Madan who formerly kept the Black Bull the Corner of West Lane Rotherhithe is the Father of such Child or Children he havg. had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of June last and as several different times afterwards at some or one of such times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the sd. James Madan & no other person did beget the same & that he is the Real & true father thereof Says she believes the father is gone to live at Blackwall , that his Br lives the Corner of D and Bear Yard in Tooley Street

Sworn this 16th. Day of April 1785 before me R Butler

The Mark Mary Dax of
