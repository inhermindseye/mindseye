Middlesex ss}  Hannah Carr the widow of Michael Carr deced maketh oath that She has been informed her said husband & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to and Served about Six Years of his Apprenticeship with Mr Thomas Sansum in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County when his Indres were by Consent of himself and his Master given up for the Purpose of being Cancelled and he was discharged from his Apprenticesp. That he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her Knowledge information or belief nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath a Child named Michael aged three Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this 15. day of January 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Hannah Carr
