Middx  Richard Middleton aged about 38 Years upon Oath says he Intermarried with Diana his Wife at the Fleet London the 24th. of August 1741 and that he hath two Children by his said Wife namely John aged about Ten Years and Richard aged about fifteen Months Says he Rented a House in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the yearly Rent of Twenty seven Pounds lived therein about four years and quitted the same about Eight Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 26th. Day of March 1756 . before}
