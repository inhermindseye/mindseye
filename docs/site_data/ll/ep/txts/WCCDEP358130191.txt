Middx  Earminea Scauby aged abt. Twenty one Years upon Oath Says She was never married that She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes where She now liveth She being poor & unable to maintain the same when Born & that one Thomas Durand a Journeyman Taylor who then Lodged at Mr. Goldsborought a Perukemaker in the Rolls Buildings did beget the said Child or Children on Her Body the said Thomas Durand having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body at a Publick house in the City of London about the Latter End of Novr. or beginning of Decd 1755. & at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which She is now pregnant & that he the said Thos. Durand & no other Person did beget the same Says

She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Bolieaur in Portugal Street & in Searl Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abt. Four Years Says She hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 3d. Day of Agust. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond 

Earminea Scauby
