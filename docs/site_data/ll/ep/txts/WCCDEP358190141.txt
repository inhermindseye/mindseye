Alice Hopkins aged about 52 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Woodfall, Printer in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for one year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds Quitted the Same about Twenty Years and hath not since kept House paid Parish Taxes or been a Yearly hired Servant for one year

Sworn the day of June 1761 Before}
