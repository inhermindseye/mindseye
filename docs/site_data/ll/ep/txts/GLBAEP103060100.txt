Aldgate Middx

Thomas Knightly Maketh Oath that about 50 years ago he was an Apprentice by Indenture and Servd Seven years as Such to Richard Spaulding a Carman in Dog And Bear alley then in the Parish of Saint Olave Southwark in the County of Surry now the Parish of Saint John in Southwark in the County of Surry And never gaind any other Settlement in any other Parish or place hath a wife named Katherine

Sworn 26th day of March 1748 before Robert Dennett Richd Ricards

Thos Knightly
