Middlesex ss}  Thomas Chaddick maketh oath That his late Mother Hannah Chaddick deced after the death of his late Father John Chaddick deced lived in and rented a house at the yearly Rent of Thirty pounds in Kingsgate street in the Parish of St George Bloomsbury in the sd County & resided thereon for Several Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & where this Deponent lived with his Said Mother as part of her Family & this Deponents Said Mother to his Knowledge or belief did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever did any Act to acquire a Settlement in his own right.

Sworn this 29 day of Janry 1778 before  Parkr Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

Thos. Chaddick
