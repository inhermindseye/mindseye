Middx  Margaret Wright Aged About Forty Five Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Emery Wright who hath been Dead about Twenty Years with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of Frinsbury in the County of Kent Says she Rented a House in Arrundell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about Three Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds or there abouts hath quitted said House about Sixteen Years and she hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the Day of January 1764 Before}

Upon Mr Olivers Complaint Overseer J. W
