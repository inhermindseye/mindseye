Middx  Rt. Cobham upon Oath Saith he Intermarried with his Wife  Sarah Cobham at May fair Chapel near 9 years ago & has by him one Child James aged three years-that he Served Seven years Apprentiship with Mr Crang an Organ maker near Temple bar in the parish of St. Clement Danes and has been out of his time upwds. of 9 yrs. Since which he has not rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworne June 22d. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Robert Cobham
