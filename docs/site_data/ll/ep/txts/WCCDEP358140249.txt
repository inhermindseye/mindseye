Middx.  Ann Lowman lodging at Mr. Smith's the Corner of Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. on the behalf of John Griffiths an Infant aged abt. five Years who was lately brought and left in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes upon Oath says that Benjm. Griffiths the father of the said infant Rented a house in the Parish of East Greenwich in the County of Kent in the Year 1755 and paid the Parochial Taxes there as appears by a Receipt dated the £14 day of July 1755. signed John Green Collector says that the sd. Benjn. Griffiths has Enlisted for a Soldier in the Light House and that the Mother has Absconded; and that they were married abt. twelve Years ago in the Parish Church of East Greenwich aforesaid as she has been Credibly informed & believes

Sworn this 8th. day of May 1759 before} Paul Vaillant  Saunders Welch

The Mark of Ann Lowman

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Joseph Burnthwaite  Overseer J: P:
