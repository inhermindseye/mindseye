Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit George Bromley aged about 19 years upon his Oath saith that he was born in Wild Street in the Parish of saint Clement Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex that he never was bound an apprentice lived as a lived yearly servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Decr. 1789 R Butler D Walker

her George Bromley Mark
