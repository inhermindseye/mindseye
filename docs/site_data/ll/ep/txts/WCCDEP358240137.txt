St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Ann Brown Aged 28 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she was bound an Apprentice for two Years with Mr. Haning who lodged at the House of Mr. Kossetor Baker in the Strand in the the precinct of the Savoy in the County of Middlesex and by Indenture or Agreement executed to Carrs the Business Occupation Act or Mystery of a Mantua Maker That She duly served her said Master during day the whole of such Term and Boarded Lodged during the last 10 ten Months of her time with her Father at his House in Crown Court Butcher Bow in the parish of Saint Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster in the said County that Since the Expiration of her time which (was about ten Years ago) she hath never Rented any House a Lodging of ten pounds a Year lived as an hired Yearly Servant or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And that She is Poor and unable to Maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn the 17th August 1792 before Me } Wm Kitchiner

Ann Brown
