Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Wheeler of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County  Singlewoman taken on oath before us Burford Camper and John Sherwood  Esquires two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County of Middlesex this 15th Day of August 1774

Who on her oath Saith That in the month of May in the year of our Lord one thousand Seven hundred and Sixty Six She this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of one Mr Robertson at the End of the Rope Walk in Artichoak Lane in the Parish of Saint George in the said County of Middlesex which said Child had been Since baptised by the name of John Anderson and is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and has done no Act to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 15th. day of August 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Sarah Wheeler
