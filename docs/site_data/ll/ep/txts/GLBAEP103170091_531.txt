Middlesex ss}  John Taylor maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house of the yearly Rent & Value of Eleven pounds & ten Shillings in that part of Saint Catherines Lane Which her in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & resided therein for Several years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the said Parish That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that he hath a Wife named mary to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 14 day of August 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

John Taylor
