Middx  Mary Collins  Singlewoman aged abt. 17 Yrs. removed from the parish of St. Martin in the feilds by Order Dated the 8th. Day of Decr. Inst. upon her Oath saith that her Father & Mother are both Deced that her sd. Father  Thos. Collins lived in & rented a house in Cowcamber Court in Ship Yard in the parish of St Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £12. for the Space of 1 Yr. & upwds. & her sd. Mother  Mary Collins Continued to live in & rent the sd. house for the Space of one Year or thereabts. that after the Death of her Father that her sd. Mother in her Life time after she left the sd. House never Rented £10. a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place that this Examinant never was as Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for 1 Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Decr. 10th. 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

Mary Collins
