Middx:  Christr. Boothman aged abt. 32 yrs. upon Oath says he intermarried with Margt . his Wife at Mayfair Chapel upwards of Eight years ago says before his intermarriage & whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired yearly Servant with Lady Bingley in Henrietta Street Cavendish Square in the Parish of St. Mary Le bone in the County of Middx. at the yrly. Wages of fourteen Pounds for Twenty Months & never Rented ten Pounds a yr. or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 17th of August 1757 before} Barth: Hammond Wm 

Christopher Boothman
