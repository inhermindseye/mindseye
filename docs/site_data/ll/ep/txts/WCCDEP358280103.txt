Mrs. Bahan lodges at Mrs. Robinson No. 12 Clements Lane

Saint Clement Danes

Middx. & Westmr.} to wit Ann Bahan aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent of £10 or paid Taxes that she was born at Kildare in Ireland And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that on the 3rd. day of May last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of one Burnett a Journeyman Taylor in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary Ann that one Mr. Alexr. Mr. Kane late a Purser on board of one his Majestys Ships who lodges at the Temple Exchange Coffee House in Fleet Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of June 1786 and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Alexander Mc. Kane is the only Real & true father thereof

Sworn this 7th. day of July 1789 } R Butler

her Ann Bahan Mark
