Middx  Margt. Evans wife of Ambrose Evans (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Ambrose Evans at the Fleet London upwds. of Ten Years ago by whom she hath one Child Griffith aged about Ten Months and that she hath been Informed and verily believes her sd. husband Served 5 years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Leroux in King Street in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. and then they parted by Consent & her sd. husband never was assigned over to any person nor ever rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant

Sworn Octr. 27th. 1752 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Margerat Evans
