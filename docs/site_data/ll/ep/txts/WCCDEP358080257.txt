Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Radon Aged about Twenty five years On her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Holloway at the Crown and Anchor Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for thirteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Seven pounds hath quitted her said Service about Ten Days ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that One George Ward who keeps the Crown in Essex Street is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime the later End of October but the particular day she does not now Remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said George Ward and no other person did begett the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 19th. day of June 1778 before  Wm. Kitchiner

her Elizth. Radon Mark.
