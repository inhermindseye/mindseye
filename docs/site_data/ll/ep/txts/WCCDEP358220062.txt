Westmr.  Mary Lucas aged about 40 Years on her Oath says she never was married and that she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bass at Knights Coffee House in Essex Street for Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath Quitted her said Service about 14 Years ago & never after rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says that on the 6th. Day of September 1767 she was Delivd. of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Mills in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized by the Name of Jane and Further Says that one of John Smith (who has been gone to India about 2 Years and an half and where he now is as she believes is the Father thereof he havg. had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of Decr. 66 and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child which she was Delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Smith and no other Person is the real & true Father Thereof

Sworn this 25th Day of August 1769 before me} R. Butler

May Lucas
