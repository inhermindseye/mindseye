Middlesex ss  Frances Gibbs  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mr Pendergrass a Victualler in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued there under Such hiring for the Space of two years That she has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever bound an Apprentice nor was she ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this. 13. day of July 1786 before J Hawley

The Mark of Frances Gibbs
