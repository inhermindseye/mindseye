Saint Clement Danes 19th. July 1786

Middlesex (to wit) Ann Mitchell aged 30 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to Walter Mitchell at the Parish Church of Saint Margaret Westmr in the County of Middlesex about two Years and an half ago that before her the does not know any Settlement the said Walter Mitchell ever gained nor where he was born that he has deserted her there two Years and she does not know where he is gone that whilst she was a singlewoman she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Berry at the Queen Head Tavern the Corner of Grays Iron in Holborn at the Yearly Wages of seven pounds for about two Years this since in thing the same she has not to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief Done any Act Matter or thing Whereby she may have gained any the Settlement-and that the this Examinant is poor and in gave as Distress and unable to maintain herself having a Young Child to Fachalr and

maintain, and that she has applied to the Overseers of the Poor of Saint Andrew Holborn being the Parish where she lived as a Yearly Servant with Mr Berry as aforesaid and they before refered to relieve her unless she should come to them regularly pased by Justices Order of Removal

Sworn this 20th. July 1786 before  Thos. Brooksbank J Leroux

Ann Mitchaell
