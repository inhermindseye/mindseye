Middx  Margt. Pike upon Oath saith she is the Widow of William Pike with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St Bennet Paulswharf about 16 Yrs. ago & by whom she hath one Child Thos . aged abt. 14 Yrs. who never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. and that her sd. husband (whilst a Singleman) lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Ravenhill in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middx for thirteen months & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £ and never after he quitted that Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year that her sd. late husband dyed in June last and she has not since his Decease rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn feby. 16th. 1753 before Tho Lane

The Mark of Margt. Pike
