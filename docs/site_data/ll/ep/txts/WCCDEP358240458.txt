Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Mary Squire aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Price in Serle Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Ten Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of Decr. 1794 } Edw Read

Mary Squire
