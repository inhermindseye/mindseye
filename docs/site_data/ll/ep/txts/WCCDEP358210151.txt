Westmr.  Eleanor London aged about 46 years upon Oath says she is the Wife of John London with whom she intermarried in May fair Chapel about Twenty Years ago by whom she has One Child named Eleanor aged about Sixteen Years. says her said Husband Rented a house in Charles Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds quitted said House about sixteen Years ago, and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 14th, day of April 1767 . before} Paul Vaillant R Kilsha

The Mark of Eleanor London
