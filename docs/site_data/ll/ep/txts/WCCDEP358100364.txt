Middlesex & Westmr.}  Swears to St. C. D .  Mary Boutflower aged 44 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That She never was married, That she lived with Mr. Lackam of Cecil Street in the Strand as a Hired Yearly Servant at the Wages of £7 per Ann. That she lived with him upwards of Two Years That the greatest part of the Time She lived with him on the East Side of the Street That about Two Months before she left his Service he moved to the West Side of the Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster , That she went with & Served him there during such Two Months, That since she quitted his Service she has done no Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement, to the best of her Knowledge or belief

Sworn before us this 19 Febry 1785 .  Wm Gascoigne  James Croft
