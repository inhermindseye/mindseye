Middlesex ss}  Frances Epord  Singlewoman maketh oath That She can give no Account respecting the Settlemt of her late Parents or either of them That She this Depont was born in Swan Alley in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County and never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year nor was She ever Married nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 21st day of December 1778 before Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Frances Epord
