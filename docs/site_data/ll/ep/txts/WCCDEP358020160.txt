Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William King aged about 40 years upon his Oath saith that on the 20th. day of July 1776 he Intermarried with Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Brides alias Bridged that he has Two Children living by his said Wife vizt. Catherine aged about Eleven years and William aged about Ten years That he this Examinant Rented and lived in a House in Capel Court Bartholomew Lane in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew by the Royal Exchange in the City of London for about Six Month at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas that he hath quitted said House about Three Two years since which time he has not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Augt. 1789  Philip Dyot D Walker

Willm. King
