Middx  Ann Cherrington upon Oath saith she never was married and that on the 8th Day of June 1751 she was delivered of a male bastard Child at the house of Mrs. Langley in St Clements Lane in the parish of St Ctlemt Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. which was baptized Thomas & is still living & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same and that Thomas Green   late a Clerk to Mr. Strut of Breams Buildings Chancery Lane did begot the said male bastard Child on her body the said Thomas Green having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the Months of Septr. 1750 and at several times afterwards at one of which he got her with Child of the sd male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid & that the said Thomas Green & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Octr 31st 1753 before} Barth: Hammond

ann Cherington
