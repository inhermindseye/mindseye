Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Charlotte Cooling aged about Twenty years upon her Oath saith That about Eight Months ago she Intermarried with Samuel Cooling her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. George Aged about Three Months That her said Husband is now Serving one Mr. Birkwood a Mathematical Instrument maker, in Crane Court on Peters Hill in the Parish of Saint Peters Paul Wharf in the City of London as an Apprentice and has been so for these five years past

Sworn this 7th day of Janry 1796 N Bond J Mathew

Charlotte Colling
