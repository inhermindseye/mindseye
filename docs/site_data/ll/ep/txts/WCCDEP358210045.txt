Westmr.  Mary Hart wife of John Hart , Labourer (lodging in Mr. Cole's Rents in Drum Alley in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster on the behalf of Jane Burnett aged about four Years and Elizabeth Burnett aged about two Years, the Children of Joshua Burnett by Ann his Wife who have both run away and left their said Children whereby they are likely to become chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes) Upon Oath says she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true that the said Joshua Burnett served his Apprenticeship to a Breeches maker in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster aforesaid and that the said Joshua Burnett with his family were in the Workhouse of the said Parish of St. George and were discharged there from about Six Weeks 

Sworn the 22d. April 1766 Before} Thos: Balack  Fran. Cary

The Mark of Mary Hart
