Westmr.  Mary Roberts aged upwds. of 22 Yrs. removed from the parish of St. Giles in the Feilds by Order dated the 25th. Day of Decr. Instant upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mrs. Biggs in Clare Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for the Space of one Year & upwds. & hath left her sd. Service about one Year Says she has not since Sh left the sd. Mrs. Biggs Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Roberts
