Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Guest aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the 5th. day of Augt . now last past she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary That one William Plant who was Servant to Mr. Randel in Garden Court Grays Inn is the father of the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 7th. day of Septr. 1790 } R Butler

Elizabeth Guest

£10 paid Expences to be allowed thereout
