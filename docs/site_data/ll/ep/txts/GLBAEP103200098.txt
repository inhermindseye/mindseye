Middlesex ss}  John Lee maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with one Mr Jones a Victualler at the Sign of the Tobeys Deg in St Pauls Church Yard in the Parish of Saint Gregory by Saint Pauls in the City of London & Continued in such Service under such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of a year & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain anothr settlement

Sworn this. 13. day of Febry 1784 before R Davies Robt Smith

The Mark of John Lee
