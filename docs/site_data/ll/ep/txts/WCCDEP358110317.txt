Westmr.  Wm. Saunderson upon Oath saith she was married to his wife Rebecca Saunderson in the Parish Church of St Clemt. Danes in the year 1740 or 1741 & has by her 4 Childn Eliz aged upwds. of 11 Years Mary aged upwds. of 7 Years Ann aged upwds. of 5 Years & Jas . aged abt. 20 months and that he rented a house of £17 by the Year in Witch Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes afsd. & lived therein about two Years quitted the sd house in the year 1742 & has not since rented ten Pounds a year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn Septr. 25th 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Wm. Saunderson
