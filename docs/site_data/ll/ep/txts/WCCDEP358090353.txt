Middx  Elizth. Grist Aged about 29 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Barnett at the Cheshire Cheese in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said service upwards of 2 Years ago since whch. time she hath gained no other Settlemt. And further on her Oath Says that on the 15th. of Octr. last she was delived of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish which hath since been baptized by the Name of James & is now Chargeable to the sd. Parish And further that one James Arnold a Waiter at the Ax & Gate in Downing Street of Westmr . but what is become of him she does not know had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her said late Masters House in Downing Street afsd. Some time in the month of January last but the Particular Day she does not now remember And that the sd. Jas. Arnold & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 6th. Day of Decr. 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Grist
