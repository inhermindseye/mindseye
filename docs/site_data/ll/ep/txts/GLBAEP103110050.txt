Middlesex ss}  John Mackmanen  Mariner aged about Fifty nine Years Maketh oath That Divers Years ago he was Duly bound to & Served his Apprenticeship with Edward Mackman in the Town of Ennie in the County of Clare in the Kingdom of Ireland And that to his Knowledge or belief he never did any Act to gain a Settlement in the Kingdom of Great Britain and lastly saith that he served in his Majestysship th the Carcass Bomb about three Years

Sworn this 28th day of April 1772 before  John Smart

The Mark of John Mackmanen
