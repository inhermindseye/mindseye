Middlesex ss}  Jane Mc Donald the widow of Alexander Mc Donald deced maketh oath that her said late husband heretofore rented a house of the yearly rent of four pounds & ten Shillings in Cross field Lane in the Parish of Saint Paul Deptford in the County of Kent & during such his residence therein he was charged to and paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the same That he did not afterwards rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt nor hath she this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself nor hath She been married to any person since his death & that about fourteen Months ago she this Depont had relief administred to her by the then Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish of St Paul Deptford as being the place of her last legal Settlement

Sworn this. 14. day of July 1785 before R Davies J Hawley

The Mark of Jane Mc Donald
