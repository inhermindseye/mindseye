Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Lockwood aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Thomas Plimpton a Hosier in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Five years ago since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 14th. day of October now last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the Licensed Laying in House in Water Lane  Fleet Street that one Thomas Neary a Porter at No. 6 Piazzas Convent Garden is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of December last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Thos. Neary is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of Novr. 1788 } R Butler

Resworn this 6th. day April 1789 before } R Butler

Ann Lockwood

Ann Lockwood to be heard of at Miss Walkers No. 1 Newcastle Court

Thomas Neary is a Short Man Pitted with the Small Pox very much Flaten Hair and has but one Eye
