Middx  Margt. Macklin Aged about 74 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or pd. Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servt . with one Nichs. Robinson  a Taylor in Ogden Court Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for four Years & an half at the Yearly Wages of £5. hath quitted her sd. Service about 30 Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. by living as a hired Yearly Servt for one Year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 7th. day of Jany. 1783 before R Bulter

her Margt. Macklin  Mark
