Westmr.  Richd. Price on behalf of Joseph Lock aged about three Years and James Lock aged about nine months the Infant Children of Chas. Lock by Jane Lock his Wife both deceased upon his Oath saith that he has been informed & beleives that John Lock the Grandfather of the sd. Infant Child lived in and rented a house of Ten pounds a Year & upwards in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middx for sevl. Years and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes and never after he quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds Year paid Parish Taxes Served an annual Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Exaiants. knowledge or beleif Says he has been informed & beleives that Charles Lock Son of the above named John Lock and father of the sd. Inft. Children was born in Rosemary Lane aforesd. in the sd. Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel and never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes Served an annual Office was an

Apprentice or Yearly Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 28th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones .

Richd: Price
