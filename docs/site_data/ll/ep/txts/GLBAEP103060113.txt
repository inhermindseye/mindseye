Aldgate Middx

Mary Dickenson  wife of Robert Dickenson (who is gon Away from her) Maketh Oath that her Said husband hath often Informd her that he Servd his Apprentice Ship t in Shoe-lane to a Chairmaker And Sawyer in the Parish of Saint Bridget Vulgarly st Brides and in the City of London and never heard him Say he ever gained Any other Settlement

Sworn 9th day of March 1748 before Richd. Ricards N. Dukinfield

the mark of Mary Dickenson
