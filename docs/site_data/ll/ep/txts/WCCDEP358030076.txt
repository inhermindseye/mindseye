Middx  Ann Brace  Wife of Jno. Brace a Soldier in his Majestys Service upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the said John Brace on the 28th. Day of Septr. 1744 at Bath Says her sd. Husband was born in the Parish of Rubra in the County of Somersett & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examints: knowledge or beleif

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Brace
