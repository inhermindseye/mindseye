Middx  Eliz. Thomas aged upwds. of 70 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of John Walton Thomas with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about 37 Yrs. ago Says she can give no Account whatever of his Settlemt. and that before her sd. Intermarriage & whilst a Singlewoman she lived a Yearly Servant with her Sister Mary Kingsmill  Millener for the Space of Two Years & upwards at the Yearly wages of £3 and lay all the time of her sd. Service in her Sisters House near Hemlock Court in Cary Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Says she has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented a house of Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn June 25th. 1751 before  Tho. Lane Elizabeth Thomas
