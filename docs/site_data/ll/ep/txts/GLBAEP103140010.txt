Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Potter the Widow of Robert Potter deced maketh oath that Several Years ago her said late husband Rented a house of Four Pounds a year Rent in Cartwright Square in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same & Continued therein for about the Space of one year & half & by means thereof as She is advised her said husband gained a legal Settlement in the sd. Parish That her said husband did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself Since her husbands Death

Sworn this 10th. day of December 1773 before  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Elizabeth Potter
