Saint Clement Danes August 1st. 1785

Middx (ss)  Passd. to St Dunstan in the West  Celia Sewey Aged Forty One years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she Lived with Mr James Tow of Cliffords Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Duston in the West in the City of London for upward of Three Years at the Annual Wages of £6 That She quitted her said service about 20 years ago-And That since she Quitted it she has not done any Act Matter or thing Whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have a Settlement elswhere

Sworn before us this First Day of August 1785  John Croft  Jno: Wright

the Mark of Celia Sewery
