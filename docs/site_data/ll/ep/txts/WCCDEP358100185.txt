Middx to wit Edwd. Mc.Kanna Aged about Twenty four years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Susannah his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about three years ago and by whom he hath two Children now living (vizt.) Sarah aged about Eighteen Months and anr. Child which is intended to be Christened by the name of Susannah aged about two Months says he served seven years Apprenticeship to one Henry Davis a Shoemaker in Hanoway Yard  Oxford Road in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middx and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Tenemt. of or Lodging of £10. a year or or.wise gained any or. Settlement than as aforementioned

Sworn this 20th day of Febry. 1784  Wm Kitchiner  Edward Hodsoll

Ed. Mc.Kanne
