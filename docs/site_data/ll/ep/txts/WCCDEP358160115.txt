Febry. 23d. 1739

Middx  The Examination of Priscilla Hudson on the behalfe of Sarah Billington  Widw . Who upon her Oath saith that Rt. Billington Husband of the sd. Sarah before his Intermarriage with her & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Bagnell Sugar Baker for Sevll. Years at the Yearly Wages of £16 And further saith yt. he lived about Six Months the latter part of his Service (imediately before his said Intermarriage with the sd. Sarah) in Prescott Street  Goodman Fields in the Parish of St. Mary White Chapel in the County of Middx And further saith yt. the sd. that Rt. Billington in his life time nor Sarah his Wife since his Death never Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examint. hath been informed & beleives That the sd. Sarah being Sick & Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above Year menconed} before} Tho Lane  Jas. Gronous

Priscilla Hudson
