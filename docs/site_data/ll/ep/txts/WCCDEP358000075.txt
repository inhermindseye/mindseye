St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Passd. to St. Giles in the fields to wit Elizabeth Grigson aged about 17 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Benjamin Grigson her Present husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn in the Month of January last That in the Month of April last she was delivered of a Male Child which has not as yet been baptized at the House of Mr. Healy at No.18 Duke Street Lincolns Inn Fields in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex where her Husband and she rented a Lodging at Nine Shillings Per week for two Months and upwards that her Husband during such Time was Clerk to Wm. Manley  Esqr . a Banister at Law in the Inner Temple being an Exparochial Place at a yearly Salary and Perquisites of near or about £100 a year That her Husband has deserted her and her Child That she is unable to maintain herself and Child that she does not know of any other Settlement over Obtained by her said Husband

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1785 R Taylor James Croft

E Gregson
