St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martins in the fields  presently after (to wit) John Vendermeulen aged 49 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith That her never was Married, that he lived as an hired as a servant for four lived under a Yearly hiring as Servant or Assistant to Mr John Mondo of New Round Court in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County of Middlesex at the Wages of about 5 Guineas, that his said Master her died & this Deponent left the House being about 28 Years ago and hath not since lived under a Yearly hiring for twelve Months together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge Information or belief, And that he is now distressed Ill and unable to maintain himself without Assistance

Sworn the 20th day of August 1792 before us } Wm. Kitchner  Gd: Mitchell

John Vandermeulen
