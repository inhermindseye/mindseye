Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Robert Gibbeson aged about 51 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as hired Yearly Servant with Robert Richard Gammon in Lawrence Poulteney Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin Ongar in the City of London for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Fourteen Pounds that he hath quitted Said Service about Fifteen Years ago that he hath quitted said Service ab since which time he has done No Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Octr. 1788  Sampn Wright M Addington

Robt. Gibbeson
