Middlesex to wit Jane Bradforth Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of John Bradford who is gone for of a Soldier with whom she Intermarried at Christ Church in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry about five Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children living Namely Mary Aged about Three Years and John Aged about Twenty Months Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Messrs. Windle and Mackentosh Glass Cutters in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex And after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 27th. day of Octr. 1778 before  Abrm Baley

her Jane Bradforth Mark
