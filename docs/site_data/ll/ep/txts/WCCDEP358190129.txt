Middx  Mary James aged about 45 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Henry James who died at Sea about Years since to whom she was Married on the 11th. day of July 1746 That her said Husband lived a hired Yearly Servant 32 Years since with one Mrs. Webb a Cook in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upwards of one year and never kept House paid Parish Taxes or was a hired Servant afterwards as her said Husband hath informed her & which Information she believes to be true Says she has One Child named Thomas aged about Nine Years

Sworn the 13th. day of May 1761 Before}
