Middx to wit Ann Boveall Aged about Fifty five Years on her Oath says She is the Widow of James Boveall who died in December last And with whom She Intermarried at Fleet London about Thirty Three Years ago and by whom She hath one Child living named Ann Boveall Aged about Twenty Three Years now provided for says her said late Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Thomas Hooker a Watchmaker in Golden Lane in the Parish of Saint Lukes Old Street in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or Otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 22nd. day of Augt. 1780 before R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

her Ann Boveall mark
