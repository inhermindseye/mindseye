Middx  John Ramsom Aged upwards of 70 Years Upon Oath Saith he intermarried with Elizth his Wife at the Fleet London about Eleven Years Since Says he rented a House in Kings Head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr : of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds lived in the same About One Year and a Half quitted said House about 25 Years and hath not since rented House or paid any Taxes

Sworn February 1st 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell

John Ramsom
