Middlesex ss}  Mary George the wife of David George maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majesties Ship the Conquestadore in his Majesties Ship Service that he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Richards a Cooper in the Parish of Godalmin in the County of Surry & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 20. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary George
