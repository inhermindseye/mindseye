Middx.  Martha Launder aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath says She is the Widow of Robert Lander who died about Three Years ago with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. George Southwark in the County of Surry And by whom she hath One Child living now provided for says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship to his Father Robert Launder at Kingston upon Thames in the County of Surry and after he was of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Decr. 1779 before R Butler

her Martha Launder Mark
