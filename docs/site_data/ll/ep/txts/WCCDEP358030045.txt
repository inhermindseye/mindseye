Middx  Ann Hewitt aged about 21 Years removed from the Parish of St. Geo. Hanover Square by Order dated the 12th: Instant upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Davis at the White horse in Blackmoor Street in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of 40s. & 50s. for the Space of One Year & Six Months & hath left her said Service near Seven Years Says she hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be bor a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that Wm. Ayres late of Acton Husbandman did begett the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her Body the sd. Wm. Ayres being a fellow Servt . with this Examinant at Mr. Lotts in Acton

aforesaid there became acquainted with her and had Carnall knowledge of her Body in a Barn belonging to the sd. Mr. Lott in the Month of April last and at severall other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the said Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Wm. Ayres & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne Novr. 13th. 1745 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Ann Hewitt
