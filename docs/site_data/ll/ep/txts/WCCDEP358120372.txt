Middx  Sarah Amos aged abt. 29 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she lived a yearly Servt. wth. Mr. Aldin at the Yorkshire Gray in Bloomsbury market in the parish of St. Geo. Bloomsbury Middx for two years & upwards at the yearly wages of £3.10.0 for the first Year & four pounds for the remr. of the said Term quitted her sd. Service abt. Six Years ago and has not Since Rented Ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn June 25th 1755 before Barth. Hammond  Tho Lane

Sarah Amos
