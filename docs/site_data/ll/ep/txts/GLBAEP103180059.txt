Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Shipley the widow of James Shipley deced maketh oath That her Said husband divers years ago & When a Singleman was hired & lived as a hired servant with Mr Waggott a Victualler in Sweet Apple Court in the Parish of St Botolph without Bishopsgate London at Ceratin Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & half & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement There That he did not afterward live as a hired servant for a year elsewhere or rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain another Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death been married or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that She was married to her Said husband at the Fleet about Twenty five Years ago

Sworn this. 25. of August 1778 before  Jno Sherwood  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Elizabeth Shipley
