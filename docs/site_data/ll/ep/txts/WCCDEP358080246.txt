Middlesex & Westmr.}  Eleanor Leach aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she Intermarred with Thomas Leach her Husband (gone from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Strand upwards of Eighteen Months ago and by whom she hath one Child now living namely William aged upwards of Eleven Months Says her said Husband is an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England either by Renting house Paying Taxes or Otherwise howsoever Says her Father Rents and lives in a house in King Street in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the said County of Middlesex and as so done for Twenty Years and upwards & Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same and where this Examinant was born and this Examinant further says she never gained any Other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year before Marriage

Sworn this 29th. day of May 1778 before R Butler John Cox .

Elanor Leach
