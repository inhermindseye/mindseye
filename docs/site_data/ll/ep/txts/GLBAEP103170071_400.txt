Middlesex ss}  Robert Williams maketh oath That divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Nevill Meale of church yard Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Brick layer at certain Wages & continued in such service for the space of two years by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement.

Sworn this 7 day of April 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Robert Williams
