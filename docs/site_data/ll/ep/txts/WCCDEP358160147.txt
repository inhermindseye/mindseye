Middx The Examination of Sarah Phipps  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 10th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith yt. on ye. 2d. Day of Febry. 1737 she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Bennetts Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & wch has been Baptized Mary And further saith yt. on ye. 4th. Day of April last she was Delivered of another Female Bastard Child in Vear Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & wch has been Baptized Jane & yt. One Jno. Williams Taylor did begett the sd. Childn. on her Body, And this Examint. further upon her Oath saith yt. some time before Febry. 1737 she Lodged in Bennetts Court aforesd. & he the sd. Jno. Williams at the same time Lodging in the same Court there became acquainted with her & yt. from the time of their acquaintance they have Lodged together & Passed for Man & Wife. And yt. the sd. Jno. Williams & no other Person is the true & reall Father of the sd. two Childn. of wch she has been Delivered of as aforesd. that he now absconds from from her & she being Poor is not capable of maintaing them

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Sarah Phipps
