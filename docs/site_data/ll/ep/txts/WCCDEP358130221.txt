Middx.  Jonathan Robinson of Bear Lane in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry Carpenter upon Oath Says he has been informed & believes that his Brother James Robinson (who is now at Sea) was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Willm Harlock a Butcher in Gravel Lane in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry & Served One Year & a half the last part of his said Apprentiship with the said William Harlock in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. & never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn Octr. 9th. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

Jonathan Robinson
