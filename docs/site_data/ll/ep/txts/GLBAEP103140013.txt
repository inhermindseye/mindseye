Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Sidebottom of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County  Singlewoman taken on oath before me Charles Digby - Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the said County this 15th. Day of December 1773

Who on her oath Saith that about eight months ago She was delivd. of a Female Bastard Child in the General Lying in Hospital in Tyburn Road in the County of Middlesex to which Place She went for the sole Purpose of being delivered the which child hath been since baptized by the name of Ann Dunn & is become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate & that one Robert Dunn late of Charles Street Westmr. in the County of Middx a Common Soldier did get her with child of the said Bastard Child And this Deponent farthr Saith that about Seven years ago she was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year with Joseph Cappur of Rosemary Lane in the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate Tobacconist at the yearly wages of Five Pounds & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the said Parish & this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 15th day of December 1773 before  Cha: Digby

The mark of Sarah Sidebottom
