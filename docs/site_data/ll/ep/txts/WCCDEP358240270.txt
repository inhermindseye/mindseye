(proper Settlement)

St Clement Danes 8th. June 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary le Strand  (to wit) George Dove Widower aged twenty two years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his late Wife Charlotte formerly Charlotte Kennedy  Spinster at the parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn (by Banns) on or about the fifth day of April 1790 that he rented and resided in a House in Drury Lane in the parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for three Months and upwards at the Weekly Rent of eight Shillings (being at the Rate of £20:16s a year) that he quitted the same in or about the Month of November or December 1793 and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief, that his said Wife Charlotte died on or about the 11th day of January last that he hath two Children by has said Wife both now liv living Namely John Benjamin aged two years four Months & upwards and Charlotte aged Seven Months & upwards and that he is unable to maintain himself & family without assistance

Sworn the 8th day of June 1793 before Us W Addington Mitchell}

George Dove
