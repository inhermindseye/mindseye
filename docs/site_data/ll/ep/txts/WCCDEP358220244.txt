Westmr. to wit Ann Grimes Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thos. Grimes Gone from her with whom she Intermarried at Northampton upwards of Three Years ago and by whom she hath One Child named Elizth . Aged about Sixteen Months Says that her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Thos. Ogleby a Shoemaker in German Street in the Parish of St. James in ye. Liberty of Westminster upwards of Seventeen Years Ago since which time he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true.

Sworn this 4th. day of March 1771 before Us} R. Butler 

her Ann Grimes Mark

Witness. Petr Sidebotham
