Middx  Ann Bowers aged abt. 27 yrs. Lodging in Kings head Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes upon Oath says she is the Wife of Wm. Bowers with whom she Intermarried at the fleet London on the 20th. of august 1747. Old style says he was bound Apprentice by Indenture dated the 26th. of Octr. 1737. To Joseph Springwell Citizen & Turner of London & served twelve Months the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Saml. Dobbins in the Precinct of the Savoy in the County of Middx. says she has one Child by him Mary aged upwards of two years & is poor & not able to Maintain herself & Child says her said husbd. never Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt. for one year as she has been informed & verily believes

Sworn this 24th. of Augst. 1757 before} Barth: Hammond  ing

The Mark of Ann Bower
