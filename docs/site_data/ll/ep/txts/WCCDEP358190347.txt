Middx  Jeremiah Lamburn Aged about Sixty Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife Aged about 58 Years at May Fair Chapel about Sixteen Years Since Says he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr Isaac Wright a Shoemaker in Clare Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx and hath been out of his said Apprenticeship near Forty Years Says he never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn ye. 4th Day of May 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

Jeremiah Lamburn
