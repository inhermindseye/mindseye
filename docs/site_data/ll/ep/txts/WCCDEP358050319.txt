Middx  Martha Hasset upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Henry Hasset deceased that her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Muale a Taylor in Castle Street in the parish of St. Mary at Shrewsbury in the County of Salop and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant nor hath this Examinant since his Decease rented ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 21st. 1750 H Fielding

The mark of Martha Hasset
