Middx  Thomas Bridges upon Oath saith he was married to Mary Ann his wife upwds. of three years ago by whom he has one Child Frances aged upwards of three years and that he this Examinant rented a house of the yearly Rent of Five pounds in the Parish of Ampthill in the County of Bedford lived therein about three Years & paid his proportion to the Poor Rate of the said Parish that he quitted the sd. house last Midsummer and has not since rented ten pounds by the Year nor paid parish taxes

Sworn Janry 31st 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

Thos: Bridges
