Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Samuel Hussey aged about 52 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a house in Paradise Row Chelsea in the Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea in the County of Middlesex for about Seven years at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds that she hath quitted said House about Ten years ago since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Janry. 1790  Sampn Wright J Pownall

Saml Hussey
