Middx  Eliz: Howard aged abt. 20 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she was born in Sheffeild Street Clare markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex where her Father lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £35  for many Years and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says she this Exaiant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 20th 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Eliz Howard
