Westmr.  Esther Pringle aged abt. 25 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Walter Pringle (who is now in the Marshalsea Prison ) with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Saviours Southwark upwards of a Year ago by whom she hath one Child John aged about 4 Months, says her said husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he Rented a house in White hart Court  Castle Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Eleven pounds lived therein about one Year and quitted same in the Year 1750 and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as he has also informed this Examinant and which information she believes to be true says before her said intermarriage with the said Walter Pringle she was the Widow of Thomas Davenport who informed this Examinant he was born in the Parish of St. Botolph Bishopsgate in the City of London And never Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or a Yearly hired Servant for one Year, says she has one Child by the said Thomas Davenport (to wit) Thomas aged upwards of four Years

Sworn 24th. Octr. 1765 , before} Thos: Balack  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Esther Pringley
