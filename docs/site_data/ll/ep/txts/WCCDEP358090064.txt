Middx to wit John Green aged about fifty Nine Years on behalf of Eleanor Talks an Infant aged upwards of Eleven Years a Bastard born on the Body of Patience Talks  Widow since deceased at the House of one John Surry at the Sign of the Shepherd & Flock in High Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex and the Examinant further says he never was Married to the said Patience Talks but that he had she aforesaid Eleanor Talks by the aforesaid Patience and can give no further or other Account of the Settlement of the said Patience than as aforesaid and this Examinant further says that he is Poor and unable to Maintain the said Child and that She the said Eleanor Talks hath gained no other Settlement Otherwise than by Birth as this Examinant Verily believes

Sworn this 19th. day of Augt. 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

John Green
