Middx  Eliz Harding aged about 24 Years upon Oath saith she is the Daughter of George Harding by Bridget his wife both deceased that she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year nor ever rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes Says she has been informed and verily believes her sd. late father George Harding rented a house between Milford Lane end & Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lived therein for sometime & pd. Scot & lot to the sd. Parish Says her sd. late father never after he quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief.

Sworn May 4th. 1751 before  Tho Lane .

The mark of Eliz Harding
