Middlesex ss}  Samuel Holyday maketh oath That divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to & Served the whole of Such Apprenticeship with Edward Bower in the Parish of Saint John in Bridlington in the County of York  Marmer & by means whereof gained a Legal Settlemt there That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent or value of Ten pounds nor Serve any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th day of August 1775 before B Camper

Saml: Hollyday
