Middlesex ss}  Mary Pankhurst the Widow of John Pankhurst deced maketh oath that since her Said late Husbands death she this Deponent lived in & rented a house of the yearly rent of Thirteen Guineas in Bermondsey Street in the Parish of St. John in Southwarke in the County of Surry & Continued in the Occupation thereof for the Space of a year & has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That She was lawfully married to her Said husband by whome She hath two Children now living namely John Aged about five years and Jane aged about Seven years her lawful Issue by her Said late husband

Sworn this 13 day of July 1784 before R Davies J Hawley

The Mark of Mary Pankhurst
