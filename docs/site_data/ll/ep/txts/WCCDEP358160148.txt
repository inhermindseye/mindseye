Middx  The Examination of Priscilla Puttnam aged about 52 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 11th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that upwards of 30 Years ago she was paid out to One Mrs. Monier Clear Starcher in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . as a Day Apprentice for the space of four Years but yt. During all yt. time she Lodged & boarded with her Mother who then lived in Exeter Street in the Parish of St. Paul Cove Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . And this Examint. further saith yt. she hath not since yt. time Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Pris Puttnam
