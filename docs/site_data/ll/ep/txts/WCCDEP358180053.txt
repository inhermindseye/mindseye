Middx  Mary Bourne on the Behalfe of John Cramp upon her Oath saith that the said John Cramp about Twenty Years since was bound an Apprentice to and Served Four Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Long at the Two Brewers in Water Lane  Blackfryers in the Parish of St. Ann in Blackfryers in the City of London that since his said Apprentiship he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place as this Examinant hath been Informed & which she verily beleives

Sworne the 29th- Decr. 1742 before} Thos Deveil

The Mark of Mary Bourne
