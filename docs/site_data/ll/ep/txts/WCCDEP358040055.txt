Westmr.  Tho. Brush aged about 58 Years upon Oath saith he intermarried with his present Wife Sophia in the Parish Church of St. Saviour in the County of Surry on the 27th. Day of Augt. 1741 Says he hath one Child (to wit) Jane aged about 8 Yrs. which Child he had by a former Wife and is poor and not able to maintain her Says he lived in and rented a house in Angel Court in the Strand  in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £18. for fourteen Years or thereabouts and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes and hath quitted the said house about 15 Years Says he hath not since he quitted the said house in Angel Court rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif Says his hath present Wife Sophia hath one Child

by her former husband Thomas Wilson Catherine aged about 12 Years Says the said Thomas Wilson Served his Apprentiship with Mr Burgess a Watchmaker in Earls Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examinant has been Informed & beleives

Sworn Septr. 4th. 1747 before} Thos. Lane

Tho Brush
