Middlesex ss.}  Jane Leveritt  Singlewoman Maketh oath that divers Years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds with Mr John Showler of Cullum Street in the Parish of St. Gabriel Fenchurch Street in the City of London and Continued in Such Service there for the Space of three Years and by Means thereof as this Deponent is advised She gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that She hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year nor rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever paid any Poors Rate or Kings Taxes nor was She ever Married nor to her Knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since her Servitude abovemenconed

Sworn this 29th day of March 1771 before Wilmot B Camper

The Mark of Jane Leveritt
