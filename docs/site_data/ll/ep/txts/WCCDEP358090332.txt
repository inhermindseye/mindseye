Middx to Wit Cath: Nicholls Aged about 21 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant to one Mrs. Cook at the City of Durham in Blackmoor Street Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of four Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her sd. Service upwards of Six Months ago since which time she hath never gained any other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 25th. day of Augt. last Past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belongg. to the said Parish which hath since been baptized by the name of Ann & is now Chargeable to the said Parish And further says that one North Jolly an Apprentice to Mr. Sanger a Poulterer in Blackmoor Street afsd. is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in his the said Mr. Sangers house several times upwards of Ten Mon Months ago And further says that the sd. North Jolly no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 28th. day of Septr. 1782 before  Thos: Gilbert

her Cath: Nicholls Mark
