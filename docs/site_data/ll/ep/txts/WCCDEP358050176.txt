Middx  Elizth. Liscomb aged about 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she has lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Broome at the Running horse in  Queen Street in the parish of St. Giles's in the fields in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of one pound ten shillings for the Space of one year to the best of her remembrance and hath left her said Service about 4 years since which time she has not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for me Years or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 16 th 1749 before}
