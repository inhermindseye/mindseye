Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Rebecca Read aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Porter in Copthall Court Throgmorton Street in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew by the Royal Exchange in the City of London for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Four pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 9th. day of July 1795 N Bond W Mathew

her Rebecca Read Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
