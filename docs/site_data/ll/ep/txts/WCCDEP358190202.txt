Middx & Westmr}   Ann Mc. Conchie aged 20 Years Removed from the Parish of St. John wapping in the County of Middx by Order dated the 21st: day of November 1761 upon Oath saith she never was Married bound an Apprentice kept House or paid parliamentary or Parochial Taxes That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. William Hall in Fountain Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for the Space of Fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds and that she Quitted the Same in July last And further upon her voluntary Oath says she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born And that one Robert Love a Seafaring Man who lodges at

the said Mr. Halls in Fountain Court did beget such Child or Children he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body about the beginning of the Month of June One thousand seven hundred and sixty one at the House of the said Mr. Hall where she was then Servant and at several times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 21st. day of Novemr. 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Mc Conachie
