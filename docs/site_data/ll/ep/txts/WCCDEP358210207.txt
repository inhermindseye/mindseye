Westmr.  Ann Ward aged about 50 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Henry Ward with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London about Eighteen Years ago, says her said husband hath ofter times informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he served two Years and upwards the last part of his Apprentice Ship with Mr. John Winard a Taylor in Saint John's Lane in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year, And further says that her said Husband is now in the Workhouse of the said Parish of St. James Clerkenwell

Sworn Novr. 9th. 1767 , before} Tho Lane  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Ward
