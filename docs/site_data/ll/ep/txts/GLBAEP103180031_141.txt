Middlesex ss} The Examination of Ann Taylor of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justice of the Peace in & for the Said County this first day of April 1778 Who Saith That on the Nineteenth day of March one thousand Seven hundred and Seventy Seven at the house of her Brother Jeremiah Taylor Situate in Darby Street in the Said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate She the Said Ann Taylor was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Which has Since been baptized & Registred by the name of Sarah Rolf And that the said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that Robert Rolf of Hatton Garden in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the Sd. County Servant to one Mr Batson of the Same place did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before P. Rowlands

Ann Taylor
