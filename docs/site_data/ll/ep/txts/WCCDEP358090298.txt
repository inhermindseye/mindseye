Middx  Frances Gunn aged about 30 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant With Mr. Drakeford in the strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of Eight Yrs. Ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement by living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year or Otherwise howsoever And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards (And to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to Maintain the same when Born) And further says that One Wm. Walford a private Soldier in the first Regiment of Guards in Colonel Conways Compny. now Quartered in the Tower is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge other Body at his the sd. Wm. Walfords Lodgings in old Pye Street St. Margts. Westmr . some time in June last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. Difft. times 9 tone of Which times he get her with Child of the Child or Children of Which she is now pregnant And that the said Wm. Walsford and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 2d. Day of March 1782 before R Butler

Frances Gunn
