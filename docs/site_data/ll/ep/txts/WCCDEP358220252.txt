Middx to wit Ann Cody Aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Felix Creighton a Joyner and Undertaker in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Seven Years ago and hath not since she quitted her said Service ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 29th. day of March 1771 before me} R. Butler

Ann Coddy
