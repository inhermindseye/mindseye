Middlesex ss}  Anne Dennis the widow of William Dennis deced maketh oath That in the year one thousand Seven hundred & Sixty five she this Deponent was passed from the Parish of Saint Matthew Bethnal Green in the said County to the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County as being the Place of her last legal Settlement And this Deponent Saith that Since Such her removal She hath not done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this 10. day of Janry 1778 before P. Rowland

The a Mark of Anne Dennis
