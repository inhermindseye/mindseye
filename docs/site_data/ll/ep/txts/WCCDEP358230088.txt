Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Rebecca North aged about 21 Years upon her Oath saith That on or about the 27th. day of July 1796 she Intermarried with James Widdell North her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Matilda aged about Seventeen Months That her said Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Marshall a Stationer in Newcastle Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster That after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted this Examinant and Refuses to Allow her and her said Child any Support & that she is poor and unable to maintain herself and Child and that she and her said Child are now Chargeable to the said Parish

Sworn this 28th. day of Augt. 1799 before me} J Reeves

Rebecca North
