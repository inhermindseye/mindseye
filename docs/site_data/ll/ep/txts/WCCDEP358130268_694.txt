Middx.  Ann Taylor Aged abt. Fifty four Years Upon Oath Says she is the Wido- of Wm- Taylor Says her sd- late Husband was born at Warsall in the County of Stafford where he Served his apprentishipp with his father Says her sd- late husband never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd- Parish Taxes or was a hired Yearly Servt.

Sworn the 21st day of Janry 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Taylor
