Westmr. to wit Sarah Triscott Aged about fifty Seven Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Anthony Triscott who has been dead upwards of Twenty two Years ago And this Examinant further says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Bigg Shrimpton  a Tabacco Cooper in Saint Nicholas Lane Lumbard Street in the Parish of Saint Nicholas Acons London upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of five Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. day of January 1774 before}  Thos. Kynaston

Sarah Triscott
