Middlesex & Westminster}  Ann Davies Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to her Husband James Davis at the Parish Church of Saint Ann Soho about four Years ago (and who is now gone to Wales for the benefit of his Health says she has Two Children by her said Husband Namely William Henry Davis Aged about Two Years and Elizth. Ann Aged about five Months Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Princes Street  Leicester Fields in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of fifty Pounds & paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted house about Eighteen Months ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement whatever.

Sworn this 22d day of April 1783 before R Butler Addington

Ann Davies
