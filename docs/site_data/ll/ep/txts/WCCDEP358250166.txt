Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Alcock aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Cooper in Castle Street Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn for Three years and upwards at the yearly wages of Four Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about four years since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath Saith that she hath Two illegitimate Children living namely Mary Ann aged about Two years born at No. 49 Carey Street and Elizth. Fras . aged about Eight Months born at No. 22 Carey Street And this Deponent further saith That one Robert Smith a Hairdresser who is gone to Philadelphia is the father of the said Two Children

Sworn this 20th. day of May 1795  J Hlund

Elizabeth Alcock
