Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} (to wit) Mary Pomeroy aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year that she was born at Eashansty in the County of Somersett where her Father, rented a Farm at the yearly Rent of £100, that her Father hath quitted said Farm upwards of 10 years ago, since which time he has done no Act to gain Subsequent Settlement to the best of her knowledge that this Ext. lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Preston of Holywell Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for 12 Months all but Two Days where she received a Twelve Months Wages, And further this Ext. saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards, and to become Chargeable to said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and further s that one George Judd who lives at Mr. Prestons in Holywell Street as aforesaid is the father thereof having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of March last and at Sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said George Judd is the only real and true father thereof And that she now lives in the of Saint Clement Danes and Poor and unable to maintain herself and Child

Sworn this 5th. Day of Decr. 1786 before me} R Butler

Mary Pomeroy

Man taken Paid-£10..10s..0d Note 6 Mos.- £5..15s.. - £16..16s..-

lives at No. 11 Wych Street
