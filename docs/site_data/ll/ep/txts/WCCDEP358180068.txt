Middx  John Howell aged abt. 43 Yrs. upon his Oath saith that on the 3d- Day of Septembr- abt. 20 Yrs- since he intermarried with his prest. Wife Theodosia at Pyke Church in ye. County of Worcester that abt. 28 Yrs. since he was bound an Apprentice to & served 4 Yrs. the latter Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Jno- Glennisson in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx that since his said Apprentiship he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif that he hath 3 Childn. viz: Eliz: aged abt. 13 Yrs. Ann aged abt. 11 Yrs. & Jno. aged abt. 9 Yrs. & being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne 9th. March 1742 before} Tho Lane

John Howell
