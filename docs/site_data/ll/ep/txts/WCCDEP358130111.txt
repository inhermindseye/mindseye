Middx  Elizabeth Carr Aged about 58 Years Upon Oath says She was Intermarried with Henry Carr her late Husband deceased about Eighteen Years since in the Parish Church of St. Brides London Says her said Husband before that time Rented a House in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster at the Yearly Rent of £15. and lived therein several Years and that her said late Husband never afterwards Rented any other House as She has been informed by him & believes Says She hath not since the Death of her said Husband Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 24th. Day of Janry. 1756 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Elizth. Carr
