Middx  Easter Davis aged abt. 43 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about twelve years ago she was hired to live a yearly Servant with Mr Geary at the White Lyon in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the yearly Wages of Five pounds and lived with him in Vere Street afsd. under that hiring & agreement for one year and Six months or thereabouts & that she has not since she quitted the sd Mr. Gearys Service rented ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Novr. 8th 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Easter Davis
