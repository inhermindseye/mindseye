Middx  Edith Oliver Aged abt. Thirty Years Upon Oath says she Intermarried with her present husband Francis Oliver in the parish Church of St. Michl. Cornhill abt. fourteen Years ago Says her sd. Husband is now a prisoner in End gate for Debt Says he rented a House in Thames Street in the Parish of St. Michl. at Queen hith London of the Yearly rent of Sixteen pounds & lived therein upwards of One Year & hath aftr the sd. House upwards of one Year Says her sd. Husband hath not since he quitted the sd. House rented Ten pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes or Served any parochiall Office & that she hath one Child named Mary Phebe Elizabeth & being poor is not able to maintain herselfe & Child

Sworne the 14th. Day of a Decr. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Edith Oliver
