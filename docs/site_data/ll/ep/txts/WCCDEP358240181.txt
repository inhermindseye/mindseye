St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westmister}  St. Giles in the fields  (to wit) Thomas Oglivie  Widower aged Sixty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he intermarried with Sophia his late Wife at the parish Church of St Andrew Holborn in or about the year 1760 that since then Rented and Resided in an House in King Street  Drury Lane in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about five years at the yearly Rent of £20 for the Service two years and £18 a year during the Remainder of the Term that his said Wife Died about fifteen years ago that he quitted that House about fourteen years ago and hath never since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement that he hath a Daughter by his said wife born in lawful Wedlock who provides for herself and that he this Examinant is poor Aged and unable to maintain himself without Assistance

Sworn the 3d. day of December 1792 before us  Sampr Wright M Addington

Thos Oglivie
