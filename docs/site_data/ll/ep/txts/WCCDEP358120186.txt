Middx  Augt: 27th. 1754 Margt. Robinson upon Oath Saith she is the wido . of Rt. Robinson who dyed upwds. of 4 Yrs. ago & that her sd. late husband rented a house of the yearly Rent of £16 in St John Street in the Parish of St Margaret in the City of Westmr. and lived therein abt. 9 Years that her sd. late husband after he quitted the abovementioned house never Rented ten Pounds by the year or paid Parish taxes nor has this Examinant Since his Decease

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before} Barth: Hammond Magt. Robinson
