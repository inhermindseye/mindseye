Westmr. 

Edwd. Durant upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Bridget at the Romish Chapell near Lincolns Inn feilds about 11 Yrs. ago Says he lived in & rented a house in Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds for Seven Years or thereabouts and hath left the same about Seven Years Says he has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 9th. 1747 before  Wm: Jones

Edward Durant
