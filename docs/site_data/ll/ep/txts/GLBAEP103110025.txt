Middlesex ss}  Jane Palmer the Widow of George Palmer deced maketh oath That divers years ago her Said late husband rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Thirty four Pounds in Abington Buildings in the Parish of John the Evangelist in Westminster in the County of Middlesex & resided there for many Years & by Means thereof as She is advised he gained a legal Settlement there That to her Knowledge or belief her Said husband never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Depont. Since his Death hath not rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to her Knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since her Said husbands Death and that She was lawfully Married to her Said Late husband in the Parish Church of Barnstead in Surry

Sworn this Third day of October-1771 before  Cha: Digby B Camper

The Mark of Jane Palmer
