Middx to wit Kezie Howard aged about Thirty Seven Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Symonds at the Queen of Bohemias Head in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of a Westmr . for Sixteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said service about Thirteen Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28 day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

her Kezia Howard Mark
