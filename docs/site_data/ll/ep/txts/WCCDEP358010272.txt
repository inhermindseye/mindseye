Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Marchant aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Almack in Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Four Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of March 1789  Wm Kitchiner

Eliz Marchant
