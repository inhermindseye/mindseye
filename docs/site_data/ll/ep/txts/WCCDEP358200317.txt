Westmr.  Elizabeth Drury aged about 49 Years upon Oath says she was never married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Turner a Carpenter in Cateaton Street next the Church in the Parish of St. Lawrence Jewry in the City of London for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, hath quitted said Service about Eight Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 9th. Novr. 1765 . before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

Elizabeat Drury
