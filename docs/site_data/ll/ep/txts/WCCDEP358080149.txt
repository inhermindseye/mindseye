Middx & Westmr.}  Thos. Pounsett aged about 22 Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented house or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Robt. Chester  Esqr . in Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Psh of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Sixteen Pounds besides Bed and Board hath quitted his said Service upwards of twelve Months ago since which time he hath never gained any other subsequent Settlement either by renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn this 29th. Day of June 1777 M Addington Thomas Pounsett
