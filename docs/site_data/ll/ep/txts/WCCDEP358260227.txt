Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Bury aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Dearn a Green Grocer in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Twelve years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement a Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th.. day of Novr. 1797 before me } W Carter

her Sarah Bury mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
