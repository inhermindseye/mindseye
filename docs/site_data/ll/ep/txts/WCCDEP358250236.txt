Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hellena Lawrikus aged about 58 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the year 1752 she Intermarried with John Christopher Lawrikus her late Husband who has been dead about 29 years at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Houghton Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Five years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Thirty pounds that after he quitted said house he never Rented any house Tenements or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent nor hath this Examinant since her said late husbands death Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of May 1796 } Saml. Edward

Hellena Laurikus
