Middx  Mary Gill aged 38 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she is the Wido: of Henry Gill & can give as Accot. of his Settlemt. other than that he was born in the County of Wheatley in the Kingdom of Ireland Says she was born in the Parish of St. George in the County of Middlesex and never was an Apprentice Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 22d. Day of March 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Gill
