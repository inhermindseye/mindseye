Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Hyde a Singlewoman maketh oath that about Twenty Years ago She was hired and lived as a hired servant by the Year at Certain Wages by & with Mr Thomas Cockerill a Victualler who kept the Sign of the Two Chairman in Warwick Street near Chairing Cross in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in Westminster in the Sd County and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two years & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 31. day of October 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Elizabeth Hyde
