Westmr.  Ann Low upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Low by whom she hath 3 Children Johan , Mary & Sarah that she hath been Informed & verily believes her Sd. late husband was born in the Parish of St John at Timberhill in the City of Norwich & never gained a Subsequent Settlemt. to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 20th 1752 before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Low
