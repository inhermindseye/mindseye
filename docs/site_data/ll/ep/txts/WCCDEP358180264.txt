Middx  Eliza. Pym aged about 41 Years upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of Thomas Pym Says the sd. Thos. Pym was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Mr. Weller in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Clock maker & hath been out of his time upwds. of 20 Yrs. that the sd. Thomas Pym hath been dead abt. Three Months Says neither her sd. late Husband in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif and that she was Removed from the Parish of Christ church London to sd. parish of St Clemt. Danes by Order dated 12th. Decr. Instant

Sworne 13th. Decr. 1744 before} Tho Lane

Elz Pym
