Middx  Ann Mogridge aged abt. Thirty two years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she never rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes or hired a yearly Servant for one year that she is the Daughter of Timothy Mogridge by Mary his Wife both decd. to whom a Certificate was Granted by the Churchwards. & Ovrs. of St Clemt. Danes Middx acknowledging them Inhabitant legally Settled in the sd Parish

Sworn May 8th 1754 before}
