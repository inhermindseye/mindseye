Middx  Cath: Boor  Wido . aged about 65 Years upon her Oath saith that about 4 Years since being at that time a Wido: she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Milward in Craven Buildings in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx for the Space of one Year & Nine months at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds that since she left her said Service she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne Augt: 18th: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Cath Boor
