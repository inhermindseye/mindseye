Middx  John Sharp upon Oath saith he was married to his Wife  Hannah Sharp abt. 20 years since in the Parish Church of St. Andw. Holborn and has by her a Son named Henry aged 17 Years and that about Six Years ago he Rented a house of the yearly Rent of £14 in the Butcher Row in the parish of St Clement Danes & lived therein about Six Months and has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year & that his sd. Son Henry never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn 29th Novr. 1754 before
