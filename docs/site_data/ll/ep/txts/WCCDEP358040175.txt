Westmr.  Mary Cise Widow of Michl. Cise upon Oath saith her sd. late husband has been dead about 10 Years Says upwds. of one Year ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Wm. Radford in Craven Buildings in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £4..10..0 for two Yrs. and upwards (having all that time no Child or Children & accept one Daughter who was married) Says she has not since she left her sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 22d. 1748 before} Tho: Burdus

Mary Cise
