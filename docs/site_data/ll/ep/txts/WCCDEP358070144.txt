Westminster ss  Sarah Wood Aged about Eighteen Years on behalf of her Father John Wood who now lies ill in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on her Oath Says that her said Father Rented and lived in a House in Sharratt Street Golden Square in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of four Years at the Yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House as she believes about Nine Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of Novr. 1773 before} John Goodchild

her Sarah Wood  Mark
