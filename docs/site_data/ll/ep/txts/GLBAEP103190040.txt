Middlesex ss}  Mary Potter the wife of William Potter maketh oath That her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Dunkirk That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to and Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Burrows a Shoemaker in the Town of Reading in the County of Berks & to her knowledge or belief he has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement That she was lawfully Married to her sd husband by whome she hath a Child now living named Mary Aged about Ten years

Sworn this. 17. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Potter
