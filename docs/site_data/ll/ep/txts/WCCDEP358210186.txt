Middx.  Catherine Chambers aged about Twenty nine Years upon Oath says She is the Wife of Charles Chambers with whom She intermarried in the Parish Church of Isslington in the County of Middlesex about 9 Years ago, by whom She hath two Children namely Jane aged about seven Years and Elizabeth aged about six Years, Says that her said Husband served his Apprenticeship of seven Years with one Mr. Honeyman a Bookbinder in Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster as She hath been informed by him and verily believes and that her said Husband hath never since Rented Ten pounds a year, paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 26th. day of Septr. 1767 before W Kelynge Paul Vaillant

Cath Chambers
