St Clement Danes 15th febry. 1792

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) John Wood Aged Seventeen years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that his Father Richard Wood Rented & Rended in a House in White House yard in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty for 3 years at £22 St Annum Rent that he quitted that house about Six years aged Died about years after & never obtained any subsequent Subsequent legal Settlement that this Deponents Mother Elizabeth Wood Services his father & Died abt twelve Months ago but never obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right neither hath this Deponent in his right-that he is lame and unable to provide for himself without Assistance & is a legitimate Child of the said Richd. and Elizabeth Wood

Sworn the 15th. day of February 1792 before me R Butler

John Wood 's Mark

Witness Benj; Newman
