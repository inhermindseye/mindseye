Middx  Dorothy Rothwell on Behalf of Jas. Hamey aged upwds. of 7 Years & Thos. Hamey aged upwds. of 5 Yrs. upon Oath Saith that the sd. Jas. & Thos. Are the Children of Wm. Hamey by Mary his Wife both deceased as she verily believes And that she has been informed & believes the sd. Wm Hamey rented a Farm in the Parish of Kisley in Bedfordshire of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds & upwards & lived therein Several Years

Sworn May 15th 1753 before} Tho Lane

Dor rothwell
