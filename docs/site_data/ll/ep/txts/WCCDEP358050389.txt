Middx  Martha Jarvis  Wido . of Wm. Jarvis who dyed abt. 6 Years since upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her sd. late husband on the 23d. of April 1742 at his own house in Naked boy Court and that the sd. Wm. Jarvis rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £14 in Naked boy Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx & lived therein upwds. of 20 Years & there dyed & this Examinant has not since his Decease rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Swron 7th. Decr. 1750 before} Tho Lane

Martha Jarvis
