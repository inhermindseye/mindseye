Westmr 

Eliz; Howard  Wido . of Robt. Howard upon Oath saith her sd. late husband dyed on the 13th. Day of July last that she Intermarried with him at the fleet London about 27 Yrs. ago and that he was an Irishman & never gained a Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or beleif Says she lived a Yearly Servt. (before her sd. Intermarry ago & whilst a Singlewoman ) with Mr. Smith at the Sun & Appletree in Whitehart Yard in the parish of St. Mary be Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £5 for five Years or thereabts.

& quitted her sd. Service abt. 11 Months before her Intermarriage with the sd. Robt Howard Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mrs. Smith rented Ten pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 16th 1750 before The Lane.

The mark of Eliz Howard
