St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to Wit) Denis Peakin Aged about thirty Nine years (on behalf of his Mother Margaret Peakin now Ill and Infirm) Maketh Oath and saith that he hath been Informed by his said Mother and verily believes that she his said Mother Margt. Peakin was Married to James Peakin his late Father deced deceased at a Roman Catholic Church or Chapel, in the Town of Kilkenny in the Kingdom of Ireland , about Forty years since, according to the Rights & Ceremony of the Church of Rome that his said Mother about twenty Seven years ago lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. James Orr a West India Merchant in Great Winchester Street (he believes at the House No.10 there,) in the parish of Alhallows on London Wall in the City of London upwards of one year and that she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatever (whereby to the best of this Deponents Knowledge or belief) She might have acquired or be intitled unto any subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent sat saith that he hath never heard from his said Mother or otherwise what Wages she contracted for or Received for such Service with Mr. Orr as aforesaid & that his said Father James Mother Margaret Peakin

has been Ill a long time of that the Complaint where with she is Afflicted has entirely so Much disordered her Mental faculties as to under her incapable of Answering (with the least certainty) any Question proposed to her, and that she is Poor and unable to support herself

Sworn the 12th May 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Denis Peakin
