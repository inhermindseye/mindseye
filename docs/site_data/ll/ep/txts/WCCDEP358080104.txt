Middlesex & Westmr.}  Ann Maccabe Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of John Maccabe (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn in June 1775 and by whom she hath one Child now living named John Aged aged about Seven months Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant at the Smerna Coffee house Pall Mall in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for Six Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds or thereabouts and after quitting his said Service he never afterwards gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 10th. day of January 1777 before  John Cox . J Hubbard

Ann Maccabe
