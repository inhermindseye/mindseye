Middx  Eliz: Bouch  Removed from the parish of St Paul Covt. Garden by Order dated the 8th. Day of Augt. Instt. upon her Oath saith she is the Wido. of Joseph Bouch  deceased and that the sd. Joseph Bouch lived in & Rented a house in Fountain Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of £24 for the Space of 3 Yrs. or thereabouts & there dyed Says she this Examint. Continued to live in & rent the sd. house for the Space of Six Months or thereabouts after the Death of her sd. late Husband & hath not since she left the same rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt . for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 9th. Augt. 1745 before} Wm. Jones Elizabeth Bouch
