Westmr. to wit Judith Bury Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with Benson Bury at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes a Nine Years ago and by om she hath Two Children now living Vizt Mary Aged about Six Years and Benson Aged about Nine Months Says that her said late Husband Served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Demnott a Bookbinder  the Corner of Swan Yard in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the said Liberty and after her said late Husband was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 17th. day of Septr. 1773 before} R. Butler

Judith Bury
