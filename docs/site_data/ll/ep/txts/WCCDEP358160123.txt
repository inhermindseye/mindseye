Middx  The Examination of Cath: Jevon  Wido . Lodging in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 14th. Day of March 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that her late Husband Danl. Jevon Deced abt. 9 Years ago lived in & Rented a House in Cross Lane in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the East  London of the Yearly Rent of £50 & lived in the same for the space of Eleven Years. And this Examinant further saith yt. her sd. Husband in his life time nor she since his Death never after Rented any other House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Samuel Clarke

Catherine Jevon
