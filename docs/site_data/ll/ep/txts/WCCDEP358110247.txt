Middx  Paul Dowdell upon Oath saith that upwds. of 20 Yrs. ago he lived a yearly Servant with Mr Wm. Todd at the George & Punch bowl at Tottenham Court in the Parish of Pancras in the County of Middlesex and for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £4 and was all the time of his sd Service a Singleman And that he has not since he left Mr Todd Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn May 12th. 1753 before} Tho Lane

Paul Dowdell
