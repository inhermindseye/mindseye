Middlesex ss}  John Sullivan maketh oath That his Father hath informed him and Which he believes to be true that he was duly bound Apprentice to and Served his Apprenticeship with one a Farrier in the Parish of Saint Mary in the Town of Dover in the County of Kent where this Deponents Father died and Where this Deponent was born That to this Depont. knowledge or belief his Said Father never did any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That he this Depont. to his knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right And that he hath a wife named Elizabeth & two Children namely John Aged about two years & half and George Aged about eight months the lawful issue of this Deponent by his Said wife

Sworn this 27th. Day of November 1772 before  Jno. Sherwood

The Mark of John Sullivan
