Middx  Eliz Newton upon Oath saith she in the Wido . of John Newton who dyed the 16th. of March last by whom she hath one Child Mary aged near 7 Years Says her sd. late husband Rented a house in Whites Alley was little Moorefields in the Parish of St. Giles witht. Cripplegate London of the Yearly Rent of Fifteen pounds and lived therein abt. four Years and quitted the same abt. Seven Years ago Since which she has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr 26th. 1757 before Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Newton
