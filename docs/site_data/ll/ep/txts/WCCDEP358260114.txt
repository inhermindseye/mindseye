Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Fanny Wells aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wilson in Cannon Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Abchurch in the City of London for Thirteen Months or thereabouts That she hath one Child living vizt. Harriet aged about Two years and an half born at the house of a Mrs. Wells in Villers Street Holloway Mount in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this Twelfth day of march 1796 }  M Addington

Fanney Wells
