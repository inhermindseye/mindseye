St. Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit  Ann Barden aged 28 yrs. or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes that she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year That some time since she this Examinant was removed by an Order of Removal from the Parish of Saint Clement Danes to the Parish of Saint Ann Blackfryars that being the place of her legal Settlement by Virtue of her Father and that she this Examinant is poor and unable to maintain herself.

Sworn this 25th. Augt 1788 before  Philip Dyot D. Walker

her Ann Barden Mark
