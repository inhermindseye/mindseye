Middx  Elizth. Jones  Singlewoman Aged about Fifty Years upon Oath Says she was never an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says she Cannot Give any Account of the Settlement of her Father Says she was Born a Little above Great Turnstile in Holborn in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx

Sworn the 7th of Septr. 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth. Jones
