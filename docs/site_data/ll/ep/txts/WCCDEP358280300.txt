Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Davis aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith That she Rented and lived in a house in Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Twice Quarters of a year at the yearly Rent of Twenty Eight Pounds that she hath quitted said house about Three Months since which time she has not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Gwyn a Hair Dresser who lives in Shire Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examts. Body and begotten the same thereon and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of Septr. 1790 } Wm Kitchiner

Mary Davies

£10. paid 17th Novr. 1790 by Swift Gwynn's partner.

Saint Clement Danes

Mary Davis No- 25 Ship Yard

Middlesex (to wit)
