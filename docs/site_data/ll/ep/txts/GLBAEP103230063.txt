Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Bean maketh oath That about twelve Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & With one Mr Fitzer a Salesman in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of three Years That She has not Since lived as hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done Any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 3. day of August 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Bean
