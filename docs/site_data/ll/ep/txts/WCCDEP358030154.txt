Middx  Cath. Deane on Behalf of Wm. Shimmall an Infant aged about Six Years upon Oath saith the sd. Wm. Shimmell was born at one Mrs: Simpson's in Fossetts Court in Wine Eager Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. Says she can't give any Acct. of the Settlement of the sd. Infants Father

Sworn May 23d. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Cath. Deane
