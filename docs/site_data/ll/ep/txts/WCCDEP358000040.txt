Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} passd. to St. Mary Islington to wit Margarett Ashburn aged 15 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Lodging paid Taxes or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant Saith that she was bound Apprentice to one Alexr. Beggs of Newington Green in the Parish of Saint Mary Islington in the County of Middx  Baker and Chandler by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes untill she should Attain the Age of 21 years or day of Marriage according to Act of Parliamt Says that she lived under such Indre for the space of six months or thereabouts and left her said Masters Service on Sunday last Says she never gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of her Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 7th day of May 1785 Alddington Thos: Gordon

her Margarett Ashburn mark
