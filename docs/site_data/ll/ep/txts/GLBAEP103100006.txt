Middlesex ss}  Edward Jencks maketh oath That divers years ago he Rented a house in Lower East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds and Continued the occupation thereof for about the space of Six Months & by Means thereof as he is advised gained a legal settlemt. there and this Depont. hath not since rented a house of the years Value of Ten Pounds nor served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or lived as a hired servant for a year nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement and that he hath a wife named Sarah to whom he hath been lawfully married.

Sworn this 11th. day of June 1768 before  Chris Scott

Edward Jencks
