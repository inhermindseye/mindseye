Middx  The Examination of Elizth Somers  Widw of Thomas Somers Deceased Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 3d. Day of February 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband Thos. Somers Deceased was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Frost in  Castle Alley in the Parish of St. Christophers in the City of London  Victualler and Served him as an Apprentice for the Space of Six Months and upwards And this Examinant further saith that her sd. Husband never was assigned over to any other Person neither did he ever rent ten Pounds a Year pay Parish Taxes or otherwise gain a Settlement in any other Place as this Examinants said late Husband hath informed her & which She Beleives That he hath been Dead abt. 5 Months & haveing two Children (to wit) Wm . aged 6 Years & upwards & Elizth . aged 3 Years & upwards & She being Poor is not able to maintain herselfe & them

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. Somers
