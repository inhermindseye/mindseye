Middx.  Cathne. Turbefield aged abt. 39 Yrs. ( Removed from the Parish of St. Brides London to the Parish of St. Clement Danes Middx) Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Fredk. Turbefield who has been dead upwards of two Years says her said husband informed her & which Information she verily believes to be true that he served three Years and upwds. the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Stratton a Brazier in Blackmoor Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and never Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn Octr. 30th. 1760 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Caths. Turbefield
