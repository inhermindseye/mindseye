Middlesex ss}  Susannah Hartley the widow of James Hartley deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he rented a house known by the sign of the George Ale house at the yearly Rent of Fifteen pounds a year in Lower East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere Nor hath she this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself And that she was lawfully married to her said late husband

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The mark of Susannah Hartley
