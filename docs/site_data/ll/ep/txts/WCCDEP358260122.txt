Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Samuel Wood aged about 44 years upon his Oath saith that on or about 22d. day of October 1786 he Intermarried with Harriet his present Wife at the Parish Church of Chiswick by whom he hath Six Children living Vizt. Susannah aged about Eight years, Ann aged about Seven years, Harriet aged about Five years, Samuel aged about four years, Catherine aged about Eighteen Months and William aged about Five Months That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a ho in Rymers Buildings  Greenwich  fa in the Parish of Greenwhich in the County of Kent for Three year and upwards at the yearly Rent of Five Guineas and paid the Poor rates due for the same that he hath quitted said House about Six years se which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this Fifth day of April 1796 } N Bond Tho Vaughan

Saml. Wood
