Middx.  Ann Gamboll aged abt. 56 years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Chas. Gamboll who hath been dead abt. four seven years says her said husbd. served his Apprenticeship of seven years to his father  Chas. Gamboll a Shoemaker in the Parish of St. Peter in the Town of Nottingham as she hath been informed & verily believes

Sworn the 2d. July 1757 before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Gamboll
