Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Watkins aged 56 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to John Watkins her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Strand on the second Day of February now last past that the Children by her said Husband that her said Husband hath informed her and she believes it to be true that he is a Parishioner legally in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that he gained such his Settlement in the said Parish by renting & living in a House in Bennetts Court in the said Parish and paying Parochial Rates for the same And this Examinant further saith that her said Husband hath deserted her and that she is poor and Destitute

Sworn this 13th. July 1789 before R Butler

Sarah Watkins
