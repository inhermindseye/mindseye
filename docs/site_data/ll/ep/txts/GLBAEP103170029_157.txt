Middlesex ss}  Thomas Myco maketh oath That he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Henry Price in the Hamlet of Hammersmith in the Parish of Fulham in the Said County Pipemaker & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath wife named Ann & a Child named Mary aged about Ten Years who hath done no Act gain a Settlement for herself the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Sd Wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 6th day of December 1776 before  Saml Wood  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Thomas Myco
