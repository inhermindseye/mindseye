Westmr. (to wit)  John Whitby aged 37 Years upon his Oath saith that he was born in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . & that his Father Ralph Whitby deceased lived in & Rented a House in Pump Court in White -hart Yard in the said Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas for the Space of one Year & upwds. & hath left the same upwds. of 37 Yrs. that he never was on Apprentice Yearly hired Servt. rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his his knowledge or beleif that he hath a Wife named Philotha & one Child viz: Benjn : aged 2 Yrs. & upwds. & being Poor is not able to maintain himself & Family

Sworne 10th. Sepr. 1742 before me  Wm. Hayton Houlson

John Whitby
