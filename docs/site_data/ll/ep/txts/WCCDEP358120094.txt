Henry Woodcock upon Oath saith he was bound apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Atkinson Glassman & after having Served his sd. Master abt. 5 Years of the sd. term during which he Lodged at Mr. Atkinson's house in the parish of St. Andrew in Holbourn above Barrs Middx he was discharged from his sd. Apprentiship and has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn April 27th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Henry Woodcock
