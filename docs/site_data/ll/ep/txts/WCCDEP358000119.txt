Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Ann Lockwood aged about 28 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented, House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Thos. Plimpton of the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 13 Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas hath quitted said Service upwds. of 18 Months since which time she hath gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid And further or her Oath saith that she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child the 17th. day of April last in Saint Clement Danes Workhouse since Christened by the name of Timothy and further this Examinant saith that our Robert Mingay a Currier who lately worked with Robt. Wyatt of Coleman Street Moorfields is the farther thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent Body sometime in the Month of and be gotten the same and that the said Robert Mingay is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 6th- day of Octr. 1785 : R Butler

Ann Lockwood .
