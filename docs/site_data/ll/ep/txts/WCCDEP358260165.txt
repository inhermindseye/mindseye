Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Lyman aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Thomas Perkins a Waterman in Round Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months at the yearly wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th 30th day of December 1796 M Addington J Robinson

her Ann Lyman Mark Witness B Newman
