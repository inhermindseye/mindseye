Middx  Eliz: Davis  Wife of Bartholomew Davis on her Oath saith she Intermarried with the said Bartholemew Davis on the 15th. Day of Augt: 1743 at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Philip Caton in Wood Street in the Parish of St. Alban Wood Street London Brass Chaser who dyed before this Examinants sd. Husband was not of his time Says he lived Six Months or thereabt: the last Part of his Apprenticeship (after the Death of Mr: Caton with the Consent of Mrs. Caton Executrix of the sd. Mr. Caton) with one Mr. William Painter in Gilbert Court in the Parish of St. Olave Silver Street London Brass Chaser as this Exainant hath been Informed & beleives Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn 27th June 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz: Davis
