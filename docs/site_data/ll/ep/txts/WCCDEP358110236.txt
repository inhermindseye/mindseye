Westmr.  Ann Stackhouse Wife of Christr Stackhouse upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the sd. Christr. Stackhouse at Mayfair Chapel on the 11th. Octr. 1740 that she hath been informed her sd husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant nor ever rented a House of Ten pounds a year or pd parish taxes and that his Mother after in her Widowhood after the Decease of her sd. husbands father rented a house of £13 by the year in Peter Street Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and lived therein Sevl. Years and quitted sd. House abt. two Years Says her sd. Husbd is gon from her & having two Childn. Mary Ann aged upwds. of two years & Jane aged abt. four Months & is not able to maintain them

Sworn April 13th. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Ann Stackhouse
