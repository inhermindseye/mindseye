Middx.  Wm. Anderson aged abt. 57 Yrs. upon Oath says he Rented a house in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx . of the Yearly Rent of Nineteen Pounds lived therein abt. three Years & hath Quitted same abt. four Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 20th. day of July 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Wm. Anderson
