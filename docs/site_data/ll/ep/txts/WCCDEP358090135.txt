Middx to wit Jane Jones aged about Twenty Nine Years on behalf of Johannah Marks otherwise Peeling an Infant aged about Eleven Years by Joseph Peeling her Father deceased her Oath says She never was Married to the aforesaid Joseph Peeling but that the said Johannah Peeling  born a Bastard on her Body in the House of the Joseph Peeling her Father in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster And further says that her said Daughter never Gained any other Settlement tha by Birth.

Sworn this 25th. day of April 1780 before M Addington George Mercer

her Jane Jones Mark
