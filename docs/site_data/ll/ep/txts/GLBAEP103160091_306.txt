Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Shaw the widow of Charles Shaw deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born in Greenwood Court in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & that to this Deponents knowledge information or belief he never did any Act to gain a settlement for himself nor hath She this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself & that she was lawfully married to her Said husband & this Deponent Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husbands Parents or either of them

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Shaw
