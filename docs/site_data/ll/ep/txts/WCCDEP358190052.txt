Middx.  Margt. Coney aged upwards of 60 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Geo: Coney who has been dead abt. 6 Months and that she was Married to him at the Temple Church about Twenty Years ago Says her said husband  Rented a house for 3 Years in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . within the County of Middx of the yrly. Rent of £20 and quitted same abt. 14 Years before his Death says her said husband in his life time after he Quitted said house nor she since his death never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid parish Taxes.

Sworn Sepr. 30th. 1760 before} Barth: Hammond

M Cony
