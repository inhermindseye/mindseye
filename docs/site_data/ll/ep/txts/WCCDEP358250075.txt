Saint Clement Danes Novr 1st. 1792

Middlesex & Westminr }  Wrong no settlemt and removed St Andrews Middx  Catherine Roberts aged 45 and upwards upon her Oath saith, That she was married at St Lukes about 26 years ago to John Roberts , That during her husbands life she rented and resided in a house in Tothill St. in the parish of Saint Andrew Holborn about the bars , at the yearly rent of 12-12-0 about 12 years Ago, that during her husband life, nor since his decease, she has not lived as an hired yearly servant paid any parochial Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 1st day of Novr. 1792 before us} N Bond Herb Whitfeld

her Cathn Roberts Mark

Jas Talboys
