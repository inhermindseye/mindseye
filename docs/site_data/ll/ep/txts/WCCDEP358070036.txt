Westmr. to wit Benj. Errington aged about Thirty Four years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Parish of Clements in the Town of Ipswich in the County of Suffolk in the year 1768 as Appears by Certificate Says he was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Rand a Peruke Maker in Saint Clements Church Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about Eighteen Years ago and Served Three Years of his Apprenticeship when his said Master and this Examinant parted by Consent since which time he has always Used the sea Service and never after parting with his said Master Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 2d. day of November 1772 before  Tho Lane

Benj: Errington
