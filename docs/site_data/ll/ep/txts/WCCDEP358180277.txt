Middx  Cath: Cunningham Aged about 26 Yrs. removed from the parish of St. Martin in the Feilds by Order Dated the 18th. Janry Instant upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she was Born in St. Clemt. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx where her Father  Jas. Cunningham  Deced lived in & Rented a house for the Space of 12 Yrs. & upwds. at the Yearly Rent of £12 & upwds. & after he left the same never rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 19th. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

The mark of Cath: Cunningham
