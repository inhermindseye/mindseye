Middlesex ss}  Susanna Knight maketh oath that She was lately hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Margaret Scott in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County widow at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space one Years & upwards under Such hiring & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year elsewhere or been Since Married or done any Act to gain anothr Settlement

Sworn this 4. day of August 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Susanna Knight
