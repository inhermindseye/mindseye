Middx to wit Mary James aged about 24 Years or her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Steel at the Hicks's Coffee house in King Street in the Parish of Saint Ann Soho in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds Seven Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of Eight Months ago since which time she hath never gained any other Subsequent either by Renting House paying Taxes or Otherwise Howsoever

Sworn this 28th. day of Octr. 1778 before  Jno. Barnfather  John Cox .

Mary James
