Middx ss}  Ann Alsop the widow of John Alsop deced maketh oath that her sd late husband has informd her & which She believes to be true That he was duly bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with Peter Skibo a Cooper in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement to her knowledge or belief That She hath a Child named Charlotte Aged about twenty months the lawful Issue of this Depont by her sd husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this 22d day of October 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Alsop
