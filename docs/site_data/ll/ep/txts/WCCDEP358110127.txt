Westmr.  Joyce Lee aged near 25 years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servt & that her father Jno. Lee rented a house of £16 by the Year in Sion Row in the parish of Twickenham in the County of Middx  lived therein Sevl Years & never after he quitted the sd house rented ten pounds a year a paid parish taxes

Sworn Novr. 24th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Joyce Lee
