Middx  Ambrose Dowse upon his Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife  Mary on or abt. the 22d. Day of Octr. 1734 at the Parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West London says he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Langley near Iron Gate in the Parish of St Catherine by the Tower in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds for the Space of 7 Years or thereof & left his sd. Service about one Month after his sd. Marriage Say she hath not since he left the sd. Mr. Langleys Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne March 26th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

Ambrose Dowse
