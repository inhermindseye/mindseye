Westmr. to wit Elizth. Thompson Aged about Sixty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Thompson with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex about Thirty Years ago and who has been dead about Nineteen Years ago and by whom she hath no Children except One who is now Married & provided for Says her said Husband before h Intermarriage with this Examinant Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Smart a Perukemaker in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and after her said late Husband was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant Since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 30th. day of March 1773 before R. Butler

Elizabeth Thompson
