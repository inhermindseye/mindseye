Middx  Margt. Davidson Aged about twenty five Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Speediman in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of £8 hath quitted her said Service upwards of One Month since which time she hath never gained any other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes (she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) Says that one Charles Douglas a Carpenter in Wych Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of October but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Charles Douglas and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn the 27th. Day of May 1777 before me}

Margreat Davidson
