Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Martins to wit Mary Hawley aged about Sixty Seven years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs Shewart in Great Newport Street in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted said Service upwards of Twenty years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 14th- day of February 1785  Sampn Wright J Reading

Meary Hewly
