Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Elizabeth Dollman aged Twenty six Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Copley a Taylor in Bolt Court Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Dunston in the West in the City of London at the Yearly Wages of £6. for the first year & 7 Guas afterwards for the peace of one year and seven Months or thereabouts that she quitted her said Masters Service about three Months ago and has not since then done any act whereby she believes she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. Decr. 1785 before  Sampr Wright J Addington

her Elizabeth Dollman Mark
