Middx  Alice Cooper  Widow of John Cooper who dyed in the Year 1723 upon Oath saith She lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Susannah Rose in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds from April 1727 to June 1728 and was all that time a Widow without Child or Children And hath not since She quitted her said Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 10th Day of Octor. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

Alice Cooper
