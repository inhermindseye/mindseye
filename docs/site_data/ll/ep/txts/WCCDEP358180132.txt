Sarah Binian  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that on the 24th. Day of September last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of Mrs. Richmond in Witch Strt. in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes which hath since been Baptized Jno. & is become Chargeable to the sd. Parish & that one John Daniel did begett the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body he the sd. Jno. Daniel having had Carnall knowledge of her Body the latter End of December or beginning of Janry. last & at Sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child which she was Delivered of as aforesd. & that he the sd John Daniel & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworne the 21st. Novr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones  Thos DeVeil

The Mark of Sarah Binian .
