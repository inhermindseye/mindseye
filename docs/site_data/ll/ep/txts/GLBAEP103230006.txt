Middlesex ss}  Rose Newman the widow of Francis Newman deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that about thirty years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indres to one Mr Coles Taylor of Breams Buildings in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex & after having Served Part of his Apprenticeship sd Master quitting the Business turned this Deponts and husband ever to his Foreman who Suceeded im in his Said Trade of a Taylor there in his Masters house where the sd Apprenticeship ended That her sd husband did not after Such his Apprenticeship to her Knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for he reject and that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 20 day of Janry 1787 before  R Davies  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Rose Newman

Mr Newman keeps warehouses the Both of Bill yard Ann in Bishopsgate Street Inquire here as to the Indres
