Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Young aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Pollard in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months and upwards the yearly Wages of Five Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Strickland who did live at the White House Inn in Fetter Land but where he is now She this Deponent doth not know is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Strickland is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1797 . before me } J Hunter

her Sarah Young Mark

Witness B Newman

Father never taken

Child born in the House the 2d. day of Janry. 1798 named James Young

Qrs. whether what become of this Child Whether any and what Allowance to the Mother
