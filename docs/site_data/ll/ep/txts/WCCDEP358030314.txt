Westmr.  Middx 

Sarah Jackson  Wido . of Joseph Jackson removed from the Parish of Christchurch in the County of Middlesex by Order dated the 8th. of Janry Instant upon Oath saith she intermarried with thesd. Joseph Jackson on or about the 16th. Day of May abt. 15 Years since at Stepney Church Says thesd. Joseph Jackson has been dead upwds. of 8 Yrs. Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years apprenticeship with Mr. Tho. Jackson a Shoe- -maker in St Clements Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx aforesd. Says neither her sd. husband in his Life time nor this Exaiant since his Dead ever rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 9th. 1746 before  Tho. Lane

The Mark of Sarah Jackson
