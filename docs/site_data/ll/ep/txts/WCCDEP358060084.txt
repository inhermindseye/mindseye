Middx  Mary Sherman  Wido of Edwd. Henry Sherman upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Edwd. Henry Sherman abt. 37 Yrs. ago & that he dyed about one Yr. & Eight months since that the sd. Edward Henry Sherman Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Hawke Surgeon in Bow Street in the Parish of St Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year Says she has not since her sd. husbands Decease rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif and that she hath one Child (now with her) by the sd. Edward Henry Sherman (viz) Wm. Henry aged abt. 1 Yr.

Sworn March 20th. 1750 before  Tho Lane

Mary Sherman
