Rebecca Burgin aged about 60 Years upon Oath says She was Married to John Burgin at the Fleet London on the 27th. Day of Decr. 1745 Says that her said Husband (who is now in Chelsea Hospital ) Served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with one George Cutbush a Taylor in the Parish of Maidstone in the County of Kent And hath not since he was out of his said Apprenticeship Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as this Examinant hath been informed by her said Husband and as She verily believes

Sworn the 16th. Day of Octor. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond  Saunder Welch

The mark of Rebecca Burgin
