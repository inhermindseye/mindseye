St Clement Danes September 19th. 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. Giles in the Fields (to wit) Jane Sale  Widow aged 54 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband George Sale at the parish Church of St George Bloomsbury on or about the 23d day of March 1769 (by License) that since their said Marriage her said Husband Rented and Resided in an House in Great Wild Street in the parish of St Giles in Fields  in the County of Middlesex one year & upwards at the Annual Rent of £32 or thereabouts that they quitted that House about Christmas 1779 and her said Husband then went to Jamaica and Died there about 20th March 1781 (as this Deponent hath been informed and verily believes And that she this Deponent hath not since been Married neither hath she since her said Husband Quitted the said House in Wild Street Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or otherw obtained any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the 19th September 1792 before us  Sampn Wright Pitchall

Jane Sale
