Middx  Judith Shepherd aged upwds. of 29 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Steel in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £4 for the Space of 18 Months & hath left the sd. Mr. Steel upwards of 6 Yrs. Says she hath not since she left her said Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she was Delivered of a female Bastard Child on the 17th. Day of May last in the Lyeing in Infirmary in Jermyn Strt. in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized Judith & is likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx she being Poor & not able to maintain the same

Sworn Novr 5 1746 before Mr: Burdus

The Mark of Judith Shepherd
