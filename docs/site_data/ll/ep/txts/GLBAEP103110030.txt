Middlesex ss}  Ann Brown  Singlewoman maketh oath That Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds with Luke Flanagan of the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Victualler and Continued in Such Service for the Space of a Year and upwards and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlemt. there That She this Depont. hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That on the first Day of February last past at the House of John Sibbin in Creed Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin Ludgate  in the City of London She this Depont. was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since Baptized in thesd Parish of St. Martin Ludgate by the name of Elizabeth Margaret Flanagan And that the Said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate and that the Said Luke Flanagan did get her with Child of the Said Bastard child

Sworn this 1st day of November 1771 before Wilmot B Camper

The Mark of Ann Brown
