Middx & Westmr}  Martha Spavan Aged about forty Six Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Robert Spavan who has been dead about Two Years with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Austin London about Twenty Years ago by whom she hath Three Children Vizt. Andrew Aged Twenty Years now Apprentice with one Mr. Rice a Book binder in the City Ann Aged about 12 Years now a Servant with one Mrs. Eyres at Cotsford in the County of Buckingham and Catherine Aged about 8 Years Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with on Mr. Miller a Bookseller in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex And after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 9th. day of May 1770 before me} R. Butler

Martha Spavan
