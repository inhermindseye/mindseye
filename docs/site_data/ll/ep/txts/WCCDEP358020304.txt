St. Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster}  Sarah Rayner Widow Aged Seventy Years and upwards Upon her Oath saith that about twenty two Years ago she Intermarried with her late Husband John Rayner at the parish Church of Saint Saviour in the Bord of Southwark in the County of Surry that her Husband Died abt. 20 years since, that since then being Single & not having any Child or Children she lived with Alexander Gerrard  Esqr . Essex Street in the Strand as an hired yearly Servant for five Years or thereabouts at the Annual Wages of Six Pounds that she quitted his said service  about Fourteen Years ago And that since quitting the same she hath not by any means whatever (to the best of her knowledge or belief) acquired any other legal settlement And that she is incapable of maintaIn maintaining herself

Sworn the 28th June 1791 before me R Butler

Sarah Rayner
