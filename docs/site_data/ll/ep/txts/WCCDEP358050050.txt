Middx 8  Richd. Hutchenson aged abt. 44 Years upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his present Wife Ann the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch about twelve Years ago to his best remembrance Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven years Apprentiship with Mr. Edwd. Jones a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex and hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship about Twenty two years says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 2d. 1749 before} Wm. Jones

Richd Hutchinson
