Middx  Phillip Samber aged near 40 years upon Oath Saith he was born in Witch Strt. in the Parish of St Clemt. danes within the Liberty of westmr. in the County of Middx & that he has been informed & believes his late father Jas. Samber Served the  year of his Clerkship wth. this Examints. Uncle  Wm Samber decd. in Lyons Inn in the same Parish & then this Examints. father went to Sea & there dyed and the sd. Philip Samber further Maketh Oath that he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. nor ever Rented ten Pounds a year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn 12th. Decr. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Phillip Sambers

Mrs. Newton Witch str. Says he kept the Angel Inn at Lymengton in Hampshires
