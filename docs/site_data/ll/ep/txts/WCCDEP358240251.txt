Militia

St Clement Danes April 24th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  (to wit) Catherine Taylor aged 35 years and upwards Wife of Joseph Taylor Maketh Oath and saith that She this Examinant being a Widow and having no Husband living was on the eleventh day of October 1790 Married to Joseph Taylor who was then a Widower and as this Deponent was informed and verily believes (having no reason to think to the contrary) was then a Widower and had no Wife living at the parish Church of St. Mary le bone by Banns that her said Husband has been duly Sworn and enrolled to Serve as Substitute for John Granger in the Parish of St Mary le bone in the said County in the Western Regiment of Militia of the said County which was last embodied and called out into actual Service on or about the 28th day of January last and hath been so ever since and that the said Joseph Taylor still serves as such Substitut Substitute in the said Militia as She is informed and verily believes that She hath resided ever since the said 28th day of January last when her said Husband joined the said Regiment and still dwells at the House of Messrs Colvin and Lowndes Dealers in Liquor in the Strand near Temple Bar in the said parish of St Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex that She hath no Children by her said Husband now living And that She is unable to provide for herself without assistance

Sworn the 24th day of April 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner D Walker Catherine Taylor
