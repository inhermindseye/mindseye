Middx  Eleanr. Thornley upon Oath saith she is the Wife of George Thornley with whom she intermarried in Portugal abt. 14 Yrs ago, that she has not Seen her sd. husband (who is master a board the Scorpion Sloop of war) for two Years past except once that when he was in the Downs about Eight months ago, and at that time she stood in a beat & spoke to her sd. husband who was then in the Sd Ship & under Sail and that she did not then go aboard the sd. Ship nor has Seen her sd. husbd. for two years past in any manner, than this once as aforesaid and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was on the 11th. Day of Augt. Instt. Delivered of a male bastard Child in a house in Vinegar Yard in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . which has been baptized William

Sworn Augt. 24th 1752 before

The mark of Eleanr. Thornley
