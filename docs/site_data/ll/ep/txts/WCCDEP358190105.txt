Middx  William Sumner aged about 51 Years Destitute of Lodging upon Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice by an Indenture for Seven Years to Mr. John Whitehead a Taylor near Hand Alley in Holbourn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex and with his said Master there served all his time out which Expired about 30 Years since and hath not since rented Ten pound a Year paid Parish Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the 26th. day of Febry 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Wm. Sumner

Upon Mr. Kirks Complt.
