Middlesex ss}  Sarah Waghorne the Wife of  John Waghorne who is gone away from her to places unknown maketh oath That her said husband has informed her and Which information She believes to be true That her said husband While a Singleman was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at certain wages with one Mr Shard in the Parish of Camberwell in the County of Surry and Continued in Such Service for divers years and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and that to this Deponts knowledge or belief her said husband did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of September 1774 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

The Mark of Sarah Waghorne
