Middx ss  Mary the Parry the wife of John Parry who is now at Sea Maketh oath that her husband has often informed her that his Father Henry Parry deced duly served his apprenticeship with Mr Lewin a Fishmonger in Leaden Hall  market in the Parish of st. Peter in Cornhill London & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And that her said husbands father never after gained any subsequent settlement And that her said husband never gained any settlemt. but in right of his said Father having Never Rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or Upwards Nor served any Parish offices nor been a hired servant by the Year nor an apprentice nor gained any other settlement than as aforesd which Information this Deponent verily believes to be true And this Deponent saith that she hath a Child Named John aged about four years the lawful issue of this Depont by her said husband

Sworn this 15th day of March 1757 before us  Richd. Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Mary Parry
