Middx & Westmr.}  Fidusha O' Neale aged about 18 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes served any Appren. or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says her father Fras. O' Neale Rented and lived in a House in the Strand Opposite Southampton Street Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for ten Years & upwards at the Yearly Rent of £53 hath quitted the same about twelve Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever gained any other subsequent Settlement an either hath this Examinant since her said Father Death and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born says that one Willm. Roberts  Apprentice to Mr. Tooms at No. 29 Foller Lane he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of March or April but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. times afterwards at one of which time he got her with Child of the Chid or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Willm. Roberts and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 1st. day of Octr. 1776 before R Butler

her Fidusha O Neale Mark
