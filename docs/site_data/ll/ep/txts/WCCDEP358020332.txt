St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster}  (to wit) Henrietta Wood Aged 37 years or thereabouts upon her oath saith that She intermarried with Nathaniel Wood at the parish of Church of St James Westminster in the said County on the 29th Septr. 1772 (by Banns) That Previous there her said Husband hath Informed her this Deponent that he was Settled & in & belonged to the parish of St Clement Danes by Service with Counsr. Baldwin in Essex in the Strand as an hired yearly Servant in the capacity of Butler & Footman  at the Wages or £12 & 12s a year or thereabouts & quitted the same abord but for what time or how long he lived in such Service this Examinant doth not know either by Information or otherwise And that she is now Poor unable to maintain herself & Children which are two Namely Edward Aged 13 years or thereabouts & John aged 1 year & Six Months or thereabouts both together by her said Husband - that her said Husband left this Deponent Children about five Months ago without her knowledge & she is now entirely Ignorant where  he is except that he has heard of his being at Sea in his Majestys Service.
