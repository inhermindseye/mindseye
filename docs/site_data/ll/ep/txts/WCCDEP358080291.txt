Middlesex  to wit Francis Salmon Aged about fifty Three years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to his late Wife  Hannah at the Parish Church of St. George Hanover Square about Twenty four years ago and by whom he hath one Boy living now provided for Says before his Intermarrd. to his said late Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with and Mrs. Raines at Charlton in the Parish of Charlton in the County of Kent for One year and Ten Months at the yearly Wages of Six pounds a year besides Meat Drink Lodging and Cloaths hath quitted his said Services upwards of Twenty four years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement with by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a year payg. Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 9th. day of Octr. 1778 before  John Cox . James Croft .

his Francis Salmon Mark
