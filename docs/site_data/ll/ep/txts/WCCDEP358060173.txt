Middx  Mary Wood aged about 19 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with Robert Wood about 12 months since at the Fleet London by whom she hath one Child (viz) Margt . aged about two months Says her sd. husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr Twocock in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and never Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 20th. 1751 before H Fielding

The mark of Mary Wood
