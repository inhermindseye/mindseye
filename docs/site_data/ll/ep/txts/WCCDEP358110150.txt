Middx  Wm. Daws upon Oath Saith he lived a Yearly Servant for the Space of four Years with Mrs. Evers at the Noah's Ark in King Street in the parish of Charles at Plimouth in the County of Devon at the yearly wages of £7 and was during all the time of his sd. Servitude a Singleman that he quitted his sd. Service upwds. of two Years ago & has not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Jany. 20th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

William Daws
