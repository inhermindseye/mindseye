Middx 

Hannah Gammon aged abt. 24 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt for one Year rented Ten pounds a Yr. or pd. Parish Taxes and that her father Robt. Gammon deced lived in and rented the Greyhound Allhouse at Oakingham in Berkshire and pd. Scot and Lot there for some Yrs.

Sworn June 12th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Hanh. Gammon
