Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

passd. to Mortlake to wit Jane Edmonds aged about 68 years on her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Saml. Edmonds at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square in the year 1748 that her said Husband has been dead upwards of Six Months that her said Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship with one John Barns a Waterman at Mortlake in the Parish of Mortlake in the County of Surry that he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere nor hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death.

Sworn this 3d. day of September 1785  Sampr Wright J Addington

her Jane Edmonds Mark
