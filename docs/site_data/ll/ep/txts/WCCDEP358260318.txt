Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Lloyd aged about Twenty years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married That on or about the Eleventh day of December now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of a Mrs. Kale on Saint Peters Hill in the Parish of Saint Peter Pauls Wharf in the City of London which hath since been Christened by the name of Mary and further the Deponent upon her Oath saith That the said Child is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 21st. day of Augt. 1798 before us  N Bond Henry Wisstea

her Mary Lloyd Mark

Witness H. Day
