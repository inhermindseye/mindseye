Middlesex to wit Thos. Silvester aged about Forty Eight years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he served seven Years Apprenticeship with one Thos. Lincon a Spectacle maker in Little Britain in the Parish of St. Bartholomew the less and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement than as aforesaid.

Sworn this 12th. day of Septr. 1783 W Addington Will Wright

his Thos Silvester mark
