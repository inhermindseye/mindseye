Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Cammock aged about fourteen years maketh oath that She has been informed by her mother Sarah Smith (Who is now living in Hackney Workhouse ) and Which information this Deponent believes to be true that She this Deponent was born a Bastard in Cock villey in the Parish of st Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County by reason that his Deponts. Mother was never married to this Deponents Father John Cammock deced That She this Deponent was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as hired Servant for the Space of a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 3. day of January 1788 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Cammock
