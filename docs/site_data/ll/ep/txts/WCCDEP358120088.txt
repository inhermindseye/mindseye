Middx  Ann Threckingham aged 26 years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Ellit upwds. of 2 Yrs. at Mr Conlstons a books in Fleur do his Court in the parish of St. Dunstan in the west London at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Shillings dyet Washing & lodging quitted her sd. Service 5 or 6 Years ago and has not since rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one yr.

Sworn April 9th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Threckingham
