Middx to wit Ann Pollard Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House  Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, Says her Father Richard Pollard Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with his Father Drake Pollard a Cutler in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born, Says that one Edward Moore a Journeyman Fishmonger who lives with one Mr. Phillips in Carnaby Market had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in this Examinants Mother House in Clements Lane the Latter end of October last and at Several different Times after at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of Which She is now Pregnant and that the said Edward Moore and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 2d day of June 1775   Tho.Lane

Ann Pollard
