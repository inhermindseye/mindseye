Middx 

Ann Mitchel aged about 20 Yrs. upon Oath saith she intermarried with Thomas Mitchel  at the Fleet London on or about the 3d. Augt. 1747 that the sd. Thomas Mitchel went from her about Six months ago and she knows not where he is Says her sd. husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid parish Taxes or Served a parochial Office to this Examinants knowledge or beleif nor can she give any Account of the Settlemt. of her husband father or mother Says she (this Examint.) never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt rented ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxe that her own father Hainstone Busk deceased was a german and was brough over to England by a Gentleman who lived in Albemarle Street as his Valet de Chambe  but knows not whether he was a Yearly Servant to this Gentleman or how long

he lived with him that her mother has been dead about 5 Yrs. & she can give no Account whatever of her Settlement before marriage that she was brought out of Essex Street in Labour and delivered of a male Child at the house of a parish Nurse belonging to the parish of St. Clemt. Danes which has been baptized Edmund

Sworn June 20th 1750 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Ann Mitchels
