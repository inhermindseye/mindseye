Middx  Eleanr. Moore  Singleman upon Oath Says she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Tho. Smith at the Boyles head in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. for the Space of two Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £5 Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Smith Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 14th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Eleanr: Moore
