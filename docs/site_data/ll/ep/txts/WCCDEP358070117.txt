Westmr.  Elizth. Henley aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Rented and lived in a House in Justices Walk in the Parish of St. Lukes Chelsea in the County of Middlesex in which Parish this Examinant was born and this Examinant further saith that she was Passed from Saint Andrew Holbourn upon the said Parish of Saint Lukes Chelsea aforesaid where her said Settlement was found to be since which time she has never gained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. July 1773 before} Tho Kynaston

Elizth. Henley
