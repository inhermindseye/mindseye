Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Nowland aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year that she this Examt. was bound an Apprentice fee to a Mr. Asseter in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for seven years that she served Twelve Months of her said Apprenticeship and then had her Indentures given up And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Thomas Downes a Journeyman Bedstead maker who lodges at No:24 Rose Lane Spitalfields is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Thos. Downes  and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 18th. Day of febry. 1789 .}
