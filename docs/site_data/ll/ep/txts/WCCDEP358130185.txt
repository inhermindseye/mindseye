Middx  Mary Martin Widow on the behalf of Mary Browne Singlewoman a Lunatic aged about 28 years upon Oath says the said Mary Browne never was married as She hath been informed and believes and that She lived a hired yearly Servant upwards of a year with one Mrs. King in St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex and quitted the same upwards of two years since And further says the said Mary Browne hath not since her quitting the Service of the said Mrs. King Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes on lived a hired yearly Servant for one year as this Examinant hath been informed and as she Verily believes

Sworn the 26th. Day of July 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham  Barth: Hammond

The mark of Mary Martin
