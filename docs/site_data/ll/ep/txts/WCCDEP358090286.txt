Middlesex to wit Alice James Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she has lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edward Bennett a Haberdasher in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of five Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and who has never gained any other Settlemt. than as aforesd. And further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the sd Parish of Saint Clement Danes (she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born) And further Says that one John Lock a Granadier in the Guards and who now lives near Old Gravel Lane the other side of Tower Hill is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of July last under a Gateway near Arrundell Street Strand and at one other time afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that the said John Lock and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn the 11th. day of February 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Alice James Mark
