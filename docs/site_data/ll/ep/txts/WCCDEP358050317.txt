Eleanr. Payne  Wido . of Chas. Payne who dyed about 8 yrs. since upon Oath saith that about 2 Yrs. ago being then a Wido. and without Child or Children she lived Yearly Servant with Mr Johnson a Taylor in Beaufort Buildings in the parish of St Clemt. Danes  Middx at the yearly wages of £6 and left him for one Year & upwds. & has not since rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year otherwise gained Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 15th. 1750 before}
