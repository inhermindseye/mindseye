Middx  Mary Trow  wido . of Rt. Trow who dyed abt. 16 years ago upon Oath saith she was married to the said Rt. Trow at the Fleet London abt. 16 Yrs. since and that her said late husband informed her that before his intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst a Singleman he lived a yearly Servant with Mr Maud at the Black Swan Lyon Inn in Holbourn in the Parish of St Andrew in Holbourn London for Eighteen months at the yearly Wages of £7. and never after he Left his sd Service rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year nor has this Examinant since his Decease Rented ten pounds by the Year pd parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant

Sworn 25th. Febry 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Trow
