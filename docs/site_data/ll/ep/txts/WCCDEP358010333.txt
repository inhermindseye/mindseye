Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Hannah Harrison aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly with Ge Harrison in Hemlock Court in the Parish of saint Clement Danes for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And this Examinant further saith That she hath Two illegitimate Children living Vizt. John aged upwards of Seven years born at the house of the said George Harrison in Hemlock Court and Wm. Henry aged about Three Months born at the house of a Mr. Rich in Kumber Street  whitefryars

her Hannah Harrison Mark
