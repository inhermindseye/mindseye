Middx & Westmr.}  Margt. Mills aged about 29 Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Walter Mills with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes upwards of 4 Years ago Says her father William Danson has rented & lived in a house in Cooks Court St. Clement Danes for sevl. Years last past and that this Examinant never gained any other subsequent Settlement other than (thro' her Father And furr. upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 2d. Day of Decr. 1776 she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in her said Fathers House whose name is Thomas and is now become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same And that one Thomas Hayward  Clerk of the Cheque to the Gentn. Pensioners who lately lodged at the Humums Bagnio in Covent Garden and lives at Whittington near Dorodess Well in Gloucestershire is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body about the Middle of May 1768 in her said Fathers hous and at several diffr. times afterwds. at one of which time he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she is was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Thomas Hayward and no other person did beget the same and that he is the Real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 6th. Day of Decr. 1776 M A

Margret Mills
