Saint Clement Danes 9th August 1786

Middx (to wit) Hannah Eaton Aged 36 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she this Examinant Intermarried with her present Husband Thomas Eaton in the Parish Church of St Sepulchie in the City of London on the 15th day of Decr. 1774 That her said Husband has Informed her that he Lived at the White House Cellar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex as a Hired yearly Servant for upwards of 12 Months and that since he quitted his said Service he hath not done any Act Matter or thing Whereby to the best of this Examinant Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Settlement and this Examinant further upon her Oath saith That she hath One Child Named Elizabeth aged upwards of Six Years now Living by her said Husband which has been for a long time Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And That her said Husband

has desorted her and her Child upward of 5 years And that she this Examinant is unable to support hereself any Larger 

Sworn this 9th day of August 1786  before  Wm Kitchiner

her Hannah Eaton Mark

Thos. Eaton to be found at every Night at Astley

Resworn this 23rd. day of June 1787 before R Butler

Hannah Eaton her Mark
