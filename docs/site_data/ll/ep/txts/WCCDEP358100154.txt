Middlesex to wit Wm. Slater aged about fifty five years on his Oath says he has an Illegitimate Child living by one Martha Laurence lately deceased namely William Aged upwards of Twelve years born a Bastard in George Yard in the Parish of St. Peters without the walls of the Tower London and that he has gained no other Settle  Settlement than by Birth

Sworn this 20th day of Novr. 1783  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

William Slater
