Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Donnolly aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 13th. day of January 1763 she Intermarried with Thomas her late Husband (who has been dead about Nine Months) at the parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Thomas aged about Nine That her late Husband Rented a Second floor of a Mr. Carruthers in Little Sliver Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Three years at the yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds and that after he quitted said Apartments he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And this Deponent further saith that her said late Husband left a Son which he had by a former Wife Vizt. Thomas aged about Nine years

Sworn this 23rd. day of December 1796 } J Reeves

Ann Donnolly
