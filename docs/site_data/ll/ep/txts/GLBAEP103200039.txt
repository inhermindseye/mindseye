Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Bean of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the sd County this First day of August 1783 -who Saith that she is now with Child And that the said Child is likely to be born a Bastard & to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that Thomas Smith of Church Lane in the Parish of St George Middx  Chandler is the Father of the said Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Bean

Father of a Bastard Child sworn to Before Birth
