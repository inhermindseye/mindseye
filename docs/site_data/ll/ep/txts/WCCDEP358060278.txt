Middlesex  Wm. Cole upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year and a half together with Mr Stephen Fairweather at the Crown Alehouse in Essex Street in the parish of St Clements Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £3 and left his said Service about 5 Yrs. ago, since which he his not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 13th. Day of 1752 before}

N. The above named Wm Cole was a Yr. wanting 6 weeks with Mr Fairweather at the End of which time he went away & Continued with his father a month(which month was not pd. for in his wages)& then returned & lived the remr. of the 18 months out.
