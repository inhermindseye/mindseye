Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Hope aged about 50 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Sarah his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Pancras about 19 years ago that he rented and lived in a House in Bedford Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars for Six Months at the Yearly Rent of £25 that he hath quitted said House about 10 years Since which time he has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of Augt. 1788 M Addington N. Forster

James Hope
