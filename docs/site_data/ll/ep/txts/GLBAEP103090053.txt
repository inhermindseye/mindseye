Middlesex ss  Mary Martin  single woman maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the Year at Certain yearly wages with one Luke Stokes in Sweed land Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx and Continued in such service for the Space of two years & half and by Means thereof as she this Deponent is Advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that she hath not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor was she ever Married Nor hath she done any Act since her servitude as above whereby to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this day of 1767 before}

Mary Martin
