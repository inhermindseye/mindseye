Middx  Sarah Haydon Aged about 25 years on her Oath says she Never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Wilson a Ship Chandler at Chadwell Dock in the Parish of Saint Pauls Chadwell in the County of Middlesex for Eleven Months or upwards but received Twelve Months Wages that this Examinant was Passed from Saint Mary Lambeth to Saint Pauls Chadwell Saint Pauls Chadwell Appealed and on hearing the Appeal the same was dismissed after quitting her said Masters Service she Never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired yearly Servant for one year And Further on her Oath says she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child on the 22 day of Decr. 1776 in the House of Mr. Hayes in Angel Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which has since been Baptized by the Name of Margaret Diana and is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish and that one Thos. Johnston a Cabinet Maker in Angell Court aforesaid is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime February 1776 and at Several Different times after at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Thos. Johnston and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 13th. day of Octr. 1778 before R Butler

Sarah Haydon
