Middx to wit Elizth Pomfrett Aged of Twenty Years or there abouts on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes nor ever lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Edwd Pomfrett rented & Lived in a house in Worship Street Moorfields in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middx  Chandler about Two years at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & paid parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Six years ago since which time he never Rented House Tent. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or or.wise howsoever

Sworn this 28th. day of Septr. 1784 before G Mercer James Croft

Elizebeth Pomfrett
