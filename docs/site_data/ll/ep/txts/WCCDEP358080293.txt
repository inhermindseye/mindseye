Middx  Catherine Bonny aged about Seventy three years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she was Covenanted to serve & learn the Trade of a Mantua Maker with one Mrs. Egan a Mantua Maker in Holles Street Clare Market for four years & after serving her said Mrs. Egan for that time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain he same says that one Saml. Eaton a Buckle Chafe Maker who uses the Sign of the Bull & Monk Publick House adjoining to the Burial Ground in Drury Lane is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings at one Mr. Smarts in Saffron Hill the later end of January last and Several Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Saml. Eaton and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 13th. day of October 1778 before R Butler

her Catherine Bonny Mark
