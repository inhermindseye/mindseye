Middx  Martha Hoy upon Oath saith she is the wife of Dennis Hoy who is as Irishman & never gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or belief and that at the time of her intermarriage with the sd. Dennis Hoy she was the wido: of Danl. Leary who was likewise an Irishman & never gained a Settlemt. in England and that her own mother Ann Peacock after the Decease of this Examinants father & whilst She Continued a wido. lived in & Rented a house of the yearly Rent of £24 in Stanhope Strt. in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for two Years or thereabts. and that she this Examinant never Rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year and that She has a Child of her first husband Leary (viz) Margt . aged abt. 16 Yrs. who never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt

Sworn 22d May 1755 before} Benjamin Cox

Martha Hoy
