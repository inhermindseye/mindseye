Middlesex ss}  Sarah Goodwin  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by and with one Mr Bristow of Epping Forrest in the parish of wanstead in the County of Essex  Engine maker & Continued in Such Service for the Space of three years & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year else where nor has She been ever married nor done any Act to gain anothr Settlemt. That She has a child named George Hammond aged about three years & eleven months born in the parish of Westham in the County of Essex & also anothr child named Ann Hammond born in the parish of St George in the county of Middx & aged about fourteen months Both of which children are Bastard by reason She this Depont was never married

Sworn this 20 day of February 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Sarah Goodwin
