Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Mansel aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that about 8 years ago she Intermarried with Joshua her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields and by whom she hath Two Childn. living Vizt. Ann aged upwards of Two years and Mary aged about 5 Months that her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Fleming Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship He never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That her said Husband has deserted her and refuses to allow her & said Children any Support.

Sworn this 29th. day of July 1788 R. Butler

her Mary Mansel  Mark
