Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Benham aged about 41 years upon his Oath saith that about 21 years ago he Intermarried with Ann his present wife at the parish Church of Saint Pauls Shadwell by whom he hath one Child living Vizt. Robert aged about four years That previous to his Intermarriage and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Roberts at the White Horse Inn in Fetter Lane in the parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Bars in the City of London for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Twenty Pounds That he hath quitted said Service upwards of Twenty years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th- day of Janry. 1796 } J Hind Edw. Read

his Wm Benham mark
