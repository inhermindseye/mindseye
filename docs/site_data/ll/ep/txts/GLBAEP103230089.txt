Middlesex ss  Sarah Hudson the widow of Robert Hudson deced maketh oath that Since her Said husbands death (who died legally Settled in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County) that She hath not lived as a hired Servant for a year or been Married or rented a house or Lodging of the year by rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that She was born in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate & was married at the Fleet to her Sd late husband about forty Seven years ago

Sworn this. 1. day of Decemr 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Hudson
