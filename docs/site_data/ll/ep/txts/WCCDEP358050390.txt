Middx 

Eliz Martin aged abt. 25 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice by Indenture dated the 29th. Septr 1740 by the Church wardens & Ovrs. of St. Clemt. Danes Middx to Margt. the Wife of Robt Nutt  Mariner till she should attain the age of 21 Yrs. and lived with the sd. Margt Nutt as her Apprentice at the Sign of the Kings Arms near Wapping new Stairs in the parish of  St. John at Wapping in the County of Middx for three or 4 Years and then ran away from her sd. Mistress and that she never was assigned over to any other person rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 7th. Decr 1750 before} Tho Lane  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Martin
