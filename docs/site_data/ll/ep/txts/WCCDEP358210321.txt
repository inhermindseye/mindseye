Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Maria Ady Aged about Twenty one years upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wright a Glazier in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years, at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds, quitted said Service about Three years ago and hath not since Rented House, paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for One year. And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards, and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born; Says that one Thomas Fisher had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of March 1768, and at times afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant, and that the said Thomas Fisher and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 20th day of September 1768 , before} Thos Kynaston

Sarah Maria Ady her Mark
