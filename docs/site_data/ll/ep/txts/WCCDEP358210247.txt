Westmr.  Ann Davison Aged about Thirty two Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Fairweather in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , Victualler , for Three Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds Quitted said Service about Twelve Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 17th. day of February 1768 , Before W Kelynge

The Mark of Ann Davison
