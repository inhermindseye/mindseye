Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Rebecca White the Wife of John White the Younger hereafter named William Bayley of Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields  Leather Dyer and Joseph White of the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  Yeoman upon their Oaths severally say and first this Ext. Rebecca White upon her Oath saith that she was married to John White her present Husband at Portsea in the County of Southampton on the 2d. day of Decr. in the year of our Lord 1782 that she has two Children by her said Husband now living the One thereof named Joseph aged 2 years & 5 Months or thereabouts (and the other Eleanor aged 6 years & a half or thereabouts) who was born a Bastard before her Intermarriage to her said Husband at the House of one Mr. Tannage a Shipwright in Cornwall Row in the Parish of Kingston at Portsea aforesaid and that the said John White her now Husband is the only real & true father of such Child that her said Husband has deserted her and she is poor and unable to maintain herself and Children And this Examinant Wm. Baley for himself saith that he hath known John White the Elder the father of the said John White the Younger the Husband of the said Rebecca White for many years that he well remembers John White the Elder lived in the Parish of all Saints in the City of Worcester Rented a House and This Examinant Guineas paid Taxes for the same There, and that the said John White the younger lived with him as Part of his family being about Seven years old when this Examinant last Worcester And this Ext. Joseph White for himself saith that he is the Son of John White the Elder that

the said John White the Younger this Exts. Brother left his said father whilst he lived in the Parish of saint all Saints at Worcester aforesaid and went into the Army and never has since lived with his father as part of his family but has been wholly Emancipated from his said father

Sworn this 22d. day of Janry. 1787  Philip Dyot D Walker

her Rebecca White Mark Joseph White Wm Bailey
