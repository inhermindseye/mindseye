Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Dunking the Widow of Gasper Dunking deced maketh oath that her Said late husband in the year 1781 Rented a house in the Parish of St Botolph Without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly renter Value of Ten Pounds or upwards for Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath a Child named Charles aged about nine years & three Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband to whom She was lawfully married which Child hath done no Act to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this. 29. day of November 1787 before  Rob: Smith

Eliz Dunking
