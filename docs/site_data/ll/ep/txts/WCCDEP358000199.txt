Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Andrew Rooke aged 50 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he never was married to his Present Wife according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England that he Rented and lived in a House in Strand Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 18 Months at the yearly Rent of £16 hath quitted said House about a Twelve month since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Settlement And further this Examinant saith that he has 4 . Illegitimate Children living vizt. Andrew Pearce Rooke aged 10 years or thereabouts who was born in Cross Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster  Elizth. Rooke aged 3 years and upwards who was born in St. John Street in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex  Mary Rooke aged 2 years and upwards who was born in St. John Street in the said Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx and Margt. Rooke aged 1 year or thereabout who was born in Strand Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further this Deponent saith that he was married to Bridgett Flanagan the Mother of the said Children at the Chapel in Duke Street by a Roman Catholic Priest according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Romish Church

Sworn this 7th- day of Janry-1786  Rt. Taylor  Jno: Wright

Andw. Rooke

Resworn this 10th. Day of Janry. 1786 Before us G Mercer G Allcock

Andw. Rooke
