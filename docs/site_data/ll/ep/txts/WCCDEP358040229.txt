Westmr. 

Eliz. Payne Wife of Thomas Payne a Soldier in his Majestys Service upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Thomas Payne on the 23d. Day of Febry 1747 at the Fleet London and that she hath been informed and beleives the sd. Thomas Payne Whilst a Singlewoman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. John How  at in the Parish of Fornham three Kings in the County of Suffolk for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds & four Shillings and hath not since he quitted his sd. Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Exts. knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 8th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Eliz Payne her Mark
