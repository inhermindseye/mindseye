Middx  Susanna Taylor  Wido . upon Oath says she Intermarried with her late Husband Wm. Taylor on the 26th. Day of March about 14 years since Says the sd. Wm. Taylor hath been dead near Eleven months by whom She hath two Children (to wit) Frances aged abt. 9 Yrs. & Wm. aged abt. two Years Says the sd. Wm. Taylor was a native of Scotland & never gained a Settlemt. in England as she hath been informed & beleives Says at the time of her intermarriage with the said Wm. Taylor she was the Widow of one Alexander Forbus Says she intermarried with the said Alexr. Forbus at the fleet London about Sixteen years since Says the said Alexr. Forbus hath been dead abt. 15 Yrs to the best of her Remembrance and Served five Years the first part of his Apprentiship with a Butcher in Fleet Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchers London Says the sd. Alexr. Forbus never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived

with any person as a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 22d 1746 , before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Susa Taylor
