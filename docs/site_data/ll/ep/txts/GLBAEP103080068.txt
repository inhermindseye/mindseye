Middlesex ss  Mathew Huscraft Maketh oath That about twenty eight Years ago he was duly bound an Apprentice by Indenture to one Joseph Cannon of the Parish of Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry  Sawyer with whom he served six years and Nine Months and was then impressed for the Sea Service And this Deponent farther Saith that he hath not since his said apprenticeship been an Apprentice nor lived as a hired servant by the year nor rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year or upwards nor served any Parish offices Nor to his knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his apprenticeship And that he hath two Children to wit Mary Ann aged about eleven years and Alexander Aged about two Years and three quarters the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his late Wife Elizabeth deceased.

Sworn this 10th: day of March 1760 before  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

the Mark of Mathew Huscraft
