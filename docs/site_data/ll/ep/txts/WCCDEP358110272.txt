Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Smith upon Oath saith she is the widow of John Smith who dyed about two Years ago And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a male (Shillborn) bastard Child on Monday the 9th. of July Inst. at the Charges of the Inhabitants of the Parish of St. Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex she being poor & unable to maintain herself in her lying in And that Thomas Hartley a Smith did beget the sd. male bastard Child on her body; the sd. Thos. Hartley having had carnal knowledge of this Examt. thrice in the Month of November last at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. and that the sd. Thos. Hartley & no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn July 10th. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jane Smith
