Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Cummins aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1755 she Intermarried with Enoch Cummins her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn that her said late Husband has been dead about 20 years that before his Intermarriage to this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Ewer in Hatton Garden in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex in the Capacity of a Clerk for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of £50 that after he quitted said Service he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of March 1788  Sampr Wright M Addington

her Ann Cummins Mark
