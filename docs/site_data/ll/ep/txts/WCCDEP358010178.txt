Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Cole aged about 17 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that about 2 years ago she was bound apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square for Seven years th to one Robert Nash late of the Strand Japan that she was discharged from her said Master yesterday that the last Forty days of her said apprenticeship to the said Robert Nash was in Catherine Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand

Sworn this 15th. day of Janry. 1788 D Walker Rapt: Clarke

her Elizabeth Cole Mark
