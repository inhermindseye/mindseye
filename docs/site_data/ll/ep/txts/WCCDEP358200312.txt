Westmr.  Ann Richardson aged about 52 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Rowland Richardson with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields about Twenty Eight Years ago says her said Husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Shoepine a Fanstick maker and lived the last Year of his time with the said Mr. Shoepine at the Corner of Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says she has two Children by her said late husband; Sarah aged Eighteen Years and John Rowland aged abt. Thirteen Years who were never Apprentices or Yearly Servants

Sworn 31st. Octr. 1765 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Ann Richardson
