Middx.  Mary Adamson aged abt. 55 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Philip Adamson who hath been dead abt. ten Yrs. with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Gregory by St. Paul London abt. thirty Years ago: says her sd. Husband served his Apprenticeship of seven Yrs. with his Brother Richd. Adamson a Perukemaker in the Arch way in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. as her said Husband informed her & as she verily believes says her said Husband in his Life time nor she this Examinant since his death, never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parochial Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the 4th. March 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Mary Adamson
