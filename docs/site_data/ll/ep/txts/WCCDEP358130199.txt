Middx  Thomas Cook aged abt. Sixty Years upon Oath says he Rented a House in Hollis Street Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds & lived therein abt. two Years & paid Parochial Taxes  hath quitted sd. House upwds. of Thirty Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says he Intermarried with Mary Cook his late Wife in the Parish Church of Dunbarton in Scotland abt. Twenty five Years since Says he hath Three Childn. by his said late Wife living at Invararo in the Shire of Argyle (to wit) John aged abt. Twelve Years, Isabella aged abt. Five Years & Thos . aged upwards of Three Years

Sworn the 11th. Septr. 1756 . before B.H. Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Thomas Cook
