Middx  Jane Hickson upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she Served 2 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mrs. Potter a Milliner in Turnstile in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx. and lay all that time in her mistress house in Turnstile afsd. and that she has not since the Expiration of her sd. Apprentiship rented ten pounds a year pd. parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Yrs.

Sworn March 6th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Jane Hickson
