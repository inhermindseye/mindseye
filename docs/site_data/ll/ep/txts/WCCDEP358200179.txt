Westmr.  Wm- Hawkins Aged about Sixty two Years upon Oath Says he Rented a House in Great Bennett Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . about Ten Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted said House about Twenty four Years & hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes.

Sworn the 8th of Decr. 1764 Before} Benjamin Cox  Paul Vaillant

William Hawkins
