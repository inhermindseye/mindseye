Middlesex and Westminster }  No settlemt in St CD for he had afterwd. Rented remds & Resided therein in Swan Yard in St Mary le Strand Vide Examt. a page 206 Book No. 24 proper settlemt. (to wit) George Dove aged 22 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that he Married with Charlotte  his late Wife formerly Charlotte Kennedy  Spinster  at the parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn (by Bann) on or about the 5th. day of April 1790 that his said Wife Died on or about the 11th. day of January 1793. that he hath two Children by his said Wife namely Benjamin John aged 2 years on the 12th day of January last and Charlotte born on the 6th. day of November last And this Examinant further saith that he served his Apprentice- ship under Indentures (duly executed) with Mr Charles Mills No 3 Grey hound Court Milford Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lodged in little Essex Street in the Strand for the last four Months of such his Apprenticeship and since the Expiration thereof he hath not acquired any legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 13th. day of March 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

George Dove
