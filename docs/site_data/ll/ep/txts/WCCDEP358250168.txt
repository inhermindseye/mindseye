Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Margaret Smith maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said Margaret Smith never gained any Settlement in her own Right That her Mother Elizabeth Smith Rented unfurnished Apartments of a Mrs. Butler in Pawlin Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards and Paid the Annual Rent of Thirteen pounds and that after she quitted said Apartments she never Rented Ten pounds a year or did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of May 1795 } John Collick N Bond

Jas Talboys
