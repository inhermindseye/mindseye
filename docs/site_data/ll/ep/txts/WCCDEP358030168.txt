Middx  Valentine Harper aged upwards of 16 Years upon his Oath saith he was born in St Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes Says he hath been informed & beleives his Father Valentine Harper Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Mr Williams a Taylor in the Parish of St. Lawrance in Ludlow in the County of Salop Says his Father never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 24 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Valentine Harper
