Middlesex ss  John Price maketh oath that about three years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Anderson Poole of Great Tower Street in the Parish of all hallows Bar King in the City of London & Continued in Such Service at Certain Wages for the Space of two years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That be this Deponent has not Since lived as hired Servant by the year elsewhere for the Space of a year or done any any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of November 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes P. Rowlands

The Mark of John Price
