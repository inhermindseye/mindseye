Middx  Martha Mullins Aged about Twenty nine years on behalf of Rebecca James her Daughter by a Former Husband On her Oath says she was Bound  an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes to John Hall a Carpenter in Dean Street Holbourn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex by Indenture Dated the 24th. day of September 1779 and Served her said Master under such Indenture for the Space of Two years and Upwards untill her said Master was arrested and  to Goal & after parting with her said Master she never gained any other settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1781 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Martha Mullins  Mark
