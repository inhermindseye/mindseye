Middx.  Ann Mc.farson aged abt. Twenty Seven Years Wife of Robt. Mc.farson (gone from her) upon Oath says She Intermarried with the sd. Robt. Mc.farson at the Fleet London in the Month of January 1749. Says the sd. Robt. Mc.farson before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & While a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant  with Robt. Dunbar Esqr. at Bethnal Green in in the Parish of St. Matthew Bethnal Green in the County of Middx. for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds as She hath been informed & Verily believes

Sworn Decr. 1st. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Mary Ann Mcfarson
