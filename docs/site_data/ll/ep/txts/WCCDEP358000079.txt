Saint Clement Danes July 23 1785

Middx(to wit)  Passd. to Exn. taken of Dohens in order to Pass his Childn. to Wentworth in the County of York in Case they should  become Chargeable  Lewis Francis Dohens aged thirty Six years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he was Born at Dunkirk in the Kingdom of France that he has never Rented any House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of £10, paid any Taxes for or in respect of any House or Tenement, Served any Parochial Office, Served as an Apprentice Lived for One year as an Hired yearly Servant or done any other Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may ever have acquired any settlement in England Saith that he Intermarried with Margaret Dohens his late Wife who died about three years ago at the Parish Church of St James Westminster according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England about 13 years ago that he has only two Children now living by his said Wife Vizt. Margaret aged between 12 & 13 years or thereabout and Mary aged eleven Years a thereabouts both of whom were Born in Lawful Matrimony And this Examinant further saith that his said

Wifes Maiden Name was Margaret Poles , Saith that the informed this Examinant and which this Examinant believes is true that she never before her Intermarriage with this Examinant gained any settlement in her own Right that she was Born in the Parish of Wentworth in the County of York in which Parish her Father Lived and rented a Farm for several years at the yearly Rent of £40 and that her said Fathers Died in the Same Parish

Sworn this 23rd Day of July 1785 before  Sampn Wright Addington

Lewis Francis Dohen
