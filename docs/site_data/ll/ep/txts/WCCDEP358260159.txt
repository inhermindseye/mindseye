Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sophia Mitchell aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Second day of July 1787 she Intermarried with Joseph Mitchell her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint James at Clerkenwell by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Sophia aged about Six years That her said Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Smith a Plaisteres at Sloke Newington in the Parish of Saint John at Hackney in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement on Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this Ninth day of November 1796 R Ford Rusl Clarke Sophia Mitchell
