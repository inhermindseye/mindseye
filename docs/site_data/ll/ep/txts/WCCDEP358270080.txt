After Birth Bastardy Elizabeth & Thomas Robinson

St Clement & Danes 10th Septr. 1792

Middx and Westminster }  Vide Entry of Settlement page 290 Bastardy Examination Book No'd 1}  Child  Reputed Father (to wit) Elizabeth Robinson  Singlewoman aged 25 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that on the thirtieth day of July last at the Workhouse belonging to the parish of St Clement Danes and situate within the same she was delivered of Male Bastard Child which hath since been Named Thomas Robinson and a become Chargeable to the same parish That one Price who is or lately was in Service as a Gentleman's Valet and who who frequently used the French Arms in York Street by St James's square and the Star and Garter in Palace Yard  Westminster is the only real and true father of the said Child of which she was not so delivered as  aforesaid he and no one else having carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body in or about the Month of November la and at several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child thereon and that he and no other person is the real true father of the said Child

Sworn the 10th day of Septr. 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Robinsons mark

Witness B Newman
