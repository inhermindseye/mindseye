Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Mackey aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she Rented and lived in a house in Red Lyon Court Russell Court in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for about Eight years at the yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds That she hath quitted said House about Eight years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a yeat paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a subsequent Settlement That she this Examinant hath Six illegitimate Children living namely Sush . aged about 20 years & Elizth . aged about 14 years both provided for Maria aged about Eleven years John aged about 9 years both began in Red Lyon Court aforesaid Mary aged about 5 years and Stanly aged about 15 Months born in Sawyers Yard in the parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 14th. day of Janry. 1795  J: hlond Rob: Burd Gabriel

her Ann Mackey Mark

Witness Jas: Talboys
