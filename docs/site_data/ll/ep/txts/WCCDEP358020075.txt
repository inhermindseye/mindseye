Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Sladar aged about 28 years upon his Oath saith That about Six years ago he Intermarried with Elizabeth his present Wife at the Parish Church of South Moulton in the County of Devon that he has Three Children living by his present Wife vizt. Mary aged about Five years William aged about Three years and Elizabeth aged about Six Months That he was bound Apprentice to a Mr. John Parsons a Shoemaker in the Parish of South Molton in the County of Devon for Seven years by his Father William Sladar that he served the whole of the Seven years (under Indentures duly executed) to the said John Parsons and in the said Parish that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of May 1788 before  Wm. Achiner

John Slader
