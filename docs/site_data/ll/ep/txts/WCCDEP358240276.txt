St Clement Danes 18th 17th June 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Andrew Wardrobe London  (to wit) Ann Groves  singlewoman aged 34 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Jones at the sign of the Sun in Addle Hill in the parish of Saint Andrew Wardrobe in the City of London for eighteen Months or thereabouts at the annual Wages of £5 that she quitted that Service about fifteen years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 18th day of June 1793 before Us A Graham W: Blamire

Ann Groves 's Mark

Should have been (Witnessed by James Talboys ) the Mark being made in his presence
