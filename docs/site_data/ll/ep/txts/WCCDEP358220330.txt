Middlesex  to wit Elizabeth Plastow Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes Says she lived as a Covenanted Servant with Mrs Bowers a Mantua Maker in Craven Buildings  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the  to learn her Trade or Business for Two Years and her Aunt Christiana Leslie paid the Sum of Ten pounds for that purpose And further says that on the fifth day of February One Thousand Seven hundred and Sixty five she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Westin in the Parish of St. Mary Islington in the County of Middx which was on the 20th. day of February then next following Baptized by the Name of Christiana Pearson and Says that Thos. Tucker Father of the said Female Bastard Child died in July 1765 following and that this Examinant being very Poor is not able to Maintain the same & that her said Child hath gained no Settlemt. whatever otherwise than being born in the said Parish of St. Mary Islington

Sworn this 9th. day of March 1772 before me}

Elizth Plastow
