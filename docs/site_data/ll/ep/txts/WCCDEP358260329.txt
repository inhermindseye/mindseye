Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Rachael Mackenzie aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 26th. day of December 1793 she Intermarried with Kenneth Mackenzie her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bone by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Sarah aged about Four years That her said Husband served Seven years apprenticeship to one Mr. Beveridge in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Septr. 1798 before me } W Carter

her Rachael Mackenzie Mark
