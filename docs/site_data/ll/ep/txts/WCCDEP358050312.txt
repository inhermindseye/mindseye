Middx  Eliz Wacket aged about 22 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Bunting at the Golden Periwig opposite Arundel Street in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Four pounds for one year and upwards and quitted her sd. Service near two years ago and has not since rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being poor and unable to maintain ye same

when born And that one Richard Humphrys now a Servant to the sd. Mr. Bunting did beget the sd. bastard Child or Children on her body the said Richard Humphrys having had carnal knowledge of her body in the month of February last in the house of Mr Soley in Deane Street Soho and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the sd. Richd. Humphrys & as other person is the true and real father thereof.

Sworn Septr. 11th 1750 before H Fielding

The mark of Eliz Wacket
