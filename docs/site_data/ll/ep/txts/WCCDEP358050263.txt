Westmr. 

Martha Cheetham upon Oath saith she Inter married with her husband Sawney Cheetham  at Stoke Church in Hampshire upwds. of 5 Yrs. ago by whom she hath one Child (viz) Sarah aged upwds. of 3 Yrs. Says her sd. husbd. now absconds from her and she knows not where he is and that by Indenture dated the 26th. April 1733 the sd. Sawney Cheetham was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr Cobley a Butcher and that during the sd. term of Seven Years he Constantly lay at Mr Cobleys house in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes

in the Liberty of Westmr. and that her sd. husband has not since the Expiration of his sd. Apprentiship rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examint knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 4th. 1750 before}

Matha Cheethem
