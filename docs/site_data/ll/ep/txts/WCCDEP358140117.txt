Middx.  Elizth. Meredith aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Howell Meredith with whom she intermarried at Wilsdon Church abt. 27 Years since says her said husband Rented a house at Kilbourn in the Parish of Hampstead in the County of Middx . of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds lived therein abt. three Years and Quitted same abt. fifteen Years since which time he not Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn the  6th day of May 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Elizth: Meredith
