Westmr.  Mary Jones upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Patrick Jones who dyed last Midsx. by whom she hath 4 Children (viz) Jas . aged abt. 13 yrs. Eleanr . aged abt. 6 Yrs. Mary aged near 5 Yrs. & Edwd . aged near 2 Yrs. Says her sd. late husband and this Examint. are both Natives of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England & that her sd. late husband was born at Ballemadon near Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland and never gained a Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 26 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Jones
