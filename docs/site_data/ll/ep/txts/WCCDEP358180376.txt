Middx  Dorothy Blazedell  removed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds by Order Dated the 7th. Day of Septr. Instant upon her Oath saith she is the Wido. of Alxr. Blazedell  deceased and that the sd. Alexander Blazedell lived in & rented a house in Clare Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £24 for the Space of Fourteen Years & upwards & hath left the same about Twelve Years and never after (in his Life time) nor hath this Examinant since his Death ever rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 9th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Dorothy Blazedell
