Middx  Wm. Cross aged about 68 years upon Oath saith he intermarried with Alice his wife aged abt. 55 yrs. in the Parish Church of St. Philip at Bristol and that about 9 years ago he rented a house of Nineteen Pounds by the Year in Earles Court Cranborn Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. lived therein upwds. of 3 Years and has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten Pounds by the year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn Febry 8th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

William Cross
