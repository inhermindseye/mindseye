Middlesex ss}  Mary Shaw the widow of John Shaw deced Maketh oath That She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late husband deced or the Settlement of his Parents But this Depont. Saith that She has been informed by her late Father deced Jacob Wilkins That he Duly Served his apprenticeship with one Barrell a Waterman in Hangman Gains in the Precinct of St. Catherine in the County of Middlesex & by means thereof he gained a legal Settlement there as She this Depont. is advised And this Depont. doth not know not can Setforth Whether her Said Father ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. And this Depont. farther Saith that her late Mother deced never did any Act to this Deponts. Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right Subsequent to the Death of this Deponts. Said late Father And that She this Depont. never did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She might acquire or derive in Consequence of her Marriage

Sworn this 30 day of Sept 1771 R Pell

The Mark of Mary Shaw
