Middlesex ss}  Sarah Jeffery the Wife of Thomas Jeffery  of the parish of Saint Nicholas Deptford in the County of Kent Marshall Court officer and John Stocks Severally make oath And first the said Sarah Jeffery for herself Saith that about Thirty five Years ago he the said Thomas Jeffery  was Married to her this Deponent at the Fleet in the City of London And the said John Stocks for himself Saith that he hath been lately informed by Mr. Macky one of the present Overseers of the Poor of the said parish of St. Nicholas Deptford and which information he verily believes to be true that the said Thomas Jeffery who now resides in & Rents a house in Users Fields in the same Parish is charged to and pays the Poors Rates there for the said house And that by means thereof he now is & for a Considerable time past hath been legally Settled in the said parish of Saint Nicholas Deptford

Both Sworn this 6th day of April 1775 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Sarah Jeffery John Stocks
