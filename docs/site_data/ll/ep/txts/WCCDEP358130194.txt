Middx  Ann Bowers aged abt. Twenty six Years Wife of William Bowers who is gone from her upon Oath says She Intermarried with the said William Bowers at the Fleet London on the 20th. of August 1747. Old Style Says he was bound Apprentice by Indre dated 26th. Oct. 1737. for Seven Years to Joseph Springwell Citizen & Turner of London & served twelve Months the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Saml. Dobbins in the precinct of the Savoy in the County of Middx. Says She hath one Child by him Mary Aged abt. Sixteen Months & is poor & not able to maintain herself & Child Says her said Husband never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as She has been informed & believes

Sworn the 5th. Augst. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Ann Bowers
