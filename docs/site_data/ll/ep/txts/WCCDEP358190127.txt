Middx  Phillippa Vigers aged 42 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stickler in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Nine Years at the Yearly Wages of Forty Shillings diet & Lodging Quitted the Same in January last.

Sworn the 11th.. day of May 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Phillippa Vigers
