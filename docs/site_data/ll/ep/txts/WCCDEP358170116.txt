Middx  Edwd. Burton aged 52 Years or thereabts. upon his Oath saith that abt. 5 Years ago he lived in & rented a House in Kings Head Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of 4 Years & upwards & that he hath not since he left the sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 26th- of August 1741 . before}
