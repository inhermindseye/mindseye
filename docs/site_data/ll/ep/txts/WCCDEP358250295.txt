Saint Clement Danes .

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Louisa Batty aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married nor ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months, nor ever Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes or ever was bound an Apprentice That her father Joseph Batty Rented and lived in a house at Charing Cross in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Five years and upwards at the uearly Rent of Fifty Pounds and upwards That he quitted said house about Twenty five years since which time he never to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of July 1797 . before us  GS Goodenough J Hunter

Louisa Batty
