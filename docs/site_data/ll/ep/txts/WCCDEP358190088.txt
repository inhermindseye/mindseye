Middx.  Lucy Wheeler aged abt. 27 Years Upon Oath says she is the wife of James Wheeler a Soldier in his Majestys Service (now Quartered at Canterbury ) with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London on the 24th. day of March 1754 by whom she has two Children  James aged abt. 3 Years & Elizth: aged abt. 5 Weeks; says her said husband informed her & which information she verily believes to be true that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Price a Carpenter in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx. & never Rented Ten Pounds a year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn Jany. 20th. 1761 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Lucy Wheeler
