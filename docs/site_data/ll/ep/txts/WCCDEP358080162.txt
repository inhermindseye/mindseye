Middx & Westmr.}  James Gibson Aged about Twenty Two Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Lewis a Peruke maker in in Mark Lane and Served his said Master under that Indenture for the Space of Three Years and an half when his said Master broke and run away and Gave this Examinant his said Indentures up but Says he never was Assigned over to any other person or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of July 1777 before R. Butler

James Gibson
