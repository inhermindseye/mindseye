Middx  Mary Cattell  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that on the 2d Day of April 1739 she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at one Mr. Eagles in Kings Gate Street in the Parish of St. George in Bloomsbury in the County of Middx which hath since been baptized Mary & further on her Oath saith that on the 27th Day of November last she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at one Mr. Briggs in White Lyon Court in Dutchy Lane in the Precinct of the Savoy in the Sd. County of Middx which hath since been Baptized Frances & is Poor & nott able to maintain them

Sworne the 9th: Augt. 1742 before us} J Bromfield Thos. Lane

The Mark of Mary Cattell
