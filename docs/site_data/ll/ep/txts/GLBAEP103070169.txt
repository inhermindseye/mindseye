Middx ss  Frances Broome  wife of Richard Broome (who is lately gone away from her to places Unknown) Maketh oath that she this Deponent hath been informed and believes that her said husband was bound apprentice to Peter Carew Carpenter in the parish of St. Mary Aldermary in ye City of London for the Term of Seven Years and served the greatest part of his time out and then went for a soldier And this Depont. saith that her said husband did not afterwards gain any other settlement to the knowledge information or belief of this Depont.

Sworn this 8 day of September 1756 before us  Richd Ricards  Walter Berry

The Mark of Frances Broome
