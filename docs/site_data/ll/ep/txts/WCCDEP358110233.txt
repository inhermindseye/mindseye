Middx  Eliz: Peers upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Thos. Peers by whom she has one Child Chas . aged abt. Ten Months and that she has been Informed & verily believes her sd husband Served his Apprentisp with Mr Mattocks a Taylor in the Parish of St. Peters at Westchester in the County of Chester & never rented ten Pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn April 13th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Peers
