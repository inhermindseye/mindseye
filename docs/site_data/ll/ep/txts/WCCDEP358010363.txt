Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to Wit)  Lucy Bullock Aged 30 years or thereabouts on the behalf of her sister Ann Bigg Aged 42 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath Saith that her said sister Ann Bigg lived as a hired yearly servant with one Mr. Thornton of Southampton Buildings in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx for 3 years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six pounds Six shillings that she hath quitted said service about 9 months, Since which time she the said Ann Bigg has been ill and unable to Maintain herself And has never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging or done any Act since living as a hired yearly servant with Mr. Thornton whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement to be the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief

Sworn this 8th. Octr. 1790 before Chas. Sheppard J Barnfather

her Lucy Bullock Mark
