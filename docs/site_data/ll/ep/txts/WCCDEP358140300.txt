Middx.  Sarah Brooks aged upwards of 71 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the wife of Thos. Brooks (if living) with whom she intermarried at the fleet London about forty five Years ago; says her said husband informed her & which Information She verily believes to be true that he served two Years the first part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Stonystreet a farrier in Red Cross Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx. and then this Examinants husband and Mr. Stonystreet parted by Consent; says her sd. husband afterwards inlisted for a Soldier and never was assigned over to any Person nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year of lived a hired yearly Servt. for one Year to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 10th. 1759 . before}

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Wilkinson Ovr. J: P:
