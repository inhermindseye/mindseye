Westmr.  Hannah Corcut aged about 60 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Peele at the Feathers in St. Clements Lane in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds; quitted said Service about fourteen Years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 27th, day of February 1767 , before} 

The Mark of Hannah Corcut
