Middlesex & Westmr.}  Mary Phillips Aged about Twenty two Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she was born in the Parish of Pash in the County of Worcester and never gained any other Subsequent Settlement And further says that on the 14th. day of May last to the best of her knowledge she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of One Mr. Paine a Staymaker in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized by the Name of Mary Ann and says that one Morrison Hodges a Calico Printer who went at Bromley Hall in the County of Middlesex is there and true Father thereof he having had Carval knowledge of her Body the beginning of August last past but the Parliament time she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Morrison Hodges and an other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof. Mary Phillips

Sworn this 30th. day of June 1773 before me  Tho. Kynaston
