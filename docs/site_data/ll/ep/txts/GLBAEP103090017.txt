Middlesex ss  Susannah Johnson the widow of Thomas Johnson deced Maketh oath that divers Years Ago her said late husband Rented a house of Ten Pounds by the year Rent & upwards in King Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx and lived in the same for about the Space of Seven Years And by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there as she this Deponent is advised And this Depont Saith that her said husband did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor serve any Parish offices nor Pay any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement Nor hath this Deponent since her said husbands death done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this day of December 1765 before}
