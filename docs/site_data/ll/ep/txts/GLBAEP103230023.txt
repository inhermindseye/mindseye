Middlesex ss}  Sarah Kitchen  Singlewoman maketh oath that about Six Years ago she was hired and lived as a hired servant with Mrs Mary Bowler in Walworth in the Parish of st Mary Newington in the County of Surrey  Tambour worker at Certain Wages and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one year and half That She has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 3. day of March1787 before me  R Davies  R Smith

The Mark of Sarah Kitchen
