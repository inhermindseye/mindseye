Westmr. to wit Thos. Seneff Aged about Sixty Two Years on his Oath Says he Married to his late Wife Mary Seneff at the Fleet  London about Thirty Years and who has been dead about Three Month ago Says he has one Son by his said late Wife who is now an Apprentice to a Waterman in the Parish of Saint Katherine by the Tower Says that about Twenty Two Years ago he Rented a House in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in Thes Liberty of Westmr . for half a Year or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the Same hath quitted the said House upwards of Twenty one Years ago and hath not since be quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any or Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 17th. day of June 1774 before W Kelyngs

Thomas Seneff
