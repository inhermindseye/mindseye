Middx  Cath: Ash upon Oath saith. that on the 26th. March 1722 she intermarried with her husband Dove Ash at the Parish Church of St. Ann Soho in the Liberty of Westmr . that her said husband has for a considerable time past and still does refuse to live with or provide for her and this Examinant being troubled with the Rheumatism is at present incapable of maintaining herself Says the sd. Dove Ash about 15 Years ago rented a house of £11 by the Year in Ogden Court in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lived therein abt. 4 Yrs. and has quitted it about 15 Years Says the sd. Dove Ash has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a or or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 8th. 1750 before}

afterwards she took a house of £11. a Year in Bear Alley Fleet market of a Hop merchant in Seething Lane her husband did not live with her then now & then came home to her she remembers he lay in that house she lived in it (as she thinks) above Six weeks never pd. any rent his abt 12 Yrs. ago
