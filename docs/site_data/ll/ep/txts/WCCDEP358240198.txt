St Clement Danes 26th January 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St C D  Children provided for  (to wit) Elizabeth May  Widow aged sixty years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband William May at the parish Church of St Sepulchre without Newgate London about thirty six years ago (by Banns) that since their said Marriage her said Husband Rented and Resided in an House in Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for about three years at the Annual Rent of £20 that they quitted that House about 29 or 30 years ago and never after Rented any House or Apartmt. of £10 a year or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement saith that her Husband died about eight years ago & since this Examinant hath not been Married nor acquired any legal Settlement in her own Right that she hath two Children by her said Husband both provided for and that in at present distressed & unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn the 26th day of January 1793 before Me } D Walker

Elizth: May 's Mark

Garrett Witness to the Mark
