Middlesex ss}  Mary Fear the widow of Samuel Fear deced maketh oath That She hath been informed by her said late husband & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound Apprentice to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Robert Rouse in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry  Fisher- man & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent hath a Child named Hannah Newport  aged about eight months which is a Bastard & was born in Greenwood Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & that one Thomas Newport of the Same Parish Labourer is the Father of the said Child.

Sworn this 3d day of October 1774 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

The Mark of Mary Fear
