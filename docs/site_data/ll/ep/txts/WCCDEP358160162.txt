Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Jackson the Wife of Thos Jackson Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 8th. Day of Augt. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. about 10 Years ago her sd. Husband lived in & rented a House in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West  London for the space of 4 years or thereabouts of the yearly Rent of £65, And this Examint. further saith yt. her said Husband Thos. Jackson hath not since he left the sd. House Rented any other House paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that he is now a Marine in his Majesty's Service And she haveing one Child (to wit) Jno. aged 7 Years & upwards & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Child

Sworn this Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous J Poulson

Elizt. Ja Elizabeth Jacson
