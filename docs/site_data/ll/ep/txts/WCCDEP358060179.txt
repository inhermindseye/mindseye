Westmr.  Ann Howel aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr Wells in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the yearly wages of £6 for Six Years and upwards and quitted her sd. Service about ten Years ago & has not Since rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 4th. Octr. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Howell
