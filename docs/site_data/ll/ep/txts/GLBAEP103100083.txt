Middlesex ss  William Merricks maketh oath That divers Years ago he was duly bound an Apprentice and Served the whole of his apprenticeship with John Walker in the Parish of St. Martin in the Town of Birmingham in the County of Warwick  White Smith And by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Deponent Saith that to his knowledge or belief he hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Ans that he hath Wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully Married about Twenty Six Years ago

Sworn this 13th: day of February 1769 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of William Merricks
