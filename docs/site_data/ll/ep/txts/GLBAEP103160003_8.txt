Middlesex ss}  Christian Callendar maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with Robert Whitehead in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd. County Cook at Certain Yearly Wages & Continued in such service for the Space of one Whole Year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor hath she ever been married or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31. day of January 1776 before Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Christian Callendar
