St. Clement Danes 6th March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Saffron Hill Liberty but turned out to be no Settlemt.  to wit John Langley aged 28 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife Ann Langley at the Christ Church in the County of Surry on or about the fourth day of June 1786 (by Banns) that previous thereto and (while he was single he served his Apprenticeship under Indentures (duly executed) with Mr Millen Pewterer in little Great Saffron Hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill or Hatton Garden in the & Ely Rents parish of St Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex and since the expiration of his Apprenticeship he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And saith that he hath 3 Children by his said Wife Namely Ann aged 5 years & upwards Elizabeth aged 2 years & upwds and John aged Eight Months & upwds

Sworn the 6th day of March 1793 before Us us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

John Langley

This family taken back he having lodged during the last 2 Months of his Apprenticeship in an parish they were accordingly taken last and the Order Abandoned but having obtained as support were not Removed to St. Sepulchre
