Middx  Cathne. Quint aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath says she never was Married Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant says her father Wm. Quint Rented a house in Checquer Alley in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Seven Pounds lived therein abt. one Year & pd. the Poors Rate & hath quitted same abt. Twenty six Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes as her said father hath informed her & as she Verily believes & further upon her Voluntary Oath says that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to Maintain the same when born says that Abraham Burdroe a Cabinet maker in Bow  Russell Street Bloomsbury had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body in the latter End of the Month of June or in the beginning of the Month of July 1757. & at one other time since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant and

and that the said Abraham Burdroe & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn this 19th. Janry. 1758 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cath: Quint
