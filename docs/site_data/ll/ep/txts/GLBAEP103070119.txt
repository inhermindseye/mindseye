Middx ss  Jane Mc. Clelan Widow of William Mc. Clelan deced Maketh oath yt. about Ten Year ago her said husband Rented a house of Ten pounds a Year in Barnes Alley in the parish of St. Pauls Deptford in the County of Kent and paid all Parish Taxes and that She never Since gained any other Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge.

Sworn this 5th day of Octr 1754 before  Richd: Ricards  Walter. Berry

Jean Mc Clelan
