Middx  The Examination of Elizth Murphey  Singlewoman aged abt. 25 Years lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 20th. Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. She lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Barneat Victualler in Arundle Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt- Danes for the Space of one Yr. & upwards at ye. yearly Wages of 4 Pounds & hath been come away from the Same abt. 3 Months-& this Examinant further saith that she hath not since that times rented Ten Pounds a Yr pd. parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt.  or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & that she is now Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. Murphey
