Westmr.  Rosamond Preston  Wife of Joseph Preston (gone from her)upon Oath saith she intermarried with her Husband Joseph Preston in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes on the 15th. April 1750 and that her sd. husband informed her and this Examinant verily believes he Served his Apprentice with a Shoemaker in the Parish of St. Ann within Aldersgate London and never Rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 16th. 1751 before M Fielding

Rosamond Preston
