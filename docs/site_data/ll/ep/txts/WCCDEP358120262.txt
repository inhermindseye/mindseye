Middx  Elizth. Hand Aged abt. Twenty four Years wife of Richd. Hand upon Oath Says she intermarryed with the sd. Richd. Hand at the Fleet London on the first day of Janry 1750 Says her sd. Husband absconds from her & that she doth not know where he is, Says he informed her he served Seven Years Apprentiship at pontacks in Abchurch Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Abchurch London & hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship abt. five Years Says he never Rented Ten pounds a year or was a Yearly Servt for one Year that she hath one Child by him named Ann aged abt three Years & is poor & not able to maintain herself and Child

Sworn the 15th. day of Janry 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Elizth. Hand
