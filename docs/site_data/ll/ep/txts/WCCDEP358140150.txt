Middx.  Philip Thomas aged abt. 78 Yrs. upon Oath says he Intermarried with Rachell his Wife abt. 12 Years ago at the Fleet London says he Rented a house in Exeter Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx . of the Yearly Rent of £30 or thereabouts Lived therein abt. five Years and hath Quitted same abt. Thirty Years; Since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Parish or place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 16th. day of Augt. 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

Phillip Thomas
