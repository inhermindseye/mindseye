Middx & Westmr.}  Joseph Totham aged about fourteen Years upon his Oath Says he was bound Apprentice by the Church Wardens and overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex by Indenture Dated the Twentieth Day of Sepr. One Thousand Seven hundred and Sixty Six untill he shod. Attain his Age of Twenty four Years to Charles Todd of the Parish of Saint Lawrence Jewry London Perukemaker as by the said Indentures may Appear Says he Served Ten Months part of his said Apprenticeship with the said Charles Todd that his said Master Using this Examinant very ill by beating Striking and Kicking him was Obliged to leave his said Master Says he never was Assigned over to any Person nor ever has done any thing whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th: Day of Janry 1769 Before me} Thos: Lane J Fielding

his Joseph Totham Mark Witness Petr Sidebotham
