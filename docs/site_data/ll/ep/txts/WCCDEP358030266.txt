Middx  Thomas Pearce aged abt. 72 Years removed from the Parish of Westhaddon in the County of Northton by Order bearing Date the 16th. Day of Octr last upon his Oath saith he intermarryed with his late Wife Prudence Pearce on or about the 15th. Day of July 1712 in the parish Church of St. Andrew Undershaft London Says before his said Intermarryage he lived a Yearly Servant with Sr. Jno. Cheshire  Knt . who lived in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Five Years or thereabout at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds and after his said Intermarriage continued to live in the same Service under the same hiring for Three Years or thereabouts Says Sr. Jno. Cheshires Coach house and Stables were in Salisbury Court in Fleet Street in the parish of St. Bridgetvulg St. Brides London where this Examinant always lay excepting when Sr. John was upon the Circuit or at his Country house Says he Quitted Sr. Johns Service between Easter Whitsuntide about 30 Years since to his

best Remembrance Says he lay the two last Months of his said Service at the said Stable in Salisbury Court in the sd. Parish of St. Bridgetvulg. Brides London

Sworn Novr. 24. 1746 before  Tho: Burdus

Tho. Pearce
