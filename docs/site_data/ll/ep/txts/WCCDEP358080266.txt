Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Morrison aged about Twenty years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Levett in Norfork Street in the Strand upwards of One year and an half at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two years ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent And further on her Oath Says she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Dixon in Worcester Street Old Gravel Lane in the Parish of St. George in the East Radcliff Highway in the County of Middlesex on the 8th. day of April last which has since been baptized Elizabeth and is now Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes Says that one William Dicker a Nursery Man who lately lived at Kensington and who is now partners with one Mr. Young a Nursery man at Bath is the Father thereof he having having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Months of June or July last But the particular Month or Days she cannot recolect and at sevl. Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the Wm. Dicker & no other person did be get the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 14th. day of July 1778 .. before M Addington

her Elizth. Morrison Mark
