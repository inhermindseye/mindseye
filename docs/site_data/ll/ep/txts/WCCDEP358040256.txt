Middx 

Mary Carroll Wife of John Carroll upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. John Carroll in the Parish Church of Mallow in the County of Cock in the Kingdom of Ireland on or a best the 24th. Novr. 1726 and that her sd. husband lived in and Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £30 in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex for Six months or thereabouts and hath last same upwds. of two Years since which the sd. Jno. Carroll has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief Says she has two Childn. by her sd. husband viz: Jno: aged abt. 4 Yrs. & Wm. aged near 2 Yrs. and that her sd. husband now absconded from her & she knows not where he is

Sworn Septr. 14th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Carroll
