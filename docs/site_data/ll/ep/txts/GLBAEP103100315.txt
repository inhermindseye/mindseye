Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Mills of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott & Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County of Middlesex this Tenth Day of october 1770 .

Who on her oath Saith that She is now with Child and that the Child or Children Where with She now goeth And is Pregnant is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate And that Thomas Smith of Camomile Street in the City of London  Coach Carver is the Father of the Said Child or Children

Sworn this Fourth Day of october 1770 before  Chris: Scott

Sarah Mills
