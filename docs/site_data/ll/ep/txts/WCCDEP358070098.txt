Westmr. to wit William Webb of Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes  Cooper on behalf of William Harris an Infant Son of William Harris late of Milford Lane in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes on his Oath Says that he hath often heard the said William Harris the Father Say that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one John Randall a Lighterman in the Parish of Saint George in the East in the County of Middlesex and as Appears by his Indentures of Apprenticeship now Produced to his Examinant and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rent House paid Taxes or Otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of May 1773 before R Butler W Kelyngs

William Webb
