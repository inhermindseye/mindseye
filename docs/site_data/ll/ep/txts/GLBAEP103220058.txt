Middlesex ss}  Susannah Worrall a Singlewoman maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents deced or either of them That She this Deponent was born in the Parish of Saint Mary Rotherhith in the County of Surry and there baptized That She never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year in any one place nor was she ever Married nor ever did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 21. day of July 1786 before J. Hawley Robt. Smith

The Mark of Susannah Worrall
