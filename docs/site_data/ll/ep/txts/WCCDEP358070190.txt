Westmr. to with Mary Croysdill Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. John Scotta Land Waiter in Prescot Street Goodmans Fields in the Parish of Saint Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of One Year Ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 28th. day of May 1774 before R Butler W Kilyngs

Mary Croysdill
