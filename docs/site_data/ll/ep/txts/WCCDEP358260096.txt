Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizabeth Henly an Infant aged about Seven years Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said Elizabeth Henly was born at the house of a Mr. Horn, Grocer in Whitecross Street in the Parish of Saint Lu in the County of Middlesex on or about the 20th. day of October 1788

Sworn this 14th. day of Octr. 1795 before us E Read N Bond

Jas Talboys
