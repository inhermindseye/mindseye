Middx.  Mary Moore aged upwds. of 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and she further Voluntarily maketh Oath that she was Delivered of a Still born male Child on Tuesday the 21st. Inst. at the house of Mrs. Davison one of the Nurses of the parish of St Clement Danes Middx Situate in old Boswell Court in the sd. Parish and that the Expence of her lying in is born by the Inhabitants of she sd. parish she being poor & unable to pay the same and that John Burnet of Newtoners Lane did beget she sd. bastard Child on her body the sd. John Burnet having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant   in the Month of December last & at sevt. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of she sd. male bastard Child of which she was delivered as afsd. & that the sd. John Burnet & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Augt. 24th 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Moore
