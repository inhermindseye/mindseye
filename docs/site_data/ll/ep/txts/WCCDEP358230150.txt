Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Page Aged about 24 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was married That on or about the 11th day of February 1794 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of a M Britten in Charles Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster which Child hath since been Christened by the Name of Ann

Sworn this 27th day of August 1800 before us} Ws Woodenough

her Sarah Page Mark

Witness B Newman
