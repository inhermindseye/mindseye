Middx  Eliz: Mecully upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband James Mecully in the parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields about Eleven Years age and that the sd. James Mecully Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Robt Tranter a Tayler in Star Court Butcher Row and in Witch Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn May 11th. 1751 before} Elizabeth Mecully
