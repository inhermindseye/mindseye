Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Robert Reed aged about 35 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the Second day of December 1788 he Intermarried with Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields James Westminster by whom he hath Three Children now living Vizt. George aged upwards of Six years Robert aged upwards of Four years and Ann aged about Two years & an half That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Hemlock Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds that he hath quitted said house about Two years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Febry. 1798 before me } W Carter

Robert Reed
