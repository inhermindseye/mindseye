Middx  The Examination of Jas. Nedrow on the Behalfe of Ann Prior an Infant aged abt. 9 Years lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 21st Day of March 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that abt. Seven Years ago Jas. Prior who is now in the Work House belonging to the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the sd. County of Middx & Father to the sd. Infant Ann Prior rented & lived in a House in the Cole Yard in the sd. Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds of the Yearly Rent of ten Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of one Year & upwards  And this Examinant further saith that the sd. Jas Prior hath not since he left the sd. House rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Places this Exaiant hath been Informed & which he beleives

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

James Nedrow
