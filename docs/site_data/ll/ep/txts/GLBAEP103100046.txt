Middlesex ss}  Sarah Townsend  single woman maketh Oath That divers Years ago She was duly bound apprentice by Indre to william Mekins of the Parish of Saint Vedast alias Foster in the City of London Carman & lived with him in the Same Parish under the Said Indre for the Space of Ten Months & by means thereof as she is Advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that she has not since lived as an Apprentice nor as a hired servant for the space of a year Nor was ever Married nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24 day of October 1768 before R Pell Rt. Darling

Sarah Townsend
