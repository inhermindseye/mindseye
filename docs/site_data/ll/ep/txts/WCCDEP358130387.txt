Middx  Ralph Barker aged abt. 74 yrs. upon Oath says that upwards of nine years ago he Rented a house in St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the yrly. Rent of Sixteen Pounds & lived therein abt. five years says he hath not since Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant

Sworn the 26th of July 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

Rlalv Brker
