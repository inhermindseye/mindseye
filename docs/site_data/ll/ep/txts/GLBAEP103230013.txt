Middlesex ss  Lewis Williams Maketh oath that about Four years & half ago he this Depont hired in and rented a house in Clerkenwell Close in the Parish of Saint James at Clerkenwell in the Said County at the yearly Rent of Twenty two Pounds & during Such his residence therein was charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Servd any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5. day of February 1787 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Lewis Williams
