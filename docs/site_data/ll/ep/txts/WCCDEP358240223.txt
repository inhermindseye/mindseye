St Clement Danes 6th March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Elizabeth Jefferson aged 48 years or thereabouts singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she was hired a yearly Servant with Mrs. Spittey in Fetter Lane London about 6 years ago that she lived with her said Mistress there under such hiring for fourteen Months and then removed who with her to Clements Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes at No. 11 there, and lived with her said Mistress in Said Clements Lane Lane for two Months under the same hiring and since she quitted that service hath not obtained Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 6th day of March 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Eliz Jeffrson
