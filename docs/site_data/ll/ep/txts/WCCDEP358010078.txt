Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Canden aged about 44 years upon her oath saith that about 16 years ago she Intermarried with John Canden her late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes that he has been dead about 4 years that she hath 2 Children living by her said late Husband vizt. Sarah aged about 15 years provided for and Wm. aged about 6 years unprovided for that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Chair Court Ship yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 4 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £8..8..0 that he paid all Taxes due for the same that he had quitted said House upwards of 18 Months before his death after which he done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath this Ext. since her said late Husbands Death done any Act whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of March 1787  Edward Hodsoll

her Sarah Canden Mark
