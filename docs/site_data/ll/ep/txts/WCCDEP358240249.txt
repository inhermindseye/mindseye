(Militia)

St Clement Danes

Middlesex and & W Westminster }  (to wit) Mary Hare Wife of John Hare and aged 42 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that on or about the first day of March in the Year of our Lord 1782 she this Examint being then a Widow and having no Husband living was Married to John Hare who as she was informed and believes (having no Reason to think to the contrary) was at that time a single man and had no Wife And this Deponent further saith upon her Oath that her said Husband John Hare was sworn and enrolled to serve as a Substitute in the Westminster Regiment of Middlesex Militia for Henry Peter of the parish of Saint George in the Liberty of Westminster the 26th day of March 1791 as she has been informed and believes And that he is now serving as such Substitute in the said Regiment which was called embodied called out into actual Service on or about the 28th day of January last and hath been ordered to March And Lastly that she this Examinant hath ever since the said 28th day of January resided and still dwells at the House of Messrs. Colvin & Lowndes Dealers in Liquor near Temple Bar in the Strand in the said parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex And that She hath no Children by her said Husband John Hare And that she has ever since been & still distressed and unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn the 20th. day of April 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

M Hare
