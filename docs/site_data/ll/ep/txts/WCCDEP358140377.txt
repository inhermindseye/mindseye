Middx.  Charles Kemmis aged abt. 70. Years Upon Oath says He Served 3 years the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. John Symms a Joyner in Water Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year.

Sworn this 24th. of May 1760 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Charls Kems
