Middlesex ss  Joseph Crump maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound by Indres to & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with Richard Bowles a Cooper in Gainsford Street in the Parish of Saint John Southwarke in the County of Surry That he has not Since his Said Apprenticeship rented a house or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this. 20. day of Janry 1779 before  Jasper Clarkey P. Rowlands

Joseph Crump
