Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Perry aged about 38 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Perry who has been dead upwards of five Years, says her said husband Rented a house in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr of the Yearly Rent of fifteen Pounds for five Years or thereabouts and after he quitted same never Rented House or paid Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief, says she has not since her said husbands death Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born, says that John Bell a Pensioner in Greenwich hospital had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime about the Middle of April last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said John Bell and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 9th. Decr. 1766 . before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Perry
