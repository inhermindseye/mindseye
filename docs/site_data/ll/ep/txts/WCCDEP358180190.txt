Middx  John Lewis aged upwards of 36 Years upon his Oath saith that he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. John Francis  Fishmonger in Vear Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlx at the Yearly Wages of £10 & 12s. for the Space of 2 Years & hath left his sd. Service about Seven Years since which time he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 8th. May 1744 before} Wm: Jones

John Lewis
