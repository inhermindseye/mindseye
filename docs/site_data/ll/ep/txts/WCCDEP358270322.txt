Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Leech aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Emblyn in Fountain Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Seven years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Timothy Hall who lives at No. 7 Ely Mews is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Timothy Hall is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of Septr. 1797 before me  G:S: Goodenough

Ann Leech

Father taken the 18th. Septr. 1797 Paid £20. Genl. Release Exted

Child born in the house 17th. Janry. 1798 named Ann Leech

Sent to Enfield the 9th. May 1798 Died at Do. the 20th. Septr. 1798
