Middx & Westmr-}  Jane Honnor aged about 26 Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Honnor who hath Deserted her with whom she Intermarried at the Parish of Saint Mary le Bone about Eleven Years ago and by whom she hath two Children now living namely Sarah aged about Ten Years and Jane aged about Six Says her said late husband Rented and lived in a House in Leather Lane the in the Parish of St- Andrew Holborn in the County of Middx for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Rent of fifteen pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said house upwards of four Year ago since which time her said husband never gained any subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house or paying Taxes

Sworn this 28th. Day of Novr- 1777 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Jena Honnor
