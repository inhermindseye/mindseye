After Birth Elizabeth Herbert and Child

St Clement Danes 22d Septr. 17

Middx and Westminster }  Woman settled at now Windsor  Child's birth in the House 

Reputed Father (to wit) Elizabeth Herbert  singlewoman aged twenty four year or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Rimbery at the three Tuns in Windsor in the parish of Windsor in the County of Berk for three years that she quitted that Service abt years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief and this Deponent on her Oath further saith that on the 21 day of September instant  at the Workhouse of and belonging to the said parish and situate within the same she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which is become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Dane and saith that one Edward Allen a Groom in the Service of a Gentleman a Grosvenor Square and who frequents the Coach and House Public House in Dover Street  in the in the said County of Middlesex and true father of the said Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid he having Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in or about December last and at several se several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the same thereon and th he no others one else is the only real & true Father thereof

Sworn the 22d. Septr 1792 before me} R Butler

Her Elizabeth Herbert mark

(Child Dead)

N this Child died in the House  4th Janry. 1793 Taken and paid £20 Rele given
