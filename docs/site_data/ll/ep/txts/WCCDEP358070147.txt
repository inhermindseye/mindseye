Westmr. to wit Catherine Branden Aged about Forty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Ann Ellis in Old Burlington Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th. day of Novr. 1773 before} J: Treslove

her Catherine Branden  Mark
