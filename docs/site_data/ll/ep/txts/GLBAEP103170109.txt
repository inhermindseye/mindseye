Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Sidnall the widow of Philip Sidnall deced maketh oath That her Said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that about Thirty two years ago & when a Singleman he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with his late Father John Sidnall  Brece her maker deced in upper Easts with field in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the sd County at Certain Wages and Continued in Such Service for about the Space of two years & thereby gained a legal Settlement there & then this Deponts. sd. husband left Such Service & was there upon lawfully married to this Depont. That this Deponts. sd husband after Such his Servitude as afore sd. did not rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any act to gain anothr Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath two Children namely Nathanainel aged about nine years & Seven Months and Sarah Aged about Seven years the lawful Issue of this Depont by her husband San And this Deponent further Saith that her Sometime before She was married to her sd husband She worked for his Said Father at his Dwelling house before referred to & that She has seen her Said husbands Father pay the Land Tax for the said house he lived in to the Collectors of the Same for the Said parish

Sworn this 28 day of November 1777 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Elizabeth Sidnall
