Middx  Eliz Eastgate  wife of John Eastgate a Soldier in Brigadior Richbells Company of Foot upon Oath Says she intermarried with the sd. John Eastgate at the Fleet London on the 27th. Day of Octr. 1743 Says her sd. Husband Served 3 Years the last part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Chas. Pearce of Arundell Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx & never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief Says she hath one Child viz: Mary aged about 18 Months

Sworn June 2d. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Eliz Eastgate
