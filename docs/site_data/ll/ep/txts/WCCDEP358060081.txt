Westmr.  Eliz: Topping aged about 18 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Cornwall in Church Row in the Parish of Isleworth in the County of Middlx at the Yearly Wages of fifty Shillings for one Year and upwards and hath Quitted her sd. Service about 18 months and has not since rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 14th. 1750 before  Tho Lane

The mark of Eliz. Topping
