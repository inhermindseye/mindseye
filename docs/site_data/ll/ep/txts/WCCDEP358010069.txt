Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Bridgett Brannon aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that She never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived with one John Fish in Arrundell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months & upwards at the yearly wages of £7 hath quitted her said service about 20 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. of Febry. 1787 R Butler

her Bridgett Brannon Mark
