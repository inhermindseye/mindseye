Middx  David Bevan upon his Oath saith that on the 18th. Day of July 1722 he hired himself to one Mr. Adam Bromley of Hollis Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx Taylor & lived with him there for the Space of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds Meat Drink Washing and Lodging Says she never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served a Parochiall Office lived with any other Person as a Yearly Servant or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 4th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones . David Beavan
