Westmr. to wit Elizabeth White otherwise Cotterell on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes or lived a lived Yearly servant for one Year Says she was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Restel or Cabinet Maker the in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields who afterwards removed to the Great Abm Westmr . in the Parish of St. Margt. Westmr . where she Continued to live with hin upwards of one Year and then her sd. Master was Arrested & went to Prison since which time she hath never gained any Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Volentary Oath Says that on the First day of Decr. last past she was delivered of a Make Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Gray in Cumberland Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westimr . which is at Present unahristred Says that one Captain Turner late belonging to the Guards and who now Uses the Rose Tavern in Bridges Street had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at Different times for Twelve Months last Past but Particularly in February and March last at one of Which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Captain Turner and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and True Father thereof

Sworn this 4th. day of January 1774  Tho Keynaston

her Elizth White  otherwise Mark Cotterell
