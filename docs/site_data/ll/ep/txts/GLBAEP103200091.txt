Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Nold formerly Taylor maketh oath that about nine Years ago She was awfully Married in the Parish Church of St George in the Sd County to one John Frederick Nold a Perukemaker who as this Depont is induced to believe was born Somewhere in or about London but the Particular Place where She Can't Set forth That he left this Depont between Seven & eight Years ago & She has not Since Seen or heard from him & don't know nor Can Set forth Whether he is now living or dead & this Depont Can give no othr Account respecting her sd husbands Settlement That about Seven Years ago as she now best remembers She Saw her Sd husbands Fathr Frederick Nold in Rupert Street in the Sd County Whose Trade was that of a Taylor & was by Birth a German as she has been informed & believes

Sworn this 26th day of Janry 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Nold
