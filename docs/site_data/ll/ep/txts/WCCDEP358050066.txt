Middx  Sarah Andrews aged about 24 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she Lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Thomas Hewit a Haller in Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the west London at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for one Year and upwards and quitted her said Service about Six weeks ago And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she, was delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the 28th. Day of June last in the house of Mr. Page in Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which is yet unbaptized and is become Chargeable to the said parish and that Mr John Kents an Ensign in General Reads Regiment is the reputed father; the said John Neale having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant sevl. times in the latter End of September or beginning of October last in Mr Hewits house at one of which times he got her with Child of the male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that he the said John Neale and no other Person is the true and, real father thereof

Sworn July 8th. 1749 before} Tho Lane . H Frilding

Sarah Andrews
