Middx  Ann Plowright aged upwds. of 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Delany in Hollywell Strt. in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £4 quitted her sd Service about two Years ago & has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn 26th Jany. 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Plowright
