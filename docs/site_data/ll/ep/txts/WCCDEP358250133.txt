Saint Clement Danes

Settlemt. Pish of Walthamstow to wit Martha King aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes lived as a Hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born at Woodford in the Parish of Walthamstow in the County of Essex And this Deponent further saith that her Father William King belongs to the said Parish of Walthamstow by living upwards of Two years with one Mr. Bacon at Woodford at the yearly Wages of Twenty Guineas

Sworn this 29th. day of October 1794  J: Whud J Mathew

her Martha King mark

Witness Jas. Talboys
