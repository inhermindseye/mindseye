Middlesex ss}  Sarah Darby  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant  by the year by & with one Elizabeth Hart a Laundress in Theobalds Court in the parish of Saint Martin in the Fields Westminster in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Two years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year else where nor was She never Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 25. day of November 1777 before P. Rowlands Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Sarah Darby
