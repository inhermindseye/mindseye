Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Margaret Woolby of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx Maketh Oath and Saith That she was married John Woolby her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew in Plymouth in the County of Devon about Seven Years ago, that she has two Children by her said Husband Vizt. Elizabeth aged about three Years who is now at Nurse paid for by the said John Woolby under the Direction of the Parish he having given a Bond with Securities for maintaining her and Thomas aged Seven Weeks or thereabouts And this Examinant further saith that she hath Made Application to her said Husband for the Maintenance of her said Child Thomas but that he hath referred to maintain it and this Examinant is poor and unable to maintain her said Child and that she hath therefore applied to & received Relief from the Overseers of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 7th. day of June 1790 R Butler

Marge Woolby
