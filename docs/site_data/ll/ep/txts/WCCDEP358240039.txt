St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster }  (St. CD)  (to wit) John Bond Aged 42 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he intermarried with Rebecca Mayne at the parish Church of St Martin in the Fields about Sixteen Years ago (by Banns) that he hath one Child Christened John born of the body of his said late Wife in legal Matrimony Aged Six years or thereabouts, that his said Wife died about 2 years & an half since that he this Deponent was bound by Indentures duly elted an Apprentice to Mr. John Edwards Lawman  Drury Lane & duly. served the whole Term of Seven years there, under such Indentures that & Slept & Lodged the whole time (except when he had leave of Absence) in his said Masters House And that since he hath not by any way acquired any legal settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 2d August 1791 before me} R Butler

John Bond
