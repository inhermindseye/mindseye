St Clement Danes 2n 7th June 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Presten in Radnor shire  (to wit) Thomas Wankling  singleman aged ab twenty years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he never served an Apprenticeship or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in his own Right, that he is informed and verily believes he is the legitimate Son of John and Mary Wankling that his Said father Rents a House with an Orchard & other premises in the parish of Presgen in the County of Radnor and South Wales of the yearly Rent of 5 Guineas or thereabouts, that he pays the poor and other Rates for the same that he resides in the said House and hath resided in the same and paid Rented and paid the Poor and other Rates for the same premises for above six year last past

Sworn the 7th.. day of June 1793 before me} D Walker

Thomas Wankling
