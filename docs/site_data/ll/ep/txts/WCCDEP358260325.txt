Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Williams aged about 53 years upon her Oath saith that about 22 years ago she Intermarried with John Williams her present Husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. John aged about 13 years That her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards as the yearly Rent of Twenty Guineas that he hath quitted said House upwards of Two years since which time she hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted her and refuses to Allow her any Support for

for herself and Child and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself and Child is therefore obliged to apply to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Relief

Sworn this 29th. day of Augt. 1798 before me  Wm Goodenough W Carter

her Mary Williams mark
