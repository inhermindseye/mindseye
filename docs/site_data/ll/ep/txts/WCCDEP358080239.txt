Middx- & Westmr-}  Sarah Godfrey aged about twenty six Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Lady Cavendish at Dovetridge in the parish of Dowbridge in the County of Derby for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Nine pounds hath quitted her said Service about sixteen Months ago since which time she never lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Oath says that on the 22nd- Day of Sepr- 1777 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at Mrs Wheelers in Essex Street in the Strand which has since been baptized Mary and is now become chargeable to the parish of St- Clement Danes And that one Richard Palmer a Taylor in Dowbridge aforesaid is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her body in Lady Cavendish's house but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl- difft- times afterwds- at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivd- as afsd- and that the said Richard Palmer and no other person did beget the same & that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn the 4th. Day of May 1778 before  Abrm Baley

her Sarah Godfrey Mark
