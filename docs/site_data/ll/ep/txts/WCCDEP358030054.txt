Middx  Cath: Mettz aged abt. 32 Yrs. Wife of Jno. Mettz gone from her upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Jno. Mettz on the 26th. Day of Novr. 1741 in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holbn. London Says her sd. Husband was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to one Mr. Pickax a Peruke maker in Chick Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchers London & after he had lived with his sd. Master about four Years was Assigned over to Mr. Hackett of New castle Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Peruke maker with whom he Served the Remainder of his time Says her sd. Husbd. never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. th. 1745 before}
