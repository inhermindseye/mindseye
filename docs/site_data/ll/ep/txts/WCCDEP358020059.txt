Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Newman aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born in Tothil Street Westminster in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster That she has one Child living vizt. Matthew Waite born a Bastard in the House of a Mr. Bownes a Taylor in Ewer Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Overys Saviour in Southwark on the 24th. day of September last

Sworn this 29th. day of Febry. 1788 .  Philip Dyot D Walker

Ann Newman
