Middx  The Examination of Sutton Brownett liveing in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 21st. Day of February 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. about 9 Years ago he served two Years & upwards the latter Part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. Wm. Wright in the Parish of St. James at Garlickhith in the City of London  Cordwainer And further on his Oath saith that he hath not since he left his sd. Master rented £10 a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife yt. he hath a Wife named Ann who is now Sick & Weak & he being Poor is not able to maintain himself & her

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Chas: Egerton

Sutton Brownell
