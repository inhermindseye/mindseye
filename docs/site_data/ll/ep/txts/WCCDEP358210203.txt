Westmr.  William Fell aged about 37 Years upon Oath says he was Married to Sarah his Wife at May fair Chapel about fifteen Years ago, says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes, nor ever was an Apprentice or lived a Yearly hired Servant for One Year, Says he hath three Children by his said Wife (to wit) William aged about fourteen Years Mary aged about Seven Years Teresa aged upwards of One Year; Says his Father Thomas Fell Rented and lived in a house in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Ten Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty one Pounds and quitted same about two Years ago, being under Misfortunes whereby he failed; and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 29th. of Octr. 1767 before W Kelynge

William Fell
