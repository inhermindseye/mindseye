St. Clement Danes 19th June 1791.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James  (to wit) Joseph Baker aged 37 Years & upwards Upon Oath saith That she he served an Apprenticeship of Seven Years with Mr. John Bullock in Archer Street in the parish of St. James Westminster within the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex (under Indenture) and Lodged in his said Master House, that his said Apprenticeship expired about Eighteen Years ago. And since he hath not lived as an hired yearly Servt. Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year paid any parochial Rates served any parish Office or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent saith he intermarried with his present Wife Margaret at the parish Church of St James Westminster aforesaid about Nineteen Years ago (by Banns) that he hath two Children by his said Wife both now living Namely William Aged Seventeen Years and upwards and Joseph Aged aged Sixteen years Years & upwards who both are both from this Deponent and provide for themselves And that he is unable to Maintain himself & Wife without Assistance

{Qe Whether this Examination right as he swears to another Settlemt. Vide Entry page 286 in the Book (if same man)}

Sworn the 19th. day of June 1792 before Us } M Addington Thos. Gordon

Joseph Baker
