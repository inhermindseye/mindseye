Middx  12th June 1784 Danl Williams aged abt. 15 Yrs. upon Oath Saith he never was an apprentice & that he has been informed & verily believes his father David Williams rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds in South Side Street in the New Church Parish at Plymouth in the County of Devon lived therein abt. three months & quitted the same abt. 7 Yrs. ago Since which he has not rented ten pounds by the year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Barth: Hammond

Daniel Williams
