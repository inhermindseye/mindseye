Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit John Sleat aged about 38 years upon his Oath saith that about 8 years ago he Rented and lived in a House in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 18 Months or thereabout at the yearly Rent of £22 that he hath quitted said House about 6 Months since which time he has not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes that he hath one Child living vizt. John aged about 2 years

Sworn this 16th. day of Novr. 1787  Wm. Hitchiner

John Sleat
