Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) John Hodley aged forty nine year or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he rented and lived in a House No.172 High Holborn  in the Parish of saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex or the yearly rent of twenty five pounds and afterwards of fifteen pounds for about two years that he quitted the said Hame about five years ago since where he hath not done any act whereby to the best of his knowledge he may have acquired any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23rd. Janry.1790 before me  Edw: Read  Wm Hyde

John Hodley
