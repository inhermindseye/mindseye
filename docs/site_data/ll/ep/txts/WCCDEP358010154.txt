Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Kean aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an apprentice That she was born in Ireland . That she hath one illegitimate Child vizt. John aged about 4 Months born the 21 day of June last at the House of Mrs.Wright in Holywell Street in the Parish of St.Clement Danes That one Wm. Fryer a Journeyman Gold Beate who works with a Mr. Wickstead in Compton Street Soho and lodges at one Mr. Whitmore in Moor Street Seven Dials is the father of the said Child

Sworn this 16th. day of Octr. 1787 R. Butler

her Mary Kean Mark
