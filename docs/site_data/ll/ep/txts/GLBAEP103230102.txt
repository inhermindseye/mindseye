Middlesex ss}  John Smith maketh oath that he was heretofore hired and lived as hired Servant by & with John Clarke a Victualler in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two Years & upwards That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or rented a house or Lodging of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10. day of January 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of John Smith
