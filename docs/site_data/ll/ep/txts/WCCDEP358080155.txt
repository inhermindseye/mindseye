Middx & Westmr-}  Jonathan Mitchell aged about forty Years on his Oath says he never was married rented house or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Humbleby a Dealer in Coals in the Old Bailey in the Parish of St. Sepulcher London for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted his said Place upwards of Ten Years ago since which time he hath never Rented house or Tenement of £10 a Year nor done any other Act whereby to gain a subseqt. Settlement

Sworn this 9th- Day of July 1777 before me  John Croft

his Jonn Mitchell Mark
