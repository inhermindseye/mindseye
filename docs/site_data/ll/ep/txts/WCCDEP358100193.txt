Middx to wit Robt. Powell aged about Forty five Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Elizth . his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middx about Twenty Years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children living Says he Rented & lived in a House in Grays Buildings  Mary Le Bone in the Parish of Mary Le Bone in the County of Middx upwards of four years at the yearly Rent of Twenty one Pounds & Paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about twelve years ago since which time he never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or or.wise howsoever

Sworn this 11th. day of March 1784 }

Robt. Powell
