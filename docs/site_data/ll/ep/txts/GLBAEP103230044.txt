Middlesex ss}  Ann Millard the widow of Isaac Millard deced maketh oath that Since her said late husbands death she was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at certain wages by & with Mr Thilton an Attorney in Princes street in the Liberty above the Bars in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the said County & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of three years That She has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor been married nor done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 4. day of May 1787 before}

The Mark of Ann Millward
