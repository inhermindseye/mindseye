Westmr. to wit Mary Taylor Aged about forty five Years on behalf of her Husband who now lies ill of a Year on her Oath Says she is he Wife of Jeremiah Taylor with whom she Intermarried at Saint Leonard Shoreditch about Sixteen Years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living Vizt. John Aged about Nine Years Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Mears, an Irony Turner in Red Lyon Street in the Parish Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex and After he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as her said Husband has Often Informed her and which Information she veri believes to be true

Sworn this 17th. day of December 1772 before Us R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Mary Taylor Mark

Witness Edwd. Field
