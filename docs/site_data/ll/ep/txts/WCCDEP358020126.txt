Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William Griffiths aged about 67 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a House in King Street Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for Six Months at the yearly Rent of Forty Guineas that he hath quitted said House about Twenty Years ago since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Janry. 1789  Sampn Wright J Addington

Wm Griffiths
