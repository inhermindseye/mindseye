St Clement Danes 30th April 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Egham Surrey  (to wit) Ann Burgess Wife of Nathan Burgess (who is now from her) and aged about 34 years Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said Nathan Burgess at the Parish Church of St John Westminster on or about the 28th day of July 1788, that he left her with their two Children hereafter named on the 19th. instant, that she hath two Children by her said Husband now living with her this Deponent namely Sarah born on or about the 12th day of May 1789, and David born on or about the 13th day of May 1791, that she is informed and believes her Husband (while single unmarried and having no Child) lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Slack, Farmer in the parish of Egham in the Country of Surrey for upwards of a year and quitted the Same several years since And this Deponent saith that since he left that service he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 30th day of April 1794 before us } Rich: Ford  Edwd: Naish

Ann Burgess 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys

N.B. The Overseer of Egham refused to rece this family when Sent. by the Beadle but being written to this they paid Maintenance & Expences & took the Pauses down to Egham at their own Expence Vide Lre with the Order of Removal
