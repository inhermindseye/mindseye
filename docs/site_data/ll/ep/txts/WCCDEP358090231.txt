Middx to wit Susannah Wood otherwise Prosser Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath says she Intermarried to One John Prosser at her this Examinants Lodgings at Mount Pleasant Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County. of Middlesex in the year 1778 by a Romish Priest which Marriage is of no Effect by the Louis of this County as this Examinant has been Informed and verily believes and further says that she has Two Children by the aforesaid John Prosser Vizt. John Aged upwards of Two years and three Months born in the aforesaid Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn and Mary Ann aged about Six Months born in Monmouth Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex And further says that her said Two Children have gained no other settlement than by Birth as this Examinant has been Informed and believes the same to be true

Sworn this 13th. day of June 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner

Susannah pros Wood
