Middx  Ann Barnes  Singlewoman Aged about Twenty two Years upon Oaths Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Sert with Mr. Wassell a Taylor in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . four Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted said Service about three Years & hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year

Sworn the Day of Octr. 1764 Before}
