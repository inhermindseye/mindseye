Middx.  Sarah Sharrar aged abt. Nineteen Years upon Oath says She never was Married; Says She has been informed & which Information she verily believes to be true that her Father John Sharrar was born in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex & That be never was an Apprentice or Rented Ten Pounds a Year & that her Grandfather Thomas Sharrar Rented a House upwards of Ten Pounds a Year in Parkers Lane in the aforesaid Parish of St. Giles in the Fields

Sworn the 27th day of October 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

Sara Sharrar
