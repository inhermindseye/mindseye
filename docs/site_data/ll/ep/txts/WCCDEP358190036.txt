Middx  Rachel Baldwin aged abt. 41 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Nathaniel Baldwin says her said husband now rents & lives in a farm of five Pounds a year in the Parish of Badby in the County of Northampton says her said husband hath kept the same abt. two years & hath paid to the Poor all that time

Sworn this 22d. day of August 1757 before me} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Rachel Baldwin
