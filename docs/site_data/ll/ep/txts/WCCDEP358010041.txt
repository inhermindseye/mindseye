Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Thos. Preston aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Mortlock a Publican in New Street Square in the Parish of Saint Bride alias Bridgett in the City of London for 18 Months at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted same about 9 years ago since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st day of Janry. 1787 D Walker R Butler

his Thomas Preston  mark
