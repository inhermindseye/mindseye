Middlesex ss}  Frances Crewdson the widow of Richard Crewdson deced maketh oath That divers years ago her Said late husband Rented a house at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds in Richmond Street in the Parish of Saint James Westminster in the County of Middx and was Charged to and Paid Parochial Taxes for the Same & by means thereof as she is advised and gained a legal Settlement there That her Said husband died there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his Death done any Act to her knowledge or belief to Acquire a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this Day of October 1772 before}
