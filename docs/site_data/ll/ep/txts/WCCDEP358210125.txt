Westmr.  Margaret Wormington aged about 17 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year, says her Father Charles Wormington a Breeches maker deceased Rented a house in Bakers Row in the Parish of St. James Clarkenwell in the County of Middlesex , for three Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds, and after he quitted said house, he removed to another House in the said Parish of the Yearly Rent of Eight Pounds, paid poors Rate for same and there dyed.

Sworn the 14th, Janry. 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The Mark of Margt. Wormington
