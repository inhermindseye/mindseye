Westmr.  Foster Holmes aged about 47 Years upon Oath Saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship Wth. Mr. Williams a Staymaker in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 9th. Decr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Foster Holmes
