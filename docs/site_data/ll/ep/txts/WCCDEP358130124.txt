Middx  George Hallett aged about 70 Years upon Oath says he served his Apprenticeship of Seven years with one Thomas Painter a Waterman in the Upper Ground in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry Says that he never Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 3d. Day of March 1756 . before} Theodr Sydenham 

The mark of George Hallett
