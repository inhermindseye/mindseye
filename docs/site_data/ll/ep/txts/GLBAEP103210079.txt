Middlesex ss  Ann Smith maketh oath that her Late husband Edward Smith deced heretofore rented a house of the yearly rent of Eighteen Pounds in upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement nor hath she this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 28 day of April 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Smith
