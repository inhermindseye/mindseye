Westminster to wit Mathew Welch aged about Twenty Six Years (on the behalf of Nathan Hart a Lunatick Aged about Twenty Three Years on his Oath says the said Nathan Hart never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says that his Father Nathan Hart (who is now dead) Rented and lived in a House known by the Name or Sign of Harts Academy in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Thirty five Pounds or thereabouts hath quitted the said House upwards of four Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House or Paid Taxes to his knowl or beleif And further saith that after the Death of the said Nathan Hart the Father this Examinant and the said Nathan Hart the Son Rented Lodgings or Appartments of one Henry Pars in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and upwards but was never Rated to or paid Taxes due for the same and therefore cannot set forth whr. this Examinant or the said Nathan Hart his partner gained any Settlemt. thereby.

Sworn this 10th. day of Augt. 1772 before me} Charles Marsh .

 Mathew Welch
