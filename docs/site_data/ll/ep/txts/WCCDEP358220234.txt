Westmr. to wit Mary Gordon Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Robert Gordon Rented and lived in a House in Beauchamp Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn in the County of MIddlesex to the best of this Examts. knowledge of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds lived therein about Nine Months and then quitted the same as her said Father and Mother has often Informed her and which Information she verily bels. to be true hath quitted the said House about Nine Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or Paid Taxes as this Examinant knows of or believes

Sworn this 6th. day of February 1771 before  Petr Planck W Kelynge

her Mary Gordon Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
