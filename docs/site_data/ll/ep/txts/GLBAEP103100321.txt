Middlesex ss  Sarah Savage (the wife of William Savage who is lately gone away from her to Sea on Board one of his Majesties Ships of war ) maketh oath That her said husband has informed her that he was born in Angel Court near Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and that to her knowledge or belief her said Husband never was an apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever Rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right And this Deponent Cannot in any wife Set forth any place of Settlement of her Sd. husbands Father But this Depont. Saith that she hath been informed by her said husbands Mother Ann Savage that she the Said Ann Savage was born in Whitby in the County of York and that she the Sd. Ann Savage when a Singlewoman lived as a Servant with one Mr. Robertson a Banker in Lombard Street London but for how long time She Continued in such service or whether she the sd Ann Savage was ever hired for a year she never informed this Depont. and this Depont. Saith that She hath a Child named John Aged about three Years the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her said husband and that she was Married to her said husband about Twenty Years ago at the Fleet

Sworn this 11th day of october 1770 before  Chris. Scott  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Sarah Savage
