Middx.  Elizth: Powell aged abt. 36 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Joseph Powell who is now at Sea with whom she Intermarried at the fleet London abt. 11 Yrs. ago & by whom she has 2 Children Ann aged upwards of Nine Years & Sarah aged abt. three Years says her said husband on the 25th. day of Decr. 1744 (he being at that time a single Man ) hired himself as a Covenant Servant for 2 Years to Mr. Isaac Couse of Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. Stay maker & served the said Mr. Couse for the Term of Two Years as aforesaid says Mr. Couse agreed with this Examinants husband to give him One Shilling P Week for the first Year of his said Service & two Shillings P Week for the second Year of his said Service says her sd. husband after he quitted his said Service never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as her said husband hath informed her & as she verily believes

Sworn this 26th. day of April 1758 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of  Eliz: Powell
