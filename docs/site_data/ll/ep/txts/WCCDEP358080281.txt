Middlesex to wit Elias Williams Aged about forty years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to his Wife Eleanor at the Parish Church of St. George Hanover Square about Eighteen years ago and by whom he hath one Child now living namely Catherine Aged about Hired years Say he is now a patient in Guys Hospital and not able to support himself or his said Child And further says that before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Churchill in Dover Street in the sd. Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for Sixteen Months at the yearly Wages of Twenty Three pounds besides Meat drink Lodging and Cloaths hath quitted his said Service Eighteen years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement whatever.

Sworn this 1st. day of Septr. 1778 before. R Butler John Cox .

Elias Williams
