Middx  Alice Smith aged about 48 Years Passed as a Vagrant from the Parish of Newington in the County of Surry upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Nicholas Hailstone a Bricklayer in Ship Yard near Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for two Years & upwards at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds Quitted the Same about twenty Years And hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant in any one Place for twelve Months together

Sworn the 4th: day of April 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Alice Smith
