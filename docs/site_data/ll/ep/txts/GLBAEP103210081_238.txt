Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Jane Watkins of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken upon oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this twenty fifth day of May 1785 who upon oath saith that on the ninth day of October one thousand seven hundred & eighty two in Coopers Court in the said Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate she the said Jane Watkins was delivered of a Male Bastard Child since baptized & registred by the name of George Skelton and this Examinant farther Saith that on the eighth day of February now last past in Coopers Court aforesaid in the said Parish she the said Jane Watkins was delivered of Another Male Bastard Child since baptized & registred by the name of William Skelton both of which said Children are now chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate she this Examinant being Poor & not able to provide for them and this Examinant farther Saith that George Skelton late of Coopers Court aforesd belonging to the Mint did get her with child of the said two bastard Children

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me R Davies

The Mark of Jane Watkins
