Middlesex ss  James Richardson Maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice to and served the Whole Term of his apprenticeship with Thomas Allen in the Parish of St Dunstan in the East in the City of London  Chimney Sweeper And by Means thereof as he is advised gained a Legal settlement there And this Depont Saith that he never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish offices or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship and that he is at Present an Unmarried Man

Sworn this day of November 1768 before}

The Mark of James Richardson
