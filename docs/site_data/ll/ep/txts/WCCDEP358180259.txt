Middx  Jane Smith aged about 60 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Jas. Gronous  Esqr . in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds & hath left her sd. Service about 13 Yrs. since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 4th. Decr. 1744 before me} Wm. Jones .

The mark of Jane Smith .
