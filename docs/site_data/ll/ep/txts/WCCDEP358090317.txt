Middx  Jane Wright aged about 28 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly servant to James Wallace  Esqr . in Lincolns Inn fields in the Parish of St. Giles's in the fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of five Years at the Yearly wages of Five Guineas hath quitted her said service upwards of Twelve Months ago since which time she never gained any other Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 14th. day of March 1781 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Humphrys in Bear Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of Mary and is now Chargeable to the said Parish & likely to Continue so and the one Thomas Richbald late a Butler to her said Master did beget such Bastard Child on her Body and that the said Thos. Richbald & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 12th. day of June 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Jane  Wright Mark
