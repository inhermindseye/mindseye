Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Dorothy Lott aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that She Intermarried with William Lott on the 22d. day of March 1784 at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch That her said Husband deserted her about December last That she has Two Children living Vizt. Elizabeth aged about 3 years by her said Husband and Mary Ann aged about Eight Months by Thomas Hurley a Journeyman Taylor at Eaton That her said Husband Wm. Lott was bound an Apprentice to his Father Wm. Lott in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1789 R Butler

Dorothy Lott
