Middlesex & Westminster}  Ann Wilson Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Wilson who has been dead about Three Years and with whom she Intermarried in the City of of Dublin about four Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Perins a Watchmaker in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One Year and Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Weeks ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Parish (she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) says that one Mr. Gilbelhausen a Phisician who lodges at her late Masters House is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime the latter end of January last But the particular day she does not now remember and at no other time afterwards at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the sd. Gibelhausen & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 3d. Day of Augt. 1776 before me  Sampn Wright

her Ann Wilson Mark
