Middx ss  Hannah Adams  Singlewoman Maketh oath that abt.Ten years ago She was bound apprentice to John Woodhouse of ye who yn lived in the Hamlet of Bethnall Green in the County of Middx  Weaver but now Called the parish of St. Mathew Bethnell Green and lived with y her sd. Master for the Space of Two Years & upwards. By Means Whereof She acquired a legal Settlemt. in ye sd parish of Saint Mathew Bethnall Green as she is advised & beleives And this Depont. Saith that she hath not done any Act Since whereby to acquire any Subsequent Settlemt-

Sworn this 25th. Day of August 1750 before us  Richd: Ricards  Saml Gower

The Mark of Hannah Adams
