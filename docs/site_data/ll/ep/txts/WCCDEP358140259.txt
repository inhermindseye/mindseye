Middx.  Ruth Ratcliffe aged abt. 17 Years upon Oath says she never was Married nor was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year; says her father Richd. Ratcliffe informed her that he served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years to John Martin a Waterman in Water Lane in the Parish of St. Ann  Blackfryars in the City of London ; says her father also informed her that he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 22d. June 1759 before} Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ruth Ratcliffe

Upon the Complaint of Mr Stratton Ovr : J: P:
