Middx  Thos. Wright Aged about Fifty Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Sarah at the Fleet London about Seventeen Years ago Says he is a Native of Ireland and never Gained a Settlement in England Says he Rented a House in the parish of Kingsale in the County of Cork upwards of Ten Years at the Yearly Rent of Five pounds Ten Shills. and paid to the poor hath quitted said House about Twenty four Years & hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says he has One Child by his said Wife Thomas Aged Ten Years & this Examinant being poor was Oblidged to ask Charity in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes

Sworn the 9th of June 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Thomas Wright

Upon Mr Moors Complt J. W
