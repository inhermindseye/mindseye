Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit George Crowder aged about 66 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Turner in Gough Square in the Parish of Saint Brides alias Bridget in the City of London for Three years and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight pounds that he hath quitted said Service about 30 years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of April 1795 } John Blatcher N Bond

George Crowder
