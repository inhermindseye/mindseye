Middx  Eliz: Weston upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of Chas. Weston  Deced that the sd. Chas. Weston was by Indre of Dated the 19th. Day of Augt. 1719 bound an Apprentice to & Served 3 Yrs. or thereabts. of his Apprentiship with Mr. Richd. Sampson of St. Martin in the Feilds  Sash Maker & then was Assigned ever to Mr. Richd. Hook of Stanhope Strt. in the parish of St. Clement Danes  Joiner with whom he served the Residue of his sd. Apprentiship that since he left the sd. Mr. Hook her sd. late Husbd. in his life time or this Examinant since his Death never rented £18. a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any Pson as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath 5 Children viz: Jno. aged 13 Yrs. & upwds. Francis aged 11 Yrs. & upwds. Mary aged 6 Yrs. & upwds. Thos. aged 3 Yrs. & upwds. & Wm. aged 10 Months & being poor is not able to maintain herself & then

Sworne the 16th. Decr. 1743 before} Cha. Egerton

The Mark of Eliz: Weston
