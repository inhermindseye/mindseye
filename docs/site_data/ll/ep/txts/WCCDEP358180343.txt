Middx  Nicholas Cannon aged abt. 65 Yrs: upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Eleanor about 16 Years since at the Parish Church of St: Michells in Dublin Says he was bound Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Nicholas Martin  Shoemaker in the Parish of St. Nicholas without in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland & never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. on any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 22d. June 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Nichs. Cannon
