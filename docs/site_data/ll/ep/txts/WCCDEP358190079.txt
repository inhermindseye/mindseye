Middx.  Hannah Cozens aged abt. 33 Years upon lodging at Mr. Armstrong's in Flemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Chas. Cozens who has been dead abt. four Years, says her said husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Flintum a Staymaker in Ely Court Holborn in the Liberty of Saffron hill Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to this Examinants Knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 30th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Hannah Cousins

Upon Mr Ponten's Complt. J: P:
