Middx to wit Mary Culbert aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Giles at the Grang Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of four Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlmt. And further on her Oath Says that on the Twenty Ninth day of June last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and is now Chargable there to (she being poor and unable to maintain the same) And that one Robert Harrison late an Hostler to her said late Master is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House sometime in the Month of October last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the sd. Robert Harrison and no person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 11th. day of August 1779 before R Butler

her Mary Culbert Mark
