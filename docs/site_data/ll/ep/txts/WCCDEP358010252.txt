Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Williams aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that on the 4th. day of June 1773 She Intermarried with James Williams her late Husband at Christ Church in the City of Waterford in Ireland by License according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England that her said late Husband has been dead about a Month that he informed this Examinant that he served Seven years Apprenticeship with a Mr. Warren in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenemt. on Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes that She hath Three Children living by her said late Husband Vizt. Thomas aged about Thirteen years and John aged about Nine years both provided for and James aged about 5 years who is with this Ext. unprovided for

Sworn this 21st. day of Octr. 1788 R. Butler

her Mary Williams Mark
