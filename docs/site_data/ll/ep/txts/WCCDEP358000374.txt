Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Jane Holmes aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that about 3 years & an half ago she Intermarried with Isaac Holmes her Present Husband at the Parish Church of St. Mary Le Bone that She has no Children living by her said Husband that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of £10 & upwards for about 3 Quarters of a Year that he said Husband has Quitted said House about 14 Months ago since which time this Ext. has done no Act to gain any other Settlement And further this Examinant saith that her said Husband her deserted her and refuses to maintain her she being Poor & unable to maintain herself is Obliged to Apply to the said Parish  of Saint Clement Danes for relief

Sworn this 2d. day Novr. 1786 M Addington

Jane Holmes
