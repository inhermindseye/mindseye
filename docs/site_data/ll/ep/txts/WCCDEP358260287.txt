Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Layborn aged about 23 years upon his Oath saith that he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That his father James Layburn was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England That he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that his Mother Susannah Layburn previous to her Intermarriage with his father James Layburn and being a Singlewoman lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Farrer a Taylor in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Five Pounds and that after she quitted said Service she never did any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of May 1798 before us N Bond P. Henderson

James Labourn
