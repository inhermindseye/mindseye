Middlesex ss}  John Crawley maketh oath That he has been informed by his Mother (and which Information this Depont. believes to be true)That he this Depont. was born a Bastard in King Street in the Parish of Saint Matthew Bethnal Green in the County of Middlesex And this Depont Saith That he was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor Rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year nor ever Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief ever and any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Birth

Sworn this 22d. day of July 1769 before W: Russell.

The Mark of John Crawley
