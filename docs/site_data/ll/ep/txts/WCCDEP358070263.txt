Middx to wit Ann Cole Aged upwards of Forty One Years on her Oath says she never was Married, Rented House, Paid Taxes or served any Apprenticeship, says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs Esther Pulley in Old Round Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the field in the Liberty of Westminster , about Eighteen Months as the Yearly Wages of Five pounds, hath Quitted her said service upwards of Seventeen Months ago, and not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th- day of May 1775  R Butler W Addington

Ann Cole
