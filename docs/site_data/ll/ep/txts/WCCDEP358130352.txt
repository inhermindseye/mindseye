Middx.  Eleanor Allen aged upwards of fifty Yrs. upon Oath says she is the wife of Edwd. Allen now abroad & beyond the Seas says her sd. husband served his Apprenticeship to Mr. Forle a Wire Drawer in the Parish of St. Catharine in the City of Dublin in Ireland as she has been informed & verily believes

Sworn the 6th of June 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Eleanor Allen
