Saint Clement Danes May 12th. 1789

Middlesex (to wit) Ann Barnby of King Street Saint James's upon her Oath Saith that Mary Bartlett an Infant aged two years or thereabouts and now chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex is the Daughter of William and Mary Bartlett (born in lawful Wedlock) and this Examinant further upon her Oath Saith that the said William Bartlett the Father hath informed this Examinant and which Information this Examinant believes to be true that he the said William Bartlett was born at Manchester in the County of Lancaster and that he never did gain or acquire by his own Act any Subsequent or other Settlement than in Manchester aforesaid

Sworn this 12th May 1789 before us D Walker Russ: Clarke }

her Ann Barnby Mark
