Middlesex ss}  Alice Thomas the Widow of William Thomas deced maketh oath that she cannot at Present discover or Set forth the Place of Settlement of her late husband William Thomas deced That She this Depont previous to her marriage with him was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Mr Charles Price a Farmer in the Parish of Chockley in the County of Chester at Certain wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of three years & has not Since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement Save what She Acquired by her Sd Marriage That She hath two Children namely Ann Thomas aged four years the lawful Issue of this Examinant by her Sd late husband & William Riley Aged one year & half who was born of this Examinants Body in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County who is a Bastard & that one William Riley a Soldier now belonging to the first Regiment of Foot did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Sworn this. 27. day of Septemr 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Alice Thomas
