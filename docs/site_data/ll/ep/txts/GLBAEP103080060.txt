Middlesex ss  Mary Mc. Clane  Singlewoman now in the Workhouse of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx Maketh oath that she hath two Children now living and in the said Workhouse to wit Mary Harley aged two years and Nine Months or thereabouts and John Harley aged about ten Months And this Deponent saith that both the sd. Children were begotten on the body of this Deponent by one William Harley a soldier now abroad in his Majesties service and were born bastards this Deponent having never been Married to the sd. William Harley the true and only father of the said bastard Children And this Depont farther saith that the first of the sd. Children Named Mary Harley was born in the sd. Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate and the other of them Named John Harley was born in the Parish of Saint James at Clerkenwell in the sd. County of Middx and baptised in the same Parish

Sworn this Ninth day of December 1760 before us  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

The Mark of Mary McClane
