Middx  Wm. Smith aged abt. 20 Yrs. upon his Oath saith that he is a Singleman & was never married & that upwards of Two Years ago he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Wm. Peerwilliams in Red Lyon Square in the Parish of St. George the Martyr in the County of Middx for the Space of Two Yrs. & four Months at the Yearly Wages of £2. for the First Year £2.10s for the 2d. Year & 3d. for the Third Year that after he left his sd. Master he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Saml. Hickford  Attorney at Law in Clemts. Inn in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Sd. County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds & Ten Shillings for the Space of One Year & upwards & during his Sd. Service he lay Five Nights in the Week in Clemts. Inn & 2 Nights in the Week at Kensington & never resided 40 Days at once in either Parish

that he hath been come from the Sd. Mr. Hickford abt. Seventeen Months that since he left his Sd. Master he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 19th. of May 1742 before} Tho. Lane .

Wm. Smith
