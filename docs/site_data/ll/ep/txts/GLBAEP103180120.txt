Middlesex ss  Sarah Fox the wife of John Fox maketh oath that her said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true That he was duly bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with John Colestock in Bulls Court Tooley Street in the Parish of St Olave in Southwarke in the Couty of Surry That her Said Husband to her knowledge & belief has not Since Such his Apprenticeship rented a house or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this 19 day of Janry 1779 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Sarah Fox
