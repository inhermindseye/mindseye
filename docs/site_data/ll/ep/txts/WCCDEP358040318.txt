Middx  Wm. Bardoe upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Thomas Keene of the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx a Cornfactor and lived the last Forty Days of his sd. Apprentiship with the sd. Mr. Keene at his house in Essex Strt. in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes Says he never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 11th. Febry 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Wm Bordoe
