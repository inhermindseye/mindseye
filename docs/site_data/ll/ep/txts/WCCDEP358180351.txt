Middx  Ann Stevens  wife of Edwd. Stevens Soldier in Coll Noells Compr. in the 2d. Regmt. of Foot Guards now in Flanders upon her Oath saith that about Eleven Years before her Intermarriage with the Sd. Edwd. Stevens she Intermarri with one Wm. Allen at the Fleet London who was a Soldier in the same Company & Regiment & hath been Dead upwards of Three Years Says the Sd. Wm. Allen was born at Brentford in Middx as she hath been Informed & beleives Says he never Rented ten pounds a Year Served any Parochial Office paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or be belief and hath one Child by him (to wit) William aged about 8 Yrs. & is Poor & not able to maintain him Says she Intermarried with her prest. Husband Edward Stevens on the 25th. Mary 1742 at the Fleet Londn. Says he was born in the Parish of Ivengoarsen in the County of Bucks where his Father lived & paid his Share of Taxes as she hath been Informed & beleive Says the Sd. Edwd. Stevens never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained

a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif & hath two Children by him (to wit) James aged about two Yrs. & Edward aged upwards of Three Months & is Poor & not able to maintain them

Sworn July 5th. 1745 before} Tho Lane . The Mark of Ann Stevens
