Middx  Richard Eaton on behalf of Sarah West aged about Twelve years upon Oath says that the said Sarah West was born in the Parish of Timsbury in the County of Somerset as John West the Father of the said Sarah West informed this Deponent and as he verily believes Says the said John West hath been dead about five Months

Sworn the 15th. Day of May 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Richard Laton
