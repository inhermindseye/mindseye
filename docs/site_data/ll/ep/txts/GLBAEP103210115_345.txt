Middlesex ss}  Mary Emmerson  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mr Kinder a Stock Broker in Great James Street in the Liberty above the Bars in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the said County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of a year and upwards That she has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9 day of November 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Mary Emmerson
