Middx  Sarah Beckley aged about 28 years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Roden at the Sign of the Northumberlands head in Stanhope Street Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of one year at the yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about One Months ago since which she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born says Heatone Timothy Roberts a Journeyman Taylor who lately Lodged at the Northumberland Head in Stanhope Street Clare Markett is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in February last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at several Different times after at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Timothy Roberts and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 19th. day of October 1778 before  Abrm Baley

her Sarah Beckley Mark
