Westr to wit John James Aged upwards of Thirty nine Years on his Oath says he is now a Widower and has no Children, now Living says he was bound Apprentices to Moses Viridet an Orris Weavers in Fetherston Street in the Parish of Saint Luke Old street in the County of Middlesex and Served his said Master under those Indentures the full term of Seven Years, and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Taxes, lived a hired Yearly Servant or any otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 8th day of May 1775 before W Addington. Tho. Lane

John James
