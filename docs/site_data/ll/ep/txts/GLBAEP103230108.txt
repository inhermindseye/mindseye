Middlesex ss}  John Barber maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house of Twelve Pounds a year in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house or Lodging of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19. day of January 1788 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of John Barber
