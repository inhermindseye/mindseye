Middx  John Sirett upon Oath saith he is abt. 27 years of age that he never was an apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever Rented ten Pounds by the year or paid parochial taxes that his father John Sirett deceased rented a house in the Old Change in the Parish of St. Gregory by St. Paul London of the yearly Rent of £20 and upwds. lived therein Sevl. Years & there dyed and his late mother Susa. Sirett after his father decease rented a house of Five pounds by the year in Pissing Alley Night Ryder Strt. in the same Parish & these dyed

Sworn June 12th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Jno Syrett
