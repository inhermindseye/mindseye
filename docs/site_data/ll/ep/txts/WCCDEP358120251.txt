Middx  Ann Kilmister aged about 68 Years upon Oath saith she is the wife of Chas. Kilmister who now lives in a Leasehold house of his own in Salisbury Street in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . Which house this Examinant verily believes would Lett for twenty pounds a year or thereabouts

Sworn 4th. Janry 1754 : before Fran: Bedwell

Ann Killmister
