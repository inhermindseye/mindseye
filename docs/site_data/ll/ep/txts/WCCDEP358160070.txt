Middx  The Examination of Ann Grear  Widw . Aged Forty four Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 27th. Day of Augst. 1739 Who upon her Oath saith yt. abt. Twenty Years ago her late Husband Jams. Grear lived in & Rented a House in Blackmoor Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £20 & lived in the same for the space of Three Years & upwards And this Examint. further saith that her said Husband in his life time nor she since his Death never after Rented any other House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her Knowledge or beleife & yt. she is now Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Ann Grear
