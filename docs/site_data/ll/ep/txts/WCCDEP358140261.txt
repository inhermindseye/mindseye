Middx.  Catharine King aged abt. 35 Years upon Oath says she never was Married; says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Jarman Wright  Esqr . in Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Seven Guineas and hath quitted said Service abt. four Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 27th. of June 1759 before} Barth: Hammond

Catharine King
