Middx  Daniel Robinson Upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Catherine his Wife abt- Nineteen or Twenty Years since at the Fleet London That he hath Two Children by his said Wife (to witt) Martha aged Seventeen Years & Daniel aged Fourteen Years That he Rented a House of Three Pounds a Year in the Parish of St. Peter in the Burrough of St. Albans in the County of Hertford for the Space of Ten Years or thereabouts & was Rated & paid to the Poor of the said Parish during his Presidence in that house that after he left the said House he lived in and Rented a house of Six Pounds a Year in the said Parish of St. Peter for the Space of Seven Years or thereabts. & was Rated & paid to the Poor of the sd. Parish for Five Years or thereabouts that this Examinant hath not since rented £10. a Yr. Served any Parochiall Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworne the 13th. June 1743 before}

Daniell Robinson
