St. Clement Danes May 11th. 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St. Mary Lambeth  (to wit) David Rogerson Aged about 47 Years Upon Oath saith That he never was Married That he served an was bound Apprentice ship of for seven Years by Indenture (executed) to Mr. Danl. Mareton a Waterman who resided in Stangate in the parish of St. Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry That he served his said Master for about two years & then ran away without his Master's knowledge or consent That during such his Service he lodged in his Master's House in Stangate aforesaid And That since he quitted his Master's Service he hath done no Act or thing whatsoever to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn the 11th May 1792 before Us } B: Kennett Wm Hyde

David Rogerson
