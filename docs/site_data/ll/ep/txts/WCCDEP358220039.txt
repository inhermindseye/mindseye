Middx & Westmr}  Ann Turner aged about fifty years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father Richard Turner who was by Trade a Shoemaker and who has been dead upwards of Thirty Years has often Informed her and which Information she Verily believes to be true that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Swan a Shoemaker in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship her said Father went to live in Stanhope Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes where this Examinant was born but never to her Knowledge ever Rented House or paid taxes

Sworn this 7th. day of July 1769 before me} Tho: Fellows

her Ann Turner mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
