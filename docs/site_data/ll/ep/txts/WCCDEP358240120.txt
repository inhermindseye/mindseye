St Clement Danes July 2d 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  Errata Vide Bastard & Despertion Book  to wit Mary Tweedle aged about 37 Years Wife of John Tweedle Upon Oath saith That she was Married to her present Husband at the parish Church of St. George  Queen Square or about the 9th. day of May 1775,. That her and Husband now Rents & Resides with his Family in the in the parish of St Clement Danes aforesaid in a Dwelling House of the Annual Rent of £13 or thereabouts Which House her said Husband hath Rented & Resided an for about the last 5 Years That she hath three Children now living by her said Husband namely Alexander aged 16 on the 9th April last Mary born 21st. January 1783. Elizabeth born 6th May 1786. that her said Husband is now as the verily believes in solvent Circumstances and well able to Maintain her this Deponent and their sd. Family but nowithstanding he refuses or neglects to allow her a necessary Provision & that that she is now destitute & unable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn the 2nd. day of July 1792 before me}
