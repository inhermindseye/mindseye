Middx  Jane England Maketh Oath that James Cordel maketh Oath that he is the Son of James and Jane Cordel of the Parish of St. Kathe: Coalman in the City of London ; and that he was born in Poor Jury Lane in the Said Parish of St. Katherine Coalman in the City of London ; and that he hath not gained any Subsequent Settlement to his Knowlidge

Sworn March ye. 14th: 1743 before me  Robert Dennett

her Jane Jane England  Mark
