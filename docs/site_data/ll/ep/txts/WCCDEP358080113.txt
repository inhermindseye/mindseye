Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Mc.Cloud Aged about Nineteen Yrs. on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one John Colles in Cumberland Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Oath Says that on the 6th. day of August 1775 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes which has since been Baptized by the Name of Sarah and is become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same And that one John Alexander a Journey man Smith who now works with one Mr. Askins a Smith in the Old Bailey is the Far. thereof he having had Carnal of her Body in her said late Mars. House sometime in the Month of July 1774 but the particular day she does not now remember and at different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which She was so delivered as afsd. And that the said John Alexander & no other person is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 29th. day of Janry 1777 before. R Butler

her Sarah Mc.Cloud Mark
