Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Davis maketh oath that divers Years ago She was duly bound Apprentice to Matthew Dowling of Darby Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County by the then Parish officers of St Luke in the sd County untill She Shod attain her Age of twenty one years That she Served her sd Master there for about the Space of four years When her sd Master left her & went to Ireland & her Indres were delivd upto be Cancelled that She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year or been Married or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17. day of April 1784 before}
