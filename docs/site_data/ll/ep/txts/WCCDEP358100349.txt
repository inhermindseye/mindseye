Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Mary Le Strand to wit Mary Ireland aged about fifty years on her oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she served five years Apprenticeship to one Mrs. Ashburn of White Horse yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes as a Day Apprentice and during the said Apprenticeship she Slept at her Parents House in Little Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 14th- day of Febry 1785  Sampn Wright J Reading

her Mary Ireland mark
