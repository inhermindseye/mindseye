St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Stone aged about 39 years upon his Oath saith that he never was married nor never obtained or acquired any legal Settlement in his own Right That he is informed and believes That his father James Stone Rented and Resided in an Apartment the Horse and Groom in Red Lyon Street in the parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Three Months at the Weekly Rent of Six Shillings That after he quitted said Apartment he never did any Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of Janry. 1795 } M Addington Edw: Naish

his James Stone James Stone Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
