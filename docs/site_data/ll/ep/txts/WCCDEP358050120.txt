Middx  John Wood aged about 22 Years upon his Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Francis White  Master of the black Prince Snow in the Merchant Service and lay on board the sd. Vessel in the Dry Dock in the parish of St. James as Liverpool in the County of Lancaster the first four months of his Apprenticeship and then went to Sra Says he never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Lived a Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn the 24th. Octr. 1749 before} Geo: Errington

The Mark of Jno. Wood .
