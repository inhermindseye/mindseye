Middx  Mary Goodman  Wido. aged about 45 Years Removed from the Parish of St. James at Clerkenwell by Order Dated the 30th: May last upon her Oath saith that by Indenture Dated the 19th. Day of April 1725 her late Husbd.  Wm. Goodman was bound an Apprentic to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Tarra Butcher in Butcher Row in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that the sd. Wm: Goodman in his Life or this Examint. since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath one Child (to wit) Wm. aged 2 Yrs. & an half & being poor is not able to maintain it

Sworne 1st. June 1744 before} Wm: Jones .

Mary Goodman
