Middlesex ss}  Jane Andrews  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Mother Susannah Andrews deced has informed her & which She believes to be true that her late Father John Andrews deced was born in King Henry Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & this Deponent Can give no othr Account respecting his Settlement That She this Depont. was born in King Henry Yard aforesd & never was bound An Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did Any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 16. day of December 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Jane Andrews
