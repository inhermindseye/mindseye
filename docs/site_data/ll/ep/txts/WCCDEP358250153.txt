Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Williams aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Choke a Surveyor in Saint Martins Street  Leicester fields in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years at the yearly Wages of Five pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time She hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing Whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1795 }

Elizabeth Williams
