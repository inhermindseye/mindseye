Middx  John Lee aged abt. 52 Years upon Oath saith he intermarried Wm. Rebecca his Wife at the Fleet London the 18th. Day of May 1744 and that he Served upwds. of Five Years the first part of his Apprentiship wth. Mr Saml Shaw a Peruke maker in Essex Strt. in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and then his sd. Master failed & this Examinant never was assigned over to Any other person nor ever Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn Febry 17th. 1753 before}
