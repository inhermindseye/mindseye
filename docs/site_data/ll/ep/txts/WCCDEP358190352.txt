Middx  John Bean Aged upwards Sixty Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with Sarah his Wife Aged upwards of 50 Years at the Fleet London about Forty Years Since Says he Rented a house in Sheffield Street  Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds lived in the same about One Year quitted Same about Four Years and hath not Since Rented house or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 23th Day of May 1763 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of John Bean
