Middlesex ss}  Ann Beavis the wife of John Beavis maketh oath that her said husband is late by gone away from her to places unknown & left her destitute of Support That She this Depont cannot set forth or give any account respecting the Settlement of her said husband never having received any information relative there to That She this Deponent divers years ago & While a Single woman was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain wages by & with one Robert Grant in Langley Street near Long Acre in the parish of Saint Martin in the Fields Westmr in the County of Middlesex  Sweep washer & Continued in Such Service there for the Space of three years & half & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or done any act to gain another Settlement save what she has Acquired by marriage with her said husband whole Settlement is not known to her.

Sworn this. 22. day of February 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Sam Wood

The Mark of Ann Beavis
