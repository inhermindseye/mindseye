Middx  Mary Burchman  Wido . of Robt. Burchman upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late husband Robt. Birchman in the Tabernacle belonging to the parish of St. Martin in the feild in the Liberty of Westmr. on the 13th. of Septr.upwds. of 20 Yrs since Says her sd. late husband dyed last Wednesday Says her sd. late husband Served four Years Apprentiship with Geo; Clavering in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr.  Taylor and hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship about 21 Yrs. Says her sd. late husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served a parochial Office lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath two Childn. viz Robt. aged abt. 9 Years & Chas. aged abt. 5 Yrs. & is poor and not able to maintain them

Sworn 24th. Novr. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Mary Burchman
