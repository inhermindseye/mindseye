2d Examn- After Birth} Susanna Douglass & Sarah Douglass her B. Child

St Clement Danes 20th November 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Child born in the House  Vide Examn. as to Settlemt on page 41 in this Book  Reputed Father (to wit) Susanna Douglass  singlewoman aged 32 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that on or about the 26 day of September Now last to the parish of St Clement Danes situate within the same at the Workhouse or Poor House of &  she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath since been half Baptized by the Name of Sarah Douglass that John Bramble who lately lived as Waiter at the Navy  Coffee House in Newcastle Street in the Liberty of Westminster in the said  County of Middlesex is the only real true father of the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid that he was of he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body sometime about the latter end of December 1792 and several times since at some or one of which times he  did beget the said Child thereon

Sworn the 20th day of November 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Susanna Douglass 's Mark

Witness Garrett
