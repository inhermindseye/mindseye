St Clement Danes 12th June 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Sarah Pattinson  Widow aged 49 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Carlton Pattinson (by License) at the parish Church of St Paul Covent Garden abt. 28 years ago, that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and resided in a House in New Inn passage in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex for abt. two years at the Annual Rent of ten pounds or upwards that they quitted that House about 20 years ago and that her said Husband never during his Life obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to best of her knowledge or belief that he Died about 6 years ago and that Since his Decease hath not been Married or otherwise obtained or become intitled unto any legal Settlement except as in right of her said Husband as aforesaid

Sworn the 12th day of June 1793 before Me} D Walker

Sarah Pattinson
