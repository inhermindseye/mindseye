Middx  Alice Wilson removed from the Parish of St. Geo: Hanover Square by Order dated the 4th. Day of Novr. Instant upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman Says she was bound Apprentice to & Served 5 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mr. Dockaty a Taylor in New Inn Passage and in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Says she hath been out of her time upwards of four Yrs. Says she hath not since her sd. Apprentiship rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Novr 5th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Alice Wilson
