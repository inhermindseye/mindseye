Westmr.  Elizth. Maiden Aged about Thirty Three years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever rented House or Paid Taxes Says that she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Rainey a Cabinet maker in Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint Martins in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of one year & a half at the yearly wages of seven Pounds hath Quitted her said Service upwards of Four years and a half ago and hath not Since she quitted same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year and further says that on the first day of February 1765 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of one Mrs. Gillett in Swan Yard Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized by the Name of William and further sayth that one William Richardson a Gentlemans servant (and who now lives an hostler some were in Northamptonshire but at what Place this Examinant cannot set forth) is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of May 1764 and at Sevl. Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was Delivered as aforesaid And that the said William Richardson of no other Person is the True and real Father thereof.

Sworn this 21st day of November 1769 before us} R Butler Thos Kynaston

Mark Eliz Maiden

Witness Fras Bishop
