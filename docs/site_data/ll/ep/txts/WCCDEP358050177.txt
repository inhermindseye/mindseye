Westmr.  Jane Stickler and upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her late husband Francis Stickler at Ockham in Surry on the 10th. August 1717 that her said husband Served his Apprentiship with a Bricklayer in the Parish of Horsham in the County of Sussex and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served a Parochial office or lived a years by Servt and that the this Examinant since the Decease of her said husband has not rented Ten pounds a Year pd parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or other- -wise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 16th. Decr. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Jane Stickler
