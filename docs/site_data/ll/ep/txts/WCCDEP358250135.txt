Saint Clement Danes

Settlement in St C. D Walter Knight aged about 49 years upon his Oath saith That about Eighteen years ago he Intermarried with Grace his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Ann 's by License That she hath four Children living by his said Wife vizt. Thos. aged about 16 years Elizabeth aged about 15 years Jane aged about 11 years and Robt. Walter aged about 8 years all unprovided for That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a House in Robinhood Court Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about four years at the yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds & paid all Taxes due for the same that he hath left said house about Two years since which time he this Deponent hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this first day of Novr. 1794  J Hlind

W. Knight
