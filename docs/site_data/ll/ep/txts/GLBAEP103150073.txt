Middlesex ss}  Grace Lee the widow of John Lee deced maketh oath that Sometime Ago her said late husband rented a House in Sweed land Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & during Such his Residence therein was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish That her Said husband Since his leaving the Said Parish hath not rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 4th day of November 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Grace Lee
