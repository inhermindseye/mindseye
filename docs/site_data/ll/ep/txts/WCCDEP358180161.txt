Middx  Charity Hawkins aged 22 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never marryed That abt. Four Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Sturgeon in Witch Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Baker at the Yearly Wages of £3. for the Space of One Year & upwards that since she left her sd. Service she hath not Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which are likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & not able to maintain the same when born And that Wm. Tindall of the Parish of Egham in the County of Surry  Yeoman did begett the sd Child or Children on her Body he the sd Wm. Tindall & this Examinant living together

as Man & Wife at his Lodgings in the parish of Warw in the County of Berks where the sd. Wm. Tindall had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the latter of End of June last and at several other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastd Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant & that he the sd. Wm. Tindall & no other Pson is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 1st. Febry 1743 before} Thos. DeVeil

The Mark of Charity Hawkins
