Middx.  Robt. Row aged abt. 64 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with his present Wife  Eliz : at the Broad Way Chapel Westmr. abt. thirty five Years ago says he Rented a house in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of twenty seven Pounds lived therein abt. Eleven Years & quitted same abt. six Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds of a Year or paid Parish Taxes & his said Wife being very ill & Lame is not able to maintain himself & her

Sworn the 7th of June 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

Robt Row
