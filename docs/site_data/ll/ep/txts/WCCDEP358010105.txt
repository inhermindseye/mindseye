Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Curtis aged about 57 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with James Curtis her late Husband (who has been dead about 12 years) at the Parish Church of Saint Bennett Grace Church in the City of London by whom she hath one Child living vizt. Makepeace aged about 13 years that her said late Husband in his Life time Rented and lived in a House in Blue Anchor Alley in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middlesex for about Two years at the yearly Rent of £10 & paid all Taxes due for the same that he quitted said House about 9 Months before his decease during which time he never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 paid Tax or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath this Ext. since her said late Husbands decease rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th- day of June 1787  Sampr Wright  James Croft

Ann Curtis
