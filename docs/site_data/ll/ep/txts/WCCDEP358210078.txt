Westmr.  Mary Wheeler aged about 28 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thomas Wheeler who has been dead about Nine Months; says her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Hornsey in the County of Middlesex by Indenture dated the 15th, day of March 1753, for the term of Seven Years to Nathaniel Pitkin of Highgate in the aforesaid Parish of Hornsey Cordwainer and served his said Apprenticeship with his said Master at Highgate aforesaid & never Rented House or paid Taxes to this Examinants Knowledge or belief, Says she hath not since her said husbands Death Rented house or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 9th, day of October 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran. Cary

The Mark of Mary Wheeler
