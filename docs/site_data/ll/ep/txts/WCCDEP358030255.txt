Middx  Susanna Green aged abt. 27 Years upon Oath Says she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Turbut in Vear Street Claremarkett for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & hath left her sd. Service about four Years Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Turbutts Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she was Delivered of a Male bastard Child on the 24th: Day of Novr. 1742 in the House of one Mrs. Cook in Little Russell Street in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury which hath since been Baptized John and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to

become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St: Clement Danes she being Poor and not able to Maintain the same when born Says that one John Miller late a Journey man to one Mr. Welford a Butcher in Vear Street Claremarkett did beget the said Bastard Child or Children on her Body this Examinant and the sd. John Miller living together as man & Wife he the said John Miller had Carnall knowledge of this Examinants Body in the sd. Mrs. Cooks House in Little Russell Street in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury aforesd. in the Month of May last & at sevll. other times both before & since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. John Miller & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne Novr 14th. 1746 before  Tho: Burdus

Susannah Green
