Middlesex ss  Mary Ray  Widow of Thomas Ray deced Maketh oath That divers Years ago and When a Widow and subsequent to her said husbands Death She this Depont. was hired and lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year and upwards at Certain Yearly Wages with Samuel Lyons in the Parish of St. Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry Victualler And by Means thereof as she apprehends gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that she has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 29th day of September 1767 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Mary Ray
