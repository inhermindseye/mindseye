Middlesex ss  William Scroop maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indenture by the Governors & Guardians of the Hospital for the Maintenance & Education of exposed & deserted Young Children to Paul Bawrie of Half Nichol Street in the Parish of St Matthew Bethnal Green in the Sd County Weaver and Served the whole of his Said Apprenticeship there That he never rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever served any Parish office nor ever Paid Any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement Since his Said Apprenticeship That he hath a wife named Elizabeth and a Child named William aged about Seventeen Months the lawful issue of this Deponent by his Sd Wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 29. day of Novemr 1786 before}

The Mark of William Scroop
