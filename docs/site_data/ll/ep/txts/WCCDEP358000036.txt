Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Passd. to St. George Hanover Square to wit Alexr. Marshall of Brownlow Street Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex  Carver and Gilder on the behalf of Sarah Marshall Singlewoman his Sister now in Lodging in Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex and so ill that she cannott Attend to be examined upon his Oath saith that the said Sarah Marshall never was Married Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes for any such was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information or belief she may have Acquired any Settlement in her own Right Saith that John Marshall their Father is dead that in his life time he Rented and lived in a House in Knightsbridge in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex at the yearly rent of £10 and upwards and paid Taxes for the same for upwards of years and after he quitted such House till the time of his Death did not nor did Sarah Marshall this Examinants Mother who Survived the said John Marshall her Husband but is since dead nor either of them Rent any other Tenement of Ten Pounds Value Per Annum or pay any Taxes

Taxes or do any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information or belief they or either of them may have gained any other Settlement than in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square as above Stated

Sworn this 28th day of April 1785  Jno Machin  James Caine

Alexander Marshall
