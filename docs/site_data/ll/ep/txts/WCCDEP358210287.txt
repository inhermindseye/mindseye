Westmr.  Hannah Mowett Aged about Sixty one Years, Upon Oath says she intermarried to Oliver Mowett at the Fleet London on the Second of April 1752, says that at the time of her Intermarriage with her said Husband she Rented a House in the cross Court of Beaufort Buildings in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds or thereabouts, and had so done for about Four Years, says her said Husband and she continued to live in and Rent the said House from the said Second of April 1752, till the Christmas following, and then quitted it, says her said Husband after he quitted said House never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 13th. day of June 1768 , before} 

Hannah Mowett
