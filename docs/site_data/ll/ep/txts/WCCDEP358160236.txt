Middx  The Examination of Thomas Dudley aged Seventy Six Years & upwards lodging in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 28th- Day of January 1740 Who upon his Oath saith That about 50 Years ago he this Examint. Rented a House in Angell Alley in Witch Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes of the yearly Rent of ten Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of one Year & upwards That he hath not since that Time rented any House Lands or Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife That he hath a Wife named Alice & being Poor is not able to maintaine himself & Wife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Thos. Dudley
