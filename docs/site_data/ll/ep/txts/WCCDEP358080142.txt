Middx & Westmr.}  Giles White Aged about seventy five Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Mary his now Wife at St. Bennetts Pauls Wharf in the City of London about 30 Years ago and by whom he has no Children now living Says he has Rented sevl- difft- houses in the Parish of St- Clement Danes for upwards of 20 Years last past the last of which was in Portugal Street at the Yrly Rent of £30 and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said last mentd. house about 13 Years ago since which time he hath never Rented house or Tenement of £10s. a Year paid Taxes or done any or. Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 23rd. Day of April 1777 before me R Butler

Giles White
