Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Yeates aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith That she Rented and lived in a first floor at No. 21 Mary le bone Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the Parish of County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Guineas that She hath quitted said Service upwards of Six years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she hath an illegitimate Child named Mary born on the 12th. day of August 1797 at Newport in Shropshire

Sworn this 6th. day of Sept. 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

Her Ann Yeates Mark

H Day Beadles
