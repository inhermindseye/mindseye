Middx ss.  Nehemiah Cooley now a soldier in the Kings service belongg to the Cold stream Regiment of Foot Guards Maketh oath that his Father he this Depont. was born in the Parish of Ivinghoe in the County of Bucks about Thirty Nine Years Ago and by Means thereof & also in right of his said Father who lived there then & for Many years since Carried on the Business of a Smith and Paid all Parochial Taxes there he this Depont. acquired a legal settlement there he this Depont having never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards Nor served any Parish offices nor lived as a hired servant by the Year nor done any Act to Acquire a settlement in his own right And this Depont. saith that he hath a Daughter Named Elizabeth aged about Nine Years the lawful issue of this Depont by his late wife Deced

Sworn this 8th day of May 1758 before us  Richd: Ricards  Walter Berry

Nehemiah Cooley
