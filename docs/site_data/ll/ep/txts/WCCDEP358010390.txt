St Clemt. Danes

Middx & Westminster}  Martha  Wife of David Prentis , Maketh Oath & saith that her said Husband about Nine Months ago in which Hired an House in Char Court Ship Yard in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the yearly Rent of £12 (except an allowance thereout of £1:7s is yearly being the Land Tax assessed on the said House) that they have Resided in the same ever since & still continue so to do

Sworn the 17th. March 1791 . before Me}

Martha Prentis 's Mark
