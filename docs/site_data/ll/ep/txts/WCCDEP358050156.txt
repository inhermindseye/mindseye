Westmr.  Eliza: Leopard  Wido . of John Leopard upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said John Leopard at the Fleet London upwds. of 20 Yrs. since and that her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Thomas Bond at the Golden heart in Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of Panor as in the County of Middx & Coachmaker and that neither her sd. late husband nor their Examinant since his Decease have rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other places to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 29th. 1749 before} Thos Lane

The mark of Eliz Leopard
