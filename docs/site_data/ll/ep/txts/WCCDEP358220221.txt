Westmr.  Grace Brown Aged about fifty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of James Brown who has been dead about Nine Years Says that being a Widow she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Cornelius Marwood late of Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Dealer in Coals for One Year and Three Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago when her said Master died and after she quitted her said Service she never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th. day of January 1770 before} R. Butler

her Grace Brown mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
