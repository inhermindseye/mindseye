Middx  Richard Jones aged about 68 - years upon Oath says he was married at the Fleet London upwards of 42 years ago to his Wife Jane by whom he has a Daughter named Sarah aged about 21 years who never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one year nor ever Rented ten pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief Says he was bound Apprentice to his Father Richd. Jones  Senr . & lived with him as his Apprentice in Tothill Street in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster for two years or thereabouts and then his Father absconded and this Examinant says he never was Assigned ever nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a year  paid parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 10th. Day of April 1756 : before} Fran: Bedwell  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Richd. Jones
