Middx  Eliz Medcalf Wife of John Medcalf (gone from her) upon Oath saith she inter- married with her sd. husband at the Fleet London about Ten Years ago by whom she hath two Eliz aged upwds. of Eight yrs. Childn. Wm. aged near 7 Yrs. Says her sd. husband whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Cooper at the White Lyon in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx at the yearly wages of £5 or thereabouts for the Space of three Years and has not Since she Quitted the sd. Service

rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 24dth 1750 Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Elis Eliz. Medcalf
