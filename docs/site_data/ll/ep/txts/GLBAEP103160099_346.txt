Middx ss}  Sarah Lanyon  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Parents have informed her and which She believes to be true that they were both born in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and this Deponent can give no other Account respecting their Settlement That She this Deponent was born in Sun Yard in the Said Parish & never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married Nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

Sarah Lanyon
