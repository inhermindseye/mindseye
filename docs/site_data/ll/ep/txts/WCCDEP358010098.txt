Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Eleanor Sisum aged about 64 years upon her Oath saith that about 38 years ago she Intermarried with Walter Sisum her late Husband (who has been dead upwards of 20 years ) at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Brokers Alley Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for 3 years & upwards at the yearly Rent of £14 that he had quitted said House about 5 years before his  Death during which time he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath this Ext. since her said late Husbands death done any act whereby she might have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of May 1787  James Croft  Jno. Barnfather

Ellen Sisum
