Middx  Mary Woodward aged abt. 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year that her father Isaac Woodward now lives in a house of his own in the parish of Woodford in the County of Essex and has paid Parochial Taxes for the same

Sworn 30th. Septr. 1754 before}
