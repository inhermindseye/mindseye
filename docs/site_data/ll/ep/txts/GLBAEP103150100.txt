Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Trahern  Single woman maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one Mathews who kept the Sun Alehouse in Sun Yard in the parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middx & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one Year & half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & ten Shillings & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor hath She ever been married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Eleanor Trahern
