Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Mary Ann Rawlins aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Rawlins her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Anns Soho that she hath Four Children living vizt. Mary Ann aged about 13 years Harriott aged about 9 years Elizth. aged about 8 years and Charlotte aged about 4 Months That her said Husband Rented Two Parlours of Mr. Ta affe in Denzell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Nine Months at the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas that he hath quitted said Tenements about 3 years ago since which time her said husband has not rented any House Tenement or Lodging of the yearly Bend or Value of £10 paid Taxes or done any act matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement and further this Examinant saith that her said Husband has deserted her & Children and refuses to allow them any supported whereby they are now Chargeable on the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 3d. day of Janry. 1788  Edward Hodsoll

her Mary Ann Rawlins Mark
