Middlesex ss}  Ann Leeming a Singlewoman Maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant with George Butt who then kept the Sign of the Bell Alehouse in Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Bridget otherwise St. Bridges in the City of London and Continued in Such Service for the Space of two years & an half at the yearly wages of Five Pounds & by Means thereof as She is advised She gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor to her knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 21st. Day of October 1772 before B Camper Rob Pell

The Mark of Ann Leeming
