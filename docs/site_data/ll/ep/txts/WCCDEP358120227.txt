Middx  Sarah French on behalf of Prudence Dixon aged abt. 8 Years Daughter of Fras. Dixon by Prudence his wife deceased upon her Oath saith she has been Informed & verily believes the said Fras Dixon about three Years ago took a house opposite the Red bull in little St Martins Lane in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. at the yearly Rent of £16 one that he lived therein near two Years and has not since he quitted the same rented ten pounds by the year nor pd parish taxes

Sworn Novr. 14th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Frnch
