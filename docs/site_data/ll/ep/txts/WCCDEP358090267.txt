Middx  to wit Henriett Marsden Aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father Henry Marsden before his Intermarriage with her Mother and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Lady Conisby in Hill Street Berkley Square for One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five pounds and upwards hath quitted his said Service about Thirty Years ago since which time he gained
