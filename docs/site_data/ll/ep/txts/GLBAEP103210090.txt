Middlesex ss}  Mary Thompson the widow of Thomas Thompson deced maketh oath that since her said husband death she this Deponent (having no Child or Children) was hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by & with Elizabeth Webb in Camomile Street in the parish of Allhallows London Wall in the City of London & Continued in such Service there under such hiring for about the Space of one Year and five Months & then this Deponent removed with her said Mistress into Fore Street in the parish of Saint Giles without Cripplegate London where she Continued in the service of her said Mistress for the space of four Months at the least & where her said service ended That she has not since lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year elsewhere or been married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 5. day of August 1785 before R Davies J Hawley

The Mark of Mary Thompson
