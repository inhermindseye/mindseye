Middlesex ss}  Ann Mandlin  Widow maketh oath that Sometime ago she was hired & lived servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with David Watson of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Victualler Continued in such service for the Space of two years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year nor been Since married or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 11th day of July 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Mandlin
