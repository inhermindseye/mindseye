Middx  Roger Russell upon Oath saith he was bound apprentice to Fras. Window a Taylor & lived with him near 4 Years & was then assign'd over to John Stout a Taylor with whom he lived as his apprentice the remaining 3 years of his time in Grt. Tower Lane in the parish of St. John Baptist in Bristol and that he never rented ten pounds by the year pd. parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn 22d. July 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Roger Russell
