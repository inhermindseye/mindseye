Middx  Richd. Barther upon his Oath saith that abt. Ten Years ago he lived in & rented a House in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for the Space of Six Years & upwds- & further saith that he hath not since he left the sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that being Poor he is not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}

The Mark of Richd. Barther
