Middlesex ss}  Mary Wilson the Wife of Edmund Wilson mariner who is now absent from her at Sea maketh oath that she hath been informed by her Said husband & which information she believes to be true That her Said husband was duly bound to & served the whole of his apprenticeship with one John Smith in the Parish of East Greenwich in the County of Kent  waterman & that by means thereof he gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponts. knowledge or belief her Said husband hath not since done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere And that she hath two children namely Francis aged about Ten years & Edmund Aged about Eight years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband The which Children have done no Act to gain a settlement for themselves

Sworn this 9th. day of February 1773 before B Camber Rob Pell

The Mark of Mary Wilson .
