Westmr ss  Mary Barnes Aged about 24 Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Thos Greenwood a Drugist On Ludgate Hill in the Parish of St Martin Ludgate London upward of one Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath Quitted her said Service about Two Years Ago & hath not Since she Quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise Gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of Augt- 1774 before R. Butler William Addington

her Mary Barnes Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
