Middlesex ss}  Ann Coggin  Widow of Charles Coggin deced maketh oath That about thirty years ago her said late husband lived in & rented a house at the yearly rent of fourteen pounds the Sign of the Two Brewers in upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & resided thereon for about one year & five Moths That her said husband did not afterward rent a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten pounds or Serve any Parish office or pay any poors rates or Kings Taxes or do any act to gain a Subsequent settlement That She was lawfully married to her said husband & since his death hath not been married again or lived as a hired Servant for a year or done any act where by to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 14 day of Novemr 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Coggin
