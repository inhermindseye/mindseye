Middx & Westmr} to wit Ann Burn Aged about 36 Years upon her Oath Says She is the Wife of Robert Burn with whom She Intermarried in the Parish Church of St Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex abt Eleven Years ago by whom She had Sevl Children all of whom are dead except one Named Elizth. Aged abt. Ten Years Says her said husband is a Native of Scotland but of what part this Examinant does not know Says her said Husband never gained any Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief That he said Husband has been gone from her about 6 years and is now in Norfolk in Virgina as She this Examinant has been Informed and verily believes the same to be true Says before her said Intermarriage with the said Robert Burn and whilst She was a Singlewoman She lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mrs Le Ploss who kept a Boarding School in the Parish of St Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds and that after She quitted sd Mrs. Le Ploss's Service never Rented a Tenemt. of Ten pounds a Year paid any Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one year with any one Else

Swon the 10th Day of Novr. 1768 Before Tho Lane  Peter Blanck

Ann Burn
