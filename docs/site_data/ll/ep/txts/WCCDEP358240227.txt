St Clement Danes 6th March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  Children  (to wit) Elizabeth Stafford  Widow aged 55 years and upwards upon her Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband George Stafford at the parish Church of Stonehouse near Plymouth in the County of Devon on or about the 11th. day of June April in or about the year of our Lord 1753. (by Banns) that since their said Marriage her said Husband Rented & resided in an Apartment unfurnished consisting of two Rooms on the first Floor at the Annual Rent of £10 for two years and upwards at Mr Allen's in Vere Street  St Clement Danes that they quitted the same about 10 years ago and her Husband died on or about the 26th day of July 1787. never having afterwards Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or done any other Act whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And that She this Examinant hath never since been Married or other otherwise obtained or become intitled unto any subsequent legal Settlement in her own Right that she hath three Children now living who provide for themselves the two eldest are Daughters and Married the Youngest Named Nathaniel is now in Wych Street and hath never acquired any legal Settlement in his own Right is about 25 years of Age

Sworn the 6th day of March 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Stafford
