Middx ss  Elizabeth Evans  Singlewoman Maketh oath that about eleven years ago she lived as a hired servant with Mr. John Renderson in savage Gardens in the parish of St. Olave Hart Street in the City of London and Continued in such Service above a year at the yearly wages of five pounds a year & upwards And by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt. there And this Depont. saith that she never was Married nor never Rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards nor never been an hired servant since nor done any act whereby to gain any Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 6th. day of Novr. 1754 before us  Richd Ricards  Chris. Scott

Elizabeth Evans  his Mark
