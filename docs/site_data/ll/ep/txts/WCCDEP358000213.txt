St. Clemt. Danes

Elizabeth Bellows aged 26 Years or thereabout when her Oath Saith That she never was Married Rented a House or paid Taxes over lived as an hired Yearly Servant or ever served as Apprenticeship Saith that her father John Bellows and her Mother Blewitt Bellows were Married at Saint Supulchre in the City of London that her father before his Intermarriage lived with one Mr. Carr of Charter House Square  4 as a lived Yearly Servant in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre in the City of London as a hired Yearly servant for upwards of three Years and Received Wages for the gaine that her said father  has been dead Some Years & died in the Workhouse belongg. to the said Parish that she knows not of any Subsequent her father ever gained nor has she this Examinant ever to the best of her knowledge gained any Settlemt in her own Right and that she was pass'd from the Parish of Sr. Siles in the fields  in to the said Parish of Saint Sepulchres abr. 4 Years ago

Sworn this 24th day of Janry. 1785 before me  Jno. Machin  John Croft

her Elizabeth Bellows mark
