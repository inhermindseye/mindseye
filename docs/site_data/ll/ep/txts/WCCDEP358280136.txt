Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Greenall aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Ward in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds that she has quitted said Service about 12 Months ago since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examt. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish That Thos. Kean a Milkman who lives in Ship Yard is the Father of the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body for Twelve Months last past and begotten thereon the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Thos. Kean is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 20th. Decr. 1787 before me} R Butler

her Mary Greenall Mark
