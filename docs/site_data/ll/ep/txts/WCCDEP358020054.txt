Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Isabella Briggs aged about 56 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with William Briggs her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint John Westminster that he has been dead about 10 days that her said late Husband Rented a Shop and Three Rooms of Mr. Savage situate the Corner of Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds Twelve Shillings that had quitted same about Sixteen Twelve years since which time he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Janry. 1788  Wm. Achiner

Isabella Briggs
