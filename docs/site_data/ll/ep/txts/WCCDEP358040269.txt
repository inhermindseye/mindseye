Westmr. 

Mary Roberts aged upwds. of 23 Years removed from she Parish of St. Andw. Holborn above Barrsby Order dated the 22d. Instant upon Oath Saith she Never was married and that upwards of two Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Biggs a Haberdasher of Smallwares in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Libty of Westmr. for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £4..10..0 and has not since She quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 24th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Mary Roberts
