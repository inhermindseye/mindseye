Middlesex ss}  Barbara Bellward the Widow of Waster Bellward deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprentice ship with Mr Chandler a Butcher in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement That She this Deponent Since his death was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Abedwards Victuallr in the Same Parish & Continued in such service for divers Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there for herself & has not since done any Act to gain another settlement

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Barbara Bellward
