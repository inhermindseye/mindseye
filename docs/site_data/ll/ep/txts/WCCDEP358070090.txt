Westmr. to wit Susan Jones Aged about Fifty One Years on behalf of Mary Rowland the Wife of Thomas Rowland who now liks Ill of a Fever on her Oath Says that the said Thomas Rowland Rented and lived in a House in Red Lyon Street Clerkenwell in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds as she believes and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Six Years ago & hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1773 before Us R. Butler Sampn Wright

her Susan Jones Mark
