Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Brunsen aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Myers a Salesman in Bridges Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for Thirteen Months at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said serve about Five Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 30th- day of June 1796 } N Bond Rusl Clarke

her Sarah Brunsen Mark
