St Clement Danes May 11th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Bride London  (to wit) Mary Gray  singlewoman aged 43 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr.. Boughton in Fleet Market at No 72 in the parish of St Bridget otherwise St Bride in the City London for a year and upwards at the Annual Wages of four pounds that She quitted that Service about two years ago and hath not Since obtained any other legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 11th day of May 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker Mary Gray
