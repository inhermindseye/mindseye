Middlesex to wit Ann Hoare Aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for One year Says she was born in the City of Dublin in Ireland and never gained any or. Settlement in England And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 28th. day of July 1774 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. Mogeridge in Stanhope Street Clare Market in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes which was soon afterwards Baptized by the Name of Jeremiah and is become Chargable to the same she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same Says that one Charles Fonville a Jeweller who lodges the next Door to a Bakers in Cursitors Street Chancery Lane did beget such Child on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body for several years last past and that the said Charles Fonville and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 27th. day of February 1776 before}

Ann Hoare
