Middlesex ss}  Alice Story the Wife of Thomas Story who is now at Kingston in Jamaica as she this Deponent believes maketh oath That divers Years ago her said husband lived as an Apprentice With Veritas Humphrys in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex to be taught by him the Trade of a Gun Smith Which the Said Humphrys then & there carried on That her Said husband Continued with him for that purpose for the Space of Six Years & upwards but was never bound by Indenture or other Writing or Instrument to the said Humphrys as he has informed this Deponent & as this Deponent Verily believes That She this Deponent can give no other Account respecting the Settlement of her said husband nor Can She give any Account respecting the Settlement of his Parents or either of them That this Deponents Father Adam Beale Several years ago Rented a House in Upper Eastsmithfield in the said parish Where he Carried on the Trade of a Taylor & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there at Which time this Deponent was a Single woman & lived with him appart of his Family That she this Deponent Went afterwards with her said Father as part of his Family to the parish of Bromley near Bow in the County of Middlesex Where he lived as a Lodger for near two Years but did not gain any Settlement there, in the Space of Which time she this Deponent was married aged 17 Years & Some the & Thomas aged about Six Years their lawful Issue to her said husband there by whom She death 2 Child Pen namely James

Sworn this 25th day of October 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Alice Story
