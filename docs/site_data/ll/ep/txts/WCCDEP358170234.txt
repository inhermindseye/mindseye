Middx  Adam Morgan aged 55 Yrs. upon his Oath saith that he never kept House or paid Parish Taxes but that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Henry Harper  Locksmith in the Parish of St. Mary in the City of Kilkenny in the County of Kilkenny in the Kingdom of Ireland & hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship abt. 32 Yrs.

Sworne the 25th. May 1742 before}

The Mark of Adam Morgan
