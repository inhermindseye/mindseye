Westminster to wit Elizth. Kelly Aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she was bound an Apprentice to and served three Years of her Apprenticeship to one Mrs. Willington a Mantuamaker in Litchfield Street in the Parish of Saint Ann in the Liberty of Westmr . has left her said Mistress about Three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 2d. day of July 1778 before  James Paine  James Croft

her Elizth. Kelly Mark
