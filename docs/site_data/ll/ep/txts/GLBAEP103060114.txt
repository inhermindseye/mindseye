Middx ss}  The Examination of Ann Jones  Singlewoman taken on Oath before us Two of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the county of Middx (whereof one is of Quorum) and both residing in or next to the Limits of the parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County this Twenty Second-Day of May in the Year of our Lord 1749 .

This Examinant maketh oath and Saith that about eleven Months ago one John Martin of the parish of St. Mary Hill London Waterman did prevail with this Examinant to suffer him to lie with her at ye house of Margaret Lewis in the parish of Saint John Wapping Middlesex and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant body Several Times whereby she conceived with Child of a Male Bastard Child of which She was delivered at the workhouse of the said parish of St Botolph without Aldgate on the eighteenth Day of April now last past which said Male bastard Child hath been baptised by the Name of John and hath been now is and still is likely to be chargeable to the Said, parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate this Examinant being poor & not able to provide for the same And this Examinant further Saith that the said John Martin is the true and only father of the said Male bastard Child and that this Examination is voluntary and that she hath not been forced or compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 22th Day of May 1749 before  Richd Ricards  Sam Gower

The mark of Ann Jones
