Middx  Ann Robertson aged about 53 Years upon her Oath saith she is the Wido. of George Robertson who hath been dead about 11 Years and that upwards of House Years since being at that time a Widow & without Child or Children she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Burrows in Chichesters Rents in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of one Year & upwards & hath not since she left the sd. Mrs. Burrows Service rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt: for one Year or othewise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 4th. 1744 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Ann Robertson
