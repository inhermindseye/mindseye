Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Turnbull aged about 67 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr Law in Cumberland Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Five one year and upwards at the yearly wages of Five pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Nine years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 22d. day of Decr. 1796 } Saml. Edward

Margt. Turnbull her mark
