Middx  Joan Hartstone aged about 71 Years upon Oath Says She is the Widow of Benjamin Hartstone who has been Dead about Twenty one Years Says her said Husband Served his Apprenticeship with one Harvey Aspree a Shoemaker in the Parish of Uttoxeter in the County of Stafford Says her said Husband never Served any Parish Office or paid Taxes of any kind whatsoever neither hath She since his Death rented any House or paid any Taxes

Sworn the 17th. day of Septr. 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Joan Hartstone
