Swears to St. Clemt. T  John Marshall aged forty Eight Years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he never rented any House Tenement or Lodging of the Value of ten Pounds a year, Lived as an hired Yearly Servant for the space of one Year, Served as an Apprentice, or did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to gain any Settlement of his own says his Father was a Water Gilder by Trade and served his Apprenticeship to Mr. Brooks in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex under Indentures of Apprenticeship duly Executed and was as this Examinant believes really married to this Examinants Mother

Sworn this 16th Decr. 1784 before R. Butler

his John Marshall mark
