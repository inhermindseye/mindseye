Middlesex ss}  Margaret Stedman  widow maketh oath That She well knows a Girl named Sarah Teague aged about Eleven years She which Child as this Depont has been informed and believes is the Danr of one Basil Teague by Sarah  his wife both deced That the Said Basil Teague as this Depont has been also informed and believes was born in Angel Court in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & Served an Apprenticeship with his Father in the Sd Parish & this Depont can give no othr Account respectg. the Sd Basil Teague s Settlement That the Said Child Sarah Teague was as this Depont. has been informed & believes born in Angel Court aforesaid & never did any Act to this Deponts knowlege or belief to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 26. day of June 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Margaret Stedman
