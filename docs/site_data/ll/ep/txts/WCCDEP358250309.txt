Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Hall aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that about Eleven years ago she Intermarried with Richard Hall her late Husband who has been dead about Six years at the Parish Church of Saint Katherines by the Tower by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Ann aged. about Seven years That her said late Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. King a Butcher in the Borough in the Parish of Saint Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Septr. 1797 before us J Hunter J. Henderson

Mary Hall
