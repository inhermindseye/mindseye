Middx  John Barnacle upon Oath saith he never was married and that he lived a yearly Servant with Jno Hines in the Parish of Covington in the County of Warwick for one Year at the yearly Wages of £5 quitted his sd. Service at Michs. last & has not since rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn Feby. 1st. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of John Barnacle
