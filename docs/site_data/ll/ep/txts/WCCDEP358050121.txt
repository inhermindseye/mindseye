Westmr.  Mary Ellitt upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Robt. Ellitt who dyed abt. two Years since and that the said Robt. Ellitt whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Warwick at the Cart and Horses at Essex Stairs in the Parish of St Clemt Danes  Middlesex for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds Dyet washing and Lodging and that her sd. late husband or this Examint. since his Decease never rented Ten pounds Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Octr. 24th. 1749 before}
