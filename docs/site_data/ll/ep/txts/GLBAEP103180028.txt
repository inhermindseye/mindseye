Middlesex ss}  Sarah Bowyer the wife of Thomas Bowyer  waterman maketh oath That her Said husband is lately gone away from her to places unknown & left her destitute of Support That She has been informed by her Said husband & which She believes to be true That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to his Grandfathr. Thomas Fowles of Water Lane in the Parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surrey  Waterman with Whom he Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship in the Said Parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he never (to her Knowledge or belief) rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish Office or ever paid any poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship That She was lawfully married to her Said husband by Whom She hath a Child now living named Rosamond Aged about Sixteen Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband.

Sworn this 24 day of March 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Sarah Bowyer

Note the Pauper being removed by Wapping to Bermondsey in June 1777 deposed as follows viz That abt 3 years ago She was Married to one Thos Bowyer That her husband was bound Apprentice to David Ward Hall  of St Dunstan in the East Londn Waterman for 7 yrs. that he duly Servd all his Apprenticesp but during all the time lodged in the house of his Grandfar. Thomas Fowles in Water Lane Bermondsey Parish.
