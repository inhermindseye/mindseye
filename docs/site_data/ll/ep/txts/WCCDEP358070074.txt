Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Crossley Aged about Sixty One Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Joseph Crossley who has been dead about Fifteen Years and with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London in the Year 1752 Says her said late Husband served Six Years apprenticeship with one Edward Edwards a Gilder in New Street fetter Lane in the Parish of Saint Brides otherwise Bridget and the said Edward Edwards the Master dying was duly discharged from his apprenticeship and after her said Husband was discharged he never Rented House or paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said late Husbands Death

Sworn this 5th. day of March 1773 before Us W Kelyngs Edwd. Bindloss

her Elizth. Crossley Mark

Witness E: Fields
