Middx.  Wm. Salter aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in Bell Yard in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westminster for 3 Years at the Yearly Rent of Nine Pounds and paid the Poors Rate for said house and hath Quitted same about Ten years and further saith that sometime in the Month of March 1758 he was Removed by an Order under the hands and seals of two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the City and Liberty of Westmr . from the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the said Liberty of Westmr . to the said Parish of St. Margaret as the Place of his last legal Settlement which Order was not Appealed against.

Sworn this 30th. April 1760 before} Tho Lane . Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Wm. Salter
