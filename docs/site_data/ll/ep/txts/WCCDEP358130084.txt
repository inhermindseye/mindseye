Middx  Ann Mogridge aged about Twenty four Years upon Oath Says she never was Married and that she never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for One Year, Says that she is the Daughter of Timothy Mogridge by Mary his Wife both deceas'd to whom a Certificate was granted by the Churchwardens and Overseers of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx acknowledging them Inhabitants Legally Settled in the said Parish.

Sworn the Day of Novr. 1755 before}
