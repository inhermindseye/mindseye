Middx & Westmr-}  James Ewer aged about 52 fifty two Years on his Oath says he never was Interr Married with Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Olaves Southwark in the Year One thousand seven hundred and seventy three and by whom he has two Children now living namely John aged upwards of three Years and James aged upwards of one Year Says before his Intermarr. with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Drummond a Tobacconist in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for upwards of three Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds besides Bed and Board hath quitted his said Service upwards of thirty two Years ago since which time he hath never gained any other subsequent Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 10th. Day of July 1777 before me  James Croft

his James Ewer Mark
