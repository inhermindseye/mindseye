Middx  The Examination of Sarah Sharp  Wife of William Sharp a Vagabond Apprehended in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and brought before me One of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the County of Middlesex aforesaid And taken before me this 27th. Day of November in the Year of our Lord 1744

This Examinat on her Oath saith that she Intermarried with the sd. Wm. Sharp in the Parish Church of Mungen near Deal upwards of four Years since Says her sd. Husband is a Native of Ireland & never gained any Settlement in England & is now a Soldier in his Majestys Service in Flanders Says she was born in the Parish of Deal where her Father rented a house & paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes before this Examinants Intermarriage with the sd. Wm. Sharp Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath one Child (to wit) Ann Aged about Seventeen Months

Sworn the Day & Year abovemenconed before} Tho Lane

Sarah Sharp
