Parish Aldgate Middx

Wm Sampson  Labourer Maketh Oath that when he was a Single person he was a hired Servant by the year and livd two years as Such at Six pounds a year with Nicholas Baker a Farmer at Peckham Rye in the Parish of Camberwell in the County of Surry and hath gaind no Settlement Since hath a wife  Elizabeth

Sworn 18th day of May 1748 before us Robert Dennett Richd: Ricards

the mark of Wm. Sampson
