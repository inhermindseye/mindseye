Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth West  Singlewoman maketh oath that She well knows & a Child named John West Aged about eight years & eight Months which was born of the Body of Mary West this Deponents Mother in the Prison of Newgate in the Parish of St Sepulchre in the City of London The which Child is illegitimate & Bastard by reason that this Deponents Mother was never Married to the Said Childs Father to this Deponts Knowledge or belief & which Child has not as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement for himself.

Sworn this 16. day of March 1786 before R Davies Robt. Smith

Elizabeth West
