Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bunyan the wife of Richard Bunyan maketh oath that her Said husband is now on board his Majesties Ship Dunkirk in his Majesties Service That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticesp with one Richard Crawley a Turner & Chairmaker in the Parish of Doncaster in the County of York & has not Since to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 12. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Bunyan
