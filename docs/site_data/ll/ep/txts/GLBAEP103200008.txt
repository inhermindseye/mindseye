Middlesex  Ann Barber  singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore daily bound Apprentice by Indre by the Parish officers of the Parish of St John Wapping in the sd County to Thomas Morris of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County sailmake r untill she shod Attain her age of Twenty one years that this Deponent duly Served the whole of her said Apprenticeship in the said Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate & allways lodged there in her Said Masters house That since the expiration of such her Apprenticeship she has not been married or lived as a hired servant for the space of a year or done any Act to gain anr settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of June 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Barber
