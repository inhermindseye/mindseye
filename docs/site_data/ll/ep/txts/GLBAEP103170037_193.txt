Middlesex ss  Mary Hunt the wife of Abraham Hunt maketh oath that her Said husband is now Absent from her & is lately gone on a voyage to Philadelphia That about fourteen years ago her Sd husband took & rented a house known by the Sign of the Ship in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at they Carly rent of Sixteen Guincas and hired there in for about the Space of Seven years & three months & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That he he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a Child named John Aged about eleven years the lawful issue of this Depont by her Sd husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this 26. day of Decemr 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Hunt
