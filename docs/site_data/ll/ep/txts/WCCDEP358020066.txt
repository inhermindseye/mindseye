Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Maydman aged about 34 years upon his Oath saith that she never wa he served Seven years Apprenticeship to Mr. Langley in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex Stationer that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. 2nd. day of April 1788  Sampr Wright N Bond

James Maydman
