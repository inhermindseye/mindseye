Middlesex ss}  Jane Cross the Wife of Richard Cross maketh oath That She well knows a Child named Thomas Careless Aged about Ten Years the Son of one Mary Careless deced The Which Child was born in Crown Court in Whilep Yard in the parish of St Mary White chapel in the County of Middlesex this Deponent being present at the Birth of Such Child That Such Child is likely to become Chargeable to the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & this Deponent can give no Account respecting the Settlement of the Father or Mother of the Said Child or otherwise.

Sworn this 9th. day of October 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith  Wm. Blackmore

The Mark of Jane Cross
