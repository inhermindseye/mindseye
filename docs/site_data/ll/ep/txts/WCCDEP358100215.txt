Middx & Westmr}  Hannah Wells Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Robert Smith an Attorney in Lyons Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Years ago since which time she never lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year or ever gained any other Settlement than as aforesd And further Says that on the 25th. day of April last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the aforesaid Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the name of Miles Wells And is now Chargable to the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes and likely to Continue so And further Says that One Miles Jackson  Esqr . who lodges at Mr. Sedgleys a Taylor in Maiden Lane  Covent Garden is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his Lodging at Briththelmstone some time in the Month of July last but the particular day she does not remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Miles Jackson and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 22d day of May 1784 before Us  Sampn Wright W Addington

Hannah Wells
