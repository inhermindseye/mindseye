Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Mary Purdy aged about 29 Years upon her Oath saith that about 7 years ago she Intermarried with John Purdey  aged about her Prest husband at the Parish Church of Wigton in Cumberland and by whom she hath 3 Children living Vizt. Francis aged about 6 Years Joseph aged about 3 Years and Mary aged about 10 Months that her said Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship to a Mr. a Taylor at Copy in Cumberland in the Parish of Boomfield in the said County Husband  out of his said Time her never did any Act that after this Depo Ext Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. saith that her sd. Husband has run away and left he in the said Parish of Saint Clement Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Ex

Danes whereby she and her Children is become Chargeable to the same

Sworn this 25th. Septr. 1786 before R Butler

her Mary Purdey Mark
