Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Palmer aged about 49 years upon her Oath saith that about 27 years ago upon her she Intermarried with Charles Palmer her late husband who has been dead about Three years at the Parish Church of Harwick in the County of Essex That her said late husband Rented and lived in a house in Mass head Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Ten Months at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and that after he quitted said house he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Decr. 1796 } Saml. Edward

Ann Palmer her Mark
