Westmr.  John Bailey aged about 65 Years upon Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to John Hayward  Hatmaker & lived one Years & upwards the Latter Part of his Apprentiship with the sd. John Hayward in little Tower Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the East London Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 3d. 1746 before  Tho Lane

John Bailey
