Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Emery aged about 41 years upon her Oath saith that about 15 years ago she Intermarried with John Emery at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch That her said Husband served Seven years apprenticeship  to one his Father George Emery a Shoemaker in Arlbemarle Street in the Parish of Saint James at Clerkenwell in the County of middlesex  for  and that after he quitted said was out of his said apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of March 1790 R Butler D Walker

her Elizabeth Emery Mark
