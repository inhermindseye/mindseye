Middx  Esther Wills  Wido . of Danl Wills who dyed about one Year since upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Danl Wills at the Fleet London about 23 Years ago and that her sd husband before marriage lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Mitchell a Hosier in Butcher Row in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of 3 Yrs. or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £8 Dyet Washing and Lodging and that neither her sd. late husband after he quitted his sd. Service nor this Examinant since his Decease have rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 7th. 1750 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Esther Wills
