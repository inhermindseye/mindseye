Middx  Elizth. Porter Aged about Twenty Six Years upon Oath Says she was Never Marryed Says she was Bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Leadbeter an Embroiderer and Served the two Years & upwards the last part of her Apprentiship with the said Mr. Leadbeter in Hollis Street Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 16th Day of October 1764 Before} Benjamin Cox

Elizth Porter
