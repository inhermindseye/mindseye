Middx  James Forster aged about Thirty Six Years a Foot Soldier in the Third Re- -giment of Foot Quards upon Oath Says he was born in the Shire of Angus in Scotland & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. Says he was married at the Fleet London upwards of four Years since to Ann his Wife who is since Dead by whom he hath one Child Ann aged upwards of Three Years, born in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex .

Sworn the 29th. Day of May 1756 . before}

James Foster
