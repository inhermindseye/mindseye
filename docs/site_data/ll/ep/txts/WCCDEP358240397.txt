St Clement Danes 5th April 1794

Middlesex  St. George Hanover Square  (to wit) Mary Green  singlewoman aged about seventy seven years Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Miss Jackson in George Street in the parish of St George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for about twenty years at the annual Wages of £7..7s..0 that She quitted such Service about fifteen years ago and hath not acquired any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 5th. day of April 1794 before us}

Mary Green
