Middlesex ss}  George Cooper the Son of Charles Cooper Bricklayer deced maketh oath that he has been informed by his Mother Ann Cooper that this Deponents said Father was bound to & served his Apprenticeship with one First & Ann a Bricklayer in Red maid Lane in the parish of Saint George in the County of Middx which information this Deponent believes to be true & this Deponent Can't Set forth or discover whether his said Father ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt Subsequent to his Apprenticeship That he this Deponent was born in the parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex & never did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself in his own right That about Twenty eight Years ago he was married at the Fleet to his late Wife Elizabeth Cooper who lately died in the Workhouse of the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex by Whom he hath two Children now living namely Richard Cooper Aged Four Years & Joseph Cooper aged about Twelve months.

Sworn this 3d day of March 1775 before  Jno Sherwood B. Camper

The Mark of George Cooper .

the above named George Cooper  Died in the Wh house St Mary Whiterley  Decr. 6th. 1816 . Aged One Hundred.
