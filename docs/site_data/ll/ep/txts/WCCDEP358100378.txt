Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  he taken & took Child to wit Ann Lochwood aged about 28 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Thos. Plimpton of the Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for 13 Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas hath quitted her said Service upwards of fourteen Months since which time she has gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Child which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further says that one Robert Mingay & Currier who lately lodged in White Cross Street had Carnal knowledge of her Body at their Lodgings in Goswell Street sometime in the Month of July last and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Robert Mingay and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof Not told where the father now lives

Sworn this 29th day of March 1785 R Butler

Ann Lochwood
