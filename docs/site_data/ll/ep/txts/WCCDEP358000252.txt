St.  Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Elizth. Pettey aged about 56 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Napper a Mantua Maker in Church Street Soho in the parish of Saint Anns Soho in the County of Middx for 15 Months or thereabouts at the yearly wages of four pounds hath quitted said Service about twelve Months ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Depts. knowledge Information on belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of March 1786 before  Thos: Gordon  Thos: Parker

Elizabeth Pettey
