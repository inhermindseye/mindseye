Middx.  Henrietta Hall aged abt. 61 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Richd. Hall who has been dead abt. Seven Years; says her said husband Rented a house in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds or thereabouts & lived therein about two Years and after he quitted said house never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says she has not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year Says she has no family and is now in the Iron mongers Almshouses

Sworn this 20th. day of June 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Henritta Hall
