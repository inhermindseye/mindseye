Middx & Westmr-}  Wm- Masters aged about 40 Years on his Oath says he Intermarrd- to his late Wife  Sarah Masters at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn about fifteen Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children now living namely William aged about fourteen yrs. and Sarah aged about twelve now both of whom now Provided for Says he served his Apprenticeship with one George Halla Button Maker in Three Fox Court Long Lane Smith field in the Parish of St- Sepulchre without in the Co- of Middx and after he was out of his Apprenticesp he never Rented house paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a subseqt- Settlemt-

Sworn this 24th- Day of Octr- 1777 before.} R Butler

William Masters
