Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Esther Bury aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith that about Twenty three years ago she Intermarried with George her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Michael Royal in the City of London That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house at Hoxton in the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds that he hath quitted said house about Sixteen years since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And saith that she is poor & unable to maintain herself and is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 19th. day of July 1798 before us J. Reeves Saml. Edward

her Esther Bury mark

Witness A W Burbidge
