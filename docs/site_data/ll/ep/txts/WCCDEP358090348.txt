Middx to Wit Elizth. Duckworth Aged upwards of 19 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for One year Says she went to live wth. one Mr. Baxton a Musician in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and was to have 6d. P Week & afterwards her said Master Agreed to give her after the Rate of 1s..6d. P Week but never was hired by the year or did she ever live under at her Agreemt. for One year or in any other place whatever Says her Father Richd. Duckworth Rented & lived in a House in fore Street Limehouse Corner in the Parish of Limehouse in the County of Middlesex for sevl. Years at the yearly Rent of £10. upwards and pd. Parish Taxes for the same hath quitted said House about Nine Years ago and after the quitting the same He never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year paid Taxes or ever gained any other Settlemt. than as aforesaid.

Sworn this 28th. day of November 1782 before R Butler Sampn Wright

her Elizth. Duckworth Mark
