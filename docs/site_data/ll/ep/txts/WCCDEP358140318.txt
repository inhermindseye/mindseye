Middx.  Fras. Price aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath says that before he was married he lived and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Wheatley Tobacconist at the East End of the New Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for 3 Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of £11 and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes. says he has a Wife named Sarah aged abt. forty Years by whom he has two Children Elizth . aged Ten Years and Sarah aged Eight Years

Sworn this 20th. Novr. 1759. before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Fras. Price
