Westmr. to wit Andrew Durand aged about Twenty Eight Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Arm his late Wife Ann at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex about five Years ago and by whom he hath three Children now living Vizt. Andrew aged about three Years Judith Aged about one Years & an half & Mary Ann aged about fourteen Days Says he served 7 Years Apprenticeship with one Lazarus Desbois Cabinet and Clock base Maker in Monmouth Court Earl Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprentice- -ship he never Rented House or paid Taxes or done any other act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Augt. 1772 before Us} Tho Lane R. Butler

Andrew Durand
