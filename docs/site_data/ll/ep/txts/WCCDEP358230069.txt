Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman on behalf of Ann Hill who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith That he hath been informed which Information he Verily believes to be true that the said Ann Hill never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hill the Corner of Bear Alley in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre without in the City of London for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Nine years since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of June 1799 before us  Saml. Edwards Menter

B Newman
