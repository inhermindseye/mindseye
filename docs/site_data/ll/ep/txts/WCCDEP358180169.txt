Middx  Catherine Hand  Wido . aged about 70 Years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her late Husband Barnaby Hand in the parish Church of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex that he was a Native of Ireland & never gained any Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or beleif & hath been dead abt. 12 Years that before her inter- -marriage with the said Barnaby Hand she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Smith a Poulterer in Claremarkett in the parish of Saint Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of £4. for the Space of One Year and upwards that since she left the sd Service she hath not Rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 2d. March 1743 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Catherine Hand
