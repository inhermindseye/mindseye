Westmr. 

Danl. Copsey on behalf of James Mitchel an Inft. aged about two Years upon Oath saith he is Uncle by the mothers Side to the said Infant that he has been Informed and beleives his Sister Rebecca the Mother of the Sd. Infant Intermarried with Saml. Mitchel the sd. Infants father at the fleet London about four Years ago that the sd. Rebacca Mitchel is since dead and that as he hath been further Informed & beleives the sd. Saml. Mitchel the Infants father is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlemt. in England that his sd. Sister Rebecca Mitchell never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one year rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes that this Esamints. father Danl. Copsey the

sd. James Mitchel the Infants grandfather rented a Farm of £31 a Year for Six Years or thereabouts and dyed upwards of four Years since and that the house and barns belonging to the sd. Farm were in the Parish of Doddinghurst in the County of Essex where this Examinants said father and his family resided to the time of his Decease

Sworn Janry 16th. 1749 before}

Daniel Copsey
