Middx & Westmr.} to Wit Jane Morris Aged about 55 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired yearly Servt . with one Mr. Bulla in Thrift Street in the Parish of St. Ann Soho in the Liberty of Westmr . for 3 Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of 12 Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever lived a Hired Yearly Servt. for One Year or other gained any other Settlemt.

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Jane Morris Mark
