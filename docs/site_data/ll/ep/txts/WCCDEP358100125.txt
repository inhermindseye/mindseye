Middlesex to wit Catherine Snelling Aged about Thirty Three Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Snelling with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Hackney in the County of Middlesex in the Year 1766 and by whom she hath four Children living Namely Ann Aged about fifteen Years now provided for Susannah Aged about Ten Years James Aged about Eight Years and Elizabeth Aged about four Years Says her said late Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one John Winter a Coach Master in the Parish of Ealing in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds as she believes and after quitting his said Masters Service he never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d day of Septr. 1783 before  James Paine J Bosworth

Cuth. Snelling
