Middlesex ss}  Sophia Male the widow of John Male decd maketh oath That about four years Ago She rented a house at the yearly Rent of Five Pounds in Farthing Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to and Paid Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof as She is advised She gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish And that She has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of 1771 before}
