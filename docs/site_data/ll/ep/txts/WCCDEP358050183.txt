Westmr.  Abigail Pennell on behalf of her Son Henry Willoughby Pennell (a Sunatiak) upon Oath saith the said Henry Willoughby Pennell upwds. of Twelve months since Lived in and rented a house of the yearly Rent of £12 and upwds. in the Old Bailey in the Parish of St. Sepulchers London for three months or thereabouts and has not since he quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examints. 4 knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 2d. 1749 before H Feilding

Aigll Pennell
