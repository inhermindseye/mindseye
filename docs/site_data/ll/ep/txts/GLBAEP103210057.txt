Middlesex ss  Peter Crane maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired servant & with Thomas Hoskins in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Tobacco & Continued in such service under such hiring and Certain Wages for the Space of two years & half That he has not since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement.

Sworn this. 1st. day of February 1785 before R Smith

The Mark of  Peter Crane
