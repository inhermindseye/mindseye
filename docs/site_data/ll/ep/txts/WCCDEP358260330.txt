Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Burge aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Thomas No. 2 Burleigh Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the yearly wages of Five Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Septr. 1798 before me } W Carter

her Hannah Burge  Mark
