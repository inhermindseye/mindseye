Middx ss  Catherine Cole of the parish of st. Leonard shoreditch in the county of middx  spinster maketh oath that about five years ago as this Dept. best remembers she this Deponent was requested and desired by one Katherine Starks (but since Married as this Depont has heard and believes to one Alexander a Sailor) and which sd. Katherine starks then lived in the house of one Mrs Williams in Back Lane in the parish of st. George Middx to leave and Drop her Child Named

Edward Hardy then aged about two Years in Nightingale Lane in the sd. Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx with an intent that the same Child Might be provided for by the said Parish which this Deponent refused to do And this Deponent farther Saith that in the Night time of ye same day in which this Depont was desired to drop ye sd. Child as aforesd. this Dept. Saw thesd. Child Stand. at or near the Workhouse Door of ye sd. parish of Aldgate Crying for its Nurse And that Soon after the sd. Katherine Starks came into the sd Mrs. Williams's House where ship Dept. then also lived & Declared there to this Dept. that she had left & Drop't her sd. Child at the Workhouse Door of ye sd. Parish of Aldgate in which Workhouse the sd Child Now is & has ever Since been as this Dept. believes

Sworn the 4th. day of October 1751 before me  Richd. Ricards

The Mark of Catherine Cole
