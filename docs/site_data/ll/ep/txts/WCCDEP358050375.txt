Mary Richardson  Wido . of Christr. Richardson upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Christr Richardson on the 30th. of June 1745 at the Fleet London & that her sd. late husband dyed abt. 2 Years & 6 months since Says her sd. late husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £20 at the Corner of Clare Court in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and lived therein about two Years & has quitted the same about 14 Years, since which neither the sd. Christr Richardson in his Life time nor this Examinant since his Decease have rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish

Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief Says she hath one Child by her sd. late Husband Viz Martha aged upwds. of 4 Yr

Sworn Novr 26th. 1750 before}
