Middx  Margaret Koonart aged about 38 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried with her late Husband Joseph Koonart at the Parish Church of Saint Brides London about Twelve Years ago and who has been Dead upwards of one Year and a half and by whom she hath one Child now living Named Elizabeth Susannah Aged about Four Years says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in New Bedford Court Catherine Street in the Strand in the Parish of St Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of three Years and a half at the Yearly Rent of Fifteen Guineas and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Thirteen Months ago since which time this Examinant hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 4th. day of Febry 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Margret Koonart
