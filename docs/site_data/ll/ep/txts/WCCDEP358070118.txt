Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Porter aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or ever served any apprenticeship paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Charles Day lately deceased at the Sign of the Ship in Wych Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of four Months ago & hath since done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain she same when Born Says that one John Lawrence a Joiner and who lately worked with an Organ builder at the White Lyon in Wych Street had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her sd. late Masters house in Wych Street the Ninth day of House last and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid and that the said John Lawrence and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this Twenty Eighth day of July 1773 before}

Elizabeth Porter
