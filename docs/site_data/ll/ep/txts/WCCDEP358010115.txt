Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Mary Tool aged upwards of 29 years upon her Oath saith that on the day of May 17 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child (which hath since been Christened by the Name of John ) at the House of Mr. Jones a Publican in Red Lyon Street Holborn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holb above the Barrs in the County of Middx

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1787 Mr. Barnfather G Bradshaws

Mary Tool
