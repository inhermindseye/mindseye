Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit James Talboys One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he believes to be true that Mary an Infant aged about 2 Years born a Bastard on the Body of Mary Cunningham (who has deserted it) was born in the House of a Mr. Baddeley in Featherstone Street in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middx

Sworn this 1st Decr. 1786 -before us  Wm. Gascoignes Mr: Wright

James Talboys
