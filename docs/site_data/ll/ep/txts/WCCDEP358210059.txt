Westmr.  Mary Smith aged about 36 Years upon Oath, says she never was Married, says her father John Smith Rented and lived in a house in St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds in which he carried on the business of a Glazier for several Years, and there dyed and further upon her Oath says that she this Examinant never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, was an Apprentice, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year,

Sworn the 2d. July 1766 . before} Tho Lane  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Smith
