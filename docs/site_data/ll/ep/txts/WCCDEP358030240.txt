Middx  Eliza. Clement aged about 36 years upon her Oath Says she is a Singlewoman Says she lived a Yearly Servant with one Mr. Cole in Little Kirby Street in the Liberty of Saffron hill Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn in the County of Middlesex for three Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds & left the sd. Service about Eight Years Says she hath not since she left the said Mr. Coles Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 3d. 1746 before}

The Mark of Eliza. Clements
