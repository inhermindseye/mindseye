Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Matthew Jackson aged about 31 years upon his Oath saith that he intermarried Martha his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Sepulchres about Eight years ago and by whom he hath One Child living vizt. William aged about four years that he this Examinant served five years Apprenticeship to his Brother Edward Clarke in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre in the City of London that after he was out of his said Apprentiship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Febry. 1789  Sampn Wright N Forster

Mattw Jackson

Passed to wrong Settlemt. being St. Sepulchres without.
