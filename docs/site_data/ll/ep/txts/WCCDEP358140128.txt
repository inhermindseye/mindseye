Middx.  Rachel Legg aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of John Legg who has been dead abt. twenty Years says that upwards of 13 Years ago she being a Widow & without Child or Children lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stevens a School Master in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for one Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted said Service abt. twelve Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Yr. paid parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 10th. day of June 1758 before} Barth: Hammond

Rachel Legg
