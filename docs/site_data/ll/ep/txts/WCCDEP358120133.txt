Middx  Margt. Simpson upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of John Simpson (gone from her) to whom she was married at the Fleet London on the 11th. Decr. 1750 and by whom she has one Child Ann aged near 20 months and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd husband (whilst a Singleman) lived a yearly Servant wth. Coll Mading for the Space of three Years at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for the first Year & six pounds by the Year afterwards and lodged the last two months of his sd Service at his Masters Stables in Ham Yard in the Parish of St. Geo Hanr Square in the Liberty of Westmr. that he left Coll Madings Service abt. 6 Yrs. ago & has not since rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt (in any part of Great Britain) for one Year and this Examinant further maketh Oath that she was at the time of her marriage wth. the abovementioned John Simpson the Wido. of Thomas Harrison to whom she was married in Dublin about 20 Years ago, that the sd. Harrison rented a house in Hady Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. afsd of the Yearly Rent of £17

lived therein two or three Years & quitted the sd house upwds. of 10 Yrs. ago; that the sd. Tho Harrison never afterwards rented ten pounds by the year or pd. Parish taxes And that She has one Child by him now living (viz) Mark aged abt. 9 Yrs

Sworn 27th. June 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Margt Simpson
