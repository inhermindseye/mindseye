St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes abovementd on behalf of Jane lake  Widow now Ill and fired to her Room Maketh Oath and Saith that she the said Jane Lake hath Informed this Depont. which he verily believes to be true that she Intermarried with her present Husband Wm. Lake at the parish Church of St Paul Convent Garden about 15 years ago that she is now of the Age of 46 years as thereabouts that since then said Marriage her said Husband Rented and with her Rented in an House in Well Street Oxford Road in the parish of St. May le bone in the Courts of Middlesex about two years at and under the Annual Rent of £18. that they quitted the same about four years ago and since that her said Husband hath not done any Act where by to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement that her said Husband left her about three weeks ago and she doth not known where he now is and that she is Ill & Poor

Sworn the 15th day of September 1791 before us D Walker R Butler

Benj: Newman
