Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Moore Aged about Sixty Six years on her Oaths says She Intermarried to her late Husband John Moore at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn about Eighteen years ago and by whom She hath no Child or Children living Says Her said Late Husband Rented and Lived in a House in Blue cross Street Leicester Fields in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for upwards of Twenty years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds and paid Parish Taxes and for the same and quitted said House about 14 years ago and after quitting the said House He never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said Husband death.

Sworn this 8th. day of Septr. 1781 before  Abrm Baley  Wm. Kitchiner

her Sarah Moore Mark
