Midlesex ss}  Frances Smith the wife of Thomas Smith whose maiden name was Frances Green maketh oath that about twelve years & half ago & long before her marriage with her said husband she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Child in the house of one Captain Grove in old Gravel Lane in the Parish of Saint George in the said County of Middlesex the which child hath been Since baptizd & registered there by the name of Charles Green and that one Robert Allday late of the Parish of st Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Basrer did get her with Child of the said Child  and which child is illegitimate & Bastard by reason that she this Depont was never married to the said Robert allday and such Child hath not as yet done any act to gain a Settlemt for himself

Sworn this 12. day of March 1787 before  R Davies  Rob. Smith

The Mark of Frances Smith
