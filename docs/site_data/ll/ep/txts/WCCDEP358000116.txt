Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

passd. to St. Mary Lambeth to wit Susannah Ludlow aged 25 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Joseph Ludlow who is At this Present time Insane at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about 3 years ago that her said Husband has Informed her when he was in his sound State of Mind that when he was a Single Man he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Ellis as a Waiter at the Sign of the Home near Hennington Common in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth for one year and upwards at the yearly Wages of five Pounds and since Quitting the said service Have not done any Act Matter or thing whereby he may have gained as Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 27th- day of Septr. 1785 M: A: Taylor Jno Machin

Susanh Ludlow
