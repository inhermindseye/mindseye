Middlesex, to wit, Ann Roof  Singlewoman , maketh Oath and Saith, That about Thirty Two Years since she was hired by and went to live as a Yearly hired Servant with William Wilson of Brick Lane , facing Phoenix Street , in the Parish of Saint Matthew Bethnal Green in the County of Middlesex , Milk Man , at certain Yearly Wages, and lived in such Service under such hiring for the space of Two Years and an half or thereabouts That she hath not since she quitted the said Service lived as a Yearly Servant with any Person or Persons for the space of one Year together, Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards, or done any other Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement-That being Poor and unable to provide for herself she hath applied to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate in the County of Middlesex for relief, and hath been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 14th Day of December 1797 before us R Davies H: Reynell

her Ann Roof Mark
