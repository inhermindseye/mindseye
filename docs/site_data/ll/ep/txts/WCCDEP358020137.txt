Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Samuel Griffin of Greenwich in the County of Kent  Fisherman on behalf of his Son Thomas Griffin who is absconded and left his Family chargeable on the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and saith that his said Son was born at Old Brentford in the Parish of Ealing in the County of Middlesex and that he was bound apprentice by the Trustees of the Charity School of the said Parish of Ealing to one Polliket a Shoemaker at Old Brentford in the said Parish of Ealing and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of April 1789 R Butler

Samuel Griffin
