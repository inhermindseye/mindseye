Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to Wit Jonathan Peter Mansfield Aged 18 Years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That Mathew Mansfield his Father lived a Year and upwds. with a Mr. Powell a Pastry Cook in St. Martins Court in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex that he Served such Year & upwds. as an Apprentice to the sd. Mr. Powell That the sd. Mathew Mansfield his Father has been Dead abt. 5 Yrs. since And this Deponent Examinant Further Upon his Oath Saith that he never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for 1 Yr. Rented any house Tenemt. or Loding for £10. a Yr. or done any Act other Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any subsequent Settlement otherwise then by his Fathers serving such time as an Apprentice to the sd. Mr. Powell to this Examinants Knowledge Information or Belief

Sworn this 3d. day of Feby. 1791 before Thos. Gordon Edw. Read

Jonathan Peter Mansfield
