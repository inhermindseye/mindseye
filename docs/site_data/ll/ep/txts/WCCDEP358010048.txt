Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Christopher Hodgson of Newport Markett in the Parish of Saint Anns in the Liberty of Westminster  Shoemaker upon his Oath saith that Sarah Hodgson on Instant Aged 5 years or thereabout is the Illegitimate Child of Sarah Haynes by Robert Hodgson this Exts. Brother and was born  at House which the said Robt. Hodgson Rented situate the Corner of Neils Passage Queen Street Seven Dials in the parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx that the said Robert Hodgson and Sarah Haynes were never married and that the are both dead

Sworn this 14th day of Janry. 1787  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordon

Christopher Hodgson
