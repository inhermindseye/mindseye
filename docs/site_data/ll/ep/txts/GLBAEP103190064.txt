Middlesex ss}  Ann Taylor maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with-Jenkins Esquire in the Victualling office in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of one year & upwards That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26 day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Taylor
