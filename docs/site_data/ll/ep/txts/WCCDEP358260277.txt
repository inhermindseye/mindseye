Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Thomas Harris aged about 45 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 24th. day of July 1777 he Intermarried with Mary his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom he hath one Child living Vizt. Elizabeth aged about Eleven years That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Half Moon Court Stanhope Street in the said Parish for Six Months at the yearly Rent of Nineteen Pounds That he hath quitted said House about Thirteen years since which time he hath not Rented Any house Tenement of Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of May 1798 before me } Saml. Edward

his Thomas Harris mark
