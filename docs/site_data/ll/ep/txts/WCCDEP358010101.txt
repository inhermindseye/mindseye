Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Hannah Fisher aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived Two years as a hired yearly Servant with one Jonah. Hopkinson a China Merchant of Knowles's Court Little Carter Lane in the City of London at the yearly Wages of £8..8..0 that the last 40 Days was of her  Service was at her Masters Country House which was in Thomas Street Deptford in the Parish of Saint Nich at Deptford in the County of Kent that she hath quitted her sd: Service about 6 Months since which time she has not Rented any House Tens. or Lodging £10 a year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of May 1787  Sampr Wright Maddington

Hannah Fisher
