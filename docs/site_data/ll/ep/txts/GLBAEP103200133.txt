Middlesex ss}  Sarah Burgen the wife of James Burgen maketh oath that divers years ago She was lawfully married to her sd husband in Shoreditch Church Middx that he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he heretofore rented a house in the Parish of sd Saviour in Southwarke in the County of Surry but What rent he Paid therefore or Whether he ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes for the same She Can't discovd or Set forth never having reced any information from him touching the same And this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting her sd husbands Settlemt

Sworn this 10. day of May 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Burgen
