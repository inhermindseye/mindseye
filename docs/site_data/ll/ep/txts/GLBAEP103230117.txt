Middlesex ss  Jane Russell  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father John Russell deced was by birth a Scotchman And never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That her Mother Kelly Russell who now lives in the Parish of St John Wapping in Middlesex never Since She Came into England ever rented a house or Lodging of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in England to this Deponts Knowledge or belief That She this Deponent was born in Scotland and Since She Came to England has not as yet lived in any one Place for the Space of a year nor has She ever been married nor done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that She now lives as a hired Servant with Mr Hughes in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County

Sworn this 2d day of February 1788 before}

The Mark of Jane Russell
