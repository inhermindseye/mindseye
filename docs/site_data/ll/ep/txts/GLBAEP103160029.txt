Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Combe the widow of Thomas Combe deced maketh oath That divers years ago her Said late Husband Rented a house of the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the Said County & was charged to and paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her Said Husband did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath two Children namely Thomas Combe aged about Six years & three Months & Mary Lakey Combe aged about three years & five Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 12. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Combe
