St Clement Danes August 17th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James Westmr }  (to wit) Robert Calvert aged 61 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married to Sarah Wallis at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about twelve Years ago that sub previous thereto he Rented and Resided in an House in Little Poultney Street in the parish of St James in the Liberty of Westminster for about a year at the annual Rent of £30 that he quitted that House about thirty twenty years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief that his said Wife hath been away from him this Examinant many Years And he knows not where she is

Sworn the 17th day of August 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Robert Calvert
