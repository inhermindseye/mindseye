Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth How Aged about 54, Upon her Oath Saith that she was born at Lambeth , that she came to London when a Child, that she never was Married, has Children but they are all provided for, New That she has been in several places but never lived twelve Months in any of them And this Examinant further Sayth that she is poor and unable to gain her Livelihood by Working.

Sworn this 11st. Novr. 1790 before me us M Addington N Bond

her Elizabeth How . Mark
