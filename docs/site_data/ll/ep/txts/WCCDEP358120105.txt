Middx  Mary Quin aged upwds. of 40 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married & that she lived a yearly Servt. with Mrs. Atkins at the Corner of New Street near Carnaby Market in the Parish of St James in the Liberty of Westmr. for two Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds, quitted her sd Service abt. 9 Yrs. ago & has not since rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn May 24th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Quin
