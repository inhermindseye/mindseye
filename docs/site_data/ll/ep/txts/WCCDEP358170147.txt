Middx  Richd. Platt upon his Oath saith that on or abt. the 27th. Day of May 1733 he intermarried with his now Wife  Jane at the Parish Church of St. Ann Soho in the Liberty of Westminster & further saith that he was bound an Apprentice to and served five Years the latter Part of his apprentiship with his Brother James Platt in Exeter Street in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand Martin in the Feilds or in the Liberty of Westmr . aforesaid that he hath not since his sd. Apprentisp rented £10. a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath Two Children (to witt) Jas . aged abt. 5 Yrs and Margt . aged one Year and upwards & he being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworn the 12th. of October 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Richard Platt
