Middx  Ann Darnborough  Wido . upon Oath saith she intermarried with her late husband Chas. Darnborough deceased in the parish Church of St. Lukes Middx abt. 16 years since and that her sd. late husband in or abt. the Year 1739 Rented a house of £28 by the Year in Arundel Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes  Middx and lived therein about two Years and that neither her sd. late husband whilst living nor this Examinant since his Decease have since they quitted the said house Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 9th 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Darnborough
