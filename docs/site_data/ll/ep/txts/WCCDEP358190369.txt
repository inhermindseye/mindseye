Middx  Catherine Browne Aged about 60 Years Upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Wm- Browne with whom she Intermarryed in the Parish Church of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middx abt. 42 Years Ago Says her said husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Pickett a Tinman in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and never after Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year nor she this Examinant hath not Since her said husbands Death Rented house Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 4th Day of July 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cathe Browne
