Middx  Susannah Bull aged abt. Thirty Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Wm. Barker at the Hand & Sheirs in New Rents in the Parish of St. Leonard Foster Lane  London  Victualler that since her Sd. Apprentishp She hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 8th. of Febry 1741 before} Tho. Lane  Jas: Gronous

Susan Bull
