Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Curtain aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 15th. day of July 1784 she Intermarried with William Curtain her present Husband who is now from her at Ratcliffe Church in Bristol by whom she hath Three Children living Vizt. Ann aged about Eight years Jane aged about Five years and William aged about Three years That her said Husband never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she hath been informed which Information She verily believes to be true that he was born at in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex where his father kept house for several years

Sworn this 26th. day of Febry. 1798 before me } W Carter

her Mary Curtain mark

Witness B Newman
