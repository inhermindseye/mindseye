Middlesex  Margaret Thompson the wife of Joseph Thompson mariner who is absent from her on board one of his Majesties Ships of war maketh oath that her sd husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born at Antigna & to her knowledge or belief he never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That she this Depont before her marriage & while a Singlewoman was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Captn Jerrett in the parish of Weymonth in the County of Dorset at Certain Wages & continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upward & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or done any Act to gain anor. Settlement Save what She has acquired by marriage & that She hath a Child named Susannah aged about Seven months the lawful If one of this Deponent by her sd husband to whom She was lawfully married.

Sworn this. 8. day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Margaret Thompson
