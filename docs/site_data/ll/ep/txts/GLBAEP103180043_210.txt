Middlesex ss}  William Ellis maketh oath That Several Years ago he rented a house of Six pounds a year in the Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain anothr Settlement to his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 13 day of June 1778 before P Rowlands

The Mark of William Ellis
