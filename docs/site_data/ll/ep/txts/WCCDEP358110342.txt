Middx.  Ann Bowles aged near 60 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Clark a Milkman in Howard Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx . for three Years & upwards at the yearly Wages of £4 quitted her sd. Service upwds. of 12 Years ago & has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servants for one year

Sworn Novr. 5th 1753 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Bowles
