Middx  John Sperrin Aged about Seventy Years upon Oath Says that whilst he was a Singleman and without Child or Children he Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hyde a Publican at the Coach & Horses in Burlington Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted said Service about Forty Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year

Sworn the 17th of May 1764 Before} Thos. Kynaston  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of John Sperrin

Upon Mr Browns Complt J. W
