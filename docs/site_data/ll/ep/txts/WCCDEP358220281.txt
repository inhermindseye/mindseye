Westmr. to wit Sarah Shepherd Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Pyke a Grocer in Clare Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented £10 a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further Says that on Monday the Tenth day of June last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Prince in Hanover Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized Mary Shepherd Hatfield and that William Hatfield new on Board the Cruttenden Captn. Baker Commander is the real and True Father thereof and no other Persons whatsoever

Sworn this 13th day of July 1771 before} R. Butler

Sarah Shepperd
