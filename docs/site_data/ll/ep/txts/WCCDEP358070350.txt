Middlesex & Westmr. to wit Margaret King Aged about Twenty years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or Served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Vardin a Piece Broker in White Horse Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of Two years at the yearly Wages of four Pounds and hath not yet quitted her Place And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that One John Johnson a Journeyman Taylor who lately ledged after said Masters house had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time the Middle of June last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Johnson and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 1st. day of March 1776 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Margt. King Mark
