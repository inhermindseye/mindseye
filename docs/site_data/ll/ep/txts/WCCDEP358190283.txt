Middx  Grace Comly aged upwards of 30 Years upon Oath Says She is the Wife of John Comly (who is gone from her ) with whom She Intermarried in the Parish Church of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster about Eight years ago by whom She has three Children  Lucy aged about Seven Years Grace aged about three Years and John Aged about one Year Says her said Husband Rented a House in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster at the Yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds a year lived therein upwards of a Year and quitted the same near three Years and hath never since Rented a House or paid any Taxes as this Examinant verily believes

Sworn the 9th. Day of October 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

Grace Comly
