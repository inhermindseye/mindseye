Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Burgess aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr Sherwin in Lillipot Lane in the Parish of Saint Ann within Aldersgate in the City of London for Sixteen Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Seven years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of Janry. 1797 before us } R. Ford Rupt Clarke

her Mary Burgess Mark

Witness B Newman
