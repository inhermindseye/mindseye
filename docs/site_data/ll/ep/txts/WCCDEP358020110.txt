Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit: Esther Cullum aged 50 Years and upwards Upon her Widow of John Cullum deceased to whom she was married at the Parish Church of Saint Martins in the Fields by License about 19 years ago Upon her Oath Saith that she had two Children by her said Husband viz: William aged 17 years who is now provided for and Mary aged 15 years unprovided for and now chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes. when her Husband was a Single Man he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Lady  Julia Pen in Springs Gardens in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for 3 Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages £13 that he quitted the said Service about 17 Years ago and has not since done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he to the best of this Examinants Knowledge and belief might have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day Novr. 1788 . G Mercer J Banfather
