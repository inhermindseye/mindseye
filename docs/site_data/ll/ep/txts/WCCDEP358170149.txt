Middx  Winifred White  Singlewoman aged abt. Thirty Years upon her Oath saith that upwards of Five Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Jos: Dolbin in a Court in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of One Year & upwds. that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 14th. Day of October 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Winnyfred White
