Middx.  Benjn. Scullerd aged abt. 59 Years upon Oath says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with John Gynes a Glovemaker in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex & never Rented Ten Pounds paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn Novr. 19th. 1760 before me}

Benjn: Scullard
