Middx  Margt. White aged abt. 54 years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thos. White who has been dead abt. 9 Weeks and that he was a Native of Scotland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief. says before her intermarriage with the said Thos. White she being then a Singlewoman lived a hired Yrly. Servt . with Anthony Clarkson at the Oxford Arms in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for One year & upwards at the yearly Wages of £4

Sworn this 19th. of April 1760 before}
