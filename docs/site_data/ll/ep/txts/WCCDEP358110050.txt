Westmr.  Eliz Archer upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Henry Archer who died the 1st. Day of Febry last to whom she was married in the parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. upwds. of 6 Yrs. ago and by whom she hath 3 Childn. Eliz aged about 4 Years Henry aged upwds. of 2 Yrs. & Thos . aged abt. 9 months Says her sd late husband lived Eight Years (whilst a Singleman) a Yearly Servant with Mr Bilson Wharfinger in Durham Yard in the Strand in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £8 and lodged the last Year of his sd. Service wth. his master in Durham Yard aforesd. and Quitted his sd. Service abt. 2 months after their marriage and that neither her sd. late husband nor she this Examinant ever Rented ten pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn May 12th. 1752 before}

The mark of Eliz Archer
