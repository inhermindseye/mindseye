Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Johnson aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that she Rented and lived in a house in Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about Three years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and upwards That she hath quitted said House about Five years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of May 1797 before me} W Carter

her Mary Johnson mark
