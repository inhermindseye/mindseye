Westmr. to wit Meredith Harris Aged about Sixty Two Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one Thomas Shaw a Shoemaker in Queen Street the Corner of Gilbert Street in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he has never done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of October 1773 before R Butler

Meredith Harris
