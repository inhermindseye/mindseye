Saint Clement Danes 25th July 1793

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit  St Bennett Pauls Wharf no Settlemt  this Pauper passed Wrong was taken back and removed to the Parish of Saint Gregory by Saint Paul London vide Examn 6th- May 1793 page 231 this Book wch being also wrong she was after removed to St Mary White chapel & no Appeal vide proper Settlemt page 245 this Book  to wit Mary Hughes Aged 49 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her Husband George Hughes at the Parish Church of Saint Bennett Paul Wharf about 12 Years ago that before her said Marriage with the said George Hughes she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Gosling of G Knight Rider Street Doctors Commons in the sd parish of Saint Bennetts for upwards of 2 years for 2 Years at the Annual Wages of 9 Guineas from whose Service she married with the said George Hughes And this deponent further Saith that the sd George Hughes left this deponent about 6 Months Ago and that this deponent has never heard any thing of him since And this deponent saith that since her Marriage with the said George Hughes she has never acquired any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponents Information or belief and that she is quite unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn the 25th day of July 1793 before us} N Bond Edwd: Naish

Mary Hughes
