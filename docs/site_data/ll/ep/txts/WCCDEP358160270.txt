Middx The Examination of Elizth . the Wife of John Rogers taken the 9th. Day of March 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about five Years ago her sd. Husband Jno. Rogers lived in and rented a House the Corner of Poulsgrave head Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the yearly Rent of forty five Pounds & lived in the Same for the Space of Six Months or thereabouts & this Examinant further on her Oath saith that her sd. Husband hath not since he left the sd. House rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband is now gone from her and haveing two Son all Children (to wit) Saml. aged 3 Years & upwards John aged 3 Months Year & She being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & them

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Elix Rogers Lived but half a year
