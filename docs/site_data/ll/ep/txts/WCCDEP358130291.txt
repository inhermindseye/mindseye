Middx  Cath: Chandler aged upwds. of Sixty Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Jas. Chandler that she hath been informed & verily believes the said James Chandler served his Apprenticeship with a Shag Weaver in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry

Sworn the 1st. day of March 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cath: Chandler
