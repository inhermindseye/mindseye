Middlesex ss}  Ann Cosgrove the widow of Andrew Cosgrove deced maketh oath that Since her said late husbands death She this Deponent took and rented a house in Long Alley in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the Sd County at the weekly Rent of Four Shillings & Six Pence and resided thereon for the Space of Six Months and upwards And this Deponent Says that Since She left Such house She rented a Room at the Rent of Four Shillings per week in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Same County & resided thereon for one year & half & has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt That he hath two Children namely Daniel aged about eight years & eight Months and Hannah aged about Six Years & ten Months the lawful Issue of this Depont by her late husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 28. day of April 1788 before  Rob. Smith

Ann Cosgrove
