Middx  George Orten a Soldier belonging to the Second Regiment of Foot Gaurds maketh Oath and Saith that about Nine Years ago he was hired a Yearly Servant to Mr . Saml. Dallman of the parish of Tamworth in the County of Warwick  Farmer and lived there one Year and upwards and received Five pounds a Year Wages And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And hath one Child named Ann Aged about Nine Months

Sworn this 19th. day of April 1746 Before us Robert Dennett Rd Ricards

his George Orten Mark
