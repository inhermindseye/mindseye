Middlesex & Westminster}  Elizabeth Steel Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant one Mr. Giffard a Broker in Holbourn in the parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted the same about Six Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 12th. day of February 1777 before  Thos. Bishop  John Cox .

her Elizth. Steel Mark
