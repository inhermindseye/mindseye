St Clement Danes Febry. 9th. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster } (to wit) Jane Murray  Singlewoman Aged 24 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs Huxley No. 18 Cecil Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Sixteen Months at the Annl. Wages of Seven Guineas that she quitted that Service on the 24th day of October now last past and hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that on the twenty Ninth day of January now last past at the Workhouse belonging to the said parish of St Clement Danes & Situate within the same She the said Jane Murray was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath since been half half Baptized by the Name of Jane Murray at the Parish Church of St Clement Danes and that the said Bastard Child ward is and from the time of it's Birth always hath been chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes And this Deponent farther saith that Sinclair of the Kingdom of Ireland Esquire but now Residing in the said County of Middlesex is the only real and true father thereof he of such Female Bastard Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body in or abouth about the Month of March last and at other times since at some or one of which times he did the said Female Bastard Child thereon And this

Deponent lastly saith that no other person but the said Sinclair Esquire hath ever had Cla Carnal Knowledge of her this Deponent's Body

Sworn the 9th day of Febry 1792 before Me} R Butler

Jane Murray 's Mark

Mr Richardson Overseer Dobbins the Beadle who has this & also had the former Warrt

Mr Sinclair is a Tall Thin Genteel Man Generally Wears Black & usually Cecil Street  Coffee House when he comes to England
