Middx 

Margt. Haines aged upwds. of 58 Years upon Oath Says she lived was never married Says she lived a yearly Servt . with Mr. Thos. Wainwright at the Golden Fleece in Kent Street in the Parish of St. Mary at Newington in the County of Surry for Seven Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Wainwrights Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived wth. any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief.

Sworn the 4th. Janry. 1746 before  Thos. Bardus

Margaret haynes
