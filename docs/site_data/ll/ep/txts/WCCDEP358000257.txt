Middlesex & Westminster  Ann Waller Aged 25 years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That about 14 years ago She was Bound an Apprentice by Indentures duly Executed for Seven years to Mr John Kelly of the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Whit maker and served under the said Indentures about 4 years and that her said Master then gave her up the Remainder of her time That since serving such Apprenticeship she hath not done any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she once have gained any Settlement elswhere than in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And that she is Poor Sick and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn this 23d. day of March 1786 before Me  Wm Hitchiner

Ann Warner
