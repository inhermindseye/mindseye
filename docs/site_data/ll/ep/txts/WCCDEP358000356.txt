St. Clement Danes

Elizabeth Hart aged 58 or thereabouts Saith That she was born in Hart Street Covent Garden That her Father Rented a House of Twenty Pounds a Year there lived in it many Years and died there that it is in St Pauls Covent Garden-that she never was Married or did any Act to the best of her Knowledge whereby she may have gained a Settlement

Sworn 18 Septr. 1786 before us  Sampn Wright  Thos: Gordon

her Eliz Hart Mark
