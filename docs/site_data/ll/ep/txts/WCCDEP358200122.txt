Middx  Elizth. Meredith Aged about Thirty Years Upon Oath Says she was Never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Barnett the Town Clerk in the Parish of Woolwich in the County of Kent Upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service about three Years & hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 7th of July 1764 Before} Thos. Kynaston  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Elizth. Meredith
