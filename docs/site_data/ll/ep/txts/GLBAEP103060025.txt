Middx  Elizabeth Ward  Widow late wife of Thomas Ward deced Maketh Oath and Saith that about Forty two years ago her said husband was bound Apprentice by Indenture for seven years to Mr Nutt at Ye end of Prestons yard in the Minories in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Liberty of the City of London and severed all his said Apprentiship in the said parish wth. his said Master and gained no Subsequent Settlement Since to her knowledge And that She hath gained no Settlement since his death and that She hath a Daughter by her said husband named Laracth Sarah aged about Twenty two years who is Impotent

Sworn 19th. day of Octr. 1744 before Robert Dennett

his Elizth. Ward Mark
