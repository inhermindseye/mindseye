Middx  Eleanor Johnson upon her Oath saith that about Eleven Years since she intermarried with her present Husband James Johnson at the Parish Church of St. John in the City of Cashole in the Kingdom of Ireland that the sd. James Johnson was bound an Apprentice to & served Six Months & upwards the First Part of his Apprentisp with Mr. James Johnson in the Parish of Blaris in the County of Antram in the sd. Kingdom of Ireland & then Enlisted himself into Generall Barrells Regimt. of Foot that since his said Apprentiship the sd. James Johnson never was Assigned over to any other Person Rented ten Pounds a Year lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to as this Examint. hath been Informed & beleives that she hath 2 Childn . Eliz: aged about Five Years & Benjn. aged 8 Yrs- & being Poor & her Husband being abroad in his Majestys Service is not able to maintaine them & that she hath Comitted an Act of Vagrancy in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx

Sworne April 9th. 1743 before} J Poulson>

The Mark of Eleanr. Johnson
