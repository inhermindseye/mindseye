Middx  Sarah Jackson Aged about Sixty Nine Years upon Oath Says She is the Widow of Greenhill Jackson who has been dead about Thirty Six Years Says that being a Widow She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Perry a Perukemaker in Essex Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Sixteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds and then quitted her sd Service from thonce She went to live as a hired Yrly. Servant with one Mr. Vittary in Devereux Court in the said Parish of St. Clement and lived with him upwards of a Year and half at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted said Service about thirteen Years Since which time She never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 13th Day of Janry 1769 Before me} Tho Lane

Sarah Jackson
