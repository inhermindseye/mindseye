Middlesex to wit Mary Day Aged about Twenty years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servt . with one Mr. Wilmott a Plaistererer near Battersea Bridge in the Parish of St. Luke Chelsea for 18 Months at the yearly Wages of £3. hath quitted her said Service about three years ago and after quitting her said Service she went to live with one Mrs. Cannon in Stanhope Street to learn the Mantua Making Business for one year & gave Eight Guineas to her said Mistress to teach her the Busin Trade as by a Pretended Indre may Appear but as the same in neither Indented or Inrolled at the Stamp Office the Indre is Void and could gain no Settlement thereby as she has been Informed & believes And further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes (she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born) And that one Thos Thos. Field  Clerk to a Proctor at No.5 Doctors Commons is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at the Bull & Gate near Little Turnstile  Holbourn sometimes in the Month of June last but the Particular day she dose not now remember & at one or. time afterwards at the same place at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Thos. Field & no or Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 17th. day of Decr. 1783 R Butler

her Mary Day Mark
