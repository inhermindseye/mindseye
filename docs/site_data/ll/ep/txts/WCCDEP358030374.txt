Westmr. 

Eliz. Doggett aged about 45 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with her late husband John Doggett deced on or abt. the 25th. Augt. 1721 at a Church in Lambard Street London Says her sd. late husband in his Life time lived in & rented a house in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of Fifty pounds for Six Years or thereabouts Says neither her sd. late husband after he quitted the sd. house in Portugal Street nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. many other place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 18th. 1747 before  Wm. Jones

Elizabeth Doggett
