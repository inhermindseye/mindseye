Middx  Sarah Robinson aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Fewsdale at Doncaster in Yorkshire was two Years at the Yearly wages of Thirty Shillings for the first Year and Two Pounds afterwards and has not since she quitted her sd. Service (which is abt. 7 Yrs. ago) rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn April 1st. 1754 before} Saml Pechell

The Mark of Sarah Robinson
