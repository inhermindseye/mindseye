Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Fidgett aged about 42 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one John Ely in Fore Street in the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate for Thirteen Months at the yearly wages of Seven pounds that she hath quitted said Service for upwards of Fifteen years since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent settlement And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that on the Twelfth day of August 1794 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the house of one Messrs. Wilkinson and Jones the Cotton of  Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Elizabeth That one William Whitehead who now lives in Milford Lane and works for Mr. Windle is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said William Whitehead and no other Person did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 19th- day of Octr. 1796 } J Reeves

her Mary Fidgett Mark

Man gave a Note for 2s. P Week
