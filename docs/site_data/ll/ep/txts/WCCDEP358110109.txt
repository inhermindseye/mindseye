Middx  Thomas Morgan upon Oath saith he lived a yearly Servant with Mr Rd Jenkins in the Parish of Llangibbey in the County of Monmouth for one year at the yearly wages of Six pounds and has not since he Quitted his said Service Rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn 2d Novr- 1752 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Thomas Morgan
