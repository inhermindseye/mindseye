Middx  Eliza. Dunn aged about 15 Years upon his Oath saith she was bound Apprentice by Indenture Dated the 9th. Novr. 1743 to James Jones of the Parish of St. Botolph Bishopgate London Ribbon weaver Says she went the same Day with the Consent of Mr. Jones & lived with one Mr Thos. Kelley in Red Lyon Court in Charter house Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchers in the County of Middx with whom this Examinant resided upwards of Twelve Months & hath left her sd. Masters Service on Acct. of ill Usage upwards of 5 Weeks Says she hath not since she left Mr. Kelleys Service lived with any other Person as a Servt. with the Consent of the sd. Mr. Jones or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 28th. May 1745 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Eliz: Dunn
