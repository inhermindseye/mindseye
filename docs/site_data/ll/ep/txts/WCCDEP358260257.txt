Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Arnold aged about Forty years upon her Oath saith That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. James in Red Lyon Court in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time She hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th.. day of March 1798 R. Ford J. Ploud

her Elizth.. Arnold mark
