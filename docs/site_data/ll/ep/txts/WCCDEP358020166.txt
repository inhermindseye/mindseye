Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Josias Passmore aged about 62 years upon his Oath saith that about 38 years ago he Rented and lived in a house in Danzell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twenty seven years at the yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds that he hath quitted said serve House about Eleven years since which time he has not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to the best of this Examinant Knowledge Information on belief to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon his Oath saith that about 20 years ago he Intermarried with Sarah his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch and that he hath Two Children living by his said Wife Vizt. Josias aged about Eleven years and Edward aged about Four years

Sworn this 16th- day of Octr. 1789 R Butler

Josias Pasmore
