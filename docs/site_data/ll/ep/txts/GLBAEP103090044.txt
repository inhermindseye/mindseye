Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Mary Nelhams of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County single woman taken before me one of his Majesties justices of the Peace for the said County this 26th day of September 1766 .

Who Saith that on the twenty seventh day of February last Past at the house of one Joseph Hall of Tash Street Grays Inn Lane in the Liberty above the Bars in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex she the said Mary Nelhams was delivered of a Female Bastard Child and Baptised in the said house by the name of Mary And that one John Allen of the Parish of St. James in the City & Liberty of Westmr . in the Said County did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this 26th day of September 1766 before  Chris: Scott

Mary Nelhams
