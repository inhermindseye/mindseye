Middx  Eliz: Rogers aged about 26 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr Whitehurst at the Crown Tavern in Bloomsbury in the Parish of St. George in Bloomsbury in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £5 for the Space of 3 Yrs. or thereabouts & hath left her sd. Service about 5 Years since which time she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 26th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

Elizabeth Rogers
