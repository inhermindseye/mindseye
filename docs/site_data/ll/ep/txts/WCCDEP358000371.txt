Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit  James Talboys One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he believes to be true that Andrew Forrest aged about 13 years was bound Apprentice by the Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish of St Clement Danes by Indres duly executed to one William Footman of  No. 26 Aldermanbury Hair Dresser in the Parish of St. Mary Aldermanbury in the City of London  on the 13th. day of April last that the said Andrew Forr served his said Master under such Indre till Yesterday that then he Came away from said Master

Sworn this 19th. day of Octr. 1786  Jno. Barnfather  James Paine

James Talboys
