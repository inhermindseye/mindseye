Middlesex & Westmr. to wit Jane Hughes Aged about Twenty Six years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or pd. Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Woodham in Musical Instrument maker in Exeter Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about five Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same And further says that one Serjeant Bean who lately Used Mr. Dykesher late Master at a Wine Vault facing Burleigh Street in the Strand but of what Regiment she does not know is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her said late Masters House some time in the Month of October last but the Particular Day she does not now Remember and at sevl. Difft. times afterwards at one of Which times he got her with Child of the Child or Childn, of Which she is now Pregnant and that the said Mr. Serjeant Bean & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 11th. day of April 1783 R Butler

her Jane Hughes mark
