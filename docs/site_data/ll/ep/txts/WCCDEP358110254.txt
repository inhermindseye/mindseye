Middx  Eliz Perins on Behalf of Esther Wright an Idiot upon Oath saith she verily believes the sd. Esther Wright never was married and that she lived a Servant with Mrs. Haswell (this Examints. mother deceased) in Arundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for twelve Years & upwards and had for such her sd. Service Dyet Lodging & Cloaths and has not since Mrs. Haswells Decease Service rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn June 2d. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Perins
