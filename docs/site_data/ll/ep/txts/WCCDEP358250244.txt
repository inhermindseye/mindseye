Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit George Joslyn aged about 33 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the Ninth day of April 1787 he Intermarried with Margaret his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Overy 's Southwark by whom he hath four Children living Vizt. Sarah Elizabeth aged about Nine years George aged about Seven years Edward aged about Three years and Margaret aged about Twelve Months That he this Deponent lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Barnet in Milfrod Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Five pounds Ten Shillings that he hath quitted said Service about Seventeen years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. June 1796 } Saml. Edward

George Joslyn
