Middlesex ss  Ann Walsby the widow of Robert Walsby deced Maketh oath that divers Years ago her said late husband lived in and rented a house at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and ten shillings by the year in orange Street in the Parish of Saint Saviour in Southwarke in the County of Surry and Continued in such house for the Space of a year and Paid the Kings Tax for the same and by means thereof as she is advised gained a legal settlement there and this Deponent Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said late husband never afterwards rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or served any Parish office or did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement Nor hath she done any Act since his death to gain a settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief And this Deponent farther Saith that she hath three Children to Wit Jane Aged about fourteen Years Margaret Aged about eight Years and Mary Aged about Six Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband

Sworn this 22d day of September 1766 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Ann Walsby
