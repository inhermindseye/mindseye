Westmr.  Catherine Owen aged about 43 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stephen Fairweather at the Crown Alehouse in Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for One year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds, Quitted said Service about Eight Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 4th. day of Feby. 1767 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Cathne: Owen
