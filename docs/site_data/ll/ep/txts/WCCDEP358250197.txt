Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman One of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes on behalf of Catherine Fallon an Infant aged about Seven years and Mary Fallon an Infant aged about Four years Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true That Nicholas Fallon and Mary Fallon the father and Mother of the said Two Children were Irish People and never gained any Settlement in England That where the said Nicholas Fallon is he this Deponent doth not know That Mary Fallon the Mother is at this time confined in a Mad house at Hoxton Insane And this Deponent further saith that he has been informed which Information he also verily believes to be true that the said Catherine Fallon was born in Wells Street Oxford road in the parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex and that Mary Fallon was born in Johns Court Hamsay Yard in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 8th. day of Septr. 1795 } John Collick B Newman Tho Wood .
