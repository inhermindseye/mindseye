St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St James Cleken well. Middx  (to wit) Frances Webb  Singlewoman Aged 31 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Henry Paul at the Apple Tree in Cold Bath Fields in the parish of St. Clement James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex about. Eight Months at the yearly Wages of £5 that then her said Master beingd Bankrupt and a Commission awarded agst. hired & Assees. appointed she continued a Servant in the same House as the Servant of the said Assees. with any Fresh hiring or Agreement whatsoever about Six Months at the Wages on the like Conditions & in the same capacity as with her said Master Paul that she quitted that Service the 5th. May 1790 that she hath not since lived as an hired yearly servant for a twelve Month together or done any other Act whereby she might obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief.

Sworn the 15th day of September 1791 before us} Philip Dyot D Walker

Frans. Webbs Mark
