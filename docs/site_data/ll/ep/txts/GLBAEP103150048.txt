Middlesex ss  Eleanor Meadows the widow of Thomas Meadows deced maketh oath that divers Years ago her Sd late husband purchased two Copy hold Tenements or houses of the Yearly Value of Thirty pounds in the parish of Chelmsford in the County of Essex & lived there in one of them for about the Space of four Years & was as this Depont. believes Charged to & paid Parochial Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Dept Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 4th. day of August 1775 before B Camper

Eleanor Meadows
