Middx  The Exaicon of Mary the wife of Edwd. Lovick in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 20th. day of Augt. 1740 Who upon her Oath Saith that her sd. Husband Edwd. Lovick was Bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven years Apprentishipp with Mr. Jno. Ruffe  Perukemaker in the parish of St. Andrew Undershaft in the City of London And further Saith yt. her sd. Husband never Rented Ten pounds a Yeare pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband is now Sick & haveing three Children (to wit) Edwd . Aged upwds. of Eight years Wm . Aged upwds. of Seven years and George Aged abt. Seven Month & being poor are not able to provide for them

Sworn the day & year above named before} J Poulson Jas: Gronous

The mark of Mary Lovick
