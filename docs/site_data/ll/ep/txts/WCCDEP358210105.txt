Westmr.  Sarah Blakesley aged upwards of 28 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Robt, Anson at No. 16 in Clement's Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds, quitted said Service about twelve Months ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 19th, day of November 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Sarah Blakesley
