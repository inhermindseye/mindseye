Westmr.  Elizabeth King Aged about Twenty one Years upon Oath Says She never was Married nor ever Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Henry King Rented and lived in a House in Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr: for five Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds or thereabouts and lived therein to the Time of his Death which happened about thirteen Years ago Says that after the Death of her said father her Mother removed to another House in Clare Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds or thereabouts and lived therein about three Years and never after Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Servant for one Year

Sworn this 13th Day of Janry 1769 Before} Tho: Lane

her Elizth. King Mark Witness Petr. Sidebotham
