Middx The Examination of Mary Symonds  Widw . of Edward Symonds Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 28th. Day of June 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. upwds. of one Year ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Jones in Russell Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . Button Seller for the Space of one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £5 having at that time no Child or Childn. but what has gained a Settlemt. in their own Right. And this Examint. further saith that she hath not since that time Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband Edwd. Symonds hath been Dead about Twenty Years And being Poor & Old is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before}

Mary Symonds
