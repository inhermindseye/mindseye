Middx to wit  Sarah Grace aged about 37 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Grace who has been Dead about 12 Years ago and with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London Upwards of 17 Years ago Says her said Husband served 7 Years Apprenticeship with Mrs. Reves a Coal Merchantess at Essex Stair in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented House Paid Taxes or lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither has this Examinant since her said Husbands Death.

Sworn this 22d day of December 1769 before me} R. Butler

Sarah Grace
