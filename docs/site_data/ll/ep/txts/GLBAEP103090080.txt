Middlesex ss  Ralph Watson maketh oath That about Fifty Years ago he was bound apprentice by Indre to; and served the Whole of his said apprenticeship With one John Harvey in Peter Street in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the City and Liberty of Westmr. in the said County of Middx  Cordwainer and by Means thereof as he apprehends gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that he never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever served any Parish offices or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his Knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his said apprentice And that he hath a wife named Elizabeth .

Sworn this 29th. Day of Septembr. 1767 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Ralph Watson
