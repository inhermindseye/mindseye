Middx.  Blanch Wise aged about 22 Years lodging at Mr. Davis's a Tallow Chandler in Blackmoor Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex upon Oath says she was never married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Backwell in Norfolk Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds hath quitted said Service about two Years and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 26th. day of March last she was delivered of a Male Bastard in the house of the above named Mr. Davis in Blackmoor Street aforesaid which hath since been baptized by the Name of George and is now living and become chargeable to the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes , says that one John Phillips who is a Master Brewer opposite the Vine Tavern at Ratcliffe in the said County of Middx had carnal knowledge of her body in the Month of June 1764 and at several other times since at one which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Phillips and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 18th. day of April 1765 . Before} Paul Vaillant

Blanch Wise
