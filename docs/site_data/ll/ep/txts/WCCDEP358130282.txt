Middx  Mary Clark aged abt. 30 Yrs. upon Oath says She is the Wife of John Clark a Soldier at Gibralter & that she Cannot give any Account of his Settlement save that he was born in Argyle Shire Says She before her Intermarriage lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Capt: Cohone in the Town of Greenough in Scotland for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds Scotch that she has two Childn. Susan aged abt. two Years & Daniel aged abt. one Month

Sworn the 22d. day of February 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Clark
