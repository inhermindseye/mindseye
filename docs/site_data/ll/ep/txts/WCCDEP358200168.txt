Westmr.  Elizth. Moulson Aged about Twenty One Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed was an Apprentice Rented House pd. Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert. for One Year Says her Father Thos. Moulson Served the last two Years of his Apprentiship with his Brother Peter Moulson a Vintner at the Ship Tavern in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & never after Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards & to Become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when Born Says One John Currill a Farrier who is run away & this Examinant Cannot tell where he is had Carnal knowledge of her Body Sometime in the Months of March or April last & at several other times since at One of which

times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the said John Currill & no other Person is the true & Real Father thereof

Sworn the 28 of Novr 1764 Before.} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth. Moulson
