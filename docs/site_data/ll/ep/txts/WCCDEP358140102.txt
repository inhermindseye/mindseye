Middx.  Mary Jenkins aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Henry Jenkins (at Sea) with whom she intermarried in Kentish Town Chapel abt. seven Years ago & by whom she hath three Children (to wit) John aged abt. five Years; Henry aged abt. three Years & Thomas aged abt. Eight Months says her said husband Rented a house in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Guineas; lived therein abt. Seven Months & hath quitted same abt. five Years and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 13th. day of April 1758 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Jenkins
