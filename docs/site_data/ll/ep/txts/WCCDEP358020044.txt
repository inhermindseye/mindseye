Saint Clement Danes 7th. Septr 1787

Middlesex & Westmr} to wit James Case of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  Taylor upon his Oath Saith that Francis Purcell an Infant of the Age of Nine years or thereabouts in the Son of William Purcell by Mary his Wife who are both dead that the sd. William Purcell in his life time rented & lived in a House in Vine Street Swallow Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster at the yearly rent of upwards of ten pounds for about three Quarters of a year that he died in such House and that the said Married Purcell the Infant never was bound out Apprentice or did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this 7th. Septr 1787 before me Wm. Achiner D Walker}

James Case
