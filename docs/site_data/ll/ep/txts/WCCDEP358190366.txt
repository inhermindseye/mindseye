Middx  Mary Mason Aged about 24 Years a Vagabond having no habitation or place of above and Lying in the Open Air in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx Upon Oath Says she was born in the Parish of Peddington in the County of Bedford that her Father  Nicholas Mason was a Carpenter and Rented a House in the said Parish of Peddington and paid the poor as this Examinant hath been informed by her Mother and which information she believes to be true Says she never was Marryed was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year.

Sworn the Day of 1763 before}
