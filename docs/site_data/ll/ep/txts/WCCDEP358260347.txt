Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Parr aged about 80 years upon her Oath saith that about Thirty years ago she Intermarried with William Parr her late Husband who has been in dead about Eight years at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields That her said late Husband Never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes  lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice nor did she ever hear him say where he was born And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that previous to her Intermarriage with her said late Husband and being a Singlewoman she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Crespigny a Silversmith the corner of Compton Street in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster  for Four years and upwards at the yearly wages of Five pounds and that after she quitted said Service she never did any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this day of Octr. 1798 before us } E Read 

her Margt. Parr Mark
