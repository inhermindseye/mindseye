Middx Eliz Turner aged 13 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she is the Daugher of Edwd Turner by Mary his Wife both decd. & that her sd. late father abt. 5 Yrs. ago rented a house  in the parish of Iver in the Town & County of Stafford and pd. Mr. Stanborough the Church warden of the sd Parish his proportion towards the Poor Date of the sd. parish of Iver

Sworn Decr. 21st. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz.Turner
