Westmr. to wit James Dillick Aged about Twenty Six Years on his Oath Says he never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes after he was out of his Apprenticeship hereafter mentioned Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Robert Pearman a Cordwainer at Rotchford in the County of Sussex and served the said Robert Pearman for Seven Years and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 18th. day of June 1771 before me W Kelynge

James Dillick
