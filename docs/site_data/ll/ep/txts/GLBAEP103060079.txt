Aldgate Middx

Mary Kyrby  Singlewoman Maketh Oath that upwards of 20 years ago she was a hired Servant by year with the Widdow Prowle in Coptehall Court in Frogmorton Street in the Parish of St. Bartholomew in the City of London with whom she livd three years and had five pounds a year And that she hath gaind no other Settlement Since

Sworn the day of Febr 1746 before us Robert Dennett Richd Ricards

Mary Kirby
