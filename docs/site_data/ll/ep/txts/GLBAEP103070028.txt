Middx ss  Sarah Wells  Widow of Wm Wells Deced Maketh oath that abt. forty Years ago (as he has been informed by her sd late husband in his life time) her sd husband Dwd. his apprenticeship to Roger Bradford a Fisherman of Hammersmith in ye County of Middx & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there And Saith that her sd. husband hath not since gained any Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge & belief And that she hath three Children to Wit Mary Aged abt. twelve Years Marna aged abt. five Years & Sarah aged abt. two Years ye lawfull issue of this Depont theresd husband

Sworn this. 15. day of March 1750 before us  Richd. Ricards  Boulton Mainwaring

her Sarah Wells Mark
