Middx.  Alice Singer aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Robt. Singer with whom she Intermarried at Windsor Church abt. four Years ago says her said husband served four years the first part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Adlard a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and then parted from his Master by Consent and never was assigned over to any Person or Ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year as her said husbd. informed this her and as she very believes; says she hath One Child by her said husband Thomas aged abt. Seven Months that her husband hath Absconded from her for ten Months past, and She being Poor is not able to Maintain her self and Child

Sworn this 21st. March 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

Alice Singer
