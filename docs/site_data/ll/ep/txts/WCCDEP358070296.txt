Middx & Westr} to wit Margaret Mair Aged about Seventy Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of James Mair who has been dead about Six Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House Called or known by the sign or name of the White Hart , Opposite Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and a half at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same and Quitted the same in the Year 1763 and after Quitting the same he never after Rented house paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement neither has this Examinant since her said Husband Death.

Sworn this 19th. day of Sepr 1775 before  David Walker

Margret mair
