Middx  Thomas Wyatt aged about 35 Yrs. upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Mary about ten Years since at the Fleet London by whom he hath one Child (to wit) Martha aged about 9 Yrs. Says he lived a Yearly hired Servt. (whilst a Singleman ) with Mr. Shropshire at the Angell in the Parish of St. Peters in Marlborough in the County of Wilts for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Two pounds ten Shillings or thereabouts & hath left his sd. Service about 20 Yrs. & hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Augt. 10th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

Thomas Wyatt
