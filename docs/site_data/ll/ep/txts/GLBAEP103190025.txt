Middlesex ss}  Susannah Page the wife of Thomas Page maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore that her said husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one William Pay a Shoemaker in the Parish of Saint Mary in the City of Bristol & has not since done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement & that she was lawfully married to her Said husband.

Sworn this 6. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Susannah Page
