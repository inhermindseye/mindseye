Desertion 

St Clement Danes 9th November 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Child  desertion (to wit) Judith Bishop Wife of William Bishop and aged twenty one years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her said Husband on or about the fifth day of February 1793 at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields (by Banns) that sub previous there to (as she is informed and believe, her said Husband served as an Apprentice (under Indres) with Mr. Paybody in Wych Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County for about six years and was there lodged with him said Master during such Service, that he was turned over female Paybody to the Brother to of this Examinant's said Husband but never served him under such Indre or any one else by his consent of neither as she is informed and believes neither hath he since the expiration of his Apprenticeship done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that she hath one Child by her said Husband now living namely Ann Catherine born on or about the 26th day of September last And that her said Husband hath deserted her & then said Child and refused to maintain her whereby she is become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn the 9th day of Novr 1793 before me} Wm. Kitchiner

Judith Bishop
