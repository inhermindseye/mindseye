Middx  Anna Maria Hodson on Behalfe of Oliver Penn a Lunatick on her Oath saith the sd. Oliver Penn lived in & Rented a house near Little Turnstile in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £20 for the Space of Six Months or thereabouts & hath left same about 5 Months Says the sd. Oliver Penn hath not since he left the sd. house Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworne April 10th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

Anna Maria hodson
