Middlesex & Westminster}  passed. to wit Isabella Crafts aged about Thirty three Years on her oath Saith That she Intermarried with John Crafts her late Husband Deceased at the parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about 16 Years ago and by whom She has 4 Children living Vizt. John aged about 15 Years provided for Elizabeth aged about 10 Years, Eleanor Aged about 6 Years and George aged about 4 Years, (unprovided for) Saith that She never Rented a House or Tenement of £10 a Year or paid Taxes and that her said Husband served an Apprenticeship of Seven Years the last 2 Years of which he Served with Mr. John King of Golden Lane in the parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate  Coal Merchant and since which time to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief he has done no other Act Matter or thing whereby he may have Gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 21st. day of April 1785 before me  Sampn Wright Tho: Gilbert
