Middx to wit Jane Burton Aged about Sixty Two Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Alexander Burt who who died about Six Weeks ago and by whom she hath one Child named Thomas now provided for Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Sugar Loaf Court Salisbury Court in the Parish of Saint Bride otherwise of Bridget in the City of London upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds hath quitted said House about Ten Years ago since which time he said Husband never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or otherwise how soever neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 11th. day of May 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Jane Burton Mark
