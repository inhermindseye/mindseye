Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Breeden aged about Nineteen years on her Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly servant with Mr. Samuel Breeden the Corner of Howard Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of One year and an half at the yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly servant for one year.

Sworn this 23d. day of Novr. 1769 before me} R. Butler

her Elizabeth Breeden mark
