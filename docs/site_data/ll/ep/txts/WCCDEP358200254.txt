Middx.  Mary Thompson aged about 60 Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Job Thompson who has been dead about four Months says her said late Husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Carlton a Taylor in Wych Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middx. and never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn 30th. of March 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Mary Thompson
