Middx.  Elizabeth Hobson aged abt. 30 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Hobson gone from her with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about three Years ago and by whom she hath two Children (to wit) John aged about two Years and Henry aged abt. four Months says her said husband before his Inter- marriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with Henry Rooke Esqr. in Carey Street in the aforesaid parish of St. Clement Danes for One Year and an half and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Twelve pounds that he Quitted that Service at the time he was Married to this Examinant and never Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn this 27th. day. of April 1765 , Before} Paul Vaillant

Eliz Hobson
