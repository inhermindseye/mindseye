St Clement Danes February 13th 1792

Middlesex & Westminster } (to wit) Ann Southall Wife of Thomas Southall Upon her Oath Saith that she is now Aged of  about about 33 years upo that she was Married to her present Husband Thomas Southall at the parish Church of St Andrew Holborn on the 5th day of November 1777 (by Banns) that her said Husband then and for several Years before and since Rented & Resided in a House in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of at the Annual Rent of about £38 that he quitted the same about fifteen Months ago and hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Deponent's Knowledge or belief And this Deponent farther u further upon her said Oath Saith that her said Husband Joseph Thomas  Southall did turned her out of the House about three or four years ago and hath ever since refused & still refuses and her to Maintain her this Deponent altho he is is being of Bodily Strenghth and able to maintain her so to do And saith that She being incapable to provide for herself without Assistance hath been is become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 19th: day of February 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Ann Southall

Richardson Overseer Burbidge & Newman Beadles Apprehended Southall on Sunday 26th Febry

Southall taken on the 26th Febry 1792 Sunday

lnot up to the Magistrate Overseers Messr Hogg Johnston & Richardson attended

he was Committed to Clerkenwell for want of Surety

6th March 1792  Southall was Bailed for his Appearance Southall a at the next Genl Quarter Session of the Peace for the County of Middx (before Mr Walker)
