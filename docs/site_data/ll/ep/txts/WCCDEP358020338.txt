Septr 26th. 1791

Middlesex & Westminster to wit  Alice Waud  Widow aged thirty five Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to William Waud her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint John the Evangelist Westminster on the 22nd. Day of Septr 1780 that her said Husband was the Son of - Waud of Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Co: of Middlesex Butcher and was born in his Father House in Clare Market aforesaid that her said Husband served his time with his said Father in Clare Market aforesaid and not under Indenture and never as this Deponent best knows and believes hired as an hired yearly Servant for one Year, Rented any House is Tenement of the Yearly Value of the pounds, paid parish or other rates or Taxes or did any other Act Matter or thing whereby gain any other Settlement than in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid

Sworn this 26th. Day of Septr 1791 before me}

Alice Waud
