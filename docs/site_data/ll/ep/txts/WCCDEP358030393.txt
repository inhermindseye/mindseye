Westmr. 

Wm. Netherclift upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house in White horse Yard in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £18 for the Space of three Years or thereabouts and quitted the same about 4 Yrs. since Says he has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his Knowledge or beleif Says he has a Son named John aged upwds. of 12 Yrs.

Sworn 17th. June 1747 before  Wm: Jones .

William Netherclift
