Middlesex ss}  Ann Gravidear maketh oath that she is the widow of Francis Gravidear deced who was by birth a Frenchman & never did any act to gain a Settlemt. in England to her Knowledge or belief That previous to her said marriage she was bound Apprentice by the then parish officers of St Matthew Bethmall Green in the sd County to one William Cotter of St Mary Whitechapel Middx  Carpenter where she Continued with him for about five Years When this Deponent removed with her said Master into Queen street in the parish of St Botolph with Aldgate in the sd County Where she Continued with her sd Master as his Apprentice for upwards of the space of three months When her sd Master went from thence to places to her unknown & left her behind & she never afterwards lived with him elsewhere That Since such her Apprenticeship and her said husbands death she has not been married or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this. 8th. day of September 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Gravidear
