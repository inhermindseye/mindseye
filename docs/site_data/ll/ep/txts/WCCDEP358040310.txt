Middx  Jas. Barrett upon Oath saith he Served two Years & upwards the first Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Straghan a Slater at the Kings Island in Limmerick in the Kingdom of Ireland and never was Assigned over to any Person rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif and that he hath a Wife and a Son now with him viz Daniel aged abt. twenty two Years who never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Se

Sworn the 9th Day of January 1748 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Jas. Barrett
