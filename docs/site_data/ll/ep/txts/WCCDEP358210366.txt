Middx & Westmr.}  Elizth. Marks aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Ann Thorpe a Baker in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of One Year and half at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service about Six Years ago and hath not since Rented house Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

On Enquiry This is false Marks never having lived with Mrs. Thorpe above 3 Months
