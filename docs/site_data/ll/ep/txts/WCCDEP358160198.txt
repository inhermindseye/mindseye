Middx  The Examination of Walter Sterley Passed from the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middx to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the same County Taken the 24th. Day of Novr. 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. in the Year 1714 he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Isaac Stretch Watchmaker at Birmingham in ye. County of Warwick & lived with him abt. one Year when his sd. Master Dyed & further saith that he was never assigned over to any Person whatever but after the Death of his sd. Master continued to live with his sd. Master Brother for the Space of four Years or thereabouts & then came to London (having at that Time abt. one Year & half of his apprentiship to serve) And agreed to live with one Mr. Thos. Allen in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx for one Year & halfe being the Remainder of his sd. Apprentiship at Fifty two Shillings P Year Wages & this Exaiant further saith

that he never rented any House paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any other Person or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Walter Sterly
