Middx  Sarah Brooks aged 70 Years and upwards Lodging in Clement's Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she was Married to Thomas Brooks at the Fleet about the Year 1714 whom she believes to be Dead as she has not heard of him for several Years says her said Husband hath informed her That he was bound an Apprentice by an Indenture for Seven Years to Mr. Stoneystreet a Farrier in Nightingale Lane in Red Cross Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex London and with his said Master there served about Two Years, then intermarried with this Examinant and Inlisted for a Soldier That he never rented a House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes also says the said Mr. Stoneystreet hath informed her that her said Husband served him as an Apprentice two Years.

Sworn the 18th. day of July 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Sarah Brooks

Resworn the 21st. day of July 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant Fran: Bedwell
