Middx ss  Esther Smith  widow of Jonas Smith deced Maketh oath that about Twenty seven years ago her late Father John Birkham deced & who was by Trade a Taylor Rented a house of Thirty Pounds by the year in the parish of Saint Martin Outwich in the City of London and lived in the same for about eight Months & died there And did not afterwards gain any subsequent Settlement And this Deponent farther saith that after her said Fathers Death she Married her said late Husband Jonas Smith who was by Birth a Norway man & served his Apprenticeship there to ye Trade of a Taylor And as he informed this Depont. & as this Deponent believes And this Deponent farther saith that her sd. husband to her knowledge or belief never Rented a house of Ten Pounds by the year nor served any parish offices nor lived as a hired servant by the year Nor did any Act to Acquire a legal settlement during his residence in the Kingdom of Great Britain And this Deponent farther saith that since the respective Deaths of her sd Father and husband or before she this Deponent has not lived as a hired servant by the year Nor Rented a house of Ten pounds a year a upwards nor done any Act to Acquire a settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 4th- day of April 1758 before us Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Esther Smith
