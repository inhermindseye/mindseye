Westmr. 

Edwd. Pickard upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Mary at the Fleet London upwards of four Years since by whom he hath one Child (to wit) Mary aged 2 Yrs. & upwards Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served four Years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Thomas Babe in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says at the End of the sd. four Years this Examint, Mother gave a Sum of Money to his master for the remainder of his time & he was discharged by Consent & never was assigned over to any other person rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 27th. 1747 before  Wm: Jones .

Edward Pickard
