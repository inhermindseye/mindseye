Middx.  Margt. Winstanley of Chandois Street  Covent Garden upon Oath says that she was well acquainted with the father & Mother of Thos. Bussell an Infant aged abt. 8 Yrs. says he was born in the house of Mrs. Jennings in the Old Bailey in the Parish of St. Sepulchre London & that Thos. Bussell the father of the said Infant Rented a house in Golden Lane in the Parish of St. Giles without Cripplegate London of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards as she hath been informed & verily believes

Sworn the 22d. of Sepr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham Margaret Winstanley
