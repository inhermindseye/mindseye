Westmr  Deodatus Collis upon his Oath saith he was never married Says he lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Ealing at the White Bear Alehouse in Old Street Square in the parish of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex for five Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds meat drink Washing and lodging and hath left the sd. Mr. Ealings Service upwards of four Years Says he hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 11th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Deodatus Collis
