Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Barbara Richardson aged about 24 years upon her Oath Saith that on or about the 24th day of January 1790 she Intermarried with her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields by whom she hath living one Child Vizt. Esther Aged about Nine years That her said late Husband rented and lived in a Shop and Parlour in Great Earl Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for near twelve Months at the yearly rent of Eleven Pounds and upwards That he hath left she said Premises about Five years since which time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 10th day of Decr 1799 before us} Ws Woodnough Saml Edwards

Har Barbara Richardson Mark
