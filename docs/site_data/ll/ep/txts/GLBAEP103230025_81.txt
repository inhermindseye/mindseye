Middlesex ss  James Turnbull maketh oath that his late father John Turnbull deced heretofore rented a house in Flushing Yard in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & during such his Residence therein he was charged to and paid Poors Rates for the same & in Consequence thereof died legally Settled in the said Parish where this Deponent was born That he this Depont. was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds nor ever served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right That he hath a wife named Catherine & four Children namely Elizabeth aged about fourteen Years Catherine aged about twelve years Eleanor aged about Seven years and Jane aged about four years the lawful issue of this Deponent by his said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 8. day of March 1787 before  R Davies

James Turnbull
