Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Joshua Prosser aged about 48 years upon his Oath saith that whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Fras. Thompson who kept the Nags Head Tavern in Leaden hall Street in the Parish of Saint Peters Cornhill in the City of London for 4 years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Twelve Guineas that he hath quitted said Service about Eleven years ago since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year Rented any House Tenement at or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of Janry. 1788 N Bond Maddington

Joshua Prosser
