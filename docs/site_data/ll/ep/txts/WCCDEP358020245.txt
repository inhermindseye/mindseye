Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit) Susannah Bentley of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon her Oath saith that she has been informed by Susannah Snowball Widow, and which she believes to be true that the said Susannah Snowball Rented and lived in Apartments in Plough Court Fetter Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the City of London at the Yearly Rent of ten Guineas for some time that she quitted her said Apartments about two Years ago and since then has not done any Act whereby she may have gained any other or Subsequent Settlement to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 11th- Day of Janry 1791 before Sampn Wright W Addington}

Sus Bentley
