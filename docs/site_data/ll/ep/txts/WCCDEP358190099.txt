Middx  Henry Habbas Aged abt. Fifty Years Lodging in May Pole Alley in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Upon Oath Saith that he Intermarryed with his Wife  Mary who is now abt. the Age of Fifty in the Parish Church of Kings near in Hamshire about Seventeen Years ago Says before his Intermarryage with the sd. Mary & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Fras. Cottinton  Esqr . in Arguile Buildings for four Years or thereabts. at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds & hath left his sd. Service abt. Nineteen Years Says the Coach House and Stables were in the Ragshead Inn Yard in Swallow Street in the Parish of St. George Hannover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . and this Examinant laid at the Ragshead Inn during the last two Months of his sd. Service

Sworn the 9th. day of Febry 1761 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The mark of Henry Habbas

Upon Mr. Pontens Complt.
