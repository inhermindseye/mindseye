Middx  Ann Hitchcock aged about 60 Years removed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds by Order dated the 26th. Day of January Instant upon her Oath saith She Intermarried with her sd. late Husband  Thomas Hitchcock at the Parish Church of St. Michael Basishaw London Says the sd. Tho: Hitchcock before his sd. Intermarriage with this Exaiant & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with the Rt: Honble the Earl of Stamford in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx about 2 Years at the Yearly Wages of £10 or thereabouts & was Discharged from the sd. Earl Service in Norfolk Street aforesaid Says the said Thos. Hitchcock never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office neither hath this Examinant since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 26th. Janry 1744 before me} Wm. Jones

Ann Hitchcocks
