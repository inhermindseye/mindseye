Westminster  Charlotte Buckley  Wido . of Godfrey Buckley who hath been dead upwards of Seven Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Godfrey Buckley about 18 Years ago at the Fleet London Says her sd. late husband upwards of 20 Years since lived in and rented a house near Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £45 for several Yrs. as this Examinant has been informed & beleives Says her sd. late husband never (after he left the sd. house nor has this Examinant since his Death rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 21st. 1747 before} Danl Gach

The Mark of Charlotte Buckley
