Middx  Alice Potter aged about Sixty Years on her Oath say's she is the Widow of Samuel Potter who died about Twenty Years ago and by whom she hath One Child named Samuel who went to the East Indies several Years ago says that being a Widow and without Child or Children Except the abovementioned Samuel her Son who was provided for she lived a hired Yearly Servant to her Brother in Law Benjn. Sharp  a Hatter in Houghton Street Clare Markett  for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said service about Seventeen Years ago since which time she hath never gained any other Subseqt. Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1779 before R Butler

her Alice Potter  Mark
