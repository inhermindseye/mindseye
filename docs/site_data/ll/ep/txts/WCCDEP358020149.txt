Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Stapleford aged about 70 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Stapleford her late husband at the Fleet London about 48 years ago that her said husband has been dead about 14 Months That he was born in Long Lane in the Borough in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry where his Father kept house for several years that her said Husband never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year in any Place excepting the Temple which is an extraparochial place

Sworn this 19th. day of May 1789 R Butler D Walker

her Sarah Stapleford Mark
