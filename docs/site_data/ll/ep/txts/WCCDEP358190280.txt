Middx  Ann Castledine aged about 31 Years upon Oath Says She is the Wife of Michael Castledine ( Gone from her ) with whom She Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex in the Month of July about six Years since by whom She hath two Children  Ann aged about four Years and George aged upwards of a Year Says her said Husband whilst a Singleman lived a hired Yearly Servant with his Uncle  Jonathan Castledine in the Parish of St. Bartholomew by the Exchange in the City of London for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds Meat Drink and Lodging and never after lived a Yearly Servant Rented a House or paid any Taxes as She hath been informed by her said Husband which Information She verily believes to be true

Sworn the 5th. day of October 1762 before } Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Ann Castledine
