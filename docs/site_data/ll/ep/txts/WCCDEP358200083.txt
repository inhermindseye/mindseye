Middx  Mary Heath Aged about Twenty five Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Nicholls a Colourman in the Parish of Putney in the County of Surry upwards of One Year and a half hath quitted said Service about Five Weeks And further Upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the Eigtht Day of February Last she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Wm. Says One William Greenoway Apprentice to Messrs. Lowton and Clare

Callico Printers had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of May Last and at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the 6th of March 1764 : Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Heath

Upon Mr Jones Ovr Complt J. W
