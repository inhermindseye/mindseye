Westmr. 

Miles Douglass aged abt. 59 Years removed from the Parish of Christchurch in the County of Surry by Order dated the 12th. Instant upon Oath saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Thos. Axton a Hatt maker in Clements Inn Passage near Clare market in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and has not since the Expiration of his said Apprentiship rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Year by Servt for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 17th. 1749 before}

The mark of Miles Douglas
