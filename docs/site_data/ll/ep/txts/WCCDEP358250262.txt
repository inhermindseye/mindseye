Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Harris aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That her father George Harris has Rented and Premises at Shaftesbury in the Parish of Saint James in the County of Dorset for these fifteen years last past at the Annual Rent of Two hundred Pounds or thereabouts

Sworn this 17th. day of Decr. 1796  R. Ford J Mathew

her Hannah Harris Mark
