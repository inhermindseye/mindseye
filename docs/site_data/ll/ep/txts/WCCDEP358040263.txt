Westmr. 

Mary Oakley aged about 66 Years upon Oath saith she was never married and that abt. Four Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Richd. Read in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds for one Year & upwards and has not since she Quitted her sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 1st. Octr. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Oakley
