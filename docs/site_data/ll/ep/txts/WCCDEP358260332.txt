Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Leatherborrow aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. James in Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Eight Months since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Septr. 1798 before me } John Collick

her Elizabeth Leather  borrow Mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
