Westmr.  William Hollis aged about 33 Years Upon Oath says, he was bound Apprentice at the Age of Thirteen Years by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the poor of the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. to Mr. Walter Burton a Sword Cutler in Little Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. aforesaid and served about Nine Years of his said Apprenticeship and then his said Master died and never was Assigned Over to any Person and further on his Oath saith that he never Rented house paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 26th. day of November 1765 before} Paul Vaillant R Kilsha

Wm. Hollis
