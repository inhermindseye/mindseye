Middx  Sarah Smithson aged about 22 Years now a Servant to Mr. Nowlton in Butcher Row in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon Oath says she served Eight Years Apprenticeship with the said Mr. Nowlton in Butcher Row aforesaid and hath been out of her said Apprenticeship about One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when Born says one Mr. Thomas Gribble Gentn. who lodges at a Gown and Sack Warehouse in New Round Court in the Strand had carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the Month of August last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Thomas Gribble and no other person did beget the same.

Sworn the 16th. day of March 1765 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Sarah Smithson
