Middlesex and Westminster}  A. to wit Jatustatiana Fell aged about Sixty four years on her Oath says she Intermarried to John Fell deceased about 40 years ago at the Fleet London and by whom she hath one Child living now provided for Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Cumberland Court formerly Called Kings Aruns Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for three years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted said House upwards of Thirty years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. 1784 before me}

her Jatustatiana Fell mark
