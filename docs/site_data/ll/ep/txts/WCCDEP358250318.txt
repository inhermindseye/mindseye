Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah  Turner aged about Sixty one years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Surridge in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months at (the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Thirty years since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Octr. 1797 before us Ru Clarke N Bond

her Hannah Turner Mark

Witness B Newman
