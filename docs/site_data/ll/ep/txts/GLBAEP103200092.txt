Middlesex ss}  Mary Bland  Singlewoman maketh oath that about Thirteen Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Day a Merchant in Great Bell Alley in the Parish of Saint Stephen in Coleman Street in the City of London & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere Nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since Such her Servitude else- -where That She hath two Children namely Matthew James Lemmon aged about twenty two Months who was born in the Parish of St George in the County of Middlesex and another Child a Boy not yet baptized aged about five Months who was born in Brown Bear Alley in the Parish St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County both of which Children are illegitimate & Bastards by reason this Depont was never Married & that one Matthew Lemmon late of Brown Bear Alley aforesd Smith is the Fathr of the Sd Children

Sworn this. 21. day of Janry 1784 before R Davies J Hawley

Mary Bland
