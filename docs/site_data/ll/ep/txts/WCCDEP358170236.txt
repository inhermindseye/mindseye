Middx  Geo: Smith aged about Forty Two Years upon his Oath saith that on or abt. the 29th. Day of Septr. 1737 he Intermarried with his now Wife  Frances at the Fleet London & that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentishipp with one Mr. Cha: James in the Parish of Isleworth in the County of Middx  Waterman & hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship about 21 Years that since his sd. Apprentiship he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Yr. Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or Beleife that he hath 2 Childn. viz: Eliz : Aged near 3 Yrs. & Thomas aged abt. Six Weeks & haveing broken his Legg & being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne the 26th. May 1742 before} Samuel. Clarke  Jas: Gronous

George Smith
