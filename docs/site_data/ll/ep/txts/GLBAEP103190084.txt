Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Cartwright the widow of Joseph Cartwright deced maketh oath that divers Years ago her said late husband rented a house known by the Sign of the Hoy & Helmet of the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was chargd to & paid Poors Rates for the same That he did not afterwds Rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since her said late husbands death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this 19 day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Cartwright
