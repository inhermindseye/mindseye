Westmr.  Judith Chater  Wido . of Joseph Chater who dyed about two Years since upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Joseph Chater in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about nine Years ago and that her sd. late husband Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £20 next Door to the Angel Inn in the Back of St. Clements in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and lived therein about one Year and hath left the sd. house about Seven Years and never after nor has this Examinant since his decease rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 4th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Judith Chater
