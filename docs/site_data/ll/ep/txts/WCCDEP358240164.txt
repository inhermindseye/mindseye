St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St John Hackney  (to wit) Jane Cook aged 31 years or thereabouts Wife of William Cook (who is now from her) Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband William Cook at the parish Church of St. Ann within the Liberty of Westminster in the County of of Middlesex on the 18th June 1781 (by Banns) that since then her said Husband Rented and Resided in an House in the parish of Saint John Hackney in the County of Middlesex upwards of Six Months at the Annual Rent of £14 and paid one Quarters Poor Rate of for the same, that she hath one Child and by her said Husband now living with her this Examinant namely John born on the 9th. day of June last that her said Husband hath not (since he quitted the said House in St John's Hackney) Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 17th day of Novr Septr. 1792 before Us  Sampr Wright  Gd: Mitchell

her Jane Cook 's Mark

Edwd. Burbidge
