Middlesex ss}  Roger Wink Maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound to & Sewed the Whole of his Apprenticeship with one Stephen Row a Cordwainer in Redcroso Street in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds aycar or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Jaxepor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of Septemr 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Roger Wink
