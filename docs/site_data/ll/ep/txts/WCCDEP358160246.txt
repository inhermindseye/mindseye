Middx  The Examination of Jane Gibson aged about fifty Years taken the 31st- Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about fourteen Years ago her Husband Irtun Gibson rented House in Ironmonger Row in the Parish of St- Luke in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of nine Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of four Years & upwards & paid all Parochiall Taxes  that her sd. Husband hath not since that Time rented any other House paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & he being now Poor & old is not able to maintaine himselfe & Wife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Jane Gibson
