Priscilla Rust aged about 25 Years Lodging in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married kept House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year That her Father Joseph Rust was a Victualler & lived in and rented a House at Hadley in the County of Suffolk for about Three Months, cannot say what Rent he paid that he dyed in the same about Twenty Years since as she has been informed and which Information she believes to be true And further upon her voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And that Richard Williams a Journeyman Taylor who lodges at Mr. Trotters the Corner of Peter Street  Clare Market  did beget such Child or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of March 1761 and at several times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 11th day of July 1761 Before}
