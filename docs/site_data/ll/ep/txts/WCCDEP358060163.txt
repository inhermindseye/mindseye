Ann Bates upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. husband at Woolverhampton in the County of Stafford about 18 months since & has by him one Child (viz) aged about 3 weeks Says she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband Served his Apprentiship with a Screw maker at Woolverhampton aforesaid and never rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 1751 before}
