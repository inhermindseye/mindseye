Westr to wit Margaret Tyrer Aged about Thirty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Joseph Tyrer who has been dead about Three Years and with who she intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn about Eleven Years ago & by whom she hath no Children now living, says her said Husbands Rented and lived in a House on Saffron Hill Ely Rents for Seven Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same that he Quitted the said House upwards of Three Years ago and never after Rented House paid Taxes on otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement - Neither has this Examinant since her said Husband Death.

Sworn this 14th day of Augt 1775 before} R Butler

Margrt Tyrer
