St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Wesminster} (to wit) Ann Macklin  Widow Aged 53 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that about Six years ago and since the Decease of her late Husband John Macklin Silk Dyer (to whom she was married at St Clement Danes Church in the strand near 21 years ago) she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Dawen at No. 38 in Lawrence Lane in the parish of St. Martin le bow London for the space of three year & an half together at Seven pounds a year Wages, and since she quitted that Service she hath not hired lived as an hired yearly Servant for one whole year together Rented a Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid any parish Taxes contracted Matrimony or done any Act Matter whatsoever which upon knowley or belief may have gained her a settlement in the parish or place And this Deponent further said that she was removed by Orders of Removal from the parish of St Clement Danes be the said parish of St. Mary Le Bow London in or about December last and or the settlement by the service aforesaid and was Maintained with their poor at Hoxton in the County of Middlesex about five Months to which Order of Removal they have not at any time appealed and that she is unable to provide for herself without some Assistance and has therefore

applied to the said parish of St. Mary le bow for Relief which they have refused without She some legally passed

Sworn the 29th Novr 1791 before us  Wm Hitchiner D Walker

Ann Macklin
