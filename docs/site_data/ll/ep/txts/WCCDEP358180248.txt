Middx  Ann Porter aged about 30 Years upon her Oath saith that she was never married that about Eight Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Wabraven in Arundell Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £5 for the Space of One Year & upwards that since she left her sd Service she hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & further upon her Oath saith that on the 1st: Day of June last she was Delivered of two Male Bastard Children at the house of Mr. Richmond in Witch Street in the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes one of which is since Dead & the other hath been baptized Thomas & is become chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same And that Thomas Ingram  Aprentice to Mr. Vokes a Dyer in Hatton Garden did begett the said Children on her Body she at such time liveing a Servt. with the sd. Mr. Vokes there the sd. Thomas Ingram became Acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of Augt. in the Year 1749 & at several other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Children of which she was Delivered as aforesd. & that the sd. Thomas Ingram & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne Novr. 6th. 1744 before}
