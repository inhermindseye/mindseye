Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Eleanor Hunter and Mary Hunter upon their Oaths severally say And first this Deponent Eleanor Hunter for herself saith that about Thirty three years ago she Intermarried with Edward Hunter her late husband (who has been dead about Sixteen years) at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster by whom she hath a Son living of the name of Edward That her said late Husband never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or where he was born she this Deponent doth not know That previous to her Intermarriage with her said late Husband and whilst she was a singlewoman she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Martin Frenchdove in the  Windmill Street in the Haymarket  for about in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for about Five years at the yearly wages of Four pounds and that after she quitted said Service she never lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And this Deponent Mary Hunter for herself saith that on or about the first day of May 1793 she Intermarried with the said Edward

Hunter the younger at the Parish Church of Saint Anne's Westminster by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Edward aged about Twenty Months That she has been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that her said Husband was born in Middlesex Hospital about 26 years ago That he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months ever bound an Apprentice or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Settlement in his own Right

Sworn this 27th. day of Novr. 1795 before N Bond W Kinnard

her Eleanor Hunter mark

her Mary Hunter mark

Witness B Newman
