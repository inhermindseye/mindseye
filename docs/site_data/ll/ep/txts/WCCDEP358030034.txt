Middx  Ann Hawkins  Wido . of Jno. Hawkins upon her Oath saith that before her Intermarriage with the sd. John Hawkins (to wit) on the 1st. Day of May 1735 she intermarried with one Wm. Hughes in the Parish Church of St. Paul Covt. Garden by whom she hath one Child (to wit) Willm. aged about Ten Years Says her sd. Husband Willm Hughes hath been dead about Seven Years Says before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with one Mr. Griffith in Smith Street in the Parish of St. John the Evangelist in the Libty of Westmr. Says the sd. Wm. Hughes lived with the sd. Mr. Griffith two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds or thereabouts-the first ten Months of the sd. Term before their sd. Intermarriage and the Remr, Part after their sd. Intermarriage but under the same Contract that was made before Marriage Says the sd. Wm. Hughes never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochiall Office or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says during the time she continued the Wido. of the sd. Wm. Hughes she did not rent ten pounds a Year pay Parish Taxes or otherwise gain a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Octr. 28th. 1745 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of  Ann Hawkins
