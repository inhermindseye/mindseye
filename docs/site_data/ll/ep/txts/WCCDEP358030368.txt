Westmr 

William Simms upon Oath saith he hath two Childn. (to wit) Wm. aged abt. 12 Yrs. Ann aged about nine Years Says that about 11 Yrs. ago he lived in and rented a house in the Parish of Minchin hampton in the County of Glocester of the Yearly Rent of Ten Guineas for Six months or there abouts Says he has not since he quitted the said house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Wm. Simms
