He was a Married Man then in such Service a to did not pass him she came back to us (to wit) Joseph Eden Aged 41 years or thereabout upon his Oath Saith That he Lived an Hired yearly Servt . with the Mr. Brook Bridges at the Parish of Holling bury in the County of Northampton for upwards of Twelve Months at the Yearly Wages of £15:15s- That he quitted his said Master's Service about 13 years ago That since he quitted his said Master's he this Examinant hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have Gained any Settlement elsewhere than in the said Parish of  Hollingbury in the said County of Northampton

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. 1785 before us}
