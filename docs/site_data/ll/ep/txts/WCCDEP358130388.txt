Middx.  Mary Wilson aged abt. 39 years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Wilson & that before her intermarriage with the said John Wilson she intermarried with Jno. Carrol in the Parish Church of St. Ann Westmr . on the 21st. day of Decr. 1736. by whom she hath one Daur (to wit) Mary aged abt. Nineteen years says the said John Carrol hath been dead upwards of Eighteen years & was a Native of Ireland & never gained any Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief says her sd. Daughter Mary Carrol was born in St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx says that she this Examinant never rented ten Pounds a year was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. says upwards of Twenty Eight years ago her father Henry Smith rented a house in Houghton Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the yearly rent of Twenty four Pounds & lived therein abt. two yrs. & after he quitted the said house never Rented ten Pounds a yr. or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 27th of July 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Wilson
