Middx  Eliza. Coleman aged about 60 Years Widow of Roger Coleman  deced upon her Oath saith that she intermarryed with her sd. late Husband in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex abt. Twenty Years since That her sd. late Husband lived in & Rented a Cellar in Castle Strt. by the Mews of the Yearly Rent of £12. & lived in the same for 6 Months or thereabouts & hath left the same abt. 6 Months that the sd. Cellar is an Intire Appartmt. of itself without having any Comunication with the other Part of the house & Consists of 3 Rooms 3 fire Places a Necessary House & Water & hath but one Door way which comes into the Street that this Examints: sd. late Husband in his Life time nor this Examint. since his Death hath not Rented Ten pounds a Yr. for the Space of 6 Weeks or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 6th. April 1744 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Eliza. Coleman
