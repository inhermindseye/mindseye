Middx  Susan Whitcomb  Wido . of Wm- Whitcomb aged Sixty Years & upwards upon her Oath saith that her sd. Late Husband was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentispp with one Mr. Rd. Whitcomb in Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Butcher that her sd. Husband in his Life Time nor hath she since his Death ever rented any House pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her Knowledge or Beleife

Sworne the 1st: Day of July 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Susan Whitcomb
