Middx to wit Ann  Hannah Chambers Aged about Twenty Eight Years On her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paids says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one William White at the Fox and Hounds at Greens Head Green in the Parish of Darne in the County of Kent for 2 Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her on Service upwards of Two Years ago since which time of hath Gained no other Subsequent Settlement And furt on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 29th. day of Ma Instant She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the  Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of William and is now Chargeable to the said Parish She being Poor and und to Maintain the same And further says that one William Hughes a Porter to Mr. Mac Cray Oil & Colourman No: 28 Red Lyon Street White Chapel is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his said Mr. Mc. Cray's house the latter End of July last and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which She was so delivered as aforesaid & that the said William Hughes and no other person did beget the same and that he is the Real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 1st. day of May 1780 before  Abrm Baley

her Ann Hannah Chambers Mark
