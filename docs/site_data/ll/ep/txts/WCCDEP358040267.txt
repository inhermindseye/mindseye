Middx 

Ann Turner aged about 19 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes that she was born in the Parish of Thame in the County of Oxford where her father lived in and rented a house and Ground of the Yearly Value of £12 and upwds. for sevl. Years and paid Scot & Lot and there dyed

Sworn the 20th. Octr. 1748 before
