Middx.  Michl. Curtis upon Oath saith he served Seven years Apprenticeship with Mr Thos. Cole a Smith at Cirencester in the County of Gloucester & never rented ten Pounds a Year pd. parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year.

Sworn Septr. 22d 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Michl. Curtis
