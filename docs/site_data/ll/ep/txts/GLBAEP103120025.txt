Middlesex ss}  Joseph Brigens a Singleman maketh oath That his Father John Brigens is now living and resides in the parish of new church in Birmingham in the County of Warwick as this Deponent verily believes That his Said Father is now legally Settled there by means of his having been Charged to & paying Poors Rates in the Said Parish That he this Depont. was born there and never did any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this 18th day of December 1772 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Joseph Brigens
