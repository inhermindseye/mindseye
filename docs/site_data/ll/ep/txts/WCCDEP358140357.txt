Middx.  Andrew Loch aged about 63 Years Upon Oath says he Rented a house in Change Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £18 lived therein about Six Years and hath quitted Same abt. Seven years and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One year.

Sworn the 8th. day of March 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

Andrew Loch
