Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Byam of Saint Clements Workhouse on behalf of Chas. Rume an Infant aged about four years Maketh Oath and Saith That she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that the said Child was born in Iewin Street in the Parish of Saint Giles without Cripplegate in the City of London And that she is informed and believes Father and Mother thereof are both Dead.

Sworn this 23rd. day of Janry. 1797 M Addington Robinson

E Byam
