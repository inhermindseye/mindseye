Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Elizth. Watson aged 23 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes  over lived or hired yearly Servant for one year or ever served any Apprenticeship that her Father Rented and lived in a House in Church Lane in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent at 14 pounds year that she lived with him as part of his Family that her Father has been dead about 3 years that since his Decease she hath done no Act matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any other Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1786  Thos Parker  John Croft

her Elizth Watson Mark
