Westmr.  Dorothy Usher upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes that her father Thomas Usher deced. lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds in Mansfield Street in the parish of St. Mary at Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex for several Years and that her late mother continued to live in and rent the same house after the Death of her father till the time of her own decease which happened about four Yrs. ago.

Sworn Octr. 26th. 1749 before H Fielding

The Mark of Dorothy Usher
