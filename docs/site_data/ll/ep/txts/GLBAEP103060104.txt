Aldgate Middx

Margaret Merridith  Singlewoman Maketh Oath that the last place where she livd as a hired Servant by the year was with Joshua Brooks a Cooper in the Parish of Saint Olave in Southwark in the County of Surry with whom she livd upwards of two years and hath not livd in any other place as a hired Servant by the year Since And that she never was marryed

Sworn 10th day of June 1748 before Robert Dennett Richd. Ricards

the mark of Margaret Merridith
