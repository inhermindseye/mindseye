Middx. and Westmr.} to wit, Jane Davis aged abt. 25 Years upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was married and that on the 8th day of September 1758, she was delivered of a male bastard Child in the house of Mr. Fox the sign of the Swan in Vere Street Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx. which hath since been Baptized Edward . says that one Edwd. Jones a Shoemaker who lodges at Mr. Chas. Thomas a Shoemaker in Queens Court Holborn did beget the said Male Bastard Child on her body of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Edwd. Jones and no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day of Novr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Jane Davies

Upon the Complaint of Joseph Burnthwaite Overseer of the Poor
