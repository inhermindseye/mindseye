Middx ss  Mary Upton Aged about Seventy Years, on her Oath Says, she Intermarried with Joseph Upton at the Parish Church of Saint Bennetts London , about Thirty Nine Years ago, and who has been dead about Twelve Years; Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children, She lived a hired Yearly Servant with her brother in Law Thomas Oliver , at the Swan in Butcher Row , Temple Bar , in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes , in the Liberty of Westminster , for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, and after leaving her said Service she hath never gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 23d day of June 1775 - before  Tho Lane

her Mary Upton Mark
