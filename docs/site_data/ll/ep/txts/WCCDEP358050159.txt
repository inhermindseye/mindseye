Westmr.  Eliz Ward aged about 44 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Willm. Ward who dyed upwards of two Years since Says she intermarried with the sd. Willm. Ward in the Parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West London about 8 Yrs. ago and that her sd. late husband lived in & rented the Inn Alehouse rear Clements Inn Gate in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Seven pounds for one Yr. or thereabouts and there dyed and this Examinant further Says she has not Since the Decease of the said Edm. Ward rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for

Westr.  one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 2d. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Eliz; Ward
