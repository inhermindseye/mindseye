Middx.  Robt. Fitzhenry aged abt. 65 Years lodging at Mr. Tippers in Blackmoor Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx. Upon Oath says he Rented a house in Wardour Street in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty five pounds for two Years of thereabt. and hath quitted same abt. five Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year nor paid Parish Taxes. Says he has a Wife named Ann aged about fifty four Years

Sworn the 15th. day of January 1761 . before  Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Robt. Fitz Henry

Upon Mr. Pontens Complt. J: P:
