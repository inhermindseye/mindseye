Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Parker Aged about 21 years upon her Oath Saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs Dawson No 15 Great Russell Street Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields for twenty Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she quitted said service about Ten Months since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belife

Sworn this 21st day of October 1799 before us G S Goodenough John Collick

Her Sarah Parker Mark
