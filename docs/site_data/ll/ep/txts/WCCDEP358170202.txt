Middx  Eliz: Walker  Wido . upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her later Husband Joseph Walker abt. Eight Years ago at Burton upon Trent that her Husband hath been Dead abt. Five Weeks that he never rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for one Year that he was bound an Apprentice to one Wm. Hascott in the Parish of Comerford in the County of Stafford Staymaker for the Term of Eight Years & Served him as An Apprentice in the Sd. Parish of Comerford for the Space of Seven Years & an halfe & then his Sd. Master Dyed & this Examinants Sd. Husband had his Indentures Delivered him & did not serve the Remaining Part of his Sd. Apprentiship with any other Person

Sworne the 19th. of Janry 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Elizabeth Walker
