Middx & Westmr.}  Elizth. Walker aged about 22 Years on her Oath says she never was marrd. Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Robt. Walker Rented and lived in a House in Bennetts Court in the Psh of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for sevl. Years at the yearly Rent of 8 Guineas & paid Land Tax due for the same and furr. upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Childn. wch. is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born says that one Willm. Ogle a late Major in a Regiment but in what Regiment she does not know & who late lived at Mrs. Bacons near the Admiralty Spring Gardens and who now uses as she believes Mary bone Coffee house has had carnal knowledge of her Body at Different times for upwards of two Years last past at Diffr. Lodgings both in Town and Country at one of which he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the sd. Major  Wm. Ogle & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 9th. Day of Novr. 1776 M Addington.

her Elizth. Walker Mark.
