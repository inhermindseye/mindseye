Middx  Richd. Moore upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to John Archibald of Hogsdon  Fanstick maker and after having lived with him upwards of two Years was assigned over for the Remainder of his time to John West  Fanstick maker and lived the last three Years of his Apprentiship with the said John West in Water a Star Street in Bridewel Precinct London and that he never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 14th. 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell

Richd Moor
