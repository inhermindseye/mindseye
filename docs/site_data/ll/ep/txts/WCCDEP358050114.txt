Westmr.  Thomas Smedley upon Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr Geo Collins a Carpenter and Served the last Five Years of his Apprenticeship with the sd. Mr Collins in Fitchfield Street in the Parish of St. Mary le bow in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif Says he intermarried with his Wife Ann at the fleet London about 8 yrs. ago by whom he hath his Children (viz) Thomas aged about 5 Years and George aged about Six months.

Sworn Octr. 10th. 1749 before}
