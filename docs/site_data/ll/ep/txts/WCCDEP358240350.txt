St Clement Danes 9th Novr 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Paul Covent Garden  Removed bef from Libty of the Rolls by Order not Appealed agst  (to wit) Catherine Sayer aged 26 years or thereabouts Wife of John Sayer Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her said Husband John Sayer at the parish Church of Allhallows Broad Street in the City of London (by Banns) on or about the eighteenth day of March 1784 by that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and Resided in a House in Brydges Street in the parish of St Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for about Nine Months at the Annual Rent of £28 or thereabouts that he quitted the same about Christmas 1790 And hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that she hath no Children by her said Husband and living that her said Husband hath deserted her & neglected and referred to maintain her whereby she is become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that she was removed by Order of Removal from the Liberty of the Rolls to the said parish

of St Paul Covent Garden on or about the 22d day of December 1791 & that she is informed & believes there has been no Appeal yet the said Order

Sworn the 9th day of November 1793 before us } Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Cath Sayer 's Mark

Witness Garrett
