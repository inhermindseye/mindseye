Middlesex & Westmr.}  William May Aged about Twenty four Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Maria his Wife at Sharford in the County of Surry about Two Years ago and by whom he hath no Children living and that his said Wife now lives with her Friends at Guildford and no ways trouble to this Examinant Says that before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Thomas Ledier a Farmer at West Horsley in the County of Surry for one Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds five Shillings and quitted same upwards of Three Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th. day of July 1776 before  Wm Kitchiner

his William May Mark
