Middx to wit James Mango Aged about Sixteen years on his Oath says he was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . by Indenture bearing Date the fifth Day of May 1780 to William Hughes of High Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and served his said Master under such Indenture for the space of six Months or thereabouts when his said master removed to Butler Court Moor Lane where he Resided with his said Master for four Weeks or thereabouts from thence his said Master removed to the Upper End of Bunhill Row Moor Fields in the Parish of Saint Luke's Old street in the County of Middlesex where he Continued to Live with his said Master under such Indenture for four Months when his said Master died and after his Master Death this Examinant went to Live with one Mr. Attwood a shoe maker in W ych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on the 24th of April last and Continued to Live with him to this Day which is five Weeks and two Days

Sworn this 1st. day of June 1781 before  Sampn Wright R Butler
