Middx  Mary Mash  Wido . aged abt. 70 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she lived in & rented a house in Clements Lane in the parish of St. Clemt: Danes of the Yearly Rent of £15: for the Space of 3 Yrs: & hath left the same abt. 2 Years since which time she hath not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 10th. 1744 before}
