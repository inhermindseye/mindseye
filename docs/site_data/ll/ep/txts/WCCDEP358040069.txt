Westmr.  Ann Miles aged about 30 Years upon Oath Says she never was married Says her father Nichs. Miles deceased Served Seven Years Apprentiship with John Wyat a Turner in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St.Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 21st. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

Ann Miles
