Middx  Dorothy Woolcock Aged about Thirty Years upon Oath says She was born in the Parish of St. Bryoak near Bodmin in the County of Cornwall Says that She has a Child named Mary aged about three Years born a Bastard in the Parish of St. Bryoak afores That her last legal Place of Settlement is in the said Parish of St. Bryoak her Father having had an Estate there & paid Parish Taxes And that She this Examinant never was married nor Rented a House nor lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the 26th. Day of September 1755 before}
