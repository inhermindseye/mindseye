Westmr. 

Edwd. Burton aged about 55 Years upon Oath Saith about 16 Years ago he lived in and rented a house in Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr of the Yearly Rent of £12 for one Year and upwards Says she has not since he quitted the Sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn the 20th. Octr. 1748 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Edwd Edwd. Burton
