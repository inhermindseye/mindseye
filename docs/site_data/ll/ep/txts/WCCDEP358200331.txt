Westmr.  Caroline Gardner wife of John Gardner maketh Oath and saith that about Nine Years ago she was married at the fleet London to Thomas Taylor a Carpenter who has been dead upwards of two Years by whom she hath one Child now living (to wit) Anne aged upwards of Seven Years, says she has been informed the said Thomas Taylor served his Apprenticeship to a Carpenter in the City of Bath in the County of Somerset , but what his Master's Name was, or in what Parish or Place this Examinant cannot tell and further upon her Oath says her said Child Anne was born in the house of Mr. Ryley in Great Wild Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex

Sworn the 10th. Decr. 1765 Before  Fran Cary R Kilsha

The Mark of Caroline Gardner
