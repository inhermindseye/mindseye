Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Griffiths on behalf of Mary Fulwood of her Sister who is at present Insane Maketh Oath and Saith that her said Sister never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that she was born at Wennersbury in the Parish of Wennersbury in the County of Stafford That this Examinants Father William Fulwood was born at Sedgeley in the Parish of Sedgeley in the said County of Stafford that he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain any other Settlement That her said Father has been dead about 20 years that after his Death her Mother this Examinant & all her Brothers and Sisters were relieved at the said Parish of Sedgeley

Sworn this 19th. day of Janry. 1788  Wm. Achiner

her Sarah Griffiths Mark
