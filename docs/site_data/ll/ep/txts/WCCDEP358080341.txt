Middx  Elizth. Neale aged 70 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of William Neale who has been Dead about 33 Years and by whom she hath no Children now living says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. James Ireddle a Butcher in Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for 2 Years at the Yearly Wages of £5..10s..0 hath quitted her said Place about Six Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 16th day of March 1779 before  David Walker .

her Elizth. Neale Mark
