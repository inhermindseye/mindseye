Middx  Rebecca Stratton aged upwds. of 19 Yrs. upon Oath saith She was never married and that her Father Nelson Stratton deceased lived in & rented a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £10 & upwds for sevll. Years & paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes & hath left same upwds. of 20 Yrs Says her sd. late Father after he left the sd. house in Drury Lane never rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif Says she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowlege or beleif

Sworn Septr. 17th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

Rebeka Stratton
