Middx to wit Phebe Evans Aged about Twenty two years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband Richd. Evans (gone from her) at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the year 1779 and by whom she hath one Child living namely Richard Aged about two years says her said Husband served four years & a half part of his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Pain a Shoemaker in Paradise Street  Rotherhithe in the Parish of St. Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry and was never Assigned over to any or. Person but believes he run away from his said Master nor did he ever gain any or. Settlemt. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10 a year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 8th. day of Augt. 1783 Sampn Wright Wm Kitchiner

her Phebe Evans mark
