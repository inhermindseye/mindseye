Westmr  Elizabeth McMekin Aged about Forty Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Samuel McMekin with whom she Intermarried Says her said Husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or Beleif Says she is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England That on the Twenty fourth Day of October 1758 she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Ryley in Little Wild Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx which hath since been Baptized by the Name of John is now Living and she being Poor is unable to maintain the said Child.

Sworn the Day of Novr. 1764 Before}
