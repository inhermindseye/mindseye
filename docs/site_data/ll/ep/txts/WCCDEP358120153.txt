Middx  Mary Gamidge upon Oath Saith she is the wife of Samuel Gamidge with whom she intermarried on the 30th. Augt. 1747 at the Fleet London and that her said husband Lived a yearly Servant for One Year upwards before his said intermarriage & a considerable time afterwds with Mr Shuckburgh a Bookseller in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the west London and quitted his Said Service about two Years ago since which she has not rented ten Pounds by the Year nor paid Parish taxes

Sworn July 18th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane Mary Gamidge
