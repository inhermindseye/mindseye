Middx  Tho: Martin aged 46 Years upon his Oath saith that by Indenture Dated the 7th. Day of April 1712 he was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Jno. Bass  Frame Work knitter in Bunhill Feilds in the parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx & Served him as an Apprentice for the Space of 15 Months & then his sd. Master went away from this Examinant that he never was assigned over to any other person Rented £10. a Yr. paid parish Taxes lived with any other person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 13th. Janry 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

Thos. Martin
