Middx 

Eliz, Whitcomb Wido of John Whitcomb upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. John Whitecomb abt. 25 Years ago in the Tabernacle belonging to the Parish of St. Martin in the fields and that her sd. late husband never rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year that his father Richard Whitcomb lived in and rented a house of £16 a Year in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlx for several Years

Sworn May 1st. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

Euxablle whilcomb
