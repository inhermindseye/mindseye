Middx  Ann Feirnside aged about 27 Years upon Oath says she never was Married Says she was on the Twenty Second day of May One thousand seven hundred and Sixty one Delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of Mr. Ogrum in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been Baptized by the name of John and is still living and likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same And that John Grayham in Kate Street  Spittal Fields  Silk Throwster had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of September One thousand seven hundred and Sixty and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the 22d. day of July 1761 Before.} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Fernside
