Middx  Wm. Powell aged upwards of 57 Years upon Oath says he Intermarried with Elizth : his Wife at the Fleet London on the 14th. day of February 1729. says his father Hugh Powell Rented a house in Serle Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds lived therein abt. thirty Yrs. & held the same till the time of his death which happened about thirty years ago says that he this Examinant never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn this 24th. day of June 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

Wm. Powell
