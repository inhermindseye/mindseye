Middx.  Mary Bradley aged abt. 70 Years upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Clever a Perukemaker next door to Mr. Scriven's in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for five Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5. and hath quitted said service abt. thirty Years and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 12th. day of August 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Bradley
