Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit James Talboys and Mary Gamon for themselves severally make Oath and might And first this Deponent Mary Gammon for herself saith that on the 29th. day of February 1784 she Intermarried with Fras. Gammon her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saml. Mary Whitechaple in the County of Middlesex And James Talboys for himself saith that he has been informed by Richd. Gammon the Father of the said Francis which Information he verify believes to be true that the said Francis was an illegitimate Child born at the Sign of the Crown in Ormand Yard in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex that he never was bound apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for one years ever Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Janry. 1788  Sampr Wright MAddington

James Talboys Has Mary Gammon Mark
