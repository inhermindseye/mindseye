Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Dennison aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born in Star Court  Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes where her Father Rented House That she hath done no Act to gain a Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 29th. day of May last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Store Street Hospital which hath since been Christened by the Name of Elizth . That one Charles Wright a Journeyman Shoemaker who lately lodged at Mr. Wilthew in Lumber Court Seven Dials and now works under the Blackbull in Dean Street  Soho is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of October 1786 and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child of which She was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Charles Wright is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 27th. day of Novr. 1788 } Wm Kitchiner

Ann Dennison

Ann Dennison lodges at Mrs. Chandlers Milk woman in Holles Street

Charles Wright is a Short Thick Man Dark Complexion wears his own hair hanging in his Neck

Man taken, Discharg'd the Woman Not opening to accept Ret. or filiate the Child
