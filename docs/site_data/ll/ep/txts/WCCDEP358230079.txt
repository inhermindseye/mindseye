Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Thomas John Joseph Walter who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith That he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Thomas John Joseph Walter was born at No. 2 Saint Bartholomew Close in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great in the City of London and that he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of July 1799 before us} Saml. Edwards J Reeves

B Newman
