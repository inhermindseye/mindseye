Middx  Margaret Davis aged about 21 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Servant with Mr. Jones Grocer near Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St.. Clement Danes aforesaid for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds Quitted the Same in July 1760 And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And that John Chambers now or late a Scholar at a Boarding School at Chelsea did beget such Child or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in or about the Month of September One thousand seven hundred and Sixty and at several times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 20th day of May 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Margt. Davis
