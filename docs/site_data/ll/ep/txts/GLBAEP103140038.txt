Middlesex ss}  William Davis maketh oath That he well Knew Mary Bromley a Singlewoman who lately lodged in a house Kept by this Deponent in Star Court in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx of whose place of Residence is at present unknown to this Depont. And this Deponent Saith that during her residence with this Depont. in Star Court aforesaid She was delivered of a Male Child there which had been Since baptised & registred in the Church belonging to the said Parish by the name of John Bromley & is aged about Sixteen Months and which Child as the Said Mary Bromley has informed this Deponent is a Bastard And that one George Ilicke of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishopsgate London  Cheesemonger is the Father of the Said Child the which Child is likely to become Chargeable to the said P arish of St. Botolph without Aldgate .

Sworn this 18th day of May 1774 before B Camper

William Davis
