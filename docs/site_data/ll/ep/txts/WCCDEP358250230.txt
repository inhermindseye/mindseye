Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Finch aged about 30 years upon his Oath saith that about Three year ago he Intermarried with Ann his present wife at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom he hath two Children living Vizt. Wm. aged about Two years and John aged about Seven Months That he this Deponent previous to his Intermarriage with his present wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Clark in Princes Street Westminster in the parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Twelve Guineas That he hath quitted said Service about Eight years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of March 1796 . J Hind Char: Charchill

his John Finch Mark
