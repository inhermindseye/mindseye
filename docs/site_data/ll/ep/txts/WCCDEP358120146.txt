Middx  Sarah Vallance aged 21 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and never was an apprentice or yearly Servt for one year rented ten pounds by the Year nor pd Parish taxes that she has been informed & verily believes her father Robert Vallance (whilst a Singleman ) lived a Yearly Servant for several Years wth. Mr Lines in Broad Street near Drapers Gardens in the Parish of St. Peter le Poor London & never after he quitted his sd. Service rented ten pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn July 12th 1754 before} Thos Lane

The Mark of Sarh Vallance
