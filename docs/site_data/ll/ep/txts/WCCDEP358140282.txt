Middx.  John Toocock aged abt. 49 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds lived therein upwards of Twelve Years and hath left the same abt. Nine Years. since which time he has not Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 30th. day of August 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of John Toocock
