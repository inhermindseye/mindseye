Middlesex ss}  John Lawrence maketh oath that many years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to Willm. Bass of Queen Street in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Baker & Served Four years of Such Apprenticeship &then this Depont Master becoming insolvent went From & left his Dwelling house in Queen Street aforesd & this Depont has not Since heard from his Sd Master That he this Depont has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds nor Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship & that he hath a Wife named Alice to whom he was lawfully Married

Sworn this 15th day of November 1775 before Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of John Lawrence
