Middx & Westmr}  Ann Dalton Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Long a Scourer in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Ten Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one Thos. Weston a Journeyman Plaisterer had Carnal Knowledge of her body some time the begining of June 1769 and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid And that the said Thomas Weston and no other person did beget the same

Sworn this 16th. day of January 1770 before me } Thos. Kynaston

Ann Dalton
