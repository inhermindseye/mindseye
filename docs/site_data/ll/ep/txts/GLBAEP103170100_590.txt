Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Profitt the widow of John Profitt deced maketh oath That divers years ago her said late husband rented a house of the yearly rent of Fifteen pounds in Play house yard in the Parish of St Luke in the Said County & resided in the same for the Space of Five years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwds or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain another Settlement That She was lawfully married to her said husband & has not Since his death been married or done any act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath a Child named Edward Profit aged about Thirteen years born in the sd parish of St Luke the lawful Issue of this Depont by her sd husband & this Depont hath two other Children namely Ann Cooley Aged about four years and

Stephen Cooley Aged about Two years & half both of whom are illegitimate & Bastards & were born in the said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this. 13. day of Octobr. 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes P. Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Profitt
