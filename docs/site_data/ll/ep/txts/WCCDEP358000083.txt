Saint Clement Danes July 26th 1785

Middlesex (to wit)  Passd. to St. Anns Blackfryars  Elizabeth Chambers  Singlewoman aged Forty four Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married saith that she rented and lived in a Tenement Situate in Printing House Yard in the Parish of Saint Chris Blackfryars at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for upwards of Forty Days, that she has quitted the same about Six years ago and since quitting the same hath not lived as an hired Yearly servant for one Year served as an Apprentice, rented any other Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds or done any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Informa tion or belief she may have gained any other settlement than the said Parish of Saint Ann Black fryars , by renting and living

Such Tenement in Painting House Yard as is above Stated

Sworn this 26th. July 1785 before G Mercers Wm Gascoigne }

E Chambers
