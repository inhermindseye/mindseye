Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Plime of the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr one of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County of Middx this 6th day of July 1769

Who on her oath Saith That she is now with Child And that Said Child or Children is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards of and to be chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that John Crawley of the Same Parish Labourer is the Father the sd Child or Children

Sworn this 6th day of 1769 before me  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Sarah Plime
