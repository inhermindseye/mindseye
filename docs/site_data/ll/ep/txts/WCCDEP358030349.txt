Middx 

Frances Smyth upon Oath saith she never was married Sans she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at Mrs. Tallants in Parkers Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the feilds in the County of Middx upwds. of 3 Years ago which has been Baptized Thomas Says she was Delivered of another Male Bastard Child at Mr. Mays over agt. the Coal Yard in Drury Lanes in the said Parish of St. Giles in the feilds upwards of one Year ago which has since been baptized James

Sworn Febry 25th. 1746 before  Tho Lane  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Frances Smith .
