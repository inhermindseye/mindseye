Middlesex ss  Hannah McGraze  Single woman Voluntary maketh oath That about five Years and eight months Ago in Sun Yard in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middx she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Which hath been Since Baptized by the game of Frances Ryan And that the Said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate & that one Thomas Ryan late of Clerkenwell Green in the County of Middx . Paviour did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child And this Deponent Saith She was born near Cork in Ireland & that She this Deponent never lived as a hired Servant for a year in any one place in Great Britain nor ever did any Act there to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 8th day of July 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of Hannah Mc Graze
