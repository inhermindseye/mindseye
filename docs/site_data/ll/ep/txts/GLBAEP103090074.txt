Middlesex ss  Ruth Carne the widow of Richard Carne deced maketh oath that her late husband has informed her that he served the last three Years of his apprenticeship with his Fathr. Richard Carne a Ship Rigger in the Parish of St. Michael in the City of Bristol which information this Depont believes to be true And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said husband never did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his apprenticeship nor hath She since his Death done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right. And that she hath two Children to Wit Sarah aged about Seven Years and Elizabeth Aged about three Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband.

Sworn this 16th. Day of September 1767 before  Chris: Scott

Ruth Carne
