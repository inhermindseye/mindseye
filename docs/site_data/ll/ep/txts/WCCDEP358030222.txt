Middx  Wm. Winslow aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath Says he was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Joseph Knight a Shop Joyner in Catherine Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 30th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

William Winslow
