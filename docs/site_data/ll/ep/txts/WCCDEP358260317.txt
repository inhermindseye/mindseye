Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Bond aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Seventh day of December 1789 she Intermarried with William Bond her present husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields  by whom she hath Three Children Vizt. William Matthew aged about Four years John aged about Three years and Samuel aged about Four Months That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house on Milbank in the Parish of Saint John the Evangelist in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds that he hath quitted said house about Five years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further saith that she is now Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 18th. day of Augt. 1798 before us R. Ford T. Robinson

her Elizabeth Bond Mark
