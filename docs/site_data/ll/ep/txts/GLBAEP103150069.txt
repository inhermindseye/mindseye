Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Weaver of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Single woman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this First day of November 1775

Who Saith That She is now with Child of a Child or Children And that the Said Child or Children is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate and that one Thomas Daley of the sd Parish Silver Smith is the Father of the sd Child or Children.

Taken & Signed the day & Year above written before me}

E. Weaver
