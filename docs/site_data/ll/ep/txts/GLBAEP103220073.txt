Middlesex ss  Richard Lanham maketh oath that in the Year one thousand Seven hundred And seventy two he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Seven Pounds in Three Tun Court in the Parish of St John Wapping in the Sd County & during Such his Residence therein which was for about the Space of one Year and half he was Charged to And Paid Poors Rates and Kings Taxes for the Same That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Paid any Poore Rates or Kings Tax or Served any Parish office or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 20. day of October 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Richard Lanham
