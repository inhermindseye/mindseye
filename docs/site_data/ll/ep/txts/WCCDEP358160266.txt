Middx  The Examination of Ann Bray  Wife of Layn Bray lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 2d. of March 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that She & her husband are both  Natives of Ireland & never gained any Settlemt. in England And further on her Oath saith that she hath one Child named Catherine born in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the same County that her sd. Husband now absconds from her & She being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Child

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
