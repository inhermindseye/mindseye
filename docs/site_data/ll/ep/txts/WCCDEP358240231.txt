St.. Clement Danes 11th March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  Thomas Batt aged 26 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he served an Apprenticeship under Indentures (duly Executed) with Mr Richard Trist  Taylor Arundell Street and lodged in his House during the whole of such Apprentice- -ship (except at different times for a Night or two together with his Master consent) that the House of Mr Trist in Arundel Street aforesaid where this Examined Examinant Served and lodged is situate in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that Since the Expiration of his Apprenticeship he hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 11th. day of March 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Thomas Batt
