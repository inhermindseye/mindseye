Middx  Margt Herring on the behalf of Phebe Johnson Upon Oath Says that the said Phebe Johnson is her Daughter by Wm. Johnson her First Husband that the said Phebe Johnson was born in the Match Walk in Ratcliff Highway in the Parish of St Paul Shadwell in the County of Middx were this Examint. said Husband Wm. Johnson  Rented A House of Eight Pounds a Year and Paid the Land Tax

Sworn the 26 Day of March 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell  Saunder Welch

Margret Herring
