Middx.  Mary Green aged about 16 years Upon Oath says her Father Thomas Green a Blacksmith Rented & lived in a House in Chatham in the County of Kent and Paid all Parish Taxes for the same several Years as She has been informed by him, and believes Says She never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant

Sworn the 8th. day of March 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Mary Green
