Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Clegg aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that about Nine years ago she Intermarried with William Robert Clegg her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Ann aged about five weeks That her said Husband informed this Examinant that he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Dennison a Silversmith in Cecil Court in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of July 1796  R. Ford Robinson

Ann Clegg

This Order was Cancelled it Appearing that her Husband Served Two years as a Pish Clerk of the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldersgate during which time he kept house in Little Greenwich in the said Parish
