Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Byron aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she this Deponent Rented a first floor of Mr. Cooper in Hollen Street in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for near Two years at the Weekly Rent of One Pound One Shilling that she hath quitted said Apartments about Fifteen years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1798 before us N Bond W Carter

her Mary Byron mark

Witness B Newman
