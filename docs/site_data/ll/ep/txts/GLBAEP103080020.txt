Middx ss  Martha Walton  Singlewoman Maketh oath that she has been Credibly informed & believes that her late Father Joseph Walton deced duly served his Apprenticeship with Mr Palmer a Waterman and Lighterman in Thames Street in the Parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And that he did not afterwards gain any subsequent settlement And this Deponent saith that she was never an A Continued as a hired servant for the space of a year nor never was an Apprentice nor hath gained any settlement but in right of her sd. Father

Sworn this 23d day of September 1757 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Martha Walter
