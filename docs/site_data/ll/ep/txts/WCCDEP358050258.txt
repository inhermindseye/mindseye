Westmr. 

Tho; Lewis aged abt. Years upon his Oath saith he was born at Warminster  in the County of Wilts where his father Lewis now lives in and rents a house and pays Scott and Lott and that he (this Ext.) never was an Apprentice Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 19th. 1750 before}
