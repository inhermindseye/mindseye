Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Hall  Singlewoman maketh oath that She has been informed by her late Father Christopher Hall deced that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with his Father Samuel Hall  Combmaker deced in East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & which information this Deponent believes to be true That her Said late Father to this Deponents knowledge information or belief never rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent ever was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this. 12. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Hall
