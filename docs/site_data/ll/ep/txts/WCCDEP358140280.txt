Middx.  Love Chalkley aged abt. 23 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Dent at the Spotted dog in Strand Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for 14 Months and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £3..10.. and hath quitted said service abt. Ten Weeks and has not since Rented Ten pounds by ye. Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 25th. Augst. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Love Chalkley
