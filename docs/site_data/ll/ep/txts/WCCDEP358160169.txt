Middx  The Exaicon of Mary Gwilliams  Singlewoman lodging in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx who upon her Oath Saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Thos. Towell in Feathers Court in Drury Lane in the parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of one yeare & Upwards at the yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings And hath left the sd. Service abt. 18 years to the best of her rembrance and further Saith that she never rented any House paid parish Taxes lived nor since that time lived with any other person as a yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one yeare or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworn the 20th. day of Augt. 1740 before} J Poulson Jas: Gronous

The mark of Mary Gwilliams
