Westmr.  John Hicks aged about Twenty Two Years on his Oath Says he never was married and that he was bound to and Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Joseph Hawkins of Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Copper Plate Printer and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th. day of August 1769 before me} R. Butler

his John Hicks Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
