Middx.  Joseph Fellows aged abt. Fifty Four Years upon Oath says he Served the last four Years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Jacob Wallis Gold Chain maker near Kensington Square in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middx: & has been out of his said Apprentiship abt. Twenty Nine Years, Says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr. 22d. 1756 before} Tho Lane . Barth: Hammond

Joseph Fellows
