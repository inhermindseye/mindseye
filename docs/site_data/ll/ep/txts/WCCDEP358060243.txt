Middx  Ruth Smith  Wido . of John Smith upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the said John Smith in the parish Church of St. Magnus London Bridge on the 14th. Day of October 1725 or 1726 and that her sd. late husband Rented t he Hand & Holly bush Alehouse behind St Clements Church in the parish of St Clemt Danes Middx at the Yearly Rent of £26 and lived therein 12 or 13 Years quitted the Same upwds. of 14 Yrs. ago & never afterwards nor has this Examinant Since his Decease rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 14th. Janry 1752 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Ruth Smith
