Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Jeffreys of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Single woman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this first-Day of July 1767 .

Who on her oath Saith that She in now with Child And that the said Child is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that one Edward Johnson an apprentice to Mr Taylor of Flushing Yard in the Same Parish Sailmaker is the Father of the said Child.

Sworn this first Day of July 1767 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Sarah Jeffreys
