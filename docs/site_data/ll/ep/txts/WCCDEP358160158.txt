Middx  The Examination of Ann Gardiner  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 15th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith yt. on the 23d. Day of Febry. 1738 She was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Bear & Harrow Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes wch has since been Baptized Thos . and yt. one Rowland Wilson since Deced did begett the sd. Child on her Body she this Examint. at such time liveing as a yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Dover in C t Eaton Street in the Parish of and he the sd. Rowland Wilson at ye. same time liveing as a yearly hired Servt . with the sd. Mr. Dover there became acquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her body Sevll. times at one of wch times he got her with the Male Bastard Child of wch she was Delivered of as aforesaid and that he the sd. Rowland Wilson was the true & reall Father thereof, And this Examint. further saith yt. about 5 Years ago she lived a yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Jas Fletcher in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Grocer at the yearly Wages of £5:10s for the space of one year & upwards that she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Fletcher Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one year or other wise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she is now Poor & not able to maintaine herself & Child

Sworn the Day & year above menconed before}
