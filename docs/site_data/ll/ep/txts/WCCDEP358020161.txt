Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Lever aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with Mr. Finch in Castle Street Leicester fields in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years at the yearly Wages of Eight Guin Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Augt. 1789 N Bond Thos. Gordon

her Elizabeth Lever Mark
