Middx ss The Examinacon of Margaret Ludgate  Singlewoman Taken on Oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the Sd. County of Middx residing in or next to the limits of the parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County this Thirtieth Day of July 1750 whereof one is of ye Quorum

This Examinant upon her oath Saith That about Michaelmas last one John Younger of Red Lyon Street in the parish of St Mary White chappell in the Sd. County Distiller with whom this Depont. then lived did prevail with this Depont. to Suffer him to lye with her at his Sd. house & had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants body Several times Whereby she Conceived with Child of a Male Female Bastard Child of which she was Delivered at the house of Mr. Trumball in White Hart Yard in the Sd. parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate on the 28th. Day of June last which Sd. Female-Bastard Child hath been Baptised by the Name of Margaret Younger & hath been now is & Still is likely to be Chargeable to the said parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate this Examinant being poor & not able to provide for the Same And this Examinant Farthr. Saith that ye sd. John Younger is the true & only father of the sd. Female Bastard Child And that this Examination is Voluntary & that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever:

Sworn this thirtieth Day of July 1750 before us  Richd: Ricards  Saml Gower

The Mark of Margaret Ludgate
