Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Mary Carter aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 15th. day of October 1776 she Intermarried with John Carter her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone by whom she hath no Child or Children living That she this Deponent hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that her said late Husband died at Jamaica in or about the Month of February 1784 That since her said late Husbands Death she this Deponent lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Price a Coal merchant in Northumberland Street Buildings in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said service about Thirteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Febry. 1796 M Addington Gr Goodenough

her Mary Carter mark

Witness B Newman
