Middx The Examination of Martha Lambourn  Wife of Jeremiah Lambourn taken the 30th. Augst. 1739 Passed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx by Order bearing date the 27th. Day of this Instant Augst. Who upon, her Oath saith yt. her said Husband Jeremiah Lambourn Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Wright Shoemaker in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the same County & hath been out of his time about 16 Years And further upon her Oath saith that he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife That her said Husband is now at Sea & that she hath two Small Childn. (to wit) Mary Aged almost 9 Years & Frances Aged about two Years & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & them

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Marth: Lambourn
