Middlesex ss}  Mary Phipps the widow of John Phipps deced maketh oath That divers years Ago & Since her Said late husband's death She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the Year by & with Andrew Paupard in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Pawn Broker at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of nine Years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor hath She been married Since her Said husbands death or done any Act to gain anr Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of April 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Mary Phipps
