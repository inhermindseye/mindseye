Middx ss.  Esther Browne  Singlewoman Maketh oath That about Nine Years ago she this Deponent was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of one Mr. Redgate a Victualler who then lived in the Parish of st. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx and wch Child was Baptised by the Name of Mary Halfpenny By Means whereof ye sd. Child gained a Settlement in ye sd. Parish as this Deponent is informed & believes And this Depont farther Says that about Seven Years ago she this Deponent was Delivered of Another Female Bastard Child at ye House of one Mr. Loggan in Ralph Red Bull Yard in the Parish of st. James Clerkenwell in ye sd. County of Middx Which sd. Child was Baptised by the Name of Elizabeth Halfpenny By Means whereof the sd. last menconed Child gained a Settlement in ye sd Parish of st. James Clerkenwell as this Depont. is informed & believes And this Depont. farther Saith that about four Years and Upwards ago she this Depont. was Delivered

of Another Female Bastard Child at her dwelling house in the Coach Yard in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the sd. County of Middx Which Child was Baptised by the Name of Margaret Half penny By Means Whereof the sd. last Menconed Child gained a Settlement in ye. sd. Parish of St. John Wapping as this Depont. is informed and believes And this Deponent Says that all ye sd. Children are living and that this examination is Voluntary.

Sworn this 24 day of July 1752 before us  Richd. Ricards  Thos: Quarrill

his Esther Brown Mark
