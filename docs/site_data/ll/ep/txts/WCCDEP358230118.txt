Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Rose Rawnslow Aged about 58 years upon her Oath Saith That about 12 years Ago she intermarried with with her late Husband (who has been Dead about Nine years) at Battersea Church That since her late Husband Death she hath lived as a hired yearly servant for two years with a Dr Robt the last ten Months of which Service was at Hammersmith in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Ten Pounds That she hath left the said Service about 14 Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for twelve Months rented any House tenement or Lodging of ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of Feby 1800 before us Ws Woodenough Winster

Rose Rawslow her Mark

Witness J. Sharling
