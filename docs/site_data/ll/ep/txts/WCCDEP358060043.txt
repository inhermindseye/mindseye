Middx  John Vicary of the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx maketh Oath. That he has made Enquiry concerng. the Settlement of Elizth Smith an Inft. aged upwds of 6 Yrs. lately dropt in the sd Parish and has been Informed by James Smith (now in the New Goal Southwark ) that the said Infant is the Daughter of him the said James Smith by Elizabeth his Wife and that he the said James Smith Served his Apprentiship with John Ellersby a master of a Ship at Hull in Yorkshire & never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year That the sd. Eliz Smith was born in Hull and that her sd. mother before their Intermarriage lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Hamilton in the same Town for 2 or 3 Years

Sworn 31st Decr. 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell

J: Vicary
