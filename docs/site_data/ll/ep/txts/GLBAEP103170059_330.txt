Middlesex ss}  Daniel Harvey maketh oath that he has been informed by his late Grand mothr deced & which he believes to be true that this Deponts late Grandfar Daniel Harvey deced rented a house in the parish of St Botolph with Aldgate in thesd County & was charged to & paid Poors Rates Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt. there & that he did not ahte marked do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That this Depont has also been informed & believes that his late Far  Daniel Hawey deced was born in the Same parish & this Depont. Can give no Account Whethr his sd Far every did any Act to gain a Settlemt in his own right never having reced any information relative there to That he this Deponent was also both in thesd parish & never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or ever Served any parish Office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this 6. day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Daniel Harvey
