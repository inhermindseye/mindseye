Sworn this. 17. of November 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

Ann Bartlett

Ano. under the Circumstances Stated in the above Examinations yr opinion's desired Whethr. the Settlement of Robert Bartlett & his wife & Child is in Aldgate Parish Middlesex or elsewhere
