Westmr. to wit Golossa Gough Aged about 23 Years on her Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented House Pd. Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Gov. of the Foundling Hospital to one Mrs. Price Black Puden Maker in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell & served her about one year & an half and was afterwds. turned over to one Job Seal a Baker in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & served him for about 7 Years & then left her said Master And further upon her Oath says that on the 12th Day of May last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Peakin the Parish Nurse of St Clement Danes witch hath Since been baptized Sarah Gough And that one Francis Flattey a Journeyman  Butcher who Latter lived with one Mrs. Ballard in Rose Street Newgate Markett & who she belives in on Board of his Majestys Ship the Albion is the real & true father of the sd. Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered afsd.

Sworn this fifth day of June 1771 before} R. Butler

her Gollossa Gough Mark

Wittness Fras Bishop
