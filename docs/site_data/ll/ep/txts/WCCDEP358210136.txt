Westmr.  Catherine Willett aged about 22 Years Upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Welborn in Rathbone Place in the Parish of St. Mary le Bone in the County of Middlesex for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Quitted said Service about five Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 16th, of Febry. 1767 , before} Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Catherine Willett
