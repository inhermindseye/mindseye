Middx  The Examination of Thos. Williams Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken ye. 8th. Day of Novr. 1739 Who upon his Oath saith yt. about Eleven Years ago he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Jno. Williams in Chichester Rents in the Liberty of the Rolls in the same County Baker & Served him as an Apprentice for the space of Six Years & upwards And this Examint. further saith yt. he hath not since he left his Said Master Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife That he hath a Wife who is Pregnant with Child & being Poor is not able to maintaine her

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous
