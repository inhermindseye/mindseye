Westmr.  Eliz: Elliot upon Oath saith she is the wido . of James Elliot who dyed last Tuesday that she was married to her sd. late husband abt. 8 Yrs. ago as the Fleet London & has by him one Child (viz) James aged Six Years and that her sd. late husband lived in and rented house of the Yearly Rent of £30 in Portugal Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for two Years & upwards quitted the same upwds. of 4 Yrs. ago & has not since rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn Augt. 11th 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Elliot
