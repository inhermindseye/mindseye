Westmr. 

Robt. Smith upon Oath saith he intermarried with his wife Eliza. at the Parish Church of Clapham in Surry about 36 Years ago Says be lived in and rented a house in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Seventeen pounds for Sixteen Years or thereabouts and quitted the same about 20 Years ago and afterwards lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £14 in Ship Yard in the same Parish for four Years or thereabouts & hath left the sd. house about 8 Years; since which time he has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 20th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Robert Smith
