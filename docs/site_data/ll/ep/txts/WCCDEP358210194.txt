Westmr.  Martha Isbell aged about 31 Years upon Oath says she never was married nor rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes nor was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her late Father Benjamin Isbell deceased informed her and which Information She believes to be true that being a single Man and unmarried he lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year with one John Newet a Staymaker in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds Dyet Washing and Lodging and never rented Ten pounds a year or Paid Taxes to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 6th. day of Octor. 1767 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Martha Isbell
