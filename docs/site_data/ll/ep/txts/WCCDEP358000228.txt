St. Clemt. Danes

Ann Sandalls aged 22 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath, saith That she  Never was Married Rented a House or Paid Taxes or over lived as an Hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months Saith That she was bound out Apprentice by a Society called the Ladies Society supported by Voluntary Subscription held in Gillspur Street in the Parish of St. Sepulchre in the City of London to Mr. John Houseman in Crown Court Russell Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex for the Term of 5 Years and served the whole of her Apprenticeship and or the said Indenture  that  since the Expiration of her said Apprentices but the has not done any Act to gain a  Subsequent Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 17th. day of Febry. 1786 before  Wm Gascoigne  Jno: Wright

Ann Sandalls
