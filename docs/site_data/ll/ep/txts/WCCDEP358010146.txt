Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Hannah Rockley aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1764 she Intermarried with Richd. Rockley her late Husband (who has been dead upwards of 13 years) at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London  that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Buckeridge Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for 12 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £12..12..0 that she hath quitted said House about 13 years ago since which time she hath not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Octr. 1787 Maddington N Bond.

her Hannah Rockley mark
