Middx  Mary Green upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she was bound an Apprentice to and Served four Years Apprentiship with Mrs. Moore a Lace Joyner who lived in Ely Court and at Mrs. Holmes a Tobacconist near Hatton Garden in the Liberty of Saffron hill Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middx and hath been out of her time about Seven Years Says she never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 5th. 1746 before  John Lawton  William Withers

Mary Green
