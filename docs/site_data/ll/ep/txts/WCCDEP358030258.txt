Middx  Timothy Lee aged about 33 Years removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds by Order dated the 15th. Novr. Instt. upon Oath Says he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Egleston at Knights Coffee house in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £5 for the Space of one Year & upwards and hath left his sd. Service upwards of Seven Years Says he hath not since he left the sd. Mr. Egleston's Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servts. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 18th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Timothy Lee
