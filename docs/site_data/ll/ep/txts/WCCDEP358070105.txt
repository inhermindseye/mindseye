Westmr. to wit Elizth. Turpin Aged about Thirty Nine Years On her Oath Says she is the Widow of Joseph Turpin who has been dead upwards of Six Weeks ago and with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstans in the West upwards of Thirteen Years ago and by whom she hath five Children now living Vizt. Joseph Aged about thirteen Years, Elizth . Aged about Ten Years, George Aged about five Years William Aged about Three Years, and John Aged about one Year and an half says that her said late Husband was born in the Parish of Studham in the County of Bedford and never gained any other Subsequent Settlemt either by Renting ten pounds a Year or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says her said late Husband has often informed that he served Seven Years Apprentice with one  Luke Brickland a Breaches Maker at St. Albans in the County of Hertford But the said Luke Brick being a Certificated Man her said Husband gained no Settlement thereby.

Sworn this 12th. day of June 1773 before W Kelyness R. Butler

Elizabeth Turpin
