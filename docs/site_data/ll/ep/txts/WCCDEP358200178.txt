Middx  John Hopkins Aged about Fifty Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Elizth. in the Parish Church of St. John the Evangelist in the Liberty of Westmr . about Twenty four Years ago Says he Rented a House in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of Ten Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds hath quitted said House about Eight Years & hath not Since Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 7th. of December 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

John Hopkins
