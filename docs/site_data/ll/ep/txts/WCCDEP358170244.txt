Middlesex  Mary Margery Baxter  Wido . of Richard Baxter upon her Oath saith that about Fifty Years since she intermarried with her late Husband Richard Baxter at the Parish Church of St. James in Dukes Place that the said Richd. Baxter before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Budgeton a Taylor in the Stable Yard in the Parish of St. Margarett in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year & upwards that the said Richd. Baxter in his Life time nor this Examinant since this Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise Gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 30th. of July 1742 before Roulson Wm. Hayton

18 Sepr 1742

The Mark of Margery Baxter
