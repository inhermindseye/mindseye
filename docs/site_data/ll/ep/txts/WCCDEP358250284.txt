Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Joseph aged about 65 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hodges in Clements Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Nine Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Fourteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 9th. day of May 1797 before me } J Hunter

Susanna Joseph
