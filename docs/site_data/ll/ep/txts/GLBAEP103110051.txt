Middlesex ss}  Francis Gregory Maketh oath That about two Years ago he rented a house at the Yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds in Road Lane in the Parish of St. Margaret Pattens in the City of London and Continued in the occupation of Such house for the Space of one Year a Quarter and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent hath not Since Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his Knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that he hath a Wife named Susanna and three Children Namely Francis Michael Aged about Twelve Years Michael Francis aged about Nine Years and Alexander Thomas aged about Six Years the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his Said Wife to Whom he was Married in the Year 1753.

Sworn this 2d. Day of May 1772 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

Francis Gregery
