Middx ss  Sarah Richardson Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father Rowland Richardson served seven Years apprenticeship to one Mr. Supine a Fanmaker  the Corner of Kings Head Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any or Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 19th. of Novr. 1772 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Goodwin Court Bedford Bury which was soon afterwards baptized George and that on the 27th. of Octr. 1774 she was delivered of another Male Bastard Child in King Street Seven Dials named Michael and that on the 21st. day of July last she was delivered of another Male Bastard Child named Samuel And further Says that Michael Reboul a Button Maker is the Father of the said Children he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body for several Years last past and that the said Michael Reboul and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 12th. day of June 1778 before  Abrm. Baley

her Sarah Richardson Mark
