St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) James Stewart Aged ten Years & upwards, Upon Oath saith that he Married with his present Wife Rebecca at the Fleet about 45 years ago that he hath three Children all provided for now living that he Served as an Apprentice with Mr. David Low under Judices duly exted executed for four Years that he lived with then his said Master fader & gave up his Judies that his said Master then lived in Swallow Street in the psts of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster was a Baker & that this Deponent lodged in his said Master's House the whole time

Sworn the 24th Janry 1792 before us  Sampr Wright Addington

James Stewart
