Middx to wit Martha Lloyd Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says she was bound out an Apprentice by the Governors & Guardians of the Foundling Hospital to One Richard Brandass a Gold and Silver Trimming maker in Mays Buildings  Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster untill this Examinant shod. Attain the Age of 21 Years or Day of Marriage That she served her full Apprenticeship and after serving her said Apprenticeship she never gained any other Settlement than as abovementioned.

Sworn this 17th. day of Octr. 1783 before  Sampn Wright W Addington

Martha Lloyd
