Middlesex ss}  Margaret Lewis the Wife of Henry Lewis who is absent from her maketh oath That can give no Account respecting his Settlement or the Settlement of his Parents or either of them That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Capn Taylor in Flushing Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued there in her sd Masters Service for about the Space of Five months & then this Depont removed with her Said Master & Mistress to Upper Thames Street in the Parish of St Andrew Ward to be in the City of London Where She Served the remainder of thesd year & by Means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She did not afterwards live as a hired Servant for a year nor do any Act to gain anothr Settlement Save what She has Acquired by her Marriage as afore sd. That She was lawfully Married to her Said husband & hath two children namely Mary aged about three years & Susanna aged about Nineteen Months the lawful Issue of this Depont by her Said husband

Sworn this. 1. day of December 1777 before P Rowlands Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Margaret Lewis
