Middx  Ann Ross upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Martin Ross (who dyed in January last) by whom she hath two Children viz. Mary aged upwds. of Four Years and Martin aged upwards of two Years says her sd. late husband Informed her he Served four Years & upwards the first Part of his time with Paul Jackeman a Cabinet maker at the Corner of Maudlin Colledge Bridge in the Parish of Saint Peter in the East at Oxford in the County of Oxford and never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 25th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones .

The mark of Ann Ross
