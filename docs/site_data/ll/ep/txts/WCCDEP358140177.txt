Middx.  Ann Brooks aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Peter Brooks who has been dead abt. Nine Years; says her said husband Rented and lived in a house in Saint Martin's Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwds. for four Years as her said husband informed this Deponent and which Information she verily believes to be True says she hath not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Parish or Place to her knowledge and belief

Sworn Novr. 3d. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Brooks
