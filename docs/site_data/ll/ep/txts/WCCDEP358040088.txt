Westmr.  James Sharp upon Oath saith he inter: :marryed with his present Wife Jane Sharp at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields on or abt. the 7th. Day of Octr.1740 Says he hath three Children (viz:) John aged upwards of Eleven Yrs. Thos. aged upwards of Four Years and Sarah aged about Eleven Months Says about 8 Years since he lived in and rented a house in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds for two Years and upwards Says he has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 22d. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

James Sharp
