Middx and Westmr.} 

Hannah Bulford upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Collins in St. James Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for one Year and upwards & hath lest her sd. Service upwards of one Year Says she has not since she left the said Mr. Collins Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a female Bastard Children the 13th. Day of March last at Mr. Bromleys a Fishmonger near Temple Barr in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx which has since been baptized Hannah & is likely to become Chargeable to the sd Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same And that Richd. Denevet  Fishmonger did beget the sd. female Bastard Child on her

Body the said Richd. Denevett living fellow Servt . with this Examinant at Mr. Bromleys aforesd. there he became acquainted with her and had carnall Knowledge of her Body in the Month of June last and at sevll. other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. and that he the sd. Richard Denevett & no man else is the true Croall father thereof

Sworn April 23d. 1747 before  Tho: Burdus  Tho Lane

The Mark of Hannah Bulford
