Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Burrell aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of August 1792 she Intermarried with John Burrell her present Husband at the Parish Church of Lowlayton by whom she hath Three Children living Vizt. John aged about five years George aged about Two years and a Child aged about Three Six weeks not yet baptized That her said Husband lived upwards of Twelve Months as a hired yearly Servant for with Mr. Kinsley at the Point Stratford in the Parish of Westham in the County of Essex and that after he quitted said Service he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging unfurnished at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th.. day of June 1798 before us } W Addington Edwd: Naish

her Elizth. Burrell mark

Witness B Newman
