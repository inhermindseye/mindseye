Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Judith Westwood  Wife of Thomas Westwood  Attornies Writer upon her Oath saith That about 30 years ago she intermarried with Thomas her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by Licence That her said Husband kept House in Horn Court Holywell Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes that she quitted said House about 12 Years ago and has since done no act matter or Thing whereby & he may have a subsequent settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge and belief

Sworn this 5th. day of December 1788 before R Butler

Juditt Westwood
