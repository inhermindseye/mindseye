Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Alice Warren aged about 17 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Pollard in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Four Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Nine Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one James Woodford a Tallow Chandler who lives at No. 57 Charles Street Westminster is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Woodford is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 2d. day of Octr. 1798 before me  Gs. Goodenough

her Alice Warren Mark

Witness B Newman
