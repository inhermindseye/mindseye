Eliz. Payne Wife of Tho Payne a Soldier in his Majestys Service upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Thomas Payne on the 23d. Febry 1747 at the fleet London and that she hath been Informed and belives the sd. Thomas Payne Whilst a Singleman lived a yearly Servant with Mr John How in the parish of Fornham three Kings in the County of Suffolk for one Year at the yearly Wages of Four pounds and four Shillings and hath not since he Quitted his sd. Service rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year Says she hath one Child by the sd. Thos. Payne (viz) John aged near 5 Weeks

Sworn Sepr. 4th. 1750 before} Tho. Lane

The mark of Eliz Payne
