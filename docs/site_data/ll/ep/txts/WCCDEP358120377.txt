Middx  Martha Bossom on behalf of her husband Robt Bossom a Lunatic upon Oath saith she has been Informed & verily believes her sd. husband never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever Rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parish taxes and that Rt Bossom  Senr . her sd. husbands father lived in a house of his own near Highbridge in the parish of St Peter in the Bailey in Oxford paid Scot and Lot sevl. Years in the said parish and there dyed

Sworn the 5th. July 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

M Bossom
