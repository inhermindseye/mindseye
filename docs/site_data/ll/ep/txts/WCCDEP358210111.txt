Westmr.  Frances Harvey aged about 29 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Alexr. Weatherstone in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, quitted said Service about Six Years ago and hath not since Rented House, paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 2d, of Decr. 1766 , before} Frans Cary

The Mark of Frances Harvey
