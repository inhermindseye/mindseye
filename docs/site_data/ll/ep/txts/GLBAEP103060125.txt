Middx ss  Ann Hudson the Widow of William Hudson Deced maketh oath that about her sd. late husband rented a house in the parish of St. Sepulchre in the County of Middx & paid ten pounds a year rent for ye Same & lived in the sd. house Several Years & also Served the office of Constable in the sd Parish By means whereof he acquired a legal Settlemt. in ye sd. parish and by that means this Depont. in right of her sd late husband is legally Settled in ye Same parish And this Dopont. hath not Since gained any Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn the Third Day of Febry. 1749 before me us.  Richd: Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

her Ann Hudson Mark
