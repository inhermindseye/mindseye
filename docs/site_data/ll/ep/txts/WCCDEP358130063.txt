Middx  Mary Stanley aged abt. Thirty Years Upon Oath Says he was never Marryed that she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Sd. Richd. Adams for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds that she lived upwards of three Months the last part of her sd. Service at Sd. Richards House in Bloombury Square in the Parish of St. George  Bloomsbury in the County of Middx & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. two Months And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says She is Pregnant with Child or Children wch is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes where She now Lodges She being poor & unable to maintain the same Says one Henry Thorp did beget the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her Body he the sd. Henry Thorp (and this Exaiant liveing fellow Servts. at Sr. Richd. Adams in Bloomsbury Square aforesd.) had Carnall knowledge of this Examinant twice in the Month of Janry last at one of wch times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with wch she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 7th. day of Octr. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Stanley
