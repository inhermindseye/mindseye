Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (ss)  Ann Jones  singlewoman aged twenty eight Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith, That she never was married, rented House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year or did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Settlement in her own Right and further that she this Examinant hath always lived and resided with her father Samuel Jones as a part of his Family and that her said Father hath rented and lived in Houses in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for many Years now last past and is thereby as this Examinant is informed and verily believes an Inhabitant and legal Parishoner duly setled in the said Parish, and that she this Examinant is also as she hath been inform'd and believes in Right of her said father and as part of his Family also legally setled in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes. And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she this Examinant is now pregnant with Child or Children which are or is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that Robert Smith who is a Tallow Chandler and lived as Journeyman with Mr- Rablus of the Butcher Row in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes  Tallow Chandler is the father of such Child or Children wherewith she this Examinant is so pregnant he the said

he the said Robert Smith having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body in or about the beginning of the Month of June one thousand seven hundred and eighty nine, and at several other times at some or one of which times he did beget on this Examinants Body the Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid, and that the said Robert Smith and no, one else is the real and true father of such Child or Children.

Sworn this 7th. Day of January 1790 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Ann Jones

Warrt. made out, not taken.
