Westmr. 

Mary Hansloe aged upwards of 55 Yrs upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Robt. Hansloe who dyed upwards of one Year ago Says the said Robt. Hansloe lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds in Yeates Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Six Years & upwds. and left the sd. house abt. 10 Yrs. ago since which neither her sd. late husband in his Life time nor hath this Examinant since his Death rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 30th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Hansloe
