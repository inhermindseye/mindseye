Westmr.  Mary Fairchild aged about 26 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant for one Year & upwds. with Mrs. Roses in Plough Court Carey Street in the parish of St Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the yearly wages of Four Pounds ten Shillings & quitted her sd. Service about two Years ago since which time she has not Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. parish of St Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the Same when born and that James Farsens Apprentice to Edmd Smith of the Bankside Southwerk Lighterman did beget the sd. bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. James Farsens having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in December last & at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Bastard Child or Children of which she is now pregnant & that the sd. James Farsens & no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn July 30th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Fairchild
