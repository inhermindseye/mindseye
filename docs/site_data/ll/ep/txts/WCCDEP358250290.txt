Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Trivillian aged about 55 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant for Three years or thereabouts with one Mrs. Cranford in Serle Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Eight years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Mary Trivillian Mark.
