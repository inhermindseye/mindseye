Middlesex ss}  Mary Brett  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant at Ceratin Wages by & with John Farr in Kings head Court in the Parish of Saint Margaret in new Fish Street in the City of London  Fishmonger & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Fourteen Months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 24. day of September 1778  Wm Blackmore  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Mary Brett
