Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Elizabeth Slaton  Wife of Richard Slaton Cooper, upon her Oath Saith that she was married to Richard Slaton her Husband at the Parish of Pancras in the County of Middlesex on the Third Day of June 1787 That the said Richard Slaton Served his time as an Apprentice in the Parish of Mary Le bone in the County of Middlesex with his own Father and Since that time has not done any other Act Matter or thing to the best of this Examinant Knowledge Information and belief whereby he may have acquired any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of March 1789  Philip Dyot D Walker

his Elizh Slaton Mark
