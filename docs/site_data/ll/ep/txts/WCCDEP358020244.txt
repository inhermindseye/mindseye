Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit) Ann Jackson widow, of Alphaha Court Stanhope Street in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster  Widow upon her Oath Saith that she has been informed and believes that a Male Infant Child now in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and deserted by its Parents is the illegitimate Child of Mary Fuller and was born in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex at the House of Mary Doran a Coalshed near the Theatre in Great Russell Street Covent Garden and never has been Christned but is called by the Name of Henry Fuller and this Deponent further Saith that she does not know the Plan of the legal Settlement of the said Mary Fuller nor where the said Mary Fuller now is.

Sworn this 7th. Day of Janry 1791 before us Sampn Wright Edwd: Naish }

her Ann Jackson Mark
