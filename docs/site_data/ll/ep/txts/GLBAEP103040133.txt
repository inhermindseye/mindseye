Middlesex ss.  Ann Reader  Singlewoman maketh Oath that about Three Years & a half ago, she was hired as a Servant by the year to Mr. Carr of Nr 18 . Argyle Street in the Parish of St. James in the City & Liberty of Westminster Gentleman at the yearly Wages of Nine Guineas and continued in his Service under such hiring for the space of One year & a half And this Deponent further saith that about Two Months after she quitted his Service she was delivered of a Female Child at the Westminster Lying in Hospital that the said Child has been Baptized by the Name of Matilda & is now living & likely to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and that John Bailey late Servant to Mr. Carr aforesd. is the Father of the said Child, & that the said Child is illegetimate & a Bastard by reason that she never was Married to the said John Bailey , And further saith that on the Seventh Day of June One Thousand Seven Hundred & Ninety Seven she was hired as a Servant by the Year to Mr. Fearon of Lower Eastsmithfield in the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate Gentleman and has continued in his Service under such hiring upwards of One Year

Sworn this 15th Day of June 1798 before  Dane Williams R. Davies

her Ann Reader Mark
