Middx.  Elizabeth Fuge aged abt. thirty three Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Edwd. Fuge (gone from her) with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of St. James Westmr. on the 29th. day of Decr. 1746 by whom she hath one Child (namely) Mary aged abt. Eight Years says her said husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Thos. Reynardson a Cheesemonger at the Corner of Lawrence Lane in the Parish of St. Luke at Chelsea in the County of Middx. for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds or thereabts. & hath quitted said Service abt. Ten Years says her said husband never after Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes as her said husband has informed her & as she verily believes

Sworn this 29th. day of Novr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Huge
