Middlesex ss  Jane Ball the Widow of John Ball deced Maketh oath that her said late husband hath informed her that about forty years ago he was duly bound an Apprentice by Indre to one Edward Peet a Poulterer of Lawrence Lane in the Parish of St Lawrence Jewry in the City of London and that by Means of such apprenticeship he gained a legal settlemt there And this Deponent farther saith that to the knowledge Information or belief of this Deponent her sd husband did not afterwards such his servitude as above Live as an Apprentice or hired servant by the year Nor Rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor serve any Parish offices Nor do any Act to Acquire any settlement subsequent to his sd. Apprenticeship

Sworn this 10th: day of April 1760 before  Chris: Scott  Tho: Quarrill

her Jane Ball Mark
