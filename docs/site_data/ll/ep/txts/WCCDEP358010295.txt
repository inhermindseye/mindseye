Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Fluck aged about 42 years upon her Oath saith That she Intemarried with John Fluck her present husband who is ill at the Parish Church of Eltham in the County of Kent that her said Husband lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Hogg in Sambrook Court in Basinghall Street in the parish of Saint Michael Bassishaw in the City of London for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Nineteen Guineas that he hath quitted said Service upwards of Twelve years since which time he has not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of June 1789 N Bond Sampr Wright

her Jane Fluck Mark
