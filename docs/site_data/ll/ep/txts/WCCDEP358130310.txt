Middx  Eliz: Weaver aged abt. 46 yrs. upon Oath says she intermarried with Saml. Weaver at the Fleet London says her sd. Husband is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England & is now abroad & beyond the Seas as she hath been informed & believes says before her intermarriage with the sd. Saml. & whilst she was a single woman she lived a hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Chivins a Baker the Corner of the Back Lane in the Parish of Lambeth in the County of Surry for four years at the yearly Wages of four Pounds ten Shillings says she hath one Child by her sd. Husbd. (to wit) Henry aged abt. seven years

Sworn the 22d day of March 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Weaver
