Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Groves Aged about 26 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant for thirteen Months with a Mr. Johnson at Mill Hill in the Parish of Hendon in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds and ten Shillings that she hath left the said Service between four and five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 28th. February 1800 before us Ws Woodenough Winster

The Mark of Ann Groves
