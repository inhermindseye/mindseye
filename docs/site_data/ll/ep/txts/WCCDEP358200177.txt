Westmr.  Sarah Eaglesfield Aged about Seventy One Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year other then being a Nurse at the London Hospitall in Goodsmans Fields in the Parish of St. Mary Whitechaple in the County of Middx One Year and three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted said Service about Sixteen Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 6th. of December 1764 Before} Benjamin Cox  Paul Vaillant

Sarah Eagelefield
