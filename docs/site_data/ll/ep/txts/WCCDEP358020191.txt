Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Maiden aged about 54 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Lancey a Cabinet Maker in Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Nine Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Twenty four years ago since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months since which time or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of April 1790 D Walker R Butler

her Elizabeth Maiden Mark
