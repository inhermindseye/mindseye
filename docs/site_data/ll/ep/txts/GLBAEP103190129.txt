Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Lucas maketh oath That Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Thomas Hope in Church Lane in the Parish of St. George in the Said County Chandler & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at certain Wages for the Space of one Year & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlemt Since Such her Servitude as aforesaid

Sworn this. 28. day of July 1780 before  Jno Sherwood Penleaze

The Mark of Elizabeth Lucas
