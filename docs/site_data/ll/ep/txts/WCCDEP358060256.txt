Middx  John Smiths aged abt. 27 Years upon Oath saith he lived a Servant with the Rd. Mr James in the parish of Bushey in the County of Hertford at the Yearly Wages of £7 for one Year & was all the time of his sd Service a Singleman: that he left the sd. Mr James abt. ten Years ago & has not Since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn 30th. Jany. 1752 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Jno. Smiths
