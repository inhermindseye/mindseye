Middx  The Examination of Ann Geale  Widow lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 14th. Day of Novr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. 20 Years ago she was married at the Parish Church of St. Sepulchres  London to her late Husband Jno. Geale & that upwards of 12 Years ago her sd. Husband Rented a House in Devereux Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the yearly Rent of £30 & lived in the same for the Space of two Years or Thereabouts & there dyed And that after the Death of her sd. Husband Jno. Geale she went & lived with one Mr. Smith Master of a free School at Chesilhurst in the County of Kent who was to give her Meat Drink Washing & Lodging for her learning 10 Girls which were in the sd. free School & continued to live under that agreemt. with the sd. Mr. Smith for the Space of Ten Years And further upon her Oath saith yt. she has not been Married to any other Man since the Death of her sd. late Husband Jno. Geale neither hath she Rented £10. a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife.

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Ann Geale
