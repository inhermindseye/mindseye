Middx  Elizabeth Phelps aged about 30 Years Lodging at Mr Seago's in Princes Street  Drury Lane upon Oath saith she never was Married That she was on the Eleventh day of April One thousand seven hundred and fifty seven Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Plow Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been & Baptized by the Name of Elizabeth and that the same is yet living and likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to provide for it, And that William Kempster who lately kept a Tallow Chandlers Shop in the Strand and now resides at Chelsea did beget the said Female Bastard Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at several times, the said William Kempster & this Examinant having Cohabited together as Man and Wife from about the Year 1751 until the Year 1760.

Sworn the 17th. day of April 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant  Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Phelps
