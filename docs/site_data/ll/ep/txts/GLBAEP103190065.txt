Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Parry the Wife of David Parry maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her at Sea in his Majesties Service that he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with one Mr Jones a Carpenter in the Parish of Whitney in the County of Oxford & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She was lawfully married to her Sd husband by whom She hath a Child & lawful Issue now living namely John Aged about Nine Months

Sworn this 27 day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Parry
