Westmr.  Uriah Sims Aged about Thirty one Years, Upon Oath says he intermarried with Mary his Wife (deceased) in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Eight Years ago, by whom he hath three Children now living (to wit) Elizabeth aged about Six Years, James aged about Two Years and Rachel aged about Two Months; Says he served Seven Years Apprentice -ship with Mr. Simon Wass a Painter in Basing Lane in the Parish of St. Mildred in Bread Street in the City of London , and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 20th, day of July 1768 , before} Thos Kynaston W Kelynge

Uriah Sims
