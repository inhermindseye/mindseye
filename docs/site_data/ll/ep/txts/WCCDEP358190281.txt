Middx  Elizabeth Lawrence aged about 35 Years upon Oath says She never was Married and that She lived a hired Yearly Servant about Seven Years with Mr. John Woodbridge Merchant on St. Dunstans Hill in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the East London and quitted the same about three Years Says She hath never lived in any other Service since Rented a House or paid any Taxes

Sworn the 6th. day of October 1762 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Eliz Lawrence
