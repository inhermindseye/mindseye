Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Sarah Ashburne aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that about 22 years ago she Intermarried with Wm. Ashburne alias Allen at the parish Church of Saint Botolph Aldgate that her said Husband has deserted her that she hath  five Children living by her said Husband Vizt. Sarah aged 20 years or thereabouts Margarett aged 15 years or thereabouts provided for Thomas aged 14 years or thereabouts James aged 10 years or thereabouts and Wm. aged 2 years and upwards unprovided for that her said Husband Rented & lived in a House in Plough Court Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 6 years & upwds at the yearly Rent of £20 hath quitted said House about 8 years ago since which time she he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of April 1786 N Bond

Sarah Ashburne
