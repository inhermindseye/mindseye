Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Smith Aged about Thirty Thee Years lodging at Mrs. Basquires a Chandlers Shop in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upon Oath says she is the Widow of Arthur Smith who has been dead about Seven Years Says her said late Husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to the best of her Knowledge or belief And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the Sixth day of March last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the said Mrs. Basquires in Wych Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of Sarah says that one John Cormock a Silversmith who lives in Wych Alley in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of June 1768 and at Several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Cormock and no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1769 before me} Tho Fellows

Jane Smith her Mark
