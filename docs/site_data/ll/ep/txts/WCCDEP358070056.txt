Westmr. to wit John Langford Aged about Sixty four Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex about Thirty Eight Years ago And by whem he hath three Children now living and all provided for Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one John Fowles a Blacksmith at Froom in the County of Somerset and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he Came to London and worked Journey work for upwards of Eight Years And further Says that he afterwards Rented Two several House in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint Saviour otherwise St. Mary Overees in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry the one at the Yearly Rent of Six pounds for about Seven Years & the other at the Yearly Rent of 5th. for about Eight Years & was rated to and Paid Land tax due for the Same hath quitted the same upwards of fourteen Years ago & hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or done any or Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day Janry 1773 before  Tho Kynaston R Butler

his John Langford Mark

Witness Petr. Sidebotham
