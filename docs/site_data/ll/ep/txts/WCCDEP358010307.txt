Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  James Pilsley aged about 42 years upon his Oath saith that about Three years ago he took the lower part of a house in the Str and adjoining Somerset house of one Robert Black for Three Months that he was to pay the said Robert Black the sum of Sixty Pounds for the said Three Months and that he lodged at the same Time at Mr. Whitemans the London Punch house on Ludgate Hill in the Parish of saint Brides alias Bridget in the City of London for which Lodging he paid the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds that he hath quitted said apartments upwards of Two years since which time he has not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th, day of Septr. 1789  Philip Dyor D Walker

Pilsley
