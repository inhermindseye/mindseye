Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Lloyd aged about 19 Years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she has been informed which Information she believes to be true that she was born at Newmarket in the Parish of Saint Mary at Newmarket in the County of Suffolk where her father and Mother kept house for several Years That she this Deponent and her Sister Susannah were after the Death of the father and Mother in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Mary's for Three or four Years

Sworn this 8th. day of Octr. 1795 } Rupt: Clarke  Thos. Bishop

her Elizabeth Lloyd Mark

Jas Talboys
