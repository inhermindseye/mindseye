Middx ss  Richard Pateman maketh oath that his Brother Rowland Pateman Deced about Twenty five years ago Rented a House in the parish of Allhallows the Great in the City of London and paid fourteen pounds a Year Rent for the same And this Depont. farthr. Saith that to the best of his knowledge & belief his said Brother never rented any other house of Ten pounds by the Year Rent nor that his Sd Brother ever gained any other Settlement than in the Sd. parish of St- Allhallows the Great And that he has left a Widow named Mary .

Sworn the 13th Day of June 1749 before  Richd: Ricards  Saml Gower

Ricd Pateman
