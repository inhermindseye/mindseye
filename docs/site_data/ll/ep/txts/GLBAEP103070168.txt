Middx ss  Peter Jones Maketh oath that about twenty one Years ago her rented a house known by the Sign of the Coopers Arms in King Johns Court in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in ye county of Surry and paid all Parish Taxes & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. saith that he hath not since gained any subsequent settlemt havg never since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor been a hired servt by ye Year nor served any parish offices nor done any Act to acquire another settlemt.

Sworn this 4th day of Sepr. 1756 before us  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Peter Jones
