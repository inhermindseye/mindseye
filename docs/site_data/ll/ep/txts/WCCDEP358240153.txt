St. Clement Danes 6th Septr. 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Mary Mc.Cue Wife of Neil Mc.Cue & Aged about 32 years Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband Neil Mc.Cue at the parish Church of St. James Westminster on the 6th day of Febry. 1780 that since then her said Husband Rented and Resided in an House in Holler Street the Corner of New Inn Passage in the said parish of Saint Clement Danes about one year & at the Annual Rent of £28 that ed that House about Eight years ago and that not since obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge Informed Information or belief And further Saith Children  that she hath three Children by her said Husband now living Namely Daniel aged 4 years on the second of March last and Thomas and Margaret both born on or about the 18th day of April 1791. that her said Husband left this Deponent and their said Children about four months ago with intent to go to Ireland And that She hath since heard that he has been in Dublin

Sworn the 6th. day of Novr. September 1792 before Me } R Butler

Mary Mc.Cue's Mark

Witness Jno Talboys .
