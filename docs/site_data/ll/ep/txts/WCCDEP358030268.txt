Westmr. (to wit  Eliza: Humphrys aged upwds. of 36 Yrs. upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr Saml. Tewksbury at the White horse in Claremarket in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr at the Yearly Wages of £3..10 for the Space of one Yr. & upwards and hath left her sd. Service upwds. of fourteen Yr. Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Tewksburys Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 85. Novr. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Eliz: Humphrays
