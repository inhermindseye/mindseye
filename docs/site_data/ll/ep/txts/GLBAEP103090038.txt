Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Ann Bradley of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Single woman taken before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the said County this 23d day of May-1766 .

This Examinant on her oath Saith That on the fourth day of January in the Year of our Lord 1765 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at her house in Cartwright Street in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate aforesaid And that the said Bastard Child (which hath been since Baptised & registred by the name of Elizth ann Miller ) is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate And that James Miller of Friday Street in Cheap side in the City of London Mariner and Master of his Majesties ship the Essex did get her with Child of the said Female Bastard Child.

Sworn the Day and Year first abovesaid before me  Chris: Scott

Ann Bradley Her Mark
