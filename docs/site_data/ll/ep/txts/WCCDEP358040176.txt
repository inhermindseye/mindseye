Westmr.  Ann Whiteman upon Oath saith she never was married and that about Six Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Page Mantua maker next door to the little Savoy Gate in the Precinct of the Savoy in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £4 for one Year and upwards Says she has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 22d. April 1748 before} Tho. Lane .

Ann Wighman
