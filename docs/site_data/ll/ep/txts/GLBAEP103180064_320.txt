Middlesex ss}  Margaret Dalton Singlewoman maketh oath that her Father Edward Dalton lately rented a house of the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the Sd County & resided thereon for the Space of Six Months & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & during Such his Residence in the Sd house this Deponent lived with him as part of his Family That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her Said Father has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent never was bound an apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 14 day of September 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Margaret Dalton
