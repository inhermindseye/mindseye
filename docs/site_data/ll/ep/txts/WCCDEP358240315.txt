St Clement Danes September 11th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields  (to wit) Ann Stephens aged 32 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She was Married to William Stephens at the parish of St George Bloomsbury on or about the 22d. day of January 1776 (by Banns) that previous thereto her said husband Served his Apprenticeship with Mr. James Brown  Shoemaker Russell Cant in the parish of St Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex that he boarded and lodged in his said Masters House House during & not Appren Apprenticeship that since the expiration of his Apprenticeship (which was about Nineteen years ago) And he hath Out Since Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year value lived as an hired yearly Servant for a Year together or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that her said Husband has been from her many years and She knows not where he is that She is distressed and become chargeable to the parish of St Clement Danes being

unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 11th day of Augt 1793 before Us Capt Clarke Thos. Gorden

Ann Stephens
