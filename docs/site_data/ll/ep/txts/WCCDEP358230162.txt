Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Thomas Kirkland aged about 37 years upon his Oath saith That about 4 years ago he intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone by whom he hath living One Child namely Sarah aged about four years That he rented and lived in a house in Plumbtree Court in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the City of London for two years at the yearly rent of 16 Guineas That he hath left the said House about 8 Months since which time he hath not rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th day of Novr 1800 before us  Saml. Edwards J Reeves

her Thos Kirkman Mark
