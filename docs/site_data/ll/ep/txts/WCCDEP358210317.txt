Westmr.  Mary Jenkins  Widow , on the behalf of Francis Jenkins Aged Eleven Years, and Mary Aged Ten Years upon Oath says they are the Children of Francis Jenkins by Susannah his Wife deceased and that the said Francis and Susannah were Married in the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about Twelve Years ago, as this Examinant hath been informed and which Information she verily believes to be true Says the said Francis Jenkins the Elder (who is gone into the Country) Rented a House in Great Saint Andrews Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds or thereabouts, quitted same about Seven Years ago and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 25th, day of August 1768, Before W Kelynge Petr Planck

The Mark of Mary Jenkins
