Middlesex ss  Millicent Chandler  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant for A year with Mrs  Mary Dolphin at the Sign of the Bull Head in Leadenhall Street in the Parish of Saint Catherine Cree Church in the City of London and Continued in Such Service for about Twenty Months at the Yearly wages of four Pounds & ten Shillgs and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that she hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year Nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st day of March 1769 before  Chris Scott  Dd: Wilmot

The Mark of Millicent Chandler
