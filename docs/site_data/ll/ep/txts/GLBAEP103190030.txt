Middlesex ss}  John Greenwood maketh oath that divers Years Ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indre to Charles Thompson of  Watling Street London  Velvet Weaver for the Term of Seven years That after he had served his Sd Master about five years & three Quarters he removed with him into Fort Street in the old Artillery Ground within the Liberty of his Majasties Tower of London where he served the Remainder of his Sd apprenticeship & has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement and that he hath a wife named Sarah to whom he was lawfully Married

Sworn this. 9. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of John Greenwood
