Middx & Westmr.} to Wit Elizth. Goreing Aged about 25 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with one Mrs. Cheeseman in Richmond Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of £4. hath quitted her sd. Service upwards of Two Years ago since whch. time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement by living as a hired yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 14th. day of Jany. 1783 before R Butler M Addington

her Elizth. Goreing  Mark
