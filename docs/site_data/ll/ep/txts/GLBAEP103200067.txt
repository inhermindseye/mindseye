Middlesex ss}  Margaret Robinson the Wife of Robert Robinson maketh oath that her sd husband who is now absent from her At Sea has informed her & which She believes to be true that he heretofore rented a house in New Port Street in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields Westminster in the Sd County at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same & to her knowledge or belief has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement & that she was lawfully Married to her Said husband.

Sworn this 10. day of Novemr 1783 before R Davies J Hawley

The Mark of Margaret Robinson
