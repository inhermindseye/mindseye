Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Ann Allaway aged about Fifty years upon her Oath saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Richard Allaway who lives in Vere Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes for near Two years at the yearly Wages of Four Guineas That the last Ten Months of her said Servitude was at the Country house of the said Richard Allaway in Peterborough Lane in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex that after she quitted said Service she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Febry. 1798 before us } Rupt: Clarke W Carter

Mary Ann Allaway

H Day Beadle
