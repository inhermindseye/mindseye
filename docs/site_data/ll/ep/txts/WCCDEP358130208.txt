Middx.  John Curtis aged upwards of 24 Years upon Oath says that he served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with Mr. Willm. Hutchinson a Perukemaker in Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year

Sworn Septr. 14th. 1756 before}

Jno Curtis
