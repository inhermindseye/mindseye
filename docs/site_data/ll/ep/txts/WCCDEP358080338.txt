Middx to wit Benjamin Organ Aged about Sixty Three Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with his late Wife Ann at the Parish Church of Brocksworth in Gloucestershire about Eleven Years ago and who has been Dead about five Years Says he has Two Children living by his said late Wife whose Friends now keeps in Gloucestershire And this Examinant further Says that he rented and lived in a House in Dacre Street near Tothill Street Westmr . in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westminster for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Twenty Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds P Annum paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 27th. day of February 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Benjn. Organ
