Middx  Mary Horton aged about 17 Years upon Oath says She never was Married That She lived a hired Yearly Servant upwards of a Year with one William Robinson a Painter who lodges with one James Eccob in Greyhound Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings and hath quitted her said Service about a Month since And further upon her voluntary Oath says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish She being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born, and that the above named William Robinson had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of February last and several other times since in the said William Robinson 's Appartment at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which She is now pregnant

Sworn the 16th. day of Octor. 1755 before } Barth: Hammond

The mark of Mary Horton
