Westmr. 

Susanna Johnson upon Oath saith she never was married Says her father & mother are both dead and she known nothing of the Settlement of either of them Says she was born in the Parish of Walk in the County of Somerset and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Febry 12th. 1746 before}
