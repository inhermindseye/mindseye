Middx ss  Rebecca Guest  widow of Richard Beck Guest deced Maketh oath that her said late husband was an Irishman but from what part of Ireland she cannot tell and never gained any Settlement in Great Britain to her Knowledge information or belief And this Depont. saith that she duly served an Apprenticeship with Edward Reynolds a Collar maker in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middx And by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt there And this Depont. Says that she hath not since gained any subsequent settlement And that she hath a Child Named Jane aged about eight years the lawfull issue of this Depont. by her said late husband

Sworn this 30 day of March 1756 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Rebecca Guest
