Middlesex ss}  John Burman maketh oath That divers years ago he lived in and rented a house in at the Russell Street in the parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the City & Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex of the yearly Rent or Value of Thirty Six Pounds & was Charged to & paid parish Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Depont hath not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 21st: day of November 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

John Burman
