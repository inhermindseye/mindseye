Middlesex ss} The above named Christian Astle maketh oath that Sometime after the death of her late husband James Astle deced She this Depont being then resident in the Work house of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County as being legally Settled there she was requested to come & live with one John Wilson of the Precinct of St. Catherine in the Said County Custom house officer & Accordingly she was taken into his house thereto live with him out of Charity but without any hiring or Contract as to Continuance Service Wages or gratuity that she lodged & boarded in the Sd Wilsons house & had at times Some Small Allowances made to her in money to find her in Snuff That she was employed in doing any thing in the Said Wilsons house which he thought fit to direct for to do And that she Continued with him in manner as aforesaid for the Space of two years & upwards And that in her own Apprehension She was at any part of the time at Liberty to quit the house of the Sd Wilson whenever she shod so think fit.

Sworn this. 30. day of July 1776 .  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Christian Astle
