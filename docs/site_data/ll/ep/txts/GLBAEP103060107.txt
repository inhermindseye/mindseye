Aldgate Middx

Mary Pipitt  wife of Abraham Pipitt of Aldgate abovesaid Maketh Oath that she had a former Husband named Matthew Bishop who often told her That he serv'd his Apprenticeship by Indenture to Hugh Lovejoy a Barber in Rattcliff High way in the Parish of Saint George Middx and never heard him Say that he ever gaind any other Settlement afterwards hath left a Daughter named Ann Bishop aged 16 years and Seven Months

Sworn 8th day of October 1748 before Richd.. Ricards N. Dukinfield

the mark of Mary Pipitt
