Middlesex ss}  Ann Hutchins  Singlewoman maketh oath That about nine years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with James Fortune of the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Taylor & Continued in Such Service for near the Space of two years & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year else where nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement else where That She hath a child named Robert Main born of this Deponts body in the Lying Inn Hospital in Brownlow Street Long Acre in the sd County who is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason She was never married and that one Robert Main mariner deced was the Father of the said child.

Sworn this 12 day of May 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Ann Hutchins
