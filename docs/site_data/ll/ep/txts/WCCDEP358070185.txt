Westmr. to wit Elizth. Talbott Aged about Thirty Years on his Oath says she Intermarried with her late Husband John Talbott at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes  about the Eleven Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Namely Eleanor Aged about five Years and John Aged about five Weeks Says her Said late Husband before his Intermarrd. with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one John Morris a Taylor  and whose Wife Dealt in Fouls & Butter for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds besides Diet & Lodging hath quitted his said service upwards of Ten Years Ago an after quitting the same he never rented House Paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 9th. day of April 1774 before R. Butler

her Elizth. Talbott mark
