Middx ss The Examination of Elizabeth Ripshaw Singlewoman taken on oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the said County of Middx (one whereof is of the quorum) residing in or next to the limits of the Parish Church of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County this day of May 1753

This Examinant on her oath Saith that about the latter End of the Month of July last past Richard Blackford who was then Servant to Joseph Mercer a Brewer in Hoxton in the County of Middx Came to the House of this Examinants Mother Sarah Ripshaw who then lived in in well Street Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Mary white chappell Middx Where this Examinant then lived and did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at he sd & Mothers house and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant Body Whereby she Conceived with Child of a Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered at the house of Mrs Anderson in Mouse Alley in the sd. Parish of saint Botolph without Aldgate on the Tenth day of April last past which said Male Bastard Child hath

been Baptised, by the name of Richard And the Same Child hath been Now is and still is likely to be Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate this Examinant being Poor and Not able to provide for the Same And this Examinant farther Saith that the said Richard Blackford is the true and only father of ye sd. Male Bastard Child And that this Examination is Voluntary And that She hath not been forced or Compelled to be Examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 2nd day of April May 1753 before us Richd: Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

The Mark of Elizabeth Ripshaw
