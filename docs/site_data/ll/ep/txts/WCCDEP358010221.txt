Saint Clement Danes

Catha Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Upon his Oath Saith that he was informed which Information he believes to be true That Catherine Lenoir aged 25 years or there abouts thatshe never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year that she never Rented House her Father and Mother kept House on Snow Hill  for 25 yrs. that her Father died there that after his death her Mother kept House in Walworththe of one year for upwards that Since she quitted said Houseshe to quitted Sd. Houseshe has done no Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained any subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge and belief

Sworn this 2d. day of July before}
