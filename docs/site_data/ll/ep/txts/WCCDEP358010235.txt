Saint Clement Danes 16th. Augt 88

Middlesex (to wit) Edward Burbidge one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  upon his Oath saith that he has been informed and believes that Marry Price a Pauper who now is in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Wife of William Priest to whom she was Married about thirty two Years ago that her said Husband rented a House in Great Chapel Street Oxford Road  in the Parish of Saint Ann within the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex a yearly Rent of upwards of ten pounds & that he has gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. Augt 1788 before us R Butler D Walker}

Edwd. Burbidge
