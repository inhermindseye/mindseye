Middx & Westmr.}  Eleanor Thoroughgood aged about thirty Years on her bath says she is the Widow of Joseph Thoroughgood who has been Dead upwards of Eight Years and by whom she hath no Children now living Says that being a Widow & without Child or Children she has lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Holloway at the Crown and Anchor Tavern Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Co. of Middlesex for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £7 and is now obliged to quit her place And furr. upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born says that one Edward Hopley Apprentice to Mr. Holloway her sd. Master is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowl of her Body sometime in the Month of May last but the partlar Days she does not now remember & at sevl. difft. times afterwds. at one of wch times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as afsd. & that the said Edwd. Hopley & no or. person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 13th. Day of Jany. 1777 R. Butler

her Eleanor Thoroughgood Mark
