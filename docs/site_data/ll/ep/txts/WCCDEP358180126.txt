Middx  Eliz: Walker upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of Willm. Walker  deced that the sd. Willm. Walker hath been dead abt. 1 Yr. that before his Intermarrd & whilst he was a Singleman the sd. Wm. Walker lived a Yearly Servt. with his Brother Mr. Jno Walker in Craven Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of £10. for the Space of 3 Yrs. that since he left his sd. Service neither he in his life or this Exaiant since his Death never Rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath one Child viz. Tho: aged upwds of 8 Yrs & being poor is not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworne Novr. 7th. 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

Elizabeth Walker
