Middlesex ss}  Martha Michael  Singlewoman maketh oath that She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents Save that her Mother has informed her & which She believes to be true That her Mother was born in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County That She this Deponent was born in Vinegar Yard in the Same Parish & was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Martha Michael
