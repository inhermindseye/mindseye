Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Meliora Abbott aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Medley in Old Round Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Sixteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent Saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn the 16th. day of Febry. 1798 before us N Bond E Read

her Meliora Abbott mark

Witness
