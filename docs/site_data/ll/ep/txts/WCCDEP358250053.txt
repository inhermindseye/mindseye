St Clement Danes July 16th 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }

St Mary le Bone } (to wit) Mary Hurlston  singlewoman aged about forty eight years Upon Oath saith That she never was Married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant wit Dyer Esquire in Queen Ann Street East in the parish of St. Mary le bone otherwise Mary bone in the County of Middlesex for two years at the Annual Wages of Eight Guineas That she quitted That service about Ten years ago and hath not since (leaving her said Master) done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge Information  or belief and that she is Poor and incapable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn the 16th day of July 1792 before us } Sampn Wright  Thos. Gordon

Mary Hurlston 's Mark

Witness Edwd Burbidge
