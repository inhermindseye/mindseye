Middx.  Mary Pettey Aged about Thirty Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says that on the 18th of August 1758 she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at Mr. Falkners in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Nancy Says One Richd. Toft at the Bankrupts Office in Lincolns Inn had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of Novr 1757 and at severall other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Female Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the 3d of January 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Pettey
