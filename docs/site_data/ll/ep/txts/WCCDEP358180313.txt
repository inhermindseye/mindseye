Middx  Carolina Phipps aged about Sixteen Years upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman & that she was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Kingson at the Naggs head in Monmouth Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx & Served him as an Apprentice upwards of the Years And then her Master & Mistress run away for Debt & this Exaiant doth not know where they are Says she never was Assigned over to any other person or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 3d. April 1745 before Mr: Fraser

The Mark of Carolina Phipps
