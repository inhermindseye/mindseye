Middlesex ss}  Ann Gibson  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father Jonathan Gibson as She has been informed & believes was duly bound to & Served abt five years of his Apprenticeship with William Manbey a Pipemaker in the Parish of St Ann in the Sd County & the Remainder thereof at Sea never returning back into any Port in England & has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief That she this Deponent was never married nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever was bound an Apprentice nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 17. day of July 1780 before  Jasper Clarke  Jno. Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Gibson
