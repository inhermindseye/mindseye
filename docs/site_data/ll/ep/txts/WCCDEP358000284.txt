Saint Clemt. Danes

Middx and Westmr}  Ann Hughes aged 40 years or thereabouts when her Oath saith That she intermarried with Thomas Hughes her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex about 18 years ago that she has 4 Children living by her said Husband Vizt. Thomas Hughes aged 9 years or thereabouts, John aged 6 years or therebouts, Ralph aged 3 years or thereabouts and Richard aged 18 Months or thereabouts that her said Husband departed this life about 4 Months ago - that her said Husband before their Intermarriage lived as a hired Yearly servant with Mr. Randall of Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex near 3 Years - that her said Husband after he quitted the said service of the said Mr. Randall hath not done any Act

Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief he may have gained any other Settlemt. nor hath This Examinant since his Death gained any Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of May 1786 before me R Butler

her Ann Hughes Mark
