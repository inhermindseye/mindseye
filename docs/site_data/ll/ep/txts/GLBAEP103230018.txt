Middlesex ss  Ann Hudson the widow of William Hudson deced maketh oath that She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her sd late husband nor can She give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late Father & Mother Arthur and Jane Hughes deced or eithr of them That She this Deponent as She has been informed & believes was born in Cinnamon Street in the Parish of st John Wapping in the sd County in or about the Month of June in the year one thousand Seven hundred & forty That She never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She may have acquired by her marriage as aforesd

Sworn this - 10. day of February 1787 before us}

The Mark of Ann Hudson

Note on Searchg Wapping Regr by the Beadle, neithr- the Birth or Baptism of this woman Cod be found
