Westmr.  Lettitia Fisher aged about 18 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Reynolds a Broker the corner of Dean Street in the Parish of St. Anne in the Liberty of Westmr ; for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service about Ten Months & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 1st. Augst. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran. Cary

The Mark of Letitia Fisher
