Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Strickland  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago She was duly bound Apprentice by the Parish officers of St Olave Southwarke in the County of Surry to one Richard Littleboy of the Parish of St Paul Deptford in the County of Rent  Victualler & Served five years & half of Such Apprenticeship or there abouts in the said Parish of Saint Paul That differences Arising between this Depont. & her mistress the Indres of Apprenticeship were by Mutual Consent delivered up & She this Deponent was there upon discharged from her Apprentice- ship & at Liberty to go Where She pleased She this Depont being then of the Age of Nineteen years or the reabonts as She now recollects That She this Deponent thereupon (her said Master & Mistress) giving her a character) hired herself & lived as a hired Servant  by the year by & with William Dimford in Stockwell Town in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth at in the County of Surry Victualler at certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Fifteen Months That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor hath She ever been married or done any Act to gain a Settlement Since Such her Servitude as aforesaid.

Sworn this. 10. day of Septemr 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Elizabeth Stickland
