Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Medhurst maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with John Pierre Pont of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at Certain yearly Wages & Contind in such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That she has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor been married or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The mark of Eliz: Medhurst
