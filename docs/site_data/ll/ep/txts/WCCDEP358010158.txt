Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Wood aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House paid Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that she was born in Charles Street Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex That her Father was an Irishman and never gained any Settlement in England

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1787  Sampr. Wright  Thos. Gordon

her Mary Wood Mark
