Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Sutton aged about 38 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yrly Servant for one Year says she was bound out an Apprentice by the Trustees of the Charity School of St- Clemt- Danes to Mr Carpenter a Glazier and Painter in Ball Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx and served the said Mr. Carpenter under that Indenture for the Space of 12 Months or thereabouts when her said Master was Arrested for Debt and left this Examinant to provide for herself Says she never was assigned over to any other person or ever gained any other subseqt- Settlemt- than as aforesaid

Sworn this 2nd- Day of April 1777 before Us  Jno. Machin  Geo: Reid

Mary Sutton
