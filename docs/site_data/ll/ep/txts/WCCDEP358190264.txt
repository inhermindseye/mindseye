Middx  Henry Abbott aged upwards of 50 Years Lodging in the Parish of St Clement Danes , Upon Oath Says he lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Fras: Cottinten  Esqr . in Argyle Buildings in the Parish of St James in the Liberty of Westmr. for three Years & upwards Yearly wages Ten pounds Says his Master Horses stood at the Naggs Head Livery Stables in Swallow Street in the Parish of St George Hanover Square where this Examinant Lodged he being at that time a Singleman without Child or Children and hath Quitted Said Service upwards of Sixteen Years and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Any Taxes

Sworn the 10th Day of August 1762 : Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Henry Abbott
