Middx to wit Elizabeth Gray Aged about Fifty Two Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband Arthur Gray at the Parish Church of Elderton in the County of Northumberland about Twenty five Years ago and who died about fourteen years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children livings Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she went and lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Strang an Ingraver in Castle Street Leicester Square in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Eight Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Ten Years ago and after quitting her said Service she never gained any other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 6th. day of June 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Gray
