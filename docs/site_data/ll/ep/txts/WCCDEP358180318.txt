Middx  John Dailey aged upwards of 60 Years upon his Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Wm. Bennett in the Parish of St. Catherine in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland  Cooper & after the Expiration of his Apprenticeship lived in & rented a House in the Parish of St. Patrick in the same City of the Yearly Rent of £5 for one Years & pd. his S to the Poors Rate there Says he hath not since he left the sd. house Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 9th. April 1745 before} Wm: Jones .

John Dailey
