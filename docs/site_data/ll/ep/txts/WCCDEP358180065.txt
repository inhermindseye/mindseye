Middx  Mary Patterson  Singlewoman upon her Voluntary Oath saith that on the 24th- Day of February last she was delivered of a Male bastard Child at the House of one Mr. Molloy in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized Charles-& is now living & likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that one Mr. Charles Welch  Wine Cooper in Cambridge Street near Carnaby Markett did begett the said Child on her Body she this Examinant at such time liveing a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs Welch the Mother of the said Charles Welch where he became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body severall times in the latter End of May or beginning of June last in the House of the said Mrs Welch in Cambridge Street aforesd. at one of which times he gott her with the sd. Male bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & that he the sd. Cha: Welch & no other Person is the true & real Father thereof

Sworne the 5th- March 1742 before} Chas: Egerton  Samuel Clarke

The Mark of Mary Patterson
