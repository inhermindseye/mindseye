Middx  Elizth. Read aged abt. Twenty four Years lodging at one Mrs. Morris's in Crown Court in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never Married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Pugh Seale  Engraver in the Fore Street in the Strand in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds for the Space of One Year & upwds. & hath left the same abt. One Month that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 8th. of January 1741 before}
