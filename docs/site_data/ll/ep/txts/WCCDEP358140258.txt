Middx.  Geo: Green aged abt. 64 Years upon Oath says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Warner a Butcher in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 20th. June 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

George Green
