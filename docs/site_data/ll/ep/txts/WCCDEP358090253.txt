Middlesex to wit Ann Steere Aged about Twenty four years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says She lived a hired yearly Servant with One Mr. Lewis in Cooks Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One year and Seven Months at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Seven Weeks ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says She is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes She being Poor and Unable to Maintained the same when born And further on her Oath says that One Samnee Batten a Servant to Mr. Marriett Doctor in Divinity who Lines in upper Berkley Street Portman Square is the Father thereof He having Carnal knowledge of Her Body in her said Masters House some time in the Month of December last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at several Different times afterwards at at one of which times He get her with Child of the Child or Children of which She is now Pregnant and that the said Samnee Batten and no other Person did beget the same and that He is the true Father thereof.

Sworn this 12th day of Septr. 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Steere Mark
