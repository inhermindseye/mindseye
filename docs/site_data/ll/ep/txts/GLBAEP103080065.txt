Middx ss.  William Steele Maketh oath that about fourteen years ago he lived as a hired servant by the Year without Mr Kirkman of Friday Street in the Parish of St Matthew in Friday Street in the City of London Ribbon Merchant and Continued in such service for the space of Two years at the yearly wages of Four Pounds and by means thereof as he this Depont. is advised gained a legal settlement there That this Deponent afterwards Married and then lived again as a hired servant by the Year with Mr Riverton a Bookseller in St. Pauls Church Yard London and Continued in such lastmenconed service for the space of a year but during such lastmenconed service he was a Married Man. And this Depont. saith that he hath not since lived as a hired servant by the year nor Rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor served any Parish offices Nor done any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlement And that he hath a wife Named Ann and two Children to wit Mary Ann aged two Years & a half and John William Aged four Months the lawful issue of this Depont. by his sd. Wife

Sworn this Thirtieth day of January 1761 before  Chris: Scott  Tho: Quarrill

Willm Steel
