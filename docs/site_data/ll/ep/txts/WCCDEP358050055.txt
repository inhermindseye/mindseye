Middx  Eleanr Mc.Funn  Wido . of Peter Mc.Funn (who dyed abt. 14 Months) upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late husband in the West Kirk near Edinburgh on the 17th. Day of May 1732 and that he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Edward Bridden a Shoemaker in the Parish of West Kirk in the Shire of Midlowding in Scotland and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 3d. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of held Eleanr Mc.funn
