Middlesex to wit John Ashmore Aged about Sixty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Martha his Wife at the Fleet London about Thirty Years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children now living Says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Colonel Whitworth in Clifford Street Burlington Gardens in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster in the Capacity of a Postillion & Second Coachman for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds hath quitted his said Service upwards of forty Years ago and after quitting his said Service he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th. day of Novr. 1778 before  James Croft  Jno Barnfather

John Asmore
