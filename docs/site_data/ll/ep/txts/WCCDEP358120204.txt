Middx  Mary Todd upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Adam Gordon at the Blackhorse opposite Catherine Street in the parish of St Mary le Strand for thirteen months & upwards (Successively) at the yearly Wages of £ four pounds ten Shillgs. that she quitted her sd. Service about five or Six Years and has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Octr 2d. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond  Benjamin Cox

The Mark Mary. Todd
