Middx  Elizabeth Collins aged 19 Years Removed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields upon her Oath saith she is the Wife of William Collins ( gone to sea about four Months since) to whom she was Married at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields above mentioned about 17 Months ago Says he informed her he was Born in London but in what Parish cannot say doth not know that he ever was bound an Apprentice rented £10 by the Year paid any Parish Taxes or was a Yearly hired Servant That she never heard him say any thing in regard to his Father's Settlement That she this Examinant was (before her Intermarriage and while Single ) a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Robinson a Councellor at Law in New Castle Court in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for the Space of One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & ten shillings diet and Lodging which Service she quitted near two Years before her Marriage with her said Husband and hath not been a Yearly Servant since That she is now pregnant

Sworn the 17th. day of Feby. 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizabeth Collins
