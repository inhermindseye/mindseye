Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

passd. to: Bloomsbury  Sarah Babb aged upwds 80 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Crowder of Duke Street Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for four years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted said Service upwards of 30 years since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 27th. day of September 1785 M: A: Taylor. Jno. Machin

her Sarah Babb marks
