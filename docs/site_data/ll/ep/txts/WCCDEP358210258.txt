Westmr.  Elizabeth Tongue Aged about Eighteen Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Peter Wadsworth (her Father in Law) in Bear Yard Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds ten Shillings for the first Year, and Four Pounds for the second Year, and after she quitted said Service never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 25th. day of March 1768 Before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Elizth. Tongue
