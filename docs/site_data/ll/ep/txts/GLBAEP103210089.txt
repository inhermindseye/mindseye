Middlesex ss  Margaret Turnbull the widow of Thomas Turnbull deced maketh oath that since her sd husbands death she was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Robertson Victualler in Lower East Smith field in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & Continud in such service under such hiring for the space of three years she this Deponent then having no Child or Children That she has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or been married or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 2. day of August 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Margaret Turnbull
