Middlesex & Westminster}  Elizabeth Skinner Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Kirk a Nursery Woman in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her sd Service upwards of Three Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath Says that on the 14th. day of August last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Robt. Cairns in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized William and is likely to become Chargable to the said Parish And further says that one Benjamin Carne a Bricklayer did beget such Bastard Child on her Body Says that the said Benjamin Carn served his Apprenticeship in Wales and never gained a Settlement in England to her Knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 27th. day of May 1778 before  Jno Barnfather  Wm. Frank

her Elizabeth Skinner Mark
