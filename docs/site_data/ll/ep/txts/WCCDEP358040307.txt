Middx & Westmr.}  Eliza; Hollis aged upwds. of 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year and that her father Nichs. Hollis deceased lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds in Clemt. Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx for one Year & upwards & there dyed and further upon her Voluntry Oath saith she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Samuel Scott of Garden Court in the Temple Peruke maker did beget the sd. bastard Child or Childn. on her body the sd. Samuel Scott having had

Carnal knowledge of her body in the Months of May & June last and at sevl. other times since at his house in Garden Court aforesd. at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastd. Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the sd Samuel Scott and no other Person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Decr. 30th: 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliza Holles
