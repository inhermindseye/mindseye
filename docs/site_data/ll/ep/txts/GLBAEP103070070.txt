Middx ss.  Ann Bellamy  Widow of William Bellamy deced Maketh oath that her sd. late Husband often informed her that about Twenty eight Years ago he lived as a hired Servant by ye Year with Willm. Royston  Esqr in Basinghall Street in the Parish of St. Michael Basinghall Bassingham London and Continued in Such Service for ye Space of one Year and upwards and reced Twelve Pounds a Year Wages and was then a Single Man and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there Which information this Deponent Verily believes to be true And ship Depont Saith that to her knowledge informatn. of belief her sd. late Husband Never rented house of Ten Pounds a Year Nor Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlemt. And that she this Depont never was a hired Servt. Since her sd. husbands Death Nor done any Act to her knowledge of belief to acquird any legal Settlemt. Since her sd husbands of Death.

Sworn this 27th day of Novr. 1752 before us  Richd. Ricards  Walter Berry

hir Ann AB Bellamy  Mark
