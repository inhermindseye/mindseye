Middx to wit Mary Passmuch Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Passmuch as she believes she having heared that He died upwards of Ten Months ago and this Examinant upon her  Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born and that One Stone Coachman to the Oxford Past Coach did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body that the last time he had  Carnal knowledge of her Body was the 14th. day of November last at the Kings Head in Wych Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and for at several times four Month prior to the time abovementioned in the same House at One of which times He got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Stone and no other person did beget the same and that He is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of July 1781 before  Sampn Wright

her Mary Passmuch  Mark
