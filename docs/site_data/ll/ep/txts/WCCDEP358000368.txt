Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Bender aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or Paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Messrs. Tompkins & Carter in Butcher Row  in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 20 years at the yearly Wages of £4 hath quitted said Service about 26 years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 12th. day of Octr. 1786 R Butler

Elizabeth Bender
