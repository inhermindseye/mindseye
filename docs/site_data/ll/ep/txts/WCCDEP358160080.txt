Middx The Exaicon of Hannah Church  Singlewoman lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes taken the 8th day of Octr. 1739 who upon her Oath Saith that she was bound an Apprentice to & Served eight Years Apprentiship with one Mrs. Blackman in Baileys Alley in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . Roll & Wiermaker and this Exaiant further Saith that she never rented any House paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath a Soare Legg and by means thereof is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Yeare above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Hanh. Church
