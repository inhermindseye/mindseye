Middx  Margt. Miles aged about 14 Years upon her Oath saith she was bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Michael in Crooked Lane London to Mr. Thomas Ives a Turner in Great Swan Alley in St. Johns Street in the Parish of St. James at Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex & lived with him about 9 Months & run away from her sd. Master about 2 Months since Says she never was Assigned over to any person nor lived as a Servt. for the Space of Six Weeks with her sd. Masters knowledge or Consent or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 20th. July 1745 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Margt. Miles
