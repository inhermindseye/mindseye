Middx,  John Edwards aged about Fifty three Years lodging at Mr. Murphy's in Vere Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex upon Oath says he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. James Fitzgerald at the Elephant and Castle  without Temple Bar in the aforesaid Parish of St Clement Danes for One Year and eight Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds and Seven Shillings, hath quitted said Service about Twenty Years, that during the time of his said Service he was a single man without Child or Children, and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 1st. March 1765 . before} Paul Vaillant

Jon. Edwards
