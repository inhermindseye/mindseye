Aldgate Middx

Mary Church  Singlewoman Maketh Oath that the last place where she was a hired Servant by the year to live One full year was with Mr. Baker a Butcher in St. James Market in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster and had five pounds a year Wages and was there about One year and about three Months And hath gaind no Settlement Since

Sworn 11th day of May 1747 before Robert Dennett  Richd Ricards

Mary Church
