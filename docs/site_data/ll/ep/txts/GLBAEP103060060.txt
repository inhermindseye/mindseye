Middx  John Hollingsworth singleman maketh Oath and Saith that about Thirty Seven Years ago he was hired a Yearly Servant to Mr . William Chittam at Lauramows Pond in the parish of St. Mary at Newington in the County of Surry and lived with him as such one Year and received Seven pounds a Year for the same And hath gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And hath a Wife named Margaret

Sworn this 5th. day of March 1745 Before Robert Dennett

his John Hollingsworth Mark
