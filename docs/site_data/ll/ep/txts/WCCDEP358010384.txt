Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Sarah Zether  singlewoman aged twenty one years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married, that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Yardley is China Street Bedford Square in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for upwards four years at the yearly Wages of seven pounds that she quitted her sd. place about eighteen Months ago & since then has not lived for twelve Months in one Service nor rented any House Tenement of ten pounds a year Value nor done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information & belief she may have acquired any other or Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. Day of Janry 1791 before  Sampr Wright M Addington

her Sarah Zether Mark
