Middlesex  Sarah Brown Aged about 27 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Davis an Attorney at Law at No. 26 in Old Lincolns Inn in the Parish St. Andrew Holbourn for Sevl. Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service upwards of 3 Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. And further on her Oath says that on the 7th. day of May 1778 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which was soon afterwds. baptized by the name of Joseph and is become Chargeable to the same Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same and further says that one John Davis an Attorney  No.2 Lyon Inn & who Uses the fountain in Clare Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sevl. Years last past at one of which times he got her with Child of the male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. and that the sd. John Davis & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day of Novr. 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Brown
