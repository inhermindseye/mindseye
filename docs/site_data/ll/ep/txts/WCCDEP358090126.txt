Middx  Mary Audin Aged about Thirty Three Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of John James Audin (now a Private Soldier in the Western Regiment of the Middlesex Militia as a Substitu for John Kenton of the Parish of Hammersmith in the said County of Middlesex ) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Shoreditch in the County aforesdid And by whom she hath one Child now living namely Sarah Aged about Seven Years and further says she is unable to provide for the same unless she has the Assistance as by Act of Parliament is in that Case made.

Sworn this 29th day of Feby. 1780 before R Butler

Mary Audiner
