Westmr.  Eliz. Morris aged about 23 Years (Lodging at Mr Halls in Butcher Row in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr.) upon Oath saith she never was married and that she Lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Lane a Farmer at Bell Hill in the Parish of Oakingham in the County of Berks at the Yearly wages of three Pounds for the Space of two Years or thereabouts and hath quitted her sd. Service abt. 2 yrs. since which she has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Childrn. which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the aforesaid Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born

and that William Remnant of Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls  Plumber did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the said Wm. Remnant having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the month of March last and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he get her with Child of the said bastd. Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant and that he the said William Remnant and no other Person is the true and real father thereof.

 Sworn Novr. 16th. 1749 before H Feilding

Elizabeth Morris
