St Clement Danes Febry. 13th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James Clerkenwell  (to wit) Mary Pinnell aged 50 years or therea thereabouts Widow Maketh Oath and saith that she was Married to her late Husband James Pinnell at the parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn by License about 30 years ago that since their said Marriage her said Husband Rented and Resided in an House in North Row Battle Bridge in the parish of St James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for about 3 years at the Annual Rent of £12 that he quitted it about 18 years ago & never after during his Life Rented any House House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief And this Examinant further said that he Died about 3 years ago and that she hath not Since been Married nor obtained or become intitled unto any other legal Settlement than in Right of her said Husband as aforesaid

Sworn the 13th day of February 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

M Pinnell
