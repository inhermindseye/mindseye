Westmr.  Eliz: Damsell aged 16 Yrs. & upwds. upon Oath saith she never was married Says she is the Daughter of Joseph Dansell by Eliz his Wife both deceased Says her sd. late father Joseph Damsell Served Seven Years Apprentiship with his Mother Eliz: Damsell Plaisterer in the Old Change in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen in Old Fish Street London and never rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as she hath been informed & beleives Says she this Examinant never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year in otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 27th. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Eliz Damsell
