Aldgate Middx

William Atkins Maketh Oath that he was a hired Servant by the year And livd one whole year as Such with one John Carrol a Barber in the Liberty of Norton Folgate in the Said County And had ten pound for his Said years Service (he being a Single man) and hath gaind no Settlement Since

Sworn 2d day of March 1747 before Robert Dennett Richd. Ricards

Will Atkins
