Middx  Ann Yeldon Aged about Fifty Four Years on behalf of Ann Bromley an Infant aged upwards of Nine Years on her Oath says she is the Daughter of Ann Spatcher lately deceased and who as she has been Informed & believes was never Married but that she had the abovementioned Ann Bromley by one Thomas Bromley who was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in London as she believes says her Mother Ann Spatcher lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Mr. Ralfe at the Sign of the Blackmors Head Macklesfield Street in the Parish of Saint Ann Soho in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about five Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. And further says that the said Ann Bromley was born in Grafton Street in the assd. Parish of Saint Ann Soho

Sworn this 15th- Day of May 1779 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Yeldon Mark
