Middlesex ss}  Susannah Inglish the widow of Joseph Inglish deced maketh oath That her said late husband has informed her & which information she believes to be true That he duly Served his apprenticeship with one Mr Forster who Kept a Publick house known by the Sign of the Sugar Leave & Punch Bowl near Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the City & Liberty of Westmr in the County of Middlesex & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent her said husband did not aferwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement elsewhere & that she has not done any Act Since his Death to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 31d day of December 1773 before Cha: Digby Wilmot

The Mark of Susannah Inglish
