Westmr. 

Margt. Brathwaite upon Oath saith she never was married Says about Eleven Yrs. ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Hampstead, Vintner in Bridges Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds for one Year & upwds. Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Hampsteads Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her a knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Febry 20th. 1746 before Tho: Burdus

Margarett Brathwaite
