Middx  Sarah Allen Aged abt. Twenty two Years Upon Oath Says She was never Marryed That She was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the Sixteenth day of Octr. last in Clemts. Inn in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx wch. hath Since been Baptized by the name of John Says that One Saml. Sparrow an Apothecary did beget the sd. Male Bastard Child he having had Carnall knowledge of this Examinant in the Month of March last & at Sevll. other times since at one of wch. times he get her with Child of the Male Bastard Child she was delivered of as aforesd. Says the sd. Child is liveing & likely to become Chargeable the sd. Parish She being poor & unable to maintain the same

Sworn the 10th day of Novr. 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Sarah Allen
