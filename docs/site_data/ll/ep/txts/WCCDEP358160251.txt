Middx. The Examination of Mary Magee  Singlewoman who was found wandering and Begging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes  in the County of Middx taken the fifth Day of February 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. three Years ago She was hired to & lived with one Mr. Sculley in Brides Street in the Parish of Brides in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland  Linnen Draper as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds and hath been come away from the same abt. Six Months And this Examinant further on her Oath saith that She hath not since She left the said Mr. Seulley rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} J Poulson

The Mark of Mary Magee
