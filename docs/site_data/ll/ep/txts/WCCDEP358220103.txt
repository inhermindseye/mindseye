Middx to wit }  Elizth. Emptage Aged about 60 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Emptage who has been dead about Twenty Three Years ago Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Peter Hallam a Cabinet- maker in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she Verily believes to be true and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither has this Examinant since her said Husband Death

Sworn this 19th. day of Decr. 1769 before Me  R Butler

her Elizth. Emptage Mark

Witness Petr. Sidebotham
