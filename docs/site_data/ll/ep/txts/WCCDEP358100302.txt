Middlesex and Westminster}  Swo to St. Clemt . A. to wit Jane Winchester aged about Thirty two years on her Oath says she Intermarried with to Benjn. Winchester her late husband deceased at the Parish Church of Saint Martin Ludgate  in the year 1766, That after their Intermarriage her said Husband rented and lived in a Shop in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Six Months at the yearly Rent of Twenty Guineas That her said Husband died about five months ago and that her said Husband in his Life time or this Examinant since his Death after quitting the said Premises  hath not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinant Knowledge or belief she may have gained a Settlemt elsewhere

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1784  Sampn. Wright

Jane Winchester
