Middlesex ss}  Frances Green maketh oath that divers Year ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with Robert Alday in the Parish of Saint Botloph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Baker at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two years & three Quarters of a Year & by means there of Gained a legal Settlement there That she this Deponent has not Since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for a year Nor dane any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And this Deponent farther Saith That on the Twenty ninth day of May one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy four in the house of one Grove in old Gravel Lane in the parish of Saint George in the Said County she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child Which hath Since been Baptised & Registred by the name of Charles Alday And that the said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate And that the said Robert Alday is the Father of the sd Child

Sworn time 11th day of August 1775 before  Burf Camper

The Mark of Frances Green
