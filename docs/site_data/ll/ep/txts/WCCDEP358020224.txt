Saint Clement Danes.

Middx & Westmr. to wit}  Mary Parker Aged 24 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she lived as a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mathews at the Lyon & Cat Carey Street for upwards of Twelve Months with Mr Hornby at the same Place for 2 or 3 Years That since she left the Lyon & Cat she has not to the best of her knowledge done any Act whatever whereby she may have gained a Settlement, That she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child on the 27. day of October last at the Appartments of a Mr- Harris in Clements Lane And Further upon her Oath Saith That she was upon the Town as a Street Walker and came of tele who is the Father thereof or begot same.

Sworn this 22d. day of Septr. 1790 Meading

her Mary Parker mark
