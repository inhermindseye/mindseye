Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Mary Jones aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Wm. Jones her present Husband who is now ill in St. Bartholomews Hospital at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields about Six years ago by whom she hath Three Children living vizt. John aged about Five years Mary aged about Three years and William aged about Ten weeks That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for about Three years at the yearly Rent of Fifty pounds That he hath quitted said house about Two years since which time he hath not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. of March 1795 N Bond T Mathew

Mary Jones
