Middx  Ann Trueman aged about 25 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Craig a Peruke maker in Essex Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £3, £4 & £5. for 3 Years & upwards and has not since she quitted her sd Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that Geo Newton late a Servant to Sr . Fras. Charlton did beget the sd. Child or Children on her body the sd. Geo. Newton lodging with his Master at the aforesd. Mr Craigs there he became Acquainted with her & had carnal knowledge of her body in the month of May last and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. bastard Child or Children whereof she is now pregnant and that the said Geo. Newton

and no other Person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Decr 26th. 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Trueman
