Middx & Westmr.}  Charlott Smith Aged about 17 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father was a Native of Scotland and never gained a Settlement in England to her Knowledge or Belief Says she was Born at the House of Mr. William in Orchard Street in the Parish of St. Margt. Westmr . and can give no other Account of her Settlemt. orwise. than as aforesaid And furr. upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 19th. Day of August last she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Wincapes in Clements Lane Clare Markett which has since been Baptized by the name of Wm. Richard and is become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same Says that one Richard Smart an Attorney at No. 1 Staple Inn is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Months of Octr. or Novr. 1775 but the partlar Day or time she cannot now recollect and at sevl. difft. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. and that the said Richd. Smart & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 13th. Day of Jany. 1777 . R Butler

Charlott Smith
