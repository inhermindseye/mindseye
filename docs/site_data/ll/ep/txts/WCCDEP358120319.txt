Westmr.  Joseph Pinkney aged abt. 36-years upon Oath Saith he was married to his wife Mary at Bawtry in the County of York on the 26th. of Decr. 1746 Old Style by whom he has 4 Child (viz) John aged 5 Years or thereabouts Wm . aged abt. 4 Years Geo aged abt. 2 Yrs. and Eliz aged abt. 18 weeks and that he rented a house of the yearly Rent of £10 in Ship Yard in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . lived therein abt. three months & quitted the same about four Years ago and that he has not since Rented (a house) of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds nor pd. parish Taxes

Sworn April 3d. 1755 before}

Jas. Pinkney
