Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Carroll aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she has lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Gale in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  for these Eighteen Months past at the yearly wages of Five Pounds And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Thomas Miles who lives with Mr. Pidcock in Exeter Change is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Thomas Miles and no other Person did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of Janry. 1796 } J Hind

her Sarah Carrole Mark

Witness B Newman

Man paid

£15.15 on the 20th. Janry. 1796 and Executed Release

Child born in the House 19th. March 1796 and Named Sarah Carroll

Sent to Enfield the 15th. July. 1796
