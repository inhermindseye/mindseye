Westmr.  Jane Arnold Aged upwards of Sixty four Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Giles a Taylor in Arrundell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds, Quitted said Service upwards of Six Years ago, and hath not since Rented House or Paid Taxes, or any other ways gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 9th day of March 1768 . Before}  W Kelynge

Jane
