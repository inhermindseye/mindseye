Middlesex  William Large A Soldier now going to Flanders and belonging to the first Regiment of foot Guards Maketh Oath and Saith that he was born in the Parish of Frodsham in the County of Cheshire and has not since gained any legal Settlement to his Knowledge And that he hath four Children by Mary his late wife deceased (Namley) John aged about Eleven years William aged about Nine years Ann aged five years and Stephen aged about two years.

Taken and Sworn before us this 10th. July 1745 Robert Dennett N Dukinfield

William Large

Memd. this I Delived a Duplicate of the above Exx to Large Bolton pd to ye Justices
