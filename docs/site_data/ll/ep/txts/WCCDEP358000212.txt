at Mrs. Elkins who lodges at Mr. Hames-No. 17 Richmond St. Old that Square

St. Clemt. Danes

Westmr. (to wit) Mary Ward aged 33 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was Married to Thomas Ward her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Duke's Old Street in the County of Middlesex about 16 Years Years ago That she has two Children living by her said Husband vizt. William Ward aged 14 Years or thereabouts and Elizabeth Ward aged 11 Years or thereabouts- That since their Intermarriage  they Rented and lived in a House in New Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of £16 for upwds. of of £12 Months and quitted the Same abt. 8 Month ago since which time her said Husband hath not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief & that her said Husband has  deserted her & Children and refuses to Maintain them

Sworn this 23d day of Janry. 1786 before D Walker

her Mary Ward Mark
