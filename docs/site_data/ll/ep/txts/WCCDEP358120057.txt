Middx.  Sarah Horsman upon Oath saith she is the wife of Wm Horsman to whom she was married at the Fleet London about ten years ago and that this Examinant has been informed and verily believes her sd. husband Served five years of his Apprentiship with Mr Hedges a Picture frame maker and then his sd Master dyed and this Examinants husband never was assigned over to any other person nor ever rented ten Pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year and that the three last Years of the sd. term of five Years her sd. husband continually lodged in the sd Mr. Hodges house in the yr to Bailey in the Parish of St Sepulchers London

Sworn Feby. 20th. 1754 -before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Horsman
