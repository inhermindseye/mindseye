Middx  Alexander Clark upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr John Child  Cheesemonger in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds & left his sd Service about 15 Years ago since which he has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr 8. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Alexr. Clark
