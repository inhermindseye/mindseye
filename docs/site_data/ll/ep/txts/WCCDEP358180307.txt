Middx  Daniel Garley aged about Thirteen Years upon his Oath saith that his Father & Mother are both Dead & he cannott give any Account of their Settlement Says he was born in the Parish of Pychley in the County of Northampton & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 20th: Day of March 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark Daniel of Garley
