Middlesex  Mary Yeates  Wido . of Henry Yeates upon Oath says she intermarried with the sd. Henry Yeates at the Parish Church of St. Ann within Aldersgate abt. 17 Years since Says her sd. late Husband hath been dead abt. 14 Years Says she lived in & rented a house in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £12 for the Space of 2 Yrs. or thereabts. & hath left the same upwds. of 2 Yrs. Says she hath not since she left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 9th. Augt. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Mary Yates
