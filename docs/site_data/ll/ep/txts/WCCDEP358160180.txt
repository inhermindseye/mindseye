Middx  The Examination of Anne Seymore  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 9th. Day of Octr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her Father Jas. Seymore lived in & rented a House in Ship Yard in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of Sixteen Years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £16 & there dyed & her Mother Ann Seymore continued to live in & rent the same & further saith that after the death of her Father she lived as a day apprentice with one Mrs. Swaile a Mantua make in Maiden Lane in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garde in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of three Years but during all that time lodged & Boarded with her Mother in Ship yard that she never rented any House or lived a yearly hired Servt. with any Person for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & being very much aflicted with Convulsion Fitts is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before} Jas Gronous

The mark of Ann Seymore
