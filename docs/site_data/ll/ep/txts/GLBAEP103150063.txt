Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Isabella Wilkinson of the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex  Single woman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the said County this. 16th. day of October 1775 Who Saith That on the Third day of September last past in the Dwelling house of her Father Jonathan Wilkinson Situate in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Said parish She this Examinant was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Which hath Since been Baptized & registred by the name of Jane Wilkinson And that the said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish And that Charles Manning now or late of Ryalls Court  Horsely Down in the County of Surry Walerman is the Father of the Said Child.

Taken & Signed the day. and Year above written before me  Geo. C. Smith

Isabella Wilkinson
