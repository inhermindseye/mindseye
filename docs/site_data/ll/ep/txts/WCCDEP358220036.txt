Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Innis Aged about forty four years on her Oath Says she never was married was an Apprentice or ever rented Ten pounds a Year Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Henry Lewis (lately deceased) in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Three Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about a Month ago and hath not since rented House paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. Day of June 1769 before me} Tho Lane

her Jane Innis Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
