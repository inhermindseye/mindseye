St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  GG (to wit) Eleanor Edwards Sig Singlewoman Aged twenty one years & upwards Upon Oath Saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Comer Baker in little Stanhope Street  May Fair in the parish of St George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for 3 Years, four pounds P Annum, that she left that Service about 5 or 6 Years ago, and since hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent further saith upon her Oath that she was delivered on the Sixth day of this Instant December (at Mr. Tanners GG House or Apartment in Milford Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County & Liberty) of a Female Bastard Child which was on the 26th Instant Christened Frances Edwards at the Church of the sd parish of St. Clement Danes GG And that the said Child hath become chargeable to the And that William Willis now Head Clerk at the Crown & Anchor in the Strand in the said parish of St Clement Danes is the only real and true Father of such Child of which she was so delivered, he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of GG March or April last and at other times since at some or only which times he did beget the said Child thereon And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that no one beside the said William Willis is the real true father thereof And

Sworn the 31st Decr. 1791 before me} R Butler

Eleanor Edwards 's Mark

Mr. Wm Willis paid £20 to Mr. Richardson which the Overseers accepted
