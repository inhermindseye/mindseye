Middx  Absalom Kettle upon his Oath saith he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in Ploughstable Alley in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year or thereabouts and has not since he Quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 1st. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

Absalom Kettle
