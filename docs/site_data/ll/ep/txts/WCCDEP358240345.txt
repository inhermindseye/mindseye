St Clement Danes 26th Octr. 1793.

Middlesex And Westminster }  (to wit) Ann Noyes aged 38 years & upwds.. Widow Upon Oath saith that she was Married at the parish Church of St Leonard Shoreditch on or about the fourteenth day of February 1794 (by Banns) to James Noyes , that she is informed and believes that her said Husband lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Burnet late of the Fountain Wych Street for eighteen Months or thereabouts but at what Wages this Examinant knows not that he quitted that service about eleven or twelve years ago.

and not acquired any subsequent legal that she settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge Information or belief that she hath two Children by her said Husband now living with her this Deponent namely James born on or about the 30th day of April 1790 and Frederick born on or about the 2d day of May 1792 And this Examinant upon her Oath further saith that she hath another Child now also living with her named Susanna Noyes  born in or about the Month of March 1781 by her Daughter by Samuel Noyes a former Husband to whom this Examinant was Married at
