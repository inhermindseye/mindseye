Middx to wit Francis Salmon Aged about fifty three years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his wife  Hannah at the Parish of St. George Hanover Square about Seventy four years ago and by whom he hath one Child living now provided for Says before his Intermarrd. to his said wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Ranies at Charlton in the Parish of Charlton in the County of Kent for one year and Ten Months at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds besides Meat Drink Lodging and Cloaths hath quitted his said service upwards of Twenty four years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement Other than Renting a Tenement of four Pounds a year in the Parish of Woolwich in the County of Kent rented the same about Nine years and paid Land Tax due for the same hath quitted the said House about Eighteen years ago since which Since he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise Howsoever

Sworn this 13th. day of October 1778 before  Jno. Barnfather  Wm. Kitchiner

his Francis Salmon Mark
