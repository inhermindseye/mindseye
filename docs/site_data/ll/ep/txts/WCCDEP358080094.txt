Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Brown aged upwards of 70 Years on her Oath says she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Messrs. Calverts Taylors in Howard Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 29 Years at the Yearly Wages of £4 that upon the Death of Mrs. Calvert she left this Examinant her house for the Remr. of the Lease then to come that she lived there in upwards of 10 Years and paid all parish Taxes due for the same after the Rate of £30 Pr. Ann. that she afterwards sold the same to one Mr. Pearson but for what Sum of Money she cannot setforth but says she had £800 in the 3 P Cent Consolidated Annuities which she has lived on for upwards of this 20 Years till the same was Exhausted to £50 and that she soldout the same at Lady Day last for about forty three pounds and that she has no Annuity or other Income whatever except what she has remaining of the sd. last mentd. £43 and that after quitting the above mentioned House she hath never gained any other subsequent Settlement either by renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. Day of Novr. 1776 R Butler

Mary Brown
