Middx  Thos. Huggins upon Oath saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Guest a Taylor in Angel Court & Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 5th. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

Tho. Huggins
