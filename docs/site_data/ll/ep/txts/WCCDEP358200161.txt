Middx  Sarah Day Aged about Fifty Six Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Chas. Day with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Bennetts Pauls Warf in the City of London about Thirty three Years ago Says that since her said Husbands Death being without Child or Children she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Pagan Shaw  Esqr . in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about Two Years & hath not since Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 16th of Novr 1764 Before} Benjamin Cox
