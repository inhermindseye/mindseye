Middx  The Examination of Barbara Greenhill lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 24th- Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that her Father Thos. Greenhill lived in and rented a House in the Butcher Row in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds for the Space of ten Years & upwards & there Dyed & this Examinant further on her Oath saith that she never rented ten Pounds a Year was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herself

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Bart: Greenhill
