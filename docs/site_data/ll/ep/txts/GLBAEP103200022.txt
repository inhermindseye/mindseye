Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bennett maketh oath that about seventeen years ago she was lawfully married to one Robert Bennett of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Lighterman That her said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with David Jones in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate  Lighterman & allways lodged during Such Apprenticeship in his Masters house in the Said Parish as she believes & this Depont has seen her sd husbands Indres That her sd husband to this Deponts Knowledge or belief after his Apprenticeship never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwds nor ever served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That upwards of Ten years ago her sd husband departed from her & went on board a Man of War & this Depont has never Seen him since Nor does She knew Whethr he is now dead or living That about five years ago a Letter was Sent by her said husband to this Deponts mothr Mrs Scott as her sd Mother has informed her & which She believes to be true that her sd husband was then living at New York in America That this Depont hath a Child named Sophia Rider which was-

To her Husbands Settlemt. by Apprenticeship

Husband left her 10 Year ago

Bastard Child born in Wapping

born of her body on the tenth day of June one thousand Seven hundred & eighty one in Wiltshire Lane in the Parish of St John Wapping in the sd County & that one John Rider now or late of the Same Parish Lighterman is the Father of the Said Child That the said Child being born as aforesaid a Bastard in the said Parish of St John Wapping & this Depont. being of inability to Nurse & Maintain the same She applied Sometime ago to the Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish of St John Wapping for relief for the sd Child who for sometime allowed this Depont a Weekly allowce for the Maintenance of the said Child & afterwards Caused a Warrant to be issued for Apprehending the Father of the said Child in order to be indemnified by him against Such Maintenance but what was the Consequence or effect of such Proceeding this Depont can't Particularly Set forth That this Depont has been lately removed by an order from the Said Parish of St John Wapping to the Sd Parish of St John Wapping Botolph without Aldgate as the Place of her last legal Settlement & her said Child has been sent with her for Nurture

Sworn this 9th day of July 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Bennett
