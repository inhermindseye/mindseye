Middx to wit Thomas Pierce Aged about Sixty Nine Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to  Mary his Wife at May Fair Chapel in the Year 1750 and by whom he hath one Child now living which is now Provided for says that before his Intermarriage to his said Wife And whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with his Grace the Duke of Montague in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent for one Year at the yearly Wages of Twenty one Pounds  quitted his said Service in the year 1750 and after quitting the same he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes on did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Novr. 1780 before R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

Thos Pierce
