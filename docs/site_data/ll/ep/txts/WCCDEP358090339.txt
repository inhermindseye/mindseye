Middx & Wetmr.} to Wit John Cooper aged about 39 Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Geo: the Martyr Queen Square about Nine Years ago and by whom he has Two Children now living Namely John aged about 12 Years born a Bastard in the Parish of Saint Giles and Saml . Aged about Seven Years Says his Father Wm. Cooper Served Seven Years Apprenticeship to One John Dorrill a Taylor in Feather Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . and after he was out of his Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlemt. as his Mother Ann Cooper has lately Inform him and with Information he verily believes to be And this Examinant further Says that he never gained anyother Settlemt. than as aforementioned having never Rented House or Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a Year service any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired yearly Servant before Marriage for One Year

Sworn this 4th. day of Novr. 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

John Cooper
