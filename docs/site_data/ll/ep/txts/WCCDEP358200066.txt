Westmr  John Peacock Aged about 74 Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Sarah in the Parish Church of St. Paul in Covent Garden in the County of Middx about Years ago Says he lived Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Thos. Bedwell in Clare Court in the parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr he being at that time a Single man and without Child or Children upwards of One Years at the Yearly Wages of hath quitted his said Service about Forty Years and hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the Day of Feby 1764 Before}

Upon Mr Ralph Williams Complt J. W
