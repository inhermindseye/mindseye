Westmr.  Richd. Garnett aged about 48 Years upon Oath Saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Henry Ordway  Vintner at the Five Bells Tavern in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 26th. Octr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Richd. Garnet
