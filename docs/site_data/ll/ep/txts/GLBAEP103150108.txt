Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Catlin the Widow of Edward Catlin deced maketh oath that divers Years ago her said late husband rented a house in Sun Yard in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the Same & also Served his Apprenticeship in the Said parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish That to this Deponent knowledge or belief her sd husband did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere & that She this Deponent Since his death has not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 12th day of Janry 1776 before Geo. C. Smith

thos Eliz Catlin
