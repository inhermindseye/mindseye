Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex )ss (to wit) Mary Rich Leech aged about 54 Years upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married that she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Fox in Vere Street Clare Market in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in in the County of Middlesex For three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £4 that she hath quitted said Service about 23 Years since which time she has not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for twelve Months or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of July 1790 R Butler

Mary Leech
