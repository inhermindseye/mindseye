Westmr. 

Sarah Bingham aged about 74 Years removed from the Parish of St. Andw. Holbn. Middx by Order dated the 23d. Instt. upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 30 Yrs ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Smith in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for Two Years and upwards Says she has not since she quitted the sd. Mr. Smiths Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 26th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sarah Bingham
