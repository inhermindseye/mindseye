Middx  Lucey Walters aged 65 Years Removed from the Parish of St. Margarett Westmr. by Order dated the 10th. Day of June last upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman & lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr Baxter in Devereux Count in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for Three Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds & hath left her sd. Service about 14 Years and since that time hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 26th. May 1744 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Lucey Walker
