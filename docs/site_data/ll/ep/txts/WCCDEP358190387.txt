Middx  Walter Joshua Jones Aged about 25 Years Removed from St. Margaret Westmr Upon Oath Says he was Bound out by the Parish of St Margarets Westmr. to David Wallis a Publican at the Sign of the Hole in the Wall in Vere Street Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. with whom he lived about three Months and then Went to Sea and he hath not Since been turned Over to any Person Rented House Paid Taxes or was a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year.

Sworn the Day of October 1763 Before}
