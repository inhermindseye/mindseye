Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Hill aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Rhodes in Clare Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Five years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish That one William Brooks an Attorney who lives at No. 6 Cliffords Inn is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Brooks is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 26th. day of April 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Catherine Hill Mark
