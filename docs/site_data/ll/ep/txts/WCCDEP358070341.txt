Middlesex to wit Mary Newally Aged about Twenty Two years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever served an Apprenticeship Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Chandler a Butterman in Clare Markett for One year land an half at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Eight Months ago since which time she hath done no Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the Said Parish She being poor and unable to maintain the same when been Says that one John Clarke  Servant to her said late Master did beget such Bastard Child on her Body he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of June last but the particular day she does now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Childen of which she is now pregnant And that the said John Clarke and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 12th. day of Feb. 1776 before R Butler

Mary Newally
