Middx & Westmr.} to wit Margt. Harwood Aged about 26 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she has lived a hired yearly Servt . to one Mrs. Griffiths a Widow Lady in Cecil Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 18 Months at the yearly Wages of Two Guineas and where she at present lives & hath gained no other Settlemt. than as afsd. And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born And further says that one Philip Warren  a Black & a Mariner who Uses one Mr Whitakers who keeps a Coal Shed the Corner of Windsor Court  near the New Church in the Strand is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at his the sd Philip Warren s Lodgings in Angel Court Strand some time in the Month of Octr. last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. difft. times afterwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children which she is now pregnant And that the said Philip Warren  and no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 25th. day of Feby. 1783 R Butler

her Mary Harwood Mark
