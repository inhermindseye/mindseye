Westmr ss  Ann Jones , Aged upwards of Sixty Six Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Daniel Jones who has been dead about Seven Years and by whom she has one Daughter named Phoebe who is provided for, Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Bishopgate Street within in the Parish of Saint Ethelburga in the City of London at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds a Year and paid all parish Taxes due for the same for upwards of Three Years, and further says that her said Husband died in the Workhouse of the said Parish of Saint Ethelburga , & that since her said Husbands Death she had never rented house paid Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or done any other Act whereby to gain any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th day of Augt 1775 before R Butler Sampn Wright

Ann Jones
