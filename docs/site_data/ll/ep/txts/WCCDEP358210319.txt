Westmr.  Ann Mandifield Aged about Forty two Years, upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Water house at the Bird in and Hand in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes , in the County of Middlesex for Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds, quitted said Service upwards of Twenty Years ago, and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 31st. day of August 1768 , before} 

The Mark of Ann Mandifield
