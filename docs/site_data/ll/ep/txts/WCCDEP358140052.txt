Middx.  John Kirkpatrick aged abt. 57 Years upon Oath says he served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years to Mr. Willm. Bridges a Carpenter opposite Salisbury Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . & never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or was a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 21st. day of Novr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

John Kirkpatrick
