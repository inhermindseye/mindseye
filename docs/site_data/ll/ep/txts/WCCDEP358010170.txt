Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Thomas Williams aged about 57 years upon his Oath saith That he Rented a Shop & Parlows of a Mr. Earle in New Round Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middx  for 4 Months & upwards at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Guineas that he hath quitted said Tent. about a Month since which time he has done no act to gain a Subsequent. Settlement That he has 2 Children living vizt. Thos. lived about 12 years and Selena aged about 5 years by this Exts. Wife vizt. Mary to whom he Intermarried with about 22 years ago at the Parish Church of Constantine in the County of Cornwall

Sworn this 18th. day of Decr. 1787  Sampr Wright Maddington

Thos. Williams
