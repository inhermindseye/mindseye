Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Swaine aged 23 years upon her Oath saith that on the 24th: day of November last past she Intermarried with Joseph Pester at the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry who at that time she thought was Single but since her said Marriage she this Deponent has learnt he had been legally married to a former wife named Ann Pester (and who is now actually living) at the Parish Church of Saint Ann's Aldersgate London on the 23d. December 1792 . That she this Examinant lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Carey the Optician in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Ten Years and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Ten Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith That that on the Third day of April now last past she this Examinant was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the London Lying in Hospital which hath since been Christened by the Name of Jemima That the said Joseph Pester who now lodges at Mr. Pye's in Saint Anns Lane Westmr . is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Joseph Pester and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 14th. day of Augt. 1795 .} E Read

her Jane Swaine Mark
