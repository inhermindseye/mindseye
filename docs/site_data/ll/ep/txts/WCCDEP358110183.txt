Middx  Bridget Ward upon Oath saith she never was married & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant that she is the Daughter of Hugh Ward decd. by Grace his Wife and was born in the Parish of Rahamley in the County of Slaghey in Ireland where her sd. late father who was an Irishman rented a house and lived therein a considerable time and that her sd Mother Grace Ward never gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants Knowledge

Sworn the 21st Febry. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Bridget Ward
