Middlesex to wit Dorothy Hall aged about forty years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Messrs. Belcher and Gray Cabinett makers in Brownlow Street in the Parish of Saint Andrews Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex upwards of One year and three Quarters at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Febry 1776 before  Tho Lane R Butler

her Dorothy Hall Mark
