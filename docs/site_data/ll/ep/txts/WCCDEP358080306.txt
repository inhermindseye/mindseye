Middx to wit Mary Wright age about 66 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried with her late Husband William Wright at the Parish Church of allhallows in Derby about Thirty Two Years ago & who has been Dead about 12 Years and by whom she hath no Children now living says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one John Cross a Shoe Maker in the aforesaid Parish of Allhallows in Derby and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 24th- day of October 1778 before R Butler John Croft

her Mary Wright Mark
