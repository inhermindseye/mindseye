Middlesex ss}  Isabella Bowman the widow of John Bowman deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her & Which She believes to be true that the was born at a place Called Bolton gate in the County of Cumberland where his Parents rented a Farm That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her Sd husband never lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in his own right Save What he might Acquire by Apprenticeship but the Person with whom he Served Such Apprenticeship this depont doth not know nor Can Set forth.

Sworn this. 8. day of December 1778 before}

The Mark of Isabella Bowman
