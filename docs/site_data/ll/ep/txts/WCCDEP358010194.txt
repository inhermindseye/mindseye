Saint Clement Danes

John Jeffreys Sealey aged 23 Years upon his Oath Saith that he rented a House and Shop in Leadenhall Markeh for 6 Months at the Yearly Rent of £40 a Year that since he quitted said House he has done no Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn before us this 9th. day of Feby. 1788  Thos. Gordon  Edw: Read

John Jefferys Sealey
