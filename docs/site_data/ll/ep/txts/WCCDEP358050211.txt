Westmr. 

Eliz Gillett on behalf of Bernard a Child aged about 15 Year upon Oath saith the said Bernard is the last Child of Ann Ayres deced; that she has been Informed and verily beleives her own father Jonas Stears was the reputed father of the said Bernard and that at the time of the Birth of the sd. male bastard Child this Examint. mother the abovesaid Jonas Stear's wife was living that she has been further Informed and beleives the said Bernard was born in the house of Mr Cook in Leaden hall Street in the Parish of St. Catherine Creechurch London

and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Janry 19th. 1749 before} F Fielding

The mark of Eliz Gillet
