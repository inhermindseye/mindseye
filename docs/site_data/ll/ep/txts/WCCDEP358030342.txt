Westmr. 

Sarah Saunders aged about 26 Years upon Oath Says she never was married Says her late Father John Saunders deceased lived in and rented a house the Sign of the Black Jack near Clare market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr.  of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds or thereabouts for several Years & there dyed Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a  Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says Wm. Lowder of Epsom  Apothecary had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of Jane last & at several other time since Says several other Persons have

since the said Month of June last had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body by reason of which she is incapable of giving an Accot. who is the father of the Child she is now pregnant with.

Sworn Febry 19th. 1746 before Wm: Jones

Sarah Saunders
