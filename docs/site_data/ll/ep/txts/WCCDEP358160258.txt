Middx  The Examination of Jno. Lewis living in ye. Parish of St. Clement Danes in ye. County of Middx Taken the 17th. Day of Febry 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that about 10 Years ago he lived with one Mrs. Francis in Clare Markett in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Fishmonger for the space of 3 Years at ye. Yearly Wages of £12, And this Examint. further on his Oath saith yt. after he left Mrs. Francis's service he went & lived with one Mr. Fortescue in the Parish of St. Giles in ye. Fields in ye. same County Fishmonger but did not at such his comeing make any agreemt. as to what Wages he shd. have or how long he shd. stay but left ye. same entirely to his sd. Master who paid him after ye. rate of £14 a Year Meat Drink Washing & Lodging & this Examint. continued to live with ye. sd. Mr. Fortescue on ye. sd. Condicons for one Year & upwards And hath not since yt. time Rented £10 a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for ye. space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworn ye. Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Cha: Egerton

John Lewis
