Middx  Mary Anderson aged 22 Yrs. removed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Libty of West by Order dated the 5th. Day of March Instant upon her Oath saith that she was never married & that her Father  Joseph Anderson was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Mr. Griffin in the Back of St. Clements in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Carpenter & never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person a Yearly hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as she hath been Informed & believes Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. or otherwise giv a Settlemt. to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 6th: March 1744/5 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Anderson
