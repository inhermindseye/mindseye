Middx  Jane Arnold Wife of Richard Arnold (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Richard Arnold at the Fleet London on the 15th. May 1744 by whom she hath two Childn. Richard aged near 4 Years & Peter aged abt. 8 months Says her sd. husbands father (as she hath been Informed & Believes rented a house in Ratcliff Street in the parish of St. Mary Redclift in Bristol & pd. Scot & Lot and that her sd. husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant whilst a Singleman for one Year rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr 6th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

Jean Aurnold

Jno. Perry the first Husband being at the Nags head in South Audley Street had another Wife being when he married the above Jane  Daur Mary born at Mr Broomhall facing  be Turnstile Holborn
