Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Layfield aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith That about 18 Months ago she intermarried with William her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn That her said Husband rented and lived in a House in Brownlow Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for three Months at the yearly rent of Seventy Pounds That he hath left the same about five months since which time he hath not rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or done any act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th day of Decr 1800 before us } Saml Edwards  Benjn Gee

Elizabeth Layfield
