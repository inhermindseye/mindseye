Middx.  Martha Stevenson aged abt. 43 yrs. upon Oath says she never was married says She lived a hired yearly Servant with Anthony Hoyle  Esqr . in Queen Square in the Parish of St. George the Martyr in the County of Middx. for upwds. of three years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abt. ten years since which time she hath Rented Ten Pounds a yr. paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant

Sworn the 14th. day of March 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

Martha Septhenson
