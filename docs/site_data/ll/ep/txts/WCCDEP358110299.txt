Westmr.  Wm. Baismore upon Oath Saith he intermarried wth his Wife Sarah Baismore at the Fleet London abt. 20 Yrs. ago & has by her one Child Jas . aged near 7 Yrs. that he Served 6 Yrs. & upwards of his apprentiship with Jno. Mince a Shoemaker at the corner of Carnaby Street  Golden Square in the Parish of St. Jas. Westmr . and then his master dying this Examint. continued to Serve out the remr. of his Apprentiship wth. Mr Mince's Wido. in the same house & that he never rented ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Augt. 21st 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Wm. Baismore
