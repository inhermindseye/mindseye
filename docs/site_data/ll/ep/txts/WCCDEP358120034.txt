Middx  John Johnson removed from the parish of St Luke in the County of Middlesex by Order dated the 1st. Day of Jannry 1754 upon Oath saith he rented a house of the yearly Rent of £12 in Kings head Court in the parish of St Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex lived therein about 12 years & hath quitted the same about twenty four years and has not Since rented the Pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt for one Year

Sworn 3d. Jany. 1754 before}
