Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Margaret Green aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Ashe in Arundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Nine Guineas That she hath quitted said service about Four Years since which time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Decr. 1797 before me} J Hunter

her Margaret Green Mark

Witness B Newman
