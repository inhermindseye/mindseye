Middx  Ann Henley  Wido : aged 59 Yrs. upon Oath saith her late Husband John Henley hath been dead abt. 19 Yrs Says she lived as Yearly hired Servant with Mr Wharf in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £4 for one Year & upwards & hath left her said Service about two Years & one Qur. Says She hath not since She left the said Mr Wharfs Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 25th 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Ann Henley
