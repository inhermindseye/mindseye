Middlesex ss}  Sarah Blake  singlewoman maketh oath That she this Deponent hath three children to Wit Thomas Bowyer aged Seven years and half who was born in Farthing Alley in Barnaby Street in the Parish of Saint Olave in Southwarke in the County of Surry & hath done no Act to gain a Settlement in his own right William Bowyer Aged four years & seven Months who was born in Hatchett alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and Joseph Bowyer Aged one year & three Quarters of a year who was born in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St John Wapping in the Same County That Thomas Bowyer now or late of the Parish Of St Mary Whitechapel in the Said County of Middlesex  Waterman did get her With Child of the Said three Children all of whom are illegitimate & Bastards by reason that she this Deponent was never married That she this Deponent Some years ago was hired by & lived as a hired servant by the year at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds with one Mrs Barns in the Parish of Saint John in Southwarke in the Said County of Surry & Continued there in such service for the space of three years & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there and that she this Deponent has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 5th. day of July 1773 before  Burfd. Camper  Jno Sherwood

Sarah Blake Her Mark

4 years 7 Months Willm Bowyer
