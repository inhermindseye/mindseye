Middx & Westmr-}  Ann Bullen late of Chicester in the County of Sussex Singlewoman maketh Oath & saith that she on the 8th- Day of June last was brought a bed of a Female Bastard Child at the house of Wm- Mr- Wills a Taylor in Clements Lane in the Psh of St- Clemt- Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - & Co- of Middx which Child was begotten on her Body by Thos- Mann late of Chicester afsd- Breeches Maker & now living at Mr- Wills as afsd- who had carnal Knowl of her Body first at Southampton & aftwds- sevl- times at Chicester afsd- from whence they came to the said Mr. Wills and this Dept- further saith that no other pson but the sd- Thos- Mann had carnal Knowl of her Body & that the sd- Thomas Mann is the Father of the sd- Child of which she was so Delivd- as afsd- And this Dept- furr- saith that the said Child is likely to become chargeable to the sd- Parish of St- Clemt- Danes the said Thos- Mann threatning to leave the said Ann Bullen her and her said Child to the Care of the Pshs

Sworn this 15th- Day of July 1777 before. G Mercer

Ann Bullen
