Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. in St. Barthw . the less  to wit Mary Northfield aged about 18 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Soley a Hatter and Hosier in the Little Cloysters near to Bartholomews Hospital in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the less in the City of London for Sixteen Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Three Pounds That she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year Rented any house Tenemt or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1794 before N Bond E Read

her Mary Northfield mark
