Middx & Westmr.} to wit Rachael Pounds Aged about 20 Years on her Oath says she never was married says she now lives a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. John Bridges a Butcher in Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and has so done for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of 5 Pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being, Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Edward Barras a Journeman Butler who lives with one Mr. Chamberlaine a Butler in Clare Markett afsd. had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of May last Past and at Several Different times Afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid and that the said Edward Barras and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn this 29th day of December 1769 before me}

Rachel Pounds
