Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Richard Dobbins One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Roberts aged about Four years and Benjamin Roberts aged about Two years Two illegitimate Children Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Mary Roberts was born in Banner Street in the Parish of Saint Luke in the County of Middlesex and Benjamin Roberts born in Middle Street Cloth Fair in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great in the City of London

Sworn this 21st. day of Septr. 1795  N Bond E Read

Richd Dobbins
