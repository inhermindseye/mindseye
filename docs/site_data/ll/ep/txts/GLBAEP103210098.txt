Middlesex ss}  Alice Lewis the widow of George Lewis deced maketh oath that her said late husband heretofore rented a house of the yearly rent of eight pounds in the Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & during such his residence therein he was charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same That he did not after rent a house of the yearly rent or value of Ten pounds or serve any parish office or pay any Poor rates or Kings taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That she was lawfully married to her said husband & since his death hath not been married again or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 8. day of Septemr 1785 before R Davies

The mark of Alice Lewis
