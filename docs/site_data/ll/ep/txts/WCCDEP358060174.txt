Westmr.  Ann Row aged upwds. of 13 Years upon Oath saith her father John Row deceased Served the last part of his Aprentiship with his Uncle Row a Peamaker in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex as this Examinants mother Sarah Row informed her and which Information this Examint. believes to be true

Sworn Septr. 24th. 1751 before}

The mark of Ann Row
