Middlesex ss}  Thomas Morris maketh oath that his late father Philip Morris deced has informed this Depont & which he believes to be true that the Place of the last legal Settlement of this Deponents sd father previous to his death was in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County and from Whence this Deponent in Consequence thereof & as deriving a Settlement there from his sd father was bound out Apprentice by the then Churchwardens & overseers of the Poor of the Same Parish to one John Towigg a Cook belonging to one of his Majesties Ships of War but this Deponent Saith that during Such his Apprenticeship he never lodged in any one Place at Land for the Space of forty days so as to gain a settlement under such Indre of Apprenticship That he this Deponent never lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in his own right to his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 28 day of May 1784 before R Davies

Thomas Morris
