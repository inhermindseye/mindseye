St. Clement Danes

Middx-(ss)  Martha Lead beater Aged 33 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that James Thornton her Son a Poor Boy of the Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Age of 12 years & upwards (having been Bound an Apprentice to William Houston of St Catherines Street in the Precinct of St Catherine near the Tower of London  Apothecary on the 24th. day of January last he duly served the said William Houston his said Master till the 19th. day of August last when his said Master departed this Life and left one Mr Houston his Wife with when the said James Thornton Lived till Monday the 25th day of this Instant September when his said Master Wife having sold all the Goods left the said James Thornton together with  one George Woolley another Boy in the House-having  before the went given the Indenture of the said James Thornton to the said George Woolley who

Finding that the said her Mrs Hourton his late Master's Wife did not return Gought him horn to this Examinant And this Examinant saith that the said James Thornton hath never been Assigned over to any person whatsoever whereby he might have gained any other Settlement since his late Masters Dea and that She this Examinant in Poor and unable to Maintain the said James Thornton her said Son

Sworn this 28th. day of September 1786 }
