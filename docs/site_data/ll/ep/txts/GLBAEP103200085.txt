Middlesex ss}  Rebecca Warner maketh oath that She lately rented a house of the yearly Rent of Twenty fourth Pounds in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same That She has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 20th. day of Decemr 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Rebecca Warner
