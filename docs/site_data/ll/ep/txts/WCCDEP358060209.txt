Middx  Jane Gillison aged about 27 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she never was married and is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx (in which parish she now, Lodges)she being Poor and unable to maintain the Same when born and that Mr. Richard Fleming of Clements Inn in the sd. Parish Gent did begot the sd. bastard Child or Children on her body the said Richard Fleming having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant at his Chambers in Clements Inn aforesd. in the month of May last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Richd. Fleming & no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Novr. 18th 1751 before  Tho Lane

Jane Gillison
