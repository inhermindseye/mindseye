Middx  Amey Frasier Upon Oath Says that She hath by Enquiry been informed & wch. information this Examinant believes to be true that Jno: Fungstone the father of Wm. Fungstone Aged abt. two Years & Jno: Fungstone  Aged abt. two Months lived in & Rented a House  near the Haymarkett in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & Upwards & lived in the same upwards of one Year

Sworn the 9th. day of Decr. 1762 before} Paul Vaillant  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Amey Frazier
