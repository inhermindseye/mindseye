Middx  Wm. Grasby upon Oath Saith he intermarried with his Wife Esther Grasby in the Parish Church of St. Andw Holbn . London about 15 years ago by whom He hath three Children (viz) Wm . aged near 13 Yrs. Jno . aged near 9 Yrs. & Sarah aged near 5 Yrs. and that he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Fras Nedham  Butcher in Gilberts Passage Claremarket in the parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middx & never rented a house of ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn Augt. 20th 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Willm Grasby
