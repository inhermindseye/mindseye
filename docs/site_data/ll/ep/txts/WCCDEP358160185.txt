Middx  The Examination of John Curtis lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the seventeenth Day of Octr. 1740 who upon his Oath saith that he was bound Apprentice to and service seven Years Apprenticeship with his Brother Nathanl Curtis in Baldwins Gardens in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the sd. County of Middx  Plaisterer and hath been out of his Time abt. Eighteen Years and further Saith that he never rented a House lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servant for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or Beleif that he has a Wife and one Child (to wit) John aged five Years and upwards and being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Wm Hayton  Jas: Gronous

John Curtis
