Middx.  Eliz: Weston aged abt. 55 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Chas. Weston who has been dead about fifteen Years; says her said late husband served five Years the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Hook Carpenter in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and never in his life time nor she this Examinant since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn this 21st. March 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eliz: Weston
