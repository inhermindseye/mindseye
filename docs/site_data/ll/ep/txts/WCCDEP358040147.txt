Westmr.  Eliza: James  Widow of James James upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said James James at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes on the 2d. Day of Augt. 1719 Says her said late husband served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Walker in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented a house of Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 6th. Feby. 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

Elizabeth James
