Middx  Eliz: Battle upon Oath saith she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant nor ever rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes and that her late father Jams. Battle deceased rented a house in Heathcock Court in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Libty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and lived therein several months & never afterwards rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn July 28th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Battle
