Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Edward Winter aged about 22 years upon his Oath saith That he was born at No. 3 in Market Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex where his father and Mother kept house for several years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and paid all Taxes due for the same and this Deponent further upon his Oath saith that he never was bound an Apprentice or lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months

Sworn this 24th. day of April 1797 before us M Addington J Robinson

Edward Winter
