Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster}  to wit Charles Tool aged about 54 years upon his Oath saith that in the Month of June 1779 he was married to Mary his present wife by a Dernish Priest (in a private Room at Ireland )  according to the Rites & Coremonies of the Roman Catholic Religion that in May 1780 the said Mary was delivered of a Male Bastard Child name John at the House of a Mr. Jones in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx that he this Ext. in Augt. 1783 was married to the said mary by a License in the Parish Church of St. Paul Covent Garden & that on the 29th. day of Janry. 1786 the said Wife was deced of a Boy which hath since been Christened by the Name of Chas . That this Ext. Rented and lived in a House in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 3 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £20 that he hath quitted said House about 3 Months since which time he has not Rented any House Tenemt. or Lodging of the yearly Rent of £10 for 40 days paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement 

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1787 }
