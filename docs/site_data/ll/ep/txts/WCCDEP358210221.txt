Westmr.  Mary Crossfield aged abt. 19 Years Upon Oath says she never was married and that she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Michael Crooked Lane London to her Uncle Thomas Crossfield a Turner in Rent Street in the Parish of St. George Southwark in the County of Surry and Served about three Years of her said Apprenticeship and then run away from her said Master, and never was Assigned over to any person, or Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 24th, December 1767 , before } John Machin

The Mark of Mary Crossfield
