Middlesex ss}  Phillis Hary Gold Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year by & with Mr Henry Suffery  Surgeon in Bishops gate Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Bishopsgate London & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain anothr Settlement That She hath two Children namely Thomas Fleming aged about Thirteen year & four months & Robert Fleming Aged about Four years & Ten Months both of whom was born of this Deponts body in the Parish of St John Wapping in the sd County That they are illegitimate & Bastards by reason that She this Deponent was never married &neithr of which Children have done any Act to gain a Settlemt for themselves

Sworn this 20 day of February 1778 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Phillis Harygold
