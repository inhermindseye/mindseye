Middx ss and Westmr.}  Margt. Alnutt aged abt. 21 years lodging at Mrs. Leonards in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Country of Middx and Liberty of Westmr. Upon Oath says she never was married and that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Haslehurst at the White hart in Clements Lane in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £4 and hath Quitted said Service Upwards of One Year and hath not Since Rented Ten pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she was delivered of a female Bastard Child on the Seventh day of February last (which has since been baptized by the name of Ann ) in the house of Mrs. Leonard above named in the said parish of St. Clement Danes Says that one Thomas Wilkinson a Slaughter man who works at the Bull head Slaughter House in Clare market did beget the said female Bastard Child on her body says the said Thos. Wilkinson had Carnal knowledge of her Body several times at the time she lived a Servant with the said Mr. Harslehurst at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Thos. Wilkinson and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Augst. 28th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Margt. Alnutt
