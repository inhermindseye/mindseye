Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Taylor aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Fifteenth day of December now last past She the said was delivered of a Female Male Bastard Child at the Workhouse in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes was delivered of Male Bastard Child which hath since been Christened by the Name of William That one William Raper who did Work with Wm. Clements at Isleworth is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said William Raper is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 27th. day of Febry. 1798 J Hunter

her Ann Taylor Mark

Witness B Newman
