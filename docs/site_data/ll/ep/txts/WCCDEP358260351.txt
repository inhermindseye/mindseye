Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Verrall aged about 78 years upon her Oath saith that about 26 years ago she Intermarried with William Verrall her late Husband who has been dead about Seven years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Beaufort Buildings for upwards of Thirty years at the yearly Rent of Fifteen pounds that her said late Husband died in the said house That after her said late Husbands decease she this Deponent never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Octr. 1798 before me } John Collick

her Elizabeth Verrall Mark

Witness AW Burbidge Beadle
