Middx The Examination of Mary the Wife of Jno. Hays Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 2d. Day of March 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that She and her Husband are both Irish Native of Ireland & never gained any Settlement in England And further on her Oath saith that she hath two Children (to wit) Mary aged two Years & upwds. & Catherine aged one Year both born in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes that her sd. Husband absconds from her & She being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe and them

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
