Middx  The Examination of Susanah Ormston  Wife of Robt. Ormston Passed from the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . by Order bearing Date the 27th. Day of June last Taken the 11th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her Husband Rt. Ormston Rented & lived in a House in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £12 for the space of 3 Years & upwds. And this Examinant further saith yt. her sd. Husband hath not since he left the sd. House Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife, That her said Husband now absconds from her & haveing two small Childn. (to wit) Sarah aged abt. 3 Years and Jane aged One Year & upwds. & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Childn.

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before}

Susanna Ormston
