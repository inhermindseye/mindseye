Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Armmur of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in And for the Said County this twenty ninth day of Ma one thousand Seven hundred and eighty Six Who Saith that on the twenty Seventh day of April last past at the Workhouse belonging to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County aforesaid She his Said Ann Armmur was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since baptized & registred by the name of Ruth Armmur And that the Said bastard Child is Since become Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that George Quested late of Crown Court in the Said Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate  Mariner did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the day & year above written before me  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Ann Armmur
