Middlesex ss}  John Throssell maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house in Sweedland Court in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and during Such his residence therein he was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house or Lodging of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Sarah and three Children namely Sarah aged Six Years John Aged about five Years & Catherine aged about Sixteen Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Sd Wife to Whom he was lawfully Married

Sworn this 23 day of January 1788 before R Davies

John Throssell
