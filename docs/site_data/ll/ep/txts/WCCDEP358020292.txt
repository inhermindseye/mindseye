St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Ann Green Aged thirty two years and upwards Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly at Her Majesty's at Buckingham House for one year and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she was hired by & served as Cook to Mrs Stainforth the then Housekeeper there that during all the time she lived in that that Service she Slept in that a Room in that part of Buckingham House which lies is in the parish of St George the Martyr Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex that she left her Maj the Service at Buckingham House befor somtime in the year 1780 and since hath not acquired any legal Settlement whatever And this Examinant further saith that her Male Bastard Child Christened John now of the Ag now of the Age of two twelve Weeks or thereabouts was born of her Body a Bastard on the 8th day of March last at Mr Burton's No 3 in Castle Street Bloomsbury in the parish of St George the Martyr Bloomsbury in the said County and is not yet weaned And this Examinant saith she is Poor and wholly unable to Maintain herself and Child

Sworn the 1st June 1791 before us Sampn Wright N Bond

Ann Green
