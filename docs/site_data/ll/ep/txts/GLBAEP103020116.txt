Middlesex ss.  Eleanor Roach maketh Oath that about Four Years ago a Roman Catholick Priest do at the Chapel in Moorfields read over to one Peter Higgins and this Deponent a Marriage Ceremony or pretended Solemnization of a Marriage between them without any preview Publication of Banns in any Church or Chapel or any License or Dispensation for that purpose first had and obtained from any Person or Persons whomsoever to this Deponents knowledge or Belief and that in Consequence thereof the said Peter Higgins & she did from thenceforth Cohabit together as if they well Man & Wife and had been lawfully Married And this Deponent further saith that she was born in the Parish of Upper Shannon in the Kingdom of Ireland , that she has been in England about Six Years & never did any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement in England that she has a Child named Johanna aged about One Year begotten on her Body by the said Peter Higgins & born in the Dwelling House of Mr. Higgins in Plough Street in the Parish of St. Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex which said Child is Illegetimate & a Bustard by reason that she never was Married to the said Peter Higgins (except as aforesd.) And that the said Peter Higgins is now on Board the Jack Tar Frigate in the Service of his Majesty

Sworn this Day of Sepr. 1794 before}
