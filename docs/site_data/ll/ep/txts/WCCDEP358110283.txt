Westmr.  Eliz Ryder upon Oath Saith she intermarried with Prissick Ryder a Printer upwds. of 23 Yrs. ago at a Church in the Old Change London; that the sd. Prissick Ryder absconds from & she knows not whether he is living or dead and that about 17 Years ago her sd. husband rented a house of thirteen pounds by the Year near the Corner of Clare Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . lived therein about two Years & has not since he quitted the sd house (to this Examints. knowledge) rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn Augt. 6th 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliza Rider
