Middlesex (ss)  Jonathan Rowell Maketh Oath and Saith that in the Year 1781 he was lawfully Married to his Present Wife  Margaret at the parish Church of Thurligh in the County of Bedford , that there are  Issue of the said Marriage One Son & One Daughter namely Thomas Aged 4 Years and Ann Aged about eighteen Months both of whom were Born in the sd. Parish of Thurligh in the County of Bedford aforesaid, That about Seven Years ago he Rented a House and Fann in the Parish of Thurligh in the County of Bedford aforesaid of the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds and continued to Occupy the same for the space of six Months for which he paid all Parochial Taxes, And this Examinant Saith that he has not at any time since his Intermarriage with his sd. Present Wife, Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards paid any Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes, Served any Parish Office or done any other Act whatsoever whereby to gain a Settlement in any other Parish Precinct or Place

Sworn the day of 1788 Before us}
