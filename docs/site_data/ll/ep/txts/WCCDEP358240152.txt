St. Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster }  St. Sepulchre Middx  (to wit) Jane East Aged 32 year or thereabouts upon Wife of Samuel East Maketh Oath and saith that she was Married to her present Husband Samuel East at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about on or about the 27th day of May last that her said Husband hath informed her & she Verily believes that previous thereto her said Husband Rented and Resided in an Apartment at Mr. Rooth's House in Charter house Lane in the parish of St. Sepulchre in the County of Middlesex for about four years at the Annual Rent of £12 that he quitted it about 14 Months ago and hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid any parish Taxes served any parochial office or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge Information or belief That her said Husband is now confined in the New Compter for Bastardy Chid that she is Distressed and and unable to support herself without Assistance

Sworn the 6th day of Septr. 1792 before us } Tho: Newplan  Gd: Mitchell

Jane East
