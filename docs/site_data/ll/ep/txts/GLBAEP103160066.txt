Middlesex ss}  Jonas Woodhead maketh oath That divers years ago he rented a house of Ten Pounds a year & upwards in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or Upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act Whereby to gain a subsequent settlement to this Deponents knowledge or belief and Whereas it has been objected to this Deponent that he had here to force gained a Settlement in the Parish of St. Mary White Chapel this Depont. on his oath Saith that he never lived in the said Parish of Whitechapel nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement there

Sworn this 13. day of May 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

Jonas Woodhead
