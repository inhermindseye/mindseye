Westmr. to wit Margaret Harrison Aged about fifty Three Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with John Parry who formerly Kept the Sign of the Crown and Taylors Arms in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Ten Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 12th. day of Decr. 1770 before me} R. Butler

her Margt. Harrison mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
