Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Richard Dobbins One of the Beadles  of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Ann George Coleman aged about Six years and John Wm. Coleman aged about Three years Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that George Coleman was born a Bastard at the house of a Mr. Butler in Boziers Court Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields Mary le bone and John Wm. Coleman born at the house of a Mr. Blunk opposite Saint Giles's Church in the said Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex both mentioned in the precceding Exaination of George Rochford

Sworn this 15th. day of Septr. 1790  Sampn Wright N Bond

Richd Robbins
