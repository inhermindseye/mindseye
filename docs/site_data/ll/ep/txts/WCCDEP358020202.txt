St: Clement Danes. 29 April 1790

Mary Best aged 20 Years or thereabouts and Elizabeth Brownley aged 44 or thereabouts upon their Oaths Say And pish this deponent Elizabeth Bronley upon her Oath Saith That her Father Richard Best & Mother Elizabeth Best were Married at Birmingham in the County of Warrick that the said Richard Best kept a House in Colman Street and in Mount Pleasent both in the parish of Old Church in the Town of Birmingham And that he paid upwards of £12 a Year for the one in Colman Street Birmingham aforesaid And that she doth not know of the said Richard Best ever gained any Settlement after he left Birmingham aforesaid or that the said Elizth. Best after his decease ever gained any Settlement till her Intermarriage with James her second Husband And that this Examinent Mary Best for herself saith that she is the Daughter of the said Elizabeth and Richard and Elizth. Best and that she hath not to the best of her knowledge done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement in her own Right to the best of her knowledge and belief

Sworn this 29th day of Apl. 1790 D Walker R Butler

Eliza Bromley Mary Best
