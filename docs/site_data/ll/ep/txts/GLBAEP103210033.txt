Middlesex ss}  Ann Hall the widow of James Hall deced maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year (She being then the widow of the Sd James Hall & having no Child or Children then living) by & with Mr Thomas Smith  Tobacconist in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & Continued in such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of one year & three Quartrs & has not since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or been married Since her Said late husbands death or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30 day of Novemr 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Hall
