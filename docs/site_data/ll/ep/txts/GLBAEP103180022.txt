Middlesex ss}  Susannah Headleott and Sarah Jenkins the widow of Robert Jenkins deced Severally make oath and first the said Susannah Headleott for herself Saith that She well knows the other Depont. Sarah Jenkins that She is the Danr of one Robert Pilkinton deced by Mary his wife also deced That many years ago they removed with their Danr the Sd Depont Sarah Jenkins from the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County to Sugar Leave Court in Woolpack Alley Hounsditch in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London Where they rented a house for Several years at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds as they informed this Deponent & which this Depont. Believes to be true & this Depont. is the rather induced to believe Such information to be true by reason Such house was a large double Room y house & was in this Deponts Indgmt worth to be set for atleast that Sum & in which house the Sd Robert Pilkington & his wife died and the Said Sarah Jenkins for herself Saith that She was lawfully Married to her late husband Robert Jenkins deced by whom She hath there Children now living Namely Mary Aged about fourteen years Frances Aged about Eleven Years and Margaret Aged about eight Months were of whom have done any Act to gain a Settlemt for themselves That this Dept Cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late husband or of his Parents or either of them And that She this Depont. Since her late husbands death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 28 of February 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Susannah Headleott Sarah Jenkings
