Westmr.  George Croft Aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath Says he was bound an Apperntice for Seven Years to Thos. Horn of the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx Says by & with the Consent of his sd. Master he Served Eight Months & upwards the last part of his sd. Apprentishipp with Mr. Thos. Crutwell periwig maker in the parish of Hamstead in the sd. County of Middx & never Rented House pd. Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 20th. of Octr. 1766 before R. Kilsha Barth: Hammond

Geo Cross
