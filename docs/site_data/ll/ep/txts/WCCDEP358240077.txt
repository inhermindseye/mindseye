St Clement Danes March 30th. 1792

Middx & Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Ann Newman aged Sixty two years and upwards Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Marrd. That she lived as an hired yearly servant, with Maskew at a House then known by the Sign of the Sun, which is Situates and Clare Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster , for three years & an half or thereabouts, at the Annual Wages of £7., that she left that Service about 30 Years ago, and hath not since lived as an hired yly Servant for a twelve month under one hiring, rented any House or Apartment of £10 Per Annum, paid parochial Taxes, or otherwise obtained any other legal Settlement as she best knows and believes

Sworn the 30th. day of March 1792 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Ann Newman 's Mark.

Witness Brewman
