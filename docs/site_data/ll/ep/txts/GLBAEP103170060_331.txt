Middlesex ss}  Ann Mabrick the wife of John Mabrick maketh oath That she well knows a Child named James Gardener aged about nine years & who as She this Deponent has been informed & Verily believes was born in Peters court in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & who has not as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right & She this Deponent cannot give ann Account respecting the Settlement of the said childs Parents or eith of them nor Can She give any Account respecting the Settlemt. of his Grand Parents or any or either of them

Sworn this. 6. day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Ann Mabrick
