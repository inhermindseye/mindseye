Middlesex & Westminster}  Passd. to St. George Hanover Square  Lydia Woodrow aged about Thirty Seven years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Edward Richardson of Chelsea in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for 12 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted said Service about five years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 27th day of June 1785 } Addington J Reading

Lydia Woodrow .
