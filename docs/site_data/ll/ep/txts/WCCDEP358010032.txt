St. Clement Danes. Decr. 12 1786.

Sarah Ashborn  Wife of William Ashborn Maketh Cath and Saith that she Intermarried with thin at the Parish Church of Aldgate dear White Chapple about 22 yrs ago. That She has Three Children now living by her Husband Vizt. Margaret who is bound out  Thomas who is bound out and James  who is Now in the Workhouse of Saint Clement Danes . That h has desertd. her and her Family and She is Poor and unable to Maintain her said Sons  James

Sworn this 12th. Decr.1786 .  Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Ashborn
