Middlesex ss}  Isabel Warren maketh oath That she Cannot in any Wife set forth or discover Where her late Parents or either of them was or were settled at the times of their respective Deaths Nor Where her late husbands Richard Skeed and Rupert Warren deced or either of them was or Were settled at the times of their respective Deaths But this Deponent Saith that she was born in White hart Yard in the Parish of saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and that she hath never lived as a hired servant for a year nor been put apprentice Nor to her Knowledge or belief hath she done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right save What she might Acquire by her respective Marriages.

Sworn this day of October 1769 before}

The Mark of Isabel Warren
