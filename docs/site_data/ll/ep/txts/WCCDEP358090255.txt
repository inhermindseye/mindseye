Middlesex  Elizabeth Edwards Aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says She Lived a Hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Richards at Mr. Hopwoods in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds Hath quitted her said Service about Six years ago since which time She hath gained neither Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 17th. day of August last She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes which  since been Baptized by the Name of John Edwards and is now Chargeable to the said Parish She being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same And further says that One James otherwise Robert Gardner at the Sign of the George iiv Broud Saint Giles is the Father thereof He having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his the said Robert Gardner s House at Different times for near Two years last past at One of which He got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which She was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Robert Gardner and no other person did beget the same and that He is the Read and true Father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day Sepr. 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth. Edwards Mark
