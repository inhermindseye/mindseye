Middx  Mary Evans aged abt. 48 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Twining in Devereux Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of £7 for one Year & upwards quitted her sd. Service about Eight Years ago & has not since rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Augt. 2d. 1754 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Evans
