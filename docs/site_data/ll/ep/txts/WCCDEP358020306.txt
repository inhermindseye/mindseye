St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Hannah White head Aged 50 years & upwards Upon her Oath saith that abt 31 Years ago she Intermarried with her present Husband John Whitehead at the parish Church of St Luke Old Street by Bauns And on (behalf of her Son John White head born of her Body in lawful Matrimony and now In same) also Maketh Oath and saith that he was bound by Indres of Apprenticeship to Mr Keeley in Grange Court in the said parish of St Clement Danes with whom her said Son served abt. three years & an half & that then he & his said Master parted by Mutual Consent & the Indres (whereby he was bound were destroyed Cancelled or are Missen, that he hath never (by an means whatever) has this Deponent hath been Informed & believes Acquired any Subsequent legal Settlement and that he is unfit to support himself 

Sworn the 28th. June 1791 before me R Butler

Hannah Whitehead 's Mark
