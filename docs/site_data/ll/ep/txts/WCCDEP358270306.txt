Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Wells aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Newman in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Ten Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about 18 Months since which time she hath done no Act whereby she might have gained a subseqt. Settlemt. And this Deponentfurther upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one William Harrison who lives Servant with the Reverend Mr. Ven at Clapham in the County of Surry is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Harrison is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of May 1797 . before me } J Hunter

Sarah Wells
