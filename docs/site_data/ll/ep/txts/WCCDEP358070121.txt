Westmr to wit}  Golossa Gough aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says she was bound an apprentice by the Governors of the Foundling Hospital to one Mrs. Price a Black Pudden maker in the Parish of St. James's Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex & served her upwards of One Year & an half and was afterward turned over to one Job seal a Baker in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and Served him for about Seven Years and then left her said Master since which time she never lived a hired Yearly Se for one Year or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement and further Says that on the 21st. day of July last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to this Parish which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Elizabeth and that One William Westbrook a Soldier belonging to the Coldstream Regiment now quarter in the Tower is the Father thereof he having har carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of September last in her late Masters House and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afore and that the said William Westbrook and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 7th day of Augt. 1773 before R. Butler

her Golossa Gough Mark
