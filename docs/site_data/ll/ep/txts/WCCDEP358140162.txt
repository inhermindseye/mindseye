Middx.  Judith Ray aged abt. 40 Years upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Shields a Perukemaker in Clements Inn in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings for one Year & upwds. and hath Quitted said Service abt. Twenty Years since which time she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 4th. day of Octr. 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Judith Ray
