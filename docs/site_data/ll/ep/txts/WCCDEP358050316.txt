Middx  Sarah Brookes aged about 40 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Dr. Green of Beaufort Buildings in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £6 for Seven Years or thereabouts and left him about 10 Yrs. ago since which she has not rented Ten pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 14th. 1750 before}

Sarah Brookes
