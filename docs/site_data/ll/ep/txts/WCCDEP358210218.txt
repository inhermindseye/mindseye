Westmr.  Elizabeth Trinner Aged about Twenty nine Years on behalf of Andrew Breman an Infant aged about Seven Years Son of Andrew Breman by Ann his Wife both deceased, Upon Oath says the said Andrew Breman the Father of the Said Infant was a Dutch man and never gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or belief, says the said Andrew Breman the Younger was born in the house of Mr. Goad a Carpenter in Princes Street Plymouth Dock in the Town of Plymouth in the County of Devon

Sworn the 19th. day of December 1767 before W Kelynge Geo: Reid

The Mark of Elizth. Trinner
