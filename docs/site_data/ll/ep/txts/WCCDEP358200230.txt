Middx  Izabella Drisdell Aged about Fifty Six Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Robert Drisdell with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about Thirty five Years ago Says that Since her said Husbands Death she being at that time a Widow & without Child or Children she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. George Carne a Peice Broker in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. two Years & a half at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds and never after Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the Day of 1765 Before}

The Mark of Izabella Drisdell
