Middx  Eliz: Bock aged 55 Years Wido . of Mark Bock upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Mark Bock in Lincolns Jan Chapell upwards of twenty Years since Says her sd. husband has been dead about Three Years Says he lived in & rented a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £24 for the Space of 4 Years or thereabouts & hath left same upwards of 16 Years Says her sd. late husbd. never after he left sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemts. in any other Place Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 21st 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Eliz Bock
