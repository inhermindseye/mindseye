Middx to wit John Clarke Aged about Forty Eight years on his Oath says He Intermarried to His Wife  Hannah at the Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea about Nineteen years ago and by whom He hath no Children living says He Rented and lived in a House in Cloth Fair Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great in the City of London for One year and Upwards at the yearly Rent of sixteen Pounds and paid Parish Taxes and for the same Hath quitted the said House about six years ago since which time he hath gained no other subsequent settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 30th day of July 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner  Thos Bishop

John Clark
