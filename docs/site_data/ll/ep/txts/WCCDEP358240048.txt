Saint Clement Danes

(St CD) (to wit) Elizth. Rapheld aged upwards of 38 Years Upon her Oath Saith that about the 18th. of Sepr. 1775 she was Married to John Rapheld at the parrish Church of Saint James in the liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex by Bans that her said Husband has been Dead about 7 Years that this she this Deponent has been informed by her said Husband that he served his Apprenticeship to John Davis of Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Hair Dresser That her said Husband during his intermarriage with this Deponent never Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year or did any other Act whereby he might have gained any Subsequent Settlement And that she this Deponent since her Husbands Death has never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year or done any other Act Matter a thing whereby to the best of her knowledge information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And that she is Poor and Unable to Maintain herself.

Sworn the 5th. day of Octr. 1791 before us} R Butler

Eliz. Rapheld
