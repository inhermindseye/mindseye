Westr to wit Sarah Bylin otherwise Byling Aged upwards of Forty One Years on her Oath says that she is the Wife of John Byling otherwise Bylin (gone from her) with whom she intermarried as the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about Sixteen Years Ago and by whom she hath no Children now alive, Says her said Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Wyatt a Copper plate printers in Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes lived hired Yearly Servant for one Year or any otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 18th day of Augt 1775 before M Addington R Butler

her Sarah Byling Mark Witness James Viger
