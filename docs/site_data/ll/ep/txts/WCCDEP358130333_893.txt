Middx.  Mary Bayne aged 24 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Wm. Bayne (now at Sea) says her sd. husbd. was Born in the Parish of in Scotland & never Rented ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes was an apprentice or yrly. Servt. to this Examinants knowledge or belief
