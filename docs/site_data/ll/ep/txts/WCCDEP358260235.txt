Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Day is aged about 53 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Brown in St. Martins Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Seven years at the yearly wages of Six pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Decr. 1797 before us R. Ford Robinson

Mary Day
