Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Ann Darton who will Maketh Oath and saith that he hath been informed by the said Ann Darton which Information he verily believes to be true that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Dodderell at Teddington in the Parish of Teddington in the County of Middlesex for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Twenty Shillings that she hath quitted said Service about Fifteen Months since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1798 before us W Kinnard Edwd: Naish

James Talboys
