Susanna Hunt & her illegitimate Child intented to be called Mary Wells

St Clement Danes April 11th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St George in the East Middx  (to wit) Susanna Hunt  singlewoman aged about 21 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she never did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own Rights that She is informed And verily believes She is the legitimate Daughter of Edward And Catherine Hunt both deceased that her said Father Rented and Resided in a House in Old Gravel Lane in the parish of St George in the County of Middlesex for sometime and several Years and paid parochial Taxes for the Same that he died quitted died about 20 years ago never having done any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that on or about the 29th day of January last at the Licensed House in Water Lane Fleet Street she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath been half Baptized by the Name of Mary and is intended to be called Mary Wells

Sworn the 11th day of April 1794 before Us} N Bond Edwd: Naish

Susanna Hunt 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
