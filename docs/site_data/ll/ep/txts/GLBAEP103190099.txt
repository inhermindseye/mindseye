Middlesex ss}  Jane Hopperton  Singlewoman maketh oath that were years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Richard Johnson in Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County Victualler & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of three years That She this Depont has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 16. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Jane Hopperton
