Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to St. C. D. to wit Mary Noel aged about 30 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Gyles of Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 5 years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of 5 Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Eleven Years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere Says that she has three illegitimate Children living by one Jas. Williams a Coachman who is Since dead vizt. Wm. aged about 11 years Jas. aged about 10 years and Thos. aged about 5 years two of which were born in the Grange Inn Carey Street and the other in Horse Shoe Court both Places are in the Parish of St Clement Danes And further says that on or about the 23d, day of December last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the name of of

Henry Moulton which is likely to become Chargeable the said Parish And further says that one Michael Moses alias Moulton who lately lodged at a Chandlers Shop in Dyot Street St. Giles's is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings in Horse Shoe Court sometime in the Month of Decr. 1783 but the Particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Michael Moses alias Moulton and no other Person did be get the same and that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 20th day of May 1785  Jno Barnfather

her Mary Noel mark
