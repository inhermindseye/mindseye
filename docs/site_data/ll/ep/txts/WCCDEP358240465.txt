Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Moor who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been this day informed by the said Marry Moore which Information he verily believes to be true that she was a Singlewoman that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr Notweller in Gate Street in the parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Four pounds that she quitted said Service about Two years since which time she had done no Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of febry. 1795  Robt. Barr Gabriel W Addington

Jas Talboys
