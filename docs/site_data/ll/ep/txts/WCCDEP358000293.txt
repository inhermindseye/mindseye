St. Clemt. Danes

Middx and Westmr.}  Susannah Allen aged 30 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Thomas Allen her late Husband at the Parish Church of saint Sepulchres in the City of London abt. 13 Years ago, That her said Husband served his Apprentice with Mr. Warren a Hair Dresser in Deverent Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes - That her said Husband departed this life abt 2 Years ago - That her said never gained any Settlemt. since his said Apprenticeship nor hath she this Examinant Since his Death

Sworn this 18th: day of Mary 1786 R Butler

her Susannah Allen Mark
