Westmr.  Philis Moley  Wife of John Moley upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. John Moley at the fleet London abt. Ten Years ago and that her said husband about Eleven Years ago rented a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £24 and lived there upwards of three months and that her sd. husband has not since he left the said house rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or belief and that he now absconds from her and she knows not where he is

Sworn Augt. 7th. 1749 before} Jas: Water

Phills Moley
