Middx  Mary Deakin aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with James Deakin at the Fleet London  on the 30th. Octr. 1733 and her sd. husbd. absconds from her & she knows not whether he is living or dead Says she has been informed and verily believes the sd. James Deakin Served the last three Years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Almet a Peruke maker in the Camb Inn in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and never did any Act whereby to gain a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief Says she was passed from the Borough of Stratford in Octr. 1749 to the Parish of St Clemt. Danes aforesd.

Sworn April 2d. 1751 before  Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Deakin
