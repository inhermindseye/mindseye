Westr.  Mary King aged upwds. of 23 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Law in Clements Inn in the parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. one Year & upwards at the Yearly wages of Four pounds and was discharged from her sd Service about a fortnight ago And further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she was delivered of a male bastard Child on the fourteenth Day of Septr. Instant at Mrs. Walkers one of the parish Nurses of St Clement Danes in Witch Alley in the said Parish which was baptized Wm  & is still Chargeable to the said parish And that Leonard Lambert late a Servt. to Mr. Rolley of Clemts. Inn afsd did beget the said male bastard Child on her body the said Leonard Lambert having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant thrice in the Month of December last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. & that the sd. Leonard Lambert & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Septr. 28th. 1752 before W Fielding

The mark of Mary King
