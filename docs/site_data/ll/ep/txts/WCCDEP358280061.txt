Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Mapeley aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Vigure in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 4 years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £4 a year hath quitted said Service upwards of five Months ago since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that on the 4th. day of Decr. last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of a Mr. Thomas in Dengell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Jeremiah That one Jeremiah Viguers a Lieutenant who lives at Mr. Viguers in Holywell Street aforesaid is the father of the said Child of which she was so delivered he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of March last and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child and that the said Jeremiah Viguers is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of Janry. 1787 } R Butler

Mary Mapeley

Man taken-Paid £20 Release Exted-and the Child sent down to Enfield .
