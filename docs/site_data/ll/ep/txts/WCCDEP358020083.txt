Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William Penny aged about 48 years upon his Oath saith that about 25 years ago he Intermarried with Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields that he this Examinant Rented and lived in a House in Cumberland Street in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for about Eighteen Months at the yearly Rent of Thir Twenty Six Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Sixteen years since which time he has not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of June 1788 D. Walker Rupt: Clarke

Wm Penny
