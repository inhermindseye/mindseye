Middx & Westmr.  Elizabeth Sirr Aged about Seventeen Years Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that on the 17th, of June last, she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Poole (Parish Nurse) in St. Clement's Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex which hath been baptized by the Name of Charlotte , Says that one John Too an Apprentice to Mr. Lorrain a Taylor in Hollywell Street in the Parish aforesaid is the reputed Father of the said Female Bastard Child, he having had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of October 1767, and at several times afterwards, at one of which times he get her with Child of the said Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Too and no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 7th, day of July 1768 , before} 

The Mark of Elizabeth Sirr
