Middx  The Exaicon of Elizth. King  Wido . Aged abt. 60 Years lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 15th. day of Febry 1739 who upon her Oath Saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Steward at the Signe of the Cannon in the Back side of St. Clemts. for the Space of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds she this Exaiant at such time being a Wido . & haveing no Child or Children liveing And this Exaiant further Saith that she hath been come away from her sd. Service abt. two Years & hath not since rented any House or lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & is Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before} Jas. Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. King
