Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Marshlin aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she was Round an Apprentice to Mr. Phillips of Stanhope Street Clare Market that she served her about 5 years of her time that she then left her Mistress for some time and was out of place that she went into the Service of Mr. Kentelo Musician of Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin the Fields that she remained in his Service about 5 Months that at that time her indenture was not expired & that her Mistress knew where she was while she was in such Service with Mr. Kentelo that since that time she has not done any Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of April 1787  Rt. Taylor  James Croft

her Sarah Marshlin Mark
