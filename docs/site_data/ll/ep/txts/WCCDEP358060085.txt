Middx  Jno. Metz upon his Oath saith he intermarried with Catherine his Wife upwds. of 9 Yrs. since at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn Londn . and has by her one Child namely Joseph aged 8 Yrs. Says he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Rd. Delafanche  Peruke maker and Served the first four Years of his sd. Apprentiship with the sd. Mr Delafanche in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West Londn. and then his P. Master broke and absconded and this Examinants father agreed with Mr Hacket of Newcastle Court St. Clemt. Danes Perukemaker for this Examinant to Serve out the Remainder of his Apprentiship with him & this Examinant did accordingly Serve the remaining three Years of his time with the sd. Mr Hacket in Newcastle Court aforesd. & Hacket provided him Lodging Dyet & Apparel during his sd. Service but his first master Delafanche was not any ways Privy to the Agreemt. between Mr. Hacket & his father nor did he (to this Examinants beleif) know of his living there Says he never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly

Servant for one Year (except as aforesd.) or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 20th. 1750 before  Tho Lane

John Mettz
