Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr} to wit John Close aged about 33 years on his Oath saith that about Nine years ago he Intermarried with Mary Clarke his late wife at the Parish Church of Saint Anns Soho  and who has been dead about Six Months by whom he has two Children living Vizt Mary aged about Six years and John aged about Seventeen Months that in the 11th. day of June 1766 he was bound Apprentice for Seven years by Indenture to Richard Carpenter of Coventry Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields  Cordwainer that he served his said Master under such Indenture Five years or thereabouts and then Run away since which time he has done no Act matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 22d. day of Decr. 1785  James Croft  Rt. Taylor

John Close
