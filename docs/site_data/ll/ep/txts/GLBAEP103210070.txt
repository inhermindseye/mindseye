Middlesex ss}  Hannah Duffe  widow of David Duffe deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was by birth a Scotchman & never did any Act to her Knowledge information or belief to gain a Settlement in England That she this Deponent previous to her Marriage with her sd husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant with Mr Worth a Butcher in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of two years & three quarters & has not Since done any Act to Acquire a settlement for herself save what she gained by her Marriage And that she was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 3. day of March 1785 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Hannah Duffe
