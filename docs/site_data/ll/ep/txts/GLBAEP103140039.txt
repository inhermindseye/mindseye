Middlesex ss}  Sarah Harris the Wife of  Harry Harris  who is lately gone away from her to places to her unknown maketh oath that her said husband has informed her and which information She believes to be true that he duly Served his Apprenticeship with a Taylor in the Parish of Arundel in the County of Sussex whose name this Deponent Can't at Present recollect That about eight years ago this Deponents said husband with his Family going to reside & dwell at Hounslow  in the Parish of Heston in the County of Middlesex the parish officers of Heston required of this Deponts sd husband a Certificate from the Parish officers of Arundel aforesaid Acknowledging this Deponents said husband & his Family to be then & there legally Settled the which Certificate was Accordingly granted That Since the granting thereof this Deponts said husband has not rented a house of the yearly rent or value of Ten pounds nor Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to her knowledge or belief done any Act Since his apprenticeship to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath two Children namely Sarah Harris aged two years & half & Elizabeth Judge Harris aged Seven Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband.

Sworn this 20th day of May 1774 before B Camper

Sara Harris
