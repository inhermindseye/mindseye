Middx  Sarah Mansfield aged about 55 Years upon Oath says She the Wife of Thomas Mansfield with whom She intermarried about Twenty two Years since Says her said Husband Rented a House in St. Clements Lane before her Marriage with him and Served the Office of Scavenger there and never after Rented a House or paid Taxes as She has been informed by him which Information She verily believes to be true

Sworn the 11th. day of Septr. 1762 before Paul Vaillant

Sarah Mansfield
