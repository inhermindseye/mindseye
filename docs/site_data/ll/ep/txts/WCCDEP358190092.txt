Middx.  Eiz Gear aged about Twenty four years Lodging at Mr Botons in Hollis Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex Upon Oath says she was never married that her Father & mother are Both Dead that her Father when living rented a house next door to the Bear & Raged Staff in Piccadilly in the Parish of St James in the liberty of Westminster of the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or thereabout as this Examinant Mother informed her & as she verryly Believes Says she never Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Jany. 24 1761 before Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Elizabeth Gear

Upon Mr. Ponters Complt.
