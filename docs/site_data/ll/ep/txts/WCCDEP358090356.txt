Middx to Wit Elizth. Whetstone Aged about 27 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Kipling who Keys a Boarding School in Chaple Street Bedford Row in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middx upwards of four Years at the yearly Wages of 4 Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of 3 Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says she hath a Child by one Fras. Dyer named Elizth . Aged about 18 Months born a Bastard in Grays Inn Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn in the sd. County of Middx who hath gained no other Settlemt. than by Birth.

Sworn this 17th. day of Decr. 1782 before us  Sampn Wright M Addington

Elizabeth Whetstone
