Middlesex ss}  Mary Snow maketh oath That about Fifteen years ago She was delivered of a Male Child in Windmill Court in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechappel in the County of Middlesex Which Child was afterwards Baptized in the Parish Church of St. Paul Shadwell Middx & there Registred by the name of Robert Black and this Depont. Saith that such Child was born a Bastard & that one Robert Black deced to whom this Depont. was never Married did get her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child and that such Child to this Deponts. Knowledge or belief hath not done any Act to gain a settlement in its own right.

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1768 before  Chris :Scott R: Pell

Mary Snow
