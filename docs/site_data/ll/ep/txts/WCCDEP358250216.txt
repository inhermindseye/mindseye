Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Leonard aged about 53 Years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 17th. day of November 1791 he Intermarried with Christian his present wife at the Parish Church of Bethersden in the County of Kent by whom he hath one Child living Vizt. James aged Three years That previous to his Marriage with his present wife and being Single he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Danby Pickering Esqr . in Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Three years at the yearly wages of Fourteen Guineas that the last forty days of his said Service was in Boswell Court aforesaid where he was discharged and that after he quitted said Service which is about Eighteen years ago he never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing where by he might have Gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Decr. 1795 }
