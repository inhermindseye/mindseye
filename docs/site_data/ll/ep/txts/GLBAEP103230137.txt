Middlesex ss}  Nathaniel Hodges maketh oath that his Father John Hodges about Sixteen Years ago lived in and rented a house at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards in New Pye Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret in Westminster in the said County & resided thereon for divers years he this Deponent hiring there with him as part of his Family That his said Father to his Knowledge or belief has not since rented a house or lodging of the yearly rent of Ten pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he this Deponent was born in the Said Parish of St Margaret in Westmr and never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house or lodging of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this. 6. day of May 1788 before  R Davies

Nathl Hodges
