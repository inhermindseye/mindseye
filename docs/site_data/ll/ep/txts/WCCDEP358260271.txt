Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Booth aged about 54 years upon his Oath saith that he hath for these Four years last past Rented & lived in a Shop in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster at the Weekly Rent of Four Shillings

Sworn this 26th. day of April 1798 before me } Saml. Edward .

his James Booth mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
