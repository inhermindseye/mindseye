Middlesex ss  Ann Wood  Single woman maketh Oath that about five years ago she was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year with one William Mallans  Hosier in Bading Lane in the Par of Saint Mary Aldermary in the City of London and Contin in such service there for the Space of one year and upwards the yearly Wages of Six Pounds and by means there of as is advised gained a legal settlement there and this Deponc Saith that she has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Sp of year nor hath she been ever married nor hath she don any act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settleme

Sworn this 3d day of March 1770 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

The Mark of Ann Wood .
