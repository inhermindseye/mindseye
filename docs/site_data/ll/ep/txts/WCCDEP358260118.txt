Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Bryant aged about 66. years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Levy in Bury Street Saint Mary Ax in the Parish of Saint Katherine Cree Church in the City of London for Sixteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly wages of Five Guineas that she quitted said Service about five weeks since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of March 1796 E Read Rusl Clarke

her Hannah Bryant mark

Witness B Newman
