Westmr.  Mary Hamey  Wido . of Wm. Hamey (who dyed near 2 Years since) upon Oath saith she intermarried with her said late husband Wm. Hamey about 16 Years ago in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn London by whom she has 2 Children (now with her) viz. Jas. aged upwds. of 3 Years & Thos. aged upwds. of one Year Says her sd. late husband (as she has been informed & beleives) Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Alsop a Butcher in the Parish of Risley in the County of Bedford and afterwards lived in and rented a Farm of the Yearly Value of Ten pounds and upwards for several Years in the same Parish Says neither her sd. late husband in his Life time nor this Examinant since his Death ever rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 1st. April 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Hamey
