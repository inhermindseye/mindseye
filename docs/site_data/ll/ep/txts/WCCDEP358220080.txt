Middlesex & Westminster }  Catherine Corbett Aged about Twenty Seven Years her Oath Says she is the Widow of Caleb Corbett decease who had been Dead about Nine Months & with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry about four Years ago Says her said late Husband served five years Clerkship with Mr. Glass Attorney at Law in New Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and never after Rented House or paid taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to pay same when born Says that one Thomas Bratson a Labou who lodges at his Mothers in New Street near Dock he had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of May last but the Particular Day she cannot rec and at sevl. Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant as afsd. and that the said Thomas Batson and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 6th. day of Novr. 1769 before me }
