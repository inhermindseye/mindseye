St. Clement Danes.

Wm. Gardiner aged about 53 years upon his Oath Saith that he intermarried with Carolina Gardiner his present Wife at the Parish Church of St. Andrews Holborn about the Month of August last-That he served an apprenticeship of Lavery Years to one Mr Maddock, of Saffron Hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the County of Middlesex that Since he quitted his said Apprenticeship he never has done any Act whereby he may to the best of his knowledge have gained a Settlement

Sworn this d: March 1787 before D Walker Wm Kitchiner William Gardner .
