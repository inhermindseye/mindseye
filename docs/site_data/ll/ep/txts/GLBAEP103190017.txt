Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Beale  Singlewoman maketh oath that she never did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right That about nine Years ago her late Fathr Richard Beale rented & lived in a house of the yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds in plough Alley in the Parish of St George in the said County & Continued in the occupation of the same for near the Space of five years & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement & this Deponent lived there with her said Father as part of his Family & this Depont. was never married but Still remains a Singlewoman

Sworn this 23 day of Februry 1780 before  Jasper Clarke  Jno Sherwood

Eliz Beale
