Middx & Westmr-}  Ann Orchard on the Behalfe of Mary Eakins her Daughter upon her Oath saith that the sd. Mary Eakins was married to her now Husband Jno. Eakins on or abt. the 6th. Day of January 1729 & that the sd. Jno. Eakins lived a Covenant Servt . with one Jno. Fair in Green Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx for the Space of one Year & upwards & that since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented £10 a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a yearly hired Servt. with any Person  or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that the sd. Mary Eakins hath 3 Childn. (namely) Ephraim aged 7 Years Wm . aged 5 Years & Priscilla aged Six Months yt. her Husband absconds. from her & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herself & Childn-

Sworne the 4th- of Septr- 1741 before} Thos. De Veil

The Mark of Ann Ann Orchard
