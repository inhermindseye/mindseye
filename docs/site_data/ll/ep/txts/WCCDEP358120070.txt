Middx  Rachael Tomkins upon Oath saith saith she is the Wife of Michael Tomkins (a Sailor abroad in his Majesties Service) and that her sd. husband informed her & she verily belives he Served Seven years apprentiship wth. Master of a Collier at Shields in the County of Durham and never was a yearly Servant nor over rented ten pounds a year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn March 6th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Rachael Tomkins
