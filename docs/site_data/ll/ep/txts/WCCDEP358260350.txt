Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Robert Nichols aged about 28 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the Third day of July 1793 he Intermarried with Elizabeth his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That previous to his Intermarriage with his said wife and being a Singleman to lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Sutton in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Fourteen Months at the yearly wages of Six Pounds that he quitted said Service about Thirteen years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Octr. 1798 before me } J Reeves

his Robert Nichols mark

Witness B Newman
