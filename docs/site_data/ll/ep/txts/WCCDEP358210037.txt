Westmr.  Thos. Percy aged about 70 Years upon Oath says he was rated to and paid the Land Tax in Lumber Court near the 7 dials in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx and hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes

Sworn March 13th. 1766 before} Paul Vaillant Frans. Caryt

Tho. Percy
