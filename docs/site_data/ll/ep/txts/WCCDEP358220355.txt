Westmr. to wit Mary Macklin Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of James Macklin with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Walthamstow in the County of Essex about Nine Years ago and who has been dead about Eight Years Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with and Mr. Hart a Dancing Master in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for one Year and Three Quaters at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about five Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further says that on the first day of Janry. 1772 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Chair Court Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the name of Ann Wenman says that one Wm. Wenman Paper Buildings Inner Temple London Esqr. is the true Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in Sometime in the Month of March 1771 but the Particular day she does not now remember & at sevl. different times afterwds. at one of which times he got her wth. Child of the Female Child of which she was delivered as afsd. And that the sd. Wm. Wenman and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 4th day of June 1772 before us}
