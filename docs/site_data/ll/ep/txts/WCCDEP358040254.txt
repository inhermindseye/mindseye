Westmr. 

Eliza: Aldersey aged about 36 Years upon Oath saith she never was married  rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year Says she is the Daughter of Benjamin Aldersey deced by Eliz: his Wife and that her sd. Mother Eliz. Aldersey since the Death of this Examinants Father (which happen'd upwards of 20 Years ago) lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £22 at Knights bridge in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr. for three Years or thereabouts and hath quitted that house about Ten Years Says her Sd. Mother hath not since she left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Places to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 13th. 1748 before} Wm. Jones

Eliz: Aldersey
