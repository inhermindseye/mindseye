Middx & Westmr-}  Mary Tims aged about 57 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes Says the last place that this Examinant lived in as a hired Yearly Servant was with one Jno- Pollard a Taylor in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lived with the said Mr- Pollard as a hired Yearly Servant for upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about three Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subseqt- Settlemt-

Sworn this 6th- Day of Septr- 1777  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Tyms
