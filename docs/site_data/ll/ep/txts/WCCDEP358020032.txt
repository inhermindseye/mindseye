Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Susannah Beddle aged about 51 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1759 she was married to Henry John Beddle at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch that her said Husband has been dead about 21 years that her said late Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to Edmund Beddle his father in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of St Clement Danes by Indres duly executed that after her said late Husband was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or dane any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22. day of Janry. 1787 R Butler

her Susannah Beddle Mark
