Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Simpson aged about 42 Years upon her Oath saith that about 3 Years ago she Intermarried with Robt. Brown Simpson her Present Husband (who is gone from her) at the Parish Church of St. Stephen Walbrook in the City of London that she hath Two Children living by her said Husband Vizt. Robert aged about Two Years and Richard aged about 8 Weeks that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in the  Walker Cot. St. James's in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr. for Seven Weeks at the Yearly Rent of £30 hath quitted said House about 2 Years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Novr. 1786  Jno Martin  James Croft

her Elizth. Simpson Mark
