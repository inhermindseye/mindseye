Middlesex ss}  James Burton maketh oath That divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages with one John Carter of the Parish of Saint Edmond in the City of Salisbury  Inn Keeper & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent afterwd listed himself as a Soldier in the Second Regiment of Foot Guard in which Service he Continued for the Space of Twenty Years & upwards That during Such time &While a Single man he with the privity & Consent of the Captain of the Sd Regiment was hired by George Hewell of the Hamlet of Mile End old Town in the parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex  Brewer to live with him there at the Rate of Four shillgs per Week to have meat Drink Washing and Lodging found him by the sd Hewell & accordingly by he entred into the Said Hewells Service under the Terms aforesd and Continued therein for the Space of Seven Years He this Deponent being all the time of such last mentioned Service a Soldier of & belonging to the said Regiment & liable to do Duty there in & accordingly did at times do duty during Such his Service with the sd Hewell although his pay as a Soldier was retained by the Captain of the said Regiment That he this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant or a Year or rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement for himself other than as is above particularly set forth That he hath a wife named Sarah to whom he was lawfully married in the parish Church of St Botolph Aldgate London & two Children namely Mary Aged about Seven Years & Sarah Aged about one Year & half the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his sd wife .

Sworn this 24th day of November 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

James Burton
