Westmr. to wit Martha Tinkler on behalf of Mary Lacey  singlewoman Aged upwards of Eighteen Years who now lives ill of a Fever on her Oath Says that the said Mary Lacey never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived hired Yearly Servant for one Years Say her late Father John Lacey rented and lived in a House in Bridgewater Gardens near Goswell Street in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate London for five Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds as she has oftentimes been informed and believes the same to be true and further Says that the said John Lacey has been dead about five Years and that the said Mary Lacey since her said Fathers Death or before never gained any or subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Decr. 1773 before R. Butler Sampn Wright

her Martha Tinkler Mark
