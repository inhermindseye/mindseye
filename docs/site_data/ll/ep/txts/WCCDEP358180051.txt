Middx  Eliza: Foreman upon her Oath saith that on the 2d. Day of March about Eighteen Years since she intermarried with her prest. Husband  Wm Foreman in Blew Ball Alley in the Mint. That her sd. Husbd. was bound an Apprentice to & served Years the first Part of his Apprentisp with one Mr. Lancaster Mans in Stoney Lane in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry that since the sd. Wm- Foreman left his sd Master he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year been assigned over to any other Person paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd.
