Lorenda Holmes

St Clement Danes September 19th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Matthew Bethnal Green  (to wit) Richard Dobbins one of the Beadles of the parish of St Clement Danes on behalf of Lorenda Holmes (who is deranged in her Lendes) Wife of Robert Holmes (Now in Prison ) Maketh Oath and saith that he hath been informed and verily believes that the said Robert and Lorenda his Wife were Married at the parish Church of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsey Surrey in or about the year of our Lord 1781 that subsequent thereto the said Robert Holmes rented and resided in a House in Queens Row in the parish of St Matthew Bethnal Green for about two years at the Annual Rent of £17 that immediately he quitted it he hired a House facing an or nearly Opposite opposite to the said said Queens Row which he Rented and resided in about upwards of three Months at the yearly Rent of £11 or £12 that he quitted the the same about upwards about two years ago and hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever where whereby to Obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 19th day of September 1793 before us} Kitchener Edwd : Naish

Richd Dobbins
