Westmr.  Judith Ray aged about 30 Years upon Oath saith she was never married and that about 10 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Sheilds a Peruke maker in Clements Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of four pounds for one Year and upwards & has not since she last the sd. Mr. Shields Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 18th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Judith Ray
