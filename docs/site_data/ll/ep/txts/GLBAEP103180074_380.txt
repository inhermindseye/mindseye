Middlesex ss}  Margaret Douglas the widow of Thomas Douglas deced maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband or of his Parents or either of them Save that her Sd husband has informed her that he was born in Scotland but the Place Where he never told her That She this Depont as She has been informed & believs was born in Berwick upon Tweed & this Deponts Father Patrick Irvin lived in prented a house in the Parish of Spittle near Barwick upon Tweed for divers Years & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same & Where this Depont lived with him as Part of his Family & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this Depont never was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Save What She has acquired by marriage as aforesaid

Sworn this 23d day of October 1778 before dead Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Margaret Douglas
