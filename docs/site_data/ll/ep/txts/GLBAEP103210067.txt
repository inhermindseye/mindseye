Middlesex ss}  Sarah Wichells  Singlewoman maketh oath that she has been informed by her late father Joseph Wichells deced & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Rattleton a Sailmaker in Flushing Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County and did not afterwards to this Deponents Knowledge informn or belief do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That she this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

sworn this 21. day of February 1785 before J. Hawley

The Mark of Sarah Wichells
