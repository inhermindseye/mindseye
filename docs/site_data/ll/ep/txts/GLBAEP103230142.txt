Middlesex ss}  Simon Pomfret maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house known by the Sign of the Ship in Lower EastSmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & was charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the same That he has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Elizabeth and a Child named Sarah the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said wife to whom he was lawfully married which Child is Seventeen months old

Sworn this 14. day of May 1788 before  R Davies

Simon Pomfret
