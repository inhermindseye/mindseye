Westmr. ss:  William Hattam Aged about fifty Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says he was Born at Redruth in the County of Cornwall and never gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Febry 1775 before R Butler

her Wm. Hattam mark

NB. Wm Hattam Marrd Barbara Lawrence Ica preceds Page
