Middlesex ss}  Samuel Meredith maketh oath that his late Brother James Meredith deced after having James a legal Settlement in the Parish of St Botolph Without Aldgate in the Said County lived in & rented Part of house at the Yearly Rent of Six Pounds in the Parish of Barking in the County of Essex And this Deponent has been informed by his Sd Brother & which information this Deponent believes to be true that his Sd Brother never paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes for the Same during his residence therein nor ever did any Act to gain Settlement for himself or his Children Subsequent to this Deponts Brother leaving the Sd P arish of St Botolph Aldgate

Sworn this third day of February 1786 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Samuel Meredith
