Middx & Westr.  Eliz Rotheram aged about 23 Years upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she never was married and that on the 20th. Day of May last she was delivered of a male bastard Child in her Fathers house in Cary Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx which has been baptized Abraham Savage & is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. parish of St Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when and that Giles Dent Savage of Wapping Hosier did beget the sd. Male Bastard Child on her body the sd. Giles Dent Savage having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the month of Augt. last & at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid & that the sd. Giles Dent Savage & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn June 11th. 1751 before  Tho Lane H Fielding

The Mark of Eliz Rotheram
