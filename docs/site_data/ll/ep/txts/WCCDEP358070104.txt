Westmr. to wit Penelope Anderson Aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath says she was bound out an Apprentice by the Govrs. & Guardians of the Foundling Hospital to one Mr. Scott a Book binder in Princes's Street Red Lyon Square and Served her Master there for one Year and upwards and afterwards her said Master removed into Villers Street York Buildings where she continued to live with him thereupwards of half a Year where her Master broke and run away into Stoctland since which time she hath never gained any Subsequent Settlement either by being turned over to any other Master or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th. day of June 1773 before R Butler

her  Penelope Anderson  Mark
