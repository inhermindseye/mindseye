Middlesex ss}  Ann Brooks  Single woman maketh oath that She was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to Paul Savigny of the Parish of St Bartholomew the Great in the City of London  Cutler for the term of Seven Years & Served the whole of Such her Apprenticeship in the Sd Parish & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or been married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30 day of October 1784 before R Davies J Hawley

The Mark of Ann Brooks
