Middx.  Sarah Dawson upon Oath says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Frances Spencer at New Inn Coffie house in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty Westmr. within the County of Middx. for two Years at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds & hath quitted her said Service about five Months

Sworn the day of 175 before}
