Middx.  Prudence Dean aged abt. 34 Years upon Oath Says She never was Married Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Bates in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx . for Six three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds & hath quitted said Service upwds. of two Years, since which time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Janry- 8th- 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Prudence Dean
