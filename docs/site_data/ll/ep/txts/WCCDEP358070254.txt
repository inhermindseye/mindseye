Westr to wit Sarah Allen  Wife of John Allen on the behalf of Eliz Stanfield an Infant Aged about four Months whose Father and Mother are lately Dead says that before the Death of the father John Stanfield her rented and lived in a house in Snow fields near Bermondsey Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the borough of Southwark in the County of Surry for five Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Nine pounds and paid Land Tax due for the same hath had Quitted the said House upwards of Two Years before his Death and never after Rented House Paid Taxes or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th day- of March 1775 before R Buller Sampn Wright

The Mark of Sarah Allen
