Middx to wit Ann Landen Aged about 39 Years on behalf of Robt. Fillery an Infant Aged about 5 Years on her Oath Says that the Father Robt. Fillery never gained any or Settlement than by Birth as she Believes says the Grandmother of the said Robt. Fillery is now Chargeable to the Parish of Lee or Lee Green in the County of Kent says that Thos. Fillery & the above Named Robt. Fillery were both Passed to Lee in Kent from the Parish of St Andrew Wardrobe London about Twelve Months & that the afsd. Robt Fillery has gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 26th day of May 1784 before R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

her Ann Landen Mark
