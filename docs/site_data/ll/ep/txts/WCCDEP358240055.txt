Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  (to wit). Mary Simmonds Aged about 56 Years upon her Oath Saith that about 15 Years Ago she Intermarried with John Simmonds at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex that about 2 Years after their intermarriage her said Husband deserted her And that she has been not heard from him these 13 Years past but was informed about 2 Years after he deserted her that he died abroad And this Examinant further Saith that between 6 & 7 Years Ago she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with a Mr. Neal of Beanford Buildings in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that for near 2 Years at the Yearly Wages of £8:8:0 That since she quitted said service she never Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year paid Taxes lived as a hired Yearly Servant for One Year or done any other Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 19th. Octr. 1791 before. R Butler.

Mary Simmonds
