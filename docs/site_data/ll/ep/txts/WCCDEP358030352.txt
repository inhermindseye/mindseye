Westmr. 

Mary Absalom aged Seventy nine Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with John Absalom deced on the 26th. March about forty Six Years since in Grays Inn Chapell Says her said late husbd. Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr Gillett a Butcher whose Shop was in Charemarkett & his house in Saint Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says her sd. late husband John Absalom never in his Life time rented a house of Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 5th. 1746 before  Thos: Burdus

The Mark of Mary Absalom
