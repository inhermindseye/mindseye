Middx to wit Samuel Scott Aged about Thirty five Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to one Samuel Houses a Stationer in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex and served his said Master under such Indenture for the Space of three years when his said Master & this Examinant Agreed to part And to Cancell his Indenture And this Examinant further says that after quitting his said Masters Service he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise Howsoever

Sworn this 11th. Day of Decr. 1780 before  Abrm. Baley R Butler

Saml. Scott
