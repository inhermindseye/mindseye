30th. June 1786

Middlesex (to wit) Frederick Wise aged thirty two Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he intermarried with Honor Wise his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the field in the County of Middlesex about Six Years ago that since their Inter marriage he rented and lived in a shop and Parlor in New Compton Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the field in the County of Middlesex at a Yearly Rent of upwards of ten pounds for about three Quarters of a year that he quitted the same about three Years ago and since that time has not done any other Act Matter or thing Whereby to the lach of his Knowledges Information or belief he may have gained any subsequent Settlement-and that he is poor and unable

to maintain himself and his said Wife she being sick and ill

Sworn this 30th. June 1786 -before  Jno. Barnfather  James Croft

Friedrick Weiss

Wise lives at a Mr Carriers- No. 2 Hogg Lane
