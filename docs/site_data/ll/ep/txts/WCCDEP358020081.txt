Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Samuel Jones aged 34 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that his Father kept a House in Oxford Street in the Parish of Saint James Westminster was by Trade a Watch Maker That he this deponent went to reside with his Father there when 2 years of Age and lived there with him till he was about 12 years of Age that he lived in the same House with a Brother in Law about 5 years and learnt this Business of Painting of him that since he quitted said House he had done no Act matter of Thing whereby he may have gained a subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge and belief

Sworn this 16th day of June 1788 before D Walker N. Conants.

his Samuel Jones Mark
