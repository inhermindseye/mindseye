Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Eleanor Albon aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cohen in Duke Street Saint James's in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Eighteen Months since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Janry. 1789 Sampn Wright M Addington.

Eleanor Albon
