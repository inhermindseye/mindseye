Saint Clement Danes Feby 11th. 1794

Middx and Westmr-}  St George Bloomsbury  (to wit) John Hickman Aged 63 Yrs or thereabouts Singleman Upon Oath saith that, he Rented and Resided in a House No 7 Duke Street Bloomsbury at the Yearly Rent of £18 for upwards of 26 Years that he left the said House about 4 Years ago and that he hath sid since obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this deponent information knowledge or belief

Sworn this 11th day of Feby 1794 before me N Bond B: Kennett

John Hickman
