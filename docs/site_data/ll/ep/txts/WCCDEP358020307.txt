St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Ann Scott  Wife of Alexander Scott being of the Age 27 years & upwards Upon her Oath (taken on behalf of two infant Children eleserted & left in the said parish of St. Clement Danes ) Maketh Oath & saith the respective Names of the said Children and Charles Scott & Mary Ann Scott that the and Charles Scott that the said Mary Ann is Aged 4 years and upwards that the said Charles was born on the 5th. November in the year of 1789 & is of the Age therefore of One year & near Eight Months, that the said Children are the legitimate Children of one John Scott now Deceased & Bridget his then Wife who were Married at the parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch as this Deponent hath been Informed & verily believes on the 15th Octr. in the year 1785 and this Examinant hath been informed & verily believes to be true that the said John Scott Rented & with his said wife resided in an Apartment Mr at the Biscut Shop facing or nearly opposite to Mr Henshaw in the Strand in the said parish of St Clement Danes about Six Months at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds that they left that Apartment abt. two years & ago that her Husband upwards never gained a legal Settlement of his life that the said Bridget Scott since for said Husband death Death Vizt about Six Months ago she Rented & lived in an House at No:10 Dennetts Court in the said parish of

St Clement Danes & continued there a Widow till abt. a Month or 5 Weeks ago when & that she then had Married but knows not to whom & left the House she had resided in an or about Saturday last And this Deponent farther upon her Oath saith that she cannot tell where she now is or whither she went when she quitted the same Apartment House and did not Know till the Sunday Night (when she went to & did to leave the Child the said Infant Child Mary Ann in the said Court) that she the said Bridget Scott was then gone And that the said Children are left by the & deserted by their said Mother without any provision for or means of support to the best of her knowledge or belief And Ann Davis  Widow aged  fifty Nine years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith the said Infant Child Charles Scott here in before mentioned was brought to her this Deponent abt. Six Weeks ago by the said Bridgett Scott to Nurse at 3s a Week that she came regularly on the every Saturday Evening & paid for the Week due the the said Weekly Allowance & last Saturday Night paid the Sum due for the preceeding Week & but did not mention any thing of her intention of deserting the said to this Examinant that she was removing her things into an Apartment in Princes Street . & same & desired this Examt. to come & see it & not to fearful for she would pay her for Nursing the Child in future four Shilligs a Week, And that Ann Davis the Dane of this Examinant being sent going into Princes Street to enquire on same Business on the Sunday she was informed that there no Mr Scott lived there And that in consequence this Deponent went  to enquire after the said Bridget Scott to the Apartment which she

had been taken to by the said Bridget Scott & which she had as aforesaid presented to have hired have been hired for her future Residence when this Depont. was Informed by a person Residence in that House that she went away in a Coach on Sunday Morning & said she should not see London again who And that then this Examinant being unable to support the said Child & having no money or provision left for the same she sent the Child by her said Daughter with directions to leave it at the Parlor Door of the House in Bennetts Court where in the Mother did Reside and that her said Daughter upon her Return Informed her she had so done

Sworn the 29th June 1791 before one D Walker
