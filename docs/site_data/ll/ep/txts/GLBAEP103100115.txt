Middlesex ss  James Dean maketh oath that divers Years ago he rented a house of the Yearly Value of Twenty Pounds in Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields Westmr in the County Middx & was Charged to & Paid Parish Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Deponent Saith that he hath not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards nor Served any Parish offices nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a wife named Ann .

Sworn this day of June 1769 before}
