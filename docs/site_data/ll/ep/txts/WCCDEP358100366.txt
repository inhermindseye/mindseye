Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster}  Swears to St. C. D . to wit Ann Williams aged about 52 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Hatfield in Chainge Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twenty Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted said Service about Six years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 23d. day of Febry 1785 R Butler

her Ann Williams mark
