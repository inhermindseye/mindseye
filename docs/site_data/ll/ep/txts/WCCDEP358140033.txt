Middx.  Mary Lamb aged abt. 24 Yrs. lodging in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon Oath says she was never married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hewetson an Apothecary in the Parish of Hexam in the County of Durham for one Year at the Yrly. Wages of fifty five Shillings & hath quitted her said Service abt. one Year & a half & further upon her voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to Maintain the same when born says that John Ross of Surry Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes  Perukemaker had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body the latter End of February & the beginning of March last about of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he & no other person did beget the said Child or Childn.

Sworn this 27th. day of August 1757 before} Barth: Hammond Mary Lamb
