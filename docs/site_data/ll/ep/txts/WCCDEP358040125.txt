Middx.  Mary Dickenson aged about 18 Years upon Oath says she never was married Says her late Father Christopher Dickenson lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £40 & upwds. between Arundel Street and Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for several Years & there dyed Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 17th. 1747 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of M Mary Dickenson
