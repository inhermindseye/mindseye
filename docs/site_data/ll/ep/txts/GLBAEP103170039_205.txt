Middlesex ss}  Rachael Major the widow of Philip Major deced maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband nor Can She give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents deced Save that her Mother has informed her & which She believes to be true That She was born in the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County where She this Depont was also born That She this Deponent never was bound Apprentice nor ever hired as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She Acquired by her Marriage

Sworn this. 3. day of January 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Rachael Major
