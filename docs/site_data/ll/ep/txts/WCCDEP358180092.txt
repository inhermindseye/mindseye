Middx  Eliza: Wood aged upwards of Eighteen Years upon her Oath saith that by Indenture Dated the 6th- Day of July 1739 she was bound Apprentice to Stephen Litler of the Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middx & lived with him as an Apprentice till his Decease & after Continued to Live with Mrs. Littler his Wife who was Ext. of her said Husband to the time of her Death which Happened about the Day of Two Yrs. since that she hath not since the Death of the sd. Mrs- Littler been assigned over to any other Person rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 20th- Day of July 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliza: Wood
