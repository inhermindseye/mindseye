Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Crowson aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she Rented and lived in a first floor of Mr. Thorpe in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages Rent of Ten Guineas that she hath quitted said Service Apartments about Three years since which time she has lodged in New Court in the Strand in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 22d day of July 1790  Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Crowson mark
