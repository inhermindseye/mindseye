Middlesex ss  Susannah Lewis  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago she was duly bound Apprentice by Indre by the Parish officers of St Mary Rother hith in Surry to one Mary Mullens of the Parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the Same County Cook and Continued with her under such Indre for about four years there & then by Consent her Indres were delivered up & she was again bound out Apprentice by the Parish officers of St Mary Magadalen Bermondsey to one Sharpa Milk woman in the Parish of St Margaret in Westminster in the County of Middx With Whom she lived for about a year & then by reason of ill usage She left her Said Mistress Sharp & did not afterwards return back to her nor hath she ever heard from her Since That she hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been Married nor done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 10th day of August 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Susannah Lewis
