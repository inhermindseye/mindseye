Middx  Mary Turbutt aged about 52 Years Lodging in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Joseph Turbutt who has been dead about Thirteen Years That since his Death having no Children living she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Turbutt Stationer in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid for about the Space of Ten Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Quitted the Same about Two Years and hath not Since kept House or lived a hired Yearly Servant in any one Place for Twelve Months together

Sworn the 16th. day of May 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Turbutt
