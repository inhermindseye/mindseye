St. Clement Danes . June 4th. 1792.

Middlesex and Westminster }  Bell Mount a Seat Somewhere in Hants}  not Sworn being very Ill & taken into the Ho. as a Casual  (to wit) Margaret Dixon  Singlewoman Aged about Sixty Years Upon Oath saith That she never was Married, that she lived an an hired Yearly Servant in the capacity of Housekeeper & Cook with Lady Clanrighet in Queen Ann Street by Welbeck Street in the parish of St Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for one Year or thereabouts that then she Removed with the Children to her Ludyshep's Seat at Bell Mount in Hampshire but this Deponent cannot Recollect what parish it is situate in where That she she continued in such Service at Bell Mount for two Months a Upwards And that she quitted that Service about ten years ago and hath not since obtained any subsequent legal Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief

Margaret D Dixon
