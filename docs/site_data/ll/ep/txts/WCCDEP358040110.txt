Westmr.  Sarah Brandley aged about 47 Years upon Oath she never was married Says about 16 Weeks ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. John Martin  Taylor in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Year by Wages of £5 for two Years and upwards Says she has not since she left the sd. Mr. Martins Service rented ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 27th. Novr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Sarah Brandley
