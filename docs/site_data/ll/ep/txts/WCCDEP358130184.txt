Middx  Sarah Hoy  Widow aged Sixty Years upon Oath Says She lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Jennings a Doctor in Physick upwards of a Year in Castle Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds ten Shillings Whilst She was a Widow & without Child or Children & quitted her sd. Service abt. Sixteen Years ago & hath not since Intermarried with any other Person Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn the 12th. Day of July 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Sarah Hoy
