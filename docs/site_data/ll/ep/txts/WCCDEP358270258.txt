Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Tarrant aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that on the 16th: day of October 1794 she was delivered of Male Bastard Child in the Licensed House in Water Lane  Fleet Street which hath since been Christened by the Name of Matthew That one Francis Spencer a Postboy at the White Hart Inn at Windsor in the County of Berks is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Francis Spencer and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 12th. day of Janry. 1796 N Kinnard

her Sarah Tarrant Mark
