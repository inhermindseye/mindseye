Middx  Mary Bowtle upon Oath says She Intermarried with Thomas Bowtle (who is gone from her) in the Parish Church of all Saints in the City of Worcester the 8th. day of April 1750 Old Style by whom She has two Children namely Martha aged about 5 years & Mary aged upwards of two years Says her said Husband Served seven years Apprenticeship with Thos. Assett in the Parish of Braintree in the County of Essex Woolcomber and never rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year as She has been informed by her said Husband & verily believes

Sworn the 15th. day of May 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Bowtle
