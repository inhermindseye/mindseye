Middx  Mary Bregs (now Wife of Thomas Bregs but late the Wife of John Hornsby deceased maketh Oath and Saith that about forty Years her Son John Hornsby was born in the parish of Stepney in the County of Middx and that this Depts. late husband John Hornbasby father of her sd. Son John Hornsby Served his Apprenticship to his father in the Town of Hemsly Blackmore in the County of York and gained no other Settlemt to her Knowledge

Sworn this 29 Oct. 1745 before Tho: Quarrill

her Mary Bregs Mark
