Westmr. to wit Elinor Briggs Aged about Thirty four Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Joseph Briggs (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex in the Year 1765 and by whom she hath one Child now living Hester Elizabeth aged about Six Months Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a single Man he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Cornelius Launder Esqr. in Wheeler Gate in the Parish of St. Peter in the Town of Nottingham in the Capacity of a Footman upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds or thereabouts and which was the last place her sd. Husband lived at before his Intermarr. with this Examinant as her said Husband has often Informed her & which Information she verily believes to be true And further says that after this Examinants said Intermarr. with her said Husband he Rented a Lodging of one Mr. James Belbin in Vere Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Six Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds but was never rated to or paid any Taxes whatever for the same hath quitted the said Lodging about three Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement since living a hired Yearly Servant with the said Mr. Launder as aforesaid to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 22d. day of May 1772 before W Kelyngs

Elinor Briggs
