Westmr. to wit Hester Henderson Aged about Twenty Six years on her Oath says She is the Widow of James Henderson with whom she Intermarried at the City of Edinburgh about Fifty years ago and who died about Twenty Three years ago and by whom She hath One Child by her said Late Husband now provided for says her said Late Husband Rented and Lived in a House in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for several years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and Lived therein two years after the Decease of her said Late Husband quitted the said House about Twenty One years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 29th: day of Septr. 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Hester Henderson Mark
