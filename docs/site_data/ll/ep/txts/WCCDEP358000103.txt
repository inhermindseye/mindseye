St. Clemt. Danes Augt 26th 1785

Middlesex and Westminster} 

passd. to Sr. Jas. Clerkenwell to wit Elizabeth Hogan aged about 43 years and Elizabeth Hogan her Daughter aged about 19 years or thereabouts upon their Oaths severall say and first this Examinant Elizth. Hogan Senr. Saith that she Intermarried with James Hogan at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about 20 years ago that her said Husband was a Irishman and never gained any Settlement in England that before her Intermarriage she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Saml. Rutter of Woods Close Carcase Butcher  for two years at in the Parish of Saint James Clarkenwell for two years at the yearly Wages of 5 Pounds hath quitted said Service about 24 years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere And this Examinant Elizth. Hogan Junr. for herself saith that she has done no Act Matter or thing to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief whereby she may have gained a Settlement anywhere in he

Sworn this 27th. day of Augt. 1785  Wm. Kitchiner J Addington

the Mark of Elizth. Hogan Senr.

the Mark of Elizth. Hogan Junr.
