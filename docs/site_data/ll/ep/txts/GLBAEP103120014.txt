Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Goodluck  Singlewoman of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County taken on oath before me Charles Digby  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this day of October 1772

Who on her oath Saith That on the Twenty fifth day of October one thousand Seven hundred & Sixty Seven She the Said Ann Goodluck was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of George Langley in Lambeth Marsh in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry ship wright which said Bastard Child hath been Since Baptized & registred by the name of Thomas Goodluck in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westmr in the Said County of Middlesex and is become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate .
