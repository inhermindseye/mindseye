Middx  Mary Ann Revel aged abt. 65 Yrs. upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Rd. Revell who dyed about 3 Years ago and that the sd. Rd Revell never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes that her sd. late husbands father rented a house of £21 a Year in the Old Bailey in the parish of St Sepulchres London & lived therein one Year and never after he left the sd. house gained a Subsequent Settlement to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr 16 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Marana M.A. Revel
