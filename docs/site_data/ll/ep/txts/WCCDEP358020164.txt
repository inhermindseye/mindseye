Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Catherine Tompkins aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she this Examt. lived as a hired yearly servant with Mr. Wayland at the Hoop Tavern in the Broad Way Westminster in the Parish of Saint Margarett in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted Said service about Six years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Sept. 1789 D Walker Edward Hodsoll

her Catherine Tompkins Mark
