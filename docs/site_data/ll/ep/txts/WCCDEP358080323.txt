Middx  Hannah Skelton Aged about 70 Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Charles Skelton who has been Dead about 40 Years and by whom she hath no Child or Children now living Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly servant with one Mr. Jonathan Gurnell a Merchant  Opposite St. Lawrence Church in the Parish of St. Lawrence Jury Church in the City of London for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds as she beleaves and after quitting the said last Mentioned service she never Rented House paid Taxes or gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 9th- day of Decr. 1778 before  Jno. Machin  John Cox

her Hannah Skelton Mark
