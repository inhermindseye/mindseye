Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Marshall aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith That on or about the 18th. day of January 1792 she this Deponent Intermarried with one Robert Marshall (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. James aged about four years and Robert aged about Three years That her said Husband Rented a first Floor unfurnished of Mr. Phillips No. 5 Grange Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Three Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and that after he quitted said Apartments which is upwards of Three years he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year for Forty days paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that her said Husband is now at New York in America as she believes she having left him there on the Sixth day of October 1793 and hath not ever seen or heard of him since That on the Sixteenth day of June 1796 She this Deponent was delivered of a female Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the Name of Caroline and that one George Sampson who did live Gardener with Sir John Amos at Putney in the said County of Surry is the father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of March 1796 before me } J Hunter

her Ann Marshall Mark
