Westmr.  Mary Toogood aged about 52 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of James Toogood who has been dead about twelve Years says her said husband before his Intermarriage with her and whilst a single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Francis a Tallow Chandler in Vere Street Clare market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds or thereabouts, Diet and Lodging and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes says she hath not since her said husbands Death Rented house or paid Taxes, and hath one Child by her said late husband (to wit) Harriot aged abt. fifteen Years who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant

Sworn the 7th. of Septr. 1765 , before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Toogood
