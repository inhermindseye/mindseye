Middx  Ann Bradford  Wido . of Humphry Bradford Passed from the Parish of St. Pancras in the County of the upon her Oath saith that her sd. late Husband Humphry Bradford lived in and rented a House in Horshoe Court in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds for the Space of Eighteen Yrs. & upwards & hath left the same abt. Seventeen Years & further saith that since her late Husband left the sd. House he in his Life never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes nor she since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 21st. Octr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Ann Bradford
