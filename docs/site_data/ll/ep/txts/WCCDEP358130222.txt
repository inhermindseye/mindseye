Middx.  Sarah Stay aged abt. 65 Years upon Oath Says She is the Widow of Thos. Stay Deced. Says her said late Husband Served the first part of his Apprentiship with his Father Thomas Stay in Carnaby Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. & there Inlisted himself into his Majesty's Service as her sd. late Husband has informed her & as She verily believes Says her said late Husband never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says She hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Octr. 15th. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Stay
