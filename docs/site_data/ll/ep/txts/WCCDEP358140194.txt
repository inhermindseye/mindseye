Middx.  Eleanor Joy aged abt. 34 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Anthony Joy who is a Soldier in his Majesty's Service with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Welldrick in Yorkshire abt. 5 Yrs. ago by whom she has one Child Thos. aged abt. 17 Months: says her said husband was Born in the Parish of Eskrett in Yorkshire aforesaid and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year and that Thos. Joy her sd. husbands father Rented a farm of £50. a Year in the said Parish of Eskrett for several Years as her said husband has informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true

Sworn this 9th. of Decr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eleanor Joy

Upon the Complaint of Mr R. Wimberley Ovr . J: P:
