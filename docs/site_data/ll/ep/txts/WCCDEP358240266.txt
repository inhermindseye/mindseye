St Clement Danes 28th May 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Sarah Rudge  Widow aged 41 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her late Husband Thos Rudge at the parish Church Church of Saint Mary Newington in the County of Surry on or abt the fourth day of December in or about the year of our Lord 1767 by Banns that She hath been informed and verily believes believes that her said Husband was the legitimate Son of Thomas and Ann Rudge both leg now deceased that the said Thomas Rudge the father of this Examts. said Husband Rented and Resided in an House known by the sign of the Wheatsleaf at the corner of Clements Lane on of Wych Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for a year or upwards at b but this Examinant cannot recollect the certain yearly Rent he paid for the Same And this Examinant further saith that her said as never Husband's father never acquired settlement any subsequent legal Settlement after he quitted the said House to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that She hath been informed and believes her said Husband never obtained any legal Settlement in her his own

one Child settled in Box Mid Bow Middx  right And saith that he died about seventeen years ago and that She this Examinant hath not Since been Married nor done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to gain any legal Settlement otherwise than in Right of her said Husband as aforesd. that She hath issue by her said Husband now living only one Son Named Thomas aged twenty five years four years on the 5th October last who is Settled by Serving his Apprenticeship in the parish of Bow near Stratford in the County of Middlesex

Sworn the 28th day of May 1793 before Me} D Walker

Sarah Rudge 's Mark

Witness Garrett
