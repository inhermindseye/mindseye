Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Alice Dekemell aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that on the 2d. day of January 1763 she Intermarried with Fras. Dekemell her present Husband (who has deserted her) at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn that her said Husband was bound and Apprentice to Messrs. Andertons Watch Makers in East Street Queen Square in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middx for 7 years that he served Six years of his said Apprenticeship that then he Intermarried to this Examt. and had his Indres given up that he never kept any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Febry. 1788 N Bond N. Forster.

her Alice Dekemell Mark
