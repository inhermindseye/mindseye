St. Clement Danes August 22d 1793

Middlesex  and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields  (to wit) Jane Kirk aged twenty five years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant & with Mr Snade in Craven Street in the Strand in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for four years at the annual Wages of Eight Guineas that she quitted that place about three years ago and hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant with for a year together under one hiring Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 22d day of August 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Jane Kirk 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
