Middx  Hanah the Wife of Jno. Butcher aged Eighty Years & upwards on her Oath saith that her sd- Husband abt. 2 Years ago rented a House in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Nineteen Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of Thirty Years or thereabouts that he hath not since he left the sd. House rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband Jno. Butcher is now Poor & not able to maintaine her

Sworne the 28th: Day of June 1741 , before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Hanh. Butcher
