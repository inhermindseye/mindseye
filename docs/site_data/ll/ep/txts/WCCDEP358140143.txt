Middx  Susannah Stretch aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath says She is the Wife of John Stretch who now absconds from her and that she was Married to him at the Fleet London abt. twenty eight Years ago says her said husband Rented a house in Exeter Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx . of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds lived therein upwards of Twelve Years and hath quitted same about four years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 12th. day of July 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Susannah Streeth
