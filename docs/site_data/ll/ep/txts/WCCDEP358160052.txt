Middx  The Examination of Martha Williams  Widw . Aged about 63 Years Liveing in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 15th. Day of May 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. 39 Years ago her late Husband Richd. Williams Rented a House in Portugal Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £36 & lived in the same for the space of 29 Years & there Dyed And this Examint. further saith that after the Death of her sd. Husband she this Examint. Continued to live in & Rent the sd. House for the space of two Years & upwds. And further upon her Oath saith that she hath not since that time Rented any other House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise & gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane .

Martha Williams
