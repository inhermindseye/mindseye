Westmr. 

Frances Molloy  Wido . of John Molloy who dyed abt. 20 months since upon Oath saith she intermarryed with the sd. John Molloy at the Street London on the 29th. Day of June 1746 that her sd. late husband (as he informed her was a Flanderkin and never gained a Settlement in England and that she this Examinant whilst a Singlewoman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Donsval a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly wages of £5 for one Year and upwds. and quitted her sd. Service abt. 13 Yr ago: Since which she has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has one Child by the Sd. John Molloy Michl aged three Yrs & upwds

Sworn Augt. 4th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

Frances Molloy
