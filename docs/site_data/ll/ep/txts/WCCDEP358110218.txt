Middx  Danl- Davis upon Oath Saith he is the Son of Solomon Davis Decd. who was a Dutchman & never gained a Settlement in England & that this Examinant was born in Broad Court in the parish of St. James in Dukes place London & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant nor ever rented Ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn March 27th: 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Danl. Davis
