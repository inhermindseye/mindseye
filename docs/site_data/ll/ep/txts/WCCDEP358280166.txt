Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Amey Gerom aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Twaddell in White Horse Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service about Two years and upwards since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 4th. day of May last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Store Street Hospital which hath since been Christened by the Name of Harriott That one James Scrufus a Waiter at Vauxhall is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said James Scrufus is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 1st. day of July 1788 } Wm Kitchiner

her Amey Gerom Mark

James Scrufus is a Middle Sized Man dark Complexion wears his own Hair hanging in his Neck is about 24 years of Age and Lisps a little
