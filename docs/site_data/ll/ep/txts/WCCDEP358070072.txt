Westmr. to wit Elizth. Swift Aged about forty Three Years on behalf of Elizth. Warner an Infant aged about Nine Months whose Mother Mary Warner now lies dead and the Father gone and left them Says that James Warner Father of the said Elizabeth Warner rented and lived in a House in Richmond Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same and after quitting the same never gained any Subsequent Settlement as Appears by an Examination of the said James Warner taken the 8th. Day of September Last Past Signed by Thos. Kynaston & R: Butler Esqrs.

Sworn this 27th. day of February 1773 before R. Butler 

Elizth. Swift her mark
