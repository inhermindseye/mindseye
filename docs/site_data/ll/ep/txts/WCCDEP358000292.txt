St. Clemt. Danes

Middx and Westmr.}  Mary Humphrys aged 29 years or there abouts upon her Oath saith That she was Married to William Humphris her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andins in the City of Dublin according to the Aites and Ceremonies of the Church of England - And saith that her said Husband previous to his Intermarriage with this Examinant served  his apprenticeship with a Mr. a Shoemaker in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes if that she has one Child Ading by her said Husband aged 9 years or thereabouts that her said Husband died all. a Month ago - that her said Husband never gained a Settlemt. since his Apprenticeship.

Sworn this 18th. day of May 1786 R Butler

Mary Humphrys
