Middlesex ss  William Jackson Maketh oath That divers Years ago he was bound apprentice by Indenture to; and served the whole of his Appreticeship with one Thomas How in Duke Street in the Liberty of the old Artilery Ground in the County of Middlesex  weaver and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that he never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards Nor ever Paid a Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever serves any Parish offices nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his said Apprenticeship And that he is a single and Unmarried Man.

Sworn this 31t: day of July-1766 before  Chris: Scott

William Jackson
