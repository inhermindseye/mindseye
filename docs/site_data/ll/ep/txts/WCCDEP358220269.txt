Westmr.  Clara Evans Aged about fifty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly with one John Felton  Esqr . at Knights bridge in the Parish of Kinsington in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Thirty Eight Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever rented Ten pounds P Annum paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th. day of June 1771 before me R A W Kelynge

Clara Evens
