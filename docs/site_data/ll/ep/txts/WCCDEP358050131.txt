Westmr.  Susa. Willis aged upwards of 26 Years upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she never was married and that on the 6th. Day of Octr. 1745 she was deceased of a female Bastard Child at the house of Mr Stoakes in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx since baptized Mary and that on the 14th. Day of Retry last she was delivered of another female Bastard Child in the house of Mr Batterson in James Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. (since baptized Eliz .)

Sworn Octr. 28th. 1749 before H Fielding

The Mark of Susa Willis
