Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Eleanor Jones aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Barber in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Sixteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of Decr. 1794 St Mathew

her Eleanor Jones mark

Witness B Newman
