Westmr to wit Eleanor Trevor Aged about Seventy Eight Years on his Oath says She is the Widow of Thomas Trevor who has been dead about Six Years and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in White Hart Yard in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields upwards of Eighteen Years and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath Quitted the said House upwards of 14 Years ago and after he Quitted the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th day of Jany 1775 before R Butler W Addington

The Mark of Eleanor Trevor
