Middx  Ann Miller aged about 64 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Thomas Miller who has been Dead about Twenty Years to whom she was Married at the Fleet about 27 Years ago That her said Husband was Born in Cumberland but what part cannot say That he never kept House was an Apprentice or hired Servant to her Knowledge That before her Intermarriage and while Single She this Examinant lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Saml. Roach  at the Lamb Inn near Clements Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds diet & Lodging Quitted the Same about Six Months before her Marriage

Sworn the day of May 1761 Before}
