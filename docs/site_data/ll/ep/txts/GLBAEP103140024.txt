Middlesex ss}  Daniel Farlam maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages with one Mr Caney in Whalebone Court in the Parish of the Holy Trinity near queenhithe in the City of London & Continued in Such Service for the Space of three years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That this Deponent had not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds nor Served any parish office nor paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that he hath a Wife named Martha

Sworn this 26th day of January 1774 before B Camper S aml. Spagg

Daniel Farlam
