Middx  James; Furnival upon Oath Saith he intermarried wth. his Wife Mary at the Fleet London on the 7th. Augt. 1739 and that he was bound an apprentice for Seven Years to Thomas Haws  Turner & after having lived with him about five Years was assigned over to John Pitcha of Turner with whom he lived the last 2 Yrs. of his apprentiship in Clare Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes that he has been out of his time abt. 14 or 15 Years & has not since rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Augt. 8th 1754 : before Fran: Bedwell James Furnivall
