Middx  Ann Robins Aged about Twenty One years On her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship says She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bengees whose wife kept a Boarding School at Kensington in the County of Middlesex for the space of Ten Months or thereabouts when her Master and Mistress removed into Soho Square where this Examinant also Removed and lived with her Master and Mistress there for the Space of Three Months Amounting in the whole to Thirteen Months and Upwards and where this Examinant was Discharged and further says that after quitting her said last mentioned Service She never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry 1781 before J Girdler Abrm Baley

her Ann Robins Mark
