Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Benjamin Haswell aged about 16 years upon his Oath saith that on the 31st. day of May 1787  s he was bound Apprentice to George Rouse of Monmouth Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields for Seven years that after he had served about Twenty Months of his said Apprenticeship his said Master moved into Brills Place Pancras in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex and that he lived with his said Master in Brills Place aforesaid for about Three Months

Sworn this 6th. day of April 1789 R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

her Benjamin Haswell Mark
