Middx  Lydia Chipendale aged upwds. of 21 yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one year rented ten pounds a Yr. or pd. Parish Taxes that her Father John Chipendale now lives in a house of his own of the Yearly Value of £4 or thereabouts in the Parish of Otley in the west Riding of Yorkshire And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is Pregnant wth. Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to becomes Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes (where she now Lodges) she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Thomas Parker Clerk to Mr. Duncomb of Lincoln Inn never Square did be get the said bastard Child or Children or her body the said Thomas Parker having had Carnal knowledge of this

Examinant in the month of May last and at Several other times since at ones of which times he got her with Child of the said bastd. Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that he the said Thomas Parker And no other Person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Decr. 12th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Lydia Chipendale
