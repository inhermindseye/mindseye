Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Hannah Facer aged about 26 years upon her saith that she never was Married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year that she was born at Deptford the parish of Saint Paul at Deptford in the County of where her Father and Mother kept House that she done no Act to gain any other Settlement than Birth And further this Examinant upon her oath saith that on the 3rd day of Octr. last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House a Mr. Waile in Bennett Court  Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been half baptized by the Name Elizabeth That one James Douglass a Journeyman Carver and Gilder who lately lived at Mr. Brow in Little Russell Street Covent Garden is the Father of said Child he having had Carnal knowledge of Examinants Body for these three years last Past and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said James Douglass is only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Novr. 1786 } R Butler

her Hannah Facer mark

Man taken-and he put in Bail to Appear the next General Quarter Sessions of the for the County of Middx Bond filled up & Exted-she has got the Child the father pays her
