Middx  Eleanor Hall aged about 28 Years upon Oath says She never was married That her Father Joshua Hall Rented a House of above Ten Pounds a Year near Kentish Town in the Parish of St. Pancrass in the County of Middlesex and lived therein about fourteen Years as She has been informed & believes Says She has a Child named Richard born a Bastard in the Work- -house of the said Parish of St. Pancrass about two Years since And that She never Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 27th. Day of Octor. 1755 before } Barth: Hammond

The mark of Eleanor Hall
