Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Holmes aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that about Eleven years ago she Intermarried with Richard Holmes her present Husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fileds by whom she hath Three Children living vizt. Elizabeth aged about Eight years Richard aged about five years and James aged about Fourteen Months That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Frith Street in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for about Fifteen Months at the yearly Rent of Forty pounds and paid all Taxes due for the same That he hath quitted said house about five years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of Septr. 1795 } John Collick  John Hatchell

M Holmes
