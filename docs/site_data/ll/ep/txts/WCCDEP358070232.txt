Westmr. ss  Ann Macklin Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with John Macklin at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Eight Years Ago and by whom she hath one Child now living Named Mary Aged upwards of Three Years Says her said Husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Charles Carpue a Silk dyer in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 15th. day of Decr. 1774 before R. Butler

Ann Mcaklin
