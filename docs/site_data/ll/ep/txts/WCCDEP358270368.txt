Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Hawkins aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Yeates in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months at the yearly wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Petree a Painter& Glazier  who lives at No. 18 Houghton Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examts. Body & begotten the said Child or Children of which she is so Pregnant & that the said John Petree is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of July 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Mary Hawkins Mark

Witness AW Burbidge
