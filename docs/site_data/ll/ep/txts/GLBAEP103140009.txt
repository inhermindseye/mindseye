Middlesex ss}  James Price  Singleman maketh oath That he Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of his late Father or Mother deced or their Parents That he this Deponent was (as he has been informed by his said late Mother & which he believes to be true) born in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square within the Liberty of the City of Westmr in the County of Middlesex in a Certain Street there Called Swallow Street And that he this Deponent to his Knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this 2d: day of December-1773 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of James Price
