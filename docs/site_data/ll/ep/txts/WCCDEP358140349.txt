Middx.  Elizabeth Meadows aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Henry Meadows deced and that being a Widow and without Child or Childn. (Except two that had gained Settlements in their own right) she lived a hired Yearly Servant for 4 yrs. with Mrs. Andrews in St. Clement's Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. at the Yearly wages of £4 and hath Quitted said Service upwards of One Year And has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt. for One Year.

Sworn this 16th. of Feby. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Meadows
