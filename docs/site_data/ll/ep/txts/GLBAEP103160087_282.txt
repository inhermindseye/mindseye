Middlesex ss}  Sarah Nichols the Wife of Thomas Nichols maketh oath that her Said husband is lately gone away from her to places unknown & left her in a Ncessitous Condition That her Said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprentice ship with his Father Thomas Nichols in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Cordwainer & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there & that to her Knowledge or belief he hath not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement & that she this Deponent was lawfully married to her Said husband in the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch Middlesex

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Sarah Nichols
