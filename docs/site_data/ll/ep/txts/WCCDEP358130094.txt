Middx  Mary Batfield Aged about Twenty four Years upon Oath Says She never was married That She Lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr. Geo: Shaw at the King's Arms in Chapel Street Red Lyon Square in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Bars in the County of Middx for Three years and upwards At the Yearly Wages of £3. and hath quitted her said Service upwards of Four years and hath not since Lived with any Person as a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year And farther upon her Voluntary Oath Says She is now Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes where she now Lodges, She being Poor and unable to Maintain the Same when Born, And that One John Rafes a Taylor Lodging at Mr. Geo: Marcers in New Court Cary Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Did begett the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her Body The sd. John Rafes having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the Month of March or April last and at several other times since in his the sd. John Rafes Appartmt. in New Court aforesd. At One of wch. times he get her wth. Child of the Child or Children which she is now Pregnant And that the sd. John Rafes and no other Person is the true and Real Father thereof.

Sworn the 13th. Day of Decr. 1755 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Mary Batfield
