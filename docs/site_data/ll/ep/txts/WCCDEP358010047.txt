Saint Clement Danes

Sarah Perry  Single woman Aged 40. or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith, That she never was married That she has Six illegitimate Children living Vizt. Thomas aged 19 years or thereabout born upon Saffron Hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill George aged 15 years or thereabouts born in Cockpit Court Shoe Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Bars , William aged Nine years born at the House of a Mr. Lumby in Apollo Court in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London , David aged Seven years born at the House of a Mr. Paine in Bell yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx Mary aged four years, born at the House of a Mr. Anderson in Great Shire Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx and Henry aged Five Months born at the House of a Mr. Bilby in Sawyers Yard in the said parish, Saith that they are the Children of Henry Perry . who has born dead Absconded and gone into Wales. And further saith that she is Poor and unable to maintain herself and Children

Sworn this 12 Janry. 1787 . before Mr: Wright Jno Machin

her Sarah Perry Mark
