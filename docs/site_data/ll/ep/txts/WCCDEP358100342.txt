Middlesex and Westminster}  C passd. to St. Mary Le Bone to wit James Piddock Aged about Thirty four Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired yearly Servant with Sir Ralph Milbank about Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Five Guineas besides Cloaths Diet and Lodging vizt. the first Six Months in Bond Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square and the last Twelve Months in Harley Street in Cavendish Square in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex where his said Master Removed to and was there discharged And after quitting his said Masters Service he never lived as a hired yearly Servant nor has he gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 19th day of January 1785 G Mercer Jno. Wright

his James Piddock mark
