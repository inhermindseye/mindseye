Westmr.  Ann Morton aged about 50 Years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Alder at the Horse and Groom in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Years ago and hath not since she quitted same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th day of December 1769 before me} R. Butler

Mark Ann Morton
