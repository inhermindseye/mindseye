Middx  Mary the Wife of Jno- Drew passed from the Borough of Daventree in the County of Northton Upon her Oath saith that abt. 5 Years ago she Intermarried with her sd. Husband at the Parish of St. Mary in Dublin that he was bred up to the Sea that he never rented any House pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt- for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place as this Examinants sd Husband informed her & beleives that he his now a Sailor in the Burford Man of War in the West Indies & being left with one Child (to witt) Jno. aged 1 Yr- & 9 Months & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Child That her sd. Husband Inform'd her that he was borne in St. Clemts-

Sworne the 30th- of June 1741 before}

The Mark of Mary Drew
