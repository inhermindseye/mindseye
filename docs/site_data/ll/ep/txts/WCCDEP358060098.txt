Middx  Edwd. Dry upon Oath saith that on or about the 27th. Day of August 1735 he intermarried with his prest. wife Mary Dry in the Parish Church of St. Brides Londn . Says he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. John Dry a Sawyer in New Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that his sd. Apprentiship expired about 15 Years since and this Examinant never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his Knowledge or beleif and that he hath one Child by his sd. Wife (viz) John aged near 3 months Says a Certificate was granted from the above sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes to this Examint. & his Wife in the month of Octr. 1744 directed to the Parish Officers of Guildford

Sworn May 5th. 1751 before  Tho Lane .

Edward Dry
