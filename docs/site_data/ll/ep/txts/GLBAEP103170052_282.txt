Middlesex ss}  John Moses maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice & Served the Whole thereof with John Sherrott a cabinet maker in the parish of Saint George in the County of Middlesex & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship

Sworn this 31. day of January 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

John Staser
