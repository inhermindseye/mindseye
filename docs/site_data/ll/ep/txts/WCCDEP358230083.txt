Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Snead aged about 34 years upon her Oath Saith that on or about the 4th day of June 1798 she intermarried with John Snead at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Whitechapel by whom she hath one Child living viz Susannah aged about 12 Weeks that her said Husband Rented part of a House No 65 High Holborn in the parish of Saintt George Bloomsbury for upwards of 2 months at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds that he hath left the said Apartments aboutt 12 Months, since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any other Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief And this Examinant further Saith that on the 6th day of the present Month her said Husband left her since which time she hath not seen or heard any thing of him

Sworn this 15th day of August 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

her Susannah Snead Mark

Witness B Newman
