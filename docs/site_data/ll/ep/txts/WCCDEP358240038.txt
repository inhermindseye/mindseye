St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  (St CD)  (to wit) John Capeland Aged fifty five years or thereabouts Upon his Oath saith that he Married with his wife Sarah whose maidename was Mayhew at Christ  Church Spittalfield about 28 Years ago (by Banns) that since their Marriage hath in Deponent resided in & rented a House called the Fish in Strand Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes about One year & nine Months that he quitted the same about Eighteen Twenty two years ago and since hath not (by any way or means whatever) acquired any other legal Settlement And that he is Poor and (at present) unable to support himself & Wife

Sworn the 26th July 1791 . before Me} Wm Kitchiner

John Copeland Mark
