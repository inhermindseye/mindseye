Middx & Westmr.}  Mary May aged upwards of 24 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Clements a farmer in the Parish of Great St. Helen's in the Town of Abingdon in the County of Berks for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Thirty Shillings and hath not since she quitted said Service Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says, she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex where she now lodges, she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born, says that Mr. Richard Digory who keeps the Green Man and French horn in Bow Street near Covent Garden did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body with which she is now pregnant the said Richard Digory having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body on or about the 14th, day of May 1766, and at one other time afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Richard Digory and no other person did beget the same.

Sworn the 7th. Novr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary May
