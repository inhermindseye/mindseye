Westmr.  Mary Macdanell on behalf of Joseph Lawrance an Infant aged upwds. of Seven Years upon Oath saith that Joseph Lawrance the Father and Mary Lawrance the mother of the said Infant are both dead and that about two Years ago to the best of this Examints. Remembrance the sd. Mary Lawrance the mother at that time being a Widow Rented a house in Tee Lane in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn London of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards and Lived in the same upwards of Six months as this Examinant was Informed and believes Says after the said Mary Lawrance Quitted the said house in Tee Lane she never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to the best of this Examints. knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 16th 1749 before H Frilding

The mark of Mary Macdanel
