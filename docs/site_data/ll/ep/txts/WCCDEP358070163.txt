Westmr to wit Elizabeth Bridgen Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Lester the Matron at the foundling Hospital in Lambs Conduit Fields in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 12th. day of January 1774 R Butler Sampn. Wright

Bridgen
