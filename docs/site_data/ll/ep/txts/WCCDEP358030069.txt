Middx  Susanh. Green  Wido . of Joseph Green upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Joseph Green at the Fleet London about 9 Yrs. since Says he hath been dead about 5 Yrs. Says her sd. late Husband Served abt. 2 Yrs: the latter Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Turner a Hatter in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Says her sd. late Husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place Says she hath not since his Death Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Child named Ann aged 7 Yrs. & upwards

Sworn Decr. 16: 1745 before W Jones

The Mark of Susa. Green
