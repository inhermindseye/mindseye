Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Tiley aged about 52 years upon his Oath saith that about 13 years ago he Intermarried with Susannah his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Overys That he this Deponent Rented a first floor unfurnished at No. 4 Water Street Arundel Street for in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Nine Months at the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas that he hath quitted said Apartments about Three years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of July 1799 before me } Saml. Edwards

John Tiley

H Day
