Middx  Joseph Noble upon his Oath saith that he lived in & rented a house of Ten pounds a Year in Knights Court in the Backside of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Two Years or thereabouts & left the same near Six Yrs. since Says he hath not since he quitted sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 7th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

Josepeh Noble
