Westmr.  Ann Hancell Aged about 28 Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived as hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Archer who Lodged with Mr. Payne on Camberwell Green in the parish of Camberwell in the County of Surry Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of three pounds hath quitted said Service about Eight Months and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 4th of January 1765 Before} Thos: Balack  Thos Kynaston

Ann Hancell
