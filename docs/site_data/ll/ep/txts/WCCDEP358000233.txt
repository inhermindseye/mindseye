Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Mr. Holmes aged 40 Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he was bound & apprenticeship was Married to Margt . his late Wife at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes that he has 2 Children living by his said Wife Oath provided for That since his Intermarriage he Rented and lived in a Houses in Crown Court Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for upwards of 2 Years at the Yearly Rent of 14 Pounds hath quitted the same about 16 years ago since which time he hath not done any act matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d day of Febry. 1786 before  Wm Hitchiner

Will Holmes
