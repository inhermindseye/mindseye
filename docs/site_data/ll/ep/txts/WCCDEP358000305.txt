Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr. } to wit Alice Metcalfe aged about 44 years upon her oath saith that about 23 years ago she, Intermarried with Richard Metcalfe her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Magdalen and who has been dead about Six years that she hath four Children living by her said Husband Vizt. Thomas aged about 19 Years  Killy aged 18 Years or thereabouts both Provided for Wm. aged 15 years or thereabouts and Alice aged about 12 years both unprovided for that her said late Husband  before his Death rented and lived in a House in Duke Street Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for 15 Months or thereabouts at the yearly rent of 26 Guineas hath quitted said House about 13 years ago since which time her said late Husband did no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23rd. day of June 1786  Sampn Wright N Bond
