Middx 

Ann Hood aged abt. 28 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 5 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Broughton at Mrs. Guys opposite Arandel Street in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £3 for two Years or thereabts. and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year pd Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn 17th. Novr. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Ann Hood
