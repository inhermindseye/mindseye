Middx  Nics. Jones upon Oath saith he was married to his Wife Eliz Jones at the Fleet London near 20 years ago that he Served Seven years apprentiship with John Grace a Cooper next the two Brewers in Wych Street in the parish of St Clement Danes and that he never Rented ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes nor lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Decr 21st 1754 before} Barth: Hammond 

Nicholas Jones
