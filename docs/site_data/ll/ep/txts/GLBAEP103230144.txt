Middlesex ss  Vertue Mully the wife of James Mully who is at present Sick maketh oath that about twenty Seven years ago her said husband lived in and rented a house at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds in Little Moor fields in the Parish of St Giles without Cripplegate in the City of London & resided thereon for the Space of one year and upwards That her said husband has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief And that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 19. day of May 1788 before  R Davies  Robt Smith

Vertue Mully
