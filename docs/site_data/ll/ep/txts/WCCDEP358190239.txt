Middx  Elizabeth Noble aged about 40 years removed from the Hamlet of Poplar and Blackwall in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Robert Noble who has been Dead about Seven Years with whom she intermarried at St. Gregory's Church by St. Paul London the 5th. July 1743 Says her said Husband was bound Apprentice for Seven years to John Exley a Shoemaker near St. Clement's Church in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and with him there served about Two or Three years when his Master ran away Says her said Husband was not assigned over to any Person never rented a House or lived a yearly hired Servant for one whole year also says that since his Death she this Examinant hath not rented any House paid Land Tax or Poor Rate or been a hired yearly Servant

Sworn the 27th. day of April 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Noble
