Middlesex ss}  Joseph Fairbrother maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to Thomas Pink of the Minories in the City of London  Joiner and Served the whole of his Apprentice ship there That he never rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt. Subsequent to his said Apprenticeship

Sworn this 11. day of January 1788 before  R Davies

Josh Fairbrother
