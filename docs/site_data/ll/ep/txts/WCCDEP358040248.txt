Westmr. 

Jane Hart widow of Samuel Hart who dyed about 21 Yrs. ago upon Oath saith she Intermarryed with the sd. Samuel Hart at Lincolns Inn Chapel about 34 Yrs. Since and that her sd. late husband lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds in May pole Allay in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of 20 Years & there dyed and this Examinant continued to live in and rents the same house upwds. of 10 Yrs. after his Decease and hath not since she quitted it rented ten pounds a Years paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemts. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Sepr. 5th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Jane Hart
