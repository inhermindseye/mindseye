Middx  Sarah Stezaker wife of James Stezaker (gone from her) upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Jas. Stezaker at the fleet London on the 24th. Day of Janry 1741 by whom she hath two Childn. (viz) Frances aged upwds. of 4 Yrs. and James aged three & Weeks says the sd. Jas. Stezaker Served his Apprentiship with Joseph Burgess in the Pigmarket in the Parish of Newbury in the County of Berks and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Prot. for one Year or otherwise gained Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 11st. 1749 before} Tho Lane

Sarah Stezaker
