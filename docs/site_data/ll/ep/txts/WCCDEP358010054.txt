Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  B to wit Elizth. Earth aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith that about 8 years ago she went to live as a hired year by Servant with lady Franks in Newman Street Oxford Road in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middx for 18 Mos. or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted said Service about 6 years ago since which time she has done no act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. Janry. 1787  Philip Dyer D Walker

her Elizth. Earth Mark
