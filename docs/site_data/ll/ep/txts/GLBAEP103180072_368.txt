Middlesex ss  Martha Lacey the wife of William Lacey maketh oath that her Said husband who is now Confined as a Prisoner for debt in Whitechapel Goal lately rented a house at the yearly Rent of Twenty five pounds in Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & thereby gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish & has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She has two Children namely Ann Aged about eight Years & James  Aged about Six Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to Whom She this Deponent was lawfully married

Sworn this 21 day of October 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

Martha Lacy
