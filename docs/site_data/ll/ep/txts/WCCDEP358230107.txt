Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Vent who is Ill Maketh Oath and saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true That she said Mary Vent Intermarried with John Veart her late husband who has been dead about Ten Years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said late Husband rented and lived in a house in Angel Court in the Parish of  Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster for two years on thereabouts at the yearly rent of twelve Pounds that after he quitted said house he never rented any house tenement or lodging often Pounds a year paid Taxes as done any Act matter on thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Exami Knowledge information or belief

Sworn this 8th day of Jany 1800 before us  Saml. Edwards G Goodenough

B Newman
