Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  (to wit) Sarah Linnell aged thirty Nine years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant of Mr. The amans in Little Bill Alley in Coleman Street in the parish Saint Stephen Coleman Street of the yearly Wages of five pounds for better than two years, that she quitted his Service about Sixteen years ago and since then has not lived for one year in any one place as an hired yearly Servant, rented any House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten pounds or done any Act whereby to acquire any Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 8th Day of Jany 1791 . before}

Sarah Linnell
