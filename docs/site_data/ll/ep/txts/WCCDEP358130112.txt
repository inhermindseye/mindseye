Middx  Charles Smith upon Oath says he intermarried with his Wife Elizabeth Smith upwards of Twenty two Years ago by whom he has one Child Vizt. Ann aged about fifteen years who never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant And that he this Examinant Served seven years Apprenticeship with, Mr. Weaver a Baker near the Opera House in the Haymarket in the Parish of St. James  Westminster & never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 26th. Day of Janry. 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane .

Charles Smith
