Middx  George Hind Maketh Oath and Saith that Richard Quintary Served all his apprentish to Mr. Edwards a pipe Maker in the parish of Charlborough in the County of Oxford and gained no other Settlement to this Deponents knowledge And that the said Richard Quintary is now dead and that his widow  Grace is now living and has gained no Settlemt Since in her own Right to this depts knowledge

Sworn 15th. Augt 1746 before Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

George Hinde
