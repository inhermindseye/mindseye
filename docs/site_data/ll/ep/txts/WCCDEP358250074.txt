Saint Clement Danes Octr. 17th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields  Isabella Simpson  Singlewoman aged Thirty Seven years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Sky who kept an Haberdashers Shop in Saint Martin's Lane  in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields within the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for upwards of one year and two Months at the yearly Wages of Ten Guineas That she quitted her Service about Fifteen years ago and hath not since obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief That she this Deponent in poo and is in great distress and unable to maintain herself.

Sworn the 17 day of October 1792 Before Sampn Wright M Addington

her Isabella Simpson Mark

Edwd Burbidge
