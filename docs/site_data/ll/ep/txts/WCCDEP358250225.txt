Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on behalf of Mary Reeves  Singlewoman Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Mary Reeves lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Wilkinson in Poppins Court  fleet Street in the Parish of St Brides alias Bridget in the City of London for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five pounds and that after she quitted said Service which is about Six Months ago she has never to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information or belief done any Act Matter or thing whereby she Might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry 1796 N Bond W Kinnard

B Newman
