St. Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster} (to wit) Mary Haines Aged 35 years or thereabouts Maketh Oath and saith that she this Examinant was Married at the parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty to her Husband Saml Haines about twelve years ago that her said Husband was bound by Indentures of Apprenticeship to Mr. Pavey of Vere Street in the said parish of St. Clement Danes that he regularly served his said Apprenticeship and lodged the last Six Months thereof at Mr.Tinklesin Vere Street aforesaid, that he hath never to the best of her Knowledge or belief gained any Subsequent settlement, that her said Husband left her with & her two Infant Children Vizt. Ann Aged about five years, and Samuel Aged about three years, with her on the 21st. October last, that her said Children are now with this Examinants Mother Mary Greaves in Digburth in the Town of Birmingham who maintains them that she is Now Pregnant with Child by her said Husband and unable to support herself during the time of her lyy lying in the Expence thereof

Sworn before me the 3d. May 1791 R. Butler

Mary Haines 's Mark
