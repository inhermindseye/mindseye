Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charlotte Daniel aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith That she Intermarried with William Daniel her present Husband (who has deserted her) at the Parish Church of Saint Augustine in the City of Bristol on or about the 27th.. day of January 1793 by whom She hath Two Children living Vizt. Charles aged about Three Years and Esther aged about Seven Months That her said Husband informed this Examinant that he was born at a Place called Stoke Sub Hamdond in the Parish of Stoke Sub Hamdond in the County of Somerset and that he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any other settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of Novr. 1797 before us} M Addington 

her Charlotte Daniel Mark
