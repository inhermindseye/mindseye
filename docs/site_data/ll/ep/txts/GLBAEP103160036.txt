Middlesex ss}  Robert Pottenger maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to his Father Richard Potenger of Grace Church Street London  Ironmonger for Seven years & Served about three Months thereof with his Said Father and then this Deponent was Assigned & turned over to Richard Gray of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Ironmonger where he Served the residue of his Said Apprenticeship & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant by the year or rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 17. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

Robert Pottinger
