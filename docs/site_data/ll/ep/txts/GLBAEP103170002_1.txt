Middlesex ss}  Thomas Wilson maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to Thomas Waight & Served the Whole thereof with him in Eastsmithfield in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County in the Trade of a Cabinet maker & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent never rented a house of the yearly Rent or value of Ten pounds nor ever Served any parish office nor ever paid any Poore Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship And That he hath a Wife named Elizabeth Wilson to whom he was married at the Fleet upwards of twenty three years ago.

Sworn this. 31. day of August 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

Thos. Wilson
