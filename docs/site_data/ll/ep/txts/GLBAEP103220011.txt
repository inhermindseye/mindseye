Middlesex  Angus Man maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by the Parish Officers of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County to William Moore of Queen Street in the Sd Parish Smith & after having Served his Said Master there for about the Space of five Years he was dischargd from his Said Apprenticeship by the Magistrates And this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any other Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement Since his Sd Apprenticeship

Sworn this fourth day of January 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Angus Man
