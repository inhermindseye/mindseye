Middx.  Bridget Cowley aged abt. 35 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with James Fitzgerald at the Elephant & Castle without Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath quitted said Service abt. twelve Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Yr.

Sworn this 24th day of Novr. 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Bridget Cowley
