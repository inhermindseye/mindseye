Westmr.  John Cross aged about 23 Years Upon Oath says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Yardley a Carver & Gilder in Fitches Court Foster Lane in the Parish of St. Olave Silver Street London and served two Years of his said Apprentice- ship and then his said Master died, says he was Assigned over to Mr. Cooper of Fitches Court aforesaid Carver and Gilder and continued with him till the expiration of the said Indenture of Apprenticeship And never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 22nd, April 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of John Cross
