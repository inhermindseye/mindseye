Middx  Ann Cherrington upon Oath saith she never was married and that on the 8th. Day of June last she was delivered of a male bastard Child in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx which was baptized Thomas and is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the Same and that Thomas Green late a Clerk to Mr Strut of Breams buildings Chancery Lane did beget the sd. Male bastard Child on her body the said Thos. Green having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant at a Publick house in Islington in the month of Septr. last and at sevl. other times since at one of which time he got her with Child of the sd. male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that he the sd. Thomas Green & no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Septr. 16th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Ann Cherrington
