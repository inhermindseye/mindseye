Middx  Jane Thomas upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Thomas who dyed abt. 13 Yrs. ago & with whom she Intermarried at Llanblythian Church in Glamorganshire in the Year 1719 Says her sd. husband rented the Swan Alehouse at the Corner of Whitehorse Yard for in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of £14 and lived therein three Years & upwards and quitted the sd. house upwds. of 12 Years since Says neither her sd. late husband nor this Examint. since his Decease have since they Quitted the above- mention'd house rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn June 1st 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell Jane Thomas
