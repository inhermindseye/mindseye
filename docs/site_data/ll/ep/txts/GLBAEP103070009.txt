Middx ss The Examination of Eleanor Bowen  Singlewoman Taken on oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the sd. County of Middx (she who not is of the quorum) residing in or Next to the limits of the parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd. County this 30th day of August 1750 .

This Examinant upon her Oath Saith that about the Month of April one thousand Seven hundred and forty Nine Nathaniel Wheelwright  Esqr . who then lodged at the house of Mrs Mary Masters in the City of Bath where this Examinant was then a Servant did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at the sd. Mrs Masters's House and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body Several times Whereby she Conceived With Child of a Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered at the house of Mr Gabriel Beck in Angell Alley in ye sd. parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate on the Sixth day of February last Which said that Bastard Child hath been Baptised by the Name of Daniel Wheelwright and hath been baptised now is and Still is likely to be Chargeable to the sd Parish of St. Botolph Without Aldgate this Examinant being poor and Not able to provide the Same And this Examinant farther Saith that the sd. Nathaniel Wheelwright is the true and only father of ye sd. Male Bastard Child And that this Examination is Voluntary and that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever.

Sworn this 30- day of August 1750 before us  Saml Gower  Richd: Ricards

The Mark of Eleanor Bowen
