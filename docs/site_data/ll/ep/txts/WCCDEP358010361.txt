Middx (ss)  Janett Cartwright aged twenty Six Years  thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married that she   knows of her over Knowledge when she was born that she was brot up by Mr. Durham a Book seller in Cortirpur Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the field in the County of Middlesex by when she was put out to Mr Murdor of that he House Court in saint Janes Street in the Parish of Saint Janes within the Liberty of Westminster with whom she lived for abouts Sixteen Months, and this Deponent further Saith that since she quitted the said Mrs. Murdor she has not lived as an hired yearly Servant for one year with any persons person whom soever an done any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of

her this Examinants Knowledge Information and belief she may have gained or acquired any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22th. Septr 1790 before me Sampr. Wright Bealing

Janet Cartwrite
