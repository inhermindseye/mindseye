Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Hill  Widow aged 78 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Daniel Hill her late Husband at the Fleet London in or about the year 1741 that he has been dead about Twenty one years that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Water Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Ten years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that he died in the said house and that this Examinant kept the same for Twenty years after and quitted the Renting the said house about fifteen Months since which time she has been a Lodger in the said house

Sworn this 22d. day of Janry. 1790 R Butler

her Mary Hill Mark
