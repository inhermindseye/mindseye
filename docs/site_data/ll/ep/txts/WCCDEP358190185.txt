Middx  Catherine Dowden aged about 24 Years upon Oath says she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Hulks Milkwoman in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Quitted the Same about Nine Months and hath not since rented any House or paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate.

Sworn the 23d. day of Septemr: 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell 

Catherin Dowden
