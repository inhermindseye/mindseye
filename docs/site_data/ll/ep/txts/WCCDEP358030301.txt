Middx 

Mary Jones  wife of Joshua Jones ( abroad in his Majestys Service upon Oath Says she intermarried with thesd. Joshua Jones in the Minster at Hereford abt. 22 Years since to her best Remembrance Says she can't give any Accot. of the Settlemt. of her sd. Husband other than that he hath informed her he was born in the Parish of Chiddoch in the County of Hereford & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served a Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge belief.

Sworn Janry 4th. 1746 before  Thos. Bardus

The Mark of Mary Jones
