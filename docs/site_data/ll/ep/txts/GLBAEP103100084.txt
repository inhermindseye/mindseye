Middlesex ss}  Samuel Johnson a Singleman maketh oath That he well knew one Elizabeth Hyam late of the Precinct of St. Catherine in the County of Middx deced And this Depont Saith that he this Depont. and the said Elizabeth Hyam Cohabited together as Man and wife for divers years in the Parish of Waltham  Abbey in the County of Essex (although in truth they were never Married) And that du Such their Cohabitation in the Same Parish She the Said Elizabeth Hyam has two Children born of her Body then to wit Mary aged about Thirteen Years and Elizabeth Aged about twelve years both of whom were baptized & registred in the Said Parish by the Surname of Johnson and have not yet done any Act to gain a Settlement in the own right. And this Depont. further Saith that he and Said Elizabeth Hyam afterwards left the Said Parish and Continued to Cohabit togethr. as aforesaid And that during Such last Cohabitation She the Said Elizabeth Hyam her Another Child born of her Body in Lower Eastsmith field in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County of Middx to wit Sarah Aged about Six years who was baptized; & registered by the Surname of Johnson the Collegiate Church of the Said Precinct of St Catherine And this Depont. furthr. Saith That the Said three Children are illegitimate (as this Depont apprenhends) their Said late Mother never having been Married to this Depont. or to a other Person to this Deponts. knowledge or belief; And that are all become Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate .

Sworn this 3d: day of March. 1769 before  Chris: Scott  Dd. Wilmot

The Mark of Samuel Johnson
