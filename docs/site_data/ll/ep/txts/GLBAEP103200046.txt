Middlesex ss}  Isabella Campbell  Singlewoman maketh oath That sometime ago she was hired lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by & with John Chambers  Victualler in Maudlins Rents in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in such service under such hiring for the space of one year & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servt for a year elsewhere nor was ever Married nor has She done any Act to gain ansr settlemt

Sworn this 23d day of August 1783 before J Hawley

The Mark of Isabella Campbell
