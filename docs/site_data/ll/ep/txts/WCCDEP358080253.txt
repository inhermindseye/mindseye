Middx to wit William Payne Aged about forty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Anns Black Fryars about Sixteen Years ago and by whom he hath one Child living namely William aged upwards of fourteen Years who now lives with this Examinant And this Examinant further Says that his said Wife left this Examinant about Eight years ago and went and Cohabited with one Thomas Child a Staymaker and by whom she hath three Children namely Ann Aged upwards of five Years Elizabeth Aged about Three Years and Thomas Aged about Two years as this Examinant is Informed and verily believes to be true And this Examinant further Says that before his Intermarriage with his said Wife he lived as a Covenanted Servant with one Robert Dacore a Taylor for three years or thereabouts the last year of which said Services was in the Parish of St. Anns Aldersgate London And further Says that it was Agreed between this Examinant and his said Master that this Examinant was to have Meat Drink Washing Lodging & Pockett Money and which this Examinant had accordingly and after he left his said Masters Service he never Rented House said Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of June 1778 before  Abrm. Baley  John Cox .

Willm. Payne
