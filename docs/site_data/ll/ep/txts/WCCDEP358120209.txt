John Golden aged 29 Years upon Oath saith he served Seven Years apprentiship with Mr Thos. Scriven a Painter and Glazier in Knightsbridge in the parrish of St Margaret in the City of Westmr. & that he never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one Year

Sworn Octr. 5th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

John Golden
