Middx  Robt. Mitchell upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Sarah on the 1st. Day of May 1736 at the Parish Church of St. Pauls Covent Garden by whom he hath two Child (to wit) Sarah aged about 9 Yrs. & Eliz: aged about 6 Years Says he lived in & rented a house over agt. Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  of the Yearly Rent of £32. for the Space of three Years & hath left same about 7 Years Says he hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 4th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

Robt. Mitchell
