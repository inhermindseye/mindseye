Middx  Sarah Thompson upon Oath saith she is the wife of John Thompson (who at present absconds from her) are by whom she hath one Child John aged about One Year that her sd husband has informed her he Served his Apprentiship with a Smith in Briersley in the County of York and never Rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year and that she has one Child John aged one year

Sworn 19th. Octr. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Thompson
