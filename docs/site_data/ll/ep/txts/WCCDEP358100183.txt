Middlesex to wit Lydia Lafortune aged about 56 years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Anthony Lafortune deceased with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of 24 years ago & who died about 8 years ago & by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said late Husband Rented & lived in a House in Fetter Lane in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London for two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £30. hath quitted said House about Ten years ago since which time she never gained any or Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Febry.. 84  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

her Lydia Laffortune mark
