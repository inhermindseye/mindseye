Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Bounds aged about 47 years now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bray in Powyss Place in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Four Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about Seven years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Janry. 1798 before us} R. Ford John Staples

her Elizabeth Bounds Mark
