Westmr.  Elizabeth Singer Aged about Twenty six Years, upon Oath says she is the Widow of Robert Singer who has been dead about Four Months, by whom she has two Children (to wit) Sarah aged about Two Years and Elizabeth aged about Seven Weeks; Says her said late Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship to Mr. Addesley a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster , and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 9th, day of August 1768 , before} Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Elizth. Singer
