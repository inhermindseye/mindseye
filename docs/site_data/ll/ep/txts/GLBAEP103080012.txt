Middx ss  John Pratt Maketh oath that he duly served the last two years of his Apprenticeship with Mr Thomas Hollister a Taylor in Creed Lane in the Parish of st. Gregory in the City of London And by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt there And saith that he has not since rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards nor been a hired servant by the year nor served any Parish officers nor done any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlement to this Depont. knowledge or belief And that he hath a wife Named Dorothy

Sworn this 1t day of June 1757 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris Scott

John Pratt
