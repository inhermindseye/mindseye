Middlesex ss}  Jane Anderson  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Mother deced has informed her & which She believes to be true that this Deponts. late Father William Anderson deced was bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with this Deponts grand far in that part of West Smithfield which is in the parish of St Sepulchre withr Newgate in the City of London & that this Deponts sd Father also rented a house in the same parish & was charged to & paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the Same & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement for himself elsewhere nor did this Deponents mother after his death to this Deponts knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That she this Depont was never an apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement for herself That she hath a child named Ann Williams aged about three years & four months which was born a Bastard in the said parish of St Sepulchre

Sworn this 29 day of March 1777 before} Jasper Clarkes  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Jane Anderson
