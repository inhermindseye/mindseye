Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Spincks  Widw . aged abt. Seventy Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 5th. day of Febry. 1738 Who upon her Oath saith that about 20 Years ago her late Husband Jno. Spincks Rented a House in New Court in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £20 & upwds. and lived in the same abt. four Months & there Dyed And further upon her Oath saith that since the Death of her sd. Husband she this Examinant hath never Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & that she is now Poor & Antient and not able to maintaine herselfe.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Chas. Egerton

The Mark of Elizth. Spincks
