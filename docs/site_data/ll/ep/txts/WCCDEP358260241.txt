Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Harris aged about 78 years now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upon her Oath saith that about 46 years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Harris her late Husband who has been dead about Thirty years at the Parish Church of Christ Church in Surry That her said late Husband was born at Westmorland as she believes but in what Parish she does not know That he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she this Deponent before her Intermarriage with her said late Husband and whilst she was a singlewoman she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Woodward in Forgate Street the City of Worcester in the Parish of Saint Nicholas in the said City of Worcester for Fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that after she quitted said Service she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thig whereby she might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Janry. 1798 before us } M Addington Edwd: Naish

her Ann Harris mark

Witness B Newman
