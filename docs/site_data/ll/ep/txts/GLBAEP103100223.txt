Middlesex ss  Margaret Holt the widow of Henry Holt  mariner deced maketh oath That her Parents were Natives of Ireland but their Place of Settlement she can't at present recollect or set forth That many years ago She was lawfully Married to her Said husband who sometimes informed her that he was born in Liverpoole in England and Sometimes Said that he was born in Ireland but the place where he never informed her That he Served his apprenticeship with a Seafaring Person but what his name was he never informed her That to this Depont. knowledge information or belief her Said late husband never rented a house of the yearly Rent or value of Ten Pounds nor ever served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right Save what he might Acquire by his Said apprenticeship That She this Depont. was born in a place Called Castle Dock near Dublin in Ireland That She never was an apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what she might Acquire by her Said Marriage.

Sworn this day of 1770 before}
