Middlesex to wit William Pritchard Aged about Years on his Oath says she Intermaried to Sarah his Wife at Parish Church of in the County of about Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children now living namely William Aged about Seven Years and Sarah Aged about Three Years. And further says that he is now a Private Soldier in the Western Regiment of Middlesex Militia that his said Wife is Poor and unable to maintain the same and therefore becomes entitled to such releif as by the Act of the 19th. of Geo: the third Chap: 71: Sec 2d.

Sworn this 21st. day of April 1780 . before}
