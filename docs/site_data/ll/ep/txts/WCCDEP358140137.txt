Middx.  Mary Boul aged abt. 23 Years upon Oath says she never was married says she lived a hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Gilkennet a Jeweller in the Strand near Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and Quitted her said Service on or about the 1st. day of May 1758. and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes She being poor & unable to Maintain the same when born Says that Joseph Hedge a Jeweller had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the house of the said Mr. Gilkennet some time in the beginning of the Month of Decr. 1757. and at several other Times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the said Jos: Hodge and no other person did beget the same

Sworn this 28th. June 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Boul
