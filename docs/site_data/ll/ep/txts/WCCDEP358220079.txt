Middx & Westmr.}  Elizth. Dunn Aged about Sixty Eight Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Richard Dunn who has been dead about Twenty Eight Years Says her said Husband whilst living Rented and lived in a house the Corner of Phoenix Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds but how long he lived therein she cannot at this Distance of time recollect Says her said late Husband and this Examinant were both Passed upon the said Parish of Saint Giles and were in the Workhouse there sometime and where her said Husband died Says that after her said Husbands Death she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly servant for one year

Sworn this 2d. day of Novr. 1769 before Us R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Elizth. Dunn mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
