Middx  Elizabeth Wiltshire Aged about Forty Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wm. Weekes a Taylor in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted said Service about Nine Years & hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the Day of 1765 Before}

The Mark of Elizth. Wiltshire
