Middx  Sarah Olfay aged about 15 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she never was married nor ever rented Ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one year And that she Served three years of her Apprentiship wth. Mrs. Banworth in Rose Lane  Limehouse in the parish of St. Ann at Limehouse in the County of Middx at the End of which time her Mistress ran away from her & she never was assigned over to any person 

Sworn Novr. 23d. 1752 before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Olfay
