Middlesex  Ann Street Aged about Twenty Nine Year on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Nichols a Dancing Master in the Old Change Cheap side in the parish of St. Faiths  London upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Six Weeks ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement and further says that on the 11th. day of August last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Wm. Clarke in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and is likely to become Chargable to the sd. last mentioned Parish and further says that one George Charles Seymour an Apprentice to her sd. late Master is the Father there of he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her said Master sometime in the Month of November last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said George Charles Seymour and no other person did beget the some and he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 26th. day of August 1784 before  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gilbert

her Ann Street Mark
