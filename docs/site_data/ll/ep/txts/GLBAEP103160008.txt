Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Leach  Singlewoman maketh oath That she has been informed by her late Father John Leach & which information she believes to be true that her said Father was hired & lived as a hired servant for divers Years by & with one Madam Fill in Bedford Row in the Liberty above the Bars in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middx & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That her Said Father to her knowledge or belief did not afterwards rent a house of Ten Pounds a Year or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement That she this Deponent never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 6th- day of February 1776 before  Geo. C Smith  Wm.. Blackmore

Elizth Leach
