Middx  Wm. Clarke Aged about thirty Four Years removed from the Parish of Avely in the County of Essex by Order Dated the 7th Day of May 1763 Upon Oath Says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Henry Pullen a Butcher in Clare Markett in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx and that the said Mr Pullens House was in Horseshoe Court in St Clements Lane in the said Parish of St Clement Danes Says he never was a hired Yearly Servant Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 17th Day of May 1763 before}

William Clarke
