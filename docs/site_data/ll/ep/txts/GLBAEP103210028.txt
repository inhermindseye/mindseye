Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Frazer  Widow of John Frazer deced maketh oath that She can give no Account respectg her Sd late husbands Settlement Save that he has informed her that he belonged to the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate Middx and that his Mother & one of his Sisters died in the Workhouse there That She has been informed by her late father John Oman deced & believes the Same to be true that She this Depont was born in Swan Alley in the Sd Parish & that her Sd father was there legally Settled by having Served his Apprenticeship there to one Downey That her Sd late husband to her knowledge or belief never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish Office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right & She can give no other Account respecting the Settlemen of him or herself That she hath two Children namely John Aged about three years & four Months & Biddy Aged about twenty two months the lawful issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband to whom She was lawfully Married

Sworn this 20 day of Novemr 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Eleanor Frazer
