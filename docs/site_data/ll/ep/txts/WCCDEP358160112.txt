Middx  The Exaicon of Margt. Giles  Singlewoman lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 16th. day of Febry 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Richd. Paine at the Crown Tavern in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the sd. County of Middx for the Space of three Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds, Eight Pounds, & Ten Pounds and hath been come away from her sd. Service abt. five Years And further Saith that She hath not since she left her sd. Service ever rented any House or lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she is now Pregnant with Child & by means thereof is not capable of Providing for herselfe

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before}

Margaret Giles
