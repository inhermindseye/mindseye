Middx  James Molloy Aged about 23 years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says the after he was out of his Apprenticeship he went to live as a hired Yearly Servant with Benjn. Watson a Nigthm & Chimney Sweeper in Woodstock Court in Woodstock Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middx and where he now lives & has so done upwards of Two years at the Yearly Wages of £6. besides Dict & Washing and Lodging and has not any other Settlemt. then as aforesaid.

Sworn this 30th. day of Novr. 1782 before R Butler

his James Molloy Mark
