Middlesex to wit Mary Chase Aged about forty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with on Mr. Millan a Taylor in the Parish of Tottenham High Cross in the County of Middlesex for one Year at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Novr. 1780 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Mary Chase
