Middx  Alice Welch aged about 23 Years upon Oath says She never was Marryed and that She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hodgson a Peruke maker in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of a Year at the Yearly wages of five Pounds and quitted her said Service about five Weeks since And further upon her voluntary Oath says She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Langley in Clements Lane in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes on the 14th. Day of March last and which hath since been baptized & is still living and become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor & unable to maintain the same and that Charles Price who is now at Sea on board his Majesty's Ship the Dolphin did beget the said Bastard Child on her Body he the said Charles Price having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinant Body in the Month of June in the year 1755 at the Vine Tavern in Holborn at which time he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child She was delivered of as aforesaid and that her said Charles Price is the true & real Father thereof

Sworn the 17th. Day of April 1756 . before }
