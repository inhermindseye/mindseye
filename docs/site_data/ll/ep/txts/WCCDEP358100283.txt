Middx & Westmr.}  passd. to George Bloomby . A to wit Sarah Painter aged about Twenty Five years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Bullock Great Russell Street  Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx for Thirteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Five Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 13th- day of Novr . before us M Addington Thos: Gordon

Sarah Painter
