Westmr. & 

Phabe Thomas upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Edward Thomas at the Fleet London on or about the 3d. Day of February last Says her sd. husband lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Guineas in Old Street in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex for one Year or thereabouts and hath left it about Eight months. since which he has not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 27th. Augt 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Phabe Thomas
