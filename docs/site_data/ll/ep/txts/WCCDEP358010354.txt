Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to Wit) Charles Newton Aged about 41 years upon his Oath saith That Samuel George Newton a Poor person who is now of the Age of 19 years or thereabouts is ill at a Lodging House in Shore Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex is this Examinants son and that he this Examinant did about 4 or 5 years Ago bound his said Son an Apprentice to one Mr. Morrison in the Brough in the parish of Saint Saviour in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry Hair Dresser by Indentures duly Stampt and Executed. that his said Son served only 2 Months of his time and during that time lived with his said Master at his House in the parish of Saint Saviour aforesaid then some Disagreement taking place this Examinant by Consent of his said Master Took his said Son away and destroyed the Indentures and this Examin at further saith that to the last of this Examinants Knowledge information and belief he has not lived has a hired yearly Servant for one year Renter any House Tenement or  Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th Augt. 1790 Wm. Kitchiner M Adington

Chas. Newtons .
