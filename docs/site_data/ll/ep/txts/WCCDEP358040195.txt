Westmr.  Mary Rogers upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound an Apprentice by Indenture dated the 27th. Day of June 1740 for five Years to one Mrs. Stevens in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says by & with the Consent of her sd. Mistress she lived upwds. of three months the latter Part of her sd. Apprentisp . with Mrs. Thorogood in the Town of Ramford in the County of Essex and after she quitted the sd. Mrs. Thorogoods Service did not live in any other Place with the Consent of the sd. Mrs. Stevens Says since the Expiration of her sd. Apprentisp . she has not rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 30th Day & Year above mentioned of May . before} Wm: Jones

Mary Rogers
