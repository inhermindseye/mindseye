Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit George Thornborough aged about 74 Years upon his Oath saith That he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Sampson at the Black Dog in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that he hath quitted said Service about Sixteen years since which time he has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of July 1789  Sampn Wright  Wm Kitchiner

George Thornborrow
