Middx to wit Mary Edwards aged about Fifty Two Years on Oath says She never was Married Rented House a paid Taxes says She lived a hired Yearly Servant One Mrs. Rutkin in Craven Street in the Parish of Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Eleven Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Thirty five Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. day of Janry. 1780 before  Sampn Wright  Jno. Barnfather

Mary Edwards
