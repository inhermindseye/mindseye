Middx.  Margarett Williams  Wido . upon her Oath saith that her late Husband Thomas Williams hath been Dead about Two Years and this Examinant cant give any Account of his Settlement. That before her Intermarriage with the said Thomas Williams & whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived a Yearly Servant with one Mr. Hepkins in Tidewaiters Court in the Parish of Firmly in the Minories  London at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds and Ten Shillings for the Space of Two Years That since she left her said Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. any other Place to her knowledge or beliefe

Sworne the 5th. Octr. 1742 before us} Wm. Hayton  Fran Hole

The Mark of Margarett Williams
