Middlesex & Westminster}  Middx. of Father Lewis Fras. - Downs in the next Back to wit Elizth. Langston of Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Singlewoman Upon her Oath Saith that Fras. Lewis Downs late of Denyell Street  Clare Markett in the said Parish & County Taylor has run away & deserted his two Children (vizt.) Margaret aged about 11 years and Mary aged Ten years or thereabouts and left the same Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Dane And this Examinant further saith that this Examinants Knowledge of his had & so done arises from this Circumstance that is to say That the said Fras. Lewis Downs had put the said Children to m with this Examinant and about Seven Weeks ago went away and entirely deserted them since which time this Examinant hath delivered them to the Parish Officers of the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes and the said Children are now in the Workhouse of St. Clement Danes aforesaid

Sworn this 5th- day of Novr. 1784 R Butler

Elizth Langston
