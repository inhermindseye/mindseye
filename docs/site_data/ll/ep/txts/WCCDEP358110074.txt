Westmr.  Jane Sone upon Oath Saith she is the widow of Francis Sone who dyed about Seven Years ago and that her sd. late husband (before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whist he was a Singleman ) lived a Yearly Servant for one Year & upwds. with Mr. Tronted Senr. & Junr. in York Buildings in the Parish of St. Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . & never after he quitted his sd. Service nor has this Examint. Since his Decease rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn July 29th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Jane Sone
