Middx ss  Ann Bishop  Widow of Thomas Bishop deced Maketh oath that she has been often informed by her sd late husband That he lived as a hired servant by the Year with Mr Webster an Woollen Draper in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And that he never afterwards gained any other settlemt. which informacon this Depont believes to be true And this Deponent saith that her said late husband had a Daughter by a former wife which Daughter is still living and was baptised by the Name of Elizabeth And this Deponent farther saith that to her knowledge informacon or belief the said Elizabeth Bishop was never an Apprentice or hired servant by the year nor ever gained any Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 3d day of September 1757 before  Richd. Ricards  Chris: Scott

Ann Bishop
