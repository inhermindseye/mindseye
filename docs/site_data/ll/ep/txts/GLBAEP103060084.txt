Aldgate Middx

Daniel Pyner Maketh Oath that he never was an Apprentice nor hired Servant by the year That he remembers his Father Richard Pyner living at the Sign of the King William and Queen Mary in East Street in the Town of Gravesend in the Parish of Milton in the County of Kent and that the House was his own and was of the Value of ten pound a year And this Deponent was Informd by his Parents that he was born in the Said House and believes his Father paid all Parish taxes for Several years and this Deponent never gaind any Settlement in his own right hath a wife named Abigal

Sworn 7th day of April 1747 before Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

Daniel Pyner
