Middx & Westmr.  Jane Higgins Aged about Twenty seven Years, Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that on the Seventh of October 1766, she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Trotter in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middlesex which hath been baptized by the Name of Ann ; says that one John Cummings a Journeyman Carpenter is the reputed Father of the said Female Bastard Child, he having had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of February 1766, and at several times afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Female Bastard of which she was delivered as aforesaid, and that the said John Cummings and no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 6th day of July 1768 , before W Kelynge

The Mark of Jane Higgins
