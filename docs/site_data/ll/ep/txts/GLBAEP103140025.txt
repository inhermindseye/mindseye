Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Ross the wife of  Wm. Ross who is lately gone away from her to places unknown to her maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting her sd. husbands place of Settlemt nor of this Parents or either of them that She this Deponent divers years ago & previous to her Marriage with her sd. husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages by & with one Mrs Chambers wife of Major Chambers in Scotland  & Continued in Such Service for three years The last year of which three years She Served her said Master & Mistress in the parish of St Mary le Bone in the County of Middlesex & then She left Such Service & was hired again & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Dimmock in the Same parish & Continued in Such Service last Menconed for Nine Months & by means of Such Services as She is Advised gained a legal Settlement in the said parish of St Mary le Bone & She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She may have Acquired by Marriage that She this Depont. hath two children namely Susannah aged about Two years & Willm. Aged about three months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom She was lawfully Married

Sworn this 17th day of Febry. 1774 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

Elis Ross
