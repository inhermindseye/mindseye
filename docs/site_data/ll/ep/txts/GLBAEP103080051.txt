Middlesex ss  Catherine king the Widow of Henry King deced Maketh oath that about eleven years ago she was Married to her said late husband who died about fifteen Weeks ago And that during such their intermarriage her said husband never rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year or upwards nor served any Parish offices nor lived as a hired servant by the year Nor to this Depont. knowledge or belief did any Act to Acquire a legal settlement And this Deponent farther saith that she doth not know Nor Can set forth where her said husband was born or where he was last legally settled before he Married this Deponent But this Deponent saith that she this Deponent about twelve Years ago lived as a hired servant by the year with Mr. Green of the Parish of Chelsea in the County of Middx Brewer at the yearly Wages of seven Pounds by the year and Continued in such service for the space of one year and a quarter and by means thereof (as this Depont. is advised) gained a legal settlemt there And this Dept. farther saith that she hath not since lived as a hired servant by the Year nor done any Act to Acquire a settlement in her own right And that she hath two Children to wit Susannah Aged about eight Years and three quarters and Mary Ann aged about Ten Months the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her sd husband

Sworn this 19th. day of November 1759 before us Chris: Scott Walter Berry

The mark of Catherine king
