Middlesex to wit}  Catharine Smith  Widow of Richard Smith  Locksmith deceased maketh oath and saith that her said late Husband about twenty six or seven Years ago took an House in Water Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lived in and Rented the said House many Years together at Twelve Pounds a Year Rent and Saith that her said Husband about a Year and an half before his Decease he quitted the said House and went to Lambeth and in Britania Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surrey he rented and lived in an House and Paid Twenty six pounds a Year Rent for the same And saith that since the Decease of her said Husband she this Examinant took the same House in Water Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex and lived in and Rented the same for several Years together and paid Twelve Guineas a Year Rent and all Parish Taxes for the same having then quitted the said House at Lambeth aforesaid And saith that she paid Rent and Taxes as the Occupier of the said House in Water Street aforesaid up to Lady Day 1789 as appears by a Receipt for poors Rate now in this Examinants Custody And saith that since the Decease of her said late Husband she hath not contracted Matrimony or done any other Act to her Knowledge or belief which could acquire her a Legal Settlement subsequent to her quitting the said House in Water Street aforesaid

Sworn the 4th. Day of December 1790 R Butler

The Mark of Catharine Smith
