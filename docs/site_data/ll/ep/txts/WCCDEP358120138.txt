Middx  John Castell aged near 73 Years upon Oath Saith he rented a house in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes of the yearly Rent of £14 lived therein about three Years and quitted the same near forty Years ago Since Which he has not rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 3d. July 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

John Castell
