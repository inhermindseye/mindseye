Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Evans aged about 21 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born in Charlotte Street Rathbone Place in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex where her father kept house for several years

Sworn this 18th. day of Decr. 1795 . before us W Kinnard N Bond

Elizabeth Evans
