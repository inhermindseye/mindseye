Middx & Westmr.}  Thos. Clean one of the Overseers of the Psh of St- Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - in the Co- of Middx on behalf of Henry Gallard an Infant aged about one Year and five Months Son of Henry Gallard (gone to America ) by Jane Gallard his Wife now deced on his Oath says that on searching the Examination Book & Pass Warrts- find that the above named Jane Gallard with her two Children were passed from the sd- Parish of St- Clement Danes to the Parish of luckfield in the Co- of Sussex by Order dated 4th- day of April 1776 under the hands & Seals of R. Butler & Wm- Kitchiner Esqs Two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the said County of Middx & City & Liberty of Westmr - & further says that the said Jane Gallard is since Dead that the above named Edward Gallard the Infant is the Son of the Jane Gallard by the said Henry Gallard & is the same Child that was passed with the sd- Jane the Mother by the above mentioned Order as he has been credibly Informed & verily believes

Sworn this 22nd- Day of July 1777 before. G Mercer Jno. Barnfather

Thos. Cleane
