Middx.  Joseph Carter aged abt. 58 Years upon Oath says he was born in Bannisters Alley opposite St. Giles's Church in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx. & never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th. day of March 1758 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane

The Mark of Joseph Carter
