Middlesex  Sarah Smith  Wido . Removed from the Parish of St. Saviour in Southwark by Order dated the 7th.. Day of August Instt: upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband Wm. Smith in the Month of November in the Year 1737 at the Fleet  London that the sd. Wm. Smith hath been dead Three Years & upwards And this Examinant cant give any Account of his Settlemt. other than that he was born in York as he hath informed her that upwards of Six Years ago being at that Time a Singlewoman she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Legross in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of £5.10s.0d for the Space of One Year & upwards that since she left her said Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when born & that one Francis Jackson did

begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body she this Examinant at such Time being a Servt . to the Sd. Mr. Jacksons Father & there the Sd. Fras.. Jackson became acquainted with her and had Carnal knowledge of her Body sevll. Times in the Month of Janry last & at sevll. other Times since at one of which Times he gott her with the sd. Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Fras. Jackson & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 12th Augt. 1742 before} Tho. Lane

Sarah Smith
