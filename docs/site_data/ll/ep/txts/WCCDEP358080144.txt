Middx & Westmr.}  Abigal Robinson Aged about fifty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one John Haisel otherwise Hassell  Esqr . at a Laceman on Ludgate Hill in the Parish of St. Bride otherwise Bridget in the City of London for one Year and Seven Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds hath quitted her said Service ever since the Year 1740 since which time he never gained any other subsequent Sentlement either by Marriage Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 19th. day of May 1777 before  John Croft  John Cox .

Abigal Roberttson
