Middx  Saml. Jason upon Oath saith he intermarried with Ann his Wife at St. Michls. Church in Southampton the 10th. June 1747 by whom he hath one Child Samuel aged about 15 months Says he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Isaac Wright  Shoemaker and Served three Years & upwds. of his sd. Apprentiship with ye. sd Isaac Wright in Clare Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex and them ran away from his said Master and never was assigned over to any person rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 15th. 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell

Saml. Jason
