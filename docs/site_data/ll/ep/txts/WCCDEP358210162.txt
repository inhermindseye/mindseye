Westmr.  Sarah Kent aged about 53 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Henry Kent who has been dead about twenty Years, says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Parker for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds says she lived three Months or thereabouts the last part of said Service with the said Mrs. Parker in Naked boy Court  Ludgate Hill in the Parish of St. Brides London, quitted said Service about Seven Years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 26th. May 1767 , before } Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

Sarah Kent
