Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Dunbar the Daughter of Robert Dunbar maketh oath that her said Father in the year one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy Seven lived in & rented of Mrs Elizabeth Baker of Swan Street in the Minories in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate London Part of her dwelling house there Situated to Wit a Parlour two Bed Chambers & use of the Kitchen at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds Part of which Apartments were furnished by this Deponents father & other part by the Sd Mrs Baker That Such Apartments so rented by this Deponents father were in this Deponents Judgment Worth to be let for at least Ten Guineas by the year unfurnished & that they have been & now are let for that Sum unfurnished & were never let for less Money as the Sd Mrs Baker has informed this Depont & which She believes to be true That the furniture which the Sd Mrs Baker provided for her Sd father was only a Bed Stead Bed & Curtains but without any other furniture thereto; Six Old Chairs a Stove looking Glass & Small Old table the use of which in this Deponents Judgment were of Very little Value That this Deponents Sd father has not Since done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 31. day of Decemr 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley Eliz Dunbar
