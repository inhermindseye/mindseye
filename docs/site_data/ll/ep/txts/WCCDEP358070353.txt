Middx & Westmr.}  Martha Newman Aged about fifty Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her late Husband Alexr. Newman at the Parish Church of Hammersmith in the County of Middlesex in the Year 1757 and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said late Husband Purchased an Estate at Hammersmith of the Yearly Val of fifty Two Pounds a Year and lived therein many Years after her said Husbands death and this Examinant further saith that after quitting the above mentioned House and Premisses she Rented and lived in a House in the Town of Hammersmith of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid all taxes due for the same hath quitted the said last mentioned House in the Year 1770 since which time she hath never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 21st. day of March 1776 before R. Butler Sampn Wright

Martha Newman
