Middx  Sarah Wilkinson aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Lowe a Fishing Tackle shop in little Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster for one year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said service about Six Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 24th. day of April 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Sarah Wilkinson Mark
