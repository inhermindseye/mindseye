Middx  Rebecca Strickland  Wido . of Wm. Strickland upon her Oath saith that she intermarryed with her sd. late Husband upwards of fifteen Years ago at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex that her said late Husband William Strickland served his Apprentiship with one Mr. Jones in Vinegar Yard the upper End of Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St. James at Clerkenwell in the said County of Middlesex Soap Caskmaker that since his said Apprentiship her sd. late Husband in his Lifetime not this Examinant since his Death never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 30th- Decr. 1742 before} Wm: Miller

The Mark of Rebecca Strickland
