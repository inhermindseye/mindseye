Middlesex ss}  Normand Mc Leod maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servt. at Certain Wages by & with John Cockburn  Victualler in the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the said County & Continued in such Service under such hiring for the Space of two years & upwards That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish Office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain anothr Settlement

Sworn this 13 day of December 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Normand Mc Leod
