Middx to Wit Ann Green Aged about 50 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband at the Parish Church  of St. Andrew Holbourn about 18 Years ago and by whom she Hath no Child or Children living Says her sd. late Husband Rented and lived in a House in King Edward Street  Wapping in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds &  Parish Taxes due for the Same hath quitted sd. House above 14 Years ago and after quitting that House Her sd. late Husband took Lease of some Apartmts. under the 3 Tunns in Hough Street Clare Markett at the Yearly Rent of £20. but was never Rated to or paid any Taxes due for the same hath quitted sd. Apartments about 10 Years ago since which time her sd. late Husband or this Examt. Never Rented House Tem  or Lodging of £10. a Year paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Settlemt. than as afsd.

Sworn this 20th. Day of Decr. 1782 before R Butler

her Ann Green Mark
