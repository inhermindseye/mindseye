Middx  Mary Bermondsey  Sarah Ross upon Oath Saith She was married to Thomas Ross at the Fleet London upwds. of 5 Years ago by whom She has one Child Mary aged 5 weeks that her sd husband is now on board his Majesties Ship Gibralter at Portsmouth and that he informed this Examinant he Served Seven years apprentiship with David Carter in the West Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry that his sd. apprentiship Expired abt. 6 yrs. ago and he hath never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year which Information this Examint. believes to be true

Sworn April 24th 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell B.H.

The mark of Sarah Ross
