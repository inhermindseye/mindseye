Middx.  Agnes Jones aged 32 years upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Abraham Jones of whose Settlement she can give no Account and that she this Examinant was bound Apprentice for five Years to Mrs. Large a Schorl mistress & lived with her upwds. of 3 Years in Hatton Garden in the Liberty of Saffron hill  Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the parish of St. Andw. in Holborn in the County of middlesex and then her sd. Mistress & this Examinant Disagreeing the parted by Consent and she never was assigned over to any other person nor ever rented ten pounds a year or paid parish Taxes

Sworn Octr. 4th 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Agnes Jones
