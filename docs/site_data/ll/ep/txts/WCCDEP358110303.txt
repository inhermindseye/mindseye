Middx.  Mary Wood upon her Oath saith she is the wido . of Richard Wood who informed her he was born in Tooleys Gate in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark where his father lived in and Rented a house and that neither her sd. late husband nor this Examinant since his decease have rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 24th. Augt. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Wood
