Middlesex ss}  Tobias Waldgrave maketh oath that many. years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with James Wylde in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Victualler at Certain yearly wages & Continued in Such Service for three years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That this Depont has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement & that he hath a wife named Ann to Whom he was lawfully married in the parish Church of St Leonard Shoreditch Middlesex & a Child named Susannah aged about eight years by a former Wife deced to whom he was lawfully married in the parish Church of St Botolph Aldgate London

Sworn this 28 of Novr 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Tobias Waldgrave
