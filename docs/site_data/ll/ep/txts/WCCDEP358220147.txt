Westmr.  Elizth. Price Aged about Sixty four Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Richard Price who has been Dead about Two Years & an half Says her Husband Rented and lived in a House in Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Several Years at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds a Year and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Sixteen Years Ago and after he Quitted the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th. day of May 1770 before me W Kelyngs

her Elizth. Price Mark

Witness Petr. Sidebotham
