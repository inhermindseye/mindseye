Saint Clement Danes September 6th. 1785

Middlesex 

Passd: to St. Thomas. (to wit) John Almett one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been in found and believes that the place of the last legal Settlement of Phoebe Lewis a Singlewoman , aged Eighteen years or thereabouts, is in the Parish of Saint Thomas in the City of Salis 

Sworn this 6th. Septr 1785 before  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

John Allmett
