Middx  Mary Bland upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Walter Bland at the Fleet London about two years ago that the sd. Walter Bland now absconds from her & that she has one Child in Ann aged about 8 months Says her sd. husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of fourteen pounds in Ship Yard in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lived therein Six weeks and pd. half a Quarters Rent for the same to Mr. Harrison of Totter Lane  Bricklayer and that her sd. husband has not since she left the sd. house (which is about a year ago) rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 29th 1750 before} Tho Lane .

Mary Bland
