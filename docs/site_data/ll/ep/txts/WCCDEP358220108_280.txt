Middlesex and Westminster }  John Richards aged about Fifty Three Years on his Oath says he was bound Apprentice to and Served upwards of two Years and an half of his said Apprenticeship with one John Oakly a Weaver in Olde Cock Lane in the Parish of Saint Matthew Bethnall Green in the County of Middlesex when this Exami- nant Parted with his said Master by Consent And after he Parted with his said Master he never afterwds. Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 2d. day of January 1770 before me}
