Middx  Thomas Venn aged about 68 years upon Oath saith he Rented a House of the yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds in Dru Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and lived therein from the year 1712 to the year 1718 and has not since he quitted the said House Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 9th. Day of Jany. 1756 before} Theodr. Sydenham

Thomas Venn
