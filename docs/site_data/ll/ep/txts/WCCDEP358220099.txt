Middx & Westmr}  Jane Hashton Aged about forty years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Thos. Hashton who has been Dead about Seven Years & with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about Seventeen Years ago Says her said late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman lived as a Covenanted Clerk with one Mr. Johnson Attorney at Law in Warwick Court in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex for sevl. Years at the Yearly sallary of forty Pounds besides Diet and after he quitted his said Place he never afterwards Rented House paid Taxes or lived as a Covenanted Clerk for one Year to the knowledge or beleif of this Examinant neither has this Examinant since her said Husbands Death ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. day of Decr. 1769 before us R Butler W Kelyngs

Jane Hashton
