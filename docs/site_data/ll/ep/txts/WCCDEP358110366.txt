Westmr.  Barw Daniel upon Oath Saith he lived in & Rented a house near the Corner of Blackmoor Street in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the yearly Rent of £26 & lived therein about One Year that he quitted the sd house upwds. of 40 years ago & has not since rented a house of ten Pounds by the year on paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Decr. 10th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Barthow Daniell
