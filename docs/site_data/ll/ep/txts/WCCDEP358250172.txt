Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Smith aged about 43 years upwards her Oath saith that in the year 1767 1777 she Intermarried with Thomas Smith her late Husband at the Romish Church in Dubbin by Licence according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England That her said Husband has been dead upwards of Six years That her said late Husband Rented unfurnished Apartments of a Mrs. Stringer in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster upwards of Three Months at the yearly Rent of Eleven Guineas that he quitted said Apartments about Thirteen years ago after which he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing where by he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant saith that since her said Husbands death she has not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a Settlement in her own Right

Sworn this 29th. day of 1st. June 1795 } N Bond

her Sarah Smith Mark

Witness. B Newman
