Middx  Mary Higgins Wife of John Higgins upon Oath saith She Intermarryed with the said John Higgins in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes upwards of Thirteen Years since says that her said Husband whilst a Singleman lived a Covenant Servant for three Years with Thomas Fairbottle and served him fourteen Months the last part of his said Service in the House of Mr. Grenough a Shoemaker near Beaufort Buildings in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which said Term of three Years Expired on the Twenty ninth day of May 1741. And further says that her said Husband never Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year since he quitted his Service aforesaid

Sworn the 4th Febry 28th Janry 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

Mary Higgins
