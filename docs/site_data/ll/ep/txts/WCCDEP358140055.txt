Middx.  Elizabeth Eastgate upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Eastgate (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London on the 27th. day of October 1743. says her said husband served three Years the last part of his Apprenticeship to Mr. Charles Pearce a Taylor in Arrundell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Parish or Place to this Examinants knowledge or belief says she hath one Child by her said husband (Vizt.) Mary aged abt. Seven Years & she being poor is not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworn this 28th. day of Novr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Eastgate
