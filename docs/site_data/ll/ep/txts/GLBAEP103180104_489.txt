Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Dennis of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said. County this Third day of December 1778 Who Saith that She in now with Child and that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that John Green of the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the Said County Carpenter is the Father of the Sd Child

Taken & Signed the day & year abovewritten before me  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Ann Dennis
