Middx  Susanna Savery  Widdow of Adam Savery deceased Maketh Oath that about Six years ago her said late husband livd in Charles Street in the Parish of St. Georges Hanover Square in the Said County where he rented a House of Thirty three pounds a year known by the Sign of the Hampshire Hogg and livd there upwards of a year and that he never gaind any other Settlement Since, that he hath been dead about three weeks hath four Children (to wit) James aged 7 years and 8 Months, William aged Six years, Mary aged 3 years and 9 Months, and Susanna Savery aged ten Months.

Sworn the 29th day of October 1746 Robert Dennett Rd Ricards

the marke of Susanna Savery
