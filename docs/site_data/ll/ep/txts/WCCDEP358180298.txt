Middx  Priscilla Curtis  Wido . of Wm. John Curtis aged about fifty - Years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with the sd. Wm. Curtis in the Parish Church of Weymouth in the County of Dorsett on the 1st. of May above 30 Years since Says her said late Husband lived in a house of his own in the sd. Parish of Weymouth in the County of Dorsett aforesaid for several Years & paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says she hath not since her sd. Husbands Death Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 25th. Febry 1744/5 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Priscilla Curtis
