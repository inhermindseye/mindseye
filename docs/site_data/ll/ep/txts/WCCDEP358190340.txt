Middx  Charlotte Yeomans Aged about 24 Years Upon Oath Says She was Never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mr Jennings at the Barley Mow in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr in the County of Middx for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath Quitted her said Service upwards of Eight Years and hath not since Rented any House or lived with any Person as a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year And Further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes She being Poor and unable to Maintain the Same when born That Thomas Collins  apprentice to Mr Lucas a Frunk Maker  the Corner of Nicholas Lane  Lombard Street did beget the said Child or Children on her Body he the said Thomas Collins having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the Month of

September Last and at Several other Times Since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn ye 27th Day of April 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Charlotte Yeomans
