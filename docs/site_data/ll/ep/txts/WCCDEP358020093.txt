Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Margarett Dowlin aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Foster in Richmond Buildings in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Two years since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st day of Augt. 1788  Philip Dyot  Rupt: Clarke

her Margarett Dowlin Mark
