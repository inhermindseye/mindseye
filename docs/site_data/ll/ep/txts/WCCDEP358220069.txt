Middx & Westmr. ss  Nathaniel Peterson Aged about 38 Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his late Wife in the Parish of Saint Sepulchres in the City of London about 16 Years Ago and who has been dead about fourteen Months Says he served Seven Years Years Apprenticeship with one Daniel Messeder a Tyre Smith in the said Parish of Saint Sepulchres in the City of London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 19th. day of Septr. 1769 before me} Tho: Fellows

Nathaniel Peterson
