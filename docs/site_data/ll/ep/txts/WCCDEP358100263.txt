Middlesex & Westminster}  taken Saint given (to wit) Catharine Bullmore  single woman aged Twenty two Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That She never was married That She served as an Apprentice with Sarah Mary prothere a Tambour Worker in Cary Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for upwards of forty Days, That she quitted her said Place about the Year one thousand seven hundred and seventy seven, That since she quitted her said Place, she has not at any one continued time lived as an hired Yearly Servant for the Space of one Year, Paid Rented for any Messnage or Tenement at ten Pounds a Year Value a paid Parochial Taxes for any such nor done any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith That she is now Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and chargeable to the said Parish, And further says That Thomas Warren an Apprentice to his Father Archer Warren who is a Hair and lives in Paved Alley  Saint James's had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body at different times out the Exact Time this Examinant cannot a at some or one of which Times he did beget on this Deponents Body the Child or Children of which she is so Pregnant - And that the said Thomas Warren the Son is the real and True father

of the said Child or Children and did beget the Same

Sworn this 13th. Octr. 1784 before R Butler

Cath Bullmore
