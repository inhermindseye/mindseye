Middx  David Glen - on Behalfe of Isabella Glen Wido. upon his Oath saith the sd. Isabella Glen intermarried with her late Husbd.  James Glen at Montross in Scotland upwards of 25 Yrs since & the sd. James Glen hath been dead abt. 20 Yrs, Says the sd. James Glen was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Alexr. Goodale in Thing Street in the parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of West Taylor & never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained Settlemt. in any other Place Says the sd. Isabella Glen since the Death of her sd. Husbd. hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne March 15th: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

David Glen
