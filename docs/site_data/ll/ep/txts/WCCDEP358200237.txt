Middx  Lydia Stevens Aged about Twenty four Years upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Robert Stevens in Hatherstone Chaple in the County of Warwick Says her said husband Served & Apprentiship with a White Smith in the said parish of Hatherstone & never after Rented House was Assigned over to any person paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year as this Examinant hath been Informed & which Information she Verily beleives to be true Says she has One Child by her said Husband Rachel Aged Two Years & a half

Sworn the 8th of Feby 1765 Before}

Lydia Stevens
