Westmr. 

Denny Clayton upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Boulton a Milliner in Henrietta Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £5..5..0 for the Space of one Year & upwards & hath left her said Service upwards of two Years Says She hath not since she left thesd. Mrs. Boulton Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge beleif.

 Sworn 13th. Janry 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Denny Clayton
