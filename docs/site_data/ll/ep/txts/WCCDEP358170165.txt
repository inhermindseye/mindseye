Middx  Wm. Mason passed from the Parish of St- Bridgett vulgarly St. Brides  London by Order bearing Date the 22d. Day of October Instt. upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr- Joshua Griffith a Goldsmith in Water Lane  Fleet street  in the Parish of St. Bridgett vulgarly St. Brides London with whom he served Three Years & then was assinged over to Mr. Jno. Brown  Junior a Shagreen Case maker at the Paulsgrave head Coffee House in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex with whom he served the Remr. of his sd. Apprentiship that his sd. Master removed the last Year of his sd. Apprentishipp into Clements Inn Passage in the same Parish that since this Examinant left his sd. Master he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise

-gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath a wife named Juliana and Two Children vizt- Sarah aged Six Years and Lydya aged Six Weeks and being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne the 26th- Day of October 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Wm Mason
