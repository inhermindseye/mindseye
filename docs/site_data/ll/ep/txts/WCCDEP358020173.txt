Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Peyton aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Waters in Berkeley Square in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Fourteen years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1789 D Walker Philip Dyot

her Elizth. Peyton Mark
