Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Payne Aged about Seventy Three Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Charles Payne who has been dead about Twenty Eight Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children living as she believes namely William Aged about forty Years and Stephen Payne who went abroad several Years ago Says her said Son William Never Rented House paid Taxes served any apprentice- ship, Covenanted Servant or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant before Marriage for one Year or otherwise gained any Settlement than as hereafter mentioned Says her said Husband when living Rented and lived in a House in Saint Pauls Church Yard in the Parish of Saint Faith London for several Years at the Yearly Rent of fifty Pounds and paid all parish taxes due for the same in which said House her said Husband died And further says that after quitting the said House she never after Rented House or Tenement paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement than as aforesaid.

Sworn this 16th. day of May 1778 before  John Croft  Jno. Barnfather

Elizabeth Payne
