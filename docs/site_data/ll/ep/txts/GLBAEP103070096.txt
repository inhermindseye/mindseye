Middx ss.  Esther Thompson  Widow of George Thompson deced Maketh oath that about Thirty five Years ago her Son in law John Whittleton deced was bound apprentice to Mr. Rawling a Barber & peruke Maker in Torlley Street then in the Parish of St. Olave but now in the Parish of St. John Southwarke in the County of Surry with whom he duly Served his apprenticeship & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that her sd. Son in law never Rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year nor Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement And this Depont. Saith that he left a Widow Named Mary and a Child Son named George Whittleton Which he had by a former wife named Esther the Danr of this Depont. which sd. George Whittleton is aged about Twenty four Years And Which sd. Mary Whittleton hath gained no legal Settlemt. but in right of her sd. Deced Husband & which ye sd. George Whittleton hath gaine no legal settlement and in right of his sd. father to ye knowledge or belief of this Depont.

Sworn this 9th day of February 1754 before us  Rd: Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

The Mark of Esther Thompson
