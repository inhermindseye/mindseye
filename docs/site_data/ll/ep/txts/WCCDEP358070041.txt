Westmr. to wit Mary Jones Aged about Sixty Three Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Henry Jones who has been Dead above Thirty Years Says that she being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Messrs. Coombs and Aldersey Mercers in Chandes Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden upwards of one Year an half at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of 5 Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 16th. day of November 1772 before Us R. Butler 

her Mary Jones Mark

Witness Edwd. Field
