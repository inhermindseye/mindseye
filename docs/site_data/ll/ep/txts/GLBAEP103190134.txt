Middlesex ss}  Sarah Wilkes  Singlewoman maketh oath That Sometime ago She was hired lived as a hired Servant for a year with George Massey in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx  Cheesemongr at Certain Wages & Continued in Such service under Such hiring for the Space of Sixteen Months That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year out of the Said Parish nor was She ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 8. day of August 1780 before  Jno Sherwood

Sarah Wilkes
