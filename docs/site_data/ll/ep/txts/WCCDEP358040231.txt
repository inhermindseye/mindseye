Westmr 

Eliz: Cable  wife of Charles Cable (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Chas. Cable at St. James's Church in the Town & County of Harwich upwards of 3 Years since and can give no Account whatever of her sd. husbands Settlement Says she was bound Apprentice for 5 Years to Mrs. Dorothy Powell a that Trimmer and had lived with the sd. Mrs. Powell as an Apprentice upwds. of two months in High Street in the Parish of St. James in Coventy in the County of Warwick aforesd. at the time of her sd. Intermarriage

Sworn Augt. 9th. 1748 before}
