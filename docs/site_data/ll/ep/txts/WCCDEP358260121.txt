Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Hewin aged about 42 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she Rented a First floor unfurnished of a Mr. Gibbons in Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas that she hath quitted said Apartments upwards of Eight years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Fifth day of April 1796 } Tho Vaughan N Bond

Cathearne Euing
