Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Perinisorve Pass to wit Mary Brewin aged upwards of Fifteen Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes lived as an hired yearly Servant or ever served any Apprenticeship Says that she was born at the Crown in in the Parish of St Mary Huntingdon in the County of Huntingdon and that she has not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elswhere

Sworn before the this 6th. Day of August 1785 before me  Wm Kitchiner

the Mark of Mary Brewin
