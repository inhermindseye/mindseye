Westmr. to wit Margarett Jones Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Joseph Jones (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Margarets Westmr . about Six Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Sarah Ann Aged about Two Years and Margt . aged about one Month Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Exeter Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year & Three Quarters at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of half a Year ago & hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn this 18th. day of Octr. 1771 before Us} R. Butler R A

Margaret Jones
