Westmr- to wit Sophia O' Neal Aged about Nineteen years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Francis O' Neal Rented and lived in a house in the Strand Opposite Southampton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - for ten years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of £53 hath quitted the same about twelve Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever gained any other subseqt- Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Fathers Death And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that John Hust als Hurst a Marshalsea  Court Officer who keeps a house in Cross Lane Seven Dials is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of June but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl- different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Hust als Hurst and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 9th- Day of Decr- 1777 before R Butler

her Sophia O' Neal Mark

No- 2 Clements Lane
