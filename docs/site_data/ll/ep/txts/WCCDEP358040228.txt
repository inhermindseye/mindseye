Westmr 

Eliz. Amey aged about 65 Yrs upon Oath saith she is the widow of Bernard Amey who dyed abt. 22 Years since Says she Intermarried with her said late husband at the Parish Church of St. James Westmr. in Decr. 1709 and that the sd. Bernard Amey was born at Brussells in Flanders and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief Says her father Wm. Danes lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £30 and upwards in Blow bladdes Street in the Parish of St. Leonard Faster Lane London for several Years & there dyed and that she (this Examinant never rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apparent or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 5th Augt 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Eliz: Aime
