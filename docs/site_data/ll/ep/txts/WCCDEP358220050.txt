Middx & Westmr}  Hannah Gilpin Aged about Twenty Three Years one her Voluntary Oath says she never was married And that on the Twenty fifth day of June last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Eltham a Coachman in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of  Margaret Says that One Joseph Gumley a Journeyman Taylor is the reputed Father of the said Female Bastard Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of September 1768 and at several other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child  the Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid And that the said Joseph Gumley & no other person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn this 22d. day of July 1769 before me W Kelyngs

Hannah Galpin
