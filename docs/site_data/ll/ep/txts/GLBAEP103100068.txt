Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Susannah Harris late of the City of Bristol but now of the Parish of Whitechappal in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County of Middlesex the 20th day of January 1769

This Examinant upon her oath Saith That upon the Twenty third day of January one thousand Seven hundred and Sixty eight in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County of Middlesex She this Examinant was delivered of a Male Bastard Child And that the said Bastard Child is likely to becom Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that William Harris of the Parish of Clifton in the said City of Bristol  Lime Burner did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child which Child hath been Since baptized & registred by the name of James Pendergrass

Sworn this 20 day of January 1769 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Susannah Harris
