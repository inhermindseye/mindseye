Middx  The Examination of Thos. Scarlew aged about 65 Years Passed from the Hamlet of Hammersmith in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middx to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the same County Taken the 6th. day of March Who upon his Oath that about Six Years ago he lived a Yearly Hired Servt . with one Mr. Scarlet in Hollis Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of Eighteen Months & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds And this Examinant further saith that he hath not since that time Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife & that he is now Poor & Antient & not able to maintaine himself

Sworne the 6th. day of March 1738/9 before me } James Gronous

Tho: Scarlew
