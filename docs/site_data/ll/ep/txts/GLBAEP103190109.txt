Middlesex ss}  Ann Wright the widow of William Wright deced maketh oath that divers Years ago her Said late husband lived in & rented a house of the yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds in the Parish of Saint Mary in the Town of Cricklade in the County of Wilts and has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor has Sae this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself And that She Was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 22. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Ann Wright
