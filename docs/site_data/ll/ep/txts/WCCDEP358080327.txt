Middx  Christian Evans Aged about 47 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Joseph Evans with whom she Intermarried at Llanbaddon in Wales about Twenty three Years ago and who died about Eighteen Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children now living and this Examinant further Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Skinner a Taboconist adjoining to Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year and three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of three Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th. day of Decr. 1778 before R Butler

her Christian Evans Mark
