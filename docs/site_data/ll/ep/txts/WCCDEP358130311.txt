Middx  Eliz: Horncastle aged abt. 42 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Horncastle who hath been dead abt. ten years says her said Husbd. was as he inform'd her a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England says before her intermarriage with the sd John Horncastle & whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a hired yearly Servant with Lady Miller on Mays hill in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent for one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of four Pounds says she has one Child be her sd. Husbd. aged abt. Eleven Years

Sworn the 22d day of March 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz. Horncastle
