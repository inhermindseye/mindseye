Middx  Eliza. Foreman upon her Oath saith that on the Second Day of March about Eighteen Years since she was marryed to her present Husband Wm- Foreman in Blew Ball Alley in the Mint Southwark that her said Husband was at that time an Apprentice to one Mr. Lancaster Mans in Stoney Lane in the Parish of St. Olave in Southwark in the County of Surry  Carman that soon after her said Intermarriage her sd. Husband absconded from his said Masters Service that her said Husband never was Assigned over to anyother Master rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gain'd a Settlement in anyother Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleife that on or about the 7th of April 1739 this Examinant was removed by an Order under the Hands & Seales of two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the Liberty of Westminster from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the sd. Liberty of Westmr. to the sd Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry

Sworne 21st. Decr. 1742 before} C. Egerton

The Mark of Eliz: Foreman
