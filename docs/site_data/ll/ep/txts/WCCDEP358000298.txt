Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizth Mackenzie aged about 66 years upon her Oath saith that about 31 years ago she Intermarried with Murdock Mackenzie at the Savoy Chapel  that she hath no Child or Children living that her said Husband was a Scotchman and never gained any Settlement in England that she was born in Peter Street Bishopgate in the Parish of Saint that her Father and Mother Rented and lived in a House in Peter Street Bishopgate in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishopgate in the County of Middx where this Examinant was born that She hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any other Settlement otherwise than by Birth

Sworn this 2nd. day of June 1786  Sampr Wright N Bond.

her Elizth. Mackenzie Mark
