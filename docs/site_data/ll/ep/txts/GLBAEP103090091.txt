Middlesex ss  John Whitebread maketh Oath that divers years ago, he was duly bound and served the whole of his Apprentice ship to one Benjamin Harvey who then lived at Limehouse hole in the parish of Saint Ann Limehouse in the County of Middlesex  Shipwright and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Deponent farther saith that since such his said Apprenticeship he lived in and Rented a house of Four Pounds a year in Ropemaker's fields in the parish aforesaid and paid Kings Taxes for the same and has not since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a Wife named Mary and two Children namely John William Aged about Eight years and Thomas Aged about five years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said Wife.

Sworn this day of January 1768 before}
