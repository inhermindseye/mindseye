Saint Clement Danes

 Middlesex (to wit)  Rebecca Clarke  singlewoman aged twenty eight years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Fossick Cheesemonger in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  yearly Servant as the Yearly wages of four pounds for upwards of one years that the Quitted such her place about Nine or Ten years ago and since then hath not lived as an hired yearly Servant for twelve Months together in any one Place nor done any Act master  or thing whereby to the best & her knowledge information a belief the may have gained any other settlement

Sworn this 28 Septr. 1789 before  Wm. Kitchiner

her Rebecca Clarke Mark
