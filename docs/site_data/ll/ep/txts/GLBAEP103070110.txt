Middx The Examination of Sarah Leggatt Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for ye County of Middx residing in or Near the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx this 3d. day of July 1754

This Examinant on her oath Saith That about Fourteen Months ago & several times since until she came from his Service 3 Months Li one David Thomas her Master who lived at the sign of the Cock the Corner of Sherborne Lane in the City of London did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at the said house and the said David Thomas had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body & so had several times since Whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she now goeth and is Pregnant

And this Examinant Saith that the said David Child or Children of which she now goeth with and is pregnant as aforesd. is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the sd. parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate she not being able to provide for the same And that the sd. David Thomas is the true & only Father of the said Child or Children so likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesd. And lastly saith that this Examination is Voluntary And that She hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 3d day of July 1754 before me  Rich: Ricards

The Mark of Sarah Leggatt
