Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Walker upon her Oath saith that on the 24th. day of Novr . last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the name of Ann That one Richard Besson a Serjeant at Woolwick Warren is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon

Sworn this 27th. day of Decr. 1787 } R Butler

her Ann Walker Mark
