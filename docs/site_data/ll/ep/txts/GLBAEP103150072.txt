Middlesex ss}  Christian Hanson  Single woman the Daughter of Christopher Hanson deced maketh oath That divers Years ago her said late Father rented a house of Eighteen pounds a Year in Rosemary Lane is the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & lived therein for many Years & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwd do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That this Deponent lived with him for divers Years in the Said house as part of his Family (Being a Single woman & not having Gained a Settlement in her own right) & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the said parish as deriving the same from her said late Father by living with him as aforesaid And this Depont Saith that she was never married nor ever was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to her know ledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 3d. day of November 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

Chrn Hanson
