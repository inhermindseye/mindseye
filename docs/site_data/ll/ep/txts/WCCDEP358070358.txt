Middlesex & Westmr.}  Henrietta Scott Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her late Husband Samuel Scott (who died about Ten Weeks ago) at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields upwards of Seven Years ago and by whom she hath Three Children now living Namely Stephen Aged about four Years Samuel aged upwards of Two Years and Mary Aged about Six Weeks, Says her said late Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Leepard a Shoemaker in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 9th. day of April 1776 before R Butler

Henrietta Scott
