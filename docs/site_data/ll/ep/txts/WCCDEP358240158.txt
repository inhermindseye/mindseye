Ann Scannan

St. Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster }  St Paul Covent Garden  (to wit) Ann Colvell aged about 54 years upon her Oath (on behalf of her illegitimate Child Ann Scannan ) Saith that she this Deponent was Married to the Father of the said Child William Scannan at Cork in Ireland according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of Rome about 28 years ago that he has been Dead about four or five year ago that her said Child Ann Scannan was born an or about the 13th day of September 1781 at the House of Mr. Dustan in Exeter Street in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County of Middlesex , that the said Child was Removed on or about the 8th of July 1789 by Order of Removal from the parish of St. Clement Danes to the said Parish of St Paul Covent Garden and by virtue thereof maintained by the said parish for sometime that there hath been no Appeal against the said Order to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 8th. day of September 1792 before us  M Addington Gd: Mitchell

Ann Colvell's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
