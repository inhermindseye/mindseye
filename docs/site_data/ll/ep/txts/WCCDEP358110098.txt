Mary Hughes aged upwds. of 50 Years upon Oath saith she is the widow of Richd Hughes who dyed upwds. of 20 Years since and that upwds. of twelve Years ago being then a Widow without Child or Children she was hired as a Yearly Servt. to Mrs. Henshaw in Arundel Street in the Parish of St Clemt Danes  Middx and lived with the sd Mrs. Henshaw in Arundel Street aforesd. at the Yearly Wages of £3 for two Years or thereabouts and has not since She quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Sert. for one Year

Sworn Septr. 29th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Huges
