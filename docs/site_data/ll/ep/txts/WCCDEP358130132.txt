Middx  Elizabeth Norris aged about 50 Years upon Oath says She never was married and that She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Roberts in Duke Street by Lincolns Inn Fields in the Parish of St. Clement Danes five Years and upwards at the yearly Wages of five Pounds and quitted her said Service upwards of Seven years & hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 26 Day of March 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Elizabeth Norris
