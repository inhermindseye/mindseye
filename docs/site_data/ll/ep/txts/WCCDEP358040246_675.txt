Westmr. 

Eliz: Hughes on behalf of her Daughter Ann Macnab widow a Lunalick upon Oath Saith the said Ann Macnab Intermarryed with her late husband John Macnab deceased about Fourteen Years ago at the Temple Church London and hath a Son by her sd. late husband now living (viz. John aged upwards of  13 Years) who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year. Says she has been informed and believes the said John Macnab before his Intermarriage with this Examinants Daughter & whilst a Singleman lived a Waiter with Mr. Pitts at the Jamaica Coffee house in St. Michaels Alley in the Parish of St. Michael in Cornhill London for Seven Years & upwards after the usual and customary manner of hiring Waiters that is for what Vailes he could get in the sd. Mr. Pitts a Service and a certain Yearly allowance Year for Washing and further Says the sd. John Macnab quitted Mr. Pitts Service about fourteen Years ago and then went to Jamaica and there dyed and that her Daughter the said Macnabs Widow has not since her sd. late husbands Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr 2d 1748 before} Tho Lane

Elizabeth Hughes
