Middx  Dorothy Ponder aged Ninetey Years & upwards Wido . of Thomas Ponder upon her Oath saith that before her Intermarriage with her sd. Husband she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. in the Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middx for the Space of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds & further saith that she Intermarried with her sd. late Husband at the Parish Church of St James Dukes Place in the City of London & yt after her Intermarriage her sd. late Husband lived in & rented a House in Kings Head Court in Hoy Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch of the Yearly Rent of four Pounds or thereabts. for the Space of Thirty Years & upwards & pd. to the Minister pd. the & Watch & Scavengers Rates & hath left the same abt. Twelve Years & that neither her Husband nor she since then left the sd. House rented Ten Pound a Year pd. Pish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworn the 5th. Day of August 1741 before}
