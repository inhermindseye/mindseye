Middlesex ss}  Ann Sabery maketh oath that She was heretofore hired and lived as a hired Servant by & with John Carrison if Brown Bear alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of fifteen months & has not Since lived as hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this. 28. day of December 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Ann Sabery
