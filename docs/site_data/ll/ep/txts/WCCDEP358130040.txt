Middx  Elizabeth Strickett Aged about Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Arthur Strickett deceased That her said late Husband served his Apprenticeship of Seven years with Mr. Putland a Vintner in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that her said late Husband in his Lifetime nor She since his Death never Rented Ten Pounds by the Year, paid Parish Taxes, nor lived a yearly Servant

Sworn the of Augst. 1755 before}
