Middx.  John Marshall of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Taylor on behalf of Charles Huddy who appears to be a Lunatic upon Oath saith the said Charles Huddy Rented a House of Ten Pounds a Year & upwards in little Moorfields in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate London and lived in the same upwards of Two Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes as this Examinant has been informed and verily believes

Sworn the 26th. Decr. 1755. before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

Jn Marshall
