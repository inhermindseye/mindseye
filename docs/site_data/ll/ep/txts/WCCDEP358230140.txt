Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Stainsby Aged about 25 years upon her Oath Saith That on or about the 4th day of November 1793 she Intermarried with Joseph Stainsby her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Ethelburga in the City of London That she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that her said Husband was born in the Parish of Saint Ann Blackfriars in the City of London That he never was bound Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 16th day of June 1800 before us} John Reeves  John Collick

Sarah Stainsby her Mark

Wittness J. Starling
