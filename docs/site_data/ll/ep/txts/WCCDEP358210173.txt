Westmr.  Danl. Frazier aged about 54 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his Wife in the Parish Church of Holy Trinity in Coventry about 34 years ago; says that on the 14th, day of September 1738, the Church Wardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster granted a Certificate under their Hands and Seals acknowledging him this Examinant and his said Wife to be Parishioners legally Settled in the said Parish of St. Martin which said Certificate was directed to the said Parish of Holy Trinity in Coventry; says he hath not since the granting sd. Certificate taken a Lease of a Tenement of Ten Pounds a year or served an Annual Parochial Office.

Sworn July 29th. 1767 . before} Saunders Welch  Edm: Byron

The Mark of Danl. Frazier
