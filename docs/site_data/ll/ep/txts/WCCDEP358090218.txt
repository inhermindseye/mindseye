Middx to wit  Margarett Davis Aged about Thirty Eight years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly servant with Mr. John Sharp in Hart Street Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for three years at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas hath quitted her said service about Six years ago since which time She hath gained no other settlement And further says that she hath one Child named James aged Three years and upwards a Bastard born on the Body of this Examinant and which was born at Rumsey in the County of Southampton  which Child hath gained no other settlement  oherewise than by Birth

Sworn this 28th. day of  April 1781 before  Abrm Baley W Addington

Margaret Davis
