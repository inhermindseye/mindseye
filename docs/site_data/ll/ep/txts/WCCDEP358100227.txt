Middlesex to wit Mary Beddle Aged about fifty Seven Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Fredrick Beddle who died about fifteen Years ago and by whom she hath One Daughter named Elizth . Aged about Twenty Eight Years now in Service in St. James Street Says her said Husband & this Examinant after his Death rented a House in Houghton Street  Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds hath quitted said House about Eleven Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid.

Sworn this 13th. Day of July 1784 before R Butler

Mark Mary Beddle
