Middlesex ss}  Mary Swale the wife of James Swale maketh oath that many years ago her said husband took & rented a house of the yearly Rent of Six pounds in Mandlins Rents in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & lived therein for many years & during & during Such his Residence therein he was charg to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & he also Served the office of Headborough in the Said parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement & there has not Since done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that she was lawfy married to her Said husband

Sworn this 5 day of Febry 1778 before  Parkr Rowlands

Mary Swale
