Middx  Mary Sidebottom upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of Samuel Sidebottom who is gone from her & she has not Where he is & that she was married to her sd husband at the fleet London about two years ago & has by him one Child (to wit) Wm. aged abt. 5 months & that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband Served his apprentiship with Mr. Windsor a Shoemaker in Wych Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and never rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Janry 11th 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Sidebotom
