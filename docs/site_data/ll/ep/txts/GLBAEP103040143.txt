Middlesex ss.  Mary Harvey  Singlewoman the Daughter of Thomas & Mary Harvey both deceased maketh Oath that she can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Father or Mother or either of them, that she this Deponent was born in a Place called the Match Walk in the Parish of St. Paul Shadwell in the County of Middlesex , as she has been informed, & believes to be true, and that she was brought up at the Charge of the said Parish, her Father & Mother both dying before she was Three Year old, that she has never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any Parish or Place for the space of One year together or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right and that she never was Married And further saith that being Poor & unable to provide for herself she has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate Middlesex for Relief & has been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 21st. Day of Septr. 1798 before R Davies H: Reynell

her Mary Harvey Mark
