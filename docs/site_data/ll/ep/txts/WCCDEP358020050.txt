St. Clement Danes.

Benjamin Newman of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes. Peruke Maker , Maketh Oath Saith that he has been informed that Frances Eaton a Female Child of Mary Eaton Singlewoman was born at Mr. Kents a Chandlers Shop in Great Cater that Westminster in the Parish of Saints John the Evangelist , And further Saith that the said Child is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and is as above Stated and this Examinant is informed Illegitimate

Sworn before us this 20 Octr. 1787 D Walker Rupt: Clarke

Benj: Newman
