Middx  Ann Watson upon her Oath saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Watson who hath been dead upwds. of Eighteen Years Says her sd. late Husband Informed her that he was bound to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with his Father William Watson in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx but in what Part of the sd. Parish this Examinant can't tell Says her sd. late Husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she this Examint. hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr 29: 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Watson
