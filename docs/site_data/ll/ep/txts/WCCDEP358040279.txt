Eliza: Brown  Wido : of Wm. Brown upon Oath saith her sd. late husband was an Irishman & never gained a Settlemt. in England and that She this Examinant was born at Leeds in Yorkshire and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief and that she hath one Child viz Wm. aged abt. 2 Weeks

Sworn Novr. 7th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Eliza. Brown
