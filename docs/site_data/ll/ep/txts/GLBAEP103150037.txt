Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bussell  Single woman maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with William Masdon in the parish of Saint Bridget in the City of London  Upholsterer & undertaker & Continued in Such Service for about the space of eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds per Year & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant by the year for the Space of a Year nor was she was ever married Nor to her knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this First day of June 1775 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Elizabeth Bussell
