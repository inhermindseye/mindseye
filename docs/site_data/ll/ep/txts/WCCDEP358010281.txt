Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Tregal aged about 69 years upon her Oath saith that She Intermarried with George her late Husband at the Fleet about 50 years ago that her said late Husband has been dead about Two years that he served Seven years Apprenticeship to Mr. James in Marsham Street in the Parish of Saint John in the Liberty of Westminster Shoemaker and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of March 1789  Thos. Collins J Barnfather

her Elizth. Tregal Mark
