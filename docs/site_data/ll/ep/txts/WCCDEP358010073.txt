Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Cassandra Parry aged about 53 years upon her Oath saith that about 22 years ago she Intermarried with John Parry her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Antholin in the City of London that he has been dead about 12 years that she hath two Children livg. by her said late Husband vizt. Cassandra aged about 19 years who is insane and unprovided for and W aged about 17 years Provided for that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Queens Street Cheapside in the Parish of St. Antholin in the City of London for 3 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £32 that he ha quitted said House about 21 years before his said Death since which time he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And furth this Ext. upon her Oath Saith that her Sd. Daur Cassandra who is insane never was bound an apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servt. or don any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 29d. day of Febry. 1787 D Walker Russ: Clarke Cass Parry
