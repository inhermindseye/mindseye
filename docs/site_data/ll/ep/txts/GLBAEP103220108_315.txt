Middlesex ss  Jane Brecknock  wife of Andrew Brecknock maketh oath that her Sd husband lately rented a house in Norwich Court in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwds or Served Any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband.

Sworn this. 13. day of Janry. 1787 before R Davies

Jane Brecknock
