Middx ss  Susannah Humphrys  Widow of William Humphrys deced maketh oath that her sd. late husband has often informed her that he was born in Parrott Alley in ye parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middx And that she has been informed by her sd husbands Mother that this Deponents Father Richard Humphrys Served his apprenticeship to an Anchor Smith in the sd. parish And this Deponent Saith that She and her two Children Mary and Susannah was passed from the sd. parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate to the said parish of Saint John Wapping about Twenty five years ago as the place of their legal settlement which Order or pass warrant to the knowledge or belief of this Depont. was never vacated or set aside And this Deponent farther saith that her sd. Daur Susannah was Never Married to her knowledge or belief And that her sd. Daur Susannah hath a Child baptised by ye Name of William who was born a Bastard in Black Horse Yard in ye sd. parish of St. John Wapping  And this Depont. is now in ye workhouse of St. Jn Wappg.

Sworn this 10th day of May 1754 before Richd: Ricards  Thos: Quarrill

his Susannah Humphrys  Mark
