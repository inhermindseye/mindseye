Middlesex ss}  Maria Hoy  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father Thomas Hoy a Painter deced heretofore rented a house at the yearly rent of Thirty two Guineas in the Precinct of St Catherine in the Sd County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the same & died there legally Settled as this Deponts Sd Mothr also did That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 19. day of July 1786 before J. Hawley Robt. Smith

The Mark of Maria Hoy
