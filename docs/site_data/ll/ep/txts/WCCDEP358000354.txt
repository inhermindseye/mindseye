Saint Clement Danes

Westminster Middx  to wit Sarah Campbell aged about 24 Years upon her Oath saith that about 5 Years ago she intermarried with Walter Campbell who has been dead about 2 years at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone that her said Husband served 5 years Appceship with Mr. Richie a Hairdresser No. 58 Oxford Street in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middx that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship her never did any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any subsequent other Settlement

Sworn this 14th day of Septr. 1786 M Addington N Bond

her Sarah Campbell Mark
