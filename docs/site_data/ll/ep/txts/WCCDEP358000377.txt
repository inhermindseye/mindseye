Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Hannah Williams aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr Groves of Ludgate Street in the Parish of Saint Brides alias Bridgett  in the City London for 15 Months at the yearly Wages of £7 hath quitted said Service about Twelve Months ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Novr. 1786 . G Mercer Jno: Wright

her Hannah Williams Mark
