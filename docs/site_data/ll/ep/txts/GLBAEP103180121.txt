Middlesex ss  Sarah Richards  Singlewoman maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlemt of her late Parents or either of them That She this Depont has been informed by her Mother & which She believes to be true That She this Deponent was born in Sunyard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County That She never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 19. day of January 1779 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Sarah Richards
