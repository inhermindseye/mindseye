Middx  Elizabeth Stevens Aged abt. Sixty Six Years Lodging in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Upon Oath Says She was never Married never was an Apprentice or hired yearly Servt. Rented Ten Pounds a year pd. Poor Rate or Land Tax and cannot give any Accot. of the Settemt. of her father or Mother Says She this Examinant was born over agt. Red Lyon Market in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx aforesd.

Sworn the 24th. May 1762 before}
