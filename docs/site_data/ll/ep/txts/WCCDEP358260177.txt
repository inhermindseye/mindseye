Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Sanders aged about 17 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a years paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That her father Joseph Sanders has Rented Premises for these Two years past in Mint Street in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Surry which Premises according to the best of this Examinant knowledge Information and belief are of the Annual value of Ten Pounds

Sworn this 16th- day of Janry. 1797 Rapt Clarke M Addington

her Ann Sanders Mark Witness B Newman
