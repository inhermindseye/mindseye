Middx to wit Martha Clarke Aged about Seventy Seven Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of George Clarke with whom she Intermarried at the Town of Northampton about 50 years ago and who has been dead about 40 Years Says her said Husband Rented & lived in a House called or known by the name of Sign of the Warwick Castle near the Palatine Houses Stoke Newington in the Parish of Hornsey in the County of Middlesex about 3 Years at the Yearly Rent of £12. hath quitted house upwards of 40 years ago and after quitting same he never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10 a year or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlemt. neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death.

Sworn this 17th. day of June 1783  Wm Kitchiner Addington

Martha Clarke
