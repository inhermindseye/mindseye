St Clement Danes January 10th.. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Luke Chelsea  (to wit) Margaret Coyte  singlewoman  aged about Sixteen Years and Martha Coyte Wife of William Coyte severally upon Oath say and first this Deponent Margaret Coyte for herself saith that she never was Married served any Apprenticeship lived an hired yearly Servant or otherwise obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right that she is pregnant with Child and unable to support the Expences of her lying in And this Deponent Margaret Coyte for herself saith that she th was Married to William Coyte at the parish Church of St Margaret Westminster to (by License) about 20 years ago that the said Margaret was born the Daughter of her this Examinant by her said Husband on or about the seventh day of November 1777 that the said William Coyte by Rented and Resided in a Messuage with Land and premises in the parish of St Sloane Street in the parish of St Luke Chelsea of the Annual Value of £10 and upwards for upwards of four Months that he quitted the same about ten or eleven years and hath not since done any act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the tenth day of January 1794 before us } W Addington Edw: Read
