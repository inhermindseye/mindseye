Middlesex to wit Mary Turner Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of William Turner (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried a the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn in Febry 1779 and by whom she hath one Child now livingna Samuel Aged about One Month Says her said Husband Served Three Years the latter Part of his Apprenticeship with One Mr. Jones an Engraver near White Count Alley Shoreditch in the Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement otherwise than as before mentioned

Sworn this 24th. day of Febry. 1780 before R Butler M Addington

Mary Turner
