Middx  Mary Taylor  Singlewoman aged 16 Years upon her Oath saith that abt. one Year since she was bound Apprentice by the Church wardens & Overseers of the poo of the parish of St. Martin in the feilds in the Libty of Westmr. to Mrs. Susannah Milson  Wife of Jacob Milson of Clare Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Taylor & lived with her as an Apprentice for the Space of Nine Months & then her sd. Mistress dyed & the sd. Jacob Milson went away from this Exaiant that she hath not been Assigned over to anyother person or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 31st. Janry 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Mary Taylor
