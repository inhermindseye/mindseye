Middx The Examination of M Greenaway on the Behalfe of Susannah the Wife of Henry Timms Taken the 27th- Day of January 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that upwards of one Year ago the above named Henry Timms rented a House in Tothill Feilds in the Parish of St. John the Evangelist in the City of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of two Years & upwards. And this Examint. further saith that the said Henry Timms hath not since that Time rented ten Pound a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place as this Examinant hath been informed & which he verily beleives That the said Henry Timms now absconds from Susanah his said Wife & she being a Sick & Poor & having one Child (to wit) Martha aged about three Years is not able to maintaine herself & Child

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Cha: Egerton

M Greenaway
