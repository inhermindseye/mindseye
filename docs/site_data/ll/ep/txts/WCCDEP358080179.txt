Middx- & Westmr-}  Harriot Elly aged 40 Yrs. on her Oath says she is the Wife of Wm- Elly (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at St- Martins in the Fields upwards of seven Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children living named Job aged two years & Nine Months and Charlotte aged 5 Months Says her said husband Rented and lived in a House known by the Sign of the Crown in Feathers Court Westons Park in the Parish of Saint Giles's in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr - and County of Middx for Eleven Months or thereabouts at the Year Rent of Eighteen Pounds and paid all Parish. Taxes due for the same hath quitted the same about Two Years ago since which time her said husband never Rented house paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a subseqt- Settlemt-

Sworn this 1st- Day of Novr- 1777 before R Butler Wm- Kitchiner

Harriott Elly
