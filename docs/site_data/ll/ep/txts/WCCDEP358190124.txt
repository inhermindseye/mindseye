Middx  Hannah Painter aged about 31 Years Lodging at Mr. Cardiff's in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middlesex upon Oath saith she is the Widow of William Painter who has been Dead about 19 Months to whom she was Married at May Fair Chapel the 17th: day of February 1751 That her said Husband lived in and rented a House in Great Shire Lane in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds and there dyed and that she this Examinant continued to Rent the same House from the time of her Husband's Decease until Lady Day last That she hath five Children by her said Husband to wit William aged Nine Years Mary aged Six Years & upwards John aged four Years & upwards Thomas aged three Years and James aged fifteen Months all of which are now with this Examinant

Sworn the 2d. day of May 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Hannah Painter
