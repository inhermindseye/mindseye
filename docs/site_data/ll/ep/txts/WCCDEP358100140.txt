Middx & Westmr. to wit Sarah Barrett aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived in the Capacity of a Servant with one Mrs. Sherbrooke in Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twenty Months or thereabouts but received no Wages for such servitude except Meat Drink Washing Lodging & pocket Money which she believes might amount to Three Pounds & upwards hath quitted the said Mrs. Sherbrooks Place about Christmas last since which time she hath gained no other Settlement than as aforsd. And further on her Oath says that On the 24th. day of Augt. last she was delived of a Female Bastard Child in the Hospital in Store Street  Tottenham Court Road which hath since been baptized Elizabeth and is now become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further says that one John Ebeck  Clerk to Mr. Loyd in Chancery Lane and who lodge at Mr. Madden in Bell yard is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings in Bell yard sometime in the Month of Novr. last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered and that the said Ebeck and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 18th. day of Octr. 1783 before R Butler

her Sarah Barrett mark
