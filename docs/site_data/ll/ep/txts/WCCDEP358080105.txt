Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jeremiah Taylor Aged about sixty Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at St. Leonard Shoreditch about twenty Years ago and by whom he hath one Child now living vizt. John Aged about thirteen Years Says he served seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Mears an Ivory Turner in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Years or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th Day of Jany. 1777 . R Butler John Croft

Jeremiah Taylor
