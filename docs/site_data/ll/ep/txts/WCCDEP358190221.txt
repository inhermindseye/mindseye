Middx   Mary Rampell aged about 26 Years Destitute of Lodging upon Oath saith she never was Married bound an Apprentice kept House paid Land Tax or Poor Rates or been a hired Yearly Servant for One year Says her Father Jacob Rampell who has been Dead about five Years was a Gingerbread baker and lived in and rented a House in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Several Years at the yearly Rent of £25 or thereabouts Says her said Father never rented any House after he quitted the same except one in Rose Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields for which he paid neither Land Tax or Poor Rate as she has been informed and verily beleives

Sworn the 16th. day of February 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Rampall
