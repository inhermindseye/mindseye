Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Shirley aged about 29 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was Married never bound apprentice rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement in her own right That she hath been Informed which Information she verily believes to be true That she was born in  New Street in the Parish of Saint Ann Blackfriars in the City of London  That further this Deponent upon her Oath Saith That on the 21st day of December 1798 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of Mr Canuthers N18 Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which Child hath since been Christened by the Name of John Thomas

Sworn this 16th day of Octr 1800 before us} Saml Edwards  John Collick

her Mary Shirley Mark

Witness B Newman
