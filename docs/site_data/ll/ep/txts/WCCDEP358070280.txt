Westmr to wit Joseph Cross Aged about fifty two Years on his Oath Says, that he Intermarried with Mary his Wife (gone from him) at the Parish Church of Saint Andrews Holbourn in the Year 1768 and by whom he hath one Child now living, namely Peter aged upwards of five Years, gone with his Mother, but where they are he does not know: Says he Rented and lived in a House in Yeomans Row Brumpton in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middlesex for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of fifteen Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath Quitted his said House about four Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th day of July 1775 before R Butler M Addington

Joseph Cross
