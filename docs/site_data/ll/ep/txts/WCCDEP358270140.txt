After Birth Lydia Yates and Elizth Holmes her B. Child

St Clement Danes September 19th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman's Settled in St Martin in the fields  Child born in Stanhope Street  Reputed Father} (to wit) Lydia Yates  singlewoman aged 23 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Barrow in the Kings Mews in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for a year and half or upwards at the Annual Wages of Seven Guineas that She quitted that Service about two years ago upwards of a year ago and hath never since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that on the 23d day of June in the year of our Lord 1792 at the House of Mr Marshall in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath been Baptized by the Name of Elizabeth Holmes that one Richard  Robert Holmes  a Hair Dresser who (as this Deponent is informed and believes) now works for Mr. Ferguson is Noble Duke Street Liverpool is the only real true father of the said Child

of which she was so delivered a aforesaid and that he and no one else is the real true father thereof

Sworn the 19th Day of September 1793 before me Mitchell

Lidia yates

Mr Yates lodges at No 35 Great Saffron Hill at Mr Beaumonts & goes by the Name of Mrs Elm Mr Helnar Mr Ferguson's Hair Dresser Noble Diche Street Liverpool was there as She heard abt 20th Septr last Middle Size Man light Hair full Light Grey Eyer Pitted or little with small Pox a well made Man Dobbins Beadle nothing done the Man went from Liverpool to Oxford
