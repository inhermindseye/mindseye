Middx to Wit James Ray Aged about forty Three years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Susannah his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Thomas in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry in the year 1777 And by whom he hath no Child or Children living Says he served Seven years Apprenticeship with one Saml. Turner Glover at Ridgwell in the Parish of Ridgwell in the County of Essex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any or. Subsequent Settlemt. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or orwise howsoever.

Sworn this 26th. day of July 1783  Sampn Wright W Addington

James Ray
