Middlesex to wit Jane Simpson aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House Paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says she at Present lives as a hired Yearly Servant  with one Mr. Anderson a Taylor in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx and as so done for some time past And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same wher born and that one Mr. John Ruitz who lodges at the Union Coffee House in the Strand had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings in Drury Lane on the 9th. day of May last  and at one other time afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Mr. John Ruitz & no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 21st. day of Septr. 1779 before M Addington

her Jane Simpson Mark
