Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Susannah Dunkley of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the sd County this Third day of Deember 1783 Who Saith that She is now with Child & that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that William Nixon of Castle Street near Liecester Fields in the sd County of Middx  Box maker is the Father of the sd Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me R Davies

The Mark of Susannah Dunkley
