Westmr.  Edward Rich upon Oath saith that upwards of 20 Years ago being then a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Blows at Esley in the County of Cambridge upwds. of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds and has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 2d. 1747 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Edwd. Rich
