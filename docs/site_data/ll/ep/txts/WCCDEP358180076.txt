Middx  Ann Willson  Wido . of Thomas Willson upon her Oath saith that on the 10th. Day of June about 25 Years since she intermarried with her said late Husband Thos. Willson at the Fleet London That the said Thomas Willson Served 5 Years the first Part of his Apprentiship with Mr Lilley in the Green Walk in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry that her sd. late Husbd. never was assigned over to any Person rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes nor hath this Examinant since his Death rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne March 29th- 1743 before} J Bromfield

The Mark of Ann Willson
