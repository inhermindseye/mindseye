Middlesex ss}  Sarah Bowen  widow of William Bowen deced maketh oath That she this Deponent can give no account respecting the Settlement of her said late husband Nor of her late Father Richard Moulds deced nor of her late Mother Jane Moulds deced save that her said Mother has informed this Deponent that she herself as well as this Deponent were both born in the Parish of St. Nicholas Deptford in the County of Kent That she this Deponent Never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound apprentice nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to Acquire a Settlement for herself Except what She gained by Virtue of her Marriage with her said late husband whose Place of Settlement is unknown to her

Sworn this 27 day of August 1773 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

The Mark of Sarah Bowen
