Middlesex ss}  Hannah Man  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages with Nicholas Spruson in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd. County Tobacconist & Continued in Such Service for Several Years in the said Parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there that She this Deponent hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was never married.

Sworn this 6th. day of November 1773 before B Camper

The Mark of Hannah Man
