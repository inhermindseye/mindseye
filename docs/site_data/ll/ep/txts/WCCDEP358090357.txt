Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Child aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Michael Child late a Sheriffs Officer  Rented and lived in a House in Holles Street Clare Market  for four Years at the Yearly Rent of Thirty five Pounds a Year & paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House upwards of Three Years ago since which time he never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Parish And further says that one John Edwin a Comedian at Covent Garden House had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of June last but the Particular day she does not now remember at his the said Mr. Edwins Lodgings under the Piazza in Covent Garden and at Sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said John Edwin and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 18th. day of Decr. 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Child
