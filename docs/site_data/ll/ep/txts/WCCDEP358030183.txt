Middx  Catherine Hitchcock  Wido . upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her late Husband Edward Hitchcock at the Fleet London upwards of twelve Years since Says he hath been dead about two weeks Says her sd. late husband Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Andrew Basil a Brush maker & lived with the sd. Mr. Basil the latter Part of his Apprentiship in as, she hath been informed & beleives Says the sd. Edwd. Hitchcock never was a Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath 3 Childn. viz Eliz aged upwds. of 11 Yrs. Sarah aged upwds. of 5 Yrs. & Cathn. aged upwards of one Year & being Sick & poor is not able to maintain them

Sworn July 14th. 1746 before}
