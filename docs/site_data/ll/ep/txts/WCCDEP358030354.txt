Westmr. 

Hannah Freelove on behalf of George Freelove her Son a Lunatich upon Oath Says that her husband George Freelove is poor & not able to maintain the said George Freelove the Lunatich says that upwards of four Years since he lived a Yearly Servant with one Mr. John Dennison at the two Angells & Crown in Clare Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Two pounds & ten Shillings or Three pounds a Year but which this Exaiant can't remember Says he hath not since he left the sd. Mr. Dennisons Service is Clare Street rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 6th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Hannh. Freelove
