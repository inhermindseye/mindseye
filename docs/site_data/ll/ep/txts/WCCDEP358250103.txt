Saint Clement Danes 20th. day of July 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James Westmr . (to wit) Mary Harman Aged 26 Years and upwards Upon her Oath Saith that she was Married to her present Husband James Harman at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster in the County of Middlesex at 8 Years ago That her said Husband lived with Mr. Hicking button late of Picadilly in the said Parish of Saint James for the space of 5 Years at the Annual Wages of £7:0:0 per Annum that he quitted that place about five Years ago and he hath never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for a Year together since rented any House or Apartment of £ 15 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information a belief That her said Husband left her unprovided for about 6 Week ass and knows not where he is save and that she is unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn this 20th. day of July 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

her Mary Harman Mark

Chas: Wycross
