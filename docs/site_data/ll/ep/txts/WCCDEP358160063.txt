Middx  The Exaicon of Jno. Deering lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 7th. Day of July. 1739 Who upon his Oath Saith that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years apprentispp with one Mr. Witworth in the Parish of St. Beneditt Vulg Bennetffink  London  Blacksmith and hath been out of his time abt. Eighteen Years and further Saith that he never rented any House or lived with any person as a yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemts. in any other place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath wife & one Child (to wit) Anne Aged abt. Eight Weeks & is now sick & poor & not able to maintaine them

Sworn the day & year above menconed before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

John Deering
