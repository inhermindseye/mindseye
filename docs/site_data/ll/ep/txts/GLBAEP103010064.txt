Middlesex, to wit, Matilda Kelly , Widow , Maketh Oath and Saith, that she has heard and believes that she was born in a House in Little Burr Street , in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate , Middlesex , which her Father George Duncan rented and lived in many years, and for which he paid Poor's Rates as this Deponent believes, and did not afterwards gain a subsequent Settlement in any other place, and that he died in Little Burr Street aforesaid, that she lived with her father as part of his Family for many Years, and never gained a Settlement in her own right, that she was lawfully married to John Kelly , as Irishman and a Mariner , at the Parish Church of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex , and that her said husband never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England

Sworn this 2d Day of July 1791 , before me R Davies Matilda Kelly
