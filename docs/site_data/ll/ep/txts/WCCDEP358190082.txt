Middx.  Eliz: Hursey aged abt. upwards of 50 Years upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Bromley a Fishmonger near Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx, for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5 and hath Quitted said Service abt. Nine Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Jany. 9th. 1761 . before } Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Hursey
