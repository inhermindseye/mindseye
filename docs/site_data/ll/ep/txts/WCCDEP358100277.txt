Middlesex & Westminster}  Swr. to Clemt.  A. (to wit) Thomas Cockburn , aged 66, years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith, That he rented part of a House in Bennett Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement danes in the County of Middlesex of a Mrs. Grimstone Widow at the Yearly Rent of ten pounds that he lived there between seven and eight, Years, and quitted his said Lodgings about thirty Years ago, and since that this has not done any Act Matter or Thing which to the best of his Knowledge Information or Belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere that in the Said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 8th. Novr - 1784 before  Thos: Gilbert .

Thos. Cockburn
