Middlesex ss}  Jane Hutchinson  Singlewoman maketh oath that about Fifty Seven years ago She was born in Fullers Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at Which time her late Father George Hutchinson deced as he has informed her & which She believes to be true rented a house there & was Charged to & paid the Scavengers Tax for such house & by means thereof as She is advised gained a legal settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her Said Father did not afterwards do any Act to gain another settlement That she this Deponent was never Married nor was ever bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year Nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Jane Hutchinson
