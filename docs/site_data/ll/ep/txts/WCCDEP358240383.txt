St Clement Danes  31st Janry. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Giles in the Fields  (to wit) Martha Bryant aged about 35 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to Isaac Bryant at the parish Church of Stroud Water in the County of Gloucester on or about the 18th day of October 1787 (by Banns) that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and Resided in a Cellar in Mont Monsmouth Street at No. 3 next Moore Street in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for two Years and upwards at the Weekly Rent of four shillings that the said Cellar was unfurnished they that he quitted the same in or about the Month of October last and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement the best of her (this Examinant's) knowledge or belief that she hath two Children by her said Husband namely Samuel  aged about three Years and four Months and William aged Nine Months And upwards that her said Husband is from this Examinant and (as she believes) at Chatham Barracks and she this Examinant is unable to Maintain herself and family without assistance

Sworn the 31st Janry. 1794 before us  Rich.. Ford B: Kennett

Martha Bryant 's Mark

Witness Garrett

Removed by B. Newman who attended when the Woman Sworn 31st Janry/94
