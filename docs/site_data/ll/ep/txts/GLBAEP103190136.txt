Liberty of the Tower ss. of London Mary Freshwater maketh oath that Some years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with John Curtis Victualler in the Parish of St John Hackney in the sd County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one year & half & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath she done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 25. day of August 1780 before  Jasper Clarke W Blackmore

The Mark of Mary Freshwater
