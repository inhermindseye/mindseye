Bastards

St Clement Danes May 30th 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  Pregnt. (to wit) Mary Crutchley  singlewoman aged 24 years or thereabouts upon Oath saith That She never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant hath never acquired any Settlement in her own Right That her Father rented and resided in a House in Houghton Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for about 3 years and an half at the Annual Rent of £30 and quitted the same about four years ago since which time heh he hath never obtained any subsequent legal Settlement and this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath further saith That she is now Pregnant Pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes when born and That William Johnson a labouring Man who works with in a Timber yard called the Hog yard in Limehouse in the County of Middlesex and resides at No. 2 in Soap House rents  Limehouse aforesaid is the only real true father of such Child or Children where with she is so pregnant he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body sometime on or about the Month of October last about Seven Months ago and once since which was about the end of March last at one of which times he did beget the Child or Children wherewith she is now pregnant on the Body

of her this Deponent and That he and no one else is the only real and true father of such Child or Children and that she is Poor and unable to defray the Expences of her and cannot support the said Child or Children when Born

Sworn the  30th day of May 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Crutchley 's Mark

Witness Edwd Burbidges

Woman in the House Man usually at Work bet of 6 & 7 o Clock & from 2 to 7 weng Always Dines at home at one
