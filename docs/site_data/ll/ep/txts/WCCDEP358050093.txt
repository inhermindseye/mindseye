Westmr.  Susn. Carrington aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Jones in Compton Street in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. for one Year and upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds and hath quitted her sd. Service upwards of one Yr. since which she has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 31st. 1749 before H Fielding

Susan Carin ten Caruton
