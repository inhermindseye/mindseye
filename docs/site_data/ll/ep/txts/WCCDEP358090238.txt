Middx  Amelia Dean otherwise Caffaty Aged about Twenty Eight years an her Oath says she Intermarried to One Phillip Caffaty an Fishman (who was by trade a shoemaker and who never gained a settlement in England either by Renting House or otherwise) at the House of Mrs. Butcher in Rope Makers Alley Moor Fields by a Romish Priest which Marriage is of no Effect by the Laws of this Country as this Examinant has been Informed and believes and further says that she has Two Children by the aforesaid Phillip Caffaty Namely Mary aged about four years born in Petty Coat Lane in the Parish of Aldgate London and Phillip Aged about Eight Months born in Rope makers alley aforesaid in the Parish of Saint Luke in the County of Middlesex And further says that Her said two Children have gained no other settlement then by Births as this Examinant has been Informed and verily believes And this Examinant further says that before such her Intermarriage with the sd Philip Caffaty and whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived a Hired yearly servant with her Uncle Cornelius Dean a Draft Board maker in Little Distaff Lane in the Parish of Saint Augustin Alias Saint Austin in the City of London upwards of Two years at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of Fifteen years ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a Hired yearly Servant for One year

Sworn this 17th. day of July 1781 before  Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

Amelia Caffaty
