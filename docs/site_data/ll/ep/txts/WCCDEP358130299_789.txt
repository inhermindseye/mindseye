Middx.  Christian Hays aged abt. 60 Yrs. upon Oath says, She never was married; says She lived in a house of her own abt. twenty Yrs. ago, on St. Michael's Hill in the Parish of St. Michael in the City of Gloucester for upwards of two Years & paid to the Poor there, & then sold the sd. House; & never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant

Sworn the day of March 1757. before}
