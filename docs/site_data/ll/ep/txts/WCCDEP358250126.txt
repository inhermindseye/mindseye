Hannah Hayes , Child of John and Hannah Hayes

St. Clement Danes June 30th 1794.

Middlesex and Westminster }  Hannah Hayes born 5th Janry 1790  Settlement in right of her Mother in Greenwich Kent} (to wit) Sarah Jones  singlewoman on behalf of her Niece Hannah Hayes Upon Oath saith that the said Hannah Hayes is the legitimate Child of John and Hayes (who this Examinant has heard is abroad) by Hannah his Wife deceased, and was born on or about the fifth or sixth day of January 1790 at the House of Mr Miller Shoemaker New Court Rope Maker Street Moor fields in the Parish of St Giles Cripplegate as she is informed & believes, And his Examinant Upon her Oath further with that she knows not nor has heard where the said John Hayes was born or legally settled that she is informed and believes her Sister the said Hannah Hayes the elder was lived as an an hired as a yearly Servant with Mrs Aldershaw late of Greenwich in the County of Kent but now of Northumberland Street in the Strand Middlesex for several year's while she was single and unmarried that the Cart four Months of year of such her service she lived with her said Mistress in King Street in the Town and Parish of Greenwich aforesaid and that she was Married to the said John Hayes in the parish Church of Greenwich (by Banns) about two Months before she quitted her said Mistress Service

Sworn the 30th day of June 1794 before us  M Addington N Bond

Sarah Jones
