Westmr.  Elizabeth Maculla aged about 45 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Daniel Maculla with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields upwards of five Years since, says her said husband has for sometime past been disordered in his Senses and is incapable of giving a rational Account of himself says her said husband (before he was afflicted with the said Disorder and when in his proper Senses) has often times informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he is the Son of Andrew Maculla Esqr . deceased late a Captain in the Coldstream Regiment of foot Guards , that her said husband was born in Bell Yard in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westminster in which place his father Rented & lived in a house for several Years says her said husband never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as he has likewise informed her and which information she also believes to be true

Sworn 22d. Augst. 1765 before} Fran Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizabeth Maculla
