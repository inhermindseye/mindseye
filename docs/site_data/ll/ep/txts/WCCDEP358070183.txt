Westmr.ss  Sarah Humphrys Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says the never was married Rented House ever or served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Geo who lately kept the Half Moon and Seven Stars the Corner of Denzell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years & an half or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Forty Shillings for the first Year fifty Two shillings for the Second and Three Pounds for the third hath quitted her said Service upwards of five Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year and Further upwards Volentary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) Says that one Edward Ward a Brawn Maker who now lives with his Mother at No. 6 Baynes's Row Cold Bath Fields is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in July last the Day after st James Day to the best of her knowledge and at Sevt. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant of and that the said Edward Ward and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 21st. day of March 1774 before W Kelynge

her Sarah Humphry mark
