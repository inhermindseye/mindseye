Middx  Mary Nash upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her late Husband Charles Nash in the parish Church of St. Clement Danes on the 12th. Day of Novr. about Seventeen or Eighteen Years since by whom she hath one Child (to wit) Margaret aged about 15 Years Says her sd. late Husband Charles Nash learnt his Trade of his Father  Humphry Nash with whom he lived as an Apprentice for Seven Years in Stone Cutters Alley near Lincolns Inn feilds in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx and where the sd. Humphry Nash then did & still continues to Rent a house of Ten pounds a Yr. & upwards and pays his Share of all Parochiall Taxes Says her sd. late Husband never Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not his Death Rented ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 19th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Nash
