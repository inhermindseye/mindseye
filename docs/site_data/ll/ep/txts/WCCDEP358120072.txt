Middx  John Pritchard aged upwds. of 60 years upon Oath Saith he Served Seven years Apprentiship with his father Jno. Pritchard a Shoemaker in the parish of Kensington in the County of Middx and never was a yearly Servt. nor ever rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes

Sworn March 6th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Jno: Prichard
