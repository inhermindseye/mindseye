Middx  The Examination of Love Townsend aged forty one Years living in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 17th. Day of March 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that about a Year ago he lived in and rented a House in St. Clements Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt Danes of the Yearly Rent of twenty Pounds for the Space of ten Years & further on his Oath saith that he hath not since that Time rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Love Townsend
