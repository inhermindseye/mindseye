Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Eallett aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 14th: day of July 1794 she Intermarried with John Eallett her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster that previous to her Intermarriage with her present husband and whilst she was a Singlewoman Vizt. on the 27th. day of January 1792 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child (which hath since been Christened by the name of Catherine ) at the house of a Mr. Tailor in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex That one Thomas Clarke a Sheriffs Officer and who lives in Webber Street Black Friars Road is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of her Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Thomas Clarke and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 12th. day of Novr. 1795 } M Addington

Mary Eallett

Mary Eallett No. 15 Amelia Row at the End of Brick Street St. Geos. Fields

Man taken 20th. Novr. 1795

Settled by paying 15 Guas Vizt. 5 Guas down and a Note for Ten Guas payable in 2 Mos. which was afterwards paid

The Mother is Allowed P week for this Child
