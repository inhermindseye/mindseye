Middx  The Exaicon of Ann Cowley  Wido . of Thos. Cowley Deced who upon her Oath Saith that her sd. Husband Thos. Cowley was Bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years apprentishipp with one Mrs. Applewhite Typemaker in Bainbridge Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx and further Saith that her sd. Husband in his Lifetime nor this Examnt since his Death never Rented any House or lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife and is now sick & Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe.

Sworn the 17th Day of Augst. 1739 before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Ann Cowley
