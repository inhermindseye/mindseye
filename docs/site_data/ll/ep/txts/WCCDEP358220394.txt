Westmr.  Mary Renn Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Saml. Prichard  Dealer in Horses at Harrow Wheel Common in the Parish of Harrow on the Hill in the County of Middlesex for fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about five Months ago & hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. of Octr. 1772 before me}  R Butler

her Mary Renn mark
