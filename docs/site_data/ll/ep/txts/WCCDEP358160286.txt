Middx  The Examination of Ann Wall aged about Sixty Years liveing in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 24th Day of March 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that abt. Six Years ago she lived in & rented a House in little Sheir Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes for the Space of four Years & upwards at the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds & further on her Oath saith that she hath not since that Time rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Ann Wall
