Middlesex ss  Isabella Younger  widow of John Younger deced maketh oath that her Said late husband about twenty four Years Ago lived in & rented a house in Log alley in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in Westmr in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds & resided there on for the space ofter Nears & upwards & did not afterwards Rent a house of Lodging of the yearly rent or Value Ten Pounds or upwards for Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath a Child named Francis Aged about twelve years & Six Months the lawful is she of this Deponent by her Sd. husband to whom She was lawfully married The which Child has not as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement for him self

Sworn this- 9. day of August 1787 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Isabella Younger
