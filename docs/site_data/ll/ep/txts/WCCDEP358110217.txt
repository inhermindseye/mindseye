Westmr.  Ann Smith upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she Served three Years Apprentiship with Mrs. Brown a Hoop Petticoat maker in Mary le bone in the Parish of St Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 27th. Day of March 1753 } Fran: Bedwell

Ann Smith
