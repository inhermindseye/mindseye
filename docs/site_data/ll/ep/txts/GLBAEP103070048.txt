Middx  Jane Harrad  wife of Samford Harrad Maketh oath that she knows Mary Walker the Wife of Thomas Walker late of Kent Street in the Parish of St. George Southwark in the County of Surry the Mother of a Female Child aged about Six Weeks born as this Depont. believes in the parish of St. Margaret Westmr. And this Depont. Saith that on Saturday last was a fortnight she was informed by one Elizabeth Bush that the Sd Mary Walker had on the Evening of the Same Day Dropped left her Sd Child at or Near the Workhouse Door of ye Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx which information this Depont believes to be true she the sd. Mary Walker who then lodged in the Same house in Kent Street aforesd. where this Depont Lodged coming home that Night with her Sd. Child & Declare. to this Dept. that she had left if in ye sd Parish of St Margarete Westmr. And this Depont has been Since Credibly informed that she Sd. Child was accordingly Dropped at

the Workhouse of thes. Parish at the time before Mentioned And that the Same is Now in the Workhouse of the sd. Parish of st. Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 19 day of November 1751 before  Richd. Ricards

Jane Harrad

Nov: ye 21 1751 Stephn Brown Complaineth in behalf of ye Parish of St Butolph with did Aldgate of ye Above said Made eaner as he is Church warden thereof & Craveth a warr to bring ye sd Mary Walker to Justice

Stephen Brown
