Middlesex}  Catherine Brady  Single woman the Daughter of Charles Brady late of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Green Grocer deced maketh oath that her Said late Father was by birth an Irishman & was lawfully married in Ireland as this Deponent has been Credibly informed & believes to this Deponents late mother Margaret Brady deced who was also by Birth an Irish woman That she this Deponent was born in Dublin in Ireland and Came over with her Said Father to England When She was about nine years old & resided with him as part of his Family in the Said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate for many years & till his Death in Which Parish he died and was there legally Settled at the time of his death by renting a house of Ten Pounds a year & upwards & being charged to and paying Poors Rates there for divers years That She this Deponent Since She left her said Father has not lived as a hired Servant in any one place for the Space of year nor hath She been bound an Apprentice nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself Since She Came into England with her Said Father Charles Brady deceased

Sworn this. 30 day of April-1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Catherine Brady
