Middx  Eliz Cox upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Thomas Cox (gone from her) that she hath been informed her sd husband never rented Ten Pounds a year or paid parish Taxes nor ever was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one Year and that her sd husbands father now lives in and Rents a house at Banbury in Oxfordshire

Sworn Augt. 6. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Cox
