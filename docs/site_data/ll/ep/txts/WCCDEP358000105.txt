Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

passd. to St. Paul Covt Gardn. to wit Margt. Bartleman aged about 21 years on behalf of Elizth. Bartleman who is insane upon her oath saith that her Sister lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Mashett of Denmark Court in the strand in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings that she her said Sister hath quitted same upwards of five years ago since which time her said Sister has done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 29th. day of August 1785  Thos: Parker  John Croft

Margaret Bartleman
