Middlesex  Hannah Wills the widow of James Wills deced maketh oath that divers years ago her Said late husband lived in & rented a house of Fourteen pounds a year Rent in Green Field Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the Sd County & Continued in the occupation thereof for one year & upwards & died there in That She this Depont. after his death lived in & rented a house of eighteen pounds a year Rent in Rosemary Lane in the Same Parish & Continued in the occupation thereof for about Six Months That She has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or been married Since her Said late husbands death or done any Act to gain another Settlement That at the time this Depont lived in Such last mentioned house her Daughter & lawful Issue Jane Wills by her Sd late husband who is at present Some what disordered in her Mind lived with this Deponent in the Same house as part of her Family And this Deponts Said Daughter never was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever Married to this Deponts knowledge or belief nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this. 24. day of June 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Hannah Wills
