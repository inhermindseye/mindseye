Middx  Eleanr Thornley upon Oath saith she is the Wife of George Thornley with whom she intermarried about 14 Years ago, in Portugal. that she hath been Informed her sd. husband was born in Quality Court in Chancery Lane in the parish of St. Andw. Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex where his father lived in and rented a house & that he Served part of his apprentiship wth. his sd. father there & never was assigned over to any other person nor ever rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes in any part of Great Britain & that she hath one Child (viz) Wm. aged two weeks

Sworn Augt. 25th. 1752 before} W Fielding Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Eleanr Thornley

1727,1728,1729
