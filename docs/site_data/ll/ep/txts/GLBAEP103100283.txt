Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Agnes Greave of the Parish of St. Botolph without aldgate in the Said County single woman taken on oath before me Robert Pell Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County of Middlesex this 26th Day of May 1770

Who on her oath saith That she is now with Child and that the Child or Children where with she now goeth & is pregnant is or Are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate And that George Gill of the same Parish Carpenter is the Father of the said Child or Children

Sworn this 26th Day of May 1770 before- R Pell

Agnes Greave
