Middx  Eliz. Willoughby upon Oath Says that on or about the 24th. Day of June 1744 she was delivered of a female bastard Child in St Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been Baptized Elizth. Says that one Henry White did begett the sd. Child on her Body she this Exaiant & the sd. Henry White living together as man & Wife in Loves Court in George Alley in the Parish of St. Bridgett otherwise Brides London where the sd. Henry White had carnall knowledge of this Examinants Body sevll. times at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. & that he the sd. Heny. White & no other person in the true & reall Father thereof Says the said

Henry White was Transported beyond the Sea as she hath been Informed & beleives

Sworn Augt. 6. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz Willoughby
