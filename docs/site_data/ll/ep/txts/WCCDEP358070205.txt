Westmr. ss  Elizth. Pound Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Edward Pound at the Parish Church of St Martins in the fields in the County of Middlesex the 18th Octr 1773 and who is now in the East Indies says her said Husband lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Bilson at No 85 in Cannon Street in the Parish of St Swithin London upwards of One Year & an half at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds as she believes hath Quitted his said Service upwards of half a Year Ago & hath not Since he Quitted the same done any other Act Whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 23d Day of Augt 1774 before- R. Butler William Addington

her Elizth Pound Mark

Wittness James Viger
