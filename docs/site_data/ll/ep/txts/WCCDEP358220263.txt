Westmr. to wit Jane Bray Aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Charles Bray who has been dead upwards of four Years and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Nicholas Cole Abbey about Eight Years ago by whom she hath one Child now living Named Ann Aged upwards of five Years says that her said late Husband served three Years the latter Part of his Apprenticeship with Thomas Stevenson a Carpenter in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House or Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands death done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of May 1771 before me R A R. Butler

her Jane Bray Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
