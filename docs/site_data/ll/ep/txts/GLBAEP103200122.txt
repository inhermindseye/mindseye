Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Norton  Singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr Poorman a Victualler in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of Six Months & then this Deponent left her sd Masters Service & was absent for more than the Space of a year & when She returnd again to her sd Master & was hired again by him & Contiuned there with him under Such last mentd hiring her about the Space of Seven Months when She was discharged again from his service that She this Deponent never lived in Any one Service as a hired Servant for the Space of a year altogether nor ever did any Act to gain a settlemt. in her own right save as above mentioned.

Sworn this. 27. day of March 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Norton
