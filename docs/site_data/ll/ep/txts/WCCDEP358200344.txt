Westmr.  John Baitson aged about 68 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his Wife in Mayfair Chapel about Twenty Seven Years ago, says he Rented a House in Crown Court Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds, and lived therein about two Years; hath quitted same about Thirty Years and hath not since Rented House of Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 8th, day of January 1766 , Before} Paul Vaillant

Jno Baitson
