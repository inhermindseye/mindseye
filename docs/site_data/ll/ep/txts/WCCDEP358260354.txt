Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Twine aged about 62 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 21st. day of February 1764 she Intermarried with Thomas Twine her late Husband who has been dead about 26 years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said late Husband previous to her Intermarriage with this Examinant and being a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Judge Nares in Carey Street in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Ten Pounds that after he quitted said Service he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 17th. day of Octr. 1798 before us }  John Collick

her Mary Twine Mark

Witness AW Burbidge Beadle
