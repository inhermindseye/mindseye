Middx  Joshua Izard aged abt. 57 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx . of the Yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds lived therein abt. three Years & quitted the same abt. Thirteen Years ago & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 8th. day of Octr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond Joshua Izard
