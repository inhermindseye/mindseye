Middlesex ss}  Daniel Fanch maketh oath that was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Thomas Merriman of Burr Street in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County Tallow Chandler & Continued there in such Service under such hiring for the space of fifteen months That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or rented a house of the yearly rent or Value or Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7 day of November 1785 before R Davies

Daniel Fanch
