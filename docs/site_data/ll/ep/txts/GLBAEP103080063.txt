Middx ss  Judith Huggins  widow of Barnaby Huggins deced Maketh oath that about eighteen Years ago her said late husband rented a house of fourteen pounds by the Year in Hog Lane in the Parish of Saint Ann in Westminster in the County of Middlesex and Paid Parish Taxes and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there as this Depont. is advised And this Deponent Saith that her said husband did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten Pounds by the Year or upwards nor serve any Parish offices nor to her knowledge or belief do any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlemt. And this Deponent saith that since her said husbands death she hath not to her knowledge or belief done any Act to Acquire a settlement in her own right

Sworn this Tenth day of Janry. 1761 before  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

The Mark of Judith Huggins
