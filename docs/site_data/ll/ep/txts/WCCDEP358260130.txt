Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Bannister aged about 58 years upon her Oath saith that about 14 years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Bannister her late Husband who has been dead about Three years at the parish Church of Saint Giles Cripplegate That her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Edward Smith of Denmark Street Saint Giles for Seven years that he Served the latter par of his said Apprenticeship with his said Master at the Vine Tavern Holborn in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex and that after he w out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might at ha gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 8th- day of June 1796 R. Ford J Robinson

her Jane Bannister Mark
