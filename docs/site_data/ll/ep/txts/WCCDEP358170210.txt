Middx  Wm. Lowen aged about 40 Yrs. upon has Oath saith that he intermarried with his new Wife  Elizabeth on the Thirtyeth Day of March last at the Parish Church of in the County of  Fleet  London that about Thirteen Yrs. ago he lived or & rented a House in Bennetts Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for the Space of One Year & upwards that since he left the same he hath not rented Ten Pounds as Year Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath Three Childn. by a former Wife Deceased Viz. William aged about Fourteen Years John aged upwards of Eight Years & The aged about Three Yrs. & being Poor & sick is not able to maintaine them

Sworne the 15th. of Febry 1741 before} Tho. Lane  Jas: Gronous

Willm. Lowen
