Middx The Examination of Hugh Jenkins Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 17th. Day of Novr. 1739 Who upon his Oath saith that abt. 7 Years ago he was hired to & lived with his Grace the Duke of Ancaster in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the same County for the space of three Years at ye. Yearly Wages of Six Pounds And this Examint. further saith that he hath not since he left the sd. Service Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife And further saith yt. he was moved to the said Parish of St. Giles in the Fields by Order bearing Date the 13th. Day of June last & was accepted of by Mr. Rumey one of the Overseers of the sd. Parish

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Hugh Jenkins
