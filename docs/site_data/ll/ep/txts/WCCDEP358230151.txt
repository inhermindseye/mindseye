Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Page Aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she never lived as a hired yearly for twelve Months rented any house tenement or Lodging of ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement in her own right That she hath been Informed which Information she verily believes to be true that she was born at Fulham in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex where her Father rented a house for 22 years at Ten Pounds a year and upwards and paid all Taxes that after he left the said House he never did any act Matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinant Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 27th. day of August 1800 before us } Ws Woodenough

her Sarah Page mark

Witness B Newman
