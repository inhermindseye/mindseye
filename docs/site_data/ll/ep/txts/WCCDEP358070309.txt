Middx to wit Ann Townsend Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Edward Townsend who has been dead upwards of Two years and with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Brides otherwise Bridget in the City of London upwards of Seven years ago and by whom she has one Child now alive named Edward aged about Two years, Says her said late Husband Rented and in House in Greyhound Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about Two years at the yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds & paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath Quitted the said House about Three years and hath since Quitting the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or any otherwise gained a subsequent Settlement neither has this Examinant since her said Husbands Death.

Sworn this 30th day of Octr 1775 Before}

Ann Townsend did not Come to be sworn
