Middlesex ss}  Ann Lynch the widow of William Lynch deced maketh oath that since her said husbands death & during her Widowhood She was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Mrs Loyd in King Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for near the space of three Years and has not since lived elsewhere as a hired for a year nor hath she been Married since her sd late husbands death or done any Act to gain a settlement since such her servitude as aforesd

Sworn this. 17. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Lynch
