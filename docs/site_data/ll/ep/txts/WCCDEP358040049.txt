Middx  Ann Dent Wife of John Dent upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said John Dent in the Parish Church of St. Sepulchers London about 33 Years since Says her sd. husband lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £12 in Change Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for twenty Eight Yrs. or thereabts. & left same about Six Yrs. ago Says the sd. John Dent has not since he left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 12th. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

Ann Dent
