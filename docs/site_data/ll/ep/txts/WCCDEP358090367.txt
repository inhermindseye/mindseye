Middx  Mary Tokely Aged about 52 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Richd. Parker a Statiary in the Strand in the Parish St. Mary Le Strand  for 12 Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of five Years ago Since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. by living as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 23d. day of Jany. 1783 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Tokely Mark
