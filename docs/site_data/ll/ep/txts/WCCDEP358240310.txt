St Clement Danes August 24th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary Newington Surry  two Children  (to wit) Elizabeth Gibbons Wife of John Gibbons (now from her) aged thirty two years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her present Husband John Gibbons at the Church belonging to the parish of Portsmouth in the County of Hants about on or abt the 9th day of September 1782 that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and Resided in a House in Kent Street in the parish of  Saint Mary Newington in the County of of Surrey for three Months and upwards at the Annual Rent of twelve pounds that he quitted that House at or about Christmas last and hath not obtained Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge Information or belief that her said Husband left her about is now from her three Weeks ago And she knows not where he is that She hath two Children by him both also from her this Examinant the one witht at Portsmouth with the father of this Deponent's Husband & the other at Nurse that she is distressed poor and unable to maintain

herself without assistance

Sworn the 24th day of August 1793 before Us} N Bond Edwd: Naish

Elizth Gibbon 's's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
