Middx  Jane Ann Murphy upon Oath saith she was born opposite the meeting house in Swallow Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. & never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever rented ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes and that her father & mother were both Natives of Ireland and never gained a Settlemt. in England to her knowledge

Sworn Febry. 23d. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jane Ann Murphy
