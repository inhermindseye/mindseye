Middx  John Evans aged about 63 Years upon Oath says he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Henry Freame , Taylor in Portugal Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middlesex and never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Land Tax or poor Rate or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year says he has a Wife named Esther with whom he intermarried about Twenty one Years ago in the parish Church of Holmer in the County of Hereford

Sworn the 6th. May 1765 . Before} Barth: Hammond

John Evans
