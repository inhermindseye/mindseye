St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

passd. to St. Alphage who appeald Wap she had not Servd. with Coment of her Master Etc 40 Days. Appeal allowd we took her back  Sarah Burch Aged Thirty one Years or Thereabouts Upon her Oath Saith That She never was Married rented a House or paid Taxes That she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex to one Robert Lander an Attorney about Ninteen Years ago, that she lived with her said Master about Five Years where he became Insolvent and she with his Consent went to live with one Benjamin Burch in Helmet Court in the Parish of Saint Alphage in the City of London with whom She staid about three Months during which time her said Master Died and since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have gained a Settlement otherwise Man in the Parish of Saint Alphage aforesaid

Sworn this 30 Septr. 1785 before Sampr Wright  James Paine

Sarah Burch
