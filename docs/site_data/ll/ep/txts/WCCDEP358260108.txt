Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizabeth Sleeman who is ill maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true That she the said Elizabeth Sleeman never was married, Rented any house, Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year, paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months, or ever bound an Apprentice, but that she was born at Holsworthy in the Parish of Holsworthy in the County of Devon where her father Daniel Sleeman now keeps a house and has done for several years last past

Sworn this 12th. day of febry. 1796 } Rusl. Clarke  Rd Thompson

Jas Talboys
