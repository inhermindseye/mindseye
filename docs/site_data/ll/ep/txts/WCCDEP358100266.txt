Middlesex and Westminster}  Swear to St. Clemt. to wit) Ann Thompson  Widow , aged thirty two Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith, That she intermarried with Charles Thompson her late Husband at the Church of Saint Bartholomew in Smithfield about fourteen Years ago, That her said Husband was by Trade a Diamond Cutter , and that he died at Islington about thirteen Months ago, That she has four Children living by her said Children Husband Vizt. Charles aged Twelve Years or thereabouts; Elizabeth aged Between Six and Seven Years; Sarah aged between four and five Years, and Roger aged between two and three Years, None of whom have ever been absent from this Examinant or her said Husband or gained any Settlement of their own; And this Examinant further Saith That since the Death of her said Husband she this Deponent hath rented a House in Old Borowell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the Yearly rent of 12s. and paid Land tax for the same and that She hired there near twelve Months and her said Children all of them lived with her

Sworn this 16th. Octr. 1784 before  Wm. Kitchiner

Ann Thompson
