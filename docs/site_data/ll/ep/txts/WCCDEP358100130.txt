Middx & Westmr}  Mary Lock Aged about 26 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever served any Apprenticeship says her Father Rented & lived in a House in King Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of one year at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about 20 years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 26 day of Septr. 1783  Wm Kitchiner R. Butler

Mary Lock
