Middx (to wit)  Rebecca Draycot Wife of Philip Draycot (now at Sea in his Majesties Service upon Oath Saith She was married to the said Philip Draycot at the Fleet London on the 28th. May 1751 by whom she has 2 Children (viz) Clement aged abt. 3 Yrs. & Mary aged abt. 11 months and that She has been informed & verily believes the sd Philip Draycot was bound apprentice for Seven years to John Thorp a Butcher and lived with him as his apprentice in the Parish of Gadesby in the County of Leicester upwds. of three years & then her sd. husband & his master parted by Convents and this Examinant has been further informed & believes that her sd husband never was assigned over, never rented ten pounds by the year pd. parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn 12th. May 1755 before}
