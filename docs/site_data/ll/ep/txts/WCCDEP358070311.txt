Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Smith Aged upwards of fifty three years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Smith who has been dead upwards of Twelve years and with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London about Thirty Two years ago & by whom she has only one Daughter now alive who is Married and provided for Says her said late Husband, (& she for three years after his Death) Rented and lived in a house in Star Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Eleven years at the yearly of Eight pounds & paid all Parish Taxes due for the same, & she hath Quitted the said house about Eight years ago, and hath not since Quitting the same ever Rented House paid Taxes lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or any otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th day of Octr 1775 before R Butler

Eliza Smith
