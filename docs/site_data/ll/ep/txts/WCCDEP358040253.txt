Westmr. 

Henry Hart upon Oath saith he intermarried with his present Wife Ann at the Fleet Londn. about 19 Year's ago by whom he hath one Child viz Saml. aged about 15 Years who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. Says he this Examinant was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Lucam a Waterman & Served Five Years of the sd. term with the sd. Lucam at Morrisey Cause way in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry and then went to Sea & continued at Sea till after the Expiration of his Apprentiship Says he never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn Sepr. 12th 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Henry Hart
