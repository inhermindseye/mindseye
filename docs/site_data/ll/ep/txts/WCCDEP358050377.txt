Middx  Patient Williams aged about 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 5 Years since she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Platt in New Street Fetter Lane in the Parish of St. Bridget Culgarly Brides London for 4 Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £3 and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 1st. Decr: 1750 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Patient Williams
