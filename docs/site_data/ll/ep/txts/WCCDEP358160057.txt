Middx The Examination of Hugh Jenkins Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 13th. Day of June 1739 Who upon his Oath, that abt. Seven Years ago he was hired to & lived a Yearly hired Servt . with his Grace the Duke of Ancaster in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the sd. County of Middx for the Space of 3 Years at ye. Yearly Wages of Six Pounds And this Examint. further saith yt. he hath not since he left his sd. Service Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas. Gronous  Tho Lane

 Hugh Jenkins
