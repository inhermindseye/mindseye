Middlesex ss}  Thomas Leech maketh oath that he this Depont & one Elizabeth Woodward  Singlewoman lately deced Cohabited & lived togethr. as Man & Wife for divers years in the precinct of St Catherine in the County of Middlesex although in Truth they were never married That during Such their Cohabitation there She the said Elizabeth Woodward was delivered of a Female Child in the said Precinct which hath Since been baptized and registred in the Collegiate Church there by the name of Ann Leech That the said child as aged nine Weeks & upwards & likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County and is as this Deponent is advised and apprehends an illegitimate Child as having been born out of Wedlock

Sworn this 5th. day of November 1773 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

Thomas Leech
