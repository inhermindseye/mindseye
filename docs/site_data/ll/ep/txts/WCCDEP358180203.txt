Middx  Eliz: Garvee aged 65 Years upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her present Husband Paul Garvee in the Parish of St. Francis in the City of Dublin about 30 Years since that the sd. Paul Garvee lived in & Rented the Horshoe & Magpye Alehouse in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt: Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £26: for the Space of 8 or 9 Years & hath left the same near 3 Years that the sd: Paul Garvee hath not since that time rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her Husbd. is now at Flanders & she being poor is not able to maintain herself

Sworne June 8th. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz: Garvee
