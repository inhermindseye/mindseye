Westmr  Eliza. Adams aged upwds. of 70 Yrs. Wido . of Jonathan Adams upon Oath saith she inter- married with the sd. Jonathan Adams at Litchfeild Minster about 40 Years ago Says her sd. husband was born in the parish of Yardley near Birmingham in the County of Warwick and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year whilst a Singleman rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as she hath been informed & beleives Says she this Exainat hath not since the Death of her said late Husband rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 16. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Eliza Addams
