Middx  Mary Scruton on behalf of Mary Dodd an Infant aged abt. 12 Years upon Oath says She has of ten heard David the Father of the sd. Mary Dodd say, he served his Apprenticeship in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry & that be never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants Knowledge or Belief

Sworn Decr. 21st. 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Scruton
