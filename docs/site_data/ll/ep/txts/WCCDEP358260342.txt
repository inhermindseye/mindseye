Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Shortin aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith That about Eighteen years ago she Intermarried with Michael Shortin her late Husband who has been dead about Three years at Kilkenny in Ireland That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Parkers Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Three Quarter of a year at the yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds that after he quitted said house he never rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement and this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she hath Three Children living by her said late Husband vizt. Margaret aged about Eleven years born in the Coach Yard and James aged about Five years born in

Stewarts Rents and Thomas aged about Two years born in Stewarts Rents aforesaid and saith that the said Three Children are now Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn this 2nd. day of Octr. 1798 before us } Jn Inns E Read

her Mary Shortin Mark

Witness B Newman
