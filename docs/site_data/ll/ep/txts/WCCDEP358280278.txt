Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Griffiths aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with one Mr. Wilks in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted said service about Thirteen years ago she Intermarried with since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 20th. day of March last past she was delivered of a female Bastard Child (which hath since been Christened by the Name of Barbara ) in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish That one John Birkett a Flax Dresser who did live in George Alley Fleet Markett but is at Present in Clerkenwell Bridewell is the only real and true father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said John Birkett is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 19th. day of April 1790 R Butler

her Elizth. Griffiths Mark
