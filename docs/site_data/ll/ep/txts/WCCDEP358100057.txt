Middlesex to wit John Holmes Aged about Thirty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Elizabeth his first Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West London about fourteen Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children living namely Elizabeth aged about Eleven Years and Thomas Aged about five Years Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one Charles Powers a Dealer in Coals in Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Settlement either by renting House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 1st. day of April 1783 before R Butler

his John Holmes Mark
