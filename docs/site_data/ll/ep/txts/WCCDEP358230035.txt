Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Sophia Bolton who is infirm Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Sophia Bolton lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Watson the corner of Crown Court  Princes Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Seven years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds that She hath quitted said Service about Fifteen Years since which time she hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief done any Act Matters or thing whereby she Might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of January 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

Jas Talboys
