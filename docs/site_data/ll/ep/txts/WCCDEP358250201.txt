St. Clement Danes

Middx. & Westmr.} to Wit Eliz. Mogford aged 25. Years upon her oath Saith that she intermarried with Thomas Mogford in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes  1st. Jany: 1792 . who is at Present a Soldier in the Northumberland Fencibles, by whom she hath two Children Viz. Gideon Mogford aged 3 Years and John Mogford aged 10 months That her said Husband Rented and lived in a Shop & Parlour No 24. King Street in the Parish of St. Giles's in the fields in the County of Middlesex , for the Space of Six Months at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds Per Ann and after he Quitted said Shop & Parlour he has never done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Octr: 1795  Bond W kennard

Elizabeth Mogford
