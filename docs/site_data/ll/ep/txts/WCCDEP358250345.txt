Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Carrington aged about 41 years upon her Oath saith that about 23 years ago she Intermarried with William Carrington her late Husband who has been dead about four years at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Tower Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Twenty four Pounds that he quitted said house about Ten Years ago after which he never rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent saith she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 1st. day of Febry. 1798 before us} Robinson John Hatchell

her Ann Carrington Mark

A Bunbidge Beadle Witness
