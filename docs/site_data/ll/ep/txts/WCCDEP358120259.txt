Eliz Russell aged 56 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. that she was born at Portsmouth in the County of  & that her father Tho Russell decd. Served his Apprentiship wth. Mr. Ilkias a Bricklayer upon Saffron hill in the Liberty of Saffronhill in the parish of St. Andw. in Holbn. Middx & never rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parochial Taxes

Sworn Janry 23d 1755 before Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz Russell
