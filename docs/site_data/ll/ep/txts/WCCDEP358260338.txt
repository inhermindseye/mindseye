Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Kearsley aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that about 24 years ago she Intermarried with John Kearsley her late Husband who has been dead about Eight years at the Parish Church of Saint Katherines by the Tower that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Great George Street Minories in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Thirty two Pounds that after he quitted said house he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 29th. day of Septr. 1798 before us  N Bond E Read

Susannah Kearsley
