Middx  Geo. Hillier aged about 40 Years upon Oath saith he never was married and that he lived a Yearly Servant with Tho. Stanniford Esqr. for three Yrs. & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds and lay the last Six weeks of his sd Service at the Craven head in Drury Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes that he has not Since he quitted his sd Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn July 17th. 1752 before}
