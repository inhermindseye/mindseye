Middlesex ss  Catherine Clifford  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years ago her late Father Dennis Clifford deced rented a house of the yearly rent of Eighteen pounds in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & at which time She this Deponent lived with him there as part of his Family That her Said Father did not afterwards rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere he & this Deponts Mother dying in the Sd house That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 12 day of October 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

Catherin Clifford
