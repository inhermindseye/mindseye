Westminster  Dorothy Burk Wido: of Francis Burk upon Oath says she intermarried with the sd. Fras. Burk at St. Georges Church in Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland about 16 Years ago Says her sd. late Husband lived a Yearly Servant (whilst a Singleman) with Mr. Jones in the Parish of Navin in the County of Meath in the Kingdom of Ireland aforesaid for the Space of One Year & upwards as she hath been informed & beleives and never (after he left the sd. Mr. Jones Services rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has not since the Death of her sd. late husband rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 17th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Dorothy Burk
