Middlesex ss}  Jane Hopperton  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Richard Johnson in the Butcher Row in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Victualler & Continued in Such Service there for the space of three years & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for the sapce of a year nor has She ever been Married or done any Act to gain anothr Settlement That She hath a Child named Richard Johnson aged about two months who was born of her Body in Milk Street in the parish of christ Church in the County Middx She which child is a Bastard & illegitimate by reason this Depont was never Married and that the sd Richd Johnson late of Lower East Smithfield aforesaid & who is lately gone into Woles as she has been informed & believes is the Father of the said child.

Sworn this. 7. day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

Jane Hopperton
