Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Langstaffe aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Harris an Optician in Hyde Street in the parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for about Fifteen Months at the yearly wages of Four pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Septr. 1796 } Rupt: Clarke  Edw Read

her Elizabeth Langstaffe Mark

Witness B Newman
