Middx:  Ann Brown aged upwds. of 70 Years Upon Oath says that being a Widow and without Child or Children She lived a hired Yearly Servt. with one Mrs. Jones next doors to Mr Philip Poole 's opposite St. Clements Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for One yrs. and upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £5 & has quitted sd. Service abt. Nine Years & has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yrly. Servt . for one Year.

Sworn this 27th. Octr. 1759 . before}
