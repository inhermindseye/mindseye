Middlesex ss}  Catherine Habberton the wife of Charles Habberton maketh oath that her Brother James Barnwell was born in St Catherines Lane in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said county and this Deponent can in no way Set forth or discover where her late Father or Mother or either of them was or were legally Settled at the time of their respcive deaths or at any time previous thereto That her said Brother to her knowledge or belief never was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor over did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this 27. day of March 1777 before  Saml Wood

The Mark of Catherine Habberton
