Middlesex ss}  Rebecca Shepson  singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime ago she was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages with one Thomas Wood in the Parish of Saint Thomas in the City of Bristol Carpenter and Continued in Such Service for the Space of one Year & upwards & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that she hath not since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlemant to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 5th day of February 1773 before B Camper

The Mark of Rebecca Shepson
