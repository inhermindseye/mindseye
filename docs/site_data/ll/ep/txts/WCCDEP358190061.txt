Middx.  Geo: Darbyshire aged abt. 45 Years (lodging at Mr. Gibbert's in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx.) Upon Oath says he Rented a house  in Cursitor's Street Chancery Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the City of London known by the Sign of the Turks head of the Yearly Rent of £36 for six Months or thereabouts and hath Quitted same Upwards of One Year and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn 24th. Octr. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

George Darbyshire
