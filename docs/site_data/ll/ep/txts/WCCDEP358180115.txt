Middx (to witt) Margt. Milbourn  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Wm. Cornelius at the Rose Tavern near Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Vintner at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the Space of one Year & four Months & hath left her said Service upwards of Sixteen Years that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 7th. Octr. 1743 before} Thos. De Veil

The Mark of Margt. Milbourn
