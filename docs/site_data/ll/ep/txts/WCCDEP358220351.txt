Westmr. to wit Robert Barrett Aged about Seventy Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Sarah his Wife at the Parish Church of Tinmouth North Shields in the County of Northumberland in the Year 1766 and by whom he hath no Children Says that he Rented and Lived in a House in Holles Street Clare Market upwards of Six Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & paid all Parochial Taxes due for the same hath quitted his said House upwards of fourteen Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rent Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further says he is now Poor and unable to maintain himself & his said Wife

Sworn this 11th. day of May 1772 before me} Tho Lane

Robt. Barret
