Westmr. 

Mary Hill  Wife of George Hill (gone from ha upon Oath saith she intermarried with the saith George Hill at the Fleet London upwards of Twenty Six Years ago Says her said husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. John Haster a Shoemaker in the Parish Town Place or Precinct of Burton upon Trent in the County of Stafford as she hath been informed and beleives Says the said George Hill never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for me Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Daur (to wit) Mary aged about 24 Years who is troubled with Convalsion Fits & never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlem. in any other Place

Sworn the 7th. April 1747 before  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Mary Hill
