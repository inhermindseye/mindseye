Middlesex ss}  James Coe maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house of the yearly rent of Thirty One Pounds in Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Chargd to & paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish Office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement that he hath a Wife named Gertrude to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 18 day of Decemr. 1784 before R Davies James Coe
