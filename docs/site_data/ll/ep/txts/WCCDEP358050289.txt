Westmr 

Mary Isbell Wido . of Benjamin Isbell upon Oath says she Intermarried with the sd. Benjamin Isbell in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields the 30th. of Octr. 1720 & her sd. husband dyed the 9th. of July last and further saith the sd. Benjamin Isbell before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant for one Year with one John Newet a Staymaker in Blackmoor Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds Dyet Washing & Lodging a her sd. late husband Informed her and she rly believes Says her sd. late husband never rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief and that she hath one Child Martha aged upwds. of 15 Yrs & is poor & unable to maintain herself & Child

Sworn Augt. 2d. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Isbell
