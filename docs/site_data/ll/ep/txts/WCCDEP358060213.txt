Westmr.  Martha Bagshaw aged abt. 26 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Wells at the Crown Alehouse opposite York Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds left her sd. Service about three Years ago & has not Since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was taken in Labour Wandering in the Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . about two of the Clock in the morning of the 16th. of Novr last being Destitute of a Lodging & having laid in the Street the preceding Night & being brought to one of the Parish Nurses belonging to the Parish of St Clemt. Danes was there a few hours afterwards delivered of a male bastard Child which has been baptized Thomas and that Thos. Woodhouse a Soldier in the first Regimt. of Foot Guards did beget the sd. male bastard Child on her body the said Thomas Woodhouse having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinant

in the month of March last and at several other times since at one of which he got her with Child of the said Male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd and that the said Thomas Woodhouse and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Janry 10th 1752 before}
