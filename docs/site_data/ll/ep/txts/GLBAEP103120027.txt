Middlesex ss}  Christian Crock the Wife of  Samuel Crock who is now absent from her at Sea maketh oath That about Thirteen years ago her said husband rented Part of a house in Salisbury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry at the yearly Rent of Four Pounds and was charged to and Paid Kings Taxes for the Same to one Mr. Mason who at that time was Collector of Such Tax there She this Deponent in her husbands absence Paying such Taxes to him And by means thereof as she is Advised her Said husband gained a legal Settlement there And that to this Deponents knowledge or belief her Said husband has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th day of January 1773 before Wilmot Saml. Spagg

The Mark of Christian Crock
