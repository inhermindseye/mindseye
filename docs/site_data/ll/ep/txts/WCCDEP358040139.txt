Westmr.  Margt. Bonham  Widow of Edward Bonham who has been dead about 4 Years upon Oath Saith she intermarried with her sd. late husband at a Church at Reading in Berkshire about twenty Years ago Says he lived in and rented a house in Peter Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of £18 for nine Years or thereabts. and there dyed Says she has not since her sd. late husbds. decease rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 23d. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Margreat Bhonham
