Middx  Cathn. Mc. guyer upon Oath saith she is the wido. of Terence Mc. guyer with whom she intermarried at Lazy hill Chapel in Dublin abt. 8 Yrs. ago that her sd. late husband dyed abt. 3 months since & she has one Child by him viz Cathn. aged abt. one month that her sd. late husband was a Native of Ireland as is also this Examinant & never gained a Settlemt. in England & that the sd. Terence Mc. guyer was born at Clougher in the County of Tyrone in Ireland & this Examint. can give no further Account of his Settlemt.

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Cathn. Mc. guyer
