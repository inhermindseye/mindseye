Middx.  Ann White aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she has been informed and verily believes she was born in the house of Mr. Kidd a Butcher in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and cannot give any Account whatever of her father or Mother And further says that she (this Examinant) never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes nor ever was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 24th. Novr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann White
