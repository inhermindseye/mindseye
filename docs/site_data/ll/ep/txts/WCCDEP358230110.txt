Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Judith Whittle Aged about 64 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs Clark in Shoe Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the City of London for near 13 Months at the yearly Wages of £7 that she hath left the said Service about 14 years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th Day of Janry 1800 before us  Wd Woodenough Winter

her Judith Whittle Mark

Witness B Newman
