Middlesex & Westmr.}  Margaret Williams Aged about Sixty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one John Nurse a Book seller in the Strand near Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of three Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Thirty Years ago since which time she hath never gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1776 before  Jno. Machin

Margret Williams
