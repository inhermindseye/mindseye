Middlesex ss}  Richard Towne maketh oath that he heretofore rented a Shop and a Room at the rent of four Shillings by the Week in the Dwelling house of Mr Wise a Printer in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Mary White chapel in the said County and resided thereon for the Space of two Months and has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement to his knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 9. day of April 1785 before R Davies

Richd: Towne
