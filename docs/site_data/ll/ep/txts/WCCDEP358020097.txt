Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Brawn aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mrs. Laverock in King Johns Square in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry for Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service in the Month of June last since which time she has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th day of Augt. 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

Sarah Brawn
