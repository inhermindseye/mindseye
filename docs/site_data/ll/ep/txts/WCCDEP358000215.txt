Saint Clement Danes

John Jackson aged about 63 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a House at Hampstead in the Parish of Saint Johns Hampstead in the County of Middlesex for 14 years at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds hath quitted said House about 20 years ago since which he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Janry 1786  Sampr Wright  Thos. Gordon

Jno Jackson
