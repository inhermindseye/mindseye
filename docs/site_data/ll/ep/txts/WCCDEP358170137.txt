Middx  Jane Harris  Wife of Thomas Harris upon her Oath saith that abt. Fourteen Years since she intermarried with the sd. Thos Harris at the Fleet  London and that abt. Six Years ago her sd. Husband lived in & rented a House in Rose Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for the Space of Two Years & upwds- & then left the same & rented a House in Hungerford Markett in the same Parish of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds for the Space of 2 Years & upwds- that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife yt. she hath 2 Childn. (namely) Jane aged abt. 7 Yrs. & Robt . aged abt. 1 Yr. that her Husband absconds from her & she being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworne the 25th. of Septr. 1741 before } W: Hayton

The mark of Jane Harris
