Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Letch aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she served Seven years Apprenticeship to one John Russell who lives in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster that she hath quitted said Master about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Robert Blyth who lives at No. 3 Whitecross Street and is by Trade a Chair maker is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this, Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant thereon and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 15th. day of Novr. 1796 G Goodenough

her Hannah Letch Mark
