St Clement Danes April 12th. 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD.  (to wit) William Clowes  Singleman Aged 36 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he never was Married, that he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Allingham in ship and Anchor Court, in the Strand near Temple Bar , in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster between eleven and twelve years, at the Annual Wages of £9 or upwards and boarded and lodged in his said Master's House during such Service, that he quitted that place in or about the Month of August last, and hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 12th April 1792 before me} R Butler

Wm Clowes
