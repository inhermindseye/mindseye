Middx  Mary Prows Aged 24 Years Wife of Wm. Prows upon her Oath saith the sd. Wm. Prows was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Robt. Ware of the Parish of Weymouth in the County of Dorsett  Mariners as this Examinant hath been Informed & beleives Says the sd. Wm. Prows  never Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and that she hath one Child viz. John aged about 12 Months

Sworn 25th. Febry 1744 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Prows
