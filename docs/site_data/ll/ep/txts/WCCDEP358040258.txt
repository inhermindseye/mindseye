Westmr. 

Mary Stone aged about 21 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor been rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Edward Stone deceased (at she hath been informed and believes) never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year and that she hath been further Informed her Grandfather Stone lived in and rented a house in Cow Crossin the Parish of St. Sepulchers Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds & upwards for several Years & after he quitted that house never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place-And Further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was on or about the 30th. Day of June 1746 delivered of a male bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Camell in Drum Alley Drurghan in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which has been baptized Senacherif Sameris and that one Peter Sameris a

Taylor is the reputed Father the sd. Peter Sameris having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body Several times in the month of Septr. or Octr. in the Year 1745 at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Male bastard Child of which she was deliver'd as aforesd. and that the sd. Peter Sameris and no other Person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Sepr. 17th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of May Stone
