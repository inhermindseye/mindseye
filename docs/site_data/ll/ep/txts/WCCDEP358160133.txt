Middx The Examination of Elizth. Kitchin  Widw . Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 5th. Day of May 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that her late Husband Jno. Kitchin lived in & Rented a House of £30 a Year in Howard Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes for the space of three Years or thereabouts And further upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband never after Rented any other House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath one Child (to wit) William aged about 6 Years & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe and Child

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Elizebeth Kitchin
