Middlesex ss}  Maria Statham the Wife of Samuel Statham (who is lately gone away from her to Places unknown to her) Maketh oath That her Said husband has informed her (and Which information She believed to be true) That he duly Served his Apprenticeship with one Mr White thin a Velvet Dyer in the Parish of Saint Mary in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland And that to her Knowledge information or belief her Said husband never did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship and that She hath four Children namely Thomas Aged about thirteen Years Mary aged about ten years Dening Aged about Seven Years & Sarah Aged about one Year & half the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband

Sworn this 25th day of July 1771 before B Camper

The mark of Maria Statham
