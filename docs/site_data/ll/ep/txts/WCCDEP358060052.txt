Middx  Mary Gill upon Oath saith that about 20 Years ago she was married to one Thomas Sturgis at Kingston in Surry who was afterwards tryd and transported for Bigamy he having a Wife living when he intermarryed with this Examinant and that about 8 Yrs. ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. John Harding in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £5 for 1 Year & 9 months that after having quitted Mr Hardings Service she went & lived with Mrs. Edwards in Holborn Row Lincolns Inn fields and that the sd. Mrs. Edwards agreed to give her 4s. a week for the first half Year to look after the house whilst the family was in the County & at her coming to Town again made a further Agreement with her for 2s..6d a week Dyet Washing & Lodging & and or this last Agreement she continued with Mrs. Edwards another half Year and that she Constantly lay in the house in Holborn Row aforesaid the whole Twelve months in rest 10 weeks in the first Yr. when she was in Lodging & had no Wages and that she has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 15th. 1750 before}
