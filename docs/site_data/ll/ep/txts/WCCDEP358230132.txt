Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Mary Moore aged thirty eight Years upon her Oath Saith that she was married to Thomas Moore her present Husband by Barnes at the Parish Church of East Greenwich in the County of Kent about Fourteen Years ago that her said Husband has been gone to her about a fortnight ago and has not rent to this Examinant any Money or often relief to that the and he two Children Thomas aged his Geard or thereabouts and Elizabeth aged three Years in thereabouts are tertitute and in want of Newparies And this Examt. for the Saith that her said Husband hath informed her that he never bound any Apprenticeship or lived as an hired Yearly servant for one Year in any Place whatsoever an Over Rented any Tenement of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds or did any other Act to degaine a Settlement in England and that she this Examinant doth not know of a Certainly where her said Husband was born but believes he was for in Ireland And further this Examinant

saith that the whilst she was single and unmarried and not having any Child or Children did live as an hired Yearly Servant with Lieu Tenant Miles in Greenwich Hospital for upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Lane Guineas and since quitting the said Mr. Miler's Service hath not done any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have acquired any other Settlmt than in the Parish of East Greenwich in which Parish the said Mr. Miles lived during and her Service with lives of

Sworn this Day of April 1800 before} Ws Woodenough Saml. Edwards

Mary Moore
