Westmr.  Jane Kirton upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwards of 12 Years since she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Light holder at the White house Alehouse in Dram Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds for Three Years and upwards Says she has not since she left her said Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn January 2d. 1747 . before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Jane Kirton
