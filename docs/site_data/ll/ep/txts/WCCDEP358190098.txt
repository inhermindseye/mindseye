Middx  Mary Crowse Aged abt. forty seven Years Lodging in St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. Upon Oath Saith She is the Wido. of Jno. Frederick Crowse who has been Dead abt. One year & a halfe Says her sd. Husband Rented a House in St. Clemts. Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Yearly Rent Sixteen Pounds for Sevll. Years & that she this Examinant Quitted the sd. House last Lady day & hath not since rented Ten Pounds a Year sd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt.

Sworn the 6th. day of Febry. 1761 before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Crowse
