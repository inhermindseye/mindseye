Middx ss  Christiana Bowler  wife of Henry Bowler maketh oath that she was present at the Marriage of the abovenamed Thomas Leviston deced with his late wife  Eleanor Leviston Deced And this Depont. Saith that there are two Children living the lawfull issue of the abovenamed Thomas & Elea Eleanor Leviston namely Isabella aged about eight Years & Robert aged abt. Five years

Sworn this 7 Day of Augt. 1750 before us  Richd Ricards  Saml Gower

his Christiana Bowler Mark
