Westmr.  Sarah Brooks upon Oath saith she never was married and that by Indenture dated the 13th. of Augt. 1739 she was bound an Apprentice to and Served her Apprentiship with Mr. Stains in Hollis Street near Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says she never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same and that John Smith a Sailor did beget the said Child or Children on her Body the said John Smith having had carnal knowledge of

this Examinant in the Month of May or June last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant & that he the sd. John Smith & no other person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworn Janry 15th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sarah Brooks
