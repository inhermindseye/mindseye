Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Beard aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That about Six years ago she Intermarried with John Beard her late Husband who has been dead about One Month at the Parish of Christ Church in the City of London by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Charlotte aged about Two years That her said late Husband Served his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Davis a Shoemaker in Orchard Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th- day of July 1796 R. Ford J Robinson

her Sarah Beard Mark

Witness B Newman
