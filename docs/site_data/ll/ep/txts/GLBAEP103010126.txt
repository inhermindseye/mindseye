Middlesex, to wit, Ann Bell , the Widow of Nathaniel Bell maketh Oath and Saith, That about the Year 1762 she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the City of Saint Petersburg , that about the Year 1765 her said late Husband rented a House  No.1 , Burr Street , in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate , in the County of Middlesex where he resided for the space of Three Years or thereabouts and was charged to and paid Poor Rates and other Parochial Taxes for the same, and never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement, That her said Husband died about Twenty Two Years ago, and ever since his death she this Deponent hath resided in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate aforesaid and during that time hath not done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement in any Parish Precinct or Place whatsoever

Sworn this 19th Day of Decr. 1792 before me}
