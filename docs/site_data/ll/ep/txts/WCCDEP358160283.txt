Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Godfrey  Widw . lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 19th. Day of March 1740 Who upon her Oath saith  that she lived as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Rt. Perrins in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes  Cook for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & further on her Oath saith that she lived with the sd. Mr. Perrins the last six Weeks of her sd. Service at one Mrs. Corbetts in the Fleet in the Parish of St. Bridget Vulg St. Brides  Londn . that she hath not since that Time rented any House pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge beleife & now being Poor & Weak is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}

Eliz Godfrey
