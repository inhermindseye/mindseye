Westmr.  Mary Withers upon Oath Saith she is the wife of John Withers (gone from her) by whom she hath one Child Mary aged four Months and that she hath been informed and verily believes her said husband rented a house of £10 by the year near Laweys Gate in the parish of St. Philip and Mary in Bristol lived therein abt. 3 Yrs. & quitted the Same about 8 Years ago Since which he has not rented ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn 12th. Decr. 1752 before Fran. Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Wither
