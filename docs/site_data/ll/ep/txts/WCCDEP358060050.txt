Middx  Mary Grayham  Singlewoman aged upwds. 60 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwds. of 20 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Stephen Bateman Esqr . in Hatton Garden in the Liberty of Saffron hill in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn above Barrs for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £6 and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 12th 1750 before  Tho Lane .

The mark of Mary Grayham
