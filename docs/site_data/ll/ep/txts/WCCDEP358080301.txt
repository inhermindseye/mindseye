Middx  Mary Maclane Aged about 76 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Nicholas Maclane who has been Dead about Nine Months and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Faith London about 38 Years ago says she has no Children now living by her said Husband and further says that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Ogden Court Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty for 2 Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of £10 Pounds hath quitted same upwards of Thirty Years ago since which time he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise how soever.

Sworn this 24th day of October 1778 before R Butler

her Mary Maclane Mark
