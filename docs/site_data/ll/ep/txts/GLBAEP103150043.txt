Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mercy Holloway of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Single woman Laken on oath before me Burf Camper  Esqr . one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this 11th. day of July 1775 - Who Saith That on the eighteenth day of June last past in the Work House of the said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child Which hath been Since baptised & registred by the name of Thomas Holloway & that the said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the said Parish and that Thomas Boddy now or late of Half Moon Alley in the parish of Saint Botolph without Bishops gate London Butcher is the Father of the Sd. Child.

Taken & Signed the day & Year above Written before me B Camper

The Mark of Mercy Holloway
