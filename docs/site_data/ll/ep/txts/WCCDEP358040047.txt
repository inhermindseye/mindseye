Westmr.  Wm. Williams aged about 43 Years with Ann his Wife aged about 43 Years & Susa. their Daughter aged about 13 Years removed from the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. by Order bearing Date the 6th. of Augt Instant upon Oath Says he intermarried with his sd. Wife Ann at the Fleet London about 15 Yrs. ago Says he was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with John Wood a Bricklayer in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship near 23 Years Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 8th. Augt 1747 before} Wm: Jones

William Williams
