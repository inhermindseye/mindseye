Middix to wit Mary Every aged about 40 Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one

Lamb Esqr . in Lincolns Inn Fields in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of £8. hath quitted her said Service about 11 Years ago & hath not since she quitted the same Ever Rented House pd. Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further Says that on the 25th. day of Novr. 1771 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Fewster at the Hatt & Feather in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which was soon afterwards baptized Mary Ann Woodward and now aged 16 Months And further Says that one Wm. Woodward who is since dead was the real and true Father of the said Female Bastard Child

Sworn this 18th. day of March 1772 before us W Kelyngs Sam Wright

her Mary Every mark
