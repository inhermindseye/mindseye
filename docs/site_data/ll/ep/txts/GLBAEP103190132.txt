Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Ealey the widow of Arthur Ealey deced maketh oath That about Thirty Years ago She was married to her Said late husband at the Fleet this Deponents Maiden name being Elizabeth Hill and this Deponent has been informed & believes the same to be true that her Sd late husband on the fourth of June 1761 was married to one Sarah Blackhouse by Licence who then lived in the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the sd County Widow (since deced) as Appears by a Copy taken from the Register of Marriages there now in this Deponts Custody And this Depont farthr Saith that She has been also informed & believes the Same to be true that her Said late husband about two years ago was married to one Mary Stevens Spinster in the Parish Church belongg to the Said Parish of St Mary Whitechapel by Banns and that the Said Arthur Ealey died in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate London about a year ago

Sworn this. 2. day of August 1780 before W Blackmore

The Mark of Eliz. Ealey
