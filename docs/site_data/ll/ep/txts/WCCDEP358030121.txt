Middx  Mary Butler aged abt. 29 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that about 6 Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servant  with Mrs. Jones in the Cross Street leading from Milford Lane to Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for the Space of one Year & upwds, Says she hath not since she left her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 27th. 1745 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Mary Butler
