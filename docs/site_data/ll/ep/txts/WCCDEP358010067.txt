Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Johnston aged about 30 years up her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Poole an Atty. at Law in Palsgra Place in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 18 Months ago at the yearly Wages of £7 hath quitted said servic about 8 years ago since which time she has not lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that she has two illegitimate Children living Vizt. Maria aged about 4 years born at the House of a Mr. Metcalfe in Castle Street Oxfor Road in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the Coun of Middlesex and Elizth . aged about 5 weeks born House of a Mr. Collins in Broad Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx  that she this Ext. is poor and unable to maintain the same whereby they are become Chargeable to the Sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 3d. Febry. 1787 before me  Wm Kitchiner

Jane Johnston
