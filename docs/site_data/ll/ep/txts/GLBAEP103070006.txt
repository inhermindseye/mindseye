Middx ss  Randall Manning Maketh oath that about Thirty Years ago he was bound apprentice by Indentures of apprenticeship to Mrs. Seth Harniss who then lived and Exercised the Trade of Peruke maker in Mark Lane in the parish of Allhallows of Staining in the City of London with whom he duly Served his Apprenticeship & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the sd. parish And says that he hath not Since been a hired Servant by the year nor rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year Nor Served any Parish office Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent.

Sworn this 3 day of Apll. 1752 before us  Richd Ricards  Tho. Quarrill

Randall Manning
