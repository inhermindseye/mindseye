Middx  Ann Jenkins Wife of Lewis Jenkins gone from her upon Oath says she intermarried with the said Lewis Jenkins at the Fleet Londn. the 21st. June 1745. says her said husband Rented a house in Ax & Bottle Yard in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & lived in the same upwards of one Year quitted said house upwards of three Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes & hath two Children by her said husbd. John aged abt. five Years & Lucy aged abt. Eleven Months

Sworn this 9th. day of Sepr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham Ann Jenkins
