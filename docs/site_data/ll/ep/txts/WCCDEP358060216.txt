Middx  Mary Topping upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year and that her father Thomas Topping Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Thomas Nixon in the Parish of St. Mary in the City of Carlisle in the County of Cumberland & never gained a Subsequent Settlemt. elsewhere to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 25th. 1751 before  Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Topping
