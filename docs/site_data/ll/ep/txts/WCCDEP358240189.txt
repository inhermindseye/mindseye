Whaling Deborah  Widow (Nil)

St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  false Account The Order was Vacates & Woman recvd back  (to wit) George Robertson  Gardiner of the Workhouse of the parish of St Clement Danes on behalf of Deborah Whaling Widow where since's are deranged Maketh Oath and saith that she is he hath been informed which he Verily believes to be true that the said Deborah Whaling was Married to her late Husband Patrick Whaling at the parish Church of Saint Ann Westminster several years ago (by Banns) that previous thereto her said Husband Rented and Resided in an House in a place called Maid Lane Beckside in the Parish St Saviour South wark in the County of Surry for upwards of six Months at the yearly Rent of £10. that he quitted paid the Rates for the same and quitted it some time before his Marriage with this Examinant and that her said Husband Died about Nine year ago and never after obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief And that She the said Deborah Whaling hath not since obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right

Sworn the 23d. day of January 1793 before Us } Wm. Kitchiner D Walker

George Robertson

Removed by Jas Talboy  Beadle 23d Janry 1793
