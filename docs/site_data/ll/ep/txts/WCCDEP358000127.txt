Saint Clement Danes 14th Octr. 1785

Middlesex ss 

A Male Child Chasd on Jno. Robinson Qr if taken Child died.  Charlotte Pearson aged 23 Years or thereabouts on her Oath Saith that she was delivered for Male Child Christened afterwards. by the Name of William Pearson in the Work house of Saint Clement Danes on or about the 20th. Day of November 1784 . that she was married and that John Robinson a Taylor who worked with Mr.  of in Palig place in the Real and true father thereof the having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants body and by them the same thereon

Sworn this 14th. Octr. 1785 before R Butler

her Charlotte Pearson Mark
