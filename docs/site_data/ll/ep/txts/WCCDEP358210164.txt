Westmr.  Sarah Charlton aged about 29 Years upon Oath says she never was Married And that she was bound an Apprentice (untill she should Attain the Age of Twenty one Years or Marriage) to Thomas Terry of the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex  Shoemaker as appears by Indenture dated the 22nd, day of August 1752, says she served three Months and upwards the first part of her said Apprenticeship with the said Thomas Terry in a Cellar under the St. Lukes head in Monmouth Street in the aforesaid Parish of Saint Giles in the fields , and then she this Examinant having frequent disputes with the Wife of the said Thomas Terry was turned out of Doors by her; and after that time never was assigned over to any Person; says she hath not since she attained the age of Twenty one Years, Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 30th, day of May 1767 , before} Fran Cary  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Sarah Charlton
