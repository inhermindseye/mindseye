Middlesex to wit Eleanor Bleak  Widow of William Bleak maketh Oath and saith that she was lawfully married to her said Husband about twenty two Years ago at the Parish Church of Saint Botolph Aldgate that her said Husband was bound apprentice by Indenture to Mr. Smith who lived in the Green Yard near to a place these called Black Jack Alley and now called Coopers Court and served him under such Indenture about three Years and a half, that his said Master dced and this Examinants said Husband was assigned over to serve the remainder of his apprentice ship with Messrs. Weatherley and Crowdie Glass Blowers whose Manufactory was in or near the Green Yard aforesaid that her said Husband during the last year of his said Apprenticeship usually slept at his Fathers House which was situate on the East side of the Green Yard aforesaid that her said Husband did not do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath she this examinant done any Act since his decease to gain any Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 12th day of Decr. 1797 before us R Davies H: Reynell

her Eleanor Bleak Mark
