Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Dennis Coleman aged about 60 years upon his Oath saith that about Fifteen years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Chymisters Alley Bedford Bury in the Parish of Saint Martin in the field s in the Liberty of Westminster for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Nineteen years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1799 before us  Saml. Edwards Menter

his Dennis Coleman mark

H Day
