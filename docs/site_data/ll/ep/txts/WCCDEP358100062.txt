Middx to Wit Hannah Johnson Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Tenemt. or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Blake an Attorney in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One Year all but Three Days hath quitted her said Service about 3 Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlemt. And farther on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish (she being poor & unable to maintain the same when Born) Says that one Samuel Harrison a Hairdresser at No. 20 Thomas Street in the Borough of Southwark is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge sometime in the Month of Septr. last but the Particular day she does now remember and at sevl. difft. times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Saml. Harrison & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 14th. day of April 1783 }
