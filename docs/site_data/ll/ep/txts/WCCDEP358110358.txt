Middx  Eliz Robinson aged upwds. of 14 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she never was married & that she was bound Apprentice by the Church Wards. & Oversr. of the Poor of the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields to Mrs. Higgens a Quilter & lived with the sd. Mrs. Higgens as her Apprentice at the Ship in Bow Street in the parish of St Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. upwds. of her Years & then ran away from her sd. Parish & never was assigned over to any other person

Sworn Novr. 27th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz Robinson
