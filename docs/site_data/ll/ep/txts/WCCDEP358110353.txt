Middx  Geo Lister upon Oath Saith he has Wife named & one Child Edwd. aged 3 weeks that he was born upon the Highseas & his Sd. Wife in Ireland & that he this Examinant rented a house of Twelve Pounds by the year in the Parish of St Peter at Cork in Ireland lived therein sevl years & has not since he quitted the sd house which as about Seven years ago Rented ten Pounds by the year or paid parochial Taxes

Sworn the 26th. Novr 1753 before}
