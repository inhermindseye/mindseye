Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Duff  Wido - of Jno. Duff passed as a Vagrant from the City of Bristol to the Parish of St- Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 6th. Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about Nineteen Years ago she intermarried with the above named Jno. Duff who was as he informed her born in Ireland but in what Part this Examinant knows not. And further upon her Oath saith that before her intermarr with her sd. late Husband Jno. Duff she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Muers in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes Bookseller for the Space of  one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £4 and that she never rented £10 a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the

Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife That she hath three Children (to wit) Ann aged abt. 7 Years. David John aged 5 Years & upwards & Esther aged 6 Months & she being Poor is not able to maintaine them.

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Elizabeth duff
