St Clement Danes 10th day of May 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields Westmr.  to wit Catherine Daley aged about 38 years Upon Oath saith that she was Married to Bryan Daley at the Parish Church of St George Bloomsbury about Nine years ago (by Banns) that her said Husband left her about six Months aged & as she is informed was impressed into his  Majisty's service that she is informed and believes her Husband is a Nature of Ireland and gained no settlement in this Kingdom that previous to her Marriage and while she was single and unmarried having no Child she lived as an hired yearly Servant at Osborn's Hotel in the Adelphi Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for upwards of a year at the annual Wages of 8 Guineas that she left that service a few days before her said Marriage with the said Bryan Daley  and no & never after obtained any legal Settlement.

Sworn the 10th day of May 1794 before us } Edw: Read  Wm. Kitchiner

Cath. Daley 's Mark

Witness Ricd Dobbins
