Middlesex ss}  Margaret Morgan the Wife of John Morgan maketh oath That her said husband who is absent from her in places unknown to her has informed her that he was by birth a Foreigner & to this Deponts Knowledge information or belief he never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in England or Wales That She this Depont. was born in Scotland & never was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year in England or Wales nor ever did any act to Acquire a Settlemt. for herself in those places That She was lawfully married to her sd husband in Scotland & hath three Children by him who are lawful Issue namely Sarah aged about nine years who was born in Parking St Lane in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the sd County & hath done no Act to gain a Settlement for herself Robert aged about Six years who was born in Rotterdamin Hosland & Ann aged about nineteen Months who was born in White Lyon Street in the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the sd County of Middlesex

Sworn this. 5. day of December 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Margaret Morgan
