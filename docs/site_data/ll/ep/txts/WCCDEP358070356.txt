Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Gallard Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to her Husband Henry Gallard (a Soldier belonging to the Third Regiment of Guards & who is now draughted off and gone for America at the Parish Church of Kensington about Three Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living namely Rebecca Aged about Two Years and Edward Aged about Two Months Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with his Father who was by Trade a Carpenter in the Parish of Cook field in the County of Sussex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly or Covenanted Servant for one Year or otherwise Gained any or Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1776 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Jane Gallard Mark
