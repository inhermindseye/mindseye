Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadle of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Maketh Oath and Saith that he he verily at 4 years born a as in the Road in t said 5 weeks born a Bastard on the Body of the said Jane Johnson was in the House of a Mr. Collins in Bro Street Saint Giles's in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the said County of Middlesex

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry. 1787  Thos: Gordons  Sampr Wright Benj: Newman
