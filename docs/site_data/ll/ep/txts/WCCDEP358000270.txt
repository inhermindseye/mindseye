St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster}  Sarah Wilson Aged 30 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she is the Daughter of John & Ann Wilson who were  Married as she has been informed by her said Father and mother That she has been informed by her said Father that she was Born in Tothill Street Westminster That she Remember her old Father keeping a House in Maidenhead Court in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westmint. about 16 years ago that he Rented the same of a Mr. Alexander of Paker Street & paid her £10 a year for the same That she Resided there with them, That he Lived in the same near 10 years, That her Sister Elizabeth Wilson aged about 16 years was Born at the said House in Maidenhead Court , That her Father never gained any Settlement after he left the same, And this Examinant further on her Oath Saith That neither She this Examinant nor her said faid Sister Elizabeth , have not nor hath either of them done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge or belief  they or either of the may have  gained any Settlement  in their or either of their own rights and that she this Examinant and her said Sister Elizabeth are poor and unable to Maintain themselve

Sworn this 10th April 1786 before  Sampr Wright  Wm Hitchiner

her Sarah Wilson Mark
