Middx.  Mary Phelps aged abt. 45 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Robt. Phelps (gone from her) by whom she hath one Child Saml . aged abt. six years says she intermarried with the said Robt. in the Parish Church of St. Mary White Chapel on the 4th of Febry 1750 says her sd. husband Rented a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds lived in same abt. three Months & quitted same abt. three Years

Sworn the 6th June 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

Mary Phelps
