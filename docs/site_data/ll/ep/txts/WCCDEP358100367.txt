Middlesex and Westminster.}  passd. to Walcot in Bath To wit Esther Margaret aged 26 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she never was Married, That she lived with Mrs. Brett of Eastmead Street , and afterwards of Eastmead Fields (both in the Parish of Walcot in the City of Bath ) as a hired yearly Servant for upwards of Four Years at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas, That she quitted such service about 4 Years ago, That since she quitted the same, she has not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for the Space of Twelve Months  nor done any other Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 28th day of Feby. 1785 before us  Sampn Wright M Addington

Ester Margaret
