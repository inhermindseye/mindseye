Middlesex & Westminster}  Sarah Sigrave Aged about Twenty Six Years now lodgeing in Wych Street on her Oath Says she is the Widow of James Sigrave who has been dead upwards of four Years and Three Quarters and by whom she hath no Children living says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Smith in Arrundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds Eight Shillings hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago since which time she never gained any Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the Twenty fourth day of Decr. last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Hutchinson in Barretts Court Henrietta Street in the parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex which has since been baptized by the Name of Sarah she being poor and Unable to maintain the same And further Says that one Luke Falkner a Footman to her late Mistress in Arrundell Street aforesaid is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at this Examinant Lodgings Two several times in Little Compton Street So he sometime in April last but the particular days she does not now remember at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Luke Falkner and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry. 1775 before W Addington

Sarah Sigrave
