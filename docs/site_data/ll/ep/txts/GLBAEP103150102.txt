Middlesex ss}.  Mary Collins  Singlewoman maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Father or Mother deced That She this Depont has been informed by her Said late Mother Sarah Collins  & Which information She believes to be true That She this Deponent was born in Bayles Rents the Corner of Church Lane the Backside of the Sun & Sword in the Parish of St Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex in Which parish this Deponts Mother died & was Buried That She this Depon never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound Apprentice nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 1st day of January 1776 before  Wm, Blackmore  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Mary Collins
