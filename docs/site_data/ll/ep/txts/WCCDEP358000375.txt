Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Susannah Baker aged about 61 years upon her Oath saith that about 37 years ago she Intermarried with Roger Baker (who has been dead about 6 years) at the Fleet London that said Husband in his Life time informed which information she believes to be true tha her said Husband Rented and lived in a Ho in York Street Covent Garden in the Parish o St. Paul Covent Garden in the County of Midd for 40 Days & upwards that after her Husband quitted said House he never any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Novr. 1786 D Walker Rups: Clarke

her Susannah Baker Mark
