St. Clemt. Danes

passd. to St. Martins}  Ann Barrett aged 22 Years or thereabouts was upon her Oath saith That she was Married to Thomas Barrett her now  Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields that she has two Children living by her said Husband Vizt. John aged 3 Years or thereabouts Thomas aged 10 Months or thereabouts and this Examinant Saith that her said Husband was bound apprentice to Mr. Jones of Castle Street in the Parish of Saint Martha in the fields in the County of Middlesex for the Term of Seven Years and Served the whole of his apprenticeship and Emt. saith that her said Husband has not gained any  subsequent Settlement to the best of her Knowledge information or belief and that her said Husband has deserted her and she knows not where he is And that she and her her Children have been in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Martin's in the fields for four Months and was discharged about a fortnight ago

Sworn this 7th. day of Decr. 1785 before me  Jno Machin  Wm. Gas.Wright

Ann Barrett
