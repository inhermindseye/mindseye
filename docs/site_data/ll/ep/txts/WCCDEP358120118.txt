Middx  Mary Ratkin upon Oath saith she intermarried with her Husband Thomas Ratkin in the Parish Church of St Clement Danes abt. Ten Years ago & has by him two Children John aged upwds. of Seven Years & Thomas aged upwards of two Years & that She has been Informed & verily believes her sd husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr Bacon in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year & that her sd husband has now absconded & left this Examinant & her family a Charge upon the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn June 11. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Ratkin
