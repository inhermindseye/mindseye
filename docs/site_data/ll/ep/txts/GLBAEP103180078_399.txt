Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bailey  wife of James Bailey maketh oath that her Said husband is now retained as Soldi in his Majesties Service That She has been informed and believes that her Sd husband was born in the Parish of Wallcutt in the City of Bath in the County of Somerse t & that he Served his time there with his Father James Bailey who was by Trade a Taylor & hath not Since done any Act to her knowledge information or belief to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She hath three Children namely James aged about five years & half Thomas aged about four years & four Months & William aged about Twelve Months the lawful Issue of this Depont by her Said husband to Whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 4. day of November 1778 before removed Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Elizabeth Bailey
