Westmr- to wit Richd- Hopkins aged about 22 Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says his Father Saml- Hopkins lived as a hired Yearly Servant before Marriage with Lord George Bentick in Green Street in the parish of St. Anns Soho is the Liberty of Westmr . in the Capacity of a Coachman for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Twelve pounds and upwards has quitted his said last mentioned Service upwards of twenty three Years ago Since which time his said Father hath never gained any other subsequent Settlemt- either by Renting house or paying Taxes

Sworn this 4th- Day of Decr- 1777 before R Butler Jno Barnfather

his Richd- Hopkins Mark
