Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Leaver aged about 31 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Herbert at Toms Coffeehouse in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, hath quitted said Service abt. Seven Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year, And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to Maintain the same when born; says that Thos. Ashfield who works at Mr. Maydwell's a Glasscutter opposite Norfolk Street in the Strand had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body on or about the 14th, day day of November 1765, and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd Child or Children and that the said Thos. Ashfield and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn Feby. 7th. 1765 , before} Paul Vaillant Jane Leaver
