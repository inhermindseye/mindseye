Middlesex ss}  Jane Traplin the Wido . of Thomas Traplin deced maketh oath that her Said late husband died abroad in his Majesties Service as a Soldier in America That he has informed her & which She believes to be true That When a single man he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with the Master of the White Bear a Publick house in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen in Taunton in the County of Somerset & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of a year & to her knowledge or belief he has not since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3. day of July 1780 before  Wm. Blackmore

Jane Traplin
