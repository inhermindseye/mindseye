Middlesex ss}  Henry Bulbrook maketh oath That several Years ago his late sister Mary Bullbrook deced Married one Michael Phipps deced by whom she had a Child who is now living Namely Elizabeth Phipps aged about Ten Years And this Depont. Saith That he Cannot in any wise set forth or discover the Place of Settlement of the said Michael Phipps But this Depont Saith that his said late sister before her Marriage with the sd. Michael Phipps her said late husband was as this Depont. has been informed & believes hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Mr Tutop a Distiller in the Parish of St. Margaret Westmr . in the County of Middx and Continued there in Such Service for Divers Years & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont Saith that to his knowledge or belief his Said Sister never afterwards did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Except what She Might gain by Means of her Marriage with the Sd. Michael Phipps whose Place of Settlement is unknown to this Depont And the Sd. Elizabeth Phipps hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 1st day of February 1771 before  Chris: Scott D Wilmot

Henry Bulbrook
