Middx & Westmr} to wit Elizabeth Warner Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hargrave an Attorney at Law in Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr & County aforesaid upwards of Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Ten Guineas hath quitted her said service about Two Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 23d day of June 1781 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in her said Masters House which hath since be baptized by the Name of Ann and is now become Chargble to the said Parish And further Says that one John Vance late a Servant to Sullivan Esqr. in Argyle Street and who now lives with Mr. Bridgman at Cambridge as she believes is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House sometime in the Month of Sepr. or Octr. in the Year 1780 and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said John Vance and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 2d day of August 1783 before  Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Warner
