Middx  Alice Alexander aged 21 years upon Oath saith she was married to Geo: Alexander at the Parish Church of St Clemt. Danes near 2 Years ago & has by him one Child Geo : aged 6 weeks that her sd husband is now at Six in his Majesties Service and she has been informed & verily believes he never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. nor ever Rented ten pounds by the year nor pd parochial Taxes and that her sd husbands father rented a house in Butcher Row in the parish of St Clemt. Danes aforesd. lived therein a Considerable time & there dyed and his mother Arabella Alexander after the decease of her husband rented two houses in Witch Alley  in the Same parish lived therein servl Years & dyed there

Sworn 12th July 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Alice Alexander
