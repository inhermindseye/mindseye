Middx  Isabella Culbert aged upwds. of 40 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman Says she lived a Covenant Servant with one Mrs. Cleinson a Mantua maker in Pickadilly in the parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. for one Year and had me at drink Washing & Lodging for her said Service in the house of the sd. Mrs. Cleinson in Pickadilly in the sd. Parish of St. James Says she left the sd. Mrs. Cleinsons Service upwards of 20 Yrs. ago says she shath not since she left the said Mrs. Cleinsons Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 10th. 1746 before  Wm.: Jones

The Mark of Isabella Culbert Isabella Culbert
