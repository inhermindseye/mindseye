Middx.  Mary Marsh aged abt. 28 yrs. Upon Oath says she never was Married and never was An Apprentice or Yearly Servant nor Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes Says  she hath been informed And which Information she verily believes to be True that Henry Marsh her Father decd. Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with a Cooper in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldersgate  London and never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn 16th. Augst. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Saunder Welch

The Mark of Mary Marsh

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Barber Overseers J: P:
