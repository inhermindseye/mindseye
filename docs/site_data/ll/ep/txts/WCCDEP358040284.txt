Middx 

Sarah Flemming aged upwards of 40 Years Wido. of Anthony Flemming upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late husband in St. Clements Church in the County of Middlesex upwards of 20 Years ago and that the said Anthony Flemming was by Indenture dated the 18th. Day of Octr. 1714 bound Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Joseph Masters a Chimney Sweeper in St Clemt Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes aforesd. and that neither her sd. late husband nor hath this Examinant since his Decease rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 14th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Sarh. Flemming
