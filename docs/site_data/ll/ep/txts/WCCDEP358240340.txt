St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Giles witht. Cripplegate London No Appeal agst this Order  (to wit) Sarah Curle  singlewoman aged Nineteen years and Upwards Upon Oath saith that she was never Married that she hath not lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own right, that she is informed and believes she is the legitimate Child of William and Ann Curle that she is leg the parish of St Giles without Cripplegate is the place of her said Father's last legal settlement by his Renting and residing in a House in Bridgwater Square in the said parish of St Giles without Cripplegate for sometime and never having acquired any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Examinant saith that she hath been maintained in the Workhouse of the said parish where she was received as a parishioner thereof from out of the Workhouse of the parish of St James Clerkenwell about six or seven years ago

Sworn the 19th day of October 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker
