Middx.  Christian England aged 25 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married and that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mrs. King in Bridges Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for the Space of One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath left her said Service upwards of one Year that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beliefe

Sworne the 9th. Septr. 1742 before}

Chirstian England
