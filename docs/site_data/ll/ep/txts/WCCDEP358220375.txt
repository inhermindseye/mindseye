Westmr. to wit Jane Bucham Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Say she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Ann Guy who kept a House in Northumbland Street  Charing Cross in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx for Two Years or upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Months ago and hath not since she quitted her said Service done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of August 1772 before R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Jane Buckham mark
