Middx.  Susannah Cooper aged abt. 36 Yrs. Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Cooper (gone from her) with whom she intermarried at the parish Church of St. Botolph Aldgate London abt. Nine Years ago and has by him One Child Jane aged abt. five Years. says her said husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Poole a Combmaker opposite St. Clements Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes as her said husband has often times informed her & which information She verily believes to be true

Sworn this 1st. Septr. 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Susah. Cooper
