Middlesex ss}  Thomas Patten maketh oath That many Years ago he was duly bound by Indres to and Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with Samuel Banks in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Basketmaker That this Deponent Since Such his Said Apprenticeship hath not lived as a hired servant for a year nor rented a house or other Tenement of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Sd Apprenticeship

Sworn this. 2d. day of Decemr 1778 before P. Rowlands

Thos. Patten
