Middx  Saml. Axtell aged abt. 55 Yrs. upon his Oath saith he lived in & rented a Shed near Lincolns Inn Back Gate in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds for the Space of one Year or thereabouts and hath left the same about 12 Years Says he hath not since he left the sd. Shed rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 29th. Novr. 1745 before W Jones

Samuell Axtell
