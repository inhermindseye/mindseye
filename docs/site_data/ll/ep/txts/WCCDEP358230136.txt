Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Elliott Aged 21 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was married, lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months, rented any House tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year, paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement, That her Father William Elliott rented and lived for several years in a House in Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Anns in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that since he hath left the said Premises he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 10th day of May 1800 before us} Ws Woodenough John Collick

Ann Elliot
