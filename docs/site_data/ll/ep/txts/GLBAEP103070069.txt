Middx ss  Jane Wilson  Single Woman Maketh oath that about Twenty five Years ago she lived as a hired Servant with Jonathan Shackelton who then kept the Castle Tavern the Corner of Jetter Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the Liberty of the City of London at the Yearly of Wages of Twelve Pounds a Year and continued in such Service for the Space of Two Years and by Means thereof gained a Legal Settlement there And this Deponent Saith that he hath Never since lived as a hired Servant Nor been Married Nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards Nor paid any Parish Taxes Nor to her knowledge or belief gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22nd day of Novr. 1752 before us  Richd. Ricards  Walter Berry

Jane Wilson
