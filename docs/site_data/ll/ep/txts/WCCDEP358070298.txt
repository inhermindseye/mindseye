Middx & Westr to wit}  John Elger Aged about Fifty Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with his late Wife  Ann at the Fleet London about Thirty Years ago by whom he has several Children who are all now provided for, Says before his intermarriage with his said Wife he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Lythes at the Hole in the Wall in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for four Years the first Year at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds, the second Year at five, the third Year at seven, and the fourth Year at Eight Pounds, when this Examinant left his said Service and after quitting the same never Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes, or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 21st. day of Sepr 1775 before  David Walker

his John Elger Mark
