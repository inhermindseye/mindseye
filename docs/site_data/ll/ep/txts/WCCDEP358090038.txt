Middlesex  Sarah Jones aged about seventy five Years on her Oath say's she Intermarried with William Jones her Husband who was a Soldier at the Fleet London about forty Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living says her said Husband was a Welshman and never gained a Settlement in London to her knowledge or belief nor can she give any Account of her said Husbands or Fathers or Mother's Settlement other than as aforesaid And this Examinant further say's that she was born in the Parish of Saint Dunston Stepney as her said Mother has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and further says that she can give no other Account of her Settlement then as aforesaid And lastly says that she this Examinant never gained any Settlement either by Renting House pay Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 20th. day of June 1779 before}

her Sarah Jones Mark
