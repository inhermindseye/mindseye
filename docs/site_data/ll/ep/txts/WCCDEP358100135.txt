Middx to wit George Green Aged about fifty Eight years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Mary Kilkenny in the Kingdom of Ireland about Twenty seven years ago and by whom he hath one Child living named Ann Aged about Ten years Says his Father Thos. Green Rented and lived in a Farm called Sugnill in the Parish of Eccles in the County of Stafford of the yearly Rent of Fifty Pounds hath quitted said Farm about Ten years ago and after quitting same he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement Neither hath this Examinant than as aforementioned

Sworn this 10th. day of Octr. 1783 R Butler

his George Green mark
