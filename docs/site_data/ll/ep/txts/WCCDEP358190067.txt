Middx.  Francis Collins aged about 60 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Susana. his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Gregory by St. Paul  London about 35 Years ago; says he Rented a house Opposite Whitechapel Mount in the Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £16 for two Years or thereabouts & hath Quitted said house abts. Five Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn Novr. 11th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Francis Collins

Upon the Complaint of Mr Ponten Overseer J : P:
