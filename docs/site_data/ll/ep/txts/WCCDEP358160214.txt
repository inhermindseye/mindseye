Middx  The Examination of Ann Chinery  Widw . of Bigery Chinery lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 20th. Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. abt. 17 Years ago she intermarryed with the aforesd. Bigery Chinery & that her sd. late husband Served 2 Years the latter Part of his Apprentiship with his Father Jos. Chinery in Vear Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt- Danes  Cordwainer & further saith that her sd. husband in his life time nor she since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Exaiants knowledge or beleife & that she hath three Children to witt Ann aged 9 Years & upwards Chas . aged 5 Yrs- & upwards & Christopher aged 2 Years & upwards & She being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe and them

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Ann Chinery
