Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex (on the Behalf Jane Middleton aged 22 years or thereabouts who is ill) upon his Oath Saith. that he has been informed by the said Jane Middleton which information he believes to be true that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year That she never lived as a hired Yearly Servant That her Father kept a House in White Hart Yard in the Parish of Saint Martins in the fields at the Yearly Rent of £22 That since she Quitted said House she has done no act matter or Thing whereby she may have gained a subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examt. Knowledge and Belief.

Sworn this 23d. day of Janry. 1788 before me  Sampr Wright N Bond

Benj. Newman
