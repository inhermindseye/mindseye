Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William Stone aged about 29 years upon his Oath saith that about 4 years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch that this Examint. never did any Act in his own right to gain a Settlement that his Father informed this Examinant which Information he believes to be true that the his said Father served his Apprenticeship to a Mr. Middleton a Stay maker in Leaden hall Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Undershaft in the City of London

Sworn this 22d. day of January 1788 }

W. Stone
