Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Ann Walker aged twenty eight Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith, that she never was married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Woodhouse of Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of one Year, at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas, that she quitted his Service about five Years ago and since that time hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have acquired a subsequent Settlement; And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be chargeable to the said Parish of Saint and that one George Taylor is the father of such Child or Children where with she is to pregnant he the said George Taylor having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of June now last past and positively Charges that the said George Taylor did beget such Child or Children, and that he and us one else is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 13th. Octr. 1787 before} Wm. Kitchiner

her Ann Walker Mark

Geo: Taylor is a Shoemaker by Trade works with Mr. Thos. Taylor of Bond Street his Brother is son to Mr. Weston No. 1-Bestley Street Piccadilly
