Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Dyson aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement of the yearly value of £10 or paid Taxes for any place whatever That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Mortimer in Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of £3 for about Two years that she quitted her said Place only about Three weeks ago and since that hath not to her knowledge acquired any subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant on her Voluntary Oath saith that she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that Stephen Walton who now is a Board Builder and lives at Green land Dock Rotherhithe is the real and true father of such Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid the said Stephen Walton having in or about the Months of May or June last at which times he was Servant to Mr. Hodges who lodged at this Deponents Mistress Mrs. Mortimer and at several other times had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body at some or one of which times he the said Stephen Walton did actually begot on this Deponents Body the said Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid and that he the said Stephen Walton and no one else is the real and true father of such Child or Children

Sworn this 9th. day of Decr. 1788 . before} R Butler

her Mary Dyson Mark

Mary Dyson lodges at Mrs. Forrest in Little Essex Street

Stephen Walton is a Tall Thin Man fair Complexion dark Hair tyed behind

taken & Paid 16 Guas. Release Exted
