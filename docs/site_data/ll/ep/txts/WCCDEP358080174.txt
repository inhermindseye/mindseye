Middx & Westmr.}  Margt- Austin aged about fifty eight Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Austin with whom she Intermarrd- at the City of Dublin about 34 Years ago and by whom she hath one Child living now provided for says her said husband Rented and lived in a house on Windmill Hill in the parish of Saint Lukes Old Street in the Co- of Middx for upwards of 10 Years at the Yearly Rent of Six pounds and was rated to and paid Land Tax due for the same hath quitted the said house about nine Years ago since which time her said husband never Rented house paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a subseqt- Settlemt-

Sworn this 15th- Day of Octr- 1777 before. R Butler Jno Machin

Her Margt Austin Mark
