Westmr.  Christr. Gibson upon Oath Saith that whilst a Singleman he lived a yearly Servant with Sir  Henry Slingsby at Scriven in Naseborough in the County of York for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £8 and has not Since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn 13th. Febry 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

Christopher Gibson
