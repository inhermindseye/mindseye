Middlesex ss}  James Emms maketh oath That about Twenty Years ago he Rented a house of the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds in the  Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & Continued therein for the Space of a Year & upwards and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement here That he this Deponent has not Since Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Tax or done any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 14th. day. of October 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

James Emms
