Middx  Ann Hart aged 29 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that by Indenture dated the 17th. June 1742 she bound herself Apprentice for 7 yrs. to Morris Newland Piece broker & lived wth. the sd. Morris Newland under the sd Indenture for the sd. Term of Seven Years in Whitehorse Yard Drury Lane in the sd. parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and that she never rented ten pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 10th. Decr 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Hart
