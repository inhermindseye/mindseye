Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaretta Davies aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Seventeenth day of January 1785 she Intermarried with David Davies her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn by whom she hath no Child or Children living That her said Husband Rented a Second floor Unfurnished of a Mr. Staples No. 6 Stanhope Street in the parish o f Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Three Months at the yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds That he hath Quitted said Apartments about Sixteen Months since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted her and refuses to Allow her any support That she is poor and unable to maintain herself therefore is without assistance from the Officers of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 11th. day of July 1797 before us } W Carter

Margta Davies
