Middx  Eliz Lavington aged upwards of 50 Years upon Oath saith she never was married Says about five Years ago she lived in and rented the Ground Floor and Cellar of a house opposite Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Fifteen pounds for one Year or thereabouts Says she had a Privy & Water for her own particular Use within the sd. Appartment Says the sd. Appartmt. was a Seperate Appartment without any Communication with the other Part of the house Says she has not since she left the said Appartment rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 18th. 1747 before.} Wm: Jones

Elizt. Lavington
