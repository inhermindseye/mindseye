Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr. to wit Ann Cowden aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that her Mother Rents and lives in a House in Water Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and has done for these 6 years Vide Post
