Middx.  Ann Oaton aged abt. 36 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Wm. Oaton by whom she hath two Childn. (to wit) Mary aged abt. fourteen years & Wm. aged about three years says her said late husband served his apprenticeship of seven years to James Watson a Stocking Weaver at Castle End in the Parish of Hinkley in the County of Leicester as her said husbd. informed her & as she verily believes

Sworn this 22d day of October 1757 before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Oaton
