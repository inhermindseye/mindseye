Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Jane Kent of Little Stanhope Street on behalf of Thos. Prime who is at present il maketh Oath and Saith that said Thomas Prime belongs to the Parish of Saint Mary Staining in the City of London as appears by a Certificate granted signed by the Churchwns. & Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish of Saint Marys on the 17th. day of Augt. 1786

Sworn this 21st. day of Novr. 1787 Sampr Wright N Bond

her Jane Kent Mark
