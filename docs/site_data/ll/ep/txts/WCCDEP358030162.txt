Middx  Ann Vaughan  Wife of Saml. Vaughan a Soldier in Brigadier Mordaunts Regimt. of Foot upon Oath Says she intermarried with the sd. Saml. Vaughan in the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middx upwards of four Years since Says her sd. Husband informed her he was bound an Apprentice to & Served 4 Yrs. the first Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Jno. Perin Distiller in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx but whether her sd. Husband has gained a Settlement in any other Place this Examint. knows not Says she hath one Child by the sd. Saml. Vaughan (to wit) Saml. aged abt. two Yr. & being Poor is unable to maintain herself & Child

Sworn June 14th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Vaughan
