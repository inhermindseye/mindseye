Middx  Gregory Sampson aged 82 Yrs. & upwds. upon his Oath saith that he Intermarr with his prest. Wife  Susannah aged 62 Yrs. & upwds. at the Fleet London abt. Eleven Yrs. since that he lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Warnerd Salesman over agt. the 5 Belly Tavern in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of 2 Yrs. at the Yearly Wages of £6. for the 1st Yr. & £8. for the 2d. Year & hath left his sd. Service abt. 55 Yrs. since since which time he hath not rented £16. a Yr. pd. parish Taxes lived with any Pson as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne 21st. Decr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Gregory Sampson
