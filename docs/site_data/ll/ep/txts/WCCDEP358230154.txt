Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Rebecca Chilton Aged about 26 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was married never lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months or done any act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement in her own right That her Father William Chilton rented a House for 25 years in Liquor Pond Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex at the yearly rent of 26£ That he hath left the said House about 14 years since which time he hath not rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 6th day of Octr 1800 before us} Benjm Gee  John Collick

Rebecca Chiton
