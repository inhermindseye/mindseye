Middlesex ss}  Mary Jones  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was born as She has been informed & believes in Crown Court in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County That She has seen her late Father Edward Jones st Indres of Apprenticeship whereby it appeared that he was bound Apprentice to Thomas Ashworth late of King Street in the said parish Brasier & She has also seen a Copy of her said Fathers Freedom by means whereof She is induced to and doth verify believe that he Sewed the whole of such Apprenticeship in the said parish & gained a legal Settlement there That to her knowledge or belief her sd Father never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That she this Depont never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any act to gain a Settlemt for herself nor was She ever married.

Sworn this 10 day of June 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

Mary Jones
