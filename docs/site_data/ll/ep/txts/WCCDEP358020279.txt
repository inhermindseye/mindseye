St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Charlotte Davies  Singlewoman Aged twenty three Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she was born in lawful Matrimony at a House in Union Court Holborn where in her late Father David Davis then lodged that he said Father since Rented and lived in a House in Church Yard Court next Fetter Lane next Door to the Black Raven in the parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Barrs in the City of London for about two Years at the Annual Rent of £12 that her said Father died there, & after which that her Mother continued to Reside in & occupy'd Rented the said House for upwards of two Months after her ssaid late Father's Decease that her said Mother hath never after did hath done any thing whatever whereby she might have acquired a Subsequent Settlement And this Deponent further saith she never was bound as an Apprentice lived as an hired Yearly Servant or did any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained or be intitled unto, any Settlement in her own Right And that she is now is pregnant with a Child or Children And that and is and is now unable to Maintain herself

Sworn the 5th May 1791 before us  Wm Kitchiner  Edward Hodsoll

Charlotte Davies
