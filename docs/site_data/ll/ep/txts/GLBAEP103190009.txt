Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Nash the wife of Thomas Nash who is now absent from her on board his Majesties Ship the Conquestadore maketh oath that her said husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Joseph Brown a Taylor in the Parish of Chadlington in the County of Oxford & has not since done any Act to gain anothr settlement to her knowledge or belief And that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 18. day of February 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Nash
