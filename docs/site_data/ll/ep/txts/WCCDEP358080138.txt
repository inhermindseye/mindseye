Middx & Westmr-}  Catharine Slade aged about sixty six Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Joseph Salvedore in White Hart Court Bishopgate Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph Bishopgate without London for two Years and six Months at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted the same upwards of fifteen Years ago since which time she hath done no other Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th- Day of April 1777  Sampr Wright R. Butler Katha: Slade
