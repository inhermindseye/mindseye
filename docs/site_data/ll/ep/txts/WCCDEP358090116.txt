Middx  Elizabeth Vaughan Aged about Forty five Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Holeman Esqr. in Park Place St. James's Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Eighteen Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 26th. day Jany. 1779 before R Butler Sampn Wright

her Elizth. Vaughan Mark
