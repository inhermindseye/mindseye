Middlesex ss}  Thomas Phillips maketh oath That he well knows a Child named Letitia Walker aged three years & five months who was born of the Body of this Deponents Daughter Ann Phillips deced in Mandlins Rents in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County and is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason this Deponents said Daughter as this Deponent has been Credibly informed and verify believes was never married.

Sworn this 2 day of May 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

Thos Phillips
