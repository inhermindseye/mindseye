Middx  Eliz: Perins on Behalf of Esther Wright (us Idiot) upon Oath saith she has been long acquainted with the sd. Esther Wright and verily believes she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servt. with this Examinants mother Eliz Haswell in Arundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Shillings for one Year & upwds. and has not since she Quitted the sd. Mrs. Haswells Service rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn June 2d. 1753
