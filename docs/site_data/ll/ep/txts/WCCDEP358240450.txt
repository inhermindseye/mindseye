Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Sophia Street aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith That about 14 years ago she Intermarried with Henry Street her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square That she hath Three Children living by her said Husband vizt. Sophia aged about 11 years Mary aged about Nine years and Henry aged about Seven years all provided for That her said Husband Rented and lived in a House at Pinner in the Parish of Pinner in the County of Middx for about Eight years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and paid all Taxes due for the same That he hath quitted said House about Two years since which time he hath not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Decr. 1794 . before J Hind J Pownall

Sophia Street
