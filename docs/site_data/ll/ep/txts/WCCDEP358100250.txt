Middx & Westmr.} to wit Frank Nicholls aged about Forty one years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House on paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says her Father Thos. Nicholls served seven years Apprenticeship with a Person at the Golden Head  Orange Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . & after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained anyoe. Settlement & she can speak with more Certainty that her Father was passed from the City of Winchester in the County of H amp shire to St. Martins in the fields & that her Father died in St. Martins Workhouse

Sworn this 3d. day of Septr. 1784 before  Jno. Barnfather G Mercer

Fras Nicholls
