Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on behalf of Elizabeth Mr. Collister Powell who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily be lives to be true that the said Elizabeth Powell was never married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with a, Mr. Hawe in Duke street Lincolns Inn fields in the Parish of saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx for four years at the yearly wages of Six Guineas hath quitted said service about seventeen years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or done any Act to the best of the Examinants knowledge whereby she may have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of novr. 1789 D Walker R Butler

James Talboys
