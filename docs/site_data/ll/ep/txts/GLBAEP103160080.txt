Middlesex ss}  Maria Dickinson maketh oath that She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents or either of them That She this Deponent was born in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 15. day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Maria Dickinson
