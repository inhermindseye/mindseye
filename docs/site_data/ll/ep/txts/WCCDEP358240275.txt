St Clement Danes June 14th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Paul Covent Garden  (to wit) Mary Gray Widow aged sixty two years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to Robert Gray her present Husband at the parish Church of Saint George Southwark in the County of Surry on or about the 22d day of April 1782 (by Banns) that subsequent thereto her said Husband rented and an Apartment and resided in part thereof at the House of Mr Thompson in the parish of St Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex and resided in part of the said for about a year and an half that they quitted the same about Nine years ago and that her said Husband hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subseqt. legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that her said Husband hath left her sometime, and that she is Aged Ill and unable to provide for herself

Sworn the 14th June 1793 before us} Mitchell J Pownall

Mary M Gray 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
