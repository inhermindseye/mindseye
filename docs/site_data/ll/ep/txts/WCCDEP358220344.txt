Westmr. to wit Robert Winter on the behalf of Thomas Watson an Infant Aged about three Years on his Oath says that Wm . & Elizth. Watson the Father & Mother of the said Thomas Watson are run away and does not know what is become of them or either of them And further says that the said Wm. Watson Father of the said Thos. Watson Intermarried with the said Elizth. Watson at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holbourn about seven Years ago as he has been Credibly Informed and Verily beleves And further Says that before the Intermarriage of the said Wm. Watson with Elizth. his Wife he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Thos. Prowse Esqr. Argyle Buildings in the Parish of St. James Westminster in the County of Middlesex in the Capacity of a Groom upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of 12 Pounds hath quitted his said service about 8 Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn this 23d. day of April 1772 before Us} Sam Wright W Kelyngs

his Robert Winter mark
