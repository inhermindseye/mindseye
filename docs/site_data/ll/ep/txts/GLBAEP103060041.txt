Middx  William Large son of John Large  Pipe maker maketh Oath and Saith that he was Born at Fradsum ; in the County of Chesere ; and never Since Gained any other Settlement; to his Knowledge he hath four Childer (viz) John Aged Eleven years and William aged about Nine years, Ann aged about Five years, and Stephen aged about Two years; the Lawfull Issue of the said William; and Mary his Wife , deceaced

Sworn May 31: 1745 before me  Robert Dennett

William Large
