Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Moore aged about 16 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or even bound an Apprentice That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Brown in Gate Street Lincolns Inn fields in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act matter or Thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1794 }

her Ann Moore Mark

Witness B Newman
