Middx.  Ann Sayer aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Gregory Esqr . near the pond in the Parish of Camberwell in the County of Surry for two (Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds and hath quitted same abt. 20 Years; says she has not since she quitted said Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 1st. day of November 1759 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Ann Sayer

Upon Mr. Burnthuwts. Complt. J: P.
