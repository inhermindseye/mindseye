Middx  The Examination of Catherine the Wife of David Portraite passed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . by Order dated the 27th. Octr. last Who upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband David Portraite rented a Shed by Lincolns Inn in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of 6 Year or thereabts. at the Yearly Rent of £10 which Shed consisted of a Shop & a Small Room over it which this Exaiant & her Husbands Servts. Used to work in & during all that Time this Exaiant & her sd. Husband lodged in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt Danes & hath left the sd. Shed abt. Years & further saith her sd. Husband hath not since rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
