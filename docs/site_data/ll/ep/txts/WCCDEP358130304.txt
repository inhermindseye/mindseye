Middx.  Patience Rowland aged abt. 26. years upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Rowland with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx. abt. upwds. of one year ago; says her said Husband hath often told her that he never gained any Settlement by any Act of his own; but that his father served his Apprenticeship to a Smith in the Parish of  Wasson in the County of Nottingham

Sworn the 15th. day of March 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Patience Rowland
