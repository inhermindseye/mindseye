Westmr.  Cathn. Mathews upon Oath saith she was married at the Fleet London to Hugh Mackdanel a painter on the 23d. Janry. 1749 and that afterwards it appeared the sd. Mackdanel had a wife living at the time of her intermarriage with him; whom this Examinant has often Seen & that the sd. Mcdanel & his sd. wife now Cohabit together in Hog Lane near Monmouth Strt. And this Examinant saith she never intermarried with any Person but the sd. Hugh Mcdanell and that she lived a Yearly Servant wth. Pearce Fitzgerald at the Queens head Ale house in Vere Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for one year & upwds. at the yearly Wages of £3 quitted her sd. Service abt. three Years ago and has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the Same when born and that Henry Beazley  a Corporal in the 3d. Regt. of Foot Guards is the

reputed father the sd. Henry Beazley having had Carnal Knowledge of her body in the latter End of May last and in the month of June Last and at Sevl. other times Since, at one of which he got her with Child of the sd. Bastd. Child or Children with Which she is now pregnant & that the sd. Heny. Beazley & no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Jan. 30th 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cathn. Mathews

Now that this Woman is Since Delivd. of Child at Mrs. Kellys house in Brewers Court  Bedfordbury
