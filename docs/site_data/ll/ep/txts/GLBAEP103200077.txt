Middlesex ss}  Sarah Rook the widow of John Rook deced maketh oath that her said late husband Previous to her Marriage with him was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Samuel Hawley  Esquire at Certain Wages in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in Such service under Such hiring for divers years he being then a Widower & having no Children living & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement to this Deponts knowledge or belief That She this Depont was lawfully Married to her Sd husband & Since his death has not done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 5th day of Decemr 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Rook
