Saint Clement Danes .

Jemima Davis aged 21 or thereabouts upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish when born she being Poor and unable to maintain the same And further Saith that one Alexander Young who lived with Mr Pitches a Baker opposite Arundell Street in the Strand (as a Journey man )is the Real and true Father of the said Child he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent sometime in the Month of March last and at several Times Since at one of which Times he did beget the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant And further Saith that she has lived with Mrs. Prince of Water Street in the above Parish upwards of Two years last past as a hired yearly Servant

Sworn before me this 13h. August 1788 . } Thos. Gordon

Jemima Davis

He at Mrs. Princes.
