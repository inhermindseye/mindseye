Bastardy after Birth) Esther Boone  Thomas Boone

St Clement Danes 11th July 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  Vide Examn. of Boon before the Childs Birth page 279 in Bastardy Book Nod. 1}  Woman St CD  Child (to wit) Ann  Esther Boone singlewoman aged about twenty eight years Upon Oath saith That she never w Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Delamotte in the Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Fanmaker for about fifteen Months at the yearly wages of four pounds and since quitting the same about two years ago hath no obtained any other legal settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief and This Deponent further upon her Oath saith That on the tenth day of April last in the Workhouse or Poor House of and belonging to the said parish of St. Clement Danes and situate therein she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which hath been Christened the Names of Thomas Boone and hath ever since his Birth been and still is solely maintain and provided for at the charges and Expence of the said parish of St Clement Danes That Evan Pugh otherwise Hughes  who is by Trade a Taylor who obtains his subsistence by Deal in Old Cloaths and lately resided somewhere on the Sa side of Black friars Bridge is the only real father of such Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he and no one else having had car knowledge of this Deponent's Body in or about the Month of and at several times at some or one of which times he did and begett  th same thereon

Sworn 11th day of July 1792 before me}

The Woman lives at Wych Street

The Man father of the Child may be sometimes sometimes one & with in Rosemary Lane Mr Rozier the Ovesr, Talboys Beadle

Sworn the 11th July 1792 before me} D Walker

the mark of Hister Men Bond

Mr Rozier Ovesr, Talboy's Beadle
