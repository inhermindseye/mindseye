Middlesex ss}  Thomas Devail of Wandsworth in Surrey  Gent " and Susannah Dighton of the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate Middlesex Spinster Severally make oath & first the Said Thomas Devail Saith that he well knows the othr depont. Susannah Dighton That She is the Daughter of Henry Dighton by Elizabeth his wife both deced with Whom this Depont was also Well Acquainted That the sd Henry Dighton in his life time rented a dwells house of upwards of the yearly rent or value of Ten pounds adjoing to the London Work house in Bishopsgate Street in the parish of St Botolph witht Bishopsgate London & carried on there in the Trade of a Tallow Chandler for many years & died in Such house and by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That his Widow the said Elizabeth Dighton lived in & rented the sd house after his death and died in the Work house belonging to the said parish of St Botolph without Bishopsgate as this Deponent has been informed & believes and that She did not afterwards to this Deponts Knowledge information or belief do any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere And the said Deponent Susannah Dighton for herself Saith that she never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Both Sworn the 20. day of August 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes  Wm Blackiney

Thos Davile
