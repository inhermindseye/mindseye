Middlesex ss  William Arthurs  Mariner maketh oath That he Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of his late Parents or either of them That he this Deponent as he has been informed & verily believes was born in the Parish of Saint James in the City of Bristol and that to this Deponents knowledge or belief he never did Act to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 17th: day of May 1774 before B Camper

The Mark of William Arthurs
