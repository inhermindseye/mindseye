Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Louisa Rogers an Infant aged about 12 years Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily, believes to be true that She was born at Yeovil in the Parish of Yeovil in the County of Somerset  She hath been maintained for Six Years paid and that she never was bound an Apprentice

Sworn this 27th. day of Septr. 1797 before us}

James Talboys
