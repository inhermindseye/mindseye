Middx  The Exaicon of Catherina Maria Fewtrell  Wido . of Thos. Fewtrell deced Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the third day of March 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that on the 21th. Septr. 1732 She this Exaiant Intermarryed with the above named Thos. Fewtrell and further Saith that her sd. late Husband before his Intermarryage with her & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Markham in Paternoster Row in the Parish of St. Faith under St. Pauls  London for the Space of two Years or thereabts. at the Yearly Wages of Five or Six Pounds as her sd. Husband informed her & as she verily beleives. that her sd. late Husband in his life time or this Exaiant since his Death over rented Ten Pounds a Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that this Exaiant hath one Child (to wit) Elizth . Aged Six Years & in is Poor & not able to maintaine her

Sworn the day & Year above named before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

The mark of Catherina Maria Fewtrell
