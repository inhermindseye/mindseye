Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Martin aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That her father James Martin Rented and lived in a House in Crown Court Butcher Row for about Six years at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards and paid all Taxes due for the same That he hath quitted said

Nil This Woman was Passed to St. Martin in the fields
