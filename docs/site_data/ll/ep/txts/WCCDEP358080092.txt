Middx & Westmr.}  Catharine Challimer Aged about twenty three Years on her Oath says she never was married rented house or paid Taxes says she has lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Rabinell Taunk Maker in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upwards of fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five pounds and has now warning to quit the same And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born says that one Captn . Robt. Garstin to belongg to the Train of Artillery at Woolwich in the Co. of Kent had Carnal Knowledge of her Body the latter end of June last in her said Masters house and at sevl. diffr times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Captn . Robt. Garstin and no other person did begot the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 31st. Octr. 1776 M Addington

Carthrine Challier

Bill to this time
