Middlesex & Westmr.}  James Oakley Aged about forty Three years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived at one Mr. Hanforths at the Swan with Two Necks in Lad Lane in the Parish of St. Lawrence Jewry in the City of London in the Capacity of a Waiter for one year & Eleven Months and made upwards of Twenty Pounds a year of his place besides Diett & Lodings hath quitted the said Mr. Hanforths place about Seven years ago since which time he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 1st. day of Janry 1776 before M Addington Wm Kitchiner

Witness My Hand Js: Oakley
