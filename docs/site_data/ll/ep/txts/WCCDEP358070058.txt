Westmr. to wit William Hewson Aged about fifty Six Years on his Oath Says he is a Widower & without Child or Children And Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one James Knight a Cane head Chaser at the Bishops head in the little Old Bailey London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship which is new upwards of Twenty Six Years ago and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 7th. day Janry 1773 before R. Butler Aaron Lumbs

William Hewson
