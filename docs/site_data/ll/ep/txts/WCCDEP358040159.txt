Middx  Fras. Clark aged about 56 Years upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Eliza. at the Fleet London on the 17th. Day of March 1726 at the Fleet London Says his late father Captn. Francis Clark lived in and Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £80 in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for sevll. Years & there dyed and that he this Examinant never rented a house of ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 4th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Francis: Clarke
