Middx  Geo. Hynds removed from the Parish of Croydon  in the County of Surry by Order dated the 5th. Day of May Instant upon his Oath saith he lived in & rented a house in Cary Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of two Yrs. at the Yearly Rent of £27 & hath left same abt. 34 Yrs. Says he hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 8. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

George Hynds
