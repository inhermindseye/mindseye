Middx & Westmr} to wit Sarah Rouse Aged about twenty four Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Rented and lived in a House in Theobalds Row Red Lyon Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Bars for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or Upwards (as she has often heard her Aunt say, and which she verily beleives to be true,) paid Taxes due for the same and where this Examinant was born, says her said Father has been Dead upwards of Eighteen Years ago as her said Aunt has also informed her since which time she never gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th day of Decr 1774 before R Butler W Addington

the Mark of Sarah Rouse
