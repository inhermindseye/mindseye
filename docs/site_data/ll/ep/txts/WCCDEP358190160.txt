Middx  Sarah Lawrence aged about 29 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Thomas Lawrence ( gone from her ) to whom she was Married at the Fleet about 14 Years since That he lived in and Rented a house known by the Name of Wych Street Coffeehouse in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for about Nine Months at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds Quitted the same about Two Years & an half and hath not since Rented any house paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate

Sworn the 22d: day of July 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Lawrance
