Westmr-  John Peco Aged about fifty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at the Fleet London about Twenty Three Years Ago Says he served five Years Apprenticeship with one John Ducet a Perukemaker in German Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th. day of Decr. 1770 before us} R. Butler John Cox
