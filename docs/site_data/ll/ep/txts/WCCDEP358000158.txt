Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  passd.. & in Caravans to wit Ann Matthews aged 47 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with Henry Matthews her present Husband at the Parish Church of Egham in the County of Surry upwards of 14 years ago by whom she has three Children living vizt. John aged 14 years or thereabouts Henry aged 10 years or thereabouts and Ann aged 8 years or thereabouts that her said Husband lived a hired yearly Servant with one Wm. Lewes  Esqr . at Chertsey in the Parish of Chertsey in the County of Surry for 18 Months upwards hath quitted said Service about 15 years ago since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere and that she Poor and unable to maintain herself and family

Sworn this 24th. day of Novr. 1785  Rt. Tayler Mr. Barnfather

Ann Matthew
