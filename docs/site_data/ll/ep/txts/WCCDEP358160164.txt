Middx  The Exaicon of Thos. Butler on behalfe of Jno. Cecill his Brother in Law a Lunatick taken the 13th. day of Augt. 1740 Who upon his Oath Saith that the sd. Jno. Cecill abt. four years since lived a yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Banner in St. Martins Court in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of Ten pounds as this Exaiant hath been Informed and beleives And further Saith that the sd. Jno. Cecill hath not since he left the sd. Service Rented any House paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other & late to this Exaiants knowledge or beleife

Sworn the day & year above named before} Jas: Gronous J Poulson

Thos. Butler
