Westmr.  Sarah Fennell aged about 34 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Richard Fennell who has been dead upwards of two Years says her said late Husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he served four Years, the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Pickle's a Brazier the corner of Maggots Court in Piccadilly in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster and never rented house or paid Taxes says she hath not since her said husbands death Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn Novr. 12th. 1765 , before} Paul Vaillant R Kilsha

The Mark of Sar Sarah Fennell
