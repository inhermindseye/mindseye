Middx  Rd Bennett aged near 60 Years upon Oath saith he rented the Queens head Alehouse in Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr at the yearly Rent of Twenty one pounds lived therein five years and quitted the same about Sixteen years ago since which he has not rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Janry 16th. 1755 before} Saml Pechilly

The mark of Rd Bennet
