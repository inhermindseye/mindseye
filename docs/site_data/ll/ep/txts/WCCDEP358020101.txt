Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Steppen aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with Wm. Steppen her present Husband (who is at present insane) at the Parish Church of Walcot in the City of Bath that her said Husband Rented and lived in a Two Rooms in Pemberton Row in the Parish of Saint Brides alias Bridgett the City of London for Six Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds that he hath quitted said Place about Twelve Months since which time he has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of Septr. 1788  Sampn Wright M Addington

Ann Steppen
