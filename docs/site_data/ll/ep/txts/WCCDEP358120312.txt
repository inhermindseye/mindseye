Middx  Hanh. Coster aged upwds. of 22 years upon her voluntary Oath saith she never was married and that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx (where She now lodges) she being poor and unable to maintain the Same when born and the Thomas Case a Servt. to Mr Leach of Blackmoor Street in the sd. parish did beget the said Child or Children on her body the said Thos. Case having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant Sevl. times on the Months of October and November last and at several other times Since at one of which he got her with Child of the bastard Child or Children with which She is now pregnant and that the sd. Thos. Case and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn March 21st. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Hanh. Carter
