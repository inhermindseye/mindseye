Middlesex ss}  Mary Steeds  Single woman maketh oath that divers Years ago her Father Thomas Steeds Rented a house & a Close of Pasture with the Apparts at a place Called High Beach in the parish of Waltham Abby in the County of Essex & was Charged to & and Poors Rates for the Same as this Deponent has been credibly informed & Verily believes & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there & in Which parish this Deponent & her said Father were also born as She has also been informed & believes That this deponent Can't Set forth Whether her Said Father be now living or dead or Whether he ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound Apprentice nor was ever Married nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 28 day of March 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of Mary Steeds
