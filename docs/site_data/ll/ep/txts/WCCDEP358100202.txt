Middlesex to wit Mary Pyle Aged about Fifty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Served any Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says she Rented and lived in a House in St. Martins Church Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the said County of Middlesex upwards of one Year and a half at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds a Year besides Taxes hath quitted said House about Thirty Years ago since which time she never Gained any other Settlement otherwise by Renting Lodgings of Ten Pounds and upwards and which were all Within the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields aforesaid.

Sworn this 7th day of April 1784 before  Sampn Wright  Thos: Gilbert

Mary Pyle
