Middx 

Mary Brick aged upwds. of 41 yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she was born in the Parish of Soham in ye Isle of Ely in the County of Cambridge where her Father lived in a house of his own & paid his Share of all Parochiall Taxes & Served the Office of Church warden of the said Parish Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented £10. a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 10th. Febry: 1745 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Brick
