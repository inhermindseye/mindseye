Middx.  Wm: Weeks aged about 66 Years lodging at Mr. Delegnard's in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx . Upon Oath says he Rented a house in Star Court Butcher Row in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and Ten Shillings lived therein about Twenty Years and hath Quitted same abt. Nine Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Decr. 18th. 1760 . before } Fran: Bedwell

Wm Weekes
