Westminster  Elizth. Barrett Aged about fifty Eight Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Richard Barrett gon from her, with whom she Intermarried in the House of Mrs. in Berry Street  St James's by a Romish Priest Says her sd Husband was an Irishman & never ganed a Settlement in England ether by renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year having always worked Chair work till the time he left her says that before her sd Intermaridg with her sd. Husband she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Crockford a Haberdasher in Pall Mall in the Parish of St James's in the Liberty of Westmr . for Six Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her sd Service about Twenty five Years ago & hath not since she quitted the same ever done Any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of July 1771 before} R. Butler Tho. Kynaston

Mark Eliz Barret

Wittness Fras Bishop
