St Clement Danes 11th Augt 1785

Middlesex to wit  Passd. to St Sepulchres  Aaron Lilly aged forty nine years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he rented and lived in a House situate in Peters Lane Cow Crop in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre without in the County of Middlesex at Yearly rent of upwards of ten Pounds for about half a Year, that he quitted the same about nine years ago and since that time has not rented any Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten pounds, Paid Taxes, lived as an hired Yearly Servant or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th Day of Augt 1785 J Reading Addington

Aaron Lilly
