Westmr.  William Wapshot aged about 56 Years Upon Oath says, he Rented a house at Turnham Green in the Parish of Chiswick in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of five Pounds and paid the Poor Rate for the same; lived therein upwards of two Years and quitted same about twenty Years ago and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 7th. Novr. 1766, before} Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

Wm. Wapshett
