Westmr.  William Fletcher aged about 46 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his Wife in the Parish Church of Hemel Hempstead in the County of Hertford about Twenty Six Years ago; says before his said intermarriage and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servt . with Mr. Wm: Essex a Publican and Brewer in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx, for Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds, Diet, Washing and Lodging and after he quitted said Service never Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 22d, day of January 1765 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Wm: Fletcher
