Middx.  Mary Carrinton aged Upwards of 29 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Edward Carrinton a Soldier in Colonel Crawford's Regmts. of Light Infantry with whom she Intermarried at Clelsea Church abt. four Years ago says her said husband informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr. Holden a Tallow Chandler in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middx. for three years he being all that time a Single Man And hath not Since he quitted his said Service Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid parish Taxes to this Examinant's knowledge and Belief.

Sworn 24th. June 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell .
