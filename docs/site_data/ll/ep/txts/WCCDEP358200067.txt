Westmr.  Wm. Mackey of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . on behalf of Ann a Female Bastard Child Upon Oath Says she said Ann was Born in St. Martins Workhouse in the Parish of St. Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Seven Months ago as this Examinant hath Been Informed and which Information he Verily Believes to be true

Sworn the Day of February 1764 Before}

Upon Mr Ralph Williams Complt J. W
