Westmr.  Wm. Hetherington aged upwards of Sixty Years Removed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . by Order dated the 31st. of Octr. 1747 upon Oath Says he rented a house in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £ 26 & lived therein near Seven Years and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes & hath Quitted sd. house near Twenty Years Says he hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 2d. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Wm: Hetherington
