Westmr. to wit Elenor Sills Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Samuel Sills (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at all hallows Staining London about Ten Years ago Says she has One Child by her said Husband now living Vizt. Robert Aged about Twelve Months Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Walkers Court in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true hath quitted his said House about Twenty Years ago and after he quitted the same he never Rented House or paid Taxes to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn this 28th day of April 1772 before R. Butler Geo. Reid

Elenor Sills
