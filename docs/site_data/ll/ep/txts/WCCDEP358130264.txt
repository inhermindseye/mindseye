Middx.  Edwd. Mc. Canna aged abt. 55 Yrs. upon Oath says he was married to Sarah his Wife by a Romish Priest abt. two Years since Says he hath one Child by her named Rose aged abt. five Months Says he hath another Child by a former Wife named Margt . aged abt. Eleven Years Says he Rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year & upwards in Bennets Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes lived therein abt. Twelve Months & hath quitted same abt. twenty Years since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Yr

Sworn Janry. th 1757 before}
