Middx  Anthony Armstrong aged 65 Years Lodging in St. Clement's Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith That he lived in and rented a House in Falconbridge Court  Soho Square in the Parish of St. Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for the Space of Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds besides Taxes Quitted the Same about Twenty Years ago and hath not since rented any House paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate That he was Married to his present Wife  Elizabeth at St. Clement' s Church upwards of Twenty Years ago

Sworn the 15th- day of July 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant  Theodr: Sydenham

The Mark of Anthony Armstrong
