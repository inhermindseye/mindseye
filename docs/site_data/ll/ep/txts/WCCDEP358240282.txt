St Clement Dane July 1st 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Giles in the fields  (to wit) Mary Ballard (Wife of Edward Ballard a private Soldier in Colonel Johnson's Company of the third Regiment of Fleet Guards now abroad) aged thirty years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present said Husband Edward Ballard at the parish Church of St Andrew Holborn London on or about the seventh day of March 1791 (by Banns) that her said Husband hath informed which she believes to be true that previous thereto her said Husband served his Apprenticeship under Indenture with Mr. John Dearne Shoemaker that during the last three Months thereof had lodged the three last Months with his said Master at No 8 in King Street  Drury Lane in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the said County of Middlesex and saith that he hath never since the expiration of his Apprenticeship he hath done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Examinant further saith that She hath one Child by her said Husband & now living with her namely Edward born on the or about the 29th Day of December 1791 And that She is Poor distressed and unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn the fist day of July 1793 before Us} W Addington Edwd: Naish

Mary Ballard
