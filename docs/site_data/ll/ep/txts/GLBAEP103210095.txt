Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Roberts  Singlewoman  maketh oath that sometime ago she was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with William Morgan of the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Grocer at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for about the Space of three years & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever married nor hath she done any act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 6. day of August September 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Roberts
