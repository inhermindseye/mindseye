Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Hicks aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the Ninth day of June now last past she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Swain in Bay life Court Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls which hath since been Christened by the Name of Sophia That one John Collins who did work with Mr. Wilton in Carey Street but where he now lives she this Examinant doth not know he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said John Collins and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 6th. day of July 1795 before} M Addington

her Sarah Hicks Mark

Witness B Newman
