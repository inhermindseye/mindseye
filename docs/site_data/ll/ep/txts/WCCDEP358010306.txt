Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Margarett Woolby aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that about six years ago she Intermarried with John Woolby her present husband at the Parish Church of Saint andrews at Devon that she has one Child living by her said husband Vizt. Elizabeth aged about Two years and Three Months That her said Husband has Rented a House in White horse Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Four months at the yearly Rent of Twenty five pounds and this Examd. further upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has refused to allow this Exam her or her Child any support

Sworn this 9th. day of Septr. 1789 R Butler

Margret Woolby
