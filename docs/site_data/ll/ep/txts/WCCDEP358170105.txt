Middx  Wm. Povey on his Oath saith that he was Married to Mary his now Wife on or abt the Tenth Day of August 1739 at the Parish Church of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr - & that before his Intermarriage with his sd. Wife & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Francis Masters  Esqr - in Arundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of Five Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Six Shillings & further saith that since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 7th- Day of August 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Wm: Povey
