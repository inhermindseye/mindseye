Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Jane Russell of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Robert Smith  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this Second day of February 1788 - who Saith that She is now with Child and that the said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate and that James Moffatt of the Same Parish Gentlemen is the Father of the said Child

Taken and Signed the day and year above written before me}

The Mark of Jane Russell
