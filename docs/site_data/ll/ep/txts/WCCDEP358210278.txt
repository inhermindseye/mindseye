Westmr.  William Brockett Aged about Sixty six Years Upon Oath says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. James Birdsill in New Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds, Diet, Washing and Lodging, and after he quitted said Service never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 31st day of May 1768 , Before} Thos. Kynaston

Willi Brockett
