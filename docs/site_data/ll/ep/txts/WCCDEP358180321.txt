Middx  Eliza. Basdeal aged 21 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married And that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs: Weatherhead Midwife in Enfeild Chace in the Parish of Enfeild in the County of Middlesex for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds & hath left her sd. Service about Five Years Says she hath not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born and that Mr. Godwin Pri of the said Parish of St. Clement Danes  Cabinett

maker did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body the sd. Godwin Prince being acquainted with this Examinant had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Months of August & September last at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant And that the sd. Godwin Prince & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne April 13th 1745 before} Wm: Jones

Elizabeth Basdell
