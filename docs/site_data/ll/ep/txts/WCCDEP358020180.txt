Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Dix aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly, Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that her Father Richard Dix Rented and lived in a house in Flower De Luce Court in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for about four years at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards That he hath quitted said house about Six years since which time he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year in done any Act to gain a Subsequent settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of Decr. 1789  Sampn Wright J Addington

Sarah Dix her Mark
