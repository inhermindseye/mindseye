St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Ann Knight  Singlewoman Aged twenty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Cox at the Swan in Vere Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County & Liberty for about Sixteen Months at the yearly Wages of £5 quitted the same about three years ago and hath not obtained any Subsequent & legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 10th day of Janry. 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Ann Knights Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
