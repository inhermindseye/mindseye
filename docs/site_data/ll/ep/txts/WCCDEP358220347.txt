Westmr. to wit John Sansum Aged about fifty Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Heath a Butcher the Corner of Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and Served the said Mr. Heath seven Years under the said Indenture and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th. day of April 1772 before} Tho Lane

John Sansum
