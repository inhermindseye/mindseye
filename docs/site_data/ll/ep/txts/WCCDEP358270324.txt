Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Eleanor Simmons aged about 16 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That her Father Samuel Simmons has Rented unfurnished Apartments of Mr. Davison in Craven Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for these Two years last past at the yearly Rent of Thirteen Guineas And this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one John Reed who did live at the said Mr. Davison and is now in the London Militia is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Reed is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 29th. day of Septr. 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Eleanor Simmons Mark

Man never taken

Child born in the house 19th. Decr. 1797 named Elizabeth Simmons .

Sent to Enfield the 30th. Janry. 1798
