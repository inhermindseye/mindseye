Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Berry aged about 18 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice That her Mother informed her that her late Father gained a Settlement in the Parish of Westham in the County of Essex where that her said late Father died in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Westham where her Mother now is

Sworn this 17th. day of December 1787  Sampr Wright Maddington

her Sarah Berry Mark
