Middx  Isaac Booth aged abt. 36 Years upon Oath saith he intermarried with his wife Elizabeth at the Fleet London about Sixteen Years ago by whom he has two Children Isaac aged Six Years & Thomas two Years and that he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with John Kitchen a Perukemaker in Howard Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Febry 14th. 1753 before} Sam Pechell

Isaac Booth
