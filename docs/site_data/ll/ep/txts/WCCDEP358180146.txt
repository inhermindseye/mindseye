Middx  Isabella Miles  Wido . of Nicholas Miles upon her Oath saith that the sd. Nicholas Miles was bound an Apprentice to & served 7 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mr. Jno. Wyatt  Turner in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes that since his sd. Apprentiship the sd. Nichs. Miles in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any Pson as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworne 20th. Decr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

Isabella Miles
