Middx  Sarah Tanner Aged about Forty five Years Upon Oath Says she Lived in and Rented a House in Clare Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of twenty two Pounds and Lived in same about three Years hath quitted same upwards of five Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 15th of May 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Tanner

Upon Mr Browns Complt J. W
