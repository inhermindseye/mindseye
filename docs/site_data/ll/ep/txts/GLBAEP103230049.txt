Middlesex ss  William Covell maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house in Goswell street in the Parish of Saint Luke in the said County at the yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds being a Publick house known by the sign of the Whitehorse and resided thereon for the Space of Seven Months That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds or a lodging or other Tenemt of that Value or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife name sarah & three Children namely Abraham Randall Covell aged about ten years Mary aged about eight years and Martha aged about three Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by this said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 30th day of May 1787 before me  R Davies  Robt Smith

Wm: Cowell
