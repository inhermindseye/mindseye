Middx ss The free and voluntary Examination of Mary Hawker of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County singlewoman taken On oath before Me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Middlesex residing in or Near the sd. Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate this 18th. Day of December 1760

This Examinant on her oath saith that about the begining of July last on Charles Ogleby of the Parish of Saint Mary White chappel in the sd County of Middx  Bed Stead maker did Prevail with this Examinant to suffer him to lye with her at the house of Mr Nettle in darby Street in the sd Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate where this Examinant then lodged And the sd. Charles Ogleby had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body several times whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she Now goeth with and is Pregnant And this Examinant saith that the sd Child or Children of which she now goeth with and is Pregnant as aforesd. is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to be Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate she being Poor and Not able to Provide for the same And that the sd. Charles Ogleby is the true and only father of the said Child or Children so likely to be born a bastard or bastards as aforesd. And lastly saith that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whatsoever.

Sworn this 18th. day of December 1760 before me  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Mary Hawker
