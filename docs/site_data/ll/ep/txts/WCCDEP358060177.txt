Westmr.  Susa. Self upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of James Self by whom She hath two Children Daniel aged upwds. of 12 years & Sarah aged upwds. of 11 Years and that she hath been Informed & believed her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship wth. Mr Gilbert a Carpenter in the Parish of St. Mary at the Devizes in the County of Wilts & never after did nor has this Examint. Since his Decease done Any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 25th Septr. 1751 before  Geo: Errington

The mark of Susa. Self
