Middx. & Westmr.  Elizth: Cort aged abt. 19 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Lister Bookseller in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for 15 Months at the Yearly Wages of £4 and hath Quitted said Service abt. 3 Months And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to Maintain She same when Born Says that Wm. Carden a Carpenters who lodges at the Magpye in Butcher Row in the said Parish had carnal knowledge of her Body at the house of the said Mrs. Lister sometime in the beginning of August 1759 and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which She is now pregnant and that the said Wm. Carden & no others Person is the true & Real father thereof

Sworn April 18th. 1760 . before me} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Cort
