Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Briggs aged about 73 years upon her Oath saith that she Rented a Shop under Exeter Change in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of a Mr. Cross for Six years at the Weekly Rent of Six shillings That she hath quitted said Shop about Three years since which time she hath not done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of April 1790 R Butler

her Mary Briggs Mark
