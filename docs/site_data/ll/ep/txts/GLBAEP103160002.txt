Middlesex ss}  Sarah Bowyer the Wife of Thomas Bowyer maketh oath That Sometime in the month of November in the year one thousand Seven hundred and Sixty eight and before her marriage with her said husband she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Hatchet Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County The which Child hath been baptized & registred by the name of William Bowyer & is now become chargeable to the Said Parish.

Sworn this 27th day of January 1776 before Geo. C Smith

The Mark of Sarah Bowyer
