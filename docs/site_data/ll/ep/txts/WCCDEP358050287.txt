Westmr. 

Ann Shelley Wido. of John Shelley who dyed abt. 7 weeks since upon Oath saith her sd. late husband was bound an Apprentice for Seven Yrs. to Mr Thos. Cope  Bookbinder in Little Sheir Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. with whom he lived abt. 4 Yrs & then his sd. master dyed & this Examinants sd. late husband continued to Serve out the remainder of his Apprentiship with Jno. Cope the Son of the aforesd. Thomas Cope in the same house Says her sd. late husband never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif and that she hath two Children by the sd. Jno. Shelley  Jno. aged upwds. of 5 Yrs. & Ann aged abt. 1 Yr. & 5 months

Sworn July 30th. 1750 before Tho Lane .

Ann Shelley
