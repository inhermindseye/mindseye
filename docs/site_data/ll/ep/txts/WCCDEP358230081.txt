St. Clement Danes

Christian Hasket aged about 65 Years Widow of George Hasket upon her Oath Saith That she intermarried with her late Husband George Hasket at the Parish Church of Downton in Willshire That she had several Children by Hem one Named John, That the said John Hasket was born in the Parish Efx St. Mary Buckland Portsea Hands, That her said Husband whilst their said son John was part of then Family Rented several Houses in the said Parish,-That the last House He Rented there was in Church Path in the Parish of St. Mary Buckland aforesaid, That her said Husband paid £14.14s a Year Rent for the same and Resided in it for Ten Months-That since Renting the said House, her said Husband or herself have not done any Act whereby they might gain a Settlement to the best

of her knowledge and belief, And She Farther Saith That the said John Hasket the Son intermarried with Ann Smith by Licence at Portsmouth , that He had one Child by the said Ann Smith who was baptized by the Name of George John Hasket that He is now living and about Six Years of age, That the said John Hasket the Father was in the Supplementary Militia & has Voluntered to go abroad, that she is unable to maintain the said Child and has applied to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes where she Resides for Relief, that she verily believes the said John Hasket her Son has never gained a Settlement in his own Right never having been bound Apprentice or Rented £10 a year for a Sufficient Time or been in Service to the best of her knowledge a Relief

Sworn this 7. day of August 1799 before us } Saml. Edwards J Reeves}

Christan Haskett

Henry Day  Beadle
