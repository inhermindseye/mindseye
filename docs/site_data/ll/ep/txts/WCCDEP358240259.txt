St Clement Danes 18th. May 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Mary Jones aged 66 years or thereabouts singlewoman ag Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Isaac Heaton Esquire in Norfolk Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about fifteen years at the Annual Wages of Nine Guineas that She quitted that Service about twelve years ago and hath never not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 18th day of May 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Jones 's Mark

Witness Garrett
