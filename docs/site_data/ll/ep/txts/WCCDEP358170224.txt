Middx  Mary Elliston aged abt. 27 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married and that upwds. of Seven Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Anderson Pastrycook in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 11th. of May 1742 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Elloston .
