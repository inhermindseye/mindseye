Middx  Mary Humphris Wido. of John Humph  upon Oath saith she lntermarried with the sd. John Humphris at the Fleet London upwds. of 11 yrs. since that the sd. John Humphris  servt . his Apprentiship with a Gardiner in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middx and had a Certificate from the said Parish to the Parish of Chelsea and never rented Ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or belief and that she has not since the Decease of her sd. late husband rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant

Sworn Decr. 12th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Humphris
