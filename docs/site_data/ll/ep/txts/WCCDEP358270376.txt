Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Harris aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the 28th. day of Decr. 1796 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the name of Elizabeth That one John Wilding a Broker and Undertaker who did live in Walkers Court Pulteney Street but where he now Resides she this Deponent doth not know is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Wilding is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of Augt. 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Hannah Harris Mark

Man never taken

This Woman belonged to St. James at Shaftsbury Orders were made out and Suspended the Woman had leave to go out on the 1st. Febry. 1797 but never Returned.

NB. she took the Child with her

she lives Servt . at Mrs Johnsons School at Hampstead

Elizabeth Harris at Enfield .
