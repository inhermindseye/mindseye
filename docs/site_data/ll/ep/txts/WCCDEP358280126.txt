Saint Clement Danes

Middx (to wit)  Phoebe Lloyd aged 25 Years or thereabouts Single Woman Upon her Oath Saith That she never was married Rented House Tent. or lodging of £10 a Year or paid Taxes she she never lived as a hired Year Servant that She was born in the Parish of Saint Andrew Worcester and has never since done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gain any other Settlement And further this Ext. Saith that on the 16th. Septr. 1786 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Hun No. 31 Clement Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which has been Christned by the name of Hesikiah That one John William as Master Taylor who lives in Featherston Buildings is the Father of the said Child of which she was so delivered he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Ext. some time in the Year 1785 & at several times since and that he is the only True and real Father thereof

Sworn this 18th. Day of Octr. 1787 before me} R Butler

her Phoebe Lloyd Mark

Mary Keans : (Examint: Entd. into the other Examts. Book 16 Octr. 1787. a Child Named John aged 4 Mos. born 23 June 1787 : at Mr. Wrights Holywell Street

(Th Gybletts Nephew)  Wm. Fryer a Journeyman Gold Beater who Works with Mr. Wickstead in Compton Street changed with it

Sworn before Mr. Bulter.}
