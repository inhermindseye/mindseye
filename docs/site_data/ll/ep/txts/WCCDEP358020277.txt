St.. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster  (to wit) Mary Medes Aged about 38 years on her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her present Husband Thomas Medes about 17 years ago at this parish Church that her said Husband Rented and with her Resided in a House called the Prince of Mecklinbergh in New James Street in the parish of St.. Mary le bone in the said County of Middlesex near two years at the yearly Rent of about £40 or £50a yearthat they quitted the said House about Ten years ago and that since her said Husband hath not Rented any House or Apartment of Ten pounds or year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to the best of her knowledge or belief she might have gained any other legal settlement

(A B passed as a Vagrant)
