St Clement Danes May 22d 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Sepulchr Sepulchre Middx  (to wit) Richard Dance aged sixty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his late Wife Sarah formerly Sarah Blunt  Spinster (by Banns) at the parish Church of St Bridget otherwise St Bride London about 35 years ago that his said Wife died on or about the 29th day of September 1789 without Issue & that he this Examinant hath not Since been Married Saith that he Rented and Resided in an House next Door to the Coopers Anns in Cow Cross in the parish of St Sepulchre in the County of Middlesex for about five years at the Annual Rent of fifteen pounds that & paid the parish Rates for the same And that he hath not since he quitted that House Rented any House or Apartment £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 22d day of May 1794 before Us W Addington J Hind

Richard Dance Eloped from the Workhouse before Sworn
