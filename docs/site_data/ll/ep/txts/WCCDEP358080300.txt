Middx  Mary Essex Aged about Seventy Eight years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Jacob Essex with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Brides London in the year 1752 and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said Husband died in the year 1766 that after her said Husbands Death she went and livied as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Heath in the Strand for three years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 24th. day of May 1778 before me R Butler

Mary Essex
