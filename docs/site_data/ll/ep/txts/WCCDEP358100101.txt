Middx to Wit George Mills aged about forty three years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Elizth . his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the year 1773 And by whom he hath no Child or Children living Says he has been married Twice But before he Intermarried to either of his Wives and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one James Duff at the Angel & Crown the Corner of New Street St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of Two years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted his said Service upwards of 28 Yrs. ago and after quitting his said Service he never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10 a year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 26th. day of July 1783  Sampn Wright W Addington

George Mills
