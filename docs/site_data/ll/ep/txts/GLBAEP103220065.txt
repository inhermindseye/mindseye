Middlesex ss  Margaret Newland maketh oath that She heretofore was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with John Platt a Pinemaker in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and Continued in such service under such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of about three years That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done Any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere

Sworn this Tenth day of August 1786 before  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Margaret Newland
