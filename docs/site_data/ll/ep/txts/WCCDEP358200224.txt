Middx  Henry Norman Aged about Fifty three Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Elizth. at the Fleet London about thirteen Years ago Says he Lived a Hired Yearly Servant whilst a Singleman and without Child or Children with Mr. Christopher Coleman an Oylman the Corner of Philpott Lane Eastcheap in the Parish of St. Andrew Hubbard London near Billingsgate One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds hath quitted said Service about Twenty three Years & never after Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 24th of January 1765 Before} Thos: Balack  Thos Kynaston

Resworn the 25th of Jany 1765 Before Paul Vaillant  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Henry Norman
