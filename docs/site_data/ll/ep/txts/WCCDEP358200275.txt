Middx  Frances Morris aged about 47 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Samuel Morris who has been dead about Nine Years says her said husband Rented a House known by the Sign of the White Horse in Beaufort Buildings in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for fourteen Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and Upwards and quitted the same about One Year before his Death says her said husband after he Quitted said house nor hath she this Examinant since his Death never Rented house or paid Taxes

Sworn June 5th. 1765 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Frances Morris
