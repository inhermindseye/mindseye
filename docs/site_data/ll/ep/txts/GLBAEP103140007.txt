Middlesex ss}  Mary Jefferys the Wife of  John Jefferys who is gone away from her to places unknown maketh oath that her said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born in the Parish of Saint Gregory in the Liberty of the City of Canterbury in the County of Kent and this Deponent Can give no othr Account respecting her said husbands Settlement and that She hath a Child named Thomas Jefferys Aged Six Years & upwards the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband

Sworn this 10th. day of November 1773 before B Camper

The Mark of Mary Jefferys
