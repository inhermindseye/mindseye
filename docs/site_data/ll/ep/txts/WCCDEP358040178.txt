Middx  Geo: Gregory Passed as a Vagrant from the City of London by a Pass dated the 29th. Instt. upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten or Twelve pounds in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for one Year & upwds. and Quitted the sd. house abt. 12 Years ago since which time he has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif and that abt. 8 Yrs. ago he was removed by Order of her Justices from the Parish of St. Andw. in Holbn. to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt Danes

Sworn 30th. April 1748 before} Wm: Jones

George Gregory
