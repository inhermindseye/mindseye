Middlesex to wit Hannah Wilstone Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Haines a Butcher in Rose Street  Newgate Markett in the Parish of Christ Church London upwards of one year at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Six Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 2d. April 1783 before R Butler G Lerous

her Hannah Wilstone mark
