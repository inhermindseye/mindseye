Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Kain aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Leigh in Beauford Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years that she quitted her said Service about Three Months ago and since which time has done not any Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the Ninth day of November last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Store Street Lyingin Hospital which has never been baptized That one Thomas Higgins who is Servant to Sir Nicholas Lawes in Pall Mall and is at present at out of Town is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Months of February or March last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Thomas Higgins and no one else did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of Decr. 1788 .} R Butler

her Mary Kain Mark

not taken Janry. 20 1789.

Mary Kain  lodged at to be found at Mr. Leighs Beaufort Buildings .

Newman the Warrt.}  Thomas Higgins is a Short Thick Man fair Complexion dark Hair Tied behind lived it Vide Examination.

20 Jany. 1799 . The Child Ordd. to Enfield . called Mary Kain born 9 Nor. 1788 in Store Street Hospital .
