Middlesex ss}  John Heathley maketh oath that divers years ago his late Father Francis Heathley deced rented a house of the yearly rent of Fourteen pounds in the Parish of St Mary Aldermanbury in the City of London & resided thereon Several years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlemt That this Depont. was born in the Said Parish & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this 26 day of September 1777 before  Jno Sherwood

John Hedley
