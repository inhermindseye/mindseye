Middx & Westmr.}  Joseph Porter Aged about Thirty Seven Years on his Oath Says he never was Married Rented House paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship Covenanted Servant or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says his Father Thomas Porter Rents and lives in a house in Bridgwaters Garden in the Parish of Saint Giles's Cripplegate London of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or upwards and pays parish Taxes for the same as he has been credibly Informed and verily believes the same to be true.

Sworn this 15th. day of August 1776 before R Butler

his Joseph Porter Mark
