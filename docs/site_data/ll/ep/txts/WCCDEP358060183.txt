Middx  Ann Rushton aged about 25 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that on the 19th. Day of July 1750 she was delivered of a female bastard Child (since babtized Elizabeth) in the house of Mrs. Lee in Witch Strt . in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex which is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same and that John Williams a Shoemaker did beget the sd. female bastard Child on her body the sd. John Williams having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the month of Octr. 1749 & at sevl. other times afterwards at one of which times he got her wth. Child of the sd. female bastd. Child of which she was deliver'd as aforesd. and that the sd. John Williams & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Octr. 9th. 1751 before  Thos Lane .

Ann Rushton
