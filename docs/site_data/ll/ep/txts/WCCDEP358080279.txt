Middlesex to wit Elizth. Ellames Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Robert Ellames (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Brides London about four years ago and by whom she hath one Child named Mary Aged about Sixteen Months Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Little Mays pond in the Parish of Saint Olave Southwark in the County of Surry for three Months at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Guineas hath quitted the said House upwards of One Year and half ago since which time her said Husband never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of August 1778 before J Wilch James Croft

Elizabeth Ellames
