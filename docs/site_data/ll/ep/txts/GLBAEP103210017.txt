Middlesex ss}  Isabella Tunnell the widow of Jeremiah Tunnell deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he heretofore rented a house in White Hart Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County but What particular rent or taxes he Paid for the Same he never told her That he informed her that one Mr Jasper Clarke once Came & demanded a Certain Tax of her Said husband there Which Tax was Accordingly Paid to the Said Mr Clarke but what ta & it was She this Deponent Cannot Set forth & She this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting her Said husbands Settlement being in the Sd Parish of St Botolph Aldgate That her Sd husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that Previous to his living in Whitehart Yard he being a Singleman was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Mr Christopher Adamson in the Parish of Wareham in the County of Norfolk & that by Means thereof he gained a legal Settlement there & She can give no other Account respecting his Settlement & this Depont since his death hath done no Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 19. day of October 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Isabella Tunnell
