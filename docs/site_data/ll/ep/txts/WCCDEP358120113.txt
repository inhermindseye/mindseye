Middx  Frances Hawton wife of Thomas Hawton now on board his Majesties Ship Minorca upon Oath saith she was Marryed to the sd. Thomas Hawton at the Fleet London on the 15th. Septr. 1748 & has by him two Children Eliza . aged upwds. of 4 Yrs. & Mary aged abt. Seven months and that she has been informed & verily believes the sd. Thomas Hawton never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. for one Year nor ever rented ten pounds by the Year or paid Parish taxes and that her sd. Husbands father Thomas Hawton Senr. Served his Apprentiship wth Mr Harday a Taylor in Howard Strt. in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes and never rented ten pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes nor ever was a yearly Servt. for one Yr.

Sworn 31st May 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Frances Hawton
