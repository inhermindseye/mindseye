Westmr.  Eliz Barker Widow upon Oath Saith she intermarried with her late husband Edwd. Barker in the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn London on the 23d. Day of June 1728 Says her said late husband Edward Barker dyed on the 26th. Day of October last Says he Informed her that he Served his Apprentiship with his father Edward Barker at Shilling borough in the County of Lincoln and which Information this Examinant beleives to be true Says he never rented a house of Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge at or beleif Says she hath one Child at home with her by the sd. Edwd. Barker (to wit) Ann aged upwards of Six Years & being poor is not able to maintain herself and Child

Sworn June 2d. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Eliz Barker
