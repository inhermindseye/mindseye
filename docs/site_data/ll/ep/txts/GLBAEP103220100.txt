Middlesex ss}  Charles Hamilton maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indres to Peter Skibo of the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County Cooper & Served the whole of his Apprenticesp there That he has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Mary to whom he was lawfully married

The Sworn this. 15. day of December 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Charles Hamilton
