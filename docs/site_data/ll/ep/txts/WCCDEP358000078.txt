Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to St. C. D to wit Daniel Aldersey aged about 32 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Hannah Aldersey at the Parish Church of Christ Church Surry and by whom he has One Child living named Henry aged about Ten years That whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Jacobs at the Dolphin in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 2 years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about 15-years since which time he has gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 21st day of July 1785  Wm Kitchiner

Daniel Aldersey
