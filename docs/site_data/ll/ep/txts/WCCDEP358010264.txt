Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Smith aged about Ten years Thomas Smith aged about Eight years and Richard Lewis Smith aged about four years the Legitimate Children of Thomas Smith who has deserted them Maketh Oath and saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Thomas Smith Rented a House and Land in the Parish of the Holy Trinity in the City of Coventry for about Six years at the yearly Rent of Eighty Pounds and upwards that he hath quitted said Premes about Six Months since which time he has not Rented any Place of Ten Pounds a year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of Febry. 1789 M Addington Sampn Wright

Benj: Newman
