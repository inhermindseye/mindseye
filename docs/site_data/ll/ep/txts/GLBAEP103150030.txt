Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Winall the Widow of William Winall deced maketh oath That her said late husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year When a Singleman by & with Mr.Castle Thorp in the Parish of Saint Andrew Hubbard in the City of London at Certain Yearly Wages and Continued in Such Service for the Space of two years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her said late husband during his being a Single man did not afterwards live as a hired Servant for a year nor rent a house of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do Any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Depont done any Act Since her said husbands death to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 5th day of April 1775 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Elizabeth Winall
