Middx  The Examination of Chas. Gosling aged 70 Years & upwards living in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 1st- Day of Janry 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt- he now rents a Shop of £10 a Year in Witch Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt- Danes & hath lived in the same for the Space of 48 Years & upwards & yt- during best Part of the Time he pd- all Parish Taxes That he never gain'd any Settlemt- in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & being Poor & Sick is not able to maintain himselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Charles Gosling
