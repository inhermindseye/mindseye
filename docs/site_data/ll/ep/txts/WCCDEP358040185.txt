Westmr. John Acret upon Oath saith he never was married Says he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Coats at the John and Jerusalem Alehouse in St. John Street in the Parish of St. John at Clerkenwell at the Yearly wages of three pounds for one Year & upwards and after he left his sd. Service went & Articled to work for one Year certain with Mr. John Godfrey of the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry at the Wages of Ten Shillings a Week for the first half Year Ten Shillings & Six pence a Week for the last half Year and work with him under that agreement a full Year and lodged upwards of Six Weeks the latter part of the sd. term in Stoney Street in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark aforesd. Says he has not since this last mentioned Service rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 12th. 1748 before
