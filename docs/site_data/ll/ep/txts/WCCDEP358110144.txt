Westmr.  Eliz Powell  Wido . of John Powell who dyed about Sixteen Years ago upon Oath Saith She intermarried with the sd. John Powell in Aldersgate  Church abt. twenty Years since and that her sd. late husband (whilst a Singleman) lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Wm. Ellingsworth a Butcher  in Hollis Street in the Liberty Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for Sevl. Years and never afterwards nor has this Examint. Since his Decease Rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn Janry 13th. 1753 . before} Fran. Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz Powell
