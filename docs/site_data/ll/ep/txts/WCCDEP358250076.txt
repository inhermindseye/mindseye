Saint Clement Danes Novr 2. 1792

Middlesex and Westminr .}  St CD.  upwd. 3 yrs 1775 3 yrs 25 to wit Henry Smith , Singleman Aged 45 Years and upwards upon his Oath saith, That he never was married, that he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Sears a Butcher in Clare Market now of Vere Street at the yearly wages of 16 Guineas for upward of one Year and a half that he quitted his Service about 17 Years ago and has been at sea over since till last September twelve month since which time he hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent knowledge or belief 

Sworn the 2nd Novr 1792 before me} R Butler

Henry Smith
