Middlesex ss  Abraham Jones Maketh oath That divers Years ago he rented a house in Mint Street in the Parish of Saint George in Southwarke in the County of Surry at the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds and lived in the same two Years & was rated and Paid Parish Taxes for the same and afterwards rented other houses in the Said Parish and Paid kings Taxes for such other houses and by means thereof as he is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that he hath not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year nor Paid any Parish or kings Taxes nor served any Parish offices nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And that he hath a wife named Mary now in St. Thomass Hospital and two Children to wit Mary aged about Thirteen Years & Jane Aged about eleven Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said Wife

Sworn this 28th day of December 1765 before  Chris: Scott Mr Jones Boulton Mainwaring
