Saint Clement Danes

Settlement in St Mary White Chapel Abba Harris of Clements Lane on behalf of Elizabeth Benjamin an Infant of the Age of Twelve years Maketh Oath and saith That the said Elizabeth Benjamin is this Deponents Sister and was born in Half Moon Alley White Chapel in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex where this Deponents Father kept House for Twenty five years and upwards and paid all Taxes due for the same And this Deponent further saith that the said Elizth. Benjamin has never been bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a Settlement in her own Right

Sworn this 7th. day of Octr. 1794 before} John M Addington

her Abba Harris Mark
