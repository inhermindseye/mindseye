Middx & Westmr-}  Rebecca Aggas aged about 40 Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Noah Aggas who (is now in America ) and with whom she Intermarrd- at Shoreditch Church about 20 Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living namely William aged about Nineteen Years now provided for and Rebecca aged about seven Years Says her sd- husband Rented & lived in a house in John Street Totenham Court Road in the Parish of St- Pancras in the County of Middx for three Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of fourteen Guineas but never paid any Rent or Taxes for the same hath quitted same about 20 Years ago since which time her said husband never gained any or. subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 2nd- Day of October 1777  James Croft  Thos. Bishop .

her Rebecca Aggas Mark
