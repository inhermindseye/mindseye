Middx  Eliz: Clifford aged abt. 30 yrs. upon her Voluntary Oath says she was never married that on the 24th. day of Decr. 1756. she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Edwards in Peter Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. says one Willm. Smith a Labourer did beget the said Bastard Child on her Body she this Examinant Lodging at the house of Mrs. Hall at Tarnham in the County of Surry where she became acquainted with him says the Willm. Smith had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of Febry 1756 & at sevl. other times since at one of which times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid & that he the said Wm. Smith & no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn the 15th. of April 1757 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Clifford
