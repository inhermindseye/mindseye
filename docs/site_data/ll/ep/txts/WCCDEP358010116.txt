Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Charles Tool aged about 54 years upon his Oath saith that in the Month of Augt. 1783 he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of St. Paul Covent Garden by License & by whom he hath Two Children living vizt. John aged about 8 years born a Bastard in the House of a Mr. Jones in Red Lyon Street Holborn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middx and Chas . aged about 18 Months That he this Ext. Rented & lived in a House in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 3 Months at the yearly Rent of £20 that he hath quitted said House about 3 Months since which time he never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent of £10 for 40 days paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1787 R Butler

his Chas Tool Mark
