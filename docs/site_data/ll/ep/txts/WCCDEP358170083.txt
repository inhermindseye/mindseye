Middx  Ann Harrison aged about Nineteen Years upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she was by Indre dated the 23d. of May 1733 bound an Apprentice to Elizth- Emmett Quilter & this Examinant lived with her sd. Mistress untill the Time of her Death which hapened on or about the Month of March last in the Parish of St. Mary Woolnoth  London where this Examinant lived with her sd. Mistress for the Space of Four Years or thereabts- that she hath not been assigned over to any Person lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife yt- she is now Poor & not able to maintaine herself

Sworne the 6th- Day of July 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Ann Harrison
