Middx  Geo. Hodgson aged Sixty five Years upon his Oath saith that about Ten Years since he lived in & Rented a House in New Street by Broad Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £18. for the Space of Two Years that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath a Wife named Christiane aged about 40 Yrs & being Poor is not able to maintain himself & Wife

Sworne 2d. Sepr. 1743 before} Thos DeVeil J Poulson

The Mark of Geo: Hodgson
