Middx  Eliz: Cox upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that upwards of One Year ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Dickerson in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & that she hath not since she left her sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that on the Twenty Seventh Day of September last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at one Mrs. Moores in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized Charlton Clements and that one Robt. Gilder who is now abroad & beyond the Seas did begett the sd. Child on her Body she at such Time liveing a Yearly hired Servt - with one Mrs- Dickerson Mother

to the sd. Mr. Dickerson in Theobalds Court in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn in the sd. County of Middx where the sd. Robert Gilder became acquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body twice in or abt. the Month of December last at one of which Times he got her with the sd. Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & that he the sd. Robt. Gilder & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 29th. of Octr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Eliz: Cox
