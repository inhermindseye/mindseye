St.. Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster} (to wit) Sarah Grant Aged Sixty Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband John Grant at the C a Church or Chapel at Holliston (in that part of Great Britain called Scotland ) according to the Ceremony of the Church of England that her said about 40 years ago, that her said Husband died about twenty Six Years since, that Sher sa she this Examinant after the Death of her said Husband she Rented and Resided in an House in the parish of St. Luke Chelsea in a Street way there called Robinson's Lane , for about two Years at the Annual Rent of £13 that she quitted the said House about twenty years ago that She hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 P a Year, or done any other Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any other legal settlement to the best of her knowledges or belief, and that she is Unable to support herself

Sworn before Us the 3d. May 1791 . R Butler D Walker

Sarah Grant
