Westmr.  Stephen Plean upon Oath saith he was born in the Parish of Torough in the County of Limerick in Ireland  where his father lived in and rented a house & pd. Parish Taxes and that he this Examint. never did any Act whereby to gain 4 Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 28th. 1749 before}
