Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Keates aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 7th. Octr. 1781 she Intermarried with Robert Keates her late Husband (who has been dead about Seven Months) at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve years at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds that after he quitted said House he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th day of february 1797 before me} Saml. Edward

her Ann Keates Mark
