Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Lucy Springhall aged 50 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that about 16 years ago she being a Widow and having no Incumbrance she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Morgan the Co of Brownlow Street Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middx for 7 years & upwards at the yearly wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service about 9 years ago since which time she has not done any Act to gain any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of March 1787  Thos: Gordons N Bond

Springhall Lucy Mark Her
