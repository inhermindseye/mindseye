Westmr to wit Barbara Lawrence Aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or was an Apprentice, Saith she lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr. Thomas Biggs at the White Lyon in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Upwards of Eighteen Months at the yearly wages on eight Guineas hath quitted her said Service about 7 Years and hath not she Quitted the same ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that One William Hattam a Pensioner belonging to the Royall Hospital at Greenwich had Carnal knowledge of her Body the 21st of April 1772 and has ever since Cohabited with her at one of which time or times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Hattam and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 15th day of Feby 1775 before}
