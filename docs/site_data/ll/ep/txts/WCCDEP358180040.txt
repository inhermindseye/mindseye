Middx  Patrick Demsey upon his Oath saith. That he lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr James Magaven in the Parish of Clinkerry in the County of Kildare in the Kingdom of Ireland at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds for the Space of one Year & upwards & hath left the same abt. That since he left the said Mr. Magavens Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife. And that he hath Committed an Act of Vagrancy in the Parish within the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex

Sworne the 19th- Novr. 1742 before Houlson

The Mark of Patr: Demsey
