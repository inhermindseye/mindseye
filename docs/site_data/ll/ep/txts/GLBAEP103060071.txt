Middx  Mordecai Osborne Maketh Oath and Saith that about Twenty One years (when he was a Singleman) he was hired a yearly Servant to Mr . Thomas Harvey at the Abbey house at ye parish of Evesholm in the County of Worcester and Served him there as such three years and Received Five pounds a year wages and has gained no Subsequent Settlemt. to his knowledge and that he hath a wife named Sarah and three Children Namely Mary aged about twelve years Ruth aged about eight years & Marsa aged about two years.

Sworn 4th day of Septbr. 1746 Before Us. Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

Mordecai Osborn
