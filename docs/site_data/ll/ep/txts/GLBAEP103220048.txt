Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Gloster the wife of William Gloster maketh oath that about one year & Seven Months ago She was lawfully married to her Sd husband that about eight Years & Seven Months ago & When She this Deponent was a Single & Unmarried Woman & (Whose Maiden name was Stapleton) She this Depont was delivered of Child (Since baptized & registred by the name of George Goulding ) in the Parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London The which Child is a Bastard by reason that She this Depont was never married to the Said Childs Father whose name was Medley Goulding & who died about two Years ago

Sworn this. 15. day of June 1786 before  Robt. Smith J Hawley

The Mark of Elizabeth Gloster
