Middx The Exaicon of Catherine Blake  wife of James Blake lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 3d. day of Octr. 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that she Intermarryed with the above named James Blake abt. Twenty two years since and that her sd. Husband before his Intermarryage with this Exaiant lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Sr. Simon Harcourt in Norfolk Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and this Exaiant further Saith that her sd. Husband never rented Ten Pounds a Yeare paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband is a Soldier in Plymouth Garrison & she being poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the day & Yeare above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Cath: Blake
