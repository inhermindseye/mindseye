Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Bennett aged about Twenty Years now living at Mr. Reidin Stanhope St Oath says she never was married rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edwd. Brickleton a Druggist in Wych Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service about 18 Months ago since which time she hath never lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year and further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Parish (she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born) and further says that one Richard Parker a Shoemaker who lives at Mr. Hoppys a Shoemaker in the Minories is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at the said Mr Reids House in Stanhope Street afsd. sometime in the Month of February last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Richard Parker and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of July 1784 before R Butler

Mary Bennett
