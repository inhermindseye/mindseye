Middx  Joseph Wilson aged upwds. of 46 years upon Oath Saith he was married to his wife Elizabeth at the Fleet London abt. 12 years ago and that upwds. of Twenty Years since he was hired to live as a yearly Servt. with Dr. Green and lived with him in Beaufort Buildings in the Strand in the parish of St Clemt. Danes upwards of one year at the yearly wages of £6. and has not since he quitted his sd. Service Rented Ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Feby. 12th. 1753 before} Tho Lane

Joseph Wilson
