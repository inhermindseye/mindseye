Westmr. 

Eliz. Smallman aged abt. 74 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Richard Smallman and that her sd. late husband lived in and rented a house over agt. Coulsons Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and that neither her sd. late husband after he Quitted the sd. house nor this Examinant since his Death rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 12th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz Smallman
