Middx.  Peter Stevenson aged abt. 66 Yrs. upon Oath says he intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields abt. Twenty Years since; says that abt. Nineteen Years ago he Rented & lived in a house in Great Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £24 for three Quarters of a Year and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 31st. of July 1758 before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

Peter Stevenson
