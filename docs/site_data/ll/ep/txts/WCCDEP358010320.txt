St. Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Field aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with Miss James in Lime Street in the Parish of Saint Dionis in the City of London for Twelve months and upwards at the yearly wages of £5 that she hath quitted said service about Three years Nine months ago since which time she has done no act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of Janry. 1790  Sampr Wright N Bond

her Elizth. Field  Mark
