Middx  Eleanor Adams aged about Forty Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wilmot the Corner of Norfolk Street in the Strand  in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Nine Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

Eleanor Adams
