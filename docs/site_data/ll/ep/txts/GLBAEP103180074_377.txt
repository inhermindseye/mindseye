Middlesex ss}  Ann Bartlett the wife of Robert Bartlett maketh oath that her Said husband is now Abroad in the kings Service in America That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said husband or of his Parents or either of them Save that her said husband has informed that he was born in Scotland but the Place Where he never told her That She this Deponent was born in Saint Christophers in the West Indies & never did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself in any place in the Kingdom of Great Britain nor did her Parents ever do any Act to gain a Settlement there That She hath a Child named Frances aged about four Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this 23d day of October 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

Ann Bartlett
