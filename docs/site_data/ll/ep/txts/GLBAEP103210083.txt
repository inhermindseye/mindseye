Middlesex ss}  Sarah Lucas maketh oath that she well knows a Child named Elizabeth norgrove now resident in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County That such Child was born in Dolphin Alley in the precinct of St Catherine in the sd County about nine Years Ago she this Deponent being present at the birth of the said Child That she this Depont was well Acquainted with the sd Childs Mother who is dead & who has informed this Deponent & which she believes to be true that the sd Child was born Bastard she the Mother never having been married to the said Child's father whose name was John Norgrove .

Sworn this. 27. day of June 1785 before  Robt Smith R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Lucas
