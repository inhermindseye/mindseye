Westmr. to wit Richd. Phillips Aged about Forty Years on his Oath Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Joseph Slaymaker a Blacksmith in Barnaby Street in the Parish of Saint John Southwark in the County of Surry and served the said Joseph Slaymaker his Master upwards of one Year when his said late Mother bought the Remainder part of his said Apprenticeship of his said Master since which time the never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 11th. Day of January 1774 R. Butler Thos Kynaston

Richard Phillips
