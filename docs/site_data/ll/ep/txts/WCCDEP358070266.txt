Westr to wit John Furba Aged about Forty Years, on his Oath Says that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Finch a Farmers in the Parish of West Anvil near Chelmsford in Essex upwards of Fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds and soon after quitting his said last mentioned Service viz: about nineteen Years ago he went and resided in the Parish of Barking in the said County of Essex , where he intermarried with his late wife Margaret (and who has been dead about Seven Years), the Parish Officers of Barking refusing to let them Start any longer unless the obtained a Certificate from the said Parish of West Anvil , that Examinant Applied for such Certificate when the Parish officers of the said Parish of West anvil granted, this Examinant such Certificate Accordingly, Since which time he hath not gained any other subsequent Settlement.

Sworn the 6th day- of June 1775 before M Addington

his John Furba Marks Witness James Viger .
