Mary Pitt & Mary Smith her illegit. Child

Saint Clement Danes April 16th.. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary le bone Middx  St Margt. Westmr}  (to wit) Mary Pitt Aged 31 Years or thereabouts Singlewoman Upon her Oath Saith That She never was Married that she Rented and resided in an House in Oxford Street No/93 in the parish of St Mary le Bone in the County of Middlesex for about six years at the annual Rent of £40 or thereabouts that She quitted the same about seven years ago and hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And this Examinant also saith that She hath a legitimate one Child now livi living with her which was born of the Body of her this Examinant out of lawful Wedlock at the House No 2 Manchester Buildings in the parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster on or about the 4th day of December 1791 which hath been Christened Mary Smith and that she is at present incapable of supporting herself and Child without assistance

Sworn the 16th day of April 1794 before us W Addington B: Kennett

Mary Pitt
