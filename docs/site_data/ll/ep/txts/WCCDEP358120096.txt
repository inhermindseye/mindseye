Middx  Teresa Johnson wife of John Johnson and Martha Gowtery severally make Oath and first this Examt. Teresa Johnson upon Oath saith about or 12 Yrs. ago she intermarried with her sd. husband Jno Johnson at the Fleet London by whom she hath two Childn. (viz) Eliza . Martha aged abt. 5 Yrs. & Frances Abigail aged abt. 2 Yrs. Says her sd. husband has been gone from her abt. 6 months and that she does not know where he is nor can she give any Accot. of his Settlemt. other than that she has heard him Say he was born at Coventry & this Examint. being poor was maintained in her lying in at the charge of the Inhabitantly of St Clemet Danes Middx. And this Examint. Martha Gowtery upon Oath saith she is well acquainted with the above named John Johnson and that the sd. John Johnson Served Six Years or thereabouts of his Apprentiship wth. Thos. Munsloe at Warsall below the still in the Parish of Warsall in the County of Stafford (this Examt. living at Warsall at the same time) and after that the sd. Johnson run away from his sd. Master & came to London as she has been informed & believes

Sworn May 6th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Teresa Johnson

The Mark of Martha Gowtery
