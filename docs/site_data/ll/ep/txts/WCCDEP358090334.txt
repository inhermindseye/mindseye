Middx to wit Hannah Twydell aged about 38 years on behalf of Sarah Singer an Infant aged upwards of 5 years whose Mother is Dead and her Father William Singer is run away Says the said Wm. Singer Rented & lived in a House in White Hart Court in the Broad Way Westmr . in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westmr . for Sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of £10 and upwards and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Three Years ago and hath not since he quitted the the same ever Rented House or Tenemt. of £10 a Year paid Taxes or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 16th. Day of October 1782 before R Butler M Addington

Hannah Tydell
