St. Clement Danes

Middlesex Westmr.} to wit Mary Swinbank aged 25 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she Intermarried with John Swinbank her a present Husband at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields about Ten year ago That her Husband was bound an Apprentice to Mr Drysdale a Stagmaker in Eagle Street Red Lyon Square with whom as she has been informed he served his Time, And she Further Saith That Eagle Street is as then believes in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars , And that she has not any other Settlement to the best of no her knowledge or belief

Sworn before us this 25 day of January 1791 . E Read Thos. Gordon

her Mary Swinbank Mark.
