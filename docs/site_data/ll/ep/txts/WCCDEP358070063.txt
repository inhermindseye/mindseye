Westmr. to wit Elizth. Farmer Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Elizabeth Sherrads in the Parish of Saint George Bloombury in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Fourteen Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived as hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 19th. day of January 1773 before Us  Tho Kynaston 

her Elizth. Farmer Mark

Witness Edwd. Fields
