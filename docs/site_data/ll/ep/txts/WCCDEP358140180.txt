Middx.  Sarah Coombs aged abt. 21 Yrs. upon Oath says she Never was Married or Ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her father Oliver Coombs deceased Rented and lived in a house near Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and lived in the same abt. Nine Years and there dyed

Sworn this 11th. day of Novr. 1758 . Before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Combs
