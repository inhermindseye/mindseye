Middx  Mary Brooks aged abt. 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Tho Hambleton at the Kingshead in Mary bone Street in the parish of St James in the Liberty of westmr. for one year & upwards at the yearly wages of £4 quitted her sd. Service abt. 8 months ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn 24th May 1755 before} Barth: Hammond  Saml Pechell

The Mark of Mary Brooks
