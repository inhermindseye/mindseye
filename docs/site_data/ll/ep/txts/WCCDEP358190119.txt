Middx  Mary Holmes aged 66 Years Lodging in St. Clement's Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of James Holmes that since his Death she lived in & rented a House in St. Clement's Lane in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds Quitted the Same about 15 Years and hath not since rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or been a Yearly hired Servant

Sworn the 20th day of April 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Holmes
