Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Edward Burbidge one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Pinnell who is at present ill Maketh Oath & Saith that he has been informed which information he believes to be true that the said Mary Pinnell intermarried with James Pinnell her prest. Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn about Twenty one years ago that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Battle Bridge in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds that after he quitted said House he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Deponent knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 30th. day of May 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

Edwd. Burbidge
