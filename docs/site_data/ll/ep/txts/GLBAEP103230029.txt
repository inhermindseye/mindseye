Middlesex ss}  Catherine Tonge the wife of Richard Peter Tonge maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her in place to her unknown That on the eighteenth day of may one thousand Seven hundred and eighty Six her said husband with herself and children went and resided upon a Dwelling house of the yearly value of Fourteen Pounds in Illford in the Parish of Barking in the County of Essex and Continued in the occupation thereof untill the nineteenth day of July then next following That this Deponents said husband in right of her this Deponent was at the time of his entering on such house and residing thereon as aforesaid and still is justly intitled to one fourth Part or share of such house (being freehold of Inheritance) under and by Virtue of the Will of this Deponents late Father Peter Duffey deced That this Deponents said husband since his leaving such house as aforesaid has not to this Deponents knowledge or belief rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That she hath four children namely Richard aged about Seven years & three months Rebecca aged abt five years & three months Walter James aged about two years & seven months & George Augustus aged about two months & ten days the lawful Issue of this Depont by her sd husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this 15. day of March 1787 before me  R Davies  Rob Smith

Catherine Tonge
