Saint Clement Danes

And  Edward Burbidge one of the Beadles of the above Parish upon his Oath saith that he has been informed which information he believes to be true that John Jeffreys Sealey aged 23 years or thereabouts Rented a House and Shop in Leadenhall Market for 6 Months at the Yearly Rent of £40 per annum that since he quitted sd. House he has done no Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge and Belief

Sworn before us this 9th. day of Feby. 1788 .

Edwd Burbidge
