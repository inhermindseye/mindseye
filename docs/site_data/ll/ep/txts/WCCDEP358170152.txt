Middx  Margaret Plunkett  Wido . of Richard Plunkett upon her Oath saith that she was married to the sd. Richard Plunkett at the Queens Head the Corner of Old Bondstreet abt. Eighteen Years since by a Romish Priest & that her sd. Husband upwds. of Eighteen Years ago he being at that Time a Singleman & not having any Child or Children lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Walker in Portugall Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for the space of Four Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds that this Examinants said late Husband in his Life never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes nor she since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gaind. a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or Beleife

Sworne the 20th- Octr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of M Margt. Plunkett
