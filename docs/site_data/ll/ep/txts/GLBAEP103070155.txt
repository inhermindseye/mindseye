Middx  Margaret Nibblett wife of John Nibblett maketh oath that her sd husband has informed her that he duly served his Apprenticeship with one Mr Collins a Cordwainer in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middx & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there & that he Never gained any settlemt subsequent to his sd. apprenticeship which information this Depont. believes to be true And this Depont. saith that she hath two Children to wit Elizth. Aged about Six Years & Philip aged about three Years the lawfull issue of this Depont by her sd. husband And this Depont. saith that her sd. husband is lately gone away from her to places unknown

Sworn this 3 day of February 1756 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

The mark of Margtt Niblett
