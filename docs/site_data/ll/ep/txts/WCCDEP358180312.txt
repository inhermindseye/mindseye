Middx  Ann Rudge on Behalfe of Elizabeth Owen aged about 13 Years & Jane Owen aged about 3 Years the Children of Robt. Owen  deced by Alice his Wife absconded from them upon her Oath saith the sd.  Robt. Owen lived in & rented a house in Hog- Lane near Denmark Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds or thereabouts for one Year & upwds & hath left the same about Six Years Says the sd. Robt. Owen after he left the sd. House never rented a house of ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place & that the sd. Alice Owen since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Exaiants knowledge or beleif

Sworne 3d. April 1745 before Mr. Fraser

The Mark of Ann Rudge
