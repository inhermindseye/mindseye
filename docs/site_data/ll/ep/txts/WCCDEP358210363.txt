Middx  Thos. White aged about Thirty Two Years on his Oath says he never was married but that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Henry Barnsby a Broker in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Fifteen Pounds hath quitted his said Service upwards of Two Years ago and hath not since he quitted his said Service Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year neither has this Examinant Done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 1st. day of March 1769 before}

On Enquiry found this Examination to be false Barnsby the Mar. never having kept house a Twelvmonth in the Parish & the Examinant only an Earrand Boy for a little time
