St. Clemt. Danes

Ann Phillips aged 23 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes.That she lived as an hired yearly servant with Mrs. Bendon in Old Boswell Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for near three years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds, That she quitted her said Service about twelve Months ago since which time she this Examinant hath done no Act Matter of thing to the best of her knowledge Information or belief to gain any other settlement and further this Examinant upon her Oath Voluntary Oath saith That she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to become a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born, and fur. this & Thos.York a Post boy living at the Bulls Head in a Street near Piccadilly the name of which she this Examinant both not Recollect in the Father thereof he having had know Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body sometime in the Month of March last & at several times since at one of which times he the said Thos. York did begot her with Child or Children of which she is so pregnant & that she sd. Thos. York & no one else is the only real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 12th. day of Decr. 1789 before me} Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Phillips Mark
