Westmr.  Eliz Grove upon her Voluntary Oath Says she never was married and that she was delivered of a female Bastd. Child on the 7th. Day of Decr. Instt. in the house of Mr. Traford in Milk Court in Peter Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which has since been Baptized Elizabeth & is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that Mr. Robt. Lawford Clerk to St . Joseph Hankey did beget the sd. female Bastd. Child on her Body the sd. Robt. Lawford having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sevll. times in the month of April last in the house of Mrs. Howard in Bridges Street Covent Garden at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. female Bastd. Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & that he the

said Robt. Lawford & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworn Decr. 18th. 1746 before  Tho: Burdus  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz: Grove
