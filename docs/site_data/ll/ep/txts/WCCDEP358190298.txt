Middx  Ann Boovall aged abt. Thirty Six Years Upon Oath Says She Intermarried with her Husband James Boovall at the Fleet London upwards of Ten Years since by whom She hath one Child named Ann Aged abt. four Years Says her sd. Husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Jno. Hooker  Watchmovement maker in Golden Lane in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx and never Rented House or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year as her sd. Husband hath informed her & with information she beleives to be true

Sworn the 8th. day of Novr. 1762 before}
