Middx  Joachim King upon Oath saith that about Seventeen years ago being at that time a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Canning Apothecary in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £14 for one Year and upwards and has not Since he quitted his sd Service rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 9th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

Joachim King
