Middlesex ss}  Ann Millard the widow of Isaac Millard deced. maketh oath that about fifteen years ago her said late husband rented a house of Seven pounds & ten Shillings a year in Sun yard in the parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County & was charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 23 day of May 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Ann Millard
