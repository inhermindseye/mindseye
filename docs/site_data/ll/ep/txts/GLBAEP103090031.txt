Middlesex ss  Humphry Jones Maketh oath That about forty one Years ago he was duly bound to and Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with John Jones in the Parish of Saint James Westminister in the County of Middx  Cabinetmaker and by Means thereof as he is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that he never lived as a hired Servant for a year Nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes Nor ever Rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever did any Act to this Deponts. knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement Save and except Nevertheless that he this Deponent Subsequent to his said Apprenticeship lived as a Lodger or Inmate in the house of one Mrs. Elizabeth Vokins in Henrietta Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the said County in the which house he this Depont. (among other Inmates or Lodgers) had & enjoyed the Use & occupation of a Room And also Part of a Shop wherein he Carried on the Business of a Chandler for the space of two years & upwards & for which this Depont. Paid twenty Pounds by the year Rent But this Depont. Avers that during such his Residence in the said house he this Depont. never Paid any Parochial or other Taxes Nor was he ever Taxes or Charged for such his Residence to his knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1766 before  Chris: Scott  Rob Pell

Humphrey Jones
