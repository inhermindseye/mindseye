Westmr  Mary Browne Aged about Thirty Eight Years Upon Oath Says she was never Married Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Peirson in Burton Street in the Parish of St John the Evangelist in the Liberty of Westmr. Upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted said Service about Ten Months and hath not Since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 27th Day of March 1764 Before R Kilster Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Browne

Upon Mr Olivers Complt Ovr J W
