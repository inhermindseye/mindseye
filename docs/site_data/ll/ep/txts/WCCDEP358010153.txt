Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Sterling aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that about 22 years ago she Intermarried with Wm. Sterling her present Husband at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields and by whom she hath 5 Children living 3 of whom are provided for and Two unprovided for vizt. Sarah aged about 11 years and Cath : aged about 6 years That her said Husband Served the latter part of his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Disterberry a Taylor in Cecil bound St. Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . that he has never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 16th. day of Octr. 1787 D Walker Rapt: Clarke

her Sarah Sterling Mark
