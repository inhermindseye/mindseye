Middx  Ann Condron Aged about 40 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of St. George Hanover Square about fourteen Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her sd. late Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship to John Howard the Younger of the Parish of St. Anns Linnh in the County of Middlesex  Carpenter and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any or. Settlemt. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds P Annl. paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this  12th day of March 1783  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

her Ann Condron  Mark
