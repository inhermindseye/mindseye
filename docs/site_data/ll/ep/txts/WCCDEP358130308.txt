Middx  Eliz: Applegarth aged 39 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Edwd. Applegarth  (gone from her) with whom she intermarried at the fleet London abt. sixteen years ago, says she hath three Childn. by her said Husband (to wit) Mary aged abt. thirteen years, William aged upwards of Nine years & John aged upwards of four years says her sd. Husband served his Apprenticeship with the Widow Archer a Papermaker in the Parish of Sittingbone in the County of Kent as her sd. Husbd. informed her & as she verily believes & that he never Rented Ten Pounds a yr. or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 18th. of March: 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz. Applegath
