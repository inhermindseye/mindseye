Westmr. to wit James Warner Aged about Thirty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of St. James in the Liberty of West upwards of Six Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children now living namely John Aged about five Years and Elizabeth Aged about Three Months Says he Rented and lived in a House in Richmond Street in the said Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years or thereabout at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the same about four Years ago & hath not since he quitted the same ever rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn this 8th. day of Septr. 1772 before us} R. Butler Tho. Keynaston R. Butler

James Warner
