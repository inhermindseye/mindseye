Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Rebecca Spencer aged about 32 years on behalf of Mary Groves Maketh Oath and Saith that the said Mary Groves was bound an Apprentice to this Examt. about 20 Months ago that she this Examt. not being able to support the said Mary Groves applied to a Justice and got her discharged that the last Forty days Servitude under the said Indre was at No. 10 Great Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 3rd day of Septr. 1788  Rt. Taylor J Barnfather

Rebecca Spencer
