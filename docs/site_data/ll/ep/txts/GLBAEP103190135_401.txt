Middlesex ss}  Jane Mathews  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years Ago her late Father rented a house in Sun Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a settlement else Where That She this Deponent lived with him there as part of his Family & never was bound An Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 15 day of August 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Jane Matthews
