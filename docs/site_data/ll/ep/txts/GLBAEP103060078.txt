Aldgate Middx

John Adkins Maketh Oath that the Serv'd Seven years Apprenticeship to James Adkins his Father a Plasterer in Liquorpond Street near Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middx that he never was a hired Servant by the year nor rented a house of £10 a year nor never paid any Parish taxes, nor Servd any Parish or Office in any Parish or place Whatsoever and that about five years ago he was maintained in the Workehouse belonging to the Said Parish of St. Andrew Holborn hath a wife  Jane

Sworn 26th day of January 1746 before Robert Dennett Richd. Ricards

John Adkins
