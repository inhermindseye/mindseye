Westmr.  Mary Russell Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with George Russell her late Husband lately deceased at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields about Eighteen Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living namely George aged about Three Years and Frances Aged about Eight Months Says her said late Husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Heath a Taylor in Arrundell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship He never Rented House Paid Taxes or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Octr. 1773 before R. Butler

her Mary Russell  Mark
