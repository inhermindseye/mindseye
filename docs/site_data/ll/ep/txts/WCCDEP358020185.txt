St Clemt. Danes.

Samuel Clubb aged 37 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he was born in Scrubs Court in The Parish of St. Andrew Holborn below she born in the City of London where his Father Rented and lived in a House at the yearly rent of That to for upwards of twelve Months That he this Examinant never Rented a House lived as an hired yearly Servant over bound Apprentice or did any other Act Matter or Thing to gain a settlement otherwise than by Saith

Sworn this 17th. day of Janry. 1798 J Addington N Ferster

S Clubb
