Middx  Ann Gwillim Aged about 30 Years Upon Oath She is the Widow of Thos Gwillim with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of Rumford in the County of Essex about Nine Years ago Says her said Husband  Rented a House in Honey Lane Market in the Parish of St. Lawrance Jewry in the City of London upwards of Three Years At the Yearly Rent of Twenty Five Pounds or thereabouts hath quitted said House about Six Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes, Says she has One Child by her said Husband Named Sarah Aged about Six Years.

Sworn the 27th Day of Septr 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell W Kelyngs

A Gwillim
