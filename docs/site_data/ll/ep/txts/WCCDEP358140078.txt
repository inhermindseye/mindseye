Middx  Elizth. Ireland aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath says she never was Married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Willet in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted said Service abt. four Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Janry 26th. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Ireland
