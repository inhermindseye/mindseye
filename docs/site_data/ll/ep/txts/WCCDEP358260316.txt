Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Page aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that about 23 years ago she Intermarried with Robert Page her present Husband who is now from  at the Parish Church of Saint Annes Westmr . That her said Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Spencer a Shoemaker in Hungerford Market in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement and further saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 16th. day of Augt. 1798 before us  N Bond Saml Edward

her Elizabeth Page Mark
