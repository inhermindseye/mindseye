Middlesex ss.  John Searle maketh Oath that about Six Years ago he Rented a House of Thirty four Pounds a Year called the Lion Man of War in Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & lived in the same about Two Years and was charged to and paid the Poors Rate for the same that he has not since he quitted the said House Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement that he has Two Children Namely Joseph aged about Six Years & a half and George aged about Five Years the lawful issue by his late Wife Elizabeth deceased to whom he was Lawfully Married in Bedfordshire about Ten Years ago John Searle

Sworn this 24 Day of July 1794 before H: Reynell
