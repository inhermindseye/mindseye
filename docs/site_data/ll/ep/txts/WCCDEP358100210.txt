Middx to wit Jas Tasker Aged about 34 Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields about 14 Years ago by whom he hath Four Children now living Namely James Aged 10 Years David Aged 7 Years Jacob Aged 3 Years Polly Aged about 4 Months Says he Served 7 Years Apprenticeship with Saml Noye of Arundell Street in the Strand in the County of Middx Peruke Maker & after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Settlement Either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year Paying Taxes or othews howsoever

Sworn this 4th day of May 1784 before}

his Jas Tasker Mark

{ Jas Tasker will not smear it.}

{on any Account whatever}
