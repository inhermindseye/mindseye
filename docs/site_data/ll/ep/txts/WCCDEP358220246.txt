Westmr  Betty Addison Aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Loft a Councellor at Law in Boswell Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for three Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six pound ten Shillings hath quitted her sd Service about ten Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same Ever Rented House pd Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 7th Day of March 1771 before} Tho Lane .

Betty Addison
