Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizabeth Tracey who is insane Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Williams a Taylor in Marybone Street Shug Lane in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months and upwards that she quitted said service in April last since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 7th. day of February 1789 M Addington John Croft

Benj Newman
