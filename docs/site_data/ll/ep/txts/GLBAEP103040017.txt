Middlesex ss. The examination of Jane Lonsdale of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken on Oath before me one of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Day of One Thousand Seven Hundred and Ninety Seven.

Who Saith that on or about the Twenty third Day of February now last past in the Dwelling House of Josh. Alexander in Angel Court in the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate She the Said Jane Lonsdale was delivered of a Male Bastard Child
