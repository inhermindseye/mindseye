Middlesex ss}  John Brown maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house at the yearly Rent of five Pounds & Ten Shillings at Ratcliffe Cross in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the Sd County & that during such his residence there in he was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the same That he has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Susannah to whom he was lawfully married and who is at Present absent from him in Places to him unknown

Sworn this- 12. day of February 1788 before  R Davies  Robt.Smith

The Mark of John Brown
