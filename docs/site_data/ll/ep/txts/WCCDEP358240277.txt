St Clement Danes June 19th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Liberty of the Rolls  (to wit) Sarah Stevens aged 51 years & upwards singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Campbell Taylor No 3 in Great Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for two years & upwards at the annual Wages of seven Guineas that she quitted that Service about six years about six years ago and hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together under one hiring or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 19th. day of June 1793 before Us} Mitchell W Addington

Sarah Stevens 's Mark
