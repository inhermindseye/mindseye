Middlesex to wit Martha Robinson Aged about Forty five years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to Omer Robinson her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of St. Anns Limehouse in the County of Middx in the year 1767 and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said late Husband never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Husbands Father Rented a Small House at Mitcham in the County of Surry where this Examinants Husband was Born as her sd. late Husbands has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true & can give no further or other Account thereof than as aforesaid.

Sworn this 1st. day of July 1783 R Butler W Addington

Martha Robbinson
