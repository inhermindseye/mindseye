Middx  Frances Hutchinson Lodging at Mr Whites a Hatter in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she was never Marryed and that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish And that William Owen  Journey- man to Mr. Hawkins Bookseller  near the Temple Gate did begett the said Child or Children on her Body the said Mr. Hawkins renting the Shopp & other Appartments of this Examinants Uncle with whom this Examinant then lived the sd Wm Owen at the same time liveing with the said Mr. Hawkins there been me acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body sevll- times in the sevll- Months of April May June July Augt. & Septr- & at sevll- other times since at one of which times he the sd. Wm- Owen gett her with Child of the sd. Child or Childn- with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Wm. Owen & neither Person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworne the 7th. Day of March 1742 before} Wm: Jones .

Frances Hutchinson
