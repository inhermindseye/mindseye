Middlesex to wit Susannah Hicks aged about 10 Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to Stephen Hicks her Husband (gone from her) at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about four Months ago Says her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to Messrs. Skreen and Chambers in Nicholas Lane Lombard Street in the Parish of St. Nicholas Acons  London and Served the first Year of his Apprenticeship and afterwards ran away and went to Sea and further says that her said Husband never gained any other Settlement thas as aforesd

Sworn this 23d day of August 1784 before G Mercer James Croft

her Susannah Hicks Mark
