Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Sarah Archer aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Smith in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Eighteen Months at the yearly wages of Eight Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Eight Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith That on the 15th: day of January now last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at No. 3 in Old Boswell Court That one William Carter a Journeyman Taylor who uses the Blue Anchor in Fenchurch Street but where he lives she this Deponent doth not know is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said William Carter and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 1st: day of Septr. 1795 } N Bond

her Sarah Archer Mark

Sarah Archer lives at Mr. Mickelworths Evers Lane near Reading Hampshire

Man never taken Qy. what become of the Child not sent to Enfield
