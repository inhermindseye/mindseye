Middlesex to wit Walter Hargrave Aged about fifty Seven years on his Oath Says He Intermarried with  Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Sidwell in the City of Exeter in the year 1744 by whom he hath one Child now living who is Married and provided for but whether is said Wife is dead or not he Cannot set forth Says He rented and lived in a House in Arrundell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for several years at the yearly Rent of forty Two Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same quitted said House in the year 1758 since which time he hath never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. 1775 before me  Wm. Kitchiner

Wr: Hargrave
