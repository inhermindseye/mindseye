Middx  Sarah Tyler aged abt. Thirty two Years upon Oath says She is a Singlewoman & that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Mary Higgens at the Lukes head Alehouse in Mercer Street Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for Three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds & hath quitted her said Service about fourteen Years to her now best Remembrance Says She hath not since She left Mrs. Higgins's Service Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 28th. Day of July 1756 . before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Sarah Tyler
