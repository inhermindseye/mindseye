Middx  The Exaicon of Mary the wife of Wm. Anderson Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 23th. day of Janry 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that abt. Twenty Years ago her sd. Husband Wm. Anderson Served his Apprentispp with one Mr. Pengrove in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. Olave in Southwark  Perukemaker And further Saith that her sd. husband never Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her Husband is on Board one of his Majestys Ships of Warr and she haveing two Children (to wit) Esther Aged upwds. of Eight Years and Wm . Aged abt. two Years in poor & not able to maintain them

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane  Jas Gronous

Mary Anderson
