Westmr.  Abigail Neale upon Oath she intermarried with Joseph Neale about Eight Years ago at the Fleet London Says her sd. husband was born in Scotland but in what part of that Country she knows not and that he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year or paid any Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge Says she lived a Yearly Servant (before her sd. marriage) with Mrs. Roberts in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £8 for 6 Years & upwards Says her sd. husband has been gone from her upwards of Seven Years and she has not since Seen or heard from him

Sworn May 9th. 1748 before}
