Middx to wit Joseph Garnham Aged about Twenty five years on his Oath says he never was married and that he lived a hired yearly servant with one Mr. Wright Attorney at Law at Thetford in the County of Norfolk for One Year at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted his sd Service about Six Months and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th. day of Novr. 1769 } R Butler

Joseph Garnham
