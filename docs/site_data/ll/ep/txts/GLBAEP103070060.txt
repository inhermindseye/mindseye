Middx ss.  Sarah Carroll  wife of James Carroll (who is lately gone away from her this Deponent to places and now to her) Maketh oath that her sd. husbands has informed her that about fourteen Years ago he was bound apprentice to Mr. William Manly of the Parish of st. Ann Lime house in the County of Middx  Pipemaker With whom he duly Served his apprenticeship & by Means thereof acquired a legal Settlemt. in ye sd. Parish as this Depont. is informed & believes And this Dept. Says that her sd. husband hath not Since rented a house of Ten pounds by ye Year or upwards nor Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief And Says that she has two Children to Wit Ann aged about Seven Years and John Aged about Six Years she lawfull issue of this Depont. & her sd. husband

Sworn this 23d day of March 1752 before us  Richd : Ricards  Thos: Quarrill

Sarah Carroll
