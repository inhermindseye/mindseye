Middlesex & Westr-} to wit Mary Child Aged Nineteen Years On her Oath says she never was married Rented House Paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Michael Child late a Sheriffs Officer Rented & Lived in a house in Holles Street  Clare Markett at the Yearly Rent of £35 a Year & Paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House upwards of Five Years ago since which time he Never Rented House Tenement or Lodgings of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any or Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the said Parish & further says on that one Robert Morgan a Journeyman Bookbinder at Mr Mr Millers in St Martins Lane had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of September last but the Particular time she does not now and at several Difft. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant & that the said Robt Morgan and no other Person did beget the same & that he is the Real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 13th- day of April 1784 before  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Child
