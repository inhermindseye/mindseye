Westmr. to wit Elizth. Welden Aged about Sixty Two Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Daniel Welden who has been dead upwards of three Years & with whom she Intermarried at Shoreditch about five Years Ago Says that her said late Husband Served Apprenticeship with Mr. Philip Poole a Combe maker in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlex on as her said Husband and the said Philip Poole as often Informed her & which Information she verily believes to be true Says that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year before his Intermarriage with his farmer Wife or of this Examinant as he has often Informed this Examinant and which she verily believes to be true

Sworn this 27th.day of Novr. 1771 before me W Kelyngs

Elizbeth Welden
