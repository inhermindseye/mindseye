St Clement Danes 23d day of Janry. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Giles in the fields  (to wit) Sarah Cox aged 67 years or thereabouts Upon Oath said that she was Married to her late Husband John Cox at the parish Church of South Collingham near Newark upon Trent in the County of Nottingham about 41 years ago saith that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and Resided in and Apartment in High Holborn nearly facing Dean Street in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex unfurrnised at the yearly Rent of £10 for about 2 years that She quitted the same about 33 years ago and and did not afterwards Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year pay parish Taxes or do any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge or belief saith that he died about 25 years ago and she hath not since obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 23d day of January 1793 before Us } Wm. Kitchiner D Walker

Sarah Cox

Removed by Edwd. Burbidge  Beadle 23d Janry. 1793
