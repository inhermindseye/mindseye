Middx to wit Susannah Dean Aged about Fifty Two Years on her Oath says She is the Widow of Jonathan Dean who died about Sixteen Years ago And by whom She hath no Child or Children living says that being a Widow and without Child or Children She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Shield Green Grocer in the Clare markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Six Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said service about Nine Months ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th: day of June 1780 before  Abrm Baley

her Susannah Dean Mark
