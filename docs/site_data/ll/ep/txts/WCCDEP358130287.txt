Middx.  Mary Wisdom aged abt. 40 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of James Wisdom (gone from her) with whom She Intermarried at the Fleet London about fifteen Years since Says her said Husband Rented a House in Pepper Alley in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry of the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds lived therein abt. two Yrs. & quitted same abt. six Years, since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 1st. day of March 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Wisdom
