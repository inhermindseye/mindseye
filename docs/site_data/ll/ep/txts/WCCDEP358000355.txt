Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Mary Edmondson aged 22 Years thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that about 2-years ago she Intermarried with James Edmondson (who has deserted her) at the Parish Church of Lambeth and by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Jas. Edmondson aged about Thirteen Mos. that her said Husband served Seve years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Dobbins of Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship her never did any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Settlement and the said Deponent  any Saith he said Husband has  and best her in the Parish of Saint Cement danes whereby She is became Chargeable to the same

Sworn this 14th. day of Septr. 1786 R Butler

Mary Edmondson

Resworn the 16th. day of Sepr 1786 Before me D Walker
