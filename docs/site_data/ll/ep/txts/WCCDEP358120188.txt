Middx (to wit)  Evian Smith upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Wm. Smith by whom she has 3 Childn. John aged 5 Years Richd. aged 3 Years & David aged 8 Months & that her sd. husband informed her Served his Apprentiship wth. John Latham a Taylor in Elbow Lane in the Parish of Liverpoole in the County of Lancaster & never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Septr. 2d. 1754 before} Barth:Hammond

The Mark of Evian Smith
