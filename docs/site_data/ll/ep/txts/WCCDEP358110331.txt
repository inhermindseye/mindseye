Middx.  John Cartwright upon Oath Saith he rented a house at the Corner of Exeter Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds Olived therein upwds. of five Years quitted the sd. house in the Year 1741 and has not since rented ten Pounds a Year or paid parish taxes.

Sworn Octr. 16th. 1753 before

lived afterwards are in knows a of £14. Yr. which he rented of Baldwin the Bricklayer near the Cole pd. drury Lane .
