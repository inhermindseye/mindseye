Middx.  Bridget Marsden aged abt. 23 Years on the behalf of Mary Godfrey an Infant aged abt. Eleven Years upon Oath says that the said Mary Godfrey was born on Sir John's Key in the Parish of St. Andrew in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland and never Gained any Settlemt. in England as this Examinant has been informed and believes.

Sworn the Ninth day of August 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Bridget Marsden
