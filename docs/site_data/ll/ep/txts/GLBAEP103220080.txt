Middlesex ss  Ann White of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Singlewoman maketh oath that She this Deponent was born in Aberdeen in Scotland That She never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in any Place in England And that She was never Married

Sworn this 4th day of Novemr 1786 before R Davies

Ann White
