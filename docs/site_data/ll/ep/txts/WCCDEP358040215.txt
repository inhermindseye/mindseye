Middx 

Martha Eliza. Argent aged upwds. of 26 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Danl. Argent with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London upwards of three Years ago Says her sd. husband dyed about Six Weeks after their Intermarriage at Mr. Vaughans in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx where he had lived as a Yearly Servt. upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of £10 and that abosets two Years ago (being at that time a wido. & without Child or Children) she lived a Yearly Servt. with her father Robt. Gwgnn in Warder Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of four pound for one yr & upwds. and has not since gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain she same when born and that

John Danson  Apprentice to Mr. House of Warder Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Mason did be get the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. John Danson having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the house of the sd. Mr. House where this Examinant lodged in the month of Novr. last and at sevt. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the sd. John Danson & no other Person is the true & rent father thereof

Sworn July 12th. 1748 before} Tho Lane

Martha Elizeb Argnt
