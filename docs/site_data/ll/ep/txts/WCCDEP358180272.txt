Middx  Hanh. Wild  Wido . aged about 60 Years removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds by Order Dated the 7th. Day of January Instant upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her late Husband  John Wild about 38 Years since at Cripplegate Church London Says the sd. John Wild lived in and Rented a house at the Corner of Clements Inn Gate in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of £24 & lived there in 5 Yrs. & upwds. & hath left the same about 11 Years since which time neither the sd. John Wild in his Life time nor this Examinant since his Death ever Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Child (to wit) Charles Aged about 11 Yrs

Sworn Janry. 8th. 1744 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Hanh. Wild
