Middx ss  Elizth. Green Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with on Mrs. Mc. Bean late of Craven Buildings Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for fifteen Months or upwards at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Three Years Ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further says that on the Twenty Ninth day of July last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Hospital in Oxford Road in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone which has since been Baptized by the Name of Elizabeth she being Poor and unable to maintain the same And further Says that one William Robinson a Sidler who lately lodged at one Mr. Walkers a Peruke maker in Turnmill Street Clerkenwell Green is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of October last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Child of which she was so delivered as aforesd. And that the said William Robinson and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1774 before} 

her Elizth. Green Mark
