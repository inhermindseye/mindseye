Middlesex and Westminster}  T Josh Andrew  Holborn  above Bars. to wit James Talboys of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of William Williams aged about 66 years on his Oath says that said he has been Creditably Informed that he Intermarried to Elizth . his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Paul  Covent Garden about 20 years ago and by whom he has Six Children living Vizt. Ann Aged about 15 years Edwd. Wm . aged 13 years Wm . aged 11 years Elizth . aged  Six years Peter Aged three and Mary aged Eighteen Months, Says he rented part of a house in Bell Court  Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Barrs at the Yearly Rent of Eleven Guineas for hath quitted the said about Eight Years ago since which time he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year

Sworn this 10th. day of December 1784  Jno: Wright James Caine

James Talboys
