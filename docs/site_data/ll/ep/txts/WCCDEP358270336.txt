Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Penelope Rogers aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the 15th. day of August now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Morgan No. 21 Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the Name of Triphila That one Elinell Todd a Livery Lace Weaver cor lodges at No. 129 Long Acre is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Elinell Todd is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 1st. day of Novr. 1797 before me } J Hunter

Penelope Rogers

Penelope Rogers lodges at Mr. Randalls No. 5 Daniel Street

7th. Novr. 1798 Father Apprehended and paid £15. Qrs. what become of this Child Whether any & what Allowance
