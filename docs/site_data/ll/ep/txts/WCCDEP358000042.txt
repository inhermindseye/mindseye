Middlesex and Westminster}  & passd. to St. Matthew Bethnell green (to wit) Elizabeth Hide Aged 17 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that She never was Married Rented a House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or unr paid Taxes or Lived as an Hired Yearly Servant Saith That She was Bound Apprentice to One Bedward of Brick Lane in the Parish of Saint Matthew Bethnal Green in the County of Middlesex by the Churchwarden and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Sepulchre in the City of London until She should Attain her Aged Twenty One Years or Day of Marriage according to the Act of Parliament Saith that She Lived under such Indenture for the Space of one year or thereabout and left her said Masters Service about Christmas last saith that She never gained any Settlement elswhere to the best of her knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 10th. Day of May 1785 before us W Addington G Kelly
