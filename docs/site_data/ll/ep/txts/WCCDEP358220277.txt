Westmr.  Margaret Maule Aged about Seventy Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Robert Maule who has been dead about Twelve Months and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Botolph Aldersgate about Twenty Eight Years ago Says her said Husband was a Native of Scotland and never gained a Settlement in England to her Knowledge or belief either by renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says that before her said Intermarriage with her said late Husband and whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Ord a Staymaker in Round Court in the Parish of St. Leonard Foster in the Liberty of St. Martin le Grand upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of 28 Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same or since her said Husbands Death ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 27th. day of June 1771 before W Kelynge Jno. Barnfather

Margaret Maule
