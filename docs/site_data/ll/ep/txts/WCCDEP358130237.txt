Middx.  Mary Price aged abt. Twenty four Years passed as a Vagrant from the Parish of Salford in the County of Oxford upon Oath says She never was Married Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year, Says She is the Daughter of Stephen Price by Hannah his Wife both deced Says She hath often heard her sd. late Father Stephen Price say he served his Apprentiship of Seven Years with one Charles Sayer in the Parish of Kineton in the County of Hereford And further says She was by a Pass under the Hand & Seal of Thos. Lane Esqr. bearing date the 26th. day of Novr. 1755. passed as a Vagrant from the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. to the Parish of Kineton in the County of Hereford aforesaid

Sworn Novr. 25th. 1756 before}
