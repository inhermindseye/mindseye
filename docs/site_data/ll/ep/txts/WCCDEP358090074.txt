Middx  Ann Winter aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she went to live as hired Yearly servant with one William Hinlock a Druggest and who lately kept a House at No. 30 Cow Lane now Called Queen Street Snow Hill in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre London of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Five Pounds as she believes and by whom she hath three Illegitimate Children now living Namely William aged about Nine Years Charlott aged about Six Years and John aged about four Years all born in the aforesaid Parish of Saint Sepulchre London and further says that the said William Hinlock is the only real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of September 1779 before M Addington

her Elizth: Winter Mark
