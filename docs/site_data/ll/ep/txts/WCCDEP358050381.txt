Middx  Johanna Huddleston upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Arthur Huddleston who dyed upwds. of 30 Years ago & that her sd. late husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £15 in Silver Street in the parish of St James in the Liberty of Westmr. and lived therein abt. Seven Years & that her sd. late husband whilst living of this Examinant since his Decease never after he quitted the sd house rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 6th 1750 before} Tho Lane

Johanna Hurleton
