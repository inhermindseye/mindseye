Middx.  Mary Cottingham aged abt. 35 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mills a Bailiff in Katharine Street in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. for three years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds and hath Quitted said Service abt. 3 Yrs. and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 7th. day of March 1760 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Cottingham
