St Clement Danes September 16th 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  No Settlemt.  All Saints Epping Essex  Removed by B. Newman Beadle  (to wit) Sarah Curle  singlewoman aged 18 years and upwards upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant at the Cock Cock Inn in the parish of St. in the Town of Epping in the parish of All Saints Epping in the County of Essex for a year and an half at the annual Wages of £3 that she quitted that Service about 5 years ago and hath not Since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together Rented any House or Apartment of Ten pounds a Year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 16th day of September 1793 before Us M Addington N Bond

Sarah Curle 's Mark

Witness Benj Newman
