St Clement Danes 14th Decr 1793

Middlesex And Westminster }  St. John the Baptist New Windsor Berks  (to wit) Elizabeth Herbert  singlewoman aged about twenty five Years Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived As a Servant (under a general hiring) with Mr Kimberley at the three Tuns public House in the parish of St John the Baptist in the Town of New Windsor in the County of Berks for two years or upwards at one time that she quitted that service about two years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief that she is pregnant with Child which is likely to be born a Bastard And become chargeable to the parish of St Clement Danes if not prevented

Sworn the 14th. Decr. 1793 before us R. Ford B: Kennett

Elizth. Herbert 's Mark

Witness Geo. Robertson
