Middx  Eliz Ayres aged upwds. of 28 Yrs. upon Oath saith She never was married and that upwards of 2 Yrs. ago she was hired as a yearly Servant to Mr Jno. Divinee a Leather Clogmaker at the yearly wages of £4 and lived wth. him under that hiring about two Years; the last Six months of which time she lodged in the house of the sd. Divinee on little Saffron hill in the Liberty of Saffron hill  Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the parish of St. Andw. in Holbn. in the County of Middx and quitted her sd. Service about her months since

Sworn June 5th. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell  Thos Lane

The mark of Eliz Ayres
