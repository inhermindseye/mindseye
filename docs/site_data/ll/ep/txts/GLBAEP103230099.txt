Middlesex ss}  Elisabeth Beburney maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servent at Certain Wages by & with Nicholas ohhogen in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in hte Sd County and Continued there in Service Service  Such hiring for the Space of a Year and upwards That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or been ever Married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 4. day of January 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Beburney
