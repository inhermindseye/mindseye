Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit John Williams aged about 72 Years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a House in Star Court Butsher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 17 years at the yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds hath quitted said House about 7 years ago since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Depts. knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Augt. 1786 R Butler

John William
