Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Close aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Werrip who kept the Spread Eagle Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster for about Two years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Seven years ago since which time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of May 1790 G S Bradshaw J Barnfather

Elizabeth Close
