Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Toppins aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that about Six years ago she Intermarried with John Topping her late Husband who has been dead about Two years at Stoke Church Plymouth That her said late Husband Served his Apprenticeship to a Mrs. Wood in Billiter Lane in the Parish of Saint Catherine Cree in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement And further saith that She is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 1st. day of Novr. 1798 before us  H Wisstead  Russ Clarke

her Ann Topping Mark

Witness H Day
