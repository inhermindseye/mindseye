Middx.  Eliz: Hart upon Oath says she is the of John Hart (now at Sea) says her said Husband informed her & which Information she verily believes to be true that he is a Native of Ireland & never gained any Settlement in England & that he was born in Red Cow Lane in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland; says she has one Child by him; Esther aged abt. Eleven Months

Sworn the 1st. March 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Hart
