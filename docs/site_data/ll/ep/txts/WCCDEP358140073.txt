Middx.  Alice Smith aged abt. 41 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Nichs. Hailstone in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for five Years & upwards & hath quitted said Service abt. Seventeen Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 20th. day of Janry. 1758 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Alice Smith
