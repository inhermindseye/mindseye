Westmr.  James Deakin removed with Martha his Wife & their two Children (viz:) James aged abt 8 Yr. and Mary aged abt. 5 Yrs. from the Liberty of Saffron hill  Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex upon Oath Says he was bound an Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to one Mr. Chapman of Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middlesex Peruke maker & lived with the sd. Mr. Chapman upwards of one Year & then was assigned over to Mr. Almet in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes  Peruke maker with whom this Examint. & his sd. Master Parted by Consent Says he never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Year by Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 24th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

James Denkin
