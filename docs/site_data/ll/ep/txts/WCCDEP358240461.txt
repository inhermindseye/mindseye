Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Ann Simons aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1785 She Intermarried with Daniel Simmons her late husband at the parish Church of Saint Mary Lambeth That her said late Husband Served informed this Examinant that he Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Ford a Mathematical Instrument maker in Crooked Lane in the parish of Saint Michael in Crooked Lane in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of February 1795 } Robt: Barr Gabriel W Addington

her Ann Simonds Mark

Witness B Newman
