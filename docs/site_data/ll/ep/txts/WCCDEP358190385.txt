Middx  Alexr Wilson Aged about Forty Five Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife  Mary in the Parish Church of St. Martins in ye Fields about Ten Years ago Says in the Year 1754 He Rented a House in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds and lived in the Same about One Year and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes Says his said Wife is Aged about Forty Six Years is now Ill and this Examinant is not Able to Maintain her.

Sworn the 26th Day of Septr. 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Alexander Wilson
