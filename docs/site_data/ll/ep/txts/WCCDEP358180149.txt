Middx  Ann Pointin in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Singlewoman upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesd. she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one Mathew Callahan , of the parish of Rotherhithe in the County of Surry Anchor maker did be gett the sd. Bastd. Child or Childn. on her Body, she liveing a at such time with Mrs. Lally her Aunt in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds & the sd. Mathw. Callahan being acquainted with her
