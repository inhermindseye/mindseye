St Clement Danes 4th January 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Jane Cane aged 65 years or thereabout upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband John Cane at the parish Church of St Michael Dublin about 46 years ago that her Husband Died about 15 years ago And that since her said Husband Death she this Deponent (being  a Widow unmarried and not having any Child or Children under the age of 26 years) lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr William in Strand Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about 1 year and ten Months at the annual Wages of £3 that she quitted that service about 9 Months ago and hath never obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 9th January 1792 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Jane Cane 's Mark

Witness B Newman
