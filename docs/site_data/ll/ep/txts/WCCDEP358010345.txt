Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Flynn of No.58 Clements Lane on behalf of James Flynn an Infant maketh Oath and Saith That the said James Flynn the Infant was born a Bastard at the house of a Mr. Kichoe in Orange Court Wild Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex some time in or about the month of april 1784

Sworn this 19th. day of June 1790  Sampr Wright M Addington

James Flynn
