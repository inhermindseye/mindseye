Westmr ss  John Dyke on the behalf of Jane Gray an Infant aged about ten Years on his Oath says that George Gray her father (who is gone away and deserted her) Rented and lived in a House at low Cross in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for one Year or thereabouts at the Yearly rent of ten pounds as he believes hath quitted said house about three Years ago since which time the said George Gray or ever rented house paid taxes or has done any other Act where by to gain a subsequent Settlement to his knowledge or belief neither hath the said Jane Gray since her said father has deserted her

Sworn this first Day of May 1776 before  R Butler Wm Kitchiner

John Dyke
