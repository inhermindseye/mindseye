Westmr. 

John Morley upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Phillis on or abt. the 2d. Day of July upwds. of Eight Years since at the Fleet London Says he lived in and rented a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly or Rent of £24 for the Space of one Year & upwds. and hath left same about Ten Years Says he hath not since he quitted sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn 23d. Janry. 1746 before  Tho. Burdus

John Morley .
