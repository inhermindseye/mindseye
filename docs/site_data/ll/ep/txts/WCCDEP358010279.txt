Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Samuel Foster aged about 30 years upon his Oath saith that on the 15th. day of January 1787 he Intermarried with Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Philip at Birmingham and by whom he hath one Child living vizt. Mary Ann aged about Eighteen Months That he this Examinant was bound an Apprentice to a Mr. James Petty a Smith in Bear Yard Clare Markett for Seven years that after he had served Five years of his said Apprenticeship his said Master moved out of Bear Yard to Great Wild Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex where this Examinant served the remainder of his Apprenticeship and was discharged that since he has left his said Master he has not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of March 1789 R Butler D Walker

Samuel Foster
