Middlesex & Westmr. to wit Martha Edwards aged about Twenty one Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a Yearly Hired Servant with Nathaniel Davis at the Temple Coffee house in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Twelve Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Months ago & hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Christopher Burn a Waiter at Dobneys Gardens Islington is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House sometime in December last but the particular day she does not now remember only that it was on a Sunday and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that the said Christopher Burn and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father there of.

Sworn this 7th. day of July 1772 before} Tho Lane

her Martha Edwards mark
