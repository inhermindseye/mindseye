Middx  Elizabeth Kiddington aged about 53 Years upon Oath says She is a Widow without Child or Children and that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Upjon a Watchmaker by the Pond at Highgate in the Parish of St. Pancras in the County of Middlesex One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds & left her said Service about nine Months since

Sworn the 25th. Febry 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Elizth. Kiddington
