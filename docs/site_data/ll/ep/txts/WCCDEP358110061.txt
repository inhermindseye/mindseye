Middx  Ann Stevens aged abt. years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about twenty Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Gibbs a Dealer in Old Cloaths in Houghton Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one Year and upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds and has not since she quitted her sd Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn June 20th. 1752 before}
