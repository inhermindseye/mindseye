Middx  Susannah Fletcher aged Sixteen Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that by Indenture bearing Date the 5th. Day of Novr. 1739 she was bound an Apprentice to one Mrs. Frances Battoll of the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . Wido . by the Church Wardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & served her as an Apprentice for the Space of Three Months & upwds. in the sd. Parish of St. Martin in the Fields & then her sd. Mistress dyed that she hath not since the Death of her sd. Mistress rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes been assigned over to any Person whatever or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 12th. of Novr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Susannah Fletcher
