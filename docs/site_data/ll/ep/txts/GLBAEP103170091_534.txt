Middlesex ss}  Ann Hudson the Wife of Ansten Hudson maketh oath that her said husband is lately gone from her to places unknown to her That her said husband has informed her and Which She belives to be true That divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with Mr Herley in Little Moor Fields in the parish of Saint Stephen in Coleman Street in the City of London at Certain Wages & While a Singleman & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Three years & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her said husband did not afterwards rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath two children namely Amelia Aged Four years & four months & John Aged two years & one month the lawful Issue of this depont by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married.

Sworn this 15. day of Aug. 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Ann Hudson
