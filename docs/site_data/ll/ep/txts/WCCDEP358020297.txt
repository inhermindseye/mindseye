St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Mary Grace Aged 50 years and upwards Upon her Oath saith that about thirty years ago she was Married to her late Husband Thomas Grace at this parish Church that her said late Husband died about Sixteen years ago that her said Husband pre that previous to his said Marriage & while he was single not having Child or Children lived as an hired yearly Servant at the White Horse in Clements Inn Foregate in the said parish of St. Clement Danes at th for Fourteen Months at the yearly Yearl Wages (as her said late Husband hath Informed her and which She verily believes to be true of 5 Guas five Guineas that he quitted that Service about a Month before his said Marriage that she this Examinant hath now living a Female Child Named Elizabeth Aged about Nine years begotten by her said late Husband on her Body and born of the said Body of her this Examinant in Matrimony And that her said Husband did not in his life, time, acquire nor hath she this Examinant since his Decease gained any other legal settlemt. to the best of her Knowledge or belief And that she is Poor and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn the 4th June 1791 before me  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Grace
