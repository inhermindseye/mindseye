Middx  Susanh. Collins  Singlewoman aged abt. Twenty Four Years upon her Oath saith that She lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Tutelear a Peruke Maker in Bridges Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of Eighteen Months & upwards & hath left the same One Year & upwards that since she left her Sd. Service She hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 9th. Day of February 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Susa. Collins
