Middx.  Elizth. Brooks aged abt. 25 Yrs. upon Oath says she intermarried with her husband John Brooks at the fleet London on the 1st. day of Sepr. 1752. says her said husband has absconded from her and where he is this Examinant doth not know says her said husband served seven Years apprenticeship with Mr. Saml. Fountain in the Parish of Alhallows in the City of York  Comb maker and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year says she has three Children by her said husband Namely Elizth . aged abt. five Years Edward aged abt. Two Years and George aged abt. five Months

Sworn the 24th. of August 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Eliz. Brooks
