Middx.  Mary Hunt on the behalf of her Sister Sarah Hunt an Infant aged abt. ten Years upon Oath says that her father John Hunt served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with his father Thos. Hunt a Stocking maker in Goal Yard in White cross Street in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx. as her said father & never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Parish or place as this Deponent hath been informed and which information she verily believes to be true.

Sworn this 27th. of May 1758 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Hunt
