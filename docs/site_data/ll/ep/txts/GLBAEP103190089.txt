Middlesex ss}  Sarah Child  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mary Bradley at the Sign of the Thistle & Crown in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & upwards That She has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 2. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Sarah Child
