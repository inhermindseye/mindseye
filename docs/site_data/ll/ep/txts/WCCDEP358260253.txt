Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Eyles aged about 54 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months bound an Apprentice Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true That she was born at New Windsor in the Parish of Saint John the Baptist in the County of Middlesex where her father and Mother Rented a house for several years and paid Taxes And this Deponent further saith that she is now Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 13th. day of Febry. 1798 before us } N Bond Jas: Dyce

her Sarah Eyles mark
