( Sarah Prosser )

St. Clement Danes July 29th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary Chatham Kent  (to wit) Sarah Richards aged 34 years or thereabouts (on behalf of her illegitimate Child named Sarah Prosser aged five Years & upwards) Upon her voluntary Oath saith that she this Examint. was Married to Watkin Richards at the parish Church of Saint Philip in the City of Bristol about seventeen or eighteen years ago that about eighteen Months after & he went as a Soldier to America that she hath not heard of him for four about fourteen years past and us knows not what is become of him Saith that she this Examinant was delivered of the said Child named Sarah Prosser at the House of Mrs Mc Nichol in Holborn Lane in the parish Chapelry of St Mary in the Town of Chatham in the County of Kent that on or about the 31st day of March 1788 that one James Prosser by trade a Shoemaker is the only real true father of the said Child named Sarah Nichol and that he hath deceased Prosser and and that she this Examinant hath never been Married Got to the said Watkin Richards as aforesaid

Sworn the 29th day of July 1793 before us} N Bond John Collick

Sarah Richardss Mark Witness Garrett Removed by Mr Robertson Master of the Workhouse
