Middx  Anna Ford aged upwds. of 27 Years upon Oath saith she never was married And that she lived a Yearly Sevt . with Mr Maitland a Merchant for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Sixteen Pounds and lived the last two months of her sd. Service wth. the sd. Mr. Maitlands Family in Lothbury in London and that she has not since she quitted that Service(which is upwds. of three Years ago)Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief And that she was delivered of a male bastard Child on the 30th. Day of Janry 1746 at Mr Ross's opposite Arundel Strt . in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx which has been baptized William & is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish St Clemt. Danes this Examint. being poor and unable to maintain the same and that John Chard a Taylor did beget the said male bastard Child

on her Body the said John Chard having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the beginning of May 1746 and at several other times afterwards at one of which times he got her wth. Child of the sd. male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. and that the sd. John Chard and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn the 18th. March 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell W Fielding

Anna Ford
