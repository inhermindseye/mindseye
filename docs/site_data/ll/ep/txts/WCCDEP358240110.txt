St Clement Danes May 26th 1792.

Middx & Westminster }  St Andrew Wardrobe London  (to wit) Phoebe Dell Wife of Wm: Dell Upon he Oath saith That she is aged about 36 years That she was Married to her present Husband William Dell at the parish Church of Fulham in the County of Middlesex about Nineteen Years ago That she She hath been informed which she verily believes to be true That her said Husband Served his Apprenticeship to a Cabinet Maker in the Parish of St Andrew Wardrobe in the City of London That and & thereby obtained a legal Settlement in that parish And hath never since done any Act or thing whereby to acquire any Subsequent legal Settlement to her the best of her knowledge Information or belief And this Deponent saith That her said Husband left her about Eleven years ago, hath never since lived with her And refuses or neglects to make any Allowance or provision for her And further that She this Deponent (together with her legitimate Son by her said Husband Named William ) was removed from the parish of St Bridget otherwise St Bride London to the said parish of St Andrew Wardrobe about Six Years ago and was by that parish for near three Years allowance the Weekly Sum of 2/6 for the Maintenance of her sd. Child which was afterwards Apprenticed out by the said parish of St Andrew And this Deponent saith that She is

Distressed and unable to provide future herself without Assistance

Sworn the 26th day of May 1792 before us D Walker R. Butler

Phoebe Dell 's Mark

Witness B Newman
