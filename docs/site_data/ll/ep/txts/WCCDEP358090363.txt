Middx to Wit George Allwood Aged about 53 Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Jam Brad a Staymaker in Mankwell Street in the Parish of St. Olave Silver Street London and after he was one of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 11th. day of Jany. 1783 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

George Allwood
