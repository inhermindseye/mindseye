Middx  Wm. Giles Aged about Seventy Six Years upon Oath Says that whilst he was a Singleman and without Child or Children he Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with his Father Walter Giles in Princes Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx & One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds & never after Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year

Sworn the 10th of January 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Wm. Giles Wm Gyles
