Middx(to wit)  Mary Lewis Aged abt. Twenty Six Years Upon Oath Says She was never married and that she lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Francis Jones a farmer in the Parish of Yarpole in the County of Hereford for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Thirty Shillings and hath quitted her sd. Service abt. three Years and hath not since rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says She is pregnant with Child or Children wth is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx where She now Lodges She being poor & unable to maintain the same when Born Says George Smout late a Servt . to Mr. Smart in Grays Inn did beget the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her Body the sd. George Smout haveing had Carnall knowledge of this Examinant in the House of One Mr. Read in the Strand the latter end of the Month of March or begining of April last and at Sevll. other times since at One of wth times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with wch she is now pregnant and that the sd. George Smout & no other person is the True and real father thereof.

Sworn Decr. 3d. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Lewis
