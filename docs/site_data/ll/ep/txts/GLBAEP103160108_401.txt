Middlesex ss}  Mary Battersby the widow of William Battersby deced maketh oath That her Said late husband was by Birth an Irishman & never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement for himself in any place in England That She this Depont was born in Aberdeen in Scotland & never lived in England as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound Apprentice nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in England to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this. 25. of July 1776 before me  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Battersby
