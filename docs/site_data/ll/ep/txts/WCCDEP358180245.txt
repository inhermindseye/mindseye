Middx  Frances Woodall aged about 23 Years Removed from the Parish St Catherine Creechurch by Order Dated the 2d. Octr. last upon her Oath saith She is a Singlewoman & lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mrs. Rebecca Munts now Mrs. Wood in Sheir Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex 1 Year & upwards at the Yealy Wages of £3. Says in the latter Part of her sd. Service her sd. Mistress lent her to one Mr. Phelps a Corn chandler in East Smithfeild till such time as he could hire a Servant and this Exaiant lived with Mr. Phelps in East Smithfield upwards of 2 Months before he was Provided with a Servant & then came back to her former Service Says after she had been back about 3 Weeks her Mistress & she Disagreed & thereupon this Examinant left her Service since which she hath not Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born & that one Jonas Bearley late a Drawer at the 3 Tons Tavern near Aldgate did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body the sd. Jonas Bearley liveing fellow Servt. with this Examinant there became Acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of January last & at severall other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now proper & that he the sd. Jonas Bearley & no other person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworne 2d. Novr. 1744 before Wm. Jones Thos. D Neil

The Mark of Frances Woodall
