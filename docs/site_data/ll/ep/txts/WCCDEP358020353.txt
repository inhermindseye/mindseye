St. Clement Danes Novr. 12th. 1791

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Frances Rogers Aged 39 years or thereabouts upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Ralph Potts at No. 5 in Huggins Alley in the parish of St. John's Lachary in the City of London for about one three one year & three Months at the yearly Wages of five Pounds but this Examinant never received more than two pounds as her Master was Insolvent & much distressed, that she quitted her said Service in the Month of May last and since has not rented ten pounds a year paid any Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing to obtain any other legal settlement

Sworn the 12th Novr. 1791 before us N Bond Thos. Gordon

Frans Rogers 's Mark

(R Dobbins prest.)
