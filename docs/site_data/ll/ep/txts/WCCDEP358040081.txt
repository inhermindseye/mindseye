Westmr.  Eliza. Wilkinson aged upwards of 35 Years upon Oath saith she never was married Says she lived a a Yearly Servant with Mr. Olivier a Dancing Master in Seething Lane in the Parish of St. Allhallows Barking London at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for Four Years & upwards & left her sd. Service about 10 Yr. since Says she has not since she left the sd. Mr. Oliviers Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 14th. Octr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliza. Wilkinson
