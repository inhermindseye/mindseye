Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to St. C. D to wit James Bennett aged about 54 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Elizth. his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint James Bath in the City of Bath about 16 years ago and died about 12 Months ago and by whom he has two Children living Vizt. John aged about 20 years provided for Lydia aged about 15 years unprovided for Says that he never gained any Settlement in his own Right Says that his Father Rented a House in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 30 years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards hath quitted said House about 30 years ago since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have Gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 20th day of May 1785  Jno Barnfather

his James Bennett mark
