Westmr ss  Martha Thornton Aged about 30 Years on her Oath Says she never was Married, Rented House, or paid Taxes, says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Barnes at the sign of the Kings Arms the Corner of Sidneys Alley near Princes Street Leicester fields in the Parish of St Anns Soho in the Liberty of Westminster for fourteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath Quitted her said Service upwards of four Years ago & hath not since she Quitted the same ever rented house paid Taxes or done any other act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement, And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 10th day of June 1774 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Wych Alley Wych Street which has since been baptized by the Name of James . And further says that one James Lacey a Shoemaker who lately lived with this Examinant in Wych Alley aforesaid had Carnal Knowledge of her Body on the 21st day of August 1773 and has ever since Cohabited with him, and that the said James Lacey and no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 1st day of May 1775 before  R Butler M Addington

The Mark of Martha Thornton
