Middx to wit James Johnson aged about Sixty Six Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Mary his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint John in the Borrough of Southwark in the County of Surry about Twelve Years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children now living And this Examinant further says that he rented & lived in a House in Red Lyon Court White heart Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath Quitted the said about Thirty Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 11th. day of January 1779 before}
