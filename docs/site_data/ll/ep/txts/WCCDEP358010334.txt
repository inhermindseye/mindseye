Middlesex  Alexander Daniel aged 58 years or thereabouts upon his hath  Saith That he this Examinant Rented and lived in apartments in Eagle street in the saint of saint andrew Holborn above the Bars  in the County of Middlesex for upwards of 12 Months at the yearly rent of £10:10 - that he quitted the same upwards of 20 years ago since which time he this Examinant hath nor to the best of his knowledge information or belief gained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1790 before  Sampr Wright N Bond

Alexander Daniel
