Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr} to wit William Willows upon his Oath saith that he has been informed which information he believes to be true that Thomas Buck was married to Sarah his first Wife at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middx about twelve years ago that his said first Wife has been dead about Seven years That he has one Child living by his said first Wife Vizt. Thomas aged Eight years or thereabouts who has never gained any Settlement in his own Right That his said first Wife Sarah died about Seven years ago and this Examinant was further informed by the said Thomas Buck that he was married to Sarah  an his Second and present Wife at the said Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square about Seven years ago by whom he has no Child or Children living that his said Wife is now gone away from him and this Examinant does not know where she now lives and further this Examinant has been informed that whilst the said Thomas Bush was a Singleman unmarried and not having

Child or Children living he was hired to Sir  Gerrard Vanneck who then lived in Stretton Street Piccadilly in the Parish of St. Geo: Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex at the yearly wages of 18 Pounds for about Three Years that since he quitted the said Sir Gerrard Vannecks Service he has done no Act whereby he may have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Janry 1786 }

William Willows
