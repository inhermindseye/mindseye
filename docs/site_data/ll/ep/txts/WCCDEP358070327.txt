Middx  Catherine Middlemost aged about fifty Two years on behalf of Ann Amos otherwise Middlemost an Infant Aged about Twelve years on her Oath Says that the Father Robert Middlemost and Ann Amos the Mother of the said Infant are both run away and desered her And this Examinant further says that the said Infant Ann Amos is a Bastard by the said Robert Middlemost born on the Body of the said Ann Amos the Mother in the House of One Mr. Jones in Tothill Street in the Parish of Saint Margetts Westminster And further Says that the said Ann Amos otherwise Middlemost never was an Apprentice or lived a hired yearly Servant or otherwise gained any Settlement except where born.

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1775 before  David Walker R Butler

Katherine Middlemost
