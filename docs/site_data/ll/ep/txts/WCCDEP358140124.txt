Middx.  Ann Sobieski Lashley aged abt. 38 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Perry a Perukemaker in Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath quitted sd. Service abt. Eleven Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 3d. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Sobieski Lashley
