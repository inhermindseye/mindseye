Middx ss  Patience Knott  wife of John Knott of old Gravel Lane in the County of Middx maketh oath that John Geaney who is at sea in his Majesties service and is an Irishman formerly lived in Marsh street in the parish of St. Stephen in Bristoll where he had two Daughters born and Baptised in the same Parish to wit Eleanor aged about fourteen years and Elizabeth aged about Eight years the lawfull issue of the sd John Geaney by a former wife Deced And this Depont. hath not heard or been informed that she sd two Children never gained any other settlemt. than in right of their birth And that their sd Father never gained any settlement in England to the knowledge or belief of this Depont.

Sworn the 11th. day of March 1755 before  Chris: Scott

her Patience Knott Mark
