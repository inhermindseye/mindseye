Middx  Amaryllis Davis Wife of Henry Davis upon Oath Says she Intermarried with the said Henry in the Parish Church of St Gregory by St Pauls in the Year 1739 Says her said Husband Rented a House known by the Sign of the Crown by Smithfield Barrs in the Parish of St Sepulchers in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of twenty five Pounds lived in the same about half one Year and hath quitted the same upwards of one Year and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn Januy. 27th. 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

A Davies
