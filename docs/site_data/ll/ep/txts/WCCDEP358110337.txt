Middx.  Wm. Carter upon Oath saith he intermarried with his wife Elizabeth Carter at Upton Church in Gloucestershire about 24 years ago and that upwds. of 30 Years since being then a singleman he hired himself to one Willm. Newman a Carpenter in Carey Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of westmr. in the County of middlesex to live with him for three years certain at the yearly Wages of £5 Dyet Washing & Lodging and lived with the sd. Mr. Newman under that hiring & agreement for three Years or thereabouts in  Carey Street afsd. and this Examinant further maketh Oath that he has not (since he quitted Mr Newman as Service) rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Octr. 26th 1753 before}

The Mark of Wm. Carter
