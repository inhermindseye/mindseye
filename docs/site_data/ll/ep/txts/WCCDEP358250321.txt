Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Alderson aged about 52 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Alderson her late Husband who has been dead about Eight years at the Parish Church of Saint James Westmr . That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Forty Pounds and upwards and paid Taxes due for the same that her said late Husband died in the said house And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that since her said late Husbands Decease she hath not rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or any unfurnished Place at the Weekly rent of Four Shillings or upwards Paid Taxes lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1797 before me} J Hunter

Elizh: Alderson
