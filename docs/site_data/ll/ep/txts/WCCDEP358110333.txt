Westmr.  Mary Humphrys upon Oath saith she never was married & that she is the Daughter of Thos Humphrys by Mary his Wife that she never was an apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes and that her said father Thos. Humphrys  rented a house of £40 by the Year known by the Sign of the black Horse in little Wild Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx & lived therein upwds. of one year & never after he quitted the sd. house Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 20th 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Humphrys
