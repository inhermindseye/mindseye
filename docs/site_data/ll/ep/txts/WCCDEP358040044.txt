Westmr.  Sarah Williams  Widow of Robert Williams upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Rt. Williams at the Parish Church of Saint Ann within Aldersgate the 30th. Augt. 1736 Says her sd. late husband dyed upwards of Seven Years since Says her sd. late husband lived in and Rented a house in Witch Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Thirty pounds for three Years and upwards & left the same upwards of Eight Years ago Says her sd. late husband after he left said house or this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 5th. Augt. 1747 before Tho: Burdus

Sarah Williams
