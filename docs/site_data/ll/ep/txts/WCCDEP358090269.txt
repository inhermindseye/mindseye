Middx to wit Sarah Preston aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath says she never was married rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Blount Shoe- -warehouse in Bridge Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surrey upwards One Year at the Yearly Wages of £3. hath gained quitted her said Service about twelve Months since which time she hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Servant for One Year

Sworn this 5th. Day of Decemr: 1781 before  Jno: Machin  Jno. Barnfather

her Sarah Preston Mark
