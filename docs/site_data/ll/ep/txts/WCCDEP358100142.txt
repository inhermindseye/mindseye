Middlesex to wit Margarett Ounsworth aged about Fifty four years on her Oath Says she is the Daughter of Henry Hounsworth who was by Trade a Shoemaker and that her said Father served One years Apprenticeship to one Richd. Richardson of the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex as by an Indenture amongst her said Father Papers after the Decease of her Mother Frances Ounsworth may more fully Appear And further says that her said Father after serving his said Apprenticeship never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither did her said late Mother after the Decease of her said Father And lastly this Examinant says she never gained any Settlement either by being Married Served any Apprenticeship Renting House Tenement or Lodging or living a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 28th. day of October 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Margret Ownsworth
