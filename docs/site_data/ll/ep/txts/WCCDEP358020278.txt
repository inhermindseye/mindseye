St.. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Thomas Haynes Aged about fifty five years that or thereabouts on his Oath saith that he Married his late Wife  Ann Haynes at this parish Church about twenty years ago, that that he rented and lived in an House in the White Hart Yard in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the said Liberty and County about twelve years at the Annual Rent of twelve pounds and twelve Shillings and quitted the same about two years ago, that he hath one Infant Infant Child born of the Body his said late Wife Ann in lawful Matrimony unprovided for Vizt. John Aged about Eleven years, that he hath this Examinant since he quitted the said House in White Hart Yard hath not Rented ten pounds any House or Apartment of Ten pounds a year a Year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby he might have acquired any other legal settlement And that he is Poor and unable to Maintain himself and his said Son

Sworn before us the third May 1791  Joseph Faikney  Wm Hyde

Thos Haynes
