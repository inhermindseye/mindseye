Middx.  Eliz: Williams aged abt. 26 Years upon Oath says she never was Married or Ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year says her father Thos Williams deced Rented  a house in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £30 for fifteen Years or thereabouts and that her Mother Continued in & Rented the said house for five Years after her fathers death and then her Mother dyed

Sworn this 3d. Feby. 1759 before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Williams
