Middx  Jno. Chope aged about 62 Years upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Ann on the 5th. Day of July 1730 at the Fleet London Says he rented a house in New Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £14 for the Space of 3 Yrs. & upwds. and left same abt. 9 yr. Says he hath not since he left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 30th. Day of Novr. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

John Chope
