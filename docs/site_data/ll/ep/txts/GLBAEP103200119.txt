Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Catherine Murrall of the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the sd County the Seventeenth day of March in the year of our Lord 1784 -Who Saith that She is now with Child And that the sd Child is likely to be born a Bastard & to be chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate and that John Everett of the Same Parish Porter is the Father of the sd Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me R Davies

Catherin Murrell
