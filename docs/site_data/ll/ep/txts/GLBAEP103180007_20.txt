Middlesex ss}  Mary Redman the widow of John Redman deced maketh oath that her sd late husband has informed her and which She believes to be true that divers Years ago he was bound Apprentice to a Person who kept a Publick house the Corner of Wapping old Stairs in Wapping Street in the Parish of St John Wapping in the sd County & who Carried on the Business of a Waterman & Lighterman & Served three years & upwards of Such his Apprentice Ship there & then left his sd. Master & went to Sea where his Apprentice Ship ended That her sd late husband never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt Subsequent to his Apprenticeship to her Knowledge or belief That She hath a Daughter named Theodosia aged about Sixteen years the lawfull Issue of this Deponent By her Said husband who hath done no Act to gain a Settlemt for herself

Sworn this 8 day of January 1778 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Mary Redman
