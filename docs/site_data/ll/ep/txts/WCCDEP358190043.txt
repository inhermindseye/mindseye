Middx.  Mary Mills aged abt. 65 years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Richd. Mills of whose Settlement she can give no further Account than that he was born at Chipping Norton in the County of Oxford & that she has not since her said husbands death Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for One year

Sworn this 10th. day of Janry 1758 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Mills
