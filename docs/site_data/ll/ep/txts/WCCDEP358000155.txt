Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to St. Clements. to wit. Mary Tyler aged about 32 years an her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes says that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Hollier a Robe Maker in Holywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 3 years and upwards at the yearly wages of Twenty five Shillings hath quitted said Service about 15 years since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elshere

Sworn this 21st. day of Novr. 1785 before R Butler

her Mary Tyler mark
