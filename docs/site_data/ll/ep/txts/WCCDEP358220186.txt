Middx & Westmr.}  William Devald Aged about Sixty One Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Jane his New Wife at the Parish Church of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex about 15 Years Ago by whom he hath no Children now living Says that before his said Intermarriage and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with the late Major Barton in half Moon Street Piccadilly in the said Parish of Saint George Hanover Square for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds & afterwards married to his said Wife and Continued to live with his said Master for Seven Years longer and to the time of his sd Masters Death and after his said Masters Death he never rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 26th. day of Octr. 1770 before Us} Thos Kynaston

his William Devald Mark

Witness Will: Hughes
