Saint Clement Danes July 24th- 1786

Middlesex to wit Isabella Cain  Singlewoman aged twenty Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married rented any house or Tenement of ten pounds a Year Value or paid any Taxes whatever that she never was bound out an Apprentice, or served as an hired Yearly servant for one Year or did any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any settlement her own right-That she does not Know either where she was born or where the Settlement of either her father or Mother was Saith that her Sister is married to one Rudd a Shoemaker who lives in Queen Street White haven And further or her Voluntary Oath saith that on the twenty fifth Day of June last part she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which has since her Christened by the Name of Benjamin that Benjamin Pyett a Copper plate Engraver who Lodged at No. 10 in Staples Inn Buildings but whom this Examinant has not heard of the

Day of March last and no one else is the real & true for this of such Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon

Sworn this 2d Day of July August 1786 before} R Butler

Isabella Cain

Benjm Pyett in a Middle sigd Ma did Lodge at No. 10 Steples Inn Buildings -wears his own Hair Tyed in a Twist powderd in acquainted with the person was Keeps the Golden Bull in Hog Lane near Campton Street  St. Giles
