Middlesex ss  Matthew Tulip maketh oath That divers Years ago he rented a house in Anchor & Rope Alley in the Parish of St. George in the County of Middlesex and was Charged to & Paid Kings Taxes for the same and by Means thereof as he is advised he gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont. Saith that he hath not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year Rent nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a Child named Peter aged about Six Years the lawful Issue of this Depont by his late wife Deced.

Sworn this day of October 1770 before}
