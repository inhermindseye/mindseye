Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to Vide Grace Curters Exr. Most to wit Joseph Sandell one of the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and Saith that Phoebe Lewis a Pauper and Daughter of Christopher Lewis whose Examination was taken on the 3d. Day of January 1785 hath been since that time Inn the Workhouse of this Parish till Friday last And therefore has not gained any Settlement Subsequent to such Examination being taken

Sworn this 7th. day of Febry. 1785 before M Addington J Reading

J Sandell
