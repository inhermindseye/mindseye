Middlesex ss}  William West maketh oath that diviers years ago he lived in & rented a house of the yearly rent of Sixteen Pounds in upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St John Wapping in the sd County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Servd any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20 day of May 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of William West
