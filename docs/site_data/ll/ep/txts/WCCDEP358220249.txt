Westmr. to wit Henrietta Kitchen Aged about 26 Years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented house or Paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Scott a Victualler in Little Russell Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for fifteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th. day of March 1771 before us} R. Butler 

Henriatta Kitchen
