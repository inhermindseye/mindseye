Middlesex ss}  Robert Savory maketh oath that he has Cohabited with a Certain Woman named Frances Wood for many years past as being his Wife although in Truth he was never married to her That during Such Cohabitation he has had Several Children by her & in particular a Child now living with him aged about Nine years who has been Baptized & registred by the name of Ann Savory and is for the reasons before mentioned illegitimate and a Bastard and was born near Love Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry and has done no Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this. 22. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith  Parker Rowlands

Robert Savory
