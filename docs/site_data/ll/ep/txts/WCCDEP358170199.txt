Middx  Sarah Savage aged abt. Eighteen Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married that she never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year That her Father Geo: Savage was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Button in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Cooper that since his sd. Apprentisp her sd. Father hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as she hath been informed & beleives & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when Corn & that one Mr. Wind a Carnett in Generall Churchill Regimt. of Dragoons now at York whose Christian Name this Exaiant. beleives is Robert did begett the sd. Child on her Body she at such Time liveing a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Ravenhill Peruke Maker in Tavistock Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westrmr . & he the sd. Mr. Robt. Wind lodging in the same House there became acquainted with her & had carnall knowledge of her Body Severall Times in the Month of May last at one of which Times he gott her with the sd. Bastard Child with which she is now Pregnant

and that he the sd. Robt. Wind & no other Person is the True & Reall Father thereof

Sworne the 12th. Day of Janry. 1741 before} Tho Lane

Sarah Savage
