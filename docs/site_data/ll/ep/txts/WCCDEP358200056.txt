Middx and Westmr}  Mary Asford Aged about 19 Years Upon Oath Says she was never Married was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servant Says her Father James Asford Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Gardener a Waterman in the Parish of Wandsworth in the County of Surry and hath been out of his said Apprentiship about Twenty Six Years and never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the parish of St. Clement Danes she

being poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Thomas Post a Postillion had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body about the Latter End of May Last and at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 25th Day of January 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

Mary Ashford

Upon Mr Jones Complt. Ovrs. J. W
