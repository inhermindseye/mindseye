Middx  Eliz. Hatwhay  Wido . of Anthony Hatwhay upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Anthy. Hatwhay at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Feilds on the 20th Day of Febry abt. 22 Years since Says he hath been dead one Yr. & upwds. Says her sd. late Husbd. lived a Yearly Servt . with Captn . Martin Laycock in Beanfort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. at the Yearly Wages of £10 or thereabouts for the Space of 2 Yrs. before his intermarriage with this Exaiant & after his sd. Intermarriage continued to live with the sd. Captn. Laycock under the same hiring & Agreemt. for the Space of 8 Yrs. or thereabts. Says her sd. late Husbd. after he left his sd. Service never rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she hath not since his Death Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived

a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 5 1746 before  Wm. Jones

Elizabeth Hatwhay
