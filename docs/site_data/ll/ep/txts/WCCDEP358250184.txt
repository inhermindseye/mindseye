Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Trivvian aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Robinson in Plymouth Dock Yard for about Three years at the yearly wages of Four Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Eight years since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she hath Two illegitimate Children living Vizt. Elizabeth born at the house of a Miss Munday No. 12 Newton Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex on the 3d. day of January 1793 and Marg John born on the 9th: day of May 1794 at the house of a Mr. Smith in Houghton Hollis Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 7th. day of July 1795 } Rupt Clarke N Bond

her Hannah Trivvian Mark

Witness B Newman
