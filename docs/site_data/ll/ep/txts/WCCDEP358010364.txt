St. Clement Danes.

Middlesex & Westmr. to wit.}  James Dodd aged 43 or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he Intermarried with Eliza Amy his present wife at the Parish Church of Holywell in the City of Oxford , about 20 years ago, That he  was put Apprentice to James Arnold of  the Parish of St Marys the Oigin  Peruke Maker , in the said City of Oxford That he served his Time there - And their Examinant Saith That he Entered into an Joint concern in business with James Patterson in Robin Hood Court Butcher Row that they took a House in conjunction together at the yearly Rent of 20t. in Robin Hood Court , and that he lived in the same Nine or Ten Months And Saith that he never since has done any Act Matter or Thing. whereby he may to the best of his knowledge have since gained a Settlement And Saith he hath one Child by the said Eliza Amy Named. James who is about 18 years of Ago.

Sworn this 25. Octr. 1790 before.  Wm Kitchner

James Dod
