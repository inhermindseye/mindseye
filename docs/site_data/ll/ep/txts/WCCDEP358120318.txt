Middx  Mathw. Warwick upon Oath Saith he Served Seven years apprenticeship with Christ Painter a Butcher at Stony Stratford in the County of Bucks and never rented ten Pounds by the year pd. Parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Served for one year

Sworn April 1st 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mathw. Warwick
