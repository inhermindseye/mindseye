Middlesex ss}  John Anthony maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house of Six Pounds a year in Brown Bear Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That he this Deponent has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15. day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of John Anthony
