Middlesex ss}  Dorothy Chrishole a Singlewoman maketh oath that She lately was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Yearly wages with Mr Blake an Attorney at Law in the Parish of Sunderland in the Bishoprick of Durham and Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and that She hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and this Depont. Saith That on the Twenty eighth day of January last in the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middlesex She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath been Since Baptized & Registred by the name of Dorothy Chrishole and is Since become chargeable to the Said Parish and that William Tenant now or late of Sunderland aforesd. Labourer did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Sworn this 7th day of 1771 before  Cha: Digby

Dorothy Chrishole Her Mark
