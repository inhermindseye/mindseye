Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mark Houldins aged about 41 years upon his Oath saith that about 17 years ago he Intermarried with Elizth. his late Wife (who has been dead about Eight years) at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch and by whom he hath one Child living Vizt. Charlotte aged About 13 years that he Rented and lived in a House in Winchester Square in the Parish of Saint Mary Overys in Southwark for 6 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £14 that he hath quitted said House about 13 years ago since which time he has not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. April 1788 . R Aruvind J Girdler

his Mark Houldins Mark
