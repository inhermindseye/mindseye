Middx to Wit Susannah Leister Aged about 40 Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Richd. Leister with whom sh Intermarried at the Parish of St. Michael Basinghall alias Bassishaw London about 15 years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says  said late Husband before his Intermarr with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Thos: Palmer a Broker in Broad Street in the Parish of St. Bene Vulgarly St. Bennett Fink London upwards of one year at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds or upwards and after quitting his said service he never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement neeth hath this Examinant since her sd. Husbands Death other than by living as a hired yearly Servant with one Ede Carty a Lodger at Mr. Townsends a Packer in Broad Street in the afsd. Parish of St. Benedict for 13 Months at the Yearly Wages of 12 Guineas hath quitted her said Service about 14 Months ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 25th. day of Octr. 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Susannah Leister  Mark
