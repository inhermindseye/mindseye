Westmr.  Digby Trim aged about 35 Years upon Oath saith he intermarried with his late Wife  Susannah at the Tabernacle belonging to the Parish of Shoreditch near Ten Years since Says his sd. Wife died on the 3d. Day of Febry. Instant & that he hath two Children by her(now living) viz: Eliza. aged about Six Years & Mary aged about two Years Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served the first Six Years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Abraham Phillips in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Taylor and that at the End of Six Years he absconded from his sd. Masters Service & never was assigned over to any other Person lived forty Days at any place with his masters knowledge or Consent rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 10th. 1747 before}
