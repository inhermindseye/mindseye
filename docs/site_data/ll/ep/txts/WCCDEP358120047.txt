Middx  Ann Pearce aged abt. 47 yrs. upon Oath Saith she is the Wido: of Danl. Pearce who dyed abt. 10 years ago & that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband was born at Westchester in the County of Chester & she can give no further Account of his Settlement & that since the Decease of her sd. husband she has not rented Ten Pounds by the year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Janry 24th. 1754 -before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Pearce
