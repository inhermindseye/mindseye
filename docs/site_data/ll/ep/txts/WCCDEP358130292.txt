Middx  Rose Brown aged abt. 42 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Benjn. Brown (now a broad) says the said Benjn. Brown served his Apprenticeship with Mr. Wm. Towzey a Tallow Chandler & Grocer in the market place in the Parish of Wantage in the County of Berks

Sworn the 2d. of March 1757 : before} Fran: Bedwell
