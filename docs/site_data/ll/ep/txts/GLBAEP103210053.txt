Middlesex ss}  Jane Langley  Single woman maketh oath that She was heretofore duly bound apprentice by Indre for the Term of Seven years to Mr Hillyard a Tambour work maker of the Hamlet of Mile End old Town in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the sd County & Served the whole of her Apprenticeship there That she has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 22d. day of January 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Jane Langley
