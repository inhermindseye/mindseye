Middx to wit}  Betty Croker Aged about Thirty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her late Father William Croker Rented and lived in a House known by the Name or Sign of the Crown in Tweezers Alley Water Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same and that since her said Fathers Death she hath never gained any or Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Children Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to said Parish of Saint Clement Danes (she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) And further says that one Joseph Hannah otherwise Hannam who lately Kept the George and Ball in Orchard Street Westmr and who now Lodges at Mr. Bagsters the Checquers at Charing Cross is the Father thereof he has had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime the latter of December last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at one other time afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as aforesaid and that the said Joseph Hanna otherwise Hannam and no other person did be get the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1775 before me  Tho Lane

her Betty Croker Mark
