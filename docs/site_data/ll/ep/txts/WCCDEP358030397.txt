Westmr. 

Geo: Bason upon Oath saith he lived in & rented a house in New Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £15 for Seven Years or thereabouts & left same abt: 7 or 8 Years since Says he has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year in any other Parish paid Parish Taxes  or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif Says he has a Son named Charles aged about 14 Years

Sworn 24th. June 1747 before  Wm: Jones

George Bason
