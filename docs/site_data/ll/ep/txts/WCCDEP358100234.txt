Middx to wit Ann Jones Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Perry a Baker in Feller Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn  London upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement and further on her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 14th. day of July Instant she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Kates a Taylor in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Frederick & is likely to became Chargable to the sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes and further that one Frederick Hood a Jouneyman Baker who lately lived at her said Masters in Fetter Lane had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her said Masters House sometime the beginning of October last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Fredrick Hood and no other person did beg the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of July 1784 before  Wm Kitchiner

Ann Jones
