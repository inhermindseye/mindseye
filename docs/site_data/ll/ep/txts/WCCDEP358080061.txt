Middx & Westmr}  Thos. Mogeridge Aged about fifty Six Years on behalf of Ann Mogeridge his Daur an Infant aged about Twelve Years on his Oath Says that the sd Ann Mogeridge his Daughter was bound out an Apprentice by the Church Wardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middx to one James Clarke a Sawyer in Goose Alley Fleet Markett in the Parish of St. Sepulchre London and lived with her said Master under that Indenture for the Space of Nine Months when her said Master removed to Goose Ally aforesaid and lived with him there under the same Indenture for upwards of One Year & Three Months till her said Master used his said Daugher ill and was obliged to leave her said Master as this Examinant has been Informed and Verily believes the same to be true

Sworn this 17th. day of June 1776 before  Wm Kitchiner  John Croft

Thomas Mugerridge
