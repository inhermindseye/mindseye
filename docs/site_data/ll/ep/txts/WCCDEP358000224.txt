Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) William Powell aged twenty five Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he never was married that he was hired by one John Thurrock  Purser of the Camel of 20 Guns a Ship in the English Service at Little Yorks in Quarries to serve him as his Servant  at 30-% a Year Cloaths and Board that he continued Servant to the said Mr. Thurrock under that Agreement for about three Years sometimes on these Board at others on shore in the different Ports the Ship was stationed at that he came to England in the said Mr Thurrocks Services and lived him for about 8 Mrs. at the Black Bear in Piccadilly when he quitted him, and since quitting him which was above Nine Months ago, has not lived as an hired yearly Servant for 12 Months with any other Person or done any Act whereby to the best of his Knowledge he may have

gained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th Feby 1786 before  Sampr Wright  Thos. Gordon

his William Powell Mark
