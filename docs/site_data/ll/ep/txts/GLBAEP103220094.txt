Middlesex ss}  Janett Goodall the widow of Alexander Goodall deced maketh oath That her Said late husband & she this Deponent were both born in East Lothian in Scotland And that her Said husband nor She this Deponent to her Knowledge or belief never did Any Act to gain a settlement in England.

Sworn this. 29. day of Novemr 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Janett Goodall
