Westmr.  Susannah Williams aged about 16 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & never was an apprentice or yearly Servant for one Year and that she has been informed & verily believes her late father Wm. Williams deceased rented Served his apprentiship with Mr. Wood a Bricklayer in May pole Alley in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of westmr. & never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Octr. 25th 1753 before
