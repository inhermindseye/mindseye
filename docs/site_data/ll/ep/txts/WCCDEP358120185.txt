Middx  Jno. Rutter on behalf of James Timms a Lunatic upon his Oath saith he has been Informed & verily believes the sd. James Timms Served the last 2 Yrs. of his Apprentiship wth. Jno. Clarkson a Peruke Maker in little Britain in the parish of St. Barthw. the lass London

Sworn Augt. 24th 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell John Rutter
