St. Clement Danes 12th. Novr. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James Westmr }  One Child  (to wit) Alice Pinch  Widow aged 30 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to James Pinch her late Husband by Banns at the parish Church of St Ann Westmr . on or about the 24th of August 1783 that her said Husband (with this Examinant) Rented and Resided in a House No 16 King Street in the parish of St James Westminster for a year at or thereabouts at the Annual Rent of Sixteen pounds that they he quitted the same on or about the 21st. day of April last 1789 and Died on or about the first of August after without having done Act whereby to obtain any legal Settlement otherwise than in the said parish of St James to best of her knowledge information or belief And this Examinant farther saith that she hath not been since her Death been Married or obt obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right that she hath one Child by her said Husband now with her Named Alice born on or about the eighth day of May 1785 on who she is unable to support without assistance

Sworn the 12th day of November 1793 before us N Bond B: Kennett

Alice Pinch 's Mark

Witness Garrett
