St. Clement Danes  22d Decr 1792

Middx & Westminster }  St James Westmr (to wit) Jonathan Woods aged 18 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that  he served for about the space of one year as an Apprentice under Indentures of Apprenticeship with Mr Knolt a Peruke Maker or Hair Dresser that he lived with his said Master during the last 3 Months of his service in Shernard Street in the parish of St James in the Liberty of Westminster  in the County of Middlesex and that his Master then left him with & giving up his Indentures or assigning him ever that since such service he hath never lived as  an hired yearly Servant or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement

Sworn the 23d. day of January 1793 before us} Wm. Kitchner D Walker

Jonathan Woods Mark

Witness R Garrett

Removed by Dalbins, Beadle 23d Janry. 1793
