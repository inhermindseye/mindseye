Middlesex ss}  Ann Jackson the wife of Joseph Jackson maketh oath that her Said husband is now living with his Father in Queen Street in the Town of Manchester in Lancashire that he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to one George Jackson of Coleman Street London Hatter & was afterwards by him duly turned over to Richard Ashton of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Hatter With Whom he served the remainder of his Apprenticeship & has not Since to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn this 30. day of October 1784 before R Davies Ann Jackson
