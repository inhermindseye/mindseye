Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Martin aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that about 10 years ago she Intermarried with Edwd. Martin her present Husband at the  Parish Church of Saint Swithins in Cannon Street that she hath Two Children by her said Husband vizt. Jane aged about 7 years and Mary aged about 5 years that her said Husband has Rented Two Parlour of one John Williams in Stone Cutter Court Crutched Fryard in the Parish of Saint Olave Hart Street in the City of London ever since the Month of June 1786 for which he pays the yearly Rent of £10 that he went away from this Ext. last Thursday and is at present to the best of this Exts. Knowledge rather Disordered in his mind

Sworn this 6th. day of Octr. 1787 }

Mary Martin
