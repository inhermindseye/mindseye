Middx.  Sarah Stairs aged abt. 46 Years upon Oath says She never was Married Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Sr. Edwd. Hulse in Norfolk Street on ye. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & a half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & hath quitted sd. Service abt. Sixteen Years since which time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Yr

Sworn Janry 8th 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Stairs
