Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr ss  Elizabeth Sharon  Widow aged Sixty Seven Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Gray in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, that she quitted her said Place about five Years ago and since then her not lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to the best of her Knowledge Information and belief And this Examinant further saith that at the time she was hired to Mr Gray & during the Whole of her Service with him she was a Widow and had no Child or Children living

Sworn this 12th Day of January 1791 before me R Butler}

E Sharon
