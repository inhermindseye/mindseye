Middx  Jane Gascoigne aged about 75 Years Removed by Order dated the 20th. day of June 1761 from the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields upon Oath saith she is the Widow of William Gascoigne who has been Dead about 30 Years to whom she was Married at Brindle in Lancashire upwards of 50 Years ago That he her said Husband was before his intermarriage and while Single a hired Yearly Servant to Mr. Graham near Cecil Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of about three Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Quitted the Same at the time of his Intermarriage with this Examt. That he never kept House rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes nor hath she this Examinant or been a hired Yearly Servant since his Death.

Sworn the day of June 1761 Before}
