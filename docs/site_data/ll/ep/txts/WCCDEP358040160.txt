Middx  Mary Robinson  Wido . of Edwd. Robinson upon Oath saith she intermarried with her said late husband in the Parish Church of Saint Lawrance Old Jewry London upwds. of 30 Years ago Says her sd. late husband died in August last and that her Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. John Sedley  Watchmaker in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has not since the Death of her sd. late husband rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place

Sworn March 5th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Robinson
