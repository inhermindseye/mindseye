Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit George Quin aged about 40 years upon his oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bean in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Fourteen years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Augt. 1798 before me } W Carter

his George Quin Mark
