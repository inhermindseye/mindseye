Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizth. Thorpe aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Thorpe her late Husband about Thirty Seven years ago at the Parish Church of Saint George in the County of Surry that he hath been dead about Fifteen years That her said late Husband informed this Examinant that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Henly at Ratcliffe Cross in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex for Two years and an half that after he quitted said Service he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby s he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of July 1798 before us R. Ford W Addington

her Elizth. Thorpe mark
