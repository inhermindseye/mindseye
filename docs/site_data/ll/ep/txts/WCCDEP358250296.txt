Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Baker aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hunt in Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for seven years at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Seven Months since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she hath One Child living by the said Mr. Hunt who is now dead named George aged about Seven years born at the house of the said Mr. Hunt in Shire Lane aforesaid

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1797 before me  Gs Goodenough

Sarah Baker
