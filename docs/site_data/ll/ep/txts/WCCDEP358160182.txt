Middx  The Examination of Sarah Batcheller on the Behalfe of Sarah Leveredge  Widdow aged Eighty- four Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the sixteenth Day of Octr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that the sd. Sarah Leveridge abt. twelve Years ago to the Best of her remembrance as to the Time rented a House in Lambeth Mash in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in ye. County of Surry of the yearly Rent of three Pound & paid to the Poor of the sd. Parish & hath not since she left the sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & the sd. Sarah Leveridge being Poor & Infirm is not able to provide for herselfe.

Sworn the day and Year abovementioned before} Jas: Gronous  Wm Hayton

The mark of Sarah Batchellor
