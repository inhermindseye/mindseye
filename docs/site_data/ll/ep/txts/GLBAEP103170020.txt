Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Gray the widow of John Gray  mariner deced maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband nor of his Parents or either of them That divers years ago & previous to her Marriage with her Said husband She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Gray in the parish of Trinity in the Minories in the County of Middlesex oilman & Cheesemongr . & Continued there in Such Service for the Space of three years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Save What She has Acquired by her Marriage with her Sd husband Whose Settlement is unknown to her That She was lawfully married to her Said husband whose Settlement is in the parish church of St George in the Sd County & hath a Child named Sarah Aged about Ten Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband

Sworn this. 25 day of October 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands W Blackmore

Elizabeth Gray
