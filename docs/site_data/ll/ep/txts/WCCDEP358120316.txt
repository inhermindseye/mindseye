Middx  Edwd. Patty aged abt. 66 Yrs. upon Oath Saith he was married to his wife Mary at the parish Church of St. Botolph Bishopgate Londn. abt. 38 Yrs. ago by whom he has a Daughter (viz) Eliz : aged abt. 19 Yrs. who never was an apprentice or yearly Servant for one year and that he (this Examint.) Served 7 Years apprentiship wth. Jno: Dry a Sawyer in Houghta Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the said County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn March 28th 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

Edward Paty
