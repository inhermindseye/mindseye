Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizabeth Foot who is Came Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that she lived as a hired yearly servant with one Mrs. Jones for Two Years and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that the last Seven weeks of her said Service was at the Corner of Har Court in Chancery Lane in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London and that after she quitted said Service she never did any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of Decr. 1796 } M Addington J Robinson B Newman
