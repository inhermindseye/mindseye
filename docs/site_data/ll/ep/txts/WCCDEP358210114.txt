Westmr.  Thos. Darley aged about 26 Years Upon Oath says he never was Married and that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Farrer, Fish monger near Templebar in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds quitted said Service about Six Months and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 6th, of Decr. 1766 , before}

Thos Darley
