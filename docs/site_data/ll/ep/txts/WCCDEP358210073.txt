Westmr.  Dorothy Denevett aged about 28 Years upon Oath says she never was Married nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year; says she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true that her Father John Denevett lived in and Rented a House in Fleet Market (known by the Sign of the Harrow) in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn London of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and the died.

Sworn the 5th. day of Septr. 1766 , before} Thos. Kynaston 

The Mark of D Dorothy Denevett
