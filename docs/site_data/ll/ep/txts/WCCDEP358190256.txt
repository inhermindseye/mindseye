Middx  Cath: Smith Aged about thirty One Years now Lodging at Nurse Pools in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx Upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Childrn: which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to be come Chargeable to the sd: Parish She being Poor & unable to maintain the same when Born Says one Saml Roberts in Kings Head Court in Drury lane in the sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes  Perukemaker did beget the sd. Child or Childn: on her Body they living together as Man & Wife he the Sd. Saml Roberts had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of Septembr: Last & at severall other times at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children she is now pregnant with.

Sworn July 19th: 1762 before}
