Middlesex  William Scroop maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by the Governors & Guardians of the hospital for the Maintenance and Education of exposed & deserted Young Children to Paul Bawrie of half Nichol Street in the Parish of St Matthew Bethnal Green in the sd County Weaver (he being then nine Years old) untill he Shod arrive to his Age of twenty four Years That he served his Sd Master there for about the Space of ten years When he left his Sd Master Without his Consent & went into the Sea Service & Continued therein for about four Years & then returned to his Sd Master & by Agreement between this Deponent & his Said Master & in Consn of Eight Guineas paid by him to his Said Master his Said Master discharged him from his Said Apprenticeship and the Indentures were exchanged & delivered up That he this Deponent never rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Apprenticesp That he hath a wife named Elizabeth & a Child Named William Aged about Seventeen Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Sd wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 30. day of Novemr 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of William Scroop
