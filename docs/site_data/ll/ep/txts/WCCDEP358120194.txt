Middx  Martha Higgens aged near 23 Years upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she never was married & that on the 23d. Day of August last she was delivered of a male Stilborn bastard Child at Nurse Davisons in old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Charge of the Inhabitants of the said Parish and that James Boots a watchman of Mr Snow the Banker did beget the said Male Stilborn bastard Child on her body the sd. James Boots having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant twice in the Months of November & January last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Male Stilborn bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said James Boots and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn the 12th. Septr. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Martha Higgens
