Westmr. to wit William Bradshaw Aged about Seventy four Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Christiana his late Wife at the Parish Church of St. Anns Aldersgate about forty Seven Years ago and who has been Dead about four Years Says he Rented and lived in a House in Carpenters Court in the Parish of Saint Mary Aldermanbury upwards of Six Years at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds and Paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted his said House about Thirty four Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same Ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 9th. day of March 1771 before Us R. Butler W Fielding

Willm Bradshaw
