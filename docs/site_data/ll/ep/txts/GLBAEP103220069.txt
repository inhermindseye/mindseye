Middlesex ss}  William Smith maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house at the Yearly rent of Five Pounds in Angel Court in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & during Such his residence therein he was Charged to and Paid Kings Taxes for the Same And has not Since done Any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this. 14. day of Septemr 1786 before R Davies

Wm Smith
