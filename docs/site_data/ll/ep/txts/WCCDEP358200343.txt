Middx.  George Wood aged upwards of 30 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife in the Parish Church of Enfield in the County of Middlesex about Ten Years ago, by whom he hath four Children (to wit) Charlotte aged about Nine Years, Emanuel aged about Seven Years George Alrington aged about four Years and Alrington aged about two Years, says he was bound an Apprentice for Eight Years to William Stubbs of the Parish of Hexam in the County of Northumberland  Shoemaker and served about five Years the first part of his said Apprenticeship and then his said Master died and this Examinant was Assigned over to William Robson of the said Parish of Hexam Shoemaker and served him two Years and upwards and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 3d. day of January 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

George Wood
