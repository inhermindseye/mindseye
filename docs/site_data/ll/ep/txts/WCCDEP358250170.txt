Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Pittiford aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That her father Rented and lived in a house several years in the Abbey Parish St. Albans in the County of Herts and paid all Taxes due for the same and this Deponent Saith that she never did any Act in her own Right to gain a Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of May 1795  J Hlund M Addington

her Mary Pittiford Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
