Middx  Elizth. Woodford Aged upwards of Seventy Years Upon Oath Says She lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Gardiner a Butcher in Claremarkett in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Ten Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds and hath Quitted her sd. Service abt. five Years & hath not since Rented a House or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year Says before She went to live with the sd. Mr. Gardiner She had two Children who had both gained Settlemt. in their own Right

Sworn the 10th. Day of Novr. 1762 before}

The mark of Elizth. Woodford
