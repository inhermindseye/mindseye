Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  Sarah Ward aged 27 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was Married to Arthur Ward her now Husband who is now in Newgate under Sentence of Transportation at the Parish Church of Saint Giles Cripplegate above four years ago by Limm that her said Husband has informed this Examinant that his Parish in Saint Giles Cripplegate aforesaid and that he is legally setled then by Virtue of his father having served an Apprenticeship to Malson or Cabinet maker at Barbican in the said Parish and this Examinant believes her said Husband has never gained any other Settlement Saith that she has one Child now living by her said Husband namely Ann Ward aged 15 Months and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself and said Child

Sworn this 25th July 1788 before us}
