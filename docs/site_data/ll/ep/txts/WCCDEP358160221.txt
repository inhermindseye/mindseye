Middx  The Examination of David Henderson now in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 24th- Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Jno- Carr  Taylor in Eagle Court in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the same County for the Space of 2 Years at the Yearly Wages of £2:10s & further saith yt. since yt. time he never rented £10 a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath a Wife named Elizth - & Child named Isaac & being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Cha: Egerton

David Henderson
