Middx  Susannah Mercer aged about 41 Years Lodging at the Crooked Billet in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of George Mercer who has been Dead about a fortnight to whom she was Married at St. Andrew's Church in Holborn about 24 Years ago That he served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Thomas Mercer a Taylor in the Parish of St. Peter in the Town of Liverpool That he never kept House paid parochial Taxes or was a hired Yearly Servant to this Examinant's Knowledge

Sworn the day of June 1761 Before}
