Middx ss  Anne Jones  Widow Maketh oath That she knew Thomas Leviston Deced And Saith that the said Thomas Leviston about Thirteen Years ago was bound apprentice to one James Brinstone a Weaver who then lived in the Liberty of Usburn in the parish of Allhallows in the County of Northumberland and lived with his said Master for the Space of Two Years & upwards & then went to Sea . By Means whereof of which apprenticeship the said Thomas Leviston (as this Depont. is advised & believes) gained a legal Settlemt. in the said Liberty of Usburn in the said parish of Allhallows

Sworn this 15th day of Day of August 1750 before me  Richd: Ricards  Saml Gower

The Mark of Anne Jones
