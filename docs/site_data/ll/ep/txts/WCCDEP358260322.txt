Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Margaret Connelly aged about 47 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Dawson the Corner of Strand Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Twenty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Augt. 1798 before me } Wm Hyde

her Margaret Connelly Mark

Witness H Day
