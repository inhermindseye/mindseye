Middx & Westmr-}  Sarah Edwards aged about twenty eight Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she was bound out an Apprentice by the Churchwdns- and Overseers of the Psh of St- Sepulchre London to one Mrs- West a Furrier in Half Moon Alley Bakers Court in the parish of Saint Botolph Bishopgate without London and served her said Mistress about four Years and then this Examinant run away since which time this Examinant hath gained no other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 24th- Day of Febry 1778 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Sarah Edwards Mark
