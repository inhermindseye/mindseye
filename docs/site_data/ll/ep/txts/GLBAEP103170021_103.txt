Middlesex ss}  Mary Stevens  Singlewoman maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Father deced nor of the Settlement of her late Mother Save that She has been informed & believes that her Mother was born in the parish of Eltham in the County of Kent in Which parish She this Deponent was also born That She this Deponent never was bound as Apprentice nor never lived in any one Service or place as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 31st. day of October 1776 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Stevens
