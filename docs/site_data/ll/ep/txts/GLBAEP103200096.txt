Middlesex ss}  Sarah Danby  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father Nicholas Danby deced as She has been informed & believes by him heretofore rented a house known by the Sign of the George in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same & did not afterwards do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement elsewhere That She this Depont never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever was bound an Apprentice nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 11th day of February 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Danby
