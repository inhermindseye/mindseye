Middx  Elizabeth Jones aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Peter Jones a Soldier at Belisle to whom she was Married at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes the 15th. day of June 1756 That her said Husband was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Sutton Staymaker in May pole Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and with his said Master there Served all his time out which Expired upwards of Seven Years ago, Since which he hath not kept House paid Parish Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant That she has One Child by her said Husband to wit Thomas aged Nine Weeks

Sworn the 27th. day of July 1761 : Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Jones
