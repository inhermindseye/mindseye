Middx  The Examination of Mary the Wife of Jno. Twentyman Passed from the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . by Order bearing date the 21st. Day of March last Taken the 5th. Day of April 1740 Who upon her Oath saith That on the-Day of Octr. 1711 She this Examint. Intermarryed with the above Named Jno. Twentyman And yt. her sd. Husband lived in & rented a House in little Drury Lane in the said Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £20 for the space of 4 Years And further saith that her sd. Husband after yt. Rented part of Craven House of one Mr. Jones in wch. Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £32 & lived in the same for the space of one Year & upwds. that the sd. Mr. Jones lives in an Apartmt. in the said House & the remaindr. letts in Lodgings and is rated & pays the Taxes due for the whole House And further saith yt. there is a Door in part of Mr. Jones's Apartmt. wch Opens into the Appartment this Examints. said Husband rented wch sd. Door Mr. Jones opened almost every Day & he & his Servts. used to go through this Examints. Apartmt. wn ever they pleased & coud wn this Examints. & her Family were abed open the same Door & go up & down their Stairs if he or his Servts. thought proper so to do and ye. Water & Privy were for the use of the sd. Mr. Jones and his Tennts. in Comon And this Examint. further upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband hath not since he left the sd. Apartmt. rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That he now Absconds from her & she being Poor is notable to maintaine herselfe

Sworn ye. Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Mary Twentyman
