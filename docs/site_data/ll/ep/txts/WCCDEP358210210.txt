Westmr.  Martha Richbell aged about 65 yrs. Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Richd, Richbell who has been dead about Ten Years, says her said Husband Rented a house in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for two Years or thereabouts of the yearly Rent of £32, and after he quitted said House never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes; says she hath not since her said Husbands Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 24th. day of November 1767 , before} 

The Mark of Martha Richbell
