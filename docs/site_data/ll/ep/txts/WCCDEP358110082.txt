Middx  Sarah Wright aged 34 Years upon Oath saith that she never was married and that about the Day of four Years ago she was delivered of a female bastd. Child in the house of Mr Wright (this Examints. father) in Star Court in the Butcher Row in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx which was baptized Mary & is still living & now Chargeable to the sd. parish and that James Gritton a Staymaker did beget the sd. female bastard Child on her body the sd James Gritton having for a Year (or thereabouts) proceding the Birth of the sd. Child cohabited with this Examinant as her husband during which time he had carnal knowledge of her body sevl. times, at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd female bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said James Gritton & no man else is the true & real father thereof and further upon her Oath saith that on the 23d. of March 1749/50 she was delivered of a male bastard Child, since baptized John James at the sd Mr Wrights house in Star Court aforesd. which is likewise living & Chargeable to the sd Parish and that the sd. James Gritton did beget the said male bastard Child on her body, they still Cohabiting together as man & wife & hat the sd. James Gritton & no other person is the true and real father thereof

and this Examinant further maketh Oath that the said James Gritton about three weeks since went from her and she cannot give any further information concerning him except that she has heard he is at present in Cornwall

Sworn Augt. 15th. 1752 before Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Sarah Wright
