Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Robert Kerr aged about 47 years upon his Oath saith that about Twenty years ago he Intermarried with Sarah his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bone by whom he hath Two Children living Robert aged about 15 years and Mark aged about 14 years That he this Deponent previous to his Intermarriage with with the said Sarah and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Oddie in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Twenty Guineas that he hath quitted said service about Twelve years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of March 1798 before me } John Collick

Robert Kerr
