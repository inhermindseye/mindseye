Middx  Thos. Williams upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Mary Williams at Bradford Church in Yorkshire about 11 Years ago by whom he hath one Child Agnes aged upwds. of 10 Yrs - Says about 25-years since he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £30 in Houghton Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and lived therein about two Years and has not Since he quitted the sd house rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 5th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

Thos. Williams
