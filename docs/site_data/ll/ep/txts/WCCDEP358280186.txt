Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Hubbard aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Clay in Token house Yard in the City of London for Three years at the yearly Wages of Nine Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlemt. And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 23rd. day of September now last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mrs. Stewart in Newcastle Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of John That one John Harvey an Attorney at Law who lives at Wellington near Taunton in Somersetshire is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examts. Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said John Harvey is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 29th day of Octr. 1788 } Edward Hodsoll

 her Sarah Hubbard Mark

Sarah Hubbard lives at Mrs. Stewart Newcastle Court

John Harvey is a Middle Sized Man fair Complexion dark Hair Tyed behind
