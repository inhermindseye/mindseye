Saint Clement Danes

Middx. & Westmr.} to wit Mary Hall aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she has lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Harris who keeps the Three Tuns in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one William Brooks a Runner at Justice Hydes who lodges on Princes Street Westmr . he having had Carnall knowledge of this Exts. Body for at sevl. times within these two years last past at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said William Brooks is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Janry. 1787 } Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Hall mark

Man taken Justice Hyde passed his word for his Appearance at the next Westmr. Sessions
