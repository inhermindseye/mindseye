Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Tayler aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That about Six years ago she was bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster to one Mr. Parsons a Bricklayer in King Street in the said Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Seven years that after she had Served Two years of her said Apprenticeship her said Master failed in business and run away and left this Deponent and that she this Deponent hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any other settlement

Sworn this 7th sixth day of July 1796 R Ford Rusl Clarke

Mary Taylor
