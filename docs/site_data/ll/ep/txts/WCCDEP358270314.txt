Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann White aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Gillam in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex near Two years at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Thomas Pring a Butcher who lives in Yeates's Court is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of the Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant thereon and that the said Thomas Pring is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 18th. day of July 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Ann White Mark

Ann White lives at No.1 Kings head Yard Duke Street

Child born in the house 22d. Septr. 1797 named Esther White .

Disd. with the Mother the 20th. Decr. 1797

Father paid £10.

Qrs. what Allowance has the Mother
