Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Rickets the Wife of John Rickets Maketh Oath That her said husband (who is lately gone away from her to Places unknown to her) has informed her and which Information She believes to be true That he the said John Rickets When a Singleman was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages with one Mr. Hedges in the Parish of Saint Clement in the City of Oxford Brewer And that he Continued in such Service for the Space of four years That he also afterwards Rented a house in the said Parish and was Charged to and Paid the Poors Rates for the Same And by Means thereof as this Depont. is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge informacon or belief her said husband hath not since rented a house of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds Nor served any Parish offices or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And that her said husband hath a Child named Mary aged about fourteen years his lawful Issue by his late Wife deced who has done no Act to the knowledge or belief of this Depont. to gain a settlemt in her own right And that this Depont. hath also two Children namely David aged about three years and John Aged about Eight Months the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her said husband.

Sworn this 14th. day of April 1768 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Elizabeth Rickits
