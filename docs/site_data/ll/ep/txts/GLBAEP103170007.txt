Middlesex ss}  Margaret Perry  Singlewoman maketh oath that Some time ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages by & with one Nicholas Foy of Salt Peter Bank in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the Said County Victualler & Continued under Such hiring & Service with him there for about the Space of one year & three quarters of a year and then She this Deponent removed with her Said Master into Bakers Row in the hamlet of Mile and new Town in the parish of St Dunstan Stepney Middx Where She Continued with him for the Space of three months & then left the Service of her Said Master & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th day of Septmr 1776 before  Saml. Wood  Wm Blackmore

The Mark of Margaret Perry
