Middx  Jane Norris aged 18 to. 22 Yrs. upon Oath Saith She never was married and that she was bound Apprentice by the Parish Officers of St. Paul Covt Garden to Limbury Morris Orrice  Weaver till she should attain the Age of 21 Yrs. & lived 2 Yrs. the latter part of her sd. Apprentiship with the sd Mr Morris in three Hearing Court in Barbican in the Parish of St. Giles witht. Cripplegate Londn. that abt. half a Yr. before the Expiration of her said Apprentiship her master being poor & unable to maintain her gave her leave to provide for herself where she could & this Examinant never was assigned over Rented ten pounds a Yr. Pd. Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 22d. 1751 before Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Jane Norris
