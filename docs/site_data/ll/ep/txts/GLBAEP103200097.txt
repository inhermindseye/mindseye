Middlesex ss}  John Lee (who was lately Passed by the name of John Clarke to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County) maketh oath that divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with John Andrews of Eastsmithfield in the sd Parish Victualler And Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of a year & has not Since done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this 12 day of February 1784 before}
