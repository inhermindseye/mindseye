Middlesex ss}  Gilbert Goullee maketh oath That he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Joseph Middap a Silk weaver in Thomas's Street in the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the said County & has not since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or. Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a settlement since his said Apprenticeship & that this Depont is now an unmarried man

Sworn this 19. day of Febry 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Gilbert Goullee
