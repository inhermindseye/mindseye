Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Charles Leary aged about 33 years upon his Oath saith that he served Seven years Apprenticeship by Indres duly executed to one Joseph Vanderiom a Chimney Sweeper in Faleon bridge Court Hoghan in the Parish of Saint Annes in the Liberty of Westmr. that since he quitted  his said Apprenticeship he has never done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Depts. knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of July 1786  Jno. Barnfather  James Croft

his Charles Leary Mark
