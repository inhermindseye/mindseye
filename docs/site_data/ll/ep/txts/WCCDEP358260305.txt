Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Williams aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that about 25 years ago she Intermarrd with Evan Williams who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes  That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Devereux Court in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Four years at the yearly Rent of £22 and paid Taxes due for the same and that after he quitted said house he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Informn. or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of July 1798 before me } Wm Goodenough

Margaret Williams
