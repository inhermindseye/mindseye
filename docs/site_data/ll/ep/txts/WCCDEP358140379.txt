Middx  Mary Fare aged upwards of Twenty Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year & a half at Mr. Smith's the Coach and horses Alehouse at Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. at the Yearly Wages of £3 and hath Quitted said Service abt. Ten Months And further upon her Voluntary Oath says is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born says that One Roger Swift a Barber had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of October 1759 at the house of Mrs. Walker the Spptted Dog in Clements Lane at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that he the said Roger Swift and no other person did beget the same And further says she has been informed & believes that the said Roger Swift is on Board One of his Majesty's Ships of War

Sworn this 13th. June 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Fare

Upon Mr. Ponten's Complaint J: P:
