Middx  Pleasant Burt aged Twenty Two Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that abt. Four Years & half ago she was hired to & lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Moffett in Hanging Sword Alley in the Parish of St. Bridgett vulgarly St. Brides  London at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds for the Space of One Year & upwds. & hath lived with her sd. Master several Times since & hath been come away the last Time abt. Six Months that since she left her sd. Master she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne the 18th. of Novr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Pleasant Burt
