Middlesex ss}  Henry Gardener maketh oath that he was born in the parish of Midcalder in the Shire of Edinborough  in that part of great Britain called Scotland That he this Deponent never did any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for himself in that part of Great Britain called England That he hath a Wife named Elizabeth and two Children both of whom were born in Lower East Smithfield in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex namely margaret Aged about eight years & ten Months & Henry Aged about Six years & three Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Said Wife to whom he was lawfully married & who is by birth a Scotch woman & never did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself in any part of England.

Sworn this 31 day of Janry 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

Henry Gardner
