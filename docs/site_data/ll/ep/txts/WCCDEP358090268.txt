Middx to wit Mary Tomlin Aged forty two Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Messrs. Fonseager & Fernandes Tobacconists in Bevis Marks in the Parish of all hallows London wall upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of 7th- hath quitted her said service about three Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or Living a hired Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 28th. day of Novr. 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

Mary Tomlin
