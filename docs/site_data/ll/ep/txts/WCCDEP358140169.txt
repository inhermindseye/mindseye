Middx.  Dorothy Sheldon aged abt. 50 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was Married or Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for one Year says she was born in the house of Mr. Surrin a Perukemaker in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and that she cannot give any Account of the Settlement of her father or Mother

Sworn this 28th. of Octr. 1758 . before}
