Middx  Ann Chapman  Widow maketh Oath and Saith that about Sixteen Years ago her Grandson William Abbott deceased was Bound Apprentice by Indre for Seven Years to his late Father John Abbott of Eastsmithfield in the parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middlesex  Ginger bread Baker and served all his Apprentiship there and never gained any Subsequent Settlement since to her knowledge

Sworn this 19th. day of Decr. 1744 Before Robert Dennett

her Ann Chapman Mark

Ann Chapman in Pennington Street
