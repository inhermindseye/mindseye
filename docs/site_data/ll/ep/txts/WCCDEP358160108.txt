Middx  The Exaicon of Mary Woolley  Singlewoman Aged abt. Twenty one Years Lodging in ye Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. County of Middx taken the Eleventh day of Febry 1739 who upon her Oath Saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Brooks at the Essex Servent in King Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . Victualler for the Space of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds and hath been come away from her sd. Service abt. Two Years And further upon her Oath saith that she never rented any House or since that time lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife and that she is now sick & Poor and not able to maintaine herselfe.

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane  Jas Gronous

mary Woolley
