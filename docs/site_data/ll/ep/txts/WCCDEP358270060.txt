Saint Clement Danes July 3d. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  Copy Enbd in Examn. Book No. 25 page 4  St George Westmr .}  Joseph Baker aged 37 Years and upwards upon his Oath Saith that he was intermarried with Margt. Baker his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint James  in the Liberty of Westminster by Bans that him and his said Wife since their intermarriage (that is to say) abt. 2 years ago they Rented and Lived took and Rented a house in No. 1 in Lees Mews North Audley Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex and lived there upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of £15 that since they left the said House they have been in Weekley Lodgings and have never since Rented any Tenemt. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or  lived a the that he never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for One Year nor has done any other Act matter or thing whereby he might have gained any other subsequent settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief that he & is said Wife is poor and the

Sworn the 3d. July 1792 before me} Addington Jrs. Dyson

Joseph Baker
