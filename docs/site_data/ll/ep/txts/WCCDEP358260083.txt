Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Stevens aged about 54 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Butler the corner of Dean Street Finsbury Square in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middlesex for about Eighteen Months at the yearly wages of Nine Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Six Months since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 11th. day of November 1795 before us} M Addington Russl: Clarke
