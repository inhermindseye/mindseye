Middlesex ss}  Ann Sistram the Wife of Abraham Sistram maketh oath that her sd husband who is now at Sea is by birth a Swede as he has informed her & which She believes to be true that to her knowledge or belief he never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England that many years ago & Previous to her Marriage with her sd husband She was duly bound Apprentice by the Parish officers of the Hamlet of Hammer Smith in the sd County to one Mrs Whiteside a Milkwoman of the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the sd County & after having Served about three years & half of her sd Apprenticeship there her sd Mistress died & this Deponents sd sd Apprenticeship thereupon Ceased & determined & this Depont has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere Save what she may have Acquired by her marriage as aforesaid

Sworn this 7 day of May 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Ann Sistram
