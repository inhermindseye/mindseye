Middx  Elizth Brindlecomb  Widow of Chas Brindlecomb Aged about 65 Years upon Oath Says she Intermarried with the said Charles Brindlecomb at ye Fleet London about Twenty Seven Years ago Says her said Husband whilst a Singleman lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Lott an Attorney in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. about three Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds or thereabouts Says her said late Husband never Rented House or paid Taxes neither hath this Examinant Since her said Husbands Death Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 6th day of Decr 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth. Brindlecomb

Upon Mr Olivers Complt. Ovrs J. W
