Middx  Henry Wagenor upon Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice to and Served five Years & upwards of his Apprentiship with Mr. Fridenburgh a Taylor in Burleigh Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and at the End of, the sd. term his master ran away & this Examinant never was assigned over rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn March 6th 1750 before  Tho Lane .

William Henery Wagenor
