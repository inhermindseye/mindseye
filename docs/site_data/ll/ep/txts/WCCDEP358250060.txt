St Clement Danes Augst. 8th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Pancras  Four Children (to wit) Benjamin Beckett aged about 42 years upon Oath saith that he intermarried with his late Wife Ann at the parish Church of St Leonard Shoreditch about 20 years ago that his said Died on the second instant That since his said Marriage he this Deponent Rented and Resided in a House situate near Basnige Wells in the parish of St Pancras in the County of Middlesex about a year, at the Annual Rent of £10 that he quitted that House about fourteen years ago and then Rented and Resided in an unfurnished Apartment of the House near the same and with in the same Parish of St Pancras about a year at the Annual Rent of £15 that he quitted that Apartment about thirteen years ago and hath not since rented any House or Lodging of ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes served any parochial office or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement and this Deponent father saith that he hath four Children by his said Wife now living Namely Sarah aged Eleven years or thereabouts Benjamin William aged Eight years and upwards Ann aged six years and upwards and Deborah aged two years and upwards all

with this Deponent That he this Deponent is Ill and unable to maintain himself and family without Assistance

Sworn the 8th. day of August 1792 before us } Thos. Gordon  Wm Kitchiner

Benjamin Beckett
