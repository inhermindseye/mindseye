Westmr. 

Mary Roe  Wido . of Thos. Roe (who dyed in Decr. last) upon Oath Saith she inter- -married with the sd. Thos. Roe at Cheshunt  in Hertfordshire by whom she hath two Childn. Wm. aged upwds. of Nine Years & Ann aged about 5 Yrs Says her sd. late husband rented the Craven Inn in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of £100 or there abouts & lived there in upwds. of 5 Yrs & there Dyed & that she this Examt. hath not since the Decease of her sd. late husband Rented Ten pounds a Yr. or pd parish Taxes

Sworn 8th. Augt 1750 before}
