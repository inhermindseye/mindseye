Middlesex ss}  Thomas Brockbridge maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to James Hawkins of the Parish of St Sepulchre London  Carver & his Master dying one Thomas Williams married his widow and this Deponent was in rolled or turned over to him for the residue of his Sd Apprenticeship and this Deponent Served the last year of Such his said Apprenticeship with his said Master in Barbican in the Parish of Saint Giles without Cripplegate in the City of London & where his Sd Apprenticeship ended That he this Deponent since the aspiration of his Sd Apprenticeship has not rented a house or Lodging of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8 day of May 1788 before  R Davies

Thos. Brockbridge
