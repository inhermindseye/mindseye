Bastardy before Birth

St Clement Danes July 23d 1792

Middlesex & Westminster}

Settlemt.

Pregnancy

Father (to wit) Sarah Earnshaw  Singlewoman aged 25 years and upwards Upon Oath saith That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Campbell Taylor Surry Street in the parish of St Clement Danes for about One year and ten Months at the Annual Wages of Seven Guineas that she quitted that service about May 1790 and hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant for twelve months together rented any House or Lodging of £10 a year paid any parish Taxes or otherwise ab obtained any subsequent legal Settlement in to her knowledge or belief and never hath been Married and this Deponent Upon her Voluntary Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County City and Liberty and that Thomas Waymark a Joun  Journey man Carpenter who lately Worked with Mr Jackson of Kingsland Road is the only real true father of such Child or Children where with she is now pregnant he & no one else having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in or about the Month of January last a at several times  since at some or on

of which times he did begot the said same thereon and lastly this Deponent saith that no person but the said Thomas Waymark hath been ever criminally connected with her this Deponent and that she is now distressed and unable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn the 23d July day of July 1792 before me} R Butler

Sarah Earnshaw 's mark

Witness Edwd Burbidge

the Woman in the House
