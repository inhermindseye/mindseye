Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Wright Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she was Bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of poor of the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. to one Mr. John Price who kept a Chandlers Shop the Corner of Chandlers Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex and Served Two Years of her said Apprenticeship and then this Examinant went away from her said Master since which time she never rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1770 before Us } Tho Lane R. Butler

her Sarah Wright Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
