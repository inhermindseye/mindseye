Middlesex to wit Thomas Phillips Aged about Thirty years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex about five years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children living Says he lived a hired yearly Servant  with one Edwd. Cotterell a Cowkeeper in the Parish of Hampstea Hampstead in the County of Middlesex for two years at the yearly Wages of £5. hath quitted his said Service upwds. of Seven years ago since which time he hath never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 29th: day of January 1784 R. Taylor

his Thomas Phillips mark
