Middx  John Albone aged upwds. of 21 Yrs. upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Cavanagh at the Ship & Star in the Butcher Row in the parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middx for 18 months or thereabts. at the Yearly Wages of £6 and quitted his sd. Service in or about Septr. last, since which he has not rented ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn June 7th. 1751 Tho Lane

The Mark of John Albone
