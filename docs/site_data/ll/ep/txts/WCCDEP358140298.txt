Middx.  Mary Place aged abt. Twenty One Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mortimer the third door from Devereux Court in the Strand towards Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for Sixteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. four Months. And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the Ninth day of September 1759. she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Sadd in Old Boswell Court in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized John says that one John FitzJohn a Carpenter (who now absconds) had carnal know- -ledge of her Body on or abt. the 27th. day of December 1758. and at one other time since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said John FitzJohn and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn October 9th. 1759 : before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Place

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Rust. Ovr. J: P:
