St Clement Danes 7th June 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  settled in St Mary le bone  (to wit) John Stringer aged sixty three years and upwards Widower Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his late Wife Mary formerly Mary Jones spirister at the Parish Church of St Bride London about 27 years ago (by License) that his said Wife died about 23 years ago leaving no issue that he hath not since been Married that while he was a Widower having no Child or Children he lived as an hired yearly Servant as Coachman with the Rivd Mr. Wynne at the corner of Wimpole Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for a year and upwards at the annual Wages of £16 that he quitted that service about fifteen years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 7th. day of June 1794 before us }

John Shinger Stringer
