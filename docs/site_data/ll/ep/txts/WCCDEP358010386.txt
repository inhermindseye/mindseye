St Clement Danes

Ann Cock Aged about Sixty five years or thereabouts Maketh Oath and saith that about twenty Nine years ago she Intermarried with Golden  Cock her late husband at the parish Church of St. Mary be Strand and that her said late Husband Rented for twenty Six years at the Annual Rent of £26 a House in Arundel Street in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which he quitted at Midsummer 1789. since which time neither her said Husband in his lifetime nor she this Examinant since his Decease (which happened on the 5th. of November last) have Rented £10 a year or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief either he in his life time or she since his Decease, might have gained any  legal Settlement

Sworn before me this 28th. day of Janry. 1791 R Butler
