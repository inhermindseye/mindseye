Middlesex ss}  Catherine Miller the widow of George Miller deced maketh oath That divers Years ago & Since her said husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Michael McDaniel in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Salesman & Continued in Such Service under the Sd hiring for the Space one Year & half & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or been married or done any Act to gain another Settlement.

Sworn this 24 day of September 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Catherine Miller
