Middx  The Examination of Jno. Steuart aged sixty five Years Liveing in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 6th. Day of January 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that on the 11th. Day of June last he intermarried with Mary Ann his now Wife at the Parish Church of St. Peters Pauls Wharf London and that about 17 Years ago he rented & lived in a House of the Yearly Rent of 24 Pounds for the Space of Six Years or thereabouts in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes and further on his Oath saith that he hath not since he left the said House rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife and being Poor is not able to maintaine himselfe and Wife

Sworne the Day and Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

John Steuart
