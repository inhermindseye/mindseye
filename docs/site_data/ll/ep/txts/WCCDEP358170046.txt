Middx  The Examination of Jno. Kimber living in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 4th- Day of May 1741 Who upon his Oath saith that abt. Twenty three Years since he was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mary Snelling in Stanhope Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes  Gold Beater & further upon his Oath saith that on the 10th- Day of Augt. 1733 he intermarried with Sarah his now Wife further saith that he never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or Beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine himselfe & Wife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas Gronous

John Kimber
