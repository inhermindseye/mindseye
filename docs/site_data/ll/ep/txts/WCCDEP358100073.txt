Middlesex to Wit Margarett Bulmer Aged about Sixty years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one John Henley  Esqr . in King Street  Bloomsbury Square in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex upwds. of one year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about 4 Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement.

Sworn this 25th. day of June 1783 R Butler Sampn Wright

Margret Bulmer
