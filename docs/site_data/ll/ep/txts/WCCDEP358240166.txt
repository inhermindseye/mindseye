St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. Martin in the fields  (to wit) Frances Layton  Widow aged 50 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to William Layton her late Husband in or about the year 1758 at the parish Church of St Andrew Holborn (by Banns) That her said Husband previous thereto and while single served his Apprenticeship with a Mr Gladman Trunkmaker at Charing Crop in the parish of St Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County as he informed this Deponent and which she verily believes to be true And this Deponent saith that he never obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge information or belief That her said Husband died about twenty two years ago and that she hath not since been Married Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And that She is distressed ill and is become Chargeable to the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty

Sworn the 25th day of Septr. 1792 before us  Sampr Wright Gd: Mitchell

Frances Layton
