Middx  Henry Elliot upon Oath Saith he rented a house of £17 by the Year at Kingston upon Hull in the County of York & lived therein about two Years quitted the sd. house upwds. of 30 Years ago & has not Since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant

Sworn the 14th. July 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Hen: Elliott
