Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Richard Dobbins one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Margaret Bagland Seely an Infant aged about Three years Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Margaret Bagland Seely  was born on or about the 27th. day of July 1792 at the house of a Mr. Lowe in Hop Gardens St. Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr.

Sworn this 27th. day of July 1795  Rob: Burd Gabriel Edw. Read

Richd Dobbins
