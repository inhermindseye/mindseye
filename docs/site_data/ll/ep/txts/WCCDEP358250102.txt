Saint Clement Danes  July 20th. 1793.

Middlesex and Westminster -} (to wit) Elizabeth Harvey  singlewoman aged 29 Years and upwards Upon Oath and Saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Madson late of Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex as Victualler for five Years at the Annual Wages of Six Guineas Per Annum that she quitted that peace above the in or about the Year 1788 and hath not since lived as an hired Yearly Servant for a Year Together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year and any Parish Taxes a otherwise obtained any legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief that she poor and distressed

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1793 before us}

her Elizabeth Harvey Mark

Witness Chas: Wyecross
