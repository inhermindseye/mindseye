Middlesex ss}  John Andrews of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Beadle maketh oath that he hath been informed (among othr Matters) by an Examination of Mary Hemming late of the sd Parish Singlewoman deced taken in Writing & upon oath the fourth day of Novemr last before two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the sd. County the Contents of which Examination this Depont believes to be true that She the said Examinant had then two Children namely Ann Curttler aged about Six years & Mary Curttler aged about four years both of whom were born on Saffron Hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the sd County & baptized & registred in the Church thereto belonging which said Children were illegitimate & Bastards by reason that she the Said Mary Hemming was never Married to their late Father Thomas Curttler deced That she the sd Mary Hemming is lately deced leaving behind her the sd two Bastard Children who are become Chargeable to the sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 17th. day of Febry 1784 before J Hawley R Davies

John Andrews
