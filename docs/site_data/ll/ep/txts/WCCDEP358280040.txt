Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Child aged about 18 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented an House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year that she was born in Wych Street and that her Father Rents and lives in a House in Ship Yard in the said Parish at the yearly Rent of £10 & upwards that this Examinant has done no Act to gain and other Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born Bastard or Bastards and become Chargeable to the said Parish And further saith that one Wm. Starton a Carpenter who works with his Father John Starton a Carpenter who lives in Old Grave Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in Month of April last and at several times since one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is Pregnant and that the said Wm. Starton is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 21st. Octr. 1786 before me} Wm Kitchiner

her Sarah Child mark

28th. Novr. 1786 Committed to New Prison by Justice Walker Bailed by the Father This is Settled-Bond given & Exted The Father had the Child
