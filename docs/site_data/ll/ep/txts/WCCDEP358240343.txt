October 26th.. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Newmarket Suffolk  (to wit) Elizabeth Cole aged Fifteen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she never lived as an hired yearly Servant or did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own right that she is informed & believes that she is the legitimate Child of John and Elizabeth Cole now of Newmarket in the County of Cambridge that her Father Rented and resided in an House in the parish of Newmarket in the County of Suffolk for sometime and paid Taxes for the same and so became a parishioner thereof that her said Father her frequently received Relief of that parish and this Examinant saith that she is unable to support herself without assistance being much subject to Fits

Sworn the 26th day of Octr. 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Elizabeth Cole 's Mark

Witness Garrett

not removed by Orders, sent down to her father by the Waggon ye Wher. the proper parish mentd. in the Examn
