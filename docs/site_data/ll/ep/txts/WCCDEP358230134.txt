Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr} to wit William Emery aged forty one Years in thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he Rented and lived in a House Situate in Hosier Street in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre in the City of London of the Yearly Value of twenty Pounds for about four Months, that he quitted the same about twelve Months ago and hath not Since then Rented any other Tenement of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds or done any other Act whereby to the best of his Knowledge he may have acquired any Subsequent Settlement and this Examinant further saith that he was married to Ann his Present Wife at Arundel in the County of surface about Eleven Years ago and hath by his said Wife two Children Namely William aged ten Years or thereabouts and Maria aged Seven Years or thereabouts who are best of there dependant on this Examinant for support and that he this Examinant being unwell and having bad Eye is unable to maintain himself and his said Wife and Children by Working at

at his made of a Shoemaker

Sworn this 23rd Day of April 1800 . before } Ws Woodenough Saml Edwards

Wm Emery

Witness H Day
