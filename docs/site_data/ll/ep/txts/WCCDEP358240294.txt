St Clement Danes July 31st 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Children  (to wit) Sarah Markham aged thirty six years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband John Markham at the parish Church of Saint Sepulchre without Newgate about seven years ago (by Banns) that she hath been informed by her said Husband which she verily believes to be true that he is a native of Scotland and hath not acquired any legal settlement in England that she hath two Children by her said Husband namely Hannah aged about four years & ten Months and William aged two years seven Months & upwards that her said Husband hath left this Examinant and her said two Children and is (as she is informed) serving on board one of the Kings Ships And this Examinant further saith that she is entitled to her last legal settlement in right of her first Husband named Samuel Linter deceased to whom she was Married (by Banns) at the parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about twenty one years ago and who informed her which she verily believes to be true that he never obtained any legal settlement in his own right that he was the legitimate Child of John and Mary Linter both deceased that his Father John Linter Rented & resided in an Apartment in a place then called Little Essex

Precinct of White Friars  the Woman and eldest Child were Removed before by An Order not Appealed agst  Street in the precinct of White Friars London for about ten years at the Weekly Rent of four shillings and did not during his Life obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Examinant saith that the said Samuel Linter died about Died fourteen years ago, that she this Examinant and the said Hannah her Daughter were Removed from the said parish of Saint Clement Danes to the said Precinct of White Friars by Order of Removal on or about the twenty ninth day of June 1790 against which said Order or Removal there hath not been any Appeal exhibited to this Deponent's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 31st day of July 1793 before Us} W Addington Edwd: Naish

Sarah Markham 's Mark

Witness Garrett
