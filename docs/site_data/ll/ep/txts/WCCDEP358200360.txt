Westmr.  John Wright aged about 54 Years upon Oath says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr, Robt. Wright a Perukemaker opposite Saint Clement's Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never Rented the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year;

Sworn the 26th. Feby. 1766 , before} Frans Cary

John Wright
