Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} Ann Hamilton aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Willing in upper Charlotte Street in the parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for about Three years at the yearly wages of Ten pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Nine years since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she hath Two illegitimate Children living vizt. Mary Ann aged Eight years born at the house of a Mrs. Capps in Brewhouse Lane  Stoke Newington in the parish of St. John at Hackney in the County of Middlesex and Thomas born on or about the 31st. day of Augt 1789 at the house of a Mrs. Bayley in Street Lane Mortlake

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1795  M Addington E Read

Ann Hamilton
