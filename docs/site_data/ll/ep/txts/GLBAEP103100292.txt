Middlesex ss}  Mary Robinson the widow of John Robinson deced maketh oath That her said late Husband duly served his apprenticeship with one John moody a Smith in the Parish of St. Mary (near or adjoining to the Town of New Castle upon Tyne) in the County of Durham and by means th as she is advised he gained a legal settlement there and this Deponent Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said husband did not afterwards to do any act to gain a subsequent Settlement nor hath she this Deponent done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own Right since her said late husbands Death.

Sworn this day of May 1771 before B Camper

Mary Robinson Her Mark
