Middx  Winifred Viney Aged about 28 Years Upon Oath Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Wm. Hutchins in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middx two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about three Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year And Further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she was never Marryed that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born that Wm. Thorp  Engine Maker had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body several times at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 16th Day of June 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

Winifred Viney
