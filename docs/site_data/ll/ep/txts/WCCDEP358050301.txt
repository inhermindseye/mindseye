Middx  Frances Simpson  Widow aged about 70 years upon Oath Saith she lived and yearly Servant with Mr. Wm. Jansson in Cecil Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of £2 and quitted her sd. Service about a fortnight ago

Sworn Augt. 27th. 1750 before} Frnces Simpson
