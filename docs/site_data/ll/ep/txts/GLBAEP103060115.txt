Middx ss The Examination of Elizabeth Freebody  Singlewoman taken on oath before us Two of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the county of Middx (whereof one is of the Quorum) and both residing in or next to the Limits of the parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County this 30th Day of May in the year of our Lord 1749

This Examinant maketh oath and Saith that about Two Years ago one Robert Percival of the parish of St. George Hanover Square in the county of Middx  Gent did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lie with her at the house of Mr . Robert Thomas in the parish of St. James Westmr. in the County of Middx  Apothecary and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body Several times whereby She conceived with Child of a Male Bastard Child of which she was Delivered at the house of Nicholas Gastrow in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx  Chandler on the Twentieth of December one thousand Seven hundred and forty Seven which Said Male Bastard child hath been baptised by the name of Thomas and hath been now is and still is likely to be Chargeable to the said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate this Examinant being poor & not able to provide for the same And this Examinant farther Saith that the said Robert Percival is the True & only father of the Said Male Bastard Child And that this Examination is voluntary & that She hath not been forced or compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 30th Day of May 1749 before  Richd. Ricards  Sam Gower

The mark of Elizabeth Freebody
