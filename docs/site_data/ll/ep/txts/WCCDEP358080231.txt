Westmr. to wit Frances King Aged about fifty Seven Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John King who died about Thirteen Years ago and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holbourn about Twenty Seven Years ago and by whom she hath no Child now living Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House the Corner of Hatton Wall in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Nine Pounds and paid Land tax due for the same hath quitted said House upwards of Twenty Years ago since which time her said Husband never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husband Death

Sworn this 23d day of March 1778 before  John Croft  Thos. Parker

Farence King
