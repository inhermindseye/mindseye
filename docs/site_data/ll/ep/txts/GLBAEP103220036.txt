Middlesex ss}  William Bull maketh oath that he heretofore was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to and Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Matthew Percival in Swallow Street in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in Westminster in the Sd County Shoemaker That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish Office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Elizabeth and two Children namely Mary aged about Six Years and Ann Aged about three Months the lawful Issue of this Depont by his Said Wife to whom he this Deponent was lawfully married

Sworn this. 4. day of May 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

William Bull
