Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Lilly Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Warcup a Cheesemonger in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Nine Month ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further says that on the Thirty first day of December last past she was deliverd. of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish which hath since been baptized by the Name of Sarah and is become Chargable to the sd. Parish and that one George Lyon late Servant to her said late Master is the only true and lawfull Father thereof

Sworn this 18th. day of March 1778 R Butler

her Elizth. Lilly Mark
