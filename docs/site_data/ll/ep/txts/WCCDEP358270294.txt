Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Taylor aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the Nineteenth day of December 1789 she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of Henry Davis No. 41 Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the name of Henry Davis Taylor That the said Henry Davis who lived in Butcher Row aforesaid and is by Trade a Hairdresser is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Henry Davis and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith That the said Henry Davis left Butcher Row between Two and Three years ago, where he went to she this Deponent doth not know nor where he is to be met with at this time she doth not know

Sworn this 7th. day of March 1797 before Me} J Reeves

Ann Taylor

Resworn this 15th. day of Febry. 1798 before us} Raph Clarke
