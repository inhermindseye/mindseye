Middlesex ss.  William Rolles maketh Oath that on the First Day of March One Thousand Seven Hundred and Forty two he was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to Thomas Heard of Victualling Office Square in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Cooper and served about Five years and a half of his said Apprenticeship with his said Master there, when his said Master not having employ for him gave him up his Indentures, that he has never Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or lived as a hired Servant by the year or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of Jany. 1793 before}
