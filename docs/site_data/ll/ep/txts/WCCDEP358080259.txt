Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Davis aged about Twenty Two years on her Oath says she is the Wife of William Davis now a Soldier in the Tenth Regiment of Foot now lying at Chatham Barricks in the County of Kent with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint James in the City of Bath about three years ago and by whom she hath one Child named Jane aged about Ten weeks and this Examinant further says that her said Husband hath other Children living by a Former wife namely William aged about thirteen years and Thomas aged about five Years says that it appears from an Examination taken in Writing the 26th. Day of June Instant that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House at Bathford in the County of Somerset for Nine Months at the Yearly Rent of £16 a year and paid Taxes due for the same and that her said Husband hath gained no other subsequent Settlement since Quiting the same which Examination this Examinant Verily believes to be true.

Sworn this 27th- day of June 1777 before me  Abrm. Baley R Butler

her Elizth. Davis Mark
