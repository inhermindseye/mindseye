Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizth. Turpin aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Jos: Turpin her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields on the 25th. day of July 1783 and whom she hath 2 Children living vizt. Elizth. aged upon of 2 years and Mary aged about 6 Months That she Ext. has been informed by her said Husband which information she believes to be true that he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Cook a Taylor in King Street Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx for 7 years that he served years of his said Apprenticeship And then run away that he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. Augt. 1787  Rup Clarke D Walker

her Elizth. Turpin mark
