Middx The Examination of Mary Magee Singlewoman  who was found wandering and Begging in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  in the County of Middx taken the Fourth  Day of  February 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about three Years ago she was hired to & lived with one Mr. Sculley in Brides Street in the Parish of Brides in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland Linen Draper as a yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds and hath been come away from the same about Six Months And this Examinant further on her Oath saith that she hath not since She left the said  Mr. Sculley rented ten Pounds Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
