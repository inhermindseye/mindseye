Middx  Jane Sheppey  Wido . of Adam Sheppey removed from the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. by Order dated the 5th. Day of Decr. Instt. upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd Adam Sheppey in the Parish Church of Guber in the County of Bedford about 15 Yrs. ago Says her sd. Husband lived in & rented a house in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £30 for the Space of One Year & upwards & hath left same about two Years Says her sd. late Husband never after rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochiall Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since his Death rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or

beleif Says she hath 3 Childn. (to Witt) Mary aged abt. 12 Yrs. Jane aged abt. 8 Yrs. & Fras. Cath: aged abt. 5 Yrs.

Sworn 6th. Decr. 1745 before W Jones

Jane Shepey
