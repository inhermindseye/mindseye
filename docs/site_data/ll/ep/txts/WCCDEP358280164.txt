Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Smith aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that about 18 years ago she Intermarried with George Smith her late Husband at the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry that he has been dead about 2 years that her said Husband was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England that she this Examinant was bound an Apprentice by Indres duly executed to Evan Vaughan of Fetter Lane for 4 or 5 years that after she had served about 12 Months her Master moved from Fetter to Chandos Street and staid the about 12 Months that then here moved into the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that after she had been about 12 Months in the Strand with her said Master she Intermarried with said George Smith that after she quitted said Service She never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one William Rice who did lodge in Old Nichol Street Cock Lane near Shoreditch and by Trade a Broad Weaver is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said William Rice is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of June 1788 } R Butler

Elizabeth Smith

William Rice is about 40 years old wears a Wig Single Row of Curls Long Features we are a Light Brown Great Coat

she delivered in the Workhouse of 2 Childn. Children both died.
