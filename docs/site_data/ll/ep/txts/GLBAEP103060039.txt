Middx  Hosea Mackown maketh Oath and Saith that about Eight Months ago he Rented a House in Flower Deluce Court in Tooley Street in the parish of St. Olaves Church in Southwark at Twenty pounds a Year and live therein about five Months and gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And hath a Wife named Elizabeth and hath three Children (namely) James Aged about Seven Years John Aged about five Years and Elizabeth Aged about one Year.

Sworn this 15th. day of May 1745 Before Robert Dennett

Hosea Mackown
