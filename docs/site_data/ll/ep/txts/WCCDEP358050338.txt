Middlesex  Ann Lockyer upon Oath, saith she Inter- marryed with her husband John Lockyer abt. 32 years ago in the parish Church of St Clement Danes and that her sd. husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £30 in Old Boswell Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lived therein abt. 3 Years & has quitted the same abt. 15 years since which time he has not Rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 15th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Lokyer
