Middx to wit Elizth. Smith aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Vaughan Esqr. in Lincolns Inn Fields in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for one whole Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Six Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th. day of April 1779 before  Abrm Baley M Addington

her Elizth. Davis Mark
