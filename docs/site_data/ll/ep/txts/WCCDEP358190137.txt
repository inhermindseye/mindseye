Middx  Margaret Markwell aged about 24 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married That she now lives a hired Yearly Servant with Dr. Lawrence in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and hath lived as such Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds & ten shillings for the first Year & Five pounds afterwards And further upon her voluntary Oath says she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to provide for the same when Born And that William Kirk late a Footman to the said Dr. Lawrence did beget such Child or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in or about the Month of August One thousand seven hundred and Sixty and once afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 27th: day of May 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

Margat Markewill
