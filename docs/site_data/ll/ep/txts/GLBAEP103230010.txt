Middlesex ss  Thomas Sharp Maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house at the yearly rent of Eighteen Pounds in Marsh Street in the Parish of Walthamstow in the County of Essex and was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same & has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25. day of Janry 1787 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

Thos Sharp
