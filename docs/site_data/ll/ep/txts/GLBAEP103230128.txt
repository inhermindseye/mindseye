Middlesex ss  Robert Wood maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St John Wapping in the said County & resided thereon for the Space of Seventeen years & upwards & during Such his residence thereon he was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the same That he has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife now living named Mary to whome he was lawfully married and a Child named Nancy the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his first wife  Ann deced to whom he was lawfully married the which child is aged about Nine years

Sworn this first day of April 1788 before me  R Davies  Robt: Smith

Robt: Wood
