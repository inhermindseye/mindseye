Middlesex ss}  Jane Dunster  wife of Peter Dunster maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her as a Marine in his Majesties Service beyond the Seas That her sd husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served about four years & an half of his Apprenticeship with Mr Butler a Carpenter in Silver Street in the Parish of Saint Olave in Silver Street in the City of London & his Said Master then be coming insolvent thereupon left him & went to Sea & did not afterwards return back to his Sd Master to her knowledge or belief That her Sd husband to her knowledge or belief never rented a house of Ten Pounds Year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt Since his Apprenticeship That She hath a Female Child aged about 12 days the lawful issue of this Depont by her Sd husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 5. day of Novr 1778 before removd. Jasper Clarkey P. Rowlands

The Mark of Jane Dunster
