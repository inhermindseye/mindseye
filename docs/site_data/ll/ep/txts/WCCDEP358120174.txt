Middx  Digby Trim aged abt. 42 Years upon Oath Saith he was bound apprentice to & Served the first 5 Years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Abraham Phillips in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx Taylor that at the End of the said 5 Years he absconded from his said Master and never was assigned over to any other Person nor ever rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 8th. Augt. 1754 before}
