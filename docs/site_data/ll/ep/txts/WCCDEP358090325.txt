Middx & Westmr}  Margt. Webster Aged about 18 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Messrs.Cooper & Miller Pawnbroker in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service this day which time she hath gained no other Settlemt. And further on her Voluntary Oath says she n pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she Being Poor and unable to maintain the same when Bond and further says that one Thos. Sowerby an Apprentice to one Mr. Miller partner with the on Mr. Cooper of wych Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her late Masters House in Wych Street the la end of Decr. last but the Particular day she does not now remember & at sevl. Different times afterwards aton of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Thomas Sowerby & no other person did Beget the same & that he the real & True Father thereof

Sworn this 2d. day of August 1782 before R Butler

her Margt. Webster Mark
