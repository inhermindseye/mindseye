Middlesex ss  Robert Haddersick maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice by Indre to one Mr Reynholds a Butcher of Allen Street in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex and served his Said Master there for the Space of Nine Months and had Board and lodging found him in his said Masters house and by Means thereof as he apprehends gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that at the expiration of the Said Nine Months he left his said Master & went to Sea And that he has not since lived as a hired Servt. by the Year or as an apprentice with any other Person Nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year nor served any Parochial office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act whereby to gain a subsequent settlement And that he hath a wife named Jane and a Child named Robert aged two years & ten Months or thereabouts the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his said wife

Sworn this 13th day of November 1767 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Rob. Haddersick
