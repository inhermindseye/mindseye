Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Eaton aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she has lived as a hired yearly with Mr. Wright Glazier in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for these four years at the yearly wages of Seven Pounds And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Thomas Cantfield an Apprentice to the said Mr. Wright is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Thomas Cantfield is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 12th. day of Febry. 1789 .} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Eaton
