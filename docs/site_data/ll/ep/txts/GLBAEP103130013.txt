Middlesex ss}  Mary Basterfield the Wife of  William Basterfield (who is gone away from her Place to unknown) maketh oath That her said husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was born in Bagging Bottle Alley in Old Street in the Parish of Saint Luke in the County of Middlesex That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her said husband never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound Apprentice nor ever rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right That this Deponent Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her husbands Parents and that about Twenty nine years ago she was married to her said husband at the Fleet .

Sworn this 30th. day of September 1773 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

The Mark of Mary Basterfield
