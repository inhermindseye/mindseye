Middlesex to wit Mary Chandler Aged about Sixty five years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Joseph Chandler who has been dead about Eleven years and by whom she hath no Childn. living Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Lewkeners Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in th Fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of Half a year at the yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same and quitted said House near Twenty years ago & never after he quitted same Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subseqt. Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death.

Sworn this 1st. day of December 1775 before R Butler

Mary Chandler
