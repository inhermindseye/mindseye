Westmr.  Mary Bourseau aged upwards of Forty Years, Upon Oath says, she never was Married; and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Captain Stamper near Blackheath in the Parish of East Greenwich in the County of Kent for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, quitted said Service about thirteen Years ago, and hath not since Rented the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds, paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 14th, day of October 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant R Kilsha

The Mark of Mary Bourseau
