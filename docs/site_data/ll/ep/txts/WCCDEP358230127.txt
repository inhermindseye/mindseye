Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Hardy Aged about 42 Years upon her Oath Saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant for 4 years with One Mr Morris at the Red Lyon Saint Clement Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Eastcheap in the City of London at the yearly Wages of four Pounds that she hath left the said Service about 20 Years since which time she hath not lived a a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of March 1800 before us} John Collick  Benjm Gee

her Ann Hardy Mark

Witness Aw Burbidge Beadle
