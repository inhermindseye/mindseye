Middx  William Shorter aged about 52 Years Upon Oath says that he was bound Apprentice by Indenture in the Year 1727 to Jacob Tomkins a Shoemaker in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes with whom he lived about three Years when his said Master ran away Says that he was never assigned over to any other Person lived a hired Yearly Servant Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 5th. day of Janry. 1763 before} Paul Vaillant

The mark of Wm. Shorter
