Middx  Elizabeth Dickenson aged 44 Years removed from the Parish of St Giles in the Fields by Order dated the 29th. Day of May last upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her prest. Husband  John Dickenson on the 10th: Day of Septr. 1721 at the Fleet London that  the said John Dickenson lived in & rented a house in Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £11: & upwards & lived in the same 2 Years & upwards & hath left same upwards of 17 Years since which time he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif that her sd. Husband is at present in the new prison at Clerkenwell & this Examinant being Poor is not able to maintain herself

Sworne June 2d. 1744 before} Wm: Jones .

Elizabeth Dickenson
