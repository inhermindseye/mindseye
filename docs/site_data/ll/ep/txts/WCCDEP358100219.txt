Middx & Westmr.}  Charlott Wood Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Mrs. Garratt als Adlamin Bennetts Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for 18 Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of 12 Month ago since which time she never lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or gained any other Settlement than as before deposed and further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish and further Says that one James Dean who drives a Team for a Farmer in Bedfordshire and when in Town lodges at the Red Horse in Change Court  Strand is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings in White Hart Yard Drury Lane upwards of Ten Months ago & at sevl. different times afterwards at One of which times he get her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said James Dean and us other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 3d. day of June 1784 before  Wm. Kitchiner

her Charlotte Wood Mark
