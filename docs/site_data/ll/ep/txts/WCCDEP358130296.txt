Middx  Cath: Moseley aged abt. 50 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Richd. Moseley who hath been dead abt. 8 Years says she cannot give any Account of his Settlement; that before her Intermarriage with the said Richd. Moseley & whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Picket a Wine Merchant on Great Tower Hill in the Parish Hamlet Tower Royal in the City of London for one Year & a half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 9th. day of March 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Cathrin Mosley
