St Clement Danes 7th. Janry 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St James Westmr } (to wit) William Freeman Aged forty years or thereabouts upon Oath saith that he intermarried with Ann Carter  Spinster at the parish Church of St George Bloomsbury about 22 years ago (by Banns) that his said Wife left this Examinant about 10 years ago that he had then a Child by his said Wife Named Ann aged about two years which she took with her and this Deponent hath not seen the said Child since: that since their said Marriage he this Deponent Rented resided in an Apartm in Rupert Street in the parish of St James in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex  for three years & upwards at the Annual Rent of £12 that he quitted that House about Nine or ten years ago and hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge or belief

Sworn the 7th day of Janry. 1793 before us Sampn Wright M Addington

William Freeman
