Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman and of the Beadles belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Phoebe Frost an Infant aged 12 Months upon his Oath Saith That he hath been Informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said Phoebe Frost is the legitimate Child of Edward Frost who rented a house for two years in Parkers Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex at upwards of twenty Pounds a year that he hath left the said House about seven years since which time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Infor- mation or belief

Sworn this 27th day of August 1800 before us } Ws Woodenough John Collick

B Newman
