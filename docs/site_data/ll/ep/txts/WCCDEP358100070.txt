Middx to wit Dorothy Collett Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. William Walker a Sugar Factor in Well Close Square in the Parish of Saint Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds hath Quitted her said Service upwards of Three Years ago since which time she hath never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further on her Oath Says that on the 16th. day of of February 1781 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Davis a Hairdresser facing St. Clement Church Strand which hath since been baptized William and is become Chargable to the last mentioned Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same And further says that the said Mr. Walker her said late Master and no other person is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of June 1783 before R Butler

Dorothy Collet
