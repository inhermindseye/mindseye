Saint Clement Danes

Middx: ss:  Mary Cooper aged 33 Yrs. or thereabouts Upon her Oath Saith, That she never was Married that she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with a Mr. Vigans for 10 about 10 Yrs., opposite the New Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes as the yearly Wages of 7 Guineas, And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is Pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become to chargeable to the sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Mr. Jeremiah Vigans Vigiers who lives with the sd. Mr. Vigiers is the Father thereof, he having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of Year 1786 and at several times since till the latter End of the Year 1788 at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant, and that the said Jeremiah Vigiers is the only true and real Father thereof.

Sworn this 13th: day of March 1789 before me } R Butler

Mary Cooper
