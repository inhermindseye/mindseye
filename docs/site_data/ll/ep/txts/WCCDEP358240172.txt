Saint Clement Danes Octr. 15th 1792.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  to wit William King aged 18 years and upwards upon his Oath Saith That he lived with Mr. James Stillman of Change Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Lamp lighter as an hired yearly Servant for one year at the Annual wages of Four Pounds four shillings that he quitted his Service about 3 years ago and hath not since been in service nor Rented any House or apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn before me this 15th. Octr. 1792 } R. Butler

his Willm King Mark
