Middx The Exaicon of Susanah Burton  Wife of James Burton Taken the 13th. Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. about Seven Years ago she Intermarryed with the above named James Burton who is an Irishman & never gained any Settlement in England, And further upon her Oath saith yt. she cannot give any Account of the Settlemt. either of her Father or Mother but yt. she was born in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . And this Examint. further saith yt. she never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife, that her said Husband is a Soldier & in Garrison at Port Mahone and haveing one Child. (to wit) Mary aged 3 Years & upwds. and being now being big & near her time is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before}
