Middx  Mary Pinckney Aged about Forty One Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Joseph Pinckney (Gone from her) with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of Burtley in the County of York about Eighteen Years ago Says her said Husband Rented a House in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. about three Months at the Yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds or thereabouts hath quitted said House about fourteen Years & hath not since Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she has three Children by her said Husband (to wit) Elizth Aged about Ten Years Joseph Aged about Six Years & Frances Aged about four Months.

Sworn the Day of Octr 1764 Before}
