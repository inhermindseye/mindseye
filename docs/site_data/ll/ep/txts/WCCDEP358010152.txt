Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Maketh Oath that Richard Chilest a Panper now in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Oath information this Examinants and which Information he believes to be true that he the said Richard Chilest rented & lived in a House in Drury Lane  in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly rent of twenty Pounds and thereby gained a settlement in the said Parish of Saint Giles and that he hath never done any that whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 13th. Octr. 1782 before D Walker Rapt: Clarke }

Benj: Newman
