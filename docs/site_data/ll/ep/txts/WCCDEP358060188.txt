Middx Isabella Ealing aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Ealing in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn London abt. 23 Yrs. ago and that her sd. husbd. is gone from her & she knows not where he is that the sd. Ealing rented a house of Twenty three pounds a Year in Butcher Row in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx & lived therein Seventeen Years and quitted the sd. house abt. 7 Years ago & has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 17th. 1751 before}
