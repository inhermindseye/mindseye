Middx  Robert Deeke aged about 40 Years upon Oath saith that he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant That his Father Robert Deeke Rented a House in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds or thereabouts & lived in the same Thirty Years as he has been informed by him and believes And after that Rented a House in Tothill Street in the Parish of St. Margt. Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds or thereabouts and lived in the same about two Years And this Examinant further saith that before his said Father Rented the said House in Tothill Street he this Examinant intermarryed with Elizabeth his Wife at the Fleet London and has by her two Children (to wit) Mary Aged fifteen Years and Ann aged upwards of four Years & being sick & Poor is not able to maintain himself & Family

Sworn the day of 1755 before}
