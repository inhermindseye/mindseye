Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Russell the wife of John Russell  mariner (who is now at Sea and absent from her) maketh oath that she Can't in any wife set forth or discover the Settlement of her Said husbands Parents or either of them But says that her Said husband has inform her & which information She believes to be true That he was born in Swan Alley in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex That he was brought up in the Sea Service that to her knowledge information or belief her said husband never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish Offices nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Birth That she was lawfully Married to her Said husband and that she hath a Child named Sarah Jane the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her Said husband.

Sworn this day of February 1770 before}

The Mark of Elizabeth Russell
