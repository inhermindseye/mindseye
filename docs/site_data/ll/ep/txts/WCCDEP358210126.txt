Westmr.  Joseph Stevens aged about 74 Years & upwards Upon Oath says he never was married and that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Rowland Linen draper in Bucklersbury in the Parish of St. Bennet Sherehog in the City of London for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds, quitted said Service about six Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 19th, day of January 1767 before} Fran Cary J Berradale

The Mark of Joseph Stevens
