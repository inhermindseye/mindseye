St. Clement Danes .

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit- Martha Roberts aged 74 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she intermarried with John Roberts her late Husband about 50 Years ago at the Fleet That her said Husband has been Dead about 33 or 34 years-That her Husband informed her and which Information she believes to be true that previous to his Intermarriage with her he lived as a Hired Yearly Servant at the Nays Head Public House in Featherstone Street in the Parish of St. Luke Old Street , that he was in such Service about 3 Years, and his Wages were about £6. per Annum that she never Know of any Settlement gained by her Husband in his Life Jane except as above nor has she since his Decease gained any Settlement to the best of her knowledge & belief

Sworn this th. day of May 1799 before us  Saml. Edwards Menter

her Martha Roberts Mark
