Middx  Eliz. Clatworthy upon Oath Saith she is the Wido. of Valentine Clatworthy decd. by whom she has one Child Valentine aged 18 Months and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd late husband was bound in the Parish of St. James at Taunton in the County of Somerset & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Augt. 3d. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizebth Clatworthy
