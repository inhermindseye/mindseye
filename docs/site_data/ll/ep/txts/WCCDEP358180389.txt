Middx  Mary Munceau  Wido . of Paul Munceau upon her Oath saith she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Cookson in Tavistock Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr.  of at the Yearly Wages of £5 for the Space of one Year & upwards being all that time a Widow & without Child or Children & hath left her sd. Service about One Year ten Months Says she hath not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 4th. Octr. 1745 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Mary Munceau
