Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster to wit}  Henry Day one of the Beadles of the parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of the Hannah Peck Who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that the said Hannah Peck never was married That she live as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Dives in Frith Street Soho in the parish of Saint Annes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Ten Pound Year That she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have Gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of August 1796 } Rupt: Clarke  Edw. Read

Henry Day
