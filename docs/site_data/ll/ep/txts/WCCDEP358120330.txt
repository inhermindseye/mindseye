Middx  Eliz Turner aged 45 years upon Oath Saith She never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Joseph Taylor a Sword Cutler in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year and upwards at the yearly wages of Four pounds that she Quitted the sd. Mr. Taylors Service about 19 years ago and has not since Rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn April 25th. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Turner
