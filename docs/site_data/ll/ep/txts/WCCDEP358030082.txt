Middx  Elizth; Knott  Wido . of Thomas Knott upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Thos. Knott at the Fleet London on the 26th. Day of Octr. about 22 Years since Says her sd. Husband Thomas Knott hath been dead about a fortnight as she hath been Informed & beleives Says the sd. Thomas Knott Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Septin Godden in the Parish of Kingston in the County of Surry  Cooper & after he was out of his said Apprentiship rented a house of Six pounds a Year in the said Parish of Kingston for four Years & upwards & paid his Share of all Parochiall Taxes & hath left sd. house about 16 Yrs. Says he never after rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 17th. 1745 before  Thos Lane

Eliza Knott
