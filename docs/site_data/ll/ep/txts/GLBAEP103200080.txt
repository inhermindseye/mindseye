Middlesex ss}  Rachael Peters  Singlewoman maketh oath that She never did any Act to gain a settlement for herself That her late Father John Peters deced died legally Settled in the Precinct of Saint Catherine That this Deponts late Mother Ann Peters after her said Fathers death removed with this Deponent into Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Where She rented a house of Ten Pounds a year & did not afterwards do any act to gain anothr settlemt She this Depont Continuing with her till the time of her sd Mothrs Death as part of her Family that She this Depont was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 15th day of December 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Rachael Peters
