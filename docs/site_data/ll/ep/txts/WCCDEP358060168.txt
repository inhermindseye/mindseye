Middx  Frances Brown upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband George Brown upwds. of 11 years since at Bloomsbury Church Middx and that her sd. husband whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant for one year and upwards with Mr Richardson in the Parish of Enfield Middx at the yearly Wages of £10 as she hath been Informed & believes and that her sd husband has not since he Quitted his sd. Service rented a house of Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 12th. 1751 before}
