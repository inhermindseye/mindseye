Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit William Smith aged about 42 years upon his Oath saith that about 18 years ago he Interm with Mary his late Wife (who has been dead about 4 y at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn that he hath 3 Children living by his said late wife vizt. Sarah aged about 16 years Cath: aged about 14 years and Elizth. aged about 5 all  are provided for that before his Intermarriage to his said late Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired yearly Servt. in the Capacity of Coachman with Perryn Esqr. who liv in Lincolns Inn New Square for 2 years & upwards at the yearly Wages of £14 that during his said Service he Slept over the Stables of the said Perryn Esqr. his master which was in Portugal Str in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx that he hath quitted his said Master Servi about 20 years ago since which time he has not lived as a hired yearly Servt. for one year while a Singleman or Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Febry. 1787 before me R Butler William Smith
