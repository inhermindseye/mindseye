Middx to wit Frances Spencer Aged upwards of Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she has lived with her present Master Mr. John Dringal  the Feathers Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year and a Quarter at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Jeremiah Taylor a Porter to Mr. Allanson Silk Mercer in St. Clements Church Yard is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in January last but the Particular day she does not now remember at at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Jeremiah Taylor and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 21st. day of June 1777 before me  Abrm Baley

her Frances Spencer Mark.
