Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Lee aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that about Eleven years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Lee her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom she hath Four Children living vizt. William aged about Nine years Susannah aged about Seven years James aged about Four years and Margarett aged about Two years That her said Husband was bound an Apprentice on the Second day of April 1767 to one Joseph Kingston of the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry  Waterman and Lighterman for Seven years that after he had served about Two years and an half of his said Apprenticeship he was Assigned over to one Nathaniel Thompson of Plough Court Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex to whom he served the remainder of his said Apprenticeship and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Augt. 1790  Wm Kitchiner

her Sarah Lee mark
