Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Cecilia Sloane aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith that about Twenty one years ago she Intermarried with John Sloane who has been abroad about Four years at the Parish Church of Saint Nicholas at Liverpool That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds that he Quitted said house about Nine years since which time he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Octr. 1797 before me } W Carter

Cecilia Sloane

H Day Beadle
