Middx & Westmr}  Ann Lewis aged about 23 Years upon Oath says She never was married lived a hired Yearly Servant for a Year Rented House or paid Taxes and that her Father William Lewis deceased Rented and lived in a Farm in the Parish of Dissert near the Town of old Radnor in South Wales and paid all Parish Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that one Mr. John Mc.Cullock a Merchant in great Trinity Lane in the City of London did beget the said Bastard Child or Children, he having had carnal knowledge of her Body in November last and at several other times since in the House of one Mr. Wicksted in St. Martins Le grand at one of which times he did beget her with Child of the Child or Children of which She is now pregnant and that the said John Mc. Cullock and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 29th. day of August 1767 before.} Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Ann Lewis
