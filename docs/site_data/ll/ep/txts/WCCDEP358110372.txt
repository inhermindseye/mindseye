Middx  Saml Major upon Oath Saith he was married to Elizth . his Wife at the Fleet London abt. 8 Yrs. ago and that before his intermarriage & whilst a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Cornchins at the Rose Tavern near Tamplebar in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for  Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £8 and has not since he Quitted Mr. Cornelius's Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for 1 year

Sworn Decr. 21st 1753 Before} Barth: Hammond

Samuell Major
