Westminster to wit Esther Newton Aged about 19 Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Ezexiel Atkins a Broker in Stanhope Street. in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for one Year and half at the Yearly Wages of £4. hath quitted her said Service about Six months ago and hath not sence she quitted the same ever Rented House or Paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant wth. Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born Says that her sd. Master Ezekiel Atkins had Carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the latter End of June or the beginning of July but the particular day she does not now remember and at Sevl. Other times Afterwards at one of which times he got her wth. Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and as afsd. and the sd. Ezekiel Atkins and no Other person did beget the same & that he his the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 20th day of January 1772 before} 

her Esther Newton Mark
