Middlesex ss}  Phebe Spencer the Wife of  George Spencer  maketh oath That She well knows a Child named Isabella White aged about four years and five months which Child was born of the Body of the Body of this Deponents Daur Isabella Brown in the house of one Mrs Good in Meeting House Alley in the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middlesex & Baptized & registred in the Parish Church there of this Deponent has been Credibly informed & verily believes The which Child is a Bastard by reason this Deponents said Daughter was never married as She has informed this Depont. & as this Deponent also verily believes and which child is likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 20th day of June 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Phebe Spencer
