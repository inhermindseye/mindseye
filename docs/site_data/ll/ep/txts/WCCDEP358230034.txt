Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Bathsheba Beard aged about 67 years upon her Oath saith that She lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Manlove Fishmonger in the Strand for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Nine Years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing Whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Janry. 1799 before me} Saml. Laward

her Bathsheba Beard Mark
