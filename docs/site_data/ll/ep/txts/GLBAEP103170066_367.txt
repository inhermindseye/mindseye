Middlesex ss}  Mary Ansley the widow of Wm. Ansley  mariner deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which She belives to be true that her said husband was by birth a Scotchman and this Deponent cannot Set forth or in any Wife discover Whethr. he was ever an Apprentice or ever lived as a hired Servant for a year or Whethr he ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or ever Served any parish office or ever paid any poors Rates or Kings Taxes or ever did any act to gain a Settlement in any part of England That She this Deponent before her marriage with her said husband & while a Singlewomen was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Peter Duffey near Gun Dock in the parish of St John Wapping in the sd. County Victualler at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one year & nine months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And that She this Deponent Since her sd husbands death hath not lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act (Save as aforesaid) to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this. 18. day of march 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands , Jasper Clarkes

Mary Ansley
