Middlesex ss}  Mary Thompson  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Knoffe a Victualler in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two years That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since Such her Servitude as aforesaid

Sworn this 16. day of February 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Mary Thompson
