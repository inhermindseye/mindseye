Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Catherine Parker who is ill Maketh Oath and saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Catherine Parker Rented and lived in a house near the Sheep Pens in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre within in the City of London for upwards of Twenty years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and that after she quitted said house she never did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 23d. day of March 1790 R Butler D Walker

James Talboys
