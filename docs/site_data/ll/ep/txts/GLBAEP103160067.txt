Middlesex ss}  John Carter maketh oath that in the year 1754 he lived in & rented a house in Darby Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

John Carter
