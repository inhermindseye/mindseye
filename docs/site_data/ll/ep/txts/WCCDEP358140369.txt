Middx.  Ann Judge aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Wm. Judge who has been dead abt. 7 years says that being a Widow & without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Mercer in New Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for One year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £4 and hath quitted that Service upwards of a Year and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn: this 9th. of May 1760 before me} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Judge
