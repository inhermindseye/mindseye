Middlesex, to wit, Elizabeth Gordon , Singlewoman , maketh Oath and Saith, That about Nine Years ago she was lived as a Servant by the Year to Robert Porrett , then of Burr Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , Gentleman , at the Yearly Wages Seven Pounds, and that she served the said Robert Porrett in Burr Street aforesaid, under such hired for the space of Six Months or thereabouts, who the said Robert Porrett removed from thence a House in Union Court , in the Parish of Peter le Poor in Allhallows on, London Wall in the City of London , and that s served the said Robert Porrett in Union Court aforesaid, under the hiring aforesaid for the space of Eight Months and upwards, when she quitted his Service That she hath not since she quitted such Service Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards, or lived as a Yearly hired Servant with any Person or Persons for the space of one Year or upwards, or done any other Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement, and that she never was Married And this Deponent further Saith, that she has two Children living begotten on her Body by one Richard Wylde of Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph

without Aldgate aforesaid, Shoemaker , to wit, William aged Four Years, baptised by the name of William Wylde , who was born in Fore Street , in the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate in the City of London , and James, baptised by the name of James Wylde , aged Two Years, who was born in Long Alley in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the said County of Middlesex , which said two Children are illegitimate and Bastards by reason that she never was Married to the said Richard Wylde And further saith, that being Poor and unable to provide for herself, she has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate aforesaid for relief, and hath been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 13th Day of April 1798 before us R Davies H Reynell

her Elizabeth Gordon Mark
