St. Clement Danes April 17th 1792.

Middlesex & Westminster }  Fulham Middx  (to wit) Charlotte Wickins the Widow of Jonathan Wickens deceased Upon her Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Jonathan Wickins on the 28th. day of March 1785, at the parish Church of St Ann Soho ; that her said Husband has been dead about six Months & that since his Decease, she hath nor intermarr'd with any person, rents Rented any House or Tenemt. of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds, Paid Parochial Taxes, or any other Rate, or done any other Act Matter or thing, whereby to acquire any settlement in her own Right: And Further this Deponent saith, That her said Husband informed her & which she verily believes to be true, that he did (before his intermarriage with this Examinant) live as an hired Yearly Servant, with & Mr. Wyndham, in the parish of Fulham ; in the County of Middlesex , for upwards of fourteen Months; and that he never after so living with the said Mr. Wyndham as aforesaid obtained any settlement whatsoever; to the best of his knowledge. And this Deponent saith, that she is incapable of maintaining herself, without Assistance.

Sworn the 17th. day of April 1792 . before us Sampn Wright M Addington

Charlotte Wickins
