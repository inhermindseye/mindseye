Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Jane Price  Singlewoman aged twenty four Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married Rented any House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year was bound or ever Served as an Apprentice or ever did any Act Matter or thing whereby to acquire any Settlement in her own Right and further this Examinant on her Oath saith that this Examinants late father Thomas Price did in his Life time Rent and live in a House in Whites Alley in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds and upwards for more than forty Days and this Examinant also lived with him aspect of his family there and this Examinant does not know or believe that her said Father nor this Examinant Further since his Dece have either of them acquired any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. Decr- 1790 before us Armstrong D Walker

her Jane Price Mark
