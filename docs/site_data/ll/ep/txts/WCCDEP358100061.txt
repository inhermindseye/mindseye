Middx  Elizth Benbough Aged about 34 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she Served Six Years the first Part of her Appren ticeship to one John Taylor a Tickett Porter in George Alley  Fleet Markett in the Parish of St. Brides otherwise Bridgett London soon after which her Master Dying her late Mistress gave her up her Indentures And further says that she hath gained no other Settlement other than by living a hired yearly Servant to her late Mistress Daughter in the aforesd. Parish of St. Brides upwards of One year at the Yearly of Wages of Three Pounds Eighteen Shillings payable Weekly and after quitting her sd. Service she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement by living as a hired yearly Servant for One Year or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 11th. day of April 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth Benbough mark
