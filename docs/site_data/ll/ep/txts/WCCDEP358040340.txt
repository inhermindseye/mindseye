Westmr.  Petty Cale upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house near Whitechapell Church in the Parish of St. Mary at Whitechapell for Nine Years or thereabouts at the Yearly. Rent of Eight Pounds and nine pounds and paid his proportion towards the Poors Rate of the sd. Parish several to that he quitted the above house about four Years ago and has not since rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 25th. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

Petty Keale
