Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Mills Aged about forty Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of John Mills who has been dead about Twelve Months by whom she hath five Children now living Namely Jane Aged about fourteen Years Ann Aged about Thirteen, John Aged about Eleven Elizabeth Aged about Six and William Aged about Three Years Says before her said Husbands Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Thorpe a Poulterer in Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds besides Diet and Lodging And this Examinant further Says that about five Months after her said Intermarriage with her said Husband he quitted his said Service and went and lived as a hired Yearly Servant with the Bishop of Worcester in the Capacity of a Cook in Brook Street in the Parish of  Saint George Hanover Square in the said County of Middlesex and lived with him upwards of five Years at the Yearly Wages of forty Pounds And after he quitted that Service he went to live as a hired Yearly Servant with one Walters Esqr. in the afsd. Parish of St. George Hanover Square and lived with him about fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of fifty Pounds and then her said Husband died And this Examinant says that since

her said Husbands Death she never rented House Paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 7th. day of Apil in the Year of our 1769 } Tho Lane

The Mark of Ann Mills Witness Petr Sidebotham
