Westmr- to wit Elizth- Ryland aged about twenty three Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Joseph Ryland Rented and lived in a house in Holles Street Clare Markett in the parish of St. Clement Danes for three Years at the Yearly Rent of £22 hath quitted the said house about 16 Years ago and after quitting the same he never Rented house paid Taxes or gained any or- subseqt- Settlemt. And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Stephen Fish a Servant to the Revd - Seymour Leake at Axley Hall near Stow Markett Suffolk is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of June but the particular day she does not now remember at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Stephen Fish and no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father throf

Sworn this 24th- Day of January 1778 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth- Ryland Mark
