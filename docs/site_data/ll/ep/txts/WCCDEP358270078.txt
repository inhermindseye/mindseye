Bastardy after Birth

St Clement Danes 10th September 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman's settlemt St Botolph without Bishopsgate }  Childn birth Child's birth  Reputed father (to wit) Elizabeth Lambert  Singlewoman singlewoman aged 29 years or thereabouts Upon Oath faith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly with Mr. Gloves in Houndsditch in the parish of St Botolph without Bishopsgate London about two years and an half, at the Annual Wages of Six Guineas; that she quitted that Service about Nine years ago & hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement and this Deponent father Farther saith that on the Ninth day of October 1783 she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child named at the House of a Mrs James in Milford Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which Child hath since been Christened (at the parish Church of St. Clement Danes ) by the Names of Sarah Lambert that one John Shephard who was at that formerly Worked at a Coal Wharf at the botto of Milford Lane aforesaid is the only real un truef true father of the said Child of which She was so delivered  he and no one & he having had Carnal knowledge of this of this Deponents Body about a year previous to the Birth of the sa Child and several times in the in believes betwixt such Connection and Birth and that he did by the said Child thereof

Sworn the 10th day of September 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Lambert
