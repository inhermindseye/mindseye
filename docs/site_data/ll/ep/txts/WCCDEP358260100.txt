Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Hatton aged about 3 years upon her Oath saith that about Seventeen years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Munday Hatton her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Ann aged about 15 years That her said Husband Rented a Shop and Parlour in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the Weekly Rent of Ten Shillings and Six pence That he hath quitted said Apartments about Seven years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Janry. 1796  Russl: Clarke M Addington

Hannah Hatton
