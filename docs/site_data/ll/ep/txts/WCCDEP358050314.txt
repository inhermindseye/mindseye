Middx  Mary Davis aged about 22 years upon Oath saith she never was married or lived with any person as an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year That her father Henry Davis deced Lived in and rented a house in Tottenham Court Road in the parish of St. Pancras in the County of Middlesex of the yearly rent of Ten pounds for  several Years & there dyed And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St Clemt. Danes (where she now Lodges) she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that James Hyde now a Servant to

Payne Esqr . at No. 4 in the Kings bene Walks did beget the sd. bastard Child or Childn.

on her body the said James Hyde having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant several times in the month of March or April last at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said James Hyde and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Septr. 12th. 1750 before H Fielding

The mark of Mary Davis
