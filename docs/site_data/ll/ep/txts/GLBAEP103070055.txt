Middx ss.  Catherine Dawkins , Widow of Thos. Dawkins Deced Maketh oath That That about fourteen Years ago her husband built Several houses in Queen Street in the Parish of st. Mary Rotherhith in ye County of Surry In one of which houses he lived for abt. Two Years & paid all Parish Taxes and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And Saith that her sd. husband did not afterwards gain any other Settlement to the knowledge or belief of her this Depont.

Sworn this first day of January 1752  Richd. Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

Catherine Dawkins
