Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  never Sworn to wit Lucy Hughes aged about 34 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House a paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr Fawcett a Habersdasher on Saint Margaretts Hill in the Parish of Saint Saviours Southwark for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted said Service about 2 years since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 11th. day of June 1785 }
