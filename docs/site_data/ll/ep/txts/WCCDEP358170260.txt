Middx.  Thomas White upon his Oath saith that in the Month of June about Fourteen Years ago he intermarried with his present Wife  Sarah and that abt. 30 Years since this Examinant being a Singleman & not having any Child or Children lived a Yearly hired Servt . with St. Simeon Stueart in the Parish of Hartley Morgett in the County of Hants for the Space of Five Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds That since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beliefe

Sworn the 19th- Augt.1742 before Houlson

Thomas White
