Middlesex ss.  Thomas Friend maketh Oath that his late Father Thos. Friend deceased has informed him that he was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years, to David Wilson who then lived at the Corner of Mouse Alley Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Carpenter & Joiner And that he duly served the whole of his said Apprenticeship with his said Master there, and never did any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement, That he this Deponent was born in the said Parish & Baptized at Aldgate Church , And further for himself saith that he has never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the year or Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement in his own right That he was lawfully Married at Shoreditch Church about Fifteen Years ago to one Elizabeth How , but where She now is he cannot tell, She not having lived with him for Several Years

Sworn this Day of 1797 before}
