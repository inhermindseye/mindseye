Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Francis Stephens aged about 29 years upon his Oath Saith That on or about the 16th day of April 1799 he intermarried with Ruth his present Wife at the parish Church of Stoke near Gosport That while Single he lived as a hired yearly Servant for near two years with one Mr Fryer Grocer corner of Mount Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex and Liberty Westminster at the yearly Wages of four pounds that he hath left the said Service about nine years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for twelve Months or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th day of July 1800 before us} John Collick  Benjm. Gee

Francis Stean .
