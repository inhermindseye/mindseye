St Clement Danes 28th May 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary le bone  (to wit) George Clarke aged 15 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he served three years five Months and upwards under as an Apprentice (under Indenture) with Mr James Murrell  Hair dresser of the in the parish of St. Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex and Lodged in his said Masters House within the same parish during such service that he was disharged from his Apprenticeship on or about the twentieth day of App April last and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the th 28th day of May 1793 before Us N Bond Mitchell

George Clarke
