Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Juliana Ryan aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith That about 14 years ago she Intermarried with Michael Ryan her present Husband in Ireland by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Christiana aged about Two years and Five Months That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Change Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Eighteen Guineas that s he said Husband hath quitted said house about Twelve Months since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that her said Husband left this Examinant last Saturday week and is as she believes gone to Ireland and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself and her said Child without Assistance

Sworn this 9th- day of Augt. 1796 } J Reeves

her Juliana Ryan Mark
