Westmr.  Elizth- Stokes aged abt. 25 Years upon her Oath saith that she was married to Usher Stokes on the 21st- Day of March 1734 he being at that Time an Apprentice to one Mr. Bradley a Baker in the Strand & served Six Years & upwards part of his Apprentiship with the sd. Mr. Bradley & after his Death with his Wido. & that her sd. Husband inlisted himsellfe in his Majestys Service & is now at Giberalter that neither her Husband nor she ever rented £10 a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as hath to her knowledge been informed or beleife & being Sick is not able to provide for year herselfe

Sworne the 9th- Day June 1741 before me}

Elis: Stoakes
