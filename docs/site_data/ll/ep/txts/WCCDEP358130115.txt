Middx  John Bowman aged about 62 years upon Oath says he Intermarried with his present Wife Mary at Burford in Oxfordshire about the Year 1746 by whom he has five Children (to wit) Mary aged about Eight Years Elizabeth aged about six Years Milicent aged about four Years Charles aged about two Years and an Infant as yet unbaptized. Says that John Bowman his Father deceased Rented a House upwards of Twenty Pounds a Year in Bridges Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster & lived therein several Years and quitted the same about thirty eight Years since as he has been informed and verily believes And this Examinant says that he never rented a House of Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a yearly Servant

Sworn the 10th. Day of Febry. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

John - Bowman
