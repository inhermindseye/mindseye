Middx  Mary Spratt  Wife of Richard Spratt upon her Oath saith that before her Intermarriage with her present Husband  Richard Spratt she was the Widow of Richard Shephard with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn upwards of Five Years since that her said late Husband hath been dead about Two Years & before his Intermarriage with this Examinant lived a Yearly Servant with one Mr. Hobson a Distiller ever agt. Little Turn Style in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex for Three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds that the sd. Richard Shephard never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. to her knowledge or beleif that she hath one Child by the sd. Richard Shephard (to wit) John aged about 4 Years & being poor is not able to maintain him

Sworne 16th. May 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Spratt
