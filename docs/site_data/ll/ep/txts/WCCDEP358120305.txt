City of Rochester (to wit)

Philip Draycot aged abt. 35 Years upon his Oath Saith he was married to his Wife Rebecca at the Fleet London on the 28th. May 1751 by whom he has two Children (viz) Clement aged 3 Years & Mary aged 10 months And that he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to John Thorp a Butcher & lived 10th. him as his Apprentice in the Parish of Gadesby in the County of Leicester upwds. of three Years & then this Examint. and his master parted by Convent and he never was assigned over nor ever rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year and this Examint. further maketh Oath that he never was married to any other woman besides the above named Rebecca his present wife

Sworn March 10th 1755 before G Gordon Mayor

Phillip Draycott
