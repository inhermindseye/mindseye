Middx  Isabella Timson  Wido . of Joshua Timson upon her Oath saith that about 3 Yrs- since being at that Time a Widow & not having any Child or Children she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Longueville in Long Acre and in Chappell Alley in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £3..10s for the Space of one Year & upwards & that since she left her said Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworn the 13th- Novr. 1742 before} Tho Lane

Isabela Timson
