Westmr. to wit John Grummitt aged about Thirty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex in the Year 1763 and by whom he hath four Children Namely thomas Aged about Nine Years Elizabeth Aged about Eight Years William Aged about Six Years and Charles Aged about One Year & an half Says he Rented and lived in a House in Angel Court White hart Yard in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds hath quitted the said House upwards of Two Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 14th. day April 1773 before Us R Butler Sampn Wright

John Grummitt
