Middx to wit Phillis Hansteen aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath says she is the Widow  Hans Hansteen who died about Twelve Years ago with whom she Intermarred at the Parish Church of Saint James in the Year 1764 and by whom she hath no Child now living says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Mr. Rainer an Exciseman in Orange Street Leicester Fields in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields for one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. day of August 1779 before  R. Butler Sampn Wright

Phillis Hansteen
