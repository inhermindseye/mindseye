Middlesex ss}  Mary Ann Hoskins Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Frederick Franks in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County Victualler & Continud in such service under such hiring for several years That she has not since lived as a hired servant for the the Space of a year in any one Service Since nor was She ever Married nor hath she done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn the. 7. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Ann Hoskins
