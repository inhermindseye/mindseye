Middx  George Cowley aged 68 Years & upwards upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with his present Wife  Elizabeth aged abt. 60 Years on  or about the 22d. Day of June last at the Fleet London that he lived in & Rented a House in Petty France in the Parish of St. Margt.  in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £13. & lived in the same for the Space of 9 Months & hath left the same abt. 17 Years since which time he hath not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne March 3d. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

George Cowley
