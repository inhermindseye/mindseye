The Examination of Rachel Herbert  Singlewoman aged twenty four Years who was found wandring in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx having no Habitation or Place of Abode taken the 30th. Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that Her Father John Herbert Deceased was by Indenture bearing Date the 2d. Day of February 1686 bound an Apprentice to and served seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Sayer in the Parish of great Marlow in the County of Bucks Baskett Maker as appears by the Examination of Alice Herbert her Mother also Deceased taken upon Oath the 31st. Day of August 1736 before Thos. Lane and Peter Guerin  Esqr . two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the said County of Middx , And further saith that her said Father never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year, And this Examinant further on her Oath saith that when she abt. ten Years of Age she was put to one Mrs. Clare Bates in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes  to learn Plain Work but under what Agreement knows not save that she hath heard her Mother say her Mistress

was to have this Examinants Labour for her Teaching & during all the Time she was with the said Mrs. Bates this Examinant came home to her Father who found her Washing Lodging Board & Cloaths that she this Examint. never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintain herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas. Gronous

The Mark of Rachel Herbert
