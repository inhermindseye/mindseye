Middlesex ss}  John Howard maketh oath that in the year 1756 he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with mr Burrows who then kept the Beehive Alehouse in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at Certain Wages & Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of three years & upwards and has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes did one any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21 day of August 1787 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of John Howard
