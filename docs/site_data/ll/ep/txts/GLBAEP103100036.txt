Middlesex ss}  Rose Banks the Widow of John Banks deced maketh Oath that her Said late husband has informed her & which information She belives to be true That he was born in the Parish of Henley upon Thames in the County of Berks and that he was duly bound apprentice by Indenture to one Weller a Smith & Farrier in Bell Street in the Same parish with whom he served the whole of his apprenticeship there and by Means thereof as this Depont. is advised gained a legal settlement there and this Depont. Saith that to her Knowledge information or belief her Said late husband after his Sd. apprenticeship never lived as a hired servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish offices or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement nor hath this Depont. since her said late husbands death done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right to her Knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 23: day of August 1768 before  Chris: Scott  John Smart

Rose Banks Her Mark
