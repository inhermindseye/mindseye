Middlesex ss  Mary Ray  single woman maketh oath that She was born in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate in the said County & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived in any place as a hired servant for the Space of a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in her own right That her Father Served his Apprenticeship in the said parish & never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in any other Parish

Sworn this 6th day of January 1778 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Ray
