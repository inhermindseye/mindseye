Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  Catherine Roberts aged about twenty three or twenty four years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married, that she never lived as an hired yearly Servant for one Year in any one Place or did any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have gained a Parochial Settlement in her own Right And this Examinant saith that she is the Legitimate Daughter of Joshua Roberts of Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Potatoe Dealer by Elizabeth his Wife and that the said John Roberts hath Rented and lived in a House of upwards of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds in Clare Market aforesaid for four Years now past and still continues to Reside Here and further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith not she is now pregnant with a Child Children which is or are likely to be born a bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid and that John Foster of Long Lane  Smithfield  Plaisterer is the real and true Father of Such Child or Children whereof she this Examinant is to pregnant he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body some time in or about the Month of April last past and

at several times since at some or one of which times he the said John Foster did beget such Child or Children whereof she this Examinant is so Pregnant, and that he the said John Porter and no one else is the Real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 1 6th. Day of December 1796 before me} John Collicke

her Catherine Roberts Mark

Far. paid £3:3:0 down on the 13th. Janry. 1797 and gave a Joint Note of hand for 12 Guas more payable at 1 Gua & P mo. Release Exted
