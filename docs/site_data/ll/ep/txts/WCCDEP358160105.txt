Middx  The Exaicon of Margt. Johnstun  Wido . taken the 29th. day of Janry 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that She Rented a House in Charles Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . & lived in the same for the Space of three Years And further Saith that she paid Thirty five Pounds a Yeare for the sd. House and hath quitted the same abt. four Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that this Exaiant now Lodges in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & is Poor & not able to maintain herselfe

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before} Tho. Lane  Jas. Groneus

Margaret Johnstun
