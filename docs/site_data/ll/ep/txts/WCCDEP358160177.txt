Middx  The Examination of Mary Lloyd  Spinster Taken the 4th. Day of Octr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her Father Thos. Lloyd abt. years ago Rented & lived in a House of the yearly Rent of £30 for Sevll. years in Butcher row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx And yt. after the Death of her sd. Father her Mother Sarah Lloyd went & lived in a House the Back side of St. Clemt. Danes  for the space of 24 Years of the yearly Rent of £24, And this Examint. further upon her Oath saith that she never Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworn the Day & year above mentioned before}
