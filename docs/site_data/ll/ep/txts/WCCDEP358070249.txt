Westmr to wit Ann Row Aged about Twenty nine Years on her Oath says that she never was Married, Rented House, Paid Taxes, or ever Served any Apprenticeship, says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr Sowerby at the Crown in Little Bridges Street Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath Quitted her said Service about Three Months and hath not since she Quitted the same ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th day of March 1775 before R Butler 

The Mark of Ann Row
