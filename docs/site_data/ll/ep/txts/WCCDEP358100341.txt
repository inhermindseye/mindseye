Middlesex and Westminster  C passd. to St. Geo: Hanr. Square to wit Sarah Buck Aged about 22 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Thomas Buck her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex about four years ago Says that while her Husband was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Colonel Fitzroy in Hanover Square in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square for upwds. of Twelve Months  that since he quitted his said Place he has done no act matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere than as aforesaid

Sworn this 21st- day of Janry. 1785  Thos. Gilbert M Addington

Sarah Buck
