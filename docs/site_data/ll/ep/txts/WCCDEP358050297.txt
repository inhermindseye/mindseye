Middx 

Mary Arney aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith she Intermarried with Thomas Arney at Paneras Church in the last week of Lent 1750 and that the said Thomas Arney is now a Servant at the Rose Tavern Temple Bar and had at the time of then Marriage lived with Mr. White Banson who keeps the sd. Tavern as a Yearly Servant at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds for one Year and upwards

Sworn Augt. 17th. 1750 before}
