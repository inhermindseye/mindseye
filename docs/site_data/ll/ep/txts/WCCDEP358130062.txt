Middx  Gilbert Hall aged about 63 Years upon Oath says he lived a Yearly Servant upwards of a Year with one Mr. Pacey in Attorney at Law in Cecil Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes & quitted the same about fifteen Years since Says that he never was married and hath not Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a hired Yearly Servant since his his leaving the said Mr. Pacey's Service

Sworn the 7th. day of Octor. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Gilbert Hall
