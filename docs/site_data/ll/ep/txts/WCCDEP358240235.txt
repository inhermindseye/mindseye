Catherine Ward & two Children

St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St C D  Children  St C D  (Skeen died abt: 1791)  (to wit) Catherine Ward  singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She is aged about 18 45 years that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Green at the Seven Stones Spotted Dog in Carey Street Clements Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for a year & upwards at the Annual Wages of £5. that She quitted that service in or about the year 1782 or 1783. Sixteen or Seventeen years ago and never after lived as an hired yearly Servant or paid Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or otherwise obtained Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Deponent further saith that she now lodges at Mrs Mr Robert's's near the Fountain Deptford and has two illegitimate Children Namely Elizabeth aged Nine years on the 4th instant & Eleanor aged 2 years on the 16th day of May 1792 both born in the Licensed Hospital in Store Street in the County of Middlesex And saith that John Skeen a Hair Dresser who lived in the Savoy at the in the County of Middlesex & is now Dead is the only real and true father of the said Children And that She is unable to maintain herself and Children without assistance

Sworn the 27th. day of March 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Catherine Ward 's Mark
