Middx ss  Edward Catlin maketh oath that about forty Nine Years ago he was bound apprentice to William Down ham of ye Parish of St. Saviour Southwarke in the County of Surry  Cooper & duly Served his apprenticeship with him in ye sd. Parish And says he was never a hired Servant by the Year Nor rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year or Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subseqt. Settlemt: Since his apprenticeship to his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 12th day of June 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  Saml Gower

Edward Catlin
