Westmr.  Robt. Yeoman and Mary Thradgill severally make Oath and Say and first the said Robt. Yeoman upon Oath saith that about 40 Years ago he Intermarried with his late Wife Isabella Yeoman in the Parish Church of Christchurch in ye Liberty of St. Martin le Grand & that the said Isabella Yeoman dyed about three Years ago Says he was bound Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Thomas Pryor a Carpenter at Stratford in the Parish of Westham in the County of Essex and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

And this Examinant Mary Thradgill-for herself says she intermarried with the said Robt. Yeoman at the fleet London about Eleven Years age and soon after her said Intermarriage found that the said Robert Yeoman had another Wife liveing (to wit) Isabella Yeoman whom this Examinant afterwards saw and Conversed with and that the said Isabella Yeoman dyed about 3 Yrs. ago Says before her sd. Intermarriage with the said Robert Yeoman and whilst a Singlewoman she lived a Yearly Servt. with Captain Kent in Redmaid Lane in the

Parish of St. George in the East in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for one Year and upwards and has not since she quitted her said Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief Says she hath a Child by the said Robert Yeoman (now at home with her) viz. Mary aged upwards of Ten Years who was born at Mr. Tindals in Grosvenor Mews in the Parish of St. George  Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn July 12th. 1749 before} Jas: Water

Robert Yeoman

The mark of Mary Thradgill
