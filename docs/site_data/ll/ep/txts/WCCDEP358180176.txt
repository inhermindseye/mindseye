Middx  Avis Jordan aged abt. 70 Years Wido. of Peter Jordan removed from the Parish of Alhallows Staining London by Order Dated 21st. March Instt. upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her sd. late Husband  Peter Jordan abt. 30 Years since who hath been gone from her near 27 Years & hath not heard of him since therefore apprehends him to be dead That the sd. Peter Jordan lived in & rented a house in Butcher Row in the parish of St Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of £20. for the Space of 3 Years & since he left the sd. house hath not (nor hath this Examint. rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 22d. March 1743 before}
