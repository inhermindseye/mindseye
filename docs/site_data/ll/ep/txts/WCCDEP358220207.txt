Westmr. to wit Mary Whittey Aged about Sixty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Whittey with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrews about Thirty Years Ago Says that her said Husband lived and Rented a House at Hatton Wall in the said Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middx for Three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds hath quitted the said House about Twenty Three Years ago and after her said Husband quitted the same he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husbands Death ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 7th. day of Decr. 1770 before Us} R Butler 

her Mary Whittey Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
