Middx ss  Elizabeth Muskett  Singlewoman Maketh oath that about four years ago she was pound apprentice to Mr. Thomas Vining a Cordwainer  of the parish of St. Botolph with Aldgate in ye County of Middx with whom she lived one year in the said parish of Aldgate and then her Master removed into Smiths Alley in ye parish of St. Olave in Southwark in Surry where She lived with her Master about half a year and this Depont. sd. Master then removed to the New Way in the parish of St. Thomas in Southwarke in ye County of Surry where she lived with her said Master about half a year And then her sd. Master put her to Service to Mrs. Elizabeth Bishop who Keepta Chandlers Shop in ye sd. parish of St. Thomas where she lived abt. a year and then returned to her sd. Master in the sd. parish of St. Thomas and lived with him again her about the space of half a year and then her sd. Master again put her to Service to a Cook in ye sd. parish of St. Olave in Southwarke  with whom this Dept. lived five weeks and 2 days or thereabts. & then She returned to her sd. Master and he refused to take her in And this Depont never lived with him afterwards And this Deponent has not Since gained any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 13 day of May 1754 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

her Elizabeth Muskett Mark
