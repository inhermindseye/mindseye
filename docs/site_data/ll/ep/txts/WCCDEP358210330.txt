Westmr.  Rebecca Merchant Aged about Fifty five Years on her Oath Says She was never Married Says She lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs: Mary Vernon  Staymaker in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr: upwards of a Year and Three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service about five Months and hath not Since Rented a Tenement of Ten Pounds by the Year paid any Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant with any other Person

Sworn the 12th. Day of Novr. 1768 Before W Kelynge

Rebecca Merchant
