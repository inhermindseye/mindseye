Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Sweet aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with a Mr. Danson in Cooks Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Four years at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Eighteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 29th. day of July last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of John That one Nathaniel Pattram a Shoemaker who lives at a Place called Till just beyond Bristol is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of where she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Nathaniel Pattram and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of Septr. 1790 } Wm Kitchiner

her Elizabeth Sweet Mark
