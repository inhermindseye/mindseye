Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Watson the wife of Thomas Watson maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with one Mr Wheeler a Cabinet maker in the Parish of St Margaret in Reading in the County of Berks & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband.

Sworn this. 12. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Watson
