Middx  Rebecca Searle Aged about 32 Years Upon Oath Says That on the Twenty Fifth Day of February 1750 She was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr Langman in Little Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been baptized by the Name of Rebecca Magdalin and is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same Says One John Saunders  Gentlemen did beget the said Female Bastard Child on her Body he the said John Saunders and this Examinant liveing together as Man and Wife and that he the said John Saunders is the true and Real Father of the said Female Bastd. Child.

Sworn the 5th Day of July 1763 before} Paul Vaillant  Fran: Bedwell

Rebecca Searle
