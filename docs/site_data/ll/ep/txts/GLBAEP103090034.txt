Middlesex ss  Robert Harris Maketh oath that divers Years ago his late Father Charles Harris deced Rented a house of Six Pounds a year in Maes Pond in the Parish of Saint Olave in Southwarke in the County of Surry & lived in the same Many Years & Carried on therein the Business of a Shoemaker And this Depont. Saith that during such his Fathers Residence in the Said house he hath often seen him Pay the kings Tax for the sd. house and Verily believed he was Charged in the kings Tax Books for the same and that by means thereof his Said Father gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont. doth not know nor was ever informed or Can Setforth whether his said Father ever did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And this Depont. Saith that he never lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever was an Apprentice Nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor ever served any Parish offices nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes Nor ever did any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in his own right And that he hath a Daughter named Elizabeth Aged about Nineteen years the lawful issue of this Deponent by his late wife  Judith deced & which said Elizabeth hath not as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 16th: day of April 1766 before  Chris, Scott R Pell

Robert Harris
