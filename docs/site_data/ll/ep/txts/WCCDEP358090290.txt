Middx  to wit William Pinnick Aged about Twenty Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House Paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship Says says he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Higgins an Oilman in Pater Noster Row in the Parish of Saint Gregory in the City of London for Three Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds hath quitted his said Service about Three Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever gained any other Settlemt. by living as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th. day of Febry. 1782 before}
