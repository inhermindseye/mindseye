Middlesex ss}  John Holmid maketh oath that he Can give no Account respecting the Place of Settlement of his late Parents or either of them That he has been informed by his Grandmother & which he believes to be true that he was born in Church Yard Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County That he this Depont never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound an Apprentice nor ever rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish Office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 17 day of December 1784 before R Davies John Holmid
