Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Pitts aged about 43 years upon his Oath saint that he lived as a hired yearly servant with one Peneston Topper who kept the sign of the Roya Hospital at Chelsea in the Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea in the County of Middlesex for Fifteen Month and upwards at the yearly wages of seven Guineas that he hath quitted said service about seven years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Febry.1790 J Barnfather Edward Gray

John Pitts
