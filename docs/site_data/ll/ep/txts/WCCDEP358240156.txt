Mary Forrest & Edward James Darby

St. Clement Danes Septr. 7th. 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Mary Forrest  Singlewoman aged 37 years on thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Thompson Porter at the of the New Inn at the Lodge belonging to the same for one year and upwards at the Annual Wages of Eight Guineas that she quitted that Service about three or four years ago and hath not Since lived as an hired yearly servant for twelve months togeth together Rented any House or or Apartment of £10 a year has or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief that she hath one illegitimate Child Named Edward Edward James Darby born on the 5th August 1791 . that one Edward Darby who has lately left her is the father thereof And that this Deponent is unable to support herself and Child without assistance

Sworn the 7th day of Septr. 1792 . before me } R Butler

Mary Forrest
