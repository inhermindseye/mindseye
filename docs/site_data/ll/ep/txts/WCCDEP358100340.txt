Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears St. C. D . A to wit Hannah Watson aged about 33 years on her Oath says she Intermarried never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Thornhill at the White Lyon  Vere street for upwards of Twelve Months hat at the yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about 14 years since which time she has gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid ago Says that she has an Illegitimate Child living by one John Stevens who was born in Old Pye Street in the Parish of Saint John the Evangelist Westminster

Sworn this 17th day of Janry. 1785 D Walker J Beete

her Hannah Watson mark
