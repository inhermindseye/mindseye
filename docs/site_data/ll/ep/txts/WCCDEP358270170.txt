Indexed GG & corrected} (2d Examination) Desertion

St Clement Danes January 16th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  desertion (to wit) Sarah Glover aged 24 years or thereabouts Wife of John Glover who hath deserted her upon Oath saith that he was Now Married to John Glover at the parish Church of St Mary le Strand in or about the Month of October last that she hath one Child by her said Husband now 1792 that she hath no Children by her said Husband now living that she hath been informed and verily believes that her said Husband served greatest part of the Term of his Apprenticeship under Indentures with Mr Scott Taylor in Arundel Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that he left his said Master before the expiration of his Apprenticeship without leave and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief that she is become chargeable unable to support herself without assistance and has likely to become been chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes being left and deserted

Sworn the 16th day of Januay 1794 before me} Wm. Kitchiner

Sarah Glover 's Mark

Witness Garrett
