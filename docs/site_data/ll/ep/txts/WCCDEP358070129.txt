Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah White Aged upwards of Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she was born the Parish of Battle in the County of Sussex where her Father Rents and lives in a House and has so done for many Years before this Examinant was born as she has often heard her said Father say and never gained any other subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children whe is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one James Glascows a Chairman who Rents a House in Kings Court Russell Street had Carnal Knowledge of her Body same time in October last but the Particular day she does not regular at the House of one Cockran the Sign of the Swan in Gavel Lane & at several times after at his the said Glascows House at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Glascows and are other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 8th. day of Septr. 1773 before  Tho. Kynaston

her Sarah White  Mark
