Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Sullivan aged about 34 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was married never bound apprentice never lived at a hired yearly Servant for twelve Months or done any act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a  Settlement in her own right That she does not Know her Fathers Settlement That she hath been Informed which Information she verily believes to be true That she was born at the Angel Inn in the Parish of Saint Mary Guildford in the County of Surry

Sworn this 13th day of Decr 1800 before us } Saml Edwards  John Collick

her Mary Sullivan Mark

Witness B Newman
