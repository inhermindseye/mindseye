Westmr. to wit Mary Carletty Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Elizth Mackey in Naked Boy Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One Year & a Quarter at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service ever since Christmas 1771 and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Charles Cornhill a Goldbeater who now works with one Mr. Ealeys a Goldbeater in Shoe Lane in the Parish of St. Brides London is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of January last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Charles Cornhill and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1773 before W Kelynge

her Mary Carletty Mark
