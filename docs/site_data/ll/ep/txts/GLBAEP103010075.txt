Middlesex, to wit, Jane Hughes , Singlewoman , Maketh Oath and Saith, that some time in the Year One Thousand Seven Hundred and Eighty Nine, or was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the Year with Mrs . Mary Smith of No. 2 , Kendall Place , in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth , in the County of Surry , at certain Yearly Wages, and continued such Service under such hiring for the space of Fourteen Months or thereabouts, that she has not since she quitted her said Service lived as a hired Servant by the Year for the space of one Year together, or done any Act to her knowledge or believed to gain a subsequent Settlement in any Parish Precinct or Place whatsoever

Sworn this 31st. Day of Octr. 1791 . before R Davies Rob Smith Jane Hughes
