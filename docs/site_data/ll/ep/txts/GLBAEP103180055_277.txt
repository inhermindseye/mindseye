Middlesex ss}  Jane Brogen  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years ago She rented a house (after the death of her late Father James Brogen deced) of the Yearly Rent of Five pounds in Vinegar Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has nor Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or lived Since as a hired Servant for a year nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6 day of August 1778 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Jane Brogen
