Westmr.  Wm: Owen aged about 35 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Mary at Islington in the County of Middx about Ten Years ago by whom he has two Children, William aged about Eight Years and Elizabeth aged about five Years says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Edward Northey Esqr. in Hanover Square in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Eleven Guineas and after he quitted said Service Never Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 5th. Sep. 1765 before} Paul Vaillant  Thos: Balack

The Mark of Wm. Owen
