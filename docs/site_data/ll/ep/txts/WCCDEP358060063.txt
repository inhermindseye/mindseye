Middx  Margt: Obrian upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Jno. Obrian (now in Ireland ) with whom she intermarried about 12 Years ago and that her said husband and this Examinant are both Natives of Ireland and never gained a Settlemt. in England that the said John Obrian Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Carey a Shoemaker in Patrick Street in the Parish of St Nichs . in Dublin in Ireland and that she hath one Child Mary aged abt 8 Yrs.

Sworn Janry 28th 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Margt. Obrian
