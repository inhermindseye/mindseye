Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth White  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago She was duly bound Apprentice by Indres by the Parish officers of Hadley in the County of Suffolk to Samuel Underwood of the Parish of St Mary Hill in the City of London Fishmonger & Served the whole of her Apprenticeship in the said Parish & afterwards rented a Shall in the Same Parish at the Rent of Ten Shillings per Week & Continued in the occupation thereof for more than a year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or paid any Poore Rates or Kings Taxes nor was She ever married nor to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain anothr Settlement

Sworn this. 7. day of July 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Elizabeth White
