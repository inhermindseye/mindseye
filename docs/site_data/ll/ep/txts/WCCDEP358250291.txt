Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Weston aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Simpkin at the Crown and Anchor Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Nine Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about fourteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Ann Weston Mark
