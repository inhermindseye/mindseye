Middlesex to wit Mary Cook Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Strutt at the Hartichoak in Clare Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for several Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath Quitted her said Service About Twelve Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes (she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) And further says that one Leonard King a Journeyman Taylor who lodges at No.3 in Bayley Court  Bell Yard & who Uses the Hartichoak in Clare Street is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her Mothers Lodgings in Holbourn Street  Clare Markett sometime in the Month of June last but the particular Day she does not now remember & at sevl different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the sd Leonard King and other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of Decr. 1783 before  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Cooke
