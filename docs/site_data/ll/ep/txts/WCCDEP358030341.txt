Westmr. 

Thos. Adams upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant whilst a Singleman with Mr. Wm. Finch at Slys hill in the Parish of Aldingham in the County of Hertford for the Space of two Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £7 Meat Drink Washing and Lodging Says he hath not since he left the said Mr. Finch's Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif Says he hath one Child viz Jno. aged abt. 7 Yrs. who was born at Illstree in Middx.

Sworn Febry 14th: 1746 Before
