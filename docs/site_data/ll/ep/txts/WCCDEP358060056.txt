Middx  Eliz: Blakeman upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of John Blakeman deceased and that her sd. late husband rented a house of £22. by the Year in Exeter Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for sevl. Years & there dyed and this Examinant commenc'd Tennt. in the sd. house at Michas last and continues still to live in and Rent it

Sworn Janry 17th. 1750 before}
