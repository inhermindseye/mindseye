Middx  Elizabeth White aged about 20 Years upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman & lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mrs. Stokes in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £3 & hath left her sd. Service upwds. of 2 Yrs. Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mrs. Stokes Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one James Lydall  Victualler did begett the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her Body she this Examinant living with the said James Lydall & as a Servant at the White horse in White horse Yard in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes where the sd. James Lydall became acquainted with her & had carnall knowledge of her Body

on the First Day of March last & at several other times since at one of which times the sd. James Lydall gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. James Lydall & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne Decr. 4th. 1744 before} Thos D Neil

The mark of Eliz. White
