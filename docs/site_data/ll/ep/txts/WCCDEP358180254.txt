Middx  Alice Clark Aged about 36 Years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her present Husband  Edward Clark on the 16th. Day of January about Twenty Years since at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & Whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Woodyer at the Shing Arms on Ludgate hill in the Parish of St. Bridgett vulgarly St. Brides London for the space of 4 yrs or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds & hath left his sd. Service abt. 18 Yrs. since which time the sd. Edwd. Clark hath not Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says the said Edward Clark  went a Sailor on Board the Rupen Man of Warr & having 2 Children (to wit) Ann aged 12 Yrs. or thereabts. & Alice Aged 4 Yrs. or thereabouts & being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworne Novr. 26th. 1744 Before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Alice Clark
