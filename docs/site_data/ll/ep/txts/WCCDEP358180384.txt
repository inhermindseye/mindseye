Middx  Mary Harford  Wife of Jno. Harford gone from her upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. Husband at the Fleet London on or abt. the 6th. Day of March upwds. of 15 Years since Says the sd. Jno. Harford Served Seven Years Apprentiship with John Mastors in Stall Street in the Abbey Parish in Bath in the County of Somerset & after he was out of his time rented a Shop of £15. a Yr. in same Street for the Space of two Years or thereabts. Says her sd. Husbd. never after Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 30th. 1745 before Wm: Jones . Mary Harford
