Middx.  Eliz: Hussey aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath says she never was married, says she was bound Apprentice to and served three Years apprenticeship with Mr. Mariot Haberdasher and Milliner in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx. abt. thirty Years ago, and that the Sum of forty Pounds was given with her as a Consideration says she never lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year or Ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 27th. day of Jany. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Hussey
