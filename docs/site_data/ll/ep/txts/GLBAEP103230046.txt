Middlesex ss}  Ann Millard the widow of Isaac Millard deced maketh oath that her said late husband heretofore rented a house in Sun yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and during Such his Residence therein he was Charged to and Paid Kings Taxes for the same and did not afterwards do any act to gain a subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Depont since his death done any act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 9. day of May 1787 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Ann Millard
