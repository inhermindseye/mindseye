Middlesex ss}  John Finch maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Michael Babba  Carpenter in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the sd County & Continued in such Service under such hiring for the Space of two Years & during Such time lodged in his Masters house there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere not rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Elizabeth & a Child named Joanna aged about a year & half the lawful Issue of this Depont by his said Wife to whom he was lawfully Married

Sworn this. 22d. day of Novemr 1783 before J. Hawley P Greene

The Mark of John Finch
