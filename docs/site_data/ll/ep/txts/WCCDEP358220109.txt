Middx  John Connisby Aged about Sixty Two Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his now Wife at Lynn in Norfolk about Thirty Nine Years ago Says he was bound Apprentice to one Richard Hine of Greyhound Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Taylor and served five Years and upwards the first part of his Apprenticeship when this Examinant and his said Master parted and after he parted with his said Master he never Rented a Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 4th. day of Janry 1770 before me W Kelyngs

John Counisby
