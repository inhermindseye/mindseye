Middx  Jane Hoare aged about 30 Years Upon her Voluntary Oath says she is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England and that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex where she now lodges she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born, says that Wm: Spence who works with a Butcher in Claremarket in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes had carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the Month of September 1764 and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Wm: Spence and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn 8d. June 1765 before} Thos Kynaston

Jane Hoare
