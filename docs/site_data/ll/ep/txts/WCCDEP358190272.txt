Middx  Elizabeth Perne aged about 33 Years Wife of Edward Perne who is gone from her says She intermarried with her sd. Husband about four Years since and that he Served above five Years of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Lewis a Peruke maker in St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields when his said Master died as She has been informed by her said Husband and believes Says that he never Rented a House or paid any Taxes as She verily believes Says She hath two Children by her said Husband namely William aged about four Years and Edward aged about Ten Months

Sworn Septr. 10th. 1762 before}
