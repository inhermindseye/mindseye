St Clement Danes August 7th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Giles in the fields  Children  (to wit) Hannah Winter aged 35 years or thereabouts Widow of James Winter deceased Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her pres late Husband James Winter at the parish Church of St Giles in the Fields on the 9th day of August in the year of our Lord 1779 that her said Husband since their said Marriage Rented and Resided in an House in Parker Street Drury Lane in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the said County of Middlesex for about five years at the annual Rent of £20 that he quitted that House about five Years ago an and never after during his Life Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds or year or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief that he died on the 21st. day of June last that she hath not since been Married or obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right that she hath two Children by her said Husband Namely Thomas born on the 25th day of December 1786 and Jeremiah aged three years on the 17th day of December 1792 And that She is unable to maintain herself & family without Assistance

Sworn the 7th. day of August 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner  Edwd: Naish

H Winter
