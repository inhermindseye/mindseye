Middx  Martha Webb  Widow of William Webb who dyed on the 3d. Day of Novr. last upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the said Wm. Webb about fourteen Years since in the Parish Church of St. Mary at Whitechappell Says her sd. late Husband lived in & rented a house in Saint John Street in the Parish of St. Sepulchers in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and lived therein One Year and a Quarter & Paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says her said late Husband Wm. Webb never after legally Rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath her Childn. Mary aged abt. 8 Yrs & Wm. aged abt. 3 Yrs. & that she was Removed from the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the County of Middx to the sd. Parish of St. Sepulchers on or abt. the 6th. Day of May last which sd. Order of Removal was not appear

Sworn 21th. Decr. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Martha Webb
