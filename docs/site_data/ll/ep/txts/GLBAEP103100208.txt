Middlesex ss  Sarah Cross the widow of William Cross deced maketh That her Said late husband has informed her That he was born in Pescott Street in the Parish of Windsor in the Co of Berks and that he also duly served his apprenticeship there with his late Father John Cross also deced who by Trade a Cordwainer And this Deponent Saith That sh hath been lately Credibly informed & verily believes the same to be true That the Said Street Called Pescott Street where her s late husband served his apprenticeship is a Street which adjoins to Windsor but his in & is part of the Parish of Cluer near Windsor in the County of Berks and this Depo farther Saith That to her knowledge information or belief her said late husband never rented a house of the yearly Re or Value of Ten Pounds or served any Parish offices nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did an Act to gain a settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship hath she this Depont. done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself since her said husband Death

Sworn this 29th day of January 1770 before  Chris: Scott  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Sarah Cross
