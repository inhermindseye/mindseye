Middx  Thomas Holder aged abt. 32 Yrs. upon his Oath saith that he served 6 Yrs. & upwds. the Latter part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Lucas Scowerer in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes & hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship 7 Yrs. & upwds. Since which time he never rented ten pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne Decr. 27: 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

Thomas Holder
