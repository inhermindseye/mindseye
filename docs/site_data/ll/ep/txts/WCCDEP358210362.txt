Westmr:  Mary Whitely Aged about Thirty four Years on her Oath Says She never was Married nor ever Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her late father John Whitly who was by Trade a Baker and who has been dead about Seven Months has often Informed her and which Information She verily believes to be true that he Rented and lived in a House many Years in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds or thereabouts and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Thirty Years and never after Rented house paid Taxes to the Knowledge or behalf of this Examinant

Sworn this 21st. Day of Febry 1769 Before me} Tho Lane

Mary Whitely
