Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Bex aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice that she was born at Verd in the Parish of Verd in the County of Kent and further saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 29th day of Augt. 1798 before us  Wm Goodenough W Carter

her Mary Bex mark
