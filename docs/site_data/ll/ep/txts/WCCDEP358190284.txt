Middx  John Hayes aged about 60 Years upon Oath says he lived a hired Yearly Servant whilst a Singleman with John Fielding a Brazier the corner of Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of £8 and quitted the same upwards of Thirty Years Says he intermarried with his Wife  Ann in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Thirty two Years since by whom he has one Child named John aged about Eleven Years Says he never rented a House or paid any Taxes

Sworn the 9th. day of October 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

John Hayes
