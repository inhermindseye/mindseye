Middx.  Geo: Cross aged abt. 21 Years upon Oath says he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to one Mr. Horne of Silver Street Bloomsbury Perukemaker and served him about four Years & a half the first part of his Apprenticeship and then was Assigned over to One Mr. Dixon of Bow Lane in the Parish of St. Thomas the Apostle in the City of London  Perukemaker  whom he served abt. one Year & a half says he then went from Mr. Dixon to Work with one Mr. Crutwell a Perukemaker at Hampstead in the Parish of Hampstead in the County of Middx. with whom he worked One Year or thereabouts says after he had lived with said Mr. Crutwell abt. three Months (his first Master) Mr. Horne came and demanded his Wages, and that he this Examinant paid him One Pound and Sixteen Shillings of lawful Money of Great Britain. says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn 20th. Novr. 1759 before} Barth: Hammond  Tho Lane

George Cross
