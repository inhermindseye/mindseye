Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster to wit Jane Hunt Aged about 43 years upon her Oath Saith that she never was married That she rented and lived in a Second floor at Mr Manning in Charles Street  Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden for Six Months or thereabouts at ten Shilling per Week that she hath quitted said apartment about Twelve years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this first day of October 1799 before us} John Collick  Benja Gee

Jane Hunt
