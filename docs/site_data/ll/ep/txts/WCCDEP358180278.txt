Middx  Susannah Mack aged about 20 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married & that has Father  Wm. Mach Deceased lived in & Rented a House in Hartshorne Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £10 for the Space of 10 Years & upwds. & left the sd. House about a Week before his Death Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she is and being Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born

Sworn Janry 19th. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

The mark of Sush. Mack
