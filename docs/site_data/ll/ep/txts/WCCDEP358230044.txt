Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Greenwood aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of June 1789 she Intermarried with Paul Greenwood her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom she hath Three Children living Vizt. Mary aged about Seven years William aged about four years and Charles aged about fourteen Months That her said Husband Rented and lived in part of a house in Grub Street in the Parish of Saint Luke Giles without Cripplegate in the County of Middlesex for Seven Years or thereabouts at the Weekly rent of Four Shillings that he hath quitted said Apartments about Three Years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of March 1799 before us G S Goodenough Saml. Edwards

her Ann Greenwood Mark
