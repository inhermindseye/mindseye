Middx.  Mary Mewstead aged abt. 59 yrs. upon Oath says she never was married Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. says her father Thos. Mewstead Rented a house of ten Pounds a yr. & upwards in allens Court in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. for sevl. years

Sworn the 13th. of June 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Mewstead
