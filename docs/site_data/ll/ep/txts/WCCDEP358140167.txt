Middx.  John Lewis aged abt. 26 Years upon Oath says he Intermarried with Lucy his Wife (Who is Now a Lunatic) in the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx. upwds. of Four Years ago. Says he Rented & lived in a house in Little Earle Street Seven Dials in the said Parish of Saint Giles in the fields of the Yearly Rent of Twenty two Pounds for Nine Months and hath quitted same upwards of one Year since which time he hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement says he has one Child by his sd. Wife named David aged abt. 1 Year

Sworn this 25th. day of Octr. 1758 . before us} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

John Lewis
