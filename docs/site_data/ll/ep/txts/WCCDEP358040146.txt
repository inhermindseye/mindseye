Westmr.  Dorothy Wilmot upon Oath saith she never was Married Says her father Dawkin Wilmot deced lived in and rented a house in Bow Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of £42 for several Years & afterwds. lived in and rented another house in Chandios Street in the same Parish Says her sd. late father after he quitted the last mentioned house never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she was born in Martlett Court in Bow Street aforesaid and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 5th. Febry 1747 before} Tho Lane

Dorothy Willmot
