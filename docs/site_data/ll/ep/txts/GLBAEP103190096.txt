Middlesex ss}  Mary Green the widow of William Green deced maketh oath That her said late husband was lately pressed & put on board his Majesties Ship the Conquestadore and is Since dead in Deal Hospital That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with one John Bird a Bricklayer in the Parish of Farnham in Hampshire & has not Since to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn this. 10. day of June 1780 before W Blackmore

Mary Green
