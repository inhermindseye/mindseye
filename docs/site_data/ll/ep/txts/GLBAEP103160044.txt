Middlesex ss}  Susannah Wright the widow of Thomas Higglestone Wright deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to and Served the whole of his Apprentice ship with his Father Thomas Wright a Baker in the Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her Said husband afterwards rented a house of the yearly Rent of Seven Guineas in the Butcher Row in the Precinct of Saint Catherine in the Said County & Continued there in for about the Space of three years That to this Deponents knowledge information or belief her Said husband during such his Residence in the Said house never paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes for such house or any other house nor ever served any Parish office nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship & that She was lawfully married to him in the Parish Church of Saint Mary & since his Saith not gained a Settlement for herself Islington Middlesex

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Susannah Wright
