Middlesex to wit Ann Tillier aged about Thirty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Charles Bidolph  Esqr . at Burton Park in the Parish of Petworth in the County of Sussex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Ten Years ago since which time she hath never lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born And that one Colonel Ryatt is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his the said Mr. Ryatts Lodgings in St Martins Street upwards of Three Years ago and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Colonel Ryatt and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. 1778 before R Butler

Ann Tillier
