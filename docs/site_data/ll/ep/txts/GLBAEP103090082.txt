Middlesex ss  Jane Thomas  Single woman maketh oath That divers years ago She this Deponent rented a house of Ten Pounds a year in Tothill Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret in Westminster in the County of Middx and Continued in such house for a year and upwards and was Charged to and paid the Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for such house and by Means thereof she is advised gained a legal settlement there and this Depont. Saith She was never Married nor hath she done any Act to her knowledge or belief whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 19th: day of October 1767 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

The Mark of Jane Thomas
