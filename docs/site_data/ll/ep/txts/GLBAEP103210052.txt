Middlesex ss}  Henry Baynton maketh oath that about thirty six Years ago he this Deponent was duly bound apprentice by Indenture to James Ely of the Parish of St George in Queen's Square in the County of Middlesex  Smith for the Term of Seven Years & served the whole of his apprenticeship there & allways ledged in his Masters house That the Sd Indres are lost & Can't be found That he this Depont never rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten pounds nor ever served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement subsequent to his sd apprenticeship.

Sworn this 19. day of Janry 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

Henry Baynton
