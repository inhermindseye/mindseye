Middx  Wm. Gilbert aged about 62 Years upon his Oath saith he can't give any Account of the Settlemt. of his Father other than that he was born in Scotland but in what Shire knows not Says he this Examint. was born in King Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex Says he never rented ten Pounds a Year Served any Parochiall Office was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 18th. 1745 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Wm. Gilbert
