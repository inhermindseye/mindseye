Middx  Walter Sterley aged upwds. of 54 years upon Oath Saith that in or abt. the year 1719 he Covenanted with Mr Thos. Atten a Watchmaker for Eighteen months certain at the yearly Wages of Fifty two Shillings Dyet Washing and Lodging & lived under that hiring & agreemt. wth. the sd. Mr. Atten in Duke Street in the parish of St Clement danes within the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middx all the sd. term of One year & Six months within a fortnight & was during that time a Singleman And that he never Rented Ten Pounds by the year pd. parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn July 26th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

Walter Sterly
