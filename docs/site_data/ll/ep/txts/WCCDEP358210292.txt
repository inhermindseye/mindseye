Westmr.  Alexander Carnagy Aged about Fifty nine Years, Upon Oath says he intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife (deceased) at the Fleet London about Seventeen Years ago, by whom he hath one Child now living, to wit, Elizabeth aged about Fifteen Years; Says he rented a House in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty eight Pounds, lived therein upwards of Two Years, Quitted same about a Year and a Quarter and never after Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 4th day of July 1768 , before} Tho's Kynaston
