Westmr-  Ann Finn who goes by the name of Ann Cockran aged about Thirty four Years on her Oath says she never was Married was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says she Rented and lived in a house in Plow Court Feller Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn London for Nine Months at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds & Paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about five Years ago since which time she never Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act wherby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. Day of June before me 1778 G Mercer G Howard

Ann Finn
