Middx.  Ann Doleman aged abt. fifty four Years upon Oath says She was never married Says abt. fourteen Years ago She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Lewis a Button Seller in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwds. of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abt. twelve Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Ye.

Sworn the 21st day of Janry 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Doleman
