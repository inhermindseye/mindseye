Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Passd. to St. George Bloomsbury to wit Margarett Cox aged about 51 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to John Cox about 19 years ago who departed this Life upwards of Six Years ago says that her late Husband informed her that he was born in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex that he never Rented House Lodging or Tenement of Ten Pounds or year, Served an Apprenticeship, lived as an hired yearly Servant or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor hath this Examinant done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 2d. day of Augt. 1785 G Mercer Jno. Wright

her Margarett Cox Mark
