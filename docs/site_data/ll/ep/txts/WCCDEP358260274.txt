Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Peter Haywood aged about 86 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a house at the back of the New Church in the Parish of Saint Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £36. that he hath Quitted said house about Ten years since which time he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1798 before us W Addington Robinson

his Peter Haywood mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
