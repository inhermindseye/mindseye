Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Bouch aged about 80 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Wm. Bouch her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the year 1773 that her said late husband has been dead about 7 years that her said late Husband was bound Apprentice to Anthony Bouch his Father in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Seven years that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th- day of May 1789 R Butler

her Jane Bouch mark
