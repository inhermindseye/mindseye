Middlesex ss}  Mary Robinson  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father John Robinson deced heartofore rented a house in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & did not afterwards do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlemt elsewhere That she this Deponent was born in the sd Parish & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 20 day of June 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Mary Robinson

To her Fathers Settlemt. by Renting a House & paying Poors Rate
