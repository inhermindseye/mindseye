Middlesex ss}  Robert Minchin maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to & served the whole of such Apprenticeship with Joseph Twyman in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Cooper & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Robert Minchin
