Middx  Mary Howard upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Jno. Howard who dyed about one Year since and that her sd. late husband rented the White Swan near Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of £14 and lived therein about 3 months & there dyed and this Examinant continued to Live in & Rent the said house about 3 months after his Decease Says she hath not since She quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant with any person for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief & that she hath one Child Ann aged upwds. of 3 yrs

Sworn Novr. 21st. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Howard
