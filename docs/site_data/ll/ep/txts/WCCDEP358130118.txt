Middx.  William Carter upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Elizabeth Carter at Upton Church in Gloucestershire about 26 Years ago and that about 32 years since being then a Singleman he hired himself to one William Newman a Carpenter in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex live with him for three Years at the yearly Wages of £5 Dyet Washing & Lodging and lived with the said Mr. Newman under that hiring & Agreement for three years or thereabouts in Carey Street aforesaid and this Examinant further maketh Oath that he has not (since he quitted Mr. Newmans Service) rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the Day of Jany. 1756. before}
