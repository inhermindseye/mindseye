Westmr. 

Cathn. Edwards aged abt. 28 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Aprenti or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes That her father Richd. Edwards deceased rented the Black bull & punch bowl Alehouse in Mite Court Henry Lane Market in the Parish of St Laurence  Jewry London at the Yearly Rent of £70 and Lived there in several Years & there dyed about thirteen Years since

Sworn July 31st. 1750 before} Tho Lane .

Cathn. Edwards  Co her Mark
