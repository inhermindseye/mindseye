Middx  Wm. Cutleff Maketh Oath and Saith that about fifteen Thirteen years ago (when he was a Singleman without wife or Child) he hired himself A Servant by the year to M s. Ellix and Lecount Merchants in Thread needle Street in the parish of St. Benedict vulgry called St Bennetsink in the City of London and Served them as such in the said parish about two Years and gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And that he hath a wife named Hannah and three Children (Namly) Susanna aged abt. twelve years Mary aged about five years and Peter Aged about one Month

Sworn this 28th. Febry 1744 Before Robert Dennett

Wm Cutleff
