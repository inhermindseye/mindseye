Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Cartwright aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months nor ever bound an Apprentice nor Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That her Father Rowland Cartwright Rented and lived in a house in Ortons Buildings in the Parish of Saint Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry for Nineteen years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that after he quitted said House he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Augt. 1798 before us  R. Ford Robinson



Margaret Cartwright
