St Clement Danes Decr. 30th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Unfurnished on 1st floor front & Back Room}  St James Westmr .}  Removed by Mr Newman  (to wit) Mary Dunn Aged 45 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She was Married to Thomas Dunn at the parish Church of St Martin in the fields  Westminster on or about the 27th day of January in or about the year 1766 that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and (with this Examinant & their family) Resided in an Apartment (consisting of two Rooms) in the House of Mr Clark in Swallow Street in the parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for about six Months at the Annual Rent of £10 that they quitted the same in or about the Month of January 1793 and her said Husband hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief And that she hath And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that she hath two Children by her said Husband namely Sarah aged about Ten Nine year or thereabouts and Mary aged about eight seven Years & th who are both with this Examinant And that her said Husband is now in Tothill fields Prison for Debt

Sworn the 30th Decem 1793 30th day of January 1794 before us} Rich.. Ford  Edwd: Naish

Mary Dunn 's Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
