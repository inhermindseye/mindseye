Westmr.  Rebecca Dowthwait aged about 42 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Dowthwait who has been dead about twelve Years says her said husband served about four Years of his Apprenticeship with John Thomas a Waterman in the Parish of East Greenwich in the County of Kent and Then Went to Sea, says her said Husband returned from Sea after the Seven Years of His Apprenticeship was Expired and Rented a House of Three Pounds a Year in the said Parish of East Greenwich for three Years or thereabouts and paid the Land Tax for the same, says her said late Husband after he quitted said House, never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes, neither hath she this Examinant since his Death, Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn 18th, Jany. 1768 , before} Tho Lane  Barth: Hammond

Rebeckah Dowthwait
