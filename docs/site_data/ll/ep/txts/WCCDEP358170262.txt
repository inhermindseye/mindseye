Middx.  Ann Feilder  Wife of Thomas Feilder upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her present Husband. Thomas Fielder at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about Twenty two Years since and that about Eleven Years since the said Thos. Feilder lived in and rented a House in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid for the Space of Twelve Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds And this Examint. further saith that her said Husband absconded from her about the Time he left the sd. House and this Examinant knoweth not where he how is that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne the 26th- Augt. 1742 before me}
