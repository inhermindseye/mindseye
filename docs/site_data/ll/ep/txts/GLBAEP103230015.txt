Middlesex ss}  Mary Puckridge  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore bound Apprentice by the Parish officers of St Sepulchre London to Jessee Gee of Grub Street London  Mantna maker untill She Should attain her Age of twenty one years That She Served Six years under her Indres of Apprenticeship but the last two years thereof She with her mistresses Consent lived and lodged with Mr Thomas Moore  Salesman in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex at the Expiration of which two years her Indres were by Mutual Consent of herself & her Mistress Cancelled by the Justices at the Session of the Peace for the sd County of Middlesex by means whereof She this Depont. became & now is Legally Settled in the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this - 7. day of Febry 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Mary Puckridge
