St. Clement Danes.

Middlesex. to wit, Christian Lucas aged 33 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she intermarried with Richd. Lucas her Present Husband at the Parish Church of Saints Martin in the Fields abouts Ten Years ago. That he has deserted her That about 7 years ago her Husband Rentd. the Boot in Cross Lane in the Parish of Mr Giles in the Fields that he had in it Six Months & was to pay £40. a Year That since that Terice he has not to the best of her knowledge done any Act whereby he may have gained a Settlement

Sworn this 26th August 1786 . G Mercer Jno Barnfather

her Christian Lucas Mark
