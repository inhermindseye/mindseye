Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Ann Dean aged about 27 years on her Oath says she never was married or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year says she Rented a Shop & Parlour of one Mr. Savage a Shoemaker in King Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr . for half a Quar. of the a year at the yearly Rent of £16. hath quitted the sd. Apartmt. upwards of 3 years ago and never gained any or. Settlement than as aforesaid And further on her Oath says that on the 25th. day of March last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Jones in Plough Court  Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . which hath since been baptized by the name of Ann and is become Chargeable to the same And further says that one Chas. May a Clerk in the Long Room in the Custom House & who lives at No. 7 in Ayliffe Street  Goodmans Fields in the County of Middx is the father thereof he having had Carnall knowledge of her Body for sevl. years last past at sevl. Places and other Lodgings in Plough Court afsd. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Chas. May & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 18th. day of Febry. 1784  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Ann Dean
