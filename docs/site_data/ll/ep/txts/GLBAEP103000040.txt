Middlesex (ss)  Robert Warren  Widower Maketh Oath and Saith that upwards of 40 Years ago he was lawfully Married to his late Wife  Lucy (whose Maiden name-was Anderson ) at the Fleet in the City of London , That he had Issue by his said late Wife two Female Children, namely Lucy now about 28 Years of Age, & Mary Aged about 16,-That this Examinant was Born about the Year 1711 in the Parish of St. Clement in Ipswich Town in the County of Suffolk And this Examinant Saith that he was never bound an Apprentice to any Person or Persons whom soever, lived a Year under one hiring, nor rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of £10, or upwards served any Parish Office, or hath done any other Act whatsoever whereby to gain a Settlement in any other Parish Precinct or Place, he having from his Infancy constantly been in the Maritime Service till within these ten Years, during all which last mentd. time he has been employed in looking after Vessells in the River Thames

Sworn the day of 178 before us}
