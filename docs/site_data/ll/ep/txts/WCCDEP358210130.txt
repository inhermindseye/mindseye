Westmr.  Rebecca Grudgeworthy aged about 36 Years upon Oath says, she is the Widow of Humphry Grudgeworthy who has been dead about twelve Years, says her said Husband Rented a House (known by the Name of the Fountain Tavern ) in Catharine Street of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and also Rented a private house adjoining the said Tavern in the Parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Forty five Pounds or thereabouts for Seven Years , says she hath not since the Death of her said Husband Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 21st. Jany. 1767 . before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

Rebecca Grudgeworthy
