Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizth. Roe aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that upwards of Six years ago she Intermarried with Thos. Roe her late Husband at  Christ Church Dublin that her said Husband has been dead upwards of Three Months that she has 4 Children living by him vizt. Robt. aged 5 years Mary Ann aged up of 3 yrs. unprovided for John aged 2 years provided for and Loving a aged about 9 Months unprovided for that her said late Husbd. Rented and lived in a House in Great Wild Street in Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx for 15 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent £25 that then he died that she this Ext. Rented & lived in the same House for full 3 Months after her said late Husbands Death that she this Ext. has not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent of £10 for 40 days or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement and that she this Ext. is poor & unable to maintain herself & Children & therefore is obliged to apply to the Parish of St. Clement Danes for relief

Sworn this 14th. day of April 1787  James Croft  Jno. Barnfather E Roe
