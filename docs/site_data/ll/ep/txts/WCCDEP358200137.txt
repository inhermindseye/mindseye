Westmr.  Alice Colethorpe Aged about Thirty four Years Upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant at the Peacock Inn in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Two pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted said Service about Sixteen Years & hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the Day of Septr 1764 Before}
