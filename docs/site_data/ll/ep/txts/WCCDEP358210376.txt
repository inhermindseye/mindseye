Middx & Westmr.}  Anthony Harris Aged about fifty Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at Cirencester in the County of Dorset about five Years Ago Says he rented and lived in a House in Bermondsey Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen in the County of Surry for many Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath Quitted said House about five Years and hath not since rented House paid Taxes or done any other thing whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1769 before us} Tho Fellows W Kelyngs

Anthony Harris
