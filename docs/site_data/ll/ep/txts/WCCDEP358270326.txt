Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Hall aged about 27 year Widow upon her Oath saith that on the Third day of August now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of one Mr. Crawford the county of Little Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the name of Mary That one Peter Cassidy who did live with this Deponent but who is left her is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Peter Cassidy is the only Real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 2d. day of Octr. 1797 before. me  Gs Goodenough

Resworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1797 before me } J Hunter

Mary Hall
