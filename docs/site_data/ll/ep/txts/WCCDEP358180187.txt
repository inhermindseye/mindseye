Middx  James Nedroe aged 42 Yrs: upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with his present Wife  Mary on the 14th. Day of April 25 Yrs. since at the Fleet London that he was bound Apprentice by the Trustees of the Charity Schoole of the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden to Mr Scott Gunner to the Chichester Man of Warr and lived with him as an Apprentice about Six Months the first Six Weeks whereof he lived at Mr Scotts house near Chatham Dock & the Remaining Part of the sd. Six Months he sometimes lay on Board & sometime at his sd. Masters house & then his sd Master turn'd him away & Ordered him to provide for himself that this Exaiant was never Assigned ever to any other Person whatever but hired himself to one Mr. Cornelius at the Ship Alehouse in James Street in the Parish of St Paul Covt Garden in the County of Middx. for one Year at the Yearly Wages of £3. & lived with him under sd. Agreement for the Space of one Year & a half & hath left his sd. Service about Twenty Eight Yrs: since which time he hath

not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne  27th. Day of April 1744 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Jas. Nedroe
