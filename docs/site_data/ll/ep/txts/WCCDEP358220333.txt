Westminster to wit Cath: Foley Aged 26 years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant wth. one Mr. John Hall at the Sign of the Nags Head in Carnaby Markett in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for 15 Months at the Yearly Wages of £5. hath quitted her said Service about 4 Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same Ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year And further Says that on the 10th. day of July 1766 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Check at a Cooks Shop in Hungerford Markett in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster which was soon Afterwards Cristened by the Name of Mary Crossby And Says that one Mick. Crossby a Porter & an Irishman which was Passed as a Vagrant about Twelve Months ago to Ireland is the Father of the said Child & that the said Child hath gained no Settlement since her Birth.

Sworn this 10th. day of March 1772 before W Kelyngs John Lane

her Cath Foley mark
