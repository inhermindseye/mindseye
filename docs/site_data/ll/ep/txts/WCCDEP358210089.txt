Middx & Westmr. to wit Sarah Ferguson aged about 25 Years Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that on the 4th, day of May 1766, She was delivered of a female bastard Child in the House of Mr. Davis in Exeter Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized by the Name of Isabella says that John Milbourne a Shoemaker who lodges at the two fighting Cocks in Dartmouth Street Westminster did beget the said female bastard Child on her body, he having had carnal knowledge of her body sometime towards the latter End of July or beginning of August 1765, and at sevl, other times since, at one of which times he got her with Child of the female bastard Child of which She was delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Milbourne and no other Person is the true and real father thereof.

Sworn the 1st. Novr. 1766 , before me}
