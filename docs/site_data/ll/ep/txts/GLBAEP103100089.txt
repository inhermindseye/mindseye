Middlesex ss}  William Wylly a Singleman Son of John Wylly Maketh oath That he hath been informed by his Parents and believes the same to be true; that divers Years Ago (and during the time that this Depont. Continued Part of their Family) his Said Father John Wylly rented a house in the Town & Parish of Gosport in the County of Southampton and was Charged to and Paid Parish Taxes for the Same and by means thereof as this Deponent is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. doth not know nor was ever informed that his Parents or either of them ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And this Depont. Saith that he was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor to his knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this day of March 1769 before}
