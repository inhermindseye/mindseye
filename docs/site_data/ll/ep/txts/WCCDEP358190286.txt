Middx  John Harris aged about 29 Years upon Oath says that he never was bound an Apprentice lived a hired Yearly Servant a year or rented a House Says that his Father  Robert Harris  deceased  Rented a House above Ten pounds a Year in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes lived therein upwards of Twenty Years and paid all Parochial Taxes as he has been informed by his said late Father and verily believes Says he intermarried with his present Wife  Mary at the Fleet London about Thirteen Years since by whom he has four Children namely Joseph aged about Eleven Years Mary aged about Eight Years John aged about Seven Years and Robert aged about three Months

Sworn the 13th. day of Octor. 1762 before}

John Harris
