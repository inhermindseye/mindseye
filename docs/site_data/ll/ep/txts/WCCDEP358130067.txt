Middx  Rebecca Shipey Aged about 24 Years upon Oath says that She Intermarryed with Joseph Shipey (who is now at Sea in his Majesty's Service) at the Fleet London about four Years since by whom She hath one Child named Alice aged about 18 Months And further says that her said Husband lived a hired yearly Servant with the Revd. Dr. Birch in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upwards of a year and quitted that Service about Eight Years since And hath not since Rented Ten Pounds by the Year Paid Parochial Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 11th. Day of Octor. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Rebecca Shipey
