Middlesex ss}  Mary Patten the wife of George Patten maketh oath that her Said husband is now Absent from her on board his Majestiesship Kennedy That about Twelve years Ago her Said husband lived in & rented a house in East Lane in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent & Continued in the occupation thereof for several Years & during his Residence therein he was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same and has not Since done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere That she hath two children namely Jane Aged about Fourteen Years & James Aged about Nine Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom she was lawfully married & which Children have done no Act to gain a settlement for themselves

Sworn this. 30. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke W Blackmore

The Mark of Mary Patten
