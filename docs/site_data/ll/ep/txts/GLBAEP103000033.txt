Middlesex ss.  Richard Pew maketh Oath that about Twenty four Years ago he lived in and Rented a House of Six Pounds Ten Shillings a Year in Cannon Street in the Parish of St. George in the County of Middlesex and was charged to and paid the Kings Tax there that he has not since he quitted the said House Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds, or paid any Poor Rates or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement that he has Three Children living namely Richard aged about Eighteen Years who is an Apprentice to John Craiger of Mill Yard Rosemary Lane  Glass Cutter , Monmouth aged about Fourteen Years Servant to Mr. Charlton at the Publick Office East smithfield at the Wages of Two Shillings Per Week and Elizabeth aged about Twelve Years who has not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself which said Three Children are the lawful Issue of his late Wife Susannah to whom he was lawfully Married at the Parish Church of St. George aforesaid

Sworn this Day of December 1788 before}
