Middlesex ss}  James Low Aged about Forty years maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice to one William Ramsey of the parish of Dundee in Scotland  Cordwainer And that to his knowledge or belief he never did any Act to gain a settlement in that part of Great Britain called England That he hath a Wife named Mary and Four Children namely Mary aged about Thirteen years John Aged about seven years Ann Aged about four years & James Aged about one year the lawful Issue of this Deponent by this Said Wife none of which Children have done any act to gain a settlement in their own right

Sworn this 31st day of March 1773 before  Jno Sherwood

James Low
