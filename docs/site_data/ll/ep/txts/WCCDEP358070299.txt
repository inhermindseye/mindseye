Middx & Westr} to wit Stacey Hodges Aged about Forty Six Years upon her Oath says She never was Married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Johnson in Arundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Ten Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds has Quitted the said service about Twenty Eight Years ago and hath not since Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a year Paid Taxes, lived a Hired Yearly servant for one Year or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d, day of Sepr 1775 before  David Walker

her Stacey Hodges Mark
