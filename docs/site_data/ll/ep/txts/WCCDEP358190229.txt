Middx and Westmr}   Ann Culbirt aged about 21 years removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields by Order dated the 4th: day of March 1762 upon Oath says she never was Married That she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Jacks a Taylor in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for upwards of Two years at the yearly wages of £3..10s Quitted the Same about Two years and hath not since rented any House or been a hired yearly Servant for one year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the Same when Born And that one John Eggom a Shoemaker did beget such Child or Children he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in or about the Month of August last and once afterwards at one of which time she got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant also says she has been informed that the said John Eggom is gone to reside at Scows near wigan in Lancashire and which information she verily beleives tobe true.

Sworn the 6th: day of March 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Culbirt
