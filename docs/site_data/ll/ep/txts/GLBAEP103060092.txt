Aldgate Middx

Ann Fudge  wife of Robert Fudge Maketh Oath that her Said Husband is an Apprentice by Indenture to William Howard in Fryday Street in the Parish of Saint John the Evangelist in the City of London Boxmaker hath one Child Mary Fudge aged ten weeks

Sworn 9th day of December 1747 before Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

Anne Fudge
