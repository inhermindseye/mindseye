St. Clemt. Danes

Middx and Westmr.}  passd. to St. Giles in the fields to wit Mary Hawley aged about 60 years Sixty seven Years on her Oath saith That she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as an hired yearly servant with one Mrs. Bank of Little Wild Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for near seven years at the yearly Wages of 6 Guineas hath quitted her said Service upwards of 4 Years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlemt elsewhere

Sworn this 28th. day of Febry. 1785 before us  Sampn Wright J. P. Andrews

Mary Hawly
