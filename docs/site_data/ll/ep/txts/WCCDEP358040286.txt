Middx 

Jane Allen aged upwds. of 60 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that near 30 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Jno. Syms a Joyner in Warder Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £4 and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 16th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Jane Allen
