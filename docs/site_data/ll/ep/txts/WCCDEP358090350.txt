Middlesex  Martha Miles Aged about 45 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hartley at the Bedford Head in Southampton Street Covent Garden in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex for Sixteen Months at the Yearly Wages of £7. hath Quitted her said Service upwards of 11 years ago and after Quitting her said Service she never afterwards gained anyother Settlemt. or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 4th.day of Decr. 1782 before  Sampn Wright R Butler

her Martha Miles Mark.
