St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Edward Dickins Aged about forty Years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with his present Wife  Mary at the parish Church of St. Giles Cripplegate London about twelve Years ago, That previous to his Intermarriage with his said Wife & whilst he was Single not having any Child or Children he lived for two Years as an Hired Yearly Servant (in the Capacity of Second Coachman ) with the Duke of Ancaster at the Yearly Wages of Ten Guineas, that during the last Forty days of his said Service with the Duke he Resided at his Grace's Country Seat at Gwinthorpe in the parish of in the County of Lincoln , that he quitted his said Service about Fourteen years since, and hath not to the best of his knowledge done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might acquire any Subsequent legal settlement

Sworn the 23d day of Febraury March 1791 before Me  Wm Kitchiner

Edward Dickins
