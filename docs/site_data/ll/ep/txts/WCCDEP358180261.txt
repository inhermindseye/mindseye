Middx  Catherine Seale aged 62 Yrs. Wife of Jas. Seale aged 40 Years & upwards upon her Oath saith She Intermarried with her sd. Husband  Jas. Seale on or abt. the 28th. Day of January 1736 at the Parish Church of St George Hanover Square Says the sd. Jas. Seale lived in & rented a house in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £18 & lived in the same upwds. of Ten Yrs. hath & left sd. House upwds. of 6 Months since which time the sd. James Seale hath not Rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 7th. Decr. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

Catherine Sall
