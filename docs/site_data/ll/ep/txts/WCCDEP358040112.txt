Westmr.  Robert Tomlinson upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £20 for Ten Yrs. or thereabouts and quitted the same about Ten Years since Says he has not since he Quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif Says he has a Daugher named Frances aged 15 Years or thereabts. (by his present Wife Mary Tomlinson ) who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Decr. 3d. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Robert Tomlinson
