Middx.  Sarah Akhurst aged about 24 Years upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Largeau a Peruke maker in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for one Year and Ten Months at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds for the first Year and five Pounds for the last Ten Months and hath quitted said Service about Two Months And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one George Lee (footman to Mr. Cartwright who lodges at Millers Warehouse in Tavistock Street near Covent Garden ) had Carnal knowledge of her Body Sometime towards the Latter end at April 1759. and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant And that the said George Lee and no other Person is the true and real father thereof.

Sworn the 7th. day of Janry. 1760 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Akhurst
