Middx.  Ann Hunt aged abt. 29 Years upon Oath says; she is the Widow of James Hunt who has been dead about four Years; says he was born in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. for one year as her said late husband Often informed this Examinant and which Information she verily believes to be true. and further upon her Oath says she was (by Order under the hands and seals of Wm. Clark and T: Dawson Esqrs. Two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the County of Surry bearing date the 19th. day of March 1756.) Removed from the Parish of St. Geo: Southwark in the County of Surry aforesaid to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes which Order was not appealed against says she has one Child by her sd. husbd. named Mary aged upwards of Six Yrs.

Sworn this 10th. of Octr. 1758 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Hunt
