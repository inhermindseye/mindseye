Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Smith aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bradley in Clare Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that she hath quitted said Service about Fourteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Reed a Private in Colonel Camels Company in the Third Regiment of Foot Guards and who is now at Winchester is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Reed is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 2nd day of Janry. 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Mary Smith Mark

Witness AW Burbidge Beadle

Man never Apprehended

Child born in the house the 26th. day of March 1798 named  John

Qrs. wher. any & what Allowance to the Mother
