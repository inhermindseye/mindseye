Middx  Mary Dodd aged about 21 Years upon her Voluntry Oath saith that she was never married Says that she was bound an Apprentice to & Served 6 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mr. Jumpson in Worley Court near Tower hill in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate London & hath been out of her sd. Apprentiship about 3 Yrs: since which time she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year Paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex she being poor & unable to Maintain the same when borne & that Wm: Oakley  Gent at No. 6 in Clements did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body the sd: Wm: Oakley having had Carnall knowledge of her Body in his Chambers aforesaid on the 11th. of June last & at Sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that he the

said William Oakley and no other Person is the true and reall Father thereof

Sworne Octr. 13th. 1744 before} Ja: Fraser

Mary Dodd
