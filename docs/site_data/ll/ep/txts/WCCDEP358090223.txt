Middx  to wit Jane Coleman Aged about Twenty One years On her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly servant with One Mr. Wellings a Coachmaker who Lodged at Mr. Coropass's a Bricklayer in Wormwood Street in the Parish of all Hallows London Wall in the City of London upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said service about Twelve Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent settlement

Resworn this 21st. day of May 1781 before  Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

her Jane Coleman  Mark
