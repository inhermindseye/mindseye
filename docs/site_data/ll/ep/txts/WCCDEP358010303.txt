Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Stalman aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that on the 13th. day of January 1783 the Intermarried with Wm. Stalman her present Husband at the Parish Church of Preston in the County of Lancaster that She hath Three Children living by her said Husband Vizt. John aged about Six years Jane aged about Four years and Diana aged about Six Months that live said Husband Rented a House in wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Six Months at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds that he hath Quitted said House about Five weeks and further this Examd. upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted her about five weeks and refused to allow her or her Children any support 

Sworn this 17th day of Augt. 1789 R Butler

Ann Stalman
