Middx  Eliz: Carr  Wido . of John Carr who dyed about Six Weeks since upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the said John Carr in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields (about 15 Years ago by whom she hath two Children viz) Ann aged 11 Years & upwds. and Mary aged about 11 Weeks Says the said John Carr lived in and rented a house near Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Sixty pounds for Two Years and upwards and quitted the said house about Eleven Years ago since which the said John Carr has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn July 14th: 1749 before}

Eliza Carr
