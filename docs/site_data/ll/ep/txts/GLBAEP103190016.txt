Middlesex ss}  Ann Hill the wife of John Hill who is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore maketh oath that her sd husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one William Jones a Smith and Farrier in the  Parish of Bramton in the County of Oxford & has not since done any Act to gain anothr Settlement to her knowledge or belief to gain anothr Settlement And that she was lawfully married to her said husband.

Sworn this. 22. day of Febry: 1780 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Hill
