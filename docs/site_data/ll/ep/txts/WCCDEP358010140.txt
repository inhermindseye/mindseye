Saint Clement Danes 14th Septr 1787

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Catharine Carney  Widow aged Sixty Six Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that since the Deceased Richard Carney her late Husband and having only one Child Vizt. John Carney who at the time of her living herself as herein after is mentioned was bound out Apprentice and had acquired a Settlement in his own right she this Examinant lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Dillon at the Blakeneys Head in Bow Street Covent Garden at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds for about fifteen Months that she quitted her Service about Six Years ago and since then hath not done any Act Whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have acquired any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. Septr. 1787 before  Philip Dyor D Walker

Catherine her Mark Carney
