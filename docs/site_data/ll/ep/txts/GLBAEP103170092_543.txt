Middlesex ss}  Sarah Smith Maketh oath That divers years ago She was bound Apprentice by the Parish officers of Bromley near Bow in Middlesex to one Sarah Russell of Seven Star Court Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County Washer woman & Served there four years or there abouts of Such her Apprenticeship when this Deponts mistess not having Sufficient work to employ this Deponent in She gave this Deponts. Indre up to her & they parted by Consent That She this Deponts hath eversince lodged presided in the said parish of St Botolph without Aldgate & hath not at anytime lived as a hired Servant for a year or been married or done any Act to gain a Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 27 day of August 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Sarah Smith
