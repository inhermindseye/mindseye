Middx  Margt. Welch Aged about Thirty years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband Peter Welch (gone from her) at the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the year 1781 and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Timothy Simms who kept a Public House in St. Michaels Lane  Crooked Lane  in the Parish of St. Michael Crooked Lane  London for sevl. years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds besides Diet & Lodging hath quitted his said Service upwards of five years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a yearly paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. 1783  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

Margret Welch
