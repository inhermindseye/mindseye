Middx  Ann Moreton aged 33 Yrs. upon Oath saith she was married to John Moreton at the Fleet London upwds. of four Years ago; that the sd. John Moreton absconds from her & she knows not where he is and that her sd. husband lived a Yearly Servant for two Years together with Mr. Fox Kerry a Grocer in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two Years & was all that time unmarried and that he has not since he left Mr Kerrys Service (which was the Day before their Marriage) rented ten pounds a year or paid Parochial Taxes

Sworn June 19th. 1753 before}
