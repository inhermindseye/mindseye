Middlesex  Alice Roop  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was duly bound to & Served two years of her Apprenticeship with John Marchant a Hatter in the precinct of St. Catherine in the Sd County & then this Deponent was Assigned & turned over to one Frances Cranaway a washer woman of king Henry yard in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County with Whom She Served the Remainder of her Said Apprenticeship & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since hired as a hired Servant for a year nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain Another Settlement.

Sworn this. 19. day of December 1776 before  Saml Wood

The Mark of Alice Roop
