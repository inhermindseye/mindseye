Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Pike aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that about 37 years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Pike her late Husband who has been dead upwards of Twenty years at the Parish Church of Saint Botolph Bishopgate that her said late Husband Served his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Sparrow in Booth Street in the Parish of Christ Clement in Spittle fields in the County of Middlesex that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of April 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

her Mary Pike mark
