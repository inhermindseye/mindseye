Middx to wit Hester Shevellier aged about Sixty One Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Dennis shevellier at the Parish of Saint Mary Radcliff  in the City of Bristol about Twenty Eight Years ago and by whom she hath one Child living now provided for says her said late Husband was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year before Marriage with this Examinant and this Examinant further says that before Intermarriage with her said late Husband she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Timothy Davis a Hott Presser in Castle Street Castle Precinct in the City of Bristol upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds and after quitting her said last Mentioned Service she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 23d. day of Octr. 1779 before R Butler

her Hester Shevellier Mark
