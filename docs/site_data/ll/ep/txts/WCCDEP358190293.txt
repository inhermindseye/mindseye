Middx  Euphemia Johnston of the Parish of St. Clement Danes  Widow on behalf of Ann Hay a Lunatick aged upwards of 40 Years upon Oath says that the said Ann Hay was never Married and that She Rented a House in Panton Square in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds lived therein upwards of two Years and quitted same about four Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant as this Examinant has been informed and verily believes to be true

Sworn the 23d. day of Octor. 1762 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Euphemia Johnston
