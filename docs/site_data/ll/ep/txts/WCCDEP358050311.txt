Middx  John Killow aged about 41 Years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 10th. Day of April 1736 he took a house of the yearly rent of £14 in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex known by the Sign of the White Swan and lived therein near Six Months and that he hath not since he quitted the abovementioned house Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 11th. 1750 before}
