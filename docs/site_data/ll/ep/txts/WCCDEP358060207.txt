Middx  Edith Hewit  Wido. of Wm. Hewit upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late husband upwds. of 10 yrs ago in the Parish Church of St. Michl at Queen hith and that her sd. late husband before marriage lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Amos Callard in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middlesex for several Years & never after he quitted the sd. Mr. Callards Service nor has this Examinant since his Decease rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 13th. 1751 before}

The mark of Edith Hewit
