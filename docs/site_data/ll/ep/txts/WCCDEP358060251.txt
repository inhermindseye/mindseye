Middx  Isaac Watlington upon Oath Saith her Intermarried with his Wife  Hannah at a Chapel in Highgate abt. 7 Years ago & has by her 2 Children Ann aged 3 Yrs. & Mary aged abt. 18 months and that he Served three Years the latter part of his Apprentiship with Mr Henry Mercer in Lothbury in the Parish of St. Margt. in Lothbury London Upholsterer and never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for ones Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn January 21st. 1752 before H Feilding Tho Lane

Isaac Watlington
