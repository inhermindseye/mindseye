Saint Clements July 8th. 1785

Swears to St. Clements  Kitty Collis aged forty three Years or there- abouts upon her Oath saith that she Was born at Boston in New England and Was married to Thomas Collis her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Brides in Dublin about the Year of our Lord 1773-That she has five Children now living by her said Husband Vizt. Thomas aged seven Years or thereabouts William aged four Years or thereabouts James aged three Years or thereabouts and Mary Ann aged ten Months or thereabouts saith that since there Intermarriage her said Husbd rented and lived in a House in the Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly rent of £35. a Year for upwards of forty Days that he is since dead, and that after quitting the said House till the time of his Dece he neither rented any House paid Taxes or did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge a belief he may have

gained any Settlement otherwise than in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes as aforesaid

Sworn this 8th. July 1785 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Kitty Collins Mark
