Middlesex (ss)  Edward Flower Maketh Oath and Saith that to the best of his recollection it is about nine Years since he was hired as a Yearly Servant by James French of Crown Court Butlers Buildings in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate  Victualler at the Annual Wages of Four Pounds and that he lived with the said James French under that hiring for the space of fourteen Months Successively and this Depont. further Saith that since his discharge from the Service of the said James French he hath not lived as a Yearly hired Servant with any Person or Persons whom soever, rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of ten pounds or upwards, served any Parish Office or done any Act whatsoever whereby to gain a Settlement in any other Parish or Place.

Sworn this day of 1788 Before Us}
