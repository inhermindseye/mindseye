Middx  Eliz Cogdell upon Oath saith she is the wife of Wm. Cogdell an Irishman who never gained a Settlement in England and that at the time of her Intermarriage with the sd. Cogdell she was the wido . of Rd. Collins decd who (as she has been informed & believes) Served the last Year of his Apprentiship with Mr Cleaver a Ship Carpenter in the Parish of Kingston at Portsmouth and never rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant

Sworn Augt. 13th. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Eliz. Cogdell
