Middlesex ss}  Hannah Young  Singlewoman Maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages with one Valentine Harris in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Town of Plymouth in the County of Devon  Mariner and Continued there in Such Service for the Space of one Year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont. Saith that She hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of year nor hath She been ever married nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that She is about Thirty eight Years old

Sworn this 13 day of December 1771 before Wilmot

Hannah Young
