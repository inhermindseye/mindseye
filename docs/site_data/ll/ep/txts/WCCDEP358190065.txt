Middx.  Elizabeth Dixon aged upwards of 22 Years upon Oath says she never was Married nor Ever Rented Ten Pounds by the Year nor was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant and that her father  Robt. Dixon Rented a house in the Parish of Old Elvet in the Bishoprick of Durham of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards for sevl. Years and further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 19th. day of January 1759 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Gilken not in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. which has since been baptized by the Name of William Dixon Thong and is still living and likely to become chargeable to the said Parish and that one Willm. Thong -an Attorney at Law who lives at the Whitehouse in Leicester fields did beget the said Child on her Body he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of April 1758 at sevl. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Wm. Thong and no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Novr. 1st. 1760 before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizabeth Dixon
