St. Clement Danes 7th June 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St John Southwark Wrong parish  (to wit) Sarah Creed  Singlewoman aged 19 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she was bound an Apprentice to Mr William Rogers then of Griffith's Rents Bermondsey Street Taylor and Served her said Master there for about four years, that he then removed to a Butcher's in Bermondsey Street in the parish of Saint John in the Southwark in the County of Surry that she served him and lodged then at the Butcher's in Bermondsey Street aforesaid for about a year and an half and that she then removed with her said Master to Hand Court Bermondsey Street in the said parish of Saint John and served him there for about seven Weeks that he then died that about a Month after she was taken into the Workhouse of the said parish of Saint John and was there provided for til she could procure a situation for herself and hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement

Sworn the 7th. day of June 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Sarah Creed 's Mark

Witness Garrett
