Middx  Elizth. Overley Aged about 18 Years Upon Oath Says she never was Married was an Apprentice or a Hired Yearly Sert. Says her Father Isaac Overley Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Chester a Watch Key Maker in Cherry Tree Alley in White Cross Street in the Parish of St. Lukes in the County of Middx and never after Rented House or Paid Taxes as this Examinant hath been Informed by her Mother and which Information she Verily believes to be true.

Sworn the 21st Day of October 1763 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Elizabeth Overley

Upon Mr Olivers Complaint Ovrs J W
