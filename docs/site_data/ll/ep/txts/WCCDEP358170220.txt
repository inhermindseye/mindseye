Middx  Thomas Child on the Behalfe of Mary Child his Neice an Inft. aaged abt. Thirteen Years upon his Oath saith that Jane Child the Mother of the Sd. Infant dyed abt. 3 Yrs. Since & that Wm. Child the Father of the Sd. Infant hath been Dead abt. 2 Yrs. & that abt. 3 Yrs. before his Death lived in & rented the Coach & Horses Alehouse on the High Ground  Picadelley in the Parish of St. George in Hanover Square in the Libty of Westrmr . of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & upwds. for the Space of Two Years & upwds. & hath not since he left the Sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Yr. Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife

Sworn the 28th. April &1742 before} Tho. Lane  Jas: Gronous

Thos: Childe
