Middx.  Sarah Bevan aged abt. 23 years upon Oath says she is the Widow of James Bevan who hath been dead abt. three years & to whom she was married at the fleet London abt. five years ago says her said late husband was born at Noke in the Parish of Pembridge in the County of Hereford

Sworn this 13th. day of Feby. 1758 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Bevan
