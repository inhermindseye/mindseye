Westmr.  Anna Gooch aged about 61 years upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her Husband Valentine Gooch decd. upwds. of 38 years ago and that her sd. late husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Guineas in Crown Court in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & lived therein about one year and has not Since he quitted the sd. house (which was abt. 9 yrs. ago) nor has this Examint. Since his Decease rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 4th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Anna Gooch
