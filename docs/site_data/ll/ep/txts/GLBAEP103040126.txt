Middlesex ss.  Elizabeth Hurley  Singlewoman maketh Oath that about Five Years ago she was hired as a Servant by the Year with Mrs. Marsh of Pheenix Street in the Parish of Christ Church Spittlefields in the County of Middlesex Silk Winster at the yearly Wages of Forty Shillings and continued in her Service under such hiring upwards of One Year that she has not since she quitted her Service lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any other Parish or Place for the Space of One Year together or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement, and that she never was Married, And further saith that being Poor & unable to provide for herself she has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex for Relief & has been believed by them accordingly

Sworn this 25th Day of May 1798 before R Davies H: Reynell

her Elizabeth Hurley Mark
