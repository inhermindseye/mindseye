Middx  Eliz: Coman  Wido . upon her Oath saith that abt. 2 Yrs. since being at that time a Wido . she lived in & Rented as house in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £17. for one Yr & upwds. that since she left the sd. House she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year Paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath 2 Childn. viz: Ann aged upwd. 9 Yrs. & Chas. aged abt. 8 Yrs. & being poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne 2d. Decr. 1743 before Wm: Jones

Eliz Coman
