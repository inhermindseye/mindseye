Middlesex  Janet Cartwright  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that she never was married, that she never lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year or ever to the best of her Knowledge acquired a legal Settlement in her own Right and that Thomas Durham of Cockspur Street  Bookseller is as this Deponent believes her father she this Examinant being an Illegitimate Child of the said Thomas Durham as he has informed her and she believes and further this Examinant saith She has been informed by the said Thomas Durham that she was born in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes And this Examinant also further on her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be chargeable

to the said Parish Saint Clement Danes and that George Phillips who is a Surradier in the third Battalion of the first Regiment of foot is the Real and true father of such Child or Children where with she is so pregnant he the said George Phillips having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same thereon.

Sworn this 9th. Novr 1790 before me R Butler}

Janet Cartwright

Mr Rhodes had Read.
