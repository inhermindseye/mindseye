Middx  Sarah Pitman  Wido . upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her late Husband  John Pitman at Gravesend Church about 37 Years since & her sd. Husband hath been Dead about Seven Years that the sd. John Pitman lived in & rented a house in Fountain Court in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £30. & lived in the same for the Space of 2 Years & upwards & hath left the same about 30 Years since which time the sd. John Pitman in his Life or this Examint. since his Death never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 13th. June 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Sarah Pitman
