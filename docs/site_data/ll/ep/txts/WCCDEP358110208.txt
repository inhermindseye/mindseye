Middx  Jane Mathews aged abt. 25 Years upon Oath Saith She never was married and she further voluntarily maketh Oath that She is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx (where she now lodges) she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that Mr. John White of Freickenham in the sd. County Chymist had carnal Knowledge of her body Several times in the Months of May and June last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

and that, the said John White and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn March 14th. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jane Mathews
