Middlesex ss  Mary Bennett  widow of Robert Bennett deced maketh oath that her Said late husband heretofore rented a house of the yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph Without Aldgate in the Sd County & was charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same and died in the Said house legally Settled in the Sd Parish That She this Deponent Since his death has not lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house or lodging of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds nor Paid any Poors Rates or king Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this. 18 day of June 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Mary Bennett
