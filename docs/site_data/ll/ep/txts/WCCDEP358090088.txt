Middx to wit Sarah Slater Aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Charles Slater with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Shoreditch Church about Thirty Years ago and who has been dead about Twenty Years and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Tomlin a Tobacconish in Hedge Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings hathquitted her said Service about Six Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day Octr. 1779 before.}  Wm. Kitchiner

her Sarah Slater Mark
