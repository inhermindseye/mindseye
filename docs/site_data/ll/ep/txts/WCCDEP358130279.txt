Middx.  John Westerfar aged abt. 31 Yrs. upon Oath says abt. fifteen Years ago he lived a hired Yearly Servant for the space of two Years with one Moses Jennings at the three Tuns in St. James's Back in the Parish of St. Stephen in the City of Bristol he being at that time a Single Man , says he hath a Wife named Ann & two Children John aged four Years & Joseph aged Eight Months & never Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Febry 15th. 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of John Westerfar
