Middx  Ann Stockton aged about 20 years Upon Oath says She never was married That her Father William Stockton Rented a House in the Parish of Droitwick in the County of Worcester and lived in the same several Years & paid to the Poor there as She has been informed and believes, and further saith She never Rented a House or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 22d. Janry. 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Stockton
