Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Barry aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that about Fourteen Months ago she Intermarried with James Barry her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields that her said Husband has deserted this Examinant about Four Months that she has Two Children living by her said Husband vizt. Diana and Mary both Turns born in the Month of January last that she this Examinant and her Two Children in the Month of June last past were passed from this Parish to the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the west upon the information of her Husband that he lived Twelve Months and upwards with Mr. Cox in Mitre Court Fleet Street in the Said Parish of Saint Dunstan as a hired yearly Servant that this Examt. has does not know where her said Husband was born whether he served any Apprenticeship Rented House Tenement on Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or where he belonged that the said Parish of Saint Dunstan Appealed against the said Order of Remove and proved that he was a weekly Servant with Mr. Cox Strange and not a yearly Servant whereupon the Sessions quashed the Order and this Examinant further On her Oath Saith that before she Intermarried with James Barry she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Miss Lock in Bedford Street Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the yearly Wages of seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Two years since which time she has done no Act to gain a except so intermarrying with the said James Barry above letter she not being is above Stated Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Septr. 1788  Sampn Wright G Mercer

her Mary Barry Mark
