Middx  Ann Newman  Wido.  passed from the parish of St. Jas: Clerkenwell by Order Dated the 3d. Octr. Instt: upon her Oath saith that on or about the 21st: Day of October 1737 she intermarried with her late Husband Henry Newman at the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn that the sd. Henry Newman hath been Dead 4 Yrs. & upwds: that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mr Johnson a Carpenter in Saint Clements Lane in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx that since the Expiration of his said Apprentiship the sd. Henry Newman in his Life or this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath One Child (to wit) Ann aged Six Years & being Poor & troubled with Fitts is not capable of maintaining herself & Child

Sworne 9th. Octr. 1744 before Wm. Jones

Ann Newman
