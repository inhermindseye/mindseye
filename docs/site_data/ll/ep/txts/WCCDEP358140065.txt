Middx.  Sarah Linys aged abt. 23 Yrs. lodging in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. upon Oath says she never was married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Simpson a Cloth Merchant in Red Cross Street in the town of Liverpool in the County of Lancaster for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds & hath quitted said Service upwards of two Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year and further upon her voluntary oath says that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born says that Edwd. Williams who now lives a Servant with one Mr. Monday a Tripe Man in St. Clemts. Lane had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body in the Month of June last & at several other times since at one of

of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn this 30th. day of December 1757 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Sarah Linys
