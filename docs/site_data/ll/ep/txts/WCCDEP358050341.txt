Middx  Sarah Pere upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Thomas Pere at the Fleet London about 15 years ago that she has been informed and verily believes her sd. husband was bound Apprentice for 7 years to his father Thomas Pere  Carpenter and after her sd. Husband had Served about Six Years of his sd. Apprentiship his sd father dyed and that she has been further Informed her sd. husband lay the last two months of his said Apprentiship in Hanover Yard Tyburn Road in the parish of and that her sd. husband never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 19th. 1750 before}
