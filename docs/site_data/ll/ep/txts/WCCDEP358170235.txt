Middx  Sarah Parsons aged Sixty four Years upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido . of John Parsons Deceased that she now lives in & Rents a House in Horshoe Court in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds that she hath one Daughter viz: Sarah aged abt. Twenty Years who is Troubled with Convection Fitts & this Examinant being Poor & Infirm is not able to maintain herself & Daughter

Sworne the 26th. May 1742 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Sarah Parsons
