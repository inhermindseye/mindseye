Saint Clement Danes  Decr- 3rd. 1784

Middlesex  o St. Geo. Bloomby (to wit) Mary Milford aged forty three Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that She never was married Saith that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Morley in Southampton Street  Bloomsbury Square in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for upwards of Fourteen Months at the Wages of Seven Guineas by the Year - That she quitted her said Place about five Years ago, and since that time hath not rented any House or Tenement of ten Pounds a Year Value, lived for one Year as an hired Yearly Servant or done any other Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information and belief She may have gained a Settlement elsewhere than in the said Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury as above is stated

Sworn this 3rd. Decr 1784 before G Mercer Jno: Wright

Mary Milford
