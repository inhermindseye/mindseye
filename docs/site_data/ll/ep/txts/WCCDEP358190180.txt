Middx  Elizabeth Bellard aged about 40 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married or bound an Apprentice That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Moore Woolen draper  the back of St. Clement's in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds or thereabouts Quitted the Same about Twelve Years and hath not since rented any House paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes nor been a hired Yearly Servant for One year

Sworn the 15th. day of Septemr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Bellard
