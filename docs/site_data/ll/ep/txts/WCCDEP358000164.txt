Middlesex  Mid Exn. of Mary Graves taken 3d. Novr. 85. where he is chargd. with Child of which she Prest name  Samuel Haynes Aged 23 Years or thereabouts Maketh Oath and Saith That about Ten Years ago he was Bound an Apprentice for seven Years by Indenture To James Flackson of Broad Street in the Bath in the City of Bath and Wells  Peruke Maker and Hair Dresser and Served his Time duly out with the said James Flackson and hath not since Rented a House or lived as a Yearly Servant or gained any other legal Settlement other than as above mentioned to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn the 7th: day of December 1785 before me D. Walker

Samuel Haines
