Middlesex ss.  Sarah Wade the Widow of John Wade maketh Oath that her said late Husband about Nine Years ago lived in and Rented a House of Nine Guineas a Year in Pennington Street in the Parish of St. George in the County of Middlesex and lived there about One Year and was charged to and paid the Poors Rate for the same that her said Husband did not after he quitted the said House live in or Rent any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds a Year or pay any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or do any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor has she since her said Husbands Death done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this Day of March 1789 before}
