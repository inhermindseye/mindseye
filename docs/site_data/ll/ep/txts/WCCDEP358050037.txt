Middlesex 

Eliz: Neale aged abt. 36 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that about Ten Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Smith of New Jun in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Attorney at Law at the Yearly Wages of £5..10..0 for One Year and that she was discharged Mr Smith Service at Wickham in Essex after having been there with the family upwards of ten months to the best of her Remembrance and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is

Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being Pror and unable to maintain the Same when born and that Wm Doubleday Carpenter did beget the sd. Child or Children on body the sd. Wm. Doubleday having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant Sevl. times in the month of Septr. last and at sevl. other times six at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that he the sd. Wm. Doubleday & no other person is the true and real father the

Sworn april 20th 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Eliz. Neale
