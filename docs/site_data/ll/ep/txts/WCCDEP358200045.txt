Middx  Lucy Kally Aged about 22 Years upon Oath says she was never Marryed says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Anguish in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about three Weeks and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likeley to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says One Stephen Morris a Footman to the said Mr. Anguish who is since gone away but where this Examinant Cannot say did beget the said Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant

sometime in the Month of April Last and at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 7th day of December 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Lucy Kally

Upon Mr Olivers Complt J. W
