Middx  Ann Cherrington aged about 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Ironson Attorney at Law in Downing Street Westmr. in the parish of St. Margt. in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of £6 for one Year & upwards and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was taken in Labour in the Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes Middx and was brought to a parish Nurses of the sd. Parish was on the 8th. Day of June Instant & delivered of a male bastard Child which has been baptized Thomas & is Chargeable to the sd. Parish and that Thomas Green  Clerk to Mr. Strut of Breams Buildings Chancery Lane did beget the said Male bastard Child on her body the sd. Thomas Green having had carnal knowledge of this Examt.

in the month of Septr. last & at several other times since at one of which times he get her with Child of the said male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. and that the sd. Thomas Green & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn 27th. June 1751 before Tho Lane Ann Cheriton
