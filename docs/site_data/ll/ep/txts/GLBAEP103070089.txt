Middx ss  James Church Maketh oath that about eighteen Years ago he rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year in the Parish of West bury upon Trim in ye County of Gloucester and paid all Parish Taxes there and Served the office of Constable in ye Sd. Parish and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Dept. Saith that he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds by ye Year or upwards Nor Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a Wife Named Mary And a Child Named Mary aged about fifteen Months the lawfull issue of this Deponent and his Said Wife

Sworn the 24th day of September 1753 before  Richd: Ricards

James Church
