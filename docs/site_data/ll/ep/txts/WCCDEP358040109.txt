Middx.  Mary Lockyear  Wido . of Thomas Lockyear upon Oath saith she intermarried with her said late husband at the Fleet London about 17 Years since Says the sd. Thos. Lockyer whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Peter White Esqr. in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year & a half or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds Says her sd. late Husband never after rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief Says she has not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has one Child by the sd. Thos. Lockyer viz. Mary aged near 14 Yrs. who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Novr. 26th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Lockyear
