Middx  Sarah Peacock on the Behalfe of William Peacock her Son aged abt. Eleven Years upon her Oath saith that abt. Nineteen Years ago Ralph Peacock her Husband lived in and rented a House in Whitechappel of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds for the Space of Six Months and upwards that the sd. Wm. Peacock never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 17th- of Septr- 1741 before} Jas: Gronous  Chas. Egerton

The mark of Sarah Peacock
