Westmr.  Ann Myrick aged upwards of 29 Years upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Robert Graham Cook for the Space of one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds Says she lived the last Eight Months and upwards of her sd. Service with the sd. Mr. Graham in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says she hath left her said Service about Eighteen Months Says she hath not since she left the said Mr. Grahams Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 4th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Myrick
