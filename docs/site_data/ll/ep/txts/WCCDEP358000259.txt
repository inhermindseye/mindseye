Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Sarah Clarke aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servt with one Mr. Rumley in Clare Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for 18 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of £4 hath quitted said Service about 3 years ago since which time she hath gained no or Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard and Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes And further on her Oath Saith that one Alexr. Everidge a Journey man Butcher who lives with one Mr. Rudley at Cupid Bridge is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of July last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she is now pregnant and that the said Accot. Everidge and no other person did beget the said Child and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

20th. taken & release given

Sworn this 24th. day of March 1786 before me  Sampr Wright

her Sarah Clarke Mark
