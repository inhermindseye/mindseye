Middx  Wm Marphey upon Oath saith, about forty years ago he Rented a house of £10 by the Year & upwds. in Jermain Street in the parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. and lived therein about three years and that he has not since he quitted the sd house rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 11th. 1750 before Fran: Bedwell

Wm Morphy
