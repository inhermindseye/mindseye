Middx  Frances Hind Aged about 25 Years Lodging at Mrs. Webleys in Houghton Street in ye Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . Upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Fras. Matthews in Houghton Street in the same Parish Shoemaker did beget the said Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the Month of February Last and at several Other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant Says she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mrs. Webley in

Houghton Street aforesaid for Seven Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted her said upwards of Seven Months and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 18th Day of October 1763 : Before} Fran: Bedwell

Francs hind

Upon Mr Robt Williams Complaint Ovr J W
