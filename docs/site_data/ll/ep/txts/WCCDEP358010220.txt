Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Hannah Bentley aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. John Bannister the Comedian  in Great Russell Street Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden for about Eighteen Months and in Bow Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden for Six Months that after she quitted said Service she nerver lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That she hath One Child living Vizt. James aged about 5 years born a Bastard at Camberwell in the County of Surry

Sworn this 14th. day od June 1788} Addington Edw: Read

her Hannah Bentley  Mark
