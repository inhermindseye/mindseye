Middx  Mary Reeves on Behalfe of her Son  John Reeves a Lunatick aged 22 Years & upwds. upon her Oath Saith the sd. John Reeves was bound an Apprentice to & Served near Six Years the first Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Legg in Grays Inn Passage in the Parish of St Andrew in Holborne in the County of Middlesex  Cooper & then Listed himself in the 1st: Regimt. of Foot Guards Says the sd. John Reeves never was a Yearly hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 20th. 1744 before} Tho Lane .

mary reeves
