Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Hannah Swales of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Single woman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this first day of December one thousand Seven hundred and eighty four Who Saith that on the eighth day of October now last past at the Workhouse in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate She the Said Hannah Swales was delivered of a Female Bastard Child since baptized & registred by the name of Isabella Swales and that the Said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that Richard Appleton of the Parish of Wandsworth in the County of Surry Callicoe Fieldman did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child.

Taken and Signed the day & year above written before me  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Hannah Swales
