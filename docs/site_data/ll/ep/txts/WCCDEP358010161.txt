Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Brown of No: 162 in the Strand in  the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of James Thomas an Infant aged about 11 weeks whose Mother is ill in St. George Hospital Maketh Oath and Saint that the said James Thomas was born in the workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields

Sworn this 16th. day of Novr. 1787 N Bond Thos. Gordon

Mary Brown
