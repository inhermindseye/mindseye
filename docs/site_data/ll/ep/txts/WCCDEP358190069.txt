Middx  Frances Sumner aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath she is the Wife of John Sumner ( gone from her ) by whom she has One Child named Frances aged abt. 4 Months says her said husband informed her & which information she verily believes to be true that the served three Years and upwards the first part of his Apprentice ship with Mr Brittenbeigh a Taylor in Oxenden Street in the Haymarket in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. & then parted by consent & never was assigned Over to any Person nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year to this Examinant knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 13th. 1760 . before} Tho Lane  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Frances Sumner

Upon Mr Elvins's Complt. J: P:
