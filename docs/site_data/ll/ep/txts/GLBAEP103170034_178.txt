Middlesex ss}  Ann Brown  Singlewoman maketh oath that about five years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one Mr Carolinia Statuary  in the parish of St Ann at Westmr in the County of Middlesex at Certain yearly Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one year & five Months & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for a year nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 16. day of December 1776 before  Jasper Clarkes  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Ann Brown
