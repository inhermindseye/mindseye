Middx  Margt: Forster on the Behalfe of Mary Griffen  Wido . of Wm. Griffen upon her Oath saith that the Sd. Wm. Griffen hath been dead abt. Thirteen Months & that the Sd. Wm. Griffen in his Life Time lived in & rented a House in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & upwds for the Space of Four Months & upwds & that since he left the Sd. House he did not in his Life nor hath the Sd. Mary Griffen since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Yr. Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Yr. or other- -wise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as She hath been Informed & believes

Sworn the 29th. of Jan:1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Margt. Forster
