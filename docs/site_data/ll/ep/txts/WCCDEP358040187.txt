Westmr.  John Acret upon Oath saith he intermarried with his present Wife Ann in the month of Augt. 1740 at the Fleet London and that before his sd. Intermarriage & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Coats at the John & Jerusalem Alehouse in St. John Street in the Parish of St. James at Clerkenwell in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of three pounds for one Year & upwards & quitted his sd. Service upwds. of 14 Years ago since which time he has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 12th. May 1748 before} Tho Lane

John Acret
