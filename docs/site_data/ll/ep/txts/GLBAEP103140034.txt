Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Wilkes  Singlewoman maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents or either of them That She this Deponent was born in Fox Court in the Liberty above the Bars in the Parish of Holborn in the County of Middlesex That She this Depont was never Married nor was ever bound apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right That She hath a Child named Ann Pearce aged near Four years which Child is a Bastard & was born in the Parish of St Mary White Chapel in the said County of Middlesex

Sworn this 23d day of April 1774 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

The Mark of Elizabeth Wilkes
