Middx  Ann Applebee aged 24yrs upon Oath saith she is the daughter of Wm. & Frances Applebee both deceased and that she never was an apprentice or yearly Servant rented ten pounds a year or paid parish taxes and never was married and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. late father Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Mimms a Taylor at the Corner of White horse yard in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and never was a an apprentice or yearly Servant rented ten Pounds a year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn Janry 7th. 1753 -before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Applebee
