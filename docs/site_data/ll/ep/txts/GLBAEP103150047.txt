Middlesex ss}  Ann Mathews the Widow of Owen Mathews deced maketh oath That during the years one thousand Seven hundred & fifty nine & one thousand Seven hundred & Sixty & for divers Years before her said late husband rented a house of the yearly Rent of Eight pounds in Parkhurst Lane in the parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx & that during Such his Residence therein he paid the Kings Tax for the same (& in Particular for one Mr. William Heritage a Collecter ) to divers of the Collectors of the said Tax who from time to time Came to require payment thereof she this Deponent being often present when Such payments were made & this Deponent once paid the same herself That She this Deponent has been informed by Mr Thomas Bampfield  Vestry Clerk of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate Middx & which She Verily believes to be true that he having lately Searched the Duplicates or Books of the Land Tax Assessmt. made for the said Parish of St Giles in the Fields for the said Years one thousand Seven hundred & fifty nine & one thousand Seven hundred & Sixty in the Custody of Stephen Clarke of Hart Street Bloomsbury  Esq . he finds the said Owen Mathews to be Charged therein for those Years as living then in Parkhurst Lane aforesd. by means of which said charge & payments this Deponts. said late husband as she is Advised & believes gained a legal Settlement there That her Said husband died in the Sd. house & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere & that She this Deponent Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 31st day of July 1775 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Mathews
