St Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster }  St Andrew Middx } (to wit) John Vaillant aged 43 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he Rented and resided in an House in Eyre Street in the parish of St Andrew Holbor above the Bars  in the County of Middlesex for about Eight years at the Annual Rent of 12 Guineas and paid all parochial rate for the same that he quitted that House about Midsummer 1792 and hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid any parish Taxes served any parochial office or done any other Act or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement

Sworn the 1st. day of October 1792 before us M Addington J Hitchen

Jno. Vaillant
