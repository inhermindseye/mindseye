Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Elmore aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with James Elmore her late Husband about Six years at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bone by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. William aged about Three years and James aged about Nine Months That her said late Husband informed this Examinant that while he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Evans a Farmer at Kingsbury in the Parish of Kingsbury in the County of Middx for Twelve Months and upwards and that after he quitted said Service he never gained any subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paying Taxes or living as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months.

Sworn this 9th. day of Janry. 1796 } Edw. Read J Hind

Elizabeth Elmore
