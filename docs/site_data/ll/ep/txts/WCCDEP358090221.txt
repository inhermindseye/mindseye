Middx to wit Elizabeth Ford aged about Twenty Nine years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a Hired yearly Servant with Mr. Aylmer in Bowell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One year at the yearly Wages of Nine Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Months ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 9th. day of April Last she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in a Hackney Coach in Wych Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of William and is now Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same and further says that One Joseph Rue late a Footman to her said Master did beget such Bastard Child on her Body in her said masters House and that the said Jos: Rue and no other Person did beget the same and that He is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 19th day of May 1781 before Helen Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth. Ford  Mark
