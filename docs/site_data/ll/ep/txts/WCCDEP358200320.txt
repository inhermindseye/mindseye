Middx & Westmr.}  Hannah Jones aged about 25 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Fairweather at the Crown Alehouse in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, hath quitted said Service about ten Months and hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes; And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 16th. day of October last she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Sadd one of the Parish Nurses of the said Parish of St. Clement Danes in Old Boswell Court in the said Parish which hath since been baptized Hannah and is now living and chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes , says that James Crisp who keeps the Rose Alehouse in Cursiters Street Chancery Lane London had carnal knowledge of her body on the Seventh day of January last and at several other times since, at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said James Crisp and no other Person is the true and Real Father thereof

Sworn the 13th. Novr. 1765 . Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Hannah Jones
