Middlesex ss}  John Shea maketh oath that in or about the year one thousand seven hundred & Sixty Seven he took and rented a house at they early Rent of nine pounds of Mr Minnitt in Nightingale Lane in the parish of St John wapping in the sd County & Continued in possion there of for near the space of a year & during such his renting the same he was Charged to & paid kings Taxes for the same & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere.

Sworn this. 26 day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

Jno. Shea
