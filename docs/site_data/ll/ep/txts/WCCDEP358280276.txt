Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Chamberlayne aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the Sixth day of March last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Jane That one William Salter who lives at Mr. Burton in Kent Street  a Mop spinner is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Wm. Salter is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 6th. day of April 1790 } Wm Kitchiner

her Jane Chamberlayne Mark
