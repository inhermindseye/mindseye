Middx  Elizabeth Brown aged about 31 Years upon Oath says She never was Married and that She lived a hired yearly Servant upwards of Two Years with Mr. Williams a Victualler at the sign of the White Lyon - in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the yearly Wages of Four pounds ten Shillings Says She quitted her said Service about nine Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes on lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 10th. Day of April 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Elizth. Brown
