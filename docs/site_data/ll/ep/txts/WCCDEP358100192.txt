Middlesex to wit Elizth. Carter Aged about Sixteen years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she was bound out an Apprentice by the Churchwdns. and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Margaretts Westmr . to one John Delarouch who kept the Sign of the Green Man in Table Street  White Chaple in the Parish of St. Mary White Chaple in the County of Middlesex and served her said Master under such Indre upwards of Three years & a  & untill such times as her said Master became Insolvent And further says she never gained any or. Settlemt. than as aforesaid

Sworn this 4th. day of March 1784  Thos: Gilbert W Gregson
