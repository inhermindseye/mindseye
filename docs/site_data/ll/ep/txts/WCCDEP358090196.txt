Middx  Ann Gabell Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or was an Apprentice Says She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Ballentine a Taylor in Southampton Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middx for 2 years and Upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Nine Months ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 8th. Day of February 1781  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Ann Gabell mark
