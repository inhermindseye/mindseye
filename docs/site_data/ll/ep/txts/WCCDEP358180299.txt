Middx  Mary Swan aged 23 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she was bound as Apprentice by Articles of Agreemt. & Served two Years Apprentiship with Mrs. Wiscard in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent Says she never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Febry 27th: 1744 before} Wm: Jones .

Mary Swan
