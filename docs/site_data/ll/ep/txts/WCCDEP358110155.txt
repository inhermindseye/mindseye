Middx  Lucy Key upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Davis in Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for four Years & upwds. at the yearly wages of £7 and has left her sd. Service abt. 7 Yrs. since which she has not rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Sevt. for one Year

Sworn Janry. 27th 1753 Before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Lucy Key
