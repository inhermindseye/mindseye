Georgiana Mary Wedge

St. Clement Danes 29th. March 1794

Middx and Westr.}  St. John the Evangelist  (to wit) Sarah Wedge aged 34 Years and upwards Wife of Wm. Wedge now Serving in his Magestys army abroad, on behalf of an illegitimate Child named Georgiana Mary Wedge, Upon her Oath saith that the said Georgiana Mary was born on or about the 1st. of Novr. 1785 at the House No. 4 Peter Street in the Parish of St. John the Evangelist  Westr . of the Body of her this Examinant who was then single and had never been Married

Sworn the 29th. day of March 1794 before us} Richard Ford B: Kennett

her Sarah Wedge Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
