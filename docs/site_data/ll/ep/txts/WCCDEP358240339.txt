St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Holliwell parish City of Oxford shire  Removed by Mr Blundell Beadle of the Rolls Liberty  (to wit) Ann Harman  singlewoman aged sixty six years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that on or about the sixth day of April 1751 she lived was hired as a Servant to Mr Samuel Hatton who lived at a House called the Cock Pit in the parish of Holliwell in the City of Oxford at the Annual Wages of £3. where she lived in that capacity for about six or eight Months and then went to see her Mother but did not stay away more than two Months and then returned to her said Master's service again and continued with him as such Servant til-about the Michaelmas 1752 when she quitted his service And this Deponent saith that her said Master did not pay off or discharge her this Examinant when she so visited her said Mother as aforesaid neither did this Examinant's said Master take any Servant to supply her place during such Absence but received her again immediately on her return from her Mother's and continued her in his service upon the Terms aforesaid without any seeming-disapprobation or any fresh Agreement except that she this Examinant offered to serve over the time which she had been about with which her said Master readily acquiesced And this Examinant further saith that she quitted her said Service about Michaelmas 1752 and hath not since done any Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge recollection or belief

Sworn the 19th day of October 1793 before us Wm Kitchiner D Walker}

Ann Harman
