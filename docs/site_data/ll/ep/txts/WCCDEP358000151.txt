St. Clement Danes

Swears to St. Clemt.  Mary Phillips aged 68 Years or thereabouts when her Oath saith that she never was Married, Rented a House or paid Taxes or ever served an Apprenticeship saith That she lived as an Hired Yearly Servant with Messrs. Heath and Wing of of the strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of 4 Years at the Yearly Wages of £7:10:0 hath quitted her said service about 11 Years age since which time  she this Examinant. hath not done why Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. 1785 before me R Butler

her Mary Phillips Mark
