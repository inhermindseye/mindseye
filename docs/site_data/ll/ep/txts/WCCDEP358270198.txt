Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Murray aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Jackson in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Six pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Two years since which time she hath not lived as an hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes when born That Thomas Lawrence who lives with Mr. Caldw a Surgeon & Apothecary in Black Fryars Road is the only real and true father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of February last and at several times since some or one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Thomas Lawrence and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 4th. day of October 1794 } J Hind

her Elizabeth Murray mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
