Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Dymott  Widw . of Walter Dymott Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 5th. Day of Novr. 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that her Husband Walter Dymott lived in & Rented a House in Kings Head Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £25 & lived in the same for the space of one Year & upwards And further saith yt. her sd. Husband after he left the sd. House rented Sevll. Tapps of Inns & paid so much P Barrell for Drawing the Beer according as the Inn Keeper and her sd. Husband sd. Agree And that her sd. Husband in his Life time or she this Examinant since his Death never after Rented any other House paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife And yt. she now is very Poor & haveing two Small Children (to wit) Richard Aged 4 Years & upwds - & Jno . Aged One Month is not able to maintaine herselfe & them

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. Dymott
