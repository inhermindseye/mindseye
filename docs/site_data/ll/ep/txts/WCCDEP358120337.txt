Middx  Cathn. Leonard aged near 36 years upon Oath Saith She was married to John Leonard in the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes near 19 years ago and that her sd. husband lived in rented a house of the yearly Rent of £27 in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . known by the Sign of the Spotted dog and lived therein near four Years and quitted the Same about 16 years ago, Since which neither her sd. late husbd. nor she has not rented ten Pounds by the year Paid Parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn May 14th. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Cathrine Linord
