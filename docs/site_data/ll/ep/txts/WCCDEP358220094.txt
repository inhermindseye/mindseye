Middlesex ss  William Lomax aged about Forty Years on his Oath says he never was Married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Woodatch a Butcher in the Parish of Saint Lukes Chelsea in the County of Middlesex for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Ten Shillings and after he quitted that Service he lived as Butcher at the Royal Hospital at Chelsea for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Ten Shillings besides Lodging and Diet hath quitted his said Service about 14 Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 2d day of December before me} R. Butler

his William Lomax mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
