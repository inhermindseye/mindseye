Middlesex  Ann Murphey wife of Benjamin Murphey (gone from her) upon Oath saith she Inter- marryd with the Sd. Benjn. Murphey at the Fleet London upwards of three years ago and has one Child by him (viz) Benjamin aged about nine months Says she hath been Informed & verily believes her sd. husband Served his Apprentiship with a Taylor in the Town of Carmarthen in South Wales and never Rented Ten pounds of a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Octr. 13th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Murphey
