Middx 

Ann Warner removed from the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. by Order dated the 5th. of Septr. Instant upon Oath saith she is the Widow of William Warner who dyed abt. Years ago Says her sd. late husband lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £48 next Door to the Rose Tavern near Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year & upwards and there dyed and this Examinant continued in the sd. house a few weeks after her husbands decease and has not since she left it rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 6th. 1748 before}
