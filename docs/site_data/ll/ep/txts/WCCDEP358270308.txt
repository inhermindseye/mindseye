Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Weston aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she was born at Hull in the County of york That on or about the Twelfth day of August now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Gibbs in Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the name of Elizabeth That one David Mills a Journeyman Baker who now lives with Mr. Ossley in Long Alley  Moorfields is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examts. Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid there one and that he is the only Real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 26th. day of May 1797 before me } John Collick

her Mary Weston Mark
