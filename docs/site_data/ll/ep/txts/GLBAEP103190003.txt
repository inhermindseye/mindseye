Middlesex ss}  Priscilla Tilly maketh oath that divers Years ago she was hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages with John Way at the Cock & Lyon in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued therein such Service under such hiring for the Space of Thirteen Years & upwards & was never married nor hath she done any act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 10. day of February 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Priscilla Tilly
