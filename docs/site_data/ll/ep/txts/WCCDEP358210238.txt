Westmr.  Thomas Everett aged about Forty seven Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Ann his Wife at May Fair Chapel in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster about Seventeen Years ago, by whom he hath Two Children (to wit) Rebecca Aged about Ten Years and Ann Aged about Seven Years and three Quarters says he served Seven Years Ap- prenticeship with Mr. Joseph Dautry , Taylor in Hollis Street and in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster aforesaid, and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 2d. day of Febry. 1768 Before} Paul Vaillant .

Thos Everitt
