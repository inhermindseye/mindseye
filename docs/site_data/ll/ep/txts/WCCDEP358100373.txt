Saint Clement Danes

own Settlemt. St. Clemts.  Mary Ann St Brides James St. Botolph Sarah Ann Morltake Eliz St. Clemts.  Elizabeth Breedon commonly Called Elizh Limb Aged 36 or Thereabouts Upon her Oath Saith That She never was Married That about 16 Years Ago she lived with Mr Breedon of Arundell Street Living Stable Keeper as Five Pounds a Year That she lived with him some Years That after she left his Service she went to live with John Limb a Lighterman That she had Seven Children by him Four of which are now Living Vizt. Mary Ann aged Nine Years now with Mrs Thompson Wife, & Saml Thompson a Trunk Maker living Opposite to London Street Tottenham Court Road which Mary Ann was Born in Mr Lucass Rents in Hanging Sword Alley  white Fryers in the Parish of Saint Brides London and his Father was the said John Limb  James aged abt Seven Years born at the House of Mr Moore in Red Cross Street East Smith, held in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate who was also by the said John Limb  Sarah Ann aged Three Years or thereabouts who was born at the House of the said John Limb at Morltake in the County of Surry was by the said John Limb and Elizth . aged 5 Weeks who was Born in St Clement Danes Workhouse That he the said John Limb died about Seven Months ago That since she quitted Mr Breedons she has not gained any Settlement

Sworn this Fourth day of March 1785 before  Jno Wright  Jno Barnfather

her Elizabeth Breedon Mark
