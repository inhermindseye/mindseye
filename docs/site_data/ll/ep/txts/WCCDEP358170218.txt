Middx  John Cramp upon his Oath saith that he is a Singleman & was never married & that about Twenty Years ago he was bound an Apprentice for Four Years to one Mr. Long at the Two Brewers in Water Lane in the Parish of St. Ann in Black- -fryers  London  Victualler & Waterman that this Examinant served him as an Apprentice for the said Term of Four Years & hath not since his Sd. Apprentiship rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 12th. of April 1742 before} Jas: Gronous  Thos. Lane

The Mark of John Cramp
