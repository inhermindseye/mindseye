Westmr.  Elizabeth Ridough aged about 46 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Ridough (gone from her), Says her said husband Rented a house in little Suffolk Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds lived therein about two Years, and hath quitted same about about Seven Years and hath not since Rented house or Paid Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn the 8th, day of October 1766 , before} Fran. Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth. Ridough
