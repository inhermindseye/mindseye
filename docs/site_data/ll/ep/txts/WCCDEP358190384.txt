Middx  Richard Evans Aged about 43 Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife in the Parish Church of St Bridgets Vulgarly St. Brides in the City of London about Fourteen Years Ago Says he Never Rented House Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says his Father Richard Evans Rented a House and Farm in the Parish of Kennerley in the County of Salop upward of Twenty Eight Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and Upwards and Paid all Taxes

Sworn the 24 Day of Septr. 1763 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Richard Evans

Upon Mr Bedwell Complaint J: Wilson
