Middx.  Mary Jackson aged abt. 68 Years upon Oath says she is the widow of Jno. Jackson who has been dead upwds. of thirty Years says her said late husband informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that he served his Apprenticeship of Seven years with Mr. Delander a Watchmaker in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year says she has not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn this 24th. day of January 1758 . before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Jackson
