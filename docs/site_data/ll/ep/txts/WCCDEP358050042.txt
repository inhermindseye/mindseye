Westmr. 

Esther Ankell aged abt. 42 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 16 Years ago she lived yearly Servt. with Mr Symson a Print sall in Russell Court in the Parish of St. Mar in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. at the yearly Wages of 40s for one Year & upwds. & has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 1st. May 1749 before}

The mark of Esther Ankell
