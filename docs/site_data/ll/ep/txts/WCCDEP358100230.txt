Middlesex to wit Mary Rippen Aged about Sixty Years on behalf of Harry Morris her Son (who now lyes ill of Consultions on her Oath Says that her said Son Rented and lived in a House in Britania Row near Westmr. Bridge in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Guineas and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Eight Years ago since which time he never gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of July 1784 before  John Machin G Mercer

her Mary Rippin Mark
