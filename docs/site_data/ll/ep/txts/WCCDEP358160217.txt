Middx  The Examination of Sarah Carr  Widow of Thos. Carr aged 52 Years & upwards taken ye. 22d. Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. abt. Years ago She intermarryed with the above named Thos. Carr & that her sd. late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Exaiant & whilst he was Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Bennett Esqr. in Essex Street in ye. Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of Yrs. & this Exaiant further saith yt. her sd. Husband never after left his sd. Service rented 10 Pounds a Yr, pd. parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif & that she is Poor & Came & not able to maintaines herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
