Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Spears aged about 56 years upon her Oath saith that she was married to one Wm. Spears at Ireland about 38 years and who has been dead upwds. of one year and that she has two Children living vizt. George a provided for that her Husband was an Fishman and never gained any Settlement in England that before her Intermarriage she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Humphry Roberts who kept the Union Coffee House in the parish of St. Clement Danes for One year and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas hath quitted said Service about 39 years ago since which time she hath done no Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16 th. day of March 1786  Wm Hitchiner

her Sarah Spears Mark
