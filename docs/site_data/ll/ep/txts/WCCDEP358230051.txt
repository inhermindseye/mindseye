Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Hall aged about 22 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a lived yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born at Eltham in the Parish of Eltham in the County of Kent where her father Rented House for Several years and paid Taxes

Sworn this 23d. day of March1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

her Elizabeth Hall Mark
