Middlesex ss  Jane Blore single woman maketh oath that several Years ago she lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages with James Wylde of East Smithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Victualler and Continued in such service for the Space of three Years and by Means there of as she this Depont. is advised gained a legal settlement in the Said Parish And this Depont. Saith that she was never Married Nor hath she since her servitude as above lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement.

Sworn this 9th. day of January 1766 before  Chris: Scott

Jane Blore Her Mark
