Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Wild aged about 61 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Gawtho a Grocer in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Four Guineas that She hath quitted said Service about Ten weeks since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3rd. day of Janry. 1797 G S Goodenough

her Mary Wild Mark
