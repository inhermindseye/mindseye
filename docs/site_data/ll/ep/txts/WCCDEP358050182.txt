Middx  Mary West upon Oath saith she was born in the Parish of North End in the Town and County of Buckingham where her father has for many years past and still does Live in a house of his own and pay Last & Lot and that she this Examinant never did any Act whereby to gain & Settlemt. in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 2d. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of  Mary West
