Westmr. 

Frances Lee aged abt. 22 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice by the Church wardens & Overseers of the poor of the parish of St Botolph Aldersgate to one Jonathan Haines till she shd. attain the Age of 21 Yrs. & lived with the sd Haines under that Indenture about 18 months at the End of which time she went & lived with one Mrs. Freeman in Cherry tree Alley  Golden Lane  in the parish of St Luke in the County of Middx for 1 Yr. and Six Months or there abouts with her master Haines knowledge & Consent & the thesd. Mrs. Freeman dyed & the sd. Jonathan Haines never afterwards Concerned himself about her but this Examinant sold fruit about the Streets & maintained herself

Sworn 27th. July & 1750 before} Ja: Praser

The Mark of Frances Lee
