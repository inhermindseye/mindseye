St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Mary Smith  Widow Aged 31 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband William Smith at the parish Church of St. Martin in the fields abt in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County of Middlesex about fourteen or fifteen years ago that previous there to and while he was Single and unmarried he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Boon a Butcher the Corner of Bedford Bury in Chandor Street in the said parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex aforesaid for Six years & upwards at the Annual Wages of £5.10:0-that he quitted that service about 18 or 19 years ago and gained no Subsequent legal Settlement during his Life time, that he Died on the 30th. day of November last and since this Deponent hath not been Married nor acquired any other legal settlement That she hath no Child now living by her said Husband Named Thomas Aged Four years Ten Months ten Months & upwards And Saith that She is poor and unable to Maintain herself and Child

Sworn the 21st. day of Febry. 1792 before us D Walker R Butler

Mary Smith 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
