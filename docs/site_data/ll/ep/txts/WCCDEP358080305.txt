Middx  Rebecca Hendry Aged about Sixty five Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Peter Hendry with whom she Intermarried at Saint Martins in the Fields about 28 Years ago who died about five Years and by whom she hath no Children living Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in White Hore Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for several Years at the Yearly Rent of £18 and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Twelve Years ago since which time her said Husband never Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th- day of Octr. 1778 before R Butler

her Rebecca Hendry Mark
