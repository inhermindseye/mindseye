Middx  Elizabeth Davis Aged about 25 Years Upon Oath saith She is the Widow of Thomas Davis and that her said late Husband hath been dead about four Years Says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex where She now Lodges She being poor & unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Thomas Smith a Merchant who lives in Nevill's Court Fetter Lane in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London had carnal knowledge of her Body at a publick House in Holborn (but the Sign She cannot now particularly remember) Sometime in the Month of May last at which time her got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 21st. Day of January 1756 before} Theodr Sydenham

The mark of Elizth. Davis
