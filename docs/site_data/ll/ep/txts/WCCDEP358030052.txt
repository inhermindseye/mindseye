Middx 

Wm. Harper of the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr.  Mason on Behalf of Ann Harper an Infant aged about twelve Years upon his Oath saith that she is the Lawfull Issue of Valentine Harper by Grace his Wife both deceased as she hath been Generally always reputed & he verily beleives and that the sd. Valentine Harper Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Williams a Taylor in the Parish of St. Lawrance in Ludlow in the County of Salop Says the sd. Valentine Harper never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Exaiants knowledge or belief William Harper

Sworn 29th. Novr 1745 before  Tho Law
