Middx  Arthur Lewis aged abt. 20 Years upon his Oath saith that abt. Four Years ago he lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Thos. Milbourne in Beauford Buildings in the Parish of St- Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of 4 Yrs. & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & that since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Pish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 8th- Day of Septr- 1741 before}
