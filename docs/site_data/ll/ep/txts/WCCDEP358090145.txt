Middx to wit Maria Sorrell Aged about Thirty Years or her Oath says She is the Widow of George Sorrell who died in May last and with wh She Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Lawrence in Reading in the County of Berks Upwards of Eight Years ago and by whom she hath one Child living Named Thomas Aged about Seven Years and is now big with Child by her said late Husband of which She Expects to be brot. to bed the latter End of next Month says her said late Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Willis a Mealman in The Markett Place in the Parish of Saint Lawrence in the Borough of Redding in the said County of Berks and after quitting his said Masters Service he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paid any manner of Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 18th. day of July 1780 before R Butler Abrm Baley

Maria Surrill
