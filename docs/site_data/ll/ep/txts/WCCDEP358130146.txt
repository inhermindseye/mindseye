Middx  Sarah Petey aged about 25 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with William Petey her Husband at the Fleet London the 28th. of June in the year 1750 Says her said Husband is now at Sea on Board his Majesty's Ship the Prince Says that he was bound Apprentice by Indenture bearing Date the 5th. day of February in the Year 1742. to Cornelius Dawkins a Shoemaker whom he served as an Apprentice about two years in white Lyon Yard  Charterhouse Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchre in the County of Middx and then absconded from his Master and went to Sea Says her said Husband was not afterwards assigned over to any other Person nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year as She has been informed by him & believes

Sworn the 16th. Day of April 1756 before} Barth. Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Sarah Petey
