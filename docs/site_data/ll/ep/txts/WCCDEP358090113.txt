Middx  Sarah Rowlatt Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or Paid Ta Says She was bound an Apprentice or Covenanted Se to One Mary Blanchard a Silk Sorter in Throgmorton Street in the Parish of Saint Bennett Fink London And Served her said Mistress under such Indentures or Covenant for the space of five Years And after quitting her said Misstresses Service which is about 5 Years ago She never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly servant for One Year.

Sworn this 21st. day of January 1780 R Butler William Hyde

Sarah Rowlatt
