Middlesex ss}  Sarah White  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Captain Gould in Burr Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one year & upwards That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement & that She was never Married.

Sworn this 28. day of Decemr 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Sarah White
