Middx.  James Plunket aged upwards of 40 Years upon Oath says he was married to Ann his Wife at a house in Swallow Street by a Romish Priest abt. 2 Years ago says before his intermarriage & whilst he was a single Man he lived a Covenant Servt. for 2 Years certain with David Sly a Taylor at the Kings Arms Alehouse in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds for the first Year & five Pounds for the Second Year & hath quitted said Service abt. Twenty two Years ago & hath not since Gained a Settlement in any other Parish or Place to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 3d. of Janry 1758 before} Barth: Hammond

James Plunkett
