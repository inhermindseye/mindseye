Westmr. to wit Sarah Rochford Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Geo Rochford at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about Six Months Ago Says that her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Bride a Jerck maker in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship He never Rented House paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of August 1772 before} R. Butler

her Sarah Rochford Mark
