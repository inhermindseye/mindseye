St. Clement Danes

Westminster (to wit)  Charles Nutting aged 43 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he was bound apprentice to Henry Williams of Islington in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex  Baker by Indentures duly Executed for Seven years or and served the under the said Indentures upwards of 4 years, and that his Master then gave him up the remainder of his time, That he has been  in the Workhouse  in belonging to the said Parish of Saint James in Clerkenwell who and was discharged there from abt. 5 years ago That since serving such apprenticeship he hath not done any Act whereby her may have gained a subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 6th. day of Janry. 1786  Sampr Wright N Bond

his Chas. Nutting Mark
