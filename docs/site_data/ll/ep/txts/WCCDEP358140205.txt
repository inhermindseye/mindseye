Middx.  John Coneby aged abt. 48. Yrs. upon Oath says he was Married to Mary his wife in the Chapel of St. Nicholas at Kings Lyon in Norfolk abt. 28 Years ago. says he served five years & upwards the first part of his Apprenticeship with Richd. Hinde a Taylor in Greyhound Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and then absconed from his said Master and went to Cambridge and worked there with several Masters for six Months or thereabouts with the Privity of his said Master Rd. Hinde and lodged all that time at the Black Lyon Alehouse in Silver Street in the Parish of St. Botolph in Cambridge aforesaid and Never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt. for One Year and that he was born in the Parish of Stow Bardolph in the County of Norfolk . says his father John Coneby was a Gardener and Rented a house in the said Parish of Stow Bardolph of about £4 a Year & Paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Decr. 29th. 1758 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Jno. Coneby
