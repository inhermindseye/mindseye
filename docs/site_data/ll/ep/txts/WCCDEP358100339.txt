Middlesex and Westminster}  C passd. to St. Geo Blooms bury  Charles Harriott aged about 55 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Ruth Harrt . his now Wife at Christ Church Spittle fields about 30 years ago and by whom he hath one Child living (vizt.) Christiana aged about 18 years Says that since his Intermarriage to his said Wife he Rented and lived in a House in Duke Street Bloomsbury in the parish of Saint George Bloomsbury for 17 years at the yearly Rent of 12 Twelve Guineas and paid Taxes for the same hath quitted said House about 6 years since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 17th- day of Janry 1785 D Walker J Beete

his Charles Harriott mark
