Middx  Eliz: Smith upon her Oath saith yt- she is a Singlewoman & was never married & yt. abt. Twelve Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Gee at the Rose Alehouse in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of one Year & Six Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & further saith that she hath not since that Time rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being now Sick & Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 26th Day of July 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Eliz: Smith
