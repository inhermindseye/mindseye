Westmr.  Rd. Beacher upon his Oath saith he lived in & rented a house in Castle Street in the parish of St. Philip in the City of Bristol of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds & upwds. for the Space of Eighteen Yrs. & upwds. & paid his Share of all parochial Taxes & hath left same upwds. of 12 Yrs. Says he hath not since he quitted sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Decr. 30th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

Richard Becher
