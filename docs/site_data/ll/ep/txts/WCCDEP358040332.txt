Westr.  Sarah Dycey upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 9 Years ago she was bound Apprentice to Mrs. Gashin a Mantua maker and Served four Years Apprentiship with the said Mrs. Gaskin in Whartons Court in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlx and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 8th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Sarrah Dicey
