St Clement Danes March 9th. 1792

Middx & Westminster }  This man Died same day of & presently after his Removal  Fulham Middx  Mr. Edwd. Hogg Overseer}  Tallboys Beadle  (to wit) William Woodley Widower Aged abt. 55 Yrs. Upon Oath saith that he Married his late Wife Ann Formerly Ann Calter  Spinster at the parish Church of Saint James Westminster about 23 years ago (be Quence) that he hath a Son & Daughter his only Children by his said Wife now living the Youngest of which is thirteen Years Old or thereabouts & with Mr. Wheeler Glover, in Church Court Strand who maintains her: that the Son is provided for having Served his Apprenticeship in Kent Street Southwark : That this Deponent said Wife Died about two years & an half ago that previous to his paid Marriage & while he was single and unmarried & not having any Child or Children he lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Willm. Matty a Coach master at the Kings Arms in Fulham in the parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex for two years upwards at the Annual Wages of £5. that he quitted his said Master's Service about 30 Years ago & about Six Months afterwards returned to his said Master & continued with him us a Weekly Servant till his Decease being about two years And this Deponent saith that since leaving the said Mr Maltby as aforesaid he hath never obtained any other legal Settlement by any means whatever

Sworn the 9th. March 1792 before us} Sampn. Wright N Bond

William Woodley
