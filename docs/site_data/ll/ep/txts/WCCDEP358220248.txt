Middlesex to wit Sarah Marsh Aged about Twenty Two Yrs. on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Spencer a Widow Lady in Castle Yard in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn in that Part which is in the City of London upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her sd Service upwards of four Years ago & hath not since she quitted her said Service she never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th. day of March 1771 before Us} Tho Lane 

Sarah Marsh
