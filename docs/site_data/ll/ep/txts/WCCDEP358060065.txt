Middx  Fras. Reason upon Oath saith he was born in the Ram Inn Yard in West Smithfield in the Parish of St. Sepulchers London and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes and that he can give no Account whatever of the Settlemt. of his father or mother

Sworn 31st. Jany. 1750 before  Tho Lane .

Francis Reason
