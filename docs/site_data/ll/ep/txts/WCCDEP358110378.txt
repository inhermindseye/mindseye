Middx  Mary Payne aged about 23 years upon her Voluntary Oath Saith She never was married and that on the Seventeenth Day of March One thousand Seven hundred & Forty nine (Old Stile) She was delivered of a female bastard Child in the house of Mr Todd in the parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of westmr. in the County of Middx which was baptized Mary Diana & is still living and likely to become Chargeable to the afsd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes She being Poor & unable to maintain the same and that Thomas Fox of Woolverhampton in the County of Stafford Iron monger did beget the Said female bastard Child on her body the Said Thos. Fox having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinant Several times in the month of June which was in the said year 1739 (Old Stile) at one of which time he got her with Child of the sd. female bastard Child of which She was delivered as aforesd. and that the said Thomas Fox & no other Person is the  true & real father thereof

Sworn Decr. 27th 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane

Mary Pain
