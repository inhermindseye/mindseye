Westmr. to wit John Dixon Aged fifty Two Years on his Oath Says that he Rented and lived in a House Known by the Name or Sign of the Feathers in little Newport Street in the Parish of Saint Anns Soho in the Liberty of Westminster for Six Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four pounds hath quitted the same about Ten Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th. day of April 1771 before me}  W Kelynge

John Dickenson
