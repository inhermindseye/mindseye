Middlesex to wit Edward Noyce Aged about Nineteen Years on his Oath Says he never was Married Rented House or ever served any Apprenticeship says he lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Mr. Adlow at the sign of the British Museum in Great Russell Street in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds when his said Master died and afterwards he Continued to live in the said House with one Mr. Wright who look the House on the Death of his said former Master and this Examinant lived with the said Mr. Wright for the Space of Nine Months which together with Twelve Months before mentioned make together One Year an Ten Months and after quitting his said Service he never after lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

his Edwd. Noyce Mark
