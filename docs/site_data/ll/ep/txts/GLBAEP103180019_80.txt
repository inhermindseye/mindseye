Middlesex ss}  Sarah Simpson  Singlewoman maketh oath that many years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year by & with Robert York Vinten in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgae in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for divers Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain anothr Settlement

Sworn this - day of Febry 1778 before}

The Mark of Sarah Simpson
