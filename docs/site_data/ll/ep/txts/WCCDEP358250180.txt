Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Beattey aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that on the 12th. day of August 1781 she Intermarried with David Beattey her late Husband who she has been informed has been dead about Eight years Months at the Parish Church of Portsea in the County of Southampton by whom she hath Three Children living namely Mary aged about Nine years Sarah aged about four years and David aged about Two years That her said late husband informed this Examinant that he was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England That she this Examinant is (as she has been informed) the legitimate Child of John Johnson & Elizth. Freeman who were married (as appears by a Certificate which this Examinant has seen) at the Parish Church of Charles within the Borough of Plymouth & County of Devon That her said Father & Mother informed this Examinant that she was born in Purlleigh Street at Lynn in Norfolk in the parish of Lyme Regis in the County of Norfolk that she this Examinant never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she

Might have gained a Settlement in her own Right And further this Examinant Saith that her she has been informed that her father was born at Lynn aforesaid and died in the Workhouse belonging to Lynn aforesaid the parish of Lynn Regis

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1795 .  G Goodenough N Bond

her Elizth. Beattey Mark

Witness B Newman
