Westmr.ss  Sarah Hood Aged about Thirty four Years in her Oath Says she is the Widow of James Hood who has been done about four Years Says her said late Husband Rented a House St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex upwards of Seven Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and paid all Taxes due for the same and further upon her Oath Says that since her sd. Husbands Death she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement and further say that on the 20th. day of June last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Moore in Surry Street Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the name of Rebecca and that one James Warburton is the Father thereof he having had Casual Knowledge of her Body at his the said Mr. Moors House some time in June last but the parties day she does not remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered a aforesaid and that the said James Warburton did beget the same & that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of Augt.1773 before  Sampn Wright

Sarah Hood
