Middx  Mary Evans  Wido . of John Evans upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her sd. late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes abt. Fourteen Years since & that her sd. late Husbd. Jno. Evans was bound an Apprentice to & served the First Year of his Apprentiship with Mr. Hen: Price  Apothecary in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . & then went to Sea with the Kings Letter & was never assigned over to any Person whatever that her Sd. late Husband Jno. Evans was killed at the Seige of Portobello as She hath been Informed & beleives that since his sd. Apprentisipp he never in his Life nor she since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 19th. of May 1742 before} Thos. Lane  Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Evans
