Middx  The Exaicon of Anne the wife of Jno. Horn Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 19th. day of Febry 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that by Order dated the 18th. day of April 1738 She this Exaiant was together with her sd. Husband Jno. Horn removed from the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes to the Parish of St. Botolph Bishopgate London and that her sd. Husband is at this Present time maintained in the Workhouse belonging to the sd. Parish and this Exaiant being Old & Poor is not able to maintain hereselfe

Sworn the day & Yeare above menconed before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

The Mark of A H Ann Horn
