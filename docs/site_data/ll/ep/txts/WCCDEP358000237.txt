Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr}  The at No. 131. Tooley that Boro:}  Settled 25d taken & rel. Exted.  Partnell  Parnnell Evershard aged 33 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that on the 1st. day of January 1782 she was delivered of a Male Bastard at the House of a Mr. Coleman at No. 6 in Little Stanhope Street which hath since been half baptized by the Name of Wm. That Wm. Hull a Barge Master at  Godalming is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of April 1781 and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of Which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Wm. Hull and no other Person did beget the said Child and that he is the only and Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 26th. day of Febry 1786 A. Fencer.

Parnnell Evershard
