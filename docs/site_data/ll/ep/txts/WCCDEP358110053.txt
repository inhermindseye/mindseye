Westmr.  Jas. Hayward upon Oath Saith he intermarried with his prest Wife Eliza . at the Fleet London abt. 30 Years ago and that in or about the Year 1720 he was hired to live with Mr Freame a Taylor at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for one Year & five Pounds for the Second year and lived with him under that Agreement in Portugal Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for two Years and has not since he left Mr Freame rented ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt for one Year

Sworn May 19th. 1752 before}

James Hayward
