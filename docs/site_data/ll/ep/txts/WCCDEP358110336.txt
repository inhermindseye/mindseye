Westmr.  Jas. Dunovan upon Oath Saith he was married to his wife Eliz. Dunovan at the Roman Catholic Chapel in Lincolns Inn Fields about Sixteen Years ago and that about 30 Yrs. since (being then a Singleman) he lived a Yearly Servant with his brother Morgan Dunovan a Taylor in Clarke Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £10 for one year & upwards and has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Octr. 25th 1753 before}
