Middx  Sarah Flowers  Wife of James Flowers upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. James Flowers on or about the 28th Day of March abt. 22 Years since at the parish Church of St. Bennett Pauls wharf London Says her sd. Husband James Flowers lived in and rented a house in Suffolk Street in the parish of St. George in Southwark in the County of Surry for the Space of Eight years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds Says her sd. has band hath been abroad & beyond the Seas abt. twelve Years & this Examinant hath not heard from him for nine Years last past Says the sd. Jas. Flowers hath not since he left the sd. house rented ten Pounds a Yr. pd. parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 22d. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sarah Flowers
