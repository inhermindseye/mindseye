Saint Clement Danes

Westmr.} to wit Thomas Wood aged 56 years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith that upwards of 35 years ago he Intermarried with Alice Wood his present Wife , at the Fleet London that he has no Child or Children living by his said Wife that he Rented and lived in a House in One Tun Alley Hungerford Markett in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds hath quitted said Service about years ago since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to best of this Exts knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd day of May June 1786 before us}
