Middx  Eliz. Bouchier  Wife of Jno. Bouchier gone from her removed from the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate London by Order dated the 15th. Day of March Instant upon her Oath says she intermarry'd with the sd. Jno. Bouchier on the 4th. Day of April 1743 at the Fleet London & that her sd. Husband Served 7 Yrs. Apprenticeship with Mr. Wood Carpenter in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Says he never was a Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examints. knowledge or beleif

Sworn 17th. March 1745 before Wm. Jones

The Mark of Eliz. Bouchier
