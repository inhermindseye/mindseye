Middx  Ann Brinner Aged about forty Three Years On her Oath Says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Pollord a Taylor in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of five Years ago and hath not since rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1769 before}

her Ann Brinner Mark Witness Petr Sidebotham
