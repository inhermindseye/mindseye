Middx  Mary Allen aged abt. 70 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Wm. Allen who hath been dead abt. thirty Years says her said late husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Hodway at the five Bells Tavern in Hollywood Street in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. & never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one yr. says she has not since his death Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Yr.

Sworn this 26th. day of Janry. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Allen
