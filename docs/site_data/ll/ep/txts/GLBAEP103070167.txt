Middx ss  Rebecca Ayres  wife of John Sawly Ayres who is now on Board one of his Majesties ships of War Maketh oath that her sd husband was bound apprentice to his Grandfather John Sawly a Hogman of the precinct of St: Catherine in ye County of Middx And that during his sd. apprenticeship this Deponent was Married to her sd husband And this Depont. farther saith that for about the Space of Twenty three Months before her said husband was out of his apprenticeship And during that time & afterwards this Deponent and her sd. husband lodged in the sd precinct of St. Catherine and did his duty as an apprentice to his sd Grand father there And this Deponent farther saith that her sd. husband to her Knowledge or belief hath not since gained any subsequent settlemt. And that she hath two children Named to wit John aged abt Five Years and Ann aged abt Three years the lawfull issue of this Depont. by her sd husband

Sworn this 20 day of Aug. 1756 before  Chris: Scott

her Rebecca Ayres Mark
