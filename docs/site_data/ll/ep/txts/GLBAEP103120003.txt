Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Saberi  Wife of  Lewis Saberi  mariner (who is lately gone from her) maketh oath That her Said husband has informed her and Which She believes to be true that he was born in the Kingdom of Spain That to her knowledge or belief he never lived as a hired Servant for a year or as an Apprentice nor ever rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in the Kingdoms of Great Britain or Ireland That she this Depont. Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Fathers or Mothers Parents That her late Mother Grace Budge deceased has informed this Deponent that She the said Grace Budge was born in the City of Carlisle but in what Parish there She never told her and this Depont. Can give no other Account respecting the Settlement of her said late Mother Previous to her intermarriage with her late Father William Budge deced That her said late Father William Budge hath informed her that he was born in the Town of Taunton in the County of Somerset (which as she has been informed & believes Consists of divers Parishes but in What Parish there he never informed her That her Said late Father hath also informed her that many years ago he lived as a yearly Servant with a Certain Person (whose Name She does not now remember) in Rood Lane in the City of London but he never informed this Depont. (nor Can this Depont. Set forth) how long

he Continued in Such Service That She this Deponent can give no other Account respecting the Settlement of her said late Father Wm Budge Save that She has been informed by one Jane Cross formerly a Servant to her said late Father and Which information she believes to be true That her said Father many years ago lived in & rented a house in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in in the said County Middlesex and was charged to and Paid the Scavengers Tax for the Same and at which time the Payment of Such Tax gained a Settlement That She this Depont. was born in Stone House Yard in the said Parish of Saint John Wapping as She has been informed by her Parents & Verily believes she this Depont. having been educated in the Charity School belonging to the same Parish That She this Deponent never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound apprentice nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself save what She might acquire by her Marriage as aforesaid That she this Depont hath three Children namely Lewis William , aged about Ten years Mary Elizabeth aged about four Years, and Ann Mary aged about Seventeen Months the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her said husband to whom she was lawfully married.

Sworn this 29th Day of May 1772 before  Cha: Digby W: Russell

Eliz Saberi
