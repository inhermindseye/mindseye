Middlesex ss  Ann Morgan  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year by & with Mr. Quick who kept the Sign of the Queens Head near the Victualling office Square in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service there for the Space of Four Years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain another Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 17 day of June 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Ann Morgan
