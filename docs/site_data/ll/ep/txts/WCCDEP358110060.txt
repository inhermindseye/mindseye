Middx  Jane Maples upon Oath saith she is the widow of Wife of Chas. Maples with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Margt. Pattens on the 2d Novr. 174 & by whom she hath one Child Chas . aged abt. 4 Years that her sd husband at present Absconds from her & she knows not where he is and that her sd. husband Informed her he was hired by his late father Thomas Maples to live with him as his Book keeper at the Yearly Wages of £20 Dyet Washing and Lodging and lived with him in Consequence of that Agreement in Norfolk Street in the Strand upwds. of three Years & that this Agreemt. Subsisted at the time of their Intermarriage which Information this Examt. verily belives to be true and she further saith that her sd husbd. never rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn June 20th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Jane Maples
