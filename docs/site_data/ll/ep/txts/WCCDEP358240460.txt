Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  to wit Mary Fearnley aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of August 1763 she Intermarried with Robert Fearnley her late Husband who has been dead about Eleven Months at the Parish Church of Saint Paul Covent Garden That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of the Westminster for about Twenty two years at the yearly Rent of Thirteen pounds and paid Taxes due for the same That her said Husband died in the said House and where she this Examinant now Resides

Sworn this 19th. day of Janry. 1795 } W Addington

Mary Fearnley
