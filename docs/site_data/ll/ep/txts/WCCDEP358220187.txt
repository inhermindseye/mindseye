Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Masters Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of William Masters (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn about Nine Years ago and by whom she hath three Two Children now living namely Wm. Aged about Three Six Years & Sarah Aged about five Years Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Hall a Button Maker in the Parish of St. Sepulchre without in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his sd Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1770 before us} Thos Kynaston W Kelyngs

her Sarah Masters Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
