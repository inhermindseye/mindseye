Westmr.  Eliz. Walker aged about 27 Years upon Oath Saith she intermarried with Mathew Walker at the Fleet London on the 9th. Febry 1752 who at present absconds from her and that she has been Informed her sd husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr Coffen a Taylor and lived the last Year of his time with the sd Mr Coffen in Bow Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. & that his Apprentiship Expired in the month of April last Says her sd husband never rented ten pounds a Year or Pd. parish taxes

Sworn Augt 14th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizabeth Walker
