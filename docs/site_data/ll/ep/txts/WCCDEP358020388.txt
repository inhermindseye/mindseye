Saint Clement Danes 28th. Febry. 1792

Middx & Westminster}  Mary Cormick  Singlewoman Aged 44 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith That she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. John Hall in the Temple Lane in the Precinct of White Friars in the City of London for three Years at the Yearly Wages of £3 for the first Year £4 for the second and £6 for the third that she quitted that Service about twelve or fourteen Years ago and hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And that She is now Poor and unable to support herself without Assistance

Sworn the 28th day of February 1792 before us B: Kennett Wm Hyde

Mary Cormick 's Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
