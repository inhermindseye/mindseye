Middx & Westmr} to wit Ann Williamson Aged about Twenty three Years on her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Smith a Lodger at Mr. Perrys a Capmaker in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months or thereabouts at the Yrly Wages of Eight pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one Mr. William Davis a Surgeon had Carnal Knowledge of her Body on the Twelfth day of May last and at sevl. Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said William Davis and no other person did beget the same

Sworn this 6th. day of Febry 1770 before me} Tho: Fellows

her Ann Williamson Mark
