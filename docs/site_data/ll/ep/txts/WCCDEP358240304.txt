St Clement Danes August 9th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary Whitechapel }  (to wit) Ann Armstrong  singlewoman aged sixteen years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married never served as an Apprentice or as an Articled or Covenant Servant never lived as an hired yearly Servant for a Year together or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement in her own right that she hath been informed and verily believes she is the is the illegitimate Child of Judith Armstrong singlew deced and was born in Wentworth Street in the parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the said County of Middlesex

Sworn the 9th day of August 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner  Edwd: Naish

Ann Armstrong Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
