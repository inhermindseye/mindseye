Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Strutton aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she served Five Years Appceship to one Mrs. Arnold of Marlborough in the Parish of Saint Peters in the County of Wilts That since She hath been out of her Apprenticeship she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that on or about the Eleventh day of April now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Bell in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been Christened by the Name of May That one John Walden who lives at Milbank Westminster is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid thereon and that the said John Walden is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 16th- day of Febry- 1797 before me} John Collick

her Sarah Strutton Mark
