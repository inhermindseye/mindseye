Middx  Johanna Huddleston upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with her late Husbd . Arthur Huddleston at the Parish Church of St. Margt: Westmr . abt. 30 Yrs. since & that he hath been dead about £20 Yrs.

Says she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mrs. Littleton in Poultney Court in the Parish of St. Jas. in the Liberty of Westmr. 2 Yrs. & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £5 being at that time a Wido . & having 1 Child (to wit) Wm. who was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Hall Organist of St. Ann Westmr . & had so been upwards of one Year before she left her said Service Says since she left the sd. Mrs. Littletons Service she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 13th. Febry 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Johanna Huddleston
