Middx  Mary Jenkyns aged abt. 40 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Joseph Jenkyns (now at Sea) says her sd. Husband served his Apprenticeship of seven years to John Strand a Fisherman in the Parish of Wandsworth in the County of Surry & never Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or was a yearly Servt.

Sworn the 4th: day of April 1757 before} Fran Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Jenkyns
