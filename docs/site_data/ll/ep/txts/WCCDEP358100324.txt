Saint Clement Danes Decr. 24t. 1784

Nothing lane C  Stephen Holloman aged Sixty Years or thereabouts Upon his Oath Saith That he served as an Apprentice to Joseph Holloman his own Uncle a Bricklayer in Bear Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex under Indentures of Apprenticeship duly executed for Service years That since he quitted his said Master he has never lived as an hired yearly Servant, Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Value paid Takes or done any Act Manner or Thing whereby to gain any Settlement elsewhere than in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes as is above Stated And further this Examinant Saith That he has no wife nor any Child born in lawfull Matrimony now living

Sworn the 24th. Decr. 1784 before me R Butler

his Stephen Holloman Mark
