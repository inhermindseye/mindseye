Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Dennis aged about 70 years upon her Oath saith that about Thirty years ago she Intermarried with Edward her late husband (who has been dead about Ten years) at the Parish Church of Wargrove That her said late husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Edgeworth at Wargrove in the County of Berks for Seven years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Sixteen Guineas that after he quitted said Service he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th day of Decr. 1796 } M Addington Rupt Clarke

her Jane Dennis Mark
