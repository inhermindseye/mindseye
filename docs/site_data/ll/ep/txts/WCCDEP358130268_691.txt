Middx  Ann Wall aged abt. Twenty Years Upon Oath Says She never was Married was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servt- Says her father Wm- Wall  Rents a Ground Chamber, at No- Nine in Fn valls Inn in the Parish of St- Andrew Holborn London of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds as She hath been informed & veily believes

Sworn the 18th. day of Janry 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Anne Wall
