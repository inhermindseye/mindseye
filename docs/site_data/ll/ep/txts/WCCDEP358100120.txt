Middlesex to wit Catherine Williams Aged about 55 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Saml. Bonnington a Grocer in Little Britain in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldersgate  London upwards of five years at the Yearly Wages of £5. hath quitted her said Service upwards of Twenty years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 8th. day of September 1783  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

her Catherine Williams mark
