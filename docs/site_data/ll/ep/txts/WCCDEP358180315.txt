Middx  Mary Lacey aged about 23 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she was bound an Apprentice to one Thomas Wright of the Parish of Westham in the County of Essex  Indigomaker & Served him as such for two Years or thereabouts & then her Master & she Parted by Consent Says she was never Assigned over to any other Person lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief Says she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the sd. Parish of Westham on the 22d. Day of October last which hath since been Baptized John

Sworne the 6th. April 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Lacy
