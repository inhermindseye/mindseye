Middx The Examination of Mary Davis  Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx residing in a Near the parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate in the County of Middx this Seventh day of September 1754

This Examinant on her oath Saith that about Seven Months ago one John Palmer who lodged then in Great Russell Street in Covent Garden in the County of Middx  Comedian at the house of one Mr. Walters a Chymist did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at the house of ye sd Mr. Walters And the sd John Palmer had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body and so had several times whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she now goeth and is Pregnant And this Examinant Saith that the sd. Child or Children of which

She now goeth with and is Pregnant as aforesd is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to ye said parish of St. Botolph Witht Aldgate she not being able to provide for the same And that the sd John Palmer is the true & only father of the sd. Child or Children So likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesd. And lastly saith that this Examinacon is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this Seventh day of Sepr. 1754 before me  Richd: Ricards

Mary Davies
