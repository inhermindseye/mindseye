Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Ann Wicks who will Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed by the said Ann which information he believes to be true that she is about 24 years of Age that she intermarried with James her present Husband at the Chapel in Kentish Town about 9 years ago that she has one Child living by her said Husband Vizt. James aged about 8 years that her said Husband served his Time as an Apprentice to one Mr. Withey a Shoemaker in Kentish Town in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex  that after her said Husband was out of his said Apprenticeship he Never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Augt. 1788 N Bond N: Forster

Benj: Newman
