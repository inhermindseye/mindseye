Middx  Ann Stoker  Singlewoman aged about 24 Years upon her Oath saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt : with Mr. Welford in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £3: the Space of one Years & Six Months & hath left  her said Service two Years & upwards since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt . for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt: in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne June 13th: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Ann Stokoer
