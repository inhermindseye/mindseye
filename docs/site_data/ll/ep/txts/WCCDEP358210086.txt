Westmr.  Mary Toole aged about 38 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Patrick Toole who has been dead about Seventeen Months, says her said husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief, says before her said Intermarriage she was bound an Apprentice for five Years to Mrs. Ann Padget , Millener in King Street Westminster in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westminster and served four Years and upwards of her said Apprenticeship with the said Mrs. Padget and then intermarried with her said late husband. says she hath not since her said husbands Death rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes, and that on the 1st. day of June 1765 she this Examinant was removed from the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the bars in the County of Middx to the aforesaid Parish of St. Margaret against which Order there was no Appeal

Sworn the 25th, of October 1766 , before} Fran. Cary  Tho. Kynaston

Mary Toole
