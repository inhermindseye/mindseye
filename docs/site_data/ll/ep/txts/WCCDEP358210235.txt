Wesmr.  Mary Cheesbrough Aged about Forty two Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Joseph Cheesbrough who has been dead about Five Years, says her said late Husband of ten times informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that before his Intermarriage with this Examinant he rented a house in Shug Lane in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and Upwards, at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds, and after he quitted same never Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 29th. day of January 1768 . before W Kelynge Tho Kynaston

The Mark of Mary Cheesbrough
