Westmr. to wit Peter Minton Aged about forty four Years on her Oath Says he Intermarried with Ann his late Wife at the Parish Church of St. Ann Black Fryars about Twenty Years Ago and who has been dead about 18 Years ago and by whom he hath one Child now living Namely Mary Aged about Twelve Years Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Thomas Anderson a Cuttler  near Smith field Bars in the Parish of Saint Sepulchres London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House of Ten Pounds P Annum paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither or hath he done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Augt. 1773 before me} R. Butler

Peter Minton
