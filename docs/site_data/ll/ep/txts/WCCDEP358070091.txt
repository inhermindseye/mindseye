Westmr. to wit Tamer Frances Jones Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Martin in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Margarets Westminster for Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 16th. day of April 1773 before R Butler Sampn Wright

Tamar Frances Jones
