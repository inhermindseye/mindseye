Middx 

Mary Warren upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Peter Warren who dyed about twenty three Years ago and that she (this Examinant) about twelve Years ago being then a Wido . rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £20. in the back of St Clements in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex & lived there in near three Years and has not since she quitted the said house rented Ten pounds a Years paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 3d. 1746 before}
