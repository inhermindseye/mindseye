Middx & Westmr,}  Mary Walford aged about 25 Years, Upon Oath, says she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or a yearly hired Servant for one Year, says she has been informed a which information she verily believeth to be true that her Father Jonathan Walford deceased served three Years the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Jaques at the Castle in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes to this Examinant knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born, says that James Little Servant to Mr. Emmett Cowkeeper in Kent Street Road in Surry had carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the Month of January last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the said James Little and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn 11th. Augst. 1767 , before} Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Mary Walford
