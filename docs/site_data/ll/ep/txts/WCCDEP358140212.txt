Middx.  Catharine Dewell aged abt. 68 Years upon Oath says she is the widow of George Dewell who has been dead abt. Eleven Years says her said late husband Often times informed her and which Information she Verily believes to be true that he served seven years Apprenticeship with me Mr. Stanhope a Shoemaker in St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never was a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or Ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says she has not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 17th. day of Jany. 1759 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Cath: Dewell
