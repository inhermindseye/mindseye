Middx  Eliz: Young aged abt. 50 yrs. Wido. of Saml Young upon Oath saith she was married to her sd husband at Stoke Church in Hampshires on the 12th. Janry 1726 that She has been informed and believes her sd. husband Served Seven Years apprentiship with Mr Stent a Carpenter in New Inn passage in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx & that  her sd. late husband did not during his Life nor hath this Examinant since his Decease rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant

Sworn Decr. 10th. 1753 before J Fulhny

The Mark of Eliz Young
