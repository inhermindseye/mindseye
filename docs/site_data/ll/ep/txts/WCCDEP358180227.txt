Middx  Margarett Boyce  Widow upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband John Boyce  Deceased on or about the 1st. Day of Janry. 1721 that the said John Boyce lived in & Rented a house the Corner of Pedley Street in the Parish of St. George in Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of £50. for the Space of 4 Months & upwds. & hath left the same abt. 27 Years since which time her sd. late Husband in his life time or this Exaiant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne Augt. 31st. 1744 before} Wm: Jones .

Margaret Bays
