Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Thomas Yeoman who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Thomas Yeoman Rented of a Mrs. Johnston in Grafton Street in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster unfurnished Apartments for about Twelve Months and paid the Annual Rent of Fifteen Pounds that he hath quitted said Apartments about Thirty seven years after which he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th- day of March 1796 E Read Rusl Clarke

B Newman
