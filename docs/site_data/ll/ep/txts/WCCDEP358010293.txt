Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Dickens aged about 54 years upon her Oath saith that he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Greenwich about 25 years ago that he was bound an Apprentice to one Randal Sidley a Taylor in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for six years that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of May June 1789  Wm. Kitchiner

John Dickens
