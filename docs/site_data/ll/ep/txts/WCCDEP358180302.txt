Middx  Frances Williamson  Removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex by Order bearing Date the 5th. Day of this Instant March upon her Oath says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Jno. Smith a Sherriffs Officer in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & Liberty of Westmr. for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds Ten Shillings & hath left her sd. Service upwards of Seventeen Years Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Smiths Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child which is likely to be borne a Bastard & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one Mr. Michael Stopelaer who now lodges at the Golden Anchor in Great Russell Street  Covent Garden did begett the sd. Children her Body he being acquainted with this Examinant had Carnall knowledge of her Body at the house of one Mr. Stone in Pump Court in White hart Yard

in the Months of July and August last at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child she is now Pregnant with & that he the said Michael Stopelaer & no other person is the times reall father thereof

Sworne 8th. March 1744/5 before me} Wm: Jones

France Williamson
