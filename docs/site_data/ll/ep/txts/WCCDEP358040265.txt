Middx 

Susanna Baker aged about 29 Years upon her is the Wide. of steward Baker who dyed upwards of two Years ago that she Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the 8th. Day of July last in the house of Mr. Few at Essex Stairs in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which has been baptized Richard  and is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same and that Richard Goodchild Son of Goodchild of Stad green near Maidenhead in the County of Berks Yeoman is the reputed Father the sd. Richard Goodchild having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in the month of October last and at several other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Said Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the sd. Richard Goodchild & no man else is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Octr. 1st. 1748 before} Wm. Jones J Poulson

Susannh Baker
