Middx 

Mary Redman upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Jacob Marally in Broad Court Dukes Place in the parish of St. James in Dukes Place London at the Yearly Wages of £4 for Six Years & upwds. & hath Quitt her sd. Service about Ten Years since which she has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yea  or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 25th. 1750 before} Hran: Bedwell  ulding

The Mark of Mary Redman
