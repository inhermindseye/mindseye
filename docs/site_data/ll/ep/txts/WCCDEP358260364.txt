Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  App Parish Book 6 P 95.}  Sarah Griss aged about 44 years upon her Oath saith that about Ten years ago she Intermarried with William Griss her late Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Blackmoor Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for Six years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds that he quitted said house about five years ago since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this first day of Novr. 1798 . before me } Saml. Edward .

her Sarah Griss Mark
