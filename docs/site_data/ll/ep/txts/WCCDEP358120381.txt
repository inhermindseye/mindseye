Middx  Jane Holland aged 23 years upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or yearly Sevant nor ever Rented ten pounds by the year nor paid parochial Taxes and that her father George Holland  rented a house in St Clements Lane in the parish of St Clemt. Danes of the yearly Rent of £16 lived therein many years and left the same abt. Nine years ago Since wch. her sd. father has not rented ten pounds by the year nor pd parish taxes

Sworn July 16th. 1755 before

The Mark of Jane Holland
