Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Christian Veitch aged about 49 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Alexr Veitch her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about 28 years ago that he has been dead about 2 years that she hath four Children livg. by him Charlotte 21 George aged 17 years Christian aged 15 years and Robt . aged 8 years or thereabouts all of whom are provided for that her said late Husband Rented and lived 2 Shops in Saville Row Passage in St. James Parish in the Liberty of Westmr. for about 18 years Months at the yearly Rent of £10 during which time he lived in Swallow Street in the said Parish that he hath quitted said House about 14 years ago since which time he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Feb March 1787 R Butler

her Christian Veitch Mark
