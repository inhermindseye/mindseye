Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Lloyd aged about Twenty years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on or about the Eleventh day of December now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of a Wm. Hale on Saint Peters Hill in the Parish of Saint Peter Pauls Wharf in the City of London which hath since been Christened by the name of Mary

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1798 before us W Addington Robinson

her Mary Lloyd mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
