Middx  The Examination of Ann Wright the Wife of Jno. Wright Taken the 12th. Day of May 1739 Passed from the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Middx By Order bearing date ye. 22d. Day of Augst. 1738 Who upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband Jno. Wright Served two Years & upwds. the latter part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. Morris Perukemaker in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about 9 Years ago And further upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband never Rented Ten, Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beliefe that her sd. Husband now absconds from her & haveing three Childn. (to wit) Ann Aged Six Years & upwds. Edwd . Aged about 3 Years & Mary Aged about 3 Months & she being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas Gronous

The Mark of Ann Wright
