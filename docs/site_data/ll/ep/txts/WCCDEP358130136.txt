Middx  Ralph Wareham Aged abt. 50 Years Upon Oath Saith he Rented a House in Turnmill Street in the Parish of St. James  Clerkenwell in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and lived therein abt. Eleven Years & hath quitted sd. House abt. Seven Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servt . for one Year

Sworn the 6th- day of Aprill-1756 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Ralph Wareham
