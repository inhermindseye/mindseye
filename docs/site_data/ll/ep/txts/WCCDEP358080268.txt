Middlesex to wit Mary Plunkett aged about forty Two Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Arthur John Plunkett with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Stepney in the County of Middlesex about Nineteen years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said Husband has rented Several House since his Intermarr- iage with this Examinant but the last House that her said Husband Rented was at No. 13 in King Street Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds payable Quarterly lived therein for five Months or upwards hath quitted the said House about four Months ago since which time her said Husband Never, Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain Subseqt. Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of July 1778 before  James Croft  Wm. Barnfather

Mary Plunkett
