Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Carter aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that Margaret Hunt the Wife of Henry Hunt late a Servant at the Gren Inn Carey Street was as she has been informed Married about Ten years ago at Sd. Bartholomew the Great in the City of London that the said Henry Hunt has informed her that he was born at Bleachingley in the Parish of Bleachingley in the County of Surry that he was brought up a Gardiner with his Father that he lived with the Dean of Westmr. in the Parish of Bleachingley aforesaid about Nine years as a yearly Servant and was discharge there by the said Dean that her Sister has one Child by him named Henry aged about Seven years that his Mother being ill is now a Casual in the Workhouse of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 19th. day of Janry. 1789  Sampn Wright R Butler

her Sarah Carter Mark
