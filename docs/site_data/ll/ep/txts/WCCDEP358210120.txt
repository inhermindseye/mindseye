Westmr.  Bridget Elizabeth Remphrey aged about 35 Years Upon Oath says, she never was married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Adams at the Cock in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds quitted said Service Upwards of Ten Years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 8th. Janry. 1767 before} Paul Vaillant

Bridgett Elizabeth Remphrey
