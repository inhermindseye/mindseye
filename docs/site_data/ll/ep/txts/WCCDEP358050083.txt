Westmr.  Frances Molloy  Wido . upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her late Husband John Molley at the fleet London upwards of Fourteen Years ago Says the said John Molley dyed about Eight months since and that he was a foreigner and never gained a Settlement in England that she (this Examinant) before her sd. Intermarriage and whilst a Singlewoman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Danovan a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds Ten Shillings for one Year and upwards and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has one Child by the sd. John Molloy viz Michael aged upwards of two Years

Sworn Augt. 11th. 1749 before}
