Sqr Appld. agst. to wit Daniel Ellis aged about 25 years on his Oath says he never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year was ever bound an apprentice or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Says his Father informed him that he was born in the parish of Saint George Hanover Square and that he this Examinant together with his Father and all the Family were in the Workhouse of Saint Georges Hanover Square at one time which was about 15 years ago and there maintained by the Sd. Parish of Saint George Hanover Square

Sworn this 8th day of Janry 1785 J. Hubbald G Mercer

Daniel Ellias
