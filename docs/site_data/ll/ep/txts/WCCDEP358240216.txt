Ann Prosser Bastard

St.. Clement Danes 13th February 1793

first Entry Vide a subsequent Examn page 227 as to another Child

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Sarah Richards aged 36 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to Watkin Richards at the parish Church of St. Philip in the City of Bristol about 17 years ago (by Banns) that her said Husband left her about 16 Years ago to go abroad and she hath not since heard of him within 10 Years last part that she hath a female Bastard Child born at the House of Mr Harris No 4 Craven Yard Drury Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx on or about the 8th day of July last which hath since (at this Deponent hath been informed & believes) been half Baptized by the Names of Ann Prosser called Ann Proser and and that one James Prosser is the only real & true father of the said Child and is now from this Deponent

Sworn the 13th day of February 1793 before Me  Wm Kitchiner Walker

Sarah Richards 's Mark

Witness G
