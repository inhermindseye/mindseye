Westmr.  Mary Todd aged upwds. of 21 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year together; that her father William Todd deceased rented a Farm in Wyesgate in the Parish of St. John at Newcastle upon Tyne in the County of Northamberland of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards and lived therein Sevl. Years and there dyed and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a female bastard Child on the 28th. of Janry last (since baptized Ann ) at the Parish Nurses of St Clement Danes Middx at the Charge of the Inhabitants of the Sd. Parish and that William Dennis  Upholsterer did beget the said female bastard Child on her Body the sd. William Dennis having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinant Several times in April & May last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. & that the sd. Wm. Dennis & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn March 6th. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell  Tho: Lane

The Mark of Mary Todd

Re'sworn June 23d. 1752 the name of Dennison being first altered to that of Dennis-before}
