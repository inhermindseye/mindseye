Middx  Sarah Rear aged about 35 Years upon Oath says She was married at the Fleet London about ten years since to John Frederick Rear who is a Foreigner & never gained any Settlement in England Says that before her Intermarriage with the said John Frederick Rear She was bound Apprentice to Mrs. Fulford in Bennetts Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes  Button maker , whom She Served Seven Years all but 4 Months, & her Mistress dying her Indentures were delivered up Says that She has three Children by her said Husband namely Sarah aged abt. nine Years, John aged about Seven Years, & Mary aged upwards of one Year and further says that She (whilst a Singlewoman ) never Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 31st. Day of Octor. 1755 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Sarah Rear
