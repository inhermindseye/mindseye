Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination Eleanor Bridges of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex single woman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this Twenty Third Day of December in the Year of our Lord 1766 .

Who on her Oath Saith That on the Twenty first day of November last Past at the house of one John Murray in Crown Court in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate she the said Eleanor Bridges was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath been since Baptises and registered by the name of Eleanor Wells And that one Edward Wells of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London an Apprentice to one Mr James a Brass Founder in the same Parish did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child Which is likely to become Chargeable to the sd Parish of Aldgate Middx

Sworn this 23d day of December 1766 before  Chris: Scott

Eleanor Bridges Her Mark
