Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Ling aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Druke in St. Pauls Church yard for about two years at the yearly Wages of £6 that she hath quitted said Service about Nine Months since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 15th. day of September now last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Murphy in Nags head Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of William That one William Gallant who lived at said Mrs. Drake his Sister in Saint Pauls Church Yard is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of Decr. last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said William Gallant is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 16th. day of Decr. 1788 } R Butler

Sarah Ling

Talboy had Warrt. not taken Janry. 20 .1789 .

Sarah Ling lodges at Mr. Murphys No: Nags head Court.

William Gallant is a Tall Stout Man about 26 years of Age fair Complexion light Hair Tied behind.

Warrt. made out.
