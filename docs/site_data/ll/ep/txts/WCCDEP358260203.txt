Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Rose Mc. Gill aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith that about 25 years ago she Intermarried with James Mc. Gill her present Husband in the Parish of Killasy in the County of Armagh in Ireland by whom she hath Seven Children living Vizt. Mary aged about 24 years Kitty aged about 22 years Barney aged about 20 years James aged about 16 years and Eleanor aged about 14 years all provided for Rosanna aged about Six years and Charles aged about four years unprovided for That her said Husband Rented Two Rooms unfurnished of a Mr. Burke in Little Russel Street in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Months at the Weekly Rent of Three Shillings and Six pence during which time he paid one Shilling per week to the Clerle of Covent Garden Market for leave to Stand in the said Market to Sell Goods that he hath quitted said Apartments about Two years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of May 1797 before us } N Bond Jno. Solpey

her Rose Mc. Gill mark
