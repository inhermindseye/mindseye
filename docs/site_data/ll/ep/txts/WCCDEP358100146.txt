Middlesex to wit Mary Chant Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Green a Hosier in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds hath Quitted her said Service upwards of Two Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement by living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further says that John Hastings a Servant to Mr. Jemeson a Merchant who lives near the New Inn Black Fryers Bridge is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time the latter End of February or the begining of March last in her late Mistresses House in Norfolk Street and at several different times before afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said John Hastings & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of Novr. 1783 before R Butler

Mary Chant
