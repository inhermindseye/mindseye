Middix.  Mary-Day upon Oath says she is the Wife of Richd. Day with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Stratford Le Bow abt. Eleven Years since says (on the behalf of her Son Isaac Baylis aged abt. fifteen Years) that Wm. Baylis this Examinants late husband & father of the said Isaac Baylis has been dead upwards of Twelve Years and that he served three Years the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Evans a Cooper in Clarkemarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn this 11th. day of July 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Day
