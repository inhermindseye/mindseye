Mr. Hatchman at Mr Winter Coachmakers in Chander's Street

Saint Clement Danes 11th April 1786

Middlesex (to wit) David Hatchman of Chander's Street Covent Garden in the County of Middlesx Coachmaker on the behalf of Ann Hatchman a Poor Woman now in sane at the House of Mr. Taylor in Arundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon his Oath Saith That be verily believes the said Ann Hatchman never was married,  or ever did any Act whereby to gain a Settlement in her own right That John Hatchman her father rented and lived in a House in Janes Street in the Parish of Saint Mary be Bone in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Value of upwards of ten pounds for more than forty Day - thats her father and Mother are both dead the father having survived the Mother and that both of them died in Marybone Workhouse or Infirmary without having as this Examinant verily believes gained any other Settlement than in the Parish of Saint Mary labour

as above stated

Sworn this 11th. April 1786 before  Rt. Taylor Mr Barnfather

David Hatchman
