Middlesex ss} Who is bound out & immediatley obliged to go on a Voyage to America John Butler  Mariner maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to his Uncle Tobias Butler  mariner for the Term of Seven years That after this Deponent had served about three years & eight months of his Said Apprenticeship his Said Master & Uncle went & lodged with this Depont Uncle Martin Butler  Mariner who lived in & rented a house in the Minories in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate London Where this Deponent always lived and lodged during the remaining part of his said Apprenticeship Save Such part as he was obliged to be out at Sea with his said Master & Uncle Tobias Butler That the last time this Deponent Came home from Sea with his Said Uncle Tobias Butler & before that his Said Apprenticeship expired he this Depont & his Said Master lodged & lived in the said house of his said Uncle Martin Butler in the Minories aforesaid for about the Space of three Months & then this Deponent went out to Sea Again with his Said Uncle Tobias Butler where he continued about nine months during Which Space of time this Deponents Apprenticeship expired this Depont having about Six months to serve of his Said Apprenticeship when he last Went out to sea with his Said Master Tobias Butler That he this Deponent has not since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship And that he hath a Wife named Mary  to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 6. day of May 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith  Wm.. Blackmore

John Butler
