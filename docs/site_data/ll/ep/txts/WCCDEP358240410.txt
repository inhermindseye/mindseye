Hannah Rose commonly called Hannah Murphy }

St. Clement Danes May 16th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Stanmore Parva psh Middx  (to wit) Hannah Rose  singlewoman aged about thirty years Upon Oath saith that she never was Married never served any Apprenticeship or did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own Right that she has been informed which she verily believes to be true that she is the legitimate Daughter of John and Hannah Rose both deceased that her said Father Rented and resided in a House known by the Sign of the White Hart  in the in Edgware Street in the parish of Stanmore Parva in the County of Middlesex for several years at the Annual Rent of £40 or thereabouts that he And this Examinant saith that her said Father died there and her Mother continued to Rent and reside in the same House for a considerable time after his death that she quitted the same about fifteen years ago to the best of this Deponents Recollection and died without obtaining any subsequent legal Settlement to this Deponents knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 16th day of May 1794 before us }N. Bond Edwd: Naish

Hannah Rose 's Mark
