Middlesex & Westminster }  Mary Thomas Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father was a Welchman and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief either by Renting Ten Pounds a Year or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says she was born in Warwick Stables in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex as her said Father and Mother has often informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true

Sworn this 18th: day of August 1770 before us  R Butler W Kelyngs

The Mark of Mary Thomas

Witness Will: Hughes
