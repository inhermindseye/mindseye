Middx  The Examination of Jane Walker  Widow of Robert Walker lodging in the Parish of St- Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 28th- Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about thirty Years ago to her now best Remembrance She Intermarried with her said Late Husband Robt- Walker at the Parish Church of St- Giles in the Feilds in the sd. County & that her said late Husband Served seven Years Apprentiship to one Mr. Johnson to a Tyer Smith in Parkers Lane in the said Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds And further on her Oath saith that her said Husband in his Life Time nor she since his Death never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being now Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Cha: Egerton

The Mark of Jane Walker
