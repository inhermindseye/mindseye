Middx  Grace Williams  Wife of James Comfort Williams (gone from her) upon Oath Says she intermarried with the sd. James Comfort Williams on the 2d. Day of Febry 1742 in the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes Middx Says her sd. husband was born in the Parish of St. John Baptist in the City of Hereford in the County of Hereford as she hath been informed and verily believes Says he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 5th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Grace Williams
