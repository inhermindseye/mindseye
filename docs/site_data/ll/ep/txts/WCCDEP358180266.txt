Middx  Peter Clifton Aged 58 Years upon his Oath saith he is a Widower & hath one Child viz: Mary aged 12 Yrs. & upwds. Says about 16 Yrs. since he lived a Yearly hired Servant with the Rt. Honoble the Countess of Westmoreland in Dover Street in the parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middx for the Space of 3 Yrs at the Yearly Wages of £6 & that since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne the 19th. Decr. 1744 before} Tho Lane .

Peter Clifton
