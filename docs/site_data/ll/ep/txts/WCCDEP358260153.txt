Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Frances Saffurn aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that she Rented and lived in a house at the back of Saint Clements in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Parish of Westminster for about One year and Three Quarters at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and upwards that she hath quitted said house about Five years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained or subsequent settlement

Sworn this 3rd. day of Septr. 1796 } J Reeves

her Frances Saffurn Mark
