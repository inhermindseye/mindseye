Middx to wit William Middleton Aged Sixty years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his present Wife Jane Esther at  the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in  or about the year 1763 and by whom he hath one Child now living namely Jane Aged about fourteen years says he Rented and lived in a House in White Harts Yard in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for Six Months at the yearly Rent of Twenty Two Pounds and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Two years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 6th. Day of February 1781 before  Abrm Baley  John Hagar

Wm Middleton
