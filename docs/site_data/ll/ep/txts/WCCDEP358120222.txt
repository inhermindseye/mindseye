Middx  Heny. Gardiner aged abt. 40 Years upon Oath Saith he was bound apprentice for Seven Years to Mr Clements a Baker and lived with him under the sd. Apprentiship about 5 Years during which time he lodged at his sd Masters house in Milford Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx that at the End of the sd. term of five Years his sd master failed and this Examinant never was assigned over nor ever rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Novr. 5th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Henry Gardner
