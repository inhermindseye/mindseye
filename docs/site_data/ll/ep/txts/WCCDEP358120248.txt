St Clemt Danes  Decr. 17th. 1754

Westmr.  Mary Jobling aged 29 years upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Saml Jobling who dyed about 8 Yrs. ago and that after the Decease of her said late husband she took a house opposite the Wheatsh in Witch Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the yearly Rent of £16 and lived therein about Six months that she quitted the sd house about Six Years ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Jobling
