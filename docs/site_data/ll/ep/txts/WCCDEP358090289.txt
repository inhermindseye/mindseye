Middlesex  to wit Mary Reading Aged about Sixty four Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with Samuel Reading at the Savoy Chapel about Eleven Years ago and who died in the Year 1773 Says her said Late Husband was an Irishman and never gained any Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief Says she Intermarried to a former Husband John Fuller a Cooper and who served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Shaw a Cooper in Little Trinity Lane in the Parish of Saint Mildred Bread Street in the City of London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House or Tenemt. of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant Since her said Husbands death.

Sworn this 14th. day of Febry. 1782 before R Butler

her Mary Reading Mark
