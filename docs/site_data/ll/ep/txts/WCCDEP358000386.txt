St. Clement Danes.

Christopher Hodgson of Newport Market in the Parish of St. Anns John Maketh Oath and Saith That Robert Hodgson the Illegitimate Child of Robert and Sarah Hodgson was born the corner of Middletons, Buildings Queen Ann Street East in the Parish of St. Mary e bone that he has been in St. Mary le bone Workhouse and since he left same has not gained a Settlement to the best of his Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 12 Jany. 1787 -before  Jno. Martin Mr. Wright

Christr. Hodgson
