Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  Elliott gave Bail on Examn before Mr Justice Walker to appear at Westminster The Bail are Wm.. Meredith of St Mary le bone Corn Dealer £20.} & John Charnett of Essex Street St Clemt Danes Milkman £200.  Mary Nicholls Aged 23 years and upwards upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Jarman Milkman in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for upwards of twelve Months at the yearly Wages of £4, And this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath further saith that she is pregnant with a Child or Children that is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes when born and further saith that James Elliott who lives at Saint George Row near Tyburn Turnpike is the Father of such Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the beginning of May last past & at several times since at one of which times he did beget the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant & that the sd. James Elliott is the only real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of October 1791 } R Butler

her Mary Nicholls Mark
