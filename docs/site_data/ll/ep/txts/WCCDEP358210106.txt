Westmr.  Thomas Charlton aged about Twenty Years upon Oath says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Morris at the Gun Alehouse in St. John Street in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for One whole Year at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds, hath quitted said Service about three Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 20th. of Novr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Jno: Sydenham

The Mark of Thos Charlton
