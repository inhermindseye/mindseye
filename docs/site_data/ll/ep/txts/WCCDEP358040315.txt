Middx  Eliz Pointon upon Oath saith she intermarried with her prest. husband Josiah Pointon abt. 12 Yrs. ago at the fleet London by whom she hath one Child Cathn. aged upwds. of 9 Yrs. Says about 8 Years ago her husband lived in and rented the Cheshire Cheese Alehouse in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of £30. for several Years and has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that her sd. husband is at present very much Afflicted with the Rheumatism and unable to maintain himself & family

Sworn 3d. Febry 1748 before Wm: Jones

The mark of Eliz. Pointer
