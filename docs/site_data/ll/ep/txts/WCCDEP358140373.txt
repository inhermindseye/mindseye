Middx.  Frances Rogers aged upwards of 60 years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Haines in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for One Year & a half at the Yearly Wages of £6 and hath Quitted said Service about 30 years And has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn May 22d. 1760 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Fras. Rogers
