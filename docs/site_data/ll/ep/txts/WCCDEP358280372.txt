St.. Clement Danes March 9th. 1792

Middx & Westminster } (to wit) Ann Broadbent  Singlewoman Aged about twenty Six Years Upon Oath saith That she never was Married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Griffin a Butcher in Blackmoor Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes  in the said County and Liberty for about one year and a quarter at the Annual Wages of £6, that she quitted that Service about three Years ago and hath not since obtained any Subs other legal Settlement And this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that She is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to be become chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes when Born, And that Michael Jordan a Bricklayers  Labourer who lodges (as he Informed this Deponent which She verily believes to be true) at No. 3 in Angel Court Princes King Street by Saint James Street Square Westminster is the only real and true father of such Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body in or about the Month of August or September last and at Several times since at one or some of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which of She is so pregnant as aforesaid thereon And that he and no one else did beget the said Child or Children on this Deponents Body

Sworn the 9th. day of March 1792  before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Ann Broadbent's Mark

Witness Edwd. Burbidge

Mr Richardson Oversr. Burbidge Beadle & he took Newman with him

Mary Broadbent in the Workhouse A Middle Size Man rather thin Dark Hair & undressed & Dark Eyes

10th March Apprehended & brot. up to Mr Walker's, Poor, had no Friends, & therefore could not offer Secy Committed (by request) to Portugal Street Watchhouse til Tuesday Eveng next

(Agreed to Marry the Girl)

13th March/92 took Note to Indemnify agst lying & in & Maintenance of Child he dischged £20
