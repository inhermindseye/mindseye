St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westmr.}  passd. to Shepton Mallett to wit Elizabeth Hurl aged 64 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she intermarried with George Hurl at the Parish Church of Saint George the Martyr Southwork about 34 Years ago That her Husband deserted her about Eighteen Years ago, That her Husband was a Seaman and never did any Act to the best of her knowledge whereby he might have gained a Settlement That she does not Know where he now is not having heard of him for above 15 years That she has been informed by her said Husband and by his Relations at Shepton Mallett and which information she believes to be True that his Father was a Clothier of Reputation and Credit there, and kept a large House which was his own property, and carryed on Considerable business in the Manufacturing Line at Shepton Mallet aforesaid, that her Husband went

to Sea, That her Husbands Father Failed at Shepton Mallett, That she never has done any Act Matter or Thing whereby She may have gained a Settlement Since her Husband left her

Sworn 3d. March 1785 before us  Jno Barnfather  James Paine

E Hurle
