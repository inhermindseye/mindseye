Middlesex ss}  Prudence Earl the widow of George Earl deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true That he was duly bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprentice ship with James White hurst in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Hatter & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponts knowledge information or belief her Said husband never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rate or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement Subseqt. to his Apprenticeship and that she this Deponent since his death hath not done any Act to gain a settlement for herself.

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Prudence Earl
