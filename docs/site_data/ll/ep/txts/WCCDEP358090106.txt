Middx to wit Mary Grace Reed aged about Thirty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Robt. Reed with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Mary White Chappel about Twenty Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living namely Mary aged about 19 Years & Esther Aged about Nine Years (one of whom is now Provided for) Says her said late Husband was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England either by renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Thomas Chamberlain Rented and lived in a House in Horn Court Bear Lane Tower Street in the Parish of all Hallows Barking Tower Street in the City of London for sevl. Years at the Yearly Yearly Rent of Nine Pounds and Paid Land Tax due for the same lived therein to the time of his Death which is upwards of Nine Years ago since which time this Examinant has gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise house ever

Sworn this 24th. day of Decr. 1779 before R Butler

Mary Grace Reed
