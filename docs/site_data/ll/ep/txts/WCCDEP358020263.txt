St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster}  Gertrude Cunningham Aged 42 Years or thereabouts on Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Cunningham at the parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx abt. 15 Years ago that her said Husband lived with her only abt. three Years next after such their Marriage, that he then left her & since she hath not (although frequently enquired after him) heard of him & that she Knows not whether he is now living or Dead, & saith that her said Husband & other persons have informed this Deponent & which she Verily believes to be true that her Husband is as bound by Indentures of Apprenticeship to Mr Cubitt Tinman Strand near St Clements Church Yard & duly severd his Apprenticeship, that he hath not Since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 Pr Ann or done any other Act to the best of her knowledge, whereby he might have gained another legal settlement

Sworn before me 11th. April 1791  Wm Kitchiner

Gertrude Cunningham
