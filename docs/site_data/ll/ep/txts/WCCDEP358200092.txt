Westmr.  Mary Cox Aged about Fifty Six Years Lodging in Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Arthur Cox who has been Dead about Seven Years with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about Nineteen Years ago Says her said Husband is a Native of Ireland and never Gained a Settlement in England as this Examinant hath been Informed by her said Husband and which Information she Verily believes to be true Says that whilst she was

a Singlewoman she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Richard Cecil an Apothecary the Corner of Bloomsbury Square in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted said Service about Twenty Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes.

Sworn the 26th of March 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Cox

Upon Mr Jones Complt Ovr. J W
