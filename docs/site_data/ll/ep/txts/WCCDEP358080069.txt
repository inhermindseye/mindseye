Middx & Westmr}  Margaret Westweather Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one James Willick a Shoemaker in Tudor Street in the Precinct of Bridewill London for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted Same about Nine Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of July 1776 before Us R Butler Jno. Barnfather

her Margt. Westweather Mark
