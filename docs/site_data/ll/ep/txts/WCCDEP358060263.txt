Westmr.  Jane Dorber aged upwds. of 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that on the 9th. Day of January last she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . which hath been baptized Mary & is become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same and that John Groves late a Servant to Mr. Cooke at No: 161 in the Clemt. Inn did beget the sd. female bastard Child on her body the said John Groves having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in April last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. & that the said John Groves & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Febry 12th. 1752 before}
