Middx.  Wm. Roberts aged abt. 38 Years upon Oath says he was Married to Martha his wife in Egham Church in Surry abt. a Week ago says he Served Two Years the first part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Seale a Baker in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and then Run away from his Master and never was assigned over to any person; nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 23d. day of November 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

William Roberts
