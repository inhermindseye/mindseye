Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Margt. Colloly aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Dawson a Woollen Draper in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Four years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about 14 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Subsequent settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Septr. 1787  R Butler

Marget Connoly
