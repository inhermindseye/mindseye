St. Clement Danes May 2d. 1792.

Middx & Westminster }  St Dunstan in the East  under taken back ders band she only Served to Months there  (to wit) Judith Stracey Aged 23 years & upwards Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Baxter in Great Tower Street in the parish of St Dunstan in the East in the City of London for upwards of a year, at the Annual Wages of £7. that she quitted that Service about 6 Years ago, and hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge or belief that She is now pregnant unable to support herself without Assistance

Sworn the 2d. May 1792 before us N. Bond Sampn. Wrigh t

Judith Stracey
