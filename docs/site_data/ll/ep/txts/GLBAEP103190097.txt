Jane Winbush the widow of John Winsbush deced maketh oath that her Sd husband died lately on board his Majestiesship the Dunkirk in his Majesties Service That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served Six Years of his Apprenticeship with one Wm Hamilton a Carpenter in the Parish of St Luke in the City of York & the Remainder of his Apprenticeship with the Sd Hamiltons widow in the Same Parish & has not Since to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 12. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Jane Winsbush
