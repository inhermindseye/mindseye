Westmr. to wit Ruth Matthews Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Matthews who has been dead upwards of Two Years Says her said late Husband had several Small Houses in Farthing alley Barnaby Street in the Parish of Saint Olave Southwark in the County of Surry for the Term of his Natural Life at the Yearly Income of Twenty five Pounds & where this Examinant lived and Paid all Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said Houses upwards of five Years ago and hath not since quitting the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Octr. 1773 before R. Butler

Ruth Matthews
