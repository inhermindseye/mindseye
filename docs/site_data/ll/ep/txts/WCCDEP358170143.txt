Middx  Martha Robuck  Wido . upon her Oath saith that on the 28th. Day of Septemer 1740 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Black Cere Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye County of Middx which hath since been Baptized Elizabeth & that one Mr. Richard Ferryman of Tothill Feilds  Cowkeeper did begett the said Female Bastard Child on her Body she at such Time liveing as a Yearly hired Servt - with the sd. Mr. Richd. Ferryman in Tothill Feilds aforesd. where he had Carnall Knowledge of her Body sevll. Times since in the Month of Decr. or January 1740 & at Sevll- other Times since at one of which Times he got her with the sd. Female Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & that he & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 2d. of Octr. 1741 before} Tho Lane . Jas: Gronous

The mark of Ma Martha Robuck
