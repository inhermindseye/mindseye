Westmr. to wit Charles Trewit Aged Sixty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Christian his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Anns Westminster about Thirty Seven Years Ago Says he Rented and lived in a House in Crown Court Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes about Two Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid Parish Taxes and Quitted same the latter End of the Year 1762 and after he quitted the same he never Rented Ten pounds a Year or Paid Taxes

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1770 before me W Kelynge

Charles Trewitt
