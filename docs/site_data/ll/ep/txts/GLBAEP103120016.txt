Middlesex ss  Hannah Farley the wife of John Farley maketh oath That her Said husband who is now Sick in St. Bartholomews Hospital hath informed her & which information She believes to be true that divers years ago he rented a house in Scallop Court in the Parish of Saint Gregory by Saint Paul in the City of London & was charged to and Paid the Kings Tax for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And that to her knowledge or belief her Said husband hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of November 1772 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Hannah Farley
