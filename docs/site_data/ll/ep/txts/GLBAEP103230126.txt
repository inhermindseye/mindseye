Middlesex ss}  Hester Williams the widow of John Williams deced maketh oath that Since her said husband death She lived with her Son John Williams in Tooley Street in the Parish of St Olave in Southwark in Surrey but did not live with him there for more than three Quarters of a year and She did not live with him as a hired Servant but was maintained by him upon Charity nor hath She this Deponent Since her Sd husbands death lived any where as a hired Servant for a year

Sworn this 26th day of March 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Hester Williams
