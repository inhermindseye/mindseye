Middx  Jane Edwards upon Oath saith she never was married and that her father John Edwards (as she hath been informed and Believes) was born at Darking in Surry and she cannot give any further Account of his Settlement & that she (this Examinant never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr. 12th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Jane Edwards
