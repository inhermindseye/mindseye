Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Mitchell  Wife of Jno. Mitchell Passed from the Parish of St. Bartholomew the Great  Londn . by Order bearing Date ye. 6th Day of March Inst. Who upon her Oath saith yt. on the 5th. Day of July 1733 she Intermarryed with the above Named John Mitchell who rented & lived in a House of Ten Pounds a Year & upwds. in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx And further upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband Jno. Mitchell now keeps the Sign of the Buttock of Beef a Cooks Shop in Ship Yard aforesd. and refuses to maintaine or allow her this Examint. any Subsistance and she being in an Ill State of Heath is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn this 10th. Day of March 1739 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. Mitchell
