Middx  to wit Mary White aged about Fifty years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived A Hired yearly Servant for One year Says she Lived as a Covenanted Servant  to learn the Business of a Mantua Maker with one Mrs. Phillips a Mantua maker in Hemlock Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and Lived with Her for the space of One year and after quitting her said  Service she never gained any other settlement

Sworn this day on before}
