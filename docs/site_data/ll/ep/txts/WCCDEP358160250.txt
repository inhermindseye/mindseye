Middx  The Examination of Elizth- Jones  Singlewoman who was found wandering in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. County of Middx haveing as Habitation or place of abode taken the 3d. Day of February 1740 Who upon her Oath saith That She was never married & that her Father Thomas Jones Deceased was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. John Menton in the Parish of Wandsworth in the County of Surry  Waterman & Served him as an Apprentice for the Space of six Years or thereabouts that her said Father Thomas Jones never rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif, And that neither hath She this Examinant ever rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she is now Poor & Sick and not able to maintaine herself

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. Jones
