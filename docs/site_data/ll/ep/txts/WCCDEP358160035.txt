Middx The Examination of Thos. Wheldale Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 15th. day of Febry.1738 Who upon his Oath saith that about Seventeen Years ago he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Kennell a Butcher in Veare Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for the Space of One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds And this Examinant further saith that he hath not since that time Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife that he hath one Child Named Mary aged about Twelve Years & he being Sick & Poor is not able to maintaine her.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane

Thomas Wheldale
