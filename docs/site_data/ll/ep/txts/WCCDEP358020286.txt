St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Ann Gilgrist Aged 40 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that about 16 years ago she was Married by Banns to her late Husband John Gilgrist at the parish Church of St. Michael in the Town of Cambridge that previous to such Marriage her said Husband had lived about Nine years as an hired yearly Servant with Mr.. Cudden in Essex Street in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty at the yearly Wages of £9 wh £20 which whose Service he quitted abt. 3 Months after his Marriage and this Examinant saith that since, he hath not gained any legal settlement to the best of her knowledge, that her said Husband Died about 3 Months ago that she hath one Child now living by her said Husband Named William of the Age of 12 years and that she is Poor and unable to support herself & Son

Sworn before me the 13th May 1791 R Butler

Ann Gilgrist 's Mark
