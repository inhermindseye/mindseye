Middx  Ann Morse aged near 24 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she is the Daughter of Peter Morse by Hannh . his Wife both deceased that her sd. late father dyed abt. 14 Yrs. ago and her sd. late mother after his decease and while she remained a Widow rented a house known by the sign of the Footman in Waiting in Feathers Court in Fox Court near Greys Inn Lane in the parish of St. Andw. in Holborn above Barrs Middx of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards and lived therein about three months and then was married to a second husband and this Ext. further saith she never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one year nor ever rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parish taxes

Sworn Octr. 19th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Morse
