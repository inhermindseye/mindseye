Middx  Richard Phillips Aged about Fifty three Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about three Years ago and by whom he has no Child or Children living says he was bound and Appren to One Joseph Slamaker a Blacksmith in Bermondsey Street in the Parish of Saint John Horseleydown in the County of Surry about Forty Years ago and served his said Master for the space of One Year or thereabouts under such Indenture And afterward his Mother Bought his time out since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 24th. day of 1780 before  Jno Barnfather  Thos. Preston

Richard Phillips
