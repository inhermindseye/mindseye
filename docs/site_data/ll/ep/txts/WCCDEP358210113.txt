Westmr.  Ann Byrne aged upwards of 17 Years Upon Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented House paid Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says her Father Michael Byrne Rented a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards for several Years and quitted same about four Years ago and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn the 6th, of Decr. 1766 , before}

The Mark of Ann Byrne
