St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Thomas Jones Aged 24 years or thereabouts Upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Charlotte his late Wife whose Maiden Name was Reid at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch abt. two years ago that his said wife Died about one year ago and he hath no Children now living that previous to his said Marriage and while he was single having no Child or Children he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. George Burnett at the them Fountain in Wych Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid for about Fifteen Months at the Annual Wages of Five Pounds that he quitted his said service about Nine years ago and that since he hath not (by any way or means whatever) acquired any other legal Settlement And that he is Poor and unable to maintain himself

Sworn the 12th. July 1791 . before us R Butler

Thos Janes 's Mark
