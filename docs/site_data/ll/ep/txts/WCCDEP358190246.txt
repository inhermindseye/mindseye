Middx  Mary Thomson aged abt. 32 Years upon Oath Says she was never Married that she Lived a hired Yearly Servt : with Mr Tilzakerly in Esex Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for two years at the Yearly Wages of £5 & hath quitted her Said Service upwds. of 15 Years & hath not Since rented any house 7d the Land tax or poor Rate

Sworn June 5th: 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Thomson
