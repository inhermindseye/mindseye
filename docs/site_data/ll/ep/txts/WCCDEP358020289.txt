Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) John Lamb Aged 42 years or thereabouts upon his Oath said that he intermarried with his late Wife Mary whose Maiden Name was Cunde at the parish Church of St Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex by Banns about 15 or 16 years ago, that his said Wife died on the 21st. day of October 1788 , that he hath now living two Children Vizt John Aged 7 Years & upwards Mary Aged near 4 years waiting three days she being Born on the 23d. May 1787 both of when were born of the Body of his said late Wife, And this Examinant further saith on his Oath saith that he hath been Informed and verily believes it to be true that his late Father Alexander Lamb was Married to one Ann Simpson this Deponents late Mother at the Fleet but this Deponent has never been informed when such Marriage was Solemnized, that he hath also hath been informed (& which he verily believes to be true) that he this Deponent was Born in a place, then called Sheppard's Market in the parish of St. Geo George the Martyr Hanover Square in the said County And this Deponent further saith that he hath never rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid any Taxes severed any parish office or done any other Act or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal

legal settlement in his own right that he is so distressed and confined in Circumstance as to render him incapable to support his two Children for whom therefore he is absolutely Necessiated to apply) for Relief

Sworn the 21st. day of May 1791 before us  Wm Hyde B: Kennett

John Lamb
