Middx  Elizth Parker Aged about 19 Years upon Oath says she was never Marryed that her Father  John Parker Rented a house in Wytch Street in the Parish of St: Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in ye County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pound & upwards and lived in the same about three Months and hath quitted said House about Twelve Years and never after Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she this Examinant never Rented House Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 28th Day of June 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth: Parker
