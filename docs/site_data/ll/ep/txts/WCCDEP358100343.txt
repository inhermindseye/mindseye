Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Geo Hanr. Square to wit Susannah Sanford Aged about 59 yrs. on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Barnard a Poulterer in David Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex and City and Liberty of Westminster for two years at the yearly wages of 5 Pounds hath quitted said Service about 40 years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 27th day of Janry 1785  Thos. Gilbert  Thos: Gordon

her Susannah Sanford mark
