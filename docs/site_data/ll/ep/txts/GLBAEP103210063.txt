Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Mangrell of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before us two of his majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this eleventh day of February one thousand Seven hundred & eighty five who saith that on the seventh day of January now last past she this Examinant was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of Mrs Sellwood in the Maze Tooley Street in the Parish of Saint Olave Southwarke in the County of Surrey which was baptized in the sd house by the name of James Mangrell & is Since become chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate She this Examinant being Poor & not able to provide for the Same and that one James Blair of the said Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate  Sailmaker did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this. 11. day of February 1785 before R Davies J. Hawley

Mary Mangrell
