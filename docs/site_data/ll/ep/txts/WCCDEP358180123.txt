Middx  Sarah Bostin  Removed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds by Order Dated the 17th. Octr. Instt. upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewomen & was never married & that abt. 7 Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Venables in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the first Qur. & £6. for the Remainder for the Space of one Year that since she left her sd. Service she hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 19th. Octr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sarah Bostin
