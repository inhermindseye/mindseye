Middx  Susa Jenkins aged abt. 32 Years upon Oath Saith She is the wife of Wm Jenkins (who is gone to Nova Scotia ) to whom She was married at the Fleet London upwds. of five Years ago and that her sd husband informed her he Served upwards of three Years of his Apprentiship with Robt Binfield a Glazier in that part of Coventry Street which is within the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and then absconded from his sd Masters Service & went to Sea & that he never was assigned to any other person nor ever rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year. which Information this Examinant believes to be true

Sworn July 10th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Susannah Jenkins
