Saint Clement Danes 10th. Octr- 1784

Middlesex (to wit) Ann Cross aged 16 years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married, that She was bound out an Apprentice from the Parish of Saint Service Southwark in the County of Surry by Indenture by the Coment and in the Pres of two Magistrates to Mr. Nicher of No. 5 Green Wall Black Figaro Road in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of surry Hatter and served under the an this for about one year when she was discharged by The Consent of the Magistrater since which time she hath done No Act to gain a subsequent settlem.

Sworn this 10th day Ocr. 1789 before Mr. Bleamire Tho Vaughan

her Ann Cross Mark
