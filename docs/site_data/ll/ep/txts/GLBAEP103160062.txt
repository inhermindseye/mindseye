Middlesex ss}  John Weston maketh oath that he was duly bound Apprentice to & served the whole thereof Save one Quarter of a year with his Father in Law John Barnett in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Cordwainer & then this Deponent by reason of ill usage from his Said Master left him & went to sea That this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year or rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a subseqt Settlement

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 b before P. Rowlands

The Mark of John Weston
