Middx  Eliz: Davis aged about Fifty Three Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with George Davis her late Husband Deceas'd at ye. Fleet London about Seventeen Years since, Says her said late Husband serv'd his Apprentiship of Seven Years with Mrs. Sanger, a Bookseller in New Court in Ye. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & never Rented £10 a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year as She hath been inform'd by him & believes Says She hath not since Ye. Death of her said Husband Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a

hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 15th. Day of May 1756 . before}
