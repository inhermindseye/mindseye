Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Dubon of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this 20th day of November 1775

Who Saith that on the Twenty Seventh day of October last Past in the Workhouse of the said Parish She this Examinant was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Which hath been Since baptised & Registred by the name of Elizabeth Dubon and that the said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the Said Parish and that John Charles of the said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate  Taylor did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the day & year abovewritten before me  Wm: Blackmore

Eliz Dubon
