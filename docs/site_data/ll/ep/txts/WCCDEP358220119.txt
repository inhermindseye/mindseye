Middx. & Westmr.}  Elizth. Lawes Aged about 28 Years on her her Oath Says she is the Wife of George Lawes gone from her with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Margt. Westmr . about 12 Years Ago Says her said Husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Jackson a Grocer in College Street in the said Parish of Saint Margarets Westmr as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither has this Examinant done any Act since her said Husbands leaving her whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day January 1770 before Us} R. Butler Thos: Kynaston

her Elizth. Jackson Lawes Mark

Witness Petr. Sidebotham
