Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Wallis aged upwards of 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes, That she lived as a hired yearly Servt. with one Wm. Therett at Hartley Row in Hampshire for 2 years & upwards that she hath left her said Service about 4 years, since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that on the 10th. day of June last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mrs. Green in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Thos. Miles That one Thos Whistley Wisley a Clerk at the Stamp Office in Cooks Court & who lodges at Mrs. Woods No. 52 in Barbacin is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Months of August or September last past, whom he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Thomas Wigley and no one else is the Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 12th. Day of July 1787 before } Rupt: Clarke
