Middx  Sarah Gardiner aged upwds. of 52 Yrs. upon Oath saith she intermarried with Oliver Gardiner in the Colledge Chapell at Chelsea abt. 11 Yrs. ago and that her husband has been gone from her about two years & She knows not where he is that the sd. Oliver Gardiner about 7 or 8 yrs. since rented a house in New Inn Passage in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £14 and lived therein abt. 3 or 4 Yrs. & hath not since he quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 21st 1750 . before}
