Middx  Sarah Asplin Aged about Fifty Years upon Oath Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Sert . with Mr. Owen in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Several Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted same about Twelve Years after that Lived a Hired Yearly Sert. with Master Clerk in Cooks Court in the said Parish One Year & Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds she being at that times a Singlewoman & without Child or Children & never after Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 4th of Feby. 1765 Before}
