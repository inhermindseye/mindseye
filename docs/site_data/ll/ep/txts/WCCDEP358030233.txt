Middx  Tho. Pearce aged abt. 72 Years removed from the Parish of West haddon in the County of Northampton by Order dated the 16th: of Octr. Instant upon Oath saith that about 30 Years ago being at that time a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Sr . Jno. Cheshire in Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds for the Space of Six Years or thereabouts Says he hath not since he left his Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. Whilst a Singleman for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 23d. 1746 before
