St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St. Faith London N.B. no Appl.  (to wit) Ann Smith Aged 35 years & upwds. Widow of William Smith Deceased Upon her Oath saith that on or about the twenty second of February 1781. she was Married to her late Husband William Smith at the parish Church of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex that Grevens to their said Marriage and while her said Husband was single not having Any Child or Children he lived as Ostler with Mr. Hapton at the Bell Inn Warwick Lane in the parish of St. Faith in the City of London for very near two years that he received as Wages or a Compensation for such his Service a certain Allowance or Sum for every Carriage Waggon Cart & Horse that came into the Inn Yard and stay'd for one Night or More, that such Sums were generally Weekly paid or at least made up & settled that he constantly boarded & Lodged with his said Master at the Inn the whole of his Service and was then always at the Command and Subject to the direction of his said Master And that since such Service he hath not gained any Subsequent legal Settlement neither hath she this Deponent since his Death acquired any settlement in her own Right that she hath two Children by her said Husband now living namely Ann Aged Six years & upwards and Robert Aged one year and Eight Month or thereabouts And this Deponent

saith that she is unable to maintain herself and the said Children without some Assistance

Sworn the 14th. day of Decr. 1791 before us} Sampn. Wright  Thos. Gordon

Ann Smith
