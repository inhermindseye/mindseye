Middlesex  Elizabeth Winbolt Aged about Twenty Five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father John Winbolt Rented and lived in a House next Door to the Golden Key in Tenter Alley little Moor Fields in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate without but within the Freedom of the City of London for several Years and where this Examinant was born as her Uncle Thos. Fountain has often Informed her and which this Examinant verily believes & can give no further or other Account of her Settlement than as afsd.

Sworn this 20th. day of July 1784 before G Mercer John Machin

her Elizth. Winbolt Mark
