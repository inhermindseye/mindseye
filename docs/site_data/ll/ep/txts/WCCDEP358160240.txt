Middx  The Examination of Jno Ford aged above Sixty Years Passed from the Parish of St. Bridges vulgary St. Brides  London to the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx by Order bearing Date the 27th- Day of January Instant Who upon his Oath saith that upwards of twenty Years ago he lived in and rented a House against Devereux Court in the Strand in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of thirty Pounds and lived in the same for the Space of five Years and upwards And further on his Oath saith that he hath not since that time rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne this 30th. Day of January 1740 before}
