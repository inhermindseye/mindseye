8th May 1786

Middlesex (to wit) Elizabeth Manning  Widow aged twenty Six Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that She was married to Henry Manning her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Demet in the West in the City of London on the second Day of September in the Year of our Lord 1779 . That since their Intermarriage they said Rented and lived in a House in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of two Years at the Yearly Rent of Eleven pounds, that her said Husband died about a Year ago,  that  the then lived in the said House in Clements Lane aforesaid and hath quitted the same about half a Year ago - That she hath not since quitting the same rented any other Tenement of ten pounds a Year Value or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have acquaint any other Settlement than in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid - And this Examinant further Saith that she hath two Children both born in Leadloch by her said Husband Vizt. James aged near Six Years and Jane aged two Year and an half or thereabouts - and that their upon and is great Distress and

unable to maintain herself and said two Children

Sworn this 8th. May 1786 before R Butler

her Elizth. Manning Mark
