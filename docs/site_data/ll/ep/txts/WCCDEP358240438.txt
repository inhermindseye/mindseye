Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.}  Settlement in St. C. D  to wit Mary Ann Stewart aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Robinson a Staymaker in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upwards of Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas That she quitted said Service upwards of Nine years since which time this Deponent has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any other Act Matter or Thing whereby she might gained any subsequent Settlement And that she is poor & unable to maintain herself and without Assistance

Sworn this 1st. day of Septr . before me } M Addington

her Mary Ann Stewart mark

Witness Jas Talboys  Beadle
