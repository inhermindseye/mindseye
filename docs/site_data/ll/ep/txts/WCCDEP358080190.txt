Middx- & Westmr-}  John Prigg aged about 63 Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his now Wife  Ann at Saint Paul Covent Garden about Eleven Years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says that before his said Intermarriage with he Rented and lived in a House in Adam & Eve Court near Oxfords Markett in the Parish of Saint Mary le Bone in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of fourteen pounds a Year and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the same upwards of fourteen Years ago since which time he hath gained no other subsequent Settlemt either by Renting house or Payg- Taxes

Sworn this 4th- Day of Decr- 1777  Abrm Baley J Hale

John Prigg
