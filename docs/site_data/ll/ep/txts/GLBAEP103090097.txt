Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Herbert  Single woman maketh oath That She hath two Children to Wit Henry aged six years and upwards and Mary aged two years and upwards both of which Children are illegitimate and were born in Darby Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht. Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex The first of which Children was baptized in the Collegiate Church of Saint Catherine near the Tower of London and registred there by the name of Henry Fox and the other of them was baptized in the Parish Church belonging to the said Parish of Aldgate and registred there by the name of Mary Fox And this Depont. Saith that divers years ago she was hired and lived as hired servant by the year at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds with one Robert James a Taylor  in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the said County and Continued in such Service for three Years and by means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that she has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subseqt. Settlement and that one Henry Fox deced to whom this Dept. was never married did get her with Child of ye sd two Bastard Child.

Sworn this 22d day of February 1768 before  Chris: Scott Rt Darling

The Mark of Elizabeth Herbert
