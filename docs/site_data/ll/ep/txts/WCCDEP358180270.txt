Middx  Hugh Hughes aged about 58 Years removed with his Wife Mary Hughes aged about 41 Yrs. from the Parish of St. Mary in Whitechappell by Order Dated the 2d. Day of Janry. Instt. upon his Oath saith he Intermarried with his sd. Wife  Mary on or abt. the 12th. Day of Septr. 1742 at the Fleet London Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served 5 Years Apprentiship with Mr. Andrew Richards in Beaufort Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Cordwainer that his sd. Apprentiship hath been Expired abt. 31 Years since which time he hath not Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 2d. 1744 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Hugh Hughes
