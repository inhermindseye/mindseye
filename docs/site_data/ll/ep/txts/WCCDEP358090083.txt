Middx to wit Sarah Vallance Aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House was Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year Says her Father before his Intermarriage with this Examinant Mother and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant to one Mr. Lines or Lisle in Bread Street near Drapers Gardens in the Parish of Saint Peter Le Poor London for Several Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds or upwards and after he quitted his said Service he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1779 before}  Wm Kitchiner

her Sarah Vallance Mark
