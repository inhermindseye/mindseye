Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Hargat of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Widow taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County the fifteenth day of July 1784 Who Saith that she is now with Child and that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate and that Angelis Gio Batta Maghara of the Precinct of St. Catherine in the Said County Mariner is the Father of the Said Child

Taken & Signed the day and Year above written before me R Davies

The Mark of Ann Hargat
