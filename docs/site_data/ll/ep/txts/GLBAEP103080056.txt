Middlesex ss  Ann Toby of Whites yard in the parish of St. Mary White Chappel in the county of Middx Widow Maketh oath that she well knows Peter Earley an Infant Male Child aged about seven Years the son of Michael Earley  Coal heaver deced who lately lived on Salt Petre Bank in ye Parish of St Mary White chappel aforesaid and where he lived for several Years And this Depont. also knew the sd. Michael Earley s wife & Mother of the said Child whom this Deponent has lately heard & been informed has been Transported beyond sea for some Robbery lately Committed by her and this Deponent saith that she was present at the Birth of the said Child and acted as Midwife on that occascon at the lodgings of the said Michael Earley Situate in Hawkins's Rents on Salt Petre Bank aforesd. which Place where ye sd Child was born as aforesd. is in the sd Parish of St Mary Whitechappell

Sworn this 7th: day of May 1760  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Ann Toby
