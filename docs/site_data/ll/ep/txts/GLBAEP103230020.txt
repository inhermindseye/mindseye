Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Armstrong  Single . woman maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Mr William Miller of the Victualling office in the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of twenty years & has not Since hired as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 24 day of February 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Armstrong
