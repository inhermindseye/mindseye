Middx.  Dorothy Jennison upon Oath saith she never was married and that on the 9th. Day of June 1751 she was delivered of a female bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Galloway a Pen maker in Hannover Yard Oxford Road in the parish of St Mary le bone in the County of Middx since baptized Mary which sd. female Child about ten Weeks age was dropt in the parish of st Clemt. Danes middx & has since been maintained at the Charge of the Inhabitants of the sd. parish and this Examinant further maketh Oath that Richard Payne a Bricklayer is the reputed father of the said Child the sd. Rd. Payne having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant several times in the month of Septembr. 1750 & at sevl. other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the said female bastard Child of which she was delivered as afsd. & that the said Richard Payne & no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Novr. 2d. 1753 before}

Dorothy Jenason

her Midwife was Mrs. Lyon warden Strt. Tho Lane
