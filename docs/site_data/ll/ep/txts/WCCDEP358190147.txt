Middx  This Examination is False  Hannah Warner aged about 47 Years Destitute of Lodging upon Oath saith she is the Wife of William Warner ( gone from her ) to whom she was Married at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about 23 Years ago That he lived in & rented a House in St. Clement's Lane in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for Four Years at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds Quitted the Same upwards of Ten Years and hath not since kept House or paid Parish Taxes That she hath two Children living by her said Husband to wit Robert aged Six Years & upwards and Ann aged 5 Years

Sworn the 27th. day of June 1761 Before.}
