Alexander Hector aged thirty two Years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith, That he inter- -married with Mary his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles Cripplegate about the Year one Thousand seven hundred and Seventy three, That he has one Child living by his said Wife, namely George Lord Hector aged ten Years or thereabouts, who is now at Woodbridge in Suffolk under the Care of this Examinants relations, that his said Wife died about three Years ago, And this Examinant further Says that white she was single and unmarried he served as an Apprentice under Indentures duly Executed to a Mr. John Shaw of Leman Street  Goodmans Field in the Parish of Saint Mary Matfellon otherwise Whitechapel Silk Thereafter for seven Years, and that the whole of his said Apprenticeship his said Master lived in Leman Street Goodmans Fields aforesaid. - and that since he this Examinant quitted his said Master he has not rented any House Lodging or other Tenement of ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or done anyother Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge he may have gained any other Settlement subsequent to his Settlement to gained

in the said Parish of Saint Mary Matfellon otherwise Whitechapel by Serving such his Apprenticeship as above is stated

Sworn this 26th. Novr- 1784 before Sampn Wright Jenk: Reading

Alexander Hector
