St. Clement Danes. July 18. 1788

Elizabeth Johnson Aged 17 Years thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That her Father kept the Taylors Arms in Clements Lane for 7 Years or upwards and paid upwards of £40 a Year Rent. That he left the Same about 12 Months ago that she lived with him till he left the Taylors Arms that her Father to the Fleet that her Mother lid. in Fleet Lane. That she was delivered of a Female Child (who was since baptized by the Name of Eliza Perrott at St. Andrews Church )-at her Mothers Lodging at the House of Mr. Mark a Hair dresser in Fleet Lane aforesaid,-that She never was Married and the House of Mr. Mark is in the Parish of St. Sepulchre London , and that she is poor unable to Maintain her Said Child

Sworn before me this 18 July 1788 R Butler

Elizabeth Johnson
