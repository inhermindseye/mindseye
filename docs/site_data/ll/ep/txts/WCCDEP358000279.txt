Saint Clement Danes

Margaret Mc.Farlane aged 16 or in thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That She was born in Perthshire , That she came from there with her Father & Mother, that her Father Name is Daniel Mc.Farlane her Mother was Ann , that they were Married as informd. her in Saith and, That her Father about Four Years ago kept the Three Suns upon Snow Hill , in the Parish of St. Sepulchre that he moved from thence to Bear Alley in the same Parish where he took a House of £12 pr. Ann, that She left him about 4 Yrs ago that since that Time She has not been 12 Months in Service or done any Act Matter or Saing whereby She may have gained a Settlement. to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn 25 April 1786 before us Jno. Barnfather  Jno. Machin

her Margaret Mc Farlane Mark
