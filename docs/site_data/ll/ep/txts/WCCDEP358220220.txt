Westmr. ss  John Harvey Aged about fifty Seven Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Austin London about Seventeen Years ago and who has been dead about fifteen Years Says he last Rented and lived in a House in the Parish of Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about five Years ago and after quitting the same he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 15th. day of January 1770 before} R. Butler Tho. Kynaston

John Harvey
