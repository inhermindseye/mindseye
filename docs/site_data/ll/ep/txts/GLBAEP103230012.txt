Middlesex  Janett Goodall widow of Alexander Goodall deced maketh oath that about Seventeen years ago her sd late husband lived in & rented a house of the yearly Rent of Eight Pounds opposite St Catherines Dock in the Precinct of St Catherine in the Sd County & during such his residence herein he was Charged to and Paid Kings Taxes for the Same She this Depont frequently herself Paying Such Taxes to the late Mr George Charles he Collr thereof & Such Receipts were delivered upto the Landlord of the Sd house on the Rent for the Said house being Paid to him That her sd husband did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly Rent often Pounds a year or upwards for Save any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes to gain him Settlement out of the sd Precinct of St Catherine nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself and that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this 26 day of Janry 1787 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Janett Goodall
