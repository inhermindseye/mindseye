Saint Clement Danes 13 Octr 1792.

Middx & Westminstr.}  Child  St. Paul Covent Garden  to wit Mary Wilson aged 43 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she intermarried with David Wilson at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields about 24 years ago. That her said Husband died about 18 Months ago, That John Elisha Wilson aged about Eleven years is her Son by the said David Wilson That her said Husband Rented a House in Denmark Court for about Two year and an half and paid £28 a year for the same beside Taxes that He died in the said House in Denmark Court which is in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middx That the said John Elisha Wilson her Son has not gained any Settlement in his own Right to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 13th. Octobr. 1792 before us. } N Bond Thos. Gordon

Mary Wilson
