Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Mary Johnson Aged 40 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was married to Richard Johnson her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about Four years ago, That before and after their Intermarriage her said Husband Rented and Lived in a Shop Parlour and  Kitchen Part of a House Situate in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Field in the county of Middlesex aforesaid at the yearly Rent of thirteen pounds for upwards of one Quarter of a year, that they quitted the same about two years ago, and since that time her said Husband has not to her Knowledge done any Act whereby to gain any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3rd. Janry 1785 before  Tho. Collin G Mercer

her Mary Johnson Mark
