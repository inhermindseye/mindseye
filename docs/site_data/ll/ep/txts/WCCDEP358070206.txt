Middlesex & Westminster}  Jane Besford Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father Francis Besford Rented and lived in a house for several Years in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same as her Mother has often informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on Saturday the 6th day of Augt. last she was delived of a Male Bastard Child and is now in the Work house belongg. to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes which has since been baptized by the name of George and is now chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same Says that one George Donadieu a Midshipman at Mrs. Donadieu's in George Street York Buildings is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at a House (and as she believes) at the Bagnio in St. Martins Lane the latter End of Decr. last but the particular Day she does not now remember at which time he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered As aforesaid And that the said George Donadieu and no Other person did beget the same and that she is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 2d. day of Septr. 1774 before  William Addington

her Jane Besford Mark
