Middx to wit Elizth. Hopwood Aged about 13 years on her Oath says she is the Daughter of James Hopwood who is now at Sea says her said Father Rented & lived in a House in Wells Street Oxford Road in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £30. hath quitted said House upwards of one year & after quitting same he never gained any other Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Father quitted the same either by being bound on true Apprentice or living a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 7th. day August 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner M Addington

her Elizth. Hopwood Mark
