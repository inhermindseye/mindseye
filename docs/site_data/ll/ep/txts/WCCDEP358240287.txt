St. Clement Danes July 15th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St John Hackney  (to wit) Elizabeth Orgar  singlewoman aged 28 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs Parquot at in Hill Street Upper Clapton in the parish of St John at Hackney in the County of Middlesex for two years and upwards at the Annual Wages of six pounds that she quitted the same about far four years ago and she hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together under one hiring or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of the Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 15th day of July 1793 before Us W Addington Wm Kitchiner Elizabeth Orgar
