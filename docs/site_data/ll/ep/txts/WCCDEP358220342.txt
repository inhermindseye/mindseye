Westmr. to wit Mary Lewis Aged about Thirty Seven Years on her Oath says she never married Rented House or paid Taxes Says her late Father Thomas Lewis Rented and lived in a House in Round Court in the Strand in the parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Thirty Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of £14 or thereabouts & where he died Says that since her said Fathers Death this Examinant never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 21st. day of April 1772 before} Tho. Lane R. Butler

Mary Lewis
