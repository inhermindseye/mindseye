Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  this Exn. after Burtle never Sworn the M Settled & Security given to wit Sarah Downes aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr Anderson who kept the five Bells in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 14 Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted said Service about 3 years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere And further on her Voluntary Oath says  that on the 23d. day of Novr. last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which has not yet be on baptized And further saith that one Richard Long who lives as a Servant with one Mr. Hillan Attorney at No. 15 Coney Court Grays Inn is the father of the said Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of March last and at several different times since at one of which time he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that he and no one else is the father of the said Child and that she is poor and unable to maintain the same

Sworn this day of Decr. 1785 before}
