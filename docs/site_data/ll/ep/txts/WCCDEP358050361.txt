Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Parrott aged abt. 27 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Joseph Parrot who dyed upwds. of two Years since and that her said late husband lived in a house of his own at Cheltenham in the County of Gloucester and lived therein two or three Years and never after he quitted the sd. house nor has she since his Decease rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of two Male bastard Childn. in the house of the late Mr. Forrester in Houghton Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes on the 15th. of October last

one of which (baptized William ) is since dead and the other, hath been baptized James and is still living and likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same  and that James Wills a Servant to Mr Breton of Carnaby market Sugarbaker did be get the said twin male bastard Children on her body this Examint. being at that time a Servant to the sd. Mr Breton the said James Wills there became acquainted with her and had carnal knowledge of her body in the month of January last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with the sd. twin male bastard Children of which she was delivered as aforesd. and that the sd. James Wills & no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Novr. 8th. 1750 before H Fielding

Ann Parrott
