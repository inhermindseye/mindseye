Westmr. to wit Ralph Miers aged about fifty Nine Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with his late Wife at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields upwards of thirty Years ago and who has been dead about Seven Months and by whom he hath no Children livings Says he rented and lived in a House the Corner of Little Wyld Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year & an half at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the same upwards of Twenty Eight Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Augt. 1772 before}  W Kelyngs

his Ralph Miers Mark
