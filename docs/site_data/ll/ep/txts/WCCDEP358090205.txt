Middx to wit Rebecca Dark Aged about Thirty six years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly servant for One year. Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwarden and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of middlesex to her Grand Father John Ivory a Carpenter at Acton in the Parish of Acton in the County of Middlesex untill this Examinant shod. attain her Age of Twenty One years or day of marriage and after she was out of her said Apprenticeship she never gained any other settlement otherwise than as aforesaid

Sworn this 23rd. day of March 1781 .. before R Butler Sampn Wright

her Rebecca Dark  mark
