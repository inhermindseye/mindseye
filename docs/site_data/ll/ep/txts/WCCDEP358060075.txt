Middx  Ann Fisher upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Godfrey Fisher who dyed upwards of 20 Yrs. since and that the sd. Godfrey Fisher was a Hamburgher and never gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or belief And that at the time of her intermarriage with the above named Godfrey Fisher she was the Widow of Rd. Dring with whom she intermarried about 40 Yrs. ago at Lincolns Inn Chapel Says the sd. Rd. Dring was a Covenant Servant three Years with Mr Bizel a Staymaker in Russell Street Covent Garden and that his sd. master provided him with Dyet Washing & Lodging during the said Term and that the sd. Rd. Dring never rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes nor has this Examinant ever rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn March 5th. 1750 before  Tho Lane .

Ann Fisher her Mark
