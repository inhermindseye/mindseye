Middx  John Dodd aged about 68 Years upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with his present Wife  Sarah aged 50 Yrs. & upwds: at the Parish Church of St. Ann within Aldersgate London about Seven Years since that he lived in & rented a house in Sheir Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £24. & lived in the same for the Space of Six Years & hath left same about five Years since which time he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworne May 16th. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Jno: Dodd
