Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  to wit William Aros of No.17 the Cloister near Bartholomews Hospital on behalf of Fra Acros this Depts. Mother who lays ill at Mrs. Henbroughs in Drury Lane Upon his Oath saith that upwards of 40 years ago his sd. Mother Intermarried with Jos: Acros his Prest. father at  May Fair Chaple that his said Father had a Lease of a House situate near the Mitre Broad Wall in the Parish  of Chri Church Surry for wch he  gave £28 subject to a Ground Rent of £3..10..0 that his said Father Paid the Land Tax due for the same that his said Father he kept same for 7 years that he has left it about 10 years since which time he has not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.
