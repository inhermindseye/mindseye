Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Grant aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that in the Month of May 1785 she Intermarried with George Grant her late husband at the Parish Church of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster That he has been dead about nine Months That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Dukes Court in the Parish of saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Forty years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds that he quitted said house about Three Months before his Decease after which he never did any act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of June 1790  Sampr Wright N Bond

Elizabeth Grant
