Middlesex ss  Benjamin Lewis maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound apprentice to John Hughes of the Parish of Talsarn in the County of Cardigan in Wales  Taylor & Served the Whole of his apprenticeship there and this Deponent farther Saith that since to wit divers Years ago he Rented a house of the Yearly Rent & Value of Ten Pounds in Shoemaker Row in Dukes the Parish of Saint James in Dukes Place in the City of London and Continued in the occupation of Such house for the space of Two Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Deponent Saith that he hath not since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that he hath a Wife named Martha .

Sworn this 7th. day of August 1773 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

Benjn Lewis
