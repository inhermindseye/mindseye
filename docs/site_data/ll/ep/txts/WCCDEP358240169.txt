Examination merely to satisfy a suspicious Husband

(Nil)

Saint Clement Danes Octr 10th. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  This a Woman a parishioner of St C D Vide Pages 4 (Bastard's Book No. 2)  to wit Charlotte Jackson the wife of John Jackson of Piccadilly Porter Maketh Oath and Saith that the Child or Children she in now pregnant with was begetter on her Body by the said John Jackson her said Husband and that he the said John Jackson and no one else in the Real and true Father of such Child or Children and this Examinant Further saith that Elizabeth Ann Jackson , William Jackson and Margaret Jackson this Deponents three Children who are now living are all of them the Children of the said John Jackson her said Husband and by him lawfully begotten on this Deponents Body and that the said John Jackson alone and no one else hath since the time of their Intermarriage had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent Body

Sworn this 10th. Octr 1792 before me } R. Butler

Charlotte Jackson
