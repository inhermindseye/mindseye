Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Lucas maketh oath That her Father Anthony Lucas who is now resident in the Parish of Saint George in the Sd County Rents a house there is now & for Sometime past has been Rated for Such house to the Poors Rates & Pays Such Rates therefore That She this Depont. has a Sister named Jane Lucas aged about Sixteen Years & half who lately lived with her sd Father in the Said Parish as part of his Family & hath done no Act to this Deponts knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right.

Sworn this. 28. day of July 1780 before Penleaze Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Elizabeth Lucas
