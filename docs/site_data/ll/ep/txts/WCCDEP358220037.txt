Westmr.  Mary Clarke Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Clarke who has been dead upwards of One Year and an half by whom she has one Child named Mary aged upwards of One Year Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Eagle Court the Corner of Bedford Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster of the yearly Rent of fifteen Pounds lived therein about Three years where he died Says she hath not since her said Husbands Death Rented House paid taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of June 1769 before Us} Tho Lane  Sam Wright

Mary Clark
