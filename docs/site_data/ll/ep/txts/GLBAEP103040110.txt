Middlesex ss.  George Timperley maketh Oath that about Thirty Eight Years ago he Rented a House of Ten Pounds a Year in Peter Street Half Moon Alley in the Parish of St. Botolph Bishopsgate in the City of London, and known or described by Nr 6 . and lived in the same upwards of Three Years and was charged to and paid all Parochial Taxes for the same, that he has not since he quitted the said House Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement, And further saith that being Poor and unable to provide for himself he has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate for Relief & has been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 16th Day of March 1798 before  Dane Williams R Davies

George Timperley
