Middx Westmr. to wit} The Examination of Jno. Nurse taken the 29th. Day of May 1741 Who upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with Margt . his now Wife on the 24th- Day of Decr- 1702 to his now best Remembrance & that before his Intermarriage with Margt. his sd. Wife he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Emanuel Howard in Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the Liberty of Westmr Poulterer & further upon his Oath saith that he never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. whilst he was a Singleman for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Samul Clarke

John Nurse
