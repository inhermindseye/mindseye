Westmr.  Sarah Kent aged about 53 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Henry Kent who has been dead about 20 Yrs, says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Parker in Church Entry in the Parish of St. Brides London for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds quitted said Service about seven Years ago and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year.

Sworn 26th, May 1767 , before}
