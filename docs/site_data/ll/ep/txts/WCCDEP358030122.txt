Middx  Sarah Pidgeon aged abt. 18 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she was born in the Parish of Keldon in the County of Essex & was putt out by the Town to Mrs. Webb of the same Parish with whom she lived one Year and upwds. & had for such Service Meat Drink Washing & Lodging Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mrs. Webb rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn 27th. March 1745 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Sarah Pidgeon
