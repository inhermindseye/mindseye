Middlesex & Westmr.}  Ann Lawrence Aged about fifteen Years upon Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year; Says she has been Informed and verily believes that she was born in King Street  Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex And Says she has been in the Workhouse in the said Parish of St. Giles for many Years and has not been out from thence above a Week.

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1770 before us} R. Butler

her Ann Lawrence Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
