Middx to wit Elizth. Roberts aged about Fifty One Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Robert Roberts who died about Twelve Month ago and by whom she hath Two Children living now provided for Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Fetter Lane the Corner of Cliffords Inn in the Parish of Saint Dunston in the West London for Three Months at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Five Pounds hath Quitted said House upwards of Twenty years ago since which time her said late Husband never rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or otherwise gained any other Settlemt.

Sworn this 19th. day of July 1784 before  Sampr Wright  Wm Kitchiner

Elisabeth Roberts
