Westmr.  Elizabeth Bowen aged about 32 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Risley in Arrundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one Year and Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds and hath quitted said Service about Five Years. since which time she hath not Rented a house of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds, paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year,

Sworn the 28th. of October 1765 , before} Fran Cary
