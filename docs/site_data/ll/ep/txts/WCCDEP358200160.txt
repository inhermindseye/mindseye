Middx  Mary Williams Aged about Twenty Seven Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Wm- Williams (Gone from her) with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of Lavingham in the County of Suffolk Says her said Husband never was an Apprentice Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her said Husbands Father Rented a House in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Nine Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Eight Pounds hath quitted said House about Fifteen Years & hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes as this Examt. hath been Informed & which Information she Verily beleives to be true Says she has One Child by her said Husband Named William Aged about Nineteen Weeks

Sworn the 16th Day of November 1764 } Benjamin Cox

Mary Williams
