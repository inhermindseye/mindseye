Middx  Thomas Gibbons aged 18 years upon Oath Saith he was bound out Apprentice by the Church wds. & Oversrs of the Poor of the Parish of St Giles in the fields to Mr. Roach a Coffin plate Engraver & lived with him in Bartholemew Close in the Parish of St. Bartholemew the Great London about two Years then ran away from his sd master & never was assigned over to any other Person

Sworn Novr. 28th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Thos Gibbons
