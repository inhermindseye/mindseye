Middx  Mary Handforth  Wido . upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Joseph Handforth who aged about five Years since and that her sd. Late husband was born at Congerton in Cheshire and Served his Apprentiship in Ireland that neither her sd. late husband nor hath this Examinant since his Decease Rented Ten pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 3d. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Handforth
