Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Passd. to St. Giles in the fields to wit Cath: Doyle behalf of Jeremiah Bryan aged about 9 years on her Oath says that Wm. Bryan the Father and Ann Bryan the Mother of the said Jeremh Bryan that they were both Natives of Ireland and were married about 14 years ago at the Spanish Ambassador Chappell in Soho Square according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Romish Church that the Father was a Taylor and that neither he nor the said Ann Bryan the Mother did according to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief ever gain any Settlement in England that his Father and Mother are both dead says that the said Jeremiah Bryan was born in Hog Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx were his Father and Mother then lived that the said Jeremiah Bryan has not gained any Settlement in his own right otherwise than by Birth

Sworn this 16th day of June 1785  Thos: Gordon  Tho: Gilbert

her Cath Doyle Mark
