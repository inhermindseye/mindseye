Westmr. 

Honor Sloman aged abt. 56 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that about 12 Yrs. ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Lally in Wear Street in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for two Years or thereabouts and has not since she left her said Service rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 24th 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Honor Sloman
