Middlesex ss}  Thomas Cunningham maketh oath That divers Years ago he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Guineas in Burnhill Row in the Parish of St Luke in the County of Middx & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & has not Since done any Act to gain Another Settlement & has a wife named Susannah to whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this. 15. day of May 1778 before P Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

Thos: Cunningham
