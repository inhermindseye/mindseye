Mary Petee  Wife of Tho: Petee (now on Board his Majestys Ship Lenox) on her Oath Says she intermarried with the sd. Thomas Petee on the 29th. Day of July 1744 at the Fleet London Says the sd. Thomas Petee Served Seven Years Apprentiship with his Father Edward Petee Sawyer and lived one Year & upwards the latter Part of his Appprentiship with his sd. Father in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Says her sd. Husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 6 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Petee
