Middx to wit Elizabeth Rust Aged about Twenty Six years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or was an Apprentice says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Thomas Clay a Victualler who kept the Sign of the Talbot the Corner of Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for one Year at the yearly Wages of Ten Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 3rd. day of Novr Decr. 1780 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Elizth. Rust Mark
