Middx  William Smith aged about 68 Years passed from the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex Upon Oath says he intermarried with his present Wife  Elizabeth in the Parish Church of Gateside in the County of Cumberland by whom he hath one Child named Jane aged about six Years Says he never rented a House was an Apprentice or hired Yearly Servant Says his Father lived in a House in the Strand near St. Clements Church in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex but what the Rent was or how long he lived therein this Examinant cannot tell

Sworn the 4th. day of Octr. 1762 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of William Smith
