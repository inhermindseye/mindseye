Westmr.  Eliza. Ramsey Wife of Jeremiah Ramsey upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the said Jere. Ramsey  in the Parish Church of St. at a Church near Saint Pauls London upwards of Eleven Years since Says her sd. husband abt. four Months ago lived in & Rented a house in Hungerford Market in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds & upwds. for three Months & upwards Says her sd. husband has not since he quitted the said house in Hungerford Market rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 29th 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Eliz Ramsey
