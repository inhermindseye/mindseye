Middlesex to wit Ann Burford Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Nash at the Shirt Ware house in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to said Parish she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born And that one George Murray a journeyman Carpenter (who lately worked near the Middlesex Hospital but his Master's Name she does not know not does she know at present what is become of him) is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her late Master Mr. Vincents House near the Middlesex Hospital some time in March last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at One other time afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And and that the said George Murray & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 3d. day of Novr. 1778 before  Abrm Baley

her Ann Burford Mark
