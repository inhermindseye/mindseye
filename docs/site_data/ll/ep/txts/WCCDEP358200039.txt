Ann Brandling Aged about 29 Years upon Oath Says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Hemmings at Norton in the Parish of Kingstone upon Thames in the County of Surry upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted said Service about Ten Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 18th Day of Novr 1763 Before} Thos. Kynaston  Benjamin Cox

Ann Brandling

Upon Mr Jones Complaint J: W
