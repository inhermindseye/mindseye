Middx  Jas. Dempster or Behalf of Mary Webb an Infant aged about 8 Yrs. & Wm. Webb an Infant aged about 3 Years upon his Oath saith the sd. Infants are the lawfull Issue of Wm. Webb deced by Martha his Wife gone from them as they was generally reputed & this Examint verily beleives Says the sd. William Webb rented a house of Ten pounds a Year in Brokers Alley in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. & lived therein upwards of Three Months & there Dyed & hath been Dead about 3 Weeks Says the sd Martha Webb hath not since the Death of the sd. Wm. Webb rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 30th 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Jas. Dempster
