Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Emerson aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That on the 25th. day of August now last past She this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Bell in Craven Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the name of Peter That one Peter Hunter an Attorney at Law who lives in Queen Street Durham is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Peter Hunter is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 19th. day of Septr. 1798 . before me. } J Hunter

Mary Emerson
