Margt. Lawson  Wido . of Thomas Lawson who dyed abt. three months since upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the said Thomas Lawson in the Parish Church of St Gregory by St. Pauls London on the 8. April 1746 by whom she hath two Childn. Francis aged about three Years & Sarah aged about Six weeks Says her said husband Informed her and She verily beleives about Eight Years ago Contracted with Mr. Patrick Sullivan Weather Glass maker to live with him for three Years & the sd Mr. Sullivan in Cosidn. of five Guineas pd. him by her sd. late husband taught him his trade and provided him Dyet Washing Lodging and Cloaths for three Years and during all the said term her said late husband lay in the Sd. Mr Sullivans house the White Swan in Duke Street Lincolns Innfields

in the parish of St Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex Says has sd. late husband never after he quitted the sd. Mr. Sullivan nor hath this Examint. since his Decease rented ten pounds a Year pd. parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 2d. 1750 before H Fielding

Mark Lawson
