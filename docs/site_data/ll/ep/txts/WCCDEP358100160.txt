Middlesex to wit Gloud Groves aged about Fifty Eight years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Mary his late Wife deceased at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields about Twenty years ago & who died about five Months ago and by whom he hath one Child or Children living Says he Rented & lived in a House in Falwoods Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds hath quitted said House upwards of Sixteen years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year paid Taxes or or.wise howsoever

Sworn this 22d. day of Decr. 1783 G Mercer Jno. Barnfather

his Gloud Groves mark
