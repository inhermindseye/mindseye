Middx  Martha Davis  Singlewoman aged abt. 70 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwds. of 30 years ago she lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Oakley in Brook Street in the parish of St. Andrew in Holborn about the Bars in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for one Year & upwards and has not since she left her sd. Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn 18. April 1752 before} Tho Lane

The Mark  of Martha Davis
