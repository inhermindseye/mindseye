Middx  Margt. Bedward aged about 20 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edward Daintree in Hollywell Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds and Ten Shillings and hath left her said Service upwards of three Months and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 17th. day of March last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Poole (one of the Parish Nurses of the said Parish) in Clements Lane in the aforesaid parish of St. Clement Danes which was baptized Thomas and is since dead, says that one Thos. Mitchell a Baker who is run away & this Examinant can't tell where he is did beget the said Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the Months of May or June 1764 and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Thos. Mitchell and no other person did beget the same

Sworn the 15th. of April 1765 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Margt. Bedward
