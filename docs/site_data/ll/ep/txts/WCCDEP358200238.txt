Middx  Ann Jones Aged about Fifty Eight Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Sert . with Mrs. Steele a Publican in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx One Year & a half at the Yearly Wages of Three pound Ten Shillings hath quitted said Service about Twelve Years & hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year

Sworn the 12th of Feby 1765 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Ann Jones
