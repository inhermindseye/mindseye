Middx  Jane Brown aged about 35 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about two Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Huntridge at the five Bells Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly wages of Four pounds and has not since she Quitted her said Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 15th. 1750 before  Tho Lane

The mark of Jane Brown
