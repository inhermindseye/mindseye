Middlesex ss}  Ann Gill the wife of Thomas Gill maketh oath that her Said husband is now Absent from her at Sea That She has been informed by her sd Husbands Mother & which She believes to be true that her sd husband was duly bound Apprentice by Indre to John Rixon of the Parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry  Cooper for Seven Years & after having & Served his sd Master there for about four years his sd Master removed with him to the Parish of St George in the County of Middx Where he Serve the remainder of his Apprentice ship Save for about one year when his Indres were delivered up & he was discharged from his Said Apprenticeship & but that during the last eighteen months of Such his Apprenticeship her Sd husband lodged in his Fathrs house in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx That her sd husband has not Since done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere That She hath two Children namely Mary aged about two Years & a Boy not yet baptized aged three Weeks the lawful issue of this Depont by her sd husband to whom she was lawfully Married.

Sworn this 10th day of Novr 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Gill
