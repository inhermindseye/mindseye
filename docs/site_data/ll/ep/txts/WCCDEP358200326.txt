Middx & Westmr.}  Susannah St. Lawrence aged about 22 Yrs. upon Oath says she was never Married and that she was bound an Apprentice for two Years to Danl. Green of Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and served One Year and Eleven Months of her said Apprenticeship and then left her said Master which is now upwards of one Year; since which time she hath not Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes , says that William Hardy a Journeyman Perukemaker had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of May last and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Wm: Hardy and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 21st. day of Novr. 1765 . Before} Paul Vaillant

Susanna St Lawrence
