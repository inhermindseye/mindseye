Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Terrier  wife of Charles Terrier maketh oath That about four Years ago her Said husband left her & Went on a Voyage from England to America & that the last time She this Deponent heard from him was by a Letter She reced from him dated from Maryland in the month of June 1775 & She doth not now know nor hath She been informed Since the receipt of the Said Letter Whether he is at present living or dead but is induced to believe that since his departure from her as aforesaid he has never returned from beyond Sea to England That her Said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that many Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages with John Hattersley of Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said Count Oilman & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for divers Years & that by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and to this Deponents knowledge & belief her said husband has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year in England or rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere in England nor hath She this Deponent Since her Said husbands departure as aforesaid done any Act with an intent to gain a Settlement for herself Separate from her Sd husband And that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 12 day of October 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Eleanor Terrier
