Westmr.  Eliz Mitchel upon Oath Saith she never was married that she can give no account of the Settlemt. of her late father John Mitchel (deceased)further than that he was born Somewhere in Devonshire & that her mother Dorothy Mitchel (likewise deceased)was born at Southmoulton in the same County That she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Janry 6th. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Mitchel
