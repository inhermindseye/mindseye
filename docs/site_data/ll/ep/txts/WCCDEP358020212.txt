Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William Giffard aged about 60 years upon his Oath saith that about 36 years ago her Intermarried with Elizabeth Carr (who has been dead about Twelve years) at the Parish Church of Medburn in Leicestershire by whom he hath Six Children living vizt. Wm. aged about 27 years Alice aged about 30 years Thomas aged about 20 years Sarah aged about 19 years and Mary aged about 17 years all provided for and Ann aged about 15 years unprovided for That he this Examinant Rented and lived in a house at Narrow Wall Lambeth Walk in the Parish of Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry for Six Months Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds that he hath done no Act since he quitted said house to gain a Subsequent Settlement and this Examinant further saith that his Daughter Ann never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this 3d. day of July 1790  Sampn Wright  Herbt: Mayo

Wm Giffard
