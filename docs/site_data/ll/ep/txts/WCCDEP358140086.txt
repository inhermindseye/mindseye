Middx.  Chas. Cooper aged abt. 59 Yrs. upon Oath says he intermarried with Ann his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. abt. Sixteen Years ago says he lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Thos. Each at the Trumpet in Great Shire Lane in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for one Year & a half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds he being at that time a Single Man & hath quitted said Service abt. Seventeen Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 22d. day of Feb: 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

Charles Cooper
