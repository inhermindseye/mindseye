Westmr. 

Mary Enstead upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Gilbert Dixon a Bottleman in While horse Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £3, £3..10s, £4 & £5. for the Space of 4 Years or thereabouts & hath not quitted her sd. Service abt. 3 Yrs. Says she hath not since she left her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Febry 7th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of mary Enstead .
