Middx  Catherine Ellis Aged abt. Fifty Eight Years Upon Oath Says She is the Wido. of Ealy Ellis who hath been Dead abt. Six Months Says her sd. late Husband Served Seven Years Apprentishipp with Mr. George Green a Cork Cutter in Holborn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx & never Rented House or pd. Taxes

Sworn the 11th. day of Decr. 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Catherine Ellis
