Saint Clement Danes 30th. August 1788

Middx to wit  Ann Johnson  singlewoman aged 16 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That She is the Daughter of James and Hannah Johnson that Her Father kept the Taylors Arms Clements Lane 5 or 6 Years, That her Mother a few Months ago. took a House in Mint Street in the Parish of St. George in the Borough of Southwark that she took it of a Mr. Henderson of Mint Street at the Yearly Rent of £12: that She lived with her Mor. there near 4 Months, that her Mothers Sister lived with Them and lent her Mother the Money To pay the Rent-that her Father is in the Fleet and she is Poor and unable to maintain herself.

Sworn before us this 30th. Augt: 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

Ann Johnson
