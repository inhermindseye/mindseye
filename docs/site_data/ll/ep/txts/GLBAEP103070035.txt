Middx ss  Jane Hatton  Singlewoman daughter of James Hatton and Jane his Wife both Deceased Maketh oath that she is the lawfull issue of the sd James and Jane Hatton And Saith that her sd. father she was never a hired Servant by the Year or Married

Sworn this 21 day of June 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards

his Jane Hatton Mark
