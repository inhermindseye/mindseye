Middx  Mary Griffith upon her Oath saith that about 10 Yrs. ago she intermarried with her prest. Husband Raunce Griffith at the Fleet  London That about Three Years since her sd. Husbd. lived in & rented a House of the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of one Year or thereabts- that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. with for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That she hath 2 Childn - viz: Sarah aged Eight Years & David aged Eight Months & being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne the 27th- Novr. 1742 before}

Mary Griffeth
