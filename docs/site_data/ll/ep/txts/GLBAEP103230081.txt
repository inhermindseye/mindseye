Middlesex ss}  Mercy White the widow of William White deced maketh oath that her late husband heretofore rented a house in Shadwell market in the Parish of St. Paul Shadwell in the Said County & during Such his Residence which was for divers Years he was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he died in the Said Parish & was there legally Settled at his death That She this Deponent Since his death hath not rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or lived as hired Servant for a year or been married or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married.

Sworn this 2 day of November 1787 before  R Davies  Robt Smith

Mercy White
