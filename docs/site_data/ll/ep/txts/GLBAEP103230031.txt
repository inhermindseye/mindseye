Middlesex ss}  James Ricthie maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in Stone Court in the Parish of st Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & during Such his residence therein he was charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same That he has not since rented a house or Lodging of the yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds for Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That he hath a wife named Elizabeth and a child named Femie aged twenty three years the lawful Issue of this Depont by his said Wife to whom he was lawfully married That this Deponts said Daughter Femie Richie is at present ordered in her Mind and never was bound and Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant in any one Place for the Space of a year nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 22. day of March 1787 before  R Davies

James Ritchie
