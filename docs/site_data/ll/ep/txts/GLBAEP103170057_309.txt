Middlesex ss}  Rachael Cannon the widow of Charles Cannon mariner deced maketh oath that her said late husband was by birth an Irishman & to her knowledge or belief he never did any act to gain a Settlement in any part of England That She this Deponent previous to her marriage with her said husband & while She was a Singlewoman was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with William Wills in Cock alley in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Victualler at Certain Wages & continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means there of gained a legal Settlemt there That She did not afterwards live as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor do any act to gain anothr Settlement save what She has acquired by her marriage as aforesaid That She has a Child named Martha aged about two months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this 21 day of February 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Rachael Cannon
