Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Amey Sidney aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Richard Powell who lived in Batemans Row in the Parish of saint Leonard shoreditch in the County of Middlesex for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that the hath quitted said service about Two Years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Decr. 1789  Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Amey Sidney
