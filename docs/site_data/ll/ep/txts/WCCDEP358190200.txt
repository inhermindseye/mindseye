Middx  Ann Wilson aged about 33 Years Lodging in Clement's Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married bound an Apprentice kept House paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes or been a hired Servant for One year Says her Father Richard Wilson (who has been Dead about 22 Years) served his Apprenticeship with a Waterman in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry and also Rented a House in Lambeth Marsh as she has been informed and which Information she verily beleives to be true

Sworn the 5th: day of Novemr. 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Wilson
