Middx & Westmr.}  Hester Blacklock Aged about Thirty Nine Years on behalf of Edward Lewis an Infant Aged about Six Years on her Oath Says that the Father Edward Lewis who now lies ill in St. Thomas Hospital Rented and lived in a House in Feathers Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary le Strand for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds hath quitted the said House upwards of Three Years ago and after quitting the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of June 1776 before R Butler James Croft

Hester Bleacklock
