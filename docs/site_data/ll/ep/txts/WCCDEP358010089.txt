Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  to wit Rachael Rolfe aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that about 15 Months ago she Intermarried with Thos. Rolfe (her present Husband is ill) at the Parish Church of St. Brides alias Bridgett in the City of London that she has one Child living by here said Husband viz. Ann aged about 4 Months that her Husband was bound Apprentice to Wm. Rolfe his uncle who was a Bookbinder in Abchurch Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Abchurch in the City of London by Indres duly executed for 7 years that her said Husbands Master died when he had served about 6 years of his said Apprenticeship that then he went to work as a Journeyman since which time he has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of April 1787 .}

Rachel Rolfee never Sworn
