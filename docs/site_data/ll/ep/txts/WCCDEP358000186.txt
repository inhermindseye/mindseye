Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Elizabeth Goddard on behalf of Mary Hague who lays Ill at Mr. Smiths the Wheatcheaf in Clements Lane upon her Oath saith that the said Mary Hague has informed her that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Jones of East Street Red Lyon Square Coal merchant in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of 6 Guineas hath quitted said Service upwards of four Months ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge this Depts Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 29th. day of Decr. 1785 before  Sampr Wright J Addington

Elizabeth Goddard
