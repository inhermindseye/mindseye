Middx  Mary Lawrence aged abt. Twenty Years upon Oath says She was never Married Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes  was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for one Year Says She has been informed & verily believes her late Father Roger Lawrence Deced Served his Apprentiship with one Mr. Peal a Fellmonger in the Parish of Uxbridge in the County of Middx & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 27th. Day August 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Lawrence
