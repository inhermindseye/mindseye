St Clement Danes 18th Janry 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Ann Westmr.}  (to wit) Livy Vinsom Aged 36 Years or thereabouts Wife of John Vinsom who now is now ill in the Middlesex Hospital Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said John Vinsom at the parish Church of St Peter Poor in Broad Street in the City of London on th or about the 22d day of August in the year of our Lord 1782 (by) Leaving of that she hath three three Children by her said Husband but all from this Examinant, that her said Husband Rented and resided in an Apartment in the House of Robert Lan t situate in West Street Seven Dials in the parish of St Ann in the Liberty of Westminster for upwards of Nine Months at the Ann annual Rent of £18 that he quitted the same about five Months ago and hath not obtained Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief that her said Husband is now ill in the Middlesex Hospital & she this Deponent is unable to maintain herself and family without assistance

Sworn the 18th day of Janry. 1794 before us Rich.. Ford B: Kennett

her Livy Vinsom Mark
