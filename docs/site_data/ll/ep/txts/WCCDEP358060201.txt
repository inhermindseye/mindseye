Middx  Ann Temple upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Cornelius at the Rose Tavern near Temple bar in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 3 Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £6 and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 6th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Ann Temple
