Middx  Wm Shepheard aged upwds. of 23 Yrs. upon his Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years his Apprentiship with Mr. Saml: Randall a Clogmaker in St. Clements Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and has been out of his time upwards of one Year Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworne 19th: Novr. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

William Sheppheard
