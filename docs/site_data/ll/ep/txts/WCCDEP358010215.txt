Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Hudd aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she is the Daughter of Thomas and Sarah Hudd who as this Deponent has been informed belongs to the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex and that this Examinant never did any Act in her own Right to gain a Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of May 1788 D Walker Rups Clarke

her Sarah Hudd Sarah Hudd Mark
