Westmr. to wit Roger Heelas aged about Seventy Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish of St. Mary le Bone Middlesex about Twelve Years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says that he Rented and lived in a house Known by the Sign of the White Hart in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for three Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Twenty three Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of July 1773 before R Butler

Roger Heelas
