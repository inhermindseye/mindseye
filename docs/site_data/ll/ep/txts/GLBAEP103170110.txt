Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Ring  Singlewoman maketh oath That She hath been informed by her Mother Barbara Sharp & which She belives to be true That this Examinants late Father Alexr Ring deced in his life time was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Edward Wooding in the Parish of Wrolham in the County of Kent  Farmer at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards to this Deponts Knowledge information or belief live as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain another Settlement That She this Depont never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 1st. day of December 1777 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Elizabeth Ring
