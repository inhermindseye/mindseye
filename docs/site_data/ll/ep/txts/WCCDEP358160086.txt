Middx The Examination of Mary Wood the Wife of Nichs. Wood aged Sixty Years Passed from the Parish of Stretham in the County of Surry by Order bearing date ye. 20th. Day of Octr. Instant Who upon her Oath saith that abt. 11 Years ago her Husband Nicholas - Wood lived in & Rented a House in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £21 & lived in the same for the space of two Years And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith yt. her said Husband hath and since yt. time Rented any other House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife And yt. her said Husband now Absconds from her & she being Poor and Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn this 23d. Day of Octobr. 1739 before me} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Mary Wood
