Middx  Ann Winshurst Aged abt. Thirty Years Upon Oath Saith she is the Wido. of Wm. Winshurst with Whom she Intermarryed at May Fair Chapel abt. Ten Years since Says her sd. late Husband Dyed in Novr. last and that before his Intermarryage with this Examinants & whilst a he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servt with Mr. Chas. Poarter Taylor in Arrundell Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds & never Rented a House or Served a Parish Office as this Examinants sd. Late Husband informed her and with information she verily beleives to be true

Sworn the 9th. day of Febry 1761 before}

Ann Winshurst
