Westmr. to wit John Meredith Aged about Sixty Years on his Oath says he intermarried with Margaret his Wife at the Parish Church of St. George the Martyr Queen Square about Twenty Years ago and by whom he hath four Children now living all of whom is now provided for says he served Seven Years Apprentice with one Mr. Sadler a Baker  the Corner of Red Lyon Passage in the said Parish of Saint George the Martyr Queen Square in the County of Middlesex, and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 7th. day of June 1773 before} 

John Mardith his marker
