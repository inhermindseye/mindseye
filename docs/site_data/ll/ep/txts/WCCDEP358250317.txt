Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Ell aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith that about Twenty one Years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Ell her late Husband who has been dead about Seven years at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West That her said late Husband lived as a hired Yearly Servant (before his Intermarriage to this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman) with a Mr. Draper in Swallow Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months and upwards but what Wages he had she this Deponent doth not know and that after he quitted said service he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1797 before us} M Addington Jno. Groves

her Mary Ell Mark
