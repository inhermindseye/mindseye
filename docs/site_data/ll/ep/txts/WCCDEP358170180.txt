Middx  David Lewis on Behalf of Saml. Cowen an Infant aged abt. Nine Years upon his Oath saith that the Mother of the sd. Infant is long since dead & that abt. Nine Years ago Saml. Cowen the Father of the sd. Infant lived in & rented a House in Howard Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx . of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwds. or the Space of One Year & upwds. to the Best of his knowledge & beleife that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his her knowledge or beleife that the sd. Saml. Cowen is a Surgeon in Coll Mortons Regiment of Marines now abroad with Admirall Vernon & the sd. Infant is left unprovided for

Sworne the 18th. of Novr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

David Lewis
