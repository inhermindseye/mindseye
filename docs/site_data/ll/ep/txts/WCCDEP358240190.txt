St Clement Danes 23d January 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Giles in the Fields  (to wit ) Elizabeth Newton commonly called Elizabeth Douglas aged Eighty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of England but according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of Rome that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Morton in Great Wild Street in the parish of St Giles in the Fields for about fourteen years at the Annual Wages of £4:10s that she quitted that Service about 30 years ago and hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant with for a twelvemonth together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 23d day of January 1793 before us } Wm. Kitchiner D Walker

otherwise Elizth: Newton Douglas Mark

Witness Garrett

Removed by Edwd. Burbidge  Beadle 23d. Janry. 1793
