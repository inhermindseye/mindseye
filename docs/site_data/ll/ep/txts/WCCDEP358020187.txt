Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Esther Sells aged about 55 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Woodger in Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for Fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Thirty years ago since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for one year Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 6th, day of Febry. 1790 N Bond J Addington

her Esther Sells Mark
