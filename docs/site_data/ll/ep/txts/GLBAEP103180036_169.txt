Middlesex ss}  Edward Catherell maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to & Served his Apprenticeship with his Brother in Law Henry Ford in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Lighterman & Waterman & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. That he hath a wife named Isabella & two Children namely William aged about two Years & half & Edward Aged about eight Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Said wife to whom he was lawfully Married

Sworn this 4. day of May 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Edward Catherell
