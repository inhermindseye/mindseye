Middx  Thomas Cooper aged about 54 Years upon Oath saith he never was Married That he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Burroughs Cheesemonger in the Old Change in Watling Street in the Parish of St. Augustin vulgarly St. Austin in the City of London for upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds Quitted the Same about Ten Years ago and hath not since rented any House paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate or been been a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the day of October 1761 Before}
