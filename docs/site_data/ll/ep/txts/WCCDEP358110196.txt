Middx  Eleanr. Osborn upon Oath saith she is the widow of Jasper Osborn with whom she married at the Fleet London about fourteen Years ago and that she hath been informed and verily believes her sd. late husband was born at Stroudwater in the County of Gloucester and she can give no further Account of his Settlement and that since her sd late husbands Decease she has not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn March 2d 1753 before}
