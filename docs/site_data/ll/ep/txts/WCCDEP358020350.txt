St Clemt. Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Jane Savage Aged 38 years & upwards upon Oath saith that she intermarried with her prest Husband John Savage 16th. September 1776 at the parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn that  he then rented an Stall & Apartment made at the White Horse in the Strand at £14 a year rent which they have her said Husband hath since contd. the rent & they have resided in until about Christmas 1789 when they quitted the same - and her said Husband hath not (by any Act or means is & every acquired any Subsequent legal Settlement That they have 3 legitimate Children now living Namely Jane Aged Seven years & one Month or thereabouts, John Aged three years & Eight Nine Months or thereabouts and George Aged two years & four Months or thereabouts that they her said Husband & self are unable entirely to provide for themselves & family & therefore are obliged to apply for relief

Sworn the 4th Novr. 1791 . before me R Butler

Jane Savage Mark
