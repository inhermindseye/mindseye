Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Cary the wife of James Cary maketh oath That her said husband who is now absent from her beyond Sea was by birth a Foreigner & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England or Wales to her knowledge or belief That She this Deponent can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents or either of them That she has been informed by her late Mother Esther Langford deced & which She believes to be true that She this Deponent was born in the Parish of St Dunstan in the West in the City of London That She this Deponent was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year during the time that She was a Single & Unmarried Woman nor Ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself Save what he has Acquired by her marriage as aforesaid and that She was lawfully married to her Said husband who is still living as She believes

Sworn this. 29 day of Janury 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Cary
