Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Eleanor Nowland aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one John Sly at the Cookshop Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Two years & upwards at Yearly wages of £6 hath quitted said Service about years ago since which time she has not lived as hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained Subsequent Settlement And further this Exts.u her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes . That one John Hughes a Shoemaker at Mr. Skinners Evangelist angels Ludgate Hill is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Exts. Body in about 9 Months ago and at times since at one of which times he did beget said Child or Children of wch. she is now pregnant and that the said John Hughes is the only real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 26th. day of March 1787 } R Butler

her Eleanor Nowland mark
