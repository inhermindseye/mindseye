Middx  The Examination of Jno. Hind aged abt. Twenty Five Years Taken the 2d. day of Febry. 1738 Who upon his Oath saith that about Three Years & a halfe ago he this Examint. lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Leech in Hauton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Distiller for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds And this Examinant further saith that he hath not since that time Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} James Gronous

Jno. Hind
