Middlesex to wit Elizth. Shaw Aged about 27 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Knight a Milliner in that part of the Strand which is in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Sixteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of four Years ago since which time she never lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year or otherwise gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of August 1784 before  James Croft  James Paine

her Elizth. Shaw Mark
