Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Pratt the widow of William Pratt deced maketh oath That She hath been informed by a Letter lately received from one Mary Parker now resident in the Town of Birmingham & by other Credible information & which this Deponent Verily believes to be true That her said late husband duly Served his apprenticeship with one Wild in the Parish of Saint Martin in Birmingham in the County of Warwick  Buckle Chasemaker and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her Said husband did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds nor Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and this Deponent can give no other account respecting her Said late husband Settlement That She hath Five Children namely Joseph aged about Thirteen years two months John aged about Eight years and five months William aged about Seven years and five months and Thomas aged about Three years and five months and Elizabeth aged about nine months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married and none of which said children have done any act to gain a Settlement for themselves

Sworn this 30th day of August 1774 before B Camper Wilmot

The Mark of Elizabeth Pratt
