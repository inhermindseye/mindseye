Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Brides A. to wit Ann Holmes Aged about Thirty years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House Tenement or Ten pounds a year ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever was bound an apprentice Says her Father died when she was about four years old That her Mother Rented and lived in a House in Harp Alley in the Parish of Saint Brides otherwise Bridgett in the City of London  till the time of her death which is about 12 years ago and paid Taxes due for the same and That this Examinant lived with her said Mother till the time of her death

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1784  Sampn Wright

Ann Holmes
