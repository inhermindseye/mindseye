Middx  Eliz Cook  Wido . of John Cook who dyed abt. Twenty Years since upon Oath saith she intermarryed with the said John Cook about thirty Years ago in the Parish Church of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex and that her said late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Blann a Taylor in Cooks Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes aforesaid and never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servant or Served a Parochial Office nor has she this Examinant since his Decease rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 30th. 1749 before} Samuel Clarke

The mark of Eliz Cook
