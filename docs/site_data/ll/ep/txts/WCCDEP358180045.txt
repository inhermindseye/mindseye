Middx  Wm. Hale upon his Oath saith that about Seventeen Years ago he intermarried with his present Wife  Mary at the Fleet  London that about Seventeen Years ago he lived in & Rented a House in Church Yard Alley near Fetter Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn  London of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds for the Space of Fourteen Years that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne 24th- Novr. 1742 before} Wm: Jones  Tho Lane

William Hale
