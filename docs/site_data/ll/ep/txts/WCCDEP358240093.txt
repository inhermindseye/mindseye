St Clement Danes April 23d. 1792.

Middlesex and Westminster }  Lewisham Kent  (to wit) Sarah Hardy aged 29 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr John Pearson of Water Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex Coal Merchant for one year and ten four Months and upwards or thereabouts at the Annual Annual Wages of Ten Guineas that the last place where she resided for forty days together while she was in such Service was at Rishey Green in the parish of Lewisham in the County of Kent , that she went there with her Master's Mother & sister in or about the Month of July last and continued continued there with them till the beginning of October next following when they together with this Deponent came to her said Master's House & in Water Street aforesaid and that this Deponent left her said Master's Service the second day after their Arrival at his House turn Rich Lewisham parish as aforesaid having had previous warning from her Mistress And that this Examinant is pregnant and unable to support herself without Assistance

Sworn the 23d. day of April 1792 before us} Sampn. Wright  Tho Vaughan
