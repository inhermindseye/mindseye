Middx & Westmr  Jane Gerney aged about Twenty Years Lodging at Mr. John Clackstone 's in Clare Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlx upon Oath saith she intermarried with Richard Gerney in the Parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West London on the First day of February 1761 says she cannot give any certain Account of this Settement hath been informed he was Born in Hertfordshire and served the two frist years of his Apprenticeship to a Baker in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields Says her said Husband hath Absconded from her and that she does not know where he is

Sworn this 19th: day of January 1762 Before}

The Mark of Jane Gerney
