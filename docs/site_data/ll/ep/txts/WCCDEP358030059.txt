Middx  Robt. Downes upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his pres Wife Eliza. in the Abbey Church in Wells in the County of Somersett upwards of one Yr. since by whom he hath one Child viz: Eliz: aged abt. 3 Months Says he lived in & rented a house in the Parish of Ditckett in the County of Somersett of the Yearly Rent of Five Pounds for the Space of 3 Yrs. & pd. his Share of all Parochial Taxes & hath left same about 6 Yrs: Says he hath not since he left sd. house rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 5. 1745 before.}
