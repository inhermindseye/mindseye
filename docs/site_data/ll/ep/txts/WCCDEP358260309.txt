Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Markham aged about Forty one years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to her present Husband  Adam Markham at the Parish Church of Saint Sepulchre without Newgate about Seven Years ago (by Bains) that she hath been informed by her said Husband which she verily believes to be true that he is a Native of Scotland and hath not acquired any legal Settlement in England that she hath Two Children living by her said Husband namely Sarah aged about Four years and William aged about Six weeks And this Examinant further saith that she is intitled to her last legal Settlement in right of her Husband named Samuel Linter deceasad to whom she was married (by Banns) at the Parish Church of 

Saint Leonard Shoreditch about Twenty one years ago and who informed her which she verily believes to be true that he never obtained any legal Settlement in his own Right and that he was the Legitimate Child of John and Mary Linter both deceased that his father John Linter Rented and lived in an Apartment in a Place then called Little Essex Street in the Precinct of White Fryars London for about Ten years at the Weekly Rent of Four Shillings and did not during his Life obtain any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn this 4th day of Augt. 1798 before us  R. Ford Robinson

her Sarah Markham Mark
