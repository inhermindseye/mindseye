Middlesex ss}  Thomas Hunston Norris maketh oath that he heretofore lived in and rented a house known by the sign of the Cock ale house in Cock Alley in the Parish of st Botolph without Aldgate in the said County at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards & during such his residence wherein he was charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the same That he has not since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That by Virtue of a Pass order under the hands and seale of two of his majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Surrey bearing date the thirteenth day of March instant he this Deponent together with one Ann Dawson (therein described and named as the wife of this Deponent) were removed from the Parish of st Mary Newington in the County of Surrey to the said Parish of st Botolph without Aldgate as the place where they were last legally Settled and this Deponent farthr saith that She the said Ann Dawson never was the wife of this Deponent in as much as no marriage was ever had or Solemnized between this Deponent and her the said Ann Dawson and this Deponent Cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of her the sd Ann Dawson

Sworn this 27. day of March 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Thomas Hunston Norris
