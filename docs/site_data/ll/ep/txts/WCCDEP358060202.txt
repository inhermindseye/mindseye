Westmr.  Mary Davis aged about 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice to Mrs. Booker and Served upwds. of five Years of his Apprentiship with her said Master in Tufton Street in the Parish of St Margt. in the City of Westmr. and never was assigned over rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 8th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Davis
