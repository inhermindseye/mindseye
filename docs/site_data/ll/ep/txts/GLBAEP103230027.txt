Middlesex ss}  Mary Edwards the widow of Thomas Edwards deced maketh oath that since her said late husbands death She hath been hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mr Thomas Gibson a Victualler in upper East Smithfield in the Parish of st Botolph without Aldgate in the said County and Continued in such Service under Such hiring for the space of one whole year & upwards & has not Since done any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 13. day of March 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Mary Edwards
