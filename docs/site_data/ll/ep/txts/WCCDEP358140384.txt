Middx.  Amelia Hollyer aged abt. 27 years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mrs. Bruce in Swallow Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. for One Year at the Yearly Wages of £5 and hath quitted said Service abt. Two Years and has not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn June 28th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Amelia Hollier

Upon Mr Ponten's Complt. J: P:
