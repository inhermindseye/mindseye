Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Day the wife of Richard Day of the Town & Parish of Axbridge in the County of Somerset  Inn holder maketh oath That she was married to her Said husband in the Year one thousand Seven hundred and thirty Seven And this Depont Saith that her Said husband now resides at and Rents the Aug Inn in the Said Town The which Tenement as this Depont has been informed and verily believes for exceeds the yearly Value of Ten Pounds And that the Same is now let to her same husband for more than that Sum by the year And that her husband is Charged to & Pays Parish Taxes for the Same being the Chief or Principal Inn in the Said Town.

Sworn this 10th day of April 1769 before  Chris Scott R Pell

Eliz Day
