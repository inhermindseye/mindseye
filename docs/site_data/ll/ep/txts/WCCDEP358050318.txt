Middx  Jane Niblet aged 66 yrs upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Robt. Niblet who dyed about 6 Yrs. since and that the sd. Robt. Niblet rented a house of £10 a Yrs. in Kings head Court  Drury Lane and lived therein about three Years and quitted the same about 28 Yrs. Ago and never after rented Ten pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place Says she has not since her sd. late husbands decease rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 21st 1750 before}

The mark of Jane Niblet
