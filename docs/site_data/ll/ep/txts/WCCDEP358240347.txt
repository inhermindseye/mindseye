Not Sworn

St Clement Danes Novr. 8th. 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Elizabeth Combar aged fifty two years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired hired yearly Servant with Mr George of Houghton Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for about eight years at the Annual Wages of Eight pounds that she quitted that Service in or about the Month of November 1792 and hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 8th day of November 1793 before Me }
