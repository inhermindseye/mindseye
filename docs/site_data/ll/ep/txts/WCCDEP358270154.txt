St. Clement Danes Novr. 15th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnancy  Reputed Father  Frances Gloyne  singlewoman aged 25 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Gill at No. 4 Craven Buildings in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for four years and a quarter or thereabouts at the annual Wages of Six Guineas for the first year and the advanced Wages of ten shillings in addition to the Wages of Six Guineas each succeding year of such her service  that she quitted the same about six Months ago and hath not since obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And this Examinant Upon her Voluntary Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes when born that William Anthony  Shoemaker who lately lived at the Cock and Magpye in Drury Lane is the only real and true father of such the Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant as aforesaid he having fast had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body sometime about five Months on or before Christmas last and at Several times since at some or one of which times he

did beget the same thereon

Sworn the 15th day of Novr 1793 before me } Wm Kitchiner
