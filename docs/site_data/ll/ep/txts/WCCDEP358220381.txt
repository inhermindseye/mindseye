Westmr. to wit Sarah Valentine Aged about Thirty Three Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her late Husband George Valentine at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about a Eleven Years ago and by whom she hath Three Children now living Vizt. Susannah Aged Six Years George Aged upwards of Three Years and Mary Aged Sixteen Months Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Jones a Taylor in Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of said Apprenticeship he never Rented House or paid Taxes or done any other thing whereby to gain a Subsequent. Settlement.

Sworn this 10th. day of September 1772 before Us W Kelyngs R. Butler

Sarah Valintine
