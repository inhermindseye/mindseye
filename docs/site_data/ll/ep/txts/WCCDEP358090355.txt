Middx to Wit Elizth. Wimbolt Aged about 23 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her late Father & Mother John & Mary Wimbolt died soon after this Examinant was born and that her sd. late Father who was a Notary Public lived in a House the next Door to the Golden Key in Tenter Alley Little, Moor Fields in the Parish of St Giles Cripplegate without London where this Examinant was born as her late Uncle Thos. Fountain has often Informed her and whch. Information she verily believes to be true and give no further other Acct. of either her Father or this Examinant Settlemt. than as above set forth And this Examinant further Says that her sd. late Uncle has often Informed her that the sd. Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate  was this Examinants Parish & that her sd. late Uncle frequently Acquainted her that he went Apply to the Parish Officers to get her in to the Poor House belonging to that Parish And this Examinant lastly says that she has a Male Bastard Child born on her Body in Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and that the father thereof is in Goal for Debt.

Sworn this 11th. day of Decr. 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner Sampn Wright

her Elizth. Wimbolt  Mark
