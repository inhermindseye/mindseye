Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Wint  Singlewoman maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or either of them That She this Deponent has been informed by her late Mother deced & which She believes to be true that She was born in the Place or Court Called Kings head Court in Swan Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County That She never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did Any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 25 day of Novemr 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Eleanor Wint
