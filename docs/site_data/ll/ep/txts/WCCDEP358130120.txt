Middx.  Mary Chandler aged about 31 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with Robert Chandler (who dyed about thirteen Weeks ago) at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn London in the Month of July 1747. Says her said late Husband served the latter part of his Apprenticeship to wit four years with one Nicholas Thornley a Farrier in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and never after Rented a House of Ten Pounds a year pd. Parochial Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year as She has been informed by her said late Husband and verily believes

Sworn the 16th. Day of Febry. 1756 . before}
