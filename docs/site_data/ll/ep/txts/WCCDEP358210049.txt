Westmr.  Francis Moore aged about 45 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in Chapel Street in the Parish of St. Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and upwards, hath quitted said house about five Years and hath not since Rented the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 8th. day of May 1766 before} Paul Vaillant  Frans. Cary

Francis Moore
