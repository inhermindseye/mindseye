Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Litman aged about 32 Years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Grenville a Music Master in Devonshire Street in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Six Years since which time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinats knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Janry. 1798 before us R. Ford 

her Elizabeth Litman Mark

Witness H Day
