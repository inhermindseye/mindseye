Middx to wit Elizabeth Wiggs Aged about Thirty Three Years on her Oath says She Intermarried with her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn about Six Years ago And by whom She hath no Child or Children living says her said late Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Lindsey a Bricklayer in Vinegar Yard Bernaby Street in the Parish of Saint Clave Southwark in the County of Surry And after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 3rd. day of October 1780 before R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

her Elizth. Wiggs Mark
