Westmr. to wit William English Aged about Forty Three Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Margaret his Wife gone from him at the Parish Church of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of 20 Years ago and by whom he hath two Children Vizt. William Aged about Twenty Years (now provided for) and Margaret aged about Eight Years Says he served 5 Years Clerkship with one Mr. Newland an Attorney in Castle Yard in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn London and after he was out of his said Clerkship he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or hath ever done any other Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn before us this 4th. day of August Septr. 1772}   Tho: Treslove

Wm. English
