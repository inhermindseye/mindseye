Middlesex ss}  Ann Wornum  Singlewoman maketh oath that sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one William Hunt who kept the Sign of the Bell Alehouse in the parish of Chertsey in the County of Surry at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Sixteen Months & by means thereof as she is Advised gained a legal Settlement there That She this Depont. has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor hath She done any Act Since her Servitude as above to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th: day of May 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Wornum
