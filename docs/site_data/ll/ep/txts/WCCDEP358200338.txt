Westmr.  Dorothy Labourne aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of George Labourne who has been dead about five Years says her said late Husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Chas. Lilley  Perfumer the corner of Beaufort Buildings in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds he being all that time a single Man , says he quitted said Service about Thirty five Years ago and never Rented House or paid Taxes to this Examinants Knowledge or belief and that she hath not since his Death Rented house paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year

Sworn the 23d. day of Decr. 1765 , Before} Frans Cary

Jonatha Labourne
