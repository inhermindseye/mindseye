Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Elizabeth Douglass aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Clay Clare Street Clare Markett in the parish of Saint Cleme Danes in the County of Middx for 13 Months at the yearly Wages of £4 hath quitted said Service about Nine years ago since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. saith that she has two illegitimate Children living Vizt. Wm. aged about 7 years born in Westons Park in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields and Esther aged about 5 years who is bound Apprentice to Mr. Roche born in the same place

Sworn this 18th. day of Decr. 1786  Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth. Douglass mark
