Middlesex ss}  Mary Shurp  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with William Barith in Ireland yard in the parish of Saint Ann Black Fryars  in the City of London  Glass Frame makr at Certain wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one year and upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor hath She been ever married or done any act to her knowledge or belief to gain another Settlement

Sworn this. 17. day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Sharp
