Middx.  Ann Stone aged abt. 38 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Cornelius Stone who hath been dead abt. Four Years says she cannot give any Account of the Settlement of the said Cornelius Stone , says before her Intermarriage with the sd. Cornelius Stone she Intermarried with one John Edwards who informed her & which information she believes to be true that he served his Apprenticeship with his Father in Kirby Street near Hatton Garden in the Liberty of Saffron hill  Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the County of Middlesex

Sworn the 9th. day of March 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Anne Stone
