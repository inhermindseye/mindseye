Middx  Jane Bryon  Wido . of Robt. Bryon who hath been dead abt. 13 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late Husband about 15 Yrs. since at the Parish Church of St. Sepulchers London Says she can give no Acct. of her sd. late Husbands Selltemt. other than that he was born in Wales but in what part this Examinant knows not Says she lived a Yearly Servt . whilst a Singlewoman with Mr. Rd. Hewett  Cole merchant at the Corner of Turnagain Lane in the said Parish of St. Sepulchers London for the Spare of 3 Yrs. at the Yearly Wages of £4 and £4..10..0 & hath not since she left her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes hied a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 17th. March 1745 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Jane Bryon
