Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Wren  Widow of William Wren deced maketh oath that She has been informed by & her sd husband and which She believes to be true that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Barnes a Cornchendler in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of st Botolph- without Aldgate in the said County and Continued in Such Service under such hiring for near the space of three years and did not afterwards rent a house or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself & that she was lawfully married to said late husband

Sworn this 7. day of March 1787 before R D
