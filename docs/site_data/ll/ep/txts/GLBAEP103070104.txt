Middx ss The Examination of Rachael Peters  Singlewoman Taken on oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the County of Middx (whereof one is of the quorum) and both residing in or Near to the limits of the parish Church of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate in ye sd. County this 27th day of April in the year of our Lord 1754.

This Examinant upon her oath Saith that about four years and Seven Months ago one Samuel Stanley of the sd. parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate  Cooper who then lived in the house of this Deponents Mother in Nightingale Lane in the sd. Parish did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at her sd. Mothers house and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body Several times whereby She with Conceived with Child of a Male Bastard Child of which She was Delivered

at the house of her sd. Mother on the Seventeenth day of August one thousand seven hundred and forty Nine which sd. Male Bastard Child hath been baptised by the Name of John and hath been now is and Still is likely to be Chargeable to the sd. parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate this Examinant being Poor & not able to provide for the same And this Examinant farther saith that the sd. Samuel Stanley is the true and only father of the sd. Male Bastard Child and that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 27 day of April 1754 before  Richd Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

The mark of Rachell Peters
