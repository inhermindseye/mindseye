Middx  Eliza: Morris  Wife of Alexander Morris  removed from the parish of St. Benedict London by Order Dated the 18th. Day of September Instant upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Alexr. Morris at the Fleet London about 5 Years since & that her said Husband lived in & Rented a house in Horshoe Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds for the Space of Seven Years or thereabouts & hath left same upwds. of 4 Yrs. and hath not since he left sd. House rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says her sd. Husband hath for some time past absconded from her & she knows not where he now is

Sworne Septr. 20 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz. Morris
