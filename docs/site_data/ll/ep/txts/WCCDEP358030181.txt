Middx  Ann Cheney aged about 61 Years upon Oath says she was never married Says upwards of 40 Yrs ago she was bound an Apprentice to Mrs. Eliza. Powell in Bedford Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster Mantuamaker with whom this Exaiant lived almost one Year of her said Apprentiship Says the sd. Mrs. Powell absconded from her said house and where the sd. Mrs. Powell went to reside this Examinant knows not and further Says she never was assigned to any person for the remainder of her sd. Apprentiship lived with any other Person as a Yearly Servant or Apprentice rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 9th. July 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Ann Cheney
