Westmr. 

Mary Barker upon Oath saith she was never married Says her Father Samuel Barker deced Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with a Taylor in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & never rented ten pounds a year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examinant has been Informed & believes Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes as or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she was removed by Order of his Justies from St Botolph witht. Aldgate together with her Mother Mary Barker (since deced) abt. Ten Years ago to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt.

Sworn Febry 7th. 1746 before}
