Middlesex & Westminster} to Wit Mary Posson Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servt. for One Year Says her Father Richd. Posson lived a hired yearly Servt . before his Intermarried to her Mother Esther Posson and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servt. in the Capacity of a Coachman with Esqr. Heaton of Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upwards of 2 years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds as she believes And after quitting his sd. Service he never gained any Other Settlemt. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10 a year or Otherwise Howsoever and further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor unable to maintain the same when Born and that one Jas. Molloy a Journeyman to one Benjn. Watson a Nightman & Chimney Sweeper in Oxford Buildings Oxford Road is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her Grandmothers House in George Street Grosvenor Square sometime in the Month of July last but the Particular Days he does not now remember and at sevl. Difft. times afterwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Childn. of wch. she is now pregnant and that the sd. Jas Molloy & no. 5 Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day Novr. 1782 before R Butler

her Mary Posson Mark
