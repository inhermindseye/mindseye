Middx.  Elizth: Young aged abt. 56 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Samuel Young who has been dead about Nine years says her said late husband served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with Mr Stent a Carpenter in New Inn Passage in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx. and never Rented Ten pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 11th. day of August 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Young
