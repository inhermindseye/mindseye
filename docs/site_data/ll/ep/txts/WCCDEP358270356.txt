Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Kendrick aged about Nineteen years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Oddy in Bear yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Three Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Watson an Apprentice to the said Mr. Oddy is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of the Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Watson is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of March 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Mary Kendrick Mark

Father Apprehended the 3d. day of May 1798 Paid £10. Genl. Release Executed

Child born in the House the 18th. day of May 1798 named John

Disd. the house  14th. Augt. 1798 .

Qrs. wher. any and what Allowance to the Mother.
