Middx. ss and Westmr.}  Jane Simmins aged abt. 22 Years upon Oath says she never was Married And that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Aisley a Sadler in Holborn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx. for One year and a half at the yearly Wages of £5 and hath Quitted said Service abt. six Years and has not since lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 12th. day of October 1759 she was deliver'd of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Chamberlain a Carpenter in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the said County of Middx & Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized by the Name of John Says that one Edmund Pain a Waiter at the Grecian Coffee house in Devereux Court in the Strand had carnal knowledge of her body in the Month of Janry. 1759 and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid And that he the said Edmund pain and no other person is the true and real father thereof.

Sworn Augst. 16th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

Jane Simmins
