Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Humphrys Aged about 26 years upon her Oath Saith That on or about the 15th day of November 1791 she intermarried with Francis Humphries her present Husband at the Parish Church of Basing in the County of Hants by whom she hath living five Children Vizt. Mary and Elizabeth (twins) aged 8 years Margerett aged 57 years Francis aged 4 years and Thomas aged 20 Months That her said Husband Served his apprenticeship to Mr Murdock Carpenter Blackman Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster  that since he hath been out of his time he hath not rented any House tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief and Further this Deponent upon her Oath saith That her said Husband hath deserted her and That she is poor and unable to maintain herself and Family and hath become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 8th day of October 1800 before me } Benjn Gee

the Mark of Eliz Humphreys
