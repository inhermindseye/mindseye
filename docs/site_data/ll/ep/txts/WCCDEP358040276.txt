Middx 

Mary Norman upon Oath saith she never was married that she was born at Upper Deptford in the County of Street and never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. and that she lived with her mother Mary Norman for the Space of a Year and upwds in a house of Ten pounds a Year which her sd. mother rented in her Widow hood in Upper Deptford aforesd.

Sworn Novr 9d. 1748 before} John Green

The mark of Mary Norman
