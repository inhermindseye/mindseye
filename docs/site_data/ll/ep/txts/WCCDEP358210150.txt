Westmr.  Mary Bouley aged about 21 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Price a Pawnbroker in Hollis Street Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, quitted said Service about five Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year

Sworn the 10th, of April 1767 . before} 

The Mark of Mary Bouley
