Middx  Mary Niblett aged about 21 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married that her Father Danl. Niblett about 12 Yrs. since lived in & rented a House in Smithfeild near Cow Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchers London at the Yearly Rent of £20. & upwards for the Space of 17 Yrs. or thereabouts that she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 10th. May 1743 before} Samuel Clarke  Tho Lane

Mary Niblett
