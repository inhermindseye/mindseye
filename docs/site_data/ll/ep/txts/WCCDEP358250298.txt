Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Nevin aged about Forty four years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months with Mrs. Stanhope in Palsgrave Place in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Ten Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or Done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief She might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Ann Nevin Mark
