Middlesex ss}  Martha Butterworth the wife of  Charles Butterworth who is gone away from her to places unknown maketh oath that her said husband was born in the Parish of Rochdale in the County of Lancaster where his Father rented a house of Ten pounds a year & upwards & paid Parish taxes for the same as this Depont has been informed & believes & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and at which time this Deponents said husband gained a legal Settlement there as being part of his said Fathers Family & by means also of his being born there And this Deponent said husband hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent hath three Children namely Charles aged about eight years James aged about Six years & Mary aged about one year & half the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom she was lawfully married.

Sworn this 14th. day of October 1774 before B Camper

The Mark of Martha Butterworth
