Middx & Westmr.}  Hannah Wells aged about Nineteen years on her oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Robt. Smith of Lyons Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of a year at the yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted her said Master John Jones Service about two years ago since which time she hath done no Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born And further says that one Miles Jackson  Esqr . who lately lodged at one Mr. Sedgleys Taylor in Maiden Lane  Covent Garden & who she believes in now at Bath is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his Lodging at Briththelmstone sometime in the Month of July last but the Particular day she does not now remember & at sevl. difft. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Miles Jackson Esqr. & no or. Person did beget the same & that he in the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of Janry 1784 ,  Wm Kitchiner

Hannah Wells
