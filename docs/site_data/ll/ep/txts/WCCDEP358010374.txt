Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Maketh Oath and saith that William Bishop Evans a poor Boy of the age of 11 years or thereabouts now in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex is, as be this Deponent has been informed and verily believes the Son of William Bishopp Evans a Hairdresser who is deceased and that he was born in lawful Wedlock - and this Deponent hath also been informed-and which he believes to be true that the said William Bishop Evans the father in his lift time rented and lived in a House in Fleurde his Court in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London at the yearly West of twelve Guineas for about a year and an half and died there-and that the said William Bishop Evans the for never has acquired any Settlement in his over rise

Sworn this 20th. Day of Nor.1790 . before us  Edw: Read  Wm Hyde

Jas Talboys
