Middx  Frances Brown upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband George Brown upwds. of 11 yrs. since at Bloomsbury Church Middx and that her sd. husband whilst a Singleman lived a yearly Servant for one year and upwards with Mr Richardson in the parish of Enfield Middx at the yearly wages of £10 as she hath been Informed & believes & that her sd. husband has not Since he left his sd. Service Rented a house of Ten pounds a yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 26th. 1751 before  Richd. Chamberlayne

Frances Brown
