Westmr.  Phoebe Ind upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of Thomas Ind who at present lives in & Rents a house known by the Sign of the Crown & Scepter opposite the Coal Yard in Drury Lane in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex

Sworn 7th. Janry 1752 before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Phoebe Ind .
