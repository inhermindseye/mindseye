Westmr. to wit Margaret Dennis aged about forty Eight Years on behalf of her Husband Ambrose Dennis who now lies ill of the Gout on her Oath Says she Intermarried to the said Ambrose Dennis at the Parish Church of Saint Georges Hanover Square in the Year 1772 and by whom she hath no Child living; Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Cursitors Street Chancery Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn London for several Years at the Yearly Rent of fifteen pounds and paid parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Eight Years ago since which time he hath gained no other subsequent Settlement either by renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 23d day of March 1778 before  Thos Parker  John Croft

Margt. Dennis
