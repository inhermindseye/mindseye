Middx  Ann Chadwick aged abt. Forty Years upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido . of George Bignell Chadwick lately deceased & that her Sd. late Husbd. abt. one Year before his Death lived in & rented a House of Fourteen Pounds a Year in Plough Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of One Year & upwards that her Sd. late Husband never after nor this Examinant since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath one Child (to witt) Anne aged near Six Year & being Poor & Ill of a Feaver is not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworne the 15th. of May 1742 before} Jas: Gronous

Ann Chadwick
