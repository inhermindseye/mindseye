St Clement Danes April 14th. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  Elizabeth Jenner Aged 50 years or thereabouts Widow of Willm. Jenner Maketh Oath & saith that she was Married to her late present Husband at the Parish Church of St Pancras in about the Month of April 1792 about 21 years ago, that after their said Marriage her Husband Rented and with this Deponent Resided in an House in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty for about two or three Years at the Annual Rent of £20 or thereabouts that they quitted that House about 12 years ago and never he Died about two years never having obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief neither hath this Deponent since her Decease acquired any Settlement in her own Right.

Sworn the 14th. day of April 1792 before me} 

Elizth. Jenner 's Mark.
