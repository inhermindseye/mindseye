Westmr.  James Nowland Aged about Sixty Three Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his late Wife in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr about 29 Years Ago Says he Rented and lived in a House in Bear Yard known by the Sign of the Lyon and Lamb in the Parish of St. Clemnet Danes in the said Liberty of Westmr upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Twenty five Years ago and after he quitted the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1769 before me} Tho Fellows

James Nowland
