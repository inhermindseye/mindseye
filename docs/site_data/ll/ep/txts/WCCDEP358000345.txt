Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Jane Christopher aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Dr. Eglinton who lodged at Mr. Cornwell Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 3 years & an half at the yearly Rent of Five Pounds hath quitted said Service about 20 years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing Whereby to the best of this Exts knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Settlement than as assd.

Sworn this 23d. day of Augt. 1786  Wm Kitchiner

her Jane Christopher Mark
