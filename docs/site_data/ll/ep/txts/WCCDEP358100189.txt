Middx  Catherine Menley Aged about Thirty two years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Radcliffe one of the Porters to his Majesty in Half Moon Street  Piccadilly in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for one year & Ten Months at the yearly Wages of £6 hath quitted her said Service about 12 years ago since which time she hath never lived as a hired yearly Servt: for one year or or.wise gained any or. Settlement

Sworn this 27th.. day of Febry. 1784  Sampn Wright Thos: Gilbert .

Catherin Meanley
