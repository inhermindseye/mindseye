Middx  Janry 22d.1750

Ann Brown upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Edwd. Champ Cook of Morden Colledge for Eighteen months or thereabts. at the Yearly Wages of 40s and that she lived the last three months of the sd. time with her sd. master in the Parish of St. Peter in the East in Oxford and has note since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the Day & Year abovemention'd before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Brown
