Westmr.  Jane Knight aged about 40 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 16 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Saml. Withers in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds £5..10..0 for three Years & upwards & has not since she quitted her sd. Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1749 before} Gabriel Fouace
