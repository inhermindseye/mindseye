Middlesex ss}  Jane Holmes the widow of Richard Holmes deced maketh oath that Since her Said late husbands death She hath been hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by & with Jonathan Marriott in  Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & half & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year elsewhere nor hath She been Since married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 9. day of October 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Jane Holmes
