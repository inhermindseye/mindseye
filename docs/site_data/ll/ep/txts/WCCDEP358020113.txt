Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Lewis aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith That about 14 years ago she Intermard. with John Lewis her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said Husband Rented Part of a House in Hog Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Three Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of £17 that he hath quitted said House about upwards of 15 years ago since which time he has not rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this First day of Decr. 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

her Mary Lewis Mark
