Middx  Robt Waters Aged about 60 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Elizth . his Wife at the Parish Church of Shoreditch about Nine years ago and by whom he hath one Child now living (vizt) Robert aged upwards of Seven years Says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven years to one John Edmunds a Button maker the Corner of Eagle & Child Alley in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn  London & served five Years part of his Apprenticeship and untill his said Masters Death & further says he never was assigned over to any or. Master nor ever gained any or. Settlemt. Renting House of £9. a year but paid no Poor Rates or Land Tax as he remembers

Sworn this 2d. day of March 1784  Wm Kitchiner  Edward Hodsoll

his Robt Waters Mark
