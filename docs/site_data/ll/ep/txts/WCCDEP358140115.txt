Middx  James Dougherty a Soldier in the third Regmt. of Foot Guards upon Oath says he intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Fleet London on the 5th. day of May 1748. says that both he this Examinant and his said Wife are-Natives of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England, says he has two Children by his said Wife namely George aged upwards of Eight Years born in the house of Paul Mitchell in St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and Jane aged upwards of one Year born in the house of Margaret Cook in Little Shire Lane in the Parish aforesaid

Sworn the 8th. of May 1758 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of James Dougherty
