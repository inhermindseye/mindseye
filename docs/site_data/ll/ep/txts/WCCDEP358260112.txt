Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Rebecca Richards on behalf of Isabella Chidlow an Infant aged about Five years and Three Months maketh Oath and Saith that the said Isabella Chidlow is the illigitimate Child of one Mary Chidlow and was born at the house of a Mrs. Gale No. 15 George Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn this 3rd. day of March 1796 } M Addington Rusl: Clarke
