Westmr.  Jane Nelson aged about 23 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Moffatt a Butcher in the Town of Sunderland in the County of Durham for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds and quitted her said Service in the Month of August 1765. And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. (where she now lives as a Servant with one Mr. Dent a Taylor) she being poor & Unable to Maintain the same when born, says that Wm: Hackworth who is Master of a Vessell in the Coal Trade called the Isabella had carnal knowledge of her Body on Board the said Vessell several times in the Month of September 1765, at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said Wm: Hackworth and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 20th. of Feby. 1766 before} Paul Vaillant

Jane Nelson
