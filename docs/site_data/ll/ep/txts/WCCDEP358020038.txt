Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Benjn. Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been Credibly informed which Information he verily believes to be true that George Clegg an Infant of the Age of 14 years or thereabouts was placed out Apprentice on the 22d. May 1786 by the Overseers of St. Clement Danes by Indres duly executed (till he should Attain the Age of 21 years) to on Wm. Aycrigg a Baker in Lilly Street in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden and Ely Rents that he served about 5 Months of his said Apprenticeship to his said Master then his said Master ran away and left him Destitute that the last 40 days of his said Service with his said Master was in Lilly Street in the Liberty aforesaid

Sworn before us this 29th. day of March 1787  Thos: Gordon M Addington

Benj: Newman
