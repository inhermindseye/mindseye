Middx & West} to wit Ann Franklin Aged upwards of Eighteen years on her Oath says she was never Married Rented House paid T or served any Apprenticeship, says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Patrick Purcell at the Marquis of Granbys Head in Bennett Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of four Pounds hath Quitted her said service upwards of Three years ago, and hath not since Quitting the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived as hired yearly servant for one year (save living with her said Master Eleven Months for which he paid her a years Wages) or done any Act whereby to Gain any any other subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 16th day of September instant she was delivered of a female Bastard Child (which is intended to Christened Ann ) at the House of Mr Michael Cochran in Bennett Court aforesaid which is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same and that her said Master Patrick Purcell is the father there of he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in his own house sometime in Novr last but the particular day she does not now remember and at several different times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Patrick Purcell and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 29th day of Sepr 1775 before R. Butler

her Ann Franklin Mark
