Middx ss  Susannah Cox Aged about Twenty Six years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Richard Down  Esqr a Banker in Thread needle Street who soon afterwards removed and lived on Fish Street Hill , afterwards in Lombard Street and last in Bartholomew Lane in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew near the Royal Exchange where this Examinant lived the last Six Months in all about three years hath quitted her said service about Seven Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 19th day of August last she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the House of Mr Warmington in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which has since been Baptized Ann and that one Thomas Wakeman a Servant to her said late Master is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Master house about the Middle of November last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and one other time afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Thomas Wakeman and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 11th day of Octr 1775 before R. Butler Wm. Kitchiner

Susannah Cox
