Middlesex ss}  Mary Kemp  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years Ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mrs Sarah Harrison a Mantua maker in the Parish of Saint Mildred in the Poultry in the City of London & Continued in such service for divers Years That She has not lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 3. day of December 1785 before R Davies Robt Smith

The Mark of Mary Kemp
