Middx  Sarah Cornelius aged 69 years upon Oath Saith she is the wido . of Wm. Cornelius who dyed abt. 28 Years ago and that abt. 11 Years since (having at the time only one son (viz) Michael aged abt. Twenty two years who had gained a distinct Settlemt. by having Served Seven Years apprentiship) she was hired as a Yearly Servant to Mr March in Hollis Strt. in the Parish of St Clement Danes at the yearly Wages of £5 and lived with him there under the sd hiring and Agreemt. near two Years and that she has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Febry 1st 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Sarah Cornelius
