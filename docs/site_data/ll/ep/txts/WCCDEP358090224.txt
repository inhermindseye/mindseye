Middx to wit Elizabeth Tuder Aged about Twenty One years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for One year says her Father was a Welch Man but of what Parish or Place she does not know neither was she ever Informed thereof says she has been Informed and which she believes to be true that she was born at Llangothlin in Denbeighshire north Wales and that she has gained no other settlement then by Birth to her knowledge or belief And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born and that One John Ross a servant to Nicholas Scottow  Esqr . No. 42 in Borner Street  near the Middlesex Hospital is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of October last but the particular day she does not now remember at which time he got her with child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Ross and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of May 1781 before R Butler

her Elizth. Tuder  Mark
