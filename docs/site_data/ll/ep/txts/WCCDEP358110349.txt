Middx.  Ann Eamondson upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Rt. Eamondson by whom she has two Children Robt. aged 8 Yrs. & Henriette aged 7 Yrs. & that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband was born at Leeds in the County of York & she knows nothing further of his Settlemt.

Sworn 17th Novr. 1753 before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of ann Eamondson
