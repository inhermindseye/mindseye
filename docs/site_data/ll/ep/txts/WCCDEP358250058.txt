Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D (to wit) Ann Harding Widow of Thomas Harding Deceased aged about 33 years Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Thomas Harding at the parish Church of Saint Margaret Westminster about Sixteen years ago by whom she had four legitimate Children who are all now Dead that her said Husband previous to his  Marriage with this Deponent he served an Apprenticeship of Seven years with Mr Warrington in Devereux Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County and lodged during such Service in his said Marten House as he hath informed this Deponent which she believes to be true, and that since the Expiration of his Apprenticeship he never obtained any subsequent legal Settlement, That her said Husband Died about four years ago And that she hath never since then been Married or acquired any legal Settlement in her own Right

Sworn the 20th. day of July 1792 before me} Wm. Kitchiner

Ann Harding 's Mark
