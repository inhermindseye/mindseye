Westminster to wit Dorothy Hawkins aged about Thirty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she was bound an Apprentice to Mrs. Mary Jenkins a Milliner in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Country of Middlesex for five Years and after she was out of her said Apprenticeship she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year and further Upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Sd. Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born says that one William Hart a Journeyman Taylor who Uses the Earl of Stairs's head in Chandos Street  Covent Garden had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her own Lodgings at one Mrs. Smiths in Great St. Andrew Street  Seven Dials sometime in May last and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as aforesaid and that the said William Hart and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of December 1771 before me W Kelyngs

Dorothy Hawkins
