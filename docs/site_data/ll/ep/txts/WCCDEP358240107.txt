St. Clement Danes May 18th 1792

Middx & Westmr}  St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars Middx  to wit Jno Strickland Singleman aged 36 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he never was Married That he was bound an Apprentice for seven years by Indenture to Mr Dickonson and served his said Master for 5 Years Stations that he this Deponent during the first Year of his Apprenticeship his Slept in his Master's Shop at then situate in Holborn Next to Grays Inn Gate in Holborn in the parish of St Andrew Holborn above The Bars in the County of Middx. That That afterwards at about the Expiration of that year his said Masters took Chambers in Grays Inn where this Deponent Slept during the Remainder of his Service, That at the End of the said 3 Years he left his Master by consent and hath done no Act or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any other Settlement than in the said parish of St Andrew Holborn as aforesaid

Sworn the 18th day of May 1792 before us H. Dive Wm Hyde

John Strickland
