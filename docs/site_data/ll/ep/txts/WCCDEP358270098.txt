Tobiathar Cridland and Mary Ann Wall after Birth

St. Clement Danes Febry. 6th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman's Settlemt St Mary Magdalen Taunton  Child's birth  Reputed Father  Tobiathar Cridland aged 23 years & upwards singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married  that she never she never served as an Apprenticeship nor lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve month together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or did any other act Mre or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own Right that she has been informed and verily believes that she is the legitimate Daughter of Samuel and Elizabeth Cridland both  deceased that her said Father Samuel Cridland served his Apprenticeship with in the parish of Saint Mary Magdalen in Taunton in the County of Somerset and never after acquired any subsequent Settlement during his Life and that died about 10 years ago and that and this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that on or about the second day of January last at the House of Mr. Lucas at the Corner of Vere and Duke Streets in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath since been named Mary Ann Wall and is become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes and that Richard Wall  of No 11 friday

a Clerk to who acts as Clerk to Messr  Ayres acton Cotton Warehouseman No. 11 Friday Street Cheapside London is the only real and true father of the said Child of which this Examinant was delivered as aforesaid he and no one else having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body sometime on or about the 15th. day of April 1792 and at Several times since and at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child thereon and that he and no other person is the only real true father thereof

Sworn the 6th. day of February 1793  before before me} Wm Kitchiner

Cridland

Mr Wall in abt 5 Feet 9 Inches High then Man Dark Complexion Hair & Dyes to be found as above or at Mr Acton's Bartholomew Clore

taken & paid £25 to Overseer (Mr Rosier) & £1:3:6 Fees
