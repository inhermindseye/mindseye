Middx to wit Christopher Cheesewright Aged about 27 Years on his Oath Says he never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Wm. Crowe  Esqr . in the Parish of St. Giles's in the City of Norwich for Three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Fourteen Pounds besides diet & Lodging hath Quitted his said Service about Five Years Ago since which time he never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 26 day of May 1784 before R Butler

Christopher Cheswright
