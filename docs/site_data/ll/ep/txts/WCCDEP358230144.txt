Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Frances King Aged about 40 years upon her Oath Saith That about 18 years ago she intermarried with James King her late Husband at the Parish Church of Christ Church That her said late Husband served his Apprenticeship to a Mr Sellers in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry That since he hath been out of his time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 17th day of July 1800 before us} John Collick  Benjm. Gee

Frances King
