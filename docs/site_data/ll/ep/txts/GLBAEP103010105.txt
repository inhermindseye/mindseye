Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Sparrow  Singlewoman maketh Oath that she has heard her Father Robert Sparrow (who is now living at Stratford in the Parish of Westham in the County of Essex ) say that he was duly Bound Apprentice by Indenture to Mr. Preston of Stratford aforesaid Callico Printer for the Term of Seven Years & served the whole of his said Apprenticeship with his said Master there, and that she believes he has since Rented a House of Ten Pounds on Year or upwards in the said Parish, that she this Deponent was Born in the said Parish and has never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any Parish or Place for the space of One Year together or Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement in her own right And further saith that about Three Years ago she was in the Workhouse of the said Parish about Three Months

Sworn this 15th Day of Septr. 1792 before}
