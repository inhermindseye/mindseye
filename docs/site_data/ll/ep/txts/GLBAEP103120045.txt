Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Mills the Wife of  Samuel Mills maketh oath That she well knows a young Woman named Grace Gilbert the Daughter of John Gilbert  Woolcomber who is now resident in the Isle of Guernsey & which Said Grace Gilbert is at Present a Lunatick so far disordered in her Senses that it may be dangerous to permit her to go abroad disordered & likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County of Middlesex and this Deponent Saith that divers years ago the Said John Gilbert lived in & rented a house of the yearly Rent & Value of Ten Pounds in the Parish of the holy Trinity in the City of Exeter in the County of Devon & Continued in the occupation of such house for Many years & by Means thereof as this Depont is advised he gained a legal settlement there and to this Deponts. knowledge or belief he hath not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement That the Said Grace Gilbert at such time was part of her Said Fathers Family and that to this Deponents knowledge or belief the Said Grace Gilbert never did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 10th. day of May 1773 before}

Eliz Milles
