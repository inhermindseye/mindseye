Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  Passd. to St. Margaretts Westmr. to wit Lucey Wheatley aged about 27 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Henry Wm. Wheatley at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about 2 years ago that her Husband Rented a Shop and Room at the Rent of 4s.8d. Per week in Westminster Markett in the Parish of St. Margaretts Westminster in the Liberty of Westminster that they Rented same for four Months hath quitted said Tenements about two Months ago that since they left those Tenements her Husband has deserted her since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 9th. day of Augt. 1785 before} Addington J Reading

Lucey Wheatley
