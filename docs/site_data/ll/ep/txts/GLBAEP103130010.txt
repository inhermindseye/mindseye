Middlesex ss}  Sarah Greenwood the wife of  John Greenwood maketh oath that about Twenty Seven years ago She was Married to her late husband William Duke  Mariner deced that she this Deponent can give no Account respecting the Place where he was legally settled at the time of his death that she this Deponent divers years ago and before her marriage with her late husband William Duke was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages with one Thomas Swetman in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex and Continued in such Services for the Space of Seven Years and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Deponent has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement save what she has acquired by Virtue of her Marriages with her former & present husbands that she this Deponent hath a Daughter now living namely Sarah Duke aged about Twenty four years by her said late husband Wm Duke who hath not done any act to this Deponent knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 11th day of September 1773 before Jno Sherwood

Sarah Greenwood
