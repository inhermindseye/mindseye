Middsex ss}  Jane Langley the widow of George Langley deced maketh oath that she has been informd by her said late husband & which she believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice & served the whole thereof to & with Mr Winter a Boat Builder in the parish of St Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry where her Sd husband also rented a house of the yearly rent of four pounds & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same to Mr Petty the then Collector of such Tax about nineteen or twenty years ago That this Deponts Sd husband did not to her Knowledge or belief afterwards rent a house of the yearly rent or Value of ten pounds or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors rates or King Taxes or do any act to gain Settlement elsewhere nor hath she this Deponent since his death been married or lived as a hired Servant for a year or done any act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 14 day of october 1785 before J. Hawley R Davies

The Mark of Jane Langley
