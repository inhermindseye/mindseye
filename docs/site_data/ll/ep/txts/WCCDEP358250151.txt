Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Swaine aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith That she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Cary a Haberdasher in the Strand in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields for Three years and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about Seven Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of March 1795 } M Addington Edw: Read

Mary Swaine
