Westr to wit  Elizth Keet Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or Served Apprenticeship & further saith she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Toft at the three Jolly Butchers in Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for Seven Months at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds and that Afterwards her said Master Quitted the House and that One John Jossy taking it she Continued to live under the same hiring & the same Wages for 8 Months making in the whole fifteen Months or thereabouts and that since that time she has never Rented House paid Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant or done any other other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th day of Feby 1775 before W Addington

Eliz Keet
