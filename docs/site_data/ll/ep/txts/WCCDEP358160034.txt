Middx  The Examination of Jno. Trower aged 23 Years Passed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . Taken the 12th. day of Febry. 1738 Who upon his Oath saith that about Three Years ago he lived with one Mr. Foisseau Chaser in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for ye. Space of one Year & upwds. ye latter part of his Apprentishipp And this Examint. further Saith that he hath not since he left his said Master Rented any House paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife & that he is now Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine himselfe.

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} C. Egerton

John Trower
