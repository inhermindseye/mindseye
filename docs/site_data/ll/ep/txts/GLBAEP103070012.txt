Middx ss  Mary Slackman  Singlewoman Maketh oath that her Father Isaac Slackman hath often informed her that he duly Served his apprenticeship with one Mr Burges a Cordwainer in the parish of Allhallows the Great in the City of London and by Means thereof Acquired a legal Settemt there Which information this depont. verily believes to be true And this Depont. Saith that in right of her sd. father that her legal place of Settlement is in ye Same parish she this depont. having never been Married nor been an apprentice nor lived as a hired Servant by the Year Nor done any Act Whatsoever Whereby to Acquire any Settlemt. in her own right.

Sworn this 16 day of October 1750 before us  Richd Ricards  Saml Gower

The mk of Mary Slackman
