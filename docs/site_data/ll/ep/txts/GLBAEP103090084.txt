Middlesex ss}  John Simpson a Singleman maketh oath that divers years ago he lived in a house of his own in the Parish of Saint Peter in Droitwich in the County of Worcester and where he was Charged to and paid Parochial Taxes and served the office of overseer of the Poor and Carried on the Business of a Cordwainer And by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Deponent Saith that to his Knowledge or belief he hath not since done any Act whereby to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 13th day of November 1767 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of John Simpson
