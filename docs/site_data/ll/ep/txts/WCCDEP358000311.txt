Saint Clement Danes July 1st- 1786

Middlesex (to wit) Sarah Wyatt aged forty Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married, saith she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Noon at the Robinhood in Butcher Kersin in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of six pounds Sixteen Shillings and six pence for upwards of one Year, that she quitted the same about fourteen Years ago and since then has done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained any settlement other than as above stated

Sworn this 1st July 1786 before R Butler

her Sarah Wyatt Mark
