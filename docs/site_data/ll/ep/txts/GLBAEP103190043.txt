Middlesex-ss} & Liberty of the Tower of London} The Examination of Elizabeth Hunt of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken upon oath before us Jasper Clarke & William Blackmore  Esquires two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County & Liberty this Twenty ninth day of March-one thousand seven hundred and eighty

Who Saith That on the fifteenth day of March instant at the Workhouse belonging to and Situate in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County she the said Elizabeth Hunt was delivered of a Male Bastard Child And that the Said Bastard Child hath been now is and still is likely to be further Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that George Burch of Princes Street Westminster in the Said County Gentn did beget the said Bastard Child on the Body of her the said Elizabeth Hunt

Taken and Signed the day and Year abovewritten before us  Jasper Clarke W Blackmore

The Mark of Elizabeth Hunt
