Westmr. to wit Mary Ainell aged about 18 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs- Davenall in Church Street in the Parish of Saint Annes Soho in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds ten Shillgs hath quitted that Service upwards of two Years ago since which time she has gained no other subsequent Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one one Year

Sworn this 16th- Day of Decr- 1777 before} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Ainell
