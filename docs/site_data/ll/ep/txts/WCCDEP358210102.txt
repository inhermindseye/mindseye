Westmr.  Thomas Rayner one of the Beadles in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster on behalf of Mary Wolf  Widow of John Wolf who has been dead near twenty Years as this Deponent hath been informed, Upon Oath says he has made diligent Enquiry concerning the Settlement of the said Mary Wolf and has been-informed and which information he verily believes to be true that the said Mary Wolf lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Ferdinandes a Merchant for one Year and upwards and lived the last Six Months of her said Service with the said Mr. Ferdinandes in Savage Gardens in the Parish of St. Olave in Hart Street  Crutched friars in the City of London she being at that time a Widow And hath not since done any Act whereby to gain a Settlement to this Deponents knowledge or belief

Sworn the 15th. day of November 1766 , before} Tho Kynaston W Kelynge

Thos: Rayner
