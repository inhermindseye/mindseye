Westmr. ss  Hannah Hart Aged about 64 years on her Oath says she is the Widow of George Hart who has been dead about 11 Years says her said Husband was born in the Parish of St. Pauls Shadwell in the County of Middlesex as her said Husband h often Informed her Etc which Information she verily believes to be true And further says that her said Husband never rented house Paid Taxes was an apprentice or Lived a Hired yearly servant for one year to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 26th Day of October before us R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Hannah Hart mark
