Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  (to wit) Margaret Walker aged 76 years and upwards or thereabouts Upon her Oath Saith That  about 48 years ago she intermarried with John Kakewit who has been Dead about 33  years That 2 years after the said John Kakewit s Death she intermarried with Thomas Walker who has been Dead  about 25 year That the said John Kakewit her first Husband informed this Examinant that he Served his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Mills a Buckle Master in White cross street in the parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate in the County of Middlesex , And this Examinant further upon her Oath Saith That the said Thos. Walker her Second Husband never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of 10 pounds a year or did any Act whereby he might have gained any sort of Settlement and that the said John Kakewit never gained any other Settlement but by serving his Apprentice with the said Mr. Mills in White cross street in the parish and County aforesaid To the best of this Examinants Knowledge information or belief

Sworn this 17th day of Novr. before me Thos. Gordon Wm. Hyde

her Margaret Walker Mark
