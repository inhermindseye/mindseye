Middx and Westmr}   Sarah Croll aged about 20 Years upon Oath says she is now a hired Yearly Servant to Mr. Coles Mathematical Instrument maker the back of St. Clements in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex and has lived with him as such upwards of one year at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she never was Married and that she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when Born And that one John Kauffman a Jeweller did beget such Child or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body on the 22d. day of September 1761 and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 17th.. day of February 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Croll
