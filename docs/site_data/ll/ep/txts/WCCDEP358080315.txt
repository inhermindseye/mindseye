Middx to wit Margaret Brown aged about 30 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. White a Ribbon Weaver in Addle Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Aldermanbury in the City of London upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 23rd. day of Novr. 1778 before J Welch Wm. Kitchiner

her Margaret Brown Mark
