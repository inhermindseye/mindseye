Middx.  Rose Hall aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath says she never was Married, says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr John Ford a Grocer in Jermyn Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for One Year at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds and hath quitted same abt. Eleven Months; since which time she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1759 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Rose Hall

Upon the Complaint of Mr Rust Ovr.
