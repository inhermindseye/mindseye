Westmr.  Elizabeth Peaton Aged about Forty two Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Edward Peaton who hath been dead about Twenty one Years, Says the said Edwd. Peaton before his Intermarriage with this Examinant, and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with this Examinants Father James Lyddall a Taylor at the White Horse in White Horse Yard Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Fifteen Pounds, Diet, Washing and Lodging and after he quitted said Service, he never Rented Ten Pounds a year, or paid Parish Taxes, Says she hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 6th. day of February 1768 , Before W Kelynge

Elizabeth Peaton
