Middx  Eliz: Feild upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido . of Robt. Feild who hath been dead about Three Years that her said late Husband about 30 Years ago lived in & rented a House in Lower Holloway in the Parish of St. Mary at Islington in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds for the Space of Three Years that since he left the said House her said late Husband in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 15th- Janry 1742 before} Wm: Miller

The Mark of Eliz: Feild
