Middx  Elizth. Baldwin aged abt. 23 Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Wm. Baldwin who hath been dead abt. Three Months, Says her sd. late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst a Singleman lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Osborne at Serle's Coffee House in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx upwds. of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & that he quitted his said Service abt. four Years before his Death Says her sd. late Husband in his life time nor She this Examinant since his death never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes Says She hath one Child by her sd. late Husband (to wit) Elizth. aged abt. 8 Months & being Poor is not able to Maintain her sd. Child

Sworn Janry 4th. 1757 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

Eliz Baldwin
