Middx to wit Sarah Tall aged about Thirty Nine Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of John Tall (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone about Nine Years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living Namely John Aged about Eight Years Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Lord Darnley in the Capacity of his Gentleman in Berkley Square in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for fourteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Thirty five Pounds besides Diet & Lodging hath Quitted his said Service about Nine Years ago since which time her said Husband never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 3rd- day of Apr 1779 before R Butler J Welch

Sarah Tall
