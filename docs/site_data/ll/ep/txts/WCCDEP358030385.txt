Westmr. 

Thos. Payne upon Oath saith he inter- -married with his Wife Letitia Payne at the fleet London upwards of 30 Years ago Says he lived in a house of his own of the Yearly Value of £20 or thereabts. in Blackmor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for the Space of Eight Months & upwards and left the same about 20 Yrs. since Says he has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 5th: 1747 before  Wm: Jones

Thomas Paine
