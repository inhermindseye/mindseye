Middx  Susannah Clayton Aged about 40 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married And that about 2 Yrs. since she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Freeman at the Crooked Billett & Macheaths Head in Hollis Street Clare markett in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £4 for the Space of 4 Years and upwards Says she hath not since she left her sd. Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 21st: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Susa. Clayton
