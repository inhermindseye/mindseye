Middx  Eliz: Cox upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & yt. upwards of 1 Year ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with one Mr. Dickerson in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of one Yr. & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & that she hath not since she left her sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which when born will be a Bastard or

Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one Robt. Gilder who is now abroad & beyond the Seas did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body she at such Time liveing a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Dickerson Mother to the sd. Mr. Dickerson in Theobalds Court in the Parish of St. Andrw- Holbourn in the sd. County of Middx where the sd. Robert Gilder became acquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body twice in or abt. the Month of December last at one of which Times he he got her with the sd. Bastard Child with which she is now pregnant & that he & no other Person is the True & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 25th. of Augt. 1741 before}
