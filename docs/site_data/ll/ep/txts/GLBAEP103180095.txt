Middlesex ss}  Richard Doowsen maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indres to Charles Gunn & Ann Gunn of Chelmsford in the County of Essex  Bruke makers for the Term of Seven years Where this Deponent Served four years of such his Apprenticeship & then he was Assigned or Turned over to George Stark of  Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County of Middlesex  Perukemaker with whom he Served the Remainder of his Said Apprentice there lodging allways in his Said Masters House That he has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year or rented a house or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 25 day of November 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Richard Doowsen
