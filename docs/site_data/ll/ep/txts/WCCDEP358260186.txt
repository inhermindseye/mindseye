Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Sturch one of the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of James Mills who is at present in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and Saith That he this Deponent hath been informed by Mr. James Scott the Vestry Clerk of the Parish of Woolwich That it appears from the Parish Books of the said Parish of Woolwich That the said James Mills Rented a House of Ten Pounds a year from Lady day 1774 to Michaelmas 1786 in Surgeons Street in the Parish of Woolwich in the County of Kent and that the said James Mills hath been Relieved by the Officers of the Parish of Woolwich and hath been more than Once in their Workhouse from which he Eloped a few weeks since

Sworn this 25th. day of Febry. 1797 before us R. Ford J Robinson Wm. Sturch
