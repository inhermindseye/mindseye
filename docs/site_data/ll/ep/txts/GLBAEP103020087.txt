Middlesex ss.  Roger Ratcliff maketh Oath that about Fourteen Years ago he Rented a House of Twelve Pounds a Year in the Broadway in the Parish of St. Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry & lived in the same about one Year that he afterwards Rented a House of Nine Pounds a Year in the said Parish where he lived some Years & was charged to and paid the Poors Rate for both the said Houses, that he has not since he quitted the said last mentioned House, Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his Knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he has a Wife named Ann to whom he was lawfully Married at the Parish Church of St. George Southwark in the County of Surry some time in the Month of September 1776 and Two Children Namely John Charles Ratcliff aged upwards of Thirteen Years who is now in the Charity School of the said Parish of St. Mary Rotherhithe , and Ann aged about Eight Years the lawful issue by his said Wife

Sworn this Day of 1794 before}
