Middx  Eliza. Atkinson aged abt. 32 Years upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her Husband Edwd. Atkinson on the 4th. Day of August 1735 at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband is now on Board the Hercules Privateer Says he lived in & rented a house in Kings head Court in Drury Lane in ye. Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £20 for one Year & upwards & hath left same upwds. of Eight Years Says her sd. husband hath not since he left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 5th. Novr. 1745 before Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz: Atkinson
