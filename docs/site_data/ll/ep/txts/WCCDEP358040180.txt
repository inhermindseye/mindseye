Westmr.  Eliz Richardson removed from the Parish of St. Martin in the feilds in the Liberty of Westminster by Order dated the 6th. Instant upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Richd. Richardson who hath been dead upwards of Seven Years and that about two Months ago she (this Examinant) lived a Yearly Servant wth. Mr. John Radford  Toyman in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for three Years & upwards at the Year by Wages of Four pounds and hath left her sd. Service abt. 15 Months Says she has not since she Quitted the sd. Mr. Radfords Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 17th 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz Richardson
