Middx.  Elizth. Currll aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath says She never was married says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with the Widow Norbury in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds ten Shillings & hath quitted her said Service abt. three Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant

Sworn this 17th. day of Septr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Currll
