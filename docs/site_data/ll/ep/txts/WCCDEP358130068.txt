Middx  Rosey King aged about 31 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with Peter Dalton an Irishman sixteen Years ago or thereabouts That he Rented a House in Kings Head Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & lived therein above two Years about twelve Years since Says that the said Peter Dalton dyed about Eight Years since after which She intermarried with Barney King (who is gone from her) at the Fleet London Says he is an Irishman & an out Pensioner of Chelsea College and that he Rented a House of Ten Pounds a year in Bennets Court never gained any Settlement in England to her knowledge & belief in the Parish of St. Clement Danes as she hath been informed by him & believes

Sworn the 11th. Day of Octor. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Rosey King
