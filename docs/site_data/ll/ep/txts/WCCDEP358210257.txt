Westmr.  John Bund Aged upwards of Thirty five Years Upon Oath says, that about Six Years ago he Rented a House (known by the Name of the spotted Dog )in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for a Quarter of a Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty six Pounds, and after he quitted same never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 24th. day of March 1768 , Before W Kelynge

John Bund
