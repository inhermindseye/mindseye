Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Westfield aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that on the 23rd. day of April 1776 she Intermarried with John Westfield her present Husband (who has deserted her) at the Parish Church of Saint George Bloomsbury that she hath Two Children living by her said Husband Vizt. John aged about Nine years and William aged about Eight years That her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Charge Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Nine Months at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas that he hath quitted said house only about  a Week And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted her and refuses to Allow her or her said Children any Support.

Sworn this 2d. day of Augt. 1790 R Butler

 Mary Westfield
