Middx  Noah Flood aged abt. 54 years upon Oath says he is the Father in Law of Susanh: Flood who intermarried with this Examinant Son  Noah Flood in the Parish Church of Hornsey in the County of Middx abt six years Since According to his best remembrance and belief and that this Sd Son Noah Flood has been dead near three Months that his Said Son Noah Served two years or thereabts: the la part of his Apprenticeship with Mr Jno Farmer a rule maker in the Sd. Parish of Hornsey that her never rented a house or paid Taxes that his said Daur in Law  Susannah Flood now lyes Dangers Ill as he has been informed & believes that She hath three Children by his Sd Son now liveing to wit Noah Aged abt 5 Years Sarah abt 3 Years and Mary Abt Seven Months

Sworn June 14th:1762 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Noah Flood
