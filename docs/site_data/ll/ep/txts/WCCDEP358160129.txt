Middx The Examination of Margt. Munn  Widw . of Vere Munn Taken the 22d. Day of Aprill 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. abt. 3 Years ago her late Husband Vere Munn lived in & rented a House of £20 a Year in Eagle Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above Barrs in the County of Middx & Continued in the same for the space of Six Weeks or thereabouts And this Examint. further saith that her sd. Husband never after he left the sd. House Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That she hath two small Childn. (to wit) Vere aged three Years & Sarah aged one Year & upwds. & being Poor is not able to maintaine them.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Chas: Egerton

The Mark M of Margt. Munn
