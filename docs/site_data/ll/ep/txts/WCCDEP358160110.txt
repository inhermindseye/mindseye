Middx  The Exaicon of Henry Parry Aged 45 Years Passed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields by Order dated the 22d. day of Decr. last taken the 15th. day of Febry 1739 who upon his Oath Saith that he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Wm. Bunsted  Esqr . ino Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of one Yeare & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas & hath been come away from his sd. Service abt. Eleven Year he this Exaiant at that time being a Singleman without Child or Childn. And further Saith that he never Rented any House or since that time lived with any Person for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her his knowledge or beleife

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane

The mark of Henry Parry
