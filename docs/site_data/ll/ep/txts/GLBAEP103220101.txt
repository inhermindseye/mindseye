Middlesex ss}  Jacob Welfar maketh oath that he is by birth a Hanoverian That he this Depont has been many Years in England but that during Such his Residence there he never rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards nor ever served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act as he is advised and believes to gain a Settlemt in any Place in the Kingdom of Great Britain

Sworn this. 19. day of Decr 1786 before R Davies

Jacob Welfar
