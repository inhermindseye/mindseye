Middx  Elizabeth Weston aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs Moore a Silver Smith in Grace Church Street in the Parish of Saint Bennett Grace Church London in the City of London for one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of Two Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly servant for one Year

Sworn this 22nd. day of June 1779 before R. Butler Abrm Baley

Elizabeth Whitson
