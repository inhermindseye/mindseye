Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Taylor the Widow of William Taylor deced maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband or of his Parents nor Can She give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late Mother deced That this Deponents late Father James Pottington was born in the Parish of Croydon in the County of Surry where he died & was buried & was legally Settled at his death as this Deponent has been credibly informed & believes That She this Deponent was born in the Said Parish of Croydon & never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year in any one place nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself save What She might Acquire by marriage with her said husband whose Settlement is unknown to her

Sworn this 17th day of June 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of Elizth Taylor
