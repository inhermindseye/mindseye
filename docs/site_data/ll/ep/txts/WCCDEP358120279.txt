Middx  Jas. Bola'tho upon Oath saith he rented a house known by the Sign of the Red Dragon in Arundal Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the yearly Rent of £38 and lived therein near four years that he left the said house about 16 years ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Feby. 8th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

James Bolatho
