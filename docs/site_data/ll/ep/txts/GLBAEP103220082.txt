Middlesex ss  William Loyd maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Miles Mason a China Merchant in Fenchurch Street in the Parish of St Dionis Back Church in the City of London & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of twenty months and has not Since done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this. 4. day of Novemr 1786 before}

Refused to be Passed}
