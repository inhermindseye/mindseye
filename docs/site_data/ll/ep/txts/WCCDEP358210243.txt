Middx & Westmr.  Ann Brafield Aged about Twenty five Years Upon Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says her Father John Brafield lived in and Occupied a House of his own in the Parish of Yearsley in the County of Hereford for a great many Years but what the Yearly Value of same was this Examinant cannot tell And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex (where she now lodges) she being Poor and not able to maintain the same when Born; says that James Peat  Servant to Mr. Downing a Musical Instrument Maker in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of April 1767, and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Peat and no other person did beget the same

Sworn the 6th. day of February 1768 , Before W Kelynge

The Mark of Ann Brafield
