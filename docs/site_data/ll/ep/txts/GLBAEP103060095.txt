Aldgate Middx

Elizabeth Sanderson  Widdow of John Sanderson deced Maketh Oath that her Said late husband often told her that he Servd his Apprenticeship to a Turner in Mark lane in the Parish of Alhallows Staining in the City of London and that he took this Deponent, and Showd her the House where he Servd his time which is now a Cheesmongers Shop and that he had relief from the Said parish and had a Badge put, upon him there

Sworn 1st day of February Ano Dom 1747 Robert Dennett Richd: Ricards

the mark of Eliz Sanderson
