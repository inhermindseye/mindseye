Middlesex ss  July Campbell  Single woman Maketh oath that about Twenty one years ago she lived as a hired servant by the Year with one Mr Maddan of Prujanes Court in the Parish of St Martin within Ludgate in the City of London with whom she lived hath space of a year at the yearly wages of Five pounds by the year And by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And gained saith that she has not since lived as a hired servant by the Year Nor Done any Act to her knowledge or belief whereby to gain any subsequent settlement

Sworn this 10th day of January 1758 before-  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Campel
