Middx.  Ann Warner aged abt. 23 Years upon Oath says she never was Married nor was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for one Year nor ever Rented Ten pounds by the Year or paid parish Taxes; says she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true that her father Wm. Warner deceased Rented and lived in a house in Gilberts Passage Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards for several Years.

Sworn this 22d. Novr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Warner
