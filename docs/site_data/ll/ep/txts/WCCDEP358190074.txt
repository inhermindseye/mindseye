Middx.  Sarah Miles aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath says she is the wife of Robert Miles to whom she was Married at St. Sepulchre's Church in London abt. One Year & a half ago says her said husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that before his intermarriage with her & whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr Bowen a Butcher in Kingsgate Street in the Parish of St. George the Martyr in the County of Middx. for Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of £2..12 diet washing & lodging and after he quitted that Service never Rented Ten Pounds a Year nor paid parish Taxes

Sworn Decr. 3d. 1760 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Sarah Miles
