Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann King aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of February 1797 she Intermarried with John King her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields by whom she hath one Child living vizt. John aged about Two years That her said Husband was born somewhere in Lancashire but where about she does not know nor where his Settlement is she this Deponent doth not know That she this Deponent Was born at Hammersmith in the Parish of Hammersmith in the County of Middlesex where her father Served his Apprenticeship and that she this Deponent hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of July 1799 before us} Menter G S. Goodenough

her Ann King mark

Henry Day
