Middlesex to wit James Feltham one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on behalf of John Jones  Elizabeth Ann Jones  Samuel Jones and Thomas Jones Infants and Children of Catherine Jones deceased by Samuel Jones their Father ( gone to sea ) maketh Oath that it appears by an Examination of the said Catherine Dated the 14th. day of December last that she the said Catherine Jones with her then three Children were passed from the said Parish of of Saint Clement Danes to the Parish of Chiswick  in the County of Middlesex as the place of their last legal Settlement and his Examinant further says that he is Informed and verily believes they have gained no other Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 25th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

James Feltham
