Middlesex ss}  Susannah Box  wife of William Box of Peel alley in the Parish of St Paul Shadwell Middlesex  Mariner maketh oath That about nine Years ago this deponents late Sister Mary Rowley deced whose Maiden name was Moore  was married to one John Rowley in the Parish Church of St Dunstan Stepney Middlesex being the Third Woman Which the said John Rowley had taken to wife as She this Deponent has been informed & believes That Since the Celebration of Such her Said Sisters marriage as aforesd She this Deponent has also been informed & believes that at the time Such marriage was So had the Said John Rowley s Second wife was then living That this Deponents Said Sister left behind her at the time of her death two Children by the Said John Rowley namely John Rowley aged about two Years & three Months & James Rowley Aged about four months one of Which Children namely John Rowley was born in that part of Angel Gardens which lies in the Parish of St Paul Shadwell Middlesex & the other of them namely James Rowley was born in the Workhouse belonging to the Hamlet of Mile End old Town Middlesex both of Which Children are become Chargeable to the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate Middlesex That about Seven years ago the Said John Rowley the Father took & lived in & rented a house in White Horse Street in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney Middlesex & was Charged to the Kings Tax for the Same as She this Deponent has been informed & believes & this Deponent is the rather induced to believe the Same to be true For that She this Deponent went once with her Said Sister for the Purpose of paying the Kings Tax for the Sd house on behalf of her Sd Sisters husband when her Sd Sister went into the house of the proper Collector intitled to receive the Same & immediately after Came out & produced to this Deponent a receipt for the Sd Kings Tax under the Collectors hand for the Same being then paid by her Sister for the Sd house which as this Deponent now recollects was for half years Tax & this Deponnet has at times been Shewn other Land Tax Receipts for the Sd house on behalf other Said Sisters husband and this Deponent has been lately informed that the Said John Rowley the Father is lately dead.

Sworn this. 28. day of October 1778 before Dec Session Jasper Clarkey

Susannah Box
