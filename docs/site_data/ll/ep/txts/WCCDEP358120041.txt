Middx  Ann Wilkinson aged 60 yrs upon Oath Saith She never was married & that her late father Thos. Wilkinson rented a house of Ten pounds by the year & upwards in Angel Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlx lived therein Sevl Years & never after he quitted the said house rented ten Pounds a year or pd. parish taxes And that she this Examinant never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. rented ten Pounds a year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn 19th. Janry 1754 before} Tho Lane
