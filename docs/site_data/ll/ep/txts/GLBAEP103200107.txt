Middlesex ss}  Sarah Hayward the widow of Robert Hayward deced maketh oath that her sd late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born in Cock Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & that he also rented a house in Cartwright Street in the sd Parish & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & this Depont Can give othr Account respecting her sd husbands Settlement That She was lawfully married to her sd husband & Since his Death has not been married again or done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this. 20. day of February 1784 before J. Hawley

The Mark of Sarah Hayward
