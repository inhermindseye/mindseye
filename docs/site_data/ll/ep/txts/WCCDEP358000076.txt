Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to Saint Clements to wit Rebecca Fox aged about 65 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to John Fox at Birmingham Old Church about 38 years ago and who has been dead about Ten years that since their Intermarriage they Rented and lived in a House in Peter Street now Denzell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 2 years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds hath quitted said House upwards of 23 years since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a further Settlement

Sworn this 19th day of July 1785  Edward Hodsoll

her Rebecca Fox Mark
