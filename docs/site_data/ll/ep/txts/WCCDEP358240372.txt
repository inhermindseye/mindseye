St Clement Danes 10th January 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  (to wit) Johanna Westcott  singlewoman aged thirty two three years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never served any Apprenticeship lived as an hired Yearly Servant or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own right of the best of her knowledge or belief that she is informed and Verily believes that she is the las illegitimate Daughter of Hannah Dawson otherwise called Hannah Westcott deceased by Richard Westcott and was born in the British Lying in Hospital in Brownlow Street  Long Acre in the County of Middlesex in or about the Month of November 1760 that the said Richard Westcott and the s said Hannah this Deponent's Mother were Married at the parish Church of at the Devizes in the County of Wilts on or about the 28th day of July 1749 but that the said Richard Westcott (before such Marriage with this Deponent's Mother) had been legally Married to a former Wife named Mary Westcott who (as this Deponents is also informed & believes) was actually living and the legal Wife of him the said Richard Westcott at the time of such his Marriage with this Examinant's said Mother as aforesaid at the Birth of her this Deponent at the time of her said Mother's decease that is to say in or about the year 1765 and long afterwards vizt. until in or about the year day of our Lord 1770 And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that she

hath been informed and verily believes that her said Mother before her Marriage with the said Richard Westcott had been legally Married to a Mr. Dawson and left a Widow by his Death saith that she hath never heard where the said Mr. Dawson was born or the place of his legal Settlement that she hath been informed and verily believes her saith said Mother Hannah was born in the Township of Macclesfield within the parish of Prestbury in the County of Chester and was legally settled therein right of her Father (who as this Deponent was informed and believes) was the owner or Tenant inhabiting Premises of considerable value in that Township And Lastly this Deponent saith that she knows not of any other settlement she is entitled to but in the said Township of Macclesfield within the parish of Prestbury in Right of her said Mother as aforesaid and that she is ill Poor and unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn the tenth day of January 1794 before us} W Addington Edw: Read

Joanna Westcott
