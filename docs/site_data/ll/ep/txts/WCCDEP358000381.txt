Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Henry Winchester aged about 34 Years upon his Oath saith that he served Seven years Apprenticeship with one Wm. Cross who lived in St. John Street in the Parish of Saint Sepulchres in the County of Middx that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain  Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of Novr. 1786  Wm Kitchiner

H. Winchester
