Middx to wit Michael Brooksbank Aged about Fifty Six years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his present Wife  Avis at the Parish Church of Saint Brides London about Eleven years ago Says  he hath one Child by a former Wife Named John Aged about Thirty Six Years now provide for says he Rented and lived in a House in Cursitor Street in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx for Two Years At the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted the said House about fifteen Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 6th. day of Jany. 1781 before  Sampn Wright R Butler

her Michael Brooksbank Mark
