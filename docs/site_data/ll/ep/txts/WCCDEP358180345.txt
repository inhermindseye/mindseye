Middx  Ann Till aged about 30 Years upon her Oath saith her Father Served Seven Years Apprentiship in the Parish of Wedgbury in the County of Stafford where this Examinant was born Says she never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Child (to wit) Mary aged about one Year & a Quarter & that the sd. Child was born in the sd. Parish of Wedgbury

Sworne 25th. June 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Till
