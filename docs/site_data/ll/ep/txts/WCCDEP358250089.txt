St Clement Danes 16th January 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Thomas Mc Quone aged 46 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife Ann at the parish Church of St. Andrews in the City of Dublin according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of England about 20 years ago that previous there to and while he was single and unmarried he served two years as an Apprentice under Indentures duly executed and then left his Master without his conford that his Matter where Names was Wm. Osborn resided in Stan Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex where this Deponent then lodged and Slept That this Deponent hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant Rented any House or Apartment a £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any legal Settlement to the best if this Deponents knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 16th day of January 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Thoms M Que
