Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Walter Petherick aged about 37 years upon his Oath saith that in the year 1773 he Intermarried with Susannah his present wife at the Parish Church of St. Brides als. Bridgett in the City of London and by whom he hath one Child living by his said and Wife vizt. Susannah aged about 13 years That he this Ext in the year 1776 went down to Plymouth and there Purchased a Lease of a House situate in the Parish of St. Andrew at Plymouth in the County of Devon that he paid £10 a year & upwards for said House & all Taxes due for the same for Six Months and upwards that he hath quitted said House upwards of 10 years age since which time he has not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day Augt. 1787 R Butler

Walter Petherick
