Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Fitchett aged about 64 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Paid Taxes ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that she was born in Aldersgate Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph within Aldersgate in the City of London where her father kept House for several years

Sworn this 26th- day of March 1789  Tho Vaughan N Bond

her Mary Fitchett Mark
