Middlesex ss}  Isaac Edwards maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to & served the whole thereof with one Evan Loyd of Burr Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Taylor & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That he this Deponent has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement.

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

Isaac Edwards
