Middx  Eliz Burchell aged upwds. of Twenty four years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant Mr. Fry in Portugal Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes at the yearly Wages of Five pounds for one year & upwards Quitted her sd. Service about 4 months ago & has not since Rented Ten pounds a year or pd. parish Taxes And this Examinant further upon her voluntary Oath sa she was delivered of a male bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Moore in Portugal Street aforesd. on the 16th. January Inst. which has been baptized John & is been Chargeable to the sd. parish of St Clement Danes this being poor & unable to maintain the same And John Wild a Soldier did beget the sd. male bastard Child on her body the sd. John Wild having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the Month of April and at several other times since at one of which time he got her with Child of the sd. male bastard Child of she was delivered as aforesd. and that the said John Wild and no other person is the true & real father t

Sworn Janry 18th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Burchell
