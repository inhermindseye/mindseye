Middx  Sarah Taylor aged 31 yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servt. with Mr. Edwards in the parish of all Saints in the City of Hereford near two Years at the yearly Wages of Two pounds and left her sd. Service near twelve months ago since which she has not rented ten pounds a year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn Febry. 25th. 1754 before Tho Lane

Sarah Taylor
