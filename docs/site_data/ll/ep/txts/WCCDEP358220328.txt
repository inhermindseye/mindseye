Westmr. to wit Thos. Hardye Aged about Thirty Years on his Oath Says he never was Married Rented House served any Apprenticeship Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one William Johnson a Butcher in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Two Years at the Yearly Wages for the first Year for Six Pounds and for the Second Year Eight pounds hath quitted his said Service about Three Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 26th. day of February 1772 } Tho. Kynaston J Girdler

Thomas Hardye
