Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Adaman aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she this Deponent Rented a Second floor unfurnished of Mrs. Butt in Goodge Street in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds that she hath quitted Said Service about Five years since which time she hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of Novr. 1796 } Rusl Clarke J Robinson Eliza Adaman
