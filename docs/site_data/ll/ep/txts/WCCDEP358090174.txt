Middx to wit Hannah Bolton Aged about Twenty four years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Richard Bolton lived in a House of his own in the Parish of Stan Harcourt in the County of Oxford where this Examinant Settlement is she having done no other Act to gain a Subsequent One And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And further says that one Bateman Mawson a Mercer in Holywell Street did beget such Bastard Child on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her some time the beginning of March last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards on her own Room at her Masters House in Holywell Street aforesaid at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Bateman Mawson and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 27th. day of Novr. 80 before  Wm. Kitchiner

her Hannah Bolton mark
