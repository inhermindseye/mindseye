Westmr.  Ann Cunningham upon oath saith she never was married, nor ever and that she was bound Apprentice for four Years to Mrs. Mary Ashbourn a Mantua maker and Served her sd. Apprentiship wth. the sd Mrs. Ashbourn in Whitehorse Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Jany. 11th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Cunningham
