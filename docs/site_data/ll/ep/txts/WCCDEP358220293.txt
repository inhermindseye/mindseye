Westmr. to wit Letitia Gosford Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath says she never was Married was an Apprentice Rented or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one William Tomlins a Lighterman at Twickenham in the County of Middlesex as her said Father has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 16th. day of Octr. 1771 before Us W Kelynge R. Butler

The Mark of Letitia Gosford
