Middx  Eliz: Pendleberry on Behalf of her mother Ann Christopher upon Oath saith that she has been informed and beleives the said Ann Christopher lived a Yearly Servant whilst a Widow with Mr. Sweating a Tobaaconist in Grubb Street in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate London for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds ten Shillings and quitted her sd. Service about Eight Years Says that at the time her sd. mother Lived with Mr. Sweating her said Mother had two Children (to wit) this Examinant & her brother who were both bound Apprentices upwds. of Six months before her mother lived a Servant with the sd. Mr Sweating Says her sd. mother has not since she left Mr Sweatings Service rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 20th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Eliz Pendleberry
