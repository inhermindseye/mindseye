Middx ss  Ann Bath  widow of James Bath deced Maketh oath that her said late husband has often informed her that about Sixteen years ago he rented a house on Addle Hill in the parish of St. Gregory in the City of London and paid Ten pounds a year and upwards for the same and likewise all parish taxes in the same Parish And by means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that her said husband hath not to the knowledge information or belief of her this Deponent since gained any other settlemt.

Sworn this 2nd day of August 1755 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Ann Bath
