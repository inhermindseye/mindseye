Middx The Examination of Saml. Shaw on the behalfe of Elizth. Kilby  Widw . of Jas. Kilby Deced who upon his Oath saith yt. the sd. Jas. Kilby sometime before his Death Rented a House in Duck Lane in the Parish of St. Bartholomew the Great  London of the Yearly Rent of £12 & lived in the same some Years And this Examinant further saith that ye. sd. Jas. Kilby after he left the sd. House nor she Elizth. his Widw. since his Death ever Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year whereby to gain any other Settlement as this Examinant hath been informed & verily beleives

Sworn this 28th. Day of July 1739 before} Tho Lane  Jas Gronous

Saml Shaw
