Before Birth 

St Clement Danes September 28th 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  Settlemt.  Pregnancy  Reputed Father (to wit) Mary Riley Rainer  singlewoman aged 22 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Constable in Butcher Row in the parish of St. Clement Danes for a year & upwards at the annual Wages of Seven pounds that she quitted that service on or about the 12th day of this instant September and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Examinant Upon her Voluntary Oath further saith that She is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes when born and that one Closely now or late a lodger at the Blackmoor's Head in Hedge Lane within the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex  Shoemaker is the only real true father of the Child or Children where with She is pregnant as aforesaid he and no one else having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body on or and that only on or about the fifth day of May last when he beget the

said Child or Children thereon

Sworn the 28th September 1793 before me } D Walker

Mary Riley's Rainer's Mark
