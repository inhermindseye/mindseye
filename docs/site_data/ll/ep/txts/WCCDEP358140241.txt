Middlesex .  John Ward aged abt. 25 Years upon Oath says he was married to Ann his wife in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. on the 22d. day of February 1756. says he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to John Rogers of Harp Alley in the Parish of St. Bridges London Brazier but laid in the house of his father John Ward  Carpenter near Surry Street Strand in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes during all the said Term of Seven Years says he has two Children John Henry aged almost three Years and Hanh . aged abt. Eighteen Months and being poor is not able to Maintain himself & family

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

John Ward
