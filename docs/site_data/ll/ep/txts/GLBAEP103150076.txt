Middlesex ss}  Sarah Wickham the Widow of John Wickham deced maketh oath That about Twenty one Years ago She was lawfully married to her Said late husband at St Johns Church Exeter in the County of Devon Who as he has informed her & which She believes to be true was an Irishman by birth & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in Great Britain That about Seven Years ago She was married to one Thomas Conner Since deced by a Romish Priest in a Dwelling house in Holloway Lane Shoreditch in the Said County of Middx Which Marriage as She this Deponent has been advised & believes was wholy Void in Law That after the death of her Said husband Wickham & Previous to her Marriage with the sd Thomas Conner & whilst she was a Widow & having no Child then living She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages with William Bradley of the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County of Middx  Victualler & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a Year and upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she this Depont has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year no done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement other than What She might have Acquired by her Marriage with the sd Wickham That She this Depont. hath a Child now living aged about Seventeen Months which was born in a place Called the Dark Entry in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate The Which Child is a Bastard & hath been Since baptized & registred by the name of Sarah Wickham & that one Thomas Bercraft late of the Same Parish Shoemaker deced was the Father of the Said Child.

Sworn this 9th day of November 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Sarah Wickham
