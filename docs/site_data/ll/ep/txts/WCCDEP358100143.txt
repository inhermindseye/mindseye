Middlesex to wit Elizth. Davis Aged about fifty Nine years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband Richd. Davis at the Parish Church of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex about twelve years ago and who died about two years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House facing the Adelphi in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr for four years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of fifty Pounds & Paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about four years ago and after quitting said House he never gained any other Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 28th. day of Octr. 1783  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

Elizabeth Davis
