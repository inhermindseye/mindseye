Middx  Thomas Macdonald upon Oath says he was born in the Parish of St. Stephen in Waterford or in the Parish of Lisnikill in Ireland That he served his Apprenticeship with Thomas Mullins in the Parish of Lisnikill in the same County Taylor and never gained any Settlement in England Says that he Intermarried with Sarah his Wife at the Fleet London about 11 Years since and hath, 4 Childn. by her namely Catherine aged about 10 Years born in Dyott Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields , Michael aged about 7 Years born in the same House, John aged abot. 4 Years born in Great Wild Street in the same Parish & Robert aged abt. 14 Months born in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and the abovenanamed Sarah upon Oath says that before her Marriage with the sd. Thos. Macdonald She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Joseph Cross at the Turks head in Dyot Street  St. Giles in the Fields 3 years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £4. and says She did not live in any other Service afterwards.

Sworn the 20th. Day of Septr. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond
