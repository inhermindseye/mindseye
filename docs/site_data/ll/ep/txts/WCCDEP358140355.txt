Middx.  Mary Isbell aged abt. 59 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Benjn: Isbell decd. says her said husband informed her and which Information she Verily believes to be true that before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired yearly Servt. with John Newet a Staymaker in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx for One year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds Diet Washing and Lodging Says her said husbd. in his life time nor she since his death never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn this 29th. Feby. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of N Mary Isbell
