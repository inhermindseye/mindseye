Saint Clement Danes.

Middlesex. and Westminster} To wit, Sarah Clarke aged 21 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That She never was Married. That on the 18th: day of July 1782 She was delivered of a Female Child at the House of Mr Marshal a Shoemaker in Stanhope Street Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes ; which was baptized at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes by the Name of Ann Split , And Further upon her Oath Saith that Philip Split a Rope Maker in Cable Street near Rosemary Lane is the the Father of the said Child he having had Carnal knowledge of this deponent in the Year 1781, and at several Times Since at one of which Times he did beget the Child above mentioned

She lives No. 1 Crown Street facing Tollen Lane Court Road.

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1786 . before R Butler

Sarah Clark
