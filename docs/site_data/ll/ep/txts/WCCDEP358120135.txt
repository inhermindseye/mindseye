Middx  Sarah Hames aged 16 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Reece a Sugar Baker in Lewins Mead in the Parish of St. James in Bristol at the Yearly wages of Three Pounds lived therein upwds. of 1 Yr. & has quitted her sd Service abt. 9 months; Since which she has not rented ten Pounds by the year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn June 27th 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Hames
