Middlesex ss}  John Lawrence maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to his Uncle James Lawrence a Wheelright in the Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the sd County & Served him there for about the Space of Two Years & then this Deponent was Assigned or turned over to Thomas Lewis a Wheelwright in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County where he Served the Remainder of his said Apprenticeship That he never rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Since Such his Apprenticeship as aforesaid

Sworn this. 28. day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of John Lawrence
