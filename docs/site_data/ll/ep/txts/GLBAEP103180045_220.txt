Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Smith the wife of Joseph Smith maketh oath that Ann Page lately deced was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with this Deponent & her Said husband in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two Years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement That during Such her Servitude She the Said Ann Page was delivered of a Female Child in this Deponents house in the Said Parish which has been Since baptized and registred by the name of Ann Page & this Deponent is induced to believe that Such Child is a Bastard by reason the Said Ann Page was never married to the knowledge information or belief of this Deponent

Sworn the. 20. June 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of e s Elizabeth Smith
