St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster (to wit) Elizabeth Tremlett aged 29 years or thereabouts whom her Oath saith That Henry Simpson and Infant aged 5 Weeks or thereabouts  a Bastard was born in the  Osle Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le Bone in the County of Middlesex & of whom she is the Mother & that one Henry Simpson who did  live with Mr. Pearson of Cooks Court is the father of such Child and that she is Poor and unable to maintain the said Child

Sworn this 6th. day of Janry. 1786  Tho Barnfather  Gs Bradshaw

Elizth Tremlett
