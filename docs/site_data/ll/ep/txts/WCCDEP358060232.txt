Middx  Geo. Pritchard upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Alice Pritchard in the parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn upwds. of 4 Yrs. since and that abt. 6 Years ago he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £12 in Red Lyon Street in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx and lived therein upwds. of Six Years and has not since he quitted the sd house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 18th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

George Pritchard
