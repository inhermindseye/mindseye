Saint Clement Danes

Settlemt. in St. C. D to wit Robert Chafer aged about 37 years upon his Oath saith that in the year 1784 he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That he has Two Children living by his said Wife vizt. Elizabeth aged about Six Years and Susannah aged about Three years That he this Deponent before his Intermarriage to his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr Pennington at the Sign of the Crooked Billed in Wych Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about 7 years at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that he hath quitted said Service about Eighteen years since which time he has not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1794 } Edw: Read

his Robert Chafer Mark

Witness. Richd. Dobbins
