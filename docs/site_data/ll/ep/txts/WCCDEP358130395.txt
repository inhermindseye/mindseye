Mary Welch aged abt. 24 years upon Oath Intermarried with Wm. Welch (who is now at Sea) at the Parish Church of Falmouth in Cornwall abt. 9 years since that says he said Husband who lift her in abt. afortnight ofter her Mamagix never hat  was born in Ireland and & that She Cannots give any Act. of the Settlement of her sd. Husband Says before her Intermarried with the said Wm. she lived a timed yearly Servant of a year with one Robt. Ambleton in the Parish of Falmouth in the County of Cornwall aforesaid at the yearly Wages of £2..10s-& hath quitted her sd. Service abt. 6 years since & further upon her Voluntary Oath says She is pregant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastard & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes wher She now lodgeth She being noon & unable to Maintain the same when ber & that one Geo: Cummings a Linnendr of who lives in the Old Cher in the City of London did beget the said Bastard Child or Childn. on her Body he the said Geo: Cummings has had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body at House of Mr. Walter Butler in Devereux Court in the Parish saint Clement Danes in the Month of February last at which time got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Childn. of a she is now Pregnant And that he the said Geo: Comming & no other Person is the true real & Father thereof Middx to wit

Sworn the 18th day of October 1756 before Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Welch
