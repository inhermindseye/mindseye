Westmr to wit Hannah Pye Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant  with one Henry Knowles who now keeps a Cooks Shops in Whites Alley Chanecry Lane in Liberty of the Rolls upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of half a Year ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of February 1774 before  Sampn Wright R Butler

her Hannah Pye mark
