Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Haseman aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Crawford for Four years at the yearly wages of Six Guineas that the last Three Months of her said Service was in Parliament Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster that She hath quitted said Service about Eight years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement and saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 21st. day of June 1798 before us  Rupt. Clarke W Addington

her Mary Haseman mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
