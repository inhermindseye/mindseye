Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Houghton aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 26th. day of January 1793 she Intermarried with William Houghton her present Husband at No. 16 Paradise row  Dublin That her said Husband is now Serving is a Private in the Supplementary Militia in Captn. Creeds Company at Bocking That She this Deponent never was married to any Person but to the said William Houghton That She never did or does now Receive from any parish office or from any Person any Money on Account of her said Husband save and except what she receives from the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 1st- day of Febry- 1799 before me Menter

her Elizabeth Houghton Mark
