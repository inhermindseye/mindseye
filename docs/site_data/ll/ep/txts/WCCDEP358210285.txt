Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Davis Aged about Thirty six Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edwards at a Bacon Shop in Newgate Street in the Parish of Christ Church London for Six Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, quitted same about Twelve Years ago, and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year; And further upon her  Oath says that on the Twentieth day of May 1764, she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Gaywood in Whites Alley in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex which hath been Baptized by the Name of Bethia , says that Leonard Keeve now a Pauper in the Rolls Workhouse did beget the said Female Bastard Child on her Body of which she was delivered as aforesaid, he having had carnal knowledge of her Body several times at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Female Bastard Child, and that the said Leonard Keeve and no other Persons is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 13th, day of June 1768 , before}  Thos Kynaston

Sarah Davis
