Middlesex ss}  Ann Cambel the wife of George Cambel maketh oath that her said husband is now Absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with a Taylor in the Parish of St Thomas in the City of Bristol & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this. 13. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Cambel
