Middlesex ss}  Anthony Pillow maketh oath That divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indres to Michael Reynolds of the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the Sd County Smith for Seven Years & Served Six Years of Such his Apprenticeship in the Said Parish & then he removed with his Said Master into Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Where he Served the residue of this Sd Apprenticeship That he has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year or rented a house or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Settlement Since his Sd Apprenticeship

Sworn this. 4. day of December 1778 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Anthony Pillow
