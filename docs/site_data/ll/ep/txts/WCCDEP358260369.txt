Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Moody aged about Sixty years upon his Oath saith that he held a House on Lease of a Mrs. Beard in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Ten years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds during which time he Resided in the said house that after he quitted same he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Novr . 1798 before me } Saml Edward

His Willm. Moody Mark
