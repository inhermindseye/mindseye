Middlesex ss}  William Wyers maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indre to one Elias Sutton of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Basketmaker & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship in the sd parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house or other Tenement of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt And that he hath a Wife named Sarah to whom he was lawfully married in the parish Church of Saint George Bloomsbury in the sd County of Middlesex

Sworn this 10th. day of November 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

Willm Wyers
