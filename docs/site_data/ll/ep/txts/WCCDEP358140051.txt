Middx.  Patience Elliston aged abt. 59 Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Edwd. Elliston & that her said late husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Watkins a Bricklayer in Great Wild Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx. & never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 1st. day of November 1757 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran. Bedwell

The Mark of Patience Elliston
