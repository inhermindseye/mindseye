Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Merryman aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with a Mr. Henley who kept the Distillers Arms in Saffron hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the County of Middlesex for Two years at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that She hath quitted said Service about Eighteen Months since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Decr. 1789 J Addington J Pownall

her Sarah Merryman Mark
