Middx  Mary Nelhams aged about 25 years upon Oath says She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the 21st. day of January 1755 in the House of Mr. King a Founder in Goldsmiths Alley  Jewin Street in the Parish of St. Giles without Cripplegate London which Child has been since Baptized William

Sworn the 10th. Day of April 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell  Theodr. Sydenham

Mary: Nelhams
