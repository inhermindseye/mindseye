Middx  Ann Clarkson aged about 36. years upon Oath says She Intermarried with John Clarkson (now a Soldier,) at the Fleet London by whom She has two Children (to wit) Helen aged about four years & Christian aged about five weeks, Says her said Husband Rented a House in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds & paid the poor Rate for the same says her said Husband quitted the said House about two Years & half ago & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 5th. Day of Feby. 1756 - before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Ann Clarkson
