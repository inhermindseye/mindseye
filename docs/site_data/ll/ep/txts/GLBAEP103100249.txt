Middlesex ss  William Lemp Maketh Oath That he well knew Henry Sherlock late of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Staymaker deced who about Thirteen years ago rented a house in Monmouth Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the said County and as this Deponent has been informed by the said Henry Sherlock and believes he the said Henry Sherlock paid for the same the yearly Rent of Twenty Four Pounds and lived in the Sam fordword years and by means there of as this Deponent is advised he the Said Henry Sherlock gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish of St. Giles in the Fields And this Depont. Saith that to his knowledge information or belief the Said Henry Sherlock did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Serve any Parish Office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And this Depont. farther Saith that the Said Henry Sherlock left behind him at his death two Children who are Still living to wit Frederick aged about Seven years and Elizabeth aged about eight years the lawful None of the Said Henry Sherlock by Mary late his wife and now the wife of this Deponent which said Children have done no Act to gain a Settlement in their own right

Sworn this 7th. day of March 1770 before  Chris. Scott  Rob Pell William ann Lemp
