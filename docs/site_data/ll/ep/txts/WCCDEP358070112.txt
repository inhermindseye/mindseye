Westminster to wit Ann Hammond aged about forty Two Years on her Oath Says she intermarried with her late Husband James Hammond deceased at the Parish Church of Saint George tho Martyr Southwark in the County of Surry upwards of Twelve Years ago and by whom she hath four Children now living Namely Mary aged about Eleven Years John and Elizabeth Two Twins aged upwards of Three Years and an half and Thomas aged about Six Months Says her said late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. William Haydon a Grocer in Blackman Street  South in the said Parish of St. George the Martyr Southwark in upwards of Seven Years County of Surry and after quitting that Service he was Rented House or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1773 before  Tho: Kynaston

her Ann Hammond  Mark
