Middx  The Examination of Ann Harris  Widw . of Wm. Harris Deced Passed from the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . by Ordr. bearing dated the 27th. Day of June last taken the 15th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that her late Husband Wm. Harris rented & lived in a Tenemt. in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly rent of £10 for the space of One Year & upwds. that her sd. Husband Paid the Poors & all other Rates, And further saith yt. he never after he left ye. sd. Tenemt. Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of One year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examint. hath been informed - & verily beleives yt. her sd. late Husband Wm. Harris has been Dead Months & she being Sick & Poor is not able to maintain herselfe

Sworn the Day & year above menconed before}
