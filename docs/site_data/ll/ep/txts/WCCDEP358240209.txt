St Clement Danes 30th Janry. 1793

Middx & Westminster }  St Margt Westmr .  (to wit) Edward Stone aged 61 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife Ann formerly Ann Ballard Spinster at the Fleet in the year 1753 or then that previous thereto and while he served the latter part of his Apprenticeship & for about the last two years und under Indentures with Mr William Stone Sawyer Cannon Row in the parish of St Margaret Westminster and lodged there at the House of Mr Cooper where his said Master lived that since the expiration of his Apprenticeship he never lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together while Single Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 30th. day of January 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Edward Stone

The Mand Man died afterwards in St Clement Danes parish & the Woman was Removed again in Janry. 1794 Vide Examn. page 293 this Book
