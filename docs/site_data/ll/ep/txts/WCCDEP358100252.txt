Middx  Martha Barry Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Latham a Baker in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upwards of Eight Months at the yearly Wages of four Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service about Eight Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to Maintain the same when born And further says that one Andrew Stevens a Surgeon of the Navy who lodges at Mrs. Tiffins No. 277 Strand is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at the said Mrs. Tiffins House afsd. sometime in the Month of May last and at sevl. difft. times aftwds at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Childn. of which she is now Pregnant & that the said Andrew Stevens & novr. person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 6th day of Septr. 1784  Wm Kitchiner

Martha Barry
