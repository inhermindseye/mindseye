Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Rachael Walter aged about 70 years upon her Oath saith that about 40 years ago she Intermarried with John Walter her late Husband (who has been dead about Seven years) at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn th her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Wells Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone for Eight years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds that after he quitted said house he never Rented any house nor did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath this Examinant since her said late husbands Death done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Decr. 1789  Sampn Wright  Wm. Kitchiner

Rachel Walter
