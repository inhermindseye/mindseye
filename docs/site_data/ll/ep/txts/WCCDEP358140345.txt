Middx.  Edwd. Mackann aged abt. 58 Years Upon Oath says he was Married to Sarah his wife by a Romish Priest five Years ago by whom he has Two Children Vizt. Rose aged abt. three Years & Frances aged abt. One Year and a half and that he has another Child named Margt . aged abt. thirteen Years by a former wife Says he Rented and lived in a house in Bennetts Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £12 for fifteen Months. hath quitted Same abt. 23 Years and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Jany. 22d. 1760 . before me} Barth: Hammond

Edward Mackann .
