Middx  Cathn. Marlow aged abt. 28 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about three Months ago she was deliverd of a male bastard Child (baptized James ) at a house in Chymisters Alley in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr.

Sworn Novr. 23d. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cathn Marlow
