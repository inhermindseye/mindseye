Westmr.  Jas. Mecully upon Oath Saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Traunter a Taylor in Star Court & in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Decr 19th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

James Mecully
