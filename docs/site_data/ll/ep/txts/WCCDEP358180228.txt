Middx  Mary Benstead aged about 17 Years upon her Oath she is a Singlewoman says she never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Edward Benstead was bound an Apprentice to & Served five Years the first Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Gregory Lamb a Shoemaker near the Bull & Gate Inn  Holborn in the Parish of St. Giles in the feilds in the County of Middx & then bought the Remainder of his time of his said Master says her sd. Father never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Exaiants knowledge or beleife

Sworne 3d. Septr: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Benstead
