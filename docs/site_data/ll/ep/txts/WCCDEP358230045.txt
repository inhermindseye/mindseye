Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Ann Hagan who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information her verily believes to be true that the said Ann Hagan Rented and lived in a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Six years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds and paid Taxes due for the same that she hath quitted said house about Four years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of March 1799 before us G S Goodenough Saml. Edwards

Jas Talboys
