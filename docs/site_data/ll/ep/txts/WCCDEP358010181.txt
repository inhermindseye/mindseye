Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  to wit William Stone aged about 29 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Mary his Present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch that he was born opposite Brownlow Street Drury Lane that his Father William Stone Rented and lived in a House in Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for about Eight years at the yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Eighteen Months that this Examinant left his Father about 4 years ago that he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this 19th. day of January 1788 }

W: Stone
