Middx  Esther Mighills  Wido . upon Oath saith she intermarried with Abraham Mighills in Great Brook Street Grosvenor Squares about Ten Years since Says her sd. Husband has been dead about Six years Says the sd. Abraham Mighills rented a house in Great Brook Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Forty Six Guineas & lived in the same one Year or thereabouts & left said house about nine Yrs. to her best remembrance Says the said Abraham Mighills never after rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or other- -wise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 1st. 1746 before  Tho Lane .

Esther Mighill
