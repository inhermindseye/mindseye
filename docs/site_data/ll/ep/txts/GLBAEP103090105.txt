Middlesex ss. The Voluntary Examination of Dorothy Lilly of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Single woman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Twenty Eighth day of May in the Year of our Lord 1768

Who on her oath Saith that She is now with Child or Children And that the said Child or Children is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that one Robert Nicholson of King Street in the said Parish Gentleman is the Father of the said Child or Children

Sworn this 28th day of May 1768 before  Chris: Scott

her Lilly Dawson Mark
