Middx  Ann Saunderson Wido. of Richard Saunderson upon Oath says She intermarried with the said Richard Saunderson in the Parish Church of St. Taukman in Shrewsbury about fifteen Years since Says the sd. Richard Saunderson hath been dead upwards of Six Years Says her sd. late has band lived a Servant with one Mr. Kinnaston one Year & upwards at the Yearly wages of Says she intermarried with the sd. Richard Saunderson when he was lived with the sd. Mr. Thynnaston Says upwds. of two Months the last part of the said Richard Sauderson s Service with the sd. Mr. Kynnaston she this Examinant with her sd. late husband lodged at Swan hill in the parish of St. Taukman in Shrewsbury in the County of Salop Says her sd. late husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath her Children by the sd. Richard Saunderson (to wit) Richd. aged abt. 8 Yrs. & Ann aged abt. 7 Yrs.

Sworn Augt. 2d. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Ann Saunderson
