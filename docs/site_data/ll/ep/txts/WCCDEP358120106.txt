Middx  Danl. Jones upon Oath saith he Served three Years & upwds. of his Apprentiship witht Mr Benjamin Hopkins a Butcher in Tower Street in the Parish of St. Alhallows Barking London and then his Master Left of Trade & gave this Examint. lease to provide for himself in what manner he thought proper and that he never was assigned over nor ever rented ten pounds by the year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn May 25th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Daniel Jones
