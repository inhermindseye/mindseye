Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Mellish Aged about 25 years upon her Oath Saith that about Six years ago she intermarried with William Mellish her present husband at the Parish Church of Saint Bartholomew the Great by whom she hath living Children Viz William Aged five years That her said Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr Clark a Boot Clover in Cloth Hair in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great in the City of London That since he hath been of his time he hath not rented any house tenement on Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examina Knowledge or belief and further that Deponent upon her Oath Saith that her said Husband hath deserted her and her said Children and that they are become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 15th day of April 1800 before us} Saml. Edwards Ws Woodenough

her Susanah Mellish Mark
