Middx  Thomas Halsey aged 48 Years upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with his present Wife Mary at the Fleet London about 5 Years since by whom he hath one Child namely Alice aged 20 Months that about Ten Years since being then a Singleman  that he lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Richard Hatton  Victualler in Aldersgate Street in the Liberty of Glass house Yard in the parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldersgate  Middx for the Space of one Year & upwds: at the Yearly Wages of £4: that since he left the sd. House Service he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt: for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne May 19th. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Thomas Hallsey
