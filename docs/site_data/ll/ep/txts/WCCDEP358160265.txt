Middx  The Examination of Lancelott Baker lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 28th. Day of February 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that abt. Six Years ago he lived in and Rented a House in Maypole Alley in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds And further on his Oath saith that he hath not Since he left the sd. House rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath a Wife & two Children (to wit) Elizth. aged abt. ten Years and Benoni aged about eight Years & being poor is not able to maintaine himselfe & Family

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Lancelott, Baker
