Middlesex to wit Henry Andrews Aged about Thirty four Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Johns in the City of Worcester about Thirteen Years ago and by whom he hath Three Children now living namely Elizabeth Aged upwards of Eleven Years Sarah Aged about Nine Years and Henry Aged about Seven Years Says before his Intermarriage to his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Serjeant Burland in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx in the Capacity of a Coachman for Nineteen Months at the Yearly Wages of fourteen Guineas besides Diet Lodging and Cloaths hath quitted his said Service about fourteen Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement other by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1780 before. R Butler

his Henry Andrews Mark
