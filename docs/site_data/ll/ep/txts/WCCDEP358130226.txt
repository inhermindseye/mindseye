Middx.  Dinah Davis aged abt. Thirty Years upon Oath Says She never was Married & that She lived a hired Yearly Servant upwards of one Year & a half with Dr. Lloyd in Deans Yard in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service about Six Years, since which time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Octr. 22d 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Dinah Davis
