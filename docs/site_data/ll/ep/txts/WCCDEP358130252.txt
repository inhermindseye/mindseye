Middx.  Caleb Hawley aged abt. forty two Years upon Oath says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Fleet Londn. abt. Sixteen Years ago by whom he has Three Children (VIZ) Mary aged abt. Thirteen Years Elizth . aged abt. Five Years & Ann aged abt. Eleven Months Says he lived a Covenant Servant for One - Year with Mr. Holister a Taylor in Searl Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx, & hath Quitted his sd. Service abt. Twenty four Years since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year & being Poor is not able to maintain himself & Family

Sworn Decr. 16th. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

Caleb Hawley
