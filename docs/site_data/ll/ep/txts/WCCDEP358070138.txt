Westmr. to wit Dorothy Vincent Aged about Sixty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife or Widow of William Vincent gone from her with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Brides in the Year 1740 Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Little St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Eight Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about fifteen Years ago and hath not since quitting the same done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her Knowledge or belief neith hath this Examinant.

Sworn this 6th. day of Octr. 1773 before} Sampn Wright

her Dorothy Vincent Mark
