Middlesex to wit Thomas Creighton aged about Twenty Six Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or ever served any Apprenticeship Says he lived or hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Mann at Sipson in the Parish of Sipson in the County of  Suffolk for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted his said Service about Seven Years ago since which time he hath never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 16th. day of Febry. 1782 R Butler

Thos Creighton
