Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Cameron aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Burnell in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said service about Eighteen Months since which time she hath do not lived as hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Janry 1796 } M Addington

her Elizabeth Cameron Mark
