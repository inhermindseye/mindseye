Middlesex ss}  Jane Louzey  Singlewoman maketh oath That She Cannot give any Account respecting her Parents Settlement That She herself was born in the Parish of Castle Jnegam in the County of Essex That She was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor to her Knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right That She hath two Children both of whom are Bastard & illegitimate Namely John Smith aged Seven Years born in the house of one Mason f acing Salt Petre Bank in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middlesex & Baptized & registred in the Collegiate Church of St. Catherine near the Tower of London and William Smith Aged three years born in Greenwood Court in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County of Middlesex and baptized & registred in the Said Church And that one John Smith now or late of Labourer did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Children

Sworn this day of 1771 before}
