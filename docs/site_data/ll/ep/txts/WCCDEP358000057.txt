Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster}  vide Post the 16th June 1785 to wit Cath: Pritchard aged Thirty years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was married Saith that on or about the 29th day of May 1784 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child (since Christened by the Name of John Currer ) at the House of a Mr Banks a Broker in Rose Lane Spital fields in the Parish of Christ Church Spital Fields in the County of Middlesex and that John Currer of High Street White Chapple  Sadler is the Father there of such Child and this Examinand further saith that the Child now produced by her at the time of her swearing the to this Examinant is the Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid

Sworn this 4th day of June 1785  Thos: Gilbert  Thos: Gordon

her Catharine Pritchard Mark
