Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Wardle  wife of William Wardle of Green yard near Nightingale Lane Eastsmithfield in the sd County Cordwainer maketh oath that Shewall knows one Ann Cosgrave now lodging on Salter Peter Bank near Eastsmithfield aforesaid That the said Ann Cosgrave finding herself ill Sent for this Deponent on Saturday last and on the Sunday following the sd Ann Cosgrave was there delivered of a Dead child She this Deponent acting as Midwife to the sd Ann Cosgrave on the occasion and this Deponent has been informed by the sd Ann Cosgrave and which She believes to be true that one Joseph Gould now or late of Virginia Street in the said County Cooper was the Father of such child

Sworn this- 15. day of February 1787 before  R Davies

Elizabth Warden
