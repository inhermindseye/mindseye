Middx.  Elizabeth George aged abt. 40 Years lodging at Mrs. Simpson's in Craven house in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middlesex Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Benjn. George a Soldier in the third Regiment of Foot Guards now in Germany by whom she has Six Children (Vizt.) Benjn. aged 13 Years James aged 9 Years Mary aged 7 Years Ann aged Five Years Willm. aged upwards of 2 Years and John aged 9 Weeks says her said husband informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hone at the Angel Inn in the aforesaid Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for three Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds and never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn 20th. Sepr. 1760 . before me}
