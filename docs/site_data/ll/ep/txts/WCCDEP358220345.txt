Westmr. to wit Thomas Davis Aged about forty Three Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the parish Church of Saint Peter in the City of Norwich about Nine Years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says his late Father Rented and lived in a House in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and where his said Father died and after the Death of his said Father who was a Brazier by Trade his late Mother carried on the said Business for upwards of Seven Years and this Examinant was bound an Apprentice to his said Mother and served her about Three Years when his said Mother quitted the said Business and gave this Examinant his Indentures up and further says he never was Assigned over to any person nor ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant.

Sworn this 24th. day of April 1772 before me} Tho Lane

Thos. Davis
