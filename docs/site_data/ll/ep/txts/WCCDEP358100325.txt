Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Brides to wit Lucey Hughes aged about 28 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Jackson a Chymist in Fleet Markett in the Parish of St. Brides otherwise Bridgett in the City of London for Twelve months at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted said service upwards of three years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid.

Sworn this 29th. Decr. 1784 before us  James Croft  Jno. Barnfather

Lucy Hughes
