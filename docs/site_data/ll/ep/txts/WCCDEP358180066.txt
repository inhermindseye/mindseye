Middx  Mary Gullis  Wido . upon her Voluntary Oath saith that on the 29th- Day of October last she was Delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of one Mrs. Trow in Witch Alley , in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been Baptized Sarah and is now living and become Chargeable to the said Parish and that one Geo: Minitharp  Chairman did begett the said Child on her Body he the said George Minitharp Lodging at the House of one Mrs- Bamber where this Examinant was then a Servant there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of December in the Year 1741 & at sevll- other times since at one of which time she gott her with the sd. Female Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & that he the sd. George Minitharp & no other Person is the true & Reall Father thereof

Sworne the 5th- March 1742 before}
