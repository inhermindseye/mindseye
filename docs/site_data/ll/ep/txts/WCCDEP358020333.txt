St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Benjamin Newman  of the Beadles of the said parish of St Clement Danes and behalf of Susanna Bingley   Widow Maketh Oath & saith that she the said Susanna Bingley  hath Informed him this Deponent & he verily believes it to be true that she rented & Resided in a House in Royal Row in the parish of St Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry for two years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of £16..16..0 and paid the Poors Rate for the same that she quitted the same in or about the Month of June 1790 and that she since hath not done any Act Matter or thing since whereby to obtain any other legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And this Deponent saith that she is now very Infirm & Lame

Sworn the 14th. day of Septr: 1791 D Walker Wm Hitchiner

Benj: Newman
