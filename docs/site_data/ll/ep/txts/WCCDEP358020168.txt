Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Mundin aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bacchus "Piano Fent Maker in Jermyn Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months or thereabouts of the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Sixteen years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented my house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to the best of this Examinant Knowledge Information or belief to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Octr. 1789  Sampn Wright N. Bond

her Jane Mundin Mark
