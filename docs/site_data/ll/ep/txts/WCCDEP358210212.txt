Westmr.  Margaret Burgess aged about Thirty two Years Widow of Anthony Burgess who died in the small Pox Hospital about Seven Months ago, by whom she hath one Child now with her (to wit) Anthony aged about Twenty Months, says her said Husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief says before Her Intermarriage with the said Anthony Burgess and whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Stone at the Lyon and Hey, in Thames Street in the Parish of Alhallows Barking London for three Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and then intermarried with the said Anthony Burgess

Sworn the 1st. Decr. 1767 before} Thos. Kynaston 

Margt Burgess
