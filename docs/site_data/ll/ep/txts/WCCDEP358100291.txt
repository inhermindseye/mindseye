Middlesex & Westminster}  J. (to wit) Thomas Walter aged fifty Years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he served as an Apprentice with James Morton of little Newport Street in the Parish of Saint Ann in the County or this the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex  Shoemaker under an Indenture of Apprenticeship for five Years and upwards that the last four Years of his said Service was in Little Newport Other aforesaid - That he quitted his said Master about thirty Seven Years ago and that since he so quitted him, he has not rented any House Lodging or Tenement of ten pounds a Year, or done any Act Matter or King whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere than in the said Parish of Saint Ann in the Liberty of Westminster aforesaid.

Sworn this 19th. Novr- 1784 before}

Thomas Walter
