Middx ss  Mary Maine  Widow of Edward Maine  deced Maketh oath that about twenty one Years ago her sd. late husband Rented a house and shop in the Parish of St. Leonard East Cheap in the City of London and paid Nineteen Pounds a Year Rent for the Same and lived there two Years and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the sd. Parish And this Deponent Saith that her sd. husband did not afterwards rent a house of Ten Pounds a Year Nor Serve any Parish office Nor gain any Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief Nor hath this Deponent gained any Settlemt. but in right of her sd. late husband at abovesaid

Sworn this 24 day of February 1753 before us  Richd. Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

Mary Maine
