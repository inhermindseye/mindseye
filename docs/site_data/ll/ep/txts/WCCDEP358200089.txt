Westmr.  Eleanor Lebass on the Behalf of James King a Child Aged about twelve Years Upon Oath Says that the said Child was Born in the Parish of St. Ann Blackfryers in the City of London about twelve Years ago as this Examinant hath Been Informed by the Mother by the Mother of the said Child and which Information she Verily Believes to be true

Sworn the 15th of March 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant R Kilshes

Elenor Lebas

Upon Mr Ralph Williams Complt Ovr J. W
