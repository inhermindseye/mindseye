Middx to wit Grace Bennett aged about 38 Years on her Oath she Intermarried to her late Husband James Benn at the Parish Church of St. Sepulchre London about Eighteen Years ago & by Whom she hath Two Children living Namely Mary Aged about fourteen & James aged about Seven Says her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to his Brother Robt. Bennett  Citizen & Gunmaker in the Parish of a Saint Botolph Aldgate London and Served his said Brother for the space of One Year or thereabouts and was a P Assigned over to one John Loden  Citizen & Gunsmith of Preston Yard in the Minories in the aforesaid Parish of a Botolph Aldgate London & Served the said Loder the remaining part of his Said Apprenticeship and after he was out of his said apprenticeship never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of £10 a Year or Otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 7th. of Feby. 1782 before  Robt. Taylor  James Came

Grace Bennett
