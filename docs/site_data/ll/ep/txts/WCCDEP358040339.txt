Westr.  Sybil Hazel aged about 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes and that her father John Hazel lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds in Lake pond Strt. in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs Middx for one Year & upwds. and her mother continued to live in and rent the same house five or Six Years after her fathers decease during which time this Examint. lived with her mother in the sd. house

Sworn March 22d. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Sybil Hazel
