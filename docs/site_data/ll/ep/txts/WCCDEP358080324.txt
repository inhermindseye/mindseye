Middx  Catherine Jones aged about 39 Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Samuel Jones who Entred as a Mercai and gone from her and with whom she Intermarried d the Parish Church of Saint Margaretts Westminster about Nine Years ago and by whom she hath three Children now living Namely John aged about Eight Years Elizabeth Ann aged about Five Years and Samuel aged about two Years says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Turnham Green in the Parish of Chiswick in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Eight Pounds a Year and Paid all Parochial Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about twelve Months ago since which time Her said Husband never Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th- day of Decr- 1778 before R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

Cathrine Jones
