Middx  Elizabeth Knolson aged about 22 years Lodging at the Barley More in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. William Lewis who keeps the Black Bull Alehouse in Milford Lane in the Parish aforesaid for the Space of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds Quitted the same last Christmas and hath not since rented Ten pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes And further upon her voluntary Oath says she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to provide for the Same when born And that the above named William Lewis did beget such Child or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of July One thousand seven hundred and sixty and at several times since at which or one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 30th: day of March 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Knolson

Upon Mr. Barber's Complt.
