Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Ramsay the widow of Charles Ramsay deced maketh oath that her late Father Andrew Ray deced Several years ago rented a house of the yearly Rent of Six pounds in Manr his Rents in the Parish of St. Botolph Without Aldgate in the said County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gains another Settlement & this Deponent lived with him in the Said house as part of his Family That this Depont. said late husband was by birth a Scotchman & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England or Wales That this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She has acquired by her marriage as aforesaid That she hath three Children namely Elizabeth aged about Ten years & Six Months James aged about Six years & eight months & Sarah aged about five years & eight Months the lawful Issue on this Deponent By her Said husband to whom She was lawfully Married

Sworn this 21 day of Janry 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

Elizabeth Ramsay
