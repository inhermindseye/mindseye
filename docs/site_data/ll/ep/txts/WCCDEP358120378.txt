Middx  Cathn. Webb on behalf of Tho Watkins aged upwds. of Ten years the Son of Thos. Watkins & Dorothy  his wife both decd. upon Oath Saith she has been informed & verily believes the abovenamed Tho Watkins (the Elder) Served his time with Mr. Knowles a Leather dresser in Morses Walk in Castle Lane in the parish of St Saviour in Southwk . in the County of Surry and never Rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn July 9th. 1755 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Cath Webb
