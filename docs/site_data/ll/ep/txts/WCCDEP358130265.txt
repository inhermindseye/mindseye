Middx  Elizth. Wright aged abt. 48 Yrs. upon Oath says She is the Widow of Henry Wright who hath been Dead upwds. of a Year Says her sd. late Husband Rented a House in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of fifteen Pounds lived therein abt. Seven Yrs. & there dyed Says She hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Janry 12th. 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

Eliz Wright
