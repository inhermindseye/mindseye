Westmr.  Penelope Blakely Wife of John Blakely upon Oath says she intermarried with her sd. husbd. at the Parish Church of St. Mary Magdalen in Old Fish Street upwds. of 26 Years since Says her said husband lived in and rented a house in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £26 for one Year or thereabouts & quitted the sd. house afrwds. of 20 Years ago Says her sd. husband has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has a Son named Jno. aged upwds. of 13 Yrs.

Sworn Janry 25th. 1747 before}

The Mark of Penelope Blakely
