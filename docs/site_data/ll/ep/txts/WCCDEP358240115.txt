St Clement Danes June 8th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) John Jones Aged about 40 Years Upon Oath saith That he never was Married That he rented & Resided in an House in Ship Yard in the parish of St Clement Danes for Six Months at the Annual Rent of £10. that while he so Rented that Home he lodged there, in the Whole about a Month or 5 Weeks & all the remainder of the time he Slept at the Lodge of Serjeants Inn Fleet Street (he being Porter of that Inn) but nevertheless he kept the House Occupied the whole time by a person with whom he lived of the Name of Ann Smith That he quitted the Rented that House from Christmas 1787 to Midsummer 1788 since which time he hath Rented no House or Apartment of £10 a year or otherwise obtained any legal Settlement And that he now Resides in the paid pari parish of St Clement Danes is Weak Infirm and unable to Maintain himself without Assistance

Sworn the 8th day of June 1792 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

John Jones
