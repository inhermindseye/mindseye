Middx.  John Carr aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath says he served the last four Years of his Apprenticeship to Mr. Freame a Cork Cutter opposite Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx . and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 5th. of May 1759 . before me} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of John Carr
