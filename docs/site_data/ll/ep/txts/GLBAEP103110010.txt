Middlesex ss  Mary Stubbs the widow of Mathew Stubbs deced Maketh oath That her said late husband as She has been Credibly informed & believes was duly bound to & Served the whole of his apprenticeship with William Harrington of Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Waterman and by Means thereof of She is advised her said husband gained a Legal Settlement there and this Depont. Saith that to her Knowledge or belief her said late husband never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his sd. apprenticeship and this Depont. Saith that Since his Death She this Depont. hath not rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this first day of March 1771 before Wilmot B Camper

The Mark of Mary Stubbs
