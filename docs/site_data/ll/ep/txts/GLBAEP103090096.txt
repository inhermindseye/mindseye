Middlesex ss  Margaret Faircloth the widow of William Faircloth Deced Maketh oath that divers Years Ago her said late husband lived in and rented a house of twelve Pounds a year Rent in Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and Continued in such house for the Space of two years and Paid Poors rates for the Same and by means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said husband never afterwards did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And this Deponent farther Saith that since the Death of her said husband She this Depont. hath not done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief And that She hath a Child named John Northston aged about three Months The which Child is illegitimate & a Bastard and was born in Well Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex And baptized and registred in the said Parish Church And that one William Northston deced is the true and only father of the said Bastard Child.

Sworn this 15th. day of February 1768 before  Chris: Scott Rt Darling

Marget Farclorth
