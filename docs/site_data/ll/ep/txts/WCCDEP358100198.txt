Middx to wit Hester Blacklock Aged about forty Six Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Peter Blackstock with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields about Eighteen Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said Husband was a North Countryman but from what part parish or place she does not know neither did he ever acquaint her therewith nor did he ever gain a Settlemt. in London either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever Says before her Intermarriage to her said late Husband she was bound an Apprentice to & served Upwards of Six Years an Apprenticeship to One Mrs. Keppen a Mantua Maker in Southampton Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden and after quitting her sd Apprenticeship she never gained Any other Settlemt.

Sworn this 22d day of March 1784 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Hester Bleaclock
