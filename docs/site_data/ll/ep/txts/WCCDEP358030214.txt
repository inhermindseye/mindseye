Middx  Martha Pell aged upwards of 32 Years upon Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman and was never married says she was bound an Apprentice to Mrs. Seymour a Mantua maker in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex and Served Six Yrs. & upwards the first Part of her Apprentiship with the sd. Mrs. Seymour in Essex Street aforesd. & then her sd. Mistress absconded from her and she never was assigned over to any other person rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 15th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The mark of Martha Pell
