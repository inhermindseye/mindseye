Middlesex & Westminster }  Elizabeth Foullon Aged about Sixty Six Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of James Foullon who has been Dead about twenty four Years Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived as hired Yearly Servant with Messrs Mackannon and Granger Cabinet Makers in Fullwoods Rent in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Six Shillings hath Quitted her said Service upwards of Ten Years ago and after she quitted her said Service she never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 27th: day of June 1770 before us} Tho: Kynaston  Sam Wright

Eliz Foullon
