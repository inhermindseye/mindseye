Westmr.  Edward Harris aged about 36 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Ann Harris his Wife at the Fleet London about fourteen Years ago by whom he hath one Child named Ann aged about One year & Eight Months; says before his sd. Marriage and whilst he was a singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Benoni Sedgley at the Ship & Anchor in  Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for One year & upwards at the yearly Wages of £6..10..0 and then intermarried with the said Ann & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 21st. day of October 1767 , before} 

Edward Harris
