Middx to wit Elizabeth Cook Aged about Forty Seven years on her Oath say's she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or was an Apprentice says she lived a hired yearly servant with one Mr. Mc. Sherson in Newcastle Court Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One year and upwards At the yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twenty years ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1781 before R Butler

her Elizth. Cook  Mark
