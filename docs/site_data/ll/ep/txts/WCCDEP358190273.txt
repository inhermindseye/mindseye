Middx  Elizabeth Perne aged about Thirty three Years upon Oath Says She is the Wife of Edward Perne (who is gone from her ) with whom She Intermarried about five Years since Says her said Husband Served five Years and upwards the first part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Lewis a Peruke maker  in St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and then his said Master died Says her said Husband never was Assigned over to any Person rented House or paid any Taxes as her said Husband hath informed her and which Information She verily believes to be true Says She hath two Children by him William aged about four Years and Edward aged about ten Eighteen Months

Sworn Septr. 10th. 1762 before} Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

Eliz: Pearne
