Middlesex ss  Lydia Roberts the widow of John Roberts deced maketh oath that she hath been informed by her said late husband That divers Years Ago and When a single man he lived as a hired servant by the Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds with Mr. Roddle of the Parish of Arksey near Doncaster in the County of York and Continued in such service for the Space of a year which information this Deponent believes to be true And this Deponent Saith that after she was Married to her Said late husband he rented a Tenement or Farm of Twelve Pounds a Year in the said Parish of Arksey and Continued the occupation of such Tenement or Farm for the Space of Three Years and there died and by Means thereof and of his Said Servitude as She is advised he gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent farther Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said late husband did not do any Act whereby to gain a subsequent settlement And that she hath four Children to wit Mary Aged about fifteen Years Sarah Aged about Thirteen Years Elizabeth Aged about nine Years and Esther aged about Seven Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband none of which said Children hath as yet done any Act to gain a settlement in their or either of their own right.

Sworn this day of September 1765 before us}
