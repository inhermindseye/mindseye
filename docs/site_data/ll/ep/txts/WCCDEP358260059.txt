Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Glascow aged upwards of Forty years upon her Oath saith that about Twenty three years ago she Intermarried with James Glascow her present husband (who is now from her) at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields by whom she hath One Child living namely Elizabeth aged about Nineteen years That her said husband at this time Rents a house in Kings head Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster of one Mr. Bourk at the yearly rent of Twelve Guineas and pays all Taxes due for the same-and has been in possession of said house for several years past

Sworn this 23d. day of June 1795 }

her Mary Glascow mark
