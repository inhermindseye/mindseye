Middlesex ss  Margaret Angus the widow of Robert Angus deced maketh oath that She and her said late husband were both born in Scotland and never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That She hath a Child named Charlotta aged Ten years & ten months the lawful issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married The which Child was born in Darby Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & hath not as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 1st. day of July 1788 before}
