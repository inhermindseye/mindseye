Middx  Sarah Powell Wido. upon Oath saith that about twelve or thirteen Years ago she Intermarried with Josiah Stanton (since deceased) at the Fleet London and that the said Josiah Stanton Served five Years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Baskum a Glass Grinder at the Sign of the hand and Marygold was Star Corner in Barnaby Street in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen  Bermondsey in the County of Surry and then Entered on board the New castle man of War and never was Assigned over rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or belief Says about three years after the death of the sd. Josiah Stanton she Intermarryed with one Wm. Powell (since also deced ) of whose Settlemt. she can give us Account

Sworn Janry 8th. 1749 before H Fielding

The mark of Sarah Powell
