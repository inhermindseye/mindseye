Middlesex ss  John Russell maketh oath that about twenty two Years ago he rented a hose in Sun Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and during Such his residence therein he was charged to and Paid Kings Taxes for the Same be having Paid Such Tax to Mr Jasper Clarke the then Collector of the same That he hath a Wife named Catherine to whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this 30th day of May 1787 before us  R Davies

The Mark of John Russell
