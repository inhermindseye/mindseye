After Birth 

Martha Bevon & Child Named Martha Bevon

Saint Clement Danes March 21st 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Mothers settlemt. St Sepulchre Middx  Child's birth  Reputed Father (to wit) Martha Bevon aged Twenty two Years Upon Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with John Scott of West  Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre  as an hired Yearly Servant upwards of Twenty two Months at the Annual Wages of Six Pounds for the first Year and Seven Pounds or thereabouts for the remainder of the Time, she quitted that Service about Three Years ago, and hath never since done any Act matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponent knowledge information or belief And this Deponent further saith that on or about the 26th Day of October 1792 at Mr Bambers No 3. Hemlock Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath since been Baptized Martha Bevon that George Scott of West Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre aforesaid is the only real and true Father of the said Child which she was delivered of as aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of February in the Year last aforesaid at which Time he did begett the said Child on her Body

Witness her  Mark and Oath

Sworn before me the Day} and year first above Written} Wm. Kitchiner

of The Mark Martha Bevon

Witness J Sharp
