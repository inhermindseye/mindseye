Middx.  Eliz: Cox  Singlewoman upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when born and that one Stephen Moore did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body this Examinant lodging at the House of one Mr. Thomas in Elbow Lane in Thomas Street and the said Stephen Moore lodging in the same House there became acquainted with her & had Carnal knowledge of her Body sevell.. Times in the latter End of the Month of June last and at sevell. other Times since at one of which Times he gott her with the said Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the said Stephen Moore & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof.

Sworn the 20th Octr. 1742 before} Cha: Egerton

The Mark of Eliz: Cox
