Saint Clement Danes

Middx  to wit Jane Morris aged about 44 years upon her Oath saith that about 22 years ago she Intermarried with Francis Morris her present Husband (who has deserted her) at the Parish Church of Saint Margarett Lothbury in the City of London by whom she hath Children living vizt. Fras . aged about 16 years provided for  Ann aged 14 years or thereabouts & Robt . aged about 9 years both unprovided for that her said Husband Rented the Tap at the Angel Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 15 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of 20 Guineas that he hath quitted said House about 19 years ago since which time her said Husband has not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. saith that her said Husband has Deserted her and refuses to maintain her & his Children she being poor and unable to maintain sam is therefore Obliged to come Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 25th. day of Janry. 1787 R Butler

Jane Morris
