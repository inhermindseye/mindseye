Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Edward Douglass aged about 35 year upon his Oath saith that about Eight years ago h Intermarried with Elizabeth his Present Wife  in a Church in the Tower by Banns & by whom hath Three Children living Vizt. Samuel aged about seven years Elizabeth aged about Three years and Edward aged about Fifteen Month That he this Deponent rented and lived in a house in Playhouse yard Whitecross Street in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middlesex for about nine months at the yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds that he hath quitted said house about Three years since which time he has not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of May 1790  Sampr Wright M Addington

Ed Douglass
