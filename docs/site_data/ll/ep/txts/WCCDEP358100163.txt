Middx to wit Sarah Cooper aged about Twenty eight years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Cambler in Great Russell Street Bloomsbury in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Six Months Years at the yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about 8 years ago since which time time she hath never lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 5th. day of Janry. 1784  Thos. Bishop  Jno: Wright
