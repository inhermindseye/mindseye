Middx  John Burkit aged abt. 42 yrs. upon Oath Saith she was bound Apprentice for Seven years to Mr. Jose. Joyce a Gold Chain maker & Served the last Year of his time with the sd. Mr. Joyce in Old Boswel Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlex and never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year on otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 29th. 1751 before}
