Middx  Dorothy Green  Singlewoman maketh Oath and Saith that about Three Years ago She was hired a Yearly Servant to Mr. Sleap of Giltspur Street in the parish of St. Sepulchres in the Liberty of the City of London and lived there one Year and upwards and received four pounds Ten Shillings a Year Wages And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge

Sworn this 19th. day of April 1746 Before Robert Dennett

her Dorothy Green mark
