Middx  Eliz: Hoare wife of Wm. Hoare upon Oath Saith she Intermarried with the sd Wm Hoare in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn upwds. of Fifteen Years ago by whom she hath one Child now at home with them (viz:) Edward aged upwds. of 5 Years Says her sd. husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr Turner a Carpenter in Carey Street and lay during his whole Apprentiship at Mr Turners in Carey Street aforesd. in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and She hath been informed & verily belives and that her sd. husband never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 29th. Septr 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz: Hoare
