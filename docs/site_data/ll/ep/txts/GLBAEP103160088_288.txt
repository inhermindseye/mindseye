Middlesex ss}  Jane Robinson the widow of Thomas Robinson deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband lived in & rented a house in Little Burr Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And that she this Deponent since his death hath not lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year or been married or paid any Kings Taxes or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or done any other Act to gain a settlement for herself.

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

Jane Robn
