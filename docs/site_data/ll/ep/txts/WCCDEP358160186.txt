The Examination of Hannah Dyke taken the twenty seventh Day of Octr. 1740 Who upon her voluntary Oath Saith she is a Widow  & that on the seventeenth Day of this Inst. Octr. she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Thier Lane in the Parish St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx which sd. Male Bastard Child is now living and likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor & not able to maintain the same & further saith that one Mr. Offree in Dove Court in Lombard Street  Teaman did beget the sd. Child on her Body she this Exaiant at such time liveing as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Harris in Pall Mall & the sd. Mr. Offree being accquainted with her used to call on her & this Exaiant & the sd. Mr. Offree went to the Cardigan Head Tavern by Charing Cross where the sd. Mr. Offree had twice Carnall knowledge of this Exaiants Body at one of which times he got her with the Male Bastard Child she was delivered of as aforesaid & that he the sd. Mr. Offree & no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before} Jas: Gronous

Hannah Dike
