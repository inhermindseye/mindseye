Middlesex ss}  Grace Dean the wife of Daniel Dean maketh oath That her Said husband has informed her & Which She believes to be true That divers Years ago & When a Singleman he was hired & lived as a hired servant with one Mr Garlanda Victualler who kept the Sign of the French Horn & Dolphin in Crutched Fryars in the Parish of St Olave Hart Street in the City of London & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & upwards & has not Since done any Act to gain another Settlement That She has two Children namely William Aged about three years & Hannah Aged about eighteen Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom She was lawfully married & that her Said husband is now absent from her beyond Sea.

Sworn this. 21. day of November 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Grace Dean
