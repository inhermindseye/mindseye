Middx.  Elizth. Wooley aged abt. Sixteen Years upon Oath says She never was Married says She lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Geo: Smith in Long Row in the Markett Place in the Parish of St. Peter in the City of Nottingham for One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. four Months

Sworn the 15th. of Novr. 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth. Wooley
