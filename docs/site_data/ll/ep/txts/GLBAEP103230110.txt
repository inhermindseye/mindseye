Middlesex ss}  Deborah Bland  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre. to Lucretia King of the Parish of St.George in Southwark in the County of Surry Glover & after having served three months of her Apprenticeship there She & her said Mistress removed into the Parish of St Saviour in Southwarke in the Same County where after Serving her said Mistress there for the Space of twelve Months Differences arising between her and her Mistress this Deponts Mother took her away & She never after returned to her Mistress nor did her said Mistress at any time after give her Consent to this Deponents living elsewhere That She this Deponent after leaving her Sd Mistress never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement after leaving the Service of her Sd Mistress.

Sworn this- 22. day of January 1788 before  R Davies  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Deborah Bland
