Middx  Mary Foxcraft aged abt. Fifty Five Years Wido . of Richard Foxcraft upon her Oath saith that about Twenty Seven Years ago she intermarried with her sd. late Husband Richard Foxcraft at a Church in or near Friday Street  London & that her sd. late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Hicks a Druggist in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Three Years or thereabts. but at what Wages this Examinant knoweth not that this Examinants said Husband never after in his Life nor she since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 19th. of October 1741 before}

The mark of Mary Foxcraft
