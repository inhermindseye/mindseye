Middx to wit Mary Carter Aged about Thirty One Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of George Carter with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Anne Westminster about Eighteen Months ago and by whom she hath One Child living namely George Aged about Ten Months Says before her Intermarriage with her said Husband and whilst she was a Singlewoman she had an illegitimate Child by one Saml. Rigley Rigley named Sarah aged about four Years born a Bastard in Cross Keys  Court Chick Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchre London and that her said Child Sarah never gained any other Settlement than by Birth.

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1784 before  Sampr Wright Addington

Mary Carter
