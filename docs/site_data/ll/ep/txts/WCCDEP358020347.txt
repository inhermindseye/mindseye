Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Robert Harris Aged 56 years on thereabouts upon Oath saith that he is the Son of John and Tobitha Harris that he hath been told his father that his Father in his Life time time Rented & lived in an House called then known by the Sign of the Rainbow & Dove in the Minories in the parish of St Botolp without Aldgate  London but what rent his said father paid for the same or how long he kept & resided in the same this Deponent hath never been Informed that his sd. Mother hath also Informed this Deponent that his said father served his Apprenticeship under Indentures  in the Minories in the said parish of St Botolph Aldgate and that since his said father or Mother never have gained any subsequent legal Settlement - And this Deponent further on his Oath saith that he perfectly remembers his said Mother was supported by the said parish of St. Botolph that he never  acquired any settlement in his own right and that he is poor & unable to support himself

Sworn the 28th Octr 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Robert Harris 's Mark
