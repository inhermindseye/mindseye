Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Willis Aged about 28 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or Paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Miss Rankin at Hackney in the Parish of St. John at Hackney in the County of Middlesex for 14 Months at the yearly Wages of Ten Guineas hath quitted said Service about 3 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain any other Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that on the 5th. day of July last she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr Burn in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been half baptized by the Name of Martha That one John a Butcher lives at Hadley whose Name she does not know is the father of the said Child he have

had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body And begotten the same thereon And that the said John and no one else did beget the said Child that he is the only Real and True father thereof And that she is Poor and unable to maintain the same

Sworn this 26th. day of Septr. 1786 }

her Jane Willis Mark

never Sworn
