Westmr.  Catharine Pallet Aged about Sixty Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Robert Pallet deceased; says that since her said Husbands Death she Rented a House in Crown Court in the Precinct of While Fryars London , at the Yearly Rent of Seven Pounds and paid Parochial Taxes for the same, and after she quitted same, never Rented, House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 11th. day of April 1768 Before}  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Catharine Pallet
