St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Res Catherine Rossat  Wife of Ferdinand Rossat Aged 22 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that about five Months ago she intermarried with her present Husband Ferdinand Rossat at the parish Church of St James in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex That Previous thereto and while he was single and unmarried not having Child or Children he hired was living as an hired himself as an a Yearly Servant in the capacity of Usher to Mr. Freselion Love Lane in the parish of Wandworth in the County of Surry sometime in the spring or beginning of the summer of the 1790 at the yearly income or Wages of £25 and so continued to live with his said Marte till about 24th June  now last when he quitted such service that he bearded and lodged all the time at his said Master's House during his continuance with him and since has not acquired any subsequent legal Settlement And further on her Oath saith that her said Husband Deserted and left her destitute on Friday last and she has not where with and is at present unable to maintain herself.

Sworn the 10th day of July 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Rossat
