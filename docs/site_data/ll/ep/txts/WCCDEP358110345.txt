Middx.  Alice Harthway upon Oath Saith she is the wife of John Harthway who served his time wth. his father at Beckingham in the County of Kent  & never Rented ten Pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year as this Examint. has been informed & believes

Sworn Novr. 12th 1753 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Alice Harthway
