Middlesex ss}  Henry Allibone maketh oath that divers years ago his mother Margaret Allibone  widow after the death of his late Father Henry Allibone deced lived in and rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds in that Part of Knights bridge as lies in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the Said County of Middlesex and resided thereon for the Space of Ten Years That to this Deponents Knowledge or belief his Said Mother has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or been married or done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 13. day of February 1786 before R Davies Robt. Smith

Henry Allibon
