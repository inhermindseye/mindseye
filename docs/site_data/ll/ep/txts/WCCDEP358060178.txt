Westmr.  Chas. Bailey upon Oath saith he has his Children by Ann Bailey his late Wife derd. Sarah aged upwds. of 16 & Chas . aged upwds. of 9 Years the latter of which is at present extremely ill and that he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Guineas in Kings Arms Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for three Years or thereabouts and quitted the same about 12 Years ago & has not Since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 3d. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Charles Bayley
