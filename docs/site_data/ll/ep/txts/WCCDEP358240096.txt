St Clement Danes May 4th. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Charlotte Tong Aged 29 years and upwards Wife of Thomas Isaac Tong Upon Oath Saith that She was Married to her present Husband Thomas Isaac Tong at the parish Church of St. Luke Old Street Middx about Nine years ago that She hath one Child by her said Husband now living by her said Named Thomas born on the 3rd day of October 1790 and that She is now pregnant with the other that her said Husband left her on the 19th April last under pretence to take place for London and She hath never heard where he went, knows not where he is That her said Husband Served his Apprenticeship of Seven years with Mr Hawkins in Little Shire Lane and She hath been informed and belives and that he lodged in his said Master House situate in the said parish of St Clement Danes And this Deponent Saith he hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge Information or belief And that She is poor & unable to Support herself without Assistance.

Sworn the 4th. May 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Charlotte Tong 's Mark

Witness Edwd. Burnbridge
