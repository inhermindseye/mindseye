Middx to wit Elizabeth Grist Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Barnett at the Cheshire Cheese in Little Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two and Three Quarters of a year at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said service Upwards of Twelve Months ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says She is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes She being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born and that one Edward Chambers a Journeyman to one Mr. Polly a Butcher in Newport Alley with Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body the latter End of December and believes it was on last Christmas day And at Two different time of towards in her said Masters House at Knights bridge & at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which She is now Pregnant And the said Edward Chambers & no Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 9th. day of Augt. 1780 before R Butler

Elizabeth Grist
