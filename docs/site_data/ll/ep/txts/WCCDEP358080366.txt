Middlesex to wit Sarah Wildbore aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath say's she is the Wife of William Wildbore (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish of Speldurst in the County of Kent about Eleven Years ago and by whom she hath Three Children now living Namely Thomas aged upwards of Eight Years Mary aged upwards of Six Years and William aged about Two Years Says her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Tourndd a Staymaker in Saint Martins Street in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and served his said Master under said Indentures for the Space of Six Years when her said Husband bought out the remaining part of his said Apprenticeship and after her said Husband bought out his said Time he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 15th- day of June 1779 before}

Capt  Ormond Thompson on Board the Rye Man of Warr .
