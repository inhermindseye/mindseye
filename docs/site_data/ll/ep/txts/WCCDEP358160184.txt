Middx  The Examination of Eliz : the Wife of Robt. Williams lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 25th. Day of Octr. 1740 Who upon her Oath Saith that abt. twelve Years since she Intermarryed with the above named Robt. Williams & that he the sd. Robert before his Intermarriage with this Exaiant & while he was a Single Man lived a yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Castle at the White Hart Tavern the Corner of Warwick Court in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above Barrs in the County of Middx for the Space of two Years or thereabts. as this Exaiants sd. Husband Inform'd her & as she verily beleives & further Saith that her sd. Husband hath not since that time rented any House pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that her sd. Husband is now a Lunatick & she being Poor is not able to maintain him and herself.

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Wm Hayton  Jas: Gronous

the Mark of Elizabeth Williams
