Middlesex ss.  Sarah Patrick the Widow of Jas. Patrick maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband on or about the Tenth Day of September 1758 at  Portze in the County of Southampton that her said Husband has informed her and which she believes to be true that he was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to Mr . John Nethercote of Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Baker and served his said Apprenticeship with his said Master there except the last Six Months when he went on Board a Man of War , for which time his Master received his Wages, that her said Husband has not since such his Apprenticeship lived in and Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds a Year or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor has she since her said Husbands Death done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right that she has Two Children living Namely Elizh . aged about Seventeen Years and James aged about Twelve Years the lawful Issue of her said Husband who have not done any Act to gain a Settlemt. for themselves

Sworn this Day of March 1789 before}
