Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Cook aged about 17 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was Married never bound apprent lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months rented any house tenement or Lodging of ten Pounds a year or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement in her own Right That she hath been Informed which Information she verily believes to be true That her Father Henry Cook served sev years apprenticeship to one Mr Jones a Millwright at Broad Wall  Lambeth in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry That after he was out of his time he never rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or Belief

Sworn this 29th day of October 1800 before us } Saml. Edwards  John Collick

Ann Cook
