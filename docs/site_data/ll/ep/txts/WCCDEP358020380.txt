St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Ann Riley  Singlewoman Aged 30 years & thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married; that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with with Mr. Allen at No. 5 in Little Chappell Street in the parish of St. Ann within the Liberty of Westminster in the said County for about fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds, that she quitted that service about Two years ago, and since hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement, And this Deponent with that she hath been Maintained in the Workhouse belonging to the said parish of St. Ann for about three Months at the Expenced of the said Parish by Virtue of an Order of Reml. from the Parish of St. Mary le bow about four years and an half ago-Against which Order there hath been no Appeal preferred, to the Knowledge or belief of this Deponent

Sworn the 27th. Janry 1792 before us D Walker R Butler

Ann Reiley
