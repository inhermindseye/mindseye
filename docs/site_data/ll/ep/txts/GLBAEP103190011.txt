Middlesex ss}  Mary Jaimeson  Singlewoman maketh oath that sometime ago she was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Mr Pailthorp a Taylor in Kingstreet in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County at Certain wages and Continued in sch service under such hiring for the Space of Thirteen Months That she this Deponent has not since lived as a hired servant for the space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain a Subsequent settlement.

Sworn this. 25. day of Febry 1780 before  Jno Sherwood

Maray Jaimeson
