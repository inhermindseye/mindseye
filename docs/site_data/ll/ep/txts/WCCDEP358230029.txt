Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Joseph Bradbury aged about 31 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 25 day of December 1787 he intermarried with Rosetta his present Wife at the parish Church of St. Mary le Strand by whom he hath Four Children living Vizt: Catherine aged about 9 Years & a Quarter, Joseph aged about 2 years & 4 months, Mary aged about 1 year & nine months & another not yet baptized, That he this Deponent lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Walton at the sign of the Bull in Milford Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for five years & upwards at the yearly Wages of 5 pounds that he hath quitted said Service about 18 years since which time he hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for twelve months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes a done any other Act matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 4th. day of January 1799 before us}

J Bradbury
