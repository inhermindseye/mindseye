Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Ditcher aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or Paid Taxes in That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Blassom a Carpenter in Leopards Court in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex upwards of Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds That she hath quitted said Service about Ten years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Decr. 1794 before J: hlind J Pownall

her Mary Ditcher Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
