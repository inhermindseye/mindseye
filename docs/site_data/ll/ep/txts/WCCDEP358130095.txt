Middx  Catherine Scoales Wife of John Scoales now in the Marshalsea Prison for Debt upon Oath saith She Intermarryed with the said John Scoales in the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn on the 20th. of April upwards of Ten Years since by whom She has two Children (to wit) Peter Henry aged about Seven Years & Ann aged upwards of Ten Months Says her said Husband Rented a House of £40. a Year the corner of Brewers Yard in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for five Years or thereabouts and hath quitted the same upwards of Five Years and afterwards Rented a seperate Appartment of 15 or £20. a Year in the same Parish and lived in the same near one Year and quitted it about four Years and has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year nor paid Parish Taxes elsewhere

Sworn the 16th. Day of Decr. 1755 before} Theodr. Sydenham  Saunder Welch

Catha: Scoales
