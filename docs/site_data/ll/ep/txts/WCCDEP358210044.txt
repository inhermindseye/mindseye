Westmr.  Elizabeth Moon aged about 31 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Robert Moon (gone from her) with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Allhallows Barking London about Eighteen Months ago; says she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true that about fifteen Years ago her said Husband Rented a house in little Peter Street in the Parish of St. John the Evangelist in the City of Westminister of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and hath not since he quitted said house Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 17th, April 1766, before} Fran. Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizabeth Moon
