Middlesex  Jane Godwin  Wido : of Wm. Godwin (who hath been dead about five Years removed from the parish of St. Botolph Bishopgate by Order under the hands & Seales of two of his Majestys Justices of the peace for the City of London dated the 30th. Day of Septr. last upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Willm. Godwin about Sixteen Years ago at the Fleet London Says her sd. late husband Served a one year & upwards the letter part of his Apprentiship with a Bed Lacemaker in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never was a Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place as she hath been informed & beleives Says she hath not since the Death of her said late Husband rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif Says she was removed from the parish of St. Botolph Aldgate to the sd. parish of St Clemt. Danes by Order dated the 19th. Day of Janry 1744 which Order was not appealed against

Sworn Octr. 3d 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Jane Godwin
