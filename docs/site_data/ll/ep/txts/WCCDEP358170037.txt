Middx  The Examination of Alice the Wife of Gyles Gardner lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye.. 7th- Day of April 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that her Husband Gyles Gardner Served One Year & upwards the latter Part of his Apprentishipp with one Sr. John Sidney in the Strand in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes Watchmaker And further on her Oath that her said Husband rented a House in the Parish of St. Lawrence in the County of Southampton of the Yearly Rent of Six Pounds Six Shillings for the Space of three Years & paid to the Watch & Minister of the sd- Parish & served the Office of Surveyer of the Common there that her sd. Husband never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. to the Poor of any Parish lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- to her knowledge or Beliefe yt. her sd. Husband lives now in the Isle of Wight Guernsey & is very Poor & She being left with Two Children (to wit) Elizth - aged abt. Thirteen Yrs- & Wm - aged Four Yrs- is not able to maintaine herself & them

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Alce Gardner
