Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Pratt the widow of William Pratt deced maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late husband nor of his Parents or either of them nor Can this Deponent give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late Father Thirst deced But this Deponent has been informed by her late Mother Elizabeth Thirst deced (whose Maiden name was Shower ) & which Information She believes to be true That her said Mother While a Singlewoman was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Mrs Ford in Houndsditch in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishopsgate in the City of London & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one year & half & this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting her said Mothers Settlement that She this Deponent was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that she hath Five Children namely Joseph Aged about Twelve years & Nine Months John Aged about Eight years William Aged about Seven years Thomas Aged about Three years & Elizabeth Aged about Four Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this 2d day of April 1774 before Cha: Digby Wilmot

The Mark of Elizabeth Pratt
