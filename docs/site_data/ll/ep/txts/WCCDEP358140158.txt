Middx. and Westmr.}  Mary Pointer aged abt. 26 Years upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Christopher Lorrain a Taylor the back side of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and Liberty of Westmr. for Fifteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £4. and hath quitted said Service abt. 7 Years ago since which time she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year. And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be

born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes where she now lodges she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born says that one Patrick Fagan a Smith in Little Hart Street Covent Garden did beget the said Child or Children on her body he the said Patrick Fagan having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body at the house of Mr. Stevens a Salesman in Bread St. Giles's in the Month of January last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the sd. Patrick Fagan and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 13th. day of Septr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Pointer
