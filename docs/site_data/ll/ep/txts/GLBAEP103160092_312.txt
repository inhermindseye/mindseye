Middlesex ss}  Esther Heritage aged Sixty one years & upward the widow of Edward Heritage deced maketh oath That divers years ago her Said late husband lived as a hired servant by the year When a Singleman as he has informed this Deponent & as She believes with one Thomas Brewer in the Parish of Saint John in the Devizees in the County of Wilts  Horse Dealer and Continued in such service for many years at certain yearly Wages & by means thereof as she is advised gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her Said late husband did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement in any other Parish or Place Nor hath she this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right & that She was lawfully Married to her Sd husband

Sworn this 22d day of May 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Esther Heritage
