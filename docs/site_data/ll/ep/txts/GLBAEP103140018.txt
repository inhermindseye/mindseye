Middlesex ss}  Margery Crichton the Wife of  James Crichton  mariner who is now absent from her at Sea maketh oath that about Six years ago her said husband took & rented a house of the yearly rent & value of Ten Pounds in new Gravel Lane in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell in the County of Middlesex & whereon his sd Wife & family Continued & resided for the Space of Ten Weeks & by means thereof as she this Depont is advised her sd husband gained a legal settlement there that her Said husband had not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds not served any Parish office nor paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to her knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She hath two Children namely Thomas aged about nine years & James aged about three years the lawful issue of this Deponent by her said husband

Sworn this 7th. day of January 1773 before  Cha: Digby Wilmot

Margery Crichton
