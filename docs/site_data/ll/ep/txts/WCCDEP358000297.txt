Saint Clement Danes

Westmr.} to wit Mary Douglass aged about 73 years upon her Oath saith That she Intermarried with George Douglass at the Parish Church of Saint Margaretts Westmr . about 20 years ago That he hath been dead about 15 years and by whom She hath no Child or Children living That her Said Husband Rented and lived in a first floor of a Mr. Rothery in Twik Street Chelsea in the Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea in the County of Middlesex for One year at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds that he quitted said Lodging about 2 years before his Death that he did no other Act whereby he may have gained any subsequent Settlement nor hath this Ext. done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained any Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of June 1786 before  Sampr Wright N Bond

her Mary Douglass Mark
