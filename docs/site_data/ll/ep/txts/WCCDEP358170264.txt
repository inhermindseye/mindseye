Middx.  Sarah Flowers  Wife of James Flowers upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with the said James Flowers on or about the 28th Day of March abt. 48 Yrs. since at St. Bennett Pauls wharfe  London and that her said Husband James Flowers lived in & rented a House in Suffolk Street in the Parish of St. George in Southwark in the County of Surry for the Space of Eight Years & upwards at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds that her said Husband hath been abroad & beyond the Sea Eight Years & this Exainant hath not heard from him for Five Years last past that since the said James Flowers left the said House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beliefe

Sworne 3d. Septr. 1742 before} Houlson W. Hayton

The Mark of Sarah Flowers
