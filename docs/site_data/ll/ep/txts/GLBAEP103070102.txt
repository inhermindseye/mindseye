Middx ss  Hannah Hines the Wife of Thomas Hines (which sd. Thomas Hines is now on a voyage to India ) Maketh Oath that her sd husband hath often informed her that about sixteen years ago he was bound apprentice by Indre to one Mr Marsh a Barber and Peruke maker who then lived in the Old Change facing the sign of the Kings Head in the parish of St. Mary Magdalen old Fish Street in the City of London And that he Continued in such Service for the space of one year and then inlisted himself for a soldier which information this Dept believes to be true And this Deponent says that her sd husband has not since gained any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge information or belief And that she has three Children to wit George aged abt. five years Sarah aged abt. twenty Months & James aged about three Months the lawful issue of this Dept. & her sd. husband

Sworn this 9th day of April 1754 before Richd: Ricards  Chris Scott .

Hannah Hines  his Mark
