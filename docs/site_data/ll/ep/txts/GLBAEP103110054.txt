Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Collins of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Widow taken on oath before me Charles Digby Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Eighth Day of May 1772

Who on her oath Saith That on the Twenty third Day of April one thousand Seven hundred and Seventy at the house of William George in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County She the said Ann Collins was delivered of a Male Bastard Child Since Baptized and Registred by the name of James Oglevie and that the Said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that James Oglevie of the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the Said County Mariner did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this 8th Day of May 1772 before me  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Ann Collins
