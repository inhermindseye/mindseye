Woman settled in St Cl

Saint Clement Danes April 11th. 1793

Middx and Westminster }  Pregnancy  Reputed Father  Sophia Augusta Hunt aged 22 or thereabouts Spinster upon her oath saith that she never was Married And voluntarily saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes when Born And that Joseph Taylor of Great Shire Lane in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes Broker did beget the said Child or Children on the Body of her this Deponent he having had carnal knowledge of her Body on or about the 28th. day of August in the Year of our Lord 1792 and at Several Times since, at some or one of which said times he did beget the said Child or Children Where with she now is pregnant and that he the said Joseph Taylor is the Real true Father of such Child or Children.

Sworn the 11th. day of April 1793 before} D Walker

Sophia Augusta Hunt
