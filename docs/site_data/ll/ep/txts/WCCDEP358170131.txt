Middx  Hanah Manser upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman and was never married & that abt. Five Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mrs. Ann Rutter Linnen Draper in Conduit Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middx for the Space of One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 15th- of Septembr- 1741 } Jas: Gronous

hannah manser
