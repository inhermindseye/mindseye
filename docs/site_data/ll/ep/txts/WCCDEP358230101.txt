Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit David Lloyd Aged about 59 years upon his Oath saith that on or abouts the year 1772 he intermarried with his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Field That he this Deponent rented and lived in a house in little suffolk street in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for three years and the yearly rent of Sixteen Pounds that he left the said House about 26 years since which time he hath not rented any house tenement on Lodging of ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th day of Decr 1799 before us J Reeves Saml Edwards .

David Lloyd
