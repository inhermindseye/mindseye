Middx  Ann Brown aged upwds. of 40 years upon Oath saith She never was married and that She lived a yearly Servt. with Mr Champ in High Street in the Parish of Allhallows in Oxford at the yearly wages of £2 for two Years or thereabts. quitted her sd. Service abt. 11 Yrs. ago & has not since rented ten Pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn May 26th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Ann Brown
