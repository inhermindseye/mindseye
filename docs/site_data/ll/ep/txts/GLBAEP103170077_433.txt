Middlesex ss}  Mary Lighton  Singlewoman maketh oath that sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at certain wages by & with Abigail Macky of Lower East Smithfield in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said county widow & continued in Such Service for near the Space of one year and a quarter and by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year else where nor was She ever Married nor has She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent is now with Child & that the said child is likely to be born a Bastard & to be chargeable to the said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that one Lawrence Powers late of the same parish mariner & Make of the Ship Salley Captain Holland Commander & now on a Voyage to the Grenades is the Father of the said child

Sworn this 12 day of May 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Lighton
