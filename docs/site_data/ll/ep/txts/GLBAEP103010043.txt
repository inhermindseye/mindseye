Middlesex ss.  William Kent maketh Oath that about Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to John Bailey of Bulters Buildings in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Gun Maker for Seven Years that after he had served about Three Years of his said Apprentice his said Master died and he continued with his Mistress there under the said Indenture about Two Months after when she declined carrying on the Business that he was not turned over by his said Mistress to any Person whatever to serve the remainder of the time then to come in the said Indenture, that he has never Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any Parish or Place for the Space of One Year together or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement subsequent to his said Apprenticeship

Sworn this Day of January 1791 before}
