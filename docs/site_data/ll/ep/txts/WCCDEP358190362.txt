Middx  Jane Bayers Aged abt 48 Years Upon Oath Says she was never Marryed and that she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mrs. Lewis at at Cook Shop in Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middx upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of 3 Pounds quitted her said Service about Twenty Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert.

Sworn the 10th of June 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jane Bayers
