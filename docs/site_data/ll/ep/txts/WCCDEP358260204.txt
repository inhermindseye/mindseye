Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Pollard aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Townsend a Silkmercer in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years at the yearly wages of Nine Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Nine years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge she might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 17th.. day of May 1797 before me } J Reeves

Ann Pollard
