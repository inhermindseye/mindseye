Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Hemming of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the said County this fourth day of November 1783 who Saith that She is now with Child & that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard & to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that John Russell of the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate a> Polisher is the Father of the Said Child

Taken & Signed the day and year above written before me R Davies

Mary Hemming
