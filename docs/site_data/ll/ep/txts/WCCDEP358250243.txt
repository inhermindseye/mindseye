Saint Clement Danes June 1st. 1796

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Tutton Aged thirty three years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that She never was Married that She lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr Hawks of Ryder Street in the Parish of St. James Westminster in the County of Middlesex  Tallow Chandler for nearly two years at the yearly Wages of Eight Guas Per Annum that She quitted the said Service abt. Six years Ago that She hath not Since she Quitted the Same lived as hired Servant twelve Months ago Rented ten Pounds a year or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have Gained a Settlement to the best of her knowledge or Belief

Sworn before Us Richd. Ford N Bond

Sarah Tutton
