St. Clement Danes.

Middlesex (to wit) Susannah Parmenter aged 20 or thereabouts and Ann Parmenter aged 40 or thereabouts severally make Oath and Say and first this Deponent Parmenter upon her Oath Saith that she intermarried with Ambrose Parmenter her present Husband at the Parish Church of St. Giles's in the Fields about 21 years yo That her Husband was Servant to Mr. De Gray of Lincolns Inn Fields afterwards Lord Walshingham for upward of 18 Months that he lived there as a hired yearly Servant at Sixteen Pounds a year as she believe which Service was before her intermarried with Rem That they took Lodgings in St. Giles in the Fields , that the other Deponent is the Legitimate Dain and that her said Husband has done no Act to gain a Settlement said he left Mr De Greys. And this Deponent Susannah Parmenter upon her Oath Saith That she left her Father & Mother about 3 or 4 Months ago. since which she has done no Act to gain a Settlement.

Sworn before us this 28 April 1790 . R Butler D Walker

Susannah The Mark Ann Parmenter
