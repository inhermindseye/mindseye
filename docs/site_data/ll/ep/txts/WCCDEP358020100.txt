Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Michael aged upwards of Three years and Mary aged about Eighteen Months Two Infants Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Two Infants were both born Bastards on the Body of Jane Wheeler in the House of a Mr. Stevens the corner of Bear Yard Lincolns Inn Fields in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 5th. day of Septr. 1788  Gs Bradshaw J Barnfather

Benj: Newman
