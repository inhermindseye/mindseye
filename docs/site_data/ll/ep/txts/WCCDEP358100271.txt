Middlesex and Westminster}  Eleanor Strahan aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith That she never was Married rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as an Hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Clark a Grocer in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint John wapping in the County of Middlesex for about 15 Months at the yearly Wages of £6 That she quitted her said Service (about years ago since which time She has not gained any other settlement to the best of her Knowledge (or Belief

Sworn this 26th day of Octr. 1784 before us}

{never come to be Sworn}
