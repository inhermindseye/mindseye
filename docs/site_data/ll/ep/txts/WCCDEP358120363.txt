Middx  Ann Grogan aged near 54 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Patrick Grogan to whom She was married at the Roman Catholick Chapel in Duke Strt.  Lincolns Inn Fields near 30 Years ago that her said husband has been dead abt. 10 Years and her sd late husband rented a house of the yearly Rent of £18 in Houghton Strt. in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes for 3 Years left it abt. 14 yrs. ago & never afterwards nor has this Examinant since his decease rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn June 19th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Grogan
