Middx.  Henry Waller aged abt. 22 Yrs. upon Oath says he intermarried with Elizth . his wife at the Savoy Chapel on the 12th. day of May 1756 by whom he has two Childn. Henry aged abt. one Year and a half and a Male Child aged abt. three weeks who is yet Unbaptized says before his intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Alexr. Hilman a farmer for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds or thereabouts says he served the said Mr. Hilman the last Nine Months of his said Service in the Parish of Horsted Keynes in the County of Sussex and after he quitted his said Service Never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Yr.

Sworn this 5th. of Novr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Henry Waller
