Middx  Eliz Wynn upon her voluntary Oath saith she is the widow of James Wynn who dyed in the Year 1751 and that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex  she being poor and unable to maintain the Same when born and that Roger Russell a Taylor did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the said Roger Russell having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant Sevl. times in the months of February March & April last (during which time he Cohabited with this Examinant as her husband) at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Roger Russell and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn July 17th. 1755 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Eliz Wynn
