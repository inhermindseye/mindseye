Middx to wit Mary Hallaway Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired yearly Servant with Colonel Roberts in Holles Street Cavendish Square in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for three Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Months ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1780 before R Butler G Mercer

Mary Hallay
