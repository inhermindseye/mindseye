Middlesex ss}  Sarah Boston the widow of George Boston  deced maketh oath that Since the decease of her said late husband & during her Widow hood She was hired by & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Yearly Wages with one Mr Rimer in King Street in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & Continued there in Such Service for the Space of Three Years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been married or rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 4th day of February 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of Sarah Boston
