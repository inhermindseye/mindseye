Middx  The Exaicon of Elizth. Loveseye  Wido . of Joseph Loveseye deced Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 4th. day of March 1739 who upon her Oath Saith that upwds. of Twenty four Years ago she Intermarryed with the above named Joseph Loveseye and that her sd. late Husband before his Intermarryage with this Exaiant & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . at the Maiden head Inn in Dyott Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the sd. County of Middx for the Space of one Yeare as her sd. late Husband informed her & as She verily beleives And further that her sd. husband in his lifetime or this Exaiant Since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Yeare pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yeare or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife and being Old & Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the day & Year above named before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

The mark of Elizth. Loveseye
