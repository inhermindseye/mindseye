Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Martin of the Parish of Saint Botolph witht. Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  single woman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr.

one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this 27th-day of January in the year of our Lord 1767 .

Who on her Oath Saith that she is now with Child And that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that one Samuel Kidd of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster in the said County of Middlesex Cork Cutter is the Father of the Said Child.

Sworn this 27 day of January 1767 before  Chris: Scott

Mary Martin
