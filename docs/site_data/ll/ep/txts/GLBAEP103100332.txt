Middlesex ss}  Mary Farrer  singlewoman Maketh oath That she is the Daughter of Edward Farrer by Alice his wife both deced And this Depont. Saith that her said late Mother has informed her (& which information she believes to be true) That divers Years ago her Father Rented a house in Maudlins Rents in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Five Pounds or thereabouts and that during such her Fathers Residence there he was Charged to and Paid the Kings Tax for the same And this Depont. Cannot Set forth wheth her Said Father; or her Mother after his Death; ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And this Depont. farther Saith that she was born in Maudlins Rents aforesaid And that she never was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 22 day of Octr 1770 before  Chris. Scott

The Mark of Mary Farrer
