Middx  Elizabeth Maxwell aged about 79 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of James Maxwell That since his Death she this Examinant lived in and Rented a house in Plough Court Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about Four Years at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds Quitted the Same about Twenty Years ago and hath not since Rented any House paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes or been a hired Servant for One Year

Sworn the 24th day of Octor. 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizabeth Eliz: Maxwell
