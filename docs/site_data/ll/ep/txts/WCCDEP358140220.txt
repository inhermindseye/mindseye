Middx.  Sarah Williams aged abt. 22 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was Married Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt . for one Year says her father Richd. Williams Served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with Mr. Peters a Painter in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes; as she has Credibly been informed & as she Verily believes

Sworn Feby. 2d. 1759 . before } Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

Sarah Williams
