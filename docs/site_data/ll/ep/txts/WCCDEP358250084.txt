St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } (to wit) Susan Cremer or Heyser aged 50 years or thereabts. Upon Oath saith that she never was Married according to the Rights & Ceremonies of the Church of England that she this Deponent never acquired any settlement in her own right that she hath been informed which she verily believes to be true that her Father Rented and resided in an Apartment in Queen Street of Lincolns Fields in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex
