Middx.  Joseph Jarvis aged abt. 54 Years upon Oath says he Rented and lived in a house the Corner of Crooked Lane in the parish of St. Margaret New fish Street London for three Years at the Yearly Rent of Thirty pounds or thereabouts and hath quitted said house about fourteen Years and has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 24th. Novr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Jos: Jarvis
