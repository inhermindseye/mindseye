Alex Dawson on behalf of Thomas Hasledine upon Oath saith he has been Informed & verily believes the said Thos Hasledine rented the Crown Alehouse in Hatton Garden in the Liberty of Saffron hill  Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the parish of St. Andrew in Holborn in the County of Middlx at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds lived therein Sevl. Years & quitted the same abt. Ten Years ago since which he has not rented ten pounds by the Year nor pd. parish taxes to this Examints. knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 27th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell Alex Dawson
