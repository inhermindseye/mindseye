Middlesex ss}  Mary Haydon the new wife of Richard Haydon & late the wife of Thomas Lacey deced maketh oath that She well knows a Woman named Sarah Tollis the widow of one Thomas Tollis deced now resident in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate Middlesex That Several years ago the Said Thomas Tollis was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with this Deponents Said late husband Thomas Lacey in the Parish of Saint John at Timber Hill in the City of Nor wic h at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for many Years And did not afterwards to this Deponts knowledge information or belief do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere and this Depont believes that the Said Sarah Tollis was lawfully married to her Sd late husband Thomas Tollis and that Since his death She hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this. 13. day of November 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Haydon
