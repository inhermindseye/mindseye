Middx.  Ann Spencer aged abt. Twenty Seven Years upon Oath says she never was Married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Anthony Inman at the Horse Shoe & Magpye in Hollis Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx. for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abts. Two Years since which time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 28th. Sepr. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Spencer
