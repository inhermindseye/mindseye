Middx  Thos. Rutter aged about 50 Years upon Oath saith he was married at the Fleet London on the 20th. Janry 1728 to his Wife Ann and that he lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Eggleston at Knights Coffee house in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £6. Dyet Washing & Lodging for one Year & upwards whilst a Singleman & has not since he quitted his sd. Service rented Ten Pounds by the Year nor pd. Parish taxes

Sworn Janry 22d. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

Thos Rutter
