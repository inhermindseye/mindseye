Middx  Sarah Peirce aged about Thirty Six Years, upon Oath says She was never married, that She never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes, or was a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year. Says her Father Saml. Peirce Rented a House near Exeter Exchange in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. County of Middx. of Ye. Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or thereabouts, & lived in Ye. same sevl. Years, & there Died, Says her Mother Tabitha Peirce continued in ye. same House about one Year after ye. Death of this Examinants Father & then quitted ye. same, & never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year, or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 14th: Day of May 1756 . before}
