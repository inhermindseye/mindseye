Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Griffin aged about 16 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the Seventeenth day of February 1795 She bound herself Apprentice by Indentures duly Executed to one Sarah Blunt of Fryars Place Acton in the Parish of Acton in the County of Middlesex for Seven Years That she Served her said Mistress under such Indenture for Eighteen Months or thereabouts when she and her said Mistress not agreeing her said Mistress Gave up her Indentures and the remained of her time that since she Quitted her said Mistress she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Augt. 1797 before us} N Bond 

Jane Griffin
