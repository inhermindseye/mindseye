Westmr. 

Eliz; Fosberry aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 12 Yrs. ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Watkins in Spur Alley and Villiers Street  in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for Eight Years or there abouts and has not since she quitted the sd. Mr Watkins Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Ser for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 7th. Augt. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz Fosberry
