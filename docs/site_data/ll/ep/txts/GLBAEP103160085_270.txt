Middlesex ss}  Mary Badman the widow of Joseph Badman deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her & Which She believes to be true That he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprentice ship with one William Gearey in Seven Star Court in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Cordwainer & did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge information or belief Rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement nor hath she this Depont Since his death been married or done any Act to gain a settlemt for herself

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Badman
