Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Smith aged about 58 Years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with William Smith her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Navington That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house on Great Saffron Hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex for Six Months at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Guineas that he hath quitted said House about Six years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1799 before us  Saml. Edwards Menter

her Margaret Smith mark

H Day
