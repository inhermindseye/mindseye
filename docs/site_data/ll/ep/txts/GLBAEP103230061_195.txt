Middlesex ss  John Quarrier maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with James Moody who kept the Gentleman & Porter Ale house in Well Street in the Parish of St Giles Without Cripplegate in the City of London and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of fifteen months that the has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of July 1787 before  R Davies  Robt: Smith

John Quarrier
