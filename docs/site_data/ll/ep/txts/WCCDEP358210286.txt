Middx & Westmr.  Mary Perry Aged about Thirty Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Largent at the Three Tunns Eating House in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds, quitted same about Eight or Nine Years ago, and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her  Oath says that on the Sixth day of October 1764, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Cow Cross in the Parish of St. Sepulchre in the County of Middlesex , which hath been baptized by the Name of John , and that on the Tenth day of April, 1768, she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Sawyers Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middlesex , which hath been baptized by the Name of Anne , Says that Robert Bryan who is the Reputed Father of the said Children is gone to the East Indies in the service of the East India Company, And she being Poor is not able to maintain herself and Children

Sworn the 13th, day of June 1768 before}  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Mary Perry
