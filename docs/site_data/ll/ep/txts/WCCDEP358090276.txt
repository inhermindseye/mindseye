Middx to wit Mary Stokes aged about 27 Years on her Oath Says she never was married rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with the Revd . John Douglass  Doctor in Divinity in Amen Corner in the Parish of Lekrist Church London upwards of Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds but her said Master having a Country House in the Cloysters Windsor this Examinant went there & resided & lived with him for the Space of Six Months or upwards & was discharged from thence which is now about Twelve Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement either by living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year or otherwise howsoever. And further on her Oath says that on the 15th. day of January last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of St. Clement Danes which has since been Baptized by the name of John and is now chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further that one John Morris a Porter in the Shop of a Cheesmonger in White Chapel and who Lodges at No. 15 Skinner Street White Chapel is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at the House of Mr. Groom a Confectioner in Fleet Street in July last but the particular day she does not now Remember and at one other time afterwards at one of which he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said John Morris & no other person did beget the same and that  the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 13th. day of February 82 before R Butler

Mary Stokes
