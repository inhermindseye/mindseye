Westmr. to wit Dorothy Gey Aged about Sixty Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Gey who has been Dead about Six Months and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew in the County of Middlesex about 27 Years Ago says her said Husband before his said Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wm. Dolley an Iron Monger in Holbourn aforesaid above the Bars Upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Nine Pounds as her said Husband has often informed her And which Information She verily beleives to be true and after he Quitted his said Service he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1770 before Us. R. Butler W Kelynge

Dorothy Gey
