St. Clemt. Danes

sent to St. James's 15 decr. 1785.}  David Read aged upwds. of 21 years upon his Oath saith that he was bound an apprentice to one James Smith of Peter James Street in the Parish of St.  James in the Liberty of Westmr. that he served his said Master upwds. of 2 years and an half that then his said Master broke & after his quitted said Master he has not been in any Regular employ whereby he may have gained a Settlmt to the best of his Knowledge & belief

Sworn this 15 day of Decr. 1785 before me  Rt. Taylor  James Croft

David Read
