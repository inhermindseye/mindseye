Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Terry aged about 38 years upon his Oath saith that about Six years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom he hath no Child or Children living That he this Deponent was born at Crundell in the Parish of Crundell in the County of Hants that he never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained any other settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of Augt. 1797 before us}

his William Terry Mark

This Pauper when taken to be Sworn by Newman the Beadle acknowledge he never was Married so that part of the Examinants expunged by
