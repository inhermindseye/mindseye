Middx  Mary Ann Ragdell  Widow upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband  Thos. Ragdell in the parish Church of Fulham abt. 19 Years since who hath been Dead about Two Years & a half that the sd. Thomas Ragdell lived in & rented a House in Blackmoore Street in the parish  of St Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £20: & lived in the same for the Space of 10 Years or thereabouts & there dyed that she hath left the sd. House upwards of 2 Years, since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath one Child viz: Eleanor aged 9 Years & being poor is not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworne 8th. May 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Ann Ragdell
