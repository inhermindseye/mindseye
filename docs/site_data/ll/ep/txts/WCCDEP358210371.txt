Westmr.  Mary Rogers Aged about Twenty One Years lodging at Mrs. Clarks in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. upon Oath Says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Mead at the Sign of the Naggs head in Drum Alley in the Parish of Saint Clements Danes for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Years and hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one John Talbott a Chairman who lodges in Haughton Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes had Carnal Knowledge of her Body one Sunday Evening in the Fields between Islington and Bloomsbury sometime the latter end of Septr or the begining of Octr. last past but the Particular Day this Examinant cannot ascertain at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Talbott and no other Person did beget the Same

Sworn this 7th. day of April 1769 before me} Sam Wright

her Mary Rogers Mark Witness Petr Sidebotham
