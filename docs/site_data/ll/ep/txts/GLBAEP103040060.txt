Middlesex ss.  Jane Winch  Singlewoman maketh Oath that She has been informed by her Mother that her late Father Giles Winch decd. was at the time of his Death legally Settled in the Parish of North Ockendon in the County of Essex , but by what means he gained a Settlement in the said Parish, whether by Apprenticeship Servitude or Renting any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds, She this Deponent cannot tell, that She has also been informed by her said Mother that She was born in the said Parish of North Ockendon , And further for herself Saith that She has never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the year in any Parish or Place for the space of One year together or Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right And that She never was Married, And this Deponent further saith that She is now with Child & being with Child is deemed & taken to be a Person chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex where She now resides

Sworn this 4th Day of Novr. 1797 before. H: Reynell R Davies

her Jane Winch Mark
