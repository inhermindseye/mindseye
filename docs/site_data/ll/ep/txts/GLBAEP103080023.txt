Middx ss  Elizabeth Mitchell  wife of Daniel Mitchell (who is gone away from her to places unknown) Maketh oath that her said husband has often informed her that he duly served his apprenticeship to his Father Mitchell a Barber in Great St. Anns Lane in the Parish of St. Margaret Westmr in the County of Middx And by Means thereof gained a legal settlmt there in And that he did not afterwards gain any subsequent settlement never renting a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor Servs any Parish offices nor living as a hired servant by ye year nor doing any Act to acquire any other settlement since his Apprenticeship which information this Deponent verily believes to be true

Sworn this 12 day of December 1757 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Eliz Mitchell
