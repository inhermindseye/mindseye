Middx to wit John Mitchell aged about Fifty Six Years on her Oath Says he Intermarried to Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes upwards of Two Years ago and by whom he hath no Child living Says he rented & lived in a House in Newport Markett in the Parish of Saint Ann in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year at the Yearly Rent of £24 & paid parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Five Months ago since which time he never Rented House Tenement or Lodgings of Ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 29d day of August 1784 before G Mercer James Croft

John Mitchell
