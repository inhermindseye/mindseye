Middx & Westmr}  Elizth Lilley Aged about 22 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Warcup a Cheesemonger in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born And further says that one George Lyons late a Servant to the said Mr- Warcup is the Father thereof Mile having had carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of January last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl- difft- times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said George Lyons and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 28th- Day of Novr- 1777  Abrm Baley

her Elizth- Lilley Mark
