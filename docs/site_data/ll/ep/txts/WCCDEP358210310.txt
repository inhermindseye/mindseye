Westmr.  Mary Osgathorp Aged about Twenty nine Years, upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Osgathorp (gone from her) with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex upwards of Five Years ago, by whom he hath two Children now living (to wit) Thomas aged Four Years and Eleanor aged about Fourteen Months; Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship to Mr. John Fillingham a Staymaker in St. Johns Lane in the Parish of St. James  Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex , and never Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 3d. day of August 1768 , before}  W Kelynge

The Mark of Mary Osgathorp
