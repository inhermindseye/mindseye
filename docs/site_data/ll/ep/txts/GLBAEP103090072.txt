Middlesex ss.  Jonas Cock aged forty five Years or thereabouts Maketh oath That divers Years ago he was bound apprentice to one Richard Franklin of the Parish of Stoke Tammerell in Plymouth in the County of Devon Farrier with whom he Served the whole of his said Apprenticeship in the said Parish and by Means thereof as he is advised gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont. Saith that he has not since done any Act to his knowledge or belief Whereby to gain a subsequent settlemt. And that he hath a wife named Elizabeth Aged forty two years or thereabouts

Sworn this 5th. Day of August 1767 before  Chris: Scott

Jonas Cock
