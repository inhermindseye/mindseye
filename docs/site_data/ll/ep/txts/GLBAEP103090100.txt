Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Jeffrys the Mother in Law of Mary Carter a single woman maketh oath That the said Mary Carter (who is at Present disordered in her Mind) hath informed this Deponent when she the sd. Mary Carte r was of sound Mind & Memory & which information this Depont. believes to be true that she the said Mary Carter divers years ago was hired and lived as hired servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages with one Mrs. Hannah Gibbons in the Parish of West Ham in the County of Essex and Continued in such service for Many years That She the said Mary Carter afterwards removed from thence with her said Mistress to the Parish of Saint Mary le Bow in the said County of Middlesex where She Continued under the Same hiring and service with her said Mistress for above the Space of one Year at Certain Yearly Wages any by means thereof as this Deponent is advised gained a legal settlement in the said last mentioned Parish And this Depont. Farther Saith that to her knowledge information or belief the said Mary Carter hath not since her servitude as above lived as hired servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this Fourth day of March 1768 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Elibth Jeffrys
