Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to Wit Ann Purdy aged 27 years upon her Oath Saith That she intermarried with James Purdy her late Husband at the Parish Church of Acton in the County of Middlesex , That he died about 4 years Ago, That he Informed her & which Information she believes to be True that he lived previous to her Intermarriage with her at Mr Bromleys a Farmer at Acton as a hired yearly Servant that he lived there upward of 2 Years

Sworn this 19th day of August 1790 D Walker

her Ann Purdy Mark
