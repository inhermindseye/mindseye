Middx   Mary Peeling aged about 60 Years removed from the Parish of St. Mary Newington in the County of Surry by Order dated the 5th. day of February instant upon Oath says she is the Widow of George Peeling who has been Dead about Eleven years says she intermarried with him at May Fair Chapel about 22 Years since That her said Husband rented a House of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards in Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and lived therein Six Months that he quitted the same about four or five years before his intermarriage with this Examinant and never after rented any House or paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes as he hath informed her and which Information she verily beleives to be true Also says that since his Death she this Examinant hath not rented any House paid Land Tax or Poor Rate or been a yearly hired Servant for one year

Sworn the 12th. day of February 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Peeling
