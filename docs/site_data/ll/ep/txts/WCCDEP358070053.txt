Westmr. to wit Mary Purslow Aged about Twenty Four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever Served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Years Servant with one Mr. Clark at the Falcon in Fal Court Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Danes in the West London upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Eleven Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 22d. day of December 1772 before R. Butler W Kelyngs

her Mary Purslow Mark

Witness Edwd. Field
