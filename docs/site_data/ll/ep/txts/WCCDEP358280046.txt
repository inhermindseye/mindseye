Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Cath: Bardin aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year that she was Born Milford Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in County of Middlesex that she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any other Settlement th by Birth And further this Examinant saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish And further saith that Joseph Randall a Waterman who lately lodged at the French Horn in Drury Lane is the father of the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in Month of May last and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children which she is now pregnant And that the said Joseph Randall is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 21st. Day of Novr. 1786 before me} Edward Hodson

her Cath: Bardin mark

22d. Novr. 1786 Committed by Justice Walker to Tothil fields Bridewell

Child dead and father Disd.
