Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Sinclair aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever served any Apprenticeship that she was born in Holywell Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes where her Father & Mother kept House for upwards of 20 years And further this Ext. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes And further this Ext. saith that one Richd. Towsey who is Clerk to an Atts. at No. 2 in New Court in the Temple is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the month of May-last and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Richd. Towsey and no one else is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 22d. day of Janry. 1787 } R Butler

Sarah Sinclair

man taken and his Master Mr. Luxmore Passed his Word for his Clerks forthwith Coming at any time Child dead-Expences Paid.
