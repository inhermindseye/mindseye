Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. in Saint Margts. Westmr .  Eleanor Brown aged about 61 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Giddis in Petty france in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Twenty years since which time she hath not lived as an hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of October 1794 before M Addington J Hind

her Eleanor Brown mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
