Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Pullen  Singlewoman maketh oath that many years ago she was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with Abraham Dunn in Vine Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Shipwright & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two years at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been married not done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 18. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Pullen
