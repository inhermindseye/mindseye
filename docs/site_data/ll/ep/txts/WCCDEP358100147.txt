Middlesex to wit Peter Drew Aged about Twenty One Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says his Father Joseph Drew Rented and lived in a House Called or Known by the Name or Sign of the Crown & Mitre Public House in Little Russell Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Months at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds hath quitted said House about five Years ago since which time his said Father hath never gained any other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 6th. day of Novr. 1783 before  Thos: Gilbert G Reading

Peter Drew
