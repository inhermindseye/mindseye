Westmr. ss.  Ann Walker Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr: Carter who kept the Oxford Arms in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath Quitted her Service about five Years and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further Says that on the thirty first day of August 1770 She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mrs: Peakin in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Catherine And further Says that one Robert Allam a Carcass Butcher in Leadenhall Market in the City of London is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of January but the particular day she does not now Remember and at several times afterwards at one of which time he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid And that the said Robert Allam and no other person is the true and Real Father thereof

Sworn this 7th: day of September 1770 before me} R Butler

The mark of Ann Walker

Witness Will: Hughes
