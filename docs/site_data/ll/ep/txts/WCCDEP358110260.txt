Middx  Sarah Page upon Oath Saith she is the wife of John Page by whom she has 2 Childn. Mary aged upwds. of 8 Yrs. & Thomas aged upwds. of 6 years & that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband (whilst a Singleman) lived a yearly Servant with Mr Halsted a Farmer near the pound in Kentish Town in the Parish of Pancras in the County of Middx for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds and was during that Year a Singleman, & has not since, he Quitted his sd. Service rented Ten pounds a year or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn June 12th- 1753 before} William Withers

The Mark of Sarah Page
