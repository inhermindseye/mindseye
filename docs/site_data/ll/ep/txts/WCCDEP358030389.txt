Westmr. 

Thomas Latter on behalf of his father Wm. Latter a Lunatick upon his Oath saith the sd. Wm. Lather lived in and Occupied a house and Corn mill of his own in the Parish of Frant in the County of Sussex for the Space of Eight Years and upwards and Sold the same about four Years ago Says the sd. Wm. Latter has not since he quitted the sd. house & Corn mill rented ten pounds a Yr paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants Knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 11th. Day of June 1747  Wm: Jones

Thomas Latter
