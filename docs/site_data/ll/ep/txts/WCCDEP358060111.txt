Westmr.  Eliz Bowden upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Stephen Bowden of whose Settlement she can give no Account whatever Says she was born in the parish of Sambich in the County of Chester where her Father for many Years past has and still does keep a house & that she this Examint. never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one Year rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn May 18th. 1751 . before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz. Bowden
