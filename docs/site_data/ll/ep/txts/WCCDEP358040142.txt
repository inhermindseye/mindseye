Westmr.  Ann Evans aged about 16 Years Daughter of Edward Evans deced by Margt. his Wife upon Oath Saith she was never married that her sd. late father Edwd. Evans lived in and rented the Blue Sugar Leaf Alehouse Horton Strt. in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of £ or thereabts. for three Years & upwds. & afterwards lived in & rented an other house in Hollis Street in the same Parish and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes for the same Says her sd. late father never after he left the last mentioned house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise or gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Janry 26th 1747 before}
