Middx to wit Ann Howard Aged 70 Years On her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever gained any or. Settlement than as hereafter Mentioned Says she Rented & Lived in a House in John Street  Golden Square in the Parish of St Jamess in the Liberty of Westr . for 2 Years at the Yearly Rent of £32 a Year and Paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about 30 Years ago since which time she Never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year Paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for 1 Year

Sworn this 7th day of May 1784 before  James Croft  Jno- Barnfather

Ann Howard
