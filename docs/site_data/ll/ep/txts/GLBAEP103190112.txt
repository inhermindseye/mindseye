Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Davis the widow of James Davies deced maketh oath That She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband That Previous to her Marriage with her Said late husband She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one Mr Fowle an Ironmonger in Eastsmithfield in the Precinct of St Catherine in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year upwards & has not Since done any Act to Acquire anothr Settlement Save What She has Acquired by marriage with her Sd husband That She hath a child & lawful Issue by her Sd husband named Susannah Aged about Six Years & half & She this Deponent was married to her Said husband at the Fleet

Sworn this. 26. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke W Blackmore

The Mark of Elizabeth Davis
