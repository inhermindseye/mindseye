Middx  John Rutter of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx maketh Oath and saith that by an Order under the Hands and Seals of Bartholomew Hammond and Francis Bedwell  Esquires Two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the County of Middlesex bearing date the Sixth day of December 1759 Elizabeth a Female Child was Removed from the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid to the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx as the Place of her last legal Settlement against which Order there was no Appeal to the Knowledge or belief of this Deponent.

Sworn the 3d. day of October 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

John Rutter
