Westmr to wit Elizth. Sells Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was Married was an Apprentice or Rented House Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Barclay at Bray in the Parish of Bray in the County of Berks for Two Years at the Yearly wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago and hath not since she quitted her said Service ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 15th. day of October 1771 before Us}  Tho: Kynaston

Elizabeth Sell
