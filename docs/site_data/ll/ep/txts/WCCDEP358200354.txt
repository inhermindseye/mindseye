Westmr.  Martha Simcock aged about 49 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Mattw. Simcock who has been dead about two Years; says her said husband serv'd Seven Years Apprenticeship with his Father Matthew Simcock a Perukemaker in Langley Street  Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster as he Often times inform'd her, and which information she verily believes to be true; and never Rented house, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to her knowledge or belief; says she hath not since his Death Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn Feby. 18th. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Frans Cary

The Mark of Martha Simcock
