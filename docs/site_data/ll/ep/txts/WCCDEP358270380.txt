Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Dunn aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that on the 31st. day of July last she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Jones in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been half baptized by the name of Ann That one John Tippings a Journeyman Plumber who works for Mr. Heslop in Lory Acre is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Tippings is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 17th. day of Septr. 1798 before me } John Collick

her Elizabeth Dunn Mark

Witnss AW Burbidge Beadle
