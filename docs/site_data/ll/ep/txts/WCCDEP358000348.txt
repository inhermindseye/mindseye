Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Elizabeth Fletcher on behalf of Mary Cloudsly  Singlewoman her Niece who is ill  Maketh Oath and Saith that she has been informed which information she believes to be true that the said Mary Cloudsly lived as a hired yearly Servant with Major Reed in lower Seymour Street Portman Square in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone for about 3 years  that the last 40 Days of the Elizth. Cloudsly  Service was in lower Seymour Street in the said Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone that the said Elizth Cloudsly has quitted  said Service upwards of a twelve Month ago since which time the said Elizth. Cloudly has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Exts. knowledge Information or belief the said Mary Cloudsly may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Septr. 1786 before  Sampn Wright M Addington

Eliz Fletcher
