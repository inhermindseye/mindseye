Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  the Child died. her lying in Etc to be got if. it can to wit Elizabeth Avery aged 21 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cox in fountain Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about 2 years at the yearly Wages of three Pounds hath quitted said Service about three years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Depts. knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further or her Oath saith that Wm. Hendy Alias Bowers a Journey man Carpenter who lives at No. 11 Little Vine Street Piccadilly is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of May Last and at Several times since and that the said Wm Hendy alias Bowers is the Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 1st. day of Febry. 1786 R Butler

Elizabeth Avery
