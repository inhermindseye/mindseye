Westmr.  Susa. Farlows on Behalf of Thomas Whaley a Child aged abt. 10 Yrs. the Son of James Whaley by Isabella his Wife both deced upon Oath saith she has been Informed and beleives that the Father of the said Infant Whilst to Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Lord Ferriersin Clarges Street in the Parish of St. George  Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds and never after he quitted the sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 14th 1748 before} Tho. Lane .

Susannah Farlow
