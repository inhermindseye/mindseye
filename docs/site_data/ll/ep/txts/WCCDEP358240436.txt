Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. in St. Giles in the fields  to wit John Prosser of King Street  Seven Dials Maketh Oath and Saith That in the Month of May 1780 he this Deponent Intermarried with Sarah Murray who is now Insane at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn That he this Deponent has for these Three Months last past kept a Rented a Shop and Parlour in King Street in the parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds

Sworn before us this 26th. day of Augt. 1794 J Hind J Pownall

John Prosser
