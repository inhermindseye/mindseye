Middx  Elizabeth Pitt Aged about Nineteen Years Upon her Voluntary Oath Says that she was Never Marryed that on the Ninth Day of January Instant she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. John Gray in Starr Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which is as Yet Unbaptized Says Thomas Pesford Watch finisher at Mr. Hands in Mary Bon Street near Pickadilly had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body Several times the latter End of March or beginning of April last and at several other times Since at One of which

times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid and that he and no other Person is the true and real father thereof.

Sworn the 23d Day of January 1764 : Before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizth Pitts

Upon Mr Jones Complt J: W
