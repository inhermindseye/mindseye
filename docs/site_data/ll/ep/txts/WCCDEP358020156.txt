Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Joseph Perrin aged about 46 years upon his Oath saith That about Seven years ago he Intermarried with Sarah his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Sepulchres That he this Examinant before his Intermarriage to his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired. yearly Servant with a Mr. Robinson of Norfolk Street in the Strand for Six years at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that the last Forty days of this Examinants Service to his said Master was at his Masters Country house at North Hall in the Parish of North Hall in the County of Hertford that since he has left his said Masters Service which is about 26 years he has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of Augt. 1789 D Walker Russ: Clarke

Joseph Perrin
