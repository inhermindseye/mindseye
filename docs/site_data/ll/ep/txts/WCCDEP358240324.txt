St Clement Danes September 19th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Nicholas Newbury Berkshire  (to wit) Catherine Field  Singlewoman aged thirty three years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly yearly Servant with Mr Willis Surgeon in the parish of St Nicholas in the Town of Newbury in the County of Berks for a year and an half or upwards at the Annual Wages of S Two pounds, ten Shillings that She quitted that Service about Sixteen or Seventeen Years ago And hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 19th day of September 1793 before us} Pitchell Edwd : Naish

Catherine Field 's Mark

Witness Garrett

Removed by Mr Allaway Overseer And Burbidge Beadle
