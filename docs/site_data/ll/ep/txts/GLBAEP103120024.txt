Middlesex ss}  Peter Augustus Couzens maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice to Mary Kneeler of the Parish of Saint Ann Limehouse in the County of Middlesex  Widow who Carried on the Trade of a Cooper there and Served the Whole of his apprenticeship there and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish That he this Depont. hath not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d Day of December 1772 before B Camper Wilmot

Peter Augustus Couzens
