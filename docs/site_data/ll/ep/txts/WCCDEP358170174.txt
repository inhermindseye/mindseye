Anne Richardson  Wife of Edwd. Richardson upon her Oath saith that abt. Twelve Years ago she Intermarried with her sd. Husband Edwd. Richardson at the Fleet  London & that he was bound an Apprentice to & served his Apprentiship with one Mr. Jonath: Whishell a Taylor in Bridges Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . & further saith that her sd. Husband hath not since he left his sd. Master rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or Beleife that he is now a Soldier in his Majestys Service at Portmahone that he hath left one Child by a Former Wife viz: Anne aged abt. Sixteen Years & this Exaiant being Poor is not able to maintain herselfe & Child

Sworne the 10th. Day of Novr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Anne Richardson
