Middx  The Examination of Margaret Mullens  Widow of James Mullens living in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 9th. Day of February 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that She Intermarryed with the above named James Mullens and that her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentship with one Mr. Blinkard in the Butcher Row in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes  Vintner , And further on her Oath saith that her said late Husband never in his Life neither hath She this Examinant since his Death rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife and being Poor is not able to maintain herselfe

Sworne the Day and Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Margt. Mullens
