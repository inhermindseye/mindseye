Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Macnamara on behalf of Mary Macnamara her Daughter who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith That her said Daughter lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bellmont in Hart Street Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months but what wages she had she this Deponent doth not know That her said Daughter quitted said Service about Twenty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of Febry. 1797 before us}

her Ann Macnamara Mark
