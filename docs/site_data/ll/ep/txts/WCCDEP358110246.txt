Middx  Cathn Burk upon Oath saith she is the wife of Nicholas Burk an Irishman who never gained a Settlement in England and with that she was married to the sd Nichs. Burk in St. Marys Lane in Dublin about Seven Years ago by a Priest of the Church of Rome. that her sd. husband has been gone from her abt. Six Years & she can give no Account of him nor whether he is living or Dead and that she this Examint. was born at Hardy no in the County of in Ireland and never rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. before Marriage

Sworn the 8th. May 1753 before}
