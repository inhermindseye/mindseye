Middx.  Elizth: Dawson on the behalf of Elizth: Berry a Lunatic aged abt. 21 Years upon Oath says the said Elizth: Berry never was Married that abt. four Years ago the said Elizth: Berry lived a hired Yearly Servant with this Examinant at the Grange Inn in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for one Year and upwards at the Yearly wages of four Pounds ten Shillings or thereabouts according to this Examinants now best remembrance and further upon her Oath says that after the said Elizth: Berry quitted this Examinants service she never Rented ten pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year as this Examinant has been informed & which information she verily believes to be true.

Sworn the day of June 1758. before}
