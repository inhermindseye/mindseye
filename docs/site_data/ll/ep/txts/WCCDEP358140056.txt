Middx.  Mary Anderson aged abt. 70 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of James Anderson who hath been Dead abt. thirty Years says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Over in Spring Garden in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for four Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds She being at that time a Widow & without Child or Children & hath quitted said Service abt. Eight Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th day of Novr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Anderson
