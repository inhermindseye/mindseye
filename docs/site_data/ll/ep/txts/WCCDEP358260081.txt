Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Holmes aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that about Eleven years ago she Intermarried with Richard Holmes her present Husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields by whom she hath Three Children living Vizt. Elizabeth aged about Eight years Richard aged about Five years and James aged about Fifteen Months That her said Husband Rented Apartments at No. 10 Wild Court in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for about Nine Months at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds That her said Husband quitted said Apartments about Five years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of October 1795 N Bond E Read

M Holmes
