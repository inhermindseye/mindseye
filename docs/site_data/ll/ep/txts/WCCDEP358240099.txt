St. Clement Danes May 8th 1792

Middx & Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Ann Long aged 36 years or thereabouts Upon Oath Saith That she never was Married, That She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Ride at Three Tunns, in Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County & Liberty for about Eighteen Months, at the Annual Wages of £5, that She quitted that Service about twelve Months ago, and hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 8th day of May 1792 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Ann Long 's Mark

Witness Richd. Dobbins
