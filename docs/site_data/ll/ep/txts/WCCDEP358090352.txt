Middx  Susannah Demain Aged about 27 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr. Fulker at the White near Exiter Change in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of £5. hath quitted her said Service about 8 Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlemt. And further on her Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Sd. Parish And further says that one Thos. Pickleton a Journeymans Shoemaker who lately lodged in a Court in Milford Lane had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her said late Masters House in the Strand some time in the Month of March last but the Particular Night she does not now remember And that the said Thos. Pickleton and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 6th. Day Decr. 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Susannah Demain Mark
