Westmr. to wit Susannah Berry Aged about Twenty three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House Paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Watts a Butcher in Carnaby Street Carnaby Markett in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for Two Years & an half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Years ago & hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30th. day of of September 1773 before W Kelynge R. Butler

her Susannah Berry  Mark
