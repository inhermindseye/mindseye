Middx (to wit)  Rebecca Lyes aged upwards of 50 years upon Oath saith that She is the Widow of John Lyes that after the Decease of the said John Lyes She Rented a House known by the Sign of the Hole in the Wall in Vear Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of westminster in the County of Middlesex of the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and lived in the same five years or thereabouts and during all that time paid Parochial Taxes Says She hath not since She quitted the said House in Vear Street rented Ten pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes Says She hath one Daughter named Rebecca aged about 24 Years who is a Singlewoman and was never Marryed Says her said Daughter never Rented Ten Pounds a year was an Apprentice or yearly Servant in England for one Year that her said Daughter is now a Lunatick and by that means rendered incapable of providing for herself

Sworn the 3d. Day of Janry. 1756 . before} Hamd. Crosse

Rebecca Lyes
