Middlesex ss}  Hannah Marshall the widow of Robert Marshall deced maketh oath that Sometime ago & Since the Death of her Said late husband She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one Stephen Taylor of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Cheesemonger & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant by the year or been Since married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 19th day of December 1775 before Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Hannah Marshall
