St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster (to wit) Mary Price  Singlewoman Aged 58 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Day Trunk Maker in Cateaton Street, the Corner of Lawrence Lane in the parish of St Lawrence Jewry in the City of London about upwards of thirteen Months at the Annual Wages of £7., that she quitted that Service about Six years ago and since hath not lived as an hired yearly Servt. for one year together and or one hiring hiring, Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise acquired any legal Settlement (except as in the parish of St Lawrence Jewry aforesaid) And that she is now unable to maintain herself without some relief

Sworn the 17th. 17th day of Novr. 1791 before us N Bond Thos. Gordon
