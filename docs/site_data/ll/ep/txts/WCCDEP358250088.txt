St. Clement Danes 16th January 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin Orgars London (to wit) Ann Lewis  Singlewoman aged 60 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Beadam in St Nicholas Lane in the parish of Saint Martin Orgars in the City of London for one year upward at the Annual Wages of Seven Guineas that she quitted that place in or about the year 1764 and hath not since lived as an hired yearly servant for a year together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid any parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent knowledge information or belief that she is poor distressed

Sworn the 16th day of January 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Ann Lewis Mark

Witness Garrett

Removed by Benjamin Newman  Beadle 16th January 1793
