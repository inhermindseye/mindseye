Middx  The Examination of Rachel Foocks  Singlewoman aged 55 Years Passed from the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster by Order bearing Date the 27th- Day of January last taken the 3d. Day of February 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that upwards of twelve Years ago She lived with one Mr. Miersin Beauford Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taylor for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of two Pounds twelve Shillings And further on her Oath saith that She hath not since She left her said Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Rac Foocks
