Middlesex ss}  Robert Dell maketh oath That he was born in the parish of Horfar in the Shire of Angus in Scotland That he this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year in that part of Great Britain Called England nor ever did any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for himself in any part of England.

Sworn this 31 day of January 1777 before  Jasper Clarke

Robt Dall
