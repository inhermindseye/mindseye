Middx  Elizth: Ryley Aged about 35 Years upon Oath Says she was never Married and that she Lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mr Johnson a Shoemaker in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn above ye Barrs in the County of Middx for two Years or there abouts at the Yearly Wages Four Pounds and hath quitted her said Service about Nine Years and hath not Since Rented house Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year Save and Except her being hired as a Nurse at the Foundling Hospital

Sworn the 10th. Day of June 1763 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth: Ryley
