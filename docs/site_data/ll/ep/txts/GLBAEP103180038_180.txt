Middlesex ss}  Frances Shrewsbury  Singlewoman maketh oath That She cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents or either of them or of her Grand Parents or any or either of them having never received any information relative thereto nor can She this Deponent in any manner Set forth or discover the place where She was born That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to her Knowledge information or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 27. day of May 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Frances Shrewsbury
