Westmr.  Sarah Morgan aged about 50 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thomas Morgan who has been dead abt. fifteen Years to whom she was Married at the Fleet London about twenty Years ago, says her said husband was a Welsh Man and that she can give no Account of his Settlement says she never was an Apprentice, Rented house, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year and that about fourteen Years ago she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Scamell in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized Sarah ; and that about twelve Years ago she was delivered of another female Bastard Child in the house of the said Mr. Scamell in Stanhope Street afore said which hath since been baptized Jane ; and that about Ten Years ago she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Hand the Corner of Vinegar Yard  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster aforesaid which hath since been baptized John ; says that John Patrick who was Father of the said Children is dead, and that she being poor is not able to Maintain them And further upon her Oath says her Father Saml. Birch Rented and lived in a house in Oxford Road in the parish of St. Anne in the said Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds for one Year or thereabouts, quitted sd. house about Ten Years and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 22d. day of Feby. 1766 before} Frans Cary  Paul Vaillant

Sarah Morgan
