Westmr. to wit Charles Kelly Aged about Twenty Years on behalf of Mary Kelly his Sister an Infant Aged about Eleven Years on his Oath Says the sd Mary Kelly was bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Christ Church Southwark in the County of Surry by Indenture bearing Date the 30th. of August last past to Jane Nugent of the Parish of St. John Clerkenwell in the sd County of Middlesex untill the said Jane Mary shod Attain the Age of Twenty One Years or day of Marriage (According to the Statute in that Case made and Provided) as by the said Indentures relation being thereunto had may more fully Appear And further says that the said Mary Kelly served four Months of her sd Apprenticeship when her said Mistress run away from her Habitation and knows not what is become of her And lastly Says that the sd Mary Kelly never was Assigned over to any Person whatever or ever gained any or Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 28th. day of February 1771 before R. Butler W Kelynge

Charles Kelly
