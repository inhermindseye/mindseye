Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Andrew Holborn to wit Elizabeth Wootten aged about 47 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to William Wooden at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars Says that her said Husband Never gained any Settlement Says that previous to her Intermarriage she lived as a Hired yearly Servant with Lady Ennison in Bedford Row for 13 Months and upwards hath quitted her said Service about 26 years ago since which time she has done no Act matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 15th. day of February 1785 before  Sampn. Wright M Addington

Her Elizth Wootten Mark
