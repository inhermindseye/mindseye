Westmr.  Grace Willford Wife of Willis Willford (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Willis Willford at the Fleet London on the 4th. Novr. 1747 and that she hath been informed & believes the sd. Willford Willis Willford Served his Apprentiship wth. Mr Jno Bicketon a Turner in Old Bedlam in the Parish of St Botolph without Bishopsgate London and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year & that she hath 2 Childn. Willis Eliz aged 20 yrs & Willis aged a fortnight

Sworn Augt. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Grace Wilford
