Middx  Jno: Taylor aged abt. Forty Three Years upon his Oath saith that on the Day of he intermarried with his now Wife  Mary & that abt. Two Years ago he lived in & rented a House in Bennetts Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds for the Space of Ten Years or thereabts. that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath Four Children viz: John aged abt. Nine Years Robt. aged abt. Six Years Joseph aged abt. Three Years Mary aged abt. Nine Months & being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine them

Sworne the 9th. of Janry 1741 before}
