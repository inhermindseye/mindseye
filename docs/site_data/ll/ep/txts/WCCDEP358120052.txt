Middx.  Rt. Thornes upon Oath saith he lived a yearly Servt. (whilst a singleman with Wm. Chitterbuck Esqr . in the Parish of Pountenham in the County of Dorset for 8 yrs. at the yearly Wages of £2 and has not since he left his sd. Service rented ten Pounds a yr. Paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly servt. for one year

Sworn Febry. 7th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Rt. Thornes
