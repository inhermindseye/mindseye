Westmr.  Hannah Davis Aged about Sixty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was Married and that she was bound out apprentice to one Mrs. Le Tour a Mantra maker in Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and Served upwards of Nine Months of her said Apprenticeship and then her said Misstress died Says she never was Assigned over to any other Person nor ever after Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year nor any other ways gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 10th. day of April 1769 before us} R Butler

her Hannah Davis Mark
