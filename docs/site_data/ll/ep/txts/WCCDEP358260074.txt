Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Amelia Makenzie aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she served Three years Apprenticeship under Indentures duly executed to one Mrs. Cowperthwaite in Great Maddox Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster and that after She was out of her said Apprenticeship she never did any Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement And this Deponent further saith That she hath Two illegitimate Children living vizt. James aged about Four years and born in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square and William aged about Seven Months born at the house of a Mr. Weston in Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 16th. day of Septr. 1795 } J Hind Russ Clarke

Amelia Mackenzie
