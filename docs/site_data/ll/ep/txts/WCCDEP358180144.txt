Thos. Williams aged abt. 54 Years upon his Oath saith That he intermarried with his present Wife  Ann aged abt. 57 Yrs. at the parish Church of St. Antholin London abt. 26 Yrs. since that he lived in & rented a House the Corner of Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £18. & afterwards lived in & rented a house in Little Sheir Lane in the same Parish of the Yearly Rent of £16. for the Space of 2 Yrs. & hath left the same abt. 11 or 12 Yrs. since which time he hath not rented £10. a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 20th. Decr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Thos. Williams
