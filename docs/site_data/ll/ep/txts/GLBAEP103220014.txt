Middlesex ss  Sarah Delap  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father William Delap deced heretofore rented a house Called the Tackle Block Ale house in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & did not afterwards do Any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this Depont was born in the Sd Parish That She never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did Any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 6. day of Janry 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Delap
