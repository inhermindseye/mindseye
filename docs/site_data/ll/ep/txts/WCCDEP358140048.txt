Middx  Sarah Allen aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath says she never was married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Maurice Hammond in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted said service abt. two Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 22d. of Octr. 1757 . before me} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Allen
