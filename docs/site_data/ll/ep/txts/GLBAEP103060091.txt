Aldgate Middx

Elizabeth Grott  widdow of John Grott late marriner Deced Maketh Oath that her Said late Husband hath often informd her that he was born and Brought up in the town and Parish of Burrowstonness within Fourteen Miles of the City of Edinburgh in Scotland and that she never heard him Say, he ever gaind any other Settlement Nor she Since her widdowhood

Sworn 17th day of Nov: 1747 before

the mark of Elizabeth Grott
