Middx  Eliz: Tyler aged abt. 32 yrs. upon Oath says she intermarried with her husband Robt. Tyler who is now at Sea in the Parish Church of St. George Bloomsbary on the 30th. day of Januray 1755. says her said Husbd. whilst he was a singleman lived a hired yearly Servant with Jonathan Watson . Esqr . in Hart Street in the Parish of St. George  Bloomsbury in the County of Middx for four year & upwds. at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds & never Rented ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes says she hath three Childn. by her sd. husband namely Eliz : & Mary aged abt. sixteen Months & Robt . aged abt. Nine Months

Sworn the 2d. of May 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham  Benjamin Cox

Elizabeth Tyler
