St Clement Danes

Children  Marrd.  (to wit) Ann Bannister aged 40 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her late Husband Blakey Bannister at the parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West London about twenty six years ago that Since then said Marriage her said Husband Rented and with this Examinant resided in an House in Stanhope Street at the Annual Rent of £12 or upwards for upwards of a year year that they quitted that House about several years ago that her said Husband Died about 3 years ago and never after during his Life obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that she hath three Children by her said Husband all Married and provided for and that since her said Husband decease she hath never been Married nor acquired any Settlement Ann Bannister 's in her own Right

Sworn the 30th day of Janry 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Mark Ann Bannister 's Mark
