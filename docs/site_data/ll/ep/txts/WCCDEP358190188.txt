Middx  Jane Moore aged about 44 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Thomas Moore who was been Dead about Six Years to whom she was Married at the Fleet London about 26 Years ago Says her said Husband hath informed her that he served the first three Years of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Almet Perukemaker and that he lay during the said three Years at Mr. Webbs opposite Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx that he never was Assigned over to any other person kept House paid Parliamentary or Parochial Tax or was a hired Yearly Servant to her Knowledge Also Says that since the Death of her said Husband she hath not kept any House or been a Yearly hired Servant

Sworn the 2d. day of October 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Jane Moore
