Westmr. to wit Mary Howard aged about fifty Nine Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of William Howard who has been dead upwards of Two Years with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Mary Abchurch about Twenty Years ago Says her said Husband rented and lived in a House in Clare Markett in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and upwards and lived therein several Years hath quitted same upwards of Thirty Years ago and never after he quitted the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither has this Examinant since his Death ever kept House paid Taxes or lived any hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 23d. day of September 1769 before me} R. Butler

her Mary Howard mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
