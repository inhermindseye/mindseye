Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Mogford aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the First day of January 1792 She Intermarried with Thomas Mogford her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said Husband Rented a Shop and Parlour in King Street Sev Dials in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Six Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds that after he quitted said Apartments he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes on done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement - and she now chargable to the parish of St. Clements Danes

Sworn this 13th. day of Augt. 1798 before us  W Bond W Carter



Elizabeth Mogford
