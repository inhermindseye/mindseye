St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Sarah Cross Aged 50 years or there abouts upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived us an hired yearly Servant with Sir  Wm. Beecham Practer Brudenell Street Berkely Square in the parish of St. George Hanover Square in the said Liberty & County about two years & Nine Months at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas a year £7 Years or Seven Guineas thereabouts that she quitted that Service abt. thirty years ago and since hath best (by any means whatsoever) acquired any other legal settlement And that she is Poor & unable to provide for herself

Sworn the 29th June 1791 . before us }

Sarah  Cross 's Mark

{not passed omitted to come for the purpose }
