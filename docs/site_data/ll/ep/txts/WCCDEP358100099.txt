Middx to Wit Mary Dax Aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Flynn in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of 18 Months at the yearly Wages of £6 hath quitted her said Service about 6 Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further Says that on the 23d. day of March last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in St. Georges Hospital  Hyde Park Corner which hath since been baptized Elizth . and is become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further says that one John Edginton a footman to one Captain Perry who lately lived at Bromley in the County of Kent and who is now at the Town of Montgomery in the Principality of Wales & is Expected in Town very soon he the said John Edington having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body in her said Mistresses House in Norfolk Street afsd. in the Month of June 1782 and at Sevl. different times afterwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Edington & no or. Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 21st. day of July 1783 R Butler

her Mary Dax mark
