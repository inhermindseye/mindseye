Middlesex & Westminster}  Wynne Williams Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she has lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Nott at the Chop House in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds quitted said Service about Three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one James Hunt a Servant to her said Master is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time the latter end of May last and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of Which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Hunt and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 22d. day of March 1776 before R. Butler

her Wynne Williams Mark
