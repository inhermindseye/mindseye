Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Best aged about Twenty years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she has been informed by Elizabeth Barber this Examinant Mother which Information she verily believes to be true That Richard Best this Examinants late father Rented and lived in a house in Long Alley Moorfields in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex for about four years at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Guineas and that after her said late Father quitted said house he never did any Act to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of May 1790  Wm Kitchiner N Bond

Mary Best
