Middlesex to wit Mary Walker aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Wharton in Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One Year and Three Months at the Yearly wages of Seven pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of One Year since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born And one Pierce Hackett a Servant to one Mr. Downs who lodges at Mrs. Hordel in Beaufort Buildings is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the said Mrs. Hordels House some time in the Month of December last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Pierce Hackett & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 1st. day of May 1779 before me M Addington

Mary Walker
