Westmr.  Mary Nicholls aged about 27 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with her Uncle Joseph Nicholls at the Black Bull Inn in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds, quitted said Service about seven Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 1st. of Janry. 1767 before} Fran Cary  Paul Vaillant

Meary Nichols
