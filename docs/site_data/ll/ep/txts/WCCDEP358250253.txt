Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Taylor aged about 86 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Abraham Sadler for Six years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that the last Forty days of her said Service with the said Abraham Sadler was in Church Lane in the Parish of Saint George in the East in the County of Middlesex and that after she left the said Abraham Sadler which is about Three years she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of Octr. 1793 before us  M Addington W Kinnard

her Mary Taylor Mark

Witness B Newman
