Middlesex ss  John Rolls maketh oath that he was born at Dundee in Scotland and never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That about five Years ago he was lawfully married at the Parish Church of St Saviour in Southwarke in Surrey to one Margaret Leahay Since deced who was born at Cork in Ireland as he has informed her & believes to be true That before her Marriage with him She lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Jonathan Hamilton of Tooley Street in the Parish of St Olave in Southwark in Surrey aforesd Victualld and that by the Copy of an Examination in Writing sworn before Peter Whalley  Esqr one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for Surrey aforesd on the nineteenth of Janry 1788 and now read to him it appears that about Ten years ago this Deponents said late wife lived with the said Jonathan Hamilton as a hired Servant at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds for one year & upwards there That he this Deponent has a Child now living named Elizabeth Rolls aged about four years the lawful issue of this Deponent by his said late wife to whom he this Deponent was lawfully married as aforesaid on the thirtieth day of September 1782

Sworn this 20 day of February 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of John Rolls
