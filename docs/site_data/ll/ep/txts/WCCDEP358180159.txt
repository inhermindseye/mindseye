Middx  Ann Maxwell  Removed from the parish of St. Martin in the Feilds by Order Dated the 21st. Day. of Janry 1743 upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of Alexr. Maxwell Deceased that the sd. Alexr. Maxwell lived in & rented a house in Bennetts Court in Drury Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £12. for the Space of 6 Yrs. & upwds. & hath left the same abt. 8 Yrs.  since which time the sd. Alexr. Maxwell in his Life time or this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or beleife

Sworne 30th. Janry 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Maxwell
