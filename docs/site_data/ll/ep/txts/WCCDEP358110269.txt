Middx  Mary King upon Oath Saith she is the wife of Henry King to whom she was married at Mayfair Chapel on the 3d. of Septr. 1750 and that her sd. husband lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £20 & upwards in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Several Years that he quitted the sd house abt. 6 Yrs. ago and has not Since rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year. and that her sd. husband is of ability to maintain this Examinant but intirely refuses to Support or Assist her, whereby she is enforced to Apply to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes for Releif she being ill with a Fever & unable to maintain herself

Sworn July 2d. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

M King
