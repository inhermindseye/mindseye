Middlesex ss  Thomas Murphy maketh oath that Several years ago he lived in & rented a house of Twelve pounds a year in St Catherines Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & resided thereon for many years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or KingsTaxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement & that he hath a Wife named Mary to whom he was lawfully married in the Church belonging to thesd parish

Sworn this 1st. day of November 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Thomas Murphy
