Middlesex  Elizabeth Milbourn aged about fifty Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Perry a Cap Maker in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  for several Years both to her Aunt and the said Mr. Perry hath quitted her said service about six Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

her Elizth. Milbourne  Mark
