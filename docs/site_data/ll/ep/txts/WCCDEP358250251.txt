Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Wesminster } to wit Sarah Papper aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Akers a Farmer at Broxburn in the Parish of Broxburn in the County of Herts for Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Twelve Months ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th day of Septr. 1796  M Addington N Bond

her Sarah Papper Mark
