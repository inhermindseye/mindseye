Middx  Alice Rickart  wife of John Christopher Rickart (who is gone aboard one of his Majestys Ships of War ) maketh Oath and Saith that about five Years ago her said Husband Rented a House in Middlesex Court in Dirty Lane in the parish of St. Martins in the Fields in the County Middlesex and paid Ten pounds a Year for the same and lived therein about three Years and gained no Subsequent Settlement and that She hath a Child named William Aged about four Years

Sworn this 20th. day of Novr. 1744 Before us Robert Dennett

her Alice Rickart Mark
