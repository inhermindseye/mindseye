Middx  John Thompson Aged near 40 Years upon his Oath saith he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Bulkelly for 2 Yrs. & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £7 & hath left her Service about 5 Years Says that he lived 2 Months & upwards the last Part of his sd. Service with the sd. Mrs. Bulkelly in Portugal Row in Lincolns Inn Feilds in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx Says he hath not since he left the sd. Mrs. Bulkelly Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 13th. Febry 1744/5 before} Wm: Jones

John Thompson
