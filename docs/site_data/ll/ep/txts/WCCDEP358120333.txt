Middx  Eliz Ridge aged abt. 26 Years upon her Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Roberts a Perfumer near Templebar in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of £6 for two Years or thereabouts that she quitted her Sd Service abt. 4 yrs. ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn May 5th 1755 before}
