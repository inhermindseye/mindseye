Rate an Apothecary  in ye Parrish of St. Clements Danes in ye. County of Middlesex ; taken upon Oath this 29th Day of December 1758 before us Willm Russell  Esqr . & the Revd. James Plowden Clark Two of his Majesties Justices for ye sd County.

This Examinant upon her Oath Saith that She is ye true & Lawfull Wife of Mr Edd. Angier late an Apothecary in Essex Street in ye Strand in ye County of Middlesex but now living as She has heard & beleives in ye Island of Jamaica That during his Residence in Essex Street He Rented a House of Sixty Pounds a Year & paid to Church & Poor & other Rates Charged upon ye sd. House: & that to ye best of this Examinants knowledge Her sd. Husbd. never did any one Act whatever to gain any Settlemt. Elsewhere Since He lived in Essex Street before He went to Jamaica where He has lived many Years; & is as She veryly beleives living then Still & Further Saith not.

Sworn before us the Day & Year aforesd. Will: Russell  James Plowden

The Mark of Ann Angier
