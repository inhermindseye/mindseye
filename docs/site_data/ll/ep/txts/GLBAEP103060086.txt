Aldgate Middx

William Haycock Maketh Oath that upwards of Thirty years ago (he being then a Single man) was a hired Servant year by the year at three pounds a year and livd Fifteen Months as Such with William Peirson a Victualer at the Sign of the Sun in Forter Lane in the Parish of Saint Vedast alias Foster Leonard in Forster Lane in the City of London , And that he hath gaind no other Settlement Since hath a wife Margaret

Sworn fourth day of June 1747 before us Robert Dennett Richd Ricards

the mark of William Haycock
