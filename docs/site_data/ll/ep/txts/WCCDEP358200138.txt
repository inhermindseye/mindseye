Middx  Mary Perry Aged about Forty two Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of James Perry with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about fourteen Years ago Says her said Husband has been Dead Five Years the Twenty seventh of June last Says her said late Husband was born in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx as she has been Informed by her said Husband and which Information she Verily beleives to be true & Cannot Give any further Account of the Settlement of her said late Husband.

Sworn the Day of Septr 1764 Before}
