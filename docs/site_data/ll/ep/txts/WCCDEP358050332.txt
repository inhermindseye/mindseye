Middx  Eliz: Mebe upon Oath saith she never is the Wido. of Thos. Mebe who dyed abt. 20 yrs. ago and that her sd. late husband was born at Gatheridge in Herefordshire and Lived in a house of his own in the same parish for Several Years and that she this Examint. hath not since the Decease of her sd. late husband rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 4th. 1750 before Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Mebe
