Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Stickland  Singlewoman maketh oath That Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with William Dimford in Stockwell Town in the parish of Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry  Victualler at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Fifteen months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement thi That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a ye nor hath She been ever Married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9 day of September 1777 before}

The Mark of Elizabeth Stickland
