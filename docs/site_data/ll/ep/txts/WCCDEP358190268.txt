Middx  Eleanor Aynsworth aged about 22 Years upon Oath says She never was Married And that She Served upwards of Five Years of her Apprenticeship with Mr. Gregory Mathematical Instrument maker in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes Says She never was assigned over to any other Person nor lived a hired Yearly Servant one Year out of the said Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn the 23d. day of August 1762 before} Paul Vaillant

The mark of Eleanor Aynsworth
