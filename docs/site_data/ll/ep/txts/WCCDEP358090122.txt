Middlesex to wit Rachael Dewxell Aged about forty four Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Thomas Dewxell with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn about Twenty Two Years ago and who has been dead about Twelve Years Says her said late Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one Andrew Janaway a Bedstead maker in Gravel Lane Hounsditch in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or ever gained any other subsequent Settlement otherwise than as before mentioned neither hath this Examinant since her said Husband Death

Sworn this 22d. day of Febry. 1780 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Rachael Dewxell Mark
