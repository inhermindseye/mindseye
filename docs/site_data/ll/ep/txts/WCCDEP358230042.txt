Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster to wit Mary Ann Allaway aged about 51 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Allaway in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two Years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of four Guineas that the last Ten Months of her said was at the Country house of the said Mr. Allaway in Peterborough Lane in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex that after she quitted she hath done on Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Febry. 1799 before us  Saml. Edwards Menter

her Mary Ann Allaway Mark

H Day Beadle
