Westmr.  James Stone Aged upwards of Forty Years, Upon Oath says he intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex about Thirteen Years ago, by whom he hath one Child now living (to wit) James aged about Twelve Years, says that before his intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Thomas Corbett Esqr. who lived in Mildman Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Thirteen Pounds thirteen Shillings and quitted said service about Fourteen Years ago, says that for Six Months or there abouts the first part of his said Service he this Examinant lodged at the Horse and Groom the bottom of Great James Street  Bedford Row in the said Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Barrs , and then he travelled with his said Master into the Country, and never afterwards during his said Service continued to lodge in anyone Parish or place for the space of Forty days together, says he was discharged his said Service in Mildman Street aforesaid about a Month after he returned from the Country with his Master And further says that he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 23d, day of April 1768 , Before W Kelynge Fran Cary

James Stone
