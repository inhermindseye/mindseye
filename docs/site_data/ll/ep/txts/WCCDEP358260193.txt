Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit John Conway aged about 62 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented unfurnished Apartments of Mr. Robinson a Staymaker in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Three years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas that he hath quitted said Apartments about Ten years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 24th- day of March 1797 } John Collick

John Conway
