Westmr to wit Sarah Burrell Aged about 30 Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Edward Burrell (who is now Pressed and gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish of Saint Clement Danes about Seven Years ago and by whom she hath one Child named Edward aged about 15 Months Says that her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examt. and whilst he was a Singleman lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Linnell at the Magpye in Butcher Row in the sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted his said Service upwards of Six Years & an half and after he quitted his said Service he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 26th. day of Novr. 1770 before me} R. Butler

Mark Sarah Burell
