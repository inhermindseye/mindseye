Westmr.  John Gallagher upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Thirty pounds in King Street near the Seven Dyak in the Parish of St Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for the space of two Years or thereabouts and hath left it about Seven Years since which time he has not rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn 28th. Octr. 1749 before H Fielding

John Gallagher
