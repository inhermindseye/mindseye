Middx  Mary Driscall aged about 36 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Driscall who has been dead about Six Years and with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Saint Finbarry in the City of Cork in Ireland about fifteen Years ago; says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mackey a Taylor in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath quitted said Service about Six Years since which time she hath not Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 19th. day of July 1765 . Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Driscall
