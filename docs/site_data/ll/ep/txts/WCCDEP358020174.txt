Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Bagley aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that upwards of Five years ago she Intermarried with John Bagley her present Husband who has deserted her at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square that her said Husband Rented the lower part of a House in Hard Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twenty Guineas that he hath quitted said house apartments about Thirteen weeks since which time he has not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Novr. 1789  Sampn Wright J Addington

Ann Baglee
