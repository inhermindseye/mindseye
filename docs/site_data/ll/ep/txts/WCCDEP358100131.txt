Middx  James Stone Aged about 48 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Mary Lambeth about 28 years ago and by whom he hath one Child living now Provided for as he believes Says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired yearly Servant to Sr. John Shaw at Eltham in the County of Kent in the Capacity of a Postillion upwards of Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds besides Diet Cloaths & Lodgings hath quitted his said Service about 30 years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlemt. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodgings of £10. a year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 1st. day of Octr. 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

James Stone
