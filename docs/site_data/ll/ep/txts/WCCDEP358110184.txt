Middx  John Cox upon Oath saith he was married to his Wife Sarah Cox at Long Collingham in Nottinghamshire Sometime in Febry last and has by her Twin Children Joseph and Mary aged about two Months and that he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Thomas Laming a Miller in the Parish of Middle Raising South Town in the County of Lincoln and never rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Febry 21st. 1753 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of John Cox
