St Clement Danes June 20th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Sarah Munday  singlewoman aged about twenty eight years Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs Robinson in a place then called Cumberland Court in Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex for about eighteen Months that she quitted that service about six or seven years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief that she is Ill distressed and unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn the 20th. day of June 1793 before Me W Addington

Sarah Munday 's Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
