Mary Bell lives at Mrs. Coles in Whitecross Street

Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Bell aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or Paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Cole in Whitecross Street in the Parish of St. Luke  Old Street in the County of Middx for 4 years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £5 that she left it about 12 Months ago and went back again in the Month of Novr. last and has continued there ever since And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that on the 10th day of Octr. last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of a Mrs. Evans in Goldens Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Charlotte That one Martin Hurdes a Journeyman Hatter who works for one Mr Christie a Hatter in White Hart Court Lambard Street and lodges at one Mrs. Tills a Coak Shop in Petticoat Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of Decr. 1785 and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Martin Hurdes (and no one else) is the Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 19th. day of July 1787 } Wm Kitchiner

Mary Bell

Lane has been Relieved Week by with Newman had the Warrt.
