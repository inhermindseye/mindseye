Middlesex ss}  James Griffin maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Beilby Porteus of the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry  Doctor in Divinity at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under such hiring for the Space of two Years & upwards & has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or servd any Parish office or rented a house of ten Pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlemt That he hath a wife named Lydia & three Children namely James aged eleven years Mary aged near eight years & William aged about nine Months which said two first mentioned Children are the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his first wife Mary deced & the last mentioned Child is the lawful issue of this Depont. by his sd now wife  Lydia to both of which sd Wives he this Depont was lawfully Married

Sworn this. 7. day of May 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

James Griffin
