Middx  The Exaicon of Isabella Canupey Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the first Day of Novr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never marryed & that abt twenty two Years ago she was bound an Apprentice to one Mrs. Horton in the Parish of Alhallows London Wall in the City of London for the Term of 4 years & this Exaiant lived with her sd. Mistress in the sd. Parish three Years & Eight Months or thereabts. & then This Exaiant & her sd. Mistress parted by Consent & further saith she hath not since she left her sd. Mistress rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & is sick & Poor & not able to maintaine herself

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before} Wm Hayton  Jas: Gronous

the Mark of Isabella Canupey
