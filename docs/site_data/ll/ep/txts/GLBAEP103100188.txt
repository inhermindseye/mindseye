Middlesex ss  Mary English  Singlewoman Maketh oath that divers years ago she was hired and lived as a hired servant by the year with Solomon Morgan of King Street in Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Cheesemonger and Continued in such servant for the space of Six Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & ten shillings And by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the said Parish And this Depont. Saith that She hath not since lived as a hired Servant for a year Nor hath she been ever Married done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement.

Sworn this 8th day of December 1769 before Chris: Scott

The Mark of Mary English
