Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Bright aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Rickards in Craven Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Ten years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she hath an illegitimate Child living Vizt. Prudentia aged about Nine years born in the Queens Hospital the Surry Side of Westminster Bridge

Sworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1788  Sampn Wright N Bond

Mary Bright
