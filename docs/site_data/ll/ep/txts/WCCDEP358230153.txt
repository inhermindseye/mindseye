Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Joseph Sharp aged about 43 years upon his Oath saith That on or about the 24th day of August 1774 he intermarried with Elizabeth his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields by whom he hath living 3 Children Vizt. Frances aged three years Durham aged seven years and William Aged 14 years That while Single he lived as a hired yearly Servant for upwards of four years with M Whaland Apothecary North Audley Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Eight Guineas That he hath left the said Service about 24 years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months rented any House Tenement on Lodging of ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st day of October 1800 before us  John Callick W Parsons

Joseph Sharp
