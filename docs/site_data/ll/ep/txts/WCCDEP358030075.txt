Middx  Dorothy Sheldon aged about 37 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married and that she was born at the house of one Mr. Surrin a Peruke maker near the Bull head Slaughter house in Clare markett in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Says her Father Sheldon Served his time with the sd. Mr Surr in & never after gained a Settlemt. in any other Place  as this Examint. hath been Informed & beleives Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Year hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examints. knowl or beleif

Sworn Decr. 26: 1745 before}
