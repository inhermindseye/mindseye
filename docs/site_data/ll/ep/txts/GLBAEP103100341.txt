Middlesex ss  Susannah Gifford  Singlewoman Maketh oath that she Sometime ago was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds with Ann Christie of Burr Street in the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  widow and Continued in such service for the space of a year & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish And this Depont. Saith That She hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor hath She been ever Married Nor done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th Day of October 1770 before  Chris: Scott

Susan Gifford
