Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Robert Burnett aged about 30 years upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice by the Trustees belonging to Saint Giles's Charity School to a Mr. Meredith a Shoemaker in Orange Street in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr for Seven years that after he served about 5 years of his said Apprenticeship his said Master moved to Gilbert Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex  that he went his said Master and served the Remainder of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1788 .  Edw : Read  Thos. Gordons

Robert Burnett
