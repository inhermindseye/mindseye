Middlesex ss}  Henry Davis Maketh oath that he is now a Pensioner in Greenwich Hospital That this Deponents late Father Samuel Davis deced many Years ago lived in & rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds a year & upwards in St John's Square in the Parish of St James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx & paid Poors Rates for the Same in which Parish this Depont was also born & this Deponent Can give no othr Account respectg his Sd Father Settlement That he this Deponent never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever was bound An Apprentice nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes not ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right That he hath a wife named Elizabeth to Whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 10. day of July 1780 before  Jasper Clarke  Jno Sherwood

Henry Davis
