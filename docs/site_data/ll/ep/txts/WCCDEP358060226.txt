Middx  Ralph Wilson upon Oath saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Richard Wilson a Tobacconist in Charles Street in the Parish of St. George in the East in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr 12th. 1751 before  Tho Lane

Ralph: Willson
