Westmr. 

Martha Abbit on behalf of Wm. Abbit her Son aged abt. 29 Yrs. upon Oath saith the said Wm. Abbit never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. parish Taxes and that Wm. Abbit Deceased (this Examinants husband & the above named Wm. Abbits father) rented a house in Duke Street Lincolns Inn fields in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £25 and lived there in upwds. of four Years and never after he quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

 Doors from the Chapel 

Sworn June 25th 1750 before}

The Mark of Martha Abbit
