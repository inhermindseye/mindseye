Middx to wit Mary Hallaway Aged about Twenty one years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Colonel Roberts in Holles Street Cavendish Square in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for three years and in half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds the last half year of which Servitude was at East Acton in the said County of Middlesex Her Master removing there And was discharged from thence about Twelve Months ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlements by Servitude or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 29th. day of November 1780 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Mary Hallay Hallaway
