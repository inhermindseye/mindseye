Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Mary Mullender aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that she is about 40 years of Age never was Married that she kept House in Petticoat Lane in the parish of Saint Mary Whitechaple in the County of Middlesex for 3 Months at the yearly Rent of £10 & upwards hath quitted said House about 10 years ago since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23rd day of Decr. 1786 D Walker R Butler

M. Mullender
