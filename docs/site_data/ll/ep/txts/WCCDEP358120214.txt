Middx  William Dan upon Oath Saith he never was married & that he lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Evers at the Noahs Ark in King Strt. in Charles Parish at Plymouth in the County of Devon at the yearly Wages of Seven pounds for three years quitted his sd Service about 4 yrs. ago and has not since rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant  for one year

Sworn Octr. 16th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

William Dan
