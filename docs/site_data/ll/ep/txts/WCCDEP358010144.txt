Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Eliz Lancaster aged 37 Years upon her Oath Saith That she is the wife of George Lancaster to whom she intermarried at Lambeth Church in the County of Surry be Banns about 9 years Ago That she has a Child by her said Husband Named George aged & years That her said Husband has deserted and left her and her said Child whereby she is unable to Maintain herself and is now become chargeable to this Parish And she further Saith that her said Husband rented and lived in a house in Newcastle Court in the said Parish for 2 years and upwards that he paid 12 Guineas Pr. Annum Rent for same that they Quitted said House about 8 Months ago and have not since done any Act or Decd to gain a subsequent settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge and belief

Sworn this 29th. day of Septr. 1787 N Bond

Elizth. Lancaster
