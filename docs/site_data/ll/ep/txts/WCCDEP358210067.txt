Westmr.  Ann Lloyd aged abt. 21 Years Upon Oath says she was never Married and that she was bound an Apprentice for five Years to Mrs. Fry a Weaver in Great George Street in the Parish of St. Matthew Bethnal Green in the County of Middlesex Served four Years and upwards of her said Apprenticeship and then upon some disagreement with her Mistress the Indentures by consent of both Parties were cancelled, says she never Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the day of August 1766, before}
