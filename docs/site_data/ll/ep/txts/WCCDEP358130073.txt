Middx  Mary James aged upwards of 40 Years Says She Intermarried with Henry James (who is now at Sea in his Majesty's Service) on the 11th. Day of July 1746 Says She has a Child by her said Husband named Thomas aged near four Years That her said Husband lived a hired Yearly Servant about Twenty seven Years since with one Mr. Webb a Cook in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes upwards of a year and that he hath not since rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parochial Taxes nor lived a Yearly Servant as this Examinant hath been informed by him & as She verily believes

Sworn the 22d: Day of Octor. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Mary James
