Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Bryan aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wright in Hill Street Berkeley Square in in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for near Two years at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Twenty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that on the 23rd. day of April now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Sarah That one Richard Varnell alias Wornell a Journeyman Shoemaker who lives in Exeter Street in the Strand is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Richard Varnell alias Wornell is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1797 before me  Gs Goodenough

her Catherine Bryan Mark.

Man never taken

Child born in the House the 23d. April 97 named Sarah Bryan

Died Disd. with the Mother 14th. Augt. 1797 Died the 25th. Octr. 1797
