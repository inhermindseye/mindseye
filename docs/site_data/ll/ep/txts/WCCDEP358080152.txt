Middlesex to wit Mary Boswell aged about Thirty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly servant with one Mr. Mort in Cold Bath Square in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex upwards of four Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th. day of June 1777 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Mary Boswell
