Middx  John Martin upon Oath saith he intermarried wth his Wife  Mary Martin in the Parish Church of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlx on or about the 19th. Octr. 1739 and that he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Creaner a Fishmonger near Temple bar  in the parish of St. Clement Danes afsd. and never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Jany. 16th. 1753 before} Tho Lane

John Martin
