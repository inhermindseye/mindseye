Middx & Westmr}  Ann Sanson Aged about fifty Six Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Neale at the Sign of the Horseshoe & Magpie in Butcher Row in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service Upwards of thirty six Years ago & hath not since she Quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year or done any other Act Whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Ninth Day of August 1774 before- R. Butler

her Ann Sanson Mark

This Woman is foresworn in this Year she has sworn that she never was married & now she is ready to swear she was & so I think it Appear she living with her Daughter therefore she wd gain no settlemt. by living as hired Yearly Servant having at that time a Child she belongs to Sherborn in Dorsetshire Therefore she is an Imposter.
