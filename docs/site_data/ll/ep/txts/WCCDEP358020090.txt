Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Pearman aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Harris at the Tunns in Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 13 Months at the yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted said Service about 5 years since which time she has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th day of July 1788 R Butler.

her Elizabeth Pearman Mark
