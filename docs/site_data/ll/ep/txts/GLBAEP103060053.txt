Middx  Edward Nichols maketh Oath and Saith that about Eighteen Years ago he was Bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to John Bass of Ironmonger Row then in the parish of St. Lukes Giles Cripplegate in the County of Middx but now in the parish of St. Luke in the County aforesaid Stockingmaker and served his Master about one Year and then his Master run away into Ireland and his Misstress gave him his Indentures And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And hath a Wife named Susannah

Sworn this Sixth day of December 1745 Before Robert Dennett

Edward Nichols
