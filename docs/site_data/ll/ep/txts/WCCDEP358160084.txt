Middx The Examination of Sarah Hayton  Widw . Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 6th. Day of Octr. 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that her late Husband Richd. Hayton Deced lived in a House of his own in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Barrs in the same County of the Yearly Value of Twenty five Pounds & lived in ye. same upwds. of 30 Years and this Examinant further saith that her sd. Husband in his life time nor she this Examint. since his Death never kept any other House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she is now Poor & Old & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Sarah Hayton
