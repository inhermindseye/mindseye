Middx  Eliz: Sadler aged abt. 62 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Henry Sadler says she being a Widow & without Child or Childn. lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year with Mrs. Mary Potter in Hoxton town in the Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middx. at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath quitted said Service abt. ten Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid the Poors Rate

Sworn this 22d. day of Feb: 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Sadler
