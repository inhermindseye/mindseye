Westmr  Thomas Phillips upon Oath saith that on the Twelfth Day of April 1748 he Intermarryed with Ann Seger  Wido . at the fleet London Says during the time the said Ann was a Widow she lived in and rented a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty eight pounds and this Examinant further Saith that after his sd. Intermarryage he Continued to Live in the same house and is to Quit the same abt. the Middle of this present month

Sworn 5th. Augt. 1749 before} Jas. Water

Thos. Phillips
