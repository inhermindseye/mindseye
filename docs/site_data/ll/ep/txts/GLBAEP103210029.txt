Middlesex ss}  Mary Rooke  Singlewoman maketh oath that She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or of her Grand Parents or any or either of them nor of the place where She this Deponent was born never having received any information relative thereto That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentce nor ever lived as a hired Servant in any place for the Space of a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 20 day of Novemr 1784 before}
