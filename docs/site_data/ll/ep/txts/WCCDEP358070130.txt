Westmr. to wit Ann Vigo Vange Aged about 26 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried with John Vange her husband gone from her at Shoreditch Church about Ten Years Ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with his Father Vange a Velvet Weaver in Gravel Lane in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate in the County of Middlesex as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes and that since she has been married to her said Husband he never Rented House Paid Taxes or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 13th. Day of Septr. 1773 }
