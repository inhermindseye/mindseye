Middlesex ss  Susannah Omer  widow of Charles Omer deced maketh oath that she has been Credibly informed & which She verily believes to be true that her Said late husbands Father John Omer deced hereto fore lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by and with Mr Dowley a Rag Merchant in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in such Service there under Such hiring for divers Years & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That to this Deponents Knowledge or belief her Sd husband never did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that she was lawfully married to her Said late husband

Sworn this. 24. day of November 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Susannah Omer
