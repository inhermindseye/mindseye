Middlesex ss  Mary Clarke  wife of John Clarke who is at present absent from her maketh oath that her said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Wages by and with Joshua Harte of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Grocer & Continued in Such Service there for three Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards to this Deponts knowledge information or belief live as a hired servant for a year elsewhere or rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain another Settlement That She hath three Children namely Margaret aged about
