Middx  Eliz: Wright aged about 43 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Lady Cary in Great Ormond Street in the Parish of St. George  in Queen Square in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds for the Space of One Year & upwds. & hath left her sd. Service abt. 9 Years since which time she hath not Rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne April 6th. 1744 before} Wm: Jones J Poulson

The Mark of Eliz: Wright
