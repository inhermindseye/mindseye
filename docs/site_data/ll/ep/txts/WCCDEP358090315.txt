Middlesex  Mary Emerson Aged about Forty Two Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband John Emerson at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes upwards of Twenty Year ago and by whom she hath Two Children namely Sarah Aged about Twenty Years now provided for and John Aged about Eleven years now in St. Clement Charity School Says her said late Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one John Pitts a Staymaker in Newgate Street in the Parish of Christ Church London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained anyother Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds P Annum or otherwise howsoever with hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 16th. day of May 1782 before R Butler Sampn Wright

her Mary Emerson Mark
