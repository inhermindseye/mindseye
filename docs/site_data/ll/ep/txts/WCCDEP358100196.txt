Middx & Westmr.} to wit Harriott Griffiths aged about Thirty Six years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House Paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says her Father John Griffiths Rented & lived in a House in Hatton Garden in the Liberty of Saffron Hill  Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two years at the yearly Rent of £30. and after quitting the said House he never gained any other Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 19th. day of Febry. 1782 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Marshall a Shoemaker in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . which has since been Baptized by the name of Elizth. Maria and is become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further says that one Robt. Carter who lived as a Valet with the late Lord Robt. Manners and who now Uses Mr. Price a Cooks Shop in James Street  Haymarkett is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings in Mary Le Bone upwards of Twelve months Preceding the time she was brot. to Bed at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Child of which she was so delivered afsd. And that the said Robt. Carter & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 17th. day of March 1784  Thos: Gilbert .

Harriot Griffiths
