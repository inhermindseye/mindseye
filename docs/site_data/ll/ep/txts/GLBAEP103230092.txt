Middlesex ss}  John Richards maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to James Butcher of the Victualling office in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Cooper and during the last two Years of Such his Said Apprenticeship he this Deponent ledged and was boarded at the Black horse in the Victualling office Square in the Same Parish where his Said Apprenticeship ended That he has not Since rented a house or Lodging of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 8. day of Decemr 1787 before R Davies

John Richards
