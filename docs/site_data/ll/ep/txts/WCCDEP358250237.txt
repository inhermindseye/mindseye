Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Fraser aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that about Seven years ago she Intermarried with George Fraser her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster by whom she hath Two Children living Vist. Mary aged about Six years and Frederick aged about Six weeks That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Fountain Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Two years ast the yearly Rent of Twenty Guineas that he hath quitted said house about Twelve Months since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has left this Examinant about One Month and is gone to Sea us she believes

Sworn this 17th. day of May 1796 } Saml. Edward .

Mary Fraser
