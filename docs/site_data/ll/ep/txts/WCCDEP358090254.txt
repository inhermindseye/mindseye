Middx  Susannah Jeffreys Aged about Forty years on behalf of Maria Matilda Robinson an Infant aged about Eleven years on her Oath says that She the said Maria Matilda Robinson is a Bastard born on the Body of One Hannah Swain a Singlewomen in the House of One Richard Lovett who kept the Sign of the Fish mongers Arms in Love Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary at Hill near Billingsgate London and can with more Certainly speak to the  thereof She this Examinant being in the Room at the Birth of said Child And that She the said Susannah Swain Acknowledged it to be a Bastard and afterwards sword One Stephen Robinson to be the Father

Sworn this 17th. day of September 1781  Wm Kitchiner  Sampn Wright

her Susannah Jeffreys Mark
