Middx  Susanna Simpson aged about 26 Years upon Oath saith she was never married Says she was born at the Corner of Crown Court in Brond St. Giles in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx Says her Father George Simpson lived in & rented a house in Bow Street by Bloomsbury Square in the sd. Parish of St. Giles of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards for the Space of One Year & upwards as this Examinant hath been informed & beleives Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Sepr. 19th. 1746 before  Ja. Fraser

The Mark of Susanna Simpson
