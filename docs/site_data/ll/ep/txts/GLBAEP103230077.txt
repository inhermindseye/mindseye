Middlesex ss}  Susannah Andrews the wife of James Andrews maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her & hath left her destitute of Support That her Sd husband was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to Walter Atkins of the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County Waterman and Lighterman & was Soon after Assigned by him to John Walker of the Parish of St John Wapping who soon afterwards Assigned him to William Sutton of the Sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate  Waterman and who then lived in Mand his Rents there for the Remr. of the Sd apprenticeship and where the Sd apprenticeship ended That her Sd husband never rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pound nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship That She hath a Child named Jane aged four Years & three Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 23. day of October 1787 before}

The Mark of Susannah Andrews
