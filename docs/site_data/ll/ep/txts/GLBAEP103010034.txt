Middlesex, to wit, Ann Patronella Shott , Singlewoman maketh Oath, that her late Father Richard Shott some time ago lived in and rented a House in Sweedland Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and was charged to and paid Poor's Rate for the same, and that her said Father never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement, That she this Deponent was never bound as an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the year in any Parish or Place for the space of one Year together, nor hath she done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right, and that she never was married

Sworn this 31 day of Decr. 1790 , before}
