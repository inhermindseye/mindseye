Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Southall aged about 44 years upon her Oath saith That on or about the Third day of February 1784 she Intermarried with David Southall her late Husband (who has been dead about Five weeks only) at the Parish Church of Saint Giles Cupplegate That her said late Husband Rented a Two Pair of Stairs Room and a Garret unfurnished of a Mr. Babe No. 33 in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Eight Guineas and that her said late Husband Rented a first of the said Mr. Babe a Garret in the said house for Twelve Months and upwards at the Weekly Rent of One Shilling and that her said late Husband never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Febry. 1798 before me } W Carter

her Mary Southall mark
