Middx  Eleanr. Egan upon Oath Saith she is the wife of Edwd. Egan & by whom she has twin Childn. (Viz) Eleanr . & Mary aged near two Years and that her sd. husband & herself are both Natives of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England and that her sd. husband was bora at Omey in the County of Tyrone in Ireland & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented Ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn 8th. Octr. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eleanr Egan
