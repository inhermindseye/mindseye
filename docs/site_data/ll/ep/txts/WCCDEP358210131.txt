Westmr.  Eleanor Mullins aged about sixty Years Upon Oath says, she never was Married; and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Sir Philip Meadows in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, quitted said Service upwards of Twenty Years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 21st, Jany. 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eleanor Mullins
