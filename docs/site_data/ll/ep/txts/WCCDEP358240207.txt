St Clement Danes 30th Janry. 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  Children  St Andrew Wardrobe London  (to wit) Mary Holmes Widow aged 32 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her late Husband John Holmes at the parish Church of St.. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster (by Licence) on or about the 21st.. day of May March 1785 or thereabouts that she hath three Children by her said Husband born in lawful Wedlock Namely John born aged 8 years seven years on or about the 21st.. day of May last Mary Jane aged 5 years on or about the 19th.. day of October last and James aged 3 years on or about about the 10th.. day of December last all of whom of are now living and neither of them settled in his to or her own right that her said Husband Since their said Marriage Rented & resided in an House in Upper Thames Street in the parish of St Andrew Wardrobe London for a year & six five Months & upwards at the Yearly Rent of £14 that he quitted it abt. Christmas 1790 and never after obtained Any subsequent legal Settlement during his life to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief And this Deponent saith that her said Husband died abt. on or about the twelfth day of January instant and this left her this

Deponent distressed and unable to maintain herself and Children without assistance

Sworn the 30th day of January 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Mary Holmes
