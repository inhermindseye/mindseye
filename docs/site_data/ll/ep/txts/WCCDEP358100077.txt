Middx to wit Mary Whitaker Aged about Sixty Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Whitehead at a Jelly Shop the Corner of Bridges Street  Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . & County afsd. for 3 years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of 5 Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Thirteen years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlemt. either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servt. for One year.

Sworn this 12th. day of July 1783  Sampn Wright W Addington

her Mary Whitaker mark
