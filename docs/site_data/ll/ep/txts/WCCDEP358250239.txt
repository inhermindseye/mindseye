Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Joel Curtis aged about 51 years upon his Oath saith that about 14 years ago he Intermarried with Ann Curtis his late wife who has been dead about Twelve Months at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bone by whom he hath Two Children living Vizt. Ann aged about 12 Years and Mary Jane aged about 11 years That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Little George Street Portman Square in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for about Eighteen Months at the yearly Rent of Fifteen Guineas that he hath quitted said House about Six Months since which time he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of May 1796  N Bond Robinson

Joel Curtis
