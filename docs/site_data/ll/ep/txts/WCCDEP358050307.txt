Westmr.  Mary Patey Wife of Edward Patey upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Edward Patey in the Parish Church of St. Botolph without Bishopgate London upwds. of 27 yrs. ago Says her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Dry at the Sugar Leaf in Houghton Street Claremarket in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten pounds a Yr pd. parish Taxes lived a yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief Says she hath a Son by the said Edward Patey viz John aged about 16 yrs. who never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant

Sworn Septr. 4th. 1750 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Mary Patey
