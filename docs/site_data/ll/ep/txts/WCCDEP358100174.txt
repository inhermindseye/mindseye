Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit James Rogers aged about Twenty nine years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year says he lived a hired yearly Servt . with one Mr. Siscotty a Staymaker in Queen Street  Golden Square in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . for 3 years at the yearly of Wages of Ten Pounds hath quitted his sd. Service upwards of three years ago since which time he hath never lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry. 1784  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

James Rogers
