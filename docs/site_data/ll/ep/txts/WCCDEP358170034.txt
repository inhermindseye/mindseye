Middx  The Examination of Jno. Nash Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken ye. 30th. Day of March 1741 Who upon his Oath saith yt. about 15 Years ago & whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Captn. Spicer in Red Lyon Square in the Parish of St. George in Queen Square in the County of Middx aforesd. for the space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5 And this Examint. further saith yt. he hath not since he left his said Service Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath Wife Named Elizth . who is now Sick & being Poor is not able to maintaine himself & her.

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before} Jas: Gronous

John Nash
