Middx  The Examination of Nichs. Mountain aged about 60 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 14th. July 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. abt. Six Years ago he lived in & Rented a House in Clare Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £36 Pounds for the space of 14 years or thereabouts And this Examint. further saith yt. he hath not since he left the sd. House Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes lived with a Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife That he is now Poor & Lame & not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before}
