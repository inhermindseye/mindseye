Middx.  Andrew Fowles Aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx on the 6th. day of Novr. upwds. of 30 years ago says before his said Marriage and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Sarah Bittany a Corn chandler in Warwick Lane in the Parish of Christ Church in the City of London for One Year at the Yearly Wages of £6 says he has not since he quitted that Service Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 12th. day of March 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Andrew Fowles

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Wilkinson Ovr. of the Poor J: P:
