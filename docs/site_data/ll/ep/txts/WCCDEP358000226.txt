Middlesex Westminster (to wit) Sarah Sedgley aged 40 Years or there abouts upon her Oath saith That she Never was Married, Saith That she lived as an Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Robt. Hanstead a Butcher in Wardour Street in the Parish of Saint Ann's Soho for upwards of 8 Years at the Yearly Wages of 6 Gunieas hath quitted her paid Service about 12 Months ago since which time she this Examinant hath not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to the best of her knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 9th. day of Febry. 1786 before  Thos: Gordon N Bond.

her Sarah Sedgley Mark
