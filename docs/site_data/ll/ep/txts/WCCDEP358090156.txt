Middx to wit James Talboys of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  Perukemaker on the behalf of Elizabeth Otter a Lunatick maketh Oath that he this day went over into the Borough of Southwark to Examine the Books kept at the Rotation Office there and whilst this Examinant was Examining the same Samuel Swabey  Esqr . came in there And on Asking the Clerk and the said Samuel Swabey whither such Examination was taken and sworn before him the Clerk and the said Samuel Swabey Acknowledged that there was such Examination And that it was his the said Samuel Swabey s hand writing Relative to the said Elizabeth Otter .

Sworn this 8th. day of Septr. 1780 before M Addington David Walker

Jas Talboys
