Westmr. 

Susa. Hodsdon wife of Zachariah Hodsdon (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Zachariah Hodsdon upwards of Seven Years since at the Fleet London Says her said husband lived a Yearly Servant at with Mr. Tewksbury Butcher in Claremarket in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the yearly Wages of £10 for one Year and upwards before his marriage with this Examinant & after his sd. Intermarriage Continued to live with the sd. Mr. Tewksbury under the same hireing and Agreement for one year or thereabouts Says her sd. has sd. never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 21st. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Susannah Hodsdon
