Middlesex  William Sargarson Aged about Twenty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn about four Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children living Namely Mary Aged about Three Years and George Aged about Nine Weeks Says his Father William Sargarson has Rented Two Several Houses in Kensington in the Parish of St. Mary Abbotts Kensington in the sd County of Middlesex for sevl. Years last past at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or upwards And further Says he never in his Own Right gained any other Settlement than as aforesd. he having never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 21st. day of Febry. 1784 before me  Edward Hodsoll  Wm Kitchiner

Wm Sargarson
