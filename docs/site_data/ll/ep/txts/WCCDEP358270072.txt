Bastardy before Birth

St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt  Pregnancy  Reputed Father (to wit) Sarah Richards  Singlewoman aged twenty one years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, she lived as an hired yearly Servt. with Mr Constable in Butcher Row in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Coffee Man for about one year and two Months & upwards at the Annual Wages of Seven pounds for the first Nine Months and Eight Guineas the residue of the time that she quitted his Service on or about the 20th. instant and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement and this Deponent Upon her Voluntary Oath farther saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which and that the said Child or Children is or one likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and  be and that to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes and that Samuel Taylor a Walter at the said Mr Constable's of Butcher Row on the said or the only real and true father of such Child or Children where with she is pregnant as aforesaid aforesaid he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body in or about the Month of December last and at several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children there or the said Body of this Deponent and saith that she was not ever criminally connected

with any Man  except the said Samuel Taylor  and

Sworn the 24th day of July 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Richards 's mark

Witness Jas Talboys
