Westmr. to wit Samuel Harrison on or behalf of Mary Harrison on who he looks upon to be a Lunatick Aged about forty four Years on his Oath Says she is the Wife of Thomas Harrison (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Abbey Church in the County of Salop about Twelve Years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living as he believes at Edgmount in Shropshire named Thomas aged about Seven Years Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with his Father in the aforesaid in the Parish of Newport has she belonged to Parish of Edgmount in the said County of Salop he being a Certificated Man and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes or otherwise Gained any other subsequent Settlement than as aforesaid to his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 1st. day of Augt. 1772 before} R. Butler Sam Wright

The Mark of Samuel Harrison

Witness Petr Sidebotham
