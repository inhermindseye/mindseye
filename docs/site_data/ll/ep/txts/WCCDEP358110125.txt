Westmr.  Mary Duck aged abt. 21 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. for one Year nor ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes that her father Jno. Duck deced rented the Bell Inn at Parkgate in the parish of Carrington in the County of Surry and lived therein Sevl. Years and never after he quitted the sd. Inn Rented ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn Novr. 23d 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Duck
