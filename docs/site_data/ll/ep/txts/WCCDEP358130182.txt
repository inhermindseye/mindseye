Middx  Judith Ray aged about Thirty nine Years upon Oath says She was never married & that about Nineteen Years ago She lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Shields a Perukemaker in Clemts. Inn in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds. Ten Shillings for One Year & upwards & hath not since She quitted the said Mr. Shields Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parochial Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 28th. Day of June 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Judith Ray
