Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Dwyer Aged Sixty four Years or thereabouts on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Dwyer who has been dead about Three Years with Whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of about Years ago Says her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that her said late Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one a Shoemaker at Shillingford in the County of Devon and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship never Rented House or paid Taxes nor ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year neither has this Examinant since her said Husbands death done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of 1769 before Us}
