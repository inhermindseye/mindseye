Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Passd. to St. Martin in the field to wit Hester Pulley aged about 19 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes ever lived as an hired yearly Servant or ever served an Apprenticeship says that Esther Pulley her late Mother Whilst she was a Widow Rented and lived in a House in Russell Street Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for five years at the yearly Rent to Twenty Pound hath quitted said House about two years ago and never in her Life time to this Examinants Knowledged gained any settlement and this Examinant hath and since her said Mother Death since which time the Examinant hath gained any other Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 28th day of June 1785  Thos. Collins R Taylors

Hester Pulley
