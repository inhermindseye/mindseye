Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Martin  Singlewoman and James Talboys one of the Beadles of St. Clement Danes severally make Oath and say and first this Deponent Elizabeth Martin for herself saith that she is about 19 years of Age never married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That her father James Martin Rented and lived in a house in Lumley Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for about Five Months but what Rent he paid for same she this Examinant doth not know That her said Father hath quitted said House about Four years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she this Deponent might have gained any other Settlement And this Deponent James Talboys for himself saith that he has been informed by Mr. Clark the Landlord of the said House in Lumley Court afsd. which the said James Martin kept and which Information he verily believes to be true That the Agreement was between the said Mr. Clark and Martin was to pay to the said Mr. Clark the yearly Rent or Sum of Fourteen pounds

Sworn this 23d. day of Decr. 1794 } M Addington Edwd. Naish

her Elizth. Martin Mark

James Talboys
