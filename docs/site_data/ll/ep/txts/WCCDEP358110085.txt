Middx  Margt. Cardiff upon Oath Saith she is a Native of Ireland & the Wife of Patrick Cardiff an Irishman who Served his time (as he informed this Examinant) with Mr Nash a Shoemaker in Patrick Street in the Parish of St Nicholas in Dublin & never rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes & that she hath one Child (viz) Ann aged abt. 13 months

Sworn Augt. 19th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Margt Cardiff
