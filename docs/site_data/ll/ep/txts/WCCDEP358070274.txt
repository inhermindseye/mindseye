Westmr ss  Samuel Mapish Aged about Fifty one Years on his Oath on his Oath says he is a Widower and hath no Child or Children now living, Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Simon Mathews a Shoemaker in the Parish of Saint Olive in the City of Exeter and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Paid Taxes or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 26th day of June 1775 before}

Samuel Mapish

NB he Married Mary Barker out of the Workhouse.
