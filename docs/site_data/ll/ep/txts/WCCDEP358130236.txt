Middx.  Winifred Maginnees aged abt. Sixteen Years on be half of her Brother John Maginnees an Infant aged abt. Seven Years upon Oath Says her Father & Mother are both Dead that they were Natives of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England that her sd. Brother John Maginnees was Born in Cross Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx.

Sworn Novr. 23rd. 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Winifred Maginnees
