Middlesex ss}  Mary Kelly  Singlewoman Maketh oath that about five years & half ago She this Deponent was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Locklin in Great Hermitage Street in the Parish of St. George in the sd County Brewer & Continued in Such Service for three years & half & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor was ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain anr Settlement That She hath a male Child born of her Body in Brewhouse yard in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Aged Six Weeks but has not yet been baptised & that one James Hashing late of Tulbot Court Little East Cheap London but now Supposed to be resident in Barbadoes is the Father of the Said Child which is illegitimate She this Deponent never having been Married

Sworn this 16 day of Decemr 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Kelly
