St Clement Danes 13th February 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Mary le bone  (to wit) Mary Clark  Singlewoman aged 23 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant Rented and Resided in an Apartmt. at No. 21 Queen Ann Street East in the parish of St Mary le bone for upwards of 3 Months at the Annul Weekly Rent of One pound five Shillings Ready furnished Saith that the said Apartment consisted of two Rooms on the first floor and she verily believes that the same are of the yearly value of £20 at the least unfurnished that since she quitted that Apartment (which was near six years ago) she hath not Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year Value paid parish Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the 13th day of February 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Mary Clark 's Mark
