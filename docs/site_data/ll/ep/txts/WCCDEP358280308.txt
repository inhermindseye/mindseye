Saint Clement Danes 28th. Decr- 1790

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Sarah Robertson aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith That she lived with Mrs- Burrough of Crown Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the Wages of Four Guineas per Annum That she has not gained a Settlement out of this Parish and further this Examinant upon her Oath saith she became acquainted with George Westbrook a Journeyman Hair Dresser living with Mr. Lony of Portugal Street  about 13 or 14 Months ago That he had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body about that Time and at several Times since at one of which times he did beget the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And further upon her Oath saith That the said George Westbrook and no one else did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so Pregnant and That he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of Decr. 1790 } Wm Kitchiner

her Sarah Robertson Mark
