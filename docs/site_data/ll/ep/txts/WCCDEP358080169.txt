Middx & Westmr-}  Cath: Harsey aged about 51 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Thos- Harsey who has been Dead about 8 Years and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said late husband Rented & lived in a house in Green Street Grosvenor Square in the Psh of St- George's Hanover Square in the County of Middx upwds- of ten Years at the Yearly Rent of £15 and paid all parish Taxes due for the same & lived therein to the time of his Death and after this Examinant quitted the same she never gained any other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30th- Day of Septr- 1777  Thos Preston  John Cox .

Catherine Harsey
