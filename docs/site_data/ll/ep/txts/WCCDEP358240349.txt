St Clement Danes November 5th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St George the Martyr Southwark  (to wit) Mary Squibb aged thirty years and upwards Wife of William Squibb who hath (now from her) Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said William Squibb at the parish Church of St. James Westminster on or about the eleventh day of April 1785 (by Banns) that subsequent thereto her said Unto Husband Rented and with this Examinant Resided in a House in Pearl Row St George's Fields in the parish of St George the Martyr in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surrey from Midsummer day last until on or about the fourth fourteenth day of October last at the annual Rent of about fourteen pounds that they quitted the Said House on or about the said 14th. of October last and her said Husband has not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that she hath four Children by her said Husband now with her namely Anna Maria born on or about the 12th. day of February 1786 William Henry born on or about the 20th day of March 1787 Mary Ann born on or about the 30th day of March 1789 and Daniel born on or about the 26th day of December 1792 that her said Husband hath left her this Examinant and with their said four Children unable without support

Sworn the 5th day of November 1793 before us } B: Kennett Edw. Read

Mary Squibb
