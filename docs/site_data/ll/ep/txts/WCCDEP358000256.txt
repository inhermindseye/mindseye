Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Mary Gould Aged 24 years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she never was Married That her Lived as a Hired yearly Servant with One Mr Lee a Surgeon in Arrundell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 1 year 6 of a year at the Annual Wages of £5 That she quitted her said Service about 3 Months ago that since she quitted her said service she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And this Examinant further on her Oath Saith that on the 4th day of this Instant March she was delivered a Male Bastard Child at the Store Street Hospital who hath since been Christened by the Name of Charles And further upon her Oath Saith That One John Porter who did Live with Mr. Monksell at Edmonton as Coachman about 8 Months ago is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the Same and  the said John Porter and one no one else is the only Real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 22d day of March 1786 before me R Butler

her Mary Gould Mark
