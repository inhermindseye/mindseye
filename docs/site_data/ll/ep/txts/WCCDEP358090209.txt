Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Bass Aged about Fifty years (On behalf of her Sister Susannah Bass a Lunatic Aged about Forty seven years on her Oath says that her said sister Susannah Bass never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or ever servant any Apprenticeship says her said sister lived as a hired yearly Servant with One Mr. Butler a Hatter in Tooley Street in the Parish of Saint Olave Southwark in the County of Surry for 2 years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twenty years ago and after quitting her said Service she never gained any other subsequent settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly servant for one year

Sworn this 30th. day of March 1781 before us  Sampn Wright  Thos: Gilbert

her Elizth Bass  Mark
