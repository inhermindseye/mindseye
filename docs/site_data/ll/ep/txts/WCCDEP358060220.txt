Westmr.  Sylvanus Landon upon Oath Saith he intermarried with his Wife  Mary Landon at the Fleet London about 14 Years ago & hath by her one Child(vizt.) Mary aged abt. 13 Years who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant And that he(this Examinant)lived a Yearly Servant whilst a Singleman with Mr. Thornhill an Apothecary in Leadenhall Street in the Parish of St. Catherine Creechurch London at the Yearly Wages of Twelve pounds for one Year and upwards and quitted the sd. Mr Thornhills Service about four months before his sd. Intermarriage, Since which he has not rented ten pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlemt. to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 2d. 1751 before Tho Lane

Silvanus Landon
