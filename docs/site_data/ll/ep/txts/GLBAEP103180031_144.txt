Middlesex ss} The above named Ann Taylor maketh oath that divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the Year by & with Mr. Thornton a Merchant in Alderman bury in the parish of Saint Mary Alderman bury in the City of London at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Four Years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And that She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this first day of April 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

Ann Taylor
