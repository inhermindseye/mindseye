Sarah Johnson upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Rt Johnson (who absconds from her) and that her sd. husband (as she has been informed & believes Served his Apprentiship wth. his father a Carpenter at Barham in Kent & never rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn Novr. 26th 1753 before Tho Lane .

Sarah Johnson

Memd. Thos. Johnson (the father of the above Robt.) his Wife & family had a Cart from Jekam in Kent to the above Parish of Barham dated 6. Octr. 1729
