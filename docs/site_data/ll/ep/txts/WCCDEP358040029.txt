Westmr.  Hannah Cole Widow of David Cole upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said David Cole in the Parish Church of St. Mary at Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland on the 21st. Day of January upwards of 22 Years since Says her sd. late husband has been dead about two Years Says the sd. David Cole lived in and rented a house in Butcher Row in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of £23 for one Year or thereabouts & afterwards lived in & rented another house in Horseshoe Court in Cary Street in the same Parish of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds for nine Months or thereabouts & quitted the sd. house in Horseshoe Court about 15 Years ago Says her sd. Late husband after he left sd. house or this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds

a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 10th. 1747 before}
