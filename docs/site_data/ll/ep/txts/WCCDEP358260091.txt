Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Rutledge aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Seventh day of December 1789 she Intermarried with Richard Rutledge her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath no Child or Children living that her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Five years at the yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath that her said Husband has deserted her and refuses her to maintain her and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn this 7th- day of Decr. 1795 } M Addington

her Elizabeth Rutledge mark

Witness B Newman
