St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Mary Atwaters Aged forty Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, lived as an hired yearly Servant, was bound an Apprentice, Rented any House or Apartment of £10, a year, paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right, that she is the legitimate Daughter of Thomas Atwaters by Frances his Wife Formerly Frances Robinson Spinster , who this Examinant hath been Informed were legally Married but she forgets at what Church Chapel or place - That her said Father lated as Clerk or Servant with Mr. Smith in Great Shire Lane in the said parish of St Clement Danes for at least 2 or three years-as he hath Informed this Deponent or mentioned in her presence he then being Single as she believes And that she hath been in the Workhouse of this parish about three Months about ten or Eleven years ago That she is now poor and incapable of Maintaining herself without Assistances.

Sworn the 26th Novr. 1791 before me R Butler

Mary Atwaters Mark
