Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of John Eaton who is insane Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said John Eaton Served his Apprenticeship to his father John Eaton in Middlesex Court Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of April 1796 M Addington Robt: Bard Gabriel

Jas Talboys
