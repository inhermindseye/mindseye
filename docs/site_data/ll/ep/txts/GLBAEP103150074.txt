Middlesex ss  Christian Brady maketh oath That Twenty Years Ago or there abouts as she this Deponent now best recollects & remembers the Ceremony of a Marriage or Pretended Marriage was had & Solemnized between this Deponent & one Charles Brady late of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx Green Grocer deced by a Romish Clergyman at the Sardimian Ambassadors Chapel near Lincolns Inn Fields in the sd County According to the Rites Ceremonies of the Roman Church by means Whereof She this Deponent then Apprehended that she became the Wife of the Said Charles Brady & that in Consequence thereof she this Deponent & the Said Charles Brady Cohabited together as man & Wife the time of his death Which happened Sometime in the Month of November 1773 And this Deponent farther Saith that the Said Charles Brady Rented a house Several Years in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate aforesaid & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same in Which Parish he died & was there legally Settled at the time of his death And this Deponent Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 4th. day of Novr. 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Christian Brady
