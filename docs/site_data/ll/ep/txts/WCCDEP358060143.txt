Middx  Mary Jenkins aged abt. 55 Yrs. upon Oath saith she is a Singlewoman & never was married & that her father James Jenkins rented a house of Seventy pounds a Year near Doyleys Warehouse in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and there dyed & her mother Mary Jenkins continued in the said house Five or Six Years after her Fathers decease & then quitted the same and never after rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief Says she this Examint. never rented Ten pounds a Year was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant nor ever did any Act to her own to gain a Settlement in any other Parish to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 22d 1751 before} Mary Jenkins
