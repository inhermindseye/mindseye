Middx  Margarett Dry  Singlewoman aged about forty Years upon her Oath saith that about Ten or 12 Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Done at the Sign of the Swan near Swan Yard in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £8. for the Space of One Year & upwards That since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd: Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 19th. Septr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Margt. Dry
