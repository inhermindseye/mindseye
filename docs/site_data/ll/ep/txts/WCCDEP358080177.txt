Middx & Westmr-}  Mary Pearcy aged about 26 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Waterhouse who kept a Hard Ware Shop in Middle Row Holborn above the Bars in the Co- of Middx for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £4 hath quitted her said Service upwds- of 10 Years ago since which time she has gained no or- subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 21st- Day of Octr- 1777 before G Mercer Jno. Machin

her Mary Pearcy Mark
