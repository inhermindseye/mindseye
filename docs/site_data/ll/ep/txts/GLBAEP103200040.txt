To her Birth Settlement not knowing her Husbands

Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Brown the widow of Martin Browne deced maketh oath that she can give no Account respecting her husbands Settlement That she this Depont was born in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & never did any Act previous to her Marriage nor Since her sd late husbands death to gain a settlemt for herself That this Deponts late Fathr Christopher Meale deced heretofore rented a house in the sd Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere

Sworn this first day of August 1783 before R Davies

Eliz Brown
