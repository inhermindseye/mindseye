Middx ss  Henry Huxford maketh oath that his late Father Samuel Huxford deced as this Deponent has been informed by him & which he believes to be true was in his life time duly bound Apprentice by Indre to this Deponts Grandfather Samuel Huxford of Stoney Lane in the Parish of St Olave in Southwarke in the County of Surry  Farrier & duly Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship there and that the this Deponents Said Father did not afterwards do any Act to gain a settlement Subsequent to his Sd Apprenticeship That this Deponents Sd Father about three Weeks before his Death had relief Administred to him by the then Parish Officers of St Olave aforesaid as being there legally Settled That he this Deponent never lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year in any one Place nor ever was bound an Apprentice nor ever Served any Parish Office nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn this. 27. day of April 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Henry Huxford
