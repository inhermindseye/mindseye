Middx  Joannah Hoare Aged about Twenty Eight Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of John Hoare (Gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London on the third Day of July 1753 Says her said Husband never was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly for One Year Says her said Husband, Father John Hoare Rented a House in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of two Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty five pounds and hath Quitted said House about Two Years as this Examinant hath been Informed by her said Husband and

which Information she Verily Believes to be true Says she has three two Childrens by her said Husband (to witt) John Aged about four Years and Sarah Aged about Two Years

Sworn the 13th Day of June 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond

Hoare

Upon Mr Browns Complt J. W
