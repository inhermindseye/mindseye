St Clement Danes August 16th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. Ann Aldersgate descriptar of the parish wray they refused to the Pauper  whereupon a fresh Examn was taken the Name of the parish obtained & the Woman deled Parish of St Ann & Agnes London Vide subsequent Examn page 98 in Book No 24 (to wit) Ann Higgins  singlewoman aged twenty four years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she is informed said verily believes it to be true that her Father Robert Higgins  Married with this Deponent Mother Susanna at the Fleet Prison according to the Ceremonies of the Church of England above Forty years ago that her Father rented and resided in an House in Angel Street in the parish of St. St Ann within Aldersgate London for several years at the annual Rent of £12 and Died there, that her said Mother continued to Rent the same House for several years after this Deponent's Father Deconed at the said Yearly Rent of £12 and left the same about ten years ago and hath not since been Married or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge Information or belief And this Deponent farther saith that she hath never served any Apprenticeship or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoover whereby to require any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her this Deponents knowledge

is belief and that she is pregnant and incapable of maintaining herself and defraying the Expences of her lying in, without Assistance

Sworn the 16th day of August 1792 before us  Thos. Gordon  Kitchen

Ann Higgins

This Pauper was taken back removed a second lane the parish being Misnamed
