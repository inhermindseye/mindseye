Westmr. Middlesex  Hanh. Yeates aged about 60 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwards of 20 Years since she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Taylor a Cheesemonger in Butcher Row in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £4 for one Year and upwards and has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a

Settlemt. in any other Places to her Knowledge or beleif Says She hath one Daughter (viz) Joanna aged upwds. of 20 Yrs. who was born in a house in Milford Lane in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes and never was an Appentice or Yearly Servt- rented ten pounds a Year or pd Parish Taxes

Sworn Janry 6th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of  Hanh. Yeates
