Middx  Esther Yeo aged about 36 Years, upon Oath saith she never was married and that Wm. Yeo her father rented a house and Laud in the Parish of Tring in the County of Hartford of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and lived therein about twelve Years & there dyed Says she was about Eighteen months since removed from the Parish of Mary bone to te aforesaid Parish of Tring agt. which Order there was no Appeal and that she has not since that time rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 19th. 1750 before W Yeilding

H Yeo
