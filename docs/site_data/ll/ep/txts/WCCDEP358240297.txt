St Clement Danes August 5th 1793.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Botolph without Aldersgate Appealed agt this Removed Removal but after being adjd. three Sessions was struck out of the Paper in Janry. Session 1794 whby Order remains confirmed  (to wit) Mary Gould aged 55 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to Michael William Gould at the parish Church of St Dunstan in the West London about thirty four years ago that her said Husband Died abroad about sixteen years ago (as She hath been informed and verily believes) that since her said Husband's Death she this Examinant being a Widow unmarried and having no Child or Children then living she this Examinant lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs Smith in Aldersgate Street in the parish of St Botolph without Aldersgate London for two years and upwards at the annual Wages of £4:10s for the first three years & £5 further Reman Remainder of such Service that She quitted that place upwards of nine years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby be obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 5th day of August 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner  Edwd: Naish

Mary Gould 's Mark

Witness Garrett
