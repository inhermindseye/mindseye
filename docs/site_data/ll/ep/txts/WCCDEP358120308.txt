Middx  Ann Davis aged upwds. of 50 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that upwards of 26 Years ago she was hired a yearly Servant to Mrs. Shelley at the yearly wages of Five pounds with whom she lived under the sd hiring upwards of Six Years in Norfolk Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that she has not since She quitted Mr. Shelleys Service rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 14th March 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Davis
