Vagt. Middlesex   Lawrence Power Aged about Sixty five Years on his Oath Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one John young a Taylor in the Parish of Warkworth in the County of Northumberland and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 1st. day. of January 1770 before me}

Vagt.
