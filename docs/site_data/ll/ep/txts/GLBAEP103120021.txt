Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Jane Kemsell of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County  Singlewoman taken on oath before me Robert Pell  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this 30th day of November 1772

Who on her oath Saith That She is now with Child And that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be chargeable to the Said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate And that John Meller of the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the Said County  Cooper is the Father of the Said Child

Sworn this 30th day of November 1772 before R Pell

The Mark of Jane Kemsell
