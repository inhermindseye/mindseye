Middx  Elizth. Woodcock aged about 59 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Robert Woodcock who died about fourteen years ago and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Aldgate about 29 Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House known by the Name of the Crooked Billett in King Davids Lane in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell in the County of Middlesex for Several Years at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds & paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Twenty Years ago and after quitting the same he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 23rd. day of Decr. 1778 before  Jno. Machin  John Cox .

her Elizabeth Woodcock Mark
