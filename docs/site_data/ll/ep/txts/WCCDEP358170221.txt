Middx  Rebecca Strickland  Wido . of William Strickland upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her said late Husband about Fifteen Years ago at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx that her Sd. late Husband Wm. Strickland served his Apprentisp with one Mr. Wm. Jones in Vinegar Yard the Upper End of Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St. James at Clerkenwell in the said County of Middx  Soap Cask Cooper that since his Sd. Apprentiship he hath not rented £10 a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife

Sworn the 8th. of May 1742 before} Tho. Lane  Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Rebecca Strickland
