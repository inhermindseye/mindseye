Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Tunks aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or Tenement or paid any Taxes that she never lived as an hired yearly Servant in one place for one year that she is the Daughter of George Tunks that she was born in the Parish of Dawley in Shropshire that her Father was a Carpenter and lived in Colebrooke Dale in Shropshire in the Parish of Maidley And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one James Lennan a Grocer at No: 43 Leadenhale Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of September last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child on Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said James Lennan is the only Real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 13th- day of June 1789 .} R Butler

her Elizth. Tunks Mark

Elizth. Tunks at No: 9 Stanhope Street

James Lennan is a Tall Man Rather fresh Coloured wears his hair Platted and tied behind is an Irish Man.

taken & gave Security Bond.
