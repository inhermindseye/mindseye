Middlesex ss}  Frances Bear the widow of Joseph Bear deced maketh oath that divers years ago her Said late husband lived in & rented a house in Angel Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards do any act to gain a settlement Elsewhere nor hath She this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself.

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Frances Bear
