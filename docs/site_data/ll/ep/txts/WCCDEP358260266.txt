Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Partin aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 21st. day of November 1790 she Intermarried with John Partin her late Husband who has been dead about Four years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said late husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Turner in Mouldmakers Row in the Parish of Saint Leonard Foster in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1798 before us W Kinnard Edwd: Naish

her Jane Partin mark
