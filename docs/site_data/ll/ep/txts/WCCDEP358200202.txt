Westmr.  Thomas Spratley Aged about Fifty Seven Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Hannah in the parish Church of St. Mary in White Chaple in the County of Middlesex about Fifteen Years ago Says he never was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says his Father Samuel Spratley Rented a House the Corner of Hollis Street Clare Markett in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds & upwards & never after Rented House or paid

Taxes as this Examinant hath been Informed and which Information he Verily beleives to be true Says he has One Child by his said Wife Named Elizabeth Aged about Nine Years.

Sworn the 18th Day of January 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox

Thomas Spratley
