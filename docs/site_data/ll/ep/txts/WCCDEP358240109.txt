St. Clement Danes May 25th 1792

Middx & Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Elizabeth Robson aged 36 years or thereabouts Widow Upon Oath saith That She was Married to her present late Husband John Robson at Berwick Chapel Church in Berwick upon Tweed about near 16 years ago That Since their said Marriage her said Husband Rented and Resided in a House Nod. 20 in Wych Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the said County about her 5 Years at the Annual Rent of £30 that her said Husband Died the on or about the 3d day of Novr. 1783 or 1784. or thereabouts That She this Deponent left the said House about a Month after her said Husbands Decease That since She quitted the same she hath never been Married; lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one year under one hiring Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year done any other Act or thing Whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlemt. And that She is Poor, Ill, without any place of Abode and unable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn this 25th. May 1792 before Me} R Butler

Eliz Robson
