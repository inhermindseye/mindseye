Westmr. to wit Ann Sams Aged about Sixty Four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. John Tyce a Butcher in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of Sixteen Years ago and not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 4th. day of Novr. 1772 before me  Tho Lane

her Ann Sams Mark

Witness Edward Field
