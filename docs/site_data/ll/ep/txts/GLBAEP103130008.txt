Middlesex ss}  Richard Yardley maketh oath that he and one Ann Thomas Since deced Cohabited & lived together as Man and Wife for the Space of Six Years although in fact they were never Married and during such Cohabitation she the said Ann Thomas was delivered of a Female Child at the house of Edward Hubbard  Baker in Ratcliffe highway in the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middlesex Which Child was Baptized in the church belonging to the said Parish by the name of Jane Yardley and is as this Deponent is advised a Bastard by reason the said Ann Thomas during such Cohabitation & before was a Widow woman & had no husband then living to this Deponents knowledge or belief and which said Child is of the age of two Years or thereabouts and likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County of Middlesex .

Sworn this 2 day of September 1773 before  Cha: Digby t B Camper

The Mark of Richard Yardley
