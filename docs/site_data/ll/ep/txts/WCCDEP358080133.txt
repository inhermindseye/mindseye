Parish of St- Georges Hanover Sq- in the Co- of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of twenty Pounds as she has been informed & verily believes hath quitted the sd. place upwards of 4 Years ago and after quitting the same he never gained any other subsequent Settlemt- neither hath this Examinant since her sd. husbands Death

Sworn this 31st- Day of March 1777  John Croft  John Lane .

her Elizth- Arnold Mark.
