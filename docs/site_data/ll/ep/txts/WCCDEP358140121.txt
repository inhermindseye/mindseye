Middx.  Martha Smith aged abt. 60 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Christr. Gome in Horse Shoe Court St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds & hath Quitted said Service abt. 2 Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 27th. day of May 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Martha Smith
