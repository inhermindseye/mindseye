Middlesex ss}  Mary Wilson the Wife of Edmund Wilson  mariner maketh oath That her Said husband is absent from her at Sea That her Said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound apprentice to & Served the whole thereof with one John Smith of the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent Waterman & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her Said husband to her knowledge or belief did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere and that She hath a Child named Edward  Aged about nine years the lawful Issue of this Depont by her Said husband to Whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 17. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith  Par: Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Wilson
