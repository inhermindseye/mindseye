Middlesex ss  Samuel Griffin maketh oath that he well knows one Thomas Hammersley who is at present absent & who divers years ago was hired & as a hired Servant by. the year at Certain Wages by & with one Mrs Barnard in the parish of Kingston upon Thames in the County of Surrey & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief the Said Thomas HammerSley  hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any poors Rates or kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a Child now living named Jane Aged about five years the lawful lsone of the Said Thomas Hammersley by Jane his late wife deced.

Sworn this 31 day of January 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

Samuel Griffin
