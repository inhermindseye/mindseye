Middlesex ss.  Joseph Short Maketh Oath that about Fourteen Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with Mr. Arnoy of Wellclose Square in the Parish of St. Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex Sugar Baker at the yearly Wages of Eighteen Guineas and continued in such Service under such hiring upwards of One Year But at the time he lived Servant as aforesaid he had a Wife living named Catherine to whom he was lawfully Married at the Parish Church of St. Botolph Aldgate London on or about the Day of And further Saith that before his said Marriage and when a Singleman he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with Mr. Jessmer of Maudlins Rents in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex at certain yearly Wages and continued in such Service under such hiring upwards of One Year & never did any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement that he has Three Children the lawful Issue by his said Wife namely Charlotte Elizabeth aged about Eighteen Years, Ann Rebecca aged about Twelve Years & John aged about Seven Years

Sworn this Day of Novr. 1791 before}
