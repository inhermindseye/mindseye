Middx to wit Mary Culbert Aged about Twenty Six Years on her her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Giles at the Grange Inn in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for fourteen Month at the Yearly Wages of four Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Three Weeks since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that one Robert Harrison late an Hostler to her said late Master is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Master House some time in the Month of October last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at several different time afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Robert Harrison & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 31st. day of May 1779 before me}

her Mary Culbert Mark
