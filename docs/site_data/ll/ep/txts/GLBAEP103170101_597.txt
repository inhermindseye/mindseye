Middlesex ss  Richard Scott maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house of Twelve pounds a year in Baileys space in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & resided thereon for Several years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year rent or upwards for Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement That he hath a wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 20 day of october 1777 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Richard Scott
