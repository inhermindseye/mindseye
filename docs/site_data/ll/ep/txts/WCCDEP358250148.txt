Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Lockhart aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married ever bound an Apprentice lived as an hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or ever paid Taxes That her father Alexr. Lockhart Rented and lived in a house in Virginia Street in the parish of Saint George in the County of Middlesex for several years and paid Taxes for the same That her said father died in the Workhouse of the said parish where her Mother now is

Sworn this 15th. day of Janry. 1795  M Addington J: Hloud

her Sarah Lockhart Mark

Witness Jas: Talboys
