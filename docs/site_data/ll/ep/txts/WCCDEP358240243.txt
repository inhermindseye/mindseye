Militia Man's Wiff Wife

St Clement Danes April 4th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  (to wit) Rachael Chapman Wife of Fredk Chapman now Residing at the House of No. 15 in Wych Street in the parish of St Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster and County Middlesex and being aged Sixty years and upwards the Wife of Frederick Chapman a Substitute now now serving in the West Middlesex Regiment of Militia Upon Oath saith that on or about the sixth day of May in the year of our Lord 1762 she this Examinant being then a Widow and unmarried she was Married in the parish Church of Great Yarmouth in the County of Norfolk (by Banns) to Frederick Chapman who as she verily believes) having no reason to think the contrary) was at that time a single man and had no Wife living And this Deponent further saith that her said Husband Frederick Chapman hath as she is informed and believes was duly sworn and enrolled on the 2d. day of March 1792 to serve as a Substitute in the West Middlesex Regiment of Militia for Christopher Cessie of Kensingtons in the sd County drawn to serve as a principal in the said Regiment and is now serving therein which said Regiment is now embodied & called out in to actual Service And this Deponent hath been so ever since the 28th. day of January last And this Deponent further saith that ever since the said 28th day of January last she this Examinant hath resided and now dwells in the said parish of St Clement Danes and is much distressed and unable to maintain herself

without assistance And her having already been obliged to pledge a part with many things for her subsistance

Sworn the 4th day of April 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Rachl. Chapman Rachael Chapman's Mark
