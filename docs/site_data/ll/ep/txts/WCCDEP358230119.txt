Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Gutteridge Aged about 48 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant for near two years with a Mr Hookins Saint Martins lane in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds that she hath left the said Service about five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of Feby 1800 before us Ws Woodenough Winster

Sarah Gutteridge her Mark

Witness J. Starling
