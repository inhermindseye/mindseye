Middlesex ss  Samuel Kidner maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre to Mr William Cook of Cow Lane in the Parish of St Sepulchre in the City of London  Cordwainer and Served the whole of his Apprenticeship there That he has not Since rented a house or Lodging of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Apprenticeship That he hath a wife named Mary to whom he was lawfully married and who is now absent from him in Places at present to him unknown

Sworn this. 20. day of Febry 1788 before  R Davies  Rob. Smith

Samuel Kidner
