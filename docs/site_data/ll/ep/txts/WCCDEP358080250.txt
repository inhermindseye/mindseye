Middx to wit  Thomas Edwards aged about Thirty Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one Richard Morris a Taylor at Highton in the County of Radnor and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Tenth day of June 1778 before R Butler

Thomas Edwards
