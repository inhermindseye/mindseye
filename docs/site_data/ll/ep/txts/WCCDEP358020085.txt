Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Williams aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that about Ten years ago she Intermarried with Wm. Williams her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Brides alias Bridget in the City of London that her said Husband is at present insane that he Rented and lived in a House in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Botolph not Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three Months at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and upwards that he hath quitted said House about Twelve Months since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23rd day of June 1788 R Butler.

Mary Williams
