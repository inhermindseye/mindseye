Middx  Martha Kidd aged abt. Years on the behalf of Jas. Mackintosh aged abt. 12 yrs. & Eliz: Mackintosh aged abt upwards of two years upon Oath says that James Mackintosh the father of the sd. Children lived in & Rented the Black Bull Alehouse in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for one year & a half or thereabts at the yearly Rent of twenty six Pounds & there dyed & hath been dead abt. one year says Mary Mackintosh the Mother of the sd. Child. is since also dead

Sworn the day of 1757 before}
