Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Catherine Glover aged thirty three Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to Thomas Glover her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex on the 26th Day of Sept 1785 - that after their Intermarriage they Rented & hired for about three Months in a House in Charles Long Ann in the Parish of Saint in the Fields at the Rent of £24 P Ann & that since  thing the said House neither her said Husband nor herself have done any Act Matter thing whereby to the last of her knowledge Information and belief  her said Husband or herself may have acquired any or subsequent Settlement and this Deponent further saith that the hath one Child now living by her said Husband Namely Elizabeth aged five Years or thereabouts and that her said Husband hath deserted & Refuses to Maintain her and her said Child and she this Deponent in poor and out of Work and in great Distress for necessaries

to maintain and provide for herself and said Child

Sworn this 28th Day of Septr 1791 before us  Sampr Wright  Thos. Gordon

Catharine his Mark Glover
