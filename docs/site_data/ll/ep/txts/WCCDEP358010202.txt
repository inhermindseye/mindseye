Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Harrison aged about of No:1 Three Herring Court Red Cross Street  Spinster  Maketh Oath and Saith That Mary Harriet Harrison the illegitimate Child of Mary Gray formerly Harrisons Now of the Age of Six year or thereabouts was born in the House of Appleyard a Baker in Charles Street Westmr. in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westmr. and that the said Mary Harrison the Mother never was that this Deponent knows of or believes Married before she was delivered of the said Child and that Richard Ireland  late of Bow Street Covent Garden deceased was as this Deponent hath been informed by the said Mary Harrison the Mother and Verily believes the real and true Father of the said Child.

Sworn this 26th. day of Febry 1788 N Bond John Croft

her Elizth. Harrison  Mark
