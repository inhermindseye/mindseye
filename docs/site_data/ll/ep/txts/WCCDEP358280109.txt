St. Clement Danes .

Elizabeth Lane aged 21 or thereabouts upon her Voluntary Oath Saith That she lived with Mrs. Levick of Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes as a hired yearly Servant at the Wages of £5 a year for 2 yrs and upwards. that she left her Service about 2 Months ago And further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith That she is Pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes And Further Saith That John Wilkinson a Hair dresser now living at No.1 Goldens Buildings in the Strand is the Father of the said Child he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent Body about the Month of January last and at sevl. Times since at one of which Times he did beget the Child or Childn. of which she is now Pregnant.

Sworn before me this 28 July 1787 .} Wm Kitchiner
