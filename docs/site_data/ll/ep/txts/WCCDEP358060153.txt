Middx  Daniel Nigh aged upwds. of 40 Years upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Rd. Newton  Esqr . in Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly wages of £6 for 3 Years & upwards & has not since he quitted that Service rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief Daniel Nigh

Sworn Augt. 15th 1751 before  Fran Bedwell
