Westmr. ss  Griffith Pritchard aged about 37 Years on his Oath says he never was married rented house or paid Taxes Says he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Thomas Blamier a Chymist on Dowgate Hill in the Parish of St. John the Baptist in the City of London for 2 Years at they yearly Wages of £14..14..0 hath quitted his said Service about 6 Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same over rented house paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 2nd. Day of May 1776 before  John Cross  Jno. Machin

Griffith Prichard
