Middlesex ss}  Frances Cargill the wife of John Cargill maketh oath that her Said husband lately lived in & rented a house of Fourteen Pounds a year in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & during Such his residence therein he was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That this Deponents Said husband has not Since rented a house or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere.

Sworn this. 22. day of Janry 1779 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Frances Cargill
