Middlesex ss  Mary Barnes the widow of Henry Barnes Labourer deced Maketh oath that her Said late husband in the Year One thousand Seven hundred and fifty nine lived in and rented a house in Hughes's Fields in the Parish of Saint Nicholas Deptford in the County of Kent and was charged to and Paid the Kings Tax for the Said house and by means thereof as She this Deponent is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Deponent Saith that her Said late husband did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent And this Deponent farther Saith that she this Deponent hath not done any Act since her said late husbands death to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 12th. day of August 1765 before us  Chris: Scott R Pell

Mary Barnes Her Mark
