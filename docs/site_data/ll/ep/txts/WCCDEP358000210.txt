Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr} to wit Christian Sheriff aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Thomas Sheriff her now Husband who has deserted her at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn about 13 years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living That her said Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Ann Gladman who lived at the Corner of the Pea Markett in Leadenhall Street in the Parish of Saint Peters Cornhill in the City of London that after her said Husband was out of his said Apprenticeship he has never done any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Depts. knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Janry. 1786 before M Addington Meading

Christian Sheriff
