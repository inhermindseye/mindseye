Middx  James Powell aged about 32 Years upon his Oath saith that he never was an Apprentice & Yearly Servant Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or any way gained a Settlement to his knowledge or beleife Says his Father  Wm. Powell Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Jno. Woodhouse in the Parish of Cheshunt in the County of Hertford & after his said Father he was out of his said Apprentiship he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt: for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt: in any other Places to this examinant knowledge or beleife

Sworne 10th: July 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of James Powell
