Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Boll aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Henry Boll her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Thomas Henry aged about Seven years and George aged about Six Weeks That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house Great Wild Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Thirty two Guineas that he hath quitted said House about Six  Months since which time he hath not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 10th.. day of August 1797 before us N Bond Jno Solfuy

Hannah Boll
