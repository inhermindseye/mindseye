Middx 

Mary Littlemoor aged abt. 59 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Wm. Littlemoor with whom she intermarried at St Cathn. by the Tower abt. 9 Years ago Says her sd. husband abt. Six Years since lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £23 in fountain Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for three Years & upwds. & there dyed and that she has not since the Decease of her sd. husband rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 18th. 1748 before} Tho Lane .

Mary Littlemoor
