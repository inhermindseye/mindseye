Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Yallop aged about Thirty Two years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Yallop (who now lives Servant with Commission Lowndes) and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Strand about fourteen years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in the Strand Opposite Hungerford Coffee House in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Sixty Pounds and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Eight years ago since which time her said Husband hath gained no other settlement

Sworn this 24th. day February 1781 before R Butler Abrm Baley

Elizth Yallop
