Middx to wit Jane Campbell aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lives as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Challenor a Butterman in Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and has so done upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and further on her Oath says that on the 3rd. day of July 1774 she was delivered of a Male Bastard in the House of Henry Leech a Staymaker at No 17 in Crown Street Westminster in the Parish of St. Margt. Westminster which was soon afterwards Baptized by the Name of Scipio and that she this Examinant is unable to maintain the same and further says that her said Child hath gained no other Settlement than by birth

Sworn this 11th. day of Septr. 1779 before R Butler 

Jen Campbel
