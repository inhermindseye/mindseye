St. Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Austin aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that with a Mr. Dean who kept the Sign of the Bag of Nails at Pimliea in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Two years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further saith that she has one Child living vizt. Sarah aged about 6 Months born a Bastard at the House of a Mr. Taylor in Houghton Street That George Bell a Soldier in the 1st. Regt. of Foot Guards is the Father thereof

Sworn this 2d. April 1788  Sampr Wright N Bond

her Sarah Austin Mark
