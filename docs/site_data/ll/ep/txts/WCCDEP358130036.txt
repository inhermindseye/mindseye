Middx  Wm. Hill upon Oath saith he was married to his Wife Margt . at the Fleet London abt. 4 yrs. ago by whom he has one Child Dorothy aged upwards of 3 years that he lived a yearly Servt. wth. Mr Banford or Butcher in Hog Island in the parish of St. Andw. in Holbn . above ha  Middlesex at the yearly Wages of £8 for one year & upwds. (and was during that time a Singleman ) that he quitted his sd. Service abt. Seven years ago and has not Since Rented ten Pounds by the year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn July 29th. 1755 before}
