Middlesex ss}  Lott Robertes maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant by with Mr Hopp a Sugar Baker in the Parish of st Ann in the County of Middlesex at Certain Wages and Continued in Such Service under such hiring there for the space of two years & upwards That he has not since lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year elsewhere nor rented a house lodging or other Tenemt of the yearly rent or Value of ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named catherine to whom he was married at the Fleet about thirty five years ago

Sworn this. 27. day of March 1787 before  R Davies  R: Smith

The Mark of Lott Roberts
