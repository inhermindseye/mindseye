Westmr. to wit Thompson Davis Aged about Fifty Seven Years in his Oath Says that he Intermarried with Martha his late wife in the City of Oxford about Thirty years ago and who has been dead about three Months and by whom he hath Six Children now living Vizt. Charles Aged about Twenty two years Vincent Aged about fifteen years both of whom are now provided for & further says that he has Four other Children and provided for Vizt. Thompson Aged about Twenty Years Elizabeth Ageed about Ten Years Thomas Aged about Seven Years Aged about five Years who never gained any Settlement either by Renting House or serving any Apprenticeship or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further says that he Rented and lived in a House in Great Russell Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for one Year and an half at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Four Pounds hath quitted the said House about Two Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. Day of Decr. 1772 before R. Butler Tho Kynaston

Tompson Davis
