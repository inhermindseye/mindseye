Westmr.  Patrick Cardiff aged about 46 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Margaret his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Nicholas in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland about Twenty seven Years ago by whom he has two Children Thomas aged about Ten Years and Robt. aged about Seven Years; says He Rented a house in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds lived therein about Two Years and then Quitted same and afterwards Rented a house in Change Court in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds lived therein about three Months and hath Quitted same about Two Years and hath not since Rented house or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn Decr. 31st. 1765 . before} Thos Kynaston

Patrick Cardiff
