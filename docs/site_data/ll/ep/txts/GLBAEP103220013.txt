Middlesex ss  John Bright of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Beadle maketh oath that it Appears by an Examination of Jane Hopperton  Singlewoman in writing taken on oath before Parker Rowlands  Esquire late one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the Sd County on the Seventh day of March one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy Seven That She had then a Child named Richard Johnson then Aged about two months which was born of her body in Milk Street in the Parish of Christ Church in the Said County the which Child was Bastard & illegitimate by reason that She the Sd Jane Hopperton was not married And this Depont. farthr Saith that it also Appears by a Pass Order bearing date the Said fifth day of March that the Said Jane Hopperton was removed from the Said Parish of Christ Church to the Sd Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate as the place of her last legal Settlement together with her Sd Bastard Child for Nurture And that in the Work house of the Sd last mentioned Parish the Said Child (as this Deponent has been informed & believes) hath ever Since remained & as Yet remains & hath not to the Knowledge or belief of this Deponent done Any Act to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this fifth day of January 1786 before R Davies Robt. Smith

John Bright
