Middlesex ss  Eleanor Mills the widow of Michael Mills deced maketh oath That since her sd husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr James Leaach a Plaisterer at Certain Wages in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the space of two years & upwards That she has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year or been married or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Swon this - 21. day of April 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Eleanor Mills
