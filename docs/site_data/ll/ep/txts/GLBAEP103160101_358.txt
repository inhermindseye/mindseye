Middlesex ss}  Jane Welch the widow of Edward Welch deced maketh oath that divers years ago her Said late husband Rented a house of Forty Pounds a year in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere Nor hath she this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Jane Welch
