Middx 

Abigall Robertson aged abt. 33 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a Male Bastd. Child on the 7th. Day of April Instt. in the house of Mrs. Whiteman in Beaufort Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex which has not yet been baptized and is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same  and that William Denley of High Wickham in the County of Bucks  Dealer in Lace did beget the sd. male bastard Child on her Body the sd. William Denley being acquainted with this Examinant had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of July last and at sevll. other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. male Bastard Child of which she was delivered

as aforesd. and that he the sd. William Denley and no other person is the true and reall father thereof

Sworn April 22d 1747 before  Tho Lane

Abigail Robertson
