Middx  Eliz: Palmer upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwds. of 30 years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Olford in Pater Street in the Parish of St. Peter in Bristol in the County of Somerset at the yearly wages of forty Shillings for one year and upward (and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 4th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of of Eliz: Palmer
