Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Martha Prentice aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the 29th. day of July 1797 She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Leo No. 97 Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been half baptized by the name of Samuel That one Samuel Payne who lives Servant with Mr. Phynn in Surry Street in the Strand is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Samuel Payne is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of Augt. 1797 before me  Gs Goodenough

M Prentice
