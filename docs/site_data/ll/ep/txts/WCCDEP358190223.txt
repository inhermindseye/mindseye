Middx   William Robert Clarke aged about Sixty Years upon Oath says that he rented a House in Craven Buildings in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx of the yearly Rent of about Thirty Pounds and lived in the same about Five years Says he hath not kept House or paid Land Tax or Poor Rate since he quitted the Same which is about thirty Years

Sworn the 20th day of February 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Wm: Robt: Clarke
