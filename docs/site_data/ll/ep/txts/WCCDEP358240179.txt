St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Mary Butler aged abt. 43 years singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly servant with Mr. King at the Jolly Butcher in St. Clement Lanes Gilbert Street Clare Market in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six pounds that she quitted that sevice about 12 years ago and hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes lived as an hired yearly Servant or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 22d. day of November 1792 before me } R. Butler

Mary Butler 's Mark

Witness Richd. Dobbins
