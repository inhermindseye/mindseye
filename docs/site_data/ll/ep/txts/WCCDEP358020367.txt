St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Mary Golston  wife of John Golston Aged 45 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband John Golston at the Chapel in Kentish Town in the County of Middlesex about  Nine years ago that her said Husband served as an Apprentice under Indentures with Mr. Thomas Norton at Turnham Green in the parish of Chiswick in the said County for about 5 years  that at the Expiration of that Term he left his Master without his consent or pervity went to sea in the Service of His Majesty and or the Reynolds in that her said Husband hath never gained any subsequent legal Settlement and this Deponent farther saith that this Deponent's said Husband hath left this Deponent about Eight days and  age and where has ago but where or on what occasion he is gone this Deponent known not And saith that about 14 Months ago this Deponent together with her said Husband werd Removed by Orders of Removal from the Liberty of the Rolls in the said County to Chiswick aforesaid and were by Virtue of the said Orders taken into the Workhouse and maintain at the charge of the said parish of Chiswick for about five Months again against which Orders of Removal there has been no Appeal to this Deponents knowledge or belief as theywere never Returned from that parish to the Liberty of the Rolls aforesaid

And Lad by this Deponent saith that she is now distressed and unable to maintain herself witht. Assistance

Sworn the 13th. Decr 1791 before me us  Sampr Wright Addington

Mary Goulston 's Mark
