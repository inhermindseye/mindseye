Westmr.  Alice Hooper aged about 36 Years Upon Oath says she is the wife of John Hooper (gone from her) with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London about fourteen Years ago; says her said husband is an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief; says before her intermarriage with the said John Hooper and whilst she was a Single woman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Grayham in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. John Southwark in the County of Surry for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds

Sworn the 11th. day of February 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Alice Hooper
