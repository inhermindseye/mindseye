Middx  Hannah Clark upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Edwd Clark to whom she was married at the Fleet London abt. 30 Years ago and that her sd. late husband rented a house of the yearly Rent of £20 in Clare Court within in the parish of St Clemt. Danes with in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx lived therein abt. twelve years and there dyed and this Examint. has not Since her sd. late husbands decease rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn June 5th. 1755 before}
