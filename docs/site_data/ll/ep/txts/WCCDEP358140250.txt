Middlx ss  Henry Read aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in Johnson's Court  Charing Cross in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds or thereabts. lived in said house about  One Year & hath quitted it abt. fifteen Years: Since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn May 18th. 1759 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Henry Read

Upon the Complt. of Mr Rust Ovr. of J: P.
