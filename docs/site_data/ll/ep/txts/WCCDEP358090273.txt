Middx to wit Bridget Field aged about Forty Years on he Oaths says she never was married, rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Vincent at the Punch Bowl in Hemlock in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of West minster for Sixteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds Hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago Since which time she hath gained no other Settlement either by renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or a to become a Bastard or Bastards and likely to be chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And further Says that One William Stent a Sh who lodges at a Green Stall in Five Fields Chelsea the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in April last, but the particular Day she does not recollect, at a House in Wych Street and at several times afterwards at One of which time he get her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Wm. Stent & no other person did beget the same & is the true Father thereof

Sworn this 3d Day of January 1782 before R Butler

her Bridgett Field Mark
