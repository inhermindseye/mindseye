Middlesex & Westmr.}  Ann Snow Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Dyer at the white Hart  Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr for One Year and Three Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish And further says that one Thomas Tooley a Journeyman Shoemaker who lodges at Mr. Stuarts a Taylor up Two Pair of Stairs is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his the said Thos. Tooley s Lodgings some time the beginning of Decr. last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at different times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Thos. Tooley and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 22d. day of Augt. 1783 before  Sampn Wright
