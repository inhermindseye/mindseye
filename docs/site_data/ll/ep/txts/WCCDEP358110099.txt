Westmr.  Sarah Walker upon Oath saith she intermarried with her prest. husband Constantine Walker in the parish Church of St Martin in the Fields on the 11th. Decr. 1749 and that her sd husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr John Williams  Gardiner at Mortlake in the County of Surry and never rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Septr. 30th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Walker
