Middx.  John Waters aged about 43 Years upon Oath says he lived in a House of his own in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St. Andrew  Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex and paid all Parish Taxes for the same several years Says he quitted his said House about Eighteen years ago and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Years paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 20th. Day of March 1756 before} Barth: Hammond  Samson Webb

The mark of John Waters
