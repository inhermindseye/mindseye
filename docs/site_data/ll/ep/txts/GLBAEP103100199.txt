Middlesex ss}  Frances Travers the widow of David Travers deced Maketh oath That She doth not know or Can in any manner Setforth or Discover the place of Settlement of her late husbands Parents or either of them But this Dept. Saith that She hath been informed by her Said late husband (& which information She believes to be true) That he was born in Mouse Alley in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and afterwards lived there as a hired Servant with one Mr. Baldwin a Butcher for more than the Space of a year and by means thereof as She is advised gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponts. Knowledge or belief her Said late husband never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement Nor hath she this Depont. Since her Said husbands death done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right And that about Twenty eight Years ago she was Married to her Said late husband at the Fleet

Sworn this day of December 1769 before}
