Middx  Deborah Martin  Wife of Joseph Martin upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her sd. Husband Joseph Martin on the 4th Day of May 1718 at the Parish Church of St. Margarett in the Liberty of Westmr . & that her sd. Husband now liveth in & rents a House in Stichbourn Court in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds this Examint. being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 24th. of Novr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Deb Martin
