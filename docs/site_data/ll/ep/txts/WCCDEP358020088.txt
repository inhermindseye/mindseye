St. Clement Danes

John Hoggin aged 60 years or thereabts. upon his Oath Saith, That he was born in Ireland , served his Time in Linmarick came to Bristol when about 20 years of Age has ever since Worked as a Journey Man Taylor , does not believe he ever Rented £10 a year or has done any Act to gain a Settlement That Mary Cornelly to whom he was Married at the Roman Chapple Lincolns Inn Fields has a Settlement in St. Mary he bone by living at the Grotto Coffee House . that his Daughter Arabella was born in Goldsmiths Street Gough Square and belongs to the Parish of Saint Andw. Holborn

Sworn before me this 11 July 1788 R Butler.

John Hoggin
