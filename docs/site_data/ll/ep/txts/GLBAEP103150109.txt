Middlesex ss}  Daniel Mc Daniel maketh oath that divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Yearly Wages with Michael Smith  Tabaccoinst in Sun Yard in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Four Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Sd parish That he this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of January 1776 before}

The Mark of Daniel McDaniel
