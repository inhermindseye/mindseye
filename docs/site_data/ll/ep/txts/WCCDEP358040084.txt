Westmr.  Johanna Gilding upon Oath saith she never was married Says upwards of two Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Goodiff in Tower Street in the Parish of St. Alhallows Barking London at the Yearly Wages of £4 for one Year & upwards Says she has not since she Quitted the said Mr. Goodiff Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 19th. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Johanna Gilding
