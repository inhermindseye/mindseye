Middx  Saml. Stackhouse upon Oath saith he intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Fleet London abt. 5 Yrs. ago by whom and that he hath one Child Saml . aged about 8 Yrs- and that he was bound apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with his Mother Ann Stackhouse Wido . & that he lived the last three months of his sd. Apprentiship with his sd. Mother in Pear Free Court Clerkenwell and that he rented a Shop in Claremarket at the Rent of Six Shillings P Week for one Year or thereabouts & during all that time lodged at Mr. Fountains in the sd. Market and has not since he quitted the sd. Shop rented ten pounds a Year or Pd. Parish taxes

Sworn June 24th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Samuel Stackhouse
