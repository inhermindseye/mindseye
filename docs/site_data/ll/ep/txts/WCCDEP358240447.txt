Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. at High Wycomb  to wit Ann Jeycock Jacock aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes  ever lived as an Hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born as she has been informed at High Wycomb in the Parish of High Wycomb in the County of Bucks where this Deponents Father and Mother kept House for several years

Sworn this 28th. day of October 1794 before J Pownall M Addington

her Ann Jeycock mark

Witness Ann Jacock
