Middlesex ss}  Martha Calder the Widow of William Calder deced maketh oath That divers Years ago & when She was the widow of Patrick Calder her first husband deced & before her marriage with the Said William Calder She this Deponent was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages by & with one Joshua Burton of the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Soap Boiler & Continued in Such Service for about the Space of Four Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That this Deponts Sd Second husband William Calder was by Birth a Scotchman & She this Deponent Cannot is any manner Set Forth or discover Where he the sd William Calder was born nor Whether he ever was an Apprentice or ever lived as a hired Servant for a year or whether he ever rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or ever Served any Parish office or ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself She this Depont never having received any Information relative thereto And this Deponent Saith that Since his death She hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 11th day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

Martha Calder
