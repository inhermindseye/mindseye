St Clement Danes 11th. May 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St George Middx  (to wit) Sarah Lockhart  singlewoman aged 34 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she hath never lived as an hired yearly Servant, served an Apprentice Apprenticeship Rented any House or apartment of ten pounds a Year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own right Saith that She hath been informed and believes She is the legitimate Child Daughter of Alexander Lockhart deceased and Mary his Wife And farther saith that her said father Rented and Resided in an House in Virginia Street in the parish o f Saint George in the East in the County of Middlesex for several years at the annual Rent of ten pounds and quitted Died there and that her said Mother hath not Since he acquired any subsequent legal Settlement House after the Death of this Examinant's father to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And that her said Mother is now maintained in the Workhouse of the said parish of St George

Sworn the 11th day of May 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker Sarah Lockhart
