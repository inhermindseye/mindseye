Middx  The Examination of Henry Ingram Passed from the Parish of Hamstead in the County of Middx by Order bearing date the 13th. Octr. Inst. Who upon his Oath saith yt. about Twelve Years ago he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Sr. Jno. Cheshire in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County aforesaid for the space of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds And this Examint. further saith that he hath not since that time Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & that he is now Sick & Poor & not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworn this 16 Day of Octr. 1739 before} Jas: Gronous

Henry: Ingram
