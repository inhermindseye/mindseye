Middx  Mary Radbourne aged 48 Years upon her Oath saith that abt. 28 Years since she intermarried with her present Husband  Thos. Radbourne at the Fleet London that the sd. Thos. Radbourne is now a Soldier in the 1st. Regimt. of Foot Guards (a broad & beyond the Seas) That the sd. Thomas Radbourne lived in & rented a House in Kings Head Court in the parish of St. Clemt Danes of the Yearly Rent of £12. for the Space of 6 Months & hath left the same abt. 13 Yrs. since which time he hath not rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 3d. Febry 1743 before} Tho Lane

Mary Radbrn
