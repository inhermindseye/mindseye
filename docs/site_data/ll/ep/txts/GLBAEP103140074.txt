Middlesex ss}  Catherine Moody  Singlewoman maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or either of them That She this Depont. has been informed by her Father & which information She believes to be true That She was born in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex That She this Depont. never lived as a hired Servant for a year in anyone place or was ever bound apprentice nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath a Child named Ann Cotton aged about twelve weeks which is a Bastard & illegitimate & was born in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex & that one John Cotton late of the parish of St George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx  Bricklayer did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child.

Sworn this 24th: day of December 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Catherine Moody
