Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Mc. Motary the wife of Thomas Mc. Motary maketh oath that her sd. husband is a soldier and now abroad in his Majesties service as this Depont. believes And this Depont. saith yt. her said husband is a Scotsman and never gained any settlement in England to her knowledge or belief But he hath informed her that he was born in ye highlands of Scotland but ye Particular Place or Parish she cannot set forth And this Depont. farther saith that she was born in the Parish of Maidstone in the County of Kent And that she was never an Apprentice nor a hired servant by the year nor hath done any Act in her own right to gain a legal settlement either before or since her Marriage And this Deponent saith that she hath three Children to wit James aged about eleven years William aged about Nine years and Ann Aged about four years the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her said husband

Sworn this 5th: day of December 1760 before  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

The mark of Elizabeth McMotary
