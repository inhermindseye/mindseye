Aldgate Middx

Joseph Ware Maketh Oath that he Servd his Apprenticeship by Indenture to John Brown of Blew Anchor Alley in the Parish of Saint Luke in Middx Citizen And Carpenter and hath gaind no other Settlement Since hath a wife named Elizabeth Ware

Sworn 12th day of December Ano Dom 1747 before Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

his Joseph Ware Mark
