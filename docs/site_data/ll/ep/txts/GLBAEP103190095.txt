Middlesex ss}  Rachael Benfield  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Dederick Hoste in Chick Lane in the Liberty of Hatton Garden in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the Said County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the space of Two Years & upwards That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 7. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Rachael Benfield
