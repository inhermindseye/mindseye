Westmr.  Catha. China  Wido . of John China upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. John China at the New Church in the Strand on or about the 24th. Day of Octr. 1731 Says he has been dead about four Years Says her said late husband lived in and rented a house in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £24 or thereabouts for two years & upwards & left same upwds. of Eight Years ago Says her sd. husband (after he left sd. house) or this Examinant since his Death have not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 25th 1747 before} Wm.Jones

The Mark of Cath. China
