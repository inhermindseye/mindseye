Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Budd aged about 49 years now chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and saith That he was bound an Apprentice to Martha Silvester of Little Moorfields in the Parish of Saint Giles within Cripplegate in the City of London for Seven years that he served under such Indenture for Five years after which he never rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Janry. 1798 before us M Addington R. Ford

Wm. Budd
