Middx to wit Elizabeth Stewart Aged about nineteen years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for One year says her Father William Stewart now Rents and lives in a House in Water Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and as so done for three years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds and  pays Parish Taxes due for the same and further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with  Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish (she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) and that One James Richardson an attorney , as she Believes, who lives at >No 6 Ground Floor Lyons Inn in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his Brothers Chambers in Lyons Inn aforesaid some time in the Months of November last but the particular Day she does not now now remember and at several Different times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Richardson and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 7th. Day of May 1781 before  Sampn Wright

her Elizth. Stewart  mark
