Middx to wit Mary Woodward aged about forty Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Rented and lived in a House in New Inn Passage for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds hath quitted same upwards of seventeen Years ago and after quitting same she never gained any other subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 27th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

Mary Woodward
