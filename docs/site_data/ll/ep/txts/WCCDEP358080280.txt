Middlesex to wit Mary Worrellow Aged about Twenty Three years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one John Crocketta  Taylor in the Parish of Hampstead in the County of Middlesex upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said upwards of Nine Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 25th. day of August 1778 before  Abrm Baley M Addington

Mary Worrellow
