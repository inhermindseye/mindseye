Middx & Westmr.} to wit Maria Oliver Aged about twenty Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father rented and lived in a House in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of £40s. a Year and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same and were her said Father Died which is about five Years ago Since which time this Examinant hath gained no other subsequent Settlemt. And furr. on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Robert Mabberley a Herald painter in Long Acre is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of April last was twelvemonth but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. difft. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as afsd. and that the said Robt. Mabberley and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 15th. Day of Janry. 1777 . R Butler

Maria Oliver
