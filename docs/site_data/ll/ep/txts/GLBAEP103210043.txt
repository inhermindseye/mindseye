Middlesex ss}  Nathaniel Hickinson maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to Benjamin Dixon  Farrier of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & after having Served five Years & half of Such his Apprenticesp in the Sd Parish he was turned over to his Grandson Joseph Dixon & Served the remainder thereof with him there and has not Since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18 day of December 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Nathaniel Hickinson
