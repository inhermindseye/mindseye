St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  Amelia Layton  singlewoman aged 43 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith That She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Wm. Geo. Freeman  Esquire in Surry Street in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One year and Eleven Months or thereabouts at the Annual Wages of Eight Guineas that she quitted that Service about 22 year ago and hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant Rented any House or Tenement of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 26th Septr. 1792 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

her Amelia Layton Mark

Witness B. Newman
