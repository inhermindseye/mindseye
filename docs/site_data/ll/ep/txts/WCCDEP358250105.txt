St Clement Danes  August 20th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit)  Battersea Surrey  John Chandler  singleman aged twenty five years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Christopher Baldwin  Esquire in the parish of Battersea in the County of Surry Surrey for one year and upwards at the annual wages of £7 that he quitted that served about Nine years ago and hath not since lived as an hired Yearly Servant for a Year together under one hiring or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement whatsoever where to the best of this Examinant knowledge or belief

Sworn the twentieth day of August 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner  Edwd: Naish

his John Chandler Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
