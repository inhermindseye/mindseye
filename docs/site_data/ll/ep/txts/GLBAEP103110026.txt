Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Osman of the Parish of Saint George in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Burford Camper - Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this fifth Day of October 1771

Who on her oath Saith That on the Twentieth Day of September one thousand Seven hundred and Seventy in King Henry Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County of Middlesex She the Said Ann Osman was delivered of a Male Bastard Child Since Baptized & Registred by the name of Richard Watkins And that he said Bastard Child is likely to be come Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that one Richard Watkins of the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry  Mariner did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Taken & Sworn the Day & year above Written before me B Camper

The Mark of Ann Osman
