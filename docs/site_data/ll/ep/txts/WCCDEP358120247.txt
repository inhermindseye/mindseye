Westmr.  Mary Smith upon Oath saith she is the widow of John Smith an Irishman of whose settlement she can give no Account that she is likewise Native of Ireland & never gained a settlemt. in England & that whilst a Singlewoman she lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Leecham at Recreaden in the parish of Newcastle in the County of Dublin for two years at the yearly Wages of Forty Shillings

Sworn the 18th. Decr. 1754
