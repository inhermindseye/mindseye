Middx  Abraham Strangeways upon his Oath saith that on the 29th- Day of July 1733 he intermarried with his prest. Wife Mary at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Feilds That abt. Eight Years since he lived in & rented a House in Widegate Alley in Bishopgate Strt. in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Bishopgate London for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds That since he left the sd. House he hath not rented £10. a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife That he hath one Child vizt. Elizth: aged upwds. of Eight Yrs- & being Poor is act able to maintain himself & Family

Sworne 4th- Decr. 1742 before} Wm. Jones

Abraham Strangeways
