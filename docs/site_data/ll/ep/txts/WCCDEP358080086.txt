Middlesex & Westmr.}  Grace Taylor Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Timothy Smart who lives at the House of one Mr. Glanville in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx upwds. of one Year at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about two Years and an half ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that one Patrick Gantley a Taylor at the Dukes Head in St. Martins Street Leicester Fields is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime about the beginning of May last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as aforesaid and that the said Patrick Gantley and no other person did beget the same and that he is the Real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 8th. Day of Octr. 1776 before R Butler

Grace Taylor
