Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Frances Nevett aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That on the 19th. day of July 1794 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of William That one William Langhorn who lives with one Mr. Storey a Butcher in Church Court in the Strand is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon And that the said William Langhorn and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 22d. day of July 1795 } 

her Fras. Nevett Mark
