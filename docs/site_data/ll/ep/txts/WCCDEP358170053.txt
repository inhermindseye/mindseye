Middx  The Examination of Anne Greenwood aged abt. Fifty four Years now in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 11th- Day of May 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that abt. 28 Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Mozely at the Rose Tavern by Temple Barr for the Space of Four Yrs- & upwds- & further on her Oath saith yt. she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Mozeleys Service rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before}

the Mark of Anne Greenwood
