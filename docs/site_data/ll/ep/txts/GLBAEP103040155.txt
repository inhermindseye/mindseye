Middlesex ss.  Elizabeth Verdon  Singlewoman maketh Oath that about Twenty Years ago she was hired as a Servant by the Year to Mr. Renshaw of Nr 8 Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Slop seller at certain Yearly Wages & continued in his Service under such hiring Upwards of Three Years that she has not since she quitted his Service lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any other Parish or Place for the space of One Year together or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain Subsequent settlement, and that she never was Married

Sworn this Day of Jany. 1799 before}
