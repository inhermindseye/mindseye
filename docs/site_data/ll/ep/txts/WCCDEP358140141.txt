Middx  Robt. Wimberley of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx, Turner on the behalf of Sarah Yates aged upwards of fifteen Years upon Oath says that Wm. Yates the father of the said Sarah Yates Rented a house in South Audley Street in the Parish of St. George  Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards and lived therein several Years & never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief And the sd. Sarah Yates upon her Oath says she never was an Apprentice or a Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Robt. Wimberley Sarah Yeates
