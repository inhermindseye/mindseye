Middx  Wm. Avery upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice by the Church wardens & Overseers of the poor of the Parish of St. Giles in the fields  Middx to the Age of 24 Years and Served the last Seven Years of the sd. Jane with Jas. Stevens a Chimney Sweeper in Red Cross Street in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate London and that he never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 9th. 1748 before } Wm: Jones

The Mark of Wm. Avery
