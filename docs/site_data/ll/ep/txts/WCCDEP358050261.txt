Middx 

Mary Bedan aged abt. 27 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt for one Year rented Ten pounds a Year or paid parish Taxes and that her father mathias Bedan lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds in Hollywell Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlx for the Space of 20 Years and there dyed

Sworn May 29th 1750 before} Tho Lane .

Mary Bedan
