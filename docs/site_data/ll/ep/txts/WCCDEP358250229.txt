Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Bunn aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of August 1789 she Intermarried with William Bunn her present Husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields That her said Husband never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That he informed this Deponent which Information she verily believes to be true that he was born opposite the Bulls head at Clapham in the Parish of Clapham in the County of Surrry where his father kept house for many Years

Sworn this 24th. day of Febry 1796  J Hind Rt Thompsan

her Ann Bunn Mark

Witness B Newman
