Middx  Eliz Smith aged about 40 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about Ten Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Day in Howard Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for one Year and upwards and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 5th. 1749 before}
