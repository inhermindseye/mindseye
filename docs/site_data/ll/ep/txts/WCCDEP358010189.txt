Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit John Barlow aged about 24 years upon his Oath saith that he never was married that he has been informed by his Father which Information he believes to be true that he was born in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint Church Danes in the County of Middlesex that he never was bound an Apprentice if never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain any other Settlement than by Birth

Sworn this 1st. day of February 1788 R Butler

John Barlow
