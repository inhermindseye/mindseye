Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Mumford of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Single woman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County of Middlesex this Day of 1771

Who on her oath Saith That about five Years ago in the sd. Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd. County She the Said Mary Mumford was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which Said Child hath been Since Baptized & registred by the Same of Mary And that about three Years ago in the Same Parish She the sd. Mary Mumford was delivered of quote Female Bastard Child Which said last menconed Bastard Child hath been Since Baptized & Registred by the name of Ann And that about Seven Months ago in the Same Parish she the said Mary Mumford was delivered in the same Parish of another Female Bastard Child which said last menconed Child hath been Since Baptized & registred by the name of Susanna And that James Russell now or late of the said Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate  Labourer did get her with Child of the Said Several Bastard Children all which are likely to become Chargeable to the sd Parish

Sworn this day of 1771 before}
