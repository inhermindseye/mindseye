St Clement Danes  August 21st 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St.Mary le Strand (to wit) Elizabeth Hartwell  singlewoman aged twenty one years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Clinch at the Eagle Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of St Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx for a year and upwards at the Annual Rent of £ Wages of six Guineas that she quitted that service about in or about the Month of October last and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement so the best of her knowledge or belif belief that she is pregnant poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 21st. day of Augst. 1793 before us N Bond John Collict

Elizabeth Hartwell Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
