Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.  to wit Eleanor Lackey aged about 70 years upon her Oath saith that about 40 years ago she Intermarried with Hugh Lackey at the Parish Church of Abbin in Ireland that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Parkers Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for 15 years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and Paid Taxes hath quitted said House about 20 Years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing Whereby she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1786  Jno: Wright  Jno Barnfather

her Eleanor Lockey Mark
