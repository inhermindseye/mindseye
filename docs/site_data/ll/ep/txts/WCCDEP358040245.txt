Westmr. 

Hannah Moore wife of Samuel Moore (gone from her) upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Saml. Moore at a Chappel in Bath Somersetshire about 12 Years ago and that her sd. husband was born at Belfast in Ireland and Served his Apprentiship with a Cooper there and never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Wm: Jones

The Hannh Mark of Moore
