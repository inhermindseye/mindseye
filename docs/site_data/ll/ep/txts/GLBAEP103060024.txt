Middx  Daniel Jeffereys maketh Oath and Saith that about Thirty Years Ago he was Bound out Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to Mr . George Manning in the parish of Chelmsford in the County of Essex  Cordwainer and served all his said Apprenticeship with his said Master in the said parish and has gained no other Settlement since to his knowledge And that he hath a Wife Named Alice and three Children (Namely) William Aged Seven Years Alice five Years and James two Years

Sworn this 23d. day August 1744 Before us Robert Dennett Rd Ricards

Daniel his Mk Mark Jeffereys
