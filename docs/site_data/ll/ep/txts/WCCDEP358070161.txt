Westminster to wit Elizabeth Hill aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath says she never was married Rent House or paid Taxes Says she lived hired Yearly Servant with Mr. George Coupland a Carpenter in Clement Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr for four Years & an half at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted herr said Serve upwards of five Months & hath not since she quitted the same done Any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard a Bastards and to become Chargable to the sd Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Edward Johnson a Carpenter who she now believes is gone to Peterborough is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some day the Week after Easter last past in the House of her said Dunstar but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Edward Johnson & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 11th. day of Janry 1774 before Tho. Kynaston

Elizabeth Hill
