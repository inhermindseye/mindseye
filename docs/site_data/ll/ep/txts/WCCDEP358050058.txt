Middx 

19th. June 1749

Mary Waters  upon on behalf of Eliz Griffith upon her Oath saith she has been Informed and beleives the sd. Eliz Griffith married her present husband John Griffith in the parish Church of St. Paul near Oxmontown Green at Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland about thirteen Years ago by whom she hath one Child viz. David aged upwds. of Eleven Years and that she hath been further Informed the said John Griffith Served his Apprentiship with a Shoemaker in St. Pauls Parish aforesd. never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before} Tho Lane

Mary Watters
