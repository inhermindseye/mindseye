Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Mary Thomas aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Matthews at the Lodge in Clements Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Sixteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Augt. 1798 before me } W Carter

her Mary Thomas Mark
