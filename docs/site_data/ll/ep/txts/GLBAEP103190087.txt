Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bryant  Singlewoman maketh oath That her late Father Joseph Bryant deced in his life time rented a house in the  Parish of St Paul Deptford in the County of Kent & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & also Served his Apprenticeship in the Same Parish & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That she this Deponent was born in the Parish of St. Paul Deptford aforesd & never lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year Save in the Middle Temple Lane in the Temple London where this Deponent lived with one Mr Jones Stationer for the Space of a year & upwards That she this Depont was never Married nor hath She done any act to gain a Settlement for herself Except as aforesaid That She this Deponent hath a Child named James Bryant aged about Ten Years which Child was born in the Lying Inn Hospital in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in Surry and is illegitimate & a Bastard & has done no Act to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 30. day of May 1780 before.  Jasper Clarke  Jno Sherwood

Eliz Bryant
