Middx  Sarah Sterrick upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwds. of three Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Parry a Peruke maker in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds, three pounds ten Shillings and four pounds for three Years and upwards and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

 Sworn Novr. 6th. 1749 before}

The mark of Sarh. Sterrick .
