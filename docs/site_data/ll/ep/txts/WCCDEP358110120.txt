Middx  Jane Batson upon Oath Saith she never was married  and that she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Thomas at the Globe Tavern in the Strand in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of  for one year & upwds quitted her sd. Service about five years ago & has not since rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr. 23d. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Jane Bettson
