Westmr. ss  Catherine Wetherspuron Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxeses Says she lived with Mr. Crofts a Wine Merchant in the parish of Twickenham in the County of Middlesex for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of four pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted his said Service upwards of One Year and four Months since which time she never gained any other Subseqt. Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child on the 25th day of Decr last in the House of Mrs. Nailer in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which has since been baptized by the Name of Hester and is now become Chargable to said Parish she beeing Poor and unable to maintain the same And that one Mr. Robert William Warburton Son of Robt. William Warburton a Distiller in Drury Lane is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at Mrs. at Mr. Spackman House at Twickenham in the sd. County of Middlesex is the he having had Carnal knowledge of her in April last Past but the Particular day she does not now remember at which time he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Robert William Warburton Junr . and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 6th. day of January 1775 . before R Butler

The Mark of Catherine Wetherspuron
