Westmr.  John Robinson upon Oath saith he was born in the Parish of Whitchurch in the County of Salop where his Father lived in and rented a house and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says he has one Child by his late wife deced (viz) Eliz. aged upwds. of Eight Years

Sworn the 6th. Day of Octr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

John Robinson
