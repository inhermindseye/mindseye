Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to St. Clements to wit Ann Mulford aged about 50 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to John Mulford her present Husband at the Parish Church of Windsor about 22 years ago and by whom she has 3 Children living vizt. John aged 21 years provided for Sarah aged 9 years and Joseph aged 7 years unprovided for That her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 5 years & upwards at the yearly Rent of £20 and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about 3 years ago since which time her said Husband has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere and that this Examinant and her two Childn. vizt. Sarah & Joseph was passed from Saint Lukes Old Street to the sd Parish of Saint Clement Danes were this Examinant and her two Children are now Chargeable and that her said Husband has deserted her about 3 Weeks ago and that he Refuses to maintain this Examinant and her Family

Sworn this 17th March 1785 R Butler

her Ann Mulford mark
