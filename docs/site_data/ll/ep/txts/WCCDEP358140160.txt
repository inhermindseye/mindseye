Middx.  James Philpott aged abt. 71 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £18 or thereabouts and hath quitted same abt. Fifteen Years, since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 16th. day of Septr. 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

James Philpott
