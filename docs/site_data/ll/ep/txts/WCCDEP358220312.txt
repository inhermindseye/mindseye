Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Powell aged about fifty Seven Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Benjamin Powell who now lives with a Relation at Barnett Says her said Husband Rented a House in Bedford Court Theobalds Row in the parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and quitted same about Twelve Years ago and hath not since she quitted the said House ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes

Sworn this 2d. day of January 1772 before R. Butler W Kelyngs

Elizth The Mark of Powell
