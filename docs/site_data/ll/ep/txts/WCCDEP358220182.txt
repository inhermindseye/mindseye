Middlesex & Westminster }  Jane Massa Aged about Thirty four Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Thomas Massa (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish of Saint Pauls Covent Garden about fourteen Years Ago and by whom she hath five Children now living Vizt: Thomas Aged about thirteen Years William Aged about Eleven Years Muchfruit Aged about Nine Years (the before mentioned three being already provided for) Charlott Aged about Seven Years and Ann Aged about One Year and on half Says that her said Husband Rented three separate Farms in the Parish of South Weal in the County of Essex for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Sixty Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said Farm about three Years ago and after he quitted the same he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 9th: day of October 1770 before us} Thos Kynaston R Butler

Jane Massa
