Eliz: Wondey aged abt. 66 Years upon Oath says he was Married to Thos. Wondey her present Husbd. at the fleet London abt. forty six Years ago Says he Rented and lived in a house in Villar's Street York buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for three Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of £30, and hath quitted said house abt. Ten Years; since which time he hath not Rented Ten pounds by the Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn this 15th. day of Jany. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell
