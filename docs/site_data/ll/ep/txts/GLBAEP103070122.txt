Middx ss  John Jones Maketh oath that about Sixteen years ago he rented a house of Sixteen Pounds a year in the parish of St. James in Dukes place London and paid Parish taxes ind also served his apprenticeship in ye said Parish and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent saith that he not since rented a house of Ten pounds a year nor upwards nor served any parish offices nor gained any subsequent settlement And this Deponent hath a wife Named Sarah And also Six Children to wit James aged about fourteen years John aged about ten years Deborah aged about seven years George aged about five years Hannah aged abt. three years ind Martha aged about eighteen Months the lawfull Issue of this Depont. by his sd wife

Sworn this 29th day of October 1754 before  Walter Berry  Richd: Ricards

John Jones
