Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Christopher Gainsford aged about 72 years upon his Oath saith That he Rented and lived in a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Forty pounds that he hath quitted said house about Thirty years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 7th. day of June 1797 before me } J Hunter

Chris. Gainford
