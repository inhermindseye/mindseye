Westmr. to wit Thomas Clayton aged about forty five Years on his Oath Says he is now a Widower and that no Children living Says that before his Intermarriage with his late Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Cripps a Goldsmith in Saint James Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year and Three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of Nine Pounds for the first Year and Ten Pounds for the last Year hath quitted his said service upwards of Twenty four Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of July 1773 before} G Wrights:

Thomas Clayton
