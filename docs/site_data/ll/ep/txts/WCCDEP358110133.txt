Westmr.  Mary Kane upon Oath Saith she is the wido. of John Kane who dyed near 4 Yrs. since and by whom she hath one Child Rose aged near 7 years and that she hath been Informed & believes her said late husband lived a Yearly Servant (whilst a Singleman) Wth. Mr Danl. Doghead a Farmer in the parish of Clift in the County of Kent and for 3 Yrs. & upwds and never after he quitted his sd. Service nor has this Examint. Since his Decease Rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr.

Sworn Decr. 1st. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Kane
