Middlesex ss}  John Beale maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice to Henry Drewett of the Parish of Westbury in the County of Wilts  Taylor and Served five years of Such his apprenticeship there And by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish That he this Deponent hath not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that he hath a Wife named Catherine and two children namely John aged about four years and Catherine aged about nine Months the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his Said Wife

Sworn this Day of December 1772 before}

John Beale
