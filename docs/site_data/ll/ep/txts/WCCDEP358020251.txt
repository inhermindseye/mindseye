St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster (to wit Sarah Read  Widow Aged abt. 50 Years Upon her Oath saith That She was Married to her late Husband John Read at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn about fourteen Years ago, that her Husband sometime abt. the Year 1780, Rented an House in Cow Cross in the parish of St. Sepulchre without , in the County of Middlesex for Six Months or thereabouts which they quitted about Michaelmas in the said Year 1780, since which time he in his life time Nor she this Examinant since her Death have Rented any House or Apartment of £10 Per Year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief her said late Husband in his life time or she this Examinant since his Decease might have gained any legal Settlement

Sworn before Us the 10th. day of febry. 1791 . E Read Wm Hyde

Sarah Reads Mark
