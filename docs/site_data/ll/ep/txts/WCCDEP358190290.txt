Middx  Mary Williams  wife of Roger Williams on Behalf of Susanna Wotton her Neice an Infant aged About Six Years upon Oath Says the sd. Susanna Wotton is the Daughter of Wm Wotton by Elizth: his Wife both Deceased Says the said Wm Wotton served his Apprenticeship with one Willm Lewis A Boat Builder in Trinity Street in the Parish of St Augustin otherwise St Austin in the City of Bristall and never rented any House or Pd: any Taxes as this Examinant hath been informed and believes.

Sworn ye 20th Day of Octr. 1762 Before} Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

Mary Williams
