Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Bridget Whyley aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Graham in Queen Street Edjware road in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Twelve Months since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Janry. 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

her Bridget Whyley Mark
