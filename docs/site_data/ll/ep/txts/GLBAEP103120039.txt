Middlesex ss}  Margaret Blair a singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago her Father Rented a House and Farm of the yearly Rent or Value of Fifty Pounds in the Parish of Morpeth in the County of Northumberland & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there & to her knowledge or belief did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement That she this Deponent was born in the Same parish & was part of her Said Fathers Family when he so rented the Said Farm and that she this Deponent never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 24th: day of March 1773 before B Camper

The Mark of MB Margaret Blair
