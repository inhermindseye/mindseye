Middx to wit Mary Holloway Aged about Thirty Two years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says She lived a hired yearly Servant with John Bennett a Dealer in Coals in New Bound Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years and an half at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for One year

Sworn this 20th. Day of Feby. 1781 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Mary Holloway Mark
