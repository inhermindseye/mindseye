Middlesex ss}  Jane Lee  Singlewoman maketh oath That She well knows a Child named John Codling aged about eight years and four months now present with her & which child was born of the Body of this Deponts. late Sister Elizabeth Cock  widow in Angel Court in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County this Deponent being present at the Birth of Such Child the Which Child is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason as this Deponent has been informed by her Said Sister Cock & believes that She was never Married to one Jonathan  which Shod alasd was the father of the Said Child Codling and which Said Child has not done any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right & is likely to become Chargeable to the Said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate .

Sworn this 10th day of Septemr 1776 before  Saml. Wood

The Mark of Jane Lee
