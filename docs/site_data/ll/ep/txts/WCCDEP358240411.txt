Elizth. Graves

St Clement Danes May 17th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Clemt. Danes  (to wit) Elizabeth Greaves  singlewoman aged twenty five years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived with Mrs. Woodrow in the parish of St. Clement Danes as an hired yearly Servant for about three years at the Annual wages of £5. that during the first and greatest part of such her service she was with her said  Mastress in Carey Street and during the last 6 Months she dwelt with her at the House of Mr. Bulmer in the Strand that she quitted the same service in or about the year 1788 and hath not obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 17th day of May 1794 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Greaves 's Mark

Witness B Newman
