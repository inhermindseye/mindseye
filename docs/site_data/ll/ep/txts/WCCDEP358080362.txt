Middlesex  Mary Hughes Aged upwards of fifty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of William Hughes who died about Eighteen Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children living she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Breanton a Widow Lady in the Capacity of a Cook in Charlott Street Rath bone Place in the Parish of St. Pancras in the County of Middlesex for One Year at the Yearly Wages of Ten Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley  Sampn Wright

her Mary Hughes Mark
