Middx 

Geo Slaney upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Mary at the Fleet London abt. 6 Yrs. ago and that he Served Six Years & upwards of his Apprentiship with Thos. Redfearn a Dyer at Uttoxeter in Staffordshire & then Enlisted into his Majestys Service & never was assigned over to any person rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 7th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

George Slaney
