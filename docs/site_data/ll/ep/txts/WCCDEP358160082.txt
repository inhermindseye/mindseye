Middx The Exaicon of Sarah Cook  Singlewoman liveing as a Yearly hired Servt . at the Temple Eating House in Shier Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. County of Middx who upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is Pregnant with Child wch when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she this Exaiant being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when born And further upon her Oath Saith that one John Williams now a waiter at the Maidenhead in Ram Alley in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West  London did begett the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her body he the sd. Jno. Williams liveing fellen Servt . with this Exaiant at the Temple Eating House as aforesd. there became acquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her body two Sevll. times in the Month of March last at one of wch times he got her with the Bastard Child or Children with wch she is now Pregnant & that he & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof and this Exaiant further Saith that she hath lived upwards of one Yeare at the sd. Temple Eating a House & hath not gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworn the 12th. Day of Octr. 1739 before me} Jas: Gronous

Sary Cook
