Middx  Hanh. Hetherley upon her Oath saith she never was married And that about 15 or 16 Years since she lived a Yearly Servt with Mr Barnes a Perukemaker in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for two Years and upwards and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 17th. 1749 before H Yeildens

The mark of Hanh. Hetherley
