Westmr. 

Mary Lane aged upwds. of 10 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes that her father Willm Lane deced Rented a Tenement which contained a Shop and two Rooms over it in Red Lyon Yard  Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex for which he paid £13 a Year and lived therein upwards of one Year that the said Tenement had no Collering under it nor any other Appartments over it than those two abovementioned and had us Comunication whatever with any other Tenement or house adjoyning that her sd. late father

after he quitted the said Tenement never Rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 5th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Lane
