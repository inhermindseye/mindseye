Middlesex to wit Elizth. Plastow Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented Ten pounds a year Paid Taxes Says she lived as a Covenanted Servant wth. one Mrs. Bowers a Mantua Maker in Craven Buildings  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex to Learn Trade or Business for Two Years and her Aunt Christiana Lestie paid the sum of 10 pounds for that purpose and further Says that on the 5th. day of February 1765 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Weslim in the Parish of St. Mary Islington in the County of Middlesex which was on the 20th day of Febry. then next following Baptized by the Name of Christiana Pearson and Says that Thos. Tucker Father of the sd. Female Bastard Child died in July 1765 following and that this Examinant being very poor is not able to Maintain the same and that the said Child hath gained no Settlem. whatever otherwise than being Poor in the said Parish of St. Mary Islington

Sworn this 10th. day of March 1772 before us W Kelyngs John Lane

 Eliz Plastow
