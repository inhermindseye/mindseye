Middx to wit Elizabeth Henley Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Rented and lived in a House in Justice Walk in the Parish of Saint Lukes Chelsea in the County of Middlesex in which Parish this Examinant was born And further says that She was Passed from Saint Andrew Holbourn upon the said Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea aforesaid where her said Settlement was found to be upon an Appeal at the Quarter Sessions since which time She has never gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 30th. July before R Butler M Addington

Eliz Henley
