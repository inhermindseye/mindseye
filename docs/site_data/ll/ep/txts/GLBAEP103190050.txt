Middlesex ss  Margaret Simpson  widow maketh oath that about Fourteen Months Ago she was hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr John Cleghorne  Victualler in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in such service under such Service under hiring for the Space of one year & upwards & has not Since done any Act to gain any Settlement.

Sworn this. 11. day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Margaret Simpson
