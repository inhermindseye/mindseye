Middx  Sarah Raney aged about 19 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith, she never was married; and that on the 27th. Day of January last she was delivered of a male bastard Child as a house in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath been baptized William and is become Chargeable to the Sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same and that John Cuthbert a Staymaker did beget the Said male bastard Child on her body: the sd. John Cuthbert having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinant in the Month of April last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the sd. John Cuthbert and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn the 23th. Febry 1753 Before} Tho Lane  Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Raney
