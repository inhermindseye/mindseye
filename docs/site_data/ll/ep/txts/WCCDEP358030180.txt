Middx  Ann Cheney aged about 61 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she was bound and Apprentice to and Served almost one Year of her Apprentiship with Mrs. Powell a Mantua maker in Bedford Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. and then her said Mistress run away from her Says she never was assigned over to any other Person lived with any Person during that  of her Apprentiship 40 Days with her Mistress'es knowledge or Consent rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 9th. 1746 before}
