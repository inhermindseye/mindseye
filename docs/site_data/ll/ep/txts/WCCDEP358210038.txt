Westmr.  Ann Taylor aged about 55 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Paylor on the Terrass in St. James's Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds, quitted said Service about Twelve Years and hath not since Rented the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 18th. March 1766 , Before} Paul Vaillant  Frans Cary

Ann Taylor
