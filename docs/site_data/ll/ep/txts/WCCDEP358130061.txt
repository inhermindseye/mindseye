Middx  Martha Noah aged about 39 Years Says She Intermarryed with William Noah her late Husband deced in the Year 174 That he lived a hired Yearly Servant before Marriage with one Mr. Heron in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the East London near three Years as She hath been informed by him & believes Says She hath two Children by her said late Husband Vizt. Susannah aged about Eight Years & William aged about Six Years Says that her said husband dyed in the Year 1745 and that he never in his lifetime nor She this Examinant since his Death Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 2d. Day of Octor. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

Martha Noah
