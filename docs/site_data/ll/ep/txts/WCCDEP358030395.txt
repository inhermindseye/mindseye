Westmr. 

Mary Cook aged about 63 Years upon Oath saith she never was married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. John Torbuck Bookseller in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £5 for two Years and upwards & left her sd Service about 13 Years since Says she has not since she quitted the sd. Mr. Torbuck Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 19th. 1747 before  Wm. Jones

Mary Cook
