(Indexed G) (After Birth) Elizabeth Cocker & Rachael her Child

St Clement Danes September 24 1792

Middx and Westminster }  Woman's settlement in St James Clerkenwell }  Child's birth  Reputed father (to wit) Elizabeth Cocker  singlewoman aged 21 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly with Mr. Bevan No. 6 James's Buildings in Rosemary Row  in the parish of St James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex  for one year (wanting one day) at the Annual Wages of £5:0:0 and Received the whole years Wages for such her Service and hath never obtained any Settlement in her own right otherwise than by such Service with Mr Bevan in James's Buildings as aforesaid and this Deponent further saith that on the 5th day of December 1791 at the House of Mrs. Knight No. 5 Houghton Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the said Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Named Rachael aged 9 and that the said Child is become chargeable to the to harsh that one John Croft who now Works at Kentish town in the said Cov as a Labourer in the Highways is the only real and truefather of such Child of which She was so delivered as aforesaid he and no one else having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent Body about four years ago and at several time since at some or one which times he did beget the said Child thereon and that he & no one else the real true father thereof

Sworn the 24th day of September 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizth

Elizth Cocker Mark
