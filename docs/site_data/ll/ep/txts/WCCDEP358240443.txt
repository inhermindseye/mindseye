Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. in St. C. D  to wit Elizabeth Biggs aged about 55 years upon her Oath saith That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Mills a Surgeon and Apothecary in Clare Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and an half at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about Fifteen years at the yearly Wages since which time she hath not lived as an hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act matter or Thing whereby She might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th- day of Septr. 1794 before N Bond E Read

her Elizth.Biggs mark

Witness Eliz Biggs
