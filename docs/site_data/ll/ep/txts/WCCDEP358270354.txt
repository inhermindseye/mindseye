Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Pamplin aged about Twenty years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Dr. Brockles by in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Eighteen Months since which time she hath Not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she Might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Hickman a Journeyman Bricklayer but with whom he works or ever did work or where he now lives ever did live she this Deponent doth not know is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Hickman is the only Real & true father thereof

Sworn this 2d. day of March 1798 before }

her Sarah Pamplin Mark

Man never Apprehended

Child born in the House the 20th. day of March 1798 named Joseph

Child sent to Enfield the 9th. day of May 1798
