Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Higgins aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with George Higgins her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields on or about the fourth day of November last That on or about the Ninth day of May 1792 she Intermarried with one Henry Redwood at the Parish Church of Saint Botolph Bishopgate That the said Henry Redwood died on or about the Fourteenth day of April 1795. That she hath Two Children living by her said late Husband Vizt. Mary aged about Three years and Three weeks and Henry aged about Two years and Three Months That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Chancery Lane in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for about Nine years at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and that after he quitted said house he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have Gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of Febry. 1796 J Hind Rt Thompson

her Hannah Higgins Mark

Witness B Newman
