Middlesex to wit Jane Johnson Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Poole Attorney in Palsgrave Place in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Nine Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath Says she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Westmr. new lying in Hospital on the 12th. day of August last which has since been baptized by the Name of Johanna Mary and is likely to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same And that one Mr. George Boswell an Attorney at Law at Witton Hall near Birmingham in the County of Warwick is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House some time in the Month of November 1777 but the particular day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said George Boswell & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 5th. Day of Decr. 1778 before M Addington.

Jane Johnson
