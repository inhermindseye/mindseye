Middx  Mary Clarke aged abt. 69 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Clarke who hath been dead abt. Ten Years says her said husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England says she was born in Sheffield Street Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servt. for one Yr. .
