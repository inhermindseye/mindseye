Middlesex to wit Elizth. Henley Aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Say her Father Rented and lived in a House in Justice Walk in the Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea in the County of Middlesex in which Parish this Examinant was born and further says she was passed from St. Andrew Holbourn upon the Parish of St. Luke Chelsea afsd. where her said Settlement was found to be upon an Appeal at the Quarter Sessions since which time she hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of February 1782 before me R Butler M Addington

Eliz Henley
