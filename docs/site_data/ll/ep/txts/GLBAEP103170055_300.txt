Middlesex ss  John Smith maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to one Perrin of Cannons Marsh adjoining to the City of Bristol  Anchor Smith That he this Deponent did not live or lodge with his said Master during his Apprenticeship but served him as his apprentice in the sea service on Board Certain ships which always came home to the port of Bristol & were moored at or near the keys there opposite the Parish of St Nicholas in the Sd. City in which Parish this Deponent in general Lodged on the return of such ships & also on Board such ships & by means there of as he is advised gained a legal settlement in the said parish of St. Nicholas That he this Deponent has not Since his Apprenticeship rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 15 day of February 1777 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of John Smith
