Middx.  James Chamberlin aged about 45 Years Upon Oath says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at the Fleet London about Twenty Years ago by whom he has One Child (Vizt.) Jane aged Ten Years, says that before his Inter- marriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Plummer a Cooper in Wych Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of One pound and six Shillings with Board and Lodging, hath quitted said Service about Twenty three Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 28th. day of March 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of James Chamberlin
