Middx  Mary Gullis  Wido . upon her Oath saith that abt. 2 Months ago since being at that Time a Wido. & not haveing any Child or Childn. she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Jones & Mr. Bamber at the Red Lyon, & Catt in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & further upon her Voluntarily Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one Geo: Minitharp  Chairman did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body he the Sd. Geo: Minitharp lodging at the sd. Mr. Bambers there became acquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body in the Month of Decr. last & at sevll. other Times since at one of which Times he Gott her

with Child of the Sd. Female Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now Pregnant & that he the sd. Geo: Minitharp & no other Person is the true & Reall Father thereof

Sworne the 10th. Augt. 1742 before}
