Westmr. to wit Mary Row Aged about Thirty Years on behalf of Mathew Row her Husband who now lies ill of a Fever on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her said Husband at the Parish Church of Cripplegate London about Three Years & an half ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Vizt. Francis Aged about Two Years and Charles Aged about One Month Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Fletcher at the Devil Tavern  Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Dunstans in the West London for several Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true hath quitted his said Service about five Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 26th. day of February 1771 before Us} Tho. Kynaston  Petr Planck

her Mary Row mark

Witness Peter Sidebotham
