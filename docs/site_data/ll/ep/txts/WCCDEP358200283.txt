Middx.  Mary Anderson aged abt. 39 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Anderson who has been dead about Sixteen Years with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Ware in the County of Hertford about Twenty two Years ago says that being a Widow she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Kennett McKenzie Esqr. for Ten Years and upwards says she lived the last two Years of her said Service with the said Kennett Mc.Kenzie Esqr. in Holles Street Cavendish Square in the Parish of St. Mary le Bone in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds, says at the time she lived with her said Master in Holles Street she had a Son named William aged about seventeen Years and who was then an Apprentice to one Mr. John Child a Carpenter at Buntingford in Hertfordshire says she has not since she quitted her said Service (which is upwards of three Years since) Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 12th. day of July 1765 . Before} Paul Vaillant  Thos: Balack
