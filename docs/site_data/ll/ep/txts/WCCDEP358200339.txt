Westmr.  Grace Westley aged about 50 Years upon Oath says she intermarried with William Westley (gone from her) at the Fleet London upwards of Twenty Years ago; says her said Husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that his Father William Westley Rented a house the corner of Fetter Lane in Holborn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the City of London of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and Upwards and lived in the same several Years in which he carried on the Business of an Oilman says her said husband William Westley never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 30th. of Decr. 1765 . before} Paul Vaillant  Frans Cary

The Mark of Grace Westley
