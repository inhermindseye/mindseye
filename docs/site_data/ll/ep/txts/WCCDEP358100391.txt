Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Bennetts to wit Rosamin Coar aged about 22 years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever served any Apprenticeship Says he was born in upper Thames Street in the parish of Saint Bennetts were his Father and Mother kept House for Eighteen years and paid all Taxes for the same and that his Father died in the said House since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 22d. day of April 1785  Sampn Wright

Rosamin Coar
