Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Catherine Johnson aged about 39 years upon her Oath Saith that She Intermarried with James Johnson her present Husband at the Old Church Manchester in October 1787 that She hath One Child living by her said Husband vizt. John aged about One year and that her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Clements Lanes in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Called or known by the name or Sign of the Taylors Arms for about Eight years-that he hath quitted said house about Two years since which time he hath not done any Act to the best of this Examinants Knowledge to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that her said husband has deserted her and refuses to Allow her and her Child any Support

Sworn this 23d. day of May 1789 R Butler

Catharin Jonsen
