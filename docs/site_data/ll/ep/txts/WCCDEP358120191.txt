Middx  John Moncock upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Saml. Cox a Carpenter in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes that he Served his sd. Master as an Apprentice for Eighteen Months or thereabouts and lodged all that time at his house in Witch Street of sd. that at the Expiration of the sd. 18 Months his said Master absconded & this Examinant never was assigned over nor ever Rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Septr. 10th. 1754 before}
