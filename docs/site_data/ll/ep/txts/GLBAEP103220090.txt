Middlesex ss}  Jonathan Wagg maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to Mr Jason a Pawn broker in Bird Street in the Parish of St Mary Le Bone in the Said County & Served the Whole of his Apprentice there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year in any one Place or done any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Said Apprenticeship

Sworn this. 22. day of Novemr 1786 before R Davies Robt: Smith

Jonathan Wagg
