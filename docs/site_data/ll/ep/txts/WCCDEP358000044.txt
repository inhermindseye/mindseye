Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  passd. to St Dunstan at Stepney (to wit) William Bayly aged twenty six Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That that he intermarried with Mary Bayly his new Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about Six Years and an half ago, that he has only one Child now living by his Said Wife Vizt. William aged four Years or thereabouts, And this Examinant further Saith That he was bound an Apprentice to one William Mifflin of Shoe Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the City of London  Sawyer , by Indentures duly executed that he served about two Years and three Quarters of the said Apprenticeship and then the said Indentures were given up that for some time Previous to his Quitting his said Master he was allowd Board Wages and the he worked with his said Master in the Day time Lodgd elsewhere and that the last Place in which he lodgd for the space of forty Days together before he quitted his said Masters Service upon the Indentures being given up as afore- said was in Church Street Coverleads Fields in the Hamlet of Mile End New Town in the Parish of Saint Dunstan at Stepul otherwise Stebbun Heath , And this Exami- nant further also saith that since her with

his said Master Service he has not done Any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 12th. May 1785 before James Croft M Barnfather}

Wm Bailey
