Middx  Ann Boyden upon her Oaith Says that she intermarried with Nathaniel Boyden her present Husband at the Fleet London on the 2d. Day of Septr. 1736 Says her sd. Husband was born in the Parish of St. Catherine by the Tower in the sd. County of Middlesex Says he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says about four Years ago she was removed by an Order under the hands & Seales of two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the County of Surry from the Parish of St. Saviour in Southwark to the sd. Parish of St. Catherine by the Tower against which Order there was no Appeale

Sworn Febry 11th. 1745 before  Wm Jones

The Mark of Ann Boyden
