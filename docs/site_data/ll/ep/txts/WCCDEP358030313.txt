Westmr. 

Ann Mead aged abt. 43 yrs. upon Oath saith she was never married Says that about ten Yrs. ago she lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. James Sipsey on Breadstreet hill in the Parish of St. Nicolas Olave London at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of Seven Years & upwards Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Sipseys Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 8th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Ann mead
