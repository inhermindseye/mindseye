Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Joseph Cockroft aged about 37 years upon his Oath saith That in the Month of Augt. 1776 he Intermarried with Jane his present wife at the Parish Church of Halifax in the County of York and by whom he hath 5 Children living vizt. Sarah aged about 11 years Elizth. aged about 10 years Richard aged about 7 years George aged about 5 years and Adam aged about 12 Months That this Examinant before his Intermarriage with his said wife and whilst he was a singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Messrs. Slaughters Cheesemongers in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 14 Months at the yearly Wages of Nine Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about 12 years ago since which time he s has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Decr. 1787 R Butler

Joseph Cockroft
