Middx  Sarah Hemming aged abt. 50 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido . of Jas. Hemmings who hath been dead upwds. of Seven Years & that before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Serjt. Bridges in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of Six Yrs. & upwds. at the Yearly  wages of Ten Pounds that her Sd. late Husband never after nor this Examint. since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne the 18th. May 1742 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Sarah Hemming
