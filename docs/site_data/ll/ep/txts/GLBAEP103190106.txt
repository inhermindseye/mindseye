Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Major the wife of John Major maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majesties Ship Conquestadore in his Majesties Service That he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly Served his apprenticesp with one John Brown a Taylor in Portsmouth in the County of Southampton & has not Since to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain anothr Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband by whom She hath a Child & lawful Issue now living named Elizabeth Aged eighteen Months.

Sworn this. 20. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Major
