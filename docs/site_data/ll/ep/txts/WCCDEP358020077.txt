Middlesex to wit Nathaniel Whitcombe aged about 37 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Bray in Mortimer Street Cavendish Square in the Parish of Saint Mary le born in the County of Middx for about three years at the yearly Wages of any turn Sixteen Guineas that he Quitted such Service about five years ago and since that time has not done any Act whereby to the best of his knowledge he may have acquired any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. May 1788 before us D Walker Philip Dyot

Nathaniel Witcomb
