Middx  The Exaicon of Richd. Davie taken the 12th- Day of January 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt- he served 3 Yrs. the first Part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. King, in Portugal Street in the Parish of St- Clemt Danes in the County of Middx  Peruke Maker & further upon his Oath saith yt- he hath not since he left his sd- Master rented £10 a Yr- pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt- for the Space of one Yr- or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt- in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Richard Davie
