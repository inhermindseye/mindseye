Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (ss)  Hester Talboys  singlewoman aged twenty Six Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married and further this Examinant on her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and further that Robert Macadam who is a Gentleman Coachman and lives with Mr. Batty of Hensington is the Father of such Child or Children he the said Robert Macadam having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body in or about the Month of May last and at several other times at some or one of which times he did beget on this Examinants Body the Child or Children where with she is so pregnant and that he and no one else is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 10th. Janry 1790 before me R Butler

Hester Talboys
