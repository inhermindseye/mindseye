St Clement Danes May 12th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Psh of Holliwell in the City of Oxford  (to wit) Rebecca Aulkin Wife of Richard Aulkin (now his Majesty's service abroad) Upon her Oath saith that she was Married to the said Richard Aulkin at the Parish Church of Newington in the Country of Surrey on or about the 22d day of August last (by Banns) that she hath been informed and verily believes her said Husband served two years and an half of his Apprenticeship under Indenture with Mr. Thomas Reynolds of the Parish of Holywell Holliwell in the City of Oxford Clock & Watch Maker and lodged with his said Master during such service that his said Master there failing he left his service and was not assigned over or ever served as an Apprentice after  or did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief, and this Examinant saith that she hath one Child by her said Husband now living with her namely Richard born on or about the 30th day of December last and hath also now living with her a Child of her said Husband's Named Lucy aged ten years & upwards which he had by a former Wife named Margaret Aulkin to whom he was Married at the Parish Church of St. George Hanover Square a long time previous to the Birth of the his said Child Lucy as this Deponent hath been informed and believes And this  Examinant further

saith that her said Husband left her upwards of 3 Weeks ago and She is become distressed and unable to support herself and his said Family without assistance

Sworn the 12th day of May 1794 before us } M Addington Edw Read

Rebecca Aulkin 's Mark

Witness. Richd Dobbins

Removed by Dobbins the Beadle
