Middlesex ss}  Ciscaley Goldsmith the widow of Willm Goldsmith deced maketh oath that since the death of her said late husband She rented a house of Five Pounds a year in King Henry Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That this Depont has not since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain another Settlement

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Ciscaley Goldsmith
