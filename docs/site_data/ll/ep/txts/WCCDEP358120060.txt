Middx  Benj. Sabire upon Oath saith he has a wife named Mary and by her a Child Benjn. Peter aged 18 months that he never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. for one Year nor did he ever rent ten pounds a year or pay parish taxes that his father was a Norman & his mother a Native of Britany in France and that he this Examinant was born in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr.

Sworn Febry. 23d. 1754 -before} Barth: Hammond

Benjamin Sebire
