Middlesex ss}  Isabella Finch the Widow of John Finch mariner deced maketh oath That her said late husband has informed her & which information she believes to be true that he was born in the Parish of Woodbridge in the County of Suffolk That he Served his Apprenticeship there with his Fath & afterwards rented a house there & was Charged to & paid Parish Taxes for the Same & by means aforesaid gained a legal Settlement there That the did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 6th day of February 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of Isabella Finch
