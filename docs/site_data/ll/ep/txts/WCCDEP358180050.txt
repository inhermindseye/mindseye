Eliza. Appleyard  Wido . of Thomas Appleyard upon her Oath saith that on or about the 23d Day of July 1728 she intermarried with her said late Husband Thomas Appleyard at the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holbourn who hath been dead near Two Years that about 12 Yrs- since her said late Husband lived in & rented a House over against Surry Street in the Strand of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Six Pounds for the Space of one Year or thereabouts that since he loss the said House her said late Husband in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath Two Children  Thomas aged 12 Yrs- & mary aged 8 Yrs- & being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworne the 20th. Day of Decr. 1742 before} Wm. Jones

Elizth Appelyard
