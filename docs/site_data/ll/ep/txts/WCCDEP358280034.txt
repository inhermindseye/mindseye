Saint Clement Danes Augt. 29th. 1786.

Middx (to wit)  Ann Clarke  Singlewoman Aged 28 or thereabouts upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that she never was married, That the latter End of March last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the Ho of Mr. Woodhouse the Temple Coffee House in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which has been since baptized by the Name of Sarah And this deponent saith That Henry Austin  Esquire late a Lodger at the said Coffee House is the True and Real Father of the said Child he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent Body.

Sworn before me this 29. Augt. 1786 } R Butler

her Sarah Clarke Mark

a Bond filled up from Mr Austin & Mr. Woodhouse to inden they look the Child-it died.

St. Clement Danes  Augt. 31st 1786

Amos Suckmore  Singlewoman
