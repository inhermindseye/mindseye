Middx ss  John Hood Maketh oath that about forty three Years ago he was bound apprentice to his Father John Hood a Bricklayer in Ratcliffe Highway then in the parish of St. Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middx but now in the parish of St George in the County of Middx with Whom he duly served his said apprenticeship & by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt there And this Deponent saith that he has not since gained any subsequent settlement having never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor been a hired servt by the Year nor served any Parish offices nor done any Act to Acquire a settlement since his sd apprenticeship And that he hath a wife named Sarah

Sworn this 30 day of January 1756 before  Richd: Ricards

John Hood
