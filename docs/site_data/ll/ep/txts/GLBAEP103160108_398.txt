Middlesex ss  Sarah Golding  singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago her late Father rented a house in the Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement to her knowledge or belief That she this Depont was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 19. day of July 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Sarah Golding
