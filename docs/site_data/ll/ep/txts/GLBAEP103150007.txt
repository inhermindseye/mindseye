Middlesex ss  Margaret Westcomb the Widow of William Westcomb deced maketh oath that divers Years ago her Said late husband Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Seventy three pounds ten Shillings with Ground thereto belonging in the parish of Bishops Waltham in the County of Southampton & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She this Deponent Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 31st day of January 1775 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Margaret Westcomb
