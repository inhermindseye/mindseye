Westmr. 

Samuel Welbeck upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds in St Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of one Year & upwards and hath quitted the same about Six weeks and hath not since rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or other wise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 18th. 1750 before
