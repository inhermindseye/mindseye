Middx & Westmr. ss  Ann Price Aged about 23 years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever rented House or Paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mary Turton who keeps the Red Lyon and French Horn in Red Lyon Street in the Par of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex upwards of one year Etc half at the yearly Wages of £4. hath Quitted her sd. Service about 4 years and hath not since she quitted Same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired year for one year

Sworn this 16th Day of November 1769 before us} Tho. Fellows R. Butler

her Ann Price mark

Witness Thos. Jones
