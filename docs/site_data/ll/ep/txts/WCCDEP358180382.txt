Middx  Sarah Wynn aged about 57 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Biggins in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London at the Yearly Wages of Two pounds for the Space of two Years & upwards and hath left her sd. Service upwds. of 20 Years Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mrs. Biggins Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt . for one Year or other wise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 20th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sarah Wynn
