Westmr. 

Bridget Mc. Kertny on Behalf of Eliz. Mc. Kertny an Infant Child aged about 7 Years born of the body of Margt Haley deced upon Oath saith she has been Informed by John Mc. Kartny the reputed father of the said Child (since also deceased) that the said Child was a bastard and that she has been further Informed and believes the said Eliz. Mc. Kertny was born in Eagle Court in the Parish of St. Maryle Strand in the Liberty of Westmr.

Sworn Janry 17th 1749 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Bridgt. Mc. Kertny
