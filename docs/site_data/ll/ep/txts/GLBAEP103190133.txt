Middlesex ss}  Mary Stevens maketh oath that about two Years ago She was married to one Arthur Ealey (Since deced) by Banns in the Parish Church belonging to the Parish Church of St Mary Whitechapel in the Said County by Banns But this Depont. has been Credibly informed and is also Advised that Such Marriage So as aforesd celebrated between this Deponent & the sd Arthur Ealey was & is a mere Nullity & Void in Law by reason (as this Deponent has been Credibly informed & believes) that he the Sd Arthur Ealey about Thirty Years ago was Married to one Elizabeth Hill at the Fleet who is yet living And this Depont farther Saith that previous to her Marriage as aforesd. She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with one Mr Bolland a Butcher in Plough Street in the sd Parish of St Mary Whitechapel & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for two years & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain Anothr Settlemt Save what She has Acquired by her Marriage as aforsd

Sworn this. 2. day of August 1780 before  Jno Sherwood W Blackmore

The Mark of Mary Stevens
