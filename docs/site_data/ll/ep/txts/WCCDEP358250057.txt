St Clement Danes July 16th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D (to wit) Dorothy Baldwin  Widow aged 70 years and upwards Upon Oath saith That she was Married to her late Husband John  Baldwin at the Fleet London on or about the 24th day of November 1751 according to the rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England That her said Husband hath informed her this Deponent which she verily believes to be true That previous to their Marriage, her said Husband served an Apprenticeship of Seven years under Indenture with Mr Bacon a Carpenter in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said Liberty of Westminster , and lodged in his said Master's House during such Apprenticeship And that since the Expiration thereof he did not in his Life time obtain any subsequent legal settlement to her knowledge information or belief and this Deponent farther saith that her said Husband Died about 33 years ago and that she this Deponent hath not since been Married, Rented ten pounds a year, in value, paid any parochial Rates or lived as an hired yearly Servant or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 16th day of July 1792 . before me} D Walker

Dorothy Baldwin Mark

Witness Edwd Burbidge
