Westmr  Susanna Phipps aged about 54 Years upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wilson a Butcher in Hollis Street Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds & hath quitted said Service about Eleven Years during which time She hath not rented Ten pounds a year nor paid Taxes-or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the day of October 1767 before}

Susanna Phipps
