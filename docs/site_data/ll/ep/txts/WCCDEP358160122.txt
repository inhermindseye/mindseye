Middx  The Examination of Mary Hendley on the behalfe of Sarah Hendley aged about 13 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 18th. Day of March 1739 Who upon her Oath saith yt. Jno. Hendley Father of the above sd. Sarah lived in & Rented a House £13 a Year in Church row in the Parish of St. Botolph by Aldgate  Londn . for the space of one Year or thereabouts And this Examint. further saith that the sd. Jno. Henley never after he left the sd. House rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife And that the sd. Jno. Hendley & Mary his Wife (Mother of the sd. Intant) are both Dead and Dying Poor have left the sd. Sarah their Daughter unprovided for

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas Gronous

Mary Handley
