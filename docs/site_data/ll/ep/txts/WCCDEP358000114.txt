St. Clement Danes Septr. 23- 1785

Middlesex and Westminster to Wit} 

Qy of taken Etc Married the Woman  Eliza Avon Aged twenty two Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married Rented a House or paid Taxes or ever lived as a Hired Yearly Servant Says that her Father Rented and lived a House near Saint Clements Church Yard in the Strand about three Years and paid Taxes for the same and that he quitted the said House about Four Years Ago and that since here Father quitted the said House she has done no Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement in her own right And Further on her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is Pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same when Born And further Saith that one William Sutcliffe a Taylor who lately lived in Bear Alley Fleet Market is the Father of the said Child or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body several different times at one of which he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said William Sutcliffe and no other Person did legal the same and that he is the Real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 23rd Sept. 1785 before Mr Barnfather

her Eliza Avon Mark
