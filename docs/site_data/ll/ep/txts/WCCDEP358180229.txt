Middx  Wm: Bass aged 71 Yrs. or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with his present Wife Jane (aged 60 Yrs: or thereabouts) at the Parish Church of Dunnegar in the County of Antram in the Kingdom of Ireland upwards of 44 Yrs. since That about Ten Yrs. since he lived in & Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £10. in Lower Shadwell in the parish of St. Paul at Shadwell in the County of Middx & lived in the same 4 Yrs. & upwds. & hath left the that since he left the sd. house he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 8th. Septr. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Wm. Bass
