Middx  Edwd. Patey upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Mary at Bishopgate Church about 20 Years ago and that he Served Seven Years Apprentiship to John Dry a Sawyer in Houghton Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlx and that about three Years ago he was removed by Order of two Justices from the parish of St. Michl. at Queenhith and has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Feb. 6th. 1750 before  Tho Lane .

Edward Paty
