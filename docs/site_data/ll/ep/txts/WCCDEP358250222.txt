Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Burdet aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that about Six years ago she Intermarried with Henry Burdet at the Parish Church of Saint James Westmr . That her said Husband Rented and lived in a Shop in King Street  Seven Dials in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westmr. for about Two years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds That he quitted said House about Six years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of Janry 1796 } Edw: Read J Hind

her Mary Burdet Mark
