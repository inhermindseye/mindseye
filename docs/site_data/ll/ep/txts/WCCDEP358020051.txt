Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Jane Meldrum aged 18 or thereabts. upon her Oath Saith that she was born in Charles Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields that her Father went to Sea & died abroad that she never gained any Settlement to the best of her knowledge that her Mother is now in Saith Martins Workhouse, That she this Examinant is Ill and unable to provide for herself

Sworn before us this 18 Decr. 1787 . Mercer J Barnfather

her Jane Meldrum Mark
