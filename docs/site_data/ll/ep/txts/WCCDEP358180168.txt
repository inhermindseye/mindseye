Middx  Mary Cook upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido . of Edwd. Cook who hath been Dead abt. 30 Years that abt. Sixteen Years since she lived in & Rented a house in Swallow Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £25. & lived in the same for the Space of 3 Years or thereabouts that since she left the sd. House she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt . for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 28th. Febry 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

Mary Cook
