Westmr.  Mary Percifull Aged about Fifty three Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Freer the corner of May Pole Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Nineteen Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds; and after she quitted said Service never Rented House, Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 15th, day of June 1768 , before R Kitston

The Mark of Mary Percifull
