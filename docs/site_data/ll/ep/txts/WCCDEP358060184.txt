Middx  Eleanr. Arnold upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwards of four Years ago She lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Busick a dealer in Worsted in Newgate Street in the Parish of Christ church London at the Yearly Wages of £5 for four Years and upwards and has not since she Quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr 11th 1751 before H Fielding
