Middx & Westmr}  Pasd. to St. Olaves J. to wit, Richard Pyuar aged about fifty four Years upon his Oath saith, That he intermarried with Martha his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Brideair London about Sixteen Years ago, and by whom he hath three Children living one of whom is provided for and two others Vizt. Samuel aged about fourteen Years and Abraham aged about seven or Eight Years who have never gained any Settlement of their or Says that he rented a House in Torbay Street in the Parish of Saint Olave Southwark in the County of Surry and lived in the same upwards of one Year at the Yearly Rent of ten pounds that he hath quitted his said House about Twenty Years ago since which time he has gained us other Settlement either by renting House Tenement or Lodging of ten pounds a Year paying Taxes, or any otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 16th. Novr- 1784 -before  James Croft  Jno: Wright

his Richd. Pyuar Mark
