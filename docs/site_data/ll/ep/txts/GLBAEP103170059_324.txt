Middlesex ss}  Rachael Fisher maketh oath That She well knows a Girl names  Mary Ann Martin Aged about fifteen years who was born in Mandlins Rents in the parish of St Botolph without Alegate in the sd County She this Depont. being her God mother and who hath done no Act to gain a Settlement in her own right to this Deponts knowledge or belief and this Deponent also knew the Mother of the Sd.  Mary Ann Martin whose name was Ann Martin & who has often declared to this Depont. that She the said Ann Martin was never Married & which information She this Deponent also believes to be true.

Sworn this. 25. day of February 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

Rachael Fisher
