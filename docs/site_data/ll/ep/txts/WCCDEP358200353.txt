Westmr.  Thomas Percy aged abt. 72 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in little St. Martin's Lane in the Parish of St. Anne in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds, lived therein several Years and hath not since he quitted said House Rented the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 14th. day of Feby. 1766 , before}
