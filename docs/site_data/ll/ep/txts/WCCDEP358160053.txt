Middx  The Examination of Amey Taylor  Widw . Aged about 50 Years Taken the 18th. of May 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that about Twenty Years ago her late Husband Charles Taylor lived in & Rented a House in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of £35 & lived in ye. same for the space of 12 Years or thereabouts And this Examint. further saith that her sd. Husband in his Life time nor she since his Death never Rented £10 a Year Said Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or Otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & that she is now Poor & Lame & not able to maintaine herselfe.

Sworne the Day & Year above Mentioned before} Tho Lane  Samuel Clarke
