Middlesex ss}  Hannah McDaniel maketh oath that divers years Ago She was duly bound Apprentice to Joseph Price of Hart Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the Sd County Victualler & Served the Whole thereof there That she has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year is anyone Place nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since Such her Apprenticeship as aforesaid

Sworn this. 13. day of July 1780 before  Jasper Clarke W Blackmore

The Mark of Hannah McDaniel
