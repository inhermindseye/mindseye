Westmr  Sarah Seddam Aged about Nineteen Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father Rented a House in Gardeners Lane Petty France in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr . two Years at the Yearly Rent of twelve Pounds & never after Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 4th. of January 1765 Before} Thos: Balack  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Sarah Seddam
