Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Hill aged about 18 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Desmond in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said service about Fifteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Thomas Hayes who as this Deponent hath been informed lives in Westminster but what particular part she doth not know is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is so Pregnant and that the said Thomas Hayes is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Octr. 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Mary Hill Mark

Witness AW Burbidge Beadle.

Man never Apprehended

Child born in the house the 6th. day of Janry. 1799 named George Hill Sepr. 1799.
