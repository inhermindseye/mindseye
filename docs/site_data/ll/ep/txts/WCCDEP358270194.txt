Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Emmerson aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wilson in Great Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls for One year & 11 Months at the yearly wages of £6. That she hath Quitted said Service about four years since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement either by living as a Servant for Twelve Months Renting House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith That on the First day of March last past she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Akerman in Butcher Row which hath since been half baptized by the Name of William That one William Baldrey a Journeyman Taylor who lately worked with Mr Powell in Shoemaker Row in the City of London and lived with this Deponent in Butcher Row and is now at Colchester as this Deponent is informed and believes is the Real and true father of the said Child he the said William Baldrey having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in or about the beginning of the year 1790 and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that he is the only Real and true father thereof And further this Examinant saith that she is poor and unable to maintain such Child without Assistance

Sworn this 27th. day of Augt. 1794 before me } J Hind

Margt Emmerson

Margaret Emmerson lodges at Mr. Bradys the corner of Stanhope Street

William Baldrey worked with Mr. Powell a Taylor in Shoemaker Row on Ludgate Hill now lives at Colchester is a Tall thin Man about 5 foot 6 Inches high rather Pock marked about 26 years of Age-his Hair Cared behind

Wood Overseer

Newman Beadle
