Middlesex ss}  Ann Churchman  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago her late Father John Churchman deced Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in Baileys Place in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & resided thereon for about the Space of Six Months & this Deponent lived with him there as Part of his Family That her sd Fathr never afterwards rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor Served a Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That She this Depont never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 16th day of Febry 1784 before R Davies

Ann Churchman
