Middx  Rebecca Dighton aged 28 Years & upwards upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her present Husband  Richd. Dighton aged 32 Years & upwards on the 18th. Day of December 1740 at the Fleet London that the sd. Richd. Dighton was bound an Aprentice to & Served 15 Months the first Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Thos. Keene in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & at the End of the sd. 15 Months went away from his sd. Master & was never Assigned over to any other Person whatever that he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath two Children by the sd. Richd. Dighton (to wit) Richd.  Richd. David Keene aged 2 Years & upwards & a Male Child aged 5 Weeks yett unbaptized that her sd. Husband absconds from her & she being Poor is not able to maintain herself & Children

Sworne 28th. May 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Rebecca Dighton
