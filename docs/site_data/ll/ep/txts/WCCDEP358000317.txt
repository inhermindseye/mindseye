St. Clement Danes.

Mary Howlett aged 26 upon her Oath Saith That she lived as a Hired Yearly servt . with Mr Watson of Upper Seymour Street for 13 Months at £7: a Year.-That it is in the Parish of St. Mary lebone That she quitted such Service about 14 Months ago. That since she quitted same she has done as Act Matter or Thing Whereby she may have gained a Settlement

Sworn July 12. 1786. before us  Rt. Taylor  Jno Barnfather

Mary Howlett
