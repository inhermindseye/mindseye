Middx  Margaret Hampshire Aged about Fifty Seven Years her Oath says She is the Widow of Stephen Hampshire with whom She Intermarried at the Fleet London Upwards of Twenty Eight Years ago And who died about Eighteen Years ago in Germany as She has been Inform and Believes Says her said Husband was Bound Apprentice to One Mr. Parnell a Shoemaker into Cheshunt in the County of Hertford and served the said Mr. Parnell five Years Under such Indenture And after quitting his said Masters Service he never Gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year before Marriage or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinants since her said Husbands Death And further say that her said late Husbands Father Rented and lived in a house at Cheshunt aforesaid of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or thereabouts for many Years as she has been Informed and Verily believes

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry 1780 before  Abrm Baley  Wm. Kitchiner

Margaret Hampshire
