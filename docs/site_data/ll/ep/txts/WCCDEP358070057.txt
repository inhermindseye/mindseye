Middx & Westmr}  Elizth. Sangster Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House was on Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Rented and lived in a House in Butcher Row Temple Bar upwards of Twenty Years before his Death and which her Mother now keeps at the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born as Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one John Rains a Distiller in Oxford Street in Parish of Saint Anns in the Liberty of Westminster had Carnal Knowledge of her Body on the Twenty fourth day April last past to the best of her Knowledge at his the sd. Mr Rains House and at the five Bells Tavern near the New Church in the Strand a few Nights afterwards & at no other Place or times one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as aforesaid and that the said John Rains and no other Person did beg the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 7th. day of January 1773 before W Kelyngs

Elizth Sangster
