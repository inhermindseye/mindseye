Middlesex to wit Hannah Fletcher Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Proctor a Perukemaker in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in County of Middlesex for five Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And further says that one Michael Ranter a Servant to Chas. Rodd  Esqr . at East Clay Bermondsey Surry is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her Masters House sometime the latter end of December or the begining of January last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And the said Michael Ranter and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 13th. day of June 1780 before  Abrm. Baley

Her Hannah Fletcher Mark
