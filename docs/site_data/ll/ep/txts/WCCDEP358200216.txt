Middx  Susannah Bristow Aged about Twenty two Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Thomas Lane Esqr. Junier in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to Be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Thomas Scriven a Sert to the said Thos. Lane Esqr. had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the House of the said

Thos. Lane Esqr. sometime in the Month of June last and at severall other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 12th of Jany. 1765 Before} Tho Lane

Susanah Bristow
