Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Jeakwell the Widow of Robert Jeakwell deced maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her said late husband That this Deponents Mother Amy Denmead deced has informed her & which She believes to be true That She this Deponts. Said Mother as well as this Deponents late Father John Denmead deced were both born in Frome in the County of Somerset where this Deponent was also born and this Deponent can give no othr Account respecting her said Parents Settlement That She this Deponent never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year under any one hiring nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself Save What She might have Acquired by Marriage with her said husband whose Settlement is unknown.

Sworn this 23d day of Febry. 1774 before B Camper

The Mark of Eleanor Jeakwell
