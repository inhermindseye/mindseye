Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizth. Powell aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mrs. Varlav in Mount Street Grosvenor Square in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for Twenty Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service about 5 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly servant for one year Rented House paid Taxes one done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of March 1788  John Croft G Mercer

Elizabeth Powell
