Middx  The Examination of Jane Piggott passed from the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. by the Name of Jane Maplesden Singlewoman by Order bearing Date the 28th- Day of January last taken the 8th- Day of April 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. Fourteen Years ago to her best Remembrance She intermarried with one Mr. Geo: Piggott that this Exaiant at the Request of the sd- Mr. Geo Piggott was privately married at a Place called Whitton in the County of Middx That her sd. Husband served one Year & upwards ye. first part of his Apprentishipp with one Mr. Tankard in Russell Street in the sd. Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden Woolen Draper

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Jane Pigott
