Westmr.  Elizabeth Mc. Cann Aged about Sixty Seven Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Chas. Mc. Cann who has been dead about Twenty Years ago Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Mackey a Staymaker in Exeter Court Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Thirteen Years at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of one Year ago & hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. day of September 1772 before} R. Butler

her Elizth. Mc. Cann Mark
