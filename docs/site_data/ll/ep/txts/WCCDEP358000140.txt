Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

Swears to St. Clemts. to wit Maria Lloyd aged about Twenty three years upon her Oath saith that she Intermard. with Thomas Lloyd at the Parish Church of Saint George in the East about 3 years ago that she has one Child living vizt: John aged 2 years that her said Husband before their Intermarriage lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Freke of Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 3 years, hath quitted said Service about 8 years, since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge, Information or belief, he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere, that her said Husband has deserted her and she is Poor and unable to maintain herself and Child.

Sworn this 29th. day of October 1785 J Addington

Maria Lloyd
