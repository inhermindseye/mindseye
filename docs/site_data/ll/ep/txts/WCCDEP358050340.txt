Middx  Mary Rawlins aged upwds. of 60 Yrs. upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Fras. Rawlins deced and that her sd. late husband was a Native of Ireland as is likewise this Examinant and neither her sd. late husband nor herself ever gained a Settlement in England that the said Fras. Rawlins was born in Limmerick in Ireland and was a Servt. for sometime with one Mr. Quin there as she hath been Informed & verily believes

Sworn Octr. 18th 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Rawlins
