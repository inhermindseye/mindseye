Middlesex ss}  Mary Moses the widow of John Moses deced maketh oath that divers years ago as he has informed this Deponent & as She verily believes he was duly bound Apprentice & Served the whole thereof with one Mr Ogden a Shipwright in the Parish of St Nicholas Deptford in the County of Kent & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That he did not afterwards rent a house of Ten pounds a year or Serve any parish office or pay any poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this deponent Since his death been Married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 20th day of November 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Moses
