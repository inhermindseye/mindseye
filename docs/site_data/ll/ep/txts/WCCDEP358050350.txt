Middx  Paul Milkin upon Oath saith he intermarried with his wife Eliz Milkin on the 13th. May 1742 at the Fleet London and that he Served Seven years Apprentiship with Saml. Cloves in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry Lighterman and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 31st. 1750 before H Fielding

Paul Milkin
