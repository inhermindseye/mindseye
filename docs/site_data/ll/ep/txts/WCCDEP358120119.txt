Middx  Edwd. Clark aged abt. 53 Years upon Oath saith he was married to Jane his Wife at the Fleet London about 11 Years ago by and that he Served Seven Years apprentiship with Thos. Smith a Perukemaker in Portugal Street & Carey Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Dane s and after the Expiration of his apprentiship lived a yearly Servant with the sd. Thos. Smith in Carey Strt. afsd. for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Fifteen Pounds Dyet Washing & Lodging & has not since he quitted Mr Smiths Service rented ten Pounds by the year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn June 12th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Edward Clark
