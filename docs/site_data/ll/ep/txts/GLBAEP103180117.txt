Middlesex ss  Ann Marling  Singlewoman maketh oath That Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Thomas Walker in Mandlins Rents in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at Certain Yearly Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of Eighteen Months That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlemt Since Such her Servitude as aforesaid

Sworn this 12th day of Janry 1779 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Ann Marling
