Middlesex ss}  Hannah Adams  Singlewoman maketh oath That She was born in Stone Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County That she was never bound an apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself And this Deponent Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or either of them

Sworn this 1st day of March 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Hannah Adams
