Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Curry  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years ago She bound herself apprentice by a Commence Indenture to Thomas Coe of Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St John Wapping in the County of Middlesex  Victualler for the Term of Seven Years to be instructed in the Business of housewife  That She Serves Six Years & Seven Months of her apprenticeship with the said Thomas Coe in the same Parish and then Some difference Arising between her and her Mistress She this Depont & the Said Thomas Coe her then Master by Mutual Consent Agrees to part And there She this Depont. with the Said Thomas Coe 's Consent & in his Present threw one Part of the Said Indies of apprenticeship into the fire & the Same & the Said Thomas Coe with this Deponts. Consent & in her Presence threw the other Part of them into the Fire and Burnt the Same And thereby (as it was then meant and understood by the Depont. and her Master thesd. Thomas Coe ) intirely Cancelled or Annitulates the Said Indentures and Put an end to this Depont Said apprenticeship (this Depont. being then as She now recollects of the Age of Twenty Years or there abouts) That is Consequence of Such Transaction She this Depont. and also the said Thomas Coe (as She believes) deemed this Depont from thenceforth to be her own Mistress and at Liberty to go where She Pleased and to Provide for herself in Such Manner as She Shod think Prop That in Pursuance thereof She of her own Accord and with the Consent Aid or interposition of the Said Thomas Coe then hired herself to live as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Year Wages with one John Bennett of the Liberty of the Old Artiller Ground in the Said County Victualler in Which Service She Cont for Five Months & no longer That She this Depont. entered into Su Contract & Service for her own Account & to receive Such Wages as had Agreed for to her own use & without any Account to be rendered to the Said Thomas Coe for the Same That although this Depont Since She left the Said Thomas Coe hath been hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year in divers Places Yet She never Continues for a year in any one Service nor was She ever Married nor to her knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since her leaving the Coe

Sworn this 6th day of July 1769 before  Chris: Scott  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Eleanor Curry
