Middlesex ss}  Ann Donne the wife of John Donne maketh oath That her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majesties Ship the Barfleur in his Majesties Service That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with John Newman a Carpenter in the Parish of St George in the City of Coventry & has not Since to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband by Whom She hath a Child & lawful issue by him now living named Mary Aged about thirteen Months

Sworn this. 16. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Anne Donne
