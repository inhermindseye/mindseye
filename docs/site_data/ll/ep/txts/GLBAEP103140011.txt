Middlesex ss}  Agnes Godfrey the Widow of John Godfrey deced maketh oath that by Virtue of a Vagrant order or Warrant under the hand & Seal of John Levy  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the County of Surry bearing date the eighth of December instant She this Deponent with her three Children hereinafter named were removed from the Parish of Saint Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex as the Place of her last legal Settlement although in truth She this Deponent never wandered or begged nor to her knowledge or belief ever Committed any Act of Vagrancy in the Said Parish of Saint Saviour Southwark as a reason or Foundation for granting Such order; That about Eight years ago this Deponents Said late husband took & rented of one Mr Robert Clarke a Dwelling house at the yearly Rent of nine Pounds & Fifteen Shillings Situate in that part of the Parish of Romsey Called the Hundred in the County of Southampton the which place or District So Called the hundred have Overseers of the Poor Annually appointed for the same & make Rates for the maintenance of the Poor resident therein That this Deponents said late husband with this Depont & their Children lived in Such house for the Space of Six months That the Said house Previous to this Deponents said husbands taking the same & during this residence the rein was of the yearly Value of; & worth to be let to a Tenant at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards; & had the said Mr. Clarke required Such yearly Rent or Value for the said house; this Deponts sd husband would freely have given him; the same therefore; And the reason which induced the said Mr Clark to let the said house; at the yearly rent of Nine Pounds & fifteen Shillings only; to this Deponents sd husband & the Tenant who Preceeded him was to prevent his being Called upon to Serve upon Jurys at the Assizes as she this Deponent was then Credibly informed & believes & was so reported & Understood in the Neighbourhood there; That after this Deponts said husband left the said house the same was let for more than the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds as she this Deponent has been Credibly informed and believes Without any Addition or Improvement Made thereto And that She this Deponent hath three Children by her said husband namely John aged Sixteen years William aged nine years & Richard aged three years and that they have not nor hath eithr of them gained any Settlement by any act or acts of his or their own.

Sworn this 15th day of December 1773 before  Cha: Digby

Agnes Godfrey
