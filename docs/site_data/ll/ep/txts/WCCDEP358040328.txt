Middx  Henry Wigg upon Oath saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Daniel Fisher a Taylor at Beckles in the County of Saffolk and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 28th. Febry 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Henry Wigg
