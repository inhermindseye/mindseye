Middx  Thomas Cox aged about 60 Years upon Oath Says that he was Married to Elizabeth his present Wife (who was by an Order dated the 15th. day of April 1761 Removed from the Parish of St. George Southwark to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx) at the Fleet London about Seven Years since Says that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Willm. Miller (Successor to Mr. Gearing) a Shoemaker opposite Arrundel Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid which Apprentice :ship expired about 35 Years since and never after rented any House paid Parish Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the 16th. day of April 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

Thomas Cox
