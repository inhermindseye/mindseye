Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William Blackey aged about 71 years upon his Oath saith that upwards of 40 years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Sunderland in the County of Durham that he this Examinant Rented a First floor of a Mr. Wilson in Castle Street in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for about five years at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds that after he quitted said Apartments he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Decr. 1788 R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

William Blackee
