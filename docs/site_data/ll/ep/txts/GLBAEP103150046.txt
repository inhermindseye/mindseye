Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bayne the Wife of John Bayne maketh oath That divers Years ago her said Husband Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Thirteen pounds in Baileys Place in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx & resided thereon for the Space of one Year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That this Deponents said Husband has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that She hath Four Children Namely John Aged about Eleven Years Elizabeth Aged about nine Years Catherine aged about eight years & percival aged about two Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband who is now retained as a Labourer in his Majesties Dock Yard at Portsmouth in the County of Southampton

Sworn this 28th. day of July 1775 before  Burf Camper

E: Beanye
