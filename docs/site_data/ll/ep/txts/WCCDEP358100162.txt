Middlesex & Westmr.}  Elizth. Naylor Aged about Thirty five years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Thos. Needham  Esqr . in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for two years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six years ago since which time she hath never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one year And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes (she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born) And further says that one Tyne a Cabinet Maker & who also keeps an Earthen Ware Shop facing the New Tavern in Kensington is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the House of Mrs. Haggett her late Mistress in Kensington Gore sometime in the month of April last but the Particular Day she does not remember and at one other time afterwards at one of which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that the said Tyne & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 23d. day of Decr: 1783  Tho Collins

her Elizth. Nailor mark
