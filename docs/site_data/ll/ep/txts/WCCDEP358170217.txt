Middx  The Examination of Martha Flaxman , on the Behalfe of Martha Flaxman an Infant aged abt. Two Years upon her Oath saith that Daniel Flaxman the Father of the said Infant was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentiship With one Mr. Haslop at the Corner of Water Lane  Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Bridgett vulgarly St. Brides  London that since his said Apprentiship he never rented any House of Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife that the Mother of the said Infant is Dead & the said Danl. Flaxman being Gone as a Marine to Giberalter the said Infant is left unprovided for

Sworne the 8th. of April 1742 before} Jas: Gronous  Tho. Lane

Martha Flaxman
