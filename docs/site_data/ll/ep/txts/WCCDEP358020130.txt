Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Richard Gedling aged about 63 years upon his Oath saith that about Fifteen years ago he Intermarried with Elizabeth his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes and by whom he hath No Child living that he Rented a Stall in Clare Market in the said Parish for about Twelve Months at the Weekly Rent of Two Shillings and Eight pence during which time he lodged at the corner of Gilberts Passage in the said Parish for which he paid the Weekly Rent of Two Shillings that he has quitted same about Twenty Seven years since which time he has not Rented any Place of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of March 1789 R Butler

Richd Gedling
