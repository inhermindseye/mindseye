Middx & Westmr.  Alice Malice Aged about Twenty three Years Upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex (where she now lodges) she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one James Wilkinson a Printer had carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the Month of November 1767, and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant, and that the said James Wilkinson and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 18th day of June 1768 , before W Kelynge

The Mark of Alice Malice
