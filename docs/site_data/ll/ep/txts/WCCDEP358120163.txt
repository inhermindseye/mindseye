Middx  Walter Vine aged upwds. of 16 Yrs. upon Oath Saith he is the Son of Walter Vine decd. by Eliz his Wife and that he has been informed and verily believes his sd. late father Served his time wth. Mr Simmons & Watch case Maker in New Street  Shoe Lane in the Parish of St Bridget onlg; Brides London and never rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year and that he this Examinant never was an apprentice or yearly Servant and further that he was passed Sevl. Years ago (with his mother) from Wandsworth to the aforesd. Parish of St Brides

Sworn 25th. July 1754 before

Walter Vine
