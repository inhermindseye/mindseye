Middx.  Jane Jackson aged abt. 34 Years Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married; says on the 2d; day of April 1755 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Hutton by the New Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the County of Middx. which hath since been baptized Ann and that on the 31st. day of March 1754 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the sd. house of the sd. Mr. Hutton which hath since been baptized Jas . which said two Childn. are now both living and are both become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. where she now lodges she being poor and unable to Maintain them; says that Jas. Pritchard who now lives in Angel Court in the Old Bailey did beget the sd. two Childn. on her body of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn this 6th. Feby. 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jane Jackson

Upon Complt. of Mr. Wimberley Ovr . J: P:
