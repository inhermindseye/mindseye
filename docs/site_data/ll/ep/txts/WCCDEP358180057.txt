Middx  Anne Eliz: Abbott aged 20 Yrs- upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married that her late Father  Wm- Abbott  deceased lived in & rented a House in Cursitors Alley , in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holbourn London of the Yearly Rent of £20. for the Space of 3 Years & never after rented £10. a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge & that this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year

Sworne Janry 18th- 1742 before} C. Egerton Wm. Hayton

The Mark of Anne Eliz: Abbot
