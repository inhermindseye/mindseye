Westmr. to wit John Hughes Aged about Sixty Six Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with his late Wife  Sarah at Salisbury about forty Years ago and who has been dead near Eighteen Years Says he rented and lived in a House in Little German Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about fourteen Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 30th. day of Decr. 1771 before} R. Butler 

John Hughes
