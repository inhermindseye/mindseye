Middlesex & Westminster }  St. Martin the Fields  to wit Thomas Broadhead Aged forty three years or thereabouts Upon his Oath saith that about Eighteen years ago he was hired as a yearly Servant by Mr. John Coggan of the Cecil Street  Coffee House where & with whom he lived as such for a year & upwards And that during all the rest of his continuance there he was only as a Waitor and or no Wages or yearly hiring And that since he do left Serving the said Mr. Coggan as an hired yearly Servant abt. Sixteen years & ten Months ago aforesaid he hath not to his knowledge or belief gained any other legal settlement And this Deponent saith that Cecil Street Coffee House where he served as aforesaid is in the parish of St Martin the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County of Middlesex And that he is now Ill Poor and unable to maintain himself

Sworn the 14th. day of December 1791 before us} Sampn. Wright  Thos. Gordon

Thos. Broadhead
