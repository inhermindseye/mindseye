Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Thomas Taylor aged about 58 years upon his Oath saith that about 13 years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields That he this Deponent was Hired by one Joseph Hart of Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster by the year at the yearly Wages of Five pounds and four Shillings but which Wages was to paid at the Rate of Two Shillings per Week That he lived with the said Joseph Hart in the such Capacity of a hired yearly Servant for Two years and upwards during all which time he slept and boarded with in the house of his Master in Shire Lane aforesaid and this Deponent further upon his Oath saith that since he quitted his said Master Service which is about Eighteen years ago he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of May 1797 before me B Goodenough

his Thos. Taylor Mark
