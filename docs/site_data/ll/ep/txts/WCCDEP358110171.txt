Mary Mills aged abt. 60 years upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Richd Mills of whose Settlement she can give no further Account than that he was born at Chipping Norton in the County of Oxford and that she has not since her Husbands Decease rented ten pounds a year or paid parish Taxes

Sworn Febry 10th. 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Mills
