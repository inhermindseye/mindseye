Middx  The Examination of Margt. Deverson  Widdow aged about Seventy Years living in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the thirteenth Day of Octr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. Eleven Years ago she took a House in Starr Court in the Butcher Row of the Yearly Rent of fifteen Pounds & lived in the Same for the Space of three Years or thereabouts & hath left the same about Eight Years & further saith that she hath not since she left the said House rented any House Paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant with any Person for ye Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or Beleife

Sworn the day and Year abovemenconed before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Magt. Deverson
