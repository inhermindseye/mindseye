Middx  Sarah Ross upon Oath saith she was married to Thomas Ross at the Fleet London upwds. of 5 Yrs. ago by whom she has one Child Mary aged upwds. of 3 months that her sd husband is now at Sea in his Majesties Service and that he informed this Examinant he Served Seven years apprentiship wth. David Carter in West Lane in the parish of St. Mary Magdalen  Bermondsey in the County of Surry that his sd. apprentiship Expired upwds. of Six Years ago & he hath never Rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year which Information this Examinant believes to be true

Sworn July 3d. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The mark of Sarah Ross
