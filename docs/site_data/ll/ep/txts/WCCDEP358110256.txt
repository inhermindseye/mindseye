Westmr.  Philip Newman upon Oath Saith he Served Seven years Apprentiship with Mathew Attmore Carpenter opposite the Duke of Devonshires in Pickadilly in the Parish of St Geo. Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn June 8th. 1753 ; before} Tho Lane .

Phill Newman
