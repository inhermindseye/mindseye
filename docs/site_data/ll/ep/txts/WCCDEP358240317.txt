St Clement Danes  September 14th 1793

Middx and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Alice Blake  singlewoman aged seventeen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Marshall at the Alphabet in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for two years or thereabouts at the Annual Wages of four pounds that she quitted that Service about about two years ago ago and hath not Since lived done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement otherwise than in the said parish of St. Clement Clement Danes that She is ill, distressed, and unable to provide for her herself without assistance

Sworn the 14th day of September 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Alice Blake 's Mark

Witness Garrett
