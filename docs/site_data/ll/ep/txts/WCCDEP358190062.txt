Middx.  Phebe Lloyd aged abt. 44 Yrs. ( Removed from the Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middx to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the same County) Upon Oath says she is the wife of John Lloyd  gone from her by whom she has two Children  Elizabeth aged upwards of 7 Years & Mary aged abt. 2 Years, Says her said husband Rented a house in Hollywell Street in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £16 and lived therein abt. 3 Years and has not since he Quitted said House (which is abt. 5 Yrs. ago) Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn Octr. 25th. 1760 before me} Barth: Hammond

Phebe Lloyd
