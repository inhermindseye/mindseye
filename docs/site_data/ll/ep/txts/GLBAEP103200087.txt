Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Brown  widow of Martin Brown deced maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband Save that he has informed her that he Came from Lincolnshire but the Place where he never told her nor Can She give any Account respecting the Settlemt of his Parents or eithr of them That She was born in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County where her late Father & Mother died legally Settled That She this Depont. never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She has Acquired by her Marriage with her Said husband

Sworn this 31st- day of December 1783 before}

The Mark of Elizabeth Brown
