Middx  Geo: Wilson upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Margt . upwds. 21 Years ago at the Fleet London and that by Indenture dated the 9th. May 1733 he bound himself apprentice for four Years and five months to Gilbert Hunt a Feltmaker and lived with the sd. Hunt upwards of three years as his apprentice and then they parted by Consent and that he lodged the last two months of the sd. three Years wth. at his brother in Law in little Peter Street in the Parish of St. John the Evangelist in Westmr and that he never rented ten pounds by the year Paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn June 19th 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Georg Willson
