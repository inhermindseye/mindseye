Middx & Westmr-}  Margt- Allen aged about 75 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Frame a Master Taylor in Portugal Street in the Parish of St- Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - for one Year and nine Months at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds ten shillings hath quitted her said Service ever since the Year 1739 since which time she hath gained no or- subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th- Day of Octr- 1777 before R. Butler

her Margt- Allen Mark
