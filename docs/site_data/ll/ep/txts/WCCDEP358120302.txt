Middx  James Dowell upon Oath Saith he is the Son of John Dowel by Ann his Wife (both deceased ) and that he was born in the parish of Goldington in the County of Bedford where his said late father lived in and rented a house of £3 by the Year, that he can give as Account of the Settlement of his late father or mother and that he (this Examint.) never was an apprentice or yearly Servant for one Year nor did he ever Rent ten Pounds by the year or pay Parish Taxes

Sworn March 8th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Jas. Dowell
