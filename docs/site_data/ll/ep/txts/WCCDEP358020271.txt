St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Ann Court  Singlewoman Aged 38 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs.. Shephard in Dirty Lane Long Acre in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the said County and Liberty about two years at the yearly Wages of £5, quitted that Service about 21 Years ago & since hath not since lived as an hired Yearly Servant for 12 Months Rented any House of or Apartment of £10 a Year or done any other Act whatever whereby she might have gained any other legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And that she is Poor and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn the 21st. April 1791 before Us  Edw: Read  Wm Hyde

Ann Court 's Mark
