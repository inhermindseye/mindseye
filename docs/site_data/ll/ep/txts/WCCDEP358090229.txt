Middx  Sarah Lowndes Aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Lowndes with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint George Bloomsbury about seven years ago and by whom she hath three Children now living namely John aged about six years William aged about four years and Sarah Aged about Twenty Months says her Husband Rented and Lived in a House in Hemlock Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for five years and Upwards at the yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds hath quitted the said House upwards of One year ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of June 1781 before R. Butler

Sarah Lowndes
