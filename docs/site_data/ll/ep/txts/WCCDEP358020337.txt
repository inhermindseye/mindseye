Saint Clement Danes Septr 26th. 1791

Middlesex & Westminster}  J, the undersigned being one of the overseers of the poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes , do hereby undertaken that the Mary day the pauper named in this Examination shall not be removed for the parish of Saint Clement Danes by Virtued the Orders signed on this Examination until she is adjudged to be able to bear such removal Josa Johnston  Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex Maketh oath and Saith, That he hath been informed, and which Information he verily believes that Mary Day  widow aged Sixty five Years or thereabouts who is now week and infirm and lodges in Nogs Head Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid at the House of one Mrs. Davis there and that she the said Mary Day togr- with her late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Hoxton in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the Co: of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of twelve Pounds, that they for four or five Years, that they quitted the Same about ten Years ago, and since then the said Mary Day hath not gained any Subsequent Settlement other by Renting any Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten pounds or other and that the Husband of the said Mary Day died at the House above mentioned in Hoxton aforesaid

Sworn this 26th Day of Septr 1791 . before us  Wm Hitchiner R Butler

Benj: Newman
