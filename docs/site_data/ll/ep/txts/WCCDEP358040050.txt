Middx  Mary Chapman upon Oath says she never was married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Duntree in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for Fifteen Months or thereabouts & quitted her sd. Service about three months ago and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same and that John Barry a Weaver did beget the said Bastard Child on her Body the said John Barry liveing at Mr. Duntree's aforesaid where he became acquainted with this Examinant & had carnal knowledge of her Body in the latter End of Novr. or beginning of December last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Bastard Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant & that he the said John Barry and no other person is the true and reall father thereof

Sworn the 19th. Augt 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Chapman
