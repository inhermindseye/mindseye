Westmr.  Thomas Etherington aged about 54 Years Upon Oath says he never was Married and that about seven or eight Years ago, he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Robert Salkield Esqr . in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds and hath not since he quitted said Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn 4th, day of August 1767 before} Thos Kynaston  Paul Vaillant

Thomas Etherington
