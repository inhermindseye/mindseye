Middlesex ss}  Rachael Kebbell the widow of George Kebbell deced maketh oath that lately & Since her said husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Mr Decosta a Jew Merchant in St Mary Ax in the parish of St Andrew undershaft in the City of London at the yearly Wages of Five pounds & Continued in Such Service for the Space of fourteen months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or been Since Married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th day of October 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Rachael Kebbell
