Middlesex ss}  Jane Price the wife of William Price maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her as a Marine in his Majesties Service That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with one Mr Green a Gardiner in the Parish of St John in the City of Bristol & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Years & upwards & has not Since to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain anothr Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 20. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Jane Price
