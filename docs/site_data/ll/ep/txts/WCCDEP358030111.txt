Middx  Eliz: Chace aged about 19 Yrs. upon her Oath says she is a Singlewoman & now lives a Yearly Servant with Mr. John Miller at the Temple Eating house in Sheir Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and hath so done for the Space of Three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £4 and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says one Joseph Kearle of Castle Street near the Mowse in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields  Shoemaker did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body he the sd. Joseph Kearle being acquainted with this Examinant had Carnall knowledge of her Body about the Middle of Octr. last in her sd. Masters house & at sevll. times since at one of which

times he gott her with Child of the Bastard Child or Children she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Joseph Kearle & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworn March 7th 1745 before  Wm. Jones

Elizabeth Chace
