Middx 

John Levy upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife Ann in the Parish Church of St. Dunstan in the west London about 15 Years ago that he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £18 in Drury Lane opposite the Coleyd. in the Parish of St Giles in the fields in the County of Middx for Six months & upwards and hath quitted the sd. house abt. 7 Years since which he has not rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn 18th. Novr. 1748 before}
