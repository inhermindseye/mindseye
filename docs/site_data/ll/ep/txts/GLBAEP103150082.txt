Middlesex  Elizabeth Whitehorne the widow of Robert Whitehorne deced maketh oath that divers Years ago her said late husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx & lived therein for Several Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement here That he did not afterwards Rent a house elsewhere of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlemt. for herself & that She was lawfully married to her said husband in the Church belonging to said parish

Sworn this 17th day of November 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Whitehorne
