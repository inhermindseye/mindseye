Middlesex ss}  Nicholas Daly maketh oath that he was born in  Finglas near Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland That he this Depont. never was bound apprentice in England nor ever lived as a hired Servant there for a year nor ever Served any Parish office there nor ever rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds there nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes there nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement there And that he hath a Wife named Sarah to whom he was lawfully Married.

Sworn this 31 day of July 1780 before  Jno Sherwood

Nicholas Daly
