Middlesex ss  Thomas Holder maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired and lived as a hired Servt by the Year at Certain Wages by & with one Green who then kept the Red Lyon Alehouse in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County and Continued in Such Service under hiring for the Space of a year That the has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or rented a house or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Susannah to whom he was lawfully Married

Sworn this 27th day of December 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Thomas Holder
