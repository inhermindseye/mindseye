Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of William Greenham who will Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said William Greenham lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Guntor a Confectioner in Berkeley Square for or in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of Twelve Guineas that he hath quitted said Service about Eight Months since which time he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of June 1798 before us J Reeves Saml. Edward

B. Newman
