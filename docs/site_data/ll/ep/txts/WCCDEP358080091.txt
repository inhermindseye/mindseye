Middx & Westmr.}  Felix Lane Aged about Thirty five Years on his Oath Says he is a Widower and has so been for Eighteen Months or thereabouts And by whom he never had a Child Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one Henry Kitchen a Copper Plate Printer in Water Lane Fleet Street in the parish of Saint Bride otherwise Bridges London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds Per Ann paid taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 29th day of Octr. 1776 R. Butler

Felix Lane
