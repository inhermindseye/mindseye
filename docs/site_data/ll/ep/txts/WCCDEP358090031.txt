Middx to wit Mary Ann Gundy Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Gundy who died about a Year and an half ago as she has heard and believes and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Rotherhith Surry in the Year 1775 & by whom she hath one Child living namely Harriot Augusta Aged about Three Years Says her said late Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship the first Three Years with his Father at Ross in the County of Hereford and the last four Years with his Uncle in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and further Says that on her said Husbands Mothers Death he went down to Ross in Herefordshire and Continued to live there for about four Months on a small Estate which fell to him & whilst he resided there he either sold or Mortgaged the same and this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that George Phillips a Ropemaker who lately worked at Mr. Hoarsncar Bell Dock Wapping & who lately also took a House at No. 3 in White Cross or white Horse Street near New Crane Ratcliffe Cross is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her lodgings in Milk Alley near Wapping Old Stairs in the Month of August 1778 and at sevl. Different times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said George Phillips and no other person did be get the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1779 before}

Mary Ann Gundy
