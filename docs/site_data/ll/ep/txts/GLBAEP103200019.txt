Settlemt. by Apprenticeship

Middlesex ss}  George Hunter maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre to Thomas Wilkinson of South Shields in the Bishoprick of Durham  Smith for the Term of Seven Years & Served five Years & two Months of such his apprenticeship when his Master died & he was discharged from his Apprenticeship & has not since done any act to gain a subsequent Settlement That he has a wife named Mary & a Child named Mary aged about fifteen years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said wife to whom he was lawfully Married the which Child has not as yet done any act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 24. day of June 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of George Hunter
