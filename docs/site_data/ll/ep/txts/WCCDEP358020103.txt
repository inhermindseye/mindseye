Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Jones aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Livingston an Attorney for Eighteen Months or thereabouts that the last forty days of her said Servitude was in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes that after she quitted said Service she never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further saith that she hath one Child living vizt. Richard Nicols born a Bastard in the House of a Mr. Diamond in Angel Court in the Parish of Saint Margarets Westmr.

Sworn this 19th. day of Septr. 1788 R Butler

Jane Jons
