Middx  Ann Harris upon Oath Saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Harris to whom she was married at the Fleet London on the 30th. of Janry upwds. of 13 Yrs. ago & by whom she has one Child Wm. aged abt. 12 Yrs. and that she has been further informed & believes her sd. late husband was born in Ram Alley in the parish of St. Dunstan in the West Londn. and, of his Settlemt. she can give us further Accot.

 Harrow School} And this Examint. further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith the sd. Wm. Harris went beyond Sea about two months after the Birth of her first Child & dyed never returned to England again but dyed abroad, without ever having Seen this Examint. from the time he first Set Sail and that about Eight Years ago she was delivered of a male bastard Child in the house of Mr Wint a Watch maker in Shoe Lane near Holborn in the Parish of St. Andw. Holbn . London which was baptized John & is still living and that Mr. Douglas who was Waggon master General of the Army did beget the sd. male bastard Child on her Body and the sd. Douglas & no Man else is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Augt. 17th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Harris
