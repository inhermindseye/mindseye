Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Wm. Acros of No. 17 in the Cloisters near St. Bartholomews Hospital on behalf of Fras. Acros this Ext. Mother who is insane Upon his Oath saith that he  is the Son of the sd. Fras. Acros that upwards of 40 years ago his sd. Mother Fras Acros Intermarried with Jos: Acros his present Father at May Fair Chaple that his sd Father about 17 years ago bought a Lease of a House situate at Broad Wallnea the Nitre in the Parish of Christ Church  Surry that he gave 28 pounds for the same subject to  a Ground rent of £3..10..0 that he kept same for abt. 17 years & paid The Land Tax due for the same That his sd. Father has quitted same about 10 years since which time he has not read any place of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th- day of June 1787 Maddington Sampr Wright

Wm: Acros .
