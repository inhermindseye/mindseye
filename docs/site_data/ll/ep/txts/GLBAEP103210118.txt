Middlesex ss  Margaret Mills the wife of John Mills maketh oath that her said husband is lately gone away from her to Places unknown That he heretofore (to wit about eighteen years ago) rented a house near the Church in the parish of Green wich in the County of Kent at the yearly rent of Five Pounds & ten shillings & during such his residence thereon he was charged to and Paid Poors Rates & other Taxes for the same That Since his renting Such house he hath not rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten pounds or served any Parish office or paid any poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 15 day of Novemr 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Margaret Mills
