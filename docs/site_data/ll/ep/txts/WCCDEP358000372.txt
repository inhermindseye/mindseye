Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Elizth. Hockly aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Marrd Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year that about 2 years & ago she went to live as a hired yearly Servt . with one Richd. Ward a Perfumer in Oxford Street in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone that she lived in his said Service about 21 Months at the yearly Wages of £7 hath quitted said Service about 7 Months ago since which time she has done no Act to gain any other Settlement

Sworn this 19th. of Octr. 1786  Jno Barnfather  James Paine

her Elizth. Hockly Mark
