Middx  Wm. Williams aged about Thirty two years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Burton in the Capacity of a Footman near St. Georges Hospital  Knightsbridge in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . upwds. of one year & an half at the yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds hath quitted his said Service about Six years ago since which time he hath never lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained any or Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 25th. day of September 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

William Williams
