Middx to wit Ann Crick Aged upwards of fifty Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Crick who has been dead about fourteen Years Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Proudfoot in Gate Street in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for Three Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pound hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1771 before Us} R. Butler Petr Planck

her Ann Crick Mark

Witness George Langley
