Middx  Cathn. Roberts on Behalf of Mary Jones an Infant aged upwards of three Years upon her Oath saith the sd. Mary Jones was born at the Paviours Arms in Water Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex as this Examinant hath been informed & verily beleives Says she can't give any Account of the Settlemt. of the Father or Mother of the sd. Infant other than that they were born in some Part of Wales

Sworn June 25th 1746 before}

The Mark of Cath. Roberts
