Middx ss:  James Teague maketh Oath that he was about fifty Years ago bound an Apprentice by Indre for ye Term of Seven Years to Nicholas Mathews then of ye Pish of St. Olive Southwarke in the County of Surry  Waterman in that part of ye sd. Parish which is now as this Dept. believes ye Parish of St. John Southwark and lately constituted a Parish & taken out of ye sd. Pish of St. Olive and that he lived with his Sayed Master upwards of one Year and was then propod into ye Govermts. Service And this Depont. Sayth yt he never was a hired Served by ye Year Since his Apprenticeship nor rented a House of ten Pounds, a Year or upwards nor Served any Pish. Offices nor done any Act to acquire a Subsequent Settlemt. to his knowledge or believe

Sworn this 31 Day of March 1753 before us- Richd: Ricards  Thos: Quarrill

his James Teague Mark
