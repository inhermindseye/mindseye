Middlesex ss}  Dinah Mead the Widow of Samuel Mead deced maketh oath that her late husband has informed her & Which she believes to be true That he was born in Yeovill in the County of Somerset Where She this Depont was also born & this Deponent Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or eithr of them or of the Settlement of her husbands Parents or eithr of them That her sd husband to her knowledge or belief never rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself Save What he might have Acquired by his Apprenticeship in manner as is herein after mentioned That is to Say That She this Deponent has been farther informd by her Said husband & which She believes to be true that he was bound Apprentice to & Served part of his time with a person who was by Trade a Shoemaker but whose name She has forgot it the Parish of Chittenhold in the County of Dorset & that he Served the Residue of Such Apprenticeship with another Person who was also by Trade of a Shoemaker in the Parish of Overshott in the Same County but with which Master he served the first or last part of his Apprenticeship She Cannot at this distance of time recollect or Set forth & therefore She Cannot discover in Which of the Parishes her said husband was Settled at the time of his death That She this Deponent hath a Child named Samuel Aged about Twenty Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to Whom she was married in the Parish Church of Saint Luke in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 15th day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Dinah Mead
