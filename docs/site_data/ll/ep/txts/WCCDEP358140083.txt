Middx  Margt. Keynd aged abt. 53 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Keynd who hath dead abt. twenty Years to whom she was Married at the fleet London in the Year 1722. says her said late husbd. before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Penny an Attorney at law for two Years or thereabouts says Mrs. Penny lived sometimes in the County & sometimes at No.2 in Clements Inn in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . says her sd. late husband lived the last three Months of his Service with the said Mr. Penny at No. 2 in Clemts. Inn in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes aforesaid & never after he quitted sd. Service Rented ten Pounds a Yr. or pd. Parish Taxes. says she hath not since his death Rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th. day of Febry 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Margt. Keynd
