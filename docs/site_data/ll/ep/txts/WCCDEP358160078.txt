The Exaicon of Sarah the wife of Andrew Francis Cossert passed from the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . taken the 28th. day of Septr. 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that on the Sixth day of March 1737/8 she Intermarryed with the above named Andrew Francis Cossert who is a Frenchman & never gained a Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or beleife And this Exaiant further Saith that abt. three Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Spencer a Cheesemonger in Shire Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath not since gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband hath absconded from her & this Exaiant doth not know where he is and haveing one Child (to wit) Sarah Aged abt. Six Weeks is poor & not able to maintaine the same

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Sarah Cossert
