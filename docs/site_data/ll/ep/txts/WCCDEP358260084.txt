Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Jones aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Eighth day of December now last past she Intermarried with Robert Jones her  late Husband who ha been dead about Six weeks at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn by whom she hath no Child or Children living That her said late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Best who kept the Sign of the Two Blue Posts in the Haymarket in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for about Three years but what wages he had she this Examinant doth not know and that after he quitted said Service he never to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief lived to the best of this Examinants as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Novr. 1795 before us} M Addington Russl Clarke

her Elizh. Jones mark

Witness B Newman
