Middx.  Sarah Hall aged abt. 27 Years upon Oath says she never was married says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with one Mr. Jones a Coal Merchant at the bottom of Beaufort Buildings in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx . for one Year & Eight Months at the Yearly Wages of fifty Shillings & hath quitted said Service abt. three Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th. day of Febry 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Hall
