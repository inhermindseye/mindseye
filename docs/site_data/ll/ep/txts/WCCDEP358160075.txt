Middx  The Exaicon Anne the wife of Jonathan Copen Lodging in ye Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the Eight day of Augt. 1739 who upon her Oath Saith that on the fourth day of Janry 1735 she this Examnt Intermarryed with the above named Jonathan Copen and further Saith that he the sd. Jonathan Copen was bound an Apprentice to & Served three Years or thereabts. the first part of his Apprentiship with one Jno. Lane in Fetter Lane in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West in the City of Londn . Perukemaker as this Examnt hath been informed and verily beleives and further Saith that her sd. Husband hath not since that time rented any House paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for the Space of one Yeare or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworn the Day & Yeare above named before} Jas: Gronous  Thos. Deveil

Ann Coppen
