Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Kitts aged about 44 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That in or about the year 1768 she was bound an Apprentice to one Philip Wathell who kept the Sign of the Green Man at Plumpstead in the Parish of Plumpstead in the County of Kent by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Woolwich for Seven years that she served her said Master under such Indenture about Two years when the Magistrates discharged this Depont. on account of her said Master ill treating her and that after she quitted her said Master she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months at any one time Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Febry. 1796 }

her Elizth. Kitts mark
