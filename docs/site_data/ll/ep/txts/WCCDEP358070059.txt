Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Burt Aged about Nineteen Years on behalf of James Burt her Brother an Infant Aged about Seven Years on her Oath Says that her Father John Burt Intermarried with her late Mother at Salisbury as she has often heard her said Father and Mother decla Says her late Father hath Seven Children by her late Mother all of whom are provided for Execept the said James Burt as she Verily believes Says her late Father John Burt Rented and lived in a House at Sutton Scotney in the Parish of Sutton Scotney in the County of Southampton known by the Name or Sign of the White Swan for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds or thereabouts and paid all Parish taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Two Years ago and after her said late Father quitted the same he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath the said James Burt her Brother

Sworn this 8th. day of January 1773 before Us.} R. Butler

Elizth Burt
