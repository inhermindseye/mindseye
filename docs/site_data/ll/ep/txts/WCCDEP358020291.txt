St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster (to wit) Frances Webb Aged Thirty Years & upwds. upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Joseph Williams at the Cock & Crown in Aldersgate Street for Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of £4, that she immediately afterwards lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Robt Band at the Star in Aldg Aldersgate Street Aforesaid for one entire year at the Annual Wages of £5, that both the Houses where she so lived as aforesd aforesaid were situate in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldersgate in the Freedom of the City of London that that And this Deponent saith that she left Mr Band's Service abt. four or Five Years ago and since hath not done any Act or thing whatsoever whereby to gain any legal settlement to the best of her knowledge Information or belief and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 23d. day of May 1791 before us J Pownall Wm Kitchiner
