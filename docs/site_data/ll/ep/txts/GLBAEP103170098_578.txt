Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Allford  Singlewoman maketh oath that her Father Robert allford Sometime ago Rented two houses of the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds in Back Lane  Cable Street in the parish of St George in the said County & resided thereon for Several years & by means there of gained a legal Settlemt. there That he has not Since to this Deponts knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this Depont is legally Settled in the said parish in right of her said Father She never having been bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year in any one place nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 1 day of October 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Elizabeth Allford
