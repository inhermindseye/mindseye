Middx  Sarah Wooton aged 20 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & that she never Rented Ten Pounds a Year was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year That her Father  Joseph Wooton lived in & Rented a House the Sign of the Bull in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds or thereabouts for the Space of four Years or thereabouts as this Exaiant hath been Informed & beleives

Sworne the 7th- June 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Sarah Wooton
