Middlesex ss}  Margaret Jolly the widow of Richard Jolly deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband rented a house of Six pounds a year in Rose & Crown Court in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & during such his Residence there in was charged to & paid the Kings Tax for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & this Deponent can give no other Account respecting her said husbands having gained a Settlement elsewhere & that She this Deponent Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement  for herself & that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 4 day of May 1777 before}

The Mark of Margaret Jolly
