St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }

St. Geo: Hanover Square (to wit)  Mary Nelson aged twenty one Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with his Grace the Dahia of Hamilton at the Yearly Wages of ten Guineas for about thirteen Months That she quitted his Service about a fortnight since, that she lived for upwards of forth Days before she quitted her said Service at his Grace House Grafton Street  Piccadilly in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in this County of Middlesex and since quitting his Service hath not done any hit whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement. and that she never has been married

Sworn this 27th. Day of June 1791 before R Butler D Walker

Mary Nelson
