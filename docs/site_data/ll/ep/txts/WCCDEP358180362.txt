Middx  Mathew Hobbs aged about 37 Years upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  mary on or about the 14th. Day of August 1734 at the Fleet London by whom he hath one Child viz Mary aged about Seven Years Says he lived a Yearly hired Servant whilst he was a Singleman with Mr. Jno. Chilton at the Upper End of St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £4 for the Space of 4 Yrs. & hath left his Sd. Service about 14 Years since which time he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 3th Augt. 1734 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Mathw. Hobbs
