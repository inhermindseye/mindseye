Middx  The Examination of Robert Howard on the Behalfe of Agnes Gray  Singlewoman aged Twenty Years & upwds. upon her Oath saith that upwards of Five Years ago the above named Agnes Gray was hired to & lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Jeffrys in little Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . Taylor for the Space of Fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds & that the sd. Agnes Gray never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & that she in Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine herself

Sworne the 1st of December 1741 before} Tho Lane .

Robert Howard
