Middx.  Hannah Sharlock aged abt. 50 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Joseph Sharlock who has been dead abt. Eighteen Years. says that being a Widow and without Child or Childn. she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Salmon in the house of Mr Williams in New North Street in the Parish of St. George the Martyr by Queen Square in the County of Middx. for one Year and upwards at the Yearly wages of £4 and hath quitted said Service abt. One Year and a half: since which time she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 9th. day of Novr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Hannah Sherlock

Upon the Complaint of Mr R: Wimberley Ovr . J: P:
