Westmr. to wit Ann Ross aged about sixty years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Ross who has been Dead upwards of twenty five years ago by whom she hath no Children now living And further says that being witht- Child or Children she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Wilson a Carpenter in Swan Yard in the Parish of Saint Mary le Strand for two Years at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted that said Service twenty Years ago since which time this Examinant hath never gained any other subseqt Settlemt either by Renting house or orwise. howsoever

Sworn this 16th- Day of Janry 1777 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Ross Mark
