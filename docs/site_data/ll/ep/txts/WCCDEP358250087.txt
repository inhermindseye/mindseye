St Clement Danes 16th January 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Ann Beadman  Widow aged 48 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband William Beadman at the parish Church of St. Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex on or about the 5th day of September 1762 that subsequent ther this Deponents Husband Rented and resided in an House in Houghton Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for one year and a quarter or thereabout at the Annual Rent of £26 that  her said Husband died there and she this Examinant quitted it about four  Weeks after and she hath not since been Married Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement that all her Children are provided for and settled in their own rights that she has been ill distressed and unable to provide for herself without assistance

Sworn the 16th January 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Ann Beadman 's Mark

Witness Garrett
