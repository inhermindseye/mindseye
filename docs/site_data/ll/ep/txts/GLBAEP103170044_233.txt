Middlesex ss  Margaret Redmond the widow of Michael Redmond deced maketh oath That Since the death of her Said late husband She rented a house of the yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds in that part of the Butcher Row Which is in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & resided there on for the Space of a year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and that She has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this. 10. day of January 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Margert Redmond
