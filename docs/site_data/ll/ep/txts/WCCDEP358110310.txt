Middx.  Joseph Bentley upon Oath Saith he intermarried with his wife Eliz. Bentley at the Fleet London near Eleven Years since and that he was bound Apprentice to Mr. Chaloner of  Surry Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes Middx & lived with him near 4 Years at the end of which he was assigned over to Wm Willis  Victualler with whom he lived about 14 months at the there Horse shoes in Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes afsd. and then the sd. Mr. Willis broke & this Examinant never rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Septr. 13th 1753 before}
