Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Elizabeth Williams aged 54 years or thereabouts Widow Upon Oath saith that she was Married to Mr. George Williams at the parish Church of Saint Mary in the City of Ely about 34 years ago (by Licence) that her said Husband Died about 10 years ago leaving only one Daughter now liv living who was then of the Age of 22 years or upwards and provided for herself And this Deponent further saith that since her said Husband Decease And during her Widowhood having no C she lived as an hired Servant with John Morris  Esquire in New Boswell Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx for a year and upwards at the Annual Wages of £6 that she quitted that Service about four years ago And hath not since obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 20th day of February 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Eliz Williams
