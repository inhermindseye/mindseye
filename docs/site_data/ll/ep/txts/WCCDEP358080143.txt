Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Slider aged about 40 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes says she lived A hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Bibby a Staymaker in Hedge Lane in the Psh of St Martin in the Fields for one Year and three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of £4 hath quitted the said Mrs. Bibbys Service upwards of four Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subseqt. Settlemt.

Sworn this 14th. Day of May 1777  Jno. Machin John Croft

her Sarah Slider Mark
