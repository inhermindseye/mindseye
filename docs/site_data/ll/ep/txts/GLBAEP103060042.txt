Middx  Thomas Nash Maketh oath and Saith that about Nine years ago he was hired a Yearly Servant to Mr Buxton in Houndsditch in the parish of St Botolph Aldgate London and served him as such two years and pd- Seven pounds a year wages And has gained no Subseqt Settlemt- And that he hath a wife named Jane and two Children Robert about four years and Mary about three years.

Sworn this 13th June 1745 before Saml Gower

Thos Nash
