Middx.  John Austin aged abt. 39 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Mary his present Wife in the Parish Church of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx. abt. three Years ago by whom he hath one Child Mary aged abt. one Year & a half & another Child by a former Wife named Alice aged abt. Five Years says he rented a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty three Pounds lived therein abt. four Years & quitted same abt. three Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 25th. day of January 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

John Austin
