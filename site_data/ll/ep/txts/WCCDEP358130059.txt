Middx  Phoebe Venden Aged about 27 Years upon her voluntary Oath says She never was married That She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor & unable to maintain the same where born and that one William Foster  Apprentice to Mr. Gardiner a Packer in little St. Helens London did begett the said Bastard Child or Children on her Body of which She is now pregnant he the said William Foster having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of January last in the House of one Mrs. Smith in Water Lane Fleet Street and at Several times since And further upon Oath says that Thomas Venden her late Father deced Rented a House of Eighteen Pounds a Year in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry and lived in the same several Years That She this Examinant never Rented a House nor lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 30th. Day of Septr. 1755 before } Barth: Hammond

Phebe Venden
