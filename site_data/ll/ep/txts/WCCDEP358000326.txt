Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Elizabeth Card  Wife of John Card of Westminster in the County of Middlesex  Laborer -Maketh Oath and saith that the said John Card her Husband hath deserted her and refuses to maintain her and that she is now maintained by and at the Expence of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lives in the Workhouse of the said Parish

Sworn this 21st. day of July 1786 before me R Butler

Elizabeth Card
