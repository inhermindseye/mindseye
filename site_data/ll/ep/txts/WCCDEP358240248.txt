St Clement Danes April 17th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D (paid him 2s for Relief)  Marriage  Children  (to wit) William Sellwood aged thirty eight Years an or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he served his Apprenticeship with Mr Adey Glass seller Clements Inn foregate in the parish of St George Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex (under Indentures) that he and lodged and boarded in his said Master's House that his Apprenticeship expired about sixteen years ago and hath not since acquired any other legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And saith that he was Married to his present Wife Catherine formerly Catherine Walker  Spinster at the parish Church of St George in the East in the County of Middlesex (by Banns) an or about the twentieth day of May 1787 that he hath two Children by his said Wife Namely William born on the 17th day of March 1788 and Mary Ann born on or about the 17th. day of July last And that he is distressed and unable to maintain himself and family without assistance

Sworn the 17th- day of April 1793 before Me}

William Sellwood
