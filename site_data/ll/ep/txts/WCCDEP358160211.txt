Middx The Examination of Grace Woodham Wife of Jno. Woodham now in the Workhouse belonging to St. Andrew Holbourn taken the 18th. Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband Jno. Woodham was bound Apprentice to one Mr. Crospy in Lickapond Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above Barrs in the County of Middx  Farrier & lived with him as an Apprentice for the Space of four two Years or thereabts. & this Exaiant further saith that her sd. Husband Jno Woodham hath not since he left his sd. Master rented £10. a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of 1 Yr. or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Exaiants Husband hath informed her & which she verily beleives.

Sworn ye. day & Year above menconed before} Cha: Egerton  Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Grace Woodham
