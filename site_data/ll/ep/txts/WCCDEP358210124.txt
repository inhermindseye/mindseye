Westmr.  Sarah King aged about 30 Years Upon Oath says she was never married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Lord Coote in Church Court  Chapel Street in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westminster for One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds quitted said Service about five Years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 13th. day, of January 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Sarah King
