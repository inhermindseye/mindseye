Westmr.  Mary Kitchen Aged Fifty five Years, Wife of Thomas Kitchen on behalf of her Daughter Mary Barber aged about Twenty three Years by a former Husband Upon Oath says the said Mary Barber never was Married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says that Robert Barber deceased father of the said Mary Barber Rented a House in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds five Shillings for Two Years and Upwards and there died

Sworn the 8th. day of December 1767 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Hitchen
