Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Ellis of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County widow takes on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this day of 1766.

Who Saith that about days ago in the house of one Edward Batchelor in East Smithfield in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County she this Examinant was delivered of two Male Bastard Children and that the said two Bastard Children are become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate And that one Henry Bagnall of the Parish of Saint George Southwarke in the County of Surry Watch Finisher did get her with Child of the said Bastard Children And that the said Bastard Children have been since Baptised by the names of James and Joseph Bagnall in the Church belonging to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this day of 1766 before dead
