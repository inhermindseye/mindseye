Middx  The Examination of Mary Bailey  Singlewoman Lodging in ye. Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 4th. Day of Augst. 1739 Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith yt. she is Pregnant with Child or Childn. wch when Born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she this Examint. being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when Born And further upon her Oath saith yt. one Patrick Hart Tripeman did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body she this Examint. at such time liveing as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Barnfield Tripeman in Hog Island & he the sd. Patrick Hart at the same time being Journeyman to the sd. Mr Barnfield & liveing in the House, there became acquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body Sevl. times at one of wch times he got her with the Bastard Child or Childn. with wch she is now Pregnant & that he & no other Person is the true & real Father thereof And this Examint. further saith yt. about two Years ago she was hired to & lived with one Mr. Betts Victualler in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes as a Yearly hired Servt . for the space of one Year at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds and hath not since that time Rented £10 a Year lived with any other person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Thos. Neil

The Mark of Mary Bailey
