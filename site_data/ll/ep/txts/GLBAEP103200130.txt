Middlesex ss  Samuel Burnell maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to Richard Gelson of the Parish of All Sainte in the City of York  Cordwainer & after having Served him there for the Space of a year his Indres were delivered up & Cancelled & this Depont. has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 6. day of May 1784 before R Davies

Saml; Burnell
