Middx  Jane Moor upon Oath Saith she  was married to her husband Thos Moore at the Fleet London upwds. of 20 Years Since and that she hath been Informed and verily believes her sd husband Served his Apprentisp with Mr. Almet a Peruke maker near the Lamb Inn behind St. Clements Church in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes and never rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn March 27th. 1753 before}
