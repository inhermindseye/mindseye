Middx.  Cathne. Baker on Behalf of Ann Saunders aged abt. Seventy one Years upon Oath says that the sd. Ann Saunders is the Widow of Richd. Saunders deced Says the said Richd. Saunders lived in & Rented a House in Off Alley in York Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for Twenty Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds & upwards & here dyed Says the sd. Ann Saunders has not since the Death of her said Husband Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn Decr. 10th. 1756 before} Tho. Lane

Kath Baker
