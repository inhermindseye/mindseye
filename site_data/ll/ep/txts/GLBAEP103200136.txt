Middlesex ss}  Ann Murray  Widow of George Murray deced maketh oath that her said late husband heretofore rented a house of twelve pounds a year & upwards in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same That She this Depont about thirty four Years ago was Married to her sd husband at May Fair Chapel That her sd husband in not after renting Such house as above do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement nor has She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 25 day of May 1784 before R Davies

Ann Murray
