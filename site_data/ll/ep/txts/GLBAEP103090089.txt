Middlesex ss  Sarah Wolley the wife of George Wolley maketh oath that her late Husband Thomas Cock deceased hath informd her that he was duly bound an apprentice and served the whole of his apprenticeship with one Winkley of the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex Cordwainer and Which Information this Depont verily believes to be true And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge information or belief her Said late husband never rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds a year nor served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship And that She hath a Child named Elizabeth Cock aged about Six Years and ten Months the lawful issue of this Deponent by her said late husband Thomas Cock .

Sworn this 7th: day of December 1767 before  Chris: Scott  Rt Darling

the Mark of & Sarah Wolley
