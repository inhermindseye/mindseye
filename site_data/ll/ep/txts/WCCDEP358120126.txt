Middx  Alice Aston upon Oath saith she was married to her late husband William Aston at a Church about Six miles from Croyden in Surry and that she has been informed and verily believes her sd. late husband (whilst a Singleman) lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Thos. Chatt  Distiller at the Corner of Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for one Year & upwards and that neither her sd. late husband after he quitted his sd Service nor has this Examint. Since his Decease rented Ten pounds by the Year pd. Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn June 19th. 1754 before

The Mark of Alice Aston
