Middx  Harvell Parish Aged about 25 Years removed from the Parish of St. James Westmr. by Order Dated the 26th. Day of January Instt. upon his Oath saith he Intermarried with his present Wife  Eliz : about 5 Years ago at the Fleet London Says before his sd. Intermarriage he lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Edwd. Hapgood at the Red Cow in the Backside of St Clements for 2 Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £4 & since he left his sd. Service hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 28th. Janry 1744 before} Wm. Jones .

Harvell Parish
