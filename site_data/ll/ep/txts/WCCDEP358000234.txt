Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Dinah Bromhall aged 47 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she Intermarried with Joseph Rowley her late Husband at the Christ Church  in the County of Surry about 25 years ago, that her said Husband Joseph Rowley Rented & Lived in a House in Off Alley in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middleses for 3 quarters of a year at the Yearly Rent of £20, That her said Husband Joseph Rowley  quitted the said House about 24 years ago, that her said Husband departed this Life about 10 years  since - that she hath Living by her said Husband  Joseph Rowley One Child Named Ann Rowley of the age of 23 years or thereabouts That  her said Husband Joseph Rowley since he so quitted the said House in his Life time nor she since his Decease nor the said Ann Rowley have not nor hath either of them done anyother Act Matter or thing whereby her said  Daughter might have  gained any Settlement elsewhere then in the said Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the said  County of Middlesex and this Examinant father saith that her

said Daughter Ann Rowley hath never Served any apprenticeship Lived as an Hired Yearly Servant or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any Settlement in her own Right and that her said Daughter is poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 24th. Febry 1786 before us  Sampr Wright Meading

Dinah Bromhall
