Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit John Cooper aged about 42 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch -about 18 Months ago and by whom he has no Children living that he has two one Children living by his late Wife  Ann vizt. Saml. aged 9 years and Wm. aged 6 years Says his Father Wm. Cooper Served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one John Dorrill a Taylor in Feathers Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Margt Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere nor hath this Examinant than as aforesaid

Sworn this 21st. day of May 1785 }
