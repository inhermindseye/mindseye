Westmr.  James Barrett Aged about 24 Years on his Oath Says he never was married Says he lived as a hired Yearly Servant in the Capacity of an Under- Butler with the Countess of Suffolk in Savil Row in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted his said Service upwards of Two years and half ago and after he quitted the same never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1769 before Us} Tho Fellows R. Butler

James Barrett
