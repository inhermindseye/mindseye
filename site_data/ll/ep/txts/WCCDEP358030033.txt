Middx  Hannah Brewis of the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middx  School mistress upon her Oath saith that Elizabeth Matram Daughter of Mary Matram (gone from her) an Infant aged about Nine Months was born in the Workhouse belonging to and in the Parish of a Battersea in the County of Surry as this Exaiant hath been informed and verily beleives Says she can't give any Account of the Settlemt. of the Father or Mother of the sd. Elizabeth Matram other then that she hath heard ye sd. Mary Matram say her Settlement was in the sd. Parish of Batterson

Sworne 19th. Octr. 1745 before J Poulson William Withers

The Mark of Hannah Brewis
