(Qd this Settlemt.) Saint Clement Danes Feby 13th. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Qd if this Settlemt. is not bad St C. D. to wit Elizabeth Cacy Aged 26 Years or thereabouts the Wife of David Casey now Under Sentence of Transfer taken Upon Oath saith that he was Married to her present Husband the said david Cacy on the 4th day of September 1791 at the parish Church of Saint Clare Southwark , - that her said Husband David Cacy lived as an hired Yearly Servant with a Mr. Nessino of No 8 Thanet place Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for Upwards of a Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of £15 that he quitted the said Service in the Month of Jany 1793 and that since he hath done no Act Matter or Thing whatsr whereby to the best of this Examinants Information knowledge or belief she he has gained any subsequent legal Settlement.

Sworn a this 13th day of February 1794} Wm Kitchiner

The mark of Elizabeth Cacy
