Middx.  Sarah Brooks aged abt. 54 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Thos. Brooks (gone from her) say her said husband Rented a house of nine Pounds a year in St. James's Back in the Parish of St. James in the City of Bristol for two years & upwds. & paid to the Poor there & hath quitted sd. house abt. two years ago & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 16th of May 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of S B Sarah Brooks
