Middx.  Jane Phillipson aged 26 Years Upon Oath Says she is the wife of Christr. Phillipson with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. about Three Weeks ago And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 19th. day of October 1754 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mrs Bates a Millener in Russell Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. which has since been baptized by the Name of Thomas and is still living; and that at the time of the Birth of the said Child she was a Single Woman & had never been Married

Sworn this 27th. Decr. 1760 before } Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Jane Phillipson
