Middlesex & Westminster }  St Martin Ludgate  (to wit) Rebecca Hollings Aged 31 Years and upwards Upon her Oath Saith that about 16 Years Ago she intermarried with John Hollings her present Husband at the parish Church of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex that soon after they were Married they took a House No. 27 Holliday Yard  Ludgate Street in the parish of Saint Martin Ludgate in the City of London and Rented and Lived in the said House for Six Months abt. 12 Months at 11 Guineas Pr. Annum that Since she and her said Husband quitted the said House her said Husband has not Rented any Tenemt. or Lodging of £10 a Year pd. Taxes or lived as hired Yearly Servant for One Year or done any or. Act Matter or thing whereby they might have gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge information or belief that She has 5 Children by her said Husband, And that he has deserted her and her said Children fr this 6 Weeks past And that she is Poor and unable to Maintain herself & 2 Small Children Viz Wm. Hollings Aged 2 Years and a half and Sarah Hollings Aged 9 Months the other three being out at Service and Provided for,

Sworn the 9th. day of Augt. 1792 before me } R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Rebekah Hollings
