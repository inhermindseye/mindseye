Middlesex ss}  Jane Young the Wife of Robert Young maketh oath that her said husband heretofore rented a house of Ten Pounds a year in King Henry Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & resided therein for the Space of two years & that her Sd husband and has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 17. day of Decemr 1783 before R Davies

Jane Young
