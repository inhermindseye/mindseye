Middx.  Elizh, Chittenden aged 35 years upon her Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servnt. with Mr Gregory a Sword Cutler in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Four pounds for two years and upwards, quitted her sd. Service upwds. of three years ago and has not since rented ten Pounds by the Yr. pd. parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Yr.

Sworn Feby. 9th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Chittenden
