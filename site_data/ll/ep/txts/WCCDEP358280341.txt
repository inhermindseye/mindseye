Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  George Robinson  Master of the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Maketh Oath and Saith that she the said Elizth. Naylor hath informed this Deponent (which information he verily believes to be true) that she was deliver'd of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse of the parish of Saint Margarett Westminster in the County of Middlesex on the eleventh day of January last past that the said Child hath been Christened by the Name of Elizabeth and Aged about eight months is now eight months Old And that One Whiting Edmonds a Soldier in Licutt. Coll. Burtons Company 3d: Regiment of Foot Guards is the Father of the said Child he having had carnal Knowledge of the said Elizabeth Naylor Examinants Body sometime in the Month of and at several times since at one of which time he did beget the said Baster'd Chid of which she was so deliver'd as aforesaid and that the said Whiting Edmonds is the only Real and true Father thereof as the said Elizabeth Naylor hath informed this Examinant

Sworn this 12th. Octr. 1791 before} Philip Dyot D Walker

George Robertson
