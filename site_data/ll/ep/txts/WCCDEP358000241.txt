Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Ann Gibson Chartres aged 17 years on thereabouts upon her Oath saith that on the 4th. day of January last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint  Paul Shadwell which hath since been Christened by the Name of Benjn. Chartres That Benjn. Haycoch a Coal Merchant in the Adelphi is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of May last and at several times afterwards at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Benjn. Haycock and no other Person did beget the said Child  and that he is the only and real and true further thereof as aforesaid

Sworn this 2nd. day a of March 1786  Edward Hodsoll

Ann Chartres

Sent her & Child to Chew castle
