Westmr.  Ann Ireland Aged about Sixty Three Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Edward Ireland who has been dead upwards of Six Years Says that before her said Husbands Intermarriage with this Examint. and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Thomas Jones an Auctioneer at Craven House in Wych Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr. for Sixteen Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds besides dyet & Lodging as her said Husband has often informed her and which information She verily believes to be true And further Says that after her said Intermarriage with her said Husband they both lived hired Yearly Servants with the said Mr. Jones for Two Years and upwards her said Husband at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds diet and Lodging and this Examint. at the Yearly Wages of five pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant or Servant for one Year

Sworn this 21st. Day of Janry 1769 Tho: Lane

Ann Ireland
