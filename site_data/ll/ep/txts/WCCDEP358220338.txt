Westmr. to wit Margaret Clinton Aged about fifty Eight Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Robt. Clinton  (gone from her) and with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about the Year 1737 Says her said late Husband was an Irishman and never gained any. Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief Says that before her Said Intermarriage and which she was a Singlewoman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. John Horbin a Distiller in Bishopgate Street in the Parish of St. Botolph Bishopgate London upwards of 3 years at the Yearly Wages of £5. and quitted her said Service upwards of 37 Years ago & hath not since she quitted her said service done any Act whereby to gain any Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 3d day of April 1772 before us}  R. Butler

Margaret Clinton
