Middx & Westmr.}  Elizth. Best Aged about Twenty Seven Years upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was married And that on the fifteenth day of March 1765 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Savage a Stocking Weaver in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Baptized by the Name of William Henry Says that one William Sturges late Book keeper to Mr. Heath Fishmonger at Temple Bar in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of June 1764 and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid And that the said William Sturges and no other person is the true and real Father thereof.

Sworn this 26th. day of May 1769 before}

her Elizth. Best Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
