Middx  Ann Davis aged about 28 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wife of William Davis a Soldier in his Majesty's Service now in Germany to whom she was Married at Bambury in Northamptonshire upwards of Two Years ago That her said Husband never kept House was an Apprentice or hired Yearly Servant to her Knowledge That she cannot tell where he was Born

Sworn the day of May 1761 Before}
