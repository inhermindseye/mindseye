Militia Man's Wife Wide Entry in Militia Examn Book

St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  unrolled 15th Janry 1791.} (to wit) Mary Ann Davis aged 27 years or thereabouts Maketh Oath and saith that on the 28th. day of July 1791 (being then a singlewoman ) she was Married at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex (by Banns) to William Davies who as she verily believes (having no reason to think the contrary) was at that time a singleman and had no Wife living And this Deponent further saith that she hath been informed (which she believes to be true) that her said Husband William Davies  was sworn and unrolled to serve as a substitute for George Thompson of the parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster in the Westminster Regnient of Middlesex Militia and now serves in the said Militia which is unbodied and called out into actual service and this Examinant of on her Oath feather saith that ever since the 28th. day of January last when he said Husband was called out into actual service she hath resided and still dwells except where with her Husband at the House of Wm. Davies at the corner of New Court to Little Shire Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex and that she hath ever since had and still hath living and dwelling with her one Child by her said Husband named John born

on the 13th day of May 1792 . And ever since her said Husband hath left her she has been distressed and is now in indegent circumstances and unable to maintain herself and Child

Sworn the 21st. day of March 1793 before me} D Walker
