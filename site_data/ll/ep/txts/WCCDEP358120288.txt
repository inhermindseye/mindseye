Middx  Rd Beverstock on behalf of Mary the wife of John Gibbins decd. upon his Oath saith he was well acquainted with the sd. Jno. Gibbins & has often heard him say that he was an apprentice to one Mr Gurr a Taylor in the parish of Grant Buckham in the County of Surry which Information he believes to be true and the aforementioned Mary Gibbins upon her Oath saith she married the sd. Jno. Gibbins in the Parish Church of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and has by him two Children Ann aged upwds. of 5 Years and Susanna aged abt. 15 months and that her sd. late husband informed her he never rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Feb. 21st 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Richard Beverstock

The Mark of Mary Gibbins
