Westmr. to wit Sarah Cossham Aged about Twenty Years on behalf of Anna Maria Humphrys an Infant Aged about Eight Weeks (whose Mother is Lately dead and the Father Thomas Humphrys gone and deserted it) on her Oath Says that the said Thomas Humphrys Intermarried with Susanna his late Wife at the Parish of Stonehouse in the County of Gloucester about Thirteen Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children now living Vizt. Thomas Aged about Nine Years now Provided for and the above Anna Maria Humphrys Aged about Eight Weeks Says that the said Thomas Humphrys Rented and lived in a House known by the Sign of the Two Pumps in Old Street in the Parish of Saint Luke in the County of Middlesex upwards of half a Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds but whether he ever paid any Rent or Taxes this Examinant Cannot set forth hath quitted the said House upwds. of twelve Months ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of March 1773 before Us R. Butler 

her Sarah Cossham Mark
