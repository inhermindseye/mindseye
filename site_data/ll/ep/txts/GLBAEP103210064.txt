Middlesex ss}  Martha Hollis  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore hired & lived as a yearly hired servant by & with Alexander Davidson  Gun Smith in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such services under such hiring for the Space of one year & upwards That she has not since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath she done any Act to gain a Settlement since her Servitude as above

Sworn this 15. day of February 1785 before J. Hawley

Martha Hollies
