St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster}  Edward Burbidge one of the Bedles Beadles of the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex (for and on behalf of Thomas Pain a Poor Man now lying Ill in the said parish) Maketh Oath and saith that he this Examinant hath been Informed by the Mother of the said Thomas Pain and which he verily believes to be true, that the said Thomas Pain is of the Age of 26 Years of or thereabouts, is the legitimate Son of Samuel and Elizabeth Pain , was Born at the House of Mr.. Clarke's in Vine Street in the parish of St John in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex where his said Father & Mother then lodged and Resided, that he hath not not ever done any Act or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge he might have acquired any legal Settlement in his own Right nor Knows of any settlement he is Intitled unto, (except by Birth as aforesaid) And that he is Poor, Ill and unable to maintain himself

Sworn the 20th April 1791 before us R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Edwd Burbidge
