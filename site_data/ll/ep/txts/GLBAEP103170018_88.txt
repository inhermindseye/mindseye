Middlesex ss}  Easter Halfpenny the widow of James Halfpenny deced maketh oath that her Said late husband was by birth an Irishman & to her knowledge or belief he never rented a house of Ten pounds a year nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in the Kingdom of Great Britain that this Depont previous to her Marriage with her Sd husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one John Franklin a Grocer near Exeter Exchange in the Strand in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Sd County at Certain yearly Wages & Continued in Such Service for three years & half & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant by the year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Save what She Acquired by Marriage with her husband whose Settlement is unknown

Sworn this 18. day of October 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Saml. Wood

The Mark of Easter Halfpenny
