Middx  Margt. Bedward Aged about Twenty Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edward Daintree a Baker in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings & hath left her said Service about three Days And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Thomas Mitchell a Baker who is now Run away & this Examinant Cannot tell where he is, had Carnall knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Months of May or June

Last & at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn ye 9th of January 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Margt Bedward
