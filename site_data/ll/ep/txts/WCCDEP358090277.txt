Middlesex to wit John Graves Aged about 50 Years on his Oath says Intermarried to Catherine Graves his now Wife at the Pari Church of St. Clement Danes about Eight Years ago by Whom he hath no Child or Children living Says befo his Inter marriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly With one M Evans a Tallow Chandler in parkers lane in the Parish St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx for 3 Years at the Yearly Wages of £4. hath quitted his said Service about Twenty Years ago since which time he hath gained no Other Settlement either by Renting House paying Tax or Otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 17th. day of Jany. 1782 . before R Butler Thos: Gilbert :

his John Graves Mark
