Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Winifred Avern aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that her Father Rented and lived in a House in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 9 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £10 and upwards that he quitted said House about 9 years since which time he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examt. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one James Collins a Journeyman Taylor who did live in Water Lane  Fleet Street is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said James Collins is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of July 1788 }

her Winifred Avern Mark
