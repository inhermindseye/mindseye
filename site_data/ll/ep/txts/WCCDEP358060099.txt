Middx  Edwd. Brady aged about 36 Years upon Oath saith in the Year 1746 he rented a house opposite Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx (then known by the Sign of the hoop and Bunch of Grapes ) and lived therein about 18 Months and that he took the house of one Marjoram with whom he agreed to pay the Yearly Rent of £30 for the said house and that he actually paid the sd. Rent of £30 for the time he continued to rent the sd house which was 18 months as aforesaid by Quarterly payments. Says he has not since he quitted the sd house rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 7th. 1751 before  Tho Lane .

Edward Brady
