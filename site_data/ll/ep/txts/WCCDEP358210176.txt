Middx & Westmr.}  Margaret Stone aged abt. 25 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Saxton at the Unicorn in Fountain Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds quitted said Service about five Months and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes. And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born, says that James Launder a Surgeon (who this Examinant has been informed now lives and resides at MitchelDean in Gloucestershire ) did beget the said Bastard Child or Children on her Body, he having had carnal knowledge thereof sometime in the Month of December last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Ye. Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said James Launder and no other Person did beget the same;

Sworn the 11th, August 1767 , before } Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Margt. Stone
