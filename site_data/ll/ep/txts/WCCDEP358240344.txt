St Clement Danes October 24th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Abbey Church parish in St Albans Herts  (to wit) Elizabeth Forrest  singlewoman aged twenty three years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never lived as an hired yearly Servant for a Year together Rented any House or Apartment of Ten pounds a Year or did any Act Matter or thing Whatsoever whereby to ob obtain any legal settlement in her own right that she is informed and believes she is the legitimate Daughter of Thomas and Elizabeth Forrest that her Father lived on an Estate of his own in the Abbey Church parish in the Town of St.. Albans in the County of Herts for several years and quitted that parish about the year 1778 or 1779 and And this Examinant that he never after during his Life obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge or information or belief that he Died in or about the Month of February last And that she this Examinant is distressed and

Sworn the 24th day of October 1793 before us}

Eliz Forrest

this Woman not Sworn dischged by the Overseers witht a Removal
