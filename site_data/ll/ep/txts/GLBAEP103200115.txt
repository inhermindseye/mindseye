Middlesex ss}  Mary Judd the widow of William Judd deced maketh oath that she has been informed by her said late husband & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Captain Rawlinson in the Parish of St Ann in the County of Middlesex That her said husband did not afterwards to her Knowledge or belief rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship That she was married to her said husband at the Fleet before the late Marriage Act took effect & has not Since her Sd husbands death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 28. day of February 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Mary Judd
