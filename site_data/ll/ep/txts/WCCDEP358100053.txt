Saint Clement Danes

Middx Westmr to wit Mary Taylor aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Hayes of Mary Bone  Street Shug Lane in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr for 15 Months at the yearly Wages of £7..10s..0 hath quitted said Service for about Thirteen years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Augt. 1786 before  James Croft G Spnadshaw

her Mary Taylor mark
