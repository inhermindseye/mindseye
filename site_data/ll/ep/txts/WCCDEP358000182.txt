Saint Clement Danes 22nd. Decr- 1785

Middx Westminster (to wit) Hannah Hoare  Widow of No. 3 Phenix Alley Long here Maketh Oath and Saith That she has been informed and which Information she this Examinant has reason to belief is true that Ann Walton who is lately deceased was married to Matthew Walton her Husband who now lives at one Sir  Thomas Edward 's in Upper Seyment Street at the Parish Church of Saint Ann Soho that she had after he said Marriage two  Children Vizt.  Matthew now aged fourteen or thereabouts and Grenada aged eight Years or thereabouts that the said Ann Walton lived with her said two Children in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about three Months was taken all there and died, that she left her said Children there who are both ill and unable to maintain them and this Examinant further saith

that she has been informed and believes that the last legal Settlement of the Said Matthew Walton is in the said Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 22nd. Decr- 1785 before  Sampr Wright N Bond

Hannah Hoare
