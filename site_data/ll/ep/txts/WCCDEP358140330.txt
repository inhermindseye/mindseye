Middx.  Mary Tye aged abt. 40 yrs. upon Oath says, she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Colehan Apothecary in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5 and hath quitted said Service upwards of Six Years, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th. Decr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Tye
