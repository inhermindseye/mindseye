Middlesex ss}  Robert Tucker maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by the then Parish offices of the Parish of St John Wapping in the Sd County to Thomas Walter of the Parish of st Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Smith untill he Shod Attain his age of twenty one years That after having Served his Said Master there for about four years & three Quarters of a year differences arising between him & his Master his Indres were delivered for the purpose of being Cancelled and he with his Masters Consent was discharged from his apprenticesp & at Liberty to go where so ever he pleased his said Master not giving his Consent to his Working with any Person for whom he did business after such his discharge

Sworn this. 18. day of Septemr 1787 before  R Davies  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Robert Tucker
