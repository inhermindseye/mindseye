Middx & Westmr.}  Rebecca Green Aged about 34 Years on her Oath says she never was married rented house or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Alexr. Philips an Attornoy at Law in Cecil Street in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for two Years and a half at the yearly Wages of £6s hath quitted her said Service upwards of nine Years ago since which time she hath never gained any other subsequent Settlement either by renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year and furr. on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born says that one Phoenix Lane a Journeyman Copper plate Printer who lately worked with one Mr. Paybody in Wych Street (and who she believes does now) had Carnal Knowld. of her Body upwards of Eighteen Months last past at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Phoenix Lane and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 22d Day of Octr. 1776 R Butler

her Rebecca Green Mark
