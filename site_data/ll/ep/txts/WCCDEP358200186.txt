Middx  John Fell Aged about Sixty Years upon Oath Says he Intermarryed with his Wife Tabistatian at the Fleet London about 25 Years ago Says he Rented a House in Kings head Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of three Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds Eight Shillings hath quitted said House about Seventeen Years & hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes Says he has One Child by his said Wife  Elizth. Aged about Twelve Years

Sworn the Day of 176 Before}
