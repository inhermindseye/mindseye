Middx  Sarah Feilder  Wido . of Young Trevor Feild who hath been dead about 20 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Young Trevor Feilder on the 23th. Day of May 1709 in the Parish Church of Alhallow's upon the Wall London Says her sd. late Husband lived in & rented a house in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £18 for the Space of Thirteen Years or thereof & there dyed and this Examinant continued to live in & rent the sd. house about Six Weeks after the Death of her said late Husband Says she hath not since she left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Decr. 20th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Sarah Feilder
