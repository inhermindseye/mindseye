Middx  Cath: Pitchford aged abt. 64 Yrs. upon Oath says She is the Widow of Wm. Pitchford who hath been Dead abt. Sixteen Yrs. says her said late Husband Rented a House in Kings head Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds lived therein abt. one Yr. & there dyed says She hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant

Sworn the 25th Feb: 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cath: Pitchford
