Middx ss.  Elizabeth Hobbs  Widow of Thomas Hobbs deced Maketh oath that her said late husband in his life time hath informed her that her said husbands Grand father Thomas John Hobbs rented a house of Ten pounds a Year in the Parish of Hitchen in the County of Hertford & paid Parish Taxes there & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And that he did not afterwards gain any subsequent settlement And also that her said husbands Father Hobbs  who is now as this Depont. believes in Hitchen Work house nor this Deponents said late husband deced nor either of them as this Deponents sd. husband also informed her this Depont. never gained any settlement but in right of their sd. Grandfather John Hobbs deced And saith that she this Deponent hath a Child Named Hannah aged about Two years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband

Sworn this 7th day of November 1757 before  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

his Elizbeth Hobbs Mark
