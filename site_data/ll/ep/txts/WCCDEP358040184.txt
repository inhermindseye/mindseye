Westmr.  Mary Miller  Widow of Richard Miller who dyed about two Months since upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Rd. Miller upwds. of 12 Years ago at the Fleet London and that she knows nothing more of his Settlement than that he was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or beleif Says she Served five Years Apprentiship wth. Mrs. Fulford a Button maker in Kings head Court and Bennets Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and never after the Expiration of her sd. Apprentiship rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year Says she has two Childn. viz Mary Ann aged upwds. of 9 Yrs. & Esther aged upwds. of 7 Years

Sworn May 10th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Miller
