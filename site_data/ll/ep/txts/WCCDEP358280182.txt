Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  Martha Howell aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she has lived as a hired yearly Servant with Sir John Sinclear at Whitehall for these Eighteen Months last past at the yearly Wages of Fourteen Guineas. And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 18th. day of Septr . last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mrs. Evans in Goldens Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Henry That one William Bull  Gardener to Lord Kinnand at Bingfield near Bracknell in Berkshire is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examts. Body sometime in the Month of December last at which time he did beget the said Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as a aforesaid and that the said William Bull is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 8th. day of Octr. 1788 } R Butler

Martha Howell

Martha Howell lives at Sir John Sinclear  Whitehall

William Bull is a Tall Man born in Scotland Pitted with the Small Pox wears dark Hair loose in his Neck
