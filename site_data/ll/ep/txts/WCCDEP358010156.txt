Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Eleanor Welch aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Donaghoe who kept the Sign of the Taylors Arms in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Twelve Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of 3 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 26th. day of Octr. 1787 R Butler

her Eleanor Welch Mark
