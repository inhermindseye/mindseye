Middx  Elizabeth Wiley  Wife of James Wiley (aged about years) Removed from St. Dunstan's in the West by Order dated the 23d. August 1762 upon Oath says that her said Husband Rented a House in Crown Court  Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds and lived in the same four Years or thereabouts and quitted the same about One Year and a half and that her said Husband hath not since Rented any other House And says She hath one Child by her said Husband named Sarah aged about seven Months

Sworn the day of August 1762 before }
