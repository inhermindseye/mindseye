Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Gibbons aged about 57 years upon her Oath saith that about 26 years ago She Intermarried with William Gibbons her present Husband at the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Field Lane in the Liberty of Saffron Hill  Hatton Garden and Ely Rent in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Fourteen Pounds That he hath quitted said house about Twenty Years since which time he hath not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1797 before us E Read  Clarke

her Sarah Gibbons Mark

Witness B Newman
