Middx to wit Jane Jones aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath says She Intermarried with Edward Jones her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint mary Le Strand about Eight Years ago and who has been d about Three Years and by whom She hath no Child or Children living says her said late Husband Served Seven Years Appren to one Mr. Edwards a Sawyer  the Corner of Nottingham Court Cross Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly servant for one Year.

Sworn this 25th. day of April 1780 before M Addington G Mercer

her Jane Jones Mark
