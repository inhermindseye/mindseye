Westmr.  Francis Palmer Aged about Thirty One Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Sarah his Wife at Great Saint Marys Cambridge about Six Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children now living Namely Francis Aged about Two Years & Mary Aged about three Months says that before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a Covenanted Servant with Peter Hurleston a Peruke maker in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds for the first Year and Ten Pounds for the Second hath quitted his said Service about seven Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same over Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any other Act where by to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of January 1774 before R Butler

Francis Palmer
