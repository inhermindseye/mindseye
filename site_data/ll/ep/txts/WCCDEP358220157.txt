Middx & Westmr.}  Hannah Gillman Aged 65 Years or thereabouts on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Saml. Gillman gone from her with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about 22 Years ago Says before her said Husbands Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with on Vere Hare who kept Sams Coffee house in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eight Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds where this Examinants also lived as a hired Yearly Servant and on her Intermarriage with her said Husband he quitted his said Service and after he quitted the same he never after Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither hath this Examinant since her said Husband left her

Sworn this 25th. June 1770 before me} R. Butler

Hannah Gillman
