Westmr.  Robert Benfield aged about 32 Years Upon Oath says he Rented a House in Prince's Street near Leicester fields in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds, hath quitted said House about five Years and a half and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year;

Sworn March 6th. 1766 Before}
