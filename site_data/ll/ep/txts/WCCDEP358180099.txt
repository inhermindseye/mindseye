Middx  Sarah Binian  Removed from the Parish of St. Mary in Guilford in the County of Surry by Order Dated the 12th. Day of Augt. Instant upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of Richard Binian who hath been dead about Two Years that her late Husband  Richd Binian was bound an Apprentice to & served 7 Yrs- Apprentiship with Mr. Thos. Clements  Butcher in Clare markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the C ounty of Middx & since his said Apprentiship neither her said late husband in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one John Daniell did begett the said Child or Children on her Body he the sd.

John Daniel having had Carnall knowledge of her Body the latter End of Febry or beginning of March last & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant & that he the sd. Jno. Daniell & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 16: Augt. 1743 before} Thos. DeVeil

her Sarah Binian Mark
