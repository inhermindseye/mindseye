Westmr. to wit.}  Ann Harris Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Thos. Hughes a Taylor in Piccadilly in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service her said Service about four Years and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th. day of Novr. 1770 before Us.} R Butler P L Perrin

her Ann Harris Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
