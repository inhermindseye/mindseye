Middx.  Mary Andrews aged abt. 40 Years upon Oath says she never was married & that upwards of twenty Years ago she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Lewis in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for four Yrs. at the Yearly Wages of fifty Shillings & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th. day of February 1758 . before}
