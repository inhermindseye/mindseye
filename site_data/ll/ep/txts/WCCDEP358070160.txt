Westmr Pridell  Ann Eames Ages about Sixteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she was bound an Apprentice by the Church Wardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry  to one Mr. Pridell a Cook in Castle Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and Served her said Master for the space of Three Months or thereabouts when her said Master absconded and this Examinant obliged to go to service Since which fine she hath not gained any subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a lived Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 4th. day of Janry 1774 R Butler Tho Kynaston

her Ann Emes  mark

Witness Charles Pass
