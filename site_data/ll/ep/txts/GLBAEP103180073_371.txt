Middlesex ss}  Ann Pack the wife of Richard Pack mariner maketh oath that her said husband has been lately pressed & is now on Board one of his Majesties Ships of war at Spithead as she this Deponent has been Credibly informed & believes That her Said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born in Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County Where his Parents formerly lived & resided & this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting the Settlement of her Said husband or his Parents or either of them & that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this. 23 day of October 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Ann Pack
