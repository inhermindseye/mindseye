Middlesex ss}  Ann Williams the widow of Robert Williams deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her and which She believes to be true that he duly Serves his apprenticeship to a Bricklayer (Whose name She has forgot) in the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & to her Knowledge or belief he did not after his Apprenticeship do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 2 day of January 1788 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Williams
