Middlesex ss}  Dorcas Williams maketh oath That She hath a niece named Hannah Davis now resident in the Workhouse of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County aged about nineteen years who was born in the house known by the Sign of the Jackle Block Alehouse in the sd Parish & is the Daughter of William Davis deced by Hannah his wife also deced The which William Davis lived in & rented the said publick house at the time of his said Daughters Birth & for Several years before and after & was charged to & paid parish Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That the said William Davis  after he left the said publick house lived in & rented a house as this Deponent has been informed in the Grange Southwark in the County of Surry but what rent or Jaseephe paid for such house this Depont Cannot set forth never having reced any Information relative there to nor can she set forth or in any way discover whether the said William Davis ever did any act to gain a Settlement after he left the said Jackle Block alehouse & that his said wife died before him & she can give no account respecting her Settlement saver her Marriage

Sworn this 9 day of June 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

Dorcas Williams
