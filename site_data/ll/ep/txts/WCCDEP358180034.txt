Middx  Sarah Elms  Wido . upon her Oath saith. That on the 22d. Day of January about Thirty one Years since she Intermarried with her late Husband  Henry Elms at Grays Inn Chappell who hath been Dead about Two Years That the said Henry Elms lived in and Rented a House in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds for the Space of Eight Years & upwards and hath left the same about Twenty Years That since the sd. Henry Elms left the said House he in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 6th- Day of Novr. 1742 before} Tho Lane .

Sarah Elms
