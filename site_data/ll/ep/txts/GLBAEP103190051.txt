Mary Malcher maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Thomas Comerford a Tobacconist in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of a year and upwards & has not Since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for a year nor was she ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since Such her Servitude as aforesd

Sworn this 12. day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Mary Malcher
