Middx  Rebecca Slight Aged about Twenty Eight Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed That she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Harris a Taylor in Cross Key Court near Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx on the 5th. Day of December 1759 which hath Since been Baptized by the Name of Rebecca Says Henry Argent One of the Virgurers of St. Pauls London did beget the said Bastard Child on her Body he having had Carnall knowledge of this Examinants Body severall times at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Female Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid.

Sworn the 12th. of January 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox

Rebekah Slight
