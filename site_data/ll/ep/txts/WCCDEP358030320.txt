Middx 

Mary Bunting aged about 20 Years upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Thomas Taylor at the Angell agt. Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for the Space of one Year & upwds. & hath left her sd. Service about four Months and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born as Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that John Bane a Taylor did be get the sd. Child or Children on her Body the sd. John Bane being acquainted with this Examinant had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of May last & at sevll. other times since at one of which time he got her with Child of the sd. Bastd. Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. John Bane & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof.

Sworn Janry 13th. 1746 before  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Mary Bunting
