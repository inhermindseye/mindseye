Middx  Ann Warren aged abt. thirty Years upon Oath says she was never married says she lived a Servant with Mr. Wade at the Chop house in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for seven years says Mrs. Wade agreed with this Examt. that if her Vails did not amount to Six Pounds a Year she would make up the deficiency & hath quitted said Service about Seven Years; says that some- time in the Month of September 1750 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which is Baptized William in the house of Mrs. Winter-in Butcher Row in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And that in the Month of July 1753. she was delivered of another Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Brotherton in Houghton Street in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes which is Baptized Joseph which said bastard Children are both living and she being poor is not able to maintain them and further saith that one Joseph Smith a Baker did beget the said Bastard Children on her Body & that the said Joseph Smith & no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn this 19th. Decr. 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Warren
