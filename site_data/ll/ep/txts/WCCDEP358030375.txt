Westmr. 

Ann Marshall upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband John Marshall at the Parish Church of St. Bennett Pauls Wharfe London upwards of Seven Years ago by whom she hath 3 Childn, (to wit) Ann aged abt. 5 Years Eliz aged upwards of 3 Years & George Frederick aged about Sixteen Months Says her sd. husband lived in & rented a house in Silver Street in the Precinct of White- -fryers London at the Yearly Rent of £20 for one Year or thereabouts and hath left the same about Six Years Says her sd. husbd. has not since he left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 18th. 1747 before  Tho. Burdus

Ann Marshall
