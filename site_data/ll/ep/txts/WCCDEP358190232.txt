Middx   John Turner aged about 20 years upon Oath says he never was Bound an Apprentice or was a hired Yearly Servant for One year Says his Father  Thomas Turner (who has been Dead about 11 Years) was a Poulterer and rented a House in Clement's Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for several years but cannot tell what Rent he paid that he quitted the Same about a Year or Two before his Death.

Sworn the 10th. day of March 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell

John Turner
