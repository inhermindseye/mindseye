Middx  Jno: Williams upon Oath saith he was married to his wife Mary Williams in the Parish Church of Saint Andrew in Holborn upwds. of 15 years ago & has by her one Child John aged upwds. of 5 yrs. and that before his sd. marriage & whilst a Singleman he lived a yearly Servant with Messr. Walkers in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for three years or thereabts. at the yearly Wages of £10 quitted his sd. Service abt. fifteen years ago & has not since rented ten pounds a year or pd. to the Poor Rate of any parish whatever

Sworn Febry 11th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

John Williams
