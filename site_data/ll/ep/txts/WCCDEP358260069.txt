St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } To Wit John Bennett aged 46 years upon his Oath Saith that he never was Married and that he this Deponent was Bound as an Apprentice under an Indenture for Seven years to Thomas Wildman  Vintner at the Bedford Head Tavern Corner of Tavistock Street in the Parish of St. Pauls Covent Garden with whom this Deponent served under that Indenture for the space of three years and a half but Mr Wildman Quitting Business this Deponent sued out for his Indentures at Guild Hall and turned himself over to Samuel Marriott Vintner  Fountain Tavern  Catharine Street in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand for the Remaining Part of this Deponents Indenture under an an Agreement to Receive for the first year Fifteen Pounds, for the Second year Twenty Pounds, with whom this Deponent lived for the space of One year and upwards at the yearly Wages above Mentioned and that after he Quitted said Samuel Marriott he never lived as a Hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or ever Paid Taxes

Sworn this 19th. day of August 1795 N Bond Cha : Churchill

his John Bennett mark

Witness Present B Newman
