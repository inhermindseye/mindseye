Middx.  Elizabeth Evans aged abt. 23 years Removed from the Parish of St. Mary le Bon in the County of Middx to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the same County by Order under the hands and seals of two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace Upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & Unable to Maintain the same when Born says that One Francis Costa als Coster a Taylor had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her Mistress's house in Gloucester Street by Queens Square in the Month of September 1759 and at several other times since at One of which times he get her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said Francis Coster als Coster and no other Person is the true & real father thereof. says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Scott a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes for One year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & hath Quitted said Service Upwards of One year And has not since Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for One year

Sworn 21st. June 1760 before} Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Evans
