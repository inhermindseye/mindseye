Middx  George Coombs with Sarah his Wife & Hannah their Child aged about 14 Weeks removed from the Parish of Agmondesham otherwise Amersham in the County of Bucks by Order dated the 9th. Day of May Instant upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his sd. Wife Sarah on or abt. the 29th. Day of Decr. 1742 at the Fleet London Says he was born in the sd. Parish of Agmondesham otherwise Amersham where this Examinants Father  John Coombs Resided by Vertue of a Certificate under the hands & Seales of the Church wardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St Clement Danes  Middx bearing Date the 6th. Day of April 1716 & Subscribed & Allowed by two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx aforesd. Says his Father dyed abt. 12 Years since & hath not since the Granting the sd. Certificate Rented ten pounds a Year Served any Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place as he hath been Informed beleives Says that he this Examint. never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish

Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 15 1745 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Geo. Coombes
