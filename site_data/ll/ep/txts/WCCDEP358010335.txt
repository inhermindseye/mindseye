St. Clement Danes. April 17 1790.

Ann Melvin aged 64 or thereof upon her Oath saith That &  Elizabeth Melvin aged 35 years or thereabouts Severally make Oath and Say, and first this Examinant ann Melvin upon her Oath faith, That she Intermarried with John Melvin her late husband at the Fleet about 38 years ago-That her said Husband was a scotchman & never gained any settlement in England, that after their Intermarriage her said Husband & this Examinant went to live in   Carteret street in the Parish of saint Margaret Westminster in the County of middlesex where her son Reginald Melvin was born, That her said son never served an apprenticeship, lived as an field yearly servant, Rented £10 a year, Served any Parish Office or ever did any other Act Matter or thing where to the best of this Examinant knowlodge information or belief whereby here said son Could have gained any settlement otherwise than by birth and this Examinant Elizabeth Melvin upon nor Oath saith That she intermarried with the said Reginald Melvin  at the Parish Church of said Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about 7 years ago That she hath 3 Childrens

living by her said Husband namely John aged 4 years or thereabouts, Elizabeth aged 3 years or thereabouts & Reginald aged 14 months or thereabouts, that her said Husband died abt. 3 Weeks ago That her said Husband never did to this Examinants knowledge Information or belief gain any Settlemt. are than as aforesd.

Sworn this 17th. day of april 1790 before W. Mrleamire J Barnfather

Ann Melvein her Elizabeth Melvin Mark
