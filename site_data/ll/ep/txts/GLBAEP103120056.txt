Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Spittle of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County  singlewoman taken on oath before me Burford Camper  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this 5th day of August 1773 .

Who on her oath Saith That she is now with Child and that the Said Child is likely to be a born a Bastard and to be Chargeable of the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that Charles Omer of the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechappel in the Said County  Carman is the Father of the Said Child.

Sworn this 5th: day of August 1773 before B Camper

The Mark of Elizabeth Spittle
