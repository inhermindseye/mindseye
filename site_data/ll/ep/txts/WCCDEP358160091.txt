Middx  The Examination of Mary Westwood  Singlewoman Passed from the Parish of St. James in ye. Liberty of Westmr . to ye. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx the taken the 14. Day of Novr. 1739 Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children wch when Born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to ye. sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes She this Examint. being Poor & not able to maintaine the same Wm. Born And further upon her Oath saith that One John Young did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body she this Examint. at such time liveing as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Harper in Clare Markett in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes and he the sd. Jno. Young at the sd. time being Journeyman to the said Mr. Harper & liveing in the House there became acquainted with her and had Carnall Knowledge of her Body Sevll. times in the sd. House att one of wch times he got her with the Bastard Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant & that he and no other Person is the true & real Father thereof

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Westwood
