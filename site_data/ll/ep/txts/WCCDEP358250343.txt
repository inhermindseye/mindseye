Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Cager aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 17th. day of January 1782 she Intermarrd. with Thomas Cager her late Husband who has been dead about 18 Months at the Parish Church of Saint John Southwark by whom She hath Six Children living Vizt. John aged about Twelve Years Elizabeth aged about Ten Years Ann aged about Seven years Thomas aged about Five years Harriet aged about four years and Sarah aged about Two years That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Twenty eight pounds and paid all Taxes due for the same  that after her said Husband died in the said house.

Sworn this 23d. day of Janry 1798 before me} J Hunter

her Sarah Cager Mark

Witness B Newman
