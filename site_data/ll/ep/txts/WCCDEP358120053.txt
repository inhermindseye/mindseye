Middx  Mary Gamidge upon Oath saith she is the wife of Saml. Gamidge with whom she intermarried on the 30th. of Augt. 1747 at the Fleet London and that her Sd husband lived a yearly Servant for two Years or thereabts. before his sd. intermarriage with Mr Shuckburgh a Book seller in Fleet Street in the parish of St. Dunstan in the West London & quitted his sd. Service abt. two Years ago; since which he has not rented ten pounds a year or paid parish taxes

Sworn Febry 8th. 1754 . before} Tho Lane

Mary Gamidge
