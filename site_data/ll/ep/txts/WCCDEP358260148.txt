Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Mary Norris aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 24th. day of November 1788 she Intermarried with Richard Norris her late Husband (who has been dead about Six years) at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan at Stepney by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Mary aged about Eight years. That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Gilberts Place in the Hamlet of Mile End New Town in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Five Pounds and paid the Watch and Poor Rates for the same and that after her said late Husband quitted same he never did any Act whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement nor hath this Examinant since her said late Husbands death done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any Settlement

Sworn this 8th- day of Septr. 1796 } John Nares . E Read

Mary Norris
