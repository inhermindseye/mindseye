Middlesex to wit Jane Coltsfoot Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father Summers Coltsfoot Rented and lived in a House in Deptford Road in the Parish of Saint Nicholas Deptford in the County of Kent known by the Name or Sign of the Green Dragon quitted same about Twenty five Years ago and after quitting that House he Rented another House for several years in the aforesaid Parish of Nicholas in Deptford of the Yearly Rent of £7 and paid all parochial Taxes due for the same and where this Examinants Father died which is about fifteen Years ago and further Says that this Examinant hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement since her said Fathers Death.

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1779 before  Jno. Machin  John Croft

her Jane Coltsfoot Mark
