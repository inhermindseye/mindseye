Middx 

Ann Parsons upon Oath saith she never was married Says she lived a Yearly Servt . with Captn. George Jenkins in Arundel Street . in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £3..10..0 for one Year & upwds. & left her sd. Service about twenty five Years ago Says she hath not since she left the sd. Captn. Jenkins Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 10th. 1747 before}
