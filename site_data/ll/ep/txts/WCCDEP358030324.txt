Westmr. 

Wm. Littleford upon Oath saith he inter- -married with his Wife  Susanna Littleford about five Years ago at the fleet London Say she lived in & rented a house in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £14 for the Space of Six Years & upwds. Says after he left sd. house he rented a house in Yeates Court in St. Clemt. Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & lived therein 4 yrs. & upwds. & hath left same upwds. of Seven Years Says he hath not since he quitted sd. house in Yeates Court rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 17th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Wm. Littleford .
