Middx  Sarah Smaley aged about 60 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of John Smaley who has been Dead about Nine or Ten years That her said Husband was Born in Lancashire but in what part this Examinant cannot say, That he never kept House was an Apprentice or hired yearly Servant to her Knowledge and that she can't give any other Account of his Settlement That she this Examinant before her Intermarriage lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Beaumont in Piccadilly in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for One year at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds.

Sworn the day of May 1761 Before}
