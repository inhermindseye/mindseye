Middx.  Eliz Jackson upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Richard Jackson (gone from her) who informed her he served his Apprentiship with a Butcher at Scarborough in Yorkshire and that her sd. husband never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year to her knowledge or belief

Sworn augt. 23d. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Jackson
