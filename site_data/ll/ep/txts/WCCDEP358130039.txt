Middx  Mary Fletcher Wife of Thomas Fletcher living in Bishops Court in the little Old Bailey London on behalf of Elizabeth Cavanaugh an Infant Aged about five Months upon Oath says She is the Daughter of Andrew Cavanaugh by Elizabeth his Wife Deceased That the said Andrew Cavenaugh was by Indenture dated the 3d. Day of Decr. 1744 bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to James Fower in Turnagain Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchres London this Examinants first Husband and Served him as an Apprentice for two Years or thereabouts & then run away That the said Andrew Cavanaugh never was assigned over to any Person for the remainder of his said Apprenticeship nor ever rented Ten Pounds a year was a yearly Servant or paid Parish Taxes to her knowledge or beliefe

Sworn the 14th. of Augt. 1755 before} Barth. Hammond  Saunder Welch

The Mark of Mary Fletcher
