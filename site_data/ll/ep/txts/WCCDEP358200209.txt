Westmr  Thomas Williams Aged about Forty Nine Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Sarah in the Parish Church of St Peter in the City of Worcester hath three Children (to witt) Martha Aged about Twenty two Years Sarah Aged about Nineteen Years & Susannah Aged about thirteen Years Says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servant Says his Father Geo: Williams was Bound an Apprentice to a Tobacconist in St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster with whom he Lived near twelve Months & then went into the Army as this Examinant hath been Informed & which Information he Verily Beleives to be true

Sworn the 4th of January 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Thos. Williams
