Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Thornton aged about 84 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with one George Thornton (who is now dead) many years ago in the County but where she this Deponent cannot now Recollect nor where her said late Husbands Settlement is She this Deponent doth not know That previous to her Intermarriage with her said late Husband She this Deponent lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. White in Lincolns Inn fields in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Three years at the yearly wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Fifty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of April 1799 before us} Menter G S Goodnough

her Elizabeth Thornton Mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
