Middx & Westr} to wit Margaret Mitchell Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Wilton in Garey Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Baker, upwards of fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, Quitted her said service the thirtieth of march last and hath not since she Quitted the same ever done any other Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to Become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes (she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born) and that one William Bishop a Journeyman Baker to her late said Master in the Father there of he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime the Latter end of November or the beginning of December last but the particular day she does not now remember, and at several other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid and that the said William Bishop and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 19th day of Aust 1775 before M Addington

her Margaret Mitchell mark Witness James Viger
