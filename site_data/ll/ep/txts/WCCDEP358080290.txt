Middx to wit John Memory Aged about Thirty Two years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Saml. Lloyd  Esqr . in Devonshire Square in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishopgate London in the Capacity of a Coachman for Six years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Ten Pounds besides Diet Lodging and Cloaths hath quitted his said Service about Nine years ago since which time he hath gained no other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 1st. day of Octr. 1778 before R Butler

his John Memory Mark
