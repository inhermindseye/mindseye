Middx  Mary Phillips aged near 80 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Sr  John Phillips at Kilgetty in the Parish of St Tessels in the County of Pembroke for two Years at the Yearly Wages of £3 and has left her sd Service near 10 Yrs. since which she has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her Knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was taken in Labour Wandering in the Street in the mean Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx in the morning of the 9th. of Decr. last and being brot. to one of the Parish Nurses of the sd. Parish was there the same morning delivered of a female bastard Child which has been baptized Mary and this Examinant further maketh Oath that at the time of her being taken in Labour as aforesd. she was intirely destitute of money & Lodging

Sworn Jany 10th. 1752 before}

The Mark of Mary Phillips
