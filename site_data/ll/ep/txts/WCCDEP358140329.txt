Middx.  Sarah Rivets aged abt. 24 years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Benjn. Rivets with whom she intermarried at St. Giles's Church  on the 16th. day of April 1759. says her said husband informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for one Year And that he learnt the trade of a Glazier with his father Edwd. Rivets who Rented a house in Church Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for sevl. Years.

Sworn this 7th. day of Decr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Rivets

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Wilkinson Ovr. J.. P..
