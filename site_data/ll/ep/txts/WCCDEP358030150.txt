Eliz Deale  Wife of Rd. Deale (gone from her) upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Rd. Deale on the 4th. Day of March 1733 at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband Served 2 Yrs. & upwards the first part of his Apprentisp with Mr. Legg a Bricklayer in Fleur de lis Court in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the west London & then his Master gave him the remainder of his time & this Examinants sd. Husband never was Assigned over to any other person lived a Yearly Servt. rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 21 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Eliz Deale
