Middlesex ss}  Catherine Williams the wife of John Williams maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her as a Soldier in his Majesties service in America That her said husband was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Williams a Shoemaker in the  Parish of St Margaret in the City of Hereford in the County of Hereford and has not since to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement & that she was lawfully Married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 18. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Catherine Williams
