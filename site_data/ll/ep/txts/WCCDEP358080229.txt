Middx & Westmr.}  Margaret Wilson Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Covin a Shoemaker in the West Bow in the City of Edinburgh North Britain for fourteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about five Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish (she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born) And that one Richard Teale who keeps t the Swan in Vere Street Clare Markett is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Months of July or August last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at Several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Richard Teale and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 23d day of March 1778 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Margt. Wilson Mark
