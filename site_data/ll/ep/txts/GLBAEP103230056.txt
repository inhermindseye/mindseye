Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Mc Donald the Widow of Patrick Mc Donald deced maketh oath that her Said late husband heretofore rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds a year & upwards in Long Lane in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great in the City of London and was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the same and did not afterwards rent a house lodging or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds for serve any Parish office or Pay Any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain A Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief nor hath she this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself And that she was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn this 25 day of June 1787 before R Davies Rob Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Mc Donald
