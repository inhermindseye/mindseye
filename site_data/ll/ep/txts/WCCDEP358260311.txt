Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charlotte Geale aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Field at Guildford  in the Parish of Holy Trinity at Guildford in the County of Surry for Six years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Five Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about 15 years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Augt. 1798 before us  Saml. Edward W Carter

Charlotte Geale
