Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Stevens aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that on the 15th. day of December last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child (which hath since been baptized by the Name of John ) in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Nathl. Whitehead a Gentlemans Servant who lately lived with Mr. Dawson a Woollen Draper in Wych Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of March last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Nathl. Whitehead is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 4th. day of Janry. 1787 } R Butler

her Elizabeth Stevens mark

Child born on the 15th. Decr. 1786 -sent down to Enfield the 12th. Janry 1787
