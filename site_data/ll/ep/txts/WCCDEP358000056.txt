Saint Clement Danes.

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swear's to St. C. D. to wit. Hannah Arslett aged 52 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she intermarried with Charles Arslett her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Bride's alias St. Bridget Fleet Street in the City of London about Twenty three years ago that before their Intermarriage her said Husband lived as an Hired yearly servant with Mr. John Nicholson in Arundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of twelve Months that her said Husband is now insans and in Saint Luke's Hospital that since her said Husbands quitting the service of Mr Nicholson her said Husband has not done any act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of June 1785 R Butler

her Hannah Arslett Mark
