Middx.  Mary Ellitt aged abt. 62 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Robt. Ellitt who hath been dead abt. Ten Yrs. & that the said Robt. Ellitt whilst a single Man lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Warwick at the Cart & Horses at Essex Stairs in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds Dyet Washing & Lodging & that her said late Husband in his life time or She this Examinant since his decease never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 12th. day of March 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Ellit
