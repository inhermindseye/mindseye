Middlesex ss  William Parce maketh oath That divers years ago he was bound Apprentice to his Father John Pace of the Parish of St John in the City of Gloncester  Pinmaker & Served about five years of such his Apprenticeship there & then this Deponent removed with his Father to the Parish of Saint George Southwarke in the County of Surry where he Served the residue of his sd Apprenticeship & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26 day of Septemr. 1777 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of William Pace
