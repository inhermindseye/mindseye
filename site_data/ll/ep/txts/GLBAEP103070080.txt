Middx ss  Thomas Allcott Maketh oath that about Sixteen Years ago he rented a house in the Parish of Allhallows the Great in the City of London and paid Sixteen Pounds a Year for the Same and likewise paid all Parochial Taxes for the Same in the sd. Parish and also to the Repairs of the Same Parish Church and by Means thereof gained a legal Settelemt in the sd. Parish as this Depont. is advised and believes And this Depont. Saith that he hath not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards nor been a hired Servant by the Year nor Served any Parish offices nor to his knowledge informacon or belief he hath done any other Act whereby to gain any other Settlement And that he hath a wife Name Ringrose and two Children to wit Rose Aged about eight Years and James aged about four Years the lawfull issue of this Dept. & his sd. Wife

Sworn this 17 day of April 1753 before us Richd: Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

by Mr. Thomas Allcott
