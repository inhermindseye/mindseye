St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Charlotte Hornsby Aged twenty three years or thereabouts Wife of William Hornsby on behalf of her Sister Camilla Henwick otherwise Howard now Insane and incapable of Answering with certainty for herself upon Oath saith That the said Camilla Henwick otherwise Howard was bound by Indentures of Apprenticeship by (with her Father's Consent) to Misses Jane and Ann Priestly in Coney Street in the parish of Saint Mary in Coney Street in the City of York for five years, of with whom under such Indentures she duly served about one year and an half and during that time slept and lodged in her Mistres Misstresses House that she them at the expiration of that time left her said Mistresses, being Ill and unable to complete her said Apprenticeship And hath never since done any Act Matter or thing Whereby she might acquire any other legal settlement, And this Informant further says that she hath heard believes that she the said Camilla was Married or United to me Howard (long since deceased) some where in France according to the Ceremony of the Church of Rome As to all which above Facts relating to the said Settlement of the said Camilla her Sister she this Deponent only speaks from information which she verily believes to be true And this Deponent further Saith that her said Sister Camilla is Insane unfit to be permitted to go abroad by herself and incapable of supporting herself

Sworn the 23d day of July 1791 before us  Sampr Wright 

Charlotte Hornsby
