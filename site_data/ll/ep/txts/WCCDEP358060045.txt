Middx  Martha Miller  Wife of Thomas Miller upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Thomas Miller at St. Brides Church in Dublin about five Years since and that her sd. husband absconds from her and she knows not where he is and can give no further Account of his Settlemt. than that she has often heard him say he was born in St. Clements Parish in Westmr .

Sworn Janry 4th. 1750 before}
