Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Jones aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that about the Month of July 1793 she Intermarried with John Jones (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Aldgate by whom she hath one Child living namely Mary aged about One Month That her said Husband informed this Examinant that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Elden a Schoolmaster at Hounslow in the Parish of Isleworth in the County of Middlesex for about Three years but what wages he had she this Examinant doth not know That he quitted said Service about Five years since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of July 1795 E Read M Addington

her Sarah Jones mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
