Middx  Mary Ranger Aged about Twenty Three Years On her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bathgate a Farrier in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of two Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says She is Pregnant with Child or Children which is on are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Changeable to the said Parish She being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same when bound And that One James Mayburn a Cutler who lodges at the Red Lyon in Saints Martins Court St Martins Lane is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of August last but the particular Day She does not now Remember and sevl. different times after at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which She in now Pregnant And that the said James Mayburn  And no other Person did beget the same and that  is the Real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry 1780 before  Abrm Baley

her Mary Ranger Mark
