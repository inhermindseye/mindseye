Middlesex ss  Ann Friend the wife of Lawrence Friend maketh oath that her Sd husband is now absent from her in France That her Said husband lately lived in and rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in Dean Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County for about the Space of nine Months That her Sd husband has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served Any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And that she was lawfully married to her said husband.

Sworn this. 6. day of July 1786 before R Davies

Ann Friend
