Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Peter Sidebotham  Clerk to Mr. Jennings of Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Gentleman on behalf of Two Children of Robt. Jenks who has been dead upwds. of a fortnight upon his Oath saith that the said late Robt. Jenks informed this Dept which Information he this Dept. believes to be true that he was about 50 years of Age that he had been married upwards of 16 years to Sarah his late Wife at the Parish Church of Walsal in Stafford shire that he had 2 Children Livy. by his said late wife Vizt. Ann aged about 15 years & Chas. aged about 8 years that he the said late Robt. Jenks in his Life time served his time as an Apprentice under Indres to one Joseph Griffiths a Buckle Chafe Maker at Walsal in the Stafford shire in the Parish of Walsal in the County of Stafford that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never had done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of March 1787 before  Philip Dyot D Walker

P. Sidebotham
