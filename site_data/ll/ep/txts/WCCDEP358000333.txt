Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Peter Dagworthy aged about 42 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Rose Dagworthy his Present wife at the Parish of St. Mary Newington That he lived with Mr. Everett at the London Apprentice Hoxton in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch for 3 years at the yearly Wages of Ten Pounds

Sworn this 27th- day of July 1786  Jno Barnfather  James Croft

his Peter Dagworthy Mark
