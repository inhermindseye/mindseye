Middx to wit Mary Buzvargus Aged about Fifty four Years on her Oath says She never was Married says She Rented and lived in a House in Off Alley York Buildings in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster Upwards of Two Years at the yearly Rent of Twenty Eight Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted her said House Upwards of Eleven Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 19th. day of July 1780 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Mary Buzvargus
