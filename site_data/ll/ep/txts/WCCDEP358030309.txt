Westmr. 

Hannah Griffith  Wido . of Samuel Griffith who hath been dead abt. 7 years upon Oath saith she Intermarried with thesd. Samuel Griffith at the Parish Church of Kinfare in the County of Stafford upwds. of 41 Years ago Says her sd. husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship in the sd. Parish of Kinfare in the County of Stafford aforesd. & lived in & rented a house in the same Parish of the Yearly Rent of 30s. for 30 Yrs. & upwds. & pd. his Shares of all Parochial Taxes Says her sd. late husband after he quitted sd. house nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 6th. 1746 before  Thos Lane

The Mark of  Hanh Griffith .
