Middx  Pleasant Burt  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Benjn: Moffett in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds for the Space of one Year & upwards & hath not since she left the said Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she Pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born and that one George Leeke  Hackney coachman did Begett the said Child or Children on her Body she being at a Publick house over against St. Giles's Pound on the 28th. Day of October last with the said George Leeke where he the sd. George Leeke had

Carnall knowledge of her Body severall times at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that he the sd. George Leeke and no other Person is the true & Reall Father thereof

Sworne the 5th. July 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Pleasant Burt
