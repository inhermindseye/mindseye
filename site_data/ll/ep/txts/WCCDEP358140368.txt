Middx.  Eliz: Ireland aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that on the 20th. day of March 1758, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Appartment of Mr. Hays a Silk dyer in a house in Worster Place in the Parish of St. James Garlick Hill in the City of London which hath since been Baptized Saml.

Sworn this 3d. of May 1760 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz Ireland
