Middx.  Mary Mc. Daniel aged abt. 29 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Stephen Mc. daniel who dyed on the 1st. day of February last says her said late husband was a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or belief says her late father James Bollington deceased Rented a house in Dyot Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex  in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds for two Years & upwds. & never after (he quitted that house) Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says she never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant & that she hath one Child by her said late husband (to wit) Ann aged abt. three Years

Sworn this 13th. day of Decr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Mc Daniel
