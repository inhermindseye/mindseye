Westmr. to wit Sarah Tunnicliff Aged about Sixty years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Joseph Tunnicliff who has been dead about five years and by whom she hath no Children now living Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired yearly Servant with one William Raven as Beadle to the Stationers Company in Stationers Court Ludgate Hill in the Parish of Saint Martin Ludgate in the City of London for one year all but Two or Three Days which her said Mistress gave her but received but received her whole years Wages hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two years since which time she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 24th. day of January 1776 before R Butler

J Tunnicliff
