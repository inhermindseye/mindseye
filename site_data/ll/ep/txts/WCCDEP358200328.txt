Westmr.  Sarah Major aged abt. 27 Years upon Oath says she was never married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Cooper in Bartlett's Court in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the City of London for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service about five Years and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says she has two Children Mary Ann aged about three Years born in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and William aged about five Months born in the Precinct of Whitefriars  London

Sworn 29th. Novr. 1765 before} Thos: Balack  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Sarah Major
