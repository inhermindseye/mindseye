Middx  Edward Gray on Behalfe of his Mother Mary Gray  Widow aged about 70 Years upon his Oath saith that his Father  Joseph Gray was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Brown Plasterer near Saint Pauls Church in the Parish of St. Gregory by Saint Paul London that since his said Apprentisp his said Father in his Life never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant nor hath his Mother since the Death of her said Husband rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleife

Sworne 26th. March 1743 before  Thos D Neil J Poulson

Edward Gray
