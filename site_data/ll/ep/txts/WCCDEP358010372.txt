Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Margaret Walker aged 76 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath Saith That about 48 years ago she intermarried with John Kakewit who has been Dead about 33 years That about 2 years after the said John Kakewit s Death she intermarried with Thomas Walker who has been Dead about 25 years That the said John Kakewit her first Husband informed this Examinant that he served his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Mills a Buckle Maker in White cross street in the Parish of Saint Lukes Old street in the County of Middlesex , And this Examinant further upon her Oath Saith That the said Thomas Walker her second Husband never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of 10 pounds a year or did any Act whereby he might have gained any Sort of Settlement And that the said John Kakewit never gained any other Subsequent Settlement but by serving his Apprenticeship with the said Mr. Mills in white cross street in the parish and County aforesaid To the best of this Examinants Knowledge information or belief

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. before one Edw. Read  Tho. Gordon

her Margaret Walker Mark
