Middx  Mary Hulston wife of Robt Hulston (gone from her) upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the sd. Robt Hulston in St Hessels Chapel in Worcester upwds. of 4 Yrs. ago and has by him one Child Robert aged upwds. of 3 Yrs. that her sd. husband rented the Swan in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Middx at the Yearly Rent of £26 & lived therein about two months and paid a Quarters Rent for the sd house and that quitted it in 1749 and has not Since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement elsewhere to this Examinants knowledge

Sworn June 17th. 1752 before}
