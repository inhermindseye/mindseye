Middx  Elizth. Bennett aged about Fifty two years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband  John Bennett (gone from her) at the Parish Church of Hallstone in the Parish of Hellstone in the County of Cornwall in the year 1763 and by whom she hath four Children all of whom are now Provided for for anything as she believes Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in the aforesaid Parish of Hallstone of £20. & upwards paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House upwards of two years ago since which time her said Husband never gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Augt. 1783  Sampn Wright

Elizabeth Bennett
