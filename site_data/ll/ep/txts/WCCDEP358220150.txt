Westmr. to wit John Dresch Aged about Twenty Eight Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Alice his now Wife at the Parish Church of Brighthelm stone in the County of Sussex in the Year 1767 Says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Robert Smith a Merchant in Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for fourteen Months or thereabts. at the Yearly Wages of fourteen Pounds hath quitted his said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 4th. day of June 1770 before me}

John Dresch
