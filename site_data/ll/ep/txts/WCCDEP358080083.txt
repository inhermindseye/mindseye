Middlesex & Westminster}  Thomas Eaton Aged about Twenty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Hannah his Wife at St Sepulchre London upwards of one Year and an half ago and by whom he hath one Child now living namely Sarah Aged about one Year Says before his said Intermarriage with his said Wife And whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Drakeford at the Sign of the White Horse in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted his said Service upwards of five Years ago since which time he never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1776 before J Hubbard

Thos. Eaton
