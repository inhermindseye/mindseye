Middx  Ann Vaughan aged about 25 Years Lodging at Mr. Edwards in Peter Street near Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edwards in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx for One Year and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds Quitted the Same about Two Years ago and hath not since rented any House paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes nor been a hired Yearly Servant for One whole year

Sworn the 17th. day of Septr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Vaughan
