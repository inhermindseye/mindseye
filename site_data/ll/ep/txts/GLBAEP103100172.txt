Middlesex ss  Sarah Bonham  Singlewoman Maketh oath That she hath four Children to wit Elizabeth Thompson aged abt. Ten Years and a Quarter Sarah Thompson Aged about Seven Years & Samuel Thompson Aged about two years and eight Months born of her Body in Swan Alley in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and Baptized and Registred there and Catherine Thompson aged about twelve Months born of her Body in Coopers Rents in the Precinct of St. Catherine in the County of Middlesex and Baptized in the Parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate aforesd. all of which Said Children are Bastards this Depont. never having been Married And this Depont. Saith that one William Thompson late of the Precinct of St. Catherine in the County of Middlesex Carman deced did get her with Child of the Said four Bastard Children And this Depont. farther Saith that She Cannot setforth or Discover the place of Settlement of her late Parents deced or either of them But says that She has been informed by her late Mother deced that She this Depont. was born in the Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex & there Baptized That She never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound apprentice nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 28th day of November 1769 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Sarah Bonham
