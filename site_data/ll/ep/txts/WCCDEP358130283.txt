Middx.  Thos. Mc. donnell aged abt. 67 Yrs. upon Oath says that abt. Four Years ago he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Mc. mahone on Saffron hill in the Liberty of Saffron hill  Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the County of Middlesex for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Nine Pounds diet washing & Lodging he being at the that time a Single Man  & hath not since he quitted his said Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the 1st day of March 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Thos. Mc. donnell
