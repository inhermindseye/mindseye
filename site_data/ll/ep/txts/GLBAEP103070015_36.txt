Middx ss  Ann Hollings Holland  Widow of John Hollings Holland Deced Maketh oath that her sd. husband has often informed her that he was bound apprentice to his Brother William Holland of ye Parish of St. Mary Abchurch London  Plaisterer and that he duly Served his sd. apprenticeship there and by Means thereof acquired a legal Settlemt. And Saith that her sd husband Did not to the

knowledge or belief of her this Depont. gain any Subsequent Settlement by Means whereof this Depont. legal Settlemt. in right of her sd husband is in ye sd parish of St Mary Abchurch

Sworn this 26th. day of Novr. 1750 before us  Richd Ricards  Saml Gower

The Mark of Ann Holland
