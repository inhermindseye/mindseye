St. Clement Danes

Not Sworn  Ann Loggon aged 28 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with Henry Loggon her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint George in the East in the County of Middlesex upwards of Six Years ago That she has 3 Children living by her said Vizt. Ann aged 6 years or thereabouts Henry aged 3 years or thereabouts and James aged 2 years or thereabouts That she never he aid where her said Husband ever served his Apprenticeship or ever gained a Settlemt. in his own Right but has he aid he was at born at Sunderland , That before their Intermarriage this Examinant lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Givains in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for upwards of 4 years at the Yearly Wages of Wages £5 that her said Husband has  deserted  her and that she is Poor & unable  herself and Children

Sworn this 5th. day of Novr. 1785 before me}
