St. Clement Danes 16th. Decr. 1791

Middlesex & Westminster }  (St CD) Admitted in the Alnn he placed there by Mr. Nurse with Consent Vestry  (to wit) Sarah Lee Widow of Leonard Lee Deceased Aged 54 Years or thereabouts, Upon her Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Leonard Lee at the parish Church of St. Nicholas Atwade in the Isle of Thanet and County of Kent on the 20th day of June in the year of our Lord 1764, that about a Quarter of a year after their said Marriage, her said Husband took an House in Butcher Row in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster and Rented & Resided therein about two years, at the Annual Rent of about £28, that they then Removed to an Apartmt. at the House next the Crown & Anchor in the Strand in the said parish of St Clement Danes , which he Rented & Resided in for about two years at the Annual Rent of £24, that when they quitted that Apartment they removed to an Apartment at No:7 Wych Street in the same parish of St Clement Danes which her said Husband Rented and Resided in for about 2 or three Years at the Annual Rent of £30 and then Rented & Resided in a Smaller Apartment in the same House at the Yearly Rent of £16 till his Decease, which happened on or about the Eighth of August 1786 And this Deponent saith that ever since her said Husband's Decease she has

Continued therein & Rented the same on her own Account at the said Yearly Sum of £16

Sworn the 16th. day of December 1791 before Me} R Butler

Sarah Lee
