Middlesex ss  Simon Nathan maketh oath that he was hereto hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages by & with Mr Mayo a Cabinet maker in Marshall Street in the Parish of Saint James Westmr in the Said County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of Nine Years that he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a Year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a Settlement Since Such his Servitude as aforesd.

Sworn this 3d day of Febry 1786 before}

The Mark of Simon Nathan
