Middlesex ss}  Mary Stevens the widow of Peter Stevens deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her & Which She believes to be true That he duly Served the last three years of Apprentice ship with one Mr Drew a Gunsmith in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that to this Deponts Knowledge or belief her Said husband did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain another settlement Nor hath She this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself & that she hath a Child named Peter Stevens aged about nine years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this of 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Stevens
