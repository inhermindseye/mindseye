Middx.  Elizabeth Pryor , aged abt. 52 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of James Pryor who hath dead abt. one Year & a half says her said late husband before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman -lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Nixon at the Dolphin in the Strand for two Years & upwards & never after he quitted that Service Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says she hath not since his death Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Parish or place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 7th. Janry. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Pryor
