Middlesex ss  Robert Pattison a singleman Maketh oath That divers Years Ago he lived as a hired Servant by the Year at the yearly wages of Seven Pounds with one John Banister of the Parish of Saint Mary at Islington in the County of Middx  Farmer and Victualler and Continued in such service for above the Space of a year And by Means thereof as he is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont Saith that he hath not since lived as as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a year nor served any Parish offices nor Paid any Poors rates or kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement to his said servitude.

Sworn this 26th day of October 1765 before us  Chris: Scott R Pell

Robert Pattison His Mark
