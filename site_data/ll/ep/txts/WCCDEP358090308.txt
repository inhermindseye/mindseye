Middlesex to Wit Mary Wills Aged about 27 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Sharp a Butcher at her Appartmts. in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for four Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said service about four Years ago since which time she never gained any Other Settlement by living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year and further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when Born and that one Robt. Moss a Journeyman Taylor who now Works at Mr. Holmes a Taylor in Lothbury and who uses the Bible in Shire Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of December last but the particular day she does not now remember and at Sevl. Different times afterwards at One of Which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of Which she is now pregnant and that the said Robt. Moss and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn the 24th. day of April 1782 before  Sampn Wright

her Mary Wills Mark
