Middlesex ss}  Jane Bell the wife of John Bell maketh oath That her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majesties Ship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believer to be true that he Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Jones a Shoemaker in the Parish of St George in the City of Bristol & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that She was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn this. 7. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Jane Bell
