Middlesex to wit Mary Peake Aged about Twenty Three Gears on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Holden on Apothecary in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One whole Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds Twelve Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement and further on her Voluntary Oath Says that on the Eighteenth day of June last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which hath since been baptized by the name of Charles and is become Chargable to the said Parish (she being poor and unable to maintain the same) And further says that one Charles Roberts a Journey Appothecary to Mr. Holden in the Strand is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the Month of September last in her said Masters House in the Strand but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Charles Roberts and no other person did begett the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 30th. day of July 1784 - before-  Wm Kitchiner

Marey Peake
