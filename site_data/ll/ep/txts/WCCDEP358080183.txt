Middx & Westmr-}  Margt- Andrew aged about 25 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Wm- Bannister at the Rose & Crown in Clare Court in the Psh of St- Clemt- Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - upwds- of twelve Months at the Yearly Wages of £4 hath quitted her said Service upwds- of four Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And furr- says that on the 21st- Day of Octr- last she was Delivd of a Female Bastard Child in the Work house belongg- to the sd- Parish which has since been baptized by the Name of Ann and is now Chargeable to the sd- Parish Says that one Jas. Thompson a Journeyman Cabinet Maker who works with one Mr- Chippendale a Cabinet Maker in St- Martins Lane is the Father thereof He having had Carnal Knowl of her Body sometime in the latter end of Decr- 1776 or Janry last past but the Particular Day she does not now rember & at sevl- difft- times afterwds- at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children Female Bastard Child of which she was so Delivd- as afsd- & that the said Jas- Thompson & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 19th- Novr- 1777  Wm Kitchiner

her Margt- Andrew Mark
