Middlesex ss}  Christian Susanna Fliggard aged Seventeen years or there abouts maketh oath that about Three years ago She was bound apprentice by the Parish officers of the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middlesex to John Orchard of the Parish of Dulwick in the County of Surry  Horse Jockey and Served two years of Such her Apprenticeship in the said Parish and then by reason of illusage from her said Master and Mistress She left them and had not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th day of July 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Christian Susanna Fliggard
