Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Sangster aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of August 1791 she Intermarried with James Sangster her present husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Mary at Reading by whom she hath Three Children living Vizt. William aged about five years Martha aged about Three years and Henry aged about Six Months That her said Husband informed this Examinant that he was bound an Apprentice to one Mrs.. Partridge at the Castle at Salthill in the Parish of Farnham Royal in the County of Bucks for Seven years that his said Mistress failed after he had served about Two years of his said Apprenticeship that then he was Turned over to a Mr. March who kept the Sign of the Windmill at the same place Salthill aforesaid in the Parish & County aforesaid where he served the Remainder of his Apprenticeship and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of June 1798 before us} N Bond Henry Wilstead

her Sarah Sangster mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
