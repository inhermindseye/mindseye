Middlesex ss}  Mary Watson  Single woman maketh oath That she was born in the parish of St James in the Town of Lynn in the County of Norfolk where her Late parents were legally settled at the time of their respective deaths That She this Deponent never was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act. to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 1 day of May 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Watson
