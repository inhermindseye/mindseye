Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & inster} to wit Benjamin Newman of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and Saith that he did take Robert Roe mentioned in the Examination on the opposite Side aged 5 years Mary Ann aged 3 years and Louis a Roe . aged about 9 months and John aged about Two years to the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields , by Virtue of an Order of Removal, that last night Three Children were left at the Door of the Workhouse of the Parish of St. Clement Danes which Three Children this Examinant verily be lives are the said May Ann Roe , John Roe and Louisa Roe whom this Deponent took to Saint Giles's as aforesaid

Sworn this 9d. Octr 1787 before D Walker Russ: Clarke Benj: Newman
