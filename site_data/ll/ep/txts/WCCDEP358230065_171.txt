Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Frances Hood aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hetley a Baker in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Twelve years since which time She hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of May 1799 before me} J Reeves

Frances Hood
