St Clement Danes 29th July 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Sepulchre Middx  (to wit) Ruth Farness  singlewoman aged sixty two years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Allen Apothecary next Door to the Cross Keys Inn in  St John Street in the parish of St. Sepulchre in the County of Middlesex for one year and upwards at the annual Wages of £5 that She quitted that Service about twelve years ago and hath never since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 29th day of July 1793 before us} N Bond John Collick

Ruth Farness 's Mark

Witness Garrett
