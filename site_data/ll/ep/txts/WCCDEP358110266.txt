Middx  Rose Page upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of Job Page by whom she has one Child (viz) Mary aged abt. 14 Weeks & that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband rented and lived in a house & pd. Scot & Lot for several years in Foregate Street in the New Church Parish at Worcester & has not Since he quitted the sd. house Rented ten Pounds a year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn June 23d. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Rose Page
