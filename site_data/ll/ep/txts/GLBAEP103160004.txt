Middlesex ss}  Thomas Dodgson maketh oath That divers Years ago he Rented a house known by the Sign of the Edinburgh Castle in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Twenty two pounds & was Charged to & paid Parish Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of February 1776 before  Wm. Blackmore

Tho Dodgson
