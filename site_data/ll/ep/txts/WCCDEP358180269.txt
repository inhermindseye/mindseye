Middx  Mary Bradford Aged 26 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married & that about 3 Yrs. since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Morris in St Clements Church Yard in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of 4 Years at the Yearly Wages of £3 Says since She left the sd. Mrs. Morris's Service she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 2d. Janry 1744 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Mary Bradford
