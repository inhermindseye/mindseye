Middlesex ss}  James Smart maketh oath that divers Years ago he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Eight pounds in Bailey & Place in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 10th day of February 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of James Smart
