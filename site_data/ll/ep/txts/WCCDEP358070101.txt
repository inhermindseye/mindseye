Westmr. to wit Isabella Thompson Aged about fifty Nine Years on her Oath says she never was married Rent House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Robert Titchbull a Merchant  at Hackney in the County of Middlesex upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of Twelve Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1773 before  Sampn. Wright  John Durden

Isablla Thompson
