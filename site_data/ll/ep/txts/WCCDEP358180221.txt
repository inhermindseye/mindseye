Middx  Ann Lawrance  Widow upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband Joseph Lawrance  Deceased in the Parish Church of St. Ann Westmr. about Eleven Years since that the sd. Joseph Lawrance lived in and Rented a House in Peter Street  Claremarkett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of two Years or thereabouts & hath left the same about two Years since which time her sd. late Husband in his Life or this Examinant since his Death have not rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath 2 Childn- (to wit) Joseph aged 6 Years & John aged about Three Years & being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne July 20th: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

 Ann Lawrnce
