Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Fulward aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that on the day of January now last past she this Examinant was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Griffiths in Nagshead Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the Name of Ann That one Edward Bishop a Journeyman Bricklayer who lodged in at Mr. Dyer a Bricklayer in Rigleys Rents  Mutton Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Edward Bishop is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of August 1788 :} Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Fulward Mark

Mary Fulward s Sister Mary Sarah Griffiths lives at No. 5 Multon Lane  Saffron Hill in Ridleys Rents

Edward Bishop is a Tall Thin Man rather pitted with the small Pox wears his Hair hanging in his Neck for further parttens Vid Examt.

On this Examination being Sworn Mary Fulward was let on she had been at Mad house but Recevd. her Father never taken: Pish at a distance
