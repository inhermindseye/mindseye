Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Holmes  Single woman maketh oath that She is the Daughter of Thomas Holmes by Frances his Wife both deced That about one Year & three Quarters of a year ago She was removed by a Pass order from the parish of Saint Luke in the County of Middlesex to the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County as being the place of her last legal Settlement in right of & as deriving the same from her said late Fathers Serving his Apprenticeship there That She this Deponent has not Since Such her removal or at any time previous thereto been married or ever lived as a hired Servant for a year or been bound Apprentice or done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 31st day of March 1775 before B Camper

Eili: Holmes
