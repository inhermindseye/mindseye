Middx ss}  Reeves Sams Maketh oath that about eleven years ago he lived as a Hired Servant by the year (at ye rate of £7 Seven pounds by the Year Wages) with his Grace the Duke of Grafton in the parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middx and Continued in Such Service for abt. the Space of four Years and was then a Singleman & Unmarried By means whereof this Deponent gained a legal Settlement in ye sd. parish of St. George Hanover Square And this Depont. Saith that he hath not Since done any act Whatsoever Whereby he has acquired or gained any Subsequent Settlemt. And that he is now a Married Man and hath wife Named Mary and two Children Namely William aged about four years and Henry aged about one year the lawfull Issue of this Depont. by his sd. Wife.

Sworn the 10th- day of July 1750 before me us  Saml Gower  Thos Quarrill

Reeves Sams
