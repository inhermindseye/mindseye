Middx ss  Elizabeth Brown  Singlewoman Maketh oath That about eleven years ago she lived as a hired servt by the Year with one Mr Williams at the sign of the White Lyon in Witch street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the county of Middx with whom she lived three years at first at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds & ten shillgs a Year & afterwards at the Yearly Wages of four pounds & ten shillgs a Year And saith yt she hath not since lived as a hired servt by the year nor ever Married nor hath gained any Subsequent settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 6th day of April 1756 before  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

her Elizabeth Brown Mark
