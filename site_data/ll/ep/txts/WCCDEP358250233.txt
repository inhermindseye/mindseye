Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Eaton aged about 49 years upon his Oath saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that he was born in Middlesex Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and that he this Deponent never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1796 } M Addington Edw. Read

John Eaton
