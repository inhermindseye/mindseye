Westmr. Alexander Dunbar Aged about Thirty Seven Years upon Oath Says that whilst he was a Singleman he Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Lane a Taylor in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted said Service about thirteen Years and hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says he Intermar'd with his Wife  Rebecca in the Parish Church of Stockdemmeril in the County of Devon about Nine Months ago

Sworn the 23d Day of February 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Alexr. Dunbar

Upon Mr Olivers Complt Ovr J. W
