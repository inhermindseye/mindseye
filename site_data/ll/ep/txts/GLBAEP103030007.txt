Middlesex ss.  Elijah Bassett maketh Oath that his Late Father Richd. Bassett about Thirty Fives Years ago Rented a House of Twenty Pounds as Year or thereabouts near the Hermitage Bridge in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and lived in the same about Six Years & was charged to and paid the Poors Rate for the same that he quitted the said House sometime in the Year 1767 and did not afterwards Rent any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Pay any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or do any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement, that he this Deponent was Born in the said House & Baptized at Aldgate Church that he has never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the Years with any Poors in any Parish or Place for the Space of One Year together or Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Real or Value of Ten Pounds or Done any Act to his Knowledge or Belief to gain as Settlement in his own right that he has a Child named Elijah aged about Five Years the Lawful issue by his late Wife Ann decd. to whom he was Lawfully Married at Aldgate Church about Fifteen Years ago & that the Said Child is with this Deponents Brother in St. Caths. Lane

Sworn this Day of Jany 1795 before}
