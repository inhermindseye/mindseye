Middlesex ss}  William Peters maketh oath That divers Years ago his late Father William Peters deced rented a Farm  of the yearly Rent of Fifty pounds in the parish of St Mary Stratford Bow in the County of Middlesex & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That this said Father died there (where this Deponent was also born) & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he this Depont was hired & lived yearly as a hired Servant at Certain Yearly Wages with his said Father in the same Parish & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two Years & by means of Such hiring & Service gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that he hath a Wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 26th day of May 1775 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

William Peters
