Middx  Eliz: Barrow aged about 30 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that about 7 Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Adams Peruke Maker in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of 14 Months at the Yearly Wages of £4 that since she left her said Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of 1 Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworn the 24th. of July 1742 before}
