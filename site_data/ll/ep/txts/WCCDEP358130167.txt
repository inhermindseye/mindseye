Middx  Winifred Maginnees aged about Sixteen Years on behalf of her Brother Jno. Maginnees an Infant aged about Seven Years upon Oath Says her Father & Mother are both Dead, that they were Natives of Ireland & never gain'd a Settlement in England, that her said Brother John Maginnees was born in Cross Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields , in the County of Middx.

Sworn the 22nd. Day of May 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

 The mark of Winifred Maginnees
