Middx  Catherine Martin of Plough Court Carey Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on behalf of Ann Martin aged abt. Ten years upon Oath Saith the sd. Ann Martin is the Natneal Daughter of this Examinants brother Gabriel Martin deceased and that the said Ann Martin was born in the parish of St Botolph witht. Bishop- gate London as she has been informed & verily believes

Sworn Novr 26th. 1754 before } Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Catherine Martin
