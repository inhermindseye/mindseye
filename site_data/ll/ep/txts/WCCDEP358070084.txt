Westmr. to wit Margt. Smith Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Owner Smith (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about Four Years ago and by whom she hath no Children and Says that her said Husband never rented House or Served any apprenticeship or lived a lived or Covenanted Servant before her Intermarriage or ever gained any Settlement but only thro his Father Owner Smith who rented and lived in a House the bottom of Lumbard Street in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the Liberty of White Fryars for Nine Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds as she believes hath quitted the said House about Fourteen Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of March 1773 before Us}
