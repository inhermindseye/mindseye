Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to Saint Clements to wit Elizth. Stevens aged about 60 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with Thomas Stevens about 38 years ago at the Fleet London and who has been dead about 11 years that her said Husband was born in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clements Danes that her said Husband was a Seafaring Man and never gained any other Settlement than by birth nor hath this Examinand since her said Husbands Death done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 20th. day of Augt. 1785  Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth Stevens Mark
