Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to Wit) Catherine Doile Aged 25 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith That she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Donolay in Bennets Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 2 Years at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds that she hath has quitted said Service about two Years and a half since which time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant, of one or done any Act whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge information or belief.

Sworn this 6th. Octr. 1790 before Wm Kitchiner

Catherine her mark Doile
