Middx  Mary Jones aged abt. 48 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Jones who is now a Prisoner in the Kings Bench Prison with whom she intermarried in the Cathedral Church of St. Finbarry in the City of Cork in Ireland on the 3d day of February 1763, says her said husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he Rented a house in Clements Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middlesex known by the Sign of the three Jolly Butchers of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds or thereabouts and lived in the same about four Years and after he quitted said house which is about twelve Years hath not Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish or Land Taxes as he has likewise informed her and which information she also believes to be true.

Sworn the 29th. May 1765 before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Jones
