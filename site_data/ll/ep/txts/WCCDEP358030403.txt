Westmr. 

Wm. Winstanley upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £22 for three Years & upwards & left the same about 11 Years since Says he has not since he quitted sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 7th. 1747  Wm: Jones

Wm: Winstanley
