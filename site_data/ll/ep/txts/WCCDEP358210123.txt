Westmr.  Mary Garrett aged about 57 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Garrett who has been dead about twenty Years, says her said late husband hath often times informed her and which information she verily believes that he served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Gregory a Bridlecutter and Whip maker in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to this Examinants knowledge and belief, says she hath not since the Death of her said late Husband Rented Ten Pounds a year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 13th, day of January 1767 . before} Paul Vaillant  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Mary Garrett
