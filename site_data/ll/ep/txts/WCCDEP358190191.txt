Middx  Margaret Burgin aged about 32 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married kept House was an Apprentice or hired Yearly Servant for One year That her Father William Burgin who has been Dead about 22 Years lived in and rented a House in Hart Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for about the Space of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds or thereabouts Quitted the Same about Six Months before his Death and never after rented any House or paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes.

Sworn the 7th. day of Octor. 1761 Before}  Fran: Bedwell Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Margt. Burgin
