Middx & Westmr-}  Elizth- Webb aged about 27 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house paid Taxes a lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Willm- Webb Rented and lived in a house in Milford Lane in the Parish of St- Clement Danes in the Co- of Middx for sevl- Years at the Yearly Rent of £10 and quitted same in the Year 1771 since which time he hath gained no other subseqt- Settlemt- neither hath this Examinant since her sd- Father quitted the same And further says that she hath one Child named John begotten by one John Davis a Bargeman born a Bastard in the said Parish of St- Lukes Chelsea in said County of Middx and that her said Child named John hath gained no other Settlement than by Birth

Sworn this 2 8th- Febry 1778 before me  Jno. Barnfather  John Cox .

Eliz Webb
