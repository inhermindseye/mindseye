Middlesex & Westminster  William Shepherd Aged about Thirty Nine years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship Says he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Robinson a Confectioner in New Bond Street (in a Capacity of a Coachman ) in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middlesex for Two years and a Quarter at the yearly Wages of Ten pounds hath quitted his said Service about Eleven years ago since which time he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or orwise gained any other subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 27th. day of Decr. 1775 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

William Shepherd
