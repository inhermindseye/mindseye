Middx.  Susannah Johnson  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Dixon a Gardiner in the Parish of St. John at Hackney in the County of Middlesex for the Space of one Year & one Quarter the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & hath left the same about Eight Years that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beliefe

Sworn the 20th. Augt. 1742 before} Houlson W. Hayton

The Mark of Susan: Johnson
