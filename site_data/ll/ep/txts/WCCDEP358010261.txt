Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Durr aged about 40 years upon his Oath saith that about 14 years ago he Intermarried with Catherine his present Wife at the State house Amsterdam according to the rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England that he this Examinant Rented and lived in a House in Falcon Bridge Court in the Parish of Saint Annes in the Liberty of Westmr. for about 3 years at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds that he hath quitted said House about 7 Years since which time he has not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of Janry. 1789 N Bond Sampn. Wright

John Durr
