Middx  Hannah Everitt aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath says she never was married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mrs. Joanna Roach in the Cleveland Court  Noble Yard in the Parish of St. James  in the Liberty of Westmr. for 2 Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted said Service abt. thirty Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 3d. day of Novr. Decr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Hannah Everitt

Resworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

N: B: She swore the first time her Settlemt. was in St. Martin in the fields
