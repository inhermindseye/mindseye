Westmr.  Richd. Becket upon Oath says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Smith at the Turks head Coffee house in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds he being at that time a Single Man and had never been Married hath quitted said Service about Ten Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 15th. May 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos: Balack

Richard Beckett
