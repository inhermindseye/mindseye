St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster}  Martha Prentis Aged 40 Years or thereabouts Maketh Oath and saith That she intermarried with David Prentis her present Husband who is now Journeyman to Mr Carpue in Serle Street in this parish about 13 Years ago at the parish Church of St Clement Danes Middlesex That her said Husband took a House in Chair Court Ship Yard about Nine Months ago at the Yearly Rent of £12 (Land Tax allowed) wherein they now live & Reside And further saith that John Prentis now of the Age of Ten Years is her Legitimate Child by the said David Prentis & has gained no Settlement in his own Right

Sworn the 7th March 1791 before Me  Wm Kitchiner

Martha Prentis Mark
