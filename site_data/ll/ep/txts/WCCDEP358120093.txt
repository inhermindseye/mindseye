Middx  Susa. Beech aged 70 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Bedwell in Cooks Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middlesex upwds. of one Year at the yearly Wages of Four pounds quitted her said Service near Eighteen months ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 26th. April 1754 before}

Susanna Beach
