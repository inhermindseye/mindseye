St Clement Danes Decr 7th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St John City of Gloucester  (to wit) Eleanor Taylor  singlewoman aged Fifty six or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she rented & resided in an House in the parish of St John in the City of Gloucester for upwards of 3 Months at the annual Rent of £7 and (as she believes) paid the Poor and other Rates for the same that she quitted that House about 8 years ago and hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year And believes She paid parish Taxes or lived as an hired Yearly Servant for a year together or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 7th day of Decr 1793 before us N Bond R Vaughan

Er Tyler

Removed by Mr Talboy Beadle Decr/93 a very troublesome Woman
