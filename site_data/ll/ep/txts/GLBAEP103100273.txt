Middlesex ss  Sarah Cross the widow of William Cross deced maketh oath That she hath been hedibly informed and verify believes the same to be true That her Said late husband was born in Pescod Street in the Parish of new windsor in the County of Berks and that he also duly served his apprenticeship there with his late Father John Cross also deced who was by Trade a Cordwainer ; and by means thereof as she this Depont. is advised her said husband gained a legal settlement there; and this deponent Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said late husband never Rented a house of the yearly Rent or value of Ten Pounds; nor ever served any Parish offices, or paid any Poors Rates, or kings Taxes; nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement subsequent to his apprenticeship; Nor hath She this Deponent to her knowledge or belief; done any Act since her said husbands not Death to gain a Settlement For herself

Sworn this 10th. day of May 1770 before  Chris: Scott

Sarah Cross her Mark
