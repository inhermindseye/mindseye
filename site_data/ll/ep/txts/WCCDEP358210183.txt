Middx & Westmr}  Ann Ely aged about 22 years upon Oath says She never was married and that She lived a hired yearly Servant upwards of two Years with one Mr. Pugson a Hosier in the Parish of St. Peter in Nottingham and hath not since lived in any other Service for a Year Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born; and that one Thomas Ranketti an Italian living with Mr. Sallow a Bust or Figure Maker in Holborn did beget the said Bastard Child or Children he having had carnal knowledge of her Body in the beginning of April last and at several other times since in the House of Mrs. Wilkinson in the Strand , within the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which She is now pregnant and that the said Thomas Ranketti and no other person did beget the same.

Sworn the 29th. day of August 1767 before } Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Ann Ely
