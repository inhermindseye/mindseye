Cathn. Kelly upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Thos. Kelly (gone from her) and that her sd. husband & herself are both Natives of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England & that her sd. husband was born at Shildare in Ireland & she can give no further Accot. of his Settlemt.

Sworn 22d Novr 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Cathn Kelly
