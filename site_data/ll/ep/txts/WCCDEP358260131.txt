Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Martha Rogers aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Walton who kept the Sign of the Bull in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Four pounds and Ten Shillings that she hath quitted said Service about Six years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she hath Two illegitimate Children living Vizt. Richard aged about Four years born at the Sign of the Cheshire Cheese in Milford Lane and Elizabeth aged about Ten weeks born at the house of a Mr. Wicks in Orchard Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1796 R. Ford J Robinson

her Martha Rogers Mark
