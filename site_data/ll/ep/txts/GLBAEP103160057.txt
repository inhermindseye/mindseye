Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Catherine Brady of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Single woman taken on oath before me George Colson Smith  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the peace in and for the said County this 30-day of April-1776 .

Who Saith that she is now with Child of a Child or Children And that the said Child or Children is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that one John Pitman of White Cross Street London  Gold & Silver Orrice Weaver is her Father of the Said Child or Children.

Taken & signed the day & year above written before me  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Catherine Brady
