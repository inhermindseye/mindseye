Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Briggs aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields to one Richard Shuter in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that after she was out of her said Apprenticeship she has never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Mark Kelly who lately lodged at Mrs. Harford No. 3 Southampton Street in the Strand Gentleman is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of December last and Several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Mark Kelly is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 29th. day of Septr. 1789 } R Butler

her Ann Briggs Mark

Mark Kelly is a Middx sized Man dark Hair black Eyes and fair Complexion wears his Hair Tied behind is about 18 years of age belongs to a Society at the Queen of Bohemias was Head.

Child dead

Man never taken
