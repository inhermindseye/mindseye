Middx  Ann Morrison Aged about Nineteen Years Upon Oath Says she was Never Marryed was an Apprentice or a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father James Morrison Rented a House in Houghton Street Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westminster One Year or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Eighteen Pounds hath quitted said House about Fourteen Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes as this Examinant hath been Informed and which Information she Verily Believes to be true

Sworn the Day of January 1764 Before}

Upon Mr Complt Ovr J. W
