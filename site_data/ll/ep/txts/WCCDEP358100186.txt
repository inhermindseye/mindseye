Middx to wit Elizth. Smith Aged about Thirty Six years on her Oath says she is the Widow of James Smith with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. James Clerkenwell about Eighteen years ago and who died about two years since and by whom she hath Three Children living (vizt.) Sarah aged about Sixteen years (at Present Provided for) Ann aged about Seven years and Elizth . Aged about 16 Months Says her said late Husband served Three years the latter part of his Apprenticeship to one Ambrose Stevenson a Tin Plate Worker in Tower Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the East London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any or. Settlemt. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year Paying Taxes or or.wise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husbands Death

Sworn this 20th. day of Febry: 1784  Wm Kitchiner  Edward Hodsoll

Elizth Smith
