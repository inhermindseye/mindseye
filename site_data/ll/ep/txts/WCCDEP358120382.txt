Middx  Henry Benson upon Oath saith She rented the half moon alehouse in the back of St Clements in the parish of St Clemt. Danes at the yearly Rent of £30 lived therein about two yrs. ago and quitted the same about 18 years ago and has not since Rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn July 17th. 1755 before} Saml Pechell

The mark of Henry Benson
