Middx.  Alice Hopkins aged abt. 50 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Henry Woodfall a Printer in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for one year and upwards at the Yearly wages of £5 and hath Quitted said service abt. 17 Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn the 28th. of Octr. 1758 . before me}

The Mark of Alice Hopkins
