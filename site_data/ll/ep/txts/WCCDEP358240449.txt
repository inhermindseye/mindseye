Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. Pish of Epping  to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of William Wilson an Infant of the Age of Thirteen years Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he believes to be true That William Wilson the Father Rented and lived in a house at Epping in the Parish of Epping in the County of Essex for about 12 Months at the yearly Rent of Six pounds And this Deponent further saith that he was also informed which Information he also verily believes to be true that the said Wm. Wilson also Rented at the same time a four stall Stable in Thoydon Garden for which he paid the Annual Rent of Four pounds and that he quitted Rented said house and Stable for about Twelve Months And that since he quitted said House and Stable her never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1794 N Bond E: Read

James Talboys
