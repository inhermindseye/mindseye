Middx 

Saml. Jervis upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his present Wife Hannah at the Fleet London about Five or Six Years ago and that about 12 Years since he Lived upon an Estate of his own called Birchfield of the Yearly Value of £30 in the Parish of Herse House in the County of Salop for sevll. Years & has not since he Quitted Birchfield rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Years or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned, before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of g a Saml. Jervis
