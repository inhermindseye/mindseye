Middx  Henry Woodward Aged about Twenty Six Years on his Oath Says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to one Thomas Lewis a Peruke maker in Star Alley Fenchurch Street in the Parish of All hallows Staining in the City of London and served his sd Master under such Indenture for the Space of Three Years when his said Master failing in Business he gave up this Examinant his Indentures and was never Assigned over to any other person since which time this he hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year or living hired Yearly or Covenanted Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. day of Febry. 1782 before R Butler M Addington

Henry Woodward
