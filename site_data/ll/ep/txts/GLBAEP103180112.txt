Middlesex ss}  George Cooper maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound by Indres to William Douglas of Duck Lane in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great London  Baker with Whom he Served abt Six year of his Apprenticeship & then by reason of this Deponts Sickness his Sd Master discharged him from his Sd apprenticeship & this Deponts Indres was delivered up to him That he this Depont has not Since rented a house or other Tenement of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a wife now living named Sarah whose residence is at Present at Darlington in the Bishoprick of Durham

Sworn this 15. of December 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

Geo: Cooper
