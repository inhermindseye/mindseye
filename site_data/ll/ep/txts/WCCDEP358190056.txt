Middx.  Benjamin Rivet aged abt. 21 Years lodging at the house of Mr. Redhead-in Kings head Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Upon Oath says and hath he intermarried with Sarah his wife at St. Giles Church abt. One Year and a half ago and that he learnt his Trade of a Glazier with his Father  Edwd. Rivet and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes nor was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for One Year says his said Father  Edwd. Rivet Rented a house in Church Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr.  of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for Sevl. Years.

Sworn Octr. 10th. 1760 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Edwd. Benjn. Rivet

Upon Mr. Penten's Complt. J: P:
