Middlesex ss}  John Jagwood the Son of John Jagwood Maketh Oath That he this Depont. hath been Credibly informd Evenly believes the Same to be true That his said Father divers Years ago was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one Mr Arnold a Cozy Sandler in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex at Certain Yearly wages & continued in Such Service for Several Years he being then a Fisherman & by means thereof gained a legal Setlement there and that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and this Deponent Saith that he never was bound apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever Rented a house of Ten Pounds a year nor ever Served Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his Knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. in his own right

Sworn this 26th. day of February 1771 before  Cha. Digby  Saml. Spagg

John Jagwood His Mark
