Middx.  Sarah Haddon Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Henry Haddon with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London abt. 15 Yrs. ago by whom she has five Children (Vizt.) Eleanor Mary aged about thirteen Years Sarah abt. 9 Yrs. Wm . about 7 Yrs. Elizth . about 5 and Henry abt. 2 Years. says her said husband before his intermarriage with her and whilst he was a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant for three Years with Mr. Barron a Brazier the Corner of Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds after he quitted said Service never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes. And this Examinant further says that her said husband is a Ticket Porter and in good Health and Strength and therefore ought to Assist. this Examinant in supporting her said family, which he refuses to do and this Examinant is not Capable of supporting her family herself

Sworn this 24th. October 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Haddon .
