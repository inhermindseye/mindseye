Middx  John Harris upon Oath saith he was in or about the year 1739 appointed Clerk of the Parish Church of St. Nicholas in Bristol & Executed that Office about Eleven Years-and that he likewise rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £8 in Baldwin Street in the sd. parish & lived therein abt. 3 or 4 years and pd. his Share to the poor Rate of the sd. parish of St Nicholas and has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 26th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

John Harris
