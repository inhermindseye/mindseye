Westmr.  Eliz Newsham on behalf of Wm. Tildesley an Infant aged upwards of Nine Years upon Oath saith the said William Tildesley is her Son by a former husband (viz) William Tildesley with whom she intermarried at the Old Church Harwood in Lands in the County of York Saith the said William Tildesley rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £17 in the Cloth market near the Bridgegate at Leeds afsd. and there dyed and this Examinant continued to Live in the same house for two Years after the Death of her said late husband Says during the time she continued a Widow she did not do any Act to gain a Settlement in any other Place until her Intermarriage with John Newsham her present husband Says the said John

Newsham is gone from her and she being poor cannot provide for the sd Wm. Tildesley her Son by means whereof he is Obliged to wander about the Streets

Sworn July 17th. 1749 before H Frilding

Elisettebeeth newsome
