Middx  Sarah Scarlett aged abt. 33 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Scarlet (Now Confined in the Marshalsea Prison for Debt) with whom she intermarried at Oxford Chapel in Tyburn Read abt. 18 Years since says her said husband Rented a house in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. of the Yrly. Rent of £16 or thereabouts and lived therein abt. Nine Months and hath quitted same abt. a Year & a half Since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn this 20th. of Septr. 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Scarlett
