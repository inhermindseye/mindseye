Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Jones Aged about 53 years upon her Oath Saith that about 26 years ago she intermarried with her present Husband at the present Parish Church of Lowardle Pembrokeshire by whom she hath living two Children Vizt. Elizabeth Aged Nine years and John Aged Seven years The she does not Know where her husband was born that her said Husband never was bound apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for twelve or ever rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement to the best of her Knowledge or belief and this Deponent Saith that while Single she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr Fly in the Parish of Note in the County of Pembroke at two Pounds a year That after she left the said Service she never did any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement except by Her Husband of which she is Ignorant as above Stated and further this Deponent upon her Oath Saith that her said Husband has for Six Weeks past refused to maintain her and has threatened to sell his Goods and desert her and her said Child and refuses to allow them any Support that they live in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and they are become Chargeable and have received relief of the said Parish

Sworn this 15th day of Feby 1800 before me Robinson

Mary Jones her Mark
