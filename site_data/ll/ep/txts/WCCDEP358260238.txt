Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Coleman aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Pringle in Henrietta Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about Eight Months since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 3rd. day of Janry. 1798 before us  Jno. Joepey R. Ford

Ann Coleman
