Westmr.  Jane Hall aged about 32 Years upon Oath says, she was never Married, nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year; says her Father Saml. Hall deceased Rented a house in Newtoners Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds for several Years and there dyed

Sworn the 17th. day of April 1766 , before} Fran. Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Jane Hall
