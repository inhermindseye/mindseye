Middlesex ss}  Ann Tozer  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago her late Father William Tozer rented a house of the yearly Rent of twelve Pounds in Lower East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & resided thereon for Several years & did not afterwards rent a house of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 16 day of October 1784 before R Davies Ann Tozer
