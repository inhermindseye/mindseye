Middx  Rachael Stainsby Aged about Fifty Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of William Stainsby who died about two Years ago says she Intermarried with her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Mary Lambeth in the Year 1769 and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said late Husband has often Informed her that he was born at Richmond in the County of Surry where her said Father was settled as she has been Credibly Informed and Verily believes and that her said late Husband never Rented House paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant before Marriage for one Year as her said Husband has also Informed her and which Information she Verily believes to be true

Sworn this 27th. day of Novr. 1778 before  John Cox . Jno Barnfather

her Rachael Stainsby Mark
