St Clement Danes 7th. Febry. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Mary Seer  singlewoman Aged 28 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Hutton no.12 on the Terrace facing Crossby Row in the Parish of St. Mary Newington in the County of Surry , about two Years, at the Annual Wages of Eight Guineas; that she quitted that Service about Six years ago, and hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement, And this Deponent Saith that she hath a Female Bastard Child now living with her Aged about two years & three Month, and which hath been half Baptized by the Name of Mary Lloyd , which was born of her Body and of lawful Matrimony at No. 5 New Bedford Court Catherine Street in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of a Westminster And that She is unable to maintain herself and Child without Assistance

Sworn the 7th. day of Febry. 1792 before us N Addington Thos. Gordon

Mary Seer 's Mark

Witness to the Mark Jas Talboys
