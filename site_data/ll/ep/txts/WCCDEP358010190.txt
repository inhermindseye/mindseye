Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Farrall aged about 58 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that she was born in King Street Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middx that her Father Rented House there for sevl. years that she has done no act to gain any other Settlement than by Ba

Sworn this 2d. day of Febry. 1788 before D Walker Rapt: Clarke

Ann Farrall
