Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Evans aged about 85 years upon her Oath saith that about Sixty years ago she Intermarried with Job Evans her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Brides that he has been dead about Sixteen years That he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Owen a Taylor in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Augt. 1798 before me} Wm Hyde

her Mary Evans Mark

Witness H Day
