Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Brock aged about 56 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Simms in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Ten years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Six pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Twenty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of April 1799 before me} J Reeves

her Ann Brock mark
