Westmr.  Ann Humphrys upon Oath saith she never was married and that she is the Daughter of Thos Humphry by Mary his Wife that she never was an apprentice or yearly Servant rented ten Pounds a year or paid Parish taxes and that her sd. father Thos. Humphrys  rented a house of £40 by the year known by the Sign of the Black horse in little Wild Street in the said Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx. & lived therein upwds. of one Year and has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten Pounds by the Year or pd. Parish taxes to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 14th 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Humphrys
