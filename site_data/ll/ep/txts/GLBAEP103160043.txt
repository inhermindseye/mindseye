Middlesex ss}  Margaret Butler the widow of John Butler deced maketh oath that since the death of her Said late husband She hath been hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with Henry Taylor of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Victualler at Certain yearly Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Fourteen Months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 18. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Margaret Butler
