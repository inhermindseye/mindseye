Middx  Elizth: Ball Aged about 32 Years Upon Oath Says she was never Marryed and that she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mr Richd Stiles a Shoemaker in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx Upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and hath quitted her said Service upwards of Three Years and hath not Since Rented house Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. And further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that One Martin Carter who Informed this Examinant he was a Soldier in the First Regiment of Foot Guards had Carnal knowledge of her Body Some time in the Month of August or September last and at several other times Since at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 14th Day of June 1763 before} Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Ball
