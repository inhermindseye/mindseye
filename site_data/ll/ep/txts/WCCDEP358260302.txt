Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Robert Page aged about 78 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a house in Grafton Street in the Parish of Saint Ann in the Liberty of Westminster  for Seven years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Thirty years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement and further saith that he is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 27th. day of July 1798 before us } N Bond Edwd: Naish

his Robt Page Mark

Witness H Day
