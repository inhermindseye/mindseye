Middx  Eliz Morris aged about 48 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Edwd. Morris (who has been gone from her upwds. of 20 Years) on the 12th. of March abt. 27 yrs. ago at St Georges Chapel in Queen Square and that her sd. husband Rented a house in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes  Middx at the Yearly Rent of £30 & lived therein about 3 years & quitted the same abt. 23 Yrs. ago & has not since Rented ten pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes to her knowledge

Sworn May 9th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Morriss
