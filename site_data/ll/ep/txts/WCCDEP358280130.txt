Saint Clement Danes

Martha Pitt aged 29 or thereabouts upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being a Single Woman never Married and poor and unable to Maintain the same when born And Further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith That James White , late Servant to Mr. Dn Merrick at Mrs. Blands Norfolk Street , had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant sometime in the Month of March-last and at Several Times since at one of which times he did beget the Child or Children of which she is so Pregnant-And saith That he hath lived at Mrs. Blands upwds of 12 Months last past as a hired Yearly Servant.

Sworn before me this 19 Octr 1787 .} R Butler

her Martha Pitt Mark

Warrt. to Burbidge Octr. 25. 87

Pitt Serv: to Mrs. Bland Norfolk Street.

Pitt says that White went to Tunbridge door not know particularly where-dischd. by Du Merrick Jas. White did live at No. 18 Norfolk Street then came to live with Mr Du Merrick a Lawyer who came from Jersey -and Lodged Mrs. Blands White is near 6 Feet High May his own Hair Tyed light Complexion, light Hair, their. (an acquaintenance of his one Geo: Morley lunas Greenwich .)
