Middlesex ss}  Harriot Lane the Wife of William Lane maketh oath that She well knows a Child named Sarah Brown aged about eight Months the which Child was born of the body of one Elizabeth Shakespear in Cable Street in the Parish of St George in the said County whose Mother hath deserted her & is gone to Places unknown to this Deponent the which said Child as this Depont has been informed & believes was born a Bastard

Sworn this 31st- day of May 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Harriot Lane
