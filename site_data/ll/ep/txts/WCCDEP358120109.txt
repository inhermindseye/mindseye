Middx  James Jones aged abt. Thirty Years Upon Oath Saith that her was Bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with his father Jno. Jones a Whipmaker in the City of Winchester in the County of Hants Says he Served the last Year of his sd. Apprentiship in the Parish of St. Lawrance in the City of Winchester aforesd. that he never Rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes Served a Parochiall Office or hired a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworne the 29th. of May 1754 before} Tho Lane

James Jones
