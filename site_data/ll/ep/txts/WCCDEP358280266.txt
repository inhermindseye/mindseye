Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Norther aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that about 14 years ago she Intermarried with John Norther at the Parish Church of South Kirby in Yorkshire that her said Husband has been dead about Nine years that her said late Husband lived as a hired yearly Servant with Edward Dickens of Lincolns Inn Fields whilst a Singleman that he Slept during the whole time over the Stables in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that after he quitted said service he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Christopher who lives a Servant with a Mr. Jones in Half Moon Street  in whose Sir Name she does not know is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant thereon and that the said Christopher and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only Real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 26th. day of Janry. 1790 } R Butler

her Elizth. Norther Mark

Elizabeth Norther

Christopher is a Tall Stout fresh Coloured Man fair Complexion wears his Hair Tied behind.

Settled pd. £15.
