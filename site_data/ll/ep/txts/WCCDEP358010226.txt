Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Taylor aged about 59 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that her Father William Taylor Served Seven years apprenticeship with a Mr. Cole a Cooper in Coopers Row in the Parish of Saint Olave Hart Street in the City of London that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of July 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

her Mary Taylor Mark
