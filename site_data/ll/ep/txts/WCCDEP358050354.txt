Middx  Mary Williams aged about 67 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Richard Williams who dyed abt. 3 Yrs. ago and that her sd. Late husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £20 in Witch Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and lived therein abt. ten Years & there dyed & this Examint. continued to live in and rents the same house upwds. of two Years after his decease & has not since she quitted the same rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 3d. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Williams
