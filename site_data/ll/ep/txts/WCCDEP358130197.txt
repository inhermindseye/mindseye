Middx  Ellis Walwork aged about Seventy Years upon Oath says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for one Year Says his Father Ellis Walwork Served his Apprentiship with Reuben Hainworth in the Township of Todington in the County of Lancaster & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 21st. of August 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Ellis Wallwork
