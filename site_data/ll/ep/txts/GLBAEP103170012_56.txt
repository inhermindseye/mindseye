Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Towns the widow of Thomas Towns deced maketh oath that her late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born in Swan Alley in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & that to her knowledge or belief he never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound Apprentice nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & this Depont Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late husbands Parents Save that She has been informed that his Mother was born in the Sd Parish of St Botolph Aldgate & that She this Depont was Married to her late husband abt thirty Years ago at ye Fleet

Sworn this 8th of October 1776 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Elizabeth Towns
