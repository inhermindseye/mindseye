Westmr.  William Cole Aged Upwards of Thirty nine Years, Upon Oath says he never was Married and that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stephen Fairweather at the sign of the Crown in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , Victualler , for One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds, Quitted said Service about Twenty one Years ago, and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 4th day of March 1768 , Before} 

Wm Cole
