Middx  Ann Barnes aged 21 Years upon Oath says she never was Married That she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wassell a Taylor in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx for Four years at the yearly Wages of £3 Quitted the same about Eighteen Months since which she hath not rented any House paid Land Tax or Poor Rate or been a hired Servant for one year

Sworn the 3d. day of May 1762 Before}
