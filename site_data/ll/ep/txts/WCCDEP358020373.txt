St Clement Danes January 4th 1792

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Robert Wall aged 40 years & upwards Upon Oath Saith that he intermarried with Elizabeth his late Wife formerly Elizabeth Baker  Spinster at the parish Church of Warley in the County of Essex about Eleven years ago that he never had any Children and that his said late Wife Died about 3 or 4 Months after their Marriage that previous to his said Marriage and while he was single & unmarried not having any Child or Children he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Green at the Spotted Dog in Clements Lane about two years at the yearly wages of £5 that he quitted that service about twenty Years ago and hath not obtained any other legal Settlement be the best of his hereby Knowledge or belief

Sworn the 4th day of January 1792 before me  Wm Hitchiner

his Robert Wall Mark
