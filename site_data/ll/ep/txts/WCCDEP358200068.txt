Margt. Burk Aged about 34 Years Wife of George Burk Gone from her upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her said Husband at the Fleet London abt. 12 Years ago Says her said Husband is a Native of Ireland and never Gained a Settlement in England and that Before his Intermarriage with her he Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Evans a Miller in the parish of Selbridge in the County of Kildare in the Kingdom of Ireland aforesaid with whom he Lived about Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds or thereabouts hath quitted said Service about Fifteen Years and he hath n Since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived

a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year as this Examinant hath been Informed by her said Husband and which Information she Verily Believes to be true Says she has One Child by her said Husband (to wit) Wm. Aged about Seven Years

Sworn the 13th Day of Feby 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Margt Burk

Upon Mr Jones Complt Ovrs J. W
