Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Pritchard aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that about Ten years ago she Intermarried with William Pritchard her late husband at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square that her said husband has been dead about Six years that her said late husband has been dead abouts that has served his Time as an Apprentice at Brecknock in Walls that she hath an illegitimate Child Vizt. Sophia Elizabeth born a Bastard at the house of a Mr. Duncan in Chancery Lane in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London

Sworn this 11th. day of Decr. 1789  Sampn Wright  Wm. Kitchiner

Eliz. Prichard
