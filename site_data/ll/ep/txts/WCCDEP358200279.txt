Middlesex .  Susannah Andrews aged about 33 Years Upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Arnold in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and hath so done for about one Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born says that Samuel Bonnamy  Esqr . had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of October 1764 and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Samuel Bonnamy  Esqr . and other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 26th. June 1765 before M Adn

The Mark of Susannah Andrews
