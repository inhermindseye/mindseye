Middx  Sarah Sunderland aged 23 Years & upwards upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman that by Indenture Dated the 22d. Novr. 1731 she was bound an Appentrice to & Served 6 Yrs & a half Apprentiship with Mrs. Elizabeth Talford Button maker in Bennetts Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex that since her said Apprentiship she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that Jonathan Pinneck a Wheelwright did begett the said Child or Children on her Body the sd. Jonath: Pinneck and this Examiant at such time liveing together as Man & Wife at the House of Mrs. Allens in Tyburn Road where the sd. Jonathan Pinneck had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of Novr. last & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Jonathan Pinneck & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne April 18: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sarah Sunderland
