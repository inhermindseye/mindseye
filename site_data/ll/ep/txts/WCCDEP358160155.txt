Middx  The Examination of Thos. Davis Passed from the Parish of St. James in ye. Liberty of Westmr . by Ordr. bearing Date the 17th. May last taken the 15th. Day of July 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Thos. Davis in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taylor & Served him as an Apprentice for the Term of Seven Years, And this Examint. further saith yt. he hath not since he left his sd. master Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath a wife & one Child Named Ann Day aged abt. 9 years & being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine them

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before}
