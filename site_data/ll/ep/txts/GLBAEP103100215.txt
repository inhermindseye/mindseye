Middlesex ss  Daniel Dunnoven a Singleman maketh oath That about Nineteen years ago he was hired by and lived as a hired Servant by the year with Nathaniel Sheppard of the Parish of Richmond in the County of Surry  Attorney at Law and Continued in such Service there for the Space of one year and upwards at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there as he is advises And this Depont. Saith that he hath not since lived a hired Servant for a year with any other Person Nor hath he ever Served any Parish Office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th day of Jan: 1770 before  Chris: Scott  Cha: Digby Daniel Donovan
