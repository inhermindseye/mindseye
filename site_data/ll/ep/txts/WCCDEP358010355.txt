Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Pitts aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that about four years ago she Intermarried with John Pitts her present husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Rebecca ag about 15 Months That her said Husband informed this Examinant that he was born at Worthington in the Parish of Worthington in the County of Northampton That he never was bound as an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That he lived as Waiter with Mr Bristol of Saint Clement Church yard for Nine years but received no Wages that he has left said Mr. Bristol about a fortnight And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has left this Examinant and her said Child about a fortnight since

Sworn this 18th. day of Augt. 1790  Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Pitts Mark
