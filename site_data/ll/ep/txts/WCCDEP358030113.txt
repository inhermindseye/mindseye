Middx  Jane Jackson upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the 28th. Day of Janry 1744/5 in the house of Mrs. Evans in Crown Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized Richard & is now living & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same and that Richard Todd  Haberdasher did begett the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body the sd. Rd. Todd living a fellow Servt . with this Examinant at Mr. Howes near the new Church in the Strand Haberdasher there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of April 1744 & at severall other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. and that he the sd. Richd. Todd & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworn March 4th. 1745 before  Wm. Jones  Tho Sane

The Mark of Jane Jackson
