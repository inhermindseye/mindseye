St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Elizabeth Foley formerly Tong Aged 36 Years or thereabouts Wife of Luke Foley Maketh Oath and Saith that she was Married to her present Husband Luke Foley abt. 16 Years ago at the Roman Catholic Chapel in Duke Street Lincolns Inn fields , That her Father in Law Peter Wandsworth Rented & lived in a House in Bear Yard abt. 16 Years at the Yearly Rent of £18 or £20 or thereabouts wherein he died and that which happened abt and that her and Mother own Mother Sarah Wandsworth continued to Reside there after her father's Deceasefull twelve Months until she her said Mother Died at the same Yearly Rent And further saith that she this Examinant never gained any Settlement in her own Right that her her two Children Vizt: Edward Aged abt. thirteen and Margaret Aged Nine Years & upwards were Born at No. 7 or 8 in Houghton Street Clare Market And that her said Children or either of them never gained any settlement in their his or her own Right

Sworn the 8th March 1791 . before Me  Wm. Kitchiner

Elizth. Foley 's Mark.
