Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Linnyman aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bowers a Grocer in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of £6 that she hath quitted said Service about Two years ago since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that on the 19th. day of Febry. now last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Hollister in Vere Street in the said Parish which hath since been Christened by the Name of Charlotte That one Henry Sanders a Journeyman Shoemaker who lately lodged at No.2 Little Shire Lane and work'd at Mr. Carpues in Serle Street is the Father of the said Child he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Henry Sanders is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 11th. day of Septr. 1787 } R Butler

her Jane Linnyman Mark

Linnyman s Child

Henry Sanders -a Shoemaker lived No. 2 Little Shire Lane work'd at Carpues in Serle Street -he is about 5 Foot 3 Inches high Dark Complexion'd wears his own Hair dark Colour

Jane Linnyman lives at No. 4 Orange Court Drury Lane Mr. Burbidge has this Warrt.

The man Taken and Committed to Bridewell for want of Securities Jany 11th. 1788
