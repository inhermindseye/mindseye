Middx & Westmr} to wit Richard Mitchell Aged about Thirty Six Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Mary in the Parish Church of St. Mary in the Isle of Ely in the County of Cambridge about Seven Years ago Says he never Rented a House or Tenemt. of Ten Pounds by the Year paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year Says his Father John Mitchell who was by Trade a Printer and lived in a House where this Examinant was born known by the Sign of the Maiden head now by the Sign of the Green Man in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for many Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or thereabouts and soon afterwards died Says he has four Children by his said WIfe Namely John Aged about five Years Richard Aged about four Years Mary aged about three Years and Elizabeth Aged about Two Years

Sworn this 11th. Day of Novr. 1768 before W Kelynge

Richd. Mitchell
