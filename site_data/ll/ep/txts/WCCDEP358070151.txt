Westmr to wit Mary Rogers Aged about fifty years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Benjamin Rogers an Attorney in Lyons Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Martin Ludgate In the City of London about Nineteen Years ago and by whom she hath three Children now living namely George Aged about Eighteen Years Hester Aged about fourteen Years and Sarah Aged about Ten Years she being Poor and unable to Maintain herself and her said Three Children says her said late Husband was several Years Clerk with one Mr. Bolt an Attorney in Lyons Inn at the Yearly Wages of Forty Pounds as she has been Informed and verily believes and also had Chambers of his Own which he purchased of the Society of Lyons Inn aforesaid during his Life and further Says that since her said late Husband Death she hath done Any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th day & Decr. 1773 before} 

Mary Rogers
