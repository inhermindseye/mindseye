Westmr.  Sarah Christian Aged about Fifty four Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Patrick Christian deceased with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. George Southwark in the County of Surry about Twenty five Years ago, says her said Husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief, says that since her said Husband's death she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Henrietta Smith in Naked Boy Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and eleven Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, quitted said Service upwards of a Year ago, and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 3d. day of May 1768 , Before} Thos: Kynaston

Sarah Christian
