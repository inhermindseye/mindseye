Middx  Elizabeth Morris aged near Twenty Years upon her Voluntary Oath says She never was Married that She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes She being poor and unable to maintain the same when born that Benjamin Lindsey  Servant to Mr. Weston in Norfolk Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes hath had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body several times at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which She is now pregnant and that the said Benjamin Lindsey and no other Person did beget the same Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Shields at the Angel and Sun in the Strand in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds-and continued in her said Service about Nine Months and then was discharged That in about three Months after her said Discharge She came and lived with the said Shields again for the space of six Months or thereabouts and then quitted her said Service again and went and lived with Mr. Burley in Piccadilly for about two Months and then came and lived with the said Shields again for three Months or thereabouts and was discharged again from that Service on the fourth day of October 1762

Sworn the 9th. day of October 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth. Morris
