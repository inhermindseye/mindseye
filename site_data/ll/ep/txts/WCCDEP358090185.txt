Middx  Ann Thomas Aged about Thirty one Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Richard Thomas served Seven Years Apprenticeship to One Bannister a Stationer in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one year neither hath this Examinant otherwise then as aforesaid

Sworn this 20th. Day of December 1780 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Thomas Mark
