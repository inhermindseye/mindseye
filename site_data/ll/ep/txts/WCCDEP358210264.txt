Westmr.  Elizabeth Douglas Aged about Fifty five Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Ignatius Thomas Douglas deceased, says her said Husband Rented a House in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds, Quitted same about Thirteen Years ago, and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 12th day of April 1768 . Before} Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Elizth. Douglas
