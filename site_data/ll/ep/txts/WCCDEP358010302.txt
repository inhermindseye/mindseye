Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Soames aged about 72 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Wm. Somes  her late husband at the parish Church of Saint andrew Holborn that her said Husband Rented a House Called or known by the name or Sign of the Kings Head Pimlico in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the county of Middlesex for Four years at the yearly Rent of Fifty two Guineas that he hath quitted said House about Twelve years since which time he has not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of July 1789 R Butler

Ann. Soames
