Middlesex ss}  Sarah Jennis aged Fifteen Years or thereabouts maketh oath That she Cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents deced or either of them But Says that She has been informed by her Said Parents & which Information She believes to be true That She this Deponent was born in Petticoat Lane Whitechappell in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate which as She has been informed and believes is in the City of London And this Deponent Saith that She was never bound apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for Year nor to her Knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since her Birth

Sworn this day of September 1771 before}

The Mark of Sarah Jennis
