Middlesex ss}  Richard Oldmeader Maketh oath That he well knew one Charles Pearce deceased And that the Said Charles Pearce Served his apprenticeship with his Father Henry Pearce a ship Corker in Upper Gun Alley in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middlesex and that to this Deponts. knowledge or belief the Said Charles Pearce never did any Act to gain any Settlement Subsequent to such his Apprenticeship and this Depont. also knew Susannah the  wife of the Said Charles Pearce who is also dead by whom he had one Child named Elizabeth who is Still living and aged about Six Years & hath gained no Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 3d Day of July 1772 before  Cha: Digby W Russell

Richd Oldmeder
