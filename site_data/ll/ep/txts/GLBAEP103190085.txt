Middlesex  Margaret Brannen  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago She was bound Apprentice by the Parish officers of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County to Wm Sibbet of the Parish of  St John Wapping Middx  Taylor & having Servd him there for about two Years She was Assigned or Turned over to Wm Dodd of the said Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate Taylor with whom She Served the residue of her Sd Apprenticeship That after her said Apprenticeship was ended She was hired & lived as a hired Servant with Richard Foreman in the Precinct of the Tower within at Certain Wages & Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one year & half there & has not Since lived for the Space of a year as a hired Servant elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement or than as before is Stated and Setforth.

Sworn this 25 day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Margaret Brannen
