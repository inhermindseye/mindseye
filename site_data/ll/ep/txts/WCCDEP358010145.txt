Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit George Blakeney aged about 17 years upon his Oath saith That in the Month of August 1781 he was bound Apprentice by the Church wardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes to one John Brown a Peruke Maker who lived in Holborn in that part of the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn below the Bars in the City of that Lies in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden & Ely Rents London for 7 years that he served his said Master under such Indre upwards of Six Months that them his said Master broke and could not keep this Ext. no Conger that he this Ext. has never been Assigned over to any person or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Octr. 1787 Maddington N Bond

his George Blakeney mark
