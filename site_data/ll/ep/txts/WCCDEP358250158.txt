Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Adey aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith that about 16 years ago she Intermarried with John Adey at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster That her said Husband Served Seven years Appreship to one Mr. Hefford a Plaisterer in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmtr. and that after he was out of his said Appreship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of March 1795  J Hlund B: Kennett

her Sarah Adey Mark

Witness Richd. Dobbins
