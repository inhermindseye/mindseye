Thomas Mackadam on Behalf of Thomas Chamberlain aged about fifteen Yrs. & Mathew Chamberlain aged about Six Yrs. the Children of William Chamberlain by his Wife both deceased upon his Oath saith that he hath been informed & verily believes that Wm. Chamberlain the Father of the sd. Infants was born in the Parish of Yeovill in the County of Somersett & was there bound Apprentice to a Glover Says the sd. Wm. Chamberlain never rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes Served a Parochiall Office lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 18th 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Thos Mackadam
