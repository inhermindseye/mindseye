St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary le Strand  (to wit) William Stocker aged 43 years or thereabts. Upon Oath saith that he was Married to Jane his present Wife at the parish Church of St Leonard Shoreditch on or abt about the 22d. day of December 177 1776 (by Banns) that he this Deponent Rented and Resided in an house in Little Drury Lane in the parish of St Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster for about six years at the Annual Rent of £20 that he quitted the same about seven years ago and that he hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Deponent further saith that he hath two Children now living by his said Wife Namely Ann aged fourteen years or thereabouts and John aged about Eight Years now with this Deponent And that he is unable to maintain himself and family without assistance

Sworn the 5th day of June 1793 } Mitchell W Addington

William Stocker
