Middx to wit Elizth. Sparks Aged about Twenty years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Little a Taylor in Leadenhall Street in the Parish of St. Catherine Christ Church  London for 18 Months at the yearly Wages of £5. hath quitted same about Nine months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement.

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry. 1784  Sampn Wright W Addington

Her Elizth Sparks mark
