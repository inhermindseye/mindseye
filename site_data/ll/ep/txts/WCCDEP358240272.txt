St Clement Danes 12th June 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. C D  (to wit) Mary Padget  singlewoman aged thirty three years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant at the with Mr Butterfield at the Rose Tavern in Ship yard in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County of Middlesex for a year and upwards at the Annual Wages of ten pounds that She quitted that place about three years ago and hath not Since Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 12th day of June 1793 before Me} D Walker Mary Padgett
