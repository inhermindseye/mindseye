Middx  Jane Fairbrother upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Jas. Fairbrother who is now in the Workhouse belonging to the parish of St. Andw. Holbn. above Barrs in the County of Middlx and that she has been informed & believes her sd husband Served his Apprentiship with a Cabinet maker an Hare Street Hill in the sd. parish of St. Andw Holborn above Barrs & never rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Augt. 16th 1753 before}

Jane. fairbrother
