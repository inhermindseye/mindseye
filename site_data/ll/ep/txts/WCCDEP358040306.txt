Westmr.  Edwd. Mackan upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Mary at the Romish Chapell in Belltree Lane in the City of Bath upwds of 4 Years ago and that he hath two Children viz: Mary aged near 13 Years & Margt . aged two Years & upwards and that about 13 Years ago he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds for nine Months & upwds. in Bennetts Court in Drazy Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and has not since he Quitted the sd. house Rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 31st. 1748 before } Wm: Jones

Edward Mackan
