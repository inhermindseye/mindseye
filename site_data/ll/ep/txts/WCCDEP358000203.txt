Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Mary Matthews aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Thomas Matthews her late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Olave Southwark upwards of 16 years ago and who has been dead about Seven weeks Saith that she has three Children living by her said Husband vizt. John aged 11 years and upwards Edwd . aged about 10 years who is Provided for and Mary aged 2 years or thereabouts Saith that her said Husband never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes that he this Exts. husband being a Singleman and without Child or Children he went to live as a hired yearly Servt with one George Madewell a Glass cutter in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of £10 - to the best of this Examts. Knowledge that her said Husband hath Quitted that Service about Nineteen years ago Since which time her said Husband has done no Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge he might have gained any other Settlement and that She is Poor and unable to Maintain  herself

Sworn this 11th. day of January 1785 before me R Butler

Mary Matthews
