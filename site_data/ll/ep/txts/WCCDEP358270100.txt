After Birth Sarah Eadey and Susanna her Child

St Clement Danes February 13th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St George Southwark  Child's birth  Reputed Father (to wit) Sarah Diley Eadey aged 21 years & upwards singlewoman Upon Oath saith that She never was Married nor ever obtained any legal Settlement in her own right that She Father is the legitimate Daughter except living as an hired yearly Servant with her Uncle Mr Messer No. 41 Elliot's Row in the parish of Saint George and Southwark of William & Ann Eadey or she hath been informed & verily upwards of a year at the Annual Wages believes that he said Father is legally Settled in Hitching of five Guineas Hertfordshire by renting or House there for several year whose he was lives and this Deponent farther upon her Oath saith that on or about the sixteenth day of January last at the House of Mr. Croydon in Holles Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath since been half Baptized by the Name of Susanna (as this Deponent hath been informed and believes) and farther saith that the said Child is Since become chargeable to the Said parish of Saint Clement Danes and that Joseph Smith of No. 2 Wickham place Kent Street Southwark Carpenter and Builder is the only real and true father of the said Child of which this Examinant was delivered as aforesaid he ha having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in or about the Month of September 1791 April March 1792 and at Several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child thereon and that he and no other person is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn the 13th day of February 1793 before me} D Walker

Sarah Eady
