Middx  Edward Mackinder aged upwards of 70 Years upon Oath saith he is a Taylor by trade and lived in and rented a House in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Three Months at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds Quitted the Same about Ten Years and hath not since Rented any House paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate

Sworn the 22d. day of Septr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

Edward Mackinder
