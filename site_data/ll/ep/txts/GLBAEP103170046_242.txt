Middlesex ss}  Mordecai Wale maketh oath That he Cannot give any Account respects his Parents Settlement or either of them That he this Depont was born in the liberty above the Bars in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex & never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any parish office or paid any poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in his own right

Sworn this 13. day of January 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mordecai Wale
