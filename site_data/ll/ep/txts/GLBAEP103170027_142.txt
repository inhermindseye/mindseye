Middlesex ss}  Ann Cox  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago She was duly bound Apprentice by the Parish officers of Saint John Wapping Middx to one Mrs Ebbing of the Parish of St George in the said County & Server the whole of Such Apprenticeship in the same parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant in any one place for a year nor been ever married nor done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this 26 day of November 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Ann Cox
