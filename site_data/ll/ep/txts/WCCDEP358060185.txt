Middx  Jas. Winn upon Oath saith he intermarried with his wife Elizabeth Winn at the Fleet London near 3 Years ago and that he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr John Butler a Taylor & lived the last three years of that term wth. the sd. Butler in little Essex Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & that he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 14th 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

James Winn
