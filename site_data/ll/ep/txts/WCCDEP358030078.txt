Middx  Richd. Millums upon his Oath saith he lived in & rented a house & Garden Ground in the Parish of Frinsbury in the County of Kent of the Yearly Rent of £24 for the Space of two Years and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes & hath left the same abt. 20 Yrs. Says he hath not since he left the sd. house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Jany. 9th 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Rd. Millum
