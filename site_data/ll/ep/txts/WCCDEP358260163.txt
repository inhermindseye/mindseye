Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Joel Curtis who is ill and his Two Children Maketh Oath and Saith That he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Joel Curtis about Fourteen Years ago Intermarried with Ann his late Wife who has been dead about Eighteen Years Months at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone by whom the said Joel Curtis hath Two Children living Vizt. Ann aged about Thirteen years and Mary Jane aged about Eleven years And this Deponent saith that he hath been informed that the said Joel Curtis Rented and lived in a house in Little George Street Portman Square in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for about Two years at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds that he hath quitted said house about Twelve Months since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1796 N Bond Rupt Clarke B Newman
