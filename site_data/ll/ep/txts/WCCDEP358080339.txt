Middlesex to wit Mary Lawrence Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Mr. Edwards a Brandy Merchant in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. Olave in the Boreage of Southwark in the County of Surry for Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds at Quitted her said Service in August last since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further says that she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the afsd Parish of St. Olave which hath since been baptized by the Name of Charles and has Two Shillings a Week allowed towards his Maintenance.
