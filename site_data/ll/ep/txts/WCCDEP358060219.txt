Westmr.  Ann Phelps on behalf of Catherine Tysom upon Oath saith she was well acquainted with John Tysom the father of the said Catherine Tysom and further saith that the said John Tysom lived in and Rented a House in Coventry Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards and lived in the same three months & upwards as she hath been Informed and verily beleives Says after the sd. John Tysom quitted the said house he never rented a house of Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office to her knowledge or beleif Says the said Catherine Tysom never rented ten pounds a Year was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 6th 1751 before  Tho Lane .

Ann Phelps
