Middlx  Ann Atkins on behalf of her Sister Alice Peach upon her Oath saith she hath been Informed and beleives the sd. Alice Peach inter- married with her late husband Wm. Peach in the Parish Church of S James Westmr. about 25 Yrs. since and that the sd. Wm Peach has been dead about Seven Years and she hath been further informed and verily beleives that the sd. Wm. Peach about twenty Years ago lived in and rented a house in Broad St Giles's in the Parish of Saint Giles in the feilds in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £30 for one Year and upwards and never after he quitted the said house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office Says her sd. Sister Alice Peach has not since the Decease of her said late husband

Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examints. knowledge or beleif and that the sd. Alice Peach is at present in a very ill State of Health and unable to maintain herself

Sworn 23d. June 1749 before H: Frilding Gabriel Finace

The mark of Ann Atkins
