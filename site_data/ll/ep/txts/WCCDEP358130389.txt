Middx.  Eliz: Edwards aged abt. twenty nine years upon Oath says she is the Wife of James Edwards who is a Prisioner in Goal for debt by whom she hath one Child Danl . aged 4 yrs. says her said husbd. before his Marriage & whilst a single Man lived a hired yearly Servt. with Wm. Nixon at the Dolphin in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for one yr. & upwards at the yearly Wages of six Pounds & hath quitted sd. service abt. Eleven Years & never after Rented Ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a yrly. Servant

Sworn the 6th of Augst. 1757 before}
