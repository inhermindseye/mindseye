Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Thomas Wood aged about 47 years upon his Oath saith That he never was married That he lived as a hired yearly Servant at the Coach and Horses in Bear Yard in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Fifteen Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that he hath quitted said Service upwards of Thirty years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon his Oath saith That he hath Two illegitimate Children living by one Mary Parker Vizt. Thomas aged about Ten years born at the a house No. 19 in Vere Street and John aged about Seven years born at the house of a Mr. Young No. 20 in Vere Street

Sworn this 26th. day of Octr. 1798 before us } W Carter Edwd: Naish

his Thos. Wood Mark

Witness B Newman
