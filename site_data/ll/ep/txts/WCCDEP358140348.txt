Middx.  Martha Aylmer aged upwards of 50 Years upon Oath says she is the wife of Jno. Aylmer who is now at sea as she has been informed and verily believes and that she intermarried with him in the Parish Church of St. Botolph Bishopsgate London abt. Twelve Years since. Says her said Husband informed her and which Information She verily believes to be true that before their said Marriage and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Lee a Taylor in Whistlers Court  Cannon Street in the Parish of St. Swithin London Stone in the City of London for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds And that he never Rented Ten pounds a Year or Paid Parish Taxes to her knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 13th. day of Feby: 1760 . before}
