Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Pike aged about 42 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Watson in New Street Square in the Parish of Saint Brides alias Bridget in the City of London for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that She hath quitted said Service about Sixteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement of Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th- day of March 1796 M Addington Rd Thompson

her Jane Pike mark

Witness B Newman
