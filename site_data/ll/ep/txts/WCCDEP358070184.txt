Westmr. to wit Elizth. Lawson Aged about fifty Nine Years on behalf of William Adamson an Infant aged about Six Years whose Father John Adamson has deserted him on her Oath Says that the said John Adamson Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry in the Year 1765 as appears to this Examinant from a Copy  of the Register of the said Parish and further Says that the said John Adamson the Father served seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Woodfall a Printer in Pater Roster Row in the Parish of Saint Saith London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn this 1st. day of April 1774 before} R. Buyler

Eliz Lawson
