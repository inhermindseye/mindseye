Westmr.  Cathr Whittle upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Mathew Whittle who Served his time at Whisterns to he near Ludlow in the County of Salop with Mr Horrick a Taylor as she has been Informed and believes and that the said Mathias Whittle never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement In any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt 5th. 1749 before H Frilding

The mark of Cathr. Whittle
