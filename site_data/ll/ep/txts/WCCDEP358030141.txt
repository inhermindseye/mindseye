Middx  Moses Carr upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Sarah Carr about 24 Years since at the Fleet London by whom he hath 2 Children Mary aged about Sixteen & Saml. aged about two Years Says his Father John Carr  served his time with a Mason in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place and that he this Examinant never was an Apprentice rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif Says he was removed with his sd. Wife & three Children from the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark by an Order under the hands & Seales of two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the County of Surry Dated the 4th. Day of Novr. 1737 which Order was not appealed agt.

Sworn May 3d. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Moses Carr
