Middlesex to wit John Collins Aged about Thirty Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant to one John Blackman a Farmer at Clapham in the Parish of Clapham in the County of Surry for Seven Years (and who he now works for) at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds besides Diet and Lodging hath left his Service under that hiring for about Two Years but hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th. day of Janry 1779 before  John Croft

his John Collins Mark

Saint Mary Lambeth Surry

John Collins of the Parish of Clapham Batchelor and Mary Bird of this Parish Spinster were Married in this Church by Banns the 15th. Day of Febry 1779 as appears by the Register of Marriages belonging to this Parish from when says the above is extracted

Lambeth Febry 15th. 1779 }

By me Thos. Pearce
