Middlesex ss  Sarah Oldis the widow of Thomas Oldis deced maketh oath that her sd late husband is lately dead as She has been informed & believes That her sd husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound to & Served his Apprenticeship with Richard Betsworth a Waterman in Maudlins Rents in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & to her knowledge or belief hath not Since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That about three Years & half ago her said late husband was Pressed on board his Majesties Ship the Inno Frigate & has ever Since Continued at Sea to the time of his death as she this Deponent Verily believes She this Depont not having Seen him nor has he had any Access to her Person for near the Space of two Years now last past That She this Deponent was delivered of a Male Child on the Seventh day of June last in the Work house belonging to the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the sd County Since baptized & registred there by the name John Oldis And that one John Eustace of the Said Parish of St George Hanover Square Coach Carver is the Father of the Said Child Which is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason that she this Depont was never Married to the sd Eustace & lastly this Depont Saith that this Examination is Voluntary

Sworn this 13th day of Decemr 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Oldis
