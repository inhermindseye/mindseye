Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Hazard the widow of Thomas Hazard  mariner deced maketh oath That about Thirty Years ago She was married to her said late Husband at the Fleet That her Said late husband was a Foreigner & used the Sea & never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in Great Britain That she this Depon was born in Swan Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year & never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gaine Settlement for herself otherwise than What she might have Acquired by Marriage with her Said late husband whose Settlement is unknown to her & this Deponent can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her said late Father or Mother other than that her said Mother has informed her that her said Father belonged to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 3d day of October 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Hazard
