Westmr.  Elizabeth Bates Aged upwards of Seventy Years, upon Oath says she is the Widow of Humphry Bates who has been dead about Eighteen Years says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Key who keeps a Bottle Warehouse in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Fourteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds, and after she Quitted said Service never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 28th, day of June 1768 , before} Thos: Kynaston

The Mark of Elizth. Bates
