Middx  Wm. Magee upon Oath saith he intermarried with his wife  Eliz Magee at the Fleet London about Eleven years ago and that about 12 Years since he rented a house in Off Alley York Buildings in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. of the yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds and lived therein seven years and has not since he quitted the said house rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 23d. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

William. Magee
