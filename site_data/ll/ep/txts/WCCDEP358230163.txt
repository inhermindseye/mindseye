Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susan Edwards aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly servant for 14 Months with Mrs Mitchell No 8 Southampton Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster at the yearly Wages of five Guineas that she hath left the said servant about five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for 12 Months or done any act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of Novr 1800 before us} Saml Edwards  John Collick

Her Susan Edwards Mark
