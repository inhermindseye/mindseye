Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Thomas Smith aged about 56 years upon his Oath saith that he served Seven years Apprenticeship to John Smith who lived upon the Narrow Wall near Cupers bridge who in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have Gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Septr. 1796  P. Henderson J. Hind

Thos Smith
