Middlesex & Westminster }  Sarah Masters Aged about thirty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of William Masters (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St: Andrew Holborn about Nine Years ago and by whom she hath three Children now living Namely William Aged about Six Years Sarah Aged about five Years and Elizabeth Aged about one Month Says that her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr: Hall a Button Maker in three Fox Court  Long Lane in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre in the City of London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th: day of Septr: 1770 before us} R Butler

The Mark of Sarah Masters

Witness Will: Hughes

St. Sepulcres Appealed to this Order wch. was Consented to by this Parish being Passed to St. Sepulcres  London & that Part of the Parish wch. the Pauper shod. have been passed to lying in the Co. of Middlesex
