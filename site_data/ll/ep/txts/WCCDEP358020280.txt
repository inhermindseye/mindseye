St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster (to wit) Mary Burdin  Singlewoman Aged 40 years or thereabouts on Oath saith that she lived never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr John Shipman at the Wheat Sheaf in Holywell Street in the said parish of St Clement Danes in the Said County and Liberty for about three Years at the Annual Wages of £5 that when her said Master Died that she after continued in the Service of her Mistress at the same Wages about Eighteen Months and quitted that she left her said Mistress about 18 Years ago and since hath not (by any way or means) acquired any legal Settlement And that She is poor and Unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 6th.May 1791 before Me R Butler

Mary Burdin 's Mark
