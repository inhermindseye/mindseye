Westmr.  Mary Patridge Aged about 30 Years on her Oath saith she never was Married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Knightly Esqr. at Bury in the Parish of Off Church in the County of Warwick upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of £4. hath quitted her said Service about 4 Years ago & hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one year And further on her Oath says that on the 17th. day of May she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Peakin the Parish Nurse in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized Priscilla Patridge And that one John Crossland a Gentlemans Servant but where he lives this Examinant cannot tell & no or. Person is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 14th Day of June 1771 before}
