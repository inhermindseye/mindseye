Middlesex to wit Frances Spencer Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servt . with Mr. Dring at the Feathers Tavern in the Strand upwards of one Year and an half at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about 12 Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 20th. day of Novembr. last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish which hath since been baptized by the name of Elizabeth and is now Chargable to the said Parish And further Says that Jeremiah Taylor did beget such Bastard Child on her Body and that the said Jeremiah Taylor is the only true and lawfull Father thereof.

Sworn this 22d. day of July 1778 before me R Butler

her Frances Spencer Mark
