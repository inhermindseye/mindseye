Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Wilcox maketh oath that on or about the twenty eighth of October one thousand Seven hundred and eighty three She this Deponent was duly bound Apprentice by the then Parish officers of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County to William Curnock then and yet of the Parish of Christchurch in the Sd County Baker untill She Should Attain her Age of twenty one Years or marriage That after this Deponent having Served her said Master there under the said Indenture for about the space of eight months Differences arising between her and her mistress her said Master and mistress against this Deponents Consent turned her away and would not any longer permit her to live with them and She has never since returned to them That although since her leaving her said Masters Service She has been hired & lived as a hired Servant in four different places yet She never lived in any one place for the space of a year

Sworn this 21. day of June 1788 before}
