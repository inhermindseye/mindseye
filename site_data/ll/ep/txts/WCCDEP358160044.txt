Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Davis  Singlewoman aged about 45 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 14th. Day of April 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. 30 Years ago she was bound Apprentice to one Mrs. Mary Gillingham  Glover in Bartholomew Close in the Parish of St. Bartholomew the Great  London & Served her as an Apprentice for the space of Seven Years And this Examint. further saith that she hath not since she left her said Mistress Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & that she is now poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Tho Lane  Jas Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. Davis
