Westmr to wit Margaret Farrell Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of James Farrell (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at Duke Street Chapel in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about five Years ago Says that her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Patrick Smith a Cowkeeper at Battle Bridge in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the sd County of Middlesex for Two Years or Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted his said Service about five Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes.

Sworn this 26th. day of January 1771 before} W Fielding John Cox

Margaret Farell
