Middx  Elizth. Moulson aged abt. 18 Years Removed  from the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars by Order under the bands and Seals of two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx. to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the same County Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that she is Pregnant  with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born says that Alexr. Ramsey a Wheelwright (who lodges at his Fathers Mr. Ramsey a Pawn Broker in Baldwins Gardens and works in Cow lane Smithfield ) had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body two several times in the Month of May last at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said Alexr. Ramsey and no other Person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn this 3d. of Octr. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz. Moulson

Upon Mr Barber's Camplt. J: P:
