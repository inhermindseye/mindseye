Middx  Lucy Green aged upwds. of 17 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant and that her late father Rd. Green (as she hath been informed & believes) Served his Apprentiship with a Stone Cutter in the Parish of Burntwood in the County of Essex and never Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr. 30th. 1752 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Lucy Green
