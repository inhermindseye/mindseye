Westmr.  Jane Graham aged about 21 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Fordham at the Feathers Alehouse in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds ten Shillings for the first Year and five Pounds for the Second Year quitted said Service about two Years and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year. And further upon Oath says that on 22nd, day of April 1766 she was delivered of a Male bastard Child in Mr. Baynes's Rents in St. Clements Lane aforesaid which hath since been baptized Thomas And that Thomas Keys who is the reputed Father of the said Child is gone abroad as a Soldier in the Service of the East India Company.

Sworn the 4th, of April 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Jane Graham
