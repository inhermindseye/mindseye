Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann White aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Baby of Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £6 hath quitted said Service about 10 Months ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any other Settlement And further this Examt. saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish And further saith that one Isaac Madement who lately lived as a Servant with Mr. Shephard a Brewer in Stanhope Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometimes in the Month of May last and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that the said Isaac Madement is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Novr. 1786 } R Butler

her Ann White mark

Warrt. made out of Maidment Child died at Enfield Money due for Exps.
