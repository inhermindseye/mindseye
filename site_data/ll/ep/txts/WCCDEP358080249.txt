Middx to wit  Mary Briscoe Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Hancorn at the Craven head in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Oath Says that on the 16th. day of May last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish which hath since been baptized by the name of James and is now Chargeable to the said Parish And further Says that one Thomas Edwards a Journeyman Taylor (who lodges at a Taylors in Stuarts Rents Drury Lane & who uses the Sign of the Kings Head in Bear Street Leicester Fields ) is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in Augt. last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd.

Sworn this 9th. day of June 1778 before}

her Mary Briscoe Mark
