Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Richard Dobbins one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster on behalf of Ann Hunt an Infant aged about Five Years Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Ann Hunt was born at the Sign of the Kings head i Saint Giles's in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 18th. day of July 1796 M Addington J Robinson

Richard Dobbins
