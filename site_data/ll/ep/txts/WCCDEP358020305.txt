St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) James Talboys one of the Beadles of the parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County & Liberty (on behalf of Sarah Smith Widow Aged 65 years or thereabouts now very) Jufirm) Maketh Oath and saith that he hath been informed and verily believes to be true that the said Sarah Smith intermarried at the parish Church of St. George in the East in the said County of Middlesex about thirty years ago with her late Husband Wm. Smith who died about 20 years ago leaving no Issue by the Sarah Smith him Surviving that after his Decease being Single & not having any Child or Children she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Messrs Phillips & Small Upholsterers in Piccadilly  in the parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster near the same parish Church  Six Years and upwards at £7 & 10s Yearly Wages that she left the same about Twelve Years ago & that since she hath not (by any means whatever) to the best as her knowledge acquired any  legal Settlement

Sworn the 28th. June 1791 before Us R Butler D Walker

Games Talboys
