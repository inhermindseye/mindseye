Westmr  Mary Browne Aged about Thirty Six Years upon Oath Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Sert. with Mr. Hale in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx she being at that time a Widow and without Child or Children fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about One Year and hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year

Sworn the 20th of Decr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Browne
