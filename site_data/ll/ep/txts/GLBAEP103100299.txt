Middlesex ss}  William Williamson maketh oath That his Parents who are both Dead were Natives of North Britain and never did any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in England And this Deponent Saith that he was born in Glasgow in North Britain and that he never did any Act to gain a Settlement in that part of Great Britain Called England

Sworn this 48th day of September. 1770 before  Chris: Scott

Willm: Williamson
