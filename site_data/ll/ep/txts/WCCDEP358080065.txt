Middx & Westmr.}  Gabriel Long Aged about Thirty Two Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Bennetts Paul Wharf London in the Year One Thousand Seven hundred and Seventy Two and by whom he hath Two Children now living Namely John Aged upwards of Two Years and Thomas aged about Three Months And this Examinant further says that he rented and lived in a House in Silver Street Golden Square in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Eight Months at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same that quitted said House about Two Years and an half ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of June 1776 before  Wm Kitchiner J Hubbard

Gabl. Long
