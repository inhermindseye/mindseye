St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Elizabeth Carter  Singlewoman Aged 30 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she was bound an Apprentice to Miss Calton in Sherrard Street Golden Square in the parish of St James Westminster in the said County for 3 years with whom she served only one year of her said time Mistress & was then seduced & taken away that since then she has Rented & lived in an Apartment in Rupert Street in the said parish of St. James  and for three years & paid for the same (being ready Furnished) One Guniea Weekly Rent and that since she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to acquire any other legal settlement than as aforesaid in the said parish of St. James And that she is now in distressed Circumstances & unable to maintain herself And lastly that she was in the Workhouse belonging to the sd. parish of St James for a fortnight & hath been out only about the space of one Month

Sworn the 23d Augst. 1791 before us }

(I.T.) (never Sworn)-(run away)

Elizth Carter 's Mark
