Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  Nathaniel Searl aged about 34 years upon his Oath saith that about Nine years ago he Intermarried with Jane his present Wife at Bow Church in Cheapside that he has Three Children living vizt. Sarah aged about Five years unprovided for Hannah aged about Three years and George aged about Two years both provided for That he this Examinant Rented and lived in a House on the New Wharf in the Precinct of White Fryars in the City of London for about Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Twelve Months since Which time he has not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Decr. 1788 R Butler D Walker

Nathl. Searl
