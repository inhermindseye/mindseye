Middlesex ss}  Charles Mayor maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to Richard Wharf of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in Westminster in the said County and Served the whole of his Apprenticeship there That he has not Since rented a house or Lodging of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement Since his said Apprenticeship

Sworn this 19. day of January 1788 before  Robt: Smith  R Davies

Charles Mayor
