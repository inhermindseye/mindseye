Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizth. Shill  aged of No. 35 Butcher Row on behalf of Two Children Vizt. Hannah Shill aged upwards of Five Years and Sarah Shill aged about Twenty two Months Maketh Oath and Saith that they are  the Legitimate Children of John Shill and Hannah Shill who were married at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn about Fifteen Years ago that the said John Shill before his Intermarriage with the said Hannah Shill lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Robinson No. 20 Lambs Conduit Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx for Twelve Months and upwards but what Wages he had she this Deponent doth not know that he hath quitted said Service about Fifteen Years ago since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenements or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 26th. day of Septr. 1797 before us N Bond R Clarke

her Ann Shill Mark
