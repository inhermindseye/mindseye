Middx  Elizabeth Allnutt aged about 35 Years Lodging in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married bound an Apprentice kept House or been a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father William Allnutt served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Giles Louder a Shoemaker in the Parish of Alverstock in the Town of Gosport in the County of Hants as she has been informed and which information she verily beleives to be true also Says he never Rented any House or paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate to this Examinants Knowledge

Sworn the 2d. day of October-1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth. Allnutt
