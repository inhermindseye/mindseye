Middx  Lucy Brown aged abt. 40 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that She was lived a yearly Servant to Mr Maxfield in Bennets Court in Drury Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes at the yearly Wages of Three pounds lived with him about two years and quitted her sd. Service upwds. of Twenty Years ago since which she has not rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Novr. 4th 1754 -before}
