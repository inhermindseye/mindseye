Middx.  Sarah Dawes aged abt. 36 Yrs. upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married; says that in the latter End of the Month of August or beginning of September 1755. she was deliver'd of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Hairbottle in Bennet's Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized Susannah ; says that one Samuel Farney a Master Shoemaker (who lived in Russell Court in the said Parish of St. Martin at the time the said Susannah was born) did beget the said female bastard Child on her Body of which she was deliver'd as aforesaid and that no other person did beget the same and further upon her Oath says the said Saml. Farney has been dead upwards of three years

Sworn this 13th. of Sepr. 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Sarah Dawes
