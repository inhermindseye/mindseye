Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Davis Aged about Twenty six Years, upon Oath says she never was Married, and that on the Eighth of September Instant she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. Powers in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx which hath been Baptized by the Name of John Baskerville Says that one John Baskerville an Auctioneer who lives in Stanhope Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes is the reputed Father of the said Male Bastard Child he having had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of December 1767, and at several times afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid, and that the said John Baskerville and no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 30th. day of September 1768 , before} 

Sarah Davis
