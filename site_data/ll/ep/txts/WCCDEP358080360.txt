Middx  Mary Neale aged about Twenty five Years on her Oaths says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Nichols a Green Grocer in Newgate Markett in the Parish of Saint Faith London for One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes on living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 3rd- Day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Mary Neale Mark
