Westmr.  Mary Dewberry aged abt. 27 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she was bound an Apprentice to and served seven Years Apprenticeship with Henry Gardener a Weaver in the Parish of Ewell in the County of Surry and never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 20th. day of November 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The Mark of Mary Dewberry
