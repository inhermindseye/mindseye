Middx  Richd Stevenson upon Oath saith he has a Wife named Sarah Stevenson . and that upwds. of 12 years ago (being then a Singleman ) he lived a yearly Servant with Mr Martin at Newberry in the County of Berks for two yrs. at the yearly wages of £3 and has not since rented ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn Febry 5th. 1754 before}
