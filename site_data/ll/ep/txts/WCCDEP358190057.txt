Middx.  Thos. Wright aged abt. 37 Years Upon Oath says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr John Perins a Distiller in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for One Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds for the first Year and after the Rate of Seven Pounds P Ann for the next half Year diet Washing & Lodging and quitted said Service abt. Midsummer 1741 and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn Octr. 10th. 1760  before} Fran: Bedwell 

Thomas Wright
