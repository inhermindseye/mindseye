Middlesex to wit Mary Ann Maddox Aged about 29 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Penny an Apothecary in Lamb Conduit Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for One year at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath Quitted her said Service about Seven years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement And further says that she has One Child Named John William Aged about 11 Months Born a Bastard in the House of Mr. Marshall at the Coach & Horses in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the said County of Middlesex

Sworn this 8th. day of r. 1782 before R Butler Sampn Wright

Mary Ann Maddox
