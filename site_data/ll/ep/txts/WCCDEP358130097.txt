Sarah Ryall aged about Twenty five Years upon Oath says She never was Married That Joshua Ryall her late Father deceased Rented at a House in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or thereabouts and lived in the same several Years and never after Rented any other House says that She never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant

Sworn the 18th. Decr. 1755 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Sarah Ryall
