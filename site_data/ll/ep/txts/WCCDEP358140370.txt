Middx  Elizth: Dove aged abt. 40 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Joseph Dove (gone from her) to whom she was Married at the fleet London abt. Ten Years ago and hath two Children by him Mary aged abt. Nine Years and James aged abt. Eight Years. says her said husband served 7 years Apprenticeship with Mr. Vell a Taylor at the Golden Ball in Pall Mall in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year.

Sworn this 12th. day of May 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Tho Lane .

The Mark of Eliz: Dove

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Barber Ovr. J: P:
