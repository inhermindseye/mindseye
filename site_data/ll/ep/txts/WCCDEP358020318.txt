St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Charlotte Hornsby Wife of William Hornsby on behalf of her Sister Camilla Henwick  Called Howard Howard now Insane and incapable of answering with certainty for herself Upon Oath Saith That the said Camilla Henwick otherwise Howard was bound by Indres of Apprenticeship by her Father's Consent to Misses Jane and Ann Priesthey in Coney Street in the parish of St Helen in the City of York for two years with whom under such  Indentures she duly served about one year and  an half and during all that time Slept and lodged in her Misses Mistress's Horne that she then left her said Mistresses being Ill and unable to Serve and hath never since done any Act Matter on thing whereby she might acquire any other legal settlement, that the said Camilla hath Informed this Deponent hath been  informed which she believes to be true that she the said Camilla was Married or United somewhere in France to Howard long since deceased according to the Ceremony of the Church of Rome And this Deponent further saith that her said sister is Insane and unfit to be Permitted to so abroad and incapable of supporting herself

 Sworn the 22d. day of July 1791 before us }

Charlotte Harnsby
