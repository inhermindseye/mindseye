Jane Watson upon her Oath saith she is the wife of John Watson by whom She has one Child John aged abt. 9 months & that she has been informed & verily believes the sd. John Watson was born at Shelfield in the County of Stafford & she can give no further Accot. of his Settlemt.

Sworn May 17th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Jane Watson

The above J. W's husband is a Taylor & has an unable a Farrier in Stroud he works wth. a Blacksmith there & has so done for a considerable time & has a Wife Family & lodges in an Alley opposite the shop near Stroud Bridge
