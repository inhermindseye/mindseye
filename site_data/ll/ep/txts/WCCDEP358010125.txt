Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Susannah Petherick aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1773 she Intermarried with Walter Petherick her present Husband at the Parish Church of St. Brides alias Bridgett in the City of London and by whom she hath one Child living vizt. Susanh. aged about 14 years That her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 2 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £40 and upwards that he hath quitted said House about 11 years ago since which time he has not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted and refuses to maintain her for 4 weeks last past & that she this Ext. is poor & unable to maintain herself & Child is therefore Obliged to Apply to the Parish of St. Clement Danes for Relief

Sworn this 7th. Augt. 1787 before R Butler

Susah Petherick
