Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

passd. to. White Fryars. to wit Susannah Sarjeant aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that she was married to her late Husband Wm. Sar at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the field in the year 1764 that her said Husband has been dead about five Months by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Ann aged about two years that her said Husband was served seven years Apprenticeship to one Charles Bodle Jeweller in the Glass House Alley Temple Street in the Precinct of White Fryars in the City of London after Quitting his said Master he hath done no Act Matter or thing when he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 26th- day of Octr. 1785  Sampr Wright J Addington

Susannah Sarjent
