Middx 

Eliz. Punnett upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Augustin Punnett with whom she Intermarryed at the Parish Church of St. Maga in London abt. 30 Yrs. ago Says her sd. late husband about 16 Years ago lived in and rented a house of thirteen pounds a Year in Dukes Lane in the Parish of Richmond in the County of Surry for five Years & upwds. and that since they Quitted the sd. house her sd. late husband or this Examinant since his Decease have not rented a house of Ten pounds a Yr or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn 23 Day of Novr. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Eliza Punnett
