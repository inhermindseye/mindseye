Middlesex ss  Sarah Plimmer the widow of James Plimmer deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her and which She believes to be true that he heretofore was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mr John Fellows a Victualler in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for divers years & did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge or belief rent a house Tenement or other Lodging of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or do any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Servitude as above nor hath She this Deponent Since his death lived as a hired Servant for a year or been married or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that she was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this 30. day of June 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Plimmer
