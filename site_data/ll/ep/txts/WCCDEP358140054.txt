Middx.  Richd. Archer aged abt. 50 Years lodging in St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath says he Rented a house in the Strand in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for several Years at the Yearly  Rent of Twenty Pounds and hath quitted same abt. twelve Months & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says he has a Wife named Sarah aged abt. fifty four Years

Sworn the 24th. day of Novr. 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Richard Archer
