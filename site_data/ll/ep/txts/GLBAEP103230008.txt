Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Cosgrave of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Widow taken on oath before me one of his majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this twentieth day of January in the year four Lord 1787 -who Saith that She is now with Child and that she said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that Joseph Gould of Virginna Street in the Parish of St George in the said County Cooper is the Father of the said Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me  R Davies

Ann Cosgrave
