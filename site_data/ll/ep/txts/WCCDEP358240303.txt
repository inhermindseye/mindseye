St Clement Danes August 9th 1793

Westminster and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Ann Tooms  singlewoman Aged 57 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Henwood at  St Clement's Coffee House in the Church yard Yard in the said parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for a year and upwards at the annual Wages of twelve Guineas that she quitted that Service about twenty four years ago and hath not since obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the day 9th day of August 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Ann Tooms 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
