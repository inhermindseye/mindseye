Westmr: 

Mary Armes (removed from the Parish of St. Brides London by Order dated the 31st. of Mary last) upon Oath saith she was never married Says she Contracted with Mr. John Brooks of St Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr.  Gilder to live with him for a Year Certain at the Wages of four Shillings a Week Meat Drink Washing & Lodging & lived with the sd. Mr. Brooks under that Agreemt. in Clemts. Lane afore sd. upwds. of four Years Says she has not since she left the sd. Mr. Brooks Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 2d. 1747 before  Tho: Burdus

Mary Armes
