Middx & Westmr. }  Elizth. Wood Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Says she intermarried with George Wood her Husband now gone to America at the Parish Church of Lime house on the 26th. of September 1774 by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said Husband was bound an Apprenticeship to one Mr. Lodge a Scowier in Fenchurch Street London and Served Six Years or thereabouts the first part of his said Apprenticeship and afterwards was Assigned Over to one Mr. Lawrence a Scowier in Somerset Street White Chapel in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate London and served the remainder of his said Apprenticeship which was upwards of Eleven Months as her said Husband has of ten informed her and which she believes to be true and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House or Tenemt. of Ten pounds P Ann. paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant or orwise gained any or Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 20th. of April 1776 before  John Chetwode  Sampn Wright

her Elizth. Wood Mark
