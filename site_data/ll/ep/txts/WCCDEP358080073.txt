Middlesex & Westminster}  Jane Stepney Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father John Stepney was a Welshman and never gained any Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief Says she was born in Clements Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes as her Aunt has often Informed her and which Information the verily believes to be true And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant wth. Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Joseph Porter a Journeyman Shoemaker who lodges at Mrs. Edwards a Chandlers Shop in Clements Lane aforesaid is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the said Mrs. Edwards's House in Clements Lane afsd. upwards of Two Years ago and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as afsd. And that the said Joseph Porter and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 14th. day of Augt. 1776 before R Butler

her Jane Stepney Mark
