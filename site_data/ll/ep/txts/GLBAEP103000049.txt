Middlesex ss.  John Fleetwood maketh Oath that about Thirteen Years ago he lived in and Rented a House of Fourteen Pounds a Years in Flower De Luce Court in the Parish of Christ Church Spittlefields in the County of Middlesex and was charged to and paid Poors Rate and Kings Tax there, that he has not since he quitted the said House, Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds a Year or lived as a hired Servant by the Year or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of January 1789 before}
