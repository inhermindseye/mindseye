Middx ss.  Elizabeth Marshall  widow of George Marshal deced Maketh oath that her said late husband was a Mariner and brought up to the Sea But where his Place of legal settlement is this Depont. doth not nor ever was informed nor Can set forth and this Depont. farther saith that about thirty Eight years ago while she was sole & before Marriage she this Depont. lived as a hired servant by ye year at ye Yearly Wages of four Pounds with Mr Benjamin Lethelear of Dowgate Hill in the Parish of St Mary Bothan at Dowgate in the City of London and Continued in such service for the space of a year And saith that she has not since lived as a hired servant by the year Nor done any other Act to Acquire settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 13 day of February 1759 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Her Eliz Marshall Mark
