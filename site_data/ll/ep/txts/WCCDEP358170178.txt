Middx  Cath: Lucas  Wido . of Abraham Lucas upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her sd. late Husband Abra: Lucas on the 2d. Day of Octr. 1720 at the Parish Church of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . & that her sd. late Husband lived in & Rented a House over agt. St. Clements Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Five Pounds for the Space of Three Years & upwds. & hath left the same abt. Eight Months & hath been dead abt. Eight Weeks & further saith that her sd. late Husband never after in his Life rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes nor hath she since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife yt. her sd. late Husband hath left 2 Childn. vizt. Mary aged Eight Years & upwds. & Jane aged Six Years & upwds. & she being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne the 16th. of Novr. 1741 before} Jas. Gronous

Catern Lucas
