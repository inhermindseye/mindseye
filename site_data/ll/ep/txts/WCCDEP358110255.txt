Middx  Ann Evans aged near 20 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one year Rented ten Pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes and that she was born in Coleham in the Parish of Abbey Forehead at Shrewsbury in the County of Salop and cannot give any Account of the Settlement of her father or Mother

Sworn 4th. June 1753 before}
