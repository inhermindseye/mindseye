St Clement Danes Decr. 10th. 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Matthew Bethnal Green  (to wit) Sarah Gilbert Aged about fourteen years & upwds  Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Mason of of Old Nicol Street  Bethnal Green the parish of St Matthew Bethnal Green in the County of Middlesex  Silkweaver (by Indenture) and served as an Apprentice under such Indre for the space of about five Months and left her said Master in or about the Month of February last without his knowledge or consent and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement

Sworn the 10th day of Decr 1793 before us } Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Sarah Gilbert 's Mark

Removed by Mr. Talboy
