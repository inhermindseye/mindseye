Middlesex (toroit) The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Sherington  Singlewoman takenupon Oath before me one of his Majesty's Justices of the peace acting in and for the said County this 8th. day of May 1788

Saith that she is now pregnant with one or more Bastard Child or Children and which when Born is or are likely to become chargeable to the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex , And this Deponent Saith that William Wilkins of East Smithfield in the said parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate Butcher is the true and only Father of such Illegitimate Child or Children

Sworn the 8th. Day of May 1788 Before me  Robt Smith

Eliz Sherington
