Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Spittle  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds by & with Charles Glynes in Swan Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Victualler & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a Year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 1st. day of December 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Spittle
