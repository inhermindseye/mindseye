Middx & Westmr.}  Peter Day Aged about forty Eight Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried Dorothy his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstans in the East about Twenty Eight Years ago by whom he hath Two Sons and one Daughter now Provided for Says he Rented and lived in a House in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds hath quitted the same about Ten Years ago and after he quitted the same he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 8th. day of June 1770 before us} R. Butler

Peter Day
