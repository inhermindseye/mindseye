Westmr.  Ann Hathwell  Widow Aged about Seventy Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Hathwell who has been dead about Eight Months and with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about Thirty Years ago Says that her said late Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one William Taylor a Taylor in Bow Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster as appears by her said Husbands Indentures and as he has often Informed her and which Information she verily bels. to be true and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rent Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 15th. day of May 1771 before} Tho Lane

R Butler
