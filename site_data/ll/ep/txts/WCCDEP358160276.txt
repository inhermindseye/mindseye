Middx  The Examination of Rebecca the Wife of Michael Gilbert living in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 12th. Day of March 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. Six Years ago She intermarried with her sd. Husband Michl- Gilbert at the Hand & Pen by Fleet Ditch he being at that Time an Apprentice to one Mr. Faire in Fountaine Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes  Jeweller and had abt. two Years of his sd. Apprentiship to serve And further on her Oath saith that her sd. Husband abt. one Year before he was out of his sd. Apprentiship absconded from her Covent to France & this Examinant hath not seen him since that She never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Sick & Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
