Westmr. 

Ryce Price upon Oath saith he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Six pounds in Bond Street in the parish of St George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . and lived therein about four Years and hath quitted the same about 22 Yrs. and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes  or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 12 1750 before} Ja Croft

Ryce Price
