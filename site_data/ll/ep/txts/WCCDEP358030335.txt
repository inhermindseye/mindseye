Westmr. 

Elizth. Forster upon Oath saith she inter- -married with her husband Henry Forster at the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn London abt. twenty one Years since Says the sd. Henry Forster before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and which a Singleman lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Vickery a Corn chandler in Houghton Street Clare market  for  in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds for the Space of one Year and upwards as this Examinant has been informed and believes Says her sd. husband has not since he quitted his sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn February 2d. 1746 before  Wm. Jones
