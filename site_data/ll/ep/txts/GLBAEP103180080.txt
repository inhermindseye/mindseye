Middlesex ss}  Catherine Mitchell  Singlewoman maketh oath That her Father Nathaniel Mitchell was born in Parts beyond the Seas as She has been informed and believes & to her knowledge or belief he never did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself in any place in the Kingdom of Great Britain & this Depont Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Mother Mary Mitchell deced Save that She has informd this Depont that previous to her Marriage with this Deponts Sd father She had gained a Settlement by Servitude in the Parish of Saint George in the Said County That She this Deponent hath a Brother named Alexander Mitchell who is now absent at Sea & who was born in Farthing Fields in the Said Parish of St George & who to this Deponts knowledge or belief never was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right That this Deponents Sd Brother hath a wife named Elizabeth to Whom he was lawfully Married.

Sworn this 5 day of November 1778 before  Jno Sherwood  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Catherine Mitchell
