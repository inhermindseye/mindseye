Middx  Jno. Sandland upon Oath saith he lived a yearly Servant with Mr Heny. Brawys in Witch Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes at the yearly wages of Seven pounds for the space of five Years before his Marriage and upwds. of a year afterwd and that he has not since he quitted the sd. Service rented ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Novr. 6th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

John Sandland
