Westmr.  Elizabeth Kime aged about 30 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Geo: Kime who died in April 1765, says her said late husband was a Native of New York in America and never gained a Settlemt. in England to this Examinants knowledge or belief says before her said intermarriage and whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with George Wanley Bowers  Esqr . Councellor at Law in Red Lion Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds and never Rented house or paid Taxes says she has one Child by her said late husband to wit Mary aged about one Year

Sworn the 1st. day of February 1766 , before}

Elizabeth Kime
