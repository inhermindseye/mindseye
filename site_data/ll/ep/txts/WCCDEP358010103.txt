Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr} to wit Mary Cust aged about 71 years upon her Oath saith about 20 years ago she being a Singlewoman & having no Child or Children living she went to live a hired yearly Servant with Dr. Watts in Charles Street Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martin on the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for 4 years & upwards at the yearly Wages of 6 Guas that He hath quitted her said Service about 16 years since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1787  Sampr Wright  James Croft

her Mary Cust mark
