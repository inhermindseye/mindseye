Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Parr aged about 55 years upon her Oath saith That about Twenty three years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Parr her late Husband who has been dead about five years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in off Alley in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for about Eighteen Months at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Guineas that after since her said late Husbands Decease she this Examinant hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry. 1797 before us N Bond J Robinson

her Jane Parr Mark
