The Examination of Robt. Norris aged abt. Sixty Years who upon his Oath saith that he was passed From the Parish of Alhallows the Great  London by Order bearing Date the 24th- Day of April Instant taken the 27th. Day of April 1741 & that he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Mathew Fareless in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Charcoal Man for the Space of Two Years had for the First Years Wages Six Pounds & for the Second Year Eight Pounds he being at that Time a Singleman not having any Child or Children & hath been come away from the sd. Mathw. Fareless Service abt. Thirty Years & further upon his Oath saith yt. he hath not since that Time rented £10 a Yr- pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine himself

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Robt. Norris
