Westmr. to wit Mary Widdon Delose Aged about Twenty one Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes says he was bound an Apprentice  to and Served five Years the first part of her Apprenticeship to one Mrs. Short a Mantua Maker who lodged with one Mr. Pinder a Chairman in Great Russell Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsburry in the County of Middlesex and never was Assigned over to any other Person nor ever gained any subsequent Settlement by renting House paying Taxes or living a Yearly hired Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 1st. day of January 1774 before R. Butler W kelynge

 Mary Middon Declose her Mark Witness James Feltham
