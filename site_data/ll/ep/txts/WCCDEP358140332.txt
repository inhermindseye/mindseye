Middx.  Susa. Cockram aged abt. 44 Years upon Oath says she never was Married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servt . with Chas. Deeves  Esqr . in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Libty. of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for One yr. & Eight Months at the Yearly Wages of £6. and hath quitted said Service upwards of two Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn this 19th. day Decr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Susanna Cockram
