Middx  Robt. Thompson a Soldier in Major Stewarts Company in the 3d. Regimt. of Foot Guards upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with his Wife Ann at the Fleet London about 14 Yrs. since & hath two Children by her (to wit) Ann aged abt. 11 Yrs. & Mary aged abt. 7 Yrs. Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Six Years Apprentiship with Anthony Bailes in the Parish of Bishop Waremouth in Sunderland in the County of Durham  Shipwright Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochiall Office lived a Yearly Servt. whilst a Singleman for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 27. 1745 before  Wm. Jones

Robert Thompson
