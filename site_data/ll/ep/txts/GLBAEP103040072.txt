Middlesex, to wit, Catherine Macguire , Widow of Richard Macguire , maketh Oath and Saith, That about Forty Seven Years ago she was hired by and went to live as a Yearly Servant at certain Yearly Wages, with Timothy Pope , a Wheelwright, at the corner of the Horse Ferry Road , in the Parish of Saint John Westminster , in the County of Middlesex , and continued in such Service, under such hiring, for the space of Two Years or thereabouts That about Thirty Five Years ago she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , that her said late Husband informed this Examinant that he was born in the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middlesex aforesaid That being Poor and unable to provide for herself she hath applied to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex for relief, and hath been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 22 Day of December 1797 . before us R Davies Dan Williams

her Catherine Macguire Mark
