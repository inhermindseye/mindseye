Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Terrier the widow of Charles Terrier deced maketh oath that her said late husband at the time of his death was legally settled in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County And this Deponent saith that since her said husbands death that she hath not lived as a hired servant for a Year or been married or done any Act to gain a settlement for herself And that she was lawfully married to her said late husband

Sworn this. 19. day of February 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Eleanor Terrier
