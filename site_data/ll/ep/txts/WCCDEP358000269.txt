Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Mary Eaton aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Huxley in Fountain Court in the Strand and in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for 14 Month at the yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted said Service about twelvemonths ago since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath saith that on the 22d. day of Septr. 1785 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the House of a Mr. Kent Peter Street Westmr. in the Parish of St. John the Evangelist in the Liberty of Westmr. And further says that her said Child never gained any or. Settlement than by Birth That her said Child has been half baptized by the Name of Frances Eaton and that she this Examinant wishes to leave same to the Care of the Parish that she may go

Sworn this 10th day of April 1786 }

her Mary Eaton Mark
