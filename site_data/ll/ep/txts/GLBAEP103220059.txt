Middlesex  Susannah Bussell  widow of James Bussell deced maketh oath that her Said late husband heretofore rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in Mercers Row in the Parish of St Paul Shadwell in the Sd County and resided there on for more than the Space of a year and died there legally Settled That She this Deponent Since her Said husbands death hath been hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant by & with one with Mr Feffenmire in the Parish of Saint John Hackney in the sd County and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two years but having a Child by her Said late husband then living during the Whole of Such her Servitude She this Deponent as She is advised & believes did by no means gain any Settlement for herself in the said Parish of Saint John Hackney

Sworn this. 25. day of July 1786 before  Robt. Smith J. Hawley

Sush; Bussell
