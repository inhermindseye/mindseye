Middlesex ss  Sarah Hardrow  Single woman Maketh oath that divers years ago she was bound apprentice by Indenture to; and served her apprenticeship with; Elizabeth Brown in the Green Yard in the Parish of Saint John wapping in the County of Middlesex  Widow and by means thereof as she is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that she hath not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And this Deponent farther Saith that about Nine Months ago she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx aforesd. which hath been since Baptised and registred by the name of John Hall And that one John Hall a Soldier belonging to his Majesties first Regiment of Guards did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child.

Sworn this 12 day of September 1766 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Sarah Hardron Her Mark
