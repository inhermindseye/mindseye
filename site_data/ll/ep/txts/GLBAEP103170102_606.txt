Middlesex ss  Jane Moore  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Andrew Ponpard in Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service at Certain Wages for the Space of three years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for a year nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5. day of January 1778 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Jane Moore
