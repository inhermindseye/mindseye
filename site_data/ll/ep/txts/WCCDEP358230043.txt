Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr. to wit Ann Kelly aged about 32 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she Rented and lived in a house in Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Four Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Seventy Pounds that she hath quitted said house upwards of Two years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon he Oath saith that she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the House of Correction on the 21st. day of December now last past which hath since been Christened by the name of Ann Elizabeth

Sworn this 26th. day of Febry- 1799 before us  Saml Edwards Menter

her Ann Kelly mark

H Day Beadle
