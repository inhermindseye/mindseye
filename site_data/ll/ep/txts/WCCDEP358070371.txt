Middlesex to wit Sarah Stone Aged about Twenty Seven Years On her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a Covenanted Servant to learn the Business of a Book binder with one William Morless a Book binder in Bear Alley in the little Old Bailey in the Parish of St. Sepulchre London for three Years and after she was out of her time she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further says that on the 23d. day of December last she was delivered of Two Bastard Children in the House of one Mr. Gregory in Change Court Saint Clement Danes which area intended to be called John and Elizabeth And that one John Smelly a Journeyman Book binder and who now lived at Mr. Bells a Bookseller in the Strand is the Father of the said Children And that this Examinant hath gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 14th. day of May 1776 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her mark
