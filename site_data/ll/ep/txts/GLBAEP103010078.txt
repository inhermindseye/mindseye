Middlesex ss.  Bartholomew Connell Maketh Oath that about Eleven Months ago he rented a House of Twenty Five Pounds a Year in Manor Row in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex of Mr. Bryan of Parsons Street, and lived in the same about Five Months, that he has not since he quitted the said House rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement, that he has a Wife named Bridget to whom he was lawfully Married at the Parish Church of St. Mary Islington on or about the Twenty Seventh Day of September 1779 and Four Children the lawful issue by his said Wife Namely Christiani aged about Ten Years John aged about Six Years, Elizabeth aged about Four Years and Edward aged about Two years

Sworn this 26th Day of Novr. 1791 before  Rob Smith R Davies. Bartholomew Connell
