Middx to wit James Mead Aged about Forty Six years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Lambeth about four years ago And by whom He hath One Child now Living namely James aged about Three years both of whom are now in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Pancras and this Examinant further says that before this Intermarriage to his said Wife and whilst He was a Singleman He Lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Huggins at the Castle in Kentish Town for several years the first year at Three Pounds the Second at Three Pounds Ten Shillings the Third at four Pounds and the fourth year at five Pounds Hath quitted his said Service about Eighteen years ago since which time He never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or Living hired yearly Servant for One year

Sworn day of Octr. 1781 before R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

his James Mead Mark
