Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Cook aged about 59 years upon her Oath saith that about Forty years ago she Intermarried with Clement Cook her late Husband who has been dead about Thirty years at the parish Church of Saint Brides alias Bridget That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Holles Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Ten years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and upwards and that after he quitted said house he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Novr. 1796 } J Reeves

her Ann Cook Mark

Witness B Newman
