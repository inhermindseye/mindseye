St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Elizabeth Lee aged 56 year or thereabouts Wife of John Lee Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her said Husband John Lee at the parish Church of Saint Luke Middlesex about 36 years ago (by Banns) that he hath left her several years that she hath no Child or Children now living and this Examinant further saith that she hath been informed and verily believes that her said Husband while single and unmarried lived as an hired yearly servant with Messrs. Planton & Longcraft in the Strand near Temple Bar for about a year and an half that he quitted that service long before his intermarriage with this Examinant and never after obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 13th day of March 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

E Lee's C Mark
