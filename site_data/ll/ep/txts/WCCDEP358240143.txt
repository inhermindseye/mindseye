St Clement Danes August 28th 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Drusilla Lane  Widow aged 76 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband William Lane at the parish Church of Layton in Bedfordshire about 54 Years ago that since their said Marriage her said Husband her said Husband Rented and Resided in an House in New Inn Passage in the parish of St Clements Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the Co. of Middx for about Six Years at the Annual Rent of £14:9:0 or thereabouts that he quitted that House about 27 Years ago to the best of this Deponents knowledge and never after Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year paid parish Taxes served any parochial Office or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement that her said Husband Died about 18 Years ago and since is Decease this Deponent has Resided in a Lodging in Bath Row Cold Bath fields for which She hath not paid or agreed to pay more than 5 Guas a Year that she hath never since been Married nor obtained or become in titled to any settlement in her own Right nor otherwise than as aforesaid

Sworn the 28th Augst. 1792 before Me } R Butler

Drusilla Lane
