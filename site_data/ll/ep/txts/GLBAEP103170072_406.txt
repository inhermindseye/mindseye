Middlesex ss}  Mary Hawker  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with Adam Anderson in Churchyard Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County chandler at Certain Wages & continued in such service for the space of three years & by means thereof gained a leagal settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was was she ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain and Settlement.

Sworn this 14. day of April 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Hawker
