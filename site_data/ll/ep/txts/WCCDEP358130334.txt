Middx.  John Avey aged abt. 35 years upon Oath says he intermd. with Mary his prest. Wife at the fleet London abt. twelve years ago says he has three Childn. by his sd. wife (to wit) Lucretia aged Eleven yrs. Wm . aged four years & Sarah aged abt. two years says he was bound an Apprentice for seven years to Wm. Haslipp in the Hamlet of Poplar & Blackwell in the Parish of St. Dunstan at Stepney in the County of Middx & served him abt. one yr. & then was assigned over to Thos. Jetter in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of St. Danstan at Stepney & lived with him abt. three Qrs. of a year & then his sd. Master dyed & never was assigned over to any Person Rented ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or was a yearly Servt.

Sworn the 14th day of May: 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell  Theodr. Sydenham

John Avey
