Middx  Margery Bulmer Aged about 34 years on her Oath says she Intermarried with her Husband John Bulmer (who was lately Pressed & gone to Sea ) at the Parish of St. Catherines by the Tower in the year 1764 and by whom she hath one Son living Namely Walter aged about twelve Years Says her said Husband was bound an Apprentice for Seven years to one Mr. Wildshire a Tanner in Long Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry and served his said Master Six years the first Part of his Apprenticeship and was Afterwards Pressed & went to Sea and his said Master Received Wages, for the last year of his said Apprenticeship and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant or her said Son

Sworn this 5th. Day of Augt- 1778 before me R Butler Thos. Bishop

her Margery Bulmer Mark
