Middlesex ss  Tyler Sutton Maketh oath That Divers Years ago he was duly bound apprentice by Indre to, and Served the whole of his apprenticeship with, Robert Patterson in the Parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry  Pipe Maker and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont. Saith that Since his Said Apprenticeship he hath not Rented a house of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish offices not paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that he hath a wife named Mary and two Children his lawful Issue, to wit Joseph Aged four Years, and Richard Aged about a Year and half.

Sworn this day of April 1769 before}
