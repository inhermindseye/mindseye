Middx  Joanna Payne aged about 28 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Joseph Payne who has been Dead about a Fortnight to whom she was Married at St. Clemts: Church about Three Years since That her said Husband was (before his intermarriage and while he was a Single Man ) a hired Yearly Servant to Mr. Isaac Hayes at Georges Coffee house in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and lived with him as such upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Twenty pounds diet and Lodging and from thence intermarried as aforesaid That he never kept House or paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate That she hath One Child by her said Husband to wit Elizabeth aged about Twenty Months.

Sworn the 15th. day of Septemr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Joanna Payne
