Middx & Westmr:}  Jane Prauge Aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath Says She never was Married and that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Willoughby a Taylor in Blackmoor Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr. upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings quitted said Service about Six Years and soon afterwards went to live with the said Mr. Willoughby again as a hired yearly Servant and lived with him upwards of Three Years and a half at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds hath quitted said Service about Two Years and hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 4th Day of Janry 1769 Before me} Tho: Lane

Jane Prauge
