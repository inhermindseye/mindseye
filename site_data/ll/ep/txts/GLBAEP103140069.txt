Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Truman the widow of Benjamin Truman deced maketh oath that She has been Credibly informed & which information She believes to be true That her said late husband previous to this Deponents marriage & While a Single man was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Peter Paris in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Victualler & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Deponent farthr Saith that to her Knowledge or belief her said husband did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since her said husbands death done any act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 13th day of December 1774 before B Camper

Elizabeth Truman
