Westmr. to wit Ann Plunkett aged about fifty Three Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Richard Plunkett who has been dead about Twenty Four Years ago Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Messrs. James Haley & Michael Sandiford  Peruke makers in that Pa of Duke Street which is in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and five Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her Service about Twenty Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th day of Novr. 1772 before W Kelynge

Ann Plunkett
