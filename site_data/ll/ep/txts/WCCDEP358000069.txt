Saint Clement Danes June 28 1785

Passd. to St. Pancras  James Talboy some of tho Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex, and William Buckingham aged between Fourteen and Fifteen Years or thereabouts upon their Oaths severall say and first this Examinant James Talboy s for himself Saith That he has been informed by William Buckingham the Father of William Buckingham the other Examinant in this Examination mentioned that the said William Buckingham the father some time ago rented and lived in a House in George Street Battle Bridge in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for about three Years at the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds four Shillings, that he quitted the said House about ten Years ago, and since that time her not rented any other House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds or done any

other Act, Matter or Thing whereby to the best of the said William Buckingham (the Father) Knowledge Information or Belief he may have gained or acquired any other subsequent settlement Rent this Examinant William Buckingham (thereon) for himself saith that he Recollects his father living at Battle Bridge as aforesaid, that he this Examinant has never lived as an hired Yearly servant for one Year, served any Apprenticeship or done any Act Matter or Thing Whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have ever gained or acquired any Settlement in his own right.

Sworn this 28th. June 1785 before us  Jno. Wright R. Taylor

James Talboys William Buckingham
