Middlesex ss}  Carolina Bell the widow of Thomas Bell deced maketh oath That divers years ago her Said late husband renter a house of the Yearly rent of Ten pounds in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Rent & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards rent a house of the yealy rent of Ten pounds or upwards or Serve any parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She This deponent Since her Said husbands death was hired & lived as a hired servant the year by with Hans Tennis of the Precinct of Saint Catherine in the Said County Victualler to wit Middx at Certain wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of three years That at the time of Such hiring & during Such Servitude as aforesd She this Deponent had two Children namely Carolina now aged about ten years & Jeremiah Aged about Eight years & upwds the lawful Issue of this deponent by her Said late husband to whome She was lawfully married both of which Children are Still living & have done no Act to gain a Settlement for themselves That She this Deponent Since her Said husbands Death hath not rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself She this Deponent being informed & advised that by her hiring & Servitude in manner as aforesaid no Settlement was Acquired by her That She this Deponent hath a Child named Peter James Hedgeley aged about fourteen Months which Since her Said late husbands death was begotten on her Body & born in the Parish of St Saviour in Southwarke in the County of Surry & is illegitimate & Bastard & that one Peter Hedgley late of the Wington in the County of Surry  Painter is the father of the Said Child

Sworn this day of September 1776 before}

Note upon a farthr Exn of the above Pauper She declared that She was never Married to Thomas Bell & that both her Children Carolina  & Jeremiah Bell were both Bastards & were born in Greenwich & taken Care of by her Friends
