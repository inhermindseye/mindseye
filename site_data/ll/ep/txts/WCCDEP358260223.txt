Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Bartlett aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith that about 15 years ago she Intermarried with Edmund Bartlett her late Husband who has been dead about Fourteen Months at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Whitechapel by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Arthur aged about Thirteen years That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Chair Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and paid Taxes due for the same that her said late Husband Quitted said House about Four years after which time he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of October 1797 before me } W Carter

Margaret Bartlett

H Day Beadle
