St Clement Danes October 1st 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Elizabeth Taylor  singlewoman aged 27 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Thornhill in Vere Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the  County of Middlesex for upwards of one year and eleven Months at the Annual Wages of £3 that she quitted that service about 12 years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 1st day of October 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Taylor 's Mark

Witness E B
