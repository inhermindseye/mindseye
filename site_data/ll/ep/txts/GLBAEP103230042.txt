Middlesex ss  James Mills maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Panton a Taylor in Baileys Place in the Parish of St Botolph. without Aldgate in the said County and Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one yar & upwards That he has not since lived as alined Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere That Since Such Servitude as aforesaid he this Deponent took and rented a Lodging Consisting of two Rooms & a Kitchen in the Dwelling house of one Mrs Bull in Bull head Court in Newgate Street in the Parish of Christ Church in the City of London at the yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds and Furnished for such Lodging himself & Continued there for about the space of two years & this Depont afterwards rented another Lodging of one Mr Norman near Grey Fryars in Newgate Street aforesaid in the same Parish at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds & furnished such Lodging himself & Continued there for about five years and has not since Rented a house or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a anothr Settlement

Sworn this- 26. day of April 1787 before  R Davies  P Greene

The Mark of James Mills
