Westminster to wit William Roberts Aged about fifty Nine Years on his Oath Says he never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one James Lambourn a Butter Seller in St. Clements Church Yard in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Eleven Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Guineas hath quitted his said Service upwards of Ten Years ago and hath not Since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 10th. day of March 1772 before}
