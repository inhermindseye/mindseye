Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Thackwell aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Reeves in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service only Three weeks

Sworn this 29th. day of March 1798 before me} John Collick

her Mary Thackwell mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
