Westmr. 

Cathr. Furlong aged abt. 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about Seven Years ago she lived a Yearly Servt. with Edward Stanvers Esqr. in the Haymarket between Coventry Court and Panton Street in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in ye. Liberty of Westmr. at this Yearly Wages of five pounds for the Space of One Year and has not Since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and  that one Patrick Dawson (late a Sailor) did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the said Patrick Dawsey having had carnal knowledge of her body in the month of Augt. last and at several othertimes since

at one of which time he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Patrick Dawson and no other person is the trace and real father thereof and further upon her Oath saith she hath been informed and verily beleives the said Patrick Dawson dyed of the Spotted fever at Ports month about 8 weeks ago

Sworn May 4th. 1750 before}

The mark of Cathn. Furlong
