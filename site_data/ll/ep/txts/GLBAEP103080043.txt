Middx ss.  Judith Bowen the widow of John Bowen deced Maketh oath that her said late husband in his life time Rented a house in Oxford Street in the parish of St Mary Le Bone in the County of Middx and paid Twenty Pounds a year Rent for the same and lived in such house for about the space of Twenty Six Years And that he did not afterwards rent a house of Ten Pounds by the year or upwards Nor serve any Parish offices nor do any Act to this Deponts knowledge or belief to Acquire a subsequent settlemt Nor hath she this Deponent to her knowledge information or belief done any Act since her said husbands Death to Acquire or gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 27th. day of January 1759 before us  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

Judith Bowen
