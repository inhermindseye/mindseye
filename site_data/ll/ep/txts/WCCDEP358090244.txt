Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Peachy Aged about Twenty five years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or ever served any Apprenticeship says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Robinson an Actress near the Bedford Arms Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Pane Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas hath quitted her said service about Eighteen Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of Augt. 1781 before  Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Peachy Mark
