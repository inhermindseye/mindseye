Middx  Jno. Gayton upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his prest. Wife  Eliza. on or abt. the 20th: Day of April 1717 at the Parish Church of St. Sepulchers London Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Thomas Powell  Cork Cutter in Fee Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn London & hath been out of his time abt. 29 Yrs. Says he never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any person a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne June 6th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

Jno. Gayton
