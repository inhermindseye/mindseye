Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Any Suckmore  Singlewoman Aged 26 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mrs. Gunthorpe in Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Brides alias Bridgett in the City of London for 18 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £6..10..0 hath quitted said Service about 2 years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Oath saith that about 5 weeks ago she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Lally a Shoemakers in Vere Street Clare Markett

Sworn this 31st. day of Augt. 1786 J Girdler J Seroux

Anne Suckmore
