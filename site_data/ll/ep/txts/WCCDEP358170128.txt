Middx  Thos. Salmon aged abt. Fifty Years upon his Oath saith that he is a Native of Ireland & never gained any Settlemt. in England & that he served his Apprentisp with one Thos. Holland in the Parish of St. Andw . in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland & that after he was out of his Time he Continued to live with his sd. Master in the sd. Parish for one Yr. certain at the Wages of Four Shillings P week that since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 11th- of Septr- 1741 before} Cha: Egerton

Thos: Salmon
