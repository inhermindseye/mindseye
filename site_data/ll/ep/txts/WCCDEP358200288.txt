Middx.  Mary Davis aged about 22 Years upon Oath says she was born in the Parish of St. James in the City of Bristol in the County of Somerset and never Rented Ten Pounds by the Year, paid Taxes nor was ever an Apprentice or a Yearly hired Servant for one Year, says she can give no Account of the Settlement of her Father other than he was born somewhere in Wales but in what Parish or place this Examinant cannot tell says she has two Children (Vizt.) John aged upwards of Two Years and James aged about Nine Months

Sworn the 3. day of 1765. Before}

The Mark of Mary Davis
