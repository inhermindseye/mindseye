Middx  Ann Savage Aged about 22 Years upon Oath Says that on the third Day of March Last she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx which hath since been baptized by the Name of Susannah and is become chargeable to the said Parish and is likely so to continue Says that Robert Pacey a Butcher who Lodges in or near Clare Market did unlawfully beget the said Child on her Body he the said Robt. Pacey having Carnall knowledge of this Examinant Body in the Month of June last and at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Female Bastard Child she was delivered of as Female B aforesaid and that he the said Robt. Pacey and no Other Person is the true and real Father thereof.

Sworn the 3d Day of May 1763 before}
