Middlesex ss}  Ann Holdbrooke the widow of Henry Holdbrooke deced maketh oath that about fourteen Years ago She this Deponent was lawfully married to her Said late husband in the Church belonging to the Parish of St George in the Said County of Middx That her Said husband lately rented a house at the Yearly rent of Sixteen Guineas in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same and died in the Sd house legally Settled in the Sd Parish That she hath three Children & lawful Issue by her Sd husband now living namely Mary aged about thirteen Years Elizabeth aged about twelve Years & Margaret Jane Aged about five Years none of which Children have as Yet done any Act to gain a Settlement for themselves That her Said late husband previous to his Marriage with this Deponent had a wife named Sarah to whom he had been lawfully married as She this Deponent has been informed & believes & left behind him at the time of his death a Son by Such wife named Jonathan Holdbrooke aged about Seventeen Years who has done no Act to this Deponents Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this. 29. day of May 1786 before  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Ann Holdbrooke
