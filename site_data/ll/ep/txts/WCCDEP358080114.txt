Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Powell aged about Twenty Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Buff an Appothecary in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of One Year at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds where she now resides And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish (she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) Says that one James Pugh a Journeyman to her said Master is the Father thereof she having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some in the Month of June last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he get her with Child of the Child or Children of which she now pregnant as aforesaid and that the said James Pugh and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 3d day of January Feby. 1777 before  Sampn Wright

Ann Powell
