Middx  William Henry Peaain aged abt. 70 yrs. upon Oath says he served five years the last part of his Apprenticeship with Thos. Pink a Shoemaker in Damask Street in the Parish of St. Andrew in the City of Dublin in Ireland

Sworn the 20th day of June 1757 before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Wm. Henry Peaain
