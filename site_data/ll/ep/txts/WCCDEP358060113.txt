Middx & Westmr.  Mary Gollins upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Wm. Gollins (who absconds from her) and of whose Settlement she can give no Account and that she was born at Newport in Shropshire & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year on otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn May 27th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Gollins
