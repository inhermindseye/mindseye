Middx & Westmr.} (viz)  Eliz. Coleman aged about 27 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that on or about the 11th. of August 1746 she was hired to his with Mr. Basinton a Baker at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and lived with him and or that agreement till sometime in September August 1747 at his house in Shire Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex and then her said master removed to a house on the other Side of the said Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls and she continued with him under the same agreement upwards of Six Weeks (to the best of her knowledge) in the house be removed to and was then discharged his Service and has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant  for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or belief And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St.Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that William Shreef a Shoemaker did be get the said bastard Child.

or Children on her body that the sd Wm Shreef and this Examinant being formerly acquainted and not having seen such other for a considerable time she met him afterwards by Chance (to wit) in the month of January last and he proffer'd to get her a place for which Purpose she went into the City with him and he carryed her to a house in Darkhouse Lane  Thames Street and having Intoxicated her with Drink prevailed on her to lay with him there all Night and time he had carnal knowledge of her body twice at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that he the sd. Wm. Shreef and no other person is the true and real father thereof.

Sworn Septr. 20th 1749 before H Fielding

The Mark of Eliz. Coleman
