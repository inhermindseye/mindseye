Middx  Ann Waight upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Geo Waight by whom she has one Child now with by Mary aged abt. 6 months & that her sd. husband rented a house in Stoney Step Court in the Parish of St. Ann in Blackfryers London of the Years Rent of £14 lived there in abt. 7 months quitted the same upwds. of 11 yrs. ago & has not since rented Ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn Decr. 15th 1753 before Fran: Bedwell

Ann Waight
