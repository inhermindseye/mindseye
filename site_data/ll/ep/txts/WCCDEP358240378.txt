St Clement Danes Janry. 21st 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Precinct of the Savoy  (to wit) Sarah Goldfinch aged about forty Years Wife of Thomas Goldfinch (who is now from her) Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said Thomas Goldfinch at the parish Church of St Mary Lambeth in the County of Surrey on or about the eighth day of July in or about the year of our Lord 1780 or 1781 that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and resided in an Apartment at the House of Mr. Alexander in the Strand in the Precinct of the Savoy in the County of Middlesex for six Months or upwards at the Annual Rent of £32 that he quitted the same about four years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief that she hath one Child by her said Husband now with her namely Henry aged two years six Months & upwards that her said Husband left her about two years ago and she is unable to maintain herself & said Child without assistance

Sworn the 21st day of Janry. 1794 before us N Bond K Mathew

Sarah Golfinch
