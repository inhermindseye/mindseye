Westmr.  Mary Thornton upon Oath Saith she intermarried with her late Husband Richd. Thornton at the Fleet London about 30 Years ago & that the sd. Richd. Thornton rented a house of £17 by the Year in Belton Street in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx and lived therein sevl. Years and that neither her sd late husband nor this Examinant Since his Decease have since then quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Feb. 19th. 1752 before Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Thornton
