Westmr.  Hellena Laurikus Aged upwards of Thirty one Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Christopher Laurikus with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Fourteen Years ago, by whom she hath two Children now with her (to wit) Mark Anthony Aged about Six Years, and Christopher Aged about Four Years; says her said Husband has been dead about nine Months, and that her said Husband rented a House in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty six Pounds, lived therein about Two Years, Quitted same about Four Years before his Decease, and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes; Says she hath not since her said Husband's death Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 29th, day of June 1768 before} 

Hellena Laurikus
