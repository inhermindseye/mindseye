Middlesex ss  James Temple maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound apprentice to and served the whole thereof with Nathaniel Hattrell in the Parish of Saint Allhallows the Less in the City of London  Lighterman and has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlemt & hath a wife named Jane to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 31. day of March 1785 before J. Hawley R Davies

The Mark of James Temple
