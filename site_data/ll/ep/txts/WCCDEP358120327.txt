Middx  Eleanr. Meredith upon Oath saith she is the wido. of Richard Meredith who dyed upwds. of three yrs. since and that her sd. late husband informed her, he Served his apprentiship with Mr. Derwine a Timplate worker in White Cross Street  near the Seven Dials in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year which Information this Examint. believes to be true And that she has not Since the decease of her said late husband Rented ten pounds by the year Paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn April 17th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eleanr. Meredeth
