St. Clement Danes 27th June 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Andrew Holborn above the Bars (to wit) Mary Ditcher  singlewoman aged twenty Eight years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Mecher Oilman in Lamb Conduit Street in the parish of St Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex about two years at the Annual Wages of Three pounds That she quitted that Service about Fifteen years ago and hath not since (leaving her said Masters Service) done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And that she is Lame Poor & unable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn the 27th day of June 1792 , before us }

her Mary Ditcher Mark

this Woman got well & was not removed  but got a Situation & provided for herself }
