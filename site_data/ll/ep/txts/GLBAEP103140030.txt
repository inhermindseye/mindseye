Middlesex ss}  William Gouging maketh oath that Sometime ago he was duly bound Apprentice to James Morris of Cock Alley in the Liberty of Norton Falgate in the County of Middlesex  Clockmaker and Served Six Months of his Apprenticeship there & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the said Liberty & has not Since lived as a hired Servant or an Apprentice nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th day of March 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of William Gouging
