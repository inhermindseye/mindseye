Middx 

Mary Perkins widow of John Perkins who dyed about Three month since upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. Plate husband on the 28th. Oath 1735 at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn London and that he informed this Examinant he lived a yearly Servt. (who a Singleman ) with Mr. Grant a Haberdasher of Sma Wares in the Strand for 1 yr. & upwds. and she has been further informed the sd. Mr. Grant lived in that Part of the Strand which is opposite St. Clemt. Church Yard in the parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middlesex Says her sd. late husband never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Tax or otherwise gaine Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge beleif

Sworn 28th. April 1749 before} Wm Jones

Mary Perkins
