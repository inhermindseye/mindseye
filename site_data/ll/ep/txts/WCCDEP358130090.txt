Middx  Sarah Pounder Upon Oath Saith that about Ten Years ago She Intermarried with Thos. Pounder at the Fleet London and has two Childer by him (to wit) Susannah Aged about Seven Years and Richard Aged about Four Years Says her said Husband Informed her and which Information she believes to be true that he Served Seven years apprenticeship with Francis Ginn a Sawer in Snow Fields in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry Says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servt. whilst a Singleman

Sworn the 3d. Day of Decemr. 1755 . before} Theodr. Sydenham  Tho Lane

The Mark of Sarah Pounder
