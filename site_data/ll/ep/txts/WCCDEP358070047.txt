Westmr. to wit Mary Storey Aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. James Brooks a Coal Merchant in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service in May last and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant Child or Children which is or are likely to be born Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Po and unable to maintain the same when born Says that One Mark Thomas late a Footman to one Mr. Sh an Irish Gentlemen who ledged at her said Master House had Carnal knowledge of her Body on Tuesday the 21st. day of April last in the House of the said Mr. Brooks and in the House of Lord Dlaraines and several different times Afterwards at one of which times he get her with Child of the Child or Children which she is now pregnant and that the said Makath and no other person did beget the same and that he the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of December 1772 before me  Tho Kynaston

her Mary Storey Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
