Middx.  Eliz: Young aged abt. 54 yrs. upon Oath says she intermarried with her late husband Saml. young in the Parish Church of Stoke in the County of Hants on the 12th. day of Janry. 1786 to her now best remembrance says her sd. husband served his Apprenticeship of with one Mr. Stent a Carpenter in New Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. says she hath not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a yrly. Servt. for one year

Sworn the 13 14th of July 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Young
