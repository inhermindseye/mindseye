Westmr  Eleanor Mortimer aged abt. Thirty Five Years Upon Oath Says She is the Wife of Paul Mortimer with whom she Intermarried abt. Eighteen Years ago in May Fair Chapel hath one Child by him named Isabella Aged one Month Says her sd. Husband Rented a House in New Round Court in the Strand in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. Yearly Rent Twenty four pounds lived in the same upwards. of One Year quitted sd. House about Eight Years & hath not since Rented House or pd. Taxes Says her sd. Husband is now ill & going into an Hospitall for Cure

Sworn the 17th. of Octr. 1766 before} Paul Vaillant  Fran. Cary

Eleanor Mortimer
