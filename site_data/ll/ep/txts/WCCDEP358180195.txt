Middx  Charity Hawkins aged 22 Years & upwards upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & that on the 5th. Day of April last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Richmond in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx which was Baptized William & dyed when about a fortnight old and that William Tindall of the Parish of Egham in the County of Surry  Yeoman did begett the sd. Male bastard Child on her Body the sd. William Tindall & this Examinant cohabiting together as Man & Wife at his Lodgings in the Parish of Warwell in the County of Berks where the said William Tindall had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the latter End of June last & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that he the sd. Wm. Tindall & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne May 23d. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Charity Hawkins
