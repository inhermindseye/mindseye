Middx  Mary Botomly aged about thirty four Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow: of John Botomly who has been Dead near Eleven Years says her said late husband served Seven Years Apprentice with Mr Young Taylor in Gloucester Street near Queen Square in the Parish of St George the Martyr in the County of Middx never rented a house paid Land Tax or poor rate as She has been informed & verily believes

Sworn the 9th day of June 1762 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Botomly
