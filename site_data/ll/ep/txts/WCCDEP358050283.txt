Westmr. 

Wm. Yeo of the parish of St. Ann  Blackfryers London on behalf of his Sister Hester Yeo a Lanatick upon Oath says that Wm. Yeo Father of the said Hester Yeo rented a house & Land in the parish of Tring in the County of Hertford of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & lived in the same Twelve Years or there abouts & there dyed Says the sd. Hester Yeo never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 12th 1750 before} G . Fouace

William Yeo .

N.B. Hester Yes was put by her late father to Mrs. Cannon a Mantra maker for 24rs. 5 Gs. was given with her she lodged with her mistrass's mother ale that time & her father pd. for her Board & Lodging; Mrs. Groves her mistrass's mother lived the last 3 months at the hand & hammer upper End of long Acra The above E. Yao was passd. from There was  Mary Bon abt. 10 Months
