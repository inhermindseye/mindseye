St Clement Danes August 6th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  proper discon  Old Artillery Ground within the Liberty of his Majesty's Tower of London  to wit Sarah Exton otherwise Blackett aged fifty two years or thereabouts Upon Oath Saith that she served as an Articled or Covenant Servant for three years under an Agreement with Mrs Layeux in Gun Street in the Old Artillery Ground within the Liberty of his Majesty's Tower of London that since the expiration of such her Service she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 6th day of August 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner  Edwd: Naish

her Sarah Exton otherwise Blacket Mark

Wittness Benj Newman
