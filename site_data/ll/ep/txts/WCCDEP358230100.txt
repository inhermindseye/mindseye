Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Buttery aged about 33 years upon her Oath Saith that about the year 1788 she intermarried with her late Husband at the Parish Church of Embrieth in the County of York by whom she hath living Three Children Vizt. Elizabeth aged Six years Hannah aged Three years and Maria aged One year That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Gee Court in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for Six Months at the yearly rent of £26 that he hath left said house about 8 years Since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 6th day of Decr 1799 before us} Saml Edwards Menter

her Catharine Buttery Mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
