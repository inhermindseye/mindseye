Middx  Mary Barns  Wife of John Barns (absconded from her) a Vagrant taken up & Acting as a Vagrant in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Upon her Oath saith that abt. Seventeen Years ago she Intermarried with her sd. Husband John Barns at the Fleet  London that before her Intermarriage with the sd. John Barns & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Wm. Renn a Gardiner in the Parish of Dagenham in the County of Essex & for the Space of One Year & upwds. that she hath a Certificate from the Church Wardens & Overseers of the Poor of the sd. Parish of Dagenham acknowledging her sd. Husband & her to be Parishioners Dated the Eighteenth Day of March 1733 that  she hath Three Children (to witt) Elizabeth aged abt. 16 Yrs. Susannah aged abt. Five Years & Joseph aged abt. Three Yrs. & is not able to maintaine them.

Sworne the 30th. of Novr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Barns
