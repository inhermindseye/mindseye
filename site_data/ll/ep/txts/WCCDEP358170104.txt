Middx  Henry Jenkinson aged abt. Forty Three Years upon his Oath saith that he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. James War of the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Eight Years or thereabts- at the Yearly Wages of Fifteen Pounds & further saith that during the Two last Years of his sd. Service he lodged by the Artillery Ground in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex that he hath not since he left his sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworn the Sixth Day of August 1741 before}

Henry Jenkinson
