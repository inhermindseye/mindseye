Middx  John Rowman aged about Seventy Three Years on his Oath says he is a Widower and hath no Child or Children now living but what are now provided for says he served seven Years apprenticeship with one Peter Webb a Silk Dyer  facing Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 27th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

John Rowman
