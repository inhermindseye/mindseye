Middlesex ss}  Margaret Jolly the widow of Richard Jolly deced maketh oath that She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her said late husband That She this Deponent divers years ago & after her said husbands death rented a house of Six pounds a year in Rose & Crown Court in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & during such her Residence there in was charged to & paid the Kings Tax for the same & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there & that She has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere.

Sworn this. 1. day of May 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Margaret Jolly
