Middx  Ann Parker aged about 40 Years upon Oath saith she never married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Whiteman in Beaufort Buildings in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlx at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the two Years & upwards and quitted her sd. Service abt. Nine months ago & has not since rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn June 8th. 1751 before
