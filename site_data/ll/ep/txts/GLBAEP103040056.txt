Middlesex, to wit, The examination of Elizabeth Davies of the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Singlewoman taken or oath before me Rice Davies  Esqr . One of his Majestys Justices of the peace in and for the said County of Middlesex this twenty eighth day of October in the year of our Lord one thousand seven hundred and ninety seven

Who saith that on or about the twenty eighth day of August now last past in the Dwelling house of Judith Coleman situate in Greenwood Court Nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate aforesaid She was delivered of a female bastard Child, since baptized in the Parish Church of Saint Paul Cl Shadwell by the name of Judith And that Francis Emanuel now or late of the Parish of Saint George in the said County Mariner is the father of the said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the day and year first above written R Davies

E Davies her Mark
