Middlesex & Westminster }  Woman's settlement  2 Children Sarah & Ann (to wit) Ann Phillips  singlewoman aged 30 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never served any Apprenticeship nor lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve month together or otherwise obtained any  legal Settlement in her own right that she believes has been informed and verily believes that she was born in Lawful Wedlock that her Father Samuel Phillips was legally settled as a Parishioner at Lyminster in the County of Hereford  by renting a House there  And this Examint Upon her Oath further saith that on or about the 3rd day of April 1788 she was delivered of Female Bastard Child at the House Mr Stephens in Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath sa

Reputed Father} been  Named  Sarah and that on the 24 day of April 1791 she was delivered of another Female Bastard Child at the House of Mr Whit Crown Court in the said parish of Saint Clement Danes since Named Ann and further saith that one William Lewis by a trade a Shoemaker now a Soldier in the Middlesex Militia is the only real Father of the said Children of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he and no one else having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent Body sometime in or about the year 1783 and at Several times times since at some of which times he did beget the said Children thereon and that he and no one else is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn the 30th day of December 1793 before me} D Walker
