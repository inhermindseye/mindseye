Middx.  Ann Hind on the behalf of her Daughter Elizth. Currant a Lunatick aged abt. 37. Years upon Oath says that Thos. Currant deced the late husband of the said Elizabeth Currant served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years to Mr. Jones a Cutler the Corner of St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 27th. day of Decr. 1757 . before}
