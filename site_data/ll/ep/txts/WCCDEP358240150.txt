St Clement Danes September 6th. 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Elizabeth Hill Aged 45 Years or thereabouts Wife of John Hill Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her present Husband John Hill about 29 Years ago at the parish Church of Saint James Westminster (by License) that her said Husband Rented and resided in an House in Newcastle Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County of Middlesex about three Quarters half a Year at the Annual Rent of £20 that they quitted that House about Christmas last and her said Husband hath not since Rented any House, nor any Apartment of £10 a Year value paid any any parish Taxes served any parochial Office or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information a belief And this Deponent further saith that she hath six Children by her said Husband now living four of which are provided for & the other two are with this Deponent Namely Mary who was aged 10 Years in or about June last and George aged Eight in or about the Month of August last. That her said Husband is now in prison for Debt and that She is at present

Weak Ill and unable to maintain herself and Children without Assistance

Sworn the 6th day of Septr. 1792 before me } R Butler

E Hill
