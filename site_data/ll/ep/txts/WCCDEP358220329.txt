Westmr. to wit Mary Gemson Aged about Twenty one Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she served five Years Apprenticeship with one Thomas Lyon a Turrier in George Street in the Parish of Saint Leonard Foster in the Liberty of Saint Martin Le Grand and after she was out of her said Apprenticeship she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th. day of March Febry 1772 before me}  Thos. Bishop

Mary Gemson
