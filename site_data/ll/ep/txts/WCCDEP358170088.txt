Middx  Mary Spencer  Wido . of Thomas Spencer upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband before her Intermarriage with him & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Horn in the Parish of New Windsor in the County of Berks for the Space of two Years or thereabts- but under what Wages this Examinant knows not that her sd. Husband never rented any House pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or other- -wise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as her sd. Late Husband hath informed her and beleives

Sworne the 11th- Day of July 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Spencer
