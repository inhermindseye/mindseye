Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Joyce aged about 62 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wilkinson in Clare Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Eight Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Four Years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 29th. day of April 1799 before me } Saml. Edwards

her Elizabeth Joyce mark
