Middx ss  Sarah Rediford  Singleman Daughter of Adam Rediford by Mary his wife deced Maketh oath that her sd. Father hath often informed her that he duly served his apprenticeship to one Mr. Heather a Bookbinder in Creed Lane in the Parish of St. Gregory in the City of London and Continued with him there during all his apprenticeship which information this Dept believes to be true And this Depont. Says that she never was Married Nor an apprentice Nor a hired Servant by the Year Nor hath she gained any legal Settlemt. but in right of her sd. father as aforesd. to her knowledge or belief (Except ye place of her birth)

Sworn this 9th day of July 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  Boulton Mainwaring

The Mark of Sarah Rediford
