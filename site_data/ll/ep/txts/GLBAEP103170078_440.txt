Elizabeth Higgerson  Singlewoman maketh oath That She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or either of there Save that she has been informed by them & which She believes to be true that her Father was a Chesoture Man & her Mother a Horthamptonshire  Woman but the respective places where they were born they never informed her That She has been informed by her said Parents & which she believes to be true That She was born near the Hermitage Bridge in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County That she was never an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year nor was ever married nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 19 day of May 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

E Higgerson
