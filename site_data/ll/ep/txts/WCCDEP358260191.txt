Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Seaborn aged about 38 years upon his Oath saith that he never was married That he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Henwood at the Parish Church Saint Clements Coffee house in the Church Yard in the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Six Guineas That he hath quitted said Service about Eighteen Months Years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 9th day of March 1797 before me J Reeves Saml. Edward

his Jno. Seaborn Mark
