Middx  Easter Davis  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Geary at the White Lyon in Vear Street  Clare markett at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the Space of 1 Year & an half & left her sd. Service the beginning of December last and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that on the 3d. Day of September Instant she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child at Mrs. Alexanders in Witch Alley in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes which hath since been Baptized Richard & become Chargeable to the sd. Parish & that one Daniel Mckerty. did begett the sd. Child on her Body she at such time liveing a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Whelpley at the King of Bohemi of Head at Turnham Green where the sd. Dan. Mckerty . was a Waiter there became acquainted with her & had carnal knowledge of her Body in the beginning of Janry last & sevll. other times since at one of which times her got her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & that he the sd. Dan: Mckerty . & no other Person is the true & real Father thereof

Sworne 20th. Septr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones . C. Egerton

Easter Davis her Mark
