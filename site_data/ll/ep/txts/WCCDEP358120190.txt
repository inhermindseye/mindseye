Middx (to wit)  Tabitha Earle aged abt. 63. Years Upon Oath Says she is the Wido. of Jno: Earle deced Says she intermarried with the sd. Jno. Earle on the fourth day of Decr. 1742 at the Fleet London and that her sd. late Husband has been Dead abt. three Months Says on the fourteenth day of Febry. 1718 he was Licenced to keeps Public House in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes as appears by a Licence of that date now in her Custody Says she has been informed and believes the sd. House was upwards of Ten Pounds a Year and that after her sd. late Husband quitted that House he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or Served any Parochiall Office

Sworn the 9th. of Septr. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell Tabtha Earle
