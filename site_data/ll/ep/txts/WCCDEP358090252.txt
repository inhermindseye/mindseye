Middx to wit Rachael Middlemost Aged about Twenty Two years on her Oath says She is the Wife of James Middlemost gone from had with whom She Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster in the beginning of the year 1779 and by whom She hath One Child now Living namely John aged One Year and Upwards Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship to One Mr. Crispin a Silver Smith  the Corner of Panton Street Haymarkett in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster & after He was out of his said Apprenticeship He never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 10th. day of Septr. 1781 before  Abrm Baley M Addington

Rachel Middemost .
