Middx  Mathew Warwick upon Oath Saith he was married to his late wife Mary Warwick deced at St. Giles Church in Northampton upwds. of 26 Years ago by whom he has two Children Hannah aged 7 Years & William aged 14 Months and that he served Seven Years apprentiship with Christr. Painter a Butcher in Stony Stratford in the parish of St. Mary Maudlin in the County of Bucks and never rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Augt. 9th 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mathew Warwick
