Westmr. to wit Sarah Richards aged about Twenty Six years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Richard Richards (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at Shoreditch Church about Seven years ago and by whom she hath Three Children now living namely Sarah Frances Aged about five years Richard Aged about Two years and Hester Aged about Twelve Weeks Says her said Husband served upwards of five years the first part of his Apprenticeship to one Henry Slawson a Shoe- maker in Tothill Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westminster and after her said Husband had served the said Henry Slawson for the space of five years his Mother gave to his said Master Two Guineas for the remainder of his time which his said Master accepted of & delivered up his Indres accordingly as this Examinant has been Informed by his said Mother and which Information she verily believes to be true and after quitting his said Masters Service he never gained any or Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one year before Marriage.

Sworn this 6th. day of July 1778 before R Butler John Croft

her Sarah Richards Mark
