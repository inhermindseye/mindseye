Middlesex ss}  Mary Loyd the Wife of Nathaniel Loyd maketh oath That her said husband is lately gone away from her to places unknown & has left her in a Necessitons Condition and that she now wants relief That her Said husband about five years ago took & rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & lived therein for about the Space of Three Years & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her Sd husband has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of February 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

Mary Lloyd
