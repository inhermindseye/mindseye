Westmr.  Martha Knighton upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 12 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Captn. Crayden in Princes Square  Ratcliff Highway in the Parish of St. George in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for one Year and upwds. Says she has not since she left the said Captn. Craydens Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 12th. Febry 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Martha Knighton
