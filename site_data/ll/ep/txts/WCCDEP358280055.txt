Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Ann Leigh aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she has lived as an hired yearly Servant with Samuel Leigh in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £7. And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes That one John Couldwill who is a Servant to Mr. Snell who lives at the said Mr. Leigh's is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of Augst. last and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Couldwill is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 4th.- day of Janry. 1787 } R Butler

Ann Leigh
