Westmr to wit}  Ann Eggier Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly servant with Mr. John Christian a Jeweller in Leaden hall Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Ax in the City of London one year and half at the yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said service about Twelve Months and hath not since she quitted her said Service never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 21st. day of October 1769 before Us} R. Butler Petr Planck

Ann Eggier
