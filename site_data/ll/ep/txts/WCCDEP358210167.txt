Westmr.  Priscilla Gilburn aged about 57 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Jno. Gilburn who has been dead about Twenty five Years; says that being a Widow and without Child or Children, she lived a hired yearly Servant with Dr. Newton in Woods Close in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex , for two Years and Upwards at the yearly Wages of five Pounds; quitted said Service about twenty years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 13th, day, of June 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos. Kynaston

Priscillar Gilburn
