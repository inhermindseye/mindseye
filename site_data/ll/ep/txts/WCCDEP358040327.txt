Middx & Westmr.  Charlotte Gates aged abt. 20 Years upon Oath she never was married and never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year Says she is the Daughter of Thomas Gates (who is this Examinant beleives is gone into Foreign Parts) by Beersheba his Wife (deceased) Says her sd. father lived in and rented a house in Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards for several Years and never after he quitted the sd. house gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 27th. 1748 before } Wm: Jones

The mark of Charlotte Gates
