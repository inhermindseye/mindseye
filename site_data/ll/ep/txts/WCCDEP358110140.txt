Westmr.  Sarah Abram aged abt. 16 years upon Oath saith She never was married and that She is the Daughter of Willm. Abram by Frideswide his Wife both deceased that her sd. late Mother Frideswide (after the Decease of her father) Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £12 & upwards in Witch Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and lived there in upwds. of three months: quitted the Same abt. three Years ago and never afterwards Rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes and that She (this Examinant) never was an Apprenticed or yearly Servant

Sworn Janry 6th 1753 before}
