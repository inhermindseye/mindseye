Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Mills aged about 62 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a House in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Eight years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and upwards that he hath quitted said House about Fourteen years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of January 1797 before me } Saml. Edward .

his James Mills Mark

Witness B Newman
