Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Mercy Holloway of the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex  Singlewoman taken on oath before me John Sherwood - Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this - Eleventh day of March 1775 .

Who Saith That She is now With Child And that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that Thomas Boddy now or late of half Moon Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishopsgate London  Butcher is the Father of the Said Child and this Examinant Further Saith that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with Joseph Smith in Upper East Smithfield in the said parish of St Botolph without Aldgate  Butcher at Certain Yearly Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one Year & Four Months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Examinant has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or was ever Married nor hath done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Taken & Signed the day & Year above written before me  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Mercy Holloway
