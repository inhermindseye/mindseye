Westmr  Mary Read upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Thomas Read Deceased says her sd. late husbd. lived in and rented a house in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of £24 for fourteen years or thereabts. & left the same upwards of twenty Years since Says her sd. husband after he quitted the sd. house or this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Placed to her Knowledge or beleif says she has a Daughter by the sd. Thomas Read named mary aged about 15 Years

Sworn Augt. 21st. 1747 before} Wm. Jones

The mark of Mary Read
