Middlesex,  Swear to St. Clemts .  George Moore aged seventy one Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he rented a House in Sawyers Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of four Years at the Yearly Rent of £14. That he quitted the sd. House about  five Years ago, and since that time has not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he may have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. April 1785 before  Wm Kitchiner

Geo Moore
