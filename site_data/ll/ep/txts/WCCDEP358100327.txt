Middlesex and Westminister}  C Vide Exn. of Grace Carter  Noot to wit Christopher Lewis aged 40 years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he was born at Ratfine Farm in Amesbury Parish in Wiltshire That his Father had an Estate there, That his Father went to live at Baverstock in Wiltshire where he took a Farm of £80. a year, That his father lived there some Years that her stay'd with him 2 years or more at Basers took that he then came to London That he examind Ann his late Wife at the Parish Church of St. Giles Cripple gate about 24 years ago. that he had. At Children by her, one of whom is and living Vizt. Phoebe aged about 18 years or thereabouts, That She has never gain'd a Settlement, that she of herself is incasle of gaining a Settlement, That he never has done an Matter or Thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement since his living with his Father at Baverstock aforesaid

Sworn before within 3d. Januy 1785 . M Addington J Reading

Chrisr Lewis
