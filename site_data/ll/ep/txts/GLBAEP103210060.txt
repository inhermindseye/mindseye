Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Jennings  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indenture by the Parish officers of the Hamlet of Poplar & Blackwell in the sd County to John Nettleship of the Parish of St Ann in the sd County Milkman untill she shod Attain her Age of twenty one years or day of Marriage That after having served him for about the Space of two years there she was discharged by her said master from her said Apprenticeship and was at liberty to go wheresoever she pleased That she afterwards lived in different services as a hired servant but never Continued in any one place for the space of a year That she was never married nor never did any Act since leaving her sd Master to gain a settlement for herself to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this fourth day of Febry 1785 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Elizabeth Jennings
