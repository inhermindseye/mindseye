Middx ss  Elizabeth Stockwell  widow Maketh oath that about twenty Years ago her late husband her late husband Arthur Stockwell Deced lived with Thomas Moor Esqr. of the Parish of Kensington -in the County of middx her said husband being then a Single Man & continued in such service for the Space of Ten years at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds a Year & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. saith that Her sd husband to her knowledge information a belief did not gain any settlement subsequent to his servitude abovesaid having never rented a house of ten pounds a Year or upwards nor served any Parish offices nor done any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlement And this Depont. Farther saith that since her sd husbands Death she hath not lived as a hired servant by the year nor done any Act to her Knowledge information or belief to Acquire a settlemt. in her own right since her sd husbands Death

Sworn this 18th day of Novr. 1756 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

his Elizbt. Stockwell Mark
