Ann Scott aged abt. 19 Years upon Oath says she never was married says she hath been informed and which information she verily believes to be True that she is the Natural Daughter of Woolaston a Plumber and Glazier by Mary Scott since deceased and that this Examinant was born in the house of James Scott a Brazier in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. as she hath likewise been informed and verily believes

Sworn this 20th. day of May 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Scott
