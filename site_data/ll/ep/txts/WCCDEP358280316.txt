St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster } (to wit) Ann Trounce Aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she was delivered of male Bastard Child at the House of Mrs. Preston No. 4 in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on the thirteenth Day of April now last past which Child has since been Christened by the Names of John Henry and this Deponent further Saith that the said Child was John Simpson who is Servant to Mr. Eaton of No. 22 in Bedford Square in the County of Middlesex is the true and Real father of such Child at which she was so delivered as aforesaid the said John Simpson having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body several times in the Month of December 1788 and June and July 1789 and at other times at some or one of which times he did beget on this Deponents Body the Child of which she was so delivered a aforesaid. and that she is distressed and unable to provide for he said Child which must therefore become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 3rd. Day of March 1791 before me R Butler

Ann Trounce

Ann Trounce lives now

John Simpson was taken pd: £10:0:0 to Mr Robinson which he paid Mr. George Red . Exted.
