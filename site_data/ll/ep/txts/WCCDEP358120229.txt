Middx  Edmd. Groves upon Oath saith he was married to Eliz : his Wife at the parish Church of St Margaret Westmr. abt. 19 Years ago and that he Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds between Parsons Green and Fulham in the parish of Fulham  in the County of Middx lived therein about 16 Yrs. & hath left the same about 18 months; since which, he has not rented ten pounds by the Year nor pd. parish Taxes

Sworn Novr. 20th. 1754 before} Tho Lane .

Edmon Groves
