Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Hawley Aged about Forty four Years, upon Oath says she is the Widow of Caleb Hawley , who has been dead about Six Years, with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London, about Twenty seven Years ago, Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Hollister a Taylor in Serle Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and never Rented House or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the second day of May 1763, about Eight Months after her said Husbands decease, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. William Lewis a Waterman in Vine Street in the Parish of St. John the Evangelist in the City of Westmr. which hath been baptized by the Name of John , Says that the said William Lewis is the reputed Father of the said Male Bastard Child he having had carnal knowledge of her Body on the Second day of August 1762, and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid, and that the said William Lewis and no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 28th, day of July 1768 , before} 

The Mark of Mary Hawley
