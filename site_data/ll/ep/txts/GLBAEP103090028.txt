Mary Phillips  Widow of Jenkin Phillips deceased  maketh oath that she never was informed nor can set forth the place of Settlement of her Plate husband deced But this Deponent saith that she's been informed and verily believes the same to be true that her Mother Mary Palmer deceased was born in Lamb Lane in the Parish of East Greenwich in the County of Kent and also that she this Deponent was born in Lamb Lane in the parish aforesaid And this Deponent further saith that the cannot set forth the Settlement of her father John Palmer deceased nor hath this deponent ever lived as a Yearly servant or done any act to gain a Settlement subsequent to her birth

Sworn the 8th day of March 1766 before  Chris: Scott  John Smart

Mary Phillips Her Mark
