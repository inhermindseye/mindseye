Middlesex ss}  Rebecca Watson maketh oath that her late Father Robert Watson deced lived in & Rented a house in Burr Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement her sd Father dying in the sd house That she this Depont never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year in anyone Place nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 15. day of Novemr 1781 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Rebecca Watson
