Middx Richd- Charritt on the Behalfe of Elizth - the Wife of Wm- Hide upon his Oath saith yt. her Husband Wm. Hide abt. 4 Years ago rented a House in East Smithfeild in the Parish of St- Botolph witht. Aldgate in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of Six Years or thereabouts that her sd- Husband hath not since he left the sd. House Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place as this Examinant hath been informed & beleives that her sd. Husband now absconds from her & she being delirious & Poor is not capable of maintaining herselfe

Sworne the 21st. of July 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Richard Charritt
