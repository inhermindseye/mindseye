Westmr. to wit Ann Hooper Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Robert Hooper  gone from her with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Brides London in the Year 1770 Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Allen a Printer in Bolt Court Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Dunstand in the West London And that after her said Husband was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Parish Taxes or orwise gained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of February 1774 before W Kelyngs R Butler

Ann Hooper
