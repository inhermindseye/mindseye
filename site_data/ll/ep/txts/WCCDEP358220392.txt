Westmr. to wit Thomas Williams Aged about Thirty Four Years on her Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he was bound an Apprentice to one James Royle a Horse Girth maker in the Hatton Garden and lived with him upwards of five Years and then his said Master moved to Hogsdon in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex where he Continued to live with his said Master for Two Years the last part of his Apprenticeship and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 6th. Octr 1772 before} Thos. Kynaston  Sam Wright

his Thos. Williams Mark
