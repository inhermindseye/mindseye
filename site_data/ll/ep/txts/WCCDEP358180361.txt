Middx  Thomas Weekly aged about 60 Years upon his Oath saith his Father lived in & rented a house in Bare Yard in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of fourteen pounds for the Space of many Years & hath left the same about 40 Years Says he this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or other -wise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 3d. Augt. 1745 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Thos. Weekly
