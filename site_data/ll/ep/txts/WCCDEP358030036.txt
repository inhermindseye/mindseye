Middx  Mary Lycest aged about 62 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Dr. Dodd in Essex Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Nine pounds for the Space of Nine Years & upwards and hath left her said Service about Seventeen Years Says she hath not since she quitted the sd. Dr Dodds Service rented a house of ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 31st. Octr. 1745 before}

Mary Lycost
