Middx  Mary Cook aged 59 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Torbuck in Clare Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Book seller at the Yearly Wages of £5 for the Space of 2 Years & upwds. & hath left her sd. Service about Twelve Years. since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 7th. Decr. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

Mary Cook
