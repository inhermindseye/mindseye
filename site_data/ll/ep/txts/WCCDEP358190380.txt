Middx  Hannah Barnesley Aged about 29 Years Upon Oath Says she was Never Married that she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mr Steward who kept a Chandlers Shop the Corner of Salsbury Court Street in the Strand in the Parish of St Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Eight Weeks and Never Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 17th. Day of Septr 1763 before} Barth: Hammond  Thos: Balack

The Mark of Hannah Barnesley
