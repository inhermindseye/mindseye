St Clement Danes Decr 10th 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  Gatcombe parish in the Isle of Wight  (to wit) Ann Wife of John Hayman late Ann Fry  Spinster aged about 33 years Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said John Hayman on or about the 5th day of January 1781 at the parish Church of Newchurch in the Isle of Wight (by Banns) that previous thereto while she was single and unmarried she that is to say on or about the 25th day of December 1779 she at the House of Mr Westland Timothy Fry at place called Gatcombe in the parish of Gatcombe in the Isle of Wight she this Examinant was delivered of a Female Bastard Child named Hannah Fry and which said illegitimate Child she this Examinant being unable to maintain is become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes having not acquired any legal Settlement in her own Right

Sworn the tenth day of December 1793 before us } Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Ann Hayman 's Mark

Removed by R Dobbins Janry & delvd to Mr. Bull Overseer of Gatcombe a Miller on 22d Janry. 1794 The Examn. sworn at the Workhouse of St C D on or abt. 10th Decr. 1793 in the presence GG. who saw that & the Act as signed by the Magistrates
