St. Clement Danes March 20th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Benjamin Errington a lodger at the House of Mr Ellams in Ship Yard in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex aged 56 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife Elizabeth at the parish Church of St Leonard Shoreditch on or about the 29th. day of August 1779 that he hath no Child ren now living that he served his three years and a half or thereabouts as an Apprentice under Indentures to Mr William Rand  Peruke Maker in St Clement's Church Yard in the parish of St Clement Danes and lodged and boarded in his said Master's House during such Service that he left him by consent about the year 1756 and hath never since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year lived as an hired yearly Servant with or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge Information or belief

Sworn the 20th day of March 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Benjn. Errington
