Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Baldwin aged about 28 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired Yearly servant with Mr. Checke in White hart Yard in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for about Fifteen Months at the yearly wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 7th.. day of September now last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mrs. Clark in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the Name of John that one Joseph West who is now board the Ferret Ship of War is the only real and true father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Joseph West is the only real and true father thereof Man never taken Qy. in wher. any & what Allowance to the Woman.

Sworn this 3rd. day of March 1796  Jas Oldham 

her Elizabeth Baldwin Mark
