Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Edwards  Singlewoman and Susannah Rachael Edwards  Singlewoman Sister of the Said Elizabeth Edwards Severally make oath and first the Said Elizabeth Edwards for herself Saith That her Parents were both native of the Kingdom of Ireland & never did any Act to this Deponts. knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in the kingdom of Great Britain That this Deponents Said Sister Susannah Rachael Edwards was born in Black Horse Yard in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the Said County She this Deponent being present at her Birth and the Said Deponent Susannah Rachael Edwards for herself Saith that She never was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in her own right.

Sworn this 15. day of July 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey  Wm: Blackmore

The Mark of Elizabeth Edwards
