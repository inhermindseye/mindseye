Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wits John Mico Chappell on behalf of Wm. Mico Chappell his Son who lays ill upon his Oath saith that his said Son was on the 13th. day of July 1776 bound Apprentice to Thos. Crosby a Chimney Sweeper in Marigold Court in the parish of St. Martin in the fields for Nine years that his said Son served 3 years of his said Apprenticeship or thereabout that then h went away to Sea whereby he has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. Janry 1797 before green Jno Machin

Jno Mico Chappell
