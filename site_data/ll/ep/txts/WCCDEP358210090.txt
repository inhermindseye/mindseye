Westmr. (to wit) Margaret Yarwood aged about 41 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Yarwood (gone from her) with whom She intermarried at the fleet London about Nineteen Years ago and by whom she hath three Childn. now with her (Vizt.) Sarah aged abt. twelve years Martha aged about Six Years and Elizth. aged about four Years, Says her said husband hath often times informed her and which Information She verily belives to be true, that he was born the bottom of Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , and never rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to her knowledge and belief.

Sworn the 3d. Novr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Margt. Yarwood
