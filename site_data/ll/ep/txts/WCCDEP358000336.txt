Saint Clement Danes

Westminster Middx to wit Cath: Sexton aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England that she has two illegitimate Children Vizt Thos. Sexton aged about 2 years and a half Provided for and Philip Sexton and aged about 15 Months born in the Haymar Kett in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr. and further said that John Sexton to whom this Examinant was married at the Chapel in Duke Street by a Roman Catholic Priest according to the rites and Ceremonies of the Romish Church of England and who has been deced about Six Months is the father of the said Children and that she this Examint. wishes to leave same to the Care of the Parish

Sworn this 8th. day of Augt. 1786 M Aaddington N Bond

her Cath: Sexton Mark
