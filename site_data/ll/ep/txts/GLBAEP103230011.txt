Middlesex  Ann Wallgrove widow of Tobias Wallgrove deced maketh oath that her sd late husband heretofore rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County and died in the Workhouse there legally Settled in the Said Parish That She this Depont Since his death has not lived ago hired Servant for a year nor been married nor done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself and that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this 26 day of Janry 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Ann Wallgroove
