Westmr.  Wm. Hinton aged abt. 66 Yrs. upon Oath saith he Served Three Years & upwds. the last part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Corbett a Joyners in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says he had a Certificate bearing date the 23d. Day of April 1737 from the Church wardens & Overseers of the Poor of the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes to the Church wardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Putney in Surry since the Granting of which he has not rented ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 2d. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

William Hinton
