Westmr.  Thos. Timpson upon Oath saith he rented a house of Sixteen pounds a Yr. in Pump Court Water Lane in the Parish of St Ann Blackfryers and lived therein three months and has quitted the sd. house about Ten Years; since which he has not rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 28th 1749 before} Thos Lane

The Mark of Tho Timpson
