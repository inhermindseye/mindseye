Middx  Martha Cullimore upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Thomas Cullimore in the Parish Church of St Philip and Jacob in Bristol upwds. of Six Years ago by when she hath two Childn. Richard aged upwds. of three Years & Thomas aged about four Months and that she hath been informed and verily believes her sd. husband Served his Apprentiship with Cary Davis a Peruke maker in Wescot Street in the Parish of St. Peter and Paul at Bath in the County of Somerset and she can say nothing further of her sd. husbands Settlemt. with Certainty

Sworn March 2d. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Martha Cullimore
