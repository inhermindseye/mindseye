Aldgate Middx

Sarah Craft  wife of James Craft , Maketh Oath that her Said husband hath often told her that he Servd his Apprenticeship to Mr Rogers a Feltmaker in  Flower: de Lives Court in Tooley Street in the Parish of Saint Olave Southwark And that the Said Rogers now keeps an Ale house in the Said Court and that her Said husband is now in the Kings Service a broad (if living) that she hath been a pentioner to the Said Parish of Saint Olave hath two Children Martha aged Nine years and Edward Craft aged five years

Sworn 3d day of October 1748 before Richd. Ricards N. Dukinfield

the mark of Sarah Craft
