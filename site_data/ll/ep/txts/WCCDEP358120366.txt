Westmr. Middx  Jas. Hawtin upon Oath saith he Served his Apprentiship wth. John Ditcher a Cordwainer  in Salisbury Court in the Parish of St Brides  London and never rented ten Pounds by the year paid Parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year and that He has 2 Childn. by his late wife Frances (viz.) Hannah aged upwds. of 5yrs. & Eleanr . aged upwds. of 3 Yrs.

Sworn June 20th. 1755 before Barth: Hammond  Tho Lane .

James Hawtin
