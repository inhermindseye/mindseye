Middx ss.  Richd. Cooper maketh Oath ye about Eleven Years ago he was bound an Apprentice by Indre for 7 Years. to Mr. John Grudee a Taylor of ye Parish of st. Martin Vintry London and Served him as Such for about 3 Years and 4 Months and was afterwards turned over to Mr. Edward Marsh in Addle Street in the Pish of st Mary Alderman bury and with him Served out the remainder part of his Apprenticeship And this Depont. Sayth he hath Wife named Jane And that he hath never kept House on to his knowledge or believed over gained any Settlement otherwise than

by Servitude as aforesayd.

Sworn of 5th Day of Decr. before us.  Richd. Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

Richard Cooper
