Middx ss.  Michael Reboul Aged about Twenty Seven years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says that he was bound an Apprentice by the French Refugee Charity to one Benjamin Pickering a Button maker in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and served him under that Indenture for the space of four Months when his said Master run away since which time he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes living as a hired yearly or Covenanted Servant or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 12th. day of June 1778 . before}

Michael Reboul
