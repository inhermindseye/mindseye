Westmr  John Peacock upon Oath Saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Heny. Hall  Taylor in Sheir Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds Meat Drink Washing and Lodging Says he has not since he quitted his sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 4th. 1747 before Wm: Jones

The Mark of John Peacock
