Middx to wit Ann Flood aged about 19 Years on her Oath says she never was married rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with James Folla a Glass Seller in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of fifteen Mon at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service about two Years ago since which times she hath never lived as a Yearly hired Servant for One Year and further on her Voluntary Oath Says says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes (sh being poor & unable to maintain the same when been) and further says that one James Mathews a Fisherman who uses the Six Bells near the Church at Chiswick is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of he Body for nine Months last past one of which times he get her with Child of the Child or Children of which sh is now pregnant and that the said James Mathews and no other person did beget the same and that he with real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 2d. February 1782 before  Thos. Gilbert :

Ann Flood
