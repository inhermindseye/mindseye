Middx  Ann Stewart aged near 10 Years upon Oath saith she is the Daughter of George & Ann Stewart that she has been informed and believes she was born at Mr. Shorts a Peruke maker's in Rose Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and that her said Father George Stewart lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards in Vine Street in the sd. Parish of St. Martin in the Fields & quitted the same about two Years ago

Sworn May 25th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Stewart
