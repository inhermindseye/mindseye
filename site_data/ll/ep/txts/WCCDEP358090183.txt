Middx  to wit Sarah Yarwood Aged about 25 Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Holton in Downing Street in the Parish of Saint Margaretts Westminster for three year at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement And further says that on or about the 2nd. day of June-last She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Margaretts Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the name of Margarett She being poor & Humble to maintain herself and Child that the Parish of Saint Margaretts Westminster is the Parish to which She belongs

Sworn this 19th Day of December before R Butler M Addington

Sarah Yarwood
