Middx  Susa. Buckley aged abt. 29 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she never was married & that she lived a yearly Servant wth. Wm Flanders at the Feathers near Buckingham Gate in the Parish of St Geo Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings & has left that Service abt. 10 years since which she has not rented ten pounds a year pd. Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn April 25th. 1754 before}

The mark of Susa. Buckley
