Saint Clement Danes .

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Furnival aged about 49 years upon her Oath saith that about Eighteen years ago she Intermarried with Ralph Furnival her late. Husband who has been dead about Three Months at Haya Church in Brecknockshire that where her said Husband was born she this Deponent doth not know that previous to her Intermarriage with he said late Husband and whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Ricd Price in New Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Twenty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 13th. day of June 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Elizabeth Furnival Mark

18th. Jan: 1806 .

She said she did not live at Mr. Price's till after married. but that who lived at Mr. Secarb in Vere Street , 5 Years @ 8 Ga. while single & that it was the last place of her living as a Servant before marriage never removed
