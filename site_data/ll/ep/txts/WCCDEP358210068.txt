Middx & Westmr.}  Rachel Jones aged about 24 Years upon Oath says, she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Evan Jones a farmer in the Parish of Newtown in the County of Glamorgan in South Wales for one Year & Upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds quitted said Service about Seven Years ago and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says, she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the said County of Middx where she now lodges she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born; says that John Cokayne an Attorneys Clerk in Lyon's Inn had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants body some time in the Month of January 1766, and at several time, Since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said John Cokayne and no other person did beget the same.

Sworn the 13th. of Augst. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

Rachel Jones
