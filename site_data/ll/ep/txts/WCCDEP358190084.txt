Middx.  Hannah Pickett aged abt. 29 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true that her Father  Henry Pickett Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Thos. Davis a Taylor in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx and never Rented Ten Pounds by the Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief and further says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for One Year nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn Jany. the 1761. before}
