Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Jane Dixon of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Single woman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justies of Peace in and for the said County of Middlesex this Day of June 1770.

Who upon her Oath Saith That upon the Seventh Day of May last in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate she this Examinant was delivered of a Male Bastard Child and that the said Male Bastard Child likely to become chargeable to the said Parish of saint Botolph without Aldgate and that Samuel Rutter of the Parish of Hampstead in the said County of Middlesex Baker did get her with child of the Said Bastard Child which child hath been since Baptized and Registred by the name of James Dixon

Sworn this 15th Day of June 1770 before  Chris: Scott

Jane Dixon Her Mark
