St. Clement Danes January 23d 1793

Middlesex and Westminster  St C D  (to wit) Mary Parry  singlewoman aged 56 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Douglas in the Strand No. 237 for two years and a quarter at the Annual Wages of £6 or thereabouts that she quitted that Service about 14 years ago and hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelvemonth together Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or done other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 23d. day of January 1793 before Me} Wm. Kitchiner

Mary Parry
