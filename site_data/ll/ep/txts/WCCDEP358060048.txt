Middx  Mary Jordan aged abt. 54 Yrs. Wido . of Henry Jordan upon Oath saith that about 5 Years and Six months ago (being at that time a Widow and having only one Daughter living, by her sd. late husband, who was then married) she was hired to Mr. Mannaton at the Crown & Anchor Tavern in the Strand in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and lived with him as a Yearly Servt . for the Space of 3 Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds and that she hath not since she left her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 12th. 1750 before  Tho Lane .

The mark of Mary Jordan
