Middx  Mary Well aged about 20 upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Joseph Mathews  Yeoman in the parish of High Easton in the County of Essex at the Yearly Wages of One pound fifteen Shillings for the Space of One Year & hath left her sd. Service 2 Years & upwards since which time she hath not Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one Walter Taylor a Taylor  did begett the sd Child or Children on her Body she this Examinant being a Servt. to Mrs. Hannah Smith in the Playhouse Passage going into Bridges Street & the sd. Walter Taylor being acquainted with this Examinant had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the latter End of July or beginning of Augt. last in the house of the sd. Mrs. Smith & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with

which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Walter Taylor & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 5th. March 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Wele
