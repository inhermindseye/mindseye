Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Frances Bennett aged about 19 years upon her Oath Saith that on or about the Ninth day of February 1796 she Intermarried with Thomas Bennett her present husband (who is now from her) at the Parish of Christ Church Newgate Street by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Thomas aged about Ten Months That her said Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to Thomas Bennett his father the last Twelve Months of his said Apprenticeship he Slelpt at No. 4 Cobham Row in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex us she hath been inforemed and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 14th. day of Septr. 1797 before us} Rupt: Clarke  Edwd: Naish

Frances Bennett
