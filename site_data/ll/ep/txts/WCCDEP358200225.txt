Middx  James George Wilson of Howard Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes on Behalf of Ann Green an Infant Aged about Seven Years the Daughter of Wm. Green by Letitia his Wife both Deceaced upon Oath Says the said Childs Father Wm. Green who has since Died Served Seven Years Apprentiship with a Baker in the Parish of Watlington in the County of Oxford as this Examt hath been Informed & which Information he beleives to be true

Sworn the 24th of Jany 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox  Paul Vaillant

Jas. Geo: Wilson
