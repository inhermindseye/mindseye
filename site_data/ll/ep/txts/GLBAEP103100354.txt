Middlesex ss}  George Hawkesford a singleman Maketh oath That divers Years ago he was duly bound apprentice by Indre to Joseph Thornwaite of the Parish of St. Michael Queen in the City of London Taylor and served three Years of his Said Apprenticeship there and then by Mutual Consent between this Depont. & his Fathr. & his said Master the Sd. Indres. were delivered up in order to be Cancelled & discharged And this Depont. Saith that he has not since lived as an apprentice nor as a hired servant for a year nor rented a house of the yearly. Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor served any Parish office nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor he his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Settlement since his apprenticeship as above

Sworn this 29th day of January 1771 before  Chris Scott  Cha: Digby

George Hawkesford
