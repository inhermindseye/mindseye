Middlesex, to wit,  Thomas Gibson of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Beadle , maketh Oath, That he has been informed that Michael Kelly and Judith his Wife were lawfully married, and that the said Michael Kelly was born in Ireland , and never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That he has also been informed that the said Michael Kelly deserted his said Wife for several Years, and that she cohabited with one Baker, and that during such absence the said Judith Kelly had one Daughter called by the Name of Sarah Baker , and that the said Sarah Baker was born in Long Alley , Moorfields , in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex , And further saith, that he has been informed by the said Sarah Baker that she can gives no Account whatever of the Settlement of her said Mother, and that she the said Sarah Baker was never bound an Apprentice, lived as a Yearly Servant with any Person or Persons for the space of one Year together, or did any other Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right And this Deponent further saith, that the said Sarah Baker being Poor and unable to provide for herself hath applied

applied to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate Aforesaid for relief, and that she hath been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 2st Day of March 1798 before us  Danl Williams R Davies

Thomas Gibson
