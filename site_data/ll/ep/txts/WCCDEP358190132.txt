Middx  Eleanor Ross aged about 50 Years Lodging in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the wife of Hugh Ross a Soldier who is now at Scotland to whom she was Married at the Fleet about Fourteen Years ago That her said Husband is by Trade a Carpenter and was Born and Served his Apprenticeship in Rossteen in Scotland

Sworn the day of May 1761 Before}
