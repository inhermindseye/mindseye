Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Frith aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly servant with one Miss Barry in Great Orchard Row Camberwell in the Parish of Camberwell in the County of Surry for Fourteen Months at the Yearly wages of Four Pounds that she hath quitted said service about Ten years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Novr. 1796 N Bond W Kinnard Ann Frith
