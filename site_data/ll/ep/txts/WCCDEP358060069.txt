Middx  Mary Evans aged abt. 31 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wife of David Evans with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London about two Years ago and that her sd. husband went from her about 4 months ago and she knows not where he now is and that she can give no other Account of his Settlement than that she hath heard him say he Served his time with his father a Shoemaker somewhere in Wales but in what part she can't remember Says her Late father Joseph Marshall deceased rented a house of £15 by the Year in Carter Lane in the Parish of St. Bennet Pauls wharf London and lived therein upwards of one Year and there dyed and this Examint. never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant and further Saith that on the 9th. of January last she was found wandering in Labour in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and was brought to the house of one of the Parish Nurses of the sd. Parish where she was on the same Day Delivered of a female Child which has been baptized Mary

Sworn Feby. 9th. 1750 before  Tho Lane .

Mary Evans
