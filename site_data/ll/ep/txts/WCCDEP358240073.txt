St. Clement Danes 2d March 1792.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD.  (to wit) Sarah Williams  Widow Aged 36 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Evan Williams at the parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields on the 3d February 1782 (by Banns) that Subsequent thereof her said late Husband Rented & Resided in an Apartment at No:2 in Burleigh Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Middlesex abt. Nine Months at the older Annual Rent of £11 that they quitted that Lodging on or about the 25th. day of March 1791. since which her said Husband did not in his Life time neither hath She this Deponent by Marriage or otherwise since his Decease obtained any other Subsequent legal Settlement That her said Husband Died on or about the 9th. day of January last

That she hath Four Children by her said late Husband now living Namely William Born on the 25th. April 1783. Sarah Born on the 18th of November 1784 June Born on the 10th of November 1788 and Mary Ann Born on the 4th. day of October 1791.

That her said Husband left at the time of his Death that he Informed he this Deponent which she verity believes to be true one other Child Child was named And Aged Eighteen Years on the 10th. October last the Daughter of Ann Williams a former Wife of this Deponents said Husband & legally Married to him

Sworn this 2d. Day of March 1792 before Me} R Butler

Sarah Williams
