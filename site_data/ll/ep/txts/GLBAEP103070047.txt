Middx ss  Judith Eadinson  Single Woman Maketh oath that about fifteen Years ago she was bound apprentice to one John Anthony Hart a Childs Pump Maker who then lived in Bell Alley in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx with whom she lived in the sd. Parish of St. Luke for the Space of about four Years and then she left her sd. Masters Service with his Consent And Saith that she was Never a hired Servt. by the Year Nor gained any Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief since her sd. apprenticeship

Sworn this 30 day of October 1751 before us  Richd Ricards  John Lawton

his Judith Eadinson Mark
