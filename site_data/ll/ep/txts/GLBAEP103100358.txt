Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Leggett of the p of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd. County of Midd Single woman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace and for the sd. County of Middlesex this Day of 1771

Who on her oath Saith That she is now with Child And t the Child or Children where with she now goeth & is Preg is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to b Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate that Joseph Nathan of the sd. Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate  Labourer is the Father of the sd. Child or Children
