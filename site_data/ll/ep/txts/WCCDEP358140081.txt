Middx  Margt. Winstanley of Chandor's Street Covent Garden upon Oath says that she was well acquainted with the father & Mother of Thos. Buerdsell an Infant aged abt. Eight Years says he was born in the house of Mrs. Jennings in the Old Bailey in the Parish of St. Sepulchre's London & that Thomas Buerdsell the father of the said Infant Rented a house in Golden lane in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of ten Pounds & upwards as she has been informed & verily believes

Sworn this 30th. day of Janry 1758 . before} Theodr. Sydenham  Tho Lane .

Margaret Winstanley
