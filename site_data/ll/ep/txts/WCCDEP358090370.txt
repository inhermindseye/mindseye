Middx  Ann Miller Aged about 27 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or pd. Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servt . with one John Harrison  Esqr . at Norton Place in the Parish of Norton near Lincoln for one year at the Yearly Wages of £6.6s.0d hath quitted her sd. Service about 2 Years ago since which time she hath  never lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year And further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born And further Says that one John Rice late Servt . to one Colonel Cobour near Grooms Hill near Greenwich and who now lives at Mr. Rutherfords in Howard Street & How the Cheshire Cheese in Surry Street Strand is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her late Masters House in Cavendish Square  some time in the Month of July last and at Sevl. different times aftwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the sd. John Rice and no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 1st. day of Feby. 1783 before,  Wm Kitchiner

Ann Miller
