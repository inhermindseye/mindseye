Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Treacy aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Spice who kept the Sign of the Kings head in Compton Street in the parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for near Three years at the yearly Wages of Five pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Twenty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Janry. 1797 } Robinson R. Ford

Mary Treacy
