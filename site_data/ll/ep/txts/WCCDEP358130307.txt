Middx  Mary Marnock aged abt. 29 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of James Marnock (now imprest into his Majestys Service) with whom she intermarried at May fair Chapel abt. nine years since, says she hath two Children by her sd. Husband (to wit). Marg . aged upwards of two yrs. & Sarah aged abt. two Months, & that her said Husbd. Rented a House of Nineteen Pounds a yr. in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx lived therein abt. six Months & quitted same abt. eight Yrs. ago, says her said Husbd. never after he quitted said House Rented Ten Pounds a yr. or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 17th. of March 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Marnock
