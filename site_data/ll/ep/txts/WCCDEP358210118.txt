Westmr.  Mary Berry aged about 37 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that on the 18th, day of September 1760, she was delivered of a female bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Best in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath been baptized by the name of Catherine and that on the 17th. day of May 1763, she was delivered of a Male bastard Child in the House of Mr. Chamberlain in St. Clements Lane in the aforementioned Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath been baptized by the Name of John Thomas and that Maynard Grendon who was the reputed Father of both the said bastard Children died about a Month ago-And further upon her Oath says that she this Examinant lived a hired Yearly Servant with Webster Esqr. in Great Marlborough Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, says she served the last nine Months of her said Service with her said Master in Great Marlborough Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . & hath quitted said Service about Ten Years, and hath not since Rented house, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 31st. of Decr. 1766 , before.} Tho Lane  Barth: Hammond
