Saint Clement Danes 15th. Octr. 1785

Middlesex 

He taken & a Security Bond given Child died & Settled (to wit) Elizabeth Harwood  singlewoman aged Twenty three years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was married, says, she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Pennington of the Crooked Billet in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of one year at the Wages of eight Guineas per Annum that she quitted her said Service about three Years ago and since that time has never to the best of her Knowledge gained any subsequent Settlements and further this Examinant on her Oath saith that she was on the third Day of September last part delivered of a female Bastard Child since Christened as the name of Margaret Harwood , at the lying in Hospital in Store street To Ches- -ham Court Road , and that John Moltre  Esquire the Son of Servant Moltre who lives at No. 51 Great Portland Street Oxford Road  and no one else in the real and true father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and be gotten this same thereon and that she this Examinant being  Unable to maintain herself and the said Child the Child be Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 15th. Octr. 1785 before J Addington

her Elizth. Harwood Mark
