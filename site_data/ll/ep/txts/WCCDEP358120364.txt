Middx  Rd. Arnold upon Oath saith he lived a yearly Servt with Mr. Moore at Bridgwater in the County of Somerset at the yearly wages of £4.4.0 dyet Lodging & washing for the Space of one year and upwds. during all which time he was a Singleman & with Child or Children & that he has not since he quitted his sd. Service rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parochial Taxes

Sworn June 20th 1755 before}
