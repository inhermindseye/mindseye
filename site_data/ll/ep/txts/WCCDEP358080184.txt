Middx & Westmr-}  Elizth- Kirby aged about 24 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Richd. Jones at the Spotted Dog in Strand Lane in the Parish of St- Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - upwards of ten twelve Months ago at the Yearly Wages of £3 hath quitted her said Service upwards of two Months Years ago since which time she hath gained no or- subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish she being. Poor and unable to maintain the same where born And further Says that one John Davis an Hostler to Mr- Hollingsworth a Horse Dealer in little Moor Fields is the Father thereof He having had Carnal Knowl of her Body sometime in April or May but the particular Day she does not now rember and at sevl- difft- times afterwds- at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that the said John Davis and no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 21st- Novr- 1777  David Walker

her Elizth- Kirby Mark
