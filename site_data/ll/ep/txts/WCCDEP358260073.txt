Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Sleeman aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born at Greenwich in the County of Kent That she hath Three illegitimate Children living Vizt. John aged about Six years born at the house of a Mr. Jewell No. 7. Poore Court in the Parish of Saint Olave in Southwark in the County of Surry Catherine aged about Four years born at the said house and William aged about Two years born at the house of a Mr. Willey in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 14th. day of Septr. 1795 M Addington Cha: Churchill

her Mary Sleeman mark

Witness Jas Talboys
