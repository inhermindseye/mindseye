Middlesex  Thomas Creek Maketh Oath and Saith that on or About the twelfth day of Novemr which was in the year of our Lord 1718 he was bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven years to Mr . Thomas Plumsted in Jerusalem Alley in Grace Church Street in the parish of Saint Bennedict Grace Church in the City of London and served All his said Apprentiship with his said Master there And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge and that he hath a wife named Elizabeth and A Child Named Henry aged about three Years.

Sworn this 9th: day of September 1743 before  Robert Dennett  Rd- Ricards

Thos Creek
