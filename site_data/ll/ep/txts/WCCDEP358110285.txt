Middx  Cathn. Gibbons upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of Garret Gibbons who at present absconds from her & by whom she has one Child Eleanr . aged near 6 Years & that herself & her sd. husband are both Natives of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England & her sd. husband Served his Apprentiship wth. Danl. Carty at Charlefield in the County of Cork in Ireland

Sworn Augt. 7th. 1753 before}

The mark of Cathn. Gibbons
