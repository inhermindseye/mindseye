Westmr.  Sarah Irish aged about 44 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Irish who is gone from her with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Midhurst in Sussex about Eighteen Years ago and by whom she has two Children Richd. aged about Seven Years and John aged about Ten Weeks, Says her said husband has often times informed her and which information she verily believes to be true, that he served four Years and upwards the first part of his Apprentice -ship with Mr. Mc. Kenzie a Snuff Box maker in Craven Buildings in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and then his Master failed, says her said husband never was Assigned over to any Person nor ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn the 4th, of April 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant

Seher Irish
