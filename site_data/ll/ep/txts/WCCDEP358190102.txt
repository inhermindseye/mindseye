Middx  William Lacey aged about 71 Years Removed from the Parish of St. Andrew by the Wardrobe London upon Oath saith That he lived in and rented an House the back of the Fountain Tavern near Fountain Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Four Years at the Yearly Rent of Fifteen pounds Quitted the Same near Thirty Years, never after rented any House paid Parish Taxes or served Parochial Office That he was Married to his present Wife  Margaret at the Fleet about Twelve Years ago

Sworn the 23d. day of February 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of William Lacey
