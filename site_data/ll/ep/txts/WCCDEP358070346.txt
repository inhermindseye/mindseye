Middlesex & Westminster}  Mary Ashford Aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House served any Apprenticeship or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says she has a Female Bastard Child begotten by one Joseph Symonds Named Jane Aged about four years which which was born near the Rose and Crown Ale house at Wandsworth in the County of Surry And this Examinant further saith that her late Father James Ashford served Seven years Apprenticeship to one William Gardiner a Waterman at Wandsworth aforesaid in the said County of Surry and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Father death

Sworn this 22d. day of Feb: 1776 before  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Ashford
