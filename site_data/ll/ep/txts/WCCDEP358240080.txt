Saint Clement Danes April 7th. 1792.

Middlesex & Westminster }  Hammersmith Middx  The Pauper brot back & fresh Orders made out to Fulham Mr. Wyndham's Its. be. gsit. witht. the Hamlet of Hammersmith Vide Subseqt. examnt. page No'd (51)  (to wit) Charlotte Wickens the Widow of Jonathan Wickens deceased Upon that her Oath says that she was marri'd to her late Husband Jonathan Wickens on the 26th. Day of March 1783 at the parish Church of Saint Ann Soho that her said Husband has been read about 6 Months and that since his Decease she hath not intermarried with any other Person Rented any House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds paid Parochial or other Rates or Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whereby to acquire any Settlement her Own Right. And further this Examinant Saith That her said Husband informed her and which she believes to be true that he did before his Intermarriage with this Examinant Live, as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wyndham at Hammersmith in this County of Middlesex for upwards of 14 Months and that he never often to living with Mr. Wyndham acquire any settlement whatsoever to the

best of his knowledge, and Further Saith her Husband always informed her his Settlement was at Accounts with

Sworn before this 7th. day of April 1792 N Bond Thos. Gordon

Charlotte Wickins
