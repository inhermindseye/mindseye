Middx  Mary Cooley aged abt. 30 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Richardson in James Street in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year & 9 months at the yearly Wages of three pounds & ten Shillings for the first Year & Four Pounds afterwards quitted her sd. Service abt. 10 Yrs. ago & has not since rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn March 15th 1754 before} Saml. Pechell  Tho Lane .

The Mark of Mary Cooley
