Middlesex to wit Francis Plesto Aged about Twenty five Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House paid Taxes Says he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship to Mr. Theops. John Carnegy a Printer at No. 19 Black Fryars in the Parish of Saint Anns Black Fryars in the City of London for Seven Years and after he was out of his Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this fourth day of October 1779 before} 

Francis Plesto
