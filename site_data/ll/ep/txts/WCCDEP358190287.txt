Middx  Ann Johnson  wife of Danl. Johnson  gon from her Upon Oath Says She is abt. Thirty five Years of Age that she Intermarried with the sd. Danl. Johnson at May Fair Chapel upwards of Twelve Years Since Says her sd. Husband never rented a House was an Apprentice or hired Yearly Servt. Says her sd. Husbands father  Bishop Johnson  Rented a House in Downing Street by Hyde Park Corner in the parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards and lived therein Sevll. Years

Sworn the 14th. day of Octr. 1762 before} Paul Vaillant  Fran: Bedwell

Ann Johnson
