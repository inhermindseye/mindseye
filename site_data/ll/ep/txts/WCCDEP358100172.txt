Middx to wit Richd. Pyner aged about Fifty years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Martha his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Bride London about Sixteen years ago and by whom he hath three Children living one of whom is now Provided for and Two others (vizt.) Samuel aged about Fourteen years & Abraham aged about Seven years Says he Rented & lived in a House in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry upwards of one year at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted the said House about Twenty years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 2d. day of February 1784.  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

Richd Pyner his Mark
