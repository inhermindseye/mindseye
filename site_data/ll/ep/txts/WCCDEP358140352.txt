Middx.  Mary Moran aged about 40 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Fras. Moran who has been dead abt. One Year says her said husband Rented the Foundling hospital  Coffee house in Red lion Street Holbn. in the Parish of St. George the Martyr in the County of Middx. for abt. Six Months at the Yearly Rent of Twenty six Pounds and Quitted it abt. 14 Years says her said husband in his life time after he Quitted that house nor she since his death Never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes in any part of Great Britain

Sworn this 26th. day of Feby. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Morand

Upon the Complaint of Mr Wilkinson Ovr. J: P:
