Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Johnson  Single woman maketh oath that she was heretofore hired & lived as a hired servant by & with one Castleton a Victuallr in the Parish of St. Mary Islington in the Said County at certain Wages & Continued therein such Service under such hiring for the Space of four Years That She has not since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That she hath a Child named Samuel Chapman born of this Deponts body on the twenty fifth day of March 1783 in Brush Alley in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd. County the which Child is illegitimate & a bastard by reason that She this Deponent was never married

Sworn this. 5. day of August 1784 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Johnson
