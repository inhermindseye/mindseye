Middx to wit Elizabeth Bainbridge Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant wth. Doctor Brocklesby in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds hath quitted her said Service about a fortnight ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when when born And that one William Crisp a Farrier in the Kings Mews did beget such Bastard Child on her Body he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at his the said Crisp's Uncles House in Chandos Street sometime in the Month of November last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Crisp and no other Person did bog the same and that he is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 30th. day of June 1779 before M Addington

her Elizth. Bainbridge Mark
