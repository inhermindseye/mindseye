Middx.  Mary Mumford aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow Of Wm. Mumford who has been dead abt. Twelve Years; says that being a Widow & without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Miles in Beaufort Buildings in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for Seven Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £5 and hath quitted said Service abt. One Year and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. Feby. 1759 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Mumford
