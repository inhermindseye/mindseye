Middx  Mary Marther upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband James Marther upwards of two Years since in the Old Church in Manchester in the County of Lancaster & hath by him one Child Cathn . aged about 17 months Says she hath been informed and verily believes her sd. husband never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes & that he was born in the Old Church Parish in Manchester in the County of Lancaster aforesd.

Sworn 29th July 1751 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Marther
