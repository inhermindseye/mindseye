St Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster }  St CD  to wit Elizabeth Hatton Wife of Francis Hatton & Aged about 45 Years or thereabouts thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband Francis at the parish Church of Bishopston in the County of Wilts about Eleven or Twelve Years ago (by Banns) that since then her said Husband Rented then took and with her Resided in an House called the House and Groom in Portugal Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex about two Months that he was to pay a considerable Yearly Rent for the same but this Deponent cannot Recollect exactly how much, only well knows it was upwards of £10 that her said Husband left the same about three Years ago and hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year paid any parish Taxes served any parochial Office or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponent knowledge Information or belief And further saith that she hath one Child by her said Husband now living Namely Maria

aged Eight Years on the 9th of April last that her said Husband is (as she believes) gone into Kent to Seek Work And that she & her said Child are exceedingly distressed and not able to support themselves witht. Assistance

Sworn the 3d day of September 1792 before Me } R Butler

Eliz Hatton
