Middlesex ss}  Sarah Darby  Singlewoman Maketh oath that divers Years ago she was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Elizabeth Hart a Laundress in Theobalds Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields Westmr in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of Three Years & has not since lived as a hired servant for a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this. 18. day of Febry 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Sarah Darby
