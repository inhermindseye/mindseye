Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Gibbs aged about 55 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the Sixth day of March 1774 he Intermarried with Jane his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in Birmingham by whom he hath Eight Children living Three of which are unprovide for Vizt. Martha aged about Twelve years Ann aged about Seven years and Charlotte aged about Two years That he this Deponent Rented an First floor and Garret unfurnished of Mr. Duvall in Change Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three Months and upwards at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds That he hath quitted said Apartments about Eighteen Months since which time he hath not Rented any unfurnished place of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Janry. 1798 before me} 

John Gibbs
