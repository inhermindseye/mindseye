Middlesex ss}  Mary Middleton the widow of John Middleton deced maketh oath that her Sd late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true That he was born in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the sd County & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right That She this Depont. Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That she this Depont hath been Credibly informed & which information she believes to be true that many years ago her said late husbands Father Thomas Middleton deced created & built at his own Costs two new Dwelling houses adjoining to each other in Homerton in the Parish of St John Hackney in the sd County by Virtue of a Lease granted to him for that purpose for a Term of Sixty one years at a Certain yearly Reserved Rent That he lived in & occupied one of Such houses for Several years where he died & let out the other of them to a Tenant and that Such houses were then & Still are of the yearly rent or Value of Twenty pounds as this Depont. apprehends & Verily Believes That after the death of the Said Thomas Middleton his widow as this Deponent has been informed by her said late husband occupied the sd house where in the said Thomas Middleton died for about a year & half Where She also died this Deponts Sd husband Continuing there with her as part of her Family That she this Deponent hath three Children namely Elizabeth Aged about Twelve years & eight Months Sarah Aged about Ten Years & Mary Aged about Six Years & eight Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this 6th day of January 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

Mary Middleton
