Middx  Christr. Harrison upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Henry Carter a Rule maker in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 14th. 1752 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Chrsr. Harrison
