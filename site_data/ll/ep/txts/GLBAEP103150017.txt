Middlesex ss}  Margaret Bone The Voluntary Examination of Margaret Bone of the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman  taken on oath before me Burford Camper  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this 28th day of February 1775

Who on her oath Saith that She is now with Child and that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the Said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that John Dilling now living as a Servant with John Dobson of the Same Parish Cheesemonger is the Father of the said Child

Taken & Signed the day & Year above Written before me B Camper

The Mark of Margaret Bone
