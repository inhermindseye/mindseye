Desertion

St Clement Danes 17th May 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Clement Danes  Desertion (to wit) Jane Daglish  Wife  Thomas Daglish now from her aged about 33 years Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said Thomas Daglish at the parish Church of St Martin in the Fields in or about the Month of August 1791 that she is informed and believes that previous thereto her said Husband Rented & Resided in a House in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for several years and paid the parochial Taxes for the same that he quitted the same about five years ago and this Deponent saith that he hath not since done any Matter or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief & that he hath left and deserted her by means whereof she is become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 17th day of May 1794 before Me } Wm. Kitchiner

her Jane Dalglish Mark

Witness Geo. Robertson
