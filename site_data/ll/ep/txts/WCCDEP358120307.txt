Middx  Eliz Woodman aged abt. 23 years upon Oath saith She never was married and that about Seven years ago she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Blunt near the half moon at Putney in the Parish of Putney in the County of Surry at the yearly wages of £3. for one year and upwards & has not Since she quitted her sd Service rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn March 12th 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz Woodman
