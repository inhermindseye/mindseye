Westmr.  Sarah Scatliff Aged about Twenty nine Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of James Scatliff gone from her with whom she intermarried at Wapping Old Chapel near Ten Years ago, says her said Husband served about three Years of his Apprenticeship with his Father Samuel Scatliff  Optician , the corner of St. Michael's Alley in Cornhill in the Parish of St. Michael Cornhill in the City of London , and then parted by his said Father Consent, says her said Husband never was Assigned over to any Person nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one whole Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief, says she hath two Children by her said Husband (to wit) MariaSelima Aged about Four Years and Martha Aged about One Year and Ten Months, and being poor is not able to maintain herself and Children.

Sworn the 2d. day of January 1768 . before  Fran Cary W Kelynge

Sarah Scatliff
