Westmr.  Martha Hudson upon Oath says she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Edward Popham  Esqr . at Littlecot in the Parish of in the County of Wilts for Six Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds and left her said Service about four Years ago Says she hath not since she quitted her said Service Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif And this Examinant further upon her Oath Says about Ten Years ago she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Lloyd in Tyburn Road in the Parish of St. George  Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. which has since been Baptized John & is now living Says upwards of three Years ago she was Delivered of another Male Bastard Child in Bedford Bury in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the said Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized James & is now living Says upwds. of one Year ago she was Delivered of a female Bastard Child in Market Lane in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr.

aforesaid which hath since been baptized Elizabeth and is now living and this Examinant being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworn Septr. 11th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Martha Hudson
