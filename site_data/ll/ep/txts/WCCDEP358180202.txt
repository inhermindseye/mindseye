Middx  Mary Wele aged 20 Years & upwds: upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & that on the 29th. Day of May last she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child at one Mr. Plunketts the Corner of Drum Alley in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clements Danes in the County of Middlesex which is yett unbaptized & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that Walter Taylor a Taylor did begett the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body she this Examinant being a Servt. to Mrs. Hannah Smith in the Playhouse Passage goeing into Bridges Street & the sd. Walter Taylor being Acquainted with this Examinant had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the latter End of July or beginning of Augt. last in the House of the sd. Mrs. Smith & at sevll: other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child of which she was Delivered as aforesd. & that he the sd. Walter Taylor & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne June 7th. 1744 before} Wm: Jones  Thos D Neil

The Mark of Mary Well
