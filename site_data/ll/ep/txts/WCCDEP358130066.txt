Middx  Mary Bland Aged about 28 Years upon Oath saith that She Intermarried with Walter Bland (who is now at Sea in his Majesty's Service) at the Fleet London about seven Years since by whom She hath two Children (to wit) Samuel aged about two Years and Sarah born the twenty fourth of May last And further upon her Oath says that her said Husband Rented a House about Six years since in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes & lived therein upwards of Six Weeks And That since his quitting the same he hath not rented Ten Pounds by the year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 10th. Day of Octor. 1755 before}

The mark of Mary Bland
