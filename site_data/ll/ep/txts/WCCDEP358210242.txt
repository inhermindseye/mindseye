Westmr.  John Tovey Aged about Sixty Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Mary his Wife at May Fair Chapel about Twenty one Years ago, says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Henry Jones  Esquire in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Three Years, at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds, Diet and Lodging, And after he quitted said Service never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the Sixth day of February 1768 , Before W Kelynge

The Mark of John Tovey
