Aldgate Middx

Sarah Newman  Singlewoman Maketh Oath that she was Informd by her late Father and Mother Henry Newman and Katherine Newman that she was born and brought up in the Town and Parish of Ryegate in the County of Surry and that her Said Father was a Shopkeeper there Several Years And paid all Parochial taxes and that she never was a hired Servant by the year nor an Apprentice That she hath been maintaind in the Work: house there

Sworn 31th day of December 1747 before Robert Dennett Rd: Ricards

the mark of Sarah Newman
