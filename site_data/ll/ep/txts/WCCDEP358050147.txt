Westmr.  John Grundy aged about 76 Years upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house in Hollis Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Rent of £18 for 25 Years or there abouts and afterwds. lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £18 in Whitehorse Yard in the same Parish for one Year or thereabouts and quitted the last mentioned house abt. 7 Years ago since which time he has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

and further Saith that he is poor and not worth twenty Shillings said his Apparel and the furniture of one room.

Sworn 22d. Novr. 1749 before} Tho Lane .

John Grundy
