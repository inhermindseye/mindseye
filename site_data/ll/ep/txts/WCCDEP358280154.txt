Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sophia Hallywell aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Froome in Hart Street  Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for 12 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service upwards of 3 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 13th. day of Janry . last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of a Mrs. Davis No. 14 Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Mary Ann that one John Sanford an Ostler who lives with a Mr. Bishop a Horse Dealer in Mount Street  Grosvenor Square is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body for these two years last past and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that he is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 11th. day of March 1788 } R Butler

her Sophia Hallywell Mark

Sophia Hallywell lodges at Mrs. Davis's No. 14 Great Shire Lane

John Sanford is a Middle Sized Man about 25 years of Age fair Complexion wears his Hair in his Neck

Man taken and Committed to Bridewell the 12th. day of March
