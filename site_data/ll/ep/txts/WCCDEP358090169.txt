Middx to wit Thomas Rumball Aged about Twenty five years (On behalf of Mary his Wife who is Insane ) on his Oath says he Intermarried to his said Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Lukes Old Street in the County of Middlesex And by whom he hath no Child or Children living says he was bound an Apprentice  for Seven years to one James Coghlan a Book binder in Duke Street Grosvenor Square in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex And served his said Master under such Indenture for 6 years when his said Master gave up this Examinant his Indenture and they Consented to part And never was Assigned over to any other Person but has worked Journey Work ever since And further says that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 8th. day of November 1780 before R Butler David Walker

Thos. Rumball
