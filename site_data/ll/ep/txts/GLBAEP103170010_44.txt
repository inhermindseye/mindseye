Middlesex ss}  Peter abbott maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to Charles Pitkaver of the Parish of St Bartholomew the Great London Watch Finisher  & Continued with him there for about Two years & then this Depont removed with his Sd Master into Cupids Court in the Parish of St Luke in the Sd County Where he Served his Sd Master for about the Space of Four years & then this Deponts Sd Master did & then this Depont Continued to live with his Sd, Masters Son Charles Weaver during the Residue of his Sd apprenticeship That this Depont has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt That he hath a wife  named Sarah to whom he was lawfully Married & three Children namely Lonisa Aged about Eleven years John Aged about four years & eight Months & Peter Aged about one year & eight Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Sd wife none of whom have gained a Settlemt for themselves

Sworn this 30th day of Sepr 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Peter Abbott
