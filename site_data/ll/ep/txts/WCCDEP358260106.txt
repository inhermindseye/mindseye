Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Sleeman aged about 25. years upon her Oath saith that she never was married, Rented any house, Tenement, or Lodging, of Ten Pounds a year, paid Taxes, lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months, or ever bound an Apprentice , that she this Deponent hath been informed by her father Daniel Sleeman which Information she verily believes to be true that she was born at Holsworthy in the Parish of Holsworthy in the County of Devon where her father has kept House for many years and that she this Deponent hath not gained any other Settlement than as aforesaid.

Sworn this 11th. day of Febry. 1796 }

Elizabeth Sleeman
