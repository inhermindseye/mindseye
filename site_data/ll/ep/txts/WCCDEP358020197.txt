Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) John Cox aged Fifty years and upwards a Porter by Trade and who resorts to the Coopers Arms in Thomas Street London upon did Oath saith that hire the Father of Richard Cox who is the Husband Ann Cox late Ann Maddox  Spi and who are both Husband and Wife now in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and this Examinant saith that the said Richard Cox was born in Bainbrig Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields and is an illegitimate Child of Sarah Theobalds since deced by him this Examinant, and this Examinant for the saith that the Said Richard Cox never as this Examinant best Believes acquired any Settlement in his own Right

Sworn this 20th April 1790 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

John Cox
