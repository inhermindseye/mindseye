Middx  Samuel Burgis upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Elizabeth on the 5th. Day of April 1741 at the Parish Church of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex by whom he hath one Child (to Witt) Samuel John aged about 4 Months Says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to his Father John Burgis a Stay maker & Served 3 Yrs & upwards the latter Part of his Apprentiship with his sd. Father in Fountain Court in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 16th. 1745 before W Jones.

Saml Burgiss
