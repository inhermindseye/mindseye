Middx ss  Sarah Longman  Single Woman Maketh oath that her father about Twenty Years ago Rented a house in the Parish of St. Nicholas in the City of Bristoll & paid Eighteen Pounds a Year Rent for the Same & was by Trade a Baker And this Depont Saith that she was never a hired Servant by ye Year nor Married nor ever bound an Apprentice And that she this Depont. to her knowledge or belief hath not gained and Settlement but in ye sd. Parish of St. Nicholas Bristoll in rights of her father & of her birth And this Dept. farther Says that She hath two Children to wit one baptised by ye Name of John aged abt. born in Hall Yard in the Minories in ye Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in ye City of London & Sarah Aged about eight Months born in Griggs Court in ye Parish of St. Mary White Chappell in ye County of Middx both of Which Children are Bastards

Sworn this 31st day of Decr. 1753 before}
