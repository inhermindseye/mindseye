Middx  Alice Higham upon Oath Saith she never was married and that about Seven Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Chapman in the parish of St. Mary at St. Edmundsbury in the County of Suffolk at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds for Seven Years and has not Since she quitted her sd Service rented ten Pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn May 25th. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

Alice Higham
