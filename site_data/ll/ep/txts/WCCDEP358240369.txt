St Clement Danes Janry 2d 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Dunstan West London  (to wit) Mary Connely aged 65 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to John Connely at the parish Church of St Clement Danes (by License) in or about the year of our Lord 1779 that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and resided in a House in Chancery Lane in the parish of St Dunstan in the West London about two years at the annual Rent of £23 that their he quitted the same About twelve years ago and her never after during his Life obtained Any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that he died about six years ago and she this Examinant hath not Since his Death done been Married or obtained any legal settlement in her own right

Sworn the 3d day of January 1794 before us N Bond B: Kennett

Mary Connley

Removed by Mr Talboy
