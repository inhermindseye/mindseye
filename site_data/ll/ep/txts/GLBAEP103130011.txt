Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Shippaway the Widow of  Edward Shippaway mariner deced maketh oath that her said late husband deced has informed her that he was born in the Parish of Saint Stephen in the Town of Ipswich in the County of Suffolk and that he was also bound an apprentice to one Captain Carnaby in the Parish of saint Clement in the same Town & served the whole of his apprenticeship with him & to her knowledge or belief he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that she this Deponent has not since her husband death done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 25th. day of Septemr 1773 before B Camper

The Mark of Elizabeth Shippaway
