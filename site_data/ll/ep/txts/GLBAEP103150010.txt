Middlesex ss}  Jane Irving  Single woman maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant  by the Year with Miss Bearman in Burr Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Five pounds Continued in Such Service for the Space of one Year & Three Quarters & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she this Depont. did not afterwards live as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was never Married.

Sworn this 6th day of Febry 1775 before me B Camper

The Mark of Jane Irving
