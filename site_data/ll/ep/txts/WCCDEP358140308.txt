Middx.  Ann Markham Widow lodging at Mrs. Handersons in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. on the behalf of Mary Ives an Infant aged abt. Eight Years the daughter of James Ives (who now abscends) by Margaret his Wife deceased upon Oath says she said Mary Ives was born in the house of Mr. Marshall a Watchmaker in Row Street  Shoe Lane in the parish of St. Bridget ats St. Brides London And that She cannot give any Account of the Settlement of the said James Ives .

Sworn this 27th. Octr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Ann Markham

Upon Mr. Wilkinsons Complt. J: P
