Middx  Eliz: Vincent aged about 22 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married And that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Nutcher in Bull head Court in Newgate Street in the Parish of Christchurch London at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds for the Space of two Years & upwards & hath left her sd. Service about 4 Years since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 25th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz Vincent
