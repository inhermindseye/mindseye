Middx.  Jannet Maskall aged abt. 29 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Maskall a Soldier in  Lord Robt. with whom she intermarried at Perth in Scotland on the 14th. of April 1755 Manners Regiment of Foot & by whom she hath one Child (to wit) Mary aged upwds. of two Years says her said husband was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to his Brother Nicholas Maskall of Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and served him three Years of the said Term of seven Years and was never assigned over to any person, or ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt.

Sworn the 8th. of May 1758 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Jannet Maskall
