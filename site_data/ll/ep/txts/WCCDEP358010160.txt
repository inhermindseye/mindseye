Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Owen aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Barker in Old Compton Street she in the Parish of St. Anne in the Liberty of westmr. for 12 Months & upwards at the yearly wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about 2 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Novr. 1787 R Bulter D Walker

her Ann Owen Mark
