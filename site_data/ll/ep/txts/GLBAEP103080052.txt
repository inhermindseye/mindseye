Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Hewson  Singlewoman Maketh oath that her Father Thomas Hewson deced Maketh oath that she hath been Credibly informed and believes that her sd late Father Thomas Hewson deced about Twenty seven Years Ago Rented a Publick house known by the sign of the Sword and Buckle Situate in Swan Alley in the Parish of St Boltoph Without Aldgate in the sd County of Middx and Paid Ten Pounds by the Year Rent for the same And saith that she never lived as a hired servant by the Year nor Never was an Apprentice Nor Married Nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to Acquire a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 30th: day of Novr: 1759 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Elizabeth Hewson
