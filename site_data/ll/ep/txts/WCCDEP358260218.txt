Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Sach aged about Sixty years Widow upon her Oath saith that she Rented and lived in a house in Browns Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds that she hath quitted said House about Sixteen years since which time She hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Octr. 1797 . before me G S Goodenough

Mary Sach
