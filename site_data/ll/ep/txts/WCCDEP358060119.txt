Middx  Isabella Mulloy on Behalf of Mary an Infant aged about 11 Years upon Oath saith she has been informed and verily believes the sd. Child was born at Mrs. Broomhalls near little Turnstile in Holborn in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields Middx & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant

Sworn June 8th. 1751 before  Geo. Errington Isabella Mulloy
