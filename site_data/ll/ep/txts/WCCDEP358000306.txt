St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} to wit Sarah White Aged forty Nine years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That Fuller White Aged about 32 years now Insane was Bound Apprentice with Mr Garth in Sale Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex by Indres of Apprenticeship duly Executed For the Term of Seven Years that he Sewed the whole of his said Apprenticeship with the said Mr Garth under the said Indres,-Since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief he might have Gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 23d of June 1786 R Butler

Sarah White
