Middx  Mary Haines aged abt. 39 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her Husband  Richard Haines in the Year 1725 at the Fleet London who hath been Gone from her abt. 14 Years That her sd. Husband served his Apprentiship with Mr. Jenkins a Brass Button Maker in Bedford Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbo n above Barrs in the County of Middlesex & hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship abt. 22 Yrs. since which time he hath not rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 10th. March 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Mary Haines
