St Clement Danes November 8th.1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary le Strand  (to wit) Mary Thomas aged thirty seven years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Munday at the Turks Head Coffee House in the parish of St Mary le Strand in the County of Middlesex for a year and Nine Months at the Annual Wages of £4. that she quitted that Service about three or four years ago and hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 8th day of November 1793 before us  Rich.. Ford B: Kennett

Mary Thomas 's Mark

Witness. Garrett
