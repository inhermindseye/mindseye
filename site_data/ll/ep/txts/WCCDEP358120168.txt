Middx  Jas. Culverwell upon Oath Saith he never was an apprentice or yearly Servant and that he is the Son of John Culverwell by Mary his Wife and that he has been informed & verily believes his sd. father rented and lived in a house in the Parish of St. James in the City of Bath of the yearly Rent of £19 & received relief some time Since from the said Parish for himself his Wife & this Examint. they being at that time ill wth. a fever

Sworn Augt. 1st 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Jas. Culverwell
