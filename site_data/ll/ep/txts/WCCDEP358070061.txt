Westmr. to wit Eleanor Everett Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Carney a Linnen Draper in Broad Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Six Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. day of Janry 1773 before Us}  T. Truslove

her Eleanor Everett Mark

Witness Edwd. Fields
