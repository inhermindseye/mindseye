Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Bell aged about Twenty years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she has lived as a hired yearly servant with one Mr. Card who keeps the Oxford Coffee house in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for these fourteen Months last past at the yearly wages of Ten Guineas And this Deponent further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one James Carney who now lives as a Cook with the said Mr. Card is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said James Carney and no other Person did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Septr. 1796 } John Collick

her Elizth Bell Mark

2d. Novr. 1796 Man paid £21:15:0 Vizt. £16:1:0 in Cash & a Joint Note of Hand for £5.14:0 payable at 4 Mos. which was paid Release Exted

Child born in the house 2d. Octr. 1796 named James Disd. with the Mother 10th. Novr. 1796

Died some little time afterwards
