Middlesex ss  Michael Owens maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre to Richard Lilly of the Parish of George in the Sd County Taylor for the Term of Seven Years & his Master dying there in about the Space of ten Months after he was bound he was there upon by his Said Masters Representative Assigned or Turned over for the residue of his apprentice- ship to Thomas Nicholson of the Parish of st Peters London  Taylor with whom he lived there for about the space of three years & then here moved with his Said Master into Popes head Alley in the Parish of Saint Mary Woolnoth in the City of London Where his Said Apprenticeship ended That he never rented a house lodging or other Tenement of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Apprenticeship

Sworn this. 25. day of June 1787 before  R Davies  Rob: Smith

Michael Owens
