Middx  Eliz Ireland upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of Wm. Ireland with whom She intermarried at the Fleet London about 5 Yrs. ago & that he abscond from her & she knows not where he is and further that her sd. husbd. lived a Yearly Servant whilst a Singleman with Mr. Thomas Miles at the Essex head in Essex Street in the parish of St. Clemt Danes at the Yearly Wages of £7 for one Year & upwards & Quitted his sd. Service abt. a fortnight before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and has not Since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her Knowledge or beleif Saith she has 2 Childn. by her sd. husband (viz) Mary aged 3 Yrs. & Saml . aged abt. 18 Months

Sworn Decr 6th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Ireland
