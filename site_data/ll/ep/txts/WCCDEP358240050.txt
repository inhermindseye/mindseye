Middlesex & Westminster }  St Saviour Southwark }  (to wit) Richard Stimpson Aged 33 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he was born in Maid Lane in the parish of Saint Saviours Southwark in the County of Surry That he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Johnson of Saint Margarets Hill in the said Parish with said Parish of Saint Saviour Southwark between 2 and 3 Years that his Mother who the lived in Maid Lane aforesaid used to receive his Wages therefore he cannot tell how much it was a Year That since he quitted said Service he lived with Mr. Nichols in Gravel Lane as a Weekly Servant And that he has lived in servant Weekly Places since but as a Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year lived as a hired Yearly Servant paid Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any Subsequent Settlement since living with Mr. Johnson in the said Parish of Saint Southwark to the best of this Examinant's Knowledge information or belief

Sworn this 6th. day of Octr. 1791 before} Philip Dyot D Walker

Richard Stimpson
