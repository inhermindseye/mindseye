St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Elizth  Glenn  Singlewoman Aged 27 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly toward Servant with Mr. Leach No. 15 Great Suffolk Street in the parish of St Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster at the yearly Wages of £8.8s that she quitted that Service only about 3 Weeks ago and hath not acquired not acquired any Subsequent legal Settlemts - Child that she is now Poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 1st: Novr: 1791 before us W Bonet Thos. Gordon

Elizabeth Glenn
