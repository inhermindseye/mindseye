Middx ss  Simon Spanrick Maketh oath that about seventeen years ago he was bound apprentice by the parish of Hungerford in Berkshire to Thomas Pye of Ramsbury in the County of Wilts  Combmaker with whom he served about four years of his apprenticeship & then this Deponts. Master went away & left this Depont And this Depont. saith that he hath not since gained any Subsequent Settlemt. havg. Never been a hired Servant by the year nor Rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any parish offices nor done any Act to Acquire a Subsequent Settlmt. to this Deponts. knowledge or belief And saith that he hath a wife Named Margaret and one Child Named John aged about thirteen Months the lawfull issue of this Depont. this said wife.

Sworn this 20th day of June 1755 before  Richd Ricards

The Mark of Simon Spanrick
