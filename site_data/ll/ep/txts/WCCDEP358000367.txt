Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr} to wit Thomas Cock aged about 26 year upon his Oath saith that about two years ago he went to live as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Spencer in Sharps Alley Cow Cross in the Parish of Saint Sepulchres in the County of Middx that he lived in the said Mr. Spencers Service for twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about twelve Months ago since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Dept Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Octr. 1786 G Mercer John Croft

Thos Cock
