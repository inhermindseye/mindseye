Middx  Sarah Wooton aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith she is a Singlewoman Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes Says her Father Joseph Wooton Deceased lived in & rented a house in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds or thereabouts for the Space of four Years as this Examinant has been informed & beleives Says her sd. father after he left the sd. house never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 25th 1746 before}
