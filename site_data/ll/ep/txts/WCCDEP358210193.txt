Westmr.  Jane Pew aged about 26 Years Wife of William Pew with whom She intermarried in the new Church in Bermingham in the County of Warwick the 14th. of March 1763 Says her said Husband informed her and which Information She believes to be true that he was bound Apprentice to one John Adams a Brushmaker in Bermingham aforesaid and served him thereabout half a year and then his said Master came to reside in Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster where he continued with, him as his Apprentice a year and half and then left him and never was assigned over to any other Person nor rented House or paid Taxes to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 6th. day of Octor. 1767 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Jane Pew
