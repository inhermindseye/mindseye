Middx  Mary  Eliz: Lawrence aged abt. 40 yrs. upon Oath says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Courtman Linnen Draper in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for two years at the yearly Wages of five Pounds for the first year & five Pounds ten Shillings for the Second year she being at that time a Widow & without Child or Children & hath Quitted her said Service upwards of three years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn the 18th day of April 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Lawrence
