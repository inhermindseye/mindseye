Westr  Thomas Harrington upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant (whilst a Singleman ) with Mr Newit a Staymaker in Blackmoor Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year and upwards and had by Agreement Three pounds for the first year and at the Rate of Six pounds ten Shillings for He Second Year Says he has not since he quitted the sd. Mr. Newits Service rented a Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Places to his knowledge on beleif

Sworn Decr. 1st. 1749 before} Samuel Clarke

Thos Harrington
