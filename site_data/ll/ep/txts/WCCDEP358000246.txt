Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit John Belchier aged about 49 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a House at Lambeth in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry for twelve Months at the yearly Rent of 20 Guineas hath quitted said House about 5 years ago since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Depts. knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of March 1786 M Addington Meading

Belchier
