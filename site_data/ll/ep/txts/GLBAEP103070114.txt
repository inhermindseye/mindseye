Middx ss  John Butler and Mary Simkins  Widow of Thomas Simkins deced Severally Make oath And first the sd. John Butler Saith that abt. forty two years ago the sd. Thomas Simkins deced was bound apprentice to James Higgins a Pipe Maker who yn lived in Kings Arms Yard in ye parish of St. Sepulchre in ye City of London And with whom he served ye last four years of his apprenticeship this Dept being Tellow Apprentice with him at ye same time And that to this Deponts. knowledge or belief the sd Thomas Simkins never Rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any parish offices nor gained any settlemt. Subsequent to his Apprenticeship And the sd. Mary Simkins for herself saith that she was Married to her sd late husband Thomas Simkins deced abt. Thirty Two years ago And that to her knowledge information or belief her sd late husband never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any parish offices nor gained any Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship abovemenconed

Both Sworn this 4th day of Sepr. 1754 before Mr.by John Butler  Richd: Ricards

John Butler her Mary Simkins  Mark

Mary Simkins Sworn before me this 6th, 7, 8 1754 Chris: Scott
