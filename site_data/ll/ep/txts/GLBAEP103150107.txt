Middlesex ss}  William Knowland maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indre for the Term of Seven Years to John Grimsdale of the Parish of Saint Mary White Chapel in the Said County Sawyer & Served him for about the Space of two Years & half (this Deponent during Such time always lodging in his Sd Masters dwelling house in the sd Parish) & then this Depont Master died there That there upon this Deponent under & by Virtue of the sd Indre. Continued to Serve Elizabeth Grimsdale his Said Masters Widow there & who Carried on the Said Trade for about the farther Term of four Years met this Deponent during such last mend Space of time always lodging in his sd Mistresses dwelling house in the Said Parish & then this Deponents & his sd Mistress & this Deponent removed to a house & yard in Rosemary Lane in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County where She Continued to Carry on the Said Trade of a Sawyer & Where this dept Continued to Serve there residue of his sd Apprenticeship being near or about the Space of Six Months as this Deponent now best remembers & Where he lodged during Such last mend Space of time That he this Depont. has not Since lived ago hired Servant for the Space of a Year nor rented a house or other Tenement of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds nor Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to Such his Apprenticeship as aforesaid.

Sworn this 11th. day of January 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of William Knowland
