Middx  Margt. Dickenson aged 23 years upon Oath Saith She never was Married nor ever was an Apprentice or yearly Servants , that She was born in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex where her Late father & mother rented a house of the yearly Rent of £18 and lived therein Several Years

Sworn Novr. 28th. 1753 before} Fran. Bedwell

The Mark of Margt. Dickenson
