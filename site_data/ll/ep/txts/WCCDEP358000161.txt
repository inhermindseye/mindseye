Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. both to Sr. Brides. to wit John Livingston aged about 65 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Martha Livingston his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Brides in the City of London upwd. of 7 years ago and by whom he has on Child or Children living Says that he Rented and lived in a House in Poppins Court , Street in the Parish of Saint Brides in the City of London for 4 years and upwards at the yearly Rent of £14 and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Six years ago since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the Best of this Deponent Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 28th. day of Novr. 1785 before J Addington Edwd: Naish

John Livingston
