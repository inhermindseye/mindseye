Westmr. to wit John Smithers Aged about Twenty Seven Years on his Oath Says to Intermarried with Sarah his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch  about five Years Ago and by whom he hath one Child now living Namely Hannah Aged about Sixteen Months Says he served seven Years Apprenticeship  with one Richard Palmer a Watch Casemaker in Red Lyon Street Clerkenwell in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d day of January 1774 before}  Tho Kynaston

John Smither
