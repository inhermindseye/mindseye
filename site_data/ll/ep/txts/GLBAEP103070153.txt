Middx ss  Mary Maund  Single woman Maketh oath that about Twenty years ago she lived as a hired Servant by the Year with Thomas Dery a Box maker in Friday Street in the parish of St. Margaret Moses in the City of London and Continued in such service for the space of two Years & upwards at the yearly wages of four Pounds & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the sd parish And this Depont. saith that she was Never Married And that she hath not since lived as a hired servt by the Year nor gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d day of January 1756 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Mary Maund
