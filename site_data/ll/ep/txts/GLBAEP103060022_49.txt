Middx  Mary Burchmore maketh Oath that about Eight Years ago William Buck the father of John Buck Rented a House in Barnaby Street in the parish of St. Olave but now St. John Southwark and lived there in about three or Four Years and paid about Twelve pounds a Year and that he gained no Subsequent Settlement since to her knowledge

Sworne this 12th. day of May 1744 Before T Quarrill

Mary Barchmore
