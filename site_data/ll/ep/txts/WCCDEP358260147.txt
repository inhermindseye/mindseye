Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Sleeman aged about 62 years upon her Oath saith that about 30 years ago she Intermarried with William Sleeman her present husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn That her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Church Court in the Parish Clement of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for about Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of Fifty Pounds that s he hath quitted said House about fifteen years since which time he hath not rent Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th- day of Augt. 1796 } N Bond Rusl Clarke

her Margt. Sleeman Mark
