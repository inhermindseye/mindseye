Middlesex ss}  Richard Yates maketh oath that divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with Thomas Twynam in the Nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Distiller at Certain Yearly Was & Continued in Such Service there for the Space of Four Years by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the sd Parish Th he this Deponent has not Since lived elsewhere as hired Servant for the Space of year nor rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds nor served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent Settlement to his Servitude abovementioned

Sworn this 5th day of January 1776 before Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Richard Yates
