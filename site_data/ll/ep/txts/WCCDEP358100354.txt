Middlesex and Westminster}  the Childn. were passd. away but dy havg. en passd. to us sometime ach. we were obliged to take turn back again to wit Ann Threader aged 52 years or thereabouts upon her Oath says That she Intermarried with John Threader her Husband about 30 years ago at the fleet That he deserted her about Two Months afterwards That she had no Children by him That since that time she has never been with seen him or known anything further than that she once heard he was married to another Woman That she also heard he has been dead some years That after he so deserted her, vizt. about 3 years after she lived and Cohabited with one Jacob Wesley a Journeyman Shoemaker That she had three Children by him vizt. Hannah a Female Child aged about 14 years who was born at a House in Bird Cage Alley in the parish of Saint George Southwark of whom the said Jacob Wesley was the father and such Child was Christened in the parish Church of Saint George Southwark by the name of Hannah Wesley That shortly after the Birth of the  Hannah she and the said Jacob Wesley went to live in Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Overys That they lived there at the House of a Mr. Cuthbert for some years that she was delivered of a Male Child thereof which

the said Jacob Wesley was the father who was afterwds. Christened at Saint Mary Overys by the name of Thomas Wesley and is about 12 years of Age That she was after that also delivered of a Female Child at the said Mr. Cuthberts of which the said Thomas Wesley was the Father that such last Child was also Christened in the Parish Church of Saint Mary Overys by the name of Catherine Wesley and is about 9 years of Age That all three Children have been at Nurse or with her from the time of their Birth and have never gained a Settlement except by their Birth as aforesaid

Sworn this 15th. day of Febry 1785  Tho: Collin  Jno Barnfather
