Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann North aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year That she lived as a hired yearly servant with one Mr. Greening in Milman Row Chelsea for Three years at the yearly wages of Seven Pounds that since she quitted said Service she has done no Act to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the Sixth day of June now last past she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the house of a Mrs. Russell No. 12 Clements Lane which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary Ann That one Richard Sherman a Clerk in the Treasury and who lives in Queen Street  Chelsea is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Richard Sherman and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 21st- day of April 1795 } N Bond

Ann North

4th. May 1795 Release Executed Paid £25

Qy. what is become of the Child 25th. March 1805

Mary Ann Sherman living with Mary Wentus 13th. Hemlock Court there on the Book
