Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Humphrys  Singlewoman Taken the 31st. day of Janry. 1738 Who upon her Oath saith that about 12 Years ago she this Examinant lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Tuexbury in Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Butcher for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings And this Examt. further saith that she hath not since that time Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Charles Egerton

Eliz: Humphreys
