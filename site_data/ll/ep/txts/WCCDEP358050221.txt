Westmr. 

Ann Askew upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Thomas Askew who dyed about 14 Years ago and that she lived in and rented a house in Vinegar Yard in the Parish of Sr. Martin in yr. fields in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £18 for three months and upwards and Mr Russell the Land lord of the said house Seized this Examints Goods for a Quarters Rent and that she quitted the sd. house abt. one month since

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Tho Lane

Ann Askew
