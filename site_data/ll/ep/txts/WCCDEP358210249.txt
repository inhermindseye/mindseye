Middx. & Westmr.  Sarah Pearce Aged about Twenty one Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she now lives a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Moses Wingrove , of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex Inn holder , and hath so done for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when Born; says that one William Symmond a Hackney Coach - Master had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of September 1767, and at several times, since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said William Symmond and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 1st. day of March 1768 , Before} Thos: Balack

The Mark of Sarah Pearce
