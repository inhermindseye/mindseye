St.. Clement Danes January 13th. 1792 .

Middlesex & Westminster } (to wit) Elizabeth Burriss  Singlewoman  Aged 25 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr James Glasscow the Spotted Dog in Strand Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & the County of Middlesex for from 25th. day of October in the year 1790 until the 2d day of January Instant at the Annual Wages of £5 that she quitted that Service on the said 2d day of January and hath not since acquired any other legal Settlement And this Deponent up her Voluntary Oath saith that She is now Pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes , And that Michael Haycock a Journeyman Taylor who lately lived at the Black Horse Wych Little Wild Street Drury Lane near Lincoln Inn Fields is the only Real and true father thereof he and no one else having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Begotten Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant thereon And this Deponent saith that no other Person but the said Michael Haycock hath Ever been Criminally Connected with her this Deponent

Sworn the 13th. day of January 1792 before me} R Butler

Elizabeth Burriss
