Middlesex ss}  John Robinson maketh oath that he was born a Bastard in Red Cross Street in the parish of Sd Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County as he has been informed by his Mother & Verily believes to be true That he was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right.

Sworn this 31 day of January 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

John Robinson
