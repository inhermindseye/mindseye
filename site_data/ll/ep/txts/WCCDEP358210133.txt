Westmr.  Alice Hooper aged about 36 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Hooper (gone from her) with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London about fourteen Years ago, says her said husband is was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief; says before her Intermarriage with the said John Hooper and whilst she was a SingleWoman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Grayham in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds,

Sworn the 7th. of Feby. 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Alice Hooper

Memorandum

A fresh Order was made to remove her to St Jno. Southwark she having mistook the right Parish (Vide Page 100)
