Middx  Robert Robinson maketh Oath and Saith that about Thirty Years ago he was Bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to Mr . Richard Wilkins of Stony Lane in the parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry  Barber and served all his Apprentiship there And hath gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge

Sworn this 4th. day of March 1745 Before. Robert Dennett

Robt. Robinson
