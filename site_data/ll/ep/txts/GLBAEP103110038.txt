Middlesex ss}  Joseph Sillcoks maketh oath That twenty Years Ago he was duly bound Apprentice to one Benjamin Possey of the Parish of St. James in the Town of Taunten  in the County of Somerset  Carpenter & Served Five years of Such his apprenticeship there & by that means gained a legal Settlement in the sd Parish That he hath never rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his Knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that he hath a wife named Elizabeth and two Children to Wit Elizabeth Aged about five Years & Mary about one year & half the lawful Issue of this Depont by his Said Wife to Whom he was lawfully Married in the Church belonging to the Said Parish of St. James.

Sworn this 25 day of January 1772 before  Cha: Digby  Jno Sherwood

Joseph Sillcoxx
