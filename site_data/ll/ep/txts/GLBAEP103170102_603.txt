Middlesex ss  James Rose maketh oath That he was born in the Parish of Mount Whiter in the Shire of Aberdeen in Scotland That he never served any Apprenticeship nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year in England or Wales nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in England or Wales That he hath a wife named Margaret & a Child named Ann Aged about Nine years the lawful Issue of this Depont by his said Wife to Whom he was married at the Fleet in the year one thousand Seven hundred & fifty two.

Sworn this day of November 1777 before dead

The Mark of James Ross
