Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Wright  Widw . taken the 10th. day of March 1738 Who upon her Oath saith that before her Intermarriage with James Wright her late Husband he the said Jams. Wright lived as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Gooding at the Kings Arms in Haughton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Inholder for the Space of five Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds or thereabts. And this Examint. further saith that her said Husband in his Life time nor she this Examint. since his Death never Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & that she is now Sick & Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Elizth. Wright
