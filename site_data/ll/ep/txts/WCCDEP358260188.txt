Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Hunt aged about upwards of Fifty Sixty years upon her Oath Saith That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Newman in Exeter Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Three years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Twelve Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th day of Febry. 1797 before me G J Goodenough

her Mary Hunt Mark Witness B Newman
