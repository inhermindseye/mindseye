Middx ss  Jane Ben Mc Lane  Widow Maketh oath That about thirty Years ago she was present at ye birth of John Wil William Williams Son of John Williams who was born in King Street then part of ye parish of St. Dunstan Stepney but now called the parish of St. George in the County of Middx And this Depont. Saith that the sd. William Williams lived as a hired Servant by ye Year with Mr Ellison Master of a Man of war whose Dwelling house was in Anchor Rope Alley in ye sd. parish of St George aforesd And believes he has not gained any Subsequent Settlement than as abovesaid.

Sworn the 30th- day of Septr 1750 before us  Rd. Ricards  Saml Gower

The Mark of Jane Mc Lane
