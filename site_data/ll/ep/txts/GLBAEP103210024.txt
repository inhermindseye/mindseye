Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Richards the Widow of William Richards deced maketh oath that Since her Said husbands decease She this Deponent having no Child or Children was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with one Mr Osborne a Victualler in King Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such Service under such hiring for the Space of Sixteen Months & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or been Married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 16. day of Novemr 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Richards
