Middx  Eliz: Hart  wife of Edwd. Hart aged about 26 Years upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Edward Hart about 16 Years since at the Fleet London and that the sd. Edward Hart lived in & rented the Anchor & Crown in Duke Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of £10 for the Space of Seven Years or thereabouts & hath left the same about 20 Years Says her sd. Husband hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year before his sd. Marriage or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge Says the sd. Edwd. Hart hath been gone from her about five Years & where he now is this Examint. knows not

Sworn 29th. June 1745 before}
