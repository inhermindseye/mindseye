Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Luke Simon aged about 30 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bridges an Attorney in Serle Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months And upwards at the yearly Wages of £10 that since he quitted said Service he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one Year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of Septr. 1788 R Butler

his Luke Simon Mark
