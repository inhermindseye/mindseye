Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Sarah Walker aged about 57 years upon her Oath Saith that on the 28th. day of Janry. 1762 She Intermarried with Benjamin Walker her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Olave Southwark that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Holles Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £40 that he hath quitted said House about Eight Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Janry. 1788  Wm Achiner

Sarah Walker
