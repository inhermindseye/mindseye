Westmr  Ann Busher upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes and that her father John Bishop lived in and rented a house in Princes Street in the Parish of St. George the Martyn in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds for Ten Years or thereabouts and there dyed

Sworn 27th. March 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Ann Busher
