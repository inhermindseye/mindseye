Middx.  John Richardson upon Oath Saith he has a wife named Ann & two Childn. Abigail aged abt. 4 Yrs. and William about 4 months and that whilst a Singleman he lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Corlaces in the Parish of Garstang in the County of Lancaster at the yearly wages of two pounds for one Year and has not Since rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Jany. 17th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of John Richardson
