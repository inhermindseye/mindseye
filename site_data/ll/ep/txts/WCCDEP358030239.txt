Middx  Eliza. Goodwin aged abt. 47 Yrs. Wido . of Robt. Goodwin upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Rt. Goodwin on the 21st Day of Sept 1739 at the Fleet London Says her sd. husband hath been dead about 8 Months Says her sd. husband was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mark Smith a Waterman who lived in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Says the sd. Robt. Goodwin never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or belief Says she has one Child (to wit) Mary aged upwds. of three Years & is now poor & not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworn Octr. 27th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Eliz Goodwin
