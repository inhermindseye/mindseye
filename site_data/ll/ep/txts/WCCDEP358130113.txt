Middx  Richard Barber Aged about 50 Years upon Oath says that about Nineteen years Ago he rented a House next Door to the Taylors Arms in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards, lived therein Six Months, and Quitted the same upwards of Eighteen Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant Says he Intermarryed with his Wife Jane at the Fleet London about Seventeen Years since by whom he hath two Children (to wit) Jane aged about Fourteen Years & Cheshire aged about Eight Years & being poor is not able to maintain them

Sworn the 28th. Day of Jany. 1756 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Richd Barber
