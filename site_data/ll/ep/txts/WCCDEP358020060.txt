Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Grimes aged about 62 years upon his Oath saith that about 28 years ago he Intermarried with Elizth. his present Wife at the Parish Church of Kingston in Hampshire that he hath Three Children living by his said wife Two of which are provided for and one unprovided for vizt. Priscilla aged about 6 years that he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one John Ausley a Waterman at Stangate Wall in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry that since he has been out of his said Apprenticeship he has not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of March 1788  Philip Dyot D. Walker

his John Grimes Mark
