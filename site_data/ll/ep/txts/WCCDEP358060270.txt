Westmr.  John Arthur upon Oath saith he Served Seven Years apprentiship with his father Andrew Arthur a Perukemaker & during the whole term of his Sd. Apprentiship lodged in his sd. Fathers house next Door to Mr. Flemings at the Corner of Vear Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 26th. 1752 before}
