Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Davis aged about 42 years upon her Oath saith that about Sixteen years ago she Intermarried with John Davis her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Patrick in Dublin according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Ten years since which time s he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1798 before me }

her Mary Davis mark

Witness B Newman
