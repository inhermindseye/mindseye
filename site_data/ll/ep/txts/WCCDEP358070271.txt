Westr to wit Christian Rankin Aged upwards of fourteen Years on her Oath says she was bound an Apprentice , by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex by Indenture bearing date the 7th day of October 1772 . to one Thomas Blencowe a Glazier  the Corner of Bloomsbury Court in the Parish of Saint Georges Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex untill this Examinant should accomplish her full age of Twenty one Years or day of Marriage as she has been Informed and believes; and as by the said Indentures of Apprenticeship relation being thereunto had may more fully appear And further says that she this Examinant never was Assigned never to any other Master or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this th day of June 1775, before}

her Christian Rankin Mark
