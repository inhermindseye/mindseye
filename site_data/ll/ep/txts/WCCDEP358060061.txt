Middx  Joseph Prior upon Oath saith he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant Rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes and that his father Nathan Prior deced Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Moore a Weaver and lived the last Year of his time with the said Moore in Bunhill Row in the Parish of St. Luke Middx

Sworn 22d Janry 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell
