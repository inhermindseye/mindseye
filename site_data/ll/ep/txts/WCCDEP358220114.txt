Middx & Westmr.}  Elizth. Blackwell Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House paid Taxes or was an Apprentice Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Newby in Warwick Court in the Parish of Christ Church in the City of London for Three Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds for the first Two Years and Three four pounds for the last Year And further Says that on the Twenty Seventh day of December last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Williams who lately kept a Chanders Shop in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized by the Name of Elizabeth Says that one Joshua Ludgate a Leather Pairer is the Father of the sd. Female Bastard Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body the latter End of February 1769 and at sevl. Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as afsd. And that the sd. Joshua Ludgate and no other person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of Janry 1770 before Us} Sam Wright W Kelyngs

Eliz Blackwell
