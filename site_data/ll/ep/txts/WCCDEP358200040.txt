Middx  Sarah Peak Aged about 19 Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Parish of Isleworth in the County of Middx to Geo: Burnett of the Parish of St. Clement Danes of the same County Bookbinder with whom she now liveth and hath Served Four Years of her said Apprentiship, And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when Born Says James Hall an Apprentice to the said Mr. Burton did beget the said Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body Sometime

in the Month of May Last and at several Other times Since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 2d Day of Decr 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Peak

Upon Mr Williams Complaint J: W
