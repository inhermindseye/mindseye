St Clement Danes  19th Janry. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  Errata Upon Enq the pson not Settled she was Removd from St Margts- (to wit) Hannah Robinson  Singlewoman Aged 21 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived with Mr. Trundle at the Red Lyon in Houghton Street  Clare Market in the said parish of St Clement Danes for abt. One Year & One Month at the Annual Wages of £5 that they left the House some time in the Month of July or August 1789 and that since they left the paid House the Deponent's hath not Obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent further Upon her Voluntary Oath saith that She is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes , And that one Rogers who Works at Mr.. a Curner's in King Street  Drury Lane and uses the Cock & Pye Public House in the said Lane is the only real & true father of the Child or Children of which she is so pregnant kept no one else having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponent's Body and begotten the Same thereon And this Deponent further saith that no other person hath ever been Criminally Connected with her this Deponent And that she is unable to support herself during her the Pregnancy or the Child or Children of which she is so Pregnant when Born

Sworn the 19th day of Janry 1792 before me}
