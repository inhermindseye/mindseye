Middx.  Elizabeth Patterson aged abt. 68 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr Barrett a Wax chandler at Mr Davenports opposite St. Clements Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the first five Years and £6. for the two last Years and hath quitted said Service abt. Six Years since which time she hath Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 26th. day of Novr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Eliz Paterson
