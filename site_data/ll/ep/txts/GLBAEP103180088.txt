Middlesex ss}  Robert Thornton maketh oath That divers Years ago he lived in & rented a house in Artichoak Lane in the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middx & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same & also Served his Apprenticeship in to parish That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor hath he Since paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Pricilla to whom he was lawfully Married.

Sworn this. 10. day of November 1778 before P Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Robert Thornton
