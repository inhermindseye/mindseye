Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Brades of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me John Smart Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this 1st. Day of September 1772

who on her oath Saith That on the Sixteenth Day of July last past at the Workhouse of the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate She the Said Mary Brades was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since Baptized and Registred by the name of Mary Brades and that the said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the said Parish and that Henry Clerges of the Parish of Christchurch in the sd. County of Middlesex  Dealer in Hardware did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Sworn this 1st day of September 1772 before  John Smart

The Mark of Mary Brades
