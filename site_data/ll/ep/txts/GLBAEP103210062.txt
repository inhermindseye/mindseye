Middlesex ss}  Mary Mangrell  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore hired & lived as a yearly hired servant by & with Mr Walker Taylor sail maker in Flushing Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of a year & upwards That she has not Since lived as a hired servant for the space of a year out of the said Parish nor was ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this. 11. day of February 1785 before R Davies

Mary Mangrell
