Middx  Elizabeth Riley  Wife of Charles Riley living next the White Horse in Moors Yard in St. Martin's Lane upon Oath saith that her Sister  Ann Vandercomb (who is a Lunatic and resides at Mr. Acton's in Wych Street ) never rented any House was an Apprentice or hired Yearly Servant for One year That her Father Claudius Vandercomb was a Chairman and never did any Act whereby to gain a Settlement to this Examts. Knowledge That his Father John Vandercomb was a Victualler and lived in and rented a House in Marlbrough Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for upwards of One year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds And upwards and there dyed about 30 Years since as she hath been informed and verily believes and also says the said Ann Vandercomb her Sister  never was Married to any Person to this Examinants Knowledge

Sworn the 22d. day of Augt. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eliz: Riley

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Figg Overseer
