Middx  Ann Bunyan aged abt. 29 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Serv for one Year rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes that her father Henry Burgar deced. (whilst a SIngleman) lived a Yearly Servant  with a poor in the Parish of Broughton in the County of Books near five Years and never after he quitted that Service rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Serv for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Octr. 3d. 1749 before} Geo: Errington .

Ann Bunyan
