Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Dickins aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Stephenson in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith That she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Nicholas Winekley an Attorney who lives at No. 6 Barnard Inn is the father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Nicholas Winckley and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 26th. day of Augt. 1795 } Rupt Clarke

her Elizth: Dickins Mark

Man taken

Settled by Paying £26:5:0 Release Exted the 3d: day of Seprt. 1795

Child born in the house the 3d. day of Decr. 1795 named Sophia Dickins

Sent to Enfield  15th. June 1796
