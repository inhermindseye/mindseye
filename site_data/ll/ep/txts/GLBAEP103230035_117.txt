Middlesex ss  Sarah Woolldridge  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by and with Mr Eunis a Merchant in Catherine Court near Tower Hill in the Parish of Alhallows Barking in the City of London and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two years and upwards That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever married nor hath she done any act to gain a- Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th day of April 1787 before or R Davies Rob Smith

Sarh Woolldrige
