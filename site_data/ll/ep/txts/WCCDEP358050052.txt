Westmr.  James Brown upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £15 for one Year & Six months or thereabouts and quitted the same upwards of two Years ago since which he has not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 3d 1749 before} Wm: Jones

James Brown
