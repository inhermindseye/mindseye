Westmr.  Harding Holden Aged about Fifty one Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Phebe his Wife at the Fleet London about Nineteen Years ago, by whom he hath seven Children Vizt. Thomas Aged about Seventeen Years, Sarah Aged about Sixteen Years, James Aged about Thirteen Years, William Aged about Ten Years, John Aged about Six Years, Margaret Aged about Three Years, and Benjamin Aged about Seventeen Months, says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Peter Harding a Leather Seller the back of St. Clements near the Angel Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex , and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 25th day of January 1768 before} 

Harding Holden
