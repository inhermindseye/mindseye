Westmr.  Ann Thomas Aged about Forty three years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Richard Thomas with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Shoreditch about Twenty Years ago, says her said Husband has been dead about Eight Years, and that she hath one Child by her said late Husband now with her, (to wit) Ann aged upwards of Eighteen Years who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant; Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship to Mr. Bannister a Stationer in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields within the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year; neither hath she since her said Husbands Death Rented House, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 20th, day of July 1768 , before} Thos: Kynaston W Kelynge

Ann Thomas
