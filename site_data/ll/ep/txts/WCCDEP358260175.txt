Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Ann Stone aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that about fourteen years she Intermarried with William Stone her late husband (who has been dead about five Months) at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom she hath One Child living Sarah Elizabeth aged about Eight years That her said late Husband never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a settlement in his own Right That his father William Stone Rented and lived in a house in High Street  Shoreditch in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex for Three Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds that he hath quitted said House about five years since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 14th- day of Janry. 1797 R. Ford J Robinson

her Mary Ann Stone Mark
