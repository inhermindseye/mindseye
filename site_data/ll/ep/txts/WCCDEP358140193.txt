Middx.  Priscilla Francisco aged abt. 36 yrs. upon Oath says she Wife of Wm. Francisco who is now at Sea with whom she intermarried at the fleet London abt. 7 Years ago says her sd. husband served his Apprenticeship with his father John Francisco a Perukemaker in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 9th. day of Decr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Priscilla Francesco
