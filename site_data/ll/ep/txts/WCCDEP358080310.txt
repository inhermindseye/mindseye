Middlesex to wit Letitia Wilkinson Aged about Eighteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Bromley a Coach painter in Parker Street Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for Two Years or thereabouts at Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Rentg. House payg. Taxes or living a hired Yearly Sevant for one Year And further on her Oath Says that on the 12th day of September last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Morgan a Taylor in New Castle Court Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which has since been baptized by the name of Ann Letitia and is likely to become Chargable to the said Parish And that one Lawrence Fields a Surveyor in Vine Street Greenwich in the County of Kent is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body about the Middle of June 1777 at his the said Fields House and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he get her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid And that the said Lawrence Fields & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of October 1778 before  Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

Letitia Wilkinson
