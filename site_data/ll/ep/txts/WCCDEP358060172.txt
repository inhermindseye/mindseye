Middx  Ann Green upon Oath saith she intermarried with her Husband Edwd. Green in the parish Church of St. Mary le Strand on the 23d. April upwds. of 13 Years since Says her sd. husband Served Seven years Apprentiship with Mr. Sherrif a Staymaker & lived the last four Years of the said Term with the sd. Mr Sherriff in the back of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Says her sd. husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 23d. Septr. 1751 before}

Ann Green
