Middx  Jno. Newton aged 14 Yrs. upon Oath saith he has was born at Gosport in Hampshire and never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. and that his father as he informed this Ext. was a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England to his knowledge or belief.

Sworn Feby. 25th. 1754 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Jno Newton
