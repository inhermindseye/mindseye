Middx  Margt. Madgin aged about 50 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido of Henry Madgin who dyed abt. 17 Yrs. since Says her sd. late husband was born at Penrith in the County of Cumberland & lived in a house of his own there & paid Parochial Taxes Says she has not since the Decease of her sd. late husband rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief & that she hath one Daur by the sd. Heny. Madgin viz Margt . aged abt. 21 Yrs. who never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt.

Sworn March 18th. 1750 before  Tho Lane

Margreet Madgin
