Middx 

Joseph Harrup aged upwds. of 15 Years upon Oath saith he was born in  the Abbey Parish in the Town of St. Allans in Hertfordshire where his father Joseph Harrup deced lived in and rented a house for some Years & paid Scot & Lot and there died and that he this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Janry 16th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

Joseph Harrup
