Westmr.  Mary Lilley Aged about 40 Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Morin as Taylor Perukemaker in Charles Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted said Service about Sixteen Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 4th of January 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant  Benjamin Cox

Mary Lilly
