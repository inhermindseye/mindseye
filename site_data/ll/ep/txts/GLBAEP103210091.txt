Middlesex ss}  Mary Goffe the widow of Nicholas Goffe deced maketh oath that her sd late husband was by Birth an Irishman & to her Knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That she this Deponent previous to her marriage with her said husband was retaind & lived as a hired servant with one Mr Patterson a Victuallr in the Precinct of St Catherine in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of two Years & upwards That she has not since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement save what she has acquired by marriage as aforesaid That she hath two Children namely Eleanor aged about eighteen years & Ann aged about thirteen years the lawful issue of this Depont by her sd husband to whom she was lawfully married neither of which sd Children have as yet been married or lived as a hired Servant in any one place for the Space of a year or done any act to gain a settlement in their own right

Sworn this. 15. day of August 1785 before J. Hawley Robt. Smith

The Mark of Mary Goffe
