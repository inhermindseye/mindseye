Westmr. 

Benjamin Burford aged about 55 years upon Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Mills a Printer in West Smith feild in the Parish of St. Sepulchers London and hath been out of his time about thirty three years Says he never rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 14th 1746 before  Tho:Burdus

Benj: Burford
