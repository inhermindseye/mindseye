Westmr. to wit Sarah Lovelock Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her said Father served his Apprenticeship with one Mr. Marshall a Leather Dresser in Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and Unable to maintain the same when born Says that one John Tagg a Journey man Wvie Drawer who works with Mr. Stackham in Little Britain in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldersgate London had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of September last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child or the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid and that the said John Tagg and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1773 before me W Kelyngs

her Sarah Lovelock Mark

Witness Ed. Fields
