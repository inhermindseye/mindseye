Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Gray aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith That on the Second day of January 1790 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Goodenough No. 6 Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been half baptized by the name of William Henry That one William Palgrave a Merchant who lives at Yarmouth is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said William Palgrave and no other Person did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said William Palgrave is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 17th. day of Septr. 1795 } Rupt Clarke

Jane Gray

Jane Gray lives at Mr. Boddeleys at Upper Clapton near the Crooked Billett

Childs name William Henry Gray Fars . Name William Palgrave a Merchant at Yarmouth.
