Middx  Ann Pope aged about 29 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married and that she was born in the Parish of St. Margarett in the City of Canterbury where her Father lived & Rented a house & paid his Share of Taxes as she hath been Informed and beleives Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn & July 5th. 1745 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Ann Pope
