Middx  Mary Waller upon Oath saith she is the Daughter of Wm. Waller decd. by Ann his wife that neither herself nor her sd. late father ever rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year and that her late Grand father Christr. Waller rented a house & pd Scot & Lot in Catrick Parish near Richmond in Yorkshire & there dyed.

Sworn June 27th. 1753 before} Sam Pechell

The Mark of Mary Waller
