Middx  Ann Harmony Wido. of John Harmony who dyed abt. 5 Years ago upon Oath saith she was married to her said late husband about 25 years ago at the Fleet London and has by him a Daughter (Viz) Jane  aged near 15 years who never was an apprentice or yearly Servant for one year and that her sd. late husband rented a house in Smarts Buildings near the Cole yard in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and lived therein about 18 months and that her sd. late husband never after he quitted the sd house nor has this Examint. since his decease rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. Parish taxes

Sworn 7th. Janry 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann: Harmoney
