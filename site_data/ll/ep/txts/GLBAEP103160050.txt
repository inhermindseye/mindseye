Middlesex ss}  Mary Boucher the widow of Patrick Boucher deced maketh oath that many years ago and previous to her Marriage with the Said Patrick Boucher She this Deponent was lawfully married to one Richard Rapley her first husband deced who has informed her & which She belives to be true that he was duly bound & served the whole of his Apprentice ship with one Peter Pardon in Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Cordwainer & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her said late husband Rapley did not afterwards rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That her said late husband Boucher was by Birth an Irishman & to her knowledge or belief he never did any Act to gain a settlement in any place in England and that she this Deponent since the respective deaths of her said late husbands has not done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 24. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Mary Boucher
