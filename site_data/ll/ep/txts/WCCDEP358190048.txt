Middx.  Mary Garrety upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Garrety who is now abroad and beyond the Seas and that she intermarried with him in the Parish Church of St. Mary in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland on the 14th. Septr. 1757 by whom she has One Child  Edwd. aged abt. Nine Months says her said husband is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England and that before his Intermarriage with her and whilst he was a single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servt. with the Primate of Ireland in the Parish of St. Mary aforesaid for Four Years at the Yearly wages of Seven Pounds or thereabouts And this Deponent being poor was obliged to ask Charity in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes

Sworn 3d. March 1759 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Garrety
