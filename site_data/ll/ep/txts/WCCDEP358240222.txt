St Clement Danes 27th February 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St John's Westmr  (to wit) Mary Woodyer  singlewoman aged 26 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married nor obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right that she hath been informed and verily believes that she was born in lawful Wedlock Saith that her Father Rented and resided in an House in Tufton Street in the parish of Saint John the Evanlegis t in the City and Liberty of Westminster in the Said County of Middlesex for upwards of 3 years at the Annual Rent of £18 that since he quitted the same about 4 years ago and hath since never Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise acquired any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 27th day of February 1793 before us} Addington Mitchell

Mary Woodyer

Removed 27th. February 1793 by Me B Newman
