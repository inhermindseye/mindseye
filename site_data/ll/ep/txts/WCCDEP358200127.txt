Middx  Jane Glass Aged about Thirty seven Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father John Glass Rented a House in Howard Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about half a Year at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds or thereabouts hath quitted same upwards of Twenty Years & never after Rented House or Paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child. or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when Born Says One John Turner a Bearer had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime

in the Months of November & December last and at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 20th 28th Day of July 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jane Glass
