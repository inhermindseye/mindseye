Westmr. to wit William Bryan Aged about fifty Years on his Oath says he never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one William Davis  Esquire in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Seven Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Eight Guineas hath quitted his said Service about Six Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented 10 Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 26th. day of July 1771 before me} R. Butler

his William Bryan Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
