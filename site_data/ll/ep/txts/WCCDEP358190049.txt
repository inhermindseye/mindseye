Middx.  Sarah Ellory aged upwards of 50 Years upon Oath says she is the wife of Nichs. Ellery ( Gone from her ) with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London on the 17th. of May 1737. says her said husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Peter Lescoll a Shoemaker in the Parish of St. Brayhog near Waidbridge in the County of Cornwall And never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief And this Deponent Upon her Oath further saith that she being Poor and unable to Maintain herself was Obliged to Ask Charity in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx.

Sworn July 17th. 1760 before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Ellory
