Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Eleanor Hassell aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Cooper in Lombard Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Woolnorth in the City of London for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Six years since which time She hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Janry 1796 } Rupt: Clarke J Mathew

Eleanor Hassell
