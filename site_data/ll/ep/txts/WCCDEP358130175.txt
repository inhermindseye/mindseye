Middx  Susanna Shelley aged about fourteen Years upon Oath Says, That James Shelley her late Father Deceased Rented a House in the Parish of Waltham Abbey in the County of Essex and that She was born there Says She was kept in the Workhouse of the sd. about two Years Parish And was discharged from the same about Three Years since.

Sworn the 7th. Day of June 1756 . before} Barth. Hammond

The mark of Susanna Shelley
