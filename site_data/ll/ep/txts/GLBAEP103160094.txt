Middlesex ss}  Judith Newman the widow of John Newman deced and late the widow of John Dudley deced maketh oath That her Said first Husband rented a house in Lower East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a legal Settlement elsewhere That her Said Second husband was by birth an Irishman & to this Deponents knowledge information or belief he never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in any Part of the Kingdom of Great Britain

Sworn this 27th day of May 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Judith Newman
