Westmr.  Charles Cotton Aged about Fifty Years upon Oath Says he Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with the Duke of Leeds in St. James Square in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Eight Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted said Service about Nine Years & hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 19th of Septr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant  Benjamin Cox

Charles Cotton
