Middx  Teresa Beedle upon her Oath saith yt. she is a SInglewoman & was never married & yt. abt. 12 or 13 Years ago her Father Benj: Beedle rented a House in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £30 for the Space of Two Years & upwards & there dyed & further saith that she never rented any House pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being now Poor & Lame is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 18th- Day of July 1741 , before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Teresa Beedle
