Middlesex to wit  Mary Quintin  Widow of John Quintin  Taylor deceased maketh Oath and saith that she is in the Fortyninth year of her age and that about 22 Years ago she was lawfully married to her said late Husband at the parish Church of St Olave Tooley Street and that he was a Frenchman and had not then nor did ever since (as he after informed this Examinant and which she verily believes to be true) do any Act since he came Born Normandy where he was born whereby to gain a legal Settlement in England or elsewhere either by Servitude renting a Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or by payment of any kind of Taxes or otherwise howsoever And this Examinant saith that her Maiden Name was Mary Bennett and at the time she was married to her said late Husband John Quintin she lived an hired yearly Servant with Mr John Gray in St. Thomas Street in the Parish of Saint Thomas Southwark in the County of Surrey where she had then lived for full two continued years together at five pounds a year Wages for the first year and Five pounds and ten Shillings for the second year And saith that since the Decease of her said late Husband which happened about two years ago she hath not lived a Servant for any space of time paid ten pounds a year Rent for an House or Lodging or any Parish or other Taxes or done any thing which to her Knowledge or Belief hath gained her a Settlement in any Parish or place

Sworn the 17th. Day of December 1790 Sampr Wright N Bond

The Mark of Mary Quintin
