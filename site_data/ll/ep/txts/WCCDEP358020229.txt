Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Elizabeth Dyke aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that about Two years ago she Intermarried with Samuel Dyke her present Husband at the Parish Church of Lambeth That her said Husband informed this Examinant which Information she verily believes to be true that he lived as a Yearly Clerk with Brent of Scotland Yard for upwards of Twenty Months that he Rented and lived in Lodgings during such Service in Northumberland Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Forty days and upwards at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and that after he quitted same he never did to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th- day of October 1790 }

Philip Dyot D Walker both together

Elizabeth Dyke
