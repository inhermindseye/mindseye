Middx  Elizth. Southgate aged Twenty Six Years On her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband Robt. Southgate (gone from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Strand about Ten Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children living now provided for says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House Opposite the Turnpike near Shoreditch Church in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex for three Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Five Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Twelve Months ago since which time her said Husband neaver gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or Otherwise Howsoever to her knowledge or Belief

Sworn this 3rd. day of April 1779 before R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

Elizabeth Southgate
