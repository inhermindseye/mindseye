Middlesex ss}  Thomas Goodwin maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house in the new walk in the Parish of Saint John in Southwarke in the County of Surry and during his Residence therein was Charged to and paid the Poors Rates for the same and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent hath not Since rented a house of the yearly renter Value of Ten pounds nor paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that he hath wife named Christian and Four Children namely John aged about eleven years Elizabeth aged about Seven years Deborah aged about Six years and Susanna aged about four months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said wife to whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this 4th. day of October 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

Thomas Goodwin
