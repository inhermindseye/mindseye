Middx.  Ursula Jarrett aged abt. 61 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Jarrett who has been dead abt. Seven Years says her said husband Rented a house in Dyot Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx . of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for 30 Years and there dyed says she Continued in the said house for One Year after her sd. husbands decease and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 3d. of Novr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ursula Jarrett
