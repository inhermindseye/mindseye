Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit David Lloyd aged about 59 years upon his Oath Saith That he lived as a hired Servant Seear Seven years with one Mrs Western No 48 Welbeck Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Sixteen Guineas that he hath left the said Service about 14 years Since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months Rented any house Tenement or lodging of ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th day of Decr 1799 before us} Saml Edwards Menter

David Lloyd
