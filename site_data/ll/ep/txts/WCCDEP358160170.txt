The Exaicon of Mary Bagnald taken the 23d. day of Augt. 1740 who upon her Oath Saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never Marryed and that on the 20th. day of July in the Year 1735 she this Exaiant was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at ye House of one Mr. Cantrell in Change Court in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx wch hath since been Baptized Mary is now liveing & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. parish this Exaiant being poor & not able to maintaine the same And further upon her Oath Saith that one Wm. Hugo did begett the sd. Child on her body she this Exaiant at such time liveing as a yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Justice Denton in Howard Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes and the sd. Wm. Hugo at the same time liveing as a Yearly hired Servt . in the same House with this Exaiant became acquainted with her and had Carnall knowledge of her Body Sevll. times in the sd. Mr. Justice Dentons House at one of wch times he got her with the Female Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesd. and that he the sd. Wm. Hugo & no other person is the true and reall father thereof

Sworn the day & yeare above named before} Jas: Gronous

Mary Bagnald
