Middx.  Wm. Roberts Upon Oath says he intermarried with Martha his Wife at Egham Church in Surry abt. a Week ago. says before their said intermarriage he had Carnal knowledge of the sd. Martha's Body sevl. times and at the time of their sd. Marriage she was and is now pregnant with Child or Children And that the Parish Officers of Egham in Surry aforesaid provided a Wedding dinner for this Examinant & his Wife which Consisted of a Shoulder of Mutton & Onions and two Gallons of Strong beer and that the said Parish Officers gave the sd. Martha a King in Order to be Married to this Examinant

Sworn this 24th. Novr. 1759 . before} Fran: Bedwell

William Roberts
