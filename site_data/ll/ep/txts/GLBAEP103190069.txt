Middlesex ss}  Henrietta Clarke the wife of William Clarke maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her at sea on board his Majesties Ship the Resolution That he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with Mr Brown a Cabinet maker in the Parish of St Thomas in the City of Bristol & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband.

Sworn this 3d. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Henrietta Clarke
