Middx. ss.  Lisse Ann James aged abt. 40 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thos. James who has been dead abt. Six Years says her said husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Davis a Watch maker in Nags head Court on  Snow Hill in the Parish of St. Sepulchre in the City of London and never in his life time nor she since his Death Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn July 3d. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Lisse Ann James

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Barber Overseer J : P:
