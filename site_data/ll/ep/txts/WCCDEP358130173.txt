Middx  Mary Lock aged about Fifty Years upon Oath Says, She is a Widow and without Child or Children, Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stevens in St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Coun -ty of Middx. for two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds and hath quitted sd. Service about Nine Years, Says She hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servt.

Sworn the 29th. Day of May 1756 . before}

The mark of Mary Lock
