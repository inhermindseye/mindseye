Middx  Eliz Longstaffe  Widow of Thomas Longstaffe upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Thos. Longstaffe upwards of 30 Years ago at Lambs Chappel near Cripplegate London Says her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Taylor a Cheesmonger in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and afterwards lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds in St. Clemts. Church Yard in the same Parish for two Years or thereabouts Says her sd. late husband never after he quitted the sd. house in St. Clements Church Yard rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place Says her sd. late husband died about 28 Years ago and she has not since his Decease rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 26th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Eliz Longstaff
