Middlesex ss}  Mary Rippon  Wife of William Rippon maketh oath that her Said husband has lately absented him Self form her in Places to her unknown and left her who by destitute of Support That her Said husband heretofore rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Six Pounds in the Parish of St Pancras in the Sd County & was charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards for Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to Gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a Child names John Aged about thirteen Months the lawful issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband to whom She Was lawfully married.

Sworn this. 18. day of August 1787 before  R Davies  Rob: Smith

The Mark of Mary Rippon
