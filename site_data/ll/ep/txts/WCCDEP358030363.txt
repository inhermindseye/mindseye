Westmr. 

Marg Sparwell upon Oath saith she never was married Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Governers of Christ hospital London to Elizabeth Deane a Sarcenet Cleaner for the Term of four Years Says she Served three Years and upwards of her said Apprentiship with the sd. Mrs. Deane in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & then her sd. Mistress dyed Says she never was assigned over to any other Person rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn 20th. March 1746 before}
