Middlesex  tostLuke Oldst  J. to wit William Brown aged about Seventeen years on his Oath says he was born in Brick Lane in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middlesex where his Father now resides And that he this Examinant never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year served as an Apprentice rented any House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or hath done any Act Matter or Thing Whereby to the best of her Knowledge he may have gained a Settlement Elsewhere than in the said Parish of Saint Luke Old Street aforesaid

Sworn this 5th. day of Novr. 1784 before me M Addington

William Brown
