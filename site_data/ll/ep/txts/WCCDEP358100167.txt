Middlesex & Westminster}  Sarah Sealey Aged about Nineteen Years on Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Wm. Sealey lives in a House of his Own in Great Saxon in the Parish of Saxon in the County of Norfolk for sevl. Years last past and that she has gained any other Settlemt. than by her Father And further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes (she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born) And that one Thomas Tatham a Carpenter who lodges at Mrs. Kilokins in Stone Cutters Alley  Lincolns Inn Fields is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of May last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at Several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Thos. Tatham & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of Janry 1784 before R Butler

Sarah Sealey
