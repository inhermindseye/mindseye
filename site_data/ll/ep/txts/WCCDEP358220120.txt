Middx & Westmr.}  John Cater on behalf of Mary Brown Aged abt. Sixteen Years on his Oath Says that John Brown Father of the said Mary Brown Rented and lived in a House in Brassey Lane in the Parish of Isleworth in the County of Middlesex for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or thereabouts and where the said Mary Brown was born as he believes And Says that the said Mary Brown was bound out apprentice to Frances Allen a Milliner in the Strand by one Mr. Maxwell belonging to the Asylum and served Three Weeks of her said Apprenticeship and was afterwards Discharged therefrom by Two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the County of Middlesex as this Examinant has been Informed and verily believes to be true And further says the said Mary Brown never gained any or. Settlement than Thro her Father to the Knowledge or belief of this Examinant

Sworn this 19th. day of January 1770 before Us} R. Butler

Jno. Cater
