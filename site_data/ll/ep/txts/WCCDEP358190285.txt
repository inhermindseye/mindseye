Ann Mathews aged about 53 Years upon Oath Says She is the Widow of Jonathan Mathews to whom She was Married in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields about Twenty Eight Years since and who has been dead about three Weeks Says her said late Husband Rented a House in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and lived therein upwards of Twenty Years and paid all Parochial Taxes.

Sworn the 12th. day of October 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Mathews
