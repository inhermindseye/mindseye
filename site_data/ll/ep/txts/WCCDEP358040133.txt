Middx.  Eliza Shelton Wife of Mathias Shelton (now a Soldier in Coll Parkers Company in the First Regimt. of Foot Guards) removed from the Parish of St. Bottolph Bishopgate London by Order dated the 23d. Day of Novr. 1747 upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Mathias Shelton in the Parish Church of St. John at Clerkenwell about 19 Years ago Says her said husband rented one Side of a Shop & three Rooms up three pair of Stairs with the use of a back kitchen in the house of Wm. Brind in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for which Appartment this Examinants husband paid twenty pounds a Year & lived therein upwds. of two Years & hath quitted the sd. Appartment about 17 Years Says the sd. Wm. Brind their Landlord was a Hosier & kept the other Side of the Shop & had other Lodgers in his house besides this Examinant & her husband Says there was but one Street

Door one Stair case & one Privy the said house which the said Wm. Brind & the rest of the Lodgers made use of in common Says her said husband hath not since he Quitted the said Appartment rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office Says she has been informed & beleives her said husband Served his Apprentisp. with Mr Coxen a Haberdasher in New Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn Janry 8th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Eliz: Shelton
