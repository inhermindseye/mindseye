Middx  Sarah Cope aged about 22 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says her Father  Wm. Cope  deced lived in a House of his own in the Parish of Uttoxeter in the County of Stafford & paid his Share of Taxes Says she this Examint. never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or other -wise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born & that Wm. Hill a Servant to Mr. Dobby in Butcher Row in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes did beget the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her Body this Examint. liveing a fellow Servt. with the sd. Wm. Hill at Mr. Dobbs aforesd. where he became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge

of her Body in the Months of March last and April last & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Wm. Hill & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworn 8th. Augt. 1745 before} Tho Deval Sarah Cope
