Middlesex ss}  Elisabeth Gardner of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County widow maketh oath that she knows and is well acquainted with one Arthur Percival late of the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the Said County Victualler but who now his dangerously ill in Guys hospital in the Burrough of Southwarke and therefore Cant Attend to be examined as to his Place of Settlement That he the Said Arthur Percival lately lived in and rented a Dwelling house in the Said Parish of St John Wapping and lived therein for the Space of Six Weeks & upwards She this Deponent living With him in the Said house for all that time When he Quitted the Same he the Said Arthur Percival having taken the Said house for half a Quarter only for which he was to pay to the Land Lord thereof one Pound and fifteen Shillings being after the rate of Fifteen Pounds by the year as he in formed this Deponent and which She verily believes to be true That it appears by a Receipt under the hand of Mr Thomas Carleton the Land Lords Rent gatherer & Produced to this Depont. by the said Arthur Percival that the Sum of one Pound fifteen Shillse was paid by the Sd Arthur Percival as Rent for the occupation of the Sd house during the Said half Quarter of a year That the sd Arthur Percival hath a Child named John Aged four years & three Quarters the lawful issue of the sd Arthur Percival by his late wife deced who is become Chargeable to the sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this- 16. day of October 1787 before  R Davies  Robt Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Gardner
