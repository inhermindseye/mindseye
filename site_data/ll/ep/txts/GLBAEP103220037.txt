Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Trevillian maketh oath that her late Father George Trevillian  mariner deced was born in Penzance in the County of Cornwall as she has been often informed by her Aunt Jane Trevillian her Said Fathers Sister & which this Deponent believes to be true & this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting her Said Fathrs Settlement That She this Deponent has been Credibly informed & which She believes to be true that at the time of this Deponents birth her Said Father had a wife then living to whom he had been lawfully married previous to his Marriage with this Deponts Mother That She this Deponent was born in Cock Alley in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County of Middlesex and never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever was lawfully married nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 5. day of May 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Trevillian
