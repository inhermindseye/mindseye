Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Bates aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that about 9 years ago she Intermarried with William Bates her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Thomas at Liverpool That her said Husband Rented Lodgings of Mr. Tinkler in Crown Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Six Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Twelve Six Months years since which time he has not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of April 1789 N Bonds N Ferster.

Mary Bates
