Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Charlottee B aihm  aged ab of No. 11 Marshall Street Carnaby Markett on behalf of Sarah Adams an Infant of the age of Eleven years & upwards her Niece Maketh Oath and Saith That James Adams the father of the said Sarah Intermarried with Eleanor at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about 14 years since that he was bound Apprentice to Oril Adams this Examinants father a Printer at Birmingham for Seven years that after he had served little better than Six years of his said Apprenticeship the said Oril Adams failed in Business that then he was duly Assigned over unto one Hett a Printer in Great Wild Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex where he served the remainder of his Apprenticeship which was about Six Months that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief.

Sworn this 7th. day of May 1789 R Butler

Charlottee Baihm
