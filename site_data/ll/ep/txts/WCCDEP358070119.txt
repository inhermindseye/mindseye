Westmr.  John Lawrence aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says he never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one William Taplin a Carpenter and Joiner the Parish of Saint Nicholas in Newport in the Isle of  Wight and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never lived a hired Yearly Servant or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of August 1773 before W Kelynge

John Lawrence
