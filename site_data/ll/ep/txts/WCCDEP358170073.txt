Middx  The Examination of Mary Mascall the Wife of Wm- Mascall taken the 24th- Day of June 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with the sd. Wm- Mascall on the 4th- Day of Novr- 1738 at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx And that her sd. Husband was by Indenture bearing Date the 19th- Day of May 1733 bound an Apprentice to Nichs- Mascall of the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Bricklayer for the Space of 3 Years & a half & after he was out of his Time he continued to live with the sd. Mr. Mascall as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year & upwards but under what Agreemt. she knows not and this Examinant further on her Oath saith that her sd. Husband never rented any House pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr- or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place as her sd. Husband hath informed her & which she beleives yt- he is now a Marine in Coll: Diwards Regimt. in the West Indies & haveing one Child (to wit ) Jno . aged one Year and upwards being & Poor is not able to maintaine it

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}

The Mark of Mary Mascall
