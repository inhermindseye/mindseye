Middx  Mary Wafer upon Oath Saith she is the wife of Joseph Wafer by whom she has 3 Children Thos. aged upwds. of 12 Years Sarah aged upwds. of 10 Years & Mary Ann aged upwds. of Seven Years that this Examinant & her sd. husband are both Natives of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England and that she has been informed and verily believes her sd husband Served Seven Years apprentiship with Mr Beech a Glass grinder at the back of the blind Quay in the Parish of St. John at Dublin in Ireland and never rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Augt. 9th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Wafer
