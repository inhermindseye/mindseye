Middx  Martha Meed Aged about 25 Years Upon Oath Says she was never Marryed and that she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mr Whennell at the Sign of the Sun in St: Clements Lane in the Parish of St: Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and hath quitted her said Service about Three Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 10th Day of June 1763 before}
