Westmr  Thos. Wilson Aged about 59 Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Mary in the Parish Church of Belfast in the Kingdom of Ireland about Thirty three Years ago Says he has One Child by his said Wife (to wit) Elizth. Aged about Sixteen Years Says he Rented a House in Little Russell Street Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. One Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds hath quitted said House about Ten Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 19th Day of October 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Thos Wilson

Upon Mr. Ralp William Complaint Ovr J W
