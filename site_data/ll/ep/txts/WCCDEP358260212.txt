Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Adam Wilcox Burbidge One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Charles Murrell an Infant aged about Seven years Maketh Oath and Saith That he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Charles Murrell is the illegitimate Child of one Catherine Murrell and was born at a house opposite to the Sign of the Plough at Homerton in the Parish of Saint John at Hackney in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 16th. day of August 1797 before us } J Ploud Saml. Edward

A. W. Burbidge
