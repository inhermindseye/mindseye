Middx The Examination of Jno. Turner aged about 34 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 6th. day of March 1738 Who upon his Oath saith that John Turner his Father about 30 Years ago lived in & Rented a House in Blackamoor Street in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds - and upwards & lived in the same for the Space of 12 Years & upwds. And this Examinant further saith That he never Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleife & that he is now Sick and Poor & not able to maintaine himselfe.

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Tho Lane

John Turner
