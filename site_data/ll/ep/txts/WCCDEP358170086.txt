Middx  Sarah Swan  Wido . of Jno. Swan upon her Oath saith yt- she Intermarried with her sd. late Husband Jno. Swan ye. 8th- Day of June 1719 at Bishop- Gate Church & yt. her sd. Husband served 7 Years Apprentisp with his Father Jno. Swan in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Cordwainer & continued to live with his sd. Father till he went abroad to the West Indies & there dyed that she hath not since the Death of her sd. Husband rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 9th- Day of July 1741 , before} Jas: Gronous

Sarah Swan
