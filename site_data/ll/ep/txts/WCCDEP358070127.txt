Middx & Westmr.}  Rachael Kite Aged about Eighteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wilton a Baker in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and four Months at the Yearly Wages of Poun hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Months ago and hath not since quitting the same ever gained o  other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Robert Dunn a Journeyman Baker who lately lived with her sd. Master and who now lives in St. Martins Lane had carn Knowledge of her Body the Beginning of Janry last Butc Particular day she does not remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he get her worth Ch of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and t the said and that the said Robert Dunn and no Other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 4th day of Septr. 1773 before}

her Rachael Kite  Mark Witness Petr Sidebotham
