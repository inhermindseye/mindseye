Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charles Ewington aged about 58 years upon his Oath saith that about Fifteen years ago he Intermarried with Hannah  his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin Ludgate by whom he hath Three Children living Vizt. Samuel aged Thirteen years William aged 13 years Samuel aged 13 years and Armenad aged about 7 years That he rented and lived in a first floor unfurnished of Mr. Elliot No. 18 Castle Street  Oxford Market in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for upwards of Twelve Months at the yearly rent of Twelve Guineas that he hath quitted said Apartments about Three weeks only and saith that he is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 28th. day of July 1798 before us } R. Ford Edwd: Naish

Chas Ewington

Wittness H Day
