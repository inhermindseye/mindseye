Middx ss  Ann Fleetcraft  Widow of Thomas Fleetcraft Maketh oath that her sd. Husband has often informed her that about forty Years ago her sd. late husband was bound apprentice to one Mr Price a Victualler Who then lived in the parish of St. Katherine Creed in the City of London and Served his apprenticeship out by Means Whereof her sd late husband gained a legal Settlemt. in the sd. parish And this Deponent Saith that her sd husband did Not do any Act to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent Whereby to gain or acquire any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th day of Septr 1750 before us  Rd. Ricards  Saml Gower

The Mark of Ann Fleetcraft
