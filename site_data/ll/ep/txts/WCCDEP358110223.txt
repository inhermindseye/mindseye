Middx  Bryan Philpot upon Oath Saith he was married to his present Wife Mary Philpot at the Fleet London upwds. of four Years since and that he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Jno. Philpot a Printed whom he Served abt. two Years and then was assigned over for the remainder of his time to Danl Stevens a Printer and lodged the last two Months of his sd. Apprentiship at the sd Mr Stevens house in Canary Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and that he never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for One year

Sworn March 31st. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

Bryan Philpot
