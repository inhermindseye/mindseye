Middlesex ss  Ann Potter  widow of John Potter deced Maketh oath That her Said late husband has informed her that he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages with a Person (whose name She Can't at present recollect who kept the Horse Guards Taphouse in the Parish of St Margaret in the City & Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex & Continued in Such Service for a year & upwards & by means thereof as she is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. is the better induced to believe Such information to be true for that about Seven Years ago her Said husband Swore his Settlement to be in that Parish And it was then admitted by the Parish officers there to be his place of Settlement And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge or belief her Said late husband never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She Since his Death done any Act to gain a Settlement herself And that She hath a Child named Thomas Aged about Six Years the lawfull Issue of this Depont. by her Said late husband

Sworn this 5th. day of December 1769 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Ann Potter
