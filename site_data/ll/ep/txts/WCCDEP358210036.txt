Westmr.  Elizabeth Mowbray aged about 46 Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Mowbray who has been dead about Years; says that being a Widow and without Child or Children, she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Osborn in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one whole Year at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings Quitted said Service about four Years; and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year,

Sworn March 11th, 1766 , Before}
