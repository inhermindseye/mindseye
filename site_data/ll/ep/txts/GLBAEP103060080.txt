Aldgate Middx

Benjamin Pitts Maketh Oath that he Servd Seven years Apprenticeship to Samuel Pairtree in Billiter Lane in the Parish of Alhallows Staining in the City of London  Chimnye Sweeper and that he hath gaind no other Settlement Since hath a wife  Mary .

Sworn the 18th day of February 1746 before Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

the mark of Benjamin Pitts
