Middx  Charles Eccles aged upwards of Sixty Years Lodging in the parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middx, Upon Oath says that he Intermarried with his wife  Sarah who is aged abt. fifty Years upwards of Twenty Six Years Since Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Edwd: Roberts who kept the Sign of the Bull faceing Stepney Church in the Parish of St Dunstan at Stepney in the County of Middx & lived there as an Apprentice upwards of Six Years & then went to Sea Says about thirty Years ago he had a Certificate from the sd Parish of Stepney to the Parish of Rumford in the County of Essex Acknowledging him to be legally Settled in the Paid Parish of Stepney

Sworn the 10th day of August 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Charles Eccles
