Middlesex ss  John Wilford Maketh oath that he well knows John Collins a Child Aged about Nine Years the son of John Collins hereto fore of Gillingham in the County of Kent Shipwright deced by Elizabeth his late wife also deced The which said John Collins the son is lately become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and hath not done any Act to the knowledge information or belief of this deponent to gain any settlemt. in his own right And this Depont. Saith that the said John Collins the Father deced in his life time informed this Depont. that he was bound apprentice by Indenture & served his Apprenticeship with John Warrin of the Parish of Gillingham in the County of Kent  shipwright & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there The which Information this Deponent verily believes to be true and this Depont. is the rather induced to believe such information to be true for that the Indre of Apprenticeship whereby the said John Collins deced Appears to have been bound to the said John Warrin is now in this Deponts. Custody And this Depont. doth not know nor was he ever informed whether the said John Collins deced ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards or ever served any Parish offices or ever Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or whether he ever did any Act to gain a Settlement subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship

Sworn this 9th day of June 1766 before  Chris Scott  Geo: Garrett

Wilford
