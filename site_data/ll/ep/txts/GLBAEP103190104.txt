Middlesex ss}  Ann Keeling the wife of Thomas Keeling maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majesties Ship the Conquestadore in his Majesties Service that he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Wheeler a Taylor in the parish of Saint Thomas in the City of Bristol & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 19. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Keeling
