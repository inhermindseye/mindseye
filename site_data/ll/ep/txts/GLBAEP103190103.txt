Middlesex ss}  Patience Holding the wife of Thomas Holding maketh oath That her Said husband is now absent from her in his Majesties Service on board the Ship Chamberain That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Clarke a Jeweller in the Parish of St Mary in the City of Bath & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 19. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Patience Holding
