Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Bunce aged about 15 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Aisted at the Sun & Anchor in Saint Dunstans Hill in the parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the East in the City of London for about Two years at the yearly wages of Five pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 4th. day of July 1796  M Addington N Bond

John Bunce
