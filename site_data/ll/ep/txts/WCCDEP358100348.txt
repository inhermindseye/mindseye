Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Paul Shadwell to wit Elizth. Woodcock aged about 66 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Robert Woodcock he late Husband Deceased at the Parish Church of Aldgate London  Saint Botolph without Aldgate about 38 years ago Says that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in King David Lane Shadwell for Eleven Years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Sixteen years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 8th. day of Febry 1785  Sampn Wright  Thos. Gordon

her Elizth. Woodcock mark
