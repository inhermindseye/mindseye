Middx & Westmr.  Mathew Cearagan Aged about thirty four Years on his Oath Says he is a Widow or & that her Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Mr. Tox in Bunhill Row whilst he was a Singleman for fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of fourteen Guineas has quitted the same about Six Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Twenty Fourth day of March 1777 before}

his Mattw. Cearagan Mark
