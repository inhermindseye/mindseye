Middx  James Hawtin upon Oath saith he was married to Frances his wife at the Fleet London about 12 Years ago by whom he has 2 Children Hannah aged 5 Years and Eleanr aged 3 Years and that he served his apprentiship with John Ditcher a Cordwainer in the Salisbury Court in the Parish of St Brides London and never rented ten pounds a year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Feby. 6th 1755 before} Tho Lane .

James Hawtin
