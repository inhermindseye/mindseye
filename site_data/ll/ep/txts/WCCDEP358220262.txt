Westmr.  Isabella Rankin Aged about 37 Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of James Rankin who has been Dead about Six Months & with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St James in the Liberty of Westminster about 12 Years ago and by whom she hath 4 Children Namely Jane Aged about 11 Years Christian Aged about 9 Hannah Aged about 7, & Magt . Aged about 3 Years, Says her sd Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Stephen Sharp in the Parish of St. Noets in the County of Huntingdon & Served him about 6 Months and was afterwards turned over to one Mr. Picks a Perukemaker  at in the Parish of St. Johns in the City of Peterborough in the County of Northampton & served him about 3 Years & an half & then left his sd. Master & came to London where he worked Journeywork for some years as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily bels. to be true, Says that soon after her Intermarr with her said late Husband He Rented a shop & Parlour behind the same and a Room up one Pair of Stairs of one Mrs. Downs in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds but never Paid any taxes (the said Mrs. Downs being the Housekeeper let most of her House out in Lodging) & pd. all Parish Taxes due for the same as appears by the Books in the Vestry Clerks Office And further says that after her said late Husband quitted the said last mentioned Lodging he never Rented House or Paid taxes neither Hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 31st. day of May 1771 before me R A Tho Lane

Isebelle Rankin
