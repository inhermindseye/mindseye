Middx  Ann Everet aged abt. 70 Yrs. upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Jas Everet who dyed abt. 30 Yrs. since & that between 5 & 6 Yrs. ago this Examt. rented a house of Fifteen Pounds by the Year in Aldersgate Street in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldersgate London lived therein upwds. of one Year & has not since she quitted the Same rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 17th May 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Ann Everet
