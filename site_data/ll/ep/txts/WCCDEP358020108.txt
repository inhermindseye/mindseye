Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Franklin aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Martin Haley in Berwick Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Sixteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £4..10..0 that she hath quitted said Service about 36 years since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of October 1788 G Mercer Tho. Collin

his Mary Franklin Mark
