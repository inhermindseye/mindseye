Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Passd. to St. Mary Le Strand to wit John Cooper aged about 42 years on his Oath Saith that he never Rented any House or Tenement of the yearly Value of Ten Pounds paid Taxes lived as an hired yearly Servant for the Space of One year Served as an Apprentice for the space of 40 Days or did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have ever gained a Settlement in his own Right And this Examinant further saith That his Father Wm Cooper is dead that his said Father in his Life time served an Apprenticeship of Seven Years under Indentures of Apprenticeship duly Executed to John Dorrill a Taylor in Feathers Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand in the County of Middlesex and after serving his said Apprentice till the time of his Death never did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to gain any Subsequent Settlement And this Examinant further saith That he was married to Ann his late Wife now deceased at the Parish Church of Saint Geo: the Martyx Queen Square in the County of Middlesex about twelve years ago that he has one Child living vizt Samuel

by his said late Wife vizt. Samuel aged Eight years or thereabouts who has never been absent from this Examinant or gained any Settlement in his own right Saith That he was married to Mary his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about Eighteen Months ago and that he is poor and Sick and unable to maintain himself Wife & Child

Sworn this 21st. day of May 1785  Jno Barnfather  James Croft

John Cooper
