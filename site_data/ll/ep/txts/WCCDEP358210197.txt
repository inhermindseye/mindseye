Westmr.  Margery Mahon aged about 32 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Patrick Mahon (who hath absconded and left about four Months) with whom she intermarried at the Sardinian Ambassador's Chapel in Duke Street Lincolns Inn Fields about fourteen Years ago, by whom she hath one Child named Winifred aged about Nineteen Months, says her said husband is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlemt. in England to this Examinants knowledge & belief says before her intermarriage with the said Patrick Mahon and whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hoy at the Elephant & Castle in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five pounds and quitted said Service about five Months before her said Marriage with the said Patrick Mahon

Sworn the 21st. day of October 1767 , before}  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Margery Mahon
