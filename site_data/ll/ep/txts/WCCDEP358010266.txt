Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Hesse aged about 16 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or ever bound an Apprentice nor to the best of her knowledge never lived as a hired yearly servant for One year at one time that she was born in East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex that her Father kept House in the said Parish for several years

Sworn this 25th. day of February 1789  Sampn Wright M Addington

her Elizabeth Hesse Mark
