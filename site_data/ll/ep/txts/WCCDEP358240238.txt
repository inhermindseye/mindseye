St Clement Danes March 20th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Elizabeth Sturney aged 54 years or thereabouts singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Holliday in Great Shire Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for fourteen Months or thereabouts at the annual Wages of £5:0:0 and quitted that place about 2 years ago since which time she hath not Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or done any any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlem Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 20th. day of March 1793 before Me}

Elizabeth Sturney 's Mark
