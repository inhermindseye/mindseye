Middx to wit Elizth. Shade Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Taite a Watchmaker near the Adam and Eve Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of St. Pancras in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th. day Novr. 1779 before R Butler M Addington

her Elizth. Shade Mark
