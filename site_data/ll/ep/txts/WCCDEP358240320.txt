St Clement Danes September 19th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Peter at Shepton Montague Somersetshire  Removal to Latton past Recited & Appeal agst it)  Elizabeth Cook  singlewoman aged about twenty two Years or thereabouts singlewoman age Upon Oath saith that she never was Married or did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own right to the best of this her knowledge information or belief that She is informed and believes her Father William Cook was legally Married to this Examinant's Mother Ann at the parish Church of St Peter at Shepton Montague in the County of Somerset previous to the Birth of her this Examinant that subsequent thereto her said Father Rented and Resided on a Farm in the said parish of St Peter at Shepton Montague for sometime whereby he become and now and is a legal parishioner thereof And And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that she is informed and believes that in the Month of May last she was Removed by Order of Removal to Lacton otherwise Latton in the County of Essex and that she is informed And believes the said Order by virtue whereof she was Removed there was Appealed against at the next Session after

Bastard Child born Birth pendg the Appeal  her Removal hold on in the same Month of May and that the hearing of the said Appeal stood adjourned unto the 12th day of September instant when the and that when the same came on before the Session and and the said Order of Removal was Quashed that pending the said Appeal vizt. on or about the seventeenth day of June last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which hath Since been Baptis at the Poor House of the said parish of Lacton otherwise Latton and which hath been Baptized by the Names of William Cook

Sworn the 19th day of September 1793 before us Panther Edwd: Naish

Elizth Cook 's Mark

Witness Garrett
