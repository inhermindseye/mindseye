Middx}  Elizabeth Watkins  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that abt. Three Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt .. with one Mr. Miles Victualler in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of three Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & that since she left her sd- Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 2d. Day of a July 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. Watkins
