Middx  The Examination of Ann Martin  Widw . of Jno. Martin Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 5th. Day of May 1740 Who upon her Oath saith That her late Husband Jno. Martin served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Taylor in Denmark Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields  Gold & Silver Orace Weaver And this Examint. Further upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband in his life time nor she since his Death never Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & that being now Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe.

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

Ann martin
