Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Warren the widow of John Warren deced maketh oath that since her said husband death she was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Captain  Jonas Dale in Burr Street in the parish of st Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in such service under such hiring for the space of eleven Years at Certain Wages & has not been Married since her said husbands death or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 21. day of June 1785 before R Davies

The mark of Elizabeth Warren
