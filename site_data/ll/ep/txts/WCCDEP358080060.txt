Middx & Westmr}  Sarah Frogley Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Thos. Frogley served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Ferly a Lighterman in High Street in the Parish of Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with his said Master upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Nine Guineas & after parting with his said Master he never gained any other Settlement to her knowledge or beleif neither hath this Examinant done any Act whereby to gain any or. Subsequent Settlemt. than as aforesaid

Sworn this 17th. day of June 1776 before  Wm. Kitchiner  John Croft

her Sarah Frogley Mark
