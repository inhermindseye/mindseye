Middx  Susanna Strugnell aged about 21 Years Lodging at Mr. Hamerton's in Water Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she was never Married That she was on or about the Twentieth day of August One thousand seven hundred and sixty one delivered at the House of the said Mr. Hamerton in Water Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid of a Female Bastard Child since Christned Susanna which is yet living and likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the Same And that James Cowen a Waiter at the Anchor & Crown Tavern in the Strand did beget such Female Child having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in or about the Month of December last at the Anchor and Crown Tavern aforesaid (she then living Servant there) and at several times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child she was delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the 30th. day of Octor. 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant

Sunh Strugnll
