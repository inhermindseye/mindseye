Middx  Ann Ellson aged abt. twenty six years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Thos. Ellson (now at sea) says her said Husband Rented a House in Chick Lane in the Liberty Saffron hill Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the County of Middx. of the yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds lived therein upwards of a year & quitted same abt. three Months; says She hath four Children by him (to wit) Ann aged abt. seven years Mary aged upwds. of five years Lucy aged abt. two years & Thos. aged abt. one year & being poor is not able to maintain herself & them

Sworn the 14th. day of March 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Ellison Ellson
