Middlesex ss  John Grimes Singleman Maketh oath That Divers Years ago he was bound Apprentice by Indre to his Father James Grimes of the Parish of Stroud Warter in the County of Glouster  Cordwainer and Served the Whole time of his Apprenticeship in the said Parish and by Means thereof as he is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that he has not since his said Apprenticeship lived as a hired servant for a year nor Rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor served any Parish office Nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act Whereby to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 26th day of May 1767 before  Chris: Scott

John Grimes
