Middlesex ss}  Joseph Marshall the widow of John Marshall deced maketh oath that her Said late husband in his life time lived in and rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Guineas in Maypole Court in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in the Occupation thereof for more than the Space of a year and did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor has She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That she hath three Children namely Margaret aged about Six Years and five Months and Joseph a Female Child aged about four Years & two Months and John aged about two years and eight Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this. 28. day of April 1786 before J. Hawley

The Mark of Joseph Marshall
