Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Hunt Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice Covenanted or hired yearly Servant for one year Says her Father Thomas Hunt Rented and lived in a House in Ironmonger Lane in the Parish of St. Lawrence Jewry London for several years at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds as she believes and paid Parish Taxes due for same to the time of his Death and that after his Death her Mother lived therein to the time of her Death which is upwards of Twelve years ago and after the Death of her said Father & Mother this Examinant never gained any or subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 22d Day of June Instant She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Seale in Water Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which has since been baptized Louisa and is become Chargeable to the said Parish, Says that one David Dean a Cheesemonger in St. John Street Clerkenwell is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of Sepr. last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at Sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said David Dean and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of June 1778 before  Wm. Kitchiner

Jane Hunt
