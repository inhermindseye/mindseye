Westmr  John Allen Aged about Twenty five Years on his Oath Says he never was Married And that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Joseph Verone a Carver & Gilder in Little Wyld Street in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 24th. day of Decr. 1770 before me} R. Butler

John Allen
