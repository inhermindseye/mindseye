Middx  Ann Green aged abt. 19 Years upon Oath says she was bound Apprentice to Ignatius Plant (of Bennets Court Drury Lane ) by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes as appears by Indenture bearing date the 15th. day of May 1754. says she served about three Months of her Apprenticeship in Bennets Court aforesaid in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & then the said Ignatius Plant dyed says she never was assigned over to any Person nor ever Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 21st. Decr. 1757 . before}

The Mark of Ann Green
