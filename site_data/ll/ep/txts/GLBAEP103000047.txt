Middlesex ss.  Hyam Zachariah maketh Oath that he has been informed by his Father and Mother who are now deceased that he was Born in Dukes Place in the Parish of St. James Dukes Place in the City of London and further for himself saith that he has never lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any Parish or Place for the space of One Year together nor never was an Apprentice or Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds a Year or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement in his own right, nor can he give any Account of the Settlement of his Father or Mother or either of them That he has a Wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully Married at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch about Twenty Years ago & Five Children the lawful Issue by his said Wife Namely George aged about Nineteen Years, Elizabeth aged about Sixteen Years Henry aged about Five Years Ann aged about Two Years & a half and Sarah aged about Five Months which said Three last mentioned Children have done no Act to gain a Settlement for themselves & are as he believes with his said Wife who Eloped from him about Nine Months ago

Sworn this Day of Jany. 1789 before}
