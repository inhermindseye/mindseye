Middlesex to wit Mary Short aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Palmer at the Sign of the Castle in Castle Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Months ago since which time she hath never lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th. day of March 1782 before us  Sampn Wright Wm Kitchiner

Mary Short
