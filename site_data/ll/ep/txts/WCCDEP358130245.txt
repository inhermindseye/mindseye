Middx.  Sarah Ryall aged abt. Twenty Six Years upon Oath says She never was married Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year That Joshua Ryall her late Father deced Rented a House in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or thereabouts & lived in the same several Years & never after he quitted the said House, Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial office

Sworn the day of 175 before}
