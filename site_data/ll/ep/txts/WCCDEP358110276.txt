Middx  Mary Holder upon Oath Saith she never was Married and that about 5 Years ago she was delivered of a female bastard Child at the Workhouse (belonging to the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex ) in Greys Inn Lane in the sd. Parish & County, which was baptized Alice & is still living

Sworn July 18th. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Holder
