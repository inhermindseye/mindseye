Westmr.  Jane Walker aged about 70 Years Upon Oath says She is the Widow of William Walker who has been dead about Twenty Years and that she intermarried with her said late Husband in the Parish Church at Kendall in the County of Westmoreland , Says her said husband Rented a house in Long Lane in the Parish of St. Bartholomew the Great in the City of London , of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds, lived in the same about one Year & paid All Parish Taxes and never after in his life time, nor she this Examinant since his Death Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 13th, day of Novr. 176 6, before} Fran Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Jane Walker
