St Clement Danes Janry. 17th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St George the Martyr  (to wit) Mary Elizabeth Love Phillips aged 26 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Bradnog Baker in New North Street in the parish of St. George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex for a year & eleven Months or thereabouts at the annual Wages of £7 that she quitted the same in or about the Month of July or August 1792 and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 17th day of January 1794 before us R. Ford B: Kennett

M E L Phillips
