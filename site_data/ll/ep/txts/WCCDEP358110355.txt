Middx  Novr 27th. 1753

Mary Winter upon Oath Saith She is the Wife of Edwd. Winter a Sailor on board the Cumberland now lying at Chatham & has been informed & verily believes her sd husbd. (whilst a Singleman) lived a Yearly Servant wth Mr Bads a Perukemaker near the Cross in Twickenham Middx for one years & upwds. & has not since rented ten Pounds by the year Paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for other year

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Winter
