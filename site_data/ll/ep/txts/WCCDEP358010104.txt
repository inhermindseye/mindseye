Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Oliver aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that on the 11th. day of Augt. 1762 she Intermarried with Thos. Oliver her late Husband (who has been dead about 3 years ) at the Parish Church of St. Mary at Kensington that before her Intermarriage with her said late Husband he lived as a hired yearly Servd. with Henry Letouch  Esqr . who lived at Kensington in the said Parish of St. Mary for 14 Months at the yearly Wages of £20 that he has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or Paying Taxes

Sworn this 19th. day of June 1787  Sampr Wright Maddington
