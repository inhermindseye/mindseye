Middlesex ss}  Margaret Gardiner the wife of John Gardiner maketh oath That her Said husband is now absent from her in his Majesties Service as a Marine That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born in the City of Ambroch in north Britain & Never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief And that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 29. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Margaret Gardiner
