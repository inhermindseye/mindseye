Middx  Jane Polfrey aged about 26 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Wilson a Jeweller in Arrundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted said Service upwards of five Months and hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 17th. day of March last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Poole (one of the parish Nurses of the said Parish) in Clements Lane in the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes which was baptized Richard and is since dead says that one Richard Chapman a Lighterman near Bull Stairs Southwark did beget the said Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid he having had Carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the Month of May 1764 and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Richard Chapman and no other person did beget the same

Sworn the 15th. of April 1765 . before} Barth: Hammond

Jane Polfrey
