Middx  James Simpson upon Oath Saith he never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever rented Ten pounds a year or paid Parish taxes and that his late father Joseh. Simpson rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year in Bishopsgate Street in the parish of St. Botolph without Bishopsgate London and lived therein Sevl. Years and never after he quitted the sd house Rented ten pounds a year or paid parish taxes

Sworn Octr 27th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jas. Simpson
