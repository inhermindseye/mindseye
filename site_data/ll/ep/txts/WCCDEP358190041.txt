Middx  Sarah Owens aged abt. Thirty six years upon Oath says she is the Wife of James Owens with whom she intermarried at the fleet London upwards of Eight Years ago says her said husband is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England & is now at Sea on board one of his Majestys Ships of War as she hath been informed & verily believes says before her intermarriage with the said James Owens & whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a hired yearly Servant for one year with Mr. Saml. Johnson in the Parish of Malkiston in the County of Darby at the yearly Wages of Two Pounds ten Shillings says she has two Children  James aged abt. seven years & Mary aged abt. five Months

Sworn this 7th. day of Decr. 1757 . before me} Saml Pechell

The Mark of Sarah Owens
