Middx  Sarah Butler Aged about Twenty two Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wm Thornton a Mercer in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service about two Weeks And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is Likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One John Thornton  Apprentice to the said Wm- Thornton had Carnal Knowledge of this

Examinant, Body severall Times within this Ten Months Last at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant.

Sworn the 25th of May 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Butler

Upon Mr Browns Complt Ovr J. W
