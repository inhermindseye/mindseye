Middlesex ss}  Charles Manning maketh oath That divers Years ago he took & rented a house in Toolens Gate in the Parish of St Olave Southwarke in the County of Surry of the yearly Rent of Five Pounds & was Charged to & Paid Kings Taxes for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 12. day of December 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey P. Rowlands

Chas Manning

Took the house abt 8 Years ago & lived in it abt 4 Yrs paid the Tax but once it was left to his wife to pay the Taxes
