Middx.  Elizth; Chantrell aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year says her father John Chantrell Rented and lived in a house in Serle Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £40 for sevl. Years

Sworn Jany. 30th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Chantrell
