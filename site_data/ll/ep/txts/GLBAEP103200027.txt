Master of the Workhouse sworn to the Birth of a Bastard Child by the Mothers Information

Middlesex ss}  John Mackelrath  master of the Workhouse of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County maketh oath that by a warrant or Pass order bearing date the fifth day of July instant  Elizabeth Bennett therein mentioned to be the wife of Robert Bennett was removed from the Parish of St John Wapping in the sd County to the sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate as the Place of her last legal Settlement and her Child Sophia aged about two years was removed Came with her for Nurture That the Said Elizabeth Bennett upon Such her Removal refused to take Care of her sd Child or in any manner to Administer any Nurture thereto although required by this Depont so to do but hath left the same in this Deponents Custody That by an Examination in Writing of the sd Elizabeth Bennett & taken upon her oath it Appears that the said Child was born in the sd Parish of St John Wapping and Which this Deponent believes to be true

Sworn this. 15.day of July 1783 before R Davies J. Hawley

John Mackelrath
