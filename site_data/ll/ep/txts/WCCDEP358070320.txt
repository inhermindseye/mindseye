Middx to wit Mary Cook aged about Fifty Seven years on her Oath says she never was Married, Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Richard Brockett at t he Civet Cat at Temple Bar for Twelve years at the yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about fifteen years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or other- -wise gained any Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 15th day of Novr 1775 before R Butler

her Mary Cook Mark.
