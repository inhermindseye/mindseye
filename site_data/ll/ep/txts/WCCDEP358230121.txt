Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Anderson Aged about 37 years upon his Oath Saith That he never was married that he lived as a hired yearly Servant for three years with a Mr. Robert Hambleys at the Coach and Horses  Castle Street  Leicester Fields in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that he hath left the said Service about Seven years ago since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. of February 1800 before us Ws Woodenough Winster

The mark of John Anderson
