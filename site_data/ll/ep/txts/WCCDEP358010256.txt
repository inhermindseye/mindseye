Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit George Michael Mills aged about 36 years upon his Oath saith that he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes Served any Apprenticeship or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year That he was born at Croydon in the Parish of Croydon in the County of Surry where this Examinants father served his Time

Sworn this 10th. day of Decr. 1788 N Bond M Addington

Geo: Michl: Mills
