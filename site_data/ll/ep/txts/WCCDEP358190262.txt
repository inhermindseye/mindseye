Middx  Henry Fifes aged about 29 Years upon Oath Says he intermarried with his Wife  Elizabeth in the Parish Church of Much Clerkton in the County of Essex upwards of Eight Years Since by whom he hath One Child named Mary aged about Seven Years Says he Served his Apprentiship with one Mr Franks a Shoemaker in the Parish of Rumbles in Colchester in the County of Essex and that he never rented Ten pounds a Year or paid any Taxes

Sworn the 10th day of August 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

Henry Fife
