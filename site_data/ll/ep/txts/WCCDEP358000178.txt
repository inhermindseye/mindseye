Saint Clement Danes 21st. Decr- 1785

Middlesex (to wit) James Tall aged thirty five Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he was married to Deborah his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Matsellor otherwise Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex in the Month of July about fifteen Years ago, that he has five Children now living (by his said Wife) Vizt. James aged fourteen or thereabouts, who has never gained any Settlement in his own Right and is now with this Examinant Deborah aged thirteen or thereabouts who has never acquired any Settlement in her own Right but is now in Place  With a Person in Newcastle Court Butcher Row  John aged Nine Years or thereabouts, who has not gained any Settlement in his own Right and now lives with this Examinant, Margaret aged Six Years or thereabouts, and Sarah aged four Years or thereabouts. And this Examinant further Saith that he rented and lived in a House in Petticoat Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Matsellor otherwise Whitechapel at the Yearly Rent of ten Pounds for between three and four Years, that he quitted the said House about Eight

Years ago, and since quitting the same hath not rented any Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds, paid any Rates or Taxes, served any Parochial Office, or done any Act Matter or King whereby to the last of his Knowledge information or Belief he may have acquired any other Settlement, than in the Parish of Saint Mary Matsellor otherwise Whitechapel as aforesaid that his Wife is came and unable to Work, and he this Examinant is Poor and not able to maintain his said Wife and Children

Sworn this 21st. Decr 1785 before  Sampr Wright J Addington

James Tall
