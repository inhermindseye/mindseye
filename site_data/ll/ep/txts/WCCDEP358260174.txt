Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Grace Luxton aged about 26 years upon her oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with one Mr Crockford in Blackfriars Road in the Parish of Christ church in the County of surry for twelve months at the yearly by wages of six pounds that she hath quitted her said Service about 4 months since which time she hath not done any act matter or thing whereby she might have gain'd a subsequent settlement.

Sworn this 13th Day of Janry. 1797 N Bond ldham Oldham

her Jane Luxton Mark
