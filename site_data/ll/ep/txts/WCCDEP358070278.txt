Middx  Elizabeth Ansley Aged upwards of fifty Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Thomas Ansley who has been Dead about four between two and three Years and by Whom she has no Children now living and further says that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Sisson a Mathematical Instrument Maker the Corner of Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years and three Quarters at the Yearly Wages Six Pounds, hath Quitted her said Service about Six Months and hath not since She Quitted the same ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 5th day of July. 1775 before   Tho Lane

Eliz Ansley
