Middx to wit Esther Burge Aged about forty Years Wife of Jonathan Burge of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Ford an Infant aged about four Months on her Oath says that Elizabeth Ford  Singlewoman Came to Lodge with her as a Married Woman by the Name of Elizabeth Barnes and who about a fortnight ago was taken ill of the small Pox and afterwards died But before her Death this Examinant having some Suspition that she was not Married asked her several Questions with regard there to and in Order that the Infant might be taken care of and the said Elizth. Barnes Acknowledged that she never was Married that her Name was Ford and that she had lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Butler in the Parish of Ewell in the County of Surry for several Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds or upwards to the best of her Remem- -brance and that after she left her Mistress Service she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement and this Examinant further says that the said Elizth. Ford also told her that her said Child was Born in Store Street Hospital in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex and that the Father of the said Child was one John Barnes at Ewell afsd in the said County of Surry who was run away and what was become of him she did not know but that he the said John Barnes was the only  Father thereof

Sworn this 19th. day of Octr. 1779 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Easter Buge
