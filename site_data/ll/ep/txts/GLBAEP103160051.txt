Middlesex ss}  Frances Treader  Singlewoman maketh oath that sometime ago she was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with Elizabeth Whiteman in East smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service for the Space of a year & half & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That she has not since lived elsewhere as a hired servant for a year nor been ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 25. day of April 1776 before  Geo.C.Smith

The Mark of Frances Treader
