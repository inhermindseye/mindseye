Saint Clement Danes Octr 7th 1785

Middlesex 

passed as a Vagrants} (to wit) Sarah Wellings aged 72 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she was married to John Wellings her now Husband in Rachington in the County of Somerset , that her said Husband is by trade a Shoemaker and served his time as an apprentice to a Shoemaker in the Parish of Saint John the Evangelist in the City of Bristol that the doc it thereof his having ever gained any subsequent settlement and that being poor and in great Distress she has lain about in the open Air in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for three or four Nights

Sworn this 7th. Octr. 1785 before R Butler

the Mark of Sarah Wellings
