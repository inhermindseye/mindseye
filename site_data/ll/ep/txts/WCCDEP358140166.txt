Middx.  Elizth: Waller aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Henry Waller (who is a Soldier and abroad in his Majesty's Service) with whom she Intermarried in the Savoy Chapel on the 12th. day of May 1756. by whom she has One Child Named Henry aged abt. Eighteen Months says her said husband was Born in the Parish of Ardenley in the County of Sussex and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year as her said husband has Often informed this Examinant and which Information she Verily believes to be true

Sworn this 14th. day of Octr. 1758 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Elizabeth Waller
