Middx  Mary London aged abt. 40 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one year Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes and that her father lived in a house of his own in the parish of Dunstable in the County of Bedford Sevl Years which he afterwards Sold for more than £80

Sworn Novr. 30th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary London
