Westmr.  Ann Branson aged about 31 Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Joseph Branson with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of Twelve Years since and that her said Husband hath been dead about three Months; says she hath one Child by her said late husband (to wit) George aged about Eight Weeks; Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man lived a hired yearly Servant with the Right Honourable the Earl of Hertford in Lower Grosvenor Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the said Liberty of Westminster for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £20 or thereabouts and lodged during that time over his Master's Stables in Brooks's Mews behind the divalling house, and after having served about four Years as aforesaid, intermarried with this Examinant, but continued in the said Earl of Hertford's Service under the same hiring about three Years longer and was discharged in Lower Grosvener Street aforesaid And after he quitted said Service never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 15th, day of June 1767, before  Geo. Reid  Saunders Welch

The Mark of Ann Branson
