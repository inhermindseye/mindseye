Westmr. 

Ann Ivory wife of John Ivory upon Oath saith her sd. husband John Ivory was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Henry Clifton a Silk dyer and lived with him as an Apprentice near moor feilds in the Parish of St. Giles without Cripplegate London for two Years or thereabouts and then intermarried with this Examinant at the Fleet London after which he continued to live with his sd. Master for a considerable time in the same Place and then absconded from his Service and was never assigned over or lived forty days with any Person with his masters Consent during the term of his sd. Apprenticeship Says her sd. husband never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief and that she hath three Childn. (viz) Jas . aged upwds. of 5 Yrs. Ann aged upwds. of 2 Yrs & Eliz aged abt. one month

Sworn July 30th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Ivory
