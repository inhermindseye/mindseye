Middx to wit Mary Carr Aged about Fifty Three years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband William Carr (gone from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Ann Soho about Seventeen years ago and by whom she hath Two Children Living Namely Mary Aged about Fifteen years and Elizth . Aged about Eleven years both of whom her Husband Maintains as she Believes says her Husband Rented Two Houses in Vine Street near Chandos Street in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Eight years and Upwards at the yearly Rent of Thirty four Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said Houses about seven years ago since which time He hath gained no other settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds P Annum paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1781 before  Sampn Wright  Abrm Baley

Mary Carr
