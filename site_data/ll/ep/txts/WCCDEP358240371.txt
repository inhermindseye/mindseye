St Clement Danes January 10th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Margt Westmr .  (to wit) Mary Hanks  singlewoman aged eighteen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Wiltshire at No 4 in King Street in the parish of St Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for about two Years at the Annual Wages of Six Guineas that she quitted that service about two Years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the tenth day of January 1794 before us} W Addington Edw: Read

Mary Hanks
