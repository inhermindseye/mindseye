Middx  Thos. Smith on behalf of his Mother Alice Smith aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath says that she Rented a house in She Field Street in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards & lived in the same several Years & hath quitted same abt. fourteen Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 11th. day of Octr. 1757 . before me} Fran: Bedwell Tho Smith
