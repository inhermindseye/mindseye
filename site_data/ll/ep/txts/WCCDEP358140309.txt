Middx.  Sarah Hickenbottom aged abt. 28 Yrs. Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Jonathan Hickenbottom who has been dead abt. a Year & a half by whom she has one Child named Jonathan aged abt. One Year says her said husband in his life time Often informed her and which information she believes to be true that he never Rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for one Year and that his father Thos. Hickenbottom Rented and lived in a house in Hollis Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for years at the Yearly Rent of Pounds or thereabouts says she has not since her said husbands  death Rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn this 27th. Octr. 1759 before}
