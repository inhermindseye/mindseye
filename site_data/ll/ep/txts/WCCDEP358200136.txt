Westmr.  Susannah Thoroughgood Aged about Twenty Six Years upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Thomas Thoroughgood in the Parish Church of St. John the Evangelist in the Liberty of Westminster about Thirteen Months ago Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Prosser a Tinman facing Paulsgrave head Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & never after Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year as this Examinant hath been Informed by her said Husband and which Information she Verily Beleives to be true Says she has One Child by her said Husband Esther Aged about four Months

Sworn the 3d Day of Septr. 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Susan Throughgood
