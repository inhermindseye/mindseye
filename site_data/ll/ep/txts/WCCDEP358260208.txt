Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Edward Bark aged about 23 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and hired in a house in Durham News in the Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea in the County of Middlesex for Three Months at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas and paid all Taxes due for the same during which time he kept at the house of a Mr. Need in Great Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the said County of Middlesex That he hath quitted Possession of the said house about Three Months since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matters or thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information for belief he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of July 1797 before me }
