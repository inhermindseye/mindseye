Westmr.  Elizabeth Lea Aged about Thirty Years upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband John Lea in the Parish Church of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex about Six Years ago Says her said whilst a Singleman & without Child or Children he Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Messrs. Longcroft & Franklin by Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx One Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds hath quitted said Service about Eight Years & hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year as this Examinant hath been Informed

by her said Husband and which Information she Verily Beleives to be true Says she has One Child by her said Husband John Aged about Three Years.

Sworn the 20th of Decr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eliz. Lea Elizth. Lea
