Middx  Mary Kelly aged about Thirty Eight Years on her Oath any She is the Wife of Nicholas Kelly (gone from her) with whom She Intermarried at Saint Michael in the City of Dublin about Twenty Two Years ago and by whom she hath one Child living Namely Margery Aged about Nine Years Says her said husband Rented and lived in a House in Whites Alley Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for three Quarters of a Year at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds but paid no Taxes due for the same as She knows of hath quitted the said House about three Years ago and after quitting the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gaine Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Decr. 1779 before R Butler

Mary Kelly
