St Clement Danes 19th May 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James Westmr }  (to wit) Frances Brown  Widow aged about 68 years Upon Oath saith that she was Married to Paul Brown at the parish Church of St Giles in the Fields in or about the year 1762 that he left this Examinant about shortly after their said Marriage to go to Bombay in the East Indie's & she hath heard he is since dead that since she so heard of her said Husband's Death and having only one Child who was had  before obtained a legal Settlement in his own right she this Examinant lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. William Massa formerly of Wych Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes and afterwards of Parwick Street in the parish of St James Westminster for the space of three years at the Annual Wages of £5 that during the last Ten Months of such Service she this Examinant lodged at the Workshop of the said  William Massa in Warwick Street in the Parish of St James aforesaid that she left that Service about 20 years ago and hath never been Married any person since her intermarriage with the said Thomas Brown as a aforesaid and hath not since quitting her service with the said William Massa done  any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 19th day of May 1794 before us }M Addington Edw: Read

Frances Brown 's Mark

Witness B Newman
