Westmr.  Mary Lea Aged about Twenty Nine Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Yeurtt a Victr in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . One Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted said Service about Fifteen Years & hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 20th of Decr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Lea
