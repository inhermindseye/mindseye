Middx to wit Thos. Harris Aged about Seventy Two Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his late Wife at Fulham Middx about forty Seven Years ago & who has been dead about 29 Years Says he has Two Children by his said late Wife both of which are now provided for Says that before his Intermarriage with his said late Wife & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one George Turbin a Farmer at Wormwell Wood Lane in the Parish of Hammersmith in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of fifty Shillings besides diet & Lodging Says that after he quitted that Service he bound himself Apprentice to one Edward Crabb a Bargmen but the said Edwd. Crabb being a Certificated Man from Mortlake gained no Settlement thereby as he has been Informed that soon after he had served the said Crabb for seven Years he Intermarried with his late Wife and rented and lived in a House at Hamersmith aforesaid for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of four pounds and Paid poors Rate due for the same and after he quitted the said House at Hammersmith he never Rented House of Ten pounds P Ann. or ever was rated to or paid any Taxes Whatever neither hath this Examinant done any Act than as before mentioned whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 3d. day of June 1772 before W Kelyngs Sam Wright

his Thos. Harris mark
