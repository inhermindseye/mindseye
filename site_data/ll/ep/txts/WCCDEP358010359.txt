Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex ss  Lydia Allen aged thirty one Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to Francis Allen her present Husband at the Parish of Wilmington in the County of Kent about thirteen Years ago by Licence and that since there Intermarriage her said Husband until and lived in a House Queens Row Portwell in the Parish of Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Guineas for about ten Weeks, that they  quitt Amls House about Six Months age and since then have not to the best of this Examinants Knowledge done any Act whereby to gain Subsequent Settlement, And further this Examinant Saith that her said Husband is now dangerously ill at this Lodging in Lych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes , and that she this Examinant upon and unable to maintain her said Husband-And this Examinant further that she has three Children by her said Husband Vizt. Thomas aged Eleven Eleven years or thereabouts, John aged Six years or thereabouts and Lydia , aged  two three years

thereabouts and this Examinant Saith that her said for House hath never acquired any Settlement in his own right

Sworn this third Day of Septr 1790 . before me M Addington W Faster.

her Lydia Allen Mark
