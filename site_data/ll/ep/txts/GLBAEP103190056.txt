Middlesex ss}  Mary Welch the wife of Thomas Welch maketh oath That her sd husband is now absent from her at Sea in the Kings Service That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served his Apprenticeship with one Thompson a Shoemaker in the Parish of St John in Chipping Norton in the County of Oxford & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlemt elsewhere & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 15. day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Welch
