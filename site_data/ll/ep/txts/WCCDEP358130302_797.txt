Middx  John Button aged abt. 63 years upon Oath says her served his Apprenticeship of 7 years with his father John Button a Stockingmaker in Worcester Street in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry & hath not since obtained any other Settlement to his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 4th day of March 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jno. Button
