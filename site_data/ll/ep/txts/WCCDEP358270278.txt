Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Bell aged about Twenty years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the Second day of October Instant she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to this Parish That one which hath since been Christened by the Name of James that one James Carney who now lives as she is informed with Mr. Card at the Oxford Coffee house in the Strand is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said James Carney and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th- day of Octr. 1796 } J. Reeves

her Elizabeth Bell Mark
