Middx.  Cathne. Katkitt aged abt. 57 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of David Katkitt who hath been dead abt. twenty Years says her said husbd. served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with one Jeremiah Ward at the Kings Arms Tavern near Chancery Lane in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West in the City of London and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year to this Examinants knowledge or Belief says she hath not since his Death Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 2d. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cathne. Katkitt
