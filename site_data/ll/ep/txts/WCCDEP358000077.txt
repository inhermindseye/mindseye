Saint Clement Danes July 20th. 1785

Middlesex(to wit)  Exr. to obtain Warrt. othe Reputed father he taken Child and  aftwds Passd to Deptford by Christ Church Surry it being an illegitimate Child of his Wifes by her former husband  James Talboys One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Maketh Oath and saith That George Dickenson an Infant aged seven years or thereabouts is now in the Parish of Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Chargeable to and maintained by the said Parish and this Examinant further Saith that he has been informed and believes that John otherwise Edward Dickenson who lived as an Ostler at the at a Livery stable Yard near Blackfryars Bridge in the County of Surry in the Father of the said Child and has left the same chargeable to the said Parish although capable to work for and maintain the same

Sworn at the Public Office in Bone Street this 20th July 1785 before  Sampn Wright

James Talboys
