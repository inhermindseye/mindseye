Middlesex ss  Ann Robinson  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago her late Father Robinson deced rented a house in Lower Well Alley in the Parish of Saint George in the Sd County & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement & at Which time this Deponent. & also her Sister Sophia Robinson who is now living & aged about Twelve Years lived with her Said Father in the Said house as part of his Family That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in her own right That this Deponents Said Sister Sophia Robinson to this Deponts knowledge information or being never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for the Space of a Year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this - day of November 1778 before}
