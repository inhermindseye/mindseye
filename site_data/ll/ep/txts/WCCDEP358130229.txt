Middx.  Susannah Fountain aged abt. Forty Six Years upon Oath says She never was Married & that She lived a hired Yearly Servant a whole Year together with Lady Devall in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx . at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & hath quitted her said Service upwards of Twenty Years since which time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Octr. 30th 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

S: Fountain
