Middlesex ss}  Gabriel Taylor maketh oath that he knows a Child named Benjamin Smallwood Aged about eight years the Child of one Smallwood by his wife who both died in America That the Said Child was brought from thence to England & for Sometime past hath resided & Still resides with one Mrs. Peariffth in Maypole Court in the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & was born in Charles Town in America his Parents being Natives of America & never did any Act to this Deponents knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in England

Sworn this. 19. day of Novr 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Gabriel Taylor
