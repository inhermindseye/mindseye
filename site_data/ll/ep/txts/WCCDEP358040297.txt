Middx 

Susanna Lemmon  Wido . of Thomas Lemmon removed from the Parish of St. George  Hanover Square by Order dated the 15th. Day of December 1748 upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late Husband in the Parish Church of St. George Southwk. about 17 or 18 Years ago and that her sd. husband lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of 32 yrs opposite Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 10 Yrs. or thereabouts and this Examinant continued to live in and rents the Same house since his Deace for 3 Years & upwards and removed from the sd. house to another of £18 a Year opposite Essex Street in the Strand in the same Parish where she lived abt. two months and hath quitted the same about 6 months since which she has not rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 16th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Susa. Lemmon
