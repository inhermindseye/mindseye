Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Salt Wife of Barnforth Salt now living in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes taken County of Middx taken the 25th. Day of Novr. 1740 Who upon her Oath Saith that abt 18 Years ago she Intermarryed with the abovenamed Barnforth Salt & that her sd. Husband Served 5 Years the first Part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. Harvey the Corner of Chancery Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above Barrs in the County of Middx  Sadler & this Exaiant further saith that her sd. Husband was never assigned Over to any Person for the Remainder of his Apprentiship that he never rented any House pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband now is for oug she knows & hath been for some Years past abroad & beyond the Seas & being Sick & Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the day & Year above above menconed before}
