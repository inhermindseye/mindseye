Middx & Westmr. to wit Elizth. Daniel Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Hugh Stratton a Carpenter in the Parish of Saint Phillip in the City of Bristol upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of fifty Shillings and after she quitted the same she went to live a Second time with the said Mr. Stratton and lived with him at Different times upwards of Three Years and after she quitted the said Mr. Strattons Service she nevr Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one Richard Bailey who now lately lived as a Porter to Mr. Const a Taylor in Norfolk Street had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time the begining of Septr. last but the particular day she does not now Remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of Which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And this Examinant further says that on the 23d. day of May 1764 she was delivd. of a Female Bastard Child at Shireness in the County of Kent which has since been Baptized Lucy And that the aforesaid Richard Bailey and no other Person is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 12th. day of May 1770 before } Petr. Planck J Girdler

her Elizth. Daniel Mark
