Westmr.  John Harry Aged about Forty Years upon Oath Says he Served One Year and a half of his Apprentiship with Richard Stars a Taylor in Southampton Buildings Chancery Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx & then Went Abroad & never after was Assigned over to any Person Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 13th of Decr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of John Harry
