Westmr.  Frances Matthews aged upwards of 40 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Edward Matthews who has been dead about Six Years with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of East Greenwich in the County of Kent about ten Years ago; says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Jackson a Taylor in Paradise Row in the Parish of St. Luke Chelsea in the County of Middlesex for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Guineas hath quitted said Service about four Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 31st. Jany. 1766 , before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Fras. Matthews
