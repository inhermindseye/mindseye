Middx  Mary Gill Aged 38 Yrs. Apprehended Wandering & begging in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx upon her Oath saith she is the Wido. of Henry Gill & can give no Acct. of his Settlemt. other than that he was born in the County of Wheatley in the kingdom of Ireland Says she was born in the parish of St. Dunster at Stepney in the said County of Middlx & never was an Apprentice Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne March 22th. 1744 before}
