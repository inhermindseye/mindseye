Middx  Mary Frisby aged about 37 years upon Oath says She never was married bound an Apprentice lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or Rented a House Says her Father Thomas Frisby deced Rented a House of Ten Pounds a year & upwards in New Rents in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry and paid all Parish Taxes there lived therein several Years and quitted the same about 27 Years as She hath been informed and verily believes

Sworn the 15th. Day of Septr. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond  John Cox

The mark of Mary Frisby
