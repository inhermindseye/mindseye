Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Hastings aged about Forty two years upon his Oath saith That he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Simmonds a Cooper in Fetter Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Bars in the City of London and that after he quitted his said Master he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Febry. 1796 M Addington Gr Goodenough

John Hastings
