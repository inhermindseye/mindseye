Middlesex ss}  Lucy Lewis the widow of John Lewis deced maketh oath that her said late Husband has informed her and Which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to and Served the Whole of his Apprentice ship with one Mr Chandler a Butcher in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and to this Deponents knowledge information or belief he did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement nor hath She Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this day of 1776 before}
