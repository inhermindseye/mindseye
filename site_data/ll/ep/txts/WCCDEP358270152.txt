After Birth- A Oakshatt & Jno Cleeves Cathie

St Clement Danes 14th November 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman's settlement in St George Southwark  Child's birth  Reputed Father} (to wit) Amelia Oakshott  singlewoman aged twenty six 30 years or thereabouts-Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never lived as an hired yearly Servant or done any other Act Matter or thing watsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own right that she is informed and believes that she is the legitimate Daughter of Francis and Elizth Oakshott that she is informed and her said Father Rented and Resided in a House in Revell's Row in the parish of St George the Martyr in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surrey for a year or upwards and paid the parochial Rates for the same that he quitted the same about thirteen twelve or thirteen years ago and hath not since done any act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this ****Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that she on the on or about the fifth day of May last at the House No 56 Clem Little Shire Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which hath since been named John Cleeves Cathie that John Cleeves Cathie a Corporal in the Grenadiers Company of in the Westminster Regiment of

Middlesex Militia is the only real true father he havg had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body first about the twelfth day of November 1787 and at Several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child on this Examinants Body

Sworn the 14th. day of November 1793 before me} D Walker

Amelia Oakshott

the Man taken and gave a Note of hand to pay indemnify the parish afsd. the Maintenance of the Child from the time he should be dischged
