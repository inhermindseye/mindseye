Middx  Anthony Needham upon Oath saith she lived a Yearly Servt. for the Space of three Years or thereabouts (whilst a Singleman ) with Mr. Fisher at the two Oaks in the Parish of Rippon in the County of York at the Yearly Wages of 50s. and hath quitted his sd. Service about Twenty Years since which he has not rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 6th 1749 before}
