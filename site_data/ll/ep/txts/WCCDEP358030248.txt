Middx  Jane Farris  Wido . of Jno. Farris upon Oath Says she intermarried with the sd. Jno. Farris in the Parish Church of St. Leonard in Shoreditch in the County of Middx abt. 30 Yrs. since Says the sd. John Farris hath been dead abt. 8 Years Says the sd. John Farris rented a house in Peter Street in Half moon Alley in Bishopgate Street in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Bishopgate London of the Yearly Rent of Eleven pounds and Lived therein Seven Years or thereabouts and hath Quitted sd. house about 16 Years Says her sd. late husband John Farris after he quitted sd. house in Peter Street never in his Life time nor this Examinant since his Death rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes Served any Parochiall Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 7th: 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The mark of Jane Farris
