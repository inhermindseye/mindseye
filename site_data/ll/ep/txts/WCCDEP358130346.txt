Middx  Cath: Price aged abt. 23 yrs. upon Oath says she never was married says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Lyon a Baker in Black Man Street in the Parish of St. George  Southwark in the County of Surry for one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted said Service abt. two years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt.

Sworn the 30th. of May 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Cath: Price
