Middx  Margt. Lyon Wido . upon her Oath saith that about 27 Years since she was married to her late Husband Richard Lyon in the House of Mr. Mc Dermont at the White house the upper End of Osmontown Green in Dublin according to the Rites & Ceremonies of the Church of Rome Says her sd. Husband hath been Dead about Six Years that her sd. Husband lived in & rented Part of a house in Devereux Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £20: for the Space of Three Months & upwards & had Water & all other Necessarys belonging to the sd. House to his own Use & that then was no Door Way or other Passage by which her sd. late Husbands Landlord could come into his Appartmts. without his Leave but that the same was in all Respects an Intire Tenement Seperate & Distinct from any other whatever that since he left the same her sd. late Husband in his Life time or this Examinant since his Death have not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworne 27th. June 1744 before me}
