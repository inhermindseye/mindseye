Middx  Clare Dunn aged upwards of 28 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she was bound Apprentice by the Trustees of St. Clements Charity School to Mr. Bradnell of Green Street near Red Lyon Street Peruke maker but lodged with her Mother in the Alms houses in the upper Church Yard of the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes Says soon after she was bound to the sd. Bradnell he assigned this Examinant over to one Mrs. Turner of Cock Lane near Snow hill with whom she never lived but still Continued with her Mother in the sd. Alms houses and further upon her Voluntary & Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes and that James Coffee Labourer did begett the said Bastard Child or Children on her Body the said James Coffee having had Carnall knowledge of this Examint.

several times at a House in Newtoners Lane at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. James Coffee & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworn May 31st: 1746 before  Wm: Jones.

The Mark of Clare Dunn
