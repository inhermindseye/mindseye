Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Christopher Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says She lives a hired yearly Servt . with the Revd . Mr. John Wenham in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and hath so done abt. 13 Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds And further upon her voluntary Oath Says She is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes She being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when Borne Says one Thomas Bean who now live's Servt . with one Mr Herbin a Mercht in Lombard Street London had carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body Some time in the Month of June or July last and at Sevl other times at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which She is now Pregnant and that he the said Thos. Bean is the Only true father thereof

Sworn this 22d. Day of Novr. 1768 Before me} Thos Kynaston

Sarah Christopher
