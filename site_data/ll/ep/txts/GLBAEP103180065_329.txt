Middlesex ss}  Mary Brett  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years Ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant at Ceratin Wages by & with John Farr in the  Southside of Kings Head Court in the Parish of St Margaret in new Fish Street in the City of London  Fishmonger & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Fourteen Months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24 day of September 1778  Jno Sherwood  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Mary Brett
