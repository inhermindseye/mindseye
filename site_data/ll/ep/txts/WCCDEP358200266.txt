Middx  Mary Brooks aged about 35 Years upon Oath she is the Wife of Michael Brooks (at Sea) with whom she intermarried in the Romish Chapel by Lincolns Inn fields about Seventeen Years ago and by whom she hath one Child to wit Dorothy aged about twelve Years, says her said Husband Rented a house in the parish of West Ham in the County of Essex for three Years at the Yearly Rent of twelve pounds hath quitted said House about twelve Years and hath not since Rented House of Ten pounds by the Year or paid the Land Tax or poor Rate

Sworn this 3d. May 1765 . Before} Thos: Balack  Paul Vaillant

Mary Brooks
