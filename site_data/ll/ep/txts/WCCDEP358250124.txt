St Clement Danes 28th March 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields (to wit) Mary Magdalen Galley  singlewoman aged about 31 years Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant at the Southerland Arms Mays Buildings in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County for upwards of a year at the Annual Wages of £8..8..0 that She quitted such Service about fourteen years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 28th day of April 1794 before us J Pownall N Bond

Mary Galley her Mark

Witness Benj: Newman
