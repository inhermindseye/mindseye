Middx  Mary Humphrys on Behalf of Martha Anginger aged about Seven Years & Susanna Anginger aged near two Years the Children of George Anginger by Mary his Wife (both gone from them) upon her Oath saith, that, the sd. George Anginger lived in & rented a house in Bullen Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards for the Space of Six Months & hath left the sd. house abt. 3 Yrs. Says the said George Anginger hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examinant hath been Informed & verily beleives

Sworn March 11th. 1744/5 before} Tho Lane .

Mary Humphry
