Middx  Ann Standfast aged upwards of Twenty Years a Vagrant taken out of the Street in Labour the 7th. Day of the Instant March in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is a Singlewoman and was on the Ninth Day of this Instant March delivered of a female bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Moore near St Clemts. Lane in the same Parish (since baptized Ann which is become Chargeable to the said Parish and that she was born at Ashford in Kent and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one year rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes and that she can give no Account of the Settlemt. of her father or mother

Sworn March 30th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

Ann Standfast
