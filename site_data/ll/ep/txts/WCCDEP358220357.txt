Westmr. to wit Elinor Pullen Aged about 41 Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of John Pullen (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at St. Mary White Chapell about 18 Years ago by whom she hath 2 Children now living Vizt. John Aged about 12 Years and Edwd . aged about 8 Years says her Husband is an Irishman & never gained any Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or belief Says that before her said Intermarriage with her said Husband and whilst this Examinant was a Singlewoman she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Sowns a Perukemaker in Newgate Street in the Parish of Christs Church London upwds. of 3 Years at the Yearly Wages of £4. hath quitted her said Service about 15 years ago & hath not since she quitted the same Her said Husband never Rented £10 P Ann. paid Taxes (Except Scavenger & Watch) nor done any act where by to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 12th. day of June 1772 before us} R. Butler 

Elnior Pullen Mark
