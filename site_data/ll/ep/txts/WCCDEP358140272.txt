Middx.  Elizth. Pittman aged about 42 Years upon Oath says that she is the Daughter of John Pittman by Sarah his Wife both deced That the said John Pittman Rented a house in Fountain Court in the Strand in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and Upwards and lived in the same about two Years as she has been informed and Verily Believes says her said father after he quitted said house in Fountain Court or her Mother after his decease never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says, she this Examinant never was an Apprentice or hired Yearly Servant nor Ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 10th. day of August. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Elizbeth Pittman
