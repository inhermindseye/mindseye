Westmr.  Mary Cooper  Widow of Thomas Cooper upon Oath saith she has been informed by her said late husband that he Served his Clerkship with an Attorney somewhere in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and that neither her Said late husband nor this Examint. Since his Decease Rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn March 25th. 1752 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Cooper
