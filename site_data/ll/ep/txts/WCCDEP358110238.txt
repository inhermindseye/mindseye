Middx  Jane Nutkins upon Oath Saith she is the wife of Geo. Nutkins (who at present absconds from her & by whom she has a Daughter (viz) Jane aged 16. Yrs. who never was Apprentice or yearly Servt. And that she has been informed her sd husband (when a Singleman) lived a Yearly Servant with one Mr Goodall a Farmer at Kenton Green in the Parish of Harrow in the County of Middx for three Years which Information this Examinant believes to be true and that abt. 5 Yrs. ago she was removed by Order of two Justices from the Parish of St Geo. Hanr Square to the afsd. Parish of Harrow which Order was not appealed agt.

Sworn April 18th. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Jane Nutkins
