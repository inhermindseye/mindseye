Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Leach aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she has an illegitimate Child living vizt. Ann aged about 4 years born at the House of a Mrs. Flaxion a Baker in Little Saint Andrew Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex that one James Smith lately deceased was the Father of the said Child

Sworn this 11th. day of March 1788 R Butler}

Meary Leach
