Westrmr. 

Wm. Stinson upon Oath saith he lived in & rented a house in the Parish of Halow in the County of Tyrone in the Kingdom of Ireland of the Yearly Rent of Six Pounds & paid this share of all Parochial Taxes Says he quitted the sd. house in May last & has not since rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 6th. 1746 before  Tho. Lane

The Mark of Wm. Stinson
