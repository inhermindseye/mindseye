Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Wilkes of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County this eighth day of August 1780 Who Saith that She is now with Child of a Child or Children and that the Said Child or Children is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to be chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate and that William Batley of South End in the Parish of Lewisham in the County of Kent  Miller is the Father of the Said Child or Children where with She now goeth and is Pregnant

Sworn this. 8. day of August 1780 before  Jno Sherwood

Sarah Wilkes
