Middlesex ss}  Mary Goffe the widow of Nicholas Goffe deced maketh oath That her said late husband was by birth an Irishman & to her knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That this Depont can give no account respecting the Settlement of her late parents or either of them having never received any information relative there to save that She has been informed that they were born in Stafordshir but the place where She cannot setforth That She this Deponent has been informed that She was also born in the same County but the place where She cannot discover or set forth That she was never an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor even did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself save what she has Acquired by her marriage as aforesaid That She hath four children namely margaret aged about twenty years Judith aged about Thirteen years Eleanor aged about Ten years & Ann Aged about five years the Issue of this Deponent by her said husband Nicholas Goffe Act of whom were born in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the county of middlesex & have done no Act to gain a Settlement for themselves

{Sworn this 29 day of March 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Goffe
