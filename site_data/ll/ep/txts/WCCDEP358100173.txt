Middlesex to wit Wm. Spencer aged upwards of Forty four years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his Wife  Phoebe (gone from him) at the Parish Church of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex about Twenty years ago and by whom he hath two Children living (Vizt.) James aged about Seventeen years now provided for and Thos . aged about Ten years who never gained any Settlemt. Says he served seven years Apprenticeship with one Thos. Brignall a Butcher in Aldersgate Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldersgate  London and after he was out of his sd. Apprentices he never gained any or. Settlement either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or or.wise howsoever

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry. 1784  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

William Spencer
