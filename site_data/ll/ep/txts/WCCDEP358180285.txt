Middx  The Examination of Elizabeth Rogers a Vagabond a Apprehended in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex & brought before me one of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx aforesaid And taken before me this Thirty first Twelfth Day of January March in the Year of our Lord 1744

This Examinant on her Oath Says That she was never married & that she was born in the Parish of Llan Bederpont Stevens in the County of Cardigan in South Wales where her Father lived and Rented a Farm of the Yearly Rent of Three pounds or thereabouts & paid his Share of all Parochiall Taxes as this Examinant hath been Informed & beleives Says she never rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 8th March 12th. 1744 before-} Tho Lane .

The mark of Eliz. Rogers
