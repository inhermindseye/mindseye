Westmr  Mary Mills Aged about Forty four Years Upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Wm. Mills at the Fleet London upwards of Nineteen Years ago Says her said Husband Served five Years Clerkship with Mr Pead Attorney at Law Says her said Husband Served the Last Year of his said Clerkship in Baldwins Court in Cloak Lane in the Parish of St. Thomas the Apostle London hath left said Apprentiship about Twenty Years and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Land Tax or Poors Rate as this Examt hath been Informed by her said Husband and which Information she Verily believes to be true Says she has One Child by her said Husband (Viz) Mary Aged about Ten Years

Sworn the 6th of March 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Mills

Upon Mr Robt Williams Complt J W
