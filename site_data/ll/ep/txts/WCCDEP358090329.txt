Susanna Manning aged about 26 years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Benjamin Manning with whom she Intermarried at Hampton in the County of Gloucester about years ago and who died about 5 years ago says her said late Husband was by Trade a Stone Mason and whose Settlement was at Hampton afsd. and after her said Husbands death she never gained either by Renting House paid Taxes or having a hired year Servant for One Year And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 13th. day of July last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Work house belonging to the Par of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by of James and is now Chargeable to the said Parish And further that one Wm. King a Tide Waiter who lately lodged the next Door to the Rose Tavern in Bridges Street Covent Garden is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at the Rose Tavern afsd. some time in the Month of Septr. last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at Sevl. Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. And that the said Wm. King & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 31st. day of August 1782 before R. Butler

her Susanna Manning Mark
