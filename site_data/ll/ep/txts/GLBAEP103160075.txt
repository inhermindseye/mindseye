Middlesex ss}  Mary Bartlett the widow of James Bartlett deced maketh oath That her said husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprentice ship with John Gardener in Burr Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Taylor & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge information or belief he did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement & that She this Depont Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlemt to herself that She was lawfully married to her sd late husband

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Bartlett
