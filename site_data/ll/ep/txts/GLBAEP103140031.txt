Middlesex ss}  Richard Hawker maketh oath That his Father Henry Hawker who is now living in the Workhouse of the Parish of sd. George Hanover Square in the said County as this Depont. believes has informed this Deponent & which information this Deponent verily believes to be true that he this Deponts. said Father was born in Bond Street in the Same Parish and that to this Deponent knowledge or belief his said Father never did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right And this Deponent Saith that he this Depont never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself And that he hath a Wife named Margaret and two Children namely Mary Aged about two years & half and John Aged about one year & half the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his said Wife to whom he was lawfully married in the Parish Church of St Leonard Shoreditch

Sworn this 10 day of March 1774 before  Rob Pell  Jno Sherwood

Richd: Hawker
