Middx  Eliz: Lader  Singlewoman aged abt. 20 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mrs. Aldrige in Tavistock Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Gardn. in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £4. for the Space of 1 Yr. & a halfe & hath left her sd. Service abt. 6 Months since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 3d. Janry 1743 before} Tho Lane C Egerton

The Mark of Eliz: Lader
