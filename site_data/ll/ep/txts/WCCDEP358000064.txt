Middlesex and Westminster}  Passd. to Christ Church Spital fields to wit Catherine Pritchard aged Thirty years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was married Saith that on or about the 29th. day of May 1784 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child (since which Christened by the name of John Currer ) at the House of a Mr. Banks a Broker in Rose Lane Spital Fields in the Parish of Christ Church Spital Fields in the County of Middlesex and that John Currer of High Street White Chapple  Sadler is the Father of such Child and this Examinant further saith that the Child now Produced by her at the time of her swearing to this Examination is the Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid

Sworn this 16th day of June 1785  Thos: Gilbert  Thos: Gordons

her Catherine Pritchard Mark
