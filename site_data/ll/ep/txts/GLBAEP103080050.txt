Middx ss  Catherine King the widow of Henry King deced Maketh oath that about eleven years ago her said late husband lived as a hired servant by the Year with one Mr Green a Brewer in the Parish of Chelsea in the County of Middx at the yearly wages of about Six Pounds by the year as where this Deponent has been inf also lived as a yearly servant And this Depont saith that her said husband Continued in such service for about the Space of one year and an half and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont farther saith that her sd husband did not afterwards live as a hired servant by the year nor serve any parish offices nor rent a house of Ten pounds by the Year or upwards nor done any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlemt to her knowledge or belief And that she hath two Children to wit Susannah aged about eight Years & three quarters and Mary Ann aged about three quarters of a year the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her sd late husband

Sworn this 29th. day of August 1759 before us  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

her Catherine King Mark
