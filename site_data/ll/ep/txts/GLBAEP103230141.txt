Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Sherington maketh oath that She never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right That her Father Sampson Sherington lately rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in Cartwright Square in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & resided thereon for divers years That her said Father to her Knowledge or belief has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Lodging or other Tenement of such yearly rent or Value or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13. day of May 1788 before  R Davies

Eliz. Sherington
