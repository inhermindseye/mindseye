Westmr.  Mary Rainsford aged about 65 Years Upon Oath says, she is the Wife of Robert Rainsford with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London about thirty six years ago; says her said Husband served seven years apprenticeship with Mr. Henry Allen a Waterman at Dartford in the County of Kent and never Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 14th, day of April 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Mary Rainsford
