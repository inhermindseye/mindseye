Middx  Elizabeth Geary aged about 24 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married That she was bound an Apprentice by an Indenture for Seven Years to Susannah Taylor a Millener in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and there Served about Two Years and then her Mistress died which is about Eight Years since Says she was not Assigned over to any other Person That she hath not kept House or been a hired Yearly Servant Since

Sworn the 13th. day of May 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz. Geary
