St Clement Danes Febry. 6th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Elizabeth Jefferies  Widow aged 45 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that (during her Widowhood being single and having no Child or Children) she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Lilly at No 16d Holywell Street in the parish parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for four years and upwards at the Annual Wages of Eight Guineas left that Service in or about the Month of December last & since hath not been Married nor obtained any subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinant Examinant's knowledge Information or belief

Sworn the 6th. day of February 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Jeffries 's Mark
