Middx  6 Yrs. & upwd  Martha Pell aged about 30 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she was bound an Apprentice to & Served 6 Years the first Part of her Apprentiship with Mrs. Seymour Mantua Maker in Essex Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx & at the End of the said Six Years her said Mistress went away from this Examint. & she never was Assigned over to any other person rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 1st. June 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Mark of Martha: Pell
