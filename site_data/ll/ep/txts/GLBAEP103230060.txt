Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Mathews the widow of William Mathews deced maketh oath that Since her Said husbands death she was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Lewis Powell a Sawmaker in old Street in the Parish of St Luke in the Sd County & Continued there in Such Service under such hiring at Certain Yearly Wages for the Space of five years She this Deponent having no Child or Children living during Such her Servitude as aforesaid and has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 28. day of June 1787 before  Robt: Smith  Herbt-Mays

The Mark of Elizabeth Mathews
