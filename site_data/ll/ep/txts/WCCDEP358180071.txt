Middx  Catherine Richardson on the Behalfe of Carolina Bethealfe an Infant Child aged about Six Years upon her Oath saith that the said Carolina Bethealfe was born in Purple Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex as she hath been inform'd & beleives & this Examinant can't give any Account of the Father or Mother of the said Child

Sworne the 16th- March 1742 before} J Bromfield Chas. Egerton

The Mark of Cath: Richardson
