Middx ss  John West maketh oath that about Thirty Six Years ago he was Bound Apprentice by Indre to one Isaac Alexander a Waterman of the Parish of East Greenwich in the County of Kent with whom he served two years of his apprenticeship and then went to sea for the Remainder of his Apprenticeship with his Masters approbacon his said Master from time to time receiving this Deponents Wages for such service By Means whereof this Deponent as he is advised acquired a legal settlement in the said Parish And this Deponent saith that he hath not since rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year or upwards Nor served any Parish offices nor been a hired servant by the year Nor done any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlement to this Deponts knowledge or belief

Sworn this 7 day of July 1758 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of John West
