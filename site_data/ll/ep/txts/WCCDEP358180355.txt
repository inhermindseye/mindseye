Mary Carter aged about 46 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Belford in Dunbarr Court in White horse Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of 1 Yr. & 7 Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & hath left her Sd. Service about 6 Months since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 15th. July 1745 before} Wm. Jones Mary Carter
