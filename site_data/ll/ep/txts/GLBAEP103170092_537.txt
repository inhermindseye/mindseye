Middlesex ss}  Mary Turner  Singlewoman maketh oath That She cannot give any Account respecting the place of Settlement of her Parents or either of them That She this Deponent hath been informed by her Father William Turner & which She believes to be true that She this Deponent was born in Lemon Street in the parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the said County & was Baptized in the Church belonging to the said parish That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor never in any one place as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this. 21. of August 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Turner
