Middlesex ss}  Jane Read the widow of Domingo Read deced maketh oath That her Said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true That he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages with Mr Williford a Coal merchant in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service for many years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards to this Deponts knowledge information or belief do any Act to gain another Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this day of 1776 before}
