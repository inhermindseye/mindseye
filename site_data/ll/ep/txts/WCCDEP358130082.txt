Middx  Mary Sherman Aged abt. Fifty Nine Years Upon Oath Says She intermarryed with her late Husband Wm. Sherman in the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes upwards of Forty Years since Says he sd. late Husband has been Dead abt. Seven Years and that before his intermarryage with this Examinant & whilst a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mrs. Hudson in Church Lane in the Hamlett of Hammer Smith in the County of Middx four Years & Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds & never rented Ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes Says She hath not since her sd. Husband death rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servt.

Sworn the 17th. day of Novr. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Shirman
