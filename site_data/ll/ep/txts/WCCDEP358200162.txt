Westmr.  Cath: Sanger on Behalf of Thomas and Catherine Peirson two Infants the Children of Thos. Peirson by Mary his Wife both Deceased Says the Father of the said Children Rented a House in St. Martins Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. about two Years at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds or thereabouts hath quitted said House about four Years and hath not Since Rented House or paid Taxes as this Examinant hath Been Informed and which Information she Verily Beleives to be true

Sworn the 13th of Novr 1764 Before} Thos: Balack  Benjamin Cox

Cathrine Sanger
