Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Fisher aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Vidler in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the yearly wages of Thirty Shillings that she hath quitted said Service about Eighteen Months since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Thomas Bray a Gentleman who lodges at No. 52 Oxford Road is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she was so is now delivered as aforesaid Pregnant and that the said Thomas Bray is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Septr. 1798 before me } J Reeves

her Elizabeth Fisher Mark

5th. Octr. 1798 . Security Bond given

Child born in the house the 24th. day of June 1798 named James Fisher .
