Middx  Wm. Richards aged abt. 65 Years upon Oath Saith he Served 7 Years apprentiship with Mr Wm. Lovet a Dyle opposite Palsgrave head Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and that he never rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year And that he has a Daughter by his late wife Elizth . namely Jane aged abt. 25 Years who never was an apprentice or yearly Servant

Sworn Feby. 10th 1755 before Fran: Bedwell

Wm. Richards
