Middx & Westmr-}  Ann Southall aged about 29 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr- Bird a Cutler in the Strand in the Parish of St- Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx upwards of two years & an half at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service near seven Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subseqt. Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the sixth day of April last she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in a Licensed House in the Precinct of White Fryers London which has since been baptized by the name of Thomas & is now chargeable to the sd- Parish of St- Clemt- Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same And furr- says that one James Gibson a Journeyman hair dresser who now works with one Mr- Hargrave a Hair dresser facing Pepper Alley near London Bridge in the Bort. of Southwark is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in her late Master house sometime in the Month of June 1776 & at sevl- difft- times afterwds- but the partlar days or times she does not now rember at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she was so Deliver'd as afsd- & that the sd- Jas- Gibson & no other pson did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father Throf.

Sworn this 25th- Day of July 1777 before}

Ann Southall
