Ann Spicer

St Clement Danes July 20th. 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St Matthew Bethnal Green  (to wit) Benjn Newman one of the Beadles of the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty on behalf of Ann Spicer (now Ill and deceased) Maketh and Oath saith That he hath been informed which he verily believes to be true that the said Ann Spicer is aged about 68 Years was Married to her late Husband Matthew Spicer at the parish Church of St Leonard Shoreditch about 24 Years ago that previous thereto and while her said Husband was single he served an Apprenticeship with a Weaver in the parish & St Matthew Bethnal Green in the said County of Middlesex and since the Expiration thereof he never obtained any subsequent legal Settlement that her said Husband Died several Years ago in the Workhouse belonging to the said parish of St Matthew Bethnal Green that he was removed to that parish from the parish of wherein Eagle and Child Alley in Shoe Lane is situate (in which Alley the said Ann Spicer with her said Husband then resided) about 21 Years ago and by Virtue thereof taken into the Workhouse of the said parish of St. Matthew Bethnal Green and maintained therein several Years that since her said Husband's Decease she hath not been Married nor acquired any legal Settlement in her own Right that she has six Children by her said Husband all out of

the Kingdom & provided for And this Deponent saith that the said Ann Spicer is now Ill with a fever but may be safely removed to their place of her last legal settlement as he is informed and believes

Sworn the 20th. day of July 1742 before us } Wm Kitchiner R Butler

Benj: Newman
