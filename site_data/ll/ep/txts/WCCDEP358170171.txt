Middx  The Examination of Anne Penny  Wife of Richd. Penny who upon her Oath saith that on or abt. the 25th. Day of June 1736 she Intermarried with her sd. Husband Richd. Penny at the Fleet London & further saith that her sd. Husband was bound an Apprentice to & served Jno. Gamull Peruke Maker in Fleetstreet in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West  London & this Examinants sd. Master Dyeing before this Examinant was out of his sd. Apprentiship he served the Remainder of his sd. Apprentiship with Mrs. Margaret Gamull the Wido . of the sd. Jno: Gamull & served Three Yrs. or thereabts. the last Part of his sd. Apprentiship with the sd. Mrs. Margaret Gamull in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx as this Examinant hath been informed & beleives & further saith that her sd. Husbd hath not since his sd. Apprentiship rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or  Beleif

that her sd. Husband is now abroad & beyond the Seas that he hath left one Child to wit John aged Four Years & she being Poor is not able to maintain herselfe & Child

Sworne the 2d. Day of Novr. 1741 before}
