Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Brown aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith that She never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that She lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Close a Victualler in Warwick Lane in the Parish of Christ Church in the City of London for about Two years at the yearly wages of Six Pounds that She hath quitted said Service about Twelve Years since which time She hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of March 1789 R Butler D Walker

her Mary Brown Mark
