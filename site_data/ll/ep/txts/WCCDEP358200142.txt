Middx  Mary Cowling Aged about Sixty One Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Charles Cowling with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of of St. Martin in the County of Wiltz about Forty three Years ago Says her said Husband Rented a House in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx One Year at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds hath quitted said House about thirteen Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds by the Year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 8th. of Septr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Cowling
