Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Simpson aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she has lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Mortimer at the New Inn Coffee house in Wych Street for these Eighteen Months past at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the Second day of March 1787 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of George That one George Battray a Musical Instrument Maker who did lodge at Mr. Criggs in Orange Street  Leicester fields is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1789 } R Butler

her Ann Simpson Mark

Ann Simpson lives at Mr. Mortimers the New Inn Coffee house

George Buttray is a Tall Thin Man Light Hair fair Complexion is about Twenty two years of Age
