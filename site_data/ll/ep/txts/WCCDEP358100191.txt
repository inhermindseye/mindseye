Middx to wit Susannah Swalthy Aged about 24 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband Barthow. Swalthy (gone from her) at Duke Street Chapell about 10 Months ago and by whom she hath no Child living Says she has been Informed and believes that such Marriage is not legal by the Lams of this Kingdom And further says that her said Husband is an Irishman & never gained any Settlemt. in London either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year or otherwise howsoever Says before her Intermarriage to her said Husband & whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Calley a Coal merchant in Piccadilly in the Parish of St. James Westmr . upwards of 14 Months at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement.

Sworn this 2d. day of March 1784  Wm Kitchiner  Edward Hodsoll

Susannah Swalthy
