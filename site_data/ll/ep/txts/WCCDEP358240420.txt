Richard Higgenson & Martha his Wife

St Clement Danes 13th June 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  settlement in St George Bloomsbury }  (to wit) Martha Higginson Aged 47 years or thereabouts Wife of Richard Higginson for and on behalf of herself and also of him the said Richard Higginson who is now Insane Upon her Oath saith that the said Richard Higginson was Married to her this Deponent at the Parish Church of Saint Ann 's Westminster about eight years ago that afterwards he rented and with this Examinant resided in a House in Bloomsbury Court  Holborn in the Parish of St George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for about two years at the annual Rent of £30 that they quitted the same about five years ago and he hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 30th. day of June 1794 before us M Addington J Hind

her Martha Higginson 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys

The Magistrater Referred A Remove them without the Husband himself  was sennable & could be sworn as to the Settlement Mr Moore the apothecary gave Certificate that He might be Removed safely
