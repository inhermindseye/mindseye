Middx  Mary Broaday aged upwds. of 30 Years upon Oath Says she never was married and that about 7 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Wade in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds for one Year and upwards Says she has not since she left her said Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 23d. 1747 before
