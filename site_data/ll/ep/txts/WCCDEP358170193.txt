Middx  Eliz: Gresham  Wido . aged Eighty Three Years upon her Oath saith that she lived in & rented a House in Cecill Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Fifty Pounds for the Space of Two or Three Years or thereabts. & hath left the same abt. Five or Six Years that since she left the sd. House she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 13th. Day of December 1741 before}
