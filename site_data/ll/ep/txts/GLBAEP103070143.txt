Middx ss}  Martha Church  Singlewoman maketh oath that her father who is now deced often informed her that he was born in Swallow Street in the parish of Saint James Westminster in the County of Middx wch this Depont. believes to be true And that she this Depont. was as she has been informed believes born in the Workhouse of the said parish And that she was bound out an apprentice to Mrs. Mary Jackson a parish Nurse of the sd parish by the sd. parish with whom she lived three Months & then went away from her said Mistress & yr. she never was turn'd over nor ever been a hired servent by the year or gained any other Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 17th day of September 1755 before  Walter Berry  Saml Gower

her Martha Church Mark
