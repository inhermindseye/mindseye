Middx  The Examination of Ruth the Wife of Batton Chamberlain lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Who upon her Oath saith yt. she Intermarried with the sd. Batton Chamberlain on the 28th- Day of July 1716 & that before her sd. Intermarriage her Husband's Father Sons Chamberlain lived in & rented a House in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr - for some time but how long this Examint. can not tell of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds & upwards And this Examinant further on her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband never rented any House paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examint- sd. Husband hath informed her & which she believes yt. he now absconds from her & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn this 13th- Day of April 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Ruth Chamb Ruth Chamberlien
