Middlesex ss}  Thomas Parton maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr Wyatt Attorney at Law in the Parish of Saint Swithin at London Stone in the City of London and Continued in such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of fifteen Months That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or rented a house of ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Charlotte & two Children namely William Aged about Seven Years and Elizabeth Aged about three years the lawful Issue of this Depont by his Sd Wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 5. day of January 1785 before R Davies Robt: Smith Thomas Parton
