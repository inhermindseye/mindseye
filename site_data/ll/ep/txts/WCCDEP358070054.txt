Westmr. to wit Elizth. Lee Aged about Forty Four Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Henry Lee who has been Dead about Eleven Years ago and by whom she has no Children now living Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Little Brittain in in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldersgate London for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same and further Says that since her said late Husbands Death she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th. day of December 1772 before Us R Butler Tho Kynaston

Eliz Lee
