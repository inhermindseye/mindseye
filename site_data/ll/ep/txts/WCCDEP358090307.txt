Middlesex to wit Elizth. Austin Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Elems who keeps an eating House in Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and that one Daniel Hyde a Farrier who lately worked with one Mr. Elliott a Farrier in Red Lyon Court Bermondsey Street in the Parish of Saint John in the Borrough of Southwark is the Father thereof she having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body for sevl. Years last past at different times & in different places she having had a Child before by him the said Daniel Hyde named John Aged upwards of Three Years born in Red Lyon Court in the said Parish of Saint John Southwark in the said County of Surry and Says that the said Daniel Hyde & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & True Father of both the said Bastard Children

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1782 R Butler

Elizth. Austin
