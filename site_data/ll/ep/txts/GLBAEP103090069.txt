Middlesex ss  Sarah Jeffreys  Single woman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the Year With one Mr Lewis who then lived in Flushing Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and Continued in Such service for the Space of one Year and three Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and by means thereof as She is advised gained a legal Settlement in the said Parish and this Depont Saith that She has not since lives as a hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this first Day of July-1767 before  Chris Scott

The Mark of Sarah Jeffreys
