Middx  Eliz Hardwick upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Gregory Hardwick to whom she was married at the Parish Church of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex on the 8th. Novr. abt. 29 Years ago and by whom she has one Daughter Henrietta aged abt. 27 Years who never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. and that her sd. late husband lived a yearly Servant with St. Tho Jones in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes at the yearly wages of Ten pounds for ten Years or thereabouts before his marriage and about four years afterwards and that her sd. late husband never rented ten pounds by the year nor paid Parish taxes nor has this Examint. since his decease rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Janry. 11th 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz Hardwick
