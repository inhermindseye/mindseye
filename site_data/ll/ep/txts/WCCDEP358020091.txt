Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Tring aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with for 12 Months and upwards at the King of Prussia's Head in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex That since she quitted said Service she never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That she hath One Child living vizt. William aged about 9 years born a Bastard at the Bull and Gate in Holborn in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 21st. day of July 1788 D Walker R Butler

her Elizabeth Tring Mark
