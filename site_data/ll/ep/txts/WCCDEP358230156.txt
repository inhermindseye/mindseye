Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Long aged about 51 years upon her Oath saith That on or about the Ninth day of Janry 1779 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr Hoarlicke near the Church in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechaple in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary Ann

Sworn this 8th day of Octr 1800 before us} Benjn. Gee  John Collick

Jane Long
