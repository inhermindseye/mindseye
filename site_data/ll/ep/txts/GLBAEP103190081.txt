Middlesex ss}  Ann Edwards the wife of Thomas Edwards maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one William Smith a Shoemaker in the Parish of St James in the City of Bristol & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She was lawfully Married to her Sd husband by whom She hath a Child & lawful Issue now living named Thomas aged four years.

Sworn this. 17. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Edwards
