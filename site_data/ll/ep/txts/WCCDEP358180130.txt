Middx  Christian Atkinson aged 32 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married that abt. 15 Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Grainger in Arundel Street in the parish of St Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of £4. for the Space of one Year & upwards that since she left her sd. Service she hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 15th: Novr 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Xtian Atkinson
