Middlesex ss}  John Cooper maketh oath that about Eight years ago he rented a house known by the Sign of the Kings head in the Parish of West Ham in the County of Essex at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and during Such his residence thereon which was for the Space of a year and upwards he was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 29. day of February 1788 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

The Mark of John Cooper
