Middx  James Roe aged about Nineteen Years On his Oath says he never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says he was born an Apprentice to one John Ward a Hairdresser in Bishop Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishopgate London and served his said Master for the space of four Years or thereabouts And was afterwards discharged by Alderman Woldrige for Non Inrollment of his Indentures and after being so discharged he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 27th. day of November 1779 before R Butler Abrm Baley

Jas Roe
