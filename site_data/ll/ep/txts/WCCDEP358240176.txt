William Burridge aged abt. 11 years

St. Clement Danes 5th Novr 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Sarah Burridge Widow of William Burridge deceased on behalf of William the Son of the deceased Maketh Oath and saith that she has been informed and Verily believes that her late Husband was formerly Married to Sarah his first Wife therebefore Sarah Griffiths  Spinster at the parish Church of Watchet in the County of Somerset that the said Child now left with this Deponent was born of the Body of the said Sarah his first Wife in or about the year of 1781 in lawful Wedlock and this Deponent further saith that the said Child hath never been bound an Apprentice nor otherwise obtained any legal settlement in his own right as she verily believes And that the said William Burridge the father this Deponents late Husband Rented and Resided in a House in Bear Yard abt three quarters of a year at the Annual Rent of £22 within the said parish of St. Clement Danes and Died there about Midsummer last, And that he left her this Deponent distressed and unable to maintain the said Child

Sworn the 5th. Novr. 1792 before me } R Butler

Sarah Burridge 's Mark

Witness B Newman
