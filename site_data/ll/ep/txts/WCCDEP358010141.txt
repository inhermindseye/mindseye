Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit William Haster aged about 58 years upon his Oath saith that in the Month of Novr. 1759 she Intermarried with Martha his Present Wife at the Parish Church of Crundell in Hampshire that he hath no Child or Children living by his said Wife That he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Jas. Clarke a Peruke Maker Srt Hair Dresser in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Septr. 1787 R Butler

his William Haster mark
