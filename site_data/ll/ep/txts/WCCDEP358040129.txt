Westmr.  Jane Jackson upon her Voluntary Oath saith she never was married & that she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the 28th. Day of January 1744 /5 in the house of Mrs. Evans in Crown Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & Liberty of Westmr. which has been Baptized Richard & became chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and that Richard Todd a Haberdasher did beget the said Male Bastard Child on her Body the said Richard Todd having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the Month of April 1744 in the house of Mr Howes never the new Church in the Strand where they lived fellow Servants together and at several other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesaid & that he the sd. Richard Todd and us man else is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Decr. 31st. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Jane Jackson
