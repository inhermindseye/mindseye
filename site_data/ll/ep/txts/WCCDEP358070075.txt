Westmr. to wit Mary Walford Aged about Thirty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice a Yearly hired Servant for one Year says she has been Informed and which Information she veryly believeth to be true that her Father Jonathan Walford deceased Served three Years the last Part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Jaques at the Castle in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Taxes to this Examinant knowledge or belief And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and Unable to maintain the same when born says that one John Langley  Servant to One John Rolls a Cow-ha in Deptford in Surry had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of June last and Several times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children or which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Langley and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 8th. day of March 1773 before me W Kelyngs

her Mary Walford Mark

Witness E: Fields
