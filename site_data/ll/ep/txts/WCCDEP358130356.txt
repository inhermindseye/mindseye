Middx  Hannah Brown aged abt. 23 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Glanville in Thames Street in the Parish of Clever in the County of Berks for five Yrs. or thereabouts & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. two Yrs.

Sworn the 6th. day of June 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Hannah Brown
