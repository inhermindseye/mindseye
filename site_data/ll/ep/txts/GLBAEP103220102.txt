Middlesex ss}  William Homden maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Edward Gray of Fore Street in the Parish of St Ann in the Sd County Esqr. & Contind there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two Years & upwards That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor Served any Parish office nor rented a house of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards nor paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 20. day of Decr 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

Wm Homden
