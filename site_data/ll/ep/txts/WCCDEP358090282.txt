Middx & Westmr.}  Penelope Stokes Aged about 30 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father John Stokes Rented and lived in a Heyse in Bush Lane Cannon Street in the Parish of St. Swithens in the City of London for Several Years at the Yearly Rent of Forty pounds and upwards a Year & paid Parish Taxes due for the same and lived thereinto the time of his Death which is about Two and Twenty Years ago since which time this Examinant never gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 2d day of Feby. 1782 before  Thos: Gilbert : Wm Kitchiner

Penelope Stokes
