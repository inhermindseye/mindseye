Father dead and was the Child named Ann passd. to St. Giles's in the Fields  Susannah Presser aged 26 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was Married according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of England never lived a  a Hired yearly servant never served any at apprenticeship or did any other Act Matter or Thing hereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlemt. in her own Right That she was an on Dowgate Hill in the Parish City of London That her Father died about 3 Months before this Examinant was born That her father Daniel after her Birth Wood in his Life time Rented and lived in a House in Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of 12 Months and at the yearly Rent of upwards of 12 Pounds And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she was has three Children now living Namely John Presser aged upwards of 6 years of Age born in Nags Head Court Drury Lane  in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid Mary Ann Presser aged 5 years or thereabouts.

who was born at No 27 Monmouth Street in the Parish of Saint  Giles in the fields in the Co. of Middx and Maria Presser aged 9 Months or thereabouts who was born in the Workhouse belongg. to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and Fesr. Saith That John Presser to whom this Deponent was Married by a Roman Catholic Priest at a House in Gray's  Tan Lane accordg. to the Rights of the Romish Church is the father of the said Children and that the sd. John Presser & no one else is the father of the said such Children

Sworn this 7th. day of Novr. 1785 before me R Butler

her Susannah Presser Mark
