Middlesex  Margt Doleman  Wido . of John Doleman who hath been dead upwards of three Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. John Doleman at the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn on the 27th. of Augt. 1719 Says the sd. John Doleman lived in & rented a house in Ship Yard in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £14 for the Space of his Years & upwards & there dyed Says she continued to live in & rents in the same house for two Years or thereabouts after his Decease & hath left the same abt. 15 Months Says she hath not since she left the sd. house rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif Says she hath 2 Childn. (viz): Sarah aged abt. 11 Yrs. & Hannah aged abt. 7 Yrs.

Sworn Octr. 3d. 1746 before Wm. Jones .

M Doleman
