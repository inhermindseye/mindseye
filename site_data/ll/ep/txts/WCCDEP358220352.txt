Westmr. to wit Ann Baker aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath says she Intermarried with Christopher Baker her Husband at the Parish Church of St. Georges Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex about Seven Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Vizt. Ann Aged about Six Years and Christopher Aged about five Months Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with The Right Honble The Earl Ferrers in Great George Street in the Parish of Saint Margarets in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Twenty five Pounds besides Diet & Lodgings hath quitted his said Service about five Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes or done any or Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12d. day of May 1772 before us} R. Butler Tho. Kynaston

Ann Baker
