Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Mary Pinnell aged forty two Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to James Pinnell her now Husband who now is in the Kings Bench at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn about twenty one Years ago, according to the Rites of the Church of England by Law Established that she has four Children by her said Husband now living Vizt. James aged Nineteen Years or thereabouts who was put Apprentice  one Mr John Brookes of Fleet Market  Pawnbroker  William Pinnell aged fifteen or thirty fourth also is now in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell  John aged from ten Years or thereabouts, is Clerk to Mr Welslim of No. 1 in furnivale Inn holborn and Daniel aged seven Years thereabouts who hath never born absent since this Examinant or his father  house gained any settlement in his own right - And further this Examinant saith that he said Husband and she this Examinant rented and lived in a House in Battle Burdge in the Parish or Saint James - Clerkenwell

in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of £12 for upwards of a Year and half that this quitted same about a Year ago and since that time her said Husband hath not done any act Matter or King Whereby to the hart of this Examinant Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any subsequent Settlement and that she is poor and unable to maintained her said Child, her Husband being in the Things Bench us above stated

Sworn this 28th day of Febry. 1786 M Addington Thos: Gordon

Mary Pinnell
