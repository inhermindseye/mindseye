Middlesex ss  Ann Kimber  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one Mr Rose a China man in new port alley in the Parish of St Ann Westminster in the said County at Certain Wages and Continued in Such Service for the Space of Fourteen months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th day of october 1777 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkes

Ann Kimber
