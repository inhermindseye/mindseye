Middlesex ss  Ann Bird  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore hired and lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr Sheen Downes a Salesman in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County at Certain Wages and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year and half That She has not Since lived As a hired Servant for the Space of a Year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 14. day of November 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Bird
