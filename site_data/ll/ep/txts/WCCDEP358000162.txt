Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.}  passd. to St. Pancrass. to wit Elizabeth Thompson aged 20 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year says that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Phielling in Charles Street in the Parish of St. Marygate Bone for Six Months and went along with that her said Master to Goodge Street in the Parish of Saint Pancrew  and there where this Deponent lived with him for the space of 9 months at the yearly Wages of 5 Pounds hath quitted said How Service about 5 years ago since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 30th day of Novr. 1785  Sampr Wright J Addington

Her Elizh Thompson mark
