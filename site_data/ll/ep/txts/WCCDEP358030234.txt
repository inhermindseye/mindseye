Middx  Maria Martin aged near 24 Years upon Oath saith she is a Singlewoman Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Mash at the Sun Alehouse in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £4 and hath left sd. Service abt. four Years Says she hath not since she left sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 23d. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Maria Martin
