Middx  Jas Jones upon Oath Saith he was married to his wife Jane Jones at the Fleet London near 15 Years ago that he Served his apprentiship wth. John Mercer a Taylor in the Parish of Yately in the County of Hants and never Rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn March 8th. 1755 before}
