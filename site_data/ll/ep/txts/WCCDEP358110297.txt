Westmr.  Judith Nailer upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Wm Nailer who was born at Nettlebed in Oxfordshire and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes as she has been informed & believes & that her sd. husbands father lived in & rented the White hart Inn at Nettlebed afsd. for one Year & upwds.

Sworn Augt 17th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Geaton
