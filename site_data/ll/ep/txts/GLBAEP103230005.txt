Middlesex ss}  Rose Newman the Widow of Francis Newman deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that about thirty years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indres to one Mr Coles Taylor of Breams Buildings in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex & after having Served Part of his Apprenticeship there his sd Master Guitting the Business turned this Deponts Said husband ever to his Foreman who Suceeded him in his Said Trade of a Taylor there in his Masters house where the Sd Apprenticeship ended That her sd husband did not after Such his Apprenticeship to her Knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 20 day of Janry 1787 before  R Davies  Rob: Smith

The Mark of Rose Newman
