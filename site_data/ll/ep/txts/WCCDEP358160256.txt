Middx  The Examination of Susannah Turner  Widow of Joseph Turner aged 60 Years passed from the Parish of Northington in the County of Southampton to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex taken the 10th. Day of February 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about twenty four Years ago at that time being a Widow and not haveing any Child or Children She lived in any rented a House in St. Clements Church Yard in the said Parish of St. Clements Danes of the Yearly Rent of twenty Pounds and kept the Same for the Space of one Year and further on her Oath saith that She hath not Since that time rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife and being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day and Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Susannah Turner
