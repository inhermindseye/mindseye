Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Prime aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with William Prime her present husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Botolph Bishopgate about Eight years since by whom she hath Two Children living namely Ann aged about Seven years and William aged about four years That her said Husband Rented a Second floor unfurnished of a Mr. Clancey in Clare Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Three years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds that her said Husband quitted said Apartments about Eighteen Months ago since which time he has not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he Might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of May 1795 } M Addington

Mary Prime
