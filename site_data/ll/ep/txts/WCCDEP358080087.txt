Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Whitter Aged about Seventy Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Robert, Phipps an Upholsterer in Hounsditch in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate London for Seven Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Twenty Three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of Octr. 1776 before R Butler Jno. Machin

Mary Whitter
