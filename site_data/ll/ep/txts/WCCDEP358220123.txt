Middx & Westmr}  Catherine Jones aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with John Goodwin a Peruke maker and Hair Cutter in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Eight Months and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one John Flack a Drummer in the Coldstream Regiment in Col Wilsons Company now quartered in the Tower had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of May last at the House of Mr. Williams who Keeps a Coal Shed in Saint Catherines and at sevl. diferent times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Flack and no other person did beget the same

Sworn this 16th. day of February 1770 before me} 

her Catherine Jones Mark

Witness Petr. Sidebotham
