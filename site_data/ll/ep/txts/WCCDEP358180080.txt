Middx  Rebecca Hill passed as a Vagrant from the Town of Manchester by Pass Dated the 21st. Day of March last upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her said prest Husband  Robt. Hill on the 29th. Day of Octobr. abt. 24 Yrs. since at the Parish Church of St. Buttolph Aldgate that the said Robt. Hill abt. 25 Years since being at that time a Singleman & without Child or Children lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Thompson at the Plough Inn in Carey Street for Sevll- Years at the Yearly Wages of £4. that since he left his said Service he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge & beleife

Sworne the 26th. April 1743 before

The Mark of Rebecca Hill
