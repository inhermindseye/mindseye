Middx  Tho: Rutter upon his Oath saith that he inter- married with his prest. Wife  Ann on the 20th- Day of January 1729 & that upwards of Fourteen Years ago being at that Time a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Eagleston at Knights Coffee House in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Eighteen Months or thereabts. at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds that since his sd. Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne 12th.. Augt. 1742 before me} Tho Lane

Thos. Rutter
