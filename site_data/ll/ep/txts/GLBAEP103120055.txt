Middlesex ss}  James Feddes being one of the People Called Quaker s upon his solemn affirmation Saith That he well knows one George Guest a Taylor & that the Said George Guest has informed this affirmant & which he believes to be true That he the Said George Guest was bound apprentice by Indre to John Bray of Wardour Street in the Parish of St. James Westminster in the County of Middlesex Taylor for the Term of five years & that he served several years of such his apprenticeship in the Said Parish & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there and this affirmant Can give no other Account respecting the settlement of the Said George Guest That the Said George Guest is lately gone away to Places unknown to this affirmant and that at the time of his Departure he left behind him his two children with this affirmant to wit Sarah Aged about Six years and George Aged near three years the lawful Issue of the Said George Guest by his late wife Mary Deced .

affirmed this 17th. day of July 1773 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

James Feddes
