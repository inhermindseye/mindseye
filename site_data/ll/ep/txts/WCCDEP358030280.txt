Middx  Margt. Booden aged abt. 60 Yrs. upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Booden who hath been dead abt. 4 Yrs. Says the sd. Wm. Booden lived in & rented a house in Paulsgrave head Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £37 for the Space of nine Years or thereabouts and left the same updwds of Twelve Years Says her sd. late husband after he left sd. house nor this Exaiant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 9th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

Margt Boodan
