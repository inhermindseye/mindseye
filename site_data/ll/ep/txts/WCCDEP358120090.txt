Middx  Ann Steward aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath saith She is the wido. of John Steward who was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief and that before her marriage with the sd. John Steward & whilst a Singlewoman she lived a yearly Servt. wth. Mr. Cornelius a Vintner at the Rose Tavern near Temple bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for three Years at the yearly wages of £6 and afterwards (being still a Singlewoman ) she was hired for a Nurse at St Thomas's Hospital in Southwark at the Yearly Wages of Sixteen pounds and her lodging and Continued one year under that Agreement during which time she constantly lodged in the sd. hospital

Sworn 19th April 1754 before}
