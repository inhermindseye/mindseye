Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Barrett aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of 10 Pods. a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that she was born in Dubl that she has 3 illegitimate Children living vizt. Wm . aged about 10 years born in the House of one Mr. Ginty in Change Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Mary aged about 9 years born in the House of one Mr. Atley in Nags Head Court in the same Parish and Michael aged about 7 years born in the House of sd. Mr. Atley in the same Place & Parish That one Jeremiah Fitzpatrick (who has been dead about 5 years father to the said 3 Children) That this Ext. was married the sd. Jereh. Fitzpatrick to in the year 1776 at Duke Street Chape according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Roman Catholick Religion

Sworn this 19th. day of July 1787  Wm. Kitchiner

her Mary Barrett mark
