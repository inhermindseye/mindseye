Middlesex ss  Jane Harley  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a Yearly hired Servant by & with John Medhurst in upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of Seventeen Months That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 29 day of July 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Jane Harley
