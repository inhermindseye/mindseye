Thos Robt. Farrell & Saml John Farrell B Chn of Mr Newman (Bastardy)

Saint Clement Danes July 6th. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  T. R Farrell B. born 27th Janry 1787  St CD  S. Jno Farrell , born 9th May/90 in St CD Reputed Father  to wit Mary Newman  singlewoman aged thirty three years or thereabouts on behalf of her illegitimate Children named Thomas Robert Farrell and Samuel John Farrell the one aged about 7 years and the other about four years  being Sworn and examined upon her voluntary Oath saith that she never was married That she on or about the 27th. day of January 1787 she was delivered of the said Thomas Robert Farrell at the House of Mr. Clarke a Telescope Maker No. 23 Houghton Street Claremarket in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and of the said Samuel John Farrell at the House of Mr. Allen Taylor No. 12 Vere Street  Clare market aforesaid on the 9th. day of May 1790 -That one Patrick Farrell by Trade a Smith formerly a Journeyman to a Master Smith in Crown Court  Saint Anns Soho is the Real Father of both the said Children-That he hath absconded that she this Examinant is poor and not in ability to maintain her said Children.

Sworn the 16th. day of July 1794 } Wm Kitchiner

Mary Newman
