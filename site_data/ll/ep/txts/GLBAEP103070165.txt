Middx ss. The Examination of Frances Sparks  Single woman taken on oath before Me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx residing in or Near the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County of Middx this 31 day of July 1756

This Examinant on her Oath saith that about eight Months ago one Thomas Glossup of Red Lyon Street in the parish of St. Mary Whitechappell in the sd County of Middx servant did Prevail with this Examinant to suffer him to lie with her at her own a house in Swan Alley in the sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And the sd. Thomas Glossup had Carnal Knowledge of this Examints. Body and so had several times Whereby she conceived With Child or Children of which she now goeth with & is Pregnant as aforesd. And this Examinant saith that the sd. Child or Children

of which she now goeth with and is Pregnant as aforesd. is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate she not being able to provide for the same And that the sd. Thomas Glossup is the true and only Father of the sd Child or Children so likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesaid And lastly saith that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 31 day of July 1756 before me  Richd: Ricards

The Mark of Frances Sparks
