Middx ss  Eleanor Hendrick Chapple  widow of John Hendrick Chapple deced Maketh oath that her sd. late husband was a Dutchman and born at Hamborough in parts beyond the Seas as this Depont. is informed & believes And this Depont Saith that her sd. husband Never was a hired Servant by the Year nor paid any Parochial Taxes Nor Never rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year & upwards in Great Britain to the knowledge or belief of this Depont Nor did he ever gain any legal Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or belief And this Depont Saith that her sd. late father rented a house of Ten pounds a Year about Twenty Six Years ago in the Parish of st. John Wapping in the County of Middx & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in thesd. Parish of st. John Wapping as this Depont. is informed & believes And this Depont farther Saith that she was never a hired Servant by the Year

Nor never rented a house of Ten Pounds by the Year me gained And that she has a Child Named Esther aged about 1yr & 11 Years Months the lawfull Issue of this Depont and her sd. late husband

Sworn this 8 day of Octr. 1751 before me  Richd. Ricards

The Mark of Hen Hendrick Chaple
