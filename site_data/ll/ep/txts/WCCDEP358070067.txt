Westmr. to wit Mary Burn Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Shipman at the Wheat at Sheaf in Holy well Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for five Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted the same upwards Five Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And Further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children w is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the when born Says that one Patrick Ward a Milkman the Seven Stars at Bromley in the County of Middlex is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the said Patrick Ward s House same time in the Month of June or July but the particular Day she does not now remember and at one other. after time at one of which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Patrick Ward and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry 1773 before me W Kelyngs

her Mary Burn Mark

Witness E: Fields
