Middlesex  Eleanor Curry  Singlewoman Maketh oath that divers Years ago She was bound apprentice by Indenture to Thomas Coe of Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of MIddlesex  Victualler for the Term of Seven Years and Served Six Years and Seven Months of the Said Term with her Said Master in the same Parish And then this Depont. and her Said Master by Mutual Consent Cancelled and burnt this Deponts. Indenture of apprenticeship And thereby (as it was then understood between this Deponent & her sd. Master) determined and Put an End to her said Apprenticeship And this Deponent Says That She has not Since been bound an Apprentice Nor lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year in any one Service nor was ever Married nor to her knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since her Apprenticeship.

Sworn this day of July 1769 before}
