Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Sarah Probeart Aged 50 years or thereabouts thereabouts and James Talboys One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Severally Make Oath and Say And first this Examinant Sarah Probeart for herself Saith That She Intermarried with Francis Probert her present Husband about Seven year ago at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry Since That their Intermarriage that they her said Husband Rented and Lived in  an Apartment in the House of the Bentley in King Street Golden Square in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for about Six or Seven Months  Saith That she doth not know what Rent her said Husband agreed to pay for the same - And this Examinant James Talboys for himself Saith That he has been Informed by the Wife of the said Mr Bentley which Information he this Examinant verily belives

to be true That the said Francis Probeart Hired the said Apartment at the Annual Rent of £12 - and That he paid for the said Apartment during the time That he Lived in the Same the Sum of £ And this Examinant Sarah Probeart for herself further Saith That Since her said House Husband Rented the said House he hath not to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief Gained any Settlement elsewhere then in the said Parish of Saint James in the said Liberty of Westminster and that She is Poor and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn before us this 10th. day of January 1786 Mercer Jno. Machin

Sarah Probeart James Talboys
