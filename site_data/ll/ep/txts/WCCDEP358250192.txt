Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Robert Jones aged about 35 years upon his Oath saith That on or about the 8th. of December last he Intermarried with Elizth . his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn That he this Deponent lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Nicholas Best who kept the Sign of the Blue Posts in the Haymarket in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for about Two years at the yearly wages of Six Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Fifteen years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of July 1795 } N Bond Rupt Clarke

Robert Jones
