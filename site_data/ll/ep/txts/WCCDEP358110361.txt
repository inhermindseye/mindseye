Middx  Frances Layng wife of Henry Layng (gone from her) upon Oath Saith she was married to her sd. husbd at the Fleet London upwds. of Eleven Years ago & has by One Child by her husband now at home with her (viz) Eliz. Ann aged upwds. of 9 Months & this Examinant further maketh Oath that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband Served his Aprentiship wth. a Perukemaker in St Martins Lane in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Send for one Year & that the Ovrs. of the Poor of the afsd. Parish of St Martin in the Fields lately apprenticed out another of this Exts. Childn. by the sd. Heny. Layng

Sworn Decr. 1st. 1753 before}
