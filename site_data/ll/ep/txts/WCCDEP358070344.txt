Middlesex to wit Joseph Lewis of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Yeoman maketh Oath that on or about the Twenty Sixth day of October in the year of Our Lord One Thousand Seven hundred and Seventy Three this Deponent rented a Certain House of Mr. James Higley called or known by the Name or Sign of the the York Arms situate in Little Shire Lane in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and lived therein for five Months or upwards to the best of his remembrance at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds but does not remember to have paid any Taxes due for the Same And this Deponent further saith that the Goods which were then standing in the House and which belonged to the said Mr. Higley were appraised and Valued to him this Deponent but he not being at that time possessed of so much Cash this Deponent applyed to one Mr. Bevan of Hatton Garden a friend of his who became Security or passed his Word to the said Mr. Higley for payment of the same and the said Mr. Bevan accordingly lent this Deponent Twenty Pounds which this Deponent Soon afterwards paid to the said Mr. Higley for abovementd. Goods Liquors Etc as by the said Mr. Higleys receipt may appear And this Deponent further saith that he was not put into the said House either by the said Mr. Higley or the said Mr. Beavan as a Trustee for them or either of them but upon his own Account only

Sworn this 17th. day of February 1776 before  Tho Lane

Joseph Lewis
