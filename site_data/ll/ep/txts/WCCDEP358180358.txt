Middx  Mary Marvell aged about twelve Years upon her Oath saith she was bound apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Parish of St. Ann in Black Fryers London to Benjamin Clements a Musician in Oxford Road in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. & lived with him about one Year & run away from her sd. Master about 2 Months since Says she never was Assigned over to any person nor lived as a Servt. for the Space of Six Weeks with her sd. Masters knowledge or Consent or otherwise gained Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or Beleif

Sworn 20th July 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Marvell
