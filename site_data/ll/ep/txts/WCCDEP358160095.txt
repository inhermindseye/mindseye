Middx The Examination of Saml. Kimbell Passed from the Parish of St. Giles without Cripplegate  Londn . by Order bearing date the 15th. Day of Novr. Inst. Who upon his Oath saith that abt. 25 Years ago he Rented & lived in a House of £18 a Year in Easter Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. County of Middx & Continued so to do for One Year And this Examint. further saith that he hath not since he left the sd. House Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife That he hath a Wife & being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the 17th. Day of Novr. 1739 before} Jas: Gronous

Saml Kimbell
