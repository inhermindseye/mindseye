Middx  Ann Price aged about 50 Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Charles Price with whom She Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Mary at Newington in the County of Surry upwards of Thirty Years Says her sd. late Husband Served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with his Father Nathaniel Price a Currier in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the sd. County of Surry as She hath been informed by him an which Information She verily believes to be true and that her said late Husband never Rented a House of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes out of the sd. Parish as she verily believes

Sworn the 18th. day of Decr. 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

Ann Price
