Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Swan aged about 53 years upon her Oath saith that about 12 years ago she Intermarried with Frederick Swan her present husband who is insane at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn That her said Husband informed this Examinant that he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Doller a Hair Dresser in Craven Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of July 1790  Sampn Wright  Herbt- Mayo

her Ann Swan mark
