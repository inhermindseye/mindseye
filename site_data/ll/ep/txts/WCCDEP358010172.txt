Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Smith aged upwards of 40 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Benman a Goldbeaters in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Two years & upwards at the yearly wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she has not lived as a yearly Servant for 12 Months or Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes

Sworn this 15th. day of Decr. 1787 D Walker Rapt: Clarke

Ann Smith
