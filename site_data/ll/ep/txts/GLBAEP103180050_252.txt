Middlesex ss}  Christopher Stevenson maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to William Wood of Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Pewterer & after having Served about five Years of Such his Apprenticeship in the Sd Parish his Said Master died & he this Deponent thereupon Went to Sea That this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement for himself Since Such his Apprenticeship as aforesaid to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn this. 11. day of July 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

Christopher Stevenson
