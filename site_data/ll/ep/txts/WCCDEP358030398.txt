Westmr. 

Francis Hone aged about 44 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with Thomas Hone in the Parish Church of St. Bennet Pauls wharf London about 14 Years since Says her sd. husband Thomas Hone now lives with Mr. Channing an Apothecary in Essex Street in the Strand Says her sd. husband rented the Globe Alehouse in Hungerford Market in the Parish of St. Martin in the feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £30 and upwards and lived therein near Six months Says her sd. husband has not since he left the sd. Globe Alehouse rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 27th 1747 before  Wm: Jones . Tho: Burdus

Frances Hone
