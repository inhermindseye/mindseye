St Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr (to wit) } Elizabeth Waters Aged 50 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Thos. Waters at the Roman Catholic Chapel Duke Street Lincolns Fields abt. 20 Years ago, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Doyle at the Crooked Billot Little Shire Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about two Years Years at the Annual Wages of £3, which she quitted abt. 1775, that she hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might acquire any other legal Settlement And further saith that she hath One Child Named Lawrence now living (who is in the School at Hackney belonging to the Irish Society ) and was born at Wych Street in Wickam Court Wych Street at Mr. Doyle's there, And that her Child (who is now upwards of thirteen Years of Age) never gained any settlement in his own Right And that she is Poor and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn the 23d. day of March 1791 . before Me  Wm Kitchiner

Elizth- Waters 's Mark
