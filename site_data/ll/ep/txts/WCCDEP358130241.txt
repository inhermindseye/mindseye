Middx.  Judith Murphey upon Oath says she Intermarried with her Husband James Murphey at the Fleet London abt. Sixteen Years ago that at present the sd. James Murphey absconds from her & she knows not where he is And that abt. Thirteen Years ago her sd. Husband Rented a House in Kings head Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Guinea's And lived in the same abt. four Months & that her sd. Husband has not since he quitted the said House, Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 1st day of Decembr. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

Judith Murphy
