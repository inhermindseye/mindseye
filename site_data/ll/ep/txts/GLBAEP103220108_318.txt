Middlesex ss}  John Brooks maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre to John Johnston of the Parish of St Paul Shadwell in the Sd County Mariner & after having Served two Years of Such his Apprenticeship he with his Said Master removed into the  Hermitage Street in the Parish of St George in the Sd County where his Apprenticeship expired That this Deponent was bound to the Sea Service and this Depont Says that he lay & lodged the last forty days of his Said Apprenticeship in his Said Masters house in the Hermitage Street aforesd That he hath a wife named Margaret to whom he was lawfully married That he this Deponent never rented a house of Ten Pounds year or upwds nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes.

Sworn this. 13. day of Janry 1787 before R Davies Robt: Smith

The Mark of John Brooks
