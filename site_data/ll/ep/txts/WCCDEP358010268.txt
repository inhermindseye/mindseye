Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Gladman aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. King a Butcher in Bridges Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex for Two years at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about four years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Febry. 1789  Sampn Wright N Forster

Mary Glaman
