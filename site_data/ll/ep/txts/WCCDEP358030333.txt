Westmr. 

Mary King aged about 21 years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a female Bastard Child on the 4th. Day of January Instant at Mrs. Lyes in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptired Elizabeth & is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same Says one Samuel Bird now Apprentice to Mr. John Hill  Watchmaker in Little Earl Street near the Seven Dials did be get the sd. female Bastard Child on her Body the sd. Examinant liveing a Yearly Servt . with the sd. Mr. Hill in Earl Street aforesd. where the sd. Samuel Bird had carnal knowledge of her Body in the month of April last & at several other times before & since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesaid & that he the sd. Samuel Bird & no other person is the true and real father thereof.

Sworn Janry 26th. 1746 before  Tho Lane  Tho. Burdus

Mary King
