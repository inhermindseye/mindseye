Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Ann Herbert aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith That about Thirteen years ago she Intermarried with William Herbert her late Husband who has been dead about Twelve Months at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Thomas aged about Eight years and Ann aged about Six years That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in flore Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds that he quitted said House about Five years after which he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement & saith that she as non Chargeable to Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn this 9th day of Octr. 1798 before us } Russ Clarke E Read

her Ann Herbert Mark
