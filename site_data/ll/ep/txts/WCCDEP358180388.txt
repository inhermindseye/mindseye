Mary Burnett  Wife of Edwd. Burnett upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with the sd. Edward Burnett on or about the 25th. Day of May 1718 at Lambs Chapell in the Parish of Cripplegate London Says the sd. Edwd. Burnett Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Chandler a Peruke Maker in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Says the sd. Edward Burnett now lives in a House in Hodesdon in the Parish of Broxborn in the County of Hertford of the Yearly Rent of £2:8:0 & pays his Share of all Parochial Taxes & hath so done for the Space of Thirteen Years or thereabouts

Sworne Octr. 3d. 1745 before Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Mary Burnett
