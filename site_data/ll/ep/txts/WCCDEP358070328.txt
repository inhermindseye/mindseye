Middlesex to wit Ann Preston Aged about forty years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Christopher Preston who has been gone from her upwards of Seventeen years but whether he is living or dead she cannot set forth having never seen or heard from him since the time he left her Says her said Husband before he deserted her He rented and lived in a House in Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for sevl. years at the yearly Rent of Nineteen pounds as she has been informed and verily believes hath quitted the said House upwards of Seventeen years ago since which time he hath never gained any other Subsequent Settlement to her Knowledge or beleif And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And further Says that one John Arnold as she believes his name to be and was late a Waiter at the Horse and Groom in Portugal Street is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Workhouse of the said Parish some time the latter End of March or the begining of April last past and at sevl. different times afterwards at one which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant & that the said John Arnold & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 14th. day Decr. 1775 before R. Butler

Ann Preston
