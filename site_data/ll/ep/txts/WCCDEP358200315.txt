Westmr.  Alice Edwards aged about 60 Years upon Oath says that being a Widow she Rented and lived in a house in Pettiford Yard in the Parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of five Pounds and Ten Shillings for Eight Years and was assessed and paid the Land Tax for the same and hath quitted said house about three Weeks.

Sworn 8th. Novr. 1765 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The Mark of Alice Edwards
