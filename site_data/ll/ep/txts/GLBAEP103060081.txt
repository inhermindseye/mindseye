Aldgate Middx

George Hobbs Maketh Oath that he was bound out an Apprentice for Seven years to Thomas Gumley of the Parish of St. Botolphs without Aldgate Middx  Carman with whom he Servd about One year and then was turn'd over to William Deal a Carman of Penington Street in the Hamlet of Wapping Stepney now the Parish of St. George Middx with whom he Servd the other Six years of his Apprenticeship and that he hath not gaind any other Settlement Since hath a Wife  Mary

Sworn 26th day of Febr 1746 before Robert Dennett Richd Ricards

the marke of George Hobbs
