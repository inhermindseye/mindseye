Westmr.  Ann Chandler aged abt. 42 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married Says her late Father  John Chandler deced lived in & rented a house in the forestreet in Hastings in the County of Sussex of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards for One Year & upwards as she hath been Informed & beleives Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt . for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 16th. 1747 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Ann Chandler
