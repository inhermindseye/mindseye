Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Turley aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith That she Rented and lived in a house in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Nine Months or thereabouts at the Yearly rent of Fourteen Guineas that she hath quitted said house about four Months since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might gained any other Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she hath Five illegitimate Children living Vizt. Wm. aged about Ten years born at No. 2 Carburton Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex , Richard aged about Eight Years born at No. 3 in Bird Street in the said Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex John aged about Six years and Mary aged about four years both born at No. 39 in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and Louisa aged about Seventeen Months born in Carey Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 5th day of Decr. 1797 before us N Bond W Kinnard

her Ann Turley Mark
