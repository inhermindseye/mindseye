Westmr  Christr. Phillipson Aged about Fifty Eight Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Jane in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about four Years ago hath One Child by his said Wife Named Jane Aged about Eleven Months Says he Rented a House in little Mays Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds & Lived therein about Two Years & Never after Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 21st of Decr 1764 Before} Thos: Balack  Tho Kynaston

Christopher Philipson
