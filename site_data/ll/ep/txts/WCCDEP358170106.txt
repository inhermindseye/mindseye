Susannah  Wido . of Geo: Lashley upon her Oath saith that she hath been Married to her sd. late Husband Geo: Lashley upwards of Twenty one Years & that he hath been dead abt. Nineteen Years & further saith that her sd. late Husband before his Intermarriage with her & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt - with one Mr. Borrett in Lincolns Inn Feilds in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx for the Space of One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds to her now best Remembrance & that since he left his sd. Service he hath not neither hath she since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 8th- Day of August 1741 } Jas: Gronous

The mark of Susanh- Lashley
