Middx ss  Mary Reading Wifeof Widow  of John Reading Deced Maketh oath that hersd. Husband has often informed her that he was bound apprentice to Joseph Stringer of ye Parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middx  Carpenter with whom he lived in the sd. Parish of Wapping about three Years of his sd Apprenticeship & then his sd. Master removed into Barnaby Street in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in ye County of Surry In which last Mencond. Parish this Depts. sd husband Served his sd. Master the residue of his sd. apprenticeship And by Means thereof gained a legal Settlemt. And this Depont. Saith that her sd. husband to the knowledge or belief of her this Dept. Never rented a House of Ten Pounds by the Year nor Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlemt. to the knowledge or belief of her this Dept. And Saith

that She hath one Child Named Elizabeth Reading the lawfull Issue of this Dept. & her sd. late husband aged about Eleven Years.

Sworn this 13 day of Sepr. 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  Boulton Mainwaring

Mary Red
