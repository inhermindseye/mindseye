Middlesex ss}  Sarah Smith maketh oath that She hath a Child named Elizabeth Cammock aged about fourteen Years which Child was born of this Deponents body in Cock Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & Such Child is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason that She this Depont was never Married to the Said Child's Father & the Said Child has not as Yet done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 9. day of Janry 1787 before  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Sarah Smith
