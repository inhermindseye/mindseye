Middx  Martha Gearing  Singlewoman upon Oath saith she never was married and that about five Years ago She was hired to live as a a Yearly Servant with Mr. Evans in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes Middx for at the Yearly Wages of £4 and lived with him under that hiring & Agreemt. about four Years & has not since she quitted her Sd. Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn April 28th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Martha Gearing
