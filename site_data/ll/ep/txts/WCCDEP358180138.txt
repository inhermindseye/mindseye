Middx  Joan Winter  Singlewoman upon her Oath voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & not able to maintain the same when borne & that one William Collins did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body he the sd. Wm. Collins at such time liveing a Yearly hired Servt. with this Exaiant at Mrs. Perham in Milverton in the County of Somersett where he became Acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of March last & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Willm. Collins & no other Pson is the true & reall father thereof

Sworne 2d: Decr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Joan Winter
