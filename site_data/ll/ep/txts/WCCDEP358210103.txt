Westmr.  Margt. Hammer aged about 62 Years Upon Oath says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stilliard at Brompton in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middlesex for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, hath quitted said Service about Eleven Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 17th. day of November 1766 , Before} Paul Vaillant  Tho Kynaston

Margett Hammer
