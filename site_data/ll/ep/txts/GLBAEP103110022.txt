Middlesex ss}  Mary Cooke the Widow of Robert Cooke deced maketh oath That her late husband in his life time rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Five Pounds in Peters Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and lived in Such house for many Years And this Depont. Saith that her Said husband has informed her (and which information She believes to be true) That during Such his residence in the Said house he was Charged to and Paid the Kings Tax for the Same and by Means thereof as She is advised he gained a legal Settlement there And that to her Knowledge information or belief her Said husband never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She Since his Death done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn this day of 1771 before}
