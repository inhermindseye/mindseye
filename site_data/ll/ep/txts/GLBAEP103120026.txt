Middlesex ss}  Ann Byrne the widow of James Byrne deced maketh oath That divers years ago her Said late husband lived in & rented a house at the yearly Rent of Nine Pounds in that part of East smithfield Which lies in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex and was Charged to and Paid the Kings Tax for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent after his Death lived in & rented the Said house & was charged to and Paid the Kings Tax for the Same and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish in her own right & that She this Deponent hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this day of December 1772 before}
