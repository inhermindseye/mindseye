Middlesex ss}  Jane Caper the wife of  William Caper (who is gone away from her to Places Unknown) maketh oath that her said husband has informed her & which she believes to be true That he duly served his Apprenticeship with Roger Sheppard in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Waterman & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That to this Depont. knowledge or belief her said husband hath not since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 2d. day of November 1773 before B Camper

Jane Caper
