Middx  Mary Wetherill Aged about Sixty Years Upon Oath Says She is the Widow of Geo: Wetherill with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of Stepney about Thirty Years ago Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with his Father John Wetherill a Clog Maker in Loyds Court in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx hath been out of his said Apprentiship about Thirty Three Years and Never after Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Nor she this Examinant hath not Since her said Husband Death Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year.

Sworn the 22d Day of Septr 1763 Before}

The Mark of Mary Wetherill
