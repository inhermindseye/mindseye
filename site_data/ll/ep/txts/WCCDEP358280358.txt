St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  GG (to wit) Ann Esterbone Hester Boon  Singlewoman Aged about Twenty eight years upon Oath saith that She never was Married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Delamotte in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes  Fan Maker for about fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of four pounds and since hath not obtained quitting the same about 2 Years ago she hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlemt. And this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish And that Evan Pugh Otherwise Hughes who now lives as she believes somewhere the Surry Side of the Black friars Bridge & is by trade a Taylor but get his living by Dealing in Old Cloaths is the only real true father thereof He and no one else having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body & begotten the Same thereon Child or Children of which is so pregnant thereon

Sworn the 3d day of January 1792 before Me} R Butler
