Middx & Westmr}  Joshua Holt Aged about fifty Three Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with John Wyndham Boyer  Esqr . in Upper Grosvenor Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds besides Diet and Lodging hath quitted his said Service upwards of Twenty Three Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of May 1784 before W Addington Thos: Gilbert :

Joshua Holt
