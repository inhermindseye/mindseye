Westmr. to wit Pennington Abel Aged about Seventy Two Years on his Oath Says he is now a Widower and has been so for upwards of Ten Years & has no Child or Children now living except a Daughter who is married and provided for Says he lived and Rented part of a House in May Poll Alley in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the same about about half a Year ago and hath not done any Act whereby to gain any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of July 1772 before me} Tho Lane

Penington Abell
