Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Theodocia St. George aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Dulpiner in Millman Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above she Bars in the County of Middlesex for 12 Months at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about 7 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act. to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Augt. 1787  Sampr Wright  Thos. Gordon

her Theodocia St. George mark
