Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Duncombe the widow of Charles Duncombe deced maketh oath that Since the death of her said late husband She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with Margaret Scott in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Widow & Continued in Such Service at Certain Wages for the Space of Several years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been Since married nor hath She done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20 day of September 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Elizabeth Duncombe
