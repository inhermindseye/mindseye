Mary Reynolds  singlewoman St. Clement Danes June 7th.. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Giles in the fields  (to wit) Mary Reynolds aged 18 years or thereabouts singlewoman and George Reynolds her Father aged about 38 years severally Make Oath and say And first this Examinant Mary Reynolds for herself saith that she never was Married or did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own Right And this Deponent George Reynolds for himself saith that he the said George Reynolds was Married to his Wife Maria formerly Maria Billington  Spinster at the Church of the United parishes of St. Vedast and St Michael Quern in the City of London on or about the fourth day of June 1775 (by license) that the said Mary Reynolds  who is the legitimate Daughter of luni this Examinant by his said Wife Maria that he this Deponent & Rented and Resided in a House in Lewknors Lane in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for about Nine years at the Annual Rent of  twelve pounds for about the first two years, of Ten pounds for about the next six years, and of £13 for the last part of the said Term of Nine Years that he quitted the said House in or about the Month of July in the year 1791 and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 9th. day of June 1794 before us} M Addington J Hind

Mary Reynolds 's Mark

George Reynolds

Witness B Newman
