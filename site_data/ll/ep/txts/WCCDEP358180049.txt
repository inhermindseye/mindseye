Middx  Frances Currer upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of John Currer  Deceased That her sd. late Husbd. John Currer served his Apprentiship with Mr. Heath an Upholsterer in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. & hath been out of his Time about Eighteen Years That since his sd. Apprentisp her sd. late Husbd. in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 14th- Decr. 1742 before} C. Egerton

Frances Currer
