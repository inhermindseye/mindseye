Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Hirst aged about 51 years upon his Oath saith that he served four Years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Battishill an Organist in Orange Street Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of March 1789  Sampn Wright  Wm Kitchiner

John Hirst
