Westmr 

Mary Tipton  Wido . of Henry Tipton who dyed abt. 10 Years since upon Oath Saith she lived a Yearly Servt. abt. Seven years ago (being then a Widow without Child in Children)with Mr. John Sharp a Carpenter in Clements Lane in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £6 for Sixteen Months or thereabouts and has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 23d. May 1749 before} Wm Jones

Mary Tipton
