Saint Clement Danes Octr. 7th. 1785

(to wit) John Wilson aged fifty Six Years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith that he was married to Hannah his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the field in the County of Middlesex about 30 Years ago, that he never served an Apprenticeship Etc found to buderly setted wth.in not sworn but and this Exn- T. N. Js.
