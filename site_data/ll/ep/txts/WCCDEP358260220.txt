Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Girdle aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that she never and married Intermarried with Richard Girdle her present Husband about Fourteen years ago (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square by whom she hath Three Children living Vizt. William aged about Seven years Mary aged about Three years both provided for and George about Five weeks unprovided for That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Newcastle Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty Nine Pounds That he hath quitted said House about Two years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that her said Husband is now in the Service of a Mr. Nugare in Cornwall where he has been for these Twelve Months last past

Sworn this 6th. day of Octr. 1797 before me G S Goodenough

Sar. Gidle
