Middlesex to wit Joseph Hills Aged about Sixty One Years On his Oath Says he Intermarried to his late Wife  Hannah at the Fleet London about Thirty five Years ago and by whom he hath no Children living, Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Cannon a House Smith in May Poll Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Term of Seven Years & served his said Master under such Indenture for three Years & upwards when his said Master gave up this Examinant his Indentures And this Examinant further Says that he was afterwards bound to or Covenanted with John Whitingham a House Smith  near Deadmans Place in the Parish of St. Mary Oversees otherwise St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry for the Term of Three Years & an half and after his Apprenticeship or Covenant was out he never gained any other Settlement either by renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds P Ann Paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 2d. day of Novr. 1780 before  Sampn Wright J Bosworn

Joseph Hills
