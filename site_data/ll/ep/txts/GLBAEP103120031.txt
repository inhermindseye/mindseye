Middlesex ss}  Catherine Pettett a singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired and lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages by & with one Ann Lastley in the Parish of Westham in the County of Essex & Continued in such service for the space of one year & upwards and by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That she this Deponent hath not since lived elsewhere as a hired servant for the Space of a year nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 4 day of February 1773 before  Rob Pell B Camper

The Mark of Catherine Pettett
