Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit John Duck aged about 77 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Eleanor his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Matfellon otherwise Whitechapel about Forty years ago That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house at Hampstead in the Parish of Hampstead in the County of Middlesex for about Three years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas and paid all Taxes due for the same That he hath quitted said House about Two years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th first day of May June 1795  J Hlund M Addington

John Duck
