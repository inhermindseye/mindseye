(No. Old lawyer Yard) St Clement Danes 28th April 1794

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) George Reynolds aged about 38 years on behalf of his Daughter Mary and Reynolds and also she the said Mary severally upon Oath say and first the said George Reynold Upon Oath saith that he was Married to Maria his present Wife formerly Maria Billington  Spinster at St Vedard Foster Lane London (by Ban License) on or about the 4th day of June 1775 that Subsequent thereto he this Deponent Rented and Resided in a House in Lewkners Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields for about Nine years at the Annual rent of twelve pounds for about the first two years of Ten pounds for the next six years or thereabouts & £18 for the remaind the latter paid of the time at £13. that he quitted that House in or about July 1791 and hath not acquired any Subsequent legal Settlemt. And this Examinant further Mary Reynolds for herself or saith that She Daughter Mary is of the age of about 18 years and hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own right, that she has not been Married and that being born in Wedlook the her last legal Settlement is in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in right of this Examinant's Father as aforesaid as she is informed & believes

Sworn the 28th day of April 1794 before us}

George Reynolds Mary Reynolds 's Mark

Witness
