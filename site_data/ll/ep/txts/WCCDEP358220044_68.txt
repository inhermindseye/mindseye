Middx & Westmr}  Elizabeth Brand  Singlewoman aged about six & Twenty years upon her Voluntary Oath says that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Borne a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex where she now Lodges she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when Born and saith on her Oath that one Hugh Hallmark a Servant to a Brewer had carnall knowledge of her Body some- time in the Month of May 1766 & at Severall times afterwards at one of which tines he Gott her with Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and That the said Hugh Hallmark and no other Person Did beget the same

Sworn this 11th Day of July 1769 before} Thos. Kynaston

The mark of the said Elizabeth Brand .

Witness Edwd. Cowbe
