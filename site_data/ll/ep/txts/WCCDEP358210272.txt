Westmr.  Hannah Woods Aged about Thirty six Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Ephraim Woods , who has been dead about Seven Years, says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Cretchley at the two Brewers in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds, quitted same about two Years ago, and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn the 7th. day of May 1768 , Before W Kelynge

The Mark of Hannah Woods
