Middx  Mary Jones Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Richard Jones Rents and lives in a House in the Hay in the County of Brecknock in South Wales where this Examinant was born as she believes And further Says that on the Tenth day of May last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Cook a Tobacconist in Houghton Street Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Jane and is become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same And that one Mathew Collins or Collie a servant to one Mr. Turner at No 2 Field Court  did beget such Bastard Child on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her Masters House at Knightsbridge in the Month of June 1778 and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he get her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Mathew Collins or Collie did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 26th. day of June 1779 before M Addington

her Mary Jones Mark
