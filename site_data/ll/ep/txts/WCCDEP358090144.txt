Middx to wit John Jenkins Aged about  Forty five Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his late Wife  Ann at the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields about Thirteen Years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children living says before his Intermarriage to his said late Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Berry at the Queens head Tavern in Holbourn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted his said Service about Eleven Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 14th. day of July 1780 before  Abrm Baley M Addington

his John Jenkins Mark
