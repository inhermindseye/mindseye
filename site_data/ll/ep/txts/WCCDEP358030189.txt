Middx  Mary Hermitage upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Steer a Grocer near the Markett Cross in the Parish of Great St. Mary in the County of Cambridge for the Space of three Years at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds & hath left her said Service upwds. of two Years Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Steers Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she was delivered of a female Bastard Child on the 22d. Day of June last in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which was Baptiz'd Mary & became Chargeable to the sd. Parish but is since dead Says that James Johnson a Taylor did begett the sd. female Bastard

Child on her Body the sd. James Johnson having had carnall knowledge of this Examinant on the 8th. Day of Septr. last at which time he gott her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. & that he the sd. James Johnson & no other person did begett the sd. female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd.

Sworn July 21 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Hermitage
