Westmr to wit Mary Gale Aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of John Gale now a Patient in the Middlesex Hospital with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Giles about Eleven Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Namely Sarah Aged about 3 Years and Mary Aged one Year and an half Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Scott a Plumber & Glazier in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. as Appears to this Examinant by her said Husbands Indentures and after he was out of his sd Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 2d. day of February 1771 before me} Tho Lane

Mary Gale
