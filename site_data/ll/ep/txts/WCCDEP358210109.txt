Westmr.  Mary Mushat aged about 30 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Brown a Butcher in Chandos Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, quitted said Service about Seven Years ago, and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt, for one Year

Sworn the 22d, day of November 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant R Kilsha

The Mark of Mary Mushat
