Middx  Arrabella Shewdell  Wido . of Thomas Shewdell upon her Oath saith abt. Thirty Years ago she Intermarried with her sd. late Husband who hath been Dead One Year & upwards & that in the Year 1713 her late Husband lived in & rented a House in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of ye- Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds for the Space of Two Years & upwds- & after he left the same he rented the Red Cow in the same Street & lived in the same for the Space of Three Months & upwds- at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Seven Pounds or thereabts. & yt. since he left ther hath this Examint. since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Pish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 17th- Day of August 1741 before}
