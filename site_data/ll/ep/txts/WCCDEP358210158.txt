Westmr.  Elizabeth Leethet Wife of Walter Leethet lodging at Mr. Dorrains in Burleigh Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Sarah Mullins an Infant aged about five Years Daughter of Henry Mullins by Sarah his Wife deceased Upon Oath says that she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true that said Henry Mullins Rented a house in New North Street in the Parish of St. George the Martyr by Queen Square in the County of Middlesex for six Months & upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds or thereabouts, and hath quitted the same upwards of three Years, since which time the said Henry Mullins hath not Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes to this deponents knowledge and belief and further upon her Oath says, the said Henry Mullins hath deserted the said Infant whereby she is likely to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes .

Sworn the 12th, day of May 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Frans Cary

The Mark of Elizabeth Leethet
