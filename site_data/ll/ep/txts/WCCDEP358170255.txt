Westmr. (to witt)}  Alice Bates  Wido . of Maximilian Bates upon her Oath saith that on the 10th. Day of Septr. 1729 she Intermarried with her sd. late Hasbd. Maximilian Bates who hath been dead abt. nine Years that the sd. Maximilian Bates lived in & rented a House in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Rent of 20.£. & upwds & hath left the same upwards of Twenty Years that since he left the sd. House he in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 12th: Augt. 1742 before me} Tho Lane

The Mark of Alice Bates
