Middx  Margt . the wife of Wm. Crathern (at Hodgsdon near Shoreditch Work house in Middx ) Maketh Oath that fourteen years agoe Edward Shepard was bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to the Depots former husband Samuel Bowles who then lived in Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St John Wapping Middx And the said Sheppard served Her said husband there about one year And then this said Dept husband removed into  Walnut Tree Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishop gate in the Liberty of the City of London and where the said Shepard Served her sd. late husband there About Nine Months and then bought out the Remr. of his time

Sworn this 24th. Janry 1742 before Nathl Chandler Rd. Ricards

the Mark of Margtt Crathern
