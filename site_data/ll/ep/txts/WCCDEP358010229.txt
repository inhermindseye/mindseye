Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Ward aged about 27 years upon her Oath Saith that about 4 years ago she Intermarried with Arthur Ward her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Giles Cripplegate & by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Ann aged about 15 Months That her said Husband never did any Act in his own Right to gain a Settlement that his Father Thos. Ward Served his time as an Apprentice to a Mr Hall a Sawyer in Barbricam in the Parish of Saint Giles without Cripplegate that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of July 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

her Sarah Ward Mark
