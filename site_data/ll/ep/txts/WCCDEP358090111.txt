Middx to wit Frances Allan Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says that about five Years ago she Intermarried to one Richard Perkins the Younger at the the Parish of Saint Olave in the City of York but the said Richard Perkins the Younger being then un Age Richard Perkins the Elder Instituted a Doctors Commons about four Years ago & in or about Michaelmas Term last the said Richard Perkins Obtained a Divorce therein And this Examinant ap her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Cla or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes (where she now lodges) she being P and unable to maintain the same when born Says the the aforesaid Richd. Perkins the Younger had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at a house facing Drury Lane Play House in Catherine Street and at one other time at a House near Lincolns Inn Fields but the Names or Signs she does not remember some time in the Month of April last or the beginning of May and at other time afterwards atone of which time she got her w Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Richard Perkins the Ygr who now lives at Messrs. Dudley & Lanes Lace Shop in Tavistock Street and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 13th. day of January 1780 before M Addington Sampn Wright  Wm. Kitchiner

Frances Allan
