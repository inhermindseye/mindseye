Middlesex ss}  Sarah Ray  wife of Mr Joseph Ray maketh oath that she wel knows a Child named George Powell aged about Eleven Years now resident in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County That the Said Child was born in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St John Wapping in the Sd County of Middlesex She this Depont being Present at his birth That Such Child was born a Bastard as she this Depont has been informed by the sd Childs Mother & which information She this Deponent believes to be true That the Said Child to this Deponts Knowledge or belief has not as Yet done any Act to gain a settlement for himself.

Sworn this. 3d. day of December 1783 before R Davies J Hawley

Sarah Ray
