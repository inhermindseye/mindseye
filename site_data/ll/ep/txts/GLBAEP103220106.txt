Middlesex ss}  Mary Davis maketh oath that about twenty eight Years ago She this Deponent lived in & rented a house at the Yearly Rent of Five Pounds on Great Saffron Hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the Sd County & during Such her residence therein which was for about the Space of three Years & half She this Depont was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same That She has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 6. day of Janry 1787 before us R. Davies Robt: Smith

The Mark of Mary Davis
