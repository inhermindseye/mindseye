Middlesex ss  William Fowell maketh oath that about Twenty years ago he rented a house in the Parish of St. Olave in the County of Surry and paid eighteen pounds a Year for the same & all Parish Taxes And saith that he hath not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor served any parish offices Nor done any Act to gain any subsequent settlement to his knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 20 day of November 1756 before  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Wm. Fowell
