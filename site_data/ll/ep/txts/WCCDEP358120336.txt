Middx  Mary Clark upon Oath saith she is the Wido: of Richard Clark to whom She was married at the Fleet London on the 4th. Decr. 1734 and that her sd. late husband Informed her he was hired as a yearly Servant to Mr Rt Stewart a & hynust & lived with him as a yearly Servant opposite Northumberland Alley in Fenchurch Street in the Parish of St Cathn. Coleman London upwds. of 5 Years whilst a Singleman and about 2 Years after his marriage with his Examinant and that he never rented Ten Pounds by the Year nor paid parish taxes which Information this Examinant believes to be true and she further maketh Oath that she has not since the Decease of her said Late husband rented ten pounds by the year pd. parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 19th. day of May 1755  before} Tho Lane  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Mary Clark
