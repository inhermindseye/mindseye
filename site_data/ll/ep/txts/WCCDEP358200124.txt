Westmr  Mary Hill Aged about Thirty three Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says that on the third Day of September 1758 she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child at Dr. Harveys in Berwick Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the County of Middx which hath Since been Baptized by the Name of Richard Says she Lived a hired Yearly Servant with John Chase  Esqr . in the Parish of Richmond in the County of Surry upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about Fifteen Years & hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 9th. of July 1764 Before R Kilsha Thos. Kynaston

Mary Hill
