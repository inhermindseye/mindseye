Middx ss  William Dill Maketh oath that about fifty three years ago he this Deponent was born in the Parish of St Mary in the Town of Reading in the County of Berks where this Deponents late Father Acquired a legal settlement by means of his said Fathers duly serving his apprentice in ye sd Parish as this Deponent has been informed and verily believes And this Depont. farther saith that his said Father did not to this Deponents knowledge information or belief Do any Act to Acquire a settlement subsequent to his sd fathers apprenticeship And this Depont farther saith that he this Depont. never was an apprentice nor a hired servant by the year nor ever served any Parish offices nor ever rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards nor never did any Act to gain a settlement in his own right to this Deponents knowledge or belief And that he hath a wife named Ann

Sworn this day of January 1761 before}
