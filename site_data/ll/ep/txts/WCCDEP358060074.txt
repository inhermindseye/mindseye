Middx  James Kinnaston aged about 56 Years upon Oath saith he intermarried with Margt . his Wife in the Tabernacle belonging to the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields on the 6th. Febry 1724 Says he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Harvey a Gunsmith in Shire Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that his sd. Apprentiship Expired about 34 Years ago and he has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 27th. 1750 before

James Kynaston
