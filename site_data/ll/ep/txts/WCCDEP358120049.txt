Middx  Sarah Wright upon her Oath Saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice for five years to Mrs. Prit a Mantua maker in Bride Lane London & Served her sd. Mistress upwards of three years of the sd. term & then absconded from her Service & never was assigned over to any other person or lived with any person as a Servant with her sd. Mistress Consent and that during her sd. Service with Mrs. Prit she continually Lodged with her Mother at Mr Johnsons the Corner of Ormond Street in the Parish of St. Geo: Queen Square in the County of Middlesex Says she never rented ten pounds a year pd. parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 29th. Janry 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Wright
