Middx.  Isabella Clark aged upwds. of 70 Years upon Oath saith she is the wido . of John Clark who dyed abt. 17 Yrs. ago. and that her sd. late husband lived sevty. Years in a house of his own in Cobbs Court in the Parish of St. Ann Blackfryers London Paid Scot & Lot in she sd. parish & Served the Office of Constable there and that neither her sd. late husband after he quitted the aforementioned house nor has this Examinant since her decease rented ten Pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Augt. 25th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Isab. Clark
