Middlesex  Sarah Brown Aged about 28 Years or her Oath says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Davis an Attorney at Law at No. 26 in Old Lincolns Inn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn for sevl. years at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service upwards of Four years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlemt. And further on her Oath says that on the 18th. day of May 1783 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which was soon afterwds. baptized by the name of Sarah and is become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same And further says that one John Davis an Attorney late of No.2 Lyons Inn & who Uses the Fountain in Clare Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body for sevl. years last past at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Davis & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 21st. day of June 1783 R Butler

Sarah Brown
