Middlesex ss}  Margaret Wright  Single Woman maketh oath that She Can give no Account respectg the Settlement of Parents or either of them never having received any information relative thereto nor doth She this Deponent know nor was She ever informed where She was born That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant in any place for the Space of a year nor was She ever married nor did She ever do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 17th day of Janry 1785 before  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Margaret Wright
