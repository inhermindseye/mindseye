Middlesex ss}  Ann Brown the widow of William Brown Deced Maketh oath That Several Years ago and Since her Said late husband's Death She this Deponent rented a house upon the Harrow Dunghill in the Parish of Saint George in Southwark in the County of Surry and lived there for about the Space of a year and during Such her Reference was charged to and paid the Kings Tax for the Same house and by Means thereof as She is advised She gained a legal Settlement in thesd. Parish and this Deponent Saith that She hath not Since rented a house nor Paid any Kings Taxes nor done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of 1771 Before  Cha: Digby B Camper

The Mark of Ann Brown
