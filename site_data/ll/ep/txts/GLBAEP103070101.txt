Middx ss  Elias Villers Maketh Oath that about forty years ago he was bound apprentice by Indre to Mr. Henry Simpson a Cordwainer who then lived in Broad St. Giless in the parish of St. Giles in ye Fields in ye County of Middx. & Continued in such Service for about four years & then was Turned over to Mr. Bernard Thatcher a Cordwainer of Lamb Alley in ye Same parish with whom he Served ye remaining part of his apprenticeship And by Means there of gained a legal Settlemt. there And farther saith that he was never a hired Servt. by ye Year nor Rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or Upwards Nor Served any parish offices nor to his knowledge or belief hath he gained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th day of April 1754 before us  Richd: Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

Elias Villers
