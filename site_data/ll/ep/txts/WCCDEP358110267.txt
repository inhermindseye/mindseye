Middx  Margt. Connor upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Rt Connor an Irishman who never gained a Settlemt. in England and that she can give no further Accot. of his Settlement. that she has 3 Childn. by her sd. husbd. (viz) Rd. aged 4 yrs. Rt . aged 18 Months & Jno. aged one Month and that before her Intermarriage with the sd. Robt. Connor she lived a yearly Servt. for three years with Mr Russell in white fryers Street in the Parish of St. Peter in Dublin at the yearly Wages of 40 Shills

Sworn June 23d. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Margt. Connor
