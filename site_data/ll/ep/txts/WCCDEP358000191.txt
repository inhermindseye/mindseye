St. Clement Danes

Ann Ward aged 35 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she intermarried with Wm. Ward at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn upwards of 12 Years ago That after their Intermarriage they Rented and lived in a House in Chancellor Court Cock Lane in the Parish of Saint Lennard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of 9 Guineas & paid Poors Rates & other Taxes for the same that they lived in the said House upwards of 12 Months that her said Husband deserted her about 8 Years ago, & that she never heard of him after and knows not whether he be living or dead  And smt. saith that her said Husband he or herself have either of them gained any subsequent Settlement to the best of her Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 3d day of Janry. 1785 before J Addington J Reading

Ann Ward
