Middx.  Winifred Reed aged abt. 46 yrs. upon Oath says she never was married Rented ten Pounds a year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servant son one Year says she was born in Little Shin Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx & further says she cannot give an Acct. of the Settlemet. of her father or Mother

Sworn the 13th of July 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Winifred Reed
