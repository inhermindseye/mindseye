Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret White aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that about Seven years ago she Intermarried with Michael White her present husband according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of Rome at No. 26 High Holborn by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Edward Edmund aged about Five years and Margaret aged about Two years That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Plough Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Seven Months since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted her and her Two Children and Refuses to Allow them any Support and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself and Two Children without Support from the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 19th. day of April 1799 . before me  Saml. Edwards H Day

Her Margaret White Mark
