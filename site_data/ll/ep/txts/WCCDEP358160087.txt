Middx The Examination of Phebe Gutheridge  Widw . Aged 73 Years Passed from the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr . Taken ye. 27th. Day of Octr. 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that her late Husband James Gutheridge Deced about 27 Years ago lived in & Rented a House agst. Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £23 & lived in the same for the space of two Years or thereabouts And this Examint. further saith that her sd. Husband in his life time nor she since his Death never after they left the sd. House Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her Knowledge or beleife And yt. she is now Poor and Old and not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Phebe Gutheridge
