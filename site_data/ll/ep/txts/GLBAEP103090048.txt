Middlesex ss  Robert Wolf maketh oath that he hath been informed by Eleanor Charrington deced that she the said Eleanor Charrington was this Deponts. Grand mother and that he this Deponent was a Bastard and born as an illigitimate Child in Moses & Aaron Alley in the Parish of Saint Mary White Chappell in the County of Middx The which Information this Depont. Verily believes to be true And this Deponent Saith that he never lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever bound apprentice nor ever Rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever served any Parish offices or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor to his knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a settlement in his own Right And that he is a single & unmarried Man and about Twenty five years old.

Sworn this 11th. day of November 1766 before  Chris: Scott

Robert Wolf his Mark
