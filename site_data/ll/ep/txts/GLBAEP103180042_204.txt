Middlesex ss}  Hester Reynolds maketh oath that Sometime ago her Sister Ann Reynolds (who is now in the Workhouse of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & So far disordered in her Mind as to be in capable of giving any Testimony respectg. the place of her last legal Settlement) was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year with one Mrs Bury in King Street in the Said Parish at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for two Years & upwards as the Sd Mrs Bury has informed this Depont & which this Deponent believes to be true That her Said Sister to this Deponents knowledge or belief has not Since lived in anyone Place elsewhere as a hired servant for a year to her knowlege or belief nor was her Said Sister ever Married to her knowledge informn or belief nor hath her Said Sister to this Deponents knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a Settlement Since her living with the said Mistress Bury as aforesaid.

Sworn the 13. day of June 1778 before P. Rowlands

Hester Reynolds
