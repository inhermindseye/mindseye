Middlesex ss}  Mary Goodwyn the wife of Thomas Goodwyn maketh oath that her said husband who is now in the workhouse belonging to the Parish of St John Southwarke in the County of Surrey has informed her & which She believes to be true That he heretofore rented a house in the said Parish & was Charged to & paid Poore Rates for the Same & has not since to this Depont. knowledge information or belief done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement & that she was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 10th. day of February 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Mary Goodwyn
