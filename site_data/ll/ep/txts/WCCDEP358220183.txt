Middlesex & Westminster }  Eleanor Gordon Aged about Forty Six Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Thomas Gordon who has been dead about four Years Says that her said Husband was Bound an Apprentice to Thomas Gordon her said Husband's Father by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with his said Father who then kept a House in Mays Buildings in the aforesaid Parish of Saint Martin's and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 13th: day of October 1770 before us} W Kelyngs

The Mark of Eleanor Gordon

Witness Will: Hughes
