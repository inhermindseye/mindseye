Westmr. to wit James Bond one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on behalf of Catherine Porter an Infant Aged about fourteen Years upon Oath Says that the said Catherine Porter was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish by Indenture bearing Date the Eighth day of June One Thousand Seven hundred and Seventy to John Pearson of the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex Teaman untill the said Catherine Porter shod. Attain her full Age of Twenty one Years or day of Marriage according to the Statute in that Ca made and provided as by the sd. Indentures of Apprenticeship relation being thereunto had may Appear And further Says that the said Catherine Porter never was Assigned Over to any other Person nor ever gained any or Subsequent Settlement to the Knowledge or belief of this Examinant

Sworn this 30th. day of Novr. 1770 before Us} Tho Lane

James Bond
