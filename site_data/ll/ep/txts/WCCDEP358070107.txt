Westmr. to wit Ann Robins Aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says she was born in the County of Somerset but in what Parish or place this Examinant cannot set forth says that on the Twenty Ninth day of Decr. 1771 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Hudson a Broker in little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of Martha Masey & says that one Richard Masey a Chelsea Pensioner and who now uses the Hatt & Feather in Milford Lane did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become charged to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes < she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and further says the said Richard Masey did beget the same they having cohabited together as Man & Wife for upwards of Eight Years and that the said Richard Masey is the real and true father the thereof.

Sworn this 17th. day of June 1773 before W Kelyngs

her Ann Robins  mark
