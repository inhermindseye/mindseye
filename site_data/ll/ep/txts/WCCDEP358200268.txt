Middx.  Sophia Bates aged about Twenty Years upon Oath says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Owen a Fishwoman in Church Lane in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for two Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds and Twelve Shillings hath quitted same about four Years and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that on the 17th. Novr. 1763 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Spriggs in Thomas's Street in the parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex which was baptized Margaret and is now living and that one William Davis a Bricklayer did beget the said female Bastard Child on her Body of which she was delivered as aforesaid

Sworn this 6th. May 1765 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sophia Bates

N: B: Mr. Vaillant sign'd the Exucon on the other side by Mistake; should have sign'd this
