Elizth Poolman  Amelia Poolman her illegit. Child

(After Birth) 

Saint Clement Danes February 20th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Mother's settlement St CD  Child's birth  Reputed Father to wit) Elizabeth Poolman aged 34 years or thereabouts Singlewoman upon her Oath Saith That She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Kennett of Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of 11 Guineas for upwards of 18 Months that She quitted his Service about 18 Months ago, and since quitting same has not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And this Examinant further Saith that she this Examinant was on the Twenty sixth day of Novemebr last delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr Norman of No. 6 Plough Court Carey Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex And that James Lloyd , who as this Examinant believes lives in Piccadilly and is a Victualler is the real and true Father of such Child (which has been privately half Baptized by the Name of ( Amelia Poolman ) he having had first carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon-And that She

this Examinant is poor and unable to Maintain herself and said Child

Sworn this 21 day of Feby. 1794 bef } Wm. Kitchiner

E Poolman

James Lloyd lives in Piccadilly keeps the Public House called the Running Horses

22d. Febry. 1794. Far. paid £21- and Executed Release
