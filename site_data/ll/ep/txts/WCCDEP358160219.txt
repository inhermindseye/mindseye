Middx  The Examination of Thos. Manning lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 22d. Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that abt. 37 Years ago he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Joseph Knight in Catherine Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . & Served him as an Apprentice for the Space of 7 Yrs. & further upon his Oath saith yt- he hath not since yt. time rented Ten Pounds a Year pd- parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & that he hath a Wife nam'd Susannah aged 64 Yrs- & he being Poor is not able to maintaine himselfe & Wife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Ja: Gronous  Chas: Egerton

Tho: Manning
