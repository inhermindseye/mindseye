Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Susannah Watkins aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith That she Intermarried with William Watkins her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about Twelve years ago by whom she hath no Child or Children living That her said Husband has been dead about Four years That her said husband lived as a hired yearly Servant at the Devil Tavern in Fleet Street in the parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for Twelve Months and upwards as her said late husband informed this Examinant but what kept the house was the Persons Name who was her said late husbands Master she this Examinant does not Recollect

Sworn this 16th. day of May 1795  J Hund Edwd: Read

her Susannah Watkins Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
