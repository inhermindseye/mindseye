Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Plaistow aged about 37 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Harvey of Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that he hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of Novr. 1797 before me} J Hunter

James Plaistow
