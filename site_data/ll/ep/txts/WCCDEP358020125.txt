Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Drayson aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that her Father Rented and lived in a House in Shepherds Markett in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for Twelve years at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and upwards that this Examinant lived in the said House with her Father as one of his family

Sworn this 14th. day of Janry 1789  Sampn Wright M Addington

Sarah Drayson
