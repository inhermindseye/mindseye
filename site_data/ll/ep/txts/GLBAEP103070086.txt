Middx ss  Robert Smith Maketh oath that he has been often informed by his Uncle Nicn. Smith that this Deponts. father James Smith dead about twenty Years ago Rented a house in Great Russell Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the sd. County of Middx and paid above Ten pounds a Year for the Same and all Parish Taxes and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there as this Depont. is informed & believes And this Depont. Saith that he hath a Sister Named Mary a Singlewoman & of. Age the lawfull issue of this Deponents sd. Father who was Never a hired Servant by the Year nor apprentice Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement but in right of her Sd. fathers Settlemt. abovemenconed to this Deponts. knowledge or belief

Sworn this 21st day of August 1753 before us  Richd Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

Robt. Smith
