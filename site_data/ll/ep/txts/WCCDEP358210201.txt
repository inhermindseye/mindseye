Westmr.  Mary Norris aged about 17 Years upon Oath says, she never was Married, and that her father William Norris Rented a Shop and Tenement in Clement's Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for five Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Nineteen Pounds and there died; says that she this Examinant never Rented House, paid Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 28th. Oct. 1767 , before W Kelynge

The Mark of Mary Norris
