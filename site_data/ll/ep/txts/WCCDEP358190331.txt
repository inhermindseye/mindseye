Middx  Phebe Johnson Aged about Twenty Three Years Upon Oath Says she was never married Says she lived a hired yearly Sert. with Mr Harris at the Bulls Head in Vear Street  Clare Market in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds quitted her said Service upwards of one Year hath one Child now living named Margt: aged about Fourteen Months born in Naggs Head Court in the said Parish of St Clement Danes Says Matthew Stone the reputed father hath absconded she doth not know where he is to be found Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards that one James Cox had Carnall knowledge of this Examinant he and she living together as Man & Wife that he ye sd: Jas Cox & no other Person did beget the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant that he ye sd: Jas Cox  dyed ye 11th Day of January 1763

Sworn February ye 16th before me} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Phebe Johnson
