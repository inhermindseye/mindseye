Middx  Elizabeth Chew aged about 35 Years Removed from the Parish of St. Mary White Chapel in the County of Middx by Order bearing date the 31st. day of October 1761 upon Oath saith she was never Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Barron a Taylor in Arrundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds Quitted the Same about Nine Years ago and hath not since kept House paid Land Tax or Poor Rate or been a Yearly hired Servant for Twelve Months together And further saith she was on or about the 21st. day of May 1756 Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of Mr. Roddart Shoemaker in Shepherd's Yard in the Parish of Trinity Minories in the Liberty of his Majesty's Tower of London which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Elizabeth and that she was on or about the 25th. day of October 1757 delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Sermon Lane  Doctors Commons in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen in Old Fish Street London which

hath since been Baptized by the Name of Samuel Mullins And that she was on or about the 29th. day of May 1759 delivered of one other Female Bastard Child in Three Gun Court in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate London which has since been Baptized by the Name of Isabella which said Three Children are likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she this Examinant being Poor and incapable of Maintaining the Same

Sworn the 4th. day of November 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond Fran: Bedwell 

The Mark of Elizabeth Chew
