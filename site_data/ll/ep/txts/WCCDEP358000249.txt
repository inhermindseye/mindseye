St. Clemt. Danes

Middx (to wit)  George Edwards aged 50 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he Intermarried with Mary his now Wife at the Parish Church of Cripplegate about 15 years ago That he hath 1 Child ren living by his said Wife Named Joseph aged 9 years or thereabouts and the Examinant further saith That he was bound Apprentice to George Draper of Baldwins Gardens in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Shoemaker for 7 years by Indenture duly executed that he served the whole of his said apprenticeship with the said George Draper since which time he this Examinant hath not done any not to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 16th. day of March 1786 before  Rt. Taylors  Jno Barnfather

George Edwards
