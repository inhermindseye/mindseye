Middlesex ss  Ruth Larwood the widow of George Larwood deced maketh oath that her said late husband hath informed her that he was bound Apprentice by Indenture and served three years and half of his apprenticeship with one John Miles in the Parish of New Brentford in the County of Middlesex  Poulterer and by means thereof as this Deponent is advised gained a legal settlement there The which information this Deponent verily believes to be true And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge information or belief her said late husband never rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor ever paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever Served any Parish offices Nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his said apprenticeship Nor hath she this Deponent done any Act since her said late husbands death to gain a settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief And this Depont. farther Saith that she hath a Daughter named Sarah aged about Thirtynine years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband who never did any Act to the knowledge information or belief of this Deponent whereby to gain a settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 3st. day of July 1766 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Ruth Larwood her Mark
