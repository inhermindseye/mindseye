Middx  Martha Jesop aged about thirty one Years upon Oath says, She Intermarried with Moses Jesop her late Husband Deceased at the Fleet London abt. Fourteen Years ago, And that he serv'd his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with Jno. Purdey a Plaisterer & Slaterer at the White Lyon Alehouse the upper End of Hog Lane in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. Says her said late Husband never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant as She has been informed by him & verify believes. and that She this Examinant since his Death hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parochial Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 15th. Day of June 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham  Barth: Hammond

The mark of Martha Jesop
