Middlesex ss  Mary Kelly  widow of Martin Kelly deced maketh oath that about twenty Years ago her said late husband rented a house of the Yearly rent of nine Pounds & ten shillings on Great Saffron hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the said County & during such his residence therein he was charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same she this Deponent having at times been present when her said husband paid such Tax to Mr Finamore the Collector thereof That her said husband did not afterwards rent a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor hath she this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That she was lawfully married to her sd husband & that such receipts so given for the Land Tax as aforesd were delivered up by this Deponents husband to Mr Mashiter of Baldwins Gardens Leather Lane his Landford upon his pay him the Rent due for the sd house

Sworn this 30. day of August 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Mary Kelly
