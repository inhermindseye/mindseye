Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Elizth. Devin aged about 25 years Widow upon her Oath saith that about 10 years ago she Intermarried with Thos. Devin her late Husband (who has been dead about 6 years as she has been informed) at the Parish Church of St. Mary at Hill at Billinsgate by License that she hath One Child living by her said Husband Vizt Thos. aged about 8 years-that since her said Husbands Death she Rented and lived in a House at Walworth in the Parish Church of St. Mary at Newington in the County of Surry for 2 Months & upwards at the yearly Rent of 18 Guineas hath quitted said House about 15 Months ago since which time she has not rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or ever did any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Informative or belief she may have gaine any Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 26th day of Septr. 1786  Sampn Wright M Addington

Eliz Devin
