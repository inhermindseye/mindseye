Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Thomas Hewitt aged about 41 years upon his Oath saith That he served his time as an Apprentice to one Mr Harding a Taylor in Grange Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a Subsequent settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of Septr. 1797 before me G Goodenough

Thos. Hewitt
