Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Mumford the widow of John Mumford deced Maketh oath That her Said late Husband divers years ago lived in & rented a house in Crown Court in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and was Charged to and Paid the Kings Tax for the Same & by Means thereof gained & a legal Settlement in the said Parish And this Deponent Saith that her Said husband did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And this Deponent farthr Saith that Since her Said late husbands Death she hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right or any Settlement by Marriage That her Said late husband had by aforesd wife one Mary Mumford who is now living & who was a Child & Part of his Family at the time he lived in Crown Court & Paid the Kings Tax there as aforesd. And that to her knowledge or belief she the sd Mary Mumford never was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year no was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this day of 1771 before}
