Middx to wit & Westmin}  Mary Lucas of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Widow and Sophia Thomas Daughter of the said Mary Lucas severally make Oath and say and first this Examinant Sophia Thomas on her Oath says that she is about Twenty five years of age that she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a Hired  yearly Servant for One year and this Examinant Mary Lucas mother of the said Sophia Thomas on her Oath says that about Twenty six years ago she became acquainted with One Thomas Thomas by whom she had her said Daughter Sophia Thomas but that she never was married to the aforesaid Thomas Thomas but that her said Daughter was born a Bastard in the House of Mr. Peters in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of middlesex and that Her said Daughter never gained any other Settlement than by a said

Sworn this 22nd. day of Aug. 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner R. Butler

Mary Lucas Sophia Thomas
