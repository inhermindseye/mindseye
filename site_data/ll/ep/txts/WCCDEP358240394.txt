St. Clement Danes 26th. March 1794

Middx and Westmr.}  St.. C. D.  Elizth. Elly aged 65 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she rented an Apartment at Mr. Moore Block Maker Surry Street Strand for 6 Months and upwards at the annual Rent of £10 Pounds that she quitted that Apartment upwards of 20 Years ago and hath not since done any act matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge or belief

Sworn this 26th. Day of March 1794  before us} Wm Kitchiner

her Eliz Elly Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
