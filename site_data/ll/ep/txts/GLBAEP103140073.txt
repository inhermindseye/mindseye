Middlesex ss}  Thomas Smedley maketh oath That in the years one thousand Seven hundred & forty four & one thousand Seven hundred & forty five he lived in & rented a house in Brook Street in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex That during Such his Residence therein he was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same to one Mr Wilcox who was the Collector of Such Tax and gave Receipts for the same to this Deponent which were afterwds delivered upto his Landlord on this Deponts. paying his Rent for the said house & that by means thereof as he this Depont. is advised he gained a legal Settlement there That he this Depont. has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th day of December 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

Thomas Smedley
