St. Clement Danes 12th July 1786

Middx (to wit) Catherine Poole Aged 21 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That John Perry an Illegitimate Child Aged about two Months was Born in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex and of whom she this Examinant is the Mother and that she is Poor and unable to Maintain the said Child

Sworn this 12th July 1786 before us  James Paine G Mercer

her Catherine Poole Mark
