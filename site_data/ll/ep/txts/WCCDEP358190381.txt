Middx  Elizth. Poole Aged about 25 Years Upon Oath Says she was Never Marryed Says she was Bound Out by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx to John Clarke a Taylor in Wych Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes about Ten Years ago with whom she lived about Nine Months and hath left her said Apprentiship about Nine Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert for One Year.

Sworn the Day of September 1763 before}
