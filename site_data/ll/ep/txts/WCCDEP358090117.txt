Middx to wit Alice Stidaway Aged about Twenty five Years On her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes say She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Mason a Butcher at Abergavenny South Wales in the County of Mon Upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Years ago since which time She hat gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 17th. day January Instant She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Burke in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which is intended to be baptized Elizabeth and is become Chargeable to the same she being Poor And Unable to Maintain the same  and that One Edw Edwards late a Waiter at the Oxford Coffee House in the St And who now lodges Opposite a Coal Shed in Exeter Street had Carnal knowledge of her Body at the Crown Eating Hous in Princes Street facing the Mansion House in the City of London the latter End of March last but the particular day She does not now Remember And at  Several different times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which She was so delivered And that the said Edwards Edwards and no other person did beget the same & that he is the Rent & true Father thereof

Sworn this 31st. day of Janry. 1780 before  Wm. Kitchiner

her Alice Stidaway Mark
