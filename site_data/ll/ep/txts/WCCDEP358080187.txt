Middx- & Westmr-}  Cathr- Lloyd aged about 70 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of William Lloyd who died about three Years ago with whom he Intermarried at the Duke Street Chappell about twenty eight Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living says her said late husband Rented and lived in a house in Jacksons Alley Russell Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr - for thirteen Years at the Yearly Rent of £9 for two Years and at £8 a Year for sevl. Years afterwards and paid Land Tax and other Parochial Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said house about twenty Years ago since which time she hath never gained any other subseqt- Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th- Day of Novr- 1777 before  John Cox . John Croft

her Cathe- Lloyd Mark
