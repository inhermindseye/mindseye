Middx.  Wm. Robinson upon Oath saith that upwds. of 20 years ago (being at that time a Singleman ) he lived a Yearly Sevt. wth. Mr Tho Hall in the Parish of Farwell in the County of Bedford for one year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Guineas Dyet Washing and Lodging and has not since he quitted his sd. Service rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly servt. for one year

Sworn Septr. 5th 1753 before}
