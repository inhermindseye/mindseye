Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Atwood aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that about Eight years ago s Intermarried with Wm. Atwood her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bone That her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Four Months and upwards at the yearly rent of Twenty Pounds and upwards & paid Taxes due for the same That he hath quitted said House about Five years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 21st- day of April 1796 } Benj Tebbs

her Sarah Atwood Mark
