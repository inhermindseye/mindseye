St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Elizth. Edwards Aged 52 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband John Edwards Deceased at the parish Church of St. Michael in the Town of Cambridge by Banns, in or about the Year 1758 that since then her said Husband Rented & Resided in a House Numbered 7 in Drury Lane in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex abt. Six Five Years at the Annual Rent of £12 which they quitted about 20 Years ago since her said Husband in his life time did not do nor has she since his Decease done any Act or thing whatsoever whereby to the best of her knowledge or belief he in his life time or she since might acquire, any legal settlement And saith that she is Poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 13th April 1791 before Us  Edw Read  Wm Hyde

Elizbeth Edwards
