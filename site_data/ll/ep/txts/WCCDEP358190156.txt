Middx  Ann Barnes aged 21 Years Lodging in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married That she was on the Nineteenth day of April 1761 Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Lying in Hospital in Duke Street near Grosvenor Square in the Parish of St. George  Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Anne says she is poor and unable to Maintain the Same

Sworn the 16th. day of July 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Barnes
