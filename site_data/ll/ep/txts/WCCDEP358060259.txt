Middx  Richd Humphrys upon Oath saith he intermarried with his present Wife (late Eliz. Wacket ) at the Fleet London on Easter Monday last and that he was bound Apprentice by Indenture dated the 9th- Day of May 1743 to Mr. Richd Wm. Bunting Peruke Maker and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with the sd. Mr Bunting in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes Middx and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 6th. 1752 before  Fran. Bedwell

The Mark of Richd Humphrys
