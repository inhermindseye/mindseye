Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Daniel Douglass aged about 49 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Elizth . his present Wife at the Parish Church of St. Luke Old Street upwards of Ten years ago and by whom he hath one Child living vizt. Sholto George aged Seven Months or thereabouts Saith he Rented and lived in a House in Silver Street White Fryars in the Precinct of White Fryars in the City of London at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted said House about 18 years ago since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Janry. 1786 before Mr: Wright Wm Gascoigne

D. Douglas
