Westmr. 

Mary Pickering  wido . upon Oath saith she inter- -married with her late husband Robt. Pickering at Sherbourn Church in the County of Hands upward of nine years ago Says who has been informed & beleives that her sd. late husband lived a Years Servant with one Mr. Justice in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx for three years or thereabouts whilst a Singleman and never after he quitted his sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has one Child by the sd. Rt. Pickering (now living) viz Rt. aged near ten Years who together was her this Examinant was removed by Order

two Justices dated the 1st. Febry 1741 from the Parish of Basings to be in the County of Southampton & since that time she has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn June 8th. 1748 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Pickering
