Middlesex ss}  Mary Emley the widow of Robert Emley deced maketh oath that Since her sd husbands decease She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with John Jerry of  Sweedland Court in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of three years & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain anr Settlement

Sworn this 21 day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Emley
