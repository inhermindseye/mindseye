Westmr.  John Smith upon Oath saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Ralph Harlis in the Parish of Yoxford in the County of Suffolk and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 19th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of John Smith
