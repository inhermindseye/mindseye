Westmr.  Susannh. Clark aged abt. 27 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she was born in Newport Street Market in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. where her father lived in & rented a house and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says she was bound Apprentice for four Years to Mrs. Bonnette a Mantua maker in Chandois Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Gardn . and Served her as an Apprentice about three Years at the End of which time her sd. Mistress dyed and that during the sd. three Years of her Apprentiship she lay at her mothers in Grafton Street & King Strt. in the Parish of St. Ann aforesd. Says she never was Assigned over to any person rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 16th. 1748 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Susa. Clark
