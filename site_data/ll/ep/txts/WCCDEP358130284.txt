Middx.  Mary Jones aged abt. 38 Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Danl. Jones who hath been dead abt. two Years says her said Husband served three Years & upwards of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Hopkins a Butcher in Tower Street in the Parish of alhallows Barking London & then his said Master left off Business & gave this Examinants Husband leave to provide for himself in what manner he thought proper & that he never was assigned ever to any Person Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes Says She hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the 1st day of March 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Jones
