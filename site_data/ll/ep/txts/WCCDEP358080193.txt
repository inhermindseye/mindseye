Westmr- to wit Elizth- Hillier Aged about 50 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Says she Rented and lived in a House Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Nark a Coachmaker the Corner of Swan Yard in Bishopsgate Street in the Parish of saint Botolph without Bishopgate London for two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds a Year hath quitted her said Service upwards of five Years ago since which time she has gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 9th- Day of Decr- 1777 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth- Hillier Mark
