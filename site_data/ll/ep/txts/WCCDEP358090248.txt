Middx to wit Catherine Shires Aged about Twenty five years on her Oaths say's she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired yearly Servant with One Mr. Jelicoe an Upholder in Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Bride otherwise Bridget London for Thirteen Months at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four years ago and after Quitting her said Service she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or Living a Hired yearly servant for One year and further says she hath One Child named  Catherine aged about five Months a Bastard born on the Body of this Examinant by one James Tiller and which said Child was born in the Precinct of White Fryers and hath gained no other Settlement then by birth as this Examinant hath been Informed and believes

Sworn this 31st. day of Augt. 1781 before  Wm. Kitchiner R Butler

Catherine Shires
