Middx & Westr} to wit Elizabeth Winter Aged about Fifty Seven Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Christopher Winter who has been Dead upwards of five Years and by whom she had no Children now living except a Daughter married and provided for, Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Holles Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Eighteen Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and paid at Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of five Years and a half ago, and after he quitted the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 19th day of Sepr 1775 before  David Walker

Elizabeth Winter her Mark.
