Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Lewis  Single woman maketh oath that divers years ago her late Father George Lewis deced Rented a house in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same & to this Deponents knowledge or belief her said Father did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor hath She this Deponent ever been bound Apprentice or ever lived as a hired Servant for a year or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Lewis
