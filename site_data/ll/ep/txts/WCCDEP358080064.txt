Middlesex & Westmr.}  Mary Bennett Aged about 23 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr. Walker a Butcher in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Years ago since which time she hath never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further on her Oath Says that on the 17th. day of April last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish which has since been baptized by the Name of Mary and is now chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same And further says that one Thomas Manwood a Journeyman Weaver at Shackle well is the Father thereof and no other person whatever.

Sworn this 21st. day of June 1776 before}

Mary Bennett
