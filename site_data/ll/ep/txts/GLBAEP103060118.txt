Middx ss  The Examinacon of Rachael Peters  Singlewoman Taken on oath before us Two of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the County of Middx (whereof one is of the quorum) and both residing in or near to the limits of the parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County this Day of July in the year of our Lord 1749.

This Examinant upon her oath Saith that about Two years and 9 months ago one Samuel Stanley of the said parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate  Cooper who then lived in the house of this Deponts Mother in nightingale Lane in the Said parish did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at her Sd Mothers house & had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body Several times whereby she conceived with Child of a Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered at the house of her sd. Mother on the Third Day of September 1747 which said Female Bastard Child hath been baptised by the name of Sarah Stanley and hath been now is & Still is likely to be chargeable to the Said parish of St. Botolph without aldgate this Examinant being Poor & not able to provide for the same And this Examinant further saith that the said Samuel Stanley is the true and only father of the Said Female bastard Child And that this Examinacon is Voluntary and that She hath not been forced or compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 2nd Day of July Augst. 1749 before me  Richd: Ricards  Saml Gower

Rachel Peters
