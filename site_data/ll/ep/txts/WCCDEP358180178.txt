Middx  Mary Miller aged 24 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman That she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Robert Howlett  Victualler in the parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of £3..10s for the Space of One Year & Nine months & hath left her sd. Service 2 Months & upwards & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when borne & that James Grant  Taylor did begett the said Child or Children on her Body the sd. James Grant Lodging at the sd. Mr. Howletts there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of Octr. last & at severall other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. James Grant & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne 29th. March 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Miller
