Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bates  Singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Joseph Bendall who Kept the Sign of the two Chairmen a publick house in Mill Bank in the Parish of St John the Evangelist in Westminster in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of four pounds & Continued there in Such Service for the Space of Three years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement that She this Deponent hath a Child named Mary Rice aged about two years which Child is a Bastard & illegitimate & was born in the Parish of St Giles Cripplegate London & that one David Rice late of Marybone in the County of Middx  Sawyer did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this 14th day of January 1774 before Jno Sherwood Wilmot

The Mark of Elizabeth Bates
