Middx  Thomason Thompson aged abt. 80 Years wife of Thos. Thompson aged abt. 70 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Thos. Thompson at Rumford Church in Essex about Twenty four three yrs. ago and that her sd. husband rented a house of £50 by the Year in Essex Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of middlesex lived therein about three Years & left the same abt. 10 yrs. ago since which he has not rented ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn Novr. 5th. 1753 before Barth: Hammond

Thomasin Thompson
