Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Flood aged about 52 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Campbell in Howard Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Eleven years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 13th- day of Febry. 1797 before me} J Reeves Catherine Flood
