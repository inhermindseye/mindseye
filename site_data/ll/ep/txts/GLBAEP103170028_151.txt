Middlesex ss}  Ann Ford the widow of Michael Ford deced maketh oath that her Said late husband was by Birthan Irishman & never did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in any part of Great Britain That She this Deponent previous to her marriage with her Said husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Guy a Salter in Lower Eastsmithfield in the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service for two years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent upon leaving Mr Guys Service was married to her Said late husband & that although during her Marriage She lived as a hired Servant by the year yet Since her sd husbands death She has not hired as a hired Servant for a year or done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this day of December 1776 before  Saml. Wood

The Mark of Ann Ford
