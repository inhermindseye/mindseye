Middlesex ss  Mary Davis the widow of William Davis deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband rented a house Called the Jackle Block Alehouse in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & afterwards removed from thence with this Deponent & his Daughter Hannah Davis  by a former wife to a House of the yearly Rent of Eleven pounds known by the Sign of the Horsed in the Grange Road in the parish of   Bepmondden in the  County of Surey and Continued in the occupation of Such last mentioned house for the Space of a Month & not more & died there at the end of Such month & this Deponent Continued on the occupation of Such house for near the end of the year being the Term for which Such house was taken the said Hannah Davis continuing there with this Deponent as part of her Family all that time.

Sworn this 10. day of June 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

her Mary Davis Mark
