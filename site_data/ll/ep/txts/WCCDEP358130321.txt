Middx  Eleanor Anderson aged abt. 41 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Anderson (now at Sea) says her said Husband served his Apprenticeship of seven years with Mr. Geo: Tinkler a Fellmonger in Twotland Street in the Parish of St. James in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland & never Rented Ten Pounds a yr. paid Parish Taxes or was a yearly Servt.

Sworn the 18th. day of April: 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eleanor Anderson
