Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Child aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that on the 19th day of Febry. last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Joseph Thos one William Starton a journeyman Carpenter w works with his Father in Old Gravell Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of April last past and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered And that the said William Starton is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 19th. day of March 1787 R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

her Sarah Child mark

Bond Given the 20th. April 1787 -by Father Son & They took the Child Vide Ante
