Middx The Examination of Ann Atkinson Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Singlewoman Taken the 23d. day of March 1738 Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith yt. she is Pregnant with Child or Children which when Born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she this Examint. being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when Born And further upon her Oath saith that one Geo: Trevis Taylor did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body she this Examint. at such time living with her Father Edwd. Atkinson in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr . Taylor & he the sd. Geo: Trevis at the same time hiring Journeyman with her sd. Father Edwd. Atkinson there became acquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body in a Bed Chamber in her sd. Father's House Sevll. times & at Sevll. times since at her this Eamints. Lodging at one of wch times he gott her with the Bastard Child or Children with wch she is now Pregnt. & that he the sd. Geo: Trevis & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof And this Examint. further saith that upwds. of one Year ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Plummer at the Crown & Scepter in St. Clements Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes for the space of two Years &

upwards at the Yearly Wages of- £4 - & hath not since that time Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before Rowlson

The Mark of Ann Atkinson
