Middlesex ss.  Sarah Butterfield  Singlewoman maketh Oath that about the Month of February One Thousand Seven Hundred and Ninety Six she was hired as a Servant by the Year to Mr. Pope of King Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Button Maker at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Sixteen Shillings & Six pence and continued in his Service under such hiring upwards of Two Years, that she has not since she quitted his Service lived as a hired Servant by the Year with any other Person in any Parish or Place for the space of One Year together or done any act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement and that she never was Married

Sworn this Day of Octr. 1798. before}

Sarah Butterfield at Stephen Butterfield 's No 73 Little Britain Mr Longlands Mother No 4 Dock Side her Name is now Dove
