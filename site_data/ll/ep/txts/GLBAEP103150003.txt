Middlesex ss}  Ann Ross the Wife of David Ross maketh oath That divers Years ago her said Husband lived in rented a house in Baileys Place in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate  in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her said husband has not since rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of January 1775 before B Camper

Ann Ross
