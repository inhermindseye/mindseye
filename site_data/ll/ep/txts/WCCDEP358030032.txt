Middx  Sarah Blasdell aged about 40 Years upon her Oath saith she is the Widow of Jonathan Blasdell and that she intermarried with the said Jonathan Blasdell on the 12th: Day of Octr. about Eight Years and a half since at the Fleet London Says the said Jonathan Blasdell whilst he was a Singleman & before his Intermarriage with this Examinant lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Jesiah Keen  Cornfactor in Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for five Years & a half at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds and one Year & a Quarter after his Intermarriage with this Examinant at the same Wages and quitted his said Service about Seven Years & a Quarter Says he never rented Ten pounds a Year Served any Parochial Office pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 19th. Octr. 1745 before  Fraser

The Mark of Sarah Blasdell
