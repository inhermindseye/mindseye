Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Mary Lee aged 52 years and upwds upon her Oath Saith that about 23 years ago She Intermarried with John Lee her late Husband at the Tower Chaple and who has been dead about 5 Months that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in  Mansell Street in the parish of Saint Mary Most fellow alias White Chaple in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of £38 for 10 years hath quitted said House about 13 years ago since which time her said late Husband hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Exts. knowledge Information or belief She may have gained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of March 1786 M Addington Thos: Gordon

Mary Lee
