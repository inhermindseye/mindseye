Middlesex ss  Mary Radford  widow of Portman Radford deced maketh oath That divers years ago her late Father Hugh Murto deced Rented a house in that part of Kent Street which his in the Parish of St. Mary Hewington in the County of Surry and was Charged to and paid Parish Taxes for the same and by Means there of as this Depont. is advised he gained a legal Settlem there and this Depont. was at such time Part of her Father Family & was then Married to her said late husband & that her said late Father to her knowledge or belief ever did an Act to gain a subsequent settlement And this Depont. Farther Saith that her said late husband (who was a Seafaring Ma hath informed her that he was born in the City of Exeter in the County of Devon but in what Parish there he never told her And this Depont. farthr. Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said husband never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor Rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upward nor ever served any Parish offices or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his Birth and that she has not done any a to gain a Settlement in her own right since her sd. husband Death that she was lawfully Married to her said husband at the Parish Church belongg. to the said Parish of St. Mary Hewington and that she hath a Child named Susanna aged about two years & five Months the lawful Issue of this Dep by her said late husband

Sworn this 24th day of april 1770 before -  Chris: Scott  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Mary Radford
