Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Pollett aged about 25 years now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of May 1795 she Intermarried with John Pollett her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Matilda aged about Twelve Months That her said Husband was born at Bushy in the Parish of Bushy in the County of Herts when his father Rented a large farm for several years that her said Husband never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months bound an Apprentice or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 19th, day of Janry. 1798 before us N Bond J Hunter

her Mark Mary Pollett Mark
