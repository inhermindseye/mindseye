Middx  Eliz: Waymark aged about 31 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about Sixteen Years ago she was hired to live as a yearly Servant with Mr Putland Vintner & lived wth. him at the Feathers Tavern in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 5 Years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds ten Shillings for three Years & Six pounds for the last two Years and that she has not since she left the sd Mr Putlands Service rented ten Pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr 27th 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz. Waymark
