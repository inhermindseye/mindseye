Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Foreman Aged about Twenty three years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she was bound an Apprentice to one Richard Law a glover corner of Greville Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex for Eleven years, that she hath quitted the said Service about Two years since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 22nd say of October 1799 before us} Saml. Edwards J Reeves

Her Elizh Foreman mark
