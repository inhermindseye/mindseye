Middx  The Examination of Mary Cole  Wido . of John Cole in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 5th. Day of May 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. Thirty Years ago She intermarried with her sd. late Husband Jno. Cole at the Parish of Charling in Somersettshire & further saith that She lived a Yearly hired Servt - with one Mr. Soundy in Clare Street in the sd- Parish of St. Clement Danes  Linen Draper for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & further upon her Oath saith that she hath not since that Time rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintain herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before}
