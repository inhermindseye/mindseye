Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Stone aged about 46 years Upon her Oath saith That on the 20th: day of February 1790 She was delivered of a female Bastard Child (which hath since been Christened by the Name of Elizabeth ) at the house of one Richd. Barry in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith That on the Third day of May 1793 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of one Richard Barry in Essex Street aforesaid That the said Richard Barry who lately kept a House in Essex Street aforesaid and by Trade a Taylor is the father of the said Two Children he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same there and that the said Richard Barry and no other Person did beget the said Two Children of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25th- day of July 1796 } John Collick

Elizabeth Stone
