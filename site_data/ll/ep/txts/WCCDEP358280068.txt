Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Cowden aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that her late father Ja Cowden in his life time Rented and lived in a House Water Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 7 years the yearly Rent of £9..15..0 & paid all Taxes due for same that since his decease her Mother has kept said House till this day and that this Ext. has been with her Father & Mother ever since she was born that she has done no Act in her own right to gain a Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that the 29th. day of April 1784 last past she was delivered of Male Bastard Child in her Mother House in the Street Parish afsd. which hath since been Christened by the of Thos. Henry Ward and that on the 17th. day of Novr. 17 last past she was delivered of another Male Bastard Child at her mother House afsd. in the Parish afsd. which has since been Christened by the name of Henry Ward . one Thos. Henry Ward a Journeyman Taylor who la at this Exts. Mothers House and work for a Mr. Triad Taylor in Arrundel Street is the father of the sd. 2 Children he ha had Carnal knowledge of the Exts. Body for these years last past at sevl. times and begotten the said Children thereon And that the said Thos. Henry Ward the only real and true father of the said Two Child

Sworn this 15th. day of Febry. 1787 before me} R Butler

Ann Cowden

Man taken he paid £10 Release Exted
