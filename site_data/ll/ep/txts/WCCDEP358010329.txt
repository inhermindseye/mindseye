Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Wood aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Clarke at old Bedland in the Parish of St. Botolph Bishopgate in the City of London for Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds that she hath quitted said service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of March 1790 D Walker Philip Dyot

her Sarah Wood Mark
