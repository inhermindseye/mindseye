St Clement Danes  12th Novr 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Sepulchres with Newgate London  to wit Margaret Anthoney aged 55 years or thereabouts Widow Upon Oath saith that she was Married at the parish Church of St Margaret Westminster to Thomas Anthoney in or about the Month of February thirty three Years ago (by Licence) that her said Husband subsequent thereto Rented & Resided in a House in Giltspur Street in the parish of St Sepulchre without Newgate London that for about five years at the annual Rent Ten pounds that they quitted the same about ten fifteen Years ago & her said Husband died abroad about four years after never having obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that She hath never since been been legally Married so as to be entitled to any settlement or acquired any legal Settlement in her own Right that all her Children are provided for & settled th but that she this Examinant is distressed and unable to provide for herself

Sworn the 12th day of November 1793 before us } Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Margt Anthoney's Mark

Witness Garrett
