Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit, Francis Bundy of John Street Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of Pancras in the County of Middlesex upon his Oath saith that he was married to Sarah his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan's West in the City of London about two Years ago, and that his said Wife died about four three Months ago, and this Deponent further saith That he has two Children by his said Wife vizt. Ann aged one Year and ten Months or thereabouts and Francis aged three Months or thereabouts who were both of then born in Lawful Wedlock and that his said Wife being dead & he this Examinant being out of Place & Poor he is unable to provide for & Maintain his said two Children who are now with the Person who has Nursed them for since time past in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster and Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And this Examinant further on his Oath saith that before his said Intermarriage with his said late wife and whilst he this Examinant was

was a singleman not having any Child or Children he lived as an hired Yearly Servant with his Grace the late Duke of Northumberland in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields for upwards of two Years & that since he quitted the said Dukes Service he has not lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year in any Places rented any House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds paid Taxes, served any Parish Office or done any other Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information & belief he may have acquired any subsequent or other Settlement

Sworn this 14th. Janry 1791 before Sampn Wright W Addington

Francis Bundy
