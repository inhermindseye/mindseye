Middx  John Raylstun upon his Oath saith that by Indre dated the 4th. Day of Janry 1722 he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Five Yrs. Apprentispp with Mr. Wm. Carr in Cary Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Peruke maker & lived with the Sd. Mr. Carr as a Journeyman after he was out of his Sd. Apprentisp for some Time that since he left the Sd. Mr. Carr he hath not served any Parochial Office rented Ten Pounds a Year lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 22d. of March 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

John Raylstun
