Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Jenkins aged about 61 years upon his Oath saith That he never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That his father informed him that he was born in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 1st- day of April 1797 before me} J Hunter

Wm Jenkins
