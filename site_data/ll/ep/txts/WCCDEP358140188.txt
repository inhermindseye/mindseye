Middx.  Ann Griffiths aged abt. 70 Years upon Oath says she is ye. widow of Thos. Griffiths who has been dead abt. Sixteen Years says her said husband Rented a house in Bennetts Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds for the first Year and Ten Pounds a Year for the other 2 Years and after he quitted said house never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes; says she hath not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 2d. of Decr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Griffiths
