Middlesex ss  Catherine Jones  Single woman Maketh oath That She lately was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Mr Gooding a Hair Dresser in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the City & Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex at the yearly wages of Six Pounds and Continued in such Service for the Space of a year and upwards & by means thereof as she is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that she hath not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor was she ever Married nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th day of February 1770 before  Chris: Scott  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Catherine Jones
