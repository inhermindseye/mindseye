Middx & Westmr. to wit Elizth. Lissiman Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly with one Thos. Lewis  Esqr . in Cooks Court Carey Street for Several Years last Past at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Ten shillings hath quitted her said Service about four Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same done Any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Oath Says that on Saturday the Eighteenth day of June East she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Work house belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which has since been baptized by the Name of Elizabeth and is become Chargable to the same she being Poor and unable to maintain the said Child Says that one Edward Boden late a Coachman with Mr. Lewis ( & who lately lived with one Mr. Lee a Bauker in Lombard Street but where the said Edward Boden now lives she does not know) is the Father thereof the having had Carnal knowledge of her Body on or about the fifteenth of Septr. last at her Masters Lodgings in Cooks Court aforesaid and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Edward Boden and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 19th. day of July 1774 before  William Addington .

Elizabeth Lissiman
