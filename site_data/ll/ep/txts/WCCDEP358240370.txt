St Clement Danes 7th Janry 1794

Middlesex & Westminster }  St. Giles in the fields  to wit Margaret Acton  Widow aged 5o Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to John Acton at the parish Church of St. Ann Westminster several Years ago that who is since Dead that subsequent to his Death she this Examinant being a Widow unmarried Rented & Resided in a House in Little Wild Street in the parish of St Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for about two years at £3 £28 per Annum Rent that she quitted the Same about 15 or 16 Years ago And hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 7th day of January 1794 before us Rich.. Ford B: Kennett

Margt. Actton

Removed by Mr Newman
