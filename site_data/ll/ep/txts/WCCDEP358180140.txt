Middx  Ann Monkton  Wido . upon her Oath saith that abt. 1 Yr. since she lived in & rented a House in Hollis Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £20. & lived in the same 9 Yrs. & upwds. being a Wido. during all that time that since she left the sd. House she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife & that she is now poor & not able to maintain herself

Sworne 10th. Decr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

Ann Monnckton
