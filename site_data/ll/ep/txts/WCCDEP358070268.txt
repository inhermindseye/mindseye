Westmr to wit  Ann Anthony Aged about Twenty two Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly servant for one Year Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint George Botolph Lane London to one Mrs. Bishop at a Chandlers Shop in Beer Lane Tower Street in the Parish of Alhallows Barking in the said City of London , and Served five Years, part of her said Apprenticeship and then left her said Mistress, Since which time she has never done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 14th day of June 1775 before R. Buller M Addington

her Ann Anthony Mark Witness James Viger
