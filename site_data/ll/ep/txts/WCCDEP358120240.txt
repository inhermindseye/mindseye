Westmr.  Cathn: Sleth upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Abraham Sleth to whom she was married at Stepney Church abt. 40 Years ago and that her sd. late husband lived in & rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten pounds in Trinity Lane in Trinity parish Londn . for the Space of One Year & upwards & has not since he quitted the sd. house nor has she since his Decease rented ten pounds by the year pd parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Decr. 10th 1754 before}

The Mark of Cath Sleth
