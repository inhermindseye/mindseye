Middx  Elizabeth Davis aged 55 Years Removed from the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn London upon Oath saith she is the Widow of George Davis who has been Dead about Seven Years to whom she was Married at the Fleet 22 Years ago That her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Sanger a Bookseller in New Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which expired about 42 Years since as she hath been informed and believes and never after Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or was a hired Yearly Servant to her knowledge nor hath she this Examinant since his Death

Sworn the 7th.day of April 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Elizth. Davis
