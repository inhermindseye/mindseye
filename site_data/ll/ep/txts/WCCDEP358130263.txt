Middx.  Sarah Ray aged abt. 40 Years upon Oath says She is the Wife of Joshua Ray to whom She was married at ye. Fleet London abt. Twenty four Years since, Says her sd. Husband now Rents & lived in a house of Ten Pounds a Year & upwards in Devonshire Street in the Parish of St. George the Martyr in the County of Middx. in which he carries on the Business of a Staymaker

Sworn Janry 8th- 1756 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Sarah Ray
