Middx to wit Catherine Hopkins aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of James Hopkins ( Pressed and gone to Sea ) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Saviour Vulg St. Mary Overees in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry about Thirteen Years ago and by whom she hath Three Children Namely Samuel aged about Six Years Matthew aged upwards of three Years and George aged about Ten Months says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. John Reynolds a Carpenter on the Bankside in the Borough of Southwark in the said County of Surry and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or otherwise Howsoever

Sworn this 29th. day of July 1779 before}

her Cathr Hopkins Mark
