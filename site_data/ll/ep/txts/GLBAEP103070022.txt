Middx ss  James Powell Son of Thomas Powell deced Maketh oath that his father hath often informed him that about fifty Six Years ago his sd. father Rented a House of a Ten Pounds a Year in ye Parish of St. Anne Westminster in the County of Middx And by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in that Parish And this Deponent Saith that he this Deponent hath a Son now living a Married Man named James Powell whose wifes Name is Elizabeth And Saith that he this Deponent & his sd Son James Powell in right of this Deponts. sd father Thomas Powell are legally Settled in ye sd. Parish of St. Anne Westmr. he this Dept. Nor his sd. Son James having ever done any Act Whereby to acquire any other Settlemt ye as aforesd.

Sworn this 17 Day of January 1750 before us  Rd. Ricards  Saml Gower

James Powell
