Middx  Mary Elston upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was never married & that she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on the 15th, Day of March last as yett unbaptized now living & become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same Says that Jno. Delafont of the Inner Temple London Esqr . did begett the Sd. female Bastard Child on her Body the sd. Jno. Delafont having Carnall knowledge of her Body on the 16th: Day of June last & 23d: of August following at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. & that no other person has either before nor since that time had  have of her ods & that he the sd. John Delafont & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworn 2d. April 1746 before  Samuel Clarke  Wm. Jones

Mary Elston
