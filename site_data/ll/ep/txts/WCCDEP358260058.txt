Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Stanfield aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that about Six years ago she Intermarried with John Stanfield her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn by whom she hath one Child living vizt. John aged about Sixteen Months That her said Husband never gained any Settlement in her own right That his father Titus Stanfield at this time Rents a House in the Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster  and has done so for these five years past at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that her said Husband left her about Ten Months ago and refuses to maintain this Examinant and her said Child

Sworn this 20th. day of June 1795 } J Hind

her Elizth. Stanfield mark

Witness B Newman
