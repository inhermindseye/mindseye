Middlesex ss}  Ann Cavener  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by & with Benjamin Levy of Colchester Street in the Parish of St Mary White Chapel in the Sd County Diamond Polisher & Continued then in Such Service under such hiring for the space of fifteen Months That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year of elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24 day of October 1785 before J. Hawley R Davies

The Mark of Ann Cavener
