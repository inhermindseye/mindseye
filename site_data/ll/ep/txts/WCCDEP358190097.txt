Middx.  Mary Wisdom aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath says she was Married to James Wisdom her husband at the fleet London about Ten Years ago says her said husband is now abroad & beyond the Seas and that he Rented a house in Pepper Alley in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry known by the sign of the Watermans Arms of the Yearly Rent of £20 lived therein abt. four Years and hath Quitted same abt. Seven Years And has not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn February Ye 17th: 1761 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Wisdom

Upon Mr Ponten's Cornplt. J :P:
