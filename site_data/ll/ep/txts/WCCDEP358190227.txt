Middx   Ann Davis aged about 60 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Giles Davis who has been dead about 20 Years Says that since the Death of her said Husband she rented a House of £16 a year the Back of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and lived therein about Eighteen Months Says she has quitted the Same about Seven Years and hath not since rented any House paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 26th. day of Febry 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Davis
