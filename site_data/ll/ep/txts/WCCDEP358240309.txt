St Clement Danes August 22d 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Mary Soane  singlewoman aged thirty years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs Sage in Browns Buildings in Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex for two years and seven Months at the Annual Wages of Five Guineas that She quitted that Service about fourteen years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 23d day of August 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Soane 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
