Middx  Agnes Knapton aged abt. 51 Years upon Oath says She never was Married & that She lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Osborn a Wheelwright in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds for the first Year & six Pounds for the second Year & hath quitted her said Service about seven Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 24th Day of Sepr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Alice Knapton
