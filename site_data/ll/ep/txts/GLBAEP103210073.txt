Middlesex ss}  William Darby maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a yearly hired servant by & with John Wilson in Clements Lane in Clare Market in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Sd County look at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of four years & has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 11. day of March 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Wm Darby
