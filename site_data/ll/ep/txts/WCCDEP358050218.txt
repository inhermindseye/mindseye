Westmr. 

Rachel Herbert aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that her father John Herbert deced was by Indenture dated the 2d. Febry 1686 bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr Layer in the Parish of Great Marlow in the County of Bucks Basket Maker as appears by the Exacon of Alice Herbert her mother also deced taken upon Oath the 31st. Day of Augt. 1736 before Tho Lane & Peter Guerin Esqrs. two of his Majestys Justices of the peace for the Said County of Middx and further saith that her said father never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. and that she this Examinant further saith when she was about Ten Years of age she was plat to one Mrs. Clare Bates in the Parish of

St Clement Danes Middx to learn Plain work with whom she continued three Years that her father during that time Provided her with Cloaths dyet washing and Lodging at his room in Essex Street in the sd. Parish of St Clemt Danes , that her Sd. Mistress had only her Labour for her teaching and this Examinant verily beleives no Indenture of Apprentiship was made between them that she this Examint. never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt for one Year except in the Temple (an extra parochial place) or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 7th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Rachel Herbart
