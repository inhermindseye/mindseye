Middx  Sarah Martin upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman and was never married and that she now liveth a Yearly hired Servt . at the Goat in Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & hath so done for the Space of 2 Yrs. & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards and likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and not able to maintaine the same when born & that one Haren Hare a Taylor did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body she the sd. Haren Hare lodging in the same house where this Examinant is Servant where he became acquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body in the latter End of May or the Beginning of June last and at sevll. other Times since at one of which Times he got her with the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that he the sd. Haren Hare and no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 10th. of Octr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Sarah Martin
