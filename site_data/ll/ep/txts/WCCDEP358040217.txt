Middx 

Ann Phillips aged upwds: of 29 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Fendall in Little Shair Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Libty of Westmr , and & qunty of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for One Year & upwards and quitted her sd. Service abt. upwards of four Years ago since which time she has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 16th. 1748 before} Samuel Clarke

The Mark of Ann Philips
