St. Clement Danes April 4th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James Westm  (to wit) Elizabeth Harwood  Singlewoman aged 31 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Brown Staymaker in Jermyn Street in the parish of St James within the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for a year and three quarters at the Annual Wages of £5:5s:0d that she quitted that service about Eleven years ago and hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together Rented any House or Apartment of Ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 4th. day of April 1793 before Us}

Elizth Harwood 's Mark

Witness Garrett
