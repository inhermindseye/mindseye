Middlesex ss}  Joseph Heriott maketh oath That divers Years ago he Rented a House of the Yearly Red of Twenty pounds in  Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 9th day of November 1775 before}
