Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Richardson Aged about 70 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Richardson by whom she hath no Children now living says her said late husband rented and lived in a House in Warwick Street the Corner of Leicester Street in the Parish of St James's Westmr . in the City and Liberty of Westmr . for one Year and a Quarter at the yearly Rent of £25 and hath quitted the same upwards of 40 Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlemt. either by renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 1st. Day of Novr. 1776  John Cox . James Croft

her Ann Richardson Mark
