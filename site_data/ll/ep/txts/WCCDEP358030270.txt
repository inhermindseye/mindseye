Westmr.  Ann Myrick aged upwards of 29 Years upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Robt. Grayham for the Space of one Year upwds. and lived the last Ten Months of his sd Service in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds and hath left her said Service about Eighteen months Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Grayhams Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 3d. 1746 before}
