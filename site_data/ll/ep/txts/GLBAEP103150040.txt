Middlesex ss}  Ann Hudson the Wife of Richard Hudson of the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Scowerer maketh oath That about Seven Months ago one Ann Singleton Came to this Deponents husbands house in the said Parish and brought with her there a Child named George Scholar now of the Age of Three Years & five Months to be taken Care of by this Depont & boarded & lodged by this Deponts said husband at the Rate of Two Shillings per Week That the said Child as this Depont has been informed by the said Ann Singleton & Verily believes the same to be true was born of her the said Ann Singleton s body on Pennington Street in the Parish of St George in the said County & is a Bastard & that one George Scholar was & is the Father of the Said Child She the Said Ann Singleton never having been Married to him or any other person That by an order of Removal under the hands & Seals of Burford Camper & John Sherwood  Esqrs . two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the Sd County of Middlesex  bearing date the Twenty Second of May 1772 the said Ann Singleton was removed from the Sd Parish of St. George to the Precinct of St Catherine in the Sd County as to the place of her last legal Settlement together with the Said Bastard Child for Nurture That the said Ann Singleton made and paid to this Deponts. Sd husband the respective Weekly paymt for the Nurture & Maintenance of the said Child till within about Seven Weeks last past When She the said Ann Singleton Ceased & forbore & Still Ceases & forbears to make any further payments or allowance to this Deponts Sd husband for the Nurture & Maintenance of the Said Child That the Said Ann Singleton  is Since gone away to places unknown to this Depont. She never having discovered to this Depont. or her husband or any other Person to this Depont. knowledge or belief where She is gone to nor doth this Depont know Where She is or Can be found That She hath left the Sd Child behind her & thereby as this Depont believes Voluntarily deserted him & that the said Child by means of such desertion is likely to be come Chargeable to the Sd parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate

Sworn this 21 day of June 1775 before us B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Hudson
