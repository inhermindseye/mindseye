Westmr:  Martha Chittum upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Alexr. Chittum to whom she was married as Stoke Church in Hampshire about 8 or 9 Years ago and that her sd husband Served Seven Years apprentiship with Mr Saml. Cobley a Butcher in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn 23d. Decr 1754 before}
