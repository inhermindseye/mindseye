Middlesex to wit John Charley Aged about fifty Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Magdalen London abo about a Year ago by whom he hath no Child living Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Jeffson a Printer in Pye Corner in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year before marriage.

Sworn this 27th. day of Octr. 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner  John Croft

John Charley
