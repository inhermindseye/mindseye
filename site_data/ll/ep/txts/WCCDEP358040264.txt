Westmr. 

Sarah Craft aged about 28 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound an Apprentice to and Served Four Years Apprentiship with Mrs. Bradley a Milk woman in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and after the Expiration of her sd. Apprent lived as a Yearly Servt. with the sd Mrs. Bradley at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds for one Year or thereabouts Says she has not since she quitted the sd. Mrs. Bradleys Service rented ten pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn & Octr. 1st. 1748 before} Wm: Jones
