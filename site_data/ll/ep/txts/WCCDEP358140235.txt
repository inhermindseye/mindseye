Middx  Wm. Lake aged abt. 56 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Elizth . his Wife at Lincolns Inn Chapel on the 30th. day of July 1729. says he Rented a house in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds  lived therein abt. Two Years and hath quitted same abt. Two Years: since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 2d: day of March 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

Wm Lake
