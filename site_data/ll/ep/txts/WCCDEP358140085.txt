Middx  Eliz: Weston aged abt. 32 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Wm. Weston (now at Sea) with whom she intermarried on the Parish Church of St. George in the East in the County of Middx. abt. Seventeen Years ago says her said husband before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Single Man lived a hired Year -ly Servant with one Mr. Warren a Butcher in St. James's Market in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. for three Years or there abouts & lay during all the said term of three Years at the house of the said Mr. Warren in Jermyn Street in the said Parish of St. James & here after he quitted said Service Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes. says she has two Childn. by her said husband (to wit) John aged abt. three Years & Elizth . aged abt. fourteen Months & she being poor is not able to maintain them

Sworn this 15th. of Febry 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Weston
