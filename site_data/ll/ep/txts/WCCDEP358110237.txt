Westmr.  Ruth Hart aged abt. 27 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she is the wido. of Robt Hart who never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. nor ever rented ten Pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes and that her sd late husbands father Saml Hart decd. rented a house in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £10. and lived therein several Years as this Examinant has been informed & verily believes that she has two Children by her sd late husband Thomas aged abt. 3 years & Jane aged abt. 14 Months & being Poor is unable to maintain them without Assistance

Sworn 14th. April 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ruth Hart
