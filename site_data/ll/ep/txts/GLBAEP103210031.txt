Middlesex ss  Ann Hardman the widow of John Hardman deced maketh oath that about nine years ago her Said late husband took & rented a house Called the Hand Tavern in Bermondsey Street in the Parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry at the yearly rent of forty pounds as She this Deponent now best recollects or remembers & resided thereon for the Space of a Quarter of a year & paid rent for the Same That her Sd husband did not afterwards rent a house or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of ten Pounds or Serve any Parish Office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that She was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn this 29 day of Novemr 1784 before J. Hawley Ann Hardman
