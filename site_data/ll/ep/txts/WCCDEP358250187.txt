Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Lane aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Pinder in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of Eight pounds That she hath quitted said Service about Eleven Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 16th. day of July 1795 } M Addington

her Elizth. Lane mark

Witness Jas Talboys
