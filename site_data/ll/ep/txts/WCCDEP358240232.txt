Militia Woman her Examination entered in a Book kept for the purpose of entering Militia Men's Wives Examns

St.. Clement Danes March 27th 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  All Saints Bristol  (to wit) Sarah Spalding aged 28 years or thereabouts upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband Richard Spalding at the parish Church of Claines in the County of Worcester on the 18th day of May 1785 that She hath two Children by her said Husband now living namely Ann born on the 21st. day of February 1790 and Elizabeth born on the 4th day of July 1791 and is now pregnant with a third And this Examt. further saith that her said Husband while single & unmarried having no legitimate Child or Children lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Davis broad Street in the parish of All Saints in the City of Worcester for about six years and left that Service about the year 1783 as she hath been informed & believes and hath not Since lived as An hired yearly Servant for a year together Rented Any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlemt. to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief And this Deponent saith that her said Husband is now a substitute in the Middlesex Militia for Robert Bradley of the parish of Saint George in the Liberty of Westminster as she is informed and verily believes

Sworn the 27th day of March 1793 before Me Wm Kitchiner }

Sarah Spalding 's Mark
