Middx  Eliz Riley upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Tho. Riley & has not since her sd. late husband decease rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr Waley a Gunlock maker in the parish of Bilson in the County of Stafford & never gained a Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 25th. Febry. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz Riley
