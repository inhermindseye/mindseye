Middlesex ss  Jane Hutchinson  Singlewoman maketh oath That about Fifty years ago she was born in Fullers Court in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex at which time her late Father George Hutchinson deced. as he has informed her & as she believes rented a house then & was Charged to & paid the Scavengers Tax for such house & by Means thereof as she is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge or belief her Said Father never did any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere And that she was never Married nor ever was An Apprentice Nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this day of January 1770 before}
