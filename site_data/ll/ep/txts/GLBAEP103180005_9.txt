Middlesex ss}  Mary Demson the wife of John Demson maketh oath That her said husband who is now absent from her in the East Indies never did any Act to gain a Settlement in Great Britain & was by Birth a Foreigner That She this Deponent previous to her Marriage was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages By & with one Mr Davis an Eastman in Court Street in the parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the said County & Continued in Such Service for about the Space of one year & half & by means thereof gained a Legal Settlement there & did not afterwards live as a hired Servant for the Space of year elsewhere or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Save What She has acquired by her marriage as aforesaid

Sworn this 15th day of December 1777 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Mary Demson
