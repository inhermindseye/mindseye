Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Welsh aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born in Ireland Ad further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 23d. day of September 1793 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Ryley No. 39 Wych street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the Name of Maria That one Robert Fox who is now confined in Newgate the New Gaol in the Borough is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants and begotten the same thereon and that he the said Robert Fox and no other person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 4th. day of July 1795 } J Hind

Mary Welsh

Father in the Borough Goal for buying Stolen Goods

Childs name Maria Welsh Admitted in the House 3d. July 1795 Disd. 13th. July 1795 Qy. what become of her afterwards.

Man never taken
