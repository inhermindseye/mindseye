Middx  Mary Goding  Wido . of George Goding upon her Oath saith that on the 5th. Day of March 1726 she intermarried with her said late Husband George Goding at the Fleet  London that he hath been Dead about 3 Years that about 12 Years ago he lived in & rented a House in Fleet street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West  London of the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds for the Space of Eleven Months or thereabts. that he never after in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that She hath one Child viz: Jno . aged abt. 5 Yrs. & being Poor is not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworne the 26th. April 1742 & before} Tho. Lane  Jas: Gronous

Mary Goding
