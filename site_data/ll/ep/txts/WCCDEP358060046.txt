Middx  John Castle upon Oath saith he rented a house of £14 by the Year in Half moon Alley near Bartholemew Close in the parish of St. Bartholemew the Great in the London and lived therein about 9 months and has left the same about 18 Years Says he has not since he Quitted the sd. house rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 8th. 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane

John Castell
