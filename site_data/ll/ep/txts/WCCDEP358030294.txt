Westmr.  Henry Cashedy upon Oath saith he was born in the parish of Killough in the County of Longford in the Kingdom of Ireland and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 24th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Henry Cashedy
