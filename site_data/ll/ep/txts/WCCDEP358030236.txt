Middx  Eleanr. Dearing aged about 55 Years upon Oath says she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Garvey at the Horseshoe & Magpye in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for the Space of two Years and upwards and hath left her sd. Service about Seven Years Says she hath not since She left the sd. Mr. Garveys Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworne Octr 4th: 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Eleanr. Dearing
