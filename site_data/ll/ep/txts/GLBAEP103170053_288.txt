Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Goodeave  widow maketh oath That divers years ago She truly & bonafide took & rented a house at the yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds in Dean Street in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & resided thereon for at least the Space of Ten Weeks & by means thereof as she is advised gained a legal Settlement there That she has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or forever any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7 day of February 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Goodeave
