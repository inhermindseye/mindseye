Westmr.  Martha Gilson aged 35 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived yearly Servant wth. Francis Williams at the White Lyon in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds quitted her sd. Service abt Years ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the Yr. paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn June 25th 1754 before}
