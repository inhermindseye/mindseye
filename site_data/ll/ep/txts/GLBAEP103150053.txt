Middlesex ss}  Hester Bowen the Widow of James Bowen deced maketh oath that divers Years ago her Said late husband lived in rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Three Pounds & ten Shillings in Sun Yard in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards is any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor hath she this Depont Since his Death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself in any other Place

Sworn this 19th. day of August 1775 before B Camper

The mark of Hester Bowen
