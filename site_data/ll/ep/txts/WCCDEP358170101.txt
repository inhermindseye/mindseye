Middx  Mary Faithfull  Wido . of Jno. Faithfull upon her Oath saith that her sd. late Husband hath been Dead abt. Eight Years & that he served Two Years & half the latter Part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. Chas. Scriven in Veare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes  Glazier & further saith that abt. Two Years & a half ago she rented a House in Kings Head Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of Three Months or thereabts. & hath not since she left the sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworn the 1st- Day of August 1741 before}

The mark of Mary Faithfull
