Middx  Solomon Allyson aged abt. 37 years Upon Oath says he was Married to Rebecca his wife at St. Geo: Bloomsbury abt. Six years ago by whom he has One Child named Edward aged abt. two Years. Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Hollister a Taylor in Serle Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for One year

Sworn this 26th. day of Feby. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

Solomon Allyson
