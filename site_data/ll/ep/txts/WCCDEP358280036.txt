Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Forrest  Widow Aged 29 Yrs ar thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Forrest her late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Mary Le Strand in the County of Middlesex in or about the Month of Novr. 1777 that by whom she have One Child living Named John aged about 5 Yrs whom this Examinant supports that her said Husband never to the best of this Ext. knowledge or belief gained any Settlement in England Saith that she was born at Bros in Shropshire where her father and Mother rented a House and lived for some years her said Husband died on the 9th. of Novr. 1782 And that she this Examinant hath done any Act Matter or thing either before her Intermarriage or since her said Husbands Death whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained any Settlement in her own right And further this Examinant on her Voluntary Oath saith that on the 4th. day Septr . Instant she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Mc.Pher

in Robinhood Court Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Elizabeth , That one Archibald Mc. Donald a Journeyman Cabinett Maker who lately lodged in Robinhood Court is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Ext. Body and begotten the same thereon And that the said Archibald Mr. Donald and no one else did beget the said Child and that he is the only Real and true father thereof And that she is Poor and unable to maintain the same

Sworn this 22d. day of Septr. 1786 before}

her Sarah Forrest Mark

Bond filled up & Exted she took the Child and the father pays her
