Wetmr. to wit Mary Storey Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. James Brooks a Coal Merchant in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service in May last and hath not sine she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant of One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that one the Twelfth day of January last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Peakin Parish Nurse in Saint Clement Lane in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of Mary Says that one Mark Thomas late a Footman to one Mr. Shea an Irish Gentleman who lodged at her said Master House had Carnal Knowledge of her Body on Tuesday the 21th. day of April last in the House of the said Mr. Brooks and at Several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Mark Thomas and no other Person did beget the same and that he his the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 15th. day of February 1773 before me} 

her Mary Storey Mark
