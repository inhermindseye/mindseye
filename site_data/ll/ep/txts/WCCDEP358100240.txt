Middx to wit Elizth. Oldridge Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Jones a Clark in the Bank who lodged at Mr. Adams the Corner of Butcher shall Lane in the parish of Christ Church in the City of London for Two Years the first Year at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds & the last Year at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Years since which time she never lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year and further on her Oath Says that on the fifth day of July last she was delived of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Tasker in little Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . which is intended to be Baptized George and is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further says that one Henry Bennett a Journeyman Bricklayer who lately lived with his Father at Wellington in the County of Somerset is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings at Mr. Burnetts the Fountain in Wych Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes sometime in the Month of Septr. last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he get her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesd. And that the said Henry Bennett & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Augt. 1784 before  Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Oldridge
