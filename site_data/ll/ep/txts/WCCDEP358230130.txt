Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Sarah Hitton who is Ill maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said Sarah Hitton intermarried with her late Husband about 36 years ago at Banbury Oxfordshire That her said late Husband rented and lived in a House in Wellclose Square in the Parish of Saint George in the East in the County of Middlesex for 7 Years at the Yearly rent of £24 That he hath left the said House about 28 years since which time he hath not rented any house tenement on Lodging of ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 10 day of April 1800 before us} Ws Woodenough John Collick

James Talboys
