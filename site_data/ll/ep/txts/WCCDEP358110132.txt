Westmr.  John Forster upon his Oath saith that about twenty Years ago he lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr Holmes a Farmer in the parish of Skefflin in the County of York for one year at the yearly wages of £4.10.0 and was all the time of his sd. Service a Singleman & that he hath not since he left the sd Mr Holmes Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Decr. 1st. 1752 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Jno. Forster
