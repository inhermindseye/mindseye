Middx  Mary Waller aged about 18 years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she is the Daughter of Wm. Waller deced by Ann his Wife that neither herself nor her sd. late father ever rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year and that her late Grandfather Christr. Waller rented a house & pd. Sest & Lot in Catrick parish near Richmond in Yorkshire and there dyed

Sworn Octr 15th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Waller
