Middx  Mary Goodman aged abt. 50 Yrs. removed from the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldersgate by an Order under the hands & Seales of two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the City of London dated the 12th. Day of this Instant Novr. upon her Oath saith she intermarried wth. Wm. Goodman her late Husbd. on or abt. the 26th. Day of August 1730 at the Parish Church of St. Martin in ye Feilds Says her sd. late Husbd. hath been dead abt. Two Years Says he was by Indre dated the 19th. Day of April 1725 bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mr. Tarra Butcher in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Says the sd. Wm. Goodman in his Life never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. with any Person for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt: in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since his Death rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. with any person for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other

Place to her knowledge or beleif & is sick poor & at present not capable of providing for herself

Sworne Novr 13th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Mary Goodman
