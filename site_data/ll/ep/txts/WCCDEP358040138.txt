Westmr.  Samuel Stedman upon Oath saith he never was married and that he lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Wood at the Yearly Wages of £10 for one Years & upwds. Says the last time he came to town with the sd. Mrs. Wood she lodged in the house of Mrs. Shaw in Arundel Street in the Strand but this Examinant all the time his Mistress continued in town which was about three months Say at the one Bell Inn near the New Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Mary Le' Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. at which Inn his Houses stood Says he went with his mistress (when she quitted Mrs. Shaws Lodging) to Littleton in Middlesex and was discharged from his sd. Service about three weeks after their arrival there Says he has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 19th 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

Samuel Stedman
