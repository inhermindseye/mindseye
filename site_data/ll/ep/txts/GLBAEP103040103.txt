Middlesex ss.  Abraham Hunt maketh Oath that about Thirty Four Years ago he Rented a Publick House called or known by the sign of the Ship in the Butcher Row Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , that he paid Sixteen Pounds a year Rent for the said House and lived in the same about Five Years, and was charged to and paid the Poors Rate there, that he has not since he quitted the said House, Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of 1798 before}

Mr. Hunt is charged on the Poor Rate for the Year 1764. Feb. 28
