Saint Clement Danes . Octr. 10th. 1792.

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Martin Ludgate to wit Elizabeth Braffett aged thirty Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to William Braffett her present Husband at the parish Church of Saint Dunstan the West in the City of London about the Month of April 1787 That her said Husband was bound an Apprentice  to Thomas Hathing in Creed Lane London Bookbinder and afterwar assigned to Ann Waller of Prujean Square in the Old Bailey in the Parish of Saint Martin Ludgate in the City of London with whom he served about two years and an half the Remainder of his Apprenticeship That since her said Husband has been out of his Apprenticsh he hath not to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information or belief done any act wherby to acquire a Subsequent settlement and this Examinant for the Saith that for Oath one Child non living namely George Braffett aged seven Months thereabouts and that her said Husband is now in Clerkenwell New Prison and she this Examinant is in  Distress and unable to Maintain herself and her said Child 

Sworn this 10th Day of October 1792 before us Sampn Wright M Addington

her Elizth. Braffett Mark
