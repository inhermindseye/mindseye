Middx.  Thos. Gibbons On the Behalf of his Sister Susannah Jones aged upwards of thirty Years upon Oath says his sd. Sister Never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Morris in Newport Alley in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. for fifteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. Six Years; and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as his said Sister has informed him and as he Verily believes

Sworn the 30th. of Decr. 1758 before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

Thomas Gibbons

Upon the Complaint of. Mr. Thos Matravers Ovr. J: P:
