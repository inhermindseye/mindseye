Middx  The Examination of Alice Scott  Widw . aged 63 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex taken the 3d. Day of May 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that upwards of 36 Years ago her late Husband Hen: Scott Served Six Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Fletcher Tripem in Clements Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes And further upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband in the life time nor she this Examint. since his Death never Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she is Poor and Old & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Alice Scott
