Middx  Jane Slawter aged about 28 Years a Vagrant taken out of the Street in Labour the 24th: Day of May last in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman & was on the said 24th: Day of May last delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes & is become Chargeable to the same Parish Says she was by Indenture Dated the 20th. Sept. 1729 bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Oversrs of the Poor of the Parish of St. Sepulchers in the City of London to one Saml: Rawson a Glover the Corner of Parkers Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the sd. County of Middlesex with whom this Exaiant lived about four Years & a half & then run away from her sd. Master Says she never was Assigned over to any other person rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. many other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne June 14th. 1745 before} Tho. Lane

The mark of Jane Slawter
