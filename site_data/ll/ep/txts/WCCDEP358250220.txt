Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit John Ridley aged about 41 years upon his Oath saith that about four years ago he Intermarried with Catherine his present wife at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath no Child or Children living. That previous to his Intermarriage and while he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Barnard Gill at Kingswood in the Parish of Kingswood in the County of Savery for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds Five Shillings that he hath quitted said Service about Twenty one years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of Janry. 1796 } J Hind Rupt: Clarke

his John Ridley Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins .
