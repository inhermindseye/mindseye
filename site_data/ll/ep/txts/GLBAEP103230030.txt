Middlesex ss}  Martha Clark the wife of John Clark maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her at Sea That her said husband lately Rented & lived in a house at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas in Virginia Street in the Parish of Saint George in the said county and resided thereon for the Space of three Months and upwards That he has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a child named Mary aged one year the lawful issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 20. day of March 1787 before  R Davies  Rob Smith

The Mark of Martha Clark
