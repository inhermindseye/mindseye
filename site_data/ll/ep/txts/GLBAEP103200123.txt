Middlesex ss}  Mary Fellows  Singlewoman maketh oath that Several years ago she was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Hugh Jones a Victualler in Cock Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one year & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servant in any one Place elsewhere for the Space of a year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent hath four Children namely Henry Ireland aged about Six years Daniel Ireland aged about four years & five months & Eleanor Ireland aged about two years & Seven Months all which sd three Children were born of this Deponts body in Crown Court in the sd Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate and John Ireland aged about ten Months which last mentioned Child was born of her body in Bluecoat Fields in the Parish of St Paul Shadwell in the sd County & baptized in the Church there The which four Children are bastard by reason She was never Married to their Father John Ireland who is now absent from her in Places to her altogethr unknown

Sworn this. 6. day of April 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Mary Fellows
