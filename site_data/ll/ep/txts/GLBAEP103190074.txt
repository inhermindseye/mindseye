Middlesex ss}  Margaret Thompson the wife of William Thompson maketh oath that her sd husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with one William Smith a Taylor in the Parish of Saint James in the City of Bristol & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband.

Sworn this. 12. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Margaret Thompson
