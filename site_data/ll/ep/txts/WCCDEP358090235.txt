Middx to wit Jane Jones Aged about Forty five years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she Lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Salisbury a Taylor in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for 5 years at the yearly Wages of seven Pounds hath quitted her said service about Four years ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement

Sworn this 22nd. day of June 1781 before R Butler

Jane Jones  her mark
