St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St. Andrew Holborn above the Barrs GG}  G G.  (to wit) Ann Granville Aged 44 years or thereabouts Upon her oath saith that she never was Married that she Rented and Resided in an Apartment in Bedford Street  Bedford Row in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for about Seven Months at and under the yearly Rent of £10 for the first Six Months & the Weekly Rent of 4 for the remaining Month that she quitted the same to the best of the her Remembrance about Christmas 1788 and since hath not Rented any House or Lodging of £10 a year, lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve Month together nor done any other Act or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And that she is now Ill & unable to support herself

Sworn the 16th day of September 1791 before us} Philip Dyot D Walker

Ann Granville
