Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Abbott aged about 69 years upon his Oath saith That he has Rented a first floor of a Mr. Rutherford in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Unfurnished ever since Midsummer day last at the yearly Rent of Eighteen Guineas

Sworn this 23d. day of June 1789 R Butler

J Abbott
