Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Debon  Single woman maketh oath That her late Father & Mother deced were Natives of Ireland & never did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in Great Britain That She this Depont was also born in Ireland & never lived as a hired Servant by the Year nor ever was bound an Apprentice nor wap ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 29th day of September 1775  Sam. Wood .

 Eliz Debon
