Middlesex  Sarah Clark removed from the parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westrnr. and County of Middlesex by Order bearing Date the 23d. Day of July last upon Oath says she is the Wido . of Thomas Clark deceased that she intermarried with the sd. Thomas Clark on the 24th. Day of Sept. 1734 in the parish Church of St Clement Danes Says her Sd. late Husband upwards of Twelve Years ago rented a Cellar near the new Church in the Strand in the parish of St. Mary be Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and lived therein about Six Months according to the best of her Remembrance Says the sd. Cellar was a Seperate Appartment of itself and had no communication with any other part of the house & had Water & a necessary house within the same Says after her sd. late husband quitted the said Cellar he rented a Cellar and

three pair of Stairs room at the corner of Blackmoor Street in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Libty of Westmr. aforesaid of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds & lived there upwards of Six Months Says her sd. late husband after he guitted the last mentioned Cellar and three pair of Stairs room never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since the Death of the sd. Thomas Clark rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath four Children (to Wit) Thomas aged about 8 Years Edwd. aged abt. 6 Yrs. Sarah aged abt. 4 Yrs. & Eliz. aged upwds. of 2 Yrs and being poor is not able to maintain them.

Sworn Augt. 29th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The mark of Sarah Clark
