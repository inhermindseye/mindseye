Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit David Cook aged about 55 years upon his Oath Saith that about she year 1779 he intermarried with Margarett his present Wife at the College in Edinburgh by whom he hath living two Children viz Elizabeth aged 14 years and Richard aged Six years That he has Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Whites alley in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas That he hath quitted said House about 2 years Since which time he hath not rented any house Tenement or lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th day of Novr 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

David Cook
