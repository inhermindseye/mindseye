Westmr. to wit Elizth Lloyd Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one William Claxton a Butcher in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clemnt. Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 22d. day of June 1771 before} R. Butler

her Elizth. Lloyd mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
