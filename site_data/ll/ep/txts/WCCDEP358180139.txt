Middx  Mary Penyal  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr Rt. Brownsword in Cary Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the Space of 5 Yrs. that since she left her sd. Service she hath not Rented ten pounds a Yr. paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 5th. Decr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Pemisall
