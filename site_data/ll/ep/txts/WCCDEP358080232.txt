Middx & Westmr.}  Lucy Sapsard aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Bird in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago since which time she never lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year and further upon her Oath says that on the Twenty fifth day of December 1776 . she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Quebeck Hospital Oxford Road which has since been baptized Mary and is now become Chargable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes And that one Edward Davis a Patern Drawer (who same times Came to his Uncles House in Derby Court the upper part of Curzen Street May Fair with whom this Examinant then lived as a Servant and at other times he worked with one Mr. Patterson a Pattern dram at Chiswick ) is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House sometime in the Month of March 1776 and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. And that the said Edward Davis and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 27th. day of March 1778 before R Butler

Mark Lucy Sapsard
