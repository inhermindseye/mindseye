Middlesex to wit Mary Hawley Aged about Thirty Eight years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says her Father Caleb Hawley served seven years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Hollister a Taylor in Serle Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement And further says that she hath one Child living named Ann Aged upwards of Eight years born a Bastard in the House of one Mr. Hayton a Chandler in Smarts Buildings Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and whose Father went a Broad as a Marine and is since dead as she believes And further says that her said Child never gained any or. Settlement than by Birth

Sworn this 14th. day of Octr. 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Hawley Mark
