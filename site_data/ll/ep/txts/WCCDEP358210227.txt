Westmr.  Mary Inwood Aged upwards of Fifty four Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Inwood with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London about Eighteen Years ago, says her said Husband has been dead upwards of Fourteen Years, and that he hath in his life time often times informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that before his intermarriage with this Examinant, and whilst he was a Singleman, he hired him -self as a Yearly Servant to Mr. Eastman a Poulterer in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds, ten Shillings and served Six Months to the said Mr. Eastman and then intermarried with this Examinant and Continued to live with the said Mr. Eastman under the same hiring and Agree ment till within about Four or Five Months of his Death, was her said Husband lodged with this Examinant for Two Years or thereabouts the last Part of the said Service at Mr. Daniel Moore  Shoemaker in St. Clements Lane in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes , and after he quitted said Service never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes, says she hath not since her said Husband's Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 9th. day of January 1768 . before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Innwood
