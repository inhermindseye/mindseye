Middx to wit Elizabeth Eadson Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says her Father Christr. Eadson Rented and lived in a House in Church Street in the Parish of Stoke Newington in the County of Middlesex Upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds or thereabouts and paid Taxes due for the same and after quitting the said last mentioned House he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said Fathers Death.

Sworn this 9th. day of Octr. 1780 before  Wm. Kitchiner  Sampn Wright

Elizabeth Eadson her Mark
