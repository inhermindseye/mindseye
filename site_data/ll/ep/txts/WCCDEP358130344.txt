Middx.  Mary Morris aged abt. 22 yrs. upon Oath says she never was married says she was born in the Parish of Montrosse in the Shire of Angus in Scotland & never Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes was an apprentice or yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn the 23d. of May 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Morris
