Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Ogilvy aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith That she never was Married Served any Apprenticeship lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year ever Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That her Father Thomas Ogilvy Rented and lived in a House in King Street Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for about four years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that he hath quitted said house upwards of Ten years ago since which time he has not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of May 1789 . Sampn. Wright N Conant
