Middx  The Examination of Jane Ealing  Singlewoman living in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx aged forty three Years taken the 30th. Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that she lived about two Years ago as a yearly hired Servant with her brother James Ealing in Devereux Court in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes  Cabinet Maker for the Space of one Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & further on her Oath saith that She hath not since that Time rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & now being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Jane Ealing
