Westmr.  Ann Ford of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster  Widow on behalf of Mary Grant an Infant Female Bastard Child Aged about Eight Months on her Oath Says that the said Child is deserted by its Mother Mary Johnson as she the said Mary Johnson Informed this Deponent and which Information she verily believes to be true And further says that the said Mary Johnson also Informed her that the said Child was a Bastard and Born in the Hospital in Totenham Court Road in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex And has also often heard the said Mary Johnson say that one John Grant who lately worked at a Timber Yard in Salisbury Court London is the Father of the said Female Bastard Child And this Dept. further says that she knows nothing where either the said John Grant or the said Mary Johnson the reputed Father and Mother now lives or resides

Sworn this 22st. day of Novr. 1770 before Us. W Kelyngs R. Butler

her Ann Ford Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
