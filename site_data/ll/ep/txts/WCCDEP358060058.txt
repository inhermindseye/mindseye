Middx  Jane Edwards upon Oath saith she never was married and that she never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year that she hath been Informed And believes her late father was bound Apprentice for 7 Yrs. to Edwd. Wrench a Shoemaker & Served the last three Years of his Apprentiship with the sd. Edwd. Wrench in Deadmans Place in the Parish of St Saviour in Southwark in the County of Surry and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn 19th Janry 1750 Tho Lane

The mark of Jane Edwards
