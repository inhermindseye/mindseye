Middx  Tho. Keeling upon Oath saith he was married to his wife Mary Keeling at the Fleet London about 20 years ago and that whilst a Singleman he lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Putland at the Feathers Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly wages of £8. (to the best of his Remembrance for one year and upwards That he quitted his sd. Service abt. 22 Yrs. ago and has not since rented ten Pounds by the year Paid Parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn 14th. May 1755 before }
