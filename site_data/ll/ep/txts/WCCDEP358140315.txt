Middx.  Dorothy Diana Chesterman aged abt. 51 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Edwd. Chesterman who has been dead about Two Years. says her said husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with his Brother Thomas Chesterman  Joyner in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. as her said late husband Often times informed her and as she Verily believes says her said husband in his Lifetime nor she since his death never Rented ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yrly. Servt . for One Year.

Sworn this 5th. day of Novr. 1759 before} Barth: Hammond

Dorothy Diana Chesterman
