Westmr. (to wit)}  Francis Nash on behalf of Ann Harrison a Lunatie upon Oath saith the sd. Ann Harrison has been for some time past and at present is disordered in her Sans as that he has been Informed and believes she never was married nor ever was a Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes and that he is well acquainted with the sd. Lunaties Father who has for fifteen Years past lived in and rented and still continues to live in and rent a house in the parish of Teddington in the County of Bedford and pays Scot and Lot in the sd. Parish.

Sworn the Tenth Day of Octr.1749 before H Fielding

Fras Nash .
