Ann Egerton and Child Thomas Vide Exam of 35 this Book

St. Clement Danes Janry 4th. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St. CD her Child Thos. a Bastard St. Paul Covt. Garden  (to wit) Ann Egerton Aged Nineteen Years upwards Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Sells an Attorney in Grange Court Carey Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for about One Year & Eleven Months at the Annual Wages of £5, Quitted that serve Served about in the Month of September last And hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that on the 17th. day of October last she was delivered of a Female Male Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Bain bri N:14 in Denmark Court in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County and which said Child hath since being half Baptized by the Name of Thomas but no Surname whatever now quitted neither hath it ever been called by any And this Depont. saith that one Patrick Neali who is an Articled Clerk with with Mr Sell in Grange Court aforesaid in the only neat true father thereof he having had Carnel Knowlegde Knowledge of this Deponents Body & be gotten the same And that she is Poor and unable to maintain herself & said Child.

Witness to this fact by Information Richd. Dobbins

Sworn the 4th day of January 1792. before us  Sampn. Wright M Addington

Ann Egerton
