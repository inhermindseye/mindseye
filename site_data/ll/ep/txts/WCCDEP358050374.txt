Middx  Ann Frignal upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwards of 12 Yrs. ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Richd. Sudbury in the parish of Egmonton in the County of Nottingham for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of 50 Shillings or thereabts. and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 22d. 1750 before Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Fringnal
