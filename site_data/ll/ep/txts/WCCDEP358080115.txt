Middx & Westmr.}  Martha Spicer Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Richard Carter  Esqr . at Great Marlow in the County of Bucks for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subseq. Settlemt. And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one Wm. Hilton a Footman to Lady Napier who lately lodged at one Mrs. Thomas in Buckingham Street York Buildings and who now lives in Duke Street York Buildings is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the said Mrs. Thomas's House some time in May last but the particular day she does not now remember & at sevl. different times afterwards one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Wm. Helton a no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this fifth day of Febry 1777 before R Butler

Martha Spicer
