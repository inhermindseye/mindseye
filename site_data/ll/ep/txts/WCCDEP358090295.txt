Middlesex to wit Presley Lock Aged about forty Seven Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Jane his Wife (gone from him) at the City of Norwich abo about Twenty Two Years ago and by whom He ha no Child or Children living Says he Rented and lived in a House in Cow Cross in the Parish of St. Sepulchre without in the County of Middlesex upwards of Six Months at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Ten Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 22d Day of Febry. 1782 before R Butler M Addington

his Presley Lock Mark
