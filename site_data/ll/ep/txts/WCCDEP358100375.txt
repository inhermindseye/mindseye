Saint Clement Danes

Midlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Martins to wit Elizabeth Timberley aged about 58 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Richard Timberley at the Fleet London about 30 years ago and who is been dead about 20 years Says that her said Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr Conner a Glazier in Scotland yard in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 8th. day of March 1785 G Mercer Tho Collins

Eliz Timberley
