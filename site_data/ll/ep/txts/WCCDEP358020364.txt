St Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  Br Br (to wit) Benjamin Newman one of the Beadler of the parish of St. Clement Danes . in the said County Liberty on behalf of Elizth. Eldin Aged thirty Nine years or thereabouts Maketh Oath & saith that she the said Elizabeth Eldin hath Informed this Deponent which he verily believes to be time that he never was married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Shr. Mr. Lane at Cecil Street Coffee House in the Strand in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex aforesaid for about three years at the annual Wages of £10..10..0 that she quitted the same about four Years ago & hath not since (by any way or means wt soever) obtained any Subsequent legal settlement And this Deponent saith that she is now Ill poor & (as he is Informed) Pregnant with an illegitimate Child or Children

Sworn the 7th Decr. 1791 before us  Wm Hitchiner D Walker

Benj: Newman
