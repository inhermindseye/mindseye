After Birth Mary Clark and Mary Clark her B. Child

St Clement Danes 26th April 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman's settlement St C D}  Child's birth}  Reputed Father} (to wit) Mary Clark aged 20 years and upwards Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that She never was married that She has lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Middleston for two years & upwds. at the yearly Wages of St Guineas that she removed with her said Master from St James's Westminster to Cecil Street in the Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex in the Month of December 1792 and hath lived with him there ever since  and that she this Examinant hath now Continues is the in the same House and is not discharged from her said service And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that on or about the sixteenth day of March last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of her said Master in Cecil Street in the Parish aforesaid which hath since been half Baptized by the name of Mary Clark and is become chargeable to the said parish that James Beaton who now lives a footman to Mr Swan No 24 in Argyle Street Oxford Road is the only real true father of the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he and no one also having had carnal connection of this Deponent's Body sometime on or about the twentieth day of June 1792 and at several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child thereon And that he and no one else is the only real true father thereof

Sworn the 26th Day of April 1793 before me} D Walker

Mary Clark
