Middlesex  Richard Macguir Maketh oath That divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Yearly wages with Thomas Watson in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Vintner and Continued in Such Service for above the Space of a year & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Sd. Parish as he is advised he this Depont being during Such his Servitude a Single & Unmarried Man And this Depont. Saith that he has not Since lived as a hired Servt. for a year nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of September 1770}
