St. Clement Danes October 3d 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D. but the Woman belongs to St Paul Covent Garden by determn. on an Appeal between the parish & St Giles in Janry Session 1794  (to wit) Jane Hunt  Singlewoman aged 31 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she Rented and Resided in an House in Duke Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex about four or five years at the Annual Rent of £35, that she quitted it about Eight years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 3d day of October 1792 before Me } Wm. Kitchiner

Jane Hunt

This Woman was afterwards Vizt. about Novr. 1793 Removed from St Giles to this parish & the Removal being appealed agst. on acct. of subsequent Settlement by Rent of a ready furnisher lodging in St Paul Covent The Order was quashed & the in Janry Session 1794 by Woman to new back by St Giles & to remove to that parish
