Middx  Thomas Glynn upon his Oath saith that about 22 Years since he at that time being a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. John Harn  Peruke maker in Hungerford Markett in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westminster at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds Ten Shillings or Five pounds but which this Exaiant doth not at present Remember for the Space of one Year Says he hath not since he left the sd. Mr. Harns Service Rented a house of Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif that he hath 4 Childn. viz: Richard aged Thirteen Years Jno. aged abt. 6 Yrs. Martha aged abt. 3 Yrs. Jas. aged Eleven Days & being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworn April 2d. 1745 before Wm: Jones . Tho Lane

Thomas Glynn
