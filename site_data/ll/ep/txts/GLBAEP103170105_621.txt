Middlesex ss  Ann Collingworth  Singlewoman maketh oath That She hath a Child named William Solby aged near eight years & born of this Deponts Body in Cow yard articheak Lane in the parish of Saint George in the said County The which child is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason this Deponent was never married & Such Child has done no Act to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 18. day of November 1777 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Ann Collingworth
