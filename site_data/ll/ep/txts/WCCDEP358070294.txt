Middx & Westr} to wit Elizabeth Richardson Aged about fifty Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Robt Richardson who has been dead near Thirty Years ago and by whom she has one Child living aged about Thirty Three Years now provided for says that several Years after her said Husbands Death she rented and lived in a House in Exeter Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for five Years and upwards at the Yearly rent of fourteen Pounds & paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards Seven Years ago, since which time she never Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or has done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 20th day of Sepr 1775 Before  David Walker

Eliz Richardson
