Middx to wit Susannah Prosser Aged about Twenty four years On her Oath says she Intermarried with her Husband John Prosser who hath acserted her in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex in the year 1778 and by whom she hath two Children now living Namely John Aged about Two years and mary Ann Aged about Three Months says her said Husband never Rented House paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired yearly servant for One year before marriage And this Examinant Elizabeth Prosser Mother of the said John Prosser whose Father William Prosser was an Fishman and never gained any Settlement in England to her knowledge or Belief otherwise then by being born in Phenix Alley Long Acre  in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Twenty four years ago and that her said son never gained any other settlement then as aforesaid

Sworn this 26th. day of March 1781 before  Sampn Wright  Wm Kitchiner

Susanna Prosser
