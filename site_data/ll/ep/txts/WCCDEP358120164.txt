Middx  Mary Egan upon Oath Saith she is the wido . of John Egan who dyed abt. 10 Weeks ago and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. late husband was born in the Parish of Balnaskreen in the County of Derry in Ireland & she can give no further accot. of his Settlemt. & that she this Examint. is likewise a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England

Sworn July 27th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Egan
