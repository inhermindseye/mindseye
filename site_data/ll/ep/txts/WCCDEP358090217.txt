Middx to wit Elizabeth Turner Aged about  Fifty Eight years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or was an Apprentice says she lived a hired yearly servant with Mr. Jones in George Street near the Adelphi in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years and Upwards at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Upwards of Three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 26th. day of April 1781 before  Abrm Baley W Addington

her Elizth. Turner  Mark
