Middx  George Neale aged 51 Yrs. upon his Oath saith that upwards of Twenty three Years since he rented a House faceing Doyleys Warehouse in the Strand in the Precinct of the Savoy in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of fifty two Pounds & lived in the same upwards of four Years that since he left the said House he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne the 14th- March 1742 before} Thos. DeVeil J Bromfield

Geo: Neale
