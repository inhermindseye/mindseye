Middx.  Mary Edwards aged abt. 30 yrs. upon Oath says she never was married; says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cornelius at the Rose Tavern without Temple  Bar in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for two years at the yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service about seven Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant

Sworn the 21st. of April 1757 before}

The Mark of Mary Edwards
