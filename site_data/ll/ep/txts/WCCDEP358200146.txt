Middx  Benjamen Alger Aged about Twenty two Years upon Oath Says he Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wilmot Hosier the Corner of Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx One Year and Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted said Service about Six Months and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the Day of 1764 Before}

The Mark of Benj Alger
