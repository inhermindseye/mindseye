Middx  Jane Flora Aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath Says she is a Native of Calcutta in the East Indias and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And that one John Hutchinson late Servant to Captain Smith of the Houton East India Man did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body on Board the said Ship And that the said John Hutchinson & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of Novr. 1778 before  Jno. Machin

her Jane Flora Mark
