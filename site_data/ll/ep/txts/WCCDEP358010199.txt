Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Thomas Ingerston aged about 76 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented & lived in a House in Phoenix Alley Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields for about 4 years at the yearly Rent of £18 that he hath quitted said House about 30 years ago since which time he has not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Febry. 1788  Wm. Gascoigne R Taylor

his Thomas Ingerston Mark
