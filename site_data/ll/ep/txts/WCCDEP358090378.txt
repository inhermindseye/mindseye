Middx to wit John Haynes Aged fifty Six Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his Present Wife  Ann at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx about three Years ago and by whom he hath one Child living Namely John Aged upwards of four Years born a Bastard in Star Court Butcher Row Says before his Intermarrd. to his former Wife  Mary and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servt . with one Mr. Emery at the Sign of the White Hart in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for One Year & upwards at the yearly Wages of three Pounds hath quitted his sd. Service about 30 Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlemt. either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 4th. day of March 1783 before R Butler

John Haynes
