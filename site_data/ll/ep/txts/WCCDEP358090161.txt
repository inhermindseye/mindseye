Middx to wit Elizabeth Cooper Aged Upwards of Twenty Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House paid Taxes  was an Apprentice says  She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Priggs a Grocer and Cheesemonger the Corner of Queen Charlotte Street Pimlico in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for Two Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago since which time She hath done no other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of Octr. 1780 before  Sampn Wright  Hugh Dive

her Elizth. Cooper Mark
