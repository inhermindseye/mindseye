Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Dorothy Jones Aged about 48 years upon her Oath Saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant for 7 years with Mr Kelly in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds that she hath left the said Service about 14 years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 months or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st day of Feby 1800 before us} Ws Woodenough John Collick

Dorothy Jones
