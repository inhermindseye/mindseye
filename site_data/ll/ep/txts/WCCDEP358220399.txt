Westmr. to wit Charles Bass one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes maketh Oath that the Examination hereunto Annexed is a true Copy of an Examination taken out of the Examination Book kept by Mr. Tapper Deputy Vestry Clerk of the Parish of Saint Pauls Covent Garden and verily believes that Catherine Lindsey is the Daughter of Mary Lindsey deceased (by John Lindsey now in Scotland) her Husband as by the Examination of the said Mary Lindsey deceased may more fully appear.

Sworn this 20th. day of Octr. 1772 before} Tho. Kynaston  Jno. Wright

Charles Bass
