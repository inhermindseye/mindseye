Middx  Eliza: North aged about 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. George Brown a Cork Cutter  near Exeter Exchange in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of fifty Shillings for the first Year and four pounds for the Second Year and Quitted her sd. Service in February last And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Laurence Holbrook  Apprentice to the said Mr Brown did beget the said Child or Children on her body the said Laurence Holbrook having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the latter End of July or beginning of Augt. last and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Laurence Holbrook and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn March 12th. 1750 before  Tho Lane .

The Mark of Eliz North
