Nil Vide proper Settlement page 245

St Clement Danes Augst 6th: 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Orders Abandd.  St Gregory by St Paul but no Settlemt. Vide proper Settlement page 245 this Book to which No Appeal has been preferred  (to wit) Mary Hughes aged forty nine years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to George Hughes at the parish Church of St Martin Ludgate London about twelve years ago that she hath never heard whether her said Husband ever obtained any legal Settlement in his own Right or where his last legal settlement is that he is now from her and she knows not whether he is living or Dead And this Examinant saith that she this Examinant while she was single and unmarried she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Gosling at his House in Great Knight Rider Street in the parish of St Gregory by St Paul London for a year and upwards at the annual Wages of Nine Guineas that she quitted that Service about ten years ago and hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the sixth day of August 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner  Edwd: Naish

Mary Hughes mark

Witness Jas Talboys

No Settlement Removed afterwards to Whitechapel Vide Subsequent Examn.
