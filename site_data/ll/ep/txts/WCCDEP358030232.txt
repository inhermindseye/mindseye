Middx  Jane Evans upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived in and Rented a house in Crown Court in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds for the Space of two Years & hath left same about upwards of two Years Says she hath not since she left sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 22d. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Jane Evans
