Middlesex ss}  Mary Jefferys the wife of Joseph Jefferys who is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore maketh oath that her said husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one John Dutton a Buckle maker in the Parish of St John in Birmingham in the County of Warwick & has not since done any Act to gain anothr settlement to her knowledge or belief and that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 18. day of February 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Jefferys
