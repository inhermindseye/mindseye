Middlesex ss}  Mary Goffe the widow of Nicholas Goffe deced maketh oath That her said late husband was by birth an Irishman & to her knowledge or belief never did any act to gain a Settlement in England That she this Depont preview to her marriage with her said husband was retained & lived as a hired Servant with one Mr Patterson a Victualler in the Precinct of St Catherine in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such Retainer & Hiring for the Space of two years & upwards & by means there of as She is advised gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for a year nor done any act to gain anothr Settlement Save What She has acquired by her marriage as aforesd That She has two children namely Eleanor aged about Ten years & Ann Aged about five years the Issue of this Deponent by her sd husband.

Sworn this. 10. day of July 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes 

The Mark of Mary Goffe
