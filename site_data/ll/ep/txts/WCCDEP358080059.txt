Middlesex & Westminster}  Mary Moore Aged upwards of Thirteen Years on her Oath Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields to one Anthony Connor a Shoemaker in that Part of Wych Street which lies in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand and served her said Master under that Indenture to the time her said Master run Away and Deserted her which was upwards of Nine Months and this Examinant does not know what is become of him And further says that since her said Master has left her she never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any other Settlement to the best of her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn this 17th. day of June 1776 before  John Croft  Edwd: Naish

her Mary Moore Mark
