Middlesex ss}  Susannah Brown the wife of John Brown maketh oath that her said husband hath for a Considerable time  absented himself from her & Still Continues So to do or to Administer any relief to her for the Support of herself & Children That her sd husband in the year 1774 lived in & rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Three Pounds in Swan Yard in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in in the sd County & during Such his residence therein was Charged to & Paid Kings Taxes therefore She this Depont having more than once Paid Such Taxes to the then Collector thereof Mr Callander Since deced That her Sd husband herself & Child were in March last by a Pass order of two Justices removed to the sd Hamlet of Ratcliffe as the Place of their last legal Settlement at the instance of the Parish officers of St John Wapping Middx & Admitted into the Work house there Again St which order no objection was ever made to this Deponts knowledge or belief That her sd husband Since his leaving the Sd house in Swan Yard has has not rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a settlement for himself elsewhere That she hath two Children namely John aged about Sixteen Years & Timothy Aged about three Years & half the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her sd husband to whom She was lawfully married neither of which Children have as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement for themselves

Sworn this 22d-day of Novemr 1783 before J. Hawley P Greene

The Mark of Susannah Brown
