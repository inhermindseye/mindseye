Middx  Ann Wright aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath Saith she is the wido . of Sacheveret Wright to whom She was married at May Fair Chapel about 16 Yrs. ago and that her sd husband rented the Ball head Alehouse in Vear Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £35 lived therein about Six months and quitted the sd. house about fifteen Years ago and that neither her sd. late husbd. after he quitted the abovementioned house nor has this Examt Since his Decease rented ten pounds by the Year pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt for one Year

Sworn July 6th. 1754 before Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Wright
