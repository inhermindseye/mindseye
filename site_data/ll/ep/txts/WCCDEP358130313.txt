Middx  Margt. Lucas aged abt. 41 yrs. upon Oath says she Wife of George Lucas (gone from her) by whom she hath one Child (Videlicet) Mary aged abt. seven yrs. says her said Husband Served his Apprenticeship with a Stay maker in the Parish of Blanesbridge in the County of Monahone in the kingdom of Ireland & never Rented ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes or was a yrly. Servt.

Sworn the 29th. of March 1757 before} 

The Mark of Margt. Lucas
