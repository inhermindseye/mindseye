Middx 

Sarah Masterson wife of John Masterson  (gone from her) upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her sd. husband at Kiltale Church in the County of Kildare in Ireland abt. 20 Yrs. ago by whom she hath one Child now with her Jane aged abt. 7 Yrs. that both the said John Masterson and this Ext. are Notices of Ireland and never gained a Settlemt. in England and that the said John Masterson her husband Served his Apprentiship wth. Mr Jas. Foley in High Street in the parish of St. Michaels in Dublin in the kingdom of Ireland

Sworn may 31st. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Sarh Masterson
