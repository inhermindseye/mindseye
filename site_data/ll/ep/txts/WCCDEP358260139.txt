Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit David Greenwood aged about 48 years upon his Oath saith that about seven years ago he Intermarried with Charlotte his present wife at the Parish Church of Paddington by whom he hath one Child living Vizt. George aged about Six years That he this Deponent lived with Messrs. Walkers in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes as a hired yearly Covenanted Servant for the Term of Five years and that after he quitted said Service he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of July 1796 } Saml. Edward

David Greenwood

10th. March Produced Articles of Agreement dated 22d. Nov. 1773 2/6 Stamp) of which the following is an Extract

That in case the said David Greenwood shall at any time during the said Term of five years absent himself from the Service of the said Joseph Walker and William Walker or neglect their business or in any other manner to mis behave himself towards them That then it shall a no may be lawful to and for the said Joseph Walker and William Walker refuse giving one month notice in writing to the said David Greenwood to dismiss and discharge him the said David Greenwood from their Service Etc
