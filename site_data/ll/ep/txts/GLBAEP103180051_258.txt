Middlesex ss}  Catherine Moody  Singlewoman maketh oath That She cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or either of them That She this Deponent has been informed by her Father & which information She believes to be true That She was born in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County That She this Deponent never lived as a hired servant for a year in anyone place nor was ever bound an Apprentice nor was ever married nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this - day of 1778 before See this Exn. Sworn to in fo 51
