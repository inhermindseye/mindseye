Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Brindley aged about 34 years upon her oath saith that on the 13th. day of May 1771 she Intermarried with Elias Brindley her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Sepulchre at Northampton that he has been dead near Three years that she has Four Children living by her said late Husband Vizt. Sarah aged about 13 years Elias aged about 8 years Elizabeth aged about 7 years and John aged about 4 years all unprovided for that since her said late Husbands Decease she this Examinant has rented and lived in a House in Knights Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Nine Months and upwards at the yearly rent of Twelve Guineas that she hath quitted said House upwards of Six Months since which time she has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of Novr. 1788  Wm. Kitchiner

her Elizabeth Brindley Mark
