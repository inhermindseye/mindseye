Middx  Tho: Blood upon his Oath saith That he is a Singleman & was never married & that about Six Yrs. ago he lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Robt. Whesson in Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds for the Space of Two Years That since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 20th.. Novr. 1742 before,} Wm. Jones .

Thos Blood
