Middx  Ann Demseed upon Oath saith she was married to Peter Demseed on the 9th. of Febry last at the Fleet London that her sd husband is an Irishman & never gained a Settlement in England and that she (this Examinant) never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. for one Year nor ever rented ten pounds a Year or paid parish taxes and that her father Mathews deceased lived in and rented a house of the yearly Rent of £40 in Gilberts Passage Claremarket in the parish of St Clemt Danes and never after he quitted that house rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn Octr. 12th 1752 before}
