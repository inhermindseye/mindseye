Westmr.  Margaret Barker aged about 60 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thos. Barker who has been dead about Twenty Years, says, that being a Widow without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Wilson a Tobacconist at Temple ba r in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for four Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds, Quitted said Service about three Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 11th, day of February 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Margt. Barker
