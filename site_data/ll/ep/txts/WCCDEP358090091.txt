Middx to wit William Crisp Aged about Twenty Six Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes says after he served the first part of his Apprenticeship with Daniel Crisp his Father he went afterwards to York where he hired himself as a Yearly hired Servant to one Joshua Leed Blacksmith in Gotherham Gate in the City of York at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds besides Boards Lodging and after he quitted the said last Mentioned service he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 22nd. day of Octr. 1779 before R Butler

Willm Crisp
