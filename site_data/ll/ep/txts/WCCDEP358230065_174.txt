Middlesex (to wit) The above named Frances Hood removed farm St. Giles in the Fields to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes by Crow of Removal of the 2d Instant Upon her Oath Saith that service being in the above mentioned Service she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant, has not rented any Premises of the yearly Rent or value of ten pounds nor upward, nor has she to her knowledge information or belief done any Act matter or thing whereby she has gained a subsequent Settlement That she has not been or moved from any Parish or Place any other Parish or Place as unto the Parish or Place of her A gaine Settlemt and that she has never been married

Sworn at the Public Office Hutton Garden this 8th. day of May 1916 before me} W M Sellor

F Hood W E
