Grace Carter : aged 57 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That Christopher Lewis the Father of Phoebe Lewis was born at Ratfine Farm in the Parish of Amesbury in Wiltshire , That his Father owned the same That she believes the said Christopher Lewis , or his Father never gained any Settlement since living at Ratfine aforesaid.

Sworn this 8th. Day of April 1785  Sampn Wright M Addington

Grace Carter

after It turnd out afterwds that this Panper belongd to St. Thomas Salisbury passd her there
