Middx 

Thomas King upon Oath saith he intermarried with his wife Sarah upwds. of 2 Years ago at the That London by whom he hath one Child Eliz: aged about one Year Says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Cates a Peruke maker in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes and lived with th him there as an Apprentice for four Years or thereabts. & afterwards went & lived with his masters consent with Mr. Pearce a Perukemaker in Forward Street in the same Parish for 6 months & upwds. & then his Master Cates dyed & this Examinant never was assigned over to any other Person rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn July 26th 1748 before} Thos Lane

The Mark of Thos. King
