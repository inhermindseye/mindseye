Middx  Eliz Spiller  Wido . of James Spiller upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. James Spiller at little Ladys Church in the City of Canterbury abt. 36 Yrs. ago and that he hath been dead abt. 14 Years Says her sd. late Husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Ross a Landship Painter in Broad Street in the Parish of St. Peter Poor in Broad Street Londn. & never rented a house of ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief Says She hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn May 22d. 1746 before Wm. Jones

Eliz Spiller
