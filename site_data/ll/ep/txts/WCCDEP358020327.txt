St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Margaret Colder Aged 51 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Clifford a Groom in the Kings Mews in the parish of St Martin in the Fields Middx about Sixteen Months at the Annual Wages of five pounds-that she quitted the same about Sixteen or Eighteen Years ago & since hath not done any Act or thing whatever whereby she might have acquired any other legal Settlement than in the said parish of St Martin in the Fields And that she in Poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 24th Augst 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Margaret Colder 's Mark
