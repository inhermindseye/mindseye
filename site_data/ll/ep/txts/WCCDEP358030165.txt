Middx  James Seale aged abt. 44 Years upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Catherine Seale on or abt. the 28th. Day of January 1736 at the Parish Church of St. George Hanover Square Says he lived in & rented a house in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £18. & lived in the same upwards of ten Years & hath left sd. house upwards of two Years since which time he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served a parochiall Office or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 20th. June 1746 before Wm: Jones

James Sale .
