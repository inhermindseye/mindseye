Middx  Hanah Jones  Wido . of David Jones upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband David Jones on or about the Twenty Sixth Day of Decr- 1723 & that her sd. late Husband served his Apprentisp with Mr. Thos. Plummer & Mr. Joshia Keene in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & that her Husband hath been dead abt. Years & that since he left his sd. Apprentisp he in his Life nor she since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Pish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath 4 Childn. (namely) Ann aged between 15 & 16 Yrs. Eliz : aged between 12 & 13 Saml . aged between 7 & 8 Yrs. & Wm - aged abt. 4 Months & being Poor is not able to Maintaine them

Sworne the 11th. of Septr- 1741 before}
