Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Robert Hutchins upon his Oath Saith that he was bound Apprentice by tings to John Kinnier of Fleet Street in the Parish of St Dunstan in the West in the City of London Oil dealer that be remd. his said Master under the said Indres for one Yeare Upwards, that afterwards his sd. Master dying his Indres were given up & that since his sd. Masters Death he hath not done any Act Matter or Thing Whereby to gain any other Settlement and that he is poor and out of Work

Sworn this 13th Janry 1789 . before Sampn Wright  Rob Abington

Robt. Hutchins
