Middlesex & Westmr.  to wit Catharine Ryan aged abt. Nineteen years Lodging in Nags head Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. Upon her Voluntary Oath says She never was Married And that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born Says that One Thomas Peeling a Sawyer in Nags head Court aforesd. had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body soon after Christmas last and at several other times since at one of which times he get her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant And that he the said Thos. Peeling and no other person did beget the same. Says she has been informed & which Information she believes to be true that her Father Chas Ryan Rented a house near the Angel Inn in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds or thereabouts and lived therein about Two Years that after her said father Quitted the house by the Angel Inn he Rented a house in a Court by the Green Man near London Wall but at what Yearly Rent or how long he lived in the same this Examinant knoweth not.

Sworn the 23d. July 1760 before me.} Barth Hammond

The Mark of Cathne. Ryan

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Ponten Overseer J : P:
