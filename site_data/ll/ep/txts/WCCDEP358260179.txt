Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Forward aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 23rd. day of September 1793 she Intermarried with James Forward her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Anne Westminster by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Catherine aged about Three years That her said Husband Rented part of a house No. 9 Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about five Months at the yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds that he hath quitted said Apartments about a fortnight only that her said Husband has deserted her and her said Child and Refuses to maintain her whereby she is become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 20th. day of Janry. 1797 } Saml. Edward Ann Forward
