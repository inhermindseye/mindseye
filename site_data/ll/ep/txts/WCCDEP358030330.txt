Westmr. 

Hannah Wrench aged about 15 Years upon Oath says she was by Indenture bearing Date the 6th. Day of July 1744 bound an Apprentice  to Elizabeth Roberts  Wife of Richard Roberts of the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Taylor Says her Mistress being Poor & not able (as she informed this Examinant to maintain her Set her this Examinant. to live with one Mrs. Petty-at the Swan & Crown in King Street in the Parish of St. Margt. in the Liberty of Westmr. with whom this Examinant lived as a Servant with the Consent of her said Mistress about three months & then was Discharged from the sd. Mrs. Pettys Service.

Sworn Janry 24th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Han Wrench .
