Middx & Westmr.  Sarah Fielder Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married rented House paid Taxes says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mackie who lodges at Mr. Hall a Peruke maker in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her said Service about a Month ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one William Hazel otherwise Hazley a Journey man Hair Dresser had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of July last in her said Masters Lodgings but the Particular Day she does not now remember and several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child or the Child or Children which she is now Pregnant and thar the said William Hazel otherwise Hazley and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 14th. day of April 1773 before me  Sampn Wright

her Sarah Fielder Mark
