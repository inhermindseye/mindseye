Westmr.  Jane Slingsby aged about 31 Years Upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Andrew Moffatt  Esqr . in the Parish of Hampstead in the County of Middlesex for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds; hath quitt said Service about three Years and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 22d, day of September 1765, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Darby in Grange Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been baptized Thomas and that being poor she is not able to Maintain herself & Child

Sworn 17th. April 1766 before} Fran. Cary  Paul Vaillant

Jane Slingsby
