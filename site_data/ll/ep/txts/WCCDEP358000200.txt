Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Richard Ling aged 18 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he has been informed he was born in Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Saith that he lived with his Father who kept a House in Bennetts Court till about years ago that his Mother died in Barnetts Court when this Examinant was about Nine years old he was then sent to School at Knightsbridge that during the time this Examinant was at School his Father went to live as a hired yearly Servt. with one Mr. Sterling of the Piazza Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden and has lived with the sd. Mr. Sterling for, this Seven years And further this Examinant saith that he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 7th- day of January 1786  Rt. Taylor  Jno. Wright

Richd. Ling
