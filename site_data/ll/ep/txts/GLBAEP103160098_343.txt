Middlesex ss}  Hannah Man the widow of James Man Mariner deced maketh oath That she Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband That divers years ago and previous to her marriage with her Said husband was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages by & with one Nicholas Spruson in the Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in such service for divers years & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement Save what she acquired by Marriage with her Said late husband Whose Settlements unknown

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Hannah Man
