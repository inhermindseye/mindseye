Middx   Sarah Scott aged about 17 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married bound an Apprentice or lived a hired Servant in any one place for Twelve Months together Says her Father Digby Scott was a Victualler and rented a House known by the Sign of the Blue Anchor in the Precinct of White Friars in the City of London for Nine Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of £24 and that he died in the Same about 5 Years ago

Sworn the 2d. Day of February 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Scott
