Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Harris aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That about Twelve Years ago she hired herself to a Mr. Greenwood who did live in Clare Street at the Yearly Wages of Fifty two Shillings that she lived with the said Mr. Greenwood for near Five Years the last Nine Months of which Service was  in East Lane  Walworth in the Parish of Saint Mary Newington in the County of Surry where she was discharged That after she quitted said Service she never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement  that she has this day been relieved by the Parish of St. Clements Lane

Sworn this 13th. day of Decr. 1797 before us} John Staples R. Ford

her Elizabeth Harris Mark

H Day
