The Examination of Elizth. Lockwood Single woman taken on Oath before me one of his Majestys Justices of the peace for the County of Middlesex residing in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx this 7th. day of August 1745

Middx This Examinant Elizth. Lockwood maketh Oath and Saith that in or about the Month of November last One John Hennington late of Buckle Street in the parish of St. Mary White Chapel in Middx  Silk Thrower did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at his Lodgings in Buckle Street in the parish of St. Mary White Chapel in Middx aforesaid and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and so had several times whereby She conceived with Child or Children of which She now goeth and is Pregnant And this Examinant Saith that the said Child or

Children of which She now goeth and is Pregnant as aforesaid is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx She not being able to provide for the same And that the said John Kennington is the true and only father of the said Child or Children so likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesaid And Lastly yt: this Examination is Voluntary and that She hath not been forced or Compelled to be Examined by any Person or Persons whatsoever.

Sworn this 7th. day of August 1745 Before Robert Dennett

her Elizabeth Lockwood Mark
