Middlesex  Ann Lee  Widow of William Lee Deced Maketh oath that her said late husband has often informed her that he lived as a hired servant by the year (when a singleman) with Mr John Tween a Farmer in the Parish of Ware in the County of Hertford and Continued in such service for  the space of a year & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent saith that her sd. husband did not  afterwards live as a hired servant for the space of a year nor Rent a house of Ten pounds a Year or Upwards Nor serve any Parish offices nor gain any subsequent settlement to the knowledge informacon or belief of this Deponent And this Depont Saith that she hath four Children to wit Ann aged about seven years William aged about four Years Elizabeth aged abt a year and Hannah Aged about five weeks the lawful issue of this Depont by her sd husband

Sworn this 2d day of August 1757 before us  Chris. Scott  Sam Gower

Ann Lee
