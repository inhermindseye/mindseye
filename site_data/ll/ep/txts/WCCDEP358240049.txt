Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Giles in the Fields  to Wit Jane Seedsman aged upwards of 16 Years upon her Oath Saith that she was bound Apprentice to Mr. Rosers of Broad Street Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex  Shoe Maker and has served with him for between 2 & 3 Years that owing to some Misunderstanding or disagreement between her and Mrs. Roger she this Deponent deserted her said Masters Service and has been for about a Year from him that about 10 Months of the time since she left her said Master she lived as a Weekly Servant with Tax Looney in Giant Yard Shoreditch and has lived in several Weekly Places since without the Covent of her said Master but ever since she left her last place which is about 5 Weeks Ago she has been in the Workhouse of Saint Clement Danes and that she has not Served the whole of her Apprenticeship neither has she been discharged there from

Sworn the 5th. day of Octr. 1791 before us} Philip Dyot D Walker

Jane Seedsman her Mark
