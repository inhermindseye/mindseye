Westmr.  Mary Richards upon Oath Saith she is the wife of John Richards who absconds from her & of whose Settlement she can give no Account. and that she (this Examt.) never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. for one Year nor ever rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes And that her late father Lewis Robert rented a house of £18 by the year in Earls Court Cranbourn Alley in the Parish of St. Ann Soho in the Liberty of Westmr. lived therein Sevl. Years & never after he quitted that house rented ten pounds a Year or paid parish taxes And that she (this Examint.) abt. 7 Years ago was removed from the Parish of St. James Westmr. to the sd. Parish of St. Ann Soho by an Order of two Justices which Order never was appealed against

Sworn Jany. 30th. 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Richards
