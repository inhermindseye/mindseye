Middx  Mary Askey aged upwds. of 50 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about Nine Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Storey in Ship Yard for one Year at the Yearly wages of five Pounds and has not since she quitted her said Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 21st 1750 before}
