St. Clement Danes 30th Novr 24th Augst 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Mary Waldon  singlewoman Aged 60 years & upwards or thereabts. Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Richardson's Uncle Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for two year & upwards at the Annual Wages of Six pounds that She quitted that Service several years ago And hath Not obtained any legal Settlement otherwise than in the said parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 24th 30th day of Augst Novr 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Waldon 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
