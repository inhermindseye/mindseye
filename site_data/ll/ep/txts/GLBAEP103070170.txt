Middx ss  Margaret Frost  Single woman Maketh oath that her Father Samuel Frost (as this Deponent has been informed and verily believes) Rented a house the Corner of Cold Bath Fields in the Parish of St James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx and paid all Parish Taxes and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there which Information this Depont. verily believes to be true And this Deponent saith that she was never Married nor an Apprentice nor a hired servant by the Year nor hath gained any settlement but in right of her sd Father to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 30th day of September 1756 before  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

his Margaret Frost Mark
