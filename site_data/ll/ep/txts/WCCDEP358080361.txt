Middx to wit James Twiddy Aged about forty Two Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife  Mary at the Parish Church of Hodsdon about Twelve Years Ago and by whom he hath Two Children now living namely James aged about Eleven Years and William Aged about Seven Years Says he Rented and lived in a House in Tennis Court Middle Row in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Two Years at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds hath quitted said House about four Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley  Sampn Wright

his James Twiddy Mark
