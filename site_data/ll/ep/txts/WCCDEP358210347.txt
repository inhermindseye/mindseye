Middx & Westmr}  Elizabeth Brewen Aged about Twenty four Years upon Oath Says She is the Wife of Thos. Brewen (gone from her) with whom She Intermarried at the Parish Church of St Thomas the Apostle in the City of London about Six Years ago by whom She has one Child Named John Aged about Two Years Says her said Husband Rented Two Seperate Houses the one in Turnmill Street at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds and the other in Swan Alley at the Yearly Rent of fifteen pounds both Situate in the parish of St: James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex and lived therein Eight Months or thereabouts and paid all Parish Taxes for the same hath quitted said lastmentioned house about Three Years and hath not Since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. Day of January 1769 Before us W Kelynge Pet. Planck

Elizabeth Brwn

Witness} Fras. Bishop
