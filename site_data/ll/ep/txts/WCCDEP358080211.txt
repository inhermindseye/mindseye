Westmr- to wit Margt- Pace aged about twenty three Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Callam a Gold & Silver Lace Maker in Buckingham Street York Buildings in the Psh of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr - for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted that her said Service about six Years ago since which time she has gained no other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 4th- Day of Febry 1778 before} M Addington

her Margt- Pace Mark
