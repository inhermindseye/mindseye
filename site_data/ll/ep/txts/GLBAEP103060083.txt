Aldgate Middx

John Clifton aged about Sixty four years Maketh Oath that he was informd by his parents that he was born and bred up in the Precinct of Saint Katherine near the Tower of London that he remembers that when he was about the Age of Eighteen years he was bound an Apprentice to his father Saml Clifton a Ballastman and Servd him Seven years in the Said Precinct of Saint Katherine and that he never was a hired Servant by the year Since nor never gaind any other Settlement Since

Sworn 30th day of March Ano Dom 1747 before Robert Dennett Richd: Ricards

the mark of John Clifton
