Middx  Eliz Strickett aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath saith she was married at the Fleet  London about 14 Years ago to Arthur Stricket and that at the time of her sd. marriage her sd. husband was an apprentice to and Continued afterwards to serve his apprentiship wth. Mr Putland at the feathers Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that her sd husband never rented ten pounds by the year pd. parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn Jany. 11th 1755 before}
