Westmr  Margaret Carter on behalf of her Son James Carter Aged about Twenty Two Years Upon Oath says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Thomas Webb  Box maker in Friday Street in the Parish of St. Matthew Friday Street in the City of London , and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 15th. day of December 1767 before}
