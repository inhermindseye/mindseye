Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Trayner the Wife of  Patrick Trayner  maketh oath that divers ago her said husband Rented a house in Rose & Crown Court in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to & paid the Kings Tax for the Same & by means thereof as She this Deponent is advised he gained a legal settlement there for himself & Family That to this Deponents Knowledge or belief her said husband has not since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office nor paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement.

Sworn this day of February 1774 before}

Eleanor Trayner  Her Mark
