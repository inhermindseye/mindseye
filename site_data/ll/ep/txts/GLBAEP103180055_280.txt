Middlesex ss}  Catherine Moody  Singlewoman maketh oath That She Cannot give any Account respectg the Settlement of her Parents or either of them That She this Deponent has been informed by her Father and Which information She believes to be true That She was born in King Henry Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County That She this Depont never lived as a hired servant for a year in anyone Service nor was She ever bound Apprentice or ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 6. day of August 1778 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Catherine Moody
