Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Carr the wife of William Carr maketh oath That her Said husband is now absent from her in his Majesties Service on board the Thunderer that he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born in the Parish of St Peter in the City of Bristol & this Deponent can give no othr Account respecting his Settlement Save that he has informed her that his Father lived in & rented house there That She was lawfully married to her Sd husband by whom She hath a Child & lawful Issue now living named named John Aged about Ten Months.

Sworn this. 4. day of July 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Carr
