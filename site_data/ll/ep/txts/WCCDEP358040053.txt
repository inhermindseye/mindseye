Middx  James Evans upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Present Wife  Christian Evans in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about 15 Years ago Says before his said Intermarriage and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Mason at the bottom of Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid at the Yearly Wages of £10 for three Years and upwards Says he has not since he left the sd. Mr. Mason Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif Says he has a Son named John aged about 14 Yrs.

Sworn Augt. 27th. 1747 before Houlson

The Mark of James Evans
