Middx  Cath: Vaughan upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her Husband Robt. Vaughan abt. Fourteen Years ago at the Parish Church of & that her sd. Husband lived in & rented a House in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Libty. of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds for the Space of Two Years & upwds. & hath left the same upwards of Two Years that her sd. Husband hath not since that Time rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath Two Childn. viz: Thomas aged abt. Thirteen Years & Hanh. aged abt. 19 Months & her Husband being Sick is not able to maintaine them.

Sworne the 30th. of Decr. 1741 before }
