Middlesex and Westminster}  Swear to St Clemt. to wit, Caroline Ferguson Widow aged 35 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was married to James Ferguson her late Husband deceased on the eighteenth Day of December one thousand seven hundred and Sixty four ; Th at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex That after their Intermarriage her said Husband rented a House in Stanhope Street  Clare Market at a yearly rent exceeding ten Pounds a Year That her said Husband lived in the said House five or six Years and paid Taxes for the same and did there - And this Examinant further saith That since the Death of her said Husband she has never again intermarried lived as an hired Yearly Servant rented above ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or done any Act, Matter or Thing whereby she may to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief have gained a Settlement for herself other than in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

aforesaid - And this Examinant also further Saith That she has two Children now livingly her said Husband, Namely Daniel Walter aged eighteen Years or thereabouts who has gained a Settlement of his own by living upwards of twelve Months as an hired Yearly Servant and Caroline aged thirteen Years or thereabouts who has never gained any Settlement of her own and is now with this Examinant in great Distress

Sworn this 22nd. Octr. 1784 before me R Butler

C Ferguson
