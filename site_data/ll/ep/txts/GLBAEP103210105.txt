Middlesex ss}  Jane Sutton  Singlewoman maketh oath that about Seven years ago she this Deponent was duly bound Apprentice by the Parish officers of St George Sourthwarke in the County of Surry to Martha Walling of the parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex untill she shod Attain the Age of twenty one years That after she lived for about the space of a year there her mistress died & her Apprenticeship ended That after her said Mistresses death she was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mrs Jackson a Laundress in the Sd Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch & Continued in such service under such hiring there for a year That she has not since lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year elsewhere or been married or done any act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12 day of October 1785 before J. Hawley R Davies

The Mark of Jane Sutton
