Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  Cath: Sexton aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married Accordg. to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England that she lived as a hired yearly Servt . with one Mr. Collis of Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 14 Months at the yearly Wages of Eight pounds hath quitted said Service about Seven years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that she hath two Illegitimate Children living (Vizt.) Thos. Sexton aged about 2 years and a Quarter born in Kinsgate Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex and Philips Sexton aged about 13 Months born in Church Street Saint Giles's in the parish of Saint

Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex And further saith that John Sexton to Whom this Ext. was married at the Chapel in Duke Street by a Roman Catholic Priest according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the  Romesh Church and who has been dead about four Months is the father of the said Children and that she this Examinant wishes to leave the same to the Care of the Parish

Sworn this 3rd. day of June 1786  James Croft  Jno Machin

her Cath: Sexton Mark
