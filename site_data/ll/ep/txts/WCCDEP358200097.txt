Westmr  Elizth. Baker Aged about 32 Years upon Oath Says she was Never Married Says she Rented a House in Bedford Court Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds hath quitted said House about Six Years and she hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says she hath One Child Named Margaret Aged about Five Years

Sworn the 12th of April 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant R Kilster

Elizth Baker

Upon Mr Ralph William Complt J. W.
