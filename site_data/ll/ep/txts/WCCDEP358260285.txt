Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Cast aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with the Revd. Mr. Subodinah in Upper Sloane Street in the Parish of Saint Luke Chelsea in the County of Middx for Five years at the yearly Wages of Twelve Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn the 22d. day of May 1798 before us N Bond P. Henderson

Susannah Cast
