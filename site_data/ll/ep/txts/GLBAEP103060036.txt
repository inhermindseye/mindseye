Middx  Elizabeth Ferry  Widow late Wife of Francis Ferry deceased maketh Oath and Saith that about Twenty two Years ago her said late Husband Francis Ferry Rented a House in Archer Street in the parish of St. James Westminster in the County of Middlesex and lived therein upwards of a Year and paid thirteen pounds a Year for the same to best of this Deponents knowledge and Belief and that She hath two Children the lawfull Issue of her said late Husband (namely) Frances Aged about Eighteen Years and Ann Aged about Six Years

Sworn this 28 2th day of Febry March 1744 Before Richd Ricards

Elizth. Ferey
