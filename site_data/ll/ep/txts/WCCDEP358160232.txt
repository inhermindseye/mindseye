Middx The Examination of Elizth- Clifton  Widow of Robert Clifton Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Taken the 23d. Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that upwards of six Months ago she lived with one Mr. Barrett of the Parish of Chelsea in the sd. County of Middx  Wax Chandler for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath not since that time rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife, And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that on the 14th- Day of September last She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Hollis Street in the sd. Parish of St- Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of Mary & that one Elias Williams  Servt . to the said Mr. Barrett did begett the said Child on her Body he at such Time living in the same House with this Examinant & there became accquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body in the Month of January last & at Several other Times since at one of which Times he got her with Child of the sd. Female Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. and yt. he the sd. Elias Williams & no other Persons the true & reall father thereof & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Child

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Samuell Clarke  Jas: Gronous

Eliz Clifton
