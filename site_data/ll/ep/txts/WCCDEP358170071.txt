Middx  Ann Howard  Wido . of John Howard passed as a Vagrant from the Parish of St. Olave South- -wark upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her sd. late Husband Jno. Howard on the 17th- of February 1717 at the Parish of St. Thos - in Oxford & that her Husband serv'd his Time with a Bricklayer in the Parish of St- Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & further saith that She & her sd. Late Husband were passed from the Parish of St. Botolph Bishopgate to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes abt. Twelve or fourteen Years ago as the Place of their last legall Settlemt. & had a Certificate granted them by the Officers of the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & that her sd. Husband in his Life Time nor she since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Yr- pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 18th Day of June 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Ann Howard
