Middx to wit Elizabeth Hawtray aged about 32 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her late Husband Ralph Hawtray in the Year 1763 in the City of Dublin and who died about five Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living and residing in the Parish of Saint Mary in the said City of Dublin and further say's that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Abbey Street in the aforesaid Parish of Saint Mary Dublin of the Yearly Rent of Forty Six Pounds to the time of his Death and lastly says that since her said Husbands Death she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Augt. 1779 before me}

Elizth Hawtrey
