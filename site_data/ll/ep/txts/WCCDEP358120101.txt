Middx  John Jarvis upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Ann Jarvis in the Parish Church of St Martin at Worcester in the Year 1712 that in the year 1710 he was hired to Mr. Morton a Druggist at the yearly Wages of £6 and lived with the sd. Mr Morton under that hiring & agreemt. opposite Creechurch in the Parish of St Cathn. Creechurch London upwds. of one Year (during which he continued a Singleman & that he has not since he left his sd. Service rented ten Pounds by the year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn May 10th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

John Jarvis
