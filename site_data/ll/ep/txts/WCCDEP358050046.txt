Westmr. 

Mary Shepherd upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Philip Shepherd who dyed about four months since upon Oath smith she has two Children by the said Philip Shepherd (now living) vizt. Hannah aged upwds. of Eight Years and William aged about fourteen month Says she has been informed and beleives the her said late husbands father Philip Shepherd whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant for several Years with a Seedsman opposite the new Church in the Strand in the parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. & never after he quitted that service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. and that her Late husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt Rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other

Sworn 23d. May 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Mary Shepherd
