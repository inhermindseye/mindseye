Middlesex ss}  Jane Bedford the Wife of John Bedford mariner maketh oath that her said husband is now on Board his Majesties ship the Hone Such on a voyage to America as She this Deponent as been informed & believes that She this Depont. Can give no Account respecting his Settlement Save that he has informed her that he was born in Ireland That She this Depont was born in the Parish of Aimsworth near Portsmouth in the County of Southampton & never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound Apprentice in any part of the Kingdom of Great Britain nor ever did any act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself Save What She may have Acquired by marriage With her Said husband That this Deponts late Father Morgan Williams deced divers years ago rented a house of the Yearly rent of Forty pounds in the Parish of Aimsworth aforesaid & was charged to & paid Parochial Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this Deponent hath a Child named Benjamin Bedford Aged about four Months the lawful Issue of this Deponentsy her sd husband to whom She was lawfully married.

Sworn this 4th day of September 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Jane Bedford
