Middlesex & Westminster}  passd to St Martins J. to wit) Elizabeth Blackman aged twenty Nine Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she was married to George Blackman her now Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about fourteen Years ago - That her said Husband is by Trade a Plasterer and is now in the County Vizt. at Lambeth in the County of Surry at Work, That she has four Children by her said Husband Videlicet Henry Aged thirteen Years or thereabouts, who has never gained any Settlement of her own George aged twelve Years or thereabouts who is now at work with his Father, Ann aged three Years or thereabouts and Charles aged Seven Months or thereabouts And this Examinant says that her said Husband never Served any Apprenticeship, lived as an hired Yearly Servant, rented any House or Tenement of ten pounds a Year Value or did any other Act whereby to gain any Settlement of his own, - That she has been informed and believes that her said Husband father George Blackman who is dead served his time or an Apprentice with Blackman Plaisterer at the Court of Featherstone Alley in Saint Martin Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and never after he was not of his said

Apprenticeship gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Novr. 1784 before us  Sampr Wright M Addington
