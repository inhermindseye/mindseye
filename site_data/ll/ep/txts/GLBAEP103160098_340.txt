Middlesex ss}  Margaret Dykes the widow of William Dykes deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & resided thereon for the Space of Six Months & by means thereof as she is advised gained a legal Settlement there That her Sd husband did not afterwards rent a house of yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that She this Deponent Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a settlement for herself That She hath two Children namely Ann  aged four years & Thomas  aged about fourteen Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband to whom she was lawfully married.

Sworn this first day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Margaret Dykes
