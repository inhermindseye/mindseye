Middx ss  Ralph Minshall of Rosemary Lane in the County of Middx maketh oath that he was present at the Baptising of a Male Child who was Baptised by the Name of Philip Movell Sometime in the Month of July left in the Parish Church of St. Mary Whitechappell in ye sd. County of Middx this Depont. being God father to ye sd Child And further Saith that the sd. child was born in the house of Mr. John Boulton in Stable Yard Court on Salt Petro Bank in ye sd. Parish of St. Mary White Chappell abt. Seven Months ago.

Sworn this 22nd day of Febry. 1754 before  Richd: Ricards

Ralph Minshall
