Westmr. to wit Thomas Bardin Aged about forty Six Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Jane his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West about Nineteen Years ago and by whom he hath Eight Children now living two of whom are now provided for)  and William aged about Thirteen Years Ann Aged about Twelve Years Sarah Aged about Nine Years Richd Aged about Seven Years John Aged about four Years and Nathaniel aged about Two Years Says his Father John Barden Served four Years the first Part of his Apprenticeship with a Glass Grinder  near Puddle Dock in the Parish of St. Andrew Wardrobe in the City of London & further says that his said Father Never was Assigned Over to any other Person nor ever gained any Subsequent Setlement either by renting House paying taxed or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as his said Father has often informed him and which Information he verily believes to be true

Sworn this 27th. day of Septr. 1773 before R. Butler

his Thos. Bardin Mark Witness Petr Sidebotham
