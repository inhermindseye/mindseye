Middx to wit James Piddock Aged about Thirty Years on his Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Say she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Sr Ralph Milbank about Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Five Ten Guineas besides Cloaths Diet & Lodging Vizt. the first Six Months in Bond Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square and the last Twelve Months in Harley Street  Cavendish Square in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in County of Middlesex where his said Master removed to be was there discharged and after quitting his said Masters Service he never lived as a lived Yearly Servant nor has he gained any other Settlement.

Sworn this 18th. day of June 1784 before  Rd. Taylor W Gregson

His James Piddock Mark
