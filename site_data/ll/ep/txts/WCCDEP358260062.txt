Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth King aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that in the Month of June 1787 she Intermarried with John King her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Giles Cripplegate by whom she hath One Child living vizt. Martha aged about Four years That her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman having no Child or Children living lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Davison in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve years and upwards but what Wages he Received she this Deponent doth not know that he quitted said Service about Eight years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted her & Refuses to Allow her any Relief and that she poor and unable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn this 30th. day of June 1795 } N Bond

her Elizth. King mark
