Middx ss.  Elizabeth Rawson  Widow of John Rawson deced Maketh oath that her sd. husband has often informed her that he duly Served his apprenticeship with one Mr. Rogers in ye Parish of St. John Wapping in ye County of Middx Mariner And by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Says that she hath not at any time Since her sd. husband decease gained any Subsequent Settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief Never having been a hired Servant Nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards Since her sd. husband decease

Sworn this 5th day of Decr. Novr. 1752 before us  Richd: Ricards

his Elizabt Rawson Mark
