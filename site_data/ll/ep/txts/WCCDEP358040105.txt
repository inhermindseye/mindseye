Westmr.  Eliza. Keen upon Oath saith she was never Marryed Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Banfeild in Parkers Lane in the Parish of St. Giles the Fields in the County of Middlesex for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings & hath left her said Service near Three Years Says she has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 14th. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Eliza. Keen
