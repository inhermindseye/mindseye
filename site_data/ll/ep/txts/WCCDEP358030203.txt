Middx  Ann Mason upon Oath says she is a Singlewoman Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. John King  Shipwright at Strand on the Green in the parish of Chiswick in the County of Middlesex for two Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of One pound & ten Shillings and left her sd. Service about four Years Says she hath not since rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief And this Exaiant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & not able to maintain the same when born says one John Holder a Victualler did be gett the sd. Child or Children on her Body this Exaiant liveing a Servt . with the sd. Jno. Holder at the Queen of Bohemia head in Witch Street

in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex the sd. John Holder there had earn all Knowledge of her Body twice in the month of January last at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that he the said John Holder and no other person is the true & real Father thereof

Sworn Augt: 14th. 1746 before Wm. Jones

The mark of Ann Mason
