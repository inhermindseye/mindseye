Westmr.  Ann Pearcy upon Oath she never was married and that about 3 yrs. ago she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Pennick in Whitefryers . Precinct in London for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds or thereabts. and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 5th 1749 before}
