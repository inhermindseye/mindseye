(Before Birth) 

St Clement Danes January 4th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  pregnancy  Reputed Father (to wit) Jane Morgan  singlewoman aged 23 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hodson Pastry Cook in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about a year & Nine Months at the annual Wages of £8:0 or thereabouts that she quitted the same in or about the Month of October or November 1792 and hath not since obtained any legal settlement And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes when born that John  Moriges who now lives (as she is informed & believes)  with the Duke of Gordon at Gordon Castle in Scotland is the only real true father of the said Child wherewith she is pregnant as aforesaid he having first had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body sometime in or about the Month of February last & at several times since & at some or one of which times he begot the same thereon

Sworn the 4th. day of January 1794 before Me } Wm. Kitchiner

 Jane Morgan

Child born in the House 21st. Feby. 1794 & named Robt. Morgan

Sent to Enfield  23d. April 94

Man never taken
