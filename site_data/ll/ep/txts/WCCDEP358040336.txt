Westmr.  Ann Eastland  Wido . of Edward Eastland who dyed abt. Seven Years since upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the sd. Edwd Eastland in the Parish Church of St Clemt Danes on the 18th. Day of Febry 1728 and that her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Geo: Dowler  Staymaker at Mr. Harts a Jeweller in Holborn opposite Brownlow Street in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year and that this Examinant since his Decease has not rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 17th. 1748 before} Tho Lane

Anne Eastland
