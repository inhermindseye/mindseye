Middx  Judith Hollis upon Oath saith she was married to her husband Benj. Hollis at the Fleet London in the month of July was 23 Yrs. ago and has by him two Children Judith aged 8 years & John aged upwards of four Years. that her sd. husband absconds from her and she Known not where he and that she has been informed & verily believes her said husband Served Seven years apprentiship with James Singer a Taylor in Hollywell Street-in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn March 22d. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Judith Hollis
