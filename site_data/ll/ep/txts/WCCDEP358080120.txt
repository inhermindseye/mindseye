Middlesex & Westmr.}  Susannah Charlton Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father George Charlton Rents and lives in a House in Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and has so done for upwards of Six Months last past at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds as she believes And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that One William Careing who keep a House the Third or fourth Court from Holbourn in Fleet Markett but what Business he follows she cannot set forth is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body on the Sixteenth of October last at his the said William Careings House aforesaid at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said William Careing & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 26th. day of Febry. 1777 before R Butler

Susannah Charlwood
