Middx The Examination of Barbara Sommers  Singlewoman aged about 63 Years Taken the 9th. Day of May 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that about 14 Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Taylor in the Butcher Row in ye. Parish of St. Clement Danes in ye. County of Middx for the Space of Seven Years And this Examinat. further saith that about four Years after she left her sd. Service she was hired to & lived with one Mr. Harvey in Shier Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of One Year & upwards and that she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Harvey Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas Gronous

The Mark of Barbara Sommers
