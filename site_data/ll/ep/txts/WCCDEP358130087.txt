Middx  Elizabeth Barsby aged about Twenty One Years upon Oath Says She never was Married, Says she has been Informed and verily believes that about Thirty two Years ago her Father Willm. Barsby Rented a House, then the Sign of the Rose and Crown the Corner of Dartmouth Street in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds and Lived therein upwards of Twelve Years, Says She has never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn the 29th. Day of Novr. 1755 -before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth. Barsby
