Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Bettyman aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that about Eight years ago she Intermarried with Henry Harman Bettyman her late Husband who has been dead about Two years at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields That her said late Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Vanheson in Rose Street in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly wages  of Eighteen Guineas that after he quitted said service he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 29th day of Decr. 1797 before us N Bond J Hunter

her Mary Bettyman Mark

Witness B Newman
