Middx & Westmr. }  Thomas Jones aged about Twenty Years on his Oath Says he never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Roberts who keeps the Union Coffee house in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted his said Service about Twelve Months ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. day of March 1770 before me} Tho Fellows

Thomas Jones
