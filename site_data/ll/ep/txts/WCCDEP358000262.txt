Saint Clement Danes, 28th March 1786.

Middlesex to wit, Sarah Winter , aged between nineteen and twenty Years, Singlewoman on her Oath Saith That She never was married, Says that She lived as a Yearly hired Servant with Mr Johnson a Sadler at Croydon in the County of Surry at the Yearly Wages of £3. that She quitted his Service about four Years ago, having lived with him in manner aforesaid for upwards of twelvemonth that since quitting her said Service She has not done any Act, Matter or thing whereby to gain any other Settlement - And further this Examinant, on her Oath saith that on the twenty second Day of January now last past, She was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Bricklay in Nags head Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which said Child has Since been Christned by the Name of Ann Sheridine and that Joseph Sheridine of Greenwich in the County of Kent  Taylor , and who lately worked with Mr. Burn of Crumbs Hill Greenwich aforesaid Taylor, the real and true father of the said Child, he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of April now last past and at several times since, at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child of which She was so delivered as aforesaid

The Running Taylor at Greenwich

Sworn this 28th March 1786 before R Butler

her Sarah Winter Mark
