Middlesex  Eleanor Pretty the widow of William Pretty deced maketh oath That her said late husband was by birth an Irishman & to her knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England or Wales That divers years ago & previous to her Marriage with her said late husband She was lawfully married to one John Deedy Since deced who in his life time rented a house of Ten Pounds a year in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement Nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself Save What She acquired by marriage with her said late husband Pretty

Sworn this 19 day of December 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

Ellenor Pretty
