Westmr. to wit Judith Bury on the behalf of William Slumbers an Infant Aged about Eight Years on her Oath Says that he is the Son of James and Mary Slumbers who on her Oath Says were Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and who are now both deceased and further says that the said William Slumbers the Infant was born in the sd Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the said County of Middlesex and that she can give us further or other Account of her of the Settlement of the Father of the said Infant nor of the sd. Infant otherwise than that he was born in the sd. Parish of St. Giles in the fields

Sworn this 17th. day of Septr. 1773 before R Butler

Judith Bury
