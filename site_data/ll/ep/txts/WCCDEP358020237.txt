St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit John Lockton now a Pensioner in the Greenwich Hospital upon his Oath saith that he was married to Jane his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London about 26 Years ago, that he has one Child by his said Wife namely Sarah aged twelve Eight Years or thereabouts who never has acquired any Settlement in her own Right And this Examinant further Saith that sometime ago and whilst he was single and unmarried he lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Thomas Lancaster who kept the Queens Head Public House in Dark house Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary at hill in the City of London for upwards of one Year that since Quitting his said Service he has not done any other Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his

Knowledge he may have gained or acquired any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th Day of December 1790 before R Butler D Walker}

John Lochton
