Middx  Sarah Fenn  Singlewoman Aged about Thirty Nine Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed that upon the Second Day of February 1754 She was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Pitkin in Houghton Street Claremarkett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Elizth. that One Robert Mason who is since Dead had Carnall knowledge of this Examinant Body Severall times within the Space of two Years of One of which times he got her with Child of the said Female Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the 9th of June 1764 : Before} Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Fenn

Upon Mr Hundsdon Complt J. W
