Middlesex ss}  Mary Tucker the wife of William Tucker maketh oath That her said husband is now absent from her & on board the Ship Conquest adore That he has informed her & Which She believes to be true That he was duly bound Apprentice by Indres to John Buckle of the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate London  Sawyer & Served him there for about the Space of two years & then his Said Master Assigned and Turned him over to William Hadley of Bridgewater Gardens in the same Parish Sawyer with whom he Served about three years more of his Said Apprenticeship there & then his Indres were by Mutual Consent of himself & his said Second Master delivered up for the purpose of being Cancelled & he was discharged from his Said Apprenticeship & at liberty to go Where so ever he pleased his Said Master Hadley by reasons of Misfortunes being of in ability to keep him any longer That to this Deponts knowledge or belief her said husband never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office or ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subseqt to his Apprenticeship That She hath two Children namely Mary Aged about two Years & half & Nancy aged about Seven Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 14. of August 1778 before  Jno Sherwood  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Mary Tucker
