Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of John Rogers an Infant aged about Ten years Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said John Rogers in the illegitimate Child of one Ann Cole deceased and that he was born at the house of a Mr. Elkins No. 9 King Street Bloomsbury in the parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this Seventh day of May 1796 Th. Mathew J lend

Jas Talboys
