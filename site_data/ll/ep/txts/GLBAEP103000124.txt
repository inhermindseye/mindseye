Middlesex, to wit, Thomas Spittle , Son of Samuel Spittle , maketh Oath, that about Twenty Five Years ago his said Father lived in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and gained a Settlement there by renting a house of Ten Pounds a year and upwards as this Deponent verily believes, that he this Deponent lived with his said Father as part of his Family and worked with his said Father in his business of a Blacksmith till he was Seventeen Years of Age and upwards that he has never rented any house or lodging of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards, paid any Poor's Rate or other Rates served any Parish Office lived as a yearly hired servant, or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement, that he has a wife living named Margaret to whom he was lawfully married on or about the Seventeenth Day of September 1758 . at Stoke Church near Portsmouth in the County of Hants by whom he has issue living Four Children, namely Samuel aged Twenty Three Years, and Thomas aged Twenty One Years, who are emancipated from him, and Mary aged Fifteen Years, and John aged Thirteen Years, who have gained no Settlement in their own right

Sworn this day of Feby. 1790. before}
