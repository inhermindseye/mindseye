Middx  Elizabeth Jordan aged about Twenty two years upon Oath saith She was never Marryed and that She lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Cole a Brewer in the Parish of Twickenham in the County of Middx for one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of Six pounds and hath Quitted her said Service about Eight Months and further upon her voluntary Oath saith that on the 28th. day of Febry last She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. Pyott near the Angel Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex and which hath since been baptized Elizabeth Says that one William Lloyd a Soldier in the Oxford Blows is the reputed Father of the said Female Child he the said William Lloyd having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant the latter end of the month of May last or beginning of June in the year 1754 last at the Sign of the Sun in Hounslow and at several times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Child She was delivered of as aforesaid And that the sd. Wm. Lloyd is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 30 day of Augst 1755 before} Theodr. Sydenham  Barth: Hammond

The mark of Elizabeth Jordan
