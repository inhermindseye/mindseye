Middx  Ann Gates  Wido . of Daniel Gates upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the said Daniel Gates at Mayfair Chapple upwds. of 4 Years since that her sd. husband dyed about Seven months ago and that she has one Child by the sd. Daniel Gates viz Geo Godfrey Gates aged about Two Years Says her sd. late husband Lived in and Rented a house near the New Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £25 for Nine months or thereabts. and quitted the same abt. two Yrs. ago since which time her sd. late husband has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 12th. 1749 before}

Ann Gates
