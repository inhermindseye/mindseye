Middx.  Isaac Henn aged abt. 28 Yrs. upon Oath says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the fleet London abt. Nine Years ago, says abt. 5 Years before his Intermarriage with the said Mary he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Robins at the Black lyon the back of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 17th. day of April 1758 before} Fran: Bedwell

Isaac Henn
