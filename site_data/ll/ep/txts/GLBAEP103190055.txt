Middlesex ss}  Ann Treague the wife of James Treague maketh oath that her Sd. husband is now absent from her at Sea in the Kings Service That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served his Apprenticeship to a Hatter in the  City of Execeter in the County of Devon but the name of the Master & the Particular Parish there She Cannot now recollect or remember & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She hath a Child named James aged about Two Years & half the lawful Issue of this Depont by her Said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this 15 day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Treague
