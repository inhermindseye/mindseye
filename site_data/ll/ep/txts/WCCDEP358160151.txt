Middx  The Examination of Mary Pellingham  Singlewoman aged 54 years & upwds. Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 12th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. 6 Months ago she lived as a yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Grigg in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Carpenter for the space of 3 years at the yearly Wages of £4 And this Examint. further saith yt. she hath not since yt. time Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she is now Poor & Lame & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & year above menconed before}
