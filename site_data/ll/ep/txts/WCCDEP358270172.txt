Sarah Hill Child not Named

St Clement Danes Jany Feby 11. 1794

Middx and Westminster }  Mother's settlemt St Martin in the fields  Child's birth  Reputed Father  Sarah Hill  singlewoman aged 23 & upwds Upon Oath saith that she never was married, that her Father Rented and resided in a House called and known by the Name of the Globe Public House Hungerford Market , in the parish of Saint Martins in the fields for upwards of 5 Years, About 7 Years Ago, but at what Rent this deponent cannot correctly say, but verily believes about 30 pounds a Year, that this Examinant hath not since done any Act Matter or Thing whatsoever whereby to the  best of her knowledge Recollection or belief she might obtain any subsequent legal settlement, And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that on or about the 24th day of Novr. last at the House of Mr Barker No 4 Clare Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the LIberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which is not yet Christened but is become chargeable to the said parish, that Thomas Wigg of Howards Coffee house  Dukes place is the only Real & true Father of the said Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid he and no one else havg had carnal knowledge of this deponents body that on or about the Middle of February last 1793 at which time the said Thomas Wigg begot the said Child thereon on this deponents body

Sworn this 11th day of Feby 1794 } Wm. Kitchiner

Sarah Hill
