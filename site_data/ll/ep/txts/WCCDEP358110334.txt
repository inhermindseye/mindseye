Westmr.  Mary Whitby on behalf of Thomas Bunting  her Son aged about 13 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with her first husband Wm Bunting (the father of the sd. Thoms.) at the Fleet London abt. 19 Yrs. ago & that her sd. late husband dyed about 11 Years since And this Examt. further maketh Oath that the sd. Wm. Bunting rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Guineas in Mill Court in Peter Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Midelesex & lived therein sevl. Years & afterwds. rented a house of thirteen pounds by the year in New Inn Passage in the same Parish lived therein about Six Months & never after he quitted the last mentioned house (nor did this Examinant whilst she continued a Wido.) rented ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn Octr. 20th 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Whitby
