Middlesex ss}  Thomas Clarke maketh oath that about thirty years ago as he now best recollects he this Deponent hired in & rented a house or Tenement in Norris's Court in the Parish of st John Wapping in the Sd County & during such his residence therein which was for about the space of three years & half he this Deponent was charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Kings Taxes for the same That he has not Since rented a house lodging or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That about twenty two years ago & since his leaving the said house he this Deponent was passed by a Pass order from the Parish of st Giles Cripplegate London to the said parish of st John Wapping as being the place of his last legal Settlement against which order no objection was ever made to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent

Sworn this - 12. day of April 1787 before  R Davies  Rob Smith

The Mark of Thomas Clarke
