Middlesex to wit Jno. Haynes Aged about 33 Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or payed Taxes Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with One Henry Lewis a Printer in Salisbury Street Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any Other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly servant for One Year.

Sworn this 9th. day of May 1782 before  Sampn Wright  Wm Kitchiner

John Haynes
