Westmr.  Jas. Evans upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Mary Evans upwds. of 20 Yrs. age in the Parish Church of St.  Alhallows on London Wall upwards of 20 Years ago and that upwds. of 12 Yrs. since he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £28 in New Inn Passage in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year & upwards and has not since he Quitted the sd. house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 9th. April 1748 before} Wm: Jones

James Evans
