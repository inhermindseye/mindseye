Middx  Eliz; Maxwell aged about 60 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of James Maxwell who dyed abt. Twenty five Years since and that after the decease of her sd. husband she rented a house of the yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds in Plough Court in Carey Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes and lived therein upwds. of one Year and has not since she quitted the sd house which is abt. 8 yrs. ago rented ten Pounds a year pd. Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn March 27th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz. Maxwell
