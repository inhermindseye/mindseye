Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr} to wit Elizabeth Vangheyn aged about 66 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired Yearly Servant for with Mrs. Stevens in East Street in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex for Seven Years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Twelve Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th day of Octr. 1795  W Kinnard N Bond

her Elizth. Vangheyn Mark
