Middx  Barnaby Gillmore upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Jno: Mortimer a Perukemaker and after having Served him four years & about Six Months his sd. Master absconded & this Examinant was discharged from his Indenture by the Quarter Session whereupon he hired himself to live with Mr Knight a Perukemaker for the remaining two Yrs. and Six months unexpired of his first Indenture and lod all that time in the sd. Mr Knights house opposite Surry Street in the Strand in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and had for his sd. Service by Agreement Six pounds dyet washing & Lodging Says he never (since he left Mr Knights Service) rented ten pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Feby. 23d. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Barnaby Gillmore
