Middx  Anna Clarke aged about 50 Years Lodging in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx upon Oath saith she is a Widow and that since the Death of her Husband she this Examinant and her Mother  Mary Raddon (being then in Copartnership) lived in and rented a House near the Armitage Bridge in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middlesex for about Five years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds Quitted the Same at Christmas 1755 since which time neither this Examinant or the said Mary Raddon her Mother have Rented any House or paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate.

Sworn the 8th. day of January 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Anna Clark
