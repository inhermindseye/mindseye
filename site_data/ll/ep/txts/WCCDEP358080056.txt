Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Gregory aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Daniel Stebbings a Tripeman in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds hath quitted her said Service about five Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that one John Nosier a Journey Butcher who now works with one Mr. Ambridge a Hog Butcher in Woods Close and who Uses the Cheshire Cheese in Woods Close afsd. is the Father there of he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body the 29th. day of August 1774 and at Sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Childen of which she is now pregnant & that the said John Nosier and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of May 1776 before M Addington

her Mary Gregory Mark
