Middlesex ss}  Isabella Fraser the widow of James Fraser  deced maketh oath that Some time ago & Since her Said late husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages by & with Abraham Figtree of Lower Eastsmithfield in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Victualler & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been Married nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13 day of November 1776 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Isabella Fraser
