Middlesex & Westminster}  Elizabeth Burt aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with the Revd. Mr. White adjoining to Bridewell in the precinct of Bridewell London upwards of four Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the Seconed day of August last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Roger Rock in Bear Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . which hath since been baptized by the name of Thomas and is become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same And that one Michael Boyd a Footman to her said late Master and who now lives with one Doctr. Warren at Hammersmith in the same Capacity is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House near Bridewell aforesaid on the fifth day of November 1775 and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Michael Boyd and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 4th. day of Septr. 1776 before M Addington.

Elizabeth Burt
