Middlesex ss}  Ann Hargat maketh oath that divers Years ago She was Married to one John Hargat who was by birth an Irishman as She has been informd & believes & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England that about three Years ago he went to Sea & she has not Since Seen him nor does She know Whether he is now dead or living That She this Depont before her Sd. Marriage was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr Danson & Inn Keeper in Wakefield in the County of York & Continued there at Certain Wages under such hiring for the Space of five years & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or done any Act to Acquire a Subseqt Settlement Save What she has gained by her Marriage as aforesd.

Sworn this 15 day of July 1784 before}

The Mark of Ann Hargat
