Westmr.  John Derenancour upon Oath saith he Rented a house in Castle Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Forty pounds and lived therein about Six Years and quitted the same in the year 1724 and has not since rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 5th Janry. 1749 before} Wm. Fielding

John Derenancour
