Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Damaris Hammerton aged about 58 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Ross at Islington in the Parish of Saint Mary at Islington in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Fourteen years since which him she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1798 before N Bond E Read

her Damaris Hammerton mark

Witness B Newman
