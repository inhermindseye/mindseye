Westmr. to wit Mary Street aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Widow Smith who keeps a House in Bear Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service about three Weeks ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Abraham Francis a Journeyman Butcher who she believes now lives at Rumford in Essex is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of March last but the particular day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Abraham Francis and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 7th. day of Novr. 1772 before} 

Mary Street
