Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Highliant  Singlewoman Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father James Highliant served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Burn a Perukemaker in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . as her said Mother has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and further says she never lived a hired Yearly Servant in any place whatever or knows no further or any otherwise of her Settlement than as before mentioned.

Sworn this 13th. day of April 1772 before} R. Butler 

her Elizabeth Highliant mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
