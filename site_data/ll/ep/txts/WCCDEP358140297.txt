Middx.  Hannah Hawkins aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Thos. Coles in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. and hath to done for two Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds Ten Shillings. and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born says that the above named Thos. Coles did beget the said Bastard Child or Childn. on her Body he having had Carnal know- -ledge of her Body sometime in the latter End of June last or beginning of July following and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 8th. of Octr. 1759 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Hanh. Hawkins
