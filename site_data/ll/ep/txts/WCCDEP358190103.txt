Middx  Ann Wheat aged about 22 Years liveing in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon Oath says that on the Tenth day of November 1759 she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Drawater a Stone Mason in Great Castle Street in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Philip Cooke , Says she is poor and not able to Maintain the Same.

Sworn the 23d. day of Febry 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant  Barth. Hammond

Ann Wheat

Upon Mr. Kirks Complaint
