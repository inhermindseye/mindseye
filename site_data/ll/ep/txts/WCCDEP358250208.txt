Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Jane Cole an Infant aged about Twelve Years maketh Oath and saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that Thomas Cole the father of the said Jane Cole Rented and lived in a house in Kent Street in the Parish of Saint George Southwark in the County of Survey for Three Months and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and that after he quitted said house which is about Six Years he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or did any Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 25th. day of Novr. 1795  J Hind E Read

James Talboys
