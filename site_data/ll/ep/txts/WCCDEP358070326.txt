Middlesex & Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Leech Aged about Twenty Three years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. John Waite at the King of Prussia's Head in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One year and an half at the yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about 13 Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to pay the same when born And that her said Master John Waite is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at different times of Two years last past or thereabouts at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Waite and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1775 . before me M Addington

her Elizth. Leech Mark
