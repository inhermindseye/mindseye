Middx.  Sarah Bunn aged abt. Twenty three years upon Oath says she was bound Apprentice to Mrs. Ogle a Mantuamaker (at Mr. Robinson's a Taylor in fountain Court) by Mr. Bowden Clerk to the Governors of Christ's hospital London and lived with said Mrs. Ogle about Ten Months and then Mrs. Ogle went to Jamaica; says she was afterwards bound Apprentice by said Mr. Bowden to Mrs. Barrow a Landress in little Mortimer Street in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bon for three years but never lived with Mrs. Barrow; says at the time she was bound to Mrs. Barrow she was a hired yearly Servant to Mr. Marter in Bond Street in the Parish of St. George Hanr. Square with whom she lived abt. Six Months with the Consent of Mrs. Barrow; and has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one year; says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born; says that One Michael Merryman who is now

now an Apprentice to one Mr. Lovell a Stone Mason in little Mortimer Street had Carnal knowledge of her Body at the house of Mrs. Smith in same Street the latter End of the Month of Sepr. or beginning of Octr. 1758. and at several other times since at one of which Times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Michl. Merryman and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 9th. June 1759 before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Bunn

Upon the Complaint of Antho. Wilkinson Overseer of the Poor J: P:
