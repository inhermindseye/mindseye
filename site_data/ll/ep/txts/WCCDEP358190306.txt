Middx  Mary Arnell aged about 22 Years upon Oath says She was never Married That She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Biggs in Bridge Street in the Parish of St. Margaret Westminster upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath quitted her said Service about Three Years and hath not since rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year That She has One Child named Charles aged about Eleven Months

Sworn the 25th. day of Novr. 1762 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Arnell
