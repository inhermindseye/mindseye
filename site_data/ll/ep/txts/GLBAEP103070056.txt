Middx ss  Mary Rickards  Widow Maketh oath that abt. twelve Years ago her late Son William Ricards deced lives a hired Servant by the Year (being then a Single & Unmarried Man) with one Mr. Daniel Brown a Farmer who then lived in the Parish or Town of Raydon in the County of Essex at the Yearly Wages of five pounds a Year and lived in Such Service for abt. the Spare of three Years and then this Depts. sd. Son Married his late wife Sarah also lately Deced by whom he had issue Now living three Children Namely William Aged abt. Eight Years John aged abt. Six Years and Sarah aged about six three Years the lawfull issue of this Depts. sd. late Son And this Dept. Says that her sd. Son after his Servitude at aforesd. did not rent any house of ten pounds by the year Nor Serve any Parish offices nor gain any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 21 day of Janry. 1752 before us  Richd. Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

his Mary Rickards  Mark
