Westmr  John Holyoak Aged about fifty Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with his late Wife  Margt . at Weston in the County of Gloucester about Twenty Eight Years ago and who who has been dead about Three Years Says he was bound an Apprentice to Joseph Elson a Taylor at preston on Stower in the County of Gloucester but this Examinants Master being a Certificated he cod. gain no Settlement at Preston on Stour And further says that he was born at Chipston on Stour in the said County of Gloucester and never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House or Tenemt. of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1778 before me  Abrm Baley

John Holyoake
