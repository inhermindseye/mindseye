Westmr.  Jane How Aged upwards of Fifty Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Robert How who has been dead about Eight Years, says her said Husband Rented a House in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of Sixteen Pounds a Year, lived therein about two Years, quitted the same about a Year before his death and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes neither hath she since her said Husbands Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 7th. day of December 1767 before} Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Jane How
