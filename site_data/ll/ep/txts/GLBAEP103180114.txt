Middlesex ss}  Catherine Smith the wife of William Smith  Mariner maketh oath That her Said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true That he was born in the Parish of St James Spithead near Portsmouth in the County of Southampton And this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting her Said husband Settlement That She hath a Daughter named Jeremiah  aged about Eleven years the Issue of this Depont. by her Said husband to Whom She was lawfully Married

Sworn this 23d day of December 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Catherine Smith
