Middlesex ss}  Rachael Kebbell the widow of George Kebbell deced maketh oath That about three years ago & Since her Said husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mr Decosta a Tallow Chandler in Bevis Marks in the Parish of St James in Dukes Place in the City of London for the Space of one year & Fortnight & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there She this Deponent during Such her Servitude as aforesaid having no Child or Children then living That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or been married or done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16 day of October 1776 before}

The Mark of Rachael Kebbell
