Middx  Ann Whittingham aged abt. 43 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Henry Bendish  Esqr . of Bedford Row  Holborn for two Years & upwards at the yearly wages of Seven pounds and that during her sd. Service she went with Mr Bendish and the family to Thamesford in Bedfordshire and Continued there about Six months, that she had warning given her during their Stay at Thamesford and was discharged from Mr. Bendish's Service & pd. the money then due to her (being one Years wages) at Thamesford aforesaid that at the time of her being pd. of & Discharged her Service as afsd. her Master's Sister desired her to Continue with them, till they went to Yarmouth (which they intended in a few Days) and promised to Satisfie this Examint. for her time after which she Stayed with them about two Months & had for her trouble a Guinea from Miss Bendish and half a Guinea from her Master

Sworn Janry. 23d. 1753 : Before} Fran: Bedwell

Anne Whittingham
