Middlesex & Westmr. to wit Thos. Pierce Aged about Sixty Seven Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at May Fair Chapel in the Year 1750 and by whom he hath one Child living which is now provided for says that before his Intermarriage to his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with His Grace the Duke of Montague in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent for one year at the Yearly Wages of Twenty one pounds P Annum hath quitted his said Service in the year 1750 and after quitting the same he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of July 1778 before R. Butler Jno. Machin

Thos. Pierce
