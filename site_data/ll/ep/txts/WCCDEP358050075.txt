Westmr.  Mary Brown aged 53 Years upon Oath Saith she intermarried with John Brown in Lincolns Inn Chapel about 25 Years ago and that her sd. husband Sailed for Newfoundland upwards of four Years ago and she has not since heard from him that the said John Brown in the Year 1739 rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds in Kirby Street  Hatton Garden in the Liberty of Saffron hill  Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the Parish of Saint Andrew in Holborn in the County of Middlesex and lived therein three months or thereabouts and that her sd husband after he quitted the sd. house did not rente Ten pounds a Year pay Parish Taxes or otherwise gain a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 20th: 1749 before H Frilding

Mary Brown
