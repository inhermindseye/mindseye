Middx  Margt. Ells aged about 24 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Henry Bucks  Chair & Cabinet maker in St. Pauls Church Yard in the Parish of St. Gregory by St. Paul London at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds and ten Shillings for the Space of one Year and upwards and hath left her sd Service upwards of two Years Says she hath not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 10th. Decr. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Margt. Ells
