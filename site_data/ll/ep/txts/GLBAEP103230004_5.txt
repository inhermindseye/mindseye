Middlesex ss}  Job Clay maketh oath That he Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of his late Father & Mother Thomas and Elizabeth Clay deced Save that he has received information from them & which he believes to be true that about fifty four years ago they lived in and rented a house known by the Sign of the Red Lyon Alehouse in the Parish of Bow near Bromley in the County of Middlesex where this Deponent about fifty four years ago was born as he has also been in formed & believes That he this Depont was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right

Sworn this- 17. day of Janry 1787 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Job Clay
