Middx to wit James Witts aged about Twenty Six years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Hannah his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Two years ago and by whom he hath one Child living vizt. James aged about Three years born a Bastard in the Parish of St. Pancras Says he served seven years Apprenticeship to one Arthur Lake a Copper Plate Printer in Goodge Street in the Parish of St. Pancras in the County of Middx and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10 in year or or.wise howsoever or gained any or. Settlement than as aforementioned

Sworn this 17th. day of March 1784  Sampn Wright Thos: Gilbert .

James Witts
