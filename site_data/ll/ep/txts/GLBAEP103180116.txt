Middlesex ss}  Susannah Brady a Singlewoman Aged about nineteen years maketh oath That She has been informed by Mr Samuel Batman  Vestry Clerk of the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the Sd. County from a Certain Writing which he read to this Deponent out of a Book & which he told her was an Examination of her Father John Brady which had been taken from him respecting the Settlement of himself & Family That She this Deponents Parents were both Natives of Ireland & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England and that She this Deponent was born at Little Tower Hill but did not mention or refer in Particular to any Parish whatever And this Deponent Saith that She can give other Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or the Place of her Birth than as before mentioned from the Writing read to her by the said Mr Batman That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year in any one Place nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right That She this Depont togethr With her Brother & Sister as She has been informed & believes When in their infancy were all found in a house in the Said parish of Saint George Hanover Square and afterwards Put out to Nurse by the Overseers of the Poor of the same Parish and this Deponents Brother and Sister were also afterwards Put out Apprentice by the Parish officers there and this Deponent have ever Since When She wanted relief been received

into the Workhouse of the Said Parish & there provided for as being Settled in the Same & where this Deponent allways Apprehended She was Settled.

Sworn this 9th day of Janry 1779 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Susannah Brady
