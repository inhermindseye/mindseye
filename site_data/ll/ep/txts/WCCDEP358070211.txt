Westr:  William Newman Aged about forty two Years on his Oath says that he Intermarried with Jane his Wife at Shoreditch Church about 14 Years Ago by whom he hath one Child now living Named Mathew aged about Eighteen Months says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr Lewis a Copper Plate Printer in Durham Yard in the Parish of St Martins in the fields in the County of Middlesex after he was out of his said time Apprenticeship he never rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d day of Sepr 1774 before  Sampn Wright  Jno. Barnfather

William Newman

Paid 2 Shillings
