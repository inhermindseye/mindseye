Westmr.  Isaac Crackinthorpe aged about 53 Years upon Oath says he Rented and lived in a house known by the Sign of the Crown opposite Villiers Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for three Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds, quitted the same about Christmas 1760 and hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes

Sworn the 12th. April 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran. Cary

Isaac Crackinthorp
