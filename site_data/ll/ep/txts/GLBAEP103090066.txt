Middlesex ss.  Philip Davis maketh Oath that he never was informed or can he in any manner set forth the place or places of Settlement of his late Parents or either of them But this deponent saith that he has been informed by his late Mother deceased that he this Deponent was born in the Parish of Malmsbury in the County of Wiltshire about Forty six Years ago the which Information this deponent verily believe to be true And this Deponent farther saith that he never was bound Apprentice or ever lived as a hired Servant for a year or ever served any Parish Offices or paid any poors Rates or Kings Taxes or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or ever did any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in his own right And that he has a Wife named Mary

Sworn this 18th day of June 1767 before  Chris: Scott

Philip Davis His Mark
