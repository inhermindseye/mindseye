Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Lacey  Singlewoman maketh oath that She has been informed by her Mothr Martha Lacey & which She believes to be true that her late Father William Lacey deced Several years ago rented a house of the yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere to her knowledge or belief That She this Depont was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 22d. day of Novemr 1783 before J. Hawley

Elis: Lacy
