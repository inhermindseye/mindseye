Middx  Ann Robertson  Wido . of Geo: Robertson upon her Oath saith that about one Year & an half since she lived with Mrs. Burrows in Chichesters Rents in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year at the Yearly Wages of £4. being at that time a Wido. without Child or Children that since she left the sd. Mrs. Burrows Service she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or other- wise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 15th. April 1743 before} C. Egerton

The Mark of Ann Robertson
