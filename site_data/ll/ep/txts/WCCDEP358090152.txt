Middx  Martha Simcock Aged about Sixty Years On her Oath says She is the Widow of Matthew Simcock who died about Eleven Years ago & with whom She Intermarried at the Fleet London about forty Years ago And by whom She hath no Child or Children living says her said late Husbands Father Rented and lived in a House in Leg Alley Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds as She believes and paid Parish Taxes due for the same And further says that her said late Husband served his Apprenticeship to his said Father And after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 22nd. day of Augt. 1780 before R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

her Martha Simcock Mark
