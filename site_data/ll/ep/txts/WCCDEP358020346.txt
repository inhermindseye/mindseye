Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Catherine Jiles aged about 24 Years Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married That she was Born in Chamber where her Father and Mother Rented and lived in a House in Chamber Street where Goodman Fields in the parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex where she this Examinant was Born That her said Father and Mother has been Dead upwards of 6 Years That soon after their Death she this Examinant was taken by as a Servant by a Mr. Ridley in Chamber Street aforesaid in the said Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel and lived there upwards of 4 Years but was not hired as a Yearly Servant as she this Examinant was generally said Weekly And further Saith that since the quitted said Service she has Lodged in the  Wych Street in the said parish of Saint Clement Danes And further Saith that she never lived as a hired Yearly servant for One Year  Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10. a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge information or belief,

Sworn this 19th. Octr. 1791 - Edw: Read  Wm Hyde

Catherine her Mark Jiles
