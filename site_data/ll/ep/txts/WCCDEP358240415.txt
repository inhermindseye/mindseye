St Clement Danes 23d May 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Clement Danes  (to wit) Catherine Byron aged 60 years and upwards the Wife of John Byron aged about seventy seven years (who is now ill and confined to his Room) Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said John Bynon at the Parish Church of St. George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex in or about the Month of August 1753 (by Banns) that he Rented and resided in a House in Bennetts Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex  for ten years and upwards at the Annual Rent of Ten pounds that he quitted the same about Nineteen years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement in the best of her this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn this 23d day of 1794 before me } Wm Kitchiner

C bynon

Witness Jas Talboys
