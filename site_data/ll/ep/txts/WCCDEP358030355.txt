Westmr. 

Jane Hersey  Wife of Richard Hersey (a Soldier in his Majesty's Service) upon Oath saith she inter married with the said Richard Hersey at the Parish church of St. Bennett Pauls wharf about 14 years ago Says her sd. husband lived in & rented the Crasen head in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of £40 Pounds for Six Months & upwds. and left the same upwards of Six years ago Says her sd. husband has not since he quitted the said house rented ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes  or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn March 11th. 1746 before  Tho: Burdus

Jane Hersey
