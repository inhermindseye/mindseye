Westmr.  Henry Harris upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Mary at the Fleet London about 3 Years ago Says he Served four Years or thereabouts the first Part of his Apprentiship with his Father John Harris in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex & then his father failed and this Examinant never was assigned over to any other Person rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 29th. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

Henry Harris
