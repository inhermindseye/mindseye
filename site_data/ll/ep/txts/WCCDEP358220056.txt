Middx to wit Elizabeth Hussey Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever rented House Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says she has often been Informed by her Father and which Information she verily believes to be true that he served seven Years Apprenticeship with one William Hoare a Glazier in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one James Glanville a Journeyman Upholsterer who lately lived with one Mr. Ward an Uplosterer in High Street  Oxford had carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of February or March last but the Particular Month or time she cannot recollect But says that the said James Glanville had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at several other subsequent times afterwards at one of which times he Got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Glanville and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this Eleventh day of August 1769 before me} Tho Kynaston

The Mark of Elizth. Hussey
