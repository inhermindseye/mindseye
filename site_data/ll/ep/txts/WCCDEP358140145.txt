Middx.  Eliz: Brown aged abt. 58 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Wm. Brown who has been dead abt. four Years says she intermarried with the said Wm. Brown on or abt. the 24th. day of April 1751. by whom she has one Child named Ann aged abt. five Years born in St. Martin's Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields says her said husband informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that he was born in Jamaica in the West Indies and never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year says before her intermarriage with the said Wm. Brown She (being at that time a Widow) Rented a house in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and lived therein upwards of three Years

Years and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 21st. of July 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Eliz Brown
