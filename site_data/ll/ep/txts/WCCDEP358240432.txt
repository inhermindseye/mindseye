St.. Clement Danes August.. 15th. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement at Epsom  (to wit) Sarah Fairbrother  singlewoman aged 29 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she never lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together or Otherwise obtained any legal Settlement in her own right, that she is informed and verily believes she is the legitimate Daughter of Charles and Elizabeth Fairbrother by Elizabeth his late Wife deceased, that she has been informed by her said father and very believes (having no reason to think to the contrary) that her said father is legally settled as a Parish coner of the said  Parish of Epsom in the County of Surrey by living as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Steward at Horton within the said parish  while he was single and unmarried 

Sworn the 15th day of August 1794 before us} N Bond Tho Vaughn

Sarah Fairbrother 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
