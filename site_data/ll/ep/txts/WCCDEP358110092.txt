Middx  Hannah Palmer aged abt. 27 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that for thirteen months past she hath lived (& still Continug to live) a Yearly Servant with Mr. Mercer in New Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that Timothy Madding a Taylor did beget the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. Timothy Madding having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant several times in the months of January & February last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. Timothy Madding & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Septr 5th. 1752 before W Fielding

Hannah Palmer
