Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Belbin aged about 15 years upon her Oath saith that she was bound an Apprentice to one James Smeaton of Upper John Street in the parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Seven years that af she served about Seven weeks of her said Apprenticeship her said Master moved from upper John Street aforesaid to North Street in the said Parish of Saint Pancras where she lived about Five Months that then her said Master has refused to maintain her

Sworn this 4th. day of May 1789 R Butler D Walker

her Ann Belbin mark
