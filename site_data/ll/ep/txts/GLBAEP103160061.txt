Middlesex ss}  John Orton maketh oath That in the year 1744 he was passed from the Hamlet of Mile End new Town to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County as to the place of his last legal settlement in right of his Fathers Settlement being there as this Deponent has been informed and believes by renting a house of Ten Pounds a year & upwards in the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate & paying Poors Rates for the Same That he this Deponent was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor even rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement for himself

Sworn this 15 day of May-1776 before Park Rowlands

The Mark of John Orton
