St Clement Danes 16th August 1792

Middx and Westminster }  The Pauper bwt. back & never to the psh. of St Ann Vide Examt Book No: 25 Page 9 which was alse wrong She was there removed third time Under this Examt. entered page Gother Book  (to wit) Ann Higgins aged 23 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she has been informed and verily believes, that her Mother Rented and Resided in a House in Angel Street in the parish of St Martin le Grand in the City of London for several Years at the Annual Rent of £16 and £12 and paid the poors and quitted the same about the twelve Years ago and that her said Father and Mother were Married at the Fleet Prison London above Forty years ago according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of England that this Deponents Father Robert Higgins Died about Eighteen Years ago and that her said Mother Susanna hath not since been Married neither hath she, since she quitted the said House in Angel Street in the parish of St Martin le Grand as aforesd. obtained any subsequent legal to the best of he this knowledge information or belief And this Deponent saith she was never bound in Apprentice lived as an hired Yearly Servant for twelve Months together nor hath otherwise obtained any legal Settlement in her Own Right

Sworn the 16th August 1792 before us } Thos. Gordon  Gd: Mitchell

Ann Huggins
