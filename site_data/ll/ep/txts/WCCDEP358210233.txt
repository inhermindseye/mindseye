Westmr.  Sarah Birch Aged about Twenty Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she was bound Apprentice by the Church Wardens and Overseers of the Poor of St. George Hanover Square of Robert Launder an Attorney at Law in little Wild Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and served about Four Years of her said Apprenticeship in little Wild Street aforesaid, and then her Master removed and went to live in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster where this Examinant served about Three Years on one of her said Apprenticeship, and then she and her said Master parted by Consent, says she never was assigned over to any other Person, nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 25th. day of January 1768 before} 

Sarah Birch
