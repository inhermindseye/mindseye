Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Gruggen  widow maketh oath That She hath a Niece named Henrietta Howard the Daughter of this Deponents Brother William Howard deced and this Depo has been Credibly informed & which She believes to be true That her said late deced Brother was at the time of his death lawfully Settled in the Parish of Armdell in the County of Sussex That this Deponents said Niece to the Knowledge or belief of this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any any act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 17. day of September 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

Eliz Gruggen
