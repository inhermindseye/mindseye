Middx.  John Scarlett aged abt. 47 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his Wife at Oxford Chapel in Oxford Road abt. Nineteen Years ago says he Rented a house in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds lived therein abt. Seven Months and quitted it abt. Two Years and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 25th. of Augst. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

John Scarlett
