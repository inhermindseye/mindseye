Middx  The Examination of Mary Hoff aged about 64 Years liveing in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 21st- Day of February 1740 Who upon her Oath Saith that She is a Singlewoman & was never married & that about 7 Years ago She lived as a yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Charles Compton at the Corner of Earles Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for the Space of nineteen Years or Thereabts. at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & further on her Oath saith that since She left her said Service She never rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for ye. space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Cha: Egerton

Mary Hoff
