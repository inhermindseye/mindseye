Middlesex ss}  John Daly maketh oath that as hath a Child named Eleanor Daly Aged about Seven years which Child was born in Brights Alley in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the Said County and was there baptized and registered the which Said Child is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason that he this Deponent was never married to the Said Childs mothers who has been dead about one year and a Quarter of a year

Sworn this. 19. day of September 1787 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

John Daly
