Middlesex ss}  Ann Goodluck  Singlewoman maketh oath That on the Twenty fifth day of October in the year one thousand Seven hundred & Sixty Seven She this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of George Langley in Lambeth Marsh  in the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry  Shipwright Which Said Bastard Child hath been Since Baptized & registred by the name of Thomas Goodluck in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westminster in the Said County of Middlesex and is become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Same County

Sworn this 29 Day of Oct 1772 before R Pell Saml. Spagg

Ann Goodluck
