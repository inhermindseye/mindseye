St. Clement Danes 30th January 1793

Middlesex and Westmister }  St Giles in the fields  (to wit ) John Carrier aged 40 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he never was Married nor obtained any legal Settlement in his own Right that he hath been informed which he verily believes to be true that he was born in lawful Wedlock that his father Rented and Resided in a House in Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex above twenty years at the annual Rent of £30 or thereabouts that he quitted the same about 12 or 15 years ago and never afterwards Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 30th day of January 1793 before us } Wm. Kitchiner D Walker

John Carrier
