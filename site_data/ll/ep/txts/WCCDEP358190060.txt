Middx.  Joseph Wilson aged abt. 50 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West London abt. 22 Years ago Says he Rented a house in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . within the County of Middlesex for Seven Years at the Yearly Rent £18 for the first Years and £20 for the last Years and hath Quitted said house upwards of Two Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 22d. day of Octr. 1760 before}
