Middlesex ss  Ann Cosgrave maketh oath that about three years & four months ago She this Deponent was delivered of a Child named Joseph Gould in Long alley in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the said County The which Child was born a Bastard by reason that She this Deponent was never married to the Father of the said Child

Sworn this 16 day of February 1788 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

Ann Cosgrave
