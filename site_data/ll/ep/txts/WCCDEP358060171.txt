Middx  Cathn. Tysom aged about 19 Years upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she was delivered of a female bastard Child on the 13th. Day of Septr. Instant in Clements Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex which has been baptized Catherine and is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same and that Mawdley Swift  Apprentice to Mr. Stevens Writing master in St Clements Lane aforesd. did beget the sd. female bastard Child on her body the sd. Mawdley Swift having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the month of December last and at Sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said female bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Mawdley Swift and us other Person in the true and real father thereof

Sworn Octr. 23d. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Katherine Tyson
