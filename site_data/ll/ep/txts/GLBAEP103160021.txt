Middlesex ss}  Sarah Meadows the widow of Matthew Meadows deced maketh oath that she Can give no Account respecting the Settlemt of her said late husband That she this Deponent many years ago and before her marriage with her said husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages by & with one Catherine Cook in the High Street in the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the Said County Widow & Continued in Such Service for the Space of three years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor paid and Parish or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement Save What she has Acquired by Marriage with her Sd late husband whose Settlement is unknown to her.

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith P. Rowlands

The Mark of Sarah Meadows
