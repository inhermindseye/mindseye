Aldgate Middx

Katherine Keys  wife of John Keys a Pipemaker who is now a Soldier bound for Flanders Maketh Oath that he Servd his Apprenticeship to James Webb a Pipemaker in Harts: horn Lane in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the City and Liberty of Westminster where she this Deponant was a Servant And that he workd Journywork with his Said Master when her Said Husband marryed her from thence And that he hath not gaind any other Settlement Since his Servitude hath two Children James Aged 9 years and John Keys aged ten months

Sworn 18th day of March Anno Dom 1747 Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

Cathrine Keays
