Middx  Eliz Glenn aged 26 years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr David Glenn a Taylor in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr in the County of Middx at the yearly Wages of £5-for Seven years or thereabouts and after his decease continued to live a yearly Servt. in the same house with his Nephew Jas. Webster for one year & upwds. at the yearly Wages of £5 that she quitted Mr. Websters Service about two years a gone and has not since rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr. 9th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizabeth Glen
