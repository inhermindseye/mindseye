Middlesex ss  Phebe Stamps the widow of William Uppington deced and now the Wife of John Stamps maketh oath that her said late husband Uppington hath informed her that he was born in the Parish of Bricksworth in the County of Northampton The which information this Deponent believes to be true And this Deponent Saith that to her knowledge information or belief her Said husband was never bound Apprentice Nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year Nor ever rented a house of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement since his birth in his own right And that she hath not done any Act since his Death to Acquire a settlement in her own right Save what she might gain by her Marriage with her now husband John Stamps And this Depont. Cannot in any Wife setforth or discover the settlement of her said late husbands Parents or of either of them And this Deponent farther Saith that she hath two Children namely Francis Uppington Aged Eight Years And William Uppington Aged Seven Years & upwards the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband Uppington Deced.

Sworn this 9th day of March 1768 before  Chris: Scott R Pell
