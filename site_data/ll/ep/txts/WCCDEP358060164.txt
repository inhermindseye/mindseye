Joseph Greathead upon his Oath saith he hath been informed and verily believes that he was born in the Year in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middex and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one Year and that he can give no Account of the Settlemt. of his father or mother

Sworn Sepr. 5th. 1751 before}
