Middx  The Exaicon of Anne Ashwell  Singlewoman lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 29th. day of Janry 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that She was hired to & lived as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Wood in St. Clemts. Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of Five Years & then this Exaiant being Sick was Obleiged to leave her sd. Service & go into St. Bartholomews Hospitall for her Health and further Saith that when she came out of the sd. Hospitall she came & lived as a Yearly hired Servt. with her sd. Mistress for the Space of one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds ten Shillings and hath been Discharged her sd. Service abt. two Months, that She never Rented any House or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beliefe & is Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before} Jas Gronous

Ann Ashwell
