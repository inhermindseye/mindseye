St. Clement Danes.

Benjamin Wingrove , maketh oath & Saith that he hath been informed and which information the believes to be true, That Mary Webb aged One Year or there abouts now in the workhouse of Saint Clement Danes is an illegetimate Child and was born at Mr Holmes's No. 21. Charles. Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields

Sworn this 10. January 1789 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

B Wingrovs
