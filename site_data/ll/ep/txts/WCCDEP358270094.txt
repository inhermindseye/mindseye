Southall Ann desertion

St Clement Danes 12th January 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  Settlement  Desertion (to wit) Ann Southall aged 36 years & upwards Wife of Thomas Southall Upon Oath saith that she was present to her Married to her present Husband Thomas Southall at the parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn on the 5th day of November 1777 (by Banns) that her said Husband then and several years before and since rented and resided in an House in the Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex at the Annual Rent of £38 that he quitted the same about two years ago and hath not since rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief and this Deponent upon her Oath further saith that her said Husband being of Bodily strength and ability negle to Work for and maintain this Deponent his said Wife neglects and refuses so to do where she is become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes being unable to provide for herself

Sworn the 12th day of January 1793 before me} R Clark

Ann Souhall
