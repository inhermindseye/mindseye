Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizth. Self aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that about 20 years ago she Intermarried with George Self her late Husband (who has been dead abot. 3 years) at the Parish Church of St. Mary at Newington in the Coy. of Surry and by whom she hath 4 Children livg. 3 of which are provided for & 1 unprovided for vizt. Margt . aged about 8 years that her said late Husband Served Seven years Appceship to one Mr. Hollingsworth a Rope maker in Lambeth Marsh in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry that her said late Husband after he was out of his said Apprenticeship never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. Augt. 1787 R Butler Wm. Hitchiner

her Elizth Selfs mark
