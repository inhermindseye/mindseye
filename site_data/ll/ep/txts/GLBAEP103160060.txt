Middlesex ss}  Ruth Rybolt the widow of William Rybott deced maketh oath that She can give no account respecting the settlement of her Said late husband That She this Deponent many years ago was bound Apprentice by Indre by the Parish officers of St Mary Whitechapel Middlesex to Joseph Anderson of Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Green Grocer untill She should attain the age of Twenty one years That she this Deponent Continued under such Indre with her said Master for divers years in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate & then this Deponent removed with her Said Master as his Apprentice to a House in Ratcliffe highway in in the Parish of Saint George in the Said County where she Continued with him as his Apprentice for the Space of two years & upwards as she now recollects & remembers and then this Deponents said Master left her to shift for herself & went into the County & she has never heard from him Since That at the time he so left her she this Deponent Apprentice that the Term for which she was bound Apprentice was not then wholy expired but Continuing That she this Deponent has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been Married or rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a settlement for herself Since her said Apprenticeship Save What She might have Acquired by Marriage with her Said late husband whose Settlement is unknown to her

Sworn this 11 day of May 1776 before Parkr Rowlands Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ruth Rybolt
