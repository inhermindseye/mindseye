Middx  Eliz: Bartlett upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that abt. Fifteen Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Anderson in the Back of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of for the Space of One Year & upwds. & further saith that she hath not since she left her sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne the 20th. of Novr. 1741 before me}
