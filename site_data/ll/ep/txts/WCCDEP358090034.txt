Middlesex  Mary Eyres Aged about Forty three Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Christr. Eyres who died about five Weeks ago and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Stephen Coleman Street about Eighteen Years ago and by whom she hath four Children now living Namely Samuel aged about Eleven Years, William aged about Ten, Years, Mary aged about Six Years, and Christr . aged about five Years, Says her said late Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Harrington a Fishmonger in Salisbury Court Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Brides als Bridgett  in the City of London and after he was out of his said apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 22nd. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Mary Eyres Mark
