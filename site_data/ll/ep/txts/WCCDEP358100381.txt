Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to Bermondsey to wit Catherine Farmer aged about Sixty years on her Oath says she Intermarried to William Farmer her late Husband at the Fleet about Thirty four years ago Says that she was informed her said Husband was born somewhere in Yorkshire and that her said Husband was a Soldier in the Army that she never heard of her Husband ever gaining a Settlement in his own Right says that before her Intermarriage she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Frohawkes in Long Lane in the parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey for one year at the yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service about Thirty four years ago since which time she has never done no Act matter or thing to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 8th. day of April 1785  Thos: Gilbert : Thos: Gordon

her Catherine Farmer mark
