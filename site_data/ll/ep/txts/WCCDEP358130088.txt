Middx  Ann Swetman Aged about Fifty five Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Robt. Swetman who Died about 12 Years ago, Says her said late Husband Served Four Years and upwards the last Part of his Apprenticeship with One Jas. Crutchfield a Carman in the Backside of Horseley Downs in the Parish of St. John Southwark in the County of Surry and never Rented Ten Pounds a year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a Yearly Servt. for One Year, Says She hath not since her sd. husbands Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the 29th. Day of Novr. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Swetman
