Middx  Penelope Mills aged about 38 Years upon her Oath Saith she was never married Says her Father  William Mills lived in & Rented a house at the Corner of Craven Buildings in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £40 & lived in the same upwds. of Seven Yrs. Says she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with her Sister  Mary Mills in Butcher Row in the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of £3: for the Space of two Years & hath left her sd. Service about Fifteen Years since which times she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 27th. Novr. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

Penelope Mills
