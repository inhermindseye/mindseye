Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William Dobson aged about 27 years upon his Oath saith that about 7 years ago he Intermarried with Fras. his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Cle Giles that he hath Three Children living by his said Wife vizt. Matthew aged about Six years Fras. aged about Four years and Eleanor aged about Nine Weeks That he this Examt. Served Seven years Apprenticeship to a Mr. Hunter at Elanwick in the Parish of Elanwick in the County of Northumberland that has been out of his said Apprenticeship about Six years since which time he has not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of Janry. 1789}
