Middx  Deborah Chandler aged about 32 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Roberts in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £3..10s for the Space of one Year & upwards & hath left her sd. Service upwards of One Year since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 19th. April 1744 before} Wm Jones

The Mark of Deborah Chandler
