Middx  Elizth: Hale aged abt. 84 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Hale who dyed in the Year 1729. says her said late husband informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that he lived in and Rented a house in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County Middx. for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards says she has not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn 15th. October 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of E H Elizth. Hale
