St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James Westmr.}  (to wit) Donald Munro aged fifty Seven years or thereabouts Upon his Oath saith that he was Married to Elizabeth his late Wife at the parish Church of Saint Ann Westminster about 23 y 24 years ago (by Banns) that she died on or about the 21st. August 1791 that he this Examinant Rented and resided in an Apartment at the House Numbered 4 in Brewer Street in the parish of St James in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for about five Years at the annual Rent of fourteen pounds & fourteen Shillings that he quitted the same about Twelve years ago and hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 19th.. day of May 1795 before us} N Bond Edw: Read

Donald Munro
