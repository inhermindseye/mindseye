Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Holliday aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that on the 2d. day of June last she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at her father house in  Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been half baptized by the Name of Harriot That one William Perks a Writer who lodges at No. 6 Plough Court Fetter Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exainants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said William Perks is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1789 } R Butler

her Mary Holliday Mark

Wm. Perks is a Short Man fair Complexion Hair Tyed behind In a Quee.

Settled pd. 13.13.6.
