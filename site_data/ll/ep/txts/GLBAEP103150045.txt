Middlesex ss}  George Eastwood maketh oath that divers Years ago he was bound Apprentice by Indre to his Brother Thomas Eastwood of the Parish of Preston in the County of Lancaster Tobacconist & Served the Whole of Such Apprenticeship there with his Said Brother & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Sd. Parish That he this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor Rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds nor served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Hannah & three Children namely Elizabeth aged about Eleven Years Mary aged about eight Years & Hannah aged about thirteen Months the lawful Issue of this Depont By his Sd wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 22d day of July 1775 before. B Camper

George Eastwood
