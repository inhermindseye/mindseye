Middx & Westmr-}  Dorothy Gould aged about 50 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Gould with whom she Intermarrd- at May Fair Chappell upwds- of 30 Yrs- ago who has been Dead upwds- of 20 Years ago Says her said husband served his Apprent- with one Mr- Taylor a Peruke Maker in Craven Stt - Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his Apprent- he never gained any other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house or orwise. howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 20th- day of October 1777  John Croft G Mercer

her Dorothy Gould Mark
