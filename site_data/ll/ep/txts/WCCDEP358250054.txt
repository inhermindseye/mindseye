Mary Saunders

St. Clement Danes 9th. July 1792

Middlesex & Westminster  St James Westminster } (to wit) Sarah Saunders Widow of John Saunders Aged about 44 years on behalf of her Daughter Mary Saunders now Ill Weak Upon Oath saith That she this Deponent was Married to her late Husband John Saunders at Carfax Church in the City of Oxford about 23 years ago That her said Husband hath informed her which she verily believes to be true That he served his Apprenticeship with his father William Saunders a Stonemason in Windmill Street in the parish of St James in the Liberty of Westminster And That since the expiration of his said Apprenticeship he never obtained any subsequent legal settlement That she hath one Child named Mary aged Nineteen years & upwards who hath never obtained any settlement in her own Right That her said Daughter is now ill with a Fever and unable to maintain herself And further saith that her said Husband was removed from the parish of St Clement Danes to St James Westminster aforesaid on or about the 19th October 1789 and that there has been no Ippert agst. the order of his removal to her knowledge or Relief

Sworn the 9th day of July 1792 before us} Sampn Wright  Thos. Gordon

Sarah Saunders Mark

Witness to the Mark of Sarah Saunders  Edwd Burbidge
