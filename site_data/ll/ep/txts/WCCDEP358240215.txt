St Clement Danes 13th February 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Charles Mayor  singleman aged 74 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he served his Apprenticeship (under Indentures) with Mr Richard Wharf  Breeches Maker  Hemlock Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex and lodged in his said Master's House in Hemlock Court during such Apprenticeship that he hath never since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain Any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 13th. day of Febry. 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Charles. Mayor
