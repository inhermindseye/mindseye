Middx ss.  Elizabeth White  wife of Richard White who is gone away from her to places Unknown maketh oath that her said husband has informed her that he duly served his apprenticeship with Mrs Haines Heayme a Cork Cutter in the Parish of saint Margaret Pattens in the City of London and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent saith that her said husband hath not since gained any subsequent settlement to her knowledge infermation or belief

Sworn this 11 day of January 1757 before us  Richd: Ricard  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Eliz. White
