Middlesex ss}  Ann Dennis  Singlewoman maketh oath That She has been informed by her Aunt Alice Parker late Alice Dennis & Which She believes to be true That this Deponts. late Father William Dennis deced was born in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County where his late Far David Dennis deced rented a house & was Charged to & paid Parochial Taxes & died there & this Deponent can give no other Account respecting the Settlement of her Said Father & Grand fathr or either of them That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 3d. day of December 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Ann Dennis
