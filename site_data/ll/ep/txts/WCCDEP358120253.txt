Middx  Sarah Rushfield aged abt. 33 Years upon Oath saith she is the wido. of Jas. Rushfield and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. late husband Served Seven years apprentiship with Mr Davis a Shoemaker opposite St Stephens Church in Westmr in the Parish of St. Mary Woolchurch London and that he never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year and this Examinant further upon her Oath Saith she hath not since the Decease of her sd. late husband rented ten Pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Janry 9th. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Rushfield
