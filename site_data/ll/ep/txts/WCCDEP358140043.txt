Sarah Bailey aged abt. 20 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married or ever Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for one Year says she is the Daughter of Chas. Bailey by Ann his Wife both deceased says her said father Chas. Bailey Rented a house in Horn Court in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds lived therein abt. Eight Years & hath quitted same abt. ten Years before his death & never after Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 8th. day of Octr. 1757 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Bailey
