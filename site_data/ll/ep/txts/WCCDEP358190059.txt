Middx.  Ann Wykes aged abt. 34 Years lodging at Mrs. Lillystone's in Greyhound Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Wm. Wykes who is now at Sea by whom she has 3 Children (Vizt.) Mary aged 4 Years Elizth. aged 2 Yrs. & Rebecca aged four Months Says he said husband informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Barnaby Backwell  Esqr. in Norfolk Street in the Strand in the said Parish of St Clement Danes for two Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of £8 and continued to live with Mr Backwell under the same hiring after his said Marriage for One Year and upwards says her said husband after he quitted Mr Backwell's Service Never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 21st. Octr. 1760 before me} Barth: Hammond

Ann Wykes
