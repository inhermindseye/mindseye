Middx  Elizth. Williams aged about 37 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Bush at the Sign of the Boot in Piccadilly in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr - for 3 Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted her said Service upwards of four Years ago since which time she hath never gained any other subseqt- Settlement either by Renting house Paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th- Day of Novr- 1777 before R. Butler Wm. Kitchiner

her Elizth- Williams Mark
