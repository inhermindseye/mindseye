Middx  Sarah Obrien aged about 54 Years on her Oath says she is the widow of Edward Obrion who died about Four Years ago and with whom she Intermarried with her said late husband at the Parish Church of St Andrew Undersharft London about Six Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in red Bull Yard in the Parish of St James Clarkenwell in the County of Middx for severel Years at the Yearly rent of Sixteen Pounds hath quitted the said House about Ten Years ago and after quitting the said House he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 11th. day of Novr. 1778 before  Jno. Machin  James Croft .

her Sarah Obrien Mark
