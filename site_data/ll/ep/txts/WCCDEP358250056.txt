Copy of an Examination grounding an Order of Removal Entered by Mistake in the "Bastards Book No. 2". in Page "6"

(true Copy )  St Clement Danes July 3d 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St George Westmr  Joseph Baker Aged 37 years and upwards Upon his Oath saith That he intermarrd. with Margaret Baker his present Wife at the parish Church of St James  in the Liberty of Westminster by Bans That him and his said Wife since their intermarrige (that is to say) about two years ago they took a House No.1 in Lees. Mews North Audley Street in the parish of St George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex and lived there upwards of one year at the yearly Rent of £15, that since they left the said House they have been in Weekly Lodging and have never since rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes that he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year nor has done any other Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 3d. July 1792 before me} W Addington Jas Dyson

Joseph Baker
