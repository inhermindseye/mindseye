Westmr.  Mary Hindes Aged upwards of Fifty seven Years, Upon Oath says she is the Wife of James Hindes (who is gone from her) says her said Husband is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief, says that since her intermar- -riage with the said James Hindes she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Felix Crayting the corner of Craven Yard  Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds, and after she guitted said Service never Rented House, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 7th. day of May 1768 , Before W Kelynge

The Mark of Mary Hindes
