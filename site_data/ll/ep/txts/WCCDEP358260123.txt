Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Sewell aged about 67 years upon her Oath saith that about 34 years ago she Intermarried with Samuel Sewell her late Husband who has been dead about Thirteen year at May fair That her said late Husband lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hantridge who kept the five Bells  man in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about four years but what Wages he had she this Deponent doth not know and that after he quitted said Service he never to the best of this Deponents knowledge Information or belief did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that since her said late Husbands Death she hath never lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement in her own Right

Sworn this Sixth day of April 1796 J lend

her Mary Sewell Mark

Witness a B Newman
