Middlesex to wit Ann Lymor Aged about 29 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Beadle in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Seven years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 5th. day of Augt. 1783 R Butler

Ann Lymor
