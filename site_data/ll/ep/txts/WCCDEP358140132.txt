Middx.  Jane Hughes aged abt. 25 Years upon Oath says she never was married says she is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with one John Flanagan a dealer in fish in Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for three Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £6. and quitted her sd. Service on the 19th. day of this Instant June And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish; she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born says the said John Flanagan her Master had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body in the Month of March last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child

of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that he the said John Flanagan and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn this 20th. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Jane Hughes
