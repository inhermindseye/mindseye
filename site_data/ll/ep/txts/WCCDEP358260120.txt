Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster to wit}  Mary Couch aged about 57 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the year 1764 she Intermarried with George her late Husband who has been dead about fourteen months at Warcot in Bath that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Pettys Court Hanway Yard in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for about Twelve years at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds that he quitted said House about Twelve years since which time he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds paid Taxes or did any Act matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th- day of March 1796 E Read Rusl Clarke

her Mary Couch Mark

Witness B Newman
