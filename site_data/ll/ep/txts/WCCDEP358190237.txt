Middx  Gilbert Hall aged upwards of 60 Years Removed from St. George Hanover Square by Order dated the 26th. March 1762 upon Oath Says he never was Married That he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Henry Butler Pacey  Esqr in Cecil Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £11 and lay during such time at his Master's Stables in Drury Lane in the same Parish quitted said Service near 20 years and hath not since rented any House paid Land Tax or Poor Rate or been a yearly hired Servant in one place for a year

Sworn the 27th. day of March 1762 Before}
