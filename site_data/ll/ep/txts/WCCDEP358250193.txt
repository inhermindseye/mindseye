Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Mary Beath aged about 41 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hammond who kept the Sign of the Spotted Dog in the Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Fifteen Months at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Five Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Augt. 1795 } Rupt Clarke N Bond

Mary Beath
