Middlesex to wit Sarah Linnell Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Lowe at the Crown Alehouse at Peckham in the Parish of Camber in the County of Surry upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her Service upwards of Nine Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th. day of Febry. 1780 before R Butler

her Sarah Linnell Mark
