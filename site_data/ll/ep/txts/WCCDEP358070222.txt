Middx & Westmr.}  Margt. Hindmarch Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father George Hindmarch Rented a House at Newcastle upon Tyne for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds as she believes and died several Years ago since whose Death she Never gained any Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes (she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) Says that one William Bowie a Footman to Colonol Stevens at Hastings near Hounslow is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of March last but the particular day she does not now remember and at Several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said William Bowie and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 10th. day of Novr. 1774 before-  Sampn Wright

Margret Hindmarch
