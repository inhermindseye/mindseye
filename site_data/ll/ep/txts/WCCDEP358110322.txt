Middx  Frances Woodall aged abt. 30 Years upon her Oath saith she never was married and this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a male bastard Child on the 9th. Day of September last at the house of Mrs. Walker in Witch Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middlesex which was baptized Michael & is become Chargeable to the Sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes and that one Petre a Foreigner whose Christian name this Examinant knows not did beget the sd. male bastard Child on her body the sd. Petre having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the Month Novr. last & at several other times since at one of which times he get her with Child of the sd male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. & that the sd. Petre & no man else is the true & real father thereof & this Examinant further maketh Oath that she verily believes the sd. Petre is gone beyond Sea.

Sworn Octr. 4th 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Frances Woodall
