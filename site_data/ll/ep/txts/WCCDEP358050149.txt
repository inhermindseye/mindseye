Westmr,  Eliza. Seagus  wido . of Moss Seagus upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Moss Seagus abt. 7 Yrs. ago at the fleet London and that he Served the first Part of his Apprenticeship with his father Moss Seagus in the Parish of Town Malden in Kent and never was Assigned over to any Person rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examinant has been Informed and beleives and that she has not since the Decease of her sd. late husband rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt.

or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn the 25th. Novr. 1749 before H Fielding

The Mark of Eliz Seagas
