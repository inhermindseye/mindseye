Middx  James Mecully aged 41 Years  Removed from the Parish of St Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . by Order Dated the 2d. Day of July Instt. upon his Oath saith that abt. 21 yrs. since he served 7 Years apprentiship with Mr. Traunter in Star Court  Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Tayler that since his sd. Apprentiship he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath 2 Childn. viz : John aged upwds. of 10 Yrs. & Thomas aged abt. 5 Yrs. by his late Wife  Jane Deceased & being Poor is not able to maintain himselfe & Childn.

Sworne the 3d. of July 1742 before}

James Mecully
