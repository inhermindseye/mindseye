Westmr.  Mary Corkin upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Russell at Mrs. Cremers in the Strand in the Precinct of the Savoy in the County of Middx for five Years at the yearly Wages of four pounds quitted her sd. Service upwds. of 12 Years ago and has not Since She quitted her sd. Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Decr 30th. 1752 before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Corkin
