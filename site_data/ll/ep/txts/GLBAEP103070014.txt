Middx ss  James Casvell Maketh oath that abt. fourteen Years ago he was bound apprentice to Mr. Willm. Manby of ye parish of St. Ann Lime house in the County of Middx  pipe maker with whom he duly Served his apprenticeship by Means whereof this Depont. acquired a legal Settlemt. in ye sd parish And this Depont. Says he has not Since gained any Subsequent Settlemt. And that he has a wife Named Sarah and two Children to wit Ann Aged about Six Years and John Aged about 5 Years the lawfull Issue of this Dept. & his sd Wife

Sworn this 2d. day of Novr. 1750 before us  Richd. Ricards  Saml Gower

James Casvell
