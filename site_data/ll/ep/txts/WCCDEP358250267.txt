Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Cassety aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of June 1781 she Intermarried with Dudley Cassety (who is now abroad) at the parish Church of Saint Andrews Holborn by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Mary Ann aged about Twelve years and Elizabeth aged about Nine Months That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Portpool Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Two years at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds that he quitted said house about fourteen years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Decr. 1796 Robinson Rupt Clarke

Mary Cassety
