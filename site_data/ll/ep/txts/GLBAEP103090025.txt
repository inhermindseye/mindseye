Middlesex ss  Sarah Gaulthorpe the widow of Robert Gaulthorpe deced Maketh Oath that her said late husband in his life time informed her that he was bound apprentice by Indenture to and duly served all his Apprenticeship with a Fishmonger in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there The which Information this Depont. verily believes to be true And this Deponent Saith that to her knowledge informacon or belief her said husband never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor never served any Parish offices nor never Paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes nor never did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his Apprenticeship nor hath this Deponent done any Act since her said husbands Death to gain a settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 25th day of January 1766 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Sarah Gaulthorpe Her Mark
