Middx  Ann Sisson Aged about Fifteen Years Upon Oath Says that by Indenture dated the Nineteenth day of July 1759 she was bound an Apprentice by the Church Wardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx to Saml Pitchford of the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the same County Green Grocer and lived with him in the sd. Parish upwards of two Years Says She hath not been Assigned over to any person that her sd. Master hath Insisted himself for a Soldier as She hath been informed and her Mistress hath discharged her

Sworn the 14th May 1762 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Sisson
