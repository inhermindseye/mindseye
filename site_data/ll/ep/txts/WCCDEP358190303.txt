Middx  Susanna Tilley Aged about 38 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with James Tilley her late Husband  deceased at May Fair Chappel by whom She has two Children  George aged about Twelve Years and Ann aged about Four Years Says her said late Husband lived a hired Yearly Servant with a Widow Gentlewoman in Red Lyon Square in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex upwards of three Years and quitted his said Service upwards of Sixteen Years as She has been informed by her said late Husband which Information She verily believes to be true Says that her said late Husband never Rented a House or paid any Taxes

Sworn the 20th. day of Novr. 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Susa. Tilley
