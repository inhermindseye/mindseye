Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Terry aged about 38 Years upon his Oath Saith. That he this deponent was born at Crundell in Hampshire That he never was bound an Apprentice lived as a Hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter  or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge. Information or belief He may have gained and other Settlement

Sworn this 16th: day of August 17 before} Rupert Clarke  Joseph Moser

his William Terry Mark

Witness B Newman
