Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Adam Burbidge  aged about One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Robert Goodman who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Robert Goodman was born at Stratford in the Parish of Stratford in the County of Essex Where his father kept house for many Years and that the said Robert Goodman never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any other  settlement than as aforesaid And this Deponent further upon his Oath saith that the said Robert Goodman is now Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 15th. day of Janry. 1798 before us M Addington Ru Clarke

AW Burbidge
