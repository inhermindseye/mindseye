Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Adey aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that about Nine years ago she Intermarried with John Adey her late Husband who has been dead about Seven years at the Parish of St. James  Westmr . That her said late Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Hefford a Plaisteres in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Decr. 1796  Rusl: Clarke M Addington

her Sarah Adey Mark Witness B Newman
