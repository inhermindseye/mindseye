Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Brookes Aged about 38 years upon her Oath Saith That about Nine years ago she intermarried with George Brookes  her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square That for five Months last past he has rented a Stall in Clare Court at Seven Shillings per Week and a Room in Drury lane in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the County of Middlesex (where he Slept) at 22 pence per Week

Sworn this 25th day of July 1800 before us} Saml Edwards . John Collick

Mary Brookes
