Middx  The Examination of Mary the Wife of Wm. Foot Taken the 27th. Day of February 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about two Years ago her sd. Husband lived in & Rented a House in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the space of one Year & upwards of the Yearly Rent of £16 or thereabouts And further on her Oath saith that he hath not since that time Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Mary Foot
