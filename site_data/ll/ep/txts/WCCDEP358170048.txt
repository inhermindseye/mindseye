Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Olfoot  Wido . of Thos. Alford now in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 5th. Day of May 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. Twelve Years ago she intermarried with her sd. late Husband Thos- Olfoot & that her sd. late Husband lived in rented a House behind St. Clement of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds for the Space of three Years or thereabts- & further saith that her sd. Husband in his Life nor this Examint. since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt- for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Elizabeth Olfoot
