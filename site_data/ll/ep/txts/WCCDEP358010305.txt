Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster to wit Ann Gow aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith that she Rented Two Parlours of a Mr. Fairly in Plumb Tree Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds that she hath quitted said apartments about Twelve seven Months years since which time she has not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of  Augt. 1789 J Barnfather Gs Badshaw

Ann Gow
