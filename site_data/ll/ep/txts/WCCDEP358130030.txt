Middx  Ann Archer upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Jacob Archer to whom she was married at the Church of St. Peter and Paul in Bath about Ten years ago & by whom she has 2 Children Wm. aged about 9 Years & Mary aged near 6 years & that her sd husband informed her he Served 3 years of his apprentiship with Wm. Batty a Taylor in Parsonage Lane in the afsd. parish of St Peter & Paul in Bath and at the End of the sd. 3 yrs. absconded from his Said Master & never was assigned over nor ever Rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year which Information this Examinant believes to be true

Sworn July 18th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Archer
