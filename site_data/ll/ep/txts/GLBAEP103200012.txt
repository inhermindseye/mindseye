By Servitude since her Husbands Death

Middlesex ss}  Ann Turvey the widow of Thomas Turvey deced that maketh oath that Since her said husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with her son Thomas Turvey in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London  Butcher at Certain Wages & Continued in such Service under Such hiring for the Space of five years & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor been married or done any Act to gain anothr settlement

Sworn this 13 day of June 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Turvey
