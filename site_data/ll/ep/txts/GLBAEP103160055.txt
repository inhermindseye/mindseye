Middlesex ss}  Jane Boston the Widow of Andrew Boston deced maketh oath that divers years ago her Said late husband rented a house of Six Pounds & ten shillings a year in Angel Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Nor hath She this Deponent since his death rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and that she hath three Children namely John  aged about nineteen years Diannah  aged about Fourteen & Jane  aged about nine years the lawful Issue of this Depont by her said late husband to whom she was lawfully married None of which Children have as yet gained a Settlement for themselves

Sworn this. 27. day of April 1776 before  Geo C Smith

The Mark of Jane Boston
