Westmr.  Mary Denny Wido. of Henry Denny upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the Seventh Day of Octr. last in the house of Mr. Rigemont in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which has been baptized John Denny and is become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and that John Hastings Labourer is the Reputed Father the said John Hastings having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the Month of December last & at sevll. other times since in the house of Mrs. Whitney at Paddington (where they Lodged together as man and Wife) at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesaid and that the sd. John Hastings & no man else is the true and reall father thereof

Sworn Novr. 2d. 1747 before} Tho: Lane  Wm: Jones

Mary Denny
