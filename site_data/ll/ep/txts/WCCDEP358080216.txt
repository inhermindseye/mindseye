Middlesex to wit Elizth. Taylor Aged about Seventy Three Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of William Taylor who has been dead about Twenty Years and with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about forty Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living says her said Husband served Seven Years apprenticeship with One Mr. Beddan a Glazier in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds P Ann paid Taxes or otherwise gained any or. Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 24th. day of Febry 1778 before  Abrm Baley

her Elizth. Taylor Mark
