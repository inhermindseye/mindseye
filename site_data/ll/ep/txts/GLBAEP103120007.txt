Middlesex ss}  Thomas Fowler maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice by Indenture to Benjamin Toft of the Parish of Broughton in the County of Lincoln Mariner for the Term of Three years and served the whole of Such his apprenticeship and by means thereof as he is advised he gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont. Saith that he has not Since done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 4th Day of July:1772 before W: Russell

Thomas Fowler
