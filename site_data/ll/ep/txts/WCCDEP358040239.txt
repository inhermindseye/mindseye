Westmr. 

Mary Lea aged abt. 17 Years upon Oath she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt . for one Year Says her late Father Richd. Lea dyed about 3 Yrs. ago and that she was born in Star Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . where her Father lived in and rented a house of Fourteen pounds a Year for Several Years and left it about 4 Years before his decease and never afterwards rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 25th. 1748 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Mary Lea
