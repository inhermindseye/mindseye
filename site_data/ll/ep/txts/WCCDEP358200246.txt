Middx.  Elizabeth Whitby aged about 47 Years upon Oath says she is the wife of Ralph Whitby (who is now abroad) with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London about fifteen Years ago by whom she hath one Child John aged about Eight Years says her said Husband was bound an Apprentice for 7 Years to one Charles Young a Taylor in Kings head Court  Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex and served about fourteen Months of his said Apprenticeship and then the said Chas. Young broke and ran away and that her said husband never was assigned over to any other person or did any Act whereof to gain a subsequent Settlement as he hath Often times informed her and which information she verily believes to be true.

Sworn the 18th. day of March 1765 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth. Whitby
