Middlesex & Westmr.}  Elizth. Wyllie Aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of James Wyllie with whom she Intermarried at the Parish of St. Clement Danes on the 9th. day of December 1754 by whom she hath One Child named Sarah aged about Ten Years but whether living or not this Examinant does not know Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes & lived therein upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and then her said Husband quitted the same about Christmas last from whener he removed to a House in Exeter Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr where he now lives at the Yearly Rent of Twenty three pounds and hath kept the same about half a Year as she has been Informed by One of the Overseers of the said Parish of St. Martin in the Fields which Information she verily bels. to be True

Sworn this 14th: day of July 1770 before us} Thos. Kynaston  Charles Marsh .

Elizbeth Wyllie
