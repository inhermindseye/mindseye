Middx.  Jane Routledge aged abt. 22 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with George Snowden Esqr. in the Parish of St. Mary at Carlisle in the County of Cumberland for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. four Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year and further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 22d. day of May 1760. she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr Blow Opposite St. Clement Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been baptized by the Name of William and that John Routledge  Servant to Govr. Drake in Gresar Square had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of August 1759 and at sevl. times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid & that the said John Routledge & no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. 1760 before} Paul Vaillant  Fran: Bedwell

Jane Routledge
