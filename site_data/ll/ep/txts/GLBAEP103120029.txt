Middelsex ss}  George Barnard maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice to and served the whole thereof with one Benjamin Junks in the Parish of Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry  Button maker & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that since his Said Apprenticeship he hath not rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor served any Parish office nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement and that he hath a wife named Susannah and four children to Wit Susannah aged about fourteen years George Aged about Ten years Stephen Aged about Seven years and Thomas Aged about four years the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his Said Wife none of Which children have as yet done any Act to gain a settlement in their own right.

Sworn this 2d. day of February 1773 before  Rob Pell B Camper

George Barnard
