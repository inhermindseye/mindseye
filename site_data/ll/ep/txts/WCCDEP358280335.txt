St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) Sarah Kimber  Singlewoman Aged twenty five years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that on the Sixteenth day of April last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the lying in Hospital Store Street in the said County that who hath Since been Christened Frederick Kimber at that one Willm. Higgins who lately lived with Mr Townsend in Old Burlington Street Piccadilly and now lives about fourteen Miles from the City of Hereford is the only real true father thereof he only & none else having had carnal knowledge of this Deponts. Body & begotten the Said Child thereon

Sworn the 8th August 1791 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Kimber
