Westmr.  Mary Moreland aged abt. 27 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she never was married And that in the Month of Octr. 1751 she was delivered of a female bastard Child at Mr Sharps in Paradise Row near Bond Street in the Parish of St. George Hanr Square in the Liberty of Westmr. which was baptized Hannah Frances

Sworn Augt. 14th. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Moreland
