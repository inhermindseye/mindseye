Middx & Westmr  Ann Hartley aged abt. 63 Yrs. upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Thomas Hartley who dyed abt. 15 Yrs. since and with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Ormskirk in the County of Lancaster in the Year 1710 Says her sd. late husband has been dead Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Thirty pounds & upwards in Cooks Court in Cary Street in the parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and lived thereabouts Nine Years or thereabouts & has quitted the same upwards of 20 Yrs. Says her sd. late husband never after he left the sd. house nor has this Examint. since his Decease rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn May 31st. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell Ann Hartley
