Middx  Mary Cox widow of Edwd. Cox upon oath saith she Intermarried with the said Edwd. Cox on the 3d. day Octor. 1741 at the fleet London says he has been Dead about Six years the best of her remembrance says the said Edwd. Cox served seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Wootten a Lighterman who Kept the Bull Ale house in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx says her said late Husband in his lifetime or this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten pounds a Year pd parish Taxes lived with any person as a yearly servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to has knowledge or belief

Sworn the Decr. 15th. 1749 before}
