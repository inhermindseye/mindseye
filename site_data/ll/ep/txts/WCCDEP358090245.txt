Middx to wit Mary Roberts Aged about Forty One years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a Hired yearly Servant with One Mr. Haywood a Taylor in Evangelist Court in the Parish of Saint Anns Black Fryers in the City of London for One year and Nine Months at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said service about Seven years ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly servant for One year

Sworn this 17th. day of August 1781 before.  Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Roberts
