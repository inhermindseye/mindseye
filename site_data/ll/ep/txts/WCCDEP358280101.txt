Saint Clement Danes  28th. June 1787.

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Susannah Giles  Singlewoman aged twenty eight Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married, that she never Rented any House or Tenement of ten pounds a Year or paid taxes nor was ever bound as an Apprentice-Saith that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stunt a Shoemaker in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds, that she has quitted his Service about seven or eight Months ago, and has not since quitting the same done any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have gained or acquired any subsequent Settlement-And further this Examinant upon her voluntary oath saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes , she this Examinant being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Edward Riley who lived as a Footman with Mr. Maniac a Jeweller at Clerkenwell is the father of such Child or Children whereof she this Examinant is so pregnant he the said Edward Riley having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body in or about the Month of November 1786 and begotten the same thereon

Sworn this 28th. June 1787 before me R Butler

Susannah The Mark of Giles
