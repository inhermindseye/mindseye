Middx  Jas. Mc. calloh upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Robt. Tranter Taylor and Served the last three Years of his said Apprentiship with the said Robert Tranter in Witch Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlx ant that he never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 21st 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

James Mccully
