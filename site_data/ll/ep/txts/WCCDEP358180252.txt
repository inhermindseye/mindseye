Middx  John Darby Aged about 37 Years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with his present Wife Ann on the 20th: Day of July 1729 at the Parish Church of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. by whom he hath 3 Children to wit Margt. Aged 11 Years Mary Aged about 7 Years & Hannah Aged 5 Years Says he lived in and Rented a House in Swallow Street in the sd. Parish of St. James of the Yearly Rent of £11 & lived in the same 9 Months & hath left same about 7 Years since which time he hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 14th Novr. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

John Darby
