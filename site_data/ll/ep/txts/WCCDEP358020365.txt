St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Mary Oglivie  Singlewoman aged about 25 years Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married never lived as an hired Yearly Servant For a Twelve month together under one hiring, served as an Apprentice, Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year, paid Parochial Taxes, or otherwise obtained any legal Settlement in her own Rights, That her Father Thomas Ogilvie 's (as he hath Informed this Deponent which she verily believes to be true) Intermarried with his late a Wife this Deponent's late another Sophia at the parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn about thirty years ago That since then her said Father Rented & lived in a House in King Street the parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for about four or five years at the Annual Rent of Eighteen or twenty pounds that he quitted the same about thirteen years ago since hath not (anywise obtained any other legal Settlement and this Examinant further upon Oath saith that she was removed by Orders of Removal from the said Parish of St. Clement Danes to the said Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in or about the Month of May 1789 against which Orders they never Appealed-that she was

maintained for a fortnight or upwards in the Workhouse belonging to the said parish of St Giles at the time she was removed by Virtue of such Orders

Sworn the 10th day of Decr. 1791 before us Addington Thos. Gordon

Mary Ogilvie 's Mark

Witness Rd. Dobbins
