Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Leatte aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Michael Leatte at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Ottry in the County of Devon in the year 1777 that her said Husband died in the same year that since her said late husbands Death she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Joseph Malpas at Chelsea in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she has not done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 20th. day of May last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Tully in Alphabet bound Stanhope Street which hath since been Christened by the Name of Wm . That one Wm. Jameson who lives at No. 4 in King Street  Westmr . is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Extd. Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Wm. Jameson is the only real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 2d. day of June 1789 R Butler

Ann Leatte

Wm. Jameson is a Tall Thin Man dark Complexion has a remarkable Turn with his Eyes sometimes wears his Wig with a single Curl and other times with his Hair is Curled uses the Newcastle Coffee house .

15th. June 1789 , Security Bond given Child dead since
