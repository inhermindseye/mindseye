Middx  Sarah Blaker Aged about years upon Oath says that She is the Widow of Peter Blaker deced That She lived a hired yearly Servant (during her Widowhood and being without Child or Children) with Mrs. Toskck in Arrundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of a year and quitted that Service above two years ago, since which time She hath not intermarried with any Person, nor Rented a House, nor lived in Service a whole year

Sworn the day of 1755 before}
