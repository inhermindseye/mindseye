Saint Clement Danes

Middelsex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Baxter aged about 57 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Jones a Distiller in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Four Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Forty two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th day of Decr. 1795 N Bond Edw: Read

her Susannah Baxter Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
