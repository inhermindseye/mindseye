Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Donnelly aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that about 38 years ago she Intermarried with Patrick Donnelly her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish of Saint Nicholas without in Dublin That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house called or known by the name or Sign of the Rose in Bridges Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Fifty Pounds and upwards that he hath quitted said House upwards of Four years since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 8th. day of April 1799 before us J Reeves Saml. Edwards

her Jane Donnelly mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
