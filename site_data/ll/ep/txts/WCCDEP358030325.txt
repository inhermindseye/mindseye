Westmr. 

Jeremiah Bath upon Oath saith he lived & rented house in Longditch in the Parish of St. Margt. in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Year Rent of £10 for the Spaces of two Years and upwards & hath left the same about Sevell. Years Says he hath not since he left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 17th. 1746 before  Tho Lane .

The Mark of Jere: Bath
