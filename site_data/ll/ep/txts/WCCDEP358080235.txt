Westmr.  Phebe Nicholson aged about forty two Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of John Nicholson (who is a Militia Man now at Hounslow ) with whom she Intermarrd. at the parish Church of St- Andrew Holborn about ten Years ago and by whom she hath two Childn- now living namely Wm - aged about six Years and Elizth- Ann aged upwards of three Years Says her said husband before his Intermarrd- with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Briscon a Fishmonger in Bloomsbury Markett in the parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the Co- of Middx upwards of six Years at the Yearly Wages of £18 besides Board & Lodging and from whence this Examinant Married-and after quitting his said Service he never Rented house paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a subseqt- Settlemt-

Sworn this 7th- Day of April 1778 R Butler G Mercer

her Phebe Nicholson
