St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Sarah Kinber  Singlewoman Aged twenty five years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Cutts No: 27 Arundell Street in the said parish of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty abt 2 years and an half at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she quitted such Service about Nine Months ago and hath not acquired any subsequent legal Settlement And further Upon Deponent or her Oath saith that to the best of her knowledge Information and belief

Sworn the 8th day of August 1791 bef. Me} Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Kinber
