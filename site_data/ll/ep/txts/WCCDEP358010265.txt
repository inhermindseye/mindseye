Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Matthew Mainwaring aged about 36 years upon his Oath saith that about 16 years ago he Intermarried with Mary his late wife deced at the Parish Church of Greenwich that he this Examinant was born at Dublin and that he never was bound an Apprentice in England lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes that he has one Child living by his said late Wife vizt. Richard aged about four years that his said late Wife before her Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst she was a Singlewoman lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Rudgeley a Bricklayer in Conpice Row in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for several years and that he this Examinant married her from the said Mr. Rudgeleys

Sworn this 16th. day of Febry 1789 R Butler

Matt Manwaring
