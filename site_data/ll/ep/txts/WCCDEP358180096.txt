Middx  Ann Horton upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married . That upwds of 2 Yrs. since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Coledam in Long Achor in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the Space of 2 Yrs. or thereabouts That since she left her sd. Service she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or other- wise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 3d. Augt. 1743 before} Thos De Veil J Poulson

Ann Houghton
