Militia

St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  (to wit) Hannah Harragan aged years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She bei about ten Years ago she this Examinant being then a singlewoman and having no Children Children Husband was Married at a Roman Catholic Church or Chapel in Dublin according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of Rome to Jeremiah Harrag Harragan who as she verily believes having no reason to think the contrary was a singleman and had no Wife being And this Dep that She hath one Child by the said Jeremiah Harragan now living namely He William born on or about the first day of July 1794 1791 Saith that the said Jeremiah Harragan was duly Sworn and enrolled to serve as a Substitute on the Westmr. Regiment of Middlesex Militia for Robert Clark of the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster as she hath been informed and believes and that he is now serving as such substitute in this said Regiment which was emboded and called out into actual Service on the 28th day of January last and has been ordered ordered to march as she hath also been informed and believes Saith that She now resides at No 26 Great Wila Street with her said Child and that they have dwelt there ever since the said 28th day of January An that she is unable to support herself and Chld without assistance

Sworn the day of April 1793 before Me}
