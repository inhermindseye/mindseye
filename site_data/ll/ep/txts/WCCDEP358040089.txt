Westmr.  Ann Spicer aged about 17 Years upon Oath Saith she is a Singlwoman Says her father Benjn. Spicer rents a house of £16 a Year near Clements Inn Gate in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year and further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and not able to maintain the same when born Says Edward Barns a Drawer at the Ship Tavern in the Butcher Row did beget the said Child or Children or her Body sometime in the beginning of May last at an Inn in Fleet Street where the sd. Edward Barnes had Carnal knowledge of her Body several times at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Children she is now pregnant with and that he the sd. Edward Barns and no other Person is the true & real Father thereof

Sworn Octr. 22d. 1747 before} Tho: Lane

The Mark of Ann Spicer
