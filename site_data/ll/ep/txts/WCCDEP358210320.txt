Westmr.  Charles Cooks Aged about Fifty six Years; upon Oath says he intermarried with Phillis his Wife at the Fleet London, about Nineteen Years ago; Says he rented House in Kennington Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry , of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds, Quitted said House about Twenty eight Years ago, and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 15th, day of September 1768 , before W Kelynge Thos Kynaston

Chs Cooks
