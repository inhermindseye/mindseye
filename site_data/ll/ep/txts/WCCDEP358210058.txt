Westmr.  Ann Gale aged about 60 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Gale who has been dead upwards of Ten Years, says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Pleasants at the Sun Alehouse in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds, quitted said Service about Michaelmas last and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn 2d. July 1766, before} Tho Lane

Ann Gale
