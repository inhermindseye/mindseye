Westmr. to wit Lucy Dowland Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with Richard Dowland at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years Ago and who was soon Afterwards transported Vizt. the 21st. May 1773 and has not been in England Since to her knowledge or belief Says that her said Husband was born in Swallow Street St. James where her said Husbands Father kept a House as she has often heard her said Husband declare and never gained any other Subseqt. Settlement either by renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 25th. of August last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Holles Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . which has since been Christned by the name of Lucy and is become Chargable to the same she being Poor and unable to maintain the said Child Sayth that Willm. Anderson at the Castle and Falcon in Little Turn Stile is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of December last but that particular Day she does not now remember and at several Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Wm. Anderson and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 12th. day of Septr. 1774 before R. Butler

her Lucy Dowland Mark
