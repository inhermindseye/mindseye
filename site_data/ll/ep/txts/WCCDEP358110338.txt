Middx  Sarah Boswell aged about 30 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about nine years ago she was hired to live with Mr Coles a Perukemaker in Beaufort Buildings in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Four pounds & lived with Mr Coles in Beaufort Buildings afsd. under that hiring & agreement upwards of one Year and that she has not since she quitted her sd. service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year and this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the afsd. parish of St Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Peter Conn a Taylor did beget the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. Peter Conn having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the month of march last & at several other times since at one of which times or he got her with Child of the bastard Child or Children with which she is now & pregnant and that the said Peter Conn and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Octr. 26th 1753 before } Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarh. Boswell

Peter Conn was married on the 3d. Novr. 1753 at the Fleet London to the above Sarah Boswell ; he was bound out by the parish of Emerton  Bucks to Saml. Brouton a Taylor at Kettering in Northans  & after having lived wth. him 79th. he Ealisted the Kings Service & never was assigned over nor ever rented ten pounds a Year or paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one year.
