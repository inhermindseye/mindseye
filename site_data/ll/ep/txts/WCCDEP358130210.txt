Middx Johnson on behalf of Sarah Johnson her Daughter aged about 18 - years upon Oath says that Samuel Johnson her Husband Rented a House in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the yearly Rent of Twenty two Pounds lived therein upwards of three years & quitted the same about Eleven years has not Rented any House since Says the said Sarah Johnson her Daughter was never bound an Apprentice or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 16th. day of Septr. 1756 . before}
