Westmr to wit Christian Storey Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath says that She intermarried with her husband William Storey (who was Transported about a Month Ago) at the Parish Church of Saint Ann Soho in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex , and by whom she has One Child now Living Named Wm Joseph Aged about 10 Months, And further on her Oath says that her said Husband Served 5 Years the first Part of his Apprenticeship with One Mr Ravenscraw  in Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph Bishopsgate London And that Since that time he has never Rented House paid Taxes lived A hired Yearly Servant or has done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of January 1775 before R Butler 

The Mark of Christian Storey
