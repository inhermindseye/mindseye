Westmr. to wit Thomas Gent Aged about Thirty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Eleanor is now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields about fifteen Years ago by whom he hath Two Children now living Namely George Aged about Eight Years and Thomas Aged about Six Years Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Thos. Oakill a Stone Mason in Long Acre in the said Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields by Indenture Dated the day of And was by Indenture of Assignment soon afterwards Assigned over to one Jarvis Snape a Goldbeater in the afsd. Parish of St. Martin in the Fields and served the said Mr. Snape under the lastmentd. Indre of Assignment Seven Years and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 26th. day of June 1771 before} Tho. Kynaston W Kelynge

Thos. Gent
