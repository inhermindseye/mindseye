Middx  Margt. Carney aged abt. 28 Years Lodging at the Cheshire Cheese in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. upon Oath says She is the Widow of Wm. Carney who hath been dead abt. Six Years Says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. John Joyner in Thomas's Street by St. Peters Church in Liver poole one Year & a half at the Yearly Wages of Forty Shillings She this Examinant at that time being a Widow & without Child or Children, Says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes She being Poor & unable to maintain the same when Born & that one Mattw. Doyle did beget the sd. Child or Children with which she is now pregnant he the sd.

Matthew Doyle living in the same house with this Examinant had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of May last & at sevl. other times since & that he the sd. Matthw. Doyle & no other Person did beget the Child or Children with which She is now pregant

Sworn Decr. 24th. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Margt. Carney
