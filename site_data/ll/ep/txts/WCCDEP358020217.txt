Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Charles Adney aged about 17 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Captain Gregory who lived in Fludyer Street for Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Thirteen Guineas that his said Master went to Scotland and then returned to England When he went to live at Chiswick that this Examinant for the last Two Months of the greater part of his said Service lived in Sutton Court in the Parish of Chiswick in the County of Middlesex that he has quitted said Service about Seven Months since which time he has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of July 1790 }

Charles Adney
