Middx  The Examination of Mary the Wife of Geo: Farmer taken the 7th. Day of Octr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that on the 16th. Day of January 1733 she Intermaryed with her first Husband Jno. Church Deceased & by him hath one Child living (to wit) Jas . aged about four years & further saith that her said late Husband Jno. Church about fourteen Years since rented a House in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds & lived in the Same for four Years or thereabouts and never after Rented any House of Ten pounds a Year paid to the Poor of any Parish or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place as this Examinant has been Informed & verily beleives that this Examinant & her present Husband Geo: Farmer are poor & not able to maintain the said Child

Sworn the day & Yeare above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Mary Farmer
