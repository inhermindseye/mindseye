Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Macleod Aged about 60 years upon his Oath Saith That he never was married That he lived as a hired yearly Servant for seventeen Months with one Mr Wingfield at the Prince of Orange Coffee House in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex  and Liberty of Westminster at the yearly Wages of One Pound that he hath left the said service near 30 years since which time he hath n lived as a hired yearly Servant for twelve Months or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of March 1800 before us} Ws Woodenough Saml. Edwards

John Macleod his Mark
