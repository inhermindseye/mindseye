Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Morris aged about 61 years upon her Oath saith that about she never was married Rented House of paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Bullock in Frith Street Soho in the Parish of Saint Annes in the Liberty of Westmr. for Two years & upwds. at the yearly wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service about 12 Years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of March 1788 J Barnfather James Croft

her Jane Morris  Mark
