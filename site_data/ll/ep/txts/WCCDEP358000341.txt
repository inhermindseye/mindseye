St. Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Benjn. Newman One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath & Saith that he has been informed by Sarah Parry who lies in at the House of a Mr Taylor in Sawyers Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes that she was married to Henry Parry by Banns about 20 years ago at Boston Church in Lincolnshire and have Six Children by him Vizt. Thomas aged 18 years now at Sea George aged 14 Wm. aged 8 years David aged 5 years Mary aged 3 years and a Infant not yet Baptized aged 8. days saith that they Rented a House of a Mr. Cleane in Greyhound Court Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds hath that they have Quitted said House about 5 years ago since which time they have done no Act Matter on thing whereby to the best of this Exts. knowledge Information or belief they have may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

And further this Ext. Benjamin Newman Saith that he has been further informed by the said Sarah Parry that her Husband Henry Parry has deserted her and family that she is Poor and unable to maintain herself & family and that the said Sarah Parry and family is relieved by the said Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn this 12th. day of Augt. 1786 R Butler

Benj: Hewman
