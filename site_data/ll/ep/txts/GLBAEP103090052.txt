Middlesex ss  Mary Pool  Widow of Benjamin Poole deced Maketh oath That divers Years ago her said late husband rented a house Called the George Ale house in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and Paid above Ten Pounds a year rent for the same and Continued therein for above the Space of a year and by Means thereof gained a legal there And this Deponent Saith that her said husband did not afterwards rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent settlement to her knowledge or belief And this Deponent Saith that since her late husbands death she hath not lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year nor been Married Nor hath done any Act whereby to gain a settlement in her own right since her said husbands death to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 17th: day of December 1766  Chris. Scott

Mary Pool Her Mark
