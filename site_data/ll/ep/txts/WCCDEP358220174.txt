Middx to wit Sarah Donnison Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Nelson an Oilman in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Weeks since and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born says that on Mr. Vandcross who now lodges at the said Mr. Nelsons House had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of June last but the particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which time she got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as aforesaid and that the said Mr. Vandcross and no other Person did beget the same that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 27th. day of Augt. 1770 before me} 

Sarah Donison
