Middx  Susannah Bignall aged abt. Twenty two Years upon Oath says She never was married says She lived a hired yearly Servant for two Years & upwards with Mr. Joel Miles a Painter in little Sheer Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Libty of Westmr. within the County of Middlx at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abt. one Year & a half & hath not since she quitted the said Mr. Miles's Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 1st. Octr. 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Suanna Bignall
