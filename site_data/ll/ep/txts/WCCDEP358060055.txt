Middx  Rd. Alver upon Oath saith he Served his Apprentiship with his father Rd. Alver decd. in the Parish of St Stephen in Brownwalls in the County of Cornwall and that he never was a Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 15th. 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Rd. Alver
