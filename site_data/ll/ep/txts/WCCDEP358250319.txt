Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Matthews aged about 31 years & who is now Chargeable to this Parish upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Samuel Matthews her present husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Peter at Saint Albans by whom she hath Three Children living Mr. Elizth. aged about Eight years John aged about Six Years and William aged about Four Years all provided for That her said Husband Rented Two houses in Seward Street in the Parish of Saint Luke in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Thirty Pounds that he hath quitted said  House about Four Years since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Octr. 1797 before us N Bond E Read

Elizabeth Matthews
