Middlesex ss}  Edward Lock maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to & Served the Whole thereof with Robert Hunt a Baker in the parish of Busdale in the County of Suffolk & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement & that he hath a Wife named Catherine to Whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 24. day of January 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Edward Lock
