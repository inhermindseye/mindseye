Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Arabella James aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that one or about the 23rd. day of April 1788 she Intermarried with Luke James her present husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Epsom by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. John aged about Nine Ten years That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Cherry Garden in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surrey for Seven Months or thereabouts at the Yearly rent of Seventeen Pounds that he hath quitted said House upwards of Two years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act. Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Janry. 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

her Arabella James mark
