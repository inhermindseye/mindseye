St. Clement Danes

Middx to wit Ann Owens aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mrs. Lambert in Church Row Aldgate in the Parish of Saint Botoplh Botolph Aldgate in the City of London for 2 years & upwards at the yearly Wages of £6 that she hath quitted said Service about 3 years since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 14th. day of April 1788  Thos. Gordon  Fra Whitfeld :

Ann Owens
