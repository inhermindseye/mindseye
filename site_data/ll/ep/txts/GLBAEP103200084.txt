Middlesex ss}  William Smith Maketh oath that he was duly bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprentices with Thomas Toms a Lighterman in Burr Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & has not since such his Apprenticeship done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere & that he hath a Wife named Sarah to whom he was lawfully Married

Sworn this 20 day of Decr 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of William Smith
