Middlesex  Margaret Minnitt the Widow of Richard Minnitt deced maketh oath that her said late husband Rented a house in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement nor hath she this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself & that she was lawfully married to her late husband

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Margaret Minnitt
