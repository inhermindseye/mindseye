Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Dean the widow of Henry Dean the younger deced maketh oath That her said late Husbands Father Henry Dean the elder deced Rented a House in Postern Row in the Precinct of the Tower Without  Within the Liberty of his Majesties Tower of London & resided therein for divers Years & that during Such his Residence there was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same as this Depont. has been informed & believes And also Served the office of Overseer of the Poor there & by means there of gained a legal Settlement in the said Precinct & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That this Deponts. Said late husbands Settlement at the time of his death was in the said Precinct in right of & as derivate from his Said Father he never having rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any any Act to this Deponts knowledge or belief to gain a Settlemt. in his own right & that She this Depont. Since her Sd. husbands death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this first day of July 1775 before Burf Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Elizth Dean
