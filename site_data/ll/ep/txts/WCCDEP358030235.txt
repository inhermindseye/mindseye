Middx  Sarah Kipping aged upwards of 54 Yrs. Wido. of Wm. Kipping upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the sd. Wm. Kipping about 14 Years since in Gravesend Church in the County of Kent Says her sd. late husband hath been dead about half a Years Says before his Death he Informed her he was born in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx where his Father Andrew Kipping rented a house of upwds. of £20 a Year & paid his Share of all Parochiall Taxes Says her sd. late husband Wm. Kipping Informed her he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with a Butcher in Claremarkett in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes Says the sd. Wm. Kipping never rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 24 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Sarah Kipping
