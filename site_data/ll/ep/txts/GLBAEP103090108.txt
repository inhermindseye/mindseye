Middlesex ss}  Ambrose Wise maketh oath That about Seventeen years ago he Rented a House in the parish of saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds and lived in the same one year and three quarters and paid the Kings Tax and other Taxes for the same and by means thereof as he is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Deponent saith that he hath not since Rented a House of Ten pounds a year nor paid any parish or King Taxes nor served any parish offices nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a Wife Named Sarah and one Child Named Esther aged about Seven Months the lawfull issue of this Deponent by his said Wife

Sworn the 3d. day of June 1768 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Ambrose Wise His Mark
