Middx  George Harper aged about 64 Years upon his Oath Saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Tho. Munday at the Red Lyon in Kensington in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds for the Space of two Years & upwards and hath left his said Service about 40 Years Says he hath not since he left the sd. Mr. Munday Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 7th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

George Harper
