Middx.  Elizabeth King aged about 26 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Westwood Hosier in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds and hath quitted that service about One Year and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born says that One Christr. Smith a Soldier in the Savoy Barracks had Carnal knowledge of her body in the house of Mr Crook a Silversmith in Pump Court in White hart Yard Drury Lane sometime in the latter End of March 1759 or beginning of April following and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and the said Christr. Smith and no other person did beget the same

Sworn 2d. Octr. 1759 before}

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Burnthwaite Overseer . J: P:
