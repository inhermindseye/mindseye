Westmr.  William Peace aged about 36 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Ann his wife in the Parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West London about Ten Years ago, by whom he has three Children, Evans aged about nine Years, Alexander aged upwards of four Years and Henry aged about seven Weeks, says he was bound Apprentice for seven Years, to Mr. Weston a Plaisterer in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and served upwards of three Years of his said Apprenticeship  and then his said Master died; says he continued to serve the Widow of his said Master until the Expiration of the said Indenture of Apprenticeship and never rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 9th, of Janry. 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant

William Peace
