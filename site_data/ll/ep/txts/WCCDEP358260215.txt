Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Frances Bowen aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Luxton in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Thirteen Months at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings that she hath quitted said service about Seven years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of August 1797 before me G S Goodenough

her Frances Bowen mark
