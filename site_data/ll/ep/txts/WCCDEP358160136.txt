Middx  The Exaicon of Elizth. the wife of Robt. Wilkinson Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 22d. day of May 1740 who upon her Oath Saith that in the Month of Febry 1736 She Intermarryed with the above named Robt. Wilkinson and that he the sd. Robt. Wilkinson lived abt. Eight Months the latter part of his Apprentisipp with one Mr. Smith in Bond Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . & hath been out of his sd. Apprentishipp upwards of two Years the sd. Robt. Wilkinson liveing with the sd. Mr. Smith at the time of this Exaiants Intermarryage with him, And this Exaiant further Saith that her sd. Husband never rented Ten Pounds a Yeare pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that this exaiant hath two Children by her sd. Husband (to wit) Robt. Aged upwards of two Years & Edwd . Aged abt. four Months & he haveing absconded from her is Poor & not able to maintain them

Sworn the day & Yeare above menconed before}

The mark of Elizth. Wilkinson
