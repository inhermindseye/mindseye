Middx.  Henry Edwards aged upwards of 40 Years lodging at Mrs. Andrews's in Change Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Upon Oath says he Rented a house in little Cranburn Alley in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Thirty pounds lived therein about One Year and hath quitted same One Year or thereabouts since which time he hath not Rented House or paid Taxes, says he hath two Children by Ann his Deceased Wife (to wit) Robert aged about thirteen Years and Thomas aged about Nine Years

Sworn the 21st. May 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant  John Cox

Henry Edwards
