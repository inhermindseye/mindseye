Westmr.  Eliz Leach upon Oath saith she is the wido . of John Leach who dyed upwds. of five Years ago and that her sd. late husband Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Eleven pounds in Craven Yard in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . lived therein Seven Years & upwards & never after he left the sd. house (nor has this Examint. since his Decease rented ten Pounds of a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn Feby. 24th. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Leach
