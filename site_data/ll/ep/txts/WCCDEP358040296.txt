Westmr. 

Ann Richardson  Wido : of Mathew Richardson who dyed about 3 Weeks ago upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd Mathew Richardson at the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn about 11 Years since and that her sd. late husband Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Mr. Scarlett an Optician near the Church in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief - that she hath three Children now living viz Richd. aged abt. 10 Yrs Mary aged abt. 6 Yrs & Andrew aged abt. 4 Yrs.

Sworn Decr. 13th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Ann Richardson
