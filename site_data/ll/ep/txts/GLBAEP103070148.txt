Middx ss  William Harrison maketh oath that about Twenty three years ago he was bound apprentice to Joseph Lodine of the parish of St. Andrew Holbourn with whom he served about two years of his apprenticeship and then this Depont. was turned over by his sd. Master to Charles Hall of Allen Street in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in ye County of Middx  Smith with whom he servd the residue of his apprenticeship & by means thereof gained a legal settlemt. in the sd Parish of St.James Clerkenwell And this Depont has not since gained any subsequent Settlemt. never having Rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor servd any Parish offices And this Depont saith he hath a wife named Margaret And three Children to wit John aged about Twelve years and halt Elizabeth aged about Ten years & Margaret aged about three years the lawfull issue of this Depont by a former wife.

Sworn this 5th day of Decr. 1755 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

William Harrisson
