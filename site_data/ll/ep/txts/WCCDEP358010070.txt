Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Slarke aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Marrd. Rented House or paid Taxes that she was bound apprentice by Indres duly executed to one Thos. Nash a Pawnbroker in Holles Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 7 years that she served about 6 years of her said Apprenticeship that she run away from her said Master since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Febry. 1787  Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Slarke
