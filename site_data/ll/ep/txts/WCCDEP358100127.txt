Middx to wit George Shearwin Aged about Thirty Seven Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Jane his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Twelve Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children now living namely Jane Aged about five Years and Rose Ann aged about Seven Weeks says he Rented and lived in a House in Bedford Bury in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Field in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of £20 and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Six Years ago and after quitting said House he rented Different apartments in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds but after quitting the said Parish he never Rented House Tenement or Lodgings of Ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained any other Settlement than as before set forth.

Sworn this 24th day of Septr. 1783 before  James Paine  Jno. Barnfather

George Shearwin
