St. Clement Danes March 27th . 1792.

Middx & Westminster }  St James Westmr .  (to wit) Elizth. Kirby aged 22 years and upwds. Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she rented and resided in an Apartment at No:12 in Berwick Street in the parish of St James in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County of Middlesex for Eight Months or upwards at the Weekly Rent of One Guinea that the said Apartment consisted of two Rooms on the one Pair of Stairs Floor and a back Room on the Floor next above that and the End of the Kitchen that the said Rooms were ready furnished and that she Verily believes the said Premises in which else so resided were unfurnished by the that She quitted that Apartment about Six years ago and hath never since obtained any other legal Settlement And that She is now pregnant & unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 25th. March 1792 before us} Sampr. Wright M Addington

Elizth. Kirby 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
