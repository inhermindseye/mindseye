Westmt.  Ann Mathews aged abt. 57 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of John Mathews deceased to whom she was marryed at the Fleet London abt. 34 Years ago that her sd. husband dyed abt. 10 Yrs. since and that he informed this Examinant he lived a yearly Servant Whilst a Singleman wth. Mr Timson a Smith in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for three Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds Dyet Washing & Lodging for the first Year and that his Wages was afterwards advanced which information this Examinant verily believes to be true and that she after the Expiration of this Service as afsd. neither her sd. late husband nor has this Ext. since his Death rented ten pounds by the Year pd. parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn July 20th. 1754 : before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Mathews
