Middlesex ss}  Joseph Hill maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre for Seven Years to Ann Gladman of the Parish of St Peter in Cornhill in the City of London  Trunkmaker & Served the whole of Such his Apprenticeship there That he never rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish Office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement subsequent to his Apprenticeship

Sworn this 11. day of Novemr 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley Joseph Hill
