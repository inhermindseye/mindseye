Middx.  Ann Kent aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath says she never was married & that she lived a a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Greenough a Shoemaker in the Strand near Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath quitted said Service abt. a Year & half & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn this 7th. day of Decr. 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Kent
