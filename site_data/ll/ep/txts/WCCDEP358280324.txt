St.. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } (to wit) Elizabeth Ware Aged about thirty Nine years Upon her Oath saith that She never was Married or gained any settlement in her own Right that her Father James Ware (who died about Six years ago in his lifte life time for Many years Rented & lived in an House in High Street in the parish of Tewksbury in the County of Gloucester at the Annual Rent of about 30 or 40 Shillings & regularly paid the Poor Rate for the same that this Deponents Mother hath since her Husband decease been relieved by the said parish of Tewksbury that she this Examinant was born in lful Matrimony And further saith that she hath for Ten years paid and upwards she hath Cohabited with James Lincoln she lately resided at No. 3 in Yeates's Court Candy Street in the said parish of St Clement Danes & is employed by Several Sheriff Officers as a Man in possession of Captured Goods & generally to be found at the Lion & Cat in Carey Street by whom she has had & been deled of Six Children Children two at which only are now living Vizt. James Aged Eight years & upwards of which she was deled on the 17th. day of July 1782 at Mr Griffins house in Clements Lane in the said parish and Sarah Aged 16 Months of which she was delivered on 9th Janry. 1790 at the House of Mr. Copes in Yeates's Court both in the said parish of St. Clement Danes And this Examinant further saith that he the said James Lincoln is the only

Relue real and true Father thereof he & no one else having several times had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body & begotten having begotten the same thereon

Sworn the 18th May 1791 before me} R Butler

Elizth. Ware 's Mark
