Westmr  Elizabeth Forrester Aged about 63 Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of William Forrester who has been dead about Twelve Years with whom She Intermarried at the Parish Church of Newport in Shropshire about Forty Years ago Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Wych Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds or thereabouts lived therein upwards of Two Years and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same and quitted Same about 20 Years and never after Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes neither hath this Examinant Since her said Husbands death Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th Day of Febry 1769 Before me} Tho Lane

her Elizth: Forrester Mark Witness Pet. Sidebotham
