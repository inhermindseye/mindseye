Middx.  Lucy Connell aged abt. 37 yrs. lodging at a common lodging house in Kings Head Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Libty of Westmr. upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Dean a Shoemaker in Clare Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid for One Year and Upward at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted said Service abt. Eight Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 10th. Sepr. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Lucy Connell
