Westmr. to wit Jane Spickanall Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived as a Hired Yearly Servant with Sir John Hind Cotton in Charles Street Berkley Square in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for Seventeen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds Six Shillings hath quitted her said Service about five Years ago and hath not since she quitted her said Service ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 17th. day of June 1771 before me} John Machin R A

Jane Spickarnll
