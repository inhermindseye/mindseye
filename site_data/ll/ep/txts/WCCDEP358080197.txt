Middx & Westmr-}  Richard Lowe aged about forty eight Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his now Wife  Hannah at the Parish Church of St- Brides about nine Years (ago and by whom she hath two Children now provided for vizt- Elizabeth aged about nine Years and Sarah aged about two Years Says he served his Apprenticeship with one Francis Mc.Canna  Brushmaker  the Corner of White Heart Yard in the parish of Saint Giles's in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr -County of a Middx and after he was out of his Apprenticeship he never Rented house paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlemt-

Sworn this 23rd- Day of Decr- 1777 before M Addington Edwd: Naish

Richd. Lowe
