Middx ss  Anthony Brush son of John Brush  Gun maker deced Maketh oath that about forty Years ago his sd father rented a house of Twenty five pounds by ye Year in Lad Lane in the Parish of St Michael Wood street in the City of London And that by Means thereof this Depont. gained a legal Settlement there in right of his sd. father And this Depont. saith that he has not since gained any subsequent settlemt. havg. Never been An Apprentice or hired Servant by the year Nor rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards nor served any Parish offices nor done any Act to Acquire a subsequent Settlemt. And that he hath a Child Named Elizth. aged seven Years & upwards the lawful issue of this Depont. by his late wife Mary Deced

Sworn this 19th day of July 1756 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

his Anthony Brush Mark
