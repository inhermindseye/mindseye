Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Joseph Pearson aged about 68 years upon his Oath saith that he is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes that he Rented and lived in a house in Duke Street York Buildings in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Twenty years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds that he hath quitted said house about Twelve years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Febry. 1798 before us } Rupt: Clarke W Carter

Josh: Pearson

H Day Beadle
