Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Walker aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Cox in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx & City & Liberty of Westmr . for 12 Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about 5 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent of £10 for 40 days or paid Taxes And further this Ext. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & unable to maintain same when born And further saith that Richard Besson a Serjeant at Woolwich Warren & who is at prest. on a Recruiting Party at Lynn Regis at Norfolk is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime the Month of March last and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the Child or Children of which she is so pregnant

Sworn this 10th. day of Novr. 1787 } Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Walker Mark

Richd. Besson is a Tall Man wears his Hair tyed in a Twist of a dark Complexion comes out of Lincolnshire

he belongs to the Train of a tilitting 5 Foot 9 or 10. a Dark Complexion at the Salutation Woolwick Child bron 24h Novr. Child and Ann Besson
