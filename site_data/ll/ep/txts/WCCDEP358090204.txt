Middx  Ann Morgan Aged about Fifty One years on her Oath says she is Widow of Abraham Morgan who died about Fourteen years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived as a hired yearly servant with one Mr. Jennings a Taylor in Henrietta Street Covent Garden in the Parish of saint Pane Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex for One year and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said service about four years ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement

Sworn this 8th. Day of March 1781 before}

her Ann Morgan Mark
