Middx  Mary King aged abt. 37 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Waite a Staymaker in C hange Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of one Year at the Yearly Wages of & hath left her sd. Service abt. 10 or Eleven Years & hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne May 25th: 1745 before}
