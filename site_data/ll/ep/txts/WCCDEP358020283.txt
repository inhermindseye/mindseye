St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster) (to wit Margaret Forrest Aged 22 years old or thereabouts on her Oath saith that on the 12th.. day of January 1790 she intermarried with her present Husband William Forrest at the parish Church of Saint Mary le Strand in the said Liberty and County that her said Husband hath Informed her this Deponent and which she Verily believes to be true that her said Husband was born at the Artichoke in White Hart Yard in the parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the said Liberty and County and she hath been Informed and believes that her said Husband is an was is an was illegitimate Son and She hath also been that he never lived as an hired yearly Servant for one year or did any other Act or thing whereby in his own Right he might have acquired any legal any any other Subsequent legal Settlement And she farther saith her said Husband is now a Seaman on board His Majesty's Ship the Inconstant and that she is Poor and unable to maintain herself at Court during her lying in

Sworn the 10th day of May 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker
