Middx to wit Bartholomew Lowe Aged about 43 Years on his oath says he Intermarried to his late Wife Elizth. Lowe Deceased at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about 10 years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children living Says before his Intermarr to his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he served seven years Apprenticeship with one Wm. Edwards a Watchmaker in in Fetter Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenemt. of £10. a year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 9th. day of Augt. 1783 R Butler

Bartholomew Lowe
