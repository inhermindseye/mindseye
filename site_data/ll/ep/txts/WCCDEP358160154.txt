Middx  The Examination of Wm. Brooks aged upwds. of 50 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 14th. Day of July 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that his late Father Mathew Brooks Rented & lived in a House in Clements Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes of the yearly Rent of £10 & lived in the same for some Years, And this Examint. further saith yt. he never Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beliefe that he is now Poor & Lame & not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before}
