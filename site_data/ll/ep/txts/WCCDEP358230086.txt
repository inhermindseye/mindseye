Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Fell aged about 46 years upon his Oath saith that about Twenty two years ago he Intermarried with Elizabeth his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Pancras That he this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That his father William Fell Rented and lived in a house in Newcastle Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Ten years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty one Pounds That after he quitted same he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Augt. 1799 before me Menter

William Fell
