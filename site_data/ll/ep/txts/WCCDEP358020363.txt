St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Ann Finch  widow Aged 38 years thereabouts Upon Oath saith that about Sixteen years ago she was Married to her late Husband Thomas Finch at the parish Church of St. Mary Lambeth since their Marriage her said Husband Rented and with her Resided in an Apartment in the House the Corner Round Court in the street in the parish of St Martin in the Fields with Liberty of Westminster in the said County for about two Years and an half at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and ten Shillings that the same Apartments during all the said Term was hired as an furnished and furnished by this Deponent said Husband that her said Husband served his Apprenticeship at he hall Intermarried this Deponent with Mr. Hurst in Hedge Lane in the said parish of St. Martin in the fields with whom he ledged the whole that she this Deponent hath been in the Workhouse belonging to and been relieved by the said parish that she was in the said Workhouse only so lately as about twelve Months ago that her Child and that her said Husband in his Life time had not nor hath she since his Decease acquired any other subsequent others legal Settlement than by Renting the said Apartment in round Court as aforesaid

Sworn the 2d Decr. 1791 before us  Philip Dyer D Walker

Ann Finch
