Middlesex & Westminster}  Sarah Blissett aged about 39 years upon her Oath Saith That she was Married to Wm. Blisset her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn abt. 15 years ago that since their Intermarriage her said Husband rented a House in Bennets Court Drury Lane for upwards of 7 years at the yearly rent of £14 during the first 5 years and the yearly Rent of £16 for the remaining 2 years that they quitted the said House abt 7 years ago that her said Husband departed this Life abt. 3 years ago that since they quitted the said House in Bennet Court aforesaid her said Husband did not gain any other subsequent settlement to the best of this Deponents Knowledge or Belief nor has this Deponent since his Death rented any House lived is Lodging of £10 a year nor lived as an hired yearly servd. nor done any or Act Mre or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 21st. day of October 1784 -}

Never Come to be Sworn
