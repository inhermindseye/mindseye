Middx to wit Catherine Davis Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Gustavus Brandor  Esqr . in Crown Street in the Parish of St. Margaretts Westmr . upwards of Six Years at the Yearly Wages of Eleven pounds Eleven Shillings hath quitted her Said Service upwards of Two Years ago since which time she hath gained on other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that one Robert Boycott  Gentleman did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal Knowledge thereof in a house near Charing Cross the 29th. day of March last and at one other time afterwards and believes it was at the same house at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Robert Boycot & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 22d. day of Decr. 1778 before.}

Catharine Davis
