Middlesex and Westminster}  Swear to St. Clemt T to wit Ann Lucey Aged about 61 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Edwd Lucey at the Parish Church of  Fleet London in the year 1749 and who died upwards of 28 years ago Says that since her said Husbands Death and having no Child or Children then being she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Redhead in Arrundell Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for upward of Twelve Months hath quitted her said Service upwards of 25 years ago since which time she has gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 13th Decr. 1784 before R. Butler

her Ann Lucey mark
