Middx 

Ann Topp upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Edward Topp with whom she Intermarried on the 13th. March 1742 at the Fleet London Says her said husband has been dead near four months and that before her Intermarriage with him he was bound by Article in writing dated the 22d. Febry 1741 to Serve John Crang of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Organ maker for the Term of Seven Years and did Serve the said John Crang the whole Term of Seven Years pursuant to the said Article and during all that time Lodged in the Parish of St Clemt Danes aforesaid

Says her said late husband in his Life time or this Examint. since his death never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 27th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Ann Topp
