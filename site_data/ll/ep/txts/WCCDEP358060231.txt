Middx  John Vicary of the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx maketh Oath that he was Informed by Mary Cholmondley lately deceased that she the said Mary Cholmondley was the wife of Thomas Cholmondley who absconded from her & that both herself & her sd. husband were Natives of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England & also that she had a Child named Eleanor aged about five Years who was born at Mr. Geos being the Sign of the Golden Rey in Shoemaker Row Black fryers in the Parish of St. Ann Black fryers London

Sworn Decr 18th 1751 before  Tho Lane .

J. Vicary
