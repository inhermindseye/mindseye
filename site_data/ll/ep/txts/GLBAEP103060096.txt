Parish of Aldgate Middx

Hannah Rice of Swan Alley in the Said Parish Maketh Oath that her late Father Dunkin Mackquin , livd in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint John of Wapping where he was a Bayliff and kept the Sign of the Hand and Elipstaf and She heard him Say that he paid ten pound a year Rent for the House And also hath heard her late mother declare the Same That She hath a Sister named Ann Mackquin aged about 21 years who is now Infirm, who was born in the Said House in Nightingale Lane And in the Said parish of Saint John of Wapping And that her Said Sister never was an Apprentice Nor hired Servant by the year

Sworn the 1st day of March 1747 before us Robert Dennett Richd: Ricards

the mark of Hannah Rice
