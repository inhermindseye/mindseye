Middx  Francis Maden (In Jacobs Well Yard Nightingale Lane ) Maketh Oath and Saith that About Twenty years ago her Brother James Lindsey was bound Apprentice by Indenture for to Wm- Elmston at Aldgate in the parish of Saint Kathren Catherine Cree in the City of London Gold Necklace Maker with whom he served there upwards of three Years And then was discharged from his said Master by Consent and Gained no Subsequent Settlement to this Depts. knowledge.

Sworn 9th. Novr 1743 Before Robert Dennett

her Frances Maden mark

Ann Gould Maketh Oath that About Eleven years ago she Intermarried with the Above named James Lindsey who And knows not of Any Subsequent Settlemt. She has gained And that she hath two Children by the sd James Ann Frances aged About ten Years and Thomas aged About Six Years having afterwds. Marrd. sd. Gould but knows of no Settlemt he Gained and is now dead.

Sworn 9th Novr. 1743 before Robert Dennett

her Ann Gould  mark
