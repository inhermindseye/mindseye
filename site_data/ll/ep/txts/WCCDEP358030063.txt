Middx  Jane Hudson  Wido . of Herbert Hudson upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Herbert Hudson in the Parish Church of St. Ann in Blackfryers about 13 Yrs. since Says her sd. Husband hath been dead about 2 Months Says he lived in and rented a house in Peter Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £50 for many Yrs. Says her sd. late Husband never after he left sd. house rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochiall Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she hath not in since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Exaiants knowledge or beleif

Sworn 6: Decr. 1745 before W Jones.

The Mark of Jane Hadson
