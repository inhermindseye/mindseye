1st Examination desertion

St Clement Danes 30th July 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  4 Children  desertion (to wit) Mary Carrad aged thirty one years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband William Carrad at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex on or about the twenty third day of July 1786 that her said Husband has now Rented and Resided in a House and premises in Star Court Butcher Row in the parish of St Clement Danes  which for four years and upwards at the annual Rent of eighteen Guineas Twenty two pounds that he still occupies and Resides in the said House premises at that Rent that She hath four Children by her said Husband now with her this Examinant namely Richard aged Six years on the 9th day of May last William aged four years in the fifteenth day of February last Mary aged three years on the 30th day of August 1792 and Thomas born on the second day of July in the said year 1792 that her said Husband neglects and refuses to maintain herself and Children and that she is pregnant destitute unable to provide for herself and family without assistance and they are thereby become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 30th day of July 1793 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Mary Carrad 's Mark

Witness Garrett
