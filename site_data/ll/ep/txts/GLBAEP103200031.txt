By Servitude

Middlesex ss}  Ann Arnold  Singlewoman maketh oath that heretofore she was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with one Martin a Cooper in the Minories in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of one year & upwards & hath not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d day of July 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Arnold
