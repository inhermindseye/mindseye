St. Clemt. Danes

Elizth. Moore aged 17 years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or served any Apprenticeship Says her Father Thos. Moore Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Dodd of Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster that after her sd. Father was out of his Apprenticeship he never did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Deponents knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Janry. 1786  Sampr Wright M Addington

Eliz Moore
