Middx.  Catherine Smith Aged abt. Thirty Years lodging at Mr. Bathridges in Kingshead Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Upon Oath Says that by Indenture dated the Seventh day of Octr. 1754 she was Bound are Apprentice to & Served three Years Apprentiship with James Adams of Coppice Row in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the sd. County of Middx Says She was never Married Rented Ten pounds paid parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn the 16th. day of Janry 1764 before} Paul Vaillant  John Lane

The mark of Cath: Smith

Upon Mr. Pontens Complt.
