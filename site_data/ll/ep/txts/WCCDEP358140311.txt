Middx.  Elizth. Brammah aged abt. 39 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Thos. Brammah (who has inlisted himself into his Majesty's Service as a Soldier) with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn about thirteen Years ago by whom she has four Children Richd. aged Ten Years Thos. aged Seven Years Elizth. aged four Years and Mary Ann aged abt. One Year says her said husband Rented a house in Newcastle Court in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds or thereabts. and lived in the same abt. Two Years, that he quitted that house abt. two Years ago and has not Since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge and belief.

Sworn this 27th. day of October 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Elizth. Brammah
