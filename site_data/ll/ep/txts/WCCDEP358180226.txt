Middx  Mary Gibbeter aged 37 Years upon her Oath saith that about 12 Years since she intermarried with her prest. Husband  Wm: Gibbeter at the Fleet London that the said Wm: Gibbeter was a Sailor on Board his Majestys Ship the Berwick in Port makone Harbour the last time this Exaiant heard from him Says her said husband was born in the House of one Mr. Gascoign in Swallow Street in the parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. and never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or served any Parochiall Office as she hath been Informed & beleives Says her Husbands father  John Gibbeter was borne in Bear Street by Leicester feilds in the Parish of St Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. aforesaid that the sd. Jno. Gibbeter never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office as this Examinant hath also been Informed & beleives

Sworne 30th: Augt. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Gibbeter
