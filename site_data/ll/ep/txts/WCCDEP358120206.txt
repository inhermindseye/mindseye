Middx  Eliz; Tresonner upon her Voluntary Oath saith She is the Wido. of Peter Tresonner who dyed upwards of three Years ago and that on the Twenty fifth or Twenty Sixth day of March last she was delivered of a female bastard Child (which was baptized Hannah ) at a house in Nightingal Lane in East Smithfield and that Richd. Brown Foreman of the Labourers in the Victualling Office did beget the said female bastard Child on her body the said Richard Brown having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant Several times in the Month of June 1753 at one of which times he got her with Child of the aforesaid female bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Richd. Brown and no other person is the true and real father thereof and that Examinant further maketh Oath that at the

Instigation of the aforesaid Richard Brown the said female bastard Child was dropt in Shings Arms Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on Monday the 27th. Day of May last by means whereof it became Chargeable to the sd. Parish & there as this Examint. has been informed dyed abt. since

Sworn Octr. 3. 1754 before Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz Tresonner
