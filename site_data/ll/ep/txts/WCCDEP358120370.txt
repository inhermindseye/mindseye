Middx  Jacob Archer upon Oath saith he was married to his wife Ann Archer at the Church of St. Peter & Paul in Bath abt. 10 Yrs ago & has by her 2 Childn. Wm. aged abt. 9 Yrs. & Mary aged near 6 Years and that he Served 3 Years of his Apprentiship wth. Wm. Batty  a Taylor in Personage Lane in the afsd. Parish of St. Peter in Paul in Bath and at the End of the sd. three Years absconded from his sd. master and never was assign over nor ever rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn June 23d. 1755 before me}
