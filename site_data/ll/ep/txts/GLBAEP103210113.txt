Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Barbara Williamson of the Parish of saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Rice Davies Esqr  on of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Seventh day of November 1785 Who Saith that on the fifth day of October last past at the Workhouse of the said Parish in the County aforesd she the said Barbara Williamson was delivered of a Female Bastard Child and that the said child is become chargeable to the said Parish and that John Bowers late of the Precinct of St Catherine in the Sd County Mariner (now on a Voyage to Maddass) did get her with child of the Sd Bastard Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me R Davies

The Mark of Barbara Williamson
