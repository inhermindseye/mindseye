Middx  Martha Brumby Aged about Forty Nine Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Thomas Brumby with whom she Intermarried in the parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. about Thirty One Years ago Says her said Husband whilst a Singleman & without Child or Children Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Thorp a Butcher in Clare Markett in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of Eight Years at the Yearly Rent of twenty Guineas & never after Rented Ten pounds by the Year or paid parish Taxes nor she this Examinant hath not since his Death Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 10th Day of August 1764 Before}

Martha Brumby
