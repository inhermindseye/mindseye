Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Worrell aged about 52 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Kelly in Dyot Street in the parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Four years and upwards at the yearly wages of Fifty Shillings that she hath quitted said Service about Thirty years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Augt. 1795 N Bond Russl: Clarke

her Ann Worrell mark

Witness B Newman
