Middx ss  Stephen Preston Maketh oath that about Ninteen Years ago he this Deponent lived as a hired servant by the year with one of the parish of Marr in the County of york Farmer and Continued in such service for the Space of one whole year at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds And by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. saith that he has not since lived as a hired servant by the year nor served any Parish offices nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor done any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlement And this Depont saith that he has a wife named Sarah and tow Children to wit James Aged about fourteen years (now at sea) and Mary Aged about Twelve years the lawful Issue of this Depont by his sd wife

Sworn this 21 day of June 1758 before us  Richd: Ricards

The mark of Stephen Preston
