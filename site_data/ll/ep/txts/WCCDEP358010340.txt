Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Rachael Spicer aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that on the 18th. day of Janry. 1783 she Intermarried with James Spicer her Present husband (who is entered on Board a ship ) at the Parish Church of saint Peter Port in the Island of Guernsey by whom she hath Three Children living Vizt. Ann aged about six years Elizabeth aged about Three years and Rachael aged about Two years That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in chair Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Six Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds that he hath quitted said house about one Month since which time he has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of May 1790 R Butler

her Rachael Spicer Mark
