Middx  Martha Stevens Aged about Thirty Two Years On her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Broughton Junr. in Gilberts Passage Clare Markett Upwards of One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service Upwards of Two Years ago since which time She hath gained no Other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath says that on Sunday the 13th. day of Feb last She was delivered of Two Bastard Children the One a Male and the other a Female in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the Names of William and Sarah And is now Chargeable to the said Parish (She being poor and Unable to Maintain the same) And that One-Mr. George Search on Attorney at No. 10 Grevill Street Hatton Garden in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time the beginning of June last at S erjant Inn Coffee House Chancery Lane and at Several different time Afterwards at One of which Ju he got her with Chit of the Children of which She was  delivered us aforesaid and that the said George search and no other person did beget same And that he is the Real and time Father thereof.

Sworn this 20th. day of March 1780 before R Butler

her Martha Stevens Mark
