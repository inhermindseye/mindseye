Middlesex  John Davis maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indres to his Father John Davis of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Served Six Years or there abouts of Such his Apprenticeship with his Said Father who was by Trade a Cooper in the Said Parish & then this Deponent left his Said Father & went to Sea where Continued So to do for many Years & did not afterwards return back to live with his Said Father That he never lived as a hired servant for a Years nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwrs nor ever Served any Parish office or ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement that he hath a wife named Sarah & four Children namely Thomas Aged about twelve Years Samuel  aged about Nine Years & Nine Months John Aged about four years & Edward Aged about one year & Nine Months the lawful Issue of this Deponet by his Said wife to Whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this. 20. day of November 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of John Davis
