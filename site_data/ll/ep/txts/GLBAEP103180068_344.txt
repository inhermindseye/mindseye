Middlesex ss}  Mary Trimble the widow of John Trimble deced maketh oath that her said late husband was by Birth an Irishman & never did any Act to this Deponents knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in any part of Great Britain That She this Deponent was born in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married bound an Apprentice nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself Save What She has Acquired by her Marriage as aforesaid & this Deponent Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents or either of them

Sworn this 5th day of October 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Trimble
