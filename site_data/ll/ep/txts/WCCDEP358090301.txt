Middx to wit Elizth. Hughes Aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to John Hughes (who hath deserted her at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn about Nine Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Crofts in James Street Bedford Row in the said Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex and after quitting the said Mr. Crofts's Service he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise  gained any other Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 12th. day of March 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Hughes
