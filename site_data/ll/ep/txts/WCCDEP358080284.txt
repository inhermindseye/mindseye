Middx to wit Elizth. Page aged about Thirty five years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Robt. Page (who is now in the Militia at Cox heath ) and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St Anns Westminster about five years ago and by whom she hath no Child now living says her said Husband was bound an Apprentice for Seven years and served most part of his Apprenticeship with one Thos. Spencer a Shoe Maker in Hungerford Markett in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and after quitting his said Masters Service he never Rented house or Tenement of £10 a year paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 14th. day of Septr. 1778 before  Wm. Kitchiner J Welch

her Elizth Page Mark
