Middx ss  The Examinacon of Elizabeth Phillips  Singlewoman Taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the County of Middx residg. in or near to the limits of the parish Church of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd. County this 21 Day of August 1749

This Examinant upon her oath Saith that in or abt the month of December last one John Bullwinkle then and now of the precinct of Saint Katherine near the Tower of London  Victualler Did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at his house in Ditchside in the said precinct of St. Katherine and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body & so had Several times whereby She Conceived with child or Children of which She now goeth and is pregnant And this Examinant Saith that the said Child or Children of which she now goeth and is pregnant as aforesaid is or are likely to be born a bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate she not being able to provide for the Same And that the Sd. John Bullwinkle is the True & only father of the Said Child or Children So likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesd. And lastly Saith that this Examinacon is voluntary And that she hath not been forced or compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 21 Day of August 1749 before  Richd: Ricards

The mark of Elizth Phillips
