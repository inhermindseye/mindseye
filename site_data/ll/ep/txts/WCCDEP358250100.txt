St. Clement Danes March 25th 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Leonard Middx (to wit) Isabella Young  singlewoman aged 25 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married never lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve month together or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby he obtain any legal settlement in her own right And this Examinant further saith that she believes she was born in lawful Wedlock that she is informed (and which she believes to be true) that her Father James Young Rents and resides in an house in Old Street Road in the parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex and which he has Rented and resided in for several years and that he says the parish Rates for the same

Sworn the 25th day of March 1793 before us} Mitchell H Mathew

Isabella  Young 's Mark

Witness Garrett
