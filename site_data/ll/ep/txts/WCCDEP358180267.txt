Middx  Gilbert Hall Aged about 46 Years upon his Oath saith he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Henry Butler Pacey  Esqr . in Cecil Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of one Year & upwards & hath left the sd. Mr. Pacey's Service upwds. of 1 Year since which time he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Decr. 19th. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

The mark of Gilbert Hall
