St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Mary Compton  Widow aged 56 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to John Compton a Mathematics Instrument Maker about 27 or 28 year ago by Bams at the parish Church of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey  Surry that he Died about 14 years ago, that she had one still born Child and another Child who died in her Infancy in the life time of her said Husband that since his Death and while she was a Widow and unmarried having no Child or Children she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Veale Decease Court 2 years or thereabouts at the Annual Wages of £5 that she quitted that Service between 3 & 4 years ago and hath never since been Married nor obtained any subsequent legal settlement

Sworn the 13th day of March 1793 before me} Wm. Kitchiner

Mary Compton
