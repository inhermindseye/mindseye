Middx ss.  Wright Dickinson Brother of Robert Dickinson deced Maketh oath that his sd. Brother was born in the Parish of Mumby  in the County of Lincoln And by Means thereof gained a Legal Settlement there And this Deponts. father Wright Dickinson deced legally gained a Settlemt. in ye sd. Parish of Mumby And this Deponent farther Saith that his sd. Brother Robert Dickinson never rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year & upwards Nor Served any Parochial offices to this Deponent knowledge or belief And that his sd. Brother Robert Dickinson left behind him a Daughter Now living Who is of Age Named Maria Dickinson

Sworn this day of Augt. 1752 before us}
