Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Bowler  Widow of John Bowler deced maketh oath That since the decease of her said late husband She this Depont. was hired and lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages with Thomas Heard of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Victualler and Continued in such Service for above the Space of a Year and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there and this Deponent Saith that she has not been Married since her said husbands Death nor hath lived as hired servant for the Space of a year since her Servitude as above Nor done any Act since her said Servitude to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of November 1767 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Eleanor Bowler
