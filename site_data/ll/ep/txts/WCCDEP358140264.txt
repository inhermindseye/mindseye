Middx.  Anthony Cook aged abt. 56 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his wife in the Parish Church of Alhallows on London Wall abt. Twenty Years ago; says before his intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Robt. Hall at the Craven head in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx for Two years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. Twenty Seven Years & hath not since Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 7th. July 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Antho Cook
