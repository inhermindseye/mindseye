Westmr. to wit Sarah Poole Aged about 23 Years on her Oath says she never was married or ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Peele a Tallow Chandler in Newport Markett in the Parish of St. Anns Soho in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further says that on Friday the fifth day of July last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Palke a Shoemaker in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized by the Name Mary and that William Woollam a Journeyman Linnen Draper who now lives with Mrs. Hussey in Hayes's Court Newport Markett is the real and true Father thereof and no other Person whatsoever

Sworn this 24 day of July 1771 before me} R. Butler

her Sarah Poole Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
