Middlesex ss}  Mary Warren the widow of John Thomas Warren maketh oath that she has been informed by her Said late husband & which she believes to be true that he was only bound by Indenture for the Term of Seven years to John Westcott of Peters Court Ironmonger Row in the Parish of St Luke in the sd County Gold & Silver Wire Drawer & after having Served about one half of his said Apprenticeship there he was at or upon his Said masters death duly Assigned or turned over to one Read of Bridgewaters Garden in the Parish of St Giles without Cripplegate London  Gold & Silver Wire Drawer in which last mentioned Parish he Served the remainder of his Sd Apprenticeship & did not afterwards to this Deponts Knowledge or belief do any Act to gain Anothr Settlement That she was lawfully married to her sd husband & Since his death has not done any Act to gain a settlement for herself.

Sworn this day of Septemr 1783 before}
