Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

taken & a Bond. given to wit Ann Rafter aged about 36 years on her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Timothy Rafter at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields about 14 years ago that and who has been dead upwards of four years that her said Husband was an Irishman and never gained any Settlement in  England that before their Intermarriage the lived as a hired yearly Servant with General Harvey in Cleveland Row in the Parish of Saint James Westmr. for two years at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted said service about 14 years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere And further on her Voluntary Oath saith that on the 28th. day of Novr. 1782 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Ellams Ship Yard which hath since been Christened by the Name of John Price that John Price who uses the Kings Head Coffee House Joy Lane is the father thereof he

having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body and begotten the same thereon and she is sick and Poor and unable to maintain the said Child whereby the same must become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 1st. Day of Novr. 1785 before me J Addington

her Ann Rafter mark
