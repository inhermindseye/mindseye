Middx  David Welding of Swan Alley in the parish of St. Botolph, witht. Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Comb maker maketh Oath and Saith that John Turney now Rents a House of upwards of ten pounds a year in the parish of St Olave Silver Street in the City of London and that he has lived therein upwards of a Year and an half to the Best of this Deponents knowledge and Belief

Sworn this 11th. day of February 1744 Before Robert Dennett

David Welden
