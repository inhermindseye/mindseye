Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Bayfield the widow of William Bayfield deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her and which She believes to be true that previous to her Marriage with him he was hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Alex Warren in the Minories in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the City of London  Linen Draper & Continue there in Such Service under Such hiring for divers years That her Said husband after the marriage Celebrated between him her this Deponent took & rented a Dining Room a Bed Chambers and the use of the Kitchen in the Dwelling house of Mr Stephen Stillwell in Dulwich in the Parish of Camberwell in the County of Surry The which Lodgings were furnished by the Sd Mr Stillwell and the Rent Paid for the Same by this Deponents husband was nine Guineas by the Year & where he resided for the Space of three Years and upwards That her Said husband to her knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex nor elsewhere otherwise than as above is State & Set forth That She hath five Children namely Elizabeth aged eleven years William aged nine years Gabriel aged Six years Thomas aged four years and Samuel aged two years the lawful Issue of this Depont by her Said late husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 26. day of October 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Bayfield
