Middlesex to wit Sarah Johnson aged about Forty five years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Edward Johnson her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx about Nineteen years ago and by whom she hath 9 Children living four of whom are Capable of getting their Bread and five are not and whose names are as follows Vizt. Maria Aged about Ten years George Aged upwards of Six years John aged about five years Mary aged upwards of Three years and Ann aged about two years Says her said late Husband Rented & lived in a House at Beconfield in the County of Bucks for twelve Months at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds hath quitted the said said House about four months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this day of January 1784}
