Middlesex ss}  Susannah Dighton maketh oath that She can give no account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents deced or either of them That She this Deponent as She has been credibly informed & believes was born in Churchyard Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County That She this Depont. never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this. 17. day of July 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Susannah Dighton
