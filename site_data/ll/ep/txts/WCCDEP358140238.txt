Middx.  Mary Williams aged upwds. of 40 Years upon Oath says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Francis Main in Bury Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath quitted said Service upwards of Twenty Years; since which time She hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year & further says she never was Married

Sworn this 22d. March 1759 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Mary Williams

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Matravers Ovr. J: P:
