Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Holmes aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1783 she Intermarried with Isaac Holmes her Present Husband at the parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone that she has no Child or Children living by her said Husband that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Carey Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 9 Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £20 & upwards that he hath quitted said House about 16 Moths ago since which time her said Husband has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And Further this Ext. saith that her said Husband has deserted her and refuses to maintain her she being poor and unable to maintain herself is therefore Obliged to Apply to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes for relief

Sworn this 30th. day of Decr. 1786 R Butler

Jane Holmes
