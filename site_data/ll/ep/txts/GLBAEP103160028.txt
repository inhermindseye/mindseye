Middlesex ss}  Luke How maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to James Taylor of New Tothill Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westminster in the County of Middlesex  Cordwainer for the Term of Seven Years & duly Served him there for the Term of Five Years & half & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & then differences Arising between this Deponent & his Said Master his Said Master took this Depont. before a Magistrate & Agreed that this Deponent might leave him and go about his Business & get himself another Master for that he would trouble himself no farther with this Deponent & promised that he would deliver up this Deponents Indres to him whenever he this Deponent Should get another Master He this Deponent being then about the Age of Nineteen Years & not more That he this Deponent thereupon left the Service of his Said Master Taylor & hired himself to live with one William Vollum of White Cross Street in the Parish of Saint Luke in the Said County Cordwainer for the remainder of the Term of his Sd Apprenticeship but without any Consent given thereto by his Said Master Taylor and this Deponent there upon sent to the Said James Taylor for his Indentures which he refused to deliver up to this Deponent or his Said Second Master Vollum That he this Deponent continued in the Said Vollums Service during the remainder of the Term for Which he was bound Apprentice as aforesaid & was never Called upon by his said Master Taylor to account with him for any Money which he might earn during his Continuance in the Service of the Said Vollum or of any other Person Nor did the Said James Taylor ever inquire after this Deponent during Such last mentioned Service as far as he knows or make any provision for him Nor did the Said Taylor ever give any Consent in Writing or otherwise to this Deponents living with the Said Vollum in Manner as before stated that he this Deponent knows of or has been informed That he this Deponent has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith P. Rowlands

The Mark of Luke How
