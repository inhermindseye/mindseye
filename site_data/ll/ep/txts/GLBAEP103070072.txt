Middx ss. The Examination of Catherine Taylor Single Woman taken upon oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of ye Peace for the County of Middx (one whereof is of ye quorum) residing in or Near to the limits of ye Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in ye sd. County this 9th day of December 1752

This Examinant on her Oath Saith that abt. two Years and a quarter ago James Wright  Mariner who then lodged in the house of one Mr. Stokes in Nightingale Lane in ye sd. Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate illegible/> did often come to ship Examinants Lodgings at one Mrs. Graingers in three Ton Court in ye Parish of St. John Wapping in ye County of Middx and did there prevail on this Examinant to lye with her and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body and often aftd. man there & at other places did lye wich this Examinant And that this Examinant Conceived with Child by him of a Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered at ye house of this Examinants in King

Harry Yard in ye sd. Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate about eighteen Months Ago which sd. Bastard Child hath been baptised by ye Name of Mary and Now is & for a Considerable time past hath been & still is likely to become Chargeable to ye sd. Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate this Examinant being poor & Not able to provide for ye Same And Lastly this Examinant Saith that ye sd. James Wright is the true& only father of ye sd. female Bastard Child And that this Examination is Voluntary And ye. she hath Not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons Whatsoever

Sworn this 9th day of Decr. 1752 before us  Richd: Ricards  Thos: Quarrill

The Mark of Catherine Taylor
