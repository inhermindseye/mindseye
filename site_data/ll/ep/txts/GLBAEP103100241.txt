Middlesex ss  John Jagwood maketh oath that divers years ago for was hired and lived as a hires Servant by the year with one Mr Arnold a Coin Chandler in allen Street in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex at Certain yearly Wages and Continued in such Service for Several years this Deponent being then a Single man and by means there of as this Deponent is advised he gained a Legal Settlement there and this Deponent Saith that he has not since lives as a hired Servant for the space of a year nor rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor hath he ever served any Parish Office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done ann act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement Since his Servitude as abovementioned and thus he hath a wife namd. Sarah .

Sworn this Second Day of March 1770 before  Chris: Scott  Rob Pell

The Mark of John Jagwood .
