Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Charles Veale aged about 46 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about four years ago that he has one Child living vizt. Ann aged about Two years that he this Examinant Rented and lived in a House in Old Nicol Street Bethnal Green in the Parish of Saint Matthew Bethnal Green in the County of Middlesex for Six Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Two years ago since which time he has not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Decr. 1788 W: Bleamire J Barnfather

his Charles Veal Mark
