Middx ss & Westmr  Mary Jupp Aged about fifty Three Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her late Husband John Jupp at the Fleet London in the Year 1774 and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said late Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Thomas Perry a Cooper  the Corner of Poland Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House or Tenement or orwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 20th. day of Augt. 1776 before  Wm. Kitchiner R Butler

her Mary Jupp
