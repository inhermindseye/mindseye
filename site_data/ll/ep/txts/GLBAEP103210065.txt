Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Furrier  Singlewoman maketh oath that she has been informed by her Grand mother Susannah Jusky & which she believes to be true that her late Father Michael Furrier deced duly served his Apprenticeship in the Parish of Saint olave in Southwarke in the County of Surrey and in Consequence thereof died in the Workhouse there as being legally Settled in the said Parish at the time of his death That she this Deponent was born and baptized there & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 15. day of February 1785 before J. Hawley, Robt. Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Furrier
