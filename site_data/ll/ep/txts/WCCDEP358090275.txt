Middx to wit Mary Beckett aged about 33 Years on her Oath says she never was married rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Lady Barrymore in Postman Square in the parish of St. Mary le Bone in the County of Middlesex a Year and Nine Month at the Yearly Wages of Twelve pounds, hath quitted said Service about three Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 14th. day of January 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Becket Mark
