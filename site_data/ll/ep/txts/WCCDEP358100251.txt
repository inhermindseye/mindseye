Middx  Ann Slaughter Aged about Twenty Seven years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Steven a Distiller in the Parish of Kingston upon Thames in the County of Surry upwds. of Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwds. of five years ago since which time she never lived a hired yearly Servant And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 20th. day of August last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Rippingate Chandler back of St. Clements which is intended to be baptized by the name of Robt . And is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further says that one James Hirst a Master Waggoner who lives at Peckworth in Sussex is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at Kingston upon Thames sometime in the Month of Decr. last & at sevl. difft. times aftwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. And that the said Robt . first & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 6th. day of Septr. 1784  Wm Kitchiner
