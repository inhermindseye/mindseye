Westmr.  Robert Hall on Behalf of John Pope upon Oath saith he has been informed and believes the sd. John Pope rented a house in Hedge Lane in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and has not since he quitted the sd house rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn 14th. June 1751 before GL. Fouace Robrt: Hall
