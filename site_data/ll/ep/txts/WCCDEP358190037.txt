Middx.  Eliz: Somerhill aged abt. 19 yrs. upon Oath says she was never married & that she was bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Syson in the County of Gloucester to Joseph Barnes a Sadlers in the Parish of St. Philip & Jacob near Bristol in the County aforesaid until she should attain the age of Twenty one years or Marriage as she hath been informed & verily believes & lived with the said Joseph Barnes several years & never was assigned over to any other Person

Sworn this 29th of Augst. 1757 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz. Somerhill
