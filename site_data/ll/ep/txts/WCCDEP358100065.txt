Middlesex to wit Nanny Walker Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Woodhouse at the Temple Coffee House in Devereux Court Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago since which time she hath never lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargable to the said Parish (she being Poor and Unable to maintain the same when born) And that one Edward Mattox a Waiter at her sd late Masters House is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the beginning of December last but the particular day she does not now remember and at several Different times afterwards in her said late Masters House at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Edward Mattox and no other person did begett the same and that she is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 2d Day of May 1783 before R Butler

her Nanny Walker mark
