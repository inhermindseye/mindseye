Westmr.  Margt. Charrett Wife of Richard Charrett (now on board the Prince George Man of Warr at Spithead ) upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the said Richd. Charrett at the Parish Church of Portsea in the County of Southampton on the 9th. Day of June 1745 Says her sd. husband Served upwards of one Year the latter part of his Apprentiship with Mr. Drew a Poulterer in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. as she hath been informed & beleives Says her sd. husband never rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 12. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Margt Charrett
