Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Connell Wife of Edwds. Connell passed as a Vagrant from the Parish of St. Saviour Southwarke by Order under the hand & Seale of Jno. Copeland bearing Date ye. 10th. Day of Decr. Instant Who upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband is an Irish Man & never gained any Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or beleife & yt. before her Marriage with the sd. Connell She this Exaiant lived a Yearly hired servt . with one Mr. Thos. Baston  Coffee-Man in Lincolns Inn Feilds in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in ye. County of Middx for the Space of one Ye. & upwards at ye. Yearly Wages of £4 And this Exaiant further saith yt. she hath not since yt. time rented £10 a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. of otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That her sd. Husband is now at Sea & yt. she hath 2 Children (to wit) Jno. aged upwds. of 3 Years & Amey aged upwds of 1 Year & being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworne this 15 Day of Decr. 1740 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Elizth. Connell
