St Clement Danes 13th Decr 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Margt. Westmr (to wit) Margaret Anderson aged 53 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her late Husband Thomas Anderson at the parish Church of St John the Evangelist  Westminster on or about the third day of July 1774 that subsequent thereto her said Husband rented and resided in a House in Gardners Lane  Duke Street in the parish of St Margaret  Westmr . at the annual Rent of £26 for about four years and quitted the same in or about the year 1781 or 2 and hath not since done did not after do any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the last of this Examinants knowledge information or belief that he Died in or about this year 1785 and she this Examinant hath not since been Married or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement in her own right

Sworn the day and year first above written before us N Bond Tho Vaughn

Margaret Anderson
