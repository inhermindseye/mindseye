Middx to wit John Dell Aged about Twenty Three years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired yearly servant for One year says his Father William Dell Rented and lived in a House on Syon Hill in the Parish of Isleworth in the County of Middlesex for five years and Upwards and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of One year and an half ago since which time this Examinant hath gained no other settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly servant for one year

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1781 . before. R Butler

John Dell
