Middx ss The Examination of Mary Huggins  Singlewomen taken on oath before Me one of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the County of Middx residing in or near the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx this 24th. Day of August 1751 .

This Examinant on her oath Saith That in or about Seven Months ago one William Griffin of George Yard in the parish of st. Michael Cornhill London  Drawer did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with of her at the Dwelling house of Mr. Nathaniel Edmonds in George Yard aforesd. Where this Depont. & ye sd William Griffin lived together as Servants and the sd. William Griffin had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body & so had Several times Whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she Now goeth and is pregnant And this Examint. Saith that thesd. Child or Children of

Which she now goeth with and is pregnant at aforesd. is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to thesd. Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx she not being able to provide for the Same And that thesd. William Griffin is the true and only Father of the sd. Child or Children so likely to be born a bastard or bastards as aforesd. And lastly Saith that this Examination is Voluntary by Voluntary And that she hath ust been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons Whatsoever

Sworn this 24th. day of August 1751 before me  Richd Ricards

Mary huggins
