Middlesex ss}  Ann Pilson the wife of John Pilson maketh oath That She hath been informed by her Said husband (and Which information She believes to be true) That divers Years ago he was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Mr Gooding a Pawn Broker in Dowling Street in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex at Certain Yearly Wages and Continued in Such Service there for the Space of a year & upwards and by means thereof as She this Depont. is advised her Said husband gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge information or belief her Said husband hath not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish offices nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act Since his living as a hired Servant in the Said Parish to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 29th day of March 1771 before Wilmot B Camper

Ann Pilson Her Mark
