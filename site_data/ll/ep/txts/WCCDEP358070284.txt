Westmr  Mary Haberley Aged about Twenty Eight Years, on her Oath says, she intermarried with Edward Haberley (gone from her) at the Parish Church of Shoreditch about Six Years ago, & by who she hath Three Children now living, namely Mary Aged about five Years, Richard aged about Three Years, & Edward aged about Two Years; Says that her said Husband has one other Child by a former Wife named Robert aged upwards of Eight Years, And further says that her said Husbands before his intermarriage with his said first Wife, and whilst he was a Singleman lived as a hired Yearly servant with Thomas Sewell  Esqr now Sr Thos Sewell  Knt in Lincolns Inn Square in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex , for Seven Years and upwards, at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds, or thereabouts, hath quitted his said Service about Twelve Years ago, and hath not since he Quitted the same ever rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, or done any other Act where to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 12th day. of Augt 1775 , before R Butler Sampn. Wright

her Mary Haberley Mark Witness James Viger
