Westmr. 

Eliz: Pugh wife of James Pugh removed from the Parish of St. Martin in the fields by Order dated the 15th. Instt. upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. James Pugh on the 20th. Day of Decr. last at the hand and Pen next Door to the China Shop facing fleet market and that her sd. husband was by Indenture dated the 6th. Day of August 1735 bound Apprentice to one Mr. Brian Robinson a Turner & Served him as an Apprentice about Six Years and then the sd. Mr. Robinson dying this Examinants sd. husband was assigned over for the remainder of his Apprenticeship to Mr. Perry a Turner in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. with whom he served out his Apprenticeship and Continued to live with the sd. Mr. Parry after he was out of his time hill his intermarriage with this Examinant Says her sd. husband never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn June 16th. 1748 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Eliz Pugh
