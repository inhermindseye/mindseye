Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Edward Mc.Carthy of No. 7 Clements Lane on behalf of Catherine Crowley an Infant of the Age of 5 years or thereabouts which was left at this Exts House on the 25th. day of June last past Maketh Oath & Saith that the said Cath: Crowley is the illegitimate of Child of one Rebecca Croke that the said Rebecca Croke Swore at the Parish of Saint John at Wapping in the Coy. of Middx the said illegitimate Child to one Chas. Crowley that the Officers of the sd. Parish took the said Chas . and bad Security from him that they allowed to the said Chas . 1s. Per week to Nurse & take care of it himself And further this Ext. Upon his Oath saith that the said Rebecca the Mother assd. lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Andrew Cailey a Publican in Sir Wm Warrens Square in the said Parish of Saint John at Wapping in the Coy. of Middx for 18 Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 that she had quitted said Service about 5 years since which time she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of July 1787 G Mercer Jno. Barnfather

Edward McCarthy
