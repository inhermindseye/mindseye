St Clement Danes

Ann Macklin  Widow aged 52 years or thereabouts maketh Oath and saith that about six Years ago and since the Decease of her late Husband John Macklin  Silk Dyer she lived are hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Darvent at No. 33 in Lawrence Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary le Bow London for the space of Three years and an half together at seven Pounds a Year Wages and since she quitted that Service she hath not lived an hired Yearly Servant for one whole Year together Rented a Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year Paid any Parish or other Taxes contracted Matrimony or done any Act whatsoever which to her Knowledge or Belief may have gained her a Settlement in any Parish or place

Sworn this 1st. Day of December 1790 Sampn Wright W Addington

Ann Macklin
