Westmr. to wit Elizth. Reeves aged about 40 Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Wm. Reeves gone from her with whom she Intermarried at the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields the 1st. day of April five years last past And by whom she hath one Child now living named Sarah aged about 9 Months Says her said late Husband served Seven years apprenticeship with one Mr. Bellamy a Book binder in Clement Lane in Lombard Street in the Parish of Saint Edmund the  Kings in the City of London and After he was out of his said apprentice Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 1st. day of February 1772 before R. Butler W Kelyng

her Elizth. Reeves mark
