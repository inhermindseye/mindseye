St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster }  (St CD)  (to wit) Mary Pettit Aged 48 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith That she never was Married that about Eight years ago she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Shergold Clare Street Clare Market in the said parish of St Clement (Connent Danes for thirteen Months wanting about five or Six days and since quitting the farne hath done us Act to acquired any subsequent legal Settlement And that she is now distreped & much wanting Relief

Sworn the 5th. Augt. 1791 before Me R Butler.

Mary Pettit .
