St. Clement Danes June 14th. 1791 .

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Mary Taylor ( Widow of Richard Taylor deced) Aged twenty two years and upwards Maketh Oath and saith That she was Married to her said late Husband Richard Taylor at the parish Church (as she believes) of St. John Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex abt. 18 Months ago that her said Husband afterwards hath since Rented & with her lived in an House Numbered 35 in Gibraltar Row in the parish of St. George in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry about a Quarter of a year at the Yearly Rent of thirteen Guineas that then he when her said Husband Died and that after his Dece she this Deponent Rented the same House & continued to dwell there in for Six Weeks or thereabouts & that then one Chas. Taylor the a Brother of her said late Husband took the said House of her this Examinant & Rented it on his own Acct. and that she since she he so took th & Occupied the said House she this Deponent hath done no Act or thing wtsoever whereby to acquire a Subsequent Settlement And this Examinant further saith she hath an Infant Male Child Named Charles Richard Aged Nine Months or thereabouts born of her bo Body in lawful Matrimony begotten by her said, late Husband who is now with her this Deponent And that she is incapable of maintaining herself and said Child

Sworn the 14th. May 1791 befor before us R Butler D Walker

Mary Talor
