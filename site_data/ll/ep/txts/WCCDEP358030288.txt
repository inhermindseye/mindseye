Westmr.  Jno. Witchell on Behalf of Eliz: Witchell aged about Sixteen Years and George Witchell aged about Eleven Years the Children of Francis Witchell by Hannah his Wife both deceased upon his Oath Saith the said Elizabeth Witchell and George Witchell are this Exaiant Brother and Sister Says the said Francis Witchell lived in and rented a house in Markett Lane in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards & lived in the same upwards of two Years Says the said Fras. Witchell nor Hanh. his Wife never after they left the said house in Market Street rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says the sd. Eliz Witchell & George Witchell never were Apprentices or Yearly Servants for one Year or otherwise gained

A Settlemt. in any other place to this Exaiant knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 19th 1746 before  Tho Lane  Tho: Burdas

John Witchell
