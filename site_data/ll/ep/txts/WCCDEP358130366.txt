Middx.  Mary Judith Wooldridge aged abt. 29 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of arthur Wooldridge an who hath been dead abt. three Weeks by whom she hath one Child Mary aged three yrs. says her sd. husbd. served three years & upwards the first part of his apprenticeship to his Brother Josias Wooldridge an aneil maker in the Parish of Old Swinford  in the County of Worcester & then his Brother & this Examinants Husbd. parted by Consent & never was assigned over to any Person Rented ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes or was a yrly Servt.

Sworn the 13th. of June 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Judith Wooldridge
