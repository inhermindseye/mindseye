Middx  Mary Craig aged about 27 Years Passed from the Borough of Portsmouth upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Samuel Craig on board the Pembroke Man of War to whom she was Married at May Fair Chapel the 15th. day of July 1752 Says her said Husband lived a Covenant Servant for Two Years with Mr. Adlard, Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx and hath left his said Master's Service upwards of Nine Years and never after Rented any House paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant, That she hath two Children by her said Husband to wit Elizabeth aged 7 Years & Mary aged upwards of One Year

Sworn the day of April 1761 Before}
