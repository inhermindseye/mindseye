Middlesex, to wit, Jane Munt , Singlewoman , maketh Oath and Saith, she hath been informed and believes that she was born at Gibraltar about the Year One Thousand Seven Hundred and Sixty, that in the following Year she came to England with her father, who rented a House in Flaggon Row in the Parish of Saint Nicholas Deptford in the County of Kent , in which he resided for the space of three or four Years, and was rated to and paid Poor's Rate and other Taxes for the same, that her said Father lived in the said Parish for the Space of Eighteen Years or thereabouts during all which time she this Deponent lived with him as part of his family, And this Deponent saith that she has not gained a Settlement in her own right in any Parish Precinct or Place whatsoever

Sworn this 30th Day of August 1792 , before}
