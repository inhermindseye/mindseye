Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Isabella Woollaston aged about 67 Years upon her Oath saith that she hath lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Botibol in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for these four Years wanting five weeks last pasd at the yearly wages of Seven Pounds

Sworn this 6th- day of Febry- 1799 before me} Jams Edwards

her Isabella Woollaston Mark
