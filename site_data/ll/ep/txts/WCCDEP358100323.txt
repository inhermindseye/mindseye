Saint Clement Danes Decr. 24: 1784

Nothing done C  Jane Morrell aged 46 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she was born at Philips Norton in Wiltshire That her Father was born in the same Parish and resided there till his Death, That her Grandfather belonged to the said Parish, That she Married one Stephen Morrell (a Native of Ireland ) who came to Rent as Journeyman with her Father, That the said Stephen Morrell came to London and this Examinant with him That the said Stephen Morrell Never Rented Ten pounds a year, paid Taxes, Served any Parish Office or did any other Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a Settlement in this Kingdom as this deponent Believes And that he departed this Life about Fifteen years ago. And this deponent hath not done any Thing since his Decease whereby She may have gained a Settlement, And this Examinant Saith that She has not Intermarried since but was on the 19t. day of January 1777 delivered of a Male Bastard Child who was Christened Wilham Holloman at Mr. Malyns the Apple Tree in Cursiter Street in the Liberty of the Rolls And that Stephen Holloman of Bear Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  Journeyman Bricklayer is the Father of the said Child.

Sworn the 24 December 1784 before R. Butler

her Jane Morrell Mark
