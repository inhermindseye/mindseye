Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Martha Cooper aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that about Twelve years ago she Intermarried with Richard Cooper her present husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn That her said Husband Served his Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Preston a Printer in Dean Street Fetter Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Bars in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1797 before us M Addington E Read

her Martha Cooper mark

H Day Beadle
