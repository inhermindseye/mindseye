Westmr. to wit Mary Osman Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Wells in the County of Summerset to one Robert Thacker a Butcher and served her said Master about Five Years at Wells aforesd. and her said Master afterwards came up to Town and resided in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and served the remainer part of her said Apprenticeship with her said Master in the Parish last mentioned since which she never did any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And Further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 14th. day of December 1769 to the best of her remembrance she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. Lyon in Luckner Lane in the parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx which as since been Baptized Jane Ann And further says that in the Month of October 1771 but the Particular Day she does not now remember she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Charter house Lane at the House of one Haggeton in that part of the Parish of Saint Sepulchre which lyes in the County of Middlesex which has since been baptized by the name of William Henry And further says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born say that one Richard Bellasyse  Buckle maker who is now gone from her had Carnal Knowledge or her body some time in the Month of August last and at several different times afterwards at one or which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as afsd. and that the said Richard Bellasyse and no other Person did beget the same and that he his real and true Father

of her said three Bastard Children as before mentioned

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1773 before me W Kelyngs

her Mary Osman Mark

Witness E: Fields
