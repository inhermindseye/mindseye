Middx 

Mary Dry aged about 28 -Yrs upon Oath saith that on or about the 27th. Day of August 1735 she intermarried with her Prest: Husband  Edwd. Dry at the Parish Church of St. Brides London Says the sd. Edwd. Dry was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Jno. Dry a Sawyer in New Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & hath been out of his said Apprentiship about 15 Yrs: since which time he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne the 10th Octr. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Dry
