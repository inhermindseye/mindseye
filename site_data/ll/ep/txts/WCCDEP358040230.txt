Westmr 

Cathr. Stacey aged abt. 32 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwards of 9 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Howard a Baker in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for one Year & upwards and hath not since she Quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge belief

Sworn Augt. 8th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Cathr. Stacey
