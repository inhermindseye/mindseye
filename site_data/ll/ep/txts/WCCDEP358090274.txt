Middlesex to wit Margaret Thomas Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or ever Served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Ryan a Milkman in Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for upwards of Twelve Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Three Years ago since which time she hath ever lived as a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and likely to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes (she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born) And further says that one Evan Jones a Journeyman Blacksmith who lives at Mr. Dawson a Farrier & Blacksmith in St. John Street Clerkenwell is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the House of Mrs. Coventry in Islington upwards of Twelve Months ago and at several different times and places afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is new pregnant and that the said Evan Jones and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 5th day of January 1782 R Butler

her Margt. Thomas Mark
