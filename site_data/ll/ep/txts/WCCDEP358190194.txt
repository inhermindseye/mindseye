Middx  Ann Rigby aged about 50 Years Lodging in St. Clement's Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upon Oath saith she is the Widow of James Rigby who has been Dead about Five Years to whom she was Married at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields near Thirty Years ago That he was a Victualler and lived in and Rented a House the Corner of Burlington Street in Piccadilly in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for Several Years at the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds Also Says she was Passed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields to the said Parish of St. James about Three Years ago and hath not since Rented any House or been a hired Servant for One Year.

Sworn the day of Octor. 1761 Before}
