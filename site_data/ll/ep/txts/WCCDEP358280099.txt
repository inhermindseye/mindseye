Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Catherine Kinney aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Peter Marshall in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 3 years & upwards at the yearly Wages of £4..10..0 that she hath quitted her said Service about 2 years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servt. for 12 Months or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Thomas Allen a Journeyman Taylor who lately worked with Mr. Gray a Taylor in Stanhope Street afsd. and lodged at No. 4 in Clare Court  Drury Lane is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of Decr. last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Thomas Allen  is the only real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day of June 1787 } R Butler

her Catherine Kinney  mark
