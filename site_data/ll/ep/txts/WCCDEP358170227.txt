Middx  Mary Corker aged Thirty Three Years upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido . of Dan & Corker lately deceased that her said late Husband before his Death lived in & rented a House in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds where this Examinant still Continues to live that she hath 3 Childn. (to witt) Hannah aged near 7 Yrs Danl . near 5 Years & Mary abt. 2 Years & being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne 15th. May 1742 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Corker
