Middx.  Mary Place aged abt. 21 Years upon Oath says She never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mortimer the third door from Devereux Court in the Strand towards Temple Bar in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for Sixteen Months at the yearly wages of Five Pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. Two Months. And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes and that one John FitzJohn a Carpenter (who lately lodged at Mr. Barker's a Silk dyer by Salisbury Street in the Strand but now absconds) had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the house of the said Mr. Mortimer on or abt. the Twenty seventh day of December last and at one other time since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or

Middx.  or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said John FitzJohn and no other person is the true and Real father thereof. and further Says She cannot give any Information where the said John FitzJohn may be found.

Sworn this 10th. August 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Place
