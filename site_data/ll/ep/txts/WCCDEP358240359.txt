St Clement Danes  Novr. Decr. 7th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Margaret Child aged twenty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married served any Apprenticeship lived as an hired yearly Servant or did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own rights that she hath been informed and believes she is the legitimate Daughter of Michael and Margaret Childs both deceased that her said Father Rented and Resided in a House in Yeates's Court in the parish of St Clement Danes for sometime and never after quitting that House did Any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief during his Life neither did her said Mother after his decease

Sworn the 7th day of December 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Margaret Child
