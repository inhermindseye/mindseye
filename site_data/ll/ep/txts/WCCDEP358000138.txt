passd. to St Mary le Strand (to wit) Sarah Towcester Aged 27 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married Rented a House or Tenement of £10 a year or ever paid any Taxes Saith That she was Bound an Apprentice to One Silvester Field of Feathers Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Co. of Middx Staymaker by the Governors of the Foundling Hospital for the Term of 7 years or Day of Marriage that She Served the said Term of 7 years According to the said Indres of Apprenticeship That since, she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or Belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of her Knowledge and belief and that she is Poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 25th. day of Octor. 1785 G Allcock G Mercer

her Sarah Towcester Mark
