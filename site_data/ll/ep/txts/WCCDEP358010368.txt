Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary King Aged 69 or there abouts upon her Oath Saith That she was born at Saffron Waldron Essex that she came to London about 56 years ago That she inter married with Jas. Currie a Hair mercht . at Chelmsford in the County of Essex that she had four Children by him all dead but one named Geo. That her husband Jas. Currie about 40 Years ago- That about thirty years ago she intermarried at the Parish Church of St Clements Danes to Walter King  Peruke maker who lived in Wych St & rented a House of £21. a year that he informed her he had been a Housekeeper in the Parish near 40 years That he died in the House in Wych Street That after his Death she kept it for 3 years & pd. £21. a year for it That since she quitted the House Wych Street she has lodged at various places in the Parish of St Clemts. Danes but never was married again or Lodged out of the Parish or done any Act to gain settlement elsewhere to the best of her knowledge Information or belief a

this Examinant further saith that S is poor and unable to gain her lively by Working.

Sworn this 1st. Novr 1790 before me R Butler

her Mary King Mark
