Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr. to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadle of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that Maria aged about 4 years born a Bastard on the Body of Jane Johnston was in the House of a Mr. Metcalfe in Castle Street Oxford Road in Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the said County of Middlesex and Elizth . aged about 5 weeks born a Bastard on the Body of the said Jane Johnston was in the House of a Mr. Collins in Brod Street Saint Giles's in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the said County of Middlesex

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry. 1787  Thos: Gordons  Sampr Wright Benj: Newman
