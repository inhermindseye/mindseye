Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Joshua Lassell aged about 68 years upon his Oath saith that about forty four years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present wife at Lincolns Inn Chapel That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Change Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for about Three years at the yearly Rent of Twenty Guineas that he hath quitted said House about fourteen years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Janry. 1796 } N Bond

Jos: Lassell
