Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit James Talboys one of Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Rachel Williams who will maketh Oath & saith that he has been informed by the said Rachel Williams which Information he verily believes to be true That her Husband James Williams Rented & lived in a house in Woods Close in the parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for Three Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards and paid all Taxes due for the same and that after he quitted said house he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of febry. 1795 }

Jas Talboys
