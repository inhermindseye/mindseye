Middx  Cath: Munday aged abt. Nineteen Years upon her Oath saith that she was never married & that abt. Six Years ago she was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Bourne in the Parish of St. Clement Danes Taylor & served him as an Apprentice for the Space of 3 Years or thereabts- but she & her Master not agreeing they parted by Consent And this Examinant further saith that she hath not since she left her sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife yt. she is now Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 25th Day of June 1741 before}

The Mark of Cath: Munday
