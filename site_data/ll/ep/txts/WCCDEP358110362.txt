Middx  Mary Low upon Oath Saith She is the wido . of Thomas Low to whom she was married at the Fleet London on the 8th. April 1736 & that her sd. late husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds behind St Clemts. lived therein abt. Six Years & quitted the same in the Year 1738 & never afterwards nor has this Examinant since his Decease rented Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Decr. 1st. 1753 before Barth: Hammond

Mary Lowe
