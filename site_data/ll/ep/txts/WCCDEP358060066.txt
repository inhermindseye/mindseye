Middx  Jas. Levalle aged about 19 Years upon Oath saith he never was married and that about three Years ago according to the best of his Remembrance he lived a Servant with one Mr Scott at the Kings Arms Alehouse the Corner of Exeter Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and hath quitted his sd. Service upwds. of one Year Says when he went to Mr Scot to be hired he asked three pounds a Year but Scot replied he would not give that Wages but would find him Dyet Washing Lodging and Cloaths in lieu thereof and under that Agreement he lived with the sd. Scot about 1 Yr. & 9 months and has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn 31st. Janry 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell

James Lavelley
