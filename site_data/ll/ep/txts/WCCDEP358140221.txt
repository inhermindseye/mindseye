Middx.  Eliz: Appleyard aged abt. 58 Yrs. upon Oath says she  is the Widow of Thos. Appleyard who has been dead about Nineteen Years: says her said late husband Rented and lived in a house for three Years ever against Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx. at the yearly Rent of Thirty six Pounds or thereabouts and quitted sd. house abt. Eight Years before his death and never after in his life time nor she this Examinant since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 3d. of February 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Elizth Appelyard
