Westmr.  Daniel Hering Aged about 23 Years on his Oath Says he Intermarred with Elizth . his Wife at the Parish Church of St Clements Danes in the County of Middlesex in June last Says that before his sd Intermarriage with his sd wife he served 7 Yrs. Apprenticeship wth. Jonathan Wood a Blacksmith in Newtoners Lane in the Parish of St Giles in the fields in the said County of Middlesex and after he was out of his sd. Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 4th. day of February 1771 before  Petr Planck W Kelynge

Daniel Herring
