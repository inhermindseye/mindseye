Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Proffet aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she never lived as a hired yearly Servant in any Parochial Place for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or  ever bound and Apprentice That she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that she was born at No. 3 Crown Court Portpool Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex and this Deponent saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 27th. day of Augt. 1798 before us } Rupt: Clarke  Henry Wystead

Elizabeth Proffett
