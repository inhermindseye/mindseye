Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Spence  Single woman maketh oath that divers years ago her late Father Julius Spence rented a house of the yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds in King Street in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was charged to & paid Poor Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor hath She this Deponent to her knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She this Deponent was part of her said Fathers Family When he lived in King Street aforesaid.

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before Parkr Rowlands

Ellinor Spence
