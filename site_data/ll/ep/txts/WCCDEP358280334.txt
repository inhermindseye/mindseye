St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) Elizth. Turner  singlewoman Aged 30 Years and upwards upon her Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr.. John Matthews at the Porter's Lodge belonging to Clements Inn in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty, about 18 Months, at the yearly Wages of Six pounds, that she quitted that Service about 21 Months ago, & hath not (by any means whatever) acquired any Subsequent legal Settlement; And this Deponent further on her Oath saith that on the  twelfth day of June last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of Thomas Turner in the Parish of Norton near Taunton in the County of Somerset who hath not yet been Christened, That one William Pearce (who lives works as as a Journeyman at Mr Deacon a Plumbers near the End of Berwick Street next Oxford Road as this Deponent believes,) is the only real & true father thereof And that he and no one else hath had Carnal knowledge of her Body & begotten the said Child (of which she was so delivered as aforesaid) thereon And that she is unable to support herself and said Child

Sworn before Me the 8th. July 1791 } Wm Kitchiner

Elizth.. Turner 's Mark
