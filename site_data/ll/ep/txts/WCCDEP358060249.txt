Westmr.  Ann Connelly upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Peter Connelly (who at present abscond from her) and that the sd. Peter Connelly is an Irish man & never gained a Settlemt. in England that she this Examinant was born in Barkers Street in the Parish of St Chads in Shrewsbury in the County of Salop and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year nor ever rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn 20th. Jany 1752 before  Saml Pechill

The mark of Ann Connelly
