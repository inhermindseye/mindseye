Middx  Eliz: Hunt aged about 43 Yrs. upon her Oath she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Carr in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds for the Space of One Year & upwards & hath left her sd. Service upwards of Six Years Says she hath not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 13th. 1745 before}
