Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Davis  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was duly bound Apprentice to John Amphlett of Brown Bear alley in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Glase Watch Grinder & Served five years of Such her Apprenticeship in the Sd parish & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain anr Settlement

Sworn this 3d day of Janry 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Davis
