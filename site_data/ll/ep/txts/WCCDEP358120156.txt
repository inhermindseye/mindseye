Middx  Mary Arter aged 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Sherrif in Stanhope Street in the Parish of S t. Clemt. Danes for the Space of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds for the first Year and after the Rate of Four Pounds for the Second Year and that she quitted her sd. Service near 4 months ago and has not since rented ten Pounds a year nor pd. Parish taxes and this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that John Houghton  Apprentice to his Mother Mrs. Houghton a Sadler at Ludlow in the County of Salop did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. John Houghton having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant several times in the months

of December Janry & Febry last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the sd. Jno Houghton & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn July 20th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Arter
