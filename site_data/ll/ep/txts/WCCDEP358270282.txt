Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster  John Catoteld to wit Crissaney George aged about Eighteen years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Reeves in Cecil Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six pounds that she hath quitted said service about Two Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Powell who now lives Servant with the said Mr- Reeves is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant thereon and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of Octr. 1796 }

Crissaney George

Crissaney George at Mr. Clements's No. 1 Plough Court Carey Street .

Man paid £20 on the 8th. Novr. 1796 and Executed a Release

Child born in the house on the 25th. day of Decr. 1796 named John George Sent to Enfield  8th. July 1797
