Saint  Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Stinton aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Lady Hope at Belfont in the Parish of Belfont in the County of Middx for two years at the yearly Wages of Ten Guineas hath quitted said Service about three years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Calendar Months or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Janry. 1787 }

her Mary Stinton  Mark

never Sworn
