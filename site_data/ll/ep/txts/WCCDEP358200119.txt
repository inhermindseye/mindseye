Zabria Himpson Aged about Thirty four Years upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Wm Himpson in the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx about Six Years who has been Dead about Two Years Says her said Husband Rented a House in Change Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about One Year at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds hath quitted said House about Two Years and hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 6th Day of July 1764 Before}

The Mark of Zabria Himpson
