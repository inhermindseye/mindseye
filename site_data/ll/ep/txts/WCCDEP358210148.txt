Westmr.  Sarah Kent aged about 53 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Henry Kent who has been dead about Twenty Years; Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children, she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Parker in Church Entry near Shoemaker Row in the Parish of Saint Anne Blackfryars  London for two Years, or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds quitted said Service about seven Years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 10th, day of April 1767 , before} John Machin 

Sarah Kent
