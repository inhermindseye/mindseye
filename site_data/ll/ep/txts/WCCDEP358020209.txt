Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Martha Sunderland aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that about Seven years ago she hired herself to one Mr. Richard Gill a Farmer at Winterset in Yorkshire as a Servant at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds that she lived with the said Richd. Gill for Twelve Months and that since she quitted said Service she has never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of June 1790  Wm Kitchiner

Martha Sunderland
