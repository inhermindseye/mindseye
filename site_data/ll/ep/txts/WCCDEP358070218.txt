Westmr ss  Prudence Ross Aged about Sixty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Ross who has been Dead upwards of Twenty five Years ago and by whom she had no Children then living Says that being a Widow and without Childr or Children she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Thos Lewis an Apothecary on Ludgate Hill in the Parish of St Bridget otherwise St Bride London upwards of Two Years & an half at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Twenty Years ago and hath not since she Quitted the same ever rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 3d- Day of Novr 1774 before R. Butler W Addington

her Prudence Ross mark
