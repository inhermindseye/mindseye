The Exaicon of Susanna the wife of Jno. Porter Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 25th. day of Septr. 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that her sd. Husband Jno. Porter was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Lampier in the parish of Trinity in the Minories in the sd. County of Middx  Victualler & Served the sd. Mr. Lampier untill such time as he attained the Age of Twenty four Years at wth ago his time Expired And further Saith that her sd. Husband never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband is on Board the Buckingham Man of Warr & she being Sick is not able to provide for herselfe

Sworn the day & Year abovemenconed before} Jas: Gronous

Susan Porter
