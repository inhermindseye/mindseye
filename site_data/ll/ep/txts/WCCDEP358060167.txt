Middx  Mary Welch aged upwds. of 25 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servt . for one Year & upwds. wth. Mrs. Pasqualine at Mr Longs a Taylor next door to the Wine Vault in Bow Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds and Quitted her sd. Service abt. a Year ago Since which she has not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 11th 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Welch
