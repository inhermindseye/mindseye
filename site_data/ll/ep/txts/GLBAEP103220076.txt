Middlesex ss}  Martha Ellard the widow of George Ellard deced maketh oath that Since her Said husbands death She this Deponent lived in and rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the Sd County presided thereon for the Space of Six Months That She has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlemt That She hath three Children namely Paty aged about Eleven Years George aged about eight Years & Nathaniel Aged about five Years & half the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this. 26. day of October 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

Martha Ellard
