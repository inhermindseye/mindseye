Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Harrison of No.1 Three Herring Court Red Cross Street  Spinst Maketh Oath and Saith That Jonathan Harrison the illegitimate Child of Mary Gray formerly Harrison now of the Age of Ten years or thereabouts was born in Store Street Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex that he was not born in the Tying in Hospital but in a Private House on the opposite Side of the Way and that the said Mary Harrison the Mother never was that this dept. knows of or believes Married before she was deled of the said Child and that Richard Ireland late of Bow Street Covent Garden deceased was as this Deponent hath been informed by the said Mary Harrison the Mother and Verily believes the real and true Father of the said Child

Sworn this 26th day of Febry. 1788 N Bond John Croft

her Elizth. Harrison  Mark
