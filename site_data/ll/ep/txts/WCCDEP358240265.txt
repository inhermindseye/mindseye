St Clement Danes May 28th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) William Wrench aged 47 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife Hannah at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes on or about the sixth day of October 1771 (by Banns) that Subsequent thereto he Rented and Resided in an House in Holywell Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Seventeen Years at the Annual Rent of Sixteen pounds that he quitted that House about five years ago and hath not Since Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes or done any any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 28th.. day of May 1793 before Me} D Walker

William Wrench
