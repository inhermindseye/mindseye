Middlesex ss}  Ann Carmichael maketh oath That about three years ago a Marriage or Pretended Marriage was Celebrated between this Deponent and one Thomas Cusser in the Parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate London That sometime after She this Deponent was informed and verily believes the Same to be true That at the time Such marriage was Celebrated as abovementioned He the Said Thomas Cusser was lawfully married to a Certain woman whose Maiden name was Clare Varnum and who as this Depont. has been informed & believes is still living That She this Depont. hath had two children by the Said Thomas Cusser both of whom are yet living to wit Mary aged about two years & a Quarter who was born in Beach Lane in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate in the City of London & was Baptized and registred in the Parish Church of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate aforedsaid by the name of Mary Cusser and the other of Such children who is Aged about Six months was born in Golden Lane in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex and was there Baptized by the name of Thomas Cusser both of Which Children are as this Deponent is advised illegitmate for the reasons before given by this Deponent

Sworn this 19th day of November 1772 before  Saml Spagg  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Carmichael
