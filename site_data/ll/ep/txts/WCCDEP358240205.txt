St Clement Danes 30th Janry. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  ye St C D  (to wit) Isabella Robson  singlewoman aged 74 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Coleham Apothecary in Butcher Row in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for a year and upwards at the Annual Wages of Six Guineas Guineas that She quitted that Service upwards of 30 years ago and hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a two year together at the annual R or done any other Act Matter or thing wt.soever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 30th day of Janry 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Isabella Robson 's Mark

Witness Garrett
