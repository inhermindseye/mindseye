Middlesex ss}  Thomas White maketh oath That about Twelve years ago he lived in & rented a house of the yearly Rent of Six pounds in Sun yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in the Occupation thereof for about the Space of one year & half & during Such his residence therein was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since paid any Kings Taxes elsewhere or done any Act to gain another Settlement That he hath a Wife named Thene To Whom he was lawfully married & a Child named Edward White aged Nine years & upwards the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said  Wife .

Sworn this 10th day of October 1777 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Thomas White
