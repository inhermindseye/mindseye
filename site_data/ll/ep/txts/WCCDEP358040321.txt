Middx  Ann Wild aged upwds. of 29 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwds. of his Years since she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Wood a Watchmaker in the High Street in the Parish of St. Chad in Shrewsbury in the County of Salop for five Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Forty Shillings and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a male Bastard Child on the 13th. of Janry last in the house of Mr. Kitchen in Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex which has been Baptized Thomas and is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same and that Thomas Bowcott of the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes  Blacksmith did beget the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body the sd. Thomas Bowest having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant several times

in the Month of April last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. and that the sd. Thos. Bowcott and no other, Person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Febry 18th 1748 before} Wm: Jones . Tho Lane

The mark of Ann Wild
