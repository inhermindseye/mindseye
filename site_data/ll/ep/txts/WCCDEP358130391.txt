Middx.  Samuel Diaper aged abt. 62 yrs. upon Oath says he was married to Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes abt. 13 yrs ago says he rented a house in Houghton Street Claremarket in the said Parish of St. Clemt Danes of the yearly Rent of £18. lived therein abt. 9 Months & paid to the Poor all that time & never after Rented ten Pounds a yr. or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 18th. day of Augst. 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Saml. Diaper
