Middlesex ss}  Jonas Woodhead maketh That in the Month of April last Past he this Depont. obtained a Certificate from the Parish officers of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex addressed & directed to the Parish officers of the Parish of Stroud in the County of Kent thereby acknowledging this Depont & his Wife & Children (all of whom are still a part of his Family) to be then legally Settled in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate and this Deponent Farther Saith that he hath not Since Rented a house or other Tenement of the Yearly Rentor Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish Office or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt That he this Deponent & his Family by reason of Sickness lately happening to them hath been & Still are in a Necess Condition & Wants to be relieved & upon Account of Rent now owing by him Which he is at present unable to pay or discharge expects that his Goods will be distrained for the Same unless Such Rent beforthwith paid.

Sworn this 27th day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

Jonas Woodhead
