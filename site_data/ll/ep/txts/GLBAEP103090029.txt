Middlesex ss The voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Clarke of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this day of March 1766.

Who Saith that about four Weeks ago in the Street Called East Smithfield Street in the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County she this Examinant was delivered of a Female Bastard Child And that the said Bastard Child is  actualy become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that one John Wilson of the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate Labourer did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child And that the said Bastard Child hath been Since Baptised by the name of Elizabeth Clarke and registred by the name of Elizabeth Clarke

Sworn this 18th day of March 1766 before  Chris: Scott

the mark of Elizabeth Clarke
