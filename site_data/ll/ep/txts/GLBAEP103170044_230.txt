Martha Walton  Singlewoman meketh oath That her late Father Joseph Walton deced as he has informed her & as She believes was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Palmer a waterman of Globe yard in the parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London on & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards to the knowledge information or belief of this Deponent Rent a house of Ten pounds year or upwards or Serve a any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain another Settlement That She this Deponent never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in her own right.

Sworn this. 9. day of Janry 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Martha Walton
