Westmr.  Mary Pond aged about 54 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of James Pond with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Fulham in the County of Middlesex about Eighteen Months ago, says her said Husband hath informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. John Soper a Lighterman in the Hamlet of Hammersmith in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex aforesaid where he Rented a Tenement of Four Pounds a Year and hath paid Land Tax for the same; and as she verily believes now Rents a Tenement of Five Pounds a Year in the said Hamlet of Hammersmith and pays the Land Tax for same

Sworn the 27th. day of November 1767 before} Paul Vaillant 

Mary Pond
