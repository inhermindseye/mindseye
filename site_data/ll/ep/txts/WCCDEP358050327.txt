Middx  Ann Cooper aged about 50 years upon Oath saith She is the Widow of Francis Cooper with whom she Intermarried in the parish Church of St. Clement Danes about 27 years since and that the said Fras. Cooper dyed near 20 yrs ago-Says her sd. late husband was born in Milford Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that his father rented a house of Ten pounds a Yr. and upwards in little Essex Street in the same parish and lived therein several Years and that  her sd. late husband never rented Ten Pounds a yr. pd. parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant as she hath been Informed And believes nor has she this Examint. Since his Decease rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 28th. Septr 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Cooper
