St Clement Danes May 18th 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  (to wit) John Strickland Aged 36 Years and upwards Singleman Upon Oath and saith That he never was Married That he was bound an he served 5 Years of his Apprenticeship under Indentures duly with Mr. Dickonson at the come of a small House then situate next Grays Inn Gate in Holborn in the parish of St Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the said County of Middlesex That he since at the Expiration of the said 5 Years he left his said Master with this Convent And since then hath he this Deponent hath done no Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to acquire any other legal Settlement And that he is Ill Distressed and unable to support himself without Assistance

Sworn the 18th day of May 1792 before us}
