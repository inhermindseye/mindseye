Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman aged abou One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Ann Sanders Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that Joseph Sanders the father now Rents Premises in Mint Street in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Surry and has done so for near Two years which Premises he this Deponent according to the best Information he can get is of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds

Sworn this 14th- day of Janry. 1797 } B Newman
