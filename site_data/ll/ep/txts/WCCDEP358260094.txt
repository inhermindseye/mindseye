Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Baynon aged about 74 years upon his Oath saith that about 4 years ago he Intermarried with Catherine his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint George the Martyr That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Bennetts Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Eighteen years ago at the yearly Rent of Eight Guineas and paid all Taxes due for the same for Fifteen years that he hath quitted said Service Hour about Eight years ago since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th- day of Decr. 1795. before us W Kinnard N Bond

John Beynon
