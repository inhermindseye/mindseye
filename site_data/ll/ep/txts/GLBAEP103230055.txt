Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Jane Brown of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Widow taken upon oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this twenty first day of June 1787 -Who Saith that on Shrove Tuesday in the year one thousand Seven hundred and eighty five in dean Street in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate She the Said Jane Brown was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since baptized by the name of Mary Ann Spearing And that the said Bastard Child is likely to Become Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that William Spearing late of Star Alley in the Same Parish  Brick layers Labourer dis get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the day and year above written before me  R Davies

The Mark of Jane Brown
