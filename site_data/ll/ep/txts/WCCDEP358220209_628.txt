Westmr. to wit Murtagh Mc. Avoy Aged about fifty Years on his Oath he is now a Widower and hath no Child or Children Says he Rented and lived in a House in Leg Alley long Acre for several Years in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Ten Years ago and after he quitted the same he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1770 before us W Kelynge Pet Planck

his Murtagh Mc. Avoy Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
