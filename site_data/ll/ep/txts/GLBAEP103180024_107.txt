Middlesex ss}  Sarah Danby the widow of Nicholas Danby deced maketh oath that her Sd late husband divers Years ago rented the Publick house known by the Sign of the George in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate in the sd County at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds a year & upwards & resided thereon for the Space of two years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself And that She was lawfully married to her Said late husband

Sworn this day of March 1778 before dead

The Mark of Sarah Danby
