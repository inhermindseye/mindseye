Middx  Sarah Price aged about 30 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Hugh Price a Lunatic who Resides at Mr. Brammah's in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes Says she intermarried with the said Hugh Price at May Fair Chapel about Nine Years ago by whom she hath one Child living to wit Nathaniel aged about Eighteen Months That her said Husband is an Attorney and lived in and Rented a House is Old Fish Street in the Parish of St. Nicholas Olave London for about Three Years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and paid all Parochial Taxes Quitted the same between three & four Years ago And hath not since Rented any House or paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate.

Sworn the 8th. day of Septemr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Price
