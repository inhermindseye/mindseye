Middx.  Ann Brown upon Oath says she never was married & that she lived a hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Champ in High Street in the Parish of Alhallows in the City of Oxford at the yearly Wages of two Pounds for two yrs. or thereabouts. Quitted her said Service abt. thirteen yrs. & has not since Rented ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes on lived a yearly Servant for one yr.

Sworn the 12th. day of July 1757 . before} Fran Bedwell  Benjamin cox

The Mark of Ann Brown
