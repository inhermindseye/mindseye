Westmr.  Sherbrook Grey aged abt. 14 Years upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly hired Servant wth. Mr. Geo Power  Linnen draper near Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westm . at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for the Space of 15 Months & left him upwds. of one Year ago Says he has not since he quitted the sd. Mr. Powers Service lived a Yearly Servt. with any other Person for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 27th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

Sherbrook Gray
