Middx  Margarett Hind  Singlewoman aged abt. 22 Yrs.. upon her Oath saith that she was bound Aprentice to & served 7 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mr. Thomas Elliott in Portugall Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx . Book- -binder & hath been out of her said Apprentiship about two Years since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one Joseph Butters  Servt. to Waller Esqr. in Lincolns Inn Feilds did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body she at such time being a Servt. to Mrs. Jones in Duke Street in the parish of St. Giles in the Feilds  Victualler & this Examinant Carrying Beer to & fetching Potts from the sd. Mr. Wallers house he the sd. Joseph Butters there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in

the Month of May last & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now Pregnant & that he the sd. Jose Butters & no other Pson is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 29th. Decr. 1743 before} Tho DeVeil

The Mark of Margt. Hind
