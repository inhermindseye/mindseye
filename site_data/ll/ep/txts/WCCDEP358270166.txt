Elizth & Wm Sherrard B.

St. Clement Danes December 27th 1793

Middlesex and Wetminster}  Settlement in Liberty of Saffron Hill  Child's birth  Reputed Father (to wit) Elizabeth Sherrard  singlewoman aged 26 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Hall in Greville Street  Hatton Garden in the Liberty of  Saffron Hill  Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the County of Middlesex for about four years at the annual Wages of  5 Guineas that she quitted that Service about four years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatever whereby to the best of her knowledge Recollection or belief she might obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that on or about the 29th sixth day of November Decr inst last she was d at the House of Mrs Patterson in New Inn Passage in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which is not yet Christened but is become chargeable to the said parish that William Snow late a Footman at Mr. War's Turnmill Lane Cannon Street London is the only real true father of the said Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid he and no one else having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body first in or about the Month

of March last and at several. times since at some or one of which times he begot the said Child thereon

Sworn the 27th day of December 1793 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Sherrard 's mark

Witness Garrett

Woman lodges at Mrs. Patterson's New Inn passage Snow is to be found by enquiring of Mr Armstrong Constable at Shoreditch Police Office-he's tall thus man pitted with the small Pox & Dark

The Child is intended to be Christened Wm Sherrard

Man not taken
