Middlesex ss}  Susannah Sheppard the widow of George Sheppard deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband rented a house of the yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds in Stone Court in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & paid Parish Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlemt. nor hath She this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Susanna Sheppard
