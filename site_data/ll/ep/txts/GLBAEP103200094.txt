Middlesex ss}  Mary Clarke  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father Alexander Clarke deced as She this Depont. has been informed by her late Mothr Elizabeth Clarke deced that her sd Fathr in his life time & When a Single man was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Pitway a Slopman on Peters Hill in the Parish of St Peter near Pauls Wharf in the City of London & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of a year & upwards & did not afterwards do any act to gain a settlement elsewhere That she this Deponent was born in the sd Parish & never was Married nor ever was an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year in any one Place nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement for herself.

Sworn this. 4. day of February 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Mary Clarke
