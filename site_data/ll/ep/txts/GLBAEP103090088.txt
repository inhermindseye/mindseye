Middlesex ss  Martha Walton a Singlewoman Maketh oath That She hath been Credibly informed that her Father Jospeh Walton was duly bound an Apprentice and Served the Whole Term of his Apprenticeship with one Mr Palmer a Lighterman in the Parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London and which Information this Depont. verily believes to be true And this Depont Saith that to her knowledge information or belief her said Father never did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his said apprenticeship And this Depont. farther Saith that she never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound apprentice nor was ever Married nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 1t. day of Decr. 1767 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

The Mark of Martha Walton
