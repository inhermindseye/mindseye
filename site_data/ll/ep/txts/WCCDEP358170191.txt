Middx  Abigall Waring aged abt. 58 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never Married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Whitelock in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middlesex for the Space of Two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & hath left her sd. Service Three Years & upwds. to the best of her Remembrance that she hath not since she left her sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 6th. of Decr. 1741 before}

The mark of Abigall Waring
