Middx  Jane Bath  Widow  removed from the Parish of St: Martin in the Feilds by Order Dated 16th. June Inst. upon her Oath saith that she Inter- -married with her late Husband  James Bath on or about the 29th: Day of June 1725 in the Parish Church of St Saviour in Southwark who hath been dead Three Years & upwards that the sd. James Bath was bound an Aprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Maidwell Glassman in the Strand in the parish of St Clemt. Danes & hath been out of his said Apprentiship about 21 Years that her sd late Husband Rented a Shop in Bedford Row in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs of the Yearly Rent of £15: for the Space of Four Years the sd. Shop being an Entire Appartmt. of itselfe & at the same time Rented a Lodging in the House next Adjoyning to the sd. Shop of the Yearly Rent of £10..10 that after her sd. Husbd. left the sd. Shop he in his Life nor this Exaiant since his Decease rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any person for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 18th. June 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Jane Bath
