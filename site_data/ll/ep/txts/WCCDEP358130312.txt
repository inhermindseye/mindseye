Middx  Sarah Shaw upon Oath says she never was married Rented Ten Pounds a yr. paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or of yearly Servt. says her father Thos. Shaw Rented a House of Ten Pounds a yr. & upwards in the Peter Street in that Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for Seven yrs.

Sworn the 22d. day of March 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Shaw
