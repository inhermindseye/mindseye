Middx  Elizabeth Tee aged about 40 Years Removed from the Parish of Hampstead in the County of Middx by Order dated the 21st. day of October 1761 upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Jeremiah Tee who has been Dead about a fortnight to whom she was Married at the Fleet London about Eighteen Years since That her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Holloman Bricklayer in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster That he never rented any House except one of Three Pounds a Year near the Flask in the said Parish of Hampstead for which he paid neither Poor Rate or Land Tax That she has Five Children by her said Husband to wit Mary aged about Fourteen Years who was never an Apprentice or hired Servant for One Year Thomas aged Eight Years James aged Seven Years Phillis aged Four Years and Jane aged Three Years

Sworn the 23d. day of October 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eliz: Tee
