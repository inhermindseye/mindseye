St Clement Danes May 22d. 1792

Middx & Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Ann Bennett  Singlewoman Aged 33 Years or thereabouts Upon his Oath saith That She never was Married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Spencer at the Kings Head Wych Street in the parish of St Clements Danes for about a year and three quarters at the Annual Wages of £4:10 for the first half year, and of five Pounds for the Remaining part of her Service that She quitted the Same about ten years ago and hath not since obtained any other legal Settlement And that She in now Distressed and unable to support herself without Assistance

Sworn the 22d May 1792 before me} R Butler

Ann Bennett 's Mark

Benj Newman Witness
