Middx & Westmr.}  Thos. Inman of Craven Buildings in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex on behalf of Mary Ricketts a Lunatic upon Oath says he has been informed and which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Mary Ricketts lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Alley in this deponents house in Craven Buildings abovementioned for Ten Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds and hath quitted her said Service three Months or thereabove

Sworn the 20th. June 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

Thos Inman
