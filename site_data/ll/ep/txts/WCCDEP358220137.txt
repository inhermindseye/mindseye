Middx: & Westmr.}  David Chumbley Aged about Twenty Six Years on his Oath says he never was married nor ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says he was bound Apprentice to one William Hawes in Neals Yard in the Parish of Saint Jamess Westmr in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship never rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th. day of April 1770 before Us} R. Butler

his David Chumbley Mark

Witness Pet Sidebotham
