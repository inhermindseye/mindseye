Westmr. 

Martha Morgan upon Oath saith she intermarried with her present Husband James Morgan (now at Sea ) on or about the 27th. Day of July 1742 at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Feilds Says the sd. James Morgan lived a Yearly Servant with John Allen Pewcey Esqr . in Portugal Row in Lineolns Inn feilds in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £7..10..0 for two Years & upwards whilst a Singleman and continued to live with the sd. Mr. Pewsey under the same hiring and Agreemt. for the Space of two Years or thereabouts after his Intermarriage with this Examinant Says her sd. Husband never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Febry 25th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Martha morgan
