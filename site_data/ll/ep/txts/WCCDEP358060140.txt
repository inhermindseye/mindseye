Middx  Abigail Larego  Wife of Thomas Larego upon Oath saith that at the time of her intermarriage with her present husband she was the Widow of John Cooper who dyed abt. 20 months since & with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London on the 25th. July about 16 Years ago Says she hath 4 Children by the sd. John Cooper (now at home with her) viz. Abigail Cooper aged about 19 Years Mary aged about 11 Years Eliz aged about 8 Years & John aged about 6 Years neither of whom ever was placed out as an Apprentice or Yearly Servant and further Saith her sd. late husband John Cooper lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Mein in Norfolk Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for one Year and upwards whilst a Singleman and never after he quitted his sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge belief

Sworn July 12th. 1751 before  Fran Bedwell Abigal Larego
