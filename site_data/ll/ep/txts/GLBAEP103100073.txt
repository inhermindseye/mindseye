Middlesex ss  Mary Gould the Widow of Benjamin Gould deced maketh oath That She Cannot in any wise Set forth or discover Where the Parents of her late husband or either of them was or were Settled at the times of their respective Deaths nor where her said husbands Settlement was at his death or during his life time But this Deponent Saith that She has been informes by her late Father Arthur- Joyner deced that he was born in the Parish of Saint Margaret Pattens in the City of London & where he afterwards Rented a house of Eighty Pounds a year or there abouts & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there which information this Depont. believes to be true And this Depont. Cannot Set forth or discover Whether her Said Father ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And this Deponent farther Saith that she was never put apprentice Nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year; nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 20th day of January 1769 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

The Mark of Mary Gould
