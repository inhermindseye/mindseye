Westmr. to wit Seth Jeffrreys Aged about Sixty Three Years on his Oath Saith he Intermarried with with Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Cirencester about forty Years ago and by whom he hath Three Childn Now provided for Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Giles Scott a Cordwainer and served him Seven Years in the said Parish of Cirencester in the County of Gloucester and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 10th. day of July 1771 before} Tho Lane R. Butler

Seth Jeffreys his Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
