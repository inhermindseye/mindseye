Westmr.  Elizabeth Lee aged about 62 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hare at Sams Coffee house in the Strand opposite St. Clements Church, in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for four Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, hath quitted said Mr Hare's Service upwards of Twenty Years, and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn March 11th. 1766 , before} Fran Cary

The Mark of Elizth. Lee
