Middx  Mary Dean aged abt. 17 Years upon Oath says she was never married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Isaac Aston in the Strand over against Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of forty Shillings & hath quitted her said Service abt. twelve Months & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or been a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th. day of Octr. 1757 . before me} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Dean
