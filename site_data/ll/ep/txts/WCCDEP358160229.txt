Middx  The Exaicon of Elizth- Godfrey lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx taken ye- 13th- Day of Janry 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Perins in the sd- Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Cook for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £6 & further on her Oath saith yt. she hath not since yt. time Rented £10 a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year  or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife yt. she is now poor & Sick & not able to maintaine herselfe.

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Eliz Godfrey
