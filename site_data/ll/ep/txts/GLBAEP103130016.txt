Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Pool Singlewoman of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County taken on oath before me Burford Camper Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this 20th. day of October 1773

Who on her oath Saith That on the Tenth day of September last Past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Star Court in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate since Baptized by the name of Albion Creaton and that the said Child is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish and that Albion Creaton of the Parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London  Carpenter did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this 20th. day of October 1773 before B Camper

The Mark of Elizabeth Pool
