Westmr.  Eleanor Tobin Aged about Twenty two Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Serjt. Davy in Bellyard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds, eight Shillings, Quitted same about one Year, and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 5th day of April 1768 , Before}
