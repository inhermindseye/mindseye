Middx to wit Ann Fewater Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Lock head at the White Hart in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service  about Seven Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. And further on her Oath says that on the 22d. day of June last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Water Lane Hospital which hath since been Baptized by the Name of John and is now become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes And further says that one Benjn. Lyon a Wax Chandler at No.2 Angel Court in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body upwards of 18 Months ago at a Butchers Shop in White Hart Yard at sevl. different Places and at sevl. different times afterwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. and that the sd. Benjn. Lyon & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1782 before  Sampn Wright

her Ann Fewater Mark
