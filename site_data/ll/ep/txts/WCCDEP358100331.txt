Middlesex & Westminster}  Swears to St. C. D . to wit  Ann Harris aged 38 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she intemarried with Wm. Harris her present Husband at Bishops Liddiard in Somersetshire about 7 years ago has had 5 Children by her Husband (Vizt.) George aged 6 years or thereabouts who is now at Barpoole in Somersetshire  Elizth . aged 5 years or thereabts Mary aged 4 years or thereabouts Sarah aged 2 years or thereabouts Jane aged 1 year or thereabouts they were all born at Kingston in Somersetshire that her Husband left Somersetshire about 15 Months ago That when he left same he came to London that for about 9 Months last past he Rented a House in Vere Street Clare Markett at £30 a year that he is in Confinement for Debt and she is big with Child and unable to maintain herself and family

Sworn this 8th day of Janry. 1785 R Butler

her Ann Harris mark
