Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Alice Elliston aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that about 14 years ago she Intermarried with Edward Elliston her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Pancras that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Nine years since which time he has not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has left her upwards of Five Months and have allowed her no Support and that this Examt. being poor and unable to maintain herself is obliged to Apply to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Relief

Sworn this 19th day of June 1788 before me  Edward Hodsoll

Alice Elliston
