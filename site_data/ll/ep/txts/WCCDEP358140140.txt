Middx.  John Aylmer aged abt. 39 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Martha his Wife who is now aged abt. 54 Years in the Parish Church of St. Botolph Bishopsgate London upwards of Ten Years since says whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servt. for Two Years with one Mr. Lee a Taylor in Whistlers Court in Cannon Street in the Parish of St. Swithin London Stone in the City of London at the Yearly wages of Seven Pounds & has left his said Service abt. Eighteen Years since which time he hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 5th. of July 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Jon: Aylmer
