Middx  Margt Spencer upon Oath saith she is the widow of Ja Spencer who dyed abt. 14 Years ago and that her sd. ha husband Informed her he never was an apprentice or yearly Servant for one year and that his father John Spencer rented a house of the yearly rent of in Carnaby market in the parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. lived therein many years and paid Scot & Lot there which Information they Examinant believes to be true

Sworn March 26th. 1755 before} Benjamin Cox

Margrett Spencer
