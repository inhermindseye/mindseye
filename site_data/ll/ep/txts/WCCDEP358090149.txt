Middx to wit Elizth. Irons aged about Thirty two Years on her Oath says She Intermarried with Abraham Irons (who has deserted at the Parish Church of Saint John's Horsley down in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry about fourteen Years ago And by whom She hath one Child living Namely Samuel Aged about one Month says her said Husband Rented a House in Lamb Court Abchurch Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Abchurch London at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and lived therein upwards of Three Months and run away by Night but kept the Key for one Year and a Quarter her said Husband having made and Agreement with the Landlord for a Year Certain) and a Quarters Marning but whither her said Husband paid either Rent or Taxes this Examinant cannot sett forth Hath quitted sd. House about 10 Years ago since which time He never Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of Augt. 1780 before  Sampn. Wright R Butler

Eliz Irons
