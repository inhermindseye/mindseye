Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Bradford aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith That she intermarried with John Bradford her late Husband at the Christ Church Surry in the Month of August in the year 1774 that her said Husband has been dead about Seven years that she has one illegitimate Child living Vizt. Jane Bradford aged about 6 years born a Bastard in Silver Street Spittlefields in the Parish of Said Matthew Bethnell Green

Sworn this 25th. day of July 1786  Jno: Wright G Mercer

her Jane Bradford Mark
