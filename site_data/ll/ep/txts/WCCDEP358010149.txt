Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Benjn. Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Edwd. Martin who is at present rather disordered in his mind Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Edward Martin in the Month of June 1786 he took of one John Williams in Stone Cutter Court Crutched Fryars in the Parish of Saint Olave Hart Street in the City of London Two Parlours for which he pays the yearly Rent of £10 that he only quitted same last Thursday therefore can have done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Octr. 1787 W Taylor James Croft

Benj. Newman
