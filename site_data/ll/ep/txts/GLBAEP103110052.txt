Middlesex ss  Thomas Bunyan Maketh oath that divers Years ago he rented a house in New Court in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex and was Charged to and Paid Kings Taxes for the Same for many Years and by means thereof as he is Advised gained a legal Settlement there That he this Depont. hath not Since paid the Kings Tax elsewhere nor hath he Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates nor to his Knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th: day of May 1772 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

Tho. Bunyan
