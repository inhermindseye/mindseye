Middlesex & Westmr.}  Deborah Burden Aged about fifty Eight Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Ellick Burden who has been dead about fifteen Years Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Doctor Ingram in the Parish of All Hallows Barking in the City of London for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 19th. day of February 1770 before Us } Tho: Fellows R. Butler

her Deborah Burden Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
