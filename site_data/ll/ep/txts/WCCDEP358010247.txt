Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Margaret Hughes aged about 48 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she has one Child living Vizt. James aged upwards of Eight years born a Bastard in the House of a Mr. Hughes in Bell Court Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex and that he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Septr. 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

his Margaret Hughes  Mark
