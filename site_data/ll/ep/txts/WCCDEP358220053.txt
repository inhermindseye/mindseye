Westmr.  Elizth. Westley Aged about forty years Wife of Samuel Westley (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Twelve Years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living named Ann aged about Eight years Says her said Husband rented and lived in a House in Vere Street in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr upwards of one Year and an half at the Yearly Rent of Twenty five pounds hath quitted same about five years ago and hath not since rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 2d day of August 1769 before me} R. Butler

her Elizth. Westley mark
