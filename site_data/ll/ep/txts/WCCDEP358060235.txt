Middx  John Bliss upon Oath saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Peter Harding  Leather dresser in Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and never rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained 4 Settlement in any other place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 19th 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

John Bliss
