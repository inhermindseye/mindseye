Middx,  Hannah Lant aged about 23 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Richard Murth  Esqr . in the House of Mrs. Powell in East Street near Red lion Square in the Parish of St. George the Martyr by Queen Square in the County of Middx for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds for the first Year and Eight pounds the Second Year quitted said Service about the 14th. day of March 1765, and hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 1st. Augst. 1765 before} Paul Vaillant  Thos: Balack

Hannah Launt
