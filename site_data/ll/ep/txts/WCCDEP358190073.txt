Middx.  Susan Phipps aged abt. 47 Years Upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Wilson a Butcher in Hollis Street  Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx for One Year and Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & hath Quitted said Service abt. Four Years during which time she has not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 1st. day of Decr. 1760 before me} Barth: Hammond

Susanna Phipps
