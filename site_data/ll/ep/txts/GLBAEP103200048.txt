Middlesex ss}  Sarah Crawford  Singlewoman maketh oath That Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by & with John Wahnbeck a Taylor in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of one year & half & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 26.day of August 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Crawford
