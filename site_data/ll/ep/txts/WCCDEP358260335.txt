Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Daniel Perrin aged about 41 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the Thirteenth day of January in 1789 he Intermarried with Mary his late wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary at Lumbeth  by whom he hath Three Children living Vizt. Elizabeth aged about Eight years Daniel aged about Six years and John aged about Four years That he this Deponent was bound an Apprentice to one Thomas Ward of Great Turnstile in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of  Middlesex for seven years that he served his said Master under such Indenture till he died which was about Three years and an half after he was bound That after his said Masters Decease he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further saith that he is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 18th. day of Septr. 1798 before us } N Bond Russ: Clarke

his Daniel Perrin  Mark

Witness H Day
