Middlesex ss}  John Sheen maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice to Anthony Lambert of  Bermondsey Street in the parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry with whom he Served about half of his apprenticeship & then this Depont was assigned & turned over to William Everett  of King Street in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex with whom he Served the residue of his said apprenticeship & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten pounds or Served any parish office or paid any poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that he hath a wife named Sarah to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 24th day of November 1774 before B Camper

John Sheen
