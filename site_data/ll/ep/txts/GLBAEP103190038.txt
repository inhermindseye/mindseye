Middlesex ss  Mary Groves the wife of John Groves maketh oath That her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Sandwich That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Mr Bailey a Painter  in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Town of Reading  in the County of Berks & to her knowledge or belief has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And that she was lawfully married to her sd husband.

Sworn this. 13. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Groves
