Middx  Ann Rayden aged abt. 44 years upon Oath saith she was never married nor ever was on apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes and that she has been informed & verily believes her late Father John Rayden Served his Apprentiship with a Shoemaker in or near Scotland Yard in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westmr. and never rented was a Yearly Servant rented ten Pounds a Year or paid parish Taxes.

Sworn Octr. 16th. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

ann Rayden
