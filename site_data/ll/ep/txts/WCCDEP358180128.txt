Middx  Mary Nicholls  Wido . Removed from the parish of St. Botolph Bishopgate London by Order Dated the 19th. Day of October last upon her Oath saith that about Ten or Eleven Years since this Examinant being at that time a Wido. lived in & Rented a House ever agt. St. Clements Church in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 3 Months & upwards at the Yearly Rent of £18. that since she left the sd. House she hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 10th. Novr. 1743 before} Cha: Egerton

Mary Nicholls
