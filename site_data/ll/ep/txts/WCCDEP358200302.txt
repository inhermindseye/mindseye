Westmr.  Thos. Gorn aged about 53 Years upon Oath says he was married to Eleanor his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Fields about four Years ago, says he can give no Account of the Settlement of his Father and that he this Examinant was born in Feathers Alley  St. Martins Lane in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt.

Sworn the 21st. Sepr. 1765 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Thomas Gorn
