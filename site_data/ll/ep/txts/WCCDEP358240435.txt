Saint Clement Danes Augt. 20th.. 1794

Middlesex & Westminster }  Settlemt. in Saint Mary Le Strand  to wit Catherine Ruddick aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that in the Month of June 1779 she this Deponent Intermarried with Henry Ruddick at the parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn that her said Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Wilkins in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster and that since he has been out of his said Apprenticeship he has not done to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 20th.  21st day of Augt. 1794 before us J Pownall M Addington

Catherine Ruddick
