Middlesex ss}  William Lindsay maketh oath That in the Year one thousand Seven hundred & Sixty three he lived in & rented a Room & shop in the Dwelling house of one Mr Joops Situate in High Holborn in the parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & Ten Shillings & lodged resided thereon for the Space of one Year That the said Shop was Situated in the Front of the Said house towards the publick Street Where this Deponent Carried on in a publick & open manner the Trade & Business of a Salesman to wit in buying & Selling of Cloaths during Such his Said residence & by means thereof as he is advised gained a legal Settlement there in the Said Parish That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house shop or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or served any Parish office or paid Any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that he hath a Wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 18 day of February 1775 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

William Lindsay
