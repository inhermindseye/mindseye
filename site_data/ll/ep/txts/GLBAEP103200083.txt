Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Plantin of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County this twenty fourth day of December 1783 Who Saith that about a Fortnight Ago at the house of Samuel Wynn in upper Eastsmithfield in the sd Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate the said Sarah Plantin was delivered of a Female Bastard Child not as Yet baptized & that the Said Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate & that one George Bishop now of late of Colcheser in the County of Essex  Farmer is the Father of the Said Child

Sworn this 12 day of January 1784 before me R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Plantin
