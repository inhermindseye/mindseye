St. Clement Danes September 9th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) Penelope Meller aged 24 years & upwards singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Graham in Arundel Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One year and six Months or thereabouts at the annual Wages of Ten pounds that she has quitted that service on the Morning of this the said 9th. day of September 1794 that And this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes when born that John Mushatt Esquire who lately lodged at the said Mr. Graham's is the only real true father thereof he having first had carnal connection of this Deponent's Body and begotten the same thereon and that he and no other person is the only real true father thereof

Sworn the 9th day of September 1794 before me} N Bond

Penelope Meller 's Mark

Witness Garrett
