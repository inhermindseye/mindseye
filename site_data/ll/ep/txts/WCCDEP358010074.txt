Saint Clement Danes

Benjamin Wingrove  Master of the workhouse of St. Clement Danes Maketh Oath and Saith that Jane robe was bound apprentice to Ann Bowerbank of Buxter Place Sambeth  Tambour Worker by the Churchwardens & Queens of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes with the Consent of Two of his Majesty Justices of the Peace on or about the 23d. Day of April last . That she went with the said Ann Bowerbank to the Parish of St. Mary Newington to live  as he has been informed and believe and that she resided with the said Ann Bowerbank there for upwards of Forty Days 4 & that she ran away from the Sd. Ann Bowerbank about the 28th. day of February last .

Sworn this 1st. day of March 1787 before D Walker Philip Dyer B Wingrovs
