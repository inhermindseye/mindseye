Middx  Mary Houlder aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath says she lived a hired Yearly Servt . with Geo: Rossiter at the Cheshire Cheese at Mount Pleasant in the Parish of St. Andrew  Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx. for 7 Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £4. and hath quitted said Service about 7 Years: since which Time she hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year and further upon her Oath says she Never was Married

Sworn this 23d. Decr. 1758 . before} Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Houlder

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Allan Ovr. J: P:
