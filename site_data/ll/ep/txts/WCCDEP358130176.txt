Middx  John Lemon aged abt. Thirty five Years upon Oath Says, he lived a hired Yearly Servant upwards of a Year with Mr. James Yeates at the Talbot Inn, in the Strand , in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and quitted the same about Seven Years since & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 12th. Day of June 1756 . before} Fran. Bedwell

The mark of John Lemon
