Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Gregory aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that about 14 years ago she Intermarried with Daniel Gregory her late present Husband who has been at the Parish Church of Saint Anne's Westmr . by whom she hath Three Children living Vizt. George aged about Six years Mary aged about Four years and William aged about Two years That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Five years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that after he quitted said house he never Rented any house Tenement or lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of Septr. 1798 before me } Wm Goodenough

Her Ann Gregory Mark
