Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Guest aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Duke in Arrundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Sixteen Months since which time she has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish That one Wm. Plant who did live as a Servant to Mr. Randall in Garden Corvet  Grays Inn is the father of the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day of June 1790 } Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Guest

Vide Examination of  7th Septr. 1790 after Birth

Wm. Plant is about 5 feet high darkish Complexion his Hair hanging in his Neck is about 20 years of Age and has a Dimple in his Chin.
