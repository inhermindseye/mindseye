Westmr. 

Christr. Burnett upon Oath saith he inter married with his present Wife Sarah about Eighteen Years since at the Fleet London Says he lived in and rented a house in Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds for one year and upwards Chath left same upwds. of Eight years Says he has not since he left sd. house rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemnt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 14h 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Christr. Burnett
