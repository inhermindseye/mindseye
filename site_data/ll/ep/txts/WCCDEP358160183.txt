Middx  The Examination of Joseph Povee on the Behalfe of Jno. Dingley an Inft. aged about Seven Years living in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 25th. Day of Octr. 1740 Who upon his Oath Saith that Jno. Dingley the Father of the sd. Inft. went as a Soldier to the Leward Islands & hath not been heard of since & further Saith Dorothy Dingley the Mother of the said Inft. rented a House in the Butcher Row in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £25 & lived in the same half a Yeare & there dyed & further saith that the sd. Jno. Dingley the Father & Dorothy Dingley the Mother of the sd. Infant before the sd. Jno. Dingley the Father went abroad as a Soldier lived togather as Man & Wife & were always Deemed & taken so to be by this Exaiant & the rest of their Neighbours

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

Joseph Povee
