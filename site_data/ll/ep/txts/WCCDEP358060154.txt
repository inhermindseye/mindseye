Middx  Frances Brown upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Geo: Brown upwds. of 11 years since at Bloomsbury Church & that her sd. husband whilst a Singleman lived a yearly Servant for one Year & upwds. with Mr Richardson in the Parish of East Barnet in the County of Hertford at the Yearly Wages of £10 & has not since he quitted his sd Service rented a house of ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 19th. 1751 before  Richd: Chamberlayne

Frances Brown
