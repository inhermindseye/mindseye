Middx  The Examination of Sarah Bright  Widow aged fifty Years & upwards living in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. County of Middx taken ye. 2d. Day of Decr. 1740 who upon her Oath saith yt. about nine Years ago (being at that time a Widow & not having any Child or Children) She lived a Yearly hired Servt. with one Mr. John Tod in Kings Head Court in Drury Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of two Years & a half at ye. Yearly Wages of five Pounds & further saith yt. she hath not since she Came away from her sd. Service Rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & that she is Sick & poor & not able to maintaine herself

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Sarah Bright
