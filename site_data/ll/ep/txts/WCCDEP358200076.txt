Westmr  Jane Fitzwood Aged about 22 Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a hired Yearly Servant with James Delamore at the Coach and Horses the Corner of Bond Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service about fifteen Months and hath not Since Rented House Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 29th of February 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Jane Fitzwood

Upon Mr Ralph Williams Compt Ovr J. W
