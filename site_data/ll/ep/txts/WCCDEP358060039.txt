Middx  Jane Linnee aged about 33 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 6 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Woolford a Butcher in Vear Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes Middx at the Yearly Wages of £4 for one Year & 6 months and has not since she quitted her sd Service rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 25th. 1750 before  Tho Lane

Jane Linnee
