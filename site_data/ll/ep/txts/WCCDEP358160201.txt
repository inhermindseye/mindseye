Middx  The Examination of Sarah Ross  Widow of George Ross Deceased lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 29th. Novr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that on ye. 30th. Day of January in the Year 1721 She Intermarryed with the above named Geo: Ross & further saith yt. her sd. Husband was bound a Covenant Servt . by Indenture dated the 1st Day of Novr. 1714 for three Years at the Wages of two Pounds for the first Year three Pounds for the Second Year & Seven Pounds for the last Year to Mr. Wm- Storey in Ship Yard in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Taylor further saith that her sd. Husband hath been dead abt. three Months & that he never since his sd. Service rented any House lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt.

in any other Place to this Exaiants knowledge or belief & she being Poor is not able to maintain herself

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas. Gronous

The Mark of Sarah Ross
