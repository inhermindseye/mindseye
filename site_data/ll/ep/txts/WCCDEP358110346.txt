Middx.  Mary Sadler upon Oath saith she never was married & never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year that her late father rented a house of the Yearly rent of £30. in the High Street in the parish of St. Mary in Oxford lived therein several years & there dyed

Sworn Novr. 13th. 1753 before Fran: Bedwell

Mary Sadler
