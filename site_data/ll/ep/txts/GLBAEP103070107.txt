Middx ss  Thomas Lay maketh oath that about Seventeen years ago he was bound apprentice  by Indre, for seven years & Mr. Robert Hawkes a Wheelwright who then lived in London Coney in the parish of Shenley in the County of Hertford and Continued in such Service for the space of five years and this Deponents Master then dying he left his Mistress And Never afterwards was a hired Servant by the year Nor never Rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any parish offices nor to his knowledge or belief hath he gained any Subsequent Settlement since his Apprenticeship And this Depont says that he hath wife named Sarah and two Children to wit Ann aged about Seven Years and Mary aged about twelve Months the lawfull issue of this Deponent and his sd wife

Sworn this 10th day of May 1754 before  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

Thos. Lay
