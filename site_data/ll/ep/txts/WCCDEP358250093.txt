Militia Mans Wife Vide try in Militia Examination Bo

St Clement Dane 13t March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Beckingham Rent (to wit) Elizabeth Swindall Wife of Richard Swindall a Substitute in the Surry Militia Maketh Oath saith that she is aged about 30 years that she was Married to her said Husband at the parish Church of St Leonard Shoreditch in or about the Month of January 1790 that she hath one Child by her said Husband now living Namely George aged Eleven Months on the 18th day of February last that she hath been informed and verily believes her Husband Father was Married to Catherine his Wife the Mother of the said Richard Swindall at the parish Church of St. Sepulchre  Newgate in or about the year 1767 that her said Husband in now of the Age of 25 years or thereabouts that he never obtained any legal settlement in his own Right to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief that she hath also been  informed and believes that her father in Law Nicholas Swindall Rented a House and premises at the parish of Beckingham in Kent for upward of a year at Eleven pounds a year and paid Taxes for the same and hath never obtained any subsequent legal settlement and this Examinant knows not of any settlement she is intitled to otherwise than in Beckingham aforesaid

Sworn the 13th. day of March 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Elizth Swindall 's Mark
