Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Susannah Rider aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that about 20 years ago she Intermarried with John Rider at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields whom she hath five Children living Vizt. Wm. aged about 21 years Mary aged about 18 years George aged about 15 years all provided for John ag aged about 10 years and Charlotte aged about Nine years an provided for That her said Husband has been dead about Eight years That her said late husband Rented and lived in a house in Nags head Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for four years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Nineteen Pounds and that after he quitted said house he never did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of April 1790 R Butler

her Susannah Rider Mark
