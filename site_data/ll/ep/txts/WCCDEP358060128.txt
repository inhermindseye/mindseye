Middx  Christr. Finless upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife  Bridget about 43 Years since at Linelly Church in Kings County in Ireland that he & his sd. Wife are both Natives of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England & that he this Examinant Served 3 Yrs. of his Apprentiship with Mr Connor a Shoemaker in Shilbegan in the County of Westmeath in Ireland & at the End of the sd. Term his master left of Trade & gave him his Indenture & that he never was assigned over or gained a Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge or belief

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before  Tho Lane Cha Finless
