Middx  Sarah Elford Wife of George Elford aged abt. Forty four Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with the said George Elford at the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes abt. Twenty three Years since, Says her said Husband Rented a house White Horse Yard in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds & paid all Parish Taxes lived therein abt. three Years & hath quitted the same upwards of Eleven Years Says her said Husband has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office Says She has one Child by her sd. Husband (to wit) Elizabeth aged abt. Thirteen Years

Sworn the 15th Octr. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Elford
