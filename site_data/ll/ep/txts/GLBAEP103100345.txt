Middlesex  Joseph Randall maketh oath That divers Years ago he was bound apprentice to Thomas Smith of the Parish of Asson near Stevenage in the County of Hertford Butcher & duly served his apprenticeship in the said Parish & by Means thereof as he is advised he gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont. Saith that he has afterwards lived in the Said Parish as hired Yearly Servant for the Space of two Years And Saith that he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of November 1770  Chris: Scott

Joseph Randall His Mark
