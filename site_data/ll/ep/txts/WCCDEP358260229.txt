Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Johnson aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Nicholson in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Six years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of Novr. 1797 before me } W Carter

her Sarah Johnson mark

H Day Beadle
