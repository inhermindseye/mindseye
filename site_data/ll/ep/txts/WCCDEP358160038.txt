Middx  The Examination of Elizth . the Wife of Brian Kanady Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken ye. 7th. day of March 1738 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her said Husband Brian Kanady is an Irishman & never gained a Settlemt. in England to her Knowledge or beleife & that before her Intermarriage with the sd. Brian Kanady she this Examint. lived with her Father Thomas Gregory who lived in & Rented a House in Bennetts Street in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry & Paid to Church & Poor for Sevll. Years And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that she hath not since that time gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife That her sd. Husband now Absonds from her & that she hath two Children (to wit) Elizth . aged 18 Months and upwards & Mary aged 5 Months & upwds. & that she is poor & Sick & not able to maintaine them

Sworne the day & Year above mentioned before} Tho Lane  James Gronous

Elezadeth Kanady
