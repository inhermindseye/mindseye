Middx.  Ann Grogin aged abt. 57 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Grogin who has been Dead abt. 16 Years says her said husband Rented a house in Houghton Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £18 and lived therein abt. 7 years. says her said husband in his life time (after he quitted that house) nor she since his death never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn March 21st. 1760 before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Grogin
