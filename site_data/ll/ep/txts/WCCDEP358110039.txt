Middx  Jane Linnee aged near 32 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Woolford in Vere Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £4 for Eighteen months or thereabouts quitted her said Service about Ten Years ago and has not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn April 18th. 1752 before} Tho Lane

Jane Linnee
