Middx & Westmr}  Eleanor Moore upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never Married & that abt. Three years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mrs. Fortune in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx for the Space of One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & hath not since that Time rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife And further upon her Oath saith that on the 25th Day of June last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at one Mr. Richmonds in Drury Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes which hath since been baptized Catherine & that one Wm. Merridith a Cabinet Maker did begett the sd. Child on her Body she this Examinant & the sd. Wm- Merridith living togeather as Man & Wife in Conduit Court in Long Acre  St. Martin in the Feilds

in the Liberty of Westmr. where the sd. Wm. Merridith had Carnall Knowledge of her Body Sevll. Times at one of Which Times he got her with Child of the sd. Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered of as aforesd. & yt. he the sd. Wm- Merridith & no other Person is the True & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 19th- Day of August 1741 . before} Jas: Gronous

Elinor Moore
