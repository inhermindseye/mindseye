St Clement Danes 3d Febry. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  (Wife in Lambeth parish)  St George Hanover Square Square Westmr.}  (to wit) Benjamin Topping aged about 56 Upon Oath saith that he was Married to Mary his present Wife at the parish Church of St James Westminster or about twenty six years ago (by Banns) that previous thereto he was hired as a Yearly Servant to Captain Garsten then of Craven Street in the Strand at the Wages of £10:16s per Annum and lived with the Said Captain Garsten as such Servant for two years or upwards that during the last 3 Months of such his service he Lodged with his said Master at his House in Little Dean Street in the parish of St George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster that since he quitted the said Captain Garsten's service he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his (this Deponent's) knowledge or belief

Sworn the 3d day of February 1794 before us N Bond B: Kennett

Benj. Topping

Benjn. Newman attended when the Magistrates signed this deposition & Rem he Removed the Pauper but on 4th Febry 1794
