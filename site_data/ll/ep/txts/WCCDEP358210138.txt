Middx & Westmr.}  Rebecca Walters aged upwards of Twenty Eight Years upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bolds in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and hath quitted said Service about five Weeks And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born, says that William Wain late a Servant to the said Mr. Bold did beget the said Child or Children on her Body; he having had carnal knowledge of her body sometime in the Month of May 1766, and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said William Wain and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 19th, day of February 1767 before} Paul Vaillant

Rebekah Walters
