Westmr.  Cathn: Pennell Wife of Henry Willoughby Pennell upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her sd. husband upwds. of Six Years since at May fair Chapel by whom she hath two Children Catherine aged about three Years and Henry aged about 5 Years Says her sd. Husband Served his Apprenticeship with Mr. Peter Midget in Orange Court in Castle Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented a house Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief.

Sworn Sept. 15th. 1749 before}

Cathe Pennell
