Middlesex ss  Amy Donelly the wife of Philip Donelly now on board his Majesties Ship the Lancaster in the West Indies Maketh oath that her said husband is an Irishman as this Depont. believes and that he never gained any settlemt in England to her knowledge informacon or belief nor did he ever inform her where his Place of settlemt. was in Ireland and therefore this Deponent Cannot set forth the Particular Place or Parish where her said husband is legally settled But this Deponant saith that about sixteen years ago she lived as a hired servant by the year with her father in law John Hender of the Minories in the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate London  Penmaker at the wages of Thirty shillings by the Year and Continued in such service for the space for two years and then this Deponents father in Law removed to the Hermitage in the Parish of St. John Wapping in ye County of Middx where this Depont. Continued to live with her said father in law at the same yearly wages and Continued in such service for the farther space of three years and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there in her own right as this Depont is advised & believes And this Depont. saith that she hath two Children to wit Mary aged about seven years and Philip aged about Nine Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband

Sworn this 6th day of June 1760 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Amy Donelly
