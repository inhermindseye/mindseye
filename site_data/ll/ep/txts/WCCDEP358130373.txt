Middx  John Easton aged abt. 32 yrs. upon Oath says he intermarried with Alice his late Wife in the Parish Church of Bramdean in the County of Southampton says he lived a hired yearly Servt. before Marriage with Henry Collins  Esqr . for three years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of six Pounds in the said Parish & hath quitted sd. service Upwrds of Spe years says he hath two Children by his said Wife Ann aged upwds. of six yrs. & John aged upwds. of two yrs.

Sworn the 17th of June 1757 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

John Easton
