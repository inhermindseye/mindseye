Middx  Elizth. Saille Aged about Twenty five Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says she Cannot Give any Account of the Settlement of her Father Says she was Born in the Parish of Wilscon in the County of Somerset as she has been Informed & which Information she Verily Beleives to be true And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the Twenty fifth Day of December last at Nurse Pooles in Saint Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized by the Name of John Sa One John Brooks a Weaver (who is gone away)

& this Examinant Cannot tell where he is had Carnall knowledge of this Examinants Body Sometime in the Months of February & March last & at severall other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child of which she was Delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the Day of 1765 Before}

The Mark of Elizth. Saille
