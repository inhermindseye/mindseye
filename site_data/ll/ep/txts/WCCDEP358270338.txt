Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Moore aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs.Featherstone of Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about Three Months only since which she hath done no Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Capeton who did live Servant with a Gentleman whose Name she this Deponent doth not know and did lodge in Salisbury Street in the Strand but where he now lives she this Deponent doth not know is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant Body and begotten

the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said John Capeton and no other Person did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 14th. day of Decr. 1797 before me } Saml Edward .

Sarah Moor

Father never Apprehended Child born in the house the 22d. Febry. 1798 named John.
