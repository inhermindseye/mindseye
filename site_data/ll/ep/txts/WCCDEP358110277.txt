Westmr.  Mary Monkhouse (removed from the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex with her three Childn. John aged 6 Yrs. Wm. aged 4 Yrs. & Thos. Simpson aged near two Years.) upon Oath saith she is the Wife of John Monkhouse (who at present absconds from her) and to whom she was married at Mayfair Chapel on the 3d. Octr. 1745 and that her sd. husband rented a house of £12 by the Year in Horse Shoe Court Carey Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and lived there upwards of half a Year & quitted the same about a fortnight ago

Sworn July 23d. 1753 -before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Monkhouse
