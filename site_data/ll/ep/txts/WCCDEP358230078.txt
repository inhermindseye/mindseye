Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Ward aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she Rented and lived in a first floor at No. 16 Noel Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Nine Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas that she hath quitted said Apartments about Twelve years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that on the 5th. day of March 1797 she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at No.7 in Bear Alley fleet Market in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre which hath since been Christened by the name of Thomas

Sworn this 24th. day of July 1799 before us} Menter Saml. Edwards

her Elizabeth EW Ward mark

Witness B Newman
