Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Elliott aged about 58 years upon her Oath saith that she Rented and lived in a first floor at No. 22 Poland Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Nine Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas that she hath quitted said Apartments about Eleven years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 4th. day of Septr. 1798 before us  Ws Goodenough W Carter

her Ann Ebbot Elliott Mark
