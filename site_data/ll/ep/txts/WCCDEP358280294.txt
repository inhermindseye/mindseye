Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Blackwell aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that on the 13th. day of February last past she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Davison in Clare Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary Ann That one Everett John Chipperfield who did live with his father John Chipperfield a Baker at Kensington but where he is now this Examinant doth not know is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she this Examinant was so delivered as aforesaid thereon and that the said John Evertt John Chipperfield is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 18th. day of Augt. 1790 } Wm Kitchiner

Mary Blackwell
