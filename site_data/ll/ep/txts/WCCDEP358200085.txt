Westmr  Elizth. Kitcherside  Singlewoman Aged about Twenty Five Years upon Oath Says that on the Eleventh Day of February One thousand Seven hundred and Sixty four she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child at a House in Tyburn Road in the County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized by the Name of George Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with One Mr Robinson at the Sign of the Hour Glass in Tuttle Street in the Parish of St. John ye Evangelist in the Liberty of Westminster One Year at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Years

and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 12th Day of March 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth. Kicherside

Upon Mr Jones Complt Overseer J. W
