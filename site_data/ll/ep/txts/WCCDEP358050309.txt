Middx  Mary Gascoign upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Henry Gascoign who dyed about 17 Years ago and left her without Child or Children-that about Ten years since she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Symonds in Manna Street in the Parish of Chelsea in the County of Middx at the yearly wages of Five pounds the spare of one Year to but thinks she was not the  in certain she was not there are and further upon her Oath saith that before she came to Mr. Symonds she lived with Mr Harod at the Cock a Coffee house in Clapham in the County of Surry for 4 Yrs. or thereabts. at the yearly Wages of £3 and quitted her sd. Service abt. 13 yrs. since that since she left the above mentioned Mr Symonds she has not

rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 7th. 1750 before

Tho Lane Geo: Errington

The mark of Mary Gascoign
