Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Elliott on behalf of Samuel Ferguson an Infant aged about Nine years upon her Oath Saith that she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that the said Samuel Ferguson was born in Elliotts Court in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre in the City of London

Sworn this 6th Day of June 1800 before us} Saml. Edwards Winster

Her Ann Eliott Mark
