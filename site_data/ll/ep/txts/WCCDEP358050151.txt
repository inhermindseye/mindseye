Middx  Joseph Manning upon his Oath saith he was born at Whitefryee Gate in the Parish of High Church in Hull in the County of York that his father Served his time with Geo Harbert a Farrier in the same Parish and that he this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 27th. 1749 before W Feilding

The mark of Joseph Manning
