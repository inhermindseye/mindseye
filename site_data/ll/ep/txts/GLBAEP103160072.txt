Middlesex ss}  Prudence Earl Martha Michael the widow of John Michael deced maketh oath that her Said late husband informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born at Hamborough beyond Sea & to her knowledge information or belief never did any Act to gain a settlement for himself in Great Britain That she this Deponent was born in Mandlins Rents in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act either before or since her Said late husbands death to gain a settlement for herself Save what She acquired by Marriage

Sworn this 15. day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Martha Michael
