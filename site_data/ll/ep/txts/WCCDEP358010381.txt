Middx to wit}  Charles Wright  Shoemaker aged near thirty years maketh Oath and saith that he never served an Apprenticeship to his said Trade but gave a person Two Guineas to teach him the Business and lived with him for seven or Eight Months but not longer at Hendon in Middlesex And saith that before he learned his said Business and about fifteen years ago he lived on hired yearly Servant with Mr Vaux an Apothecary in Pudding Lane in the Parish of Saint Magnus by London Bridge in the City of London for the space of fourteen Months together at five pounds a year Wages And since he quitted that Service he hath not lived an hired yearly Servant for any Space of time whatsoever rented a Tenement or Lodging at so much Rent as Ten pounds a year paid any kind of Taxes or done any other Act to his Knowledge belief whereby to acquire a legal Settlement in any Parish or place.

Sworn the 16th. Day of December 1790 Edward Hodsoll

Charles Wright
