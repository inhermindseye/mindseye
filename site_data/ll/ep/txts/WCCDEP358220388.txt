Westmr. to wit John Miller Aged about Twenty Eight Years on his Oath saith that he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one William North a Baker in Great Wyld Street  Lincolns Inn Fields in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for One Year at the Yearly Wages of five pounds Twelve Shillings besides Diet & Lodging and after he quitted his said Service he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th. day of Septr. 1772 before} R. Butler 

his John Miller Mark
