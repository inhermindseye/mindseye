Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Hale aged about 44 years upon her Oath saith that She lived as a Rented a House in Chair Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for near Two years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and paid Taxes that she hath quitted said House about Eight years since which time she hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 25th- day of Febry- 1797 before me} J Hunter

her Catherine Hale Mark Witness B Newman
