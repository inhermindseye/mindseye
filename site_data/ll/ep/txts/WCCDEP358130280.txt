Middx  Dorothy Gallington aged abt. 53 Yrs. upon Oath says She is the Widow of Benjn. Gallington who hath been dead abt. two Years Says her said late Husband Rented a House in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Brides in the City of London of the Yearly Rent of Fifty two Pounds lived therein abt. one Year & quitted same abt. Sixteen Years ago Says she hath not since her said Husbands Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the 22d day of Febry 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Dorothy Gallington
