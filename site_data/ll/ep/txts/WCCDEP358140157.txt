Middx.  Grace Masters aged abt. 51 Years on the behalf of her Son Wm. Masters aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath says he never was Married and that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Ashton a Butcher in Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for Two Years at the Yrly. Wages of £8. and lay During the said Term of Two Years in the house of the said Mrs. Ashton the Corner of Clare Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and hath quitted said Service upwards of one Year since which time he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 13th. of Septr. 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

Grace Masters
