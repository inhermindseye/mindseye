Middx to wit Alice Bagnall Aged about Twenty three Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servt . with Mr. John Harding a Taylor in Grange Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes  in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year & Eight Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her sd. Service about Six years ago since which she hath never gained any other Subsequent Settlemt. And further on her Oath says that on the 14th day of Feby. last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the lying in Hospital near Westmr. Bridge in the County of Surrey which  is intended to be baptized by the name of Valentine and is now chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further says that one Thomas Butler Junr ... Clark to his Huck  Thos. Butler in the Modelling Room in the Tower is the Father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the month of June last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. and that the sd. Thos Butler the Younger & no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 26th. day of March 1783  Wm Kitchiner

Alice Bagnall
