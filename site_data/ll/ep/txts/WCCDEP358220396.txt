Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Bucker aged about Thirty Three Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Richard Bucker who died about fortnight ago with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Kingston upon Common near Portsmouth in the County of Hants about Eight Years ago by whom she hath Three Children now living Namely John Aged about Seven Years Mary Aged about Three Years and James Aged about Eight Months Says her said late Husbands Father was Born in the Parish of Bishop Stoke in the County of Southampton or otherwise belongs to the said Parish of Bishopstoke he having a Certificate therefrom Says her said late Husband Served Seven Yrs. Apprenticeship with his Father at Petworth in Sussex but he being a Certificated Man gained no Settlement thereby as she is Advised and bels. And further says that her said late Husband has of her Informed her he never gained any Settlement whatever orwise than as aforemd.

Sworn this 20th. day of Octr. 1772 before} Tho. Kynaston  Jno. Wright

Her Elizth. Bucker mark
