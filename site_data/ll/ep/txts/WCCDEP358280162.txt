Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Bennett aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Joseph Walker a Land waiter in Dean Street Borough in the Parish of Saint for 5 years and upwards at the yearly Wages of £6 that she hath quitted said Service about 5 years since which time she hath done No Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 31st. day of March 1784 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Robinson in New Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Joseph That the above named Joseph Walker who now lives in the City Road is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Joseph Walker is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of June 1788 before} M Addington

Sarah Bennett

the Father Sd Joseph Walker taken £30 paid & Release Exted
