Middx.  Ann Lyon aged abt. 41 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Lyon who has been dead abt. One Year by whom she has two Children Sarah aged abt. four Years and Lucy aged abt. Sixteen Months says her said husband was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to his Uncle  Charles White a Shoemaker and served him two Years & Upwards the first part of his said Apprenticeship in Broad St. Giles's in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx. and then absconded from his said Master and was never assigned Over to any Person or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year or Ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes as her said husband inform'd her and as she verily Believes: says she hath not since her said husbands deceased Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt to her knowledge or Belief

Sworn this 15th. day of Decr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Ann Lyon

Upon the Complt. of Mr. R: Wimberley J: P:
