Middlesex & Westmr.}  Mary Hyslop Aged about fifty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Wm. Hyslop (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at Dublin in Ireland forty Years ago Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Wardour Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for five Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about five Years ago and after quitting the same her said Husband never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husbands having her

Sworn this 21st. day of Novr. 1770 before Us R Butler W Kelyngs

Mary Hislop
