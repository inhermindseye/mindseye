he was passd. to St. Anns it turn'd out it was St. Jas: St. Anns Appeald. took him & passd. him to St. Jas's to wit David Read aged upwards of Twenty one years upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to one James Smith of Peter Street Soho in the Parish of Saint Anns in the Liberty of Westminster that he served his said Master upwards of two years and an half that then he said Master broke and after he quitted said Master he has not been in any Regular Employ whereby to the best of his Knowledge or Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 22d. day of Octr. 1785 G Mercer James Croft

David Read
