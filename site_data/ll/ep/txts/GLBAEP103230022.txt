Middlesex ss}  Mary Rous the widow of John Rous deced maketh oath that her said late husband heretofore rented a house of the yearly rent of twenty pounds in the Victualling office Square in the Parish of st Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County and resided thereon for the space of nine months That her said husband did not afterwards rent a house or Lodging of the yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds or served any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath two Children namely Mary aged about five years & two months & Elizabeth aged about eight months & half the lawful issue of this Deponent by her sd husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this- 3d. day of March 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Mary Rous
