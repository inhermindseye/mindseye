Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Lawrence aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with John her Present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Annes  upwards of Seventeen years ago that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Nine Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds a year and upwards that he hath quitted said House about Twelve Months since which time he has done no Act to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of Septr. 1788 R Butler

her Ann Lawrence  Mark
