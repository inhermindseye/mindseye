Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Rebecca Spencer aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that about Seven years she Intermarried with  George Spencer her late Husband at the parish Church of Bow in the County of Middx that her said Husband hath been dead about 4 years that her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to Thomas Rusco of Bell Wharf Shadwell in the parish of Saint Paul Shadwell in the County of Middx  Pipe maker for 5 years by Indres duly Executed that he served the whole of his said Apprenticeship with the said Thos. Rusco since which time her said late Husband has not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Etc. knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any subsequent Settlement And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that she hath not since the Death of her said late Husband done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of  her knowledge she may have gained any Settlement in her own right and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 25th. day of March 1786  James Croft  Edwd Webster

Rebecca Spencer
