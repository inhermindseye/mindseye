Middx & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Brabrook Aged about Thirty Seven Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or Served any Apprenticeship says She lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Hargrave who keeps the Rainbow Coffee House in Cornhill in the Parish of Saint Michaels Cornhill London for three years at the yearly Wages of seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service About Eight Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 16th. Day of September last She was Delivered of a male Bastard Child at No. 2 at Mrs. Buzzards in Wych-Alley in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Ruiz Brabrook and is become Chargeable to the same She being Poor and Unable to Maintain the said Child and further says that one John Ruiz who Lately Lodged at the Union Coffee House in the Strand had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of December 1779 at the Sign of the Bull & Gate in Holbourn and at sevl. Different times  Afterwards at her this Examinants Lodgings at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which She was so Delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Ruiz and no other Person Did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of Jany. 1781 before  Sampn Wright

Elizabeth Brabrook
