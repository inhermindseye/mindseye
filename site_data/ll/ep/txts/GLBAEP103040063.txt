Middlesex. to wit, Elizabeth Bowyer  Wife of Richard Bowyer maketh Oath and Saith That about Thirty four Years since she was lawfully married to her said Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex That her said Husband about Twenty one years since rented a House in the Cow Yard in the Parish of Saint George in the said County for which he paid the Land Tax and Resided in the same for the Space of Thirteen Years and upwards, That her said Husband hath not since he quitted the said House rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or upwards paid any Parochial or other Taxes or done any other Act to gain a subsequent Settlement. That she and her said Husband being Poor and unable to provide for themselves have applied to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex for Relief and have been Relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 5th Day of December 1797 before us H: Reynell Dan Williams

her Elizth Bowyer Mark
