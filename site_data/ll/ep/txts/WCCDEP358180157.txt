Middx  Mary Briggs on Behalfe of her Mother  Elizabeth Farban upon her Oath saith that the sd. Eliz. Farban is the Wido. of John Farban  Deceased That her sd. late Father  John Farban was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentishipp with Mr. Jas. Newman in Brewers Lane in the parish of Greenwich  Waterman that since his sd. Apprentiship the sd. Jno. Farban in his life or the sd. Elizabeth Farban since his Death never rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place as this Examinant hath been informed & beleives

Sworn 18th. Janry 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

Mary Briggs
