Westmr.  Peter Duncan aged abt. 55 Years lodging in Plough Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upon Oath says he was Married to Margaret his wife in the Parish Church of Christ Church Spittal fields upwards of Ten Years since, says he Rented a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the said Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty One Pounds lived in the same upwards of Ten Years and hath quitted same about 3 Quarters of a Year and hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes

Sworn the 8th. day of October 1765 before R Kilsha Fran Cary

Peter Duncan
