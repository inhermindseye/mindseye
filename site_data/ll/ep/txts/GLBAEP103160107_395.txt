Middlesex ss  Sarah Wheeler  Single woman maketh oath that divers years ago her late Father William Wheeler deced rented a house in Flushing Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there at Which time this Deponent was part of his Family That she this Deponent never lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever bound Apprentice Nor was ever Married nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 17. day of July 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Sarah Wheeler
