Middlesex ss}  Susannah Skelton  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father Thomas Skelton has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was born in Ireland & never did any act to her knowledge information or belief to gain a settlement in England That her late Mother Susannah Skelton deced has informed her & which She believes to be true that her Said Mother was the Daughter of Henry Aston late of the Parish of Saint Michael in the Town of Lewes in the County of Sussex  Glover That he rented a house there & was charged to & paid Parish Taxes for the Same & was born there & legally Settled there at his Death That this Deponents Sd Mother has also informed this Deponent & which she believes to be true that her Said Mother never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what she Acquired by Marriage with this Deponents Father And this Deponent Saith that She herself was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any act to gain a settlement for herself & that She was also born in the Said Parish of Saint Michael.

Sworn this. 23. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Susannah Skelton
