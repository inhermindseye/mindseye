Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Henderson Aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Isaac Henderson who has been dead about four Years and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about fifteen Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Johnson a Buckle maker in Church Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields and served his said Master for five Years or thereabouts and then her said Husband listed and went for a Soldier and never was Assigned over to any other Master neither did her said late Husband gain any other subsequent Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 8th. day of May 1779 before  Wm Kitchiner M Addington

her Elizth. Henderson Mark
