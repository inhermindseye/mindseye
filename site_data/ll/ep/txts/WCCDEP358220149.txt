Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Keele Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Nott a Weaver in the Parish of Saint Mary in Reading in the County of Birks upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds hath Quitted her said Service about Three Quarters of a Year ago and since she quitted the same she never after Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And this Examinant further says that the Seventh day of February last past she was delivered of Male Bastard Child in the House of William Collinson a Picture Frame Maker in Milk Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr which hath since been baptized John Says that her said Master the above named John Nott is the Father of the said Male Bastard Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time the latter end of May 1769 but the Particular day she cannot now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which time she got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Nott and no other person is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of May 1770 before me }

her Jane Keele Mark
