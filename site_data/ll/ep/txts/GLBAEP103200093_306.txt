Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Stevens of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the sd County this Third day of February 1784 -Who on oath Saith that five Weeks ago at the house of Henry Moore Situate in Red Cross Street in the sd Parish Bricklayer She this Examinant was delivered of a Male Bastard Child Since baptized by the name of George Moore And that the Said Child is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that the said Henry Moore is the Father of the Said Child

Taken and Signed the day & year above written before me R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Stevens

Touchd. the Father of a Bastard Child After Birth
