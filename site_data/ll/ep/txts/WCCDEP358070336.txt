Middlesex to wit William Bason Aged about Sixty one years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Rebecca his late at the Savoy Church about Thirty four years ago and by whom he hath no Children living except One now provided for Says he Rented and lived in a House in Pettyford Yard Little Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand upwards of four years at the yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Twenty Nine years ago and after quitting the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Janry 1776 before J. Hale Wm Kitchiner

William Bason
