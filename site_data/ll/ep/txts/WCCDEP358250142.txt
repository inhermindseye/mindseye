Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Ann Grey aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 15th. day of August 1790 she this Deponent Intermarried with Hugh Grey who has enlisted for a Soldier at the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That she hath Two Children living by her said Husband vizt. Hugh aged about Two years and an half and Christopher David aged about Twelve Months That her said Husband was a Scotchman and never gained any Settlement in England That she this Deponent previous to her said Marriage and whilst she was a Singlewoman lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Webb in Newcastle Court in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes for near Two years at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that after she quitted said Service she never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Decr. 1794  N Bond Cha: Churchill

Mary Ann Gray
