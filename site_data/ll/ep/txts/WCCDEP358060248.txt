Middx  Edwd Sawle upon Oath saith he Served 4 Years the latter part of his Apprentiship wth. Mr Wm. White  near the Change in the Parish of Christchurch at Cork in Ireland & never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other place to his Knowledge or beleif and that he has a wife named Mary & 2 Childn. Eliz & Luke

Sworn Jany. 16th. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

Edwd: Sall
