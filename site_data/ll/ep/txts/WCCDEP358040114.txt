Middx. Westmr.  Amelia Holloman on behalf of Ann King an Infant aged 5 Yrs. Daughter of John King deceased by Sarah his Wife (who has absconded from the sd. Infant) upon Oath says the said Child was born in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says she has been informed and beleives that John King the father of the sd. Infant Served 3 Years & upwards the last Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. John Dews a Butcher in Clare market in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 7th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

Amelia Holloman
