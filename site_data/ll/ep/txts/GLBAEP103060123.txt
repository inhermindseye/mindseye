Middx ss  Sarah Burr the Widow of Charles Burr Deced maketh oath that her sd. late husband as this Deponent has been informed & believes was born in Ireland but the particular place of his birth this Depont. Can in nowise setforth And this Depont. has been often informed by her Mother that She this Depont. was born in the parish of Dagenham in the County of Essex And that by means of such her birth She acquired a legal Settlemt. in the sd. parish of Dagenham And this Depont. saith that she was never a hired Servant by the year or ever Served any apprenticeship or Did any act to acquire any Settlemt since her birth to the knowledge or belief of this Depont. (her marriage excepted) And this Depont. doth not know nor believe that her sd late Husband ever acquired any legal Settlemt. in England Nor doth she know nor was ever informed that he ever Acquired any Settlemt. in Ireland except in right of his birth & where he was born in Ireland this Depont. can't Setforth

Sworn the 3 Jany Day of January 1749 before me  Richd Ricards  Saml Gower

The mk of Sarah Burr
