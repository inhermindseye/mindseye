Middx  Wm. Prows aged abt. 31 Years upon his Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Robt. Were of the Parish of Weymouth in the County of Dorsett  Mariner & hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship abt. Sixteen Years & hath not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 23d Febry 1744 before Tho Lane

The mark of Wm. Prows
