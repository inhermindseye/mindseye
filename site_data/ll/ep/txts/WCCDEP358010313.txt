Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  James Allen of Vere Street Clare Market  Glazier on behalf of Carolina Mountague an Infant Maketh Oath and saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that she was born a Bastard in the Parish of Saint mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex  for and that she never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 7th. day of Novr.1789 D Walker Wm Kitchiner

James Allen
