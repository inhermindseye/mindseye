Middx to wit John Ryder Aged about Fifty five Years on his Oath says he Rented and lived in a House in Nags Head Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for One Year and a Half at the yearly Rent of £14 and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted his said House about Eighteen Months ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 20th. day of Decr. 1780 before R Butler

John Ryder
