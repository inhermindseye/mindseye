St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Mary Thomas aged 24 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Lawes in Gilbert Passage Clare Market for three Years two Years and upwards at the Annual Wages of £4, that She quitted that Served about ten or twelve Years ago and hath not since since obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn the 14th day of August 1792 before us } R Butler

Mary Thomas 's Mark

Witness Richd. Dobbin
