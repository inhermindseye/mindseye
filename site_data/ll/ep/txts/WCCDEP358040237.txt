Westmr 

Thos: Martin aged abt. 63 Yrs. removed from the Parish of Kensington in Middlesex by Order dated the 22d. of this Instt. August upon Oath Saith about 12 or 14 Years ago he lived in and rented the Grace Dragon Alehouse in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of £27 for One Year & upwds. and has not since he Quitted the sd. house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn 24th Augt. 1748 before}

The Mark of Thos. Martin
