St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) George Pigott aged 50 years or thereabouts Widower Upon Oath saith that he has neither Wife nor Child now living that while he was single and unmarried he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Child of Lincolns Inn Fields and as for sixteen Months at the Yearly Rent of £9 in the capacity of second Coachman that he lodged over his said Mastal Master's Stables in Portugal Street in the parish of St Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex during such Service that he about 27 years ago he quitted that Service and hath not since obtained Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 13th day of February 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner
