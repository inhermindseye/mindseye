Middx  Margaret Horton aged about 21 Years upon her Oath saith she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Hedge a Baker at the Yearly Wages of £3 for the Space of 1 Year & 3 Months or thereabouts & that she lived with the sd. Mr. Hedge about Nine Months the latter part of the sd. Term opposite the New Inn in Holborn in the parish of St. Giles in ye Feilds in the County of Middx & hath left her sd. Service about 3 Years Says she hath not since rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 20th 1745 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Margt. Horton
