Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Alphey aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Mullar in Villers Street York Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for 18 years Months & upwards at the yearly wages of £7 hath quitted said Service upwards of 2 years ago since which time she has not lived as an hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of March 1787 Mr Barnfather Tho: Collins

her Elizabeth Alphey Mark
