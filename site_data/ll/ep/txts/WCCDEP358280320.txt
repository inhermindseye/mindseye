St. Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster } (to wit) Margaret Callaughan singlewoman Aged twenty two years Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married (except by the Ceremonys of the Roman Catholic Church) that she is now single was born & Resided with her Father at his House in Ireland until about Eighteen Months ago when she came to England that she hath never lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve months together in this Kingdom or done any other Act or thing whereby she might be entitled have acquired any legal settlement here that her said Father Owen Callaughan Rents and lives at a Farm at One Ballis having in the County of Cork and Kingdom of Ireland where he has Resided ever since his Marriage as she hath been informed & believes And this Examinant farther saith that she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of Mr Coleman at No 7 in Harford place in this parish on Tuesday 19th. April last where Child hath since at the Roman Catholic Chapel in Duke Street  Lincolns Inn fields been Named or Christened John and that that Michael Coleman by trade a Whitesmith who works a a Labourer in the day time is a Watchman in the beat in Red Lion Street Holborn & Resided at No. 4 Mr Welch's No. 4 in Bainbridge Street Saint Giles's & no one else is the real and true Father of such of which she was so

delivered as aforesaid he having Several times had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body at since or one of which times he did beget the same Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid

Sworn before Me the 4th. May 1791 } R Butler

Margt. Callaughan 's Mark

Child Died before Man taken, he dischged (done with)

Michl. Coleman taken Escaped as going to the Magistrates 3d. May 1791 Retaken discharged 4th by the Magistrate as to the Bastard the Child being Dd..
