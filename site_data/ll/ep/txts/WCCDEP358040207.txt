Westmr. 

Lucy Baldwin wife of Wm. Baldwin upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Wm. Baldwin on the 14th. Day of March 1742 at the Fleet London Says her sd. husband now rents a house of Twenty pounds a Year & upwards at the Corner of Things head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex and that he has turned her (this Examint,) out of Doors and now refuses to provide for her by means whereof she is obliged to apply for relief to the Overseers of the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she not being also to maintain herself

Sworn June 17th, 1748 before} J Coulson

Lucy Baldwin
