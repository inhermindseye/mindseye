Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Susannah Williams aged about 37 years upon her Oath said that she hath an illegitimate Child living Vizt Susannah Grage aged about Sixteen years born in the Queens Hospital that her said Daughter never was boud an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever did any Act Matter or thing to gain a Settlement in her own Right That she this Deponent being Single and unmarried lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs Mason in St. James's Market in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Eighteen years ago since which time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months

Sworn this 2d. day of Janry. 1795 } J Hind Rupt Clarke

her Susannah Williams Mark
