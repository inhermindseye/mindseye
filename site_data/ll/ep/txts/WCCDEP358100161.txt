Middlesex to wit Wm. Griffiths Aged about Sixty two years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Margarett his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Margarett Moses Bread House Street in the year 1766 and by whom he hath no Child or Children living Says he Rented & lived in a House in King Street in the Parish of St. Geo: Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for Six Months at the yearly Rent of £40. hath quitted said House upwards of 15 years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year Paying Taxes or or.wise howsoever

Sworn this 23d. day of Decr. 1783  Tho Collins  Jno Barnfather

Wm Griffiths
