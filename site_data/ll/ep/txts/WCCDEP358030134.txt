Middx  Margt. Hackett  Wido . of Nicholas Hackett upon Oath says she intermarried with the sd. Nichs. Hackett on the 26th. Day of January about 25 Years since in Georges Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland Says her sd. Husband Dyed on the 15th: Day of Decr. last & was a native of Ireland as is also this Examinant & never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servant in England whereby to gain a Settlement to this Examinants Knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Daughter by the sd. Nicholas Hackett (to wit) Elizabeth aged upwards of 7 Yrs. who was born in Salisbury Court in Fleet street in the Parish of St. Bridgett vulgarly Brides London & being Poor is not unable to maintain herself & Child

Sworn April 15 1746 before  Tho Lane .

Margret Hackett
