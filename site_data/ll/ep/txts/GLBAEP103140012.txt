Middlesex ss}  Christopher Owen maketh oath That he well knew his late Uncle James Owen & Ann his Wife both deced and this Depont. hath been informed by the Said James Owen & which he believes to be true that he the said James Owen was duly bound apprentice to a Certain Person who lived in the Parish of Mortlake  in the County of Surry & Carried on the business of a waterman there but whose name is unknown to this Depont. And that by means thereof he this Deponts said Uncle gained a legal Settlement there for himself & Family and this Deponent is the better induced to believe such Information to be true for that about six Months Ago his said late Uncle (as he informed this Depont & which he believes to be true) took a Certain Child of his named Elizabeth Owen aged about Seven years out of the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Mortlake Where he the Said James Owen & his Wife & Family had been lately relieved upon account of the said parish being the last place of their legal Settlement and this Deponent dont know or believe that his said Uncle ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor Can he give any other Account respecting the Same And that the said Child is likely to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex .

Sworn this 15th day of December 1773 before  Cha: Digby Wilmot

Chrisr. Owen
