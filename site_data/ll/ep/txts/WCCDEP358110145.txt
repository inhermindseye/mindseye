Westmr.  Priscilla Tanner upon Oath Saith She is the Wido. of John Tanner who dyed upwds. of 2 Yrs. ago and with whom She intermarried at the Fleet London abt. 15 Years ago and that her sd. late husband informed her he Served his Apprentiship with Mr Joshua Murphey a Chimney Sweeper in Newtoners Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in ye Fields in the County of Middlesex & after the Expiration of his Apprentiship lived a Servt. with the sd Mr. Murphey for one year in Newtoners Lane afsd. and never after he quitted the Said Mr. Murpheys Service nor has this Examinant Since his Decease Rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Jany. 15th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Prisc Tanner
