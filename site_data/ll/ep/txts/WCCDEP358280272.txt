Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Sadler aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married bound Apprentice on ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year That she this Examinant was born in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes where her Father kept house for sevl. years And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one James Laurie a Taylor in New Street Square Fetter Lane is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said James Laurie and no other Person did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 22d. day of March 1790 } R Butler

her Mary Sadler Mark

Mary Sadler at Mr. Mays Clements Lane

James Laurie is a Tall Thin Man rather dark Complexion wears his Hair Tied behind wears black

Settled pd. £10
