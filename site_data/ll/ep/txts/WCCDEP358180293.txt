Middx  Eliz: Parker aged upwds. of 60 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she lived a Yearly by hired Servt. with Mrs. Bracegirdle in Heward Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £5 for 8  Years or thereabouts & hath left her sd. Service abt. 2 Years since which time she hath not Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 19th. 1744 before} Tho Lane .

E. Parker
