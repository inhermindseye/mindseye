Middlesex, to wit, Sarah Solomon , Singlewoman maketh Oath and Saith, that some time ago she hired a House No. 5 , situate in that part of Saint Catherine's Lane which lies in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate , in the County of Middlesex , at the Yearly Rent of and that she has resided in the said House under such hiring for the space of Three Months last past

Sworn this 19th Day of February 1791 , before}
