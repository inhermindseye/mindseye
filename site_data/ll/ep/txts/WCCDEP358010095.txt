Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Ann Pollard aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married as a hired yearly Servant for one year bound an Apprentice ever Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 for 40 days or paid Taxes tha she was born in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clem Danes that she has done no Act to gain a Settlement otherwise than by Birth that she has 5 illegitimate Children living vizt. Carolina aged about 12 years born at Mrs. Pollards in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes  Fras. aged about 10 years born in Wych Street in the same place Parish Ann aged about 4 years born at Mr. Lambleys a Butcher in Street in the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate Mary aged about 3 years. born at the same Place in the said Parish and Edwd. aged about 2 years born at Mr. Fields an Iron Shop in upper Thames Street in the Parish of Allhallows  less in the City of London that she this Examinant being Poor & unable to maintain her said Children is obliged to leave same to the care of this Parish

Sworn this 23rd. April 1787 R Butler

 and Pollard
