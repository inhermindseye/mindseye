Middx. & Westmr.  Ann Poole aged abt. 27 years upon Oath says she never was Married And that she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mrs. Foot in the Parish of Maidstone in the County of Kent for Three Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and Ten Shillings; hath quitted said Service abt. three Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year. And further Upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 27th. day of June last she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Foulks in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlx which has Since been baptized by the name of Elizabeth and is now living and become chargeable to the said Parish says That one John Ling a Journey man Taylor who lodges at Mr Cox's (No. 20) in Stuarts Rents in the parish of St. Giles in the fields did beget the said female Bastd. Child on her body he the said John Ling having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body the later End of the Month of Sepr. 1759 & at sevl. other times Since at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Ling and no other person is the true & real father thereof.

Sworn Sepr. 10th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

Ann Poole

Upon the Complt. of Mr Kirk Ovr . J: P:
