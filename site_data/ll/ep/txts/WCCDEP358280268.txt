Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Martha Dunk aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the 13th. day of January last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Tully in Alphabett Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath been half baptized by the Name of John That one John Beaumont a Grocer at Rochester is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child thereon of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Beaumont and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 16th. day of Febry. 1790 } R Butler

Martha Dunk

Martha Dove lives at Mr. Tullys
