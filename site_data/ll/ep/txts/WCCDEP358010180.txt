Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  to wit William Stone aged about 21 years upon his Oath saith that about 4 years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present wife at the parish Church of Saint Leonard Storeditch that he was born in Brook Street Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Bars in the City of London that her never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Janry. 1788 }

W: Stone
