Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Mansel aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Pigmore Parliament Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster for 2 years at the Yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted said Service about 11 Years ago since which time she hath done no Act to gain any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Novr. 1786 D Walker Rups. Clarke

her Sarah Mansell Mark
