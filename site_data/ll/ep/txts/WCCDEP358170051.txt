Middx  The Examination of Jno. Rydler aged abt. Twenty Five Years now in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 5th- Day of May, 1741 Who upon his Oath saith yt- his late Father Jno. Rydler lived in & rented a House in the Butcher Row in the sd- Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds for the Space of Years & there Dyed & this Examinant further saith that he never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt- for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
