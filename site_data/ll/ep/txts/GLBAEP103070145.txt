Middx ss The Examination of Ann Bond  Singlewoman taken on oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the County of Middx residing in or near the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx this 6 day of October 1755

This Examinant on her Oath says that about Two years and a quarter ago one William Binmoore a Mariner who lodged then at the Hermitage in the Parish of St. John Wapping Middx Came to this Examinant at the house of Mrs. Hall a Baker in Fetter Lane London where this Examinant yn lived & did Prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at the sd house and then this Examinant with the sd. William Binmoore removed into the parish of St. John Wapping where they lived together for about three quarters of a Year And the sd. William Binmore during such his Cohabitation with this Examinant had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body several times whereby she Conceived with Child of a Male Bastard Child of which she was Delivered at the house of Mr. Phillips in the

Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in ye sd County about Seventeen Months ago which sd Female Bastard Child hath been baptised by the Name of William And hath been now is and still is likely to be Chargeable to the sd Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate this Examinant being poor & not able to provide for the same And this Examint. farther saith that the sd. William Binmoore is the true and only father of the sd. Male Bastard Child And that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been trad or Compelled to be Examined by any person or persons whatsoever

Sworn this 6th day of October 1755 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Ann Bond
