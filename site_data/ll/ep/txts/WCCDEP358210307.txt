Westmr.  John Whittington Aged about Forty Years, Upon Oath says he intermarried to Isabella his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about a Year and half ago, by whom he hath one Child now living (to wit) John Aged about Six Months; Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship to Mr. Symmonds in little Russell Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster  Peruke Maker , and never Rented House or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 28th. day of July 1768 , before}  Fran Cary

John Whittington
