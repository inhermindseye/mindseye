Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Fennelly on behalf of Elizabeth Bridges her Sister who is now ill Maketh Oath and Saith that she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that the said Elizabeth Bridges Intermarried with one James Bridges (who is now deced) at the Parish Church of Saint Annes Westmr . about Thirty years ago That the said James Bridges Served his Apprenticeship to one Alexander Smith a Cabinet Maker in Castle Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might lived gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of May 1799 before us G S Goodenough Saml. Edwards

her Mary Fennelly  mark
