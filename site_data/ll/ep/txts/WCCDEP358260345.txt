Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Lydia Buckstone aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that about Eight years ago she Intermarried with Samuel Buckstone her present Husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church belonging to Worksworth in the county of Derby by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Jarman aged about Seven years and Jonathan aged about Twenty one Months That her said Husband informed this Examinant which Information she verily believes to be true that her Served his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Bridlebank of Worksworth in the Parish of Worksworth in the County of Derby Shoemaker and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement and further Saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 8th. day of October 1798 before us }

her Lydia Buckstone Mark
