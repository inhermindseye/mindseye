Middx  Thomas Kempter upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his present Wife  Hanah Kempter on or about the 16th. Day of Augt. 1735 at the parish Church of Aylesbury in the County of Bucks by whom he hath one Child (to wit) James aged abt. 9 Months Says he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Holden in Cooks Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £14 for the Space of 3 Yrs. and upwards being all that time a Singleman & hath left his sd. Service abt. 14 Yrs. Says he hath not since he left the sd. Mr. Holden rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 17th. Octr. 1745 before} Wm: Jones . Tho Kempster
