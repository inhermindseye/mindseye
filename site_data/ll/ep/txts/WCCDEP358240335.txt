St Clement Danes Octr 12th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Johanna Westcott  singlewoman aged thirty two years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never served any Apprenticeship lived as an hired yearly Servant or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own right to the best of her knowledge or belief, that she is informed and verily believes that she is the Daughter of Hannah Westcott otherwise Dawson deced by Richard Westcott and was born in the British lying in Hospital in Brownlow Street  Long Acre in or abt the Month of October November 1760 that the said Richard Westcott and Hanh.. Westcott or Dawson were Married at the Devizes in Wiltshire on or about the 28th day of July 1749 but that he the said Richard Westcott before the time of such Marriage had been Married to a former Wife (Mary Westcott) who (as this Deponent is informed and believes) was living at the time of such Marriage and of the birth of her this Examinant as also at the time of this Deponent's said Mother's Decease and long afterwards And this Examinant upon her Oath further saith that she hath been informed & verily believes that her said Mother before her Marriage with the said Richard Westcott she had been legally Married to a Mr.. Dawson who was

is distressed and unable to maintain herself and Child without assistance

Sworn the 11th October 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Elizth Cooke 's Mark

Witness Garrett

Township of Macclesfield in Prestbury parish Cheshire  then Dead but saith that she hath never heard where he was born or the place of his legal Settlement that she this Deponent hath been informed and verily believes her said Mother was settled born at in the Township of Macclesfield within the parish of Prestbury in the County of Chester and was legally settled in the said Township of Macclesfield within the parish of Prestbury afsd.. in right of her Father who (as this Deponent is informed & believes) was the owner Tenant inhabiting premises of considerable Value in that Township And that she this Deponent is distressed and unable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn the 12th day of October 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D. Walker

Joanna Westcott

Not Removed upon this Examination but discharged by the Overseers & afterwds. becoming chargeable in January 1794 her Examination was taken again & she Removed by Newman the Beadle Vide page 291
