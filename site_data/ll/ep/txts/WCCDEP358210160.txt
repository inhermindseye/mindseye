Westmr.  Celia Sourey aged about 27 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Bridget Maggot -in Cliffords Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, quitted said Service about the Month of September or October 1763, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Oath says that on the 21st, day of August 1764, she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Wright in Ogden Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized Anne and that Thomas Mears the reputed Father of the said Child died on the first day of this Instant May.

Sworn the 19th. day of May 1767 . before.} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

Celia Sourey
