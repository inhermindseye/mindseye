Middx  Jane Chilton aged about 27 Years upon Oath says She is a Singlewoman and never was Married  an Apprentice or Yearly Servant Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes Says her Father Thomas Chilton now Rent a House in Cursitors Street in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex & pays the Poor Rate Says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Liberty of the Rolls and that William Howard a Printer in Brook Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn did beget the said Bastard Child or Children of which She is now Pregnant to the said William Howard having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of June or July last in the House of Mr. Deall in Chick Lane near West Smithfield at which time he did beget the Bastard Child or Children of which She is now pregnant

Sworn the 13th. March 1756 . before Barth: Hammond

Jane Chilton
