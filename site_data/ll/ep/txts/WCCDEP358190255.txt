Middx  Jane Moulson aged about fifty Years removed from the Parish of St Andrew Holborn above Barrs upon Oath Says that she is the Wife of Thos Moulson that she Intermarried with the Said Thos: Moulson at the fleet London upwards of fifteen Years since says her said Husband Served two Years the last part of his Apprentiship with Mr Peter Moulson at the Ship Tavern by Temple Barrs in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the said County of Middx as She hath been informed and believes that he never rented a House paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her said Husband Absconds from her that he is labourer to One Mr Honey Bricklayer upon Snow hill and is able to provide for this Examinant

Sworn July 17: 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jane Moulson
