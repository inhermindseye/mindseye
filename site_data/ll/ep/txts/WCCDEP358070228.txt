Westmr to wit William Woodstock about Sixty Seven Years of Age on his Oath says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at Saint Johns Chappel Clerkenwell about 5 Years ago and by whom he hath no Children living Says before he Married with his any of his former wives & while he lived as a Single man he lived with one William Chase Esqr in Church Street Edmonton in the Capacity of A Footman for 2 Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds, has Quitted his said Service in the Year 1732 and after he Quitted Service he never Rented House of ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d day of Novr 1774 before}

NB this Examinants wife Died between the time of Exn & his Coming to be sworn so he was not passed he only wanting that on acct of her being sick
