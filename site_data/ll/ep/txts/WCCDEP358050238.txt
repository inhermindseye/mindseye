Westmr; 

Mary Prick Wido. lodging at Mr Perrys the Corner of Hollis Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. on behalf of Ann Bugrove a Lunatio upon Oath says that she the Sd. Ann Bugrove is the wife of Nichs. Bugrove who is now a Prisoner in the Kings Bench Prison for Debt as this Examinant has been informed and verily beleives Says the said Nichs. Bygrove rented a house & Garden Ground in Tothil fields in the parish of St. Margt. Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and upwards and lived in the same upwds. of two Years and hath quitted sd. house and ground near one Year and that the said Nichs. Bygrove has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examints. knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 26th. 1750 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Mary Prick
