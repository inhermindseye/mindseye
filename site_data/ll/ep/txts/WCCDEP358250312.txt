Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Beadle aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 24th. day of November 1795 she Intermarried with Henry Beadle her present Husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bow in the City of London That her said Husband lived as a hired Yearly Servant with General Markins at Mitcham in the Parish of Mitcham in the County of Surry for Two Years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Twelve Guineas that he hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief  gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 26th. day of Septr. 1797 before us N Bond R Clarke

Eliz Beadle
