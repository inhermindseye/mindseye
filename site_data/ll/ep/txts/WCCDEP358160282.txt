Middx  The Examination of Ann Young  Wido . of Edwd. Young passed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx by Order bearing Date the 16th. Instt Who upon her Oath saith that abt. 15 Years ago her sd. Late Husband Edwd. Young rented a Shop under Lincolns Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the same County the yearly Rent of thirteen Pounds or thereabts. & kept the same for the Space of 10 Years & upwards & further on her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband never after he left the sd. Shop neither hath She this Examinant since his Death rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintain herselfe

Sworne this 17th day of March before} Jas Gronous

The Mark of Ann Young
