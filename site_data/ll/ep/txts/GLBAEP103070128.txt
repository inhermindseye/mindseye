Middx ss  Patience Hankins  wife of Richard Hankins  Maketh oath that her said husband lately went away from this Deponent to places Unknown and hath not as yet returned And this Depont. Saith that her sd. husband hath informed her that he was born in the Parish of St. John at Hackney in the County of Middx And that this Deponts. husbands father Richard Hankins deced when a single man lived as a hired servant by the year in the same Parish & gained a legal settlemt. there which information this Dept. believes to be true And this Depont. farther saith that to her knowledge information or belief her said husband never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any Parish offices nor never was an Apprentice or a hired servant by the year nor that he or his said father hath gained any settlement subsequent to the sd. Richd Hankins the fathers living as a hired servant as abovesd. And saith farther that she hath four Children to wit Richard aged about seven years Patience aged about four years Richard aged about Two years & six Months

and Mary aged about thirteen Months the lawfull issue of this Depont. by her sd husband.

Sworn this 24 day of December 1754 before  Richd. Ricards  Chris: Scott

The mark of Patience Hankins
