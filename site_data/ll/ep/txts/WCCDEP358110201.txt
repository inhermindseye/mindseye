Middx  Cathn. Mathews upon Oath Saith she was married at the Fleet London to Hugh Mc danell a Painter on the 23d. Jany. 1749 and that afterwards it Appeard the sd. Mc. danell had a Wife living at the time of her Intermarriage with him whom this Examinant has often Seen and that the sd. Mc. danell and his sd. Wife now Cohabit together. And this Examint. further maketh Oath that on the 6th. Feby. 1755 she was married at the Fleet London to Henry Bazley a Corporab in the 3d. Regt. of Foot Guards and that she has since been informed the sd. Bazley had a Wife living at Banbury in Oxfordshire when he intermarried with this Examt. and the sd Henry Bazley acknowledged the same And this Examint. further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she was Delivered of a female Child on the 6th. Febry last at a house in Brewers Court Bedford bury in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. which has

since been baptized Sarah

Sworn March 7th. 1753 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Cathn Mathews
