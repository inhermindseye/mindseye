Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Armmur of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this twenty fifth day of April One thousand Seven hundred & eighty Six Who Saith that She is now with Child and that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard And to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that George Quested late of Crown Court in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate  Mariner is the Father of the Said Child

Taken and Signed the Day and year above written before me R Davies

The Mark of Ann Armmur
