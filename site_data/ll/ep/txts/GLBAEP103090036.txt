Middlesex ss  William Creed maketh oath that divers Years ago he was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds with one Thomas Deale in New Red Cross Street in the Parish of Saint George in Southwark in the County of Surry  Broker and Continued in such service for near the Space of Three Years and by Means thereof as he is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that during such his Servitude he was and still is an Unmarried Man and that he hath not since lived as a hired servant by the Year nor hath he ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever served any Parish offices nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes Nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his servitude as above

Sworn this 9th. day of May 1766 before  Chris: Scott

William Creed
