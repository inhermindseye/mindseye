Aldgate Middx

Samuel Nibbs Maketh Oath that about Seventeen year's ago he livd in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for two years And upwards and kept the Alerhouse know by the Sign of the Green Dragon for which he paid Eight and Twenty pounds a year rent for the Said House and paid all Parish taxes and hath gaind no other Settlement Since

Sworn 7th day of March Ano Dom 1746 before Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

the mark of Samuel Nibbs
