Westmr to wit John Harpham Aged about Thirty Eight Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Alice his Wife at Saint Leonard Shoreditch upwards of Eight Years ago, and by whom he hath Two Children now living namely John Aged about Seven years and Ann Aged about five Years says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife & whilst a Single Man he lived as hired Yearly Servant with the Honourable  William Stourton  Esqr Commonly Called Lord Stourton at Chipping hill in the Parish of Witham in the County of Essex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds, hath Quitted his said Service upward of fourteen Years and hath not Since he Quitted the same ever Rented house paid Taxes lived A hired Yearly Servant or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th day of May -1775   Sampn Wright  John Goodchild

John Harpham .
