Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elsibar Watson aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that about 9 years ago she Intermarried with William Watson her late husband at the Parish Church of Saint Giles Cripplegate that she has no Child or Children living by her said Husband that her said Husband has been from this Examinant about Seven years that she heard about Three years ago that he was dead that she has not seen or heard from him since she had this Information which said Information she verily believes to be true that her said Husband Served his Time as an Apprentice to a Mr. Hill in Little Britain that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her oath saith that on the 6th. day of August last she this Examinant was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Crutchley No. 15 Old Boswell Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of Samuel Thomas That one Thomas Acton  Coachman to Mr. Armstrong in Carey Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body for these Six years last past and begotten the same thereon and that the said Thomas Acton is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1788 } R Butler

her Elsibar Watson Mark

Elsibar Watson lives at Mr. Crutchlays

Thomas Acton is a Short Thin Man wears his own Hair Curled in his Neck

taken Attdd. Bow Street Sr. Sampson Wright on the Woman payining herself dischd. the Maces.
