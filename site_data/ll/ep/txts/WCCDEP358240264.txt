St Clement Danes May 28th.. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary Lambeth  (to wit) Mary Newinn  Singlewoman aged eighteen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired Servant with Mr Bennett of Walot Walcot Terrace in the parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry for two years and two Months or thereabouts that her said Master provided her with Meat Drink Washing Lodging and Cloaths and he or her Mistress occasionally gave her Money for her non private use that She left her said Master's Service without his knowledge or consent about four years ago and hath not Since lived as an hired Servant for a twelve month together Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 28th day of May 1793 before us N Bond Mitchell Mary Newin
