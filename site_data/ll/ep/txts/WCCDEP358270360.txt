Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charlotte Hart aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Nicholson in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponents upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Thomas Taylor a Journeyman Blacksmith who did work with Mr. Brodie in Milford Lane and lodged in Huggins Lane is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Thomas Taylor is the only Real & true father thereof

Sworn this 19th. day of April 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Charlotte Hart Mark

Witness B Newman

Man never Apprehended

Child born in the House the 27th. day of April 1798 named Charlotte Hart .

Sent to Enfield  14th. June 1798 .
