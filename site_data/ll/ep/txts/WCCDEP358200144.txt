Westmr.  Catherine Dickenson Aged about Twenty Seven Years upon Oath Says she was never Married was an Apprentice Rented House or paid Taxes Says her Father Richard Dickenson Rented a House in Exeter Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of two Years at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds or thereabouts and never after Rented House or Paid Taxes Says that on the fourth of August last she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the Lying Inn Hospital in Tyburry Road in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middx which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Lucy and is now Living

Sworn the 15th of September 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Cath Dickenson
