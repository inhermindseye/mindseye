Leicestershire to wit }  Elizabeth Fitchett Aged about 39 years upon Oath says she intermarried with her late Husband  Francis Fitchett in the Parish Church of Saint Bennetts Paul's Wharf London about twelve Years since and hath by him four Children all now living (to wit) John Aged upwards of Ten years, Elizabeth aged upwards of Eight years, Mary Aged upwards of Seven years And Francis Aged upwards of six years, Say's her said husband rented a publick house known by the sign of Ben Johnsons head in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex of the yearly rent of Twenty two pounds, and lived in the same about three years and there died Say's she hath since her said husbands Death rented a small house at Ashby Dela Zouch in Leicestershire of the yearly rent of three Guineas, but hath not paid the Land Tax, or any rates or levies for the same.

Sworn the 13th.-day of March 1762 . Before me} W Davis One of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Leicester

Eliz: Fitchet
