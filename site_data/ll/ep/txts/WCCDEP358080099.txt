Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Fielder aged about twenty seven Years on her Oath says she never was married rented house or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Peter Sidebotham  Attornoy in Caroy Street Lincolns Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of £5s. hath quitted her said Service about two Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any Act where by to gain a subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born says that one Thomas Layton A pprentice to one Mr. Rugg working silversmith at No. 35 in St. John Square Clerkenwell is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body the latter end of May last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Thos. Layton and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 18th. Day of Decr. 1776 before me M A

her Sarah Fielder Mark
