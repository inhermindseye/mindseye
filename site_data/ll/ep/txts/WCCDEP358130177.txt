Middx  Elizabeth Knight aged about 49 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with Richard Knight her late Husband deceased at the Fleet London on the 11th. of Febry. 1748. Says her said late Husband before his Marriage lived a hired yearly Servant for one year & upwards with Mr. Mason in Clements Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the yearly wages of Five Pounds & never Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or served any Parochial Office to the best of her Information & belief Says her said Husband has been dead about three Years & She hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant.

Sworn the 12th. Day of June 1756 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Elizth. Knight
