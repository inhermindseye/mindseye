Middx  The Examination of Rebecca the Wife of Wm- Wyatt in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 2d. Day of May 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with the sd. Wm- Wyatt on the 23d. Day of April 1737 And that Wm- Wyatt the Father of this Exaiants. Husband lived in & rented a House in the Parish of St. Anne Blackfryers of the Yearly Rent of £20 for the Space of Years & further saith that her sd. Husband never was an Apprentice rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife Yt. her sd. Husband is delirious & she being Poor is not able to maintaine him.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
