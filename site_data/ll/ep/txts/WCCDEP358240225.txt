St. Clement Danes 6th March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Sarah Ray  singlewoman aged eighteen years and upwards upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she was bound an Apprentice by the parish of St James Clerkenwell to Mr Beale Umbrella Maker in the Strand facing  Norfolk Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex in or about the year 1789 with whom she served as such Apprentice for upwards of Nine Months & afterwards ran away on Acct. of her Master's ill usages that she boarded and lodged in her said Master's House during such Service & since she left her said Master she hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 6th day of March 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Ray 's Mark

Witness Garrett
