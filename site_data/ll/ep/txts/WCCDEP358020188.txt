Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Clarke aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that about Three years ago She Intermarried with Robert Clarke her late husband who has been dead about Four Months at the Parish Church of Saint Clement D Andrew Holborn that she hath one Child living by her said late husband Vizt. Robt aged about Seventeen Months That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Six Months at the yearly Rent of £40 that after he quitted said house he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Febry 1790  Wm. Kitchiner

Ann Clark
