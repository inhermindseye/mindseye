After Birth Ann Hanlon and Joseph her B. Child

St Clement Danes August 5th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman's settlement in St Giles in the Fields  Child born St CD Bennett's Ct.  Reputed Father (to wit) Ann Hanlon  singlewoman aged Nineteen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never lived as an hired yearly for a year or did or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any  legal Settlement in her own right that she is Informed and Verily believes it to be true that she is the legitimate Daughter of William and Ann Hanlon that her Mother now Rents and Resides in an House in Wilds Court in Little Wild  Great Wild Street in the parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and has Rented and Resided therein for four Months or upwards at the Annual Rent of Ten pound pounds that she has never been away from her Mother but for about three Months when she went in Service And that She knows not of any other settlement she is entitled to but in the said parish of St Giles in right of her said Mother as aforesaid And this Examinant upon her Oath that on the on or about the Nineteenth day of July 1792 at the House of her said Mother in Bennetts Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County she this Examinant was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which hath been since named Joseph that the said Child is become chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes And that Stephen Garner

an Apprentice to Mr. Pearson Coach Harness Plater in Drury Lane in the said County in the father of the said Child he having had carnald knowledge of this Examints Body first some time in or about the Month of Septemeber 1791 and at Several times since at so between that & the Birth of the said Child and that he at some or one of which times he did begett the said Child thereon and that he & no one else is the real true Father thereof

Sworn the 5th. day of August 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner D Walker

Ann Hanlon

Stephen Garner was taken  be bound over to the Session Order of Adjudication made & filed Mr Pearson his Master came forward & Settled with the Clveruers for £10:10:0 & he to pay Expences & Save Note for two Months which has been paid

The Child died abt. Novr or Decr. 1793
