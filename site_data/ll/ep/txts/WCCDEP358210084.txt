Westmr.  Mary Billingsley Aged abt. Fifty Two Years Upon Oath Says She is the Wido . of John Billingsley with whom She Intermarried in May fair Chapel abt. Sixteen Years ago Says her sd. late Husband has been Dead upwards of Four Years and Long before his Death Rented a House in Theobalds Row in the Parish of St. George in Queen Square in the County of Middx & lived in same abt. abt. five Years & never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or pd. Taxes Says She hath not since the Death of her sd. Husband Rented House or pd. Taxes

Sworn the 20th. of Octr. 1766 before R Kilsha Barth: Hammond

Mary Billingsley
