Saint Clement Danes Augt. 18th- 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. in St. Matthew  Bethnal Green  to wit Elsaber Barker aged about 15 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she never was married that about Eleven Months ago she was bound Apprentice to Nicholas Blossom who lived in the Jews Walk in the parish of Saint Matthew Bethnal Green in the County of Middlesex Tambour Worker by the parish officer of the said Parish of Saint Bethnal Green for seven years that she lived with the said Nicholas Blossom till within this Ten days last past since which time she has been wandering abroad

Sworn the 18th. day of August 1794 before us M Addington H Mathin

her Elsaber Barker mark
