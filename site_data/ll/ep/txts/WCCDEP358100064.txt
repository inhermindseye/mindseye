Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Bale Aged about Thirty Seven Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Stratford Bow in the County of Middlesex in the Year 1767 and by whom she hath one Child living namely Elizabeth Aged about Seven Years Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one William Lucas at the White Horse in Bow in the said County of Middlesex for Sixteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds hath quitted his said Service upwards of Sixteen Years ago and after he quitted his said Service he never gained any other subsequent Settlemt. either by renting House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or orwise howsoever

Sworn this 24th. day of April 1783 before  Thos: Gilbert Mercer

her Elizth. Bale mark
