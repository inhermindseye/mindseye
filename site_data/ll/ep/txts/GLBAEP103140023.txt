Middlesex ss}  Susannah Lewis  Singlewoman maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or either of them That She this Deponent was born in King Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex That She never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound an Apprentice nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 24th day of January 1774 before  Saml. Spagg  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Susannah Lewis
