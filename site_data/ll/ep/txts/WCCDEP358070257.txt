Westmr to wit Elizabeth Morris Aged about Forty two Years on her Oath says she never was married, Rented House, paid Taxes or served any Apprenticeship; Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs Flynn in Norfolk street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of Five Years and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 11th day- of April 1775 before  R Butler M Addington

Elizabeth Morris her marks
