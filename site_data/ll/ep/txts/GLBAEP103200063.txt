Middlesex ss}  Mary Curttler the widow of Thomas Curttler deced Maketh oath That her sd late husband heretofore Rented a house of the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor hath She Since his death done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere That She hath three Children namely Ann Aged about Six Years Mary Aged near four years & Thomas Aged about two years the lawful Issue of this Depont by her sd husband to whom She was lawfully Married

Sworn this. 1.day of November 1783 }

This Exam was not Sworn to by reason She was never Married to Curttler.
