Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Archer aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that has lived as a hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Luff the Butcher in Sheffield Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for One year and last past whereby she has done no to gain any other Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard and or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes That one Thos. Wardrobe now living with Mr. the Butcher in Clare Markett is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Bo about 5 Mos. ago and at sevl. times since at one which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that the said Thos. Wardrobe is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Febry. 1787 } R Butler

her Ann Archer mark

This is settled by their being Married
