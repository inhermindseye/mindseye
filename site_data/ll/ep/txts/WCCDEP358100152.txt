Middx & Westmr.} to wit James Sanders Aged about Twenty one years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Andrew Rooke who lately kept the Golden Cross in Cross Lane  Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for two years at the yearly Wages of fifty two Shillings besides Diet and Lodging hath quitted said Service upwards of Six years ago since which time he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement by living as a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 12th. day of Novr. 1783  Wm Kitchiner R Butler
