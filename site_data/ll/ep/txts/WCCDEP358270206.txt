Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Penelope Meller aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that on the 2d day of this present Month November she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Elizabeth That one John Muchatt Esquire who lately lodged at Mr. Grahams in Arundel Street in the Strand is the only real and true father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same thereon and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 19th. day of Novr. 1794 } J Hind

her Penelope Meller Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
