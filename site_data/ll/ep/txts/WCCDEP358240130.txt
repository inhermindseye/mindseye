St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster }  St CD Mr Rozier Overseer Mr Burbidge Beadle}  (to wit) Ann Walker aged 50 Years or thereabouts Singlewoman Upon Oath saith That she never was Married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Cox of No. 18 Devereux Court for one Year and about a fortnight, at the Annual Wages of Eight Guineas, that she left her said Master's Service about ten Years ago, and hath since that her said Masters House in Devereux Court wherein she served and lodged, is situate in the parish of St Clements Danes in the County of Middlesex Saith that since she left her Service, with Mr Cox as aforesaid she has never lived as an hired Yearly Servant for twelve months together, Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a Year, paid any parish Taxes, or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 27th. day of July 1792 before Me } R Butler

Ann Walker 's Mark

Witness Edwd. Burbidge
