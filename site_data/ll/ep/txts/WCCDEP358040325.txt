Middx  Saml: Vaughan upon Oath saith he never was married and that he Served about 4 Years the first part of his Apprentiship with Mr. John Perins  Distiller in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and then parted from his sd. Master by Consent Says he never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 23d. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Saml. Vaughan
