Middx  Lucey Walker on Behalfe of Edwd. Stroude an Infant aged abt. 9 Years upon her Oath saith that Mary the Mother of the sd. Infant hath been dead abt. 2 Weeks & that Thos. Stroude the Father of the sd. Infant is abroad & beyond the Seas That the sd. Thomas Stroude was born in the parish of St. Bridgett vulg: St. Brides London & Learnt his trade of his Father  Edwd Stroude who lived in & rented a house in the sd. Parish of St. Bridgett vulg. St. Brides & paid his Share towards all Parochiall Taxes as this Examinant hath been informed & beleives that the sd. Tho: Stroude the father of the sd. Inft. never rented £10. a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworne 17th. Novr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Lucey Walker
