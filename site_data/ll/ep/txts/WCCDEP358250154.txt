Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Sterling aged about 5 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Myer a Limner in Tavistock Row in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for Sixteen Months at the yearly wages of Eight Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Twelve years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1795 } N Bond Edwd: Naish

her Elizabeth E. A Sterling Mark Witness B Newman
