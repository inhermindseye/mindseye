Middx  John Thomas aged about 22 Years upon Oath says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Thomas Tinsley  Innkeeper at the White Hart Inn in the Parish of St. John at Colchester in the County of Essex at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds upwards of two Years and quitted his said Service about three Years and hath not since lived a hired Yearly Servant elsewhere

Sworn Janry. 8th. 1763 before me} Fran: Bedwell

John Thomas
