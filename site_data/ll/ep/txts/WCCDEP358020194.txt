Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Thomas aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that about 13 years ago she Intermarried with George Thomas her present husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That she hath four Children living by her said husband vizt. Susannah aged about 10 years provided for Sarah aged about Eight years Ann aged about Six years and Elizabeth aged about Twelve Months all unprovided for That her said Husband whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Roughsedge a Grocer in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Two years at the yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds and that her said husband has continued to live as a servant at the said house till this present time

Sworn this 12th- day of April 1790 R Butler

her Elizabeth Thomas Mark
