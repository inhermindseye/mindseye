Middx  Wm Napper upon Oath saith he was married to his Wife Hannah at the Fleet London abt. 2 Years ago and that he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Parry a ship Carpenter & lodged the first 3 years (or thereabouts) of that term in his sd masters house in Poplar in the parish of St. Dunstan at Stepney in the County of Middlesex and the remaining four Years in a house to which his sd master removed in Blackwall Yard in the same parish that he never rented ten pounds by the year pd. Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr 28th. 1754 before} 

Wiam Napier
