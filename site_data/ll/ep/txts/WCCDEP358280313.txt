St.. Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  tard to to wit Jane Jones Aged Singlewoman Aged abt. 23 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married lived as an Hired Yearly Servant with Mr Wilson Brass founder Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of £5 that she quitted said Service sometime in the that since she quitted said Service she hath not to the best of her knowledge Information or belief gained any Subsequent any other Settlement And further this Examinant on her Oath saith that on the 25th.. of January last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the lying in House No. 2 George Yard Water Lane Fleet Street London which hath Since been half Baptized by the Name of Thomas And that one Thomas Richardson an Apprentice to Mr. Pearce Kings Head Tavern Middle Row Holborn and no one else is the real and true father of such Child he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body Body and begotten the same Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid

Sworn the of February 1791 before Me  R Butler

Jane Jones 's Mark

Mr Pearce Applied & undertook for his Apped Pearce paid 10 Guas for him Rele prepared
