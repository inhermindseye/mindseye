Middlesex ss}  Catherine Sutor  Singlewoman maketh oath That she was born in Swan Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex That she was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself That her Mother has informed her that she was a New Castle upon Tyne Woman but how she was Settled there she dont Know or Can set forth & that her Fathr Came from Dundee in Scotland but Can't set forth whethr he ever did any Act to gain a settlemt in that part of Great Britain Called England.

Sworn this first day of March 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Catherine Sutor
