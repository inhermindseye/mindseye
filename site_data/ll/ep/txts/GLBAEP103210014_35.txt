Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Jacobs the widow of William Jacobs deced maketh oath that her Said late husband heretofore rented a house of twenty five Pounds a year in  upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph Without Aldgate in the Sd County & resided thereon for about the Space of Six Months & did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or Serve any Parish Office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That She Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath a Child named Edith Jacobs Aged Ten years & Seven Months the lawful issue of this Deponent by her Sd late husband to whom She was lawfully Married the which Child hath done no Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 4. day of October 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Jacobs
