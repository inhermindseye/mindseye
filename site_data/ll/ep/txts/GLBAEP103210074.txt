Middlesex ss}  Rachael Hutchins  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago she was hired and lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mrs Munns a saleswoman in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of fourteen years That she has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain a Settlement since such her Servitude as above mentioned

Sworn this. 16 day of March 1785 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Rachael Hutchins
