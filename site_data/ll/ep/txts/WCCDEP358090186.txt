Middx  Ann Butter Aged about Sixty Eight Years on her Oath says She is the Widow of George Heron Butter who died about Thirty Six years ago and by whom She hath no Children now living says She Rented and lived in a House in Back Street in the Parish of Saint John Horsley down in the County of Surry at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds to the best of her Remembrance and paid Land Tax due for the same and hath not since She quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of Decr. 1780 before R Butler William Hyde

A Butter
