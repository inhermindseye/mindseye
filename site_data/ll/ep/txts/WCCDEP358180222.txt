Middx  Margt. Cleaver  Wido . upon her Oath saith that on the 5th: Day of March last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Cross key Court near Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Baptized Jacob & is likely to become & Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same And that one Jacob Bear  Peruke maker did begett the said Child on her Body the said Jacob Bear & this Examinant liveing together as Man & Wife in Cross Key Alley aforesd. where the sd. Jacob Bear had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Months of May and June last was Twelvemonth and at severall times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that he the sd. Jacob Bear & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne July 25th: 1744 before} Tho Lane . Thos D Neil

The Mark of Margt. Cleaver
