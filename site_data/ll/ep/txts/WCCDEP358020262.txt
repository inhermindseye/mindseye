Middx & Westmr (to wit) John Gibbons a Roug a Rogue & Vagabond Apprented in the parish of St- Clemt Danes in the said County & Liberty on Oath saith he serves an Apprenticeship to Mr Watson Tallow Chandler Thomas Street in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland and since hath not done any at he gained any or. legal Settlemt. to the best best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn before me Ninth April 1791 in Court  Edw: Read

Mark of John Gibbon
