Middx  Daniel Smith upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife  Phabe about Six Years ago at Pancras Church and that he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. and never rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes in any part of Great Britain or Ireland that his father Smith deced lived in and rented a house & Land near St. Thomas's Street in the City of Durham of the Yearly Value of £18 or thereabts. and never after gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examint. knowledge or beleif.

Sworn the 11th. Octr. 1749 before}
