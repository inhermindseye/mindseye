Middlesex and Westminster}  Swear to St. Clemt. (to wit) Sarah Candash Singlewoman , aged twenty one Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith, That she never was married, rented House Tenement or Lodging of ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, served any Apprenticeship or ever lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Years And this Examinant further saith That her Father Peter Candash rented and lived in a House Doors from the seven Stars in Stanhope Street Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , for about Two Years and paid Taxes for the same - That this Examinant was born as she has been informed and verily believes in Bennetts Court in the said Parish of Saint Clements Danes - and that her said Father after quitting his said House in Stanhope Street aforesaid did not at any time in his Life time do any Act Matter or Thing whereby to this Examinants, Knowly Information Belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere - and further this Examinant Says that her said Father died about three Years ago

Sworn this 14th. Octr. 1784 before  Wm. Kitchiner

Mark of The Sarah Candash
