Middx & Westmr.  Mary Poole Aged about Twenty one Year, Upon Oath says she never was Married, And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 19th, day of November 1767, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Doland in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster , which hath since been baptized by the Name of Thomas , says that one William Jenkins a Taylor is the reputed Father of the said Male Bastard Child, he having had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of February 1767, and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said William Jenkins and no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 22d. day of April 1768 , Before} Thos: Balack

The Mark of Mary Poole
