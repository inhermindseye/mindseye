Middx.  Barbara Turner aged abt. 25 Yrs Upon Oath says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Richd. Spencer a Butcher in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for two years or thereabouts at the Yearly wages of £5 and hath Quitted sd. Service abt. three Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year with any Person And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that on the Ninth day of October 1758. She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Sadd in Old Boswell Court in the aforesaid Parish of St. Clemt. Danes . says that Danl. Stobbings a Tripe Man who lives in Clements Lane in the said Parish had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of January 1758 and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Danl. Stebbings and no other person did  beget the same

Sworn Decr. 21st 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Barbara Turner
