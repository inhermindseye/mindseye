Westmr.  Jas. Jones aged about 15 Years upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice by Indenture dated the 1st Novr. 1750 to Mr Jno. Jarman a Pipemaker with whom he lived about five months & then was assigned over to Mr Croskar a barver & Served the sd Mr Croskar as an Apprentice about 17 months in Newtners Lane in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx and left the sd Mr. Croskar in the latter End of last month

Sworn March 2d. 1752 before  Tho: Lane

James Jones
