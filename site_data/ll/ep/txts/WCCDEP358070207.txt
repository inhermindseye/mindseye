Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Pees Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Messrs Berridge and Anderson at the five Bell Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Two Months ago since which she hath done no Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Evans Rice late a Porter to her said Master but where he now is she does not know had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her sd. Masters House the latter end of Decr. last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at several Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Evans Rice and no other person did beget the same And that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 2d. day of Septr. 1774 before  William Addington

her Sarah Pees Mark
