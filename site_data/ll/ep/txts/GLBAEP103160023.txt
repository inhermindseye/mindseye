Middlesex ss}  Margaret Westcomb the widow of William Westcomb deced maketh oath That divers years ago her Said late husband Rented a house of Seventy three Pounds & ten Shillings a Year in the Parish of Bishops Wastham in the County of Southampton & was Charged to & paid Parish Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That she hath a Child named James Westcomb aged about Seven years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom She was lawfully married.

Sworn this 19. day of March 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Margaret Westcomb
