Middx  Amaryllis Davis aged about 34 years upon Oath says She Intermarried with Henry Davis her husband in the Parish, Church of St. Gregory by St. Pane London on the first day of Janry. in the year 1739 old stile by whom She hath two Children Vizt. Margaret aged about sixteen years & John aged about Seven years and further says her said Husband Rented a House in Coleman Street in the Parish of St. Stephen Coleman Street in the City of London at the Yearly Rent of Seventy Pounds and lived therein upwards of six Months and quitted the same about Twelve years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parochial Taxes.

Sworn the 10th. Day of July-1756 before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

Amaryllis Davis
