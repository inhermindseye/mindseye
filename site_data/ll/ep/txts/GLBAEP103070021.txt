Middx ss  John Smith maketh oath that abt Twenty Seven Years ago he was bound apprentice by Indre to James Charlton of Blosom Street in ye Liberty of Norton Falgate in ye County of Middx  Worsted Weaver & lived with his sd. Master two Years in ye Liberty of Norton Falgate aforesd. and then thesd. Master removed into half Moon Alley in Bishopsgate Street in ye Parish of St. Botolph without Ald Bishopsgate London and there Served three Years More with his sd. Master & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlemt in ye sd parish of St. Botolph without Bishopsgate And this Depont. Saith that he hath not Since gained any Subsequent Settlemt. And that he hath a wife Named Mary and two Children Named to wit Elizabeth aged abt. eleven ten Years and Mary Aged about five Years the lawfull issue of this Deponent & his sd. Wife

Sworn this 17 Day of January 1750 before us Rd. Ricards  Saml Gower

John Smith
