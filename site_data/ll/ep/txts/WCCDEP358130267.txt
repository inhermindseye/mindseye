Middx  Tabitha Burrow aged abt. Nineteen Years Upon Oath Says she was never Married that She lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year & upwards with Mr. Mathias Flemming in Whitehorse Yard in the Parish of St- Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of three pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service upwards of three months and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says She is pregnant with Child or Children wch is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same when Born Says the sd. Mathias Flemming did beget the sd- Child or Children on her body in the Month of July 1756 and that the sd- Mathias Flemming & no other person did beget the sd- Child or Children with wch she is note pregnant

Sworn Janry 18th- 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Tabitha Burrow
