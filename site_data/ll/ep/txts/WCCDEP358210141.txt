Westmr.  Mary Bottomly aged about 40 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Bottomly who has been dead upwards of fifteen Years, says her said husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Young a Taylor in Gloucester Street near Queen Square in the Parish of St. George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex and never Rented House or paid Taxes, as she has been informed by her said late husband, and verily believes; says she hath not since her said late Husband's decease Rented House, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 28th, day of February 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The Mark of Mary Bottomly
