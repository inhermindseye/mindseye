St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to Witt Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Hill who is Lanes, Maketh oath and Saith that he hath been informed which information he verily believes to be true that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Joshua Swingland for one year at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds in Harp Lane in the Parish of St. Dunstans in the East in the City of London , and that after she Quitted said service she never did any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23 day of Decr: 1796 N Bond J Robinson B Newman
