Middx to wit Thos. Shrimpton Aged about Thirty Three Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Elizth . his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the Year 1772 and by whom he hath Three Children now living namely Jeremiah Aged about Seven Years John Aged about Four Years and Stephen aged about Twenty Months, Says he was bound an apprentice to Archelain Hill a Shoemaker in the Parish of St. Bartholomew the Great London and served his said Master under such Indenture for the Space of Two Years or thereabouts and afterwards was assigned over to his Father Thos. Shripton of Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr  Steve Mason & Served his said Father under such Indenture tile this Examinant was out of his time which was for five Years or thereabouts and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of July 1784 before  Wm Kitchiner

Thos. Shrimpton
