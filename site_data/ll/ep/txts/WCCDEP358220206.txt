Westmr to wit Joseph Cooper Aged about fifteen Years on his Oath Says he never Rented 10 pounds paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says his late Father William Cooper served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Knight a Plaisterer in Petty France in the Parish of Saint Margarets Westmr . and after as he has often heard his Mother say and which he verily believes to be true and after his said Fathers Death this Examinant was put into the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Margarett where he Continued for Eight Years or thereabout untill he was discharged about a Year and a Quarter ago & has had Three Places since in any one of which he did not live above Seven Months

Sworn this 7th. Day of Decr. 1770 before Us} R Butle 

his Joseph Cooper Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
