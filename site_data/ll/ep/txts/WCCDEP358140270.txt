Middx.  Mary Devans aged abt. 40 Years upon Oath says she is the wife of Patrick Devans (now at sea) to whom she was Married at Wards hill in Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland abt. fourteen Years ago by whom she has three Children Mary aged abt. Twelve Years Thos. aged abt. three years and Eliz : aged abt. Sixteen Months say's her said husband Rented and lived in a house in Peter Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Eleven pounds lived therein abt. Four years & hath quitted Said House about two years & hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 30th. day of July 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Devin
