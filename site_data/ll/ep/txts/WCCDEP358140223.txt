Middx.  Abraham Brooks aged abt. 63 Yrs. upon Oath says he Intermarried with Catherine his wife at the Parish Church of St. Mary hill by Billings gate London about 27 Years ago by whom he has two Children Joseph aged abt. Ten Years and Robert aged abt. Eight yrs. Says he Rented a house in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex . of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and lived in the same abt. One Year and three Quarters and hath quitted it about 25 Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 3d. Feby 1759 before me} Barth: Hammond

Abram Brooks
