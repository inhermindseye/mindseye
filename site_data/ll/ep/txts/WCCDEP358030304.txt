Westmr. 

Terence Macdonawhay aged upwds. of 12 Years upon Oath Says his father & mother are both Dead & he can give no Account of their Settlement Says he was born in the Parish of Kendelegley in the County of Slaghey in the Kingdom of Ireland & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge belief.

Sworn Janry. 6th. 1746 before  Wm. Penes

The Mark of Terence Macdonawhay
