Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Hill aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the Twenty fourth day of April now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of a Mrs. Blackwell in Holles Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the name of Ann That one Joseph Penny a Lighterman who lives in Water Lane Blackfryars is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Joseph Penny is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of Augt. 1798 before me } J Hunter

Sarah Hill
