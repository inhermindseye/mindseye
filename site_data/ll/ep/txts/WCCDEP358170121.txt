Middx  Ann Dwyer  Wife of Jno. Dwyer upon her Oath saith that she was married to her sd. Husband on or abt. the Tenth Day of Octr- 1736 & yt. her sd. Husband is a Native of Ireland & never gained any Settlemt- in England & was born in the Parish of Killuragh in the County of Triperary & further saith that she hath one Child Named Sarah aged 2 Yrs- & upwards that her Husband now absconds from her & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Child

Sworne the 3d. of Septr. 1741 before} Thos De Veil

The mark of Ann Dwyer
