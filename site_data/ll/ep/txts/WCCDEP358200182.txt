Middx  James George Wilson of Howard Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx on Behalf of John Donifont An Infant Aged about Six Years upon Oath Says that Elizth. Donifont the Mother of the said Child is Dead That she Informed this Examinant and which Information he Verily Beleives to be true that the said John Donifont was Born in Little Wild Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx

Sworn the 14th of Decr. 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Jas. Geo: Wilson
