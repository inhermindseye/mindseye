St. Clement Danes 10t. Octr. 1786

James Tasker aged 39 or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he intermarried with May present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint dr in the Field about Seventeen Years age That he has Four Children by her now being Vizt Jaca aged 13 or thereabouts, David aged 9 or thereabouts Jacob aged 5 or thereabouts, and Mary aged 3 or there abouts And Further Saith That about 18 years ago he Rented a House in Portsmouth Street  in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields that the paid £10. a year Rent for the Same and quitted is about 4 years ago That his Wife and a great part of his Family were about 4 years ago in the Workhouse of Saint Giles in are Fields .

Sworn before us this 10th. Octr.1786 Amereen John Cross

James Tasker  his mark

This Mans Wife was passd: to St. Giles's-who Appeald on the Grod. that Pasker had Rentd. 4s. a Week in Hankope Street
