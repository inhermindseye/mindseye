Westmr. to wit John Clayton Aged about Sixty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Susannah his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Andrews about Sixteen Years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says he Rented and lived in a House in Roy Oak Yard Hatton Garden in the Liberty of Hatton Garden in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex for One Year and a Quarter at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the same upwards of Eighteen Years Ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Octr. 1773 before R. Butler Tho Kynaston

John Clayton
