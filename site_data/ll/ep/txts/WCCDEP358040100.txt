Middx  Thomas Pruden upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servt. (whilst a Singleman ) with Wido. Seymore in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £18 for one Year & upwards & left his sd. Service upwds. of 24 Years ago Says he has not since he Quitted the sd. Wido . Seymores Service rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 10th. 1747 before Wm: Jones

Thomas Pruden
