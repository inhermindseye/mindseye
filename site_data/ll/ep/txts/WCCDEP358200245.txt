Middx  Ann Ventum aged about 36 Years upon Oath says, she was never married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Thorp a Baker in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex for three Years and a half at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds hath quitted said Service about two Years and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 18th. day of March 1765 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Ann Ventum
