Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Robinson  Wife of Thos. Robinson Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 22d. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband Thomas Robinson was bound & Served One Year & upwards the first part of his Apprentiship to one Mr. Saml. Glass in the Precinct of White Fryers in the Liberty of the City of Londn . Cordwainer . And this Examint. further saith yt. her said Husband hath not since he left his said Master Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband is now on Board his Majesty's Ship the Romney & yt. she hath one Child Named Thomas aged abt. 9 Months & upwards & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe and Child

Sworn the Day & year above menconed before} Jas Gronous J Poulson

The Mark of Elizth. Robinson
