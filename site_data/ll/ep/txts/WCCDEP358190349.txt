Middx  Thomas Forshall Aged upwards of Sixty Years upon Oath Says he rented a House in Crowne Court in the Butcher Row in ye Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds Ten Shillings and lived therein upwards of Two Years and hath quitted the same about Five Years and hath not since Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn ye 4th Day of May 1763 Before}
