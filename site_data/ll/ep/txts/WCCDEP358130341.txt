Middx.  Mary Rain aged abt. 18yrs. upon Oath says she never was married Rented ten Pounds a yr. paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. says her father Thos. Rain served seven yrs. apprenticeship to Mr. Wodler a Ropemaker in Bridewell Alley in the Parish of St. George Southwark in the County of Surry says her sd. father never Rented ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. as her sd. father informed her & as she verily believes

Sworn the 23d. of May 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Rain
