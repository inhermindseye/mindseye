Westmr. to wit John Langley aged about Thirty Years on his Oath Says he Never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Benjamin Bombay a Farmer in the Parish of Stoke in the County of Bucks for Two Years the first Year at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Three Shillings & the last Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds five Shillings hath quitted h said Service upwards of five Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. day of March 1773 before one}

his John Langley Mark

Sarah Cossham at Mr. Reynolds the cary in Great Portland Street Golden Square
