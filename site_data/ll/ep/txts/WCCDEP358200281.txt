Middx.  Mary Collins aged abt. 47 Years Upon Oath says she was never married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Doctor Macnamara for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds and hath quitted the said Doctor Macnamara's Service about twelve Years, says the said Doctor Macnamara lived sometimes in the Country and sometimes at Mr. Barron's in Howard Street in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that she lived the last three Months (or thereabts.) of her said Service at and was discharged by her Master from the house of the said Mr. Barron and hath not since she left the said Doctor Macnamara's Service Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn 5th. July 1765 before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Collins
