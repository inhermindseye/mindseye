Middlesex ss}  Mary Calendar  Singlewoman maketh oath that She cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents deced or either of them Save that She has been informed that her late Father Andrew Calendar was by Birth a Sootchsman That She this Deponent was born in King Henry yard in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 24. day of Janry 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Calendar
