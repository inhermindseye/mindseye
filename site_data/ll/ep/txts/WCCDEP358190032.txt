Middx.  Barney Onail aged abt. 50 years of upon Oath says that whilst he was a single man he lived a hired yearly Servt. upwards of one year with Mr. Timberlick a farmer at Castle Bear in the Parish of Ealing in the County of Middx. at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds & hath quitted sd. Service abt. Eleven years

Sworn the 27th day of June 1757 before me} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Barney Onail
