Middx  Geo Hallam upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Rebecca on the 4th. Day of Febry 1744 /5 at the Fleet London Says he Served almost Six Years of his Apprentiship with Rt. Penrose Leather Breeches maker and lived with the sd. Rt. Penrose the last Six months of the sd. Term at the Fountain Alehouse in the Minories in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate London and then run away from his sd. Master and went to Sea Says he never was Assigned over to any other Person lived with any Person as a Servt. forty Days with his masters knowledge or consent rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. whilst a Singleman or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 8th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

George Hallam
