St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster  (to wit) Margaret Sherman Aged 27 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married-That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Russel Hair Dressor Fishers Street Red Lyon Square about One year, an half at the yearly Wages of £ that she quitted said Service about a year, an half since,-since which time she has not to the best of her knowledge Information or belief gained any other Settlement

Sworn the 14th day of February 1791 . before Me }
