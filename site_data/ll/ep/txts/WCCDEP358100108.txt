Middx to wit Sophia Powell aged upwards of Eighteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says he has served upwards of Six Years of her Apprenticeship to Mr. Hedges a Jeweller in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County aforesaid and hath gained no other Settlements than As aforesaid And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish (she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born) And further says that one Major David Johnson who lately lodged at her said Masters House and who now Uses Perceys's Coffee House in Rath bone Place is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of March last but the particular day she does not now remember and at one other time afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Major David Johnson and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of Augt. 1783 before W Addington

Sophia Powell
