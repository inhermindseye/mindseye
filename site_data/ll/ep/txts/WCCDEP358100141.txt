Middx to wit Mary Giles Aged about fifty Eight years on behalf of John Haynes aged about Sixty years her Cousin on her Oath Says that John Haynes the Father Rented and lived in a House called or known by the Name or Sign of the White Hart in Bridges Street  Covent Garden in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Six years at the yearly Rent of forty Pounds & paid Parish Taxes due for the same and after quitting the said House he never gained any or. Settlement And further says that the said John Haynes the Son never gained any or. Settlement than as afsad. either by serving any App Apprenticeship Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired yearly servant for one year

Sworn the 24th. day of Octr. 1783  Jno Barnfather G Mercer

Mary Gayels
