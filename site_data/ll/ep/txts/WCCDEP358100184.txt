Middx to wit Thos. Dagnall aged about 50 years on his Oath says he Intermarried with his Wife Margarett at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields in the year 1764 and by whom he has two Children living namely Mary Ann Aged about 12 years now Provided for and Sarah Aged about 8 years Says before his Internarriage to his said Wife and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Lea a Corn Chandler in Goswell Street in the Parish of St. Luke Old Street in the County of Middx upwds. of two years at the yearly Wages of £10. besides Diet & Lodging hath quitted his said Service upwds. of 20 years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year paying Taxes or or.wise howsoever

Sworn this 19th. day of Febry. 1784 D Walker Philip Dyot

Thomas Dagnall
