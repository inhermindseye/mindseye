St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St George the Martyr Middx  Children  to wit Mary Smith aged 39 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her present late Husband Samuel Smith at the parish Church of St. Martin's fields on or about the 8th day of November 1786 (by Banns) that subsequent thereto her said Husband died Rented and Resided in an Apartment at Mr Griffiths's House in Fishers Street in the parish of St George the Martyr  Queen Square in the County of Middlesex for three Months and upwards at the Annual Rent of £13 or thirteen Guineas that he quitted the same in or about November 1791 that he never afterwards Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief that her said Husband died on or about the 5th day of Nove December 1792 and that She hath not since been Married or obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right Saith that she hath two Children by her said Husband Namely Samuel born the 24th. day of March 1787 and and Lucy Hannah born on or about the 24th. November 1789 and that she is distressed and unable to maintain herself and Children without Assistance and in that they are become

chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 3d day of May 1793 before Us W Addington Mitchell

Mary Smith 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
