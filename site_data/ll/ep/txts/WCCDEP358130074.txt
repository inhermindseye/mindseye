Middx  Sarah Blaker aged about 36 Years upon Oath says She is is the Widow of Peter Blaker who dyed on or about the fifteenth day of October 1751 Says her said late Husband Served three Years and upwards the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. John Gorgett a Fanstick maker in Drum Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex Says her said Husband in his life time nor She since his Death never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the Day of Octor. 1755 before}
