Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Swaine aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she this Examinant lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Carey an Optician in the Strand for the space of Two years at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that She hath quitted said Service about Two years and four Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said parish That one John Rowe who did lodge at No. 5 Craven Buildings by Trade a Journeyman Carpenter and who now works at the new buildings the bottom of Fleet Street is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which She is now pregnant and that the said John Rowe is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1797  J Hunter

her Jane Swaine Mark
