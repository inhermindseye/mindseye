St Clement Danes June 28th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Sarah Demaine  singlewoman aged 39 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. William at the Wine Vaults in Clare Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for a year and upwards at the annl. Wages of £6 that she quitted the same in or about years January 1792 and hath not since obtained Any other legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 28th day of June 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Demaine's Mark

Witness Garrett
