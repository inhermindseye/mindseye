Westmr. to wit Joseph Jepson Aged about Thirty two years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Eight years ago and by whom he hath Two Children now living Named Joseph Aged Six years and John Aged about Four years Says he Served 7 Years Apprenticeship with one Roger Bowchier a Button maker in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House or paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of September 1772 before me} R. Butler

Joseph Jepson
