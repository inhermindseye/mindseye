Middx  Burgess Fergus  wido . of Andw. Fergus who dyed abt. 4 years ago upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the sd. Andw. Fergus at the Fleet London abt. 24 years in that the sd. Andw. Fergus was a Scotchman & She come give any Account of his Settlement And that before her sd. intermarriage & whilst a Singleman she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Daniel Howell at the Sun Tavern on the South Side of St Pauls in the parish of St Gregory by St Paul London at the yearly Wages of £4.10.0 or £5 (she cannot remember which) for the Space of Eighteen months & upwards and has not Since She quitted her sd. Service rented pounds by the Year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn march 12th. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Burgess Fergus
