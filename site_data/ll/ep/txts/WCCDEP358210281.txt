Westmr.  Catharine Mc.donald Aged about Thirty seven Years, Upon Oath says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Fitzgerald who kept the Queen's head in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Victualler , for One Year and Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds for the First Year, and four Pounds for the second Year, and after she quitted said Service never Rented House, Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 1st. day of June 1768 , Before} Fran Cary

The Mark of Cathre Mcdonald
