Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Mary Jones Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Calvert a Staple keeper in Theobalds Row in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr Queen Square in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of 8 Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Two Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlmt. And further says she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Brassatt in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the name of James and is become Chargable to the same and that one Thos. Rawlins late a Cookman to one Mrs. Benham who lodged in Red Lyon Square is the Father thereof what is become of him she does not know otherwise than she has been informe that he went of for Portsmouth

Sworn this 7th. day of Novr. 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

Mary Jones her Mark
