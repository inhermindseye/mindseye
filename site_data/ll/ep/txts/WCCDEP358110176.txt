Middx  Richd. Blake upon Oath Saith he intermarried with his Wife Elizabeth Blake on the 23d. Febry 1718 in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that he was bound Apprentice for Seven years to Mr- Ashford by a Peruke maker with whom he lived upwards of 3 yrs. and them upon Mr. Ashfordbys leaving of Trade: this Examinant was assigned over to Mr Radley a Perukemaker with whom he Served out the Remainder of his time opposite Norfolk Street in the Strand in the aforesd. Parish of St Clement Danes and that he never Rented ten Pounds a Year or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year And further that a Certificate Dated 15th. Novr. 1718 (a Copy whereof this Examt. now her with him) was granted by the Ch. wds. & Oversrs. of St Clemt Danes afsd. to the Parish of Walthamstow in Essex acknowledging him & his Wife Inhabitants legally Settled on St Clemt Danes

Sworn Feb. 14th 1753 before} Sam Pechell

Richard Blake
