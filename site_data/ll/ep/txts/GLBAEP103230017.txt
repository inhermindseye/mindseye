Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Gradley  wife of Daniel Gradley maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her in Places to her unknown That he has informed her and which She believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to James Harrison of the Parish of st George in the County of Middx  Ropemaker and after Serving a Small Part of his Apprenticeship there this Deponts sd husband with his sd Master removed into the Hamlet of Mile End Old Town in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the sd County Where he Served the remainder of his said Apprenticeship That her said husband Since his Apprenticeship never rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship to her Knowledge or belief That She hath a child named Mary aged five Years the lawful issue of this Deponent by her sd his band to whom She was lawfully Married That about four years ago She this Deponent had relief from the overseers of the Poor of the said Hamlet of Mile End old Town & the Settlement of her husband & herself was then Admitted by them to be there and this Depont has Seen her husbands Indres & believes his Master is now living

Sworn this - 10. day of February 1787 before me  R Davies  Wm. Blackmore

The Mark of Eleanor Gradsen
