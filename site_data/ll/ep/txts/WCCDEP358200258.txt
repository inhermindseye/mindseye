Middx  Hannah Hawkins aged about 28 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with John Sadd in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middlesex for One Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds & ten Shillings hath quitted said Service about three Years & hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year and further upon her Voluntary Oath says that sometime in the Month of November 1763 she was delivered of a Male bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Robertson in Newtoner's Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the said County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized Thomas and is now living and become chargeable to the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes , says that a Black whose Name was Thomas did beget the said Male Bastard Child on her body he having had carnal knowledge of her body sevl. times at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Thomas and or other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 15th: of April 1765 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Hannah Hawkins
