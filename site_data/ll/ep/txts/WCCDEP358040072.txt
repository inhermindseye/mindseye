Westmr.  Ann Gower on Behalf of Sarah Waring aged upwds. of 13 Years upon Oath Saith the sd. Sarah Waring is the Daughter of Robt. Waring deced by Eliz. his Wife Says the sd. Robt. Waring Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. George Leeson a Millmaker in Joyner Street in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Places to her knowledge or beleif Says the sd. Sarah Waring never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Septr. 30th. 1747 before} Wm. Jones

Ann Core
