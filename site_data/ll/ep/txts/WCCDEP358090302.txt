Middx to Wit Mary Evans aged about Twenty one Years on her Oath says she never was Married rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year nor has she gained any Settlement whatever Otherwise than by birth which was by the Llahan in the County of Carmathen And further on her Oath says that on the 1st. of March Instant she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the name of Sarah and is now Chargeable thereto she being Poor & unable to maintain the same and that one John Maddox an Attorney in Stanhope Street is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her late Master's House in New Inn  the latter End of June last  particular  now remember and at Sevl. different times after at One of Which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of Which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Maddox and no Other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 27th. day of March 1782 before R Butler Sampn Wright

her Mary Evans Mark
