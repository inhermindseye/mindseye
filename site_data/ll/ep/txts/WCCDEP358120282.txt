Middx  Geo. Green upon Oath saith he was married to his wife Elizabeth at the Fleet London about 14 Years ago and that he was bound Apprentice to and Served the first four Years or thereabouts of his Apprentiship with Mr Warner a Butcher in Clare Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlx that his master then broke and this Examinant never was assigned over to any person nor ever rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Febry 15th. 1755 before}
