Westmr.  Eleanor Withers wife of Wm. Withers lodging in James Street in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the  Liberty of Westmr. upon Oath saith that the Infant Child that was advertized in the daily Advertizer of the 8th. Instant & therein said to be Dropped in Hollywell Street in the parish of St Clement Danes was born in the house where in this Examinant lodges in George  James street in the Parish of St. George  Hanover Square aforesaid and That Thomas Daniel the father or reputed father of sd. Child is a Carpenter & works for Mr Phillips a Carpenter in Brook Street Grose on or Square & that Sarah Daniel the mother of the sd. Infant Child hath absconded and this Examinant doth not know where she is & that from sevl. Circumstances this examinant hath reason to belive the sd. Child was dropped with the Privity & knowledge of the sd. Thoms. Daniel

Sworn Sept. 10th 1753 before W Fielding

The Mark of Elanr. Withers
