Middx  Elizth. Mallard aged abt. Twenty five Years Upon Oath says she was never Married & never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. for One Year says her father Richd. Mallard Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Jas. Bend a printer in the precinct of White Fryers London says her sd. father never rented Ten pounds a year pd. parish Taxes or Served any parochiall Office

Sworn the 11th. day of Decr. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Mallard
