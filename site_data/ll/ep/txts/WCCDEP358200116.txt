Middx  Richard Phillips upon Oath Says he was Bound Apprentice to Joseph Slaymaker Blacksmith in Barnaby Street in the parish of St. John Southwark in the County of Surry Says that he this Examinant with Hannah his Wife were by Order under the Hands and Seals of Barthw. Hammond and Francis Bedwell Esqrs. two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx (One of the Quorum) Bearing Date the Eigth Day of May One Thousand Seven Hundred and Fifty four Removed from the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County of Middx to the said parish of St. John Southwark

Sworn the 28th of June 1764 Before} Thos. Kynaston  Benjamin Cox

Richard Phillips

Upon Mr Hays Complt J. W
