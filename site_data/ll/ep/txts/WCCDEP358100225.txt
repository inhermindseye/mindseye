Middx  Sarah Watts aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Lady Blackwood in Somerset Street  Portman Square in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months at the Yearly Wages of £8. hath quitted her said Service about Two Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement says she hath One Child named Hugh born a Bastard aged about five Months in Oxford Road in the said Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone which hath gained no other Settlement than by Birth.

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1784 . before  Sampr Wright Thos. Gordon

her Sarah Watts Mark
