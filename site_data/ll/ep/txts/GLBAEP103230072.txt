Middlesex ss}  Drucilla Baker maketh oath that her Pale Father Thomas Baker deced as She has been informed & believes was heretofore duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Nevil Meale  Bricklayer in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself to her Knowledge or belief and that She was born in Cartwright Street in the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 26. day of Septemr 1787 before}

The Mark of Drucilla Baker
