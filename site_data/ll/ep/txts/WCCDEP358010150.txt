Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Taylor aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith that about 17 years ago she Intermarried with Jas. Taylor her late Husband (who has been dead about 7 years) at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields that since her said late Husbands Death she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Lawrie in Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 13 Months at the yearly Wages of £12..12..0 that she hath quitted said Service about 2 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. Octr. 1787 R Butler

her Mary Taylor mark
