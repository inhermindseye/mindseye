Middx  Ann Mandefield Aged about 40 Years Upon Oath Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Waterhouse a Haberdasher in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upwards of Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath quitted her said Service about Sixteen Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 29th Day of Octr. 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Mandefield

Upon Mr Robt Williams Complt J W.
