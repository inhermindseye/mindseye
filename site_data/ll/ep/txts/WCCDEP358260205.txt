Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charlotte Scruby aged about 44 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Catherine Smith in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Seven years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Four Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1797 before me } W Carter

her Charlotte Scruby mark
