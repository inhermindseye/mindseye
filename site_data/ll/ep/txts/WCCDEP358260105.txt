Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Hassall aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That on or about the 21st- day of October 1773 she was bound an Apprentice by the Officers belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes to one Philip Hassall a Farmer at Edmonton in the Parish of Edmonton in the County of Middx for Seven years or until day of Marriage that she lived with the said Philip Hassall until he died which was about five years after she was so bound Apprentice as aforesaid and this Deponent further Saith that she never was Served over by the said Philip Hassall in his life time or by his Executors or Administrators to any Person

Sworn this Ninth day of Febry. 1796 } N Bond Rusl Clarke
