Middlesex  Ann Day Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with with on Richard Sankey  Esqr . at the Parish of Camberwell in the County of Surry for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds for the first Year and so on till five Pounds a Year hath quitted her said Service about fourteen Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Oath Says that on the 25th. day of Novr. 1765 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Great Almoney in the Parish of St. Margarets Westmr . which hath since been baptized Mary Whitling and that Thos. Whitling who has been Dead this Three Years was the Father of the sd. Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid

Sworn this 26th. day of Janry 1771 before} John Cox  Tho: Kynaston

Ann Day
