Middx  George Stratton aged about 70 Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his present Wife  Ann in the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex about five Years since Says he rented a House in the Strand between the New Church and Pissing Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or thereabouts and lived in the same upwards of six Months and quitted the same upwards of Twenty Years and hath not since Rented a House or paid any Taxes
