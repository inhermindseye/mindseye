Middx  Mary Chassey aged upwards of Sixteen Years upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she was born in the Parish of Elmestery in the County of Somerset where her Father Henry Chassey lived in & rented a house & paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes as this Examinant verily beleives Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 19th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Chassey
