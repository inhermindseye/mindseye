St Clement Danes August 8th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Andrew Holborn below the Bars  (to wit) Mary Ann Elizth Barnicott  Singlewoman aged twenty nine years and upwards upon Oath saith that she never was Married never served any Apprenticeship lived as a Covenant Articled or Yearly Servant or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own right, that She has been informed and verily believes she is the legitimate Daughter of Joseph and Elizabeth Barnicott both deceased that her said Mother Elizabeth after the Death death of her Husband and while she was a Widow Rented and Resided in an House in Norwich Court Fetter Lane in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn below the Bars Bars London for 3 Months & upwards at the annual Rent of Sixteen Pounds or six sixteen Guineas that she died there on or about the 27th day of February 1792 that she this Examinant lived with the her there as part as part of her Family and had never really been seperated from her said Mother during her Life except when she has been out at Work of a day time in the way of her Business

Sworn the 8th day of August 1793 before us N Bond M Addington

Mary Barnicott
