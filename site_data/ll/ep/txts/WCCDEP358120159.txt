Middx  Cathn. Crump upon Oath Saith she is the wife of John Crump to whom she was married at the Fleet London upwds. of 4 Yrs. ago and that her sd. husband he Informed her he lived a yearly Servant with Ld. Thanet for the Space of one Year and upwards (whilst a singleman) and lay the last three months of his said Service over the Stables adjoining to my Lordshouse in North Audley Street in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. (which Information this Examinant believes to be true) and that her sd husband has not since he quitted Ld Thanet Service rented ten Pounds by the year paid Parish taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn July 22d. 1754 : before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cathn Crump
