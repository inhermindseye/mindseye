Westmr.  Thos. Jackson aged about 63 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Ann his Wife at the fleet London about 16 Years ago; Says he Rented a house in Crown Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds Ten Shillings and lived in the same upwards of three Years, Quitted said house in the Year 1735, and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 12th. Novr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Thos. Jackson
