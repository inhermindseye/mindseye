Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Clement aged about 74 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a house in Fountain Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds that he hath quitted said house about Four years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1797 before me  Gs Goodenough

W Clement.
