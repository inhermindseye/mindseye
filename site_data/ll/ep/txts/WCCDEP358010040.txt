Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Susannah Allen aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1773 she Intermarried with Thomas Allen her late Husand at the parish Church of Saint Sepulchre that her said Husband has been dead about 3 years that she hath Three Children living Vizt. Susannah Aged about 7 years Ann aged about 5 years both provided for and Margt. aged about 3 years unprovided for that her said Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Warren a Hair Dresser in Devereux Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or done any Act Matter or thing to the best of this Exts knowledge Information or belief whereby he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of Janry. 1787  Wm Kitchiner

her Susannah Allen mark
