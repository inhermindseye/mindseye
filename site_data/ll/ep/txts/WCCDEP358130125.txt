Middx  Elizabeth Davis aged about 25 years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Thomas Davis and that he said late Husband hath been dead about four Years and further upon her voluntary Oath says that on the fourth day of February last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and which hath since been baptized Thomas & is still living & likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor and unable to maintain the same Says that one Thomas Smith a Merchant who lives in Nevills Court Fetter Lane in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London had carnal knowledge of her Body at a publick House in Holborn (but the Sign She cannot now particularly remember) sometime in the Month of May last at which time he got her with Child of the Male Child she was delivered of as aforesaid and that the said Thomas Smith is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 5th. Day of March 1756 . before}
