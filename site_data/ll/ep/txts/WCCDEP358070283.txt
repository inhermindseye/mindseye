Middx to wit Sarah Miller Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was Married, Rented House or paid Taxes, Says She lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Samuel Edwards a Wine Merchant , in Beaufort Buildings , in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes , in the County of Middlesex, for fourteen Months or thereabouts as the Yearly Wages of Six pounds; hath Quitted her said Service about Three Weeks ago, and hath never since she Quitted the same Rented House, paid Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or any otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement, And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is, or are, likely to be born a Bastard, or Bastards, and to been Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes , (she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when borns) Says that one Simon Warren who now lives a Servant with one Mr. Laddet No 3 in Portman Square , is the father thereof, he having had Carnal knowledge of her body in the said Mr. Edwards House in Beaufort Buildings aforesaid, sometime in the beginning of January last the particular day she does not now remember, and at several different times afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child, or Children, of which she is now pregnant, and that the said Simon Warren and no other person did beget the same, and that he is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 3d day of Augt 1775 before}
