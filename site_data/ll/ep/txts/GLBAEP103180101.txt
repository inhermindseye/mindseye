Middlesex ss}  Betty Mathews the widow of Ephraim Mathews deced maketh oath that divers Years ago & Since her Said husbands death She this Deponent took & rented a house in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the Same That She has not Since been married or lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes elsewhere or done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of December 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Betty Mathews
