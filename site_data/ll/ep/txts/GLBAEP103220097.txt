Middlesex ss}  Catherine Tonge the wife of Richard Tonge maketh oath that her Sd husband is now absent from her in Places to her unknown That about two Years & half ago her Sd husband lived in & rented a house of the Yearly rent of Twelve Guineas in Red mans Row in the Hamlet of Mile End old Town in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the Sd County & resided thereon for about eighteen Months That her Said husband has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement Save that her Sd husband & herself for the benefit of her Childrens health in May last went & resided upon a house at Ilford in Barking Parish in Essex and Continued thereon for about the Space of two Months but was not to Pay any Rent or Taxes for the Same in as much as Such house was & is the Sole Property of her Brother Charles Peter Duffey untill he Shall attain his Age of twenty one Years and that then one fourth Part thereof will become the Property of this Deponents Sd husband in the right of her this Depont. The which house in this Deponts Judgment were it Put into Tenantable repair would not Self for more than One hundred Pounds And this Depont is advised & believes that Such Residence

on Such house at Ilford as aforesd did by no means gain her husband & herself a legal Settlement And this Depont has three Children namely Richard aged about Seven Years Rebecca Aged about five Years & Walter James aged about two Years & four Months the lawful Issue of this Depont by her Sd husband to whom She was lawfully Married

Sworn this. 11. day of December 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

Catherine Tonge
