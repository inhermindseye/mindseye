Middx ss The Examination of Catherine Lambert Singlewoman taken on oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the sd. County of Middx (one whereof is of the Quarum) residing in or Next to the Ra limits of the parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the sd. County this 26 day of November 1754

This Examinant on her oath Saith That about Christmas last was twelve month one Henry Walker who then lived as a servant with Mr William Walker in ye sd Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate did prevail with this Examint. to Suffer him to lye with her at the house of one Mr. Stockbridge known by ye sign of the Ship Alehouse in the sd. Parish and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body several times Whereby she conceived with Child of a Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered at the house of

Mrs Elizabeth Burch in Swan Alley in the said Parish on the sixteenth day of March now last past which sd. Male Bastard Child hath been baptised by the Name of Peter And hath been and still is likely to be Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate this Examinant being Poor & not able to provide for the same And this Examinant farther saith that the sd. Henry Walker is the true and only father of the said Male Bastard Child And that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 26 day of Novr. 1754 before us  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

Katren Lambert
