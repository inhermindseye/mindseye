Middx & Westmr (to wit) Thomas Buck aged thirty five years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he was married to Sarah his first Wife at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex about twelve Years ago, that his said Wife has been dead about seven years  that he has one Child living by his said first Wife Vizt. Thomas aged Eight Years or thereabouts who has never gained any Settlement in his own Right that his said first Wife Sarah died about seven Years ago And this Examinant further Saith that he was married to Sarah his Second and present Wife at the said Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square about seven Years ago by whom he has no Child or Children that his said now Wife is gone away from him and this Examinant does not know where she now lives And further this Examinant on his Oath Saith That  whilst he was a Singleman unmarried, and not having Child or Children he was hired 

was hired to Sir  Gerrard Vannecks who then lived in Shotton Street Piccadilly in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the  County of Middlesex Westminster and County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of 18d. For about three Year, that since he quitted the said Sr. Gerrard Vannecks  service he has neither rented House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of £10. paid any Taxes or done any Act Master or King whereby to gain any subsequent Settlement to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief and that he is Poor and enter and unable to maintain himself & Child

Sworn this 2nd. Janry 1786 before

Tho Buck
