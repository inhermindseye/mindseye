Middx  Margaret Fleetwood aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith She never was Marryed that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Walker a Chair maker in the Parish of St. Brides London upwards of a year at the yearly wages of Four pounds and hath quitted her said Service about four years and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year and further upon her voluntary Oath says that on the 28th. day of January last She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Sutherland a Poulterer in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and which hath since been baptized Samuel & is still living and likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor & unable to maintain the same And that Samuel Hilliard now a Servant to Mr. Wills in Princess Street Rotherhith did beget the said Male Bastard Child on her Body he the said Samuel Hilliard having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinant's Body in  the Month of March in the Year 1753 in the House of Mr. Shirley at the Dover Castle in Lambeth March and at several times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Child She was delivered of as aforesaid And that the said Samuel Hilliard is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 29th. Day of Decr. 1755 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

Margt Fleetwood
