Middx. ss. Richd. Combes aged abt. Sixty Six Years upon Oath says he was bound Apprentice to and served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Richard Doleman a Printer in Swan Court in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & hath been out of his said Apprenticeship abt. forty five Years since which time He hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or Served any Parochial Office

Sworn Novr. 6th. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

Richard Coomb
