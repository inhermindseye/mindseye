Middxss.  Frances Clapham aged abt. 20 Years removed from the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westmr . Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she served five Years Apprentice ship with Mrs. Lister a Millener in the Strand  Opposite Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx And never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 9th. day of October 1760 , before} Fran: Bedwell

Frances Clapham
