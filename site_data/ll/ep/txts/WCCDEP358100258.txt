Middx  Mary Bleechman aged about Twenty five years on her Oath says she never was married rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Drawater in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upwards of four years at the yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born And further says that one Wm. Brown a Servant to her said late Master is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at the said Mr. Drawaters House afsd sometime in the Month of April last and at sevl difft. times aftwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children which she is now pregnant & that the said Wm. Brown & no or. person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 1st. day of Octr. 1784 R Butler

her Mary Bleechman Mark
