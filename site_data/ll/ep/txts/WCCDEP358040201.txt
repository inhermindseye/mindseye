Middx  Thomas Cox upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Elizabeth upwards of Six Years ago at the Fleet London by whom he hath three Children James aged about 5 Years Susannah aged about 3 Years & John aged about one yr. says he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Miller a Shoemaker in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and afterwards lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year & upwards with the Sd. Mr. Miller in the same Places at the Yearly Wages of £10 and that since he left the Sd. Mr. Miller he has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn June 11th 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Thomas Cox
