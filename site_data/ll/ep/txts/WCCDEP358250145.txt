Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Bailey aged about 41 years upon his Oath saith That he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Harrison who kept the York Arms in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Ten Pounds during which time he this Deponent Slept. in the said House That he has quitted said Service about Seven weeks only

Sworn this 31st. 2d. day of Decr. Janry. 1795 } Edw Read

his Wm. Bailey Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
