Middlesex  Eliz; Milkin upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with Paul Milkin the 13th. Day of May 1742 at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband was bound an Apprentice to and Served 7 Yrs. to Saml. Cloves in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry  Lighterman Says he never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servant or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says her sd. Husband is a a Sailor in his Majestys Ship of Warr the Revenge now at Giberalter & hath been on a Cruize in the sd. Ship upwards of three Years Says the last Letter She received from her sd. Husband was on or abt. the 18th. Day of Septr. last & was dated from Port Mahone the 25th Day of August 1745 and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a

Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that one Daniel Field  Wharfinger did begett the said Child or Children on her Body he the sd. Danl. Field being acquainted with this Examinant had Carnall Knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings on the 22d. of Septr. last & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gett her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Danl. Field & no other Parson is the true & reall father thereof

Sworn March 5th 1745 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Eliz Milkin
