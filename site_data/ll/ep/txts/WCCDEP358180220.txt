Middx  Margt: Purnell aged about 47 Years upon her Oath saith she lived a Yearly Servt. with one Mr Stammadine in Hanging Sword Court in the Parish of St. Bridgett vulgarly St. Brides London for the Space of Six Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £4: this Examinant at that time being a Singlewoman without Child or Children Says she hath left her sd. Service near Nine Years & hath not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt: for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt: in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne July 13th: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Margt. Purnell
