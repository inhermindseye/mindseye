Middx  Esther Woolridge aged upwds. of 60 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that above thirty Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Withers an apothecary in St Mary Axe in the Parish of St. Andrew & Undershaft London for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and has not since she Quitted her sd Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn May 8th 1753 . before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Esther Woolridge
