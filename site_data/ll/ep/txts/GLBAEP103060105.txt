Aldgate Middx

Hannah Newbery  Widdow of Andrew Newbery deced Maketh Oath that her Said late husband often told her he Servd his Apprenticeship by Indenture to a Carver the corner of Phenix Alley in long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields

Sworn 28th day of July 1748 before Robert Dennett

the mark of Hannah Newbery
