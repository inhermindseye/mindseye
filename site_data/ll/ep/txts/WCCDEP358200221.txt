Middx  Elizth. Pendlebury Aged about Twenty One Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Collins Pendlebury with whom she Intermarried in the parish Church of St. John the Evangelist in the Liberty of Westmr. about One Year ago Says her Husband is an Apprentice to Jos: Grindell a Trunk Maker in Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. with whom he now lives & has Served upwards of Six Years of his said Apprentiship Says she has One Child by her said Husband Elizth. Aged about Six Weeks.

Sworn the 22d of January 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox  Thos. Kynaston

Elizabeth Pendlebury
