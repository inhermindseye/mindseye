Middx  Rd. Barber upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Kingsmil Evans  Esqr . for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £12 and when in Town constantly lay at his Masters house in Boswell Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx Says he has not since he left his said Service rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn 14th Janry 1750 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Rd. Barber
