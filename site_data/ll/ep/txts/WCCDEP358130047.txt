Middx  John Connell upon Oath says that he is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England That whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with James Poe Esqr . in the Parish of Ballingary in the County of Tipperary in the Kingdom of Ireland above 4 Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds

Sworn the 3d. Day of Septr. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

John Connell
