Saint Clement Danes 7th March 1794.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James Westmr . to wit) Julia Hayman Wife of Richard Hayman aged 62 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was Married to her said Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le Bone in the County of Middlesex on or about the 18th day of December 1774 that subsequent there her said Husband rented and resided in a House in Princes Street  Soho in the Parish of Saint James Westminster in the County of Middlex at the Annual Rent of £30 for about upward of Six Months and quitted the same about 1781 and did not do any Act Matter or Thing that her said Husband since that time never did to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief any act Matter or Thing whereby to gain any subsequent legal Settlement to the

Sworn this 8th Day of March 1794 before us} Rich Ford B: Kennett

Julia Hayman
