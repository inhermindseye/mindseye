Middlesex ss  Margaret Gordon  singlewoman maketh oath that she can give no Account respecting her Parents Settlement save that she has been informed they were Natives of Scotland That she this Deponent was born in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County That she was never bound as apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year in any one Place nor was she ever Married nor did she ever do any act to gain a settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 16th day of April 1785 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Margaret Gordon
