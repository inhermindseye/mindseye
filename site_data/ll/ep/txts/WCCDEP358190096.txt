Middx.  Hannah Stone aged abt. 32 Years ( Removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the fields ) Upon Oath says she is the wife of George Stone with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of of Middx , abt. Six Years ago by whom she has One Child named George aged abt. four Years, Says her said Husband Rented a house in Ship Yard in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and lived therein abt. two Years, hath Quitted same abt. four Years and has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn 28th. Jany. 1761 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Hannah Stone
