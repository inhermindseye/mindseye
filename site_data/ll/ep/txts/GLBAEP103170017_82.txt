Middlesex ss}  Martha Hasseltan the widow of John Hasselton deced maketh oath that divers years ago her Said late Husband rented a house of Ten pounds a year in Cartwright Street in the Parish of St. Botolph wihtout Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in possion there of for Several years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her Said husband did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten pounds a year nor Serve any parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement for himself nor hath She this deponant Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this. 18. of October 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Martha Hasselton
