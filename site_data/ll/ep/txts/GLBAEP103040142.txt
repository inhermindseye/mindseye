Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Tilfer the Widow of John Tilfer deceased maketh Oath that she was Married to her said late Husband at St. Johns Church at Dundee in Scotland about Twenty Seven years ago that her said Husband was born in Scotland and Served his Apprenticeship to a Shipwright there as he has informed her, and never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in England, that she this Deponent about Three years before her said Marriage and when she was a Singlewoman was hired as a Servant by the year to a Mr. Burgess of Richmond under the Hill in the County of Surrey Gentleman at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas & continued in his Service under such hiring upwards of Two years that her said Husband died about Seventeen years ago in St. Kitts Hospital , and that at the time of his Death she lived as a hired Servant by the Year with Sr . Thos. Mills of the Adelphi in the Parish of St. Mary le Strand at the yearly Wages of Twenty Guineas, that she lived in his Service about Four years, But that she had at that time Two Children living the lawful issue by her said late Husband, And further saith that being Poor & unable to provide for herself she has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate Middx for Relief & has been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this Day of Sepr. 1798 before}

Eliz Tilfer
