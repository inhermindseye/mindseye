Middlesex ss}  Thomas Cunningham maketh oath that he well knows one Richard Jennings who is at present resident in the parish of St.Botolph witht aligate in the sd County & continued to his Room by reason of Sickness That the Sd Richd Jennings has informed this Depont & which he believer to be true that divers years ago he was bound Apprentice to a person whose name is unknown to this Depont. in the parish of Deptford in Rent & was afterwards assigned or turned over to one Mr. Dawkins a House James in queen Street in the Parish of St. Mary Rotherhith in the County of Surry where he served the last two years of his Apprenticeship by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards to this Deponts Knowledge or belief Rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or do any act to gain anothr Settlement & this Depont. is the Better induced to believe such information to be time for that some years. ago the Sd Richd. Jennings was deced into the Workhouse of the said parish of St Mary Rotherhith & had relief as being legally settled in the same Parish last mentioned.

Sworn this 11 day of February 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands 

Thos Cunningham
