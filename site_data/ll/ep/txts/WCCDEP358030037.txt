Middx  Mary Miller aged about 59 Yrs. upon her Oath she is the Wido . of George Miller who hath been Dead one Year and upwards Says she intermarried with the said George Miller in the Parish Church of St. Nicholas Coleabby London Says her sd. Husband rented a house in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £20 & lived there in two Yrs. & upwds. & hath left same upwards of Eleven Yrs. Says he never after rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Novr. sd. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Miller
