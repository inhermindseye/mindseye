Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Alice Storey of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this fourteenth day of October one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy Six

Who Saith that about Ten years & five Months ago She this Examinant was lawfully married to one Thomas Storey a Gun Smith Who about three years & eleven Months ago left this Examinant & Went to reside at Kingston in Jamaica  That She this Examinant hath never Seen him nor hath he had any Access to her Person or had any Carnal knowledge of her Body Since he left her as aforesaid She this Examint having ever Since till within the Space of a few Weeks resided at her Fathers house in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the Said County nor doth this Examinant Know or belive nor hath She ever been informed Whether the Said Thomas Storey hath ever been in any part of Great Britain or Ireland Since his departure from this Examinant as aforesd. nor doth She now know Whether he is now dead or living That the last time this Examinant heard from him was by a Letter received from him & dated at Kingston in Jamaica  aforesd in the Month of April one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy four That about Sixteen Months ago & not more one James Fleming a Butcher Who then lodged in the house of this Examinants Father Adam Beale in Little Grays Inn Lane Holborn in the Said County Where this Examinant also then lived did Prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him the Said James Fleming to lie with her and to have

Carnal Knowledge of her Body Whereby She Conceived With Child of a Male Bastard Child of Which She Was delivered in the Workhouse of the Said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate on the twelfth of April last past The Which Child hath been Since baptized & registred by the name of William Storey and is become chargeable to the Said parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate She this Examinant being Poor & not able to provide for the Same That the Said James Fleming is the true & only Father of the Said Child that this Examination is Voluntary & that She hath not been Compelled to be examined by any person or persons Whomsoever

Taken and Signed the day & year above Written before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Alice Storey
