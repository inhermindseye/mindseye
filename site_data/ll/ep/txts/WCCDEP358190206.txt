Middx   Rachel Cowlthired aged about 42 Years upon Oath saith she was never Married That she was bound an Apprentice by an Indenture for Three Years to Mrs. Elizabeth Bouch  Mantua Maker in that part of Burleigh Street which is in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and with her said Mistress there served all her time out which Expired about 23 Years ago says she hath not since rented any House paid Land Tax or Poor Rate or been a hired Yearly Servant for One year

Sworn the 11th: day of Decemr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

Rachel Cowlthired
