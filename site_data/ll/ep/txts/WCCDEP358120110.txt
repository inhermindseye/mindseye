Middx  John Wilkinson a private Centinel in the 2d. Troop of Horse Grenadiers & Andrew Medcalf Shoemaker living in Craven Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. Severally make Oath and first this deponent John Wilkinson maketh Oath that the male Child mentioned in an Advertizement published in the daily Advertizer of the 28th. Inst. & therein said to be dropt in the Ground Staircase of No. (18) in Clements Inn is the Child of Mary Elsam als White and was born about three months ago in the house of Mrs. Baldwin at the Ship in Craven Court in the afsd. Parish of St. Martin in the Fields & baptized John and the above Andrew Medcalf for himself maketh Oath that the sd. Child was the reputed Child of Rt White who as this Examint. believes was married to the above Mary Elsam abt. 18 months ago at ye. Fleet and that the sd. Rt. White has a former wife now living whose Maiden name this dept. believes was Mary Drake and that who is now (together wth. her two Childn. Rt . & Thomas ) maintained & for four Years past has been Supported by the Parish of Reedham near Yarmouth in the County of Suffolk in right of the

sd. Rt. Whites Settlement and as his Wife and Children

Sworn May 29th: 1754 before me Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane

John Wilkinson Andrew Metcalfe
