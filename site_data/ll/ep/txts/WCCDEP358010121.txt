Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Bayzand aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that in the Month of Janry. 1784 she Intermarried with one Jonan. Seddon Bayzand her present Husband (who has deserted her about 2 years at the Parish Church of Portsea in the County of Southampton and by whom she hath one Child living vizt. Harriott Lucy aged about 2 years & a half that her said Husband Rents and lives in a House in Pennington Street in the Parish of Saint George in the East in the County of Middx and he done for these 12 Months past or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £10 and pays the Poors Tax due for the same

Sworn this 24th. day of July 1787  Thos. Gordon Mornant.

her Sarah Bayzand mark
