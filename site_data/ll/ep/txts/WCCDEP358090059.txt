Middx  Judith Graves aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Thomas Gould at The Sign of the Jack of Newberry the Corner of Grubb Street in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of five Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 6th. day of Augt. 1779 before}  John Cox

her Judith Graves  Mark
