Middx to wit Elizth. King Aged about 35 Years on her Oath says She never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servt . with one Wm. Gladman at a Cooks Shop in Barnaby Street in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her sd. Service about 7 Years ago and after quitting her sd. Service she never gained any other Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 2d. day of January 1783 before  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

her Elizth. King Mark
