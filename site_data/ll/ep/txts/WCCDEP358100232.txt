Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Eaton Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year says her Father Richd. Eaton rented a House in Yates's Court for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of £10 or thereabouts hath quitted said House about Twenty Years ago and after quitting some he never gained any other Settlemt. And further says that on the 22d. day of June last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish which hath since been baptized by the name of Elizabeth and is now Chargable to the same And further says that one Henry Aylesby a Staymaker who works at Mr. Boots a Staymaker near Great Turnstile Holbourn and who belongs to a Lottery Clubb at the Horse & Groom in Whetstones Park is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her Mothers Lodgings in Vere Street Clare Markett sometime the beginning of October last and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. And that the sd. Henry Aylesby & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 22 day of July 1784 before  Wm Kitchiner

Mary Eaton her Mark
