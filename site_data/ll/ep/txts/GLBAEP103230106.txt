Middlesex ss}  Frances Miller maketh oath that She was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by the then Parish officers of Saint Saviour in Southwarke in the County of Surry to Michael Allen of the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex  Hatter untill She Should Attain the Age of twenty one years to learn the business of a Mantra maker which his Wifes Dans. then Carried on That after having Served there under the said Indenture for near the Space of three years Differences Arising between this Deponent and her Mistress She was by the Justices Smith And Green discharged from her said Apprenticeship and her Indres. delivered up and Cancelled That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant in anyone Place for the Space of a year or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement Subsequent to her said Apprenticeship

Sworn this. 17. day of January 1788 before  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Frances Miller
