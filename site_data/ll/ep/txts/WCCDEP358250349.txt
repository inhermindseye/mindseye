Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Short aged about 63 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hady in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Seven Years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Nine Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Fifteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of Febry. 1798 before me} 

her Elizth Short Mark

H Day Beadle
