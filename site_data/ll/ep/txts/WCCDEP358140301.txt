Middx  Mary Middleton aged abt. 49 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Wm. Middleton who has been dead abt. Twenty six Years Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children She lived a hired Yearly Servt. with her father in Law Edwd. Middleton a Brewer in Milford Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for five Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. Seventeen Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 13th. Octr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Middleton
