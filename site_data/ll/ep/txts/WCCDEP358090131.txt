Middlesex to wit Joseph Wainwright Aged about forty Two Years on his Oath Says he never was married  beet that he lived with a Person by whom he Eight Seven Children four of whom are now living Vizt M Aged about Eighteen Years born the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West  Sarah Aged about 15 Years and Elizabeth Aged about Twelve Years the Three last of which were born in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which Children are all Provided for except the last And this Examinant further says He rented and lived in a House in Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for One Year or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Nine Pounds and paid parish Taxes due for the same and afterwards this Examinant entered into partnership with one Samuel Oxborne and Rent a House in Hemlock Court in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds Rented same about Two Years and paid Parish Taxes due for same hath quitted said House about Twelve Month ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 21st. day of April 1780 before R Butler

Jos: Wainwright
