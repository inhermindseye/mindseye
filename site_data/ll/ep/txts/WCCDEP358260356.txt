Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Worledge aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Seventh day of June 1794 she Intermarried with Samuel Worledge her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Annes Westmr. Soho by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Thomas aged about Two years and Elizabeth aged about Five  weeks That her said Husband now Rents a Second floor of Mr. Hill No. 3 Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  at the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas and has done so for these Four Months last past

Sworn this 24th. day of Octr. 1798 before me } W Carter Edwd: Naish

her Sarah sw Worledge Mark

Witnss AW Burbidge Beadle
