Middx  Eliz: Nichols aged 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound apprentice by the Church wardens & Overseers of the poor of the Parish of St Mary le Strand to Mrs. Snowden till she should attain the age of 21 Years or marriage and that she lived wth. her sd Mistress (under the Indenture in Middle Street Cloth fair in the parish of St. Barthw the Great London about 5 Years & then her sd Mistress dyed and this Examinant never was assigned over nor ever rented ten pounds by the year pd. parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Decr. 4th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz Nichols
