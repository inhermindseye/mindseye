St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Hannah Tatum  singlewoman  aged 18 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never lived as an hired yearly for a year together under one hiring, never served any Apprenticeship or Rented any House or Apartment of £10. a year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own right to the best of her knowledge or belief And this Examinant further saith that she hath been informed and verily believes she was born in lawful Wedlock saith that her father Rented and resided in an House in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for two years or upwards at a considerable yearly Rent and paid Taxes father same that he Died in the said House and since his Death this Examinant Mother during her Widow head never resided out of the parish or did any act whereby this Examinant might become untilted to any legal settlement otherwise than in the parish of St Clement Danes aforesaid to her knowledge belief

Sworn the 13th day of March 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Hannah Tatham
