Middlesex ss}  Mary Coutanch the Widow of John Coutanch deced maketh oath that her Father Robert Parsons many years ago rented a house of Fourteen pounds a year in the Parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsen in the County of Surry & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & during Such his Residence therein this Deponent lived with him aspart of his Family That this Deponent afterwards married her said late husband and about Twenty Six years ago he rented a house in the Same Parish & was charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement nor hath she this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 13. day of Novr 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes P Rowlands

Mary Coutanch
