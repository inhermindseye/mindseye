Middx.  Rose Brady aged about 30 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Brady who has been dead near Ten Years says she now Rents and lives in a House in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds and hath so done for near two Years And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes says that James Jeffs who now keeps the Ram's head Taphouse in Tooley Street Southwark had carnal knowledge of her body on the 13th. day of February last past and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said James Jeffs and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 27th. day of June 1765 . Before} Saunders Welch

Rose Brady
