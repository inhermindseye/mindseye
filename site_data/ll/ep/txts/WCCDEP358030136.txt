Middx  Mary Bingle upon her Oath saith she was never married & that about four Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Wayte in the Parish of Vaxley in the County of Wilts for the Space of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds & hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Vaxleys Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 24th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Mary Bingle
