Middx  Eleanr. Innys upon Oath Saith she was born at Leverpoole in the County of Lancaster and never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servant and that She can give no Account of the Settlemt. of her father & mother

Sworn Jany. 17th. 1753  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eleanr Innys
