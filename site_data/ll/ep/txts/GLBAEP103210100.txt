Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Brassett  widow of Thomas Brassett deced maketh oath that many years ago her said late husband rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds (known by the name of the Thistle & Crown in the parish of St Mary at Ishington in the said County & during such his residence therein he was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & did not afterwards rent a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten pounds elsewhere or serve any parish office or pay any poors rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That she this Deponent for sometime after her said husbands death lived in and rented the said house and since her leaving the same has not rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten pounds or upwards or paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes elsewhere or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement for herself & that she was lawfully married to her said husband & has not since been married to any other person.

Sworn this 23d day of September 1785 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Elizabeth Brassett
