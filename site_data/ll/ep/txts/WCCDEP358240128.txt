St. Clement Danes July 21st 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  (not Sworn)  (to wit) Thomas Smith Aged Sixty Years upwards Upon Oath saith That he Rented and Resided in a House in Laystall Street in the parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the said County of Middlesex for about Six Months at the Annual Rent of £10 and paid Taxes for the same that he quitted the same about 20 Years ago, and hath never service obtained any legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge Information or belief Saith That he was Married to his late Wife Elizabeth formerly Elizth. Smith  Spinster at the parish Church of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey about 37 Years ago that She Died on a about the 7th Augst. 1790 U that he hath Six Children by his said Wife now living all provided for and that the Youngest is near 21 Years of Ago

Sworn the 21st. July 1792 before Me }

Thos. Smith
