Middlesex ss}  Mary Giddings the widow of John Giddings deced maketh oath That her Said late husband divers years ago was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one Captn Lawson in Burr Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for Several years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement That She this Depont Since her said husbands death has not been married nor  lived as a hired Servant by the year nor done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself That She hath two Children namely Sarah Aged about Nine years & four months & Elizabeth Aged about five years & five months the lawful Issue of this Depont by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married.

Sworn this 13. day of Novemr 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Giddings
