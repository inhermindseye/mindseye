Middx  James Viger aged 57 Yrs. upon his Oath saith that about 11 Yrs. since he lived in & Rented a House in Arundell Street in the Strand in ye. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £36 for the Space of Six Years or thereabouts Says he hath not since he left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge a beleif

Sworn Febry 28th. 1745 before  Wm. Jones

James Viger
