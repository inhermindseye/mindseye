Middlesex ss}  Jane Staple  Singlewoman maketh oath that about Six years ago she this Deponent was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Bristow a Victualler who kept the Publick house known by the Sign of the two Queen's Heads in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Middx and Continued there in Such Service under such hiring for the space of one year & upwards That after such Servitude to wit about four years ago she was hired and lived as a hired Servant at the Wages of five Pounds a year by & with one Mr Yeoman at the Sign of the Bunch of Grapes in Baileys Place in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County and Continued there in such service under such hiring for about the space of five months as she now best remembers When her said Master dying his widow thereupon left the house & Let it to one Mr Caffrey to whom this Deponent hired herself again at the same yearly wages & with whom She Continued in the same house for about the space of eight months That She has not since lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year elsewhere or been lawfully married or done any act to gain a Settlement otherwise than as is above Stated

Sworn this 7 day of November 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Jane Staple
