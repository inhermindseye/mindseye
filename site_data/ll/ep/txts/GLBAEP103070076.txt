Middlesex ss  Thomas Meidel Maketh oath that about fifteen Years ago he Articled himself as a Clerk to Mr Isaac Spratt of the Parish of St. Michael Wood Street in the City of London an Attorney at Law and Served him in that Capacity for about the Space of three Years And this Deponent Saith that he was Never a hired Servant by the Year Nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards Nor Served any Parish offices Nor done any Act whereby to acquire a Settlement Subsequent to his Clerkship at abovesaid

Sworn this 24 day of February 1753 before us  Richd. Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

Thos Meidel
