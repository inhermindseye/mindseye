Middx. and Westmr.  Mary Smart aged abt. 20 years Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that on the 22d. day of June 1758 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Richardson in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx which hath since been Baptized Jane and is still living & likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish says that one Langley Phillips a Musician who lodges at Mr. Goddard's a Shoemaker in Wardens Street Soho did beget the said female Bastard Child on her body the said Langley Phillips and this Examinant living together as Man & Wife and that the said Langley Phillips and no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of April 1760 . before}

The Mark of Mary Smart

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Wilkinson Overseer J: P:
