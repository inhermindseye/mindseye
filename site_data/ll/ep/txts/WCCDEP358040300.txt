Middx Westmr.} 

Ann Lucas aged abt. 31 Years upon Oath saith she never was married rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year and that her father Richd Lucas deceased lived in and the Kings Arms Ale house in Horton Street Clarence in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for a considerable & paid Scot & Lot time and never after he quitted that house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief and that her mother is now a Pensioner in one of the Alms houses of the sd. Parish And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the 12th. Day of December Instant at Nurse Alexanders in Peter Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes aforesd. which has been baptized Richard and is become chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same and that William Johnson a Joiner who worked in King Street Westmr. (and who as she has been Informed and verily believes is since deceased was the reputed father the said William Johnson

having had carnal knowledge of her body One in the month of March last at Mrs. Basons in Stanhope Street where this Examinant then Lodged and to which Place he followed her one Evening from Paternoster Row under Prentence of Seeing her home and by that means became acquainted with her and that the sd. Wm. Johnson and no other Person is the true and real father of the sd. male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd.

Sworn Decr. 29th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones  Samuel Clarke

Ann Lucas
