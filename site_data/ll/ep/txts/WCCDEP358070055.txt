Westmr. to wit William Crue Aged about Fifty Seven Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Jane Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Seven Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children now living Olivia Aged about Six Years and John Aged about Ten Weeks Says he Rented lived in a House at Ratcliffe Cross in the Hamlett of Ratcliffe in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney otherwise Steben-heath in the County of Middlesex for Three Years or thereabouts and paid Parish Taxes for the same at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted the said House up wards of One Year and an half ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of Janry 1773 before Us} Tho. Kynaston

William Crue
