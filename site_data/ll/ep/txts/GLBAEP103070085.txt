Middx ss  Sarah Smith  Widow of Thomas Smith deced Maketh oath that her sd. late husband hath informed her that about Thirty Years ago he lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one Mrs. Cook a Cowkeeper in the Parish of St. John at Hackney in the County of Middx and Continued in Such Service for Several Years and during Such Servitude this Deponts.sd husband was a Single Man And by Means thereof gained a legal Settlemt. there as this Deponent is informed and verily believes And this Deponent Saith that her sd. late husband to her knowledge or belief did not Since Such Service as aforesd. rent a house of Ten Pounds by ye Year or upwards Nor Serve any Parish offices Nor do any other Act to gain any Subsequent Settlemt. And yt She hath a Child Named Sarah aged about Sixteen Years who has Never been apprentice or a hired Servant Nor gained any Settlemt but in right of her sd. father Thomas Smith deced

Sworn this 27 day of July 1753 before us  Richd: Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

Sarah Smith her Mark
