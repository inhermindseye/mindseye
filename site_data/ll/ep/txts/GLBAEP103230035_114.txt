Middlesex ss  Isabella Ms Coy maketh oath that she can give no account respecting her Parente Settlement or either of them Save she has heard them say they were Natives of Scotland That She this Deponent was born in a Certain Place Called the Dark Entry in the Parish of st Botolph witht Aldgate in the said County as she has been informed by her Mother and which She believes to be true That some years go she was married to one John Robertson but can give no account respecting his Settlement That she this Deponent Sometime after such marriage had was credibly informed & which she believes to be true that she said Robertson had before been married to anothr Woman who at the time this Deponent was married was then living whereby this Deponents marriage (as she is advised became Void in Law That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant in any one Place for a year nor ever rented a house or Lodging of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 16. day of april 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Isabella Mc Coy
