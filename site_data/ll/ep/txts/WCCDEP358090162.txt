Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Hammond Aged about Fifty Years on her Oath says She Intermarried to her late Husband James Hammond at the Parish Church of Saint George the Martyr in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry about Twenty Years ago and by whom She hath Two Children unprovided for Namely John and Elizabeth Two Twins aged upwards of Ten Years says her said late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Haydon a Grocer in Blackmay Street in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr aforesaid in the County of Surry and after quitting his said Masters Service he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husband Death

Sworn this 27th. Day of October 1780 before  R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

her Ann Hammond mark
