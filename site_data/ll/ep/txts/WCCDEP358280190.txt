Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Poldon aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Polden her late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Anns in the City of York on the 8th. day of Augt. 1784 that her said Husband has been dead about Three years and that she has no Child living by her said late Husband that her said late Husband Served Three year Apprenticeship to Mr. Lambert in Saint Clements Church Yard that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the the 18th. day of September now last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Store Street Hospital that one Benjamin Livesey a Journeyman Hair Dresser who lived with one Mr. Hardy a Hair Dresser in the City of York is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of December last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Benjamin Livesey is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 11th. day of Novr. 1788 } R Butler

Ann Poldon

Ann Polden lives at a Coalshed in Little Russell St . Drury Lane

Benjamin Livesey is a Short Thick Man fair Complexion has Light Hair Tied behind
