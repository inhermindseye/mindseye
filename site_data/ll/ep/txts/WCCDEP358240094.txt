St Clement Danes April 25th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields  (to wit) Janet Scott Aged 36 years and upwards Wife of Walker Scott Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband at the West Kirk in Edinburgh in Scotland about ten years ago that her said Husband hath since their said Marriage Rented and lived in an House in Little Moor's Yard in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County for about Six Years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and paid all the Rates assessed on the Tenant for the same that they quitted that House about Nine Months ago and that her said Husband hath not since acquired any Subsequent legal Settlement by any act or means whatsoever to this Deponents knowledge or belief And this Deponent further saith she hath four Children by her said Husband now living namely Elizabeth aged near Thirteen Years, Robert aged ten years and upwards John Aged Eight years and upwards, and Jane aged two years and upwards, that her said Husband left this Deponent about on the 10th day of April Instant to go to Manchester for Work, and this Deponent hath not since heard from, or any thing of, him And that She is unable to support herself and family any longer without Assistance

Sworn the 25th day of April 1792 before us  Sampn. Wright M Addington

Janet Scott
