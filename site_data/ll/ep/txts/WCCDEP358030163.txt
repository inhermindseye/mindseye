Middx  Mary Whelldon  Wido . of Thomas Whelldon upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Thomas Whelldon at the Parish Church of St Clement Danes about 24 years since Says her sd. late Husband before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Cannall a Butcher in Vare Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes aforesd. for the Space of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds Says her sd. late husband never after he last sd. Mr. Cannells service hired Yearly Servt. whilst singleman rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she hath not since his Death Rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 16th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Whelldon
