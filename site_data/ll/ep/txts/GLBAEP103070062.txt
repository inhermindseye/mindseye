Middx ss.  Stephen Howard Maketh oath that about Sixty three Years ago he was bound apprentice by Indre to Mr. John Danson a Taylor who then lived in Billiter Lane in the Parish of st. Catherine Cree Church in the City of London with whom he lived Nine Years in ye sd. Parish & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement And this Depont. Saith that he never was a hired Servant by ye Year Nor Never rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards Nor Served any Parish offices Nor paid any taxes Whereby to gain a Settlement

Sworn the 13 day of May 1752 before us  Richd. Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

his Stephen Howard Mark
