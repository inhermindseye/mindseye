Middlesex ss}  Patrick Flanagan maketh oath That divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Edward Flanagan a Taylor in Globeyard in the Parish of St Magnus by London Bridge in the City of London at Ceratin Wages & Continued in such Service under such hiring for the Space of five years That he has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this. 22. day of February 1780 before  Jno Sherwood  Jasper Clarke

Patrick Flanagan
