Middx.  Susannah Varding aged abt. 40 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Robt. Varding who has been dead abt. a fortnight by whom she has 4 Children (to wit) Sarah aged abt. 6 years Robt. aged abt. 4 Yrs. Jane aged abt. two Years and Mary aged abt. Seven Months. says her said husband lived in and Rented a house in Bell Lane in the Parish of St. Mary le Bone in the County of Middx and paid to the poor there and hath quitted said house abt. five Yrs. and never after Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid parish Taxes.

Sworn this 29th. Sepr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Susa. Varding

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Stratton Ovr . J: P:
