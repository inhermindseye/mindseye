Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Patten aged about 47 Years Upon Oath says, she is the wife of James Patten with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about three Months ago, says the said James Patten rented a house in Houghton Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds lived therein about five Years and hath quitted same about Five Years and hath not since Rented house or paid Taxes as this Examinant hath been informed by her said husband and which information she verily believes to be true; says her said husband hath deserted her, whereby she is become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn the 7th, day of Novr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

Sarah Patten
