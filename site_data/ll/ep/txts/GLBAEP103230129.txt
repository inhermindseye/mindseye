Middlesex ss  Judith Hamilton the widow of Francis Hamilton deced maketh oath that her said late husband heretofore rented a house at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds in Cartwright Square in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same and died there legally Settled That She has not Since her said husband & death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She was lawfully married to her said husband That She hath a Child named Eleanor Aged about Ten years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband

Sworn this. 3. day of April 1788 before  R Davies

Judith Hamilton
