Westmr to wit Sarah Radbourn Aged about Thirty Seven Years On her Oath says she never was Married, Rented House, was Apprentice, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year; Says her father Thomas Radbourn Rented and lived in a House in Angel Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex at the Yearly rent of fifteen Pounds and paid Taxes for the same and further says her said father has been dead about Twenty Two Years and that since that time this Examinant has never gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th day of Jany 1775 Before W Addington John Goodchild

The Mark of Sarah Radbourn
