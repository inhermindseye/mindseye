Middlesex & Westminster  Ann Simpson Aged upwards of Eighteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she was bound out an Apprentice by the Govrs. & Guardians of the Foundling Hospital to one Mr. Boulton a Hair Dresser in the Strand untill she shod. Attain her Age of 21 Years or Day of Marriage and lived with her sd. Master upwards of half a Year and was afterwards Assigned Over to one Mr. Mackfarlane a Bookbinder in Ship Yard in the parish of St. Clement Danes and has lived with him under such Assignment for about Seven Years and hath never gained any other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that One William Lewis a Hair Dresser who lodges in her said Masters House is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in June or July last in her said Masters House but the Particular Month or days she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as aforesaid and that the said William Lewis is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 18th. day of February 1777 before M Addington

her Ann Simpson Mark
