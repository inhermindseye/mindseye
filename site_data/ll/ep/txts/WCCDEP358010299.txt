Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Scannan aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that She Intermarried with Wm. Scannan her late Husband in a Room at Cork by a Roman Catholic Priest about 26 years ago that her said Husband has been dead about 14 Months That her said Husband was an Irishman and never gained any Settlement in England and That she this Examinant is an Irishwoman and never gained any Settlement in England That she has one Child living by her said late husband vizt. Ann aged about Eight years born at the house of a Mr. Dunstan in Exeter Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1789 D Walker Rupt: Clarke

her Ann Scannan Mark
