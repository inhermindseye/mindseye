Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Eleanor Ellsop of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this 28th day of December 1776 who Saith that on the fifteenth day of November last in the Workhouse belonging to the Said parish She this Examinant was delivered of a Female Bastard Child And that the Said Bastard Child hath been Since Captired & registred by the name of Margaret Ellsop and is become Chargeable to the Said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate this Examinant being poor & not able to provide for the Same And that one George Booge late of the Same parish Mariner & now belonging to the Ship Elizabeth on a Voyage to Halifax is the Father of the Said Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Eleanor Ellsop
