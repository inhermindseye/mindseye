Middx  The Examination of Jane Lilley  Wido . of Thos. Lilley in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx taken the Day of June 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. Eighteen Months ago her sd. late Husband lived in & rented a House by Clemts. Inn Gate in the Parish of St. Clemts- Danes aforesd. of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds for the Space of six Yrs- or thereabts- & further saith that her sd. Husband in his Life Time nor she since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt - for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
