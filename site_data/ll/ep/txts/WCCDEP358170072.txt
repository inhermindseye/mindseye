Middx The Examination of Mary Hargess  Singlewoman Lodging in ye. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken ye. 23d. Day of June 1741 Who upon her Oath saith yt. about nine Months ago she lived with one Mr. Rock of the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Hosier for the space of four Years at ye. Yearly Wages of £5 And hath not since yt. time Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife, And this Examint. further upon her Voluntary Oath saith yt. on the 29th. Day of Decr. last she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child at one Mrs. Roberts in Old Boswell Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes wch hath since been Baptized by the Name of Edward and yt. one Mr. Edward Trevor  Jeweller did begett the said Child on her Body he at such time be being a lodger in the said Mr. Rocks House there became acquainted with this Examint. and had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of March 1740 & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child wch she was Delivered of as aforesd. and that he the sd. Edwd. Trevor & no other Person is the true and reall Father thereof

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Samuel Clarke

The Mark of Mary Hargess
