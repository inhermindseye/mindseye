Westmr.  Alice Darcy aged about 37 Years Widow of Michael Darcy with whom She intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Margaret Westminster upwards of nine Years ago Says her said Husband rented and lived in a House in Newcastle Court Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of 20 Pounds a year and upwards lived therein about four Years and quitted same about Eight years ago and hath not since Intermarried with any other Person nor lived a hired yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 1st. day of October 1767 before} Paul Vaillant

Alice Darcy
