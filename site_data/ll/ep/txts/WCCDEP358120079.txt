Middx  Mary Eliz Wilson upon Oath saith she is the wido. of Major Wilson who dyed about two Years ago and by whom she has two Children (viz) Esther aged upwds. of 7 Yrs. and John aged upwds. of four Years and that she was informed by her sd. late husband he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to John Stone Junr. a Baker and after having lived with him about a year was assigned over to John Stone Sevl. with whom he Served the remaining five Years of his sd. apprentiship at Edmonton in the County of Middx which Information this Examinant verily believes and that neither her sd. late husband in his life as he further informed her nor has this Examint. Since his Decease rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn March 23d. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Willson
