Middlesex ss}  Mary Gregory the widow of George Gregory deced & formerly the widow of Kendrick Rayndors deced and John Andrews Beadle of the Parish of St Botolph Aldgate Middlx Severally make oath & Say & first the Said Mary Gregory for herself Saith That She Cannot give any Account respecting the Settlements of her Said late husbands or of either of them or of their respecting Parents or any or either of them That She this Depont. was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant in anyone place for the Space of a year to the best of her Knowledge & now recollection nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She has Acquired by her Several Marriages as aforesd That she hath three Children namely Mary aged about Seven Years & five Months Christopher Aged about four Years & Thomas Aged about one year & Seven Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband George Gregory to whom She this Deponent was lawfully married and the Said Deponent John Andrews for himself Saith that he has been lately informed by the Sd other Deponent Gregory's Father Thomas Parttrick & Which information the Said Deponent Andrews believes to be true That divers years ago & When his Said Daughter Mary Gregory was a Singlewoman and part of his Family he the Said Thomas Parttrick rented a house in the Parish of Woolwich in the County of Kent and was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And that he has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 30: day of March 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Mary Gregory John Andrews
