Saint Clement Danes July 22nd. 1786

Middlesex (to wit) John Card of the Little Almonry near Deans Said Westminster maketh Oath & Saith That he Married Elizabeth Card his present Wife about 14 Years ago at St: Mayard Westminster , That about the Year 1776 he went to live at Deptford & kept the Sign of the 2 Brewers in Giffin Street in the Parish of St. Paul Deptford that he lived there 1 year and a Qua & paid 30th. a Year for the said House and Taxes. That his wife Elizabeth Card and Two Childrens were in the Workhouse of St Paul deptford , That

Two Children died & were buried at Deptford, That since he quitted Giffen Street he has not done any Act Matter or Thing to the best of his Knowledge or belief whereby he may have gained a Settlement.

Sworn before us this day of July 1786  Jno Barnfather  James Croft

John Card
