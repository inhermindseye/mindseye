Middx  Frances Perchard Aged about Fifty Eight Years upon Oath Says She is the Widow of Daniel Perchard who has been dead about Twelve Years Says her said Husband Rented a house in Fountain Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Fifteen Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and then her said Husband died Says that upon her said Husbands Death She quitted Same and hath not Since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 9th Day of Janry 1769 Before me} Tho: Lane

Frances Perchard
