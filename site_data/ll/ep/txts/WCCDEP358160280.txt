Middx  The Examination of Elizth Fozard Passed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . by Order bearing date ye. 14th- lnstt- to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Who upon her Oath saith yt. she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that in December 1739 She was hired to one Mr. Fred: Bourne in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Taylor & lived with him as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds And this Examinant further on her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Childn- which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when born And further saith that one John Weight  Taylor did begett the said Children Childn. on her Body he at that time working as a Journey man with the sd. Mr. Bourn & Lodging in the same House there become accquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body in the Month of Novr. last & at several other times one of which Time she got her with the Bastard Child or Childn. which She is now pregnant with & that he & no other Person is the true & rent Father thereof

Sworn this 16th. Day of March 1740 before} Jas: Gronous
