Westmr.  Martha Pridham aged about 82 Years Upon Oath says that being a Widow she Rented a House in King Street  Golden Square in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds ten Shillings lived therein upwards of Eighteen Months and quitted same about Sixteen Years ago, and hath not since Rented the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 22d. May 1766 . Before} Paul Vaillant  Tho. Kynaston

Martha Pridham
