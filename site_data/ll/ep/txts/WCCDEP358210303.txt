Westmr.  Ann Price Aged about Forty three Years, Upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Price (who is gone from her) with whom she intermarried at May fair Chapel about Sixteen Years ago, by whom she hath one Child now with her (to wit) Ann aged about Five Years; Says her said Husband before his intermarriage with this Examinant lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bertles at the Ship Tavern in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds, diet and Lodging, quitted said Service about two Years ago, and hath not since Rented House, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 25th. day of July 1768 , before} J W

Ann Price
