Middx  Sarah Blasdell  Wido . of Jonathan Blasdell upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Jonathan Blasdell the 12th. Day of Octr. upwards of 8 Yrs. since at the Fleet London Says the sd. Jonathan Blasdell hath been dead upwds. of 6 Yrs. Yrs. & that he was born in the Village of Golgant in Elhill in the Parish of Cockram in the County of Lancaster as this Examint. hath been Informed & beleives Says that neither her sd. late Husband in his life nor she since his Death ever rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 21st. Janry 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sarah Blasdell
