Middx  Margt Jones upon Oath saith she is the wife of Chas Jones to whom she was married at the Fleet London abt. 17 Yrs. ago that her sd husband absconds from her and she knows not where he is and that he informed this Examint. her Served his apprentiship with a Shoemaker in High Street in the Parish of Saint Michael at Bristol in the County of Somerset  and never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year which Information this Examinant believes to be true

Sworn 6th. March 1755 before}
