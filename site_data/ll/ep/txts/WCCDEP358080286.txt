Middx to wit Hannah Dearham Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thos. Dearham who is pressed and gone to sea with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Allhallow Staining in the City of London about Six years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living Namely Rebecca Aged about Sixteen Months Says that her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in the Butcher Row East Smithfield in the Parish Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex for Six Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds besides Taxes hath quitted the said House about five years ago since which time her said Husband never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 17th. day of September 1778 before  James Croft  Jno. Barnfather

Hannah Dearham
