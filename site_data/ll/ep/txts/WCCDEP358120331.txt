Middx  Mary Hows aged abt. 32 years upon Oath saith she is the wife of John Hows (gone from her) to whom she was married in Woodford Church in Essex the 23d. April 1758 & by whom she has 1 Child (viz) Wm. aged abt. 15 months and that her sd. husband informed her he Served his apprentiship with his father Thomas Hows at Walthamstow in Essex and that his sd. father was a Certificate person from the parish of St. Olave Southwark to the parish of Walthamstow afsd. and further that her sd. husband never rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year to this Examints. knowledge or belief

Sworn April 30th 1755 before} Thos Lane

Mary Howes
