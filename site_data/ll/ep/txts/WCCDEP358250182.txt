Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Grimmit aged about 44 years upon her Oath saith that about Twenty years ago she Intermarried with William Grimmit her late husband who has been dead about nine years at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn by whom She hath one Child living namely Margaret aged about Nine years That her Husband Served his Apprenticeship to one Mrs. Clowder a Pocket Book maker in Baldwins Gardens in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex that he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1795  G Goodenough N Bond

Elizabeth Grimmitt
