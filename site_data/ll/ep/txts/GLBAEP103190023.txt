Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Brown of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Second-day of March 1780 who upon oath saith that on the Fourteenth day of January now last past at the Workhouse in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County aforesaid she the said Sarah Brown was delivered of a Female-Bastard Child which hath since been baptized by the name of Hannah Brown And that the said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that Thomas Evans now or late of Crutched Fryars in the City of London  Merchant is the Father of the said child

Taken & Signed the day & year abovewritten before me  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Sarah Brown
