Westmr.  Ann Blackwood aged abt. 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that by Indenture dated the 2d. Septr. 1738 she was bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex to Mary the Wife of Thomas Gillmore  Cordwainer till she should Attain the Age of Twenty one Years or Marriage and lived with the sd. Mary Gillmore under the sd. Indenture upwards of 40 Days in Leather Lane in the Liberty of Saffron hill  Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex that her master & mistress afterwards absconded from here she was never assigned over to any other Person rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 25th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Blackwood
