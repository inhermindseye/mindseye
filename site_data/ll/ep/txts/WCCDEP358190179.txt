Middx  Mary Lowther aged about 59 Years Lodging in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of John Lowther who has been dead about Six Years to whom she was Married at the Fleet London about 37 Years ago That he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Moses Allbrook a Waterman at Essex Stairs in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid which Expired about a Year before his intermarriage with this Examinant That he never Rented any House paid Land Tax or Poor Rate or was a hired Yearly Servant to her knowledge and also Saith She this Examt. hath not kept House paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate or been a hired Servant since the Death of her said Husband

Sworn the 15th. day of Septemr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Lowther
