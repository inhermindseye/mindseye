Middx  Susanah Marks aged 34 Years & upwards upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of John Marks who hath been Dead 2 Yrs. & upwards that the sd. John Marks was bound an Apprentice to & Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Hutchinson a Taylor in Cary Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Taylor that since his sd. Apprentiship the sd. John Marks in his Life time or this Examinant
