Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to St. C. D. to wit William Young aged about Thirty years on his oath says he never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Says he Served Five years Apprenticeship to Christopher and David Steady Glass Cutters in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That Since he quitted same he has done no Act matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 2d. day of February 1785 }

his William Young Mark
