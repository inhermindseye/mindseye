Westmr.  Jas. Anderson on behalf of Laetitia Collis upon Oath saith he has been informed by the sd. Laetitia Collins that she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Quelch at a Seed Shop at the Bridge foot in the Parish of St. Magnus by London Bridge London for three years or thereabouts & has not since She quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn April 24th. 1752 before} Tho Lane

James Anderson
