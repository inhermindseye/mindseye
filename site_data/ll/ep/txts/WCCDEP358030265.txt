Middx  Mary Bridgwood  Wido . of Thos. Bridgwood upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late husbd. in the Parish Church of Carswell in the County of Stafford abt. 40 Years since Says he hath been dead abt. 12 Yrs. Says the sd. Thos. Bridgwood lived in and Rented a house in the sd. parish of Carswell at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Shillings for the Space of five Years & paid his Share to the poors Rate of the sd. parish Says her sd. late husband since he left sd. house nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Yr paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 24th. Novr 1746 before  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Mary Bridgwood
