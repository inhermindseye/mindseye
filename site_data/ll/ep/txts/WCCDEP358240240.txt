Militia Man's Wife St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  (to wit) Elizth Jackson Wife of William Jackson now in the Wes East Regiment of Middlesex Militia Upon Oath saith She is aged 26 years and upwards that about 8 years ago (being then single) she was Married in the Church belonging to the parish of Elstry in the County of Middlesex to Willm. Jackson who as she verily believes (having no reason to think the contrary) was at that time a Widower and had no Wife living And this Deponent further saith that her said Husband William Jackson was duly sworn and enrolled to serve as a Substitute for James Chapman of Sadlers Wells in the parish of St James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex in the East Regiment of Middlesex Militia and hath now serves therein which Regiment is now embodied and called out into actual Service And this Deponent Upon her Oath further Saith that ever since the said Regiment hath been called out into actual Service she hath resided where she still dwells at the House of Mr. Potter at at in Clement's Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex And that she hath ever since the 28th day of January last being the time the said Regiment was called she hath four three Children by her said Husband living and dwelling with her who was also with this Examinant Namely Mary Ann aged Six years &

upwards Ann aged 2 years on the 2d. June last William born on the 9th April 1792 And that She is in indigent circumstances and unable to maintain herself and Children and hath been so ever since her said Husband left her

Sworn the 27th day of March 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Jackson 's Mark
