Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit George Garth aged about 35 years upon his Oath saith That he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Richards in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Five Guineas that he hath quitted said Service about Fifteen years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Novr. 1798 before me } Saml. Edward

his George Garth Mark

Witnss Day
