St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster }  St C D  to wit Geo Smith aged 56 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was Married to Ann Jones  Spinster at the parish Church of St Dunstan Stepney on or about the 24th. day of April in the year of our Lord 1768 by Banns that he hath no Children by his said Wife that he hath been informed and verily believes knows not for certainty whether she is living or Dead saith that he Rented and Resided in a House in Clare Street in the parish of St Clement Danes for about three years & three three Quarters at the Annual Rent of £50 that he quitted that about fourteen or fifteen Years ago th and hath not Since obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 18th day of May 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Georg Smith George Smith 's Mark

Witness Garrett
