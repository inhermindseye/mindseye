Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Curry aged about 18 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly servant for one year or ever Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year That she was born in Ireland And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 12th. day of April Instant she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child (which hath been Baptized by the Name of Dennis ) at the house of Mrs. Ginty in Nags head Court  Drury Lane That one Matthew Delaney a servant at the Constitution in Drury Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid.

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1790 } R Butler

her Mary Curry Mark
