Middx 

Eliza: Brooks aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that neither this Examinant nor her late father Willm Brooks deceased ever rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or was an Aprentice or Yearly Servant Says her Grand father William Brooks lived in and rented a house near the Lamb Inn behind St Clemts. Church in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex as she has been Informed and beleives but at what Rent or how long he kept it this Examinant knows not

Sworn April 8th. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Eliza Brooks
