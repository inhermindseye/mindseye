Middx  Geo. Fisher upon Oath saith he rented a house in Castle Street Oxford Road in the Parish of St Mary le bone in the County of Middlx of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwds. lived therein about 3 years and hath left the sd house about five years, since which he has not rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Janry 10th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

Georg Fisher
