Middx  born at Leeds in Yorkshire }  Ann her Mother  Hannah Naylor aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath saith she never was married Says her late father Joseph Naylor rented a house  near Essex Stairs in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middlx for sevl. Years & quitted the sd. house abt. 27 Years ago says she (this Examinant) never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn March 30th. 1750 before}
