Middx  Hester Shevellier Aged about fifty Nine Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Dennis shevellier at the Parish of Saint Mary Redclift in the City of Bristol  about Twenty Eight Years ago and by whom she hath on Child living Named Dennis aged about Twenty five Years now Provided for says her said late Husband was an Irishman and never gained a settlement in England says before her Intermarriage with her said late Husband she lived a hired Yearly servant with one Mr. Timothy Davis a Hott Presser in Castle Street Castle Precinct  Bristol upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds and can give no other or further Account of her Settlement then as aforesaid

Sworn this 27th day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

her Hester Shevellier Mark
