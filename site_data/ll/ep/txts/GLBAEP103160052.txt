Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Phillips  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers year ago She was duly bound Apprentice to & Served Six years of such Apprenticeship with Catherine Pitt in Oliver Row in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the said County Boxmaker & then her Said Mistress died That she this Deponent has not since lived as an Apprentice or as a hired Servant for a year or been married or done any Act to gain a settlement since the death of her said Mistress deced

Sworn this 26. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C Smith  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of  Elizabeth Phillips
