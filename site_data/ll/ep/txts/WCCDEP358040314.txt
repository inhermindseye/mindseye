Middx  Eliz Watson upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Wm. Watson by whom she hath 4 Children Bridget aged abt. 9 Years Wm. aged abt. 6 Years & John Thomas aged about 5 months and that her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Chapman a Breeches maker at Salisbury in the County of Wilts and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office or lived with any Person as a Yearly Servant and that she has not since his Decease gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 30th. Janry 1748 before}
