Middlesex ss}  Sarah Ulance the widow of Robert Ulance deced whose maiden name was Sarah Lincoln maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents deced or of her Said late husband deced or Any or either of them That about Twenty five Years Ago She this Deponent (as she has been informed by her late mother & which She believes to be true) was born in the Hamlet of Mile End old Town in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the Sd County & Baptized in Stepney Church That She this Deponent was never bound an Apprentice That about thirteen Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages with Mr Wm Jones a Victualler in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County but Whether She Continued in Such Service for A Year tis at Present a Doubt her Sd Master who is Still living having declared that She did not live with him So long to the best of his now recollection And She this Deponent hath not before or since living with the Said Mr Jones lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a settlement for herself Save What She Acquired by Marriage with her Sd late husband

Sworn this 3d. day of December 1785 before R Davies N Blackmore

The Mark of Sarah Ulance
