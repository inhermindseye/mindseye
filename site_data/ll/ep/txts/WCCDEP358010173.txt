Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Ryman aged upwards of 50 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Robt. Ryman her late Husband (who has been dead about 6 Months) at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields that her said late Husband was Book keeper and Porter to Mr. Pryke Bradley at the Peacock Inn Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the yearly Rent wages of £10 for 30 years & upwards

Sworn this 15th. day of Decr. 1787 R Buller

her Ann Ryman Mark
