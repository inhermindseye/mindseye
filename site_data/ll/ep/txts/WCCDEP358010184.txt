Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Sale aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that about 19 years ago she Intermarried with George Sane her late Husband (who has been dead about 7 years) at the Parish Church of Saint George Bloomsbury that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in great wild Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of £33 that he quitted said House about 15 Months before his Death during which time he never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes nor hath this Ext. since her said late Husbands Death Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 22d. day of Janry. 1788 D Walker Rapt: Clarke

Jane Sale
