Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Esther Harrold aged about 52 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she Rented and lived in a house in Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years Months at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds That she hath quitted said House upwards of Twelve Months since which time she hath not as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of Octr. 1798 before me } W Carter

her Esther Harrold Mark

Witnss AW Burbidge Beadle
