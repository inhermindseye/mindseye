Middx.  Ann Griffen upon her Oath saith that She is a Singlewoman & was never married & that abt. Fourteen Months ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Jona: Sisson  Mathematical Instrument Maker at the Corner of Beauford Buildings for the Space of 2 Yrs.. at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the space of one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beliefe

Sworne the 10th. Sepr. 1742 before}
