Westmr.  Mary Jennison aged upwds. of 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that her Father Ralph Jennison deced lived in and rented a house next Door to the Ship Tavern in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for sevll. Years & there dyed Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 14th. 1747 before Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Jennison
