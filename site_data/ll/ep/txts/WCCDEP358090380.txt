Middx to wit Mary Chapman Aged about about 40 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or Tenemt. of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Hughes a Lamp Lighter in Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Seventeen Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her sd. Service about Eleven years ago & hath not since she quitted the same ever lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained any other Settlemt.

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Chapman Mark
