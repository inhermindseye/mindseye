Middlesex ss} Sarah Fox the wife of Nathaniel Fox maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her & detained in Prison That he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he Served the Remainder of his apprenticeship being about Three Years & half with Mr Smith a Taylor in Hatton Garden Liberty in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the sd County & to her knowledge or belief has not Since done any Act to gain anr Settlement That She was lawfully Married to her sd husband by whom She has five Children now living namely John Aged about Twelve Years Frances aged about Nine Years & half Nathaniel Aged about five Years Thomas Peter aged about Three Years & Seven Months & William Aged about four Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her sd husband

Sworn this. 22. day of April 1780 before Jasper Clarke Jno Sherwood

Sarah Fox
