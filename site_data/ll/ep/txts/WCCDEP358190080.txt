Middx.  Jane Talbot aged abt. 28 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Wm. Talbot who has been dead about three Months; with whom she intermarried in the parish Church of St. Mark in the City of Dublin about 13 Years ago, and by whom she has 5 Childn . Margt. aged 10 Years Cathne. aged 8 Years John George aged 6 Years Valentine aged 3 Years and Aeneas Henry aged One Year and a half says her said husband  Rented a house in Exeter Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for five Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds & Rented same till the time of his Death

Sworn Janry. 5th. 1761 . before } Fran: Bedwell

Jane Talbot
