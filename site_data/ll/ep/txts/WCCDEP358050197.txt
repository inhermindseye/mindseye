Westmr.  Eliz: Watkins Wife of Wm. Watkins upon Oath saith she Intermarryed with the sd. Wm. Watkins at Woolverhampton Church in Staffordshire by whom she hath one Child William aged about fourteen months and that her said husband Served five Years of his Apprentiship with his father John Watkins at Langatock near Benavel in the County of Monmouth Taylor that at the End of the 5 Years the sd. John Watkyns dyed and this Examinants husband never was Assigned over or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 10th. 1749 before}
