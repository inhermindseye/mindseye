Middx.  Willm. Skinner aged abt. 30 Years a Soldier in the first Regt. of Foot Guards lodging at Mr Barrow's in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx Upon Oath says he has two Children by Sarah his deceased Wife (Vizt.) Hannah aged abt. Six Years and George aged abt. four Years; Says he Rented and lived in a house in Green dragon Court in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry for two Years at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds and hath Quitted same abt. Nine Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn 16th. Sepr. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Saunder Welch

William Skinner .

Upon Mr Ponten's Complt. J P
