bought that for £4.4.0. to wit Elizth: Holland aged 18 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That about 4 years ago she went to live with Mr. Rider the Broker at Woolwich in Kent as a hired yearly Servant she Stay'd with him 2 years at the yearly Wages of £3..10..0 that since that Time she has not done any Act matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere that she never was married That on the 16th. day of March last she was delivered of a male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Bocock in Angel Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which was baptized by the name of John Robert Leggett That Robert Leggett late a Lichtenant in Militia now living at the White Hart in Church Lane London Street in the Father of the said Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Depts Body sometime in the Month of May 1784 and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child and that he and no one else is the father of the said Child and that she is Poor and unable to maintain the same

Sworn this 10th. day of November 1785 before  Sampr Wright

her Elizabeth Holland Mark
