Middx  Jane Bethel upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Richd. Bethel who dyed abt. 12 years since and that she intermarried with the sd. Richd. Bethel abt. twenty years ago in the parish Church of St Brides London that her sd. late husband rented a house in St Clements Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Fifteen pounds and Lived therein about one Year and that after he Quitted the sd House neither her sd. late husbd. nor this Examinant since his Decease have rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 6th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Jane Bethel
