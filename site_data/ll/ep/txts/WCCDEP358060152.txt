Middx  Mercy Clus upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Jno. Clus deceased who dyed abt. 23 yrs. since & that her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr John Burrowston  Brush maker in Bull Street in the parish of St. Philip at Birmingham in the County Straford of Warwick & never afterwards nor has this Examinant since his Decease gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 19th. Augt 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mercy Clus
