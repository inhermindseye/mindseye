St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } (to wit) Elizabeth Broughton Aged twenty one years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Cook the Silk Dyer facing Essex Street in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex & Liberty of of Westminster for about Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages or Seven Guineas that she quitted the same about Eighteen Months ago, & Since hath obtained no other legal settlement And this Deponent further saith upon her Voluntary Oath that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes And that Thomas Caery otherwise Carey now or late a footman to Mr Cocker in Nassau Street Soho is the only real & true father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge this Deponents Body sometime in April Last & at several times since and having begotten the same thereon And the Deponent further upon her said Voluntary Oath Saith that other person save & beside the said Thomas Caery otherwise Carey is the has ever had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body

Sworn the 12th day of Decr 1791 before me} R Butler

Elizabeth Broughton
