Middx  Eleonor Blunt Aged about 22 Years Singlewoman upon Oath Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Sert . with Mr. Palmer in the Parish of Spackley in the County of Worcester One Year at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service at Michmas 1764 & hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 30th. of Octr. 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eleonor Blunt
