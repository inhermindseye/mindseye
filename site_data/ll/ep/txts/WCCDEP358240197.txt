St Clement Danes 26th. Janry. 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Martha Pilgrim , aged 30 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Henwood in Saint Clement Church yard in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster  County of Middlesex for upwards of two years or upwards at the Annual Wages of £5 that she quitted that Service abt. thirteen years ago and that not since lived as an hired yearly Servant for twelve month together and afterwards lived as an hired yearly servant at Mrs. Strut's in Clare Street with in the same parish for about a year and a quarter at the Annual Wages of £4 for the first Quarter & £5 for the Remainder that She quitted that Service about Nine or ten years ago & hath not Since obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge Information or belief

Sworn the 26th. day of January 1793 before Me } D Walker
