Middx. & Westmr.} to wit Ann Brinner aged about Fifty Eight years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly servant with Mr. Pollard a Taylor in D evereux Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Sixteen Months at the yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said service Upwards of Twenty years ago since which time she hath never gained any other settlement And further says that in the year 1765 she became Acquainted with One Hugh Davis a Plane maker on Newington Causeway in the Parish of Saint mary Newington in the County of Surry but knew nothing of the settlement of the said Hugh Davis and further says that in the year 1768 she was bros. to bed of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Bates on Newington Causeway aforesaid which was soon afterwards baptized by the name of Elizabeth & is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes this Examinant being passed with said Child for Nurture in the year 1769 And further that her said Child never gained any other settlement then by Birth

Sworn this 9th. day of April 1781 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Ann Brinner  Mark
