Middlesex ss.  Ann Ross the Widow of John Ross decd. maketh Oath that She was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shorditch sometime in the Month of October 1786 that her said late Husband was a Native of Scotland & never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in England, that She this Deponent was born in Ireland & before She quitted Ireland She was hired as a Servant by the year with Captn . Thos. Huntley of Paul Street in the City of Cork  Mariner at Certain Yearly Wages that She continued in his Service there about Sixteen Months, when he came from thence to England & Rented a House of Twenty Four Pounds a year or thereabouts in London Street in the Hamlet of Ratcliff in the Parish of St. Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex that She continued in Mr. Huntley's Service with One of his Children about Five Months in Ireland after Mr. Huntley had quitted that place, when he paid her passage to England, & She continued in his Service under the said hiring at his House in London Street aforesaid about Seven Months, that She has not since She quitted his Service lived as a hired Servt. by the year in any other Parish or Place for the Space of One year together or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. except her Marriage as aforesd. that she has a Child named John aged about Ten years the lawful issue by her said late Husbd. And further saith that being Poor & unable to provide for herself & Child She has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of St. Botolph without Aldgate for Relief & has been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 10th Day of Feby 1798 before  Dan Williams R Davies

her Ann Ross Mark
