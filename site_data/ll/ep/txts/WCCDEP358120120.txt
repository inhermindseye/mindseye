Middx  Ann Cawthorne on behalf of Eliz Johnson an Infant aged Eleven Years the Daughter of Thomas Johnson by Elizth . his Wife both deceased upon Oath saith the sd. Thos. Johnson Informed this Examinant he Served his time with Mr Barnes a Cutler at Shefield in the County of York and never rented ten pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn June 12th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Ann Cawthorn
