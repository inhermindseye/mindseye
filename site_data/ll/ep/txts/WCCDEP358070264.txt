Middlesex to wit Sarah Mc Cloud Aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. John Colles in Cumberland Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds, hath Quitted her said Service about a month ago and hath not since she Quitted the same ever done any act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement and further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born says that one John Alexander a Journeyman Smith who now works at a master Smiths in Coleman Street London had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Masters house some time in July 1774 but the particular day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which time or times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that he said John Alexander and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 30th day of May 1775 before M Addington

The Mark of Sarah Mc Cloud
