Middx  The Examination of Mary Dixon  Widow of Wm. Dixon Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken ye. 22d. Day of Novr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. abt. 19 Years ago she Intermarried with ye. abovesd. Wm. Dixon & that her sd. late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Exaiant & whilst he was a Single Man lived a yearly hired Servt . with Sr. Peter King in Norfolk Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of 2 years at the yearly Wages of £7. And this Exaiant further saith that her sd. Husband never after he left his sd. Service  neither has she this Exaiant since his Death Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath one Child which is in the Charity School but being Poor & Lame is not able to maintaine herselfe & Child

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Mary dixson
