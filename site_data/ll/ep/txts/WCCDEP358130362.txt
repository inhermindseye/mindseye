Middx.  Eliz: Constable aged abt. 40 years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Saml. Constable (now at Sea) with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London abt. Eight Years ago says her sd. husband was born in the Parish of Osperth in the County of Oxford

Sworn the 9th of June 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Constable
