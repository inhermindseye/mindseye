Middlesex & Westminster }  Mary Holles Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of William Holles who has been Dead about 5 Years and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Ann Soho in the Liberty of Westminster about 12 Years ago Says that her said Husband served Seven Years apprenticeship with one Richard Freeman Peruke Maker in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither has this Examinant since her said Husband's Death ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 20th: day of June 1770 before me.} R. Butler

Mary Holles
