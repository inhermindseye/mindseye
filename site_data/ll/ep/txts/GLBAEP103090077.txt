Middlesex ss  Sarah Twynam the wife of Joseph Twynam (who is now on a Voyage to the East Indies as she believes) maketh oath That on or about the twenty eighth day of February one thousand Seven hundred and Sixty one She this Depont. was married to her said husband at St. Andrews Church in Plymouth in the County of Devon That She & her husband resided together for a Month at Plymouth and then he left her & went on Board his Majesties Ship Shrewsbury That the Said Ship thereupon Set Sail for the Streights but returned again in about Seven Weeks by reason of her Springing a Leake & Continued in the Sound for four Days and then Sailed again for Gibralter as this Depont. has been informed and believes That her said Husband Continued absent from her & had no Access to her Person for the Space of Twenty Six Months -or thereabouts During the which time one John Wisedell then a Mariner belonging to his Majesties Ship the Intrepid (& now a Waterman resident in the Parish of St Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry ) had Carnal knowledge of this Deponts. Body Whereby She Conceived With Child of a Male Child of Which She was delivered in or about the Month of December one thousand Seven hundred and Sixty one in the Parish of Cornwell in the Town of Millbrook in the said County of Devon Which hath since been Baptized by the name of John Wisedell & is likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate in the County of Middx And this Depont. farther Saith that the Said John Wisedell now resident at Lambeth aforesd. did get her with Child of the said Male Child during the absence and Non Access of her said husband as aforesaid.

Sworn this day of 1767 before}

Sarah Twynam
