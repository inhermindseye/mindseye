Middx  John Marshal aged about 70 Years upon Oath saith That he lived in and rented a House (known by the Name of Lyons Inn Coffeehouse ) in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of One year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds Quitted the Same upwards of Ten Years and hath not since rented Ten pounds a year paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate That he was Married to his present Wife  Esther (aged upwards of Sixty Years) at the Parish Church of Ramsbury in the County of Wilts upwards of Forty Years ago.

Sworn the 1st. day of July 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

Jno: Marshall
