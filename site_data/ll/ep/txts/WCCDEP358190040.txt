Middx.  Ann Cook aged about 27 years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Jonas Cook ( gone from her ) by whom she hath one Child  Thos. aged upwards of three years says her said husband served his Apprenticeship of Seven years to Mr. John Murphet a Taylor at Heckmanwike in the Parish of Bursell in the County of York & never Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year as her said husband informed her & as she verily believes

Sworn this 5th. day of Decr. 1757. before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Cook
