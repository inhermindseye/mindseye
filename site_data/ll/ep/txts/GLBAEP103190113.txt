Middlesex ss}  Charlotte Henslow the wife of Thomas Henslow maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship Conquestadore in his Majesties Service That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Davis a Carpenter in the Parish of St Mary in the City of Bath & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband.

Sworn this. 27. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Charlotte Henslow
