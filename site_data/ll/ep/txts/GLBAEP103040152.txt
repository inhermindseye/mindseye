Middlesex ss.  Elizabeth Seamark  Singlewoman maketh Oath & saith that she was born at Chatham in the County of Kent that she never was bound as an Apprentice, never lived as a hired Servant with any Person or Persons under one hiring for the Space of One Year, nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right that She has heard her late Father say he Served his Apprenticeship to a Millwright in the Parish of troud in the said County of Kent, but to whom in particular this Deponent Cannot give any information, that her said late Father never Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement, that she is now Pregnant With one or More Child or Children Which Child, or Children is or are likely to be born as Bastard or Bastards & that Robt. Wells now or late of Mutton Hill near Hatton Garden in the County of Middlx  Bricklayer is the Father of the said Child or Children

Sworn this 13 Day of Decr. 1798 before H: Reynell R Davies

Elizabeth Seamark
