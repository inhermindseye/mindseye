Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Hennycan  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 8th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about Six Years ago she lived as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Singer in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes  Mercer for the space of one Year at the Yearly Wages of £5 And this Examint. further saith yt. she hath not since yt. time Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she is now Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before}
