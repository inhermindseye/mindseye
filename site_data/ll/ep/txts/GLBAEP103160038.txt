Middlesex ss}  George Davidson maketh oath That Fourteen years ago he rented a house of the yearly Rent of Seven Pounds in Brown Bear Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & during his residence there he was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Said house & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid Any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes elsewhere or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a wife named Ann to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 18 day of April 1776 before  Geo C Smith

The Mark of George Davidson
