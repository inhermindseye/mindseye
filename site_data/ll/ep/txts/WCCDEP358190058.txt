Middx.  Mary Allegre aged abt. 42 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Augustin Alegre who has been dead abt. Four Years Says her said husband was a Native of Spain and never gained a Settlement in England and that at the time of her intermarriage with the said Augustin Alegre she was the Widow of Nathl. Westen Radford a Woolen Draper who Rented and lived in a house in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two Years at the Yearly Rent of £20 or thereabouts and quitted said house abt. the Year 1738 and never after in his life time nor she since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn Octr. 15th. 1760 before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Alegre
