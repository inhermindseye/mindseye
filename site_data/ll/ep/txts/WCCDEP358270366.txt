Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary England aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Adlington in Cook Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about One Month And this Deponent further upon her voluntary Oath Saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastard and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Charles Jenkins who lives with works for one Mr. Holland a Fleecy Hosiery Manufacturer at Godalming in the County of Surry is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Charles Jenkins is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 4th. day of June 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Mary England Mark

Man never Apprehended

Child born in the House 25th. day of Augt. 1798 named Martha England

Sent to Enfield  1st. Decr. 1798 .
