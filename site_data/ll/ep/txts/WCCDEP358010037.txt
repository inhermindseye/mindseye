Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Jane Davis aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Wm. Davis her present Husband at the parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn abo 3 Months ago that her said Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Pouch a: Jeweller in Aldersgate Street in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldersgate  in the City of London that after he was out of his said application he has done no Act to gaint a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Exts. Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 23rd. day of Decr. 1786 D Walker R Butler

Jane Davis
