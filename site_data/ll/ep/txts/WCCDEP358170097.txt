Middx  Joseph Cooper upon his Oath saith that abt- Six Years ago he lived in & rented a House in Clare Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds for the Space of Twenty five Years & upwards that he hath not since he left the sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 27th- Day of July 1741 before} W Hayton

Joseph Cooper
