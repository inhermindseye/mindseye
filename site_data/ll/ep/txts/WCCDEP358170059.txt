Middx The Examination of Elizth. Stevens  Singlewoman passed from the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in ye. Parish of St. Andw. Holbourn in the County of Middx by Order dated ye- 23d. Day of April 1741 to the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx taken the Day of May 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. Twenty Years ago she lived with one Mr. Blythman in Searle Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes for the Space of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & further saith that since she left her sd. Service she lived with her Uncle  Thos. Stevens in the Town of Monmouth for the Space of one Year & upwards & had for her sd. Service Meat Drink Washing & Lodging & some Money & further saith that after she left her sd. Service she lived with Mr. Dupee her Brother in Law in Conduit Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square for the Space of Ten Years or Thereabts- & had for such her sd. Service Meat Drink Washing Lodging Cloaths & some Money & hath left the same abt. a Year & a half & further upon her Oath saith that she never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
