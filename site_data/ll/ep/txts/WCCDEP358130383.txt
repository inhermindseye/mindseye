Middx  Eliz: Lediard aged abt. 26 yrs. upon Oath says she never was married says she lived hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Ford a Fanmaker one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. one years says the last three Months or there abts. of her said Service the sd. Mr. Ford lived in Leaden hall Street over against the India Coffee house in the Parish of St. Catherine Creechurch  andrew. widow half London

Sworn the 16th of July 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham  Fran: Bedwell

Resworn the 18th of July 1757 before} Theodr: Sydenham  Saunders Welch

The Mark of Eliz: Lediard
