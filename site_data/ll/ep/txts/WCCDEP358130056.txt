Middx  Grace Coles Aged about 53 Years upon Oath Says She is the Widow of Thomas Coles who dyed about Ten Years since Says he Served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. John Hoare a Carpenter in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and further says She never Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year since the Death of her said late Husband

Sworn the 29th. Day of September 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

Grace Coles
