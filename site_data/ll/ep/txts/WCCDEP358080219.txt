Middlesex to wit Elizth- Hudson aged about 55 Years on her Oath says she is the We - of William Hudson with whom she Intermarried at the parish Church of St- Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - and who has been Dead about twenty Years ago and by whom she has no Children now living Says her said husband Rented and lived in a house in St- Clements Inn Passage for two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of thirty Pounds hath quitted the same upwards of thirty Years ago since which time her said husband never gained any other subseqt- Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said husbands Death

Sworn this 25th- Day of February 1778 before  Abrm Baley

her Elizth- Hudson Mark
