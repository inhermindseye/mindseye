St. Clemt. Danes

passd. with her 2 Childn. to Woolwich  Sarah Bayer aged 27 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was  Married to Andrew Bayer her lathe Husband at the Parish Church of Woolwich in the County of Middlesex Kent  upwards of five Years ago that she has 2 Children living by her said Husband Vizt. Mary aged 2 Years or thereabouts and Joseph aged 12 Weeks or thereabouts That her said Husband was a Stand or of Ireland and never gained any Settlement in England to the best of this Examinant Knowledge or belief that her said Husband departed this Life in the Month of May last and this Examinant further saith that before her Intermarriage with the said Andrew Bayer she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Alexander Forrestor at Woolwich for upwds. of 2 years at the yearly Wages of £8 that she quitted her said service at the time of her Marriage and this Examinant saith she hath not done any act to the best of her Knowledge to gain a best subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 15 day of Dect. 1785 before us  Sampr Wright J Addington

her Sarah Bayer Mark
