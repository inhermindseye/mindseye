Middx  Sarah Haydon Aged about 25 Years on her Oath says she Never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Wilson a Ship Chandler at Shadwell Dock in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell in the County of Middlesex for Eleven Months or Upwards but received Twelve Months Wages that this Examinant was Passed from Saint Mary Lambeth to Saint Paul Shadwell ; Saint Paul Shadwell Appealed and on hearing the Appeal the same was dismissed and she settled in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell and this Examinant further Says that she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 19 day of Jany 1779 before G Mercer Jno. Machin

Sarah Haydon
