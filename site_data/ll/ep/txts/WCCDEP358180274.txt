Middx  Easter Davis upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was never married and that she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born & that one William Blackitt a Watch Chaser did begett the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her Body the sd. William Blackitt lodging at Mr. Gearys at the White Lyon in Claremarkett where this Examinant lived at Servant there he became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of July last & at several other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Wm. Blackitt & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne Janry 14th. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

The mark of Easter Davis
