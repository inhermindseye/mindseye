Middx  The Exaicon of Anne the wife of Humphry Tozers taken the 31th. day of Decr. 1739 who upon her Oath Saith that on the 26th. day of March 1735 She Intermarryed with the above named Humphry Tozers and that before her Intermarryage with him he lived in and Rented a House in Ram Alley in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West in the City of London of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for the Space of One Year & upwards And this Exaiant Further Saith that he hath not since he left the sd. House Rented Ten Pounds a Yeare paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her Husband is gon from her & She being Poor & Sick is not able to Provide for herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above monconed before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

The mark of Anne Tozers
