Middx  Jonathan Willson aged about 46 Years upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven years Apprenticeshipp with one Mr. Catherine Hill at the Bell in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx . that since his said Apprenticeship he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 2d. Novr. 1742 before} Tho Lane

Jonathan Wilson
