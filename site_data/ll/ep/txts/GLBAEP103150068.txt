Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Weaver  Single woman of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this First day of November 1775

Who Saith That She is now with Child and that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard & to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate And that one Thomas Daley of the said parish is the Father of the Said Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above Written}
