Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Richard Jude aged about 43 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 19th.. day of May 1779 he Intermarried with Ann his present Wife according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England at the Parish Church of Shannon in Cork That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in New Bedford Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds and upwards and paid Taxes due for the same That he hath quitted said House about Two years since which time s he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief s he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Novr. 1797 before us N Bond G Kinnard

Richard Jude
