Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit Elizth Stannenot Aged 17 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she was bound by Indrer by Indres of Apprenticeship to Mrs. Elizth Crisp at No. 73 in Jermyn Street in the parish of St. James Westminster with whom she Regularly served as an Apprentice for four Years and then (being about 4 Months ago) left her said Mistress Service that since she hath not done any Act or thing wtsoever (whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or bele belief) she might have gained any other legal Settlement And that she is Poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 9th. May 1791 before Us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Elizth. Stannenot 's Mark
