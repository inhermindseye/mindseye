Aldgate Middx

John Richardson one of the Watchmen belonging to the Custom: house Maketh Oath that he hath been Informd that his late Father John Richardson was born and brought up in the Parish of st. James in the City and Liberty of Westminster where his Father was a Housekeeper and livd in Peter Street and keept a Cook Shop And this Deponant never heard that his late Father was ever an Apprenticer or hired Servant by the year or Housekeeper to pay any taxes or ever gaind any other Settlement after his birth hath left a widdow whose name is Elizabeth Richardson

Sworn 6th day of June 1748 before Robert Dennett Richd Ricards

John Richardson
