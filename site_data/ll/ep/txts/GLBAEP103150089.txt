Middlesex ss}  Sarah Watts  Singlewoman maketh oath That about Forty Years ago She was bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Botolph George in the County of Middlesex to one Elizabeth Lawes of Petticoat Lane in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London who Carried on the Business of a Chandler & Served about Four Years of her Apprenticeship there & they this Deponent & her Mistress parted by Consent & her Indres were delivered up for the purpose of being Cancelld That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlemt Subsequent to Such her Apprenticeship.

Sworn this 8th day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith  Wm: Blackmore

The Mark of Sarah Watts
