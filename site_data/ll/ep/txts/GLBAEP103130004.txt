Middlesex ss}  Sarah Blake Single woman maketh oath That by a Pass order under the hands and seals of Burford Camper and John Sherwood  Esqrs . two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Middlesex bearing date the Fifth day of July last Past she this Deponent was removed to the Parish of Saint John Southwarke in the County of Surry being the Place where she this Deponent was last legally Settled by Virtue of her living there as a hired Servant for a year together with her illegitimate Child William Bowyer aged four years & Seven Months born in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex who was directed to go with her for nurture till she age of Seven Years That she this Deponent was thereupon with her said Child received into the Workhouse of the said Parish of St John Southwarke & soon after Carried before two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Surry & Sworn to the birth of her child That she this Deponent was Permitted to stay in this workhouse for about a Fortnight when  the the Parish officers there insisted that she shod. Stay no longer there and thereupon her said child was removed by a Pass Order to the said Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate as to the Place of its Settlement and this Depont. Saith That she never wilfully deserted the said child and the same still Continues in her Custody as a Nurse child to be taken care of by her which she is willing & desireous to do

Sworn this 12th: day of August 1773 before B Camper

The mark of Sarah Blake
