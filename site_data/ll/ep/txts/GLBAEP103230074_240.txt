Middlesex ss}  Catherine Bleasdile the widow of John Bleasdile deced maketh oath that her Said late husband divers Years ago rented a house in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he did not afterwards rent a house lodging or other Tenement of the Yearly rentor Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Serve any Parish office or Pay any Poor Rates or kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath three Children namely Charles aged about eight years William aged about five years and Catherine Aged about three years the lawful issue of this Depont by her Said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this first day of October 1787 before}

The Mark of Catherine Bleasdile
