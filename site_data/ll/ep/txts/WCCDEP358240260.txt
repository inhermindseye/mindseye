St Clement Danes May 18th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Andrew Holborn London  (to wit) Mary Tayler  Widow aged 45 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Robert Tayler at the parish Church of Saint John the Baptist in the City of Gloucester about 27 years ago (by License) that her said Husband died about Eighteen years ago that She hath two Children by her said Husband who both provided for for themselves that she since her said Husband's Decease She this Examinants while she (being a Widow) Rented and Resided in a House then Numbered 80 in Fetter Lane in the parish of St Andrew Holborn below the Bars London for upwards of a year at the Annual Rent of £25 that She quitted that House about 16 or 17 years ago and hath not since been Married or otherwise obtained or become intitled unto any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 18th. day of May 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Mary Tayler 's Mark

Witness Garrett Mr. Phells Manglen
