Middx.  Cathne. Coy aged abt. 30 Yrs. upon her Voluntary Oath says she Never was Married and that in the Month of June 1749. she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Douglas in the Great Piazza Covent Garden in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized Charles John says that one John Mumford a Surgeon who now lives in the City of Worcester did beget the said Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that he the said John Mumford & no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of March 1758 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Cathrin Coy
