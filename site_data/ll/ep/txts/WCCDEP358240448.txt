St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster  Settlement in St. Pancrass }  to wit Elizabeth Evans aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any house Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born in Charlotte Street Rathbone place in the parish of Saint Pancrass in the County of Middlesex where her father kept house for a Number of years

Sworn this 7th day of Novr. 1794 } N Bond Edwd: Naish

Elizab. Evans
