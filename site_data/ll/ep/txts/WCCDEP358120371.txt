Middx  Esther Ellitts upon Oath saith she was married to her husband Thomas Ellitts at the Fleet London upwds. of 5 yrs. ago by whom she has three Child Eliz aged upwds. of 4 years Mary aged upwds. of 2 years & Christian aged 6 months and that she has been Informed & verily believes her sd. husband Served his apprentiship with John Godwin a Sawyer  in St. Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes and never Rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn 26th. June 1755 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Esther Ellitts
