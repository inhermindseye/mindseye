Westmr.  Francis Bishop of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr on behalf of Elizth Spencer an Infant Aged about fourteen Years upon Oath Says that the said Elizth: Spencer was bound Apprentice by the Church Wardens and Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish of St Clement Danes by Indenture Dated the 24th Day of July One thousand Seven hundred and Sixty Eight to Wm Cleaver of the Parish of St John Wapping in the County of Middx Carpenter untill the sd Elizabeth Spencer Shod Attain her full Age of 21 yrs or Marriage According to the Statue in that Case made and provided as by the said Indentures of Apprenticeship relation being thereunto had may more fully and at large Appear And further Says that the said Elizth. Cleaver never was Assigned Over nor ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement to the Knowledge and belief of this Examinant

Sworn this 11th Day of Febry 1769 Before} Pet: Planck J Fielding

Fras. Bishop
