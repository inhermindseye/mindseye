Middx to wit Samuel Eaton Aged about Thirty four years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he was bound an Apprentice and served Seven years Apprenticeship with one John Parker a Jackmaker at Bilston in the Parish of Bilston in the County of Stafford and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he went to live with one Mr. Harper and afterwards to Mr. Holden both of them Buckle Chafemakers at Walsall in the Parish of Walsall in the said County of Stafford and served both his said Masters for Twelve Months each and after quitting his said last mentioned Master service he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Octr. 1778 before M Addington

Samuel Eaton
