Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Mary Roe  singlewoman aged twenty seven years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was Married, that she never lived as an hired yearly servant for one year or did any other Act Matter or thing whereby to gain a Settlement in her own Right that she has been informed and believes that her father and Mother John Roe and Jane Roe who are both deceased did each of them before their Intermarriage live as hired yearly Servants with a Gentleman in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards four Year during which time they were unmarried and without Child or Children And this Examinant further saith that her said Father or Mother did not either of them afterwards gain any other Settlement to this Examinants Knowledge, and that her said Mother died in the Workhouse of Saint Clement Danes and that she is poor and unable to Maintain herself being at Present lane with a bad Leg

Sworn this 11th. day of Decr. 1794 }

Mary Roe
