Westminster to wit Ann Booth Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Lockhead at the White Hart in the Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about one Year or thereabouts and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Robert Withecombe a Journeyman Taylor who lately worked with Mr. Moor a Taylor in Surry Street and who lately lodged at the said Mr. Lockheads House had Carnal knowledge of her body on or about the 17th. day of April in the Evening at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid And that the said Robert Withicombe and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day of Novr. 1771 before me W Kelynge

Ann Booth
