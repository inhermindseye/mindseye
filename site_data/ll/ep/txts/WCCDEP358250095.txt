St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Mary Johnson aged 34 years or thereabts. Upon Oath saith that she was Married to John Johnson at the parish Church of St Margaret's Chatham in the County of Kent by licence abt Eleven years ago that her said Husband after their said Marriage Rented and resided in an House in Drury Lane the corner of Nugs Head Court for two years or upwards at the annual Rent of £60 that they quitted the same about seven years ago and never after during his Life Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to acquire any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief that her said Husband Died about years ago and this Examinant hath never since been Married or otherwise acquired any legal settlement since her said Husbands decease that she hath only one child by him now living namely Joseph aged ten years upwards and that she is unable to support herself and Child without assistance

Sworn the 13th day of March 1793 before me}
