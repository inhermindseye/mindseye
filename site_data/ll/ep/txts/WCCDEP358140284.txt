Middx.  Mary Davis aged about 28 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of David Davis who has been dead abt. four Months by whom she has one Child Elizabeth aged about one Year and a half says her late husband informed her & which information she verily believes to be true that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Fennell a Perukemaker in St. Clements Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and that her said husband Also informed her that he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year nor paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 3d. Septr. 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Davis
