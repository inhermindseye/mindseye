Middlesex ss  Andrew Ray maketh oath that divers Years ago he lived in & rented a house in Mandlins Rents in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at the Yearly Rent of nine pounds & during Such his Residence therein which was for many Years he was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this. 20. day of Janry 1779 before  Jasper Clarkey

Andw. Ray
