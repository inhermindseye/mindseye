Saint Clement Deans 21st. February 1786

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit) Elizabeth Card  Widow aged 35 - Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to John Card her late Husband. at the Parish Church of Saint Margaret Westminster about 14 Years ago, that after there Intermarriage they rented and lived in a House in Clare Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of thirty four Pounds for upwards of forty Days, that her said Husband the has been informed and believes died about a Year and Eight Months ago, that since quitting the said House in the her said Husband in his Lifetime nor she this Examinant since his Death have done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. Febry 1786 before R Butler

Elizabeth Card
