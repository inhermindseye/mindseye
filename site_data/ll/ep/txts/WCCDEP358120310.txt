Middx  Eliz Clough on behalf of her Son Joseph Clough upon Oath Saith she has been informed & verily believes he was married to his wife Isabella Clough at the Fleet London on the 2d. Feby. 1737/8. O.S. by whom he has 3 Children (viz) Catherine aged 9 Yrs. Eliz aged near 4 Yrs. & Joseph Chas . aged 14 months and that he was bound Apprentice by Indenture dated the 4th. July 1729 to Thomas damarce a Waterman & lived with him as his apprentice in the Broad way in the parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry for her Years or thereabouts of the sd. Term after which he went to Sea and never was assigned over nor ever Rented ten pounds by the years paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn March 20th. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Clough
