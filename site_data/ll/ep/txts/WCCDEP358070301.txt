Middx & Westr} to wit Sarah Williams Aged about Fifty six years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Thomas Williams who has been dead about five years, and by whom she has no Children; Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Stanhope Street Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one year and upwards at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or thereabouts, and after he Quitted the same he never afterwards Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes or has done any other Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement neither has this Examinant since her Husbands Death ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year or any otherwise Gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d of day of Sepr 1775 before R Butler

Sarah Williams
