Middx  Mary Morgan aged about 24 Years Lodging at Mr. Batridge's in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Senhouse, Appraiser and Broker in Lambeth Marsh in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry for One Year and Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds diet and Lodging Quitted the same about Five Months That she never rented a House or paid Parochial Taxes.

Sworn the 24th. day of July 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell Paul Vaillant

Mary Morgan

Upon Mr. Farrer's Complaint
