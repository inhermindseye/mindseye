Middx  Ann Serjeant  Wido . of Wm Serjeant removed from the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn in the County of Middx by Order dated the 11th. Day of Decr. Instt. upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Wm. Serjeant about 18 yrs. since at the Fleet London Says her sd. late Husbd. was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Yrs. Apprentiship with Mr William a Cordwainer in Portugall Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Says her sd. late Husband never rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she hath not since his Death rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 12th. Decr. 1745 before W Jones

The mark of Ann Serjeant
