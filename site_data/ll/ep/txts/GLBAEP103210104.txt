Middlesex ss}  Ann Piddington  Singlewoman maketh oath that sometime ago she was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Mr Blore an undertaker in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the space of one year & half & has not since lived as a hired servant for the space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever married nor hath she done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt That She this Deponent hath a Child named or Called George but which hath not as been baptized aged about two years & five months born of this Depon body in a place Called Mossom at the house of one Mrs Moody there opposite the Blue Lyon in the Parish of Chelmsford in the County of Essex the which child is a Bastard & illegitimate by reason that She this Deponent was never married and that one Thomas Newport late of the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate  Japanner is the Father of the Sd Child

Sworn this 5 day of October 1785 before J. Hawley R Davies

Ann Pidington
