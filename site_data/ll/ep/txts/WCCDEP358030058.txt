Middx  Eliza. Glover upon her Oath saith she inter- -married with her prest. Husband Joseph Glover on the 17th. Day of April last in the Parish Church of Wakefeild in the County of York Says her sd. Husband Served his Apprentiship with Rd. Wharf in the Parish of Hunslett in the sd. County of York  Clothworker Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif.

Sworne 3d. Decr 1745 before W Jones

The Mark of Eliz: Glover
