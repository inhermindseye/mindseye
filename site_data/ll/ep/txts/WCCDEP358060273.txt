Westmr.  Susanna Davis upon Oath Saith She never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Gossan in Villars Street in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of £5 for three Years & upwards quitted her sd. Service upwds. of four Years ago & has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was upwds. of three Years ago delivered of a female bastard Child at Mr. Molloys in little Queen Street in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr . which was baptized Susanna.

Sworn 5th. March 1752 before  Saml Pechell

The Mark of Susa Davis
