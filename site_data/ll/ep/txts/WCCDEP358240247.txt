St Clement Danes April 15th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Jane Bouch  Widow aged Eighty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her present late Husband William Bouch at the parish Church of St Clement Danes on or abt the Ninth day of December November 1773 (by Banns) that previous thereto her said Husband or She hath been informed and believes Rented and Resided in an House at the corner of Baker's Court in the said parish of St Clement Danes for about ten years & that he Served his Apprenticeship with his father Anthony Bouch in Milford Lane in the said parish of Saint Clement Danes and lodged with his said Master in Milford Lane aforesaid And as she this Examinant hath been informed and believes And saith that he did not during his Life obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief That She this Deponent hath not been Married since his Death (which happened about twelve or thirteen years ago) nor done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own Right

Sworn the fifteenth day of April 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Jane Bouch 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
