Middx  Elizth. Lewis Aged abt. Twenty four Years Upon Oath Saith She was never Married that She lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Griffith Roberts at the Kings Head Tavern in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx for three Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. Twelve Months Says on the NIneteenth of April 1761 She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of the sd. Mr. Roberts in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls aforesd. wch hath since been Baptized by the Name of John Says the sd. Griffith Roberts did beget the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body that the sd. Griffith Roberts is Since Dead & She being poor is unable to maintain the sd. Child

Sworn the 16th. of April 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

Elizabeth Lewis
