Middx to wit Eleanor Johnson Aged about Twenty Six years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one John Baker a Stone Mason in Water Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years and a half at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath Quitted her said Service upwards of Ten years ago and hath not Since Quitting the same ever Rented House paid Taxes lived a hired yearly Servant for one year any otherwise gained a subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 17th day July 1773 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child named Mary , in the house of one Mr Martin a Blacksmith in Wild Court Wild Street in the Parish of St Giles in the fields in the said County of Middlesex , and further upon her Oath say that one William Waits , late a footman to John Barwell  Esqr in Golden Square and who is now (as she believes) gone to India , but with what Captain and on board what Ship she cannot set forth, but that he the said William Waits and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 13th day of Novr 1775 before}

her Eleanor Johnson Mark
