Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Griffiths aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that in the Month of June 1785 she Intermarried with Thos. Griffiths her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Bennetts Court in the said Parish for 2 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £10 and paid Taxes that he hath quitted said House about 12 Months since which time he has not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted her and refuses to maintain her she being poor and unable to herself is therefore obliged to Apply to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for relief

Sworn this 27th. day of July 1787 . R Butler

her Mary Griffiths mark
