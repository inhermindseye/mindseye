Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bean  Singlewoman maketh oath that some time ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servt. by & with one Mr Fitzer a Salesman in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of two years & upwards & has not since lived elsewhere as a hired servant for the Space of a year nor was she ever Married nor hath she done any Act to gain Anothr settlement

Sworn this first day of August 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Bean
