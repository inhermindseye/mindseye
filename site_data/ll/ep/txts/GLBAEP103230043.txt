Middlesex ss}  Ann Spooner  Singlewoman maketh oath That She was born in the Parish of Saint George in the Said County of Middlesex and never did any act to gain a Settlement for herself and this Deponent farther Saith that She Cannot with certainty Set forth the Place of the last legal Settlement of her late Parents deced or either of them

Sworn this. 3. day of May 1787 before}

The Mark of Ann Spooner
