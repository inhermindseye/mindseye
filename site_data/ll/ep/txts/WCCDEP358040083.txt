Middx  James Watson upon Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Year to Mr. John Taylor a Taylor and lived with him as an Apprentice for the Space of four Years or thereabts. and then Enlisted himself into his Majestys Service Says he lived the last two Years & upwards of the said term of four Years with the sd. Mr. Taylor in Arundel Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & Says he never was Assigned over to any Person rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif Says he intermarried with his present Wife Eleanor on or abt. the 25th. Day of January 1746 at the Fleet London

Sworn Octr. 19th. 1747 before} Tho Lane

James Watson
