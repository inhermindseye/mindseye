Middx  Susa. Roper aged abt. 50 Yrs & upwds. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that abt. 21 Yrs. since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Thomas Amas in Horton Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of four pounds, one year & upwards & hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Amas Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn May 21st 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Susa. Roper
