Middx  Jane Mytan Wido . of Dennis Mytan upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Dennis Mytan in St. Clemts. Church abt. 20 Yrs. ago and that her sd. late husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of 12 Guineas in Kings Court Russell Street in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and lived therein about 7 Years and there dyed and this Examint. has continued in the sd house sometime after his Decease and has not since she left the same rented Ten pounds a Year pd parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 6th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Jane Mytan
