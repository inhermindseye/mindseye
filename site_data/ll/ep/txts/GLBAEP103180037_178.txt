Middlesex ss}  Jacob Osborne maketh oath that divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certian Wages by & with Mrs Holdsworth in Darby Street in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one Year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this day of May 1778 before}
