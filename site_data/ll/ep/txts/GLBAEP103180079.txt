Middlesex ss}  Thomas Dixon maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to Caleb Cook of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Cordwainer untill he Should attain the age of Twentyone years by the Parish officers of the Parish of St Michael Bassishaw London that he Continued to Serve his Said Master in the Sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate till within about one year & half of the Term for which he was bound Apprentice When he left his Said Master against & Without his Said Masters Consent & never afterwards returned back to him nor did his Sd Master after this Deponents So left him ever give his Consent to this Deponents living or Serving him elsewhere nor was this Deponts Indres ever delivered up to him or Cancelled That this Depont never rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any act to gain a Settlement Since his Apprenticeship That the hath a wife named Sarah & a Child named Mary Ann Aged Two years or thereabouts the lawful Issue of this Depont by his Said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 5 day of November 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Thomas Dixon
