Middlesex ss}  Samuel Weston a Pipemaker Maketh oath that Divers Years ago he lived in and Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds in Cock Alley in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex and was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates and Kings Taxes for the same and lived in Such house for many years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the said Parish That he this Depont. hath not Since rented a house elsewhere of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his Knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that he hath a Wife named Elizabeth to Whome he was lawfully Married in the Church of St. Sepulchre in London

Sworn this 9th Day of May 1772 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

The Mark of Samuel Weston
