Middx  The Examination of Martha Monfort who was found wandering in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx having no Habitation or Place of Abode taken the 10th. Day of March 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that She is a Singlewoman & was never married & that abt. eight Years ago She lived as a Yearly hired Servant with Right Honble the Earl of Orrery in Downing Street in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds for the Space of one Year and upwards & further on her Oath saith that She hath not since She left her sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
