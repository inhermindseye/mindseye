Westmr.  Joseph Ellitt upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Ann Ellitt in the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes upwards of Seven Years since and that he lived a Yearly Servant whilst a Singleman with Mr. Carne Woolen draper in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds for one Year & upwds. & quitted his sd. Service about four Weeks after his Marriage Says he has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 1st. 1747 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Jose. Ellit
