Middx  Alice Holloway Upon Oath Says She intermarryed with her Husband Richd. Holloway in the Parish Church, of St. James in Westmr . on the 20th. day of Augt. 1746 by whom She hath four Children (to wit) Wm . Aged abt. Eight Years May abt. Six Years Alice abt. five Years & Elizth . upwards of one Year Says her sd. Husband Rented a House in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx of the yearly Rent of Twenty five pounds & lived in the same abt. two years & hath quitted same abt. five years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn the 9th. of Augt. 1755 before} Barth. Hammond

Alce Holloway
