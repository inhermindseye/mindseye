Middx  Susa. Figg aged about 35 Years Wife of Thos. Figg upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. husband Thomas Figg on the 1st. Day of April about Sixteen Years ago at the Fleet London Says her sd. husband hath been gone from her about Ten Yrs & She hath not heard from him these 4 Yrs. Says the sd. Thos. Figg Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Massey a Shoemaker near Craven Building in Drury Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes and never was a Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 30th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones .

The Mark of Susa. Figg
