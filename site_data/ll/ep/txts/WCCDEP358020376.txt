St Clement Danes January 17th. 1792

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Ann Seddon  Singlewoman Aged forty one years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she Rented and Rended in an House in Kentishtown near the Chapel but on the other side of the Way in the parish of St. Pancras in the County of Middlesex about One year and a half at the Annual Rent of £15 that she quitted that same about Eleven years ago and since hath not obtained any other legal Settlement and this Deponent saith that she hath a Female Bastard Child born on the seventeenth day of November in the year 1790 at the House of Mr. formerly belonging to, or in the Occupation of, Mrs. Burton in Narrow Marsh in the parish of St. Peter in the Town of Nottingham which hath service been Christened by the names of Jane Seddon of that the said Child is not yet Weaned

Sworn the 17th day of January 1792 before us  Thos Vaughan N Bond

Ann Seddon
