Westmr.  Cornelia Thorn wife of Samuel Thorn (a Soldier in his Majesty's Service) upon Oath saith she intermarryed with the said Saml. Thorn at the Fleet London on or abt. the 18th. Day of April 1744 Says she has been informed & belives her sd. husband Served his Apprentiship with his Unkle Mr. Thorne a Plaisterer in Church Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx. and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 7th. 1747 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Cornelia Thorn
