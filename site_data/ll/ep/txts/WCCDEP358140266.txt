Middx.  Sarah Williams aged abt. 22 years upon Oath says she never was Married nor Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says, her father Richd. Williams served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with Mr. Peters a painter in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year as she has been Credibly inform'd and which Information she verily believes to be true

And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born; says that One John

in the upper Ground in the Parish of Christ Church Southwk. Hammer a Lighterman had Carnal knowledge of her body in the Month of October last and at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that no other person did beget the same

Sworn this 9th. July 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Williams

Upon the Complaint of Joseph Burnthwaite  Oversr . of the Poor J: P:
