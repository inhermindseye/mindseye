Westmr.  Bridget Rock Aged about Twenty three Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Conner in Jackson's Court in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds, quitted said Service about Five Years ago, and never after Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 15th, day of August 1768 , before}  Fran Cary

The Mark of Bridget Rock
