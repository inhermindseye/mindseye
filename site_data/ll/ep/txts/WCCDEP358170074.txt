Middx The Examination of Elizth. Sexton on the Behalfe of Jno. Williams an Infant aged abt- Seven Years Taken the 25th- Day of June 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that Saml. Williams Father of the abovesd- Jno. Williams was bound an Apprentice & served Seven Years Aprentispp to one Mr. Williams of the Parish of St. Paul Shadwell in the County of Middx aforesd. Waterman & further saith yt. the sd. Saml. Williams never rented any House pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt- for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Exaiaint hath been inform'd & beleives yt. the Mother being dead & Saml. the Father gone as a Sailor in his Majesty's Service hath left the sd. Infant unprovided for

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Elizth. Sexton
