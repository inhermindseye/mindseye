Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Pickering Aged 33 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Ridgway in White Horse Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Six years at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted said Service about Eight years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath saith that She is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one John Rider a Waiter who lives at the Sign of the Six Bells in Dove Court Lombard Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examts. Body sometime in the Month of April last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said John Rider is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 4th. Janry. 1787 } D Walker

her Mary Pickering Mark

Vide over Leaf for instructions

Mary Pickering s Examination taken or the other Side by Mistake

John Rider is a Short Man Dark Complexion wears his Hair Tied behind Smooth Pale Face Thin Man
