Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Georgiana Hargrave aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That her father Walter Hargrave Rented a House in Arundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for several years at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds And upwards and paid all Taxes due for the same that after he quitted said House he never did to the best of this Examinants knowledge gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of July 1795 } N Bond

Georgina Hargrave
