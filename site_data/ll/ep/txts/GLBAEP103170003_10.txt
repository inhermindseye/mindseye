Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Marshal  Singlewoman Maketh oath that her late Father Edward Marshal deced divers years ago rented a house in Baileys Place in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there at which time She this Deponent was part of his Family & in which place She was born That her said Father to her Knowledge or belief did not afterwards in any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent never was bound and Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 6th. day of September 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands .

Elizabeth Marshall
