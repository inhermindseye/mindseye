Middlesex ss}  Margaret Giles the Wife of Samuel Giles who is lately gone away from her to places unknown maketh oath That her Said husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was born in the Parish of Saint John in Southwarke in the County of Surry & that he served his apprenticeship there with one George Burr a Waterman & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That to her knowledge or belief her Said husband never rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement and that she hath a Child named Eleanor aged about Six years the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her Said husband to Whom she was lawfully married.

Sworn this 15th day of April 1773 before. B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Margaret Giles
