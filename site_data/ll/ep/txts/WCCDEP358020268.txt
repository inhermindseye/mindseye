St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Mary Taylor Aged 26 Years or thereabouts On Oath saith that she Intermarried with Benjamin Taylor her present Husband at the parish Church of St George Hanover Square in or about the year 1784 that Since then her said Husband Rented and with her Resided in an Apartment at No 6 in Houghton Street Clare Market in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said Liberty and County for about Six five Five Months at the Weekly Rent of 5s being at and after the Rate of £13 Pounds Pr. Annum that they quitted the the same Apartment about Christmas 1789 and since her Husband hath not done any Act or thing whereby he might have acquired any other legal settlement And that she hath three Children by her Husband Vizt. George Aged 5 Years or thereabouts Mary Aged about three Years & Elizabeth of the Age of Ten Months or thereabouts And this Examinant further saith that her said Husband was on Tuesday last was Impressed for Service in his Majesty's Navy And that she is Poor and unable to Maintain herself & Children

Sworn the 15th. April 1791 before Me R Butler

Mary Taylors Mark
