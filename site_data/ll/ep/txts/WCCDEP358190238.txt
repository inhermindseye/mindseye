Middx  Susanna Flood aged about 27 years Lodging in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Noah Flood who has been Dead near One Month to whom she was Married in the Parish Church of Hornsey in the County of Middx year Six Years ago That he served Five years the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. John Farmer  Mathematical Instrument maker near St. Sepulchres Church in the Parish of St. Sepulchre London which Expired in the year 1755 That she hath Three Children living by her said Husband to wit Noah aged 5 years Sarah near 3 years and Mary aged about 5 Months Says her said Husband never rented a House or paid any Tax whatever

Sworn the 21st. day of April 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The mark of Susan: Flood
