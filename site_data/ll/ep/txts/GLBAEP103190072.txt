Middlesex ss}  Mary Hall the wife of Richard Hall maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board  his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Thomas Davenport an Woolcomber in the Parish of Bradninch in the County of Devon & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 12. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Hall
