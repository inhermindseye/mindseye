Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Carrington of New Inn Passage on behalf of Eleanor Twinbull her Mother who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that the said Eleanor Twinnbull lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Peltin in Piccadilly in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years and upwards at the Yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she quitted said Service about Five Years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Decr. 1795 N Bond Edw. Read

Ann Carrington
