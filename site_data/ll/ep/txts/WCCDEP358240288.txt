Saint Clement Danes 23rd. July 1793.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Sarah Rowland  Widow aged 60 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath Saith that she was Married to her late Husband about John Rowland about 37 Years ago at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and Resided in a House in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the sd. Liberty of Westmr. and County for about Six Months at the Annual Rent of £25 that they quitted that House about thirty Years ago And that her said Husband never during his Life obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent knowledge Information or belief that he died about 8 Years ago and that since his Decease hath not been Married or otherwise obtained or become intiled unto any legal Settlement Except as in Right of her said Husband aforesaid

Sworn the 23d. day of July 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner

her Sarah Rowland Mark

Witness Cha: Wycross
