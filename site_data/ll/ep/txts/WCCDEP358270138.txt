After Birth Vide S. A. Hunt & Child

St Clement Danes 28th August 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Child born in the House (to wit) Sophia Augusta Hunt aged 22 years or thereabouts Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never served any Apprenticeship lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief that she is the Daughter of Samuel Hunt by a person with when he lived and Cohabited who was called by the Name of Ann Hunt  who was ever reputed to be his Wife and who this Deponent always conceived to be and still believes was really his Wife that her said Father Samuel Hunt now Rents and Resides and for in a House in Great Shire Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of a Westminster in the County of Middlesex of his own the Freehold of which he purchased about upwards of fifteen years ago and has resided therein during that space of time that she was born at No. 10 in Hemlock Court in the said parish of St Clement Danes And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the Workhouse of and belonging to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes and situate within the same on or about the 21st. day of July last & that the said Child hath been and is chargeable to the said

Reputed Father parish of Saint Clement Danes And that Joseph Taylor late of Shire Land in the said parish is the real true father of the said Child of which she was delivered or aforesaid

Sworn the 28th day of August 1793 before me } D Walker

Sophia Augst Augusta Hunt

Man not taken & the Child died on 29th August 1793 in the Workhouse  belongin
