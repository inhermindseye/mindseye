Middlesex ss}  Richard Shea aged about Sixteen Years maketh oath that about five years ago as he now recollects was bound Apprentice by the then Parish officers of the Parish of St James Westmr in the Said County  to Wm Douglas of the Same Parish & County Baker and after having Served him there for about the Space of four years he removed with his Said Master from thence into the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Where he Continued to Serve his Said Master for at least the Space of two Months When heren away form his Said Master and Left his Service against his said Masters will & Consent and has not since returned to him and has been absent from him for about the Space of a year & has not Since lived any where with his Masters Consent

Sworn this 11th day of August 1787 before  R Davies

Richard Shea
