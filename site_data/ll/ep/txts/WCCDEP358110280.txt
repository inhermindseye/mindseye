Middx  Eliz: Ulric  wido . of Henry Baldazy Ulric upon Oath Saith she was married to her sd. late husbd. at the Fleet London in or about the month of Decr. 1748 that her sd. husband was a German & never gained a Settlemt. in England to her Knowledge & dyed abt. 7 Yrs. since and that she this Examinant was bound Apprentice about 27 Yrs. ago to Mrs. Gregory a Mantua Maker for five Years & after she had Served about half her time the sd. Mrs. Gregory left of Trade and assigned this Examinant over to Mrs. Brotherton a Mantua Maker with whom she Served out the remainder of her Apprentiship and lodged during the last Year of her time wth. the sd Mrs. Brotherton opposite St Clements Church in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and after the Expiration of her Apprentiship lived a Yearly Servt. wth. the sd Mrs. Brotherton in the same house at the Yearly wages of £5 for one Year or thereabts. that since she left Mrs. Brotherton she has not rented ten Pounds a Year paid

Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Augt. 1st. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

Ulrick
