Middx ss  Michael Reboul aged about Twenty Seven years on his Oath Says he never was married but that this Examinant has three Children by Sarah Richardson  Singlewoman which he proposes to Marry to wit George aged about six years Michael aged about four years and Samuel aged about twelve Months and this Examinant further Says that he Rented and lived in a House in Lilley Street Saffron Hill Elly Rents in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn London for Six Months at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds a year but Paid no Parish Taxes due for the same hath Quitted the same House about two years ago since which time he hath never Rented House Paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14 Day of June 1778 before  John Cox .

Michael Reboul
