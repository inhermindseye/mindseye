Westmr.  Mary Ireland Aged about Forty Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she served Five Years Apprenticeship with Mrs. Ashburne a Mantua Maker in White horse Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster , and after she had served said Apprenticeship she continued to work Journey Work with the said Mrs. Ashburne upwards of Two Years, Quitted said Service upwards of Fifteen Years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 14th. day of January 1768 before W Kelynge

The Mark of Mary Ireland
