Middx  Sarah Hall aged abt. 24 years Upon Oath Says She was never Maryed that she lived a yearly Servt. with one Mr. Jones a Carman at Kings Wharfe in Beauford Buildings in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the yearly Wages of Fifty Shills. and hath quitted her sd. Service abt. ten Months

Sworn the 20th. day of Augt. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Sarah Hall
