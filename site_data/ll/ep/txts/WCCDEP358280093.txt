Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Sarah Candash aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an Apprentice that her Father informed her which Information she verily believes to be true that she was born in Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  in the County of Middlesex And further this Examint. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Morris Morris a Private Soldier  in his Majestys 1st. Regt. of Foot Guards and who is Quartered at the Sign of the Bell a Public House in Charles Court in the Strand , is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body for these four years last past at several times at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Morris Morris is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day of May 1787 } R Butler

her Sarah Candash  mark
