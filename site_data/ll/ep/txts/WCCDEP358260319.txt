Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Howe aged 22 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 20th. day of August 1792 she Intermarried with Robert Howe her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Robert aged about Three years and Mary aged about Seventeen Months That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Holles Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of £36 that he hath quitted said House about Three years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Augt. 1798 before me } W Carter

her Mary Howe Mark

Witness H Day
