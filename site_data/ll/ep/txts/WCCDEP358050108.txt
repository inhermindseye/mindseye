Westmr.  Ann Kelly  wido . of Lake Kelly upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late husband abt. 31 Years ago at the fleet London and that the sd. Lake Kelly whilst a Singleman Lived a Yearly Servant with a Gentleman in Orange Court in the Parish of St Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for Two Years & upwards and that her sd. late husband after he quitted the said Service this Examinant since his Decease have not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Serv. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to this Examints. to knowledge or belief.

Sworn Septr. 28th. 1749 before H Fielding

The mark of Ann Kelly
