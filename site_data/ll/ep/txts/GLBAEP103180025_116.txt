Middlesex ss}  Susannah Lee the widow of William Lee deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband as he has informed her & which She believes to be true was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Francis Peete a Broker in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service for divers years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement nor hath She this Depont. Since his death been married or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her Said late husband.

Sworn this 14 day of March 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Susannah Lee
