Aldgate Middx

Frances Tucker  Spinster Maketh Oath that her late Father Walter Tucker was about Six years ago a Housekeeper at Number Twenty Six in Prescott Street in the Parish of White Chapel where he livd upwards of two years and paid thirteen pound a year Rent for the Said house and that he never routed a House of ten pound a year Since nor paid any Parish taxes, nor Servd any Parish office Since his living in White Chapel this Deponant hath three Sisters (to wit) Maria Tucker Aged about 21 years, Elizabeth aged 12 years and Sarah Tucker aged Eight years And that her Said Sister Marie never was an Apprentice nor hired Servant by the year

Sworn 22th day of July 1747 before us Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

Frances Tucker
