Westmr. 

Wm. Lyming upon Oath saith he has one Daughter (now living) by his late wife  Mary Lyming viz. Elizabeth aged upwds. of Eleven Years and that about 20 Years since he rented a house of Sixteen pounds a Year known by the name of the white Swan Alehouse near Eastlane in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen  Bermondsey in the County of Surrey and lived therein abt nine months and has not since be quitted the said house rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 25th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

William Lyming
