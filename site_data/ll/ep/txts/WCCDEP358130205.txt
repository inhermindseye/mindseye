Middx  Mary Hubbard aged abt. Sixty one Years upon Oath says abt. Seven Years ago She lived a yearly hired Servant with Mr. Shalhaws a Staymaker in King Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds She being at that time a Widow & without Child or Children Says She hath not since She left Mr. Shalhaws Service Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Septr. 6th. 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Mary Hubbard
