Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Jane Herindine aged about 74 years upon her Oath saith that about 43 years ago she Intermarried with Wm. Herindine her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Brides in Fleet Street that her said late Husband has been dead about 26 years That since his Decease She this Examinant Rented a Ground Floor of a Mr. Miller in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Six years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds that She furnished that said Apartments with her own furniture That she has quitted said Premises about Sixteen years ago since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of March 1788 R Butler

Jeane Herindine
