Middlesex ss}  Sarah Green  widow of John Green deced maketh oath that her Sd late husband about fifteen years ago he rented a house of the yearly Rent of Six Pounds in Angel Court near Sun Yard in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & Paid Kings Taxes for the Same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain A Settlement elsewhere That She this Depont after her sd husbands death rented the sd house & was Charged to & Paid Kings Taxes for the Same & has not Since done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere & that She was lawfully Married to her Said husband.

Sworn this. 31. day of october 1783 before R Davies

Sarah Green
