Middx.  Mary Mose  Wife of Robt. Mose aged about 30 Years upon her Oath saith that on the 26th Day of August 1728 she intermarried with her said Hubd. Robt. Mose at the Fleet  London that her said Husband is now in the Marshal- -sea Prison for Debt but Continues to rent a House in Bennetts Court in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £11.10s.0d & hath so done for the Space of Five Years where this Examinant & her Family now reside that she hath Three Children viz Sush . aged Twelve Years Eliz : aged 8 Yrs. & Jose : aged 5 Yrs. & being Poor is not able to maintain herself & them

Sworne 6th.. Sept. 1742 before}
