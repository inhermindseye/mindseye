Middx  Thomas Picken aged 84 Yrs. & upwds: Vagabond Aprehended in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx upon his Oath saith he is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England Says his Father  Ralph Picken lived in a house of his own in the Parish of Creskillen in the County of Farmanaugh in the Kingdom of Ireland aforesd. of the Yearly Value of £10 for Sevll. Yrs. Says he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 19th. Febry 1744 before} Wm: Jones .

The mark of Thos. Picken
