Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Fleuty aged about 64 upon her Oath saith that about Thirty eight years ago she Intermarried with Nicholas Fleuty her late husband who has been dead about Ten years at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Change Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds that after he quitted said House he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of April 1798 before me } Saml. Edward

her Elizth. Fleuty mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
