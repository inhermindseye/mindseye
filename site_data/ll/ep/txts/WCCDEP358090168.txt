Middx to wit Ann Hayward Aged about Thirty three Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year says her Father James Hayward who died many Years ago Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Smith a Hartshorn Rasper in the Old Change in the Parish of Saint Augustine otherwise Saint Austin in the City of London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said Fathers Death.

Sworn this 4th. Day of Novr. 1780 before R Butler M Addington

An Hayward
