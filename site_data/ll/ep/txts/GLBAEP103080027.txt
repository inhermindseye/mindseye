Middx ss  Mary Watson widow of John Watson deced Maketh oath that about Twenty Eigt

Middx ss  Mary Johnson  wife  widow of George Johnson deced Maketh oath that she hath been informed by one Mr Insetture of the Parish of Bromley Saint Leonard in the county of Middx Waterman that her said late husband about thirty five years ago was duly bound an Apprentice to the said Mr Insetture who then and still lives in the said Parish and served there the whole of his apprenticeship Except about the Space of two Months & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there which Information this Depont. believes to be true And this Deponent saith that to her knowledge informacon or belief her said husband did not after his apprenticeship Rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor live as a hired servant by the Year Nor serve any Parish offices nor gain any subsequent settlemt. Nor hath she this Depont. to her knowledge or belief gained any settlement in her own right since her said husbands Death

Sworn this 30 day of August 1758 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

the Mark of Mary Johnson
