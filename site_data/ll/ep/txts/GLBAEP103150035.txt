Middlesex ss}  Jane Johnson  Single woman Maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Mr Nathan in Upper East Smithfield in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Salesman & Continued in Such Service for near the Space of two Years at Certain Yearly Wages & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she this Deponent hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor was She ever Married nor to her Knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th day of May 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of Jane Johnson
