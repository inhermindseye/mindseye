Middx  Sarah Jones upon Oath Saith she is the Wido. of Wm Jones who dyed abt. 14 Years ago and that after the Decease of her sd. husband being without Child or Children she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Henry King at Strand Coffee house near Exeter Exchange in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one year and upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £3..10..0 Quitted her sd.  Service upwds. of 12 yrs. ago and has not since rented ten Pounds a year pd. parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 24th. March 1753 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Sarh. Jones
