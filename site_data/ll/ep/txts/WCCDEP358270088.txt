St Clement Danes Decr. 4th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnancy  Reputed Father  Mary Harvey  singlewoman aged 29 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly servant with Mr Henry Birks in Gilbert Street Clare Market in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex and City and Liberty of Westminster about Seven years at the annual Wages of about £6 for the last two year of the said Term that she quitted that service  on or about the third day of May last and hath not since rented any House  or Apartment of £10 a year on or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement and this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath further saith that she is pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes  and that John Burton of Stanhope Street in the said parish of Saint Clement Danes within the said Liberty and County did beget the said Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant as aforesaid on this Deponent's Body he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime  in or about the Month of September 1791  at several times since and particularly on or about the 5th. day of June now last past  and that he and no or else is the only real  and true father

 of the said Child or Children wherewith She is pregnant as aforesaid

Sworn the 4th day of December 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Harvey 's mark

Witness Richd Dobbins

Father taken & paid Mr. Collett £20 gave release to be Christened John Burlon the Child which was born in the House 29th Janry 1793 in or abt Febry. 1793 the Child Died so on after its birth
