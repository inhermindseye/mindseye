Westmr  Mary Eddes Aged about Twenty four Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Chevalier in the House of Mr. Thompson Perukemaker in Mount Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for Three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds, quitted said Service about Four Years, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 2d. day of Febry 1768 before} Paul Vaillant  Thos. Balack

Mary Eddes
