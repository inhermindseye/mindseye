Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Rebecca Raffetey aged thirty two years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was married to Thomas Raffetey her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about twelve or thirteen Years ago, that her said Husband was Apprentice to and served his with Mr. Anderson of Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and after quitting his said Master hath not done any Act Matter or thing Whereby to the best of this Examinant Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Settlement other than as above stated and further she this Examinant Saith that her said Husband has deserted her and that she is poor and unwell and at present in great Distress and totally unable to maintain herself and that she does not Where her said Husband now in has she been him nor any way heard of him father four Months past

Sworn this 30th. Octr 1789 before me R Butler

her Rebecca Raffety Mark
