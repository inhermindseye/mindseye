Westmr.  John Albone aged abt. 39 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Ruth his Wife in the Parish Church of Cainson in Somersetshire about Twelve Years ago. says before his said intermarriage & whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Cavanagh at the Ship & Star Alehouse (now known by the Sign of the Thatched House) in Butcher row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, quitted said Service about Sixteen Years ago & hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 15th, Octr. 1766 , before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of John Albone
