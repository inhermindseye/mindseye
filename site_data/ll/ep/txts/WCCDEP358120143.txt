Mary Bagnald aged 60 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with the late Mr Justice Denton in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx lived for three Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds quitted her sd Service about Sixteen Years ago & has not Since rented ten pounds by the Year pd Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn July 6th. 1754 : before Fran: Bedwell

Mary Bagnald
