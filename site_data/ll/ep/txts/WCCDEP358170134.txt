Middx  Wm. Brown passed from the Parish of Chelsea in the County of Middx by Order bearing Date the 17th. Day of September 1741 upon his Oath saith that abt. 14 Years since he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Jno. Vernon at the Swan & Rummer in Finch Lane  London  Vintner & served him as an Apprentice for the Space of 3 Years & a half or thereabts. & then was assigned over to one Mr. Crawford at the Bear & Harrow Tavern in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx & lived with him for the Space of 2 Yrs- or thereabts- & then left the sd. Mr. Crawford & lived with one Mr. Goostree at the Devil Tavern  witht. Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West  London for the Space of Five Months or thereabts. & that after he left the sd. Mr. Goostrees Service he lived with one Mr. Jepson at the Bedford Head in Southampton Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middx for the Space of Three Months he  at such Time not being out of his sd. Apprentisp that he hath not since he left his sd. Master rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife that

he is Poor & not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the 22d. of Septr- 1741 before}

William Brown
