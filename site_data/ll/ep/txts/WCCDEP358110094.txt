Westmr.  Margt. Jones aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that on the 5th. Day of June 1750 she was delivered of a female bastard Child (since baptized Mary ) in the house of Mr Rogers known by the Sign of the Whitehart in the parish of Morton in Marsh in the County of Gloucester

Sworn 25th. Sepr. 1752 before} Benjamin Cox

Margaret Jones
