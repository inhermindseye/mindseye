Middlesex ss}  Susanna English the Widow of Joseph English deced Maketh oath that her Said late husband and has informed her That divers Years ago & when a Singleman he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages with one Mr Foster a Victualler who kept the Sign of the in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one year & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponts. Knowledge or belief her Said husband never rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish offices or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subseqauent to his Said Servitude nor hath She this Deponent Since her Said husband Death done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this day of 1772 before}
