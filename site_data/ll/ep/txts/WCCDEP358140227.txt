Middx.  Eliz: Williams aged abt. 72 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Jno. Williams she has been dead abt. 14 Yrs. says her sd. late husband Rented a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £26 and lived in the same Seventeen Years and there dyed says she Continued in & Rented the said house for 5 Years after her husbds. death and hath quitted it abt. Seven Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. Feby. 1759 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Williams
