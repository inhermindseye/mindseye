Middx  The Exaicon of Elizth. Guillam  wido . aged 53 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 28th. day of June 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that her late Husband David Guillam before his Intermarryage with her & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Bonthey by Bow Church in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bow in Cheap Side London for the Space of Nine Months or thereabts. and after the sd. Intermarryage continued to live & reside in the same or Service Under the same Agreemt. Three Years & upwds. and further Saith that her sd. Husband in his life nor this Examant since his Death never rented any House neither hath this Examnt since his Death lived with any person as a yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yeare or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife & is poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Yeare above menconed before} Tho Lane  Wm. Cayton

The mark of Elizth. Guillam
