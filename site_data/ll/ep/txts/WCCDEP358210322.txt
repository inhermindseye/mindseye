Westmr.  Catharine Cundall Aged about Twenty eight Years, on behalf of her Bastard Child named Catharine Elizabeth Aged near Eight Years, upon Oath says the said Catherine Elizabeth was born in the House of one Mr. Stephen Pickhaver in Banbury Court, Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex

Sworn the 22d, day of September 1768 , before}  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Cathne. Cundall
