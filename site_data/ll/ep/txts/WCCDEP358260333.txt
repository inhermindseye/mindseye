Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Eleanor Clarke Smart aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of October 1780 she Intermarried with Green Smart her present Husband who has deserted her at the Trinity Church in Hull That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Houghton Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of Thirty Six Pounds That he quitted said House about Two years since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief done any act matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement and this Deponent saith that she hath One Child living with her by her said Husband Vizt. Sarah aged about Seven years

Sworn this 17th day of Septr. 1798 before me } John Collick

her Eleanor Smart  Mark

Witness AW Burbidge Beadle
