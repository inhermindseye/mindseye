Middx to Wit John Pritchard Aged about Sixty Eight years on Behalf of Ann Hart his Grand Daughter who is now raven Mad on his Oath says that his said Grand Daughter Intermarried to her Present Husband Patrick Hart about Seven years ago who is now down at the Nore as he believes Says that the said Patrick Hart Rented and lived in a House in Fleet Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchre London for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas & Paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about four years ago since which time he never gained any other Settlement otherwise than as aforesaid

Sworn this 16th. day of July 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner
