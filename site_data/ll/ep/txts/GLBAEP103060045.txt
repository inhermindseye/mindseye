Middx  Ann Wright  Singlewoman maketh Oath and Saith that about Twenty Years ago her father Robert Wright deceased Rented a House in Mint Street in the parish of St. George Southwark in the County of Surry and lived therein Ten Years and upwards and paid Eighteen pounds a Year for the same And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge

Sworn this 5th. day of August 1745 Before Robert Dennett

Ann Wright
