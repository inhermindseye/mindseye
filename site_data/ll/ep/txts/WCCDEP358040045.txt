Westmr.  Richd. Savill upon Oath saith he inter- -married with his Wife Dorothy at the Fleet London upwards of Nine Years ago Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Jno. Mackenzie  Taylor & Salesman in King Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the feilds in the County of Middx Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 5th. Augt. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

Richard: Savill
