St Clement Danes 2d.. March 29th February 1792

Middx & Westmr.} (to wit) Benjamin Newman One of the Beadles of the parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Isabella Prosser Maketh Oath and saith that he hath been Informed which he verily believes to be true that the said Isabella Prosser was Married to her present Husband James Prosser at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes but cannot tell exactly the time, that afterwards he Rented and resided in an House in Clements Lane in the Said part in for about 3 or 4 Months at Rent  £16 P Ann this Deponent the Yearly Rent of £ that he left the same in or about the year 1777. & his this  Deponent said Wife therein & never afterwards returned to her that the said Isabella Prosser was taken into into the Work house belonging to the said parish of St. Clement Danes in or about the Month of July 1777 and has been supported therein for several Years & that he the said James Prosser Neglects to maintain the Said Isabella Prosser his Wife Altho he is of sufficient Strength and Ability so to do as this Deponent is Informed & believes And this Deponent saith that he hath seen the said Isabella Prosser and that She is ver her Mental Faculties Appear so far impaired that she is incapable of being Examined herself

Sworn the 2d day of March 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Benj. Newman

Mr Hogg Oversr. Newman Beadle

6th March 1792  Jas Prosser taken on & brot up before Mr Justice Walker

Poor, dischged by Consent of Mr Hogg the Overseer attending)
