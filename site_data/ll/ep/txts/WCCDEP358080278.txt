Middlesex to wit Ann Holton Aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired Yearly or Covenanted Servant with one Mr. Oliver a French Trinning maker in New Bond Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds besides Diet & Lodging hath quitted her said Service about four years ago since which time she never gained any or Subsequent either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 20th. day of Augt. 1778 before  Abrm Baley  James Croft

Ann Holton
