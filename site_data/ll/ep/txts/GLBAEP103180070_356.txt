Middlesex ss}  Stacey Ward the widow of Job Ward deced maketh oath that her Said late husband was bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with Thomas Far of Princes Street in the Parish of St Stephen in the City of Bristol That her Said husband Since Such his Apprenticeship hath not done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She hath two Children namely John aged about eight years & Grace aged about Six Years & four months the issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 7 day of October 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Stacey Ward
