Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Halfpenny  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was Sent by a warrant or pass order to the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the Said County (in which Parish She was born) as being the Place of her last legal Settlement & was reced into the Work house thae as one of the Poor legally Settled there & Continued therein for many years That She this Depont never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & She can give no Proper Account respecting her Parents Settlement

Sworn this 3d day of October 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Halfpenny
