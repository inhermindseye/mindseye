Middlesex ss}  Ann Peck the widow of John Peck deced maketh oath that her sd late husband in his life time rented a house of the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St John Wapping in the sd County & resided there on for the Space of Three Years & upwards & did not afterwards do ant Act to gain anothr settlement nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully Married to her Sd husband That She this Depont hath a Daughter named Susannah Ruth Peck Aged twenty two Years & Six Months Who hath done no Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 19. day of Janry 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

Ann Peck

To her Husbands Settlemt. By Renting a House and paying Taxes
