St. Clement Danes August 16th 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St Mary Lambeth  (to wit) James Dollett aged 39 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife Elizabeth at the Chapel of Gosport in the parish of Alverstoke in the County of Southampton in or about the Month of February 1789 That he this Deponent previous thereto was bound an Apprentice by Indenture to Mr John Fasby at Morris's Causeway in the parish of St Mary Lambeth  for Seven Years and duly Served his said Master Five Years and upwards and then left his said Master by consent and went to Sea in his Majesty's Service That during such Service will his said Master he Boarded and lodged in his said Master's House And since discharge from his Apprenticeship he hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement and to the best of his knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 16th day of August 1792 before us } Thos. Gordon  Gd: Mitchell .

James Dollett
