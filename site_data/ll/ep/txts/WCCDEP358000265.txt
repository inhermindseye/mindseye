Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Susannah Harford aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Thos. Harford her late Husband at the parish Church of Fulham about 11 years ago  that he has been dead about 6 years that she hath two Children living by her said late Husband Vizt. Ann Harford aged 9 years who is in provided for and John aged about 8 years who is in the Workhouse at Hammersmith that her said late Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Bartlow or Fisherman at Hammersmith in the parish of Hammersmith in the County of Middx by Indres duly executed that after he quitted his said Apprenticeship he never done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1786  Rt. Taylor Mr. Barnfather

Susannah Harford
