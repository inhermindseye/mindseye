Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit George Rochford aged about 40 years upon his oath saith that he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Bride a Whitesmith in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon his Oath saith that he has Three illegitimate Children living by one Ann Coleman vizt. George aged about Six years born at the house of a Mr. Butler in Boziers Court Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields  John Wm. aged about 3 years born at the house of a Mr. Blunt Opposite Saint Giles's Church in the Parish of Saint Giles's and James Saml aged about Nine Months born at the house of a Mrs. Lazeab Pritchard in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 14th. day of Septr. 1790 R Butler

George Rochford
