Middx Westmr. to witt} The Examination of Joseph Massey taken the 6th. Day of June who upon his Oath saith that on the 24th- Day of June 1707 he was bound an Apprentice to & served 7 Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Robt. Mothersall in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd- Liberty of Westmr . & further saith that since he left his sd. Apprentiship he never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he intermarried with Susanah his Now Wife on or abt. the 30th- Day of Novr- 1737

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}

Joseph Massey
