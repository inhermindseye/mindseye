Middx  Eleanor Molyneux aged about 33 Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of John Molyneux (who is Pressed and gone to Sea ) with whom she Intermarried at North Church in the County of Hertford upwards of Ten Years ago and by whom she hath four Children now living Namely Mary aged about Ten Years Isabella aged about Eight Years Matthew aged about five Years and Paul Aged upwards of Three Years Say's her said Husband is a Native of and born in Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true other by Renting House paying Taxes serving any Apprenticeship or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year before Marriage, or otherwise Howsoever And this Examinant further Says that before her Intermarriage to her said Husband she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Charles Chappell a Farmer at Harsfoot End near Brickle Green in the Parish of Great Berkhamstead in the County of Hertford upwards of four Years at the Yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of Thirteen Years and after quitting mentioned Service she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30th- day of Novr. 1778 before J Welch Thos. Parker

her Eleanor Molyneux Mark
