Middx  Ann Wish aged 23 years upon Oath saith she never was married & never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. for one Year and that her father Austin Wish now rented a house of the yearly Rent of £10 Pounds opposite the horse & Groom in Eagle Street in the parish of St. Geo in Queen Square in the County of Middlesex and has lived in & Rented the said house for 4 or 5 yrs. last past

Sworn March 6th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark Ann Wish
