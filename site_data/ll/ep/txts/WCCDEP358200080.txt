Westmr.  Thomas Norgrave Aged about Fifty One Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Mary in May Fair Chapel about Twenty Years ago who has been Dead about Five Years Says that whilst he was Singleman and Before his Intermarriage with his said Wife he Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr Whiteacre in Lime Street in the Parish of Saint Dionis Backchurch in the City of London upwards of Two Year at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds. hath Quitted his said Service about Twenty One

Years and he hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes.

Sworn the 15th Day of March 1764 Before R Kilsters Paul Vaillant

Resworn the 7th of March 1764 Before R Kilsters Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Thos. Norgrave

Upon Mr Olivers Complt Ovr. J. W
