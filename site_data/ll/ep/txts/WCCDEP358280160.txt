First Examination Overseers 1788

Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Ann Buckler of the Parish of Saint Anthony  Watling Street in the City of London  Singlewoman upon her Oath Saith that she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Robert in Clements her Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex in the Month of January last and which said Child has never been Christened by the House of that She this Deponents never was married that Samuel Ellis of Lunan Street Cheap in the City of London  Stationer is the Father of such female Bastard Child he having in the Month of April 1787 and at several other times had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and by other the Same thereon at one of the said times and this Examinant further Saith that she is unable to maintain the said Child and that the said Child sum of been Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid

Sworn this 19th. May 1788 before me} R Butler

Ann Buckler
