Middx  Thomas Jones aged about 50 Years upon Oath says he Intermarried with his Wife  Elizabeth at the Fleet London upwards of Twenty Years since Says he hath one Child by her named Mary aged about four Years Says that he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Nicholas Humphrys in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster  Shoemaker and hath been out of his Apprenticeship near Thirty Years and hath not since lived a hired Yearly Servant a Year Rented a House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 26th. October 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

Thos Jones
