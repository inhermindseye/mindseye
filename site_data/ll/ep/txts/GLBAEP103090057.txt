Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Margaret Vance of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  single woman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this 27th day of January in the Year of our Lord 1767 .

Who on her oath Saith that on the Sixteenth day of December last Past at the Workhouse of the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate she the said Margaret Vance was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which has been since Baptised and Registred by the name of William Walker and is become Chargeable to the said Parish And this Examinant farther Saith That one Charles Stapleton a Servant to one Mr Williamson of the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the City and Liberty of Westminster in the said County did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this 27 day of January 1767 before  Chris: Scott

Margaret Vance her Mark
