Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Pugh maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the settlement of her late Parents or eithr of them That she this Deponent was born in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant by the year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Esther Elizabeth Pugh
