Westmr.  Mary Merchant upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one Year and that she was born in the Parish of St. Pauls in the Town & County of Bedford and her father John Merchant now lives in and rents a house upon the Bridge in Bedford in the aforesaid Parish of St. Pauls

Sworn 12th Novr. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Merchant
