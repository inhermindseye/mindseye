Middx ss  Catherine Curtis  Widow of Thomas Curtis deced Maketh oath that her sd. late husband has often informed her that he was bound apprentice to his Father Henry Curtis a Painter who during his apprenticeship lived in the parish of St. George Hanover square in ye County of Middx And that he duly Served his Apprenticeship with his sd father in ye sd. parish & by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt. there which Information this Depont. belives to be true And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge or belief her sd. husband never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards Nor served any parish offices Nor gained any subsequent settlemt. And that she hath three Children to wit Thomas aged abt. seven years Sarah Aged about five years & Catherine Aged about two years the lawfull issue of this Depont. by her sd late husband

Sworn this 4 day of Sepr. 1754 before  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

Kathrin Curtis
