Middlesex 

passd. to St. Giles's. (to wit) Benjamin Wingrove  Master of the Work house of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Maketh Oath and saith that he has been informed and which Information be believes to be time that Catherine Green a Singlewoman aged thirteen years or thereabouts Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes was born at Number fourteen in towards Rents in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields , and has never acquired any settlement in her own Right

Sworn this 20th Septr. 1785 before  Sampr Wright J Addington

B Wingrove
