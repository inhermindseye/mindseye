Middlesex ss}  Abigail Wood  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years ago she was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages by & with one Mr Phipps a Stationr in the Parish  Liberty of Norton Folgate in the County of Middlesex & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Three Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she this Depont has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor was ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement That about Five months ago she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the house of Mrs Harrison in Virgin Court in Artichoak Lane in the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middx which hath been since baptised & registred by the name of Rebecca Holdbrook & that one Joshua Holdbrook now or late of Meston in the County of Norfolk is the Father of the sd Child

Sworn this 1st. day of March 1776 before  Jasper Clarkes P. Rowlands

The Mark of Abigail Wood
