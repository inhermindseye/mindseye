Middx ss  Mary Barrett  Singlewoman Maketh oath that about Nine Years ago she lived at a hired Servant by the year with Captain Poynter in East Lane in the Parish of St. Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsly in ye County of Surry at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds a Year and continued in Such Service for about the Space of fifteen Months And Saith that She was Never Married nor lived in any Service Since Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10 day of March 1753 before us Richd: Ricards  Boulton Mainwaring

Mary Barrett
