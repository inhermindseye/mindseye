St Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster} (to wit) Dinah Grover Aged forty six years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly servant with Mr. Andrew Becophna  Goldsmith at No.112 in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about Fourteen Months of the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she quitted that Servie about Eighteen years ago and since hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatever whereby she might acquire any subsequent legal Settlement and that she is poor and unable maintain herself

Sworn the 22d day of July 1791 before me R Butler

Dinah Grover
