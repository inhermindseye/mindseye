Middlesex ss}  Sarah Hallsey  Single woman maketh oath That her late Father Richard Hallsey deced has informed her & which information She believes to be true That he was bound apprentice to & served the whole of such apprenticeship with Thomas Palmer the Elder in Hand & Pen Court in the Precinct of the Tower without within the Liberty of his Majesties Tower of London & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponts. knowledge or belief her said Father never did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his apprenticeship and that she this Depont. never lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever bound an apprentice nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 6th. day of August 1773 before B Camper Cha: Digby

The Mark of Sarah Hallsey
