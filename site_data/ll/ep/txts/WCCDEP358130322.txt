Middx  Mary Smithers aged abt. 50 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Smithers who hath been dead abt. eight says her sd. husbd. Rented a house of fourteen Pounds a year in the Parish of Stornton in the County of Sussex for four years or thereabouts & never after he quitted sd. house Rented ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes says she hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant

Sworn the 18th day of April 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Smithers
