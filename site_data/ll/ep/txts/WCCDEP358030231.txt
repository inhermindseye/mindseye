Middx  Joseph Herbert aged upwards of 62 years upon Oath saith he lived in & rented a house in Bennetts Court in Drury Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £12 for the Space of Eleven Years & hath left same about Sixteen Years Says he hath not since he left sd. house rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Places to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Octr. 22d 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark Joanl of Joseph Herbert
