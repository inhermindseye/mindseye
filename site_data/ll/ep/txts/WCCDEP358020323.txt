St Clement Danes

Middx Westminster} to wit Jonathan Porter Aged 23 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he was bound by Indres of Apprentice with the Consent of Richard Friend his Guardian to Mr. James Price at No 13 Craven Buildings Drury Lane in the Psh of St Clement Danes with whom he regularly served the Term of 7 Years under such Indenture, & lodged at her said Masters the whole time except when he had leave of Absence that he quitted his said Master House on the 1st March 1790 the day of Expiration of her Apprenticeship and hath never since (by any means) acquired any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his Knowledge or belief And that (being Ill) he is at present rendered incapable of providing for him himself

Sworn the 28th July 1791 . before me  Wm Hitchiner

Jon Porter
