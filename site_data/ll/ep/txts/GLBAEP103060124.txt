Middx ss The Examinacon of Elizabeth Haines  Singlewoman Taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justies of the peace for the County of Middx residing in or next to the limits of the parish Church of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County this 6th. Day of January 1749 .

This Examinant upon her Oath Saith That about eight Months ago one Mathew Vernoll then and now of the sd. parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate  Yeoman Did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to Lye with her at the House of Mr . John Everall of ye Victuallg. office  Victualler with whom this Examinant & the sd. Mathew Vernoll lived as Servtt . and the sd. Mathew Vernoll had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body and so had Several times whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which She now goeth and is pregnant And this Examinant Saith that ye sd Child or Children of which she now goeth and is pregnant as aforesd. is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to be Chargeable to ye sd. parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate she not be able to provide for the Same And that the said Mathew Vernoll is the true & only father of ye sd. Child or Children so likely to be born a bastard or bastards as aforesd. And lastly Saith that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn the 6th. Day of January 1749 before  Richd: Ricards

The Marke of Elizabeth Haines
