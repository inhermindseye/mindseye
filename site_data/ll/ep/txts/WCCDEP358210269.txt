Westmr.  Margarett Carr Aged about Twenty seven Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Ferrol Master of the Royal Bagnio in Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, quitted said Service about Three Years ago, and hath not since Rented House, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly for one Year

Sworn the 26th day of April 1768 , Before} Thos. Kynaston  Fran Cary

The Mark of Margarett Carr
