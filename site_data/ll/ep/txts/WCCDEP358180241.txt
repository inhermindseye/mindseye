middx  Edwd. Sexton aged 64 Yrs. & upwds: removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds by Order Dated the 22d. Day of Octr. 1744 upon his Oath saith that he is a Singleman that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Thos. Shaw a Baker in Claremarkett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship abt. 41 Years since which time he hath not Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 23d: Octr. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Edward Sexton
