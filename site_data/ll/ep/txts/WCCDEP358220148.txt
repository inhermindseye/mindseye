Middx & Westmr.}  Samuel Smith of Blackmoor Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Haberdasher on behalf of Mary Smith a Lunatick Aged about Thirty Seven Years maketh Oath that the said Mary Smith lived as a hired Yearly Servant with the Revd. Mr. Parr in the Parish of Austin field in the County of Stafford hath quitted her said Service about thirteen Years ago and after she quitted her said Service she never afterwards to his Knowledge or belief ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 18th. day of May 1770 before } Tho Lane

S Smith.
