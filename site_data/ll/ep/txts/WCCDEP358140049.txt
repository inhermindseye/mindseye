Middx  Ann Roberts aged abt. 32 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Saml. Pechell  Esqr . in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx for one Year & a half at the Yearly Wages of Eight Guineas & hath quitted her said Service abt. four Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 22d day of October 1757 . before me} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Roberts
