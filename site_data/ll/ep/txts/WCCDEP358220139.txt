Middx & Westmr.}  Hannah Wharton Aged about 26 Years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Scott a Butcher in Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further says that on the 29th. day of January last past she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of one Mr. Thompson in Garden Court Balwins Gardens in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Ann Says that one William Mayo a Journeyman Shoemaker who lately lodged at a Cooks Shop in White Hart Yard and worked for one Mr. Papper a Shoemaker under Lincolns Inn Gate Way is the Father of the said Female Bastard Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time the begining of May 1769 but the particular Day she does not now Remember and at sevl. times afterwards at one of which times he get her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as afsd. and that the said Wm. Mayo and no other person is the real & True Father thereof

Sworn this 2d day of May 1770 before me}

her Hannah Wharton Mark

Witness Petr. Sidebotham
