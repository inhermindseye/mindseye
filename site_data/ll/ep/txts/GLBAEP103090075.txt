Middlesex ss  Rachael Manger the Widow of Philip Manger deced maketh oath that her said late husband hath informed her that he was born in the Island of Jersey And this Depont. believes such Information to be true And this Depont. doth not know nor was she ever informed nor Can she in any Manner Set forth whether her said husband ever did any Act to gain a settlement in his own right subsequent to his Birth nor Where his Parents were settled.

Sworn this 16th day of Septembr. 1767 before Chris; Scott

Rachel Manger
