Middx. & Westmr.}  Judith Hart aged about 36 Years Upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 30th, day of April 1756, she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the House of her Brother Mr. Nathan Hart in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which is called by the Name of Phebe , Says that Mr. Hart Walleck who keeps Exeter Exchange Coffee house in the said Parish did beget the said Child on her body he having had carnal knowledge of her body several times at one of which times he got her with Child of said Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Hart Walleck and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn the 12th. May 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Judith Hart
