(not Sworn)  St Clement Danes October 21st 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin Salisbury (to wit) George Amor  singleman aged 30 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Henry Lenton  Miller & Malster of the parish of St. Martin in the City of Salisbury for about seven years at the Annual Wages of Ten Guineas and Lodged in his Master's House in the said parish of St Martin during such Service that he left that Service about five years ago and hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent & legal settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 21st. day of October 1793 before us}

George Amor
