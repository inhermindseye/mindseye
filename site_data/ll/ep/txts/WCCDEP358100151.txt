Middx & Westmr.} to wit Amy Hutchinson Aged about Thirty Eight years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Paisley in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Nine Guineas hath quitted said Service about Six months ago since which time she has gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 29th. Septr. last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Hospital in Store Street in Tottenham Court Road which hath since been baptized George and is now become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further says that one Edwd. Wright a Journeyman Shoemaker who lodges at the sign of the three Masons in Greek Street  Ruthbone Place is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his the said Edwd. Wright s lodgings sometime in the month of December and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as Aforesaid And that the said Edward Wright and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 12th. day of Novr. 1783  Wm Kitchiner

her Amy Hutchinson mark
