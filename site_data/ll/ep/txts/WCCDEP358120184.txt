Middx  Ann Vaughan upon Oath saith she was married at the Parish Church of Alhallows in Thames Street on the 12th. Febry 1749 to her late husband Joseph Vaughan decd. by whom she has three Children Eleanr . aged upwds. of three Years Henry abt. two Years and Esther one Month that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. late husband lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Roach at the Lamb Inn behind St Clements in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of four pounds for one Year & upwards and was during all the time of his sd. Service a Singleman And that her sd husband never after he left Mr Roach's Service rented ten Pounds a year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief and that she has not since her sd. late husbands decease rented ten pounds by the year nor paid Parish taxes

Sworn 22d. Augt. 1754 before} Ann Vaughn
