Westmr  Sarah Burn Aged about Thirty Six Years upon her Oath Says She never was Married Rented a Tenement of Ten pounds by the Year paid Taxes nor ever was an Apprentice or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one year Says her Father Patrick Burn who was by Trade a Taylor Rented and lived in a House in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Forty pounds or thereabouts for fifteen Years and upwards and quitted the same about Twenty five Years and dyed soon afterwards

Sworn the 11th Day of Novr 1768 Before} J Fielding

her Mary Burn Mark
