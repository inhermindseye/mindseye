Middx  Mathew Haley upon Oath saith he was born in the Parish of Tulleigh Halin in the County of Loughit in Ireland where his father rented a Farm & paid Scot & Lot and that he never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn 15th. Janry 1750 before
