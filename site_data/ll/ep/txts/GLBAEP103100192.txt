Middlesex ss}  Martha Murphy the wife of Richard Murphy maketh oath That her Said husband is lately gone away from her to places unknown & left her destitute of Necessaries And this Depont. Saith that divers years ago her Said husband rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Fourteen Pounds in Old Ford in the Parish of St Mary le Bow in the County of Middlesex and Continued in the occupation of such house for a year & upwards & by Means thereof as this Depont. is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge or belief her Said husband hath not since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor served any Parish office nor paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 14th day of December 1769 before  Chris: Scott  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Martha Murphy
