Middlesex ss}  Rebecca Ball the widow of John Ball deced maketh oath That her sd late husband was by Birth an American & to her knowledge & belief never in any Act to gain a settlement in Great Britain That he died at Leghorn about three years ago as She has been Credibly informed & Verily believes That this Deponts late Father Samuel Spittle deced heretofore rented a house in Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere That she this Depont lived with him there as part of his Family & never did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 28. day of February 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Rebecca Ball
