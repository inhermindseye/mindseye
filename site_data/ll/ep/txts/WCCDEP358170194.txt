Middx  Eliz: Fortune  Wido . of Thos. Fortune passed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . by Order bearing Date the 28th. Day of December Instant upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with her sd. late Husband Thos. Fortune at the Parish Church of St. James within the Liberty of Westmr . aforesd. & that her sd. late Husband was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Timothy Fortune in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Upholder that her sd. late Husband in his Life Time nor this Examinant since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintaine the same when born & that one Saml. Hepwell did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body she at such Time liveing with the sd. Saml. Hepwell as his Wife at the Thatch'd House in the Strand for the Space of Five Months or thereabouts & that he the sd. Saml. Hipwell & no other Person is the true & Reall Father thereof

Sworne the 29th. Day of Decr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Eliz: Fortune
