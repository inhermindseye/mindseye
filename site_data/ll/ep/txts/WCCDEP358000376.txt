Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr} to wit Ann  Millot aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Smith in Gloucester Street in the Parish of Saint George th Martyr in the County of Middx for 13 Months at the yearly Wages of £7 hath quitted said Service about 2 years ago since which time she has not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Setts

Sworn this 11th. of Novr. 1786 D Walker Rups. Clarke

Ann Millot
