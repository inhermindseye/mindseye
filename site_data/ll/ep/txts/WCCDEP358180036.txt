Middx  John Whillshear aged about 21 Years upon his Oath saith that his Father Wm. Whillshear Served his Apprentiship with one Mr. Dolton in the said Parish of Watford in the County of Hertford  Linnen Weaver and after he was out of his said Apprentiship rented a House of about Six Pounds a Year in the same Parish and paid his Share towards all Parochial Taxes as this Examinant hath been Informed & beleives That he never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworn the 11th- Novr. 1742 before} Wm. Jones

John Willshear
