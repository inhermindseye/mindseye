Middx  Sarah Tarlington  Wife of Thos. Tarlington upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her sd. Husband on the Eighteenth of June 1736 at the Fleet  London & that her sd. Husband abt. Ten Years ago he being at that Time a Singleman and not having any Child or Children lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Agar Esqr in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of One Year & upwds. but at what Wages this Examinant can't tell that since he left his said Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her Husband absconds from her & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 30th. of Septr. 1741 before me}
