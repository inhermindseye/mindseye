Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Gass aged about 28 years now Chargeable to the parish of Saint Clement Danes upon her Oath saith that on or about the Sixth day of October 1790 she Intermarried with James Gass her present husband who has deserted her at the parish Church of Saint Margaret Westminster by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Wm. Jas aged about Nineteen Months That her said Husband Rented a Second floor unfurnished of a Mr. Buckstone in Great Marlborough Street in the parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds that he hath quitted said Apartments about Three years since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry. 1798 before us} M Addington Rupt: Clarke

Mary Gass
