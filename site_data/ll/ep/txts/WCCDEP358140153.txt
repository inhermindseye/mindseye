Middx.  Jane Wilson aged abt. 23 Years upon Oath says she is the wife of John Wilson ( ) with whom she intermarried at the fleet London on the fifth day of March 1753. says she has one Child by him Vizt. James aged abt. three Months says her said husband lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Fortune a Taylor in St. Catharines Lane in the Parish of St. Botolph with- -out Aldgate in the County of Middx. for two Years at the Yearly wages of Six Guineas for the first Year and eight Guineas for the Second Year he being all that time Unmarried and never Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says her said husband is now a Soldier in North America

Sworn the 24th. day of August 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Jane Wilson
