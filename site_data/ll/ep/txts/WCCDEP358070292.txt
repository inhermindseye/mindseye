Middx & Westr} to wit Samuel Passa Aged Upwards of Sixty Six Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Ann his present Wife at the Fleet London about Thirty Years Ago and by Whom he has only one Son now provided and that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Thomas Bennett a Staymaker in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented house or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th day of Sepr 1775 Before  David Walker

his Samuel Passa Mark
