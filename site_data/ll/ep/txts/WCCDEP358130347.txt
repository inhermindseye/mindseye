Middx  Mary Powell aged abt. 64 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Wm. Powell who hath been dead abt. three years says the sd. Wm. Powell Rent -ed a house near the College in the Parish of Sidbury in the City of Worcester of the yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds lived therein abt. fourteen years & quitted same abt. five years before his death & never after Rented ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes says she hath not since his death Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant or Intermarried with any Person

Sworn the 30th of May 1757 . before} Theodr Sydenham

The Mark of Mary Powell
