Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Woodward aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 27th.. day of March 1785 she Intermarried with John Woodward at the Parish Church of Saint Swithine in the City of Worcester by whom she hath Four Children living Vizt. Ann aged upwards of 12 years Jane aged upwards of Ten years Thomas aged upwards of Seven years and Sarah aged upwards of Two years That her said late Husband Rented and Resided in unfurnished Apartments at No. 6 Burleigh Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Four Months or thereabouts at the Weekly Rent of Four Shillings that he quitted one of the said Apartments about Six Months since which time he hath only paid Three Shillings per week and that this Deponent saith that her said Husband hath been dead about Three weeks

Sworn this 19th. day of July 1798 before me } J Reeves

her Elizth. Woodward mark

Witness A W Burbidge
