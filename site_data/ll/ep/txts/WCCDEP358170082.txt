Middx  Jas. Hopes upon his Oath saith that he rented a House in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds for the Space of Nine Years or thereabts- that he hath not since he left the sd. House rented any other pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath one Child named Elizth - aged 9 Years & upwards & being Poor is not able to maintaine himselfe & Child

Sworne the 5th- of July 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

James Hopes
