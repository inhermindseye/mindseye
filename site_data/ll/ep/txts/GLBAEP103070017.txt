Middx ss  James Goodman Maketh oath that about Eighteen Years Ago he lived a hired Servant for the Space of two Years & upwards with one Mr Emmerson & Co. Sugar Bakers of ye parish of St. John the Evangelist in the City and Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds a Year And that during Such his Servitude he was a Single Man & Unmarried And Saith that he hath not Since gained any Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a Wife Named Elizabeth

Sworn this 4 Day of Decr. 1750 before us  Richd: Ricards  Sam Gower

James Goodman his Mark
