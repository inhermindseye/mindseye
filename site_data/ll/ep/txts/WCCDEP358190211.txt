Middx & Westmr   Martha Davis aged about 25 Years Lodging in Carey Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Godolphin Edwards Esqr . in the P arish of St Chads in the Town of Shrewsbury for upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of £3 quitted the same about 5 Years ago and hath not since rented any House paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant for one whole year in any one place And further upon her voluntary Oath says she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the Same when Born and that one John Smith  Shopman to Mr. Clark  Woolen draper the Corner of Blackmoor Street in Drury Lane did beget such Child or Children he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in or about the Month of May 1761 at the house of the said Mr. Clarke where she was then a Servant and at several times since at one of which times he got on her Body the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant.

Sworn the 18th. January 1762 Before me} Fran: Bedwell

Matha Davies
