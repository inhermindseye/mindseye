Middx  Mary Paty aged about 65 Years upon Oath saith she is Widow of Edward Paty who has been Dead about Twenty Months to whom she was Married at the Parish Church of St. Botolph Bishopgate London about 43 Years ago That her said Husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. John Dry  Sawyer in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which Expired about a Year before his Intermarriage with this Examinant That he never kept House or paid Parish Taxes nor hath she this Examinant since his Death

Sworn the 28th day of May 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Paty
