Middlesex ss.  Ann Volem the Widow of David Volem decd. maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Botolph Aldgate London on or about Seventeenth Day of Feby. 1794 . that her said Husband was born in Ireland and never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in England, & that she has a Child named David aged about Three Years the lawful issue by her said late Husband And this Deponent further saith that before her said Marriage and when She was a Singlewoman she was hired as a Servant by the year to Mr . Wm. Franks of the Parish of Clapham in the County of Surry  School Master at the Yearly Wages of Nine Pounds and continued in his Service under such hiring for the space of Sixteen Months and never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent settlement (except her Marriage as aforesaid, And further saith that being Poor & unable to provide for herself & Child, She has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate Middlesex for Relief & has been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 10th Day of March 1798 before H: Reynell R Davies

her Ann Volem Mark
