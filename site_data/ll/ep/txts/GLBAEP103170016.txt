Middlesex ss  Adam Beale Father of the above named Examt. Alice Storey maketh oath that about three years & eleven Months ago his Son in Law Thomas Storey & husband of his Said Daughter Alice Storey Went to reside at Kingston in Jamaica That he this Deponent hath never Seen him Since nor doth he know or believe nor hath he ever been informed Whether the Said Thomas Storey hath ever been in any part of the Kingdoms of Great Britain or Ireland Since his departure from England as aforesaid nor doth this Deponent know whethr he is now dead or living That the last time this Examinant heard from him was by a Letter received from him & dated at Kingston in Jamaica in the Month of April one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy four in which Province this Depont hath been lately informed he the Said Thomas Storey Still resides.

Sworn this 14th day of October 1776 before  Jno Sherwood

Adam Beale
