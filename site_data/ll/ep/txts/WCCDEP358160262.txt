Middx The Examination of Sarah ye. Wife of Bryan Philpott Passed from ye. Parish of St. Sepulchres  Londn . to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. County of Middx taken the 25th. Day of Febry 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her Husband Bryan Philpott served  five Years or thereabouts the latter part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. Danl. Stevens in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes  Printer And this Examint. further on her Oath saith yt. during the last  six Months or thereabts. she this Examint. with her Husband Lodged in Brewers Yard in Shoe Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn  Londn . & that the sd. Bryan Philpott never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife yt. her sd. Husband now absconds from her & haveing two Childn. (to wit) Richd . aged two Years & Edwd . aged Six Months and she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe and them

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

Sarah Philpot
