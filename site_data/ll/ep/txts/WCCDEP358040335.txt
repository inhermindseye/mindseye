Westmr.  Eliz: Lane Wife of Thos. Lane (gone from her) upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Thos. Lane at St. Patricks Church in Dublin abt. 16 Years ago Says her sd. husbd abt. 3 Yrs. since lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds in the Court now called Dukes Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westr . for 2 Yrs. or thereabts. & has not since he left the sd. house rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 16th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones .

The mark of Eliz Lane
