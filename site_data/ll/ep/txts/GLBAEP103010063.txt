Middlesex ss.  Elizabeth Brown  Singlewoman Maketh Oath that about Eleven Years ago she was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the Year with Mrs. Musgrove who then kept the Cock & Lion at Lady Parsons's Stairs in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings and continued in such Service under such hiring about Eighteen Months that she has not since she quitted her Service lived as a hired Servant by the Year with any Person in any Parish or Place for the space of One Year together or done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that she never was Married.

Sworn this Day of July 1791 before}
