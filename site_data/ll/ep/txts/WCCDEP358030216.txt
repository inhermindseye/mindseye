Middx  Cathn. Brown  Wido . of Wm. Brown Removed from the Parish of St. Bridgett London by Order dated the 16th. Day of Septr. Instant upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Wm. Brown at the parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx abt. 16 Yrs. ago Says her sd. late Husband Served his Apprenticesp with Mr. Pickett a Tinmar in Vere Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or other wise gained a Settlemt. in any other place Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlmt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 17th. Sepr. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The mark of Cath. Brown
