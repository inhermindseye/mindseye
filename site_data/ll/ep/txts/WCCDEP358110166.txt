Westmr  Eliz Jones aged upwds. of 70 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Hitchcock at New Inn Coffee house in Witch Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of £4 or four Guineas quitted her sd. Service near 40 Years ago & has not since rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Febry 3d. 1753 before}
