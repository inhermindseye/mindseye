Middx  James Stanfeild aged Sixty Years & upwards upon his Oath saith that he lived in & rented a House in Little Wild Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the Country of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds for the Space of Seven Years & upwards & hath left the same about Five Years that since he left the Sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworn the 2d. of Febry. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of James Stanfeild
