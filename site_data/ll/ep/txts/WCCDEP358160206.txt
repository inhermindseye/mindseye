Middx  The Exaicon of Elizth. Williams  Widow of Saml Williams lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 6th. Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. late husband Saml. Williams served 4 Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Hugh Powell  Taylor in Portugall Street in the sd Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & soon after her sd. late husband was out of his Apprentiship he rented a House in Pettiford Yard in little Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Le' Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . & lived in the same for the Space of 4 Years or thereabts. of the Yearly Rent of £8: but is not certain whether her sd. late husband pd. Taxes for the same or no And further saith yt. her sd. Husband in his life time nor this Exaiant since his Death ever Rented any other House lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Exaiants knowledge or beleife & that she is Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Elizabeth Williams
