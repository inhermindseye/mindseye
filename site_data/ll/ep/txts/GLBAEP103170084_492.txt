Middlesex ss}  John Bartlett maketh oath That many years ago he rented a house in Baileys Place (now occupied by James Donneh in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & lived there in for year the Space of two years & during Such his Residence therein he was charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gaine a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Settlement else where.

Sworn this 25. day of June 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes  John Bartlett
