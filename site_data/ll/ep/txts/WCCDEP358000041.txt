Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Swears to St. C. D. (to wit) Sarah Lock Aged 28 Years or thereabout Upon her Oath saith That She never was Married, Rented a House or paid Taxes saith That She Lived as an Hurd Yearly with a Mr Laney a Taylor in Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for upwards of Twelve Months. That since She quitted her said Service She hath not done any other Act, Matter or thing whereby she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of her Knowledge, Information or belief and that She is Poor and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn this 9th. Day of May 1785 before Me  Jno Kitchiner

her Sarah Lock Mark
