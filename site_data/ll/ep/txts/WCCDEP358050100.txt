Middx  Joseph Thompson upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife Catherine abt. 27 Years ago in St Mary Gate Church near the City of York and that he was born in the Parish of Great Osborn in the County of York where his father lived in and rented a house several Years and pd. towards the poors Rate of the sd. Parish and that he this Examint. never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 9th. 1749 before H Fielding

Joseph Thompson
