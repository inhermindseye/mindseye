Middlesex to wit Mary Davis aged about Sixty one years on her Oath says she never was married says she Rented and lived in a House in Northumberland Passage in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middlesex for two years at the yearly Rent of twenty Pounds & paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House upwards of Eighteen Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of Octr. 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Mary Davis
