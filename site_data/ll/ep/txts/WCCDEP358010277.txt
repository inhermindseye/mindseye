Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Eleanor Harris aged about 60 years upon her Oath Saith that about 35 years ago She Intermarried with Adam Harris her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Chads in Shrewsbury that her said late Husband Rented a House at Islington in the Parish of Saint Mary at Islington for about Ten years at the yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds that she has one Child living vizt. Susannah aged about 27 years that her said husband Daughter never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or ever bound an Apprentice

Sworn this 16th day of March 1789  Sampn Wright N Bond

Elner Harris
