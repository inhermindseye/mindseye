Middlesex (to wit) Mary Hannah Harris aged thirty five Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to Francis Harris her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint John Westminister about fourteen Years ago, that her said Husband served his time as an Apprentice by Indenture duly executed to John Harris of Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Butcher and after be quitted his paid Uncle he never did any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge he gained any subsequent Settlement thus he died about four Years ago and this Examinant has not Since his Decease done any Act where by to gain any Settlement in her own Right-that she has only one Child by her said Husband namely Francis aged about thirteen years-that she is

poor and unbale to maintain herself 

Sworn this 9th June 1786 R Butler

Mary Hannah Harris
