Middx.  Fras: Rogers aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Haines in Surry Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of six Pounds and hath quitted said Service about thirty Years and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 20th. Octr. 1759 before}

The Mark of Fras: Rogers

Mr. Burthwte.
