Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit James Grewer aged about 32 years upon his Oath saith that on the 16th. day of Janry. 1766 he Intermarried with Sarah his present Wife at the Parish Church of St. Paul Covent Garden that he hath no Child living by  said  that he hath one Child living named Esther aged about 5 years by a former Wife that he this Ext. Rented and lived in a House in Nags head Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middle for 4 years & upwds. at the yearly Rent of 17 pounds hath quitted said House upwards of 2 years ago since which time he hath not Rented any Place of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of May 1787 .  James Grewer  Wm Kitchiner
