Elizth Senhouse

Saint Clement Danes 6th. Febry 1794.

Middlesex & Westminster  St Clement Danes  (to wit) Elizabeth Senhouse  Widow upon her Oath Saith That she is aged fifty eight Years, that She was Married to Peter Senhouse at the Parish Church of Saint John Westminster Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about fourteen years ago by Banns That her said Husband about thirteen years ago Rented a House in New Inn Parish Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the annual Rent of £20 That her said Husband hath died about Eight years ago without having gained any subsequent legal Settlement That she this Examinant hath not since his decease been married or in her own Right obtained any legal Settlement that she is now distressed and poor And totally incapable of maintaining herself without Assistance.

Sworn the 6th. day of February 1794 Before} Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Stenhouse Senhouse her Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
