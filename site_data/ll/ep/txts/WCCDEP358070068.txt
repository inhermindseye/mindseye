Westmr. to wit Mary Mogridge Aged about Thirty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Henry Mogridge (gone from her with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of of St. Botolph Bishop Gate about Twenty Years ago and by whom she hath several Children now living Three of whom she hath now with her Vizt. Amelia Aged about Seven Years Major John Aged about Six Years and George Aged about three Weeks Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Brazier a Gunsmith in Ratcliffe Broad Street in the Hamlett of Mile End Old Town in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex and after he was not of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry 1773 before Us R Butler 

her Mary Mogridge Mark

Witness E: Fields
