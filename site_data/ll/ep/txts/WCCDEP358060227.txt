Middx  Decr 13th. 1751

Jane Dabb upon Oath saith she never was married and that She lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Somerset in East Street near Red Lyon Square in the Parish of St. George the M in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £5 for one Year & upwards quitted her sd. Service about 8 or 9 Years ago & has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn the Day & year abovementioned before  Fran: Bedwell

Jane Dabb
