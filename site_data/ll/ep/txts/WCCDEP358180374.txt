Middx  Mary Mansfeild Removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds by Order dated the 23d. Day of July last upon her Oath saith she was born in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and that her Father Henry Mansfeild Served Seven Years Apprentiship with her Grandfather Henry Peirson in Witch Street in the same parish and never after his sd. Apprentiship nor hath this Examinant in her life time ever rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 7th 1745 before}
