Middx  The Examination of Mary Haslip  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 23d. Day of June 1739 Who upon her Oath saith her Father Wm. Haslip Deced abt. Seven Years ago Rented a House in Exeter Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £20 & upwards & lived in the same for the space of 5 Years & upwards And this Examint. further saith yt. her sd. Father after he removed from the aforementioned House never Rented any other House neither did this Examint. either before or Since the Death of her sd. Father Rent any House Lands or Tenemts. of the Yearly Rent of £10 live with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or as an Apprentice or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife that she is now Pregnant with an Illegimate Child or Childn. & by means thereof is uncapable of Providing for herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane J Poulson

The mark of Mary Haslip
