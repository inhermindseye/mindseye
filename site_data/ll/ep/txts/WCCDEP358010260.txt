Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William Treves aged about 30 years upon his Oath saith that he served Seven years Apprenticeship all but four Months with one James Duxton in Wells Mews Oxford Road in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex  Chimney Sweeper that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Janry. 1789 N Bond Sampn Wright

his William Treves Mark
