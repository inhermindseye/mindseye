Westmr. 

Juliana Mason wife of Wm. Mason (gone from her) removed with her Child Lydia aged about 8 Yrs. from the Parish of St. Brides London by Order dated the 24th. Instant upon Oath saith she Intermarry'd with the sd. Wm. Mason in the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes upwds. of 18 Yrs. ago and that her sd. husband was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Joshua Griffith a Goldsmith in Water Lane  Fleet Street with whom he Served about three Years & then was assigned over to Mr. Jno. Brown Junior a Shagreen Casemaker with whom he Served his time out and that she sd. Mr. Brown during the last Year of her husbands apprentiship lived in Clements Inn Passage in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr Says her sd. husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief and that about Seven Years ago she was removed with her sd. house and Child from the aforesd. Parish of St. Brides to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes

Sworn Augt. 25th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Juliana Mason
