Middx.  Ann Conway aged abt. 31 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with John Conway at the Fleet London the 29th. Octr. one thousand Seven hundred & forty Nine says her said Husband is a Parishioner legally Settled in the Parish of Ruthyn  in the County of Denbigh in North Wales as She hath been informed by him & Verily believes & that He non Rents & lives in a Houses there, in which he Carries on the Business of an Apothecary

Sworn the 11th. Sepr. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

Ann Conway
