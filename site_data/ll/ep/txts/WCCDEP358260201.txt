Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Eaton aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 13th. day of April 1789 she Intermarried with William Eaton her present Husband (who is now at Sea) at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields by whom she hath Two Children living vizt William aged about Three years and an half and Joseph aged about Twelve Months That her said Husband Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Gifford a Timber Merchant in the Little Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never to the best of this Examints. knowledge Information or belief whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of May 1797 before us } Edw Read N Bond
