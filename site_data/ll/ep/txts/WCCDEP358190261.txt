Middx  Hannh: Holt aged about 40 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Edwd. Holt to whom she Intermarried in the Year 1739 Says her late Husband rented a House in Wych Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx, for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards and hath Quitted the said House abt. Ten Years says she hath not since the Death of her said Husband rented any House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year and that she is poor & unable to maintain herself

Sworn Augt: 6th: 1762 Before } Fran: Bedwell

Hannah Holt
