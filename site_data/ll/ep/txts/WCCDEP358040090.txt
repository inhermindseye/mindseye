Middx Westmr.  Mary Footman on Behalf of John Martin aged about Fourteen Years upon Oath saith she sd. John Martin is the Son of John Martin Peruke maker by Esther his wife both deceased Says the sd. John Martin the Father Served the latter Part of his Apprentiship with a Peruke maker who lived in the Strand between the New Church & St. Clemts. Church in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex as she has been informed and beleives Says he never rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn 24th. Octr. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

Mary Footman
