Westmr.  Elizth. Parker Aged about Twenty One Years upon Oath Says she was Never Marryed that her Father John Parker as she has been Informed and which Information she Verily beleives to be true Rented a House in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Three Months at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted said House about Fourteen Years & never after Rented House or paid Taxes says she never was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year.

Sworn the 28th. of Novr. 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth. Parker
