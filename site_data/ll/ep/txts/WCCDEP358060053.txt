Middx  John O'Neal upon Oath saith he was born at Shanes Castle in the County of Antram in Ireland where his father wanted a Farm of Ten pounds by the Year and that he this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Jany. 14th. 1750 before W Yeilding

The mark of Jno. O'Neal
