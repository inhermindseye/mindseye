Middx & Westmr.}  Maria Counley aged about Thirty Seven Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Joseph Colney who has been dead about four Months with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Seven Years ago by whom she hath Two Children now living to wit Joseph Aged about five Years and John Aged about Three Years says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House the bottom of Milford Lane in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds Sixteen Shillings lived therein upwards of One Year and then her said Husband died but says she does not know whether he paid any Parish Taxes due for the same.

Sworn this 26th. day of July 1769 before me} 

Maria Counley
