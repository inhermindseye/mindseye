Middx  Elizabeth Bainbridge aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Doctor Brocklesby in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said service about three Months ago sine which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 26th. day of Augt last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath sine been baptized by the Name of William and is now Chargeable there to (she being poor and unable to Maintain the same) and that one William Crisp a Farrier in the Kings Mews is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his the said Crisp's Uncles House in Chandos Street some time in the Month of November last but the particular day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which time she got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said William Crisp and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 27th: day of Septr. 1779 before R Butler

her Elizth: Bainbridge Mark
