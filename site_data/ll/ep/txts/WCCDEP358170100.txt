Middx  David Doulier upon his Oath saith that by Indenture Dated the 5th- Day of June 1724 he was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Bedan in the Back of St. Clements in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminstr . and County of Middx  Glazier & that he hath not since his sd. Apprentiship rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or Beleife & being Sick & Poor is not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the 29th- Day of July 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

David Doulier
