Middx.  Ann Griffith aged abt. Thirty Years Wife of Joshua-Griffith upon Oath Says that abt. five Years ago. She Intermarried with her said Husband at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & Whilst he was a Single Man as She hath been informed and believes, lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Spice a Hosier  near Aldgate in the Parish of St. Catharine Creechurch Londn. for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds, Says on or abt. the 18th. Sepr. 1753. She this Examinant was removed with her Said Husband from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx to the sd. Parish of St. Catharine Creechurch London

Sworn the 8th. Decr. 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell  Theodr. Sydenham

Ann Griffith
