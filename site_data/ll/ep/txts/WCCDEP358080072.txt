Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Houseman Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Jocan Houseman who died the Twenty first day of January last and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes upwards of four Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living namely Jane Mary Houseman Aged upwards of Three Years and John Aged about Ten Weeks Says her said late Husband was a Native of Germany and never gained any Settlement in England either by Renting House paying taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year as her said late Husband has often informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true And this Examinant further says that before her Intermarriage with her said late Husband she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Stephen Pitta Book binder in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two Years or thereabouts as the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service on her said Marriage which is upwards of four Years ago since which time this Examinant or her said late Husband never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of August 1776 before R Butler

Mary Houseman
