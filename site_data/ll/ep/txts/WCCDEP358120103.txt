Middx  Hannah Dalton upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Saml. Dalton by whom she has one Child Cathn. aged near 8 Yrs. that her sd. late husband was an Irishman and never gained a Settlemt. in England and that she this Examinant was born in West Street in in the City of Westchester where her father lived in & rented a house as she has been informed and that she never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. nor ever rented ten pounds by the year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn May 14th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Hannh. Dalton
