Middx  Hamper Toocock removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds by Order dated the 13th. Day of Novr. Instant upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Thos. Miles  Victualler in Essex Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds and hath left his sd. Service upwards of Ten Years Says he hath not since he left the said Mr. Miles Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Novr. 17th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Hamper Twocock
