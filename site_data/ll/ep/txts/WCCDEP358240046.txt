Eleanor Barnwell

St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  This woman was not removed before she Died in the House  N 10 Mr Joshua Johnson one of the Overseers Appreared this day before Sr. Sampson Wright & Underlook that Eleanr Barnwell shod. not be Removed from St. Clement Danes parish to her own untill she was Recovered to that she cod. bel Removed with Safety,  Staines Middx  (to wit) James Talboys one of the Beadles of the said Parish of St Clement Danes on behalf of Eleanor Barnwell Maketh Oath and saith that she the said Eleanor Barnwell hath Informed this Deponent and which he Verily believes to be true that she is now of the Age of about 25 years that she is the Daughter of John Barnwell & Jane who his Wife that she knows not by Information or otherwise whether her said Father or Mother were intilled unto or acquired any legal Settlement that she the said Eleanor Barnwell hath never gained any Settlement in her own Right nor been Married that she was born in Staines in the Parish of Staines in the County of Middlesex that she was Maintained & be right up by at the Charge of the said Parish of Staines in the Workhouse there, for seven years or thereabouts her Father & Mother dying when She was very young & leaving her destitute & in Want And that she knows of no settlement she is intilled unto save as aforesaid by Birth in Staines And that she is Ill & very Infirm

Sworn the 22 day of Septr. 1791 before us} Sampr. Wright N Bond.

Jas. Talboys .
