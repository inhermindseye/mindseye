Middlesex ss}  John Charles maketh oath that divers Years ago he rented a house of Ten pounds a year in Baileys Place in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & paid Parish Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds nor Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of July 1775 . before B Camper

John Charles
