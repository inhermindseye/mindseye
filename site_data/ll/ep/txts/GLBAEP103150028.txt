Middlesex ss}  Ann Bullimore the Widow of Thomas Bullimore deced Maketh oath that Since the death of her said late husband she this Deponent was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages by & with one John Branham in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Six Years & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Depont has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or been Since Married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of March 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of Ann Bullimore
