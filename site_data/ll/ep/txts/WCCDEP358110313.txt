Middx.  Ann Ingram upon Oath saith she was married to Giles Ingram at the Fleet London abt. 6 yrs ago by whom she has one Child Sarah aged one year & 6 months and that she has been informed & believes her sd. husband (Whilst a Singleman ) lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Durant a Pastures near St. Martins Church in the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £ for one year & upwds. & has not since he quitted his sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Septr. 14th 1753 before}
