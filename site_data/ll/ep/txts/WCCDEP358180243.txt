Middx  Danl: Collings aged about 64 Years removed from the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. by Order dated the 30th. Day of October Instant upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with his Prest. Wife  Elizabeth at Trinity in Church in the City of Exeter about 32 Years since Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Henry Harms  Wire Drawer in Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship about 45 Years since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 31st. Octr. 1744 before} Wm: Jones .

Daniel Collins
