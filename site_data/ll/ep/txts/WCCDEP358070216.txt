Middlesex & Westmr.}  Mary Anthony Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. James Brownley a Smith in Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Ten Days ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one William Richardson a Journeyman Blacksmith who lately worked with her said Master and where he now lodges had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime the latter end of March last in her said Masters House but the particular day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Richardson and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 27th. Octr. 1774 before  Sampn Wright

Mary Anthony
