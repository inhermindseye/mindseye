Middlesex to wit Margaret King Aged about Twenty years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Vardina Taylor in White Horse Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that One William Rose who lately kept the Buffalo Tavern and who now lives near Bagnage Wells had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime the latter End of December at a Tavern Through Temple Bar but the name she does not now recollect and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Rose and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 29th. day of June 1778 before R. Butler

her Margaret King Mark
