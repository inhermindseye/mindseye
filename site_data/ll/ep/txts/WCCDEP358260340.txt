Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Daniel Kean aged about 27 years upon his Oath saith that he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That he was born in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 29th. day of Septr. 1798 before me }

his Daniel Kean Mark
