Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Mary Tweedie aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Tweedie her present husband about Twenty three Years ago at the Parish Church of Saint George the Martyr by whom she hath Three Children living Vizt. Alexander aged about 22 years and Mary aged about 15 years both provided for & Elizth . aged about 13 years unprovided for That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Five years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that he hath quitted said house about Four years since which time he hath not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that her said Husband has deserted her and refuses to Allow her any Support whereby she is now Chargeable to the said Parish

Sworn this 30th. day of Decr. 1797 before me} 

her Mary Tweedie Mark

Witness B Newman
