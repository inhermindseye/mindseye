Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Bagshaw  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers Years ago she was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Yearly Wages by & with James Wylde of Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx  Victualler & Continued in such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she this Depont has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year nor hath she ever been married nor hath she done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26. day of February 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizth. Bagshaw
