Middx.  Mary Tye aged abt. 38 Years upon Oath says she never was Married; says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Colehan  Apothecary in the Butcher Row in the  Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5. & hath quitted said Service abt. five Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 13th. of May 1758 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Tye
