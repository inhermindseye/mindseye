Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Mary Smith  Singlewoman  maketh oath that her Mother Mary Smith has informed her & which information this Deponent believes to be true that her late Father William Smith deced heretofore rented a Publick house in Lower East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & during such his residence therein was charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That she this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 21. day of Febry 1785 before J Hawley

Elis Mary Smith
