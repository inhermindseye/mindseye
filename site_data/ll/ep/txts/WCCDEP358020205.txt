Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Crowe aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Brown a Taylor who lived at Islington about Three years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds that the last Five Months of her said Service was in Ave Maria Lane in the Parish of Saint Gregory by Saint. Pauls in the City of London that she hath Quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath Not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months nor ever Rented any house Tenement on Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. May 1790 N Bond R. Tayler

her Sarah Crowe Mark
