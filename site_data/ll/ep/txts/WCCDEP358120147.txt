Middx  Eliz: Carr aged 32 Years upon her Oath Saith She is the Wido. of John Carr who dyed upwds. of 3 Yrs. since & to whom she was married at the Fleet London about thirteen Years ago and that her sd. late husband rented a house of then Yearly Rent of £6 in Duke Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes lived therein about two Years and kept the same at the time of his Death and this Examinant has not since rented ten pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn July 13th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizabeth Carr
