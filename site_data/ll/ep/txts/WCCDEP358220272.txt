Westmr. to wit Jane Finch Aged about forty Nine Years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one William Barrington  Esqr . in Little Ormond Street in the Parish in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds or thereabouts hath quitted her said Service about Eleven Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 21st. day of June 1771 before}  John Lane

her Jane Finch mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
