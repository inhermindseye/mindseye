Westmr.  Robert Wood aged about 43 Years upon Oath says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Jno. Twocock a Waterman in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , and served about four Years of His said Apprenticeship and then was press'd into his Majesty's Service, and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 18th, day of November 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

Robt. Wood
