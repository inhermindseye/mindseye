Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Ogilvy aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that about Thirteen years ago she Intermarried with Alexr. Ogilvy her late Husband (who has been dead about Eight years) at the Parish Church of Saint Johns Westmr . That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Charles Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years at the yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds

Sworn this 5th day of Janry. 1797 M Addington Rapt: Clarke

her Mary Ogilvy Mark
