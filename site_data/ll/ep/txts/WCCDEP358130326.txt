Middx  Cath: Cain wife of Terrence Cain upon Oath says she intermarried with her first husbd. Michl. Mc.gennis in the Parish of St. Pancras in the County of Middx on the 1st day of June abt. Eleven years ago says the sd. Michl. Mc. gennis dyed abt. five years ago & that the Child called Cath: Cain in a Certificate under the hands & Seals of the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. bearing even date herewith is a Child she had by the sd. Michl. Mc. gennis her first husband & that she had the Baptized Cath Cain says the said Cath Cain was born in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes where her father the said Michl. Mc. gennis Rented a

Back house in Howard Street in the sd. Parish of the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds & lived therein sevl. years & after he quitted sd. House never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 29th day of April 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Cath Cain
