Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Timothy Gaywood aged about 28 years now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upon his Oath saith that he Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one John Beziah a Copper Plate Printer that he served the last Three Months of his said Apprenticeship to the said John Beziah in Charterhouse Street in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre without in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Janry. 1798 before us M Addington R. Ford

his Timothy Gaywood Mark
