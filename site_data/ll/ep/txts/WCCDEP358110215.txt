Westmr.}  Eliz Kent aged abt. 21 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that She lived a yearly Servant with Mr King in Newcastle Street near the Fleet Market in the Parish of St. Sepulchers London for one Year & Six Months at the Yearly Wages of £5. and Quitted her sd Service abt. Seven Weeks since

Sworn March 26th 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane .

The Mark of Eliz Kent
