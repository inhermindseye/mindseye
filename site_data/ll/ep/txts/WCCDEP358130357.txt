Middx  Ann Goodman on the behalf of Gertrude Bannister aged abt. 50 Yrs. upon Oath says the said Gertrude Bannister is the Widow of Jospeh Bannister says the sd. Joseph Bannister abt. seventeen Yrs. ago Rented a house in Baldwins Gardens in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Bas in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of ten Pounds & lived in the same three Months & upwards & never after Rented ten Pounds a Yr. or pd. Parish Taxes to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 6th of June 1757 before} Theodr Sydenham

The Mark of Ann Goodman
