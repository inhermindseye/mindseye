Middlesex ss  Hannah Duffe  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was born in the Parish of St Clement in Ipswich in the County of Suffolk That she never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servt for the Space of a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 2 day of March 1785 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Hannah Duffe
