Middlesex ss.  Rebecca Watson  Singlewoman Maketh Oath that about Twenty Years ago her late Father Robert Watson deceased rented a House of Twenty Pounds a Year or thereabouts, in Burr Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , and died legally settled there, And that her late Mother Sarah Watson lived in the said House about Seven Years after his Death, that her said Mother after she quitted the said House never Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or did any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement That she this Deponent had never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any Parish or Place for the space of One Year together, or done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement in her own right, And that she never was Married

Sworn this Day of 1791 before}
