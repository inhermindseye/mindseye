Westmr. 

Ann Smith aged about 31 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about three Years since the lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Bullcock an Attorney at Law in great St. Bartholomews Close in the Parish of St Bartholomew the Great London at the Yearly wages of Five pounds for the Space of fifteen months or thereabouts and has not since the quitted her said Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 20th April 1750 before} Tho Lane

Ann Smith
