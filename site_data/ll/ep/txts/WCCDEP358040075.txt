Westmr.  Ann Potter  Widow of Thomas Potter upon Oath saith her sd. late husband has been dead near 12 Years Says about 8 Years ago being then a Widow she lived in and rented house in Carpentrs Court in Witch Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds for one Year and upwards Says she has not since she left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has one Child viz Joyce aged 11 Yrs. & upwds.

Sworn Octr. 5th. 1747 before Danl Gaell

The Mark of Ann Potter
