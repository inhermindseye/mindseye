Middlesex ss}  Thomas Chapman maketh oath that he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one Philip Daw of the parish of Oakham in the County of Surry  Farmer at the yearly Wages of Four pounds and Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or Upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a Wife named Mary & a Child named Mary aged about a year & half the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his Said wife to whom he was lawfy married.

Sworn this. 2. day of December 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Thomas Chapman
