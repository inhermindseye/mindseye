Ann Whitfield aged about seventy four Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Jos: Whitfield who died about 6 Months ago in the Workhouse belonging to St. Johnson the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry Says her said husband Rented and lived in a house in Tooley Street in the said parish of St. John at the yearly Rent of £10 as she bets. hath quitted said house about 8 Years ago and after quitted same to never gained any other subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her sd. husbands Death.

Sworn this 22d. day of May 1776 before  R. Butler Jno. Machin

her Ann Whitfield Mark
