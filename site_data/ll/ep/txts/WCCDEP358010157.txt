Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Eleanor Sweet aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever bound an apprentice that she has done no act in her own right to gain a Settlement that her Father & Mother Rented and lived in a House in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Six years & upwards at the yearly Rent of £10 & upwards that he hath quitted said House about 5 years since which time he has not Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year Paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Octr. 1787 R Buller

her Eleanor Sweet Mark
