Middx  Ann Courtney Aged about Thirty Two Years now living at No. 46 in Shire Lane on behalf of Ann her Daughter an Infant Aged upwards of Ten Years by John Morey the Younger her Father on her Oath Says that the said Courtney this Examinants Daughter was born a Bastard in Dyott Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields  in the County of Middlesex And Says that this Examint is poor & unable to maintain the said Child and that her said Child has done no Act whereby to gain a Settlemt otherwise than by Birth

Sworn this 15th. day of Janry. 1783 before us R Butler. Sampn Wright

Ann Courtney
