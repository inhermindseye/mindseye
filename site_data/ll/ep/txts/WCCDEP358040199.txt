Middx  Mary Martin Wife of John Martin (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. John Martin in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on or about the 19th. of Octr. 1739 Says her sd. husband was by Indenture dated the 21st. June 1721 bound Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Creamer a Fishmonger near Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid Says after the Expiration of his Apprentiship her sd. husband as she has been Informed and beleives was hired to and lived with Mr. Foskew a Fishmonger over agt. the Vine Tavern in Holbn. in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds Middx and lived with him upwards of her Years at the Wages of £18 by the Year but was paid weekly and beleives he lodged during his sd. Service with his Aunt Mrs. Glew in Bear Yard Says since he left the sd. Mr. Foskew which is abt. 10 Yrs. ago her sd. late husband has not rented a house of ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has one Childing Thos. aged abt. Seven Years

Sworn June 10th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Martin
