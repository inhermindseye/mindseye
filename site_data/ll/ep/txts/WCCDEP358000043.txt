Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  C Swears to St. C. D. to wit Mary Drew aged 25 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Noon of Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the C-of Middx for 18 Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of £4-10-0 hath quitted said Service about Seven Years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 10th day of May 1785 R Butler

her Mary Dunn Drew mark
