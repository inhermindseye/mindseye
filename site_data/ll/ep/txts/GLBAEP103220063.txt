Middlesex ss}  Mary Fairlam a Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a Yearly hired Servant by & with Mr Willis a Merchant in Savage Gardens in the Parish of St Olave in Hart Street in the City of London at Certain Yearly Wages and Continued in such service there under such hiring for the Space of Eighteen Months That She has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath she done Any Act Since such her servitude as aforesaid to gain a Settlement for herself elsewhere.

Sworn this. 7. day of August 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Mary Fairlam
