Westmr.  Ann Hooper aged 66 Years upon Oath says she is the widow of William Hooper who has been dead about Twenty six Years; says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Perkins in Red lion Square in the Parish of St. George the Martyr by Queen Square in the County of Middlesex for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds, Quitted said Service about twelve Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn the 11th, day of June 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant W Kelynge

Ann Hooper
