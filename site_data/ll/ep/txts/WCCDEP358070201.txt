Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Besford Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Francis Besford Rented and lived in a house in Stanhope Street Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds & paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted sd House upwards of Twenty Two Years ago & hath not since gained any or subseqt Settlemt. And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one George Denejean at a Midshipman at Mrs. Denejeys in George Street York Buildings had Carnal knowledge of her Body at a House and as she believes at the Bagnio in St. Martins Lane the latter end of December last but the Particular Day she does not now remember at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said George Denejean and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this Twenty Seventh day of July 1774 before  William Addington

her Jane Besford Mark
