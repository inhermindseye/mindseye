Middx  Ann Smith aged about 33 years upon Oath saith she was married to John Smith at the Fleet London about Sixteen Years ago that upwards of Six years ago the sd. John Smith went to Sea and (as this Examint. believes) dyed abroad she not having seen or heard from him since that the sd. Smith informed her he Served his apprentiship with Mr Beard a Butcher in Hollis Strt. Claremarket in the parish of St Clemt: Danes within the Liberty of Westmr in the County of Middx and never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year which Information this Examinant believes to be true And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of St Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that Anthony Webb a Shag Weaver in Old Nichol Street Shoreditch did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body this Examinant having constantly Cohabited with the sd. Anthy. Webb as his wife for Ten Years past and that he had carnal Knowledge of her body Several times in the Months

March April & May last at one of which times he got her with Child of the bastard Child or Children with which She is now pregnant and that the said Anthy. Webb and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Octr. 31st. 1754 before}
