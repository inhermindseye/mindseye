Sarah  Jury and Child named Nathaniel Simpson

(After Birth Bastardy)

Saint Clement Danes July 16th 1794

Middlesex & Westminster }  Child born 16th June 91 in St CD  Named Nathl. Simpson }  Reputed Father} to wit- Sarah Jury  Singlewoman aged twenty nine Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married that on the Sixteenth Day of June 1791 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Shaw in Stanhope Street Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex who is still living and hath been Christened by the Name of Nathaniel Simpson -and this Deponent further saith that Nathaniel Simpson who lives in Tooks Court Cursitor Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the City of London who is a Clerk in the South Sea House is the real and true father of such Child whereof she was as delivered as aforesaid he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and further this Examinant saith that she is unable to maintain such Child without Assistance

Sworn this 16th Day of July 1794 before } Wm. Kitchiner

S Jury
