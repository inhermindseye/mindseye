Middlesex ss.  Sarah Maile the Widow of Henry Maile decd. maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Dunstan Stepney about Twenty One Years ago, that she can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her said Husband or his Father or Mother, that she this Deponent was born at Kensington Gravel Pits in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middlesex & has never lived as a hired Servant by the year or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right except her Marriage as aforesd. that she has Three Children Namely Henry aged about Sixteen Years who now lives Servant with Mr. Hedges in Artichoke Lane in the Parish of St. George Middlesex & has lived with him about Two Years Absalom aged about Twelve Years & Sarah her said late Husband

Sworn this Day of 1798 before}
