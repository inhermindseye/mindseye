Middx  Ann Insgo aged upwds. of 23 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the parish of St. Clement Danes where she now lodges she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Charles Silleter now Porter to Mr Burghall of the sd. parish Cheesemonger did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the said Charles Silleter having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinant several times in the Months of September and October last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. bastard Child or Children  with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. Charles Silleter & no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Febry 22d. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Insgo

Memd. the above named C. Silleter sev his time wth. Jno Wheathers of Bastards one Staffd Shire  Butcher
