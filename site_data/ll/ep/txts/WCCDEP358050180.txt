Westmr.  Jas. Perry upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Mary at Step Church upwds. of 20 Yrs. since by whom he hath she Son John aged about Sixteen Years who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. Says he this Ext. was by Indre dated 3d. Augt. 1719 bound Apprentice to and Served Seven years Apprentiship with Nichs. Le Secur a Fanmaker at the Corner of Rocindeer Yard in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 23d. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Jas. Parry
