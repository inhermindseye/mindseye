Cathn. Underwood upon Oath saith she was married about four Years ago at Cheshunt Church in the County of Hertford to her husband Rd Underwood and that the sd Rd. Underwood rented the Cross Keys Alehouse in Cheshunt afsd. lived therein Sevl. Years & Served the Office of Constable in the sd. Parish and that he quitted the sd house abt. Six Months ago

Sworn June 26th. 1754 before}
