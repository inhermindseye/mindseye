Middx  Jas. Hawkins aged abt. 46 Years upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Wainsmore a Baker opposite Arundel Street in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds ten Shillings for one Year and upwards & was all that time a Singleman & that he hath not since he left the said Mr Wainsmores Service(which is abt. 23 Years ago) Rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 20th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

James Hawkins
