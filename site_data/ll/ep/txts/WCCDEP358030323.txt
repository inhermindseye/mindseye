Westmr. 

James Phipps upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife  Elizabeth upwards of 17 Yrs. ago at the fleet London Says he was born in the Parish of Chiswick in the County of Middx and that his father James Phipps deceased lived in & rented a house at Turnham Green in the sd. Parish of Chiswick of the Yearly Rent of £12 for sevll. Years & there dyed Says he this Examinant never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 14th. 1746 before}

James Phipps
