Middlesex ss  Caroline Blood  Singlewoman , maketh oath that her Father matthew Blood who is now absent from her in Ireland lately rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in the Parish of Saint Mary Islington in the Said County & was Charged to and Paid Poors rates for the Same She this Depont living with him in the Said house during the time here Sided therein which was for the Space of one Year & half when he went from thence to Ireland That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in her own right

Sworn this. 24. day of October 1787 before  R Davies  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Caroline Blood
