Middx  Mary Marshall Aged about Sixty years On her Oath says She is the Widow of Joseph Marshall with whom She Intermarr at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square in the year 1745 And who has been dead about Ten years And by whom She hath three Children now Living all of whom are now provided for Says her said Late Husband Rented and Lived in a House in Little George Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Three years at the yearly Rent of Thirty Six Pounds And paid Parish Taxes due for the Same hath quitted the said House about Thirty Three years ago since which time Her said Husband never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either  or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 9th. day of Octr. 1781 before me  Wm Kitchiner M Addington

Mary Marshall .
