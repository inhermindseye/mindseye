Middlesex Westmr. to wit Mary Nicholls aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath Says that she has gone by the Name of Mary Ashby for several years past on Account of her Cohabiting with a Man Whose Name was Robert Ashby but that she never was married to him and that he has been dead for upwards of Ten years Says that after his Death she Rented and lived in a House under the Piazza Covent Garden upwards of Six Months at the Yearly Rent of forty five Guineas P Annum that she lived therein upwards of Six Months and paid half a years Rent for the same but never paid any poors Rate or other Taxes to her knowledge or beliefe hath quitted the said Houses about five years ago since which time she hath gained no other subseqt. settlemt. either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 24th. day of June 1778 before  Abrm. Baley  John Cox .

Mary Nickolls
