Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Job Nichols aged about 40 years upon his Oath saith that on the 16th. day of December 1770 he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn by whom he hath 4 Children living vizt. Job aged upwards of 16 years Elizth. aged upwards of 8 years Thos. aged upwards of Three years and William aged about 12 Months that he this Examint. Rented and lived in a House in Robinhood Court Shoe Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Barrs in the City of London for 2 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas hath quitted said House about 7 years ago since which time he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of May 1787  Sampr Wright  Tho. Vaughan Job Nickals
