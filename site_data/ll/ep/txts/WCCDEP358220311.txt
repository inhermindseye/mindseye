Westmr. to wit Ann Wilson aged about Sixty One Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Wilson who has been dead about Sixteen Years as she has been Informed and Verily believes and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Chalfont about 38 Years ago Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Chancery Lane in the Parish of Saint Dunstans in the West London upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about sixteen Years ago and after quitting the Same neither her said Husband nor this Examinant hath done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent

Sworn this 31st. day of Decr. 1771 before} R. Butler Tho Kynaston

Ann Wilson
