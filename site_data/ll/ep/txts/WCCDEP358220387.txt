Westmr. to wit Tompson Davis Aged about Twenty Years on behalf of his Father Thompson Davis who now lies ill on his Oath Says that his said Father has four Children now living by his late Mother Martha Tompson lately deceased Vizth. this Examinant who never gained any Settlement either by Renting House or serving any Apprenticeship or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further say that his said Father has Three other Children unprovided for Namely Elizabeth Aged about Ten Years Thomas Aged about Seven Years & Martha aged about five Years Says his said Father Rented and lived in a House on Holbourn Hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden in Ely Rents in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted his said House about four Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any or Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement as his said Father has often Informed him & believes the same to be true

Sworn this 24th. day of Septr. 1772 before W Kelyngs Tho. Kynaston

Tompson Davis
