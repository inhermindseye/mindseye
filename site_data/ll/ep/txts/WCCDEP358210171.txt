Middx. & Westmr.}  Elizabeth Calvert aged upwards of 22 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Dawson Linnen draper in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5 for the first year and £6 a year afterwards, quitted said Service about two Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born; & that Thomas Young late a Servant to Mrs. Willoughby at Knightsbridge did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her Body, he having had carnal knowledge of her body in the Month of December last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the said Thos. Young & no other person did beget the same

Sworn the 7th. July 1767 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth Calvert
