Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Judith Burnett aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that about 5 years ago she Intermarried with Robert Burnett her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields that her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to one William Meredith a Shoemaker in Orange Street in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex  out of the Charity School belonging to the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields for Seven years that about 5 years after he was bound his said Master removed from Orange Street aforesaid to the Corner of Gilbert Street in Compton Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex  that this Examinants husband went with his said Master to Gilbert Street aforesaid and served the Remainder of his said Term of Seven years that after her said Husband was

out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1788 }
