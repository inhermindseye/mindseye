Middx.  Ann Boucher aged upwards of 70 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of James Boucher who has been dead about Eighteen Years says her said husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England And that she this Examinant served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr Bullock a Button and loop maker in King Street Westminster in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westminster and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn Decr. 23d. 1760 . before } Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Boucher
