Middx  Wm. Anderson Aged about Sixty Years upon Oath Says he Rented a House in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Three Years & upwards at the Yearly Rent of Nineteen Pounds hath quitted said House about Three Years & hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 7th of February 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox

Wm. Anderson
