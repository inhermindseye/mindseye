Middx & Westmr.  Jane Slingsby aged about 31 Years upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married that on the 22d, day of September 1765 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Darby in Grange Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized Thomas ; says that Thomas Essex  Coachman to the Earl of Macclesfield in Harley Street Cavendish Square had carnal knowledge of her body the beginning of January 1765, & at sevl. other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Thomas Essex and no other person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 29th. of April 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

Jane Slingsby
