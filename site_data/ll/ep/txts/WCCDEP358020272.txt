St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Thomas Thornton Aged 28 Years or thereabouts on Oath saith That he lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Craigs Craig a Pipe Borer in the parish of Battersea in the County of Surry at the Yearly Wages of £8 or Eight Guin for Arear Five Years his Wages raised almost every Year that he Ae agreed for & Reced the Wages of £8 or Eight Gunieas for the last Year he so lived with said Mr. Craig that he quitted the said Service about Nine Years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing Whatsoever whereby to the best of his knowledge or belief he might have acquired any Subseq other Settlement

Sworn before Me 21st. April 1791 R Butler

Thomas Thornton 's Mark
