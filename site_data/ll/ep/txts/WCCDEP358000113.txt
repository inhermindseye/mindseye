Saint Clement Danes Septr. 26th. 1785 .

Middlesex 

passd. to St. Nichs. Acons (to wit) Susannah Hicks aged twenty years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was married to Stephen Hicks her now Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about one year ago, that her said Husband has informed this Examinant that he served an Apprenticeship to Messrs. Green and Chambers Smith is in Lambard Street in the Parish of Saint Nicholas Acons. in the City of London and that he has not to this Examinant Knowledge gained any Subsequent Settlement, that her said Husband is now at Sea, and this Examinant is poor and Lichs and unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 26th Septr. 1785 before  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordon

the Mark of Susannah Hicks
