Middlesex ss  Ann Lawson the wife of John Lawson Mariner  maketh oath that her Sd husband is a Seafaring man & now on board the goodship Called the Princes orange Captn Christie Commr now on a Voyage to Hew castle for Coals That her Said husband who lately left her in a necessitous Condition and altogether destitute of Support Some years ago rented a house of Eight pounds a year in Flushing Yard in the Parish of St Botolph witht aldgate in the Sd County & was charged to & paid poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 14. day of December 1776 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Ann Lawson
