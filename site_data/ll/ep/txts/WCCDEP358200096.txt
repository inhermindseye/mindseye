Westmr  Wm Legg Aged about Forty four Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Elizth. at Mayperton in the County of Dorset about Ten Years ago Says that whilst he was a Singleman and without Child or Children he Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Thomas Staniford  Esqr . in Grange Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and hath quitted his said Service about Twenty One Years Says he hath not Since he quitted his said Service Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says he has three Children by his said Wife (to wit) Joseph Aged about 9 Years Job Aged about Seven Years & Solomon Aged about five Years Says he was Discharged Mr Stanifords Service at Portsmouth in the County of Southampton

Sworn March ye 27th 1764 Before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Wm. Legg

Upon Mr Stiles Complt & Wn J. W
