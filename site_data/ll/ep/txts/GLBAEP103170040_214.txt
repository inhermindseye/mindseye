Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Davis of the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the peace in & for the Sd County this 4. day of January 1777 who Saith that She is now with Child & that the Sd Child is likely to be born a Bastard & to be Chargeable to the Sd parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate And that one Mountague Wale of the Parish of St John Clerkenwell in the Sd County Bricklayer Labourer is the Father of the Said Child.

Taken & Signed the day and year above written before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Davis
