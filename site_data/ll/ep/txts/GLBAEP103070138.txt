Middx ss The Examination of Elizabeth Dennis Singlewoman taken on oath before us from of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the County of Middx residing in or Near the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx this 22 day of July 1755

This Examinant on her oath says that about Ten Months ago one William Meekings a Carman who then kept a house in Slaters Court in Blue Anchor Yard in the Parish of St. Mary White chappell in the sd County did Prevail with this Examinant to suffer him to lye with her at his said house where this Examinant then lived And the said William Meekings had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and so had several times whereby She Conceived with Child of a Male Bastard Child of which She was delivered at the workhouse belonging to the sd Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate on the Twenty Eighth day of June last which said Male

Bastard Child hath been baptised by the Name of William And hath been now is and still is likely to be Chargeable to the said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate this Examinant being poor & not able to provide for the same And this Examinant farther saith that the said William Meekings is the true & only father of the sd Female Bastard Child And that this Examination is voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 22 day of July 1755 before us  Richd. Ricards  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Eliz: Dennis
