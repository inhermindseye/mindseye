Middx  Grace West aged about 39 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant to Mr. Harding the Back of St. Clement's in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Thirty Shillings Quitted the Same about 20 Years ago and hath not since done any Act whereby to gain a Settlement to the best of her knowledge

Sworn the 21st. day of October 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Grace West
