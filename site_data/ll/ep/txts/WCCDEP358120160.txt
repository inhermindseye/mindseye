Middx  Sarah Jones aged near 70 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Thos Jones and that her sd. late husband rented a house and Land in the Parish of Abberbury in the County of Salop of the yearly Rent of Sixty Seven Pounds lived therein many years & there dyed about four Years ago and this Examinant has not since her husbands decease rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant

Sworn July 22d 1754 before}
