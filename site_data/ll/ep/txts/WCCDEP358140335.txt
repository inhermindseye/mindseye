Middx.  Mary Stuart aged abt. 31 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bromfield at the Thistle and Crown near Charing Cross in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Libty of Westmr. for Seventeen Months at the Yearly Wages of £4 and hath quitted said Service abt. Two Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 7th. day of Janry. 1760 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Mary Stuart
