Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Orme Aged about forty Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Orme who has been dead about Three Weeks Says that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in New Castle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr for Six Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Two pounds hath quitted her said House about Three Years ago and after he quitted the same he never after Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year
