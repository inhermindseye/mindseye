Westmr. 

Rt. Thomas upon Oath saith he intermarried with his present Wife Mary at the Fleet London about five or Six Years since and that upwds. of 20 Years ago (and whilst a Singleman ) he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. David Avery Wine merchant in Adams Court in Bread Street in the Parish of St. Peter le Poor London at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds for one Year & upwards Says he has not since he quitted his sd. Service rented ten pounds Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 24th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Robt Thams
