Westmr.  Mary White Aged about Twenty Seven Years Upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says that on the Sixth Day of May 1764 she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at Mr. Kings in Starr Court Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Hermiena Says that One James Cummins a Boot Closer had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Months of July and August last and at several other Times since at One of which times he got her Child of the said Female Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid And this Examinant further Sayth that she was Born in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx and never Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Says she Cannot Give any Account of the Settlement of her Father or Mother other that she has been Informed that they was Born in the Bishoprick of Durham but in what Part this Examinant Cannot say.

Sworn the 14th of July 1764 Before}

The Mark of Mary White
