Middlesex ss  John Burdon maketh oath that he lately rented a house in St Catherines Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same And hath not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlements

Sworn this 3. day of August 1787 before  R Davies

The mark of John Burdon
