Jane Nichols aged abt. 59 Years Wido . of Hugh Nichols upon her Oath saith that on the 11th. Decr. abt. 38 Years since she intermarried with her Sd. late Husband Hugh Nichols who hath been Dead abt. 18 Yrs. that her sd. late Husband lived in & rented a House in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £28. for the Space of Seventeen Years & there Dyed & this Examinants Continued to live in & Rent the same House after his Decease for the Space of One Year and upwards & hath left the same abt. Fourteen Yrs. that this Examint. hath not since She left the Sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of 1 Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 8th. Day of May 1742 before} Jas: Gronous

Jane Nichols
