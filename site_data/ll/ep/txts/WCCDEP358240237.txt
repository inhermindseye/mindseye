St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Jane Chri Christopher  singlewoman aged 45 years or thereabouts a lodger at Mr. L a House of Mr Cox's situate in Clare Market in the parish of St Clement Danes Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Dr. Eglam a lodger at Mr Cornwall in Stanhope Street for 3 years and an half or thereabts. at the annual Wages of £5 that she quitted that Service about 20 years ago and hath never since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year lived as an hired yearly Servant or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the 20th day of March 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Jane Christopher's Mark

Witness Garrett
