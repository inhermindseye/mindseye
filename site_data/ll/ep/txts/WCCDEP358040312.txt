Middx  Frances Beadle aged about 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about five Years ago she Lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Bowers in Howard Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for two Years and upwards and has not since she Quitted her sd. Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 12th 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Fana bell Frances Beadle
