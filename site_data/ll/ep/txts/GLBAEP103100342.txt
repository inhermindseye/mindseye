Middlesex ss  Elizabeth German the Wife of John German (who is on board as a Sailor in one of his Majesties Ships of War) maketh oath that her Said husband has informed her that he was born Apprentice to Mr Coulson of Scarborough in the County of York  House Carpenter & Served Five Years of his said Apprenticeship there with the said Mr Coulson & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Deponent Saith that to her knowledge or belief her Said husband has not since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that she hath a Child named John aged about four Months the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her Said husband

Sworn this 7th. day of Novr. 1770 before  Chris. Scott

The Mark of Elizabeth German
