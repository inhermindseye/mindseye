Middx  The Examincon of Martha the Wife of Josiah Pearson Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 21st. Day Janry 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. on the 22d. April 1739 she Intermaried with the above Named Josiah Pearson & yt. her sd. Husband before his Intermariage with this Examint. & whilst he was a Singleman lived with his Brother Saml. Pearson in Ryders Court in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr . Cordwainer for the space of Eleven Years or thereabouts and for such his Service during yt. time had Meat. Drink Washing Lodging & Cloaths, And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that her said Husband Josiah Pearson hath not since he left his said Brother's Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That her said Husband is now a Marine in Colonel Hanmores Regiment of Foot and haveing one Child Named Henry aged about Ten Months & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Child

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mart: Pearson
