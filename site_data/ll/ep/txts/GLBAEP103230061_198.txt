Middlesex ss  Ann Williams maketh oath that about five Years ago She was lawfully married to one John Williams who went from & left her & She has not Seen him for the Space of four years last past & does not know whether he is dead or living nor Can She give any Account respecting his Place of Settlement That She this Deponent was born in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hire Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She may have Acquired by ger Marriage as aforesd and that She is the Daughter of John Clayton a Shoemakr who is now living in the Same Parish & there legally Settled

Sworn this Sixth day of July 1787 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Williams
