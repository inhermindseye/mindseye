Middx 

Eliza, Peters  widow of Samuel Peters who dyed abt. three years since upon Oath saith she intermarryed with the said Samuel Peters abt.

and that her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr.  a Waterm at the Sign of the in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlx and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Serv for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and that she hath two Children (now a home with her) viz. Eliz aged abt. 11 and Margt. at 8 years

Sworn May 10th. 1749 before
