Westmr. 

Hannah Smith aged abt. 55 Years Wido . of Benjn. Smith who has been dead about 18 Years upon Oath says she was married to the sd. Benjn. Smith by a Romish Preist at a house in East Street near Red Lyon Square about 29 Yrs. ago Says her sd. late husband lived in & rented a house in Clare Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £28 for sevll. Yrs. Says  neither her sd. late husband after he left the sd. house nor this Examinant since his death rented ten any other house pd. parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. fosr one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 30th. 1747 before  Wm: Jones

Hannah Smith
