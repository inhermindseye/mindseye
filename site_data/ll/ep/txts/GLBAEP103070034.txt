Middx ss  Sarah Barber  Single Women Maketh oath that she has been often times informed by her Mother to ther persons that she this Depont Was born a Bastard in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of St. Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middx Which information this Deponent verily believes to be true And says that she was never a hired Servant by ye Year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a Year & upwards nor Served any Parish office Nor gained Settlement Since her Birth to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 25 day of June 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  Saml Gower

his Sarah Barber Mark
