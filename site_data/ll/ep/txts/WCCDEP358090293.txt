Middx to wit Sarah Scarsbrook Aged about Thirty Seven Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with Richard Scarsbrook her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields about Eleven Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said late Husband never Rented House paid Taxes was ever bound an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year before Marriage Says her said late Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that his Father Richard Scarsbrook Rented & lived in a House in Charles Court Hungerford Markett in the sd Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds hath Quitted said House above Twenty Years ago since which time her sd late Husband never gained any or Subsequent Settlement than as aforesaid neither hath this Examinant since her sd Husbands Death

Sworn this 20th. day of Febry. 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner  Sampn Wright

her Sarah Scarsbrook Mark
