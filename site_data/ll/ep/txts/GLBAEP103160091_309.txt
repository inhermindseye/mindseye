Middlesex ss}  Susannah Haslewood the widow of George Haslewood deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband rented a house in Black Dog alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subseqt. Settlement Nor hath she this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself.

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Susannah Haslewood
