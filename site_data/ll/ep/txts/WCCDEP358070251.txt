Westmr to wit Susannah Chivers Aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House Paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship Says the Grandfather of this Examinant lived and Rented a House in Arrundel street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly of Twenty Pounds since which time this Examinant nor her Father ever gained any Subsequent Settlement as her said Father, and Mother has often informed her and which information she verity believes to be true and further Upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes (she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born,) And that One Henry Rogers a Journeyman Taylor who now lives with his father in Stanbury Court Pickadilly is the Father therefore he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime the latter end of May or the beginning of June but the Particular day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Henry Rogers and no other Person did beget the same, and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 22d- day of March 1775 before M Addington.

The Mark of Susannah Chivers Witness James Viger
