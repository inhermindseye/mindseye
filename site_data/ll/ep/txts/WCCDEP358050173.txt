Westmr.  Chas. Cowper upon Oath saith he Intermarryed with his present wife Ann in the Parish Church of St Clemt. Danes on the 5th. Day of Oct. 1742 and that before his said Intermarriage and whilst a Singleman he Lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Thomas Each at the Trumpat in Sheir Lane in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of £5 for one Year and upwds. and has not since Quitted his said Service rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 12th 1749 before Tho Lane

Charles Cooper
