Middx  Ann Reason aged about 62 years upon Oath says She Intermarried with George Reason her late Husband deced in the Parish Church of St. Bennet at Pauls Wharf in the City of London about Thirty two years since Says that he Rented a House of Thirty five Pounds a year near Clare Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes lived therein upwards of two years and quitted the same about Thirty years and that She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year since the Death of her said late Husband and that She hath not Intermarried with any other Person

Sworn the 13th. Augst. 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Reason
