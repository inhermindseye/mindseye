Middlesex ss}  Mary Dixon the Widow of George Dixon deced maketh oath that She can give no Acct respecting her sd husbands Settlement That her late Father Alexander Patten deced heretofore rented a house of Ten Pounds a year & upwards in Baileys Place in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & was charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same & did not afterwards do any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere & this Depont. lived with him as Part of his Family and this Depont. never was bound an Apprentice nor ever hired as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in in her ownright Save what She has Acquired by Marriage

Sworn this 20. day of January 1787 before  R Davies

Mary Dixcon
