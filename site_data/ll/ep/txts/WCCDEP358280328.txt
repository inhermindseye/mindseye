St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) Mary Liddell Aged twenty Eight Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith That she never was Married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Harcourt at No. 9. in Beaufort Buildings Strand in the said parish of St. Clement Danes about t near two years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds that she quitted her said Service about three years ago and since hath not acquired or become intitled unto any other legal settlement And this Deponent further saith that on the 25th day of November 1789 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the Store Street Hospital in the said County of Middlesex who hath since been Christened by the Name of William That one Poppelton who is a Clerk in the an office or Department belonging to three Per Cent Consols in the Bank of England & lately Resided in Primrose Street near Spitalfields & as this Deponent be is the only real and true father of such Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he and no one other person having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body at several times and begotten the same Child thereon And that she this Deponent (being confined in Circumstance is incapacitated from providing for such Child

Sworn the 15th June 1791 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Liddell
