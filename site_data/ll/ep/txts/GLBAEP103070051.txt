Middx ss.  David Adams Maketh oath that about five Years Ago he rented a house in the Parish of st. George in the County of Middx and paid Eight pounds a Year for the Same and paid all Parish taxes of for the sd. House Whilst he lived in the Same and Served the office of Head borough in the sd Parish and  by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that he hath not Since rented any other house Nor Paid any Parish Taxes Nor hath this Depont. to his knowledge information or belief Acquired any Subsequent Settlement And Says that he hath a Wife Named Elizabeth and two Children to Wit David aged about Nine Years and Elizabeth aged about one Year & ten Months the lawfull issue of this Deponent his sd. wife

Sworn the 26th day of Novr. 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  Tho. Quarrill

David Adams
