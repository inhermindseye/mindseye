A. to wit Mary Louiza Dolcho aged about 60 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says that she and her Family went and lived in a House near the Castle and Falcon  Aldersgate Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldersgate London for Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted the said House about 29 years ago since which time she hath never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 17th- day of Novr. 1784 before us M Addington Thos: Gilbert
