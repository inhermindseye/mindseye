Westmr  Martha Evans Aged about Twenty three Years Upon Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year, says her Father John Evans now lives in a house of of his own in the Parish of Rixham in Denbighshire in North Wales in which he carries on the Business of a Carpenter and Undertaker , And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the Twentieth of November 1767, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Holmes in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath been Baptized by the Name of Robert, says that Robert Edwards an Attorney at No. 3. Ground Floor in Lincolns Inn Now Square did beget the said Male Bastard Child on her Body he having had carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of February 1767, and at several times afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid And that the said Robert Edwards and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 19th. day of December 1767 before W Kelynge

Martha Evans
