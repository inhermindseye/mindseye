Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Benjamin Newman  Beadle of the Parish Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Maketh Oath and Faith that Mary Colt  Singlewoman now ill at the Crown Public House in Panhope street Clare market in the Parish saint Clement Danes aforesaid to the in pound this deponent and while Information he believes to be true that she that said Mary Cole never was married and that about his years age the was lived as a yearly Servant at the wages of our Pounds by the year to a Mr. Pierce who kept the Sign of the shoulder of Matter Cole in the Parish of saint John Hesley in the County of Middlesex that she lived with him for upon and of a years under and Having and since quitting his Service hath not gained at the best of her knowledge any subsequent settlement

Sworn this 28th. May 1790 before  N Bond Sampr Wright

Benj: Newman
