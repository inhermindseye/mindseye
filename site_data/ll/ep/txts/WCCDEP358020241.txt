Saint Clement Danes.

Middlesex (ss.) & Westminster  Elizabeth Campbell aged thirty four Years or thereabouts, upon her Oath saith That she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with William Rous , a Grocer at the Corner of Wimpole, and Wigmore Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex for upwards of twelve Months that she quitted his Service about Eight Years ago, that since quitting such Service she has not lived in any other Service for twelve Months nor done any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have acquired any other Settlement

Sworn this first Day of January 1791 before us E Read Wm Hyde }

E

The Mark of Elizabeth Campbell
