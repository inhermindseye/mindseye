Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Sullivan aged about 61 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Michael Sullivan who has been Dead upwards of two Years and by whom she hath no Children now living except one who is married and provided for and further says that her said husband Rented and lived in a House at Nutkins's Corner in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or thereabouts and paid all parish Taxes due for the same and quitted said house upwards of twenty Years before his Death and after quitting the said house he never gained any other subsequent Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her said husbands Death

Sworn the 23rd. Day of May 1777  Wm. Kitchiner  John Cox .

her Mary Sullivan Mark
