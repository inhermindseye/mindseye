Middx.  Joseph King aged abt. 37 years upon Oath says that whilst he was a singleman he lived a hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Jas. Wilshin  Cornchandler in Claremarkett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for two years at the yearly Wages of nine Pounds for the first year & twelve Pounds twelve Shillings for the second year & hath quitted sd. service abt. twelve yrs. ago & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes says he has two Childn. by his late wife Mary King (to wit) Mary aged abt. 8 years & Jonathan aged abt. 5 yrs. & being poor & lame is not able to maintain them

Sworn the 30th day of April 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

John Jos: King his Mark
