Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Cock aged about 57 years upon her Oath Saith that about Ten years ago she Intermarried with James Cock her late Husband who has been dead about Six years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Colvill Court Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and paid Taxes due for the same and that after he quitted said house he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement and that she is now Chargeable to the parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn this 16th. day of March 1798 before us } N Bond Rupt Clarke
