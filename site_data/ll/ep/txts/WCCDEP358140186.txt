Middx.  Mary Fairbrother aged abt. 60 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of James Fairbrother who has been dead abt. Eleven Years; says her sd. husband Rented a house in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds and lived in the same abt. Two Years and never after Rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes; Says she has not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge and belief

Sworn this 21st. day of Novr. 1758 . before} Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

Mary Fairbrother

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Thos. Matravers Overseer
