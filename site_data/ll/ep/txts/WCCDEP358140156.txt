Middx.  Edmund Battersby aged 59 Yrs. upon Oath says he Rented a house in New Castle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £26. lived therein abt. three Years and quitted same abt. 18 Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 1st. day of Septr. 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

Edmund Battersby
