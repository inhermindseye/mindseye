Middx  Ann Wyat aged abt. 30 Yrs. upon Oath says that on the 6th day of March 1751 old Style she being at that time a Single Woman was delivered of a female bastard Child in the house of Mr. Briersly in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized Susannah says one Wm. Moorcock did beget the sd. Child on her body she this Exaiant living as a yearly servt. with Mrs. Hodgkins at Dulwich where she became acquainted with the sd. Wm. Moorcock who has Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the house of the sd. Mr. Hodgkins sevl. times in the Months of May & June 1750 at one of which times he got her with Child of the female bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid & that he the sd. Wm. Moorcock & no other Person is the true & real father thereof & she being Poor is not able to maintain the same

Sworn the 23d of May 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Wyatt
