Middx  Jno. Mc. Danell upon Oath saith he intermarried with Mary his Wife on the 29th. Janry 1743 & that himself & his sd. Wife are both Natives of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England and that he Served his Apprentiship with Edwd. Mc. Danell a Weaver in Fordhams Alley in the parish of St. Patrick in Dublin Ireland & never gained a Subsequent Settlemt. to his knowledge or belief Says he has 4 Children by his sd. Wife (viz) Bridget aged abt. 5 Yrs. Chas . aged abt. 4 Yrs. Mathw aged upwads. of 2 Yrs. & Cathn . aged one month

Sworn June 27th. 1751 before  Tho Lane John Mc Danel
