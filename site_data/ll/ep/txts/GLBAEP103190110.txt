Middlesex ss}  Hannah Williams  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years Ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with John Baker a Victualler in Portsmouth in the County of Southampton and Continued in Such Service under such hiring for the Space of two years & half & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor hath She done any Act to gain anr Settlement

Sworn this. 22. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Hannah Williams
