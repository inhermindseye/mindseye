Middlesex, to wit, The Examination of Catharine Traxlir , Singlewoman , taken on Oath before one of His Majesty's Justices of the Peace in and for the County of Middlesex, this Day of December in the Year of our Lord One Thousand Seven Hundred and Ninety Seven

Who saith that on or about the Fourteenth Day of October last in the Dwelling House of Wright in Dean Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , she was delivered of a female Bastard Child, since Baptised by the Name of Sarah , and that the said Bastard Child is become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate, and that Meredith Jones , Surgeon of His Majesty's Ship Resolution now lying at Portsmouth , is the Father of the said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the Day and Year first above written before me}

her Catherine Traxlir Mark
