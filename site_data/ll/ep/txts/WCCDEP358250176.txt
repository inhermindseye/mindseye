Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Chandler aged about 63 years upon her Oath saith that about 26 years ago she Intermarried with Daniel Chandler her late Husband who has been dead about Ten years at Reigate Church That her said late Husband informed this Examinant that he served his Apprenticeship in Epsom in the Parish of Epsom in the County of Surry and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she Might have gained a subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 9th. day of June 1795  N Bond E Read

Ann Chandler
