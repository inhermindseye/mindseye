Middlesex ss}  Mary Davies the widow of Andrew Davies deced maketh oath that Since her said husbands death She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mr Robert Holdgate a Salesman the Corner of King Street in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the said County and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring there for the Space of a year and half that She has not Since lived as a hired Servant in any place for the Space of a year or been married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 28. day of February 1788 before  R Davies

Mary Davies
