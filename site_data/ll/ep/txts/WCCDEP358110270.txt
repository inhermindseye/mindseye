Middx  Susa. Crawley aged abt. 25 Years upon Oath saith she never was married And that she lived a yearly Servant with Mary Reader in Fore Street in the Parish of Alhallows  in the Town & County of Stretford for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds for the first Year, Two Pounds five Shillings for the Second Year & Two Pounds ten Shills. for the two last Years of her Service that she quitted her sd. Service abt. two Years ago & has not since rented ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn July 3d. 1753 before} Sam Pechell

The Mark of Susa. Crawley
