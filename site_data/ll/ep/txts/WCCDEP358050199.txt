Westmr.  Eleanr. Bryan Wido . of James Bryan who dyed the 4th. Instant upon Oath saith she Intermarryed with the said James Bryan in the Spanish Ambassaders Chapel in Ormond Street upwds. of Sixteen Years ago by whom she hath two Children viz James aged upwds. of 12 Years & Catherine aged upwds. of one Year Says her sd. late husband rented a house of £13 Yrs. a Year in Cow Cross in the Parish of St Sepulchers in the County of Middlesex and lived therein upwds. of One Year and quitted the same about Six Years ago and has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 12th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

Eleanr Bryan
