Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Trulock  Singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime Ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Thomas Moore  Hatter in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd. County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for near the Space of two Years That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 12 day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Eliz Trulock
