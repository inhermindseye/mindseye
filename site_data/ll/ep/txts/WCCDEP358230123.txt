Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Hill Aged about 60 years upon her Oath Saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant for Eight years with Mr. Palmer in Bridge Street in the Parish of Saint Margarett in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas That she hath left the said Service about four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of March 1800 before us} Ws Woodenough John Collick

The Mark of Mary Hill
