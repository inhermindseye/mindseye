Middx  John Waby upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Joseph Chorkley in St. John Street in the Parish of St. Sepulchers in the County of Middlesex  Stick maker & hath been out of his said Apprentiship about Six Months that since his said Apprentiship he hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 30th. June 1743 before} Wm. Jones  Tho Lane

John Waby
