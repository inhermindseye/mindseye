Middx  James Wylde Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath Read heard and does verily believe that Mordecai Osborne when he was a Singleman he was hired a yearly Servant to Mr . Thomas Harvey at the Abby house in the parish of Saint Lawrence in the Town or Burrough of Aversham in the County of Worcester and lived there as such three years And that he hath two Children Mary aged about twelve years a Ruth about Eight yearly Sarah his now wife

Sworn 23d. Septr. 1746 before Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

Js. Wylde
