Middx  Barbara Clark  Wido . upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with her late Husband  Anthony Clark at Lincolns Inn Chapell about 13 or 14 Years since by whom she hath one Child to wit Mary aged upwds. of Ten Years Says the sd. Anthy. Clark lived in & Rented a house in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Six pounds or thereabouts for the Space of five Years & hath left the same about Ten Years Says her sd. late Husband never after he left the sd. House rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place nor hath this Examinant since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 21st. Septr. 1745 before} Wm: Jones . Barbara Clark
