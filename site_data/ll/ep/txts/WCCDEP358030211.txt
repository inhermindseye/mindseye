Middx  Susanna Povey  Wife of Wm. Povey (gone from her) removed from the parish of St. Leonard Foster Lane London by Order dated the 23d. Day of Augt. Instant upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Wm. Povey on or abt. the 5th. Day of February 1744 Says her said husband (whilst he was a Singleman) lived a yearly Servt . with Mr. King at the Strand Coffee house in the Strand in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of one Year & three months at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds Says the sd. Wm. Povey never rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Child viz: James aged abt. 7 Months & being poor is not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworn Augt. 30th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Susa. Povey
