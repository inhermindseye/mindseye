Middx.  Lettitia Green aged upwards of 25 years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Wm. Green with whom she intermarried at the fleet London abt. Six years ago & by whom she hath three Childn. (to wit) Willm. aged upwards of five years Mary aged upwds. of three years & Frances aged upwards of one year says her said husband was born in the Parish of Watlington near Nettlebed in the County of Oxford

Sworn this 21st. day of Febry. 1758 before me} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Lettitia Green
