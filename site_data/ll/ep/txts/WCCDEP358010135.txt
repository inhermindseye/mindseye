Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr} to wit Martha Lowden aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that on the 10th. day of March 1773 she Intermarried with John Lowden at the Parish Church of St. George the Martyr that she this Ext. has not heard of or seen her said Husband for these 9 years last past therefore she does not know whether he be a live or dead that she never heard or does she know where her said Husband was born whether he ever was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year for 40 Days or paid Taxes That she this Ext. Rented a Two Pair of Stairs Floor and a Shop in Gate Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields for which she paid the yearly Rent of £17 that she lived in same for 2 Months and upwards that she hath quitted said Apartment about 3 years ago since which time she never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. Augt. 1787  Thos: Gordon Maddington

Martha Lowden
