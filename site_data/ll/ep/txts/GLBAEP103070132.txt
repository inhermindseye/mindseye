Middx ss  Margaret Freke  Singlewoman Maketh oath that her Father Henry Freke deced hath often informed her that about forty eight years ago he was bound apprentice to John Emmes a Waterman of the parish of St. Olave in Southwark in the County of Surry & duly served his apprenticeship with his sd Master in the sd parish & by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt. there which information this Depont. verily believes to be true And this Depont. doth not know nor has she been informed that her sd. father subsequent to his sd. apprenticeship ever Rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish offices or did any Act to gain any settlement after his sd apprenticeship And this Depont. saith that she was never a hired servt. by the year nor Married nor ever bound an apprentice And that she hath not gained any settlement but in the sd. parish of St. Olave in right of her sd. father & other birth

Sworn this 8th day of March 1755 before me  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Margaret Freke his Mark
