Middx.  Grace Clarke aged upwds. of 50 Yrs. upon Oath says she intermarried with Joseph Clarke at the fleet London abt. fourteen Years ago And that her said husband has been dead abt. Eight Years. Says One Mrs. Elizabeth Greenwood this Examinants husbands Mother inform'd this Examinant that the above named Joseph Clarke was an Illegitimate Child born of her the said Elizabeth Greewoods Body in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex which Information this Examinant believes to be True Says her said late Husband Blacked Shoes for a livelihood in St. Clemt. Church Yard and Never was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servant for One Year as she has been informed and verily believes says since the Death of her husband she hath not lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn 13th. Augst. 1760 before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Grace Clarke
