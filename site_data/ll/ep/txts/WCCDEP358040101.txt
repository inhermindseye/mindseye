Middlx Westmr.  Robt. Crosfeild aged about 70 Years upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of £20 for one Year or thereabouts & left the same abt. 40 Years ago Says he has not since he Quitted the sd. house in Butcher Row Rented a house of Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 11th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Robert Crossfield
