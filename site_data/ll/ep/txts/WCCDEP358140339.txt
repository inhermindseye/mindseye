Middx.  Ann Sawyer aged abt. 30 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Sr. Francis Head  Bart : in Rathbone Place in the Parish of St. Mary le Bon in the County of Middx. for three Years and upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £5 and hath quitted said Service abt. five Years And hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn this 9th. day of Jany. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Saunder Welch

Ann Sawyer

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Burnthwaite J: P:
