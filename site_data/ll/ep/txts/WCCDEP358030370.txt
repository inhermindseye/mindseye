Westmr. 

Mary Atherton aged about 29 Years upon Oath saith she never was married Says she lived in and rented a house in little Grosvenor Street in the Parish of St. George in Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of £36 for one Year & upwards & hath left same upwards of Eight Years Says she hath not since she quitted the sd. house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 4th. 1747 before  Tho: Burdus .

Mary Atherton
