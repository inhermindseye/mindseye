Middx   Mary Gregory aged about 60 years Lodging in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx upon Oath saith that about Eight Years since she was hired as a Yearly Servant by the Matron of the Small Pox Hospital in Cold Bath Fields in the Parish of St. James  Clerkenwell in the County of Middx and lived therein in the Capacity of a Nurse for about Four years and had Eight pounds a year Wages which was paid her by the Secretary of the same Hospital and hath not since she quitted the same rented any House paid Land Tax or poor Rate or lived a hired Servant for One Year also says that she now is and was at the time of her said hiring a Widow and without Child or Children

Sworn the 22d day of Decr. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Gregory
