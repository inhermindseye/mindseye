St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Mary Marshfield Aged 54 years or thereabouts (on behalf of John Eades with whom she has Cohabited for 18 years or thereabouts but to whom she was never legally Married) Upon her Oath saith that the said John Eades while he was Single & unmarried not having any Child or Children lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bullock a Cook in South Audley the Corner of Green Street in the parish of St George Hanover Square in the said Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex about 2 years & an half at the year Wages of 30s for the first & 40d the second year that he quitted the as he hath Informed her this Deponent & which she verily believes to be true And he hath also Informed this Examinant which she verily believes to be true That he Since he quitted the same (which he  hath been told was when he was about the Age of twelve years) he hath not rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds, a year served as an hired yearly Servant or done any other Act whereby he might obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief with at he is now Poor & dangerously Ill

Sworn the 13th day of Septr. 1791 . before us D Walker Wm Hitchiner

Mary Marshfield Mark
