Middx.  Elizth: Burnett wife of Owen Burnett on behalf of Michael Thomas Horner aged about Eight Years Son of George Horner deceased by Ann his Wife Upon Oath says the said Michael Thomas Horner was born in Gardiner's Lane,  King Street Westminster in the parish of Saint Margaret in the City of Westmr. within the County of Middlesex , And that the said George Horner was a German and never Rented house paid Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn March 6th. 1765 . Before} Paul Vaillant  Benjamin Cox

Elizabeth Bunett
