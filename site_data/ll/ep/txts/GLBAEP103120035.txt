Middlesex ss}  Ann Normington  singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime ago she was hired by & lived as a hired servant by the year with one Mr. Dowry a Cook in Oxford Road in the Parish of St. James in Westminster in the County of Middlesex and Continued in such service for about the space of two years at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds & by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt. there and this Depont. Saith That she hath not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And this Depont. farthr. Saith that on the Twenty Sixth day of January last she this Depont. was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Eastcheap Swan Alley in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and that William Beacher late of Oxford Road aforesaid Gardner did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child which hath not as yet been Baptized.

Sworn this 24th: day of February 1773 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

The Mark of Ann Normington
