Saint Clement Danes April 30th. 1790 .

Middlesex to wit Susannah Parmenter  Singlewoman aged twenty Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married or ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own Right as she best known or believes And further this Examinant saith That she hath been informed and believes that her Father Ambrose Parmenter is a Parishioner duly Settled in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex by Virtue of his hiring and Service for upwards of twelve Months to Lord Walsingham And this Examinant further on her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid, and that Thomas Bullwright who is a Gardiner and works with Mrs. Eglish of Hampstead aforesaid is the Real and true father of

Such Child or Children whereof she is so pregnant as aforesaid he the said Thomas Balwright having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of August now last part and at several other times since at Some or one of which times he did beget or this Examinants Body the Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid and that the said Thomas Balwright and no one else is the real and true father of such Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid.

Sworn this 30th. Day of April 1790 before me} R Butler

Susannah her Mark Parmenter

Settled by paying £13 Rele Exted

The Mans Name Turnbull he pd. as above paid £
