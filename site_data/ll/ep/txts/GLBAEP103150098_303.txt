Middlesex ss.}  Thomas Hinton maketh oath That divers Years ago he rented a house at the Yearly Rent of Ten nine pounds near Paradice Street in the Parish as St Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry known by the sign of the Radlyon & during Such his Residence there in was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a wife named Mary to whom he was lawfy Married

Sworn this 28th day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith  Wm: Blackmore

Thomas Hinton
