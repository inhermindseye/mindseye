Middx & Westmr} to wit Ann Deimer Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Cave a Silversmith in Gutter lane in the Parish of St. Peter Cheap in the City of London upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Guineas hath quitted her said service upwards of one Year ago and hath not since she quitted the same over Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year and Further says that on the Twenty fifth day of October 1768 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in House of her Master Thomas Hall a Price Broker in White horse Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . which was soon afterwards baptized by the Name of George and is now chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same Says that the aforesaid Thomas Hall is the Father there of he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time the beginning of the Year 1768 and at Sevl. Different times afterwards at one of Which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Thomas Hall and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 26th. Day of May 1774 before  Sampn Wright

Ann Deimer
