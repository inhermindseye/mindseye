Middx  Martha Murphy aged about 70 Years Removed from St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Thomas Murphy who has been Dead about Seventeen Years and that about Twelve years ago being then a Widow and without Child or Children she was hired as a Yearly Servant to Mr. Woodley a Taylor at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds and lived with him under that Agreement in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes near Three Years and hath not since she Quitted that Service kept any House paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate nor been a hired Servant for One year

Sworn the 25th. day of Augt. 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Martha Murphy
