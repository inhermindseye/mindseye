Middx  Frederick Smith Aged about 25 Years upon Oath Says he Served One Year & a half of his Apprentiship with Mr. Sermon a Brush maker in Castle Street in the parish of St. Philip & Jacob in the City of Bristol & never after was Assigned over to any person Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 22d of Decr. 1764 Before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Frederick Smith
