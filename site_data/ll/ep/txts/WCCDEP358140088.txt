Middx  Eliz: Canter aged abt. 48 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Robt. Canter who hath been dead abt. four Years says her said late husband Rented a house in Plumtree Court in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & lived in the same abt. half a Year according to her now best Remembrance & never after Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes says she has not since her said husbands death Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Elizth. Canter
