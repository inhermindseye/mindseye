Middlesex ss}  James Graham maketh oath that about three years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant . at Certain Wages by and With Rich and Farrell in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Brass Founder & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of Two Years and upwards and has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elswhere order any Act to gain another Settlement.

Sworn this. 24. day of November 1787 before  R Davies

James Graham
