Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Elizabeth Laighton aged 35 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that She was married to Alexander Laighton her present Husband according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of England by Banns duly published at the Parish Church of Kendal in the County of Westmorland on the 10th. Day of Novr- 1777 that She has one Child by her said Husband namely Margaret aged Eleven years a thereabout who is now maintained by this Examinant and this Examinant further Saith that her said Husband and this Examinant rented and lived in a House in Harford Place Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of £25 for upwards of his Weeks and quitted the same about five Weeks ago that since then they have not gained any subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information and belief and further this

Examinant saint that been said Husband hath gone away from and deserted this Examinant and by her unprovided and lestitute and then the this Examinant is poor and unab to maintain Read and Child

Sworn this 9th.July 1789 R. Butler

her Elizth Laishton Mark
