Middlesex to Wit Judith Scarborough aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she has lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Richd. Prior a Butcher in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of Three years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about a fortnight ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children Etc and further says that William Birks an Apprentice to the said Mr. Prior is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House some time in the Month of December last but the Particular Days she does not now remember and at Sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said William Birks and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 29th. day of Augt. 1783 R Butler

Judith Scarbrough
