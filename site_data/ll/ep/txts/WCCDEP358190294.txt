Middx  John Godwin Aged About 46 Years Upon Oath says that when he was a Single Man he lived A Hired Yearly Servt. with John Barber  Esqr . in Queen Square in the Parish of St Georges Queen Square in the County of Middx Four Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds and hath Quitted his said Service upwards of Twenty Years and hath not since rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn ye 22th. Day of October: 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

John Godwin
