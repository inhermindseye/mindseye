Middlesex ss.  Rebecca Cowee the Widow of John Cowee Maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Mary at Newington in the County of Surry sometime in the Month of May 1751 and that her said late Husband about Forty Eight Years ago rented a House of Twenty Pounds & Year or thereabouts in Charles Street in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City & Liberty of Westminster known by the name or Sign of the Hampshire Hog & lived there about Sixteen Years, that her said Husband did not after he quitted the said House rent any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or pay any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or do any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement, And that she has not done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right since her said Husband Death

Sworn this 22d. Day of Septr. 1791 before  Rob Smith R Davies R Cowie
