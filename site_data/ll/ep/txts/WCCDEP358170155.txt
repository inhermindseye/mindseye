Middx  Jno. Richardson aged abt. Thirty Five Years upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to and served Two Years the First Part of his Apprentiship with his Mother Eliz: Richardson in the Parish of St. Olave in the County of Surry & after his Mothers Decease went and served the Remainder of his sd. Apprentiship with his Brother James Richardson in the sd. Parish of St. Olave that he hath not since he left his sd. Brother rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath a Wife named Eliz : and Two Childn. Vizt: Mary aged abt. Five Yrs. & Eliz : aged abt. Two Yrs. being Sick and Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne the 20th. Day of October 1741 before} W Hayton

Jno Richardson J
