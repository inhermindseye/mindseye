Middx  Eliza. Brewen aged abt. 32 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice by the Church wardens & Overseers of the Poor of the parish of St Brides London to Sarah Smith an Oyster Woman and lived with the sd. Smith as her apprentice 8 years in Stanhope Street Claremarket in the parish of St Clement Danes and that she never was a yearly Servt. for one Year nor ever rented ten pounds a year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn May 8th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz Brewen
