Middx.  Susannah Elizabeth Pennington aged abt. 35 Years upon Oath says she is the wife of John Pennington who is aged abt. Forty Two Years with whom she intermarried in May fair Chapel on the 24th. of June 1749. says her said husband Rented a house in the Strand near the New Church in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £32. lived therein abt. one Year & a half & hath quitted same upwards of Eight Years says her said husbd. after he quitted the said house in the Strand Rented & lived in Chambers up one pair of Stairs at No. 5 in Holborn Court  Grays Inn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above ye. Bars in the said County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of fifteen pounds for four

four Years and upwards and hath not since he quitted the said Chambers Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Parish or place to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 30th. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

J E Pennington
