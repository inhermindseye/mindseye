Saint Clement Danes 12th. Augt 1788

Middlesex (to wit) Ann Murray  Widow upon her Oath Saith that she is the Widow of William Murray her late Husband who was an Irishman born and never as this Examinant believes acquired a Settlement in this Thingdon and this Examinant further Saith that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant  whilst she was Single and unmarried and not having Child or Children with Mr. Hitchcock a Porter Butcher in Saint James's Market in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster  and County of Middlesex for upwards of one Year-that since Earthing her Said Service she hath nor to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. Augt- 1788 before us D Walker Russ: Clarke

Ann Murray
