Middlesex ss}  Nathaniel Harris maketh oath That in or about the year one thousand Seven hundred & fifty two he this Deponent rented a house at the yearly Rent of Five Guineas in Darby Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued therein for about the Space of three years & during Such his Residence there in was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 16. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C Smith

Nath Harris
