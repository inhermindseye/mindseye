Middlesex & Westmr.}  Johanna Hoare Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Hoare who has been dead about Three Months with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London in the Year 1753 Says her said late Husband was Partner with his Father John Hoare and one Mr. Shugden Taylors who rented and Lived in a House in that Part of Duke Street which is in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years and an half at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds or upwards and paid all parish Taxes due for the same Says that her said late Husband on quitting such Partnership went to Sea in the Year 1756 and never after Rented House or paid Taxes neither has this Examinant since her said late Husbands Death Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn this 18th. day of Decr. 1769 before us } R Butler

Johannah Hoare
