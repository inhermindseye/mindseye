Middx  Mary Anderson aged upwds. of 50 Yrs. upon Oath Says she is a Singlewoman & was never married Says she was born in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx where her Father Wm. Anderson Rented a house of upwards of Ten pounds a Year for two Years & upwards & there dyed Says she never rented ten pounds a Year was an apprentice lived with any person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 7th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Anderson
