Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Raffel aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly servant with Mr. Kent in Fetter Lane in the parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for near Two years at the yearly Wages of Nine Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about Nineteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that on or about the 29th. day of September 1793 she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Soloman No. 7 Old Round Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the name of Thomas

Sworn this 24th. day of Augt. 1797 before us } N Bond Joseph Moser

her Ann Raffel mark
