Westmr.  Ann Clough  Widow of John Clough who dyed abt. three Years since upon Oath saith she intermarried with her said late husband abt. 31 Years ago in the Parish Church of St. Sepulchers London Says about 30 Years ago the said John Clough lived in and rented a house in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £30 for one Year & upwards Says her sd. late husband never after he left the said house nor has this Examinant since his Death rented ten pounds Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinant knowledge or beleif

Sworne Febry 3d. 1747 before}

Ann Clough
