Middlesex ss}  Thomas Williams maketh oath That divers Years ago his late Father Thomas Williams a Taylor deced maketh oath Rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Twenty eight Pounds in Great Windmill Street in the Parish of Saint James in the City & Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex and by Means thereof gained & a legel Settlement there And this Deponent Saith that at the time his Said Father lived there he was Part of his Family and was taught by his Father the Trade of a Taylor there the he was never bound apprentice to him or to any other Person That to his knowledge or belief his Said Father did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he this Depont. never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right And that he hath a wife named Elizabeth to whom he was married at the Fleet the Day before the late Marriage Act took Place.

Sworn this 17th day of December 1770 before  Chris: Scott Burf Camper

Thomas Williams
