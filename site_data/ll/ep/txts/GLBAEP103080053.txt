Middlesex ss The Examination of Jane Hilliard  Widow Taken on oath before Me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the said County of Middx residing in or Next to the Limits of the Parish Church of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County this fourteenth day of December 1759

This Examinant on her oath saith that about fourteen Months ago one William Gardiner who then lived in Denmark Street in the Parish of St George in ye sd County Shoemaker did Prevail with this Examinant to suffer him to lye with her at the House of one Mr Hall in the same Street and Parish Cooper and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body whereby she Conceived with Child of a Female Bastard Child  And this Examinant farther saith that about four Months ago she this Examinant having Occasion to go to Billings gate in the Parish of St Mary At hill London to buy Fish she then and there fell in Labour and the Beadle of ye sd Parish of St Mary At hill immediately took her into his Custody and Care and Carried her in a Coach to Hogsdon Town House in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in ye sd County of Middx where she this Examinant was delivered of the sd Female Bastard Child which sd. Female Bastard Child

has been since Baptised by the Name of Mary and is likely to be chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate this Examinant being Poor and Not able to Provide for the same And this Examinant farther saith that the sd William Gardiner is the truly and only Father of the said female Bastard Child And that this Examination is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whatsoever

Sworn this fourteenth day of December 1759 before me  Chris: Scott

Her Jane Hilliard Mark
