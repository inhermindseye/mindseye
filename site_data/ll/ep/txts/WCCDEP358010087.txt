Saint Clement Danes

Middx Westmr.} (ss)  William Lamport aged about 55 years upon his Oath saith that he rented lived in House in Cooks Court in the Parish of St. Clement Panes about 16 years at the yearly Rent of £30 hath quitted Sd. House since the year 1781 since which time he went to live as a hired yearly Servant with one Thos. Eyre Esqr. at Hassop Hall in the Parish of Bakewell in the County of Derby for 11 Months at the yearly wages of £55.. & upwds. that since quitted said House in Cooks Court he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 paid Taxes or done any act to gain any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this th day of April 1787}

never Sworn
