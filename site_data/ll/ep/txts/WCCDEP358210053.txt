Westmr.  Deborah Burton aged about 46 Years upon Oath says that in the Month of March 1757, She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Burnett in Lombard Street in the Precinct of Whitefriers in the City of London which hath been baptized William Ross

Sworn the 13th, June 1766, before} Paul Vaillant  Fran. Cary

The Mark of Deborah Burton
