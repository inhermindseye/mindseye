Middx 

Eleanr Jones Wife of Owen Jones gone from her upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Owen Jones at the Fleet London upwds. of Six Years ago and that as she hath been Informed and believes her sd. husband was born at Portelly in the County of Carnaroan in North Water and never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief and that she hath two Children Jane aged abt. 5 Years and Mary aged abt. 3 Years

Sworn Novr. 5th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Eleanr Jones
