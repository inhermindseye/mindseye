Middx  Hannah Heron aged about 30 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Richard Heron on the 22d. Octr. 1739 at the Fleet London & that the sd. Richard Heron absconded from her about 5 Years ago and this Examint. knows not whether he is living or dead Says the said Richard Heron rented the White horse Alehouse in Dram Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of £14 & lived therein for one Year or thereabouts Saith her sd. husband hath not since he Quitted the sd. house (which is abt. 11 Years ago) Rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 12th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Hanah Heron
