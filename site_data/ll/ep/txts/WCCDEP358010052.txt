Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  N to wit Mary Ditcher aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Meacher in Lambs Conduit Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted said Service about 4 years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Janry. 1787 G Mercer Jno. Machin

her Mary Ditcher Mark
