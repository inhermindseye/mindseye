St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Mary le bone  (to wit ) John Jones  singleman aged 27 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he was bound an Apprentice for seven year (by Indenture) to Mr. Howgate in Portland Street in the parish of St Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex Sadler and served him under such Indentures for about a year and an half when his said Master Died he this Deponent went abroad and Returned about a Fortnight ago to this Kingdom consequently hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge information or belief That he is Distressed and hath become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 23th day of September 1792 before Us  Sampr Wright  Gd: Mitchell

John Jones
