Middx  The Examination of Jno. Lewis living in ye. Parish, of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 16th. Day of February 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. about Ten Years ago  he lived with one Mrs. Francis in Clare Markett in ye. sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Fishmonger as a Yearly hired Servt . for the space of three Years at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds, And this Examinant further on his Oath saith yt. he hath not since he left his sd. Mistress Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he is now Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine himselfe.

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before}

John Lewis
