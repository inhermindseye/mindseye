Middx  Ann Heritage upon Oath saith she was married to Joseph Heritage at Camberwell Church upwards of two Years ago by whom she has one Child Ann aged upwds. of one Year (that her sd. husband at present absconds from her) and she has been informed & verily believes he Served his Apprentiship with his father Mr Heritage a Carpenter in the Parish of Bansworth in the County of Worcester and never rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworne the 10th. Augt. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell Ann Heretage
