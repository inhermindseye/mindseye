Middlesex ss}  James Jones maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to & Served the whole of his apprenticeship with John Ballenger  Mathematichal Instrument maker in Crown Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he hath not since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement & that he hath a wife named Hannah  to whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this. 18. day of April 1776 before  Geo C Smith

James Jones
