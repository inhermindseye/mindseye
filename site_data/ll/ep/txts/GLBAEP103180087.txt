Middlesex ss}  Jane Bee  widow maketh oath That She well knows & is Acquainted with one Ann Batton now residing & inhabiting in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Whom this Deponent believes to be a Single & unmarried Woman And this Deponent also knew & was well Acquainted with the Said Ann Batton s Father & Mother who for many years lived in & rented a house in White horse Court in New Gravel Lane in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell in the Said County (this Deponent having Nursed the Sd Ann Batton s Mother on her Lying Inn of her third Child there) and in Which place She the Said Ann Batton was born as her said Mother informed this Depont & Which this Depont believes to be true That the Said Ann Batton to this Deponts knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That this Deponent can give no Account respecting the Settlement of the said Ann Batton s Parents Save that She has been informed they were Natives of Ireland & rented Such house as aforesd That She this Deponent has also been informed & believes That in the Months of October one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy Six She the Sd Ann Batton was delivered of a male Bastard Child in the Sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate Since baptized by the name of Joseph Dixon & which hath been & Still is Chargeable to the said Parish.

Sworn this 10 day of November 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Jane Bee
