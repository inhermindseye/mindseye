St Clement Danes 26th July 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement in St Andrew Holborn Middx }  in Right of her Father  (to wit) Mary Gardner  singlewoman aged sixteen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she never lived as an hired yearly Servant or did any Act to  acquire any legal settlement in her own right, to the best of her knowledge or belief, that she is informed and very verily believes she is the legitimate Daughter of Simon and Elizabeth Gardner , that her Father the said Simon Gardner  Obtained his legal settlement in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex by Renting and residing in a House in Leather Lane in the said Parish that she is the more induced to belief that the place of his last legal settlement is in that Parish as she her Mother  with this Examinant & two to other Children have been received in the House for the reception of the Poor of that parish & her Sister was bound out an Apprentice by the Officer's thereof that her Father & Mother hath deserted her & she is unable to provide for herself without assistance

Sworn the 26th July 1794 before us } J Hind Tho Vaughn

Mary Gardner 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
