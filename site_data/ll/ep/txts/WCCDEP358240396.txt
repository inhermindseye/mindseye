St Clements Danes 4th April 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the Fields  to wit Elizabeth Falkner aged 33 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Gardner in the Strand on the North side between Burleigh Street & Southampton Street within the parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for a Year at the Annual Wages of about £8 that She quitted that service several Years ago and hath not acquired any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 4th day of April 1794 before us} Wm Kitchiner  Elington

Elizth Falkner 's Mark

Witness Benj Newman
