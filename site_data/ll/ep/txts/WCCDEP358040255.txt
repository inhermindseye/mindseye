Middx 

Eliz: Case removed from the Parish of St. Mathew Bethnal Green in the County of Middlesex by Order dated the 10th. Instt. upon Oath Saith she never was married and that about 18 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servants with Mr. Hill Haberdasher of Hats near the New Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £4..10..0 for three Years & upwds. and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to he Knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 14th. 1748 before}
