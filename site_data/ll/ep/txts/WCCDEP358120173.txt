Middx  Richd: Geo: Gridley upon Oath Saith he has been informed & verily believes his late father Michal. Gridley decd. lived a yearly Servant whilst a Singleman wth. Mr Mathew Woodward a Corn chandler at the Corner of Wormwood Street in Bishopgate Street in the Parish of St. Ethelburga London for one Year and & upwards at the yearly Wages of £10 and quitted the sd Mr Woodwards Service abt. 40 Years ago and never afterwards rented ten pounds by the Year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year And this Examinant further maketh Oath that he was married to his Wife Eliz. Gridley at Birmingham new Church about Eighteen months ago by whom he has one Child (viz) Richd. aged upwds. of three Months and that he this Examinant never was an apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever Rented ten Pounds by the Year nor paid parochial Taxes

Sworn 3d. Augt. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond Rich George Gridley
