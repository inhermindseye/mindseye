Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Catherine Furguson an Infant aged about Five years Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Catherine Farguson was born at the house of a Mr. Readings in Brick Lane in the Parish of Saint Matthew Bethnal Green in the County of Middlesex and saith that the said Catherine Ferguson is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 29th day of Septr. 1797 before us  Jn Inn E Read

Jas Talboys
