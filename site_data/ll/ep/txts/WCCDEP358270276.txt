Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Rebecca Pollard aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Bowers in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the yearly wages of five Pounds that she hath quitted said service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Harris by Trade a Nailer and who lives in White House Alley  Turnmile Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examts. Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant thereon and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Man taken and Committed to Bridewell Disd. at the Goal Dely. Child born in the ho: 17th March 1797 named Wm . Sent to Enfield  30th. Jany. 1798

Sworn this 13th.. day of Octr. 1796 } J Reeves

her Rebecca Pollard Mark
