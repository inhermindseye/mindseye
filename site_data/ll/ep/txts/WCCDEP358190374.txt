Middx  Wm- Powell of Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx on behalf of Cath: Manethorp his Grand Daughter an Infant Aged about three Years Upon Oath Says that his Daughter  Cath: Manethorp Deceased  Mother of the above Named Infant Intermarried with Thos. Manethorp at the Fleet London about Ten Years Says he has Serched the Land Tax Books in the Parish of St. Bridgets Vulgarly St. Brides London & finds by the said Books that his Son in Law the abovenamed Thos. Manethorp Rented a House in Greens Rents in the said Parish of St. Brides & was Rated and paid ye Land Tax there and never after Rented house or paid Taxes

William Powell

Sworn the 24th Day of August 1763 : Before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant
