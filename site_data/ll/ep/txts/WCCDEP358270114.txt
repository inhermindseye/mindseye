2d Examination Desertion

St. Clement Danes June 17th 1793

Middlesex & Westminster  Settlement  3 Children  desertion (to wit) Charlotte Saunders aged 27 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband John Saunders at the parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex (by License) on or about the 3d day of December 1783 that Subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and Resided in an House in Holles Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for about 4 year at the Annual Rent of Eighteen pounds that he quitted the same about near three Months ago and hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief-Saith that  she this Examinant hath three Children by her said Husband now living with her namely Elizabeth aged seven years William born on the seventh day of January 1787  Margaret born on the fifteenth d and upwards Caroline aged about four years and Joseph aged about thirteen Months that her said Husband hath deserted and left her and their said Children  and neglected to provide for them whereby they are become chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn the 17th day of June 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Charlotte Saunders
