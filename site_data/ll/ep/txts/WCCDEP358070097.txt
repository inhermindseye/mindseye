Westmr. to wit John Eastwood Aged about four Score Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his now Wife at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Fileds in County of Middlesex about Seven Years Ago Says he Rented and lived in a house in Bett's Street in the Parish of Saint George in the East in County of Middlesex about three Years at the Yearly Rent of four Pounds & was rated to and Paid Land tax due for the same hath quitted the said House about Seven Years ago and hath not Since he quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or other wise gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 20th. day of May 1773 before R Butler W Kelyngs

John Eastwood
