Westmr- to wit Richard Knowlands Aged about 40 Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Robert Webster a Brewer in Mercer Street Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr - and County of Middx for eight Years at the Yearly Wages of Nine Pounds nine Shillings hath quitted that Service upwards of Sixteen Years ago since which time he hath gained no other subseqt- Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 10th- Day of Decr- 1777 before  Wm Kitchiner  John Goodchild

his Richard Knowland Mark
