Middx  The Examination of Margt. Munn  Widw . of Vere Munn Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 24th. Day of March 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that her late Husband Vere Munn Served his Apprenticeship with one Mr. Henry Bolney  Perukemaker in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . And this Examint. further saith that her said Husband in his life never Rented any House paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath two small Childn. (to wit) Vere aged three Years & Sarah aged one Year & upwards and being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Samul Clarke

The Mark M of Margt. Munn
