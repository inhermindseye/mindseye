Middx  Willm. Taylor on the Behalf of Eliz: Skilton upon his Oath saith that the sd. Eliz: Skilton is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she never rented any House was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. to his knowledge or beleife that Henry Skilton Father of the sd. Eliz: Skilton lived in & Rented a House of upwds. of Ten Pounds a Year in Channell Row in the Parish of St. Margarett in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of Ten Years & upwds. & hath left the same abt. Eight Years as this Examinant hath been Informd. & beleives that the sd. Henry Skilton never after rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 11th. of Novr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

William Taylors
