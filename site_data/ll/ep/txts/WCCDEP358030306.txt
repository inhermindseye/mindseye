Westmr. 

Eliza. Lewis aged abt. 42 Years upon Oath says she was never married Says she Served 7 Yrs. Apprenticeship with Mr. Arms a Cork Cutter whose Shop was under Exeter Encharge in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Says she Ledged during the whole Term of her said Apprenticeship at her Masters house is Bennet Court in the feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. Says she never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 6th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones  Thos. Lane

The Mark of  Eliza. Lewis
