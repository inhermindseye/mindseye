Middx  Martha Naylor aged about 30 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that about 10 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Sopp at the Wheatsheaf in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £4 and has not since she quitted her sd. Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn April. 22d 1752 before}
