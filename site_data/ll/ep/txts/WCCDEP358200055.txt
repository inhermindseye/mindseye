Middx  Elizth. Whitby Aged about Forty Five Years Wife of Ralph Whitby who is now abroad in his Majestys Service Upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her said Husband at the Fleet London about Fourteen Years ago Says her said Husband was Bound an Apprentice to Charles Young a Taylor in Kings Head Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx with whom he lived about One Year and then his said Master run away and he hath not Since been Assigned Over to any Person Rented House or paid Taxes as this Examinant hath been Informed and which Information she Verily Believes to be true

Sworn ye Day of Jany 1764 Before}

Upon Mr Jones Complt Overseer J. W
