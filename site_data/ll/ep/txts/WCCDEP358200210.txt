Middx  Elizabeth Keats Aged about Twenty One Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Phillips a Pastry Cook the Corner of Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds Quitted said Service upwards of One Year & hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Thomas Spooner a Glass

Cutter had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of May last and at severall other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant.

Sworn the 7th of January 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth. Keats
