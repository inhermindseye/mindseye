Middx  Jno. Harley upon his Oath saith that on the 6th- Day of Octr. 1714 he being at that Time a Singleman & not having any Child or Children was hired to & lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Sherrard Fishmonger in Veare Street by Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds & that since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 13th- Day of August 1741 before}
