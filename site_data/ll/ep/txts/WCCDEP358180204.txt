Middx  Wm Jones aged about 70 Years lodging at Mr. Rylands in Hollis Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon his Oath saith that he is Obliged to go Wandering & begging about the Streets for a Livelyhood having no other Support that he intemarryed with his present Wife  Sarah about Ten Years since that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with his Father  William Jones in the Parish of St. James in Bury St. Edmonds in the County of Suffolk that after he was out of his said Apprentiship he lived in & Rented a House in North gate Street in the sd. Parish of St. James for 10 Yrs: or thereabouts of the Yearly Rent of £4: & paid all Parochiall Taxes for the same that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt: for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne June 8th. 1744 before} Tho Lane

William Jones
