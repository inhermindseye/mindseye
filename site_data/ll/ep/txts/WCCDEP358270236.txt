Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westmr.} to wit Hannah Trivvian aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Robinson in Plymouth Dock Yard for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Four Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Eight years since which time she hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that or the Ninth day of May 1794 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Smith a Hairdresser in Holl's Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of John That one Thomas Cokeley who did live with Mr. Cooper in Bow Street is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examts- Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Thomas Cokeley and no other person did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 7th- day of July 1795 } N Bond

her Hannah Trivvian Mark

Witness B Newman
