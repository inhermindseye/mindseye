Middx to wit Richard Murray Aged about fifty Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes Says he lived as a hired Yearly or Covenanted Servant with one Mr. Davison a Portrait Painter the west side of Leicester Square in the Parish of St Ann in the Liberty of Westminster for five Years and was to receive of his said Master after the Rate of £13 Per. Ann payable as this Examt. thought proper but his Mother was to find him Lodgings for the first three Years and that after the first three Years were Expired he was and did Lodge in his said Masters House till the time of his quitting his said Masters service and after quitting the same he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Rd Murray
