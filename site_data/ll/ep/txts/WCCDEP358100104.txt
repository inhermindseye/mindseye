Middlesex to Wit Sarah Selby aged about 67 Years on her oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant for with one Mr. Story a Taylor in Ship yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the yearly Wages of 5 Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of forty years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 4th. day of Augt. 1783 R Butler

Sarah Selby
