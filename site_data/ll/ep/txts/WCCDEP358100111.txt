Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Morewood Aged about forty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with on Mr. Hicklin a Silkman in Bread Street in the Parish of Allhallows Bread Street London upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Ten Years ago since which time she hath never lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of August 1783 before G Mercer Tho: Collin

Eliz Morewood
