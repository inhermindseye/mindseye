Middx  Susanna Ballard upon Oath Saith She was married to Joseph Ballard (at prest. a Soldier in Scotland ) at the Fleet London upwds. of two Years ago. that she can give no Accot. of the Settlemt. of her sd. husband that she this Examint. never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one year nor ever rented ten pounds a Year or paid parish taxes and that her late father Richd. Stedman lived in and rented house in Cullum Street in the parish of St. Dionis Backchurch Londn. known by the Sign of the Ipswich Arms & never after he quitted the sd house rented ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn Novr. 23d 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Susa. Ballard
