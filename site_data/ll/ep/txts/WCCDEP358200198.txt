Westmr  Elizth. Goldsmith Aged about 30 Years upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband John Goldsmith in the parish Church of Mickham in the County of Surry about Six Years ago Says her said Husband Rented a House in Wild Court in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx Two Years & a half at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds hath quitted said House about three Quarters of a Year & he hath not Since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 22d of Decr. 1764 Before} Fran Cary

The Mark of Elizth. Goldsmith
