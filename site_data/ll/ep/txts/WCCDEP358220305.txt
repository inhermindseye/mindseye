Westminster to wit Elizth. Pritchard Aged about Twenty five Years on her oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with William Dansen a Taylor in Cooks Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Seven Weeks ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes or And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born says that the said William Danson her Master had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in December 1770 and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as aforesaid And that the said William Danson & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of December 1771 before me W Kelyngs

Elizabeth Pritchard
