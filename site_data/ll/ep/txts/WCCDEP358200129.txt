Martha Jones Aged about Fifty four Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Edward Jones who has been Dead about Seven Years with whom she Intermarried in the parish Church of St. Bridgets Vulgurly St. Brides in the City of London about Forty Years ago Says her said Husband whilst a Singleman & without Child or Children Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs Warner at the Peacock Inn Seven Years but what Wages this Examinant say and never after Rented Ten pounds by the Year or paid parish Taxes nor she this Examinant hath not since his Death Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the Day of 1764 Before}
