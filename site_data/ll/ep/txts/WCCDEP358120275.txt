Fras. Band upon Oath saith he was married to his Wife Eliz . at the Fleet London near 18 Years ago and that he was bound apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. John Booker a Sawyer and lived with him about 5 Years of the said term and then his Said Master broke that the last two Years of the sd. term of 5 Years this Examinant lodged in his sd masters house on the East Side of Fleet Ditch in the Parish of St. Ann in Blackfryers London and that he never was assigned over to any other Person nor ever Rented ten pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Feby. 4th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Fras. Band
