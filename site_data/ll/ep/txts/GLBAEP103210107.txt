Middlesex ss  Joshua Arnold maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house of the yearly rent to Ten pounds in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & during such his residence therein he was charged to & paid poors Rates for the same That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any act since he left the said Parish to gain or acquire a Subsequent Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 19th. day of October 1785 before R Davies Josh Arnold
