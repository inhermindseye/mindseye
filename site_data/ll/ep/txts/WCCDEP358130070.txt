Middx  Henry Carey upon Oath says he Rented a House in Cary Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of £40. and lived therein about 5 Years and quitted the same upwards of thirteen Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds by the Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 18th. day of Octor. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

Henry Cary
