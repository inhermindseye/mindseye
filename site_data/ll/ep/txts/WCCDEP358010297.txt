Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Slade aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried was Stephen Slade her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Phillips and Saint Jacobs at Bristol about 14 years ago that she hath Two Children living Vizt. Richard aged about Eleven years and Mary aged about Eight years that her said husband Served his Time as an Apprentice to one Slade in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement and further this Examinant upon her Oath Saith that her said Husband has deserted this Examinant about a Month and refuses to Maintain her

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1789 R Butler

her Mary Slade Mark
