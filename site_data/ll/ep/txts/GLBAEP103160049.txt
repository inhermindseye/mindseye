Middlesex ss}  Mary Redmond maketh oath That about thirteen years ago & not more as she this Deponent now recollects & remembers the Ceremony of a Marriage or pretended Marriage was Solemnized after the manner of the Church of Rome between this Deponent & one Patrick Bryant a Bricklayer (now resident in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate Middlesex ) by a Romish Priest at a Popish Private Chapel or Room in Virginia Street in the Said County by means Whereof she this Deponent then apprehended that She became the wife of the Said Patrick Bryant but Which Marriage So as aforesaid Celebrated she this Deponent has been since informed & advised is a Mere Nullity & of no force or effect in Law That she this Deponent has not before or since been married to the Said Patrick Bryant in any other manner than as before mentioned And that she this Deponent since she Came from Ireland to England has not lived as a hired Servant for a year or been Married save as aforesaid or rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a settlement for herself save as aforesaid

Sworn this. 23. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Mary Redmond
