Middx & Westmr} to wit Philip Stephens Aged about Sixty Nine Years upon Oath Says he never was Married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says his Father James Stephens who has been dead upwards of thirty Years Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one John Robins a Shoemaker in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr. where this Examinant was born Says his Father never Rent a House of Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 10th. Day of Decr. 1768 Before} Tho. Lane

Philip Stephens
