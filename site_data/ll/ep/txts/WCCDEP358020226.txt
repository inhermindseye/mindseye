Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to Wit) William Fitzgerald Aged 75 Years or thereabouts Upon his Oath Saith that he intermarried with Mary Fitzgerald his present Wife at he parish Church of Saint Mary le Strand in the County of Middlesex that since their intermarriage they Rented and lived in a House in Bennetts Court Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds for about Ten Years that since which That he hath quitted said House about 5 Years since which time he has Lodged in Hemlock Court in the said parish of Saint Clement Danes and that he now Lodges in Clements Lane but has not gained any other Settlement Rented any Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year since liwing in Bennetts Court that he has done any no Act matter or Thing whereby he might have gained any other Subsequent Settlement to the best of his Knowledge information or belief

Sworn this 6th. Octr. 1790 before Wm Kitchiner

William Fitzgerald
