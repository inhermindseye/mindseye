St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  (to wit) Ann Green aged thirty two years and upwards on behalf of her illegitimate Son John Maketh Oath and saith that her said Son is now of the Age of Eight Months or thereabouts that he was born a Bastard of her Body on the 8th. day of March last at Mr. Burton's in Castle Street or Thorney Street  in the parish of St. George the Martyr Bloomsbury in the said County of Middlesex and has since been Christened when And this convenient saith she is unable to maintain the said Child

Sworn the 7th. day of Novr. 1791 before us} (The Order Pauper gone)

Ann Green
