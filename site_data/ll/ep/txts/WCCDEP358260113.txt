Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Kirby aged about 57 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wood in New Court the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for four years at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Twelve years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th- day of March 179 6} J Reeves

her Margt. Kirby mark
