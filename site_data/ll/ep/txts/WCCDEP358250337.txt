Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Goodman aged about 22 years now Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes upon her Oath saith that on or about the 14th.. day of June 1795 she Intermarried with Robert Goodman her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said Husband informed this Examinant which Information she verily believes to be true that He was born at Stratford in the Parish of Westham in the County of Essex where his father kept house for several years, That he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes ever bound an Apprentice or ever did any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained any other settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 16th. day of Janry. 1798 before us Ru Clarke N Bond

her Mary Goodman Mark
