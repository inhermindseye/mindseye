Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit William Ambridge aged about 43 years upon his Oath saith that about Nineteen years ago she Intermarried with Elizabeth his late wife who has been dead about a fortnight at the Parish Church of Saint James Westmr. by whom he hath five Children living Vizt. Wm aged about Sixteen years Robert aged about 12 years Mary aged about Eight years Joseph aged about five years and Thomas aged about Nine Months That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in King Street  Golden Square in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr. for about Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of Forty Pounds That he hath quitted said House about Seven years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of July 1795 J Hind J Mathew

his William Ambridge mark

Witness B Newman
