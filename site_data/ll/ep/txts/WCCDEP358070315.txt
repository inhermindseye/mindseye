Middx to wit Ann Cope Aged about Twenty two years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes, Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs Bride in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of £4 and Quitted her said Service on Saturday last since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are like to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born and Says that one George Rochford a Journeyman Jackmaker who lives with her said Mistress is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in January last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said George Rochford and no other Person did beget the same, and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 7th day of Novr 1775 before  David Walker

her Ann Cope Mark
