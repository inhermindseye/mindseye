Middlesex ss}  Susannah Cornelius  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father Levy Cornelius as She this Depont has been informed by her Mother & which She believes to be true was born in the Parish of St Ann in Westmr in the Sd County & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever rented a house or othr Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt for himself That her Said Fathr Parents as She has been Credibly informd & believes were by Birth Foreigners & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That She this Depont never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this day of February 1779 before}
