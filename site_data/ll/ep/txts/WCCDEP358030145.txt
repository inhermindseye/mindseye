Middx  Eliza, Lewis aged about 26 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that about four Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Jeremiah Langham at the Crown in Bridges Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £5 for the Space of three Years & upwards Says she hath not since she left the said Mr. Langhams Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 9th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz: Lewis
