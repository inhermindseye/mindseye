Middlesex ss}  Bridgett Boston maketh oath that divers years ago She rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds in Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve a pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Bridgett Boston
