Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Hewer an Infant of the Age of Two Months Maketh Oath and saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said Mary Hewer is the legitimate Child of Israel and Elizabeth Hewer That Israel Hewer Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Loftus in Charlotte Street  Portland Place in the parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of July 1795 N Bond M Addington

Benji Newman
