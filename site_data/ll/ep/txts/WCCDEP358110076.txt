Middx  July 31st. 1752

Mary Jones upon Oath Saith she is the Wido of Wm Jones by whom she hath a Daughter named Ann aged upwds. of 9 Yrs. and that her sd. late husband was born at Finchley in Middlesex where his father Rented a house

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Jones
