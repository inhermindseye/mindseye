St.. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) Mary Johnson Aged twenty years & upwards Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Thomas Huxley in Fountain Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County of Middx and Liberty of Westminster for fifteen Months (wanting only about fourteen days) at the yearly Wages of Six pounds that she quitted that Service abo on or about the Eleventh day of April last and since hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes And also upon her said Voluntary Oath saith that Joseph Brassington an Apprentice to the said Mr Thomas Huxley is the only real and true father of such Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body some time in the Month of March last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the Child or Children of which she is so pregnant thereon And last that no other person whatever (except the said Joseph Brassington) hath ever had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body

Sworn the 2d Decr. 1791 before me} R Butler

Mary Johnson Mark

Joseph Brossington is a short thin Man light Complexion, Brown Hair tied behind generally at Mr Huxley's his Master till 7 in the Evening when he usually goes to the Plough Beaufort Buildings

Mary Johnson lodges at No. 10 Robin Hood Court  Shoe Lane  Fleet Street at Mr Hopkins's
