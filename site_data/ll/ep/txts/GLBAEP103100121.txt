Middlesex ss  Sarah Davenport  wife of John Davenport a Soldier  maketh oath That her said husband has been absent from her for many Years and She doth not know nor Can Setforth whether he be now dead or living nor where his Place of Residence is That divers Years ago & Previous to her Marriage with the Said Davenport She was lawfully married to one Edward Webb Since deced Who (as She has been Credibly informed & verily believes) did before his marriage with her and when a Single man live as a hired Servant by the Year with one Mr Hyson in the Parish of Burwell in the County of Cambridge and Continue in Such Service for a year and upwards & by means thereof as She is advised gained a legal Settlement there That to her knowledge or belief her said first husband after his Servitude as aforesaid did not live as a hired Servt. for a Year nor rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor Serve any Parish offices nor Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1769 before  Chris. Scott  Cha: Digby

The mark of Sarah Davenport
