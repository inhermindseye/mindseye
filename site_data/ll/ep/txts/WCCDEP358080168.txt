Middx & Westmr-}  John Bennett aged about 32 Years on his Oath says he Intermarrd- with Frances his now Wife at the Parish Church of Wapping in the County of Middlesex upwards of 7 Years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr- Grace a Peruke Maker in Russell Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr - & after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other subseqt- Settlement

Sworn this 26th- Day of Septr - before me  John Cox . Jno. Machin

John Bennett
