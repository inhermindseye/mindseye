Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Carolina Gardner aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that about 6 Months ago she Intermarried with Wm. Gardner her prest. Husband at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn that her said Husband served 7 years apprenticeship to one Mr. Maddocks a watchmaker on Saffron Hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the County of Middlesex that since he quitted said apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that this Ext. hath 3 Children living by her late Husband vizt. Benj. Pallyn aged upwards of 18 years Benjn. aged upwards of 12 years and Charlotte aged upwards of 3 years and that they have  done no Act in their own right to gain a Settlement

Sworn this 2d. March 1787 before me}

(The above Examination Wrong never )
