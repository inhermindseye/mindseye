Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Henry Day one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on Behalf of Sarah Davis who is Ill maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he Verily believe to be true that the said Sarah Dav never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant for five years with one Mr Bearcroft in High Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that she hath left the said Service about 4 years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the Best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 25th. day of Feby 1800 before us} Ws Woodenough John Collins

Henry Day  Beadle
