Middx to wit Elizth. Millett Aged about 21 Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says she was born in the Parish of Welscome in the County of Somerset & Can give no further or other Account of her said Settlement than as before Mentioned says that on the 20th day of March Instant she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr Thomas a Chandlers Shop in Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Patty & is now Chargeable to the said Parish And further Says that One James Sticks  Journeyman Butcher who lately lived at one Mr Finch the Corner of St Martins Court and who lives as she Believes at Mr Farthing No 3 Elm Court Temple up three Pair of Stairs And that the said James Sticks & no other Person did beget the same And that he is the Real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 16th day of April 1784 before R Butler

her Elizth Millett Mark
