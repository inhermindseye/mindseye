Middlesex ss  Mary Hunt the Wife of Abraham Hunt maketh oath that divers Years ago her Said Husband Rented a House known by the Sign of the Ship of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds & Sixteen shillings in the Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & lived therein for the Space of Three Years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her Said husband has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rentor Value of Ten Pounds nor served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That her Said husband is now resident in the Town of Derby in Derby Shire as She has been Credibly informed & Verily believes & where she & her Child John Hunt aged about Ten Years are now going to live & Reside

Sworn this 6th day of October 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Mary Hunt
