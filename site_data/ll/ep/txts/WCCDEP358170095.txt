Middx  Dorothy the Wife of Chas. Lawne  upon her Oath saith that abt. Four Years ago her sd. Husband Chas. Lane Lawne lived in & rented a House in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds for the Space of One Year & upwards that he hath not since he left the sd. House rented any other pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that he now absconds from her & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the 23d. July 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Dorothy Lawne
