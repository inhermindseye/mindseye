Middlesex to wit Rachael Brooksbank Aged about Eighteen years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant to one Mr. Willey a Perfumer in Portsmouth Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Field in ye County of Middlesex for fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago since which time she hath done no other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d Day of July 1778 before.}

her Rachael Brooksbank Mark
