Westmr  Sarah Holloway Aged about Twenty eight Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Dawson opposite the Fountain in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds quitted said Service about Four Years ago, and hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 12th, day of May 1768 , Before W Kelynge

The Mark of Sarah Holloway
