Middlesex ss}  Nathaniel Morris maketh oath That Sometime ago he was hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by & with George Lewinburgh in Burr Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Potter & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two Years & upwards & lodged during Such his Servitude in his Said Masters house in Burr Street aforesd That he has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any act to gain a Settlement Since Such his Sd Servitude and that he was never Married.

Sworn this day of 1778 before}

Nathaniel Morris
