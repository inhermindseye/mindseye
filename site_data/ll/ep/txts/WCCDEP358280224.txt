Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Nowland aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married ever bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year ever Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes That she was born in Ireland And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards an to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Alexander Mc. Kean a Journeyman Taylor who lodges at Mr. Walkers No:1 Three King Court in the Minories is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of September last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Alexander Mc Kean is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 2nd. day of April 1789 } R Butler

her Ann Nowland Mark

Alexander Mc. Kean is a Tall Thin Man dark brown Hair Tied behind fair Complexion

Want. made out.
