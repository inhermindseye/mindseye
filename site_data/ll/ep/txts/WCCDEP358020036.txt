Saint Clement Danes

Middx (ss) to wit Joseph Cook of No. 3 Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Shoemaker upon his Oath saith that he was Married to Elizth. his now Wife at the Parish Church of Laughton le Morthin in Yorkshire the 6th. day of March 1764 that he has One Son named Joseph by his said Wife besides four other Children that his said Son Joseph is about 25 Years of Age that he has never to the best of this Examinants Knowledge gained or acquires any Settlement in his own right unless as this Examinant himself conceived but is now informed Otherwise he might have acquired such Settlement serving him this Ext. for 7 Years under an Indorsement in writing without Stamp on the back of this Exts. own Indres of Apprenticeship that this Ext. was bound an Appr . by Indre duly executed to Richard Binney of Worksop in the County of Notting ham  Shoemaker for the Term of 7 years and Duly served the whole of his sd. Appship to his sd. Master there except. abt. 8 Months the than residue there of which his Father bought out and his Indres were then delivered up that since Quitting his sd. Master's Service he has not rented an house Land or Tenement of the yearly Value of £10. pd. any rates of or Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge informed or belief he may have gained or acquired any subsequent Settlemt. other than in the sd Parish of Worksop in the County of Notting ham And this Ext. further Saith that his said Son Joseph is in an

ill State of Health and unable to Work to Maintain himself and that he this Examinant is poor and unable to Mantain him having his Other Children and Wife to Support

Sworn this 26th. March 1787 before Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordon }

Jos. Cook
