Middx ss The Examination of Elizabeth Dennes Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of ye peace for ye county of Middx residing in or Near the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx this 7th day of April 1755

This Examinant on her oath says that about six Months ago & one William Meekings a Carman who then kept a house in Slaters Court in Blue Anchor yard in the parish of St. Mary White chappell in ye sd. County did prevail with this Examinant to suffer him to lye with her at his sd. house where this Examinant then lived And the sd. William Meekings had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body & so had several times whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she now goeth & is Pregnant And this Exaicon. saith that the sd Child or Children of which she now goeth with & is pregnant as aforesaid is or are likely to be born a Bastard or

Bastards and to be Chargeable to the sd parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate she not being able to provide for ye same and that the sd William Meekings is the true and only father of the sd Child or Children so likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesd. And lastly saith that this Exaicon is voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever

Sworn this 7th day of April 1755 before}

The mark of Elizth. Dennes
