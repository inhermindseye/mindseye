Middx.  James Deakin Senior father of Jas. Deakin aged 19 Years upon Oath says he his Examinant was bound Apprentice by Indenture for seven Years to one Mr. Chapman of Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. Perukemaker and lived with the said Mr. Chapman upwards of One Year and then was assigned over to one Mr. Almett of Clements Lane in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Perukemaker with whom this Examinant lived upwards of three Years and then this Examinant & his said Master Parted by Consent says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

James Deakin

And James Deakin aged abt. Nineteen Years upon Oath says the above named Jas. Deakin is his father and that he this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 1st. of February 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Jas. Deakin Junr.
