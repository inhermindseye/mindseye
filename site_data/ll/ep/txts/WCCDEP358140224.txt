Middx.  Ann Cuddy aged abt. 30 Years Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married says on the 15th. day of January 1754. she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Curtis a Taylor in Horn Court Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. which hath since been Baptized James who is now living and likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same; says that one Jas. Pointer a Glazier who now lives in Exeter Street in the Strand did beget the said Bastard Child on her Body of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said James Pointer (and no other person) is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Feby. 3d. 1759 . before me} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Ann Cuddy

upon the Complaint of Alexr. Allanem of the Overseer of the Poor J: P:
