Westmr.  Frances Clay upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of Thos. Clay (who at present absconds from her & to whom she was married at the Fleet London on the 6th. of Febry 1745 and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband Served three Years & upwards of his Apprentiship with Mr Barradel a Cork cutter in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the End of which time he intermarried with this Examinant as afsd. & never was assigned over nor ever rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes and that she has two Childn. by her sd. husband (viz) Wm. aged 5 Yrs. & Eliz aged 5 Weeks & being Poor is incapable of maintaining them without Assistance.

Sworn June 18th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Frans. Clay
