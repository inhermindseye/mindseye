Westmr.  Sarah Surbutt aged about 23 Years Upon Oath says, She never was Married, nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes, was an Apprentice, or lived  a hired Yearly Servant for One Year says, she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true that her Father Joseph Surbutt deceased Rented a house in Coffin Court, St. Dunstan's Hill in Tower Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the East in the City of London for one Year and upwards of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and Ten Shillings.

Sworn the 4th, day of November 1766 , Before  Fran. Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Sarah Surbutt
