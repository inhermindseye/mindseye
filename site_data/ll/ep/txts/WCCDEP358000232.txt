Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmister} to wit Sarah Lassiter aged 30 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married rented any House or Tenement of svr. a Years Value paid Taxes Saith that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr- Wright in Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of one year a 17 Guineas a year that she quitted his Service about one Year eight and hath not since then gained any other Settlement by living as an lived Yearly Servant or otherwise

Sworn this 22nd. Febry 1786 before R Butler

her Sarah Lassiter Mark

Sarah Lesster
