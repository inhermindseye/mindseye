St Clement Danes July 3d 1794

Middx & Westminster }  Settlement in  St Dunstan's in the West London  wont 7th May/92 & came away 2 Months after Whitsemtide in the followg. year }  (to wit) Mary Pooley  singlewoman aged twenty one years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she was never Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant for fourteen Months & upwards together at the annual Wages of four pounds that with Mrs. Holmes in Felter Lane in the Parish of St Dunstan in the West London that she quitted the same about in or about the Month of July 1793 & hath not  obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And lastly this Examinant saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards

Sworn the 3d day of July 1794 before us } M Addington Russ Clarke

Mary Pooley 's Mark

Witness Jno Talboys
