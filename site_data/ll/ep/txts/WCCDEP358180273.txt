Middx  Fanny Reading  Wife of Thomas Reading upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with the said Thomas Reading the 21st. Day of Febry 1730 at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband has lived Seperatr from her upwds. of Ten Years Says she lived in and Rented a house in Earles Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . for the Space of 3 Months & upwds: at the Yearly Rent of £17 & hath left the same about 4 Years since which time this Examinant hath not rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes neither hath the sd. Thos. Reading rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn the 14th. Janry 1744 before}

Hanny Reading
