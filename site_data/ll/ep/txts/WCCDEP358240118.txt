St. Clement Danes 27th June 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Pancras  (to wit) Maria Taylor aged 24 Years & upwards Upon Oath saith That she never was Married That she was bound an Apprentice by the parish of Edmonton to Mr. Fletcher in Ivy Lane in the City of London about ten Years ago That he after she had served there about two or three Years they removed to No Tottenham Court Road in the parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex where she this Deponent continued to Serve her said Master as an Apprentice as aforesaid In about two Years that she quitted then quitted the her said Master's Service by their consent & since Leaving her said Master she hath never lived as an hired Yearly Servant for twelve months together or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever Whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And that she is pregnant & unable to Maintain herself

Sworn the 27th. day of June 1792 before Us} Sampr Wright  Thos. Gordon
