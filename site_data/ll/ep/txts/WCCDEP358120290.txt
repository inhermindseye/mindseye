Middx  Edwd. Polhill aged 62 years upon Oath saith he was married to his prest. wife Mary Pollhill  at the Fleet London on the 13th. July 1747 that near 40 Years ago (being then a Singleman) he was hired to live wth. Mr. Copes take a Taylor for two years certain at the wages of two Shilling Pr Week, that he lived under this hiring and agreemt. with Mr Copestake during the said term of two Years and lodged all that time upon Saffron hill in the Liberty of Saffronhill  hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the parish of St. Andw. in Holbourn in the County of Middx and has not Since he left his sd Service rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Febry 27th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Edwd. Polhill
