Middx  Sarah Harendine aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an apprentice or yearly Servt. nor rented ten pounds by the year or pd Parish taxes and that her late father Abrahm. Harrendine decd. rented a house in the Strand between Surry Street & Norfolk Street lived therein & pd Scot & lot Sevl. Years & there Dyed & her late mother never after her fathers decease rented ten pounds by the year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn May 5th 1754 before}

Sarah Harendine
