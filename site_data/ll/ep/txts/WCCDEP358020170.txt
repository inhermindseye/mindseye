Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Mary Jones aged Nineteen years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married, rented House is Tenement of the yearly Value of ten Pounds lived as an hired yearly Servant for one year together served as an Apprentice, paid any Taxes or did any other Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have acquired any Settlement in her own Right and further this Examinant saith that she was born at Wingfield in the County of Bertis and is, as she has been informed and verily believes the illegitimate Child of Mary Clifton Taylor who afterwards married to Daniel Clifton of Wingfield aforesaid and died about three Months ago and this Examinant further saith that she has been informed and believes that her said Mother in her life time was examined before a Magistrate and Same that this examined was her Child and also to the Identity of this Examinants Father.

Sworn this 29th. Octr. 1789 before N Bond Tho Vaughan

her Mary Jones Mark
