Middlesex ss}  Archibald Campbell maketh oath that he is the Father of a Female Child named Sarah Davison aged about Six Years & half who was born of the body of one Sarah Davison (lately deced) in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County the which Child was born a Bastard by reason that he this Deponent was never lawfully married to the said Childs Mother and which Child hath been lately passed from the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel Middx to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate as being the Place of her Legal Settlement

Sworn this. 6. day of April 1785 before  Robt. Smith

Archibald Campbell
