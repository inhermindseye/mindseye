Middx.  Ann Scott aged 25 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an apprentice or Yearly servd. for one Year rented ten Pounds a Year or paid parish taxes and that her late Father & Mother

Scott (both deceased) rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £12 in May pole Alley in the parish of St Clemt Danes  & lived therein till the time of their respective Deaths.

Sworn 14th Novr. 1753 before}
