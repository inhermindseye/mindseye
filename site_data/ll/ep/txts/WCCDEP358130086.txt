Middx  Mary Price aged about 23 Years Upon her Oath Says she never was Married and that she never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Say's she is the Daughter of Stephen Price by Hannah his Wife both Deceased Says she hath Often heard her said late Father Stephen Price say he Served An Apprenticeship of Seven Years with One Charles Sayer in the Parish of Kingston in the County of Hereford .

Sworn the 26th. Day of Novr. 1755 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Price
