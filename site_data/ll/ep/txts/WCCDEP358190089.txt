Middx.  Lowry Jones aged abt. 70 Years lodging at Mr. Webbs in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath says she is the Widow of David Jones who has been dead abt. Thirty Years says she has been informed and which information she verily believes to be true that her said husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with a Weaver in the Parish of Llannidlees in the County of Montgomery and Rented a house of £5 by the Year in the said Parish of Llannidlees for sevl. Years. says she has not since his death Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn Jany. 22d. 1761 . before}
