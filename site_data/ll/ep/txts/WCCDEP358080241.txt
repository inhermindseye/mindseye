Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Spencer Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Years Says she was bound and Apprentice by the Churchwd. and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clement Danes to one John Stacey a Shagereen Case Maker at Epping Forrest in the Parish of Wanstead in the County of Essex untill this Examinant shod. Attain her Age of Twenty One Years or Day of Marriage as by the Indnes relation being there may appear Says that her said Master afterwards went and Rented a House at Statford in the said County of Essex when her said Master soon afterwards run away and what is become of him she Cannot Say; Says that soon after her said Master run away her Mistress Rented and lived in a House in Bethlem Green in the Parish of St. Mathew Bethlem Green for upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards and where this Examinant Continued to live till her Indenture were Expired and this Examinant further saith that she does not Knows what is become of either her Master or Mistress but this Examinant Positively Saith that she lived with her said Mistress upwards of Eight Months before she left her Mistress and that she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of May 1778 before  Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

her Elizabeth Spencer Mark
