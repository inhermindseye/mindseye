Middx   Mary Clarke aged about 21 Years Lodging at Mr. Lochheads opposite Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Kedson at the Sign of the Bushell in Wisbick in the Isle of Ely for upwards of One Year at the yearly Wages of £3 quitted the Same about Two years ago and hath not since rented any House or been a hired Yearly Servant for One year And further upon her voluntary Oath saith that she was on the 15th: day of this instant January 1762 Delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of the said Mr Lochhead in the Strand and Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid which hath since been Baptized by the name of Thomas John and that the same is yet living and likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the Same Also Says that one Thomas Slaoen a Chairman whose Stand is at the Corner of Duke Street St. James's  did beget such Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body about the beginning of the Month of May last at which time he got her with Child of the Child she was delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the 26th. day of January 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Clarke
