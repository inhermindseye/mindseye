Mary Briggs on Behalf of Eliz. Clarkston a Lunatick upon Oath says that the said Eliza. Clackston lived a Servant with one Mr. Cowdrey in Manchester Court in Chanell Row in the Parish of St Margt. in the Liberty of Westmr. for one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of Five pounds or there abouts and hath Quitted her said Service about one year 9 months an this Examinant has been Informed and verily beleives and that she hath been further Informed and beleives the said Eliz Clarkston was never married

Sworn 12th. May 1749 before} Wm: Jones .

The mark of M Mary Briggs
