Desertion

St Clement Danes July 2d. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  Children (to wit) Mary Tweedy aged about 37 years Wife of John Tweedy upon oath saith That she was Married to her present Husband at the parish Church of St George Queen Square on or abt. the 9th May 1775 That her said Husband has rented and resided in a House in Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and Liberty of Westminster for about 5 years last past at the annual rent of £13 or thereabouts and continues now to rent & beside therein as she verily believes That she hath three Children now living by her said Husband now living namely alexander  Alexander aged 16 years on the 9th April last Mary born on the 21st day of January 1783 and Elizabeth born on the Sixth day of May 1786 who are were all with this Deponent's said Husband in Clements Lane aforesd. in on yesterday Evening That he is as she verily believes of sufficient bodily strenghth and ability to Work for  and maintain her this Deponent as well as himself & Children but neglects and refuses so to do unless compelled by Law and That she is Poor and unable to maintain her hersef herself witht. Assistance

Sworn the 2d. day of July 1792 before me} R Butler

Mary Tweedy's Mark

B. Newman

Witness

July 4th Apprehended & agreed to take his Wife home & discharged paid Captain & fees
