Middx & Westmr.}  Penalope Hunt aged about 40 Years on her oath says she never was married rented house paid taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship, says, she lived a hirely yearly servant with one Mr. Osborne a Pilot at the Waterman Arms the Cornor of Bread Street Ratcliffe Cross in the Parish of Saint Dunstans Stepney for three Years at the Yearly Wages of £4.10s. hath quitted her said service upwards of 18 Years ago & hath not since she quitted the same over rented house paid taxes or lived an hirely yearly servant for one Year

Sworn this 27th. Day of April 1776 before M Addington J Hubbard

her Penelope Hunt Mark
