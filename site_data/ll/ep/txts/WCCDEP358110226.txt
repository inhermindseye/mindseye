Middx  Eliz Elmer als Lawrance upon Oath Saith she lived a Yearly Servant whilst a Singlewoman with Jno. Chamberlain in the Parish of Darking in the County of Surry for one Year at the yearly wages of £2. and that after she quitted that Service she was married to Jno. Lawrance at the Fleet London of whose Settlement She can give no Account and that the sd Jno. Lawrance dyed abt. 8 Years ago That abt. Seven Years since she went to a house ye Fleet with Saml. Elmer since deceased with Intent to be there married to him but as both herself and the said Elmer were at that time quite intoxicated with Liquor this Examinant can't tell whether the Marriage was Solemnized or not but she is pretty certain there was no Ring used, and that the sd. late Saml. Elmer told her the morning after this Affair happen'd that he did not know whether they were married or not, that no other person besides one Howell was with them at the Fleet & that the sd Howell is since deceased That about a year before this Margt. Elmer (the wife of the above mentioned Saml Elmer,) was Transported and the sd. Saml. Elmer told this Examinant he had heard his said Wife

was dyed in the Voyage And this Examint. further Saith she can give no Accot. of the sd. Elmers Settlement

Sworn April 4th. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Eliz Elmer ats Lawrance
