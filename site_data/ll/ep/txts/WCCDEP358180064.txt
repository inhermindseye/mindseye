Middx  Alice Squire upon her Oath saith that about Three Years ago she intermarried with her present Husband John Squire who is gone from her & where he now is this Examinant knows not That the sd. John Squire about Seven Years since being at that Time a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mitchell Esqr. in Lincolns Inn Feilds in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex for the Space of one Year but at what Wages knows not That since he left the sd. Mr. Mitchell he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 9th- Feb: 1742 before}
