Middlesex ss.  Ann Jones  Singlewoman maketh Oath that she has been informed that her late Father Samuel Jones deceased was duly Bound Apprentice by Indenture to his Uncle Samuel Fisher of Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph Without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Stove Smith for the Term of Seven Years and served the whole of his said Apprentice with his said Master there, that her said late Father never Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or did any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement that, her Mother died in the Workhouse of the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate about Four Years ago, And that she this Deponent has never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any Parish or Place for the space of One Year together or done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement in her own right and that she never was Married

Sworn this Day of 1794 before}
