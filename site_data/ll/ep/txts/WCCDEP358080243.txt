Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Cotterell Aged about Eighty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says she served Seven Years Apprentice ship to One Robert Moore a Taylor in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and after she was out of her said Apprenticeship she never did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of May 1778 before me  Abrm Baley

her Ann Cotterell Mark
