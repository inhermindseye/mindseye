Middlesex ss}  John Cotsford maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house of the Yearly rent of Thirty Pounds in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the Said County & was Charged to And paid Poors Rates for the same that he has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath five Children namely John Aged about thirteen Years Jane aged about Eleven Years Robert Aged about nine Years Benjn. Aged about Seven Years & Thomas Aged about four Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his late wife deced to whom he was lawfully married none of which Children have as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement for themselves

Sworn this. 27. day of July 1786 before J. Hawley R Davies

John Cotsford
