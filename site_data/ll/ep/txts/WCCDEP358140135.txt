Middx.  Rice Price aged upwards of 50 Years upon Oath says he Intermarried with Phebe his Wife who is aged abt. Sixty Years in the Parish Church of St. Sepulchre London abt. 14 Yrs. ago says he Rented a house in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of thirty Pounds lived therein abt. two Years & hath quitted same abt. Eight Years & hath not since Rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn this 24th. of June 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Rice Price
