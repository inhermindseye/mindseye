Mary Cross  Wido . of Anthony Cross who dyed upwds. of 4 Years since upon Oath saith she Intermarry'd with the sd. Anthy Cross at the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes aforesd. about 15 or 16 Years ago and that her sd. late husband (as she hath been informed and believes) lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds in the Parish of St. Iles in the City of Oxford and pd. Scot and Lot & Served the Office of Constable there and that neither her sd. late husband after he Quitted the sd. house nor this Examinant since his Death have ever rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 2d. 1748 before Wm: Jones

The mark of Mary Cross
