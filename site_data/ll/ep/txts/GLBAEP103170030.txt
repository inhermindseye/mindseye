Middlesex ss}  John Andrews of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Beadle maketh oath that he has been informed by one Mrs Barranger who is now in the Workhouse belonging to the parish of St James Clerkenwell in the sd County & Mother of one Richard Barranger a poor man now in the Workhouse belonging to the sd parish of St Botolph without Aldgate & some what disordered in Mind & which information this Deponent believes to be true That her husband Richard Barranger deced & Father of the Sd Richard Barranger her Son rented a house of Twelve pounds a year in the Said parish of St James Clerkenwell  & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement & that the Said Richard Barranger her Son is legally Settled in the Said parish of St James Clerkenwell in right of his Said Fathers Settlement there He the Said Richard Barranger the Son having never been bound an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 6th day of December 1776 before  Saml. Wood  Jasper Clarkes

John Andrews
