Middlesex ss}  Mary Dunnin the wife of Daniel Dunnin maketh oath that her said husband who now belongs to his Majestiesship Froudyant is by birth an Irishman & to her knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a settlement in England That she this Depont previous to her Marriage with her sd husband & while a Singlewoman was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr Saunderson Victualler in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in such service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for near the Space of two years & has not since lived as a hired servant for a year elsewhere nor done any Act to gain an settlemt Save what she has Acquired by marriage as aforesd That she hath two Children namely Daniel aged about eight years & nine months & Mary Ann aged about Seven years & four Months the lawful Issue of this Depont by her sd husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this 11. day of Febry 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Dunnin
