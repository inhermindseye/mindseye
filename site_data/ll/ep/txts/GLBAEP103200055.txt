Middlesex ss}  William Elzee maketh oath that he has been informed by his late Father Peter Elzee deced & which he believes to be true that his sd Fathr duly Served his Apprenticeship with a Shoemaker in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & that in Consequence thereof he was by a Certificate under the hands & Seals of the Churchwns & Overseers of the Poor of the sd Parish of Saint Botolph witht Aldgate in the year 1713 addressed to the Churchwns & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Dartford in Kent acknowledged to be legally Settled in the sd Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate That this Deponts sd Father before & after the granting Such Certificate lived in the sd Parish of Dartford where this Depont was born & where his sd Fathr died That this Depont resided there with his sd Father & When of the Age of twenty Six Years he was there lawfully married to his now Wife  Mary That after Such Marriage had this Deponts Sd Fathr Rented a house of the yearly Rent of Six Pounds & also Anothr house of the yearly Rent of Four Pounds & ten Shillgs at one & the Same time in the sd Parish of Dartford makg togethr the Yearly Rent of Ten Guineas That this Depont aftr his sd Fathrs Death rented a house of Four Pounds & ten Shillings in Dartford aforesd & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the same That this Depont. has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes elsewhere nor was he ever bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a settlemt for himself than as before is Set forth

Sworn this. 8.day of October 1783 before R Davies

William Elzee
