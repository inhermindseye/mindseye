Middx to wit Eleanor Warden aged about 26 years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Warden deceased with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . about Nine years ago & who died about 5 years ago as she has been Informed & verily believes and by whom she hath no Child or Children living says that being a Widow & without Child or Children living she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Alcock a Sword Cutter on Ludgate Hill in the Parish of St. Martin Ludgate  London upwards of one year at the yearly Wages of 6..10..0 hath quitted her sd. Service upwards of four years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or or.wise howsoever.

Sworn this 14th. day of Febry. 1784  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

her Eleanor Warden mark
