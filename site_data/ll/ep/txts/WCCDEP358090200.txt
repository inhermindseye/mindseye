Middx to wit Charles Halfpenny Aged about Forty five years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Rebecca his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Brides London about Seventeen years ago and by whom he had One Child both of which died about Twelve years ago Says that afterwards being a Singleman & without Child or Children he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Chapman a Plumber in the Capacity of a Clerk in Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years and Upwards at the yearly Wages of Forty Pounds hath quitted his said Service about Seven years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for One year

Sworn this 22nd. day of Feby. 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner  Abrm Baley

C Halfpenny
