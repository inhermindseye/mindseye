Middx.  Gabriel Jeffs aged abt. 33 Years upon Oath says he was Married to Mary his wife at the Fleet London abt. Ten Years ago by whom he has Two Children Mary aged abt. Eight Years and Ann aged abt. two Years; says he served seven Years apprentiship with Mr. George Price a Barber in St. Clements Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for One Year or Otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 22d. Feby. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Gabriel Jeffs
