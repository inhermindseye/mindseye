Saint Clement Danes Feby 13th. 1794

Middx and Westminster }  St Margt. Westmr . (to wit) Charles Foster Aged 40 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was bound out an Apprentice to Mr William Auld a Gardner in Cabbage Lane Petty France in the parish of Saint Margarets Westminster in the County of Middlesex that he served his sad Master for upwards of One Year and About and that he Quitted his said Master about 26 Years Ago and that since that time to the best of this Examinant Information knowledge or belief he has done no Act Matter or Thing whatsoever whereby to gain any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn this 13 day of February 1794 N Bond W: Bleamire

Chas Foster
