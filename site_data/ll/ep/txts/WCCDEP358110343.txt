Middx.  Ann Beecham upon Oath saith she is the wife of Joseph Beech of whose Settlement she can give no Account and that she was bound apprentice for 5 Years to Mrs King a Quitted & after having lived with her sd. Mistress about two Years ran away & went to live worth Mr Carr at the Crown Inn at Harlow in Essex with whom she continued about three Years and about a fortnight after she left Mr Carr intermarried wirth the sd Joseph Beech and that a short time after her coming to Mr Carr her sd Mistress King came to her & convented to her staying his service

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Beecham
