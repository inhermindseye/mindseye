Middx  The Examination of John Gullis aged about 59 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 24th. Day of Febry 1740 Who upon his Oath saith yt. about 30 Years ago he lived as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Saml. Boone  in the Parish of Tottenham high Cross in the same County Butcher for the space of one Year at the Yearly Wages of £10 And this Examinant further on his Oath saith that he hath not since he left his sd. Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine himselfe.

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

John Gullis
