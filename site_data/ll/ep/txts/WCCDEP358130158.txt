Middx  Richard Eaten on behalf of Sarah West aged about 12 Years upon oath says that her Father John West who dyed about five Months since was a Parishioner legally settled in the Parish of Timsbury in the County of Somerset as this Examinant has been informed by the said John West & believes And further says that the said Sarah West was never an Apprentice or yearly Servant

Sworn the 15th. day of May 1756 . before}
