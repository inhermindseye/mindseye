St. Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster }  St Andrew Holborn  the Bars (to wit) Mary Taylor aged 21 years and upwards singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Evans No. 5 Dean Street Red Lion Square in the parish of St Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex  for one year and upwards at the Annual Wages of £4, that she quitted that  service about 5 years  and three Months ago and hath not since lived as an hired yearly servant for one year together in any one place, Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year, paid parish Taxes or done any other Act or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement

Sworn the 24th Augst. 1792 before us  Sampn Wright  Hitchen

Mary Taylor
