Middlesex .  William Giffard aged about 58 Years upon Oath says he was Married to Mary his Wife at the Fleet London abt. 27 Years ago says his father Willm. Giffard Rented a house in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the yearly Rent of Fifty Pounds; lived in said house abt. Thirty five Years and there dyed; says that he this Deponent never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Wm. Giffard
