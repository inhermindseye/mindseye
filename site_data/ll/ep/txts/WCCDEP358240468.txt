St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  To Wit, Jane Edmonson aged 34 years or thereabouts saith upon Oath that she was married to John Edmonson a Private Soldier in the Horse Granadiers Jany: 2. 1780. (by Bans) at the Parish Church of St. Martins in the fields that before her said marriage she lived as an Hired yearly servant with Mr Bell Bookseller Strand in the Savoy Precinct for the Space of Two years at Eight Pounds On Ann. and this Deponent further Saith that the said John Edmonson left this Deponent 5 months after they was married and that this Deponent has not heard any thing of him since he Deserted her in May 1780. but since his Desertion she has Cohabited with Wm: Philips a Taylor by whom she has a Female Illegitimate Child named Ann Phillips Born in Water Lane Fleet Street 1790 but has not  any Child or Children by Husband John Edmonson now living and this Deponent saith that since her marriage with the said John Edmonson she has never acquired any subsequent Legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents information or belief & that she is Quite unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn the 16th. day of April 1795 W Addington J Mathew

Jane Edmonson
