Middx  Ann Thomas Wife of Edward Thomas Lodging at the house of Mr. Gibson dealer in Coals in Salisbury Street in the Strand upon Oath saith she very well knows Richard Thomas an Infant aged about three Months whom she verily believes to be the lawful Issue of Benjamin Thomas (who is not to be found ) by Judith his Wife Says the said Benjamin Thomas lived in and rented a House in that Part of Exeter Street which is in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for the Space of about Two Months at the Yearly Rent of Twenty five pounds or thereabouts quitted the Same about Eighteen Months and hath not since rented any House or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinant's Knowledge and also says the said Richard Thomas was Born at a House opposite Green Street in Castle Street in the said Parish of St. Martin in the Fields and that the Same is now with Mr. Thomas in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn the 18th. day of April 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Ann Thomas
