Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Harding aged about 70 years now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upon her Oath saith that about Thirty three years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Harding her late Husband who has been dead about Six Months at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bone That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Church Street in the Hamlet of Mile End New Town in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex for Five Years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Eight Pounds and Paid the Poor and all other Taxes due for the same That after he quitted said House he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief s he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Janry. 1798 before us } M Addington Edwd: Naish

her Hannah Harding mark

Witness B Newman
