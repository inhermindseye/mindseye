Middx.  Mary Harris aged abt. 21 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married says she lived & hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Martin Flynn at the Castle in Claremarket for five Years at the Yearly Wages of two Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abt. three Years since which time she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Yr.

Sworn the day of Febry 1757, before}
