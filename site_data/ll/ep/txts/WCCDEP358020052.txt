Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Eaves aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Clarke a Hatter and Honer in High Holborn in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted said Service about 18 Months ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Janry. 1788 D Walker

her Ann Eaves Mark
