Westmr. to wit Charles Yalden Aged about Thirty Two Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Godard at the Craven head in Dury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Thirteen Pounds besides Diet and Lodgings hath quitted his said Service about four Years ago & hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 11th. day of April 1772 before me} Tho Lane

Charles Yaledon
