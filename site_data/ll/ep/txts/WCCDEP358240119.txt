Middlesex & Westminster }  St Giles in the fields  (to wit) Mark Wiltshire Aged 24 Years & upwards Upon Oath saith that he never was Married That he was placed as a Covenant Servant with Mr Bugow in Monmouth Court in the Monmouth Street in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex Shoemaker to learn his Business that her served his said Master about two Months and then left him without consent That no lodged with his Father William Wiltshire at the Three Tuns in Oxford Street near St Giles's Bounds in the parish of St Ann in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County during such service and that since leaving his said Master he hath not obtained any subsequent Settlement And This Deponent further saith That his said Father is a parishioner legally settled in the parish of St James Bristol as he hath informed this him this Deponent which he believes to be true And this Deponent saith his said father also informed him that he was born the said parish of St James Bristol in lawful Wedlock and never did any Act whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement otherwise than as upward in the parish of St Ann

Sworn the 28th day of June 1792 before Me } D Walker

Mark Wiltshire Mark
