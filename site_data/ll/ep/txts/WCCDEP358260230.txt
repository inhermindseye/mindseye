Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Darbin aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Kemp the corner of the Deans Street  Oxford Road in the Parish of Saint Ann in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself without assistance from the Parish

Sworn this 18th. day of Novr. 1797 before us M Addington Robinson

her Susannah Darbin mark

Witness H Day Beadle
