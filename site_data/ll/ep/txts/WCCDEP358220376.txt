Westmr. to wit Dorothy Gey Aged about Sixty one Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Gey who has been dead about Eighteen Months ago with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew in the County of Middlesex about Twenty Eight Years ago Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Dolley an Ironmonger in Holbourn above the Bars in the Sd. County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Nine Pounds as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and after he quitted his said Service he never Rented House or paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husband Death

Sworn this 29th. day of August 1772 before} R. Butler Sam Wright

Dorothy Gey
