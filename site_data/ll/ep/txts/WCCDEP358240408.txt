St. Clement Danes May 12th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Parish of Edgeware Middx but no Settlemt there the House being sit. in the Parish of Stanmore Pawa }  Vide Examr page 324 this Book  (to wit) Hannah Rose  singlewoman aged about thirty years Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never served as an Apprentice never lived as an hired yearly Servant or did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own right, that she has been  of informed which she verily believes that she is the legitimate Daughter of John and Hannah Rose both deceased that her said Father Rented and Resided in a House known by the sign of the Rohita Hart in Edgware in the Parish of Edgware in the County of Middlesex for several years at the Annual Rent of £40 or thereabouts that he died there and her Mother  continued to Rent and Reside in the  same House for a considerable time after his Death that she quitted the same about fifteen years ago to the best of this Deponent's Recollection, and died with without obtaining any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 12th day of May 1794 before us } M Addington Edw: Read

Hannh. Rose's Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
