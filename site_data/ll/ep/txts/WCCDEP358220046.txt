Middx & Westmr ss  Pleasance Coker aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married and that she now lives a hired Yearly Servant with Nehemiah Spicer in Fountain Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Seal Engraver and hath so done upwards of Two Years and a Quarter at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one Robert Bates a Taylor who lives in Bird Street Grosvenor Square had Carnal knowledge of her Body the latter end of December 1768 and on the first day of January last past at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid and that the said Robert Bates and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 18th. day of July 1769 before me} Thos Kynaston

her Pleasance Coker mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
