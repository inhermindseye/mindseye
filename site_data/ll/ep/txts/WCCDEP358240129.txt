Saint Clement Danes July 25th. 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields  (to wit) Rachael Collier Aged 62 Years and upwards Single woman upon Oath Saith that She never was married That She lived as an was Hired as a Yearly Servant with Mr Prince in Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the paid County of Middx upwards of forty Years ago that she served under such hiring for about one Year and six Months that during the last 3 or four Months of such her Service she Resided with her said Master at a House situate in the Strand near Durham Yard in the parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the said Liberty that since she quitted he said Master's Service she hath not lived as an hired Yearly Servant, Rented any House or Tenement of £10 a Year Value paid any parochial Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby she might obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief belief, And that she is Poor and unable to maintain herself without Assistance, and is now become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 25th. July 1792 before us  Sampr Wright M Addington

Rachell Collier
