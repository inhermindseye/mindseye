Westmr.  Crispin Pratt aged about Twenty Seven Years upon Oath says he  bound himself Apprentice by Indenture dated the 21st, day of March 1766, to Wm: Newberry of Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Brushmaker for the term of three Years, and is at present in the Service of the said Mr. Newberry, under the Covenants of the said Indenture of Apprenticeship says, he hath a Wife named Martha with whom he intermarried in the Parish Church of  St. Clemt. Danes aforesaid in the Month of July 1766,

Sworn the 22nd, day of August 1767 , before} 

Crispin Pratt
