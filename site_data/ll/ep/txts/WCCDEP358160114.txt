Middx  The Examination of Ann Billington  Widw . Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken ye. 23d. Day of Febry. 1739 who upon her Oath saith that her late Husband Rt. Billington before his Intermarriage with her & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Bagnell Sugar Baker in the Parish of

for the Space of Sevll. Years at the Yearly Wages of £16 That Mr. Bagnell haveing tell Sugar Houses his Servants used to be moved from one to ye. other but that the last Place of Resident of her late Husband Rt. Billington was in the Service of the sd. Mr. Bagnell was in Prescott Street  Goodman Fields in the Parish of St. Mary White Chapell where he Continued to live for the Space of Six Months Months or thereabts. & after the sd. Intermarriage continued to live & reside in the same Service & under the same Agreemt. for sevll. Years
