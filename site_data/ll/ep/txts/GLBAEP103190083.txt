Middlesex ss  Mary Wicker the wife of Joseph Wicker maketh oath that her Sd husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Dunkirk That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with one Smith who Carried on the Trade of a Smith in the Parish of Henley upon Thames in the County of Oxford & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 18. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Wicker
