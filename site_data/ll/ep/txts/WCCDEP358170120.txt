Middx & Westmr.}  Eliz: Raplee  Wido . of Richd. Raplee upon her Oath saith that abt. Twenty two Years ago she was married to her sd. late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holbourn & that her sd. late Husband hath been dead abt. Fourteen Years & further saith that Jno. Raplee her sd. Husbands Father lived in & rented a House in Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the  Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of for the Space of Years & further saith that her sd. Husband in his Life Time nor she since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 26th- of Augt. 1741 before}
