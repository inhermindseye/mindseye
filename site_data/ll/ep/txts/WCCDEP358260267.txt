Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Bryan aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wright in Hill Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Eighteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of April 1798 before us W Kinnard Edwd: Naish

her Catherine Bryan mark
