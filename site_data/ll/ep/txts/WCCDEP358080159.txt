Middx  Thomas Mann aged about twenty nine Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented house or paid Taxes Says he lived as a Covenanted Servant with one Francis Green a Breeches Maker at Claverly in the County of Salop for three Years and after he was out of his Apprenticeship or under the above Covenant he never gained any or- subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yrly Servant

Sworn this 19th- Day of July 1777 before M Addington

Thos Mann
