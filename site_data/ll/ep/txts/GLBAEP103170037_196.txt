Middlesex ss  Ann Lambert  Singlewoman Matketh oath that She has been informed by her pate Fathr George Lambert deced & which She believes to be true That he was bound to Served his Apprenticeship with his Father George Lambert also deced in the parish of Alarden in the County of Rent & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement That She this Depont never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant in any one place for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right That She hath a Child named Thomas Wilson Aged about Twenty Months born of her Body in Crown Court in the parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County of Middlesex & is a Bastard & that one Thomas Wilson late of the Same parish Mariner is the Father of the Said Child.

Sworn this 27. day of December 1776 before  Jno Sherwood

Ann Lambert
