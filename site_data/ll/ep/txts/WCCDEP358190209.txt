Middx & Westmr}   Sarah Large aged 20 Years removed from the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster by Order dated the 28th. of Decemr. 1761 upon Oath saith she never was Married or bound an Apprentice That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Butler a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middx for Two years at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds Quitted the Same about five Months and hath not since rented any House or paid Land Tax or Poor Rate And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the Same when Born And that one Geo: Pulley a Journeyman Perukemaker who is to be found at a Picture Shop next the Kings head Tavern in Fleet Street London did beget such Child or Children he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body on the 13th. day of May 1761 and once afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 28th day of Decr. 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Large
