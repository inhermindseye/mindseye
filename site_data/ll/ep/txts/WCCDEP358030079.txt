Middx 

Cathn. Waldgrave aged about 28 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Wall a Tobacconist at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of two Years and upwards and hath left her said Service about two Years Says she lived upwards of two Months the latter Part of her said Service with the sd. Mr Wall in the Fleet markett in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn London Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr Wall rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry. 13th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Cathn. Waldgrave
