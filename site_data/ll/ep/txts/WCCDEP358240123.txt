St. Clement Danes 14th July 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Jane Jones aged about twenty five Years Singlewoman Upon Oath saith That she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Wilson Brass Founder Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster for fourteen Months or thereabts. at the Annual Wages of five pounds that she quitted that Service about Six Years ago and hath not since lived as an hired Yearly Servant for a twelve month together Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year or otherwise obtained any legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 14th day of July 1792 before Me } Wm Kitchiner

Jane Jones 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys  Beadle
