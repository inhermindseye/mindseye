Middx  Mary Bourne  Wido . aged 77 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that her late Husbd. Jno. Bourne hath been dead abt. 5 Yrs. & this Examinant not haveing any Child or Childn. lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mrs. Bramston in New Castle Court in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of £2. for the Space of 1 Yr. & a Qur. & hath left her sd. Service abt. 1 Month that being poor & Infirm is not able to maintain herself

Sworne 2d. Janry 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Bourne
