Middlesex ss}  Martha Cammak  Singlewoman Maketh oath That She was born in the Parish of St Swithin in the City of Lincoln Where her Parents who are dead were legally Settled That She was never an Apprentice nor hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in her own right That She hath three Children born of her Body in Red Cross Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County all of whom are Bastards and illegitimate namely Nathaniel True Aged about ten years & one Guarter of a year Mary Ann True  aged about Seven years & John True Aged about four years none of whom have gained a Settlement in their own right

Sworn this 31 day of December 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

Martha Cammak
