Middx  Edward Cooper Aged about Thirty Years upon Oath Says he never was Married and that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Clarkson Threadmaker and Haberdasher in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds besides diet Washing & Lodging hath quitted said Service about Six Years and hath not Since Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 4th. Day of January 1769 } Tho: Lane

Edward Cooper
