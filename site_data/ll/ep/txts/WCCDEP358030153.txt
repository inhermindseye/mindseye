Middx  Thos. St. Hill aged abt. 75 Yrs. upon his Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servt . whilst a Singleman with the Rt honoble the Earl of Clarendon at the Yearly Wages of £20 for the Space of Seven Years & upwards Says his Lordships Town house was in Berkley Street in the Parish of St Geo Hanover Square in the Libty of Westmr but not being large enough for his Lordships Family this Examinant lodged upwds. of Six Months the last Part of his said Service at a Shoemakers the Corner of Albermarle Street in the said Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr Says he hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 22d. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Tho: St: Hill
