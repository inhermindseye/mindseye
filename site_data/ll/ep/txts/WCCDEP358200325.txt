Westmr.  Elizabeth Line aged about 48 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of James Line who has been dead upwards of Nine Years says that being a Widow she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Houseman a Cabinetmaker on Clerkenwell Green in the Parish of St. James  Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pound's hath quitted said Service about five Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 20th. day of November 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The Mark of Elizabeth Line
