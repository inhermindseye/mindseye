Middx  Ann Morris aged 43 Years & upwards upon her Oath saith that on or about the 7th. Day of Janry 1736 she Intermarried with her present Husband  Robt. Morris who hath been gone from her upwards of 3 Weeks & this Examinant hath not since during that time heard from h or knows where he is Says her sd. Husband was born in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office as she hath been Informed and beleives Says her Husbands Father John Morris Lived in & Rented a House in Horns Court in the Back of St Clements in the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £11 & lived in the same Severall Years that after the sd. John Morris left the sd. house he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as she hath also been Informed & beleives

Sworne 22d. Septr. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Ann Morris
