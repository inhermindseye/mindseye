St Clement Danes 28th Febry. 1792

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Ann Wood  Singlewoman Aged 48 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Joshua Constable  Butcher , the Corner of Clare Street Clare Market in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster , for 13 years and 3 Months, at the Annual Wages of £5, that she quitted that service about Fourteen years ago and Since hath done no Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any other legal Settlement And that she is unable at present to support herself without Assistance

Sworn the 28th. day of February 1792 before me R Butler

Ann Wood
