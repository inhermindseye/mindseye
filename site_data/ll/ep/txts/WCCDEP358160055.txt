Middx  The Examination of Mary Masters  Singlewoman Aged 60 Years & upwards taken the 4th. Day of June 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that about Nine Years ago she this Examint. lived a Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Samworth in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds And this Examint. further upon her Oath saith yt. she hath not since yt. time Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & that she is now Poor & Old & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Masters
