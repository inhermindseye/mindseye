Westr to wit Mary Parkison  Widow aged about forty Years on her Oath Says that she Intermarried with William her late Husband at the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate about Fifteen Years ago & who has been dead about Six Years ago, and by whom she has no Children now living and further on her Oath says that her said Husband lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Wallington a Soap boiler in Red Cross Street in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate London upwards of five Year at the Yearly Wages of forty Pounds and since her said Husbands death she has never done any Act Whereby to Grain any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th day of March 1775 - before R Butler Sampn Wright

Mary Parkison
