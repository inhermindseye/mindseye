Middlesex ss  John Wells maketh oath That divers years ago here a house in Saint Martin Le Grand in the Parish of Saint Leonard in Foster Lane in the City of London at the Year Rent of Sixteen Pounds & lived there for the Space a year & upwards & was Charged to and paid Parish Tax for the same and by means there of as he is advised gained a legal settlement there and this Depont. Saith that he ha not since rented a house of the yearly Rent or value of Ten Pounds nor served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or beli  hath he done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a wife named Elizabeth

Sworn this 15th. day of March 1770 before  Chris: Scott  Cha: Digby

John Wells
