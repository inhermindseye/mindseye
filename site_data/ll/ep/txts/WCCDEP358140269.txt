Middx.  Hewer Elizth: Jones aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Samuel Jones (at sea) with whom she intermarried in the parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes abt. ten Years ago says her said husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Noell in Essex Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for one year at the yearly Wages of Ten pounds and then intermarried with this Examinant says he continued to live with Mr. Noell in Essex Street under the same hiring abt. One year after his Marriage. and quitted sd. Service abt. Eight Years and a half and hath not since Rented Ten pounds a year or paid parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 28th. July 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Hewer Elizabeth Jones
