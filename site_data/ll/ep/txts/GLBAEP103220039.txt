Middlesex ss  William Read maketh oath that he knows one Francis Zweich a German Who as this Deponent has been informed & believes hath lately absented himself from his wife and Children & left them destitute of Support That he the Said Francis Zweich Sometime ago rented of this Depont a Part of this Deponents Dwelling house in Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at the yearly rent of Seventeen Pounds and found his own Furniture and resided thereon for about the Space of four Months & Paid this deponent a Quarters Rent for the Same and this Deponent Can give no other Acct. respecting the Settlement of the Said Francis Zweich That he this Deponent has been informed & believes that the Said Francis Zweich hath left behind him his wife  Catherine Zweich & two Children namely Rose aged about twelve years and Charles aged about nine years his lawful Issue Who are all likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 17 day of May 1786 before  Robt, Smith

Wm Read
