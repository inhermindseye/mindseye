Westmr. to wit Richard Massey Aged about forty five Years on his Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his late Wife at the Parish of Wensome in the County of Somerset about Nineteen Years ago and by whom he hath one son named John Aged about Eighteen Years but who is now provided for And this Examinant further saith that he was born in the said Parish of Wensome in the said County of Somerset but never gained any other Settlement either by Renting House paying taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant before marriage.

Sworn this 18th. June 1773 before}

his Richd. Massey mark Witness Prts Sidebotham
