Middlesex ss}  Mary Johnson the widow of Robert Johnson deced maketh oath that her Sd late husband never did any Act to gain a settlement in England & She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents Save that her Mother was born (as She has informed this Depont) in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County where She this Deponent was also born That She this Depont many Years ago was bound Apprentice to Daniel Phillimore of the Parish of St Mary White Chapel in the Sd County Silk Throwster to learn the Sd Trade or Business But that during the whole of Such Apprenticeship She this Depont allways lodged in East Smithfield in the Sd Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate & She this Deponent has done no Act to gain a Settlement for herself than as before is Set forth

Sworn this. 22. day of Decemr 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Mary Johnson
