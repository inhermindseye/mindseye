Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Joseph Walbrook who is Ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been Informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said Joseph Walbrook rented and lived in a House in Great Bath Street in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for Sixteen years at the yearly rent of Sixteen Pounds that he hath left the said House several years Since which time he hath not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th day of Jany 1800 before us W Woodenough Winter

B Newman
