Middx  Thomas Marshal upon Oath saith he was married to his late wife Margt. Marshall in Shoreditch Church about 23 Years ago by whom he has a Son John aged abt. 19 Years who never was an apprentice or yearly Servant for one year and that he this Examinant Served Seven years apprentiship with Jno Brooks a Water Gilder and lodged during the last Six Years thereof with his sd Master in St Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes and that he never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn March 8th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Tho Marshal
