Westmr  Christian Rice Aged about 23 Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed, Says she was Bound an Apprentice to Miss Smith a Mantua Maker in Warder Street in the Parish of St. Anns in the Liberty of Westminster and hath been out of her said Apprentiship about 2 Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 4th Day of January 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant R Kilsters

The Mark of Christian Rice

Upon Mr Olivers Complt Ovr J. W
