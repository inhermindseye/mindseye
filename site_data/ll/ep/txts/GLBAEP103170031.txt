Middlesex ss}  John Andrews of the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County Beadle maketh oath That he wells knows a Child named Dorothy Granger aged about nine years who on the Twenty Second of December one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy five was duly bound Apprentice by the Parish officers  of the Sd parish of St Botolph without Aldgate to one Elizabeth Taylor of the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch  Tambour worker That he this Deponent has been Since Credibly informed & believes the Same to be true That the sd Mistress Taylor removed with her Said Apprentice to the parish of St Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surrey in the month of March last where the Said Apprentice Continued with her Sd Mistress till Some time in the Month of June last when the Mother of the sd Apprentice Wrongfully & against the Consent of the sd Mistress Elizabeth Taylor took the Sd Apprentice away from her Sd Mistress & She is Since become Chargeable to the sd parish of St Botolph without Aldgate & hath done no Act to gain a Settlement Since her Departure from her Sd Mistress as aforesd

Sworn this 6th day of December 1776 before  Saml. Wood  Jasper Clarkes

John Andrews
