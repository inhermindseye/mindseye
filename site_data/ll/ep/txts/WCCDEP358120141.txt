Middx  Susanh. Sidey aged 55 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Wm Sidey (now a Pensioner in Greenwich Hospital ) to whom she was married abt. forty Years Since in the Parish Church of St. Ann Soho to That her sd husband rented a house in Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes of the yearly Rent of £23 lived therein upwds. of One Year & quitted the sd house abt. Eleven Years ago Since which he has not rented ten pounds by the Year or pd Parish taxes

Sworn July 6th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Susa. Sidey
