Westmr.  Ann Stalling aged about 19 Years on her Oath Says she never was married and that she now lives a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edmund Brickleton a Chymist in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr and hath so done upwards of One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one John Ryley of Great Wyld Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex Dealer in Coals had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of January last past but the Particular day she cannot ascertain but remembers it was on a Sunday and at many other subsequent times at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid and says that the said John Ryley and no other person did beget the same

Sworn this 16th. day of August 1769 before me} Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Ann Stalling

Witness Fras Bishop
