Before Birth Bastardy

St Clement Danes 14th day of July 1792.

Middx and Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Mary Tomlinson aged 25 years and upwards singlewoman Upon Oath saith that She never was Married, That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Robt. Weston Esquire at No 27 Norfolk Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County Middlesex for three years (wanting about fourteen days) at the annual Wages of Ten Guineas that she quitted that Service on or about the 13th day of February last, and hath not since obtained any other legal Settlement to her knowledge information or belief And this Deponent Upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she never was Married is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish  That Joseph Banner  by Trade a Baker who was late a  Footman to the said Robert Weston Esquire and is now as she believes believes was his Uncle Mr Banner Hardware man at Birmingham in the County of Warwick did beget is the only real true Father of such Child Children where with she is so pregnant as aforesd he and no one she having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body in or about the Month of December last and at several times since at some or on of which times he did beget the said Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant the

on the Body of her this Deponent and that she is at present distressed and unable to defray maintain herself or to defray the Expences of her lying in without Assistance

Sworn the 14th day of July 1792 before No me} R Butler
