Middx  Mary Geaton upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of John Geaton who never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes to her knowledge and that she has been informed and believes her sd. late husbands father lived in a house of his own in High Street Portsmouth

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Geaton
