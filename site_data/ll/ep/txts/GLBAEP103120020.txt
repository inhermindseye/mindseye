Middlesex ss}  Jane Kemsell  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain yearly Wages with Joseph Hart in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Victualler who kept the Sign of the Black Horse Alehouse there and Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish That She this Depont. hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor to her knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th Day of November 1772 before R Pell

The Mark of Jane Kemsell
