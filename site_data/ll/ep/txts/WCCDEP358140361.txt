Middx.  Alice Wilkinson aged abt. 65 years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Matthew Wilkinson (if living) says her said husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year with Robt. Osborn in the Parish of Rushton in Northamptonshire as her said husband informed her and which Information she Verily believes to be true.

Sworn this 19th. day of March 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Alice Wilkinson

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Jos: Burnthwte . Overseer of the poor J: P:
