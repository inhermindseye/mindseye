Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Wells  Single woman maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with George Hainster of Eastsmithfield in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Victualler at the yearly Wages of Five pounds & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two Years and upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish That She this Deponent has not Since lived Elsewhere as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor hath She ever been married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of January 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Wells
