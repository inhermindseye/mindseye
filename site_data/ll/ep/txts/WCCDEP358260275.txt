Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Adam Wilcox Burbidge on behalf of Susannah Mackenzie who is ill upon his Oath saith that he hath been informed by the said Susannah Mackenzie that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Inglish in Shoreditch in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the yearly Wages of Pounds that she quitted said Service about Four years since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1798 before us W Addington Robinson

A W Burbidge Beadle of St Clement Danes
