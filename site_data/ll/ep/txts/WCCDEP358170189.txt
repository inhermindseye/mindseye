Middx  Mary Shoegar on Behalfe of Thomas Walker her Father upon her Oath saith that the sd. Thos. Walker lived in & Rented a House in St. John's Street in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds for the Space of Six Months & upwds. & hath left the same for the Space of Eight Years or thereabt. that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 2d. of Decr. 1741 before}
