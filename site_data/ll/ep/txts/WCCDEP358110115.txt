Robt. Pearse upon Oath saith he rented house in Burleigh Street (the Second from Exeter Exchange ) at the yearly Rent of £24 and lived therein about two years quitted the Same about in the year 1740 and has not Since Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr. 17th. 1752 before}

Robt. Pearce
