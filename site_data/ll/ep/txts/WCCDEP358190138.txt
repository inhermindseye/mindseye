Middx  Mary Lock aged 56 Years upon Oath saith that since her Husband's Death having no Child or Children living she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stevens a Schoolmaster in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds Quitted the Same about 15 Years and hath not since kept House paid Parish Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 5th day of June 1761 Before}
