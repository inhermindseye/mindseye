Middx to wit Elizth. Peck aged about 24 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with One Mr. Neuk Farmer Rekeld near Diss in the County of Norfolk Upwards of 12 Months at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Years and since which time she hath near lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further on her Oath says that an the 10th. of October last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Willsin Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath been Baptized by the Same of Jane and is become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & Unable to maintain the same And further says that one Thos. Williams late a Silk Mercer in Fleet Markett and Who now uses Mr. Baileys No. 56 in Bread Street near the the Old Change or at Mr. Festern Silk Mercer No. 77 Hennsditch is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body at his the said Mr. Williams House in Gutter Lane Cheap Side in January 1781 and at Sevl. Different times afterwards at one of Which times he got her With of the Female Bastard Child at Which She was Delivered as aforesaid And That the said Thos. Williams & no other person did beget the same & that he is the rent & true father thereof

Sworn this 5th. of Feby. 1782 . before R. Butler

Elizabeth Peck
