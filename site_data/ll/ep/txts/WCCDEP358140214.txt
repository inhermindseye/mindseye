Middx.  Eleanor Scriven aged abt. 35 Years upon Oath says she is the wife of Wm. Scriven with whom she Intermarried in the Month of Decr. 1752. and that she hath two Children by him (to wit) Eleanor aged upwards of five Years and Mary aged upwards of two Years says her said husband Rented and lived in a house in Marsham Street in the Parish of Saint John the Evangelist in the Liberty of Westminster for Six Months and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and hath quitted same upwds. of One Year says her said husband now absconds from her and hath not since he quitted the aforesaid house in Marsham Street Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to the best of this Deponents knowledge or belief

Sworn this 25th. Jany. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eleanr. Scriven

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Thos. Matravers Overseer J: P:
