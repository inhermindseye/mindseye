Westmr.  Eliza. Sweatman aged upwds. of 22 Years upon Oath saith she was never married Says her father Benjamin Sweatman deceased rented a house in Blacheaven Court in Seething Lane in the parish of Alhallows Berking London of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards & lived therein upwds. of Ten Years & paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 23d 1746 before  Tho Lane

Tho Mark of Eliz Sweatman
