Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  B & T to wit John Berryman aged about 34 years upon his Oath saith that he never Rented any House Tenement Lodging of £10 a yearly that about 14 years ago he went to live as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Gray who kept the White Horse Inn in Friday Street in the parish of Saint Margt. Moses in the City of London that he lived there for the Space of Two years at the yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted said Service about 12 years ago since which time he has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year or done any Act to the best of this Exts. knowledge Information or belief whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Janry. 1787 D Walker Rups: Clarke

John Berryman
