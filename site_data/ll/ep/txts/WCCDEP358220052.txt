Westmr.  Dove Ash aged about Seventy Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Catherine his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Anns so he in the Liberty of Westmr . in the Year One Thousand Seven hundred and Twenty Two but what is become of his said Wife this Examinant Cannot set forth he having parted with her about 30 Years ago Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Reakstrow a Cabinet maker to wit five Years in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields and Two Years the latter Part of his Apprenticeship in Maiden Lane in the Parish of Saint Pauls Covent Garden where his said master removed to And never after Rented House of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 26th. day of July 1769 before Us R Buller W Kelyngs

Dove Ash
