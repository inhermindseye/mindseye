Middlesex ss}  Mary Newman the wife of Thomas Newman maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That She has been Credibly informed and Verily believes the same to be true That her said husband duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Mr John Jones a Carpenter in the Parish of Saint James in the City of Bristol & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 3d day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Newman
