Westmr.  John Williams aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath says he served five Years and upwards the first part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Thomas Sandery a Waterman and Lighterman in Milk Alley Wapping in the Parish of St. John at Wapping in the County of Middlesex and then Entered on Board one of his Majesty's Ships of War, and never Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 2nd. of May 1766 . before} Paul Vaillant  Fran. Cary
