Middx & Westmr.}  James Gullesor aged about 57 Years or his oath says he never was married rented house or paid Taxes says lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Lemon at Star Eating house in George Yard Lombard Street in the Parish of St. Edmund the King London for one Year and half at the yearly Wages of eight pounds hath quitted her Service about nine Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever rented house paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th. day of May 1776 before  John Cox  John Croft

his James Gullisor Mark
