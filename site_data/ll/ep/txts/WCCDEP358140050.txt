Middlesex  Elizth. Francis aged abt. 32 Years upon Oath says she never was married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Rebecca Dechamps in the house of Mr. Saml. Dent a Taylor in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex for two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted the said Mrs. Dechamps Service abt. two Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Parish or Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 26th. day of Octr. 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell Elizabeth Francis
