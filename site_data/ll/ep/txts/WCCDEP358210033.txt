Westmr.  Hannah Allen aged about 26 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Christian Long in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and Quitted her said Service in May 1765 since when she hath not Rented house or paid Taxes

And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the Same when born, says that Wm: Darwell who is Turnkey at the Gatehouse Prison in Westminister had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of April 1765, and at several other times since; at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said Wm: Darwell and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn March 7th. 1766, before} Fran Cary .

Hannah Allen
