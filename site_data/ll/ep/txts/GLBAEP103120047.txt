Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Caley  singlewoman maketh oath that about one year & half ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year With one Mr Rose a White Smith in the Parish of Saint James in the Town of Bury St. Edmunds in the County of Suffolk and Continued in such Service for the Space of one years & upwards at the yearly Wages of Thirty shillings & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there That she this Deponent hath not since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 18th day of May 1773 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Elizabeth Caley
