Middx  William Pullen aged about Forty three Years upon Oath says that he Rented a Farm in the Parish of Rippon in the County of York at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and lived thereon four Years and hath quitted the same upwards of two Years Says he hath a Wife named Elizabeth aged about thirty three Years and one Child by her named William aged about Seventeen Months

Sworn the 15th. day of Decr. 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

Willm Pullan
