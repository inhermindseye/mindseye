Middlesex  Catherine Middleton aged 70 Years Wife of Dorsey Middleton aged about 70 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Dorsey Middleton about 37 Years ago & that her said husband rented a house in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £24 & lived therein abt. 8 Years & hath quitted the same abt. 5 Years Since which he has not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn March 26th. 1751 before  Tho Lane .

The mark of Cathn. Middleton
