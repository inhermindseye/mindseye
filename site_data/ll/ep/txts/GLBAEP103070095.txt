Middx ss  William Ricketts Maketh oath that about a Year ago this Deponent Rented a House in High Street in the Parish of St. Mary White Chappell in ye County of Middx known by the Sign of the White Horse and Leaping Rail and paid Thirty pounds a Year Rent for ye Same and lived there for abt. the Space of one Year and a Quarter and paid all Parochial Taxes And by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. hath not Since gained any Subsequent Settlement And Saith that he hath a wife Named Hosea and one Child named William abt. five Weeks old the lawfull issue of ship Dept. & his sd. Wife

Sworn this 15th day of Janry 1754 before  Richd: Ricards

Wm. Ricketts
