Middlesex ss}  Margaret Mahoney the Widow of Daniel Mahoney deced maketh oath that She cannot give any Account respecting her sd late husbands Settlement Save that She was by birth an Irishman That previous to her Marriage with her husband & While a Single woman She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Robert Evers a Victualler in Lower East Smithfield in the parish of St Botolph with Aldgate in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one Whole year there & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since done any act to gaint another Settlement in her own right save her Marriage as afore said

Sworn this 11 day of August 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Margaret Mahoney
