Westmr.  Abigail Conner Aged about Fifty Years upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Barnaby Conner at the Fleet London about Twenty Years ago Says her said Husband Rented a House the Back Side of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards hath quitted said House about Ten Years and hath not Since Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the Day of 1764 Before}
