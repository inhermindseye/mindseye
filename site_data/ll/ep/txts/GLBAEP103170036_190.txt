Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Rowning the widow of William Rowning deced maketh oath That her Said late husband in the month of Janry 1771 took a Lease jointly with one John Upton who is yet living of the Exors of Richd Mount late of the Precinct of St Castle  in the Sd County Carpenter deced at the yearly reserved Rent of Thirty Six pounds of a Dwelling house & Shops with the Appurts Situate in the Sd Precinct for a Term of years which is yet unexpired with usual Covenants That her Sd husband there upon went & resided & Continued in the Occupation of the Said dainsed Dwelling house Shops & premes Where in the Tradcota Smith was jointly Carried on by him & the Sd Upton for their Mutual benefit till the death of this Deponts sd husband Which happend in the month of Septemr 1775 That the Sd Upton did not at all reside or dwell on the Sd demised premes till about the Space of four Months previous to the death of this Deponts Sd husband That her Sd husband as She is advised by the means aforesd was legally Settled in the Sd Precinct at his death he dying there That She Since his death hath not been Married nor done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She has two Children namely Elizabeth aged about ten years and William aged about eight years the lawful issue of this Depont by her Sd late husband to whom She was lawfully Married.

Sworn this 28 of December 1776 Before  Parkr Rowlands  Jno Sherwood

Elia Rowning
