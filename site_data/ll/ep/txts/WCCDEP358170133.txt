Middx  John Davis aged about Forty Five Years upon his Oath saith that he is a Singleman & was never married & that abt. 32 Years ago he lived a Yearly hired Servt - with one Henry Roberts Taylor by Clements Inn Gate in the Parish of St- Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the 1st. Year & Six Pounds for the 2d. Year & that since he left his sd. Master he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 19th- of Septembr- 1741 before}
