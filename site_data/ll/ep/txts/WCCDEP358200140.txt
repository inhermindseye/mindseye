Westmr.  Elizth. Lewis Aged about Thirty four Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Turner a Chineas Man in Piccadilly in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath quitted said Service about Six Weeks

Sworn the 7th of Septr. 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizth. Lewis
