Middx  The Examination of Jno.. Brown aged abt. 21 Yrs- Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 27th: Day of April 1745 Who upon his Oath saith that abt.. Eight Years ago he lived with one Mr. Wm- Dayles in the Parish of Saint Stephen in Coleman Street  London Victualler for the Space of Three Years & upwards & had for such his Service Meat Drink Washing Lodging & Cloaths & further on his Oath saith that he never rented £10 a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & being Sick & Poor is not able to maintaine himself

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous J Poulson

John Brown
