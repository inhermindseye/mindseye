Middx  Richd Airs upon Oath saith he Rented a house in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middlx of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds and lived therein upwds. of two Years quitted the sd house about Seventeen Years ago & has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 19th. 1752 before}
