Middlesex ss  Esther Kilhog  Single woman Maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Peter Jewett who then lived in East Smithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and Continued there in such service for the Space of Eight years at the yearly wages of Four Pounds and by means thereof as She is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that she has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor hath she was paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Settlement since her servitude as abovementioned

Sworn this day of February 1770} Ester Kilhog
