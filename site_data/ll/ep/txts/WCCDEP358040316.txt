Middx  Mary Robins Wife of Joseph Robins upon Oath saith she Intermarryed with her sd. husband upwds. of 30 Years ago in Beswick Street Chapell and that the sd. Joseph Robins Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Shawa Baker in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlx and never rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Years or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 5th. 1748 before Wm: Jones

The mark of Mary Robins
