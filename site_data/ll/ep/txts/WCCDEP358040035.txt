Westmr.  Mary Symons aged upwds. of 20 Years removed from St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs by Order dated the 17th. Day of July Instant upon Oath Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Church wardns. & Overseers of the Poor of the sd. Parish of Saint Andrew in Holborn to Mrs. Williams a Milkwoman in Hollis Street Claremarket with whom this Examinant continued to live about two Years & a half and then this Examints. sd. Mistress went into Kent a Hopping & gave this Examinant Liberty to provide for herself where ever she could & this Examinant hired herself to one Mr. Clayton a Gardiner in Garden Court in the Temple & lived with the sd. Mr. Clayton Six months and informed her

Master where she lived and he advised her to be a good Girl Says after she quitted Mr. Claytons Service she never lived in any Place with the knowledge of her Master or Mistress other than by their free Consent to provide for herself as well as she could

Sworn July 20th 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Simonds
