Middx  Henry King upon Oath saith he was married to his Wife  Mary King at Mayfair Chapel on the 3d. of Septr. 1750 and that about 3 Yrs. ago he rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds in Mercey Street in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and lived therein upwds. of two months and has not since he quitted the sd house rented ten pounds by the Year or paid Parochial Taxes

Sworn May 30th. 1754 before} Tho Lane

Henry King
