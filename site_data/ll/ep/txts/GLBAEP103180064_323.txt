Middlesex ss}  Stephen Stevens maketh oath that a Child named Margaret Holiday aged about Seven Years & now residing with this Deponent in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County is the Daughter of one William Holiday whose place of Residence is unknown to this Depont & which Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph Aldgate That divers years ago the Sd William Holiday was bound Apprentice by the Trustees of the Charity School of the sd Parish of St Botolph Aldgate to William Jones of the Precinct of St Catherine in the Sd County Cooper & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship in the Sd Precinct That the Sd Willm Holiday to this Deponts knowledge or belief has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Settlement Since his Apprenticeship nor hath his Sd Danr done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 14 day of Septr 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey W Blackmore

Stephen Stevens
