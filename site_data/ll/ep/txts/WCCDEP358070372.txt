Middlesex & Westmr.}  Mary Garthwaite Aged about Thirty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Charles Garthwaite who has been dead about a Year and an half ago and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. James Westmr about Nine Years ago and by whom she hath one Child Named Charles Aged upwards of Seven Years Says her said Late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Johnson Court Charing Cross in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and lived therein to the time of his Death since which time this Examinant or her said son have gained no other Settlement.

Sworn this 14th. day of May 1776 before R. Butler Wm. Kitchiner

Mary Garthwite
