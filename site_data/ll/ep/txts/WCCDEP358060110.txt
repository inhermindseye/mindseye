Middx  Mary Brogden aged abt. 22 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice by the Church wardens & Overseers of the poor of the parish of St. Clement Danes to Mrs. Walker in Peter Street in the sd. parish of St Clemt Danes tile she should attain the Age of 21 Yrs. or Marriage and that she never was assigned over rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn May 15th. 1751 before}
