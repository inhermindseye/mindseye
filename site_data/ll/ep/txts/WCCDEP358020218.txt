Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Charles Adney Singleman upon his Oath saith that he lived as an hired Yearly Servant for eighteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of thirteen Guineas with Captain Gregory that he was discharged from his said Masters Service about Seven Months ago at Chiswick in the County of Middlesex and since then hath gained no other Settlement to the best of his Knowledge

Sworn this 31st- July 1790 before Philip Dyot D Walker}

Charles Adney
