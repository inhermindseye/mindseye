Middx  Thos. Yarrow aged upwds. of 70 yrs. upon Oath says that whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Henry Honour a farmer in the Parish of Stanbridge in the County of Bedford for two years at the yrly. Wages of £5. & never after he quitted his sd. service Rented ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a yrly. Servt. for one yr.

Sworn the 9th. May 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Thos. Yarrow
