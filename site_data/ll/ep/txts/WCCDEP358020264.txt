St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Thomas Westwood Aged 69 Years or thereabouts Upon his Oath saith That he intermarried with his present Wife  Judith Westwood at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes on the twenty Eighth twelfth Day of February in the Year of our Lord 1759 That since then that he Rented and lived in a House in Stanhope Holywell Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for several Years at the Annual Rent of £16. which House he quitted about thirteen Years ago & that he hath not Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or done any other Act where or thing whereby he might acquire any Subsequent Legal settlement Since quitting the said House as above Stated

Sworn before me 12th April 1791 R Butler

Thos: Westwood
