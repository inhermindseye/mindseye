Middx ss  William Wiltton Maketh oath that about Sixteen years ago he was bound apprentice to Roger Wetcheat a Mathematical Instrument maker of the Parish of St. Sepulchres London with whom he Served three years and then by Consent he was bound & turned over to John Dibbett a Fisherman of Barking in ye County of Essex with whom he Served three years and upwards and then was pressed into ye Sea Service And this Depont. saith that he was never a hired Servt by ye year nor gained served any parish offices nor Rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor gained any settlement subsequent to his sd. Apprenticeship And farther saith that he hath a wife Named Mary and a Child named Eliz aged about Fifteen Months the lawfull issue of this Depont. & his sd wife

Sworn this Seventh day of October 1754 before us  Richd Ricards  Chris Scott

The mark of Wm. Wilton
