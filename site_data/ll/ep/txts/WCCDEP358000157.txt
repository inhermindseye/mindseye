St. Clement Danes

passd. to Shadwell  Ann Gibson aged 16 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented a House or paid Taxes ever served an Apprenticeship or ever lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one year Saith That she was (born in Spring Street ) in the Parish of Saint Paid Shadwell in the County of Middlesex where this Examinants Father and Mother lived in Lodgings that she never knew of any the Settlemt. of either her Father or Mother and they are both now dead that since her Birth she hath not according to the Best of her Knowledge Information or belief gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of Novr. 1785 before us J Addington Thos. Gordon

Ann Gibson
