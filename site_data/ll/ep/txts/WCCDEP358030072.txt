Middx  Mary Merritt upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Jones at the Oxford Arms in Horton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for the Space of Seven Years or thereabouts and hath left her sd. Service upwards of 9 Years Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Jones Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 21th. Decr. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Merritt
