Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Green aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months bound an Apprentice Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes that she has been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that she is the illegitimate Daughter of Isabella Green and was born at the House of a Mrs. Parke the corner of Clare Court Blackmoor Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn this 28th- day of June 1796 } John Collick

her Ann Green Mark
