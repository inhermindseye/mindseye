St. Clement Danes April 30th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C.D.  to wit Mary Griffiths  Widow aged near 40 years Upon Oath saith that She was Married to James Griffiths her per late Husband at the Parish Church of Portsmouth in or about the year 1779 or 1780 (by Banns) that he died in or Died about October 1792 that he never lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Walker using Sun Drury Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for a year and upwards at the Annual Wages of £9 (as she is informed and believes) while he was single and no Child that he quitted the same about two years before their Marriage and never afterward did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief that since his Death she this Examinant hath not been Married or otherwise obtained or become in Ritted unto any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the 30th day of April 1794 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Mary Griffiths 's Mark

Witness Jnr Talboys
