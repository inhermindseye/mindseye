Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Godfrey aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the 4th. day of Septr. 1778 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of one Mr. Wheeler in Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christend by the Name of James That one Edward Cooper who lives as a Servant with one Colonel Fotheringham at Ealing Place in the County of Middx is the Father thereof he having had Carnal of this Ext. Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Edward Cooper is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 15th. day of Octr. 1787 before me} Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Godfrey Mark

Man taken 17 Octr. 1787 : by Pedlingham & Talboys a Security Bond given
