Westmr.  Richard Mansfield Aged about Sixty Two Years upon Oath Says he Rented a House in Kings head Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds Ten Shillings lived therein about Eight Years and hath quitted Same about Twenty Years And hath not Since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 3d. Day of Febry 1769 Before me} Tho Lane

Richard Mansfield
