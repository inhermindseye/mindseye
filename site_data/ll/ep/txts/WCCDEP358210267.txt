Westmr.  William Walford Aged about Forty five Years, Upon Oath says he intermarried with Dorothy his Wife at the Fleet London about Twenty Years ago, by whom he hath two Children now living (to wit,) Jane Aged about Thirteen Years and William Aged about Nine Years; says he Rented a House in the Great Minories in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate London for Seven Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds, quitted same about One Year, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 22d. day of April 1768 , Before} Thos: Balack  Thos. Kynaston

William Walford
