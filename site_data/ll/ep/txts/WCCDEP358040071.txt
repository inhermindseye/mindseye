Westmr. Middx  Hannah Noxon Wife of Henry Noxon a Soldier in his Majestys Service upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the sd. Henry Noxon in the Parish Church of Little Hereford in Worcestershire Says her sd. husband whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Nill in the Parish of Tedston in the County of Worcester aforesaid at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds & ten Shillings for the Space of two Years or thereabouts Says he has not since he left the said Mr. Nills Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Child viz Jas. Noxon aged one month

Sworn Septr. 28th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Hannh. Noxon
