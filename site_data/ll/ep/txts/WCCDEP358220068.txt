Middlesex & Westminster ss:  Jane Jones aged about 22 years on her Oath says she Never was Married and she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Richard Holloway at the Crown and Anchor Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of 6 Pounds hath Quitted her said Service about four Months ago and hath Not since rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one John Goodman at at the said Crown and Anchor Tavern in the Strand had Carnal Knowledge of her Body on the first day of March last past and at sevl. different times afterwards at one yr which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that the said John Goodman and no other person did beget the same and then he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 7th. day of Septr. 1769 before me} Tho Fellows

her Jane Jones mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
