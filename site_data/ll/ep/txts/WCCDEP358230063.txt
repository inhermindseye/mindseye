Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Shepherd aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on or about the Fifteenth day of March 1796 she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Lyath in Hemmings Row in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the name of Edward

Sworn this 6th. day of May 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

her Hannah Shepherd Mark
