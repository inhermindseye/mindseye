Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Day aged about 53 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Brown the corner of Saint Martins Street in the Parish of Saint Martins in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Seven Years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Two Years since which time she hath not to the best of her knowledge Information or belief done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1797 before us} N Bond Jno Groves

Mary Day
