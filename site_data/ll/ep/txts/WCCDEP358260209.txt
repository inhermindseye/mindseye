Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Luck aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Eleventh day of June 1792 she Intermarried with Thomas Spencer Luck her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint Brides by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Prudence aged about Four years and Ann aged about seventeen Months That her said Husband never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months nor ever bound an apprentice nor where he was born she this Deponent doth not know that she this Deponent never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born at Kettering in the Parish of Kettering in the County of Northampton where her father kept house for several years and that she this Deponent never did any Act whereby she might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of August 1797 before us } Russ Clarke  Joseph Moser

her Elizabeth Luck mark

Witness B. Newman
