Middx  Sarah Mold upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid parish Taxes or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt . for one Year that her late father rented a house at the Back of Greys Inn Gardens in Greys Inn Lane in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £15 and lived therein several Years & there dyed

Sworn Decr. 6th 1750 before} Tho Lane

Sarah Mould
