Middx.  Henry Chinnery aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house of upwards of Ten Pounds a Year in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and lived in the same upwards of one Year and hath quitted the same upwards of Twenty Years and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 21st. day of April 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Henry Chinery
