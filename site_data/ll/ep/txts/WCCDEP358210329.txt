Middx & Westmr} (to wit) Sarah Nicholls Aged about 30 Years on her Oath Says that She is the Widow of Francis Nicholls lately deceased with whom She Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about Nine Years ago by whom She had Several Children all of whom are dead except one Named Mary about four Years Old Says her said Husband before his Death lived a Yearly hired Servt . with John Vernon  Esqr . of New Bond Street in the parish of St George Hanover Square in the said County of Middx in the Capacity of a Coachman for the Space of fourteen Months or Upwards but what Wages her said Husband had this Examinant cannot Possitively Say Says Since her said Husbands Death She never kept a House Rented a Tenemt: of Ten Pounds by the Year paid any Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person

Sworn the 11th Day of Novr. 1768 Before us} Jno Wright  Peter Blanck

her Sarah Nicholls Mark
