Middlesex ss  William Shepheard maketh Oath that about Twenty Six years ago he Rented the Publick House called or known by the Name of the Anchor & Crown in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , & paid Twenty Eight Pounds a year Rent for the said House, and lived in the same about Six Months, that he has not since he quitted the said House Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of Jany. 1798 before}
