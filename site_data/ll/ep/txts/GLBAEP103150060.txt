Middlesex ss}. The Voluntary Examination of Catherine Doe of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this 10th day of october 1775

Who Saith that she is now with Child and that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that one Arthur Fraser late of the said parish of Saint Botolph without aldgate  Armorer and now on Board the Ship Talbot on a Voyage to the East Indies is the Father of the Said Child And this Examinant Farther Saith that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Yearly Wages with one Thomas Webb of the said parish of St Botolph without Aldgate  Victualler and Continued in Such Service for the Space of Fifteen Months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she this Examinant has not Since lived in any other place for the space of a year as a hired Servant nor hath She been ever married nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Taken & Signed the day & Year above Written before me  Geo. C. Smith

The mark of Catherine Doe
