Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Ann Weatherhead the wife of Thos Weatherhead of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Clock maker taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County this twenty fifth day of March 1784 Who on oath Saith that about Six years & four Months ago She this Examinant (being then a Single and unmarried Woman) in Butlers Buildings in the sd Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since baptized & registred by the name of Sarah Bond & that the sd Child is likely to become Chargeable to the sd Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate And that one Samuel Bond of the Parish of St Mary White Chapel in the sd County Carman is the Father of the Said Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Ann Weatherhead
