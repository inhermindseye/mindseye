Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. in Christ Church Surry  to wit Mary Courcy aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cunningham who kept the Rising Sun in Black fryars Road in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surry for 4 years at the yearly Wages of Five pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Seven years since which time she has not lived as a Hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of October 1794 M Addington St Mathew

her Mary Courcy mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
