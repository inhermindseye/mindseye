Middx  Mary Fare aged about 22 Years Upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Smith at the Coach and Horses at Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds hath quitted said Service about five Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 27th. day of March last she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Poole (one of the Parish Nurses of the said Parish) in Clements lane in the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes which was baptized by the Name of Elizabeth and is since dead Says that one James Graham a Clogmaker did beget the said female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants body sometime in the Month of June 1764 and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said James Graham and no other person did beget the same

Sworn 15th. of April 1765 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Fare
