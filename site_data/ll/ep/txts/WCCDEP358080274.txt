Middx  Hannah Nightingale aged about 23 years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year says her father Samuel Nightingale before his Intermr. with this Examinants Mother & Whilst he was a Single man he lived as a hired Yearly servant with one Mr. Weston a Tobacoo Merchant in Coleman Street in the Parish of St. Stephen Coleman Street London for several years at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds and after quitted the said last Mentioned Service he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Rending house or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 8th- day of Augt- 1778 before us  Thos. Bishop  Jno. Barnfather

her Hannah Nightingale Mark
