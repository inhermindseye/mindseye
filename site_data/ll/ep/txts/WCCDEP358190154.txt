Middx  Catherine Latham aged about 22 Years Lodging at Mr William Payne 's in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon her voluntary Oath saith she never was Married That she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the Same when Born And that Cheney Hamilton  Book keeper to Mr. Predoe in Billiter Square did beget such Child or Children he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of October One thousand seven hundred and Sixty at the House of Mrs. Morgan in Plough Court in Fetter Lane (where the said Cheney Hamilton and this Examinant lived together as Man and Wife ) and at several times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 14th day of July 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant

Catherine Latham
