Middx.  Ann Sawyer aged abt. 58 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Stephen Sawyer who hath been dead abt. Twenty Years says her said late husband served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years to his father Edwd. Sawyer a Carpenter in Sawyers Yard in St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year says she hath not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Yr.

Sworn this 6th. of May 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Sawyer
