Middx  Lucretia Pountney upon Oath saith she was never married says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Francis Greenough a Shoemaker in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for the Space of one Year & upwds. & left her said Service in the Month of July last Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mr. Greenoughs Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that one Nathaniel Sweeting  Shoemaker did be get the sd. Child or Children or her Body the sd. Nathaniel Sweeting living a fellow Servant with this Examinant at Mr. Greenoughs aforesd. there became acquainted with her and had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Latter end of the month of April or beginning of May last and at sevd other times since at one of which times

he got her with Child of the said Bastard Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that he the said Nathaniel Sweeting & us other Person is the true & real Father thereof

Sworn Decr. 16. 1746 before  Tho Lane

Lucretia Pountney
