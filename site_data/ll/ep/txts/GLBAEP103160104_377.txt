Middlesex ss}  Sarah Davis  Single woman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one John Lashbrook in Horsely Down Lane in the Parish of Saint John Southwark in the County of Surry  Victualler & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Three years at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year nor was She ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this 7th day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Sarah Davis
