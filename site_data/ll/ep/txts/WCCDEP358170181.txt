Middx  Eliz: Morris passed from the Parish of St. Sepulchers London by Order bearing Date the 16th. Day of Novr. Just upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her sd. Husband Alexr. Morris at the Fleet  London abt. a Yr. & a half ago & that abt. Six Months ago her sd. Husband lived in & rented a House in Horshoe Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds for the Space of 7 Yrs. or thereabts. that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of One Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband is now in his Majestys Prison of the Fleet for Debt. that she hath one Child vizt. Elizabeth aged abt. 4 Months & being Poor is not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworne the 20th. of Novr. 1741 before}
