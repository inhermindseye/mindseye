Middx  Jane Perry aged about 30 Years Lodging at Mr. Longvill's near the five Bell Tavern in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon Oath saith That on or about the Ninth day of August 1760 she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of the said Mr. Longvill in Wych Street in the Parish aforesaid which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Sarah and that the same is yet living and likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to provide for it, And that James Martin a Shoemaker did beget such Child they he having Cohabited together with me as Man and wife several Years and that no other Person did beget the Same and that the said James Martin is gone away & saith she doth not know where he is to be found.

Sworn the 9th day of March 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Jane Perry
