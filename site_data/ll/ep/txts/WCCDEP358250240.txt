Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizabeth Smith who is ill Maketh Oath and saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Elizabeth Smith is aged about 82 years that she never was married that nor did any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Settlement in her own Right that she was born in Mile End New Town in the Hamlet of Mile End New Town in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex where her father Rented House for several years.

Sworn this 27th. day of May 1796 } N Bond Rupt. Clarke

B. Newman
