Middx  Joseph Woodward Aged about 17 Years upon Oath says that his Father John Woodward (who has been dead about two Years) Rented a House in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and lived in the same about Eleven Years as he has been Informed and by his said late Father & believes Says that he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant

Sworn the 27th. Day of Septr. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

Joseph Woodward
