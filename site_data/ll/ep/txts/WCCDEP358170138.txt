Anne Allinge  Wido . of Edwd Allinge upon her Oath saith that her Husband Edwd. Allinge hath been dead abt. Thirteen Years & that abt. Seventeen Fourteen Years ago he lived in and Rented a House in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Four Pounds for the Space of Eighteen Years or thereabts- that her sd. late Husband never after in his Life Time nor this Examinant since his Death & rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt- for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 25th- of of Septr. 1741 before}

The mark of Anne Allinge
