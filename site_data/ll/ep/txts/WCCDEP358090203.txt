Middx to wit Thomas King aged about Forty One years On his Oath says he Rented and lived in a House near Battle Bridge in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for Three Months or upwards at the yearly Rent of Ten guineas hath quitted the said House about One year and a half ago since since which time he hath gained no other settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 7th. Day of March 1781 before  Sampn Wright  Wm Kitchiner

Thos: King
