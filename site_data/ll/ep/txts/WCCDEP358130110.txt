Middx  John Labdon aged about Twenty eight Years upon Oath, says he served about Three years & a half of his Apprenticeship with William Madder a Taylor in the Parish of St. Pancras in the City of Exeter and was not assigned over to any other Person for the remainder of his said Apprenticeship Says he never rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the Day of 1756 before.}
