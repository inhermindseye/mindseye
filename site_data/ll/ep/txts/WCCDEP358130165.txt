Middx  Hannah Johnson aged about Twenty Years upon Oath says That She was never Marri -ed that She never was an Apprentice or a Yearly Servt. for one Year Says her Father Henry Johnson was a Foot Soldiers in Ye. First Regiment of Foot Quards, & has been Dead about Sixteen Years, Says She hath been informed & Verily belives, that her sd. Father was born in Ye. Parish of Bradford in Ye. County of Wilts, & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes, was an Apprentice , or a Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn the 15th. Day of May 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell

 Hannah Johnson
