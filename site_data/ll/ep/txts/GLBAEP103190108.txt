Middlesex ss}  Henrietta Henley the wife of William Henley maketh oath That her Said husband is now absent from her in his Majesties Service on board the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with one Mr Brown a Bricklayer in the Parish of St Mary in the City of Bath & hath not Since done any Act to gain anothr Settlement to her knowledge or belief & that She was lawfully married to her Said husband.

Sworn this. 21. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Henrietta Henley
