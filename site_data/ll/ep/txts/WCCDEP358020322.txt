St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Elizabeth Walker Aged 35 years or thereabouts Widow of Thomas Walker Maketh Oath and saith that about Seventeen years ago she was Married to her said late Husband Thomas Walker at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty that previous thereto, while he was single and unmarried not having any Child or Children he lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edward Hitchiner in the parish of Hitchin in the County of Herlford for about 3 or 4 years as he informed this Deponent and which she believes to be true; that he quitted the said Edward Hitchiner s as the and came immediately to William Hitchiner 's Esquire which she thinks was about Eighteen or Nineteen years that since their said Marriage her said Husband Rented and with her Resided in  an unfurnished Apartment in Crown Street otherwise Hogg Lane in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the said County of Middlesex  for Six Months at the yearly Wages Rent of Ten Guineas that they said left the same about Nine years ago and that since her said Husband in his life time not hath this Examinant since his Decease (by any way or means to her knowledge or belief) acquired any other legal Settlement

Sworn the 26th July 1791 before us  Wm Hitchiner R Butler

Elizabeth Walker
