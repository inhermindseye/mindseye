Middx.  Sarah Norton aged about 20 Years upon Oath says she never was Married was an Apprentice or a Yearly hired Servant for one Year Rented Ten Pounds by the Year or paid Taxes says her Father James Norton Rented a house in King Square Court Soho in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds or thereabouts lived therein about Ten Years & there dyed,

Sworn the 7th. day of Augst. 1765 . before} Paul Vaillant  Thos: Balack

S Norton
