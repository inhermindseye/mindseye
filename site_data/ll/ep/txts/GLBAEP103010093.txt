Middlesex ss.  Thomas Hawkes maketh Oath that his Father Thomas Hawkes about Thirty Five Years ago lived in and Rented a House of Eight Pounds Ten Shillings a Year or thereabouts in the Parish of Trinity in the Minories within the Liberty of his Majesty Tower of London in the said County & was Chargeable to and paid Poors Rate & Kings Tax for the same & lived there about Fifteen Years, and never did any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement in any other Parish or Place whatsoever, & that he is now living and maintained at the charge of the said Parish That he this Deponent was Born in the said Parish has never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any Parish or Place for the space of One Year together or Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or Believes to gain a Settlement in his own right, That he has a Wife named Esther to whom he was lawfully married at the Parish Church of St. Mary at Hill in the City of London on or about the Sixteenth Day of March 1786 and a Child named Thomas aged about Four years the lawful Issue by his said Wife

Sworn this 22d Day of May 1792 before  Rob Smith R Davies Thomas Hawkes
