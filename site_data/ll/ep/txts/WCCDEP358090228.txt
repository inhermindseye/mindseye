Middx  to wit Mary Pasquier Aged about Seventy four years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Francis Joseph Pasquier (who was a Frenchman & never gained a Settlement in England) at the Fleet London in the Year 1727 and by whom she had five Children who all died in their Infancy says her Husband went away from her about Twenty Two years ago into the Spannish Service and who soon afterwards died as she believes says that being a Widow & without Child or Children she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one George Wychart a Taylor in Saint Martin Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for 14 years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about 21 years ago since which time she hath gained no other settlemt either by renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a year paying Taxes or living a hired yearly servant for one year

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1781 before W Addington Wm Kitchiner

Mary Pasquier
