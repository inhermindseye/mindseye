Middlesex ss.  Sarah Burls the Widow of Wm. Burls Maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Luke Old Street in the County of Middlesex on or about the Sixth Day of February 1779 that her said late Husband about Eleven Years ago Rented a House of Sixteen Pound a Year in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and lived in the same about One year and a half and was charged to and paid Poors Rate for the same that her said late Husband did not after he quitted the said House, Rent any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or pay any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or do any act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a subsequent settlement, that her said Husband died the Thirty First Day of March last & that she has not done any Act to gain a Settlement since his Death, that she has Two Children the lawful Issue by her said late Husband namely Sarah aged about Nine Years and Mary aged about Three Years who have not done any act to gain a Settlement for themselves

Sworn this 20th Day of May 1791 before  Robt Smith Sarah Burls
