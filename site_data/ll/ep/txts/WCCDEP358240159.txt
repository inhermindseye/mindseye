Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster }  Irish Casuals St. C D  (to wit) Ann Colvell aged about 54 years upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband Charles Colvell at the parish Church of St Clement Danes about 4 years ago that her said Husband hath never obtained any settlement in the Kingdom of England neither hath She this Deponent acquired any settlement in her own Right that her said Husband Served his Apprenticeship at Kinsale in the Kingdom of Ireland and hath never obtained any subsequent in that Kingdom that her said Husband & this Deponent Reside in Clements Lane at Mr Hands in the said parish of St Clement Danes that they are now very Ill Weak and unable to support themselves without some Casual Relieve

Sworn the 8th day of September 1792 before Me } Wm. Kitchiner

Ann Colvells Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
