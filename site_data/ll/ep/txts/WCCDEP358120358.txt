Middx  Peter Field upon Oath saith he was married to his Wife Johanna Field at the Parish Church of Ludlow  in the County of Salop upwds. of 7 yrs. Since by whom he has 3 Childn. (viz) Johanna aged 4 Yrs. Sarah aged 3 Yrs. and Mary aged 8 months and that he Served his apprentiship wth. Reginald Wright a Shoemaker in Ludlow afsd. that and afterwards lived in a house of his own there and Served the Office of Constable of the sd. Parish of Ludlow since which he has not rented ten Pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor Served any Parish Office

Sworn 19th. June 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Peter Field
