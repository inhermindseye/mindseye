Middlesex ss}  Jane Sutor Maketh oath that she was heretofore hired & lived as a hired servant by & with John Dickson of the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County at Certain in Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hirg for near the Space of a year And this Depont Fathr saith that She was heretofore also hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Captn Bull in the sd Parish at Certain Wages & Continued in Such service under Such hiring for the Space of one year & half That She was never Married nor hath she done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d day of Febry 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Jane Sutor
