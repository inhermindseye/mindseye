Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Pearce aged about 67 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a house in Essex Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Twenty years at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Twenty years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of Decemr. 1796 . } J Reeves

Benjn Pearce
