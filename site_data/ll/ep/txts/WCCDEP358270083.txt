Sarah Caton & Child After Birth

Saint Clement Danes Octr. 19th. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  Woman's Settlement in St Bride Fleet Street  Child born the House  Reputed Father} to wit Sarah Caton  Singlewoman aged 27 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married That she lived as an hired hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Simmonds No. 25 Poppins Court Fleet Street in the parish of Saint Brides otherwise Bridget in the City of London for 2 years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of 6 Guineas That she quitted said Service abt. 7 Years ago and hath not obtained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information a belaf belief and this Deponent further upon her Oath Saith that on the 29th. day of July last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the Navy Coffee House in New Castle Street in the sd parish of Saint Clement Danes which Male Bastard Child is now in the Workhouse of the said parish and further Saith that One John Perry a Coach Painter who Worked for Mr. Ken Campbell a Coachmaker in Long Acre as a Journeyman, is the Father of the said Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body some time in or about the Month of October last and at several times since at One of which times he did beget the  said Child and that the said John Perry and no One else is the Real and true Father thereof,

Sworn the  19th. Octr. 1792 before Sampr Wright Mitchell

Sarah Caton

Man never taken
