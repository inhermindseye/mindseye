Westmr. to wit Margaret Eason Aged about fifty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father George Eason Rented and lived in a House in the Strand facing Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and upwards hath quitted the same upwards of Twenty Years ago and after he quitted the said House he never afterwards Rented House or paid Taxes neither hath this Examinant since her said Fathers death done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of June 1772 before} Tho. Lane

Margt. Eason
