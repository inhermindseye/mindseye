Westmr. to wit William Westbrook Aged about Twenty Two Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with his now Wife late Golossa Gough at the Parish Church of Saint Sepulchre the Tenth of February 1774 Says he was boud an Apprentice  to one William Hussey a Rule Maker in Butlers Alley Grub Street in the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate  London and Served Six Years the First Part of his said Apprenticeship and then listed for a Soldier since which time He Never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year nor ever gained any other Settlement whatever

Sworn this 10th. day of February 1774 } 

William Westbrook
