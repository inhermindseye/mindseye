Middlesex ss  Dugald Patterson maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and ten Shillings in Ben Street in the Parish of St. Margaret Westmr. in the County of Middx and was Charged to and paid Parish Taxes for the Same and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that he has not Since Rented a house of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds nor Paid any Parish Taxes nor Served any Parish offices or Paid any Kings Tax nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a wife named Sarah and two Children to wit Christian aged about nine years by his late wife  Ann deced; and John Aged about Nine Weeks by his Present wife Sarah his lawful Issue

Sworn this 31st day of March-1769 before  Chris Scott  Dd: Wilmot

The Mark of Dugald Patterson
