Middx  Frances Bagshaw aged abt. Seventy Years upon Oath says she is the Wido. of Danl. Bagshaw who dyed on or abt. the Sixth day of Novr. 1742 Says her sd. late husband Danl. Bagshaw was by Andre  dated the Twenty fifth day of March 1696 bound an Apprentice for eight Years & Served eight Years Apprentishipp with one James Brown a Taylor in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx as her sd. late husband informed her and as she verily beleives Says her sd. late husband in his life time nor she since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworn the 13th. day of July 1750 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Frances Bagshaw
