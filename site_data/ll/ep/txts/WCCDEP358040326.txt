Middx  Mary Davis upon Oath saith she never was married and that about Seventeen Years ago she was Lived a Yearly Servant for two Years and upwards with Mr Knight at the Blue Last Backfryers in the Parish of St. Ann  Blackfryers London at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds and never since rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 25th. 1748 before } Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Davis
