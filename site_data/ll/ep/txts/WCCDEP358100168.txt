Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Hitchcock Aged about Twenty eight years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says her Father Henry Hitchcock Rented & lived in a House at Rockford in the Parish of Ellingham in the County of Hants for several years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards as she believes & paid Parish Taxes due for the same and lived therein to the Time of his Death which happened about Thirteen years ago since which time this Examinant never gained any or. Settlemt. than as before mentioned

Sworn this 23d. day of January 1784  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

Eliza Hitchcock
