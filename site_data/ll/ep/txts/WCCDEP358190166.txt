Middx  Mary Lovell aged 59 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of William Lovell who has been dead about 18 Years That her said Husband was a Bricklayer and lived in and Rented a House in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about Seven Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and there dyed and that she this Examinant continued to live in the Same House for about One Year after his Death and hath since Rented Ten pounds a Year That she now Rents a House of Three pounds Nineteen shillings a Year in Three Coney Walk in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry and hath lived in the Same about Fourteen years Says she hath often paid the Land Tax which her Landlord took as part of Rent

Sworn the 12th day of Augt. 1761 Before}

Mary Lovell
