Middlesex ss}  Sarah Burton the Widow of John Burton deced Maketh oath That She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband or the Settlement of his Parents, That She this Deponent has been informed by her Parents & Which Information She believes to be true that She this Depont was born in the Parish of St Giles in the Town of Northampton in the County of Northampton and that She has not Since her Birth done any Act to Acquire of Settlement for herself Save what She might gain in Consequence of her Marriage with her Said late husband Whose Place of Settlement is unknown to her nor can this Depont. give any Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents

Sworn this day of October 1771 before}
