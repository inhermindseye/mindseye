Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Baker aged about 75 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Richard Baker her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about 32 years ago that he has been dead about Eight years that her said late Husband was born in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That he never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlemt. otherwise than by Birth

Sworn this 14th. day of May 1789 R Butler

her Ann Baker mark
