Middx  Ann Dew Aged about Seventy Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to her late Husband Samuel Dew deceased at the Fleet London upwards of forty Years ago and by whom she hath One Son living named Samuel (now provided for) Says her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Wakelin a Leather Dresser in Windmill Court in the Parish of St. Bartholomew the Less London and served his said Master under such Indenture to the time of his Master Death which was about Six Years and further says that her said Husband never was Assigned ever to any other Master nor ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes or ever Living a hired Yearly Servant for One year before Marriage neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands death

Sworn this 20th. day of August 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

her Ann Dew Mark
