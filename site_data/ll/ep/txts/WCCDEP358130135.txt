Middx  Isabella Smith aged about 22 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with Thomas Smith (who is gone from her) at the Fleet London about 5 Years ago and that She has a Child by her said Husband named Thomas aged about 2 Years & six Months Says her said Husband lived a hired Yearly Servant upwards of a Year, with one Mrs. Holmes Widow at the Black Bull Inn in Holborn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn London and had yearly Wages Meat drink & Lodging And that he hath not since he quitted his said Service Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year as She hath been informed by her said Husband & verily believes

Sworn the 10th. Day of April 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell  Theodr. Sydenham

Isebela Smith
