Middx.  Mary Hindes Aged about Seventeen Years On her Oath says She never was Married Rented House Paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year says her Father Edward Hindes Rented and lived in a House known by the Sign of the Bulls Head at Margretton in the Parish of Margretton in the County of Essex where her said Father died And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 16th Day of January last She was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Sowerbys in Butcher Row Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which is Baptized by the Name of Martha and is become Chargeable to the same She being Poor and Unable to Maintain the said Child And further says that One Luke Newby of Ingateston in the County of Essex had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of April last but the Particular Day She does not now remember and at several Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which She was so Delivered as aforesaid and that the said Luke Newby and no other Person Did beget the said and that he is the Real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry 1781 before J Girdler

her Mary Hindes mark
