Saint Clement Danes 1st. February 1786

Middlesex (to wit) Eleanor Parr singlewoman aged thirty Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married, rented House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten pounds paid Taxes for any such or ever was bound an Apprentice - That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with one Mr Cornish a Printer who lived at the seven Houses Battersea in the Parish of Battersea in the County of Surry - for upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of eight Pounds that she quitted the said Mr Cornish's Service about five Years ago, and since that time has not done any thing whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st Febry 1786 before  Jno Machin  John Croft

her Eleanor Parr Mark
