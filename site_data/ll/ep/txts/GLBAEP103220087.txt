Middlesex ss  Christian Benevis maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a Yearly hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Cain a Sugar Baker in Wood Street in the Parish of St Michael in Wood Street in the City of London & Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two Years & half & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 18. day of November 1786 before J. Hawley R Davies

The Mark of Christian Benevis
