Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Joseph Hadley aged about 44 years upon his Oath saith that about Three years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present wife at the Parish Church of High Wycombe in Bucks That he was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Bowerelier in Arundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Seven years that he Served Six years or thereabouts of his said Apprenticeship when his said Master gave up to this Deponent the Remainder of his time and that after he quitted her said Master he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Octr. 1795 } J Hind

Joseph Hadley
