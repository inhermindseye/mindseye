Middlesex ss}  Rose Young the widow of James Young deced maketh oath that about five Years ago her sd late husband rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Guineas in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & resided therein for about the Space of two years & did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this Deponent was lawfully married to her said husband & Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a settlement in the own right

Sworn this. 17. of June 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Rose Young
