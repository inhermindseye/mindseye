Middx  Mary Todd upon Oath saith she never was married and that her late father Wm Todd rented a Farm in Wyesgate in the parish of St John at Newcastle upon Tyne in the County of Northumberland of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & Upwards lived therein Several years and there dyed and than she this Examinant never rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Septr. 30th. 1754 before}

The Mark of Mary Todd
