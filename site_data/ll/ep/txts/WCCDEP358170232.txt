Middx  Mary Taylor aged abt. 25 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that she never was an Apprentice  rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year that her Father George Taylor lived in & rented a House by Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Fifty Pounds & upwards for the Space of Nine Years & then left the same & went & lived in & rented a House in Blackmoor Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Eight Pounds a Year for the Space of Seven Years or thereabts. & hath left the same abt. Five Years that since he left the same House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the

Space of One Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she is Poor & Lame & not able to maintain herself

Sworne the 24th of May 1742 before} Jas: Gronous

Mary Taylor
