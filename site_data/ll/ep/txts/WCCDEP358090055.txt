Middx to wit Henry Croasdale one of the Beadles belongg. to the Parish of Saint Clement and Constance Avern  Wife of Edward Avern  Cockcutter severally make Oath and first this Examinant Constance Avern for herself Saith that she is the Wife of Edward Avern with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London the first day of March in the year 1740 and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said Husband now Rents and lives in a House in Marquis's Court  lately Called Vinegar Yard in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields as she has been Informed and believes and is of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or upwards as she also believes and Can give no other Account than as aforesaid And this Examinant Henry Croasdales  for himself Says that he has lately made Inquiry who Rented the abovementioned House and was Informed that the abovementioned Edward Avern  did and has so done for Six Months or thereabouts and was also Informed by this Occupier the next house that the Rent of the said House is £18 a Year.

Sworn this 15th. day of July 1779 before  Abrm Baley  Sampn Wright

her Constance Avern Mark Henry Croasdell
