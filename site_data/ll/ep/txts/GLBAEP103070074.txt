Middx ss  Elizabeth Green  wife of Noah Green Maketh oath that she has been often informed By William Chamberlain (who is lately gone to Sea) us this dept. informed & believes that he duly Served his apprenticeship with one Mr. Abbott a Carpenter in Cow Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchre in the Liberty only City of London and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And that he was never a hired Servant by ye Year Nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards Nor Served any Parish offices nor gained any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief And that the sd. William Chamberlain hath a wife Named Mary

Sworn this 3d day of January 1753 before us  Richd. Ricards  Thos. Quarrill

Elizabeth Green
