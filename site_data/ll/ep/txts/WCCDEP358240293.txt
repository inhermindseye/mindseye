St Clement Danes 31st July 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St George Hanover Square  (to wit) Jane Wright aged twenty years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant under a General hiring with Mr Wilkinson in lower Mount Street  Grosvenor Square in the parish of St George Hanover Square for three two Years and three quarters or thereabouts at the Annual Wages of Five pounds that she quitted that service on or about the 20th day of August 1792 and hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 31st day of July 1793 before us} W Addington Edwd: Naish

Jane Wright 's Mark

Witness Garrett
