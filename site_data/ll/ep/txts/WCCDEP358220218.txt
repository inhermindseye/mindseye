Westmr. to wit Margt. Wright Aged about fifty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Thos. Wright who has been dead about a Year & half ago with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about Twenty five Years ago Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Portugal Street known by the Sign of the Black Dogg upwards of one Year at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Two Pounds hath quitted the same about Twenty five Years ago and after her said Husband quitted the same he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death done Any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of Janry 1771 before me}
