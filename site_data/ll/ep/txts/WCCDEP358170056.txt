Middx  The Examination of Thos. Lewis passed from the Parish of St. George Hanover Square to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx by Order bearing Date the 19th. Day of May Inst. taken the 27th. Day of May 1741 Who upon his Oath saith that abt. Twenty Six Years ago he lived in & rented a House in Witch Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds for the Space of Six Years and upwards & further saith yt. he hath not since he left the sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he intermarried with Jane his now Wife on or abt. the Day of Janry 1736 & haveing one Child (namely Mary aged abt. Ten Months & being Poor is not able to maintaine himselfe & Family

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}

The Mark of Thos. Lewis
