Middx.  Jane Brenham aged about 42 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with George Brenham her late Husband deceased in the Parish Church of Bishop Hill the Elder in the City of York about Nineteen Years since. Says her said late Husband Rented a House in Cavendish Square Street in the Parish of St. Mary le Bone in the County of Middx. & paid the Poor Rate for the same several Years And further says her said late Husband never after Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 29th. Day of March 1756 before} Barth: Hammond  Samson Webb



The Mark of Jane Brenham
