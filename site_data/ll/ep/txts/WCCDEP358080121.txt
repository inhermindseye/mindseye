Middx & Westmr}  Elizth. Faron Aged about 37 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Faron who has been Dead upwards of Twelve Months ago And by whom she hath one Child living named Charles aged about thirteen Months Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Theobalds Row in the Psh of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Barrs in the County of Middx for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of £10 and paid Parish Taxes due for the same and quitted the same upwards of 18 Yrs. ago since which time he never Rented House or Tenemt. of £10 a Year paid Taxes or orwise. gained any other Subseqt. Settlemt. neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husbands Death.

Sworn this 5th. Day of March 1777 before me.  James Paine

her Elizth. Faron Mark
