Middx  Thomas Nash maketh Oath and Saith that about Thirty Nine Years ago he was Bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to Mr. Glancey in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex  Cordwainer and served all his Apprentiship there And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge

Sworn this 21st. day of July 1746 Before Robert Dennett Richd Ricards

his Thomas Nash Mark
