Westmr.  Ann Bretton aged near 70 Yrs. upon Oath saith she is the wido. of Saml. Bretton with whom she intermarried at Mayfair Chapel the 3d. Day of August 1747 that her sd. late husband rented the Crooked Billet Alehouse next Door to Henleys Oratory near Lincolns Inn fields in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of middlesex  at the yearly Rent of £20 for servant Year & there dyed upwds. of 5 Years age & this Examinant has not since his decease rented ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish taxes or lived Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Septr. 12th. 1753 before} Geo. Errington  Philip Dyot

The Mark of Ann Bretton
