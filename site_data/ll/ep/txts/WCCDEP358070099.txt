Westmr.  Elizth Carrier Aged about fifty One Years on her Oath says she is Widow of Robt. Carrier who has been dead about Years and with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about Twenty Years ago and by whom she hath one Child named Elizabeth Aged about Nineteen Years Says said late Husband before he Intermarried with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Knowles at the ri Sun in Vere Street Clare Markett upwards of One Year an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds and after quitted the said last mentioned Service he never Rented Ten pounds a Year or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 2d. day of June 1773 before  Sampn. Wright

her Elizth. Carrier Mark
