Middlesex ss}  Thomas Johnston maketh oath That divers Years ago he was duly bound apprentice to William Webb of the Parish of Saint Warbus in the City of Westchester Screwffiler & Served the whole of his Said apprenticeship in the said Parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont. Saith that he hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And this Depont. Saith that he hath a wife named Margaret and three Children namely Catherine aged about five years Mary Magdalen aged about three years & Elizabeth aged about three weeks the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his said Wife

Sworn this 20th day of June 1771 before Bs. Camper

Thomas Johnston
