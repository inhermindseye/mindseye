Middx}  The Examination of Margt- Webb  Wido . of Jno Webb liveing in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 4th- Day of April 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. Ten Years ago her late Husband John Webb lived in & rented a House in ye. back side of St. Clement in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of Fifteen Years or thereabts- at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & further on her Oath saith that her sd. Husband in his Life Time nor hath She this Examinant since his Death ever rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor & old is not able to maintain herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}

The Mark of Margt. Webb
