Saint Clement Danes 27th. April 1786

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Susannah Selby aged about 34 Years on the behalf of Jane Peet an Insant. of the Age of 14 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she has been informed and which Information she believes to be true that the said Jane Peet was placed out Apprentice by the officers of the Parish of Saint Paul Deptford, to one Catharine Peet , the said Jane Peet s Aunt, who at the at time lived in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell , that she served about two Years of her time with her said Aunt - And this Examinant further Saith that the said Catherine Peet then placed the said Jane Peet with this Examinant to whom she agreed to pay for her Board aud it was agreed between the said Catharine Peet and this Examinant that this Examinant should procures Service for the and Jane Peet and this Examinant further Saith that She this Examinant did according procure a place for the said Jane Peet to live with one Mr. Pain a Watch Care maker in Dear Street Father Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the City of London at the Wages of one Shilling per Week besides Board - that the said Jane Peet lived with the said Mr. Pairs under such Agreement for about fifteen Weeks and gnitted said place about three Months ago - That she this Examinant received the Wages for the said Jane Peet s Service the greater part of which she has laid out for Cloaths for the said Jane Peet

This was the Girl appeard. to by Clerkenwell

Sworn this 27th. April 1786 before  James Croft  Jno. Barnfather

her Susannah Selby Mark
