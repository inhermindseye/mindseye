Westmr.  John Brunt Aged about Thirty seven Years, Upon Oath says he never was Married, nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes was an Apprentice, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, Says his Father Joshua Brunt Rented a House (known by the Name of the Wheat Sheaf ) in Arrundel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds and Upwards and after he quitted same never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes

Sworn the 8th. day of February 1768 , Before} 

John Brunt
