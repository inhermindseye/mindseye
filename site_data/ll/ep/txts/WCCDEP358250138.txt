Middlesex and Westminster }

Settlemt. Precinct of white Fryars to wit William Harrington aged about 53 years upon his Oath saith That he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Nicholas Noyes a Printer in Dogwell Court in the Precinct of White fryars in the City Garden and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Novr. 1794  N Bond E Read

William Harrington
