(After Birth) Elizth. Turner & Susanna Turner

St. Clement Danes Decr. 7th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement of the Woman St CD }  Child Born in the Workhouse }  Reputed Father (to wit) Elizth Turner aged 33 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Matthews Clements Inn for about sixteen Months at the annual Wages of six Guineas that she quitted that Service about five years ago and hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And this Examinant further saith that on or about the 14th. day of April last at the Workhouse of and belonging to the parish of St Clement Danes and situate within the same she was delivered of Female Bastard Child that married Susanna Turner wch is become chargeable to the said parish that William Tait now or late of Bucklers Butters Alley Little Moorfield Barber is the only real true father of the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he having first had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in or about the Month of April 1792 & at several times  since at some or one of which times he did beget the same thereon

Sworn the 7th day of Decr. 1793 before me } D Walker

Elizth Turner 's mark

Witness Garrett
