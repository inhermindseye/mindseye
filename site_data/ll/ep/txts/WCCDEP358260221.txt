Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westmister} to wit William Hersman aged about 27 years upon his Oath saith that about Six years ago he Intermarried with Charlotte his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Paul at Deptford by whom he hath One Child living Vizt. Ann aged about Three years That he this Deponent Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Bilby a Carpenter in King Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Octr: 1797 before us } R. Ford Rupt Clarke

Wm. Hesman

H. Day Beadle
