St. Clement Danes 5th Novr. 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields  (to wit) Ann Jones  Widow Maketh Oath and saith that she is aged about 46 years that she intermarried with her late Husband Thomas Jones on or about the 18th March 1763 at the parish Church of St Giles in the fields  Middlesex that he died about 15 years ago leaving only one Child surviving him Namely Ann aged about Eleven years who died abt. 14 years ago that since the Decease of her said Husband and Child and during her Widowhood not having any Child or Children living she this Deponent being unmarried lived as an hired yearly servant with Mrs Harris at No. 4 Martletts Court Bow Street in the parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for about Six years at the Annual Wages of six pounds that she quitted that service about Seven Months ago and hath not since been Married or obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 6th. Novr. 1792 before us } M Addington Thos. Gordon

Ann Jones
