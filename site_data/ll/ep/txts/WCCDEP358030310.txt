Westmr. 

Cathn. Leonard  Wife of Dennis Leonard (now aboard the Falmouth Man of Warr ) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Dennis Leonard in St. Michaels Church in Dublin upwds. of 7 Yrs since Says her sd. husband is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England Says she has been informed & believes she was born in the Parish of St. John in West- -chester in the County of Chester & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has two Childn. namely Jas. aged upwds. of three Years & Dennis aged abt. 8 Months.

Sworn Janry 17th. 1746 before  Tho Lane .

The Mark of Cathn Leonard .
