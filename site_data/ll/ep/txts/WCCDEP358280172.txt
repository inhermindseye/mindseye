Saint Clement Danes

Budje Town to wit Ann Beath aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1779 she intermarried with John Beath her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch who is at present in the East Indies that he Sailed from London in the Month of February 1785 since which time she has not seen her said Husband that he served his time as an Apprentice to a Mr. Howell in Surrey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Taylor that after he was out of his said Appceship he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 30th. day of June now last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Joseph Hipsley by Trade a Bookbinder who lives at Bridge North at Bombardoes Barbados is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of October last and at several times since at one of which times she did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Joseph Hipsley is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 29th. day of July 1788 before me } R Butler

Ann Beath
