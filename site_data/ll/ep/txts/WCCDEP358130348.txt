Middx.  Ruth Jones aged abt. 47 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married or Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes was an apprentice or yearly Servt. says her father John Jones Rented a house (known by the name of norths Coffee house ) in King Street near Guildhall in the Parish of St. Martin  Ironmonger Lane in the City of London of the yearly Rent of £50 & upwards for upwards of twenty years & there dyed

Sworn the 30th of May 1757 before me} Theodr. Sydenham

Ruth Jones
