Middx  Frances Gotobed upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she is the Daughter of Henry Gotobed  by Mary his Wife both deceased that her sd. late father rented a house in St Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx & lived therein a Considered time and paid Scot & Lot for the same & afterwds. rented and lived in a house in Peter Street in the Said parish & after he quitted the last mentioned house never rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parish Taxes and that she this Examinant never was an apprentice or yearly Servant for one year nor ever Rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parish Taxes

Sworn 12th Novr 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Francis Gotobed
