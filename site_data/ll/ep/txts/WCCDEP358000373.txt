Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Benjn. Newman One of the Beadles  the Parish of St Clement Danes on behalf of Eliz Harper who lays ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which information believes to be true that the said Elizth. Harper belongs to the Parish of Allhallows the Less the City of London by her Husband Daniel Harp living for upwards of One year as a hired year Servant with One Mr. Tash of Suffolk ha in the said Parish of Allhallows the Less th after he quitted his said Service he never any other Act to gain any other Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of Octr. 1786 D Walker Rups; Clarke

Benj: Newman
