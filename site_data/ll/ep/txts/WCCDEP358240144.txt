St. Clement Danes August 29th. 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Ann Parler aged 35 Years and upwards Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Thompson Porter  New Inn at the Lodge of the said Inn for one Year and Eleven Months and upwards at the Annual Wages of Seven Guineas that she quitted that Service on or about the 4th day of November last and hath not since been in service Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the 29th Augst 1792 before Me } R Butler

Ann Parler
