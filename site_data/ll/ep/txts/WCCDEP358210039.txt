Middx & Westmr.}  Amy Carter aged about 24 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she was born at Wimbourn in Dorsetshire where her father James Carter did Rent and live in a house in which he carried on the business of a Distiller says she never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr, and County of Middx where she now lodges, she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born says one Henry Errenshaw late Clerk to Sir Wm: Baker one of the Aldermen of London, had carnal knowledge of her body in the beginning of October last and at several other time since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant, and that the said Henry Errenshaw and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 9th. day of April 1766 , Before} Paul Vaillant

Amy Carter
