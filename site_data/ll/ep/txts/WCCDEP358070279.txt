Westmr. ss  Elizabeth Kendall Aged upwards of 60 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Robinson a Quilter who lodged with on Mr. Smith a Builder in Durham Yard in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for fifteen Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Twenty Years ago since which time she never Rented House paid Taxes of otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlmt.

Sworn this 6th. day of July 1775 before R. Butler Sampn. Wright

The Mark of Elizabeth Kendall Witness James Viger
