Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Ann Gilson aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cort in Lincolns Inn Field in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas hath quitted said Service about 14 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Janry. 1788 D Walker Rapt: Clarke

Ann Gillson
