Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Margarett Mc. Guire aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that about 14 years ago she Intermarried with Michael Mc. Guire (who is gone to sea ) in Dublin according to the Giles and Ceremonies of the Church of Rome by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Peter aged upwards of Two years born at the house of a Mr. Gibson in white hart yard in the Parish of saint Mary  Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn this 21st. day of april 1790 N Conant J Barnfather

her Margt. Mc.Guire Mark
