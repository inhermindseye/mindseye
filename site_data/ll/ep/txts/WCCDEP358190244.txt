Middx  Margt. Hill Aged abt. Thirty Years Upon Oath Says She Intermarried with Saml. Hill on the Eighteenth day of Augt. 1760 in the Parish Church of St. Ann in in the Liberty of Westmr. Says her sd. Husband  Saml. Hill Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Wm. Worsley  Perukemaker in King Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. aforesd. as She hath been informed & verily beleives Says her sd. Husband hath Absconded from her that he never Rented Ten pounds a Year pd. the Land Tax or Poors Rate that She hath one Child by him named Mary aged abt. two Months

Sworn the 22th. May 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Margaret Hill
