Westmr.  Elizth. Mose aged about 31 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bloomer Ireland a Taylor in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year & Upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, Quitted said Service about Eight Years ago, and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 7th. Novr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant
