St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Eleanor Anthony Aged 50 Years or thereabouts On Oath saith that she Intermarried with her late Husband Wm. Anthony at the parish Church of St.. James Westminster about 1766, that since then her said Husband Rented & with her Resided in a House Numbered 4 in Star Court Butcher Row in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said Liberty County and Liberty for about Six Months at the Annual Rent of £16 that they quitted the said Hous House about 11 or 12 Years ago that after her said Husband in his Life had did not do, neither hath she this Examinant since his Decease (which happened about the year 1788) done any Act or thing whereby they or either of them might acquire any Subsequent legal settlement

Sworn the 15th. April before Me R Butler

Eleanor Anthony 's Mark
