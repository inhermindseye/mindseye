Middx.  John Baker a Soldier in the 3d. Regiment of foot Guards upon Oath says he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Arthur Herretta Nailer at Blockmoor Green in the Parish of Kings Swinford in the County of Stafford and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year says he intermarried with Alice his Wife at the Fleet London on the 7th. of Febry abt. 9 Years ago by whom he one Child Joseph aged abt. Eight Years

Sworn this 6th. of May 1758 . before} Sam Pechely

The Mark of John Baker
