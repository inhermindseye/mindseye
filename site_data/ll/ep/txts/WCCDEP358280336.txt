St. Clement Danes Septr. 6th: 1791

Middx and Westmr. to wit  Lucy Slaughter aged 24 or thereabouts Upon her Voluntary Oath Saith That she never was Married That she has lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Poole of Thanet Place for upwards of Two Years last past And Further Saith That She is pregnant with a Child or Children, That is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes when born, And Further Saith That James Poole (the Son of the said M Poole) now Clerk to Mr Lowten in the Temple is the Father of the said Child He having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Months of January or February last past and at several Times since, at one of which Times He did beget the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that he and no one else is the Real and true Father thereof

Sworn before me this 6t. day of Septr. 1791 .} R Butler

Lucy Slaughter
