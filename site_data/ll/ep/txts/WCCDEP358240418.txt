St Clement Danes 7th June 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement in St Clemt Danes }  Six Children vizt. Joseph aged 15 Yrs settled in Walsall Stafford shire by Appceship  Harriet aged abt 10 or 11 years Thos abt 9 or 10 Wm. six years Edwd five Amey 4  (to wit) Elizabeth Myatt  Widow aged 39 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she intermarried with William Myatt at the Parish Church of St John the Evangelist in the Liberty of Westminster on or about the 10th day of December 1777 (by Bann's) that subsequent there to her said Husband Rented and with her this Examinant resided in a House in a Place called Golden's Buildings in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Country of Middlesex for three or four years at the least, at the annual Rent of £12 that he quitted the same in or about in or about the Month of January 1787 and died on or about the 27th day of January 1791 not having acquired any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief, that she hath five six Children by her said Husband born in Wedlock namely Joseph aged fifteen years or thereabouts who is bound an Apprentice to Joseph Birch of the Parish of Walsall in the Country of Stafford and hath served about two or three years of his Apprenticeship with his said Master in the Parish of Walsall aforesaid, is thereby thereby become legally settled there in his own right Harriet aged about ten or eleven years, Thomas aged about Nine or Ten years, William aged six years and upwards Edward aged about five years-and Amey aged about four years-& the five last named Children are living with her Examinant. And lastly this Deponent saith that she hath not been Married since the Death of the said William Myatt nor done any

Act Matter or thing since that time whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own Right

Sworn the 7th day of June 1794 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizth. Myatt 's Mark

Witness a B Newman

This Woman Family live at a Village called the Windmil l in the Parish of near a Walsall Staffordshire

St. John's Register was searched for the Marriage-wch was found to be a stated, Mr Myatt's Maiden Name was Elizth Jones
