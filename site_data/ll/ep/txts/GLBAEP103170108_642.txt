Middlesex ss  Ann Hughes the widow of Hugh Hughes deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband rented a house known by the Sign of the Angel & Sugar Leave in the Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at the yearly rent of Twenty eight pounds & resided thereon for the Space of Eleven Months & also was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her said husband did not afterwards rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain an Settlemt nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 27 day of November 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

Ann Hughes
