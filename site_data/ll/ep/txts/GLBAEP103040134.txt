Middlesex ss.  Thomas Gibson maketh Oath that one Ann Reader of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman by her examination taken in Writing & on Oath the Fifteenth Day of June instant declared that about Three Years and a half ago she was hired as a Servant by the year to Mr. Carr of Nr 18 Argyle Street in the Parish of St. James in the City & Liberty of Westminster Gent at the yearly Wages of Nine Guineas and continued in his Service under such hiring for the space of One Year & a half And that about Two Months after she quitted his Service she was delivered of a Female Child at the Westminster lying in Hospital  which has been Baptized by the Name of Matilda ; that the said Child was born on the Twenty first Day of Septr. One Thousand Seven Hundred & Ninety Six as he has since been informed by the said Ann Reader , that the said Ann Reader has deserted her said Child, and that the said Child is become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 25 Day of June 1798 before R Davies Dan Williams

Thomas Gibson

Adam Wallace was sent by an Order of Removal from Wapping the 22d. June 1798
