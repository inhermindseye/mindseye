Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Joggen the wife of Daniel Joggen maketh oath That She well knows a Child named Eleanor Joggen aged about Eight Months now in the Workhouse of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County The which Child was born of the Body of Martha Carnmak in the house of John Elliott in Great Buckle Street in the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the Sd County & is illegitimate & a Bastard as She this Deponent has been Credibly informed & verily believes

Sworn this 3d day of June 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey  Wm. Blackmore

The Mark of Eleanor Joggen
