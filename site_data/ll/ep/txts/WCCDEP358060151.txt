Middx  Augt. 14th. 1751

Ann Row aged abt. 13 yrs. upon Oath saith she was born with in 4 Doors of the Black Bull Alehouse in Green Dragon Stable Yard in the parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middx & never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the Day & year abovementioned before  Thos Lane .

The mark of Ann Row
