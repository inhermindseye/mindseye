Middx  Mary Shallcross wife of Wm. Shallcross a Lunatic Upon Oath Says She Intermarried with the said Wm. Shallcross at May Fair Chapell upwards of Eleven Years Since Says her said Husband  Rented a House in the Old Bailey in the Parish of St Sepulchers London of the Yearly Ground Rent of Five Pounds lived therein Upwards of four Years and was Rated and Paid the Kings Tax quitted said House about Five Months and hath not Since rented House or Paid Taxes Says She hath three Children Elizth Aged upwards of 10 Years Wm Upwards of Nine Years and Mary Upwards of two Years

Sworn the 24 Day of March 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

Mary Shallcross
