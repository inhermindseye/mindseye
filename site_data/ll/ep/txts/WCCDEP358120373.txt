Middx  Ann Fox aged upwards of 22 Years upon Oath saith she was married to Thomas Fox at Kings land Chapel in Middx upwards of Six years ago by whom she has one Child Bridget aged upwards of Five years and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. husband never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. nor ever Rented ten pounds by the year nor paid Parish Taxes and that her sd husbands father Henry Fox lived a yearly Servt. with-Fleet Esqr. in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for sevl. years whilst a Singleman & never after he quitted Mr Fleets Service (which is upwds. of 27 Yrs. ago) rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn June 27th 1755 before} Tho Lane .

Ann Fox
