Westmr.  Edwd. French upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Eleanr. French upwds. of 11 Yrs. ago in Dublin & has by her one Child Margt . aged near 7 Yrs Says he was bound an Apprentice for 4 Years to Thomas Kirwan a Grocer and Served his said Apprentiship with the said Thomas Kirwan in Midele Street in the Parish of St. Nicholas in Gallway in Ireland and that he has not since gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn May 14th. 1751 before  Tho Lane Edwd. French Junr
