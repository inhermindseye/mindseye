Middx  Martha Bossom upon Oath Says She is the Wife of Robert Bossom by whom She hath four Children (to wit) Mary Aged abt. Nine Years Jno . abt. Six years Wm . abt. four Years Elizth . abt. two years Says She has been informed & verily beleives her Husband, never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. Says he never rented ten pounds a yeare or pd. Parish Taxes Says her sd. Husband father Robt. Bossom lived in a house of his own, of the yearly Value of twelve pounds near the High bridge in the parish of St. Peter in the Bailey in the County of Oxford paid Scott & Lott Served all Parochiall Offices and there dyed

Sworn the 9th. day of Augt. 1759 before} Fran: Bedwell  Theodr. Sydenham

Martha Bossom
