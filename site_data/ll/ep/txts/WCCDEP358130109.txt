Middx  Mary Pearce Wife of Joseph Pearce Aged about 49 Years upon Oath says her said Husband served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with; his Father in the Parish of Osesstry in Shropshire Says her said Husband never Rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 23d. Day of Janry. 1756 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Marry Pearce
