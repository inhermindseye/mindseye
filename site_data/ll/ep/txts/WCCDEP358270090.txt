1st Examination Desertion

St. Clement January 3d. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Child  Settlemt  Desertion (to wit) Sarah Glover Wife of John Glover who hath deserted her Upon Oath saith that she never was Married to her present Husband John Glover at the Parish Church of St. Mary le Strand  in or about the Month of October last that She hath one Child by her said Husband now living born in lawful Wedlock called Martha but never Christened aged 5 Weeks or thereabouts that She hath been informed & which she  verily believes to be true that her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Scott Taylor in Arundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and  served the best part of his Apprenticeship out  and was never after assigned but ran away without his Master's cornont and hath never since rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or done any other Act matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief-And this Examinant further saith that her said Husband deserted  her on Saturday last and left her destitute with or

any allowance is provision for herself & Child & that She is unable to provide for herself and Child without assistance and into whereby they are become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn the 3d day of January 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

her Sarah Glover mark

Witness Richd Dobbins

Man never taken
