Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Marney aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that about Seven Years ago she Intermarried with William Marney her present husband (who is now at Sea) in Ireland according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Catholic Religion That her said Husband and this Examinant are both Irish and never gained any Settlement in England that she hath Two Children living by her said Husband Vizt. Mary born at the house of a Mr. Croneil in Bainbridge Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex on or about the 25th. day of December 1792 and William born at the house of a Mr. Cranfield in Change Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said county of Middlesex on or about the Thirteenth day of October 1794

Sworn this 9th. day of Decr. 1795  M Addington J Hind

her Mary Marney Mark
