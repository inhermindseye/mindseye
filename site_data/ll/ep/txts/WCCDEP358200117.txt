Middx  Valentine Rumley on Behalf of Elizth. Rumley A Lunitick Aged about Fifty three Years Upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Valintine Rumley Gone from her Says her said Husband whilst a Singleman and without Child or Children Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Counceller White in Tooks Court Chancery Lane in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx upwards of four Years but what Wages he had this Examinant Cannot tell hath quitted said Service about Twenty Years and hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes as this Examinant hath been Informed and which Information he Verily Believes to be true

Sworn the 25th of June 1764 Before Thos. Kynaston

Valentine Rumley

Upon Mr Stiles C W Complt J. W
