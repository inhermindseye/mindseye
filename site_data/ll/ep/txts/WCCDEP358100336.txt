Middlesex(ss)  Swears to St. C. D . A  Elizabeth Middleton Aged Fifty two Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith she never was Married rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Thomas Allin  Tobacconist in the Strand for Upwards of One whole Year at the Yearly wages of Five Pounds Hath Quitted her said Service about Eleven or Twelve Years ago, Since which time she never rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year lived as a Hired yearly Servant or done any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement Elsewhere

Sworn this 13th. day of January 1784  Thos: Gillbert

Elizbeth Middleton
