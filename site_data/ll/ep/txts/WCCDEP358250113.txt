Saint Clement Danes Feby. 7th. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Dunstan West London (to wit) Mary Talbot aged 48 Years or thereabouts Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she now was married that she rented Apartments and resided in a House No. 10 Johnsons Court  Fleet Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes Dunstan in the West in the City of London at the Weekly Rent of 8 Shillings for upwards of Eleven Months that she quitted the said House about the Year 1783 and that she has done no Act matter or Thing whereby to gain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this deponents knowledge Information or belief

Sworn the 7th day of February 1794 } N Bond Edwd: Naish

Mary Talbot

Jas Talboys
