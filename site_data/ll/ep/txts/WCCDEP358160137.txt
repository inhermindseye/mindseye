Middx & Westmr.} The Exaicon of Michll. Garnon Aged upwds. of Sixty Years lodging in Drum Alley in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & Liberty of Westmr . taken the Twenty Eighth day of May 1740 Who upon his Oath Saith that he lived in & rented a House of Eleven Pounds a Yeare in Kings Head Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. County & Liberty for the Space of four Years & hath left the sd. House upwds. of Six Years And further Saith that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Yeare pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Yeare or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife and being Old & Poor is not able to maintaine him- -Selfe

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before}

Michaell Gernon
