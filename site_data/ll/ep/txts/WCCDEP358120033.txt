Middx  Ann Page aged about 34 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Geo Page who has been dead upwards of four Years and that she has not intermarried with any other person since his Decease And this Examinant further voluntarily maketh Oath that she is pregnt with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx (where she now lodges) she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that Joseph Rivers a News Carrier did beget the said bastard Child or Children or her body the said Joseph Rivers having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant in April last at his house in Windmill Court near Smithfield London (where this Examint. lived with him as a Servt) and at several other times Since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said Joseph Rivers & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Decr. 28th 1753 ; before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Paige
