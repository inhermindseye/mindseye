Middlesex ss  Sarah Barber  Single woman Maketh Oath that divers years ago she was hired; and lived as a hired servant by the year at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds and ten shillings with one James Giddes in the Parish of East Greenwich in the County of Kent  Victualler and Continued there in such service for the Space of Fifteen Months and by Means thereof as she is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont Saith that she has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement subsequent to her servitude as above

Sworn this day of February 1768 before  Chris: Scott Rt Darling

Sarah Barber Her Mark
