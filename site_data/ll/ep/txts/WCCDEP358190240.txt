Middx  Sarah Shaw aged about 22 Years removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married bound Apprentice or lived a hired yearly Servant for One year Says her Father  Thomas Shaw (who has been Dead about Twelve years) lived in & rented a House of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds and upwards in Peter Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for several years & Quitted the same about 16 years ago

Sworn the 27th. day of April 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Shaw
