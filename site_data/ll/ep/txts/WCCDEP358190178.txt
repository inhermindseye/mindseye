Middx  Elizth. Kirk the Mother is dead  Elizabeth Kirk aged about 36 Years upon Oath saith that about Nine Years since she intermarried with one Giles Sturgis at the Fleet London (who is now Dead ) by whom she hath One Child named Elizabeth aged about Eight Years who was Born in the House of Mr. Geo Savage next the Vine Tavern in Holborn in the Parish of St. George  Bloomsbury in the County of Middx says at the time of her said Marriage the said Giles Sturgis had a Wife living and that she then was the Widow of Stephen Kirk deceased .

Sworn the 10th. day of Septemr. 1761 Before}
