Middlesex ss}  Thomas Bowyer Maketh oath That divers Years ago he was duly bound apprentice to David Ward Hall of the Parish of St. Dunstan in the East in the City of London Waterman and Lighterman and served the whole of his Said Apprenticeship in the Said Parish and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that he hath not since rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor served any Parish offices nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a settlement since his said Apprenticeship And that he hath a wife named Sarah & two Children to wit Thomas Aged about five Years & William Aged about two Years the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his Said Wife

Sworn this 18th day of January 1771 before  Chris. Scott R Pell

Thomas Bowyer
