Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr} to wit John Conaway aged 15 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that on the 30th. day of August 1784 he was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwn and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes to one Morris Jones  of the parish of Barking in the County of Essex Fisherman and served his said Master under such Indenture till within this Eight Months that since he left his said Master he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 17th day of March 1786  Gs Bradshaw  James Croft

his John Conaway Mark
