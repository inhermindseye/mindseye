Middx  Frances Macclesfield Aged about Thirty Six Years Upon Oath Says She was never Married that She Served Five Years apprentishipp with Mrs. Nanfann a Mantua maker in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and Says She never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a hired yearly Servant.

Sworn the 4th. Day of Decr. 1755 . before} Fran: Bedwell  Macline

Frances Macclesfeild
