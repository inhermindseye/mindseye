Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminter}  Vide or side  John Myers aged about 74 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Sarah his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of 27 years ago That he hath no Child or Children living That his said Wife departed this Life about 20 years ago That before his Intermarriage he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Dodd a Dry Salter and Oilman on Dowgate Hill in the Parish of Saint Anthonys outgarly called Saint Antholins in the City of London for upwards of Six years at the yearly Wages of Ten Pounds hath quitted said Service upwards of 30 years ago since which time he has done no Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 24th day of May 1785  Sampn Wright  Addington

Jno Myers
