Middlesex ss}  Hannah Carr the widow of Michael Carr deced maketh oath that She has been informed which She believes to be true from her Said late husbands information that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship (Save about the Space of a year) with Mr Thomas Sausum in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County When his Indres were by Consent of himself & Master given up for the Purpose of being Cancelled & he was discharged from his Apprenticeship That he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor has She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for her Self

R Davies See this Exam. in Pa 10g}
