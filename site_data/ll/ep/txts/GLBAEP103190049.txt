Middlesex ss}  Mary Brown the wife of Henry Brown  Mariner maketh oath That her Said husband who is now Absent from her on board his Majestiesship Thornton has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Alexander Gill in the Town of Newbury in the County of Berks  Glover & Breeches Maker & to her knowledge or belief her Said husband has not Since done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere That she was lawfully married to her said husband by whom She hath a Child & lawfull Issue now living named  Martha  aged about Five Years & three Months

Sworn this 4 day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Brown
