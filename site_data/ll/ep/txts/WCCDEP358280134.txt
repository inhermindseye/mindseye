Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Dorothy Arthur aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that about 16 years ago she Intermarried with Richd. Arthur her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Olaves Southwark that this Ext. has not seen her said Husband for these 13 years last past that she heard about 10 years ago that he died abroad that this Ext. rents and lives in a House in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and has done for 40 days at the yearly Rent of £40 And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that she has 2 illegitimate Children vizt. Elizth. aged about 8 years Barn in Clements Lane in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and John aged about 4 years born in Clare Street in the said Parish That John Morgan an Earthenwareman in Clare Market in the father of the said 2 Children he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body for 10 years last past and begotten the same thereon

Sworn this 15th. day of Novr. 1787 } Wm Kitchiner

Dorothy Arthur

Arthur Child

Man Taken and Security Bond given

He or the Woman have the Children

He owes the Parish for the Bond. 1:3:6.
