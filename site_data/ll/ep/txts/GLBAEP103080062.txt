Middx ss.} The free and Voluntary Examination of Isabel Morgan of the Parish of Saint Botolph witht. Aldgate in the said County singlewoman taken on oath before Me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx residing in or Near the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate this twenty third day of December 1760 .

This Examinant on her Oath saith that sometime in the Month of April last one Thomas Jones of Coopers Court in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate  Custom house officer did Prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lie with her at the house of the said Thomas Jones in Coopers Court aforesaid in the said Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate And the said Thomas Jones had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body several times whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she now goeth with & is Pregnant And this Examinant saith that the said Child or Children of which she now goeth with and is Pregnant as aforesd is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate she being Poor and Not able to Provide for the same And that the said Thomas Jones is the true and only father of the said Child or Children so likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesaid And lastly saith that this examination is voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whatsoever.

Sworn this Twenty third day of December 1760 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Isabel Morgan
