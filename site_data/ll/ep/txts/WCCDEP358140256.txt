Middx.  George Beel aged abt. 55 years upon Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his wife at the fleet London abt. Twenty Years ago says he served seven Years Apprenticeship to Mr Wm. Heath a Butcher in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. and never did any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. June 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

George Beell
