Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  to wit Mary Best aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant or ever bound an Apprentice that her Father Wm. Jas. Best has Rented and lived in a house in Church Street Saint Giles's in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1790 }
