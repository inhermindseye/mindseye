Middlesex to wit Rose Taylor aged about fifty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Charles Taylor with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn  London about Seventeen Years ago and who has been dead upwards of Ten Years Says that beeing a Widow and without Child or Children she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hussey an Apothecary the Corner of King Street St. James's in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Services upwards of Six Years ago since which time she never lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any or Settlement.

Sworn this 5th. day of Augt. 1784 before  James Croft  James Paine

Rose her Mark Taylor
