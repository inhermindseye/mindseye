Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Louisa Rogers an Infant aged about Twelve years Maketh Oath and Saith That he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Louisa Rogers was born at Yeovil in the Parish of Yeovil in the County of Somerset and that the said Louisa Rogers hath been till within these Seven Months last past been maintained at the Expence of the said Parish of Yeovil

Sworn this 27th. day of Septr. 1797 before us R  R Clarke

Jas Talboys
