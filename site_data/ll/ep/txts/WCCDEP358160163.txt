Middx & Westmr.} The Exaicon of Sarah Padmore  Singlewoman , Aged abt. Twenty Years liveing as a yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Carey in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and Liberty of Westmr . taken the 12th. day of Augt. 1740 who upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that she was never marryed and that she is pregnant with Child or Children wch when born will be a Bastard or Bastards and likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish this Exaiant being poor & not able to maintain the same when Born And further upon her Oath Saith that one Saml. Prosser  Apprentice to one Mr Kenderick in Exeter Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body she this Exaiant at such time liveing a Servt . with the sd. Mr. Kenderick and he the sd. Saml. prosser liveing in the same House there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body Sevll. times at one of wch times he got her with the Child or Children with wch she is now Pregnant and that he and no other person is the true & reall father thereof And this Exaiant further Saith that she lived a yearly. hired Servt. with the sd. Mr. Kenderick in Exeter Court for the Space of One year & upwards at the yearly Wages of four Pounds and hath been come away upwds. of Six Months

Sworn the day & year above named before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Sarah Padmore
