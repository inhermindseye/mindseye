Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Alice Storey of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Twenty fifth day of May one thousand seven hundred & Seventy Six.

Who Saith That about Ten years ago She this Examinant was lawfully married to one Thomas Storey a Gunsmith who about Three years & half ago left this Examinant & went to reside at Kingston in Jamaica That She this Examinant hath never Seen him nor hath he had any Access to her Person or had any Carnal knowledge of her Body Since he left her as aforesaid She this Examinant having ever since till within a few weeks past resided at her Fathers house in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the Said County Nor doth this Examinant know or believe nor hath She ever been informed Whether the Said Thomas Storey hath ever been in any Part of the Kingdoms of Great Britain or Ireland Since his Departure from this Examinant as aforesaid Nor doth She now know nor hath She been informed Whether he is now dead or living That the last time She this Examinant heard from him was by a Letter received from him and dated at Kingston in Jamaica aforesaid in the month of April one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy four That about Eleven months ago and not more one James Fleming a Butcher who then lodged in the house of this Examints Father Adam Beale in Little Grays Inn Lane Holborn in the Sd County Where this Examinant also then lived did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him the said James Fleming to lie with her & to have Carnal knowledge of her Body Whereby She Conceived with Child of a Male Bastard Child of Which She was delivered in the Workhouse of the Said Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate on the twelfth of April last past The which child hath been Since Baptised & registered by the name of William Storey & is become Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph She this Examinant being Poor & not able to Provide for the Same That the said James Fleming is the true & only Father of the Said Child that this Examination is Voluntary & that she hath not been Compelled to be examined by any Person or persons Whomsoever.

Sworn this. 25. of May 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Alice Storey
