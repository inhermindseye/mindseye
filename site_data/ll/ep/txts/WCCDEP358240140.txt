St Clement Danes August 21st 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Edmund the King London  (to wit) John Thorowgood aged 59 Years & upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife  Johanna at the parish Church of St. Mary Aldermanbury in the City of London about 29 30 Years ago that since his said Marriage he Rented & with his said Wife Resided in an Apartment at the House of Mr. Bland in Clement Lane three Fox Court in St Clements Lane in the parish of St Edmund the King in the City of London for about three Months Six Months & upwards at the Annual Rent of £5. & 5s that when he had been there about three Months sometime he hired another Room in the same power and Rented and Resided therein for three Months & upwards at the Yearly Rent of £5 that he quitted that Lodging there Rooms about Eight Years ago And he hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year paid any parish Taxes served any parochial Office & otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement that he hath two Children by his said Wife now living the Youngest of which is 23 Years of Age and both provide for themselves

Sworn the 22d. August 1792 before Us } Sampt Wright  Gd: Mitchell

John Thorowgood
