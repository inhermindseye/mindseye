Westmr.  Margt. White Aged about 36 Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Richd. White gone from her with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Hampsted upwards of one Year & half and Says that before her sd Intermarriage with her said Husband she had four Children by him all now living Namely Richard Aged about 14 Years Charlott Aged about 10 Elizth . Aged about Nine & Priscilla Aged about 4 Years all born in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster all born before this Examinant was Married to the Richd. White Says that her said Husband Rented & lived in a House in the Parish of St Mary Islington Known by the Sign of the upwards of half a Year at the Yearly Rent of forty Pounds & quitted the same about a year ago when he deserted her & knows not now where her said husband lives or where he can be met with

Sworn this 7th. Day of June 1771 before}
