Middx  William Vise aged Sixty five Years upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Eggerton, in the Parish of Bassage in the County of Stafford & had a Cert from the Parish of Bassage to the Parish of St. Martin in the Town of Brimingham that he never rented Ten Pounds a Year lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife and that he hath Committed an Act of Vagrancy within the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx

Sworne 11th. Octr. 1742 before}

The Mark of Wm. Vise
