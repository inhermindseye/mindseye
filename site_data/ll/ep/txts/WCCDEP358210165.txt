Westmr.  Stacey Hodges aged about 38 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Johnson in Arrundal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds; quitted said Service about twenty Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 11th. of June 1767 , before}
