Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Taylor aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired year Servant with Mr. Phillips in Holywell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months a the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one William Raper a Labouring Man who did work with Mrs. Clement at Isleworth is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Raper is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Octr. 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Ann Taylor Mark

Witness B Newman

Man never taken

Child born in the house 15th. Decr. 1797 named Wm. Taylor

Died in the House the 2d. Day of April 1798
