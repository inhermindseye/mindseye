Middx  Sarah Adams aged about 20 Years upon Oath says that Joseph Adams her late Father deceased was born in the Parish of Aldenham in the County of Hertford and lived a hired yearly Servant there upwards of a year and never afterwards lived a hired yearly Servant elsewhere or Rented a House or paid Parish Taxes as She has been informed By him & believes Says She never lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 15th. Day of May 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Sarah Adams
