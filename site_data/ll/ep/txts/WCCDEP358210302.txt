Westmr.  Mary Williams Aged about Twenty eight Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Williams with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Lavingham in the County of Suffolk , says her said Husband has been dead about Eleven Months, and that she has one Child by her said Husband now with her (to wit) William aged about Four Years; Says her said Husband never Rented House, paid Taxes. was an Apprentice or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year, Says her said Husbands Father Rented a House in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Nine Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty eight Pounds hath quitted said House about Eighteen Years and hath not since Rented House, or paid Taxes as this Examinant hath been informed and which Information she Verily believes to be true

Sworn the 22d. day of July 1768 , before} Thos. Kynaston

Mary Williams
