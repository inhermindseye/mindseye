Middlesex ss}  Ann Mountford the widow of George Mountford deced maketh oath that her said late husband lately rented a house of Sixteen Pounds a year in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & resided thereon for about the Space of three Quarters a year That her said husband did not afterwards Rent a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Depont since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath two children namely George aged Seven years and Elizabeth aged four years the lawful issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 17. day of July 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Ann Mountford
