Middx to wit Sarah Nicholls aged about 41 Years on her Oath says she is the wife of Thos. Nichols gone from her with whom she Inter- -married at Spittle Fields Church about 21 Years ago & by whom she hath 3 Children all of whom are now provided for say's her said Husband Rented & lived in a House Known by the Sign of the Angel in Goswell Street in the Parish of St. Luke Old Street in the County of Middx for Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of £18. and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about 14 Years ago and after quitting the same he never gained anyother Subsequent Settlement to her Knowledge or Belief

Sworn this 8th. day of July 1782 before  Sampn Wright  Thos: Gilbert

her Sarah Nicholls Mark
