Middx  Mary Mackhan on the Behalfe of Elizabeth Lattimore aged about Thirteen Years upon her Oath saith that John Lattimore & Alice Lattimore the Father & Mother of the sd. Eliz: Lattimore are both Absconded from her & where they now are knows not Says the sd. John Lattimore lived in & rented a House in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex for two Years or thereabouts of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards Says after he left the said house he lived in & Rented a house in Baldwins Gardens in the sd. Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn for one Year or thereabouts of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds as this Examinant hath been Informed & beleives Says the sd. John Lattimore hath not since he left the sd. house in Baldwins Gardens Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne April 17th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Mackhan
