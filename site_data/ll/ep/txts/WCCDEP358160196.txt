Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Thompson Smith Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 25th. Day of Novr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never Marryed & that abt. 12 years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Gee at the Rose Alehouse in St. Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of one Year & Six Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & hath been come away from the same about 12 Years & further saith that she hath not since that time rented any House pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & is at this Time Sick & Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year abovemenconed before}
