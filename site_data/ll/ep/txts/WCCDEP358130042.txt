Middx  Sarah Breach Aged upwards of Forty two years upon Oath says that She is the widow of John Breach who dyed about four years since, That her said late Husband Rented a House at the yearly Rent of £14. in little Drury Lane in the Parah of St. Mary Le Strand in the County of Middlesex and lived in the same upwards of two years And that She has not since his Death Rented Ten pounds by the year, paid Parish Taxes, nor lived a yearly Servant

Sworn the 27th. day of Augst. 1755 . before } Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Sarah Breach
