St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Robt. Jackson Aged Nineteen years & upwards Upon his Oath saith that he never was Married that he lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Swyffin at the Talbot as Spotted Dog Tottenham Court Road in the parish of St. Pancras in the said County and Liberty abt. 13 Months at the Yearly Wages Rent of £5 that he quitted that Service abt. thirteen Months ago  and since then hath not done any Act or thing wtsoever whereby to his knowledge or belief he might acquired any other legal Settlement and that he is Ill Poor & unable to support himself

Sworn the 22nd. June 1791 . before us D Walker R Butler

his Robert Jackson Mark
