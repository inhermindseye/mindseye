Middlesex  The Examination of Ann Place  Singlewoman Lodging in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the Sixteenth day of September one thousand Seven hundred and Forty . Who on her Oath Saith That about five years Since She lived a yearly hired Servant with Wollaston in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County aforesaid Esqr for the space of one year and a half at the yearly wages of Five pounds, that since she hath left the said Mr. Wollaston's service she hath not lived as a hired Servant for the space of one year with any other person, or rented any house or Tenement of the yearly Value of Ten pounds or otherwise gained a Settlement in Anyother place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworn the day and year Above mentioned before}
