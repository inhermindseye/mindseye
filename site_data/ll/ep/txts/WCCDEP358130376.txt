Middx.  Barry Onail aged abt. 50 yrs. upon Oath says that whilst he was a single Man he lived a hired yearly Servant upwds. of one year with Mr. Timberlick a farmer at Castle Bear in the Parish of Ealing in the County of Middx. at the yearly Wages of seven Pounds & hath quitted sd. Service abt. Elect years

Sworn the 27th of June 1757 before}
