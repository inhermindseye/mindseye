Middx 

Susanna Stears aged about 21 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice for Five Years to Mr. Saml Randal  Clogmaker and lived with the sd. Mr Randal as an Apprentic  for Four Years & 9 Months or there abouts in St Clemts. Church Yard in the Parish in St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and then her sd. Master gave up her Indre Says she never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt 16th. 1750 before} Tho Lane .

Suaner Steers
