Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Burton the widow of John Burton deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served the whole of his apprenticeship with one John Combe a Cooper in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the sd County & did not afterwards to this Deponts Knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That She was Married at the Fleet about Forty years ago to her sd late husband by whom she hath a Daur now living namely Elizabeth Burton aged about thirty Seven years who hath done no Act to gain a settlement for herself & that she this Depont Since her said husbands death hath done no Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this. 29.day of July 1783 before R Davies J Hawley

The Mark of Elizabeth Burton
