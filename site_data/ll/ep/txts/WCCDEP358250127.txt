Saint Clement Danes August 6th. 1794.

Middx and Westminster }  Settlement in St C. D } (to wit) Ann Wright  Widow aged 34 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she was Married to Thomas Wright at the Parish Church of Walcott Batt in the County of Somerset about 17 Years ago That after her said Marriage her said Husband Rented and Resided in a House Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the yearly Rent £10 for the space of One Year and a half that they quitted the same about ten Years ago that after quitting the same her said Husband in his life time did not Rent neither hath she this Examinant since his decease rented any House at the yearly Value of Ten Pounds paid any Taxes, lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year or done any other Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her knowledge information or belief She may have gained any subsequent legal Settlement.

Sworn the 8th. Augst 1794 before me } Edw: Read

her Ann Wright Mark
