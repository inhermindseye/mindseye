Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Lloyd aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that in the Month of Janry. last she Intermarried with Wm. Lloyd her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Margat Lambeth that her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to a Mr. Hutton in the Temple Hairdresser that he was then turned over to a Mr. Sherwood in Saint Martins Lane and afterwards to a Mr. Kitchen nearly facing Hatton Garden in the Parish of Saint andrew Holborn below the Bars in the City of London where he served the last part of his apprenticeship and that after he was out of his said apprenticeship he never rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act Matter or Thing to the best of this Examinants knowledge Informed or belief to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of October 1789  Edward Gray J. Barnfather

Ann Lloyd
