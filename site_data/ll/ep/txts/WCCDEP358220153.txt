Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Patey Aged about 22 Years on her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever Rented a House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year Says her father served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Thacker a Butcher in Clare Markett and after he was out of his said Apprentiship he Rented and lived in a House in the Parish of St. Clement Danes as her said Father as often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be True And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor and unable to maintain the same when born says, that one John Cartwright Lewis a 3d Male on boar ye. Dorothy Captain John Crayton Commander is the Father of the said Bastard Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time the latter End of August last past & at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid and that the said John Cartwright Lewis and no other person is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 12th. day of June 1770 before me} R. Butler

Mary Peatey
