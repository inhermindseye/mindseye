Westmr. to wit Catherine Greenough Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says lives as a hired Yearly Servant with one George Warren at the Crown Alehouse in Stanhope Street Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and has so done upwards of Three Years last Past at the Yearly Wages of four pounds Ten Shillings And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that and John Hannam otherwise Hannam a Labourer who works with Mr. Bannister Brickly in Stanhope Street Clare Markett is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of July last but the Particular Day she does not remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at her said Masters House at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And then the said John Hannam otherwise Hannam did beget the s and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 16th. day of Novr. 1773 before  Tho Kynaston

her Catherine Greenough Mark
