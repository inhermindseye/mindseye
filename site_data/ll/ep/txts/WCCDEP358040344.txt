Middx  Mary Burgen aged about 21 Years a Vagrant tak out of the Street in Labour on the first Day of this Instant March upon Oath saith she is a Singlewoman and that she Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mrs. Carter a Clear Starcher in Thackems Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was on the sd. first Day of March Inst. delivered of a Male Bastard Child in a Room belonging to Mr. Gilberthorpe in little Sheir Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx which has been Baptized David Bird .

Sworn March 31st. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Burgen
