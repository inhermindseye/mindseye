Middx.  James Parker aged abt. 47 Yrs. upon Oath says he intermarried with his Wife Mary in the Chapel of the Savoy upwards of Twenty three Years since by whom he hath one Child (to wit) Ann aged abt. Nine Years says his Wife is ill and this Examinant being poor is not able to provide for himself and family says he Rented two houses in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for twenty Years or thereabouts one of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds and the other of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds and hath quitted the last house upwards of

of Two Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Parish or place to this Examinants Knowledge or belief

Sworn the 6th. of May 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Jas. Parker
