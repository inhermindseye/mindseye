Middx to wit Sarah Telby Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House paid Taxes Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr. Fowler at the Sign of the Crown in Brewer Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster Upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service Upwards of Two Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of June 1780 before M Addington Sampn Wright

her Sarah Telby Mark
