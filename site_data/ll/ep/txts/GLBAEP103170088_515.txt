Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Griffiths  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Mother Jane Griffiths deced has informed her & which She believes to be true that her late Father Owen Griffiths deced in his life time rented a house in Jewin Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldersgate in the City of London & that during Such his residence there in he was Charged to & paid all Parochial Taxes for the Same & by means thereof as She this Deponent is advised he gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents Knowledge or belief her said Father did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly rented or Value of Ten pounds or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this Deponent was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 17. day of July 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes 

The Mark of Elizabeth Griffiths
