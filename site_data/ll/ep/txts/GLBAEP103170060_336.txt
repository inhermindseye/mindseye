Middlesex ss}  Mary Slann the wife of Joshua Slann Mariner maketh oath that her said husband hath been lately pressed & carried on board one of his Majesties Ships of war & is now absent from her That this deponts sd husband divers years ago as appears by his Indres of Apprenticeship now in her Custody was bound Apprentice to Captn  John Bulman of the parish of St Danstan Stepney in the sd County for Seven years to learn the Act of a mariner That after her sd husband as She has been informed & believed had few about two years of his Apprenticeship his Sd Master removed from Hence into Little Alie Street in the parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the sd County where he lived & resided for a Considerable time That differences arising between this Deponts sd husband & his Master as She this Depont has also been informed & believes by her sd husband this Juires were delivered up to him & he was discharged from his Apprenticeship by his sd Masters Consent for a Considerable time before the appirs. of the Term for wch he was bound Apprentice That by a Letter received from her sd husband from on board his Majesties Ship the Conquestador at the more respecting the Settlement of her said husband & addressed to Mr Thomas Bampfield  Vestry Clerk of the said parish of St Botolph without Alegate it appears thereby that for the last Forty days before her said husband left his said Master he lodged at his Masters House in  Little Alie Street Goodmans Fields in the Sd parish of St Mary Whitechapel 

& which information this Deponent believes to be true; She this Deponent also Lodging in the sd house at the same time. and She this Depont. can give no othr Account respecting her sd husbands Settlement; That She this Deponent has two Children namely William Slann aged about three years & four months & John Slann aged about fourteen months the lawful Issue of this Depont by her said husband; to whom She was lawfully married.

Sworn this. 6. day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Mary Slann
