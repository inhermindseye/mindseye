Middx & Westmr}  Lucy Gurney Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. B in Caven Buildings Drury Lane for One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is o are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and further says that one Philip Tillier a Farmer who lately lived with one Mr. Ellwood at Sydenham Court near Windsor who run away from thence on Account of a Bastard Child & who she believes is now in or near Cookam near Maidenhead  in the County of Berks is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of Oct at her Mothers House in Sydenham aforesaid at and at divers other times and places at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid and that the said Philip Tillier & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 6th. day of July 1782 before D Walker

Lucy Gurney
