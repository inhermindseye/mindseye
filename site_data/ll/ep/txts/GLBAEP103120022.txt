Middlesex ss}  John Beale maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound apprentice to Henry Drewett . of the Parish of Westbury in the County of Wilts  Taylor and Served five years of Such his apprenticeship there and by Means thereof he gained a legal Settlement in the Sd. Parish and this Deponent Saith that he hath not Since Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes not to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a Wife named Catherine and a Child name Catherine aged about nine Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Said Wife

Sworn this first day of December 1772 before}
