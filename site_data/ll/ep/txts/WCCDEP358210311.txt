Westmr.  Mary Clarke Aged about Thirty nine Years, upon Oath says she is the Widow of Vincent Clarke who died on the Ninth of December 1767, by whom she hath Five Children (to wit) John aged Eleven Years, Sarah aged Ten Years, Ann aged Five Years, Vincent aged about Three Years and Francis aged about Fifteen Months; Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with John Pindar Esqr. for the space of about Seven Years and then he intermarried with this Examinant he being still in the same Service and under the same hiring as when he was a Single Man and Continued to live with the said John Pindar Esqr. under the said former hiring and Agreement for the space of Five years and then quitted his said Service which happened about Eight Years ago, says her said Husband during the whole time of his Service always laid in his Master's house, and he lodged and lay the

the last Two Months of his said Service in the House of His said Master in Queen Square in the Parish of St. George the Martyr by Queen Square in the County of Middlesex , and then quitted his said Service as aforesaid, says her said late Husband never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, or paid Taxes

Sworn the 5th day of August 1768 , before}  Fran Cary

Mary Clark
