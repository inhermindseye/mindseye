Middx.  Christian Dalby aged abt. 48 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Jos: Dalby & that the said Jos: Dalby upwards of Twenty six Years ago & before his Intermarriage with this Examinant lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wm. Young at the Kings Arms Inn in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 27th. day of Sepr. 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond Christian Dalby
