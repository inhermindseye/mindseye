Middx to wit Alice Inwood aged about fifty Years on her Oath says She is the Widow of Robert Inwood who died about Twenty Months ago with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Ten Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living says her said Husband served seven Years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Smith a Poulterer in Gilberts Passage Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he Rented and lived in a House near the Punch in Clements Lane for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds and after quitting the said last Mentioned House he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 20th. day of Augt. 1779 before R Butler

her Alice Inwood  Mark
