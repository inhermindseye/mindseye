Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to Wit) James Talboys one of the Beadles of the parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Adam Marcham an Infant aged five Years and upwards Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been credibly informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Adam Marcham was born a Bastard at the house of a Mr. Vaughan Little New Street Hill in the parish of Saint Brides alias Bridgett in the City of London

Sworn this 31st- day of July 1790 N Bond W Ferster.

Jas Talboys
