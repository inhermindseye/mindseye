Middx  Isabella Rogers upon her Oath saith she is the Widow of George Rogers that she intermarried with the said George Rogers in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Feilds on or about the 6th. Day of March 1730 Says the sd. George Rogers lived in & rented a house in Rupert Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four pounds or thereabouts for Six Years and upwards and quitted the same about Six Years Says he never after rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochiall Office or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath not since his Death rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworne 19th. Octr. 1745 before  Fraser

The Mark of Isab: Rogers
