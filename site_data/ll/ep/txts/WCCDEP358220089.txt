Middlesex & Westmr.}  Thomas Ward Aged about Fifty five years on on his Oath says he Intermarried with Sarah his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Luke Chelsea in the County of Middlesex on the 4th day of Septr. 1768 says before his Intermarriage & whilst he was a Single man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Miles Cotton a Farmer in the Parish of Childerley in ye County of Cambridge for 2 Years at the Yearly wages of Six Pounds hath Quitted his said Service about Twenty Seven Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same Ever rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 23d day of Novr. 1769 before Us}  R Butler

Thomas Ward
