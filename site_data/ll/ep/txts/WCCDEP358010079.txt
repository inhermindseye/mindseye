Saint Clement Danes

Middx (ss)  Elizabeth Holmes aged 45 years or thereabout upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived with one John Stevens  Linen Drapers the Corner of St Brides Passage in the Parish of Saint Brides alias Bridget in the City of London as a yearly hired Servant for 19 Months or there abouts at the yearly Wages of £7. hath Quitter said servi about 11 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant or done any act matter of Thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of March 1787 Maddington Thos: Gordon Eliz Holmer
