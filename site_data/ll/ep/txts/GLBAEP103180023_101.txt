Middlesex ss}  Mary Barnes  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with James Abiton a Distiller in Cable Street in the Parish of St. George in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th day of March 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey P. Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Barnes
