Westmr ss  Margt. Eglin Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Anthony Eglin Rented and lived in a House known by the Name or Sign of the Three Kings and Red Lyon at North end in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or upwards as she believes and was rated to and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Eight Years ago and hath not since gained any other subsequent Settlement And further says she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child on the 19th. day of February 1772 in Clements Lane which was soon afterwards baptized by the Name of Ann , And was also delivered of a Male Bastard Child on Twenty Seventh Augt last past in Water Lane Fleet Street which was soon afterwards baptized by the Name of Thomas and that the Father of both the said Bastard Children died on the Sixth day of August last past

Sworn this 16th. day of Novr. 1774 before  Sampn Wright W Addington

Margaret Eaglin
