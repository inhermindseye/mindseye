Bastards  Martha Rogers and Richard her Bastard Child

St Clement Danes May 19th 1792

Middx (ss) & Westminster }  St CD Woman  Child  St C D (to wit) Martha Rogers  Singlewoman aged 24 years and upwards Upon Oath saith That She never was Married That She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Walton at the Bull in Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the said County for about two years at the Annual Wages of £4:10 for the first and £6 for the Second years that She quitted that Service about two years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement and this Deponent further saith that on the 23d day of December 1791 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child named Richard which hath since been Christened by the Name of Richard (as this Deponent hath been informed & believes) that the said Child was born in the House of Richard Hunt called the Cheshire Street situate in Milford Lane aforesaid in the said parish That the said Child is likely to become chargeable to the said parish act his Deponent is unable to maintain the same and this Deponent Saith that one Richard Portsmouth a Coal Carman to Mr Wood of Scotland Yard Westminster is the only real and true father of such Child of which She was not so delivered as aforesaid her he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body on or about the 19th

day of March 1791. and at Several times since at who some or one of which times he did be get the said Child thereon and that he and no one else is the only real and true father there of such Child

Sworn this 19th day of May 1792 before me} R Butler

Martha Rogers 's Mark
