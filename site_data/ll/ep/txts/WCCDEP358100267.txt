Middlesex & Westminster}  Swear to St. Clemts to wit Sarah Roe aged about Fifty Eight years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Andrew Rust a Book binder in Naked Boy Court Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Twelve years ago since which time she hath never lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten  Pounds or year or otherwise gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of October 1784 R Butler

her Sarah Roe mark
