Middx  Thomas Humphreys maketh Oath and Saith that about five Years ago he Rented a house in Gulston Street in the parish of St. Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex and lived therein about Three Years and paid Ten pounds a Year for the same And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And hath a Wife named Jane and four Children (namely) Elizabeth Aged about Seven Years Jane Aged about Six Years Ann Aged about four Years and Thomas Aged about one Year

Sworn this 16th day of January 1745 Before Robert Dennett

his Thomas Humphreys Mark
