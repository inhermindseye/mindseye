Middx  The Examination of Ann Richmond  Singlewoman aged abt. 29 Years lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx taken the 24th- Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. abt. 2 Years ago She rented a House on Clerkenwell Green in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in ye- same County of the Yearly Rent of £17 for the Space of one Year & upwards & further on her Oath saith yt- she hath not since that Time rented any other House pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous A Godfrey

Ann Richmond
