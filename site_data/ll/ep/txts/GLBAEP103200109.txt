Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Jane Sutor of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the sd County this Twenty third day of February in the year of our Lord 1784 -who Saith that she is now with Child and that the sd Child is likely to be born a Bastard & to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate and that Robert Clarke of Deptford in the County of Kent Shipwright is the Father of the said Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before me R Davies

The Mark of Jane Sutor
