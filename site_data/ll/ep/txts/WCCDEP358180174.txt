Middx  Ann Bailey aged abt. 25 Year's Wife of Thomas Bailey  Removed from the parish of Berkhamsted St. Peters in the County of Hertford by an Order dated the 10th: Day of February last upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her present Husband  Thomas Bailey at the Fleet London upwards of Four Years since & before her Intermarriage with the sd. Thomas Bailey she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Tharp Butterman in Claremarkett in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £4. for the Space of 2 Years And this Examinant cant give any Acct. of the Settlemt. of her sd. Husband other than she hath heard say that he was born in Yorkshire That the sd. Thos. Bailey hath absconded from this Examinant upwards of three Years & this Examinant knows not where he is

Sworne 21st: March 1743 before} Tho Lane :

The Mark of Ann Bailey
