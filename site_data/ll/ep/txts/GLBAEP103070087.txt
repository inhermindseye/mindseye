Middx ss The Examination of Martha Snell  Singlewomen taken upon oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for ye County of Middx one whereof is of the quorum) residing in or Near to the limits of the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd. County this 8th day of Septr. 1752

This Examinant on her oath Saith that about the latter end of October last past one James Blagdon a Currier Who then lived in the Parish of St. George In Middx did often come to this Examinant at the house of Mr. John Mason at the Sign of the Bull Head in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell in the County of Middx and did there Prevail on this Examinant to lye with her and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants body and often at afterwards ther & at ther Clare did lye with this Examinant And that this Examinant Conceived with Child by him of a Male Bastard Child of Which She was delivered in a Street Called Nightingale Lane in ye sd. Parish of St. Botolph Without Aldgate about a Month ago Which sd. Bastard Child

hath been Baptised by the Name of James and Now is & Since it's birth hither to hath been ye Still is likely to be Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate this Examinant being poor & not able to provided for the Same And lastly this Examinant Saith that the sd. James Blagdon is the true & only father of ye sd. Male Bastard Child And that this Examination is Voluntary And that She hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whatsoever

Sworn this 8th day of Sepr. 1753 before us  Richd. Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

her Martha Snell Mark
