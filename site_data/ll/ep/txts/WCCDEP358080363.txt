Middlesex to wit Jane Clinch Aged about Twenty Nine years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Coventry at the sign of the Catherine Wheel at Egham in the County of Surry for Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Two Guineas besides Vales hath quitted her said Service about about Eleven Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath Says that on the 5th. day of March last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Swift in Hemlock Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the name of William and is become Chargable to the same she being Poor and unable to maintain the sd. Child And that one John Wopshot a Taylor at Egham aforesaid did beget such Bastard Child on her Body And further says that the said John Wopshot and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley  Sampn Wright

Jane Clinch
