Middx  Eleanor Wilmott upon her Oath saith she intermarried with James Wilmott about 14 or 15 Yrs. since at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband was born in the Parish of Old Swinford in the County of Stafford where his Father Edwd. Wilmott rented a house and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes as she hath been Informed & beleives Says her sd. Husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 9th: 1745 before}  Fraser

The Mark of Eleanr. Wilmott
