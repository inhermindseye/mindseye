Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Ralph Aged about Twenty six Years, upon Oath says she never was Married and that she now lives a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Lacey a Hosier in Clement Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex and hath so done for the space of Six Years at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds; And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born Says that one Henry McDonald who is now an Apprentice to the said Mr. Lacey had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of January last and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said Henry Mc. Donald and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 29th. day of August 1768 . Before W Kelynge

Mary Ralph
