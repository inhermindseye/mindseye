Middlesex to wit Jane Alldridge aged about Fifty nine Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Alldridge who has been Dead about eighteen Years ago with whom she Intermarried at Shoreditch Church about 30 Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said husband lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Gwynn Goldstone a Wine Merchant in Howard Street in the Parish of St- Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of £12 besides Bed and Board hath quitted the same upwards of 30 Years ago since which time he never gained any other subseqt- Settlemt- neither hath this Examinant since her said husbands Death

Sworn this 25th- Day of Febry 1778 before R Butler

her Jane Alldridge Mark
