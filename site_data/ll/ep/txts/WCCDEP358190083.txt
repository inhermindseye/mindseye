Middx.  Ann Whart aged abt. 22 Years lodging at Mr. Smiths in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married and that on the Tenth day of November 1759 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Drawwater a Mason in Great Castle Street Oxfordmarket in the Parish of St. Mary le Bone in the County of Middx which hath since been baptized by the Name of Philip

Sworn Jany. 9th. 1761 . before}
