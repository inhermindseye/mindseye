Middlesex ss  Jane Henly  Single woman Maketh oath That one Ann Chance about eight years ago was bound Apprentice by Parish Indenturers to her this Deponent who then lived in Harwood Court in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex And this Depont. Saith that the sd. Ann Chance lived with and served this Depont. as this Deponts. Apprentice by Virtue of the sd. Indres of apprenticeship for the Space & time of two Month & upwards in the said Parish and then the sd. Ann Chance went from & left this Depont. And this Depont. farther Saith That the said Ann Chance since she left this Depont. as aforesaid hath not done any Act Whereby to gain a settlement subsequent to her Said Apprenticeship to the knowledge information or belief of this Deponent.

Sworn this 3d. day of March 1767 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Jane Henly her Mark
