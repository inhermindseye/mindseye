Middx  Mary Wall aged 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Wagstaff in Bandy leg Walk in the parish of St. Savrs . Southwark in the County of Surry for three Years at the Yearly wages of £5. quitted her sd Service about 9 Yrs. ago and has not since rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Jany. 31st. 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Wall
