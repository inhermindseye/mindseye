Westmr.  Anthony Armstrong aged upwards of 70 Years upon Oath say he rented a house in Falconbridge Court in the Parish of St. Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for Three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds quitted same about Twenty Years ago, and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 21st. day of August 1766 , before  Fran. Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Anthony Armstrong
