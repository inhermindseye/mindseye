Middlesex ss}  Charles Stewart maketh oath That he heretofore rented a house of the yearly rent of five Pounds ten Shillings in Kent Street in the Parish of Newington Butts in the County of Surrey & during Such his residence therein he was Charged to & Paid Kings Taxes for the Same and has not since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 24. day of Decemr 1784 before  Robt. Smith R Davies

The Mark of Charles Stewart
