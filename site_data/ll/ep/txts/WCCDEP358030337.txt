Westmr. 

Mary Mott  Wido : of Wm. Mott upon Oath saith the sd. Wm. Mott hath been dead about 19 Years. Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Masters in Bear Yard in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of £4 for the Space of 14 Years or thereabouts being all that time a Wido. without Child or Children Says she hath left her sd. Service about 5 Yrs. & hath not since rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Places to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Febry 7th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Mott .
