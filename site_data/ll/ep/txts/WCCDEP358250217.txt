Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Fray aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 23d. day of February 1794 she Intermarried with George Fray her late Husband who has been dead about Twelve Months at the Parish Church of Little St. Annes in Bull & Month Street by Banns by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. William aged about Three years That her said late Husband Rented a Shop under Lincolns Inn Gateway for about Two years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and that he Rented at the same time a Three pair of Stairs front Room at the Bible in Great  Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for which he paid the Annual Rent of Four Pounds and that never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st day of Decr. 1795 W Kinnard N Bond

her Elizth. Fray Mark
