Middx  Frances Champion aged about Years upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of Rt. Champion Deceased that the sd. Rt: Champion never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. of his own Accord to this Exaiants knowledge or beleife that her sd. late Husbands Father  Wm. Champion lived in & rented a House in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £30. & lived in the same the Space of 16 Years or thereabouts

Sworn & July 9th: 1744 before}
