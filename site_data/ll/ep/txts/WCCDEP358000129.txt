Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

Swears to St. Clements. to wit Frances Brooks aged about 33 years on her Oath saith that the Intermarried to Jane Brooks at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London about Nine years ago that after their Intermarriage her said Husband & this Examinant Rented and lived a House in Shire Lane in the Parish of a Saint Clement Danes at the yearly Rent of 13 Pounds for 3 Months and upwds hath quitted said House about Six years ago that since that time her sd. Husband has not gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere that her said Husband has deserted her and she is Poor an unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 17th- day of Octr. 1785  Wm Kitchiner

her Frances Brooks Mark
