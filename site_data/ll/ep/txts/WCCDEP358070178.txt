Westmr. to wit Henry Collins Holdaway Aged about Thirty Nine Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Susannah his Present Wife at Bishop Storford in the County of Essex about Two Years ago and by whom he hath one Child Named Samuel Aged about Eight Months Says he was bound Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one William Rooke a Pene Cutter on Stepney Green in the Parish of St. Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he Rented and lived in a House in Mutton Lane in the Aforesaid Parish upwards of one Year at the Yearly Rent of Nine Pounds and paid Land Tax due for the same but no poors Rate and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship & Renting the above House he never gained any other subsequent Settlement whatever.

Sworn this 23d day of February 1774 before W kelyngs Sampn Wright

Henry Collins Holdaway

Paid 2s.
