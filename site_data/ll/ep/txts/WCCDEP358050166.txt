Westmr  Mary Saunders upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Thomas Mercer in Vinegar Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the Field in the Liberty of Westmr. for two Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £3 and hath left her sd. Service upwds. of two Years since which time she has not rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 8th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of  Mary Saunders
