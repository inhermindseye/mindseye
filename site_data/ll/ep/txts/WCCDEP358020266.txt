St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) John Ross Aged about Sixty Five Years Six Years or thereabouts upon Oath saith that he intermarried with his present Wife  Isabella Ross at a Curk at Edingburgh Scotland in or about the Year 1752 that he Rented and lived in a House in Change Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Annual Rent of £18 or thereabouts for about Nine Years that he and his said Wife quitted the said House about 12 or 13 Years ago and since he Hath not done any Act or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he might gain any other legal Settlement

Sworn the 14th April before me  Wm Kitchiner

John Ross
