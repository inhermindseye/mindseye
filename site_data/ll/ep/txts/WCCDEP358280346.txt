St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster } (to wit) Jane Murray Aged 24 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was never Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Huxley No. 18 Cecil Street  Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about 16 Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she left that Service on the 24th. day of October instant And this Examinant further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And that Sinclair of the Kingdom of Ireland a Student of the Law who uses the Cecil Street Coffee House in the Strand generally in Term is the only real & true father of such Child or Children of which she is so pregnant he and no one else having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same thereon

Sworn the 31st. October 1791 . before me} R Butler
