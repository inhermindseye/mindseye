Saint Clement Danes Febry 24th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C. D.  (to wit) Orindia Hudson aged 30 Years or thereabout Upon her Oath saith that she never was married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Sucoss who resided at the Angel Inn  Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes , at the Wages of Four Pounds Per Annum, upwards of Twelve Months, that she quitted that service about Fourteen Years since, and hath not done any Act matter, or thing, whatsoever; whereby to the best of her knowledge; information, or beliefe, to obtain any subsequent legal settlement

Sworn this 24th Febry 1794 before us} Wm Kitchiner

her Orindia Hudson Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
