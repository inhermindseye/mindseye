Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Buckin aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes That she lived as a hired yearly servant with Mr. Bristol who keeps Saint Clements Chop House in Saint Clements Church Yard in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Five pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Six Months since which time she hath done no Act to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 19th. day of April now last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Store Street Hospital which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary That one Thomas Sargeant a Waiter at the Georges Coffee House in the Strand is the father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of July last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she this Deponent was so delivered of as aforesaid and that the said Thomas Sargeant is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 27th. May 1795 Before me} Cha: Churchill

her Mary Buckin Mark

Witness B Newman

Thos. Sarjeant is about 26 years of Age is about 5 feet high dark Complexn dark hair wears his Hair tyed and Powdered wears a brown Coat & Yellow Waiscoat & dark Herseymere Breeches

30th. May 95 Man taken and paid £25 10 and Executed Release

Child Sent to Enfield 4th. Septr. 1795
