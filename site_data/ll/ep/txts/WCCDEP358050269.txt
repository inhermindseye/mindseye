Westmr. 

Arabella Rhodes aged about 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she at present is and for two Years past has been a Yearly Servant to Mr. Plunket at the Corner of Drum Alley in Drury Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly wages of £4 And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that Robert Clayton  a Perukemaker did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. Robert Clayton lodging at Mr Plunkets aforesd.

these became acquainted with her and had carnal knowledge of her body in the month of Septr last and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that she the said Robert Clayton and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworne June 12th. 1750 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Arabella Rhodes
