Middx  The Exaicon of Thos. Richardson on the behalfe of Judith Richardson the Wido . of Nichs. Richardson his Brother Deced taken the Second day of Septr. 1740 Who upon his Oath Saith that abt. Thirty five years ago his sd. Brother Nichs. Richardson lived a yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Burleigh a Farmer in ye the parish of Hatfield Bishop Hatfield in the County of Hertford for the Space of three years or thereabts. And further Saith that the sd. Nichs. Richardson hath not since he left the sd. Mr. Burleigh Rented Ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes lived a yearly hired Servt. with any other person for ye Space of one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworn the day & yeare above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Geo: Tarry

The mark of Thos. Richardson
