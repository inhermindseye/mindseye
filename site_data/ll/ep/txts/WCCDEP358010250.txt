Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Hood aged about 39 years upon her Oath saith that on the 18th. day of Augt 1783 she Intermarried with Samuel her late Husband (who has been dead about Nine Months) at the Parish Church of Camberwell  that she hath Two Children living by her said late Husband Vizt. Samuel aged about Five years and John aged about Nineteen Months that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House the Corner of Angel Alley without Bishopgate  in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Bishopgate in the City of London for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £30 that he quitted said House about 4 years since during which time he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year or did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. Octr. 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

Mary Hood
