Westmr.  Elizabeth Dugdale aged about 26 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the poor of the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster to John Hill of Ratcliffe highway in the Parish of St. George in the East in the County of Middlesex Silk winder and served about half a Year of her said Apprenticeship with the said John Hill in Ratcliffe high way aforesaid and then her said Master absconded and has not since been heard of, and this Examinant further upon her Oath says she was informed by her Mother that she was discharged from her Apprenticeship with the said John Hill by one of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace who dwelt near West Smithfield , says after she was informed of her being discharged from her said Apprentice- ship she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Martin a Pawnbroker in Reeves's Mews in the aforesaid Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted said Service about Eight Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn 13th. Novr. 1765 . before} Fran Caryt  Paul Vaillant
