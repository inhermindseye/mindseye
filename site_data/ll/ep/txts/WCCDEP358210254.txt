Westmr.  Elizabeth Russell Aged about Twenty Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she served Three Years or thereabouts the latter Part of her Apprenticeship with Mrs. Bond a Mantua Maker the corner of Allens Court in Leadenhall Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Axe London , and then she and her Mistress parted by Consent this Examinant having then about Six Months of her said Apprenticeship to serve, says she never was Assigned over to any Person, nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year, Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 12th day of March 1768 , Before}  W Kelynge

Eli Russell
