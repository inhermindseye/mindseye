Middlesex ss}  Mary Hemming  Single woman maketh oath that She lately Rented a house of the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & lived in the same for the Space of Three quarters of a year & has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She hath three Children namely Ann Curttler aged about Six Years & Mary Curttler aged about four years both of whom were born on Saffron Hill in the Liberty of Saffron Hill or Hatton Garden in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the sd County & baptized & registred in the Church thereto belonging; And Thomas Curttler aged about two years who was born in the sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate Middx & baptized & registred in the Church thereto belonging All which Said three Children are illegitimate & Bastards by reason that She this Deponent was never Married to their late Father Thomas Curttler deced.

Sworn this fouth day of November 1783 before R Davies J. Hawley

Mary Hemming
