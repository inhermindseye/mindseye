Middlesex ss}  John Rolls maketh oath that he was born at Dundee in Scotland and never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That about five years ago he was lawfully married to his late Wife Margaret Lehyc deced who was born at Cork in Ireland as She has informed this Deponent and which he believes to be true and who never did any Act to this Deponents Knowledge information or belief to gain a Settlement for herself in England That he this Deponent hath a Child named Elizabeth aged about four years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his said late wife who was born in Lower East Smithfield in the Precinct of Saint Catherine in the Said County of Middlesex and was there Baptized and Registred

Sworn this 9. day of January 1788 before  R Davies  Rob. Smith

The Mark of John Rolls
