Middx  Margt: Bedward Aged about 30 Years removed from St Andrew by the Ward Robe London by Order Dated the ninth of May 1763 Upon Oath Says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Sert with Mr. Hugh Treble at the White Lyon in Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx One Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath quitted her said Service upwards of Seven Years and hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says she has Two Children to wit Mary Aged about Three Years born in Ashen tree Court in the precinct of White Fryars London and John Aged about Nine Months born in Huggin Lane  Old Bread Street Hill in the Parish of London.

Sworn ye. 11th Day of May 1763 Before}
