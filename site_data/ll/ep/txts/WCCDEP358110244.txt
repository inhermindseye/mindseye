Westmr.  Mary Fitzwalters aged 37 Yrs. upon Oath Saith, she never was married & that upwards of two Years ago she was hired to live a Yearly Servant with Mr. Morgan at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds and lived with the sd. Morgan under that hiring and Agreement in New Broad Court in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year & upwards And that, since she quitted her sd. Service She has not Rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn May 5th. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Mary Fitzwalters
