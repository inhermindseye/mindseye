St Clement Danes 30th April 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Ann Westmr .  to wit George Russell aged Seventy years or thereabouts Widower Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his late Wife Mary formerly Mary Hunt  Spinster about 30 years ago that subsequent thereto he Rented and resided in a House in Milk Alley Dean Street in the parish of Saint Ann Westminster for about Eleven years that at the Annual Rent of £15 that he quitted the same about Seven years ago and hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief Saith that his said Wife died about Seven years ago and hath & that he is distressed and unable to maintain himself without assistance

Sworn the 30th. day of April 1793 before us N Bond Mitchell

George Russells Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
