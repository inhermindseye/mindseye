St Clement Danes 6th March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Esther Leach aged 23 years and upwards singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Matthews No 11 Wych Street facing the back of St Clement Coffee House in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of £5 that she quitted that Service about two years ago and hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve month together or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 6th day of March 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Esther Leach 's Mark

Witness Garrett
