Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charlotte Searle Aged about 37 years upon her Oath Saith That about the year 1785 she intermarried with her late Husband who has been Dead about Six years at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom she hath living two Children Vizt. Charlotte Aged about 13 years and Arthur Aged about Seven years That her said late Husband rented and lived in a house in Church Street in the Hamlet of Mile End New Town in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex for Six Months at the Yearly rent of Eleven Pounds That he hath Left she said House about Nine years since which time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement and put on this Deponent upon her Oath Saith that on the 14th day of May 1798 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of one Mr Draper in Baldwins Place  Grays Inn Land in the Parish of Saint Andrew above the Bars in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by she of Ann

Sworn this 21st day of Jany 1800 before us} Ws Woodenough Saml. Edwards

Charlotte Searle
