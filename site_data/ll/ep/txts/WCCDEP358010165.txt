Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Bunn aged about 52 years up her Oath saith that about 21 years of age she Intemarried with Thos. Bunn her late Husband (wh has been dead about 8 years) at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the field that her said late Husband in his Life time Rented and lived in a House at the Back of St. Clement Danes in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 8 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £20 and paid Taxes due for the same that she hath quitted said House upwds. of 6 years since which time she hath not rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Novr. 1787 R Buller

her Sarah Bunn Mark
