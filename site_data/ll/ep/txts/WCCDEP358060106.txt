Middx  Johanna Hills upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Hills who dyed about Six weeks ago Says she intermarried with her sd. late husband at the Fleet about Seven Years since and has by him 3 Children (viz.) Wm. aged upwds. of Six Years Jas . aged upwds. of 2 Yrs. & John aged about Nine months Says she hath been Informed and believes her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Thomas a Butcher in Bear Yard in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn May 10th. 1751 before}

The Mark of Johanh Hills
