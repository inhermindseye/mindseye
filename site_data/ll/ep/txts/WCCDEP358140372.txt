Middx.  Jannett Bell aged abt. 32 Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she was Bound Apprentice for seven Years to one Mrs. Joell a Child Coat maker at the Sign of the Sun in Fleet Street Opposite Shoe Lane in the Parish of St. Bridget alias St. Bride in the City of London And served her abt. One year and a Qur. and then Absconded from her said Mistress and never was assigned Over to any person nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Parish Taxes And further upon her Oath says that on the 14th. of August 1756. She was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of One Mr. Cholick in Lambeth Marsh in the Parish of St. Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry which hath since been Baptized Ann and that One James Collett who was the Reputed father of the said Child died about Two Months ago.

Sworn this 21st. of May 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Jannett Bell
