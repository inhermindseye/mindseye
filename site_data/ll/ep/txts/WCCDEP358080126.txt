Middx & Westmr-}  Mary Morris aged about 25 Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Isaac Morris who is pressed and gone to Sea and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St- Giles in the Fields upwards of two Years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living named James Aged about Ten Weeks also another Child by a formar Wife named Isaac aged about 7 Year says her said husband served his Apprenticeship with one Wm- Blackborow  Esqr . a Brass Founder near Clerkenwell Green in the Parish of St- James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a subseqt- Settlemt-

Sworn this 12th- Day of March 1777 before me  Jno. Machin  John Croft
