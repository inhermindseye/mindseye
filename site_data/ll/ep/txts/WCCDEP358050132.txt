Westmr.  Billah Dixon Wife of Jonah Dixon upwards Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. husband at Blackfryers Church in London upwds. of 14 Yrs. ago by whom she hath six Childn. (viz) John aged upwds. of thirteen Years Elizth. aged upwds. of Eleven Years Billiah aged upwds. of 6 Yrs Hanh. aged upwds. of five Years Jonah aged upwds. of two Years & Robert aged one month and further saith the sd. Jonah Dixon never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes and that he was born in the Hamlet of Hatton Roof and Low End in the Parish of Greystock in the County of Cumberland where his father lived in and rented a house and paid Sevt and Lot as she hath been informed and verily beleives.

Sworn Octr. 28th. 1749 before H Fielding

The mark of Billah Dixon
