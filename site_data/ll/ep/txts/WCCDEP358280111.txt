Saint Clement Danes

She passed from Newington Batts. here. to wit Elizabeth Edwards aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes, That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Burnett a Bookseller in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 12 Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about 3 years ago since which time she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement, And further this Ext. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish, That one George Roberts a Servant to Sir Watkin Lewes in the Kings Road is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Exts. Body and begotten the same thereon

Sworn this 29th. day of Augt. 1787 } R Butler

Eliz Edwards
