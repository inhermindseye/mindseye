St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Mary Gilmour Aged 22 years & upwards upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an is the legitimate Daughter of John Gilmour and Mary his late Wife -that her said Father Rented and Resided in a House Situate in Lumby's Court in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster & Co of Middx about two years at the Annual Rent of £12 or upwards that he quitted the same about 4 or 5 years ago and since hath not (by any) means) Acquired any legal Settlement that she this Deponent hath never been bound or served as an Apprentice hath never lived as on hired yearly Servant for a twelve month under one hiring nor done any other Act Matter or thing Whereby she might acquire any legal Settlement in her own Right

Sworn the 2d Septr 1791 . before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Mary Gilmour 's Mark
