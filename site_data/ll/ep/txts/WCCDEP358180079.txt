Middx  Isabella Urqhuirt aged 24 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that about 2 Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Richards over agt. Exeter Exchange in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds for the Space of one Year & a half that since she left the said Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & having lat the Use of her left Arm is not able to maintain herself

Sworne the 20th. April 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

Isebl Urqhut
