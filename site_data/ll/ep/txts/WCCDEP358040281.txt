Westmr. 

Hannh. Smith aged upwds. of 50 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwds. of 20 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Lally a Dancing master in Hatton Gardn. in the Liberty of Saffron hill  Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holbn. in the County of Middlesex for three Years & upwds at the Yearly Wages of £6 and has not since she quitted Mr. Lallys Service Rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 8th. 1748 before  Wm: Jones

The mark of Hanh. Smith
