St Clement Danes Novr. 29th 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  a Child  St George Westmr  (to wit) Ann Seakings aged 25 Years or thereabouts Wife of Thomas Seakings who is now at Sea in his Majesty's Service Upon Oath saith that She was Married that she lived as an to her Husband Thomas Seakings at the Church of the united parishes of Christ Church and St Leonard Foster London (by Banns) on or about the 11th day of September 1791 that she hath only one child by her said Husband named Thomas born on or about the 31st day of July 1792 who is now with this Examinant that She is informed & believes her said Husband hath never did any acquired Any legal Settlement in his own Right that her said Husband is the legitimate Son of Thomas Seakings deced that his who lived as an hired yearly Servant with M that her said Husband's father was a parishioner of the said parish of St George in the Liberty of Westminster and several of his Children were brot. up in the Workhouse of the said parish of St George And this Examinant saith that She Knows not of any Settlement he is entitled to save in the said parish of St George as aforesaid

Sworn the 29th day of November 1793 before us R Ford B: Kennett

Ann Seekings
