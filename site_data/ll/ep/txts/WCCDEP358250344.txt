Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Mc. Grath aged about 44 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the 20th.. day of May 1774 she Intermarried with John Mc. Grath  her late Husband who left this Examt. about Eight years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Milk Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds That her said Husband quitted said house about Eight years since which time he hath not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Janry. 1798 before me} J Hunter

her Margaret Mr. Grath Mark

Witness B Newman
