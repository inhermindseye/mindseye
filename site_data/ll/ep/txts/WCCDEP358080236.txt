Westmr. to wit Deborah Ward Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Adamson at the Sign of the Fountain in Holles Street Clare Markett for One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of four Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. day of April 1778 before R Butler

her Deborah Ward Mark
