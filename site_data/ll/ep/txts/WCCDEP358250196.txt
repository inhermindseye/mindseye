Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Esther Battison aged about 61 years upon his Oath saith That her father William Johnson Rented a house in Mill Pond Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry about Two years at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas and paid all Taxes due for the same that after he quitted said House he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of Augt. 1795 N Bond Geo: Reid

Esther Battison
