Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Houghton and James Talboys severally make Oath and say and first this Deponent Sarah Houghton for herself Saith that she is about 26 years of Age that she does not know where she was born that she never was married Rented any house Tent. or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months at any one time to the best of this Examinants knowledge or ever bound an Apprentice And this Deponent James Talboys for himself saith that he has been informed by one Mrs. Williams the Aunt of the said Sarah Houghton that the said Sarah Houghton is the illegitimate Daughter of one Elizabeth-Houghton and was born at the house of a Mrs. Dawson at Leeds in the Parish of Leeds in the County of York .

Sworn this 22d. day of May 1795  J Hlund M Addington

her Sarah Houghton Mark

Jas Talboys
