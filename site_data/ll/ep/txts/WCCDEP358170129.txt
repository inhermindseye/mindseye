Middx  Mary Randall  Wife of Richd. Randall passed from the Borough of Samford in the County of Lincoln upon her Oath saith that abt. 13 Years ago she Intermarried with the sd. Richard Randall at the Fleet  London & that the sd. Richd. Randall was born in the Parish of Chistick in the County of Warwick & that her sd. Husband never rented Ten Pounds a Year was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & further saith yt. before her Intermarriage with her sd. Husband & whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Powell in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Eleven Pounds for the Space of one Yr. & upwards that she hath 3 Childn. namely Ann aged abt. Two Years Phillis aged abt. Four Years & Mary aged about Eleven Years & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe and them & this Exaint further Saith that she was passed from the Parish of St. John in the Burrough of

Lincoln to the Parish of Chistick in the County of Warwick

Sworne the 14th. of Septr- 1741 before}

The Mark of Mary Randall
