Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Chery on the behalf of Agnes Gray  Singlewoman Aged Eighteen Years & upwards Taken the 26th. Day of May 1739 Who upon her Oath saith yt. upwds. of three Years ago the above Named Agnes Gray was hired to & lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Jeffrys in little Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the County of Middx  Taylor for the Space of fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds And this Examint. further saith yt. the sd. Agnes Gray never Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & that she is now Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Tho Lane J Poulson

Eliz. Chery
