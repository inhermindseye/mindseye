Before Birth 

Saint Clement Danes 25 July, 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnancy  Reputed Father (to wit) Susannah Duglass  Singlewoman Aged 32 Years and Upwards Upon her Oath Saith that she never was married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Dawson Wollen draper late of Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Wollen draper for 2 Years and 4 Months at the Annual Wages of eight Guineas that she quitted that place on or about the 15th day of July Instant and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to be the best of this deponents knowledge and belief And this deponent upon her Voluntary Oath further Saith That she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born and become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes when born And that John Bramble is the only Real and true Father of the Child or Children wherewith she is Pregnant as aforesaid he having first had Carnal knowledge of this deponents Body about Six Months Ago and at several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children thereon And that he and no else is the Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25 day of July 1793 before us } Wm. Kitchiner

her Susannah Duglass Mark

Witness J Hawkins
