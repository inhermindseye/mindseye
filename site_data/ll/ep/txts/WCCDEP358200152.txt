Middx  Elizabeth Hays  Singlewoman Aged about Twenty two Years upon Oath Says she was Bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of St. Sepulchres in the City of London to Miss Jenkins a Milliner in the Strand in ye Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx with whom she Lived about three Years and then Went away and never was Assigned over to any Person Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert. for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is Likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards & to Become

Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Chas. Harper a Shoemaker had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of March Last & at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant.

Sworn the Day of 1764 Before
