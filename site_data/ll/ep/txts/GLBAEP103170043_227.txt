Middlesex ss}  Christian Marsh the widow of Luke Marsh a Soldier deced maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said late husband or the place of his birth or of her Sd husbands Parents or eithr of them That She this Deponent was born at Montrose in Scotland & her parents or either of them to her knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in any place in England That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year in England nor ever did and Act to gain a Settlement in her own right Save what She Acquired by her Marriage as aforesaid

Sworn this. 8. day of January 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Christian Marsh
