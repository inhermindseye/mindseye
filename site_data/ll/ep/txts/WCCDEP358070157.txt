Westmr.  Mary Marr  Wife of James Marr on behalf of Eleanor Price an Instant aged about Ten Years whose Father William Price has deserted her on her Oath Says that the said William the Father of the Said Eleanor Price Rented and lived in a House in Bow Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for half a Year or upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Two pounds a Year as she has been Informed and verily believes hath quitted the same upwards of one Year & out of since which time he never gained any subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or beleif neither hath the said Eleanor Price the Infant gained any Settment by living a hired Yearly Servant or otherwise to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 30th. day of Decr. 1773 before R. Butler W Kelynge

her Marry Marr mark

Mary Whiddon
