Middx  Jonathan Parker upon his Oath saith that abt. Nineteen Years ago & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Knight in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of one Year & Six Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds & that since he left the sd. Mr. Knight's Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife & that he hath one Wife named Elizth - & being Poor is not able to maintaine himself & Wife

Sworn the 1st- Day of August 1741 before}

Jona Parker
