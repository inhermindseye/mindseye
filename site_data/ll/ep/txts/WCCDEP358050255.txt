Westmr. 

Jno. Hubbard aged near 19 Yrs. upon Oath saith he is the Son of Jno. Hubbard  by his wife (both deceased) and that his said late father Jno Hubbard rented a house commonly known by the name of the White horse Alehouse in Drum Alley in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for and lived therein two or three Years and quitted the same about four Years ago and never after rented ten pounds a Yr. or pd. parish Taxes Says his mother dyed when he was about 7 Yrs. old and his father dyed about two Yrs. ago and that he this Examinant never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year paid parish

Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 19th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Jno. Hubbard
