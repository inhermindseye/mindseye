Westmr. to wit William Gyblett one of the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster on behalf of Mary Bedward an Infant aged about fourteen Years upon Oath says that the said Mary Bedward was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish by Indenture bearing Date the 16th. day of October 1770 to Margaret Lebatt of the Parish of of Kensington in the County of Middlesex Widow untill the said Mary Bedward shod. Accomplish her full Age of Twenty One Years or Day of Marriage according to the Statute in that Case made and provided as by the said Indentures of Apprenticeship relation being thereunto had may more fully Appear And further says that the said Mary Bedward never was Assigned to any or person nor ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn this 4th. day of April 1771 before Us} Tho Lane R. Butler

Willm. Gyblett
