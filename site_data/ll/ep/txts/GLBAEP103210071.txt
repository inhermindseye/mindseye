Middlesex ss}  John Thompson maketh oath that his late Parents died legally Settled in the Parish of St Luke in the County of Middx & in which parish this Deponent was born & that he this Deponent never was bound An Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor ever did any act to gain a Settle in his own right

Sworn this. 8. day of March 1785 before  Robt Smith

The Mark of John Thompson
