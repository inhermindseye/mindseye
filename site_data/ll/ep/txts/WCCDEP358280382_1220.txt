St. Clement Danes May 11th. 1792 .

Middx & Westminster } Elizabeth Robinson  Singlewoman aged 25 years and upwards Upon Oath saith That she never was Married That she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Snookes a Grocer in Butcher the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about two years at the Annual Wages of 7 Guineas That She quitted That Service about 5 years ago & or upwards And hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement And This Deponent Upon her Voluntary Oath saith That She is now Pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes And That Mr. Price who is or late in Service as a Gentlemans's Valet, and frequently Uses the French Arms in York Street by St James's Square and the Star & Garter in Palace Yard

Westminster in the said Liberty & County is the only real and true father of such Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant, he having had Carnal kno knowledge with of this Deponent's Body sometime in or about the Month of November last and at Several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the same Child or Children thereon And that he and no one else is the real and true father thereof

Sworn the 11th day of May 1792 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Robinson 's Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins

Thick set Man about 5 Foot 3, Dark Eyes & Complexion Light Brown Hair Dressed with a Club

did live with a Gent at Swinham Green

Mr Robinson is in the House.
