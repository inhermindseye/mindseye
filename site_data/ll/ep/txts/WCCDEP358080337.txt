Middx to wit Susannah Brown Aged about forty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Woodnorth the Two blue Posts in Holbourn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn London for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Twelve Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequt. Settlement And further on her Oath Says she hath one Child living named Rachael Mary aged about 13 years a Bastard born in Fetter Lane in the said Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn London and that her said Daughter was never Bound out an Apprentice lived a hired Yearly Servant or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 26th. day of February 1779 before  Abrm Baley R. Butler

her Susannah Brown Mark
