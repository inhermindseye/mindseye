Westmr.  The Examination of Mary Melvell who upon her Oath saith that abt. Twelve or Thirteen Years since to her now best Remembrance she being then a Wido - & not haveing any Child or Children lived a Yearly hired Servt - with one Mrs- Russel in Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - for the Space of 2 Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds & hath not since she left the sd- Service rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 9th- Day of June 1741 before me}

Mary Melvell
