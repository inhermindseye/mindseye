Middlesex ss. The Voluntary examination of Esther Cowling of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Singlewoman taken on Oath before me Daniel Williams  Esqr . one of His Majesty's Justices of the Peace in & for the said County this Twenty Sixth Day of Septr. 1798 .

Who saith that she is now with Child or Children and that the said Child or Children is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph Without Aldgate And that John Halford Apprentice to Mr . John King of the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate  Leather Cutter is the Father of the said Bastard Child or Children.

Taken & signed the Day & Year above written before  Danl Williams

her Esther Cowling Mark
