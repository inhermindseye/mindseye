Middlesex ss}  Frances Ricebrook the widow of Wm Ricebrook deced maketh oath That divers years ago her said late husband was duly bound Apprentice to & Served the Whole thereof with Roger Silvester of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Waterman Lighterman & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 16 day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Frances Ricebrook
