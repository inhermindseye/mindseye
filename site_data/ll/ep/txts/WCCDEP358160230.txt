Middx  The Examination of Martha Pearce  Singlewoman lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 20th- Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about 5 Years ago she was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Moore of the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the sd. County of Middx  Victualler & served him as an Apprentice for the Space of 3 Years & upwards, and further upon her Oath saith that She hath not since that time rented £10 a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Marth: Pearce
