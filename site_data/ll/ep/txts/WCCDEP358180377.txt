Middx  Margt. Leason aged about 60 Years up Removed from the Parish of St. Mary Le bon by Order dated the 12th Day of Septr. Instt. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mrs. Price at Mr. Masons in Cooks Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the C ounty of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £6 for the Space of 10 Yrs. or thereabts. & hath left her sd. Service abt. 14 Years Says she hath not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 19th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones Margaret Leason
