Westmr.  Richard Bry aged about Thirty five Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes upwards of One year & an half ago and by whom he that no Child new living Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Joseph Morris a Staymaker in Vine Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr and service his said Master under those Indres upwards of five Months when his said Master Broke and run away since which time he never Rented House paid taxes lived a hired Yearly or Covenanted Servant for one Year

Sworn this 23d. day of July 1773 - before}

Richd. Bry
