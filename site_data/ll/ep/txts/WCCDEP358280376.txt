St.. Clement Danes March 24th: 1792 .

Middx & Westminster } (to wit) Elizabeth Kirby aged 23 years or thereabouts Singlewoman Upon Oath saith that she never was Married nor gained any settlement in her own right that her Father she hath been informed and verily believes her Father Thomas Kirby intermarried with this Deponents Mother Sarah Burnhill at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes that her said Father Rented and Resided in an House in Stanhope a Street then called little Stanhope Street but now Newcastle Street in the said parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx & Liberty of Westminster about 18 y
