Middx  Sarah Allen Aged about 28 Years Upon Oath Says she Lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Hammond in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx upwards of Four Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Seven Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Sert for One Year

Sworn ye 9th Day of April 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Allen
