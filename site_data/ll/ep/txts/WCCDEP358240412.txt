Elizth. Griffiths illegitimate Child of Elizth. Graves

St Clement Danes 17th. May 1794

Middx and Westminster }  St James Clerkenwell Middx  (to wit) Elizabeth Greaves aged 25 years and upwards singlewoman Upon Oath saith she was never Married that she this Deponent was delivered of a Female illegitimate Child Named Elizabeth Griffiths on or about the twenty second day of June 1791 at the House of Mr. Rowley let in Tenements situate in Castle Court  Castle Street in the parish of St James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex

Sworn the 17th day of May 1794 before us } M Addington Edw: Read

Elizth Greaves 's Mark

Witness B Newman
