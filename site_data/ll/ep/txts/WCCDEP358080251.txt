Middlesex to wit Mary Middleton Aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Robert Middleton who died in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes about five years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living namely Eleanor Aged about Six years Says that since her said Husband Death she never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one John Daney a Chairmaker is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of November last but the particular day she does not now remember and at Sevl. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Daney and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of June 1778 before.  Abrm. Baley

her Mary Middleton Mark
