Middx  Eliza. Bowman  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Williams at the Ben Johnsons Head in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of 3 Years at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds & hath left her said Service about 2 Years since which time she hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Chid or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to Maintain the same wh. borne & that one James Airey  the Younger did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body she at such time liveing a Servt. with Mr Braya Victualer near St. Clement Church Yard in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes where the sd. Jas. Airey became acquainted with her and had

Carnal knowledge of her Body on the 24th. Day of May last being Whitsan tuesday in a Hackney Coach at which time he gott her with Child of the sd. Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. James Airey  the Younger & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworn 13th. Octr. 1743 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Eliz Bowman
