Middx The Examination of Wm. Ibbes Aged abt. 57 Years Passed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx Taken the 19th. Day of Octr. 1739 Who upon his Oath saith that about 30 Years ago he lived in & Rented a House in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in ye. same County of the the Yearly Rent of £20 & lived in the same for the space of One Year & upwards And further saith yt. after he removed from sd. House Mr. Rich let this Examint. have one of the Houses he had Purchased in Bow Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden (to Pull down in ordr. to build the new. Play house) to live in wch was as this Examint. beleives of the Yearly Value of £30 & upwds & this Examint. lived in the same for the space of two Years That he hath not since yt. time Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife & now being Poor & Old is not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Willm Ibbes
