Westmr.  Ann Leppington on Behalf of Willm Poole upon Oath saith the said Willm Poole has Informed her he lived a Yearly Servant with the Right Honble the Earth of Darnley at the Yearly wages of five pounds for the Space of One Year and upwards being at that time a Widower and without Child or Childn. and lag the last 2 months of his sd. Service at the horse & Groom in Stratton Strt. St. Geo. Hanover Small and that he has not since he quitted the sd. Earls Service rented ten pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place and this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she never was married and that upwds. of three Years ago she was delivered of a male bastard Child in the house of Mr. Harwin in New Inn Passage in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty

of Westminster which was baptized John and that the forementioned Willm. Poole did beget the said male bastard Child on her body they having lived together for several Years past as man and wife and that she sd. Willm. Poole and no man also is the true and real father of the Male bastd. Child of which she was delivered as aforesd.

Sworn Octr. 28th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Ann Leppington
