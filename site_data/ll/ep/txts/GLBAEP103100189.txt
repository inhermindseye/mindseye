Middlesex ss  Jane Lake the widow of Joseph Lake deced maketh Oath That her said late husband deced duly served his apprenticeship with Richard Coolley a Bedstedmaker in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex And by Means thereof as she is Advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge or belief her Said late husband never rented a house of the Yearly value of Ten Pounds nor ever served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his apprenticeship nor hath she this Depont. ever done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right since her Said husbands Death And that she hath a Child named Joseph Aged about fifteen months the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her Said late husband.

Sworn this day of December 1769 before}
