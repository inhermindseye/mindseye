Westmr. to wit John Kingsley Aged about fifty five Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House served any Apprenticeship or paid any Taxes whatever Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Carter a Hatter on London Bridge in the Parish of Saint Olave Southwark in the County of Surry for Three Years at the Yearly Wages if five pounds besides Diet and Lodings hath quitted his said service about Thirty Seven Years ago hath not Since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 6th. day of May 1772 before} Tho. Lane  Sam Wright

John Kingsley
