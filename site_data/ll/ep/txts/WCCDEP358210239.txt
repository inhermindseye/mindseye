Westmr.  Mary Barker aged abt. 27 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married, And that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Pyott at the Black Lion the Back of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the first Year, and after the Rate of five Pounds for the next Year, quitted said Service about two Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 4th. day of February 1768 , before} Thos: Balack

The Mark of Mary Barker
