Middx  Ann Roffee Aged about 45 Years Upon Oath Says about One Year and half ago she Intermarried with Her Husband  Henry Roffee Aged about Sixty Years in the Parish Church of St. James in ye Liberty of Westminster Says her said Husband  Rented a House in Cathern Wheel Yard in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster aforesaid about Six Years at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds or there abouts and hath quitted said House about Eighteen Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes.

Sworn the 5th Day of October 1763 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Ann Roffee
