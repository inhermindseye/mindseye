Middx & Westmr}  Wm. Barton aged about 67 Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Sarah his late Wife at the Parish Church of St. Helen in the Borough of Abingdon in the County of Berks about 39 Years ago by whom he hath Seven Children (all of whom is now provided for) Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Wm. Carpue a Joyner in Easter Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middlesex & served his sd. Master Six Years & an half or thereabouts when his sd. Master went to sea, Saith that about Six Weeks after this Examinant went to Abingdon in the County of Berks to see his Mother Diana Barton & worked as a Journeyman with one Wm Prayers there for about Two Years Says that this Examinants Mother growing Old & Infirm Applyed to this Examinant to take him in an Assistant & to Carry on the sd. Business of a Carpenter & Joyner (wch. she Carryed on after this Examinants father's Death) this Examinant Accordingly Entered into Articles of Agreemt. to serve & Carry on the sd. Business at the Yearly Wages of Eighteen Pounds & upwards Payable Weekly or as this Examinant shod. think Proper Exclusiveve of board & Ldging & Continued as a Covenanted Servant with his sd Mother upwards of a Year when this Examinant soon afterwards Married to his sd late Wife & after his sd. Intermarriage this Examinant Rented & lived in a House & Shop in the sd Parish of St. Helen upwards for 28 Years at the Yearly Rent of £8 & Paid all Land Tax due for the same Says He

verily belives that the Churchwardens Overseers of the Poor & Ancient Inhabitants of the Parish of St. Helen was so sensible that this Examinant belonged to them that they gave him 20s. the last Christmas as a Poor Almsman belonging to the sd Parish

Sworn this 19th. day of June 1771 before R A

his Wm Barton Mark.
