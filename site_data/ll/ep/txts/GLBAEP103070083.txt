Middx ss  Daniel Hands Maketh oath that his Sister Elizabeth Hands  Singlewoman about Twelve Months ago rented a house of Twenty one Pounds a Year called or known by the Sign of the Black Jack  in Sheffeild Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx and paid all Parochial taxes in the said Parish and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that his sd. Sister has not Since rented any other house Nor been a hired Servant by ye year nor to this Deponts. knowledge or belief hath she done any other Act whereby to Acquire any other Settlemt. Nor hath she ever been Married to this Deponts. knowledge or belief

Sworn this 23d day of May 1753 before us  Richd: Ricards  Thos: Quarrill

Danl Hands
