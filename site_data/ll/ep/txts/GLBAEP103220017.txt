Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Hoskins of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Robert Smith  Esqr  one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County this Seventh day of January One thousand Seven hundred & eighty Six who Saith that She is now with Child & that the Said Child is likely to be born a bastard and to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County And that William Woodbridge of New Street in Old Street Middx  Cooper is the Father of the Said Child.

Taken and Signed the day and Year above Written before me  Robt Smith

The Mark of Mary Hoskins
