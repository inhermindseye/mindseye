Middx.  Ann Nisby  Widow aged abt. Thirty six Years upon Oath says that being a Widow & without Child or Children She lived a hired Yearly Servt. for Two Years with Mr. Hollyer in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx , at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds four Shillings & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. one Year, since which time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year or Intermarried with any Person Whatsoever

Sworn the day of 175 before}
