Middlesex ss}  Margaret Pridham  Widow of William Pridham deced maketh oath that her said late husband heretofore rented a house of the yearly rent of twenty five pounds in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same & deced in such house legally Settled in the said Parish that she this Deponent after her said husbands death was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mrs Uppom of James Street in the Parish of St Paul Covent Gardne in the sd County Robemaker at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of Eleven Months & no more & received only eleven Months Wages She this Depont being obliged by reason of Sickness to leave Such her Service & did not afterwards return to her sd Mistress That She this Depont Since her said husbands death has not lived in any one place for the Space of a year or been Married or done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her sd. husband.

Sworn this 17. day of Janry 1785 before R Smith

The Mark of Margaret Pridham .
