Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster to wit Edward Burbridge one of the Beadle  of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upon his Oath saith that he hath been informed and which Information he believe to be true that Sarah Hadd a and in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid in the Daughter of Thomas and Sarah Hadd of the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex and never gained any legal Settlement in her own right ever been married.

Sworn this 13th. Octr. 1787 before D Walker Rapt: Clarke

Edwd. Burbidge
