Middx  Ann Thorinton aged abt. forty six years Upon Oath Saith She is the Wido. of Edmd. Thorinton and that her sd. Husband has been Dead abt. Sixteen Years to her now best Remembrance Says her sd. Husband Edmd. Thorinton before his death rented a House by Popeshead Court near Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds or thereabts. Says She hath not since the Death of the sd. Edmond Thorinton rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 29th. May 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Ann Thorinton
