Eliza. Read on Behalf of Roger Vallely an Infant aged abt. ten Months upon Oath Saith the sd. Roger Vallely is the Son of Terence Vallely and Bridget his Wife who were both Irish People & never gained a Settlement in England to this Examint. Knowledge And that the sd. Infant was born at the Plough Alehouse in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx

Sworn Feby. 24th. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

Elizabeth Read

Lodges at the hole in the Wale Vear Strt.
