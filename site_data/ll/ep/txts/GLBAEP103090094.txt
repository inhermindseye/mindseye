Middlesex ss  Grace Buckman the Widow of John Buckman deced Maketh Oath that her said late husband deced was an Irishman by birth And this Depont. doth not know nor can in any Wife discover where her said husband or his Parents were last legally settled And this Depont farther Saith that her Father John Aldridge who was by Trade a Plumber divers Years ago rented a house of Forty five Pounds a Year in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Sd. County of Middlesex & Continued in the Occupation of such house for several years and died therein And this Deponent farther Saith that she lived in the said house with her sd. late father as Part of his family & was Married from thence to her said late husband And that She never did any Act to gain a settlemt. in her own right Save What She might Acquire by her said Marriage.

Sworn this 8th. day of February 1768 before  Chris: Scott R Darling

Grace Buckman
