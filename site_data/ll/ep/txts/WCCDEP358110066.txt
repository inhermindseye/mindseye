Westmr.  Rebecca Roberts upon Oath Saith she never was married and that upwards of forty Years ago she Rented a house of Sixteen Pounds by the Year in Doddington Street near Brooks market in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holbn. above Barrs Middx and lived therein upwards of four months and has not since she quitted the sd. house Rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn 14th. July 1752 before W Fielding

Reb Roberts
