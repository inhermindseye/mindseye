Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Milne aged about 32 years upon his Oath saith that he served his Time as an Apprentice with Mr. Hopwood a Taylor in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that after was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Tenement on Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or ever lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 5th. day of Decr. 1788 R Butler

Jas. Milne
