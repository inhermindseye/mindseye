Middx  Mary Foreman aged upwds. of 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Norman a Ropemaker at Yalden in Kent for one year at the yearly Wages of £3 and quitted her sd. Service about 3 weeks ago

Sworn Novr. 21st 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Foreman
