Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Davis aged about Forty years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Wood in Herberts Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that She hath quitted said Service upwards of Eighteen Months since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of July 1799 before me  Saml Edwards Menter

H Day

her Susannah Davis mark
