Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Powell aged about Thirty Three years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Phelps Attorney at Law in Lyons Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Two years at the yearly Wages of four pounds four Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of fourteen years ago since which time she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for One year.

Sworn this 31st. day of January 1776 before M Addington

her Elizth. Powell Mark
