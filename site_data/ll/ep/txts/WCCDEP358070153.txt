Westminster to wit Mary Worral Aged about Forty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rent House in her own right Says that this Examinant lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Davis a Pewterer in the Parish of Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds since which time hath sd her said Service upwards of Fifteen Years ago & hath not Rented House in her own right Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 19th. day of Decr. 1773 before  Sampn Wright W Kelynge

Mary Worral
