Middlesex ss  Catherine Murrell  Singlewoman maketh oath that she was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Richard Sherlock a Cordwainer in Rosemary Lane in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service under such hiring for the Space of one year & nine months That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent settlemt

Sworn this 24th day of Septemr 1785 before R Davies

Cathrn Murrell
