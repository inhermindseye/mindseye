Saint Clement Danes 20th. April 1793.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Luke Middx to wit Martha Roberts  Widow aged Sixty six years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She was Married to John Roberts at the Fleet about forty four two years ago that her said Husband previous thereto her said House (while he was single and unmarried) lived as an hired yearly Servant with his Brother Mr. Roberts in Featherston Street in the parish of Saint. Luke in the County of Middlesex for a year or upwards at the (as she hath been informed and believes) but what Wages he agreed for or received for such Service this Deponent knows nor And that he quitted the same for sometime before their Marriage and never after acquired any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent's know legal or belief that she has been informed & believes he Died at New York in North America about thirty seven years ago And she hath nor Since been Married or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own Right

Sworn the 20th. day of April 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Martha Roberts 's Mark
