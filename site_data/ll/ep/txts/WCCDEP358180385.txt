Middx  Mary Ann George on Behalfe of Henry Cutler Wainwright an Infant aged about 8 Yrs. upon her Oath saith that Cutler Wainwright the Father of the sd. Infant lived in & rented a house in James Street in the Parish of St. Martin in ye Feilds in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of £20. & upwds. for the Space of one Year or thereabouts & hath left the same upwds. of 15 Yrs. as she hath been informed & believes says the sd. Cutler Wainwright hath not since he left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworne Septr. 30th. 1745 before Wm: Jones

Mary Ann George
