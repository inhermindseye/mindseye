Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Robinson aged about 17 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That about on the 30th. day of September 1785 she was bound an Apprentice to a Mrs. Drake in Norfolk Street in the Strand by the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Seven years that after she had served about Three years of her said Apprenticeship her said Mistress took a Lodging at Islington that she desired this Deponent to go to live with a Mr. Smith a Bookseller in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster where she lived for Three Months and that since she quitted said Mr. Smith's she has not lived with her said Mistress for Forty days

Sworn this 5th. day of Augt. 1789  Philip Dyot D Walker

her Elizabeth Robinson Mark
