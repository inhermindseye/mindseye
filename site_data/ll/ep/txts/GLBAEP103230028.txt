Middlesex ss  Ann Norris the wife of Thomas Hush Norris maketh oath That her said husband who is now disordered in his mind heretofore rented the Cock Alehouse in Cock alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said county and during such his residence therein he was charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That her sd husband has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes & done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere and that she was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 14. day of March 1787 before}

The Mark of Ann Norris
