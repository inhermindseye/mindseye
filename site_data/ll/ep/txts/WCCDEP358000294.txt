St. Clement Danes. May 20th: 1786 .

Elizabeth Harper aged 49. or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she intermarried with Daniel Harper her late Husband at the Parish Church of Kingston upon Thames about Thirty four Years ago, That before their Intermarriage and whilst her said Husband was a single man and unmarried not having Child or Children, her said Husband lived as an hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Tash of Suffolk  Lane in the Parish of Allhallows the Less - in the City of London Wine Merchant at the Yearly Wages of for upwards of one Years that he did not quit his said place till after their Intermarriage - that he died about twenty two Years ago, and that her said Husband after quitting his said place until the time of his Death did not do, nor hath this Examinant since his Death done, any Act, Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information and belief she may have gained any other Settlement then in the Parish of Allhallows thereof above stated and this Examinant further saith that she is poor and Lame and unable to maintain herself and

being in much Want hath applied to the Overseers of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Relief.

Sworn this 20th May 1786 before M Addington Tho: Gordon

her Elizth Harper Mark
