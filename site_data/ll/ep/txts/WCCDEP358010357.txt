a Martin Connon aged 44 or thereabouts. upon his Oath Saith that he was placed as an Apprentice to Patrick Relieve of Callan in the Kingdom of Ireland who died & he then was turned over to Chas. Blake of Kikenny Beaches Make will whom he Served the last Three years of his Time, And Further Saith the hath been in England about Seven years where he has Worked Piece Work & never has done any Act to gain a Settlement to the bu of his knowledge & belief

Wm. Kitchiner

Martin Connon
