and Liberty of Westmr. to wit}  Mary Lindsey (Wife of John Lindsey now in Scotland) on her Oath saith that she is Aged forty Years and upwards and that she was married to her said Husband at the Fleet  London about 16 years ago That she doth not know any thing of her said Husbands Settlemt. and that at the time of her Intermarriage with him she was a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. John Craven at the Cravens Arms in Southampton Street within the Parish of Saint Pauls Covent Garden in the Liberty aforesaid Vintner in whose Service she Continued for the Space of Three Years or thereabouts Vizt. one year and an half before Marriage and one year and an half afterwards or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds that she has one Child now living by her said Husband namely Catharine of the age of Nine Years and upwards.

Sworn the 11th. day of February 1769 before W Kelynge

The Mark of Mary Lindsey
