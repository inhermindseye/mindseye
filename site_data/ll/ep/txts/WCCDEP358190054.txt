Middx.  Charlton Hill aged as he believes abt. 60 Yrs. upon Oath says he lodges at a common Lodging house  in Drum Alley in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with his Father  Charlton Hill in that part of Drury Lane which is in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year.

Sworn this 9th. Octr. 1760 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Charlton Hill
