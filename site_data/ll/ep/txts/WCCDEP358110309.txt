Westmr.  Margt. Lock upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Sanders Lock a Sidtchman of whose Settlement she ann give no account that she is likewise a Native of Scotland and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes and that her late father Jas. Skirlaugh lived in and rented a house of the yearly Rented of Ten pounds Scots in the parish of Liberton near Edin burgh

Sworn the Day & year above intermarried before}

The Mark of Margt. Lock
