Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Barnett  Singlewoman maketh oath that She can give no Account respecting her Parents Settlement or either of them That She was born in Churchyard Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Barnett
