Westmr.  Mary Slater aged upwards of Fifty Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Peter Slater who has been dead upwards of Ten Years, says Her said husband informed her & which information she verily believes to be true that he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Cerson a Cabinet maker on Clerkenwell Green in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex and served him three Years & a half the first part of his said Apprenticeship & then his said Master died & never was assigned over to any other person nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, Says she has not since her said husbands death done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement, and that she hath a Daughter by her said late husband (to wit) Eleanor aged about Eighteen Years who never was Married or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant.

Sworn the 2d. of Octr. 1766 . Before} Fran. Cary  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Slater
