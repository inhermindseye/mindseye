Middx  Mary Watkins aged about 23 Years upon Oath says She never was Married and that her Father Thomas Watkins deceased lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hugh Powell a Taylor the corner of Portugal Street near Lincolns Inn Back Gate in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for three years at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds Meat Drink & Lodging as her said Father informed her and as She verily believes Says She never was an Apprentice rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the Day of 1756. before}
