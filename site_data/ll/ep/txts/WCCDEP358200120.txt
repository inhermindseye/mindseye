Westmr.  Sarah Cook Aged about Twenty Nine Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Rice Pritchard in Bond Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the County of Middx upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted said Service about Six Years & hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further Sayth that after she Quitted her said Service she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Swallow Street in the said Parish of St. James in ye Liberty of Westmr.

Sworn the Day of July 1764 Before}
