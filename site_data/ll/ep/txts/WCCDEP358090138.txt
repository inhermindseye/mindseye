Middx  Mary Banger Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Bathgate a Farrier in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service Upwards of Two Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 24th. day of April Instant She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish which hath since been baptized by the Name of James and is now Chargeable to the said Parish and likely to Continue so She being poor and Unable to maintain the same And further says that One James Mayburn a Cutler who lodges at the Red Lyon in Saint Martins Court Saint Martins Lane in the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of August but the particular day She does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which She was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said James Mayburn and no other person did beget the same and that he is thereas and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 23rd. day of May 1780 before  Abrm. Baley

her Mary Ranger Mark
