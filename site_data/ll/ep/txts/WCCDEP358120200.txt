Middx  Eleanr. Lambert upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Wm. Lambert who was an Irishman & never gained a Settlemt. in England & that she this Examinant was born at Prescort in Lancashire of which parish her father was Rector & that she never rented ten pounds by the year pd. Parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Septr. 28th. 1754 before}
