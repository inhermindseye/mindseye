Middlesex ss}  Henry Howard maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to And Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Mr John Perry a Watch Engraver in Hoxton in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the Said County That he never rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gains a Settlement Subsequent to his Apprenticeship

Sworn this. 22. day of Novemr 1787 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

Heny Howard
