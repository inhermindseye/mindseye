Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Margarett Sopp aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly servant with one John Bolt the Corner of Berners Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted her said Service upwds. of three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of June 1787  Thos. Gordons  Sampn Wright

Margaret Sopp
