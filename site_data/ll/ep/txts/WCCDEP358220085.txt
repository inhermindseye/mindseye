Westmr.  Ann Crawley Aged about Sixty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Patrick Crawley who has been dead about fourteen years says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Lewis who kept a Poor Shop in Shug Lane in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of Two years at the yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Seven Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly servant for one year

Sworn this 15th. day of Novr. 1769 before us} Barth: Hammond  Tho Fellows

Ann Crawley
