Middx.  Mary Wilson aged abt. 62 Years upon Oath says she never was married was an Apprentice Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or was a Yearly Servant , says her father Edward Wilson Rented a House of Sixteen Pounds a Year in Plumbtree Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx. for upwards of twenty Years & there dyed

Sworn the 14th day of March 1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Wilson
