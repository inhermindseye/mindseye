St Clement Danes  Febry. 2d. 1792.

Middlesex & Westminster }  Vide Settlemt. page 263  Mr Richardson Overseer Dobbins Beadle} (to wit) Elizth. Fynmore  Singlewoman Aged 30 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that on the 1st. day of January now last past she was delivered of Female Bastard Child at the Workhouse belonging to this parish which hath Since been Christened by the Name of Sarah Fynmore at this parish Church and which now is & from the time of the Birth hath been chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes And this Deponent further upon her Oath Saith that Thomas Master 's (who lately lived as Footman to Mr Lee in Tooks Court Cursitor Street in the said parish of St County Clement Danes of Middlesex and Absconded from his said Master's Service on the 22d day of June last but where he then went or now Resides this Deponent doth not know nor hath ever heard) is the only Real and true father of the said Child of which she was So delivered as aforesaid he and no one else having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponent's Body and begotten the same Child thereon

Sworn the 2d. day of Febry. 1792 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Fynmore
