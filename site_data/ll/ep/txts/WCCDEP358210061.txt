Westmr.  William Robinson aged about 33 Years Upon Oath says he served three Years & upwards the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Vincent a baker in New Street Square , Shoe Lane in the Parish of St. Bridget alias St. Bride 's London and never rented House paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 17th, day of July 1766 , before} Thos: Balack  Paul Vaillant

William Robinson
