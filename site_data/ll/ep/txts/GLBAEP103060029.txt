Middx  Phebe the wife of Angle Darling Maketh Oath and Saith that her husband is a Sweedish Man and never gained Any Settlemt. in this Kingdom and is gone to Sea and does not know whether he be dead or living And that her Father Samuel Rutter above twenty years ago Rented A house in St. Anns Lane in the parish of Saint Ann Aldersgate in the Liberty of the City of London and lived therein about a year and Paid above ten pounds a year for the Same And that She hath one Child named Sarah Dare A Bastard by one Edward Dare which was born in the parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry .

Sworn this 11th. Decr. 1744 before Robert Dennett

Phebe Dareling
