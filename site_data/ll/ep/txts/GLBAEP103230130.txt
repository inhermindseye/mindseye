Middlesex ss  Catherine Jones  widow of David Jones deced maketh oath that lately & Since her said late husbands death She this Deponent lived in and rented a house in Coopers Court in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County at the neat yearly rent of Ten Pounds and resided thereon for the Space of Six Months and has not Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or been married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th day of April 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of  Catherine Jones
