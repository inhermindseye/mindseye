St Clement Danes March 22d. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  parish of Beappow near Bridport Co. Dorset (to wit) Margaret Homan aged forty two years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband Henry Homan at the parish Church of Bridport in the County of Dorset about thirteen years ago that since their sa Marriage her said Husband Rented and resided in an House and rented a shop therewith in the parish of Brappow near Bridport in the County of Dorset for three years & upwards at the Annual Rent together of £10 that they quitted that House and shop about four years ago and he hath never since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that she hath two Children by her said Husband Namely Harriet aged about six years and samuel aged about two years now with her That her said Husband left her and the Children unprovided for on Thursday the fourteenth instant and knows not at where he is save as she hath informed and believes he is inlisted and gone for an East India Soldier and that she is unable to maintain herself and Children without assistance

Sworn the 22d. day of March 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Margt. Homan 's Mark
