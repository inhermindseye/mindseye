Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Charlotte Lander aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that about Thirteen years ago she Intermarried with William Lander her late Husband (who has been dead about Thirteen Months) at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes  about by whom she hath two Children living Vizt. Wm. Henry aged about Twelve years unprovided for and Eleanor aged about Eight years provided for That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Water Lane  fleet Street in the Precinct of the White fryars in the City of London for about Six Months at the yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds That after he quitted said house which is about Eight years ago he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th- day of  Novr: 1795 N Bond W Kinnard

her Charlotte Lander mark

Witness Richd Robbins
