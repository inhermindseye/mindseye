Middlesex ss}  Samuel Smith maketh oath That Several Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Yearly Wages with one Mr. Davis in Woods Close in the parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex & Continued in Such Service for the space of Several years & this Deponent by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of April 1775 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Samuel Smith
