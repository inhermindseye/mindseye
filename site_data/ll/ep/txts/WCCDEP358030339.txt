Westmr. 

Mary Miller lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. upon Oath saith she was never married Says her father Garret Miller lived in and rented a house in Newtoners Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds  in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twenty four pounds for several Years and never after he quitted sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn 10th. Febry 1746 before}
