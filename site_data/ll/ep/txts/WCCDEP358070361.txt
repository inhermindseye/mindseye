Middlesex Westminster to wit Constantine Conner Aged about Thirty Nine Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to his late Wife  Catherine at the Parish Church of St. Mary White Chapel about Ten Years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says that before his Intermarriage with his said late Wife he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Darby at the Sign of the Plough now Called by the Sign of the Wheatsheaf in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for One Year Three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds hath quitted his said Service about Twelve Years ago and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement to his Knowledge or beleif.

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1776 before J Kings

Constantine Conner
