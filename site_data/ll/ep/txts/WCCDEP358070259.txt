Middx ss  Mary Cole Aged upwards of Seventy one Years on her Oath Says She is the Widow of William Cole who has been dead about Twenty three Years and by Whom she hath no Children now Living says that after her said husbands death she rented and lived in a house in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes lb/>in the liberty of Westminster upwards of Three Years at the Yearly rent of forty Pounds and Paid all Taxes due for the same till the time that she was burnt thereout which is upwards of thirteen Years ago since which time she hath never rented house paid Taxes lived a hired Yearly Servant or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 6th day- of May 1775 before} Mary Cole  Tho. Lane

Mary Cole
