Middlesex and Westminster }  St George Martyr South Wark (to wit) Hannah Pattifor Wife of John Pattifor aged 36 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband John Pattifor at the parish Church of St. Ann Westminster (by Baners) on  or about the  Ninth day of June in the year of our Lord 1787 that since their said Marriage her said Husband rented and resided in an House in Mansfield place in St George's Fields in the parish of St George the Martyr in the Borough of Southwark in this County of Surry for 3 Months and upwards at the Annual Rent of Seventeen pounds that they quitted that House about three Months ago and he hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief And this Deponent further with that she hath three Children by her

said Husband Namely Sarah aged 4 years & upwards Louisa aged three years on or about the 21st. day of December last and Harriet aged fifteen Months & upwards And that her said Husband left her on or about the 12th day of November last with intention to go abroad and she hath not since heard form him And this Deponent saith that she is now pregnant distressed and unable to maintain herself without assistance

Sworn the 16th. day of January 1793 before me} Wm. Kitchiner D Walker

Hannah Pattifor

(Removed by Richd Dobbins  Beadle  16th. Janry. 1793 )
