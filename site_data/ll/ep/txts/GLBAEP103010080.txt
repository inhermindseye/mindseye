Middlesex ss.  Ann Fleming  Singlewoman Maketh Oath that about Three Years ago she was bound Apprentice by Indenture by the Church Wardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Mary White Chapel to Patrick Dempsey of Cartwright Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and that she served her said Master there under the said Indenture about Two Years when he deserted her and went over to Ireland as she has been informed & has not been in England again to her knowledge or belief, that she never was turned over to any other Person to serve the remainder of the Term to come in the said Indenture nor has she served any Person under the said Indenture with her said Masters Consent or done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of Novr. 1791 before}
