Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Goodall Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Thos. Latter a Staymaker in the Precinct of the Savoy in the Dutchy Liberty of Lancaster upwards of Three Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Eight Months ago & hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Oath Says that on the 13th. day of Decr. last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Berry a Pawnbroker in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been baptized Elizabeth and that John Hooton late a Partner with Mr. Geeves a Wine Merchant near Craven buildings in the Strand is the true and real Father of the said Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid.

Sworn this 18th. day of January 1771 before}  Petr Planck

her Elizth Goodall mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
