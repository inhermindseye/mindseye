Middx and Westmr}   Sarah Cartwright aged about 22 years upon Oath says she never was Married Says she is now a hired Yearly Servant to Mr. Durham Book Seller near Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and has lived with him as such upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of £6 And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child on the Thirtieth day of September 1761 at Mr. Stavelys a Perukemaker in Wych Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Susanna and that the same is yet living and likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same And that one Obadiah Wright  Hosier in High Holborn did beget such Female Bastard Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of

her Body in the Month of December 1760 at the House of Mrs. Bowers in Lambs Conduit Passage near Red Lyon Square and at several other times since at one of which times he did beget on her Body the said Female Bastard Child.

Sworn the 9th. day of March 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Cartwright
