Middx  Jane Bradford Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Bradford who died Three Years ago and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Christ Church Southwark in the County of Surry about Seven Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Messor. Wondler Medward Glass Cutters in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any or subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath Says that some time in the Month of January 1781 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of One John Hunter a Gardiner  facing the Church of St. Matthew Betnall Green which hath since been baptized by the of Jane and is now Chargable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being passed thereon with her Child for Nurture And further says that one James Houseman a weaver who his time with one Mr. Blyth a Weaver in Virgina Row in the afsd. Parish of St. Matthew Bethall Green is the Father they he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body for near Two Years last Past at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Hunter & no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 2d day of March 1782 before R Butler

her Jane Bradford Mark
