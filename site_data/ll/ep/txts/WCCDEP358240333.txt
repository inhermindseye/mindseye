St Clement Danes October 11th.. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St James Clerkenwell  One Child  (to wit) Elizabeth Cooke aged about Thirty years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to John Cooke on the or about the seventeenth day of August 1789 at the parish Church of St Martin in the Fields (by Banns) that previous thereto She is informed and believes that previous thereto her said Husband served was bound an Apprentice under Indentures to Mr Thomas Clare on or about the first day of October 1776 and served his said Master has as such Apprentice at his House on Clerkenwell Green in the parish of St James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex until in or about the Month of March in the year 1787 and lodged with his said Master there during such Service that he was discharged in or about the year 1787 and saith that she hath not done any Act Matter or thing Whatsoever since whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Examinant further saith that She hath one Child by her said Husband now with her Namely Hannah aged three years two Months and upwards, that her said Husband hath deseret deserted them and she believes is gone to Sea and that She

is distressed and unable to maintain herself and Child without assistance

Sworn the 11th October 1793 before us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Elizth Cooke 's Mark

Witness Garrett
