Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Edward Burbidge One of the Beadle the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Thos. Spencer an Infant Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he believes to be true that Wm. Spe the Father of the said Thomas Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Thos. Brignall a Butcher in Aldersgate Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldersgate in the City of London and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Novr. 1786 D Walker Rups: Clarke

Edwd Burbidge
