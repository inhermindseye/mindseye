Middlesex to wit Ann Pugh Aged about Sixty Seven Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of William Pugh who died about Seven Years ago and with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London upwards of Twenty Years ago and further says that her said late Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one John Simpson a running Poulterer in Hog Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx And after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant before Marriage for One Year neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 24th. day of January 1780 before  Jno. Barnfather  Thos Preston

her Ann Pugh Mark
