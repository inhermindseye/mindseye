Middlesex and Westminster}  Swear to St. Clemt. A. to wit Ann Harper Aged about 42 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to John Harper her late husband Deceased at the parish Church of Saint Nicholas in France about 18 years ago and who is been had about Sixteen years by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says that since her said Husband Death she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Smith in Bear yard about 9 years ago in the parish of Saint Clement Danes upwards of three years ago at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six years ago since which time she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 23d. day of Novr. 1784 before me  Wm Kitchiner

Ann Harper
