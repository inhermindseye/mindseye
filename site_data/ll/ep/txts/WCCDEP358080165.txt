Middx & Westmr-}  Elizth- Webb aged about 26 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says her Father Willm- Webb Rented and lived in a House in Milford Lane in the parish of St- Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx for sevl- Years at the Yearly Rent of £10 and quitted same in the Year 1771 since which time he hath gained no other subseqt- Settlemt- neither hath this Examinant since her said Father quitted the same and further says that she hath three Children now living all of whom were begotten by one Jno- Davis a Bargeman Vizt- John aged upwards of seven Years born a Bastard in the Parish of Saint Lukes Chelsea in sd- County of Middx Willm- aged five Years and four Months Rebecca aged two Years and eleven Months both born Bastards in the parish of Richmond in the County of Surry that none of her said Children hath gained any other Settlement than by Birth

Sworn this 7th- Day of August before me  Jno- Barnfather  James Croft

Eliz Webb 2s.
