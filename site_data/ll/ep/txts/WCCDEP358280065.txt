Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Palmer aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that about 5 months ago she Intermarrd. with Joseph Palmer her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone that a before her Intermarriage to the said Joseph Palmer she lived and Cohabited with one James Dyer a Bricklayer in Star Court Butcher Row by whom she had sevl. illegitimate Children one of which are living Vizt. Edward Manners born on the H born at the House of a Mr. Southed in Star Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes on the 22d. Novr. 1779 he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body for sevl. years and begotten the same thereon and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 6th. day of Febry. 1787 } R Butler

her Ann Palmer mark

to be found at the sign of the red Lyon and Speed Eage Yard White Chapel Husband a Farrier
