Saint Clement Danes.

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Gilson aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year at the yearly Wages of £6. that since she quitted Wm. Gibbons she has not lived any where as a hired yearly servant or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this day of August 1786}
