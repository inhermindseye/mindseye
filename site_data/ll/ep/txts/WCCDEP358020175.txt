Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Earnest aged about 54 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Shedall a Taylor in Chair Court Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said service about Six years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for One year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

House renting £25 a year or there abts married at Dantzick to John Earnest

Sworn this 28th. day of Novr. 1789 R Butler

her Elizth. Earnest Mark
