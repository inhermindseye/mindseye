Middx  Thos. Holford upon Oath saith he has Child viz Mary aged near 9 Yrs. which Child was born of the body of Mary Turner  Widow decd. at the White Lyon in Clare market  in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes and this Examinant further upon his Oath saith he Served upwards of three Years of his Apprentiship with Rd. Eaton a Weaver in Brick Lane in the Parish of Christchurch in the County of Middx and then this Examinants sd master broke and he never was assigned over nor ever Rented ten pounds by the Year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a Yearly Servt. for one year except wth. one Mr Stull a Sadler near Naked boy Court in the Strand wth. whom he lived abt. 2 Yrs. but was then a widower and had a Son named Rt . who was then abt. 14 Yrs of age and had never been an apprentice or yearly Servants

Sworn Feby. 7th 1755  Fran: Bedwell

Thos Holford
