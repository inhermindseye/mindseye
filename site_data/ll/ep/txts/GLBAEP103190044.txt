Middlesex ss}  Margaret Brown the wife of John Brown maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Sultan That her said husband has informed her & which she believes to be true That he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Thomas Wasty in the Parish of Whitney in the County of Oxford & has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief & that she was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 23 day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Margaret Brown
