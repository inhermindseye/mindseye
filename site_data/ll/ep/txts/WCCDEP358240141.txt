(Proper settlement & Removed wrong tured before St. Clement Danes August 23d. 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St Ann & Agnes Aldersgate London  (to wit) Ann Higgins  Singlewoman Aged 23 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she has been informed and Verily believes that her Mother Father Robert Higgins Married to this Deponent's Mother Susanna at the Fleet Prison London according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of England above Forty Years ago The her said Father for Many Years before his Decease Rented and Resided in an House in Angel Street in the parish of Saint Ann and Agnes Aldersgate within Aldersgate London at the Annl. Rent of £12 and Died there about 18 Years ago That after the Decease of her said Father, her said Mother Rented and Resided in the same House for several Years at the said Yearly Rent of £12 and left it about ten Years ago & hath not since been Married or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information or belief. And this Deponent farther saith that her said Mother hath informed her which she verily believes to be true that she now Receives an Allowance from the said parish of St Ann and Agnes as a parishioner thereof And Lastly she this Deponent hath never been legally bound an Apprentice lived under any Yearly hiring for one Year

together he Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year Value paid parish Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement in her own Right, And that she is now pregnant and unable to support herself without Assistance

Sworn the 23 day of August 1792 before Us } W: Bleamire Rupt: Clarke

Ann Higgins

This Woman Examined twice before but the parish was Misnamed She was now moved & reced
