Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Thomas Smith aged about 40 years upon his Oath saith that about 14 years ago he Intermarried with Esther his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint James Westmr. that his said Wife has been dead about Fourteen days that he has Three Children living vizt. Mary aged about Ten years Thomas Amos aged about Eight years and Richard Lewis aged about Four years. That he this Examinant Rented a House and Land in the Parish of the Holy Trinity in the City of Coventry for about Six years at the Yearly Rent of Eighty Pounds and upwards the he hath quitted said Premises about Five Months since which time he has not Rented any Place of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of Janry. 1789 }
