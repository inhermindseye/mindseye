Middx & Westmr-}  Elizth- Mark aged about 37 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs- Thorpe a Baker in Blackmore Street in the Psh of St- Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr - upwards of fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of £3 hath quitted her said Service upwds- of seven Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th- Day of Novr- 1777 before me R Butler

her Elizth- Mark Mark
