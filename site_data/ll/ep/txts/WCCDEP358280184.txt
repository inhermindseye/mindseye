Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Gale aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she was bound Apprentice to a Mr. Walker in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Seven years that she served upwards of Five years of her said Time that then this Examts. Master failed that this Examinant then went into Service but never lived Twelve Months And further this Examt. upon her Oath saith that on the first day of August now last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at a Licensed House in Mountague Close Borough which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Ann that one William Underwood who works with one Mr. Footdall in Old Bilton Street  Long Acre  Glass Polisher is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said William Underwood is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Octr. 1788 } R Butler

her Sarah Gale Mark

William Underwood is about 5 foot high fair Complexion wears dark Hair tyed behind in a Club
