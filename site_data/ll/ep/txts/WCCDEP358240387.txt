St.. Clement Danes  3d Febry. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  The Order Quashed Girl being young at that time & not emancipated}  (to wit) Ann Crossby  Singlewoman aged about twenty three years Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She h She hath not lived as an hired yearly Servant for a year together or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own Right that she is informed and verily believes she is the legitimate Daughter of Anthony and Sarah Crossby both deceased that her said Father lived an an hired yearly Servant in the capacity of Housekeeper at the Hackney Coach Office and for about the last six years of such his Service he lodged where the Office was kept in Queen Street  Lincolns Inn fInn Fields in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the said County of Middlesex & that he died there having survived his Wife (this Deponent's Mother) and not obtained Any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her (this Deponent's) knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 3d day of Febry.. 1794 before us Rich.. Ford B: Kennett

Ann Crossby

N. B This Order Appealed agst & Quashad May/94
