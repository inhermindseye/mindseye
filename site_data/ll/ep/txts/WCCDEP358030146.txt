Middx  Mary Dailey  Wife of Edwd. Dailey now a Soldier in Coll Frazers Regimt. of Marinas upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Edward Dailey about five Years since at the Fleet London Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with a Shoemaker in the Parish of St. Brides in the City of Chaster in the County of Chaster & never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examinant has been informed & beleives

Sworn May 14th. 1746 before Wm: Jones

Mary Daily
