Middx.  Elizth: Cross aged abt. 54 Years upon Oath says she never was Married; says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Theophilus Prosser a Tinman in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two years at the yearly Wages of three pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. Thirty Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th. day of August 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Cross
