Westmr.  Jane Turbutt Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Benjamin Robt. Turbutt who has been Dead about Five Years as she has been Credibly Informed and verily believes to be true and Saith whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about Eleven Years ago and by whom she has Two Children now living Viz Benjamin Aged about Eight Years and Jane Aged about Seven years now provided for at the City of Winchester Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Benjamin Turbutt a Stationer in Vere Street  Clare Markett as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 20th. day of October 1772 before us} Tho: Kynaston

Jane Turbutt
