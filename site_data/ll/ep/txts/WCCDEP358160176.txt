Middx  The Examination of Amey Beezley  Spinster aged abt. 50 years Taken the 2d. Octr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that she was never Marryed & that upwds. of 2 years ago she lived as a yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Styles in Knights Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Taylor for the Space of 2 years & upwards at the yearly Wages of £3-10s, And further upon her Oath saith that she hath not since she left her sd. Master Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife; that she is now Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe.

Sworn the Day & year above mentioned before.} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Amey Beezley
