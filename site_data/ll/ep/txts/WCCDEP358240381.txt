St Clement Danes 24th Janry- 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  (both Catholics)  One Child  St C D  Cornelius Driskall aged about thirty three Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was Married to Ann his Wife at the Chapel or Church of St Catherine in Dublin according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of Rome about 10 several years ago, that he hath one Child by his said Wife named Mary aged about five years which was born at the Feathers Public House in St Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster that this Examinant Rented and Resided in that House for about a year at the annual Rent of £40 £20 & upwards about £30 & was Rated to the poor of the said parish and & paid such Assessment or some part thereof that he quitted that House in or about the year 1788 and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 24th day of January 1794 before us} Wm Kitchiner

Cornelius Driskall 's mark

Witness Jas Talboys
