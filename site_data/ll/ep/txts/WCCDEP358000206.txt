Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Macdonald aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married according by the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England, that she never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever served any Apprenticeship Saith that her Father Patrick Macdonald Rented and lived in a House in new Bedford Cowet in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. at the yearly Rent of £10 and upwds. and paid Taxes due for the same that her Father lived in the sd. House upwds. of Sixteen years that this Ext. has done no Act whereby she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. saith that she has one  Illegitimate Child living vizt. Mary Macdonald aged about 10 Months born in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Martin in the fields And further this Ext. saith that she was married to James Nevill deceased the Father of the said Child at the Chapel in Duke Street by a Roman Catholic Priest according to the Kites and Ceremonies of the Romesh Church

Sworn this 12th. day of Janry 1786 before Mr: Wright Gs Bradshaw

her Elizth Macdonald mark
