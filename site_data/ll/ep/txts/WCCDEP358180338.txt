Middx  Mary Goodman aged about 40 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married And that her Father Thomas Goodman lived in & Rented a house in Witch Alley in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Eight Pounds for the Space of Thirty Years & upwards and was Rated & paid towds. the Releif of the Poor and there dyed says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne May 30th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Goodman
