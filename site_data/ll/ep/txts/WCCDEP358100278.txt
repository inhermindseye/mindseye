Middlesex & Westminster}  passd. to St. Giles's to wit Thomas Smith Aged about 37 Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Margarett his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of Nine years ago and by whom he hath no Child living Says he Rented and lived in a House in Great Earl Street  Seven Dials in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County aforesaid for two years at the yearly. Rent of Thirty Six Guineas Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Seven years ago since which time he never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or otherwise gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Novr. 1784 before us M Addington Thos: Gilbert

Thos Smith
