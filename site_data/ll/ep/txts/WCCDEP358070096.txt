Westmr. to wit Ann Hughes Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House Paid Taxes or was an Apprentice Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one David Price a Green Grocer in Gouge Street near Middlesex Hospital in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for One Year and three Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 8th. day of May 1773 before R. Butler 

her Ann Hughes Mark
