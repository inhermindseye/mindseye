Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Singer aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that She never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Wild in King Street Golden Square in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service in March last since which time She has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of Octr. 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

Ann Singer
