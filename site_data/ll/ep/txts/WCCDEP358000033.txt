Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} Athe & Ricd passd-to St. Mary Le Bond to wit Mary Vincent  Wife of John Vincent of New Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Taylor on behalf of Hugh Ward a Child aged Eighteen Months or thereabout on her Oath saith that Sarah Watts this Examinants Sister who is the Mother of the said Hugh Ward  informed this Examinant that the said Hugh Ward was born in Oxford Road in the Parish of Saint

Sworn day of J M.
