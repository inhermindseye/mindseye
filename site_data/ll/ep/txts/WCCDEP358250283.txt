Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Parker aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of April 1782 she Intermarried with John Parker her present Husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Giles Leonard Shoreditch by whom she hath Six Children living Vizt. Mary Ann aged upwards of 14 years Fredk. aged about 12 years Elizth. Esther aged upwards of Seven years Jno. Wm . aged upwards of five years Sarah Davis aged upwards of Three years and Wm . aged upwards of Twelve Months That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Harford place in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for Five years at the yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds

Sworn this 7th. day of April 1797 before me } J Hunter

Ann Parker
