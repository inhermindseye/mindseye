Middx  Isabella Morrison Aged about 20 Years Upon Oath Says she never Rented House or lived a hired Yearly Servant, Says her Father  Wm. Morrison Served his Apprenticeship with Mr. Chacer Painter in Cockish Lane in the City of Norwitch but the Name of the Parish does not know neither does she know whether her said Father rented House or Paid Taxes Says she has One Sister Named Margaret Aged about Ten Years.

Sworn the 28th Day of April 1763 before}

The Mark of Isabella Morrison
