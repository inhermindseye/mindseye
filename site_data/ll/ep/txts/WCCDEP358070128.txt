Westmr. to wit Hannah Burn Aged about Seventy Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Burn who has been Dead upwards of Eight Years Ago and with whom she Intermarried at St. Mary Church in Dublin about Thirty Years ago Says that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year & an half at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Eight Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Eight Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of September 1773 R. Butler

Mary The Mark of Burn
