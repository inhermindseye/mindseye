Westmr.  Elizabeth Hopkins on behalf of her Son John Hopkins a Lunatick aged about upwards of Twenty Years upon Oath says the said John Hopkins was bound Apprentice by Indenture dated the 12th, day of Novr. 1760, to Henry Taylor of Tottenham Court Road in the County of Middlesex  Watch case maker , and that on the 29th, day of June 1764, the said Apprentice was turned over to Henry Billinghurst of Aldersgate Street in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldersgate in the City of London Watch case maker as appears by an Indorsement on the back of the said Indenture of Apprenticeship; and served the said Mr. Billinghurst till about three Months ago, and never was assigned over to any Person afterwards,

Sworn the 4th, day of July 1767 , before.  Fran Cary W Kelynge

Elez Hopkins
