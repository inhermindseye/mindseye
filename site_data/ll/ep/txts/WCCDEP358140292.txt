Middx.  Mercy Wood aged abt. 67 Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Wm. Wood who has been dead about Ten Years to whom she was Married about Twenty Years ago at the fleet London says her said husband Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that he served four Years and upwards the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Boulton a Pipemaker in Baldwins Gardens in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the bars in the County of Middx: Says her said husband in his life time nor she this Examinant since his death never Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 2d. day of Octr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Wood Mercy

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Stratton Ovr . J: P:
