Middx  Johanna Nash upon Oath saith she was married to her late husband Shiley Nash at the Fleet London in the year 1727 that her sd. late husband dyed upwds. of 23 Yrs ago and that she has been informed & verily believes he Served Seven years apprentiship with the late Mr. Twining Teaman in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & after he was out of his time lived three Yrs. a Servant with the sd. Mr. Twining in Devereux Court afsd. and that after her sd. late husband quitted Mr Twinings Service he never rented ten Pounds by the Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year to her knowledge or belief nor has this Examint. since his Decease rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt.

Sworn the 20th Day of Septr. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Joha. Nash
