Before Birth (Indexed & exd GJ) 

Hassall Sarah (pregnant)

St Clement Danes May 19th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman's settlement in St Clement Danes -}  Pregnancy (to wit) Sarah Hassall  singlewoman aged twenty seven years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never lived as an hired yearly Servant or did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal settlement in her own right that she has been informed and verily believes she is the legitimate Daughter of Richard Hassell and his Wife, where Name, to the best of this Deponent's recollection, was Elizabeth that her said Father Rented and Resided in a House in Butcher Row in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County for several years & that her said Father died in that House and her Mother continued in the said House for some time short time afterwards and this Examinant saith that after her said Mother quitted that House she never did any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain or become intitled unto any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Deponent saith that she has been informed and believes that she was born in the House in Butcher Row which her Father so Rented as aforesaid And this Examinant Upon her Voluntary Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said

Reputed Father parish of St. Clement Danes when born that Joseph Crosley who now Works  in the Garden of Mr. Bull in Islington & with whom this Examinant has Cohabited with for Ten Months last past is the only real true father of the said Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant and did beget the same on the body of her this Examinant

Sworn the 19th day of May 1794 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Hassall 's Mark

Witness B Newman

 Crosley a Tall thin Man Dark Compexion & Hair generally to be met with at Mr. Bull's  before six in the Evening

he born at Southall near Biggleswade Bedford st declar'd this himself 24 Octr. 1794
