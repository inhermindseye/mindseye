Westmr.  Mary Broderick aged about 36 Years Upon Oath says, she is the Wife of John Broderick (who hath absconded and left her about five Months) with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Eleven Years ago by whom she hath four Children Mary aged upwards of Nine Years Charles aged upwards of Seven Years Eleanor aged upwards of five Years and William aged upwards of two Years; says her said husband is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge and belief Says that before her intermarriage with the said John Broderick and whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a hired yearly Servant with her Uncle Thomas Fielding a Staymaker in Denmark Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields aforesaid for one Year and upwards at the yearly Wages of Two pounds Ten Shillings and then intermarried with the said John Broderick .

Sworn the 21st. day of October 1767 .-before}  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Mary Broderick
