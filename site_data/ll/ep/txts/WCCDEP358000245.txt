Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Richard Dunton aged 27 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Lambert of St. Clements's Church Yard about 14 years ago That he served about 5 years of his Time to him and Mr. Lambert and he not Agreeing Mr Lambert Consented that he should go and work as a Journey man upon allowing him the said Mr. Lambert 5s. Per week out of his Earnings - That he accordingly did go out to work and worked at several Places for 2 & 3 weeks and then at Stratford in the Parish of West Ham where he worked with a Mr. Alsop a Peruke maker and Hair Dresser at the Wages of 10s. Per week and board that he paid Mr Lambert several times 5s. after he left his Service that he stayed with Mr. Alsop near 3 years that his Indentures were never given up That he Intermarried with Christian Dunton his now wife at the Parish Church of St. Brides London about 8 years ago That he has 3 Children by her Vizt. Jas. aged 7 years Richd. aged 5 years and Ann aged 3 years That he has not done any act whereby he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of March 1786  Wm Gascoigne  John Croft

Richard Dunton
