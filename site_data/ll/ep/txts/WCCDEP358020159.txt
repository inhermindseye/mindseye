Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Hannah Clarke aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Boddam at Broomyard in the Parish of Bromyard in the County of Hereford for Twelve Months and Upwards at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings that since she quitted said Service She has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Augt. 1789 D Walker Russ: Clarke

Hannah Clark
