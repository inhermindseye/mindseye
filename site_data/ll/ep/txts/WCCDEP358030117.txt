Middx  Annis Rogers aged abt. 13 Yrs. upon her Oath says she cant give any Account of the Settlemt. of her Father or Mother Says her Mother is dead ad her Father has been gone from her abt. 2 Yrs. & where be now is this Examinant having not Says she was born in the Parish of Dudley in the County of Worcester & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant

Sworn 17th. March 1745 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Annis Rogers
