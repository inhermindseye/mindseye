Middx & Westmr-}  Lucy Jones aged about 38 Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Jones (gone from her) with whom she Intermarrd- at the new Church parish at the Town of Birmingham in the County of Warwick about nine Years ago and by whom she hath three Children namely Thomas aged about eight Years ( apprenticed out) Octave aged about seven Years and Henry aged about four Years Says her said husband after he was out of his Apprenticeship he Rented and lived in a house in Bull Street in the new Church Parish in the Town of Birmingham in the sd. County of Warwick for sevl- Years at the Yearly Rent of £12 and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said house about three Years ago since which time he hath gained no other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house or Tenemt- of £10 a Year or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 6th- Day of Octr- 1777 before  John Croft  Jno. Machin

Lucy Jones
