Middlesex ss}  William Benefield maketh oath that he never was informed nor Can set forth where he was born Nor doth this Depont. know nor Can he in any Manner set forth the Place of settlement of his Parents or either of them And this Deponent Saith that he was never bound an Apprentice nor never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor never Served any Parish office nor never Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes And this Depont. farther Saith that about Twenty five Years ago he was Married to his now wife Elizabeth (whose Maiden Name was Stevenson ) The which Elizabeth this Deponts wife before and at the time of this Deponts Marrying her lived in & rented a house of Fifteen Pounds by the Year Rent in the Parish of Camberwell in the County of Surry And that immediately from & after such Marriage he this Depont. and his Said wife lived in and Continued together in Possession of such house at & under such Yearly Rent as aforesaid for above the Space of Forty days and by Means thereof as he this Deponent is advised & apprehends he this Depont. gained a legal settlemt. is the Said Parish And this Deponent farther Saith that he hath not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor hath he done any Act to his knowledge or belief to Acquire a subsequent settlement And that he hath also a Child named Mary aged about Twelve Years the lawful issue of this Deponent by his said wife.

Sworn this 3d day of January 1766 before  Chris: Scott Bt Darling

William Benefield His Mark
