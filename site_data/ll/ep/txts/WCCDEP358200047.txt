Middx  Mary Vaux Aged about 29 Years Removed from the Parish of Chipping Barnett in the Liberty of St. Allbans in ye County of Hertford by Order dated the 17th. Day of December 1763 Upon Oath Says she was never Marryed says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Hutchins in Millford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Years and hath not Since rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the Day of Decr 1763 Before}

Upon Mr Robt Williams Compt J. W
