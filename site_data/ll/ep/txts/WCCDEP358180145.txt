Middx  Jno. Windsor aged near 20 Years upon his Oath saith that he was born in the parish of Hanslopp in the County of Bucks & never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt . rented £10. a Yr. or Pd. Parish Taxes that his Father John Windsor was bound an Apprentice to & Served 6 Yrs. or thereabts. the first Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. William Stone in Little Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand  Button mold maker & then went abroad & hath not since been heard of that having lately had the Small Pox he is not at Prest. capable of Providing for himselfe

Sworne 20th. Decr. 1743 before} Ant. Wroth .

The Mark of Jno. Windsor
