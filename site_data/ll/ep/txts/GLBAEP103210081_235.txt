Middlesex ss}  Charles Kennedy maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a yearly hired servant by & with Mr Samuel Road a Farmer in that part of Hoxton which is in the Parish of St John Hackney in the sd County & Continued in such Service under such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of about Sixteen Years & has not since lived as a hired Servt for the Space of a year elsewhere nor rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors rates or Kings taxes or done any act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 12. day of May 1785 before R Davies Robt Smith

The Mark of Charles Kennedy
