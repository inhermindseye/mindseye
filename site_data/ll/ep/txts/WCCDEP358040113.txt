Middx  Martha Capham Wido. of John Capham deced removed from the Parish of St Giles in the feilds by Order dated the 3d. of Decr. Instt. upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. John Capham at Lincolns Inn Chapel abt. 21 Yrs. since Says her sd. late husband about 12 Yrs. ago lived in and rented a house in Burleigh Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds for five Years or thereabts. & never after he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she has not since the Death of her sd. late husband rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has two Children (now at home with her) viz Eliz aged abt. 16 Yrs. & John aged near 12 Yrs.

Sworn 4th. Decr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Martha Capham
