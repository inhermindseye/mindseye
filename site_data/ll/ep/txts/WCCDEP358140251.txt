Middx.  Elizth: Hailstone aged abt. 62 years upon Oath says she never was married; says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Tyce a Butcher in Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx . for One year and Upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds and hath quitted that Service abt. Two years: since which time she hath not Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn this 1st. June 1759 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eliz: Hailstone
