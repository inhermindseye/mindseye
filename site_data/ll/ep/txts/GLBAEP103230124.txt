Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Trehorne maketh oath that divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Mathews at the Sign of the Sun in Sunyard in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the said County and Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two years and ten Months and has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married not hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Since Such her Servitude to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this. 1. day of March 1788 before R Davies

The Mark of Eleanor Trehorne
