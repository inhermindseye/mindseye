St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Thomas Strode Aged 37 Years or thereabouts Singleman Maketh Oath and saith that he lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edward Lloyd in Fenchurch Street in the parish of St Duins Backchurch in the City of London for about 15 Months or upwards at the Yearly Wages of 12 Guineas that he quitted the same Service in the Year 1782 and that since he hath not done any Act or thing whatsoever whereby he might have acquired any Subsequent settlement And that he is Ill Poor and unable to maintain himself

Sworn at the 19th. April 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Thomas Strode 's Master
