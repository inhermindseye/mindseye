Middx  Mary Woodward aged abt. 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was hired to live a servant with Mr. Ashby a Collar maker for one Year certain at the Yearly Wages of thirty Shillings & lived with the sd. Mr. Ashby under the sd. hiring and Agreemt. in the parish of Chigwell in the County of Essex for one Year that she left her sd. Service near 6 Yrs. ago & has not since rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 30th. Septr. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Mary Woodward
