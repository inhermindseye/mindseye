Middx  Martha Matthews Aged about 20 Years upon Oath Says she was never Married Says she was Bound an Apprentice to Mr. Smith a Hair Cutter in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx with whom she lived about Three Years and a half and then came away and hath quitted her said Apprentiship about Three Years and hath not Since Rented House or paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One William Williams a Midshipman had Carnal Knowledge

of this Examinants Body about the Latter End of September Last and at several other times Since at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the 26th Day of January 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Martha Matthes

Upon Mr Olivers Complaint Ovrs J. W
