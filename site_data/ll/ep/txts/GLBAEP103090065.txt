Middlesex ss  Anthony Duess Maketh oath That Divers Years ago he rented a house in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex at the Rent of Seventeen Pounds by the Year and Continued therein for Ten years and was rated to and Paid all Parochial Taxes for the Same and by means thereof as he is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Dapont. Saith that he has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards Nor served any Parish offices nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act Whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement And that he hath a Wife named Ann

Sworn this 27th: day of May 1767 before  Chris: Scott  Rich: Hard

Anthony Duess His Mark
