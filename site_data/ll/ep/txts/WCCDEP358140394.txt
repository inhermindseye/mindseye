Middx.  Sarah Ryall aged abt. 30 Years Upon Oath says She Never was Married nor Rented Ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year. That Joshua Ryall her Father deced lived in and Rented a house in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds for sevl. Years and after he quitted that house Never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid parish Taxes

Sworn Augst. 7th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Ryall
