Middlesex ss.  Mary Newport maketh Oath & Saith that about Thirty years ago she was Married to her late Husband Thomas Newport at the Church belonging to the Parish of St. Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry that she hath a Daughter living named Hannah issue of the said Marriage born in the  Parish of St. Mary Lambeth aforesaid and who hath not gained any settlement in her own right as this Deponent verily believes, And this Deponent further Saith that she cannot give any Account of her said late Husbands Settlement nor can she give any Account of her own settlement prior to her said Marriage except that she has been informed and believes that she was born in the said Parish of St. Mary Lambeth , And also except that Prior to her said Marriage she lived as a yearly hired Servant by the year at certain yearly Wages for two Years or thereabouts with Mr . Thos. Cook of the Parish of St. Mary Lambeth aforesaid, And this Deponent further Saith that her said Daughter being Poor not able to provide for herself she has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County for her Relief & she has been Relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 2d Day of Novr. 1797 . before R Davies H: Reynell

her Mary Newport Mark
