Middlesex ss.  Elizabeth Mc.Carty the Wife of Florence Mc.Carty (who has left her) maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said Husband at the Parish Church of St. Paul Shadwell in the County of Middlesex on or about the Twelfth Day of August last that she this Deponent at the time of her Marriage as aforesd. & for several Years before that time lived in and Rented a House of Twenty Pounds a year in King Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , that her said Husband quitted the said House about Three Months ago & took an Apartment in the Dwelling House of Mrs. Hobbs near King James's Stairs in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the Said County But was to pay no more than Eight Pounds a Year for the same, that he lived there about Three Months And has not done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement since he quitted the House in King Street aforesaid

Sworn this Day of July 1797 before}
