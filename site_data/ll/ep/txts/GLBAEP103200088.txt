Middlesex ss}  Edward Currey maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre to William Turpin of Seething Lane in the Parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London  Lighter & after having Served three years of his Apprenticeship in the Sd Parish he was Pressed on board one of his Majesties Ships of War & always Continued at Sea during the remainder of the Term for which he was bound Apprentice & did not afterwards return to his Said Master That he never rented a house of Ten Pounds A year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement Since his Sd Apprenticeship & that he hath a w

Sworn this 2d day of January 1784 before J. Hawley Robt. Smith

Edw Currey
