St Clement Danes January 12th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster Vide farmer Lansnt. (Page 217) (to wit) Deborah William Aged 45 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that while she was single & unmarried and a Widow not having any Child or Children she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Vigne in Albion place Surrey side of Blackfryan Bridge in the parish of Christ Church in the County of surrey about 17 Months at the Annual Wages of Ten Guineas quitted that she quitted that Service abt Six or Seven years ago & since hath not done any Act or the thing whereby be the best of her knowledge or belief she may have gained any other legal Settlement and Last by that she was removed from their parish to the said parish of Child Church by Orders of removal in the Month of April last was then maintained in the House for Six Weeks assd. which Order they have not ever appealed to their Deponent knowledge or belief and that She unable to maintain herself with and Assistance Assistance

Sworn the 12th day Janry. 1792 before us  Sampr Wright Meading

Deborah Williams
