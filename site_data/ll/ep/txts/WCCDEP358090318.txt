Middx  Sarah Creeve Aged about 24 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex to One Mr. Wadsworth a Sheriffs Officer in Linisters Alley Bedford Bury in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . & Served her said Master upwards of Seven Years & after quitting her said Masters Service she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 9th. day of Decr. last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of One Mr. Smith in Bennetts Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized by the name of George & is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further that one George Mitcham a Runner at Mr. Justice Walkers in Hyde Street Bloomsbury in the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body on the 12th. day of March last was Twelve Months and at Sevl. Different times afterwards at one of Which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said George Mitcham & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 22nd day of June 1782 R Butler

her Sarah Creeve Mark
