Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Spivigun aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on or about the Fourth day of December now last past she this Deponent was delivered of a female Bastard Child at No. 7 in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath Since been Christened by the Name of Grace That one John Collins a Cook who did live at Mr. Spencers in Bow Street but where he is now she this Deponent doth not know is the father there of he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that he is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 9th. day of May 1798 before me } Saml. Edward .

her Mary Spivigun Mark
