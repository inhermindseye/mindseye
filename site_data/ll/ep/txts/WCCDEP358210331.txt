Westmr  Mary Gordon Aged about Twenty Six Years on on her Oath Says She was never Married that She lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. John Smith a Breechesmaker in Carey Street in the liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted said Service upwards of five Years and hath not Since Rented House or Tenemt. of Ten Pounds a Year or lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for one Year Since quitting her said Service And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 2d Day of June last past She was delivered of a Femal Bastard Child in the House of Mary Johnson at Mays Pond in the Parish of St Thomas in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry which has Since been baptized by the Name of Elizth. is now living & become Chargeable to the Parish of St Clement Danes Says that one John Milstead a Stage Coach man did got beget the said Female Bastard Child on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body Sevl times at one of which times he got her with Child of which She was delivered as aforesd. and that the said John Milstead is the true and real father thereof And further upon her Oath Says the sd John Milstead now Absconds but Says she has been Informed and wch Information she verily believes to be true that the sd John Milstead lives or resides at Dartford in the County of Rent he having been often Seen thereforth

Sworn this 14 Day of Novr. 1768 Before} Tho: Lane
