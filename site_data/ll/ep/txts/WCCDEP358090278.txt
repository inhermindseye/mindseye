Middx to wit Elizabeth Browne otherwise Wingfield aged about 55 Years on her Oath Says she intermarried with one John Wingfield an Irishman (who was a Dealer in Fish and Poultry ) in the Sardinian Ambassador's Chappel in Duke Street Lincoln's Inn fields by a nomish Priest which Marriage is of no Effect by the Laws of this Country as Examinant is informed and believes And further Says that before such her Intermarriage with the said John Wingfield and whilst she was a Singlewoman she served four Years Apprenticeship to the Mantua making Business wth. Mrs. Bird in South sea Court in the Mint in the Parish of Saint George in the Borough in the County of Lesney since which time she hath gained no other Settlement either by renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 21st. day of 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Elizabeth Brown Mark
