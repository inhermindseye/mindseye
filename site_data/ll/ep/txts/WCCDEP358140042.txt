Middx  Susa. Shannon aged abt. 44 Years upon Oath says she never was married says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. James Yates in Howard Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for two Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abt. two Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Parish or place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 8th. day of Octr. 1757 before me} Barth: Hammond Susanna Shannon
