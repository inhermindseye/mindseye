Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Richman aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that about 20 years ago she Intermarried with John Richman who has left her about Seven years at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square That her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Bow Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for about Fifteen Months at the yearly Rent of Sixty Pounds that after he quitted said House he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any other Settlement and further saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 14th. day of Novr. 1798 . before us  Jn Innd  Cha: Bayle :

Sarah Richman
