Middx 

Mary Alloway a Vagrant taken out of the Street in Labour in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is about 28 Years of Age and never was married and that on the fifth Day of this Instant June she was delivered of a male bastard Child (since baptized Thomas) in the house of Nurse Walker one of the parish Nurses of thesd. parish of St Clement Danes And further upon her Oath saith she never rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes was a Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year and that her father Thomas Alloway  lived in and rented a house in the Parish of Henley upon Thames in the County of Oxford for several Years and paid Scot and Lot and there dyed

and that the time of her sd. fathers death happened about Six Years ago

Sworn June 20th. 1750 before} Tho Lane  Geo: Emington

The Mark of Mary Allowas
