Middx ss  Letitia Eadsall  Widow of William Eadsall Deced Maketh oath that about three Years ago she her sd. late Husband rented a house in Little Deans Court in the parish of St. Ann Within Aldersgate in the City of London and paid Ten pounds a Year rent for ye Same & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there as this Depont. is informed & believes And this Depont. Saith that her sd. husband did not afterwards gain any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief And this Depont Saith that she hath not gained any all Subsequent Settlemt. Settlemt. Since her sd. husband Death to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 31 day of Augt: 1751 before us  Richd: Ricards  John Lawton
