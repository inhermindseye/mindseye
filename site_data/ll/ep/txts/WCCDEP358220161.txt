Middlesex & Westminster }  Margaret Hill Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with the late George Westron a Hosier in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds and then her said Master died and this Examinant further Saith that one Mr: James Green on her said Master's dying took her said late Master's House who continued her as a Yearly hired Servant at and under the same Wages and has lived with the said Mr: Green for Six Months or thereabouts And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Philip Westron  Gentleman who (as this Examinant has heard and believes) now lives at Mr: Kings in Fore Street in the City of Exeter had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of September last but the particular day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant as aforesaid And that the said Philip Westron and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 12th: day of July 1770 before me W Kelyngs

Margret Hill
