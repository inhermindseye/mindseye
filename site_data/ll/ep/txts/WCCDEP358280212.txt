St. Clemt. Danes

Middlesex (ss)  Elizabeth Kirby aged 28 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she Never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly servant with Mr. William Morris in the Parish of North Luffnam in the County of Rutland for upwards of 12 Months at the yearly Wages of £3:10:0 that she hath quitted her said service about 4 years ago that she hath gained no Subsequent Settlement to the best of her Knowledge or belief; And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she was on the Eighteenth day of January last deliver'd of a Female Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Thomas of Denzell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes , That one Humphrey Jones who lives at she New Exchange Coffee House is the Father thereof he having had Carnal of this Examinants body sometime in or about the Month of January March last and sometimes since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so deliver'd as aforesaid and that she said Humphry Jones is the only real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of January February 1789 before} Wm Kitchiner

her Elizabeth Kirby Mark

Humprey Jones is a Short man Dark Hair Tied behind in a Cub Dark Complection Smooth face Black Eyes Inclinable to be Lusty.

Warrt. made out.
