Middx to wit George Walker Aged about Twenty Three Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he was bound by Articles for Three Years to his Uncle Walker a Bricklayer & Mason at Holbrook Woodhouse in the Parish of Cooking in the County of Nottingham and after serving his said Uncle for the aforesaid Space of Three Years and after leaving his said Uncle he never gained any other Settlement than as aforesaid.

Sworn this day of July 1784 before}
