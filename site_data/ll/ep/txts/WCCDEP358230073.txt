Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Gottschen aged about 31 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly with one Mr. Swain at the George in Brook Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that he hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1799 before us  Saml. Edwards Menter

his John Gottschen mark

H Day
