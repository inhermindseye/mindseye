Middx  The Examination of Mary Lacey aged about Eighteen Years who was found wandering in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 6th. Day of March 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that She is a Singlewoman & was never married & that about three Years ago she was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Thos. Wright of the Parish of East Ham in the County of Essex  Indigo maker and served him as an Apprentice for the Space of one Year or thereabouts And further on her Oath saith that She hath not since She left her sd. Master rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife and being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Lacey
