Middx.  Cathne. Sumpter Aged abt. 37 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Bedford at the Crown and Rolls Tavern in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx. for one Year and upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & hath quitted said service abt. Twelve Years And hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 26th. day of Sepr. 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

Cathr. Sumpter
