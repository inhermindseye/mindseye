Middx  Ann Plunkett aged about Sixty Years one of the Paupers belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes maketh Oath that on or about the 17th. of September last a Female Child was found dropped at the Workhouse door in Carey Street and on this Examinants looking at the Child believed she knew the same tobe one Ann Matthews  otherwise Kennedys and the more so because that on Undressing the said Child a paper was found in its Bosom with the Name Wrote thereon Mary Kennedy and after this Examinant had given herself some Trouble found out where the said Ann Matthews otherwise Kennedy lived and on applying to her and asking her how she could be so base and cruel to drop her Child the said Ann Matthews otherwise Kennedy Answered that the Child was sure to be taken care of where it was dropped That the Father of the said Child was the at sea and that on his return which she believed wod. be about Christmas she wod. take the Child away whither it was in the Workhouse or at Low Layton And Possitively acknowledged it to be her Child and that she had so dropped it as before relate

Sworn this 25th. day October 1779 before } 

Ann Plunkett
