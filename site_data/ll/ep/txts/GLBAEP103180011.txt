Middlesex ss}  Margaret Evans the widow of Richard Evans deced & Elizabeth Evans her Daughter Severally make oath And first the said Margaret Evans Saith that divers Years ago her said late husband rented a house in Gillams Court in the Parish of Saint Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry at the yearly Rent of Five pounds & ten Shillings & lived in the Same for Several years & that during Such his Residence therein he was Charged to and paid the Kings Tax for the same and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself And the said Elizabeth Evans for herself Saith that She never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived in any one place as hired servant for the Space of a year under any one Contract or hiring nor was ever Married nor to her Knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this. 14. day of January 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey  Parkr Rowlands

Margt. Evans The mark of Elizabeth Evans
