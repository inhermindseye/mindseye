St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westmister}  St C D Maiden Settlement}  (to wit) Ann Morgan Aged 23 years and upwards Wife of John Morgan Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband John Morgan at the parish Church of St Clement Danes about 4 years ago (by Licence) that her said Husband in a Native of Ireland and never obtained any or. settlement in this Kingdom as She hath been informed and verily believes, that She this Depont. previous to her said Marriage & while she was single and unmarried not having any Child or Children she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Sncoks a Grocer not in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County about 15 Months at the Annual Wages of £10:10:0 that she quitted that Service about 4 years and an half ago and hath not Since lived under any yearly hiring or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement that She hath one Child by her said Husband which hath he born on the 7th. December 1791 which hath been half Baptized by the Name of John that she is Distressed and unable to maintain herself and Child without her Assistance that her said Husband left her & said Child on or about the

17th day of August last and that she hath not since heard an nor also she and know not whereas he is

Sworn the 12th day of September 1792 before me } R Butler

Ann Morgan
