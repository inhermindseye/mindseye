Middx  Mary Wall upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Silvester Wm. Wall and that her sd. late husbd. informed this Examint. he Served his Apprentiship with Mr Williams a Carpenter in Chiswell Street in the parish of St Luke Middx. and never Rented ten Pounds by the Year paid parish taxes nor lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year which information she believes to be true and this Examint. further maketh Oath that she has not since the Decease of her sd. late husband rented ten pounds by the Year Paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn March. 28th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Wall
