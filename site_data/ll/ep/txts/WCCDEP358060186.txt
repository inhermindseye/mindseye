Middx  Sarah Ryal aged about 21 years upon Oath saith She never was married and that she is the Daughter of Josha. Ryal by Sarah his wife both deceased that her sd. late Father Joshua Ryal rented a house of Twenty pounds by the year & upwards in Newcastle Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lived therein Sevl. Years & there dyed and that she this Examinant never Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr 15th. 1751 before}
