Middx ss  Marsh  Richard Marstone Maketh oath That he has been informed by his Father and Mother Deced that he this Deponent was born in the Parish of Churbury in the County of Salop And this Deponent saith that about forty three Years ago his said Father removed from Cherbury aforesaid to the parish of Hawford in the sd. County of Salop and Rented a Farm of One hundred pounds a Year and paid all Parish Taxes And this Deponent farther saith that he this Deponent in his sd. Father life time Married the Daughter of one Thomas Stedman a Farmer who then also lived in the sd. Parish of Hawford and he this Depont. & ye sd Thomas Stedman became Partners together & lived in & rented a Farm of forty Pounds a Year in ye said Parish and paid Parish taxes And afterwards this Deponent Rented a Walthouse in ye same. another Parish but paid No Taxes And this Deponent saith that he hath not since gained any settlement then as above to his knowledge or belief having Never since rented a house or Farm of Ten pounds a Year or upwards Nor been a hired servt or Apprentice Nor served any Parish offices And that he his a  wife who  is Named Sarah

Sworn this 16. day of September 1757 before us Chris: Scott  Walter Berry }

Richd Marzton
