Middx  Ann Holmes aged about 29 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married and that about One Year Since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Wm Jones  Esqr . in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of 3 Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5..10..0 for the first Year & £6 for the Remainder of the sd. Term Says she hath not since she left her said Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Febry 5th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Holmes
