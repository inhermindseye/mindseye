Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Christiana Neaves aged 67 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was married to George Neaves her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the year 1764 That her said Husband has been dead about Eight years That her said late Husband Rented and Occupied a House in Little Milford Lane for about Three years where this Deponent Resided with him and paid the Annual Rent of £30 or thereabouts for the said House That after she this Deponent and her said late Husband quitted said House they want to live in a House in Holles Street where they resided about Three years and paid upwards of £30 per Annum That after they quitted said House they went to Reside in Clare Market and had Apartments for which they paid £20 per Annum and lived five or Six years in the same That then this Deponent & her said late Husband Removed to Bloomsbury where her said late Husband died That then this Deponent came back to Clare Market when she has Resided about Four years and paid upwards of Twenty Pounds per Annum And this Deponent further saith that she has not Intermarried with any one since the Death of the said George Neaves

Sworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1794  J Hlind

Christiana Neaves
