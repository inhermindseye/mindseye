Middx ss  Elizabeth Pritchard  Widow of John Pritchard Deced Maketh oath that her said husband has often informed her that about Twenty three Years ago (when a Single Man) he was a hired Servant by the Year to one Mr. Lewis a Sugar Baker of ye Parish of St. Nickolas Olave in the City of London with whom he lived for above ye Space of a Year at ye Wages of Seven pounds a Year by Means where of he acquired a legal Settlemt in ye sd. Parish And this Depont. Saith that her sd. husband did not do any Act to ye knowledge information or belief of this Depont. Whereby to gain or acquire any Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 18. Day of Decr. 1750 before us  Saml Gower  Boulton Mainwaring

his Elizbt. Pritchard Mark
