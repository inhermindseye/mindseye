Middlesex to wit Ann Rawlinson Aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried to her late Husband William Rawlinson at Broomsgrove in the County of Worcester about Nine Years ago and who has been dead about Nine Years Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Chawner in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for four Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago since which time she hath gained no or. Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 24th. day of Febry 1778 before  Abrm Baley

her Ann Rawlinson Mark
