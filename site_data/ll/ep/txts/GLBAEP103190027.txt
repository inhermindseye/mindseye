Middlesex ss}  Mary Hill the wife of Joseph Hill maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her being lately pressed & put on board his Majesties ship the Conquestadore & is now in his Majesties Service That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Williams a Sawyer in the Parish of Hay Green near Sturbridge in the County of Worcester & has not since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief & that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 7. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Hill
