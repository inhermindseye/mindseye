St Clement Danes

Middlesex  Eleanor Young  Singlewoman aged near 30 years maketh Oath and saith that about Six years ago she lived an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Shergold at the Sun Tavern in Clare Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for a Year and an half together at Six Pounds a Year Wages and since she quitted that Service she hath not lived an hired Yearly Servant for one whole Year together though she hath been in divers Services for the space of five or Six Months all which Places were in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And saith she was never married nor ever Rented a Tenement or Lodging at so much Rent as ten Pounds a year paid any kind of Taxes or did any other Act to her Knowledge or Belief which might gain a Settlement in any Parish or place other than her said Service with the said Mrs. Shergold in Clare Street aforesaid

Sworn the 1st. Day of December 1790 } R Butler

The Mark of Eleanor Young
