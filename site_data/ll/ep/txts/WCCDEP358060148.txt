Middx  Martha Cook  Wido . of Thomas Cook who dyed about Ten Years since upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Thomas Cook at the Savoy Chappel abt. 15 Years since and that her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Gross a Staymaker in Newcastle Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and never afterwds. now has this Ext. since his Decease rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 5th. 1751
