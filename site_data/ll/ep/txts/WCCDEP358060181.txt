Cath . Burrell upon Oath Saith she is the wife of George (at present as this Examinant believes at Nova Scotia ) of whose Settlement she can give no Account whatever Says that she was at the time of her marriage with her prest. husbd. Burrell the Wido . of John Cassell a Scotchman who never gained a Settlemt. in England to this Examinanants knowledge and that when she intermarried with her Second Husband Cassel she was the Wido . of John Douglas with when she intermarried abt. 30 Years ago at Clerkenwell Church and that the sd. John Douglas rented a house facing the market at Aylesbury in the County of Bucks of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds & lived therein upwards of three months & never after he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Oct. 7th 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Cathn. Burrell
