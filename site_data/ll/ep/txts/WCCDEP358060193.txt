Westmr.  Mary Bates aged upwds. of 16 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and is the Daughter of Joseph Bates by Jane his wife both deceased that her sd. late father rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £10 at Highgate in the parish of Hornsey in the County of Middlesex and Lived therein Sevl Years and there dyed and this Examinant never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief

Sworn the Day & year abovementioned before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Bates
