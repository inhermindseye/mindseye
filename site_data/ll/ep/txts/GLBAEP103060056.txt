Middx  Judith Banks  Widow late Wife of Edward Banks deceased maketh Oath and Saith that about Twelve Years ago her said late Husband Edward Banks rented a House in the parish of St. Mary at Newington in the County of Surry and lived therein a Year and upwards and paid Twelve pounds a Year for the same And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge And that She hath gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge And hath two Children (namely) Edward Aged Ten Years and Henry Aged Seven Years

Sworn this 23d: day of January 1745 Before Robert Dennett

her Judith Banks Mark
