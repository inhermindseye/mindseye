Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Ann Robinson aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or Paid Taxes That she was bound Apprentice by the Parish Officers of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . till she was Twenty one year of age to one Mr. Strutt a Collow Manufacturer at Rickmansworth in the parish of Rickmansworth in the County of Herts That she served her said Master duly and truly until she Allained her said Age of 21 years That after she was out of her said Apprenticeship she never lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Decr. 1794 } Edw: Read N Bond

her Ann Robinson mark

Witness B Newman
