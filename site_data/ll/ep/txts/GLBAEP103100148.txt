Middlesex ss}  Ann Goodluck  Singlewoman maketh oath That Sometime agreed She was hired by one Mrs Ann Underwood who was Deemed & passed for the Wife of Thomas Banks late of the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middx  Tobaccoinst deced to live in the house of the Said Thomas Banks as his Servant During Such time only as it Should Please both Parties & without any Mention being Made (at the time of Such hiring) between this Depont. & thesd. Mrs Underwood of this Deponents. being hired for the Space of a year either to the said Mrs Underwood or thesd. Thomas Banks And this Depont Saith that in Consequence of Such Agreement She lived thesd house of the said Thomas Banks for upwards of a Year (witht being at all hired by him or thesd. Mother in any other Manner than as before is setforth) and received after the Rate of Five Pounds a Year for Such her Servitude And this Depont. Saith that on the Twenty fifth day of October one thousand Seven hundred and Sixty Seven She this Depont. was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Parish of St. Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry And that the said Thomas Banks did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child Which Child has been Since Baptized & registered by the Name of Thomas Goodluck in the Parish of St. Margaret Westmr in the County of Middx And lastly this Depont Saith that She has been informed by the said Thomas Banks & believes the Same to be true that although the said Banks & Mrs Underwood Passed & were deemed as Man & Wife Yet they were never Married

Sworn this 31st day of Augt: 1769 before  Chris Scott  Cha: Digby

Ann Goodluck
