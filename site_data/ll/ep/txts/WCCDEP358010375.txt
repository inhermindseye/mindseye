Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the  County of Middlesex Maketh Oath and saith that Susannah Clare Snelling a Poor Girl of the Age of Eight Years and an half or thereabouts now in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid is (as he this Deponent hath been informed and verily believes) the Illegitimate Child of Isabella Williamson  Singlewoman and was born at the House of a Mr John Gordon a Tanner in Grange read in the Parish of Saint Ch Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry and never has gained or acquired any Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 20th. Novr 1790 Edw. Read Wm Hyde

Jas. Talboys
