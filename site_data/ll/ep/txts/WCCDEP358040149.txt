Middx.  Ann Wolfe Wife of Sheppard Wolfe (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Sheppard Wolf upwds. of 6 Years ago at the Fleet London Says her sd. husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Fransises a Peruke maker in Beaufort Buildings in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 10th. 1747 before}

The Mark of Ann Wolfe
