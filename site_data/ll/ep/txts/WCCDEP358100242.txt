Middx to wit John Davis Aged about Forty Six Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with to his late Wife  Sarah at the Parish Church of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex about 18 Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children living namely John Aged about Three Years and Sarah Aged about Two Years Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Richard Webb a Boatsmith at Kings Arms Stairs near Cupbit Bridge in the Parish of St. Mary Lambeth in in the County of Surry and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year or ever gained any other Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 23d day of August 1784 before M Addington

his John Davis Mark
