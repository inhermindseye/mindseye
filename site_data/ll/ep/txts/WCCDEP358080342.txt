Middx to wit Ann Mansfield Aged about 19 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Nicholas Mansfield was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England other then by Renting a House in Duke Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for three Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of £9 but paid no Parish Taxes due for the same but this Examinant saith that she was born in the aforesaid House and that her Mother Hannah Mansfield now Hannah Nichols before her Intermarriage to this Examinants Mother Father she lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Mr. Toderick at a Tea Shop in Great Russell Street in the afsd. Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the said County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds from whence her said Mother married to her said Father as her said Mother has Informed her & which Information she verily believes to be true

Sworn this 19th. day of March 1779 before  Abrm Baley

Ann Mansfield
