St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Henry Lewis Aged 53 years & upwards Upon his Oath saith that he did about Seventeen or Eighteen years ago intermarried with Hannah his late Wife where Maiden Name was Griffiths at the parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields - that his said Wife Died on or about the 22d of November 1789 . that previous thereto and while he was to his said Marriage being single and not having any Child or Children he lived as an hired yearly Servant for about years at the three years at the Annual Wages of £25 with Mr. Henry Deale who then resided at the Corner of Brook Street Holborn in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn below the Bars in the Liberty of the City of London and during the whole of the last year he slept in his said Master Apartment which was situate in the same parish and within the said Liberty that he quitted his said Master's Service about the year 1767 or 1768 and since hath not obtained any other legal settlement and that he is at present unable to maintain himself without Assistance

Sworn the 19th day of Novr. 1791 before us N Bond Thos. Gordons

Henry Lewis
