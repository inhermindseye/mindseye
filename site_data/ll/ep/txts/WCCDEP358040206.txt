Sarah Davie upon Oath says she is the wido. of Richard Davie and now Resides in the Parish of Carlton in the County of Badford by Vertue of a Certificate and at the hands and Seals of the Church wardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex bearing Date the 12th. Day of January 1740 Says her sd. late husband Richard Davie dyed as or about the Twenty seventh Day of June 1744. By whom she hath has Children (viz) Mary aged  six years & one Quarter and Sarah aged about Three Years & an Half says the said Richard Davie has not since the Granting of the sd. Certificate rented ten pounds a Year or Served an annual Office whereby to gain a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief and that she (this Examinant hath not since) the Death of the sd. Richard Davie rented ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Before me one of his Majesty Justices of the peace in & for the County of Bedford living near ye parish of Carlton aforesd this 5th July 1748 Row. Alston

The Mark of Sarah Davie
