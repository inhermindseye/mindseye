Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Joseph Smith aged 40 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that  about 10 years ago he rented and lived in a House in Strand Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for 10 Months at the yearly Rent of £10 and paid Taxes due for the same, hath quitted said House about 9 years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of July 1786 : R Butler

Joseph Smith
