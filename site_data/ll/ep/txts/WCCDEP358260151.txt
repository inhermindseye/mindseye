Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charlotte Jones aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that about Nine years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Jones her present Husband at the parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom she hath One Child living vizt. Solomon aged about Twenty Months That her said Husband lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months and upwards but what Wages he had she this Deponent doth not know and that after he quitted said Service he never did any Act Matter or they whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th- day of Septr. 1796 } John Collick

her Charlotte Jones Mark
