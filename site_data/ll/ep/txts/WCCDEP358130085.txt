Middx  Catherine Mackartney Aged upwards of 70 Years Upon Oath Says She intermarried with Florence Mackartney upwards of Thirty Years ago, says he Rented a house in Peter Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the yearly Rent of £18, Lived in the same about 12 Years And quitted the same upwds. of 13 Years ago, Says her sd. Husband Florence Mackartney is since Dead and and this Examinant has not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a Yearly hired Servt.

Sworn the 28th. Day of Novr. 1755 . before}

The Mark of Catherine Mackartney
