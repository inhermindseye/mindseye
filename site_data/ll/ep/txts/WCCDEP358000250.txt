St. Clement Danes

Middx (ss)  Ann Bentley aged 37 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married, saith that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Ch Johson Tallow Chandler of Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for upwards of 12 Months at the Yearly Wages of £6 hath quitted her said Service upwards of 4 years ago since which time she hath not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 16th. day of March 1786 before  Thos. Taylors

her Ann Bentley Mark
