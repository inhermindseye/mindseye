Middx  Ann Worriker Aged about Twenty Five years On her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes, says she lived & hired yearly Servant with Mr. Linley in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of Ten guineas hath quitted her said Service about Eight Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement and further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that One George Stevens late Servant to her said Master and who now Lodges at Mrs. Probers a Washer Woman in Broads yard Turnmill Street Smithfield is the Father thereof He having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of October but the particular Day she does not now remember and at several Different times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of Which she is now Pregnant and that the said Geo: Stevens and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 18th. day of April 1781 before  Abrm Baley

Ann Wariker
