Middx  Elizth: Hitchcock Singleman Living in Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx Upon Oath says That George May an Infant Child aged about three Weeks was born in the Lyingin. Hospital in Duke Street by Grosvenor Square in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . as this Examinant hath been inform'd & which information She Verily believes to be true

Sworn Augt: 6th. 1762 Before } Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth: Hitchcock
