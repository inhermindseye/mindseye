Middlesex to wit Mary Holmes Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her she is the Widow of Thomas Holmes with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Mathew Bethnall Green about Seven Years ago and who has been dead about Six Years and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children living she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one James Westbrook at the sign of the Robinhood and Little John Hogsdon in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex for five Years and upwards off and on but this Examinant Says he lived upwards of One Year and a Quarter the last time she lived with her said Master at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a lived Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th. day of May 1779 before. R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Holmes Mark
