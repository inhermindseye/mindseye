St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Mary Evans Aged 27 years and upwards Wife of William Evans Upon her Oath saith That she intermarried with her present Husband at the parish Church of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty on or about the 5th October in the year 1782 to the best of her Remembrance and belief that she she hath never been informed by her said Husband or otherwise where he particularly he was born or legally Settled except Generally that he was born in Scotland And this Examinant further saith previous to her said Intermarriage being Single & having no Child or Children she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Bickerstaff at No 199 in the Strand in the said parish of St. Clement Danes for a year & an half at the Annual Wages of Ten Pounds that she left that Service about a year previous to her said Marriage and since hath not (by any means) gained any others legal Settlement in her own Right And further that she hath now living two legitimate Children together by her said Husband Vizt. Sarah Aged seven Six years & Eleven Months or thereabouts & William Aged five Years two Months or thereabouts & who are their only Children now living And that her said Husband deserted her this Deponent & their said Children about three Month, since which time she hath never seen him or received any thing from him for the support of herself & said Family

That she hath been Informed and believes he Cohabits with one Elizth. Johnson & lives some where in Chelsea And Lastly that she is totally unable any longer to support her said live Children or either of them

Sworn before Me the 11th. day of July 1791  Wm Hitchiner

Mary Evans 's Mark
