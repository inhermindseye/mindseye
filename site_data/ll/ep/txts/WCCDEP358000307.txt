Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Elizabeth Bromfield aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Bromfield at the Parish Church of Saint Mary White Chaple about 3 years ago that her said Husband lived but 5 Weeks after his Inter marriage to this  that she had no Child on Children by her said Husband that since her said late Husbands Decease and having no Child or Children living she went to live as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Reed in High Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex that she Continued  to live in her said Service for 18 Months or thereabouts hath quitted her said Service about Six Months ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23rd day of June 1786  James Croft G Mercer

her Elizth. Bromfield Mark
