Middx  Ann Wheat aged about 22 Years liveing in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived in and rented an House in Hanover Yard in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bon in the County of Middx for the Space of Three Months and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds Quitted the Same upwards of Six Months.

Sworn the 24th. day of Febry 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Ann Wheat

Upon Mr. Kirks Complt.
