Middx  William Pontifix aged about 30 Years upon oath Says he intermarried with his Wife  Mary at Mary Fair Chappel in the Year 1752 by whom he has two Children  Ann Aged about Nine Years and Mary aged about two Years Says that he Rented a House in Hand Court in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Bars in the County of Middlesex Yearly Rent Sixteen Pounds lived in the same about two Years and quitted the same about Seven Years and hath not since Rented a House or paid any Taxes

Sworn the day of Septr. 1762 before}
