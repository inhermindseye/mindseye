Westmr.  Rd. Lewis upon Oath saith he rented a house in Brook Street in the Parish of St Geo Hanr Square in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Rent of £30 for two Years or thereabouts and hath left it about 9 Years & has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 14th. 1750 before  Tho Lane .

Richd Lewis
