Middlesex ss}  John Smith maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to John Newton of Church Yard Alley now Called Cartwright Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Taylor and after living there with his Said Master for about the Space of Sixteen Months he was discharged from his Said Apprenticeship & at Liberty to go and Provide for himself wheresoever he Pleased That he has not Since lived as an Apprentice or as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house or Lodging of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 8 -day of Janry 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of John Smith
