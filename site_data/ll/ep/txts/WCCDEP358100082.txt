Middx to Wit Martha Penny Aged about Forty one Years on her Oath says she Intermarried with James Penny (gone from her) at the Parish Church of St. Martins in the fields about 15 Years ago and by whom she hath Six Children now living namely Fras . Aged about 14 Years John Aged about 12 years (out on liking) Hugh Aged about 11 Years Mary about Six years Richard Aged about 4 years and James aged upwards of One year Says her said Husband Rented & lived in a House in Alesbury Street Clerkenwell Green in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years at the yearly Rent of £20 & paid Taxes due for the Same hath quitted said House about Three Years ago since which time he never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodgings of £10. a year for 40 Days or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 19th. day of July 1783  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

Martha Penny
