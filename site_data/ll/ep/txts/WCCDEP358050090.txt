Westmr.  Mary King aged upwds. of 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year and that her father John King rented a house in Portugal Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £32 and lived therein sevl. Yeares and that her sd. father since he quitted the sd. house has not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examints. knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 17th. 1749 before

The mark of Mary King
