Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to Hackney  to wit to wit Edward Preston aged about Eighteen years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House paid Taxes  lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or ever was bound an Apprentice Says that his Father informed him that he Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Pearce and Smith in Church Street Hackney in the parish of Saint John Hackney in the County of Middlesex  this Examinant was born and that he has gained no or. Settlement than as afsd.

Sworn this 4th- day of April 1785 M Addington J Reading

Edward Preston
