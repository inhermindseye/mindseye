Middx  Eliz: Spier aged upwds. of 70 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Spier who hath been dead abt. twelve years says her sd. late husbd. before his intermarriage with this Examinant & which he was a single Man lived a hired yearly Servt. with Sr. Thos. Wheat at in the Parish of Glimp near Woodstock in the County of Oxford for 3 yrs. & upwards & never after Rented ten Pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes says she hath not since his death Rented ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes or lived a yrly. Servt

Sworn the 7th of July 1757 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Spier
