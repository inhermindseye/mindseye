Middx  Cathn Taylor upon Oath saith she is the widow of Joseph Taylor an Irishman who never gained a Settlement in England to her Knowledge and that she Served five Years of her Apprentiship with Mrs. Cable a Bomb boat Woman and Lodged all that time at Gosport in the County of Southampton , that at the End of five Years or thereabts. she intermarried with her sd husband Taylor & never Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Feby. 24th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cathn Taylor
