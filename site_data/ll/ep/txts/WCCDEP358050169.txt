Westmr.  Joseph Taylor upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house in White house Yard in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of £18 for the Space of twelve Years or thereabouts and quitted the said house in August last since which time he has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief and that he has no Annuity or Income wherewith to maintain himself and is in extreme low Circumstances & unable to provide for himself without Assistance from the Parish

Sworn Decr. 11th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Joseph Taylor
