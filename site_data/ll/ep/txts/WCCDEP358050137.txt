Westmr.  Susn. Blackman a Vagabond taken out of the Street upon Oath saith she intermarried with one Joseph Blackman a Shoemaker about 6 weeks ago at the Street London that the sd. Joseph Blackman absconds from her and she knows not where he is nor can she give any Information about his Relations or any Account of his Settlemt. or Place of Birth and that she lived a Yearly Servt. whilst a Singlewoman with Mr. Lowry at a Brandy shop opposite Bean fort Buildings in the Strand in the parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly wages of £5 for two Years or there abt. and hath quitted her sd. Service about 3 months

Sworn the 2d. Novr. 1749 before H Feilding

The Mark of Susa. Blackman .
