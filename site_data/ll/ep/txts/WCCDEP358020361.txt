St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Mary Miller Aged Sixty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Robert Jeffreys , for about two years at the Annual Wages of five pounds, that the said Robert Jeffreys then kept and resided in the House known by the Sign of the Punch Bowl in Hemlock Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty that she quitted his Service at his Death, which happened about 20 or twenty one years ago - and since hath not acquired any other legal Settlement And that she is now unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 26th. Novr. 1791 R Butler

Mary Miller 's Mark Witness
