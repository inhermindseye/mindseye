Middx  Jane Black aged about 31 Years upon Oath says She was never Married Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with John Cleveland Esqr. at the Admiralty Office in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of a Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds quitted her said Service upwards of two Years and hath not since lived a hired Yearly Servant for a Year Says She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Twenty fifth day of November last in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the sd. Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx which hath since been Baptized James and is become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor and unable to maintain the same And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that one Ralph Jennings  a Footman living at Mr. Sterk's a Watchmaker in Lombard Street did beget such Male Bastard Child he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body the latter end of February or beginning of March last and that no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 17th. day of Decr. 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Jane Black
