Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Berry Aged about 29 years upon her Oath Saith That she never was married That her Father Richard Berry before his Marriage lived as a hired yearly Servant for upwards of twelve Months with one Mr Bauloh at Westham in the County of Essex but at what Wages she this Deponent doth not Know and this Deponent upon her Oath Saith that on or about the 17th day of December 1787 she was passed from the Parish of Saint Clement Danes to the said parish of Westham in the said County of Essex under Orders of Removal Signed by Sir Sampson Wright and Waddington Esqr Two of His Majestys Justices of the Peace for the said County of Middlesex which said Order was not appeated against as she verily believes

Sworn this 24th day of May 1800 before us} John Collick  Benjm Gee

Sarah Berry her Mark

Witness S. Starling
