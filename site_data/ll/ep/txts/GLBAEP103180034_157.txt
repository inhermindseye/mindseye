Middlesex ss}  William Johnson maketh oath That divers years ago he rented a house of Fifteen pounds a year in Little wellbank Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the Said County & lived in the Same for the Space of Seven Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 23. day of April 1778 before P. Rowlands Wm Blackmore

William Johnson
