Middlesex ss  Ann Bevan  Wife of John Bevan who is now absent from her at Sea Maketh oath that she Can give no Account respect the Place of Settlemt of her Sd husband nor of his Parents or eithr of them That She this Depont previous to her Marriage with her sd husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with one Doctor Cocks in St Catherines Lane in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & Continued in such service under such hiring for about the Space of three years & has not Since done any Act to gain another settlement save What she has Acquired by Marriage with her sd husband

Sworn this 22d day of Novemr 1783 before J Hawley

The Mark of Ann Bevan
