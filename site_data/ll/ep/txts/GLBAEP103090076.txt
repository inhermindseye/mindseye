Middlesex ss  Sarah Twynam the Wife of Joseph Twynam (who as this Depont. believes is now on a Voyage to the East Indies ) maketh oath That her said husband has informed her that he was bound apprentice to and Served three years and half of his apprenticeship With Nathaniel Saunders of the Parish of Saint Botolph Without Aldgate in the County of Middx The which Informacon this Depont. believes to be true And this Depont Saith that to her knowledge information or belief her said husband never did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his Said apprenticeship and that she hath two Children namely Joseph aged three Years and William aged Eighteen Month or thereabouts the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her said Husband.

Sworn this day of 1767 before}

Sarah Twynam
