St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Deborah Williams  Widow Aged 45 Years or thereabouts upon Oath saith That she about twenty five Years ago she intermarried with her late Husband William Richard Williams at the parish Church of St. Botolph Bishopsgate ab who died about 14 Years Since leaving a Son then Aged 4 Years or thereabouts him Surviving, who also Died abt. June the Month of June then Next That during her Widowhood & after the Decease of her said Son she (not having any other Children or Child) lived as an hired yearly Servt . with Mr. Vigne Albion place Surrey side of Blackfriars Bridge in the parish of Christ Church Surry about 17 Months at the Annal Annual Wages of 10 Guineas that she quitted that Service abt. 5 Years ago & since hath not done any Act or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge or belief she may have gained any other legal settlement

Sworn before us 8th April 1791  Philip. Dyot D Walker

Deborah. Willems
