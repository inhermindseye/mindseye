Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Jones Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she Never was married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father rented and lived in a House in the Town of Carnarvan in the Principality of Wales to the time of his Death and after his Death her Mother kept the said House for several Years after and where this Examinant was born & never gained any other Settlemt. than as aforesaid And further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and likely to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes (she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born) And further Says that one John Chamberlain a Journeyman butcher who lately lived at Mears a Butcher the Corner of Bartlett Square and who now lives at the Dutchess of Ancasters in Bartlett Square afsd. is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at the said Mr. Mears's House sometime in the Month of June last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at no other time and that the said John Chamberlain and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day of Febry. 1784 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Jones mark
