Middx 

Jas. Viger aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath saith he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £35 in Arnndel Street in the Strand in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx and lived there in upwds. of five Years & Quitted the sd house in the Year 1734 Says in the Year 1746 he was hired by the Rd Mr Holmes to be an Usher of his School at Meastham near Ryegate in the County of Surry & lived there upwds. of two Years Says his Agreement was to be paid weekly after the late of Twenty pounds a Year

Sworn Augt. 4th. 1750 before} Tho Lane .

James Viger
