Middx  Rachael Pugh Aged about Twenty years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Hitchen a Haberdasher in Holywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes about three years at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Eighteen Months ago since which time she hath gained no or. Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born And further says that one Thos. Husley a Tallow Chandler who times at one Mr. Roberts in German Street Piccadilly is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at one Mr. Nevits in Frith Street soho on the Eight day of February last and at no other time and that the said Thos. Hasley and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day of Septr. 1784 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Rachael Pugh mark
