St Clement Danes 24th Septr. 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St Mary le bone  Nathaniel Whitcomb Aged 42 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith That he never was Married that he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Bray No 48. in Mortimer Street Cavendish Square for about two years at the yearly Wages of about 18 Guineas for the first year and £20 for the second that he quitted that service abt nine years ago and hath not obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge or belief And this Deponent further that the House of the said Mrs Bray No. 48 an Mortimer Street aforesaid was and is situate in the parish of St Mary le bone in the said County

Sworn the 24th day of Septr. 1792 before us  Sampn Wright J Hitchen

Nathaniel Witcomb
