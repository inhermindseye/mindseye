Middx  Margt. Carney aged 18 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Hume Manwell in the West bow in the Old Church Parish at Edinburgh in Scotland for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Q at the yearly wages of Four pounds and has Quitted her sd. Service near Twelve Months

Sworn March 28th. 1753 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Margt Carney
