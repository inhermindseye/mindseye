Middx  Jane Townsend Aged about Twenty Four Years upon Oath Says she never was Marryed that on the Fourth Day of this Instant April she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the house of Mrs Guillam in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which is yet unbaptized Says that John Rides now Ostler at the Talbot Inn in the Strand did beget the said Female Bastard Child on her Body he the said John Rides having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of July Last and at Several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Female Bastard she was delivered of as aforesaid and that he the said John Rides and no Other Person is the true and real Father thereof Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr Nott Innkeeper in ye Parish of Ashon Underline in the County of Lancashire for One Year at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Quitted her said Service

Upwards of Three Years and hath not Since Rented House or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn ye 28th Day of April 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Jane Townsend
