Middx to wit}  Sarah Sanders aged about fifty three Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of James Sanders who has been Dead about four Years and with whom she Intermarried in the Fleet about twenty five Years ago and by whom she hath two Children now provided for says her said late husband rented and lived in a house on Clerkenwell Green in the parish of St. James's Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the yearly rent of forty pounds hath quitted the same upwards of twenty Years ago since which time she never rented house paid taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. Day of June before M Addington John Hagar

her Sarah Sanders Marks
