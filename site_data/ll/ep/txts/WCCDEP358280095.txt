Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  & Westminster } to wit Sarah Russell aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Thos. Burton  Sheriffs Officer in Houghton Street Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  in the County of Middx for 16 Months at the yearly Wages of £6-that she hath quitted her said Service about 11 months since which time she has not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish And further that one Edward Groombridge who lives at his Fathers at Richmond Carpenter is the father of such Child or Children of which she this Examt. is so pregnant he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of Janry. last and at sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so Pregnant and that the said Edward Groombridge is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 1st. day of June 1787 } R Butler

Sarah Russell

Warrt. made out- Sarah Russell lives at the Bulls Head  Hungerford Markett Man to be met with at Richmond rather Tall rather Lusty is abt. 22 yrs. of Age wears his Hair hanging in his Neck has a Scar under his right Cheek and Lisps a little
