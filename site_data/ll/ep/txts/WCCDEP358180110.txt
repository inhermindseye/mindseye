Middx  Eliz: Slack upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman that she never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year that her late Father  Edmd. Slack  Deceased was by Indenture Dated in the Year 1711 bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Leggitt Shoemaker in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex That since his said Apprentiship her sd. late Father never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as she hath been informed and beleives

Sworne 9th. Sepr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz: Slack
