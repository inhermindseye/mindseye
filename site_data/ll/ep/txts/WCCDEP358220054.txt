Middlesex to wit Frances Hazzard aged about 61 years on behalf of Martha Hallem and Edward Hallem Infants Vizth. Martha Aged about Thirteen Years and Edward aged about 12 years says she knew and was well acquainted with their Father and Mother Benjamin and Jane Hallem to the time of their Deaths and that she hath often heard their Father say that he severed Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Samuel Summer a Wool- Comber in the Borrough of Leicester and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he rented and lived in a House in the Parish of Saint Marys in the said Borough of Leicester for some Years at the Yearly Rent of £12. or thereabouts but this Examinant cannot set forth the Particulars how long he rented the same or what Yearly Rent he paid But Says that after he came to reside in London he never rented House paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year he always living in Lodgings and follg. the Business of a Porter.

Sworn this 4th. of Augt. 1769 before Us me} J Fielding

Vagt. Pass
