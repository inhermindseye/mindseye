Middx.  Abraham Parfet aged abt. 60 Years Upon Oath says he Never was Married and that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Sir Wyndham Natchbull for three years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds. says Sir Wyndham used to dwell in Kent during the Summer half Year, and the Winter half Year in Golden Square in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. says he was discharged his said Service in Golden Square where he lived abt. three Months before such discharge which is abt. Twenty Years ago And hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn 24th. day of June 1760 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Abraham Parfet
