Westmr.  John Vernon upon Oath saith he intermarried with Sarah his wife at the Fleet London about Twenty Years ago and that he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Arthur Tetherley  Shoemaker and lived with the sd Mr. Tetherley in the parish of St. Paul at Shadwell in the County of Middx the first four Years or thereabouts of this sd. Apprentiship at the End of which time he went away from his sd. master & never was assigned over to any other person nor ever Rented ten Pounds a Year Pd parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn July 15th. 1752 before} Tho Lane

John Vernon
