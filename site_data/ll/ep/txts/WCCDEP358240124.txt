St Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster }  St Luke's  (to wit) Thomas Lay Aged 40 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his late Wife Mary formerly Mary Ward  Spinster at the parish Church of St. Sepulchre London about fourteen years ago that he hath one Child by her now living aged fourteen Years and upwards who now lives with his Uncle John Armstrong Comb Maker Hatfield Street Gorwell Street St Luke's Middlesex and who provides for him that this said Wife Died about Seven Years ago and he hath not since been Married That he was bound an Apprentice to Mr George Boyton a Peruke Maker in Tottenham Court Road in the parish of St Pancras in the said County of Middlesex for seven years served his said Master during the whole Town and boarded and lodged in his said Master's House that that in since the expiration of his Apprenticeship (which was about Nineteen Years ago) he hath not Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid any parish Taxes served any Parochial Office nor (while Single) lived as an hired Yearly Servant for twelve months together or done any other

Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief

Sworn the fourteenth day of July 1792 before Me } Wm. Kitchiner

Thos Lay
