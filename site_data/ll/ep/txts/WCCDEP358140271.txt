Middx.  Thomas Sharrard aged upwards of 60 Years upon Oath says that being a Single man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Langer masterley a Taylor in Peterborough Court in Fleet Street in the parish of St. Brides London for two Years and a half at the yearly Wages of four pounds for the first Year Six pounds for the second year and three pounds ten Shillings for the last half Year and hath quitted said Service about forty Years to his now best Remembrance says he has not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One year

Sworn this 31st. day of July 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Thomas Shered

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Heny. Stratton Overseer J: P:
