Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Rundell aged about 16 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Broughton who kept the Black Jack in Portsmouth Street in the parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Four Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Three Months since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Janry. 1797 before us  M Addington E Read

her Mary Rundell Mark

Witness B Newman.
