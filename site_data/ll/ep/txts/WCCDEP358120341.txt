Middx  Thos. Stockens upon Oath saith he the sd. Thos. Stockens rented a house of the yearly Rent of £35 at the Corner of Palsgrave head Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx lived therein abt. one Year and Quitted the same abt. Lady day 1753 and that he has not Since Rented a house of ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a Yearly Servant for one year and that he intermarried with his Wife Mary Stockens at the parish Church of St. Ann within Aldersgate London in September 1751

Sworn May 19th. 1755 before} Benjamin Cox

Thos. Stockings
