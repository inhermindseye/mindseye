Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Dean aged about 59 years upon her Oath saith that she Rented a Shop under Exeter Charge at the Annual Rent of Fourteen Guineas also a floor at the same time at No. 9 Exeter Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the Annual Rent of Nine Guineas for Seven years or thereabouts at and that after She quitted said Shop & Apartments she never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Decr. 1796 } Saml. Edward

her Ann Dean Mark
