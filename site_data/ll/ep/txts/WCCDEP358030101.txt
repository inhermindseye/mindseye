Middx  Mary Vanderbank aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith she was never married says about Ten Years since she lived a yearly hired Servant with Mr. Geo. Tickle at the George Alehouse in Clare markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the space of two Years & upwards says she hath not since she left her said Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Feb. 24. 1745 before}
