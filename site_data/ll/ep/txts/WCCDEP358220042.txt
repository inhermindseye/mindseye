Middx & Westmr}  Ann Taylor aged about Thirty four Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of James Taylor who has been Dead upwards of Seven Years by whom she hath one Child named Ann Aged about Ten Years Says her Mother Rebecca Miller after this Examinants Fathers Death Rented and lived in a House in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourne in the County of Middlesex upwards of Eleven Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and paid all Taxes due for the same And further says that since her said Mothers Death this Examinant never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 7th. day of July 1769 before Us} Tho: Fellows  Thos. Kynaston

Ann Taylor
