Middlesex ss}  Margaret Burton  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mrs Drew in king Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two Years and by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain another Settlement.

Sworn this. 24. day of July 1778 before P. Rowlands

Margt Burton
