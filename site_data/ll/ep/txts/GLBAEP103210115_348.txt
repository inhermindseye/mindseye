Middlesex ss}  Margaret Dempcey the wife of James Dempcey maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her in Parts beyond the Seas That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to Mr Mathews a Cooper in Wood Street in the Parish of St Alban in Wood Street in the City of London That She this Deponent has also been informed by the said Mr Mathews the Master (who is Still living in Wood Street) & which she believes to be true that her said husband after having Served about four years of his Apprenticeship in Wood street aforesaid absented himself from his said Masters Service & never afterwards returned to him & to her Knowledge of belief has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That she this Deponent hath two Children namely Margaret aged about four years & half and James aged about thirteen Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this 10 day of November 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

Margt: Dempcey
