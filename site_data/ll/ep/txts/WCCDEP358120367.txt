Middx  John Faulconbridge upon Oath saith he never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. never rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parochial Taxes & that he has been informed & verily believes his late Father Rd. Faulconbridge Served his apprentiship wth. Rd Gravenor a Joiner in King Strt. Holbourn  in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex & never Rented ten pounds by the year nor Paid Parish Taxes nor lived wth. any person as a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn 21st. June 1755 before} 

The mark of Jno Faulconbridge
