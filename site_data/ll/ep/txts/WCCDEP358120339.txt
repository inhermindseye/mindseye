Westmr.  Latitia Payne aged abt. 40 Years upon her Oath Saith she never was married and that she never was an apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever rented ten pounds by the year nor paid parochial Taxes and that her father Thomas deced had a house of his own in Blackmoor Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the yearly Value of Ten pounds and lived therein abt. 2 years quitted the same about 35 Years ago and never after rented ten pounds by the year or pd. parochial Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 16th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

Letitia Pane
