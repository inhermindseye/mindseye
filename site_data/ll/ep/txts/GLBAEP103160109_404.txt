Middlesex ss  Jane Darking the widow of John Darking deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her that he was born in Dublin in Ireland and to this Deponents knowledge or belief he never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That She this Deponent previous to her Marriage with her Said husband & While a Single woman was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Thomas Holmes of Rose Mary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Victualler & Continued in such Service for the Space of Six years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That she this Deponent has not since lived as a hired Servant for the year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or  any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a settlement for herself Save what She may have acquired by her Said Marriage.

Sworn this. 26. day of July 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Jane Darking
