Middx  Richard Moore aged upwards of 80 Years upon Oath saith he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lived therein about three Years and quitted the same about 3 Yrs. since Says he hath not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn April 5th. 1751 before  Tho Lane .

Richard Moore
