Middx ss  Catherine Cofield  Singlewoman Maketh oath that about thirty Years ago she lived a Hired Servant by the Year with one Mr Chapman a Brasier in the parish of St. Catherine Cree Church in the City of London and continued in Such Service for the Space of 7 Seven Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds by the year By means whereof this Depont. gained a legal Settlement in the sd parish of St. Catherine Cree Church And this Depont. Saith that she hath not Since done any Act Whatsoever whereby she has Acquired or gained any Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn the 10th- Day of July 1750 before me us  Saml Gower  Tho: Quarrill

her Catherine Cofield Mark
