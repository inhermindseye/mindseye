Middx  John Parsons aged about 21 Years Lodging at a Shoemaker's in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex upon Oath saith he was Born in the Almshouse in St. Clement's Church Yard in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that neither he this Examinant or his Father  Giles Parsons ever rented a House was an Apprentice or hired Yearly Servant That this Examts. Grand Father  William Parsons served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Everett Chairmaker in Duke Street facing Great Wild Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid that he never Rented a House or paid Parish Taxes as this Examinant hath been informed and which Information he verily believes to be true.

Sworn the 28th. day of April 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

John Parsons
