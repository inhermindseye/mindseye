Middlesex ss}  Ann Walker  Singlewoman maketh oath that about four Years Ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr Hawkins a Printer in Sheer Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Said County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & upwards That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year in any Place elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d day of March 1786 before  Robt, Smith  Tho: T: Lotton

The Mark of Ann Walker
