Middx and Westmr}  Swears to St. C. D . A  Elizabeth Turner aged 47 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married Rented a House or paid Taxes. Saith That she lived as an Hired yearly Servant with Mr. John Jacobs of the Dolphin Alehouse near Saint Clemt. Church for about 13 Months at the yearly Wages of £5. That she quitted her said service abt. of 22 years ago since which time she this Examinant has not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or Belief she may have gained a settlemt elsewhere

Sworn this 13th day of Janry. 1785  Thos: Gilbert

her Elizth Turner Mark
