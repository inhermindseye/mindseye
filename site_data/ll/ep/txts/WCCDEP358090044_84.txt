Middx  Mary Maudson aged about Sixty three Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Field a Sword Cutler in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two Years and a half at the Yearly Wages of £4..10..0 hath quitted her said service about five Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

Mary Maudson
