Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Ele to wit Elizabeth Higginbotham aged about 15 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That her father James Higginbotham Rented & lived in a house in Pentonville in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds

Sworn this 1st. day of June 1799 before us  Saml. Edwards J Reeves

her Elizth. Higginbotham mark

Witness B Newman
