Westmr. 

Jane Terrell wife of John Terrell upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said John Terrell in the Parish Church of St. Bennett Pauls wharf upwards of 12 Years ago by whom she hath her Children viz: Jane aged upwards of 8 Years & Mary aged upwds. of three Years Says her Sd. husband now lives in a house in Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and hath lived in and rented the sd. house upwds. of one Year at the Yearly Rent of Twelves Pounds

Sworn June 11th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Jane Terrell
