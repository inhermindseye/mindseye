Middlesex to wit Sarah Walker Aged about Twenty Seven Years On her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant witho Simon Place a Sheriffs Officer in Denmark Court near Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of £4. and Quitted same upwards of Ten Years ago and after quitting that service she went down to the City of Rochester her native places where soon after she hired herself to one Colonel Chenbal and lived with her said Master for Sixteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds Vizt of the first Nine Months in the Parish of Saint Margaretts in the said City of Rochester and the last Seven Months in C hatham Dock Yard in the Parish of Chatham in the County of Kent and after quitting her said service she never gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of April 1782 R Butler

her Sarah Walker Mark
