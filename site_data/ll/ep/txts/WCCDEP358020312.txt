St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Elizabeth Holborn Aged 45 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith That she was Married to her present Husband Thomas Holborn about Nine or Ten years ago that since their at the Parish Church of Saint Olave in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry (by Banns) that sence that the time of her saith Intermarriage her said Husband Rented and with her Resided in an House Numbered 11. Vine Street in the parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the said Liberty an and County about One year at the Annual Rent of £16 or upwards that her said Husband quitted that House in or about November or December last and that since he so quitted the said House he hath not (by any way or means whatever) acquired any other legal Settlement to this Deponent's Knowledge or belief and this Deponent further on her Oath saith that her said Husband is now in the Workhouse belonging to the said parish of St. Martin in the Fields , and that she is Poor and incapable of providing for herself

Sworn the 5th July 1791 . before us Edw: Read Wm Hyde
