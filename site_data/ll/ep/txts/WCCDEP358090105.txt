Middx to wit George Allwood aged about Fifty Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes he was bound an Apprentice to One Joseph Hilton a Staymaker who at that time lived in the Castle and Falcon in Aldersgate Street London And his said Master sometime afterwards failing this Examinant was Assigned Over to One James Bradd a Staymaker in Winchester Court Monkwell Street in the Parish of Saint Albans London and Served the said Mr. Bradd under such Assignment for the Space of Ten Months and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship or Assignment he never Gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise Howsoever

Sworn this 23d. day of Decr. 1779 before  Sampn Wright

George Allwood
