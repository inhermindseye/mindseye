Middlesex & Westmr.} (to wit) The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Smith of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Singlewoman Aged Twenty one years or thereabouts taken the 15th. Day of October 1768

This Examinant on her Oath Saith that She never was Married and that She hath known Thomas Guy  Apprentice to George Rows Taylor in Pall for upwards of Ten Months last past and that he the said Thomas Guy on or about the 15th. Day of December last at the House of Dagworthy a Porter in Shire Lane Within the Parish of Saint Clement Danes and at many other Subsequent times had carnal knowledge of this Examinats Body at one of which times the the said Thomas Guy begot on the Body of this Examinant is now Pregnant and which when born is likely to be a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes

Voluntarily Sworn and taken the 15th. Day of Octr. 1768 before me W Kelying

her Elizabeth Smith Mark
