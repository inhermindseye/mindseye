Middx  Lucy Connell Aged about 37 Years Lodging at a Common Lodging House in Kings Head Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. upon Oath Says she never was Married and that she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr Dean a Shoemaker in Clare Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes aforesaid for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted said Service about Ten Years and hath not Since Rented ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year.

Sworne February 11th 1763 : Before} Fran: Bedwell

the Mark of Lucy Connell
