Westmr.  Margt. Bothamley  Wido . of John Bothamley upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said John Bothamley at the Parish Church of St. Paul Covent Garden abt. 23 Years ago and that her sd. late husband lived in and rented a house in Water Lane in the Parish of St. Brides London of the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds for two Years & upwards and there dyed Says about nine Years ago the Servant of one Mrs. Pyke in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. being ill & Obliged to go into the County for her Health the said Mrs. Pyke Sent for this Examinant to live with her during the Absence of her Servant and this Examinant Says she lived with the sd. Mrs. Pyke upwards of one Year but never hired herself to the sd. Mrs. Pyke as a Yearly Servant or made any Contract with her for Wages but left it to Mrs. Pyke to Pay her what she thought proper for her sd. Service and Mrs. Pyke paid her at the Rate of Four pounds a Year Says she never reckoned herself a Yearly Servt. to Mrs. Pyke because

she might have left her sd. Service whenever she pleased without giving any Warning and Mrs. Pyke might have discharged her whenever she thought proper without giving any warring to this Examinant

Sworn May 9th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Margt. Bothamley
