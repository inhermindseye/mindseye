Westmr.  Grace the widow of Robert Clap who was master of the Newport a Merchant Ship trading from New England to London upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Robert Clap at a Church near Chatham about Sixteen Years ago and that the said Robert Clap hath been dead upwds. of 9 Years Says near 9 Yrs. ago she intermarried with one John Wood at a Church near Windsor by whom she hath Three Children (viz) John aged about Eight Years born in Castle Street in the parish of St. Saviour Southwark  Charles aged upwds. of five Years born in Mill Street in the Parish of St George Hanover Square and Martha aged about Ten months born in Gun powder Alley in the Parish of St. Bridget valgarly St Brides London Says that sometime after her Intermarriage with she said John Wood she was informed he had a Wife living at the time of her Intermarriage with him which Information this Examinant beleives to be true and real which said wife as this Ext. has been further informed is still living in or near the City of Exeter in the County of Devon

Says she can give no Account of the Settlement of the said Robert Clap other than that he was born in the West Country says she was born in the Parish of St. Mary in Taunton Dean in the County of Somerset and never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant or otherwise did any Act to gain a Settlemt. in any other place except her sd. Intermarriage with the sd. Robt. Clapp and John Wood .

Sworn Novr. 1st 1749 before H Fielding G Huddleston

Grace Clapp
