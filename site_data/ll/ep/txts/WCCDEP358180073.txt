Middx  Saml. Smitheman upon his Oath saith that he Served Four Years the latter Part of his Apprentiship with Mr. John Johnson in Colchester Street in the Parish of St. Mary in Whitechappell in the County of Middx  Joyner & Sash maker that since his said Apprentiship he hath not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 18th- March 1742 before} Chas: Egerton

The Mark of Saml. Smitheman
