Middx & Westmr.} to Wit Elizabeth Pratt Aged about Twenty Three years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Mitchell in Salisbury Street in the Parish of St. Martins in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of 15 Months at the yearly Wages of £5. since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement Says that her said Mistress removed into Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes with whom this Examinant has lived One Month & five days which this Examinant is Informed and verily believes she has gained no Settlement thereby.

Sworn this 18th. day of July 1783 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth- Pratt mark
