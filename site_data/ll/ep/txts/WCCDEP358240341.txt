St Clement Danes October 21st 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Bride London  (to wit) Ann Preston  singlewoman aged 54 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Evans Hair Dresser Fleet Street corner of Bride's Passage in the parish of St Bridget orse St Bride London for a year & an half or upwards at the Annual Wages of £6 that she quitted that place about five or six years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing Whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 1st day of October 1793 before Us M Addington B: Kennett

Ann Preston
