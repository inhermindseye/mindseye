St. Clement Danes

Jane Dickson aged 33 Years or thereabouts when her Oath saith That she never was Married Rented a House or paid Taxes ever lived as an Hired Yearly Servant or ever served an apprenticeship or ever did any Act Matter or Thing whereby do the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may gained any Settlement in England Saith That she was born at Dunstar in England And saith that about 11 years ago she became acquainted with one James Wallace who is a Paid ate Soldier in the guards who was then in Scotland and saith that she came to London with him and ever since that time they have lived together and passed as Man and Wife And further this Examinant Saith that also has 4 Children living by the said James Wallace Vizt. John Wallace aged 8 Years or thereabouts who was born in Hyde Park Hospital & was baptized in the said Hospital Gaizel Wallace aged 6 years or thereabouts who was born in Angel Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and was baptized at the

Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes  Mary Ann Wallace aged 5 Years or thereabouts she who was born in Angel Court aforesaid and was Baptized at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes  George Wallace aged 15 Months born in Angel Court aforesaid and Baptized at the Parish of Saint Clement's and further this Examinant saith That the said James Wallace and no one else is the Father of the said Children & that he did beget the same And that the said James Wallace has deserted his said Children

Sworn this 2d day of January 1786 before R Butler

her Jame Dickson Mark
