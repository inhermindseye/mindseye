Middx  Phillis Morley aged abt. 31 Years upon Oath saith she was married to her present husband John Morley at the Fleet London about fourteen Years ago and that her sd husband upwds. of 14 Yrs. since rented a house of £20 by the Year or upwards in Houghton Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes  Middx and lived therein a considerable time and has not since he quitted the sd. house Rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge & that her sd. husband now absconds from her and she knows not where he is

Sworn July 5th. 1752 before}
