Middx  George Whitehead upon his Oath saith that about 3 Years since he lived in & Rented a House in Kings Court in Russell Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of £11:11s:0d for the Space of Six Years or thereabouts Says he hath not since he left the said house Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 17th. May 1745 before}

Gorge Whitehead
