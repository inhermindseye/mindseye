Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Samuel Bruton Aged about 44 years upon his Oath Saith that on or about the 17 March 1781 he intermarried with his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martins in the Fields by whom he hath living one Ch namely Samuel Aged Seven years and upwards That he rented a First Floor of Mr Bowman in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middle for two Months at the yearly rent of 12 Guineas that he hath left the sa Apartments about 17 years since which time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th day of June 1800 before us Winter 

Saml. Bruton
