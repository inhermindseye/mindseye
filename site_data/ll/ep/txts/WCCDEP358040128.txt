Westmr.  Ann Hall aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath says she never was married & that abt. 14 or 15 Years since she lived a Yearly Servant with Edward King  Esqr . Mill Street in the Parish of Bromley in the County of Kent at the Yearly Wages of 40s for one Year & upwards Says she has not since she left the sd. Mr Kings Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 29th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Hall
