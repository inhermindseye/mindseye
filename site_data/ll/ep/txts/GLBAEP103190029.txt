Middlesex ss}  John Greenwood Maketh oath that divers Years Ago he was duly bound apprentice by Indre to Charles Thompson of Watling Street London Velvet Weaver for the Term of Seven years That after this Depont had served his Sd Master about five Years & three quarters he removed with him into Fort Street in the Liberty of Norton Folgate in the Said County where he served the Remainder of his Sd apprenticeship & has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain anothr Settlement That he has a wife named Sarah to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 7. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke Jno Sherwood

The Mark of John Greenwood
