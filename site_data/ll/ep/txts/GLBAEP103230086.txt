Middlesex ss  Charlotte Knight the Daughter of Henry Knight by Elizabeth his late wife both deced maketh oath that her Said late Father and Mother both died legally Settled in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County And this Depont Saith that She never lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor ever did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlemt for herself

Sworn this. 29. day of Novemr 1787 before  Rob Smith

The Mark of Charlotte Knight
