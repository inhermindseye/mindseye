Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Daniel Coleman aged about 73 years upon his Oath saith that  he Rented and lived in a House in Grays Inn Lanes in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middx for 6 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £15 hath quitted said House upwards of 30 years ago since which time he has not Rented any Place of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of February 1787 D Walker  Edward Hodsoll Daniel Coleman
