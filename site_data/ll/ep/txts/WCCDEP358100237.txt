Middlesex to wit Henry Townrow Aged 78 Years on his Oath Says he never was Married Says he Rented and lived in a House in Red Lyon Street  Red Lyon Square in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Barrs in the County of Middx for One Year & an half at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds year & paid Parish Taxes due for the sam hath quitted the said House about fifty Years ago and after he quitted the same he never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or ever gained any other Settlement.

Sworn this 30th. day of July 1784 before Sampr Wright Thos. Gilbert

Henry Townrow
