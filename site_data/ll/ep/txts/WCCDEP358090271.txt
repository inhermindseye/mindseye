Middx to wit Mary Evans aged about Twenty One Year on her Oath says she never was married rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year nor has she gained any Settlement whatever otherwise than by Birth wh was the slahan in the County of Carmarthen And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to become a Bastard or Bastards and likely to beco chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and further Says that One John Madden  Attorney in Stanhope Street Clare Market is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body at her late Master's House in New Inn Buildings the latter and of June last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times her got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Maddox and no other person did beget the same and that he is the true Father thereof.

Sworn this 8th. Day of Decemr. 1781 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Evans Mark
