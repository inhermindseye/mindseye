Middx  Ann Taylor aged about 25 Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of John Taylor who has absconded from her & she does not where he is says she Intermarried with the sd. John Taylor in the Town of Charlewill in the County of Cork in the Kingdom of Ireland upwds. of Six Years Since & that her said Husband was Born in the Town of Rathkeil in the Kingdom of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt in England to her knowledge or Belief Says she hath two Children by him (to wit) Mary aged abt. five Years & Henry Aged upwards of One Year & that she has been Obliged to ask Charity in the Sd. Parish of St Clemt Danes in Order to Support herself & Children

Sworn July 14th: 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Taylor
