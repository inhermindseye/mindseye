Middx  Ann Tufton  Wido - aged 50 Years & upwards upon her Oath saith yt- abt. 17 Years ago her late Husband Thos. Tufton rented a House in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £50 & lived in the same for the Space of Six Months or thereabts- & then went into Lodgings in Portugal Street in the Parish of St- Clemt- Danes in the same County & rented Chambers in Clements Inn of the Yearly Rent of £16 untill his Death & that her sd. Husband in his Life Time nor she since his Death never rented any other House or pd. Parish Taxes & being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 30th- of June 1741 before}
