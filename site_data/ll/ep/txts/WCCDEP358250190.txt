Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Sarah Gadd aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith that about Nineteen years ago she Intermarried with George Gadd (who is at present insane ) at the parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square by whom she hath no Child living That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Middle New Street in the parish of Saint Brides alias Bridget in the City of London for about Three Quarters of a year That he quitted said house about Seven years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th day of July 1795 } Rupt: Clarke R Lewis.

her Sarah Gadd Mark
