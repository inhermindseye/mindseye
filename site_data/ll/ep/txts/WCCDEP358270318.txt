Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Bond aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that on the 25th. day of October 1787 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the house of a Mrs. Spyers in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the Name of Jane That one George Jermain who keeps the Sign of the White Lyon in Cross Street  Hatton Garden is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said George Jermain is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 15th. day of August 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Mary Bond Mark
