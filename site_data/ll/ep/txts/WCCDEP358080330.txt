Middlesex  to wit Rachael Stedman Aged about Thirty Three Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Joseph Fowthrop Stedman (who is pressed and gone to sea ) Says she Intermarried to her said Husband at the Parish Church of St. Sepulchre London about Eleven Years ago and by whom she hath four Children now living namely George Aged upwards of Nine Years James Aged about five Years Elizabeth aged about Three Years and Catherine Aged about Eleven Months Says her said Husband Served part of his the first part of his Apprenticeship with one Thomas Bradford a Lapedary in St. Bartholomew Close West Smith field in the Parish of St. Bartholomew the Great for about Two Years & his Sd. Master into the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldersgate London said in the London for the space of Nine Months or thereabouts when her said Husband bought his time out and after quitting his said Masters Service he never gained any other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly or Covenanted Servant for one Year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 11th. day of Janry 1779 before R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

her Rachael Stedman Mark
