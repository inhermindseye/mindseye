Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Clear aged about 57 years upon her Oath Saith That she Rented a Shop in Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Seven Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds during which time she lodged at Mr. Page's house in Clare Market aforesaid and that she hath quitted said Shop only last Monday.

Sworn this 19th. day of Octr. 1797 before me }W Carter

her Mary Clear mark

H Day Beadle
