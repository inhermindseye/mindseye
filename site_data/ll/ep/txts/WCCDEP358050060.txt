Westmr.  Ann Pope aged upwds. of 30 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was born in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Canterbury whose her father lived in and rented a house and paid his Share of Taxes as she hath been Informed and beleives Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt . for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 21th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Ann Pope
