Middx to wit Sarah Skegg  Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship says She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Sidebotham an Attorney in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says She is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes She being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born And further says that one John Kidney a Grocer in Castle Street Shoreditch did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her late Masters House in Basinghall Street London some time in the Month of April last but the Particular Day She does not now remember And at several Different times afterwards at one of which She is now Pregnant And that the said John Kidney and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of Novr. 1780 before R Butler

Sarah Skegg
