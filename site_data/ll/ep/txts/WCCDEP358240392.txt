(Nil) Kelly Judith properly Wightman Judith

(Married Woman Settled in Warwickshire Orders cancelled)

St. Clement Danes 25th. Feby. 1794

Middx and Westmr.}  Rolls Libty  No Settlemt.  Orders cancelled Vide Rolls Liberty Examn Book for proper Settlement bef Mand Woman her Husband's Name Clemt Wightman Settled at Tamworth Warwickshire Vide Entry in Roll Liberty Examn Book March 5th 1794  (to wit) Judith Kelly aged 56 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was married in Ireland in the County of Cork about thirty six years ago to John Kelly that her said Husband Died abroad about twenty years ago (as she hath been informed and verily believes) that since her said Husbands Death she this Examinant being a Widow unmarried and having a Son named John Kelly aged about 27 Years who is provided for that she lived with a Mr. Leonard in Bell Yard within the Liberty of the Rolls for three Years at the annual Wages of £5 that she quitted that place upwards of thirteen Years  and hath not since done any act matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her (this Deponents) knowledge or belief,

Sworn this 25th. day of Feby. 1794 before us R. Ford B: Kennett

her Judith Kelly Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
