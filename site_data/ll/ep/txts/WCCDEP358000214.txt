No 16 Robin hoods Court Shoe Lane

St. Clement Danes

Ann  Hannah Sewell aged 18 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married saith That she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Elizth. Cowley of Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of 3 years at the Yearly Wages of £5. hath Quitted her said Service about 2 Years ago since which time she this Examinant hath not Done any Act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement to the best of her knowledge Information or belief And further upon her Voluntary Oath that she is preg pregnant with Child and or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being loss and unable the same when born and further Saith That one John Jones of No 12 Holliwell Street is the father there of he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant Body sometime in the Month of June or July last and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he did be get the said Child or Children of which she in pregnant and that the said John Jones and no one else is the real true father thereof

24th. Janry 1786 -this paid for and selled by Mr. Jones

Sworn this 24th day of Janry. 1785 before R Butler

Hann Sewell
