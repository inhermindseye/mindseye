Middx  Sarah Thomas wife of John Thomas now Soldier in Major Sparks Comps. in Coll Dunbar's Regt. of Foot in Ireland upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Jno. Thomas in the Cathedral Church of Waterford in Ireland on the16th. July 1750 by whom she has one Child John aged abt. 20 months and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd husband Served Sevel Years apprentiship with Mr. Henry Freame a Taylor in Portugal Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx and never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Febry 20th. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of John Thomas
