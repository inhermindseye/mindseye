Middx.  Susannah Johnson aged abt. 32 Years upon Oath says she never was Married And that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Morris at the Taylor Arms in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and County of Middx. for two years and Upwards at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. five Years And hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 9th. day of October 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Susa. Johnson
