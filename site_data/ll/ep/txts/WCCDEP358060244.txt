Westmr.  Benj. Baldrey upon Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Nathaniel Larking a Locksmith and Served his Sd. master as an Apprentice 5 Years in St Jones's Court in the parish of St James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx & then ran away from his sd master & never was assigned over to any person rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 13th. 1752 before
