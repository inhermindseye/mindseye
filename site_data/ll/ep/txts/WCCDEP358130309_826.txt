Middx  Wm. Pitkin aged abt. 73 yrs. upon Oath says he Rented a House in the Parish of Sunbury in the County of Middx . of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds lived therein abt. two years & quitted same abt. twenty years ago & hath not since Rented Ten Pound a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 22d. day of March 1757 . before} Barth: Hammond

William Pitkin
