Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

Swears to St.- Clements.} to wit Thomas Hide aged upwards of 42 years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House Lodging or Tenement of £10 a year Says he Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Isaac Couse a Staymaker in Yeates's Court Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his sd. Apprenticeship he never done any Act Matter or thing to the best of this Deponents Knowledge Information or belief whereby he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 7th- day of Octr- 1785 R Butler

Thos. Hyde
