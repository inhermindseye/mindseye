Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Eleanor Newland aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that she never married that on the 31st. day of May 1787 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at house of a Mr. Thomas in Stewarts Rent in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex which hath been Christened by the Name of Elizabeth

Sworn this 12th. day of March 1790 R Butler D Walker

her Eleanor Newland Mark
