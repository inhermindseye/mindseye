Middx  Wm. Charnock aged abt. 38 Yrs. (passed with Dorothy his Wife aged abt. 40 Yrs. & their two Childn. Eliz . aged abt. 3 Yrs. & Wm . aged abt. 12 Months as Vagrants from the Parish of St Michael in the City & County of Coventry to the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx by a pass under the hand & Seal of John Hewet Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the sd. City & County bearing Date the 27th Day of April 1754) upon Oath Saith he intermarried with his sd. Wife Dorothy in the Parish Church of St. Nicholas at Galway in Ireland on the 2d. day of Octr. 1750 and that before his marriage & whilst a Singleman he lived a yearly Servant with Mr Stanley of Essex Strt. in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes abt. two Years at the yearly Wages of Eight pounds, that he was discharged from the sd Mr. Stanleys Service at his Country house at Isleworth in the sd. County of Middlx where he had been with the family upwds. of two months before he was discharged Says he has not since he quitted the sd. Service rented ten Pounds by the year pd. Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn May 11th 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Wm Charnock
