Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex: ss:  Alice Kairins aged about 40 years Upon her Oath Saith that that she lived a Mr. Boulton for 18 months the last Six Months of which was in York Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £8 that since She quitted said Service She has never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one year nor done any Act matter or Thing to the best of this Examinants Knowledge and belief whereby she may have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of March 1789 before}

her Alice Kairins Mark
