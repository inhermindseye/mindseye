Middlesex ss.  Thomas Bulk maketh Oath that about Twenty Eight years ago he was hired as a Servant by the year with Mr. Munday of Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Pewterer at the yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds & continued in his Service under such hiring for the space of One year & Boarded and lodged in his Masters house the whole of the said Term that his Master wanted him to continue with him another year at the like Wages, But this Deponent would not consent to live with him another Year unless he gave him Thirteen Pounds, & therefore they parted, that he has not Since he quitted his Service lived as a hired Servant by the year in any other Parish or Place for the Space of One year together or Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of Jany. 1798. before}
