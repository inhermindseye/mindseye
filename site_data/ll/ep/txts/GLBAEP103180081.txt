Middlesex ss}  Mary Robinson  Singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Captain Halfnight in Virginia Street in the Parish of Saint George in the Said County at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain anor Settlement

Sworn this. 9: day of november 1778 before}
