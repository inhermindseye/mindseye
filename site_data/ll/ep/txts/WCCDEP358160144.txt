Middx (to wit) The Examination of Margt. Forshew  Widw . of Wm. Forshew Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 3d. Day of July 1740 . Who upon her Oath saith that about 13 Years ago she Intermarryed with the above sd. Wm. Forshew And yt. her sd. late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examint. And whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Slaughter in Church Street in the Parish of St. John at Hackney in the County of Middx for the space of two Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £8 And this Examint. further saith yt. her sd. Husband never Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That she hath two Childn. (to wit) Hugh aged upwds. of Nine Years & Jane aged Seven Months and she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Childn.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Chas: Egerton  Samuel Clarke

The Mark of Margt. Forshew
