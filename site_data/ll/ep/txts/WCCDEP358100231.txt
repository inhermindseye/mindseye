Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Stanley Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with her Uncle Joseph Walker in Little Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Six Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Weeks ago since which time she hath done no act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. and further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and further says that One George Walker her Cousin who is now in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body on or about the 17th. day of Novr . last in her Uncles House and sevl. different times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said George Walker & no other person did begett the same and he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 22 day of July 1784 - before-  Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Stanley
