Middlesex ss}  Andrew Hardie maketh oath that divers Years ago he rented a house of Eleven Pounds a year Rent in the Parish of St Olave in Silver Street in the City of London & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the same That he has not since rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a settlemt elsewhere That he hath a wife named Mary to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 19. day of Febry 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

Andrew Hardie
