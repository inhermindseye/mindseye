Middx ss  Mary Ingham  wife of Jeremiah Ingham who is a Marine & gone to India Maketh oath that about Sixteen years ago he was bound Apprentice to John Shore a Carman in Great Swan Alley in the parish of St. James Clerkenwell in ye County of Middx with whom he Served about four years in the sd parish and then left his Masters Service And farther saith that her sd. husband Never was a hired Servant by the year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards Nor served any parish offices Nor gained any subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief And farther saith that she hath two Children to wit William aged about Nine years & eleven Months & Jeremiah aged about five years & eleven Months the lawfull issue of this Deponent & her said husband

Sworn this 7th day of August 1754 before  Richd Ricards  Chris. Scott

Mary Ingham
