Middx  Mary Pearson Wife of Christopher Pearson upon her Oath saith That she intermarried with the said Christopher Pearson on the 1st. Day of June 1740 at the Fleet  London That her said Husband  Christopher Pearson Served Three Years & an half the last Part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. Pearcy Silversmith and Cutler by Durham Yard in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster That her sd. Husband never rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworne Novr. 3d. 1742 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Pearson
