Middlesex to wit Mary Plunkett aged about forty Two Years and Oath Says she is the Wife of Arthur John Plunkett with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Stepney in the County of Middlesex about Nineteen years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Fetter Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbour London for Six Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and upwards hath quitted the said House about Twelve years ago since which time he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a year paid taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 1st. day of July 1778 before R Butler Jno. Barnfather

Mary Plunkett
