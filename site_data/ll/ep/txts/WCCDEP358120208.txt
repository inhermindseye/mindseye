Middx  Grace Gill aged abt. 42 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith She is the Wido. of Joseph Gill who dyed upwds. of eleven Years since & that She is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the parish of St Clemt. Danes  in the County of Middx where she now lodges she being poor & unable to maintain in the same when born and that Thos. Catton a Nightman did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. Thos. Catton having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the month of March last & at sevl. other times since during which they Cohabited together as man & wife and that the sd. Thos. Catton & no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Octr 4th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond .

Grace Gill
