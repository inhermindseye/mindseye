Westmr. 

Ann Bartholemew aged abt. 33 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married Says her father Henry Bartholemew abt. 4 or 5 Yrs. since lived in & rented a house in the Parish of  Street in the County of Surry of Hertford the Yearly Rent of £10 & upwards. & hath for sevll. Years as this Examinant has been informed & believes Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one year rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 23d. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The mark of A B Ann Bartholemew
