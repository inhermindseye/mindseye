Middx  Dorothy Yeo (on behalf of her sister Esther Yeo a Lunatic) upon Oath saith the sd. Esther Yeo never was married to her knowledge or belief and that Wm. Yeo Father of the sd. Esther rented a house and Land in the Parish of Tring in the County of Hertford of the yearly Rent of £10 and lived therein Sevl. Years and there dyed and that the sd. Esther Yeo never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Feby. 21st 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

Dorothy Yeo
