Middx to wit Ann Appleby Aged about Twenty Two years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for One year with any person whatsoever says her Father Charles Appleby Rented and lived in a House in the Parish of Witcombe in the City of Bath in the County of Somerset for Sworne years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards hath quitted said House about Ten years ago and after quitting said House he never gained any other subsequent settlement to her knowledge or Belief and further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 20th. day of February last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Baily in ship yard near Temple Barr in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes which is Intended to be baptized by the name of William and is now Chargeable to the said Parish and further says that One Anthony Meyer late a servant to Wrexall Esqr. her late master and who afterwards went to live with a Gentleman who Lodged at No. 1 in Cushion Court Broad Street near the Royal Exchange London and who is now gone abroad did beget such Bastard Child on her Body in her said late masters Lodgings in Bond Street aforesaid some time in the Month of July last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at several Different times afterwards at One of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Anthony Meyer & no other person did beget the same & that he is the Father thereof

Sworn this 19th day of May 1781 before me Wm Kitchiner }

her Ann Appleby  mark
