Westmr. 

Jane Dorber aged about 26 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwards of four Years ago she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. George Bright near Clements Inn Gate in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for Three Years or thereabouts and has not since she Quitted the sd. Mr. Brights Service rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 26th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Jane Donber
