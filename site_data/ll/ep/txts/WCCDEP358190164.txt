Middx  Mary Hill aged about 41 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Robert Hill who has been dead about Six Years to whom she was Married at the Fleet about 15 Years ago Says her said Husband never kept House was an Apprentice or hired Yearly Servant to her Knowledge That her said Husband's Father  Robert Hill (who now Resides at Norwich by virtue of a Certificate acknowledging him to be an Inhabitant legally Settled in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. ) is a Shoemaker by Trade and lived in and rented a house in Ship Yard in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for several years as she has been informed and verily beleives Says she has one Child by her said Husband named Robert aged upwards of Six years

Sworn the 3d. day of Augt. 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Hill
