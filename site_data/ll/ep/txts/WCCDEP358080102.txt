Middlesex & Westminster  Mary Page aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with Francis Page her Husband (gone from her) at the Parish Church of St. Temple in Bristol about Seven Years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living Namely Ann Aged about Six Years, Says her said Husband Served five Years the first part of his Apprenticeship with one Francis Pistila a Painter in Warden Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster who afterwards ran away soon after which his Master dying his Masters Widow delivered her said Husbands Indentures up and further says that her said Husband was never assigned over or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlemt. to her knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 1st. day of Janry 1777 before M Addington John Cox .

Mary Page her Mark

Henry Croasdell
