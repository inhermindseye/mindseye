Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Marks the Widow of William Marks deced maketh oath that her said late husband rented a house of Sixteen Pounds a Year in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That about four months ago she this Deponent was married to one Samuel Morris in the Parish Church belonging to the Sd Parish who is lately gone away from her to places unknown That she this Deponent since the Celebration of such marriage has been informed by the Said Samuel Morris & by the Information of other Credible Persons & which she believes to be true That at the time of Such her marriage with the Sd Samuel Morris as aforesd he the sd Samuel Morris had been lawfully married to another Woman the which woman was then Actually living And this Deponent Can give no Account respecting the Settlemt of the Said Samuel Morris .

Sworn this 23d day of February 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

Eliz Markes
