Middlesex ss  James Leach maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds in upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 6. day of July 1786 before R Davies

James Leach
