Middx  Eliz: Knight  Wido . of Jasper Knight upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with the sd. Jasper Knight on the 15th. Day of May abt. 15 Yrs. since at the Fleet London that her sd. late Husbd. is a Native of Ireland & never gained any Settlemt. in England That this Examint. before her intermarriage with the sd. Jasper Knight lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mrs. Cole in Blackmoor Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of 5 Pounds for the Space of 1 Yr. since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Yr. paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath one Daur Mary aged 10 Yrs. & being poor is not able to maintain her

Sworne 1st. Decr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

Eliz Knight
