Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Mary Carrad aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Wm. Carrad her present Husband at the parish Church of St. Martin in the fields about 9 years ago by whom she hath four Children namely Richard aged about Eight years Mary aged about five years Thomas aged about Three years and Charlotte aged about Eleven Months That her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Star Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for several years at the yearly Rent of £22..0..0 that he hath quitted said House about Three Months since which time he has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That her said Husband left her & the Children unprovided about five weeks ago and know not where he is and that she is unable to maintain herself and Children without Assistance

Sworn this 18th. day of Febry. 1795  J: Hund

her Mary Carrad Mark

Witness B Newman
