Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charlotte Falkner aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that in or about the Month of Febry. last she Intermarried with Thos. Falkner her present husband (who is now from her) at the Parish Church of Saint Nicholas at Deptford That her said Husband Served his Apprenticeship to one Mr. Cressey a Leather Cutter at Greenwich in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent and that after he Quitted his said Master he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly servant or done any Act matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Augt. 1797 N Bond J.. Tolpey

his Charlotte Falkner mark
