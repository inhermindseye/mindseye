Middx & Westmr.}  Elizth. Watkins aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or was an Apprentice Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Hilliard a Clerk in Goldsmiths Hall for one Year and an half at the Yearly wages of fifty Two Shillings a Year hath quitted her said service about Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 17th. day of October 1771 before me W Kelynge R. Butler

her Elizh. Watkins mark
