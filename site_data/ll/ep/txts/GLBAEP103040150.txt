Middlesex ss  Eliza Surridge  Widow maketh Oath and saith that about forty six years ago she was lawfully married to her late Husband John Surridge at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster in the County of Middlesex . that about thirty four years ago her said Husband rented a House in Margaret Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the said County at the Yearly rent of Thirty Pounds and resided therein for the space of Six Years and upwards, that he did not, to her knowledge or belief, gain a subsequent settlement, that he died about Six years ago. that she this deponent has not, since her said Husbands decease done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right, that being Poor and unable to Provide for herself she has applied to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate Middlesex for Relief and hath been relieved by them accordingly.

Sworn this 12 day of Novr. 1798 before us R Davies H: Reynell

her Eliza Surridge Mark
