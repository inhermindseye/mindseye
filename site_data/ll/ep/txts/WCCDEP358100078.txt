Middx to Wit Mary Gosling about Sixty years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Joseph Gosling her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn about Thirty four years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said late Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman He lived a hired yearly Servant with one Edward Vernon  Doctor in Divinity in Bloomsbury Church Yard in the, Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years before this Examinant Intermarrd to her late Husband & Two Years afterwards at the Yearly Wages of Nine Pounds and Quitted his said Service upwards of 26 years ago and after quitting his said Service he never gained any or. Settlement either by Renting House or Tenemt. of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes or orwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husbands Death.

Sworn this 14th day of July 1783 R Butler G Allcock

her Mary Gosling mark
