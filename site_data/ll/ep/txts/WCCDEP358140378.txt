Middx.  Sarah Shillem aged abt. 41 years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Matthias Shillem who has been dead abt. Ten Years and that her said late husband Rented a house in Peter Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex of the yearly Rent of £18 for several Years and lived therein till he died says she has not since her said husbands death Rented Ten pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes And that about 7 years ago she lived a hired yearly Servant with Thos. Leigh  Esqr . in Sheet Street in the Parish of Windsor in Berkshire for One Year and Received £7 Wages for that Years Service but had at that time a Child living (Vizt.) Wm : aged then upwards of Nine Years who at that time had never been an Apprentice or yearly Servant And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to Maintain the same when born And that Wm. Joes Esqr. who lives at Warfield in Windsor forest had carnal knowledge of her Body in the beginning of December 1759. at  which times he get her with Child of the Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant and that no other person did beget the same.

Sworn June 5th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Skillem
