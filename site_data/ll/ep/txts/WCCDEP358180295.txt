Middx  John Manning aged 30 Yrs. upon his Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Mr. Wm. Poole of the Parish of Luckham in the County of Somersett  Husbandman and hath been out of his said Apprentiship about Six Years & hath not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne 23d Febry 1744 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Jno. Manning
