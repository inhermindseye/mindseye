Middx  Mary Rossitter  Wido . upon Oath saith she intermarried with her late Husband John Rossitter at the Fleet London upwards of four Years since Says the sd. John Rossetter hath dyed in the Month of Novr. in the Year 1744 and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in Kings head Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex on the 23d. Day of April last which hath since been Baptized Leonora & is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish & that Thos. Athinson now Oather at the George Inn in Southwark did begett the sd female Bastd. Child on her Body the sd. Thos. Atkinson being acquainted with this Examinant had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the

Month of July last & at sevll. other times Since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. female bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & that he the sd. Thomas Atkinson & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworn June 25 1746 before Wm: Jones  Tho Lane .

The Mark of May Rossetter
