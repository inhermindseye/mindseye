St. Clement Danes Janry. 31st. 1788 .

Richd. Coxe aged 32 or thereabouts upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with Rebbecca his Present Wife about 4 years age at allhallows Church Thomas Street , that about 2 months before such Intermarriage she was delivered of a Child who was Richard Coxe in an Hospital yearly gained (as this deponent believes) siter all beyond Oxford Street Turnpike That she the said Rebecca which single had lived as a Hired Yearly Servant at the Dyer Anns in Thames Street in the Parish of Saint allhallows the Great that she lived there about 4 or 5 years as

this Deponent believes at the Yearly Wages of 4 or 5s. That time she quitted such serviceshe has not done any act matter or thing to gain a Settlement to the best of this Deponents Knowledge or belief

Sworn before as this 31st. Janry. 1788 Mercer Barnfather

his Richd. Cox Mark
