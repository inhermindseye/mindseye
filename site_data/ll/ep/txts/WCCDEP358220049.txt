Middx to wit}  Margaret Evans on behalf of Thos. Evans her Husband who now lies ill of a Fever on her Oath says she Intermarried with her said Husband in the Parish Church of Saint Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex about Twenty Eight Years ago Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Cloth Fair called or known by the Name of the Welch Harp in the Parish of Saint Bartholomew the Great London  of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds lived therein upwards of Six Years and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same and quitted same about Twenty Years ago and hath not since Rented any House of Ten Pounds by the Year paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 22d. day of July 1769 before Us W Kelyngs Pet Planck

her Margaret Evans mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
