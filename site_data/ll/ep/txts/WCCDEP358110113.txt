Middx  Ann Fisher upon Oath saith she is the wife of John Fisher with whom she intermarried in the parish Church of St. George Hanover Square near four years ago & by whom She hath two Children Eliz aged abt. 17 Months & Jane aged about one Month and that her sd. husband rented a house at the upper End of Drury Lane in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex  of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds & ten Shillings & lived therein two Years quitted the same about 18 months & has not since rented Ten pounds a Year or paid parish Taxes

Sworn 15th. Novr 1752 before} Tho Lane  Geo. Errington

The Mark of Ann Fisher
