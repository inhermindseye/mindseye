Middlesex & Westmr.}  to St.Jas. Westmr. to wit Hannah Bush aged about Twenty Seven years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Wm. Robinson a Trunk Maker in Bond Street in the Parish of Saint James Westminster upwards of Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Weeks ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement

Sworn this 27th- Day of Octr. 1784 R. Taylor Jno Barnfather

Hanna Bush
