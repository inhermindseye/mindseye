Middx  papd. to St. Mary somerset} (to wit) Ann James aged about Thirty Five Years upon her Oath Saith That she was married to Evans James at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew in the County of Glamorganshire in Walls about 20 Twenty Years ago That about 17 Seventeen Years ago her said Husband went to Jamaica in the West Indies for the recovery of his Health since which time this Examinant Bath beard Nothing of him and therefore Concludes he hath bee long since Dead And this Examinant Further Upon her Oath Saith that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Samuel Darby an Iron Merchant in the Parish of Saint Mary Somerset in the City of London for Upwards of Twelve Months Yearly Wages of Nine Pounds that since she quitted her said Service she has not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or Belief she may have a gained a Settlement Elsewhere

Sworn this 12th: day of Octobr. 1784 before M Addington Reading

Ann James
