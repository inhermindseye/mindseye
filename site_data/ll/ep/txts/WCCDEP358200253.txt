Middx.  Jacob Westbrook aged about 54 Years Upon Oath says he served seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. George Cole a Cooper in Houghton Street Clare- Market in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middx. and hath been out of his said Apprenticeship about thirty Years since which time he hath not Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn March 29th. 1765 . Before} Benjamin Cox

Jacob Westbrook
