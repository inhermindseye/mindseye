Middx  Ann Blake upon her Oath saith that she is the Wife of Benjamin Blake & that the said Benjamin Blake about Six Years since & before his Marriage with this Examinant lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Robert Henley in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds as her said Husband hath informed her & which she beleives that since he left the sd. Mr. Henly's Service he hath not rented ten Pounds paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath Two Children viz: Mary aged 2 Yrs- & upwds. & Ann aged 3 Months & her Husband being ill of a Consumption is not able to maintaine them

Sworne 19th. March 1742 before} Cha. Egerton

Ann Blake
