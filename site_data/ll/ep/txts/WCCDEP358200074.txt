Middx  Susan Nubley Aged about Twenty four Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Edwd Nubley (Gone from her) with whom she Intermar'd in the Parish Church of St. Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Five Years ago Says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Burkett a Hair Merchant in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex hath Left his said Apprentiship about Eight Years and hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year as this Examinant hath been Informed by her said Husband

and which Information she Verily believes to be true Says she has three Children by her said Husband (to wit) Sarah Aged about three Years Samuel Aged about Two Years and Frances Aged about One Year.

Sworn the 27th of February 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Susan Nubley

Upon Mr. Robt Williams Complt Ovr. J. W
