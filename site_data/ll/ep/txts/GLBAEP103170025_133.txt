Middlesex ss}  Ann Leggatt  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at they early Wages of Four pounds by & with Doctor Haskins in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continued in Such Service for about the Space of Three years & then this Deponts Master died & She this Deponent Continued on her Service with his Widow & Executrix Ann Haskins in the Said parish at the Same yearly Wages of Four pounds for about the Space of Eleven Months after her Said late Masters death & then this Deponts. Said Mistress Married one John Garrett of the Parish of Saint Mary Newington in the County of Surrey  Glasier & there upon She this Depont removed with her Sd Misterss to her Said Masters house at Newington aforesd Where She Continued in Such her Service as aforesaid for Seven Weeks & was there paid her Wages & dischargd from her sd Service That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor hath done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 16. day of Novr 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Ann Leggatt
