Middx  Margarett Dorrell upon her Oath saith that she is the Wife of John Dorrell who is now at Sea in his Majestys Service That John Dorrell the Father of this Examinants sd Husband lived in & rented a House in George Street  York Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Value of Twenty Pounds & upwds. & pd. his Share towards all Parochiall Taxes & hath left the same upwds. of 20 Years That her sd Husbd. never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt- rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examinant hath been Informed & beleives That she was removed by an Order under the Hands & Seales of two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace from the Parish of St. Cathns. by the Tower to the sd. Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds abt. 10 Yrs- since

Sworne that 12th. Febry 1742 before}

Margt: Dorrell
