Westmr  Ann Evans Aged about Twenty One Years Upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year Says she Cannot give any Account of the Settlement of her Father other then he was Born in Wales but in what part Cannot Say That she has been Informed by her Father & Mother & which Information she Verily beleives to be true that she was Born in Langley Street  Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the field in ye Liberty of Westmr.

Sworn the 22d of Novr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Ann Evans
