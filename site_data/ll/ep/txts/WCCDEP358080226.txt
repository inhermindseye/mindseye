Middlesex  Ann Pearce Aged about Thirty Five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Leach a Butcher in Saint James's Markett in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of four pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of Nine Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Marriage Renting House paying taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. day of March 1778 before  Abrm Baley G Howard

Ann Pearce
