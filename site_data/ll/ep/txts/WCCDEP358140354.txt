Middx.  Bridget Cowley Wife of Christr. Cowley lodging at Mr. Coulstons a Carpenter in Barley Court in the Coal Yard Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx upon Oath says abt. Ten years ago that being before her said Intermarriage with Christ Cowley she being then a Single Woman was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Robt. Johnson in Parkers Lane in the said Parish of St. Giles in the fields which hath since been baptized Elizabeth says that One Matthew Downey did beget the said Child on her body of which she was delivered as Aforesaid; And that the said Matthew Downey is now dead.

Sworn Feby. 28th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Bridget Cowley
