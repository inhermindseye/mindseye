Westmr.  Mary Watkins aged about Seventeen Years upon oath says she was never married says her Father Watkins lived a Yearly Servt. with one Mr. Hugh Powell a Taylor in Searle Street near Lincolns Inn Beck Gate in the parish of St. Clements  Danes in the liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx for three Years at the yearly Wages of Twelve pounds meat Drink washing and Lodging as her said father informed her as she verily believes says she never was an Apprentice rented ten pounds a Year pd parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servt for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 15th. 1749 before}
