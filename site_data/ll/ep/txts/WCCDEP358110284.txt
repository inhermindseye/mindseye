Middx  Fras. Clew aged 53 Yrs. upon Oath Saith he never was married and that upwds. of 14 Years ago he was hired to live with Mr Nixon at the Dolphin Alehouse opposite Essex Street in the Strand at the Yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings & continued with the sd Mr Nixon (& with his Wido . after his Decease) under the sd. Agreement for Sixteen Months during all which time he lay at his Masters house (the Dolphin afsd.) and has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Augt 6th. 1753 before}
