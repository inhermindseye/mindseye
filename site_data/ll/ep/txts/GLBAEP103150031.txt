Middlesex ss}  Susannah Sheppard the Widow of George Sheppard deced maketh oath That divers Years ago her said late husband lived in & rented a house of the Yearly rent of Thirteen pounds in the parish a Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his oath done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 5th. day of April 1775 before B Camper

Suss Sheppard
