Middlesex & Westmr.  Joseph Monk Aged about Nineteen Years on his Oath says he never was married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes Says he was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr to John Jones of Grosvenors Mews a Hackney Coachman in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex and served four Years and an half the first part of his Apprenticeship and then his said Master died since which time he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 27th. day of March 1770 before Us} R. Butler Thos. Kynaston

his Joseph Monk Mark
