Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit John Conaway aged about Sixteen years on his Oath says he was bound an Apprentice by the Overseers of the Poor of the parish of Saint Clement Danes to one Morris Jones of Barking in the County of Essex for Seven years and that he served his said Master under such Indenture the space of Nine Months after which he run away

Sworn this 22d. day of April 1785  Sampn Wright  Thos: Gilbert :

his John Conaway mark

sent to Barking
