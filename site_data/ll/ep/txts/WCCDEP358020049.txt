Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Thomas Sedgewick aged about 56 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Ann his Present Wife at the Parish Church of Chiswick about 30 years ago that he Rented and lived in a House in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 3 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Eleven Guineas that he hath quitted said House upwards of 8 years ago since which time he never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of £10 paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Septr. 1787 R Butler

his Thos. Sedgewick Mark
