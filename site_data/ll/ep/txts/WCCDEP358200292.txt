Middx.  Mary Smith aged about 20 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Fox a Brazier in the parish of St. Peter in the City of Lincoln for One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of fifty Shillings hath quitted same about Six Years and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a yearly hired Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex where she now lodges she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one John Welch a Journeyman Perukemaker (who is gone away & this Examinant cannot tell where he is) had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of December 1764 and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said John Welch and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn 7th. Augst. 1765 . before}

The Mark of Mary Smith
