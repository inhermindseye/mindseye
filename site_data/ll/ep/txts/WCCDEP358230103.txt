Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Tunnell Aged about 22 years upon her Oath Saith that about the year 1797 she intermarried with her present Husband who is at sea at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath living one Child Vizt. George aged about 2 Months That where her said Husbands Settlement is she Cannot tell That while Single she lived as a hired yearly Servant for 3 years with one Mr Nodder a Painter in Brewer Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas a year that she left the said Service about 3 Weeks after her Marriage since which time she hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th day of Decr 1799 before us} Ws Woodnough 

Her Jane Tunnell Mark
