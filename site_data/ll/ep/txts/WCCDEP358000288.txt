St. Clement Danes. 16 May 1786

Westminster to wit Ann Hughes aged 39 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she never was Married, That an about Eighteen years ago she lived with Mr. the white Lyon Wych Street as a hired yearly Servant at £7:0:0 a year & continued in his Service 7 years - That since she quitted his Service she has not to the best of her knowledge gained any other Settlement Aud. Further Saith That when she left the Service of the Said Mr. Weitz she went to live and Cohabit with one Thomas Williams a Shoemaker that he died in the Month of Decr last and further Saith That she had 9 Children by him, Four of whom are now living. Thomas aged 9 Nine Years or thereabouts who was born at there in Harding Rents Snows Fields in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey John aged about Six Years who was born in the Same place. Ralph aged 3 Years or thereabouts born at a Chandlers Shop in Golden, Lane in the Parish of St. Lukes Old Street . Ricd aged 1 Year & an half born in the same place - and Saith that

They were all lay the said Thomas William ,  that she was never Married to him and he died about Xmas last a

Sworn May 19th, 1786. before me Sampr Wright M Addington

her Ann Hughes Mark

Resworn this  St. day of May 1786 2nd. day of June 1786  Sampr Wright N Bond

her Ann Hughes Mark
