Middlesex ss.  Elizabeth Fisher the Wife of John Fisher a Corporal in his Majesty's Second Regiment of Foot Guards, who was also the Widow of William Jones her former Husband maketh Oath that She was lawfully Married to her said Husband William Jones at Aberystwith in Cardiganshire South Wales about Sixteen years ago that her said late Husband about Three years & a half ago Rented the First Floor of the Dwelling House in the Occupation of Mr. Robinson situate in Little Earl Street Seven Dials in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex & known by the Nr. 17 , and paid Eleven Pounds a year for the said Apartment & lived in the same upwards of Four Months, that her said Husband did not after he quitted the said Apartment Rent any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement, that She has a child named Elizabeth aged upwards of Three years who was born in Brownlow Street Hospital the lawful issue by her said late Husband, And further saith that being Poor & unable to provide for her said Child she has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate Middlesex for Relief & has been Relieved accordingly And this Deponent further saith that she was lawfully Married to her present Husband John Fisher at St. Edmonds, Bury in the County of Suffolk about Two years ago that her said Husband is a Native of Huntingdonshire & that she has a child named Sarah aged about Fourteen Months the lawful issue by her said Husband

Sworn this 7th Day of Feby. 1798 before  Dan Williams H: Reynell

her Elizabeth Fisher Mark
