Middx. & Westmr.}  Margaret Beaumont Aged about Forty Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of William Beaumont who has been Dead about Seven Years with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster by whom she hath Two Children (to wit) John Aged about Fourteen Years and Elizabeth aged about Eight Years says her said Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Knight a Taylor in Durham Yard in the said Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither has this Examinant since her said Husbands Death ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 25th day of April 1770 before Us }

her Margaret Beaumont Mark

Witness Thomas Jones
