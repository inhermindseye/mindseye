Middx.  Henrietta Elliot aged abt. 24 Years upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was married says on the 19th. day of January 1758. She was delivered of a Male bastard child in the house of Mr. Scriven in Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. which hath since been baptized Chas. Thomas & that Thos. Sybourn a Sail & Sackmaker in the upper Ground by Marygold Stairs Southwark had carnal knowledge of her body in the Month of April 1757. & at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid & that the said Thomas Sybourn & no other person did beget the same

Sworn this 23d. day of February 1758 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Henrietta Elliot
