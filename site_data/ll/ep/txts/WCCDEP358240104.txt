Brimmer  Sarah Robert

St. Clement Danes May 17th. 1792

Middx. & Westmr.}  Sarah & Robt . leg. Children of Richd. Brimmer Deced & Sarah his Wife  St Paul Covent Garden  (to wit) Sarah Brimmer Widow Aged 25 Years or thereabouts on behalf of her two Children thereafter Named Maketh Oath and Saith That She this Examinant was Married to her late Husband Richard Brimmer at the parish Church of St Mary le Stand on or about the 12th. day of October 1788 That he hath Informed this Deponent & she Verily believes it to be true That previous thereto her paid Husband Rented and Resided in a House in Jackson's Alley Row Street in the parish of St Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster for about 3 Years at the Annl. Rent of £11. That he quitted the same between 5 and 6 Years ago and never obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to this Deponent's knowledge Information or belief And This Deponent further saith That he Died on or about the 11th. day of June 1791. That She hath two Children now living by her said Husband Namely Sarah aged two Year born on or about the 3d day of September 1789 and Robert born on the 2d day of February last And That they are now in the said parish of St. Clement Danes and likely to become

that She is unable to maintain the said Children or either of them And This Deponent herself lives as an hired Yearly Servant with Mrs Holliford in Queen Ann Street West and left her two Children with her Sister in Strand Lane about two Months ago where they have continued till the present time but she being unable to support them or either of the occationed the Application to the this parish for Relief

Sworn the 17th day of May 1792 before us } B: Kennett Wm Hyde

Sarah Brimmer 's Mark
