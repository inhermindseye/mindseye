Westmr.  Elizabeth Griffin Aged about Sixteen Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year with Mr. Mayberry a Taylor in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds hath quitted said Service about Five Years & hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the Day of 1764 Before}
