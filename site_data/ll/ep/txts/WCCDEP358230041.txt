Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Williams aged about 65 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Scott a Carpenter in Salisbury Square in the Parish of Saint Brides alias Bridget in the City of London for Four Years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about four Years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Febry. 1799 before us J Reeves Saml. Edwards

her Ann Williams Mark
