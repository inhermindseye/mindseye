Middx  Alice Hopkins aged abt. 43 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Henry Woodfall a Printer in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one Year & upwards at the yearly Wages of Five pounds that she left her sd. Service abt. 13 Years ago & has not since Rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Novr. 29th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Alice Hopkins
