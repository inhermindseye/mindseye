Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Spencer aged 21 years upon her Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Anthy. Taman at the Horseshoe & Magpye in Hollis Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr within the County of Middlx for one year and upwds. at the yearly Wages of £5 and quitted her sd. Service about Six months ago and this Examint. further upon her voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the Same when born and that James Hill of the Same parish Perukemaker did beget the said bastard Child or Children on her body the said James Hill having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the month of September last and at sevl. other times since

at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said James Hill and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn April 11th. 1755 before} Samuel Welch

The mark of Ann Spencer
