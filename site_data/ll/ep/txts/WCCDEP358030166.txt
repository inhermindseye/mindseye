Middx  Eleanr. Pervill aged about 60 Years Wido . of Oliver Pervill upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late Husband (who has been dead about 7 Years) at the parish Church of St. Dunstan in the west about 19 Yrs. since Says her sd. late Husband lived in & rented a house in Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of ten Years at the Yearly Rent of £11 & hath left sd. house about ten Years Says the sd. Oliver Pervill never after he left sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 21st. 1746 before}
