Middx  Mary Smith upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband John Smith at the fleet London about three Years ago that her sd. husband is an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or beleif and that he is gone from her and this Examinant knows not where he is Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Gilbert Dixon in Whitehorse Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £5 for two Years & upwards before marriage and has not since she quitted that Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 11th. Febry 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Mary Smith
