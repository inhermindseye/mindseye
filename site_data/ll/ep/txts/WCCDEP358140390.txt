Middx.  Wm: Ross aged abt. 47 years Upon Oath says he Intermarried with Margt . his wife at St. James's Church Bristol abt. Seven Years ago by whom he has One Child Jane Aged abt. 3 years. Says he Rented a house Opposite Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 3 years at the Yearly Rent of £30 and hath Quitted same Upwards of 5 years And hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn this 19th. July 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

William Ross
