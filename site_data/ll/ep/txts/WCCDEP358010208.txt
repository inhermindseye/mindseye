Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Martha Rasdale aged about 67 years upon her Oath saith that about 29 years ago she Intermard. with her Fras. her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes that he has been dead about 4 years that he Rented and lived in a House in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement  Dane s in the County of Middlesex for 20 years & upwards at the yearly Rent of £26 that after he quitted said House he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of March 1788  Wm Kitchiner

Mark Martay Rasdale
