Middx  Rebecca Spike aged about 31 Years upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman & that she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Lambert Butcher in Claremarkett in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of five Years at the Yearly Wages of £5. and was Discharge from her sd. Service this present Day And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being Poor & Unable to Maintain the same when born and that one John Dodd a Butcher did Begett the sd. Bastard Child or Children on her Body the sd. John Dodd living a fellow Servt. with this Examint. at the sd. Mr. Lamberts had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the beginning of Augt. last & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with which Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. John Dodd & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne May 7th: 1745 before} Wm: Jones

Rebekar Spike
