Middx.  Frances Beard aged upwards of 50 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Beard who has been dead abt. Eleven Years; says her said husband Rented and lived in a house of £12 a Yr. for three Years in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middx. and quitted same abt. Ten Years before his death and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says she hath not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th. day of Novr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Fras. Beard
