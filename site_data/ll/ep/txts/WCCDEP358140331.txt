Middx.  Elizth. Udell aged about 21 Years upon Oath says she never was Married nor Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her father Thos. Udell lived in and Rented a house in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of £16 for several Years, and that after he quitted said house never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 15th. day of Decr. 1759 . before}

Elizabeth Udell
