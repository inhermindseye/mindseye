Middx  Elizth. Peeke aged upwards of 39 Years upon Oath says she is the wife of Wm. Peeke (gone from her) says she intermarried with the said Wm. Peeke in the Parish Church of Halton in Hamshire on the 7th. day of Novr. abt. 15 Yrs. ago; says her said husband served his Apprentice- -ship of seven Years with one Chas. Burwood a Shoemaker in Palsgrave head Court near Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. and never was a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes as she has been informed and Verily believes says she has three Children by her said husband Namely Mary aged abt. 14 Years Catherine aged abt. 6 Years and Susannah aged abt. three Years and being poor is not able to Maintain herself and them

Sworn this 21st. day of August 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Peeke
