St Clement Danes Febry 6th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  Alice Darcey aged 62 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Michael Darcey at the parish Church of Saint Margaret Westminster several years ago that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented & Resided in an House in Newcastle Court about three years or Upwards at the Annual Rent of £24 being situate in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex that her said Husband Died about 34 or 36 several years ago that since his Death she hath never been Married nor obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right neither did her said Husband after he quitted the said House obtain any legal subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 6th. day of Febry. 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Alice Darcy
