Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Stearn aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with Mr. Roberts at Enfield in the Parish of Endfield in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that She hath quitted said service about four Months since which time she has not done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of Decr. 1789  Philip Dyot D Walker

her Sarah Stearn Mark
