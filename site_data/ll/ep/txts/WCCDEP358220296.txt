Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Isabella Currant Aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of James Currant who has been dead upwards of Three Years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living named Mary Aged about Eight Years Says her said Husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief Says that on the 2d. day of August last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Eleanor Fear in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been baptized by the Name of William Says that one John Mc Daniel a Porter who keeps a House in Parkers Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of December last Past but the Particular day she does not now remember in his the said Mc. Daniels House & that he and no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 21st. day of October 1771 before me R A

her Isabella Currant mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
