Middx.  Eliz: Campbell passed from the Parish of St. Mary Whitechappell by Order Dated the 7th Day of August Instt. aged abt. 60 Years upon her Oath saith that abt. Sixteen Years ago being at that Time a Wido . she was hired to Mr. Peter Hernett in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx to live with him as a Yearly Servt . at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds that this Examinant lived with the sd. Mr. Hemett for the Space of one Year & upwds. abt. Ten Months part thereof in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & 2 Months & upwds. other Part thereof at her sd. Masters in the Parish of St. Mary Le bone in the sd. County of Middx . that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 11th Augt. 1742 before The Lane.

The Mark of Eliz: Campbell
