Middx & Westr} to wit Elizabeth Fluck Aged about Seventy three Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Fluck who died about Thirty years ago, and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said late Husband served seven years Apprenticeship with one Mr Maples a Taylor in Newcastle Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement, neither has this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 27th day of Sepr 1775 before  David Walker

her Elizabeth Fluck Mark
