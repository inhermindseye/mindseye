Middx.  Eliz Lowen upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Wm. Lowen to whom she was married on the 30th. of March 1741 at the Fleet London and that upwds. of 20 Years ago her sd. late husband rented a house of £10 by the Year as the hath then Informed in Bennets Court in the Strand in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and lived therein upwds. of one Year and that her sd. late husband after he quitted that house are this Examinant since his Decease have not Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief and that she hath a Child with her viz Thos . aged abt. 14 Yrs. who is the Son of the above sd. Wm. Lowen by a former Wife.

Sworn March 18th. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz. Lowen
