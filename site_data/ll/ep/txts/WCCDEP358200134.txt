Middx  Wm- Saunderson Aged about 55 Years Upon Oath Says he Rented a House in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Seventeen Pounds hath quitted said house about Twenty Years & hath not since Rented house or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 22d Day of August 1764 Before}
