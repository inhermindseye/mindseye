Middx & Westmr.  Charlotte Read Aged about Thirty Seven years on her Oath says she is the Wife of John Read  gone to Sea with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. James Westminster about Twelve years ago and by whom she hath one Child now living Named Ann Aged about Ten years Says her said Husband Rented & lived in a House in Langley Street Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Field in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of one year at the yearly Rent of £16 paid Parish Taxes due for the same since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement other by Renting House or Tenement of £10 a year or Otherwise Howsoever

Sworn this 22nd. day of Septr. 1778 before  Jno. Barnfather  Geo: Reid

Charlotte Read
