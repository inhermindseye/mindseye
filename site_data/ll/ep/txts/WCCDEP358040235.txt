Middx 

Ann Bell aged abt. 44 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 15 Yrs. ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Antho: Millar a Bookseller at the Corner of Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & Ten Shillings for One Year & upwds. and has not since she quitted her sd. Service Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Sevt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge belief

Sworn Augt. 23d. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Ann Bell her Mark
