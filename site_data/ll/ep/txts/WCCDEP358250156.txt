Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charles George Meadows aged about 60 years upon his Oath saith That he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Wilcox in Jockeys fields in the parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx for about Eighteen Months at the yearly wages of Seven pounds That he hath quitted said Service upwards of Thirty years ago since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1795 } N Bond Edwd: Naish

George Meadows
