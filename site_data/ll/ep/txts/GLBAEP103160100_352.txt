Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Moor the Wife of Richard Moor who is now absent from her maketh oath that about four years ago he rented a Room & shop part of a house in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph with Aldgate in the Said County at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas & resided thereon for the Space of two Months and Carried on therein the Trade of a Gunsmith & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & has not since done any Act to gain another Settlement That she has a Child named Susannah  aged about Six years & half the lawful Issue of this Depont by her Said husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this 3 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizth. Moor
