Middx  Sarah Ashby aged upwds. of 10 Yrs. upon Oath Saith She never was married and that she was hired a Servant for two years certain to Mr Smith a Shoemaker and lived wth. him in the Irongate in the parish of Allhallows in the Town & County of Derby for the sd. term of two Years and had for Such her Service Dyet Washing Lodging & Cloaths and that She has not since she left Mr Smith (which is abt. 5 Yrs. ago) Rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn 29th May 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Sarah Ashby
