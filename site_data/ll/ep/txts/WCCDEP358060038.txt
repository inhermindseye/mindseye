Middx  Sarah Boswell aged about 24 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about Six Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Coles Peruke maker in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds and has not since she quitted the sd. Mrs. Coles Service rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that George Dawson who is at present Sailor on board the Cales Galley did beget the sd. bastard Child or Children on her body the said George Dawson having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant four times in the month of June last at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said George Dawson and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Decr 20th. 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Sarh. Boswell
