Middlesex ss}  Mary Gouger  Singlewoman maketh oath that Several years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with Mr Fenwick of Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at Certain yearly Wages & Continued in such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That she has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor was She ever married nor hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 22. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Mary Gouger
