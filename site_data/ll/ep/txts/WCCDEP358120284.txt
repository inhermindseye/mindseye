Middx  Rachel Hopcroft aged abt. 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servt. with Mr Thompson a Joiner in the Corn market in the Parish of St. Mile's cox at Oxford for four Years and upwards at the yearly Wages of 50 Shillings and has not Since she quitted her sd Service belief is abt. 2 Years ago, Rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she was delivered of a male bastard Child on the 29th. of Janry last in the house of Nurse Davison one of the Parish Nurses of the parish of St Clement Danes which sd. Child has been baptized Wm . and is now living & Chargeable to the said Parish and that Wm. Watkins a ship Carpenter who (as this Examint. has been informed and believes is now a board the fame a merchant Ship which Sailed abt. five months ago for the Madeir as) did beget the said male bastard Child on her body the said Wm Watkins having had

Carnal knowledge of this Examinant Several times in the month of May last at one of which times he got her with Child of the said male bastard Child of which she was delivered as afsd. and that he the said Wm. Watkins and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Feby. 20th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Rachel Hopcroft
