Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Leat aged about 19 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months That her father Andrew Leat Rented and lived in a house at the back of Saint Clements in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for about Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of £20 And this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she this Deponent is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Thomas Oakley an Apprentice to Mr. Benwell a Sadler at No. 4 Bartholomew Lane is the only real and true father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examts Body sometime in or about the Month of Decr last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that he and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 22nd day of Novr. 1794 } J Hind

Mary Leatt

Man taken Paid £20

Release Exted on the 24th. Novr. 1794

Child born in the house  21st. Decr. 1794 & named Thos. Leat

22d. April 1795 Sent to Enfield
