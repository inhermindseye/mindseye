Middlesex ss}  Thomas Martin maketh oath That divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with George Dought near Salt Petre Bank in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex  Sugar Refiner at the yearly Wages of Sixteen Guineas & Continued in such Service for the Space of Three years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house or Shop of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that he hath a Wife named Mary to whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this 9th day of November 1775 before  Wm Blackmore  Geo. C. Smith

Thomas Martin
