Middx.  Frances Spencer aged near 50 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Matthew Spencer deceased and that since his Death She Rented a Tenement called New Inn Coffee House in the Parish of St. Element Danes at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and lived therein upwards of four Years and quitted the same about three Months and hath not since rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 4th day of Septr. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Frances Spencer
