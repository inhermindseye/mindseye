Middlesex ss  Solomon Mc Daniel maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant  by & With Mr Fry a Victualler in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County at Certain Wages and Continued in such Service under such hiring for the space of Fifteen months That he has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or Served any Parish office or rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement out of the Sd Parish

Sworn this 20. day of June 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Solomon Mc Daniel
