Middlesex ss}  Mary Bush the wife of Joseph Bush maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was hired & lived as a hired Servant with one Ford an Hostler in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Coventry in the County of Warwick at certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the space of five Years & upwards & has not Since done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain anothr settlement & that she was lawfully Married to her said husband

Sworn this. 23d. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Bush
