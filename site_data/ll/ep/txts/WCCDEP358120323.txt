Middx  Hannah Dolton aged near 40 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that She lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Wright a Pastry Cook in Old North Street in the parish of St. Geo: the Martyr in the County of Middx for three years at the yearly wages of Four Pounds that She quitted her sd. Service 8 or 9 Years ago and has not Since rented ten Pounds by the year pd. parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one year

Sworn April 8th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Hanh. Dolton
