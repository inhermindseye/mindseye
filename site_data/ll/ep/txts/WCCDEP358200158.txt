Westmr.  Sarah Owen Wife of Hugh Owen Gone from her with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. about upwards of Seven Years ago by whom she has two Children (to wit) Robert Aged about Seven Years & Mary Aged about five Years Says her said Husband Rented the Thistle & Crowne Alehouse in Denmark Court in the said Parish of St. Martin in the Fields at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds Lived in same about five Years hath quitted same upwards of two Years & hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 10th of Novr 1764 Before} Benjamin Cox  Thos: Balack

The Mark of Sarah Owen
