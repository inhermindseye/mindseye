Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Anthony aged about 25 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 25th.. day of July 1790 he Intermarried with Jane Rebecca his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom he hath Four Children living Vizt. Martha Catherine aged about Seven years Joseph aged about Four years and an half Jane Rebecca aged about Five years and an half and Matilda Eleanor aged about Nine Months That he this Deponent Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Edward Justice of Wild Street Printer that he Slept for the last Twelve Months of his said Apprenticeship at the house of a Mr. Battison in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th.. day of July 1798 before us } W Carter

William Anthony
