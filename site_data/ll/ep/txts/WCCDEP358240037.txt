St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. CD  Elizabeth Judd Aged 39 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married, that he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. John Robertson at the Old Swan Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5 that she quitted that Service in or about the Month of April 1788 that since, she hath not (by any way or means whatever) acquired any other legal Settlement And she is Ill, Poor, and in capable of Maintaining herself

Sworn the 11th. July 1791 before me} R Butler

Elizth Judd
