Middx ss  Mary Walker  Wife of Thomas Walker (wch sd. Thomas Walker is now in the Workhouse belongg. to the Parish of St. Margaret in the City & Liberty of Westmr. ) Maketh oath that she has been often informed by her sd. Husband that his Mother rented a house of ten pounds a Year & upwards in thesd. Parish of st. Margaret Westmr. & paid Parish taxes for the Same And by Means thereof in right of his sd. Mother he the sd. Thomas Walker gained a legal Settlement in the sd. Parish Which information this depont. believes to be true And this Dept. doth not know know or hath been informed that her sd Husband hath since gained any Subsequent Settlement And Saith that she hath a Child Named Mary aged about Nine Weeks the lawfull Issue of this Depont. by her sd. Husband

Sworn the 26 day of November 1751 before us  Richd: Ricards  Tho: Quarrills

Mark Mary Walker
