The Examination of Diana Bully Singlewoman taken in Writing upon Oath before me one of his Majestys Justices of the peace for the County of Middlesex residing in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County this 24th. day of July 1746 .

Middx This Examinant Diana Bully maketh Oath and Saith that in or about the Month of December last One Archibald Crawford of Eastsmithfield in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Marriner did prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at the house of John Milner of Eastsmithfield in the parish of Aldgate aforesaid and had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and so had several times at one of which times he did beget her with Child or Children of which She now goeth and is Pregnant And this Examinant saith that the said Child or Children of which She now goeth

and is Pregnant as aforesaid is or are likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx She being poor and not able to provide for the same And that the said Archibald Crawford is the true and only father of the said Child or Children so likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards aforesaid And Lastly that this Examination is Voluntary And that She hath not been forced or Compelled to be Examined by any Person or Persons whatsoever

Sworn this 24th. day of July 1746 Before Robert Dennett

her Diana Bully Mark
