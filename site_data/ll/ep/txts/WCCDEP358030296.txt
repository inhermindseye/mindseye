Westmr  Mary Fitzackerley aged about upwds. 40 Years upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Wilcox a Clear Starcher at Mr. Johnsons an Oylshop in Maddock Street in the Parish of St: George in Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds for the Space of Three Years & upwards & hath left her said Service about two Years Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mrs. Wilcox Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 26th. 1746 before  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Mary Fitzackerley
