Middx  Chas Thorne aged 35 Years upon his Oath Saith he was bound apprentice for Seven Years to Wm Thorne a Bookbinder and Served the sd Wm Thorne as his apprentice about 3 Years during which time he lodged in his said Masters house in St Clements Church Yard in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex that at the End of the sd. three Years his sd master failed & this Examint. never was assigned over nor ever Rented ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes nor lived a Yearly Servt for one year

Sworn Decr. 11th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Chas Thorne
