Middx  Roger Wheatley Maketh Oath that abt. 32 Twenty eight years ago he was hired a yearly Servant (when he was a Singleman) to Jer Aisleby  Esqr . in Stretton Street in the parish of St. George, Hanr. Square in ye libty Westmr Middx and Served him as such five years and Reced Seven pounds a year wages And this Deponent Saith he hath gained no Subsequent Settlement to his Knowledge and that he hath two Daughters Elizabeth aged abt. 29 years and Mary aged abt. 23 years Elizabeth by his porte former wife  Mary Deced & Mary by his late wife  Mary And that his said Daughters never gained any other Settlement than in right of this Depot.

Sworn this 4th. March 1742 before Rd: Ricards

his Roger Wheatley  Mark
