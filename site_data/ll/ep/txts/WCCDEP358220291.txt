Westmr. to wit Jane Tandy Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Joseph Wilton  Esqr . in Great Portland Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 15th. day of Octr 1771 before Us} Tho. Kynaston R. Butler

Jane Tandy
