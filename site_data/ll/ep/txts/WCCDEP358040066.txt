Westmr.  Mary Roades upon Oath saith she never was married Says she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at Mr. Cannons in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster on the 17th. Day of Augt. 1746 which has been Baptized Mary and is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same and that one Edward Newberry a Servant to Mr. Harcourt in Bedford Row is the Reputed father this Examinant having lived a fellow Servt. with the sd. Edward Newberry at Mr. Harcourts aforesd. there he became acquainted with her and had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the month of November 1745 & at sevll. other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child of which she was Delivered as aforesaid and that he the said Edward Newberry & no other Person is the true and reall father thereof

Sworn Septr. 21st 1747 before} Tho: Lane  Tho: Burdus

Mary Roades
