Middx to wit Catharine Neff aged about 23 Years On her Oath says she never was Married rented, House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Montgomery a hair Merchant in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about a Year and a half ago Since which time she hath gained no Other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath says she was delived of a Female Bastard Child on the 4th. of Febry last in Store Street Hospital which hath been Baptized Mary which Hospital by Act of parliamt. is priviledge from any Child being born therein from gaining any Settlement And further says that One James Hobbs a Hair Dresser who Lodges at One Mr. Shepherds who keeps a public House in Cross Street Carnaby Markett is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her last Masters House in Carey Street some time in the Month of June last but the particular day she does not now Remember and at Sev Different times afterwards at One of Which times he get her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of Whe she was so delivered as afrsd. And that the sd. Jas. Hobbs and no Other person did beget the same And that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 2d. Day of March before R Butler

her Catherine Neff Mark
