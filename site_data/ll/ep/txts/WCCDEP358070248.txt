Westmr to wit Elizabeth Francis Aged about Twenty two Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with One Mr George Roberts Senr (Now dead) A piecebroker in White horse Yard Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the liberty of Westminster upwards of one Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds hath Quitted her said Service upwards of five Months ago and hath not since she Quitted the same ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement; And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children that is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born & says that one George Roberts Junior who now lives with his Mother Mrs Roberts piecebroker in Whitehorse Yard Drury Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of August last (but the particular day she does not now remember) at his said Mothers house and at several times since at one of which times or times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said George Roberts Junior and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 20th day of Feby 1775 before W Addington

E Francies
