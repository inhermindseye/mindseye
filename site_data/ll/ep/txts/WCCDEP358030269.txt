Westmr. (to wit)  Eliz. Wadsworth aged abt. 44 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with Wm. Wadsworth at the Fleet London on the 10th. Day of March abt. ten Years since to the best of her remembrance Says her sd. husband is on Boardan Hospitall Ship at Plymouth as she hath been informed & beleives Says her sd. husband was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentisp with one Mr. Withers in Blackman Street in the Parish of St. Mary at Newington in the County of Surry Says her sd. husbd. never rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 28th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz Wadsworth
