Middx  Elizabeth Harley aged about 22 years upon Oath says that She Intermarryed with Alexander Harley (who is now a Marine in his Majesty's Service) the 22d. Octr. 1748 Says that her said Husband was born at Sea, That his Father was a Scotchman & his Mother an Irishwoman and never gained any Settlement in England to this Examinant's knowledge or belief and that her said Husband never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant nor Rented £10 by the Year to this Examinant's knowledge or belief Says that She hath one Child by him namely Alexander Harley aged 10 Weeks That She never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant whilst a Singlewoman Says that her Father John Mann Rented a House in Charles Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of £16. and lived therein about a year & half and Quitted the same about 21 years as her sd. Father has informed her & as She verily believes

Sworn the 12th. Septr. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond  Saunder Welch

Elizabeth Herley
