Middx.  Mary Blow aged abt. 44 Years upon Oath says She is the Wife of Wm: Blow to whom she was married in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields abt. Twenty one years ago. Says her said husband Rented a house in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Parish of St. Clement Danes County of Middx. of the Yearly of £30 or there abouts for 3 yrs. And hath Quitted said house abt. Seventeen Years & hath not since Rented a house of Ten Pounds by the year or paid Parish Taxes. Says her sd. husband Refuses to do any thing for her whereby She is become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes .

Sworn Augst. 19th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Blow
