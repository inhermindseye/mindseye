Middlesex ss}  Ann Gates  singlewoman Voluntarily maketh oath that sometime Ago she was hired and lived as a hired servant by the year at the yearly Wages of the Six Pounds With sir  George Kelly in the Parish of Speldherst in the County of Kent and Continued in such service for the space of one year and upwards & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That she this Deponent has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement That she this Deponent hath a Child Aged about four Weeks born in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & Baptized & registred in the Church belonging to the Same Parish by the name of William Gates the Which Child is illegitimate and that one Andrew Brograve late of Aldgate High Street  shopman did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Sworn this 5th. day of July 1773 before  Burf Camper  Jno Sherwood

Ann Gates Her Mark
