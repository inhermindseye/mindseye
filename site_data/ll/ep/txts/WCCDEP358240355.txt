St Clement Danes 16th. Novr 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary Magdalen Milk Street London  (to wit) Mary Kendal  singlewoman  aged twenty six six years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Davis of Milk Street  Cheapside in the parish of St. Mary Magdalen  Milk Street in the City of London for a year or upwards at the Annual Wages of £4:10s that the last two Months of Such her service she lodged at her Mistress Apartment in Milk Street within the said parish of Mar St Mary Magdalen to the best of her Recollection that She quitted her said Mistress about three or four years Ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Examinants knowledge or belief And that She

Sworn the 16th day of November 1793 before us N Bond B: Kennett

Mary Kendal 's Mark

Witness Garrett
