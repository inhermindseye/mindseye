Middlesex ss}  Robert Nightingale a Singleman Aged about Forty eight Years Maketh oath That divers Years ago he was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one John Furnace a Farmer in the Parish of King Rippon in the County of Huntingdon with whom he lived one whole year at Certain Yearly Wages and this Depont Saith that he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement in

Sworn this 28th day of Janry 1772 before  John Smart

The Mark of Robert Nightingale
