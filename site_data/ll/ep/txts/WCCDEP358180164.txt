Rebecca Hodges aged about 60 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mrs. Hale in Norfolk Street & Essex Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of £5. 10s. for the Space of Two Years & hath left her sd. Service abt. 24 Years, since which time she hath not rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife that being afflicted with the Stone & Gra & Consulsions on her Nerves she is at present Incapable of Maintaining herself

Sworne 3d. Febry 1743 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Rebecca Hodges
