Middlesex ss}  Catherine Christian maketh oath that her late Fathr Patrick Christian deced heretofore rented a house in Gravel Lane at the Yearly Rent of Eight Pounds in the Parish of St Saviour in Southwarke in the County of Surry where this Depont lived with him as part of his Family & in which Parish She this Depont was born That during Such her Fathers residence in the Sd house he was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates And Kings Taxes for the Same & died there legally Settled That She this Depont never lived as a hired Servant in any one Place for a year nor ever was bound an Apprentice nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 13. day of Janry 1787 before R Davies Robt: Smith

The Mark of Catherine Christian
