Middx  John Adamson maketh Oath and Saith that about Thirty Three Years ago when he was a Singleman he was hired a Yearly Servant to Mr . John Masden of the parish of St. Bride in the City of Chester and lived with as such there five Years and upwards and received four pounds a Year for the same And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And hath a Wife named Eleanor

Sworn this 6th. day of February 1745 Before Robert Dennett

his John Adamson Mark
