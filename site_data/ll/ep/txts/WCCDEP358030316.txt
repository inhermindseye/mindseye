Westmr. 

Humphry Williams upon Oath Says he intermarried with his Wife Eliz. Williams in the Parish Church of St. Sepalchers London about 20 Years ago Says he rented a house of Ten pounds a Year in Feathers Count in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr. for Seven Years or thereabouts & pd. his Share of all Parochial Taxes & hath left same upwds.of 20 Yrs. Says he hath not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Yrs. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. any other Place to his knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 10th 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Humphry Williams
