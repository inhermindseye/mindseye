Middlesex & Westmr}  Mary Walford Aged about 27 years on her Oath says she never was Married nor ever was an Apprentice or a yearly hired Servant for one year says she has been Informed and which Information she verily believeth to be true that her Father Jonathan Walford deceased served three years the Last Part of his Apprentice -ship with Mr. Jaques at the Castle in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born says that William Arman Servant to one Mr. Bethen a Cow-keeper at Walworth in the County of Surry had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of April last and Several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which is now Pregnant and that the said William Arman and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 27th day of November 1769 before me}

her Mary Walford mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
