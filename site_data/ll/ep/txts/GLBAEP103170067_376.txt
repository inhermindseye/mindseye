Middlesex ss}  Sarah Jones the widow of Roger Jones deced maketh oath that Sometime ago her said husband was hired & lived as a hired Covenant Servant by the year at Certain wages by & with William Frost of Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County Taylor & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement to her knowledge or belief nor hath She this Deponent Since his Death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She hath a Child named Sarah Jones aged about four years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said late husband to whom She was lawfully married.

Sworn this. 21. of March 1777 before - arkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

Sarah Jones
