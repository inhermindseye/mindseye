Middx  Eliz Metcalf aged upwds. of 20 yrs. upon Oath saith She was married at the Fleet London to her late husband Jno. Metcalf abt. 8 Yrs. ago. that her sd. late husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlemt. in England to her Knowledge or belief and that she (this Examinant) before her sd. Intermarriage lived and whilst a Singlewoman lived a yearly Servt. wth. Mrs. Dennis at the yearly wages of One pound ten Shillings for two Years and upwards and lodged during all the sd. time with her sd. mistress at the Corner of Newcastle Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx that she quitted her sd. Service abt. 10 Yrs. ago & never afterwards rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year and that she has 4 Childn. Mary aged 7 Yrs. Eliz aged 4 Yrs. & Thos. aged 2 Yrs. & Ann aged 4 Months

Sworn June 5th 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz Metcalf
