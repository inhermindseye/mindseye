Middlesex ss}  Ann Smith  Single woman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one Mr Chamberlain in Hounds ditch in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in ye City of London  Cock Founder & Continued in Such Service at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds for the Space of one Year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Depont. has not Since lived elsewhere as a hired Servant for the Space of a Year nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th day of November 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith  Wm: Blackmore

The Mark of Ann Smith
