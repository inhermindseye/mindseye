Westmr. ss  Jane Evans Aged about Thirty Two Years Wife of John Evans gone from her on behalf of Peter and Fanny Rotchett her Children Vizt. Peter Aged about 11 Years and Fanny Aged about Seven Years by a former Husband upon Oath Says that her said late Husband John Rotchett Father of the said Peter & Fanny Rotchett Rented and lived in a House in Hemmings's Court in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and upwards and where the said Peter & Fanny Rotchett were both born and further says that after her said late Husband quitted the said House which is upwards of Ten Years ago he never afterwards Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 22d. day of Sepr. 1769 beofore Us }  R Butler

Jane Evans
