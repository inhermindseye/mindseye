Westmr. to wit Sarah Boulter Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with with one John Henry at the Golden Hart in Parkers Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes.

Sworn this 7th. day of December 1771 before W Kelynge R. Butler

her Sarah Boulter mark
