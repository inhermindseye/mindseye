Westmr.  Richard Carrier Aged about Fifty Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Mary his Wife at St. Bride's Church London about Five Years ago, says about Thirty Three Years ago being a Single Man and had never been married, he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Stanley  Grocer and Confectioner in Ludgate Street in the Parish of St. Martin Ludgate in the City of London for Two Years and Upwards, at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds, and hath not since he quitted said Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn the 23d. day of December 1767 before}  John Machin

Richard Carryer
