Middx  The Examination of Mary Wright on the behalfe of Mary Sax  Widw . of Jno. Sax Deced Taken the 7th. Day of June 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that the above named Jno. Sax lived in & Rented a House in Chequer Alley in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of five Pounds & lived in the same for the space of 30 Years & there aged & During all or Part of that time was assessed and Paid towards the necessary Releife of the Poor of the said Parish as this Examint. hath been informed & wch she verily beleives And this Examint. further saith that the sd. Mary Sax hath not since the Death of her sd. Husbd. Jno. Sax Rented any other House Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & yt. she is now Sick & Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous  Samuel Clarke

Mary Wright
