Middlesex ss}  Hannah Young  Singlewoman Maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Yearly Wages with one Valentine Harris in the Parish of saint Martin in the Town of Plymouth in the County of Devon . Mariner and Continued there in Such Service for the Space of one Year & upwards & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that She is about Thirty eight Years old

Sworn this day of January 1772 Wilmot

Hannah Young
