Middlesex ss}  Catherine Gerrard maketh oath that She was heretofore hired and lived as a yearly hired Servant by & with Captain Knight of Burr Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & after living there with her said Master for the space of Six months She this Deponent with her said Master removed from thence to the house of one Mr Hoppey in the Minories in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London where She Continued with her Said Master and in his Service for the space of Nine Months and received after the rate of Seven Pounds a year for Wages for the last twelve Months That She has not since lived as a hired Servant in any one Place for the Space of a year nor been married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 6. day of May 1788 before R Davies Robt Smith

Catherine Gerrard
