Middlesex ss}  Sarah Kennedy the wife of Timothy Kennedy maketh oath that her Sd husband is lately gone away from her to places to her unknown That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with Nathaniel Gave a Butcher in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the Sd County & to her knowledge or belief her Sd husband never rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever served any parish Office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship And that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this 17 day of December 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Sarah Kennedy
