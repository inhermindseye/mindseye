Middlesex ss}  Sarah Emmerton maketh oath that She was heretofore lawfully Married to one Robert Emmerton Since deced That her Said late husband (as he has informed her & which She believes to be true) that he was hired & lived as a hired Servant with this Deponts Mother Ruth Chandler in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County at Certain Wages and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for Three years & did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Serve any Parish Office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor hath She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this. 13. day of Janry 1784 before R Davies

Sarah Emerton

To her Husbands Settlemt. By Servitude Before Marriage
