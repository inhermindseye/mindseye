Middx  Mary Kew aged upwds. of 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England and further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes  Middx (where she at present lives) she being poor and unable to maintain the Same when born and that John Caper  Journeyman to Mr Butler Apothecary near Temple bar did beget the said bastard Child or Childn. on her body this Examinant at that time living a Servt at the Said Mr Butlers; there the sd. John Caper had carnal knowledge of her body in August last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said John Caper and no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn May 1st. 1752 before W Fielding

The Mark of Mary Kew
