Middlesex ss  John Randole maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound & assigned Apprentice to Thomas Waite of East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Cabinet maker for the Term of Three years being the Remr of his said Apprenticeship & Served the whole thereof & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Depont has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 16th. day of December 1774 before  Burf Camper

John Randoll
