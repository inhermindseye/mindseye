Middx  Elizabeth Yates aged 35 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thomas Yates who died on the 16th. day of October 1760 by whom she hath One Child named Sarah aged about Nine Years says her said late Husband whilst a Singleman lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. David Thomas in Clement's Inn in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx for several Years as appears by a Certificate under the hand of the said Mr. Thomas dated the 26th. November 1760 says her said Husband in his life time nor she since his Death never Rented any House

Sworn the 10th. day of April 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eliz: Yates
