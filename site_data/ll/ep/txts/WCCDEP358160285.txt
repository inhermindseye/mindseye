Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Hill  Wido . of John Hill living in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middx taken the 21st. Day of March 1740 Who upon her Oath Saith that abt. 25 Years ago her sd. late Husband Jno. Hill was bound an Apprentice & served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Plunkett Upholster in St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westminister & further on her Oath saith that her sd. Husband in his Life Time nor hath She this Examinant since his Death ever rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Eliz Hill
