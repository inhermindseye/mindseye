Middx  Ann Fowler upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband George Fowler at Alverstoke in the County of Hants upwds. of 30 Years ago and that her sd. husband at Christmas last took a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds in Fleet Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchers Londn & has Continued to live in & Rent the sd house ever since

Sworn Feby. 21st 1753 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Ann Fowler
