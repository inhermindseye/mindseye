St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Ann Tilderley Aged twenty two years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she was Articled, for three years to a Mr Bage in Aldersgate Street in the parish of St Botolph without Aldersgate London Mantua maker that she served under Indentures such Agreement near two year and lodged in her Mistress House during such Service and hath never obtained any subsequent legal Settlemt to the best of her knowledge or belief And this Deponent further on her Oath saith that on the third sixth day of October 1788 at the a House Mr Dan Stevens Schoolmaster the Coiner of Newcastle Street in Wych Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster , she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child wch which hath since been Christened by the Name of Thomas Frede Frederick Washborn And saith that the said Child is become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes And that one Robert Washborn who now Resides at Mr Ravens in Portugal Street in the said parish is the only real & true father of such Child of Which she was so delivered as aforesaid he having, had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body about five years ago and at several times since at some one of which times he did beget the said Child thereon And this Deponent further saith that she was also delivered of another Male Bastard Child which on the 24th February last at Mr Elsworth's in Clare Street in the said parish which was after

Christened, half Baptised by Name of Robert Washborn who Died on the 23d day of March last And that she the said Robert Wash born now living at Mr Raven's as aforesaid was the only real and true father of such last mentioned Child, he & no one else having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same thereon, And this Depont. further saith that the Bill for the Funeral of the said last mentioned Child is now remaing due, and unpaid she this Deponent is unable to satisfy the same

Sworn the 10th day of April 1792 before me} R Butler

Ann Tilderley

12th  April Wash born Apprehended

13th. he paid Messrs Hogg & Richardson £20 Exted Rele & they Signed Rect. the parish to pay all Expences

April 13th. The Mother has the Child, she lodges at Mr Elsworth's Clare Street
