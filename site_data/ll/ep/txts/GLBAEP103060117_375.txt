Middx ss  Mary Beacher  Widow maketh oath that her husband Edward Beacher Deced was bound apprentice to Anthony Ireland of the Hamlet of Mile End old Town in the County of Middx  Ironmonger & Smith for Seven Years and Served his Said Master During the said Term And this Depont. Saith that her Sd. husband never Served any parish officers nor Rented a House of Ten pounds a year nor never gained any other Settlemt than as abovemenconed to the knowledge or belief of this Depont.

Sworn this 13th Day of June 1749 before  Richd Ricards  Saml Gower

The Mark of Mary Beacher
