Middlesex ss.  Mary Dimmock the Widow of John Dimmock maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex about Twenty Seven Years ago and that her said late Husband has informed her that he was duly Bound Apprentice by Indenture to his Uncle Leech of the Parish of Streatham in the County of Cambridge  Bricklayer , where and with whom he duly served his said Apprenticeship that her said late Husband has never Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement, And further saith that since her said Husbands Death she has not done any Act to her knowledge or Belief to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this Day of Janry. 1793 before}
