Middx The Examination of Ann Meads  Singlewoman Passed from the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in ye. County of Middx by Order bearing date ye. 4th. Day of Decr. Inst. Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith yt. she is Pregnt. with Child or Childn. wch when Born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. - . County of Middx aforesd. she being Poor & not able to maintaine ye. same wn Born And further upon her Oath saith yt. one Thos. Whitebread  Carter to one Mr. Stanley in Knightsbridge did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body they at such time Lodging together for ye. space of Six Months or thereabouts in Tyburn Lane where he the sd. Thos. Whitebread had Carnall knowledge of her Body Sevll. times at one of wch times he gott her with ye. Bastard Child or Childn. with wch she is now Pregnt. & yt. he ye. sd. Thos. Whitebread is the true & reall Father thereof And this Examint. further saith yt. she was Bound an Apprentice to one Mrs. Cole in Portugall Street in ye. sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Milkwoman with whom this Examint. lived with for the space of one Year or thereabts. and yn. this Examints. sd. Mistress sent her to her Mother with whom she lived at ye. Bull Head in May fair in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square aforesaid for ye. space of one Year & yn. leaving her sd. Mother went & provided for herselfe & so Continues to do And yt. she hath not since she left her sd. Mistress rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. for ye. space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworne ye. 12th. Day of Decr. 1739 before} Jas Gronous

The mark of Ann Meads
