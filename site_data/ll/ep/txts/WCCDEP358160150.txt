Middx  The Examination of Judith Richardson  Widw . of Nicholas Richardson Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 11th. Day of July 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. on ye. 22d. Day of July 1728 she Intermarryed with ye. above sd. Nichs. Richardson that her sd. Husband was Born in the Parish of Essington in the County of Hertford that he never Rented £10 a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as this Examint. hath been Informed & verily beleives, And this Examint. further saith yt. her sd. late Husband was a Soldier in Captn . Wm. Daws Company of Foot yt. he has been Dead about Six Months & haveing left her Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Chas: Egerton

The Mark of Judith Richardson
