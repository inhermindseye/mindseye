Middx  The Examination of Mary the Wife of Robert Onslow now living in the Parish of St- Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 28th. Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about four Years ago her said Husband Robert Onslow rented a house in Yeates Court in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the yearly Rent of twelve Pounds lived in the same for the Space of four Years & upwards And further upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband hath not since that time rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}

The Mark of Mary Onslow
