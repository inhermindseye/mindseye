Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Wheeler  of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Robert Pell Esqr one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County of Middlesex this 26. day of July 1769 Who on her oath Saith That She is now with Child And that the said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that John Richey of Deptford in the County of Kent  Mariner is the Father of the said Child where with She now goeth & is Pregnant

Sworn this 26 day of July 1769 before R Pell

The Mark of Sarah Wheeler
