Middlesex ss}  Mary Chambers the widow of Edward Chambers deced maketh oath that about twelve years ago her said late husband rented a house of the yearly rent off Five Pounds in that Part of West Lane which live in the parish of Saint Mary magdalen Bermondsey  in the County of Surry Resided there in for about three years & during Such his Residence he was charged to and paid King Taxes for the same to one Mr. Coats a Collector of the Said Tax for the said Parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her said husband did not afterwards rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain anothr Settlement Nor hath she this Deponent Since his death been married or lived as a hired Servant for a year or done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that she was married to her sd late husband about forty year ago at the Flect

Sworn this 25 day of Novemr 1777 before P. Rowlands Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Mary Chambers
