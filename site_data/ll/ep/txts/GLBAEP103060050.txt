Middx  John Burtwell maketh Oath and Saith and Saith that about Thirty Eight Years he was Bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to Mr . Edward Drainer of Lombard Street in the parish of St. Mary Woolnoth in the City of London  Vintner and served all his Apprentiship there and has gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And that he hath a Wife named Frances .

Sworn this 13th. day of August 1745 Before Robert Dennett

John Burtwell
