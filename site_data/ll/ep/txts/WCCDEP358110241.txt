Middx  Susa Kelsey aged 15 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant for three Years with Wido Lane at Saltsford in the High Parish at Warwick at the Yearly Wages of thirty Shillings & quitted her sd. Service about one Year ago

Sworn May 1st. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Susa. Kelsey
