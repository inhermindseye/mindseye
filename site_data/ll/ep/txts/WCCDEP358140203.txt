Middx.  Rebecca Herring aged abt. 22 Years upon Oath says she Never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Alexr. Robinson at the Lemon tree in Bedford Bury in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for fourteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and hath quitted sd. Service abt. Six Months and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 21st. day of Decr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Reba. Herring
