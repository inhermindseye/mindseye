Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit John Pritchard aged about 78 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Peter in the City of Worcester That he rented and lived in a House in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for four years at the yearly Rent of £15 & paid Taxes due for the same that he hath quitted said House about 15 years since which time he has not rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Novr. 1787 R Buller

his John Pritchard Mark
