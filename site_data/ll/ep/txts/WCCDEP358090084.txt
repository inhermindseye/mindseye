Middx to wit Mary Evetts aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr John Moody a Player at No. 8 New Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upwards of Five Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about fourteen Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish (she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born) says that the said John Moody her said late Master did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge thereof at his the said Moodys Chambers upwards of Two Years last past at one of which times he got her with child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said John Moody & no other Person did beget the same and that he is real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 2d. day of Octr.1779 before M Addington

her Mary Evetts Mark
