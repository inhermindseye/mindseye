Middx to wit Elizabeth Yarwood Aged about Eighteen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Jaques at the Spotted Dog in Holywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Eighteen Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 2d. day of April Instant she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Work house belonging to the said Parish which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Martha and is now Chargable to the said Parish and likely to Continue so she being Poor and unable to maintain the same And further says that one William Harby a Hair worker in Essex Street in Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his the sd. Mr. Harbys House the begining of August last and at one other time afterwards at one of which times she got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said William Harby & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & two Father thereof

Sworn this 29th. day of April 1780 before  Abrm Baley

her Elizth. Yarwood Mark
