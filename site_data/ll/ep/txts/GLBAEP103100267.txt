Middlesex ss  Frances Robertson  Singlewoman Maketh oath That divers years ago she was hired and lived as a hired servant by the year with one Mr Lane a Merchant in Scotch yard in the Parish of St. Mary Bothaw in the City of London and continued in such Service for the Space of three years at the yearly wages of Five Pounds and by means thereof as she is advised gained a legal settlem there and this Depont. Saith that she hath not since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor Rented a house of the yearly Rent or value of Ten Pounds nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor hath the been ever Married Nor to her knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to her Servitude as above

Sworn this 24th. Day of April 1770 before  Chris: Scott  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Frances Robertson
