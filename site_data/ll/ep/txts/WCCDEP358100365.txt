Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to Richmond to wit Martha Tilt aged about 23 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs. Collick of Richmond in the Parish of Richmond in the County of Surrey for fifteen Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted said Service about four Months ago since which time She has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 21st. day of February 1785 G Mercer James Paine

her Martha Tilt mark
