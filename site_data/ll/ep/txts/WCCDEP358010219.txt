Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit, Margaret Easton  Singlewoman aged forty five Years or thereabout upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Lathram a Taylor in Newcastle Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds for about a Year and an half-that she quitted his Service about Seventeen Years ago and that since her quitting the same she has not to the best of her knowledge Information or belief done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement in any the Parish than the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 13th. June 1788 before me R Butler

Margret Easton
