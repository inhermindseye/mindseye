St. Clement Danes

Ann Gibson aged 16 Years or thereabouts on her Oath upon her Oath saith That  never was Married Rented a House  ever Paid Taxes or ever served an Apprenticeship or ever lived as an  hired yearly Servant for 12 Months saith that she was born in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell and that after he that this Examinants  Father Henry Gibson  Rents and lives in a House in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of Nine Gunieas that he has lived in the same upwards of 7 years and Pays the Kings Tax for the Same but she this Examinant believes that he is not Rated or pays to the Pop find this Examinant Saith that she has not according to the best of her Knowledge information or belief gained any Settlemt. in her own Right

Sworn this 24th. day of Novr. 1785 before us}
