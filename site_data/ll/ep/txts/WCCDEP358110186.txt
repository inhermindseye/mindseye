Westmr.  Martha Waters upon Oath saith she was married to Thomas Waters at the Parish Church of St. Mary le Strand about Eight Years ago by whom she has two Children Eliz aged four Years and Charles abt. 18 months and that her sd. husband Served his Apprentiship with his father John Waters a Waterman in Feathers Court in the parish of St Mary le Strand aforesd. and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Feby. 21st. 1753 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Martha Waters
