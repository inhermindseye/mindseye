John Aylward aged 75 years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he Rented and lived in a House in North Street in the Parish of Saint Peter Colchester in the County of Essex for near Forty Years at the which House of this was this Examinants which and was Rated only at £4 a year that he paid all Manner of Poors Rate and other Taxes that he quitted his said House about 28 Years ago since which time he has not rented a House or Tenement of £10 a Year  Paid Taxes or done any other act Matter or Thing whereby so the best of his Knowledge Information or Belief he may have gained as Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 15 day of Decr. 1784 }
