St. Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Hastings aged about 34 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Eleanor his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Brides about Twelve years ago That he served Seven Years Apprenticeship to a Mr. Simmons a Cooper in Fetter Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Bavs in the City of London that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he a Never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th day of Decr. 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

John Hastings
