Middx  Thos. Turpin Aged abt. Forty Six Years Upon Oath Says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to his father Jno. Turpin in the Parish of Haddenham in the County of Bucks & served him as an Apprentice in the sd. Parish of Haddenham upwards of four Years & then his sd. father Dyed Says he never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt.

Sworn the 13th. day of Novr. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Thos. Turpin
