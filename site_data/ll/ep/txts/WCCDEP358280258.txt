Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Amy Sidney aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that on the Fifth day of May last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Tully in Alphabet Court Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Ann Harriott That one William Heath a Writer who lately worked with Mr. Bake in Boswell Court and lodged near Rolls Buildings is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said William Heath is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 16th. day of Decr. 1789 .} R Butler

Amy Sidney

Amy Sidney lodges at the YorkshireGrey Rolls Buildings

Wm. Heath is about 5 feet high Dark Complexion wears his hair Tied behind

Wart. Exted. he disd.
