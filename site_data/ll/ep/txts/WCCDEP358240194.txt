St. Clement Danes 26th January 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St C D  (to wit ) Elizabeth Hill aged 45 years upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband John at the parish Church of Saint James Westminster about 29 years ago that her said Husband hired and Resided in an House or Apartment in Newcastle Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the parish as Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for upwards of six Months at the Annual Rent of £22 or upwards that he quitted that House about January 1792 and did not afterwards during his Life time Rent any House or Apartment of £10 a year pay any parish Taxes or otherwise obtain any subsequent legal Settlement that he Died in Prison on or about the 31st Decr. last and that She hath six Children by her said Husband now living four of which have obtained Settlements in then own Right or are provided for & the other two with this Examinant Namely Mary aged ten years in or about December last & George aged Nine years sometime in or about since or July 1792 that she is now pregnant with Child by her said Husband and unable during her pregnancy

to provide for herself & Children without assistance

Sworn the 25th day of January 1793 before Me } D Walker

E Hill
