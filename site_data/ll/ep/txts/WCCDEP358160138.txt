Middx  The Examination of Ann Clements the Wife of Paul Clements Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 5th. Day of May 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that her said Husband served three Months the latter part of his Apprentispp with one Wm. Watts  Esqr . in Pater Noster Row in the Parish of St. Gregory by St. Paul London And this Examint. further upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband hath not since he left his said Master Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife that he is now on Board his Majestys Ship the Grantham Grafton And she being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Samuel Clarke  Chas: Egerton

Ann Clemments
