Westmr. & Middx}  John Allmett of Hemlock Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes  in the maketh Oath & Saith, That upon Enquiry he has been informed, That Margaret Dokens . and Mary Dokens Two Children of Lewis Francis Dokens were passed from the Parish of St. Mary Matfelon otherwise White Chapple to the Parish of Saint James Westminster , that they were in St James's Workhouse for a long Space of Time that the Father took Them from thence and kept Them sometime but being out of employs carried Them to the Workhouse of St. James's again, That Since that he has taken em out and put Them to N Board in Bear paid in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes that he has deserted em and left Them a Charge upon

the said Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn this 19th February 1785 . before us  Wm Gascoigne  James Croft

John Allmett

These Children having been passed sometime ago from White Chapple to St. James &, St. James's Appealed and prevailed in the appeal, Their Settlement is in Yorkshire therefore wish to have the Man Apprehended & Request a warrant upon the above Examination him for deserting his Children
