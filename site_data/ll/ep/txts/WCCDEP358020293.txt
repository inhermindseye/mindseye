St Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster (to wit) Mary Perry Aged Sixteen years and upwards upon her Oath saith that she never was Marrie Married nor did any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby she may have acquired any legal settlement in her own Right that she her hath heard her late Father James Perry (who died about 15 Months ago) say that he Rented and Resided in a House at the bottom of Princes Street in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the said County & that he paid Poor Rate for the same during such Occupancy at the Annual Rent of £6 which she verily believes to be true And this Examinant further saith that she perfectly Remembers living with her said Father in the said House and seeing the Officers coming to Collect the Poor Rate and being paid for the same And this Examinant further saith she is poor and unable to maintain herself and that he r never to her knowledge or best belief acquired Subsequent legal settlement

Sworn the 1st. June 1791 before us  Sampn Wright N Bond

Mary Perry 's Mark
