Middlesex ss}  Richard Palmer maketh oath That divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired servant by the Year at Certain Wages with John Ditcher of Salisbury Court in the Parish of St Bridget in the City of London Tobacconist & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two Years upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year elsewhere or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19 day of September 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey  Jno Sherwood

Richard Palmer
