Middx  Jane Fulton of the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. Widow upon Oath saith she has Seen a female Child (lately dropt in the parish of St Clement Danes  Middlesex ) and knows it to be the Child of Ann Smith (who cannot be found) and that the sd. Ann Smith was delivered of the sd. female Child about five weeks since at Mr Brookers a Butcher at the Corner of Dukes Court in Drury Lane in the parish of St. Martin in the fields aforesaid & was baptized Elizabeth }

Sworn April 1752 before W Fielding

The Mark of Jane Fulton
