Westmr  Elizth Davis Aged about 45 Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Richard Marly  Esqr . in Tooks Court Cursitors Street in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx Four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath Quitted her said Service about Four Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 5th of January 1764 Before}

Upon Mr Ralph Williams Complt Ovrs J. W
