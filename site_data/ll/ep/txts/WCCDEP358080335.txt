Middx  Susannah Watson Aged about 27 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Mr. Porter a Cook in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of three Years at the Yearly Wages of £4..10s..0d hath quitted her said service about Ten Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Oath says that in the Month of March 1772 she was brought to bed of a Female Bastard Child in the House of one Farrall a Cork Cutter in Clements Lane which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Sarah Ann And that in the Month of Decr. 1775 she was delivered of another Female Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. Holton in Wych Street which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Mary both of which are likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same and that one John Hicks a Copper Plate Printer who now works at one Mr. Hambletons in St- John's Passage near Hicks's Hall is the Father of Both the said Bastard Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body for Ten Years last past and that the said John Hicks and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 22nd- day of Febry 1779 before}

her Susannah Watson Mark
