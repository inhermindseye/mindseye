Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Edmund Murphy aged about 65 years upon his Oath saith that in the year 1749 he Intermarried with Anne his present Wife at the Parish Church of St. Mary Pickworth in the County of Lincoln that he this Ext. Rented and lived in a House in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 8 years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of £10 and paid Taxes due for the same that he hath quitted said House about 8 years ago since which time he never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Septr. 1787 R Butler

her Edmund Murphy Mark
