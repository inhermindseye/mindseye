Middx & Westmr}  Mary Buttery aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Henry Winchester a Butcher in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. One Year and Seven Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further Says that on the 22d. day of April 1769 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of one Mrs. Cribb who keeps a Chandlers Shop in Clements Lane aforesaid which hath since been baptized by the Name of Susannah Says that one Thos. Wood a Journey Butcher who now lives with one Mr. Kirk a Butcher in Gilberts Passage Clare Markett is the Father of the said Female Bastard Child he having had Carnal Knowledge of her on the 22d. day of July 1768 and at sevl. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as afsd. and that the said Thos: Wood and no other person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn this 14th. day of March 1770 before} Tho Fellows

Mary Buttery
