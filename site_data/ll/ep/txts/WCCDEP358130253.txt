Middx  Lachlin Mc.Kennon aged Sixty Six Years upon Oath says that whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Jones a Peruke maker in Devereux Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds, Diet, Washing & Lodging & hath quitted his sd. Service upwards of Thirty Years since wch. time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. or served any Parochial Office

Sworn Decr. 18th. 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell

Lachlin Mc. Keon
