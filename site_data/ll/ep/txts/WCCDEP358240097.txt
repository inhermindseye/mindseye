St Clement Danes May 5th 1792.

Middx & Westmr.}  St CD  (to wit) Elizabeth Carter  Singlewoman aged 57 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married That She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Graham at the Blue Anchor Clare Street Clare Market in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty for two years and upwards at the Annual Wages of £7. that She quitted that Service about 32 years ago And hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement as She best knows & believes

Sworn the 5th. May 1792 before Me} R Butler

Elizth. Carter 's Mark

Witness Jas. Talboys
