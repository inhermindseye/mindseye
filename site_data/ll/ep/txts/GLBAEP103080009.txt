Middx ss  Mary Sprigmore  Widow of Anthony Sprigmore deced Maketh oath that about twenty years ago her sd late husband lived as a hired servant by the Year when a Singleman with Mr Eagle of the Hamlet of Mile End Old Town in the County of Middx Brewer and Continued in such service for the space of several Years And by means of such service gained a legal settlement there as this Deponent is advised and believes And this Deponent farther saith that her sd. husband did not afterwards to her knowledge information or belief live as a hired servant by the Year with any person or Persons Nor Rent a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards Nor serve any Parish offices Nor do any Act whereby to gain a subsequent settlement And this Depont farther saith that she hath three Children to wit Elizabeth aged about thirteen Years Susannah aged about Six Years and Thomas aged about three Years the lawful issue of this Depont. by her sd late husband

Sworn this 29 day of April 1757 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris. Scott

Mary Sprigmore
