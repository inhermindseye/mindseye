Middx ss  John Andrews maketh oath that about Twenty years ago his father Thomas Andrews Rented a house of Ten pounds a year & upwards in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middx & carried on there the Trade of a Carrier And this Depont. also lived there with his said Father & learn't of him the sd. Trade but was not apprentice to his sd father And this Depont farther says that he never Rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any parish offices Nor Never was an apprentice nor a hired Servd by the year Nor hath gained any settlement but in right of his sd. father as abovesaid And saith that he hath a wife Named Ann & Seven Children to wit Ann aged twenty one years Mary aged about Nineteen years Elizabeth aged about sixteen years Sarah Aged about twelve years Thomas aged about ten years Martha aged about eight years & Lydia aged about five years the lawfull issue of this Depont. & his sd wife The first three of which sd Children are now out at Service

Sworn this. 23d day of Decr: 1754 before us  Richd. Ricards  Chris: Scott

John Andrews
