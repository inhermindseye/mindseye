Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Winterflood aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that she never married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Thomas Wright in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Three years and upwards at the yearly wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Thirteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of Janry. 1797 before me} J Reeves

her Mary Winterflood Mark Witness B Newman
