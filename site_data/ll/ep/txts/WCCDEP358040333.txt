Middx & Westmr.  Eliz Farewell aged upwds. of 67 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice for five Years to Messrs. Sarah and Jane Lowe Milliners and Lived with them as an Apprentice about one Year & Six months in St Clemts. Church Yard in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the End of which time a Disagreemt happening between them the Indenture was Burnt and this Examinant never was Assigned over to any other Person rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 13th 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Eliz: Farewell
