Middx  Patrick Reed aged abt. 59 years upon Oath says that he is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlement in England says that abt. forty years ago he lived a hired yearly Servant with Terence Grant a Farmer in the Parish of Clunfeigle on Ireland for one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of Twenty Shillings or thereabouts & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 2d. day of January 1758 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Patrick Reed
