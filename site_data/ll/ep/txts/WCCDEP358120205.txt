Middx  Ann Sawyer aged abt. 24 years upon her Oath saith she never was married and that on the 14th. Day of May last she was delivered of a female bastard Child at alhouse of in Orange Court Drury Lane in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx which was baptized Mary

Sworn Octr 3d. 1754 before} Benjamin Cox

Ann Sawyer
