Middlesex ss }  Grace Martin  Single woman maketh oath That divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year by & with John Fry & of Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She this Depont. has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or Been Married or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th day of January 1776 Before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Grace Martin
