Middlesex  Margarett Lucas upon her Oath saith that she is the Widow of Richard Lucas who hath been dead about Eleven Years that her said late Husband lived in and rented a House in Horton Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of £63. for the Space of Three Years that since he left the said House her said late Husband in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 4th- Febry 1742 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Margt. Lucas
