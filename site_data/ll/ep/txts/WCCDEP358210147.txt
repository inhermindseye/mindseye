Westmr.  Catherine Purcell aged upwards of Twenty Years upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Royer a Perukemaker in Bull and Mouth Street in the Parish of St. Anne and Agnes Aldersgate London for 3 Years at the Yearly Wages of £3 for the first Year £3..10..0 for the Second Year and £4 for the third Year, quitted said Service about One Year and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 10th.. day of April 1767 before} John Machin 

Cathrine Pursell
