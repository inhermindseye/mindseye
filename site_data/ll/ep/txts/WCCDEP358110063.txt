Middx  Margt. Griffith aged abt. 60 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant wth. Mrs. Stevens in Craven Buildings in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for two Years at the Yearly Wages of £5 and quitted her sd Service abt. 12 Yrs. ago since which She has not rented ten pounds a Year Pd. parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 27th June 1752 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Margt. Griffith
