Middx ss  Elizabeth Mann the wife of John Mann Maketh oath that her said husband is now living at the Parish of Shallford in the County of Surry as this Deponent is informed and verily believes And this Depont. saith that her said husband sometime ago served the office of the Overseer of the Poor in the said Parish of Shallford and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there as this Depont is informed and believes And that her said husband hath not since gained any subsequent settlement as to the knowledge information or belief of this Deponent And this Depont. saith that her said husband went away from her several years ago & refused to Suffer her to live with him or to Provide for her

Sworn this day of August 1759 before us
