Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Robert Gibbeson aged about 50 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Gammon in Lawrence Poulteney Lane in the Parish of Saint

for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about.

since which time he has done no Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3rd day of Octr. 1788 }
