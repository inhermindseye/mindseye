Middlesex ss}  Charlotte Jackson the wife of William Jackson maketh oath That her Said husband who is now belonging to his Majestiesship the Sea Horse has informed her & which she believes to be true that he duly served his apprenticeship with one Jones a Shoemr in the Parish of St Mary in Lynn in the County of Norfolk & has not since done any Act to gain anothr Settlement & that she was lawfully Married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 10. day of February 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Charlot Jackson
