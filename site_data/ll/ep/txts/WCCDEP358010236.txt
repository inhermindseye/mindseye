Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Susannah Nags aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that on lthe 10th. day of April now last past she this Ext. Intermarrd. with Thomas her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex that her said Husband served his Time at Lisson Green in the County of Durham that on the 28th. day of Augt. 1786 she this Examinant was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Jones in Shorts Gardens in the said Parish of Saint Giles in the fields  in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the Name of Thomas

Sworn this 18th. day of Augt. 1788 Philip Dyot D Walker

her Susannah Nags Mark
