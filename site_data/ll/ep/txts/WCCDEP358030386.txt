Westmr. 

Hannah Dunton upon Oath says she Intermarried with James Dunton in the Parish church of St. Ann Westmr. on the 28th. of Janry abt. 23 Yrs. since Says her sd. late husband James Dunton has been dead about Seven Years Says her sd. late husband further Dunton rented a house of the Yearly Value of £30 in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for several Years and paid his Share of all Parochial Taxes Says her sd. late husband James Dunton never rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes Served a parochial Office or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant to her Knowledge or beleif Says she has not since his Death rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one

Year or otherwise gained a Settlement is any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 6th. 1747 before Wm: Jones

The Mark of Hannah Dunton
