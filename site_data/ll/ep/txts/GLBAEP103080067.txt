Middlesex ss  Ann Dill the Widow of William Dill deced Maketh Oath that she hath been informed by her said late husband that he was born in the Parish of St. Mary in the Town of Reading in the County of Berks where her said late husband (as he also informed this Depont.) Acquired a legal settlement by means of his said birth and in right of his said Fathers being legally settled there in Consequence of his duly serving an Apprenticeship in the sd. Parish which information this Depont. verily believes to be true And this Depont. farther saith that to her knowledge information or belief her said husband was never an Apprentice Nor a hired servant by the Year nor ever served any Parish offices nor ever Rented a house of Ten Pounds by the year nor upwards nor ever paid the Land Tax And that she this Depont. hath not since her said husbands death done any Act to her knowledge or belief to Acquire a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 21 day of February 1761 before  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

the Mark of Ann Dill
