Middx & Westmr.  Hannah Filter aged about 23 Years Lodging at Mr. Scotts in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upon Oath says She was never Married and that She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes She being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that Jeremiah Gibison  Son of Mr. Gibison Attorney at Law in Hernlock Court in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes did beget the said Bastard Child or Children on her Body he the said Jeremiah Gibison having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of May last at the House of Mr. Pepper in Grange Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes (with whom She then lived a Servant ) and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which She is now pregnant

Sworn the 20th. Decr. 1762 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Hannah Filter
