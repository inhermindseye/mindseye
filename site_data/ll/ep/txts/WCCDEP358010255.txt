Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit William Bradbury aged about 34 years upon his Oath saith that about 13 years ago he Intermarried with Ann his present wife at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes that he has four Children living by his said Wife vizt. William aged about Twelve years George aged about Ten years Joseph aged about Eight years and Ruth aged upwards of Three years that he has been informed by his Mother which information he verily believes to be true that he was born in High Street Saint Giles's in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex and that he has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Decr. 1788 N Bond M Addington

his William Wm. Bradbury Bradbury Mark
