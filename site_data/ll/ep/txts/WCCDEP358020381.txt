St Clement Danes 6th febry.1792

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Edward Burbidge one of the Beadles of the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County and Liberty aforesaid on behalf of John Troy a Poor Boy aged 13 years and upwards On Oath saith that he that been Deponent hath been Informed and verily believes the said John Troy is the Illegitimate Son of Margaret Connell otherwise Troy , that he was Born in Great St. Andrews Street Seven Dials in the parish of St. Giles in the fields on the Seventh day of July 1778 and hath never obtained any legal Settlement in his own Right

Sworn the 6th febry. 1792 before us  Thos. Gordon  Sampr Wright

Edwd. Burbidge
