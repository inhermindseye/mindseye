Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Grant aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 27th.. day of February 1794 she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Brownlow Street Hospital which hath since been Christened by the Name of Isabella That she this Deponent lived as a hired yearly Servant with George Gresham at the Sign of the Coachmakers Arms in Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas

Sworn this 1st day of Janry. 1798 before us M Addington Robinson

Jane Grant
