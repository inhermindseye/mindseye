Middx  Mary Brown aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice to one Mrs. Kidder and lived with her for the Space of one Year at Garlickhith in the Parish of St James at Garlickhithe London, that she afterwards absconded from her sd Mistress & never was assigned over nor rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Augt. 17th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Brown
