Anna Maria Hull aged about Sixty one Years Wido . of Johns Hull upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her Sd. late Husband John Hull on or abt. the Sixteenth Day of March abt. 26 Years since at the Parish Church of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry & that her Sd. late Husband was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentishp with Richd. Brisington in the Fore Street in the Sd. Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth  Waterman that this Examinants said late Husband in his Life Time never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes nor hath this Examinant since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beliefe

Sworne the 1st of March 17th before} Tho. Lane

The Mark of Anna Maria Hull
