Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Totthill aged about 26 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or Says she lives as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Hall a Mate in the East India Service who now lodges at Mr. Barrons a Brazier in Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and has so done for upwards of one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born And that one Willm- Taylor  Clerk to the said Mr Barron but where he now lives she does not know is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowl of her Body sometime in the Month of June last but the partlon Day she does not now remember & at sevl- Different times afterwds- at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which he is now pregnant & that the sd- Wm- Taylor & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 6th- Day of March 1777 before me R Butler

Mary Tothill
