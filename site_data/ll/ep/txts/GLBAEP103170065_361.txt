Middlesex ss}  Christopher Walker maketh oath that divers years ago his late father Leonard Walker deced rented a house of eight pounds a year in Gun powder alley in the parish of Saint Olave Hart Street in the City of London & was charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that his said Father did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge or belief rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere that this Deponent lived with his Father in the said house as part of his Family That he this Depont never was bound apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwds nor ever Served any parish office or ever paid any poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 13th day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarke

Chrisr: Walker
