Middx  Hannah Palmer aged abt. 27-years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a Stillborn male bastard Child on the twelfth Day of this Instant March at Mrs. Barrets in St Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes at the Charge of the Inhabitants of the said Parish and that Joseph Ralph a Taylor did beget the said male bastard Child on her body the sd. Joseph Ralph having had carnal knowledge of this Examint. several times in the month of August last at one of which times he got her with Child of the said male bastard Child of which she was delivered as afsd. and that the said Joseph Ralph and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn March 27th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Hannah Palmer
