(Mann Sarah Wid.)

St Clement Danes June 24th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Kensington  (to wit) Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the parish of St Clement Danes on behalf of Sarah Mann  Widow aged sixty years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she hath was Married to been informed by the said Sarah Mann which he verily believes to be true that She was Married to John Mann in or about the Month of August 1775 at the parish Church of St Mary le bone  Middlesex that Subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and with this Examinant Resided in a House in Little Chelsea in the parish of Kensington in the County of Middlesex for about several years at the annual Rent of Ten Guineas that they th quitted that House about eight years ago and her said Husband never after during his Life obtained any subsequent legal Settlement that he is now Dead and that since his Decease she the Said Sarah Mann hath not been since Married nor otherwise acquired or become intitled unto any legal Settlement in her own Right

Sworn the 24th day of June 1794 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker Benj Newman
