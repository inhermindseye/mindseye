Westmr.  Ann Cross aged about 32 Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of George Cross with whom She intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Botolph Aldersgate London about Six Years ago by whom she hath one Child named Amelia aged about 17 Months,  says she hath been informed and which information she verily believes to be true that her said husband served Eight Months the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Crutwell a Perukemaker in the Parish of Hampstead in the County of Middlesex and never rented House, paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 4th, Novr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The Mark of Ann Cross
