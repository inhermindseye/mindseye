Middx  Mary Peacock  wido . of Saml. Peacock upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Saml. Peacock at the Fleet London abt. thirteen Years ago and that her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with Mrs. Bunn a Fine Drawer in Fenchurch Street in the parish of St. Cathn. Creechurch London and lived 4 Yrs. or thereabouts a yearly Servt. with his sd. Mistress after the Expiration of his Apprentiship and that neither her Sd. late husband nor this Examinant since his Decease over rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 28th. 1749 before H Fielding

The mark of mary Peacock
