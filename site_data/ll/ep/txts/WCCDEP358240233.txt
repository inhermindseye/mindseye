Militia Man's Wife

St Clement Danes March 15th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  (to wit) Elizabeth Bayley aged 44 years or thereabouts maketh Oath and Saith that about two years ago (being then a Widow) she was Married in the Church belonging to the parish of Ealing in the County of Middlesex to Anthony Bayley (who as she verily believes having no reason to think to the contrary was at that time a Widower and had no Wife living And this Deponent further saith that She hath been informed and which she believes to be true that her said Husband Anthoney Bayley was in or about the Month of August last sworn and enrolled to serve as a substitute for George Sloper of New Inn in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex in the Western Regiment of Militia of the said County and hath served therein ever since and now serves in the said Militia which is embodied and called out into actual Service that she is about to go with the said Regiment and hath now living and dwelling with her three four legitimate Children by her said Husband Namely Anthony aged Eleven ten years 6th day of May last William aged ten years on the 9th day of November last Charlotte aged eight years 17th. day of August last Ann aged six years on the 22d. day of December last and saith that she is in indigent circumstances and not of ability to support herself and Children without assistance And such Lastly this Deponent saith that her said Husband hath informed

her which she verily believes to be true that he served his Apprenticeship with an Hemp Spinner at Sutton in the County of Berks and hath not served obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief And that her said Husband hath been out in actual Serv Service since the 21st day of January last

Sworn the 15th day of March 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Bayley 's Mark

Witness B Newman
