Middlesex ss}  Henrietta Maria Francis maketh oath the widow of John Francis deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her & which she believes to be true That he was duly bound apprentice to & served the Whole thereof With one Christopher Sadler in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in Westminster in the County of Middx  Perukemaker & by means thereof he gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her Said late husband did not subsequent to such his apprenticeship Rent a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rate or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement That she this Deponent since her Said husbands Death hath not done any Act to gain a settlement in her own right and that she hath two Children namely Jane aged about Two years & seven Months & Elizabeth Aged about one Year the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband to whom She was lawfully married at the Parish Church of Saint Margaret in Westminster .

Sworn this 24th day of February 1773  Jno Sherwood B Camper

Henrietta Maria Francis
