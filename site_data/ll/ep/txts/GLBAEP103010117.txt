Middlesex ss.  George Williams maketh Oath that about Twenty One Years age he was Bound Apprentice by Indenture to Samuel Palmes of King Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Shoe Maker for the Term of Five Years that he duly served his said Master there under the said Indenture about Two Years and a half but Boarded with his Father the first year, that his said Master gave him up his Indentures & he continued to work with him for some time as a Journeyman that he has not since his said Apprenticeship rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of Novm. 1792 before}
