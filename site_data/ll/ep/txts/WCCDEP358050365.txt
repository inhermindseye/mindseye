Middx  Thomas Moore upon Oath saith he Intermarried with Jane Moore his present Wife at the Fleet London about Twenty Years ago and that he was bound Apprentice to and Served the fist three Years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Almet Perukemaker and lay during the said three Years at Mr Webbs opposite Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Says he never was assigned over to any other person rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn 10th. Novr. 1750 before} Fran Bedwell

The mark of Thomas Moore
