Middx & Westmr.}  Elizth. Burt aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Tays says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with the Revd. Mr. White adjoining to Bridewell in the Precinct of Bridewell London upwards of four Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 3d day of Septr. 1776 before  John Cox : Jno Barnfather

Elizabeth Burt
