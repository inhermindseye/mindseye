Middx  Mary Todd aged about 21 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Henry Clark  Victualer at the George & Vulture near Griffin Street in Shadwell in the Parish of St. Paul at Shadwell in the County of Middlesex for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds and left her sd. Service about 4 Years ago & has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in Any other Place to her knowledge or belief And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the parish of St. Clemt. Danes (where she now Lodges) she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that William Dennis  Upholsterer did beget the sd. bastard Child or Children on her body the said William Dennison having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant several times in the months of April & May last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the said William Dennis & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Octr. 30th. 1751 before H Fielding Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Todd
