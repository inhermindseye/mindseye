Middx  Jas. Tozer upon Oath Saith his father Thomas Tozer lives in and Rents a house & Ground in the parish of A fealme in the County of Devon and that he (this Examint.) never was an Apprentice or yearly Servt for one year nor ever rented ten pounds a year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn 23d Octr 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

James Tozer
