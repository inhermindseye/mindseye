Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Susannah Gifford of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex  Singlewoman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County of Middlesex this Day of October 1770

Who on her oath Saith That She is now with Child and that the Child or Children where with she now goeth and is Pregnant is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate And that David Murphy of the Temple London  Esquire is the Father of the Said Child or Children

Sworn this 29th day of October 1770 before  Chris: Scott

Susan Gifford
