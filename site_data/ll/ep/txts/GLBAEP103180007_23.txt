Middlesex ss}  Mary Corbet the wife of James Corbet maketh oath that her sd husband who is now Absent from her Beyond the Seas was by birth a Foreigner & never did any Act to this Depts. knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in England That about Thirty years ago She was married at the Fleet to one George Haddock who as he has informed this Depont was duly bound to & Served three Years & upwards of his Apprenticeship with his Far. George Haddock  of the Precinct of St Catherine in the sd County Waterman & afterwards went to Sea where his Apprenticeship ended That her sd. late husband Haddock rented Part of a house in Two Brewers alley St. Catherines Court in the sd. Precinct & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same & thereby gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anr Settlement nor has She this Depont Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself Same what She has acquired by her Second Marriage

Sworn this 3. day of Janry 1778 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkey

Mary Corbet
