Middx  Mary Grundy Aged about Twenty four Years upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Cole in Ship Yard in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of 1 Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of £2..10s..0d & hath left her sd. Service about five Years since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt . for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 15th. 1744 before} Wm. Jones

The mark of Mary Grundy
