Westmr. to wit Sarah Patience Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Goodwin a Surgeon in Blacks Fields in the Parish of Saint Johns Southwark in the County of surry for one Year & an half or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn This Thirteenth day of Decr. 1771 before}  Tho. Kynaston

her Sarah Patience mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
