Middx  Mary Dunstan  Singlewoman maketh Oath and Saith that about fifty Nine Years ago her late Father Robert Dunstan deceased was Bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to his Father Robert Dunstan of the Back Lane then in the parish of St. Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex  Ropemaker but now the parish of St. George in the said County and served all his Apprentiship there And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge And that She hath gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry 1745 Before Robert Dennett

her Mary Dunstan  Mark
