St.. Clement Danes 20th Febry. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. Martin in the fields  (to wit) Ann Stubbs aged eighteen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with John Thompson in Hungerford Market in the parish of St Martin in the fields  Westminster upwards of a year at the Annual Wages of £3 that she quitted the same in or about the Month of March 1791. and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 20th day of February 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Ann Stubbs Mark
