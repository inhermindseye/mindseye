Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Grosvenor aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith That she Intermarried with Wm. Johnson her late Husband who has been dead about four years at the Parish Church of Little Saint James Dukes Place that she hath one Child living by her said late Husband Vizt. Elizth. aged about Eight years th her said late Husband Served Seven years Apprentice- -ship to a Mr. Wood in Mark Lane in the Parish of Allhallows Staining in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of Augt. 1789  Philip Dyot D Walker

Elizabeth Grosvenor
