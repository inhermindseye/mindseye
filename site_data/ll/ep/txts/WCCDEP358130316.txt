Middx  Esther Radbourn aged abt. 19 yrs. upon her Voluntary Oath says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Saviour in Southwark to Joseph Rowlington in little Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn until she should attain the Age of twenty one years or Marriage & served three yrs. the latter part of her said Apprenticeship with Mrs. Lucas a Washerwoman in Arrundel Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes where she now lodges she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born & that one Richd. Lucas a Rulemaker did beget the sd. Child or Children on her

Body she this Examinant living with the said Richard Lucas as his Wife in high Holborn where the sd. Richd. Lucas has Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of Decr. last & at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. Richd. Lucas & other Pson is the true & real father thereof

Sworn the 2d. day of April 1757 before} Fran Bedwell

The Mark of Esther Radbourn
