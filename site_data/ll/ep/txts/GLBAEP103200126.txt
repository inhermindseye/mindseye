Middlesex ss}  William Satchell maketh oath that Many years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to John Satchel of Redcross Street in the Parish of Saint Giles without Cripplegate in the City of London Citizen & Feltmaker for the term of Seven Years & Servd the whole of Such his apprenticeship there & has not Since rented a house of ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 10. day of April 1784 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Wm- Satchell
