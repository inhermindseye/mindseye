Middlesex ss  Isabella Lumley the widow of John Lumley deced maketh oath that her Said late husband heretofore rented a house at the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds in Swan Alley in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County and in Which Parish he also Served the office of Constabr- Headborough for Several Years That he did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or serve any Parish Office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Depont hath two Children namely John Aged about Seven Years & Nine Months & William aged about Six Years & three Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her sd husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 12. day of October 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Isabella Lumley
