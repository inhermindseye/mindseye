Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Edward Burbidge on behalf of Thos. Keppell who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed by the said Thos. which Information he believes to be true that the said Thos. is about 50 years of age that he Rented and lived in a House in Horsley Down Back Street in the Parish of Saint John Southwark  for 9 Months & upwards at the yearly Rent of £18 - that he hath quitted said House about 16 years ago since which time he has not Rented any Place of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Decr. 1787 C Mercer Barnfather

Edwd. Burnbidge
