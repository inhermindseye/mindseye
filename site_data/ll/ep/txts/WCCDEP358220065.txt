Westmr  William Willoughby Aged upwards of Sixty on the Behalf of his Grandson William Willoughby Aged about Seventeen Years on his Oath says that his said Grandson never was married nor ever rented House paid taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says his Son John Willoughby and deceased Father of the abovenamed William Willoughby served seven Years Apprenticeship with Isaac Smith of the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. Wire Worker and after his said Son John Willoughby was out of his said said Apprenticeship he never rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 6th. day of Septr. 1769 before Us } R. Buller Sam Wright

William Willoughby
