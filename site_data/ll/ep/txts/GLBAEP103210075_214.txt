Middlesex ss}  John Johns maketh oath that about Six years ago he this Deponent lived in & rented a house at the yearly rent of six Guineas in Lower Street in the Parish of St Nicholas in Deptford in the County of Kent & resided therein for about the Space of three years & half & during such his residance therein he was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same such Taxes being always deducted out of the Rent when he paid his Landlord for the same That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a Child named Mary Johns aged about seventeen years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his late wife Ann Johns deced to whom he was lawfully married the which Child has not as yet done any act to gain a settlement for herself.

Sworn this. 5. day of April 1785 before  Robt. Smith  Wm. Blackmore

John Johns
