St Clement Danes January 23d. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Lydia Ruleman  Widow aged 44 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her Late Husband Frederick Ruleman in or about the year 1772 at the parish Church of the united parishes of Sr Andrew by the Wardrobo and St Ann Blackfriars by Banns that previous thereto and which he was single and unmarried her said Husband lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Hilt in Houghton Street Clare Market in the parish of St Clement Danes for upwards of two years at the Annual Wages of 10 Guineas or upwards as she hath been informed and believes And this Deponent saith that since he quitted that Service (which was about the time of his Marriage) he never Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or did any other Act Matter or thing Whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge or belief And this Deponent further saith that her said Husband died on or about the 18th day of January 1790 and she hath not since

Children settled  done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement And that she hath two Children now living who are legally settled in their own rights

Sworn the 23d day of January 1793 before Me } Wm. Kitchiner

Lydia Ruleman 's Mark

Witness Garrett
