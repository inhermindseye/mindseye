Middlesex ss  John Jefferys maketh oath That divers years ago he rented a house No 5 in St Catherines Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas & was Charged to and paid Poors Rate for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20 day of December 1776 before  Saml Wood

The Mark of John Jefferys
