Westmr. to wit Susannah Hester Surlant aged about Sixty four Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Anthony Clod Surlant who has been Dead about Thirty five Years ago Says that her said Husband was a Native of France and never gained a Settlement in London to her Knowledge or belief Says that she was born in Grafton Street in the Parish of Saint Anns Westmr . at one Mr. Gregory a Bakers as her Mother has often Informed her and which Information she verily bels. to be true And Says that this Examinant never Rented House Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th. day of Decr. 1770 before Us W Kelynge Petr Planck

Suzanne Ester Serlant
