Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Davis aged about 18 years upon her Oath saith that about Three years ago she Intermarried with John Davis her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr. that her said Husband has been dead about Three Months that she has one Child living by her said late Husband Vizt. Mary Ann aged about four Months that she has been informed which Information she believes to be true that her said late Husband was born at Bristol that his Father kept House the Corner of Princes Street Bristol in the Parish of Saint Stephens in the City of Bristol for several years and that her said late Husband never did any Act to gain a settlement in his own Right according to the best of this Examinants Knowledge

Sworn this 28th, day of July 1789 D Walker Russ: Clarke

Mary Davies
