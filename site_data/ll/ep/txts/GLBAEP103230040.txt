Middlesex ss}  Margaret Collins  widow of Daniel Collins deced maketh oath that She has been informed by said late husband and which She believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice to & served the greatest part of his Apprenticeship with Mr Wood a Pipemaker in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the said County and did not afterwards is any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her sd husband and as a further reason for this Deponents late husband being Settled in the said Parish the overseers of the Poor there heretofore bound out as Apprentices two of this Deponents Children

Sworn this. 25. day of April 1787 before  R Davies  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Margaret Collins
