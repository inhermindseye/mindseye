Middx  Mary Rose Aged abt. 18 Years Upon Oath Says she was Never Marryed and that she was Bound Apprentice by the Parish of Arnel Parvey to John Rose a Sawyer in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr with whom she lived about Eleven Months and hath quitted her said Apprentiship about One Year and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 4th Day of August 1763 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Rose
