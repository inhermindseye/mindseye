Westmr .  to wit Mary Gordon Aged about Nineteen Year on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her late Father Robert Gordon Rented and lived in a House in Charles Court  Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Sixteen Years or thereabouts as her Father and Mother has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true

Sworn this 4th. day of February 1771 }
