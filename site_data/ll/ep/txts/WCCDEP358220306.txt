Westmr. to wit Sarah Smith Aged about Sixteen Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Alexr. Smith Rented and lived in a House in Little Scotland Yard known by the Name of the Windsor Castle in the Parish of Saint of Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Several Years at Yearly Rent of fifteen Pounds or thereabout & her said Father died Says that since her said Fathers Death she hath done no Act whereby to gain as Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Eleventh day of Decr.1771 before W Kelyngs Tho. Kynaston

Sarah Smith
