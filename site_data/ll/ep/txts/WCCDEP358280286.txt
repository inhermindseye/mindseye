Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Pearman aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Harris who kept the Three Tuns in Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Thirteen Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish That one Samuel Hill who lives Servant with Coll. Harridge in Rathbone Place is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant as aforesaid thereon and that the said Samuel Hill is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of May 1790 } R Butler

Eliz Pearmen
