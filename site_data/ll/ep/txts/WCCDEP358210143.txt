Westmr.  Mary Fleming aged about 66 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Francis Fleming who has been dead about 30 Years, says her said Husband rented a house in St. John's Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchre in the County of Middx , for one Year and Upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds or thereabouts and there died says she hath not since the Death of her said Late husband Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 5th, day of March 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

Mary Flemming
