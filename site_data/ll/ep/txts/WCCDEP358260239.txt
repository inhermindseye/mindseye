Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Martin aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Fourth day of November 1792 she Intermarried with Andrew Martin her present Husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Manchester in the County of Warwick by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Ann aged about Two years and One Quarter and Joseph aged about Three Months That her said Husband served Four years Apprenticeship to one Amaisus Backers Harpischord Maker in Jermyn Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster when his said Master died after which her said Husband never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby s he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of Janry. 1798 R. Ford J Ploud

her Elizth. Martin mark
