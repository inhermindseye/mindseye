Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann Brown of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Burford Camper - Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this 11th day of october 1771

Who on her oath Saith That on the first Day of February last past at the house of John Sibbin in Creed Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin Ludgate in the City of London She the Said Ann Brown was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since Baptized in the Said Parish of St Martin Ludgate by the name of Elizabeth Margaret Flanagan And that the Said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that Luke Flanagan of the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate  Victualler did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Taken & Sworn the Day & Year above written before me B Camper

The Mark of Ann Brown
