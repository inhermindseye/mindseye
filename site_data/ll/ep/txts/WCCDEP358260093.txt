Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hester Talboys aged about Twenty two years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Forbes a Druggist at Croydon in the Parish of Croydon in the County of Surry for about Three years at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Eighteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th- day of Decr. 1795 before us W Kinnard N Bond

Hester Talboys
