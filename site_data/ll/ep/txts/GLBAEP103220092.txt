Middlesex ss}  John Robertson maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house at the Yearly rent of Eleven Guineas in Sun Yard in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & lived therein for four Years & paid Kings Taxes for the same That he has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath three Children namely Alexander aged about twelve years Robert aged about ten Years & William aged about Seven Years the lawful issue of this Deponent by his now wife  Amelia Robertson to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 25th day of Novemr 1786 before R Davies

John Robertson
