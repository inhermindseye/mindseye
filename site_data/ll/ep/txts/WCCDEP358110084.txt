Middx  Eliz Richards aged 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she lived a Yearly Servant with Miss Hide in Angel in the Parish of St Nicholas in the City of Worcester for two Years at the Yearly Wages of £4 and has not since she left that Service rented ten pounds a Year Paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 18th. Augt 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz Richards
