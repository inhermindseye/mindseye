Middlesex ss)  Rebeccah Crick  Singlewoman Maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Anthony Longate in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and Continued in Such Service for Several Years at Certain Yearly wages & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont. Saith That She hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this day of August 1771 before}
