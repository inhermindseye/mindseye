Hunt lodges at No.10 White Hart Court Charterhouse Lane

Saint Clement Danes 2nd. August 1785

Middlesex (ss)  taken and paid for  Susannah Hunt  Single woman aged twenty seven Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she never was married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Richard Williams of Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of twelve Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds that the quitted her said service about Three Weeks ago, and further this Examinant on her Voluntary Oath saith That she is now Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards, and that Richard Adams who now is Horse Keeper at the Swan Inn Fleet Market had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body about the Month of January last Part and at several times since at home or one of which times he got her with Child of such Child or Children

Where with She is so Pregnant and that he the said Richard Adams and no one else is the real and true father thereof and that this Examinant is Poor and unable to maintain herself and such Child or Children when born

Sworn this 2nd. Augt. 1785 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Susannah Hunt Mark
