Middx  Jas. Hammond upon Oath saith he was married to his wife Alice in the parish Church of St. Ann Soho abt. 18 years ago and that he Served his apprentiship with Rt Harvey a Gunsmith on the West Side of Sheer Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes and never Rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn April 15th 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

James Hammond
