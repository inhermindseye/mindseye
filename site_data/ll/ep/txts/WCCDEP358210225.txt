Westmr.  Bathsheba Clark Aged about Forty four Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thomas Clark , with whom she intermarried at St. Peter's Church at Wisbech in the Isle of Ely in the County of Cambridge upwards of Eleven Years ago, and that her said Husband hath been dead about Eight Years, says her said Husband has often times informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that he was born at Green Bank in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middlesex and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, neither hath she since her said Husbands Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 7th. day of January 1768 . before} Thos. Kynaston  Barth: Hammond

The  Mark of Bathsheba Clark
