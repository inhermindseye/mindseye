St Clement Danes Augst 6th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  (to wit) Sarah Blackett otherwise Exton aged 52 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she served as an articled Servant for 3 years with Mrs Larjeux in Gun Street in the Old Artillery Ground within the Liberty of his Majesty's Tower of London that since the expiration of Such her Service she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the sixth day of August 1793 before Us}
