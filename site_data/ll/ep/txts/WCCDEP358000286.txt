Saint Clement Danes 13th. May 1786

Middlesex to wit, Jane Cook  Widow aged fifty three Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that the since the Death of her Husband being Single and unmarried and not having Child or Children lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Henry Bromwich of Strand Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Waterman at the Yearly Wages of three pounds for upwards of four Years that since She quitted his service she has done us Act Matter or thing to gain any Settlement elsewhere than in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid.

Sworn this 13th. May 1786 before R Butler

Jane Cook
