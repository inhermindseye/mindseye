Middx to wit John Evans aged about fifty Three Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West London about Twenty Years ago and by whom he hath one Son living now provided for Says that this Examinant Rented and lived in a House known by the Name of Bagnio's Wells in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years & an half at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds a Year & paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Sixteen Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Janry 1779 before J. Welch John Croft

John Evans
