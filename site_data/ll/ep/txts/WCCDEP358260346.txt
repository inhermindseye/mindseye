Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadle of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Catherine Farguson an Infant aged about Five years Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Catherine Farguson was born at the house of a Mr. Reading in Brick Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles without Cripplegate in the County of Middlesex And further saith that the said Catherine Farguson is now charge  to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 9th. day of Octr. 1798 before us } E Read Russ. Clarke

Jas Talboys
