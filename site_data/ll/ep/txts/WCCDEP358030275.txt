Middx  Sarah Boswell aged upwds. of 23 Yrs. upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Cole Peruke maker in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of one Year & upwds: and hath left her sd. Service about Six Years Says she hath not since she last the said Mr. Cole's Service rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1746 before  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Sarah Boswell
