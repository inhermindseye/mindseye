Middx  Thomas Spencer Aged about Sixty four years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of St. James Clerkenwell about Twenty two years ago and by whom he hath one Child living vizt. John aged about Nineteen years Provided for Says he Rented & lived in a House in Aylesbury Street in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx upwds. of Six years at the yearly Rent of £14. & Paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Six years ago since which time he never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year or or.wise howsoever

Sworn this 18th day of March 84  Thos: Gilbert  Sampn Wright

Thomas Spencer
