Middx  Eliz: Blakey aged abt. 60 yrs. upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Andrew Blakey with whom she intermarried at the fleet London abt. twenty three years ago says her said Husband informed her & which information she believes to be true that he served his Apprenticeship of seven years with Mr. Wood a Baker near Clay Hill in the Parish of Epsom in the County of Surry

Sworn the 14th Day of March 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Blakey
