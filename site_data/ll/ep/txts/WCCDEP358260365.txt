Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Susannah Petherick aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Fletcher at the Mecklenbrough Coffee house in Cockspur Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for Fifteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Eight Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Eighteen Months Since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this first day of Novr. 1798 before us  H. Wisstead Russ Clarke

her Susannah Petherick Mark

Witness H Day
