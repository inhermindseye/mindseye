Middx.  Ann Elson aged upwds. of 50 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the widow of Wm. Elson who hath been dead abt. Ten Years and that before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stevens a Distiller in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr in the County of Middx. for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds as this Examinants said late husband hath informed her and as she verily believes says her sd. husband in his life time after he quitted this said Service nor she since his death never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 19th. day of April 1758 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Elson
