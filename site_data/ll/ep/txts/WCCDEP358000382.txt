Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Charlotte Hope aged about 33 Years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Wm. Hope her late Husband (who has been dead about 7 years) at the Parish Church of Hemsworth in Hampshire and that having no Child or Children living she went to live as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Eyre  Haberasher who lived in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields that she lived there for two years and 7 Months that then her sd. Master moved to Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes without Discharging this Ext. where she continued to live in her sd Masters Service for 11 Months at the yearly Wages of £7 hath quitted said Service about 3 Years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th day of Novr. 1786 }

Charlotte Hope

This Woman was passed to we ill we had some doubt about her Settlement & when she got well we took this Exn.-& as she was going with the Beadle to swear to it-she ran away
