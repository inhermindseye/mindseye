Westmr  Sarah Roe Aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she never was Married nor ever rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or was an Apprentice Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Andrew Risk a Book binder in Naked Boy Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for One Year and four Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about a Year and half ago & hath not since she quitted same Ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 12th. day of Janry 1770 before Me R. Butler

Sarh Roe
