Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Pritchard Singleman aged thirty Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was Married, that on the thirteenth Day of February in the Year of our Lord one thousand seven hundred and Ninety two she was delivered of a female Bastard Child (which has since been Christened by that Name of Sarah) at the House of Mr. Muras a Cheesemonger in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that this Deponent further saith that John Harrimmen who is Coachman to Lord Lucas in Charles Street Beshley Square in the Court of Middlesex , to the father of the child where she this Deponent was so delivered as above Stated he the said John Harrimmen having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same thereon And that the said John Harrimmen and no other Person whatsoever did beget the same and that he is the Real and true father thereof and that this Deponent is poor and unable to Maintain but Child which must thereupon become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 20th Day of Decr. 1795 before} J Hind

Elizabeath, Pritchard

Elizth. Pritchard at Mr. Ges: Pritchard's No. 13 Wood Street 1t. Oxford Evad

Man taken 11th. Janry. 1796

Settled by paying £15..15..0 Vizt. 5 Guas down 5 Guas in 3 Mos. & 5 Guas in 6 Mos. All paid but 2 Guas
