Middx  Mary Craig Aged about Twenty three Years Wife of Samuel Craig , upon her Oath Says she Intermarried with the said Saml. Craig at May Fair Chapel in the Parish of St. George  Hanover Square within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx On the 15th. Day of July 1752 by whom she has a Child Named Elizabeth Aged about 2 Years, Says her said Husband Lived a Covenant Servant for two Years Certain with One Mr. Adlard in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County of Middx and has left his sd. Service upwards of Three Years ago Says her sd. Husband hath not since he Left the sd. Mr. Adlards Service Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the Day of Decr. 1755 Before}

Mary Craig
