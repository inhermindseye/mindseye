Middx.  Mary Sheriffe aged upwards of 30 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Sheriffe who has been dead about Nine Years says her said late husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Thos. Sheriffe (his Uncle) a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middlesex and never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to her knowledge or belief says she has not since the Death of her said late husband Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 20th. day of April 1765 , Before}

N B the above Pauper was moved to St. C. D: from St. Geo: Hanr. Sqr . but the Apprentice duty not being pd. at the Stamp Office St. Clements Appealed and the Order was Quarter
