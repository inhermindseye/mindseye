Middlesex ss}  Margaret Bradshaw  Widow of Micah Bradshaw deced maketh oath that She Can give no Account respecting the Settlemt of her Said late husband nor of his Parents or eithr of them That this Deponts late Father hereto fore rented a house in Sharpp Buildings (& whose name was John Grayson ) in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd Count of the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & resided thereon for many years & this Deponts said Father & also her Mother died legally Settled in the said Parish That She this Depont. never was bound an Apprentice nor ever hired as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself Save what She may have Acquired by Marriage.

Sworn this - 16. day of Janry 1787 before R Davies

The Mark of Margaret Bradshaw
