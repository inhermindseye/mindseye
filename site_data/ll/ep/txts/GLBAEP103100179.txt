Middlesex ss  Charles Cunnick maketh oath That Subsequent to his apprenticeship he was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds with one George Leyburn in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex  Victualler and Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year and upwards he this Depont. being then & during all such time a Single & an Unmarried Man And this Depont. Saith that he has not Since done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement Subsequent to Such his Servitude And that he hath a wife named Jane to whom he was lawfully married in the Parish Church of St. John Wapping aforesd. and a Child named Joseph aged about four Months the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his Said Wife

Sworn this 28th Day of Novr: 1769 before  Chris: Scott

Charles Cunnick
