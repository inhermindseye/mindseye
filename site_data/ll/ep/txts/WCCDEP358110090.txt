Westmr  Thos. Jones upon Oath saith, that in or about the year 1715 he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £18 Coael & Horses Alehouse in Thrift Street in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. and lived therein about nine months and has not since he quitted the sd house rented ten pounds a Year pd parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Augt. 26th 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Tho Jones
