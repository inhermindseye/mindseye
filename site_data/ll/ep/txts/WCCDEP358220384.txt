Westmr. to wit James Latimer Aged about fifty Three Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Temprance his Wife at the Parish of St. Brides otherwise Bridget about Twenty five Years ago and by whom he that Three Children now living (Two of which are now provided for) and the Goundgest of which is named William Aged about Ten Years Says he rented and lived in a House in York Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the Field Villers Street, Years about five Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds & paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted his said House near fifteen Years ago & hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any or Act where by to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Septr. 1772 before me R. Butler W Kelyngs

James Lattimer
