Westmr.  Phebe Barnes upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Wm Barnes with whom She intermarried in Christchurch at Canterbury abt. 15 Years ago and that she has been informed and verily believes her sd. late husband Served Six Years of his Apprentiship with Mr Webster a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the End of which time his Master dyed And that neither sd. late husband whilst living or this Examint. since his Decease never Rented ten pounds a Year  paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn June 18th. 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Phebe Barnes
