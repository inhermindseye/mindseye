Westmr- to wit Elizth- Anderson aged about twenty one Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwdns- and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of S aint Saviours Southwark in the County of Surry to one Edward Noulton who kept a Cook Shop in Butcher Row in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx untill this Examinant shod- Attain her said Age of Twenty one Years or Day of March And further saith that her said Master removing out of the parish of Saint Clement Danes this Examinant went and lived with him near Chad Wells in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middx for two Months or upwards but her said Master using this Examinant ill she left him since which time she hath gained no or- subsequent Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th- Day of Decr- 1777 before  Jno. Machin John Croft

her Elizth- Anderson Mark
