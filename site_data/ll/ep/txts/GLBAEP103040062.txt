Middlesex to wit, Elizabeth Bayles  Singlewoman maketh Oath and Saith, that on the fourteenth day of April One thousand seven hundred and Ninety one at the House of Samuel Hill in Star Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate Middlesex, she was delivered a Male Bastard Child since Baptised at Aldgate Church by the name of John Samuel , and that John Hilpin of Hanslope in Buckinghamshire . Farmer , is the Father of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this 4th. day of December 1797 before H: Reynell

Eliz Bayles
