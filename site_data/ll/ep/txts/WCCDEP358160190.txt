Middx  The Examinacon of Ann Willis now in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 5th. Day of Novr. 1740 . Who upon her Oath saith yt. she was never married And yt. upwards of Twenty Years ago she lived as a yearly hired Servt . with the Revd. Mr. Spavan in Arundell Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes for the space of 13 Months at the Yearly Wages of £3 And further upon her Oath saith yt. she hath not since she left the sd. Service Rented £10 a year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife.

Sworn the Day & year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Ann Willis
