Middx  John Sharp Aged about 42 Years upon Oath saith he was Married to his present Wife  Hannah about 20 Years since in the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holborn Says that about Eight - years ago he rented a House of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and lived therein about six Months and has not since he quitted the sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 30th. Day of Jany. 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell

John Sharpe
