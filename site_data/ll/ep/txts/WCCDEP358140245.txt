Middx.  Jane Owen aged abt. 59 years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Isaac Owen who has been dead abt. Twenty Years; says her said husband Rented and lived in a house of Eighteen Pounds a year in King Street  Bloomsbury in the Parish of St. Geo: Bloomsbury in the County of Middx. for five years or thereabouts  and after he quitted said house never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes; says she has not since her sd. husbands death Rented Ten Pounds a Year or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of April 1759 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Jaen Owen

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Matravers Ovr. J: P:
