Middx 

Mary Porter aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Richd. Porter who hath been dead about upwds. of four Years and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a female Bastd. Child on the 28th. Day of April  last in the house of Mrs. Mary Shewdall in the back of St. Clements in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx which has been baptized Elizabeth and is now living & likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same and that Captn. Bellamy now lodging at Mr. Leakes a Druggist near the New Church in the Strand did beget the sd. female Bastard Child on her Body the sd. Captn. Bellamy having had Carnal Knowledge

of this Examinant in the sd. Mrs. Shewdals house several times in the Month of Augt. last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child of which she was Delivered as aforesaid and that the sd. Captn. Bellamy & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworn May 18th. 1747 before  Tho: Burdus  Tho Lane .

Mary Porter
