Middx  Wm. Ivatts aged about Forty Five Years upon his Oath saith that on the 1st. of August abt. 12 Yrs. since he intermarried with Eliz : has now Wife at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx That he was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Wm. Brace in Castle Street in the Sd. Parish of St. Giles  Farryer & hath been out of his Sd. Apprentiship upwards of Sixteen Years that he never rented Ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath Two Children viz. Mary aged abt. Two Yrs. & Ann aged abt. 11 Weeks & being Poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworne the 10th of Febry 1741 before} Tho. Lane  Jas: Gronous

William Ivatts
