St Clement Danes  9th Janry 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  St. CD (to wit) Elizth. Brooker aged 20 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she never lived as an hired yearly servant Rented any House or Apartment of £10 year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement in her own right And this Examint upon her Oath further saith that she hath been informed which she verily believes to be true that her father Serv his Apprenticeship with a Whitesmith in the parish of St Clement Danes and never after during his Life obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 9th day of January 1793 before me} Wm. Kitchiner

Elizth Brooker 's Mark

Witness B Newman

She was pregnant Vide Bastard & Examination No. 26 Bastards Examination Book No. 2 was Married to Edward Still before the Birth of the Child Vietes Examn. on the otherside as to his settlement
