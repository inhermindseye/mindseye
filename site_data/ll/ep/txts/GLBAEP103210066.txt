Middlesex ss}  Lydia Marriott  widow of John Marriott deced maketh oath that about thirty eight years ago this Deponents said late husband rented a house of the Yearly rent of four Pounds in Brush Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & was Charged to and paid kings Taxes for the same That her said husband did not afterwards rent a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That she this Deponent was lawfully married to her said husband and since his death has not lived as a hired Servant for a year or been Married nor done any Act to gain a settlement for herself.

Sworn this 21. day of February 1785 before J. Hawley

Lydia Marriott
