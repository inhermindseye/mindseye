Middx ss}  Thomas John Tripp Maketh oath about fifteen years ago He duly served his Apprenticeship by Indenture with Thomas Pontter a Cooper who then lived in Queen Street in parish of Saint Savior Southwarke in the County of Surrey he apprehends he gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent farther sayth he hath not gained any subsequent settlement to his knowledge or belief haveing never since his Apprentice ship rented a house of Ten pounds a years or upwards not served a hired servend by the year nor served any parish offices

Sworn this 13 day of Febry 1756 before me  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

John Tripp
