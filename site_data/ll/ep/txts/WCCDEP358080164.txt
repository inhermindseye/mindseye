Middx & Westmr-}  Elizth- Stevens aged about thirty seven years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Cornish a Distiller in the Haymarket in the Parish of St- Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr - and County afsd- for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of six pounds six shillings a Year hath quitted her said Service about twelve Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this sixth Day of August 1777 R Butler Jno. Machin

Her Elizth. Stevens Mark
