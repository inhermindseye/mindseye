Middlesex ss}  Ann Ruby  Singlewoman maketh oath that her late Father John Ruby deced as She has been informed & believes was bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with his Father in Law Wilkinson a Butcher in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge or belief do any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this Depont was born in the said parish & was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right That She hath a child named Ann Ruby aged about eleven years & nine months who was born a Bastard in the said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 5 day of April 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Ann Ruby
