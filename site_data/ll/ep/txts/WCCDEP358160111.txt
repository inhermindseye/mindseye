Middx  The Exaicon of Elizth. Shields  Wido . Aged 34 Years Passed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields by Order dated the 24th. Janry last taken the 15th. day of Febry 1739 who upon her Oath Saith that abt. five Years ago this Exaiant Intermarryed with her late Husband James Sheilds & that her sd. Husband Dyed abt. Ten Weeks since And further Saith that her sd. late Husband Served his Apprentiship with one Mr. May a Sawyer in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. County of Middx Excepting the last halfe Year his Master abt. that time Dying his Mistress gave him his Indenture that her sd. late Husband as he informed this Exaiant was never Assigned ever to any Person for the remainder of his sd. Apprentiship or ever rented any House or lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that this Exaiant hath two Children Anne Aged four Years & Jas . aged upwds. of one Year and being Poor & troubled with fitts is not able to maintain them

Sworn the day of Year above menconed before} Tho Lane

The mark of Elizth. Shields
