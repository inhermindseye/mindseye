Westmr.  Sarah Hannikin on behalf of James Mitchel an Infant aged about Eighteen months upon her Oath saith she is Grandmother of the said Jas. Mitchel by the mothers Side Says her Daughter Rebecca the mother of the said Infant Intermarryed with Samuel Mitchel the father of the said Infant at the Fleet London about three Years and a half ago Says her sd. Daughter Rebecca Mitchel is dead and that she hath been Informed and believes that the said Samuel Mitchel is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England and cannot give any Account

where the said Samuel Mitchel is to be found Says her said Daughter never was bound Apprentice lived a Yearly Servant for one Year rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes And this Examinant further Saith that her late husband Daniel Copsey the father of the said Rebecca Mitchel and Grandfather of the said Infant lived in and rented a house of Five pounds a Year in the Parish of Dudden hurst in the County of Essex for three Years or thereabouts and p the poors Rate for the said house and there dyed Says during the time she continued a Widow she did not rent Ten pounds a Year pay Parish Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement in any other Parish until her Intermarriage with her present husband John Hannikin

Sworn the 19th. July 1749 before}
