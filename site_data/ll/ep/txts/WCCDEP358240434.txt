St. Clement Danes Augt.19th. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster } 

Settlement in St. Giles in the fields  Hannah Winter aged about 36 years Widow of James deceased upon Oath saith that she was married to her late Husband deceased  James Winter deceased at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields on the  9th. day of August in the year of our Lord 1779 that her said Husband since their said Marriage Rented and Resided in a House in Parker Street Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the said County of Middlesex for about 5 years at the Annual Rent of £20 that he quitted that House about Six years ago and never after during his Life Rented any house or Apartment of Ten Pounds a year or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge or belief that he died on the 21st. day of June 1793 that she hath not since been married or obtained any legal Settlement in her own right that she hath Two Children by her said Husband namely Thomas born the 25th. day of Decr. 1786 and Jeremiah aged Three Four years on the 17th. day of Decr. 1793 And that she is unable to maintain herself & family without Assistance

Sworn this 19th. day of August before us } N Bond Edwd: Naish

Hannah Winter
