Middlesex ss}  James Brown maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to Jacob Hopegood of Millstairs in the Parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surrey  Waterman for the Term of Seven Years and served the whole of his apprenticeship there save and except the last nineteen days of such his Apprenticeship when he was turned over to one charles Ross Jilly of the Parish of St John in Southwarke in the sd County of Surry where his sd apprenticeship ended That he has not since lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year or rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Settlement since his said apprenticeship. That he hath a wife named Maria to whom he was lawfully married & five Children namely Charles aged about three years and a Female child not yet baptized aged Six days the lawful issue of this Deponent by his said wife

Sworn this 30th day of May 1787 before  R Davies  Robt: Smith

James Brown
