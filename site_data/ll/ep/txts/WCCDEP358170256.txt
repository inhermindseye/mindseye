Middx.  Mary Gullis  Wido . upon her Oath saith that (being at that Time a Wido. & not haveing any Child or Childn. she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Jones & Mr. Bambi at the Red Lyon & Catt in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of. Six Pounds and hath left her sd. Service abt. 2 Weeks and further upon her Voluntarily Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one George Minithorp  Chairman did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body he the sd. Geo: Minitharp lodging at the said Mr. Bambi there became acquainted with her & had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of Decr. last & at sevell. other Times since at one of which Times he Gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now Pregnant & that he the Sd. Geo. Minitharp & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 12th. Augt. 1742 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Gullis
