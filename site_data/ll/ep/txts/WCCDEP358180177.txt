Middx  Jane Dixon aged 20 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married that her Father  Robert Dixon lived in & rented a house in Great Tothill Street in the parish of St. Margarett in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of £17. for the Space of one Year or thereabts. & hath left the same abt. one Year that she never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt . or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 28th. March 1744 before} Wm: Jones  Tho Lane

The Mark of Jane Dixon
