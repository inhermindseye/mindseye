Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Goodenoughss the wife of Daniel Goodenoughss maketh oath that divers years ago her said husband was bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with James Upton in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Staymaker & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and has not Since to this Deponents knowledge or belief rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement and that She hath three children namely Susannah aged about eight years Ann aged near Seven years & Sarah aged about five years & a quarter the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this. 21. day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Goodenoughss
