Middx  Jane Smith aged about 25 Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with Thomas Smith (who is now at Sea in his Majesty's Service) at the Fleet London the 20th. Day of March 1754 Says She has a Female Child by her said Husband as yet unbaptized and that her said Husband Served the last part of his Apprenticeship to wit about two years with one John, Pouch a Working Brazier in little St. Andrew Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and that he never afterwards rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year  as this Examinant has been informed by him & verily believes

Sworn the 18th. Day of March 1756 before} Barth: Hammond 

The Mark of Jane Smith
