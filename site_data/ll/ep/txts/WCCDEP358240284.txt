St Clement Danes July 14 15th 1793.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Andrew Undershaft  (to wit) Esther Weatherspoon  singlewoman aged seventeen years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived served five years or thereabouts under an Indenture of Apprenticeship with William Prince Cook in St Mary Axe in the parish of Saint Andrew Undershaft in the City of London that She left her said Master about thr on account of ill usage about two Months when she was discharged of her Apprenticeship ago and hath never since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or information or belief

Sworn the 15th. day of J July 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Esther Weatherspoon's Mark
