Middlesex to Wit}  Ann Dennison  Singlewoman Maketh Oath and saith that she his 25 Years of Age and hath never lived an hired Yearly Servant for one whole Year together nor was ever bound out Apprentice neither hath she ever done any Act whatsoever to her Knowledge or Belief whereby to gain a legal Settlement in any Parish or Place in her own Right and saith that James Dennison  Bricklayer her late Father deceased lived in and Rented an House in Star Court Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for several Years but at what Rent she doth not know but well remembers to have heard her said Father and Mother say that they Paid Parish Taxes for the said House and never after he quitted the said House got any other Settlement And saith that her said Father had for some Years before his Death been supported in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish where he died And saith that she this Examinant hath been informed that she was born in lawful Wedlock in Star Court aforesaid And saith that she is now Pregnant and that Charles Wright  Cordwainer now or late a Lodger at Mr. Negus's No. 4 in Vine Street Hatton Wall is the true and real Father of the Child or Children with which she now goeth and no other Person whatsoever he the said Charles Wright having lately cohabited together with her this Examinant and often had the Carnal Knowledge of her Body whereby she conceived with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes to which she belongs she being in nowise able to provide for the same

Sworn the 14th Day of December 1790 Wm Kitchiner

Ann Dennison
