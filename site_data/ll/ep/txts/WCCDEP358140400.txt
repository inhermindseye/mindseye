Middx.  Ann Sharp Wife of Wm. Sharp lodging in Mr Penny's Rents in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and liberty of Westmr. Upon Oath says she intermarried with the said Wm: Sharp in the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes on the 15th. day of June 1756 says her said husband is very ill and keeps his bed and this Examinant is not able to Maintain him says her said husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that before his intermarriage with (her) this Examinant, and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Gwillum in Milford Lane aforesaid in the said Parish of Saint Clemt. Danes for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds And hath not since Rented Ten Pounds by the Year or paid parish Taxes

Sworn August 28th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Sharp
