Middx  William Millington aged 21 Years upon Oath Saith he is the Son of Wm. Millington decd. by Hanh . his Wife That his sd. late Father rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards in Hollis Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . lived thereon Sevl. Years & there dyed and this Examinant never was an apprentice or yearly Servant for one Year nor ever rented ten Pounds by the Year or pd. parochial taxes

Sworn July 24th 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell Wm: Millington
