Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Plantin of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the sd County this eighth day of April 1784 Who Saith that in the Month of December last past at the house of Samuel Wynn in upper Eastsmithfield in the sd Parish She was delivered of a female Bastard Child named Sarah Plantin & that the Said Child is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish & that George Bishop now or late of Colchester in the County of Essex  Farmer is the father of the Said Child

Sworn this. 8. day of April 1784 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Sarah Plantin
