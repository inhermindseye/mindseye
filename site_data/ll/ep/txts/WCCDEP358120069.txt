Middx  Saml Long on behalf of Ann Long his Daughter aged abt. 12 Yrs. maketh Oath that he intermarried with his late Wife Ann Long the mother of the sd. Ann Long abt. fourteen years ago at Crofton Church in Yorkshire and that before his sd. marriage and whilst a Singleman he lived a yearly Servant with Sr Rowland Hynn at Noveall hall in the Parish of Rugby in the County of York upwds. of two years at the yearly Wages of Seven pounds that he was married to the said Ann Long while in Sr. Rowlands Service & has not since rented ten Pounds a year or paid parish taxes

Sworn Feby. 26th. 1754 before} Barw. Hammond

Ann Watts Saml Long
