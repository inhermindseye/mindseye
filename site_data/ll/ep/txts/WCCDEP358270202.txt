Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Leech aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year on paid Taxes That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Emblyn at the Sign of the Coal Hole in Fountain Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as an hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith That on the 24th. day of September now last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard child in Store Street Hospital which hath not been as yet baptized That one Timothy Hall who was Groom to Captn. Munro and did lodge at No. 19 Quaker Street Spitalfields but where he is now this Deponent doth not know is the Real and true father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of December last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child of which she this Deponent was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Timothy Hall is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 22d. day of October 1794 J lend

Ann Leech

Timothy Hall is about 26 years of Age about 5 feet 3 Inches high rather Round made rather fair Complexion short Hair curled round wears a brownish Coat with a Velvet Collar

Mr. Evans Overseer Dobbins Beadle

Man taken on the 19th- day of Janry. 1796 Settled by Paying £10 down and a Note of hand for £10 Payable at Six Nos.

The Note was afterwards paid
