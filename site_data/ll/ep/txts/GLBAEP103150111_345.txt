Middlesex ss}  John Cowden maketh oath that about Thirty three Years Ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indre to John Booth of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Brushmaker & Served him as his Apprentice for about the Space of one Year & half & then by reason of ill usage from his said Master he this Deponent left him & entred himself on Board Certain of his Majesties Ships of War & never returned to his Said Masters Service That he this Deponent never lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor ever rented a house or other Tenement of the Yearly Rentor Value of Ten pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself Since his Said Apprenticeship That he hath a Wife named Elizabeth to Whom he was lawfully married in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Plymonth & two Children namely Mary aged about Six Years & Four Months & Elizabeth Aged about Four Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his Said Wife

Sworn this 17th day of January 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

John Cowden
