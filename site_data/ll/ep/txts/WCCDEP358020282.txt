St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the said parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty (on behalf of Susanna Prickman Widow a Poor Woman now lying Ill at the House of No..18 in Clements Lane in the said parish) Maketh Oath and saith that she the said Susanna Prickman hath Informed this Deponent which he verily believes to be true that she is of the Age of 62 years or thereabouts that about Fifteen years ago she was Married to her late Husband John Prickman at the Parish Church of Wellington Somersetshire that her said Husband died about 2 years since has that he in his life time Rented and with her Resided in a House in Providence Court George Street Grosvenor Square in the parish of Saint George the Martyr Hanover Square in the said Liberty of Westmr. in the said County about 4 years at the Annual Rent of Fourteen Pounds that they quitted the same about three years ago & that he in his Life time did not do nor hath she since his Decease hath not done any Act or thing whereby she might acquire or become intitled unto any Subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the 19th May 1791 before Us  Philip Dyot D Walker

Benj: Newman
