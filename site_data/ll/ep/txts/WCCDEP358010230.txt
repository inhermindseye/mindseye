Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Smith aged about 30 years upon her Oath Saith that about 4 years ago she Intermarried with John Smith her late Husband who has been dead about 15 Months at the Parish Church of Marylebone That She hath one Child living by her said late Husband Vizt. John aged about 2 years and an half That her said late Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examt. and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr Yeatman in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Twelve Months and upwards that after he quitted said Service he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of July 1788 R Butler

Mary Smith
