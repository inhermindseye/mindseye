Westmr.  Mary Barton Aged about Sixty three Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Page Master of Wills Coffee House the Corner of Searle Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Six Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds, and after she quitted said Service never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 14th. day of April 1768 Before} 

The Mark of Mary Barton
