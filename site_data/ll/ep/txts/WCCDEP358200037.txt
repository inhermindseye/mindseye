Middx  Mary Meadows Aged about 47 Years Upon Oath Says she lived a hired Yearly Sert . with Mr Thomas Evans at the Thistle & Crown in South Lambath in the Parish of St. Margat Lambeth upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of two Years. she being at that time a Widow & without Child or Children and hath not since she left her said Service Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 27th Day of October 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Meadows

Upon Mr Jones Complaint J W
