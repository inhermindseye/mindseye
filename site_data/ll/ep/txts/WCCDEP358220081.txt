Westmr.  Mary Street aged about 21 years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes says she Lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Stephens a Cheesemonger in East Street in the Parish of Christ Church Spittle Fields in the County of Middlesex for 2 Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath Quitted her said Service about 6 Months ago Etc hath not since she quitted same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 7th. day of November 1769 before us} R. Butler Thos Kynaston

her Mary Street Mark

Witness Thos. Jones
