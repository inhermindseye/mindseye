Westmr. 

Grace Wood (wife of John Wood gone from her) Removed with her two Children John aged abt. Six Years & Charles aged abt. 4 Years from the Parish of St. Giles in the fields by Order dated the 20th. Instant upon Oath saith That she intermarried with the sd. John Wood at a Church in Windsor in the County of Berks upwds. of Seven Years since and that her sd. husband lived in and rented the Rising  Sun Alehouse near Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of Thirty three Pounds for one Year and upwds. and Quitted it at Midsummer last and at the same time Absconded from this Examint. and she has not since heard from him but beleives he has not rented any other house since

Sworn Augt. 23d 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Grace Wood
