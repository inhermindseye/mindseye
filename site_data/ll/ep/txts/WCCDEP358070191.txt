Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Latham Aged about Thirty Seven Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her late Husband Charles Latham at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields about Sixteen Years Ago and who has been dead about Three Months Says she has Two Children now living by her said late Husband Namely Henry Aged upwards of Eight Years and Thomas aged upwards of five Years Says that her said late Husband as he has often Informed her lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Benjamin Sedgley at the Ship and Anchor Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upwards of One Year but at what  Wages cannot say and after quitting that Service he went to her with one Mr. Marr a Hatter the Corner of Arundel Street in the Strand in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and lived with him upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds besides Diet and Lodgings as she believes And after quitting the said Mr. Marrs service he hired himself and went to live with one Mr. Morry weather Haberdasher in Hatts in the Parish of Saint Edmunds the King  Lombard Street London at the Yearly Wages of fifteen pounds and served his said Master accordingly for that time and received Wages agreeable thereto But says that this Examinant intermarried with her said late Husband about Six Months after he went to live with the said Mr. Merryweather on where Marriage or seven afterwards her said Husband with his Masters Consent took Lodging in Nicholas Lane in the Parish of St. Nicholas Acons where he Continued to lodge till the time he left his said Master which is about Eight Years ago since which time he never Rented House or otherwise gained any subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 31st. day of may 1774 before G. Merces W Kelyngs

Elizabeth Latham
