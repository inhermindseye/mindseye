St Clement Danes 30th Janry 1793

Children  (to wit) Susanna Muller Wife of Charles Augustus Muller (now from her) Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband Charles Augustus Muller at the parish Church of St Sepulchre without Newgate  London on or about the 30th. day of August in or about the year 1780 1779 1780 (by Banns) that her said Husband Rented & with her this Examinant resided in an House Numbered 226 Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about 5 years at the Annual Rent of £70 that they quitted the same in or abt. the Month of July last and hath no her said Husband Husband hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or otherwise obtained Any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowleg knowledge information or belief that She hath four Children by her said Husband now living neither of whom hath obtained any legal in his or her own right Namely Charles Augustus born on or about the 5th day of November in or about the year 1781 Susanna Maria born on or about the 25th day of Febry. 1787 Augustus Alexander born on or about the 4th day of April 1789 and Mary Ann born on or about 2d. day of June 1791 that her

said Husband hath left her upwards of a Month ago And that she is destitute and unable to provide for herself & the three younger Children now living with her this Examinant without some Assistance

Sworn the 30th day of January 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Susanna Muller 's Mark

Witness Garrett
