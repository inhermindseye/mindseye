Middx  Wm. Waters . upon Oath saith he never was married and that he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Marriner in the Parish of Ash in the County of Hants at the Yearly Wages of £5 quitted his sd Service about 7 yrs. ago & has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any otherplace to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 14th. 1751 before Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Wm. Waters
