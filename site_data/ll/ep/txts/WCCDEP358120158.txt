Middx  Sarah Mitchel upon Oath saith she is the wido . of James Mitchell to whom she was married at the Fleet London abt. 25 Yrs. ago that her sd. husband has been dead abt. 14 Yrs. and that about 5 Yrs. before his Decease he rented a house of the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes & lived therein about three Months & that after he quitted the sd. house neither her sd. late husband Whilst he lived nor has this Examint. since his Decease rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn July 20th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell Sarah Mitchell
