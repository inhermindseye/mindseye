Middx  Ann Bradford  Widow late Wife Joseph Bradford deceased maketh Oath and Saith that about Sixteen Years ago her said late Husband Josh: Bradford was Bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to John Arnold of the Bankside in the parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry and Served all his Apprentiship there and has gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge And further Saith that She hath gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge

Sworn this 12th. day of July 1745 Before Robert Dennett

her Ann Bradford Mark
