Middx  Hannah Davis  Widow late Wife of John Davis deceased maketh Oath and Saith that about Forty Five Years ago her said Husband was Bound out Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to Mr . William Waters of the parish of St. Peters in the City of Bristol and Served his said Master there about four Years and then was turned to Mrs . Margaret Noble of the parish of St. Phillip at Bristol in the County of Gloucester  Dyer and Served his said Mistress there about three Months and then went to Sea And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge And that She has gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge And hath four Children Elizth . Aged about thirteen Years John Aged about Eleven Years Benjamin Aged about Six Years and Edward Aged about four Years

Sworn this 11th day of September 1746 Before us Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

her Hanh. Davis Mark
