Middx  Mary Mogridge aged about 24 Years Upon Oath says she was never Married nor was an Apprentice or a Yearly hired Servant for One Year nor ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes says her Father David Mogridge informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he served seven Years Apprenticeship to a Taylor in the parish of Dunster in the County of Somerset and never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx where she now lodges she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born says that one Benjamin Deane a Picture framemaker who works in Wych Street in the parish of St. Mary le Strand had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Christmas Week 1764 and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said Benjn. Deane and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn this 31st. day of May 1765 . Before} Saunders Welch

The Mark of Mary Mogridge
