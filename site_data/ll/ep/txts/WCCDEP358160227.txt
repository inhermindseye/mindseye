Middx  The Exaicon of Martha Blie  Singlewoman living in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the County of Middx taken the 12th- Day of January 1748 Who upon her Oath saith yt. she lived as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Cooper in the Parish of St. Faith under St. Paul  London  Pastry Cook at the Yearly Wages of £4..10s for the Space of two Years and upwards & further upon her Oath saith yt. since she left her sd. Service never rented £10 a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being sick & Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Martha Blie
