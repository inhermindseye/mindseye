Westmr.  Diana Middleton upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Richard Middleton on or about the 4th. of Augt. 1740 at the Fleet London Says her sd. husband has for three Years last past lived in and rented a house of £26 a Year in Cary Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . Says abt. three months ago her sd. husband absconded from her and she still continues to live in the sd. house and hath her Children now at home with her John aged abt. 4 Years & Dorothy aged abt. 3 Months

Sworn March 31st. 1748 before} Tho. Lane

Diana Middelton
