Middx ss  Sarah Norton  Widow of Thomas Norton deced Maketh oath that about Twenty one Years ago she lived as a hired servant (being then a singlewoman) with Mr. Hinds a Glassmaker in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry & by means there & Continued in such Service for the space of one Year at the Yearly wages of Three pounds & ten Shillings And this Depont doth not know where her said late husbands legal settlement is

Sworn this first day of Decr 1755 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

his Sarah Norton Mark
