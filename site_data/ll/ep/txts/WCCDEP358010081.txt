Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster To wit Christian Vetch aged about 49 Years upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with Alexander Vetch her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about 28 years ago that he has been dead about 2 Years that she hath 4 Children living by her said late Husband vizt. Charlotte aged about 21 years George aged about 17 years Christian aged about 15 years and Robert aged About 8 years that her said late Husband Rented 2 Shops in Saville Row Passage in the Parish of Saint  James in the Liberty of Westmr . for about 18 months at the yearly Rent of £10 during which time he lived in Swallow Street in the said Parish that he hath quitted said House about 14 years ago since which time he never did any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of March 1787  Sampr Wright  Wm. Kitchiner

his Christian Veitch Mark
