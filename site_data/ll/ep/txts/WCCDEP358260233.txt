Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Argent aged abouts Eighteen years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that she was born in Houghton Street Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 29th. day of Novr. 1797 before me } W Carter

her Elizh. Argent mark

Witness B. Newman
