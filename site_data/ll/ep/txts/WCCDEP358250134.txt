Saint Clement Danes

Settlement at Kingswood in Gloucester to wit Hannah Swain of the Borough Market on behalf of William Pike aged about 13 years and Matthew Pike aged about Two years and an half Maketh Oath and Saith That the said Matthew and William are the Two legitimate Children of William and Esther Pike (both deceased) who were married at the Parish Church of Saint Bennetts  Upper Thames Street about 18 years ago That the said Wm. Pike the Elder was born in Bristol as this Deponent has been informed but in what Parish she this Deponent does not know, That he never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge Information or belief And this Deponent further saith that she has been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that Esther Pike the Mother was born at Kingswood in the Parish of Kingswood in the County of Gloucester but whether the said Esther Pike ever lived as an Hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months ever bound an Apprentice Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes this Deponent doth not know

Sworn this 1st. day of Novr. 1794 before } J Hlind J Mathews

Hannah Swain
