Middx  Mary Byrn  Wido . of John Byrn upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said John Byrn at Eversley in the County of Hunts upwards of 15 Years since Says her sd. late husband lived a Yearly Servant with Sr. Jno. Cope  Bart . at Eversley aforesd. for one Year and upwards Part thereof before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & the remainder of the sd. Term after Says her sd. late husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 9th. 1747 before}
