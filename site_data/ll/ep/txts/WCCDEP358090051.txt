Middx  Eleanor Hirkton aged about Fifty four Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one George Crawford  an Attorney at Law in Cross Lane St. Mary at Hill in the City of London for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Nineteen Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. day of July 1779 before  Abrm Baley 

her Eleanor Kirkton Mark
