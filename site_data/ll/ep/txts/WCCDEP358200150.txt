Middx  Lydia Midford Aged about Thirty Six Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Joseph Midford with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. John in the Town of Glacingbury in the County of Somersett who has been dead about Three Years And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the Seventeenth Day of October Last in St. Clements Lane in in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Richard Says One Richd. Selwyn an Ostler at the Ax Inn in Aldermanbury in the City of London

had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body about the fifteenth or Sixteenth of January Last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the 5th Day of November 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Lydia Midford
