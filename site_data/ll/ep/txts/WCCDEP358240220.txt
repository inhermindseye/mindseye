St Clement Danes 20th Febry. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  Children provided for  (to wit) Sarah Lott aged 62 years or thereabts. Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present late Husband William Lott at the parish Church of Crediton near Exeter in the County of Devon (by Banns) 24th June 1759 . that her said Husband after the said Marriage her said Husband Rented and resided in an House in Great Shire Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex between seven and eight years at the Annual Rent of £15 that He quitted that House about five years ago and never after during his Life Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise acquired any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief and that since her said Husband Died about ten Weeks ago and she hath not since been Married or obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right that she hath two Children both provided for

Sworn the 20th day of February 1793 before me} Wm Kitchiner

S Lott
