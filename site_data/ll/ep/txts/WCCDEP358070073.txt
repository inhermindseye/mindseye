Westmr. to wit Mary Duce Aged about Twenty Three Years on on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Samuel Duce (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at Christs Church in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry about Two Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says that her said Husband Served Seven Years apprenticeship with one George Small a Spur Maker Colmer in the Parish of Blockwich in the County of Stafford and After he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House Paid Taxes or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of March 1773 before Us. R. Butler 

her Mary Duce Mark

Witness E: Fields
