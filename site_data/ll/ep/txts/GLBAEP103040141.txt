Middlesex ss.  Amelia Trundell  Singlewoman maketh Oath that about Five Years ago she was hired as a Servant by the Year to Mr. Purser of Nr. 5 Brunswick Street in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Surrey Surveyor at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds or thereabouts & continued in his Service under such hiring for the space of Seventeen Months that she has not since she quitted his Service lived as a hired Servant by the Year with any other Person in any Parish or Place for the space of One Year together or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that she never was Married, And further saith that she is now with Child and being with Child is deemed & taken to be a Person chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate where she now resides.

Sworn this 5th: Day of Septr. 1798 before H: Reynell R Davies

her Amelia Trundell Mark
