Middlesex ss}  Henry Long maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds in Glen Alley Tooley street in the Parish of saint olave in Southwark in the County of Surrey and resided thereon for the Space of five Months That he has not since rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act whatsoever to his Knowledge or belief whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 25. day of April 1787 before  R Davies  Robt:Smith

The Mark of Henry Long
