Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Mumford the widow of John Mumford deced maketh oath that many years ago her said late husband rented a house in Crown Court in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aligate in the said County & was charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain another Settlement That She this Depont Since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath a Daughter name Mary Mumford by her said late husband who lived with this Deponent & her sd husband in Crown Court as part of their Family and who was river bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year or was ever married nor who ever did any act to gain a Settlement in her own right to this Deponts knowledge or belief

Sworn this. 6. day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Mumford
