Middlesex ss}  Mary Henman the wife of Thomas Henman maketh oath That her said Husband who is now absent from her at sea divers Years ago rented a house of the yearly Rent of nine Pounds in Peter Street in the Mint in the Parish of Saint George in Southwark in the County of Surry and during his residence therein he was Charged to & paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent hath four Children namely Ann Aged about Twelve Years & four Months Mariah aged about nine Years mary aged about Five Years & ten Months & Elizabeth aged about three Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to Whom She was lawfully married none of Which Children have done any Act to gain a Settlement.

Sworn this. 8. day of September 1778 before P. Rowlands Wm Blackmore

The Mark of Mary Henman
