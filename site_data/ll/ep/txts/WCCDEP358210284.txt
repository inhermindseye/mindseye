Middx & Westmr.  Mary Lund aged about Twenty three Years, Upon her Voluntary Oath says she never was Married, and that on the Twenty fourth day of March last past, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Mitchell in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex ; which hath been Baptized by the Name of John Says that one John Lewin a Waiter at the Hum Mums Bagnio in Covent Garden is the reputed Father of the said Male Bastard Child, he having had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of July 1767, and at several times afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid, and that the said John Lewin and no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 11th, day of June 1768 , before W Kelynge

Mary Lund
