Westmr. to wit Joseph Chappellon aged about 88 Years on his Oath says he never was married Rented Ten pounds a Year or Paid Taxes Says he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Saml. Norman a Wollen Draper at Henley upon Thames in the County of Oxford upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds hath quitted his said Services upwards of 4 years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 19th. day of June 1772 before us} Tho Lane R. Butler

Joseph Chappellon
