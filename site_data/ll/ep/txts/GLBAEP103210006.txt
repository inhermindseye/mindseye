Middlesex ss}  Ann Paddington  Single woman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr. Blore an Undertaker in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & half & has not since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was she ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent hath a Child named or Called George (but which Child was not as Yet been baptized) aged thirteen months born of this Deponents body in a Place Called Molsom in the Parish of Chelmsford in the County of Essex the which Child is a Bastard & illegitimate by reason that She this Deponent was never married and that Thomas Newport late of the Sd Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate  Japanner is the Father of the Said Child

Sworn this 26. day of July 1784 before R Davies Ann Paddington
