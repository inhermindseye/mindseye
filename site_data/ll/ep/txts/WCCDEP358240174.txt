Elizabeth Evans

Saint Clement Danes 24. Oct. 1792

Middx and Westminster }  St James Clerkenwell  to wit Mary Evans aged about 40 years (on behalf of her illegitimate Child Elizabeth Evans ) upon her Oath saith That she never was married That her said Child was born at the House of John Taylor of No. 8 Saint Johns Lane in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex Bricklayer between 7 and Eight years ago That the said Child was born on the Body of her this Deponent out of lawful Wedlock That her said Child was christened by the Name of Elizabeth Evans at the Parish Church of Saint Johns Clerkenwell in the said County That her said Child hath never been bound an Apprentice or otherwise obtained or become intilled unto any subsequent legal Settlement and that she this Deponent is unable to provide for the said Child without Assistance

Sworn the 24th. day of Octr. 1792 Before  M Addington Gd: Mitchell

her Mary Evan Mark
