Middx to wit Martha Robinson Aged about Fifty Three Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes or was an Apprentice says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Pritchard a Surgeon in Hart Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds Eight Shillings hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago since which time She hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1780 before R Butler Abrm. Baley

her Martha Robinson Mark
