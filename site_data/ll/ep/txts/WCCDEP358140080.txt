Middx.  Elizth. Reed aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thos. Reed who dyed the 18th. day of Octr. 1757. says her Sd. late husbd. whilst he was a single Man lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Chas. Scriven a Glazier in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for 2 Years at the Yearly Wages of twelve Pounds & never after Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn this 28th. of Janry 1758 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Eliz: Reed
