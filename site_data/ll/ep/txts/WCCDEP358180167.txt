Middx  Eliza: Coleman aged abt. 60 Years Wife of Roger Coleman aged upwards of 50 Years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with sd. Husband at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex abt. Twenty Years since that abt. two Years ago her sd. Husband rented a House near Windsor Court in the Parish of St Mary Le Strand in the Libty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £26 & lived in the same for the Space of One Year & pd. Rent for that time at the sd. Rate since which time he hath not rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 15th. February 1743 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Eliz: Coleman
