Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Day aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Berry in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Sixteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Seven years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she on or about the sixth day of November 1792 she this Deponent was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of One Mrs. Hanlon in Bennetts Court in the said parish which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary Ann That one John Wright a Glazier who did live in Stanhope Street and now lives as she believes at No. 52 Lambeth Marsh is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid thereof and that the said John Wright is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 11th- day of March 1797 before me} Saml. Edward

her Mary Day Mark
