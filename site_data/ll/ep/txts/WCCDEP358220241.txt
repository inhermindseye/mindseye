Westmr.  William Tippett Aged about fifty Six Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Susannah his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint George in the Borrough of Southwark in the County of Surry about four Years ago Says that before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Eleanor Goodson a Milkwoman for Six Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds Vizt. the first 3 Years in the Parish of St Mary Overs & the last Three Years in the Parish of St. Olave in the said Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry hath quitted his said Service about four Years Ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn this 25th. day of February 1771 before} R. Butler 

his William Tippett Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
