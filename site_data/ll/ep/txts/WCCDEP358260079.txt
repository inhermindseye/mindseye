Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit William Slemaker aged about 50 years upon his Oath saith That about Three years ago he Intermarried with Jane his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That previous to his Intermarriage and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Dawson who kept the Sign of the Cock and Bottle in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for about Fourteen Months at the yearly wages of Ten Guineas That he hath quitted said Service about Thirty years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 19th. day of Octr. 1795 } Jno Groves

William: Slaymaker .
