Middx  Jane Bromley Wido. upon Oath saith she intermarried with her late husband Saml Bromley at the Fleet London upwards of three Years ago by whom she hath one Child Robert Chas . aged about two Years and that her sd. late husband (as she hath been informed and verily believes) Served five Years of his Apprentiship with Mr Edwards a Peruke Maker opposite Great Wild Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the End of which time his Master dyed and her sd. late husband never was Assigned over nor ever rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year and that the said Saml Bromley dyed in October last since which she has not rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes and this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath further Saith that she never was married to any other person but

the said Saml. Bromley and that about Seven Years ago she was delivered of a female bastard Child in Charles Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. which was baptized Ann and is still living

Sworn Febry 3d. 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Jane Bromley
