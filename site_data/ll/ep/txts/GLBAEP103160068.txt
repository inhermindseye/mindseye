Middlesex ss}  John Arnett maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to & serve the whole thereof with James Douglas in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Sackmaker & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Dept. has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of John Arnet
