Middlesex ss}  Mary Brown the wife of John Brown maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore that her said husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Thomas Smith a Shoemakr in the Parish of St Dunstan in Canterbury in the County of Kent & has not since done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement & that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this 25 day of February 1780 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Mary Brown
