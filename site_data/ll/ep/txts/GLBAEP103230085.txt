Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Graham the widow of James Graham deced maketh oath that her Said late husband was by birth a Scotchman and never rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of they early rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in England nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for hersoff But this Deponent Nevertheless farther Saith that previous to her Marriage with her Said husband She was hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant by & With Mr Henry Foster a Tinman at Certain Wages in the Parish of St John Wapping in the Said County and Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Years & three Quarters and upon leaving Such Service was Married to her Said husband

Sworn this. 24. day of November 1787 before  R Davies  Rob. Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Graham
