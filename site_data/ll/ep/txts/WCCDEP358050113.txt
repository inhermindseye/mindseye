Westmr.  John Blyss aged about 59 Years upon Oath saith he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr Harding a Leatherseller, in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr. 6th 1749 before}

John Bliss
