Middx.  Stephen Pyke aged abt. 52 Years upon Oath says he lives a hired Yearly Servt. with Mrs. Mary Pyke  Grocer in Sheffield Street Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. and hath so done for about thirty Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds, says that he has no family and is going to live in the Fish Mongers Almshouses

Sworn this 8th. Septr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Stephen Pyke
