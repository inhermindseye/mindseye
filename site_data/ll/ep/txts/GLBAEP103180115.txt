Middlesex ss}  William Cale maketh oath That divers Years ago he lived in & rented a house at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pouds in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and Continued in the occupation of the same for about the Space of Six Months That he has not Since rented a house or other Tenement of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement elsewhere.

Sworn this 9th. day of January 1779 before  Jasper Clarkey

Wm. Cale
