Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  N  Margaret Ballantine aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that about 13 years ago she Intermarried with Robt. Ballantine her late Husband (who has been dead about 6 years) at the Parochial Chaple of Saint Nicholas at Liverpool and by whom she hath two Children living by her said late Husband Vizt. Elizth . aged about 11 years and James aged about 8 years both provided for that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Virginia Street in the Parish of St. George in the East in the County of Middx for 9 years at the yearly Kent of £15 hath quitted said House above 4 years since which time she has done no act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22th. day of Janry. 1787  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordons

her Margarett Ballantine Mark
