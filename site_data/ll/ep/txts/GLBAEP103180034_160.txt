Middlesex ss}  William Johnson maketh oath that he well knows a Child named Hannah Candelow aged about Eleven Years who as this Deponent has been informed & believes was born in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the Said County and is as this Deponent has also been informed & believes a Bastard & illegitimate the Mother of the Said Child having never been Married as she informed this Deponent & this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting the said Childs Settlement

Sworn this 24. of April 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

William Johnson
