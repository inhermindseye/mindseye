Middx  Susannah Bradin aged 65 Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr Howlett Butcher in Peter Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £4..10s for the Space of 4 Years & hath left her sd. Service abt. 3Yrs. since which time she hath not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne March 21st. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sus: Bradin
