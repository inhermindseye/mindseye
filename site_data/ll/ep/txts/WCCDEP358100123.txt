Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Wiseman Aged about Twenty two years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she has lived a hired yearly Servant with one Wm. Marriot a Chimney Sweeper in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upwards of one year & where she now lives at the Yearly Wages of fifty two Shillings & has gained no other Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born And further says that one Edward Miles a Journeyman to her said Master is the further thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of Decr. last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at several difft. times times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Edward Miles & no or. Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 15th. day of Septr. 1783 R Butler

her Sarah Wiseman mark
