Middx  Susa. Underwood aged about 28 Years upon Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Phillipson in Kings head Court in the Parish of St. Andrews in Holborn London for the Space of One Year & Six Months at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds & left her said Service in the Month of February last Says she never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 10th. July 1746 before Tho Lane

Susannah Underwood
