Middlesex ss}  Sarah French the widow of Robert French deced maketh oath that Since her Said husbands death She was hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr John Thompson a Victualler in the Minories in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate London and Continued in Such Service there under Such hiring for the Space of Fourteen Months And has not Since done any Act to gain anothr Settlement

Sworn this. 30. day of Novemr 1786 before R Davies

Sarah French
