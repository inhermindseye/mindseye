Middx  John Hudson upon Oath he was bound Apprentice to Mr Reddish of Covent Garden with whom he lived about four Years & then was assigned once to Mr Lucas Vintner with whom he lived as an Apprentice for three Years or thereabouts in Brookes Market in the Parish of St. Andw. in Holborn above the Barrs in ye County of Middx that his sd. Apprentiship Expired in last and he has not since rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn Jany. 22d. 1753 Before
