Middlesex ss}  James Patrick maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre to John Neathercoate of the Parish of St Botolph witht. Aldgate in the Sd County Baker for Seven Years & after having Six Months Served Six Years & half of Such term he went on board a Man of War & his Sd Master received his Wages for the last half year thereof as he has been informed & believes That Since Such his Apprenticeship he has not rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement nor Since Such his apprenticeship has he lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year & that he has Wife name Sarah & two Children namely Eliz aged 12 Years & James aged Seven Years

Sworn this 11th day of Febry 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of James Patrick
