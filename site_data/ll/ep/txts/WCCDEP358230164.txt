Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizabeth Bertles who is Ill upon his Oath saith That he hath been Informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said Elizabeth Bertles never did any act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement in her Own right That her Father Benjamin Bertles rented a House for 30 years in New castle Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechaple in the County of Middlesex at the yearly rent of Sixteen Pounds where her Father and Mother Died

Sworn this 27th day of Novr 1800 before us} Saml Edwards  John Collick

Jas Talboys
