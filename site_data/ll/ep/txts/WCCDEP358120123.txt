Middx  Fras. Clues upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servt. with Mrs. Nixon at the Dolphin opposite Essex Strt. in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for fifteen months at the yearly Wages of £4. quitted his sd Service abt. 18 Years ago and has not Since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn June 18th. 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Fras. Clues
