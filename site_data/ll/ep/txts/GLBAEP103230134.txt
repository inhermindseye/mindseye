Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Sarah Dean of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex Singlewoman taken on oath before me Robert Smith  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this twenty Second day of April one thousand Seven hundred and eighty eight - Who Saith that on the Ninth day of April instant at the house of Mr Carnley Grocer in Lower East Smithfield in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate She the said Sarah Dean was delivered of a Male Bastard Child and that the Sd Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that Richard Roberts of Fenchurch Street London Wine Merchant did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the day & year above Written before me  R Smith

The Mark of Sarah Dean
