Middx.  Mary Hurst aged upwards of 40 Years upon Oath says, she never was Married, says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Shudall a Robemaker in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for four years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. Nine Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 5th. day of October 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Hurst
