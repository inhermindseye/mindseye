Westmr.  Wm. Hawkens Aged about Sixty One Years Lodging in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Elizabeth an St. Martins Church in the Parish of St Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Thirty Six Years ago Says he Rented a House in Great Bennetts Court Opposite Beauford Buildings in the Strand in the said Parish of St. Martin upwards of ten Years at the Yearly Rent of ten Pounds 

and upwards and Paid all Taxes hath quitted said House about Twenty two Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the 17th of March 1764 Before}
