Westmr. to wit Elizth. Bristow aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr James Ward a Cheesemonger in Black Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Six Months and hath not since Rented House or done any other act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she was Yearly delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to this Parish and further says that one Isaac Sleigh  Journeyman to the said Mr. Ward had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in his the said Mr. Wards house sometime in the Month of October last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Isaac Sleigh and no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 14th. day of July 1773 before R. Butler

her Elizth. Bristow Mark
