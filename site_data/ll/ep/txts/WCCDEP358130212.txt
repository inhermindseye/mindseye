Middx  Joanna Row aged about 40 years upon Oath says She never was married Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant upwards of a year with Mr. Neve a Watchmaker the corner of Fountain Court in the Strand at the Yearly wages of Five Pounds and has quitted her said Service upwards of two Years and has not since lived in any other Service for one Year Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn the Day of Septr. 1756. before }
