Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Homer the widow of John Homer deced maketh oath that she can give no Account respecting the settlement of her late husbands Parents deced or eithr of them That her said late husband has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was born in  Swan Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & to her knowledge or belief he never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement since his birth That she hath two Children namely John Aged about nine Years & Sarah Aged about fifteen months the lawful issue of this Deponent by her sd late husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this. 11. day of February 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Homer
