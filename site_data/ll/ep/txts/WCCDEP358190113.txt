Middx  James Edwards aged about 44 Years Removed from the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate London upon Oath saith that he was Married to Elizabeth his Wife at St. Leonard's Church Shoreditch the 14th: day of July 1748 Old Stile by whom he hath one Child to wit Daniel aged Eight Years That he was (before his Intermarriage and while he was a Single Man ) a Yearly hired Servant to Mr. Nixon at the Dolphin near Saint St. Clement's Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and lived with him as such upwards of One year at the yearly Wages of Six pounds Quitted the same about Two Years before his Marriage and hath not since kept any House paid Parish Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the day of April 1761 Before}
