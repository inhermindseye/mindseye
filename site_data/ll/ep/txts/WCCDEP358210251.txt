Middx & Westmr.  Mary Jarrett Aged about Twenty six Years upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Doctor Lawson in New Inn No. 7 on the Ground Floor, in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex , for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds, four Shillings, Quitted said Service about Eight Weeks ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds or paid Taxes And Further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when Born; says that one Robert Newman late a Clerk to Mr. Stoke, Attorney at Law in New Inn aforesaid had carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of May 1767, and at several times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant, and that the said Robert Newman and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 8th day of March 1768 , Before} 

The Mark of Mary Jarrett
