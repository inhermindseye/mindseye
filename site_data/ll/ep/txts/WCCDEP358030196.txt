Middx  Horatio Merry aged 65 Yrs upon Oath says he intermarried with Martha his Wife at the Fleet London about 12 Yrs. since Says she is about 55 Yrs. old Says he was bound an Apprentice to George Rosselier & Served five Years or thereabouts the last part of his Apprentiship with the sd. Mr Rosselier in Beaufort Buildings in the parish of Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Says he has been out of his sd. Apprentiship abt. 44 Yrs Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif Says he is poor & not able to maintain himself & his sd. Wife

Sworn Augt 6. 1746 before  Tho Lane

Horatio Merry
