Middx The Examination of Jane Seymour  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 20th. Day of Decr. 1739 Who upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Childn. wch When Born will be a Bastard or Bastards and likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintaine the same wn Born And further upon her Oath saith that one Jos: Leaver  Gardiner to Henry Masterman  Esqr . at Hillindon in the County of Middx did begett the sd. Child or Childn. on her Body she this Examint. at such time liveing as a Servt . with the sd. Hen: Masterman Esqr. & he the sd. Jos: Leaver liveing in the House there became acquainted with her & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body sevll. times in the Months of June & July last in the sd. House at one of which times he got her with the Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now Pregnant And that he & no other Person is the true and reall Father thereof And this Examint. further saith that her Father Jno. Seymour rented a House in Ship Yard in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of £16 or thereabouts lived in the same for the space of 16 or 17 Years & there Dyed And that she this Examint. never was an Apprentice or lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained any other Settlemt. to her Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

Jane Seymour
