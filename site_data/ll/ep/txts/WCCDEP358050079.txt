Westmr.  Eliz Hodges upon her Oath saith she is the Widow of Henry Hodges , with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of St Geo. Queen Square upwards of 19 Years ago Says her sd. late husband lived a Yearly Servt. whilst a Singleman with Mrs. Stoaks in Gloucester Street in the Parish of St George Queen Square aforesaid for one Year and at the Expiration of the Year intermarried with this Examinant and still continued to live with Mrs. Stoaks under his first agreement about two months longer

Says neither her sd. late husband or this Examint. since his Decease have ever rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 2d. 1749 before} Jas. Water

The mark of Eliz Hodges
