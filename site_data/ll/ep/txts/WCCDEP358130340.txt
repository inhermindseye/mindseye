Middx.  Thos. Holland aged abt. 46 yrs. upon Oath says he never Rented ten Pounds a year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servt. says his father Thos. Holland served seven years Apprenticeship with Mr. Crouch a Plaisterer in Crutched fryars in the Parish of St. Olave Hart Street in the City of London says his sd. father never Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or was a hired yearly Servt.

Sworn the 23d. of May 1757 before} Benjamin Cox

Tho: Holland
