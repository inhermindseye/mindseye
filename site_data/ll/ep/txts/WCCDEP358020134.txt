Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Bissett aged about 58 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Dr. Davis in Carey Street for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Ten years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this First day of April 1789  Wm Kitchiner .

Ann Bisset
