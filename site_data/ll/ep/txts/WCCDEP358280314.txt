St.. Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster } (to wit) Harriet Vitu Aged about 25 Years Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married, that she was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Alexander Dow of Great Mayes Buildings  St. Martins in the Lane in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex  Mantua & Stay Maker for six Years with whom she served the whole of her Apprenticeship which expired about Six years ago since which time she has not to the best of her knowledge Information or belief gained any Subsequent Settlement And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that on the 1st. day of January last she this Examinant was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at her lodging No. 22 Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County which has since been half Baptized by the Name of James And that James Sanders No. 18 Tash Street Grays Inn Lane Holborn Trirner & no one else is the real & true father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her this Examinants Body & begotten the same thereon And this Examinant further says she is in Indigent Circumstances and unable to Maintain the said Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid

Sworn the 22d. day of Febry. 1791 before me} R Butler

Harriet Vitu

Sanders Surrendd himself let go by Mr. Halm to find Security

Bond of Jas. John & Geo. Sanders Reled to Indemnify the parish 28th. March 1791 (Sanders paid Exps.)
