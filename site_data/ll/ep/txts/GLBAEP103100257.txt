Middlesex ss  Esther Ogier  Singlewoman Maketh oath That in the year one thousand Seven hundred and forty five she was bound apprentice by Indre to Mary Holloway  Patron Reader then the wife of Isaac Holloway of the Hamlet of Bethnal Green (but now called the Parish of St. Matthew Bethnal Green) in the county of Middlesex  Weaver for the Term of Four years & served the whole of her said apprenticeship there, That at the expiration of her said Apprenticeship (the said Isaac Holloway being then dead) she this Deponent was hired by her said Mistress then a Widow ) and lived with her as a hired servant by the year in the same place and Continued in Such Service for a year at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds and by Means there of as she is advised gained a legal settlement there and that she this Deponent hav not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married Nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th: Day of April 1770 before  Chris: Scott  Rob Pell

E S O Esther Ogier Her Mark
