Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Margaret Charlton of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in in the Sd County Singlewoman taken upon oath before me Rice Davies  Esqr  one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County this Sixth day of November one thousand Seven hundred & eighty Six Who Saith that on the tenth day of October last past at the Work house in the Said Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate She the Said Margaret Charlton was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since baptized & registred by the name of Rachael Charlton and that the Said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the Sd Parish And that John Way land late of Swan Alley in the Same Parish Labourer did get her with Child of the Sd Bastard Child

Taken & Signed the day & Year above written before me R Davies

The Mark of Margaret Charlton Junr.
