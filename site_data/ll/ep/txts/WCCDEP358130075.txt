Middx  Alice Mackquay Upon Oath Saith She is the wife of Nenle Mackquay that the sd. Nenle Mackquay was born in the Parish of Clenvekill in the Kingdom of Ireland and Served three Years Apprentishipp with one Mr. Macure in the same Parish as She hath been informed & beleives

Sworn the 25th. day of Octr. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Alice Mackquay
