Middlesex ss}  Perthenea Worner the Widow of Thomas Worner  mariner deced maketh oath That about Thirty Four Years ago She was married to her Said late husband at the Fleet That She this Deponent has been informed by her Said husbands Mother (whose Maiden name was Susanna Bean & which She believes to be true That She the said Susanna Bean was born in the Precinct of Saint Catherine near the Tower of London & that her Son this Deponts sd late husband was born in King Henry Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx & Baptized at the Church belonging to the Said Parish of St Botolph Aldgate That this Deponents sd. late husband was born a Bastard & illegitimate being begotten on the Body of her said Susanna Bedu by one Thomas Worner to whom She was never married & during the true of her Widow hood That this Deponents sd husband to her Knowledge or belief never was bound an Apprentice nor over lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 30th day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Pertenea Worner
