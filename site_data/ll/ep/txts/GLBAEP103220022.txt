Middlesex ss}  Simon Norton maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages by & with Mr Mayhew a Cabinet maker in Marshall Street near Broad Street Carnaby market in the Parish of Saint James in Westmr in the Sd County & Continued in Such service under Such hiring for the Space of Seven Years & upwards That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish Office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8. day of February 1786 before R Davies Robr Smith

The Mark of Simon Norton
