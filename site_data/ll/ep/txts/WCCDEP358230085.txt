Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminter} to wit Ann Tunley Aged about 38 years upon her Oath Saith that she lived as and hired yearly Servant for 15 Months with Mr Arnold of Elerton Heath in the Parish of Uxbridge in the County of Middlesex that she left the said place about the year 1980 since which time she hath not lived as an hired yearly Servant Rented any house tenement or Lodging of the yearly Value of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any other Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief and this Examinant further Saith that about the year 1783 she intermarried with her late Husband Matthew Tunley who she verily believes was born in Herefordshire but that she this Examinant does not know the Place of his Birth or whether her said husband ever gained a Settlement in his own Right and further this Examinant saith that her said Husband died on or about the year 1786

Sworn this 17th day of August 1799 before us
