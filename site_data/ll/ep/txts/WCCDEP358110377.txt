Middx  Joseph Drake on Behalf of Susannah aged abt. 17 Yrs. on his Oath Saith the sd. Susannah is the Daughter of Hannah Breers  Singlewoman decd. & this Examinant  acknowledges himself to be the putative father of the sd. Susannah and further Maketh Oath that she was born at his house in Jerusalem Court in the Parish of St. James at Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex this Examint. & the sd. Susannah's mother at that time living together as man & wife and that the sd. Susa. never was an apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Decr 26th 1753 before} Barth: Hammond

Joseph Drake
