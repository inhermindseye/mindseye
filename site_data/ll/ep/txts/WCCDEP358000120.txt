Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

not Sworn  Judith Westwood Aged about 55 Years on her Oath Saith that the Intermarried to Thomas Westwood her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the year 1759 and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Saith that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Holywell Court in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds for upwards of Five Years and since he quitted the said House hath not to the best of this Examinants Knowledge information or belief done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the May have gained a Settlement Elswhere.
