Middlesex ss}  Mary Michell the Wife of Michael Michell maketh oath that her Said husband is by birth a Foreigner being born at Rome as he has informed this Deponent & as this Depon Verily believes That to her Knowledge or belief her Said husband never did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself in Great Britain That this Deponent can give no Account respecting the Settlement of his Parents That She this Depont before her marriage with her said husband was hired by & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Yearly Wages with her Aunt Isabella Bellamy in Anchor Rope Alley in the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middlesex & Continued in Such Service for Several Years & by means there of gained a Legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement other than what she might have Acquired by her Marriage in manner as before mentioned That she this Deponent hath a Daughter non living named Catherine Michell aged near Twenty one Years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband who was born in Anchor Rope Alley in the Said parish of Saint George who is at present likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & who hath never been bound an Apprentice or ever lived as a hired Servant for a Year or done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 10th day of october 1775 before  Wm Blackmore  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Mary Michall
