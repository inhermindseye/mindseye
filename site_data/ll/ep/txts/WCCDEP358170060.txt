Middx  The Examination of Margt- Tanner aged about Nineteen Years passed from the Parish of St Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr - by Order bearing Date the 24th. Day of April last taken the Day of May 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. one Year ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Adams in White Horse Yard for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £2..12S & further saith that since she left her sd. Service she never rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
