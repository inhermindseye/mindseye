Middlesex ss  James Sellers maketh oath that his late Father John Sellers deced heretofore rented a house at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere to this Deponents Knowledge or belief That he this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right That he hath a wife named Lucy to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 16. day of June 1786 before R Davies

James Sellers
