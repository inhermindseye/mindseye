Westmr.  Richard Platt upon Oath saith he intermarried wth. his wife Jane Platt in the parish Church of St. Ann Soho  on the 27th. May 1733 by whom He has a Child named Rd . aged abt. 6 years & that he served seven years apprentiship with James Platt a Shoemaker in Exeter Street in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Septr. 29th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Richard Platt
