Middx  Lucey Biggs  Wido . of Thos. Biggs upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Thos. Biggs on the 6th, Day of October 1736 at the Fleet London Says the sd. Thos. Biggs hath been Dead upwards of two Years Says he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Bryan a Brush maker over agt. Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Says her sd. late Husband never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 21st. 1745 before  Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Lucey Briggs
