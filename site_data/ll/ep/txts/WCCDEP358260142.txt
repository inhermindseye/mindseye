Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Frier of Little Shire Lane on behalf of Ann Gifford who is Old and infirm Maketh Oath and Saith that she has been informed which Information she verily believes to be true That the said Ann Gifford lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Williams a Linnen draper near Temple Bar in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards and that after she quitted the said Service she Never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief did any Act Matter or thing whereby the said Ann Gifford might have gained any other settlement

Sworn this 26th- day of July 1796 } J Reeves

her Elizabeth Frier Mark
