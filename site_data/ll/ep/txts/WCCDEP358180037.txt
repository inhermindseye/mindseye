Middx  Isabella Timson upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of Joshua Timson  Deceased that her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to & Served his Apprentiship with a Cobler at the Corner of King Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex that since his said Apprentiship he in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 13th- Novr. 1742 before}
