Middx   Susanna Cammock aged about 18 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married bound an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant in any one Place for Twelve Months together says her Father  John Cammock who has been Dead about 15 Years was a School Master and lived in and rented a House in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster (as her Mother hath informed her) but cannot tell what rent he paid or how long he kept the same also Says the officers of the said Parish of St. James granted her said Father a Certificate to Ashford in Middx thereby Acknowledging him to be an Inhabitant legally Settled in their said Parish

Sworn the 18th. Jany 1762 Before Me us} Fran: Bedwell  Saunder Welch

The Mark of Susanna Cammock
