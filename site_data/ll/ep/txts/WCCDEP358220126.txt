Middlesex & Westminster }  Mary Toole aged about 40 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Patrick Toole who has been Dead about Four Years and an half By whom she has one Child to wit Susanh . aged 12 Years says her said Husband was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief says before her said Intermarriage she was bound an Apprentice for 5 Years to Mrs. Ann Padget Milliner in King Street  Westminster in the Parish of Saint Margarett in the City of Westminster and served 4 Years & upwards of her said Apprenticeship with the said Mrs. Padgett & then Intermarried with her said Husband says she hath not since her said Husbands Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Taxes.

Sworn this 21st. day of Febry. 1770 before us } Tho Fellows

Mary Toole
