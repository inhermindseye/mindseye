Middx  Mary Bickerton upon Oath saith she intermarried with her husband Samuel Bickerton at the fleet London about 10 years ago & hath one Child by him (viz) Eliz. aged upwards of Seven years and that she hath been Informed and verily believes her sd. husband was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to George Paget a Mason and after having lived with him about five years the sd. George Paget hired this Examinants sd husband out to one Mr. Daintree a Mason who paid Paget Twelve Shillings P Week for his Labour that  her sd. husband continued to work with Mr. Daintree under these Circumstances for Twelve months or thereabouts and lay all that time at Mr. Archers at the Sign of the White Bear the Corner of Barbican in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldersgate London his sd Master Pages allowing him all that time Six Shillings P week to pay for his Lodging and Dyet And that she hath been further informed by her sd. husband that after he left Mr Daintree his Master Paget gave him leave to provide for himself as he could & never received money for his Service from any other person during his Apprentiship-Says her sd husband

never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 22d 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

Mary Bickerton
