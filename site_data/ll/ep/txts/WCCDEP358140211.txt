Middx.  Elizabeth Bacon aged abt. 36 Years upon Oath says; she never was Married. and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Bacon a Bedstead maker in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds and hath quitted said Service about One Year and not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn January the 12th: 1759 . Before} Paul Vaillant

Elizth bacon
