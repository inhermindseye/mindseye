Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Holland of No. 18 Wych Street on behalf of Thomas Stedman his Nephew who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said Thomas Stedman lived as an hired yearly Servant with an Apothecary in North Audley Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards That he has quitted said Service about five years since which time he has not lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or Belief whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Septr. 1790 }
