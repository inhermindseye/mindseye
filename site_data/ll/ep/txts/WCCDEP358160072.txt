Middx  The Examination of Mary Howfen  Wife of Dadrick Howfen taken the 30th. Augst.. 1739 Passed from the Parish of St. Ann in Blackfryers  Londn . by Order bearing date the 2d. of this Instant Augst. Who upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband Dadrick Howfen about 5 or 6 Years ago lived in & Rented a House in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £14 & lived in the same for Sevll. Years and further upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband never Rented any other House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife. That he now absconds from her & she being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Mary Howfen
