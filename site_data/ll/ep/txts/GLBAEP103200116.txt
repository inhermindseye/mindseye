Middlesex ss  Grace Dalton the Wife of Daniel Dalton maketh oath that her sd husband who hath lately absente himself from her to places to her unknown has informed her & which she believes to be true that he served the last two Years of his Apprenticeship to a Taylor (whose name he never mentioned to her) in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & She Can give no othr Account respecting his Place of Settlement That she this Depont on the third day of March instant (& not before) was lawfully married to the Sd Daniel Dalton in the Parish Church of St Saviour in Southwarke in the County of Surry That she hath a Child named Charles Dalton aged four Years & three Months who was born of this Deponents body in the Parish of Margate in Kent & baptized & registred in the Church there & anothr Child named John Dalton Aged about one Year & three Months who was born of her body in Chatham Barracks in the Parish of Chatham in Kent & baptized & registred in the Church there both of which sd Children are illegitimate And Bastards by reason that She this Deponent at the respective times of their several Births had not been married to the sd Daniel Dalton who is the true & only Father of such Children

Sworn this 11 day of March 1784 before R Davies

Grace Dalton
