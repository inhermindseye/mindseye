Westmr.  Benjn: Stevenson upon Oath Saith he intermarried with his late Wife  Sarah deced at the parish Church of St. Margt. Westr. about Seventeen Years ago. Says he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Saml. Stevenson  Brazier in Blackmoor Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif Says he has four Childn. by his sd. Wife (viz) Eliz aged 14 Yrs. Saml . aged near 11 Yrs. Sarah aged 5 Yrs. & Ann aged 2 Yrs. & Six months neither of which Children over was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant

Sworn 20th. Febry 1752 before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Benj: Stevenson
