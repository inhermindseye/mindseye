Middx  April 9th. 1753

Alice Ogden upon Oath Saith she is the Wido . of John Ogden who dyed abt. 13 Yrs. ago & that her sd. late husband rented a house of £11 by the Year in Long Millgate in the Old Church parish at Manchester in Lancashire & lived therein abt. 2 Yrs. & never afterwards nor has this Examinant Since his Decease rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt.

Sworn the Day & year Above mentioned before}
