Middx  Mary Drury upon her Oath saith that she is a Single Woman & was never marryed & that abt. 5 Yrs. ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Pd. Perry in Salisbury Court in Fleetstreet in the Parish of St. Bridgett regularly St. Brides  London at the Yearly Wages of £4. for the Space of one Yr. & upwds. that since she left her Sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year Pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 26th. of Janry 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Drury
