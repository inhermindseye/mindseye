Middx  James Bourne upon Oath saith he has been informed he was born in the parish of Dromisahen in the County of Lought in Ireland & that he lived a yearly Servant for one year and upwds. with Patrick Costaghan in the sd. parish and has not since rented ten pounds by the year or paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn March. 21st. 1754 before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of James Bourne
