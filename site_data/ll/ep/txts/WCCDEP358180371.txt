Middx  John Malson aged about 55 Years upon his Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Thos. Hatton in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx  Watch Graver and hath not since his said Apprentiship Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn August 21st. 1745 before} Wm: Jones John Malson
