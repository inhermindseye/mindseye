Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Brown Aged about 62 years upon his Oath Saith That he lived as a hired yearly Servant for 15 Months with one Mr Merville in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas That he hath left the said Service about 30 years since which time he hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for 12 Months, rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th day of Feby 1800 before us} Saml. Edwards  John Collick

John Brown
