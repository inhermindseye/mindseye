Middx  The Exaicon of Prudence Rice on behalfe of Mary Griffen now in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx who upon her Oath Saith that Wm. Griffen husband of the above named Mary Griffen is now a prisoner in Wood street Counter for Debt and that upwards of Twelve Months since the sd. Wm. Griffen lived in & Rented a House in the Strand in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds or upwards & continued to live in the same for the Space of one Year or thereabts. And further Saith that the sd. Wm. Griffen hath not since he left the sd. House rented Ten pounds a Year pd. parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examnts Knowledge or beleife & that the sd. Mary Griffen is Sick & poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the 21th day of Augst. 1739 before} Jas. Gronous

The mark of Prudence Rice
