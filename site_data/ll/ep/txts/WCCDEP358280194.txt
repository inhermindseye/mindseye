Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Susannah Owen aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Gardner in Lyons Inn for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examt. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Edward Pearson  Journeyman Shoemaker who lodged at Mrs. Arnolds Harmer a Chandlers Shop the Back of Saint Clements is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Edward Pearson is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 21st .day of Novr. 1788 } Wm. Kitchiner

her Susannah Owen Mark

Susannah Owen at Mrs. Harmer in Little Wych Street

Edwd. Pearson is a Middle Sized Man dark Complexion Thin Face wears his own Hair tied behind

not taken (nor worth taking) a Cobler
