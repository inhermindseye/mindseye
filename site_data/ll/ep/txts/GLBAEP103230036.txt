Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Robinson the widow of James Robinson deced maketh oath that her said late husband lately rented a house in Baileys Place in the Parish of st Botolph witht Aldgate in the said County & during his Residence therein he was charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same & has not since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this Deponent since his death hath not done any act to gain a Settlement for herself & that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 18-day of april 1787 before R Davies Rob Smith

Eliz Robinson
