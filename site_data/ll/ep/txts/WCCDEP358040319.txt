Mary Howell upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt . rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish & Taxes and that her father Served his Apprentiship with a Carpenter at Rayden in the County of Suffolk and never gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 15th 1748 before} Jno Lane

The mark of Mary Howell
