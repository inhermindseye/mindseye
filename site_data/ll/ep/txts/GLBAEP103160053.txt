Middlesex ss}  Thomas Pearce maketh oath That divers years ago he was hired by Richard Hopkins of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Victualler as his Servant for Which this Deponent was to be found & allowed meat Drink Washing & Lodging & Cloaths When he wanted them but there was no Agreement for wages or for any particular time of hiring That he this Deponent Continued under such hiring & agreement with the Said Richard Hopkins in his said house for the Space of one year & half & upwards & has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any parish office or paid any Poor Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 26. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Thomas Pearce
