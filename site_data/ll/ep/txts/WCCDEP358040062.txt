Westmr.  Mary Chapman aged about Twenty two Years upon Oath saith she never was married says she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Dunot a Jeweller in Durham Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the feilds in the Liberty of Westminster at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for one Year and upwards & hath left her said Service about two Years Says she hath not since she quitted ther said Mr. Dunotts Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year without Intermission or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 15th 1747 before} Wm.Jones  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Mary Chapman
