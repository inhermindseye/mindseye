Middlesex ss}  William Oram maketh oath That divers Years ago he was bound apprentice to Francis Arnold of Mouse Alley in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Lockmaker by Indenture and served the whole of his apprenticeship in the said Parish And this Depont. Saith that he never lived as a hired servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor ever served any Parish offices or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a settlement since his Said apprenticeship And that he hath a wife named Ann .

Sworn this 3d day of December March 1769 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of William Oram
