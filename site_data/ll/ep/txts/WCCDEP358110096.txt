Westmr-  Margt. Tarret wife of Richard Tarret upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Rd Tarret on the 9th. June 1745 at the Parish Church of Portsea in the County of Southampton and that her sd. husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Drew a Poulterer in Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. And further upon Oath saith she was delivered of a female bastard Child on the 12th. Day of July 1740 on board the Dreadnought man of War at the Nore (being at that time a Widow) and that the reputed father of the sd. Child John Critchat dyed upwds. of 8 Yrs. since

Sworn Septr. 28th. 1752 before}

The mark of Mary Tarret
