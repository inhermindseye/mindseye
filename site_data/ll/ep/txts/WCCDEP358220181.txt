Westminster to wit Isabell Little Aged about Thirty Nine Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of James Duneason who has been Dead about two Years Says she is a Native of Scotland and never Gained a Settlement in England either by Renting Ten Pounds a Year paying Taxes or living a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Oath Says that on the Twentieth day of September last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Joseph Field in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Elizabeth And that Archibald Bland a Stone Mason who now Works with Mr: Adams in Durham Yard is the real and true Father of the said Female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid

Sworn this 3d: day of Octr. 1770 before me} R. Butler

her Isabell Little Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
