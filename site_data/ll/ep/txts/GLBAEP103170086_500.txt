Middlesex ss}  Nicholas Cook maketh oath that Several years ago he rented a house in King Street in the parish of Christ Church in the County of Middlesex & lived therein for many years & during Such his Residence therein he was charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere.

Sworn this. 26. of June 1777 before Jasper Clarkes

Nichs Cooks
