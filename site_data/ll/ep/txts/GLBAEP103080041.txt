Middlesex ss. The Examination of Elizabeth Barber  Single woman taken an oath before Me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx residing in or Near the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx this Nineteenth day of August 1758.

This Examinant on her oath saith that about Nine Months ago one John Hollond a soldier belonging to his majesties First Regiment of Foot Guards did Prevail with this Examinant to Suffer him to lye with her at the dwelling house of one Mrs. Hines in Good mans Fields in the Parish of St Mary White chappel in ye sd County Where this Examinant and the said John Hollond lodged And the said John Hollond had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and so had several times whereby she Conceived with Child or Children of which she Now goeth and is Pregnant And this Examinant saith that the Child or Children of which she now goeth with and is Pregnant as aforesaid is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate she Not being able to Provide for the same And that the said John Hollond is the true and only father of the said Child or Children so likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards as aforesaid And lastly saith that this Examinacon is Voluntary And that she hath not been forced or compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whatsoever

Sworn this 19 Day of August 1758 before me  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Elizabeth Barber
