Ann Brooks upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Peter Brooks with whom she intermarried at the Fleet about 5 Yrs. ago and that her sd. husband informed her he lived in and rented a house in St Martins Lane in the parish of St Martin in the Fields Westmr. for sevl Years that he dyed abt. Ten Weeks ago and this Examint. has not Since his Decease rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes

Sworn July 2d. 1752 before Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Brooks
