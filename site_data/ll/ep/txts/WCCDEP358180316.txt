Middx  Richard Lewis aged 65 Years & upwards upon his Oath saith that about 4 Yrs. since he lived in and Rented a house in Brooke Street in the Parish of St. George in Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of £30 for the Space of 1 Year & upwds. & hath not since he left the sd. House Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 9th. April 1745 before} Wm: Jones

Richd Lewis
