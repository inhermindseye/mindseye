Middlesex ss}  Ann Curtin the widow of Francis Curtin deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her and Which She believes to be true that he was born in Cork in Ireland & to her knowledge information or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in Great Britain That she this Deponent previous to her Marriage with her Sd husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one Low a Shipwright in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in such service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there & has not since done any Act to gain a settlement for herself save what she Acquired by Marriage

Sworn this. 15. day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands.

The Mark of Ann Curtin
