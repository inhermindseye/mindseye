Middx  Jas. Smith aged abt. 20 Years upon Oath saith he Served three Years the last part of his Apprentiship with Mr. George Clark a Jackmaker in Little Queen Street Holbourn in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx . & never rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 24th. 1752 before  Tho Lane

James Smith
