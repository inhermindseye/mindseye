Middlesex  Ann Holmes  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that on the 10th. Day of October last she was Delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of Mrs. Richmond in Witch Street in the parish of St Clement Danes which hath since been Baptized Ann & is now liveing & Likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish & that one Joseph Beaucock  Labourer did begett the sd. Child on her Body he the sd. Joseph Beaucock at such time liveing as a hired Servt. with this Examinant at the sd. Mrs. Whites at Stratford there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body sevll. times before Christmas last at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Female Bastard Child which she was Delivered as aforesd. & that he the sd. Joseph Beaucock & no other person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworne 9th. Novr. 1743 before} Wm. Jones  Cha. Egerton

The Mark of Ann Holmes
