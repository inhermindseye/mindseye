Before Birth 

Saint Clement Danes 20th. July 1793.

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnancy  Reputed Father (to wit) Elizabeth Harvey aged 29 Years and upwards Upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Hudson late of Wych Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Victualler for five Years at the Annual Wages of Six Guineas that she quitted that place in or about the Year 1788 and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief And this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath further Saith That she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born and become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes when being that the said That Alexander Killgard  Tayler is the only real and true Father of the Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant as aforesaid he having first carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body about three Years ago and at several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children thereon and that he and no one else in the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of June 1793 before us } Wm. Kitchiner

her Elizabeth Harvey Mark

Witness Benj Newman
