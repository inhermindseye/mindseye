Saint Clement Danes 26th Janry 1786

Middlesex to wit - Mary Brown aged 17 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married And or ther Voluntary Oath saith that on the 9th Day of Janry Instant she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which has since been Child by the Name of John Brown and further saith that she is unable to maintain the said Child, and that Mr Lewis (where Christian Name this Examinant does not know) Son to Mr Lewis who keeps Silversmiths and Comb Shop in Saint Pauls Church Yard, is the real and true father of the said Child he having at several times had Casual of this Examinants Body and together the same thereon

Sworn this 26th Janry 1786 D Walker

her Mary Brown Mark
