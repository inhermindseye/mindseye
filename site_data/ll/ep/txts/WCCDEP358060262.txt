Middx  Susanna Brooks upon Oath Saith she intermarried with her husband Christr. Brooks at Fenny Stratford in Buckinghamshire  on the 17th. April 1749 by whom she hath one Child Mary aged about one Year and that she hath been Informed & verily believes her Sd Husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year nor ever rented ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes & that his father Henry Brooks deced Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Remnant a Carpenter in little Essex Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & never gained a subsequent Settlement in any other place

Sworn Febry 10th. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Susa. Brooks
