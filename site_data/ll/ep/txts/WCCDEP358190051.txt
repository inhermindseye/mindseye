Middx.  Ann Goodin aged abt. 45 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she now lodges at Mr. Nurseys in Wych Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx. says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Obadiah Weeks a Linnen Draper at the Sign of the three Nunns and Hare in Cheapside in the Parish of St. Peter's Cheap in the City of London for One Year & a half or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £6; and hath Quitted same about Twenty two Years and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year.

Sworn Sepr. 25th. 1760 before} Barth: Hammond S. Witch

The Mark of Ann Goodin

Upon Mr Penten's Complt. J: P:
