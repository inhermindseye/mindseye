Middx   Mary Peak aged about 26 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Richard Peak who has been Dead upwards of Three Years to whom she was Married at the Fleet London about Ten Years & an half ago That he lived in and rented a House known by the Sign of the Horse & Groom in Middle Row in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx for about One Year at the Yearly Rent of £27 and there dyed and that she this Examt. Continued in the Same for about Six Months after his decease and hath not since rented any House or paid Land Tax or Poor Rate That she hath one Child named Richard aged Six years & upwards

Sworn the 19th. day of Decr. 1761 Before}  Fran: Bedwell Barth: Hammond

Mary Peak her mark
