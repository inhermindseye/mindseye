Middx & Westmr.}  Richard Hargrave aged about 48 Years on his Oath says he never Rented House or Paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Thomas Carter  Horse Dealer who lived at the Red Lyon Hyde Park Corner in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds four shillings hath quitted his said Service upwards of Thirty Years ago since which time he hath gained no other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th- Day of March 1777 before us  Geo: Reid  John Croft

his Richd- Hargrave Mark
