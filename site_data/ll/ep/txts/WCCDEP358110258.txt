Middx  Jane Lewis aged 23 years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servt. with Thos. Richards in the Parish of Tolgolthie in the County of Merioneth in Wales for two Years at the yearly Wages of 29s for the first year & 30s for the Second year quitted her sd. Service abt. 3 Yrs. ago & has not since rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year and this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she was delivered of a male bastard Child on the 26th. Day of March last at Mr Stanboroughs in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which was baptized John and has been left chargeable upon the sd. Parish and that Joseph Connor a Writer did beget the sd Male bastard Child on her body the said Joseph Connor having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant sevl. times in the Month of June last several times, at the house of Mr Swallow a Writer

in Chancery Lane where this Examint. then lived a Servant. at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesd. and that the sd. Joseph Connor and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn June 11th. 1753 before W Fielding

The mark of Jane Lewis
