Middx  Elizth. Codd aged abt. Fifty Seven Years upon Oath She never was married Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says that David Codd her late Father deced Rented a House in Exeter Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes upwards of Twenty Pounds a Year & lived therein upwards of two Years & quitted the same  about the Year 1710 & that he never after Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes as She hath been informed & verily believes.

Sworn the 30th Day of July 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

Eliza Codd
