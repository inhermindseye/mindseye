Mary Williams  Wife of Peter Williams of Nightingale Lane in the parish of St. Ann at Limehouse in the County of Middx maketh Oath and Saith that Timothy Kenneday Son of George Kenneday who was an Irishman and as Born in the parish of Saint Ann at Limehouse in the County of Middx aforesaid And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge

Sworn this 23d. day of June 1746 Before Robert Dennett

her Mary Williams Mark
