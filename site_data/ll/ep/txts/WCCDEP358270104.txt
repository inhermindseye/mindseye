1st Examination Desertion

St Clement Danes March 28th. April 6th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Desertion  Settlemt.  3 Children } Charlotte Saunders aged 27-years or thereabts. Wife of John Saunders now from her Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband John Saunders at the parish Church of St. Giles Fields in the County of Middlesex (by License) on or about the 31st day of December 1783. that her said Husband hath neglected to provide for this Deponent and her Children hereafter Named and she  being incapable to maintain herself and them actually become chargeable to the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster . And this Examinant saith that her said Husband hath Resided in the parish for two years last part or thereabouts & Rented Stable therein in Houghton Street and an Apartment at Mr Hart's in the Said Street about Six Months at the yearly Apartments at Six Guineas a year the Rent of the Stable this Deponent knows not that afterwards he Rented and Resided in Holle Street in the said parish in a Home of the yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds as this Deponent hath been Informed and believes which and that he quitted that House a few days ago that this Deponent hath three Children by her said Husband Named Elizabeth aged seven years and upwards Caroline near four years Joseph aged near eleven Months all with this Deponent Mrs Saunders-Lodges at Mr Bowstead's No 53 St Martin's Lane

Man not taken

Sworn the 6th Day of April 1798 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Charlotte Saunders
