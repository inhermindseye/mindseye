Westmr.  Mary Odel aged abt. 19 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she was born in the Parish of Cranfield in the County of Bedford where her father lived in & rented a house & that she never rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servt for one year

Sworn Novr. 24th. 1752 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Odell
