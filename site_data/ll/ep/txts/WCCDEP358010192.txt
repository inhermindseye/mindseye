Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Charles Brown aged about 70 years upon her Oath saith that about 23 years ago he rented and lived in a House in St. James's Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr. for 3 years or thereabout at the yearly Rent of £60 hath quitted said House about 20 years ago since which time he has not rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Febry 1788  Wm. Gascoigne Mercer

Chas Brown
