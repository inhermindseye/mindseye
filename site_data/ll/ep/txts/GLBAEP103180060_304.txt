Middlesex ss}  William Fenbrae maketh oath That many years ago he rented a house in Johns court Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day - of Septemr 1778 before}

The Mark of William Fenbrae
