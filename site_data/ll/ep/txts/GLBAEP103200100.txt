Middlesex ss}  John Richards maketh oath that he was about Sixteen Years aged duly bound Apprentice by Indre to James Butcher of the Parish of St Mary White Chapel in the sd County Cooper for the Term of Seven Years & the Sum of Ten Pounds was paid by this Deponts Father to his Said Master as an Apprentice Fee That this Deponent duly Served his Said Master for the said Term & lodged the last three Years of his Sd Apprenticeship in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County where his Apprenticeship expired That this Depont has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this. 16. day of Febry 1784 before R Davies

John Richards
