Middlesex to wit Frances Cleland aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she was bound to a lived as a Covenanted Servant to Messr Paulin & Coat Milliners in Tavistock Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster and lived with them for the Space of Three Years under such Covenant and after leaving the said Messrs. Paulin and Coats's service she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 14th day of Sepr. 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Frances Cleland
