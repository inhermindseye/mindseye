Middx  The Exaicon of Jno. Curtis Lodging in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 15th. day of Augst. 1739 who upon his Oath saith that he was bound an Apprentice to & Served Seven Years Apprentishipp with his Brother Nath Curtis in Baldwins Gardens in the parish of St. Andrew Holborns above Barrs in the sd. County of Middx  Plaisterer and hath been out of his time abt. Seventeen Years And further Saith that he never Rented any House lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath a wife & one Child (to wit) Jno. Aged abt. four Years & being poor is not able to maintaine them

Sworn the day & Year above named before} Jas: Gronous

John Curtis
