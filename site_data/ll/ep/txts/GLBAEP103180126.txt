Middlesex ss}  Mary Faldo the widow of Nathaniel Faldo deced maketh oath That after her Said husbands death She this Deponent took & rented a house of the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the same That She has not Since rented a house or other Tenement of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or been Since Married or done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere.

Sworn this 25. day of Janry 1779 before  Jasper Clarkey

Mary Faldo
