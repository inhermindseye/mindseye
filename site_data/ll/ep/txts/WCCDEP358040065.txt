Westmr.  Mary Gibbeter aged about 40 Years upon Oath saith upwds. of 15 Years since she intermarried with her present husband Wm. Gibbeter at the Fleet London Says her sd. husband was born in Swallow Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. Says he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office as she hath been Informed & Beleives Says her sd. Husbands Father John Gibbeter was born in Bear Street by Leicester Feilds in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. aforesaid Says the said John Gibbeter never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office as this Examinant hath also been Informed and Beleives Says she was by Order of Removal under the hands & Seals of St. Thos. Detail Knt. & Wm. Jones Esqr two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the County of Middlesex (one of the Quorum) bearing Date the 30th. Day of August 1744 Removed from the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. County of Middx and Liberty of Westminster to the said Parish of Saint Martin in the Feilds against which Order there hath been no Appeal

Sworn Septr. 18th. 1747 before} Wm. Jones  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Mary Gibbeter
