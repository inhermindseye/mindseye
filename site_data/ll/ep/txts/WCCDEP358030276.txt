Middx  Elizabeth Winter on Behalfe of Mary Gray an Infant Child aged upwards of three Years upon Oath saith she has been informed and beleives the said Child was born in the house of one Mrs. Potter a Button maker in Gardiners Lane in Petit France in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr. Says she knows not where the father or Mother of the said Child now are

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1746 before}

Eliz Winter
