Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Veart aged about 55 years now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Veart her late Husband who has been dead about Nine years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Angel Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds that after he quitted said house he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry. 1798 before us } Robinson R. Ford

her Mary Veart mark
