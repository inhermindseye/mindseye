Middx  Ann Steward upon Oath saith she is the Widow of John Steward with whom she intermarried at a Church in Yorkshire about 25 Yrs. ago Says her sd. late husband rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £12 opposite St Giles's Church in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx & lived therein Sevl Years & there dyed and this Examint. continued to live in and rent the said house for some months after her sd. husbands decease and quitted is abt. one Year ago since which she has not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 17th. 1751 before  Fran Bedwell Ann Stuart
