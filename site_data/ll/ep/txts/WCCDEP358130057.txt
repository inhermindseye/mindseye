Middx  Dorothy Duddleston Aged about Twenty Years upon Oath says She never was married That She served about four Years of her Apprenticeship with one Ann Lythall a Poulterer in the Parish of St. George  Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex and further says She never Rented Ten pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes nor lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year nor was She ever assigned over to any Person for the remainder of her said Apprenticeship

Sworn the 29th. Day of Septr. 1755 before}

The mark of Dorothy Duddleston
