Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Ryder of No. 36 Bell Yard on behalf of Bridget Mary Brannan on Infant of the Age of Ten years and four Months Maketh Oath saith that she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that the said Bridget Mary Brannan is the illegitimate Child of one Susannah Ryder who is dead and was born at the house of a Mrs. Wood in Nags head Court  Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn this 9th- day of Septr. 1796 } Saml. Edward

her Mary Ryder Mark
