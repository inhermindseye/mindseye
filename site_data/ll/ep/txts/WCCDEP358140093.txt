Middx.  Elizabeth Simmons aged abt. 54 Years upon Oath says she intermarried with one Jonathan Husband Osborn at the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of middx who hath been dead abt. twelve Years as she has been informed & verily believes says the said Jonathan Husband Osborn had at the time of her said Intermarriage a Wife living as this Examinant was sometime afterwards informed & which information this Examinant believes to be true says before her sd. Intermarriage with the said Jonathan Husband Osborn she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Philip Bureau a Jeweller in Fountain Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for five Years at the Yearly Wages of six Pounds & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Yr.

Sworn March 4th. 1758 . before} Fran: Bedwell

Eliz Simmons
