Wm Huggins of Salisbury Street in the Strand Maketh Oath that he knew Joseph more is when he rented a house opposite Durham yard in the Strand in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and that he believes the Rent of the sd house was upwd. of twelve Pounds by the year & that the sd. Joseph Morris lived in the upwd. of two months And the sd. Joseph Morris has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 12th. 1754 before} Tho Lane

Wm Huggins
