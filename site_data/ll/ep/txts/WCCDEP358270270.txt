Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Dole aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly servant with one Mr. Cook in Fountain Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Sixteen Months at the yearly wages of Five Guineas that she hath quitted said service about Five years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said parish That one Thomas Powell a Plaisterer who lives at the house of one Mr. Edwards near the Hospital at Chelsea is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Thomas Powell and no other Person did beget the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 27th- day of Augt. 1796 } Thos. Robinson

Jane Dole

Man taken his own Recogt. taken 12th. Septr. 1796

Respited to next Sessions

Child born 10th. Jany. 1797 in the house named Thomas died the 26th. Jany. 1797
