Middlesex ss}  Ann Le Peas the widow of James Le Peas deced maketh oath that her Sd late husband was by birth a Foreigner & to her Knowledge or belief never did Any Act to gain a settlement in England That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlemt of her late Parents or either of them at She this Depont about forty Six Years Ago was born in the Parish of St Nicholas Deptford in the County of Kent That She was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement for herself Save what She may have Acquired by her Marriage as aforesd

Sworn this 6. day of Janry 1786 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Ann Le Peas
