Westmr to wit Martha Jones Aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or was served any Apprenticeship or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father John Jones Rented and lived in Two Houses the one on Saffron Hill ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrews Holbourn in the County of Middlesex the Other in Union Court in the said Liberty  the first of which Mentioned House he paid upwards of Ten Pounds a Year and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same but what Rent her said Father paid for the last mentioned House this Examinant Cannot set forth Since which time her said Father or this Examinant ever gained any subsequent Settlemt. to her Knowledge or belief and as she has been Informed her Bother George Jones

Sworn this 29th. Janry 1774 before R. Butler Sampn Wright

her Martha Jones  Mark
