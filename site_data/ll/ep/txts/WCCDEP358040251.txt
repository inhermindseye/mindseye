Westmr. 

Ann Spencer Widow on behalf of her Daughter in Law Ann Spencer upon Oath saith the sd. Ann Spencer is the Daughter of Samuel Spencer by his Wife both deceased and that she hath been Informed & believes the sd. Samuel Spencer was bound Apprentice to & Served his Apprentiship wth. Mr. Henry Taylor a Poulterer near Temple Bar in ye. Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Septr. 11th. 1748 before
