Middlesex ss}  Ann Bright the wife of John Bright who is now absent from her at Sea maketh oath that he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly Served his Apprenticeship with one Thompson a chairmaker in the Parish of St James in the City of Bristol & to her knowledge or belief has not Since done any Act to gain anothr Settlement That She hath a Child named George aged about two years & half the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her sd husband to whom She was lawfully married.

Sworn this 13 day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Ann Bright
