Westmr. 

Frances Moore upon Oath says she is a Singlewoman Says she lived a Yearly Servt . with Mr. Richard Bridges at the Woolpack the Cor of Strand Lane in the Parish of St. Clement & Danes in the County of Middx for Eleven Yrs. & three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & hath left her said Service about 4 Years Says she hath not since she left thesd. Mr. Bridges Service rented ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 8th. 1746 before  Tho Lane

Frances Moor
