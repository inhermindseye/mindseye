Middx  Eliz: Hutton on the Behalfe of Alice Pearce her Daughter aged abt. Seventeen Years upon her Oath saith that abt. Nineteen Years ago she Intermarryed with Chas. Pearce the Paupers Father & that before his Intermarryage with this Examinant & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Voyce a Linnen Draper in the Parish of St. Michael in Cornhill at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Six Pounds or thereabts. for the Space of One Year & upwds. that the sd. Chas. Pearce in his Life Time nor this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 29th- of Septr- 1741 before us } G Poulson Tho Lane

Eliz Hutton
