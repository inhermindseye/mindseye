St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Jane Waltham Aged 19 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married nor acquired any Subsequent legal Settlement in her own Right, that she doth not know where her Father (whose name was John Waltham ) Married to his Wife  Sarah formerly Sarah Alice Hale  Spinster but that she hath been Informed believes her father was legally Married to her Mother & that he served his Apprenticeship with Mr. Hunt a Carpenter in Butcher Row in this parish and hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement and that she is now Poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 23d. day of May Decr 1791 before me  Wm Hitchiner

Jane Waltham

By the Order of Mr. Johnston Newman  Beadle
