Westmr. to wit Mary Whitcroft Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of William Whitcroft (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at Saint Clement Danes about Two Years and an half Says her said Husband Served seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Dent a Taylor in Carey Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes as her said Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true and further says that after her said Husband was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 21st. day of April 1772 before} Tho. Kynaston

her Mary Whitcroft mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
