Middx  Rachell Wright aged about 26 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married & that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Abraham Heath in Arundell Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £4 10s for the Space of 2 Yrs. or thereabts. & hath left her sd. Service 1 Year & upwds. Says she hath not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 5th. March 1744 before}
