St Clement Danes 11th Novr 1793

Middlesex  and Westminster }

St C D

Child  (to wit) Elizabeth Simpson aged 25 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her Husband William Simpson  at the parish Church of St Giles Cripplegate London about thirteen Months ago (by Banns) that her Husband Served about ten Months under an Indenture of Apprenticeship for three years with Mr Long a Copper Plate Printer in Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex and then loft his said Master And this Examinant saith that he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And further saith that she hath one Child by her said Husband now living Named Mary born in or about the Month of March last

Sworn the 11th day of November 1793 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Simpson 's Mark
