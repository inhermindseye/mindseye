Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary O'Daniel aged about 30 years upon how Oath saith that about four years ago she Intermarried with Owen O'Daniel at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields that her said Husband has deserted her about Six Months that he was an Irishman and never gained any Settlement in England That she this Examnt lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Flower in George Yard Lombard Street in the Parish of Saint Edmund the King in the City of London for Two years on thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said service about Six years since which time she has not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of Decr. 1788 R Butler D Walker

her Mary O'Daniel Mark
