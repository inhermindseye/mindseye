Middlesex & Westminster to wit  passd. to St. Martins C  George Clark aged 55 years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he intermarried about 35 years ago at the Parish Church of Bewdley in Worcestershire to Sarah his late Wife That she departed this Life about 2 years ago. That he had Issue by her 13 Children Two of whom have not gained Settlements Vizt. James aged 16 (and Samuel aged 14 who is now with this deponent Ill) Saith That he kept a House in little Catherine Street , in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields , that the Rent thereof was £16. a year & he resided in it 3 Quarters of a year That he Quitted the same at 8 years ago That since that Time he has done no Act whatever whereby he may have gained a Settlement And Saith That his Son Samuel (who is now with him Sick) was sent to Sea by the Marine Society and never has gained or Acquired any Settlement as he verily believes

Sworn this 3rd January 1785 M Addington Thos. Gordon

his George Clark Mark
