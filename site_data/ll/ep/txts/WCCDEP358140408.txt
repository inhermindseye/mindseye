Middx.  Henry Faithfull aged abt. 45 Years a Soldier in the third Regiment of Foot Guards lodging at Mr. Oatessin Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. within the County of Middx. Upon Oath says he was born in the Parish of Chawton in the County of Southampton And never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for One Year nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes And that he cannot give any Account of the Settlement of his Father or Mother Says he has two Children by Alice his deceased Wife (Vizt.) Alice aged almost Seven Years and Elizth : aged almost four Years both born at Mr. Hardy's a Cheesemonger in Clements Inn Passage in the aforesaid Parish of St. Clemt. Danes

Sworn Septr. 15th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Saunder Welch

Herny Faithfull

Upon the Complt. of all the Overseers J: P:
