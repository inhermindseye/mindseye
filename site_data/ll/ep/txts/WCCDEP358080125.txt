Middx & Westmr-}  Mary Grady aged about 32 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Edward Grady who has been Dead about 5 Years and with whom she Intermarried at Limirick in the Kingdom of Ireland about Ten Years ago and by whom she hath two Childn- now living namely Darby Grady aged about Seven Years. & Jno- Grady aged about 5 Years both living in Ireland Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Thos- Cane a Milkman in the Psh of St- Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx and never gained any or- subseqt- Settlemt- in London than as before monta- Says that on the 5th- Day of Febry last she was delivd- of a Male Bastd- Child in the Workhouse belongg- to the sd- Psh of St- Clemt- Danes which has since been Baptized by the name of Thomas & is become chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same and that one Matthew Cearagan  Servant to one Mr- Rhodes a Cowkeeper at Hogsdon is the Father thereof he havg- had carnal Knowl of her Body sometime in the Month of May last past but the particular Day she does not now remember at Mr- English's a Milkman in Swan Yard sd- Mary le Strand & at sevl- difft- times afterwds- at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastd- Child of which she was so delivd- as afsd- and that the sd- Mathew Cearagan & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 6th. Day of March 1777 before me R Butler

her Mary Grady Mark
