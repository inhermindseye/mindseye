Middx  Roger Pugh aged abt. Seventy two Years upon Oath says he intermarryed with his Present wife Mary in the Parish Church of St. Mary at Ashington in the County of Middx upwards of Twenty  years ago, and that she is abt. fifty Years of Age Says he rented a House in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Nineteen Pounds for Ten Years or there abouts & hath quitted upwards of Twenty three Years Says he has not since he quitted sd. House rented Ten Pounds a Yeare pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif or Say he is poor & not able to maintaine himself & Wife

Sworn the 12th. July 1750 before}
