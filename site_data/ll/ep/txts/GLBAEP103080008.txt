Middx ss  Giles Bean Maketh oath that about seventeen years ago when a singleman he lived as a hired servant by the year with George Barber in Monmouth Street in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx  Salesman at the yearly wages of Four Pounds and continued in such service for the Space of Two Years and upwards And saith that he has not since lived as a hired servt by the year Nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any Parish offices nor done Any Act whereby to gain any subsequent Settlemt. to his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 5 day of April 1757 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris. Scott

Giles Bean
