Middx  Sarah Parrott aged abt. 28 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Jane Parrott aged abt. 28 Years deceased and that being a Wido. & without Child or Children She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Tomlinson a Woodmonger opposite Old Gravel Lane in the Parish of St. John Wapping in the County of Middx. for One Year & upwards at the Yearly wages of £5 and hath quitted said Service abt. 15 Months and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes where she now lodges she being Poor & Unable to Maintain the same when born; says that Edwd. Delany  Servant to Mr. Bromley a Fishmonger at Temple bar had carnal knowledge of her body in the Month of July last and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that no other Persons did beget the same

Sworn Janry. 4th. 1760 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Parrott

Upon Mr Rust's Complt. J: P:
