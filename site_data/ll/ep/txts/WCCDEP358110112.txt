Middx  Eliz: Whitney  Widow of John Whitney who dyed abt. 17 years since upon Oath Saith she intermarried with her sd late husband about thirty years ago at the Fleet London and that the said John Whitney rented a house of fourteen pounds by the year in May pole Alley in the parish of St. Clement Danes and lived there in upwds. of three Years & never after he quitted the said house nor has this Examt. Since his Decease rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Novr 14th. 1752 before}

The Mark of Eliz. Whitney
