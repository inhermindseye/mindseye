Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Brown aged about 64 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Barris in Seething Lane in the Parish of Allhallows Barking in the City of London for Three Years at the Yearly wages of Seven Guineas That she hath quitted said Service about Twenty Years since which time she hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Octr. 1795  J Hind Rup: Clarke

her Sarah Brown Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
