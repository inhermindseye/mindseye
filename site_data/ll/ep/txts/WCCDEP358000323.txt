Saint Clement Danes July 20th- 1786

Middlesex (to wit) Jane Bradford of the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Widow upon her Oath saith That she in her married with John Bradford her late Husband at the Christ Church Surry in the Man that August of the Year 1774-that her said Husband before their late mar- riage served his time as an Apprentice under Indentures to Messrs. Windle and Madem Glass Cutters at the Kings Army in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex -that after their Intermarriage she does not know of any Act Matter or Thing be ever did whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement that her said Husband died about Seven Years ago at

□ Giber alter , and that she this Examinant hath not since his Death lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year or done any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have acquired any Settlement in her own right-and further this Examinant on her Voluntary Oath saith That she is now Pregnant with Child or Children Which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that John Pears  one of the Rummers of the Public office in Bow Street is the Zealand true father of such Child or Children he having sometime in the Month of latter End of the Year 1785 had Camal Knowledge of this Examinant Body and that he the said John Pears and no one else did he got such Child or Children

on this Examinants Body of Which she is  Pregnant as aforesaid and that the said John Sears and no one else in the real and true father of such Child or Children

Sworn this 20th July 1786 before  Wm. Kitchiner

her Jane Bradford Mark
