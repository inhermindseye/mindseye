Middlesex ss}  David Patrick maketh oath That he is by Birth a Scotchman but the particular place where he was born there he Can't at present recollect or remember That his Parents were as he has been also informed natives of Scotland & this Depont. Can give no other Account respecting their Settlement That this Depont. never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year or ever Served any Parish office That this Depont. about Thirty three years ago rented a house of the yearly Rent of Eight Pounds in  Angel Court in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & lived therein divers years but whethr he paid any Taxes for the said house so as to gain a Settlement there he Can't recollect or Setforth & this Depont. Can give no othr Account respecting his Settlement.

Sworn this 3d day of January 1775 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of David Patrick
