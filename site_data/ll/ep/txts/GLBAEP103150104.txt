Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Wells of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County of Middx  Single woman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Said County this Fourth day of January 1776 -Who Saith that She is now with Child And that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard & to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that one Robert Emingtan of the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the Said County Porter & Watchman is the Father of the Said Child Where with She now goeth & is Pregnant

Sworn this 4th day of January 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of } Elizabeth Walls
