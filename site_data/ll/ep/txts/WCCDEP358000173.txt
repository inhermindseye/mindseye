St. Clemt. Danes

passd. to St. Andw. Holborn.  Mary Phillips aged 27 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married Rented a House or paid Taxes ever lived as an hired yearly Servant or ever was bound apprentice or ever did any Act to the best of her Knowledge whereby she may have gained a Settlmt. in her own Right. And further this Examinant when her Oath saith That her father William Phillips and Mary her Mother Mary Phillips were at this Examinant is informed believes Married at the Parish Church of Saint Bride Fleet Street in the City of London about 40 Years ago that she this Examinant was born in Exeter strand in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden and her said Father was bound by Indenture of Apprenticeship to Thos. Jeffery's of Kirby Street Hatton Garden in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Bars in the City of London for seven Years that he served the whole of his said Tenn with his said Master since which time her said  Father hath not done any Act to the best of this Examinants Knowledge or belief to gain any Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 15th. day of Decr. 1785 before us  James Croft  Rt. Taylor

her Mary Phillips Mark
