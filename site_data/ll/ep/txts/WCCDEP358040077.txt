Westmr.  Alice Ogden upon Oath saith she is the widow of John Ogden who Dyed upwards of Six Years since Says her sd. late husband lived in and rented a house in Millgate in the Town of Manchester in the County of Lancaster of the Yearly Rent of £11 for two Years & upwards and never after he quitted the sd. house (nor hath this Examinant since his Death) rented ten pounds a Yr. paid a Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has a Daughter viz; Elizabeth aged upwards of twenty Years who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn 7th. Octr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Alice Ogden
