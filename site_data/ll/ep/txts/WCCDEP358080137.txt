Middx & Westmr}  Elizth. Faron Aged about 37 Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Faron who has been dead upwards of Three Years and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says she hath one Child living by one John Wilkins decead Names Charles Aged about fourteen Months Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Theobalds Row in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Eighteen Years ago since which time he never Rented House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or otherwise gained any or Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death.

Sworn this 3d day of April 1777 before  Jno. Barnfather  Cha. Matthews .

her Elizth- Faron Mark
