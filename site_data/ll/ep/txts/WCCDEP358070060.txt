Westmr. to wit Ann Fordham  Wife of Richard Fordham (gone from her) Aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her said Husband at Saint Georges Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex about Seven Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Vizt. Ann Aged about five Years and Mary Aged about Eight Months Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Mills as Peruke Maker in Saint Johns Court in Saint Johns Square in the Parish of Saint James in Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or Done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Janry 1772 before Us  Tho: Kynaston R. Butler

her Ann Fordham Mark

Witness Edwd. Fields
