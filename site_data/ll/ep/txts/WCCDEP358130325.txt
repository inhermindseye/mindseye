Middx  Mary Lucas aged abt. 40 yrs. upon Oath says she never was Married says she served two years & half the first part of her Appren -ticeship with Mr. Dorman in Fountain Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & there she & her sd. Mistress parted by Consent & this Examinant never was assigned over to any Person Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly hired Servt. for one year

Sworn the 27th of April 1757 before}
