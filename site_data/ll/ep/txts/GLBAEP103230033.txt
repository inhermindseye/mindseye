Middlesex ss}  Thomas Tate maketh oath that the heretofore was hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant by & with John Smith of the Parish olstoke Newington in the county of Middlesex  Gent at certain Wages & Continued in Such Service under such hiring for the Space of two years & upwards That she has not Since hired as a hired Servant for the space of a year elsewhere or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor has he ever rented a house or Lodging of the yearly renter satue of ten Pounds

Sworn this. 29. day of March 1787 before as  R Davies  Robt: Smith

Thos. Tate
