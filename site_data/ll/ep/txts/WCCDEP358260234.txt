Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Darbin aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that she was born at Woodford in the Parish of Woodford in the County of Essex

Sworn this 9th. day of Decr. 1797 before us }

her Susannah Darbin mark
