Westmr.  Sarah Middleton aged about 40 Years upon Oath Saith she is the Wido . of John Middleton who dyed about Years ago; and that about five Years since being at that time a Wido. without Child or Childn. she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Henry Marsh a Carpenter in Clements Inn passage & in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds and has not since she left the sd. Mr. Marsh Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 6th. March 1752 before}
