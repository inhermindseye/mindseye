Middx  William Warner aged about 43 Years upon Oath saith he rented a Shop of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in the lower Market House in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Two Years and that he paid the Poor Rate for the same also says that during all the time he kept such Shop he lodged in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes Quitted the same about 18 Years and hath not since kept House rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes That he was Married to his present Wife  Hannah at St. Clement's Church about 20 Years ago by whom he hath two Children living to wit Robert aged about Nine Years & Ann aged about Seven Years

Sworn the 29th. day of June 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

Wllam Warner
