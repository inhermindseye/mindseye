Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit & Westminster }  St. Jno the Evangelist Westmr.  Mary Purser  widow aged forty three Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she was married to John Purser her late Husband, at Dunkirt in the Kingdom of Marie according to the Eights of the Roman Catholic Church about twenty six Years ago, that both she this Examinant is and her said Husband was of the Roman Catholic Religion that since their Intermarriage her said Husband and herself have rented and lived in a House in Dartmonth Westrminster in the Parish of Saint John the Evangelist  Westminster at the Yearly Rent of twenty Six pounds that they quitted the said House (having hired therein for about two Years) about thirteen Years ago that her said Husband died in March last, and that neither her said Husband an his Life time after quitting the said House in Dartmonth Row aforesaid did Rent, nor have this Examinant since his Decease rented any other House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds or do any other Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinant Knowledge Information or belief her said Husband

Husband in his Life time or this Examinant since his Death may have gained or aquired any other Subsequent Settlement and that she this Examinant is poor & Rich and unable to maintain herself.

Sworn this 12th. Day of October 1791 before us} Sampn. Wright  Thos. Gordon

her Mary Purser Mark
