Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Jones maketh oath that divers year ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Charles Augenbow a Surgeon in  Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the said County & Continued in Such Service for many years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29 day of Aug: 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Elizabeth Jones
