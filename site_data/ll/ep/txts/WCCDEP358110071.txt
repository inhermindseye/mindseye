Middx  Eliz Fisher upon Oath says she is the Wido. of Willm. Fisher of whose Settlement she can give no Account that she never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes & that her late father Chas. Murgytrorde at the time of his Decease & for a considerable time before lived in and Rented a house in Eastgate in the parish of St. Peters above hill in Lincoln & paid to the poor Rate there

Sworn July 18th. 1752 before me} Geo: Errington

E Fisher
