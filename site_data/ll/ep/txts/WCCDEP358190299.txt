Middx  John Mc.Michael Aged abt. Forty Seven Years Upon Oath Says he rented a House in Cucumber Court in ShipYard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds ten Shills. lived in the same upwards of two Years & hath Quitted the same Upwards of Twenty Years & hath not since rented House or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn the 8th. day of Novr. 1762 before}

Jon: Michael

Witness Petr Sidebotham
