Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St. Giles in the field  (to wit) Mary Griffiths And 28 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath Saith that about 3 Years Ago she intermarried with Thomas Griffiths her present Husband at the parish Church of Saint James's in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex by Bans That her said Husband was bound apprentices to a Mr. Griffiths of White Lyon Street seven deals in the parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the sd. County of Middlesex Sawyer but only served five Year and a half of his time that since he quitted said the service of his said Master he has been several times in his Majestys Service at sea And is now pressed from her in the said Service And further saith that since he left his said Master he never Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year lived as a hired Yearly Servant paid Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any Subsequent to the best of this Examinants Knowledge information or belief And further saith that she has One Child living by her said Husband named Thomas 2yrs and is pregnant with another that on accounts of her said Husband being away from her she is totally unable to maintain herself and Child.

Sworn this 12th. Octr. 1791 before} Sampn. Wright  Thos. Gordon

her Mary Griffiths Mark
