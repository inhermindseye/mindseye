Middx  The Examination of Thos. Howell Passed from the Parish of St. Ann in Blackfryers  Londn . to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx by Order bearing Date the 24th. Day of March Inst. Who upon his Oath saith that about Twenty Eight Years ago he rented & lived in a House of Twenty Eight Pounds a Year in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the space of Six Months & upwards And further saith yt. he hath not since he left the said House Rented any other Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath a Wife who is lame & being poor & Old is not able to maintaine himselfe & Wife

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Thomas Howell
