Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Sedgwick Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Dixon an Attorney in Norfolk Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same lived as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any other Settlement And further on her Oath says she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Anthony in Star Court Butcher Row in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes on the 10th. day of Octr. last which hath since been Baptized by the name of Ann and is now become Chargable to the sd Parish And further that one George Fidler a Cabinet Maker who now ledges at a Bakers Shop in Thames Street Opposite St. Martins Lane is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body for several Years last past and in her said Masters House about Two Years ago and at sevl. Different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so deliverd as assd. and that the said George Fidler & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 5th. day of July 1782 before R Butler

Mary Sedgwick
