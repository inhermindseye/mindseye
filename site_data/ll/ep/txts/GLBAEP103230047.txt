Middlesex ss}  Isabella Hudson the widow of James Hudson deced maketh oath that Since her sd late husbands death She hath lived in & rented two Rooms Furnished by herself in the Dwells house of Christopher Frogatt  no 43 upper East Smithfield in the Parish of st Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County at the Rent of Four Shillings per Week & hath resided thereon for more than the space of forty days & by means thereof hath as She is advised gamed a legal Settlemt there and hath not as yet done any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 28th day of May 1787 before me R Davies

The Mark of Isabella Hudson
