St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Giles in the fields (to wit) Susan Cremer otherwise Heyser aged 50 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married according to the rights and Ceremonies of the Church of England that she this Deponent never acquired any legal Settlement in her own right that she hath been informed and which she verily believes to be true that her father Thos (remer rented and resided in on Apartment at Mr Graydon in Great Queen Street  Lincolns Fields in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex at the yearly  rent of £11 for several years that since he quitted that Apartment he did not rent any Apartment of £10 a year payd parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any legal settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 16th day of January 1793 before an} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Susan Cremer 's Mark

Witness Garrett

Removed by James Talboys  Beadle  16th Janry. 1793
