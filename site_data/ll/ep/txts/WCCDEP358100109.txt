Middx to Wit James Talboy one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes on behalf of William Maddan an Infant Aged upwards of 3 years on his Oath Says that he has been credibly Informed and verily believes and Doubts not prove that the said Wm. Maddan was born a Bastard on the Body of Eleanor Maddan  Singlewoman in the House of Mr. Mainwaring in Phillip Lane in the Parish of St. Alphage in the City of London And further says that the said Eleanor Maddan was removed by a Vagrant Pass from the aforesaid Parish of St. Alphage And that the said Eleanor Maddan has deserted her said Child and is now Committed to Bridewell as a Vagrant on that Account.

Sworn this 12th day of Augt. 1783  Sampn Wright W Addington

James Talboys
