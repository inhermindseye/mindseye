Middx ss  Margaret Bambridge  Widow Maketh oath that her Son John Bambridge a Brasier deced about Ten Years and half ago Rented a house on Saint Mary Hill in the Parish of Saint Mary Hill Near Billingsgate in ye City of London at the Yearly Rent of Thirty pounds and lived in ye Same about half a Year but did not pay any Rent or Parish Taxes to ship Deponents Knowledge or belief And this Deponent Saith that her sd. Son hath not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or upwards nor Served any Parish offices nor gained any Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief And that her sd. Son left behind him at his death a Son Named Johnson Bambridge his lawfull Issue aged about ten Years who hath gained No Settlement Subsequent to his fathers Settlement abovemenconed

Sworn this 19th day of March 1754 before us  Richd. Ricards  Chris: Scott

his Margaret Bambridge Mark
