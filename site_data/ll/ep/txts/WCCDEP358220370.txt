Westmr. to wit Daniel Furzer Aged about Sixty One Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with his late Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles's in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about Thirty Years ago & who has been dead about Twenty Seven Years ago and by whom he hath no Children living Except a Daughter who is married and provided for Says he served five Years Clerkship with Mr. Thomas Heath an Attorney at No.9 in New Inn his late Uncle deceased and lived with him after he was out of Clerkship to the time of his said Uncles Death upon whose Death this Examinant Rented and lived in the same Chambers and Carried on Business for himself for Many Years and paid for the same the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds and upwards hath quitted the same upwards of fifteen Years ago and hath not since he quitted his said Chambers ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or done any or Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Augt. 1772 before me} Tho Lane

Dan: Furzer
