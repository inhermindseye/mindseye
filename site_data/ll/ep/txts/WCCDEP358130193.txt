Middx  Mary Avery Wife of Richard Avery aged abt. Thirty two Years upon Oath Says She Intermarried with her said Husband on the Sixth Day of February about Fourteen Years Since in the Parish Church of St. Sepulchre 's London Says he Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Robert Smith a Shoemarker in Whites Alley in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx. & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says he is now as She hath been informed on Board a Privateer & She being Poor is unable at present for Want of Cloaths to maintain herself

Sworn the 4th. Day of August 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Avery
