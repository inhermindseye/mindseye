Middx  Richd. Perry aged abt. 62 Years upon Oath saith he never was married and that he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Bucket & Mr. Warrington at the White Lyon in Witch Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlx at the Yearly wages of £6. and £8 for Sevl Years or thereabouts and hath left his Service about 30 Years and has not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Janry 9th. 1750 before}

Richard Perry
