Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Orindia Hudson aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cooper in Cecil Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for 4 years & upwards at the yearly Wages of £5. that she hath quitted said Service about 5 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Jas Daily a Journeyman Taylor who lodged at Mr. Haseldens in Browns Buildings and used the Taylors Arms in Blackmoor Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of for these Two years last past and begotten the same thereon and that the said Jas. Daily is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 20th. Day of Febry 1788 } Wm Kitchiner

her Orindia Hudson Mark

Jas. Daily is a Middle Sized Man dark Complexion wears his Hair hanging in his Neck-little Pitted with small Pox an Irishman

Newman has this Warrt.

brot. to Bed of 2 Children baptized she took away both dead { Wm . the other Orinda
