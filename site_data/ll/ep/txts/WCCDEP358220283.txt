Westmr.  Elizth Tubb Aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath says she is the Wido. of Jno. Tubb who has been dead about 6 Years with whom she Intermarried at Newington in the Year 1751 says that her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Wm. Ryal a Carpenter & Joyner in Chequer Parish above Hill in the City of Lincoln and never gained any other Subsequent Settlement afterwards niether hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 22d day of July 1771 before}

Vagt. Pass
