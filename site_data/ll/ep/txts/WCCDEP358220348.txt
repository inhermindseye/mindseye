Middx  to wit John Aylmer Aged about fifty four Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Martha his Wife who is now Aged about Seventy Years at the Parish Church of Saint Botolph Bishopgate London about Twenty five Years ago Says that before his said Intermarriage and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a Covenanted Servant with one Mr. Lee a Taylor in Whistlers Court Cannon Street in the Parish of Saint Swithen in the City of London for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds hath quitted the said last mentioned Service upwards of Thirty Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 6th. day of May 1772 before} Tho. Lane  Sam Wright

Jon Aylmer
