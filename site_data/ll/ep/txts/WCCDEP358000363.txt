Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Sarah Scriven aged about 43 year Widow upon her Oath saith that about 17 years ago she Intermarried with Thos. Scriven her late Husband (who has been dead about 18 Months) at the Parish Church of St. Mary Le Bone that she hath two Children living Vizt. Thos. Jas. aged about 11 years and Peter Michael aged about 9 years both Provided for that her said Husband rented and lived in a House in Cross Keys Yard Whitecross Shreet in the Parish of St. Giles without Cripple gate for 12 years at the yearly Rent of 15 Pound and Paid Taxes that after quitting said House she has done no Act Matter or thing where to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Septr. 1786  Sampn Wright M Addington

Sarah Scriven
