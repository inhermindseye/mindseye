Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mercy spire who is ill Maketh Oath and saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Mery Spire is the Widow of Richard spire with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of saint Brides about Twenty three years ago that the said Richard Spires Rented and lived in a house in Tooley Street in the Parish of Saint Olaves southwark for about Two years at the yearly Rent of Twenty two Pounds and that after he quitted said house he never did any Act to the best of this Examinants knowledge whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of May 1790  Sampr wright M Addington

James Talboys
