Westmr. 

Tanaquil Harrison removed from the parish of St. Mary Westmr. by Order dated the 14th. Instt. upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife Martha  at the fleet London on the 2d. June 1746 by whom he hath two Children Thomas aged near 3 Yrs. & Martha aged near one Yr. Says he lived a Yearly Servt. whilst a Singleman with Mr. Nichs. Humphrys  Cordwainer in Sheir Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds for 1 Year & upwards & has not since he quitted the sd. Mr Humphrys Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 18th. May 1750 before} Tho Lane .

Tanaquill Harrison
