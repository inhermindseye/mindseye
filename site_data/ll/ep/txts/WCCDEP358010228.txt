Saint Clement Danes

Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles  of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he believes to be true that Elizabeth Perrott an Infant was born a Bastard at the House of a Mr. Marsh a Hair Dresser in Fleet Lane in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre in the City of London

Sworn this 21st. of July 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

Benj. Newman
