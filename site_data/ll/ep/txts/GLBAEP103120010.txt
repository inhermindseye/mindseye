Middlesex ss}  John Martin a Singleman maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to, and Served the whole of his apprenticeship with one Robert Weaver in the Parish of Saint Michael Royal in the City of London and by means thereof as advised he gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to this Said Apprenticeship

Sworn this 27th Day of August 1772 before  John Smart  Cha: Digby

John Martin
