Westmr. ss  Christian Rankin Aged upwards of fourteen Years on her Oath Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex by Indenture bearing Date the Seventh day of October 1772 to one Thomas Blencowe a Glazier  the Cornor of Bloomsbury Court in the Parish of Saint Georges Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex untill this Examinant shod accomplish her full Age of Twenty one Years or day of Marriage as she has been Informed and believes and as by the said Indentures of Apprenticeship relation being thereunto had may more fully Appear And further says that she this Examinant never was Assigned over to any other Master or ever gained any or Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of Octr. 1774 before R. Butler Sampn Wright

her Christian Rankin mark
