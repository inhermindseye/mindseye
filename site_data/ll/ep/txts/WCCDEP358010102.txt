Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Benjn. Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizth. Price aged about 44 years who is ill upon his oath saith that he has been informed which by the said. Elizth. Price Information he verily believes to be true that she never was married Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or paid Taxes on that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Walther a Baker opposite Shoreditch Church in the Pish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex for 14 Months at the yearly Wages of £7..0..0 that she has quitted same about 10 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servd. for one year or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 30th. day of May 1787  Sampr Wright Maddington

Benj: Newman
