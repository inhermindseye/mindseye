Middlesex ss}  Mary Ashley the widow of Jacob Ashley a Soldier deced maketh oath That she can give no Account respecting the Place of Settlement of her Said late husbands Parents or either of them That her Said husband has informed her & which information she believes to be true that he was born in the Town of Yarmouth in the County of Norfolk That to this Deponents knowledge information or belief her Said husband was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever served any Parish office or ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right And that She this Deponent since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 25. day of May 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Mary Ashley
