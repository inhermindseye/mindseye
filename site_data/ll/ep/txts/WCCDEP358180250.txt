Middx  Eliz: Harris  Wido. upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband  Peter Harris in the Year 1739 at the Parish Church of St. Ann within Aldersgate Says her sd. late Husband was by Indenture Dated 12th. Day of July 1728 bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Edmund Davis in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex  Shagreen Case maker & that his sd. Master dyed a few Weeks before the Expiration of his said Apprentiship Says he never was Assigned over to any other Person rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that She hath 2 Children viz. John aged near 3 Yrs. & Peter aged 8 Months & being Poor & pregnant with Child is unable to maintain them

Sworne 8th. Novr. 1744 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Eliz: Harris
