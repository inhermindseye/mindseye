Middx to wit Elizabeth Broadhurst Aged upwards of Sixty four years on her Oath says she never was Married, Rented House or paid Taxes, says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mrs Phillips at Craven House in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster , upwards of Eleven years at the yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of Twenty years ago and has not since Quitting the same ever Rented House paid Taxes lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or any otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 10th Day of Octr 1775 before.  David Walker

the Mark of Elizabeth Broadhurst
