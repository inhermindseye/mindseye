Middx ss  Richard Walker Maketh oath that he duly served his apprenticeship with Thomas Cradock  Wheelwright of Hartshorne Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middx and by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And this Depont saith he has not since gained any Subsequent Settlemt. having since his apprenticeship Never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor been a hired servt. by ye year nor served any Parish offices

Sworn this 3d day of September 1755 before  Chris: Scott  Richd: Ricards

Richd Walker
