Middlesex ss}  Susannah Worrall the Daughter of William Worrall by Mary his wife both deced maketh oath that She Cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of her sd Parents or either of them That She this Deponent has been informed by her said Mother & which She Verify belives to be true that She this Deponent was born in the Parish of Saint Mary Rotherhith in the County of Surry & there Baptized That She this Depont never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 14 day of october 1777 before  Jno Sherwood  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Susannah Worrall
