Middlesex ss}  Mary Burton the wife of William Burton maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Sandwick That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Mr White a House Carpenter in the Parish of Bradninch in the County of Devon & to her knowledge or belief he has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlemt. And that she was lawfully Married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 13. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Burton
