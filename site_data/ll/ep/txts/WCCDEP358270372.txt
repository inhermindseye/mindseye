Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Crayton aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she is a Native of Ireland and never gained any Settlement in England And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one John Pettit a Journeyman Blacksmith who lives in Milford Lane is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examints. Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Pettit is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 8th. day of Augt. 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Margaret Crayton Mark

Witness B Newman
