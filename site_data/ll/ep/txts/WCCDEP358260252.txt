Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Joseph Spencer aged about 60 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 21st.. day of December 1798 he Intermarried with Eleanor his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That he Rented and lived in a house in Hemlock Court in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds that he hath quitted Said house about Seventeen years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Febry. 1798 before me } Edwd: Naish

Joseph Spencer
