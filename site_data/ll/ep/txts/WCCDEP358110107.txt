Middx  Eleanr Thornley upon Oath saith she is the wife of George Thornley with whom she intermarried about 14 Years ago at Lisbon in Portugal and that she hath been informed and verily believes her sd husbands father George Thornley deceased rented a house next Door to the Horn and Horseshoe Tavern in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex and paid Scot and Lot there and that her sd husband never Rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year and further Saith that she has one Child (now with her) namely William aged near Ten weeks

Sworn Octr. 28th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eleanr Thornely
