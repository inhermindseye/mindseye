Westmr. to wit Mary Crosdale Aged about Thirty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or was an Apprentice to any Person Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. John Seetta Land  Waiter in Prescot Street Goodmans Fields in the Parish of Saint Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex for upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of One Year ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further says that in the 3d. day of December last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Poor House belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which has since been baptized by the Name of Timothy Says that one Lawrence Bocks Porter to one Mr. Simmonds an Apothecary in German Street Saint James's is the Father there of the having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of March 1779 but the particular Day she does not now remember and at Sevl. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Lawrence Bocks and no other Person did beget the same and that he is his real and True father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of January 1774 before}

Mary Crosdale
