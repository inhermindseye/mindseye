Middx  Eleanor Smith Aged abt Twenty three Years Upon Oath Says that she was bound an apprentice by the Church wardens & Overseers of the parish of St Clement Danes to Adam Allen a Distiller in Blackmoor Street in the Sd. parish with whom she lived upwards of One Year & that her Sd Master failed & went abroad Says after that she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr Crofts at the White Lyon in Wych Street in the Same Parish for five Years Yearly wages from four pounds to five pounds ten Shillings & hath quitted Sd Service upwards of three Years And further Upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the Said Parish she being poor and unable to Maintain the Same when Born that One Francis Jerrard had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of Sepr. Last in the house of One Mr Waggit in the parish of St Botolph Bishopgate London where this Examinant lived as a Servt , between Six & Seven Months and at Sevll, other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is pregnant and that the Sd. Francis Jerrard is a Sargeant in the Queens Fusilears now at Bellisle as she hath been Inform'd & Believes

Sworn July 24.. 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Eleanor Smith
