Margaret Cunneh  Wife of Thos. Cunneh St Clement Danes May 15 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields  (to wit) Thomas Cunneh aged about 31 years Upon Oath saith that he was Married to Margt. Cunneh formerly Margaret Lloyd  Spinster at the Parish Church of St. Oswald in the City of Chester on or about the 16th day of July 1764 (by Banns) that he Rented and Resided in  an Apartment consisting of two Rooms on the Ground floor in the House of Wm Lumley No 1 Legg  Alley Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County for six Months or upwards at the Monthly Rent of £1 (being at the Rate of £12 a year) that he quitted the same in or about the Month of October 1792 and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief that he this Deponent left his Wife several years ago and that he now resides at No. 14 Charles Court in the said Parish of St Martin where he hath dwelt for upwards of three Months last past

Sworn the 15th day of May 1794 before us M Addington J Pownall

Thomas Cunneh
