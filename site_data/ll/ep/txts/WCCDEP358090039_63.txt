Middx  Mary Hatsell aged about fifty Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes say she lived a hired Yearly Servant to Mr. Pyle a Surgeon in Cecil Street Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said service about 2 Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn this 27th. day of June 1779 before Abrm Baley

her Mary Hatsell Mark
