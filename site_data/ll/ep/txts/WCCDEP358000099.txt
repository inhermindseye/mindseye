St Clement Danes 19th. Augt. 1785

Middlesex (to wit)  Swears to St. Clements  Mark Curtis aged 24 Years or thereabouts upon his Oath saith That he is as born in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Vizt. in the House of one Frances Polman in Vere Street Clare Market where his father and Mother Lodged-that his Father and Mother were both of them born in Ireland and never to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information and belief gained any Settlement in England and that this Examinant has never rented any House or Tenement of the Yearly Value of £10. paid Taxes, as an Apprentice, lived as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any Settlement in his own right

Sworn this 19th Augt. 1785 before  Jno: Wright

Mk Curtis
