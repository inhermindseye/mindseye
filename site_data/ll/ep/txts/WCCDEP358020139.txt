Middlesex (to wit) Elizabeth Griffin aged 28 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to Thomas Griffin her now Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Brides otherwise Saint Bridget London about four years ago that she has only one Children living by her said Husband namely Thomas aged fourteen Months that her said Husbands Parish by having servd. his Apprenticeship to Mr. Poliket a thereon her is in the Parish of Ealing in the County of Middlesex that time Serving his Apprenticeship he has not gained to this Examinant Knowledge any other Settlement than in Ealing aforesaid and that she this Examinant is poor and unable to maintain herself her said Husband having deserted her

Sworn this 22d. Apl 1789 before  Sampn Wright J Addington

her Elizth Griffin Mark
