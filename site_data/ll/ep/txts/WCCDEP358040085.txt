Westmr.  Mary Hughes upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Jonathan Molton a Broker for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5 Says that she lived the last four months of her sd. Service with the sd. Mr. Molton at Mr. Sarneys in Change Alley in the Parrish of St. Michael in Cornhill Londn. Says she has not since she left her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. (where she now liveth) she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Samuel Ireland of the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Victualer as the reputed Father this Examinant having formerly lodged in the house of the sd. Mr. Ireland there he became Acquainted with

her and had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the months of January and February last & at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. Samuel Ireland & no other Person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Octr. 20th. 1747 before} Tho: Burdus

Mary Hughes
