Westmr. to with Mary Crofts Aged about Twenty Six Years her Oath says she is the Widow of Peter Crofts who died about Twelve Months ago and with whom she Intermarried the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by Licence the Month of December 1767 by whom she hath no C now living Says her said late Husband Rented and l in a set of Chambers in New Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted the same upwards of one Year and an half and after quitting the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gaine Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 27th. day of January 1773 before me} 

her Mary Crofts Mark

Witness Edwd. Fields
