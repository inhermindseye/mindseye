St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Martin in the fields  (to wit) Benjamin Poore aged 54 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith That he was Married to his present Wife Susanna formerly Smith Susanna Smith at the parish Church of St James in the City of Bristol on the 27th. day of March 1763 . that since their said Marriage he Rented and with his said Wife resided in a House in a place called Johnson Court at Charing Cross in the parish of St Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the said Coun County about two Years at the Annual Rent of forty five Guineas that he quitted it in or about the year 1784 and hath not since obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief by any way or means whatever And this Deponent further saith that he & his said Wife were was removed by the Order of removal from the parish of St Mary Whitechapel about May or June 1791 to the said parish of St. Martin in the fields against Which Order or removal the said parish of St Martin in the field, have not Appealed to this Deponents information or belief

Sworn the 11th. day of July 1792 before us } Sampr Wright  Thos. Gordon .

Benjn. Poore
