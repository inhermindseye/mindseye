Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Taylor aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she Rented a Second floor of a Mrs. Walker in Crown Court Russell Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for about Four Months at the Weekly Rent of Sixteen Shillings That she hath quitted said Apartments about Twelve Months since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of April 1796 } J Hind Richd. Ford

her Sarah Taylor Mark

Witness B Newman
