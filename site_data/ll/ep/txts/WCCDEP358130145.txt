Middx  Jane Foster Aged about 23 years upon Oath Says She never was married & that She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex She being poor and not able to maintain the same when born and that Richard Hargest did beget the said Bastard Child or Children on her Body he the said Richd. Hargest living a fellow Servant with this Examinant at Wills Coffee house in Searle Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes there became acquainted with her and had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of July last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which She is now pregnant and that the sd. Richd. Hargest and no other did beget the same

Sworn the Day of April 1756 before}
