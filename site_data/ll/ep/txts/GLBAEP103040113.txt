Middlesex ss. The Examination of Ann Hutchinson of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Singlewoman taken on Oath before me Hickes H. Reynell  D.D one of His Majesty's Justices of the Peace in and for the said County of Middlesex this 5-Day of April, One Thousand Seven Hundred and Ninety Eight .

Who saith that on the Fourth Day of March now last past, in the Dwelling House of Mrs. Robinson situate in Red Cross Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate aforesaid She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child, since Baptized at Aldgate Church by the Name of Charles Robinson , And that one John Anderson (who went by the Name of William Robinson ) late of St. Catherines Lane but now of Portsmouth in the County of Hampshire  Mariner , is the Father of the said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the Day and year first above written before H: Reynell

her Ann Hutchinson Mark
