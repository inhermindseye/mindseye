Middx  Mary Clacey aged about 25 Years upon her Oath saith she is a Singlewoman and that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Lewis in Surry Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & hath left the sd. Mrs. Lewis's Service about Six Years & hath not since Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Childn: which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that William Nokes a Soldier in Coll Duells Regimt. of Grenadiers did begett the said Child or Children on her Body the said Wm. Nokes being acquainted with this Examinant had Carnall knowledge of her Body sevll. times in the Month of October last at the house of one 

Mrs: Smith near Saint Giles's Church at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. William Nokes & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne April 25th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Clacey
