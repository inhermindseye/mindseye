Before Birth 

St Clement Danes August 5th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnancy  Reputed Father (to wit) Margaret Jones  singlewoman aged twenty three  years or thereabouts upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Davis of No. 6 Clements Inn in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for about two years at the annual Wages of six pounds or thereabouts that she quitted that service about three years ago and hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve month together or done any other Act Matter or thing Wtsoever Whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement And this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes when Born And that Charles Baylife who now Works as a Journeyman with Mr. Ford Box Maker in the Minories London is the real father of the said Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant's Body first in or about the Month of December last and at Several times since at some or one of which times he did

beget the said Child or Children thereon And that he and no one else is the only real true father thereof

Sworn the 5th. day of August 1793 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Margt Jones 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys

Man taken and Settled with the Overseers
