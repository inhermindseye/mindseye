Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Richard Dobbins one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Ralph Hall who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Ralph Hall served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Joseph Sanders a Silversmith in Maiden Lane in the Parish of Saint John Lachary in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never according to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 10th- day of Septr. 1796 P Henderson E Read

Richd Dobbins
