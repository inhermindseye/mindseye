Middlesex to wit, Margaret Pinches , Widow of William Pinches , Blacksmith , deceased, maketh Oath and Saith. That she was lawfully Married her said late Husband at the Parish Church of Barrington in the County of Salop upwards of Thirty Four Years ago. That about Twenty Eight Years her said Husband rented a House in the Victualling Office Square in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex , at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards and lived in the same several Years, and that her said Husband did not after he quitted the said House, nor has she this Deponent since his decease Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or done any other Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this Day of February 1798, before me}
