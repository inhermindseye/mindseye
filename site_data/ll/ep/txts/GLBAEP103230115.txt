Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Hicks the widow of John Hicks deced maketh oath that She Cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of her late husbands Parents deced or either of them Save that She has been informed & which She believes to be true that her said husbands Father Rented a house heretofore at Little Stone Stairs in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of St Dunstan Stepney in the Sd County but what Rent or Taxes was Paid for the same She Can't Set forth That She this Deponent has also been informed by her said husband & by his Godmother that her said husband was born at Stone Stairs aforesaid & which Information She believes to be true That to this Deponents knowledge information or belief her Said husband never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever rented a house or Lodging of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in his own right That She was lawfully married to her said husband and has not Since his death been married or lived as a hired Servant for a year or done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 1. day of Febry 1787 before R Davies Rob: Smith

The Mark of Elizabeth Hicks
