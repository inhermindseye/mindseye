Middx.  Wm. Wakeling aged abt. 69 Years upon Oath says he Intermarried with Elizth . his Wife who is aged abt. Seventy Years in Lincoln's Inn Chapel on the 1st. day of January 1713. by whom he hath one Daughter named Mary -aged abt. Twenty Years who is a Cripple and uncapable of providing for herself says he served as a Covenant Servant by Indenture dated the 23d. day of January 1711 for three Years to Maxemillian Divilly a Taylor in Bishops Court in Durham Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds for the first Year Fifteen Pounds for the Second Year and Twenty Pounds for the third Year says after his said Intermarriage and during the last Six Months of his said Service he Lodged at Mr. Fielders a Pewterer ever against Devereux Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. and never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. with any other person

Sworn this 22d. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Wm. Wakeling
