Middx.  Sarah Beele aged abt. 75 Years upon Oath says; she is the Widow of Willm. Beele who has been dead abt. Twenty four Years says that She being a Widow and without Child or Children lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Lister Milliner opposite Devereux Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for five Years or thereabouts at the Yearly wages of four Pounds and hath quitted said Service abt. One Year; Since which time she (this Deponent) hath not Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn this 4th. April 1759 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Sarah Beele
