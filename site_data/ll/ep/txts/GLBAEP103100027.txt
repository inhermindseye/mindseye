Middlesex ss  Charles Scales the elder & Father of Charles Scales the Youngr maketh oath That divers Years ago his Said son was duly bound apprentice by Indre by the officers of the Parish of St. Giles witht. Cripplegate London to linn this Depont. who then lived Maidenhead Court in Aldersgate Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldersgate in the City of London in Which last mentoned Parish the said Charles Scales the Younger remained & Continued as this Deponents Apprentice as aforesaid for the space of three Months & during such time lodged & boarded in this Deponts. house & did business there as his apprentice & by means thereof as this Depont. is advised gained a legal settlement there and this Depont. Saith that to his knowledge or belief his Said son hath not since lived as an apprentice or as a hired servant for a year nor rented a house of the yearly Value of Ten Pounds or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 11th.day of July 1768 before  Chris Scott  Richd. Hare

Charles Scales
