Middx  Catherine Edley  wife of Richd Edley (who is gone for a Soldier and doth not live with her) Maketh Oath and Saith that her said Husband never gained any legal Settlement other than in right of his Father John Edley And that the said John Edley the father about Eleven years ago obtained a legal Certificate from the parish of Edmondton in the County of Middx owing and acknowleing him the said John Edley to be And his family to be legally Settled in the sd parish of Edmondton

Sworn this 5th Oct. 1745 before Robert Dennett

her Catherine Edley Mark
