Westmr.  Jane Hayland aged upwds. of 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Delareve in Gerard Street in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. and for one year at the yearly wages of four pounds and quitted her sd. Service about 6 months ago

Sworn Novr. 23d. 1752 before Fran Bedwell

Jane Ayland
