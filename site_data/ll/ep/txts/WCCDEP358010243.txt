Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Bishop aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that about 18-years ago she intermarried with Thomas -her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square that he has been dead about Six Months that she has Two Children living by her said late Husband Vizt. Henry aged about 4 years and Elizth. aged upwards of Four Months that her said late Husband Rented a First Two Rooms Floor of Mr. Clarke in Houghton Street Clare Markett for Two years and upwards at the yearly Rent of £12..12..0 that after he quitted said Place he Never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of Septr. 1788  Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Bishop  Mark
