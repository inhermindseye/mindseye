Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Topppins aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith That about Six years ago she Intermarried with John Toppins her late Husband who has been dead about Two years at Stoke Church Plymouth That her said late Husband informed this Examinant That he Served his Apprenticeship to a Mrs. Wood in Billiter Lane in the Parish of Allhallows Staining in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement and saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn this 30th. day of Octr. 1798 before us  H Wisstead Jn Bacon

her Ann Toppins Mark

Witness H Day Beadle
