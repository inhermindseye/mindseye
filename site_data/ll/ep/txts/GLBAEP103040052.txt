Middlesex to wit, Ann Gunbie  Singlewoman Maketh Oath and saith that about Twelve Years ago she hired a House in Blackfriars Church Yard in the Parish of Saint Andrew by the Wardrobe in the City of London at the Rent of Eleven Pounds Eleven Shillings per Annum and Resided therein several Years and hath not gained a Subsequent Settlement that being Poor and unable to provide for herself she hath applied to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Botolph without aldgate in the County of Middlesex for Relief and hath been Relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 19th day of August 1797 before us H: Reynell Jno Robinson Ann Gunbie
