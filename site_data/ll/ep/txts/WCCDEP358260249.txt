Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Donnelly aged about 58 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a house in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Eight years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th.. day of Febry. 1798 before me } W Carter

James Donnelly
