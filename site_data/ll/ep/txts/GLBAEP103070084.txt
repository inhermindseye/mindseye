Middx ss  John Conyers being one of the People Called Quakers upon his Solemn affirmation Saith that about Thirty four Years ago he lived in Roed Maid Lane then part of the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middx and Since & Now Part of the Parish of Saint George in the sd. County of Middx and during this affirmants residence there which was for Several Years he this affirmant Paid all Parochial Taxes And by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this affirmant hath not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year Nor Served any Parish offices Nor gained any Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge or belief

Affirmed this day of July 1753 before  John Conyers
