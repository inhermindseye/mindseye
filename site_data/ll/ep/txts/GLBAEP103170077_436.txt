Middlesex ss}  Rachael Cannon the widow of Charles Cannon deced maketh oath That She has been informed by her late mother Sarah Jones deced & which information She Believes to be true That She was born in  Hanway Street in the Parish of St Paneras in the County of Middlesex That this Depont can give no account respecting the Settlement of her late husband nor of his Parents or either of them nor can She give any Account respecting the Settlement of her Parents or either of them That She this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right save what She has acquired by her Marriage as aforesd. That she was lawfully married to her said husband by whom She hath a child now living named Martha aged about Six months.

Sworn this. 17. day of May 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Rachael Cannon
