Westmr. to wit Jane Prague Aged about forty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Franklin a Widow Lady who lodged at one Mr. Carlers an appothecary the Corner of Broad Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for three Years at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings payable Weekly or as this Examinant thought proper and the Two last Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Seven Months ago & hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of October 1772 before  Tho Kynaston G S Bradshaw jane Prague
