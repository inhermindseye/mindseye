Saint Clement Danes 30th Augt 1787

Middlesex & Westminster} to wit Ann Brennon  Singlewoman aged forty nine Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married, that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Perry the Brop Pounder in Browns Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for upwards of four years at the yearly Wager of four Pounds that she quitted her Service about fourteen years age and since then has never to the best of her knowledge done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th Augt 1787 before me  Edward Hodsoll

her Ann Bernnon Mark
