Middx  Eleanor Jones  Singlewoman aged about 30 Years upon Oath says She was never Married and that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Simpson Printseller deceased in Russell Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of three Years and quitted same near six Years and hath not since lived a hired Yearly Servant Rented a House or paid any Taxes

Sworn the 20th. day of Novr. 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

The mark of Eleanor Jones
