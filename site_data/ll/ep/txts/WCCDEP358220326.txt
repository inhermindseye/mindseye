Westmr. to wit Elizth. Rogers Aged about fifty Six Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Joseph Rogers who has been dead about Ten Years and with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Bennetts Coal Wharf in the City of London about Twenty Eight Years ago says that after her sd. Intermarriage with her said late Husband He Rented and lived in a House in Burleigh Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted same about Twenty Years ago & says that her said late Husband after quitted the same never Rented House or Paid Taxes neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death done Any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 24th. Febry 1772 before} 

Elz. Rogers
