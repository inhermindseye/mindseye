Middlesex ss}  Sarah Blakeman  Singlewoman maketh oath that about twenty Years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year with Frances Upton in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County widow & Continued there under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of a year That She has not Since lived as a hired Servt. for the Space of a year elsewhere nor she ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 13. day of June 1786 before R Davies,

The Mark of Sarah Blakeman
