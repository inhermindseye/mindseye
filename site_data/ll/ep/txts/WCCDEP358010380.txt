Middlesex to wit.}  Mary Beath  Singlewoman aged 30 years and upwards on her Oath saith that about 20 Months ago as she believes it may be she lived an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Atkinson Victualler at the Sign of the Watermans Arms at Wansworth in the County of Surrey for upwards of one whole year together at Four pounds a year Wages and since she quitted that Service she hath not lived an hired yearly Servant for one whole year together been an Householder contracted Matrimony or done any other Act whatsoever to her Knowledge or Belief whereby to gain a legal Settlement in any parish or place

Sworn the 14th Day of December 1790 Philip Dyot D Walker

Mary Beath
