Middx to wit Thomas Usher Aged near Sixty Years on his Oath said that he Intermarried with Magdalen his Wife in the City of Edinburgh about Twenty Seven Years ago, and by whom he has only One Child alive namely Alexandrina a Woman grown and now provided for; And further on his Oath Saith that he Rented and lived in a houses in Naked boy Court in the Strand , in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Rent of fifteen Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes for the same has Quitted the said House about Three Years and half ago. and hath not since he Quitted the same ever Rented a House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 24th day of March 1775 before  Tho Lane

Thomas Usher
