Middx  Mard at a Church in Old Ch. 23 Yrs ago last Novr.  Corr Clare Court The Examinacon of Elizth. Ryder Passed from the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx by Order bearing date 31st. Octr. last, Who upon her Oath saith yt. abt. 4 Years ago her Husband Prissick Ryder Rented & lived in House in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx aforesd. for the space of 3 Years & upwds. of the Yearly Rent of £30; And further saith yt. her sd. Husband hath not since he left the sd. House Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband Prissick Ryder now absconds from her & she being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn this 10th. Novr. 1740 before me}
