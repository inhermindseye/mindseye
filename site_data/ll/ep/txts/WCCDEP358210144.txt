Westmr.  Elizth. Wadesell aged about 49 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of Jno. Wadesell who has been dead about Twelve Years, says her said husband Rented a house in Heathcock Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds, lived therein about six Months and there died; says she hath not since the Death of her said late husband Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 26th, day of March 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant W Kelynge

The Mark of Elizabeth Wadesell
