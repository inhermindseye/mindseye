Westmr. to wit Elizth. Timms Aged about Forty three Years on her Oath Says she is the wife of William Timms (gone from her) upwards of Twelve Years ago and dos not no know now whether he is now living or Dead and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex about Twenty Years ago and by whom she hath one Childr no living named ( Ann ) Aged about Seventeen Years, say before her Intermarriage with her said Husband and whilst he was a Single man he living a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Walker a Cow-keeper near St. Pancras in the County of Middlesex for Four years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds and After she was Married to her said Husband she never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And Says that her said Dauther has never done any act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 28th. day of October 1772 before us  Tho: Kynaston R. Butler

her Elizth. Timms Mark

Witness Edwd. Field Clk to Mr. Sidebotham
