Middx  Ann Brush aged about Twenty Three Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman and was never married and that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Old a Haberdasher in Fenchurch Street in the Parish of St. Margt. Pattons Gabriel in a Fenchurch Street  London for Fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath left her sd. Service abt. Three Years and further upon her Oath saith that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 24th- Day of October 1741 before} Jas: Gronous G Poulson

Ann Brush
