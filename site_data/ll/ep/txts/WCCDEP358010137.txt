Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Egen of No. 19 Little Shire Lane on behalf of Eleanor Crafts aged about 9 years and George aged about 6 years Two Children of Isabella Crafts in lays in at No. 19 in Little Shire Lane Maketh Oath and Saith that she this Ext. hath been informed by the said Isabella Crafts that she Intermarried with John Crafts (the Father of these Two Children) at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch that he has been dead about 2 years & an half that he served 7 years Apprenticeship to one John King a Coal Dealer in Golden Lane in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate in the City of London that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Septr. 1787  Wm. Kitchiner R Butler.

Jane Egen
