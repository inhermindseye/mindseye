Middx  The Examination of Margt. Birmingham a Vagabond Apprehended in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken before me one of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the sd. County of Middx this 4th. Day of Decr. 1744

This Examinant on her Oath saith that she Inter- -married with her present Husband  George Birmingham about 28 Years since at Church Street near the Batchellors Walk in Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland Says her sd. Husband is a Native of Ireland & never gained any Settlement in England and was born in the County of Fingall in the sd. Kingdom of Ireland but in what Parish there this Examinant can't tell Says her sd. Husband never rented ten Pounds a Year or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for One Year & where his Settlement is this Examinant knows not Says she was born without the West Gate in Lock Bay in the Parish Place or Precinct of Drogheda in the County of Mellivant in the Kingdom of Ireland aforesaid & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place

to her knowledge or beleif & that she hath one Child (to wit) Elizabeth aged Three Weeks

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before} Tho Lane

The mark of Margt Birmingham
