Westmr. to wit Mary Stephenson aged about Ninteen Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she was bound out an Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Mary White Chappel to one James Lawe a Weaver in High Street in the Hamlet of Mile and New Town in the Parish of St. Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex and served five Years part of her said Apprenticeship and her Mistress Dying and her said Master gave up her Indrentures since which time she has done no Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement Either by Renting House or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. day of October 1772 before} R. Butler 

Mark Mary Stevenson
