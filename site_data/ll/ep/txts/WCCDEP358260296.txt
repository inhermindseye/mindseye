Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Mackey aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith That she Rented and lived in a house in Red Lyon Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Eight years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds that she hath quitted said house about Twelve years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Sixth day of July 1798 before us R. Ford W Addington

her Ann Mackey mark
