Middx  Susannah Winnington upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman and was never married and that she was born in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent that her Father Wm. Winington lived in & rented a House of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards for the Space of Three Years and upwds. & there dyed & that her Mother continued to live in and rent the same House untill she dyed and that further saith that she never was an Apprentice or lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd. a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 10th- of Octr. 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Susannaer Winnington
