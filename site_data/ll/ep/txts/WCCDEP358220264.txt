Westmr. to wit Elizth. Studd Aged about fifty Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Thos. Studd with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Martins about Eight Months ago Says that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House known by the Sign of the White Bear in little Newport Street in the Parish of Saint Anns Soho in the Liberty of Westminster for Six Months at the Yearly Rent of fifty Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Two Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn this fifth day of June 1771 before} Tho Lane R Butler

Elizah Studd
