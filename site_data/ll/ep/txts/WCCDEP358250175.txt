Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Margaret Parr aged about 75 years upon her Oath saith that about Thirty two years ago she Intermarried with James Parr her late husband who has been dead about Five years at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Vinegar Yard in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for about Ten years at the yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds That and paid all Taxes due for the same That after he quitted said house he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or did any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1795 .  M Addington N Bond

her Margaret Parr Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
