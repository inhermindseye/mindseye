Middx ss  Allcock Collings Maketh oath that about twenty two years ago he was bound apprentice to John Forster a Mathematical Instrument Maker who then lived in ye Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in ye County of Middx  with whom he lived about six years then his Master removed into the Precinct of the Savoy in the County of Middx and kept a shop there known by the sign of the Mariner & Globe with whom this Deponent served the residue of his time with his said Master in the sd. Precinct And this Depont saith that he never was a hired servt. by the year nor never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any parish offices Nor gained any Settlement Subsequent to servd his apprenticeship as aforesd. And saith that he has a wife named Mary .

Sworn his 30 day of Novr. 1754 before us  Richd Ricards  Chris: Scott

Allcock Collins
