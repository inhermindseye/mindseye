Middlesex Westmr} to wit Elizth. Higham aged about Twenty Six years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year says her Father served seven years Apprenticeship with his Father Charles Higham in the Parish of St. Mary in Warrington in the County Palatine of Lancaster since which time her said Father never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 7th. day of March 1781 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at Mr. Pykes in Denzell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . which has since been baptized by the name of Lawrence and is become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same And further says that one Lawrence Donavan a Journeyman Taylor who lodges in Water Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings in Stewarts Rents some time in the month of June 1780 and at several difft. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Lawrence Donavan and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 11th day of Novr. 1783 R Butler

Elizabeth Higham
