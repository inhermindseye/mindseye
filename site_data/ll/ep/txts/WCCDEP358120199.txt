Middx  Mary Todd aged upwds. of 24 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she never was married & that she is pregnant wth. Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx where she now lodges she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born & That Thomas Wolf a Soldier in the 3d. Regt. of Foot Guards did beget the sd. bastard Child or Children on her body the sd . Thomas Wolf having had carnal knowledge of this Examt Several times in the Month of Febry last at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. Thomas Wolf & no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Septr. 28th 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Todd
