Westmr.  Thos. Rogers Aged about 40 Years on his Oath saith he rented a House in Hedge Lane in the Parish of St. Martins in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds and Paid all parish Taxes Due for the Same and lived therein Two Years or thereabout & quitted the Same at Michs. last and hath not since Done Any Act where by to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this fifth day of May 1769 before us} Tho Fellows R. Butler

Thos Rogers Witness Petr Sidebotham
