Middx to wit William Jones Aged about Sixty four years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Eleanor his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Martins in the fields about nine years Ago and by whom he hath no Children now Living, says her Rented and lived in a House in Change Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for four years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath Quitted the said House upwards of One year and a half ago & hath not since Quitting the same ever rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 30th day of Octr 1775 before R Butler

Wm Jones
