Middx  Eliz. Jones upon her Oath saith that on or abt. the 1st Day of Febry 1732 she inter- -married with her prest. Husband John Jones at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Feilds and that abt. 6 Months ago he lived in and Rented a House in Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of 10 Guineas for the Space of 1 yr. that since her sd. Husband left the sd. House he hath not rented £10. a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath 2 Childn. Jas. aged 7 Yrs. & Jas . aged 4 Yrs. & her Husbd. absconding from her & she & being Poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne the 23d. of July 1742 before}
