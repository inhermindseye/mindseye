St. Clemt. Danes

Mary Green aged 19 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes ever lived as an hired Yearly servant for Twelve Months or ever did any act to gain a settlement in his own Right That she was born in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Co. of Middx were her Mother was duly settled and is now in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Cath Saith That she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the the said Parish, And that one William Alderidge who lives Serv . with Mr. Hayward of Lincolns Inn is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants body sometime in the Month of Novr. last and at several times since at & one of which times he did beget her with Child of such Child or Children when with she is pregnant and that the said Mr. Aldridge & no One else in the only Real & true father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of Octr. 1789 before} R Butler

Mary Green

Wilfra Oldridge Man taken Release Executed. paid £20.
