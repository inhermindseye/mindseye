Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Gow aged about 43 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a lived yearly Servant with Mrs. Seymour in Oxford Road in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for Twenty Months at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said service about six Months since which time she has not done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of augt 1789 D Walker R Butler

Ann Gow
