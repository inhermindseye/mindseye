Middlesex ss}  Sarah Furnyfall the widow of Richard Furnyfall deced maketh oath That Sometime ago her Sd late husband rented a house in Sweedland Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the same & by means thereof as she is advised he gained a legal Settlemt there That her Said husband to her Knowledge or belief did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath she this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That she hath a Child named Richard  Aged about three Years & half the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd late husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this Second day of March 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Sarah Furnyfall
