Middx  Eliz: Needham aged about 19 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Wm Needham at Stratford in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £3 for two Years and upwards and Quitted her said Service about Six weeks Since

Sworn Decr. 14th. 1750 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz Needham
