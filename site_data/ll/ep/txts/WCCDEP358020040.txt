Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Peter Sidebotham  Clerk to Mr. Jennings of Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Gentleman on behalf of Two Infants of Robert Jenks who has been dead upwards of Two Months upon his Oath saith that the said late Robert Jenks informed this Dept which Information he this Dept. verily believes to be true that he was about 50 years of Age that he had been married upwards of 16 years to Sarah his late Wife at the Parish Church of Walsal in the County of Stafford that he has 2 Children living by his said late Wife vizt. Ann aged about 15 years and Charles aged about 8 years that he served his time as an Apprentice under Indres to one Joseph Griffiths a Buckle Chafe maker at Walsal in Staffordshire in the Parish and County afsd. that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain any other Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of May 1787  James Croft  Jno. Machin

Peter Sidebotham
