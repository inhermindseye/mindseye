Middlesex ss  John Hensman of the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate Middx  Beadle of the Said Parish Maketh oath that he hath been Credibly informed and verily believes That one John Chapman a Carpenter (who as this Depont has also been informed and believes is abroad in foreign Parts) was born in the Parish of Tunbridge in the County of Kent where his Settlement now is as this Deponent hath been lately informed by his Father George Chapman and his Sister in Law Martha Hopkins and Which information this Deponent believes to be true And this Deponent Saith that from the Information he hath received from the Said George Chapman and otherwise he this Depont. is induced verily to believe that the said John Chapman his son never gained any derivative or Acquired settlement since his Birth And this Deponent farther Saith that there is a Child now living in the work house of the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate named George Chapman aged about five years who (as this Deponent has been informed by this Martha Hopkins ) is the lawful Issue of the said John Chapman by his lat  wife  Ruth deced And which Said Child is lately become Chargeable to the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate

Sworn this 6th day of Sepr 1765 before us  Chris: Scott R Pell

John Hensman
