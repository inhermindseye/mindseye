Middlesex ss}  Renee Iring  Singlewoman Voluntarily maketh oath That She hath been informed by her mother Renee Iring (& which information She believes to be true) That She this Depont. was born in the Butcher Row in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex That She this Depont. was & is an illegitimate Suld by reason that her late Father Roger Iring deced had been Previously Married to another woman before his Marriage or Pretended Marriage with her said Mother which woman was living at the Birth of this Depont. & for a Considerable time after And this Depont. for herself Saith That She this Depont. never lived as hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right That She hath two Children namely Sarah Harvey who was Six years old the twenty fifth day of February last past & was born at the house known by the Sign of the Flying Horse in Lambert Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the sd. County of Middlesex & baptized and registred in the Same Parish and John Harvey who was three Years old on the Seventh day of August one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy & was born at the house of one Mrs Arnold on Artichoak Hill in the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middlesex & baptized & registred in the Same Parish both of which said Children are illegitimate & Bastard by reason that She this Depont. was never Married And that Wiliam Harvey of the said Parish of Saint Botolph witht. Aldgate  Gunpolisher did get her with Child of the Said two Bastard Children

Sworn this 6th day of March 1771 before  Cha: Digby B Camper

The Mark of Renee Iring
