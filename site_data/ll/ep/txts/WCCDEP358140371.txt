Middx.  James Alford aged abt. 22 Yrs. Upon Oath says he Never was Married And that he lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Witz at the White Swan in Hollywell Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of £6 & hath Quitted said Service abt. 5 Months & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn May 20th. 1760 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of James Alford
