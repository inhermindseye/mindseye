St. Clement Danes 28th February 1792

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Ann Marshall Aged 22 years & upwards Wife of John & Robt Marshall who hath deserted & left her inprovided for Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about on the 18th. day of January 1791 . that since their said Marriage he Rented & with her this Deponent Resided in an Apartment in Grang Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster about for three Months and upwards that they quitted that Service Apartment about a Month ago and since neither her said Husband hath not obtained any other legal Settlement that he left this Deponent on or about the 14th day of February Instant and hath Informed her by Letter that he is gone for America and this Deponent saith that she hath one Child by her said Husband now living which hath been Christened James & was born on or about the Seventeenth day of September last

Sworn the 28th day of February 1792 before me R Butler

Ann Marshall 's Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins
