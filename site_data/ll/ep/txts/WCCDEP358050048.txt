Middx 

Eliz. Wells wido. upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her first husband John Dennis at the Roman Catholick Chapel in Lincolns Inn fields about 10 or 11 Years since and that the sd. John Dennison dyed about five years ago Says the sd. John Dennison was a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlemt. in England and that she this Examinant being there before her intermarriage with the said John denning being then a Single woman lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Cornelies at the Rose Tavern near Temple Bar for one Year & upwards at the Year Wages of Two pounds and left Mr. Cornelias's Service about Ten months before her marriage with the said John Dennison

Sworn May 30th. 1749 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Eliz Wells

The Daughter of Eliz. Wells by her first husband Dennison was removed from Chalsan
