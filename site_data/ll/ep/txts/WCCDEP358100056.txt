Middx to wit Elizabeth Oswalston Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant to One Mr. Phillips a Wine Merchant in Idle Lane  Tower Street in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the East London upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of Twelve Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlement And further on her Oath Says that on the 8th. day of January last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Bolton a Staymaker in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of William and is now become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further Says that one Jerry Scorer Clerk to one Thos. Walker  Esqr . a Banker in White Hart Court Grace  Church Street London is the Father thereof

Sworn this 31st. day of March 1783 before  Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Osvoldston
