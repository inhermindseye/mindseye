Middlesex ss}  Lucy Colepepper maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with John Hughes of King Street Tower Hill in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for about the Space of two years & half that She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10 day of May 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Lucy Colepepper
