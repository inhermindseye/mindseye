Middlesex to Wit Robt. Wilcock Aged about 21 Years On his Oath says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever Gained any Settlement whatever Other than that was bound An Apprentice for seven Years to one Wm. Adams a Horner in Petticoat Lane in the Parish of Aldgate London and has served his said Master under said Indenture upwards of Three Years That this Examinant at present Lodges in Three Tunn Alley in the Parish of St Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex and Further says that he fully Intends and means to serve out his said Apprenticeship with his said Master And further says that he does not Intend to ever Lodge or Come into the Parish of St. Clement Danes during his said Apprenticeship

Sworn this 25th day of April 1782 before  Sampn Wright  Wm Kitchiner

his Robt. Wilcock Mark
