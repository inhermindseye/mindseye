Middx  Elizabeth  wife of John Man Maketh Oath and Saith that About five years ago her said Husband rented a house At hour Craydon in the parish of Craydon in the County of Surry and pd. thirteen pounds a Year for the same and lived therein One whole Year and upwards and has gained no Subsequent Settlemt: to her knowledge

Sworn this 28th. day of March 1744 . Robert Dennett

Eliz Mann
