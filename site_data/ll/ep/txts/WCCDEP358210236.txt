Westmr.  Peter Smith Aged about Sixty six Years Upon Oath says he never was Married, and that he rented a House in Bennett Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds, lived therein about Two Years and an half, quitted the same about Twenty five Years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 1st. day of February 1768 before} 

Peter Smith
