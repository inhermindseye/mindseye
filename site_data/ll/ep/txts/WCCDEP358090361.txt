Middx  James Knight aged about 62 Years on behalf of Ann Singlewoman Aged about 80 Years who now  Ill on his Oath Says that the said Ann Stacey Lived as hired Yearly Servant with the Revd. Mr. Delaport at the Privy Seal Office Opposite the Horse Guards in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middlesex for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her sd. Service about Seventeen Years ago since wch. time she hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a Ye  paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 9th. day of Jany. 1783 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Jas. Knight
