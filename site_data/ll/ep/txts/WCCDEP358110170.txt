Middx  Mary Marsh aged 23 years upon Oath saith she never was married & never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant rented ten pounds a year or pd. parish taxes that she hath been informed are believes her father Heny. Marsh decd. Served his Apprentiship with a Cooper in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldersgate London and never lived a yearly Servant for one yr. Rented ten pounds a year or pd. Parish taxes and that she (this Examint.) together with her father & mother were removed from the Parish of St. George Southwark to the afsd. Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldersgate abt. 18 years ago and her father dyed in the Workhouse of the last mentioned Parish

Sworn Febry 7th. 1753 Before Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of  Mary Marsh
