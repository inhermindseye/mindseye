Middx  Jno. Egan upon Oath Saith he was born in the parish of and Town of Roscommon in Ireland & lived a yearly Servant wth. one Mr Jones in the same Parish for one year & upwds. at the yearly wages of £3 for one year & upwds. and has not since he quitted his sd Service rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn March 14th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Jno Egan
