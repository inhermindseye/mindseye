Middx  The Exaicon of Elizth. Roberts  Wido . of Jno. Roberts Deced. Aged abt. Fifty four Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 11th. day of Janry 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that abt. Seven Years agoe she this Exaiant was by an Order under the Hands & Seales of two of his Majestys Justices of the Peace for the sd. County of Middx (one of the Quorum) removed from the Parish of St. Leonard in Shoreditch to the Parish of St. Katherine by the Tower as the place of her last legall Settlemt. wch sd. Order of removall was never Appealed agt. as this Exaiant ever heard And this Exaiant Further Saith that her sd. Husbands father had a Certificate from the sd. Parish of St. Katherine by the Tower and that her sd. Husband never was an Apprentice Rented Ten Pounds a Yeare lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Yeare or otherwise gained a Settlemt. to her knowledg or beliefe & that she is Lame & Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

The mark of Elizth. Roberts
