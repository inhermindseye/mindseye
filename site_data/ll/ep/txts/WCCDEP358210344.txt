Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Simpson Aged about Eighteen Years on her Oath Says She never was Married nor never Rented House Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father John Simpson deceased has Often Informed her and which Information She verily believes to be true that he lived many Years as a hired Yearly Servant in the Parish of St: George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex to a Gentleman there whose Name this Examinant cannot now recollect But Says that her said Father falling into decay was taken into St: Georges Work house and lived therein Several Years and there died

Sworn this 29th. Day of Decr. 1768 Before me} J Fielding Thos Kynaston }

The of Mark Ann Simpson Witness} Fras: Bishop  Beadle
