St Clement Danes July 2d 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement in St C D }  (to wit) Elizabeth Dunkin  singlewoman aged 60 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Ogden at the York Anns in Clements  Little Shire Lane for a year or upwards at the annual Wages of Six pounds that she quitted that service about upwards of four years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 2d. day of July 1794 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Elizth Dunkin
