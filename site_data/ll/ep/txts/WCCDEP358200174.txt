Westmr  Rachel Bishop Aged about Twenty Eight Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year Says her Father Daniel Bishop Served Seven Years Apprentiship with his Father the Corner of New Court in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & never after Rented Ten pounds by the Year or paid Parish Taxes as this Examinant hath been Informed & which Information she Verily Beleives to be true And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to Become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Thos. Watson a Quarter Master on Board

of his Majesty's Ship the Dolphin had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body Some time in the Months of April or May last & at several of her times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant.

Sworn the 3d of December 1764 Before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Rachel Bishop
