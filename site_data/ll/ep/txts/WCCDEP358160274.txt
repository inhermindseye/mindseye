Middx  The Examination of Thos. Patrick living in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 11th. day of March 1740 Who upon his Oath saith That abt- Eighteen Years ago he this Examinant rented a House in Naked boy Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds & lived in the Same for the Space of three Years or thereabts. That he hath not since that Time rented any house Lands or Tenemts. of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife That he hath a Wife who is now delirious in her Senses & this Exaiant being Poor is not able to maintaine her

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Tho Patrick
