Middx  The Exaicon of Mary Magee living at one Mrs. Dickinsons in new Inn Passage in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & Liberty of Westmr. Taken ye. 8th. Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her voluntary Oath saith yt. she is a Single woman & was never marryed & is pregnant with Child or Children which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards & likely to become Chargeable to ye sd Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintaine ye: Same when born And further saith that one Thos. Baldwin at Number 4 in Elm Court in the Temple  Esqr . did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body she at such time living with one Mr Sculley in Bride Street  Dublin & there he became accquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body at one Mr. Knowles at the Sign of the Globe in Essex Street  Dublin Sevll. times in ye.

Month of June last at one of which Times he got her with the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he & no other Person is the true real Father thereof

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Magee
