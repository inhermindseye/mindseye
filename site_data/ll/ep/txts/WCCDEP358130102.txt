Middx.  Ann Atkinson Aged about 45 Years upon Oath saith She is the Widow of Arthur Atkinson deceased who rented a House in Sheer Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Thirteen pounds lived therein upwards of Eight Months and quitted the same in the Year 1745. Saith her said late Husband whilst living nor She this Examinant since his Death have not rented Ten pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes elsewhere not has She lived a yearly Servant for one year
