Westmr. 

Eliz: Wakefield  Wido : of Wm. Wakefield upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd Wm. Wakefield on the 16th. Day of May 1731 in the Parish of St. Botolph Bishopgate and that her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Hilla Turner in Moore Lane in the Parish of St Giles with Cripplegate London and never rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 8th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Elizabeth Wakefield
