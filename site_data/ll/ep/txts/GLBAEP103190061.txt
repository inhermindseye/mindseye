Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Wilson the wife of George Wilson who is now absent from her at Sea in the Kings Service maketh oath that he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly Served his Apprenticeship with Mr Jackson Printer to the University of Oxford in the City of Oxford but the Particular Parish there She Can't at Present recollect & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She was lawfully married to her sd husband by whom She hath a Child lawful Issue now living named Eleanor Aged about three Months

Sworn this 21. day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Eleanor Wilson
