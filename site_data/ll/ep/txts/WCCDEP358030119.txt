Middx  Mary Barker aged abt. 40 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married & that her Father Jno. Barker lived in & rented a house in Great St. Helens in the Parish of St. Helens near Bishopgate London for the Space of Seven Years or thereabouts of the Yearly Rent of £25 & paid his Share of all Parochiall Taxes Says she never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 17th. 1745 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Barker
