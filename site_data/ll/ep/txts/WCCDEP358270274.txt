Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Catherine Naish aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Miss Pearce at No- 202 in the Strand and in Wych Street both in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for near Sixteen Months at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained or subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Broad who did lodge at Mrs. Willis's opposite the Dover Castle on the Narrow Wall  Lambeth and now works near the same place is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Broad is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 6th- day of Octr. 1796 } J Reeves

Catherine Naish

Catherine Naish lives at Mrs. Coopers No. 26 Duke Street Aldgate

Man out upon Bail Man afterwards gave a Note of Hand for £17:0:0: which was afterwards Paid

Child born in the house 7th: April named John

Sent to Enfield  3d. June 1797
