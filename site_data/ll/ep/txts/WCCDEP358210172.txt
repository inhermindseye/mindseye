Westmr.  Edmund Morgan aged about 66 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Sarah his Wife at Shoreditch Church about four Years ago by whom he hath One Male Child aged about one Month as yet unbaptized, says he Rented a House in Dirty Lane  Long acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Nine Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Thirteen Pounds, Quitted said house about Seventeen Years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid parish Taxes.

Sworn the 21st, day of July 1767 , before} John Cox  Saunders Welch

The Mark of Edmund Morgan
