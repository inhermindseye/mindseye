Westmr. to wit Edward King Aged about Twenty Six Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex about five Months ago and who now Lies ill in Saint Bartholomews Hospital and by whom he hath no Children now living Says before his Intermarried with his said Wife and whilst he was a Single man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Davis at the Swan and hoop Tavern in Swan and hoop Alley Cornhill in the Parish of Saint Mary Woodnoth in the City of London upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted his said Service about Eight Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 13th. day of March 1773 before Us  Sampn Wright R. Butler

F Edwd King
