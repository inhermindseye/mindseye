Middx ss  Susannah Bryley  wife of Garrett Bryley maketh oath that her sd husband is now at Sea on Board the Bristoll Man of War And that he is an Irishman But where his place of settlement is she Cannot setforth And this Depont saith that she lived as a hired servant by the Year about eighteen Years ago being then a Singlewoman with My Lord Inchiquen in Grovesnor Square in the Parish of st. George Hanover Square in the County of Middx at ye Yearly Wages of Six pounds & Continued in such service for the space of a Year & upwards & by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt there And saith that she hath not since gained any subsequent settlemt. in her own right. And that she hath three Children to wit Frances aged about twelve Years Julian aged about ten years and James aged about three Years the lawful issue of this Depont by her sd husband

Sworn this 1st day of Febry 1757 before us  Richd: Ricards  Walter Berry

the Mark of Susanna Bryley
