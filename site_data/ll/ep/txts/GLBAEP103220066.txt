Middlesex ss  George Firth maketh oath that he heretofore rented a house of the Yearly Rent of twenty five Pounds in Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Presided there in for about the Space of one year & half That he has not Since rented a house of the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid Any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Mary to whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this. 22. day of August 1786 before R Davies

George Firth
