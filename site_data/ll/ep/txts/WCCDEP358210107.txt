Westmr.  Elizabeth Brown aged upwards of 50, Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Charles Brown who has been dead about fourteen Years and with whom she intermarried at the Fleet London on the 12th, day of June 1730; says her said husband before his intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with this Grace the Duke of Somerset at Charing Cross in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for two Years or thereabouts but at what Wages she this Examinant cannot tell and never Rented House or paid Taxes; says she hath not since her husbands Death Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 21st. of Novr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Elizth. Brown
