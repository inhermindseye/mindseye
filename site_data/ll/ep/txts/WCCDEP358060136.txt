Middx  Eliz. Chambers aged upwards of 16 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she hath been informed & verily believes she was removed with her father & mother from the parish of St Mary at Islington to the parish of St Clemt Danes Middx by Order under the hand and Seal of two of his Majestys Justices of the place for the sd. County Dated the 22d. Decr. 1736 which Order was not appealed against and that since the sd. Removal this Examinant hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived as an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become chargeable to the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Thomas Rumbold a farmer at Battle bridge did beget the sd. bastard Child or Children on her body the sd. Thomas Rumbold having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in October last & at sevl. other time since at one of which times he got her with Child

of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said Thomas Rumbold & no other person is the true and rent father thereof

Sworn July 5th. 1751 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz Chambers
