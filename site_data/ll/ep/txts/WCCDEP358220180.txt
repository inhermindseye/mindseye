Westminster to wit John Kingston Aged about fifty Six Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his late Wife at the Parish Church of Shadwell in the County of Surry about Twenty Years ago and who has been Dead about Thirteen Months And this Examinant further says that he was born in Liverpool in the County of Lancaster and Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Head a Merchant in Liverpool afsd. about forty Years ago and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th. day of September 1770 before me} R Butler

his John Kingston Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
