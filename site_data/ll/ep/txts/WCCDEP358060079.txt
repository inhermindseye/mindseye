Middx 2d. over above Dru Alley  Eliz Foxall upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of William Foxall with whom she intermarried at Bishopgate Church about Twenty nine Years since Says her sd. husband lived in and rented a house in Drury Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £30 for several Years and there dyed and this Examinant has not since her sd. husbds. decease rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 11th. 1750 before}
