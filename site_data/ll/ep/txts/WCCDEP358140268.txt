Middx.  Thos. Seward aged abt. 74 Years upon Oath says he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Thos. Fennell a Baker in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. says he served four Years the first part of his Apprenticeship with the sd. Thos. Fennell in Essex Street aforesaid and then absconded from his said Master's service and went to Sea says he never was assigned over to any person nor Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 14th. July 1759 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Thos. Seward
