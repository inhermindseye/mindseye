Middx  Eliza: Hudson  Singlewoman aged about 18 Years upon her Oath saith she was bound Apprentice to Rebecca I y of Barebinder Lane in the parish of St. Mary Woolnoth  Silver Burnisher with whom this Examinant lived about one Year and then was Assigned over to are John Deale a Chimney Sweeper with whom this Examint. lived about 3 Years Says the said Jno. Deale removed into Thatch Alley in Chick Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchers London Says she lived with her sd. Master in Thatch Alley about Ten Weeks & hath absconded her sd. Service about 3 Months Says she hath not since she left her sd. Service lived with any person with the Consent of her sd. Master or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 20th. 1745 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Eliz: Hudson
