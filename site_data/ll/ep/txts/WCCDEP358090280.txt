Middlesex to wit Jane Skillen Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Charles Piggot  Esqr . in South Audley Street in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Ten Guineas hath quitted her said Service upwards of Ten Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 23d. Janry 1782 before Mr. Gregson Wm Kitchiner

Jany Skillen
