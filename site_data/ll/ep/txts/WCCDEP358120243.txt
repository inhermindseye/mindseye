Middx  Mary Dougan aged abt. 21 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Hughes at the yearly Wages of Fifty Shillings for one Year & upwards that the last three months of her said Service she constantly lay in her sd masters house in Great Wild Street in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex that she Quitted her sd Service abt. 6 months ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the year nor pd. parish Taxes

Sworn 11th: Decr. 1754 : before Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Mary Douggan
