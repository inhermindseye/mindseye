Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D. but no Settlemt there, his psh is St. Mary Le Strand as appeared by a subsequent enquiry  (to wit) James Newcome aged 32 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was Married to Grace Shephard his late Wife at the Parish Church of St Anns Westmr . (by Banns) about 11 years ago that he had one Child by his said Wife who is since Dead, that he heard she (his secd Wife) went abroad to St Vincents in the West Indies about 6 years ago & that she died about two years since that he Rented and Resided in a House in Knights Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes for about three years at twelve Guineas a year the that he quitted the same about eight years ago and he hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 25th day of June 1794 before me } Wm Kitchiner

James Newcome
