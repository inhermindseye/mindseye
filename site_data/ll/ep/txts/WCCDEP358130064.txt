Middx  John Luff Aged abt. Forty two Years upon Oath Says that whilst he was a Singleman he liverd a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Jno. Douglass  Linnen Draper the Corner of St. Clemts. Lane in Cannon Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. near Eastcheap in the City of London for four Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds & there Married since wch time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or Served any Parochiall Office

Sworn the 8th. day of Octr. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

John Luff
