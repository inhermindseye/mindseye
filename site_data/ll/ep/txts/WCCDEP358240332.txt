St Clement Danes October 11th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Columpton Devonshire  (to wit) James Talboys one of the Beadles of the parish of St Clement Danes (on behalf of James Whitby the aged four years or thereabouts deserted by his Parents) Maketh Oath and saith that he is informed and verily believes that the said James Whitby was born sometimes in or about the Month of November 1789 at the Lying in Hospital in Brownlow Street  Long Acre that he is the legitimate Child of Risbon and Mary Whitby who were legally Married at the parish Church of the parish of Shipton Mallet in the County of Somerset on or about the 29th day of November 1788, that the said Risbon Whitby 's best legal Settlement obtained his Settlement in the parish of Columpton in the County of Devon by serving his Apprenticeship with his father Thomas Whitby a Surgemaker and this Deponent saith that he hath never heard that the said Risbon Whitby ever acquired any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the 11th day of October 1793 } Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Jas Talboys
