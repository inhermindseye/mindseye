St Clement Danes 23d March 1792

Middx & Westminster }  Taken such tie House being in Stepney parish he Husbands Settlmt. As afterwds discovered & the woman & Children remr. there Wide Eleanr. page 48  (to wit) Hannah Pain Aged about & 25 Years Wife of John Pain who hath left her Upon oath Saith that she was married to her Husband John Pain at the Parish Church of Saint Michael Royal & St Martin Vintry in the City of London on the 22d. June 1798 (by Licence) that she knows not nor hath ever heard where her Husband legal Settlement is that she hath frequently asked him to inform her the place of his Settlement but he never gave Answer to such questions that She this Deponent while She was Single and unmarried lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mathews at the Cock & Hook in Bakers Row in the parish of Saint Mary Matfolon otherwise White chapel in the County of Middlesex for one year at the Annual Wages of Four Grounds that She quitted that Service about Six or Seven years ago and hath never done any Act. Matter or thing whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement in her own right as she best knows and believes And this Deponent farther saith that She hath one legitimate Male Child by her said Husband which is now living Named John and was Aged Eight Months on the first day of this Instant March And this Deponent Saith that she is unable to provide for herself Child with Assistance And then her said Husband left her & Child abt. Six Months ago & that she hath not since heard of her in & knows not where he is

Sworn the 22d. March 1792 before Us} Sampn. Wright N Bond

Hannah Pains Mark

Witness Edwd. Burbridge
