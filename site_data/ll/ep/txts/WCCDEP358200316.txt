Westmr.  Elizabeth Scuttle aged about 39 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thomas Scuttle who has been dead about Ten Years says her said husband hath often times informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he was bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the poor of the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn London to Thomas Smith a Chimney sweeper and served his said Apprenticeship with the said Thomas Smith in the Coal Yard in Holborn in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx and lived as a Yearly Servant with his said Master in the Coal Yard aforesaid for three Years after his said Apprenticeship expired, and then intermarried with this Examinant, says her said husband in his life time nor she since his Death never Rented house or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 9th. Novr. 1765 , Before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The Mark of Elizabeth Scuttle
