Middlesex ss  John Loveless maketh oath that about twenty Nine Years ago he was duly bound an apprentice by Indre for the Term of seven years to one William Gregory of Slutts Hole in the parish of Chiswick in the County of Middx  Fisherman with whom this Depont duly served his apprenticeship in ye same Parish & by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt. there And this Depont. saith that he has not since lived as a hired servant by the year Nor been an Apprentice Nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards Nor done any Act to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent to gain any subsequent settlement And that he hath a wife Named Ann

Sworn this 11th. day of July 1759 before us  Chris: Scott  Walter Berry

his John Loveless Mark
