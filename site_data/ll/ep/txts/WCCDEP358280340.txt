Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to Wit Elizabeth Studd Aged upwards of 21 years Upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married that she lived as a hired hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Mo Simpson late Mrs. Morris in Stanhope Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 15 Months at the Yearly Wages of £3 And this Deponent Examinant further Saith that she is Pregnant with a Child or Children That is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes when born, And further Saith that a Man who lives at Mr. Hodges on Mill Bank a distiller but Lodged at Mr. Forty's who keeps the Ship at Milbank where this Examinent lived 5 Months since she left Mrs. Simpson's late Mrs. Morris in the father of such Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant he having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body some time in the beginning of May last past and at several times since at One of which times he did beget the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant And that he and no One else is the Real and true Father thereof And further saith that she does not know the said of the Father of the sd. Child or Children which she is now Pregnant with but that his Christian Name is Joseph that he is a very Lusty Man & tall Generally wares a Short Round Frock & An Oil skin Hat & has a Dark Shock Head of Ha

Sworn this 7th of October 1791 before R. Butler

Elizabeth her Mark Studd
