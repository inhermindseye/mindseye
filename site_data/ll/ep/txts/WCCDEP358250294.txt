Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Mackenzie aged about Eighteen years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cherry a Butcher in Spital fields market in the Parish of Christ Church  Spital fields in the County of Middlesex for Sixteen Months at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1797 before us  Robinson Rich. Ford

her Ann Mackenzie Mark
