Middx  The Examination of Sarah Mixon  Wife of James Mixon passed from the Parish of St. Olive Hart Street London by Order bearing Date ye. 19th- Day of Decr. Instant who upon her Oath saith that her sd. Husband James Mixon abt. 15 Yrs- ago rented a House of thirty Pounds a Year in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & lived in the Same for the Space of one Yr. or thereabts- & this Examinant further saith that her sd. Husband James Mixon hath not since yt. time rented any other House pd. parish Taxes lived with any person a Yearly hired Servt . for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & that her sd Husband now absconds from her & She being big with Child & near her time is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the 20th Day of Decr. 1740 before}
