Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Hall aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that about 33 years ago she Intermarried with John Hall her present Husband at the parish Church of Saint James Westmr. by whom she hath Six Children living Three of Whom are un provided for vizt.  Richard aged about Eleven years James aged about 9 years and Thomas aged about Seven years That her said Husband rented and lived first floor of Mr. Aaron Carcass No. 10 Change Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for Three Months at the yearly Rent of Ten Guineas That he quitted said Apartments about fifteen Months since which time she has done no Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of March 1795 } N Bond

Elizabeth Hall
