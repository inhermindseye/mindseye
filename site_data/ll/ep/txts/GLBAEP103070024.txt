Middx ss  Rachael Smith  Wife of Thomas Smith Maketh oath that her Son William Costen (who is is Now at Sea ) was about eighteen Years ago bound apprentice to John Fletecher of Hogg Lane in ye Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in ye County. of Middx  Smith with whom he lived four Years & upwards and then his Master delivered up ye Indies & they parted by Consent And this Depont. Saith that her sd. Son hath not Since gained any Subsequent Settlement to the knowledge or belief of her this Deponent And that her sd. Son hath Now a Son named William Costen aged about five Years the lawfull issue of her sd. Son

Sworn this first day of February March 1750 before us  Richd. Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

her Rachael Smith Mark
