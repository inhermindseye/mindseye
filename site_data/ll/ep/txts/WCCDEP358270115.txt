Elizth. Pearman and Lawrence & James Pearman Bastard Children}

St Clement Danes June 1st of 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement (to wit) Elizabeth Pearman  Singlewoman aged 30 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly servant with Mr Harris at 3 Tuns Clare Market for a year  & upwards at the  annual Wages of six pounds that She quitted that Service about seven years ago and hath not obtained any Subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge Information or belief And upon this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that on or about the 18th day of May last she was delivered of two Male Bastard Children at the Workhouse of the said parish of St. Clement Danes both since half Baptized the one by the Name Lawrence Pearman and the other James Pearman that she this Examinant has been in the Workhouse that of the said parish for twelve months last part that she first was connected with Lawrence Warren the father of the said Children on or about the 26th day of May 1792 and at Several times since at some or one of which times he did legal the said Children thereon on on the Body of this Examinant And that he and no one else is the only real true father thereof that he the said Lawrence Warren is a Pauper maintained in this Workhouse of the said parish and that she first became acquainted with him there Warren the father of there Children has been several years a pauper in the House taken before Mr. Bleamire who objected to commit them or do any thing more than represn and of them

Sworn the 18th day of June 1793 before me Mr: Bliamire

Elizth. Pearman Mark
