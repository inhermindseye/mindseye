Middlesex ss}  George Norris maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mr William Benson a Farmer in the Parish of Teddington in the Said County and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Whole year That he has not since lived as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere nor rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath two Children namely Sarah aged about three years & two months & Martha aged about Nine months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his late wife deced to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 10. day of April 1788 before  R Davies  Robt Smith

The Mark of George Norris
