Westmr. 

Ann Kelley  Wido . of Malachi Kelley aged abt. 34 Years upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said Malachi Kelley abt. 19 Years ago at the Romish Chapel near Lincolns Inn feilds Says he has been dead about 9 Yrs. Says her sd. late husband about 12 Years since lived in & rented a house in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of £30 for two Years or thereabouts Says neither her sd. late husband after he last sd. house nor hath this Examinant since his Death rented ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn 7th. April 1747 before}
