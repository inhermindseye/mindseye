Westmr. 

Eliz: Adams aged abt. 21 Years upon Oath saith she never was married Says she was born in the Parish of Fyfeild in the County of Essex where her father Wm. Adams deceased lived in a house of his own of the Yearly Value of Fifty Shillings or thereabouts several Years & there dyed Says she never was an Aprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 5th 1746 before  Tho Lane

Eliza Adams
