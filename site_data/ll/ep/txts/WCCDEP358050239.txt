Middx 

Mary Owen on behalf of Abrahm a Male bastd. Child upon Oath saith the mother of the sd. bastard Child (whose name is Sidney Tollah ) at present absends & this Examinant knows not where she is and that she hath been informed & verily beleives the sd. Child is a bastard and that the sd. Sydney Tollah the mother has been Sworn touching the reputed father of the sd. male bastard Child and that the sd. Sydney Tollah was delivered of the sd. Male bastard Child in this Examinants house in Charles Court

in the parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . on or about the 9th. Day of this Instant April

Sworn April 28th. 1750 before} Samuel Clarke

Mary Owen
