Middlesex ss}  Ann Jones the wife of  George Jones who is gone to Sea maketh oath That her Said husband has informed her that he was born in the Parish of Westbury in the County of Gloucester & that his Fathr rented two Farms there but what Rent or Taxes he Paid for the Same She Cannot Set forth That to her knowledge or belief her Said husband never did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right.

Sworn this 25. day of November 1772 before}

a Vagrant Pass made on to for this afterwards dead

The Mark of Ann Jones
