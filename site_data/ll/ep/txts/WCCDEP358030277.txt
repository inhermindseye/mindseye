Westmr.  Eliz: Winter on Behalf of Mary Gray an Infant Child aged upwards of three Years upon Oath saith she has been informed and beleives the said Mary Gray was born in Gardiners Lane in Petit France in the Parish of St. Margt. in the Liberty of Westmr. Says she cannot tell where the Father and Mother of the said Infant now are neither can she give any Account of their Settlement

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Eliz Winter
