Saint Clement Danes

Ann Jandrells aged 16 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was Married lived as an hired Yearly Servant for twelve Months. ever see bound apprentice or did any Act Matter or thing to gain a Settlement in her own right to the best of her knowledge information or belief and this Examinant further upon her Oath saith that her Father Benjn. Jandrells while single and unmarried lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bowler a Tobacconist in Bishopgate Street in the Parish of Great Sr. Helens in the City of London for upwards of twelve Months at the ye and received Wages for the same, that he quitted his service about upwards of 20 Years ago, since which times her said father hath not gained any subsequent Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge or belief That her said Father & Mother were Married as this Deponent believes at the Parish Church of Saint Ann's Blackfriars

Sworn this 11th. day of October 1788 before R Butler D Walker

Ann Jandrell
