Middx Mary Lowndes aged abt. Thirty five Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with Saml. Lowndes in the Parish Church of St. Cathn. Coleman Street London abt. Fourteen Years ago says her said Husband Samuel Lowndes Rented a House in Charles Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden at the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds lived therein abt. Eight Years & quitted the same abt. Nine Years since when he left this Examinant & She hath not seen him since but hath heard that he dyed in Holland abt. four Years ago & further says that from the time of his quitting the said House in Charles Street he never Rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes as She verily believes & that She hath not since the hearing of the Death of her said Husband Rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 27th. Day of July 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond  Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Mary Lowndes
