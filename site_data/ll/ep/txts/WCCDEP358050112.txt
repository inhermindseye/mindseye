Westmr.  Eliz. Wakefield  wido . of Wm. Wakefield upon Oath saith she Intermarried with the sd. Wm. Wakefield on the 16th. Day of May 1731 in the Parish Church of St. Botolph Bishopgate and that her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr Hill a Turner in Moore Lane in the Parish of St Giles without Cripplegate London and that neither her sd. late husband of this Ext. since his Bank never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Octr. 4th. 1749 before H Fielding

Elizabeth Wakefield
