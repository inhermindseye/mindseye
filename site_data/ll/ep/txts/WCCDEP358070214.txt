Westminster to wit Benjn. Morley Aged about Twenty Eight Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlex in November last past and by whom he hath no Child Says he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Ealing at an Academy at Kensington Gravel Pitts in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middlesex upwards of Sixteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds hath quitted his said Service upwards of Eight Months ago since which time he hath never gained any subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1774 before} 

Benjamin Morley
