Westmr.  Sarah Coe aged about fifty Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Ralph Coe who has been dead about Twenty Years, says the said Ralph Coe was a foreigner (being born abroad and beyond the Seas but in what part she cannot tell) and never gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge and belief says before her intermarriage with the said Ralph Coe, and whilst she was a Singlewoman She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Wilson a Taylor in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds and then inter- -married with the said Ralph Coe; says she hath not since her said Husbands Death, Rented the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 31st. Octr. 1766 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Sarah Coe
