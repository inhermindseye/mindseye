Middx  John Robt Bowman aged abt. 63 yrs. upon Oath says he Intermarried with his prest. Wife Mary at Burford in Oxfordshire abt. the year 1746. by whom he has five Children (to wit) Mary aged abt. nine years Elizabeth aged seven years Millicent aged abt. five years Charles aged abt. three years & John aged abt. one year Says that John Bowman his father deceased Rented a house upwards of twenty Pounds a year in Bridges Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. & lived therein sevl. years & quitted the same abt. thirty nine yrs. since as he has been inform'd & Verily Believes and this Examinant says that he never Rented a house of Ten Pounds a yr. pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servt

Sworn the 30th of April 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell  Theodr Sydenham
