Middlesex ss}  Jane Anderson the Wife of Jonathan Anderson maketh oath That her Said husband who is abroad in the Army was by birth a Sestchman & never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England That She this Depont previous to her Marriage with her said husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with James Hamlet in the Parish of Morton in the County of Sales Harmer at Certain Wages & Continued in such service for the Space of a year & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year so as to gain a Settlement elsewhere nor has she done any other Act to gain and Settlement save her marriage as afore sd

Sworn this 20 day of Septemr. 1777 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Jane Anderson
