Middx  Ann Tufton  Wido . of Thomas Tufton upon her Oath saith that her sd. late Husband hath been Dead abt. 9 Years & that in the Year 1720 he rented a House in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £50 & lived in the same for the Space of Six Months & upwds- & after her sd. late Husband left the sd. House he went into Lodgings in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the sd. County & rented Chambers in Clements the same Parish of the Yearly Rent of Eight Pounds that he hath not since he left the sd. House nor she since his Death rented any House of Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Fourteenth of August 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

Ann Tufton
