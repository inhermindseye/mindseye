Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Ann Smith  Singlewoman aged about forty five years on his Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Barlow a Silk Mercer at the Wheatcheaf in King Street  Covent Garden in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . for fourteen months in the Capacity of a Cook at the yearly Wages of twelve Guineas hath quitted her said Service upwards of three years ago since which time she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 31st. day of January 1784  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

her Ann Smith mark
