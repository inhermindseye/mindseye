Mary Hull aged near 17 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she never was an apprentice or yearly Servant rented ten pounds by the year nor paid Parish taxes that her late father Chas. Hull deceased rented a house in Great Newport Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. lived therein Sevl. Years & quitted the same abt. Ten Years ago & never afterwards rented ten pounds by the Year or paid Parish taxes

Sworn Augt. 10th . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Hull
