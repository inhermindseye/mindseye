Middx  Mary Cole  Wife of Geo: Cole upon her Oath saith she intermarryed with the sd. George Cole on the 26th. Day of Febry 1704 at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husband Served two Years the last Part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. Cambridge a Taylor in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Says her sd. Husbd: had a Certificate from the Church wards. & Oversrs. of the Poor of the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes dated the 2d. Day of Decr. 1714 acknowledging this Examint. & her sd. Husbd. with their two Childn. (to wit) Geo: then aged 7 Yrs. & Diana then aged 4 Yrs. to be Inhabitants legally Settled in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Says her sd. Husbd. hath absconded from her abt. 7 Yrs. & hath not since the Granting the sd. Certificate rented ten pounds a Yr. Served a Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Novr. 13th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Mary Cole
