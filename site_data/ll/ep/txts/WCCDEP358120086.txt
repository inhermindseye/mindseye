Middx  Susa. Smith aged about 13 Years upon Oath saith she is the Daughter of John Smith deceased by Susanna his wife (who has left this Examinant) and that she has been often told her sd. late father was born at Skillgate in the County of Devon and Served his Apprentiship with a Carpenter there which information She believes to be true and that she this Examinant never was an apprentice or yearly Servant for one year

Sworn April 3d. 1754 before} Tho: Lane

The Mark of Susa. Smith
