Middx to wit Ann Collin Aged about 25 years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says she was born at Colverstown in the County of Kildare in the Kingdom of Ireland & never gained any other Settlement than by Birth and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further says that one James Counick a Journeyman Sugar Baker who now works at one Mr. Jermaine a Sugar Baker in Distaff Lane London is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her Lodging in Little Stanhope Street sometime in the Month of Decr: last and at sevl. Different times afterwards at one of Which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Counick & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 3d. Day of Septr. 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Collin Mark
