Middx  Wm. Clark upon his Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife  Sarah in the Abbey Church in Bath on the 30th. Day of May abt. 15 Years since Says he lived a Yearly hired Servt. (before his sd. Intermarriage) with Mr. Harvey in Searle Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £5 for 2 Years & upwds. Says he hath not since he left the sd. Mr. Harveys Service Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 4th. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Wm. Clark
