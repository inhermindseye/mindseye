Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Bryan aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Lee in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Sixteen Months at the yearly wages of seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Six weeks since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have or gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Octr. 1797 before me } W Carter

her Mary Bryan mark

Witness B Newman
