Middx  Mary Ponder aged 29 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Wm. Elvins  Peruke maker for one Year & upwards in Howard Street, in Arundel Street & in the Strand all in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Four pounds that she quitted her sd. Service abt. ago & has not since Rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Decr 10th. 1754 before}
