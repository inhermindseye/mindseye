Middx & Westmr}  St. Martins J.  Ann Bartlett aged twenty nine Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived with Mr. Hayman a Turner in the Hay market in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex as an hired Yearly Servant for upwards of two Years and an half That she quitted her said Place about ten Years ago and since that time has not done any Act Matter or King whereby of the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere than in the said Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields

Sworn this 30th. Novr - before G Mercer

Ann her Mark Bartlett
