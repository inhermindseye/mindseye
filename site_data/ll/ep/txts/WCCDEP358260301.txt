Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Demaine aged about 47 years upon her Oath saith that about Twenty years ago she Intermarried with John Demaine her late Husband who has been dead about four years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said late Husband was an Irishman and never gained any Settlement in England that previous to her Intermarriage with her said late Husband and being a Singlewoman she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Fulker at the White Horse in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for near Five years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that after she quitted said Service she never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of July 1798 before me } Saml Edward

her Susannah Demaine  Mark

Witness H Day
