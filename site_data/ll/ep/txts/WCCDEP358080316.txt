Middx & Westmr} to wit Ann Fewater Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Lock head at the White Heart in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of five pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of Three Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born And that one Richard Price a Hackney Coachman in Pools Rents in the Strand is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of April or May last in a Hackney Coach in Wych Street & at the Bull & Gate in Holbourn and one other time since at one of which time she got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Richard Price & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 25th. day of Novr. 1778 before  Jno. Machin

her Ann Fewater Mark
