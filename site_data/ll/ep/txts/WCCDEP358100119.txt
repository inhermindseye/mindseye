Middlesex to wit Lewis James aged about Fifty years on behalf of of Thos. James his Son an Infant aged about fourteen years on his Oath says that his said Son never gained any other Settlemt. than through this Examinants Renting and living in a House in Bedford Court Catherine Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for sevl. years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted said House about Ten years ago since which time he hath never Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 3d. day of Septr. 1783  Thos: Parker  Will Wright

his Lewis James mark
