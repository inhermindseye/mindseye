Aldgate Middx

William Burridge Maketh Oath that about Thirty Years ago he was bound an Apprentice to William Child a Cooper in Rattcliffe highway (then the Hamlet of Wapping Stepney ) now the Parish of Saint Geo : Middx with whom he Servd Seven years That he never was a hired Servant Since, nor never rented a house of ten pounds a year, nor never paid any parish taxes nor Servd any Parish Office hath a wife  Mary Burridge

Sworn 12th day of November 1746 before me Robert Dennett Richd. Ricards

Wm- Burridge
