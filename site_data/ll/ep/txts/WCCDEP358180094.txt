Middx  Sarah Sandford aged about Sixteen Years Removed from the Parish of St. Bridgett vulgarly St. Brides London by Order Dated the 10th. Day of June 1743 upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnt. with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born And that one James Cooper  Button maker did Begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body she at such time liveing with her Aunt in the sd. Parish of St. Bride London where the sd. James Cooper became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body several times in the Month of May last at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Childn. with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. James Cooper & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne July 20th. 1743 before}
