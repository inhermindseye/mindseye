Middx to wit William Slater aged about fifty four Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Elizabeth his late Wife deceased at the Parish Church of Saint Michael in the City of Oxford about Twenty four Years ago & who died  about Seven Years ago and by whom he hath no Children living Says he has Two illegitimate Children living by one Martha Lawrence lately deceased namely William Aged about Twelve Years and Catherine Aged about five Years the first born in George Yard Tower Hill and the latter in Long Lane Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre Middlesex and that they have gained no other Settlement than by Birth Says that this Examinant Rented and lived in a House in the Corn Markett in the aforesaid Parish of Saint Michael in the City of Oxford for One Year & an half at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds hath quitted said House about Twenty Three Years ago and hath not since he quitted the some ever done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th. day of Febry. 1783 before R Butler

Wm. Slater
