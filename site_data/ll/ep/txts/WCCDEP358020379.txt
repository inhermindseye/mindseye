St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster}  Witness Jas. Talboys (to wit) Elizabeth Campion  Wife of James Campion Ag Maketh Oath & saith that she was Married to her said Husband James Campion at the parish Church of St. James Westminster on or about the fourteenth day of September in the year 1783 that she hath two Children now living by her said Husband namely Ann Aged about One year & Eleven Months or thereabouts and John Aged Eight Months & upwards-, that her said Husband hath left her & is gone to seek for Work that he Served of his Apprenticeship with a (under Indres duly executed) for the last year with his Father John Thomas Campion in Coventry Court in the Haymarket in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County and Lodged Boarded in his said father's House and he hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge or belief

Sworn the 25th Jan day of Janry 1792 before us Addington Edwd. Naish

Elizat. Campion Mark
