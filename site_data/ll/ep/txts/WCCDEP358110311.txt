Westmr.  John Bedford upon Oath saith he was born in Bedford bury near the Corner of Mays buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of westmr . that he can give no Account whatever of his late father or Mothers Settlemt. and that he never was an Apprentice rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes and never lived a Yearly Servant for one year whilst a Singleman

Sworn Septr. 13th. 1753 before}
