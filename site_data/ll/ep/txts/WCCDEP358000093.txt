Saint Clement Danes 16th Augt. 1785

Middlesex (to wit)  Pass'd. to Saint Paul Covent Garden  Elizabeth Athey aged forty five Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was Married to Pearce Athey her now Husband at the Parish Church of Ham in the County of Kent stat before then Intermarriage her said Husband lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Doctr Herne Bow Street in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of upwards of ten Pounds that since he quitted the said Mr Herne's Service he has not to this Deponents Knowledge done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere than in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden that she has three Children by her said Husband Vizt. Susan aged Eleven Years on thereabouts, Indeneas aged Eight Years or thereabouts and William aged seven Years or thereabouts that her said Husband has deserted her and her said Children and that she is Poor and unable to maintain

herself and her said Children name of whom have ever gained a Settlement in their own Right

Sworn this 16th Augt. 1785 before  James Croft  Wm Gascoigne

Elizth Athley her Mark
