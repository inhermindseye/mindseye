Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Eleanor Lackey aged about 69 years upon her Oath saith that about 40 years ago she Intermarried with Hugh Lackey at the Parish Church of Abbin in Ireland Saith that her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Parkers Lane in the Parish of Saint  Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for 15 years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and paid Taxes hath quitted said House about 20 years ago since which time she hath done no Act matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any other Settlemt.

Sworn this 3rd. day of March 1786  Thos: Gordon M Addington

her Eleanor Lackey Mark
