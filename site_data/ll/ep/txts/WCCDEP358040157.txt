Westmr.  Susanna Josslyne  Wido . removed with her Daughter Sabra aged 19 Years from the Parish of St. John at Wapping by Order dated the 25th. Instt. upon Oath saith she intermarried with her late husband Robt. Josslyne upwds. of 30 Years ago at the Fleet London Says her sd. late husband Served Seven Years. Apprentiship with Mr. Robinson a Cane Chair Turner in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never in his life time nor has this Examinant since his Death rented ten pounds Year pd: Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says her sd. Daughter Sabra never was married or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Febry 27 1747 before}
