Middlesex to wit. Elizabeth Want Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year, says her father Edward Want lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Edward Lewen a farmer in the Parish of Roxburn in the County of Hertford for several years but at what yearly Wages she cannot set forth And further says that her said father now Rents a small Tenement in the aforesaid Parish of Roxburn where this Examinant was born but whether he pays any Taxes due for the same she cannot say, And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on Monday the 23d day of October Instant she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which is intended to be Baptized by the name of Elizabeth , And that one Fairfax Morrisby a Hardware man in King Street Covent Garden is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her body on the 19th day of February last and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Fairfax Morrisby and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 31st day of October 1775 before  David Walker

her Elizabeth Want Mark
