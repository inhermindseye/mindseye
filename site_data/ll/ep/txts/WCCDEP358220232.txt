Westmr. to wit Mary Millett Aged about Thirty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was Married was an Apprentice Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Thomas Osborn a Carpenter the Corner of Exeter Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for fifteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago and after quitting the same she never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. day of Janry February 1771 before} Petr Planck

her Mary Millet Mark

Wittness Fras Bishop
