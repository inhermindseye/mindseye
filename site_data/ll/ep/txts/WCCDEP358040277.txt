Middx 

Eliz: Layn aged abt. 27 Years upon Oath saith she never was married & that she was on the 30th. Day of June last delivered of a male Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Jane Fraser in Duck Lane in the Parish of St. Bartholemew the Great London which has been baptized Saml. and that the Child which she has now with her and which has been since dropped in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes is the same Child of which she was deliver'd as aforesd.

Sworn Novr. 4th. 1748 before Tho Lane

Elizabeth Layne
