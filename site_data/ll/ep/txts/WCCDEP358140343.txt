Middx.  Wm. Appley aged abt. 70 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Charity his wife in St. Clemts. Church in the Strand abt. the Year 1724 Says he Rented a house in Bennett's Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for 16 Years at the Yearly Rent of £12. and hath quitted same abt. Nine Years & hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 21st. Jany. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

William Appley
