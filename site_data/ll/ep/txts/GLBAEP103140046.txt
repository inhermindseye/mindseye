Middlesex ss}  Richard Watts maketh oath That his late Brothr Samuel Watts deced was duly bound apprentice to and Served the Whole time of his Apprenticeship with Gifford Pascall of the Precinct the Old Artillery Ground in the Liberty of his Majesties Tower of London in the County of Middlesex  Perukemaker and by means thereof gained a Legal Settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief his said Brother did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that this Deponents said Brother left behind him a Child now living named Samuel Watts aged about Fourteen years who has never been bound an Apprentice nor lived as a hired Servant by the year nor done any Act to this Deponents Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 18th: day of July 1774 before  Saml. Spagg B Camper

The Mark of Richard Watts
