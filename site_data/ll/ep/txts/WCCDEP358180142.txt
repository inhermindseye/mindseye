Middx  Jno. Metcalfe aged abt. 31 Years removed from the Parish of St. Brides London by Order Dated the 7th. Day of Decr. Instt. upon his Oath saith that on the 26th. Day of May 1741 he intermarried with his present Wife  Elizabeth at the Fleet London that abt. 4 Yrs. since he lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Robt. Cooper at the Corner of Vear Strt. in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the Space of One Yr. & 10 Months that since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented £10. a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived with any Pson as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif that he hath one Child viz. Eliz: aged one Year & 10 Months & being Poor is not able to maintain himself & family

Sworne 16th. Decr. 1743 before} C. Egerton

John Metcalfe
