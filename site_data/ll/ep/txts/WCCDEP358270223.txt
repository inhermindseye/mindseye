Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Hicks  Singlewoman aged twenty Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married nor did ever do any Act whereby to gain any Settlement in her own right, that her father John Hicks who is a Copperplate Printer sei ed his Apprenticeship to Mr. Hawkins of Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Printer and since he so served the same hath not as this Deponent is informed and believes acquired any other Settlement than in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further this Examinant on her Voluntary Oath saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes, and that one John Collins who now works with Mr Wilton of Carey Street Coal Dealer is the real and true father of such Child or Children of which she is so pregnant he the said John Collins having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Months of June, July August and September last and at several times, since at some or one of which times he the said John Collins did beget the said Child or Children where with the this Examinant is so pregnant, and that he the said John Collins and no one else is the real and true father thereof. Man gave a Joint Note of Hand to pay 2s. P week

Sworn this 6th. Day of April 1795 before} M Addington

Sarah her Mark Hicks
