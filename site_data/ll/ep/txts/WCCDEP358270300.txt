Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Webster aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the Twentieth day of December 1795 She this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Webster No. 251 in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the name of Joseph That one William Prosser a Smith who lives at No. 5 on Back Hill the bottom of Hatton Garden is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinats Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid thereof and that the said William Prosser is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 11th- day of April 1797 before me} J Hunter

her Hannah Webster Mark

Hannah Webster at Mr. Jas. Westons No. 29 Fenchurch Street

Man taken and paid £20, the 18th. day of April 1797 . Release Exted

Child sent to Enfield  29th. April 1798.

NB. the Brother of Wm. Prosser was Apprehrd. by the Beadles through Mistake he gain them a Release
