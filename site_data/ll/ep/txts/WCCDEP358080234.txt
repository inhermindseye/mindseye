Westmr. to wit Henry Hubbard Aged about forty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with his late Wife Sarah at the Parish Church of St. Bartholomew about Ten Years ago and by whom he hath Three Children Two of whom are now in the Workhouse belonging to St. Giles Cripple gate London & the or is now provided for Says this Examinant Rented and lived in a House in Pauls Alley in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate London of the Yearly Rent of fifteen pounds lived therein for about half a Year hath quitted same about Ten Years ago since which time he hath gained no other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 31st. day of March 1778 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Henry Hubbard
