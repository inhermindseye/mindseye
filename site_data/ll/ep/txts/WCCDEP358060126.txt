Middx  Eliz North aged about 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that on the 13th. Day of May last she was delivered of a male Stilborn bastard Child (at Mrs. Moores one of the parish Nurses of the parish of St Clemt Danes Middx ) at the Charge of the Inhabitants of the said parish And that Laurence Holbrook Apprentice to Mr. Brown of the said Parish Corkaitter did beget the said male bastard Child on her body the sd. Laurence Holbrook having had Carnal Building of this Examinant in the month of August last & at several other times since at one of which time he got her with Child of the said male bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Laurence Holbrook and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn June 20th. 1751 before  Tho Lane  Geo: Errington

The Mark of Eliz North
