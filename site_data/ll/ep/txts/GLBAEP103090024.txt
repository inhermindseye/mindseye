Middlesex ss  Hannah Briggs  Single woman Maketh oath That about Sixteen Years ago she was bound apprentice by the then Churchwardens and overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx to one John Mac Daniel who then kept a Green Shop in Whites Yard in the Parish of St. Mary White chappel in the said County with whom she lived under such Indenture for the Space of two Months And then her said Master Mac Daniel being obliged to go to Ireland (as he informed her) Entred into an Agreement with one John Smith of Whites Yard aforesaid Taylor That She this Deponent shod. serve him the said John Smith under the Said Indenture for the remainder of her Apprenticeship to which this Deponent was Prevailed on & gave her Consent and thereupon in Consequence of such Agreement & Consent she this Deponent went to & lived with the said John Smith as his Apprentice in Whites Yard aforesaid and this Deponents Indre was delivered up to the Said John Smith and fifteen Shillings Paid by the said John Smith to the said John Mac Daniel as the Consn of this Deponents being turned over to him the said John Smith to live with him in Manner as aforesaid as she this Depont. was then informed by her said Master Smith and verily believes to be true And this Depont farther Saith that she lived with the said John Smith in Whites Yard aforesaid in Pursuance of such Said Agreement for about the Space of two Months When her said Master Smith removed from thence to the Corner of Black Jack Alley in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the said County where she also lived with her said Master Smith for the Space of divers Months to the best of this Deponts. now remembrance & belief and then her said Master left her, & went away to Places Unknown to her And this Depont. Saith that she hath not since lived as an Apprentice nor as a hire servant for a year nor was ever Married nor hath she done any Act since her servitude as above to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of January 1766 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

The Mark of Hannah Briggs
