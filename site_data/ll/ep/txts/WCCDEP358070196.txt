Westmr to wit Isaac Gurney Aged about Thirty Two Years on Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizth . his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le Bone upwards of five Years ago and by whom he hath three Children now living Namely Mary Ann Aged about four years Charlott Aged about Three Years and Carolina Aged about Eighteen Months Says that before his Inter- marriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Thomas Whillingham at a Carpett Warehouse  in Bread Street Allhallows in the City of London  for one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds besides Diet and Lodging hath quitted his said Service upwards of Six Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1774 before R. Butler William Addington

Isaac Gurney
