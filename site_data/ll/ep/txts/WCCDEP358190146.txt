Middx  Hannah Doleman aged about 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married bound an Apprentice kept House Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant in any one place for Twelve Months together That her Father  John Doleman who was a Copper Plate Printer lived in and Rented a House in Cucumber Court Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds & there dyed about 14 Years ago

Sworn the day of June 1761 Before}
