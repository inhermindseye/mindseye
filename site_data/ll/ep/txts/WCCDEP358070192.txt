Middlesex to wit Ann Wynn Aged about Sixty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever Served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Green a Doctor of Musick in Beaufort Building in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex upwards of Seven Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Thirty Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Sixth day of June 1774 before} 

her Ann Wynn mark

Witness
