Middx to wit Elizth Nevett Aged about Eighteen years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Young a Taylor in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of 4 Guineas hath quitted the said Service about 2 Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement & further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same says that one William Mather a Journeyman Taylor who uses the Trumpett in Great Shire Lane is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House some time in the Month of December last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at Several Different times after at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of she is now Pregnant and that the said William Mather and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 13th. day of October 1778 before R Butler

her Elizth Nevett Mark.
