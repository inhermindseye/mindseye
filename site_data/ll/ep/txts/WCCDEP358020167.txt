Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Lloyd aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that about Nine Months ago she Intermarried with William Lloyd her present Husband (who has deserted her) at the Parish Church of Saint Mary at Lambeth that her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to a Mr. Hutton in the Temple Hair Dresser that he was Turned over from the said Mr. Hutton to a Mr. Sherwood who lived in Saint Martins Lane and afterwards from the said Mr. Sherwood to a Mr. Kitchen who lived in Hutton Garden in the Liberty of Saffron Hill Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the County of Middlesex where he lived Three Months and upwards and that after he quitted said Mr. Kitchen he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information on belief to gain a Subsequent settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of Octr. 1789  Sampn Wright J Addington

Ann Lloyd
