Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Thomas Mogford aged about 31 years upon his Oath saith That on the first day of January 1792 he Intermarried with Elizabeth his present Wife at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom he hath Two Children living namely Gideon aged about Two years and John aged about 17 Months That he this Deponent Rented a Shop and Parlour in King Street  Seven Dials in the parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Six Months and upwards at the yearly Rent of Fourteen pounds that he hath quitted said Apartments about Seven Months since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Augt. 1795 } N Bond Russl Clarke

Thomas Mogford
