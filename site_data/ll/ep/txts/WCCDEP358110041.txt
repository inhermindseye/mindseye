Middx  Ann Richardson upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Edward Richardson who dyed about 12 yrs since & that her sd. late husband Served his apprentiship with a Taylor in Bridges Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year as he informed this Examinant and that she herself has not since the Decease of her sd. late husband Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn April 22d. 1752 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Ann Richardson
