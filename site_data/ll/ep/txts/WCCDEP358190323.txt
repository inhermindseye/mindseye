Middx  Mary Jennison Aged about 47 Years Upon Oath says she is the Daughter of Ralph Jennison  Fishmonger who rented a House next Door to the Ship Tavern in Butcher Row in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds or thereabouts Says she never Rented a House was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn Janry: 18th 1768 Before me} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Jennison
