Middx.  Mary Brusty  Wido . of Abraham Brusty upon her Oath saith that her said late Husband Abraham Brusty before this Examinants Intermarriage with him and whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Nathaniel Hatt a Brazier in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds Ten Shillings that her said late Husband never after nor she since his Death lived with any Person one Year rented House Paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 1st. Octr. 1742 before} Houlson W. Hayton

Mary Brusty her Mark
