Middx & Westmr}  Ann Jones Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Jones who has been dead upwards of Two Years ago Says her said late Husband lived as a Covenanted Servant with a Shoemaker at Wrexham in the County of Denbeigh in Wales & where her said Husband was born but what her said Husbands Masters Name was she cannot set forth but that he never gained any or. Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 26th. day of June last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in County of Middlesex which has since been baptized by the Name of John and is now chargable to the same Says that one John Culverwell a Servant to one Mrs. Tindall at Stoney Hill Bristol is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body some time in the Month of October last but the particular day she does not remember at which time he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said John Culverwell and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of July 1776 before M Addington

her Ann Jones Mark
