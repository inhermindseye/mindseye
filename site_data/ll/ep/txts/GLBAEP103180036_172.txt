Middlesex ss}  Charlotta White maketh oath that divers Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the Year with one Richard King in  Mansfield Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the Said County Gentleman at Ceratin Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one Year & three Quarters & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a Female Child aged about Six Weeks born of her Body in Brown Bear Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County which Child is illegitimate & a Bastard & one Gray Arnold is the Father of the Said Child.

Sworn this. 5. day of May 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

Charlotta White
