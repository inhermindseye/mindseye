Middx.  Alice Rice aged abt. 30 Yrs. upon Oath says she w the Wife of Neale Rice (now at sea) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Clunfeigle in Ireland abt. two Years since; says her said Husband was born in the said Parish of Clunfeigle as she has been informed & believes & never gained any Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or belief says she has one Child by him (to wit) Mary aged abt. 18 Months

Sworn the 1st. day of March-1757 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Alice Rice
