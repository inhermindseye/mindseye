Middlesex  Ann Wright  Wife of John Wright of Haughton Street Clare Market maketh Oath and saith that she hath a Daughter named Sarah (by her said Husband) who is aged near 20 years and is now every weak and infirm And saith that her said Daughter Sarah Wright hath never been married and about five years ago she lived an hired yearly Servant with Mr Edward Jones at No. 4 in Pitt Street Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for upwards of two years together at Fifty Shillings a year Wages And since she quitted that Service she hath never lived an hired Yearly Servant for one whole year together having for the greatest part of the time at Intervals been very infirm and disabled and mostly supported by this Examinants said Husband and hath done not Act which could acquire a Settlement subsequent to her Service with the said Mr. Jones in Pitt Street aforesaid to her this Examinants Knowledge or Belief.

Sworn the 6th Day of December 1790 . Wm. Kitchiner R Butler

Ann Wright
