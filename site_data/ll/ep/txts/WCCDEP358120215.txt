Middx  Dymoke Longstaff upon Oath saith he rented a house of the yearly Rent of £33 at the Corals of Essex Strt. in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx lived therein near two Years and quitted the sd. house abt. 9 Yrs. ago & has not since rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year that he was married to his Wife Esther Longstaff at the Parish Church of St Clemt. Danes afsd. near 14 Yrs. ago and has by her two Children Esther aged 9 Yrs. and Alice aged 4 Yrs.

Sworn Octr 18th 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Dymoke Longstaff
