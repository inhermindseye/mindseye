Middx  Wm. Mingal upon Oath saith he rented a house of £12 by the Year in Crown Court Russell Street in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the County of Middx and Lived therein about 20 Years & hath left the same about 6 months since which he has not rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 27th. 1750 before Tho Lane .

Wm Mingal .
