Sarah Davis and her Child named Wm Edward Bastardy after Birth

St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman settled in Wimbledon Parish Surry  Child St CD (to wit) Sarah Davis  Singlewoman aged twenty years and upwards Upon Oath saith That she never was Married That she lived as an hired year yearly Servant with Mrs Mr Carter foreman of the Copper Mill at Garrett in the parish of Wimbledon in the County of Surry for one Year at the and annual Wages of never obtained any Settlement in her own right to the best of her knowledge Information or belief That she hath been informed her father John Davis  Richard Davis was legally Married to the Deponent's Mother Elizabeth , but  she knows not when or where That the her said Father lived as an hired yearly Servant in the capacity of Astler with Mr Carter at the Rose and Crown in Wimbledon in the parish of Wimbledon in the County of Surry for sometime and thereby obtained his settlement with at parish and saith That her said Father and Mother both Died in the parish House the and were buried at the Expence of that parish and that she this Deponent with one of her Sister Elizth.  were brought up and maintained in the Poor House of the same parish and place out by the parishioners thereof And this Deponent saith That she hath not nor is entilled unto any legal settlement save as aforesaid by her said father in the  said parish of Wimbledon And Deponent farther upon her oath saith Tho

she was delivered on the 27th day of October last at the House of Mrs. Wilson in Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child which hath since been Christened by the Names of William Edward and is become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes That Edward Dickason a  Shoemaker who now as is as she believes at Chatham in the County of Her Kent with his Mother who resides near the Hare and Hounds on Rochester Bank is the only rel real true father of such Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he and no one else having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime about Chris Christmas  1790 and at Several times afterwards at some or one of which time h times he begot the said Child on her Bo thereon and that she hath never been criminally connected with any other person but the said Edward Dick Dickason and that she is unable wholly to maintain the said Child without Assistance

Sworn the 10th. day of July 1792 before me} R Butler
