Middx  Ann Mathews aged about 24 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she Lived a Yearly Servant with Miss Vaughan in Norfolk Street in the parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds for one Year & upwds. and was discharged her sd. Service on the 28th. of March last And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same where born and that Thomas Hill late a Servant with the abovenamed Miss Vaughan did beget the sd. bastd. Child or Children on her body the sd. Thomas Hill having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in her Mistress's House afsd. in the month of February last and at Sevl. other times Since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Thomas Hill & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn Septr. 6th. 1751 before me  Richd. Chamberlayne

The Mark of Ann Mathews
