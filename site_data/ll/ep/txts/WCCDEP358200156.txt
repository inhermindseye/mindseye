Middx  Cath Baker Aged about 28 Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Henry Baker with whom she Intermarried in the Parish of Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about Ten Years Since Says the said Henry Baker is Gone from her where he is she knoweth not neither Can she Give any Account whether he is Living or Dead Says her said Husband Informed her & which Information she Verily beleives to be true that he Served the first part of his Apprentiship with a Butcher in Red Street in the Parish of South fleet in the County of Kent

Sworn the 30th of Octr 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cath Baker
