St Clement Danes Novr. 28th. 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  Liberty of Saffron Hill Etc  (to wit) Elizabeth Parker singlewoman aged sixty three years & upwards Upon Oath sat saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Dr. Miersbank in Hatton Garden in the parish Liberty of Saffron Hill  Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the County of Middlesex from the Month of May 1776 to the Month of June 1777 at the annual Wages of ten pounds, that she quitted that service in or about the said Month of June 1777 and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to best of her this Examts. knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 28th day of November 1793 before us M Addington B: Kennett

Elizth Parker
