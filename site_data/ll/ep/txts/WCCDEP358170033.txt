Middx  The Examination of Stephen Turner taken the 27th- Day of March 1741 Who upon his Oath saith that on or about the 14th- Day of October 1739 he Intermarried with Elizth - his now Wife & that abt- fifteen Years ago he was bound an Apprentice & served Seven Years Apprentiship to one Mr- Fearon in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes Taylor . And further on his Oath saith that he hath not Since he left his sd. Master rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt- for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife.

Sworne the Day & Year abovemenconed before} Jas: Gronous

Stephen Turner
