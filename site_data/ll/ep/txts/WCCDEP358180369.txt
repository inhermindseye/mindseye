Middx  John Harris aged about 60 Years upon his Oath Saith he lived in & rented a house in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of £26 for the Space of Nine Years or thereabouts & hath left the same twenty five Years & upwards since which time he hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant with any person for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 20th. Augt. 1745 before Wm: Jones Jno: Harris
