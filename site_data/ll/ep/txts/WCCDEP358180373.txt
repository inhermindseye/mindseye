Middx  Wyborough Wood aged abt. 27 Yrs. removed from the Parish of St. Paul at Deptford by Order dated the 2d. Day of Septr. Instt. upon her Oath saith she is Singlewoman & was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servt. with Mrs. Fendall in Arundell Street and in Sheir Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £2. for the Space of Seven Years or thereabouts & hath left her sd. Service abt. 4 Yrs. since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 7th. Septr. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Wyborough Wood
