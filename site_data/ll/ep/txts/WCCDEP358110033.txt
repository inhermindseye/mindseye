Middx  Edwd. Finch aged about 42 years upon Oath Saith he was bound Apprentice for three years to Mr James Stephens  Haberdasher of Hatts by Indenture dated the 5th. of January 1727 and lived as an Apprentice with his said master near the little Savoy Gate in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex near two years at the End of which time his Master broke and this Examinant never was assigned over to any person nor ever Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 28th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Edward Finch
