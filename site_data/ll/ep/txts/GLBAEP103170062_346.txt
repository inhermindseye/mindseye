Middlesex ss}  Mary Mumford  Singlewoman & Daughter of the above named Elizabeth Mumford maketh oath that She this Depont is lawfully Settled in right of her late Father John Mumford deced in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County She this Depont never having been as Apprentice nor lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever been Marked nor done any Act to gain a Settlemt in her own Right That She hath two children namely Mary Russell aged eleven years & two months & James Russell aged two years & Six weeks both of whom are Bastard & iIlegitimate & born in the parish of St John Wapping in the sd County & have done no Act to gain a Settlement in their own right & that one James Russell now or late of Swan Alley in the sd parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate  Labourer is the father of the said Children

Sworn this. 6. day of March 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Mumford
