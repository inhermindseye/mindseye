Westmr.  Margaret Shaw aged about 33 Years upon Oath says she was never Married and that she was bound an Apprentice by the Church wardens and Overseers of the poor of the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster to James Fry of Swallow Street in the parish of St. James in the said Liberty of Westminster  Lamp lighter and served about Seven Years of her said Apprenticeship and then her Master gave her the remainder of the time she had to serve which was about four Years; says she never was Assigned over to any person, Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year.

Sworn the 10th. of Augst. 1765 . Before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The Mark of Margt. Shaw
