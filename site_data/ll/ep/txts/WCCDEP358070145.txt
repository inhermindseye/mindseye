Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Collis Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented house or paid Taxes she lived a hired Yearly Servant with her Brother John Collis a Waiter in Cumberland Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of one Year & an half ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the fourth day of Febry 1770 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in her said Brothers House in Cumberland Court aforesaid who has since been Baptized by the Name of Frances she being Poor and unable to maintain the same Says that one Mr. Thomas Holman a Custom house Officer & who belongs to the Searchers Office is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her Lodgings at one Mr. Vaughams in Wych Street some time in the Month of Feby 1769 and at  times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered a aforesaid and that the said Thomas Holman and us other pur did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father th

Sworn this 3d. day of Novr. 1773 before W Kelynge

Mary Colles
