Middx to Wit Martha Robinson Aged about Forty five years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Owen Robinson her late Husband deceased at the Parish Church of St. Anns Limehouse in the County of Middx in the year 1767 and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says her said late Husband never Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for One year Says her Husbands Father Rented a Small House at Mitcham in the County of Surry where this Examinants Husband was Born as her said late Husband has often Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true & can give no further or other Account thereof than as aforesid And this Examinant further saith that on this Examinants being passed by the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on the Parish of Mitcham in the County of Surry the Parish of Mitcham Appealed to the then next Genl. Qr. Sessions of the Peace for the County of Middx where it was Proved that Wm. Robinson the Father of this Examinants Husband Owen Robinson was a Certificated Man from the Parish of Fulmer in the County of Sussex to the Parish of Mitcham in the said County of Surry as by a Certificate bearing Date the 14th. day of May 1737 may more fully Appear The Original of which is now in the Hands of the Parish Officers of Mitcham in the said County of Surry.

Sworn this 26th. day of July 1783 J Leroux

Martha Robbinson
