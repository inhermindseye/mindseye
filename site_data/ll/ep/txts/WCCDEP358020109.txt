Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Joseph Bird aged upwards of 60 years upon his Oath saith that he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Eyre at Sans Coffee House in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service upwards of 18 years since which time he has done not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th- day of Novr-1788 R Butler

Joseph Bird
