Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Dunbar aged about 41 years upon his Oath saith That he Rented a Shop in Newport Market in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for about Three years and paid Two Shillings and Six pence per week for the same and this Dept. further saith that he Rented at the same time an unfurnished Room of Mrs. Harbut in Newport Market in the said Parish of Saint Anne for which he this Deponent paid the Sum of Two Shillings per week That he hath quitted said Shop and Room about Fifteen years since which time he this Deponent hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of Decr. 1794  N Bond Cha: Churchill

James Dunbar
