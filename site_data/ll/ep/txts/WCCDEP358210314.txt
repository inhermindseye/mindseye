Middx & Westmr.}  Frances Squibb Aged about Twenty Four Years, upon Oath says she never was Married and that on the Thirtieth day of November 1766, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Staveley in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex which hath been Baptized by the Name of John , Says that one John Brent a Copper plate Printer who works with Mr. Lewis in Houghton Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes is the reputed Father of the said Male Bastard Child he having had carnal knowledge of her Body the beginning of March 1766 and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid, and that the said John Brent and no other Person is the true and real Father thereof

Sworn the 6th. day of August 1768 , before} Thos Kynaston .

Frances Squibb
