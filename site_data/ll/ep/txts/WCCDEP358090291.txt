Middx  to wit Osmond Cook Aged about forty Six Years on her Oath Says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife about Tw Years ago and by whom he has Two Children living namely Amelia Aged about Sixteen Years and Mary Aged about fourteen Years both of whom are now provided for Says he rented and lived in a House in New North Street near Queen Square in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr Queen Street in the County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds hath quitted said House about Two Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year paying Taxes or orwise howsoever

Sworn this 15th. day of Febry. 1782 before}
