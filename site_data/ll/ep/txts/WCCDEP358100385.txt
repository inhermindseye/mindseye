if she twds. to d or he taken  Isabella Rock aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith That she was never was Married Rented a House or Lodging or ever lived as an Hired yearly Servant for One year saith that her late Father John Rock and her Mother Mary Rock were as this Deponent hath been informed and believes Married at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes that she this Examinant was born in Greyhound Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes That her Father Died abt. 4 Years ago in the Workhouse of the sd. Parish and further when her Voluntary Oath saith That she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born And further saith that one Rosaman Cane who is a Hackney Coachman and formerly lived with the Clark of Cripplegate and is now in St. Bartholomew Hospital is the Father of the said Child he having had Carnal of this Examinants Body sometime in Month of May last and at many times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the sd. Rosaman Cane & no other Person did beget the same, he is the real & true Father thereof

this 21. day of April 1781 R Butler

her Isabella Rock Mark
