Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Read the wife of William Read maketh oath That her Said husband is now Confined as a Prisoner for Debt in the Marshalsea Prison That her Sd husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with his Father Thomas Read a Cooper in the Parish of Saint James Kings Cliffe in the County of Northampton That her Sd husband to this Deponts. knowledge or belief never rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Apprenticeship That She hath three Children namely William Aged about Ten Years & three months Sarah aged about Seven years & five Months & George Aged about two years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband to whom She was lawfully Married

Sworn this 23. day of October 1778 before removed Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Elizabeth Read
