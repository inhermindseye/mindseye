Abigail Skett Aged about Sixty four Years Upon Oath Says she is the Wife of John Skett gone from her with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster about Nine Years ago Says her said Husband Rented a House in Bear Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted said house about Two Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes.

Sworn the 7th Day of July 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Abigail Skett
