Middlesex ss}  George Robinson maketh oath That about Forty Six Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indre to one Daniel Forrester of Upper East Smithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Cordwainer & Served about three Years of Such Apprenticeship & then his Master died there & then this Deponent left the Sd Parish & afterwards went into the Sea Service That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement Since his Said Apprenticeship

Sworn this 29. day of March 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of George Robinson
