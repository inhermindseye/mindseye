Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Mary Grendon Aged about forty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Webster Esqr. in Great Marlbrough Street in the Parish of St James in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Eighteen Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of April 1776 before  Wm Kitchiner M Addington.
