Middlesex ss}  Lewis Morgan maketh oath That about two years ago he was bound apprentice by Indenture by the Parish officers of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex to one William Hudson of Church Street in the Parish of St. Ann Westminster in the County of Middlesex  Perukemaker and Continued there with his Said Master as his apprentice for Three Months or there abouts And then this Deponts. Said Master with this Depont. removed into Little Princes Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex where this Depont. was put by his Said Master to Serve one Mr Roberts a Perukemaker there and where this Depont. resided for the space of three Months and lodged in the Said Roberts's house & his Master Hudson received one Shilling a week for his Wages as this Depont. was informed & believes And by means thereof as this Depont: is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. soon after left his Said Master who refused to Provide for him & hath not done any Act to his knowledge information or belief to gain a settlement since he left the Said Parish of St. Giles in the Fields .

Sworn this 5th: day of December 1769 before  Chris: Scott  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Lewis Morgan
