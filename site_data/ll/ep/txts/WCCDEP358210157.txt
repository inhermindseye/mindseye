Westmr.  Elizth: Harvey aged about 27 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year, says she has been informed by her Mother (who is now dead) that she this Examinant was born in Great Swan Alley in St. John Street in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex where this Examinants Father Rented & lived in a house but what Rent he paid for same this Examinant never heard.

Sworn 2nd. May 1767 , before } Barth: Hammond  Thos. Kynaston

Elia Harvey
