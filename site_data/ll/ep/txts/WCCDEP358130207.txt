Middx ss.  John Rutter one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex or behalf of Silvester and Sarah Eakin the Children of John Eakin by Mary his Wife both deceased upon Oath Says that by Order under the hands and Seals of Ambrose Godfrey & Thomas Deveil Esqrs. two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the said County of Middlesex bearing Date the 4th. Day of Decr. 1741 Mary Eakin Wife of John Eakin (who was therein said to be gone from her) with their three Children therein named were removed from the Parish of St. Clement Danes to the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields as the Place of their last legall Settlement as appears by the said Order of Removal Says he has been informed and which information he verily believes to be true that the above named Silvester Eakin and Sarah Eakin are the Children of the said John Eakin & Mary his Wife.

Sworn the 13th Day of September 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

John Rutter
