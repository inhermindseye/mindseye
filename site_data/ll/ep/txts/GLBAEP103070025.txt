Middx  Eleanor Wormlayton  Singlewoman Maketh oath that about two Years and three quarters ago she lived with as a hired Servant with Henry Gregory of ye parish of St. Andrew Under Shaft in the City of London at the Yearly Wages of four pounds and Continued in Such Service for a Whole Year & upwards & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement And this Deponent Saith that she hath not since gained any Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this first day of February March 1750 before us  Richd. Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

her Eleanor Wormlayton Mark
