Westmr.  Richard Coombe aged about 29 Years Upon Oath says he never was Married, And that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Cawley Apothecary in the Strand opposite St. Clements Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for three Years or thereabouts and was found in Clothes of all sorts in the room of Wages, and Diet Washing & Lodging, Quitted said Service about Ten years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year, paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn Octr. 28th. 1767 , before. W Kelynge

Richd. Coombe
