Westmr.  Elizabeth Buchannan Aged about Fifty five Years, Upon Oath says she is the Widow of David Buchannan who has been dead about Fifteen Years, says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Rhodes a Stay maker in Clare Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Five Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds quitted said Service about Six Years ago, and never after Rented House, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 20th, day of May 1768 , Before} Tho's. Kynaston

Eliz: Buchanan
