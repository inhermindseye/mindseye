St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr. (to wit) Sarah Dunn  Singlewoman Aged 34 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cox the Oxford Arms Houghton Street Clare Market in the said parish of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty about 13 Months at the Annual Wages of £5 that she quitted that Service about 14 years ago and since hath not (by any means whatever) obtained any other legal settlement and that she is now it Distressed Circumstances & necessitated to apply to the Parish for Relief

Sworn the 13th. day of Septr. 1791 . before one  Wm Hitchiner

Sarah Dunn 's Mark
