Middx  John Seath aged about 14 years upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Walker at the Hole in the Wall in Chancery Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £3 for one Year & upwards quitted his sd. Service about two Years ago and has not since lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn April 27th. 1752 before W Fielding

The Mark of  John Seath
