Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizabeth Trepas aged about 62 Years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 3rd. day of December 1751 she Intermarried with Henry Trepas her late Husband (who has been dead about Four Years) at the Fleet London by whom she hath no Child or Children being That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a hour in Eagle Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for Six Months at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and that after he quitted said house which is about Fifteen Years ago he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter a thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of Decr. 1795 before us  W Kinnard N Bond

Elizabeth Treapas
