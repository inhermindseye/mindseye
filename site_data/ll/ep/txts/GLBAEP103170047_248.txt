Middlesex ss}  James Pitman maketh oath yt his late Father James Pitman deced divers years ago rented a house in the parish of St Paul Shadwell in the Sd County & was Charged to & paid poors Rates & kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anr Settlement to this Deponts knowledge or belief That he this Depont never lived as a hired Servt for a year nor was ever bound Apprentice nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt in his own right

Sworn this 21. day of Janry 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of James Pitman
