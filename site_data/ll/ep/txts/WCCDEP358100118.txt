Middlesex to wit Mary Hatfield Aged about Thirty five Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Hatfield deceased with whom she Intermarried at Christ Church  London about four Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she went to live as a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Rosier a Button Seller in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Eight Guineas and where she at present lives And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes (she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born) And further says that one Charles Walker Shopman to the said Mr Hosier did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body in his the said Mr Hosiers House some time in the Month of December last but the particular day she does not now remember at at different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant of and that the said Charles Walker and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof.

Sworn this 1st. day of Septr.1783 before R Butler

Mary Hatfield
