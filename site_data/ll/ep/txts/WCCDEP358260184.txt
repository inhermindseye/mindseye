Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Julia Herbert aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Aspinal in Surry Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Ten Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of February 1797 before me} John Collick

her Julia Herbert Mark
