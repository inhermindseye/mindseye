Middx  John Clark aged about 36 years upon Oath saith about two years ago being at that time a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Channing Apothecary in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds for her Years and upwards and hath not since he quitted his sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servt. for one year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Septr. 29th 1750 before}
