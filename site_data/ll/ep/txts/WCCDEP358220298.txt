Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Powell Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Holbert a Cheesemonger in Great Tower Street in the Parish of Saint Dunstans in the East in the City of London for One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service one Year and an half ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 7th. day of Novr. 1771 before me R Butler W Kelynge

Elizth. Powell
