Middx  Ann Bishop aged about 22 Years Destitute of Lodging upon Oath saith she never was Married That she was bound Apprentice by the Officers of Trustees of the the Parish of St. Clement Danes to Mr. Walker Smith in Wych Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx untill she should attain the Twenty one Years or day of Marriage says she served all her time out with her said master in Wych Alley aforesaid which Expired about a Year since That She never kept House or was a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the 11th. day of Augt. 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Bishop
