Middlesex ss}  Catherine Goodman the widow of Peter Goodman deced maketh oath that about fourteen years ago her said late husband took and rented a house at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds in upper EastSmithfield in the Parish of St John Wapping in the said County and resided thereon for the Space of two years and was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the Same That her said husband did not afterwards rent a house lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten pound or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself And that She was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 15. day of May 1788 before  R Davies  Robt Smith

The Mark of Catherine Goodman
