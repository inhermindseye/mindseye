Middx  Martha Briggs aged about 31 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Chaddock in King Street in the Parish of the New Church in Manchester Lancashire at the Yearly wages of fifty Shillings for four Years & upwards and quitted her sd. Service about 7 years ago, since which she has not done any Act whereby to gain a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Octr. 27th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Martha Briggs
