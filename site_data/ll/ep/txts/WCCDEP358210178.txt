Westmr.  Joseph Dunlop aged about 35 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Margaret his present wife in the Parish Church of St. Margaret in the City of Westminster about four Years ago; says that being a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Henry Fox Lane  Esqr . (now Lord Bingley) in Cavendish Square in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middx, for two years at the Yearly Wages of Thirty five Pounds and continued in said Service about one year after his marriage with his first Wife, and after he quitted said Service never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes, says his first Wife has been dead about Six Years, and that from the time of her Death to the time of his Second marriage (being about two Years) he never lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 22nd, day, of August 1767 , before}
