St Clement Danes 30th April 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Ann Westmr.  (to wit) Elizabeth Lawrence  Singlewoman aged forty seven years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Sir Saml Hayes  Leicester Square on the West Side of the said Square in the parish of St Ann within the Liberty of Westminster for two years and upwards at the Annl. Wages of ten Guineas that She left there about Nine years ago & hath not Since Rented any House or Apartm Apartment of ten pounds a year paid parish Taxes lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve month together or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 30th day of April 1793 before us W Addington B: Kennett Elizth Lawrence
