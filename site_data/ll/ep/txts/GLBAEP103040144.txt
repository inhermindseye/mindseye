Middlesex ss.  Eleanor Connor of Half Moon Alley Pettis Lane the Widow of John Connor decd. who was also the Widow of Cornelius Sullivan her first Husband maketh Oath that she was Married to her said first Husband Cornelius Sullivan in Ireland upwards of Fifty Years ago and that she has issue living by him a Son Named John Sullivan who was born in Ireland , that her said Son about Twenty four Years ago was Married by a Roman Catholic Priest at a House in Glass House Yard in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate London to one Elizabeth Hyde now decd. and that he has Four Children living by the said Elizabeth Hyde , namely Eleanor aged about Twenty Years who was born in Irish Court in the Parish of St. Mary Whitechapel in the County of Middlesex , Cornelius aged about Fourteen Years, Elizabeth aged about Nine Year who were both born in Glass House Yard Aforesaid and Hannah aged about Seven Years who was born in King Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Neither of which said Children have done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in their own right, And this Deponent further saith that she verily believes her said Son John Sullivan was never Married to the said Elizabeth Hyde the Mother of the said Children in any other manner than as aforesaid, And that she is informed that the said Eleanor Sullivan is now chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 28 Day of Septr. 1798 . before H: Reynell R Davies

her Eleanor Conner Mark
