Middlesex ss}  Margaret Dennahough the widow Dennis Dennahough deced maketh oath that her sd late husband was by birth an Irishman & never did any Act to gain a settlement in England to her Knowledge or belief That many Years before her marriage with the sd Dennis Dennahough She was lawfully married to one Stephen Robinson who many Years ago rented a house of the yearly Rent of Eight Pounds in Lower Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & Paid Kings Taxes & other Taxes therefore & did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That she this Deponent Since the death of her sd late husband Robinson has not done any Act to gain a settlement for herself Save What She Acquired by Marriage with her sd Second husband Dennahough

Sworn this. 28. day of February 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Margaret Dennahough
