Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Robert Inwood on behalf of Matilda Pollett an Infant aged about 4 years upon his Oath Saith That he hath been Informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said Matilda Pollett is the legitimate Child of John and Mary Pollett

Sworn this 17th day of July 1800 before us} John Collick  Benjm Gee

Robt Inwood
