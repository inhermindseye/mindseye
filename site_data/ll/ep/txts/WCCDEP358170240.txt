Middx  Ann Manfield aged abt. Years upon her Oath saith that about Twelve Months ago she intermarried with her present Husband Jas. Manfield at the Fleet  London & that her said Husband is a Native of Ireland & never Gained a Settlemt. in England that abt. 2 Years ago & before her Intermarriage with the said Jas. Manfeild she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Putland at the Feathers Tavern in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of One Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds
