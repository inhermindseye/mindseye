Saint Clement Danes

Settlemt. D Epping to wit William Wilson aged about 37 years upon his Oath saith that in the year 1780 he Intermarried with Martha his late Wife who has been dead about 8 years at Holy Trinity Church at Hull in Yorkshire by whom he has one Child living vizt. Wm. aged about 13 years That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house at Epping in the Parish of Epping in the County of Essex for about Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of Six pounds That he this Deponent also Rented at the same time a four Stall Stable of a Mr. Tripp who is now deceased which Stable was situate in Thoydon Garden and paid the yearly Rent of four pounds for the same and that since he quitted said house & Stable he this Deponent has not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 12th. day of Novr. 1794 }

Wm Wilson

This Man Ran away from the Beadle before he was Sworn
