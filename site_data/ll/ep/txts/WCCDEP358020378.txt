St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Wesminster} (to wit) Ann Kilner  Widow Aged about forty three years Upon Oath saith that she intermarr was married to her late Husband James Kilner at the parish Church of Chipping Barnet in the County of Herts  about on or about the 30th day of September in the year 1784 that her said Husband at the time of their Marriage and since Rented and Rended in a House in Clements Inn in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County about but what Rent he paid for the same she cannot say And saith that he paid the parish Rates for the same or at breast was Assessed thereto the Officer's having demanded tha same of this Deponent-that he quitted that House abt four years ago and since hath not obtained any no Subsequent legal Settlement that he Died about 3 years & ago and she this Deponent hath gained no Settlement in her own right since his Deed

Sworn the 25th Janry 1792 before us R Butler

Ann Kilner 's Mark
