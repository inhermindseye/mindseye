Westmr  Ann Courser Wife of Thomas Courser upon Oath says that her said Husband has a Child by a former Wife named Margaret aged about Ten Years as she hath been informed by him and verily believes, and says that the said Thomas Courser lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Veal a Glover in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten and Twelve pounds and quitted same about five years ago and hath not since lived in any other service for one Year nor rented Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes as she hath also been informed by her said Husband which Information She verily believes to be true

Sworn the 3d. day of October 1767 before}
