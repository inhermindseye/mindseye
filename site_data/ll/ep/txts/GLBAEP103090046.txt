Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Horrocks of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  single woman Maketh oath That divers Years ago she was hired and lived as a hired servant for a year at Certain Wages with one Thomas Tyson in Queen Street in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And by means thereof as she is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that she hath not since lived as a hired servant for a year or done any Act whereby to gain a subsequent settlement to her knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 11th day of October 1766 before  Chris: Scott

Mary Horrocks Her Mark
