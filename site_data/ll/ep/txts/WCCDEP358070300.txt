Middx & Westr to wit }  Martha Mitchell Aged about Seventy five Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of William Mitchell who has been dead about Six Years ago and with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Paul Shadwell London on the 14th day of Novr 1735 and by whom she has Two Children (now provided for) says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Bennetts Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster about Seven Years and Paid Land Tax due for the same has Quitted the said House about Twelve Years ago and after Quitting the same he never rented House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or any otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement, nor hath this Examinant since her said Husband Death.

Sworn this 23d day of Sepr 1775 before  David Walker

her Martha Mitchell Mark
