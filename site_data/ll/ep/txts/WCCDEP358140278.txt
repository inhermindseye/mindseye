Middx.  Sarah Lambert aged abt. 30. Years upon Oath says she intermarried with Wm. Lambert at May fair Chapel abt. Twelve Years since and has by him One Child named Charlotta aged abt. Nine years and that she has been informed and believes the said William Lambert was articled a Clerk for five years to Mr. William Robinson of the Inner Temple London Gent: and served him as his Clerk about three years of the said Term and lodged upwards of Two Months the Latter part of the said three Years at Mr. Ross's a Taylor in Red lion Street Holborn in the parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middx. And that her said husband never Rented Ten pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to this Examinants knowledge or belief; that her said husband now absconds from her and where to find him she cannot tell

Sworn this 20th. Augst. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Sarah Lambert

Upon Complt. of Mr. Wilkinson Ovr. J: P:
