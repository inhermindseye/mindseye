Middx  The Examination of Elizth. Sparrow  Singlewoman aged about 20 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 19th. Day of March 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that her late Father Edward Sparrow before his Intermarriage with Ann her Mother and whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Lilley Apothecary in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Gard. in the Liberty of Westmr . for the space of Six Months and upwds. and after the sd. Intermarriage continued to live and reside in the same Service & undr. the same agreemt. two Years or thereabts. And further saith that her sd. Father in his life or Ann her Mother since his Death never Rented any House neither hath she this Examint. lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or as an Apprentice or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Samuel Clarke

Eliz Sparrow
