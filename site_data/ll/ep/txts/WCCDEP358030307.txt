Westmr. 

Sarah East aged about 40 Yrs. upon Oath Says she was never married Says she lived a Yearly Servt . for one Year & upwards with Mrs. Gray at the White Swan in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £4 & hath left her sd. Service abt. 5 Yrs. Says she hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or belief.

Sworn Janry 6th. 1746 before  Wm. Jones  Tho. Lane

The Mark of Sarah East .
