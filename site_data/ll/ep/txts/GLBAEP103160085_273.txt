Middlesex ss}  Isabella Smithson the widow of George Smithson deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & Which She believes to be true That he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprentice ship with one Trentham in Books Rents Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Chairmaker & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards to this Deponts knowledge or belief Rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement nor hath she this Deponent Since his death been married or done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 3d day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The mark of Isabella Smithson
