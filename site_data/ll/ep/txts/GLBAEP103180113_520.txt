Middlesex ss}  David Harley maketh oath that Sometime ago he took & rented a house of the yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the Said County & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 21st day of December 1778 before me  Jasper Clarkey

David Harley
