St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Susannah Hayward Aged twenty five years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Chawner in Hart Street in the parish of St George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for one year & three Months at the Annual Wages of Seven Guineas that she quitted that Service about two years ago and since hath not to the best of her knowledge or belief gained any other legal settlement and that she is now Ill Poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn the 14th day of December 1791 before us R Butler D Walker

Sush. Haywards Mark

Witness. Edwd. Burbidge
