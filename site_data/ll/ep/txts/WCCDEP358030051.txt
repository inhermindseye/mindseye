Middx 

Alexr. Marshall upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Judith in Peter Street in the Mint upwards of 12 years since by whom he hath one Child Eliz: aged 9 Yrs. & upwds. Says he Served 8 Yrs. Apprentiship with Rd Shanghton in Swan Yard in the Parish of St. Mary at Newington in the County of Surry  Pack thread Spinner Says he never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or belief

Sworne 28th: Novr. 1745 before W Jones

The Mark of Alexr. Marshall
