Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Marshall  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago her late Father Edward Marshal deced rented a house of Ten pounds a year & upwards in Baileys Place in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & lived therein for many years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain Anothr Settlement That She this Deponent was born in the Sd house & never was bound an apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servt. for a year nor was She ever married nor ever did any any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this day of March 1778 before}

Elizabeth Marshall
