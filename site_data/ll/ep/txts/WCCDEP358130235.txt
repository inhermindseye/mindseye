Middx.  Mary Davis aged abt. Twenty Eight Years upon Widow of Evan Davis upon Oath says She Intermarried with the said Evan Davis at the Fleet Londn. abt. Five Years since, Says her said late Husband has been Dead abt. three Months as She hath been informed and verily believes Says She Cannot give any Account of his Settlement other than that he was a Shropshire Man Says abt. Ten or Elevan Months ago She was removed by an Order or Pass Warrant under the Hands Seals of St. Saml. Gower Knt. & Richd. Ricards Esqr. two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx (one of ye. Quorum) from the Parish of St. Mary White Chapel in the sd. County of Middx. to the Parish of Kennington in the same County the Place of her last legal Settlement which Order was not Appealed against

Sworn Novr. 22d. 1756 before} Tho Lane . Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Davis
