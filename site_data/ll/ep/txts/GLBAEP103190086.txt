Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Whitaker the wife of John Whitaker maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Prince George That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one John Hayward in the Parish of Poole in the County of Dorset  Cabinet maker & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subseqt Settlement That She was lawfully married to her said husband by whom She hath a Child & lawful Issue now living named James aged about Ten Years

Sworn this. 29. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Eleanor Whitaker
