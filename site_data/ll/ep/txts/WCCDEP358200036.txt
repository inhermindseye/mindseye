Middx  Mary Jenkins Aged about 35 Years Wife of of Henry Jenkins gone from her with whom she Intermarried in Kentish Town Chappel about Twelve Years ago Says her said Husband Rented a House in Tooley Street in the Parish of St Olaves Southwark in the County of Surry Seven Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Eleven Guineas and Paid all Taxes and hath quitted said House about Ten Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes.

Sworn the 21st Day of Octr 1763 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Jenkins

Upon Mr Ralph Williams Complaint Ovrs J W
