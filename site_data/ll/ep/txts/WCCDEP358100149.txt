Middlesex to wit John Goff aged about Twenty Six years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Hannah his Wife (gone from him) at the Parish Church of St. St. Clement Danes about three years ago and by whom he hath one Child living namely John aged about two years Says he was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Parish of St. Luke Old Street in the said County of Middlesex to one John Johnstone a Hair Merchant No. 14 St. Johns Lane in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the sd. County of Middlesex & served his said Master under such Indre upwards of three years And untill his said Master Run away And further says he never gained any or. Subsequent Settlemt. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodgings of Ten Pounds a year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 8th. day of Novr. 1783  Wm Kitchiner R Butler

John Goff
