Middx  Susannah Brush aged abt. 54 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thos. Brush who has been dead about Ten Years says her said late husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Guest a Taylor in Surry Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex . and that he Rented a house in Angel Court in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for fourteen Years as her said late husbd. informed her and as she verily believes but what Rent he paid she never heard him say says her said husband after he quitted said house in Angel Court nor she since his death never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. Feby. 1759 . before me} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Susa. Brush
