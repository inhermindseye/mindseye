Middlesex ss}  Sarah Hollyman  Singlewoman maketh oath that her Father Thomas Hollyman (who is now in the Workhouse of the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the Said County as being there legally Settled) has informed this Deponent & which she believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with one Watts a Hand kerchief Printer in the Same Parish & has not Since to this Deponents Knowledge or belief done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this deponent never was bound An Apprentice nor ever lived in a Place as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 10. day of June 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Sarah Hollyman
