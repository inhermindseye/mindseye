Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Esther Lowe aged about 62 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly servant with Mr. Crowther in Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Five years and upwards at the yearly wages of Ten Pounds that she hath quitted said service about four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 17th day of Janry. 1797 } Saml. Edward Esther Lowe
