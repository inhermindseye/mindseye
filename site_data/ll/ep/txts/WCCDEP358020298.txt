St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} (to wit) Rose Hannah Broadhead  Wife of Matthew Broadhead Aged 49 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband at the Old Church Manchester in the County of Lancaster (by Banns) about on 25th Janry.. 1772 . that since, her said Husband Rented and with her resided in a House No. 92 next the George in Little Drury Lane in the parish of St. Mary le Strand in the said County and Liberty for about 5 or Six years at the Annual Wages Rent of £20, that they quitted the same about four Years ago and since her said Husband has not Rented any House or Apartment of Ten pounds a year or done any other Act Matter or thing whereby he might aquire any legal settlement, that her said Husband left her above has not suffered to be outer put her out of the Apartment wherein he lived on since Monday 30th May last and Refused her living with him again and that she is incapable incapable of supporting herself

Sworn the 6th June 1791 before Us  Sampn Wright J Pownall

Rose Hannah} Broadhead 's Mark
