Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Taylor aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Scott at Hammersmith in the Paris Hamlett of Hammersmith in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas hath quitted said Service about Six years ago  since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing where by to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of June 1786  Sampn. Wright M Addington

Sarah Taylor
