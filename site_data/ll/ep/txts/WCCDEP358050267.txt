Middx 

Eliz Glover ats Edwards upon Oath Saith she  was married to Wm. Edwards a Shoemaker about Six months ago at a house in Wild Street by a Person whom she beleives to be a Priest of the Church of Rome that the said Wm. Edwards is at present disordered in his Senses and that she hath been Informed & beleives the said Wm. Edwards Served his Apprentiship with John Morgan a Shoemaker at Portally in the County of Carnaroon and never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place and this Examinant further upon her voluntary Oath saith that she was till the time of her sd. Intermarriage the Wido. of Edward Glover who dyed upwards of 8 Yrs. ago and that about five

Years ago she was delivered of a female bastard Child in the house of Mr Hollomar in Bear Yard in the parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middx which was baptized mary and that the afore sd. Wm. Edwards did beget the sd. female bastard Child on her body this Exami  at that time living with the sd. Edwards as his wife and that about 3 Yrs. ago she was delivered of a male Bastard Child (which was baptized William ) at Mr Hollemans afore sd & that the sd. Wm. Edwards did also beget the sd. male bastard Child on her body they still Cobabiting together as man & wife And that about Six months ago she was delivered of another female bastard Child at Mr Whites in Vear Strt. in the same parish of St Clemt Dane which has been baptized Margt. and that the aforesd. Wm. Edwards did also begat the last mentioned female bastard Child on her body they still living together as man & wife

Sworn June 11th. 1750 before} Tho Lane .

The mark of Eliz Edwards
