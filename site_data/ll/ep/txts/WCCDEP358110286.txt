Middx  Eliz: Clark upon Oath Saith she was married to her husband Richard Clark on or about the 9th. Augt. 1743 at the fleet London and that her sd. husband lived a Yearly Servant with Benjn Paulin  Esqr . of Marsh Gate near Richmond in Surry for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds and quitted his sd. Service in the Month of April 1743 that her sd. husband came to Town with his sd Master on or about the 1st. Febry preceding the time of his Discharge & lay from that time till the 22d. or 23d. of March at the Craven head in Drury Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes where his masters horses Stood & then her sd. husband being taken ill with the Small Poor, was removed to ye Nurses & discharged his masters Service in the following Month as aforesd. that her sd. husband never rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes and that he now absconds from her & has left her with 4 Childn.. Ann aged upwds. of nine Years Eliz aged near 7 Yrs. Rd . aged near 3 Yrs & Joseph aged near one Year Which she is unable to maintain

Sworn Augt. 10th- 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizabeth Clark
