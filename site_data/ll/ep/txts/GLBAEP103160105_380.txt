Middlesex ss  Margery Clarke the widow of Eneas Clarke deced maketh oath that Since the death of her Said late husband She this Deponent rented a house of the yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds in Baileys place in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwds. or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or been Since married or done any Act to gain another Settlement to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 15th day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

Margry Clarke
