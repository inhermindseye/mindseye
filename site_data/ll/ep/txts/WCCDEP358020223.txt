Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Frances Ashly aged 48 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she married to James her present Husband at St. Andrews Holborn about 21 Years Ago that Since their Intermarriage her Husband has not to the best of this Examinant knowledge gained any Parochial Settlement in any Way whatever that her said Husband hath Informed this Examinant that he was bound Apprentice to and duly Served his Apprenticeship with John Bedle a Waterman at Bankside in the parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry and that since quitting his said Master he never had gained any other Settlement. And this Examinant further Saith that her said Husband has deserted her and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 16th. Septembr 1790 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Frances her Mark Ashley
