Middx  Andrew Pratt upon Oath Saith he was born at Duffy in Ireland & never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement to his Knowledge & that he has a wife named Mary & one Child (viz) Ann aged abt. 11 Yrs.

Sworn Jany. 16. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Andw. Pratt
