Westmr- to wit Elizabeth Ryland aged about twenty four Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Joseph Ryland Rented and lived in a House in Holles Street Clare Markett in the Psh of St. Clement Danes for three Years at the Yearly Rent of £22 hath quitted the sd. house about 16 Years ago and after quitting the same he never Rented house paid Taxes or gained any other subseqt- Settlemt- Says that on the 6th. day of March last she was Delivd. of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said parish which hath since been baptized by the name of James and is become chargeable to the said parish and that one Stephen Fish a Servant to the Revd . Seymour Leake at Oxley Hall near Stow Markett Suffolk is the only true and lawfull Father thereof

Sworn this first Day of May 1778 before  Abrm Baley

her Elizth. Ryland Mark
