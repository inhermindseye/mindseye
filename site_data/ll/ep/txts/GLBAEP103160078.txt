Middlesex ss}  Esther Hymns the widow of John Hymns deced maketh oath That she has been informed by John Hensman late Master of the Parish Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & which she believes to be true That her said husband rented a house in the Said Parish & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement & that she this Depont Since his death hath not been married nor done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Esther Hymns
