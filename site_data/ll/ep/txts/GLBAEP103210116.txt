Middlesex ss}  Gerry Mum maketh oath that about fifteen years Ago as he now best remembers he lived in & rented a house at the yearly rent of Six pounds in Seven steps Alley in the Precinct of St Catherine in Middlesex & during such his residence therein he was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same to the then Collector of Such Tax whose name he can't at present recollect & this Deponent lived in such house for abt five years That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten pounds or served any Parish office or paid any poore rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Mary to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 14 day of Novemr 1785 before R Davies Robt. Smith

The Mark of Gerry Mum
