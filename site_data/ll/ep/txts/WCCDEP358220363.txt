Westmr. to wit Elizabeth Broadmeadow Aged about Twenty Nine Year on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Taylor a Silk Mercer on Little Tower Hill in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate London upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Months ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn this 30th. day of June 1772 before us} Tho Lane  Tho. Kynaston

her Elizth. Broadmeadow mark
