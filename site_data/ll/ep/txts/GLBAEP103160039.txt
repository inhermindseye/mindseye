Middlesex ss}  Jane Kincaird the widow of William Kincaird deced maketh oath that many years ago her said late husband rented part of a House in Star Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County & during Such his residence therein was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the Same & by means thereof as she is advised he gained a legal Settlement there That her Said husband did not afterwards Rent a house of the yearly Renter Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 18 day of April 1776 before  Geo C Smith

The Mark of Jane Kincaird
