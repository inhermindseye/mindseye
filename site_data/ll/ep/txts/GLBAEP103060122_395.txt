Middx ss  Jacob May of old Cartridge Street in ye parish of St Mary White Chappell in the County of Middx  Ropemaker Maketh oath that a Child about eleven Years ago was born in the house of Elizabeth Childeroy Situate in high Street in the Hamlet of Mile End New Town in the County of Middx whose Mothers Name was Jane Woolf which Sd. Child was afterwards Baptized by the name of Robert Woolf as this Depont. was has been informed & verily believes.

Sworn this 10th Day of November 1749 before Richd. Ricards Saml Gower

The Mark of Jacob May
