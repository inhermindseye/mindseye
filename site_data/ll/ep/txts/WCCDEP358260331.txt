Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Norther aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Stephenson at the Angel Inn the back of Saint Clements in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years and upwards at the yearly wages of Ten Pounds that She hath quitted said house about Two years and an half since which time she hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Septr. 1793 before me } J Reeves

her Elizabeth Norther Mark
