Middx  Eliz Rotheram aged about 23 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year and that her father Roe Rotheram now lives in and rents a house of the Yearly Rent of £30 in Carey Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Giles Dent Savage (now living near the Hermitage Bridge in Wapping ) Hosier did beget the sd. Child or Children on her body the said Giles Dent Savage having had carnal knowledge of her body in the month of Augt. last and at sevl. other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. Giles Dent Savage and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn May 7th. 1751 before W Yielding

The mark of Eliz Rotheram
