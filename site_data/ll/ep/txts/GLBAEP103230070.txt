Middlesex ss  Ann Neddon the Widow of Henry Neddon deced maketh oath that her Sd lake husband about twenty year ago lived in & rented a house at the yearly rent of fifteen Pounds in Ratcliffe Broad Street in the Hamlet of Ratcliffe in the Parish of Saint Dunston Stepney in the Sd County & resided thereon for the Space of nine Months & did not afterwards rent a house lodging or othr. Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself

Sworn this. 19. day of Septemr 1787 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Ann Neddon
