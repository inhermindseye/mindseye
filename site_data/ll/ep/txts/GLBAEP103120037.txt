Middlesex ss}  Mary Carpenter  singlewoman Maketh oath that divers years ago she was bound Apprentice by Indre to one Hill a Breeches maker of  Long Lane near West Smithfield in the parish of St. Bartholomew the Great in the City of London for the Term of Seven years & duly served three years & half of such her apprenticeship in the Said Parish at Which time her Master died there and by means thereof as she is advised she gained a legal settlement there That she this Deponent never lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever married Nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of March 1773 before}

The Mark of Mary - Carpenter
