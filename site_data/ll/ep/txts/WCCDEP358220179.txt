Middlesex & Westminster }  Jane Chapman Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with the Bishop of Worcester in Upper Brook Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Oath Says that on the Second day of November One Thousand Seven Hundred and Sixty Nine she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Thomas Wilson in Burleigh Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Anthony And that Anthony Coleman late a Servant with Mr: Mylne of Arrundel Street in the Strand is the true and real Father of the said Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid

Sworn this 28th: day of September 1770 before us} Jno: Wright

Jane Chapman

St. Georges Appealed to this Order and the Appeal allowed.
