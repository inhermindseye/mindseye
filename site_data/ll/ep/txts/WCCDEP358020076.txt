Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit, Martha Martin aged fifty Eight Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith, That she never was married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with John Adams of Knights Coffee House in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex is the yearly Wages of eight Pounds for about three years and an half that she quitted his Service about Six or Seven years ago and that since she has to quitted him she has not lived as an hired yearly Servant for one year rented any House or Tenement of ten pounds a year Value, paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to gain any other or Subsequent Settlement to the best of her knowledge Information & belief

Sworn this 27th. May 1788 before  Wm. Achiner

Martha Martin
