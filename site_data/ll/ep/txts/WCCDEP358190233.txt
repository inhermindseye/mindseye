Middx   Mary Brown aged about 37 years Lodging in Clement's Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx upon Oath Says she never was Married That she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Henry Joynes ( Clerk of the work ) in Kensington Palace in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster for about Two Years & Nine Months at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds says she Quitted said Service about Seven years ago and hath not since rented any House paid the Land Tax or Poor Rate or been a hired yearly Servant in any one Place for one year.

Sworn the 11th: day of March 1762 Before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Brown
