Middx  Susa. Buley upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of William Buley with whom she intermarried at the Parish Church of Bromley in Kent upwds. of 14 years ago and that by whom she hath three Children (vizt) John aged about 10 yrs- Jas . aged five Years & Chas . aged about 10 months the two Eldest of which at present lay ill with the small Pore and that her sd. late husband Rented the Hob in the Wall Alehouse in Clements Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes Middx of the Yearly Rent of £20 and lived therein about two Years and that neither her sd. late husband Since he quitted the sd house nor this Examint. Since his Decease have rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn April 22d. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Susa Buley
