Middx  Rachel Broadbridge aged 18 Years upon Oath Saith she is the Daughter of John Broadbridge by Mary his Wife deceased that her sd. father informed her he Served his time with Jno. Jane a Thread Throwster in the parish of St. Martin at Reading in the County of Berks and that she this Examinant rever was married nor ever was an apprentice or yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn the 24th. July 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Rachel Broadbridge
