Middlesex ss}  Margaret Moore  Singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with William Ginder in College Street in the Parish of Saint John the Evangelist in Westmr in the County of Middlesex Book Seller at Certian Wages & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16 day of November 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

Margaret Moore
