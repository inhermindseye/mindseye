Middx  Eliz Baker & Rd Watts severally make Oath. and first this Dept. Eliz Baker for herself Saith she never was married and that on the 8th. of March 1718 she was delivered of a male bastd. Child at Mr Whites Coffee house in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which was baptized Richard and is the identical person now Sworn wth. this Dept. and the sd. Rd Watts for himself maketh Oath that he never was an apprentice or yearly Servt. nor ever Rented ten pounds by the year nor paid Parish taxes and that upwds. of 15 Years ago he was married at the Fleet London to Mary his wife by whom she has 3 Childn. (Viz) Jas . aged abt. 12 Yrs. Jno. aged abt. 9 & Mary abt . 6 Yrs.

Sworn 22d Jany 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell
