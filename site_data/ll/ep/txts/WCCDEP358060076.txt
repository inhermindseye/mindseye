Middx  Eliz Russel aged abt. 35 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she was bound Apprentice by the Church wardens and Overseers of the poor of the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middlx to James Stevens  writing Master till she should attain the Age of 21 Yrs. or marriage & that she Served her sd. Apprentiship with the sd. Mr Stevens in St Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St Clemt Danes aforesd. and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn March 5th. 1750 before  Tho Lane .

The mark of Eliz Russel
