Westmr. to wit Martha Stephenson Aged about Sixty Eight on behalf of William Stephenson her grandson an Infant Aged about Twelve Years son of George & Elizth. Stephenson both deceased on her Oath says George Stephenson the Father of the sd. William Stephenson the Infant Rented and lived in a House known by the Name or sign of the Two Blue Posts near Porters Block West Smith field in the Parish of St. Sepulchre Middlesex for upwards of four Months at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds Eighteen Shillings hath quitted the said House upwards of Eight Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of June 1773 before R. Gusla Sampn Wright

Martha Stephenson
