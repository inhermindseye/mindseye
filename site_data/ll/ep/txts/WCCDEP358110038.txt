Middx  Margt. Sanvaish upon Oath saith She is the Wido. of William Sanvaish with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Bennets Paulswharf London and that her sd. late husband rented a house of Eighteen pounds by the Year in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and lived therein about 8 Yrs. & never after he left the Sd house nor has this Examint. since his Decease Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn April 18th. 1752 before}
