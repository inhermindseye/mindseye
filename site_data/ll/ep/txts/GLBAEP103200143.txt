Middlesex ss}  Sarah Boston the widow of George Boston deced maketh oath that about thirty Six years ago her sd late husband lived in & rented a house of the Yearly rent of twenty Pounds the Corner of Stewards Rents in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the sd County & resided thereon for the Space of Six Months or more & in which house her sd husband died & was buried in the Churchyard belonging to the Parish Church of the sd Parish That this Deponent Since her sd husbands death lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Mr Lyddall in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & afterwards was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Mr Rymer in the Same Place & Continued in Such last mentd Service for the Space of two Years & upwards but that during Such several Services She this Deponent had a Child living who had done no Act to gain a Settlement for himself And that She this Depont Since her sd husbands death hath done no Act to gain a settlement for herself Except the Services before mentioned Can be deemed to have gained her settlement which she is informed they do not

Sworn this. 26. day of June 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Sarah Boston
