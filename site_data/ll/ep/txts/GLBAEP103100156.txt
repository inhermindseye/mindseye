Middlesex ss}  Sarah Wickham the widow of John Wickham deced maketh Oath That divers Years ago and when a widow woman She this Depont. was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year with Daniel Roberts in Red Cross Street in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Victualler and Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upwards and by Means thereof as She is advised gained a legal Settlemt. there And this Depont. Saith that although She hath been Since hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year yet She never Continued in any Such last menconed Services for the Space of a year nor hath She Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And this Depont. farther Saith That about five Months ago a Ceremony or Pretended Ceremony of a Marriage between her & one Thomas Farrell (who lately left her & is gone to Places unknown to her) was Pronounce by a Person (who this Depont. was informed was a) Romish Priest in a Certain house in the Parish of Shoreditch the County of Middlesex which Marriage or Pretended Marriage this Depont. has been Since informed is of nor force or effect in Law And lastly this Depont. Saith that She is now with Child And that the said Thomas Farrell is the true & only Father of the Child where w She now goeth and is Pregnant.

Sworn this 6th day of Octr. 1769 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Sarah Wickham
