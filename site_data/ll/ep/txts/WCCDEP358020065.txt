Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sophia Hallywell aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Froome in Hart Street Bloomsbury in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for 12 Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service upwards of 3 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of April 1788  Sampr Wright N Bond

her Sophia Hallywell Mark
