Middx  Grace Wood aged a upwds. of 19 yrs. upon Oath saith she intermarried with Thomas Wood on the 4th. of July 1749 at the Fleet London and that her sd. Husband informed her he was bound Apprentice to his father Mr. Thomas Wood of Beaufort buildings Wharfinger and has at this time one Year of his sd. Apprentiship to Serve

Sworn Octr. 17th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

Grace Wood
