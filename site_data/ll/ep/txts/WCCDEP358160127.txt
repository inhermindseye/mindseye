Middx  The Examination of Mary the Wife of Richd. Grove Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 1st. Day of April 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that her said Husband Rd. Grove Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Wm. White in the Parish of Chiswick in the same County Fisherman and hath been out of his time 20 Years & upwds. And this Examint. further saith that he hath not since he left his said Master Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That her sd. Husband is now on Board his Majesty's ship ye. Cumberland & yt. she hath one Child (to wit) Mary aged one Year and she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe and Child

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Samuel Clarke

The Mark of Mary Grove
