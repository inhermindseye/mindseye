Middx.  Mary Martin upon Oath says she is the wife of John Martin to whom she was Married at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx abt. Twenty years ago Says her said husband Served 7 years Apprenticeship with Mr. Creamer a Fishmonger by Temple Bar in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes and never Rented Ten pounds a years paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year as her said husband has Often times informed her & which Information she verily believes to be true

Sworn Augst. 16th. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Saund

Mary Martin
