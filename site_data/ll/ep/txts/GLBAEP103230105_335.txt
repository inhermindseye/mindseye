Middlesex ss}  William Horton maketh oath that he can give no Account respecting the Settlement of his late Parents deced or either of them That he this Deponent never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year (Save that about Six years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant for a year with Mr Hatfield in Sunyard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County but during Such his Servitude he was a Married Man That he never rented a house or Lodging of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in his own right and he can give no Account where he was born That he hath a wife named Ann to whom he was lawfully Married

Sworn this. 17. day of Janry 1788 before  Rob: Smith

The Mark of William Horton
