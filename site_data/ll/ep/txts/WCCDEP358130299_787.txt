Middx.  Eliz: Pendril aged abt. 88 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thos. Pendril who hath been dead abt. twenty Years says her said Husband served his Apprenticeship of seven Years with his father in Duke Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. & never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn the day of March 1757. before}
