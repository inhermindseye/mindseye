Middx  Eliz Sale aged about 44 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she hath been Informed and believes her late father John Sale Served his Apprentiship with Michael Green a Joyner in the City of Lincoln and that neither her sd late father nor herself ever rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 5th. 1750 before Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Sale
