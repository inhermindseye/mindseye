Westmr.  Eliz. Cox aged about 20 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she is the Daughter of Thos. & Mary Cox both deceased that she has been informed & believes her sd. late father rented the Horse Shoe Alehouse in Shag Lane in the parish of St James in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Rent of £24 and lived therein upwds. of one year

Sworn June 5d 1752 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Eliz Cox
