Middx  Sarah Hall aged abt. 25 years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she never was married and that she was delivered of a male bastard Child at the house of Mrs. Todd in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes on the 15th. Day of August which was in the Year One thousand Seven hundred and fifty two which was baptized James -& is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same And that James Donnelly a Stationer did beget the said male bastard Child on her body the said James Donnelly having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant in the Month of November one thousand seven hundred & fifty one & at sevl. other times afterwards at one of which times beget her with Child of the sd. male bastard Child of which She was delivered as aforesd. and that the sd. James Donnelly & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn 7th. June 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Hall
