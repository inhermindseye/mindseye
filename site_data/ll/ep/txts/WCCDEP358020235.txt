Middlesex to wit }  Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex maketh Oath and saith that Sarah Walker  Widow of William Walker Servant deceased did this Seventeenth Day of December Instant make Oath that her Child named Elizabeth Mary Ann aged about three Years was born a Bastard in Sherrard Street in the Parish of Saint James within the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex as more particularly appears to this Examinant by the written Examination of the said Sarah Walker taken and sworn this Day before R Butler Esqr. one of His Majestys Justices of the peace for the said County to which she hath subscribed her Name and which Examination was so taken and sworn in the Presence of him this Examinant And saith that the said Bastard Child Elizabeth Mary Ann is now become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn the 18th. Day of December 1790 Philip Dyot D Walker

Benj: Newman
