Westmr.  Clementinah Russell aged about 37 Years, upon Oath says she was never Married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year; says her Father Richard Russell who has been dead about Seven Years Rented a house in Smith's Square in the Parish of St. John the Evangelist in the City of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds lived therein about five Years and quitted same about Twelve Years before his Death and never after Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 11th. day of January 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant  Frans Caryl

Clementina Russell
