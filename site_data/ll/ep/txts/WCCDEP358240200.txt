John Newton & four Children

30th Janry 1793 St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  Children  By Birth of St C D of the Father in St C D  John Newton aged 42 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife Hannah Maria Newton formerly Hannah Maria Taylor Spinster on or about the 4th day of Febry. 1776 (by Banns) that he this Examinant hath 4 Children by his paid Wife now living Namely Sarah born on or about the 26th day of Febry. 1780 Anthony born on or about the tenth day of October 1781 Isaac born on or about the 29th day of May 1784 and Phoebe born on or abt. the Seventeenth day of July 1792 all of whom are unprovided for & not settled otherwise than in Right of this Examinantthat he this Examinant never obtained any legal Settlement in his ever Right and they Examinant further saith that he hath been informed and verily believes he is the Son of John and Mary Newton and saith that he verily believes he was born in lawful Wedlock in the said parish of Saint Clement Danes And saith that he is not intitled to any legal Settlement otherwise than by Birth to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief

Sworn the 30th day of Janry. 1793 before Me } Wm. Kitchiner

John Newton 's Mark
