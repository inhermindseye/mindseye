Eliz Jones aged abt. 22 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes that her father Rd. Jones deceased was born in the Isle of Anglesey and never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England to her Knowledge or belief that her Mother Margt. Jones deceased was a Servant to St. Jno. Bromfield in Featherston Buildings Holborn before marriage (as this Examinant hath heard) but how long or under what agreemt. she knows not and that she can give us further Account of her sd. late mothers Settlemt. Says she (this Examinant was born  opposite Great Turn Stile in Holborn in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx

Sworn Septr. 28th 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Eliz Jones
