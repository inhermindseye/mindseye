Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Gray aged about 22 years upon his Oath saith That he was bound an Apprentice to one Thomas Jones a Taylor in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for Seven years That he served his said Master for Two years or thereabouts that then he quitted his said Master since which time he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of July 1799 before us} Menter G S Goodenough

Wm. Gray
