Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Sacheverell aged about 28 Years Upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 28th, day of February 1766, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Walker-Hair Dresser in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been baptized by the Name of John ; says that John Jakins , Servant to the Secretary of Count Brahl in Piccadilly is the reputed father of the said Male Bastard Child he having had carnal knowledge of her body in or about the Month of June 1765 and at several other times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid And that the said John Jakins and no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn the 13th, day of October 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant

Ann Sacheverell
