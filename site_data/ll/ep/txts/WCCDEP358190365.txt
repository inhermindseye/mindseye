Middx  Martha Mead Aged about 25 Years upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the Sixth Day of June Instant she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. Poole in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath Since been baptized by the Name of Thomas Says One Thomas Little a Waiter at the Anchor and Baptist Head Tavern in Chancery Lane did beget the said Male Bastard Child on her Body she this Examinant and the said Thos. Little liveing together Fellow Servants at the Mitre Alehouse in Chancery Lane aforesaid their became acquainted with her and had Carnal knowledge of her Body two Different times at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn 28th Day of June 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Martha Meed
