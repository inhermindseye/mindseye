Middlesex ss}  Thomas Goding maketh oath That his Father Thomas Gordon Deced Divers Years ago Rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds & upwards in the Parish of Kingston in the County of Surry & was charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same at which time this Depont. was part of his Fathers Family & by means thereof derived a Settlement there from his Said Father And this Deponent Saith That he hath not Since done any act to gain a Settlement for himself elsewhere And that he hath a wife named Mary and two Children to Wit Thomas Aged about Eleven Years and Jane Aged about three Years the Lawful Issue of this Depont. by his Said Wife.

Sworn this 20th Day of May 1772 Before  Cha: Digby

Thomas Goding
