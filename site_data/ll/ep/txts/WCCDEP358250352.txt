Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Bridget Field aged about 38 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Vincent in Hemlock Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for near Two years at the yearly Wages of Four Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Sixteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 12th. day of Febry. 1798 before me} J Hunter

her Bridget Field Mark
