Middlesex to wit Ann Ratcliff Aged about Twenty Three Years On her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Manning a Honer near Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of one and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further on her Oath Says that on the fourteenth Day of July last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Saint Bartholomews Hospital London which hath since been baptized by the Name of Mary Ann and is likely to become Chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same And that one Wm. Manning Junr . did beget such Bastard Child on her body he having had Carnal knowledge thereof same time in the Month of September last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at several times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered and that the said William Manning and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of Augt. 1779 before R Butler

Ann Ratcliffe
