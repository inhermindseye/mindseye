Middlesex ss}  Amelia Smith the wife of John Smith Labourer maketh oath That she well knew one Ann Standart late of Star Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex deced & was Acquainted with her for about the Space of Six Years next before her death which happened Sometime in the Month of March last That She the Said Ann Standart at the time of her death left behind her a Child named Elizabeth Standard Still living Aged as this Deponent believes upwards of Seven Years The which Child as she this Deponent has been informed often times by the Said Ann Standart & believes the same to be true was born of the Body of her the Said Ann Standart in the Publick house Known by the Sign of the King George the Thirds Head Ale house near the Blue Boar Inn in Holborn within the Liberty above the Barrs in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex where She the said Ann Standart then lived as a Lodger and that Such Child was a Bastard & illegitmate she the sd Ann Standart never having been married & that one Squire Brown was the Father of the said Child And this Deponent Farther Saith that the Said Ann Standart the said Child's Mother Whilst in her Sickness & Some Short time before her death informed this Deponent & which she believes to be true That the Said Child had been formerly placed in the Workhouse belonging to the Said Liberty & been relieved by the Overseers of the Poor there as being legally Settled in the sd Liberty by means of Such her Birth as aforesd And this deponent can't Set forth whether the Said Child was did any Act to gain a Settlement in its own right.

Sworn this 28th. day of October 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith  Wm Blackmore

The Mark of Amelia Smith
