Middlesex ss}  Mary Arondall the wife of John Arondall maketh oath that her said husband is at present disordered in his Mind That in the year 1777 this Deponents sd. husband lived in & rented a house of the yearly Rent of Eight Pounds in Red Maid Lane in the Parish of St George in the said County & during such his Residence therein he was charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same That he has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement & that she was lawfully married to her said husband.

Sworn this 25 day of Febry 1780 before  Jasper Clarke W Blackmore

Mary Arrondall
