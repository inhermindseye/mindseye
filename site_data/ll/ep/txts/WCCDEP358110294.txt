Middx  Hannh. Adams upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten Pounds a year or pd. Parish Taxes And that she has been informed & believes her father Tho. Adams decd. Served his Apprentiship with a Sawyer in Bridewall Precinct London and never rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Augt. 16th. 1753 before}

Hannah Adams
