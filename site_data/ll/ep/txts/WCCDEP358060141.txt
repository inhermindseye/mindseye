Westmr.  Mary Grundy aged about thirty one Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she Lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Cole in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Two pounds ten Shillings and hath Left her said Service about 120 Years since which she hath not rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 17th. 1751 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Mary Grundy
