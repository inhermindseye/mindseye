Middlesex ss.  Edward Robinson maketh Oath that about Two Years ago he Rented a House of Twelve Pounds a Year in Nightingale Lane  in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and lived in the same about Eleven Weeks But paid One Quarters Rent for the said House, that about Six Months after he quitted the said House he took a House of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds in the New Buildings at the back of Bethnal Green Mad House in the Parish of St.Matthew Bethnal Green in the said County and lived in the same about One Quarter of a Year, when he quitted the said House without paying either Rent or Taxes, that he has not since he quitted the said last mentioned House, Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of 1794 before}
