Westmr.  Robt. Anderson upon Oath saith he hath for several Years past lived in and rented and still Continues to live in and Rent a house of the Yearly Rent of Forty three Pounds in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . Says he has a Son named Robert aged about 15 Years

Sworn 20th. Novr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

Robert Anderson
