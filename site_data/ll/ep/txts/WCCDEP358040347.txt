Middx & Westmr.}  Sarah Oakley  Wido . of Thomas Oakeley upon Oath says she Intermarried with the said Thomas Oakley at the Fleet London upwards of Twenty Years ago Says her sd. late husband has been dead abt. two Years and that her late husbands father William Edwd. Oakley lived in and rented the Turks head Punch house in Rose Street Newgate market in the Parish of St. Faith under St. Pauls London of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwds. for several Years and there dyed Says her sd. late husband never rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she has not since her sd. husbands death rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 1st. 1749 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Sarah Oakley
