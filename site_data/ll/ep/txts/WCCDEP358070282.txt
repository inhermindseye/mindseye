Middlesex to wit Mary Hussey aged upwards of Sixty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Daniel Hewitt who Kept a boarding School at Bromley near Bow in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Twenty five Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year or orwise. Gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st. Day of July 1775 - before  Tho Lane  Sampn Wright

her Mary Hussey Mark
