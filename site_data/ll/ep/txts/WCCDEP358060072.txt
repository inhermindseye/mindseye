Middx  Eliz Smith  Wido . of John Smith who dyed about 6 months since upon Oath saith she intermarried with the said John Smith at Clerkenwell Church abt. 10 Years ago by whom she hath two Children Benjn . aged abt. 3 Years and Jose aged abt. one Year Says her sd. late husband was born in Horseshoe Court in St Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlx  & he learned the Trade of his father who lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £10 and there dyed. and her sd. late husband continued to live in the said house sometime after the Decease of his father Says neither her sd. late husband after he left Horeshoe Court or this Examint. since his Decease never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Febry 20th. 1750 before  Tho Lane .

The mark of Eliz Smith
