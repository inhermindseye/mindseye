Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Hurst aged about 63 years upon his Oath saith that upwards of 30 years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Anne's  John That he this Deponent Rented of a Mr. Brett in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Six years and upwards Two Unfurnished Rooms for which he paid the Weekly Rent of Five Shillings that he hath quitted said Apartments about Nineteen years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of march 1796 } J Reeves

his John Hurst Mark

Witness B Newman
