Middx  Rebecca George  Wido . upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with her late Husband George on or about the 9th. Day of April 1724 at the Parish Church of St. Bottolph without Aldersgate the Says her sd. late Husband hath been Dead upwards of Seven Years & that about 13 Years since he lived in and rented a house in Arundell Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of 3 Months and upwards at the Yearly Rent of £40 that since he left sd. House neither the sd. George in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death ever Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 14th. 1744 before}
