Westmr  Dorothy Simpson Wife of Cornelius Simpson upon Oath Says she Intermarryed with the sd. Cornelius Simpson at the Fleet London upwds. of Nineteen Years ago Says the sd. Cornelius Simpson Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. John Scott a Gunsmith in Red Cross Yard in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate London Says her said husband never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 11th. 1747 before}

The Mark of Dorothy Simpson
