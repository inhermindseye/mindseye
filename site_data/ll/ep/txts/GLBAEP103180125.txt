Middlesex ss}  Thomas Pritchard maketh oath That his late Father Thomas Pritchard deced in his life time Rented a house in Cambridge Heath in the Parish of Saint John Hackney in the Said County & Served the office of Headborough in the Said Parish & did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That he this Deponent was born in the Said Parish & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever rented a house or other Tenement of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt for himself

Sworn this. 25. day of Janry 1779 before  Jasper Clarkey P. Rowlands

Thomas Pritchard
