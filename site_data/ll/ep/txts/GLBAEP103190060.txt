Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Wyatt the wife of William Wyatt who is now absent from her at Sea in the Kings Service maketh oath That he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly Served his Apprenticeship with one Thomas Coleman a Carpenter in the Town of Reading in the County of Berks & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She was lawfully Married to her sd husband by whom She hath a Child & lawful Issue now living named Sarah aged about Eleven Years

Sworn this. 21. day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Wyatt
