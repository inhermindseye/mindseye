Middx  Eliz: Grieve aged abt. 35 Years upon Oath says she never was Married or Ever Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for One Year says her late Father Isaac Grieve deceased informed her and which Information she Verily Believes to be true that before his Intermarriage with Ann this Examinants Mother he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Henley a Coal Merchant at Essex Stairs in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for several Years says her late father also informed her  that he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 30th. Decr 1758 before me} Barth: Hammond

Eliz Grieve
