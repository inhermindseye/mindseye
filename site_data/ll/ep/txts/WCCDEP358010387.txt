St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit ) Hannah Gibbons Aged forty two years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith she intermarried with Matthew John Gibbons her late Husband abt. Six years ago in the parish Church of St Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex by whom she had one female Child who Died abt. five years since after living only abt. Six Weeks that her said Husband Died abt. four years since having no Issue surviving him that since her said Husband Death she lived as an hird yearly Servt .-with-Crofts Esquire No. 39 King Street Covent Garden about One year and Ten Months at the yearly Wages Eleven Guineas-that she quitted  said Services in the Month of August 1789 since which time she has not done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn the 14th. February 1791 . before us B: Kennett M Addington

her Hannah Gibbons Mark
