Middx to wit Rachael Middlemost Aged about Twenty two years on her Oath says she is the Wife of James Middlemost (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint James in the beginning of the year 1779 and by whom she hath one Child now Living namely John aged about Nineteen Months says her said Husband served seven years Apprenticeship to One Mr. Crispin a Silver Smith  the Corner of Panton Street Haymarket in the Parish of St. Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and after He was out of his said apprenticeship he never gained any other settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 17th. day of July 1781 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Rachel Middlemost
