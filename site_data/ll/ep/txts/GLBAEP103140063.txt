Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Palmer  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Yearly wages with one Mr Grantham in the Parish of West Thurrock in the County of Essex and Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that She this Deponent hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor to her Knowledge or belief hath She done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 14th day of Novemr 1774 before B Camper

The Mark of Elizth. Palmer
