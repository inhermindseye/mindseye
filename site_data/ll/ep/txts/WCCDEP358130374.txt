Middx  Mary Fielding aged abt. 57 yrs. on the behalf of Christopher Baggs an Infant aged abt. six years upon Oath says that Joseph Baggs the father of the sd. Infant is in the New Goal Southwark & that the Mother of the sd. Infant is dead says the sd. Joseph Baggs lived in & Rented a house in Kings Court in little Russell Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for three years or thereabouts & hath quitted same abt. Ten Months

Sworn the day of 1757 before}
