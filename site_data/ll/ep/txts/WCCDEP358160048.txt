Middx  The Examination of Jno. Ash Lodging in Bear Yard in ye. Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 8th. Day of May 1739 Who upon his Oath saith that upwds. of Eighteen Years ago he Rented a House in Hoxton in the Parish of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds & Eleven Shillings and lived in the same for the Space of Ten Years & upwds. And further upon his Oath saith yt. he hath not since yt. time Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Samuel Clarke  Jas: Gronous

John Ash
