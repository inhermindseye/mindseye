The Exaicon of Mary Newby  Singlewoman liveing in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 19th. day of Augt. 1740 who upon her Oath Saith that she is a Singlewoman and was never Marryed, and that abt. 14 years ago she lived a yearly hired Servt . with Mr. Eddows Perukemaker in Arrundell Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of two years upwds. at the yearly Wages of five pounds and further Saith that since she left her sd. Service she never rented Ten pounds a year (save & Except an Appartmt. wch this Exaiant now Rents of Mr. Reach at ye Lamb Inn consisting of a Shop & back room of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds) paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife And the Exaiant further Saith that on the 24th. of Decr. 1727 she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Kings Head Court in ye sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes wch hath been Baptized Cutbirth & is now liveing and that Robt. Maddison who was there puted father is long since Dead And further upon her Oath Saith that on the 4th. of Febry 1738/9 this Exaiant was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Appartmt. she now lives in

wch hath since been baptized Anne and is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being poor & not able to maintain the same and that one Lockland Bunn did begett the sd. Child on her Body she this Exaiant at such time liveing with the sd. Lockland Bunn as his wife and that he the sd. Lockland Bunn had Carnall knowledge of her Body sevll. times at one of wch times he got her with the sd. Female Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesd. and that he the sd. Lockland Bunn and no other person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworn the day & yeare above named before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Mary Newby
