Middlesex(ss)  T passd. to St. Giles in the fields  Sarah Wilkins aged between fifteen and Sixteen Years on her Oath Saith That the never was married that she was bound Apprentice be Indentures duly executed to Mr. Thomas of Queen Street Lincolns Inn Fields in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex about ten Months ago, That she served as such Apprentice to the said Mrs. Thomas in Queen Street aforesaid for about five or Six Months, that she quitted her said Mistress about four Months ago, and since that time has been  with her Sister Rachael Williams in Haughton Street Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 12th. Janury 1785  before me R. Taylor G Mercer

Sarah Wilkins
