Elizabeth Man  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago her late mother Hannah Man deced & after the death of this Depont late Father rented a house of Ten pounds a year & upwards in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd county & was charged to & paid poors Rates for the Same & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr. Settlement That She this Depont lived in the Sd house with her Sd Mother as part of her family. That She was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 31 day of January 1777 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Elizabeth Man
