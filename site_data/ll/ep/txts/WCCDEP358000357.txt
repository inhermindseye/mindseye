Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Charles Mayers aged about 66 years upon his Oath saith that he served Seven years Apprenticeship with one Richard Wharfe a Breeches Maker  who lived in Hemlock Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a Year Paid Taxes or ever lived as a hired Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn this 20th. day of Septr. 1786 Butler

Charles Mayer
