Middx  Humphrey Limber Aged about Fifty Six Years upon Oath Says he Rented a House in Bennetts Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Five Years at the Yearly Rent of Eight Guineas and paid the Land Tax hath quitted said House about Years & hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the Day of 176 Before}
