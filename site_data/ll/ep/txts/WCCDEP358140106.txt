Middx.  Margt. Cardiff aged abt. 34 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Patrick Cardiff with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Michael in the City of Dublin about Eighteen Years ago and by whom she hath two Children (namely) Thos. aged upwards of four Years & Robt. aged about five Months says her said husband Rented a house in Bennets Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds lived therein abt. four Years & quitted same abt. Six Years & hath not since Rented Taxes

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1758 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Margt. Cardiff
