Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Robert Hughes Aged about 61 years upon his Oath Saith That about 33 years ago he intermarried with Martha his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Botolph Aldersgate That he rented and lived in a house in Russell Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for 18 years at the yearly rent of 22£ That he hath left the same about four Months since which time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of 10£ per Annum or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th day of October 1800 before us } Saml Edwards  John Collick

Robt Hughes
