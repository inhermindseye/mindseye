Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann King aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Nineteenth day of May 1793 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the house of a Mr. Quin in Kings head Court Little Russel Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the name of James Basset and that the said Child is now Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes

Sworn this 10th. day of July 1798 before us N Bond W Carter

her

her Ann King mark

Witness B Newman
