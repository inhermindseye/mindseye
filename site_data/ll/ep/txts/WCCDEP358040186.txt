Westmr.  Eliz. Pearse aged abt. 57 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwds. of 27 Yrs. ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Burges in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for two Years or thereabouts and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 12th. May 1748 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Eliz Pearse
