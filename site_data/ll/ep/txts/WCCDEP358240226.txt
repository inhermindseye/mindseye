St Clement Danes 6th March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Son settled in St George Middx  St Botolph witht Aldgate  (to wit) William Robson Robertson aged forty years and upwards Widower Upon Oath saith that he intermarried with his first Wife Ann at the little Trinity Church Minories by Banns about 18 years ago that she Died about Seven years ago that he hath a Son now living by that Wife and bound out an Apprentice to Mr Rippon Undertaker Corner of Cannon Street  Ratcliffe Highway  St George's in the East and now legally settled in that parish that he this Examinant hath since been Married to Jane his second Wife who is also Dead by whom he had no Children And this Examinant further saith that he Rented and resided in an House facing the Sign of Sir John Falstaff in Houndsditch in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate London for three years or upwards at the Annual Rent of £10 or upwards that he quitted that House about Eleven years ago and hath not Since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any subsequent legal Settlemt to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief that he is Poor Lame and unable to maintain himself without Assistance

Sworn the Sworn the 6th day of March 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

Willm. Robertson
