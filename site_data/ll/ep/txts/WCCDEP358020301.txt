St Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster (to wit) Jane Butler Aged 48 Years or thereabouts  Upon her Oath Saith That she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly sevt with Mr Moggride a Taylor No. 16 Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid about One year and Nine Months at the Annual Wages of five pounds that she quitted that Service about 14 or 15 years ago and since hath not lived as an hired Yearly sevt. for one Year together under one hiring Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby she this Deponent might obtain or become in tilled unto any legal Settlement And that she is Poor & incapable of providing for herself

Sworn before me the 15th June 1791  Wm. Hitchiner

Jane Butler
