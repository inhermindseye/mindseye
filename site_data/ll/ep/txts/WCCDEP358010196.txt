Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Baxter aged about 40 years upon his Oath saith that about he has an illegitimate Child living by his late Wife Jane vizt. Margarett aged about 7 years born a Bastard at No. 8 Crown Court Whites Yard Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary White Chapel alias Matfellon in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 14th. day of Febry. 1788  Sampr. Wright Maddington

James Baxter
