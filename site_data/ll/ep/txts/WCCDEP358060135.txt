Middx  John Bull upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Alice Bull at the Fleet London abt. 15 Years ago by whom he hath one Child John aged about 11 Years Says he was bound an Apprentice when abt. the age of 9 Years of age to his uncle John Bull a Taylor till he should attain the age of 21 Yrs. and lived as an Apprentice under that Contract abt. 10 Yrs. as Swafham in Norfolk & then run away from his sd. Uncle & never was assigned over to any pay on rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn July 5th. 1751 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of John Bull
