Wm Hart Aged about Fifty One Years Upon Oath Says he was Bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr Sylvester a poulterer in Bishopgate Street in the parish of St Botolph Bishopgate London with whom he Served the first five Years of his Apprentiship Says he Served the last two Years of his said Apprentiship with Mr Vesey a poulterer in New Bond Street in the parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster & hath been out of his said Apprentiship upwards of twenty Years Says he never Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 6th of June 1764 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Benjamin Cox

William Hart

Upon Mr Moors Complt Ovr. J. W
