Middx & Westmr}  Ann Langridge aged about forty two Years on her Oath says she Intermarried with Edward Langridge her husband (gone from her) at the Parish Church of St- Dunstan in the West London the 15th- day of May in the Year 1768 and by whom she hath two Children now living Namely Sarah aged about eight Years and Elizabeth aged about two Years Says her said husband Rented and lived in a House known by the Sign of the Ship in Lombard Street in the Precinct of White Fryers London of the Yearly Rent of £23 and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the sd- House about five Years ago since which time he hath gained no other subseqt- Settlement either by Renting house or Tenemt- of £10 a Year or orwise howsoever

Sworn this 4th- Day of Octr- 1777 R Butler Jno. Barnfather

her Ann Langridge Mark
