Middx  Mary Fairchild aged upwds. of 26 Yrs. Voluntarily maketh Oath that she never was married & That on the Twenty Seventh Day of October last she was delivered of a male bastard at the Charge of the Inhabitants of the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx & Liberty of Westmr in the House of Nurse Moore in Old Boswel Court in the said parish which has been baptized Richard and that James Farsens Lighterman did beget the said Male bastard Child on her body the said James Farsens having had carnal Knowledge of this Examinant in December last and at Sevl. other times Since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said male bastard Child of which She was delivered as afsd. & that the sd. James Farsens & no other person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn 6th. Novr. 1752 before } Fran. Bedwell
