Saint Clement Danes 9th Debr 86

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Benjn. Wingrove  Master of the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes Maketh Oath & Saith that he has been informed which Information he believes to be true that one Sarah Holmes is aged abo 20 years that She was bound an apprentice by the Parish Officers of Saint Sepulchres to a Mr. King who kept a Cloaths Shop on Saffron Hill in that part of the Parish of Saint and Holborn which  in the Liberty of Saffron that after She had served about 4 years of her Sai Apprenticeship her Master broke and that the said Sara Holmes has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Extr. Knowledge Information or belief Hill Hatton. Garden and Ely Rents.

Sworn this 9th. day of Decbr. 1786 R Butler Wm Litchiner

Benjn. Wingrove

This Woman was passed to Saint Sepulchres
