Middx  John Marshall Aged about Twenty Eight Years Upon Oath Says he never Rented a House Paid Taxes was An Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says his Father Thomas Marshall Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Brooks a Water Guilder in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says his said Father never Rented House Paid Taxes or was a hired Yearly Servant for One Year as this Examinant hath been informed and which Information this Examinant. Believes to be true.

Sworn the 24th Day of September 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of John Marshall
