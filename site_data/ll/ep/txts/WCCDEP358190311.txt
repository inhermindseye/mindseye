Middx  Wm. Pullen Upon Oath Says he is a Native of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England that he Rented a Farm in the Parish of Clendalken in ye sd. Kingdom of Ireland of the Yearly Rent of Ninety Pounds lived in the same abt. two Years & hath Quitted said farm upwards of One Year or hath a Wife named Elizth . & one Child named Wm. Aged abt. Seventeen Months

Sworn the 11th. day of Decr. 1762  before}
