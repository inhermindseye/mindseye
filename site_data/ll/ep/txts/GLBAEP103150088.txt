Middlesex ss}  Sarah Patrick the Widow of Thomas Patrick deced maketh oath That about Nine Years ago She Rented a house in Brown Bear Alley in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to and paid the Kings Tax for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & that she this Deponent has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Sarah Patrick
