Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Lee of No. 11 Vere Street on behalf of Catherine Sankey who his ill maketh Oath and Saith that she has been informed which Information she believes to be true that the said Catherine Sankey Rented and lived in a House in Plummers Court in the parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards and paid Taxes for the same and that after she quitted said house she never did any Act Matter or Thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th day of June 1795  G Goodenough N Bond

her Jane Lee Mark
