Westmr.  Hugh Phillips upon oath saith he Intermarried with his Elizabeth at the Parish Church of St. Brides London upwds. of 25 Years ago Says he lived in and Rented a house in Crown Court in Butcher Row in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds and upwards fourteen Shillings for One Year or thereabts. & left the same about 7 Years ago Says he has not since he left the sd. house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or belief Says he had Daughter by his sd. Wife Viz: Sarah aged upwds. of 19 Years who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn 14th Novr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Hugh Phillips
