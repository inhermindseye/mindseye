Westmr.  Sarah Tyler aged abt. 42 Years Upon Oath says, she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Mary Higgins at the St. Lukes head Alehouse in Mercer Street Long A in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds quitted said Service about Twenty four Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 31st. Decr. 1766 before.} Tho Lane  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Tyler
