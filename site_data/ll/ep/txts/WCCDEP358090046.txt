Middx  Sarah Woodward aged about Forty Nine Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Robert Woodward who now works at Mr. Lays Wharf in Milford Lane with whom she Intermarried at Saint Sepulchre London about Twenty Seven Years ago and by whom she hath three Children all of whom are now provided for says that her said Husband never Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year says his Father John Woodward Rented and lived in a House at appleby in the County of Stafford for several Years at the Yearly Rent of and where her said Husband was born as her said Husband has Informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1779 before R Butler

her Sarah Woodward Mark
