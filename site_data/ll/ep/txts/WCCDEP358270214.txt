Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Charlotte Price aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born at Wantage in Berkshire where this Deponents Father Kept house And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith That on the 9th. day of November Instant she this Deponent was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Black in Browns Building in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been half Baptized by the Name of Mary That one Jarvis Hall who lives in Duke Street Lincoln Inn fields is the only Real and true father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body for these Three years last part and begotten the same thereon and that the said Jarvis Hall and no other person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 29th- day of Novr. 1794 .} J: Hind

Charlotte Price

Man taken

Paid £20, Release Exted the 3d. day of Decr. 1794

Child named Mary Jarvis Hall Sent to Enfield the 17th. Decr. 1794
