Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Wm. Freeman of Nags head Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes on behalf of Magdalen Galley who lays ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which information he believes to be true that the said Magdalen Galley lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Geo: Swindley of Mags Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the County of Middx for 2 years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of £7 that she hath quitted said Service about 8 years since which time she the said Magdalen hath done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of March 1787 ampr Wright Maddington Wm. Freeman
