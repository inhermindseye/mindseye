St Clement Danes 23 Janry. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St John's Gloucester  (to wit) Eleanor Taylor  singlewoman aged Fifty six years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she Rented and Resided in an House in the parish of St John in the City of Gloucester for upwards of 3 months at the Annual Rent of £7 and paid the Poor and other Rates for the same that she quitted that House abouts 8 years ago and hath not Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelvemonth together or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponent's knowledge or belief

Sworn the 23d day of January 1793 before us } Wm. Kitchiner D Walker

Eleanor Tyler

Not removed being discharged at her own request to go to her Sister at Reading but was afterwards passed to St. C D as a Vagrant and then a fresh Examination taken & the Woman removed the St. John's  Gloucester Vide Entry page 282 this Book
