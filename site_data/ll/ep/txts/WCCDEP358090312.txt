Middx to Wit Bridgett Field Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Vincent at the Punch Bowl in Hemlock Court in the parish of St. Clements Danes in the liberty of Westminster for Sixteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds Hath quitted her said service about Three Years ago since which time she hath gained no Other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further on her Oath says that on the 13th. day January last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the said parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the Name of Ann and is now Chargeable to the said Parish And further that One Wm. Stent a Shoemaker did beget such Bastard Child on her body sometime in April 1781 and at Sevl. Different times afterwards at One of Which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of Which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Wm. Stent & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & True father thereof.

Sworn this 22d. day of May 1782 Before  Sampn Wright R Butler

her Bridgett Field Mark
