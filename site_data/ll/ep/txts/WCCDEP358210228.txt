Westmr.  Mary Cragg aged about 32 Years upon Oath says She is the Wife of Samuel Cragg with whom She intermarried at Mayfair Chapel about fifteen Years ago, says her said husband hath often times informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Adlard a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster , for two years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds, Diet Washing and Lodging, Quitted said Service about two Months before his intermarriage with this Examinant, and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn the 9th, day of January 1768 , before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Carige
