Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Wm. Williams aged about 40 years upon his Oath saith that about 17 years ago he Intermarried with Sarah his late Wife (who has been dead about five Months) at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom he hath Six Children living Vizt. Josiah Aged about 14 years Ann aged about Ten years Sarah aged about 9 years Mary aged about 7 years Benjamin aged about five years and Elizabeth aged about Seventeen Months That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in White horse Yard in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about Three years at the yearly Rent of Fourteen Guineas and paid Taxes due for the same that he hath not quitted said house about Six years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds paid any Taxes whatever or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3rd. day of Janry. 1797 G S Goodenough W William
