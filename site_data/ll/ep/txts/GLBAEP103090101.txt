Middlesex ss  Ann Holmes the widow of Edmund Holmes deced maketh Oath That her said late husband has informed her (and which information this Depont. believes to be true) That divers years ago he was duly bound to & served the whole of his Apprenticeship with William Letts in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex  Ginger Bread Baker and that by Means thereof he gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge information or belief her sd husband Never rented a house of the yearly Value of Ten Pounds Nor ever served any Parish offices Nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship And that She hath not done any Act since his Death to Acquire a settlement in her own right And this Depont. hath a Child named Frances -aged about Ten Years now in the Workhouse belonging to the sd Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her said husband.

Sworn this 18th. day of March 1768 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Ann Holmes her Mark
