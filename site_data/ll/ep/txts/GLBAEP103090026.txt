Middlesex ss  Ann Crafts  widow of John Crafts deced Maketh oath that she never was informed Nor Can set forth the Place of settlement of her said late deced husbands Parents or of either of them And this Depont. Saith that her said husband has informed her that he was born in Great hermitage Street in the Parish of St. George in the County of Middlesex which information she believes to be true And this Depont. farther Saith that she doth not know nor was ever informed Whether her said late husband was ever bound an apprentice or ever lived as a hired servant for a year Or whether he ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or ever served any Parish offices or ever Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes Or whether he ever did any Act to gain a settlement in his own right And lastly this Deponent Saith that she was Married to her said late husband about Eighteen Years Ago And that since his death she hash not done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right.

Sworn this day of February 1766 before}
