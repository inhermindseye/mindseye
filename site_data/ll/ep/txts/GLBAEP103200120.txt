Middlesex ss}  Ann Weatherhead the wife of Thomas Weatherhead maketh oath that She has been informed by her sd husband & which She believes to be true that divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with a Certain Gentle woman (whose name She has forgot) in the Parish of Chelsea in the County of Middx at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of two years & has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement to this deponts Knowledge or belief & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband in Shoreditch Church about three years & three months ago.

Sworn this. 25th. day of March 1784 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Ann Weatherhead
