Middx  Jane Broughton the Wife of Thomas Broughton (who is gone for a Soldier ) maketh Oath and Saith that about Eighteen Years ago her said Husband Thomas Broughton was Bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven Years to Mr . Edward Hilliard of Leadenhall Street in the parish of St. Peter in Cornhill in the City of London Sieve maker and served his said Master there about five Years and then he Sued out his Indentures as She hath heared and verily believes And has gained no Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge And that She hath two Children Thomas Aged about five Years the lawfull Issue of her said Husband and Barbara, Wall , Aged about Eleven Years which her said Husband had by his late Ann deceased

Sworn this 25th. day of February 1745 Before Robert Dennett

her Jane Broughton Mark
