Middx  Joannah Sharp  Widow of John Sharp Aged about Forty Years Upon Oath Says she Intermarryed with her said Husband in the Parish Church of St: Katherine Coleman in the City of London about Seven Years ago Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with a Plumber in Water Lane in the Parish of Allhallows Barkin in the City of London hath been out of his said Apprenticeship about Twenty Seven Years and Never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year Says she hath not Since his Death Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year.

Sworn the 7th Day of July 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell  Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Joannah Sharp
