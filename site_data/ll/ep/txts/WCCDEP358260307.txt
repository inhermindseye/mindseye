Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Lane aged about 46 years upon her oath saith that about 28 years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Lane her late Husband who has been dead about Eight years at the Parish Church of Saint James Westmr . that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Milk Court Denzel Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas that after he quitted said House he never rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Augt. 1798 before me } W Carter

her Mary Lane Mark
