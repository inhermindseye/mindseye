St.. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Catherine Smith Aged about 50 years upon (on the behalf of Patrick King an Infant of the Age of Nine Eight Months & upwards she has been left at this Deponents by his Mother about 4 Months ago) Upon her Oath saith that the said Patrick King is the Son of Patrick King ) and Mary his Wife that she hath been Informed and verily believes to be true that the said Patrick King & Mary his Wife were Married at the Town of Carlisle about 3 Years ago that the said Patrick Rented and with his said Wife Resided in an House called the three Pipes in Dacre Street in the parish of St Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr. and Co. of Middx from the 16th February 1790 to the thirteenth of May then next following, when they quitted and the same & shortly after (before the expiration of the same Month) the said Patrick King the Father went abroad and that Since the said Mary hath not done any Act or thing whereby she might have gained any other legal settlement and this Deponent believes the said Mary King is now at Carlisle and saith that she the said Mary King left the said Child with this Deponent without any Recompence or allow Allowance for supporting the same about four Months ago

Sworn the 18th May 1791 before us  Philip Dyot D Walker

C Smith
