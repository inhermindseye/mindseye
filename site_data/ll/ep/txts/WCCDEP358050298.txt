Westr.  Michael West passed with Martha his Wife and their Child Michael aged between two and three Years from the Parish of St. Olave South wark by a pass Dated the 20th. Instant upon Oath saith he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to his father Robert West Brickley and Served his sd. Apprentiship with his sd. father in Rain Deer Yard Vear Street & Blackmoor Street all with  the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says he never Rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year

or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 22d 1750 before} Hran: Bedwell

Michael West
