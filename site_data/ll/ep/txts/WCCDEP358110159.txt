Middx  Eleanr Hall aged about 20 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that her late father lived in and rented a house in Highgate in the Parish of Pancras in the County of Middx . for several years and there dyed & that she was born at Highgate afsd. and never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant nor ever Rented ten Pounds a Year or pd. Parish taxes

Sworn Jan 31st. 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eleanr Hall
