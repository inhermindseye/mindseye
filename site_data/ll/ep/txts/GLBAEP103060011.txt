Middx  Robert Evans Maketh Oath that about fourteen years ago he was bound Appr by Indenture for Seven years to Mr  Lewis Hughes in Dukes places in the parish of St James Dukes place in the Liberty of the City of London with Whom he Served about Six Years and an half of his sd Apprentiship and the remaind in the sd parish and the Remaindr he was at Sea And this Dept Saith he hath Gained no Subsequent Settlement to his knowledge And that he hath a wife Named Ann

Sworn this 2d. May 1743 before Robert Dennett Rd. Ricards

Robt Evans
