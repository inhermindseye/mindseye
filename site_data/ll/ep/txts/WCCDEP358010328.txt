Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Mary Waine aged about 58 years upon her Oath saith that about 28 years ago she Intermarried with Wm  Thos. Waine who has been dead about Eighteen months at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Overys That her said late Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman lived as a hired yearly servant with one Saml. Lloyd a Tobacconist in Threadneedle street in the Parish of saint Bennett Fink in the City of London for Twelve months and upwards at the yearly wages of Forty Guineas that after he quitted said service he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or did any act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of March 1790  Sampr Wright M Addington

her Mary Waine Mark
