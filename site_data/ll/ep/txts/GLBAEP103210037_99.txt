Middlesex ss}  Sarah Bullen the wife of Edward Bullen maketh oath that her Said husband is lately gone away from her to places to her unknown & She can give no Account respecting his place of Settlement Save that he has informed her that he was a Native of Ireland That She previous to her Marriage with her Sd husband & When a Single woman was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with Mr. Young a Victualler in the parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of one Whole year & upwards & has not Since done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere Save what She may have Acquired by her Marriage That she was lawfully married to her Sd husband by whom She hash a Child now living Aged two Months namely Mary the lawful issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband.

Sworn this 11. day of Decemr 1784 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Sarah Bullen
