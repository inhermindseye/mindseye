Middlesex ss}  Ann Maxfield  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Gallant a Victualler in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & Continue the & in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of three Years that She has not Since lived as a hired Servat for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to her Said Servitude

Sworn this 28. day of December 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Ann Maxfield
