Middlesex, to wit, Elizabeth Bayles  Singlewoman Maketh Oath and Saith, that on the Fourteenth Day of April 1791 last at the House of Mr . Samuel Hill in Star Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph Aldgate Middlesex , she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child since baptised at Aldgate Church by the Name of John Samuel and that John Kilpin of Hanslope in Buckinghamshire , Farmer , is the Father of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this 18th Day June 1791 , before  Rob Smith

Elis Bayles
