Middlesex  Eliza. Osborne upon Oath saith she intermarried with Jonathan Osborne in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx on the 19th. Day of Octr. 1734 Says the sd. Jonathan Osborn rented a house in the Parish of Margaret near Chatham in the County of Kent & was rented & paid to the Poor there Says on the 15th. of March 1739 she was hired as a Yearly Servt . to the Rt. Honble the Coualess of Marr in little Grosvenor Street in the Parish of St. Geo. Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds & lived there upwards of Eleven Months Says she having laid out about four Shillings for her Ladys use & her Ladyship (having lost the Bill of Partient paid this Deponent a full Years Wages

Sworn May 15th. 1746 before}

Eliz Osborne
