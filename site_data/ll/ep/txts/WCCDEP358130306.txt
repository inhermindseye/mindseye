Middx  Ann Hilliard aged abt. 17 years upon Oath says she never gained any Settlement by any Act of her own; but that her father John Hilliard Rented a house upwards of twenty Pounds a yr. by Tottenham High Cross in the Parish of Tottenham High Cross in the County of Middlesex for Ten yrs. & upwards & never after he quitted said House Rented ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn the 16th. of March 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Ann Hilliard
