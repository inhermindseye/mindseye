Middx Westmr. to wit} The Examination of Robt. Curant on the Behalfe of Margt. Curant his Daughter a Lunatick taken the 21st. Day of May 1741 Who upon his Oath saith that he lives in & rents a House in White Horse Yard in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds & hath so done for the Space of Eighteen Years or thereabts- & further upon his Oath saith that his sd. Daughter never was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed, before  Val Hilder

Robt. Currant
