Middlesex ss}  Joseph Burnish  mariner maketh oath That about Seventy Six years ago he was born in the Parish of Maningtree in the County of Essex That he was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right and this Deponent Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of his late Parents or either of them.

Sworn this 7th day of May 1774 before  Jno Sherwood

Joseph Burnish His Mark
