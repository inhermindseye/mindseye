Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Harriet Brooks aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that about Six years ago she Intermarried with William her present husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint John at Hackney by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. William aged about Two years and an half and Mary aged about One Month That her said Husband Rented a Second floor unfurnished of a Mr. Dax in Cooks Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Six Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds That he hath quitted said Apartments about Three years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th.. day of March 1798 before me } John Cowick

her Harriet Brooks mark

Witness A. W. Burbidge Beadle
