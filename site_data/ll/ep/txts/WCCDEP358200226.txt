Middx  Mary Brooks Aged about Thirty five Years upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Michael Brooks in the Romish Chaple by Lincolns Inn Fields Says her said Husband Rented a House in the Parish of Westham in the County of Essex three Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds hath quitted said House about Twelve Years & hath not Since Rented Ten pounds by the Year paid Land Tax or poors Rate

Sworn the 29th of January 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant Tho's Kynaston

Mary Brooks
