Middx & Westmr.}  Eleanor Larken living at Mr. Jarvis's in Hemlock Court aged about 28 Years on behalf of Alexr. Talland Aged about Seven Years and William Talland aged about four Years Infants and Children of Alexr. Talland by Alice Lords who has been dead about Six Months on her Oath says that the said Alexr. Talland and Alice Lord never was Married as she has been Informed and verily believes Says that the said Alexander Talland the Father who is now pressed and gone to Sea was a Native of Scotland and never gained a Settlemt. in England other than his Renting and living in a House in Leicester Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the Co. of Middx at the Yrly Rent of £9 In which House the sd. Alexr. Talland was born & the said Wm. Talland in Croson Court purple Lane in the said Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn but Whr. the said Alexr. Talland the Father ever paid any Taxes due for the same she cannot set forth And that the said Alexr. Talland & Wm . the Children never gained any Settlemt. other than being born in the aforesd. Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn .

Sworn this 5th. Day of March 1777 before Us  Jno. Machin  John Croft

her Eleanor Larkin Mark
