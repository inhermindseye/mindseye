Middx  The Examination of Jno. Darby on the behalfe of Edward Johnson aged about 55 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 11th. Day of May 1739 Who upon his Oath saith that about Eleven Years ago the above Named Edwd. Johnson Rented a House in Swallow Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of Five Years And this Examinant further saith that the sd. Edwd. Johnson hath not since he left the sd. House Rented any Lands or Tenemts. of the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife & that he is now Sick & Poor & not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane  Samuel Clarke

John Darby
