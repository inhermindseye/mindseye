Westmr. to wit Sarah Mansell Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says never was married Rented House paid Taxes or served any Apprenticeship whatever Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Elvey a Watchmaker in the New Buildings  Fore Street in the Parish of Saint Giles's Cripplegate London for upwards of Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Shillings for the first Year forty Shillings for the Second and Three pounds for the Third Year hath quitted the said last mentioned service about Three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th. day of May 1772 before} R. Butler 

her Sarah Mansell Mark
