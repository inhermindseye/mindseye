Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Gurney aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that on the 17th. day of June 1786 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Wade the corner of New Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Ann and that on the 15th. day of Decr . last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. White in Wych Street in the said Parish which hath since been Christened by the Name of Michael That one Michael Murphy a Boot and Shoemaker who lately worked with Mr. Cleane in Clare Street and is at present at Bath is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Two Children thereon and that the said Michael Murphy is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of Septr. 1788 } Wm Kitchiner

her Elizabeth Gurney Mark

Michael Murphy is a Tall Stout Man near Six foot high wears his own hair Tued Curled at the Sides and Combed over his forehead very Pale Complexion a Mole over the left Eye and a Mole on his left Cheek comes from Ireland been at Bath about Seven Months
