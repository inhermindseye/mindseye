Middx ss  John Burkitt maketh oath that about Twenty Seven Years ago he was bound apprentice by Indre of apprenticeship to Joseph Joyce a Goldsmith of Hoop Alley in Old Street then in the Parish of St. Giles Cripplegate in the County of Middx but now called the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middx where he lived with his Master five Years and half and then by sd. Master removed into Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of the City of Westminster in the sd. County of Middx where he served his sd. Master a Year & half being the residue of his sd. apprenticeship and by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt. there And this Dept. Saith that he was Never a hired Servant by the Year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a Year & upwards nor Served any parochial office Nor gained any other Settlemt. then in right of his sd. apprenticeship as aforesd. to his knowledge or belief

Sworn this 27th. day of August 1751 before us  Boulton Mainwaring  Walter Berry

John Burkitt
