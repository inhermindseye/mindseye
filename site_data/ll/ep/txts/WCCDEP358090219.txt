Middx to wit Catherine Bury Aged about Thirty Six years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or was an Apprentice says she lived a hired yearly servant with Mrs. Miller in Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for 12 years at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said service about Fifteen years ago since which time she hath gained no other settlement

Sworn this  Day of May 1781 before  Abrm Baley  John Hagar

her Cath: Bury Mark
