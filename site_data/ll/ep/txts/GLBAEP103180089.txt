Middlesex ss}  Alice Roop  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was duly bound to and Served two years of her apprenticeship with John Marchant a Hatter in the Precinct of Saint Catherine in the Said County and then this Deponent was Assigned and turned over to one Frances Cranaway a Washer woman of King Henry Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County With Whom She Served the remainder of her said Apprenticeship and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain another Settlement.

Sworn this. 12. day of November 1778 . before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Alice Roop
