Middx  John Marlton upon Oath saith he Served his Apprentiship with Jno. Poole a Hatter in the Maze in the Parish of St. Olas  Southwark in the County of Surry and never rented ten pounds by the Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn March 28th 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

John Marlton
