Middx  The Examination of Sarah Morris  Widow aged 62 Years living in ye. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. County of Middx taken ye. 6th Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that her sd. late Husband Mathew Morris upwards of Sixteen Years ago lived in & Rented a House in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  for the Space of Eight Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & this Exaiant further saith that her sd. Husband in his life Time nor she since his Death never after Rented any other House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & that she is now Poor & infirm & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Sarah Morris
