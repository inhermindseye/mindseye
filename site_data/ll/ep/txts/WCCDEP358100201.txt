Middlesex to wit Robert Morgan Aged about Twenty Six Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he was bound an Apprentice to one Alexr. Bean a Bookbinder for Seven Years and Served his said Master the last Three Years of his said Apprenticeship in Little Russell Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any other subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of March 1784 before R Butler

Robert Morgan
