Westmr.  Susannah Smith aged about 34 Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of William Smith who has been dead about One Year, says her said late Husband informed her and which information she very believes to be true that he was bound Apprentice for seven Years to Mr. Clark a Fan Stick maker Opposite St. Clement's Church in the Strand and served four Years or thereabouts of his said Apprenticeship with the said Mr. Clark opposite St. Clements Church aforesaid in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and then run away and went to Sea, and never was Assigned over to any Person, Rented Ten Pounds a Year, Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, Says she hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes.

Sworn Octr. 30th. 1767 before} 

The Mark of Susannah Smith
