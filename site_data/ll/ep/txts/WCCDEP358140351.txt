Middx.  Mary Froy aged about 60 Years, upon Oath says she is Widow of Wm. Froy who has been dead abt. Ten Years says her said husband Rented and lived in a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds for about four Years in Kings head Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. and after he Quitted Boat house never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes. Says she has not Since her said husbands death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One year.

Sworn this 22d. day of February 1760 . before}
