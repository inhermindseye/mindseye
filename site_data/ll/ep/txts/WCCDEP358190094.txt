Middx.  William Dalziel aged abt. 60 Years lodging at Mr. Montgomery's at Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Upon Oath says he Rented a house in Hungerford Marblin the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards for Several Years; in which he carried on the Business of a Tobacconist

Sworn Jany. 28th. 1761 before } Paul Vaillant  Fran: Bedwell

Wil Dalziel

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Elvins Overseer J : P:
