Middx  Hannah Naylor aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish taxes Says she has been informed and verily believes her late father Joseph Naylor deced was born a Burstle near Leeds in Yorkshire and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year (whilst a Singleman) or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn April 2d. 1761 before  Tho Lane

The mark of Hannh. Naylor

born in the one bell Inn her father was ostler at the one bell .
