St.. Clement Danes 6th March 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  Child  (to wit) Mary Williams aged 30 years or thereabouts Wife of George Williams now a Seaman on Board His Majesty's Ship Leopard Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her said Husband George Williams at the parish Church of Saint Mary le Strand about 7 years ago that her said Husband previous to their Marriage lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Nott in Butcher Row in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for two years but at what Annual Wages this Examinant knows not saith that since he quitted that Service he not hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge or belief saith that she hath only one Child by her Said Husband Christened Jane aged 6 years on the 10th. day of November last

Sworn the 6th day of February March 1793 before Me}

Mary Williams 's Mark
