Middx  Pleasant Burt  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that on the 31st. Day of July last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been Baptized William & is become Chargeable to the said Parish & that one George Leeke  Hackney Coachman did begett the said Child on her Body she being at a Publick house over against St. Giles Pound on the 28th. Day of October last with the said George Leeke where he had Carnall knowledge of her Body sevll. times at one of which times he the sd. George Leeke gott her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesaid & that the sd. George Leeke & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne Augt. 23d. 1743 before} C. Egerton

The Mark of Pleasant Burt
