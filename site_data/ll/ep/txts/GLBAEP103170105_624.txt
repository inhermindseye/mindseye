Middlesex ss  Martha Toffield maketh oath that her Father Richard Toffield deced divers years ago rented a house of Ten Pounds a year in Cow Cross in the parish of Saint Sepulchre London & resided thereon for Several years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain and Settlement That she this Depont was never Married nor was ever bound an Apprentice nor ever did live anyone place as a hired Servant for a year nor ever do any Act to gain a Settlemt. in her own right

Sworn this 19 of Novemr 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Martha Toffield
