Middx ss  Elizabeth Spencer  Singlewoman Maketh oath that about Twenty Years ago she was bound apprentice by Indre to one Sebaatus Anoldus Gatzen a Hair Cutter who then still lives in old Bond Street in the Parish of St. George, Hanover Square in the City County of Middx and duly Served a Seven Years apprenticeship with the sd. Mr. Gatzen in the sd. Parish And this Depont. Never Continued as a hired Servant by the Year for ye Space of a Year of upwards nor was Never Married Nor rented a house of Ten pounds by ye Year or a love

Sworn this 5th day of February 1753 before us  Richd: Ricards  Boulton Mainwaring

Elizabeth Spencer
