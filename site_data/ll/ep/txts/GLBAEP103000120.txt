Middlesex ss.  Nathan Haswell maketh Oath that in the Year 1763 or 1764 he Rented a House of Twenty Pounds a Year in Sharps Buildings in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and lived in the same about Three Years and was charged to and paid Poors Rate for the same that he has not since he quitted the said House Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of February 1790 before}
