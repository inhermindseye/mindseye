Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Edward Ballard aged about 39 years now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes upon his Oath saith that on or about the Seventh day of March 1791 he Intermarried with Mary his late Wife who has been dead about Fourteen days at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn by whom he hath Three Children living Vizt. Edward aged upwards of Six years Thomas aged about upwards of Four years and John aged about Two years That he this Deponent Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one John Dearne in Lambs Conduct Passage in the parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of Febry. 1798 before us } M Addington Rupt Clarke

Edward Ballard
