Middx  William Budd aged abt. Thirty Two Years, upon Oath Says he was bound an Apprentice to, and served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. John Garthwait in Naked Boy Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. & hath been out of his sd. Apprentiship Six Years, Says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant For one Year. & having lost the Use of his Hands is not able to provide for himself

Sworn the 27th. Day of May 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of William Budd
