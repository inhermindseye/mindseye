Middx to wit Jane Coxson Aged upwards of Twenty four years on her Oath says she intermarried with John Coxson (gone from her) at the Parish Church of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster the 25th day of May 1770 , and be whom she has only one Child now living namely John aged about four years, says her said Husband served upwards of five years the first part of his Apprenticeship with one Mr John Davis a perukemaker in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster , when his said Master broke and left the Business, and that her said Husband was never assigned over to another Master nor never since that time Rented House, paid Taxes, lived a hired yearly Servant for one year nor any otherwise gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th Day of Novr 1775 before  David Walker

her Jane Coxson Mark
