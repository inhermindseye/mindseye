Westmr. Mary Cropp Wido . of Anthony Cropp who dyed in the 14th. of August 1749 upon Oath saith she intermarryed with the sd. Anthony Cropp at the Fleet London on the 11th. of April 1747 whom she hath one Child (viz) Henry aged about Sixteen Months Says the said Anthony Cropp rented a house of Fourteen Guineas a Year in Crown Court in the Butcher Row in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and lived therein about Six months and quitted the sd. house about 12 Years ago since which the sd. Antho. Cropp has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt 30th. 1749 before H Fielding

Mary Cropp
