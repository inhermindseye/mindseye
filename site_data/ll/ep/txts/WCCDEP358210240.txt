Middx. & Westmr.}  Catherine Deiley aged about 29 Years Upon Oath says she is the Wife of Derby Deiley with whom she intermarried in the Parish Church of Wimbourne Minster in the County of Dorset about the Month of April 1763, by whom she hath one Child named Mary aged about four Years which was born in the said Parish of Wimbourne Minster, says her said husband is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or belief, and that about two Months after her said Marriage the said Derby Deiley went to Newfoundland, and is there now living as this Examinant hath been credibly informed and believes; Says that at the time of her said Marriage with the said Derby Deiley she was the Widow of George Collis with whom she also intermarried in the said Parish Church of Wimbourne Minster, says the said George Collis served Six years of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Thomas Madden a Cooper in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex and then his Master died; and that her said Husband George Collis never was Assigned over to any Person nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that since her present Husband Derby Deiley went to Newfoundland as is abovementioned she hath never seen him, And that she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes , she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when born, says that William Lewis who now keeps the Blackmost

Alehouse in one of the Streets leading into the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry did beget the said Child or Children on her Body, the said William Lewis having had carnal knowledge of her Body in the House of Sir William Hanham Baronet at Wimbourne Minster aforesaid on or about the 29th, day of July 1767, and at one other time afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant, and that the said William Lewis and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn the 4th, day of February 1768 , before} Thos: Balack

The Mark of Catherine Deiley
