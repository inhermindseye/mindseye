Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Grace Gray of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County  Singlewoman taken on oath before me Buford Camper - Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the said County this 13th Day of July 1774

Who on her oath Saith That She is now with Child And that the said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that Thomas Bell of the said P arish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate is the Father of the said Child.

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1774 before B Camper

Grace Gray
