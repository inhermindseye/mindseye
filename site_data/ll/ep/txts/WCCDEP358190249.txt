Middx  Joseph Churchill aged about fifty five Years upon Oath Says he Lodges at the fountain in Shire Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx that he lived in and Rented a House in St Martins Court in the Parish of St Martins in the Fields in the liberty of Westmr: of the Yearly Rent of twenty pounds for seven Years & hath quitted the Same upwards of twenty Years hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Land Tax or poor Rate

Sworn the 9th day of June 1762 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

Jos Churchill
