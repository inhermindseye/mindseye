Westmr.  Nowel George Van Reder Aged about Eight Six Years on his Oath Says he has been a Widower for many Years And that he Rented and lived in a House in Mark Lane in the Parish of Alhallows Staining in the City of London for one Year and a Quarter at the Yearly Rent of forty Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Thirty five Years Ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 21st. day of January 1771 before}  P L Perrin

Noel George Van Reder
