Middx  Samuel Dodd Aged about Forty Nine Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes say's he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Trotter a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise Howsoever

Sworn this 28th. day of June 1779 before R Butler

his Samuel Dodd Mark
