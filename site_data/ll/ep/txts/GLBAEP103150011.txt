Middlesex ss}  Dorothy Jones the Wife of John Jones maketh oath That her said husband is lately gone away from her to places unknown & left her destitute That She this Deponent divers Years ago was lawfully married in the parish Church of St Andrew Holborn London to one James Cutler of the Trinity Minories London Gun Polisher Since deced by whom She hath a Child now living namely Joseph Cutler aged about five Years & half Whose legal place of Settlement in right of his said late Father James Cutler is in the parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel in the said County of Middlesex by his Said late Fathers Renting two Tenements of the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds & residing thereon in Chamber Street in the Said parish of Saint Mary Whitechapel about Five Years ago & being Charged to & paying Poors Rates for the Same in which Parish he died & by his not doing any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement elsewhere That She this Deponent after her said first husbands death was lawfully married to her present husband John Jones in the parish Church of St Dunstan Stepney Middx who by Jude Beasingdale the Twentieth day of May 1758 was duly bound apprentice to John Godin of the parish of St Matthew Bethnal Green in the said County of Middlesex  Silkweaver with whom he duly Served the whole of his said Apprenticeship there as he has informed this Deponent & as this Deponent verily believes & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her Sd husband Jones did not afterwards Rent a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor do any Act to this Deponents knowledge or belief to gain a Subseqt Settlement That She this Depont hath a Child named John Aged about Nine Weeks the lawful issue of this Deponent by her Said husband Jones

Sworn this 6th. day of February 1775 before B Camper Saml. Spagg

The Mark of Dorothy Jones
