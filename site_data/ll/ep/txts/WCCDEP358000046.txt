Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Child passd. to St Paul Covent Garden to wit Elizth. Faithfull aged about 36 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Wm. Sowster of Roe Hampton Surry in the Parish of Putney for 15 Months & upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted said Service about 8 years since which time She has gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid Says that she has one Child living named Robert aged upwards of Seven years born a Bastard in the House of one Mr. Browning a Publican in Hart Street Covent Garden in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex And further says that her said Child never gained any other Settlement than by Birth

Sworn this 18th day of May 1785  Sampr Wright W Addington

Elizabeth Faithfull
