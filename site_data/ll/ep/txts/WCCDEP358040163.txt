Westmr  Bridget Mc.kerty on Behalf of Eliza. Mc.kerity an Infant Child aged upwards of 5 Years born of the Body of Margt. Haley - deceased upon Oath saith she has been informed by John Mc.kerity the reputed father of the sd. Child (since also deced) that the sd. Child was a Bastard & that she has been further informed & beleives that the said Eliza. Mc.kerty was born in Eagle Court in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster

Sworn March 15th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Bridgt. Mc.kerty
