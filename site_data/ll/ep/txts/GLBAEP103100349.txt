Middlesex ss}  John Foster maketh oath That divers Years ago he rented a house in the Parish of St. Nicholas Deptford in the County of Kent and was charged to and Paid the Kings Tax for the Same and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont Saith that he hath not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a Danr. named Mary Aged about Twenty one Years his lawful issue who was Part of his Family when he lived in the said Parish of St. Nicholas Deptford And that she was never married nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year not was ever Married nor hath She done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 16th Day of December 1770 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

John Foster
