Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Gordon the Widow of John Gordon deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband lived in & rented a house of the yearly Rent of Twenty three Pounds in Little Earle Street in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to & Paid Parish Taxes for the Same & hired there for about the Space of four years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont Saith that her Said husband did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief nor hath She done any act to gain a Settlement for herself Since his death.

Sworn this 18th day of December 1773 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

Elizabeth Gordon
