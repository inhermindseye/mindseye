Middx.  Mary Yallely on the behalf of Elizth. Hunt an Infant aged abt. 15 Years upon Oath says that Edwd. Hunt the father of said Elizth : Rented and lived in a house of the Yearly Rent of £13 for sevl. Years in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx.

Sworn this 22d. Janry. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Yalleyley
