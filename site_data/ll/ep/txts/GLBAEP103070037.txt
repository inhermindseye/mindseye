Middx ss  Ann Wanner  Wife of George Wanner (who is lately gone away from her to places unknown) Maketh oath that hersd. Husband hath often informed her that he Served his apprenticeship with Mr. Gittens a Wheelwright in the parish of Silso in the County of Bedford and lived in ye Same Parish during all his apprenticeship And that he was never a hired Servant by ye Year or rented a house of Ten pounds a Year or Served any parish office which information this Dept. believes to be true And Saith that in right of her sd. husband she apprehends herself to be legally Settled in Silso aforesd. And she this Depont. hath a Son Named George aged abt. fourteen Months the lawfull Issue of this Dept. & herrd. husband

Sworn this 11 day of July 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  John Lawton

Ann Wannar
