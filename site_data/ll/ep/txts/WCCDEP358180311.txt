Middx  Sarah Beazley aged about 69 Years upon her Oath saith she is the Wido. of Edwd. Beazley who hath been Dead about Sixteen Years Says the sd. Edward Beazley  was bound an Apprentice to and Served 7 Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Lewis in Catherine Wheel Yard in St. James Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr.  Blacksmith & hath been out of his time about 32 Years Says the sd. Edwd. Beazley in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworne April 2d. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sarah Beazley
