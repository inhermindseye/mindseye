Middlesex and Westminster } (to wit) Elizth. Cooper Aged 24 years or thereabts. on Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Silvester at the Lyceum in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes near two years at the Annual Wages of Six Guineas that she quitted her said Service abt. Eighteen Months ago and since hath not (to her knowledge or belief) acquired any other legal Settlement) And this Examinant also on Oath saith that on 3rd. of Augst .

August 1790 at the Store Street Hospital she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath since been Christened Elizabeth and that Paul Podevinte a Watch Glass maker who lately resided at the Saint Luke's Head in Little Pulteney Street is the only real and but Father of such Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he and no one else having several times had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body at some or one of which times he did beget the same thereon

Sworn the 7th. May 1791 before me} R Butler

Eliz Cooper

Paul Podevinte is a Middle size Man his Hair Black & hanging loose abt. 22 Years old left the St Lukes Head on Saturday Saturday the 30th. April intending as she believes to go to Sea she cannot tell where he now is or to what Ship he belongs
