Middx The Examination of Jno. Mars Aged about Sixty Nine Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 16th. Day of June 1739 Who upon his Oath saith that in March 1683 he was bound an Apprentice to Rapent Starth Citizen & Cordwainer of Londn . & was some short time after he was so bound an Apprentice as aforesd. Assigned over to his this Examinant's Father who upwards of 6 Months the latter Part of this Examints. Apprentiship Rented a House in Chanders Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster And this Examint. Further upon his Oath saith that he never Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife that he has a Wife & being Poor & Old is not able to maintaine her.

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Tho Lane

John Mars
