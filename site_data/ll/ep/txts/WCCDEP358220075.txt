Westmr to wit John Bratt aged Eighty Two Years and upwards on his Oath says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster about Thirty Years ago Says he Rented and lived in a house in Tooley Street in the Parish of Saint John Southwark in the County of Surry for Ten Years or thereabouts of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same Says he has quitted the said House near Thirty Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly servant for one year.

Sworn this 10th. day of October 1769 before Us} R Butler 

John Bratt
