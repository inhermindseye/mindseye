Westmr.  Ann Wait aged abt. 24 Years upon Oath says she never was married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with the Hungarian Ambassador in St. James's Square in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr , for five Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds for the three first Years and and Eight Pounds for the Two last Years; quitted said Service about four Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes, or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One year.

Sworn the 15th, day of August 1767 , before W Kelynge Fran Cary

The Mark of Ann Wait
