Westmr.  Mary Bottomley Aged about forty Two Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Bottomley who has been Dead about Nineteen Years Says her said late Husband served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Young a Taylor in Gloucester Street near Queen Square in the Parish of Saint George the Martyr in the County of Middlesex and never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither hath this Examinant since her said Husband Death done Any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Janry 1771 before Us} R Butler

her Mary Bottomly Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
