Middx  John Davis upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Elizabeth in Brides Alley in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland about 44 Years since Says he rented a house & Land & Coramill in the Parish of Daninbrook in the sd. City of Dublin of the Yearly Rent of £100 for 34 Yrs. or thereabouts & hath left same abt. 22 Yrs & hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne April 26. 1746 before  Wm. Jones

The Mark of John Davis
