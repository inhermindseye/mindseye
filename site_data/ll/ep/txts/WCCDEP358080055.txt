Middx & Westmr.}  Eleanor Donkin Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Holland a Mantua maker in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Months ago and since that time hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. And further Says that on the fifteenth day of February last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to said Parish which was soon afterwards baptized by the name of Francis and is now chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes And further upon her Oath Says that one Francis Dunn a Journeyman Taylor who lately worked with Mr. Levitt a Taylor in Norfolk Street and who uses the Sun and Appletree in the White Hart Yard is the only true Father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of May 1776 before.  M Addington

E Doonkin
