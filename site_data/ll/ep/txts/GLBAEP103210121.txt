Liberty of his Majesties Tower of London ss} John Foster maketh oath that he was heretofore hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Joseph Phillips & a Wine Cooper in Idle Lane in the Parish of St Dunstan in the East in the City of London & Continued in such Service under Such hiring for the Space of three years & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this first day of March 1785 before R Davies

Jno. Foster
