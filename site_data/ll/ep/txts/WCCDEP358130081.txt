Middx  Francis Barns aged abt. Forty Years Upon Oath Says he intermarried with his wife Esther abt two Years ago in the Savoy Chapel Says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven years to Jno. Bucker and Served abt. three Quarters of a Year the last part of his sd. Apprentishipp with his sd. Master in the Parish of St. Ann in Black Friers in the City of London Says he never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt.

Sworn the 14th. day of Novr. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Francis Barns
