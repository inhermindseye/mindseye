Middx & Westmr}  Ann Roffee Aged about Sixty Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Henry Roffee who has been Dead about Two Months Says that before his Intermarrd. with this Examinant her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in little Saint James's Street in the Parish of Saint James in the liberty of Westmr . for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds or upwards & Paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted sd. House about Twenty Years ago and after he quitted the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th. day of June 1770 before Us} R. Butler

Ann Roffee
