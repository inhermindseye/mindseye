Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Richard Turner aged about 66 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint James Clerkenwell on or about the 2d. day of August 1772 That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Stidwell Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Fifteen years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Eight Guineas and paid the Poor Rates due for the same that he hath quitted said house about Twenty one years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter on thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Octr. 1796  M Addington W Kinnard

Richd. Turney
