Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Colleshaw of No.18 Wych Street on behalf of Ann Duncan an Infant aged about Seven years Maketh Oath and Saith That the said Ann Duncan is the legitimate Child of Samuel and Ann Duncan both now deceased That the said Samuel and Ann Duncan were married at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Overys in the County of Surry about Eight years ago That the said Samuel Duncan was a Scotchman and never to the best of this Examinants Knowledge gained any Settlement in England That the said Ann Duncan before her Intermarriage to the said Samuel and being a Singlewoman lived as hired yearly Servant with our Mr. Sellers a Baker in Maid Lane in the Parish of Saint Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry for Twelve Months and upwards and that after she quitted said Service she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or ever paid Taxes

Sworn this 13th. day of Augt. 1795 N Bond E Read

her Sarah Colleshaw mark
