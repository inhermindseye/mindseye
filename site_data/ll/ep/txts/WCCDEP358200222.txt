Middx  John Watson Aged about Thirty three Years upon Oath Says he was never Marryed Says he Served seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Holt a Shoemaker in Leather Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn in the County of Middx & never after Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 22d of January 1765 Before} Thos: Balack  Benjamin Cox

John Watson
