Middx.  Sarah Gimber aged abt. 31 Years upon Oath says she is the widow of Francis Gimber who died on Friday the 10th. day of this Instant Novr. by whom she has one Child named Ann aged abt. one Year and nine Months says her said husbd. was by Indenture dated the 23d. of Sepr. 1749 bound Apprentice for seven Years to Thos. Stead of St Pauls Church Yard in the Parish of St. Gregory by St. Pauls in the City of London  Stationer and served six Years of his said Apprenticeship and then the said Mr. Stead died says her said husband was never assigned ever to any person or Ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year according to the best of her knowledge of belief

Sworn this 18th. day of Novr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Sarah Gimber

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Alexr. Allan  Overseer
