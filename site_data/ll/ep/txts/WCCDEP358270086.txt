Before Birth

St Clement Danes 15th Octr. 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt of the Woman  Pregnancy  Reputed Father (to wit) Elizabeth Rainer  singlewoman aged 27 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she  was hired as a yearly Servant fourteen Months ago  to Mr Toulmin Essex Street in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the yearly Wages of 9 Guineas, that she has  ever since such hiring lived  with the said Mr Toulmin as such hired Servant in Essex Street aforesaid and is not discharged from his said Service  And this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born or Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes and this Deponent saith that John Barnes  Hair Dresser who is now Foreman to the Widow Whitehouse of Church Street  Birmingham  is the only real true father of the said Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant as aforesaid he & no one else having had carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in or about the Month of March last & at  other times since at some or one of which time he did beget the said Child or Children wherewith she is pregnant as aforesaid on the body of this Deponent and that he and no one else is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn the 15th. day of November 1792 before me} R Butler

Elizabeth Rainer
