Westmr  Sarah Williams Aged about fifty One Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Thos: Williams who has been dead about five Years Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Stanhope Street Clare Markett in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds or thereabouts and after he quitted the same he never afterwards Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 12th. day of April 1770 before me} R. Butler

Sarah Willams
