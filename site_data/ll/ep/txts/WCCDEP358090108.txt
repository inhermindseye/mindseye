Middx to wit Dorothy Jones Aged about forty Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Pennery an Apothecary in Tooley Street in the Parish of Saint Olave in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry for Thirteen Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said Service Upwards of seven Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Years Servant for One Year

Sworn this 5th. day of 1780 before M Addington Sampn Wright

her Dorothy Jones Mark
