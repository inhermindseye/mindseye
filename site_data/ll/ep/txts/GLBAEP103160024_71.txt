Middlesex ss}  Lydia London the Widow of Jonathan London deced & Elizabeth Atkinson  Singlewoman her Daughter Severally make oath and first the Said Deponent London for herself Saith That many Years ago She this Deponent & one William Atkinson deced & previous to this Deponents marriage with her Said late husband Jonathan London Cohabited & lived together as Husband & Wife although in truth & in Fact no Marriage of Ceremony of a marriage was ever had or Solemnised between this Deponent & the Said William Atkinson That during such Cohabitation She this Deponent was delivered of the Said other Deponent Elizabeth Atkinson in Three Pidgeon Court in Houndsditch in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London That the Said William Atkinson was the Father of the Said Depont Elizabeth Atkinson who by reason of this Deponts never having been married to him she the Sd Depont Atkinson is a Bastard illegitimate And the Said Deponent Elizabeth Atkinson for herself Saith That she was never married nor ever was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 22d. day of March 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith  Parkr. Rowlands

The Mark of Lydia London

The Mark of Elizabeth Atkinson
