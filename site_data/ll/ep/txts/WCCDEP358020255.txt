St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster  Mary Hughes Aged about 31 years upon her Oa Wife of John Hughes on behalf of her Sister Jane Wright Aged about Seven Years upon her Oath saith That Charles Wright this Examinants father was Married to Jane Wright his late & only Wife at the parish Church of Sedgley near Wolverhampton Staffordshire abt the Year 1755 that her said Father Rented and lived in a House in Brown's Buildings Drury Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for about four Years at the Yearly Rent of abt. £18 or £20. that her said father quitted that House about 3 or 4 years ago since which time he has not to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief gained other legal Settlement And further saith that her said Sister Jane Wright is a Legitimate Child of the said Charles Wright & Jane his late Wife & has gained no Settlement in her own Right

Sworn the 7th. March 1791 before Me  Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Hughs Mark
