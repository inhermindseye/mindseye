Middx  Mary Rochester Aged about Sixty Eight Years on her Oath says she Intermarried with her Husband John Rochester at the Fleet London upwards of forty Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Namely Ann Aged about forty Year and Mary Aged near forty both of whom were provided for before she went to lived as a Servant Says her said Children being so provided for she went to live as a hired Yearly Servant one James Smith a Shagareen Case maker in Little New Street in the Parish of Saint Brides otherwise Bridget in the City of London for five Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Seventeen Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 20th. day of Febry. 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner  Sampn Wright

her Mary Rochester Mark
