Middlesex ss}  Stephen Gale maketh oath That his Father William Gale hath informed him & which information he believes to be true That many Years ago this Deponts. Said Father rented a house in Leppards Court Baldwins Gardens in the Liberty above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the County of Middlesex (where he this Deponent was also born) & was charged to & paid Parochial Taxes for the Same & by means thereof became legally Settled there That to this Deponts. knowledge Information or belief his Said Father never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That he this Deponts. never was bound an apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever rented a house of the yearly Rent or Values of Ten Pounds nor did he ever pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor do any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right And that he hath a wife named Elizabeth .

Sworn this 26th day of February 1773 before  Cha: Digby R Pell

The Mark of Stephen Gale
