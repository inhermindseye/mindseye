Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Grove Singlewoman maketh oath that She well Knows a Child named John West aged about eight Years and Nine Months which was born of the body of Mary West this Deponents Mother in the Prison of Newgate in the Parish of St Sepulchre in the City of London the Which Child (as this) Deponent has been informed by her Said Mother & which this Deponent Verily believes to be true) is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason that She this Deponents Mother was never married to the Said Childs Father That Such Child has not as yet done any Act to gain a Settlement for himself That this Deponents Sd Mother before & previous to her being Sent to the Said Prison of Newgate (where She was So as aforesd delivered rented a house of about Forty Pounds a year in Berkley Square in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Sd County of Middlesex & resided thereon for the Space of a year or more to the best of this Deponents now recollection from which Place She was Committed to the Sd Prison of Newgate That after this Deponents Said Mother was discharged from Such Prison She went from thence lived in & rented a house (by the name of Mary Grove ) in the New Buildings in Leather Lane in the Liberty of Saffron Hill in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the Said County of Middlesex at the yearly rent of twenty Pounds and Continued therein for the Space of three Months & there died.

Sworn this 13. day of April 1786 before. R Davies Robt. Smith

Elizabeth Grove
