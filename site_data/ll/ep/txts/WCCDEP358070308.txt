Middx to wit Hannah Lightfoot Aged about Nineteen years on her Oath says she never was Married, Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Robert Barrow a Stationer in Saint Brides passage Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Brides otherwise Bridget in the City of London for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of four Pounds hath quitted her said Service about nine months ago since which time she hath gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th day of Oct 1775 before.  Wm. Kitchiner R. Butler

her Hannah Lightfoot Mark
