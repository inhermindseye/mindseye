Middx  John Hills aged about 67 Years Removed from the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London upon Oath saith That he was bound an Apprentice by an Indenture for Seven Years to Mr. John Sidley a Clockmaker with whom be served all his time and saith that during the last three Years of such Apprenticeship his Master lodged at Mr. Riley's a Turner at the Corner of the Talbot Inn in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and hath not since rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or served Parochial Office

Sworn the 12th: day of March 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

John Hills
