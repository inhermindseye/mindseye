Middx  Fras Harding upon Oath Saith he was married to Mary his Wife at the Fleet London in the month of April upwds. of 4 Yrs. ago and that before his sd marriage & whilst a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Edwd Buckley Esqr . in Essex Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of £10..10..0 for one Year & upwds. and lay all that time in Mr Buckleys house in Essex Street that he quitted his sd. Service abt. Six or Seven Years ago and has not Since rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one Year

Sworn 6 July 1754 before Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Fras Harding
