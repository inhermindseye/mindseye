Westmr.  Peter Ashley removed from the Parish of Christchurch in the County of Surry by Order bearing Date the 30th. Day of July last upon Oath Says he was bound out an Apprentice (by the Society for the Reformation of manners) to Edward Vaughan in Bennets Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. Basket maker for Nine Years & lived with his sd. Master in Bennets Court aforesd. Seven Years & then the sd. Edwd. Vaughan removed into Vear Street near Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. aforesd. where this Examinant lived with his sd. Master the last two Years of the sd. Term of Nine Years & hath been out of his sd. Aprentiship upwds. of 20 Years Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif Says he intermarried with Mary his Wife at the fleet London upwds. of 19 Years since & hath by her two Children (to wit) James aged abt. ten Yrs. & Mary Ann aged abt. 7 Yrs. & is Poor & not able to maintain them

Sworn Augt. 4th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Peter Ashley
