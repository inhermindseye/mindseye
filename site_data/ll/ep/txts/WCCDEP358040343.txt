Middx  John Smith upon his Oath upwards of fifteen Years ago being then a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. James Lambourn Button Seller in St Clemts. Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  at the Yearly Wages of Ten pounds for One Year & upwards and has not since he left his sd. Service rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 31st 1749 before}
