Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} to wit Elizabeth Lawrence aged about 63 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Carr a Taylor in Little Russell Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Serv about 14 - years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. Augt. 1787  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gordon

Elizabeth Lawrence
