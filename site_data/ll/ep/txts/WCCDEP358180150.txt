Middx  Ann Pointin in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Singlewoman upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when born & that one Mathew Callahan of the Parish of Rotherhithe in the County of Surry  Anchormaker did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body she at such time liveing a Yearly hired Servt. with Mr. Davis in Mansfelt Street in the Minorys & the sd. Mathew Callahan being acquainted with her used to Visit her & had carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of Octr. last at the House of the sd. Mr. Davis & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastd. Child or Childn. with which she is now Pregnant & that he the sd. Mathew Callahan & no other person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworn 27th. Decr. 1743 before} Thos. DeVeil

The Mark of Ann Pointin
