Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Williams aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Garnham in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Seventeen Months at the yearly wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Twelve Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Chafe a Journeyman Shoemaker who did lodge at Mr. Bignolls on Little Shire Lane but where he is now she this Deponent doth not know is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Chafe is the only Real & true father thereof

Sworn this 8th. day of Augt. 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Elizth. Williams Mark

Witness B Newman
