Westmr.  Thomas Green upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Ann in Lothbury Church London abt. 10 Yrs. ago by whom he hath a Child named Sarah aged upwds. of 8 Yrs. who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year Says about 7 Years ago he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £22 in King Street in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr. for three Yrs. or thereabouts and has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 27th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

Thomas Green Taken for his Daur's Admission into St. Clemts.  Charity School
