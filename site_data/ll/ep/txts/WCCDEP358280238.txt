Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Jones aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Marshall in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Lather a Journeyman Carpenter who works in Charles Court in the Strand and lodge at Mr. Leeson in Marigold Court in the Strand is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examts. Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Lather is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of July 1789 } Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth. Jones Mark
