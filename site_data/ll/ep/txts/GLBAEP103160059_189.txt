Middlesex ss}  Ann Hill the Wife of Thomas Hill who is lately gone to America maketh oath that her Said husband has informed her & Which She believes to be true that he was duly bound Apprentice to one James Sparkes a Shoveller for the Term of Seven years & that he was afterwards assigned over to Joseph Sparkse served the last Two years & half of such Apprenticeship with his said Master Joseph Sparkes in Cartwright Square in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents knowledge or belief her said husband hath not since lived as a hired Servant for a year or rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a Child named Edward  aged about Fifteen months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this 10. day of April 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

Ann Hill
