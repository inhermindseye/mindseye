Middx  The Examination of Sarah Breech ye. Wife of Jno. Breech Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. County of Middx Taken ye. 18th. Day of Decr. 1739 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her sd. Husband Jno. Breech Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Bacon Bedsteadmaker in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & hath been out of his time abt. 6 Years And further upon her Oath saith that he hath not since he left his sd. Master Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife That her said Husbd. is now on Board his Majesty's Ship the Tyger & that she hath one Child (to wit) Susanah aged near Six Years & she being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe & Child

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Sarah Breech
