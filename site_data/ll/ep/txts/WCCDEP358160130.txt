Middx The Examination of Elizth. Fooron  Spinster Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 26th. Day of April 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that she was Born in White hart Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . that she Cannot give any acct. of the Settlemt. of either her Father or Mother but saith yt. her Father & Mother both Dyed in the Workhouse belonging to the sd. Parish of St. Martin in the Fields and yt. she this Examint. was in the same Workhouse same time in the Year 1733 she at such time not being Capable of maintaining herselfe And this Examint. further upon her Oath saith that she never Rented £10 a Year was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That she is now Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas: Gronous

Elizabeth Fooron
