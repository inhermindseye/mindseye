Middlesex to wit Mary Woodward Aged about Twenty Nine Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House paid taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her late Father Simon Woodward Rented and lived in a House in New In Passage many Year at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds & where this Examinant was born as she believes And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one William West a Journeyman Carpenter who lately worked in St: Giles's but with whom this Examinant cannot set forth had Carnal Knowledge of her Body some time about last Christmas and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant as aforesaid and that the said William West and no other person did beget the same & that his is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 15th. day of Novr. 1770 before me W Kelyngs

Mary Woodward
