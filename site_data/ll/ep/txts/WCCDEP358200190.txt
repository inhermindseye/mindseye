Westmr  Mary Arrance Aged about Twenty Eight Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Vandenbergh in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted said Service about One Month & hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes

Sworn the Day of 176 Before}
