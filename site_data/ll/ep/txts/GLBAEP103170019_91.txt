Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Ellsop  Singlewoman Maketh oath That She was lately hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with James Fortune of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Taylor at Certain yearly Wages and Continued in Such Service for the Space of one year & three quarters & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the sd parish & has not Since done any Act to gain anothr Settlement

Sworn this 23d day of october 1776 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Eleanor Ellsop
