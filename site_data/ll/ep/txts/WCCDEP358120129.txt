Middx  John Ranson aged abt. 64 Years upon Oath Saith he rented a house of the yearly Rent of £12 in Kings head Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . lived therein upwds. of one Year and quitted the same about Nine Years ago and has not since rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 24th June: 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

John Ransom
