Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Fellows maketh oath that about eighteen years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with Mr. Crispe a Victualler in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County and Continued in Such Service under such hiring for about the Space of one year & half That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to her Servitude as aforesaid

Sworn this 24. day of June 1788 before  R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Fellows
