Middx ss  Mary Maring  Widow of William Maring Maketh oath that abt. Eleven Years ago she was hired Servant by the Year to Paul Marsh at the Sign of the White Bear in ye parish of St. Dunstan in the East London with whom she lived upwards of a Year & reced five Pounds by ye Year Wages And hath not Done any Act Whereby to gain any Subsequent Settlemt And that she was never Married Nor a hired Servt- by ye Year yn as aforesd

Sworn this 26th. day of Novr. 1750 before us Richd. Ricards Saml Gower

The Mark of Mary Maring
