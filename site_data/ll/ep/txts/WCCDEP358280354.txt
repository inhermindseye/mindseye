St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } (to wit) Mary Johnson  Singlewoman Aged twenty years & upwds. Upon her Oath saith that on the Seventh day of December Instant she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to this parish and which is now chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes And this Deponent saith that one Joseph Brassing an Apprentice to Mr Thomas Huxley of fountain Court in the parish of St Clement Danes is the only Real & true father thereof he having Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body & begotten sometime in the Month of March last & at several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid thereon And this Deponent further saith that no other person except & besides the said Joseph Joseph Brassington hath ever had had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponent's Body and that be she is now & hath been ever since the fifth day of December Instant Maintained in the said Workhouse of the parish of St Clement Danes at the Sole Expence of the Said parish

Sworn the 13th day of December 1791 before me} R Butler

her Mary Johnstone mark

Joseph Brassington is a short thin Man light Complexion, Brown Hair tied behind generally at his Master's till 7 in the Even'g when he usually goes to the Plough in Beaufort Buildings

Warrt made out given to Overseer this Examn & Warrt. by Mr Richardson's Order
