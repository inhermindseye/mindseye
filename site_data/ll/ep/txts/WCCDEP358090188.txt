Middx to wit Mary Parker Aged about Sixty one Years on her Oath says She is the Widow of Thomas Parker with whom She Intermarried at the Fleet London in the year 1745 and by whom She hath no Children living says her said Husband was a West Countryman as he has been Informed but from what Parish or Place She does not know nor did her Husband ever Acquaint her thereof neither does She know  where her Husband was born or did he ever gain a Settlement by Renting House paying Taxes or Otherwise howsoever says before her Intermarriage with her said late Husband and whilst She was a Singlewoman She lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Lewis Smart in Holbourn in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Thirty five Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of £10 a year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 5th. day of Janry. 1781 before R Butler David Walker

Mary Parker
