Middx  Sarah Armstrong  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that she was bound an Apprentice to and served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mrs. Eliz: Cornish in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above Barrs in the  County of Middx  Milliner that since her sd. Apprentisp she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 28th. Day of October 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Sarah Armstrong
