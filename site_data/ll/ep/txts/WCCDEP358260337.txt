Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Ann Kates on behalf of Martha Randall an Infant aged about Fourteen years Maketh Oath and saith that she is the legitimate Child of William Randall who kept a House in Charles Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that after he quitted Said house he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that the said Martha Randall never did any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Settlement in her and own Right and Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 20th. day of Septr. 1798 before us N Bond Wm Goodenough

her Ann Kates Mark

Witness H Day Beadle
