Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Mary Simcock aged about 41 Years upon her Oath saith that on or about the 23rd. day of June 1794 she Intermarried with George Simcock her late Husband who has been dead about Ten Months at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Lambeth That her said late Husband informed this Examinant that he was born in Bennetts Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes where his father kept house for several Years and that he never did any Act in his own right to gain any other Settlement And this Deponent further saith that she hath Three Children living by the said George Simcock Vizt. Mary Ann Simcock born on or about the 25th. day of January 1789 at the house of a Mr. Munday in Bennetts Court in the Strand  George born on or about the Third day of January 1791 at No 5 Bennetts Court in the Strand aforesaid and John aged about Ten Months born in Gardiners Lane Westmr .

Sworn this 20th. day of Novr. 1795 before me W Kinnard

her Mary Simcock Mark
