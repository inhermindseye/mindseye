Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Hannah Jordan aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the Tenth day of February 1794 she Intermarried with Francis Jordan her late Husband who has been dead about Five weeks at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the fields by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt Jane aged about Three years and Thomas aged about Twenty Months That her said late Husband lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Caddick in Piccadilly in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for near Two years but what wages she had she this Deponent doth not know that after he quitted said Service he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement - and she it now Chargable to the parish of Saint Clements danes

Sworn this 13th. day of Augt. 1798 before us  W Bond W Carter

her Hannah Jordan Mark
