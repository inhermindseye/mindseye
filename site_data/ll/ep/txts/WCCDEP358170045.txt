Middx  The Examination of Rebecca Friend James Read on the Behalfe of Wm. Wyatt a Lunatick in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 4th- Day of May 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that Wm. Wyatt the Father of the sd. Lunatick lived in & rented a House in the Parish of St. Ann Blackfryers of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds for the Space of Two Years as this Examinant has been informed & beleives & further saith that the sd. Lunatick never was an Apprentice rented Ten Pounds a Year pd Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place as the sd. to this Lunatick hath informed this Examinant when Examinant knowledge or beleife in his Senses

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous W Hayton

James Read
