Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Winifred Arthur aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Moore at Stoke Newington in the Parish of Hornsey in the County of Middlesex for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Five years ago since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act Matter or Thing to gain Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of June 1788 }
