Westmr.  Mary Garman aged about 22 Years Upon Oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Seymour a Maltster in the Parish of Ewell in the County of Surry for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds, quitted said Service about three Years & hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 11th, day of October 1766 , before} Fran. Cary  Thos. Kynaston

Mary Garman
