Middlesex ss  Margaret Vance the widow of Joseph Vance deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her That he was born in the North of Ireland And this Depont. doth not know nor was ever informd nor Can in any manner setforth whether he ever did any Act to gain a settlement in any Part of Great Britain And this Depont. Saith that divers Years Ago & when a single woman she was h ired by; & lived as a hired servant by the Year, at the yearly Wages of four Pound with one Richard Rook who kept the sign of the Wheat Sheaf & Star in New Gravel Lane in the Parish of St. Paul Shadwell in the said County of Middlesex & Continues in such service in the said Parish for a year & upwards & by Means thereof as She is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that she has not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement subquent to her Said servitude Save and Except what she might gain or Acquire by her Marriage with her Said late husband whose Place of settlement is altogether Unknown to her

Sworn this day of 1767 before}
