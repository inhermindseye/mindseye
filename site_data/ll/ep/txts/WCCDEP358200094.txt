Middx  Ann Jackson  Singlewoman Aged about Fifty Years Upon Oath Says she was never Married Says that on the Sixteenth Day of June 1754 she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child at Mr Edwards a Tallow Chandler in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Says One Thomas Jackson a Shoemaker did beget the said Child and that the said Thomas Jackson has been Dead about four Years & this Examinant being Poor is unable to Maintain the said Child.

Sworn the 26th Day of March 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Ann Jackson

Upon Mr Jones Complt Ovr J. W
