Middx.  Amy Andrews aged upwds. of 40 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Carswell a Perukemaker in Witch Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of £5 for Seven Years or thereabouts Quitted her sd. Service about twelve Years ago and has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly servant for one Year and this Examinant further maketh Oath that upwds. of 11 Yrs. since she was delivered of a female bastd. Child (since baptized Priscilla ) at Mr Ellitts in King Street in the parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of middx.

Sworn Novr. 8th. 1753  beofre } Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Andrews
