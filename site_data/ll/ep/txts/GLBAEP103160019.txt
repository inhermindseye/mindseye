Middlesex ss  Mary Sherrard the widow of Thomas Sherrard deced Maketh oath that divers Years ago her Said late husband rented a house in Rose & Crown Court Rosemary Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd. County & was charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a subsequent settlement & that She this Deponent since his death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself.

Sworn this first day of March 1776 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Sherrard
