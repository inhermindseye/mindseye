Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Allen aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Treble in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Sixteen Month or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Eleven years since which time she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby she Might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath Saith That she hath Four illegitimate Children living Vizt. Sarah aged Six years or thereabouts Josiah aged Three years and an half Mary aged Two years or thereabouts and John aged Six Months or thereabouts all born at No. 36 Seacoal Lane in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre within in the City of London all of whom are now with this Deponent in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that she is not willing to Part with any of them until they Respectively Attain their Ages of Seven years

Sworn this 9th day of Novr. 1798 before us }

her Ann Allen Mark
