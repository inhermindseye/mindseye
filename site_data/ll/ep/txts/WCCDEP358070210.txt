Westmr. to wit Hester Channing Aged about Sixty Three Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with her late Husband Robert Chaning at May Fair Chapel the 10th. of Decr. 1752 and by whom she hath no Children now living Says that before her Intermarriage with her said late Husband he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Brown an Attorney in Clements Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Seven pounds as she believes and after quitting his said last mentioned Service he never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 17th. day of Septr. 1774 R. Butler

Hester Chaning
