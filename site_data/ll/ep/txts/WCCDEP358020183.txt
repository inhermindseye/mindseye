Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Talboys one of the Beadle of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Cordelia Duncombe Benedictor an Infant aged about Two years and an half Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been credibly Informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Cordelia was born a Bastard at the house of a Mr. Stoney a Carpenter in Temple Lane in the precinct of White Fryars in the City of London

Sworn this 21st day of Decr. 1789 R Butler D Walker

Jas Talboys
