Middx & Westmr-}  John Greenwood one of the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx on behalf of William and Rebecca Davis Infants by John Davis the Father on his Oath says that on searching the Exaimcon & pass Warrant Books find by an Order under the hands and Seals of Jass Clitherow and Jno- Drinkwater  Esqr two of his Matys Justices of the Peace for the Co- of Middx bearing date the 9th- Day of November 1776 whereby Elizth- Webb  singlewoman was passed with her then four Children vizt- John aged six Years & upwds- born a Bastard in the Parish of Chelsea in the sd. Co- of Middx  William aged four Years and eight Months Rebecca aged two Years and a quarters both born Bastards in the Parish of Richmond in the County of Surry and Philip aged about six Months since deced born a Bastard in the parish of Isleworth And this Deponent further says that the said Elizabeth Webb and the Father of the said Bastard Children have both deserted them as this. Deponent has been credibly informed and verily believes

Sworn this 14th- day of Augt- 1777 before R. Butler M Addington

John Greenwood
