Middx ss  Benjamin Pickard Maketh oath that about Twelve Years ago he was bound apprentice to his Uncle Edward Pickard a Baker of the parish of St. Dunstan in the West in the City of London With whom he lived upwards of three Years by Means Whereof this Depont. (as he apprehends) Acquired a legal Settlement in the sd. parish of St Dunstan in the West London And this Depont. Says he has not Since gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn before us this 25th day of October 1750  Richd. Ricards  Saml Gower

Benjamin Pickard
