Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Mybuss aged about 66 years upon her Oath saith that about 50 years ago she Intermarried with John Mybuss her late Husband at the Fleet London That he hath been dead about Twenty three years That her said late Husband informed this Examinant that he Rented and lived in a house in Bennetts Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months and upwards and pain Ten Pounds a year for same and all Taxes and that after he quitted said House he never did any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1795 . } Bond W Barnard

his Mary Mybuss Mark

Witness B Newman
