Middx  Eliza. Latter upon her Oath saith she is the Wido . of John Latter Says she intermarried with the said John Latter on the 27th. Day of June 1739 at the Fleet London that he hath been dead about five Weeks Says her said late Husband lived in and rented a house in the Parish of Epsom in the County of Surry and paid his Share of all Parochiall Taxes & Served the Office of Headborough in the said Parish and hath left same about Eight Years Says her sd. late Husband hath not since he left the said House rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochiall Office lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 19th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Elebeth Hatter
