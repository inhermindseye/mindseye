Middlesex ss}  Mary Cotton the Widow of George Cotton deced maketh oath That She has been Credibly informed by Letter received from Randle Brooke of Malpas in Chesshire and Which information She believes to be true That her said late husband died possessed of a Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Seven pounds & ten Shillings Situate in the Township of Maceton in the Parish of Malpas in the County of Chester which he held for Term of his Life by Lease from Lord Cholmondeley with whom he lived as a Servant for many years in the Township of Cholmondely in the said Parish of Malpas And that by means thereof as She this Deponent is advised & informed he gained a legal Settlement in the said Parish of Malpas and this Deponent Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said late husband did not do any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 15th day of august 1774 B Camper

The Mark of Mary Cotton
