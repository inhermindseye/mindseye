Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Long a singlewoman maketh oath That Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year at the yearly Wages of Four Pounds with one Mr. Moreer a Notary Publick in the Parish of St. Martin Outwich alias Oleswich in the City of London & Continued in such service for the Space of one year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there and this Deponent Saith that she hath not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 10 day of April 1773 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Elizth. Long .
