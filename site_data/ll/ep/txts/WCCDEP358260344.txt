Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sophia Stildolfe aged abou 14 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months ever bound an Apprentice That her father William Stildolfe Rented and lived in a house at Bromley in the Parish of Bromley in the County of Kent for Four years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 2nd. day of Octr. 1798 before us }  E Read

Stidolfe
