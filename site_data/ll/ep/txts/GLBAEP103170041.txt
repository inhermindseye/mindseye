Middlesex ss} The Examination of Alice Storey the wife of Thomas Storey late of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Gunsmith taken on oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County one Where of is of the Quorum and both residing in the Limits of the Parish Church within the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate aforesd. in the sd County this Seventh day of January 1777 Who Saith That about ten years & eight months ago She this Examinant was lawfully married to her Said husband Thomas Storey who left this Examinant about four years & two Months ago & went from London to reside at Kingston in Jamaica That She this Examinant hath never Seen him Since nor hath he had any Access to or Carnal knowledge of her Body Since he left her as aforesaid nor doth She know or believe nor hath She ever been informed Whether he hath ever been in any part of Great Britain or Ireland Since his departure from her as aforesaid nor doth She know Whether he is now living or dead That about nineteen Months ago one James Fleming  a Butcher who now resides in the Said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate but who then lodged in the house of this Examinants Father Adam Beale in Little Grays Inn Lane Holborn in the Sd County where this Examinant also then lived did prevail with this Examt to Suffer him the Sd James Fleming to he with her & to have Carnal knowledge of her Body where by She Conceived with child of a Male Bastard child of

which She was delivered in the Workhouse belonging to & Situate in the Said parish of St Botolph without Aldgate on the tweltth day of April now last past the which child hath been Since Captised & registred by the name of William Storey & hath been & Still is likely to be further Chargeable to the Said parish of St Botolph with out Aldgate She this Examinant being Poor & not able to provide for the Same That the Said James Fleming is the true & only father of the Said child that this Examination is Voluntary & that She hath not been Compelled to be examined by any person or persons whomsoever.

Taken and Signed the day & year first above written before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of  Alice Storey
