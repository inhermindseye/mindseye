Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Gerrett  Singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages by & with one Samuel Nichols in Brick Lane in the Parish of Christ Church in the Sd County Silk Throwster & Continued in Such Service for the Space of two years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She has act Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been married nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 11. day of October 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

El. Gerrett
