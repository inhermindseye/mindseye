Westmr.  Winifred Alie upon Oath saith she is the Wife of Mathw. Alie that her sd. husband & herself are both Natives of Ireland & never gained a Settlemt. in England and that the sd. Mathw. Alie (as she hath been informed & believes) Served his Apprentiship with a Taylor in Turlis in the County of Tipper in Ireland afsd. & never rented ten pounds a Year pd. parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Feby. 5th. 1753 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Winifred Alie
