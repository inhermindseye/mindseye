Middx & Westmr} to wit Jane Forshall aged about 23 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for One Year says Father Rented & lived in a House in Crown Court Temple Bar for sevl. years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted the said House upwards of 18 years ago since which time she hath gained no other Settlmt. And further on Voluntary Oath says on the 13th. day of March 1778 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Blanchard Wilkins in Houghton Street Clare Markett which was so on afterwards Baptized by the Name of William And that she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable maintain the same when born And further says that one Wm. Cook a Chimney Sweeper is the father of the above named Bastard Child as also the Child she is nor pregnant off and that the sd. Wm. Cook & no other person did beget the same & that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 5th. day of Octr. 1782  Wm Kitchiner

Jane Forshall
