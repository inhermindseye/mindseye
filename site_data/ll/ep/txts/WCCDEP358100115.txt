Lewis Aged about Twenty two years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship Says he lived a hired yearly Servt . with one. Mr. Rock an Apothecary in Hatton Street in the Liberty of Saffron Hill  Hatton Garden in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn for one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas besides Diet Lodging & Cloaths hath quitted his said Service upwards of Twelve Months ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of August 1783  Sampn Wright  Thos: Gilbert
