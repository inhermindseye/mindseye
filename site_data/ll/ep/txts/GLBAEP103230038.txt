Middlesex ss  James Burgin maketh oath that his late son James Burgin deced was heretofore hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant by & with a Stationer whose name he has forgot in the Parish of st Catherine Cree in the City of London & Continued in Such Service under such hiring for the space of one year & three quarters & has not since done any Act to gain a Settlement for himself That he left behind him at his Death a Son & lawful Issue aged about nine years & nine Months by his widow  Margaret Burgin which Child has done no act to gain a Settlement for himself- note this person after being examined as above went with a Letter.
