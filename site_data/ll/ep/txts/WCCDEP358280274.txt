Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Reynolds aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Reeves a Colourman in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Seven Weeks and further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Inwood an Apprentice to sd. Mr. Reeves is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is so pregnant And that the said John Inwood is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 31st. day of March 1790 } Wm Kitchiner

her Ann Reynolds Mark
