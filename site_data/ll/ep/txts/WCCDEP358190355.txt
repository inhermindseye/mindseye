Middx  Mary Frater Aged abt 20 Years Upon Oath Says she was never Marryed and that she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mrs. Crown in Craven Buildings in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx Upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds quitted her Said Service about One Year and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert.

Sworn the 4th Day of June 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Frater
