Middx  Margarett Hind upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman and was never married and that she was bound an Apprentice to & served near seven Years Apprentisipp with one Mr. Thos. Elliot in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & still Continues to live as a Servt . with the sd. Mr. Elliott & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which when born will be a Bastard or Bastards and likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she not being able to maintaine the same when born & that one Chas. Clark a Servt . to one Mr. Lampton in Lincolns Inn did begett the sd. Child or Children on her Body she this Examinant she took a Walk abt- Nine Weeks ago with the sd. Chas. Clark to St. Pancras Church & Comeing backover the Fields he the sd. Thos. Clark laid this Examinant down upon the Grass & had Carnall Knowledge of her Body at when Time he got her with the sd. Bastard Child with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Chas. Clark & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 30th- of Septr- 1741 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Margt. Hind
