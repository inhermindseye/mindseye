Middx The Examination of Ann Gardiner  Singlewoman taken on Oath before us two of his Majestys Justices of the peace for the County of Middx (whereof one is of the Quorum) and both residing in or next to the Limmitts of the parish Church of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County) this 26th. day of July in the year of our Lord 1744

This Examinant Ann Gardiner Maketh Oath and Saith that in or about the latter end of the Month of

March which was in the year of our Lord 1742 George Denniston of Cammomile Street in St. Mary Ax  Taylor Did Prevail with this Examinant to suffer him to Lye with her at the House of Mr . John Gardiner in Burr Street in the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in Middx Chad Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body several times whereby She Conceived with Child of a Male Bastard Child of which She was Delivered at her Lodgings in Swan Alley in the said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate on the 30th. day of Decr. which was in the year of our Lord 1742 which said Male Bastard Child hath been Baptized by the name of George and hath been now is and Still is likely to be Chargeable to the said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate this Examinant being poor and not able to Provide for the same and this Examinant further saith that the said George Denniston is the true and only father of the said Male Bastard Child and that this Examination is Voluntary

Sworn this 31st- day of July 1744 Before Robert Dennett  Saml Gower

her Ann Gardiner Mark
