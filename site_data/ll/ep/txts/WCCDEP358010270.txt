Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Jane Norman of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes aforesaid upon her Oath Saith that Mary, Ann Norman a poor Child who in now in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes is the Child of Jeffry Norman by living his first Wife and that the place of the legal Settlement of the said Jeffry Norman the father is in the Parish of Saint Mary Newington in the County of Surry he having rented a House of the yearly Value of Sixteen Guineas in that Parish and lived therein serve two years Since when he hath with done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement & this Examinant further Saith that the said Mary Ann Norman is about four years old, that her Father hath deserted her and left her with this Deponent and that She this Examinant is not able to maintain her

Sworn this 26th. day of Febry 1789  before Sampn Wright N Forster}

Jane Norman
