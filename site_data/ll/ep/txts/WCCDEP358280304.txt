St Clement Danes

Middlesex  Mary Harvey Wife of David Harvey a Soldier now in His Majestys Service but she knows not where maketh Oath and saith that upwards of Eight years ago she was lawfully married to her said Husband David Harvey at the Parish Church of St Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex And saith that it appears to her this Examinant by an Indenture of Apprenticeship Dated the tenth Day of October 1754 which is now in her Custody that her said Husband was bound Apprentice to Richard Sheffield of the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields aforesaid in the said County of Middlesex  Cordwainer for the Term of Seven Years And saith that she hath been informed by her said Husband and which she verily believes to be true that he did actually live with and duly serve his said Master for the full Term of seven years and he lived all that time with his said Master in New Street in the said Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the said County of Middlesex And since the Expiration of his said Apprenticeship he hath never rented a Tenement or Lodging of so much Rent as Ten Pounds a Year paid any Parish or other Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever which could acquire him a Settlement in any Parish or Place

Sworn the 29th. Day of November 1790 } Philip Dyot D Walker

Mary Harvey
