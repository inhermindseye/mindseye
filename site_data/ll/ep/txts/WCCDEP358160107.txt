Middx  The Exaicon of Diana Burrows  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Who upon her Oath Saith That she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Edwd. Jerningham  Esqr . in Featherston Buildings in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above Barrs in the sd. County for the Space of one Yeare & three Quarters at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds four Shillings and hath been come away from her sd. Service abt. four Years And further upon her Oath Saith that she never rented any House or since that time lived with any Person as a yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledged or beleife and that she is now Sick & Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the 5th. day of Febry 1739 before us}
