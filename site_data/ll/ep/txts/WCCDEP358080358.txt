Westmr. to wit John Pierce Aged about Twenty Eight Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to his late Wife at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes about Nine Years Ago and by whom he hath Two Children Living Namely Anna Maria Aged about four Years and Mary Aged about Two Years Says he rented and lived in a House the Corner of Wooden Street Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for Three Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of forty four Pounds hath quitted the said House upwards of Seven Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 29th. day of May 1779 before R Butler M Addington

John Pierce
