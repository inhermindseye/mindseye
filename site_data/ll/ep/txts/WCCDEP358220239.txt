Westmr.  Catherine Bamford Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Harding a Painter who lived in a House in Charles Street wch. by Act of Parliament belongs to the Parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of forty Shillings hath quitted her sd Service about Ten Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 19th. day of February 1771 before Us} Thos. Kynaston  Petr Planck

her Cathrine Bamford Mark

Wittness Fras Bishop
