Middx  Chas. Carpenter aged abt. Sixty Years upon his Oath saith that upwds- of Fourteen Years ago he lived in & rented a House in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds for the Space of Twenty Years or thereabt. & that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 8th- Day of Septr- 1741 before}
