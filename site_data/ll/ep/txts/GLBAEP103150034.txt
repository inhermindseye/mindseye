Middlesex ss}  Samuel Byton maketh oath That divers Years Ago he was duly bound Apprentice by the Parish officers of the Parish of Dola Zouch in the County of Leicester to one John Nichols of the Parish of Appleby in the same County Taylor & Served two Years of his Apprenticeship in the said parish of Appleby & then left his said Master & did not afterwards return to him & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15 day of May 1775 before B Camper

Samuel Byton
