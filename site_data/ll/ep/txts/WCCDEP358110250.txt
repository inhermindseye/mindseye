Middx Rose Page upon Oath Saith she is the Wife of Job Page by whom She has one Child (viz) Mary aged 10 Weeks & that she has been Informed & verily believes her sd. husband rented a house & pd. Sevt & Lot for Several Years in Foregate Street in the New Church Parish at Worcester & has not since he quitted the sd. house Rented ten Pounds a year Paid Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn May 26th 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Rose Page

When she married Page she was the Widow of Edwd. Williams who dyed upwds. 6 yrs. ago man to him in Christchurch Surry abt. 9 yrs. he Ser his time wth. his own father Jno. Williams somewhere war the Old Bailey she  at Stimridge in Hertfords. where her father rented house upd. Scot & Lot. Williams left her wth. 2 Childn
