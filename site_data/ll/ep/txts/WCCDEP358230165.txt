Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Sayer Aged about 53 years upon her Oath That on the 24th day of December 1787 she intermarried with John her present Husband (who hath deserted her) at the Parish Church of Saint Thomas Salisbury That her said Husband served seven years appren- ticeship to a Mr Sanders a Brush Maker Snow Hall in that part of the Brush of St Sepulchre which hath in the City of London That since he hath been out of his time he hath not done any act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 27th day of Novr 1800 before us } Saml Edwards  John Collick
