Middlesex ss  Margaret Mortimer maketh oath that in the year 1773 She this Deponent was bound Apprentice to Mary Whiteman of the Butcher Row Lower East Smithfield in the Precinct of St Catherine in the County of Middlesex  Widow by the Parish officers of St George Middlesex untill She Should Attain her full Age of Twenty one Years or day of Marriage That this Depont. duly Served her Sd Mistress under the sd Indre in the Said Precinct of St Catherine for the Space of Three years and upwards & her sd Mistress dying She was there upon discharged from her sd Apprenticeship & her Indre delivered up That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was She ever Married nor has She done any Act to gain another Settlement

Sworn this 19. day of April 1780 before  Jasper Clarke  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Margaret Mortimer
