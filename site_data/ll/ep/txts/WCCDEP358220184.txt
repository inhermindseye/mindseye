Middx & Westmr}  Mary Fox Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father who was is by Trade a Bird Cage maker Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Thomas Greenaway a Bird Cage maker the Corner of Gilt Spur Street in the Parish of St. Sepulchres in the City of London and after her said Father was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn this 13th. day of Octr. 1770 before Us R. Butler W Kelyngs

Mary Fox
