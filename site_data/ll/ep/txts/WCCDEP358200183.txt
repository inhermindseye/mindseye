Middx  Wm- Blackett Aged about 45 Years upon Oath Says he Lived a Hired Yearly Sert with Mr Jos: Joyce a Goldsmith & Jeweller at No 19 Craven Buildings in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Ten Years at the Yearly Wages of Thirty five Pounds Meat Drink & Lodging hath quitted said Service about fifteen Years he being all that time a Singleman without Child or Children & hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 15th of Decr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

Wm: Blackett
