Middlesex ss}  Susannah Dunkley  Singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with William Muscrop in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Victualler at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of one year & half And has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a settlement since such her Servitude as aforesaid.

Sworn this Third day of Decemr 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Susannah Dunkley
