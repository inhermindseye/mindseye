Middlesex ss }  Elizabeth Wells a Single woman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Isminster a Victualler in Lower East Smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two years & upwards & has not Since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this. 8. day of October 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Wells
