Middlesex ss  James Frith maketh oath that he lately rented a house at the Yearly rent of Ten Pounds in the Parish of Saint Leonard in Shoreditch in the Said County & resided thereon for the Space of Six Months That he has  Since rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath a wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 29. day of September 1787 before  R Davies  Robt: Smith

James Frith
