Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Mary Eaton aged about 31 years upon her oath saith that she never was married that on the 20th. day of December last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Thos . That one Thomas Teasdale who is a Sailor on board his Majestys Ship the Sandwich is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Robert Teasdale is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Janry. 1788 } Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Eaton Mark
