Middx  The Examination of Mary Jackson  Widw . of George Jackson Taken the 20th- Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. Geo: Jackson her late Husbands Father was bound Apprentice to one Mr. Thos. Jackson late of the Parish of St. Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx  Cordwainer & Served him as an Apprentice for the Space of 7 Years And this Examinant further saith yt- the sd. Geo: Jackson in his life Time never rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year neither did this Examinants sd. late Husband Geo. Jackson or was an Apprentice or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt- in any other Place as her sd. late husband inform'd her & which she verily believes & that she is now Poor Sick & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Jackson
