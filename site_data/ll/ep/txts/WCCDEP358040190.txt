Westmr.  Eliz: Vincent Wido. of Thomas Vincent who dyed about three months since upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Thomas Vincent upwds. of 27 Yrs. ago in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn London Says her sd. late husband was by Indenture dated the 16th. Day of June 1715 bound Apprentice to Philip Brady a Baker & Served Seven Years Apprentiship with the sd. Philip Brady in Maypole Alley in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says her sd. late husband never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 20th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz Vincent
