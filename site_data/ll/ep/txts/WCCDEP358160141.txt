Middx  The Examination of Frances Bilboa  Wido . aged 57 Years. Taken the 23th. Day of June 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that upwds. of three Years ago her late Husband Jno. Bilboa Rented a House in Starr Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £13 and lived in the same for the space of two Months & there Dyed And this Examint. further saith that after the Death of her sd. Husband she this Examint. Continued to live in & Rent the sd. House for the space of three Years, And further upon her Oath saith yt. her Landlord turned her out of the said House about one Month ago And Yt. she has a Son Named Robt . aged 21 Years who is not capable of Maintaining himselfe her Neither is she this Examint. capable of maintaining herselfe being Poor & Infirm

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Samuel Clarke

Frances Bilboa
