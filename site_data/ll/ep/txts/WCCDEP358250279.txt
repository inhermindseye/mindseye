Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Shrieve aged about 74 years upon his Oath saith that about Thirty years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present Wife at the parish Church of Saint George in the East That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Ship Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about Four years at the yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and upwards and paid Taxes due for the same That he hath quitted said House about Eight years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of February 1797 before me } John Collick

Willm. Shrieve
