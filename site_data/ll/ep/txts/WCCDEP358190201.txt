Middx   Magdalen Mackenzie aged about 25 Years Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middx Upon her Voluntary Oath Says she was Born in Scotland that she never was Married was an apprentice or hired Servant for one year That she was on the Second day of this instant November 1761 Delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Watson Watchmaker near Temple Bar in the aforesaid Parish of Saint Clement Danes (which has not yet been Baptized) and that the Same is yet living and likely to become Chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the Same And that Dougal Mackenly a Carpenter who lodges at an Alehouse known by the Sign of the Man in the Moon at whitechapel did beget such Male Bastard Child he having had carnal Knowledge of her Body in or about the Month of February last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget on her Body the Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the 14th. day of Novemr. 1761 Before  Fran: Bedwell B. Hammond

Magdalen Mcknzie
