Westmr.  Elizabeth Masterman aged about 21 Years lodging in Crown Court Butcher row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster Upon Oath says she has been inform'd and believes she was born in flower de luce Court in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London and never Rented house paid Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born, says that Thomas Axford a Grocer in Blackmoor Street Claremarket in the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes had carnal knowledge of her body Sometime in the Month of Septr. 1765, and at several other times since; at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Thomas Axford and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn March 5th, 1766 ; Before} Frans Cary

The Mark of Elizth: Masterman
