Middx  Jane Shelley  Wido . Removed from the Parish of St. George Hanover Square by Order Dated the 21st: Day of June 1745 upon her Oath saith she intermarried with her late Husband  William Shelley about Seventeen Years since at the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn & the sd. Wm. Shelley hath been dead about 1 Yr Says her sd. late Husbd. lived in & rented a house in Vear Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Seven Pounds for the Space of 5 Yrs. & upwds. & hath left the same abt: 10 Yrs. since which time the sd. Wm Shelley in his Life time or this Examint. since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne June 22 1745 before}

The Mark of Jane Shelley
