Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster .} to wit John Porter of No. 19 Vere Street Clare Market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes (aged 26 or thereabouts) Taylor For and on the behalf of William Taylor Porter his Father who is Insane and unable to An for himself maketh Oath and Saith That His said Father about Eight Years ago took a House in Middle Row at the End of Broad Street  Saint Giles's where he carried on the business of a Taylor and Salesman that he lived there for about 12 Months That he then took a House next adjoining of Mr. Snell a Cabinet Maker. wherein he caused in the Same business for about Ten Months That such called House was in Middle Row aforesaid, and that both the said Houses are in the Parish of Saint Giles's in the Fields and this deponent saith That he believes either of the said Houses are worth upwards of £20: per Annum and that he is Assured his Father must have Paid upwards of Ten Pounds a Year And this Deponent Saith That since his Father left the said Houses in Middle Row aforesaid He has never Rented £10 per Annum or done any Act Matter or Thing to the best of

this deponents knowledge or belief And whereby he may have gained a Settlement And this deponent Saith that his said Father, Resides with him at his this Deponents Lodging in a Garett No. 19 Vine Street aforesaid And that he is become within a few days now pass very insane and at Times Violent

Sworn this 8th. day of May 1795 before } Benj Tebbs  John Callick

John Porter
