Middx  Mary Cotterell upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of Saml. Cotterell : That the sd. Saml. Cotterell lived in & Rented a House in Chapel Street in the Parish of St. Ann within the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £16. & upwds- for the Space of 20 Yrs- & upwds- & hath left the same abt. Seven Yrs- that since he left the sd. House the sd. late Saml. Cotterell , in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath one Child viz: Ann aged near 3 Years & being Poor is not able to maintaine herself & Child

Sworne the 10th- Augt. 1743 before Thos DeVeil J Poulson

Mary Cotterell
