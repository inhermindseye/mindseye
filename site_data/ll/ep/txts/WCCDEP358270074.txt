Desertion

St Clement Danes & Augt. 8th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. (to wit) Rebecca Barnes aged 60 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband John Barnes on or about the second day of February 1754 at the parish Church of St George Bloomsbury that since their said Marriage her said Husband rented and resided in a House in Chaw Court Ship Yard in the parish of St Clement Danes about two years at the Annual rent of £12 and paid the Poor rate for the same that she he quitted that House about Seven or Eight years ago and hath never Since done any act or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Deponents knowledge Information or Belief and this Deponent farther saith that she her said Husband rented has refused to maintain her or allow any thing for her her support not withstanding he is of Bad is well able so and of Bodily of Strength and ability so to do as she this Deponent verily believe

Sworn the 8th Augst 1792 before me R Butler D Walker

Rebecca Barnett mark
