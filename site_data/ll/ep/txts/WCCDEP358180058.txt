Middx  Martha Sweatman upon her Oath saith that she is the Widow of Roger Sweatman who hath been dead about one Year that her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to & served Two Years & one half the First Part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. John Swallow in the Parish of Castle Cary -in the County of Somersett  Mason & then his said Master broke & her said late Husband never was Assigned over to any other Person as this Examinants sd. late Husband hath Informed her that her said late Husbd. in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise Gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath one Child  Joseph aged 12 Years & being Poor is not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworne the 19th. Janry 1742 before} Chas. Egerton

Martha Swetman
