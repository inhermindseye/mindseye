Middlesex  June Chapman Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Mylne of Arrundel Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Three Weeks ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes And Further upon her Oath says that on the second Day of November in the Year of our Lord One thousand seven hundred and sixty Nine she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Thomas Wilson in Burleigh Court in the parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Baptized by the Name of Anthony and that Anthony Coleman late a Servant with Mr. Mylne of Arrundel Street in the Strand is the true and Real Father of the said Male Bastard Child of which she was so Delivered as aforesaid.

Sworn this 21st day of Novr. 1770 before me} 

June Chapman
