Middx.  Elizth. Banson aged abt. Thirty Seven Years upon Oath Says She is the Wido. of White Banson  who has been Dead abt. five Months Says the said White Banson Rented a House in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes known by the Name of Sams Coffee house at the Yearly Rent of Fifty Pounds & lived therein one Year & hath quitted said House abt. two Years & never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Says She hath three Children by the said White Banson (to wit) Elizth. Henrietta aged abt. Six Years William aged abt. four Years & Charles Barley aged abt. Three Years

Sworn the 4th Octr. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Benson
