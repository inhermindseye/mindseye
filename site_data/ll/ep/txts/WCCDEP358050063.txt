Westmr.  Ann Beech  Wido . of Mathew Beech upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her said late husband in the Parish Church of Saint Olave Southwk. upwds. of 30 Years ago and that he has been dead upwds. 20 Years Says her sd. late husband was arhilad as a Clerk to Mr Wright an Attorney at Law and Served his Clarkship with him in Cliffords Jan in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the west London and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place and that she hath one Daughter by the said Mathw. Beach now at home with her viz Mary aged upwds. of 20 Years and that neither she (this Examinant) nor her sd. Daur Mary have every done any Act whereby to gain a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 26th 1749 before H Frilding

Ann Beech
