Westmr.  Sarah Gibbons aged about 33 Years upon Oath says she was never married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hamman's a Boxmaker in the Butcher row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds, hath quitted said Service about Seven Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, And further upon Oath says that on the 25th, day of March 1765, she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Ryley an Undertaker in Dirty Lane  Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been called baptized by the Name of Ralph

Sworn the 22d. day of Feby. 1766 , Before} Frans Cary  Paul Vaillant

Sarh Gibbons
