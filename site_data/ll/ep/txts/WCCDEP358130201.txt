Middx  Mary Mitchell aged abt. Fifty four Years upon Oath Says She never was Married & that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Edwd. Salmon a Painter for Twenty four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds Says She lived the three last Months of her sd. Service with the said Mr. Salmon in Little Carter Lane in the Parish of St. Gregory by St. Paul & there her said Master Dyed Says after the Burial of her sd. Master, Mrs. Salmon her Mistress, being Ill told this Examinant that She was left very Poor & could not Afford to pay this Examinant any Wages but hoped this Examinant would not leave her Saying She believed She should not live very long & this Examinant Consented to Continue & did live with her said Mistress untill the time of her Death which happened upwards of one Year after the Death of the said Mr. Salmon & lived with the said Mrs. Salmon in New Street Square in the Parish of St. Bridget Vulgarly St. Brides Londn. near one Year where her said Mrs. Dyed Says She hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Septr. 3d. 1756 before} Theodr. Sydenham  Barth: Hammond

The mark of Mary Mitchell
