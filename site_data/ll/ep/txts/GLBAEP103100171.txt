Middlesex ss}  Thomas Long maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound apprentice by Indenture to Richard Midwinter of the Parish of St. Bridget in the City of London Founder and Served the whole of his apprenticeship in thesd. Parish and by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that he never lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor ever Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or Pair any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement Subsequent to his sd apprenticeship

Sworn this 13th day of November 1769 before  Chris: Scott

Thos Long
