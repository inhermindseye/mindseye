Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Ann Loden aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Marrried Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice That she was born in Cork And further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that on the 28th. day of March 1793 she this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Archer No. 21 Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath since been Christened by the Name of Richard That one Richard Field an Engraver who lives in Goodman Yard in the Minories is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid thereof and that the said Richard Field is the only Real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 4th. 27th. day of July August 1797  J Hunter

her Mary Ann Loden Mark

Man never taken

Child named Richd. Loden Passed from Saint Mary Whitechapel

Admd. in the house  25th. Augt. 1797 Taken away by the Mother the 16th. Janry. 1798

Qrs. wher. any & what Allowance to the Mother
