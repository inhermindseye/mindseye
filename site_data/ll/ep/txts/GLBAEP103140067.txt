Middlesex ss}  Jane Robinson the widow of Thomas Robinson deced maketh oath that divers years ago her said late husband lived in & rented a house in Little Burr Street in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the same & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponents Knowledge or belief her said husband did not afterwards do any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this 25th day of November 1774 before B Camper

Ann Robin
