Middx  The Examination of William Brown Aged Sixteen Years a Vagabond apprehended in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex and brought before me one of his Majestys Justices of the peace for the sd. County of Middlesex aforesaid and taken before me this 13th. Day of December in the Year of our Lord 1744

This Examinant on his Oath Says That he hath one Brother named Rias aged Twelve Years and that he & his Brother were born in the parish of St. Sidwell in the City of Exeter in the County of Devon Says his Father & Mother are both Dead and cannott give any Account of there Settlemt. Says neither he this Examinant or his Brother  Rias were ever Apprentices or Yearly hired Servts. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne the Day & Year abovementioned before} Tho Lane

William Brown
