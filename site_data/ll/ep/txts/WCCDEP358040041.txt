Westmr.  Ann Grogan  Widow of Patrick Grogan upon Oath saith she intermarried with her sd. late husband at the Fleet London about 18 Years ago Says the sd. Patrick Grogan hath been dead upwards of two Years Says he lived in & rented a house in Houghton Strt. near Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £18 for three Years & upwards & left same abt. 5 Yrs. since Says her sd. husband after he quitted sd. house nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 30th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Grogan
