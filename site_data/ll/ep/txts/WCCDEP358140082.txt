Middx.  Ann Price aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thomas Price who hath been dead abt. two Years by whom she has two Children Elizth . aged 7 Years & Sarah aged abt 4 Years says her said late husband served his Apprenticeship with Richd. Kent a Fishmonger  without Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year says she hath not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn this 1st. of Febry 1758 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Price
