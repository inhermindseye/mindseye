Middx  Eliz. Spiller  Wido . of James Spiller Robert Winbolt and John Emmett and severally make Oath and first this Deponent Eliza. Spiller Says she inter- -married with her late Husband James Spiller at Little Ladys Church in the City of Canterbury about 36 Years since Says the sd. James Spiller never rented a house of ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office to her knowledge or beleif and this Dept. Robt. Winbolt for himself Saith that the sd. James Spiller was bound Apprentice to James Anselym  Citizen & Painter Stainer of London by Indenture dated the 1st. Day of April 1702 as appears by the binding Book of the sd. Company and this Deponent John Emmett for himself saith that the sd. James Spiller

lived with the sd. Mr. Anselym as an Apprentice for the Space of her Years or thereabouts in Crown Court in thread Needle Street in the Parish of St. Martin Outwich London

Sworn July 4th. 1746 before  Tho Lane  William Withers

Eliz Spiller Robt: Winbolts Juo: Emmett
