Middx  Jane Shelley  Wido . Removed from the Parish of St. George Hanover Square by Order Dated the 21st. Day of June last upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with her late Husband  William Shelley about Seventeen Years since at the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn & the sd. Wm. Shelley hath been Dead about one Year Says her sd. late Husband lived in & rented a house in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Seven Pounds for the Space of Five Years & upwards & hath left the same about Ten Years Says her sd. Husband (after he left the sd. house in Vear Street lived in & Rented a house in Lloyds Court near St. Giles Church in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the Sd. County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of £11. for the Space of 2 Years or thereabouts & hath left the same about 8 or 9 Years Says her sd. late Husband after he left the sd. house in Lloyds Court or this Examint. since his Death never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne July 9th. 1745 before} Wm. Jones The Mark of Jane Shelley
