Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Harris Aged upwards of Twenty one Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Goodwin at the Coach and Horses the Corner of Mill Street in Great Conduct Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex upwards of one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of five five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago since which time she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Yearly.

Sworn this 16th. day of Septr. 1776 before  John Croft  John Cox .

Mary Harris
