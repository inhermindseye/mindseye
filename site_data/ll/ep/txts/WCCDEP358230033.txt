Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Peter Williams and family who are Ill maketh Oath and Saith That he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Peter Williams Intermarried with Mary his present Wife upwards of Four Years ago at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square by whom he hath Two Children living Vizt. William aged upwards of Two Years and Mary aged Fifteen Months and upwards That the said Peter Williams Rented and lived in a house in Rathbone Place in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for Three Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Thirty two Pounds that he quitted said House about Nine Months since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Janry. 1799 - before us G S Goodenough Menter

Jas Talboys
