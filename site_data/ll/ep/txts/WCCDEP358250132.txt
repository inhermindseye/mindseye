Saint Clement Danes

Settlement at Castlecomb in Wilts to wit Mary Neal aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that about 12 years ago she Intermarried with Albert Neal at the Parish Church of Saint James Westminster That she hath Two Children living by her said Husband vizt. Deborah aged about 8 years and Roger aged about Nine Months That her said Husband has been dead about five Months That her said late Husband informed this Examinant that he Served Seven years Apprenticeship to Roger Neal his Father who was a Grazier at Castlecomb in the parish of Castlecomb in the County of Wilts and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as an Hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 27th. day of October 1794  M Addington J Mathew

Mary Neal
