Westmr. Margt Gahagan on behalf of Chas. an Infant Child aged upwards of three Yrs. upon Oath saith the sd. Charles is the Son of Mary Lawrance Deced by Arthur Fingless (who has absconded) as this Examint has been Informed and believes and that she hath been further Informed that the father and mother of the said Child were never married and that the said Inft. Child was born next Door to the Wheelwrights in Cross Lane  Long Acre in the parish of St. Martin in the fields in the County of Middlx

Sworn Augt. 17th. 1749 before H Frildin

N. Mrs. Gahagan lives within two Doors of the Golden Last Parkers Lane

The Mark of Margt. Gahagan
