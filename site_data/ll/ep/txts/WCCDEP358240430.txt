Saint Clement Danes Augt. 4. 1794

Settlement in St C D } Hannah Mayes at Mr. Knollers House Painter Noble Street  Foster Lane  Cheapside Singlewoman being examined upon her Oath saith That about fifteen or Sixteen years ago she went into the Service of William Seullard  Esquire in Boswell Court in Parish of Saint Clements Danes  where she contain as an hired yearly Servant at the annual Wages Rent of about £7.7.0 and continued in such Service for about two years and an half since which time she this Examinant saith that she hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 4th. day of August 1794 } N Bond

Hannah Mayes
