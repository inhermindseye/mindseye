Middx  Eliz: Lucas aged abt. Twenty Four Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman and was never marryed and that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Hide in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above Barrs in The County of Middlesex for the Space of Six Years and upwards at the Yearly wages of 40s. for the 1st Year & £3 for every year after & hath left her sd. Service abt. Three Years and further upon her Oath saith that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 24th- Octr- 1741 before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

The mark Elizth. Lucas
