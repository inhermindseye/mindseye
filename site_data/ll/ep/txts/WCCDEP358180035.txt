Middx  Eleanor Tuff  Wido . upon her Oath that about Thirty one Years since she Intermarried with her late Husband  John Tuff at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes who hath been Dead upwards of One Year That her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to and Served his Apprentiship with Mr Port in Arundell Street in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes Staymaker that since his said Apprentiship he in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beliefe That she hath Five Children viz. Hannah aged 11 Years Humphry & William aged 9 yrs & John & Robt. aged 4 Years & being Poor is not able to maintain them.

Sworne the 9th- Novr. 1742 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Eleanr. Tuff
