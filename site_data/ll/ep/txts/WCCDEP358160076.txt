Middx  The Exaicon of Mary Agnes Charlton Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 13th. day of Septr. 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never marryed And that upwards of Twenty Years ago her father Michll. Charlton rented a House in New Street Square in the Parish of St. Bridgett Vulg St. Bridgett in the City of London of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds and lived in the same for Sevll. Years & untill he Dyed And this Exaiant further Saith that she never Rented any House was an Apprentice or lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworn the Day & Year above named before} Jas: Gronous  Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Agnes Charlton
