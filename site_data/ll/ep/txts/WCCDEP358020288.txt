St.. Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Maria Evans Aged 23 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that she lived as an hir never was Married, that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Thomas Thomas  Cowkeeper & Milkman in Rodney Row at No 6, near the Elephant & Castle in the parish of St. Mary Newington in the County of Surry two Years at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas, that she left that Service about Eight Months ago and since hath not Rented, any House or Apartment of £10 Pr Annum paid any parish Taxes or done any other Act or thing by means whereof she might acquire any legal settlement

Sworn the 19th May 1791 before us N Bond R Tayler.

Maria Evans 's Mark
