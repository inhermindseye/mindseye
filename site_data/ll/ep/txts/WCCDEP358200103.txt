Westmr  Mary Cowley Aged about 20 Years upon Oath Says she was never Married Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Wm. Mason a Grazier in the Parish of Thornley in the County of Lancaster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of two Pounds. Four Shillings hath Left her said Service about two Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the Sixteenth Day of March Last she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster which hath Since been Baptized by the Name of John Says One

Signor Tassoni a Player  Dancer at Drury Lane Playhouse had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body Sometime in the Month of May Last and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid

Sworn the 1 Day of April 1764 Before}

The Mark of Mary Cowley

Upon Mr Jones Complt J. W
