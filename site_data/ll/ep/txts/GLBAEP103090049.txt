Middlesex ss  Ann Wildman  single woman Maketh oath that divers Years ago she was bound apprentice by Indre for Seven Years and that she served the last five years of such her apprenticeship with Mrs. Mac Donald in Eastsmithfield in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and by Means thereof as she is advises gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent Saith that she hath not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever Married Nor to her knowledge or belief hath she done any Act since her said Apprenticeship whereby to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this day of 1767 before}
