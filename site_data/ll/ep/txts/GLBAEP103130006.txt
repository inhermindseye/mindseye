Middlesex ss}  George Davidson maketh oath That divers years ago he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Six Pounds and ten Shillings in Brown Bear Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and during such his residence there was Charged to and paid the Kings Tax for the said house and by means thereof as he is advised gained a legal Settlement there That he this Depont. has not since paid any Kings Taxes also where nor rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rates or Served any Parish office nor done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that he hath a wife named Ann to whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this day of August 1773 before Cha: Digby

The Mark of George Davidson
