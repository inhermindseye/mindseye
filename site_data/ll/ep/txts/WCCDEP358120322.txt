Middx  John Davis upon Oath Saith he Served Seven years apprentiship with Geo. Nutton in Petticoat Lane in the parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate London Dyer and lived with the sd. Nutton after the Expiration of his Apprentiship Sevl. Years as a yearly Servant in Petticoat Lane afsd. and has not Since he left his sd master rented ten pounds by the year pd. parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn April 8th 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Jno: Davis
