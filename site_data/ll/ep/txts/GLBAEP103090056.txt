Middlesex ss.  William Perry singleman maketh oath That divers Years ago he was hired and lived as a hired servant by the Year at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds with one John Carter in Thames Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the East in the City of London and Continued in such Service for the Space of a year and upwards and by Means thereof as he is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that he hath not since lived as a hired servant for a Year nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards or served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes Nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act whereby to gain a settlement since his sd. Servitude

Sworn this-day of January 1767 before Dead
