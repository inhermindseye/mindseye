Middx ss. The Examination of Elenor Scott  Single woman taken on oath before us two of his Majesties Justices of the peace for the County of Middx residing in or near to the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County Middx this 25th: day of June 1756

This Examinant on her oath saith that about April last was a Twelve month Ago one William Jones who then lived in Hog Yard in the parish of St Mary Whitechappell in Middx did prevail with this Examint to Suffer him to lie with her at the house of Mrs Cartey in Hog Lane Yard aforesd And the sd William Jones had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body & so had several times Whereby she Conceived with Child of a Female Bastard Child of Which she was delivered at the house of Sarah Rayner in Swan Alley in the sd parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate on the tenth day of February last Which sd. Female Bastard Child hath been Baptised by the Name of

Elizabeth And hath been Now is and still is likely to be Chargeable to the sd parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate this Examinant being Poor & not able to Provide for the same And this Examinant farther saith that the sd William Jones is the true & only father of the sd. female Bastard Child And that this Examination is voluntary And that she hath not been forced or Compelled to be examined by any Person or Persons whomsoever

Sworn this 25th day of June 1756 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott .

her Elenor Scott Mark
