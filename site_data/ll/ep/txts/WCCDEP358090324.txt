Middx  Mary French Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says she was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwardens & Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Mary bone in the County of Middlesex to one Mr. Lyon near Cupers Bridge in the Parish of St. Saint Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry untill she shod. Attain the Age of Twenty one Years or day of Marriage according to Act of Parliament And says that she lived under such Indenture for the space of Three Years or thereabouts and after leaving her said Masters Service which is about four Years ago she never gained any other subsequent Settlement whatever

Sworn this 13th. day of July 1782 before}
