Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Davidson of the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex Widow taken on oath before me Burford Camper  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Sixth day of October 1773

Who on her oath Saith That on the Twenty fifth day of October in the year one thousand Seven hundred and Sixty three she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Brown Bear Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex Since Baptized in the Church belonging to the said Parish by the name of John Coates and that the said Child is become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and to this Deponents knowledge or belief the said Child hath done no Act to gain a Settlement for himself and that Edward Coates now a Servant & living with Mr Robinson a Confectioner in new Bond Street in the sd County did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child.

Sworn this 6th day of October 1773 before B Camper

Mary Davidson
