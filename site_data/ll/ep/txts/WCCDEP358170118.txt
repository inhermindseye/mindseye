Middx  Margaret Buffett  Wido . of Wm. Buffett upon her Oath saith that she was married to her sd. Husband Twenty Eight Years ago or thereabts. at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr . & that her sd. late Husband hath been dead Twelve Years & upwards & further saith that her sd. late Husband lived in & rented a House in White Horse Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of 4 Years or thereabts- & that her sd. late Husband never after neither hath this Exaittant since his Death) rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr- or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 26th- Day of Augt. 1741 before}
