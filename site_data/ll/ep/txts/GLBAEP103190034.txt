Middlesex ss}  Mary Hemmet the wife of Joseph Hemmet maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her on board  his Majestiesship the Terrible That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was duly bound to & served his apprenticeship with one Thomas a Weaver in the Parish of Banton near the City of Oxford in the County of Oxford & to her knowledge or belief has not since done any act to gain a subsequent settlement & that she was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this. 11. day of March 1780 before W Blackmore

The Mark of Mary Hemmet
