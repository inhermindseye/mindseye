Middx  Martha Wale upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of George Wale and that her said late husband whilst a Singleman lived a Covenant Servant for one year with Philip Smith a Taylor in Bear & Harrow Court in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. as he informed this Examinant & which she verily believes & that her Sd late husband never afterwards nor has this Examinant Since his Decease gained a Subsequent Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr 21st 1751 before  Tho Lane

The mark of Martha Wale
