Middx & Westmr.}  Mary Dunn Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Edward Cork a Captain of the Ship or Vessell called the Mary and Elizabeth in the Isle of Wight for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Twenty five Shillings and after she quitted that Service she never rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says that on the Twenty Ninth day of May last past 1769 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of John Fogarty in the Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. which hath since been Baptized Mary And that Thomas Edderly  Carpenter and Joiner who now works with one Mr. Lillie in Great Wyld Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex is the real and true Father of the said Female Bastard Child of which she was Delivered as aforesaid

Sworn this 10th. day of Novr. 1770 before me W Kelyngs

her Mary Dunn Mark

Witness Peter Sidebotham
