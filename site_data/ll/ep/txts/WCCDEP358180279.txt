Middx  The Exacon of Ann Robinson a Vagabond aprehended in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx & brot. before me One of his Majestys Justices of the pence for the County of Middx aforesaid And taken before me this 23d. Day of January in the Year of our Lord 1744

This Examinant on her Oath says she Intermarried with her prest. Husband John Robinson abt. 7 Yrs. since at the Fleet London that the sd. Jno. Robinson is now a Soldier in his Majestys Service in Flanders & this Examinant can give no Acct. of his Settlemt. other than that he was born in the County of Stafford but in what Parish this Examint. knows not Says she was born in the Parish of Alhallows in the parish Towns of Newcastle upon Tyne in the County of Northumberland & never was an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Janry 23d. 1744 before}

The mark of Ann Robinson
