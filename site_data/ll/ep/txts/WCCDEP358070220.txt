Middlesex & Westmr.}  Mary Webber Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says her Father Wm. Webber Rented and lived in a House known by the Sign of the Crown and Septer without Northgate in the City of Exeter for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds and that this Examinant never gained any other Subsequent Settlement And further Says that on the fourteenth day of October last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Brickletons in Ogden Court Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which has since been baptized by the Name of Willia and is become Chargable to the same she being poor and unable to Maintain the said Child Says that one Logan Henderson a Lieutenant on half pay as she believes and who now lives at Mr. Slades a potter in Copperas Lane Deptford or at Mr. Ervin near Black Fryer Bridge is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at his Lodgings in Bride Lane Court Fleet Street sometime in January last the particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of Which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Logan Henderson & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of Novr. 1774 before W Addington

Mary Webber
