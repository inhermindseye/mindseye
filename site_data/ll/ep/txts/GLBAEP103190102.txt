Middlesex ss}  Jacob Brockhall now belongg. to the Cold Stream Regiment of Foot Guards maketh oath That divers Years Ago he was duly bound apprentice to Joseph Pestle a Worsted Weaver in the Parish of St Paul in the City of Norwich & Served his Said Master there about one year & half When his Said Master died & this Depont has not Since done any Act to gain another Settlement That this Deponent has two Children named to wit Lucy Aged about Eleven Years and Amy Aged about Seven years & Six months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by his late Wife  Jane Brockhall deced

Sworn this. 16. day of June 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Jacob Brockhall
