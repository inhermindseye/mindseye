Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Johnston aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. upon her Oath saith that she has two illegitimate Children living Vizt. Maria aged about 4 years born at the House of a Mr. Metcalfe in Castle Stree Oxfor Road in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the Coun of Middlesex and Elizth . aged about 5 weeks born House of a Mr. Collins in Broad Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx  that she this Ext. is poor and unable to maintain the same whereby they are become Chargeable to the Sd. Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 3d. Febry. 1787 before me  Wm. Kitchiner

Jane Johnston
