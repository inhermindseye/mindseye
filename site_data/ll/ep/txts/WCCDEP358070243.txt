Westmr to wit Robert Gilmour Aged about Sixty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his now Wife at Shrewsbury in the County of Salop about Six Years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says before his Intermarriage with his said Wife he lived as a Warrant Servant in the Capacity of a Turnbroach with her Royall Highness the Princess Dowager of Wales in the Parish of Saint James in the liberty of Westminster for several Years at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Pounds hath Quitted his said Service about 5 Years ago and hath not since he Quitted the same ever done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th day of Feby 1775 before W Addington John Goodchild

Robert Gilmour
