Middx  John Blackman upon Oath Saith he intermarried with his Wife Sarah at the Fleet London upwds. of 15 Years ago and has by her a Son named Daniel aged about 12 Years that about 25 Years ago being then a Singleman he hired himself to Mr Rd. Lloyd of Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx to live with him three Years, for which Service Mr Lloyd contracted to pay him three pounds & find him in Dyet Washing & Lodging And that he lived with the sd Mr. Lloyd under that Agreement for the sd. Term of three Years in Devereux Court aforesaid and has not since he left Mr Lloyd rented ten Pounds a Year pd. parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn July 28th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

John Blackman
