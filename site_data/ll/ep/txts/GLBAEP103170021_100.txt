Middlesex ss}  Ann Hesseldenn  Singlewoman maketh oath That She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her late Parents or either of them She this Deponent never having received any information relative thereto either as to their Birth or otherwise That She this Deponent was born in Churchyard alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 29 day of October 1776 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Ann Hesseldenn
