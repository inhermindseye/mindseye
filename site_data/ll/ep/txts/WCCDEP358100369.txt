Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  passd. to to White Chaple to wit Margaret Swanscoat aged forty Years or thereabouts on her Oath saith That she never was married rented House or Tenement of ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes for any such And further this Examinant on her Oath saith That she was bound Apprentice from the Parish of Saint Olave Hart Street in the City of London to Henry Holdaway of Whitechapel in the Parish of Saint Mary Matfellon otherwise Whitechapel  Pen cutter and served he Apprenticeship of Seven Years with her said Master in Whitechapel aforesaid, That since he has quitted her said Master she has not lived as a Hired Yearly servant for twelve Months or done any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or Belief she may have gained any other settlement than by her said service in the said Parish of Saint Mary Matfellon otherwise Whitechapel aforesaid

Sworn this first Day of March 1785 before M Addington Thos: Gordon

Margaret Her Mark Swanscoat
