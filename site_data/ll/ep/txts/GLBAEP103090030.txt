Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Clarke  Single woman Maketh oath that several Years ago She was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the year at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds with one Anderson of Long Alley in the Parish of Saint Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middlesex  Victualler and Continued in Such Service in the said Parish for the space of two years and by Means thereof as she this Deponent is advised gained a legal settlement in the same Parish And this Deponent Saith that she was never Married nor hath she since her Servitude as above lived as a hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And this Deponent farther Saith that about four weeks ago in the Street Called East Smithfield Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath since been Baptised by the name of Elizabeth Clarke and registred by the name of Elizabeth Clarke

Sworn this 18th day of March 1766 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

the Mark of Elizabeth Clarke
