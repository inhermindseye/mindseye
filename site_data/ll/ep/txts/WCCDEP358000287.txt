Saint Clement Danes 13th May 1786

Middlesex (to wit) Catharine Ward aged thirty three Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that she never was married, says that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Green of the Sported Day Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of five pounds for upward 12 Months and since quitting hire has not done any Act Matter or thing to gain any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th May 1786 before R Butler

her Cath. Ward Mark
