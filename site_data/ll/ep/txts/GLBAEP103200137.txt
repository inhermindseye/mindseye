Middlesex ss}  John Andrews of the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County Beadle maketh oath that he has been informed by an Examination of Margaret Douglas late of the Same Parish Single woman taken in Writing & upon oath before Rice Davies  Esqr a Justice of the Peace for the sd County on the eleventh of April 1782 that she the said Margaret Douglas on the twentieth of April 1777 at the then dwelling house of one Mr Russell a Callenderman in Mugwell Street in the Parish of St Olave in Silver Street in the City of London was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since baptized by the name of Sarah Hitchcock who hath long Since been & still is Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate And that Richard Hitchcock then of Angel Street Saint Martin le Grand in the City of London  Plaisterer did get her with Child of the sd Bastard Child the which Examination this Deponent Verily believes to be true.

Sworn this 25 of May 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

John Andrews
