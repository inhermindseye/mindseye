Middx  Eliza. Simonety  Wido . of Vincent Simonety upon her Oath saith that he she intermarried with her sd. late Husband at the Parish Church of Chelsea about 17 Years since that the sd. Vincent Simonety hath been Dead abt. 3 Years that abt. 12 Years since he lived in & Rented a House in Berry Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £50. for the Space of Six Years that since he left the sd. House the sd. Vincent Simonety in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented £10. a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or other wise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 11th. Octr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

Eliz Simonety
