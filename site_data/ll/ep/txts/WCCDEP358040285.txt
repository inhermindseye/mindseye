Middx 

Mary Stone upon her Oath saith she never was married and that she was born at Islington in the Parish of St. Mary at Islington in the County of Middlesex where her father Edwd. Stone deced was likewise born and that neither her sd. late fathers nor this Examinant ever rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 16th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Mary Stone
