Middx.  Mary Baraban aged abt. Twenty Three Years upon Oath says She is the Wife of Anthony Baraban who is gone from her Says She Intermarried with the said Anthony at the Fleet Londn. about four Years since says her said Husband was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Joseph Flude Founder in Red Lyon Court in Grubb Street in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex Says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says She has one Child (to wit) Andrew aged abt. one Year & a half

Sworn Octr. 4th. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Baraban
