Middx  Elizabeth Wright aged about 25 Years Upon Oath says she was never married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Swift a Coachharness maker in Great Queen Street near Lincolns Inn fields in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for One Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds for the first Quarter of a Year and three pounds and ten Shillings for the remaining part of the said time quitted said Service about two Years ago and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the fourth day of December 1763. she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the House of Mrs. James in New Inn Passage in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster with -in the County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized by the name of Elizabeth Mary and that Samuel Wyatt a Coachmaker who works at Mr. Harley's in High Holborn did beget the said Child on her body of which she was delivered as aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of her body in the Month of March 1763 and at several other times afterwards and that no other person did beget the same.

Sworn 27th. March 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Elizabeth Wright
