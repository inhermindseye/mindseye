Middx ss  Mary Powell aged abt. Nineteen Years upon hath Says She never was Married, Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes, was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Jonathan Powell Rented a House of Twenty Six Pounds a Year in New Round Court in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster and paid all Parish Taxes lived therein upwards of Eighteen Years & hath quitted the same about Six Months Since which time he hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn Decr. 3d. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mary Powell
