Middx & Westmr.}  John Holt Aged about 52 Years on his Oath Says he Intermarr with Jane his late Wife at the Fleet London about 24 Years ago and who died about Ten Days since and by whom he hath 2 Childn. namely William Aged 15 Year's & Thompson Says he never Rented house paid Taxes served any Apprenticeship or lived a hired Yearly Servt. before Marriage for one Year Says his Father John Holt Rented and lived in a House in Gunpowder Alley New Street Shoe Lane in the parish of St. Brides otherwise Bridget in the City of London for sevl. Years at the yery Rent of £10 as he has been Informed & verily believes hath quitted same upwards of 40 Years ago and after Quitting the same he never Rented House Paid Taxes or otherwise gained any or. subseqt. Settlemt. neither hath this Examinant since his sd. Fathers Death.

Sworn this 5th. Day of March 1777 before me  Jno. Machin  John Croft

John Holt
