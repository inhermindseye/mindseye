St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to Wit Robert Loder on behalf of his Son Robert Loder aged 23 Years who is an Idiott, Saith on Oath that this Examinant Intermarried with Mary Loder at Bishopgate Church June 23d. 1766 . but who is now Dead by whom there is two Children living Viz. Robert aged 23. Year and Eliz . aged 20 years, who is now from him, that this Deponent Rented a House in Robin Hood Court , Shoe Lane in the Parish of St. Andrews below Barrs in the City of London three years at the yearly Rent of Fifteen Pounds and Paid Taxes and that after this Deponent Quitted Said House, he has not done, any Act, Matter, or thing, so as to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 6th. day of Feby: 1796 } Rupt. Clarke  Rd. Thompson

Robt Loder .
