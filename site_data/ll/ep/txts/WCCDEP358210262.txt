Westmr.  Eleanor Norris Aged upwards of Thirty four Years Upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Norris deceased, by whom she hath One Child now with her (to wit) Jane aged near Two Years, says her said Husband in his life time hath of ten times informed her and which information she verily believes to be true, that, before his Marriage and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Henry Hyde a Hackney Coach Master in Bond Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and Upwards but what Yearly Wages this Examinant cannot tell, says she hath not since her said Husbands Death Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 6th. day of April 1768 , Before} Saunders Welch  Edn. Byron

The Mark of Eleanor Norris
