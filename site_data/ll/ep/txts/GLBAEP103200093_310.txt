Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Stevens  Singlewoman maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with one Moore a Victualler in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the said County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such service under Such hiring for the Space of one Year & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a Child named George Moore Aged five Weeks born in Red Cross Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Which illegitimate & a Bastard & that one Henry Moore of the Same Place Bricklayer is the Father of the sd Child

Sworn this Third day of Febry 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Elizabeth Stevens

By Servitude
