Middx.  Hannah Hall aged near 20 years upon Oath saith she never was married and she further voluntarily maketh Oath that she is pregnant with Child or Childn. which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex (Where she now lodges) she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born and that Thomas Axford of Child or Children on her body the said Thomas Axford having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant twice in the latter End of January or beginning of February last at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that the sd. Thomas Axford & no man else is the true and real father thereof

Sworn Octr. 9th. 1753 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Hannh Hall
