Westmr. 

Mary Davin aged abt. 50 Years upon Oath she is the wido . of Patrick Davin deced with whom she intermarried at the fleet London about 14 Years since Says her said late husband was born in the County of Shillmay in Ireland & that she knows nothing more of his Settlemt. that she this Examint. was born in Christchurch parish in the City of Cork in the same kingdom and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. whilst a Singlewoman

Sworn 24th. April 1750 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Mary Dean
