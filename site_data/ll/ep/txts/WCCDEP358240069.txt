St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Clemt Danes  (to wit) William Davison Aged 25 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he served her Apprenticeship under Indur with Mr. Thomas Sedgwick who lived the last twelve months of this Deponents time in Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty where this Deponent always lodged except when he Slept in the Boat. And that he hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement that about 3 Years upwards he intermarried with his present wife Rebecca late Rebecca Southall Spinster at the parish Church of Chinwick in the County of Middlesex that he hath one Child living by his said Wife Namely William Aged about two years And that he is unable to Maintain himself & family witht. Assistance

Sworn the 12th. day of January 1792 before Me} R Butler
