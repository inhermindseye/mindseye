St Clement Danes 15th July 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Olave Southwark  (to wit) Sarah Creed  singlewoman aged Nineteen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she was bound an Apprentice to Mr Wm Rogers of Griffiths Rents Bermondsey Street Taylor and served her said Master there for about four years that he then removed to a Butchers in Bermondsey Street in the parish of Saint John in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry and continued there for about a year and an half and that She then removed with her said Master to Hand Court in the parish of St Olave Southwark aforesaid in the County of Surrey aforesaid that he then died and about a Month after she was taken left her Mistral was her was discharged by her said Mistress from her service which was upwards of a year ago and hath not been assigned over to any person for the remainder of her Apprenticeship lived as an hired Yearly Servant for a year together or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And that she is now pregnant & distressed

Sworn the 15th day of July 1793 before Us} Wm Kitchiner D Walker

her Sarah Creed Mark

Witness Garrett
