Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit John Stevens aged about 29 years upon his Oath saith that in the year 1782 he Rented and lived in a House in the Savoy in the Precinct of the savoy in the County of Middlesex for Three Months at the yearly Rent of Thirty pounds that he hath quitted said house about seven years since which time he hath not rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes on done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 13th. Febry. 1790 Sampr Wright M Addington

John Stevens
