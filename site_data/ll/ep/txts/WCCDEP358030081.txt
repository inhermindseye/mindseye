Middx  Joseph Lane removed with Ann his Wife & two Children Mary aged about twelve Years and Joseph aged about nine Years from the Parish of St Giles in the Feilds by Order dated the 15th. Day of January Instant upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his sd. Wife Ann on the 6th. Day of July 1732 in the Parish Church of St. Thomas in the City of Salisbury Says he lived in & rented a house in Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds for the Space of 1 Year & upwards & hath left the same upwards of two Years Says he hath not since he left the said house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Janry 16th 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Joseph Lane
