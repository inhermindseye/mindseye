Middx  Elizabeth Craig aged about 62 Years Lodging in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon Oath saith she never was Married That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Wilson at the Ship in Ship Yard in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for the Space of One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds Quitted the Same about Seven Years and hath not since rented any House or been a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months together

Sworn the 4th. day of March 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Elizabeth Craig
