Westmr.  Mary Cope aged abt. 28 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year and that her father John Cope rented a house in little Shire Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the yearly Rent of £14 and lived therein sevl. Years & has not since he quitted the same (which is abt. 18 Years ago) Rented ten pounds a Year or paid parish taxes

Sworn Augt. 25th. 1752 before}
