Westmr.  Robt. Williams aged upwds. of 14 Years upon Oath saith he has been Informed and believes he was born in the house of Mr. Miller in Burleigh Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . Says he never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Janry 4th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Robt Williams
