Middlesex ss.  Jane Brecknock the Widow of Andrew Brecknock decd. maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at Kentish Town Chapel on or about the Eighteenth Day of October 1762 , that her said late Husband about Eighteen years, ago Rented a House of Ten Pounds a year in Norwich Court in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex & lived in the same for several Years and was charged to and paid the Poors Rate for the said House, that her said Husband did not after he quitted the same, Rent any other House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or do any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement that her said Husband died in the Workhouse of the said Parish about Six years ago and that she has not since her said Husbands Death Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds, or lived as a hired Servant by the year or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this Day of 1798. before}

Andw. Brecknock is charged to the Poors Rate in the Years 1780 & 1781 Feb. 10.

13th. Jany. 1787  Mrs . Brecknock was sworn to an examr. touchg. her Husbds. Settlement before Rice Davies  Esqr .

Wm. Stollard is charged to the Poors Rate in the 1st & 2d. Taxs. for the Year 1751. Feb. 8 .
