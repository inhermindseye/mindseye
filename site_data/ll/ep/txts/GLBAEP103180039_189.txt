Middlesex ss}  Jonathan Hubboll maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice for the Term of Seven years to Richard Rook of the Parish of St George in the County of Surry  Distiller and this Deponent Served the remaining part of Such his Apprenticeship being about the Space at two Years with his Said Master in Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Apprenticeship That he was lawfully Married to his late wife  Ann Hubboll deced by whom he hath now living Three Children namely ann Aged twelve years Jonathan Aged Seven Years & Elizabeth Aged three years & upwards

Sworn this 26 day of May 1778 before P. Rowlands

Jona Hubboll
