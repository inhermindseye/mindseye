Middx to wit Hester Cook aged about forty three Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Holland a Taylor in Norfolk Street Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said service upwards of six Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 27th day of June 1779 before  Abrm Baley

Hester Cook
