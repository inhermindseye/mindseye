Middx  Eliz: Ross aged abt. 44 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Ross (gone from her) by whom she hath one Child James aged abt. six years says her sd. husband served his Apprenticeship of seven years to Mr. Brown a Taylor in the Parish of St. Peter in the City Town of Nottingham

Sworn the 13th. of June 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Ross
