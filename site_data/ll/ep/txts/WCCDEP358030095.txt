Middx  Henry Mash on Behalfe of Sarah Middleton (a Lunatick aged about 29. Yrs.) upon his Oath saith that the sd. Sarah Middleton lived a Yearly Servant with this Examinant in Clements Inn Passage and in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of two Years at the Yearly Wages of £5 (being all that time a Singlewoman without Child or Children & hath left this Examinants Service about two Years Says the sd. Sarah Middleton hath not since she left his said Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Feb. 6th. 1745 before}
