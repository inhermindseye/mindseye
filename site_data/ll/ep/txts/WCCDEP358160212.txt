Middx  The Exaicon of Jane Wright  Widw . of John Wright passed from the Parish of Monks Kerby in the County of Warwick by Order bearing Date ye. 5th. Day of Novr. last taken ye. 19th. Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. on the 8th. Day of Sept. In the Year 1731 She intermarryed with the above named Jno. Wright & that she this Exaiant before her Intermariage with the sd. Jno. Wright lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Baines in St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martins in the Feilds in the liberty of Westmr . for the Space of 2 Years & upwards at ye. Yearly Wages of four Pounds 10s & this Exaiant further saith that her sd. Husband Jno. Wright as he informed her in his Life Time & as she verily believes was born in the Parish of Monks Kerby in the sd. County of Warwick & was there brought up & lived with his Father Richd Wright & of him learned the business & trade of a Taylor And yt. her said

Husband never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Exaiants Knowledge or beleife & this Exaiant hath by her sd. Husband Jno. Wright two Children (to wit) John aged 6 Years born in the aforesd. Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds & Wm . aged one Yr. & upwards born in the Parish of St. Botolph without Bishopgate  Londn & this Exaiant being Poor is not able to maintaine ym.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Jane Wright
