Middlesex ss}  Ann Lampright the Daughter of  John Lampright deced maketh oath That She has been informed by her Mother Martha Lampright & which information this Deponent believes to be true Father said late Father Served his Apprenticeship with one Jonathan Pinchbeck in Round Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex  Fan Painter & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there that to this Deponents knowledge or belief her her said Father did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That to this Deponts knowledge or belief her said mother Since her said Fathers death hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She this Deponent never was bound apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself nor was She ever Married That She this Deponent hath a Child named John Mortland aged about one year which Child is a Bastard & was born in the Parish of Saint Bridget in the City of London .

Sworn this 23d: day of April 1774 before  Jno Sherwood B Camper

The Mark of Ann Lampright
