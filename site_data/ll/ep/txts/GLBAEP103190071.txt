Middlesex ss}  Margaret Seymour the wife of Robert Seymour maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majesties Ship the Greenwich That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with one Robert Walker a Weaver in the Parish of Saint Alban in the City of Oxford & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this 3d day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Margaret Seymour
