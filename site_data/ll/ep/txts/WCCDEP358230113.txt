Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Joanna Partridge Aged about 37 years upon her Oath Saith that about 12 years ago she intermarried with her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Martins in the Fields by whom she hath living Three Childrens Vizt. William Aged Nine years George Aged four years and Frederick Aged about four Months That her said late husband rented and lived in two Rooms at a Mr Bells at the Horse Shoe in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for near three Months at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that he hath left the said apartments upwards of Eight years since which time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th. day of Feby 1800 before us  Wd Woodenough Winster

Johanah Partridge
