Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Rebecca Ball of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the said County this twenty eighth day of February 1784 -Who on oath Saith That about eight months ago She this examinant at her house Situate in Long Alley in the Parish of St John Wapping in the sd County was delivered of a Male Bastard Child Since baptized & registred by the name of James Sidnell & that the said Child is likely to become Chargeable to the sd Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that one James Sidnell of the same Parish Breechesmaker is the Father of the sd Child

Taken & Signed the day & year above written before R Davies

The Mark of Rebecca Ball
