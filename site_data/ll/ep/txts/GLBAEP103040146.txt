Middlesex ss.  Sarah Hollindrake the Widow of Jones Hollindrake decd. maketh Oath that she had a Daughter named Eleanor , who died on or about the Twenty fifth Day of June now last past that her said Daughter for several years before her Death cohabited with one Richard Hicks lately decd. and had several Children by him, who are all deceased but one, name Sarah , that her said Daughter was delivered of the said Child, on or about the Thirtieth Day of June One Thousand Seven Hundred & Ninety three in the Dwelling House of Mr. Harris Nr 45 Water Lane in the Parish of St. Ann Blackfriars in the City of London , and that the said Child has Baptized at St. Ann's Church , And this Deponent further Saith that she was present at the Birth of the said Child, & that she heard her said Daughter say but a short time before her Death that she never was Married to the said Richard Hicks the Father of the said Child, that being Poor & unable to provide for the said Child she has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate for her Relief & has been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 15th Day of October 1798 before  Danl Williams R Davies

her Sarah Hollindrake Mark
