Middx  Edwd. Carpenter upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Richard Lee in the Parish of Hambleton in the County of Surry at the Yearly wages of £6 for two years (& was all the time of his sd Service a Singleman and that he hath not since he left the sd. Mr Lee rented £10. a year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr 17th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Edward Carpenter
