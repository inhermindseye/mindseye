Middx  The Examination of Margt. Crawley on the behalf of John Hudson an Infant aged about Four Years living in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 6th. Day of April 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that John Hudson Father of the sd. Infant (now in the Workhouse of the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Barrs in the County of Middx ) Rented a House abt. 12 Months ago at Mount Pleasant in the sd. Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Barrs of the Yearly Rent of £21 & lived in the same for the Space of One Year & upwds. And this Examint. further saith that the sd. John Hudson hath not since he left the sd. House Rented £10 a Year Said Parish taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examints. Knowledge or beleife.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Margt. Crayley
