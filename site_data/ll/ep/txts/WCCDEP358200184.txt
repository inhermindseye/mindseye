Middx  Maria Linell Aged about 25 Years upon Oath Says she Intermarried with her Husband Wm. Linell in the Parish Church of St. Sepulchres in the County of Middx on ye 11th Day of Augt 1760 Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Pearce a Watch Finisher in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & never after Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert. for One Year as this Examinant hath been Informed & which Information she Verily Beleives to be true Says she has One Child by her said Husband Mary Aged about four Months

Sworn ye 15 of Decr. 1764 Before}
