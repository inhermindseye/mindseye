Middx ss  Rebecca Hawkins  wife of John Hawkins who is now at Sea maketh oath that her sd Husband has often informed her that about Thirty one Years ago he was bound apprentice to Mr. William Styles a Dyer in the parish of St. Stephen in Coleman Street in the City of London and served about five years of his apprenticeship in the sd. Parish and then went to Sea & by Means thereof gained a legal settlemt. there And this Depont. farther saith that to her knowledge information or belief her sd. husband never rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards Nor served any Parish offices nor was a hired servant by ye year And that she hath two Children to wit Rebecca aged about Six years & Margaret aged about two years the lawfull issue of this Depont. by her sd husband

Sworn this 29 day of March 1755 before  Richd Ricards  Chris. Scott

The mark of RH Rebecca Hawkins
