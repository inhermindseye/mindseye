Middx  Catherine Peirson aged upwards of 27 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married And that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Peale at Barnett Castle of in the County of Durham at the Yearly Wages of £3 for the Space of one Yr. & Six Months or thereabouts & hath left her sd. Service about 4 Years & hath not since Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servants for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the 4th. Day of May last in the House of Mr. Ridgement in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Baptized Richard & is become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same

and that Richard Ebberel late of the sd. Parish of St Clement Danes  Brush maker did begett the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body he the sd. Richard Ebberel having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant several times in the Month of July last and at several other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child of which she was Delivered as aforesaid and that he the said Richard Ebberel & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworn July 3d. 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Cath: Peirson
