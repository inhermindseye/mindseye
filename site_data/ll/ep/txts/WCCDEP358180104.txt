Middx  Margarett Smith  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that on the 26th. Day of August last she delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of one Mr. Smith in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Baptized Thomas & that one Thomas Brown did begett the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body he the said Thomas Brown having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of October One Thousand Seven hundred forty one & at several other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child Which she was delivered of as aforesaid & that he the sd. Thos. Brown & no other Person is the two & reall Father thereof

Sworn the 24th. Augt. 1743 before} C. Egerton

Margret Smith
