Middx  Sarah Johnson Wife of George Johnson upon Oath says she Intermarried with the said Geo: Johnson at the Fleet London about four Years since Says her sd. Husband was by Indre bearing date the 2d. day of March 1748. bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Robt. Marcellus of the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields  Painter & Lodged one Year & upwds. the last part of his Apprentiship at Mr. Southwell 's in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx.

Sworn the 3d. day of Decr. 1756 -before} Barth: Hammond

Sarah Johnson
