Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Thomas Hubbard aged about 36 years upon his Oath saith that about 4 years ago he Intermarried with Mary his now Wife at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields that he has no Child or Children living that before his Intermarriage with his said Wife he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Geo: Weatherstone in Kirby Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Barrs in the City of London for 12 Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted said Service about 16 years ago since which time he has not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 30th day of Janry. 1787 G Mercer Jno. Barnfather

his Thomas Hubbard Mark
