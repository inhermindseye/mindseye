Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Joel Curtis who is ill Maketh Oath and saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Joel Curtis Rented and lived in a house in Little George Street Portman Square in the Parish of Saint Mary Le bone in the County of Middlesex for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds and that after he quitted said house he never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Decr. 1796 } Rusl Clarke M Addington B Newman
