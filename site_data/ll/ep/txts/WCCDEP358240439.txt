St Clement Danes 8th Septr. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlemt. Pish of Kensington  (to wit) Elizabeth Cook aged upwards of thirty years Upon Oath Saith that she was Married to John Cook at the parish Church of St. Ann Westminster about thirteen years ago that subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and resided in a House in Church Lane in the parish of Kensington in the Co. of Middlesex for 3 or 4 years or thereabouts at the a considerable Annual Rent and that she paid the parish Taxes for the same that She hath two Children born in Wedlock namely John aged ten years or thereabts. and Lawrence aged ten Months or thereabouts that her said Husband is now from her and she is ill and unable to maintain herself and family without assistance And that since he quitted the said House in Church Lane , which was about four years ago, he hath not obtained any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her this Examinants' knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 8th day of Septr. 1794 before us }M Addington Russ Clarke

her Elizh. Cook mark

Witness B Newman
