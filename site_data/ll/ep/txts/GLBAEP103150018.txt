Middlesex ss}  Margaret Bone  Singlewoman maketh oath That about Three Years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant  by the year with one Mr Boon in the parish of Blyth in the County of Northumberland at Certain Yearly Wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of one Year & half & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of March 1775 before  Burf Camper

The Mark of Margaret Bone
