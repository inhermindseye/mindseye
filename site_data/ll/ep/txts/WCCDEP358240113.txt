St. Clement Danes June 8th. 1792.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St. CD.  (to Wit) Mary Sanders  Widow Aged 71 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith The she was Married to her late Husband Richard Sanders at the Fleet abt. 35. Years ago, That he Died abt. 27 Years ago, That since her said Husband's Decease she being a Widow unmarried and not not having any Child or Children, she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr Setree at No. 6 in Clements Inn in the parish of St. Clement Danes about four years, at the Annual Wages of five Guineas That She left his Service abouts many years ago and since she quitted the same she hath not done any Act Matter or thing Whatsoever Whereby to obtain any Subsequent legal Settlement And that she is Aged Infirm & incapable to Maintain herself Without Assistance

Sworn the 8th. June 1792 . before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Sanders 's Mark

Witness B Newman
