Middlesex ss}  Mary Allen  Singlewoman the Daur of John Allen by Elizabeth his wife both deced maketh oath that She has been informed by her said late mother & which information She believes to be true That her said late Father lived in a Leasehold Tenement of his own in the Parish of St Pancras in the County of Middlesex & was Charged to & paid Kings Taxes for the same & also Served the office of Overseer of the Poor there & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the sd Parish & that he did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That her said Mother after her Fathers death married again but that during the time of her Widowhood She did not to the knowledge or belief of this deponent do any Act to gain a Settlement for herself And that She this Deponent to her knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right never having been bound apprentice nor having lived as hired Servant for a year or been Married.

Sworn this 9th day of March 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

Mary Allen
