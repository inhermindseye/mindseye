Middx  Eliz: Clark  Wife of Fras. Clark gone from her aged about 34 Years upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Fras. Clark at the Fleet London on the 17th. Day of March about 19 Yrs. since Says her sd. Husbds: Father Captn. Clark lived in & rented a house in Essex Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Fifty pounds for severall Years and dyed therein Says her sd. Husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served a Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 25th; 1745 before W Jones

Eliz Clarke
