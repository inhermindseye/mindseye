Middlesex ss}  John Davies maketh oath That divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one Thomas Edmunds in Vere Street near Clare Market in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Sd County Soap Boiler and Tallow Chandler & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & upwards by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Servd any parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 7th day of october 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of John Davies
