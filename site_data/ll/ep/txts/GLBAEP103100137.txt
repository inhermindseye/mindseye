Middlesex ss}  Sarah Humphrys  Wife of George Humphrys maketh Oath That divers Years ago her Said husband was bound Apprentice to and Served the whole of his Apprenticeship with John Scott  Lighterman in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and by means thereof as this Depont. is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge or belief her said husband hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24 day of July 1769 before  Rob Pell

Sarah Humphirs
