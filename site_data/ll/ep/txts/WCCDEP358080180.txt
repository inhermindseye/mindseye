Middlesex to wit Charles Salkeld aged about forty eight Years on his Oath says he Intermarrd. to his now Wife  Hannah at Saint Mary at Lambeth about six Years ago and by whom he hath one Child now living namely Charles aged about three Years Says that before his said Intermarrd. with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived with one Isaac Merryweather a Staymaker in Holles Street Clare Markett for the space of one Year and an half or thereabouts part of the three Years for which he was Covenanted for and untill his Master broke and went abroad after which this Examinant was forced to provide for himself And further says he never gained any other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th- Day of Novr- 1777 before R Butler

Chas. Salkeld
