Middx  Martha Roberts aged abt. 30 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she never was married & that on the 24th- of Octr Instant last she was delivered of a female bastard Child at Mrs. Bradleys in Hemlock Court in the parish of St Clemt Danes within the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middlex which has been baptized Martha and is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same and that Edwd Jones a Servant to Mr. Crayman in Great Ormond Street did beget the said female bastard Child on her body the said Edwd Jones having had carnal knowledge of this Examinant Sevl. times in the month of January last and at Sevl. other times Since at one of which times he got her with Child of the sd. female bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the said Edward Jones & no other person in the true & real father thereof

Sworn Novr. 25th Octr. 26th 1751 before  Thos Lane Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Martha Roberts
