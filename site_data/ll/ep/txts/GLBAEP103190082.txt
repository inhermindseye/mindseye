Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Powell the widow of James Powell deced maketh oath That her sd. late husband in his life time rented a house of the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds in Virginia Street in the Parish of St George in the Sd County & lived in the Same for the Space of a year & was charged to and Paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the same & did not afterwards Rent a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Nor hath She this Deponent Since his death done any Act to gain a Settlement for herself That She hath four Children namely James aged about twelve years Elizabeth Aged about Eleven years Anna aged about Ten Years & Charlotte aged about five years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd late husband to whom She was lawfully Married which Said first mentioned Child James is now at Sea & has gained a Settlement for himself and which Said three last mentioned Children are now resident with this Deponents Father Mr Pound at Hannington in the County of Wilts and as yet have done no Act to gain a Settlement for themselves.

Sworn this. 17. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

Elizabeth Powell
