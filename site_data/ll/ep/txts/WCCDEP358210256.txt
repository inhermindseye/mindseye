Westmr.  Mary Folly Aged about Twenty Six Years Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. William Goddard a Victualler at the sign of the Black Horse in the Strand in the County of Middlesex for Four Years at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds twelve Shillings for the first Year, Three Pounds for the second Year, Three Pounds ten Shillings for the Third Year, and Four Pounds for the Fourth Year; Quitted said Service about Seven Years ago, and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the day of March 1768, Before}
