Middx  Margaret Soames Aged about 19 Years upon Oath says She never was Married That She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Pembrook a Grocer in St. Paul's Street in the Parish of St. Paul in Canterbury for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Forty Shillings & hath left her said Service upwards of one Year and not since lived with any Person as a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and that one Peter Largeau did begett the said Bastard Child or Children on her B he the said Peter Largeau having had Carnal knowledge of her Body the latter end of December or beginning of January last & at several other times since in the House of his Brother James Largeau in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes Middx with whom this Examinant lived as a Servant abt. 9 M or thereabouts

Sworn the 29th. Day of Septr. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Margt. Soames
