Middx  Mary Dennison aged about 30 Years Says She Intermarried with John Dennison her Husband at the Fleet London on the 7th. Day of June 1749 by whom She hath one Child named Mary aged about 5 years Says her said Husband is a native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England as She hath been informed by him and believes Says that before her Marriage with the said John Dennison and whilst She was a Singlewoman She lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Chas. Newport a Coal Merchant by Essex Stairs in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upwards of a year

Sworn the Day of Septr. 1756. before}

The mark of Mary Dennison
