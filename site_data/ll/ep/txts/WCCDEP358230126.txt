Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Foster Aged about 26 years upon her Oath Saith that an or about 26 June 1794 she intermarried with her Present Husband who has deserted her at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath living three Children Vizt. Hannah Aged five years George Aged two years and Elizabeth Aged about Eight Months That her said Husband never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant rented any house Tenement or Lodging often Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement That his Father Thomas Foster  rented and lived in part of a house in Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for 2 years at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards That he hath Left the said Apartments about two years since which time he hath not rented any House tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 27th day of March 1800 before us John Collick 

her Jane Forster  Mark

Witness Aw Burbidge Beadle
