Westmr to wit Mary Palmer Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Edmund Palmer (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Hill near Billings gate London in the Month of April One Thousand Seven hundred and Sixty Six by whom she hath one Child living named John Aged about fifteen Months Says that before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Shellito deceased at the Sun at Billingsgate for Two Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds or thereabouts Says that after he quitted his said Service he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 8th. day of Augt. 1769 before us} Tho Fellows  Pet Planck

The Mark of Mary Palmer
