Middx.  Mary Robins aged about 19 Years upon Oath says her Father John Robins Rented a House in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes of the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds, lived therein upwards of three Years and never afterwards Rented a House elsewhere as She has been informed & verily believes Says She never Rented a House or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 13th. Day of March 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham

The mark of Mary Robins
