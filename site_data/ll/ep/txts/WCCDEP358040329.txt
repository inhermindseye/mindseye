Middx  Philip Connelly upon Oath saith he Intermarryed with his Wife Mary abt. 22 Yrs. ago at St. Mannings Church at Wexford in Ireland by whom he hath two Sons Terence aged abt. 19 Yrs. & Philip aged upwds. of 25 Yrs. Says he lived a Yearly Servt. whilst a Singleman with Mr Meadows a Tallow Chandler in the Parish of Salisbury in the Town & County of Wexford in Ireland for one Year at the Yearly Wages of 40s and has not since rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 28th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Philip Connelly
