Middx.  Chas. Higgins aged abt. 54 Yrs. upon Oath says he Intermarried with Jane his Wife in the Parish Church of St Nicholas at Newcastle upon Tyne abt. Ten or Eleven Years since by whom he has four Childn. Wm . aged abt. six Years David aged abt. four Years Sarah aged upwds. of two Years and Margt . abt. Six Months; says before his Intermarriage with the sd. Jane and whilst he was a Single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Jackson in Boswell Court by Queen Square in the Parish of St. George the Martyr by Queen Square in the County of Middx. for One Year and upwards at the Yearly wages of £10 and never Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes

Sworn Janry 4th. 1759 before} Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

The mark of Chas Higgins

Upon the Complt. of Mr. Wm. Hale Overseer J: P:
