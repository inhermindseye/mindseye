Middlesex ss}  Sarah Mosely maketh oath that divers years ago her Daughter Ann Mosely was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with one Mr Bolton a Cornfactor opposite Hog Lane in the Parish of St Leonard Shoreditch in the sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of two years & has not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this. 3. day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke W. Blackmore

The Mark of Sarah Mosely
