Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Adam Wilcox Burbidge one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes of Behalf of John Boyllin who is Ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said John Boyllin served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr John Wyatt a Copper plate Printer in Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster That since he hath been out of his time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 31st day of Jany 1800 before us Wd Woodenough Saml. Edwards

Aw Burbidge
