Middx.  Ralph Boote upon Oath saith he Rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £23 in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . within the County of Middlesex & lived therein four Years or thereabouts and afterwards Rented a house of £24 by the Year in Hollis Street in the same parish & lived therein upwds. of 6 Yrs. & has not since he quitted the last mentioned house Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn March 21st. 1752 before}

Ralph Boote
