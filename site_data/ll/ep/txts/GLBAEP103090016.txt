Middlesex ss  Mary Day  single woman Maketh oath that divers Years ago She was bound Apprentice by Indenture by the then Parish officers of the Parish of Saint Mary White chappell in the County of Middx to one George Hog of Swan Alley in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of Middlesex and serves her said Master for the Space of a year under such Indenture of apprenticeship and then left him And this Depont. Saith that since such her said Servitude she hath not lived as a hired servant for a year nor was she ever Married Nor to her knowledge or belief hath She done any Act to gain a settlement subsequent to her said Apprenticeship And this Deponent farthr. Saith That about two Years Ago She this Deponent was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Parrott Alley in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the said County since Baptised by the name of William at the Parish Church of Saint George in the Said County of Middx & registred by the name of William Walters And that the Said Male Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate And that Robert Walters late of the Said Parish of Saint John Wapping  Labourer did get her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child

Sworn this 7th. day of December 1765 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Mary Day Her Mark
