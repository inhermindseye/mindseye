Westmr Middx 

Mary Gibb aged abt. 32 Years upon her Oath saith she is the Wido. of Thomas Gibb who dyed abt. 10 Weeks ago that She Intermarried with the sd. Thomas Gibb at the Fleet London on the 12th. of May last Says her sd. late husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Bath a Peruke maker in the Strand in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 3d. 1748 before Wm: Jones

The mark of Mary Gibb
