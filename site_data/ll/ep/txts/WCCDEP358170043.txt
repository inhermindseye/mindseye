Middx  The Examination of Olymphia Tomling  Wido . of Robt. Tomling living in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 27th- Day of April 1741 Who upon her Oath saith that on the 2d. Day of May 1716 she Intermarried with her sd. late Husband Robt. Tomling  & that her sd. late Husband rented a House in Burleigh Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt- Danes of the Yearly rent of Twenty Five Pounds for the Space of Nine Yrs- & hath left the sd. House abt. Sixteen Years & further upon her Oath saith that her sd. late Husband in his Life Time nor she since his Death hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person, as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt- in any other Place to her Knowledge or Beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed, before} Jas: Gronous

Olymphia Tomling
