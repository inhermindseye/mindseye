St Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster} (to wit) Mary Blackbird James Talboys one of the Beadles of the parish of St Clement Danes aforesaid on behalf of Mary Blackbird  Widow of Maketh Oath and saith that the said Mary Blackbird hath Informed servt which he verily believes & that about 40 years ago or refused she intermarried with her late Husband John James Blackbird at the Chapel of St. John Wapping then belongg. to the Chapelay of and belonging to the parish Church of St. Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex That after their said Marriage her said late Husband Rented and with her Resided in an House House situate in Grays Inn Lane in the parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the sd. County for about 4 years at and under the Annual Rent of £14 that they quitted the same about twenty five years ago and since her said Husband in his Life time and not gain nor hath he this Examinant the said Mary Blackbird since his Decease acquired any other Subsequent legal Settlement- and that she hath farther Informed this Deponent which he verily believes to be true that only about 8 Weeks she was aclieved as a Pensioner belonging to the said parish and so had been for same time then past - And that she is unable to maintain herself

Sworn before us the 29th novr. 1791  Sampr Wright  Thos. Gordon

Jas Talboys
