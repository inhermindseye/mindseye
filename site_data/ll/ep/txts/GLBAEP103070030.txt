Middx ss  Mary Angell  Widow of Thomas Angell decd Saith that about Thirty Eight Years ago her Husband Rented a House Situate at the Corner of Hedge Lane in Suffolk Street in the parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the City & Liberty of Westminster and paid Thirty five pounds a Year Rent for ye Same And Saith that her sd. husband Never afterwards gained any Subsequent Settlement to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent And by Means aforesd. this Deponent in right of her sd. husband apprehends herself to be legally Settled in ye sd. Parish of St. Martin in the Fields as being the place of ye last legal Settlement of her sd. husband she this dept. not having done any Act since where to Acquire Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 15 Day of May 1751 before us  Richd. Ricards  Saml Gower

Mary Angell
