St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} (to wit) Mary Harris  Singlewoman Aged 26 years or thereabouts Upon Oath Saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Mudgrave a Taylor in Stanhope Street in the said parish of St Clement Danes about Fifteen Months at the Annual Wages of £6 that she quitted the same about ten years ago and since hall not lived as an hired yearly Servt for a twelve month together under and living, rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year, paid any parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any any Subsequent legal Settlements to the best of her knowledge or belief and that she is now incapable to maintain herself without same Assistance

Sworn the 23d day of Decr. 1791 before me  Wm Hitchiner

Mary Harris

By the Order of M' Godfrey Newman  Beadle }
