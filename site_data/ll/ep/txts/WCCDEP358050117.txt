Westmr.  Sarah Tipden aged upwds. of 17 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes and that her father Samuel Tipden was bound an Apprentice to and Served the last three months and upwards of his Apprenticeship with Mr Clks a Smith in Drum Alley in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Octr. 14th. 1749 before}
