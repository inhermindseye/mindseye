Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Dorothy Lott aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that on the 22d. day of March 1784 she Intermarried with William Lott at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch That her said Husband in the Month of January 1787 Sailed in the Warren Hastings East India Man for China and Bombay That he did not Return till October 1788 and during all the Time he was absent she never heard from him And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 11th. day of November last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Bray in Hemlock Court which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary Ann That one Thomas Hurley a Journeyman Taylor who lives with a Mr. Franklin a Taylor at Eaton is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of October 1787. and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Thomas Hurley is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 5th- day of June 1789 } R Butler

Dorothy Lott

Resworn 14th Janry. 1790 before.

Thomas Hurley is a Short Man dark Complexion wears his Hair loose in his Neck about 23 years Old.
