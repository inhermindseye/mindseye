Middx  John Perry aged 47 Years upon Oath saith he lived a Yearly Servant with Esqr Temple now Lord Palmerston in St. James Square in the Parish of St James in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly wages of £5 for One Year and upwards (being all that time a Singleman). and quitted his Lordships Service about 30 Years ago and has not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes live a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn May 4th 1751 before Tho Lane .

John Perry
