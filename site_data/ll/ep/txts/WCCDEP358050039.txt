Middx 

Ann Falkner on Behalf of Mary Ballance her Grandaughter an Inst. aged upwds. of three months upon Oath saith that the sd. Instant is the Daughter of Middleton Ballance by Sarah his Wife and that the sd. Middleton to Ballance lived a Covenant Servt. whilst a Singleman with Mr Wade a Taylor in Billeter Lane in the Parish of Athallows Staining London at the Yearly Wages of £10 Dyet and Lodging for the Space of four Years and has not since his sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif she mark rem of Ann Falkner

Sworn April 21st 1749 before} Wm: Jones
