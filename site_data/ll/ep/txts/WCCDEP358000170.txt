Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  The past is this Woman belongs to Lewishaw by less 40 days there with Mr Delton with whom she lived near 29th. in the Temple  to wit Hannah Grinsted aged about 18 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House  paid Taxes  or ever served any Apprenticeship Says that her father John Grinsted Rented and lived in a House in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls at the yearly Rent of 50 Guineas where this Examinant was born  as she has been informed since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing where by to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere And further on her Oath saith that on the 11th. day of Octr. last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of a Mr. Williams in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which has not yet been baptized And further saith that one James Brady  Clerk to  Councellor Jeykin of the Kings Bench Walks in the Temple is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body sometime in the Month of March or April last and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as afsd. and that the said James Brady and no one else did beget the same and that he is the real and true father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Decr. 1785 before R Butler

Hannah Grinsted

This was Settled by 10 Gas & Ext. down & a Note of himself Councellor Jekyll & Smart for 10 Gs. at 12 s.
