Middx  Avis Harris aged abt. Twenty Six Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that abt. Two Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Heath in Arundel Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds for the Space of Eight Years or thereabts. that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 7th. of Decr. 1741 before}
