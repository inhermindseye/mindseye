Middx  Eliz. Pym aged upwds. of 51 Years (removed from the Parish of St Giles without Cripplegate London by Order dated the 4th. Day of July 1755 upon Oath Saith she is the wido. of Tho. Pym deceased to whom She was married at the Fleet London upwds. of 17 Yrs. Since that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. late husband Served Seven years apprentiship with Mr Weller a Clock maker in Devereux Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and never rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year and that Since the Decease of her sd. late husband (which happened abt. 10 yrs. ago) she, this Examint. has not rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt.

Sworn July 5th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

Eliz Pym
