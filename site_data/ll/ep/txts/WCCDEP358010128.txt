Saint Clement

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Thomas Shidley aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith That he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Benjn. Smith a Cornfactor in Orange Street Leicester Fields in the parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex for 15 years at the yearly Wages of £10 that he hath quitted said Service about 15 years ago since which time he has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. Augt. 1787 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

his Thos. Shidley mark
