Middx.  Cath: Brew aged abt. 60 Yrs. upon Oath says she never was married says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Robins Esqr . in Lincolns Inn fields in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx. for two Yrs. & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of six Pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. fifteen Yrs.

Sworn the 6th. of June 1757 before}
