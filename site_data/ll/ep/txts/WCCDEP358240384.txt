St Clement Danes 30th Janry 1794

Middx and Westminster }  St Bridget orse St Bride London  (to wit) Augustin Newce aged 23 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he never was Married that he on or about the twenty fifth day of May in the year of our Lord 1789 he was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Thomas Rickaby  Printer then of Little Queen Street  Middlesex for the Remainder three Years being the Remainder of a Term of Seven Years three of which were then un unexpired expired that he this Examinant served the whole of the said Term for which he was bound as aforesaid and lived with her Master lodged during the last Eight Weeks of such his Apprenticeship in Hanging Sword Alley in th parish of St Bridget otherwise St Bride London and hath not done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or belief

Sworn the 30th day of Janry 1794 before us W Addington B: Kennett

Augustine Newce

Dobbins Attendd. when Pauper sworn & delvd him 30th Janry. 1794
