St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  To wit Job Ridgeway aged about 55 years or thereabouts upon oath Saith that he was married to his present Wife Ann at Christ Church in the County of Surrey on or about the year 176 (by Banns) that he hath no Children now living that Previous to his Marriage while he was Single this Examinant lived as an Hired yearly Served with William Wood  distiller  Jore street in the Parish of St. Mary Lambeth for the Space of three years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas Per Ann. and since Marriage this Examinant hath never Rented any House or Apartment to the Value of Ten Pounds, or done any Act or Matter or thing whatsoever to obtain a subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief.

Sworn this 16 day of April 1795 W Addington J Mathew

Job Ridgway
