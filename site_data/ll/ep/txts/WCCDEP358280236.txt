Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  to wit Mary Scott aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Jefferys in Tottenham Court Road in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middx for Two years at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds for two that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she has not done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 4th. day of January now last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Bamber in Hemlock Court which hath since been Christened by the Name of George That one Charles Cohant who lives at No. 3 in Eagle Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts- Body for these Two years last paid and begotten the same thereon and that the said Charles Cohant is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 24th. day of July 1789 } Wm. Kitchiner

her Mary Scott Mark

Mary Scott lives at No. 1 Bell Yard
