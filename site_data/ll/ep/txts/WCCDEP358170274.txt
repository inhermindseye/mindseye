Middx  Benjamin Blakely aged Sixty Years upon his Oath saith that he lived in and Rented a House in the Strand near Beauford Buildings in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Seven Years at the Yearly Rent of Forty eight Pounds & hath left the same about Fifteen Years that he never after rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person to her a Singleman & witht. Child or Childn. as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 8th. Octr. 1742 before} Tho Lane

Benjamin Blakely
