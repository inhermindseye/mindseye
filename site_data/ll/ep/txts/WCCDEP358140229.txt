Middx.  Katherine Baker on the behalf of Ann Saunders aged abt. 72 Years upon Oath says that the said Ann Saunders is the Widow of Richd. Saunders deceased says the said Richd. Saunders lived in and Rented a house in Off Alley York Buildings in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr. for Twenty Years at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds and upwards and there dyed. Says the said Ann Saunders has not since the death of her husband Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year as the said Ann Saunders hath informed this Deponent and which Information this Deponent verily believes to be true

Sworn Feby. 8th. 1759 before} Paul Vaillant  Sam Pechell

Kath Baker

Upon Mr Wimberleys Complt. J: P:
