Westmr  Anthoney Guilder Aged about Twenty Four Years Removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex By Order Dated the Eleventh Day of April 1764 Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Mary Ann in the Parish Church of St. Anne in the Liberty of Westmr. on the Second Day of January Last Says he never was an Apprentice Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says he was Born Opposite the Spotted Dog in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and that his Father was a Native of Holland and Never Gained a Settlement in England

as this Examinant hath Been Informed by his Mother and which Information he Verily Believes to be true.

Sworn the 16th. Day of April 1764 : Before} Fran: Bedwell

Anthony Guilder

Upon Mr. Olivers Complt J. W
