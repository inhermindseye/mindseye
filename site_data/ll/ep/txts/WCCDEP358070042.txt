Westmr. to wit Mary Dell Aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Williams a Tobacconist in Queen Street in the Parish of Saint Saviours Southwark in the County of Surry upwards of One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of five Years and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 23d. day of November 1772 before Us}  Jno Lane

her Mary Dell Mark

Witness E: Field
