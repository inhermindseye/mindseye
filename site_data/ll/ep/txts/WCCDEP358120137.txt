Middx  Thos. Jones upon Oath saith that in or abt. the Year 1715 he rented a house Known by the Sign of the Coach & Horses in Thrift Street in the Parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly rent of Eighteen pounds lived therein about Nine months and has not Since rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn July 2d. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane

Thomas Jones
