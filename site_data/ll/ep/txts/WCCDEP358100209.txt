Middx to wit John Jay Aged about 23 Years on his Oath says he intermarried to Rebecca his Wife at the Parish Church of Old Street in the County of Middx about Fifteen Months Ago & by whom he hath One Child now living named Sarah Aged about three Months Says he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Robt Burton the Sign of the Star Inn in Carey Street in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx for fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds besides Diet and Lodgings hath Quitted his said Service about Five Years ago since which time he hath Gained no Other Settlement Neither by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of £10 paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th day of April 1784 before G Mercer Jno Barnfather

his John Jay Mark
