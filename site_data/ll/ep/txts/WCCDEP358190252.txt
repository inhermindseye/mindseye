Middx  Eleanor Brooks Aged abt. Sixty years upon Oath Says she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr Goodwin a Chymist near Suffolk Street in the Parish of St Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . for One Year & Upwards at the Yearly Wages of four pounds & hath quitted Said Service upwards of Twenty Years says She was at that time a Wido: & without Child or Childn: hath not Since rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Land Tax or poor Rate or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn July 3d.: 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eleanor Brooks
