Middlesex ss}  John Andrews  Beadle of the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate Middx maketh oath That he has been informed upon the Creditable Testimony upon oath of Mary Wilson  wife of Edmund Wilson mariner That her Said husband who as this Depont has also been informed & believes is now absent from her at Sea was duly bound Apprentice to & Served the whole thereof with one John Smith in the Parish of Greenwich in the County of Kent  Waterman & that her Sd husband did not afterwards rent a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She hath a Child named Mary aged about Sixteen Years the lawful Issue of the Sd Mary Wilson the Mothr by her Sd husband to Whom She was lawfully married & which Child hath done no Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 2d. day of December 1778 before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey

John Andrews
