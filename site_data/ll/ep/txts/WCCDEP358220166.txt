Westmr.  Rebecca Seaborn Aged about 35 Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one John Davis a Perukemaker in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years and an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Years ago and after quitting that service she hath never since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 19th. day of July 1770 before me W Kelyngs

The Mark of Rebecca Seaborn

Witness Will: Hughes
