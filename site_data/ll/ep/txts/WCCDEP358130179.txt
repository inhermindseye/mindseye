Middx  Elizth. Twist Wife of Danl. Twist upon Oath says, She Intermarried with Daniel Twist at the Fleet London about Twelve Years ago; Says her Husband is a Soldier in Lord George Mannor 's Regimt. of Foot now in Scotland , & that he serv'd his Apprenticeship of Seven Years to one Mr. Walker a Shoemaker in the Parish of Tiverton in Namptwich in the County of Chester . as She hath been informed & verily believes, & further says her said Husband never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Parish Taxes or Served any Parochial Office, & that She hath two Children by him (Videlicet) Eleanor aged Ten Years & Jane aged Six Years, and She being Poor is not able to Maintain them.

Sworn the 19th. Day of June 1756 . before}
