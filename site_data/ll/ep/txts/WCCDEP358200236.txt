Middx  Robert Fullham Aged about Fifty One Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Elizth. in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx about Twelve Years ago Says he Rented a House in Butcher Row in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes One Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds hath quitted said House about Nine Years & hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 8th of Feby. 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Robert Fullham
