After Birth (2d. Examn) Mary Rainer & Margt Rainer her B. Child

St Clement Danes 29th December Novr 1793

Middlesex & Westminster }  Vide settlemt. to Examination before Birth page 51 in this Book }  Child born in St CD Workhouse }  Reputed Father (to wit) Mary Rainer  singlewoman aged 22 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that on or about the 29th day of October last at the Workhouse or Poor House belonging to the  parish of St Clement Danes and situate within the same she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which  hath been half Baptized by the name of Margaret Rainer and become chargeable to the said parish that Clovely Shoemaker now or late a Lodger at the Blackmoor's Head in Hedge Lane in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex is the only real true father of the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he having carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body on or about the fifth day of May last and begotten the said Child thereon

Sworn the 29th day of November 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Mary Rainer 's Mark

Witness Benj Newman
