Middlesex ss  Samuel Firmin maketh Oath That divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture to Samuel Freeman of the Parish of Bocking in the County of Essex Wheelwright and Served five years of his said apprenticeship in the said Parish and by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and this Deponent Saith he has not since done any Act Whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th day of June 1767 before  Chris Scott  Rob Pell

Samuel Firmin
