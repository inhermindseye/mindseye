Westmr. to wit Mary Hall aged about forty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Colonel Monntalieu in Arlbemarl Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for One Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Nine Years ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 18th. day of October 1781 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Mary Hall
