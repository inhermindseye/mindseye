Westmr. 

John Taylor upon Oath saith about 16 Years since he rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £17 in Bennets Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and lived therein about Sixteen Years and has not Since he quitted the sd house rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif and that he hath two Childn. by his late Wife  Mary Taylor decd. namely Josiah aged near 12 Yrs. and Mary aged abt. 9 Years

Sworn Janry 16th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of John Taylor
