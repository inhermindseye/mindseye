Westmr. 

Geo Nutkins upon Oath saith he intermarryed with his present Wife Jane at Paddington Church upwards of Eighteen Yrs. since and that about 20 Yrs. ago and whilst a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Thos. Goodall in Kenton Green in the Parish of Harrow in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £9 for two Years & upwds. and has not since he Quitted his sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 9d 1748 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Geo Nutkins
