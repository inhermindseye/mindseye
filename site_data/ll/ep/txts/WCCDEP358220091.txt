Westmr. to wit Sarah Wilson Aged about Twenty Eight years on her Oath says she never was married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a year or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly servant with one William Archer who kept the Flying Horse in Grubb Street in the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate for Two years at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said service about Eight years ago Etc hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 27th day of November 1769 before us} Thos Kynaston 

her Sarah Wilson mark

Witness Thos. Jones
