Middx  John Forster upon Oath saith he Intermarried with Mary his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Martin in Salisbury upwds. of 9 Yrs. ago by whom he hath one Child Mary aged upwds. of 2 Yrs. Says he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Paul Cashman a Nail maker in West Bromwich in Staffordshire & never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr. 6th. 1750 before Tho Lane

The Mark of John Forster
