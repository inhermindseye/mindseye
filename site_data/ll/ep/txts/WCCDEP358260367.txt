Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Lewis aged about 35 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she never lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or ever bound an Apprentice That she hath been informed which Information She verily believes to be true that she was born in Greyhound Court Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 8th. day of Novr. 1798 before me } Saml. Edward

her Ann Lewis Mark
