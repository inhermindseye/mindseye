Middx  Sarah Lloyd aged abt. 59 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife Widow of John Lloyd who hath been Dead abt. seven yrs. & that she cannot give any Acct. of his Settlement says before her intermarriage wth. the said John Lloyd & whilst she was a Single Woman she lived a yearly hired Servant with Mr. John Welch at the Rose & Crown the Bottom of Stoney Lane in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry for five years at the yrly. Wages of five Pounds.

Sworn the 4th. day of April 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Lloyd
