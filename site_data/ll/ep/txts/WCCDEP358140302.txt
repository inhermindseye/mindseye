MIddx.  Martha Isbell aged upwds. of 23 Yrs. upon Oath says she Never was Married nor Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year Says her late father Benjamin Isbell deced informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true that being a Single Man and Unmarried he lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year with one John Newet a Staymaker in Blackmoor Street in the parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds dyet Washing and lodging and never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 13th. day of Octr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Martha Isbell
