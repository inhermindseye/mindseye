Wistmr to wit Elizabeth Douglas otherwise Purnell Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says the never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further says that she was born the Corner of one Tan Alley facing Hungerford Street in the Parish of saint Martin in Fields in the Liberty of Westmr and that one Mr. Pumell who kept the Publik Home there is the reputed Father of this Examinant as she has been Informed and Verity believes the same to the true and that after her said reputed Fathers death Death she never gained any subsequent Settlement as above mentioned

Sworn this 18th.day of Janry 1774 before R. Butler

Elizth Douglas
