Middlesex ss}  James Fowler maketh oath That divers Years Ago he was duly bound to & Served the Whole of his Apprenticesp with Robert Crew in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Adersgate London  Gold & Silver Wire Drawer & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he has not Since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain anothr Settlement That he hath a wife named Elizabeth & three Children namely Sarah aged about Seven years James Aged about three years & Jane aged about eight Months the Issue of this Deponent by his Said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 30. day of June 1778 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkey

James Fowler
