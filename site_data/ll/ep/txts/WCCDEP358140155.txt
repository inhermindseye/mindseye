Middx.  Maria Andrew aged abt. 57 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Andrew who has been dead abt. 18 Years says her said late husband Rented a house (next door to the Tabot Alehouse) in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £14 for three Years & there dyed says she has not since her said husbands death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn the 31st. day of August 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

Maria Andrew
