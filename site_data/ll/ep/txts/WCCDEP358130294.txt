Middx  Sarah Bull aged abt. 38 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Edwd. Bull who hath been dead abt. 13 Yrs. says she intermarried with the said Edwd. Bull at the Fleet Lond: on the 13th of August 1733. says her sd. late Husband served upwards of a Year the last part of his Apprenticeship with a Cloggmaker near Clemts. Inn Gate in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx. & then absconded from his Master & never was assigned over to any Person or Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says she hath not since his Death Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes that she hath one Child by her said Husband now living (to wit) Sarah aged abt. Fifteen Yrs.

Sworn the 4th. March 1757 before}
