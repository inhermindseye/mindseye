Middx  Sarah Cole Aged about Forty five Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Wm. Cole with whom she Intermarried on the Twelfth Day of Novr. 1738 at the Fleet London Says her said Husband Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Wilford a Butcher in Vere Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & never after Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year. & she hath not since her said Husbands Death Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 19th Day of February 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant

Sarah Cole
