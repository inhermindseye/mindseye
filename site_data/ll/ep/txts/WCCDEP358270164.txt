Margt Woodburn & Ann Robinson her B. Child

(After Birth)

St Clement Danes Decr 9th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman's settlement}  Child's birth  Reputed Father (to wit) Margt. Woodburn aged Twenty years & upward singlewoman Upon Oath Saith that she never was Married never served an Apprenticeship lived as an hired yearly or did any other Act matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlements in her own right that she is informed and believes she is the legitimate Child of Roger and Bridget Woodburn that her her father served the latter part of his Apprenticeship (under  an assigned Indre) with Mr Green at the parish of Ulverston near Liverpool and died on or abt about five Months ago never having obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Examinant Upon her Oath further saith that on or about the 16th day of January 1792 at the house of Mr Brooks's Bell in Bear yard in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child which hath been since Christened by the Name of Ann Robinson that Jacob Robinson now or late of Ulverston aforesaid Malster is the only real true father of the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid he having first had carnal knowledge of this Deponents sometime in or

about the Month of April 1791.  & at several times since at some or one of which times he did beget the said Child on her Body

Sworn the 9th Decr 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Margt Woodburn 's Mark

Witness Garrett
