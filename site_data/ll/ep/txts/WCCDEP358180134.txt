Mary Carroll upon her Oath saith that on the 25th. Day of July she intermarr with her prest. Husbd. Wm. Carroll that the sd. Wm. Carroll lived a Yearly hired Servt . (being then a Singleman ) with this Exaiants father  Paul Garvey at the Horse shoe & Maypye in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes  Taylor at the Yearly Wages of £10. for the Space of Two Yrs. & upwds. that since his sd. Service he hath not rented £10. a Yr. paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Exaiants knowledge or beleif that the sd. Wm. Carroll is now a Soldier in his Majestys Service & she having Two Childn. viz: Wm. aged abt. 3 Yrs. & Danl. aged abt. 14 Months & being poor is not able to maintain them

Sworne 29th. Novr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Carroll
