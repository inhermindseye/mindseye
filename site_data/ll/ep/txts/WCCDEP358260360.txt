Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Hartwell aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith That about Three years ago she Intermarried with John Hartwell her late Husband who has been dead about Ten Months at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath One Child living Vizt. Sarah aged about Four Months That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Three years at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds that her said Husband died in the said House

Sworn this 31st day of October 1798 before me } W Carter

her Sarah Hartwell Mark

Witness Day
