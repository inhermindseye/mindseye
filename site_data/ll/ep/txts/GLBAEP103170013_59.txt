Middlesex ss}  William Cook the Brother of Elizabeth Cook Singlewoman maketh oath that his Said Sister is at present disordered in her Mind That he this Deponent has been informed by his late Parents & believes the Same to be true that he & also his Said Sister was both born in  Blue Boar Court now called Princes Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London & this Depont. has also been informed & believes the Same to be true that his late Father John Cook deced Served his Apprenticeship With a Cabinet maker (whose name he has forgot) in the Minories in the Same parish & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & this Deponent Can give no other Account respecting his Said Fathers Settlement or of his late Mother deced That to this Deponents knowledge or belief his Said Sister never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 10. day of October 1776 Before  Parkr Rowlands  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of William Cook
