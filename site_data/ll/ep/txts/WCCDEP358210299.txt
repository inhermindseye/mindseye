Middx & Westmr.  Ann Shingler Aged about Twenty two Years, Upon Oath says she never was Married, and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Becket a Farmer in the Parish of Whitchurch in the County of Salop for Three Years or there -abouts at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds, Quitted same about Three Years ago, and hath not since Rented House, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year; And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes within the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex (where she now lodges) she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born; Says that one Saul Richardson a Ticket Porter in the City of London had carnal knowledge of her body the latter End of the Month of October 1767, and at several times afterwards, at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant, and that the said Saul Richardson and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn the 16th, day of July 1768 , before} 

Ann Shingler
