Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Motram aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That she hath lived as a hired yearly Servant with Thos. Batrich of Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for these Four years and upwards last past at the yearly wages of Five Pounds And this Deponent further upon her Voluntary Oath saith That she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Male who did live with Mr. Batrich but who died on or about the Third day of July now last past the Father of the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant the said John Male having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of January last past and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Male was the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 4th. day of Octr. 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Mary Motram Mark

Witness B. Newman

Father dead Vide Examn. Died on board the Inflexible

Child born in the House 10th. Decr. 1797 named Elizth. Motram

Taken away by the Mother the 2d. day of Janry. 1798

Qrs. what Allowance has she
