Middlesex ss  Elizabeth Hall  Single woman Maketh oath That she has been informed by her Father Christopher Hall that he was bound to and duly Served the Whole to his apprenticeship with Samuel Hall his Father in Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate in the County of Middx  Comb- maker The which information this Deponent verily believes to be true and this Depont. Satih that to her knowledge information or belief her said Father never did any Act to gain a Settlement subsequent to his said apprenticeship And this Depont. farther Saith that she was never married nor ever was bound apprentice or lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right And Lastly this Depont. Saith that She hath a Child named Richard Kelly aged about three Years and ten Months The Which Child is illegitimate and was born in Frying Pan Alley in Bells Yard in the Parish of Christ Church Spittle Fields in the said County and baptised in the Parish Church there and that one Michael Kelly late of the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate London weaver to Whom this Depont. was never Married did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child.

Sworn this first Day of July-1767 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

The Mark of Elizabeth Hall
