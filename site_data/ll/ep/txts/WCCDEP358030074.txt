Middx  Judith Haseldon aged about 43 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she was by Indenture dated the 3d. Day of July 1716 bound an Apprentice to & Served Five Years Apprentiship with Mrs. Beatrice Gary of a Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Mantua maker Says she never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Decr. 24th 1745 before  Wm: Jones

Judith Haseldon
