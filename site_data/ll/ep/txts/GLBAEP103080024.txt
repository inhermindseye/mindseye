Middx ss  Mary Watson  widow of John Watson deced Maketh oath that about Twenty eight years ago her said late husband was bound an Apprentice to Joseph Hall a Grocer who then lived in Leaden hall Street in the Parish of Saint Catherine Cree Church in the City of London with whom he duly served all his Apprenticeship And by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there And that her said late husband did not afterwards live as a hired servant by the Year nor Rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor serve any Parish offices nor gain any subsequent settlement to this Deponents knowledge information or belief

Sworn this ninth day of January 1758 before  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

Mary Watson
