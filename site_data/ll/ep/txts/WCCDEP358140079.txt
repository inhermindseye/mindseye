Middx.  Mary Bateman aged abt. 65 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Rowland Bateman who hath been dead upwards of thirty Years says her said late husband Rented a house the Corner of Bennets Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of thirty Pounds or thereabouts lived therein abt. two Years & quitted same abt. two Years before his death & never after Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says she has not since her said late husbands death Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 27th. day of January 1758 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Bateman
