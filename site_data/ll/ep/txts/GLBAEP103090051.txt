Middlesex ss  John Clear aged about Sixty Six Years maketh Oath that he hath been Credibly informed & verily believes the same to be true That this Deponent Father James Clear deced was born in the Kingdom of Ireland And this Deponent doth not know or was ever informed whether his said Father ever did any Act Whereby to gain a settlement in that Part of Great Britain Called England or in Scotland And this Deponent farther Saith that he hath been informed by his late Mother Dorothy Clear deced that she herself and also this Deponent were born in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Comity of Middlesex the which information this Deponent verily believes to be true And this Depont. doth not know or was ever informed whether his said Mother ever did any Act whereby to gain a settlement in her own right subsequent to her Birth and before her Marriage with this Deponents said Father And this Deponent father Saith that he never lives as a hired servant for a Year or was ever bound Apprentice or ever rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or ever served any Parish offices or ever Paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a settlement in his own right subsequent to his Birth And that he this Deponent is a single & unmarried Man.

Sworn this day of 1767 before}
