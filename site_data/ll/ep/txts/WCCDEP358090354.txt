Middx  Ann Green Aged about Thirty Two Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Joseph Green who died about 7 Years ago and by whom she Hath no Children Living Says Her said late Husband before his Intermarrd. with this Examinant and whilst He was a Singleman He lived a Hired Yearly Servt. with One Mr. Beech at Eastbrough in the Parish of Rickmansworth in the County of Herts upwards of One year at the Yearly Wages of Eight Guineas and after quitting his said Masters Service he never gained any other Subsequent Settlemt neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husbands Death And further on her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children whch. is or are likely to be born an Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when Born And further says that one Charles Wrenn a Coach harness Maker who now lives in Wimpole Street St. Mary Le Bone did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at his the sd. Mr. Wrenns Hour  Mary Le Bone Lane on the 8th. Day of March last and at Sevl. difft. times afterwds. at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the Sd. Chas. Wrenn & no other Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 7th. day of Decr. 1782. before  Wm Kitchiner

Ann Green
