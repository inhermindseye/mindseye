Middx  Cath: Halding  Wido . of Jose: Halding upon her Oath saith that she was married to her late Husband Jose: Halding at the Parish Church of St. Andw. Holbourn on the 18th.. Day of September 1718 & that he hath been Dead about One Year & further saith that her sd. late Husband was by Indenture bearing Date the 1st- Day of May 1711 bound an Apprentice to & served his Apprenttispp with one Mr. Low in Clare Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & that her sd. late Husband in his Life Time nor this Exaiant since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 26th- Day of Augt. 1741 before}
