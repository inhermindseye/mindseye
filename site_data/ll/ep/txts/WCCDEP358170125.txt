Middx  Robert Inwood aged about Twenty Six Years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with his now Wife Eliz : on the 31st- Day of September 1737 & that abt- 5 Years ago & whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Smith in Clare Markett in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds for the Space of one Yr. & upwards & that since he left his sd. Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of One Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that he hath 2 Childn- (namely) Eliz : aged abt. Two Years & Stephen aged 8 Months & being Poor is not able to Maintaine them

Sworne the 10th- of Septr- 1741 before}

Robert Inwood
