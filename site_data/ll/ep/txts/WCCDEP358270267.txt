Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Hickman aged about 24 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Heath in Crown Place in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two Years at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Thirteen Months since which time she hath not done any Act matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes That one Edward Carwood a Journeyman Taylor who lodges at No. 9 Little Camberwell Place near Westminster Bridge and who works for a Taylor near Charing Cross is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Edward Carwood and no other Person did beget the same & that he is the only real and true father thereof

14th. June 1796 Far. gave a Note for £15. at 5 Installmts. Vizt. £3. each at at 3, 6, 9, 12, & 15 Mos. all of which were duly Paid Child born in the house 9th. May 1796 named Francis sent to Enfield  15th. June 1796 .

Sworn this fifth day of May 1796 } Benj Tebbs

her Margaret Hickman Mark

Man gave Notes for £15.
