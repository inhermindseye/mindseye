Westmr.  Martha Thorpe Aged about Twenty Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one John Mead at the Nags head in Naggs Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for Sixteen Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and after quitting the same she never rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 2d. day of February 1771 before} Tho Lane

her Martha Thorpe mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
