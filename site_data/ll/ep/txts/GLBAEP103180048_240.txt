Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Gardner  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servt by the year by & with Mrs Spelter Tallow Chandler in Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for abt five Months That this Deponent was born in the Sd Parish & never was an Apprentice nor was ever married & never did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself and this Deponent Can give no account respecting her Parents Settlement Save that her late Mother died in the Workhouse belonging to the Said Parish

Sworn this 30 day of June 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Elizabeth Gardner
