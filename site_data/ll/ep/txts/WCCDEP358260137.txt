Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster on behalfe of Thomas Winter an Infant aged about Nine years and Six Months Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Thomas Winter is the illegitimate Child of one James Winter deceased and that the said James Winter Rented and lived in a house in Parker Street Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for about five years at the yearly Rent of £20. and that after the said James Winter quitted the said house he never to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this first day of July 1796 N Bond Rust: Clarke

James Talboys
