Middx  Eliz: Bolt aged abt. 55 Yrs upon Oath says she never was married was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant says her father Jas. Bath served one Year the last part of his Apprenticeship with Mr. Chas. Doe a Comb maker at Chain Gate in the Parish of St. Saviour Southwark in the County of Surry

Sworn the 4th of June 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Eliz: Bolt
