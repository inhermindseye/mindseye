Middx  Eliz Bailey upon Oath Saith she never was married and that never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year that her father John Bailey deceased rented two houses (in one of which he lived) in Stable yard in the Parish of St. Margaret in the City of Westmr . and that the Rent of one of the sd houses was £5 and of the other £5.10 by the Year and that her sd. late father never after he quitted the sd houses rented ten Pounds a Year or Paid Parish taxes

Sworn the 16th. Augt. 1753 before}

The mark of Eliz Bailey
