Saint Clement Danes 5th April 1786.

Middlesex (to wit) Ann Walker  singlewoman aged 20 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith, that She never was married, saith that she lived as an hired Yearly Settlemen  Servant with Mr. Hawkins a Printer in Little Hire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £3. and 3s:10d for upwards & a Year that she quitted her said service about five Years ago, and since that time has not to the best of her Knowledge gained any subsequent Settlement; And further this Examinant on her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes, and that Christopher Swales a Gentleman's Servant , who now live with Captain   who did lodge in Bond Street Piccadilly is the real and true father of such Child or Children, be having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of July last several times at some or one of which times be did beget such Child or Children with which she is so pregnant

Sworn this 5th. Apr. 1786 . before R Butler

her Ann Walker Mark
