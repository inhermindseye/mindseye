Middx  Mary Hubbard Aged about Twenty Two years on her Oath Says She never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Roberts at the Union Coffee house Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that on the first day of September 1777 she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in an Hospital Called the Westminster new Lying in Hospital near Westmr. Bridge in the County of Surry which was soon afterwards Baptized Hannah and is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same And that one Thomas Box a Footman to Lady Dashwood in St. James Palaces is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of October 1776 but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at sevl. different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Thomas Box & no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of August 1778 - before}

her Mary Hubbard Mark
