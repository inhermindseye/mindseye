St. Clement Danes 9t Janry. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster } (to wit) Elizabeth Fraser  Singlewoman Aged 23 years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Trowten Mathematical Instrument Maker in fleet Street in the parish of St Bride Bridgot in the City of London about Eighteen Months And year & Nine Months at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that she quitted that Service on the 4th day of December 1787 . and since hath not obtained any Subsequent or other legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge belief And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that on the fifteenth day of July last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the House of Mr. Shade in Holywell Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster which Child hath been Christened by the Names of Ann Harrison at the Church of the said parish of St. Clement Danes And saith that Edward Thomas Harrison  a Mathematical Instrument Maker who now lives at No 21 on Clerkenwell Green is the only real & true father there of such Child he & no one else having had Carnal Knowledge of this Deponents Body & begotten the Same thereon And Lastly that the said Child hath become Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes she

destitute having become destitute & incapable of maintaining it without Assistance

Sworn the 9th day of Janry 1792 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Eliz Fraser
