Middx  Geo. Webb aged abt. 53 Yrs. upon Oath saith he intermarried with Ann his Wife abt. 12 Years ago in Wamgate Church in York & has by her two Children Ann aged abt. 10 Yrs & Eliz aged abt. 8 Yrs. Says he Served Six Years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Edward Holden at the Crown Tavern in Fleet street in the Parish of St Dunstan in the West London and at the End of the sd. Six Yrs. went away from his sd. Master and Enlisted into his Majestys Service Says he never was assigned over to any person for the remainder of his Apprentiship nor ever rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in Any other place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn March 27th. 1750 before}
