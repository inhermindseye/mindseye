Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Morgan aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant for Thirteen Months and upwards with one Mr. Spencer who kept the Kings head the back of St. Clements at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said service about Seven Months years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly servant for One year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Decr. 1789 R Butler

her Sarah Morgan Mark
