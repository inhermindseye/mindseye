Saint Clement Danes 7th. Septr. 1787

Middlesex to wit}  Ann Dumolo  singlewoman aged between twenty one and twenty two Year upon her Oath saith That she never was married that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Evans a Taylor in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Six Guineas for about three years that she quitted such his Service about elevan Weeks since And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is now Pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and that one George Aram a Journeyman Taylor who lately lived & worked with the said Mr. Evans, and no one else is the real & true father of such Child or Children he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponent Body in the Months of January and February last several times at some or one of which times he did beget the Child or Children which of she is so pregnant as aforesaid

Sworn this 7th Septr 1787 before me Wm Kitchiner

Ann Dumols

Dumolo 's Child

George Aram is a middle sized Man Pale faced light Brown Hair wears it draped (father lives at Thurgator in Nottingham alive is a Taylor there) generally wears blue or brown Cloaths is a thin man-gray Eyes large Eye brows and the Apprentice  white Hart Yard  Drury Lane as a House of Call

he left his Address Mr. Geo: Aram to be left with Mr. Cheatham at Mr. Robert Settles Southwell Nottinghamshire

Mr. Burbidge has this Warrt. Mr. Geo: Arain  Jun Taylor at Thurgoton Ferry near Newark Nottinghamshire

Child born 1st Nov. 1787 : Christened Geo. Dumolos
