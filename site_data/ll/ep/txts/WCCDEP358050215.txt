Westmr. 

Eliza. Povall upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 16 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr.  Chas at the George Inn Aldergate Street in the Parish of St Botolph witht. Aldergate London at the Yearly wages of Seven pounds for One Year & upwards and has not since She quitted her said Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 25th. 1749 before Tho Lane

The mark of Eliz Povall
