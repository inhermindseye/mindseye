Middx  Antipas Sherrington aged about 63 Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife in the Parish Church of Hayam in ye County of Norfolk upwards of 25 Years Since Says he served 5 Years the First part of his Apprenticeship with Mr Haughton Tinman in Middle Row Holborn in the Parish of St Andrews Holborn above the Barrs in ye County of Middx and never was assigned over to any other Person Says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid any Taxes or Lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn February 8th 1763 Before}
