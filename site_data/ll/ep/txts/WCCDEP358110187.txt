Middx  Wm. Dymock aged near 16 Years upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice by the Parish Offices of St. Giles witht. Cripplegate London to John Chambers Childs Pump maker till he should attain the Age of Twenty four Years and lived with the sd. Chambers upwards of two Years as his Apprentice during the last Year of which term he lodged in his Masters house in Harrow Alley  whitechapel in the Parish of St Botolph witht. Aldgate London and that he never was assigned over to any person nor ever lived a Servt. for forty days in any place with his Masters Knowledge & Consent

Sworn Feby. 22d. 1753 before} Tho Lane

William Dimmock
