Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  Settlemt. in the Rolls Liberty  to wit Edward Mascall aged about 43 years upon his Oath saith that on the 24th. day of October 1773 he Intermarried with Jane his present wife at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That he has One Child living by his said Wife namely Elizabeth aged about 18-years That he served Seven years Apprenticeship with Mr. Robinson a Painter & Glazier in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex That he has gained no subsequent Settlement either by Renting house Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paying Taxes living as a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 28th. day of August 1794 before us M Addington J Pownall.

Edward Maskall
