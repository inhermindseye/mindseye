Middlesex & Westminster}  Swear to St. Clemt.  A to wit Mary Holles  Widow aged forty two Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith She is the Widow of William Holles who has been dead about nineteen Years, and with whom She intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Ann soho in the Liberty of Westminster about twenty Six Years ago, Says that her said Husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with one Richard Freeman  Peruke Maker in Dreverens Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and that her said Husband did not after he was not of his said Apprenticeship till the time of his Death, nor has this Examinant since her said Husbands Death ever rented ten pounds a Year paid Taxes lived as an hired Yearly Servant or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Deponents Knowledge he or she may have gained a Settlement Elsewhere than in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 13th. Novr 1784 before M Addington

Mary Holles
