Middx  Mary Holladay  Widow aged about Eighty Years upon Oath Says she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Margarett Molloy in Appollo Court in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the west in the City of London for Seven Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Two pounds ten Shillings and hath quitted her said Service about four Years since Says she hath not since she left the sd. Mrs. Molloys Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn July 22d 1746 before  Danl Gach

The Mark of Mary Holladay
