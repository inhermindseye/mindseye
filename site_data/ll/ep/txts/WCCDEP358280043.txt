Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizth. Stevens aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Dutton who loged Lodged at Mr. Herbst in Cooks Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 18 Months at the yearly Wages of Six Guineas hath quitted said Service about 18 Months ago since which time she has not done any Act to gain any Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her oath saith that she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and become Chargeable to the said Parish And further saith that one Nathl. Whitehead a Gentleman Servt. who lately lived with Mr. Dawson a Woollen Draper in Wych Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of March last and at Sevl. times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Nathl. Whitehead is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 31st Day of Octr. 1786 } R. Butler

her Elizth. Stevens mark

(Vid Post.)
