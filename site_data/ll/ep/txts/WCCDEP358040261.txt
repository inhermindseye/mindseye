Westmr. 

Ann Littleton aged about 50 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of David Littleton who dyed upwards of Seven Years ago and that her sd. late husband lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £21 in Hollis Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. for several Years and there dyed and this Examt. continued to live in and rent the same house for Six months or thereabouts after the Death of her sd. husband and has not since she Quitted the sd. house rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants Knowledge or belief and further upon has Oath saith she has a Child now with her namely Eliz Corner Fowley aged about Seven Years who as this Examinant has been Informed and believes is the Daughter of Edward Corner Fowley by Eliz. his Wife both deceased

and was born in Salisbury Court the Parish of St. Bridget calgary Brides London and that the sd. Edward Corner Fowley the Father of the sd. Child was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England

Sworn Sepr. 28th. 1748 before} Jas. Traser

The Mark of Ann Littleton
