Middx  Mary Bayne aged abt. 24 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Wm. Bayne (now at Sea) says her sd. husbd. before his intermarriage with this Examinant & whilst he was a singleman he lived a hired yrly Servt . with Mr. Boswell a Cow keeper in the Parish of Plymouth in the County of Devon for one year & upwds. & after he quitted his said Service never Rented ten Pounds a yr. paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt.

Sworn the 10th. May 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Bayne
