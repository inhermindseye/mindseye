Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Litchfield aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Thorpe in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Sixteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of Novr. 1797 before me} J Hunter

her Elizabeth Litchfield Mark

Witness A Burbidge
