Saint Clement Danes 25th. Augt. 88

Middlesex to wit Lawrence Foy aged thirty five or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith that he lived as an hired Yearly Servant for upwards of a Year with Mrs. Leigh of Cross Street Islington in the Parish of Saint Mary Islington in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of ten pounds or thereabouts that he was single and unmarried not having Child or Children during such his Servitude that he has quitted the said Mr Leigh Service about three Years ago, and since quitting the same has not done any Act whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other or Subsequent Settlement And this Deponent further Saith that he was married to Elizabeth his now Wife at the Parish Church of Kensington in the County of Middlesex by Barns according to the rites of the Church of England about One Year and three Quarters of a Year ago, that he this Deponent is poor and unable to maintain himself and his said Wife

Sworn this 25th. Augt 1788 before us Philip Dyot D Walker}

her Lawrence Foy Mark
