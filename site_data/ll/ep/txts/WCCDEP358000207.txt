Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Edward Oldis aged about 51 years upon his Oath saith that he Intermarried with Ann his late Wife at the Parish Church of St. Michael Royal about 18 years ago and who has been dead about a week that he has 4 Children living by his said Wife vizt. Edwd . aged 17 years or thereabouts Provided and Joseph aged 13 years or thereabouts both Provided for Edoth aged about 2 years and a Quarter and Sarah aged abouts a fortnight both unprovided for that he Rented and lived in a House at Kingston in the Parish of Kingston in the County of Surry for 3 years at the yearly Rent of £18 hath quitted same about 13 years ago since which time he hath done no Act Matter or thing to the best of his knowledge Information or belief whereby he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of January 1786 before Mr: Wright Gs Bradshaw

Edwd Oldis
