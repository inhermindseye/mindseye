Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Corney Aged about forty five years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Samuel Littlefear a Cutler in London Hall Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Undershaft in the City of London upwards of Two years and an half at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twenty Eight years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained any Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 30th. day of Novr. 1775 before R Butler David Walker

Eliz Corney
