Middlesex ss}  John Lloyd maketh oath That divers Years ago this Deponents Son Joseph Lloyd who is at present disordered in his Minds was duly bound apprentice to Joseph Dudd of Coleman Street in the Parish of St Stephen Coleman London  Watchmr with whom he Served about Six Years of Such his Apprenticeship & then was Assigned or Turned over to one Mr Tregigian a Watchmaker of Furments Inn Court in the Liberty of London in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn London with whom he Served all the Remainder of his Sd Apprenticeship & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That to this Deponts Knowlege or belief his Said Son has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain another Settlement.

Sworn this 23d. day of May 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey W Blackmore

John Lloyd
