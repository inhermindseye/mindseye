Middlesex ss  Barbara Sharp now the Wife of James Sharp of Chatham in Kent  Musician & late the widow of Alexander Ring deced & Elizabeth Ring  Singlewoman Dant of the sd Alexander Ring Severally make oath & first the Sd Barbara Sharp Saith That her said late husband Alexr Ring has informed her & which she believes to be true That in his life time he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with James Wooden of the Parish of Rutean in the County of Kent  Farmer at Certain Wages & Continued in Such Service for the space of one year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards to this Deponents knowledge information or belief live as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any act to gain anor. Settlement and the said Elizabeth Ring for herself Saith that she never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself & She can give no Account respecting her sd Fathers Settlement than as afore sd

Sworn this 25 day of Novemr 1777 before Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Barbara Sharp

The Mark of Elizabeth Ring
