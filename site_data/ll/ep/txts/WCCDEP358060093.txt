Middx  Ann Darnbrough upon Oath saith she intermarried with her late husband Charles Darnbrough decd. in the Parish Church of St Lukes Middx abt. 16 Yrs. since and has by him one Child Lucy aged upwards of 11 Yrs. Says her sd. late husband in or about the Year 1739 rented a house of £28 by the Year in Arundel Street in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the sd County of Middx and lived therein about two Years and that neither her sd. late Husband whilst living nor this Examint. since his Decease have (since they left Arundel Strt .) rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in an other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn April 4th. 1751 before}

Ann Darnbrough
