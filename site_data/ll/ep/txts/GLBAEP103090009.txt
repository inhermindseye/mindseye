Middlesex ss  James Osmane maketh oath That about eight Years Ago he lived as a hired servant by the year at the Yearly wages of Ten Pounds with Mrs. Holds worth of Darby Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Brewer and Continued in such Service for the Space of a year and that during such his servitude he this Deponent was a single and unmarried Man And by means thereof as he is advised gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish And this Deponent Saith that he hath not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor rented a house or other Tenement of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor served any Parochial offices nor Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act Whereby to gain a settlement since his said Servitude

Sworn this day of September 1765 before me}
