Middx  Mary Fletcher aged about 35 years upon Oath says that about 17 years since She Intermarried with Robert Ruttlidge in the Parish Church of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. by whom she has two Children namely Robert aged about Eight Years & Eleanor aged about Six Years Says that the said Robert Ruttlidge Rented a House in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes if Thirty two pounds a year & lived therein about nine Months & then dyed about three years since, Says that about Eleven Months after the Death of the said Robert Ruttlidge She intermarried with Richard Fletcher in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes who rented the House occupied by her former Husband in Portugal Street about a year and that he hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes to her knowledge or belief Says that the said Richd. Fletcher left her about half a year since and She being poor is not able to maintain herself & Children

Sworn the 7th. Day of Febry. 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Fletcher
