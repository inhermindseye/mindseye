Middx  Ann Redding Aged abt 68 Years Upon Oath Says She was never Marryed and that she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mr Windom Bughes in Weston Green in the Parish of Thames Dillon in the County of Surry upward of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds and hath quitted her said Service upwards of 22 Years and hath not Since Rented house Paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 10th Day of June 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Ann Redding
