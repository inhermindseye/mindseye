Westmr  Margt Bellew upon Oath Saith she is the Wido. of Mathew Bellew who dyed abt. 3 Yrs. ago and left this Examinant Childless and that she has lived a Yearly Servt. for two Years (or thereabts.) since the Decease of her sd. late husband with Mr Haydon in Vear Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the yearly Wages of £4 and has been Discharged from her sd. Service abt. three Weeks

Sworn June 8th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Margt. Bellew
