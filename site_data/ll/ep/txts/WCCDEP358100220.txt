Middx to wit Margt. Bell aged about 17 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says that about Two Years ago on Calling in Stanhope Street  Clare Markett at her Uncles House to Enquire after this & her Aunts Health was informed by her Uncle that her Aunt was very ill and on Asking her Uncle whether she cod. be of any service during her illness (her Aunt keeping no Servant) her Uncle sayd she Cod. and that if she wod. Assist him during her illness that he wod. pay her what she deserved but made no other bargain; accordingly she sayd with her Uncle for the space of 15 Months or thereabouts but never was hired or made any other Agreement than as aforesd. Says her Uncle twice gave her 4s or 5s and now and then a Shilling and bought her Cloaths at Two different times besides Diet & Lodging but this Examinant thos. her self at Liberty to part from her Uncle without any Warning or to go when ever she thos. proper and believes her sd. Uncle took her more out of Humanity than otwise (she being out of place) That on leaving her Uncle she hired herself as a Servant to one Mr. Wakelin in St. John Street  Clerkenwell and lived with him for the Space of Four Months or thereabouts, that on Calling a 2 time on her Uncle he proposed to hire her as a Servant at the Yearly Wages of £4..10s..0d to which this Examinant Ageed to and she continued to live with him for the space of Eight Months or thereabouts and on quitting her said Service she never gained any other Settlemt. than as aforesaid

Sworn this 11th. day of June 1784 before R Butler

her Margt. Bell Mark

At the Lyon & Lamb  Charles Street  Horsely Down
