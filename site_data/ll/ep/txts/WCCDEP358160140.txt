Middx  The Examination of Ann Clements the Wife of Paul Clements Lodging in St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 5th. Day of June 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that in the Month of  Janry. 1739 She Intermarryed with the above Named Paul Clements & yt. he the sd. Paul Clements Served three Months the latter Part of his Apprentishipp with one Wm. Watts  Esqr . in Pater Noster Row in the Parish of St. Faith under St. Paul  London And this Examinant further upon her Oath saith That her sd. Husband hath not since he left his said Master's Service Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife That her sd. Husband is now on Board his Majesty's Ship the Grafton & she being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Samuel Clarke

Ann Clemments
