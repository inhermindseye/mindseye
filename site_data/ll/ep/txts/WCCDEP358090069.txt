Middx to wit Mary Harris Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lives as a hired Yearly servant with Mr. Sherwin an Ingraver  near Temple Bar in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and as so done upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds Ann further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be come Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born and that one Stephen Newnham an apprentice to her said Master and who now lodges at No 8 Mitre Court Fleet Street did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her in her said Masters House some time in the Month of January last but the particular day she does not now remember and at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she in now Pregnant and that the said Stephen Newnham and no other person did beget the same and that he in the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 11th. day of Septr. 1779 before} 

Mary Harris
