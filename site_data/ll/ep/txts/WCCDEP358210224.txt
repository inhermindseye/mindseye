Westmr.  Thomas Lane Aged about Forty seven Years, Upon Oath says he intermarried to Ann his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster about Eighteen Years ago by whom he hath Six Children now living, (to wit) Thomas Aged about Fifteen Years, Ann aged about Thirteen Years, William aged about Eleven Years, Elizabeth aged about Nine Years, George aged about Six Years, and John aged about Four Years, says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. Parry a Carpenter near Drum Alley in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster aforesaid and never rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 2d. day of January 1768 . before} Fran Cary

Thos Lane
