Middx  Susannah Barry on Behalfe of Honor Abraham an Infant aged about 12 Years upon her Oath saith that the Father & Mother of the sd. Infant are both dead Says that Thomas Abraham the Father of the sd. Honor Abraham lived in & rented a house the Corner of Cross Street near Saffron Hill  for in the Liberty of Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn above Barrs in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £12 for the Space of one Year or thereabouts that after the sd. Thomas Abraham left the sd. house he never Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or other- -wise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife Says the sd. Honor Abraham never was an Apprentice or lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any person for one Year.

Sworne 2d. Novr 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Sussanna Barry
