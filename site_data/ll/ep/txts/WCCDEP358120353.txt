Middx  Jane Grant upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of John Grant to whom she was married at the Fleet London upwds. of 40 Yrs. ago & that her sd. husbd. rented a House in Kings head Court in the parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr . in the County of Middx of the yearly Rent of £14 lived therein upwds. of 10 Yrs. & quitted the Same upwds. of 20 Yrs. ago & that her sd. husband never afterwards nor has this Examinant Since his Decease rented ten pounds by the year pd. parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn June 5th 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jane Grant
