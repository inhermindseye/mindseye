Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Mary Allford of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the said County of middlesex  Singlewoman taken on oath before us Rice Davies and Robert Smith  Esquires two of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this thirty first day of March one thousand Seven hundred and eighty eight - who Saith that on the twenty ninth day of January last past at Peters Court in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate She the said Mary Allford was delivered of a Male Bastard Child And that he said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that James Cooper of Lambs Conduit Passage adjoining to Red Lyon Square in the said County Chairman did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the day and year above written before us  R Davies  Robt Smith

Mary Allford
