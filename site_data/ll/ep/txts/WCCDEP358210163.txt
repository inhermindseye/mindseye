Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Hughes aged about 27 Years upon Oath says she never was married nor ever rented Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one year; says her Father Nicholas Hughes now Rents a house & farm in the Parish of Lancarvan in the County of Glamorgan in South Wales of the Yearly Rent of One hundred Pounds or thereabouts, and hath so done for a great many Years And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex where she now lodges; says that Jonathan Davis a Journeyman Taylor had carnal knowledge of her body about the latter End of the Month of January last and at several other times since, at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Jonathan Davis and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn 29th, May 1767 before} Paul Vaillant

Ann Hughs .
