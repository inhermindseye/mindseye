Westmr.  Wm: Hopkins aged about 67 Years Upon Oath says he Rented a House in Whitehorse Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of fourteen Pounds lived therein about three Years and quitted same about Twenty Years ago, and hath not since Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 6th. Novr. 1766 , before} Paul Vaillant

Wm. Hopkins
