Middx  Catherine Frisell upon Oath says She is the Widow of Charles Frisell who hath been dead upwards of Seven Years Says her said late Husband rented a House in Goswell Street in the Parish of St. Luke in the County of Middlesex of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and lived in the same about fourteen Years and paid all Parochiall Taxes Says She hath not since the Death, of her said late Husband Charles Frizell rented Ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or been a yearly Servant

Sworn the 30th. Day of Augst. 1755 before} Theodr Sydenham  Barth: Hammond

Kathe Frisell
