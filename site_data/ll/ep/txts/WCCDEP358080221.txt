Middlesex to wit Elizth- Osborne aged about forty three Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Osborne who is gone from her with whom she Intermarrd- at the parish Church of St- Andrew Holborn about twenty Years ago by whom she has two Children now provided for Says her said husband Rented and lived in a houses in the Strand in the parish of St- Clement Danes for sevl- Years at the Yearly Rent of £130 and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the same about twelve Years ago since which time this Examinant hath gained no other subseqt- Settlemt- either by Renting house paying Taxes orwise howsoever

Sworn this 25th- Day of Febry 1778 before R Butler

her Elizth- Osborne Mark
