Bastardy before Birth

St Clement Danes 23d February 1793

Middx and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnancy  Reputed father }(to wit) Sarah Woodhill  Singlewoman aged tweny one years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Jackson Bookbinder in Herberts passage Fountain Court Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the  County of Middlesex for about a year and upwards at the Annual Wages of £6 or thereabouts-that she quitted that Service about four years ago-and hath never since lived as an hired yearly Servant for a twelve month together kept any House or Apartment of £10 a year paid parish Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby she might obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief And this Examinant Upon her Voluntary Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes when born and saith that William Wood house a private Soldier in his Majesty's Second Regiment of Foot Guards in the First Battalion and Duke Company  did beget the said Child or Children on the Body of her this Deponents he having first had carnal knowledge of this Examinant's Body sometime in or about the

Month of January 1792 and at several times since particularly in or about the Month of October last and that he and no other person is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn the 23d. Day of February 1793 before me} Wm. Kitchiner

Sarah Woodhill

Man taken & escaped
