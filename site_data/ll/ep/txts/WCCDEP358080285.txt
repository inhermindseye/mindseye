Middx to wit Alice Benbow Aged about 20 years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Elliott in Butcher Row Temple Bar upwards of one year at the yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted the said Service upwards of one year ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one Doctor  George Bell who Lodges at Mr. Fossetts her said Masters in Thannett Place is the Father thereof he having had Carnall Knowledge of her Body at her said Masters house the latter end of Decr. last but the Particular Day she Does not now remember and at Several different times afterwards at one of which times he got with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant & that the said Dr. Bell and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the Father thereof

Sworn this 15 day of Septr. 1778 before  Wm Kitchiner

Alice Bamber her Mark
