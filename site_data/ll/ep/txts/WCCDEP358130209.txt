Middx.  Elizth. Perry aged abt. 44 Years upon Oath Says She never was Married & that She lived a hired Yearly Servant upwds. of a Year with Mrs. Rutland in the Strand near Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abt. Seven Years, since which Time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Sepr. 14th. 1756 before}

Eliz. Perry
