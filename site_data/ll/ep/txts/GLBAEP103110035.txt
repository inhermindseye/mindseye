Middlesex ss}  Ann Blake  Singlewoman Maketh oath That about twelve Years ago a Marriage or Prentended Marriage was had between her & one Benjamin Terroll a Montross in his Majesties Service at Woolwich in Kent where She this Depont then lived as a Servant That She was afterwards removed by a Pass order from Woolwich to Church Higborne in Berkshire as being the Place of the said Benjn. Terrolls last legal Settlement That it afterwards appearing that her Marriage with the Said Benjn Terroll was void by reason he had a lawful Wife living at the true of Such this Deponents Marriage She was there upon removed from Such place of Settlement of the Sd Benjn. Terroll to her last legal Settlement being at Stratford Bow in the County of Middlesex which Settlement was lawfully Acquired by her being hired & living as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain yearly Wages with one Mrs Lofty there and her Continuing in Such Service for the Space of a year and upwards That She this Depont. has not Since lived as a hire Servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a Child Named Susanna & aged Eleven Years by the Said Benjn Terroll who by reason of this Depont. Marriage being illegal & Void is illegitimate & a Bastard & was born in the Said Parish of Stratford Bow & has done no Act to gain a Settlement in her Own right

Sworn this 10th day of January 1772 before  Cha: Digby  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Blake
