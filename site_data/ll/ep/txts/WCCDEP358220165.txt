Westminster to wit. Mary Fether the Wife of Henry Fether Maketh Oath That she has a Child named Elizabeth Bennett aged about Eight Years by her Former Husband William Bennett that she was Married to her said late Husband at Saint Margaret's Church Westminster that he was a Scotchman Born at Edinburgh never was an Apprentice Servant or Householder that this Examinant before Marriage to her said late Husband lived as an Hired Yearly Servant to Mr: Layland in Maidenhead Court in the Parish of Saint Thomas the Apostle London for thirteen Months at £5: a Year Wages and Intermarried with her said late Husband while in that Service

Sworn the 12th: day of October 1768 before } John Phillips  Wm: Timbrell

The Mark of Mary Fether

Westmr: to wit. Henry Fether Second Husband of the above named Mary Fether deceased Maketh Oath that the above mentioned Examination is a true Copy of what his said late Wife Swore to before Mr. Justice Phillips and Mr: Justice Timbrell at the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square the 12th: day of October 1768 And which said Examination this Examinant verily believes to be true

Sworn this 18th: day of July 1770 before us }  W. Kelyngs

Henry Fether
