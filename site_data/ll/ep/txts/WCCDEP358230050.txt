Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Martha Smith aged about 55 years upon her Oath saith that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Matthew Smith in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Fifteen years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of March 1799 before me Menter

her Martha Smith Mark
