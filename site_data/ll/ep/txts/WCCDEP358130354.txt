Middx.  Eliz: Gregory aged abt. 50 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of Wm. Gregory says her said husbd. when he was a Single man lived one Yr. & upwds. a hired Yearly Servt. with one Mr. Cook at Bromton in the Parish of Kensington in the County of Middx as she has been informed & verily believes

Sworn the 6th of June 1757 before} Theodr. Sydenham

The Mark of Eliz: Gregory
