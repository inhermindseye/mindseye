Westmr. to wit John Spriggs Aged about Sixty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Pauls Covent Garden about Six Years Ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says he lived in a House of his own in Adam & Eve Court in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex upwards of Twelve Years and was Rated at Fourteen Pounds a Year and paid at Parish Taxes due for the same and quitted the said House in the Year 1768 and after quitting the same he never Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of May 1773 before Us} John Goodchild  New Overseers

John Sprgs
