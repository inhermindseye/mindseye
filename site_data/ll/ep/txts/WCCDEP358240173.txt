Saint Clement Danes Oct. 24th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Bride London  to wit Mary Evans aged 40 years or thereabouts Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Lucas a Fanmaker at No. 44 or 45 Ludgate Hill in the Parish of Saint Bridget otherwise Saint Bride in the City of London for the spare of one year at the annual Wages of Eight Pounds that she quitted the said Service about nine years ago and hath not since lived as an hired yearly Servant Rented any house or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year paid parish Taxes or otherwise obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge information or belief and that she is now very sick poor and distressed and unable to maintain herself.

Sworn before me this 24th. day of Oct. 1792 M Addington Gd: Mitchell

her Mary Evans Mark
