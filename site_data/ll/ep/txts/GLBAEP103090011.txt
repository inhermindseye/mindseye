Middlesex ss  Sarah Cannon the widow of William Cannon deced Maketh oath That She well knows her Niece Mary Tapping  single woman who for sometime Past hath been and Still is disordered in her Mind and Senses and is now likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex And this Depon Saith that the Said Mary Tapping is the Daughter of Amos Tapping by Mary his wife this Deponents late Sister both of whom are dead And their Place or Places of Settlement And also of their respective Parents wholy unknown to this Deponent And this Deponent farther Saith that her said Niece Mary Tapping was born in Shad Thames formerly in the Parish of Saint Olave but now in the Parish of Saint John Southwarke in the County of Surry and is of the Age of Thirty five Years or thereabouts And that to her knowledge information her said Neice was never an apprentice Nor never lived as a hired servant for a year Nor was never Married Nor never did any Act to gain a settlement in her own Right.

Sworn this 16th. day of October 1765 before us  Chris: Scott R Pell

Sarah Cannon
