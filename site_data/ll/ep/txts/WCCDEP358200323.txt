Westmr.  Mary Davis aged about 22 Years upon Oath says she was born in the Parish of St. James in the City of Bristol in the County of Somerset and never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says she can give no Account of the Settlement of her Father other than he was born somewhere in Wales but in what Parish of place this Examinant cannot tell, says she has two Children, John aged upwds. of two Years and James aged upwards of one Year

Sworn the 20th. day of November 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

The Mark of Mary Davis
