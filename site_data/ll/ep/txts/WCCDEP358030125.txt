Middx  Joseph Brama upon his Oath saith he lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Chas. Scriver in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for the Space of Five Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds for the first Year Seven pounds for the sd. Eight pounds for the third nine pounds for the fourth & Ten pounds for the Fifth  Year & hath left his sd. Service abt. Ten Years Says he hath not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 2d. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Joseph Brama
