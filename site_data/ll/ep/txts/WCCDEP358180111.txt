Middx  Elizabeth Slack  Wido . of Edmond Slack upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her sd. late Husband  Edmd Slack at the Parish Church of St. Anns Westminster about 20 Years since That he hath been Dead near 9 Years that her said late Husband was by Indenture Dated in 1711 bound an Apprentice to & served 7 Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Leggit Shoemaker in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that since his sd. Apprentiship he in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 5th. Septr. 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Eliza. Slack
