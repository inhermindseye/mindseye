Middlesex ss}  James Plimmer maketh oath that Several Years ago he was hired by & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with one Mr Freeman in Upper East Smithfield in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Victualler & Distiller & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Three years at the yearly Wages of Four pounds & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since Such Servitude lived as a hired Servant  for a year nor rented a house of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that he hath a Wife named Sarah to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 23d day of March 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of James Plimmer
