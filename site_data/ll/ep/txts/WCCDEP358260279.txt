Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Allen aged about 36 years upon her Oath saith that about fifteen years ago She Intermarried with Thomas Allen who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square by whom She hath One Child living Vizt. Henry aged about Seven Months That her said Husband Rented a first floor unfurnished in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three Months at the yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas That he quitted said Apartments about Eleven years since which time he hath not done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of May 1798 before me }

her Mary Allen mark
