Middx  Ann Jones aged 36 years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 12 years ago she lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Roebuck in Craven Buildings in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes for two years at the yearly Wages of £4 and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Febry. 7th 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Jones
