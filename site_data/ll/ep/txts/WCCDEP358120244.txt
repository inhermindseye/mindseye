Middx  Alice Jenks Aged abt. Twenty five Years Upon Oath says she was never Married and that she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Gibbison, in Hemlock Court in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes for one year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five pounds & hath quitted her sd. Service abt. Seven Months and further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that she is pregnant with Child or or Children wch is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards. and to become chargeable to the sd. parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to maintaine the same when Borne Says Wm. Bending a peruke maker did begett the sd. Child or, Children on her Body haveing had Carnall knowledge of her in the month of June last at the house of Mr. Saml. Smith in the Butcher Row in the sd. parish of St. Clemt. Danes where this Examinant then Lodged & at Sevll. other times since at one of wch times he got her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with wch she is now pregnant & that he the sd. Wm. Bending & no other person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworne the 11th. Day of Decr. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Alice Jenks
