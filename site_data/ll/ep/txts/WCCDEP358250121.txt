Saint Clement Danes 19th March 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary le Bone  Middx (to wit) John Seccull aged about Sixty Six Years Upon Oath saith that he kept rented and resided in a House in Princes Street  Cavendish Square in the Parish of Saint Mary le Bone in the County of Middlesex upwards of Four Years at the Annu rent ed of Twenty Pounds; about Twelve Years ago, that he this Deponent further saith, that he has not rent or resided in any House or Apartment or lived as an Hired Yearly Servant whereby to the best of his knowledge information or belief to obtain him any subsequent legal settlement since he resided in the House in Princes Street aforesaid

Sworn this 19th of March B: Kennett R. Ford

John Seccull
