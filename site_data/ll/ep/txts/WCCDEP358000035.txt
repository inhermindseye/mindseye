Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Athe & Ricd passd-to St. Mary le Bone to wit Mary Vincent  Wife of John Vincent of New Tom Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  Taylor on behalf of Hugh Ward a Child aged Eighteen Months or thereabout on her Oath saith that Sarah Watts this Examinants Sister who is the Mother of the said Hugh Ward informed this Examinant that the said Hugh Ward was born in Oxford Road in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex

 Sworn this 27th day of April 1785  Jno Machin Mr: Wright

her Mary Vincent Mark
