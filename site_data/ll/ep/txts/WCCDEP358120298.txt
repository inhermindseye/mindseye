Middx  Mary Woolhead aged abt. 22 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she never was married and that on the 19th. Day of Novr. last she was delivered of a female bastard Child in the house of Mrs. Polhill in Strand Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex which was baptized Juliet Frances Parker and is still living

Sworn March 7th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Woolhead
