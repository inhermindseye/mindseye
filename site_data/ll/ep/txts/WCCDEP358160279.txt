Middx  The Examination of Chas. Long aged abt. eighteen Years who was found wandering in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken ye. 14th- Day of March 1740 Who upon his Oath saith that he was born in the Parish of Dunster in the County of Somerset And cannot give any Account of the Settlement of either his Father or Mother other than that his sd. Father rented & lived in a House of abt. Six or Eight Pounds a Year in the sd. Parish of Dunster but whether he paid any Parochiall Taxes this Examinant knows not And further on his Oath saith that he never was an Apprentice lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife that his Father & Mother are both Dead & he being Poor & Sick is not able to maintaine himselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before

The mark of Chas. Long
