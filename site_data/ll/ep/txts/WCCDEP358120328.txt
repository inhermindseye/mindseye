Middx  James Townsend aged near 19 years upon Oath saith he was bound apprentice by the Churchwds. Oversrs. of the poor of the Parish of St. Andrew in Holbourn in the County of Middx to Mr Brooks & Pencil & Brush maker and lived With him as his Apprentice about Six Months in Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr . after which his master & mistress dyed and this Examinant was assigned over and never rented ten pounds a year paid Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn April 19th 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Jas Townsend
