Middlesex and Westminster}  Warrt. made out  the Husband for Desertion but never taken to wit Elizabeth Malone aged about 32 years on her Oath says she was married to Lawrence Malone her now Husband in the year 1775 at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London that she has no Children now living by her said Husband that since their Intermarriage her said Husband hath Rented and lived in a House in Bennetts Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clemt Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for upwards of two years that this Exmts. said Husband quitted the said House about four years ago since which time he hath not Rented any other House Lodging or Tenement of the yearly Value of Ten pounds paid Taxes or done any other Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained any other Settlement 

than in the parish of Saint Clement Danes as aforesaid And further this Examinant saith that her said Husband has gone away from and deserted her and now Cohabits with another Woman who as this Examinant is informed and believes keep the Public House Called the Black Boy and still the Corner of Armitage Street Wapping and wholly refuses to support or maintain this Examinant and that she this Examinant is poor and out of Work and unable to maintain herself.

Sworn this 4th. day of Janry. 1785 R Butler

Eth. Malone
