Middlesex & Westminster to wit Mary Ann Heath Aged about Eighteen Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Lady Keith in German Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster Upwards of Twelve Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds six Shillings hath Quitted her said Service about Eight Months ago since Which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. day of Feby. 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Mary Ann Heath
