Middlesex ss. The examination of Ruth Clark late of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Singlewoman taken on Oath before Rice Davies  Esqr . one of His Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the said County of Middlesex this Twelfth Day of February in the year of our Lord One Thousand Seven Hundred & Ninety Eight

Who Saith that on the Tenth Day of October One Thousand Seven Hundred and Ninety Five in the Workhouse of the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate she the said Ruth Clark was delivered of a Female Bastard Child since Baptized at Aldgate Church by the Name of Lucy that the said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate And that Joseph Bray of Upper Eastsmithfield in the said Parish Gun Lock Forger is the Father of the said Bastard Child

Taken & Signed the Day and year first above written before R Davies

her Ruth Clark Mark
