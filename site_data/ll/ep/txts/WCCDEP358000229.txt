Saint Clement Danes 21st Febry 1786

Middlesex & Westmr to wit}  Catharine Peet aged Eighteen Years or thereabouts singlewoman on behalf of Jane Peet her Sister aged 14 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that Their father and Mother are both Dead that she has been informed and believes that her fathers Settlement at the time of his Death was in the Parish Saint Paul Deptford in the County of Kent, that the said Jane Peet was bound Apprentice from the Parish of Saint Paul Deptford afd to one  Catharine Peet this Examinant Aunt who then lived in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell by Indres duly executed that the served with her there for about two Years, that then her Aunt placed her with a Mrs- Selby and paid her for her Board - that Mrs. Selby aged with her Aunt to get her into place that by Mrs- Sebbys Prouration she was to live with Mr- Pain a Watch Care maker in Islington Row facing Salers Wells in the Pas: of Saint Mary Islington at st. which Mrs- Selby was to receive lay out for her lived thereabts. 18 Weeks quitted abt. a fortnight since
