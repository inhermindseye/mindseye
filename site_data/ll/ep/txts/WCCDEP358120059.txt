Middx  Mary Lewis aged abt. 26 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servt. with Mr. Phillips in the parish of Yarpoole in the County of Hereford upwards of one Year and has not Since rented ten pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn Feby. 23d. 1754 before Tho Lane  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Lewis
