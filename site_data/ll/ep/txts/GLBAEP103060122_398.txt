Middx ss  Lydia Clay the Widow of Jeremiah Clay Deced maketh oath that she has been often informed by her Sd. late husband Jeremiah Clay that her Sd. late husband duly Served his apprenticeship to a Barber and peruke maker in Devereaux Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the City & liberty of Westmr. in the Sd. County of Middx And that during Such his apprenticeship he gained a legal settlemt. in the Sd. parish of St. Clement Danes which informacon this Depont verily believes to be true And this Depont. farther Saith that her sd. late husband to the knowledge

or belief of her this Depont. was never a hired Servant by the year nor never rented a house of Ten pounds by the year or Served any parish office or ever did any other Act whereby he gained any Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn the 3d Day of January 1749 before me  Richd. Ricards  Saml Gower

Lydia Clay
