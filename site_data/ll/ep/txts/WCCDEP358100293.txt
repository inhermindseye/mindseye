Middlesex and Westminster}  Swear to St Clemt . J. to wit James Wilson aged about 36 years on his Oath says he never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says he lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Duel an Attorney at Law in New Inn in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for Fourteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Ten Pounds hath quitted has said Service upwards of Twelve years ago since which time he never lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 25th. day of Novr. 1784 before one  Wm. Kitchiner

his James Wilson mark
