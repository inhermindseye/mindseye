Westmr ss  Moses Wagstaffe Aged about fifty three Years on his Oath says he intermarried to Elizabeth his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Sepulchre London about Twenty One Years Ago and by whom he hath One Child now living Namely Thomas Aged nine Years and he further saith that he served seven Years Apprenticeship to one Mr Alexander Pay an Glover in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the City and Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he has never rented House paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th- day of Novr 1774 before  John Goodchild

Moses Wagstaff
