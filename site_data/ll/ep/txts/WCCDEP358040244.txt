Westmr. 

Eleanr. Hull aged upwds. of 23 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she never was married and that between Six and Seven Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Taylor a Cle archer in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Sd. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr at the Yearly Wages of 30 and 40 Shillings for two Years and upwds. and has not since she last the sd. Mr. Taylor rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Augt. 29th. 1748 before} Wm. Jones

Ellener Hull
