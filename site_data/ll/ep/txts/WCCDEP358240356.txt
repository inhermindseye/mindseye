St Clement Danes  Novr 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St John the Baptist New Windsor  (to wit) Elizabeth Herbert  singlewoman aged twenty five years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly servant with Mr Rimbery Kimberley at the Three Tuns public House in the Town parish of St John the Baptist in the Town of New Windsor in the County of Berks that she quitted that so for three years or upwards at the Annual Wages of that she quitted that Service about about two Yeares also ago And hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her this Deponent's knowledge information or belief & that she is now pregnant with Child & unable to support the same when Born

Sworn the 21st day of November 1793 before Us}

Elizth Herbert 's Mark

Witness Geo. Robertson
