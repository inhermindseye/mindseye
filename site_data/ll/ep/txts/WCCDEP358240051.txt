Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  to wit James Searle Aged about 33 Years Upon his Oath saith that in the Year 1788 or thereabouts he took and Rented a Publick House the Corner of Holles Street Clare Market in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex then called or known by the Sign of the Horse Shoe and Magpye but now of the Fishmongers Anns And kept the saince about 16 Months at the Yearly Rent of £45 that since he left the said Publick House he has never Rented any Tenement of Lodging of Ten pounds a Year lived as a hired yearly Servant paid Taxes or done any Act Matters or thing whereby he might have gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of his Knowledge information or belief And further Saith that he has a Wife nor living and 4 Children and is Poor and unable to maintain them therefore humbly Seeks relief of the said Parish

Sworn this 8th. Octr. 91 before R Butler

James Searle
