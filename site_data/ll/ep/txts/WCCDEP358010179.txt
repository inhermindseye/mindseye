Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex  to wit Mary Stone aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with William Stone at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch on the 19th. day of Decr. 1781 that her said Husband informed this Examinant that he was born near Grays Inn Lane in Holborn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex that he never Rented any Houre Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Janry. 1788 }

her Mary Stone Mark
