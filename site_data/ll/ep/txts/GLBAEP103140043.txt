Middlesex ss}  Thomasin Ward the Widow of John Ward deced maketh oath that her said late husband has informed her & which Information She believes to be true That he duly Served his Apprenticeship with one Joseph Arthur in Long Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry  Gardiner That this Deponents said husband also rented a house in the same Parish & was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the same & by the means aforesaid gained a legal Settlement there as She is Advised & That her said husband did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25 day of June 1774 before Wilmot B Camper

The Mark of Thomasin Ward
