Middx.  Mary Bovenfton on the Behalfe of Sarah Browne an Infant aged about Twelve Years Daughter of John Browne Deceased by his Wife also Deceased upon her Oath saith that the said John Browne the Father of the said Infant lived in a House of his own the Corner of Browns Court in Green Street in the Parish of St. George in Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Value of Thirty Pounds for the Space of Six Years & hath left the same about Four Years that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 23d. Septr. 1742 before} Wm. Hayton  Fran Hole

Mary Bevenfton
