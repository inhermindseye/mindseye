Middlesex ss}  Ann Colledge the wife of Robert Colledge who is now absent from her maketh oath That She has been informed by her Said husband & which She believes to be true that divers Years ago he was duly bound Apprentice by Indre to James Bollard of the Parish of St Botolph with Aldgate in the Sd County Waterman for the Term of Seven years That this Deponts Sd husband Served his Sd Master there for about Six years & then his Said Master & himself removed to a publick house known by the Sign of the old Rose & Crown in the Parish of St John Southwarke in the County of Surry Where this Deponts Sd husband Served the Remainder of his Said Apprenticeship & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there to Wit the Sd last mentioned Parish That this Deponents Said husband has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year or rented a house or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten pounds or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors. Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent was lawfully married to her Sd husband

Sworn this. 26. day of June 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Ann Colledge
