Middlesex ss}  Ann Bullimore the widow of Thomas Bullimore deced maketh oath that her Since her Said late husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr John Branham at the Permit office in the upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County and Continued there under such hiring for the Space of Seven Years That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor been Since married nor hath She done any Act to gain a a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 14. day of Decemr 1786 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Bullimore
