Middx to wit Lydia Glover Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says She  was bound an Apprentice by the Churchwarden and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of Saint Mary at H Thames Street London to one Don Bartholomew Shaw an Orange Merchant at No. 3 Saint Dunstans Alley in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the East London and servt. her said Master under such Indenture for the space of five Years when her said Master gave up her Indenture And after quitting her said Masters Service She never gained any other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 24th. day of July 1780 before  Wm Kitchiner  Sampn Wright

Lydia Glover her Mark
