Middlesex ss  Sarah Cross the widow of William Cross deced maketh Oath That her Said late husband has informed her (and which Information She be lives to be true) That he was born in Pescott Street in the Parish of Windsor in the County of Berks and that he also duly served his apprenticeship there with his late Father John Cross also deced who was by Trade a Cordwainer And by means thereof as this Depont. is advised her Said late husband gained a legal Settlement there And this Depont Saith that to her knowledge information or belief her Said husband never rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor served any Parish Offices nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement subsequent to his apprenticeship Nor hath she this Depont. done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself since her said husbands Death.

Sworn this 15th day of January 1770 before  Chris Scott  Cha: Digby

The Mark of Sarah Cross .
