Westmr.  Ann Poole wife of Wm. Poole upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Wm. Poole at the fleet London about 15 Years ago by whom she hath one Child viz; John aged upwds. of three Years and further that her sd. husband hath informed her he lived a Yearly Servant after the Decease of his first wife (being then a Widower without Child or Children with the Rt Honoble the Earl of Darnley for one Years and upwds. at the Yearly Wages of five pounds and lay during the last two months of his said Service at the Horse and Groom in Strutton Street in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . and the her sd. husband hath not since he quitted the Earl of Darnley Service rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for

one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place as her sd. husband hath Informed her and which she verily beleives.

Sworn Octr. 27th. 1749 before}
