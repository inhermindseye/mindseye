Middx  Samuel Birch aged about 72 Years Lodging at the Spoted Dog in Blackmoore Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith That he lived in and rented an House the Corner of Hog Lane in Oxford Road in the Parish of St. Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for the Space of Two Years & upwards at the Yearly Rents of Seventeen pounds Quitted the Same about Twelve Years ago and hath not since rented Ten Pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes That he was Married to his present Wife  Mary at Prestbury Church in Cheshire in the Year 1715

Sworn the 26th. day of June 1761 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Saml. Birch

Upon Mr. Figg's Complt.
