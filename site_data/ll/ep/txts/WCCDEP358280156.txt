Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Catherine Jennings aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year or ever bound an Apprentice . That she was born at Plimptree in Devonshire That she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith That she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes And further Says that one Mr. Hughes Clerk to Mr. Gosling the Bankers in Fleet Street is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of September last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Childn. of which she is so pregnant and that the said Mr. Hughes is the only real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 8th. day of April 1788 } R Butler

Catherine Jinnings

Catherine Jennings lived at Mrs. Nobes's Sheffield Street

Mr. Hughes is about 22 years old short Thin Man Pale Complexion has a Cast with his Eyes

£20 paid Rcl. Exted & Rev given 2 Child died
