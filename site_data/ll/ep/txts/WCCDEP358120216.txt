Middx  Mary Driver aged abt. 45 years upon her Oath saith she is the Wido. of Fras. Driver who dyed upwds. of six Years ago and that about 3 years ago being then Childless she was hired to live a yearly Servt. with Mr Farrer a Fishmonger at the yearly wages of £5 and lived with him under this hiring and Agreement at his house at Temple bar in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one Year and upwards and has not since she quitted her sd Service rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Octr 19th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Driver
