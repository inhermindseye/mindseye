Middlesex ss}  Mary Nettle  Singlewoman maketh oath That divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr James Hixon a Coalmerchant in Burr Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued there in Such Service under the Said hiring for the Space of Fifteen months & by means thereof gained a legal Settlemt there That She has not Since lived as a hired servant for a year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this - day of September 1778 before}

The Mark of Mary Nettle
