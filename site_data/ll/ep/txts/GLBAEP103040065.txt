Middlesex, to wit, Catherine Traxlir , Singlewoman , maketh Oath and Saith, That in or about the Month of May 1796 she was hired by and went to live as a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Kannen of Wellclose Square in the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middlesex , Surgeon, at certain Yearly Wages, and continued in such Service under such hiring for the space of Fifteen Months or thereabouts, and hath not done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 29th Day of 1797 . before us H: Reynell R Davies

her Catherine Traxlir Mark
