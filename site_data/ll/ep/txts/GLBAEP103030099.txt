Middlesex ss.  Eleanor Hughes the Widow of  Richard Hughes deceased maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Mary Rotherhithe in the County of Surry about Sixteen Years ago that her said late Husband then Rented a Houses of Twenty five Pounds a Year in Church Row Houndsditch in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate in the City of London known or described by No 4. & lived in the same about Thirteen Years and was charged to and paid the Poors Rate for the same that he died in the said House & that she continued to live therein about Six Months after his Death, that she has not since she quitted the said House rented any other House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to her Knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement, That she has a Child named William aged about Seven Years the lawful issue by her said late Husband

Sworn this Day of June 1796 before}
