Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Hannah Martin aged 21 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she Intermarried with John Martin her Prest. Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn about 4 Months ago-That he lived in Lyons Inn at the Time she Married him & has Continued there since and Paid the Poors Rate for his Chambers That he had 2 Children when she married him (Vizt.) James aged 11  years or thereabouts bo in Scotland & John aged 4 years or thereabouts born at Mc. Roxburgh in Clare Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 22d. day of July 1786 before  Wm. Kitchiner

Hannah Martin
