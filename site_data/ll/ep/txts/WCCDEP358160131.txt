Middx The Examination of Ann Maria Perrin the Wife of Jas. Perrin Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 3d. Day of May 1740 Who upon her Oath saith yt. her Husband Jas. Perrin Served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Balbert in New Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr.  Silversmith And further upon her Oath saith that he never Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife, That her sd. Husband will not do any thing for her and that she hath one Child (to wit) Hanh. Maria aged Six Months & upwds. & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe and Child

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Samuel Clarke

Ann maria perrin
