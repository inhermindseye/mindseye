Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Margaret Wright of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Single Woman taken on oath before me Robert Smith  Esquire  one of his majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Seventeenth day of January One thousand Seven hundred and eighty five who Saith that on the twenty Sixth day of December one thousand Seven hundred and eighty three in the Said parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate She the Sd Margaret Wright was delivered of a Male Bastard Child Since baptized & registred by the name of John James Wright and that the Said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that John Beriman of the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the Sd County Cordwainer did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Taken and Signed the day & year above written before me  Robt: Smith

The Mark of Margaret Wright
