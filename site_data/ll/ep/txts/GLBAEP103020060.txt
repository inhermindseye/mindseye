Middlesex ss.  Catherine Morrison the Wife of Alexander Morrison a Soldier in the Duke of York's Regiment maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said Husband at Stone Hive  in the Shire of aberdeen about Seven Years ago that her said Husband has informed her that he was Born at Gibraltar & never did any Act to her knowledge or Relief to gain a Settlement in England, that she this Deponent was born at Inverness is Scotland and about Four Years ago she & her said Husband with their Infant Child named Alexander then aged about Seven Months came from Aberdeen in the Ship called the Duke of Buccleugh & were landed at Hawley's Wharf in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and a few Days after her said Husband took a Room in the Dwelling House of Mr. Bowman in Fullers Court in the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate where he lived until the Regiment he belongs to was Ordered to embark for Foreign Service which is about Nine Months ago, that she this Deponent now lodged in the Dwelling House of Mr. Morrison Violin Maker Mr. Eastsmd. and that she has Two Children the lawful issue by her Sd. Husband Namely Alexander who was born in Edinburgh & is now about Four Years old & Mary aged about Eighteen Months born in Fullers Court aforesaid & Baptized at Aldgate Church

Sworn this Day of Novr. 1793 before}
