Westmr.  Ann Axtell  wife of Samuel Axtell Upon Oath saith before she intermarried wth. the sd. Samuel Axtell she intermarried with one Richd. Theene since deced Says the said Richd. Theene Served his Apprentiship with Mr. Thos. Houship a Shoemaker in the parish of Clapham in the County of Surry and never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place Says she hath one Child by the said Richd. Theene to wit Elizabeth aged abt upwds. of 4 Yrs. & being Poor is not able to maintain the same

Sworn Decr. 15th 1746 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Ann Axtell
