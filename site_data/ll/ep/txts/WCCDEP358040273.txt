Westmr. 

Jereh. Bigg upon his Oath saith he Intermarried with his present Wife Ann at the Parish Church of Wandsworth in Surry upwds. of 20 Years ago Says her Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Jereh. Grigg a Carpenter in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn the Day of Novr. 1748 before}
