Middlesex ss}  Ann Millard the widow of Jane Millard deced maketh oath that She cannot give any Account respecting her sd late husbands Settlement That previous to her marriage with him She was hired and lived as a yearly hired Servant at Certain wages by & with one Mr Hilton an attorney at Law of the Parish of st George the Martyr in the said County and Continued there in such Service under such hiring for about the Space of five years from whence She was soon after married to her said late husband by whom She had children That after her said husband death & whilot a widow she was again hired by the sd Mr Hilton as an early hire servt at Certain Wages who then lived in Princes Street in the Liberty above the Bars in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn in the said County where she Continued for about the Space of two years and upwards but having two children by her said late husband then living during such her last mentioned Servitude She Could not as she is advised & believes gain any Settlement for herself in such lastmentd Place and she hath done no other act since her said husbands death to gain a Settlement elsewhere and that she was lawfully married to her sd husband

Sworn this. 5. day of May 1787 before}
