Middlesex ss}  William Woodbridge maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound Apprentice by Indre to John Boadman of the Maze Tooley Street in the Parish of St Olave in Southwark in the County of Surry  Cooper & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with him there & this Deponent afterwards to Wit in or about the year 1763 lived in & rented a house in the Same Parish & during Such his residence therein he was Charged to & paid Poors rates for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish Office or paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement That he hath three Children namely Thomas Woodbridge aged about Sixteen Years Ann Woodbridge aged about twelve years & Elizabeth Woodbridge aged about Nine Years the lawful issue & this Depont by his late wife Elizabeth Woodbridge deced the two first of which Children have gained Settlemts for themselves That this Deponent hath another Child named Mary Ann aged about twenty Months which was born of the body of Mary Hoskins in New Street in Old Street in the Parish of St Luke in Middx & baptized in the Church belonging to St Lenoard Shoreditch Which Child is illegitimate and a Bastard by reason that he this Deponent was never married to the Mother of the Said Child

Sworn this 11. day of Janry 1786 before  Robr: Smith

Willm: Woodbridge
