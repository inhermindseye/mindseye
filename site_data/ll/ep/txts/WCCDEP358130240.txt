Middx.  Elizth. Roxburgh aged abt. Fifty five Years upon Oath says She is the Widow of Jas. Roxburgh says the said James Roxburgh abt. Eighteen Years ago Rented a seperate Tenement of Mr. Lucas next to the Sun Tavern in Clare Street Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds, lived in the same about two Years & hath quitted the same upwards of Sixteen Years Says her sd. late Husband after he quitted the said Appartment never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or served any Parochial Office

Sworn the 30 day of Novr. 1756 before} Theodr. Sydenham

E Roxbrgh
