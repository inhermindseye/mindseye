Westmr  Maria Wright  Singlewoman Aged about Thirty Years upon Oath Says she Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Hooper a Ribond Weaver in King Street  Cheap Side in the Parish of St. Lawrance Jewry in the City of London One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages Six Pounds Quitted said Service about three Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn the Day of February 1764 Before}

Upon Mr. Olivers Complt J. W
