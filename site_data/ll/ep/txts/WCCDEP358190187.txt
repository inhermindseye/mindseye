Middx  Elizabeth Kirk aged about Nine Years upon Oath says that in or about the Month of December One thousand seven hundred and fifty nine She this Examinant was by an Order under the Hands and Seals of Two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the County of Middx Removed from the Parish of St. Clement Danes to the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the said County of Middx as the place of her last legal Settlement against which Order there was no Appeal to the best of her Knowledge and belief.

Sworn the day of 1761 Before}
