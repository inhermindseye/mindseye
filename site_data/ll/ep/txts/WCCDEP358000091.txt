St Clement Dane 11th Aug. 1785

Middlesex(to wit)  Sign of the Beckford Head}  Vide the other Side were Sworn witht.  Aaron Lilly aged forty Nine Years or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That he rented and lived in a House Situate in Peters Lane Cow Crop in the Parish of Saint Sepulchre in their the City of London at a Yearly Rent of upwards of ten Pounds for about half a Year that he quitted the same about Nine Years ago and since that time has not rented any Tenement of the Yearly Value of ten Pounds paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his knowledge, Information or belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th Augt. 1785 before  Rd. Taylor  James Paine

Aaron Lilly
