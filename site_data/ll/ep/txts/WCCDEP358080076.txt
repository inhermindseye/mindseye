Middx & Westmr.}  Jane Stains Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Jones at the Sign of the Queens head in Little Poultney Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Six Months ago since which time she hath never gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 22d day of August 1776 before R Butler James Croft .

her Jane Stains Mark
