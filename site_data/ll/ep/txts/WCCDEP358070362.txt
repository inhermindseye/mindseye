Middlesex & Westminster}  Hannah Grove Aged about 58 Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Saml. Grove who has been Dead about nine Years and by whom she hath four Children now living all of whom are now provided for except Hannah aged about nineteen who has never gained any other Settlement than thro' her father and furthr says that her said late husband Rented and lived in a House in Suffolk Street in the Mint in the Parish of Saint George in the Borough of Southwark in the County of Surry for 12 Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of twelve pounds and after he quitted the said house he never afterwards rented house paid Taxes or otherwise gained anyother subsequent Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said husbands Death.

Sworn this 26th. Day of April 1776 before  John Cox

Hannah Grove her Mark
