Middx.  Thos. Reed aged abt. 50 yrs. lodging at Mrs. Longs in Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the Country of Middx Upon Oath says he Intermarried with Mary his wife at Swapham Church in Norfolk abt. 31 Yrs ago Says he lived in a house and paid the Poors Rate for One Year in the Parish of House Bardolph in the County of Norfolk and hath Quitted same abt. 14 Years; since which time he hath not Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid parish Taxes.

Sworn 30th. Augst. 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond  Henry Cheere

Thomas Reed
