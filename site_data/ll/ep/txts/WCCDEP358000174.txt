St. Clemt. Danes

passd. to St. Martins}  James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex When his Oath saith That she has been informed which Information he this Examinant believes to be true that Esther Pulley aged 20 years or thereabouts is the Daughter of George and Esther Pulley who were as this Deponent is informed Married at the Parish Church of Great Ealing in the County of Middlesex upwards of 20 years ago That her Father died abroad & after his Death her Mother Rented & lived in a House in Little Russell Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster upwds. of 3 Years at the yearly Rent of £20 & upwards & that her said Mother was passed from the Parish of Saint Clemt. Danes to Saint Martin in the fields afored. abt. a Year & a half ago & died in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish of Saint Martin in the fields And this Examinant fmr. says that the said Esther hath not accordg to the best of his Death Knowledge or belief gained and Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this 15th day of Decr. 1785 before us  James Croft  Rt. Taylor

James Talboys
