Westmr  Eleanor Fox on behalf of her Father Thomas Wilson who now lies Ill in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Upon Oath Says that her said Father Rented a House in Little Russell Street in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the said Liberty of Westmr. for One Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and Paid to the Poor there hath quitted the said House about Ten Years and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes Says he Intermarried with this Examinants Mother Mary in Belfast Church in Ye County of Antram in ye Kingdom of Ireland hath one Child (to wit) Elizth Aged about Sixteen Years.

Sworn the 18th Day of October 1763 before}

Upon Mr. Ralph Williams Complaint Ovr J W
