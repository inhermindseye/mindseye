Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit James Carpenter aged about 26 years upon his Oath saith that he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year that he was bound an Apprentice to one Robt. Newberry a Copper plate printer in Milford Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Seven years that he served his said Master under Such Indenture Six years that he Run away to Sea that Since he quitted his said  Apprenticeship he hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his knowledge Information on belief he may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1786  Js Carpenter R Butler
