Westmr.  Ann Miers  wido . of Henry Miers upon Oath saith her sd. late husband died about Eight Years ago and left her without Child or Children since which she has not unmarryed with any other purpose and that about five Years since she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Wm Symonds  Upholsteres in Bell Yard in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for one Year & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Three pounds and that this Examinant continued in the sd Mr. Symonds Service to the time of his Death which happend a Week after he quitted the aforesd. house in Bell Yd. That she has not since the Decease of the sd. Mr Symonds rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish

Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Novr. 20th. 1749 before H Fielding

The Mark of Ann Miers
