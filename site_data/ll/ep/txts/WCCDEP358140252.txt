Middx.  Randolph Turner lodging at Mr Corbynes in little Russell Street Bloomsbury on the behalf of Hannah Gregory  Singlewoman a Lunatic aged abt. Twenty four years Upon Oath says that the said Hanh. Gregory lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Welch at the three black lions in Hollywell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for One year and upwards at the Yearly wages of Four Pounds Ten Shillings and hath quitted said Service abt. four Years as the said Hanh. Gregory has often times informed this Examinant when she was in her Senses and as he verily believes says she has not since she quitted said Service Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt. for one Year as she hath also informed him says that the said Hannah Gregory has been Mad abt. Six Weeks

Sworn this 2d. June 1759 before} Paul Vaillant

Randolph Turner
