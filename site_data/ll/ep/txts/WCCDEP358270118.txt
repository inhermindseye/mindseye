After Birth 

Jane Sheepy and Richd Sheepy her B. Child

St Clement Danes 24th June 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement St CD  Child born in Store Street Hospital  Reputed Father (to wit) Jane Sheepy aged 35 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr Carter  in the Strand in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for a year & upwards at the Annual Wages of Seven Guineas that she quitted that Service in or about the Month of June 1792 and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief And this Examinant's Upon Oath saith that  in or about the Month of July or August next Last  she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child since named Richard Sheepey at the Store Street Hospital which is become chargeable to the  said parish of St Clement Danes that Richard Jenkins who Lives as Shopman at Mr Yates's the Hosier Corner of  Villiers Street in the Strand in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body on in or about the Month of February July 1791 and at Several times between that and the birth of the said Child and that he and no one else is the real true father thereof

Sworn the 24th Day of June 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Jane Sheepy's Mark

Witness Benj Newman
