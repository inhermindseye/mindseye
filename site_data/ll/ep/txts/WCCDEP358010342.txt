Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Thomasin Thomas  Widow aged fifty nine years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that time she has been a Widow she has lived for twelve Months and upwards as a lived yearly Servants on the Mr steel a stagmaker his Canver Court Infell street  Charles Streets day in the Parish of saint months in the Fields at the Yearly wages of his pounds that she quitted his service about twenty years ago and since that her not done any act Matter or things whereby to the best of her knowledge she may Rent acquired any subsequent Settlement

Swron this 31st. May 1790 before  Sampr Wright M Addington

Thomasin Thomas
