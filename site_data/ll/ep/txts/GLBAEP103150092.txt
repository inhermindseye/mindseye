Middlesex ss}  Mary Burley the widow of James Burley mariner deced maketh oath That divers Years ago & after her Said late husbands death She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Robert Bentley in the parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Coachman at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds & Continued in Such Service for the Space of a year & by means thereof gained a legal there That She this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year or been Married nor hath She done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of } Mary Burley
