Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  Susannah Delap aged about 47 years upon her Oath saith That about 17 years ago she Intermarried with Robert Augustus Delap her late Husband who has been dead about Ten years at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes That her said late Husband Rented a first Floor of a Mrs. Carter the corner of Spring Garden Gate in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Nine Months or thereabouts at the Weekly Rent of One Guinea that after he quitted said Apartments he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement and Saith that she is now chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes

Sworn this 30th. day of Octr. 1798 before us  H. Wisstead Jn Bacon

her Susannah Delap Mark

Witness H Day Beadle
