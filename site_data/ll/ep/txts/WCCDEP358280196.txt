Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Inwood aged about 21 years. upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr Millington a Butcher in Clements Inn Passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of £4 that she hath quitted said Service about Three weeks since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Morris Winter a Butcher who lives with his Brother Mr. Winter in King Street  Seven Dials is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of March last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Morris Winter is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Novr. 1788 } R Butler

her Sarah Inwood Mark

Sarah Inwood at Mrs. Kershaws at No. 11 Clemts. Inn Passage

Morris Winter is a Tall Thin Man wears his own Hair hanging in his Neck fair Complexion.
