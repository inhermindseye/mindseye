Middx & Westmr.}  Ann Hayley Aged about Eighteen Years upon Oath Says She never was Married now ever Rented a House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Luke Hayley who was a Milkman Rented and lived in a House Situate in Kinghead Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the liberty of Westminster for many Years But what Yearly Rent he paid for the same this Examinant cannot Setforth Says She was Born in the said House as She has been Informed and Verily believes

Sworn this 21st. Day of Decr 1768 Before me}

her Ann Hayley Mark Witness Petr Sidebotham
