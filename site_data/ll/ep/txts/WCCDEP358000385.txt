Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit James Talboys One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Martha Shelton who lays ill Maketh Oath and Saith he has been informed which Information he believes to be true that the said Martha Shelton Rented a House and Garden in Little Chelsea in the Parish of Saint Lukes Chelsea in the County of Middlesex for 2 Years at the Yearly Rent of £10 hath quitted said House about 18 Years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 1st. Decr. 1786 before us  Jno: Wright  Wm Gascoignes

James Talboys
