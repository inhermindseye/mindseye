Middx.  Cathne. Easton aged abt. 25 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Easton (now at Sea) by whom she hath one Child John aged abt. 8 Months says her said Husband was born in Caple Street in the Parish of St. Mary in the City of Dublin in the Kingdom of Ireland .

Sworn Febry 15th. 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cathne. Easton
