Middx  Mary Cole aged about 62 Years upon her Oath saith she is the Wido . of Geo: Cole decease Says her sd. late Husband Geo: Cole had a Certificate from the Church wardens and Oversrs. of the Poor of the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex dated the 2d. of Decr. 1714 acknowledging this Examinant & her sd. late Husband with their two Children (to wit) Geo: then aged 7 Yrs. & Diana then aged 4 Yrs. to be Inhabitants legally Settled in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Says her said late Husband after she granting of the sd. Certificate did not rent Ten pounds a Year or Serve any Parochial Office neither hath this Examinant since his Death rented ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif & is Poor and not able to maintain herself

Sworne 13th. Novr. 1745 before}
