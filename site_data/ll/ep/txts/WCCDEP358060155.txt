Middx  Ann Murray upon Oath saith that whilst a Singleman she lived a yearly Servant with Mr Singer at Perry Oaks in the Parish of Armsworth in the County of Middlesex and hath not since she quitted that Service rented ten pounds a yr paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year Says she intermarried with one James Murray a Native of Ireland who hath absconded from her & whether he is Living or dead this Examint. knoweth not

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before  Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Murray
