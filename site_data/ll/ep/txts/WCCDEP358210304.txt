Westmr.  Ann Schellinger Aged about Twenty seven Years, upon Oath says she intermarried with Edward Schellinger in the Parish Church of Enfield in the County of Middlesex about Nine Years ago; Says her said Husband Rented a House in Grace Church Street in the Parish of St. Peter in Cornhill London, of the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds, lived therein about a year and half, & Quitted same about Five Years and half ago; Says her said Husband Absconded from her about Five Years and half ago and after he quitted said House in Grace Church aforesaid, never Rented Ten Pounds by the Year or paid Taxes to her knowledge or belief, says since the time her Husband Absconded from her she hath not seen or heard of him, and that about Two years and half ago, she this Examinant Rented and lived in a house of Twenty five Pounds a Year for the space of Two Years the back of St. Clements in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster but without the knowledge, privity consent or any act or Deed whatsoever of the said Edward Schellinger her Husband; She during all the time she Rented said House the back of St. Clements never having seen or heard of her said Husband as aforesaid

Sworn the 26th, day of July 1768 , before} Thos Kynaston  Petr Planck

Ann Schellinger
