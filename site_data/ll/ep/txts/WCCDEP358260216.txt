Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Andrews aged about 39 years upon his Oath saith that about fifteen years ago he Intermarried with Mary his present wife at the parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch by whom he hath Four Children living Vizt. Susa. aged about Thirteen years provided for Elizabeth aged about seven years Catherine aged about Six year and Nathan Parrin aged about Three years unprovided for That he this Deponent Rented Two Rooms unfurnished at No. Elevens in Clements Lane or in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Six Months at the Weekly Rent of Two Shillings and Six pence during all which time he Rented a Stall of a Mr. Peacock at Butcher Row the back part of Saint Clements in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes at the yearly Rent of Four Pounds That he hath quitted said apartments and Hall about Three years since which time he hath not Rented any Place or Places to the Amount of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his knowledge information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 5th. day of Septr. 1797 before me } Saml. Edward

John Andrews
