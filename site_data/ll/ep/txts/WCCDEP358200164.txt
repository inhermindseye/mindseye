Westmr  Mary Fare Aged about Twenty two Years upon Oath Says she was never Married Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Sert . with Mr. Smith at the Sign of the Coach Horses at Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds hath quitted said Service about five Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Sert for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to Become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One James Graham a Clog Maker who now

Works in Of Alley in York Buildings in the Strand had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of June Last and at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that he the said James Graham and no other Person did Beget the said Child or Children

Sworn the 28th. Day of Novr. 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Mary Fare
