Middx 

Thos. Read upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife Elizabeth in Stepney Church upwards of 12 Years ago by whom he hath 2 Children Mary aged abt. 6 Years & Sasa. aged abt. 2 Years Says he lived a Yearly Servant (whilst a Singleman with Mr. Services Glazier in Vear Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £12 for one Year & upwds. and left his sd. Service abt. the Year 1735 since which time he has not rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 21st. 1748 before} Tho Lane

Tho Read
