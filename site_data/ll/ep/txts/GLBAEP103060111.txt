Aldgate Middx

Thomas Bridges Maketh Oath That he Servd Seven Years Apprenticeship by Indenture to John Sanders of Panton Street in the Parish of Saint Martins in the Fields in the City and Liberty of Westminster Silk Dyer And hath gaind no Settlement Since hath a wife  Elizabeth

Sworn 11th day of February Ano Dom 1748 Richd Ricards N. Dukinfield

Thomas Bridges
