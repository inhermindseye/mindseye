Middlesex ss}  Richard Bonham Maketh oath that he Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of his Parents or either of them That his late Mother Joanna Bonham deced has informed him that he this Depont. was Born in Sweetland Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex which information he believes to be true That he this Depont. never rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or Paid any Poor Rates or Kings Taxes nor was he ever bound apprentice Nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor to his Knowledge or belief hath he ever done any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right and that he hath a wife Named Sarah to Whom he was Married to at the Fleet about Twenty two Years ago

Sworn this 23 day of January 1772 before  Saml. Spagg

The Mark of Richard Bonham
