Middx  Ann Abbott  Widow late Wife of William Abbott deceased maketh Oath and Saith that about Ten Years ago She was Married to her said late Husband William Abbott at St. Andrews Church Holbourn in the Liberty of the City of London And this Deponent further Saith that She hath got three Children (namely) Agnes Aged about five Years Adam Aged about four Years and Ann Aged about One Year and half the lawfull Four of her said late Husband

Sworn this 19th. day of Decr. 1744 Before Robert Dennett

her Ann Abbott mark
