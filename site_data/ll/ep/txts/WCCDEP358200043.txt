Middx  Mary Miller Aged about Eighteen Years upon Oath Says she was Bound an Apprentice to John Sadd in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr: and Served time as such about three Years and then was discharged her said Masters Service And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likeley to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor & unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Charles Mackabe  Apprentice to Mr. Neale in Clements Inn in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes did beget the said Child or Children on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant Sometime in the Month of February last and at several other times since at One of which times

he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now pregnant

Sworn the Day of Decr 1763 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Miller

Upon Mr Robt Williams Compt J. W
