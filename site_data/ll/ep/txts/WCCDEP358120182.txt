Middx  Rd. Ludkins aged abt. 48 Years upon Oath Saith he was married to Ann his Wife at the Fleet London abt. 14 Years ago by whom he has two Children (viz) Sarah aged abt. 11 Years and Lydia aged 10 Months and that he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Edwd Bond Shoemaker in little Sheer Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes has been out of his time abt. 28 Years and never Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Augt. 17th. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Rd. Ludkins
