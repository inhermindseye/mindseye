Middx.  Thos. Morris upon Oath says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his  wife lately deceased in the Parish Church of St Brides in the City of Chester abt. 12 Years since by whom he has two Children Chas . aged abt. 5 Yrs. born in the lying in hospl. in Aldersgate Street and Mary aged abt. 4 Years born in the lying in hospl. in Brownlow Street says he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Robt. Jones a Carpenter at Greenfield in the Parish of Hollywell in the County of Flint and served him 3 quarters of a Year the first part of his said Apprenticeship and then this Examinant and his Master parted by Consent and never was assigned over to any Person: says he lived in a house of his own of the Yearly Value of three Pounds for One Quarter of a Year in the Parish of Dermerchion in the sd County of Flint and never after Rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yrly. Servant for one Year

Sworn this 29th. day of Novr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Thos. Morris

Upon the Complaint of Mr. Stratton Decr. 1759. The Childn. were Removed 27th. July 1757 B: H: P: V:
