Middlesex ss}  Jane Mortiss  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago her late father Jonathan Mortiss deced lived in & rented a house Called the Blackhorse Alehouse in Swan Alley in the Parish of St James Clerkenwell in the Sd County & during such his residence therein was charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same as she this Deponent has been Credibly informed & Verily believes to be true That her Said Father did not afterwards to her knowledge or belief do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere out of the Sd Parish That She this Depont lived with her Sd Father as part of his family in the Sd house & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor over did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this. 18. day of Novemr 1784 before R Davies Robt: Smith

Jane Mortiss
