Middlesex ss}  Jane McDougall the wife of Dougle Mc Dougall maketh oath that her sd husband who is now in America was by birth a Scotchman & never did any Act to gain a Settlemt in England or Wales to her Knowledge information or belief That this Deponent divers years ago & When a single woman was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year by & with Mr John Speller a Tallow Chandler in upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County at the Yearly Wages of Five pounds & Continued in Such Service for three years & thereby gained a Settlement there That she did not afterwards live as a hired servant elsewhere for a year or do any Act to gain Anothr. Settlement Save What She has Acquired by her marriage as aforesaid That She was lawfully Married to her Said husband by whom she hath three Children now living namely Elizabeth McDougall aged Seven years William McDougall aged five years & Elizabeth McDougall aged three years & nine Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her sd. husband

Sworn this 16 day of Janry 1778 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Jane McDougall
