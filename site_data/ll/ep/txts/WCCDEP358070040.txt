Middx to wit Catherine Rogerson Aged about Fifty five years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly. Servant with one Mr. Elizabeth Tostock in Arrundale Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Three years and an half at the yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Fourteen years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 14th. day of November 1772 before me}
