Middx  Mary Smith Aged abt. Forty six Years Upon Oath says, she is the Wido. of Jno. Smith Deced with sd. Jno Smith was by a Vagrant pass dated the Thirtyeth day of March 1749 passed from the parish of St. Mary White Chapell to the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye County of Middx Upon his Oath that he the sd. Jno. Smith lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Lambourn a Button maker in St. Clemts. Church Yard in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes whilst he was a Singleman for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds & never Rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes and this Examinant further saith that she hath not since his Decease rented Ten pounds a Year or pd. Parish Taxes that she hath our Child by him named Mary aged abt. three Years

Sworn the 8th. day of March 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Mary Smith
