Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Garrett aged about 45 years upon his Oath saith that about 15 years ago he Intermarried with Elizabeth his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Giles Cripplegate That he this Deponent Rented and lived in a house in Hanway Yard in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for about Eleven years at the yearly Rent of Twenty five pounds That he hath quitted said House about Ten years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 28th-} day of July 1796 N Bond Rust: Clarke

John Garrett
