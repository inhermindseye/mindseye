Middlesex ss} The above named Hannah Davis  Singlewoman maketh oath That She was born in the house known by the Sign of the Jackle Bock ale house in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd county That She is the Daughter of the above named William Davis by Hannah his Wife both deced That her said Father as she has been informed & belives rented the sd house for divers years & paid parish Taxes for the same That he removed from thence to a house in the Grange Walk Southwarke in the parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermond  say in the County of Surry being the Horse Shoe alehouse but how long he lived there or whether he paid any or What rent or Taxes She cant set forth That She never was bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 10 day of June 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Hannah Davis
