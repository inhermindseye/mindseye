Middlesex & Westmr}  Mary Park Aged about Twenty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Parker a Taylor in Cooks Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years and five Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Months ago & hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish she being Poor & unable to maintain the same when born says that one Charles Ellis a Captain of the Fryant Man of Warr and who is now abroad in the Mediteranean is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in her said Masters House about the Middle of July last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at one other time afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said Charles Ellis and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 13th. day of Febry 1772 before W Kelyngs

Mary Park
