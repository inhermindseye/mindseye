Middx & Westmr.}  Margaret Mitchell aged about 23 Years lodging in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex Upon Oath says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Sir Richard Corbett in the Parish of St. Chad in the Town of Shrewsbury in the County of Salop for One Year and upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Twenty Shillings and hath not since she quitted her said Service Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargeable to the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born says that Owen Mccarty  Clerk to Mr. Mc. carty at No. 8 in Cursitor Street Chancery Lane London had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body the latter End of May or beginning of June 1765 and at several other times since at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Childn. with which she is now Pregnant, and that the said Owen Mc. carty and no other Person did beget the same.

Sworn Feby. 19th. 1766 before} Paul Vaillant

Margaret Mitchell
