St. Clement Dane August 20th. 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD (to wit) Mary Rollins aged Seventeen years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married lived as an hired yearly Servant nor otherwise obtained any subsequent legal settlement in her own Right Saith That she hath been informed her Father Edward Rollins  intermarried with this Deponents Mother Ann at the parish Church of St Margaret Westminster about twenty two years ago that since their said Marriage her said Falter Rented & with his family resided in an House in Holles Street in the parish of St Clement Danes  in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx about 5 years at the annual Rent of £20 and quitted it in or about the year 1780 and saith that she is informed he hath not since Rented any House nor  Rented any Apartment of £10 a year or otherwise obtained  any subsequent legal settlement and that she is Poor and unable at present to provide for herself without assistance

Sworn the 20th day of August 1792 before me} R Butler

Mary Rollins 's Mark

Witness Edwd Burbidge
