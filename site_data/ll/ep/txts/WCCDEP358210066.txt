Westmr.  Benjamin Pack aged about fifty Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Mary his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Martin in the fields about Seven Years ago, says that by Indenture bearing date the 17th, day of March 1731-2 he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to James Smith an Engraver in the Parish of St. George  Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex , served about two Years of his said Apprenticeship and then his Master removed to St. Clements Dane & afterwards was Arrested and sent to the Fleet Prison, after his said Master came out of the said prison he resided in Middle row in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the said County of Middlesex where he continued till the Expiration of the abovementioned Indenture of Apprenticeship and this Examinant further upon his Oath says that he continued to work for his said Master during the said Term of Seven Years and with the consent of his said Master laid the last twelve Months of his said Apprenticeship at the house of this Examinants Father in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Dunstan in the West London ;

Sworn the 4th. Augst; 1766 before} Tho. Balack 

Bej: Pack
