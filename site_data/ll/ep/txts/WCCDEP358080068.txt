Middx & Westmr}  Sarah Griffiths Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Humphry Sandforth  Esqr . at the Isle in the Parish of Saint Shadds in the County of Salop hath quitted her said Service upwards of four Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath Says that on the 17th. day of June last she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which has since been baptized by the Name of William and is now Chargable to the said Parish Says that one William Robinson who lately lived with one Mr. Mountain in Woodstreet and who now kept a publick House at Old Buckingham in Norfolk (as she has been Informed and verily believes) is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of October last but the particular Days or times she does not now remember and at sevl. diferent times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Wm. Robinson & no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of July 1776 before M Addington.

Sarah Griffiths
