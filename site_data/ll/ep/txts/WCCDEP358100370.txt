St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.}  passd. to St. Mary Le Strand  Mary Coe : aged 70 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith, That she intermarried with Vincent Coe her late Husband at the Parish Church of Christ church Surey about 44 years ago. That her Husband lived with Messrs. Mayd & Co. of the Strand and at the same Time kept a House in Stones Buildings in the Parish of Saint Mary le Strand at the yearly Rent of £14: That they inhabited the same some Years That since that Time her Husband died in the Workhouse of Saint Mary le Strand , That she has not (nor Did her Husband to the last of her knowledge) gained any Settlement since he Rented the said House in Stones Buildgs.

Sworn this 3d. March 1785 . before  Thos: Gilbert : Sampn Wright

her Mary Coe Mark
