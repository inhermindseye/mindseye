Middx  Wm. Vaughan upon his Oath saith that he inter- -married with his present Wife  Johannah Vaughan about 4 Years since in the Parish Church of St: James at Clerkenwell that in the Year 1694 he was bound an Apprentice to & Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Captn:  Thomas Rathbone in Rathbone Place in the Parish of St. Mary Le bone in the County of Middx & since the Expiration of his sd. Apprentiship hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne 18th. June 1744 before} Wm. Jones

William Vaughan
