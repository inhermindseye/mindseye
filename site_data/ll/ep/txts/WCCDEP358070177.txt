Westmr. to wit Bartholomew Lowe Aged about Thirty Six Years on his Oath Says he never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he was bound on Apprentice to one William Edward a Watchmaker in Fetter Lane the Corner of Bartletts Buildings in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn London and served his said Master under the said Indenture upwards of Two Years and and half when this Examinant wanting to go to Sea his said Master Consented to deliver up his said Indentures which was done accordingly and this Examinant very soon afterwards went to sea but says that after his said Indentures were delivered up he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or Otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Febry 1774 before} 

Bartholomew Lowe
