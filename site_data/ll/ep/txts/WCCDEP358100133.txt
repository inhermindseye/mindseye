Middx  Mary Cooper Aged about 37 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired yearly Servant for one year Says her Father Wm. Cooper served seven years Apprenticeship to one John Dorril l a Taylor in Feathers Court  Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never gained any or. Subsequent Settlemt. as her Mother Ann Cooper has lately Informed her and which Information she verily he believes to be true And this Examinant further says that she never gained any other Settlemt. than as aforementioned

Sworn this 8th. day of Octr. 1783  Sampn Wright  Wm Kitchiner
