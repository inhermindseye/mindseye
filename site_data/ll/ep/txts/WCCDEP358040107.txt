Westmr.  James Barbo removed from the Parish of St. Luke Middx. upon Oath saith he intermarry'd with his present Wife Mary at the Fleet London upwards of 30 Years ago Says he lived in and Rented the White Swan Alehouse in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Rent of £14 or thereabts. for four Year & upwards & left the same about 20 Years ago Says he has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 21st. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

James Barbo
