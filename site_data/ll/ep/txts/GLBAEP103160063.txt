Middlesex ss}  Richard Bowyer maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to & Served the whole thereof with his Father William Bowyer in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Waterman & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

Richard Bowyer
