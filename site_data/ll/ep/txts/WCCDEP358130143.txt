Middx  Ann Gates aged about 30 Years says She is the Widow of Daniel Gates deceased And that her said late Husband Rented a House of Twenty five pounds a year at the East end of the new Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes & lived therein upwards of Nine Months & quitted the same about nine years ago & never after Rented Ten pounds a year or paid Parish Taxes Says She hath not since the Death of her said Husband Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 16th. Day of April 1756 . before} Barth. Hammond

The Mark of Ann Gates
