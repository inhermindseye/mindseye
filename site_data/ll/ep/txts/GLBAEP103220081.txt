Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Ann White of the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken upon oath before me Rice Davies  Esquire  one of his Majesties Justice of the Peace in and for the Sd County this Fourth day of November one thousand Seven hundred & eighty Six Who Saith that on the twenty ninth day of August now last past at Norwich Court in the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate She the Sd Ann White was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since baptized & registered by the name of Rosanna Tidcount And that the Said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that Richard TidCount now living as a servant with one Mr Wheeler in Green Lane Stamford Hill in the Said County of Middlesex did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child.

Taken and Signed the day & Year above written before me R Davies

Ann White
