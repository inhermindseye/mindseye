Westmr.  Ann Clark aged about 24 Years upon Oath says she was never Married, Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes was an Apprentice or a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, says her Father John Clark Rented a House in Whitcomb Street in the Parish of St: Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and upwards and there died, and further upon her Oath says that on the 4th. day of January 1764, she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the house of Mr. Robinson (a Chandler's Shop) in Water Lane  Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Bridget alias St. Brides London which hath since been baptized Alkemia Ann

Sworn the 2d. day of January 1766 , before} Thos Kynaston R Kilsha

The Mark of Ann Clark
