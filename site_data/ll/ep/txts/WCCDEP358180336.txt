Wm. Dickenson aged about 15 Years upon his Oath saith he was bound Apprentice by Indenture dated the 5th: Day of February 1742 to Mr. Thos. Russell of Frog Lane in the Parish of St. Mary at Islington in the County of Middx  Clogmaker & lived with his sd. Master abt. 6 Months & then run away from his sd. Master on Account of Ill Usage Says he hath not been Assigned over to any Person or lived with any person as a Servt. with his sd. Masters knowledge or Consent or otherwise gained a Settlemt. to his knowledge or beleif Says he hath been Informed his said Master dyed since this Examint. last his sd. Service

Sworne 28th. May 1745 before} Wm: Jones

William Dickinson
