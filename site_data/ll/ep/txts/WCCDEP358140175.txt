Middx.  John Eugene aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath says he served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years with Mr. Chase a Copper Plate Printer in Milford Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx. and never Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 3d. of Novr. 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond

John Eugene
