Middx  George Wilson upon Oath Saith he was bound Apprentice by Indenture dated the 5th. Octr. 1730 to Henry Facer  Feltmaker for Seven Years that he lived with the sd. Henry Facer as his apprentice three or four Years in Barnaby Street in the Parish of St. Olave  Southwark in the County of Surry and then his sd Master dyed & this Examinant never was assigned over nor ever rented ten Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year that upwds. of 20 Yrs. ago he was married to Margt . his wife at the Fleet London.

Sworn 25th May 1754 before}
