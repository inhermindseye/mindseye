Middlesex (ss)  John Wilson Maketh Oath & Saith that in the Month of February 1786, he this Examinant was lawfully married to his present Wife  Mary Ann (whose maiden name was Hugman ) at the Parish Church of St. Peter in the City of Norwich in the County of Norfolk , and that there is Issue of the said Marriage One Son namely John Hugman Wilson aged Thirteen Months-That in the year 1775 he this Examinant was hired by Mr . Robt. Smith of Wicklewood , in the Parish of Wicklewood , in the County of Norfolk as his Yearly Servant at the Annual Wages of Six Pounds, and continued to live with him under that hiring for the space of one Year That he was hired a second Time by the said Robert Smith at the said Annual Wages of Six Pounds, and lived under that second Hiring for one other year And this Examinant further saith, that he hath not since his quitting the Service of the said Robert Smith lived a Year with any person or persons Whomsoever, rented any house or lodging of the yearly rent or value of Ten Pounds or upwards or done any other act whereby to gain a settlement in any parish precinct or place whatsoever.

Sworn the day of Decr. 1788 Before us}
