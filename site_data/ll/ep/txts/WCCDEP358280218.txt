Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Price aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Price in Craven Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Fifteen Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she has not lived as a hired a yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that on the 26th- day of January last past she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the Store Street Lying Inn Hospital That one Peter Murphy who is Servant to a Mrs. Coghlan who is at present in France but when in London lives at No:1 New Cavendish Street is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Peter Murphy is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of febry. 1789 } R Butler

Ann Price

Peter Murphy is a Middle sized Man about 30 years of Age fair Complexion Wears his Hair tied behind his Livery is Drab Coat Gold Lace Red Waiscoat and Black Breeches & Gold Laced Hat.

Notes given for £15:0:0 Mr Coghlan Done Barnes joined at 2 4 & 6 Mos.

Release Exted.
