Middlesex ss  Ann Beevan  widow Maketh oath that Divers Years ago she rented a house in Stoney Lane Houndsditch in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the City of London & Paid Ten Pounds a Year Rent for the Same & by Means thereof she gained a legal Settlemt. there as she is advised And this Depont. Saith that she hath not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of October 1770 before}
