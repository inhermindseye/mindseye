Middx  Elizabeth Seago aged about 29 Years Lodging in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Thomas Seago who has been Dead about Three Months to whom she was Married at the Fleet upwards of Seven Years ago says that her said Husband hath informed her he was Born in Piccadilly (where his Father and Mother kept a Fishmonger's Shop ) in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster and never kept House was an Apprentice or hired Yearly Servant to this Examinant's knowledge That she knows nothing concerning the Settlemt. of her Husband's Father otherwise than as aforementioned That she hath Two Children by her said Husband to wit Elizabeth aged upwards of Three Years and Thomas aged One Year and upwards.

Sworn the 3d. day of July 1761 Before.}
