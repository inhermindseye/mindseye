Middx  Susannah Bennett Wife of Mark Bennett gone from her with whom she Intermarried in the Parish Church of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx about Ten Years ago Says her said Husband whilst a Singleman lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Weaver a Carver in Kings Head Court  Drury Lane in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of hath quitted his said Service about Fourteen Years and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes as this Examinant hath been informed by her said Husband and which information she Verily believes to be true Says she has three Children by

her said Husband (Viz) Sayer  James Aged about Eight Years Mark Aged about Six Years and Thomas Aged about Four Years

Sworn the Day of 1763 Before}

Upon Mr Olivers Complaint J: W
