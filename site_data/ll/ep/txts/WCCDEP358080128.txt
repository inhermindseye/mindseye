Middx- & Westmr-}  Wm- Shadewell Aged about thirty four Years on his Oath says he Intermarried with Mary his Wife at St- Anns Black Fryers about Ten Years ago and by whom he hath no Childn- now living says before his Intermarrd- with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one John Harrison who kept the Sign of the Swan in Water Street Black Fryers in the said Parish of St- Anns Black Fryers in the City of London upwards of eight Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted the same about eight Years ago Since which time he hath gained no other subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 22nd- Day of March 1777 before me. R Butler Jno. Machin

William Shadewell
