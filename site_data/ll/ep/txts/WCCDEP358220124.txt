Middlesex . & Westmr.}  John Sewell Aged about forty Two Years on his Oath Says he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Bockett a Farmer at South Mims in the County of Middlesex for Seven Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of Eight pounds hath quitted his said Service about Twenty Years and after he quitted the same he never Rented Ten pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn this 19th. day of Febry 1770 before me } R. Butler

his John Sewell Mark

Witness Petr. Sidebotham
