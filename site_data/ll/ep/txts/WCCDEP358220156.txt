Middlesex & Westmr.}  Elizth. Boys Aged about Thirty Two Years Wife of John Boys gone from her with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Le Bone about Nine Years Ago by whom she hath one Child named Mary Aged about Two Years and an half Says her said Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a single Man he lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Schooly at the Green Man at Paddington in the said Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for five Years or there -abouts at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted his said Service about 9 Years ago and after he quitted his said Service he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husband left her.

Sworn this 21st. day of June 1770 before us  R Butler W Kelyngs

Mark Eliz Boyce

Wittness Fras Bishop
