Middlesex ss  Jane Powell  Singlewoman maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one Mr White an Apothecary in the Back Road in the Parish of St Mary Ishington in the sd County & Continued in Such Service at Certain Wages for Several years & by means there of gained a legal Settlement there That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15 day of Septemr 1777 before  Parkr Rowlands  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Jane Powell
