St. Clement Danes

Middx & West minister } To Wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes on Behalf of Eliz. Kingston aged 50. Years who is Insane Maketh Oath and Saith that he has been informed which Informa -tion he Verily believes to be true, that the Said Elizabeth Kingston has lived three Years and Six Months as Matron in the Orphans School in the City Road at Twenty Pounds Per Ann. has Quitted said School Five Years back, since that have not done any Act, Deed, Matter or thing so as to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Octr. 1795 }
