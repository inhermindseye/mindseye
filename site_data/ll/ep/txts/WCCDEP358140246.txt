Middx.  Eliz: Beezley aged abt. 88 Yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Thos. Beezley who has been dead abt. Sixteen Years and that she can give no account of her said husbands Settlement says before her intermarriage with her said husbd. she was bound Apprentice for 7 Years to John Ellis her father in law in Maypole Alley opposite the Marshalsea prism in the Borough of Southwark in the Parish of St. George  Southwark in the County of Surry and served five Years of her sd. Apprenticeship and then her said Master dyed; says she never was assigned over to any Person or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other parish or place to her knowledge & belief

Sworn this 20th. day of April 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Eliz: Beezley

Upon the Complaint of Mr Wimberley Ovr . J: P:
