Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.} to wit Sarah Painter aged about 40-years her Oath saith that she never was married That on the 14th. day of May 1783 she was delivered a Female Bastard Child in the House of a Mr. Anders in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of and that on the 25th. day of July 1786 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at the House of a Mr. Bit in Sawyers Yard in the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the Name of Henry That one Henry Perry a Printer & Joiner is the father of the said Two Children he having Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body for sevl. time years last past at sevl. times and begotten the Two Children thereon And that the said Henry Perry is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 2d. day of March 1787 before} Wm Hyde

her Sarah Perry mark
