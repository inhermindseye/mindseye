Middlesex ss}  Catherine Bleasdale maketh oath that her late husband John Bleasdale deced divers Years ago rented a house in Queen Street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County at the yearly Rent of Ten Pounds & upwards & was Charged to And Paid Poors Rates for the Same That he did not afterwards rent a house Lodging other Tenement of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That about Seven Years ago She this Deponent was lawfully married to her Said late husband by whom she hath two Children & lawful Issue now living namely William aged about five Years and Catherine Aged about three Years That about eight Years ago and Previous to her Marriage with her Said husband She this Deponent was delivered of a Child baptized & registered by the name of Charles Bleasdale -in the Parish of St Mary Lambeth in the County of Surrey which is illegitimate & a Bastard by reason that She was not then married to her Said husband who was the Father of Such Child

Sworn this. 2d. day of October 1787 before  R Davies  Tho: T: Cotton

The Mark of Catherine Bleasdale
