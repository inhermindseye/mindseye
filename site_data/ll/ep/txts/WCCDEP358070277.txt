Westr to wit Samuel Vaughan Aged upwards of Thirty three Years his Oath Says he never Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or ever Served any Apprenticeship, Says that he intermarried with his present wife  Mary at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about 11-Years ago and by whom he hath three Children now living namely Ann Aged about Eight Years, Susannah Aged about two Years and Amelia aged about 10 Weeks, Says that his father Samuel Vaughan served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Arnold an Apothocary in Salisbury Court in the Parish of Saint Brides otherwise Bridget in the City of London and never after Rented House paid Taxes or any otherwise Gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d day of July 1775 before M Addington

Samuel Vaughan Paid 2s
