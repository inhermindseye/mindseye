Middx  Elizabeth Ridge aged about 35 Years upon Oath says She was never Married and that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Roberts a Tobacconist & Perfumer near Temple Bar in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds quitted her sd. Service about Eight Years and hath not since lived a hired Yearly Servant a Year Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 15th. day of Decr. 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth. Ridge
