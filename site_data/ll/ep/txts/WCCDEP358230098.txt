Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Adam Wilcox Burbidge one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizabeth Dunn who is Ill Maketh Oath and Saith That he hath been informed which information he verily believes to be true That the said Elizabeth Dunn lived as a hired yearly servant for five years with Mr Thomas Cumbridge No 2 Hornsey Row in the Parish of Saint Mary Tolington in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Nine Guineas That she hath left the said Service about Sixteen years Since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3rd day of December 1799 before us G S Goodenough Menter

A W Burbidge
