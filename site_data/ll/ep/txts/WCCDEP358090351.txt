Middx  Elizth. Clarke Aged about 47 Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a Year or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servt . with One Mr. Bellas a Jeweller the Corner of Market Lane Pall Mall in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted the same about Seven Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of December 1782 before  Sampn Wright R Butler

Eliz Clark
