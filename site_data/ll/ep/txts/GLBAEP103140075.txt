Middlesex ss}  William Harding maketh oath That divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to Thomas Collins of the Parish of Saint John in Southwarke in the County of Surry  Millwright & Served him there for the space of one year & half & then this Depont. was assigned & Turned over to Peter Davis of the Parish of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry  Millwright with whom he Served there for the Space of two years more when his said Second Master died & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Depont. has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent of ten pounds or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or Served any Parish office nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement to his Knowledge or belief

Sworn this 31st day of December 1774 before B Camper R Pell

The Mark of Wm Harding
