Middx  The Exaicon if Sarah Palmor  Singlewoman Loaging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 11th day of Janry 1739 who upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that on the 20th. day of Decr. Last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes wch hath since been Baptized Hannah And Further Saith that one Jno. Freckingham did begett the sd. Child on her Body she this Exaiant at such time living as a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Jno. Furber  Brick layer in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes And he the sd. Jno. Freckingham at the sametime being an Apprentice to the sd. Jno. Furber there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body Sevll. times in the Month of March last and at Sevll. other times since at one of wch times he gott her with the Female Bastard Child She was delivered of as aforesd. and that he the sd. Jno. Freckingham & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof And this Exaiant further Saith that she lived in her sd. Service upwards of one Yeare & came away from the same in November last & hath not since to her knowledge gained a Settlemt. in any other Place & is Poor & not able to maintain the sd. Female Bastard Child

Sworn the day & Yeare above menconed before Tho Lane } Jas: Gronous

The mark of Sarah Palmer
