Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Elizabeth Williams aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she this Deponent and her Sister Ann Williams Rented and lived in a house in Sherard Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for about Four Months at the yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds That after she this Deponent quitted said house which is about Fourteen Months since, hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of March 1795 } N Bond Edwd: Naish

Elizabeth Williams
