Middx  Ann Hales aged about twenty four years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented house paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticesp Says she lived a hired Yrly Servant with one Mrs- Cooksey in the Parish of Saint Clements in the City of Worcester for upwards of one year at the Yearly Wages of forty shillings hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago since which time she hath never gained any other subseqt- Settlemt- in London than as before mentd- Says that on the 8th- day of July last she was Delivd- of a Male Bastd- Child in the Workhouse belongg- to the parish of St- Clemt- Danes which has since been Baptized by the name of Andrew & is become Chargeable to the said Parish she being poor & unable to maintain the same and that one Mr- Andrew Parry an Attorney at Law in Clements Inn in the Parish of St- Clement Danes is the Father thereof he having had carnal Knowl of her Body sometime in the Month of Septr- last past but the particular Day she does not now rember and at sevl- difft- times afterwds- at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was 10 Delivered as afsd- and that the said Andrew Parry and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 29th. Day of July 1777 before me R Butler

Ann Hales
