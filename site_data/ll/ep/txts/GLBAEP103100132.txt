Middlesex ss}  Edward Baley Wooliston maketh oath That divers Years ago he bound himself with his Mothers Consent apprentice by a Common Indenture to John Coventry  of Graves Lane in the Parish of Saint Saviour in the County of Surry  Packthread Spinner and Served three years of his Said apprenticeship under Such Indre in the Same Parish and than this Deponent & his Said late Master by Mutual Consent Agreed to part and thereupon by Mutual Consent burnt & destroyed this Deponts. Indres of apprenticeship and thereby (as it was then meant and understood by this Deponent & his said Master) intirely Cancelled or Annihilated the Said Indre & put an end to this Deponents said apprenticeship And this Depont thereupon left his said Master That during Such his apprenticeship as aforesaid this Depont lodged & lay in his said Masters house for the Space of a year That Since this Depont left his Said Master he this Depont. hath not lived as a time Servant for a year in any one place nor hath rented a house of the Yearly Rented value of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor Serves any Parish offices nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his Knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Settlement Since he left the Said John Coventry

Sworn this 26 day of July 1769 before  Rob Pell W Russell

The Mark of Edward Baley Wooliston
