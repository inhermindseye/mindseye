Westmr. 

Rosamund Gascoyne aged abt. 38 Years upon Oath Says she is the Wido . of James Gascoign deceased Says the sd. James Gascoyne about Seven Years ago rented a house in Stoney Lane in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry of the Yearly Rent of Eleven pounds or thereabouts & lived there in upwds. of Six Months Says the sd. James Gascoigne after he quitted said house in Stoney Lane in his Life time  this Exaiant since his Death never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served an Anuall Office or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 17th. 1747 before  Wm: Jones  Tho: Burdus

The Mark of Rosamund Gascoyne
