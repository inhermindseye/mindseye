Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Hutton Aged about 48 years upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant for 7 years with one Mrs Love N 5 little Wild Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of 52s That she hath left the said Service about 3 Months Since which time she hath not rented any house tenement or lodging of Ten Pounds a year or done any act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th Day of January 1800 before us  Wd Woodenough W

Mary Hutton
