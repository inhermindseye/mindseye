Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Mary at Islington to wit Mary Rooke aged about 43 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to John Rook at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about 20 years ago that her Husband lived with Mr Turning of Devereux Court as a Porter and rented a House in Essex Street at £30 Pounds a year and kept same for five years That after her Husband died she kept it 12 Months That she went to Islington and lodged there about a year then went to Needle Work then went to live with Mr. Meriton the Coal Merchant at Islington in the Parish of Saint Mary at Islington in the Co. of Middx as Ladys Maid They agreed to make it as good as Eight Pounds a year to her she stayed there about 15 Months and she received to the Amount of Eight Pounds a year that since she quitted said Service she has not rented Ten Pounds a year lived Twelve Months in Service done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 7th day of June 1785  Sampn Wright  Addington

Mary Rooke
