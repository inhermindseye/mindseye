Westmr.  Solomon Morris upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a house of the Yearly Rent of £17 in Westmr. Market in the Parish of St. Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr . for three Years & upwards & quitted the said house about 9 Yrs. ago; since which he has not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 14th 1747 before} Tho Lane .

Solomon Morris
