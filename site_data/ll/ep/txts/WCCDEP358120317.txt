Middx  Eliz Croaney aged abt. 37 Yrs. upon Oath saith she was married to Fras. Croaney at the Fleet London abt. 11 Years ago that her sd. husband is an Irishman & never gained a Settlemt. in England to this Examints. knowledge or beleif and that before her sd. Intermarriage and whilst a Singleman she lived a yearly Servant with Mr. John Melcher-at Melcher Coffee house in Friday Street in the Parish of London for Seven years at the yearly wages of £5 and that she has not since She quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds by the year pd parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn March 28th. 1755 before} Barth: Hammond

Eliz Croaney
