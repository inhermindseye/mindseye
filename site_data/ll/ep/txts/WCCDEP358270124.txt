Before Birth 

2d. time of this Woman's being with Child

St Clement Danes 29th July 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnancy  Reputed Father (to wit) Elizabeth Fynmore  singlewoman aged thirty three years & upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Robert Pemberton Esquire Cooks Court Serle Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty for two years and upwards at the annual Wages of Nine Guineas and hath not obtained any subsequent legal settlement And this Examinant on her voluntary Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is  or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards to become chargeable to the said parish of St Clement Danes and that Thos Masters who,  as this Deponent believes, lives as an hired yearly Servant Footman with Mr Graham in Holborn Row  Lincolns Fields is the only Real true father of the said Child or Children wherewith she is so pregnant as aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body sometime on or about the fifteenth day of April last  and begotten the same thereon And that he and no one else is the real true father thereof

Sworn the 29th day of July 1793 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Elizabeth Fynmore

Mrs Fynmore lives at Mr Moody's No 45 Glass House yard Black frairs Master lives with Mr Jas Graham  Lincolns Inn Fields was taken but nothing done the Woman was afterwards Removed to St Ann Blackfrairs London and died there abt. June or July 1794

The Daughter Sarah Fynmore removed from Christ church London 15 April 1809 Book B P.S.
