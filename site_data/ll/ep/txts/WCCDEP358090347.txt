Middx to wit Saml Dew Aged about 33 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his Wife  Martha Dew at the Parish Church of St. Andrew Holbourn about 10 years ago and by whom he hath 4 Childn. now living Namely Ann Aged upwards of 8 year Saml . Aged about 7 years Martha Aged about 2 years and another Child not yet Christened Aged about 16 Days Says before his Intermarrd. to his sd. Wife and whilst he was Singleman he served seven years Apprenticeship with One Saml. Foskett a Leather Dresser the latter Part of who sd. Apprenticeship he served his sd. Master in Bell Sava Yard in the Parish of St. Bride otherwise Bridgett London and after he was out of his sd. Apprenticeship he never gained any other Subsequent Settlemt. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10 a year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 26th. day of Novr. 1782 before M Addington Wm Kitchiner

Saml. Dew
