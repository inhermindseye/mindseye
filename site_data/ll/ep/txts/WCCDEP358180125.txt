Middx  Eliza. Cousins aged 56 Years upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido. of John Cousins  Deceased that her sd. Husbd.  John Cousins lived in & rented a house in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £13. for 2 Years & upwds of abt. 16 Years since & after the death of her sd. Husbd. she lived in & rented a house in Ship Yard aforesd. at the Yearly rent of £24. for the Space of one Year & hath left the same abt. 12 Yrs. since which time this Examint. since his Death hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 5th. Novr. 1743 before} Wm Jones

Eliz Cousins
