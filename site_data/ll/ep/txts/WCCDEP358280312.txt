St Clement Danes

Sarah Gray  Singlewoman Aged abt. twenty three Years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as a Hired Yearly Servant to with Mr. Jackson Brass founder Hemlock Court in the parish of Saint Clement Dane in the County of Middlesex for upwards of two Years at the Yearly Wages of £5 which Service she quitted abt. April last, and that she has since never done any Act whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or belief she might have gained any other Settlement And this Examinant upon her Oath further saith that she is now pregnant with a Child or Children likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be to be Chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes and that Robert Crawford  Journeyman to Mr Hoster Cheesemonger and Tallow Chandler  Fetter Lane & no one else is the real and true father of such Child or Children where with she is so pregnant he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same thereon

Sworn the 1st. day of Febry. 1791 before Me } Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Gray

Crawford taken and paid £10:0:0. Rel. Exted.
