Middx The Examination of Hanh. Stacey  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 26th. Day of April 1739 Who upon her Oath saith That upwards of Ten Years ago she was hired to & lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Lewen who Keeps a China Shop in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the same County for the space of Nine Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds. And this Examint. further saith yt. she hath not since she left the sd. Place Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes Lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife & that she is now Sick & Poor & not able to maintaine herself

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Samuel Clarke  Jas: Gronous

Hannah Stacey
