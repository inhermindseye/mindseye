Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Elizabeth Sawyer on the behalf of Elizabeth Constantia Sawyer her Child Aged about 13 Months upon her Oath Saith that the said Elizth. Conta. Sawy her Child was born at the House of a Mr. Richardson Situate on the Road Side near the Elephant & Castle in the parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex that she is the Illegit Child of this Examinant she this Examinant having never been Married And this Examinant further Saith that the said Child is now at nurse with Mr. Wood 2:9 Strand Lane in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex - And she this Examinant in Poor and unable to maintain her said Child

Sworn this 18th. day of Octr. 1791 before

Addington J Townall

Elizabeth her Mark Sawyer
