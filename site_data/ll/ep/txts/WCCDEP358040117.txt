Westmr.  Rachell Monk Wife of Richard Monk (gone from her) upon Oath says she intermarried with the sd. Richard Monk upwds. of 11 Years since at a house in Blue Ball Court in the Mint Southwark by whom she has two Children viz John aged abt. five Years & Mary aged about Nine Months Says her sd. husband about 14 Yrs. ago and whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Jay an Apothecary 3 Doors on this Side of Temple Barr in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds or thereabts. for one Year and upwards and has not since he left the sd. Mr. Jays Service rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn Decr. 9th. 1747 before} Wm. Jones

The Mark of Rachell R Monk
