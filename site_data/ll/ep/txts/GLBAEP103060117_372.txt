Middx ss  Sarah Raven  Widow of James Raven Deced maketh oath That her said husband in life time has often times informed her this Deponent that he was bound apprentice to Hamlett Toon in the parish of St. Faith in the City of London  Butcher for Seven Years and Served him as his apprentice during the sd. Term And that her said husband never rented a house of Ten pounds by the Year nor never Served any parish offices

Sworn the Tenth Day of June 1749 before  Richd: Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

Sarah Raven
