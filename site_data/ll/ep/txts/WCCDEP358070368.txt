Middx to wit Ann Jones Aged about Twenty Eight Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she now lives and has so Done for upwards of Two Years and an half as a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Brigstock and Attorney in Serle Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster at the Yearly Wages of Nine Guineas And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable wholly to maintain the same when born Says that one John Culverwell a Servant to One one Mrs. Tindall at Stoney Hill Bristol is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body the latter End of Septr. or the beginning of October 1775 but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at no other time afterwards at which time he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said John Culverwell and no offer person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 4th. day of May 1775 before}
