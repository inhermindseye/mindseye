Middlesex ss}  Anna Maria Peele  widow maketh oath that she is well Acquainted with one Mary Hays a Singlewoman now resident in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex who is now Sick & Cant attend to be examined with respect to her place of Settlement And this Deponent Saith that sometime ago the said Mary Hays was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at certain yearly Wages by & with one Mr Colepepper in Saint Mary the Parish of Saint Helen near Bishopsgate in the City of London & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Two years & upwards & by means thereof as this Depont is advised She the said Mary Hays gained a legal Settlement there and this Depont Saith that to her knowledge or belief the said Mary Hays hath not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 13th day of April 1774 before B Camper Jno Sherwood

Anna Maria Peele
