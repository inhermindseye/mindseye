Westmr to wit Ursula Barrett Aged about Nineteen Years on her Oath says she never was Married, Rented House or ever served any Apprenticeship, says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Dowdall An Attorney in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the liberty of Westminster upwards of one Year and an half at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath Quitted her said Service about Ten Months ago since which time she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 7th day of Feby 1775 before R Butler

Ursula Barrat
