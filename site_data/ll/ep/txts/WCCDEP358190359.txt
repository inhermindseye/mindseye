Middx  Jane Harmoney Aged about 21 Years Upon Oath Says she is a Singlewoman Says she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mr Ingall a Watchmaker who Lodged at Mr Woodcocks in Fleet Street in the Parish of St. Bridgets Vulgerly St. Brides for One Year at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds Ten Shillings and hath quitted her said Service upwards of Four Years and hath not Since Rented house Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn the 9th Day of June 1763 before} Barth: Hammond Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jane Harmoney
