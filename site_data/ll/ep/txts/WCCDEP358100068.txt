Middx & Westmr.}  Thos. Clarke Aged about 38 Years on his Oath says he never was Married Rented House or pd. Taxes Says he was bounds out an Apprentice and served upwards of five Years the first Part of his Apprenticeship with one Edwd. Bailey a Stocking frame Knitter in Swan Yard in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . And his Master Using this Examinant very Ill was by Order of Two Justices discharged from his said Masters Service after Which he never gained any other Settlemt. either by Renting House or Tenemt. of £10. a year Paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn this 28th. day of May 1783 J Leroux Wm Kitchiner

Thos Clark
