Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Catherine Doe of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County  Widow taken on oath before me John Sherwood  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this 15th. day of October 1773

Who on her oath Saith That she is now with Child and that the said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate and that Davis Williamson late of the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate  Mariner is the Father of the said Child.

Sworn this 15th day of October 1773 before  Jno Sherwood

The Mark of Catherine Doe
