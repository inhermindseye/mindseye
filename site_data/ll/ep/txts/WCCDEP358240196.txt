St Clement Danes 26th. Janry. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Parish of Stow Gloucestershire  (to wit) Thomas Dennis aged 80 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife Martha formerly Martha Froggatt Widow about twelve years ago (by Banns) at the parish Church of Saint Clement Danes that previous thereto and while he was single and unmarried he lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Thomas Pitman at the White Hart in Stow Gloucestershire for about three years at the Annual Wages of 30s in the capacity of Ostler and branded and lodged in his said Master's House that he quitted that Service about several years ago and hath not Since obtained any subsequent legal to the best of this Examinants knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 26th day of January 1793 before Me } D Walker

Thos Dennis 's Mark
