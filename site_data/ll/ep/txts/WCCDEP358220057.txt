Middx to wit Elizabeth Heaton Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of David Heaton who has been dead about Three Months with whom she Intermarried at Ferns in French Flanders in the Month of April 1768 Says that her Husband was Born in the County of York but what Parish or place she cannot tell nor can give no Account of his Settlement says that before she Intermarried with the said David Heaton and whist she was a singlewoman she lived a Hired Yearly Servant Mrs. Brown a Glazier in St. Clements Church Yard in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the said County of Middlesex for Thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Two Years ago and hath not since rented house paid taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 11th. day of August 1769 before me} Thos Kynaston

The Mark of Elizth. Heaton

Witness Petr Sidebotham
