Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman one of the Beadles of the parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Jane Marks who is ill Maketh Oath and saith that he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Jane Marks Rented a House in Bury Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . for Seven years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Thirty Six Pounds that she hath quitted said House about Twelve years since which time she hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of June 1796 R. Ford J Robinson

B Newman
