Middlesex and Westminster}  T to wit Edward Steers Aged about 74 years on his Oath says he lived as hired yearly Servant with Sir. William Bunbury in upper Grosvenor Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex  about for two years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of £16 hath quitted his said Service about 18 years ago since which time he has gained no other Settlement than as aforesaid

Sworn this 14th. day of Decr. 1784 R. Butler J Leroux

his Edwd. Steers Mark

Appeared from St. Geotlanr. Sqr.
