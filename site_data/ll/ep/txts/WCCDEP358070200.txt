Westmr. to wit Sarah Pimm Aged about forty five Years on her Oath Says she Intermarried with John Pimm at the Parish Church of Saint Nicholas in Nottingham in the Year 1750 And by whom she hath Two Children now living both of whom are now provided for Says her said Husband Rented and lived a House in Sheffield Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of Three Years at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds hath quitted the said House about fourteen Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlemt. And Further says she is now Poor and unable to maintain herself without releif.

Sworn this 28th. day of July 1774 before R. Butler

Sarah Pimm
