Middlesex ss}  Adam Beales Father of the above named Examinant Alice Storey maketh oath that about four years and two Months ago his Son in Law Thomas Storey  & husband of his Said Daughter Alice Storey left this Deponts Said Daughter & went from London to reside at kingston in Jamaica That he this Depont hath never Since Seen him nor doth he know or believe nor hath he ever been informed Whether he the Said Thomas Storey hath ever been in any part of Great Britain or Ireland Since his departure from London as aforesaid nor doth this Depont know whether he the Said Thomas Storey is now living or dead

Sworn this 7th day of January 1777 before} Parkr Rowlands  Jasper Clarkes

Adam Beales
