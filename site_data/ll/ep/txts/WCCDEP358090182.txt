Middx  Elizabeth Mary Polly Aged about fifty Seven Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Thornhill a Breechesmaker in Shire Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for fourteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Eighteen Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Decr. 1780 before R Butler Abrm Baley

her Mary Polly Mark
