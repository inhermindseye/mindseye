Middx  Ann Watkins Aged about 25 Years on behalf of her Sister  Rebecca Wackett Aged about 24 Years Upon Oath says that her said Sister Rebecca Wachett is a Singlewoman and was Never Married and that she Lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mrs Atkinson at the Sign of the Plow in Kingsgate Street in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx for One Year at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds & hath quitted her said Service upwards of Six Years and hath not since Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year

Sworn ye 26th Day of March 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

Ann Watkins
