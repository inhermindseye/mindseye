Middx  James Mason Aged about Forty Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with Izabella his Wife in the Parish of Gray Fryers in the City of Edingborough about Eleven Years since has five Children now Living Peter Aged Ten Years Izabella Six Years Mary Five Years Wm four Years and Charlotte Aged about Nine Months Says in the Year 1760 he Rented a House near the East End of the New Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds Lived therein about Nine or Ten Weeks & hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes.

Sworn the 10th of Novr 1764 Before} Benjamin Cox

James Mason
