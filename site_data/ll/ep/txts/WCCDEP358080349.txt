Middx  Jane Flora aged about Seventeen Years on her Oath says she is Native of Calcutta in the East Indias and never gained a Settlement in England to her knowledge or Belief And further on her Oath says that on the 12th. day of March last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which has since been baptized by the Name of Mary and is now Chargeable to the said Parish And that one John Hutchinson late Servant to Captain Smith of the Horton East India Man did beget such Bastard Child or Children on her Body on Board the said Ship and that the said John Hutchinson and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof

Sworn this 19th. day of April 1779 before}

her Jane Flora Mark
