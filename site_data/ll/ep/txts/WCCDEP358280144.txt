Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Pettit aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Burchells in Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted said Service about 6 years ago since which time She hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Richard Norton a Master Baker in White Chaple is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of June last past and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said Richard Norton is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 24th. day of Janry. 1788 } Wm Kitchiner

Elizabeth Peetitt

Elizth. Pettit lodges with Mrs. Reeves No. 1 Old Street Square

Richard Norton is a Middle Sized Man dark Complexion wears his Hair hanging in Curls in his Neck a Prisoner in the Kings Bench
