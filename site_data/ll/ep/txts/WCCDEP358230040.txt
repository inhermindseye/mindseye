Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Brown aged about 37 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Punten in Stanhope Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Six Years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Five Years since which Time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve months rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of February 1799 before us} Saml. Edwards

her Jane Brown Mark
