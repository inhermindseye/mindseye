Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Battison aged about 20 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a Settlement in her own Right that she was born in Clements Lane that her father William Battison Rented a house several years in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes And this Deponent further upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one William Smith who lives at Johnstons Sunday Monitor Office on Ludgate hill is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said William Smith is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 25th. day of Janry. 1798 before me } J Hunter

her Ann Battison Mark

Witness B Newman
