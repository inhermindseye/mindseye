Middx  Hannah Griffin Aged abt. nineteen Years upon Oath saith She was never Married, that She lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Eady at ye. Wheat Sheaf the Corner of Hosier Lane in ye. Parish of St. Sepulchre's London for two Years & upwards at ye. Yearly Wages of four Pounds quitted said Service abt. three Years; hath not since She quitted said Service rented Ten Pounds a Year, Paid Parish Taxes, or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year Says She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the twenty Eighth Day of May 1755. in the House of Catharine Melampey in Gold Smiths Alley in Lukenors Lane in ye. Parish of St. Giles in ye. Fields. which hath since been Baptized Francis.

Sworn the 1st. day of May 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Hannah Griffen
