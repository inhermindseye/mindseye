Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Jane Watson of the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me Burford Camper Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the Said County this 9th Day of March 1773

Who on her oath Saith that on the fifth day of May one thousand Seven hundred and seventy two She was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Swan Alley in the Said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate since Baptized by the name of Isaac Riddle And that the Said Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish And that Richard Riddle of the Same Parish Peruke Maker did get her with Child of the Said Bastard Child

Sworn this 9th. day of March 1773 before B Camper

The Mark of Jane Watson
