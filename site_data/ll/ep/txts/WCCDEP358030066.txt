Middx  Eliz Jones aged about 22 Yrs. upon her Oath saith she was never married Says she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Thos. James  Wine merchant in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of £3..10..0 for the Space of four Years & upwards & hath left her sd. Service about 3 Yrs. Says she hath not since rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 13th. Decr. 1745 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliz: Jones
