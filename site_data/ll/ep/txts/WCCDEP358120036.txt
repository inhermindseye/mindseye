Middx  Ann Dodd aged 21 yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Ogilby in Warwick Street in the Parish of St James in the Liberty of Westmr. at the yearly wages Four pounds for two Years or thereabouts quitted her sd. Service about two years ago & has not since rented a ten Pounds a year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn 7th Janry 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Dodd
