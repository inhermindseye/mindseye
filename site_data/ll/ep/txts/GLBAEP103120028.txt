Middlesex ss}  Eleanor Mc Donald the widow of Wm. Mc Donald deced maketh oath That her Said late husband has informed her that he was by Birth an Irishman That to this Deponts knowledge or belief he never rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in England That She this Deponent was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor to her knowledge or belief ever did any Act to gain a settlement for herself Save what she might Acquire by her Marriage That she this Depont. Cannot set forth or discover the settlement of her own or her husbands Parents That her Far John Whitham has informed her that she this Deponent was born in Cherry Garden Street in the Parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry & which she believes to be true And that she this Depont hath two children namely Daniel Mc Donald aged about Four years & John McDonald aged about Eleven Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Said late husband to Whom she was married in the Parish church of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middx .

Sworn this 25th Day of January 1773 before  Saml. Spagg B Camper

The Mark of Eleanor McDonald
