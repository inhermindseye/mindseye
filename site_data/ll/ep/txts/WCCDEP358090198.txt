Middx  Ann Donovon Aged about Thirty years on  behalf of Josiah Collins aged about Eleven years On her Oath says that the said Josiah Collins is a Bastard born on the Belly of Mary Collins lately deceased & further says that the said Mary Collins never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly Servant to her knowledge or Belief nor does She know were the said Mary Collins Settlement was says that the said Josiah Collins was born in the House of Mr. Tregedion a Watch Maker in Furnivals Inn Court Holbourn in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn in the County of Middlesex about Eleven Years ago and whose Father was Josiah Hale a Sugar Baker in Pye Corner Smithfield  as she this deponent hath heard him acknowledge and who Died soon after the Birth of the said Josiah Collins

Sworn this 20th. day of February 1781 before  Abrm Baley  Sampn Wright

Ann Donoven
