Middx  Hannah Collins aged abt. 42 yrs. upon Oath says she is the Widow of Luke Collins who hath been dead abt. four years by whom she hath one Child (to wit) Sarah aged abt. six years says her sd. late Husbd. his Apprenticeship was born in the Parish of Naes in the County of Kildare in the Kingdom of Ireland & never Rented Ten Pounds a yr. paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servant

Sworn the 4th. day of April 1757 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Hannah Collins
