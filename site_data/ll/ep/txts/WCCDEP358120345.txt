Middx  Mary Scott upon Oath saith She never was married and that She lived a yearly Servt. with Mr Kilsha at Somerset house  Coffee house in the Strand in the parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . at the yearly wages of £7 for one year within a few Days and that she received the full Sum of £7 for her sd Service which she quitted four or five years ago and has not Since rented ten pounds by the year paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn May 22d. 1755 before us} Benjamin Cox  Jno: Goodchild

The Mark of Mary Scott
