Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Alice Aylsworth alias Pritchard aged about 50 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with John Aylsworth her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Sepulchres about 20 years ago That he has been dead about 4 years that he served his Time as an Apprentice to a Peruke Maker in Aldersgate Street in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldersgate in the County of Middlesex that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 9th. day of May 1788 . J Barnfather Mercer

her Alice Aylsworth alias Pritchard Mark
