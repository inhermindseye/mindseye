Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Elizabeth Browning aged about 30 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Lindsay at the White Hart  in Newport Market in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging or Ten Pounds Pr Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement and further this deponent saith that she is now Chargable to the Parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn this 3d. day of September 1798 before us  Ws Goodenough W Carter

her Elizh Browning Mark
