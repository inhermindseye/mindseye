Middx  Jane Prague aged about 25 Years upon Oath saith she never was Married nor bound an Apprentice That she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Willoughby a Tayler in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for about three Years at the Yearly wages of Fifty shillings Quitted the Same about Two Years and hath not since rented any House or been a hired Servant for One Year

Sworn the 3d. day of September 1761 before me} Barth: Hammond

Jane Praage
