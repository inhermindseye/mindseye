Middx  The Examination of Mary Andrewes  Widow Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 7th. Day of Octr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that She this Examinant not having any Child or Children was hired to & lived a yearly hired Servt . with one Mr Garner in Dux Court in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn London for the Space of two Years at the yearly Wage of six Pounds & hath left her said Service about three Years and further saith that she has not since she left her said service rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or Beleif.

Sworn the day & yeare above mentioned before} Wm Hayton  Jas: Gronous

Mary Andrews
