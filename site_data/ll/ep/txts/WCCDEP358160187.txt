Middx  The Examination of Eliz Lucas Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the first Day of Novr. 1740 Who upon her Oath Saith She is a Singlewoman & was never marryed & that she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Martin at the Golden Key the Corner of white Hart Yard for the Space of one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds & hath left her sd. Service abt ten Years to her now best Remembrance & further saith that since she left her sd. Service She hath not rented ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & is poor & not able to maintain herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before} Wm Hayton  Jas: Gronous

Elizabeth Lucas
