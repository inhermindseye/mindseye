Middx ss: The Examination of Mary Walker  Single Woman taken upon Oath before us two of his Majestyes Justices of ye Peace for ye County of Middx (one whereof is of ye Quorum) residing in or near to the Limitts of ye Parish of St. Botolph without Oldgate in ye sd County this 28th Day of Febry 1750 .

This Examination upon her Oath Sayth. that about two Years ago Mordecai Tinley who now resides near Drury Lane was a Servts. in Ye House of her Father William Walker in Castle Street near Lester fields where this Examinant then lived And this Examinant saith that that the sayd Mordecai Tinley did there prevail on her to lye with her and had Carnell knowledge of this Examinants body and often afterwards there and at other Places did lye with this Examinant and that this Examinant conceived with Child by him of a Female Bastard Child of which She was delivered at ye House of Mr. George Selway in Queen Street in ye sayd Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate aforersayd

on ye 4th. Day of Janry last which sayd Bastard Child has been now in and Still is likely to become chargeable to ye sayd Pish this Examinant being Poor and not able to provide for ye Same And this Examinant Sayth that ye sayd Mordecai Tinley is the true and only Father of ye sayd Female Bastard Child And that this Examinant is Voluntary and that she hath not been forced or compelled to be examined by any Pson or Persons whomsoever

Sworn this 28th. 2nd. Day of Febry March 1750 before us  Richd: Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

Mary Walker
