St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster to wit }  Alexander Scott Aged 29 years or there abouts on behalf of Mary Ann & Charles Scott the two Infant Children Mentioned in the next preceding Examinant) Upon his Oath saith that John Scott his Brother the Father of the said Children serve was bound served as an Apprentice with his own Father John Scott in the Canningate in the parish of canningate in the Suburds of the City of Edingburgh in Scotland and since hath not to this Deponents Information knowledge or belief done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have Acquired any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn the 29th: June 1791 before one D Walker

A Scott
