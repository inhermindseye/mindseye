Middx  Geo. Cooper on behalf of Jno. Pink a Lunatic upon Oath saith the sd. Jno. Pink is the Son of Thomas Pink by Ann his Wife & that the sd. Thomas Pink hath Rented a house for a considerable time in the parish of St Thomas in Salisbury in the County of Wilts and pd. Scot & Lot & still continues to live in and rent a house in the same parish and that John Pink (the Son) never did any Act whereby to gain a Settlement elsewhere to this Examinants knowledge

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned, before  Fran: Bedwell

George Cooper
