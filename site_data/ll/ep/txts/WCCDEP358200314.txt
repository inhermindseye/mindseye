Westmr.  Mary Jenkins aged about 40 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Henry Jenkins gone from her with whom she intermarried in the Chapel at Kentish Town in the Parish of St. Pancras in Middlesex about fifteen Years ago; says her said husband Rented a house in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds & eleven Shillings lived therein about one Year & hath quitted same twelve Years or thereabouts and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 7th. Novr. 1765 before} Thos: Balack  Frans: Caryt

Mary Jenkins
