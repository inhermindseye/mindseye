Middlesex ss}  Ann Bolton  Singlewoman maketh oath that she has been informed by her Grandmother Bridged Sargeant & which she believes to be true that her late Father John Bolton deced heretofore rented a house in Baileys Place in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates & Kings Taxes for the same & died there legally Settled in the sd Parish That she this Depont in the Year 1774 was bound an Apprentice to Messieurs Ann & Sarah Cuff of Cartwright Street Rosemary Lane in the sd Parish Mantua makers for three years but for West of the Stamp Duty being Paid for the Consn money mentioned in the sd Indre this Deponent is advised & believes that such Indre & Apprenticeship are a mere Nullity & wholy void That she this Depont was never Married nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right save as aforesaid.

Sworn this. 11.day of July 1783 before R Davies

The Mark of Ann Bolton

To her Fathers Settlemt. by Renting a House & paying Poors Rate

She being Bound Apprentice & no Duty paid for the Cons Money
