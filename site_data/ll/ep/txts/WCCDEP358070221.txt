Middlesex & Westmr.}  Esther Robertson Aged upwards of Twenty Year on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Rented and lived in a House in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes at the Yearly Rent of Twenty four pounds hath quitted the said House upwards of fifteen Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any or Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become chargable to the said Parish she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one Thos. Mason a Journeyman Taylor  Foreman to Mr. Bass a Taylor in Pickering Court St. James's Street and who now uses the Kings Arms in Houghton Street is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body on Wednesday the 27th. of July last at the one Bill Inn in the Strand and at sevl. Different times afterwards at different Places at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which he is now pregnant and that the said Thos. Mason and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 7th. day of Novr. 1774 before} 

Esther Robertson
