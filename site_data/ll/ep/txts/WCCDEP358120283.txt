Middx  Mary Jones aged abt. 22 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she was bound apprentice by the Churchwds. & Overs. of St. Bennet Pauls wharf to Rachel Groves till she shd. attain the age of 21 Years, that after having served the sd. Mrs. Groves abt. 8 Years she was assigned over to Mary Lewsmore with whom she lived in Sheer Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes abt 3 months after which Mrs. Lewsmore having no further Occasion for her Service got her a plate wth. Mrs. Horne in Essex Strt. in the sd. Parish with whom she lived abt. 7 months and after this the sd Mrs. Lewsmore never concerned herself any further with this Examt. but left her to Provide for herself in what manner she thought Proper and this Ext. further saith she never Rented ten pounds by the Year pd. Parish taxes nor lived a yearly servt. for one year

Sworn Feby. 28th 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Jones
