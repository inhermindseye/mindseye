Middlesex ss}  John Brown maketh oath that divers years ago he was duly bound Apprentice to John Whitaker a Gardiner in the Parish of Watonbury near maidstone in the County of Kent & served him there for the Space of five years & then he left his Master & went to sea & did not afterwards return back to him and has not since done any Act to gain a settlement elsewhere And that he has a wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this. 6: day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of John Brown
