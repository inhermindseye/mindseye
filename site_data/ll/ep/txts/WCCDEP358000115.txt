Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster} 

passd. to Allhallows to wit Elizabeth Wells aged 39 years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant or ever was bound an Apprentice says that her Father served his Apprenticeship with a Farrier the Corner of Dowgate Hill in the Parish of Allhallows the Great in the City of London and that she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 26th- day of Septr. 1785  John Croke Mr Barnfather

the mark of Elizabeth Wells
