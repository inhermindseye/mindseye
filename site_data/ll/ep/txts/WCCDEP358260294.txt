Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Frances Porter aged about 51 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bold in Essex Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Three years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Five years since which time She hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2nd. day of July 1798 before me }

her Frances Porter mark
