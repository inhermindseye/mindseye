Middx & Westminster}  Jane Bardin Aged about 50 years on the behalf of her Daughter Ann Bardin aged about 27 years upon her Oath Saith That she Intermarried with Thomas Bardin her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London about Thirty years ago That she hath Eight Children Living by her said Husband (four of whom are now provided for) And the others now Residing with her Vizt. the said Ann Bardin Aged about 27 years Richard Aged about 19 years  John Aged upwards of 15 years and Charlotte Aged about 10 years Saith That she hath been Informed by her said Husband and which Information she verily believes to be true That her said Husbands Father served the first Four years of his time under Indentures of Apprenticeship duly Executed with a

a Glass Grinder near Puddle Dock in the Parish of Saint Andrew by the Wardrobe in the City of London That her said Father Husband Father never was assigned over to any person whatsoever for the remainder of  time now ever Rented any House or Lodging of £10 a year paid any Taxes Lived as an Hired yearly Servant

or ever did any other Act Matter or thing whereby he have gained any other Settlement as her said Husband Father was Informed by his said Father And this Examinant further on her Oath saith that her said Husband has Informed her That he  never did any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any Settlement in his own Right And further saith That her said Daughter Ann Bartin hath Lived with her Father ever since her Birth  and never gained any Settlement in her own Right and  this Examinant further saith That the and her said Husband together  with their Eight Children have been Relieved by the said Parish of Saint Andrew by the Wardrobe And that her said Daughter Ann Bardin being more Insane her said Husband is unable to Support her

Sworn this 12th. April 1786 before us R Butler Wm. Hitchiner

J Bardin
