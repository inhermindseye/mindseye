Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  St. George Hanh. Square  to Wit Sarah Haley Aged 38 years and upwards Upon her Oath Saith that she never was Married that about 17 Years Ago she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Schooler in New Bond in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Street No:127 for one Year and 7 Months that since she quitted said Service she never Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year lived as a hired Yearly Servant for One Year any where paid Taxes or done any other Matter or thing whereby she Might have gained any Subsequent Settlement to the best of he Knowledge information or beleif

Sworn this 21st. Octr. 1791 before. R Butler D Walker

Sarah her Mark Haley
