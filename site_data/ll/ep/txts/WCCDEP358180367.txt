Middx  Jane Watkins aged about 23 Years upon her Oath saith she was never married and that she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Griffith a Cheesemonger in Middle Row in the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £4 for the Space of one Year & upwards & hath left her sd. Service about four Years & hath not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the 27th. Day of March last in the house of Mrs. Stoakes in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the sd. County of Middx which hath since been Baptized James & is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same & that James Burn now a Servt. to Mr. Mc. Daniel Innkeeper at Sydenham did begett the sd. Male Bastard Child on her Body this Examinant at

such time liveing a fellow Servt. with the sd. James Burn at Mr. Mc.Daniels aforesd. where he became acquainted with her & had carnall knowledge of her Body in the latter End of the Month of May last was twelve months & at sevll. other times since at one of which times hegott her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & that he the sd. James Bunn & no other Person is the true & real Father thereof

Sworne Augt. 13th. 1745 before  Thos. D Neil  Wm. Jones

The Mark of Jane Watkins
