Middx  Eliz Povey aged 67 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that she served five years apprentiship with one Mrs. Archer an Embroiderers  and during the last Six Years of the sd. term lodged in her Mistresses house in Old Boswell Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and that she never Rented ten pounds by the year paid parish Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant  for one year

Sworn Janry 7th 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Elizbeth Povey
