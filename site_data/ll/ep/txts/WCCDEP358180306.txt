Middx  Wm. Chimmow of the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex on the Behalfe of Mary Merry aged abt. Eleven Yrs. Hannah Merry aged abt. Six Years & Elizabeth Merry aged about Eleven Months the Children of Thomas Merry by Mary - his Wife  deceased upon his Oath saith that he hath made Enquiry after the Settlement of the said Thos. Merry & hath been Informed & verily beleives that the said Thomas Merry upwards of Twelve Months since Rented a house in Fee Lane in the Liberty of Saffron hill Hatton Garden & Ely Rents in the County of Middx aforesaid of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds and lived therein for the Space of two Months Says he hath also been Informed that immediately after the said Thomas Merry left the said house in Fee Lane he rented a Lodging in St Clements Lane in the said Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & abt. Six Months after he had lived in the sd. Lodging went to Worcester & left the sd. Children behind him & was there Arrested for Debt & is now in Worcester Goal

Sworne 18th. March 1744 before} Tho Lane .

Wm. Chimmow
