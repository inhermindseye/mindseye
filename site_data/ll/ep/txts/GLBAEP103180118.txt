Middlesex ss}  William Elliot maketh oath That he was duly bound by Indres & Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with Edward Booth in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Waterman and has not Since rented a house or other Tenement of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Said Apprenticeship

Sworn this 9th day of January 1779 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of William Elliot
