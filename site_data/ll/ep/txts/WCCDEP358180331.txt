Middx  Mary Wilkinson  Wido . upon her Oath saith she Intermarried with her late Husbd. Rd. Wilkinson at the Kings head near Fleet Markett on or abt. the 2d. Day of June 1741 & the sd. Rd. Wilkinson hath been dead abt. 5 Months Says she can give no Acct. of the Settlemt. of the sd. Rd. Wilkinson other than that he was born in St Clemts. Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she hath one Child viz. Richard aged 2 Yrs & upwds.

Sworne May 8th 1745 before} Wm: Jones

The mark of Mary Wilkinson
