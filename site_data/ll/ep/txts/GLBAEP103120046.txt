Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Mills the Wife of  Samuel Mills maketh oath That she well knows a young Woman named Grace Gilbert  the Daughter of John Gilbert  Woolcomber who is now resident in the Isle of Guernsey and which Said Grace Gilbert is at present a Lunatick so far disordered in her Senses that it may be dangerous to permit her to go abroad and is become Chargeable to the parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County of Middlesex and this Depont. Saith that divers years ago the Said John Gilbert lived in & rented a house of the yearly Rent & Value of Ten Pounds in the Parish of the Holy Trinity in the City of Exeter in the County of Devon  & Continued in the occupation of such house for many years and by means thereof as this Depont. is advised he gained a legal settlement there and to this Deponents knowledge or belief he hath not since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement That the Said Grace Gilbert  at such time was part of her Said Fathers Family and that to this Deponts. knowledge or belief the Said Grace Gilbert never did any Act to gain a settlement in her own right

Sworn this 13th. day of May 1773 before  Burf Camper  Jno Sherwood

Eliz: Mills
