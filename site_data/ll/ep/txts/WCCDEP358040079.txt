Westmr.  Eliz: Carey upon Oath says she never was married Says on the fifth Day of September last she was delivered of a Stilborn Child in the house of Mr. Alexander in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Says she being Poor & not able to pay for her Months Lyeing in the same was paid by the Overseers of the Poor of the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that one Mr. Mackfall a Hair Merchant now Lodging at Mr. Charltons a Shoemaker in Long Acre did beget the sd. Child on her Body the said Mr. Mackfall having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body on the first Day of January last and on the 20th. Day of the same January in the sd. Mr. Charltons house at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Stilborn Child she was Delivered of as aforesd.

Sworn Octr. 13th. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Eliza; Carey
