Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Ann Ragan aged about 34 years upon her Oath saith that about 15 years she Intermarried with John Ragan her late Husband (who has been dead about 9 years) at the Parish Church of Christ Church Dublin according to the Rites and Ceremonies of the Church of England that after her said Husbands Death and having no Child or Children living she went to live as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Gamonon a Taylor in Denzell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and continued to live with him for 18 Months at the yearly Wages of £5 that she hath quitted said Service about 4 years ago since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or lived as a hired yearly done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 17th. day of Septr.1787 R Butler

her Ann Ragan mark
