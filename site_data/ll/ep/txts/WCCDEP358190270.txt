Middx  Ann Ingram  Wife of Giles Ingram a Lunatic upon Oath saith She Intermarried with her said Husband at the Fleet London on the Thirtieth day of March One Thousand Seven hundred and forty eight Says that her said Husband whilst a Singleman lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Durand a Pewterer in St. Martins Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster two Years or thereabouts as She hath been informed by her said Husband which information She verily believes to be true and Says that he hath not since Rented a House or paid any Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant in one Place for a Year as She verily believes Says She hath one Child by her said Husband named Giles aged upwards of Seven Years

Sworn August 31st. 1762 before} Barth: Hammond  Paul Vaillant

The mark of Ann Ingram
