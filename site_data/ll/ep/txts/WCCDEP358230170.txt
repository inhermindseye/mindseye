Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Madden aged about 40 years upon her Oath Saith That about six years ago she intermarried with Dennis her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Anns Westminster by whom she hath living two Children Vizt Catherine aged three years and Eleanor aged 18 Months That her said Husband was born in Ireland and hath done no Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement in England That which Single she lived as a hired yearly Servant for 3 years with Mr Watch horn in Brownlow Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds That she left the said Service about three Months before her Marriage after which time she did no act Matter or thing whereby she may have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 31st day of Decr 1800 before us } Saml. Edwards  John Collick

Ann Madden her mark

Witness J. Starling
