Middex 

Saml. Nibbs upon Oath saith he lived in & rented the Green Dragon Alehouse in Milford Lane in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex at the yearly Rent of £28 for two years & upwards & hath left same about 17 years Says he has not since rented ten pounds Year paid Parish Taxes lived a yearly Servant for one year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 14th. 1746 before}
