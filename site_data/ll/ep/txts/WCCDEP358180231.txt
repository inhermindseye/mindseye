Middx  Mary Mash  Wido . aged about 70 Yrs. Removed from the Parish of St. Margt. Westmr. by Order Dated 31st: Augt. last upon her Oath saith that she lived in & rented a house in St Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £15. for the Space of 3 Yrs. or thereabts. & hath left the same about 2 Yrs. since which time she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 10th: Septr: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Mary Mash
