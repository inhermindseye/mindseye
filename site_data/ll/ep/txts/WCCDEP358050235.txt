Westmr 

Joseph Lathbury upon Oath Saith he Intermarried with his wife Margt. in the parish Church of St Johns in Marshland in the County of Norfolk abt. 15 Yrs. ago Says he was bound Apprentice to and Served 7 Years Apprentiship with Mr. Thos. Ford Smith in May pole Alley in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 6th. 1750 before}
