Middx  Ann Martin removed from the Parish of St. Andrew in Holbourn above the Banes by Order dated the 1st. Day of March Instant upon her Oath saith she is the Wido. of Wm. Martin who hath been dead about 4 Yrs. Says she intermarried with her sd. late Husband abt. 30 Yrs. since at the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn aforesaid Says the sd. Wm. Martin Served Seven years Apprentiship  with Mr. Pritchard a Bedstead maker in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes  in the County of Middlesex Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place Says she hath not since her sd. late Husbands Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she was removed from the sd. Parish of St. Andrew in Holborn to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt Danes by Order dated on or about the 11th. Day of Febry 1741 which sd. Order was not appealed agt.

March 4th. 1745 Sworn before}
