Westmr.  Waybro Bradford aged about 60 Years Upon Oath says She never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr, Wm. Davis a Lapidary in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds ten Shillings quitted said Service about fourteen Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 20th. Novr. 1767 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Waybro Bradford
