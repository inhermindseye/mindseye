Middlesex  Mary Minors aged about 40 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Edward Minors (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about fifteen Years ago by whom she hath four Children now with her (to wit) Thomas aged about Ten Years James aged about five Years Ann aged about Two Years and Edward aged about Six Weeks, says her said Husband informed her and which information she verily believes to be true that he never Rented Ten pounds by the Year nor paid Taxes nor was an Apprentice or a Yearly hired Servant for one Year says her said husband has also informed her that he was born near the Market House in the Parish of Campden near Broadway in the County of Gloucester where his Father Rented a house and carried on the Business of a Perukemaker for upwards of Thirty Years

Sworn the 25th. day of June 1765 Before} Edm: Byron  Henry Lambe

Mary Minors
