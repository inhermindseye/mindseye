Middx  Ann Greene aged about 7 Years upon Oath says She was bound Apprentice to Ignatius Plant by the Parish of St. Clement Danes as appears by Indenture dated the 15th. Day of May 1754. Says She served about three Months of her Apprenticeship when the said Ignatius Plant dyed and says She has not since Rented 10 Pounds by the Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant
