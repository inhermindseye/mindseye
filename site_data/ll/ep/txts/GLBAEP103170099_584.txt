Middlesex ss}  Ann Bevan the wife of John Bevan who is absent  maketh oath That her Said husband was by birth an Irishman as he has informed her That this Deponent Can give no Account respecting her said husbands Settlement That She this Deponent before her marriage with her said husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year by & with one Mr Cox a Surgeon & Apothecary in St. Catherines Lane in the parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service for divers years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there & did not afterwards do any Act to gain anothr Settlement Save What She has Acquired by her marriage as afore said That She was lawfully married to her Said husband & hath a child named Ann Bevan Aged about fourteen years the lawful Issue of this Depont by her sd husband

Sworn this 11th day of October 1777 before  Jasper Clarkes

The Mark of Ann Bevan
