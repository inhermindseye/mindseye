Middx  Mary Trow aged abt. Sixty five Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with Robt. Trow her late Husband Deced at the Fleet London abt. Eighteen Years ago Says the said Robt. Trow before his Intermarriage with this Examinant & Whilst a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Maud at the Black Swan Inn Holborn in the Parish of St. Andrew Holborn London upwards of a Year at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds as She hath been informed by her said late Husband & as She verily believes & that he never after Rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year & further says She hath not since the Death of her said late Husband Intermarried with any other Person Rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 28th. Day of August 1756 : before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Trow
