St Clement Danes 14th June 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Son Thos Capper settled in St CD by Appceship }  his Mother's settlemt in St C D}  (to wit) Margaret Capper aged 65 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She was Married to Robert Capper her late Husband at the Parish Church of St Margaret Westminster about thirty years ago that he died about ten years ago the leaving a Son Named Thomas Capper  in then of the Age of about Twenty three years & who had previously acquired a legal Settlement in his own right by serving his Apprenticeship with his Father in the Parish of St Clement Danes that subsequent to her said Husband's Death and being a Widow unmarried and having no other Child than the said Thomas Capper  his then living, she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Pryce Linen Draper in Blackmoor Street in the said Parish of St Clement Danes for a year and Nine Months or thereabouts at the Annual Wages of £8 that she quitted the same in or about the Month of 179 May 1793 and hath not since acquired any other legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief

Sworn the 14th day of June 1794 before me } Wm. Kitchiner

Margt. Capper 's Mark

Witness Jas Talboys
