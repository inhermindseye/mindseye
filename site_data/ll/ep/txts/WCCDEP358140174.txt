Middx  Mark Stanley aged abt. 27 Yrs. upon Oath says he intermarried with Catherine his Wife at the Fleet London abt. Seven Years ago by whom he has three Children Cathne . aged abt. Six Years Ann abt. four Yrs. and George abt. Ten Weeks: says (abt. Nine Years ago he being at that time a Single Man ) he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Copinger an Attorney at Law in Cooks Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. for 2 Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds or thereabouts and after he quitted sd. Service never Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn the 3d. of Novr. 1758 . before me} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

Mark Stanley
