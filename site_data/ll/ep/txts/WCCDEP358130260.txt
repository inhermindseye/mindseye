Middx.  Jonathan Walford upon Oath says he Intermarried with Hannah his Wife at the Fleet London abt. Seventeen Yrs. since by whom he has two Children (to wit) Mary aged Ten Yrs. & upwards & Jonathan aged Eight Yrs. & upwards Says he was Bound Apprentice out of St. Brides School to Michl. Humphrys at the Cherry Tree at Southgate in the County of Middx. Says he served the last Three Years of his Apprentiship with Mr. Jaques at the Castle in Portugal Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx.

Sworn Janry 7th. 1757 before}
