Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Caroline Ferguson Aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith That she never was married That on or about September 1794 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of a Mr Redding in Brick Lane in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Christened by the Name of Catherine

Sworn this10th day of Octr.1800 before us} John Collick  Benjm Gee

Carolina Ferguson
