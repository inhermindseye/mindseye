Aldgate Middx

Jane Thompson  wife of Philip Thompson (now at Sea ) Maketh Oath that her Said Husband Serv'd his Apprenticeship by Indenture to Mr Constable a Lighterman near Dog and Bell Yard in the Parish of Saint John in Southwark in the County of Surry and hath gaind no other Settlement Since And that she hath been relievd by the Officers of the Said Parish

Sworn 23d day of December Anno Dom 1748 Richd. Ricards N. Dukinfield

the mk of Jane Thompson
