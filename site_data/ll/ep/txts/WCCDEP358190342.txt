Middx  Arabella Flynn Aged about 30 Years now Lodging in Nags Head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in ye Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middx Upon Oath says She is a Native of Ireland and never gained any Settlement in England Says she never was Married And Further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is Likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says James Leonard  Labourer did beget the said Child or Children on her Body he the said James Leonard having had Carnal knowledge of her this Examinant in the House of One Mrs. Hamilton in Church Lane in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the said County of Middx in the Month of June Last and at several other times Since they liveing together as

Man and Wife at one of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 28th Day of April 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Arabella Flynn
