Middlesex, to wit, Ann Bullen , Widow , Maketh Oath and Saith, that on or about the Twenty Second Day of May 1788 , she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at the Lying In Hospital in the City Road , since Baptised by the Name of Mary Ann , and that John Radford now or late of the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey , in the County of Surry , apothecary is the Father of the said Bastard Child

Sworn this 22d Day of Feby. 1791 . before me R Smith

Her Ann Bullen Mark
