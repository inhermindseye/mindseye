Middx to wit Wm. West Aged about fifty nine Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at the Parish Church  of St. Clement Danes abt. Twenty Eight years ago & by whom he hath Two Children namely Elizth . Aged about Nineteen Years now provided for & Sarah Aged about 12 Years says before his Intermarriage to his Wife & whilst he was a Single man he lived a hired Yearly Servt . with one Mr. Henley a Coal Merchant in Milford Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes for four years & upwards the first three years at the yearly Wages of three pounds and the last Year at the yearly Wages of five Pounds resides Diet & Lodging hath quitted her said service about Thirty Eight years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. whatever

Sworn this 4th. day of March 1783 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Wm. West  Mark
