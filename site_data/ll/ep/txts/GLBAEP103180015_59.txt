Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Davis the wife of Henry Davis maketh oath that her Said husband is now absent from her at Sea That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was born in St John Square in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the Said County & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right And this Deponent Cannot give any Account respecting the Settlement of her said husbands Parents or either of them save that her Said husband has informed her that his Father carried on the Business or profession of an Apothecary in the said Parish of St James Clerkenwell & that She was Married to her Said husband at the Fleet many years before the late marriage Act took place.

Sworn this 24 day of January 1778 - before P. Rowlands Jasper Clarkey 

The Mark of Elizabeth Davis
