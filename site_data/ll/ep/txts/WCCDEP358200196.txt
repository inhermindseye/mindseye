Westmr.  Henry Prosser Aged about Thirty Six Years upon Oath Says he Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Dr. Neasbet in Kings Street  Cheapside in the Parish of St. Mildred in the Poultrey London three Years at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds he being at that time a Singleman & without Child or Children hath quitted said Service about Ten Years & hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 22d Day of Decr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant  Benjamin Cox

Henry Prosser
