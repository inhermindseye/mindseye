Middx.  Elizth. Hurssey aged abt. forty Nine Years upon Oath Says She never was married & that She lived a hired Yearly Servt. upwards of Two Years with Mr. John Bromley a Fishmonger near Temple  Bar in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the Yearly Wages of £5. & hath quitted her said Service abt. 5 Yrs. since wch. time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn Decr. 23d. 1756 before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Elizth. Hursey
