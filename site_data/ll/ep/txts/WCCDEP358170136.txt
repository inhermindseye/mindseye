Middx  Anne Lindsey aged abt. Twenty Three Years upon her Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that on the 6th- Day of December 1739 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in Little Sheir Lane in the Parish of St- Clemt- Danes in the County of Middx which hath since been baptized James & is now liveing & likely to become Chargeable to the same & that one Savell Peacock a Baker did begett the sd. Child on her Body she at such Time lodging with one Mrs. Hawkins in St. John Street in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middx aforesd. where the sd. Savell Peacock had Carnall Knowledge of her Body in the Month of March 1738/9 and at sevll. other Times since at one of which Times he got her with the sd. Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & Yn. he & no other Person is the True & Reall Father thereof

Sworne the 23d. of Septr. 1741 before G Poulson

Anne Lindsey
