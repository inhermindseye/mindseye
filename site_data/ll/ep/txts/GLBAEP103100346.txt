Middlesex ss}  Christian Astle the widow of James Astle deced maketh oath that divers Years ago her said late husband Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds in the Parish of St. Botolph without aldgate in the County of Middlesex and was Charged to & Paid Parish Taxes for the Same & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish And this Depont. Saith that her said late husband to her knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement and that she was lawfully married to him & hath. not done any Act since his Death to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this day of December 1770 before}
