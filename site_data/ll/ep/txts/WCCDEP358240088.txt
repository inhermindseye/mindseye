St Clement Danes 12th April 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Bride Fleet Street  (to wit) Mary Reed  Widow aged 28 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband Richard Reed at the Parish Church of St. Bridget otherwise St Bride with in the Liberty of the City of London about Fifteen or Sixteen she years ago hath been Informed and believes that Subsequent thereto her said Husband Rented and with this Deponent resided in an House in Goldsmith Street Pemberton Row Gough Square in the parish of St. Bridget otherwise alias St Bride in the Liberty of the City London for about two years and eight Months at the Annual Rent of about Sixteen Guineas and paid all the Taxes for the said House that he quitted the said House about fourteen years ago went immediately to Ireland, and Died about two years after never having obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Deponents knowledge or belief And this Deponent saith that since her said Husband's Decease she has never been Married nor acquired any Settlement in her own Right And this Deponent abt. saith that she about Fourteen years ago she this Deponent was removed by Order of Removal from the parish of St Andrew Holborn  Middlesex to the said parish of St Bridget otherwise St Bride St Bridget ago and that the said last ever which parish now Appealed against such Order as this Deponent best known and believes and that She is now pregnant and not able to support herself

without Assistance

Sworn the 12th April 1792 before Us} Sampn. Wright J Pownall

Mary Reed 's Mark
