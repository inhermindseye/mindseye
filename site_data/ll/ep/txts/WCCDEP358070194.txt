Westmr. to wit Elizth. Henley Aged about Twenty five Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House was an Apprentice or lived a Hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Rented any lived in a House in Justice Walk in the Parish of St. Lukes Chelsea in the County of Middlesex in which Parish this Examinant was born And further says that she was passed from St. Andrew Holbourn upon the said Parish of Saint Lukes Chelsea Afsd. where her said Settlement was found to be upon an Appeal at the Quarter Sessions since which time she has never gained any other Subseqt. Settlmnt.

Sworn this 15th. day of June 1774 before W Kelyngs R. Butler

Eliz Henley
