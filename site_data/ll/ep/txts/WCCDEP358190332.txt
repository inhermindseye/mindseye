Middx  Johanna Wase Aged upwards of Sixty Years Lodging at the Barley Mow in Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the County of Middx Upon Oath Says She rented A House known by the Name of the Sign of the Plow near three Coney Walk in the Parish of Lambeth in the County of Surry of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds and lived therein upwards of One Year and quitted said House about Eight or Ten Years and hath not Since rented any House or lived a hired Yearly Servt. for One Year

Sworn ye 22 day of March 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell  Tho Lane

the Mark of Joanna Wase
