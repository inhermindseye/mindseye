Middx  Rt Hayward aged 77 Years upon Oath Saith he was married to his Wife  Ann Hayward at Grantham in Lincolnshire abt . 33 Yrs. ago.

That upwards of 50 Years (being than a Singleman ) he Covenanted (by Indenture in Writing) to live with Mr John Shaw a Gunsmith for four Years certain at the Wages of two Shillings by the week for the first Year three Shillings by the week the second & third Years & four Shillings by the week during the last Year. besides Lodging & Dyet during the whole term and that he lodged during the sd. four Years in Mr Shaws House at the Horse Guards in the parish of St Margaret in the Liberty of Westmr. That he has not since he quitted the sd. Service rented about of Ten Pounds by the Yr. paid parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one Year

Sworn July 29th 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

Rt Hayward Hayward
