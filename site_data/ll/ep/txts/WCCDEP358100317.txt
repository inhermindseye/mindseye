Middlesex and Westminister}  T  Elizabeth Pain aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith That she never was Married to Benjamin Pain her late Husband at the Parish Church of Lyme Rejis in the County of Dorset about 22 years ago That she hath two Children living by her said Husband Viz Benjamin aged 8 years and James about 6 years and that her said Children is now in the  Nortchoure of St. Clemt. Danes and her said Husband detacted this Life about 14 Months ago That during their Intermarriage her said Husband Rented a House in Browns Buildings in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about 6 Months That he quitted the said House about 2 years ago that since her said Husband or quitted the said House in Brown's Buildings he has not gained any other Settlement she has this Examinant since his Decease gained a Settlemt. either by service, Renting a House or Lodging of £10 a year, or done any other Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information or Belief she may have gained a settlement elsewhere and further upon her Voluntary Oath

Warat. made out but he not taken urcher and him ride water  Etc. saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastard's to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes and further saith That one Thomas Edwards who lately lived near York & Buildings in the strand and who is by trade a Jouney man Taylor in the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body sometime in The Month of March last but the Particular day she does not now remember and at several times afterwards at one of which he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Thomas Edwards and no one else is the father thereof

Sworn this 22d day of Decr. 1784 R Butler

Elizabt. Pain
