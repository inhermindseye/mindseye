Middlesex & Westminster (to wit) pd. to  William Voll aged about thirty eight Years upon his Oath Saith, That he was born in PallMall in the Parish of Saint James Westminster , where his Father rented a House at fifty Pounds a Year That he lived there with his Father, and Mother for twenty three Years, That he never served any Apprenticeship, rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes, lived as an hired Yearly Servant or has done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement to the best of his Knowledge or Belief

Sworn this 11th. Octr. 1784 before Sampr Wright Thos. Gordons

Wm Voll
