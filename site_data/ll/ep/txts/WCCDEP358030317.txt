Westmr. 

Eliz Willings aged upwds. of 17 Years upon Oath saith she was never married Says she knows nothing of the Settlement of her father & mother who are both dead Says she was born in Bannet Street in the Parish of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry & never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif.

Sworn Janry 10th 1746 before  Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Eliz Willings
