Middx  John Jennings aged about Years upon Oath says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Webb at the Fountain in Clare Street one year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds ten Shillings quitted said Service upwards of Twenty Years and hath not since Rented a House-paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the day of Decr. 1762 before}
