Middx  Francis Smith removed from the Parish of St. James Westmr . by Order Dated the 29th Day of October Instt. upon his Oath saith that he is a Singleman & was never married upwards of One Year ago he lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Jno. Clark  Bookseller at the Corner of Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx . at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds Twelve Shillings for the Space of 1 yr. & upwds.. that since he left the sd. Mr. Clarks Service he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or other- wise Gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or Beleife

Sworne the 30th. Octr. 1742 before me}
