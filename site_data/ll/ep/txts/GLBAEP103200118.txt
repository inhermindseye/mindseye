Middlesex ss}  Catherine Murrall  Singlewoman maketh oath that sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by & with Richard Sherlock of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Shoemaker at Certain Wages & Continued in such service under such hiring for the Space of a year & upwards & has not since done any Act to gain a subseqt Settlemt

Sworn this. 17. day of March 1784 before R Davies

Cathrn Murrell
