Middx  Elizabeth Hills aged about Sixty Years Lodging in that part of Bear Yard which is in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Joseph Hills who has been Dead about thirteen years to whom she was Married at the Fleet about 27 Years since That her said Husband was a Cordwainer by Trade and lived in and rented a House in Prescot Street  Goodman's Fields in the Parish of St. Mary White Chapel in the County of Middlesex for the Space of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds besides Taxes Quitted the Same several Years before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and never after rented any House or paid Parish Taxes as he hath informed her and which Information she verily believes to be true That she hath not kept House rented ten pounds a year paid Parochial Taxes or been a hired Yearly Servant Since the Death of her said Husband

Sworn the 16th. day of May 1761 Before} Barth: Hammond  Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Elizabeth Hills
