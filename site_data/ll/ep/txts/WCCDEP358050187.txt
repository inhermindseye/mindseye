Westmr.  Sarah Wood wido. of Thomas Wood who dyed upwds. of two years since upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Thomas Wood at St. James's Church the 17th. May 1741 and that her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with his Father Thos. Wood two Doors above Drum Alley in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Bakers and that neither her sd. late husband nor this examint since his Decease have rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge beleif

Sworn Janry 6th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

Sarah Wood
