Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Gordon aged about 58 years upon her Oath saith that about Ten years ago she Intermarried with William Gordon her late Husband who has been dead about Eight years at the Parish Church of Saint Paul Covent Garden That her said late Husband Rented and lived in a house in Exeter Court in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eight years or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards that after he quitted said House he never did any Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of Novr. 1796 } John Collick

her Ann Gordon Mark
