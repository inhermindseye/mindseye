Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Giles in the fields A to wit Jane Jackson Aged about Fifty Eight years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year, says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Thomas in Great Queen Street  Lincoln Inn Fields in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields for 13 Months & upwards at the yearly Rent of Wages of Six pounds hath quitted said Service upwards of One year since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing Whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 20th day of Decr. 1784 G Mercer Jno. Wright

her Jane Jackson mark
