Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Glynn aged about Thirty years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Bagshaw the corner of Turnstile in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Fifteen Months since which time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement and further this Deponent upon her Oath saith that she is now Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 20th. day of March 1798 before us } N Bond Rupt. Clarke

her Elizabeth Glynn mark

Witness B. Newman
