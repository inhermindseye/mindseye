Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Talboys one of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Crizland an Infant aged about Five years Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Mary Crizland is the legitimate Child of John and Elizabeth Crizland who were married at the Parish Church of Saint Johns Westminster about Seven years ago That the said John Crizland was born somewhere in the Parish of Saint Anne in the Liberty of Westminster but where particular he doth not know That he hath also been informed which Information he also believes to be true that the said Mary Crizland hath with her father and Mother been Passed by virtue of an Order of Removed from the Parish of Saint Johns Wapping to the said Parish of Saint Annes Westminster which said Order hath never been Appealed against

Sworn this 18th. day of March 1799 before us} Menter John Collick

Jas Talboys
