Middx.  Elizth. Wilson wife of Joseph Wilson a Lunatick upon Oath says that She Intermarried with the said Joseph Wilson at ye. Fleet London upwards of Sixteen Years since Says her sd. Husband when a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with Dr. Green in Beaufort Buildings in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for One Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & hath quitted his said Service abt. Twenty three Years as her said Husband hath informd. her & as She Verily believes Says her Sd. Husband never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant

Sworn Octr. 2d. 1756 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Elizth. Wilson
