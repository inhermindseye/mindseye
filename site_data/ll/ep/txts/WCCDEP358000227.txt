Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Bond given to an demnify the Parish 11th Feby 1786 to wit Frances Johnson aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Powell in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for upwards of two years at the yearly Wages of 5 Pounds hath quitted said Service about 7 years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Depts knowledge Information or belief whereby she may have gained any Settlement And further on her Oath saith that on the 27th. day of Octr. 1782 she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the Sign of the Trumpet in Great Gabb Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes which has since been baptized & by the name of Mary  And further saith that one Daniel Gabb a Journey man Shoemaker who lives of No. 6 in Theobalds Road is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Deponents Body and begotten the same And that the said Daniel Gabb is the only and Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry. 1786 before D Walker

her Frances Johnson mark
