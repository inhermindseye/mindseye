Middx  Sarah Arnott Wife of Henry Arnott (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Henry Arnott in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex abt. 7 Years ago and that her sd. Husband about two Years since lived in and rented a house of £20 by the Year in Stanhope Strt. in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for one Year & upwards and has not since he quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 7th 1748 before } Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Sarah Arnot
