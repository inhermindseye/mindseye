Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Talboys One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that Henry aged about Three years was born a Bastard on the Body of Elizabeth Tremlelt in Ogle Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex and Thomas aged about Seven Months was born a Bastard in Rodneys Row Shadwell in the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell in the County of Middlesex

Sworn this 1st. day of Decr. 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

James Talboys
