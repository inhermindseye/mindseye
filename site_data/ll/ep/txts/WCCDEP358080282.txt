Middx to wit Elizth. Goolbey Aged about Thirty Three years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Thos. Goolbey (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the City of Dublin about Sixteen ago and by whom she hath Three Children which her Uncle now keeps Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in White Lyon Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex about Two years at the yearly Rent of Twenty Six pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of One year ago since which time her said Husband never Rented House or Tenemt. of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 3d Day of Septr. 1778 before  James Croft  Geo: Reid

her Elizth. Goolbey Mark
