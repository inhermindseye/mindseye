Middx & Westmr}  Joseph Kirkman Aged about Twenty Eight Years on his Oath says he intermarried with Elizabeth his now Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Luke in the County of Middlesex about five Years and by whom he hath no Children now living Says he was bound an Apprentice by Indenture to one Mr. John Brown a Watch Glassmaker in Little Deans Court St. Martins le Grand in the Parish of Saint Anns within Aldersgate London and Served his said Master upwards of four Years the last half year of which time he Served his said Westmr. Parish of Saint Ann's and after leaving his said Master he never Rented House of Ten pounds P Ann paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this Eighteenth day of March 1778 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

Jos. Kirkman
