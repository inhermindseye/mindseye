Middx to wit Mary Clear lived about 41 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Edward Clear her late Husband linasor at the parish Church of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . about 17 Years ago and by whom she hath Two Children now living Namely by Edward Aged about 10 Years and Mary Aged about Eight Years says her said late Husband served seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Githama Glazier in Middlex Row in the Parish of Brompton in the County of Kent and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House or tenement of 10 P Ann paid Taxes or did any other Act Whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1781 R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Mary Clear her Mark
