Middx.  Mary Darby aged about 30 Years Wife of William Darby (gone from her) Upon Oath says she intermarried with the said William Darby in Rumford Chapel in Essex about Ten Years ago and hath four Children by him (to wit) Susannah aged upwards of Eight Years John aged about five Years Mary aged about four Years and Margaret aged about six Months says her said husband Rented a house in Sawyers Yard St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster within the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds lived thereinabout Six Months and Quitted same about the Month of May last and hath not since Rented Ten pounds by the Year nor paid Parochial Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn the 24th. day of July 1765 , Before} Paul Vaillant

Mary Derby
