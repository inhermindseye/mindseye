Westmr. to wit Mary Goodall Aged about forty four Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of William Goodall who has been dead about Twelve Years and with whose she Intermarried at May Fair Chappel about Twenty Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living. Says her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House in Gloucester Court  St. James Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of one Year at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of fourteen Years ago & hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House or paid Taxes neither hath this Examinant done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 21st. day of October 1772 before Us} Thos. Kynaston  Sam Wright

mary Goodall
