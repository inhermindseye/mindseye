St. Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr}  the Child Charlotte Hoghton passd. to St. Luke Old Street to wit Elizabeth Box aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Baxter in Arrundell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes for 18 months at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted said Service about two years ago since which time she has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained a Settlement elsewhere Says that she has one Child living named Charlotte Horton aged 17 Months or thereabouts born a Bastard in the House of a Mr. Blencow No. 8 Ratcliff Mount in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middx , And further says that her said Child never gained any other Settlement than by Birth And this Examinant further saith That she has hired herself as a yearly Servant to a Baker opposite Barnhill Row in Chiswell Street with whom she is to go to live tomorrow night  that her said Child has been Christned by the Name of Charlotte Hoghton , is weaned and that she this Examinant wishes to leave same to the Care of the Parish that she may

go to her place to which she is hired as aforesaid

Sworn this 19th Decr. 1785 before  Sampr Wright J Addington

Elizabeth Box
