Middx  Eliz Armstrong upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Joseph Armstrong who dyed abt. 8 yrs. since and that since the Decease of her said late husband she lived a Yearly Servant with Messrs. Mathews & Bernard Apothecarys in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly wages of £6 for three Years and upwards and quitted her said Service at Michas 1748 Says when first she was hired to live with the said Mr Mathews & Barnard She had two Sons living by her sd late husband namely Joseph and William who were both then not of their times having Served Seven Years Apprentiship the one with Mr Seagood a Carpenter in Bell Alley  Grace Church Street the other with Mr. Sallis a Chairmaker in Easters Court near Cripplegate Says she has not since Michs. 1748 Rented Ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 13th. 1749 before Tho Lane

Eliz: Armstrong
