Westmr.  Roger Adams aged upwards of 40 Years Upon Oath says he never was Married and that he lived a hired Yearly Servant at St. Luke's hospital for Lunatics in Moorfields in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middlesex for two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds, quitted said Service in the Month of March last and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn the 5th. Novr. 1767 , before} Paul Vaillant  Fran Cary

Roger Adams
