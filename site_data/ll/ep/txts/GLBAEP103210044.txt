Middlesex ss}  Grace Jones maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Mathews a Victualler in Sun Yard in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two years & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23 day of December 1784 before R Davies Grace Jones
