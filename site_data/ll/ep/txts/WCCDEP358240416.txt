Harris Mary & her three B. Chn John Elizth & Mary Harris

St. Clement Danes June 3d 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement in St CD }  he three illegit Children vizt}  John Harris settled at Farnham by at service}  Elizth. settled by Birth in Farnham}  & Mary settled in Portsmouth by Birth  (to wit) Mary Harris  singlewoman aged 39 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs Staines in a Vere Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for upwards of a year that she quitted that Service about three or four & twenty years ago and hath not Since lived as an hired yearly Servant or done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement of the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that she three hath three Children which are all illegitimate vizt. John Harris aged Nineteen years or thereabouts born in the Workhouse of the Parish of St Clement Danes but who is settled in his own Right by living as a yearly Servant with Mr. John Knight a Farmer at Farnham in the County  of Surrey for about five years last past- Elizabeth Harris born on or about the 28th day of May 1784 in the Workhouse at Farnham aforesaid, and May Harris born at the House of Mrs Grant in the Town of Portsmouth in the County of Hants on or about the 27th day of May 1787 And this Examinant saith that she is distressed and unable to provide herself without assistance

Sworn the  third day of June 1794 before me} Wm Kitchiner

Mary Harris Mark

Witness B Newman
