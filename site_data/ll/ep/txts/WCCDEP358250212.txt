Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Samuel Ingram aged about 34 years upon his Oath saith that he never was married That he served Seven Years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Crace a Painter in Long Acre in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of Decr. 1795 before us J Hind M Addington

Saml. Ingram
