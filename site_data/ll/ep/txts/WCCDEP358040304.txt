Middx & Westmr.  Sarah Bostin aged abt. 37 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about ten Years ago she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr. Venable at the Ball head in Vear Street Clare market in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of five pounds for the first Qur. or thereabouts and Six pounds for the Rest of the Year & upwards and has not since she quitted her sd. Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in anyother Place to her knowledge or beleif and further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she was delivered of a female Bastard Child on the 9th. Day of December Instant at Nurse Alexander in Peter Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes aforesaid which has been baptized Ann and is become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same and that Patrick Richardson late a Porter in the market but now a Sailor on board

the Thander Coomb Sloop is the  father thereof the said Patrick Richardson having had carnal knowledge of her body several times in the month of March last in her Lodging at Mrs. Hawkins in Witch Alley in the sd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes at one of which times he got her with Child of the female Bastard Child of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that the sd. Patrick Richardson and no other Person is the true & real father thereof and further upon her Oath saith she was delivered of another bastard child on the 20th. Day of February 1738 in the house of Mrs. Boys in Great Wild Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx which was baptized Philip Dunn and is at present Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes aforesaid

Sworn Decr. 29th 1748 before } Samuel Clarke  Wm: Jones

The Mark of Sarah Boston
