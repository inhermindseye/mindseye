Middlesex & Westminster}  Catherine Lowe Aged about Thirty five years on behalf of Jane Low her Female Bastard Child on her Oath says that Richard Cundall who was the Father of her said Child is now Dead and further says that the said Jane Lowe the Infant is upwards of Seven years old and was born in the House of one John Mead in Whites Alley in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex and that she the said Jane Low her Daughter hath done no Act to gain a Settlement in any other Parish or Place

Sworn this 6th. day of February 1776 before R Butler M Addington

her Catherine Lowe Mark
