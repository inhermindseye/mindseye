Middlesex ss.  Elizabeth Wise the Wife of Wm. Wise of Nr 4 . Maypole Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the said County Cooper maketh Oath that she was lawfully Married to her said Husband at the Parish Church of St. Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry on or about the Twenty Second Day of April 1787 , that her said Husbd. has informed her that he was duly bound Apprentice by Indenture for Seven years to his Father Wm. Wise of the Parish of St. Ebbs in the City of Oxford  Cooper , that his Father died there before the expiration of his Apprenticeship & he was then turned over to his Uncle Robt. Wise of the same Parish Cooper with whom he duly served the remainder of his said Apprenticeship that her said Husbd. has never Rented any House or Lodging of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement, that She has Two Children Namely Susannah aged about Ten Years & William aged about Two Years the lawful issue by her said Husband And this Deponent further saith that by reason of her said Husband neglecting to provide for her & her said Two Children, she has been obliged to apply to the Overseers of the Poor of the said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate for Relief & has been relieved by them accordingly

Sworn this 25th Day of April 1798 . before H: Reynell

Elizabeth Wise
