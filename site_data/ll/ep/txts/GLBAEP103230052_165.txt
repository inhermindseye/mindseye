Middlesex ss  Sarah Andrews the wife of Robert Andrews maketh oath that her said Rusland about Eleven Years ago lived in prented a house known by the Sign of queens head in King street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County & during Such his Residence therein he was charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same such house being total the Yearly Rent of twenty eight Pounds That her Said husband has not Sice rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the Yearly renter Value of Ten Pounds or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That she hath two Children namely John aged about twelve years and James Aged about Six years the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband to whom She was lawfully married neither of which Children have gained a Settlement for him Self.

Sworn this day of June 1787 before}

Sarah Andrew
