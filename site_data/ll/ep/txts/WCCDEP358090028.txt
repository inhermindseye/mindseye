Middx & Westmr}  Sarah Wright aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Lady Macklesfied in Arlbemarl Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Ten Pounds hath quitted her said Service about Twelve Months ago since which time she hath gained no other subsequent Settlement And further upon her Voluntary Oath says on the fourteenth day of February last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Smith a Shoemaker in Browns Buildings Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been baptized by the same of Sarah and is likely to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same And that one le Pere otherwise Peter Muttal  Butler to her said late Mistress did beget such Bastard Child on her Body he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body in her said Mistresses House sometime the middle of May 1778 and at several different times afterward at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Lepere otherwise Peter Muttal did beget the same and that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1779 before  Sampn Wright

Sarah Wright
