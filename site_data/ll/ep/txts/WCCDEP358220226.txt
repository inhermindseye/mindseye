Westminster to wit Ann Anthony Aged about Twenty Two Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Shipley a Perukemaker in Gutter Lane in the Parish of Saint Michael Woodstreet Vedast London for one Year at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service about three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yrly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30th. day of January 1771 before W Kelynge 

her Ann Anthony Mark

Witness Petr Sidebotham
