Middx to wit Elizabeth Dodman aged about 38 Years on her Oath says she Intermarried to her Husband Richard Dodman (gone from her) at the Parish Church of St. Mary Islington about 11 Years ago and by whom she hath one Child living Named William aged upwards of 9 Years Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a Shop and Parlour the Lower part of a House of one Mr Richardson situate in Totenham Court Road in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex for Six Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds but paid no Parish Taxes due for the same But that her said Husbands Goods were seized for one Qars. Rent due and in Arrear to the said Mr. Richardson Says her said Husband Quitted the said House in the Year 1769 since which time her said Husband never gained any other subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief neither hath this Examinant since her said Husband left her

Sworn this 23rd day of Febry 1779 before R Butler Jno. Machin

her Elizth Dodman Mark
