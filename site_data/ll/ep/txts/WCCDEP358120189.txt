Middx (to wit)  Jno: Park aged abt. Twenty Nine Years upon Oath Says he intermarryed with his wife in the Parish Church of Hendon in the County of Middx upwards of five Years since Says he Rented a house in Silver Street near Golden Square in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds & lived therein upwards of Six Weeks Says he quitted the sd. House in the Month of Augt. 1749 since wth time he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or Served any Parochiall Office

Sworn the 7th. day of Septr. 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell John Parke
