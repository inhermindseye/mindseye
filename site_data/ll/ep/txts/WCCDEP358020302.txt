St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  Appealed agst. subsequent setllemt found Vide P. 283 (to wit) Mary Burgess Aged 25 years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith That she  was Married to her present Husband Edward Burgess at Christ Church in the County of Surry on the 27th Day of August 1787 . by Barnes That previous thereto and while he was single not having any Child or Children her said Husband lived as an hired Yearly Clerk or Servant with Messrs. Sandom & Co. in America Square John Street Minories for two Years or thereabouts at the  Wages of Fifty pounds for the first, and Sixty pounds for the second Year, that during the last three or four Months of such Service he lodged and constantly slept in the House of Mr Beridge at Newington Cause way in the Parish of St. Mary Newington in the County of Surry That he left the Service of Messrs. Sandom & Co. about four or five years ago and  since hath not done any Act or thing whatsoever to acquire or whereby he might be entitled unto any other legal Settlement That she hath one Child now living with her born of her Body (in legal Matrimony) and be gotten by her said Husband Named Edward Aged 20. One Year Eight Months or thereabouts, And that her said Husband is now with his own Friends, Dangerously Ill and that she is Poor and unable to provide for herself and said Child;

Sworn the  4th 17 18 June 1791 - before Us H. Dive Wm Hyde

Mary Burges
