Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to Wit) Elizabeth Holden Aged about 34 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath Saith that she intermarried with Edward Holden her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le Strand in the County of Middlesex that since their Intermarriage they rented and lived in a House in Lanleys Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex at a Yearly Rent of upwards of ten Pounds for about two Years that since quitting the said House she does not know of any other Settlement her said Husband has any way acquired and that she this Examinant is poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 1st. Octr 1796 before D Walker W Ferster

Elizabeth her Mark Holden
