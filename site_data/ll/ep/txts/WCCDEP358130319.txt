Middx 

Susannah Butler aged abt. 28 yrs. upon Oath says she was never married & that she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Edwd. Pryor a Butcher in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx for one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath quitted her said Service abt Months and further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clemt Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same when born & that one John Denton a Labourer did beget the sd. Child or Children on her Body he the said John Denton having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body in yr. Month of July last in her sd. Master's house & at sevl. other times since at one of wch. times he got her with Child of the Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant & that he the sd. John Denton & no other Person is the true & real father thereof

Sworn the 15th. of April 1757 before}
