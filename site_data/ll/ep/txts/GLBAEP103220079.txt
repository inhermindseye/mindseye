Sarah Smith a Singlewoman maketh oath that She has been informed by her late Mother Sarah Smith deced & which information She believes to be true that her late Father Thomas Smith deceased heretofore was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year at Certain Wages by & with one Mrs Cook in Hummerton in the Parish of St John Hackney in the Said County and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for many Years That he did not afterwards live as a hired Servant for a year elsewhere or rent a house of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or Serve any Parish office or Pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She this Deponent was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever Married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself

Sworn this. 31. day of October 1786 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Sarah Smith
