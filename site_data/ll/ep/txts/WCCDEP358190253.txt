Middx  Susana: Flood aged about 27 Years upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Noah Flood who has been Dead upwards of three Months to whom She was married in the parish Church of Hornsey in the County of Middx near Six Years ago that he served two Years or thereabouts the last part of his Apprentiship with Mr John Farmer  Rule maker in the Said Parish of Hornsey that he never rented a House or paid Taxes

Sworn July 13th. 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Susanna Flood
