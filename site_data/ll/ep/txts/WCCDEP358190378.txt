Middx  Sarah Webb Aged about 30 Years Upon Oath Says she was Never Marryed and that she lived a hired Yearly Sert. with Mr Robert Kirk in Clements Inn in the Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds quitted her said Service about three Months and hath not Since Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child on the Ninth Day of June Last in the Lying In Hospital in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middx which hath Since been baptized by the Name of Richard and is likely to become Charge to the said Parish of St. Mary Le bone she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same And Further upon her Voluntary Oath Says that One

Richd Pearce a Head Load Man Living at Mr Jones at the Princes Head Alehouse in Hampton Court did beget the said Male Bastard Child on her Body he the said Richd Pearce having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinant the beginning of September Last One Thousand Seven Hundred and Sixty two And at Several Other times Since At One of which times he got her with Child of the said Male Bastard Child she was Delivered of as aforesaid and that he the said Richd Pearce and no Other Person did beget the same.

Sworn ye 17th Day of Septr 1763 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarah Webb
