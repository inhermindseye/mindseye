Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Forward aged about 26 years upon his Oath saith that on or about the 23rd. day of September 1793 he Intermarried with Ann Forward his present Wife at the parish Church of Saint Anne Westminster by whom he hath One Child living Vizt. Catherine aged about Three years That he this Deponent never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an Apprentice that he was born at Taunton Somersetshire That his father William Forward belongs to the parish of Little Sandford in the County of Somerset and hath Received Alms several times from the said Parish of Little Sandford.

Sworn this 21st. day of Janry. 1797  Richd. Ford Robinson

James Forward
