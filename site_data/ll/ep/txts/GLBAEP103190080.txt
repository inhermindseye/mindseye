Middlesex ss}  Margaret Dunsfort Aged twenty four years the wife of John Dunsfort maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which She believes to be true That he duly served the whole of his Apprenticeship with a Tinman whose name She don't Know in the City of Exeter in the County of Devon & to her knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt And that She was lawfully married to her Said husband

Sworn this. 13. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Margaret Dunsfort
