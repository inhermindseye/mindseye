St. Clement Danes 12th. September 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Clement Eastcheap London  (to wit) Mary Hur Sarah Hutchinson Aged 65 years or thereabouts Upon Widow of William Hutchinson Deced Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her late Husband William Hutchinson in a about the Month of February next before the Marriage Act took place at May Flair Chapel according to the Ceremonies of the Church of England that her said Husband Died in or about the year 1760 or 1761 that Subsequent thereto and while she was unmarried having only one Child, as a Daughter Named Elizabeth being then aged about sixteen years and placed as an Apprentice with Mrs. Brown a Child Bed Linen Worker in Hungerford Street in the Strand she this Deponent hired herself as a yearly servant with Mr. Sutton an Attorney in Clements Lane in the parish of St Clement  Eastcheap in the City of London with that she continued with him under such hiring for about 4 years (until his Decease) at the yearly Wages of £6 that she quitted that Service about 12 years ago and hath done no Act not since been Married nor done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement and that She is Ill Distressed and incapable of

Providing for herself without Assistance

Sworn the 12th day of September 1792 before us  Sampr Wright M Addington

Sarah Hutcheson
