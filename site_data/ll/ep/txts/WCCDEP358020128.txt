Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Esther Brown aged about 54 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Bungay in Elbow Lane in the parish of Saint Michael Royal in the City of London for about Three years at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry. 1789  Philip Dyot D Walker

Hester Brown
