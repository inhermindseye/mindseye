Middx ss  John Collings Maketh oath that about Eighteen Years ago he was bound apprentice to John Edwards of ye Parish of St. George the Martyr in the County of Surry  Cordwainer With Whom he duly Served his apprenticeship And this Depont. Saith that he hath not Since gained any Subsequent Settlement And that he hath Wife Named Sarah and one Child aged abt. Six Years Named John ye lawfull issue of this Depont. & his sd. Wife.

Sworn this 21. day of February 1750 before us  Richd. Ricards  Saml Gower

The Mark of John Collings
