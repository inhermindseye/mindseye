St. Clement Danes.

Ann Thorp aged 68 or thereabouts upon her Oath Saith That she knew James Patterson & James Dodd . That she Remembers their, Entering in Partnership together, that they were so For about Six Months. That the furing was carried on in Robin Hood Court That the Rent was to be Paid out of the Profits received. That she believes Rent was paid by Dodd or Patterson And Saith That she took the House and not either Dodd or Patterson took it, - that she vinters Dodd calcue to live in the House but cannot till whether he or she went away first from the House but believes she went first away and that Dodd Remained.

Sworn 25. Octr. 1790 . before  Wm. Kitchiner

her Ann Thorp Mark
