Middx.  Ann Mooreland aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of James Mooreland who is gone from her and is abroad & beyond the Seas as she has been informed & which Information she verily believes to be true, says she intermarried with the said James Mooreland in the Parish Church of St. James in the City of Bath about thirteen Years since, says that she this Examinant & her said husband are both Natives of Ireland & never gained any Settlement in England says she has two Children by her said husband (to wit) Daniel aged abt. Six Years born in the Lying in hospital in Brownlow Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middx. and James aged abt. two Years born in the lying in hospital in Duke Street Grosvenor Square in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr.

Sworn this 11th. day of March 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond  Sam Pechell

The Mark of Ann Mooreland
