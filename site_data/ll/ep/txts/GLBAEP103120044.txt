Middlesex ss}  Mary Mears a singlewoman maketh oath That her late Fathr. Thomas Mears deced has informed her that he was born in the Parish of East Grinstead in the County of Sussex and this Depont. Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Said Far. or her late Mother deced and this Depont. Saith that to her knowledge or belief she never did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 4th day of May 1773  Jno Sherwood

Mary Meers
