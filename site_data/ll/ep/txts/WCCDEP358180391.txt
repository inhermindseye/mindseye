Middx  Thomas Fletcher upon his Oath saith he intermarried with his present Wife  Susannah Fletcher on or abt. the 7th. Day of May 1743 at the Fleet London Says he lived Yearly hired Servt. for with Mr. Barradell Victualler in Carey Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Two pounds Three pounds & Three pounds ten Shillings for the Space of 4 Yrs. & upwards being all that time a Singleman & hath left his sd. Service about 9 Yrs. Says he hath not rented ten since he left the sd. Mr. Barradells Service rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworne Octr. 15th. 1745 before  Wm: Jones Thomas Fletcher
