Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Susannah Steers aged about 54 years upon her Oath Saith that she was never married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with James Anne  Esqr . No. 12 Essex Street Strand in the parish of Saint Clement Danes for the space of 2 years & 1 Quarter at the yearly Wages of 10 Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about 11 Months Since which time She hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for twelve months rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid taxes or done any Act matter or Thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement to the best of her Knowledge or Belief

Sworn this 12th. day of April 1797 before me G J Goodenough

her Susannah Steers Mark
