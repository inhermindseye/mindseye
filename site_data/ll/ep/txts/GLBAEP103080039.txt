Middx ss  Abigail Burges  Widow of John Burges deced Maketh oath That about forty Years ago her said husband was bound an Apprentice By Indre to Humphry Hopkins a Lighterman and Waterman of Shad Thames  then Called in the Parish of Saint Olave But now Called the Parish of Saint John in Southwarke in the County of Surry and duly served his Apprenticeship By means whereof he gained a legal settlement there And farther saith that to her this Deponents knowledge information or belief her said late husband did not at any time rent a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards Nor serve any Parish offices nor live as a hired servant by the year nor gain any subsequent settlement Nor hath she this Deponent to her knowledge or belief done any Act since her sd husbands Death to Acquire a settlement in her own right

Sworn this day of July 1758 before us}
