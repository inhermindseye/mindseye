Middx.  Margarett Cooke  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith That she lived a Yearly hired Servant with one Mr. Morrice Hosier in the Strand near Somersett House in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds for the Space of one Year & an half and hath left her said Service about Eight Years that since she left the sd. Mr. Morrices Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settemt in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 6th- Day of Novr. 1742 before} Tho Lane .

Margaret Cooke
