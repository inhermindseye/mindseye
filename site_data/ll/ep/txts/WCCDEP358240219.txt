St Clement Danes 20th Febry 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) Sarah Donnelly aged 61 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived never lived as an hired yearly Servant or did any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any legal Settlement in her own right that at her said father Rented and Resided in an House in Sheffield Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for a year or upwards at the A a Considerable Annual Yearly Rent & quitted it upwards of about 30 years ago and after he quitted the said parish of Saint Clement Danes never Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a year or acquired any legal Settlement saith that She was born in lawful Wedlock

Sworn the 20th day of February 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

Sarah Donnelly 's Mark
