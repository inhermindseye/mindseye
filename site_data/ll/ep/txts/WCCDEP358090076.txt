Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Russell Aged about Twenty Three Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Ward in the Parish of Kiverton in the County of Hereford  for one Year at the Yearly Wages of One Pound five Shillings hath quitted her said services upwards of five Years Ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt. And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when born Says that one William Augustus Howard  a Physician who keeps a House at No. 2 Westcott Buildings in the City of Bath & who now ledges at Jacks Coffee house in Dean Street so he is the Father there of he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body sometime in the Month of November last but the particular Day she does not now remember and at Several different times afterwards he attempted to do the same and believes he  at one of which time she got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now pregnant And that the said William Augustus Howard and no other person did beget the same and that he is the real true Father thereof

Sworn this 23d day of Septr. 1779 before  Abrm Baley  Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth: Russell Mark.
