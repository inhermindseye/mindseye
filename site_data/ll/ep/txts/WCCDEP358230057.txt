Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Lemon aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the Fifth day of April Instant she this Deponent was delivered of a female Bastard Child at the House of a Mrs. Barnes in Stewards Rents in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex which Child hath not as yet been Baptized

Sworn this 22d. day of April 1799 before us G S Goodenough J Reeves

her Sarah Lemon mark

H Day
