Middx  Margt. Wright upon Oath saith she never was married that she was born in Crown Court near King Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex and cannot give any Accot. of the Settlemt. of her father and mother and that she never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Novr. 6th. 1750 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Margt Wright
