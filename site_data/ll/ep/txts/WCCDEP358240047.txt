St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  Woolwich Kent  (to wit) Sarah Cole aged fourteen years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she was bound Apprentice to Mr. Bushton of Woolwich in the County of Kent Cooper and has served with him for between two and three Years, that owing to some Misunderstanding there fore Mother Margaret Cole brought her away this Deponent deserted her said Masters Service and has been for about Three Months from him, and that for the first part namely about five Weeks at the time she has been is absent from her said Master she has been with her Mother in Bartho Womens Hospital but that since then she has been in the Workhouse of Saint Clement Danes and has not served the whole of her Apprenticeship neither has she been discharged there from.

Sworn this 28th. day of Septr. 1791 . before Sampr. Wright  Thos. Gordons .

her Sarah Cole Mark
