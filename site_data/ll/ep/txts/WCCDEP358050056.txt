Westmr.  Ann Rogers aged about 22 Years upon Oath saith she never was married and that about 3 or 4 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Wildman a Butcher in Hollis Street in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for two Years or thereabouts and has not since she left her sd. Service rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn June 8th. 1749 before} Tho Lane .

Ann Rogers
