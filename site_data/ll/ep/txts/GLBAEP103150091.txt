Middlesex ss}  Norman McLeod maketh oath That divers Years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the Year with John Cockburn in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middx at they early Wages of Seven pounds & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Four Years & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That he this Deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor been an Apprentice nor ever Rented a house of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that he hath a wife named Ann to whom he was lawfully married & two Children namely Catherine Aged about eighteen Years & Jane Aged about Fifteen Years the lawful Issue of this Depont. by his sd Wife

Sworn this 15th day of December 1775 before  Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of Norman Mr Leod
