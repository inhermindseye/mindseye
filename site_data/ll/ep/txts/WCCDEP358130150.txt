Middx.  Hannah Bray aged about 32 years upon Oath says she lived a hired yearly Servant upwards of four Years with Mr. Carter in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and quitted her said Service about five years ago and hath not since She quitted the said Mrs. Carters Service Rented Ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year And further upon her voluntary Oath says that on the 25th. day of Septemr. in the year 1751 She was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the Parish of Cirencester in the County of Gloucester -  in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes which hath since been baptized Elizabeth and is still living & likely to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes Says that Joseph Ralph a Taylor who now Lodges at Mr. Mercer's in new Court in the said Parish did beget the sd. Female Bastard Child on her Body he the said Joseph Ralph passing at that time as this Examinants Husband and further upon her voluntary Oath saith that She is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to Become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes

She being poor & unable to maintain the same when born and that the said Joseph Ralph did beget the said Child or Children with which She is now Pregnant he the said Joseph Ralph having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body in the Month of July on August 1755 in the House of Mr. Mercer in new Court aforesaid And that the said Joseph Ralph & no other Person beget the same

Sworn the 20th. Day of April 1755 . before} Barth: Hammond

Hannah Bray
