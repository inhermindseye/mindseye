Middx  Rebecca Willson  Singlewoman upon her Oath saith that abt. Two Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Lady Northey in Essex Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds for the Space of One Year & upwards that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 21st. Day of October 1741 before} Jas Gronous

The Mark of Rebecca Willson
