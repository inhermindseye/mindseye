Middx The Examination of Fras. Ealey  Widw . of Jas. Ealey Deced Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 27th. Day of June 1740. Who upon her Oath saith yt. about 20 Years ago she Intermarryed with the above sd. Jas. Ealey & that her sd. late Husband before his Intermarriage with this Examint. & whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Dunton Meal Man in Clare Market in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the space of two Years & upwds. but under what Wages this Examint. cannot tell. And further saith that her sd. Husband in his life time or this Examint. since his Death never Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or othewise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that She is now Poor & Sick & not able to Maintaine herselfe.

Sworn the Day & Year above mentioned before} Jas. Gronous

The Mark of Fras. Ealey
