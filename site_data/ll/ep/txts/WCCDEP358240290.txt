St Clement Danes July 29th. 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary in Carlisle  (to wit) Ann Clark aged twenty one years and upwards Upon Oath saith that She was Married to John Clark her present Husband at the parish Church of St Mary at Carlisle in the County of Cumberland in or about the year 1788 to John Clark her present Husband that the said John Clark served his Apprenticeship under Indentures of Apprenticeship with Mr John Wilkinson a Shoemaker in Scotch Street in the parish of St Mary in Carlisle aforesaid and since the Expiration of his Apprenticeship he hath never done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlemt. to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief that she hath one Child by her said Husband now with her namely William born on or about the eighteenth day of January 1792 and that her said Husband is now from her on a Recruiting party and she is distressed pregnant and unable to provide herself without assistance Sworn the 29th day of July 1793 }

Ann Clark 's Mark

Witness Garrett
