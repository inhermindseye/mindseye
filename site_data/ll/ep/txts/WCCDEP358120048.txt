Middx  Henry Hussey upon Oath Saith he was married to his wife Ann Hussey at the Fleet London about 14 years ago & has by her three Childn. (Viz) Henry aged 9 Yrs. Edwd. aged 8 yrs. & Benjamin aged thirty Months and that before his sd. Intermarried & whilst a Singleman he lived a yearly Servant in Mr. Orlando Hamlin for the space of Fourteen Months at the yearly Wages of Six pounds Six Shillings and during the last Six Weeks of his sd. Service lodged at his sd. masters house in Boswell Court in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlx that he quitted his sd. Service abt. 17 yrs. ago & has since rented ten pounds a year pd. parish taxes lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn Janry 29th. 1754 before}

The Mark of Heny. Hussey
