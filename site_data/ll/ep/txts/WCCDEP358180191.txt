Middx  Hanah Lewin  Widow aged about 60 Years upon her Oath saith that about 3 Years since being then a Widow & without Child or Children she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Doleman in Cucumber Court in Ship Yard in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £5. for the Space of 4 Years that since she left her said Service she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 8th. May 1744 before} Wm: Jones

The Mark of Hanah Lewin
