Westmr.  Hannah Mann Aged about Forty Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mrs. Wilson in Fountain Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx One Year at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted said Service about five Years & hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 2d of Jany. 1765 Before}
