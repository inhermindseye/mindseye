Middx ss  Sarah Hawkins the Widow of James Hawkins deced maketh oath that her sd. late Husband has often informed her that about Twenty five Years ago he was bound apprentice to Mr. George Wallis a Cooper who then lived on Garlick Hill in ye City of London with whom he Served five years of his apprenticeship and his sd Master dying he was turned over to one Mr. Blake a Cooper of ye parish of St. George in Bloomsbury in ye County of Middx with whom he served two years being ye residue of his apprenticeship & by Means thereof gained a legal settlement there which information this Depont. believes to be true And this Depont farther saith that to her knowledge information  or belief her sd. husband never rented a house of Ten pounds by ye year or upwards nor served any parishes offices nor gained any subsequent settlement And that she hath a Child Named Edward aged about Six Years the lawfull issue of this Depont. & her sd late husband

Sworn this 20 day of April 1754 before  Richd. Ricards  Tho: Quarrill

his Sarah Hawkins Mark
