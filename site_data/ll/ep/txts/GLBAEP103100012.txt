Middlesex ss}.  Mary Clark  singlewomen maketh oath that divers years ago She was hired & lived as a hired servant by the Year at Certain Yearly Wages With Thomas Hart in the Precinct of St. Catherine in the County of Middlesex That during this Deponents servitude as aforesaid She removed with her Said Master into the Parish of saint Botolph without Aldgate in the sd. County Where she Continued with her Said Master under such hiring & service for the Space of a year & upwards and by Means thereof as she is advised gained a legal settlement there and this Depont. Saith that she hath not since lived as a hired servant for a year nor was ever Married nor hath she done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 22 day of June 1768 before  Chris: Scott .

The Mark of Mary Clark
