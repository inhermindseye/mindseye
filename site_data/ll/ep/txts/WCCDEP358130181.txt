Middx  Mary Slater Widow of Peter Slater Deceased aged upwards of Forty Years upon Oath says She Intermarried with her said Late Husband at ye. Fleet London about Sixteen Years ago Says her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr. Cerson a Cabinetmaker on Clerkenwell Green in the parish of St. James  Clerkenwell in the County of Middx. & served him three Years & a half the First part of his Apprenticeship & then his said Master Dyed & never was assigned over to any other Person as her said Husband hath informed her & as She verily believes Says her sd. late Husband never Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes was a Yearly Servant for one Year or Served any Parochial Office Says She hath two Children by her said Husband (Videlicet) John aged abt. Eleven Years and Eleanor aged abt. Eight Years Says her sd. late Husband died the third Day of this Instant June & She being poor is not able to maintain herself & Children

Sworn the 24th. Day of June 1756 . before} Theodr. Sydenham  Barth: Hammond

The mark of Mary Slater
