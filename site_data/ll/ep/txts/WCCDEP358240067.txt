St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster }  Kirk Livington near Carlisle Cumberland  Jane Markham Aged 55 years being one of the people called Quaters upwards Widow Upon Oath saith Solemnly Affirms that she never was Married except by the Ceremony of the Roman Catholick Church that she never served any Apprenticeship nor lived as an hired yearly Servant or otherwise obtained any legal Settlement in her own Right, that her father lived in his Life time on an Eite of his Own of the Yearly Value of about £200 in the parish of Kirk Livington near Carlisle in the County of Cumberland & Died than about 37 years ago in London being still in the Occupancy of that Estate And that she hath never obtained or become intilled to any Subsequent legal Settlement to her knowledge or belief And that She is now unable to Maintain herself without Assistance

Affirmed the 9th. Janry 1792 before me} R Butler

Jane Markham
