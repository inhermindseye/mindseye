Middx  Mary Taylor Wife of Wm. Taylor (gone from her) upon Oath Saith she intermarried with the sd. Wm. Taylor at Dainham Church in Cheshire abt. 7 Yrs. ago and has by him two Childn. Mary aged abt. 6 Yrs. and Ann aged about four Years, that she can give no certain Account of the Settlemt. of her sd. husband & that her father Rt. Bostocks lived in a house of his own for several Years at Warmingham in the sd. County of Chester and this Examinant never rented ten Pounds a year Paid Parish taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servt.

Sworn June 19th. 1753 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Mary Taylor
