Middx The Examination of Henry Chinery Taken the 26th. Day of June 1739 Who upon his Oath saith that abt. Four Years ago he lived in & Rented a House in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £24 & Continued in the same for the Space of One Year & upwards And further saith yt. he hath not since he left the sd. House Rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleife that he hath a Wife & two small Childn. & he being Poor is not able to maintaine them.

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before}

Henry Chinery
