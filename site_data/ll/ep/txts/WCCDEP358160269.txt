Middx  The Examination of Ann Basset  Wido of Daniel Basset liveing in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 29th. Day of March 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that about thirty two Years ago her late Husband Danl. Basset lived in any rented a House in Plough Stable Alley in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes for the Space of four Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of twelve Pounds & further on her Oath saith that her sd. late Husband in his life Time nor hath She since his Death ever rented any other House of the Yearly Rent of ten Pounds paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before}
