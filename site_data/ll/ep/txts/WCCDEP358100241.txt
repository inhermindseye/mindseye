Middlesex to wit Eleanor Jones Aged about Twenty Six Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thomas Jones (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Mary Lambeth in the County of Surry about Nine Years ago and by whom she hath one Child living named Robert Aged about Eight Years Says her said Husbands Rented and lived in a House in Kennington place in the Parish of St. Mary Lambeth in the said County of Surry for Nine Months or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Guineas but Whether he paid Taxes due for the same she does not know hath quitted said House about Six Years ago since which time her said Husband never Rented House Tenement or Lodgings of Ten pounds a Year or otherwise gained any other Settlemt.

Sworn this 16th. day of August 1784 before G Mercer Jno. Machin

her Eleanor Jones Mark
