Middlesex and Westminster}  Swear to St. Clemts. to wit - Daniel Healey aged forty Six Years or thereabouts, upon his Oath Saith That he lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Norcott in Clements Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , at the Yearly Wages of £12 for upwards of Twelve Months, That he quitted his said Place about eight Years ago - that when he so quitted his said place he was sick, and was received into the Work house of Saint Clement Danes , as having gained a Settlement in the said Parish of Saint Clement by Virtue of such his Service and this Examinant further saith that he continued in the said Workhouse absent Six Months, and that since he quitted the said Workhouse he hath not live as an hired Yearly Servant for one Year rented any House or Tenement at ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge he may have gained any other Settlement than a aforesaid

Sworn this 27th. Octr. 1784 before R Butler

Daniel Healy
