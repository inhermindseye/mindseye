Middx  The Examination of Mary Eason  Singlewoman Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 10th. Day of March 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that above 20 Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mr. Alexr. Hatan in Bow Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . for the space of 3 Years & upwds. at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds And further upon her Oath saith that she hath not since that time rented £10 a Year Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife And that she is now Poor & Sick & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworn the Day & Year above menconed before} Tho Lane  Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Mary Eason
