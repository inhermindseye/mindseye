Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Getter aged about 26 years upon her Oath saith that about Nine years ago she Intermarried with William Getter her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath Two Children living named Samuel William aged about five years and Elizabeth  Sarah aged about Two years That her said Husband Rented and lived in a house in Clements Inn passage in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . for about Three years at the yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds and paid Taxes due for the same that he Quitted said House about Six years ago since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement And this Deponent further saith that her said Husband has deserted her & Allows her no support and that she is poor and unable to maintain herself and Two Children

Sworn this 12th. day of April 1796 } John Collick

Mary Getter
