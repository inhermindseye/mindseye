Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Martha Ranson aged about 32 years upon her Oath saith that in the year 1770 she Intermarried with Robt. Ranson (her late Husband who has been dead about 11 years) at the Parish Church of St. George Southwark that she hath Three Children living by her said late Husband vizt. John Phadre aged about 16 years and Lentillus aged about 11 years both provided for That her said late Husband Rented and Lived in a House the Corner of the Kath: Wheel Inn in the Borough in the Parish of St. George Southwark  for Three years & upwards at the yearly Rent of £90 and paid Taxes due for the same that he had quitted said House about 12 Months before his Dece after which he never Rented any House Tent. or Lodging of £10 a year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement nor hath this Ext. since her said late Husbands Death done any Act whereby she might have gained a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 3d. day of Septr. 1787 R Butler Wm. Kitchiner

Martha Ranson
