Middx  Eliz Shearman aged abt. 33 Yrs. upon Oath Saith she is the Wido. of John Shearman who dyed abt. Seven Years ago & by whom she has one Child living (viz) John aged abt. 13 Years who never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for One year and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with a Taylor in the Parish of Begaldy in the County of Radnor and never rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one year and that She hath not since her sd. late husbands Decease rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Parish taxes And this Examinant further upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx (where she now Lodges) she being poor & unable to Maintain the same when born and that John King a Taylor (being acquainted with this Examinant had carnal

Knowledge of her Body several times in August last and frequently since at one of which times begot her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said John King and no other person is the true and real father thereof

Sworn March 17th. 1753 : before Fran: Bedwell

Elizabeth: Shearman
