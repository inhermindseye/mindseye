Middx.  Richard Coe aged abt. 70 Years upon Oath says he Rented a house in St. Clements Church Yard in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds lived therein abt. three Years and hath Quitted same about thirty Years and hath not Since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes.

Sworn this 1st. day of April 1760 before} Barth: Hammond

Rd. Coe
