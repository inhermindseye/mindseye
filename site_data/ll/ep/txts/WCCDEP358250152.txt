Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Partin aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith That she Intermarried with John Partin at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about four years ago That her said Husband informed this Examinant that he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Turner a Lapidary in Mouldmakers Row in the Parish of Saint Leonard Foster in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year for forty days or done any Act Matter or King whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 2d. day of Febry. 1795 . } N Bond J Hlund

Jane Partin
