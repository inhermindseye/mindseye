St. Clement Danes 21st Augst. 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) William Wood  Widower aged Sixty Years and or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that he intermarried with Elizabeth his late Wife at the parish Church of St George Hanover Square about 28 Years ago, that he had 5 Children by his said Wife all born in legal Matrimony, that his said Wife & Children are all now Dead, that he since his said Marriage Rented and Resided in an House in Houghton Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said Liberty and County about 12 Years, at the Annual Rent of about 60, Guineas, that he quitted that House about 12 Years ago, and hath not since Rented any House or Apartment of £10 a Year, paid any parish Taxes, served any parochial Office, or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement

Sworn the 21st Augt. 1792 before Me } Wm Kitchiner

W Wood
