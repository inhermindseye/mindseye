Middx to wit Oliver Blanchfield Aged about Sixty Seven years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Hester  his late wife at the Parish of St. Margarets Westmr . in the year 1740 And by whom he hath four Children all of whom are now provided for Says that this Examinant Rented and lived in a House in Diott Street in the Parish of Saint Giles in the Fields in the County of Middlesex upwards of four years at the yearly Rent of Eighteen pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Twenty years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement Eigher by Renting House or Tenement of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 19th. day of Octr. 1778 before  Abrm. Baley J Welch

Oliver Blanchefield
