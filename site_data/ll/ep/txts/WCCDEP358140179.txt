Middx.  Mary Sandypher aged abt. 33 Yrs. upon Oath says she is (the widow of Michael Sandypher who has been dead abt. Eleven Months says her said husband Rented a house in Duke Street in Ye. Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty seven Pounds lived therein upwards of two Years and quitted same abt. Eight Years before his death and after he quitted said house never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes says she hath not since her said husbands death Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes or Otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th. day of Novr. 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Sandefor
