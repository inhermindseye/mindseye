Middx  Elizabeth Chapman aged about 24 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wife of William Chapman a Soldier in one of the Marching Regiments under the Command of the Duke of Richmond to whom she was Married at Woolwich Church in the Month of January 1754 That he served seven Years Apprenticeship with a Taylor in the Parish of Southwell in the County of Bedford That he never kept House paid Parliamentary or Parochial Taxes nor hath been a hired Yearly Servant since his Apprenticeship which Expired about Six Months before his intermarriage with this Examinant That she hath one Child by her said Husband to wit Mary aged upwards of Four Years and also Says her said Husband was Born in the said Parish of Southwell

Sworn the 18th day of Augt. 1761 Before} Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Chapman
