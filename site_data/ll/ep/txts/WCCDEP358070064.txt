Westmr. to wit Joan Barnard Aged about Ninety Years on her Says her Husband William Barnard Rented and lived in a House in Leicester Fields in the Parish of Saint Anns So ho in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the same about Twelve Months ago and hath not since she quitted same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th. day of January 1773 before me}

Vagt. Pass
