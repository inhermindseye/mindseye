Westmr.  David Hopkins aged about 58 Years Upon Oath says he Rented a house in Leather Lane in the Liberty of Saffron Hill  Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds lived therein about three Months and quitted same about Ten Years ago and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act whereby to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn the 5th. day of Novr, 1767 , before  Paull Vaillant  Fran Cary

David Hopkins
