Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Talboys One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex on behalf of Ann North an Infant of the Age of Nine years or thereabouts Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been credibly Informed which Information he verily believes to be true That William North the Father of the said Ann North Rented and lived in a house in Lumber Court in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields for about Twelve Months at the yearly Rent of Twenty Six Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Twelve Months since which time he has not done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 24th. day of Octr. 1789 J Addington Tho. Vaughan

James Talboys
