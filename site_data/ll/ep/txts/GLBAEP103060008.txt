Middx  Ann Sheppard  wife of Edwd. Sheppard (who is in parts beyond the Seas) Maketh oath that About Eleven Years ago She was Married to her Said husband (who is within mentioned and that She hath four Children (that is to say) Elizabeth aged about Nine years, Oliver Edwd . aged abt Eight years, Mary aged abt. Six years, and Robt. Austin aged abt- Five years, the lawfull Issue of this Dept. and her said husband

Sworn this 24th day of Janry. 1742 Nathl. Chandler Rd- Ricards

The mark of Ann Ann Shepherd
