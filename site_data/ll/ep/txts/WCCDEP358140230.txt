Middx  Sarah Ward aged abt. 56 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Ward who dyed in the Year 1739. Says that being a widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. James Plaxton Marshall Attorney at Law in Clements Inn in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for 5 Years at the Yearly Wages of 4 Guinea's and hath quitted that service abt. 13 Yrs. and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise done any Act whereby to gain another Settlemt.

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Sarah Ward
