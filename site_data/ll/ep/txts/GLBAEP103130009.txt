Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Crooks the wife of  William Crooks who is lately gone away from her to Places unknown maketh oath that she can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her said husband or of his Parents that she this Deponent was born in the Parish of St. James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex where her late Father Benjamin Waters deced also rented a house of the yearly rent of Twenty Pounds as she this Deponent has been informed by him and believes & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there and that he did not afterwards to her knowledge or belief do any act to gain a Settlement elsewhere that she this Deponent never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever bound apprentice nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement for herself save what she Acquired by Virtue of her Marriage with her said husband whose Settlement is unknown to her that she this Deponent hath a child named William George Crooks aged about Thirteen months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband that she this Deponent well knows a Child named Ann Crooks the Daughter of her said husband by a former Wife deced aged about six years and who as this Deponent has been Credibly informed & verily believes was born in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the said County of Middlesex & has done no act to gain a settlement for herself & is likely to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the said County and this Deponent can give no Account respecting the Settlement of such last mentioned Child's Mother.

Sworn this 7th. day of September 1773 before B Camper Jno. Sherwood

The Mark of Elizabeth Crooks
