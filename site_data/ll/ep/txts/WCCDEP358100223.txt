Middx to wit Robert Watson aged about thirty four Years on his Oath Says he never was Married Rented or ever Rented House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes Says he lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Payne a Doctor & Apothecary the Corner of Coventy Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Twelve Pounds besides Diet & Lodging hath quitted his said Service about three Years ago since which time he Never lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any other Settlement.

Sworn this 19th. day of June 1784 before  Sampr Wright  Thos: Gilbert

Robert Watson
