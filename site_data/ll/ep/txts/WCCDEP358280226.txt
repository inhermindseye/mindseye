Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Catherine Hitchcock aged about 22 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Cox in Boswell Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Five Months since which time she has not done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one John Dowham who is Clerk to the said Mr. Cox is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of August last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Bastard Child or Children of which she is so pregnant and that the said John Dowham is the only real and true father thereof.

Sworn this 7th. day of April 1789 } R Butler

Catherine Hitchcock

Catherine Hitchcock lodges at Mr. Fletchers in Carey Street .

John Dowham is a Tall Thin Man dark Complexion wears his own Hair Tied behind

Mark taken Release Exted. £15..0..0 Paid.
