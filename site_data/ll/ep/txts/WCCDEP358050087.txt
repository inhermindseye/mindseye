Westmr.  Mary Davies Wido. of Thomas Davies who dyed abt. 8 Years since upon Oath Saith she Intermarried with the sd. Thos. Davies at a Church near St. Pauls Church London about 15 Years ago that the sd. Thos. Davies Served his Apprentiship with his Uncle Thomas Davies a Taylor in Old Boswell Court in The Parish of St. Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and that her sd. late husband nor this Examint. since his Decease never rented Ten pounds a Yr. pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant with any Person for 1 Yr. or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn augt 6th. 1749 before} Dan Gach

The mark of Mary Davies
