Middx to wit Elizth. Lyre aged about 24 years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Holloway an attorney in Breams Buildings in the Liberty of the Rolls for one year at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas hath quitted her said Service upwards of 18 Months ago since which time she hath gained no or. Settlement And further on her Oath says that on the 26th. day of Feby. 1782 she was delivered of a Female Bastard at Mr. Gents in Denzell Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . which has since been baptized by the name of Lydia and is become Chargable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor & unable to maintain the same And further says that one Wm Thos- Wilby late a Clerk to Mr. Holloway in Breams Buildings and who now lives at one Mrs. Jordans Opposite the Goat & Compasses Tottenham Court Road is the Father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body at her said Masters House some time in the Month of May 1781 and at sevl. difft. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Female Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Thos. Wilby and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the real & true father thereof.

Sworn this 15th. day of Augt. 1783  Jno. Machin

Elizabeth Lyre
