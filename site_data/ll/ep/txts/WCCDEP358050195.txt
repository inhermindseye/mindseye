Westmr.  Janry 10th. 1749

Job Lee aged upwards of 33 Years upon his Oath saith that upwards of Nine Years ago he Intermarried with his Wife Rachell at a Church near Highworth in the County of Berks by whom he hath two Children (to wit) Job aged abt. 5 Years & Thomas aged rear two Years Says he was bound Apprentice to and Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. Jeremiah Lucas  Vintner in Clare Street was Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. and County of Middlesex and hath been out of his times abt. 15 Years and Further upon his Oath Saith that before his Intermarriage with his said Wife Rachell and Whilst he was a Singleman be lived as a Waiter with one William Townsend  Innkeeper at Staple hall at Whitney in the County of Oxford for five Years or thereabt. Says he had no wages but Mr. Townsend found him Dyet washing and Lodging for his sd. Service and he had in Lieu of Wages what Vails he could get besides the benefit of the Tap for which this Examinant paid his sd. Master

Six pounds Seven Shillings the brewing Says the Proffits arising from the Tap and the Vails of the house amounted to more than if he had been paid £5 a Yr. Wages

Sworn the Day and Year abovementioned before} Thos Lane

Job Lee
