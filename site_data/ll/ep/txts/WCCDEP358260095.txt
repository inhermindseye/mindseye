Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Hollingsworth aged about 62 years upon his Oath saith that he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one David Tibero a Jeweller in Saint Martins Le Grand in the Parish of Saint Ann Agnes in the City of London and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th- day of Decr. 1795 before us J Hind M Addington

Jno Hollingsworth
