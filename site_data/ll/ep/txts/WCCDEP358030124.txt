Middx  Martha Walker  Wido . of Henry Walker upon her Oath with she intermarried with the sd. Henry Walker at Fulham Church on the 30th. Day of March 1728 Says her sd. Husband lived in & rented a house in Henrietta Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covt. Garden in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £55 for the Space of Seven Years & upwards & left the same near Seven Years ago Says the sd. Henry Walker never after he left the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place Says she hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 2d. 1746 before  Tho Lane

The Mark of Martha Walker
