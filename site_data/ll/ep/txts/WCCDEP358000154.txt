St. Clement Danes

Middlesex 

She Passd. to St. Martins in the Field who appeald. it turn'd out she was bot. to Mr. Dunkerton the. duty pd. & Hamping an appeal session determd Indre ocd & Rent her to us back again (to wit) Elizabeth Kirby aged 21 Years or thereabouts when her Oath Saith That she never was Married Rented a House or paid Taxes or ever lived as an Hired Yearly Servant Saith That she was bound by Indenture of Apprenticeship to one Dunkerton of the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex for the Term of 3 Years. That she served about Eleven Months of the said Term and then ran away with the from the Service of the said Dunkerton since which time she this Examinant hath not done any  Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of Novr. 1785 before us Mr. Wright Mr Barnfather

her Elizth Kirby Mark
