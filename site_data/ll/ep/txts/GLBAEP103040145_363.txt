Middlesex ss.  Esther Cowling  Singlewoman maketh Oath that about Two Years and Eight Months ago she was hired as a Servant by the Year to Mr . John King of Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Leather Cutter at the Yearly Wages of Eight Pounds and has continued in his Service under Such hiring until this present time, and that she never was Married.

Sworn this 26th Septr. 1798 before Danl Williams

her Esther Cowling Mark
