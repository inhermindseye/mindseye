Middx  Eliz: Gough  Widow upon her Oath saith that she intermarried with her late Husband  Saml. Gough about 22 Years since at the Parish Church of St. Martin in the Feilds that the sd. Wm. Gough lived in & rented a house in the Alphabett Court in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Year 1729 & paid Six Shillings & Nine pence towards the Poors Rate for the said Year that since he left the sd. House the sd. Samuel Gough in his Life time or this Examinant since his Death have not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt: in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif that she hath one Child viz: Thomas aged Eleven Yrs. & upwards & being Poor is not able to maintain herself & Child

Sworne June 9th: 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Elizabeth Gough
