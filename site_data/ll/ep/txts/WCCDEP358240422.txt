Saint Clement Danes . June 18th. 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement in St C D }  (to wit) Ann Wormington  Widow aged about 73 years Upon Oath saith that she was Married to Charles Wormington her late Husband at the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes several years ago (by License) that Subsequent thereto to his Death she Rented and resided in a House in Little Shire Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for about 20 years at the Annual Rent of £14 that She quitted the same about several years ago and hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of her knowledge Information or belief

Sworn the 18th day of June 1794 before me } Wm Kitchiner

Ann Wormington 's Mark

Witness Garrett
