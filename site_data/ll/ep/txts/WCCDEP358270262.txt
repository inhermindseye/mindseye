Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Mary Haynes aged about 19 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Harris in Blackmoor Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for fourteen Months at the yearly wages of Four Guineas that she hath quitted said service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent settlement And this Deponent further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one James Randall  Junr who lives with Mr. Thomas Randall his father a Farmer at Kent house at Beckingham in the County of Kent is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in or about the Month of October last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said James Randall is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 3rd day of March 1796 } M Addington

her Mary Haynes Mark

Man paid on the 8th. March 1796

£20. and his father Thomas Randall Executed a Release

Child born in the house 10th. Septr. 1796 named Mary Randall Haynes Disd. the 30th. Septr. 1796 Qy. wher. any & what Allowance to the Mother
