Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Jane Edwards aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Conbson at the Kings Arms in Palace Yard in the Parish of Saint Margaret in the Liberty of Westminster for One year and five Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Three years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 11th day of Janry. 1797 R. Ford M Addington

her Jane Edwards Mark
