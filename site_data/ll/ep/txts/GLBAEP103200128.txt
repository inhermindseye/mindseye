Middlesex ss}  Jane Bland the wife of Richard Bland maketh oath that her sd husband is absent from her at sea & living as she Verily believes That divers Years ago her sd husband lived in & rented a house of twelve pounds a year in St Catherines Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to & paid Poors Rates for the same that he has not Since rented a house of ten pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain Subsequent Settlement & that She was lawfully married to her sd husband in Aldgate Church  Middl 

Sworn this. 19th. day of April 1784 before R Davies

The Mark of Jane Bland
