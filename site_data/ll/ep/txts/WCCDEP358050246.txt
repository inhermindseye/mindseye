Middx  Sarah Parker upon Oath saith she intermarried with her present husband John Parker in the Parish Church of St. Andrew in Holborn on the 10th. Septr. 1741 by whom she hath three Childn. (viz) Eliz . aged upwds. of 7 yrs. John aged upwds. of 6 Yrs. and George aged upwds. of 2 Yrs. Says her sd. husband lived in and rented a house in witch Street in the parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £20 for Six months or thereabouts and hath Quitted the sd. house abt. two Years; since which time he has not rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif

Sworn May 4th 1750 before} Geo: Enington

The mark of Sarh. Parker
