Middlesex ss.  Susannah Wallace the Wife of William Wallace late a Seaman on Board the Oporto but now a Prisoner of War at Orleans maketh Oath that she was Married to her sd. Husband at Cork in Ireland the Twenty Sixth Day of Decr. 1781 . that her said Husband was born in Scotland and has never been an Apprentice or lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any Parish or Place for the space of One Year together or Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in England, that she this Deponent was born in Ireland & never did any Act to her knowledge or belief to gain a settlement in her own right (except her Marriage as aforesd.) that she has Three Children Namely James aged about Twelve Years who was taken Prisoner with his Father, Adam aged Five years the Twenty first day of Jany . last who was born in Angel Court in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate Middlesex , and Jane aged about Two years who was born in Maidinhead Court in the Parish of St. John Wapping the lawful issue by her said Husband

Sworn this Day of 1798. before}
