Middx  Duke Dotchin aged Eighty One Years upon his Oath saith that he lived in and Rented a House in Montague Alley in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds for the Space of Twenty Years & upwds. that since he left the sd. House he hath not rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleife

Sworne the 14th. of Novr. 1741 before}

The mark of Duke Dotchin
