Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Mould Aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith, that she never was married rented House or paid Taxes, that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Backhofner of the Strand Jeweller in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Thirteen Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas hath quitted said Service about Seven years ago since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her knowledge Information belief she may have gained any Subsequent Settlement And further on her Voluntary Oath saith that on the Eighteenth Day of March last past she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in Saint Georges Hospital which hath since been Christened by the Name of Amelia Elizabeth That one James Dixon a Pawnbroker in the Strand is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said James Dixon and no one else did beget the said Child and that he is the only real and true father thereof And that she is Poor and unable to Maintain the same.

Sworn this 24th. day of May 1786 before me} R Butler

Jane Mold

24 May made out reant.- gave to Mr Smart Dixon lives in the Strand is a Short thick Man Dark Complexion wears his own Hair tyed behind

Jane Mold Lodges at Mrs. Millers at Mr. Stones Hackney Man Hole in the Wale Hart Street Bloomsbury ; 24th. Dixon Attendd-postpond to Friday 26. Attendd. and Payd Mr Smart £25: Release Exted Ct.

what done with Childs: ( Amelia Eliz Mould ) born 18 March 1786 : sent to Nurse at Enfield
