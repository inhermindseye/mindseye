Middx & Westmr-}  John Barker aged about fifty Eight Years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Violett his wife at the Parish Church of St. Giles in the Fields about 23 years ago and by whom he hath One Child now provided for And this Examinant further Says that he Rented and lived in a House in Exeter Street in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westmr . for One year & an half at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Three Pounds & paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Seven Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settemt. either by Renting House Tenement or Lodgings of £10 a year or otherwise Howsoever.

Sworn this 30th. day of Octr. 1782 before  Sampn Wright W Gregson

John Barker
