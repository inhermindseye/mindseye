Middlesex ss}  John Fisher maketh oath that about Sixteen years ago he lived in & rented a house in Crown Court in the Parish of St George in the Sd County at the Yearly Rent of Four Pounds & resided thereon for about the Space of two Years & during Such his residence there he was Charged to & Paid Kings for the Same to Mr Meader the then Collector of Such Tax and the receipts for the Same were delivered up by this Deponent to his Landlord on payment of his Rent And this Deponent Afterwards lived in Anothr house in Glass house Yard in the Same Parish Where he also paid Kings Taxes for the Same That he has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served Any Parish Office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlemt elsewhere & that he hath a wife named Sarah to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this fourth day of January 1786 before R Davies Robt: Smith

The Mark of John Fisher
