Middx  Elizth. Turner aged abt. Forty five Years upon Oath says She lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year with Mrs. Hoblin on Richmond Hill in the Parish of Richmond in the County of Surry at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds Seven Shillings & hath quitted her said Service abt. Three Years since wch. time She hath not Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 21st. Augst. 1756 . before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz. Turner
