Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Margarett Bell aged about 14 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or Paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Richard Bell her Uncle in Stanhope Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for 16 Months at the yearly Wages of £4..10..0 hath quitted said Service about Three years ago since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Ext. saith that on the 1st. day of Decr. Inst. she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Workhouse of this Parish which hath since been baptized by the name of Harriett That one Thos. Vobe a Waiter who lately lived at the Devil Tavern in Fleet Street is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Exts. Body sometime in the Month of April last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid And that the said Thomas Vobe is the only real & true father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of Decr. 1786 } R Butler

her Margt. Bell mark

Child born on the 2d. Decr. 1786 Sent to Nurse at Enfield the 12th. Janry. 1787
