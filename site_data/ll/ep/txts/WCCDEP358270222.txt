Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Fairbrother aged about 29 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired Yearly servant for Twelve Months or otherwise obtained any legal settlement in her own Right That she has been informed which Information she verily believes to be true that her father is a Parishioner of the Parish of Epsom in the County of Surrey by living as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Stewart at Horton within the said Parish while he was single and unmarried And this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that on the 5th. day of August last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at a house belonging to Mr. Richardson in Clements Lane which hath since been Christened by the Name of Catherine That one Richard Middleton a Post Boy who lives at the Sign of the Horse Shoe and Magpye in Burleigh Street in the Strand is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Richard Middleton and no other Person did beget the same and that he is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 2nd day of Janry 1795 } N Bond

her Sarah Fairbrother Mark
