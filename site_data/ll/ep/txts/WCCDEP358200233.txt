Middx  Richard Parker Aged about Fifty Six Years upon Oath Says he was never Marryed Says he Rented a House in Old Brandford in the Parish of Great Eling in the County of Middx One Year & upwards at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted said House about Twenty Six Years & hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year.

Sworn the 4th of Feby 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant

Richd. Parker
