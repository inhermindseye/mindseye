Middlesex ss  Ann Rigby  Single woman Maketh oath that about thirteen Years ago she was duly bound apprentice by Indre to Edward Boxhall of the Parish of St Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry  Pinmaker with whom she served about five years in the same Parish and then left her said Master And this Deponent saith that she hath not since been an Apprentice nor a hired servant by the year Nor hath done any Act to her knowledge or belief to Acquire any settlement subsequent to her said Apprenticeship

Sworn this 12th Day of December 1758 before us  Richd: Ricards  Walter Berry

her Ann Rigby Mark
