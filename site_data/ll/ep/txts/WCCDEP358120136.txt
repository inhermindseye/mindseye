Middx  Cathn. Walker aged abt. 58 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that she lived a yearly Servant with Mrs. Smallwood at the Sugar Leaf in Houghton Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes for Seven Two Years at the yearly Wages of Three pounds and has left her sd Service about three Years: Since which she has not rented ten pounds by the year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn June 29th 1754 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Cath Walker
