Middlesex to wit Elizth. Mercy aged about Fifty Two Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of Philip Mercy with who she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Martin the Fields about thirteen Years ago and who died about S Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living Say her said late Husband Rented House in the Strand the sd Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields for several Years at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Two Years ago and after quitting the same he never Rented Ho or Tenement of Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes or other gained any other Subsequent Settlement either hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death

Sworn this 9th. day of March 1782 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Elizth. Mercy Mark
