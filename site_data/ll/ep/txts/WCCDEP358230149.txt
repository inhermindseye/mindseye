Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Smart on behalf of Charles Smith an Infant aged about Sixteen Months upon her Oath Saith That she hath been informed which Information she verily believes to be true That the said Charles Smith in the legitimate Child of Canyers and ann Smith who rented a house in Brewer Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for two years at the yearly rent of Thirty Pounds That he hath left the said House about ten years since which time he hath not rented any house tenement or Lodging of ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or Lane any Act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 14th day of August 1800 before us} John Collick  Benjn Gee

Elizabeth Smart her Mark
