Middx  Mary B y eard upon Oath Saith she is the Wido. of Henry B y eard who has been dead near 4 years and that her sd. late husband (as she has been informed & verily believes Served his Apprentiship with John Storey a Blacksmith at Walham Green in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex and never rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year no has this Examinant since his Decease rented ten Pounds a year paid parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn 19th. Janry 1754 before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Beard
