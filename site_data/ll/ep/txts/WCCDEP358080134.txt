Middx & Westmr.}  Leonard Jones Aged about 46 Years on behalf of his Wife  Ann Jones who is now Insane on his Oath says he Intermarrd- with his said Wife at Shoreditch Church about 5 Years ago and by whom he hath no Children now living Says he is a Native of Ireland and never gained a Settlement in England either by renting house paying Taxes or living as a hired Yearly Servant before Marriage for one Years says that before his said Intermarrd- with his said Wife she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr- Rallans a Cabinet Maker in Goodwins Court St- Martins Lane in the Parish of St- Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westmr - upwards of one Year at the Yearly Wages of £4 10s hath quitted the said Mr- Rallans Service about 15 Years ago since which time this Examinant or his sd. Wife never gained any other subseqt- Settlemt-

Sworn this 2nd- Day of April 1777 before me  Jno. Machin  Geo: Reid

Leonard Jones
