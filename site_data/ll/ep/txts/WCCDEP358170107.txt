Middx  Ann Hunt  Singlewoman aged abt. Twenty Four Years upon her Oath saith that her Father Leonard Hunt rented the Slaughter House in St. Clements Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of forty Pounds & lived in the same many Years & there dyed & that her Mother Sarah Hunt continued to live in & rent the same House after the Decease of her Father Severall Years & further saith that she never was an Apprentice rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived a Yearly hired Servt. with any Person for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt- in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being sick & Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Twelfth. Day of August 1741 before}
