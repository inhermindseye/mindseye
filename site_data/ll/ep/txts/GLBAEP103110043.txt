Middlesex ss}  Susanna English the Widow of Joseph English deced Maketh oath That She hath been Credibly informed & which information She believes to be true That about Twenty three Years Ago her Said husband was bound apprentice by Indre for Ten Years to Ralph Foster of Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex and that he Continued to Lodge and Board with his Said Master till he died which was about Nine Years That after the Death of his said Master one Thomas Wright took the said house with whom her Said husband Served the Remainder of his Apprenticeship and Continued to Lodge and Board with the said Thomas Wright in the Said house and to this Deponts. Knowledge or belief her Said husband never did any Act to gain any Settlement Subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship Nor hath She this Depont. Since her said husband Death done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right

Sworn this 4 day of March 1772 before  Rob Pell  Cha: Digby

The mark of Susanna English
