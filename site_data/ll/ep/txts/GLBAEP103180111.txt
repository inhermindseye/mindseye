Middlesex ss}  Margaret Smart  Singlewoman maketh oath That She is the Daughter of Peter Smart deced by Isabella his wife That her Said late Fathr Sometime ago rented a house in Lower Eastsmithfd in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the said County of the yearly Rent of Ten pounds & upwards & was Charged to & Paid Poors Rates & other Taxes for the Same & died there legally Settled That her Sd Mother to her knowledge or belief has not Since her Said Fathers death done any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself That She this Deponent who lived with her Said Fathr as part of his Family in the Said Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate has not Since his death lived as a hired servant for a year or been bound an Apprentice or was She ever Married or done any Act to gain a Settlemt in her own right

Sworn this 15 day of December 1778 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Margaret Smart
