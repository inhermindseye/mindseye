Middlesex ss}  Hisemayne Bermingham maketh oath that Sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Thomas Fellows in the Parish of St Olave in Hart Street in the City of London & Continued in such Service under such hiring for the Space of a year & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the space of a year elsewhere or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 10. day of April 1784 before R Davies Robt. Smith

hisemayner Bermingham
