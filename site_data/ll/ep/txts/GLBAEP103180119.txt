Middlesex ss}  Margaret Hubbard the widow of John Hubbard deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true That he was duly bound to & Served the Whole of his apprenticeship with his Grandfather Roger Brewer a Waterman in Pepper Alley in the Parish of Saint Saviour in Southwark in the County of Surry That her Sd. late husband to her knowledge or belief has not Since Such his apprenticeship rented a house of Ten Pounds. a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That She hath a Child named Margaret aged about Four years & three Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 19. day of January 1779 before P. Rowlands Jno Sherwood

Margaret Hubbard
