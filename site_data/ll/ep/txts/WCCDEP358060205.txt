Middx  John Ilger aged abt. 30 Yrs upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his first wife  Ann on or about the 24th of Octr. 1743 & hath one Child by her now Living (to wit) Wm . aged about 5 Years Says he intermarried with his prest. Wife  Ann on or about the 9th. Day of May 1749 & hath one Child by her now living (to wit) Mary aged about one Year & a half Says before his Intermarriage with his first wife & Whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servt . with one Mrs. Lyes at the Sign of the Hole in the Wall in Vear Street in the Parish of St Clemt Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx for one year & upwds. at the yearly Wages of Three pounds & Soon after intermarried with his sd. first wife Ann Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his) knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 13th 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Jno Ilger
