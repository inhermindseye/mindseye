Middx.  Eliz. Kirk aged abt. 35 Years upon Oath says she was Married at the fleet London abt. Eight Years ago to one Giles Sturgis who is now dead, by whom she hath one Child Ellizabeth aged abt. seven Years who was born in the house of Mr. Savage next door to the Vine Tavern in Holborn in the Parish of St. George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx. says at the time of her said Marriage she said Giles Sturgis had a Wife living and that she then was the Widow of Stephen Kirk deceased who Rented a house in Stanhope Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx. for 5 Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of £26. says she has not since her said husbands death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn 6th. Decr. 1759 . before} Barth: Hammond  Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz: Kirk

Upon Mr Burnthwaites Complt. J: P:
