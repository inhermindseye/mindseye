Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Brewer aged about 29 Years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married That she lived as a hired yearly with Mr. Campbells of Soho Square for Two years and upwards at the yearly wages of Nine Guineas that the last Forty days of her said Service was at Mr. Campbells Country house at Fulham in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middlesex that she hath quitted said Service upwards of Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired Yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 23d. day of Feby. 1796 N Bond Rt Thompson

her Sarah Brewer Mark

Witness B Newman
