Middlesex & Westmr. }  Sarah Mears Aged about Sixty Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr Asheton Ally in Brooke Street Holborn for fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Six pounds hath quitted her said Service about 40 Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same done any Act whereby to gain a subseqt. Settlement

Sworn this 9th. Day of Octr. 1776 before R Butler M Addington

Sarah Meres
