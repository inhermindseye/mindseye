Middx  Jane Sommers upon Oath says she was never married Says on the 14th. Day of Septr. about 12 Yrs. since she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child at Mrs. Geatleys in Ship Yard in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx since baptized John now liveing & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish And further Says about 4 Yrs. since she was delivered of a female Bastard Child at Mr. Norburys in Vere Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes since Baptized & now living & become Chargeable to the sd. Parish this Examinant being Sick Poor & not able to maintain them Says that Mr. Thomas Yeates formerley Clerk to Mr. David Thomas of Clements Inn did begett the said two Children on her Body the sd. Thos. Yeates & this Examinant living together as Man & Wife had Carnall knowledge of her Body and gott her with Child of the aforesaid Male & female Bastard Children of which she was delivered as aforesaid and that he the said

Middx  Thomas Yeates & no other Person is the true and reall Father thereof

Sworn March 1st. 1745 before
