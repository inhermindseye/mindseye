Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Eleanor Queen aged about 46 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with John Austin at the Sign of the Angel & Crown in Hayden Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster for Three Years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of Four Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Thirty Years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Febry. 1799 before us  Saml Edwards Menter

her Eleanor Queen Mark
