Middlesex ss}  Ann Robinson  Singlewoman maketh oath That She hath a Sister named Mary Robinson now in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County That her said Sister has informed her & Which this Deponent believes to be true that Sometime Ago this Deponents Said Sister was hired & lived as a hired servant at Certain Wages by and with one Captain Halfnight in Virginia Street in the Parish of Saint George in the Said County and Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of a year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish of Saint George That to this Deponents knowledge information or belief her Said Sister Mary Robinson hath not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a Year elsewhere nor was She ever Married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlemt

Sworn this. 9. day of November 1778 before}
