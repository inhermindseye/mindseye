Middx.  Ann Marchant aged abt. 26 Years upon Oath says she is the Wife of John Marchant to whom she was Married in the Parish Church of St. Mary Hill London on the 16th. May 1754. by whom she has two Children Elizabeth aged abt. four Years and Frances aged abt. four Months and that her said husband absconds from her and where to find him she does not know; says her said husbd. informed her he Rented a house of Mr Scriven in Vere Street Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. & County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds and lived in the same abt. Six Months and hath quitted same abt. Sixteen Years and has not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief

Sworn this 22d. June 1759 . before} Paul Vaillant

Ann Marchant
