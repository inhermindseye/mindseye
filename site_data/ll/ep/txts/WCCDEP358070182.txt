Westmr. to wit Abner Osman Aged about fifty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Rachael Osman now in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of St. George Southwark  in the County of Surry at the Parish Church of St. George  aforesaid about Twenty five Years ago and by whom he hath Two Children Namely Elizabeth Aged about Twenty Three Years and Rachael aged about Eighteen Years Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one William Frame a Cork Cutter in the Borough of Southwark  in the County of Surry and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Neither hath the said Elizabeth or Rachael his Children done any Act whereby to gain any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 19th. day of March 1774 before}  Sampn Wright

Abner Osman
