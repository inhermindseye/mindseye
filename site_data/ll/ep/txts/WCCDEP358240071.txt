Saint Clement Danes Febry. 13th 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St CD  (to wit) Thomas Bromley aged 60 years or thereabouts upon his Oath Saith That he was Married at Lambeth in this County of Surry in January 1790 to Hannah his present Wife That he rented a House for Seven Years at £30 or Year upwards in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes that he quitted same in the year 1768 that since that time he has done no Act Matter or Thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement to the best of his Knowledge or Belief

Sworn the 13th. day of Feby. 1792 before us.} Wm Kitchiner

Thos. Bromley
