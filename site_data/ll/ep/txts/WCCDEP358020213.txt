Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Redhead aged about 57 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Joseph Laver who was a Sheriff Officer in Chancery Lane in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middx for Three years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Ten years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 12th. day of July 1790 Sampn W Addington

her Ann Redhead mark
