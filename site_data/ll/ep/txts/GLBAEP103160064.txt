Middlesex ss}  Stephen Bartholomew maketh oath that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired servant by the year by & with one John Everal of the Victualling office in the Parish of St. Botolph Aldgate Sd County Victualler at Certain yearly wages & Continued in Such Service for the Space of Seven years & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That he has not since lived as a hired Servant elsewhere for the Space of a year or rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 15 day of May 1776 before P. Rowlands

The Mark of Stephen Bartholomew
