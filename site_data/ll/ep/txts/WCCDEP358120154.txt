Middx  Ann Twitty aged abt. 30 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and that (Jointly with her Sister Mary Twitty ) she rented a house near Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of £26. lived therein upwds. of one Year & quitted the sd house abt. five Years ago and that she has not since rented Ten Pounds by the Year pd. Parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one Year

Sworn July 20th. 1754 before Fran: Bedwell Ann Twittey
