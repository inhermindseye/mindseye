After Birth Elizabeth Leeme and Chas. Edmunds

St Clement Danes 30th July 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Woman's Settlement St CD }  Child Charles born an St CD}  Reputed Father (to wit) Elizabeth Leem aged 39 years and upon or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Breeden in Water Street in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for three years and upwards at the annual Wages of Six pounds that she quitted that service about twenty years ago and hath not Since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal settlement And this Examinant on her Oath further saith that she hath two illegitimate Children  by one John Edmunds heretofore of White Frairs London both born of the Body of this Deponent namely Maria Edmunds born in or about of August 1791 in Union Court within the Liberty of Hatton Garden Saffron Hill and Ely Roads  and Charles born on or about the fifth day of March last at the House of Mr Elliot in Water Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex that the said John Edmunds is the only real true father of her said Child named Charles  of which she was delivered  on or about the said fifth day  of March last in Water Street aforesaid he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body  on or about the Month of September or October 1791 and at Several times between that and the birth of the said Child named Charles and that she is at some or one of which times he did bege the same thereon And that he is the only real true father thereof And that there

Child is become chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes she this Examinant is be being herself unable to maintain it

Sworn the 30th. day of July 1793 } Wm. Kitchiner

Elizth. Witness Leem's Mark

Witness Richd Dobbins

this Man never taken
