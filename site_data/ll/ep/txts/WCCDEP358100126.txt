Middlesex to wit Edmund Kempster Aged about fifty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Susannah his now Wife at the Parish Church of St. Sepulchre  London about Thirty Two Years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children living Says he Rented and lived in a House late Called Princess Amelia Court near Duke Street  Marylebone in the Parish of St. Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Rent of Eleven Pounds hath quitted said House about Nine Years ago since which time he hath never Rented House Tenement or Lodgings of Ten pounds a Year or ever gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 23d day of Septr. 1783 before  James Paine J Bosworth

Edmd Kempster
