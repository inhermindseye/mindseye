Westmr.  Mary Mc.owen aged about 40 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of Roger Mc.owen who has been dead upwards of Two Months, says her said husband was an Irishman and never gained a Settlement in England to this Examinants knowledge or belief; says she served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mrs. Hosier a Glover in Wardour Street in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster and never Rented house, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 10th. day of Jany. 1766 . before} Paul Vaillant R Kilsha

The Mark of Mary Mc. owen
