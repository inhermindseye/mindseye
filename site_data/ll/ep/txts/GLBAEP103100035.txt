Middlesex ss}  Samuel Knott maketh oath That divers Years ago the rented a house of the yearly Rent and Value of Twenty Pounds next Door to the Sun Ale house in Rosemary Lane in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Country of Middlesex and Continued in the occupation of such house for divers years & was Charged to and paid Parochial Taxes for the Same & by Means thereof as he is advised gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish and this Depont. Saith that he has not since rented a house at the yearly rent or of the yearly Value of Ten Pounds or upwards nor served any Parish offices or paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes or lived as a hired servant for a year or done any Act to his knowledge or belief to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this Third day of August 1768 before  Chris: Scott

Saml. Knott.
