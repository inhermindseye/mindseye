Westmr. 

Tabitha Henshaw aged abt. 78 Yrs. upon Oath saith she is the Wido . of Jeremiah Henshaw who has been dead about 18 Yrs. Says she intermarried with the said Jeremiah Henshaw at the Parish Church of St Clemt. Danes  upwds. of 40 Yrs. ago Says her sd. late husband lived in and rented a house in Great Sheir Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of £16 for several Years & there dyed And this Examinant continued to live in & rent the sd. house for 2 or 3 Yrs. after the Death of her sd. late husband Says she has not since she quitted the sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or other- -wise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn 16th. May 1747 before  Wm: Jones

Tabitha Henshaw
