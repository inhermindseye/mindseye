Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Mead of Carey Street on behalf of John Mead his father who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that the said John Mead his father Rented and lived in a house in Little Russel Street in the Parish of Saint George Bloomsbury in the County of Middlesex for upwards of Thirty years at the yearly rent of Ten Pounds and upwards that he quitted said house about five years since which time he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of Novr. 1796 M Addington Rusl: Clarke J Mead
