Middx  July 9th. 1754 Martha Murphy aged upwds. of 60 Years upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Thomas Murphy who dyed never Nine Years ago and that about five Years ago being than a Wido . & without Child or Children She lived herself a Servant to Mr Woodley a Taylor at the Yearly Wages of Land lived with him under that Agreement in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes near three Years & has not Since she left her sd. Service rented ten pounds by the Year pd. Parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servt for one Year.

Sworn the Day & Year abovementioned before}
