Middlesex ss}  Esther Votier the Wife of Jonathan Votier Who is gone away from her to Places unknown maketh oath That her Said husband has informed her That he was bound apprentice to one Campbell a Perukemaker who then lived in Tooley Street in the Parish of St. Olave in Southwarke in the County of Surry but he never Made Known to her how long he Served his Master as his apprentice nor doth She know nor hath She ever been informed by any other Person how long her Said husband Served of his Apprenticeship That She Can give no Account respectg. the Settlemt. of her sd husbands Parents or either of them That her Said husband has informed her (& which information She believes to be true) That he was born in Hanging Sword Alley in Water Lane in the Parish of St. Bridget in the City of London That to her Knowledge Information or belief her said husband never lived as a hired Servant for a year nor ever ever Rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office or Paid any Poor Rates or Kings Taxes nor Can She Setforth whether he ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right & That She hath a Child Named Hannah Votier Aged Six Years & upwards the lawful Issue of this Depont. by her Said husband

Sworn this 22d day of November 1771 before B Camper Wilmot

The Mark of Esther Votier
