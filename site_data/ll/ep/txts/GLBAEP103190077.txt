Middlesex ss}  Mary Darber the wife of William Darber who is now absent from her on board his Majesties Ship the Prince George maketh oath That her said husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he duly Served the whole of his apprentice Ship with a Shoemaker in the Parish of St Margaret in Reading in the County of Berks & to her Knowledge or belief he has not Since done any Act to gain anothr Settlement & that She was Married to her said husband at the Fleet in the year 1745

Sworn this 13. day of May 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Darber
