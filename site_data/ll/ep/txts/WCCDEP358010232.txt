Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Lucy Taylor aged about 25 years upon her Oath saith that about 3 years ago she Intermarried with Thomas her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes that her said Husband has been dead about Five Months that her said late Husband served Seven years Apprenticeship to a Mr. Clarkson in Windmill Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster Plater that after he was Out of his said Apprenticeship he never did any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That this Examinant has Two Children living by her said late Husband Vizt. John aged about Two years and Ann aged about 4 Months

Sworn this 8th. day of Augt. 1788  Philip Dyot D Walker

her Lucy Taylor Mark
