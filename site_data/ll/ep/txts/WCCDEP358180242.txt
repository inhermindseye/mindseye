Middx  Mary Price aged about 28 Years upon her Voluntary Oath saith she is a wido. and that on the 15th. Day of Septr. last she was Delivered of a Female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Parker in Milford Lane in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex which hath since been Baptized Mary & is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish St Clement Danes she being Poor & Unable to maintain the same And that Mr. John Nichols  Clerk to Mr. Benton in Sheir Lane did begett the sd. female Bastard Child on her Body this Examinant liveing a Servt. with the said Mr Benton where the sd. John Nichols became Acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body on the 8th. Day of January last in the house of the sd. Mr. Benton & at sevll. other times since at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. female Bastard Child of which she was Delivered as aforesaid & that he the sd. John Nichols & no other Person is the true & Reall Father thereof Says she lived a Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Clitter a Coach Maker in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds for one Year at the Yearly Wages of £5: & hath left her sd. Service about Two Years Says she hath not since rented £10 a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt: for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne Octr. 29th. 1744 before} Jas: Fraser  Wm: Jones

Mary Pryce
