Middlesex ss}  Thomas Reeve maketh oath That he knows one Mary Robinson a Singlewoman now in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County That the Said Mary Robinson as this Deponent has been Credibly informed & believes Sometime ago was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with one Captain halfnight in Virginia Street in the Parish of Saint George in the Said County & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of a year & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish of Saint George That to this Deponents knowledge information or belief the said Mary Robinson hath not Since lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 9. day of November 1778 before  Jasper Clarkey  Jno Sherwood

Thos Reeve

Poor and must of necessity be Relieved by the Parish of St Botolph Aldgate where they are come to Want as that Parish can not Remove them to any other as the place of their last legal Settlement

John Silvester Carey Street 17 Nov. 1778
