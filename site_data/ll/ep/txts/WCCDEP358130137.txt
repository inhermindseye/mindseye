Middx  Christian Audley Aged abt. Thirty Eight Years Upon Oath Says she is the Widw . of Robt. Audely deced Says her sd. late Husband informed her & with informaton she beleives to true that her Served Seven Years Apprentiship with his father at Grantham in the County of Lincoln Says he never Rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or was a hired Yearly Servt. for one Year Say she hath not Since his Death rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Sevt. for one Year

Sworn the 8th. Day of April 1756 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Christian Audley
