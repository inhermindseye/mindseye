St. Clement Danes

Swears to St. Clements by Service with One Montgomery Strand  Hannah Staden aged 35 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she never was Married, never Rented a House or paid Taxes never served an apprenticeship Saith That she lived an as an hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Montgomery of the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clemt. Danes in the County of Midd Persons or for upwards of 12 Months at the yearly Wages of £5 hath quitted her said Service about 6 Years ago since which time she this Examinant hath not gained any subsequent settlemt. to the Best of her Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 9th. day of Dec. 1785 before me R Butler

the Mark of Hann Maden
