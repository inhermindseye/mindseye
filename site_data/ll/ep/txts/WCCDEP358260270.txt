Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit James Ashley aged about 63 years upon his Oath saith that he served Seven years to one John Bedell a Waterman who lived in the Pye Gardens in the Parish of Saint Saviour Southwark That after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he might have gained a subsequent Settlement and he is now Chargable to the Parish of St Clement Danes

Sworn this 26th. day of April 1798 before us W Addington Robinson

his James Ashley Mark

Witness A W Burbidge Beadle
