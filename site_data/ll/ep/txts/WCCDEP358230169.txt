Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster }  to wit Adam Wilcox Burbidge one of the Beadles belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of John Horwood an Infant aged about 12 years upon his Oath saith That he hath been Informed which Information he verily believes to be true That the said John Horwood was born in Margeretts Street Colchester in the County of Essex That he never was bound apprentice or did any act matter or thing whereby he may have gained a Settlement in his own right to the best of this Deponent Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 20th day of Decr 1800 before us} Saml. Edwards  Benjn. Gee

AW Burbidge
