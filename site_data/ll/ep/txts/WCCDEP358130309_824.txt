Middx  Margt. Dry aged abt. 60 yrs. upon Oath says she way never married & that she lived a hired yearly Servt. with Mr. Done at the Swan Tavern in the Strand in the Parish of St. Mary Le Strand in the County Liberty of Westmr . for one year & upwds. at the yrly. Wages of Twelve Pounds

Sworn the day of March 1757 before}
