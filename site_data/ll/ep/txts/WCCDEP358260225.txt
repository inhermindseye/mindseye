Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit William Hosmer aged about 27 years upon his Oath saith that about Six years ago he Intermarried with Charlotte his present Wife at the Old Church in Deptford by whom he hath One Child living Vizt: Ann aged about Three years That he this Deponent Served Seven years Apprenticeship under Indentures duly executed to one Mr. Bilby a Carpenter in King Street Holborn That he this Deponent Slept at the House of a Mr. Howard in Charlton Street Sommers Town in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex at the Expiration of his said Apprenticeship and had done so for Twelve months or thereabouts previous, by and with the knowledge and Consent of his said Master.

Sworn this 21st.. day of Octr. 1797 before me M Addington W Carter

William Hosmer

H Day Beadle
