Middlesex ss}  Mary Bagnall the wife of William Bagnall (who is at Present disordered in his Senses) maketh oath That her said husband hath informed her (& which information she believes to be true) That he was born in the Parish of Wellington in the County of Durham And this Depont. doth not know nor Can Set forth whether her said husband were did any Act to gain a Settlement in his own right Save that this Deponts. husband has informed her that he was bound to and Served Part of his Apprenticeship with his Brother Bagnall in the Same Parish of Wellington

Sworn this day of January 1769 before}
