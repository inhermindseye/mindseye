St Clement Danes June 20th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  St C D  (to wit) George Bartholomew aged 37 years and upwards Upon Oath saith that he was Married to his present Wife Mary at the parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch on or about the seventeenth day of April 1775 or 1776 (by Banns) that previous thereto this Examinant served his Apprenticeship under Indenture with William Rosser in Wych Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the said Liberty and County and his Widow that his Apprenticeship expired about October in the same year that he Married & that he hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement that he hath five Children by his said Wife Namely George aged 13 years on the 9th day of November last Elizabeth born on the 23d day of April 1786 , William born on the in or about the Month of June or July 1787 Ann born on or about the 28th day of May 1790 and Thomas born on or about the 14th day of December 1791

Sworn the 20th. day of June 1793 before Me} Wm Kitchiner

George Bartholomew 's Mark

Witness Garrett
