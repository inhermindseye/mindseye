Middx to wit Edward Langbridge aged about fifty Nine Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to Jane his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex about Nine Years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children living Says he Rented and lived in a House in Little Stanhope Street May Fair in the aforesaid Parish of Saint George for sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Four Pounds & paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Three Years ago since which time he hath gained no other Settlement either Renting House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 30th. day of October 1781 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Edwd Langbridge
