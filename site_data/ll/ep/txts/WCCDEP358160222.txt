Middx  The Examination of Elizth Tarver lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 24th- Day of Decr. 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that abt. 26 Years ago she lived a Yearly hired Servt . with one Mrs. Allcon in Arundle Street in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt- Danes for the Space of 2 Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £5. & further on her Oath saith yt. since that times She never rented £10 a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Yr. or otherwise gain'd a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the Day & Year above mencon'd before}

eli tarver
