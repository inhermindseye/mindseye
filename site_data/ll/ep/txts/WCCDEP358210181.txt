Middx & Westmr.  Susanna Lloyd aged about 30 Years upon Oath says She never was married and that She lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Mr. Moore at the Castle at Hammersmith in the County of Middlesex upwards of three years at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds, quitted said Service about two Years and hath not since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year and further upon her Oath says that on or about the twelfth day of August 1765 She was delivered of a female Bastard Child in the House of Mr. Anderson in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the sd. County of Middlesex which hath since been baptized by the Name of Elizabeth ; says that Paul Headlin otherwise Edlin late, a Waiter at the Griffin Tavern in Holborn is the Father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of her Body several times in the House of the sd. Mr. Moore and that he and no other person did beget the same

Sworn the 24th. day of August 1767 before} Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Susanna Lloyd
