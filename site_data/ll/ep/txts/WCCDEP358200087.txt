Middx  Mary Casady  Widow Aged upwards of Seventy Years Lodging in Change Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex Upon Oath Says that her Late Husband Patrick Casady was a Native of Ireland and never Gained a Settlement in England as she has been Informed and which Information she Verily Believes to be true Says she Cannot give any Account of the Settlement of her Father other then he was Born in Wales but in what Part she Knoweth not Says she was Born in Brewers Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the

Fields in the Liberty of Westminster Says she Never Rented House Paid Taxes was an Apprentice or a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 15th: Day of March 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Casady

Upon Mr Jones Complt Ovr. J. W
