Middlesex ss}  Sarah Smith the widow of Thomas Smith deced & Elizabeth Smith her Daughter Severally make oath & first the Said Sarah Smith the Mother for herself Saith that her Said late husband has informed her & Which she believes to be true that he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain Wages with one Mrs Cook in Hummerton in the Parish of Saint John Hackney in the Sd County & Continued in such Service for many Years & by means theerof gained a legal Settlement there That he did not afterwards live elsewhere as a hired Servant for a year or Rent a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or serve any Parish office or pay any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That the Said other Deponent Sarah Smith is the Daughter & lawful Issue of this Depont by her Said late husband & has not done any Act to this Deponents knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for herself And the Said Deponent Sarah Smith the Daughter for herself Saith that She was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right That about three years ago She this Deponent was delivered of a Female Child Since baptised & registered by the name of Elizabeth Cormick in Cock Alley in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Which Child is illegitimate & Bastard by reason that She this Deponent was never married And that one John Cormick of the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate  Porter is the Father of the Said Child.

Sworn this 3d. day of April 1776 before  Geo. C. Smith  Parker Rowlands
