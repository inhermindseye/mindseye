Middx  Sarah Dowling aged abt 26 Years upon Oath says She intermarried with Nicholas Dowling on the Ninth day of July 1758 by whom she hath one Child named Nicholas aged near two Years Says her husband is a Native of Ireland and never gaind any Settlement in England that before her intermariage with the Sd. Nicholas She was bound Apprentice to and served seven Years Apprentiship with Mr Mary Shuckforth a Milliner in Southampton Street in the parish of St George Bloomsbury in the County of Middx that her said Husband is now a Soldier in Collonel Berrys Regiment.

Sworn the 5th. June 1762 before} Fran: Bedwell

Sarah Dowling
