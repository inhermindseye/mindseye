Westmr. to wit Martha Hodges Aged about Twenty One Years on her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House or ever served any Apprenticeship Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. John Gibbs a Chymist in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year at the Yearly Wages of four pounds hath quitted the sd. last mentioned Service about a Year ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30th. day of March 1772 before} R. Butler 

Martha Hodges her mark
