Westmr.  John Gardner aged about 37 Years Upon Oath says he intermarried with Caroline his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes on or about the 5th. day of Septr. 1764 by whom he hath one Child Martha aged about ten Weeks; says he never Rented Ten pounds a Year paid Taxes was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year, says his Father Robt. Gardner served Seven Years Apprenticeship with this Examinants Grand father Henry Gardner a Butcher in Clements Inn Passage Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. as this Examinants Father hath often times informed them and which information he this Examinant verily believes to be true and that his said Father never Rented House or paid Taxes to this Examinants knowledge & belief

Sworn 10th. Decr. 1765 Before} Fran Cary

John Gardiner
