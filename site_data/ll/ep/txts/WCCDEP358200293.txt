Westmr.  Elizabeth Hart aged about 36 Years upon Oath says she was never married Rented Ten Pounds by the Year paid Taxes nor ever was an Apprentice or lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year, says her father John Hart who was by Trade a painter Rented and lived in a house in Great Hart Street Covent Garden in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster of the Yearly Rent of Twenty pounds or thereabouts for a great many Years and there died,

Sworn the 7th. of August 1765 . before R. Kilsha Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Elizabeth Hart
