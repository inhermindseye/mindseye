Westmr.  Ignatus Plant of Guys Court near Vinegar Yard in the Parish of St. Martin in the Feilds in the Liberty of Westmr. on the Behalf of Richard Trevis an Infant aged upwards of One Year and Ann Trevis an Infant aged about two Weeks upon Oath Saith he hath been informed and believes that Charles Trevis the Father of the said Infants hath lately Inlisted himself into his Majestys Service & where he now is this Examinant cannot tell Says the Mother of the said Infants is dead Says the sd. Chas. Trevis Served Seven Years Apprentiship 20th. one Mr. Tomkins a Taylor in the Town Parish Places or Precinct of Bentley in the County of Stafford Says the said Chas. Trevis never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn 12th. Novr. 1747 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Ignatus Plant
