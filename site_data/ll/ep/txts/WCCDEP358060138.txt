Middx  Jane Dorber aged about 26 Yrs. upon Oath saith she never was married and that upwards of 7 Years ago she lived a Yearly Servant with Mr Geo. Bright  near Clemts. Inn Gate in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for there Years or thereabouts and has not since she quitted the said Mr. Brights Service rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 9th. 1751 before
