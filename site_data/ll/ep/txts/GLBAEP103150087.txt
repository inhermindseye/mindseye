Middlesex ss}  John Dowlington maketh oath That he can give no Account respecting the Settlement of his late Father or Mother deced or either of them That he this Dept has been informed by his late Aunt Mary Dowlington deced & which information he believes to be true that he this Depont was born in Mouse Alley in the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middlesex And this Deponent Farther Says that he was never bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor ever Rented a house or other Tenement of they early Rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any act to his Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement for himself

Sworn this 8th. day of December 1775 before] Geo. C. Smith

The Mark of John Dowlington
