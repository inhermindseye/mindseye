Westmr.  Mary Wallers upon Oath saith she never was married that she is the Daughter of Wm. Waller decd. by Ann his Wife that neither herself nor her sd. late Father ever rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Year and that her late Grandfather Christr. Waller rented a house & pd. Scot & Lot in Catrick parish near Richmond in Yorkshire & there dyed

Sworn Febry 12th. 1752 before  Sam Pechell

The Mark of Mary Wallers
