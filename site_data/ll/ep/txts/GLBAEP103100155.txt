Middlesex ss  Richard Bunham Maketh oath That he Cannot in any wife discover or setforth where his late Parents or either of them was or were Settled at the times of their respective Deaths But this Depont. Saith That he hath been informed by his late Mother Joanna Bunham deced That he was born in Sweedland Court in the Parish of St. Botolph witht. Aldgate in the County of Middlesex which information this Depont. believes to be true And this Depont Saith That he was never bound Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for Year nor rented a house of the yearly Renter value of Ten Pounds nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief never did any Act to gain a Settlement Since his Birth And that he hath a wife named Sarah

Sworn this 16th. day of September 1769 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Richard Bunham
