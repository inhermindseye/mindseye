St Clement Danes 29th. Decr. 1791

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary Le Strand  to wit Jane McCLeland  Singlewoman Aged 30 Years and upwards Upon Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Wilson in the Strand in the parish of St Mary Le Strand Dealer Manchester Stuffs on Wares for about two years and an half at the Annual Wages of about £5 that she quitted that Service about Six years ago and hath never since quitting it (by any Means whatever) obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement And that she has been supported in the Workhouse belonging to the said parish of St Le Strand at the Expence of the same parish for about three Months and hath been thereout only since the beginning of November last And that she is now Infirm and unable to maintain herself without same Assistance

Sworn the 29th day of December 1791 before us} N Bond Thos. Gordon

Jane McCleland
