Middlesex ss}  Charles Kennedy maketh oath that he was heretofore hired and lived as a yearly hired Servant by & with Mr Samuel Road a Farmer in that part of Hoxton which is in the Parish of St John Hackney in the Said County and Continued in such Service under Such hiring at Certain Wages for the Space of about Sixteen Years and has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or Served any Parish office or paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this. 5. day of October 1786 before J. Hawley R, Davies pd for

The Mark of Charles Kennedy
