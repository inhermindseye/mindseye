Middx  Ann Culley upon Oath saith she is the wido . of Mathias Culley who was (as he informed this Examint.) a Native of Brussells and never gained a Settlemt. in England and that before her sd. marriage & whilst a Singlewoman she lived a yearly Servant wth. Mr James Dodd a Printer  in Stanhope Strt. in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx at the yearly wages of £5 for one year and upwards that she quitted her sd. Service upwds. of 20 Yrs. ago & never afterwards rented ten pounds by the year pd. parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn June 3th. 1755 : before} Fran: Bedwell

Ann Carlier
