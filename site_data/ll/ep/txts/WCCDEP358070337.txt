Middlesex & Westmr. to wit Susan Inman Aged about Twenty Two years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Middleton a Taylor in Little Portland Street in the Parish of Saint James in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middlesex upwards of Two years at the yearly Wages Vizt. the first year at 4 Pounds and & the remaining other time at four pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service upwards of four years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 19th. day of January 1776 before  Wm Kitchiner  John Goodchild

her Susan Inman Mark
