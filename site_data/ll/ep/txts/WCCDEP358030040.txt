Middx  Margt. Bishop  Wido . of Mathew Bishop removed from the Parish of St. Andrew Holbn: above the Barrs by Order dated the 9th. Day of Novr. Instant upon her Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Mathw. Bishop on the 30th. Day of Janry in the Year 1721 or 1722 at the Fleet London Says her sd. Husbd. whilst he was a Singleman lived a Yearly hired Servt . with Councellr. Hungerford in Cooks Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for the Space of Three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of £8 or thereabouts Says her sd. late Husbd. never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes Served any Parochial Office or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif Says she this Examinant hath not since his Death rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived as a Yearly hired Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 11th. Novr 1745 before Wm: Jones

The Mark of Margt. Bishop
