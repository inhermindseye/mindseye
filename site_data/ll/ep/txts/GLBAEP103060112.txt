Aldgate Middx

Ann Franklin  Widow Maketh Oath that her Son Thomas Franklin  Linespiner (who is gon down to Norwich ) Servd Seven Years Apprenticeship by Indenture to one Mr. Beesly a Linespiner in the Hamlet of Mile End Old Town and that he hath gaind no other Settlement Since That his late wife  Elizabeth Franklin is dead and that he hath one Daughter named Ann Franklin aged about 8 year's That her Said Son was Some time ago Sick and was maintaind in the Work: house belonging to the Said Hamlet and the Officers of the Said Hamlet get him into the Hospital

Sworn the 14th.. day of February Ano Dom 1748 before N. Dukinfield Rd. Ricards

the mark of Ann Franklin
