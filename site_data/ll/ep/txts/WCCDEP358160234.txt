Middx  The Examination of Mary Burton  Wido . on the Behalfe of Mary the Wife of Mathew Hefford lodging in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx taken the 24th. Day of January 1740 Who upon her Oath saith that upwards of Six Years ago the above named Mathew Hefford rented a House in Pall Mall in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of ten Pounds and upwards & lived in the same for the Space of 2 Years & upwards as this Examinant hath been informed & which she verily beleives And this Examinant further saith that the said Mathew Hefford hath not since that Time rented any house lands or Tenements of the Yearly Rent of ten Pounds or otherwise gain'd a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife, That the sd. Matt. Hefford now absconded from Mary his sd. Wife & she being Sick & poor is not able to maintaine herselfe.

Sworne the Day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

Mary burton
