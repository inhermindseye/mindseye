Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Jane Challis aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Dobey in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Eighteen Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Four Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Eight years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Adam Wood who lives Servant to Mr.Orme the Distiller in the Borough is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of May last and at several times since at one of which times he did beget the said Child or Children of which this Examinant is so pregnant And that the said Adam Wood is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 20th. day of Decr. 1787 } R Butler

her Jane Challis Mark

Jane Challis lodges at Mr. Vincents No. 9 New Inn Passage

Adam Wood is a Tall Thin Man dark Hair Curled round his Neck comes out of Suffolk his Livery is brown turned up with Red

Mr. Talboy has this Warrant

Father Apprehended-Paid £10-Gave Note for £5.1.0 at 12 Mos. Note dated 12th. Janry. 88
