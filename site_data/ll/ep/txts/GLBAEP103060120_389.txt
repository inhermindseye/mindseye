Middx ss  Thomas Mabyn maketh oath that about Ten Years ago he rented a house in the Warden Street in the precinct of Bridewell in the City of London and lived there a Year and paid for rent of yr the Same Twenty four pounds and all parochial Taxes & never rented any other house Since nor gained any Subsequent Settlemt. & Saith that he hath a wife named Mary

Sworn this 13-Day of Sepr. 1749 before Richd. Ricards Saml. Gower

Thos: Mabyn
