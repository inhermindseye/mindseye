Saint Clement Danes June 21st 1785

3 Childn. Passd. to Mary Le Bone the 4th. in School  Jane Holmes aged twenty eight Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she was married to Isaac Holmes her now Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Mary le bone about two Years and a Quarter ago, that the has as Children living by her said Husband, and that since their Intermarriage her said Husband has rented and lived in a House in Carey Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex at a yearly rent of upwards of ten pounds for upon and of one Quarter of a mean and since he quitted the same hath not done any Act Matter or Thing to gain any subsequent Settlement and further this Examinant on her Oath saith that Previous to her Intermarriage with her said Husband Isaac Holmes she has there Children namely George now aged eight Years or thereabouts Samuel now aged seven Years or thereabouts and John aged Six Years or thereabouts, which said three Children were all born illegitimate and before this Examinant was ever married and were Christend by the Christian Names above mentioned and by this Examinants Maiden Name of Mc. Law and this Examinant also further saith

that her said three Children were born all of them in the Parish of Saint Mary le bone in the County of Middlesex and that the father of the said three Child is since deceased

Sworn this 21st Day of June 1785 before  Sampn Wright  Addington

Jane Holmes
