Middx to wit Henry Croasdell one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County of Mid on behalf of Lewis Brooks an Infant Aged about 12 years On his Oath says that the sd. Lewis Brooks was bound an Apprentice to One John Leeson of the Parish of Hammer smith in the County of Middlesex  Fisherman by Indenture bearing Date the 27th. day of Decr. 1781 by the Church and Overseers of the Poor of the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes as by a Duplicate of the sd. Indenture appears and that the sd. Lewis Brooks has ever since served his sd. Master in the sd. Parish of Hammersmith to the time of his being Discharged by the Justices as this Examinant has been Informed and verily believes.

Sworn this 9th. day of Novr. 1782 before  Wm Kitchiner  Thos: Gilbert

Henry Croasdell
