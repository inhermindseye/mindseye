Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Fraser  Single woman maketh oath that sometime ago She was hired & lived as a hired Servant with one Donald Fraser in King Henry Yard in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds & Continued in Such Service under Such Suring & Service for the space of one Year & half & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there And this Deponent has not since lived as a hired Servant for a Year nor done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 20th day of January 1775 before B Camper

The Mark of Elizabeth Fraser
