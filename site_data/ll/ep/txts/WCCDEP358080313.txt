Middx  Jane Sharp aged about 43 Years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Thos. Sharp (gone from her) with whom she Intermarried at St. Bartholomew Cloth Fair about 14 Years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House the Corner of Kent Street in the Parish of Saint George Southwark in the County of Surry for several Years at the Yearly Rent of £12 and paid all Parochial Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Seven Years ago since which time her said Husband never Rented House paid Taxes or did any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 14th. day of Novr. 1778 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

her Jane Sharp Mark
