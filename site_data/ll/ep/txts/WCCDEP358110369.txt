John Perry upon Oath Saith he was married to his Wife  Jane Perry abt. 16 Yrs. ago at the Church of St Catherines by the Tower and that whilst he was a Singleman he lived a Yearly Servant with Mr. Rd. Sherrard at North End in the Parish of Fulham in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds for the Space of Sixteen Months quitted his said Service upwds. of twenty Years age & has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Decr. 13th. 1753 before } Barth: Hammond

John Perry
