Middx  Thomas Wine upon Oath saith he was bound Apprentice for Seven Years to Mr Tomlin of Burleigh Street in the parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx and after he had been with his said Master about Four Years was assigned over to John Joynes Gloves with whom he Served the remaining three Years of his Apprentiship in Stanhope Street in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex aforesd. Says he never rented Ten pounds a Yr. paid parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn Novr. 9th. 1750 before} Fran: Bedwell

Thomas Wine
