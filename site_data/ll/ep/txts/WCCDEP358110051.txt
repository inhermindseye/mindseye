Westmr.  Robt. Poole upon Oath saith he was born in Rain deer Yard in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes within the Liberty of Westmr. in which Place his father Robt. Poole as this Ext. has been informed & believes Rented two Coach houses Stabling for Six horses & two Lodging Rooms but what Rent he paid for these Premises this Ext. knows not that sevl. other persons Rented Stabling & Lodging Room in the same Yard and used one Privy in common & likewise Water from a Pump in the Yard and that all the Persons who lodged in the said Yard went up one common Stair Case which was on the outside of the Building and that afterwards his sd. late father lived in & Rented a Stable Yard in Buckridge Street in the parish of St. Giles in the Fields Middx consisting of a Standing place for two Coaches with two lodging Rooms over & Stabling for 5 or Six horses with a Privy & Water and continued therein one Year or longer and that these last mentioned Premises was an intire distinct Tenement having a pair of Gates to the Yard Door which used to be shut every Night Says his sd late father was born at Morvan under the hill in Gloucestershire and that he this Examinant never Rented ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly

Servant for one Year whilst a Singleman

Sworn May 13th. 1752 before Fran: Bedwell

Robeart Poole
