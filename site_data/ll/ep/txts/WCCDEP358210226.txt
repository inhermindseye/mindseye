Westmr.  Phebe Barnesley Aged about Thirty six Years, Upon oath says she never was Married and that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Plows a Victualler at the sign of the Lamb and Flag in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex for Five Years and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds, quitted said Service upwards of Ten Years ago. and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 7th. day of January 1768 before} Thos. Kynaston

The Mark of Phebe Barnesley
