Middlesex ss}  Richard Waterhouse maketh oath that he was heretofore duly bound apprentice to James Tooley of the Parish of St Augustin otherwise Austin in the City of London  Lapidary for Seven years & after having Served him there for about the Space of two years his Said Master did & his Said apprenticesp was thereupon determined and this deponent has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year nor rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish Office or paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 8. day of Novemr 1784 before R Davies J. Hawley Richd Waterhouse
