Westmr.  Robert Davis Aged about Thirty five Years Upon Oath says he never was married and that he lived a hired Yearly Servant with William Lloyd  Esqr . in Bedford Row in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex for Five Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds, quitted said Service about Eight years ago, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year.

Sworn the 1st. day of January 1768 before}  Thos: Balack

his Robert Davis Mark
