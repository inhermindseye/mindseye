Middlesex ss}  Mary Parr  Single woman maketh Oath that her father Henry Parr hath Informed her that he lived in and rented a house of Five pounds and ten Shillings a year in the Grange Road in the parish of St. Mary Magdelen Bermondsey in the County of Surry and continued in such house Sixteen years and upwards and paid kings Taxes for the same and to her Knowledge Information or belief her said father hath not since rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor paid any Poors Rate or kings Taxes or served any Parish Offices or done any Act to gain a Subsequent settlement nor hath she this Deponent done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 19th Day of January 1768 before  Chris: Scott R Pell

Mary Parr
