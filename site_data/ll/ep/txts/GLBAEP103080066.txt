Middlesex ss  Mary Bodean  widow of John Bordean deced maketh oath that she hath been informed by her said late husband that about Twenty Nine Years ago he was duly bound an Apprentice to his Uncle Mr Read a Glasier who then lived in Durham Yard in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex And this Deponent farther saith that her said husband to her knowledge or belief did not after his apprenticeship Rent a house of Ten Pounds a Year or upwards nor serve any Parish offices nor live as a hired servant by the Year nor do any Act to Acquire a subsequent settlemt. And this Depont farther saith that since her said husbands Death she hath not done any Act to Acquire a settlement in her own right to her konowledge or belief.

Sworn this 19th. day of Febry 1761 before  Chris: Scott  Boulton Mainwaring

Mary Bodean
