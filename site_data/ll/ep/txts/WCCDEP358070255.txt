Westr to wit Elizabeth Austry Aged about Nineteen Years in her Oath says She never was Married Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Thomas Austry Rented and lived in a House in Queen Court near Little Turn Stile Holbourn in the Parish of Saint Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds a Year lived therein for forty Days and Upwards and Paid Thirty Shillings for the six weeks he lived therein hath Quitted the same ever since Midsummer 1763 since which time her said Father, hath never Rented House Paid Taxes or done any act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement as her mother has informed her and which Information She verily believes to be true

Sworn this 29th day of March 1775 before R Butler Sampn Wright

The Mark of Elizabeth Austry
