St. Clement Danes March 30th. 1792

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Paul Deptford  (to wit) Hannah Pain Aged about 25 Years upon her Oath saith that she was Married to her Husband John Pain at the parish Church of the united Parishes of St Michael Royal & St Martin Vintry in the City of London on 22d Lane 1790 (by Licence) that she hath been informed which she believes to be true that he served 5 years under Indentures duly exted. with Mr. John Wood of the parish of St Nicholas Deptford Black Smith , that her said Husband Lodged with Mr Back at in back Lane in the parish of St Paul Deptford aforesaid in the County of Kent during the last two or three Months of such his Service, and aforesaid at the end of the said 5 Years her said Husband was discharged from his said Apprenticeship And this Deponent saith her said Husband hath never obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of her knowledge or belief And saith that the hath One ilegitimate Child by her Said Husband which is now living Named John who was Aged Eight Months on the first day of March Instant And this Deponent with her said Child about Six Months ago and She doth not know nor hath heard where he went or now is And that She is unable to maintain herself & Child without Assistance

Sworn the 21st 31st March 1792 before us M Addington J Brading

Hannah Pain 's Mark

Witness of the Mark Richd. Dobbins
