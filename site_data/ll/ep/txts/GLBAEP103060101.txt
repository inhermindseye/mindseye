Aldgate Middx

Samuel Cade  Cane: Chair: bottom maker Maketh Oath that above Nine years ago he livd and rented a House of thirteen pounds a year in the Parish of Saint James at Clerkernwel in the Said County of Middx and hath not rented a House of ten pounds a year nor paid any Parish taxes nor Servd any Parish office in any Parish or place Since his living at Clerkenwel and that he livd there but four Months and paid a Quarters rent and then went away hath a wife  Margaret and one Child named Jane Cade aged Eight years

Sworn 30th day of April 1748 before us Robert Dennett N. Dukinfield

his Samuell Cade Mark
