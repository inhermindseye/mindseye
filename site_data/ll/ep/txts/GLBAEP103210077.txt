Middlesex ss}  Frances Ashburne the Wife of John Ashburne maketh oath that her said husband is lately gone away from her to places to her unknown & left her destitute of Support & maintenance That her sd husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was heretofore duly bound apprentice by Indre to Richard Paisthorpe of King street in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the sd County Taylor for seven Years & after having served two years of such his Apprenticeship there he was by the Mutual Consent of his Master himself & his father discharged from his said Master & his Apprenticeship Put an End to as She this Deponent has been informed by her said husband & believes That in the Month of August one thousand seven hundred & eighty three this Deponts said husband (being then of about the age of seventeen Years & five Months) was Married to this Deponent in the Parish church of St Botolph without Bishopsgate London She this deponent being then of age That she this Depont & her sd husband were at that time Inhabitants of the sd Parish of Bishopsgate & such marriage was Celebrated by a due publication of Banns previous thereto but no Consent was given to such Marriage by her said husbands Parents or any Guardian for him

Sworn this 6th day of April 1785 before  Robt Smith

Francis Ashburne
