Before Birth 

Burrows Mary  singlewoman and

St Clement Danes 19th June 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  Pregnancy  Reputed Father (to wit) Mary Burrows aged twenty years & upwards Upon Oath saith that She never was Married that She lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr George Hudson in Ship and Anchor Court in the Strand near Temple Bar in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex for a year and upwards at the annual Wages of six teen pounds, that She quitted that Service on or about the twenty third day of May last and hath not obtained any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge in belief And this Examinant upon her voluntary Oath further saith that She is now pregnant with a Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards & to become chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes when Born, that the said George Hudson (with whom she so lived as Servant as aforesaid) is the  father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Deponent's Body sometime in or about the Month  of September last and at several times since at some or one of which time he did beget the said Child or Children thereon and that he and no one else is the real true father thereof

Sworn the 19th day of June 1793 before me} Wm. Kitchiner

Mary Burrows 's Mark

Witness Garrett

Esther Burrows her Child

Burrows Hudson taken

25th: June 1793 paid £20. to the Overseers, and gave a Note for £10 more payable at 12 Months which Run it is agreed shall not be paid then unless the Child shall be living one the Note is to be given up the Overseers Jointly Settled this Business Esther Burrows the Child born 6th July 1793 Sent to Enfield 25th Octr 1793

25th July/94 The Child was living at this time being the end of the year but The Overseers at the request of the year 1794 gave up the Note to Hudson in consideration of his being a parishioner and having not with been lately much distressed on acct of his Wife's illness

14th Augst 1794  G G. deled to the Note to Mr Wood at his request this day
