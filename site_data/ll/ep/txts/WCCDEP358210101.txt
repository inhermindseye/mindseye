Middx & Westmr.}  Martha Yon aged about 33 Years Upon Oath says she is a Native of the Island of St. Helena in the East Indies and that she is the Widow of John Yon (a black) who has been dead about Ten Years says her said husband in his life time nor she this Examinant since his Death never Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year; And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a bastard or bastards and to become Chargeable to the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County & Liberty she being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when born says that Captain Coutts of the Prince of Wales East India Man now abroad did beget the said Child or Children on her body he having had carnal knowledge of her body several times on board the said Ship at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now pregnant and that the said Captain Coutts & no other person did beget the same.

Sworn the 14th. Novr. 1766 before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Martha Yon
