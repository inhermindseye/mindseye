Middlesex ss}  Thomas Eden maketh oath that about Thirty Six Years ago he was hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant by and With Mr Thomas Reynardson a Cheesemonger in the Parish of Chelsea in the said County at Certain Wages and Continued in Such Service under such hiring for about the Space of two years That he has not Since lived as a hired Servant for a year in any one place nor ever rented a house Lodging or other Tenement of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards nor ever Served any Parish office nor ever paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act Since Such his Servitude as above to gain a Subseqnt Settlement That he hath a wife named Agness and three Children namely Hannah aged about Seven years Thomas aged five years and Edward aged three years the lawful issue of this Deponent by his said wife to whom he was lawfully married

Sworn this 5. day of July 1788 before  R Davies  Robt. Smith

Thomas Eden
