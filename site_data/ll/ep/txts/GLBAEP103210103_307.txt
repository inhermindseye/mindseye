Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Trench the wife of James Trench  mariner maketh oath that her said husband is now absent from her in parts beyond the Seas that he has informed her that he was born in Ireland & that he to his Knowledge or belief never did any act to gain a Settlement for himself in England That She this Deponent was born in Lumber Court in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the Sd County & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlemt for herself Save what she may have Acquired by Marriage with her said husband to whom she was lawfully married That She has been informed by her late Aunt Martha Grepe and Which she believes to be true that this deponts late Father Thomas Beal deced that he duly served his Apprenticeship in the said Parish and died there Legally Settled That She this Deponent hath a child named Martha aged about Seventeen Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her said husband

Sworn this 7th day of October 1785 before J. Hawley R Davies

The Mark of Elizabeth Trench
