Middlesex ss}  Margaret Peet the Wife of  Ninion Peet maketh oath That she knows John Bristow a Negro Man now under Sentence of Transportation in his Majesties Prison of Newgate and this Deponent also knew his late Wife Susannah Bristow deced and this Depont. also knows a Child named Thomas Bristow aged about three years the son of the said John Bristow before named by the said Susannah his late Wife That the said Child was born in Minuitts Rents in the Parish of St John Wapping in the County of Middlesex She this Deponent being Present at the birth of Such Child and acted as Midwife upon that occasion And this Depont. Can give no Account respecting the Settlement of the Parents of the said Child or either of them.

Sworn this 9th Day of June 1772 before Cha: Digby W: Russell

The Mark of Margaret Peet
