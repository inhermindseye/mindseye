Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Ann Hutchins of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the Sai County Single woman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the peace in and for the Said County of Middlesex this 21st Day of February 1770.

Who upon her oath Saith That she is now with Child an that the Child or Children where with She now goeth and is Pregnant is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to be Chargeable to the Said Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate and that Robert main of the Same Parish Mariner is the Father of the said Child or Children.

Sworn this 21t day of February 1770 before  Chris Scott

The Mark of Ann Hutchins
