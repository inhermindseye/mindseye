Middx  Eliz Mc.Quire upon Oath saith she is the wife of Hugh Mc Quire ( now at Sea ) by whom she hath three Children Mary aged 8 Yrs. or thereabts. Jas . aged 6 Yrs. & Cathn . aged 16 Months Says her sd. Husband rented the Crown Alehouse in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . at the Yearly Rent of Twenty two pounds and lived therein one Year & upwards quitted the same abt. 4 Years & has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 22d. 1752 before  Fran. Bedwell

The Mark of Eliz. Mc.Quire
