Middx to wit Lucy Wilson Aged about Fifty Seven Years on her Oath says She Intermarried to David Wilson her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holbourn London about Three Years ago And who has been Dead about One Month Says her said late Husband has Two Children now living by a former Wife Namely Mary Aged about Nine Years And David Aged about Five Years And further says that her said late Husband Rented and lived in a House known by the Sign of the Wheat Sheaf in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holbourn above the Barrs in the County of Middlesex for One Year at the yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds as She believes And Paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about five Years ago since which time he hath  gained no other Settlement either by Renting House Paying Taxes or otherwise Howsoever.

Sworn this 10th. Day of Decr. 1780 before  Abrm Baley Wm. Kitchiner

her Lucy Wilson Mark
