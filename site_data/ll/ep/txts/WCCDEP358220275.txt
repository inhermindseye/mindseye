Middx & Westmr.  Martha Drover aged about 37 Years on her Oath Says she is the Widow of John Drover who has been dead about Eight Months & by whom she hath five Children now living namely Mary Aged 13 Years Wm. Aged 9 Years Elizth Aged 8 Years & Geo Aged 5 Yrs. and Sarah Aged about 3 Years Says that her said late Husband Rented & lived in a House known by the Name or Sign of the Fountain in Clave Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for 2 Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of £34. hath quitted the same about 2 Years ago & after quitting the same he never Rented £18. P Year or Paid Taxes Says that since her said Husbands Death & having a Family of Children had some thoughts of taking a House to teach School and to let the same out in Lodgings in Order the better to suport herself & Family Accordingly this Examinant hearing of one at Chelsea Applyed to one Mr. Hales the Landlord thereof but this Examinant being a Widow & a Family of Children the said Mr. as she Acts did not & have to let the same to her unless some Person wod Pass his Word for the Accordingly this Examinant Applyed to an Acquaintance one Mr. Vigures who took the said House for her at the Yearly Rent of Twenty Eight Pounds and belives his name is rated in the Parish Books belonging to the Parish of St. Geo. Hanover Square not withstanding which this Examinant letts the sd House out in Lodgings and receives Rent for the same and does now as she always did

pay Rent for the sd House and looks upon herself lyable & bound to pay for the same Not withstanding the Landlord gives recipts in the sd. Mr. Vigures Name

Sworn this 20th. day of Augt. 1771 } Thos. Kynaston W Kelynge

Martha Drover
