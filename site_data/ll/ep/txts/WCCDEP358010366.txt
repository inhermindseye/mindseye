Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Nicks aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that about Six years ago she Intermarried with William Nicks her late Husband who has been dead about Twelve Months at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Mary aged about Ten Months That her said late Husband was a Soldier and was born in the Army abroad but where this Examinant doth not know that he never was bound an Apprentice, lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year, Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a Settlement in his own Right That she this Examinant before her Intermarriage to the said Wm. Nicks and whilst she was a Single woman she lived with a Wm. Clay in Clare Street for Seven years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Five Guineas that after she quitted same she never did any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 30th. day of Octr. 1790 R Butler

Thos. Elizabeth. Nick . Mark
