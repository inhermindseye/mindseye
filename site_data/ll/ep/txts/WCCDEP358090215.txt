Middx to wit James Hell Aged about Fifty years on his Oath says he Intermarried to his present Wife Eleanor at the Parish Church of Saint Clement Danes about five years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children says before his Intermarriage to his said Wife and whilst he was Single man he lived a hired yearly servant with One Mr. Ward a Victualler in Saint Martins Lane in the Parish of Saint martins in the Field in the Liberty of Westminster for four years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Ten Guineas hath quitted his said Service about  Eleven years ago since which time he hath gained no other subsequent settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 19th. Day of April 1781 before W Addington R Butler

James Hell
