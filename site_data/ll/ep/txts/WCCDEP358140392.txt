Middx.  Elizabeth Wilson aged abt. 21 yrs. upon Oath says she never was Married And that she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Partridge the Fountain in Great Shire Lane in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx for One year & 3 Months at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds & hath Quitted said Service abt. One Year & has not since Rented Ten Pounds a year paid parish Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn this 24th. July 1760 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz: Wilson
