Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Susannah Homer of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Widow taken on oath before me Rice Davies  Esquire one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this first day of February 1788 who Saith that She is now with Child and that the Said Child is likely to be born a Bastard and to be Chargeable to the said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that Michael Tobin late of the Same Parish Taylor is the Father of the said Child

Taken and Signed the day & year above written before me  R Davies

The Mark of Susannah Homer
