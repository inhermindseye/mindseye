(Nil) St. Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  not Sworn  (to wit) William Lovejoy Aged 25 Years & upwards Upon Oath Saith that he never was Married that he was bound by Indres of Apprenticeship the Mr. Richd. Yours  Change Court  Exeter Change in the said parish of St. Clement Danes with whom he regulary served abt. two Years of his time that then his said Master gave up his Indenture & they were destroyed & he & his said Master parted by Natural Consent And that he hath not lived as an hired Yearly Servant or any otherwise aquired any Subsequent legal Settlement to the best of his knowledge or beleif And that his Ill Poor and unable (in his present State) to support himself

Sworn the 29th. June 1791 before Me}
