Westmr. 

Ann Osborn aged about 10 Years upon Oath Saith she was bound Apprentice to Charles Hendred in the Parish of St. Brides London Fruiterer and lived with the sd. Chas. Hendred abt. 2 Weeks and afterwards  Assigned the sd. Chas. Hendred by & with the Consent of her this Examinant assigned her together with the sd. Indenture of Apprenticeship for the remr. of her sd. Apprentiship to Thos. Dollason of the same Parish Fruiterer and this Examinant lived with the sd. Thos. Dollason in White Lyon Court in Fleet Street in the sd. Parish of St. Brides upwds. of Six Months & then absconded from her sd. Master Service and hath not since done any Act to gain a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn July 11th. 1748 before

The Mark of Ann Osborn
