Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Brown aged about 54 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with James Brown her late Husband at the New Church Birmingham about Twenty five years ago That her said late Husband has been dead about Eleven Months That her Said late Husband Rented Apartments of Mr. Ralph Jolly in Vere Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for about Two One year s and a half at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds that he quitted same about Twelve years since which time he never Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes on did any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 15th. day of March 1790 R Butler

her Sarah Brown Mark
