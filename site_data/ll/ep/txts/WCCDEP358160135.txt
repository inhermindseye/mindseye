Middx} The Exaicon of Alice Shrimplin Lodgin in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the Ninth day of May 1740 who upon her Oath Saith that She is a Singlewoman & was never Marryed and further upon her Voluntary Oath Saith that on the 25th. day of March last she was Delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes wch hath since been Baptized Robt . wch is likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor & not able to maintain ye same and that one Robt. Nicholls did begett the sd. Child on her body she this Exaiant at such time liveing as a Yearly hired Servt . with Sr. Windham Knatchbull at his Seat called Masum Hatch in the County of Kent and he the sd. Robt. Nicholls at ye same time having as a Yearly hired Servt . with the sd. Sr. Windham Knatchbull there became acquainted wth her & had Carnall Knowledge of her body once in the Month of June last & at Sevll. other times since at one of wch times he got her with the Male Bastard Child She was Delivered of as aforesd. and that he the sd. Robt. Nicholls and no other Person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworn the day & Year Above menconed before} Jas: Gronous  Samuel Clarke

Alice Shrimplin
