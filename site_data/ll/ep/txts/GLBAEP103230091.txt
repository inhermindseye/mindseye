Middlesex ss}  Sarah Jones the widow of Thomas Jones deced maketh oath that her Said late husband heretofore rented a house known by the name of the Swan in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County and was Charged to And Paid Poors Rates for the same That to this Doponts Knowledge information or belief her Sd husband never afterwards rented a house or Lodging of the Yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or did any Act to gain Another Settlement That She hath two Children namely John Aged about Three Years & ten Months and Charlotte Aged about Nineteen Months the lawful Issue of this Deponent by her Sd husband to whom She was lawfully married

Sworn this. 6. day of Decemr 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Jones
