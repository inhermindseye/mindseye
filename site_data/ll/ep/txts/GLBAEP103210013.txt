Middlesex ss}  Ann Toome the Wife of Samuel Toome maketh oath that her Said husband is now Confined as a Prisoner in Clerkenwell Prison Middx. That he has informed her & which She believes to be true that he was duly Bound to and Served the Whole of his Apprenticeship with one Joseph Parks a White Smith to wit part thereof in Church Yard Alley otherwise Cartwright Street & the remaining part thereof in East smithfield near Where the Maypole formerly Stood in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County That her Said husband to her knowledge or belief never rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds nor ever Served any Parish Office nor ever Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor ever did any Act to gain a settlement Subsequent to his Said Apprenticeship That She hath two Children namely Frances aged near this thirteen years & Thomas aged about nine years & three Quarters of a year the lawful issue of this Depont by her Said husband to whom She was lawfully Married in the Old Church at Birmingham in Warwickshire

Sworn this 30 day of August 1784 R Davies

The Mark of Ann Toome
