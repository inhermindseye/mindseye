Westmr.  Mary Wheat upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Robert Wheat who dyed about 25 Years ago Says she lived a Yearly Servt. with Mr Henry Tate at Wigleys Gardens in the Parish of St. Mary at Lambeth in the County of Surry at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds for one Year & upwards (having at that time no Child or Children) and Quitted her sd. Service upwds. of 18 Months ago since which time she has not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Febry 22d. 1747 before} Wm: Jones

Mary Wheatt
