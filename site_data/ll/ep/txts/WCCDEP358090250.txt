Middx to wit Henry Croasdell one of the Beadles belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Elizth. Thompson  Widow (who this Deponent looks upon not Capable of making an Oath) On his Oath says that on the Strictest Inquiry he can make finds that Her late Husband James Thompson Rented and Lived in a House in Cross Court Gun Dock in the Parish of Saint John Wapping in the County of Middlesex for two years and Upwards at the yearly Rent of nine Pounds Ten Shillings and paid Land Tax and for the same and Quitted she said House in the year 1774 as Appears to this Deponent by the Landlords Receipt and after Quitting the last mentioned House she said James Thompson never Rented House except One in the Parish of Saint Catherine near the Tower of the yearly Rent of Five Pounds five shillings but paid no parish Taxes due for the same as he has been Credibly Informed by the Landlord thereof and which Information He verily believes to be true

Sworn this 6th. day of Septr. 1781 before  Wm. Kitchiner R Butler

Henry Croasdell
