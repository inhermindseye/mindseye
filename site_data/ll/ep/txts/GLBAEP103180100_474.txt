Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Spittle of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Said County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd. County this First day of December 1778 Who Saith That on the Twelfth day of October in the year one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy three in Swan Alley in the Said Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County She the Said Elizabeth Spittle was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Since Baptized by the name of Sarah Oman and that the Said Bastard Child is likely to become Chargeable to the Said Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate And that one Charles Oman late of the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the Sd County Carman did get her With Child of the said Bastard Child

Taken & Signed the day & year abovewritten before me P. Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Spittle
