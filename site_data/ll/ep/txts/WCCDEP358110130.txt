Middx  Sarah Stiff aged upwds. of 21 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married and never rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year and that her father Thomas Stiff about 8 years ago rented a house of £16 by the year in Drury Lane near the Cole Yard in the parish of St Giles in the Fields and lived therein about 1 year & has not since rented ten pounds a Year or pd. parish taxes

Sworn Novr. 30th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarh. Stiff
