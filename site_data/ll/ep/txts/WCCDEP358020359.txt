St Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Mary Wood  Widow Aged 31 years on thereabouts Upon Oath saith that about four Years ago she intermarried with her late Husband John Wood at the parish Church of St. John Hackney in the County of Middlesex by whom she hath two Children now living Namely Ann Aged 2 years & ten Months or thereabouts and Charles Aged one year and four Months or thereabouts that Ann the Elder & of her said Child is now living with and supported by her Uncle the of this Deponent's Husband's Sister who resides at Cambridge & keeps the led Lyon by the Bridge there - that her said Husband was killed on Board his Majestys Ship the sheerness then at Barbadeer about Eighteen Months ago, that he hath informed her that previous to the Marriage of this Examinant with her said Husband John Wood and while he was single and unmarried not having any Child or Children he lived as an yearly Servant with Mr. Purcell Sheriffs Officer on Houghton Street Clare Market in the parish of St Clement Danes aforesaid for a year & a half or upwards but at what Wages this Deponent hath never been Informed that her said Husband her he quitted such Service about ten years ago & since hath not by any means what ever acquired any other legal settlement And that she is now poor and unable to maintain herself and Child

Sworn the 26th Novr.1790 before me  Wm Hitchiner

Mary Wood
