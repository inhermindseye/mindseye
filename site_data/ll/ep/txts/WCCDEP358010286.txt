Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex ss  Mary Peat  Widow aged 41 years on thereabouts upon her Oath Saith that since her Husbands death and being Single and unmarried She has lived as an hired yearly Servant with Mr. Thomas Gearl a Sugar Baker in Saint Bennetts Hill in the Parish of Saint Bennett in the City of London for fifteen Months that she quitted her Place about four Years ago and since quitting same has not gained any other or Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22nd. April 1789 before  Sampn Wright M Addington

her Mary Peat Mark
