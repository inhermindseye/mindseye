Middx & Westmr}  Earminea Scanby Aged about 33 Years upon Oath Says She was never Married Says She lived a Yearly Servant with one Mr. Check Apothecary deceased in Searl Street in the Parish at St: Clement Danes in the liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middlesex upwards of fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted said Service abt fifteen Years and hath not Since Rented any house Paid Taxes or lived a hired yearly Servt. for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says She is Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of St. Clement Danes She being poor and Unable to Maintain the same when Born Says that one Wm King a Chelsea Pensioner had carnal knowledge of her Body in the Month of Febry or March last past and at Sevl other times Since at one of which times he got her with Child or of the Child or Children with wch She now Pregnant And that the said Wm King is the only true and lawfull father

Sworn this 29th Day of Novr. 1768 before Thos. Kynaston }

Earminea Scanby
