Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Allen the widow of John Allen deced maketh oath That She Can give no Account respecting the Settlemt. of her said late husband That divers years ago & when a Singlewoman She this Deponent was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year with one Mrs. Hill in the Parish of Swanscombe in the County of Kent at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds & Continued in such Service for the Space of three years & by means thereof gained a legal settlement there That she this Deponent has not Since lived as hired Servant for a year nor done any Act to gain a Settlemt Since in her own right Save what She has Acquired by Marriage with her said late husband whose Settlement is unknown to her

Sworn this 14 day of January 1774 before  Jno Sherwood Wilmot

The Mark of Elizabeth Allen
