Middlesex ss The Voluntary Examination of Catherine Thornton of the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex single woman taken on oath before me Christopher Scott  Esqr. one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County this Twenty Ninth Day of September in the year of our Lord 1767 .

Who on her oath Saith That on the Twelfth Day of august last Past in Swan Alley in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate She this Examinant was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Which has since been Baptized and registred by the name of Elizabeth Martin and is become Chargeable to the Said Parish And this Examinant farther Saith That one George Arnold of the Parish of Allhallows Barking London Gentlemen and a Clerk in his Majesties Navy office did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child. And this Examinant Saith that she is a single woman and was never Married.

Sworn this 29th. Day of September 1767 before  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Catherine Thornton
