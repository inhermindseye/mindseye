Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Mary Hunter aged about 23 years upon her Oath saith that on or about the first day of May 1793 she Intermarried with Edward Hunter her present husband (who is now at Sea) at the Parish Church of Saint Anne's Westminster by whom she hath one Child living Vizt. Edward aged about Twenty Months That her said Husband was born in Middlesex Hospital That he never to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief gained a Settlement in his own Right either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes living as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever being bound an Apprentice That previous to her Intermarriage with her said Husband and whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Lieutenant Estcourt at No. 45 Five fields Chelsea in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westminster for Thirteen Months at the yearly wages of Six Guineas that she hath quitted Said Service about Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this day of Novr. 1795 before us}
