Middx  Ann Porter aged about 30 Years upon her Oath saith that she was never married that about 8 Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mrs. Wabraven in Arundell Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes at the Yearly Wages of £5: for the Space of one Year & upwds. that since she left her sd. Service she hath not Rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife & further upon her Oath saith that on the 1st. Day of June last she was Delivered of two Male Bastard Children at Mrs. Richmonds in Witch Street in the sd Parish of St Clement Danes one of which is since dead & the other hath been baptized Thomas and is become Chargeable to the sd. Parish she being Poor & not able to maintain the same And that Thomas Ingram  Apprentice to Mr. Vokes a Dyer in Hatton Garden did begett the sd. Children on her Body she at such time liveing a Servt. with Mr Vokes there the sd. Thomas Ingram became Acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body in the Month of August last & at severall other times since at one of which times he gett her with Child of the sd. Male Bastard Children of which she was delivered

as aforesaid and that he the sd. Thomas Ingram & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne July 4th. 1744 before}
