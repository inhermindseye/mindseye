Westmr. to wit Jane Plenius Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married or ever Rented House or Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her Father Rented and lived in a House in South Audley Street  Grosvenor Square in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for Ten Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of fifty Pounds hath quitted the said House about Eight Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Taxes.

Sworn this 24th. day of April 1771 before}  Jona Durden

Jane Plenius
