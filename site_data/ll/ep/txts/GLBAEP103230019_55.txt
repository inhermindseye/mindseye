Middlesex ss}  William Moore maketh oath that divers years ago he rented a house of the yearly rent of eighteen Pounds in Gneen Street in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the sd County & was Charged to and Paid Poors Rates for the same That he has not Since rented a house of the yearly rent of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement & that he hath a wife named Ruth to whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this. 15. day of February 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of William Moore
