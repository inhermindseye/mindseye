Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Martha Betagh aged about 55 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married according to the rites and ceremonies of the Church of England that she this Examinant whilst she was a Singlewoman lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs- Herbert in Park Street Grosvenor Square in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex for Five years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath left said Service about 34 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly servant for one year Rented house Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 26th- day of March 1789  Philip Dyot D Walker

Martha Betagh
