Westmr  John Bristow aged about 60 Years upon Oath Saith he Rented house next Door to Johns Coffee house in Shire Lane in the parish of St Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Fourteen Pounds and lived therein upwds. of five Years quitted the same upwds. of Ten Years ago & has not Since rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn Jany. 21st. 1752 : before  Fran: Bedwell

John Bristow
