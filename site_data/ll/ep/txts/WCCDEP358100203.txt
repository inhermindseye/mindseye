Middlesex to wit Jane Powys Aged about 38 Years on her Oath says she intermarried to John Powys her late Husband Deceased at the Parish Church of St. Andrews Holbourn above 13 Years ago and by whom she hath One Child Named John Powys living aged about 11 Years Says her said late Husband Rented & lived in a House in Red Lyon Street in the Parish of St. Andrew Holbourn above the Bars for Several Years at the Yearly Rent of £35 a Year besides Taxes hath Quitted said House about 4 Years ago since which time she never Gained any or Settlement neither hath this Examinant since her said Husbands Death Either by Renting House Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 10th Day of Aprill 1784 before us D Walker Philip Dyot

Jane Powys
