Middx & Westmr.}  Hannah Skelton Aged about Sixty four Years on behalf of her Daughter Elizabeth Shelton who now lies Ill on her Oath Says that her said Daughter never was married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained any other Settlement otherwise than thro' this Examinants late Husband who before his Intermarriage with this Examinant and whilst he was a Singleman he lived a hired Yearly Servant to one Jonathan Guirnell a Merchant  facing Saint Lawrences Church Yard near Guild Hall in the Parish of St. Lawrence Jewry Church in the City of London for sevl. Years at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds as she believes and after quitting the said last mentioned place he never Rented House paid parish Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 28th. day of Janry 1777 before  John Cox . Jno. Machin

her Hannah Shelton Mark
