Middx  Susa: Howard upon Oath Saith she is the Wido . of John Howard deced. & that the Sd. Susa Howard after her husbands decease & in the times of her Widow hood rented a house in Tennis Court in Holborn of the Yearly Rent of £14 and lived therein four or 5 Years-quitted the same about Seven Years ago & has not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish taxes or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other places to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn 17. Febry 1752 before  Fran Bedwell

The Marks of Susa Howard
