Westmr.  George Dishouser Aged abt. Sixty Years Upon Oath Says he Intermarried with Ann his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Katherine by the Tower abt. Thirty Years since Says he Rented a House over against White Chapel Church in the Parish of St. Mary White Chapel in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds lived in the same abt. Thirteen Years & hath quitted sd. House abt. Twelve Years and hath not since Rented House or pd. Taxes

Sworn the 18th. of Octr. 1766 before  Fran Cary R Kilsha

George Dishouser
