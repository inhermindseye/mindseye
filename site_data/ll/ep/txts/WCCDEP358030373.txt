Westmr. 

Christr. Windowmor upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Mary Windowmor about 14 Years age at the Fleet London Says he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. and Mrs. Fleetwood Tripe Sellers Says he lived two Years or thereabouts the latter Part of his sd. Apprentiship with the sd. Mrs. Fleetwood in Peter Street near the Horse ferry in the Parish of St. John Evangelist in the Liberty of Westmr. Says he never rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn April 18th. 1747 before  Wm: Jones

The Mark C W of Christr. Windowmor
