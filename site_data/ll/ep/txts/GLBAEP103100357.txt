Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Susan  Gifford of the Parish of St. Botolph witht Aldgate in the County of Middlesex  Singlewoman taken on oath before me Charles Digby  Esqr ;  one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in and for the said County of Middlesex this 16th Day of Febry 1771

Who on her oath Saith That on the Twenty Second day of November now last past in the said Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex She the Said Susan Gifford was delivered of a Female Bastard Child Which Said Child hath been Since Baptized and Registred by the name of  Susannah Gifford and that David Murphy of Pump Court in the Temple London  Esquire did get her with Child of the said Bastard Child which is be come Chargeable to the Sd Parish

Sworn this 16th Day of Febry 1771 before  Cha: Digby

Susan Gifford
