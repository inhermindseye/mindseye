Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westminster }  Fulham Co. Middx  to Wit Jane Fleming Aged about 36 Years upon her Oath Saith that about 15 Years Ago she intermarried with John Heming at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex That her said Husband had been Dead near 3 Years that during his life informed this Examinant that he Served part of his time to his Father in Ireland who was by Trade a Taylor And Ranaway from him before his time was out That during their intermarriage the said John Fleming never Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year paid Taxes or did any Act Matter a thing to gain any Settlement in England, And this Examinant further saith that she was Born at the White horse at Parsons Green in the parish of Fulham in the County of Surry where her Father and Mother lived near 20 Years And further Saith that she never lived as a hired yearly Servant either before her intermarriage with the said John Fleming or since his decease nor Rented any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained any other Settlement than by her Birth to the best of this Examinants Knowledge information or Belief.

Sworn this 21st. Octr. 1791 before. R Butler D Walker

Jane Fleming
