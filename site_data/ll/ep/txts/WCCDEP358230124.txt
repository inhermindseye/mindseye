Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Ann Lunan Aged about 42 years upon her Oath Saith That about the year 1787 she intermarried with her present Husband who is at Jamaica at the Parish Church of Saint Luke Chelsea by whom she hath living two Children Vizt. Chirstianna Aged about Nine years and Charles aged about Four years That her said Husband rented and lived in a house in Whitcomb Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the County of Middlesex for near two years at the yearly rent of £24 That he hath left the said House about 4 years since which time he hath not rented any House tenement or Lodging of ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any act matter in thing whereby he may have gained a Subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinants Knowledge Information or belief

Sworn this 14th day of March 1800 before us} Ws Woodenough John Collick

Ann Lunan
