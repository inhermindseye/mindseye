Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Bulmer aged about Twenty years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she hath been informed that she was born in Farthing fields in the Parish of Saint in George in the East in the County of Middlesex where her father kept house for several years at the yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards that she this Examinant upon her Oath further saith that she never was bound an Apprentice lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year or paid Taxes And this Deponent further saith that she hath an illegitimate Child living named John aged about Ten Months born in Store Street Hospital

Sworn this 10th. day of Febry. 1797 before us  N Bond Robinson

her Mary Bulmer Mark
