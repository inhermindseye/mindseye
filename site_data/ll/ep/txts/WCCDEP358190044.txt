Middx.  John Rutter of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx on the behalf of Rachel Herbert a Lunatic  Widow of Joseph Herbert upon Oath says that the sd. Joseph Herbert before his Intermarriage with the said Rachel & whilst he was a single Man lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Stone a Brewer in Egham Hive in the Parish of Egham in the County of Surry for several years as the said Mr. Stone informed this Deponent & as this Deponent verily believes

Sworn this 31st. day of January 1758 . before} Fran: Bedwell

John Rutter
