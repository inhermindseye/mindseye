Middlesex ss  Arthur Goodwin maketh oath that divers years ago he lived in and rented a house of Twelve Pounds a year in Black Marys Hole in the Parish of Saint Pancras in the County of Middlesex and Continued in such House for the space of a year and by Means thereof as this Depont is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Depont. Saith that he hath not since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor served any Parish offices nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement & that he hath a Wife named Mary

Sworn this 26th: day of February 1768  Chris: Scott

The Mark of Arthur Goodwin
