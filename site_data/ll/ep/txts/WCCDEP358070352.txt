Middlesex  to with Elizabeth Wood Aged about Thirty Six Years on behalf of Ann Meadows her Female Bastard Chid on her Oath Says that Michael Charles who was the Father of the said Child is gone to Ireland but whether living or dead she does not know Says that the said Ann Meadows the Infant is upwards of Seven Years of Age and was born in the House of One Andrew Thomas a Shoemaker  within Two Doors of White Hart Yard Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster & County of Middlesex and that the said Ann Meadows her Child hath gained no Settlement other than by Birth.

Sworn this 15th. day of March 1776 before R Butler Wm Kitchiner

Elizth. Wood her mark
