Middx  Ann Holmes aged near 20 Years Removed from the Parish of Westham in the County of Essex by Order Dated the 14th. Day of July Inst. upon her Oath saith that she was bound an Apprentice by the Parish of Westham to one Mrs. Hughes & that she served one Year & a half Part of her said Apprentishipp with the sd Mrs. Hughes in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clement Danes & then went & lived with her Mother at Stratford about one Month & from thence went & lived a Yearly Servant with one Mrs. Lowe at Westham Abbey in the said Parish of Westham one Year & 3 Months at the Yearly Wages of Three Pounds Ten Shillings & then lived with one Mrs. White at Stratford abt. Three Months this Exaiants Apprentiship at such time not being Expired And further upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be borne a Bastard or Bastards & to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx she being Poor & not able to maintain the same when borne

& that one Joseph Beaucock  Labourer did begett the said Child or Children on her Body he the said Joseph Beaucock at such time liveing as a hired Servt. with this Examinant at the sd. Mrs- Whites at Stratford aforesaid there became acquainted with her & had Carnall knowledge of her Body sevll- times before Christmas last at one of which times he gott her with Child of the sd. Bastard Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant & that the sd. Joseph Beaucock & no other Person is the true & reall father thereof

Sworne the 15th. July 1743 before} Wm: Jones .

The Mark of Ann Holms
