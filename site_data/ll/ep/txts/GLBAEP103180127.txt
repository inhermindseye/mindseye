Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Viner  Singlewoman maketh oath That her late Father Giles Viner deced heretofore rented a house in the Hamlet of Poplar & Blackwell in the County of Middx & was Charged to and paid Poors Rates for the Same and to the knowledge or belief of this Deponent her Said Father did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Settlement elsewhere That She this Deponent was born in the Said Hamlet & never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for the Space of a year nor was ever married nor ever did any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this. 27. day of Janry 1779 before  Jno Sherwood  Jasper Clarkey

The Mark of Elizabeth Viner
