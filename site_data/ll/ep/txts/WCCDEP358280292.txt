Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex (to wit) Jane Drew aged about 47 years Upon her Oath Saith that about 27 years ago she intermarried with Wm. Drew her present Husband and that she and he said Husband Rented a House in Clerkenwell Green in the Parish of Saint James Clerkenwell in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Rent of Twelve pounds and that her Husband has deserted her about twelve Months since And that she this Examinant is Poor and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn this 1st. day of July 1790 before Sampn Wright N Forster

her Jane Drew Mark
