Middx   Sarah Barker aged about 76 Years Lodging the Back of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx upon Oath saith she is the Widow of Henry Barker who has been Dead about 20 years with whom she intermarried at Kingstone Church Portsmouth about 34 Years ago That he served Seven Years Apprentice ship with his Father  Henry Barker a Corker in Shipwright Street in the Parish of St. Mary Rotherhith in the County of Surry That he never rented Ten Pounds a year paid Land Tax or Poor Rate or was a hired Yearly Servant since his Apprenticeship which Expired about 50 Years as she has been informed and verily believes

Sworn the 2d. day of February 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Sarah Barker
