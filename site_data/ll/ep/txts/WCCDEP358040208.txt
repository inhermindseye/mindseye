Westmr. 

Saml. Axtell aged about 57 Years upon Oath saith he lived in and rented a Shed near Lincolns Inn Bachgate in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds for one Year or thereabouts and quitted the same about 14 Years ago, since which time he has not rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to his knowledge or belief Says he intermarried with his present Wife  Ann Axtell in the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch on the 1st. of June 1746 and that she is now pregnant with Child and near the time of her deliver and this Examinant being poor is not able to maintain her

Sworn 18th. June 1748 before} Wm. Jones

Samuell Axtell
