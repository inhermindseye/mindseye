Middlesex ss }  John Whitebread maketh oath that he was heretofore bound Apprentice by Indre to Benjamin Harvey of the Parish of Saint Ann in the Said County Shipwright & Served the whole of Such Apprenticeship in the Sd. Parish & this Deponent also rented a house there & was Charged to and paid Kings Taxes for the Same That he this Deponent has not Since rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards or served any Parish Office or paid any Poors rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent settlemt

Sworn this 5th day of August 1784 before R Davies John Whitebread
