Middx  Mary Woollerton Lodging at Mr. Bests a Perukemaker in Witch Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes upon Oath saith that Edward Simpson an Infant Aged about Six Months was born in Belt Lane near Spittal Fields in the Parish of Christ Church in the County of Middlesex

Sworn the 30th. Day of Octor. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Mary Woollerton
