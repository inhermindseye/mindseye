Westmr.  Robert Broughton Aged about Fifty Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Elizabeth in the Parish Church of Perth in the Kingdom of Scotland about Nineteen Years ago Says he Lived a Hired Yearly Sert . whilst a Singleman & without Child or Children with Mr. George a Victualler by St. Clements Church in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx One Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Two Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted said Service about Twenty three Years and hath not Since Rented House Paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says he has three Children by his said Wife (to wit) Elizabeth Aged about Eleven Years Mary Aged about four Years

and Robert Aged about Three Years and a half

Sworn the 21st of Decr 1764 Before} Paul Vaillant

The Mark of Robt. Broughton
