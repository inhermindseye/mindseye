Westmr. to wit Jane Taylor Aged about forty Two Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of Randall Taylor with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about Twenty Eight Years ago and who has been dead about Twelve Years and by whom she hath Two Children now living Vizt. Randall aged about Eighteen Years now at Sea and Mary Aged about Sixteen Years Says her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Ramsey a Carpenter in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand and served about Three Years of his said Apprenticeship and soon afterwards married to this Examinant and left his said Master after which he never Rented Ten pounds a Year or paid Taxes And further saith that She hath Two Children now living by one Edward Shaw who is now on board the Egmont Captn. Jackill Commander the one named Edward Aged upwards of Six Years) and the other John Aged about five Years both born in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster And Say that the said Edward Shaw did beget such Bastard Children in her Body and that he is the real and true Father thereof.

Sworn this 27th. day of June 1771 before} R. Butler

her Jane Taylor Mark

Witness Geo: Langley
