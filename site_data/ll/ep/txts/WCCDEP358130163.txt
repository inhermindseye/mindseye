Middx  Ann Norris aged about 21 years Says that John Norris her Father deceased Served his Apprenticeship of Seven Years to a Hath Dyer in the Parish of St. Olave Southwark in the County of Surry & never Rented a House or lived a hired yearly Servant as She has been informed by her said late Father & believes and further says She never was an Apprentice or yearly Servant for one year

Sworn the 15th. day of May 1756 . before} Barth: Hammond

The mark of Ann Norris
