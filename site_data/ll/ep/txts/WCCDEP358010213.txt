Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Digweed aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was Married that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mrs. Goddard at Speen in Berkshire in the Parish of Speen in the County of Berks for Two years and upwards at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about 2 years since which time she has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for One year or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th day of March 1788  Thos. Gordons  Edw : Read

Sarah Digweed
