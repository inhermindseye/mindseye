Westmr. to wit Duncan Mc Arthur Aged about forty Three Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried with Agnes his Wife at the City of Edinburgh about Twenty Years ago and by whom he hath no Children Says that he this Examinant Rented and lived in a House in Lancaster court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for Nine Months at the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about Eleven years ago and hath not since he quitted the same Ever Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn this 31st. day of March 1772 before} Tho. Kynaston  Wilm Timbrell

Duncan Mc Arthur
