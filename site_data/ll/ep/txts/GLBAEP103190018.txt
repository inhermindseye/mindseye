Middlesex ss}  Mary Day maketh oath that divers years ago her Father John Day rented a house of the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds in Goodmans yard Goodmans Fields in the Parish of St Mary Whitechapel in the sd County & Continued in the occupation thereof for Three years & upwards & did not afterwards to her knowledge or belief do any Act to gain anr Settlement & this Deponent lived there with him as part of his family That she this Depont. never was bound an Apprentice nor ever lived as a hired servant for a year in any one service That she this Depont in the month' of October one thousand seven hundred & seventy eight was married to one Thomas Gobby in the Parish Church of St Mary Whitechapel aforesd whose Settlemt. then Appeared upon an Examination taken in Writing on the oath of the said Thomas Gobby to be in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the East London by Virtue of his Apprenticeship there with one Sacheverill Dangerfield That since such Marriage So Solemnized between this Deponent & the said Thomas Gobby she this Deponent has been informed by Creditable Testimony and which she verily believes to be true and in Particulr by the Copy of a Register of a Marriage alleged

to be taken from the Register Book of marriages belonging to the Parish of Saint Paul Shadwell in the sd County that he the said Thomas Gobby was married by Banns to one Jane Howard of the same Parish Spinster in the Church there on the Eleventh day of April one thousand seven hundred & seventy four and this Deponent hath been farther Credibly informed & believes the same to be true that the said Jane Gobby is still living & has now relief Administred to her by the Overseers of the Poor of the sd Parish of St Dunstan in the East London in Consequence of her marriage with the sd Thomas Gobby as aforesaid & in right of his settlement being there still by Virtue of his sd Apprenticeship wherefore this Deponent is Advised & Apprehends that the Marriage so Celebrated between this Deponent & the sd Thomas Gobby as aforesaid is a Mere nullity & of no force or effect in law and lastly this Deponent saith that she hath a Child now living named Sarah aged about Sixteen Months which was born in St Catherines Lane in the Precinct of St. Catherine in the sd County And that she said Thomas Gobby is the Father of such Child.

Sworn this 24 day of February 1780 before W Blackmore Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Day
