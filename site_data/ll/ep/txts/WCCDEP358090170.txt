Middx  Sarah Sharratt Aged about Forty Two Years on her Oath says She never was Married Rented House Paid Taxes or ever served any Apprenticeship says She lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Vincent a Hott Presser in Bow Lane in the Parish of Saint Mary Aldermary in the City of London for two Years at the Yearly Wages of Four Pounds hath quitted her said Service Upwards of Eight Years ago since which time She hath gained no other Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 13th. day of Novr. 1780 before  Sampn Wright  John Croft  Wm. Kitchiner

her Sarah Sharratt Mark
