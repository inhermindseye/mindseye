Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex & Westmr.}  Hatfield Herts  to Wit Joseph Howard Aged about 14 years upon his Oath Saith that he was Born at Hatfield in the County of Hertford that on Account of loosing his Father and Mother and not being able to get any Work he was taken into the Workhouse of the sd. Parish of Hatfield as a Parishioner belonging the said Parish And Ran away from them And further Saith that he never lived as a hired Yearly Servant for One Year pd. Taxes or done any other Matter or thing whatsoever whereby he might have gained any Subsequent Settlemt. to the best of this Examinants Knowledge information or belief.

Sworn the 25th. day of Octr. 1792 before N Bond Tho : Gordon

His Joseph Howard Mark

Witness Richd: Dobbins
