St. Clement Danes May 12th 1792.

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Mary le Strand  (to wit) Frances Ogilvie Wife of James Ogilvie Aged 34 Years or thereabouts Upon Oath Saith that She was Married to her present at the parish Church of  St Lenard Shoreditch on the 23d November 1778. that since their Said Marriage her said Husband Rented and Resided a House in a House Nod. 10 in Swan Yard in the Parish of St Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County of Middlesex for about two Years at the Annual Rent of £14. That they quitted that House about Four Years ago That her said Husband and Never obtained any Subs. and that since her said Husband hath not obtained any Subsequent legal Settlement to her knowledge or belief And this Deponent further saith that her said Husband went in as she believes now on board The Mary Hard West Indian Indiaman in which out he went out about Christmas last

Sworn the 12th day of May 1792 . before us } B: Kennett Wm Hyde

Frances Ogilvie
