Middx.  Elizth. North aged abt. 28 Years upon Oath says she never was Married says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. George Brown a Cork Cutter near Exeter Exchange in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx. for three Years & upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds for the first Year & four Pounds for the Second & third Years & hath quitted said Service abt. five Years & hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived  a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 11th. of April 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Eliz North
