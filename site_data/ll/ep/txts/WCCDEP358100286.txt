Middlesex & Westminster}  passd. to. St. Lukes J. to wit Samuel Wheeler aged about 65 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to Mary his Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about Twenty years ago and by whom he hath no Child or Children living Says he rented and lived in a House in King Street in the Parish of Saint Luke Old Street in the County of Middlesex for Nine Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Rent of fourteen pounds, and that since he quitted the said House he has not rented any House or Tenement of ten pounds a Year, lived as an hired Yearly Servant or done any Act Matter or Thing Whereby to the best of his Knowledge he may have gained a Settlement else where than in the said Parish of Saint Luke old Street and that is poor and ill and unable to Maintain himself

Sworn this 16th. Novr- 1784 before James Croft Jno: Wright

of The Mark Samuel Wheeler
