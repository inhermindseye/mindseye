Middlesex to wit}  Mary Marratt Aged upwards of Fifty nine years her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes; Says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one Le in Norfolk Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex upwards of Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Seven Guineas has Quitted her said service upwards of Ten years as near as this Examinant can recollectt and hath not since quitting the same ever Rented House paid gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 1st day of Novr 1775 before  David Walker

her Mary Marratt Mark
