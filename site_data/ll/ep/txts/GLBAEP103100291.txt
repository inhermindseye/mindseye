Middlesex ss  Ann Mitchell the widow of Simon Mitchell deced maketh oath That divers years ago her said late husband rented & lived in a house in Crown Court in the Parish of Saint Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex and was Charged to and Paid Kings Taxes for the Same and by Means thereof as this Depont. is advised her said husband gained a legal Settlement in the Said Parish and this Deponent Saith that since her Said husbands Death she hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this day of 1770 before}
