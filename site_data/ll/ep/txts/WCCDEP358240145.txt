St. Clement Danes

Middx and Westminster }  St Andrew Holborn Middx  (to wit) Richard Bradford aged 54 Years and or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that that he was Married to his late Wife Mary at the parish Church of St George Bloomsbury abt. 27 Years ago that he Rented and Resided in a Lodging in Grays Inn passage in the parish of St Andrew Holborn above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for five Years or upwards at the Annual Rent of Ten pounds that when he quitted that he Rented and Resided in an Apartment in Theobald Road in the said parish of St Andrew Holborn about two Years and an half at the Annual Rent of Twelve pounds, that he quitted that Apartment about ten Years ago and hath not since done any Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement And this Deponent further said that his said Wife Died in the Workhouse belonging to the said parish about a Month ago and had been received by the said parish same time before

Sworn the 1st. day of September 1792 before Us  M Addington Gd: Mitchell

R Bradford
