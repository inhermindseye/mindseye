Middx  Cathn. Thompson upon Oath saith she is the Wido. of Joseph Thompson decd. and that she has been informed & verily believes her sd. late husband never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servt. nor ever rented ten Pounds a year or pd. Parish taxes and that his father Solomon Thompson decd. rented & lived in a house & pd Scot & Lot in the parish of Grt. Osborn in the West Riding of york for Sevl. Years & that her sd. late husbd. & herself had a Certificate Dated 27th. May 1725 from the sd. parish of Gt. Osbourn to All Saints Peasholm in the sd. County of york acknowledging them legally Settled in Great Osborn afsd.

Sworn May 28th 1753 : before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Cath: Thompson
