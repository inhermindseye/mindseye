Middx ss  John Hassler Maketh oath that about Twenty five years ago he lived as a hired servant by the year (being then a singleman) with Dutton Seaman Esqr . who then lived in the parish of Saint Michael Bassishaw in the City of London at the wages of Seven pounds by the year and Continued in such service for the Space of a year & by Means thereof gained a legal Settlement in the same parish And this Depont. saith that he hath not since gained any subsequent Settlement havg never since been when a Single Man a hired servant by ye year nor rented a house of Ten pounds a year or upwards nor served any Parish offices nor done any Act to Acquire anyother settlemt. And that he hath a Wife Named Mary and three Children to wit Margaret aged abt. eighteen Years Mary aged about twelve Years & John Aged about Tweelve eleven Years the lawfull Issue of this Depont. by his sd wife.

Sworn this 9th day of Janry 1756 before us  Richd: Ricards  Chris: Scott

his John Hassler Mark
