Middx to wit Sarah Ellmoor Aged about 42 Years on her Oath say she Intermarried to her late Husband Thos. Elmoor at the Parish Church of St. Saviours, Southwark about 20 Years ago and by whom she hath no Child or Children living says her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Thos. Burgess a Waterman in Queens Arms Court near Black Fryers Bridge in the Parish of Christ Church Southwark in the County of Surry and served his said Master four years the first Part of his sd. Apprenticeship when his sd. Master gave him up his Indentures & afterwds. went to Sea And further Says that her sd. late Husband never gained any other Settlemt. either by Renting House Tene or Lodging of £10. a year pd. Taxes or otherwise howsoever neither hath this Examinant since her sd. Husbands death

Sworn this 30th. day of Jany. 1783 before  Thos. Gilbert  Wm Kitchiner

her Sarah Elmoor Mark
