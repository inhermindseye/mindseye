Middx  Eliza: House upon her Oath saith that she is the Wido - of Henry House who was bound an Apprentice to & served Seven Years Apprentiship with one Mr. Hancock Copper Smith in Turnagain Lane in the Parish of St. Sepulchers London that since his said Apprentiship her said late Husband in his Life nor this Examinant since his Death never rented ten Pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servant for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne March 16th. 1742 before me} Cha. Egerton

The Mark of Eliza House
