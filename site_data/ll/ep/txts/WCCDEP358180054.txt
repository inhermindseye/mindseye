Middx  Mary Cotterell upon her Oath saith that she is the Widow of Samuel Cotterell that her sd. late Husband about Two Years since lived in and rented a House in Chappell Street in the Parish of St. Ann Soho in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds & upwards for the Space of Ten Years & upwards that since he left the sd. House the sd. Samuel Cotterell in his Life nor this Examinant Since his Death never rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that She hath Two Children viz: William aged Five Yrs. & Ann aged Two Years

Sworne 29th- Decr. 1742 before}
