Westmr.  James Bond one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and County of Middlesex on the behalf of Hannah Sawyer an Infant aged about Ten Years and Elizabeth Sawyer an Infant aged about Eight Years the Children of William Sawyer by Hannah his Wife deceased, Upon Oath says, he this Deponent as been informed and which information he verily believes to be true that the said William Sawyer Rented and occupied two Houses in Chick Lane in the Liberty of Saffron hill Hatton Garden and Ely Rents in the County of Middx the one of the Yearly Rent of Twenty five Pounds the other of the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds, for the space of Ten Months or thereabouts and quitted the said houses about the Month of April 1766 and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes to this Deponents knowledge and as he verily believes.

Sworn the 10th, day of April 1767 , before} John Machin 

James Bond
