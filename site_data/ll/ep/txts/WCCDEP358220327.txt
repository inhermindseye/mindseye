Westmr. to wit Catherine Lee Aged about forty Six Years on her Oath says she is the Widow of John Lee who has been dead about Fourteen or fifteen Years ago says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Collin a Cabinet Maker the Corner of Greet Street Soho in the Parish of Saint Anns Soho in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of fifteen Months at the Yearly Wages of five pounds hath quitted her said Service about Eleven Years and hath not since she quitted
