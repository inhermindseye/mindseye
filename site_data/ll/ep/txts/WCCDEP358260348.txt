Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Squires aged about 55 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mrs. Wilmot who lodged at Mr Williams a Watchmaker in the Strand near Catherine Street in the Parish of Saint Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly wages of Ten Guineas that she hath quitted said Service upwards Four years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby She might have gained a Subsequent Settlement and further saith that she is now Chargeable to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this day of Octr. 1798 before us }  E Read

her Mary Squires Mark
