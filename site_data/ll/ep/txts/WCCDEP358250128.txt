Middlesex and Westminster }

Settlement in St. C. D Mary Thompson aged about 60 years upon her Oath saith that about 32 years ago she Intermarried with John Thompson her late Husband (who has been dead about Six years) at the Parish Church of Saint Marys at Hull in Yorkshire that her said late Husband informed this Deponents that he served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Benjamin Davis of Clare Market  Butcher and that after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never to this Deponents knowledge Information or belief lived as an hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year Paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby he might have gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 30th. day of Septr. 1794 before} N Bond

her Mary Thompson mark

Witness Richd. Dobbins
