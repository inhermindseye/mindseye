Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit John Weston aged about 73 years upon his Oath saith that he Rented and lived in a house in the Strand opposite Surry Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for about Six years at the yearly Rent of £34 that he hath quitted said house about Forty years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent settlement

Sworn this 20th- day of April 1797 J Hunter

John Weston
