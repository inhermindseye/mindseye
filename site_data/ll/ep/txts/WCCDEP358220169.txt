Westminster  Jane Murphy Aged Forty five Years or thereabouts on her Oath Saith She is the Widow of Edward Murphy who has been Dead about Sixteen Years Says that being a Widow and without Child or Children except one named James (now Provided for) she lived as a Hired Yearly Servant with Rowland Cartwright at the Fountain in Clare Street Clare Market in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster upwards of thirteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about four Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 30th: day of July 1770 before me} 

Jane Murphy
