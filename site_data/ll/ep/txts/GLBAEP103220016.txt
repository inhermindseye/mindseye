Middlesex ss  Mary Hoskins  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was heretofore hired & lived as hired Servt at Certain Wages by & with Frederick Franks in Upper East smithfield in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Sd County Victualler & Continued in Such Service under Such hiring for about the Space of Six Years That She has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a year elsewhere nor was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to her Knowledge or belief to gain a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 7. day of January 1786 before  Robt. Smith

The Mark of Mary Hoskins
