St. Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to Wit Mary Chiswick aged 40. Years upon her Oath Saith that She never was Married, and that this Deponent lived as a Hired yearly Servant with Mr Davidson Printer Russell Place, Russell Street, in the Parish of St. Martins in the fields for the Space of Eighteen Months at the yearly wages of five Pounds, and that She Quitted said Service in augt: 1793. Since which time she has not done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10. day of May 1796 before us N Bond W Kinnard

her Mary (M. C.) Chriswick Mark

Witness Present Jas Talboys
