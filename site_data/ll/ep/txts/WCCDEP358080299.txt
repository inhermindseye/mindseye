Middx to wit Mary Craven Aged about Fifty Five years on her Oath says she is the Wife of Lancelot Craven who now is in France as she believes and with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint George Southwark in the County of Surry about Six years ago Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House in Stanhope Street Clare Markett for four years and upwards at the yearly Rent of Thirty four Pounds hath quitted the said House about four years ago since which time her said Husband never gained any other Subsequent Settlement to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 24th. day of Octr. 1778 before me R Butler

Mary Craven
