Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Robert Gibson aged about 50 years upon his Oath saith That-he Rented a Shop and Parlour unfurnished of a Mr. Lock in Clements Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months or thereabouts at the yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds Ten Shillings That he hath quitted said Apartments about Twelve years since which time he hath not Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 14th. day of May 1798 before me } Saml. Edward

her Robert Gibson mark

H Day Beadle
