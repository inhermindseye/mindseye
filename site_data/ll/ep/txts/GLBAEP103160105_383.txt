Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Kemble the widow of Thomas Kemble deced maketh oath that her Said late husband has informed her & which She believes to be true that divers years ago he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by the year at Certain wages by & with a Certain Person who kept a publick house (but whose name this Deponent has forgot) in the Parish of Banbury in the County of Oxford & Continued in such Service for a year & upwards & by means thereof gained a legal Settlement there That her Said husband died in the Sd Parish & his Settlement was admitted to be there at his Death That to this Deponts knowledge or belief her Said late husband did not after Such his Servitude as aforesd. do any Act to gain another Settlement Nor hath She this Deponent Since his death been married nor done any Act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 19 day of June 1776 before  Parkr Rowlands

The Mark of Elizabeth Kemble
