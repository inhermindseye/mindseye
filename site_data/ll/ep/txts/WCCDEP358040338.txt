Westmr.  Christr Windowmor upon Oath saith he Served Seven Years Apprentiship with Mr. and Mrs. Fleetwood Tripe sellers and lived two Years or thereabts. the latter Part of his sd. Apprentiship with the sd. Mrs. Fleetwood in Peter Street near the Horseferry in the Parish of Saint John Evangelist in the Liberty of Westmr. Says he never rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to his knowledge or beleif

Sworn March 20th. 1748 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Christr. Windowmor
