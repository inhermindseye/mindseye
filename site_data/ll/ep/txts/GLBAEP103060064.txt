Middx  John Burges Maketh Oath and Saith that about he served all his Apprentiship of Seven years (to which he was bound by Indenture) to Thomas Taylor in Blue Anchor Yard in the parish of Saint Mary White Chapel in the County of Middx  Carman and has Gained no Subsequent Settlemt- to his knowledge And that he hath a wife named Ann

Sworn 30 May 1746 before us Robert Dennett

his Jno. Burges mark

Burges was blind
