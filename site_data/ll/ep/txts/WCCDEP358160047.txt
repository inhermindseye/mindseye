Middx The Examination of Mary Taylor on the behalfe of Jane the Wife of Henry Taylor Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx Taken the 4th. Day of May 1739 Who upon her Oath saith that upwards of Twenty Years ago the above Named Henry Taylor Rented a House at Twickenham in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of Thirty Pounds & lived in the same for the Space of Seven Years & upwards And this Examinant further saith that the sd. Henry Taylor hath not since that time Rented any House Lands or Tenements of the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife That the sd. Henry Taylor now Absconds from Jane his sd. Wife & she being Sick & Poor is not able to maintaine herself

Sworne the Day & Year above mentioned before} Tho Lane  Jas Gronous

Mary Taylor
