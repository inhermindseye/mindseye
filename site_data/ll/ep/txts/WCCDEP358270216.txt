Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Tarrant aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that on the 9th. day of July 1788 she Intermarried with John Tarrant her late Husband who has been dead about four years at the Parish Church of Laleham That she hath no Child or Children living by her said late Husband That before her Intermarriage to her said late Husband and whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Corbett at No. 92 in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex and Liberty of Westminster for Twelve Months and upwards at the yearly Wages of Eight Guineas that since she quitted said service she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing to gain a subsequent settlement And further this Deponent further upon her Oath saith that on the 16th. day of October 1794 she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Licensed House in Water Lane fleet Street which hath since been Christened by the Name of Matthew That one Frances Spencer a Post Boy at the White Hart at Windsor in the County of Berks is the father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said Frances Spencer is the only real and true father thereof

Sworn this 5th. day of Decr. 1794 before} John Hitcher

her Sarah Tarrant Mark

Child named Matthew Tarrant Sent to Enfield the 31st. Decr. 1795

14th. Janry. 1796 Man taken Paid 5 Guineas down gave a Joint Note of Hand for £7..7..0 payable on the 10th. Octr. 1796

19th. Octr. 1796 5 Guas pd. in part 2 Guineas now due

14th. Jany. 1796 Release Exted
