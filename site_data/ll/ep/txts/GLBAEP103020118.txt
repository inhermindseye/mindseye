Middlesex ss.  John Everitt maketh Oath that about Fifteen Years ago he was hired and lived as a hired Servant by the Year with Mr. Eate of No 61 Upper Eastsmithfield in the Parish of St. Botolph without Aldgate in the County of Middlesex Cheesemonger at the Yearly Wages of Seven Pounds and continued in his Service under such hiring about Seven Years that he has not since he quitted his Service lived as a hired Servant by the Year in any other Parish or Place for the space of One Year together or Rented any House or Lodging of the Yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or paid any Poors Rate or Kings Tax or done any Act to his knowledge or Belief to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this Day of Sepr. 1794 before}
