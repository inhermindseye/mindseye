Saint Clement Danes .

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Maria Goft aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Sarah Brady who kept the Assembly House at Mile End in the Hamlet of Mile End Old Town in the Parish of Saint Dunstan Stepney in the County of Middlesex for Twelve Months at the yearly Wages of Three Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Seven years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 16th. day of June 1797 before us } Jas Oldham Oldham Jr. Solsey

her Maria Goft Mark
