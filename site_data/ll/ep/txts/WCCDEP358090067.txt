Middx to wit Mary Ann Welch Aged about Twenty one Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House or Paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr. Lynch at the Sign of the Chequers in Shire Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Two Years and upwards at the Yearly Wages of five Pounds hath quitted her said Service about three Months ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 26th. day of Augt. 1779 before}

her Mary Welch Mark
