Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Mary Charlotte Turbill aged about Nineteen years upon her Oath saith that she never was married lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months, bound an Apprentice or ever Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year or paid Taxes That her father Ethelburt Turbill Rented and lived in a house in George Street in the Parish of Saint George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . for Twenty Years and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Twenty two Pounds that he hath quitted said House about Seven years since which time he hath not Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a Year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby he might to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief  have gained a subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 29th. day of Septr. 1797 before us N Bond J Hunter

Mary Charlotter Turbill
