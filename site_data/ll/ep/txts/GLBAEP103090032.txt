Middlesex ss  Mary Sandford  widow of Martin Sandford deced Maketh oath That her said late Husband has informed her that divers years ago he rented a house in Mill Street in the Parish of Saint Mary Magdalen Bermondsey in the County of Surry at the yearly rent of Eleven Pounds and Paid all Parochial Taxes in the said Parish and by Means thereof as this Depont is advised gained a legal settlement there And this Deponent farther saith that to her knowledge information or belief her said late husband never afterwards rented a house of Ten Pounds a year or upwards nor served any Parish offices nor Paid any Poors Rates or kings Taxes nor did any act to gain a Subsequent Settlement Nor hath this Deponent done any Act since her said husbands death to gain a Settlement in her own right to her knowledge or belief

Sworn this 9th. day of April 1766 before  Chris: Scott  Rob Pell

Mary Sandford
