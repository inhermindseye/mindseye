Middx.  Mary Coward aged abt. 65 Years upon Oath says she is the widow of Robt. Coward who has been dead abt. Nine Months says her said husband Rented a house in Church Lane in the Parish of St. Martin in the fields in the Liberty of Westmr . of the Yearly Rent of Twenty Pounds lived therein abt. seven One Year and quitted it abt. eight Twenty Years before his death and never after Rented Ten Pounds a Year or Paid Parish Taxes says she has not since his death Rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or Otherwise done any Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 15th. of Novr. 1758 . before} Paul Vaillant  Barth: Hammond

Mary Coward
