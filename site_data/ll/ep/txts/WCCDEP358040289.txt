Middx 

Frances Champion  Wido . of Robert Champion upon Oath her sd. late husband was an Articled Clerk to Mr. Knipe of Garden Court in the Temple but lay all that time at his fathers opposite St Clemts Chamber and never rented ten pounds a Year pd Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. an any other Place to her Knowledge or belief Says she has not since her sd. husbands decease rented ten pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly Servant for one Yr. and that her sd. late husbands father Wm. Champion before this Examints. Intermarriage with his Son rented a house in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx of the Yearly Rent of £30 for Sixteen Years or there abts.

Sworn Novr. 19th. 1748 before} Wm: Jones

Frances Champion
