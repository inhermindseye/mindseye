Middx to wit Mary Clarke Aged about Thirty Six Years on her Oath Says she is the Wife of Alexander Clarke (who now lies ill) with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of Saint Martin in the Fields about Eighteen Years ago and by whom she hath no Children by him now living Says her said Husband Rented and lived in a House known by the Sign of the White Lyon in Hemings's Row in the Parish of Saint Martin in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster for several Years at the Yearly Rent of thirty five pounds & also rented other houses to the amount in the whole of £60 Pounds and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Ten Years ago since which time her said Husband hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenemt. of £10. a Year paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever.

Sworn this 17th. day of Febry 1779 before  Abrm Baley R Butler

her Mary Clarke Mark
