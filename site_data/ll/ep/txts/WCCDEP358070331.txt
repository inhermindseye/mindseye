Middx & Westmr}  John Chappell aged about fifty Three years on his Oath Says that He Rented and lived in a House in Grays Inn Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew above the Bars in the County of Middlesex for several years at the yearly Rent of Sixteen pounds and paid all parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House upwards of Ten years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained any other Subsequent Settlement.

Sworn this 29th. day of Decr. 1775 before M Addington

Jno. Chappell
