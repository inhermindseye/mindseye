Middx  Grace Doughty aged about 25 Years Removed from the Parish of St. Giles in the Feilds in the County of Middx by Order Dated the 10th: Day of Septr. Instt: upon her Oath saith that she was never married that about 12 Years ago she was bound an Apprentice to Mr. Danl. Chandler the Corner of Dirty Lane in the Strand in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx  Fanmaker and Served him as an Apprentice abt. 5 Years & then the said Mr. Chandler & this Exaiant parted by Consent Says that she never rented Ten pounds a Year paid parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleife

Sworne 11th. Septr. 1744 before} Wm: Jones

Grace Doughty
