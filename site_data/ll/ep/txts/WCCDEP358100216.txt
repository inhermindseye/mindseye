Middx & Westmr.}  Martha Palmer Aged 23 Years on her Oath says she never was Married Rented House Paid Taxes or ever lived a hired yearly for one Year says her Father Jno Palmer rented & lived in a House Brighthelmstone at the Yearly Rent of £10 & Paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath Quitted the said House about 12 Months ago since which time she never gained any or Settlement than as aforesaid And further on her Voluntary Oath says that on the 30th day of April last past she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the House of Mr Mc Grigger No 2 Plough Court  Cary Street which is Intended to be Baptized by the name of John, and is now Chargeable to the said Parish of St Clement Danes & likely to Continue so And further says that one Christopher Cheesewright  Servant who lately lived with Captn Thornton & who now lives with a Gentn. who now Uses the St. James's Hotell in the Hay Markett is the Father thereof he having had Carnal knowledge of her Body at her Fathers House at Brithelmstone aforesaid in the begining of September last but the Particular day she does not remember & at several different times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Male Bastard Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid & that the said Christopher Cheesewright & no or Person did beget the same & that he is the real & true Father thereof

Sworn this 24th day of May 1784 Before R Butler

Martha Palmer
