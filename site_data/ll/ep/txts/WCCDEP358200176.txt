Westmr.  Rachel Williams Aged about Fifty four Years upon Oath Says she is the Widow of Wm. Williams with whom she Intermarried at the Fleet London about Twenty One Years ago who has been Dead about Twenty Years Says that since her said Husband Death she being without Child or Children Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Coombes a Taylor in Wych Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster One Year and a half at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about Seven Years & hath not Since Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant for One Year

Sworn the 1st. of December 1764 Before}

The Mark of W Rachel Williams
