Westmr  Elizth. Waller Aged about Thirty four Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Henry Waller a Soldier with whom she Intermar'd in the Savoy Chapel on the twenty Sixth of May 1756 by whom she has two Children (Viz) Henry Aged about Six Years and Elizth. Aged about Two Years Says her said Husband whilst a Singleman Lived a hired Yearly Servant with Alexr. Hillman a farmer for One Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of three Pounds or thereabouts says he served the said Mr. Hilman the last Nine Months of his said Service in the parish of Horsted Keynes in the County of Sussex and after he quitted his said Service Never Rented Ten pounds by the Year pd. Parish Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Sert. for One Year. as this Examinant hath been Informed by her said husband and which Information she Verily believes to be true

Sworn the 8th Day of February 1764 Before} Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Waller

Upon Mr Ralph Williams Complt Ovr J. W
