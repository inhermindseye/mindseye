Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Andrew Holborn below the Burs to wit Mary Bayley aged about 25 years on her Oath says she Intermarried to Wm Bayley at the Parish Church of Saint Leonard Shoreditch about Six years ago who is now insane and by whom she hath two Children living vizt. William aged four years and upwards James aged One Month and upwards Says that her said Husbd. Served Seven years Apprenticeship to one Wm. Mifflin a Sawyer in Shoe Lane in the Parish of Saint Andrew Holborn below the Barrs in the City of London and that after he quitted his said Apprenticeship he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of her Knowledge Information on belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewere

Sworn this 16th. day of Febry 1785 G Mercer Jno Barnfather

Mary Bailey
