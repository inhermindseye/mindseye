Middx  Mary Brown aged 59 Years lodging in Castle Street  Long Acre on the behalf of her Grand daughter Maria Louisa Nicholson an Infant aged upwards of two Years the daughter of Lewis Nicholson by Sarah his Wife both absconded from her upon Oath says that the said Lewis Nicholson Rented a house next the Slaughter house in Sheffield Street Claremarket in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. in the County of Middx. of the Yearly Rent of Eighteen Pounds lived therein upwards of two Months & Quitted same at Michaelmas 1757 and hath not since Rented ten Pounds a Year or paid Parish Taxes to this Examinants knowledge or belief.

Sworn this 9th. day of June 1758 . before} Barth: Hammond

The Mark of Mary Brown
