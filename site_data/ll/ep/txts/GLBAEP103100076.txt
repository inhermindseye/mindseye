Middlesex ss  Luke Macklin maketh oath That divers Years ago he rented a house known by the Sign of the Black horse Alehouse in Diet Street in the Parish of St Giles in the Fields in the County of Middx at the yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds & Continued in the occupation of such House for Six Months and was Charged to & paid the Poors Rate for the Same And by Means thereof games a legal Settlement there And this Depont. Saith that he has not Since rented a house of the yearly Rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards nor Served any Parish office nor Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes nor to his knowledge or belief hath he done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that he hath a wife named Margaret to whom he was lawfully married about fifty years ago in the Kingdom of Ireland .

Sworn this 10d day of February 1769  Geo: Garrett  Richd. Haris

Luke Macklin
