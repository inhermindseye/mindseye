Middlesex (to wit) Mary Thells  singlewoman aged twenty four Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith that she never was married according to the sites of the Church of England-that she lived as an hired Yearly Servant with Lloyd in Cecil Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of ye. for upwards of a Year that she quitted her Service about three Years ago and since their hath not done any Act whereby to the best of her Knowledge she may have gained any subsequent Settlement that on the Fifth Day of February in the Year four Lord 1786 she was delivered of a Male Child at the  Worhouse of the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields in the County of Middlesex since Christmas by this Name of John Mc Cullough and that  Lockham Mc Cullough who as a Bricklayer by Made and to whom this Examinants was married by a Roman Cathelic Priest is the father of the said Child

he having had Casual Knowledge of this Examinants Body and by the same thereon

Sworn this 12th. June 1786 before  Wm Kitchiner

her Mary Thells Mark

the father is now a Pensioner of Chelsea Hospital -Bran Out Pensioner
