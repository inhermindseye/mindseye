St Clemt Danes

Middlesex & Westminster} (to wit) Martha Buckan  Wife of Alexander Buckan Upon Oath saith that she is now of the Age of Sixty three years or thereabouts that she was Married to her said Husband Alexander Buckan at the parish Church of St. Dunstan in the West abt 28 years ago (by  Licence), that since their said Marriage her Husband Rented and with her Resided in an House sit in Clements Lane in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the said County and Liberty about one year & a quarter at the Yearly Rent of £15 that her said Husband quitted that House about 26 or 27 years ago and since hath not done any Act or thing to entitle him to any other legal Settlement to this Deponents knowledge or belief that they have no child or Children now living And that her said Husband (being now dangerously Ill) is become incapable of providing for himself and  this Deponent

Sworn the 23rd July 1791 before me  Wm Hitchiner

Martha Buckan 's Mark
