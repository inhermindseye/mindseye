Middlesex ss}  Joseph Perry maketh oath that divers Years ago he was duly bound to & Served the whole of his Apprenticeship With Charles Rattey in Old Brentford in the Parish of Ealing in the County of Middlesex  Fisherman That since such his apprenticeship he has not rented a house of the yearly rent or Value of Ten Pounds or upwards or Served any Parish office or Paid any Poors Rates or Kings Taxes or done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement That he hath five Children & lawful Issue by his late wife deced now living namely John Aged about Eleven Years Richard Aged about Ten Years Thomas Aged about Five years & three Quarters of a year Henry Aged about three years & James Aged about two Years none of whom have as Yet done any Act to gain a Settlement in their own right & that he this Deponent was lawfully married to his Said late wife

Sworn this day of 1780 before}
