Middlesex ss}  Elizabeth Gray maketh oath that she has been informed by the Examination of her Father William Gray upon oath & which She believes to be true that he was born in Liquor Pond Street in the Liberty above the Bars in the Parish of St Andrew Holborn in the said County & that he never did any act to gain a settlement in his own right That She can give no Account respecting the Settlement of her Fathers Parents or eithr of them & that She was never bound an apprentice nor ever lived as a hired Servant for a year nor was ever married nor ever did any act to gain a settlement for herself

Sworn this 20th day of June 1783 before R Davies J. Hawley

The Mark of Elizabeth Gray
