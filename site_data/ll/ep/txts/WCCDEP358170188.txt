Eliz: Callahan upon her Voluntarily Oath saith that she is a Singlewoman & was never married & that on the Sixteenth Day of Novr. last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at one Mrs. Fosters in a Court over agt. St. Clements Church in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx & that one Robert Burlington a Baker did begett the sd. Child on her Body he the sd. Robt. Burlington sometime in the Month of February last having Carnall Knowlege of her Body at the Strand Coffee House in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes & at Sevll. other Times since at one of which Times he got her with Child & the sd. Female Bastard Child which she was delivered of as aforesd. & that he & no other Person is the true & reall Father thereof

Sworne the 1st. of December 1741 before} Jas: Gronous

The mark of Eliz: Callahan
