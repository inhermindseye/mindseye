Desertion 

St Clement Danes May 30th 1793

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement  three Children  Desertion to wit Charlotte Jackson Wife of John Jackson aged thirty seven years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that She was Married to her present Husband John Jackson  aged at the parish Church of Saint Ann in the Liberty of Westmr . on or about the twenty third day of August 1793 1777 . (by Banns) that the her said Husband Rented and Resided in a House called the Stars in Carey Street in the parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for about two years at the  Rent of twenty pounds for the first year and twenty five pounds for the second and quitted the same about  Nine years ago and hath never since Rented any House or Apartment of ten pounds a year or done any other Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby to obtain any subsequent legal Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief. Saith that she hath four three Children by her said Husband born in lawful Wedlock now living with her this Examinant Namely William born on the seventh January 1787 Margaret born on or about the fifteenth day of March 1790 and Frances born on or about the twenty second day of October 1792 And saith that her said Husband hath refused to provide for this

Deponent and her said Children and that she is or unable to support herself and said Children without assistance and that they are become chargeable to the said parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn the  30th. day of May 1793 before me} Wm. Kitchiner

Chalotte Jackson
