Middx  Ann Tod (in Nightingale) Maketh Oath that About A Year and a have half Ago John Pool (who is now gon to Sea ) Rented a house in Oxford Road in the parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middx And paid Twenty pounds and year for the same and lived therein three Quarters of A year as this Deponent hath heard and verily believes And that the said John Pool s wife  Mary is dead and left two Children by her said husband Ann aged about Nine years and Sarah aged about three years as this Deponent hath Also heard and Verily believes

Sworn this 20th. day of Augt. 1743 before Robert Dennett

Ann Tod
