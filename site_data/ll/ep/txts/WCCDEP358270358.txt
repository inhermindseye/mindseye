Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Young aged about 19 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That on the First day of March Instant she was delivered of a Male Bastard Child in the Workhouse belonging to the Parish of Saint Clement Danes which hath since been Christened by the name of James That one Robert Stickling alias Stickland who lodges (As this Deponent believes) at the White House Inn in Fetter Lane is the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child of which she was so delivered as aforesaid and that the said Robert Stickling otherwise Stickland is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 28th. day of March 1798 . before me } J Hunter

her Sarah Young Mark

Man never Apprehended

Child born in the house the 2d. day of Novr. 1797 named James

Qrs. what become of this Child Wher. any & what Allowance.
