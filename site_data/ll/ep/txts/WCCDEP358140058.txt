Middx.  Mary Barton aged abt. 45 Years upon Oath says she never was married & that she lived a hired Yearly Servt. with Mr. Lawrence Page at Wills Coffee house in Searl Street opposite Lincolns Inn Back Gate in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx for four Years at the Yearly Wages of six Pounds & hath quitted said Service upwards of Eight Years & hath not since rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or lived a Yearly hired Servant for one Year

Sworn this 3d. day of Decr. 1757 before} Barth: Hammond

Mary Barton
