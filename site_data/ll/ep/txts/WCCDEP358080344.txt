Middlesex to wit Thos. Dobbs Aged about fifty Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to his late Wife Phebe at the Fleet London about Thirty Years ago and who died about fourteen Months ago and by whom he hath Three Children all of whom are now provided for Says before his Intermarriage to his late Wife he lived as a hired Yearly Servant with one Mr John Tavernor a Butcher at Chingford in the County of Essex upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of four pound Ten Shillings besides Meat Drink washing and Lodgings and after this Examinant Intermarried to his said late Wife he Rented and lived in a House at Chigford aforesaid for Sevl. Years at the Yearly Rent of five Pound and paid Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the said House about four Years ago and hath not since he quitted the same ever Rented House paid Taxes or done any other Act whereby to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of March 1779 before R Butler

his Thos. Dobbs Mark
