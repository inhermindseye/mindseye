Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Ann Macgee aged about 28 years upon her Oath saith that about 7 years ago she Intermarried with Thomas Mc.gee her Present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Andrew Holborn that her said Husband has left this Examinant about 12 Months since which time she has been informed that he is at Sea that her said Husband Rented a House in Nags head Court Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes for Two years and upwards at the yearly Rent of £18 that he hath quitted said House about 12 Months since which time he has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of May 1788  Wm Achiner

her Ann Mc.gee Mark
