Middex. & Westmr}  Sarah Haynes Aged about 25 Years on her Oath says she never was Married nor ever Rented House or Paid Taxes says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with General Whitmore in the Parish of Lower Slaughter in the County of Gloucester for 5 Years at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds Five Shillings hath Quitted her said Service about Four Years ago & hath not Since she quitted the same ever Rented House Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Charge- able to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to Maintain the same when born Says that one Butler Powell a Journeman Glover who Lives at one Mr. Linghams at Somerset Watergate had Carnal Knowledge of her Body on the Sixth Day of May last at the House of her Mother in Fryar Street in the City of Worster and at Sevl. times afterwards at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant and that the said Butler Powell and no other Person did beget the same

Sworn this 16th day of December 1769 before me } Pet Planck

Sarah Haynes
