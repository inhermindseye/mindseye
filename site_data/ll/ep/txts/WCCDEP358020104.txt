Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Sarah Morrell aged about 33 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Branches a Brushmaker the corner of Buckingham Street in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of £4 that she hath quitted said Service about Seven Months since which time she hath done No Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 22d. day of Septr. 1788 G Mercer Tho. Collin

Sarah Morrell
