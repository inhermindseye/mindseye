Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Tweedie aged about 40 years upon her Oath saith that she Intermarried with Crofts Tweedie her present husband who is now from her at the Parish Church of Saint James Westmr . about Seven years ago That her said late husband informed this Examinant that whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with John Tweedie at the Sign of the Hannah of Venison in Bell Yard in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London for Eighteen Months or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Seven Pounds and that since he quitted said Service he never to the best of the Examinats knowledge Information or belief did any Act Matter or thing whereby he Might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 6th. day of Febry. 1798 before us M Addington Ru Clarke

her Elizabeth Tweedie Mark
