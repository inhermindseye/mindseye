Westmr.  Elizabeth Frow aged about 41 Years upon Oath says that being a Widow and without Child or Children she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Stirridge a Breeches maker in Hemlock Court in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for one Year and upwards at the Yearly Wages of three pounds and ten Shillings and hath Quitted said Service about one Year since which time she hath not Rented house paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 8th. day of October 1765 , before} R Kilsha

The Mark of Elizth. Frow
