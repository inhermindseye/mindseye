Westmr  Jane Polfrey Aged about Twenty Six Years upon Oath Says she was never Marryed Says she Lived a Hired Yearly Servant with Mr. John Wilson a Jeweller in Arrundell Street in the Strand in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr. upwards of One Year at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds hath quitted said Service about Two Months and hath not since Rented House or paid Taxes And further upon her Voluntary Oath Says she is pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to Become Chargeable to the said parish of St. Clement Danes she being poor and unable to Maintain the same when Born Says One Richard Chapman  Servt .

to Mr. Minver a Lighterman near the Bull Stairs Southwark had Carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body sometime in the Month of May last and at several other times since at One of which times he got her with Child of the said Child or Children with which she is now pregnant.

Sworn the 7th Day of January 1765 Before} Paul Vaillant

Jane Polfrey
