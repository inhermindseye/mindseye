Middx  Eliz: Wife of William Foreman upon her Oath saith that on the 2d. Day of March abt. Sixteen Years since she intermarried with her sd. Husband Wm- Foreman & that her sd. Husband was bound an Apprentice to and served Part of his Apprentiship with one Mr. Lancaster Mains in Stoney Lane in the Parish of St. Olave  Southwark in the County of Surry  Carman and run away from his sd. Master that her sd. Husband hath not since he left his sd. Master rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes lived with any Person as a Yearly hired Servt. for the Space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that she hath one Child vizt. Willm. aged abt. 14 yrs. and being Poor is not able to maintaine herselfe and Child

Sworne the 24th- October 1741 before} G Poulson Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Eliz: Foreman
