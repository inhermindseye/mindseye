Middlesex ss}  Dinah Ager  Singlewoman maketh oath that She was here to fore hired & lived as a yearly hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with James Killby  Chandler in the Parish of St Botolph witht Aldgate in the Said County & Continued there in Such Service under Such hiring for the Space of two Years & upwards & has not Since lived as a hired Servant for the Space of a years elsewhere or was She ever married nor hath She done any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this. 6. day of Decemr 1787 before  R Davies

The Mark of Dinah Ager
