Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Edward Burbidge one of the Beadles of the Parish of St. Clement Danes on behalf of 2 illegitimate Children of Ann Pollord who has deserted them vizt. Ann aged about 4 years and Mary aged about 3 years Maketh Oath and Saith That he has been credibly informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the Ann the younger was born a Bastard in the Hou of one Mr. Lambley a Butcher in Fore Street in the Parish of Saint Giles Cripplegate in the City of London and that said Mary was likewise born a Bastard at the sd. Mr. Lambleys House in the Parish and City aforesaid

Sworn this 20th day of July 1787  Philip Dyor D Walker

Edwd. Burbidge

Passd: 21st: July 1787 . to Cripplegate
