Middx  Andrew Brinkworth aged Fifty Years upon Oath Says he Rented a Farm in the Parish of Lindhurst in the County of Hants of the Yearly Rent of Forty Pounds and Lived On the same about Five Years and hath Quitted the same about Nine Years, and hath not since Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a Yearly Servt. Says he has One Child Rebecca Aged Nineteen Years, & that She never Rented Pounds a Year Paid Parish Taxes or Lived a Yearly hired Servant .

Sworn the 9th. Day of Decr. 1755 Before} Fran: Bedwell

Andw. Brinkworth
