Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster}  passd. to St. Mary Le Strand to wit John Browne aged about 45 years on his Oath says he Intermarried to never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Mervillan Haberdasher in the Parish of Saint Mary Le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr facing of Somersett House in the Strand for Eighteen Months at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted said Service about Twelve years ago since which time he has done no Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of his Knowledge Information or belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere

Sworn this 18th day of Febry 1785  Wm Kitchiner J Reading

J Brown
