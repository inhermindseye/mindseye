St Clement Danes July 25th 1794

Middlesex and Westminster }  Settlement in Rolls Liberty Middx  Their legitima- 3 Children Alicia born 24th July, 1787 Thos. 12th July, 1789 Ann 29th Mar/94  (to wit) Elizabeth Lawrence Widow of Joseph Lawrence and aged 45 years or thereabouts Upon Oath saith that she was Married to the said Joseph Lawrence at the Parish Church of Waddington in the County York on or about the 24th day of March in or about the year of our Lord 1772 that since after their said Marriage her said Husband Rented & with her this Examinant resided in a House in Whites Alley in the Liberty of the Rolls in the County of Middlesex for a year or upwards, at the annual Rent of £20 or thereabouts, that they quitted the  same about ten or eleven years ago and that he never after did any  Act Matter or thing whatsoever whereby the obtain any subsequent legal settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief; that he died about on or about the  sixteenth day of June last and hath left her this Examinant three legitimate Children who are now living namely Alicia born on or about the 24th. day of July 1787, Thomas born on or about the 12th day of July 1789, and Ann born on or about the 29th day of March 1794 that she is unable to support herself and family without assistance

Sworn the 25th day of July 1794 before us } N Bond Edwd: Naish

Elizth Lawrence 's Mark

Witness B Newman
