Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Margaret Carr aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Francis Todd in Clement Inn in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Nine years or thereabouts at the yearly Wages of Six Pounds that she hath quitted said Service about Six years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief she might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 8th. day of Novr. 1797 before me G S Goodenough

her Margaret Carr mark

A W Burbidge Beadle
