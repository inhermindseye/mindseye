Middx & Westmr}  Ann Rowe Aged about 20 Years on her Oath says she never was married Rented house paid Taxes Served any Apprenticeship or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Says her father Willm. Rowe Rented and lived in a House in Middlesex Court Long Acre in the Parish of St. Martins in the Fields in the Liberty of Westminster at the Year Rent of £10 or thereabouts and paid all Parish Taxes due for the same hath quitted the same upwards of eight Years ago since which time neither her said Father nor this Examinant hath or ever gained any other subseqt. Settlemt.

Sworn this 3d. Day of Decr. 1776  David Walker R. Butler

her Ann Rowe
