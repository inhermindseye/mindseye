Middx  Margt Crone Aged upwards of 40 Years Upon Oath Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr Hoy in Stanhope Street in the Parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middx for One Year and Upwards at the Yearly Wages of Five Pounds and hath quitted her said Service upwards of Six Years and hath not since lived a hired Yearly Servt Rented a House or paid any Taxes

Sworn February 2 1763 Before}
