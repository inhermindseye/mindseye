Middx to wit David George Opiney  Aged about Nineteen Years On his Oath Says he never was married Rented House paid Taxes was an Apprentice or ever lived a hired Yearly Servant for One Year Says his Father Joseph Ocum Opiney Rented a House in Little Duke Street  Moor Fields in the Parish of St. Leonard Shoeditch in the County of Middx upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards and paid Taxes due for the same hath quitted said House about Ten Years ago since which he never gained any other Settlement

Sworn this 24th. day of March 1784 before R Butler Edward Hodsoll

his David George Openey Mark
