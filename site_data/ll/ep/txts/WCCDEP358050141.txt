Westmr.  Mary Witchell (on behalf of Eliz: Witchell her Sister aged about 19 years upon Oath saith the sd. Eliz. Witchell is the Daughter of Francis Witchell by Hannah his Wife both deceased and that the said Francis Witchell lived in and rented a house in Market Lane in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westmr. of the Yearly Rent of Ten pounds and upwards and lived therein upwards of two Years Says the said Eliz. Witchell never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to this Examinants knowledge or beleif.

Sworn 11th. Novr 1749 before H Fielding

Mary Witchell
