Middx The Examination of Elizth. Pendrell Passed from the Parish, of St. Giles in the Fields in ye. County of Middx by Order bearing date ye. 10th. of Novr. Inst. Who upon her Oath saith that her Husband Thos. Pendrell was bound an Apprentice to Pendrell his Father who was an Apothecary in Duke Street in ye. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in ye. same County & Served him as an Apprentice for the space of Seven Years And this Examint. further saith that her sd. Husband never Rented any House Paid Parish Taxes lived with any other Person as an Apprentice or Yearly hired Servt. for ye. space of one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleife That she is very Poor & not able to maintaine herselfe

Sworne the 14th. Day of Novr. 1739 before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of A Elizth. Pendrell
