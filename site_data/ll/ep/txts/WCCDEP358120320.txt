Middx  Ann Oliver aged upwards of 50 years upon Oath Saith she is the wido . of George Oliver and that since the Decease of her Said late husband she rented a house in Hemlock Court in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes at the yearly rent of Sixteen pounds & lived therein upwards of one year, that She quitted the said house abt. 5 Years Ago and has not Since rented ten pounds by the year paid Parochial Taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn April 4th. 1755 before} Fran: Bedwell

The mark of Ann Oliver
