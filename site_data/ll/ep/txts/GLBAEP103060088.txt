Aldgate Middx

Isaac Jones Maketh Oath that about Five and Twenty years ago (he being then a Single Person) Went and livd as a hired Servant by the year with John Carter of the Butcher Rowe in the Precinct of Saint Catherine near the Tower  Butcher with whom he livd upwards of five years together at four pound a year for four years and had five pounds the last year and hath gaind no Settlement, Since hath a wife named Hannah and one Child named Elizabeth aged Nine years

Sworn 25th day September 1747 before Robert Dennett Richd Ricards

Isaac Jones
