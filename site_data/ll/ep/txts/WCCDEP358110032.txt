Middx  Richd. Brookings upon Oath saith he intermarried with his Wife Ann at the Fleet London on the 26th. of June 1746 by whom he hath two Children John aged upwds. of two Years and Honor aged upwds. of 5 months and that whilst a Singleman he lived a yearly Servant with Mr. Anthy. Davie a Farmer in the Parish of Tiverton in the County of Devon for one Year and nine months at the Yearly Wages of three pounds ten Shillings for the first Year and four pounds for the Second Year quitted his said Service upwds. of Ten yrs. ago and has not since rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to his knowledge or belief

Sworn March 25th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

Richard Brookings
