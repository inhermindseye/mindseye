Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Sarah Farrer aged about 31 years upon her Oath saith that about Eleven years ago she Intermarried with Michael Farrer her present Husband at the Parish Church of Saint Giles in the fields by whom she hath Two Children living Vizt. Samuel aged about five years and Thomas aged about Thr years That her said Husband served Five years Apprenticeship to one Mr. Cooper a Pawnbroker the last Forty days of which Apprenticeship was in Wych Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster and that after her said Husband was out of his Apprenticeship he never Rented any House Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act matter or thing whereby he might have gained a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 24th- day of March 1797 } John Collick

Sarah Farra
