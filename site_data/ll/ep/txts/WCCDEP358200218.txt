Middx  Susannah Beadle Aged about Twenty Nine Years upon Oath Says she is the Wife of Henry Beadle with whom she Intermarried at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch in the County of Middx on the 9th. of November 1758 Says her said Husband never was an Apprentice Rented House paid Taxes or Lived a Hired Yearly Servant Says his Father Rented a House in Clemt. Inn Passage in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . One Year and upwards at the Yearly Rent of Sixteen Pounds as she has been Informed & which Information she Verily Beleives to be true.

Sworn the 18th of Jany 1765 Before} Benjamin Cox

The Mark of Susan Beadle
