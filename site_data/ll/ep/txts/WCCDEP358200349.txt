Westmr.  Geo: Stone aged about 35 Years upon Oath says he intermarried with Hannah his Wife in the Parish Church of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about Nine Years ago by whom he has two Children (to wit) George aged near Eight Years and Ambrose aged about Two Months, says he Rented a house in Ship Yard in the aforesaid Parish of St. Clement Danes of the Yearly Rent of Twelve Pounds lived therein upwards of two Years and hath quitted same about Eight Years and hath not since Rented the Yearly Value of Ten Pounds or paid Taxes

Sworn the 22d. Jany. 1766, before me} Frans Cary

George Stone
