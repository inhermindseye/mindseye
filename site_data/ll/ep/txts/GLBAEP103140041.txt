Middlesex ss}  Cornelius Manning  Mariner aged about Fifty four years maketh oath that he was born in Cork in the Kingdom of Ireland And this Deponent Saith that to his knowledge or belief that he never did any Act to gain a Settlement for himself in any Place in the Kingdom of Great Britain and that he hath a wife named Elizabeth to whom he was lawfully married.

Sworn this day of June 1774 before}
