Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit James Talboys One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes maketh Oath and Saith that he has been credibly Informed which Information he believes to be true that Rebecca Cox the Mother of Richd. Cox an Infant aged about 4 years born a Bastard in a Licensed Hospital lived as a hired yearly Servant when Single at the Dyers Arms in Cole Harbour in the Parish of Allhallows the Less in the City of London for 12 Months and upwards at the yearly wages of £4 or £5 that after she quitted said Service she has done no act to gain any other Settlement

Sworn this 4th. day of Febry. 1788  Sampr Wright  Wm. Hitchiner

James Talboys
