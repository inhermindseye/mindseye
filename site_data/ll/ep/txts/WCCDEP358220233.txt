Westmr. to wit Elizth. Young Aged about Twenty four Years on her Oath Says she never was married nor Ever Rented House or Paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with one Benjamin Birch a Cloth Drawer in Bell Alley in the Parish of St. Stephen Walbrook in the City of London upwards of four Years at the Yearly Wages of four Pounds Ten Shillings hath quitted her said Service about three Years ago and hath not since she quitted the same ever Rented Ten Pounds a Year Paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 5th. day of February 1771 before} Petr Planck  Thos. Kynaston

Elizabeth Young
