Westmr.  Joshua Glassington Aged about Thirty Six Years Upon Oath Says he was bound to and Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with his Father Joshua Glassington a Butcher in Clare Market And further Says that his said Father Rented and lived in a House in Horse Shoe Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the liberty of Westminster for Several Years and paid all Parish Taxes for the same And this Depont further Says that after he had Served his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 9th: Day of Janry 1769 Before me} Tho Lane

Josa. Glassington
