Middx & Westr} to wit Elizabeth Plowman Aged about Twenty five Years in her Oath Says she never was Married Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year, Says her father John Plowman Served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Mr. Love a Dyer facing Essex Street in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , and afterwards lived as a Journeyman with the said Mr. Love for several Years afterwards, but at what Wages this Examinant does not know, and after Quitting his said Service he never Rented House paid Taxes or otherwise gained a Subsequent Settlement and further upon her Voluntary Oath says that on the 3 1st day of July 1770 , she was delivered of a female Bastard Child in Green Willow Court in Morris's Rents in the Parish of Saint Margaret Westminster which has since been baptized by the name of Elizabeth And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is Pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes she being poor and unable to maintain the same when born and this One Thomas Price   Ostler who now lives with Mr. Edward Avery in the Haymarket is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of her body sometime in the Month of September last but the Particular Day she does not now remember and at several different times afterward at one of which times he got her with Child of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant and that the said Thomas Price and no other Person did beget the Same and that he is the real and true father of the first mentioned Bastard Child & of the Child or Children of which she is now Pregnant

Sworn this 16th day of June 1775 Before M Addington

her Elizabeth Plowman Mark Witness James Viger
