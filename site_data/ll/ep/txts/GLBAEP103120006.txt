Middlesex ss}  Mary Power maketh oath That She knows John Bristow a Negro Man now under Sentence of Transportation in Newgate and this Depont. also knew his late Wife Susannah Bristow deced and this Deponent also knows a Child named Sarah Clarke aged about Eleven years the Daughter of the Said Susannah Bristow by Thomas Clarke her former husband but whether he be living or Dead She doth not know nor Can set forth and this Depont. hath been informed by the Mother of the said Child & which this Depont. believes to be true that the said Child Sarah Clarke was born in the Town of Portsmouth in the County of Southampton And this Depont. Can give no account respecting the Settlement of the Parents of the Said Child or either of them or of the said Child save her Birth as aforesaid.

Sworn this 9 Day of June 1772 before Cha: Digby W: Russell

The Mark of Mary Power
