Westmr.  Mary Ilderton on behalf of William a Child aged near 4 Years upon Oath Saith she said William is the Son of Cathn. Ilderton  Singlewoman and was born at Mr Rawlins in Crown Court Duke Street in the Parish of St Margaret in the City of Westminster .

Sworn March 8th. 1753 before} Tho Lane .

The Mark of Mary Ilderton
