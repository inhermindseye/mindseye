Middlesex ss}  Mary Morgan the wife of Walter Morgan maketh oath That her said husband is now absent from her on board his Majestiesship the Conquestadore That he has informed her & which she believes to be true that he was hired & lived as a hired Servant by & with one Mr Jones in the Parish of Lanarth in the County of Monmouth & Continued in such service for the Space of a year & upwards & has not Since done any Act to gain a subsequent settlement to her knowledge or belief & that she was lawfully married to her said husband

Sworn this 23 day of March 1780 before  Jasper Clarke

The Mark of Mary Morgan
