Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Elizabeth Frost Aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds paid Taxes lived as hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or ever bound an apprentice That her Father George Frost Rents and lives in a House in George alley  Fleet Market in the Parish of Saint Brides alias Bridgets in the City of London of the yearly Value of Ten Pounds and upwards

Sworn this 23rd day of Octr 1799 before us} Saml. Edwards J Reeves

Elibeth Frost
