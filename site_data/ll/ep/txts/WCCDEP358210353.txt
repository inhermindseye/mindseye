Westmr.  William Johnson Aged About Thirty Years upon Oath Says that about Six Years ago he lived as a Covenant Servant with John Bradshaw a Glazier and Painter at Hide Park Corner in the Parish of St George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex upwards of Two Years at the Yearly Wages of Thirty Pounds and upwards and never Since Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year Since that time

Sworn this 11th Day of January 1768 Before} J Fielding W Kelynge

his William Johnson Mark
