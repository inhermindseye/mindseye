Westmr.  Mary Green aged abt. 43 Years upon Oath says she is the Widow of John Green who has been dead about two Years with whom she intermarried in the Old Church in Birmingham in the County of Warwick about Eighteen Years since; Says her said late husband whilst he was a Single Man lived a hired Yearly Servant with Dr. Alltree in Norfolk Street in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex for three Years or thereabouts at the Yearly Wages of and never Rented house or paid Taxes says she has two Children by her said late Husband, Sarah aged about Ten Years & Joseph aged abt. Six Years

Sworn the Decr. 1765 before}
