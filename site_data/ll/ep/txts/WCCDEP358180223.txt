Middx  Mary Baines aged 22 Years upon her Oath saith that she was never married that about five Years since she lived a Yearly hired Servant with Mr. Upton Pastry Cook in Grays Inn passage in the parish of St. Andrew in Holbourn above Barrs in the County of Middlesex at the Yearly Wages of £3: for the Space of one Year & upwards that since she left her sd. Service she hath not rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived with any person as a Yearly hired Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworne 1 Day of Augt. 1744 }
