Westmr.  Eliz Furlong upon Oath saith she Intermarried with her husband Richard Furlong at the Pound Church in Dublin about twenty eight Years ago and that her sd. husband upwards of Eight Years since rented a house of Ten pounds a Year in Kings head Court in Drury Lane in the Parish of St Clemt. Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . and lived therein about Six months and has not since he quitted the said house rented Ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived & Yearly Servt. for one Years or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Janry 6th. 1749 before} Tho Lane

The mark of Eliz Furlong
