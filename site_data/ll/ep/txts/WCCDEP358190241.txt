Middx & Westmr}  Prudence Meredith aged about 24 years Lodging in Mr. Poole's Tenements in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clement Danes and County of Middx upon Oath saith she never was Married or bound an Apprentice, That she lived a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Hawkes a Brazier the Corner of Surry Street in the Strand in the said Parish of St. Clement Danes for one year & upwards at the yearly Wages of £4 quitted the same about Four years and hath not since rented any House or been a hired Servant for one year And further upon her Voluntary Oath says she is now pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be Born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable be the said Parish of St. Clement Danes she being Poor and unable to maintain the same when Born and that one Robert Rosser (who lives Clerk with Mr. Fleming an attorney at No. 9 in Castle yard Holborn ) did beget such Child or Children he having had Carnal Knowledge of her Body in the Month of September 1761 and at several other times since at one of which times he did beget her with Child of the Child or Children with which she is now Pregnant

Sworn the 27th day of April 1762 Before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Prud: Meredith
