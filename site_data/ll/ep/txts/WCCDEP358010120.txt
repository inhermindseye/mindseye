Saint Clement Danes

Middx & Westmr.} to wit Elizth. Henderson aged upwards of 50 years upon her Oath saith that she is the Widow of Isaac Henderson who has been dead about 16 years & with whom she Intermarried at the Parish of St. Clement Danes about 30 years ago and by whom she hath no Children now living Says her said late Husband was bound an Apprentice to one Mr. Johnson a Buck maker in Church Court in the Parish of Saint Martin in the fields and served his said Master for 5 years or thereabouts and then her said Husband Listed and went for a Soldier and never was Assigned over to any other Person neither did her said late Husband gain any other subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or otherwise howsoever

Sworn this 23rd. day of July 1787 Maddington Edw: Read

her Elizth. Henderson mark
