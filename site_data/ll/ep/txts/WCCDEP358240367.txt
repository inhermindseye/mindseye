St Clement Danes July 3d. 1792 2d. this Settlemt

Middlesex and Westminster }  St George Hanover Square ye this a Right Settlemt as a Man & Woman of this Name Davis settlemt in St James Vide Entry page 78 this Book  Joseph Baker aged 37 Years & upward Upon his Oath saith that he intermarried with Margaret Baker his present Wife at the parish Church of St James in the Liberty of Westminster (by Banns) that him and his said Wife since their intermarriage (that is to say) th about two years ago they took a House No 1 in Lecs Mews North Audley Street in the parish of St George Hanover Square in the County of Middlesex and lived there upwards of one year at the yearly Rent of £15 that since they left the said House they have been Weekly Lodgings and have never Since Rented Any Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes that he never lived As An hired Yearly Servant for one year nor Sai has done Any other Act Matter or thing whereby he might have gained any subsequent Settlement to the best of this Examinant's knowledge information or belief

Sworn the 3d. July 1792 before Me W Addington Jas Dyson

Joseph Baker
