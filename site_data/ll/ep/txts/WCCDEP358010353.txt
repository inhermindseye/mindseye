Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Joseph White aged about 50 years upon his Oath saith that about Eight years ago he Intermarried with Ann his present Wife at the Parish Church of Saint Olave Southwark That he this Examinant before his Intermarriage with his said Wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with a Mr. Angell a Sugar Baker in Well Close Square in the Parish of Saint George in the last in the County of Middlesex for about Two years at the yearly Wages of Thirteen Pounds that he hath quitted said Service about Fifteen years since which time he has not lived as a hired yearly Servant for one year Rented any how Tenement or Lodging of Ten Pounds a year paid Taxes or done any Act to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 3d. day of Augt. 1790 N Bond Wm. Kitchiner

Joseph White
