Middx & Westmr.}  Elizth. Williams aged about Thirty Eight years on her Oath says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes says she lived a hired yearly Servant with one John Hawkins a Linnen Draper at Pimlico in the Parish of St. George Hanover Square in the Liberty of Westmr . & County of Middx for three years at the yearly Wages of Eight Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of seven Years ago since which time she hath gained no other Subsequent Settlemt.

Sworn this 4th. day of Decr. 1783  Sampn Wright Thos. Gilbert .

Her Elizth Williams mark
