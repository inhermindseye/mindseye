Patrick Agan Aged upwards of Sixty Years upon Oath Says he Intermarried with his Wife Margaret at the Fleet London about Twenty Eight Years ago Says he Rented a House in Bennetts Court in the Parish of St. Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westmr . upwards of One Year at the Yearly Rent of Ten Pounds hath quitted said Service about Twenty Years & hath not Since Rented House or paid Taxes

Sworn the 8th of Octr. 1764 Before}
