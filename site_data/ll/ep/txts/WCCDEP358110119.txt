Middx  Ann Stevens aged about 15 Years upon Oath Saith she never was married nor ever was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year rented ten pounds a Year or paid parish taxes that she was born (as she hath been informed & verily believes) in the parish of St. Mary le Strand in the Liberty of Westmr . and can give no Account of the Settlement of her father & mother

Sworn Novr. 23d. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Ann Stevens
