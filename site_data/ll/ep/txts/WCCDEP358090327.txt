Middx. to wit Heny. Wharton aged about 43 years on his Oath says's he Intermarried to Susannah his wife at the Parish Church of Saint James Clerkenwell upwards of 18 Years ago and by whom he hath three Children now living two of whom are now provided for & one unprovided for namely Thomas aged about 12 years says before his Intermarriage with the said wife and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Boston who kept the si of the swan in Shoe Lane in the Parish of St. Andrew Holb London for 4 years at the yearly wages of five Pounds be Board washing & Lodging hath quitted his said service about Thirty years ago since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House or Tenement of Ten Pounds a year paying Taxes or other howsoever

Sworn this 15th. day of Augt. 1782 before  Thos: Gilbert  Sampn Wright

Henry Wharton
