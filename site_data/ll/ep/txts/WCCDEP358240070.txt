Thomas illegitimate Child of Ann Egerton

St Clement Danes January 18th. 1792.

Middlesex & Westminster }  St Paul Covent Garden  (to wit) Richard Dobbins One of the Beadles of the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes on (behalf of The Illegitimate Child of Ann Egerton Named Thomas Aged about three Months left and deserted by his Mother) Upon her Oath saith that he hath been Informed which he verily believes to be true that the said Child is a Male Bastard Child was born of the Body of Ann Egerton on the Seventeenth day of October last at the House of Mr. Bainbridge No. 14 in Denmark Court in the Parish of St. Paul Covent Garden in the Liberty of Westminster in the County of Middlesex And that the said Child hath since been Be half Baptized by the Name of Thomas but had no Surname given it And Lastly that the said Child hath been left by the Mother in the House Celenging to this parish and is become chargeable to the same

Sworn the 19th. day of January 1792. before Us B: Kennett H. Dive
