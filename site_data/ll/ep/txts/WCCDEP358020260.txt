St. Clement Danes

Middx & Westminster} (to wit) Sarah Lee Aged 58 Years or thereabouts Upon her Oath saith that about 1762 she was Married to her late Husband Joshua Lee at the Parish Church of Ellasmere in the County of Shropshire Salop that her said late Husband in his life time Rented & lived in a House in Chair Court Ship Yard in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex about three Years at the Annual Rent of £12. that they she & her said Husband quitted the said House abt. three years ago and that since, her said said Husband had not in his life time nor has she this Examinant since his Decease did to the best of her Knowledge Information or belief gained any legal Settlement And that she is Poor and unable to Maintain herself

Sworn the 31st. March 1791 before Me R Butler

Sarah Lee 's Mark
