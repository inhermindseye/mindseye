Middx  Alice Cooper  Wido . of John Cooper who dyed in the Year 1723 upon Oath Saith she lived a Yearly Servant with Mrs. Susa. Rose in Houghton Street in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx at the Yearly Wages of Four pounds from April 1727 to June 1728 and was all that time a Wido . without Child or Children and hath not Since she Quitted her sd Service Rented ten Pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt in any other Place to her Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Novr. 13th. 1751 before  Fran: Bedwell

Alice Cooper
