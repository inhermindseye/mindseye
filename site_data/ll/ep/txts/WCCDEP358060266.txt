Westmr.  Ralph Jackson upon Oath saith he Intermarried with his Wife  Eleanr. Jackson at St Brides Church in Westchester upwds. of 7 Years ago and that he Served his apprentiship with his father in Bridge Strt . in the parish of St. Michael in Westchester aforesd & never gained a Subsequent Settlemt. to his Knowledge or beleif

Sworn Feb. 19th. 1752 before  Fran: Bedwell

Ralph Jackson
