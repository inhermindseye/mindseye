Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex to wit Elizabeth Thompson aged about 32 years upon her Oath Saith that She Intermarried with Wm. Thompson in the year 1775 in Dublin according the Rites and Ceremonies of the Popish Religion that She this Examinant is an Irishwoman and never did any Act to gain a Settlement in England and further this Examinant upon her Oath saith that she has 4 Children living illegitimate vizt. James aged about 6 years born at Mr. Folke in Houghton Street  John aged about 4 years born at the House of a Mrs. Guy in New Street Fetter Lane in the Parish of Saint Brides alias Bridget in the City of London  Robert aged about Eighteen Months born at the House of a Mr. Pailov in Clements Lane and Sarah aged about Five Weeks at the house of a Mr. Palmer in Ship Yard That James Palmer is father of the said Children who has deserted this Examinant

Sworn this 23d. day of May 1789 R Butler D Walker

Elizabeth Tompson
