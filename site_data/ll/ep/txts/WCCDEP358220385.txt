Westmr. to wit Thomas Thompson Aged about Thirty Seven Years on his Oath Says he never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with one Alexander Mc. Culloch a Printer at the Lamb and Bible near Palsgrave head Court in the Strand in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in County of Middlesex and after he was out of his said Apprenticeship he never Rented House paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn this 17th. day of Septr. 1772 before W Kelyngs

Tho. Thompson
