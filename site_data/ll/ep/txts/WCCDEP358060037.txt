Middx  Alice Binfield  Wido . of John Binfield who dyed upwds. of 30 Years ago upon her Oath saith that since the Decease of her said late husband she rented a house in Star Court in the Butcher Row in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes Middlesex of the Yearly Rent of Ten Guineas (the Landlord paying the Water Rent) and lived therein about 20 Yrs. and that she paid towards the Poors Rate of the said Parish and hath quitted the sd house upwds. of 3 Years, since which she has not rented Ten Pounds a Year paid Parish taxes lived a Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlement in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn Decr 18th. 1750 before  Fran: Bedwell

Aice Binfield
