Middx  Sarah Orr upon Oath saith she is the widow of Willm. Orr who was an Irishman & never gained a Settlemt. in England and that whilst she was a Singlewoman she lived a yearly Servant with Mr John Garnet in Change Court in the parish of St Clement Danes in the County of Middlxs for three yrs. & upwds at the yearly wages of three pounds for the first year and four pounds a year for the Remainder of the sd Term and has not since she left Mr Garnet Rented ten pounds a year paid Parish Taxes or lived a yearly Servant for one year

Sworn Novr. 13th. 1752 before} Fran: Bedwell

The Mark of Sarh. Orr
