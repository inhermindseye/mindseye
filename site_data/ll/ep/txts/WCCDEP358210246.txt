Westmr.  John Cartwright Aged about Forty three Years Upon Oath says he Intermarried with Elizabeth his Wife in the Parish of Benson in Oxfordshire about Six Years ago, Says he served Seven Years Apprenticeship with Mr. George Cornelius  Peruke Maker in Devereux Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex , and after he served said Apprenticeship never Rented Ten Pounds a Year, paid Taxes or lived a hired Yearly Servant for one Year

Sworn the 11th. day of February 1768 , Before W Kelynge

John. Cartwright
