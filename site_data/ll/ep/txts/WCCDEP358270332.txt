Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Grace Butler aged about 27 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married That she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Smith in Grange Court in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the Liberty of Westminster for Fifteen Months at the yearly wages of Eight Guineas that she hath quitted said Service about Two years since which time she hath not lived as a hired yearly Servant for Twelve Months or done any Act Matter or thing whereby she might have gained a subsequent Settlement And further this Deponent upon her Voluntary Oath saith that she is pregnant with Child or Children which is or are likely to be born a Bastard or Bastards and to become Chargeable to the said Parish That one Thomas Hardy a Journeyman Taylor who lives at a Bakers shop in King Street  Drury Lane in the father thereof he having had carnal knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the said Child or Children of which she is now pregnant and that the said Thomas Hardy is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 10th. day of Octr. 1797 before me } J Hunter

her Grace Butler Mark

Witness AW Burbidge
