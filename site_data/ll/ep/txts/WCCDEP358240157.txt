St. Clement Danes September 7th 1792

Middlesex and Westminster }  St Ann  to wit Mary Priest aged 56 years and upwards Upon Wife of Willm. Priest Upon Oath saith that she was Married to her present Husband William Priest about 38 years ago in the City of Dublin according to the Rights and Ceremonies of the Church of England that her said Husband hath Rented and Resided in the House where in he now dwells for several years that she is informed and verily believe that he paid the Annual Rent of £ 24 for the same And this Deponent saith that the said House is situate in Great Chapel Street in the parish of St. Ann in the Liberty of Westminster in the said County that she this Deponent hath been parted from her said Husband many years and that She is now distressed and unable to maintain herself without Assistance

Sworn the 7th day of September 1792 before us  M Addington Tho: Gordons

Mary Priest
