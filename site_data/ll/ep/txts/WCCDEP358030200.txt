Frances Thomas  Wife of Philip Thomas (gone from her) upon Oath saith she intermarried with the sd. Philip Thomas on or abt. the 13th. Day of Octr. 1717 at the Parish Church of St. Paul Covt. Garden Says her sd. husbd. lived in & rented a house in Exeter Court in the Strand in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of £25 & lived there in abt. ten Yrs. & hath left same abt. 19 Yrs. Says her sd. husbd. hath not since he left sd. house rented ten pounds a Year paid Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or beleif

Sworn Augt. 9th 1746 before  Wm: Jones

Frances Thomas
