Middlesex & Westminster}  passd. to St. Paul Covt. Garden (to wit) Mary Earle aged 50 Years or thereabouts upon her Oath saith That she intermarried with James Earle her now Husband about Sixteen Years ago, at the Parish Church of Saint James in the Liberty of Westminster - That since their Intermarriage her said Husband rented Lodgings Demark Court in the Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden in the County of Middlesex of one Startings a Taylor at the Yearly rent of 10s. that they lived in their said Lodging for upwards of forty Days and since they quitted the same her said Husband has not done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to this Deponents Knowledge Information or Belief he may have gained a Settlement elsewhere therein the said Parish of Saint Paul Covent Garden aforesaid, That this Examinant is poor and unable to maintain herself

Sworn this 11th. Novr 1784 before M Addington Thos. Gilbert

Mary Earle
