Middlesex ss} The Voluntary Examination of Elizabeth Gerrett of the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County Singlewoman taken on oath before me one of his Majesties Justices of the Peace in & for the Sd County this 11th. day of October 1776 -Who Saith That in the Month of August one thousand Seven hundred & Seventy one at the Dwelling House one Wm Knox in Nightingale Lane in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate aforesd She the Sd Elizabeth Gerrett was delivered of a Female Bastard Child and that the Said Bastard Child is become Chargeable to the Said parish of St Botolph Aldgate & hath been baptized by the name of Elizabeth Leaver and that one Samuel Leaver late of Birchin Lane London  Taylor & now on Board the Ship Gatton Captn Money Commr on a Voyage to the East Indies did get her with child of the Said Bastard Child.

Taken & Sworn the day & year above written  Parkr Rowlands

E. Gerrett
