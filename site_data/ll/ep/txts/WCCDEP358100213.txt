Middlesex & Westmr}  Sarah Hoyle Aged about fifty Years on her Oath Says she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes Says she lived a hired Yearly Servant with Mr. Robert Welch a Surgeon in Panton Square near the Haymarkett in the Parish of St. James in the Liberty of Westminster for Eighteen Months at the Yearly Wages of Nine Pounds hath quitted her said Service upwards of Two Years ago since which time she never gained any other Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 18th. day of May 1784 before W Addington Thos: Gilbert :

Sara Hoyle
