Middx  Alice Wilkinson (on behalf of John Silk , Sarah Silk & Jonas Silk the Children of Jonas Silk by Jane his wife) maketh Oath that the sd Jonas Silk , the father of the above Children rented a house in Exeter Street in the parish of St. Paul Covent Garden  in the Liberty of Westmr. in the year 1749 and paid to the poor Rate of the sd. parish and that since he quitted the sd house the sd. Jonas Silk has not rented ten pounds a year nor paid Parish Taxes as this Examinant has been informed & believes

Sworn 9th. Decr 1754 before} Tho Lane

The Mark of Alice Wilkinson
