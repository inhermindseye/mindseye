Saint Clement Danes

Middlesex and Westminster } to wit Benjamin Newman One of the Beadles of the Parish of Saint Clement Danes on behalf of Mary Ann East who is ill Maketh Oath and Saith that he hath been informed which Information he verily believes to be true that the said Mary Ann East never was married lived as a hired yearly servant for Twelve Months bound an Apprentice Rented any house Tenement or Lodging of Ten pounds a year or paid Taxes That her Father East lived upwards of Twelve Months as a hired yearly Servant with Messrs. North Hoare Nanson and Simpson in Fleet Street in the Parish of Saint Dunstan in the West in the City of London and now is in the Workhouse belonging to the said Parish And this Deponent further Saith that the said Mary Ann East is now Chargeable to the said Parish of Saint Clement Danes

Sworn this 29th. day of Janry. 1798 before us G S Goodenough W Carter

B. Newman
