Middx  Ann Johnson aged abt. 33 years upon Oath says she is the Wife of Ralph Johnson (now at Sea ) says her said Husband was born in the Parish of St. Andrew in the Shire of Inverness in Scotland & never Rented ten Pounds a year paid Parish Taxes was an Apprentice or yearly Servant as her said Husband informed her & as she verily believes

Sworn this 15th. day of August 1757 before me} Theodr Sydenham

The Mark of Ann Johnson
