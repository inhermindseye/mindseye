Saint Clement Danes

Middx and Westmr.} to wit Mary Bayley aged about 24 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married that she Rented and lived in a house in Newman Street in the parish of Saint Mary Le Bone in the County of Middlesex for about Nine Months at the yearly Rent of Thirty two Pounds that she hath quitted said house about Nine Months since which time she hath done no Act Matter or thing to gain a subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 25th. day of Augt. 1795 .} Rupt Clarke N Bond

Her Mary Bayley Mark
