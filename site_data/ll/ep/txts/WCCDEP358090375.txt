Middx to wit Thos. Chaddock Aged about Fifty Nine Years on his Oath Says he Intermarried to his late Wife  Mary at the Parish Church of St. Leonard Shoreditch about Twenty Years ago & who has been dead about 12 Years Says he was bound an Apprentice for Seven Years to Wm Strahan  Esqr . A Printer in New Street in the Parish of Saint Bride otherwise Bridgett London and served the sd. Mr Strahan for five Years the first part of his Apprenticeship & afterwards ran away & went to Sea and further Says he never gained any other Settlemt. than as afsd. either by Renting House Tenemt. or Lodging of £10. a year paying Taxes or living a hired Yearly Servant for one year.

Sworn this 27th. day of Feby. 1783 before R Butler Thos: Gilbert

Thos. Chaddock
