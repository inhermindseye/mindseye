Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Lucy Evans aged about 21 years upon her Oath saith that she never was married Rented House or paid Taxes that she lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Clarke in Portugal Street in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middx for Two years or thereabouts at the yearly wages of £6 that she hath quitted said Service about 6 Months since which time she has done no Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And further this Examinant upon her Oath Saith that on the 28th. day of November last she was delivered of a Female Bastard Child at a Licensed House in George Court Water Lane which hath since been Christened by the Name of Mary That one David Jenkins an Attorney as this Deponent believes who lodges at No. 7 in Breams Buildings Chancery Lane is the father thereof he having had Carnal Knowledge of this Examinants Body and begotten the same thereon and that the said David Jenkins is the only Real and true father thereof

Sworn this 30th. day of Janry. 1788 } R Butler

her Lucy Evans Mark

Lucy Evans lodges at No. 45 Red Lyon Street

David Jenkins is a Tall Stout Man wears his Hair tied behind fair Complexion

Man taken and Bond given
