Westmr.  Margaret Davis aged about Seventeen Years upon Oath says, she never was Married, and that she was bound Apprentice by the Churchwardens and Overseers of the Poor of the Parish of St. Anne in the Liberty of Westminster by Indenture dated the 3rd, day of November 1762, to James Robinson the corner of Denmark Street in the Parish of St. Giles in the fields in the County of Middlesex  Baker untill she should Attain the Age of Twenty one Years or day of Marriage says she served her said Master from the day of the date of the said Indenture of Apprenticeship untill the Month of January 1766, and that on the 30th day of the said Month of January 1766, she was discharged from her said Apprenticeship by Saunders Welch and Edmund Byron Esqrs. two of his Majesty's Justices of the Peace for the County of Middlesex on the Complaint of the said James Robinson for reasons in the said discharge assigned; and this Examinant further says she hath not been bound Apprentice to any Person Since her discharge from her said Master.

Sworn the 3rd. day of October 1766 , Before} Paul Vaillant  Fran. Cary

The Mark of Margt. Davis
