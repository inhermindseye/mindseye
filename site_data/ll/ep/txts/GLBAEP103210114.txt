Middlesex ss  Sarah Mercer the wife of Thomas Mercer maketh oath that her said husband is now on a Voyage to the west Indies That she this Deponent can give us Account respecting the place of Settlement of her said husband nor of his Parents or either of them That she this Depont Previous to her marriage with her said husband was hired & lived as a hired Servant at Certain Wages by & with Mr Sutherland a Victualler in the Parish of St Botolph without Aldgate in the Sd County at the Tackle & Block Alehouse there & Continued there in such service under such hiring for the space of a year previous to her said marriage That she has not since done any Act to gain another Settlement elsewhere save what she acquired by marriage with her said husband to whom she was lawfully married

Sworn this 8 day of November 1785 before R Davies

The Mark of Sarah Mercer
