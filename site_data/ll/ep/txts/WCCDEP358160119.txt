Middx  The Exaicon of Catherine the wife of Patrick Killaley Lodging in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes in the County of Middx taken the 7th. day of March 1739 Who upon her Oath Saith that abt. 12 Years since she Intermarryed with the above named Patrick Killaley & that her sd. Husband Rented a House in Drury Lane in the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes of the Yearly Rent of Twenty four Pounds for the Space of two Years & hath been come away from the same abt. Ten Months & hath not since rented Ten Pounds a Year pd. Parish Taxes or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other Place to her knowledge or beleife that her sd. Husband hath Absconded from her & having one Child (to wit) Thos . Aged abt. two Years & being Pregnt. with Child & near her time is not able to maintaine hereselfe

Sworn the day & Year above menconed before} Jas: Gronous

The Mark of Cath: Killaley
