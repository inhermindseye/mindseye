Middx  Rebecca Gibbons Wido. of Rd. Gibbons upon Oath Saith She intermarried with the sd. Richd. Gibbons in the parish Church of St. Giles in the Fields abt. 10 Yrs. ago & that She hath been Informed & verily believes her sd. late husband Served his Apprentiship with Mr Isaac Salmon a Shoemaker in Arundel Strt. in the parish of St. Clement Danes in the County of Middx and never rented ten pounds a Year pd. parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one year & that she this Ext. has not Since the Decease of her sd. late husband Rented ten pounds a Year paid parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one year

Sworn Decr 12th. 1752 before}

Rebecca Gibbons
