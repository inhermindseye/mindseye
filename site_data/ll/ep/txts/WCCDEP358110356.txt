Middx  Elizath. Roberts aged Thirty Six Years upon her Voluntary Oath Saith she never was married and that in the Month of December last she was delivered of a male bastard Child and Mr. Austine in Drury Lane in the Parish of St. Clemt. Danes which was baptized Jno . & is & likely to become Chargeable to the sd. Parish of St. Clemt. Danes she being Poor & unable to maintain the same and that she is now Pregnant with Child or Children which is likely to be born a bastard or bastards & to become Chargeable to the afsd. Parish of St Clemt. Danes and that John Ragg a Dresser at Drury Lane Play house did beget the aforesaid Male bastd. Child named John on her body & likewise the sd bastard Child or Childn. with which She is now Pregnant the sd. Jno. Ragg & this Examint. having for Sevl. Years past cohabited together as man & Wife during which he the sd. Regg has had carnal Knowledge of her body Sevl. times at one of which times he got her with Child of the afsd. male bastd. Child named John & of the Bastd. Child or Childn with which she is now Preganant & that the said John Ragg & no other person is the true & real father thereof and this Examinant further upon Sworn Novr 27. 1753 before} her Oath Saith she lived a yearly Servant with sd. Limmerick in Privy Garden Whitchall in the Parish of St. Margt. Westmr .

for one Year & upwards at the Yearly Wages of Six Pounds & has not Since the quitted her sd. Service (which is upwds. of twelve Years age) rented ten Pounds a Yr. pd. Parish taxes or lived a Yearly Servt. for one Year.

Sworn Novr. 27th- 1753 : before} Fran Bedwell  Barth: Hammond

Elizabeth Roberts
