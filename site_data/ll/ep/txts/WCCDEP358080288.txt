Middx & Westmr  John Herbert aged about Thirty Nine years on his Oath says she never was Married Rented House or paid Taxes says her lived a hired yearly Servant with one Mr. Lilley a Silver Smith in Saint John Street in the Parish of Saint Sepulcre without in the County of Middlesex upwards of one year and a half at the Yearly Wages of 4 Pounds hath quitted the said Service near Eighteen years ago Since which time he hath gained no other Subsequent Settlement either by Renting House paying Taxes or living a hired yearly Servant for one year

Sworn this 22nd. day of Septr. 1778 before  Jno. Barnfather  Geo: Reid

his John Herbert Mark
