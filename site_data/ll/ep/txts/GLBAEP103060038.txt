Middx  Henry Beck maketh Oath that About Tenn years agoe he was bound an Apprentice to one Thomas Shean  Cooper at the Corner of King Henrys yard in the Parish of St. Buttolph with out Aldgate in the County of Middx ; and Served him as Such; for the Space of Five years; and then Enterd him Self for a Foot Souldier in in Colonall  John A Mordant s Regiment; hath a Wife Named Ann ; and a boy named Peter aged fifteen Months; the Lawfull Issue of this Deponent declares that he never gained any other Subsequent Settlement to his Knowlidge

Sworn April 30.. 1745 before me  Robert Dennett

Henry Beck
