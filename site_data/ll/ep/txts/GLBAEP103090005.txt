Middlesex ss.  Thomas Kierman maketh oath that he hath been Credibly informed and Verily believes the same to be true That John Carnes late of the Parish of Saint George in the County of Middx  Chandler deced about fifteen Months Ago rented a house in the sd. Parish of Saint George was charged to and Paid the Poors Rates & King Taxes for the Same & by means thereof as this Deponent is advised gained a legal Settlement there And this Deponent Saith that the said John Carnes to this Deponts. knowledge information or belief did not afterwards do any Act to gain a Subsequent Settlement And that the said John Carnes left behind a Widow now living named Mary (who is now detained at Maid Stone in Kent in order to take her Trial at the ensuing Assises there) And also two Children to wit John Aged about four Years and William aged about three Years the lawful Issue of the said John Carnes deced by the Said Mary his Widow And lastly this Deponent Saith that to his knowledge information or belief the said Mary Carnes Since the death of her Said late husband hath not done any Act to gain a Settlement in her own right.

Sworn this 2d day of July 1765 before us  Chris: Scott R Pell

Thos, Kiernan
