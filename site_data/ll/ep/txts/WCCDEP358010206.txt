Saint Clement Danes

Middx to wit Patience Crutchfield aged about 45 years upon her Oath saith that about 21 years ago she Intermarried with James Crutchfield her late Husband at the Parish Church of Saint James Westmr. that her Said late Husband died about 3 years ago that she has Two Children living Vizt. Elizth. Ann aged about 16 years provided for and Thos. aged about 11 years unprovided for that her said late Husband before his Intermarriage to this Examt and whilst he was a Singleman he lived as a hired yearly Servant with Mr. Golthrip in Craven Yard Drury Lane in the Parish of Saint Clement Danes in the County of Middlesex  for 21 years and upwards at the yearly Wages of 16 Guineas & that he lodged and boarded with said Mr. Goltrip the Whole of the time that after he quitted said Service he never Rented any House

Tenement or Lodging of £10 a year paid Taxes or done any Act Matter or Thing whereby to the best of this Examinants knowledge Information or belief he may might have gained a Subsequent Settlement

Sworn this 10th. day of March 1788 R Butler

Patience Crutchfield her Mark
