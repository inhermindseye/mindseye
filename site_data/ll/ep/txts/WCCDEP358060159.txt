Middx  Jane Cameron upon Oath Saith she is the Wido . of Alexr. Cameron a Native of Scotland who never gained a Settlemt. in England to her knowledge or belief and that she hath 4 Children by the said Alexr. Cameron  Jas . aged about 12 Yrs. Jane aged abt. 9 Yrs. Margt . aged upwds. of 3 Yrs. and Alexr . aged upwds. of Ten Months Says her own father John Wallis rented the Bull Inn at Hounslow in the Parish of Hossen in the County of Middx at the Yearly Rent of £10 and upwards for the Space of Five Years & quitted the same near thirty Years since & that she this Examint. never was an Apprentice or Yearly Servant for one Year or otherwise gained a Settlemt. in any other place to her knowledge or belief

Sworn 30th. Augt 1751 before  Geo: Errington

Jane Cameron
