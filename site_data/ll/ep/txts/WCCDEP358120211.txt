Middx  Wm. Adams of Theobalds Court near Red Lyon Square (on behalf of Mark King aged abt. 10 years Cathn King aged 3 Yrs. and William King aged abt. 10 Months the Children of Thos. King by Rose his wife both decd.) upon Oath saith the said late Thos King father of the abovenamed Children Served his Apprentiship with Richard Hagget a Taylor in the Parish of Wells in the County of Norfolk and afterwards lived in a house of his own there and paid to the poor of the sd. parish and that afterwards the Parish Officers of the parish of Wells granted a Certificate to the said Thomas King acknowledging him to be an Inhabitant legally settled in the sd. Parish and that the sd Thomas King never after he left Wells rented ten pounds by the year paid parish taxes nor lived a yearly Servant for one Year to this Examints knowledge or beleif

Sworn Octr 11th. 1754 : before} Fran: Bedwell

William Adams
